Civil police in Manaus detained a 25-year-old man and a 16-year-old boy as they
unloaded 400 kilograms of cannabis from a truck in the west of the city on the
afternoon of 2 May 2017. [@1] Officers arrested the man, Anderson Duarte dos
Santos, and took the teenager into custody at about 15:00 on Rua São Tomé, in
the Comunidade Parque São Pedro, bairro Tarumã. [@1] They were carrying 412
blocks of pressed marijuana, described as skunk, into a house where several
children and an elderly woman were living. [@1]

The DRCO, the state's organised-crime repression department, called it its
largest seizure of the year. [@1] The load was produced in Colombia's Cauca
region and came into Brazil at Tabatinga; traffickers now use the Japurá river
to avoid checks on the Solimões, the DRCO said. [@1] DRCO director Guilherme
Torres said the load would have supplied local drug trafficking. [@1] Police
said the shipment had been tracked from the border since an anonymous tip two
months earlier, and that officers moved in as the drugs were unloaded. [@1] The
two were to be paid about R$10,000 to store the load for a third person expected
to collect it for distribution. [@1]

Anderson Duarte dos Santos was charged with drug trafficking, association for
trafficking and corruption of a minor, and was to appear at a custody hearing at
the Henoch Reis forum in Manaus. [@1] The teenager was taken to the Deaai, the
police station that handles offences by minors. [@1] The two were presented to
journalists in Manaus on the morning of 3 May. [@2] Portal do Holanda valued the
load at more than R$2 million. [@3] Portal do Holanda described the seizure as
the largest in Amazonas in four months. [@3] Portal Amazônia put the value at
about R$3 million. [@2] Torres said the arrested man had named the owners of the
load, who were being sought. [@3]
