Police in Salima arrested a 30-year-old man for being found in possession of 53
cobs of Indian hemp, known locally as chamba. [@1] The man was identified in
police files as Maziko Banda. [@1] He had wrapped the cobs around his arms and
legs to hide the drug. [@1]

On the day of the arrest he was travelling along the lakeshore road on a Dulux
Coach bus, registration number NN 8768, from Dwangwa towards Mangochi. [@1] At
the Kaphatenga police roadblock in Salima, an officer became suspicious of his
appearance. [@1] A search of the vehicle and the man revealed Indian hemp
wrapped on his legs and hands, and he was taken to the police station. [@1]

Salima Police Station spokesperson Gift Chitowe said Banda had been charged with
being found in possession of Indian hemp without a licence. [@2] Chitowe said he
was in police custody and would soon be taken to court. [@2] Banda comes from
Chiseka village in Lilongwe district. [@1]
