Police in South Andros arrested two Bahamian men after officers acting on
intelligence found a large quantity of marijuana in a bushy area at Mars Bay
around 6am on Thursday 18 May 2017. [@2] Magnetic Media reported that the two
men were found at the scene where the stash was hidden in bushes. [@1]

The marijuana weighed 1,602 pounds, about 727 kilograms. [@2] It was packed in
44 nylon sacks, a suitcase containing a number of small packages and 19
individual packages of various sizes. [@2] Police put the estimated street value
at $1.6 million. [@2] Magnetic Media described the haul as more than one
thousand pounds of marijuana in 44 nylon sacks, 19 small bags and a suitcase.
[@1] Magnetic Media reported that over a million dollars in drugs was uncovered.
[@1]

A team from the Drug Enforcement Unit travelled to Andros and returned to New
Providence with the contraband and the suspects at 5.30pm. [@2] A police
statement described the South Andros officers as "courageous" and said they had
struck a "big blow in the operations of drug traffickers in the Bahamas". [@2]
