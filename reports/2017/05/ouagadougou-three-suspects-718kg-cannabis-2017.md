Burkina Faso's anti-drug unit presented three suspected cannabis traffickers at
a press conference in Ouagadougou on 2 May 2017. [@1] Police identified the
suspected ringleader as Koffi Agyekum, a 33-year-old foreign national. [@3] His
two accomplices were a Burkinabé transporter and a warehouse keeper at a bus
station in Ouagadougou's Kamsonghin neighbourhood. [@1] The three men, aged 31
to 33, bought the cannabis in a neighbouring country and shipped it to
Ouagadougou in bales of second-hand clothing. [@1]

Investigators arrested the suspected trafficker and his two accomplices and
seized 718 kg of cannabis. [@3] Commissioner Richard Bélem said the seizure was
worth between 45 and 50 million FCFA. [@1] Police commissioner Richard
Wendlassida Bélèm, who led the operation, gave the details at the press
conference. [@4] The cannabis was unloaded at night at the bus station in
Kamsonghin, Bélem said. [@1] Part of the shipment was kept for local consumption
and the rest sent to another neighbouring country. [@2] The investigation began
after the anti-drug unit received information on 10 January 2017. [@1]

The three suspects were to be presented to the prosecutor at the Ouagadougou
High Court and the cannabis incinerated. [@1] The police said the men faced ten
to twenty years in prison under the penal code. [@2]
