Police led by Special Branch chief Surachet Hakphal raided a rented house in a
housing estate on On Nut soi 65 in Prawet district on 17 June 2017 and seized
over 400 kg of marijuana. [@1] They arrested Udomchai Triphakdi, known as
Bungarm, and his wife Jittaya Yarangwong. [@1]

## Operation

Surachet said the raid expanded a crackdown on drug sales by post in the
Prawet area, in which his team had already arrested seven suspects. [@1]
Investigators had monitored a post office in On Nut and questioned two
suspects who came to mail about 4 kg of marijuana to clients; the pair
pointed to Bungarm as the man who had hired them to do so. [@1] Police said
Bungarm had sold the drug for two to three years, supplied by an agent in
Laos, moving 20 to 60 boxes a month for 300,000 to 400,000 baht in
profit. [@1]

Officers also seized bottles of cough mixture, kratom leaves and assets worth
over 40 million baht, including a condominium on Sukhumvit soi 103/4, 8.7
million baht in cash, gold bars weighing 28 baht, 6 million baht in bank
accounts and two cars. [@1]
