The justice system in the Comoros destroyed more than 25 kilograms of seized
cannabis on a Tuesday. [@1] The drugs had been seized in a mains propres (clean
hands) operation run by the security forces. [@1] Prosecutor of the Republic
Hamidou Ali attended the destruction and said it was a strong signal to the
authorities in the fight against drug trafficking. [@1]

Ali promised to punish with an iron hand dealers, consumers and everyone
directly or indirectly involved in the traffic of narcotics, hemp, cocaine and
other hard drugs. [@1] He said everything found during searches, whether goods
or means of transport, would be confiscated. [@1]

The prosecutor said his approach had two policies, prevention and sanction. [@1]
The report says drug trafficking is becoming an alarming problem in the country
as anti-drug brigades increase surveillance at the borders and inland. [@2]
