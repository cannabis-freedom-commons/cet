Mali's anti-drug brigade, the Brigade de la lutte contre les Stupéfiants,
arrested a Senegalese man accused of cannabis trafficking in Bamako. [@1] The
arrest was made on the Saturday before the feast ending Ramadan, which the
accounts place on 24 June 2017. [@3] Officers found more than 107 kilograms of
cannabis hidden inside three wooden doors that were being sent to Senegal. [@1]

The cannabis was packed as 53 tablets or slabs of broken cannabis pieces, and
the abnormal weight and size of the doors drew the investigators' attention.
[@1] The brigade estimated the value of the cannabis at more than 5 million CFA
francs. [@1] The arrest came after several weeks of investigation by the
brigade. [@1] The brigade described hiding cannabis inside wooden doors as a
method it had not met before, having been used to drugs hidden in grain sacks or
luggage. [@1]

Commissioner Mamoutou Togola of the brigade said the suspect claimed he had not
acted alone but had not yet given the names of his accomplices. [@2] Togola said
the seizure had been possible thanks to cooperation between the police and the
public. [@1] The Senegalese account, crediting the Pana agency and the newspaper
Les Echos, said the man was arrested as he tried to reach Senegal, his country
of origin. [@3]
