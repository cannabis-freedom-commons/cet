Police arrested two Royal Bahamas Defence Force marines and two civilian men in
separate drug seizures on Marlborough Street in Nassau over a 24-hour period.
[@2] Each incident involved one marine and one civilian travelling together in a
vehicle. [@1] Both interceptions were made by officers of the Tourism Policing
Unit patrolling Marlborough Street. [@1]

On the Thursday, officers stopped a recklessly driven vehicle at around 7pm,
held its two male occupants and found a quantity of marijuana, taking both men
into custody. [@2] On the Friday, officers intercepted a Suzuki carrying two men
in the same area at around 6pm, searched it, found marijuana and arrested both
occupants. [@2] In both cases the stops followed erratic behaviour in the
vehicles, which prompted the searches of the cars and led to the marijuana being
found. [@1]

The four men were expected to face drug-possession charges in court during the
week after the arrests. [@1] The accounts describe each seizure only as a
quantity of marijuana. [@2]
