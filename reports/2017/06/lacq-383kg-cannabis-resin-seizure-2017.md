Gendarmes raided Lacq in the Pyrénées-Atlantiques early on Saturday 10 June 2017
and seized 383 kg of cannabis resin. [@2] Three men were arrested as they parked
three vehicles full of the resin outside a detached house. [@4] They were placed
in police custody and presented to an investigating judge on Monday 12 June; two
were remanded and one was placed under judicial supervision. [@1] A fourth
suspected trafficker was arrested on Monday and was still in police custody the
next day, making four people detained in the case. [@3]

Le Parisien reported the haul as nearly 400 kg of resin from a Franco-Spanish
trafficking network based in the Pyrénées-Atlantiques. [@1] Three vehicles used
to carry the drugs were also seized. [@1] About 40 gendarmes from the Pau
research section, the departmental gendarmerie group and the Toulouse GIGN took
part. [@3] The operation followed a seven-month inquiry with the Bayonne customs
operations directorate and Spain's Guardia Civil. [@2] France Bleu estimated
that the drugs cost about 1 million euros to buy and would have brought about
2.5 million euros on resale. [@1]

About four tonnes of drugs in total were believed to have passed through the
network. [@1] The gendarmerie said this organisation had its centre of gravity
in the department, unlike usual large seizures by networks that merely pass
through it. [@1] The three were investigated under a warrant for importing
narcotics and organised criminal association. [@2] Those offences carry a
maximum sentence of 30 years' imprisonment. [@2] A linked Spanish police
operation found a Kalashnikov rifle and a handgun in a hideout in Santander.
[@1] The inquiry had begun after a burglary several months earlier. [@4]
