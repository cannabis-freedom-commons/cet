In Saint-Louis, Réunion, gendarmes of the local brigade discovered zamal at the
home of a Saint-Louis family. [@1] The Réunion gendarmerie publicised the find
as an important seizure made in Saint-Louis by publishing a photograph of it.
[@1]

The seizure photograph was posted with a humorous comment quoted as "l’arbre qui
cachait... le Tepee (véhicule)". [@1] In that joke, the tree in the photograph
was presented as hiding the Tepee, which the comment identifies as a vehicle.
[@1]

Those identified as responsible were to be summoned before the court to answer
for their acts. [@1] The substance involved was identified as zamal. [@1]
