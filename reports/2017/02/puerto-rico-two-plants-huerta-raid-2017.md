Officers of the Toxicomanía Division of the Unidad Regional IV, the Misiones
provincial police command responsible for drug offences, seized two marijuana
plants in the vicinity of the Loma Alta neighbourhood of Puerto Rico. [@1] The
taller of the two plants measured 1.82 metres and the shorter 1.32 metres. [@1]
The raid was carried out within what the police described as intensified
operations against drug trafficking in the area. [@1]

At 18:00 on Tuesday 21 February 2017 the uniformed officers searched a property
there as part of an investigation into the possible existence of a 'huerta de
cannabis', a cannabis garden, in the zone, acting within intensified anti-drug
operations. [@3] With the corresponding search warrant, the uniformed officers
entered the home and found the two plants there. [@1] The substance was
corroborated by means of the Toxicomanía test. [@1]

Police seized the narcotics and a federal judge took intervention in the matter
after the substances were tested. [@2]
