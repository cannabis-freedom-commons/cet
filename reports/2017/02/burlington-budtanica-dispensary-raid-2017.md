A Hamilton man was charged after police raided a Burlington business that
officers said was selling medical marijuana to people directly and also to
people without a licence to possess it. [@1]

The raid was carried out by the Halton Regional Police Services Burlington
Street Crime Unit, working with City of Burlington bylaw enforcement. [@1]
Officers investigated Budtanica Pain Relief Group, which operated from 5125
Harvester Rd., Unit 1, east of Appleby Line. [@1]

Halton Regional Police said the raid of the medical marijuana dispensary on
Harvester Road took place on 14 February 2017 and that officers seized items
from the premises. [@1]
