A 55-year-old man from Rodrigues was arrested at the Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam
international airport on Thursday 16 February 2017. [@1] He was returning from
Madagascar when officers of the Anti Narcotics Section intercepted him at the
airport. [@1]

Officers searched him and found ten sachets of the drug in his luggage. [@1] The
cannabis he carried weighed 90 grams and was valued at an estimated Rs 60,000.
