Lior Avi Ben Moyal was handed over to Israeli police authorities and deported to
Israel from Mumbai airport. [@2] He had been scheduled to leave for Israel via
Mumbai on 16 February 2017. [@1] India's Ministry of Home Affairs cleared his
repatriation to Israel under a treaty signed with Israel under the Repatriation
of Prisoners Act, 2013. [@1] The letter from the ministry was received by the
Inspector General of Prisons in Chandigarh on the Monday before his departure.
[@1] He had been serving time at the Model Burail Jail since 2005. [@1]

The Narcotics Control Bureau, in a team headed by Ravi Kant Pawar, arrested Lior
in February 2005 along with a Czech national, Andreevi Jiri, and a Kullu
resident, Narender Kumar. [@1] Officers recovered 154.50 kg of charas, a
cannabis resin, from them, described as worth more than Rs 45 crore on the
international market. [@1] Lior was sentenced to 16 years of rigorous
imprisonment under Section 20. [@1] He and the two co-accused were convicted in
July 2011, with the court also applying Section 29 for abetment of criminal
conspiracy of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985. [@2]

A joint team of the operations cell and Model Burail Jail, led by Inspector
Neeraj Sarna, escorted him from the jail to Mumbai airport. [@2] A senior jail
officer said he was handed over in the presence of officials from the Israeli
embassy after legal formalities were completed. [@2]
