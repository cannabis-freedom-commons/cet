St Maarten police continued their general controls on the Dutch side of the
island on Thursday 9 February 2017. [@1] These controls led to the arrest of
three men, aged 26, 21 and 19, for possessing a substantial amount of marihuana
found in the vehicle they were driving. [@1] The marihuana was found by the
Justice K-9 Unit, called in to help search the vehicle. [@1] All three were
arrested on the spot and taken to the Philipsburg Police Station, where they
stayed in custody for further investigation. [@2]

One control ran from 8 p.m. to midnight at several locations, including the
roundabout by the Grand Marche supermarket in Cul-de-sac, where 46 vehicles were
stopped. [@1] Police confiscated the vehicle and the marihuana for further
investigation. [@1]

The police account noted that pictures of the drugs were attached. [@2] The
police said the controls would continue to intensify. [@1]
