Palm Beach police arrested a West Palm Beach man in an undercover operation into
marijuana sales in the town. [@1] David Sotelo Montenegro, 26, was charged with
marijuana possession and with possession of marijuana with intent to sell, both
felonies, a police report said. [@1] The charges came from a two-month
undercover investigation in which an officer bought marijuana from Montenegro in
Palm Beach. [@1]

A detective first texted Montenegro on 9 December, asking him to deliver 28.35
grams of marijuana for $200. [@1] At 2:44 p.m. Montenegro drove his blue Jeep
Wrangler into town and parked behind the detective's undercover vehicle in the
300 block of South Ocean Boulevard. [@1] He got into the back seat and exchanged
the marijuana for $200 in cash. [@1] The detective took the marijuana back to
the Palm Beach Police Department, where it tested positive for THC, the active
ingredient in marijuana. [@1] The detective texted Montenegro on the Tuesday and
again on the Thursday to ask whether they could meet at the beach. [@1]

Later that Thursday Montenegro called to say he had driven past the agreed spot
but wanted to do the deal in his Jeep, and the detective told him to park in the
100 block of Clarke Avenue. [@1] Montenegro parked there at 4:27 p.m., and a
sergeant and another detective approached the Jeep and ordered him out. [@1]
Officers found marijuana in his front jacket pocket and a plastic bag of
marijuana in Montenegro's center console. [@1] The marijuana weighed a total of
28.3 grams. [@1] Montenegro was booked into the Palm Beach County Jail and
released the next day on $6,000 bond. [@1] He did not return a phone call
seeking comment. [@1]
