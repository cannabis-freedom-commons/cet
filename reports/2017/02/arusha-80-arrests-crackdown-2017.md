Police in Tanzania's northern city of Arusha arrested 80 suspects for alleged involvement in narcotics use, trafficking and trade during a special crackdown across the region. [@1] Arusha regional police commander Charles Mkumbo said 54 of them were netted for possessing 3,845 rolls of marijuana during the month-long search. [@1]

One of the 80 arrested was a police officer, D/CPL Zakayo, who was being interrogated over allegedly soliciting bribes from drug dealers during the crackdown. [@1] Mkumbo said Zakayo had gone around blackmailing people to cash in on the cleanup, and he declined to name the other suspects or value the drugs for fear of harming the investigations. [@1]

All of the suspects were later charged in courts across the region. [@1] The crackdown followed President John Magufuli's directive ordering the security and defence forces to apprehend all narcotics suspects irrespective of their status. [@1]
