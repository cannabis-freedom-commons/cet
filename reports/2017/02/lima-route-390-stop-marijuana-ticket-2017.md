Livingston County sheriff's deputies said they pulled over a car on Route 390
for vehicle and traffic law violations and then found drugs on the driver and a
passenger. [@1] The driver, 20-year-old James Knefley of Wellsville, was found
to have marijuana. [@1] Knefley was issued an appearance ticket for unlawful
possession of marijuana, along with multiple traffic tickets. [@1]

Deputies said a passenger in the car, 18-year-old Dominica Howells, was found
with ten bags of crack cocaine and ten bags of heroin. [@1] Howells was arrested
on a Thursday in early February 2017. [@1] Howells was charged with criminal
possession of a controlled substance in the third, fifth and seventh degrees,
the third and fifth as felonies. [@1] She was arraigned in Lima Town Court and
remanded to the Livingston County Jail in lieu of $5,000 cash bail or $10,000
bond. [@1]

The marijuana found on the driver was separate from the heroin and crack cocaine
found on the passenger. [@1]
