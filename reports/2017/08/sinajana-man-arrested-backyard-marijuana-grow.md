Guam Police Department officers conducted a search warrant on the Sinajana home
of Peter Pangelinan Reyes on Aug. 14 and found suspected marijuana plants
growing in the backyard, court documents state. [@1,2] Reyes was 39 years old. [@2]

Police thwarted a man attempting to "fulfill his lifelong dream" of growing
marijuana plants at his Sinajana residence, according to a magistrate's
complaint filed yesterday in the Superior Court of Guam. [@1]

Reyes asked police why they were searching his residence and said he had been
growing the plants for several months. [@1,2]

Reyes tried to explain that he was "trying to fulfill his lifelong dream" of
growing marijuana plants at his home. [@1,2]

Reyes told authorities he "had a dream and felt that (the dream) was a sign from
God, telling him that he should start growing marijuana." [@1,2]

A test on a sample of the suspected marijuana tested positive for the presence
of THC. [@1,2]

Inside the residence officers located what appeared to be methamphetamine
paraphernalia. [@1,2]

A chemical field test on the item revealed a presumptive positive response for
the presence of methamphetamine, according to the magistrate. [@1]

Reyes admitted to owning and abusing the illegal drug, stating he had last
smoked methamphetamine "about a month prior." [@1,2]

Reyes was arrested and charged with possession of a Schedule I controlled
substance with intent to deliver as a first-degree felony and possession of a
Schedule II controlled substance as third-degree felony. [@1,2]
