The Los Angeles County Sheriff's Department City of Industry Station special
assignment team opened its case after receiving a tip about possible illegal
activity inside two warehouses on the premises. [@1] After about two months of
investigation, the special assignment team leader obtained a two-location search
warrant covering the 1100 block of Fullerton Road in Industry. [@1] Deputies
served the warrants on 3 August 2017 at about 9:30 a.m. [@1] Deputies found a
sophisticated indoor marijuana grow operation at both locations. [@2] Eight
Asian male adult suspects, all residents of San Gabriel and Rowland Heights aged
between 35 and 50, were in the process of being arrested and booked on suspicion
of cultivation of marijuana. [@1]

Deputies seized more than 16,000 marijuana plants from the two warehouses. [@2]
The sheriff's department put the street value of the seized marijuana at over
$50 million. [@1] No money or firearms were seized in the operation. [@1] The
suspects' names were not released. [@1] The investigation was still ongoing.
