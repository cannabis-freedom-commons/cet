Nkhotakota police arrested 30-year-old Briden Banda at the Kaombe police
checking point after law enforcers stopped the Scania bus he was travelling in.
[@1] Banda hails from Kaiya village in the area of Traditional Authority Ganya
in Ntcheu district. [@2]

Police came across two black laptop bags, one black expand bag and one China
sack bag full of the illicit drug concealed with clothes. [@1] The seized
material was loose cannabis sativa weighing 56.5 kilograms. [@1] When officers
tried to trace the owner of the bags, Banda came forward and voluntarily
admitted to be the owner of the drugs. [@1] Officers had stopped the Scania bus
he was on at the Kaombe checking point before tracing the illicit drug to him.
[@2]

He was behind bars in Nkhotakota for possessing the loose cannabis. [@1] He was
due to appear before the Nkhotakota first grade magistrate court to answer
charges of possessing Indian hemp without a licence under sections 4(A) and 19
of the Dangerous Drugs Act. [@1]
