On Tuesday 8 August 2017, between 2:30 p.m. and 5:30 p.m., gendarmes and border
police ran a joint anti-delinquency operation against drug and arms trafficking
in the Epinay district of Sainte-Luce. [@1] Officers stopped a 61-year-old
driver who was carrying 70 grams of cannabis and was also growing cannabis at
his home. [@1] A search of the man's home seized 12 cannabis plants. [@1] He was
released and is to be tried later by the Fort-de-France criminal court. [@1]

The operation was carried out jointly by the gendarmerie and the Lamentin border
police (PAF). [@1] The operation also seized a bladed weapon and 70 grams of
cannabis packed as seven 10-gram bags. [@1] RCI confirms the same joint
operation also yielded the bladed weapon and the 70 grams of cannabis. [@2]
Outre-mer La 1ère confirms the man was summoned to the Fort-de-France court.
