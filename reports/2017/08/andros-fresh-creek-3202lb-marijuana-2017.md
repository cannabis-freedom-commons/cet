Bahamian police uncovered 78 large packages of marijuana in bushes in Fresh
Creek, Andros. [@1] Three adult males were arrested in connection with the find.
[@2] The three were Brandon Adderley, 25, Devon Culmer, 37 and Perry Miller, 46,
who appeared before Chief Magistrate Joyann Ferguson-Pratt on 11 August 2017.
[@4] They faced eight charges: two counts each of possession of dangerous drugs
with intent to supply, importation of dangerous drugs, conspiracy to import
dangerous drugs and conspiracy to possess dangerous drugs with intent to supply.
[@4] All three accused pleaded not guilty. [@4] They were remanded to the
Department of Correctional Services. [@4]

Around 4.30pm on Emancipation Day, officers from the North Andros Division went
to an area in Fresh Creek where they saw a go-fast boat that appeared to have
run aground. [@4] A search of the nearby bushes uncovered 78 large packages
containing nearly 3,202 pounds (about 1,452 kilograms) of marijuana. [@2] Senior
Assistant Commissioner Stephen Dean said the search turned up 78 packages and
led to the arrest of three adult males. [@3] Police estimated the street value
of the drugs at $3.2 million. [@2] Assistant Commissioner of Police Samuel
Butler, officer-in-charge of the Drug Enforcement Unit, called it the biggest
drug bust of the year. [@2] Stephen Dean described the seizure as a significant
blow to the operations of drug traffickers. [@1]

The men were alleged to have plotted to import Indian Hemp into The Bahamas.
[@4] Miller's lawyer said his client was a fisherman who had broken down in
Fresh Creek and had no connection to the other two men. [@4] A status hearing
was set for 17 August for a report on the lawyer's complaint. [@4]
