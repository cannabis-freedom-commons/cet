On the afternoon of 3 August 2017 a law enforcement helicopter crew found a
marijuana garden growing at a private residence on Hyde Road in the Resaca
community. [@1] The resident, David Long, was arrested there without incident.
[@1]

Sheriff's detectives removed dozens of large marijuana plants from the grounds,
along with more marijuana packaged for resale and packaging materials. [@1] They
also seized an assault rifle with several magazines, a police-style pistol,
other firearms and a large quantity of ammunition. [@2]

Long, 71, of 890 Hyde Road, Resaca, was charged with manufacturing, possession
and intent to distribute marijuana and other drug offences, and with being a
felon in possession of firearms. [@1] Sheriff Mitch Ralston put the street value
of the plants at more than $170,000. [@2] Long remained in jail pending bond.
[@1]
