Italian police, with help from Montenegrin and Albanian colleagues, arrested two
Albanian citizens in a joint Adriatic operation. [@1] During Operation Triton,
Italian helicopters and boats found a motorboat moving towards Italy, and the
smugglers threw the drugs overboard before police caught and arrested them. [@1]
The chase lasted about five hours and ended when Guardia di Finanza units from
Termoli and Bari, directed by a Frontex aircraft, detained the two men offshore.
[@2] The operation was a tripartite Albania-Italy-Montenegro action with Frontex
air support, and the speedboat was blocked in the Adriatic Sea. [@2]

Police recovered 110 packages of cannabis from the sea. [@1] After weighing, the
load was about 1.9 tons of narcotics bound for the Italian coast. [@2] The
seized drugs were estimated to be worth 20 million EUR. [@1] The high-powered
three-engine speedboat was sequestered, and the traffickers and the boat were
handed over to the Italian judicial authorities as the investigation into the
trafficking network continued. [@2]
