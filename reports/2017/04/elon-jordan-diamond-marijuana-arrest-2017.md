Elon University sophomore Jordan Diamond was arrested on Friday, April 28, 2017.
[@1] The felony allegation was possession of a Schedule 1 controlled substance
with intent to manufacture, sell or deliver. [@1] He was also charged with
misdemeanor possession of marijuana and marijuana paraphernalia. [@1] Diamond
was a sophomore at Elon University at the time of the April arrest. [@1]

The marijuana allegations were charged as misdemeanors, distinct from the felony
Schedule 1 allegation. [@1] The felony charge involved alleged intent to
manufacture, sell or deliver, while the marijuana counts alleged possession and
paraphernalia possession. [@1] A court date in the case was set for May 17. [@1]

Under North Carolina's Controlled Substances Act, Schedule 1 controlled
substances are described as having a high potential for abuse and no medical
use. [@1] That category is described as including heroin, ecstacy, GHB,
methaqualone, peyote, opiates and others. [@1] That classification provides the
legal context for the felony possession allegation against Diamond. [@1] The
case was therefore a mixed drug case, with a felony Schedule 1 allegation
alongside separate misdemeanor marijuana allegations. [@1]
