Members of Barbados's Drug Squad arrested and formally charged two Jamaican
nationals for drug offences committed at Grantley Adams International Airport on
Friday 15 September 2017. [@2] They are 19-year-old Breanna Ann-Marie Brown and
24-year-old Joel Williams, who arrived by air from Jamaica that day. [@2] Brown
and Williams were charged with unlawful possession of cannabis, possession of
cannabis with intent to supply, trafficking of cannabis and importation of
cannabis. [@1]

Having cleared immigration, they proceeded to Customs, where a search of their
individual luggage was conducted. [@1] During the search, three plastic-wrapped
packages and fifteen brown taped packages, all containing cannabis, were found
concealed in false compartments in their suitcases. [@1] The police account said
those packages were in the suitcases of Brown and Williams respectively, and
that the two were then handed over to the police and charged. [@2]

Both accused appeared before Acting Magistrate Wayne Clarke in the District A
Magistrates' Court on Saturday 16 September 2017 and were remanded to prison.
[@2] They were to reappear in the District B Magistrates' Court on 19 September
2017. [@2] Acting Inspector Roland Cobbler, public relations officer, released
the statement of the arrest and the charges. [@2]
