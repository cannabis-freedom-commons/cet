Afghan security forces seized around 1,260 kilograms of hashish in central Logar
province. [@1] Local security officials said the hashish was packed in bags and
loaded onto five camels that smugglers were trying to move to an unknown
location. [@1] Officials said the camels were spotted in the Aab Chakan area,
near the provincial capital. [@1]

General Abdul Raziq, commander of the 4th Brigade of the armed forces in Logar,
said foreign forces spotted the camels while watching the area from security
balloons. [@1] He said the narcotics had been placed inside the bags so they
would not be recognised and could be smuggled quickly. [@1] Raziq said the
narcotics had been loaded in Nangarhar province and the smugglers were
apparently heading for a southern province. [@1]

He said the seized hashish would be burnt in front of government officials once
the security forces finished their formalities. [@1] It was not clear whether
any suspect had been arrested in connection with the hashish. [@1]
