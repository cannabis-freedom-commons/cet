Officers of the Koutiala regional branch of the Office Central des Stupéfiants,
Mali's central anti-drug office, detained two drug traffickers found with 58
bricks of cannabis. [@1] Lieutenant Salikou Traoré, head of the Koutiala
regional branch, announced the operation and said both traffickers were Malian
nationals. [@1] The two men were transferred to the central prison in Sikasso
after their arrest. [@1]

The regional branch seized a total of fifty-eight bricks of cannabis in the
Sikasso region of southern Mali. [@1] The office estimated the value of the
seized cannabis at 120 million CFA francs. [@1] The seizing unit was the
Koutiala regional antenna of the Office Central des Stupéfiants operating in the
Sikasso region. [@1]

The seized cannabis had come from Ghana and travelled via Burkina Faso before
reaching Mali. [@1] One part of the consignment was intended for consumption
inside Mali. [@1] The other part was to be delivered onward to Senegal. [@1] The
regional chief described the case as the work of an international
drug-trafficking network. [@1]
