Limbe police arrested 20-year-old Samuel Chokomba and 19-year-old Eliza
Sinsamala for selling lotion containing cannabis. [@1] Police acted after
receiving a tip that the pair processed cannabis into lotion sold at Limbe
market. [@1] Limbe Police Deputy Public Relations Officer Widson Nhlane
identified the two suspects and described the operation. [@2]

Officers seized 18 bottles of lotion containing Cannabis Sativa, locally known
as chamba. [@1] The second account describes the same 18 bottles as Vaseline
into which chamba had been put for sale in Limbe township. [@2] The drug section
at Limbe Police Station received information from well-wishers and launched
investigations that led to the arrests and seizure. [@1]

Police said the seized lotion was taken to Bvumbwe Research Station for
laboratory tests and examination. [@1] The two were to be taken to court soon to
answer charges of being found in possession of Cannabis Sativa. [@1] Chokomba
hails from Mtambalika Village in the area of Traditional Authority Kapeni in
Blantyre. [@1] Sinsamala hails from Chiwembu Village in the area of Traditional
Authority Nthache in Mwanza. [@1]
