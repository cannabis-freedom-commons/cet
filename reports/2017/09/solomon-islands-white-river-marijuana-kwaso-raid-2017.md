Police officers from White River and Honiara Central police stations raided
Karaina roadside and Savo Heights at White River in West Honiara on Wednesday 20
September 2017. [@1] The raid followed information from the public that
marijuana and kwaso were being sold beside the road. [@1]

Three men were arrested in connection with the confiscated items. [@1] The three
were expected to be interviewed on 21 September, the day after the raid. [@1]
The Island Sun reported that the trio had been interrogated the day before. [@2]
Photographs released with the police statement showed confiscated marijuana
plants and bottles of kwaso. [@2]

Inspector Walter Manefakale, who commanded White River Police Station, said he
was pleased with the outcome and the public's cooperation in supplying the
information that led to the raid. [@1] He urged community members to keep
reporting people who sold and brewed kwaso and sold marijuana. [@1] Manefakale
said it is unlawful to grow, use, sell or possess marijuana, and illegal to brew
and sell kwaso without approval from the responsible minister. [@1]
