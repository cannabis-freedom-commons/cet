Mobile Division officers of the Royal Bahamas Police Force arrested two men on
Crooked Island Street in Nassau shortly before 3pm on Saturday 18 March 2017
after a search found a quantity of marijuana in their possession. [@2] Around
9:30pm the same night, officers on routine patrol at McCullough Corner arrested
a third man after finding 70 small packages of marijuana. [@2] The Tribune
reported the same two Saturday street incidents and said three men were
arrested. [@3]

Police described the Crooked Island Street find only as a quantity of marijuana
and published no weight for either seizure. [@2] Magnetic Media, reporting the
police account on 21 March 2017, said the two men on Crooked Island Street were
arrested around 3pm Saturday and that seventy packets of illicit drugs were
found later that night at McCullough Corner. [@1]

The same Mobile Division statement said four men were taken into custody over
about 48 hours, including a separate arrest shortly before 6pm on Friday 17
March on Fleming Street after a man threw down a 9-millimetre Taurus pistol with
12 live rounds. [@2] The Tribune said investigations into the Saturday drug
arrests were ongoing. [@3]
