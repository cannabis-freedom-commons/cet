Octavia Nickoda Lucimia Taylor, a 25-year-old Jamaican woman, was held on
Saturday after arriving on a flight at Grantley Adams International Airport in
Barbados. [@1] On Monday she was ordered to pay $8,000 or spend 12 months in
prison in Barbados for possession of narcotics. [@1]

One kilogramme of cannabis was found in a haversack she was carrying. [@1] She
was arrested and charged with possession of cannabis, possession with intent to
supply, and trafficking. [@1]

In court on Monday, Taylor pleaded guilty to all the charges and was fined in
connection with the offence of trafficking. [@1] She was convicted, reprimanded
and discharged for the other offences. [@1]
