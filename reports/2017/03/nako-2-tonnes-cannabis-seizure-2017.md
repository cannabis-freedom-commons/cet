Police in Nako, in south-western Burkina Faso, seized nearly two tonnes of
chanvre indien (cannabis). [@1] The operation took place on 23 March 2017 after
investigations in Topera village, Poni province. [@1] The cannabis was packed in
20 sacks. [@2] Officers detained two suspected traffickers. [@3] The suspects
were in police custody at the time of the report. [@3]

Police valued the cannabis at more than 170 million FCFA. [@2] The commissioner
of the Nako district police, Wahabo Kaboré, gave the details of the operation.
[@1] He said investigators had identified the dwelling where the cannabis was
stored. [@3] He called the quantity the first of its kind. [@3] He said
investigations were continuing to dismantle the rest of the group. [@3]

Nako lies in Burkina Faso's Sud-Ouest region. [@2] Near the Ghana border, the
town is described as a transit zone for drugs and smuggled goods. [@2] The
seizure recalled a November 2016 customs seizure at the customs post in Nako.
[@3]
