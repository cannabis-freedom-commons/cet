Members of the Drug Squad, the Police Marine Unit and the Barbados Coast Guard
intercepted a vessel off the shores of Barbados on Sunday 2 July 2017. [@1] Two
men of Bermudian nationality were assisting police with their investigations.
[@1]

Officers seized 171 pounds of cannabis, about 77.6 kilograms, and a yacht during
the operation. [@1] The cannabis was given an approximate street value of
$684,000. [@1]

The interception was the third marine law-enforcement operation in seven days in
which large quantities of illegal drugs were seized. [@2] On Thursday 29 June a
boat intercepted off Barbados yielded 50.5 kilograms of cocaine, with a street
value of $2,525,000, and 160 pounds of cannabis valued at $640,000. [@2] Three
men, two Barbadians and a Guyanese national, were charged in connection with the
29 June seizure. [@2] Earlier that week a 29-year-old Barbadian was charged over
the seizure of 279 pounds of cannabis, estimated at $1,116,000, from a fishing
vessel off Barbados. [@2]
