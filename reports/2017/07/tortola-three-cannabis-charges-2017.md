The Royal Virgin Islands Police Force charged Nickail Chambers, 26, his father
Owen Chambers, 52, and Selroy Hanley, 28, with unlawful possession of cannabis
and unlawful possession of cannabis with intent to supply. [@2] Police said the
cannabis charges followed search warrants at the men's homes that uncovered the
drugs. [@2] Nickail Chambers, a Jamaican native living at Lower Estate on
Tortola, was charged on 11 July 2017 with unlawful possession of a firearm with
intent to injure. [@2] The firearm charge stemmed from shots fired at Fort Hill
on 22 June 2017. [@1]

Hanley, of Purcell Estate, was additionally charged with unlawful possession of
ammunition. [@2] He appeared in the Magistrate's Court on 11 July 2017 and was
remanded until a later appearance. [@2] Hanley was also charged with unlawful
possession of cocaine with intent to supply. [@2] Nickail Chambers was due in
the Magistrate's Court on the morning of 12 July 2017. [@1] The force said the
firearm-related arrests resulted from efforts to detain people seen with,
suspected of having or importing illegal firearms into the territory. [@2]
