Police charged Jazz Freeman, 24, of Fahie Hill, and Allison Vanterpool, 47, of
Josiah Bay, both on Tortola. [@1] The charges followed a stop and search on
Waterfront Drive near Queen Elizabeth Park in Road Town on the morning of 25
July 2017, when traffic officers pulled over a vehicle driven by Vanterpool.
[@1] The two were jointly charged with carrying an unlicensed firearm and
unlawful possession of ammunition. [@1] The men were also charged with unlawful
possession of marijuana found in the search. [@1] Freeman faced an additional
drug possession charge after police searched his premises. [@1]

A motorcycle officer smelled burning marijuana coming from the vehicle, the
court later heard. [@2] The officer rode alongside the vehicle and ordered
Vanterpool to pull over. [@2] The court heard that a further search of a bag
recovered a 9mm semi-automatic pistol and four rounds of ammunition. [@2] Police
said the pistol was loaded. [@1] Vanterpool told the court the gun was his and
that Freeman did not know about it. [@2] Freeman pleaded guilty to the drug
charges and not guilty to the firearm charges. [@2] Vanterpool was not granted
bail. [@2] Freeman was offered $70,000 bail. [@2] The court ordered him to pay
$15,000 up front, observe a 9pm-to-6am curfew and surrender his travel
documents. [@2] Both men were due back in court on 11 September 2017. [@2]

Freeman then appeared before Senior Magistrate Tamia Richards and pleaded guilty
to two marijuana possession charges. [@3] The court heard the cannabis in those
charges was 5.3 grams found in the street and 2.1 grams found at his Fahie Hill
home. [@3] Richards fined him $5,000 for the two possession charges. [@3] She
also activated a $2,000 bond he had been given in January. [@3] The fines
totalled $7,000. [@3] Freeman also faced gun and ammunition charges, to which he
had pleaded not guilty. [@3]
