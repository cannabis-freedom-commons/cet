A 21-year-old car washer from Belle Vue was spared a penalty in the Magistrate's
Court after pleading guilty to possessing 25.4 grams of marijuana. [@1]
Shaquille Benjamin was charged with possession of 25.4 grams of marijuana and
pleaded guilty. [@1] At his sentencing hearing before Senior Magistrate Tamia
Richards in July 2017, Benjamin said he was studying marine courses. [@1]

Richards placed him on a $1,500 bond probation and told him he would not have to
pay if he completed the marine studies. [@1]

Royal Virgin Islands Police Force officers on mobile patrol in the Cane Garden
Bay area on 4 March 2017 smelt marijuana and accosted him. [@1] A search
retrieved a transparent bag from his person which reportedly contained green
vegetable matter found to be marijuana. [@1] When cautioned, Benjamin initially
said the bag was not his. [@1] He was charged and further investigations
continued. [@1]
