Officers carried out a search of the house of Giorgi Jakhua, a deacon of a
women's monastery, in Salkhino village in Martvili district in Georgia, and
seized cannabis and weapons. [@1] Jakhua himself publicly disclosed the house
search at a press conference in Kutaisi. [@1] He said the operation took place
during the night of 17 January. [@1]

Jakhua said he used cannabis to treat hepatitis C, category B diabetes, a
malignant liver tumour and cholecystitis, and said he was not taking any
medicines for those serious conditions. [@1] He accused the law-enforcement
officers who carried out the house search of exceeding their powers during the
operation. [@1] He said the police officers involved in the search made him pose
for a selfie with the cannabis. [@1]

He said the weapon found by officers in his house was an antique weapon that had
served as a toy for his children. [@1] He said his children had played with the
antique weapon and that the police officers put it in a pocket and took it away.
