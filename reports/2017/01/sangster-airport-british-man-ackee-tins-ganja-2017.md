A British man was arrested at Sangster International Airport after 12.5 pounds
(about 5.67 kg) of ganja was detected in his luggage during screening on the
previous day. [@1] The arrested man was identified as 56-year-old Michael Meakin
of Ketley Hill Road in England. [@1] Detectives from the Narcotics Division said
Meakin arrived at the airport at 2:20 pm to board a flight to Birmingham,
England. [@1]

Security personnel scanned his luggage as he arrived to board the Birmingham
flight and detected anomalies in the bags. [@1] Officers then carried out a
thorough search of the luggage following the scan. [@1] That thorough search
found ten tins marked ackees in the luggage, and each of those tins was found to
contain ganja. [@1]

Police estimated the street value of the seized weed at J$68,750. [@1]
