Law enforcement officers were continuing their fight with marijuana farmers on
the islands of Virgin Gorda. [@1] A number of marijuana farms were destroyed on
Virgin Gorda on the weekend before 23 January 2017. [@1] Nearly 600 marijuana
plants were uprooted during the police operation along Tower Road. [@1] The
plants were taken to the Virgin Gorda Police Station. [@1]

It was not yet clear if anyone had been arrested. [@1]
