Around 10 p.m. last Friday on Oleander Street in Dundas Town, Abaco, two men
took off running when they saw police, one was caught in the chase and found
with marijuana, and that man was arrested. [@1] Shortly before 10 p.m. on
Friday, police in Abaco who were on routine mobile patrol in the area of
Oleander Street, Dundas Town, saw two men who ran on seeing the officers. [@2]
Officers gave chase and captured one of the suspects, who was found in
possession of a quantity of suspected marijuana, and he was arrested and taken
into police custody. [@2]

Shortly after 10 p.m., officers of the Rapid Response Unit in the area of East
Sunrise Highway in Grand Bahama arrested a 28-year-old man of Mayfield Park who
was found in possession of 10 plastic bags of suspected marijuana. [@2] The
arrests were reported during an intense nationwide crime crackdown by the Royal
Bahamas Police Force in which reports of drug arrests were increasing. [@1]

The suspects were all expected to be arraigned early that week. [@2]
