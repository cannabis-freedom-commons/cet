Police arrested two persons in possession of 38 kilograms of marijuana at Gajuri
Bazaar in Gajuri Rural Municipality-1, Dhading district, on Thursday morning, 14
December 2017. [@1] Police told reporters that the identities of the two
arrested persons had yet to be disclosed, and that only one of them had been
named so far. [@1] Police arrested the driver Hridaya Prasad Mahato, aged 27,
from Kalyanpur Rural Municipality-4, and impounded the truck. [@1]

Acting on a tip-off, police stopped and frisked the truck, registration Na 2 Kha
7311, which was headed from Kathmandu towards Birgunj that afternoon, according
to Superintendent of Police Dhruba Raj Raut at the Dhading District Police
Office. [@1] Police found the contraband hidden in 22 sacks kept inside the
cabin of the vehicle. [@1]

Thirty-eight kilograms of powdered marijuana had been seized and police made the
material public in Dhading on Thursday, 14 December 2017, the same day as the
seizure. [@1] Further investigation into the seizure was under way. [@1]
