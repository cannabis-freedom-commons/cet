Police in Afghanistan's southwestern Nimroz province detained one person and
recovered 319 kilograms of drugs from a house in the Masoomabad area of Zaranj,
the provincial capital. [@1] Lt. Col. Ahmad Parvez Sistani, the province's
counternarcotics director, said officers searched the house and seized the drugs
there during the operation. [@1]

Forty kilograms of the 319-kilogram consignment was hashish. [@1] The hashish
was found together with the heroin, opium and chemical in the same house. [@1]
The rest of the haul was 42 kilograms of heroin, 229 kilograms of opium and 8
kilograms of chemical. [@1] The heroin, opium and chemical are not counted in
this report's cannabis figures. [@1] Sistani said the hashish and the other
drugs were being smuggled to neighbouring Iran. [@1]

The person detained over the consignment was under investigation, the director
said. [@1] The case was at the investigation stage when the seizure was
announced. [@1] No estimate of the drugs' value was published. [@1]
