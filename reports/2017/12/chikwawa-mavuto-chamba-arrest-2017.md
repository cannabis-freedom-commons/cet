Police in Chikwawa arrested a 32-year-old woman after she was found with
cannabis sativa, popularly known in Malawi as chamba. [@1] Chikwawa police
station public relations officer Constable Foster Benjamin named her as Maria
Mavuto of Kalima village, in the area of Traditional Authority Maseya in the
district. [@1] Benjamin said well-wishers from the area had given police a tip,
and that officers arrested her after receiving it. [@1]

The arrest was made during a sweeping exercise that police carried out in the
district the previous week. [@1] In the same operation, officers also
apprehended other people who were found in possession of medical drugs, the
police statement said. [@1] Benjamin said police in the district had since
intensified their operations ahead of the festive season. [@1]
