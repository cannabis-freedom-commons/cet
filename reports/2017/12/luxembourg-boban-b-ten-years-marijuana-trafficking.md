The case was tried before the 12th correctional chamber. [@1] The trial opened
on 3 October 2017 and lasted more than a month, with 20 hearing days spread over
seven weeks. [@1] Ten people in total were on trial, Boban B. plus nine
co-defendants. [@1] Principal defendant Boban B. received ten years of firm
imprisonment and a 10,000-euro fine, half the sentence requested by the
prosecution. [@1] The six other defendants prosecuted for taking part in Boban
B.'s marijuana trafficking were convicted on the Thursday morning of the
judgment, with penalties ranging from two years suspended to six years firm
imprisonment. [@1]

Kevin K., described by the prosecution as deputy and head of marijuana
distribution, received six years in prison, two of them suspended, plus a
5,000-euro fine. [@1] Frank V., described as the cannabis supplier, was
sentenced to six years firm imprisonment and a 7,500-euro fine. [@1] The court
gave dealers Patrick P., Paulo F. and David G. four-year prison terms and
2,500-euro fines. [@1] The last two of those three dealers were granted a
two-year suspension. [@1] Boban B.'s former girlfriend, tried as an accomplice
for helping him rather than directly distributing drugs, received a two-year
suspended prison term and a 1,500-euro fine. [@1]

Feti P., Geert D. and Luca S., prosecuted only over alleged cocaine trafficking
as supplier, intermediary or dealer, were all three acquitted. [@1] According to
investigators, Boban B. allegedly imported and sold 160 kg of marijuana over 18
months between 2012 and 2014, earning 1.36 million euros. [@1] The court also
imposed driving bans on the convicted defendants, except for Boban B.'s former
girlfriend. [@1] All parties had 40 days to appeal. [@1]
