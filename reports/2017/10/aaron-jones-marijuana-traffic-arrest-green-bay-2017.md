Former UTEP running back Aaron Jones was arrested on 1 October 2017 before his
first NFL start for the Green Bay Packers. [@1] Jones was pulled over at 11:37
a.m. on Sunday, 1 October after driving at 79 miles per hour in a 55
mile-per-hour zone. [@1] The stop was on Highway 172 east of the Pilgram Way
exit in Green Bay and was made by Wisconsin State Patrol Trooper D. Larson. [@3]
The trooper smelled marijuana coming from the vehicle, observed that Jones had
red and bloodshot eyes, and Jones said he had smoked marijuana after breakfast.
[@3] Jones was charged with three offences of operating a vehicle with a
controlled substance in his system, speeding and operating without a valid
licence. [@2]

Jones was placed under arrest for operating with a restricted controlled
substance, and the vehicle was searched and no contraband was found. [@3] A
second trooper had Jones do field sobriety tests, noting he used his arms for
balance during the walk. [@3] Jones was taken for a blood test at St. Vincent
Hospital in Green Bay and was released from custody at 1:33 p.m. after Packers
cornerback Donatello Brown signed a responsibility form to take care of him.
[@1] He entered a not guilty plea on 15 November 2017. [@2] His next court
appearance was scheduled as a final pre-trial on 1 February 2018. [@1]

Packers head coach Mike McCarthy said he was aware of the situation and had
spoken to Jones after the incident. [@1] The NFL was not expected to discipline
Jones until the legal case was resolved. [@2]
