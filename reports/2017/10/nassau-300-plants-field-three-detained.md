Three suspects, two men and a woman, were taken into custody for questioning,
according to a Nassau Guardian report relayed by Jamaican media. [@1] Two of the
suspects were Jamaican and one was Bahamian, though it was unclear which gender
applied to which nationality. [@1] Police in the Bahamas linked Jamaicans to the
ganja find. [@1]

Bahamian police seized almost 300 marijuana plants on Friday after discovering a
large cannabis field at the back of a building in Nassau. [@1] Officers from the
Drug Enforcement Unit went to the area after receiving a tip. [@1] The field was
at the back of a building on Portago Road, off Thompson Boulevard. [@2]

Police had not estimated the value of the plants by Sunday but said the setup
showed a sophisticated marijuana operation. [@1] The area around the field was
heavily overgrown with weeds and other plants, including okra and mint. [@1]
Police theorised that the surrounding bushes were likely used to disguise the
field. [@1]
