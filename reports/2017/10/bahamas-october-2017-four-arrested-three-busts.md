Police arrested a man on Crooked Island for possession of marijuana after
finding him with the illegal drug when officers on routine patrol searched the
man in the self-drive car around 10am on Monday. [@1] Two Nassau men were
arrested in a similar stop-and-search on Monday afternoon near the six-legged
roundabout in Nassau after five pounds of marijuana was discovered in the
vehicle they were driving. [@1]

One man was arrested on Sunday around 9pm at a Kemp Road home in Nassau after
over five pounds of marijuana was found in the house where he was present,
according to a police crime report issued on Tuesday. [@1] The find near the
six-legged roundabout in Nassau on Monday afternoon was much larger than the
amount found during the routine patrol stop and search on Crooked Island earlier
that morning. [@1] The stash found at the Kemp Road home on Sunday night was a
similar stash to the drugs found in the earlier stop and search vehicle seizures
that weekend. [@1]
