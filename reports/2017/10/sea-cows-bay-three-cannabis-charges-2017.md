Three men from the island of Jost Van Dyke were charged with drug offences on 4
October 2017, a day after a police operation in the Sea Cows Bay area of
Tortola. [@2] They were Dwayne Borris Donovan, 28, Shomarie Jahmeed Chinnery,
27, and Rubbie Pickering, 27, all from Jost Van Dyke. [@2] The two charges
against them were unlawful possession of cannabis and unlawful possession of
cannabis with intent to supply. [@1]

The Royal Virgin Islands Police Force said its officers, together with officers
from the Cayman Islands, the United Kingdom and Bermuda, stopped and searched a
vehicle during curfew hours. [@1] Police said that during the search they
allegedly found 'a large quantity of both drugs and cash'. [@1] A second account
reported that the officers were conducting patrols after curfew hours when they
stopped the vehicle in which the drugs and cash were uncovered. [@2]

The cash and drugs were confiscated and, according to the RVIPF, remained part
of a police investigation. [@1]
