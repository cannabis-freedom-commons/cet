Law-enforcement officers searched the home of a Novopolotsk man born in 1979 and
found drugs there. [@1] The man was detained following the search. [@1] Police
said the man had stored the drugs with the aim of selling them. [@1]

Officers seized 2 kg 80 g of dried marijuana at the residence. [@1] Officers
also found hallucinogenic mushrooms at the same residence. [@1] Vitebsk regional
police representative Vitaly Sapronenko called the haul the largest marijuana
batch seized in Vitebsk Oblast since the start of 2017. [@1] The seizure took
place in Novopolotsk, with the announcement made from Vitebsk. [@1]

A criminal case on illegal trafficking in narcotic drugs was opened against the
detained man under part 3 of article 328 of the Criminal Code. [@1] The offence
carries 8 to 13 years of imprisonment with possible confiscation of property.
