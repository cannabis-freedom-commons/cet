Algeria's national anti-drug office, ONLDT, said its services seized more than
38 tonnes of cannabis resin during the first eight months of 2017, 80.62% of it
in the west. [@1,2] The office put the exact total at 38,485.9 kilograms, of which
31,025.6 kilograms came from the western region. [@1,2] The figures were based on
the reports of three services: the Gendarmerie, the DGSN national police and
Customs. [@1,2]

The office said the quantity seized in the first eight months of 2017 was 51.83%
lower than in the same period, mainly because security had been reinforced at
the borders. [@1,2] A source close to the office said the amounts seized remained
high despite the fall. [@1,2] The regional breakdown put 16.6% of the seizures in
the south, 1.6% in the east and 1.05% in the centre. [@1,2]

The same bilan recorded 985.7 grams of heroin seized in the first eight months
of 2017, down from 1,366.3 grams a year earlier, a fall of 27.8%. [@1,2] Cocaine
seizures fell 92.56%, from 53,084.6 grams in the first eight months of 2016 to
3,951.4 grams in the same period of 2017. [@1,2] Seizures of psychotropic tablets
fell slightly, from 750,831 to 745,996, a drop of 0.64%, with 29.12% seized in
the west. [@1,2] The services implicated 25,735 people in drug-related cases
during the eight months, including 134 foreigners, and recorded 403 people on
the run. [@1,2] The services handled 19,929 drug cases in the eight months, against 19,900 a year earlier, of which 4,929 concerned illicit drug trafficking, 14,985 possession and use, and 15 cannabis cultivation. [@2] The office said the number of people implicated was up 4.64% on the same period, and that 5,236 of those implicated were cannabis resin traffickers and 2,959 traffickers of psychotropic substances. [@2]
