Pueblo County sheriff's detectives, FBI agents and DEA agents executed search
warrants at two properties in Pueblo County, Colorado, early on Monday 9 October
2017. [@2] Four men described by the sheriff's office as having ties to Cuba
were arrested. [@2] Jose Alberto Gonzalez, 31, Sergio Luis Martinez-Suarez, 44,
and Yoandris Bacallao-Ramos, 40, were arrested at the 58th Lane house. [@2]
Francisco Jose Sanchez, 50, was arrested at the Quail Road property. [@3] All
four were booked into the Pueblo County Jail. [@3] The three arrested at 58th
Lane were held on suspicion of possession of marijuana with intent to
distribute, cultivation of marijuana and conspiracy. [@2] Sanchez also faced a
charge of possession of a weapon by a previous offender. [@2]

Detectives found 368 marijuana plants in total. [@1] At the 58th Lane house they
found 85 mature plants in three indoor commercial-type grows with lighting and
irrigation systems. [@1] At Quail Road they found 283 mature plants in two large
outdoor grows, one fenced in and one hidden under a large fabric awning. [@4]
Officers seized five firearms and growing equipment. [@1] The sheriff's office
put the street value of the plants at about $1 million. [@2]

Investigators believed the two operations were related and that the men knew
each other. [@4] The sheriff's office said the marijuana had been processed in
Colorado and shipped out of state for sale. [@2]
