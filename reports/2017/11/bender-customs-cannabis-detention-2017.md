Customs officers from the anti-smuggling unit of Bender Customs detained a man
near the state border on 15 November 2017 as he tried to bring drugs into
Transnistria. [@1] An operational-investigative team of the Transnistrian
Interior Ministry arrived at the scene, according to the State Customs
Committee. [@1]

He was carrying two packages of plant material resembling crushed cannabis and
told officers it was a drug-containing substance he used personally. [@1] A
special metal smoking pipe was found on him. [@1]

The case materials were passed to the Bender Department of Internal Affairs.
