Gardaí from the National Drugs and Organised Crime Bureau and the Special Crime
Task Force searched two commercial premises at Muckerstown, Ashbourne, on
Wednesday 15 November 2017 as part of a planned international operation.
[@1,3] They found cannabis herb packed in wrapped one-kilogram parcels inside
crates, alongside cocaine; gardaí valued the combined haul at €7 million.
[@1,3] Two men in their thirties were arrested at the scene and detained under
section 2 of the Criminal Justice (Drug Trafficking) Act 1996 at Ashbourne
station. [@1,3]

Dubliners Michael Smullen, 31, and Keith Donoghue, 37, appeared before Trim
District Court on Friday charged with possession of drugs with intent to sell
or supply and were remanded in custody. [@2] The court heard it was alleged
that Smullen had been making boxes from flat packs for his co-accused to pack
cannabis into. [@2] A parallel operation in the Netherlands the next day saw
eight further suspects detained and more than 160 kilograms of cannabis and
cocaine seized. [@1]
