Four people were arraigned before Senior Magistrate Aretha Ford in Belize City
late on 30 November 2017 over possession of eight rounds of ammunition and 40
grams of cannabis. [@1] Those arraigned were 61-year-old Sonia Shepherd, her
40-year-old son Kenrick Danderson, her daughter Aretha Danderson and her
18-year-old grandson Ahkeem O’Brien. [@1] They all pleaded not guilty to the
charges. [@1] The charge of keeping ammunition without a gun licence could not
receive bail in the Magistrate’s Court, but defence attorney Dickie Bradley had
already filed bail applications for the four at the Supreme Court. [@1] Justice
Adolph Lucas agreed to hear the bail applications soon after the arraignment.
[@1] The four were then escorted to the Supreme Court, where they were each
offered bail of $7,500. [@1]

Police said the charges followed a search of a house in Hattieville on 28
November 2017. [@1] Officers reported finding eight rounds of ammunition in a
clothes barrel and 40 grams of cannabis under a mattress during that search.
[@1] All four people who were present in the home at the time of the search were
arrested and charged. [@1]
