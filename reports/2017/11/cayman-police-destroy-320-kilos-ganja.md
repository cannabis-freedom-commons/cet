Police destroyed hundreds of kilos of drugs on Tuesday morning. [@1] According
to the Royal Cayman Islands Police Service, some 184 kilos of ganja seized in
arrests and operations was destroyed. [@1] About 140 kilos of ganja found after
washing up on shore was destroyed alongside the seized ganja. [@1]

The combined ganja destruction totalled 320 kilos. [@1] Both component amounts
were worded as approximations, with the 184-kilo and 140-kilo figures totalling
about 324 kilos. [@1]

Police said 2.5 kilos of cocaine that had been found was also destroyed. [@1]
The 2.5-kilo amount was described as cocaine, not ganja. [@1]
