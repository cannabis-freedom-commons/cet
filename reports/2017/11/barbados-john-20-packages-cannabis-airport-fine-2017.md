Akil Xavin Jovanni John, a 25-year-old Vincentian, was detained in Barbados and
charged with unlawful possession of cannabis, unlawful possession with intent to
supply, trafficking and importation. [@1] The cannabis was intercepted on
Wednesday 15 November 2017 at Grantley Adams International Airport, where
members of the Drug Squad arrested and charged him. [@1] He was questioned
shortly after arrival, then handed to Customs, where a search of his Pullman
duffel bag revealed 20 packages of marijuana. [@1] He had arrived from Kingstown
and was interviewed by members of the Drug Squad. [@3]

He appeared before magistrate Elwood Watts in the Oistins Magistrates' Court on
Thursday 16 November 2017 and pleaded guilty. [@1] He was fined $15,000
forthwith, or two years in prison, for importation of cannabis, and was
convicted, reprimanded and discharged for the other offences. [@2] The fine was
BDS$15,000, with a two-year prison term as the alternative. [@5] WE FM also
reported a two-year prison term as the alternative to the importation fine. [@3]

The luggage had passed through Argyle International Airport in St Vincent
without an alert being raised. [@1] Investigations had already led to the
dismissal of one Argyle employee. [@1] Three employees were later dismissed and
two suspended as the Argyle inquiries continued. [@4] The Royal St Vincent and
the Grenadines Police Force was leading those inquiries. [@4] In March 2018
Argyle manager Hadley Bourne said the suspected facilitation had been dealt with
and that the security system had worked. [@5] Bourne did not say whether those
involved were charged, suspended or fired. [@5]
