Johannes Nkengelezi (50) was arrested at Nkurenkuru in the Kavango West region
last Thursday. [@1] The arrest was in connection with being in possession of six
cannabis plants at Nkurenkuru. [@1]

The six plants weigh about 2,18kg, reported here as 2.18 kg. [@1] Police put the
street value of the plants at about N$21 800. [@1] He made his first court
appearance in the Rundu Magistrate’s Court and was granted bail on Monday. [@1]

Nkengelezi was released on bail of N$2 000. [@1] His case was postponed to 21
February. [@1]
