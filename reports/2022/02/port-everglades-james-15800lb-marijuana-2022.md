The crew of the Coast Guard Cutter James offloaded 54,500 pounds of cocaine and
15,800 pounds of marijuana at Port Everglades in Fort Lauderdale on the morning
of 17 February 2022. [@2] US Coast Guard crews made the seizures in several
separate interceptions in the eastern Pacific Ocean and the Caribbean Sea. [@1]
Capt. Todd Vance, commanding officer of the James, said the crew took 30 metric
tons of drugs in their purest form, the largest tonnage seizure in the vessel's
history. [@2]

Coast Guard officials valued the whole haul at more than $1.06 billion, a
combined estimate covering the cocaine as well as the marijuana. [@2] Karl
Schultz of the Coast Guard said the cargo resulted from 25 separate
interceptions and collective work by many people. [@1]

The Coast Guard said it had increased its presence, with allies, in the eastern
Pacific and Caribbean, transit routes for drugs from Central and South America.
[@1] At the time, the authorities had not said which country or countries the
drugs came from. [@1]
