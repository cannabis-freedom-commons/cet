Police carried out a large-scale investigation in the Gare district of
Luxembourg City on the evening of Tuesday 8 February 2022, an operation that led
to the arrest of one person. [@1] During the operation officers identified one
individual in the district as a suspected major drug dealer. [@1] Officers
arrested the man by order of the prosecutor's office, making him the only
reported detainee in the operation. [@1]

Officers seized 300 grammes, about 0.3 kilograms, of hashish from the man during
the same Gare operation. [@1] The seizure and the arrest concerned the same
suspected dealer, and no wider group of suspects was reported in this operation.
[@1] After the arrest the dealer had to face magistrates for the next step of
the case. [@1]
