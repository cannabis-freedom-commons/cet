Customs Anti-Narcotics Unit (CANU) officers intercepted motorcar PKK 4588 at
Number 69 Village, Corentyne, on 22 February 2022 and seized 129.6 pounds (58.8
kg) of cannabis from it. [@4] Two male occupants got out of the car and opened
fire on the officers when confronted. [@2] Both men escaped, and no arrests were
reported. [@3] News Source Guyana reported the haul as 129 pounds of marijuana
found in several large parcels in the car. [@1]

CANU put the street value of the cannabis at GY$17,640,000. [@3] The unit said
it was suspected the cannabis was intended for transshipment to neighbouring
Suriname. [@2] INews Guyana placed the interception in Region Six (East
Berbice-Corentyne). [@3] News Source Guyana said CANU described its Corentyne
operations as part of efforts to curb the flow of narcotics in the Berbice
region. [@1] HGPTV reported that CANU said investigations were ongoing. [@4]
CANU also warned that people caught trafficking narcotics would be prosecuted
and their assets confiscated. [@4]
