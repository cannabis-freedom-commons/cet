Nicaragua's army detained three Nicaraguan men in the community of Santa Rosa,
Jalapa municipality, Nueva Segovia department, on the night of 16 February 2022.
[@1] Troops of the 1st Regional Military Command stopped them at a military
checkpoint about 8 km northwest of Jalapa at around 23:00. [@3] The detainees
were Eddy Antonio Sequeira Mejía, 26, Jaime Antonio Cáceres, 26, and Jader
Manuel Duarte Vásquez, 21, each riding a motorcycle. [@1] The men, the drug, the
money, the weapon and the motorcycles were handed to the competent authorities.
[@1]

The army said the three were carrying 100 marijuana ovules, small egg-shaped
packets of the drug, brought illegally from Honduras. [@1] Officers also seized
8,000 córdobas, 111 US dollars, a .38-calibre revolver with 21 cartridges and
two mobile phones. [@1] La Nueva Radio YA reported the same revolver with 20
rounds. [@2]

Colonel José Hilcias Rizo Rizo, head of the 1st Regional Military Command, gave
the detainees' names and said the drug was carried on the motorcycles. [@2]
Canal 2 reported that the detainees, the drug, the money and the three
motorcycles were handed over for judicial proceedings. [@3] The operation formed
part of the state's "Muro de Contención", or Containment Wall, strategy and its
Border Protection Plan. [@4] The army said troops followed the health ministry's
COVID-19 protection measures during the seizure. [@1]
