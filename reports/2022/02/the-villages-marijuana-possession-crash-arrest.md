Russell Kornegay, 44, of Wildwood, was driving southbound on Morse Boulevard in
The Villages when he struck a vehicle stopped at a traffic light on San Marino
Drive on Monday 28 February 2022. [@1] He was arrested after the three-vehicle
crash. [@1] The collision forced the first vehicle into the rear of the car
ahead of it. [@1]

A Sumter County Sheriff's Office investigator suspected Kornegay was under the
influence of narcotics and called for backup, including a K-9 unit whose dog
alerted on the Ford Focus. [@1] A search turned up a burnt cigarette containing
marijuana. [@1] Officers also found 53 grams of amphetamine and a loaded
syringe, which are excluded from the cannabis figures. [@1] Kornegay was
transported to a local medical facility for evaluation. [@1]

He was arrested on charges of trafficking in amphetamine, possession of
marijuana, possession of drug equipment and driving under the influence. [@1] He
was booked at the Sumter County Detention Center on $13,000 bond. [@1]
