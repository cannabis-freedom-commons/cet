Cameroon's East Region gendarmerie legion intercepted an ACTROS truck at the
Bonis weighbridge at the entrance to Bertoua on 16 February 2022, acting on
intelligence. [@2] A search in the presence of the Bertoua public prosecutor
found 101 sacks of cannabis hidden beneath the truck's declared load of beans.
[@2] The gendarmerie said the load weighed 3,716 kg, was worth about 60 million
CFA francs and was bound for Garoua in the north. [@2] The people intercepted in
the traffic were referred to the judicial authorities to answer for the offence.
[@2]

The 101 sacks of cannabis were incinerated on 17 March 2022 at the HYSACAM waste
centre in Bonis, under the prefect's supervision and in the presence of a
dedicated commission. [@2] KOACI reported the seizure as a record of more than
three tonnes of cannabis resin by Cameroon's national gendarmerie. [@1] Another
account reported that the 101 sacks were seized on 16 February and burned on 17
March in Bertoua's Nkolbikon district. [@3]

KOACI noted that the East Region is a crossroads between Adamaoua and the Centre
for goods in transit and supplies neighbouring countries. [@1]
