Papua Police's drug-crime directorate arrested suspected distributor FW at
Jayapura Port as he was about to depart on KM Cirimai. [@1] Police said FW was
arrested on Tuesday 15 February 2022 with 20 ready-to-distribute ganja packs
allegedly meant for circulation in Manokwari, West Papua. [@1] Officers
investigated around Jayapura Port where the ship was berthed after receiving
information about a ganja shipment on the vessel. [@1] Officers caught the
suspect as he was climbing the ship's boarding stairs. [@1]

On inspection, officers found 20 large clear plastic packs of suspected ganja
wrapped inside black plastic carry bags. [@1] The black plastic bags were wound
with brown tape and stored inside a black cloth tote bag. [@1] Police said they
received initial information about a ganja shipment using KM Cirimai and carried
out follow-up inquiries. [@1]

FW and the seized packs were secured at the Drug Crime Directorate office for
further investigation. [@1] Police said the suspect was charged under Article
111(1) of Law No. 35 of 2009 on narcotics. [@1] Papua Police public-relations
chief Ahmad Kamal announced the arrest in Jayapura on Wednesday. [@1]
