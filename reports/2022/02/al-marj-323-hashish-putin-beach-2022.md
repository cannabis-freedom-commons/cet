Libya's anti-drug brigade seized 323 bars of hashish, each weighing 250 grams,
at Al Marj in the north-east of the country on Saturday 5 February 2022. [@1]
The load came to about 80.75 kg of cannabis resin. [@1] Each bar bore a portrait
of the Russian president, Vladimir Putin, in a black suit and tie. [@1] The
General Administration of Criminal Investigation in Al-Marj said a local
resident found the bars on the seafront and reported them. [@2] Al Marj lies
about 100 km east of Benghazi. [@4]

The anti-drug service announced the find on Monday 7 February, and an official
told AFP that the cargo had washed up on a beach near Al Marj. [@1] The same
official could not say where the shipment came from or why Putin's portrait was
on the bars. [@1] The Libya Observer reported that the bars had probably come
from a smuggled consignment that sank offshore. [@2] An anti-narcotics officer
told Independent Arabia that no one was caught with the load, so its origin and
intended destination remain unknown. [@4]

Photographs of the stacked, cellophane-wrapped bars circulated widely on social
media. [@3] Days earlier, residents of Talmitha and Boutraba found hashish
stamped with a portrait of the Colombian drug lord Pablo Escobar. [@2] Libya's
drug trade has grown markedly since the 2011 fall of Muammar Gaddafi loosened
controls on its borders. [@1]
