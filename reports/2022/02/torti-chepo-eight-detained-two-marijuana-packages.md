Panama's National Border Service (Senafront) seized two packages of marijuana
and detained six Panamanian citizens, three of them minors, and two Colombians
for their alleged link to the illicit substance. [@1] Commissioner Adrián
González, head of the eastern Panama Senafront brigade, said the operation
detained five adults, two of them Colombian, and three minors, and seized two
further packages of dried herb known as marijuana. [@1] Agents of Senafront
carried out the action with the anti-drug prosecutor's office of the Public
Ministry in the locality of Tortí, in Chepo district, east of the capital. [@1]

The same seizure included 499 packages presumed to be cocaine, which are not
counted as cannabis. [@1] Officers also seized an AK-47 rifle, a 20-gauge
shotgun and a 9 millimetre pistol. [@1]

González said Senafront units on a routine patrol came upon a column of backpack
couriers who answered with gunfire when ordered to halt. [@1] He said the
operation dismantled a drug-trafficking group operating in national territory.
[@1] González warned that remote areas of the country do not escape organised
crime and said Panamanian community members strongly assist organised crime and
drug trafficking. [@1]
