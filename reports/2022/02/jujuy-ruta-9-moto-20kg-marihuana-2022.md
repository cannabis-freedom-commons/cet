Gendarmes of the Pumamarca group of Squadron 53 'Jujuy', deployed at kilometre
1751 of National Route 9 in Jujuy province, saw a motorcycle approaching their
checkpoint. [@1] The rider switched off his lights, turned them back on and
swerved sharply away at speed, leading the gendarmes to begin a controlled
pursuit. [@1] During that pursuit the motorcycle struck the force's control
point. [@1] The woman with him was detained at once and the rider was detained
after a few minutes, having tried to hide among weeds. [@2]

Searching the motorcycle, officers found 12 packages inside two backpacks, some
holding a compact substance and others plant material. [@1] A field Narcotest
returned positive for 20 kilograms 270 grams (20.27 kg) of marijuana and for 2
kilograms 10 grams of cocaine. [@1] The federal prosecution unit of Jujuy, the
Unidad Fiscal Federal, ordered the two people detained and the drugs and the
motorcycle seized. [@1]
