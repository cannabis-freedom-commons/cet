On 8 February 2022 police raided a company licensed to grow cannabis for medical purposes in the southern town of Strumica and seized about 1.5 tonnes of cannabis that had not been declared, Interior Minister Oliver Spasovski said the next day. [@1] Spasovski said in a written statement that the quantity was confiscated over irregularities in the firm's operation; no arrests were made. [@1]

North Macedonia has issued more than 60 medical-cannabis cultivation licences since 2016. [@1] A later review of the diversion of licensed crops to the black market noted that no arrests ever followed the Strumica raid. [@2]
