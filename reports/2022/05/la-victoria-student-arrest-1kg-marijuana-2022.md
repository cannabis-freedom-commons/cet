Peru's National Police detained a physics student at a bus terminal in La
Victoria district, Lima, as he collected a kilogram of marijuana. [@1] The
detainee was Daniel Mamani Velarde, 23, who was due in a Proyectos e
Investigación class at 7 p.m. that evening. [@1] Officers of the Escuadrón
Emergencia Centro, a National Police emergency-response unit, stopped him just
after he picked up the parcel sent to him by bus courier. [@1] The intervention
took place at a terminal on the tenth block of Avenida Paseo de la República.
[@1]

Officers said a strong smell came from one of the packages. [@1] Inside the
cardboard box they found eucalyptus leaves and a bag of marijuana. [@1] A police
officer said the eucalyptus had been used to mask the drug's characteristic
smell. [@1]

He smiled at the cameras and sent greetings to his professor, saying he would
just miss the class and be out later. [@1] One officer told América Noticias
that the young man seemed not to grasp the serious risk he faced. [@1] He was
handcuffed as officers spoke to the press. [@3] He was taken to a police station
and faces investigation for the suspected offence against public health. [@2]
