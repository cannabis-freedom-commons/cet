Two men were arrested for operating a drug lab in a space that had housed a
synagogue in Tiberias, according to Israel Police. [@1] Superintendent Aviv
Benzino, head of the Investigations and Intelligence Department at the Tiberias
Police, said officers opened an undercover investigation after receiving
intelligence about the lab a few days earlier. [@1]

Over the weekend, officers raided the lab and seized about 1,700 cannabis
plants, weighing a total of about 320 kilograms (705 lbs). [@1] Officers found
signs of the venue's previous use as a synagogue, including a support column
with a Hebrew inscription reading 'Know before whom you stand'. [@1] Police said
the laboratory operated inside an apartment complex, part of which used to serve
as a synagogue. [@1]

The lab was used for growing, selling and distributing drugs, police said. [@1]
The attorney of the two suspects said he had not yet received the investigation
material because it was at an early stage, and described his clients as two
religious Jewish men from Bnei Brak and Jerusalem who came to live in Tiberias.
[@1] A court extended the suspects' remand by six days. [@1]
