Police on the East Coast of Demerara arrested a 17-year-old schoolboy and his
uncle after marijuana was found at the home of a 65-year-old businessman at
Middle Walk, Buxton, East Coast Demerara. [@1] The teenager and his uncle were
at the house at the time of the operation and were arrested. [@1]

Police Headquarters said the drugs were found in 22 ziplock bags and weighed
37.2 grams (1.3 ounces). [@1] Police ranks found the marijuana during a search
of the businessman's home. [@1]

The businessman was identified as Clairmonte Barnes, called ‘Rastaman’ and
‘Chubby’, who is presently before the court for possession of narcotics for the
purpose of trafficking. [@1] Investigations are ongoing. [@1]
