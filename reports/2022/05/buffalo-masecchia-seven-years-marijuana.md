U.S. District Judge John L. Sinatra Jr. sentenced Michael Masecchia, a longtime
English teacher at Grover Cleveland High School, to seven years in prison on 4
May 2022 for trafficking marijuana into Buffalo and its suburbs. [@1] Masecchia,
56, of Williamsville, was one of several local men targeted in a federal
investigation into suspected organized crime. [@1] He pleaded guilty in December
2020 to possession with intent to distribute marijuana and possession of
firearms in furtherance of drug trafficking. [@1] His sentencing had been
delayed after he was diagnosed with lymphoma and treated with chemotherapy. [@1]

He admitted running a marijuana growing facility in rural Cattaraugus County and
trafficking marijuana beginning around 1999. [@1] The U.S. Attorney's Office
said he and others grew marijuana in Ellicottville and Franklinville and
distributed the harvest to resellers and users. [@2] Law enforcement officials
conservatively estimated that he trafficked at least 2,204 pounds of marijuana
between 1999 and 2019. [@1] The plea agreement recommended a prison term of
seven to eight years. [@1] Judge Sinatra allowed him to surrender to federal
prison officials at a later date. [@1]

On 23 August 2019, investigators from Homeland Security Investigations, the FBI,
the Erie County Sheriff's Office and the Niagara County Sheriff's Office
searched his Main Street home and seized $27,950, firearms and ammunition,
marijuana in Mason jars and small containers, THC edibles, cannabis syrup and
suspected hashish. [@1] He told officials that former DEA agent Joseph
Bongiovanni had helped him and other traffickers avoid arrest. [@1] Bongiovanni
has denied the allegations, including that he received $250,000 in bribes. [@1]
