Customs officers of the Abengourou regional directorate intercepted a truck
carrying an empty 40-foot container in Kotokossou, a village in Damé
sub-prefecture of Agnibilékrou department in eastern Côte d'Ivoire, on the
evening of 1 May 2022. [@1] Inspecting the vehicle, they found suspicious
parcels in a false bottom built between the container and the truck's floor.
[@1]

On the instruction of the customs director general, Da Pierre Alphonse, the
Abengourou regional director, Colonel Sanogo Ousmane, transferred the truck to
the base of the Unité Mobile d'Intervention Rapide (UMIR), the customs
rapid-response unit, at Yopougon-Gesco. [@1] The search there uncovered 1,300
plaquettes, or packets, of cannabis weighing 1,300 kilograms in total, or 1.3
tonnes. [@1]

Presse Côte d'Ivoire attributed the account to customs services and reported
that 1.3 tonnes of cannabis were seized at Agnibilékrou on 1 May 2022. [@3]
Ivoirematin, citing a customs note, reported the same 1,300 packets and a total
weight of 1,300 kg. [@2]
