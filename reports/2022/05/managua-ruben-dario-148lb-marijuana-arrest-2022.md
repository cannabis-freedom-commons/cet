Nicaragua's National Police arrested David Jonathan López Sánchez, 35, in barrio
Rubén Darío, in District I of Managua, at 10:20 pm on Wednesday 11 May 2022,
after profiling a Hyundai car with plate M 177 006 on a public street. [@1]
Commissioner Manuel Zambrana, head of the District I police delegation in
Managua, presented the case as a result of the Plan Muro de Contención, a
National Police strategy against organised crime and drug trafficking. [@1] El
19 Digital, reporting the same police briefing, put the value of the 148.86
pounds of marijuana at 14,800 US dollars and gave the same date, time and place
for the car stop. [@2]

A search of the car found three black plastic bags holding 150 egg-shaped
packages wrapped in adhesive tape and filled with green plant material. [@1] A
field test came back positive for marijuana, with a weight of 148.86 pounds,
about 67.5 kg. [@1] Criminalistics investigators carried out a chemical test
that concluded positive for marijuana. [@1] Officers applied the Scintrex Trace
particle detector, which registers marijuana particles, to the car and to the
detainee's back and palms, with positive results. [@1] The evidence police
listed included the 148.86 pounds of marijuana and the car with plate M 177 006,
which they said was used to move the drug. [@1]

The man and the evidence were to be sent to the competent authorities in Managua
so that a judge could set the prison time he must serve for the offence of
abastecedor de droga, a supplier of drugs. [@1] The National Police said its
officers constantly carry out raids and patrols in streets, neighbourhoods and
markets to curb crime. [@1]
