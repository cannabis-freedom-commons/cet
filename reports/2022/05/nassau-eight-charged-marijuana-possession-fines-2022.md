Eight people were charged with marijuana possession in separate cases in Nassau
on Monday, and four received fines rather than custodial sentences. [@1] Of the
eight matters, five people pleaded guilty while three denied the charges. [@1]
The four fines were ordered in guilty-plea cases involving two grams to just
under four ounces of Indian Hemp. [@1]

Ancheco Deveaux, 37, was arraigned over three and a quarter ounces of marijuana
with intent to supply from a 29 April arrest. [@1] He pleaded guilty and was
fined $500 or three months at the Bahamas Department of Correctional Services.
[@1] Prosecutors alleged Billy Carter Jr had 14 grams of marijuana and $260 from
money laundering on 29 April. [@1] He pleaded guilty to both charges. [@1] He
was fined $500 for drugs and $500 for money laundering. [@1] Jermeckia Adderley
was found with seven cannabis brownies and one Rice Krispies edible on 29
January. [@1] She pleaded guilty. [@1] The magistrate ordered her to pay a $500
fine or face three months and placed her on a year’s probation. [@2] Andre Dames
faced possession over an alleged two grams of marijuana. [@1] He was fined $500
or two months imprisonment. [@1] The court also ordered Dames to undergo six
months’ probation. [@1]

Jamal Hanna was arrested on 29 April allegedly with 15 grams of marijuana. [@1]
He pleaded guilty. [@1] He was remanded for treatment and evaluation at
Sandilands Rehabilitation Centre. [@1] Hanna is set to return for sentencing on
July 28. [@1] Antonio Glinton was charged with possession of two grams of
suspected Indian hemp. [@1] He pleaded not guilty to the charge. [@1] His bail
was set at $200 with one surety. [@1] Aracheo Robert Poitier, 32, was charged
with possession with intent to supply. [@1] Prosecutors alleged Poitier had just
under four ounces of Indian Hemp on April 28. [@1] He pleaded not guilty. [@1]
The court granted him $10,000 bail. [@1] The matter was adjourned to June 7.
