Dante Baldocchi, 30, who lives in California, was sentenced to 21 months in
federal prison and four years' supervised release for illegally exporting
marijuana grown in Oregon to Georgia for resale and laundering the proceeds.
[@1] The sentencing took place on 23 May 2022. [@2]

Between January 2017 and June 2020, Baldocchi bought marijuana in Oregon and
arranged to ship it by freight to Georgia, where he and others sold it to
distributors in the Atlanta area and in North Carolina. [@1] Officials said he
and his associates shipped portions of the proceeds from Georgia back to Oregon
in vacuum-sealed bags and other portions concealed in buckets of drywall
spackle. [@1] On 9 June 2020 a federal search warrant was executed on
Baldocchi's then-home in Milwaukie, where investigators found more than 330
pounds (about 150 kg) of marijuana. [@1] On 19 August 2021 Baldocchi and his
associate Hunter Lacaden, 27, of Portland, were charged by criminal information
with conspiring to distribute marijuana and commit money laundering. [@1]
Baldocchi pleaded guilty to both charges on 13 October 2021. [@1]

Lacaden pleaded guilty to conspiring to distribute marijuana on 21 September
2021 and was sentenced on 28 March 2022 to 30 months in federal prison and three
years' supervised release. [@1] Homeland Security Investigations and
IRS-Criminal Investigation investigated the case, assisted by the Clackamas
County Sheriff's Office and Portland Police Bureau. [@2] U.S. Attorney Scott
Erik Asphaug of the District of Oregon announced the sentence. [@2] Assistant
U.S. Attorney Julia E. Jarrett prosecuted the case. [@2]
