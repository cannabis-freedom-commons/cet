Nicaragua's National Police announced the seizure of 1,102 pounds of marijuana,
equivalent to half a tonne. [@1] The seizure was made in the community of Buenos
Aires, in Estelí department, at about 3 pm on Thursday 12 May 2022, when a
patrol on surveillance duty saw people moving about a house. [@1] Investigators
raided the property and arrested David Jonathan López Sánchez, Harvin Nicomedes
Gómez Torrez and Jairo Antonio Talavera Ruiz. [@1] El 19 Digital gave the three
men's ages as 35. [@2]

Officers seized 17 white and red sacks holding 933 oval packages wrapped in
clear adhesive tape. [@1] A field test came back positive for marijuana, with a
weight of 1,102 pounds (half a tonne). [@1] Police valued the marijuana at
110,200 US dollars. [@1] Officers also seized a shotgun and three 16-gauge
cartridges. [@1] El 19 Digital reported that police were also looking for a
fourth man, Felipe Natanael Mercado Romero, over drug trafficking and illegal
possession of firearms. [@2] El 19 Digital described the three detained men as
held for drug trafficking and illegal possession of firearms. [@2]

Forensic chemical and ballistics tests confirmed marijuana and found gunpowder
residues on a shotgun that appeared to have been fired recently, and on the
hands and backs of the detained men. [@1] Investigators ran the Scintrex Trace
particle detector over the men's clothing, hands and backs, with positive
results for marijuana particles. [@1] The men and the evidence were handed to
the competent authorities in Estelí. [@1]
