On the Tunisian-Algerian frontier, border brigades stationed at Bouderies and
Foussana in Kasserine governorate seized 155 slabs of Zatla cannabis resin
weighing nearly 15.5 kg from a car intercepted at dawn on Monday 24 October
2022. [@3] The car had refused to obey officers' orders to stop before it was
intercepted and searched. [@3] The car's driver and his passenger escaped into
Algerian territory, so no arrest at the scene was reported. [@2]

The land border division of the National Guard at Bouderies reported that its
officers made the seizure aboard a car at 1 a.m. on 24 October 2022. [@1]
Officers put the load at 15.5 kg of Zatla cannabis contained in 155 slabs. [@2]

A security source said the operation followed information received by the forces
that an individual planned to carry a quantity of drugs from Algeria into
Tunisia. [@3] The authorities said the necessary judicial measures were taken
against the two fugitives after their escape. [@1]
