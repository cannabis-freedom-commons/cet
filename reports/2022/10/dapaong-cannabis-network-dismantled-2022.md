Defence and security forces carried out unannounced checks in the city of
Dapaong, specifically the Natbagou-Kpakoite zone, on Tuesday 18 October 2022.
[@1] The gendarmerie, the police and the military jointly carried out identity
verification in that zone during the checks. [@1] The 18 October action was
described as a surprise descent by the defence and security forces in Dapaong.
[@1] At the end of the operation, the forces dismantled a network of cannabis
traffickers. [@1] The forces seized several vehicles without papers during the
same operation. [@1]

According to the authorities, the checks formed part of the fight against
terrorism and banditry in the Savanes region. [@1] The sweep followed several
motorbike thefts and banditry cases in the city of Dapaong. [@1] Motorbike theft
was named among the cases preceding the surprise descent in Dapaong. [@1] The
joint checks brought together gendarmerie, police and military personnel acting
as the defence and security forces. [@1] The operation was expected to extend to
every neighbourhood of the city of Dapaong. [@1]
