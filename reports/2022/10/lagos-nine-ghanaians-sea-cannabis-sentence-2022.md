Nine Ghanaians who attempted to smuggle 10,843.95 kg of cannabis into Nigeria through the sea were convicted and sentenced on Friday 14 October 2022. [@1,2] Justice Akintayo Aluko of the Federal High Court, Lagos, convicted them of conspiracy and dealing in cannabis and sentenced each of them to eight years in prison, 72 years in total. [@1,2]

The convicts are Victor Wuddah, Freeman Gazul, Adotete Joseph, Sottie Moses, Sottie Stephen, Christian Tette, Kanu Okonipa, Daniel Koyepti and Kanu Natte. [@1,2] They had been arraigned under charge number FHC/L/292C/2021 after investigations by the National Drug Law Enforcement Agency (NDLEA). [@1,2]

The nine had first been intercepted by the marine unit of the Nigeria Security and Civil Defence Corps on the high sea at about 2.00 a.m. on 20 February and paraded in Lagos with 201 bags of Cannabis Sativa, estimated at over N200 million. [@3] The Corps said each bag weighed between 45 and 50 kg, that the men used two big boats to convey the substance from Ghana, and that the suspects and exhibits would be handed to the NDLEA for further tests and prosecution. [@3]
