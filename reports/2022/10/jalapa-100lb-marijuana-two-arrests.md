Police in Nueva Segovia captured Honduran Juan Eliseo Vázquez and Nicaraguan
William Evelio Espinoza Flores in possession of 100 pounds of marijuana. [@1]
Sandinista Police officers made the captures while the two men were moving
through the department of Nueva Segovia. [@1] A police checkpoint stopped their
two motorcycles, one with plate NS 25463 ridden by the 44-year-old Espinoza
Flores and one without plates ridden by the 40-year-old Honduran, according to
the departmental police version carried by local media. [@1]

The load consisted of 100 pounds of marijuana, which the police report valued at
10,000 dollars on the United States market. [@1] The National Police weekly
account confirms that the two men were arrested in Jalapa, Nueva Segovia, with
officers seizing the 100 pounds of marijuana and the two motorcycles. [@2] The
National Police presented those Jalapa arrests as part of operations carried out
from Monday 17 to Sunday 23 October. [@2]

Criminalistics investigators carried out chemical tests that came back positive
for marijuana. [@1] Both men were remitted to the authorities to face criminal
charges, and the seized evidence was placed in the custody of the authorities.
