Panama's National Police carried out a search inside the La Joyita Penitentiary
Centre under Operativo Armagedón. [@1] The 17 October search was conducted
jointly by the Penitentiary System, the National Aeronaval Service and the
National Police. [@1] Officers focused on Pavilions 1 and 2, as reported by
prison-security police chief Luis García. [@1]

Inside those pavilions officers seized 38 bags of dry herb presumed to be
marijuana. [@1] In the same pavilions they reported 934 straws of white powder
presumed to be cocaine and 20 plastic bags of presumed illicit substance. [@1]
They also reported 95 bladed weapons including knives and 150 high-end mobile
phones in those pavilions. [@1] The overall operation total was described as 934
wrappers of presumed illicit substance, 18 digital scales, 150 metal sheets and
150 technological devices. [@1]

In August 2022 the prison held 2,233 convicted and 1,834 accused people, an
overpopulation of 1,230, according to the penitentiary directorate. [@1] The
article notes a similar 26 August find of ammunition, presumed illicit
substances, lighters, digital scales, sharp objects and mobile phones in
Pavilion 9. [@1]
