An investigation directed from Marseille dismantled an international network
that imported cocaine from Belgium and cannabis from Morocco. [@1] The operation
led to the arrest of the suspected network leader and a major Marseille drug
trafficker, according to Marseille prosecutor Dominique Laurens. [@1] Twenty-two
people aged 21 to 59 were arrested on Tuesday and Wednesday in Bouches-du-Rhône,
Alpes-Maritimes, Haut-Rhin, Jura and Côte-d’Or. [@1] A second account confirms
22 people aged 21 to 59 arrested across five French departments on Tuesday and
Wednesday in the Marseille-led operation. [@2]

The network resold the drugs in the Paris region, Occitanie, Provence-Alpes-Côte
d’Azur and Bourgogne-Franche-Comté. [@1] Officers seized 291 kg of cannabis
resin, along with 13 kg of cocaine, nearly 130,000 euros in cash, weapons, ten
vehicles and luxury watches. [@1] The second account likewise reports 291 kg of
cannabis resin, 13 kg of cocaine, nearly 130,000 euros in cash, weapons, ten
vehicles and luxury watches seized. [@2]

Seven of the 22 were placed under judicial investigation on Thursday for
organised-gang drug importation, drug and weapons offences, criminal conspiracy,
aggravated money laundering and failure to justify resources. [@1] Five of the
seven were remanded in custody and one was placed under judicial supervision,
while the seventh requested a deferred hearing. [@1] Seven others were due
before the Marseille investigating judge on Friday with detention requested,
while those arrested around Dijon faced arrest warrants for investigation. [@1]
The operation came under a judicial investigation opened in Marseille in March
after two inquiries from Bouches-du-Rhône and Dijon were combined. [@1]
