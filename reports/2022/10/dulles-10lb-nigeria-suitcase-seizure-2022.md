U.S. Customs and Border Protection officers seized more than 10 pounds of
marijuana from checked baggage at Washington Dulles International Airport on 18
October 2022. [@3] The marijuana was packed as 10 vacuum-sealed packages
concealed inside clothing in a traveler’s suitcase on a flight to Lagos,
Nigeria. [@1] Officers could not find the traveler at the departure gate or
elsewhere in the airport, and an investigation continued. [@2] The packages
weighed a combined 4.7 kilograms, or about 10 pounds and six ounces. [@2]

CBP estimated the marijuana’s street value at about $8,000 in the United States
and about $30,000 in Nigeria. [@1] Marijuana is illegal in Nigeria for both
recreational and medical use. [@1] CBP said Dulles, nearly 5,500 miles from
Lagos, was an unusual route for marijuana smuggling. [@1] Kim Der-Yeghiayan,
acting area port director for CBP’s Area Port of Washington, D.C., said
intercepting a marijuana load crossing the Atlantic to Nigeria was quite
unusual. [@1]
