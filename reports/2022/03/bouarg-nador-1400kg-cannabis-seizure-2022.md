Moroccan police arrested two suspects on Saturday for alleged links to an
international drug trafficking network. [@1] The operation was carried out in
Bouarg locality near the northern city of Nador. [@1] Nador is described as a
northern city. [@1] An investigation was being operated to determine the
trafficking routes of the seized drug. [@1] The trafficking routes had not yet
been established when the operation was reported. [@1]

Officers seized 1.4 tonnes of cannabis in the same Saturday operation. [@1] The
two arrests and the cannabis seizure are reported as parts of a single police
operation. [@1] The reported 1.4 tonnes equals 1,400 kilograms of cannabis. [@1]
The police account of the operation was carried by the official news agency.
[@1]

Efforts to crack down on cannabis have continued in Morocco during the past
decade. [@1] Morocco remains one of the world's largest producers of the
narcotic plant. [@1] The UN Office on Drugs and Crime is the source of that
producer assessment. [@1]
