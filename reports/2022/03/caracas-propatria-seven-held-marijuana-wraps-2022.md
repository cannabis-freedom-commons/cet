Officers of the CICPC drugs division arrested seven men in Propatria, in western
Caracas, on suspicion of microtrafficking, the small-scale retail sale of drugs.
[@2] The investigation began with complaints sent to Douglas Rico, the national
director of the Científica police, through his Instagram account that drugs were
being sold openly in bloque 4 of Propatria and that feuds between the sellers,
nicknamed Mario, El Chavo, Nosle and Ñoño, were unsettling residents. [@1]
Officers went to the block, kept a static watch and detained seven men aged
between 19 and 52. [@2]

In the operation officers seized 165 wraps of marijuana, along with 118 wraps of
crack and 52 wraps of cocaine. [@2] Officers also took cash, five mobile phones,
six two-way radios, seven bags, seven scales, 21 rounds of ammunition and two
ammunition magazines, the equipment Rico said the group used across the sector.
[@1] Rico said the unit would keep answering citizens' reports in real time to
reduce crime in the most vulnerable areas. [@1]

The case was referred to the Public Ministry. [@2]
