Daniel Lloyd Taylor, 25, of Moelfre on the Isle of Anglesey, was sentenced to
ten months in prison. [@1] He had pleaded guilty at Caernarfon Crown Court to
being concerned in the supply of cannabis, possession with intent to supply
cannabis and money laundering. [@1] The sentencing was on 3 March 2022, when a
judge at Caernarfon Crown Court said the offences had been on a "big scale".
[@2] He was jailed for ten months on each of the supply and possession counts,
the terms to run concurrently. [@2] Philip Clemo, defending, said Taylor had
been a cannabis addict who got into a substantial debt and was left with little
option but to work it off. [@2]

North Wales Police were alerted by Royal Mail staff to a suspicious package at
their Llangefni sorting depot on 22 October 2020. [@2] The package smelled
strongly of cannabis and was addressed to Taylor. [@2] It contained 14 tubs of
cannabis worth £100 each, a potential yield of £1,400. [@2] Officers then
searched Taylor's home and found more cannabis weighing just over 1.5 kg, worth
up to £15,750, and £3,105 in cash. [@2] Police also seized a mobile phone with
drug-related messages. [@2] The judge put the total potential value of the
cannabis at £17,000. [@2]

North Wales Police said the sentence should serve as a deterrent to others
thinking about drug supply. [@1] Investigating officer T/Sgt Dylan Thomas said
1.5 kg of cannabis and a substantial amount of money had been recovered. [@1] He
said police would continue to work with the public to gather intelligence and
act on it. [@1]
