Agents assigned to the Fajardo Drugs Division of the Puerto Rico Police seized
marijuana on Tuesday 8 March 2022 inside an abandoned residence in the Demajagua
barrio of Fajardo. [@1] They intervened with Manuel Correa Figueroa, a
36-year-old resident of Maunabo, and Saúl García Luquis, a 43-year-old resident
of Fajardo, and both men were arrested during the operation. [@1] Police
identified the two as Manuel Correa Figueroa, 36, of Maunabo, and Saúl García
Luquis, 43, of Fajardo. [@1] The Puerto Rico Police Bureau said the stop was an
anti-drug intervention and formed part of the police operation 100 x 35. [@2]

Police said the two men possessed 18 pounds of marijuana, about 8.16 kilograms,
84 marijuana plants and 1,128 dollars in cash. [@1] The Police Bureau described
the marijuana taken from the abandoned residence as about 18 pounds. [@2] The
same account said the men had the 84 plants and the 1,128 dollars in cash when
agents stopped them. [@2]

The two detainees remained in police custody while officers consulted the duty
prosecutor about the possible filing of charges. [@1] A further account the next
morning again placed the seizure in the Demajagua barrio and repeated the same
two names, the 18 pounds of marijuana, the 84 plants and the 1,128 dollars in
cash. [@3]
