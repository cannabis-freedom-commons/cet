In Thiruvananthapuram, officers working under Kazhakootam police station limits
arrested two men with cannabis at two separate spots falling under that
station's jurisdiction. [@1] Police named the first man as 25-year-old Alameen
Khan, a resident of Sultan Cottage at Chandramangalam in Anad, and said he was
taken into custody from near Thekkemukku. [@1] Police named the second man as
24-year-old Shikhil, a resident of Chaithram house at Pulippara in Nedumangad,
and said he was taken into custody from under the Kazhakootam flyover. [@1]

From the two men together, officers seized about two-and-a-quarter kilograms of
cannabis, equivalent to about 2.25 kg, as the total quantity recovered in the
two actions. [@1] Police described that entire stock as cannabis that the two
men had kept for the purpose of sale to others. [@1]

The two arrests resulted from inquiries carried out as part of a narcotic
special drive operation. [@1] After the arrests, police produced both of the
accused before a court, which then ordered both of them to be held on remand.
