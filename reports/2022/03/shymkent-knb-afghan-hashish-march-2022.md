On 18 March 2022 the National Security Committee's department detained a man in
Shymkent suspected of involvement in the supply of large quantities of drugs to
Kazakhstan. [@1] The committee identified the detainee only as citizen S. [@2]
The committee presented the operation as the elimination of an international
narcotics channel. [@2]

During procedural measures officers seized more than 10 kilograms of
high-quality Afghan hashish from illegal circulation. [@1] The committee put the
preliminary black-market value of the seized drugs at more than 50 million
tenge. [@1]

A pre-trial investigation was registered under part 3 of article 297 of
Kazakhstan's Criminal Code. [@2]
