Greek anti-narcotics police arrested three men accused of belonging to an
international network that imported and trafficked large quantities of raw
cannabis. [@2] The detainees were two North Macedonian men aged 41 and 45 from
Skopje and a 40-year-old man of Albanian origin. [@2] Police described the
41-year-old as the ringleader, with the 45-year-old as his direct accomplice.
[@2] An arrest warrant had already been issued against the alleged leader for
drug trafficking. [@3]

In a midday operation on a rural road near the Greek border with North
Macedonia, officers found two bags holding 40 packages of raw cannabis weighing
44 kg and 122 grams. [@2] The Albanian Daily News reported that authorities
stopped a car and found two bags containing 40 packages of 44 kg of marijuana.
[@1] The two North Macedonians were arrested at the border despite strong
resistance, police said. [@2]

Later that day the third suspect, the 40-year-old Albanian, was arrested in
Diavata, Thessaloniki, where police said he had come to collect the drugs. [@2]
He was carrying 2,400 euros that police said was payment for transporting the
cannabis to Thessaloniki. [@2] Officers also seized four mobile phones, a
portable radio and a car. [@2] The investigation was run with the DEA office in
Athens, North Macedonian authorities, the Kilkis security police and the Paionia
police department in Kilkis. [@2]

A case file was opened against the three for criminal organisation, drug-law,
immigration-law, violence-against-officials and electronic-communications
offences, and they were to be taken before the judicial authorities. [@2] Police
said the 41-year-old had a previous conviction carrying a two-year prison
sentence for criminal association. [@2]
