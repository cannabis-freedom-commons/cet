Police arrested two men on the old road connecting the municipality to
Guadalupe, Huila, and seized 24,610 grams of marijuana. [@1] The two men were
travelling on a motorcycle into the capital of Caquetá when Criminal
Investigation Section officers searched their belongings at the location. [@1]
Officers found the marijuana hidden inside a backpack carried by the men. [@1]

The police account describes the seizure as 24 kilograms of marijuana captured
in the south of the country. [@1] The operation was based on investigative work
by Criminal Investigation Section officers in coordination with the National
Army. [@1]

Police presented the operation as part of continued action against hallucinogen
trafficking affecting Colombian families' health, lives, honour and property.
[@1] The men were transferred to the Immediate Reaction Unit of the Prosecutor's
Office to be prosecuted for manufacturing, trafficking and possession of
narcotics. [@1] Investigators found the detainees were repeat offenders with
four prior judicial records for the same drug offence. [@1] Police said the
seizure prevented the sale and consumption of more than 24,000 doses of
narcotics. [@1]
