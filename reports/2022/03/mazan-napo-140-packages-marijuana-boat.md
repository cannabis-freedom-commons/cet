The National Police of Peru, the Peruvian Navy and the Public Ministry carried
out a joint operation and stopped a boat carrying more than 140 brick-type
packages of creepy marijuana. [@1] The stop took place near the Napo River in
Mazán district, Maynas province, in the Loreto region. [@1] Interior Minister
Alfonso Chávarry Estrada led the presentation of the results and said three
people were detained with the illegal cargo. [@1]

Colonel José Rengifo, head of Dirandro Iquitos' anti-drug manoeuvre division,
said the seizure showed coordinated work by the police, the armed forces and the
prosecution. [@1] He said the authorities were allied against the scourges
developing in the Peruvian Amazon and would detect the routes used to move
illegal cargo. [@1] Navy personnel found a second boat with people described as
security for the traffickers. [@1] After an exchange of gunfire those people
fled and abandoned the boat. [@1] No one among the security forces was wounded
or killed. [@1]
