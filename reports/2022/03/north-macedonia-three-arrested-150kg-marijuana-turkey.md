Police in North Macedonia arrested three men suspected of being part of an
international drug-smuggling ring. [@2] Two of the arrested men were Kosovo
citizens and the third a Macedonian national. [@2] The interior ministry said an
Audi A6 with Kumanovo licence plates was seen in a hotel car park carrying two
men from Podujevo in Kosovo and one from Kumanovo; two tried to flee but were
caught, and all three were deprived of their liberty. [@1]

Officers seized about 150 kilograms (330 pounds) of marijuana that was believed
to have been destined for Turkey. [@2] The drugs were found packed in plastic
bags in the car's trunk late on Wednesday, and police put their street value at
half a million euros (about $554,000). [@2] Officers found 92 packages of
marijuana in the back of the car, along with phones, Kosovo and Macedonian phone
cards and money. [@1]

According to police, the route ran from Kosovo through North Macedonia to
Turkey. [@1] A criminal complaint would be filed against the three, and the
person who was to collect the drugs in Štip and take them to Turkey was also
wanted. [@1] Authorities were also hunting a Turkish truck driver whom police
suspected was meant to take over the shipment. [@2]
