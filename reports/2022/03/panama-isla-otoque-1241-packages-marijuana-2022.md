Panama's national aeronaval service (Senan) seized 1,241 packages of suspected
drugs from a fast launch carrying three people south of Isla Otoque, in the
Pacific. [@1] The operation took place on 2 March 2022. [@2] A Senan patrol cut
across the launch's course; the men fled, officers gave chase and the boat and
crew were secured. [@3] The three men surrendered minutes later and were
detained. [@2] An official source told Efe that the three detainees were
Colombian and the substance presumed marijuana. [@1]

The packages were packed into 30 sacks. [@1] The boat, its crew and the load
were taken to the CF DEM Noel A. Rodríguez naval base. [@1] The Drug
Prosecutor's Office (Fiscalía de Drogas) counted the packages there. [@2]

Panama's Pacific coast is a transit route for drugs produced in South America,
with the United States as the main market. [@1] Panama reported seizing 128.7
tonnes of drugs in 2021, a total the authorities called historic. [@1]
