Three men were arrested by the Customs Anti Narcotics Unit and faced drug
trafficking charges after more than 1,500 pounds of marijuana were found in
their possession in the Upper Berbice River. [@1] CANU named them as Massiah
Durant, 52, Shawn Lindie Snr, 44, and Shawn Lindie Jnr, 29. [@1] The three were
arrested during the search of a building in the community of De Veldt, Upper
Berbice River. [@1]

With assistance from the Guyana Defence Force Coast Guard, CANU seized 717
kilogrammes (1,580 pounds) of compressed marijuana. [@2] CANU said the discovery
involved GY$79 million worth of marijuana at a house in De Veldt, Upper Berbice
River. [@2] The agency said the cannabis was believed to have been intended for
transshipment to another territory. [@2]

The trio was charged on 28 March 2022 with trafficking the cannabis. [@4] Durant
pleaded guilty while Shawn Lindie Snr and Shawn Lindie Jnr denied the charge.
[@3] The magistrate sentenced Durant to 50 months in jail and fined him
$215,100,000, and granted each of the Lindies $2 million bail. [@3] In June 2025
the Kwakwani Magistrate's Court dismissed the case against Shawn Lindie Snr and
Shawn Lindie Jnr after the magistrate found reasonable doubt in the
prosecution's evidence. [@4]
