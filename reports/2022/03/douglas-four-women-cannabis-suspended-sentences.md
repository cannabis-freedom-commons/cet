Four women in their 20s were sentenced at Douglas Courthouse for supplying
cannabis on the Isle of Man. [@1] Nadine Wade was 23, Phoebe McKee was 20,
Sophie Harrington was 26 and Beth Davies was 22. [@1] All four had pleaded
guilty to supplying cannabis and possession with intent to supply. [@1] Wade was
sentenced to 12 months, McKee to 10 months, and Harrington and Davies to nine
months each, all suspended for two years. [@1] Two-year supervision orders were
also handed down to Wade, Harrington and Davies. [@1]

Police found cannabis after searching two flats where the women lived in Douglas
on 13 May. [@1] Phone messages showed the four had been supplying cannabis in
early 2021. [@1] At Wade's flat on Loch Promenade, police found cannabis with a
street value of £99 in a Manchester United bowl near the sofa. [@1] An earlier
hearing report said police found 3.3 grams of cannabis in Wade's flat and 4.8
grams in a communal hallway. [@2] A search of the Mona Drive flat where the
other three women lived found more than £2,800 worth of cannabis bush, along
with scales, snap bags and heat-sealed bags with cannabis residue. [@1] At the
Mona Drive address police found 93.8 grams of cannabis, valued at £2,814. [@2]
Wade admitted money laundering after £5,935 in cash was found under her bed,
although the court accepted it was not drug proceeds and she had been holding it
for someone else. [@1]

Messages on their mobile phones showed all four had been involved in arranging
sales of the drug to other people. [@1] Deemster Graeme Cook said cannabis was
"dangerous" and the courts would "not tolerate" its supply. [@1] McKee,
Harrington and Wade had been committed to the Court of General Gaol Delivery for
sentencing after admitting their roles. [@2]
