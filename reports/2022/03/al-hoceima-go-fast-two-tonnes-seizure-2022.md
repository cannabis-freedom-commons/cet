Morocco's Royal Navy coast guard seized about two tonnes of cannabis from a go-fast boat east of Al Hoceima on Friday 11 March 2022. [@1,2] The banned substance was packed in 53 bundles on a boat heading to Spain. [@1,2]

The smugglers abandoned the drugs and fled at high speed when the coast guard units approached, so no arrests were made. [@1,2] The recovered cannabis was transported to the port of Al Hoceima and handed to the Royal Gendarmerie. [@1,2]
