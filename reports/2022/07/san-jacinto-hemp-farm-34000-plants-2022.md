Riverside County's Cannabis Regulation Task Force (CRTF), led by the District Attorney's Bureau of Investigation, served a search warrant on 28 July 2022 at a business in the 18700 block of Bridge Street in the unincorporated area of San Jacinto. [@1,2] The business held a permit as an industrial hemp farm but was not permitted to grow or sell marijuana. [@1,2]

On the property the task force found 124 greenhouses in which marijuana was being cultivated. [@1,2] Officers seized more than 34,000 marijuana plants, removed for destruction, along with more than 4,600 pounds (about 2,086.5 kg) of processed cannabis and a ghost handgun. [@1,2] The next day investigators searched the Temecula home of the farm's permit holder and seized several more pounds of marijuana as evidence. [@1,2]

## No arrests yet

No one had been arrested or charged and the investigation was continuing. [@1,2] The task force, which brings together the District Attorney's investigators with Corona, Hemet and Riverside police, the state Department of Cannabis Control, the California National Guard counter-drug task force and county code enforcement, said it targets criminal activity while leaving legally operating businesses alone. [@1,2]
