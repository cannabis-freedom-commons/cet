Floyd County police arrested three members of one family at their home in the
Shannon community on 7 July 2022. [@1] Officers were in Shannon on follow-up
inspections when they saw Stephen Shuler, 35, riding a dirt bike without a
helmet, and he fled when they tried to stop him. [@1] While preparing a warrant,
officers found a marijuana field, which led to three outdoor growing operations
and one inside a mobile home at 926 Second Street Extension. [@1] Police found
137 marijuana plants in various stages of growth and several firearms on the
property. [@1]

Stephen Shuler was charged with felony drug trafficking, aggravated cruelty to
animals, possessing a firearm during the commission of a felony, possessing a
firearm as a convicted felon, and misdemeanours including obstruction and
driving with a suspended licence. [@1] Darrin Shuler, 56, and Wendy Shuler, 54,
were charged with felony aggravated cruelty to animals, possession of a firearm
during the commission of a crime and possession of marijuana, plus misdemeanour
cruelty to animals. [@1] Stephen Shuler was held without bond on Friday, while
Darrin and Wendy Shuler were held on $15,000 bond each. [@1] Another account
says Stephen Shuler was charged with felony drug charges including manufacturing
and distributing marijuana, firearms violations and several traffic offences.
[@3]

Officers found the remains of 63 chickens in various stages of decay, and a
mother dog and puppies with severe signs of malnutrition; Animal Control took
the dogs, a duck and a chicken. [@1] Floyd County police Sergeant Chris Fincher
said the arrests came from concentrated patrols meant to clean up and revive the
area, after a similar anti-blight operation in Lindale. [@2] The DEA's High
Intensity Drug Trafficking Area task force, the metro drug task force, the HEAT
unit, Rome police's crime suppression team, Georgia State Patrol Aviation and
Floyd County Animal Control took part. [@2]
