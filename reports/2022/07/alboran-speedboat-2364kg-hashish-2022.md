A Customs Surveillance aircraft on routine patrol spotted three rigid-hulled inflatable boats moored together 15 miles south-east of Alborán Island in the early hours of 7 July 2022, with one loaded with burlap and raffia bales of the kind used to move hashish. [@1] The patrol vessels Colimbo II and Fénix III, both based in Motril, were sent to track them. [@1]

Fénix III closed in on the loaded three-engine speedboat, whose crew fled while throwing part of the cargo overboard and ignoring stop signals; the patrol boat then boarded it. [@1] The four crew members, all Moroccan nationals, were arrested with the vessel and its cargo, while Colimbo II recovered the jettisoned bales. [@1]

The seizure totalled 68 bales weighing about 2,364 kilos of hashish resin, which the agency valued at over 15 million euros; the drugs, vessel and detainees were taken to the Motril base. [@1] A Motril duty court remanded the four detainees in custody. [@1]
