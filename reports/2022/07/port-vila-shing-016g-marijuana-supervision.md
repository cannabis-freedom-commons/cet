Yoshua Shing, a Vanuatu table tennis player, was charged with one count of
possession of marijuana. [@1] State Prosecutor Florence Sewen said Shing was
caught on the main road towards Lapetasi Wharf in possession of marijuana
weighing 0.16 grams. [@1] The charge was laid under section 2(62) of the
Dangerous Drugs Act CAP12. [@1]

Shing pleaded guilty to the charge and was convicted accordingly. [@1] His
sentencing was heard on 8 July 2022. [@1] The court considered the mitigation
factors before imposing a six-month supervision order of general standard
condition. [@1] The sentence was six months' supervision on general standard
conditions, not a prison term. [@1]

The case rested on the single possession count. [@1] That count concerned the
0.16 grams of marijuana found on Shing. [@1] He was convicted on his guilty
plea, and the court sentenced him at the hearing on 8 July 2022. [@1]
