Muhammad Hakam bin Suliman pleaded guilty to trafficking 23 blocks and 5 packets containing not less than 499.99 g of cannabis near a coffeeshop at 21 Kian Teck Road on 27 September 2018. [@1,2] Central Narcotics Bureau officers found the drugs in a green car with three of the five people involved in the transactions. [@1]

A second charge of drug possession was taken into consideration for sentencing, and the prosecution dropped a third charge over 6,639.15 g of cannabis mixture. [@1]

On 7 July 2022 the High Court sentenced the 25-year-old to 24 years' imprisonment and 15 strokes of the cane. [@1,2] The judgment records that he had appealed against his sentence. [@1,2]
