Gardaí from the National Drugs and Organised Crime Bureau, assisted by Revenue customs officers and officers from the Eastern Region, searched a business premises at Ballyhale, County Kilkenny, on 11 July 2022. [@1,2]

They recovered cannabis with an estimated street value in excess of €6.9 million. [@1,2] The premises was believed to have been set up as a distribution hub for imported cannabis. [@2]

Two men aged in their 40s and 30s were arrested on suspicion of drug trafficking and detained at Kilkenny station. [@1,2]
