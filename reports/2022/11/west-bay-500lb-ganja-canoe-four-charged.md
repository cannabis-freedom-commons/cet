Shortly after midnight on 5 November 2022, a Cayman Islands Coast Guard crew on
patrol spotted a fishing canoe-type vessel approaching the northern coastline of
West Bay. [@2] The canoe tried to evade the Coast Guard vessel but was soon
stopped and brought under control. [@2] The three men aboard, Jamaican nationals
aged 35, 42 and 57, were arrested on suspicion of importation of ganja. [@2]
Officers searching the nearby shore found a 40-year-old man from West Bay with
several fuel drums. [@2] He was arrested on suspicion of being concerned in the
importation of ganja. [@2]

A search of the vessel found several large packages containing suspected ganja
totalling over 500 lb, about 226.8 kg. [@2] A second account described the find
as several large packages of suspected ganja weighing more than 500 pounds. [@1]
All four men were charged with being concerned with the importation of ganja and
appeared in court on 7 November 2022. [@2] The three Jamaicans were remanded in
custody while the West Bay man was granted bail, with all four due back in court
on 6 December. [@2]

The case was reported as a weekend drug bust in which the Coast Guard stopped
the boat on Saturday and four men were charged. [@1]
