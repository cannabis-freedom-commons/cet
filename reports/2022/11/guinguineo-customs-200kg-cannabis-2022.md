Customs officers of the mobile brigade in Guinguinéo, in Senegal's Kaolack
region, seized four parcels of chanvre indien, the term used in Senegal for
cannabis, estimated at 19 million CFA francs. [@1] The parcels weighed 200
kilograms in total. [@1] Officers found the load at about 2 a.m. on the night of
Saturday 19 to Sunday 20 November 2022. [@2] The cannabis was in a Peugeot 406
coming from the Gambian border. [@1]

At the bridge by the village of Kayemor the driver, boxed in, abandoned the car
and ran off. [@1] An investigation was opened to find the driver and anyone else
involved in the shipment. [@1]
