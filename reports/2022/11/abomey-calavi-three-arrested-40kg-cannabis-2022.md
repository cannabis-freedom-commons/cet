Police républicaine officers arrested three people suspected of trafficking
chanvre indien, the French term for cannabis, on Saturday 19 November 2022 in
the commune of Abomey-Calavi, Benin. [@1] The first suspect was intercepted at
the carrefour Bidossessi after a pursuit, when officers searched the package on
his Bajaj motorcycle and found 15 packages of chanvre indien. [@2] A police
investigation then led to the arrest of two accomplices of the motorcyclist, one
detained in Adjagbo the same day and the third in Agassagodomey. [@1]

Officers seized a total of 40 kg of what police called psychotropic products,
together with accessories used to consume them, in the operation. [@1] The
quantity was reported as about 40 kg of psychotropic products, with consumption
accessories among the items seized. [@2]

All three detainees were referred to the Office Central de Répression du Trafic
illicite des Drogues, or Ocertid, Benin's central office against illicit drug
trafficking. [@1] They were expected to appear in the coming days before the
special prosecutor of the cour de répression des infractions économiques et du
terrorisme, or Criet. [@1] An investigation was opened after the discovery. [@2]
