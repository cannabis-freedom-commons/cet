Officers from Bedfordshire Police's Operation Costello executed a pre-planned
warrant in Ashburnham Road, Bedford, in the early hours of Tuesday 8 November
2022. [@2] The warrant was part of Operation Costello, the force's specialist
unit against organised crime. [@1] Inside, officers found a large cannabis
factory containing around 270 cannabis plants. [@1] Bedfordshire Police put the
plants' street value at around £224,000. [@1] A man in his 30s from Bedford was
subsequently charged with cannabis cultivation. [@1]

Detective Sergeant Tracey Joyce, from Operation Costello, said growing and
selling cannabis is illegal and can be a means of funding other criminal
enterprises. [@1] She said the warrant was intelligence-led and would not have
succeeded without information from the public. [@1] She said illegal drug
activity harms communities and vulnerable people who may be coerced, forced or
exploited into carrying out illegal activities for organised crime gangs. [@1]

Bedfordshire Police said the plants were seized after a warrant targeting
organised crime, and cited Crimestoppers' estimate that 94 per cent of cannabis
farms are in domestic homes. [@1]
