Police in India blamed rats for destroying nearly 200kg (440lb) of cannabis
seized from dealers and kept in police stations. [@1,2,4] Judge Sanjay Chaudhary
said police told the court that 195kg of cannabis had been "destroyed" by rats
in one case. [@1,3,4] The court had asked police to produce the stash as evidence in
cases of drug peddling. [@1,4] The court set the next hearing for 26 November 2022. [@3]

In another case involving 386kg of the drug, police filed a report saying "some"
of the cannabis was "eaten up by the rats". [@1,2,4] The judge cited Mathura police as blaming the rodents for destroying a total of more than 500 kilograms of cannabis seized in various cases and stored at the Shergarh and Highway police stations. [@2] The 386 kg had been seized in May 2020 from a truck stopped near Jatwari village in the Shergarh area. [@3] Judge Chaudhary said some 700kg
of marijuana seized by police was lying in police stations in Mathura district,
"all of it under danger of infestation by rats". [@1,2,4] The judge said police had
no expertise because the rats were "too small", and suggested auctioning the
drugs to research labs and medicine firms with the proceeds going to the
government. [@1,4] The court laid down guidelines for the police to auction or dispose of the cannabis. [@2]

MP Singh, a senior police official of Mathura district, told reporters that some
of the marijuana was "damaged due to heavy rains" and not destroyed by rats.
