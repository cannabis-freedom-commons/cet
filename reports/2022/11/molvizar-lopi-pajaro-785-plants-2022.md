Customs Surveillance officers and Guardia Civil officers dismantled a cannabis
production centre belonging to an international criminal organisation in
Molvízar, Granada, in Operation Lopi/Pájaro. [@1] They seized 785 Cannabis
sativa plants at various stages of growth, 185 of them in flower and 600 not
yet flowering, along with bags of seeds and a blank-firing pistol modified to
fire live rounds. [@1]

The Guardia Civil in Salobreña began investigating the site the previous
October and, after Motril's Court of Instruction No. 4 authorised a search,
officers found the grow inside a house on the outskirts of the town. [@1]
Electricity workers confirmed two illegal connections to the grid feeding the
operation. [@1]

A 21-year-old Albanian man with no criminal record was in the house; he fled
into nearby woods when officers arrived but was caught and arrested for drug
trafficking, membership of a criminal organisation, unlawful possession of
weapons and electricity fraud. [@1] He was remanded in custody while the search
continued for other suspects. [@1]
