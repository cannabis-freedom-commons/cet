The Hawks, assisted by members of the Zeerust K9 unit, arrested three men on the
Wednesday before their Thursday court appearance. [@1] The three men were
identified as Mothusi Ramogerisa, 33, Siphesihle Magagula, 30, and Melvine Gule,
23. [@1] They appeared in the Zeerust Magistrate’s Court on Thursday 15
September 2022. [@1] They were charged with dealing in dagga and contravention
of the Drugs and Drug Trafficking Act. [@1]

Officers acting on information about dagga allegedly being transported from
Swaziland (eSwatini) to Botswana stopped the men's vehicle on Zeerust road just
before the Swartkopfontein port of entry. [@1] A search of the vehicle found
five bags of dagga weighing over 100 kilograms. [@1] The Hawks put the estimated
street value of the dagga at R1.2 million. [@1] Officers seized the vehicle and
the dagga for further investigation. [@1]

The case was postponed to 20 September for a formal bail application. [@1] A
second account confirms three men were arrested with dagga worth R1.2 million
near Swartkopfontein and describes the same alleged Swaziland-to-Botswana
transport. [@2]
