Two women aged 18 and 22 were arrested over their alleged part in transporting
marijuana from south Epi to north Efate by boat. [@1] The marijuana was hidden
in a bag of kava that came with other parcels on the boat. [@2] The two women
collected the kava bag at Emua Wharf and were travelling to Port Vila when
police arrested them. [@2]

The Vanuatu Police Force's media unit supplies the Police Briefs column to the
Vanuatu Daily Post every week. [@2] The column said it is illegal to grow,
harvest, transport and consume marijuana in Vanuatu, and that offenders can be
jailed for years or fined heavily. [@2] It warned that transport owners can be
arrested if they are found carrying the drug. [@2] It said women are
increasingly involved in cultivating, transporting and consuming marijuana, and
that some people turn to it to make an income. [@2] The brief urged people to
consider the effects of cannabis, particularly on mental health. [@2]
