At 22:30 on Thursday 1 September 2022, officers of the border police (police aux
frontières) at Le Perthus, in the Pyrénées-Orientales, checked the load of a
driver who presented himself at the main toll barrier of the crossing. [@1] The
Perthus unit belongs to the police aux frontières, France's border police. [@1]
The man was an Albanian national living in France with an Italian residence
permit. [@1]

He was carrying 20.2 kilograms of cannabis herb, which the officers seized. [@1]

The Alliance Police Nationale 66 union, a police trade union, said the seizure
came as drug trafficking and the number of foreigners in an irregular situation
at France's internal borders were both rising. [@1] Marc Dufresne, speaking for
the union, also pointed to a sense of impunity among drivers who refuse to stop
for police and to an aggravation of violence against officers. [@1] The union
called for an urgent reinforcement of border officers. [@1]
