The U.S. Coast Guard offloaded drugs seized at sea at Coast Guard Base Miami
Beach on Thursday 15 September 2022. [@2] In total the Coast Guard recovered
24,700 pounds of cocaine and 3,892 pounds of marijuana, about 1,765 kilograms of
cannabis. [@1] The Coast Guard valued the whole haul at an estimated $475
million. [@1]

The drugs were interdicted in international waters of the Caribbean Sea and the
Eastern Pacific Ocean. [@2] Two Coast Guard cutters, a U.S. Navy ship and a
Dutch navy vessel made the interceptions, the Coast Guard said. [@1] NBC 6
reported that Coast Guard and partner crews from the Netherlands seized the
24,700 pounds of cocaine and 3,892 pounds of marijuana in the Caribbean Sea.
[@3] Cmdr. Jeremy M. Greenwood, commanding officer of the Legare, said the drugs
seized through the coordinated effort would result in significantly fewer
drug-related overdoses. [@1]

The Legare is a 270-foot Famous-class medium-endurance cutter stationed in
Portsmouth, Virginia. [@2] The Coast Guard said stopping traffickers on the high
seas can require coordination with other agencies and other nations, including
the Netherlands, which has three territories in the Caribbean: Aruba, Curaçao
and Sint Maarten. [@1]
