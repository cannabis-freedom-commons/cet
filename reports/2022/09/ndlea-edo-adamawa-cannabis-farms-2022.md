In a statement on Sunday 25 September 2022, NDLEA spokesman Femi Babafemi said
operatives had stormed cannabis farms at Chigbite and Utese forest in Ovia
North-East Local Government Area and Ekudo forest in Uhunmonde Local Government
Area, both in Edo State. [@1,2] Six cannabis farmers were arrested on the
farms: David Hanson, Ufuoma Progress, Marvelous Armstrong, Marvelous Efe,
Joshua Abubakar and Elijah Abubakar. [@1,2] The agency destroyed over 10
hectares of cannabis plantation and recovered more than 193 kilogrammes of
processed cannabis. [@1,2]

The same statement said a 75-year-old grandfather, Usman Bokina Bajama, also
called Clemen, was arrested in the early hours of Tuesday 20 September at
Anguwan Sate, Mararraban Tola, in Mayo Belwa council area of Adamawa State,
where he kept a cannabis plantation from which 49 kilogrammes were recovered.
[@1,2]

Babafemi said 22 suspects were arrested altogether in operations across seven
states, with 2,536 kilogrammes of cannabis seized and over 10 hectares of
cannabis farms destroyed in Edo and Adamawa. [@2] The operations also took in
1,001,387 bottles and capsules of the banned substances Akuskura and tramadol,
which are not cannabis and are excluded from the figures above. [@1,2]
