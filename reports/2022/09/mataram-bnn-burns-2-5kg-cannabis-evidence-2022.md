Indonesia's National Narcotics Agency (BNN) burned seized cannabis and
methamphetamine evidence on the field by the Yusuf Abu Bakar auditorium at
Universitas Mataram in Mataram, West Nusa Tenggara, on Friday 16 September 2022,
in a ceremony led by BNN head Petrus Reinhard Golose. [@1] Golose said the
evidence was destroyed so that there would be "no lie between us". [@1] The
burning followed a public lecture he gave to Unram students on preventing drug
abuse during a working visit to the province. [@1] BNN said he inaugurated an
incinerator and then destroyed drug evidence from cases cracked by the agency's
West Nusa Tenggara office. [@4]

The cannabis destroyed weighed 2.5 kilograms and was burned in the provincial
BNN office's equipment together with 5 ons of methamphetamine. [@1] Radar NTB
put the destroyed amounts at 2,502.01 grams of cannabis and 519.07 grams of
methamphetamine. [@2] The head of BNN in West Nusa Tenggara, Brigadier General
Gagas Nugraha, said the evidence came from five drug cases. [@1] One cannabis
case involved 505.68 grams and two suspects, whose roles Antara said had been
established. [@1] Radar Lombok reported that 502.01 grams of that batch were
burned, with 2.37 grams held back for laboratory testing and 1.30 grams for
trial. [@3] The other 2 kilograms of cannabis came from a case on Sumbawa
island, in which the suspect was identified by the initials IJ. [@1] Gagas said
that case was still under investigation. [@1]

Radar Lombok instead described the last cannabis batch as an intercepted
parcel-shipping delivery whose owner had not been caught and was still being
traced. [@3]
