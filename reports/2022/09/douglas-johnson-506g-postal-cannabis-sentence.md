Scott Anthony Johnson, 40, was sentenced to 16 months for importing cannabis.
[@1] He pleaded guilty to importation of drugs, attempting to possess the drug
with intent to supply, and being concerned in its supply. [@1] Eight months of
the 16-month term was imposed for breaching his licence while on probation for a
separate offence. [@1]

On February 8, Isle of Man Customs and Excise intercepted a parcel sent to the
island from America. [@1] The parcel was addressed to a Bradley Williams in
Ramsey and held 506.4 grams of cannabis, which police valued at £10,128. [@1]
Police replaced the cannabis with a dummy package and delivered it to the
intended address. [@1] Johnson was the only person present there and, asked if
he was Bradley Williams, answered yeah, yeah. [@1] Police then searched the
property and found the package in a rucksack. [@1] Johnson, who lives in Furman
Close in Douglas, was arrested and gave no-comment answers in interview. [@1]
Examination of his mobile phone found evidence of drug dealing and a tracking
number for the parcel. [@1]

He said he owed £20,000 in drug debt to the dealer who sent it and had been
promised the debt would be wiped out if he accepted delivery. [@1] His advocate
said he had supplied only smaller amounts to friends for medicinal use and to
fund his own use. [@1] His own use was described as self-medication for mental
health, arthritis in both wrists and back pain from a slipped disc. [@1] The
court heard he had previous convictions for importing cannabis and MDMA in 2019
and 2002. [@1] At an earlier hearing Deputy High Bailiff James Brooks declined
summary jurisdiction and committed him to the Court of General Gaol Delivery for
sentencing. [@2]
