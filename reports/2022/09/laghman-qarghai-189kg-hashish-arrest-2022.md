Police in eastern Laghman province arrested a man carrying 189 kilograms of
hashish on 8 September 2022. [@1] Mohammad Yunas Yusufzai, a police spokesman,
said security forces arrested the man, whom he described as a smuggler, along
with the hashish on the Kabul-Nangarhar highway within the limits of Qarghai
district. [@1] The man was arrested on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@2]

Yusufzai said the detainee was a resident of Guldara district in Kabul and
wanted to transfer the hashish from Kabul to Nangarhar. [@1] The smuggler was
being investigated, he added. [@1]
