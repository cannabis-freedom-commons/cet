Niger Inter reported that OCRTIS officers stopped a Peugeot with Nigerian plates
at a police post on the Tahoua road in Abalak on 11 January 2022. [@2] The
vehicle was carrying 36 bricks of cannabis weighing about 35 kg when it was
intercepted travelling from Tahoua to Agadez, with 60,000 naira also found. [@2]
ActuNiger reported that two people aboard the vehicle fled at the checkpoint and
that security forces alerted were actively searching for them. [@1] Niger Inter
identified the driver as Daoui Hassan, 55, of Kontagora in Nigeria, and said he
was detained on suspicion of high-risk international drug trafficking. [@2] He
admitted loading the drugs at Kamba in Birnin Kebbi, Nigeria, and the driver,
the drugs and the vehicle were referred to the Abalak prosecutor. [@2]

The cannabis was carefully hidden in the four doors of a Peugeot 406 carrying
the registration of a neighbouring country, according to ActuNiger. [@1] OCRTIS,
Niger's central office for repressing illicit drug trafficking, found the load
at a barrier check. [@1] Niger Inter said 14 bricks were found in the two front
doors and 22 in the two rear doors, with the naira hidden in the boot. [@2]
ActuNiger said the quantity and market value of the 36 bricks were still being
assessed by the competent services. [@1] Officers also seized foreign currency
alongside the vehicle and its cargo for the purposes of the ongoing
investigation. [@1]

ActuNiger placed the seizure against a rise in drug interdictions in Niger,
which it described as a preferred transit route for traffickers. [@1]
