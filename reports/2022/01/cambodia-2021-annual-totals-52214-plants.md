Law enforcement officers investigated 6,242 drug cases nationwide in 2021 and
arrested 13,765 people. [@1] Those detained included 802 women and 362
foreigners of 13 nationalities, including 45 foreign women. [@2] The National
Authority for Combating Drugs warned that anyone who cultivates marijuana,
processes it for business, or abuses it will be punished under the Anti-Drug
Law. [@1] National Authority for Combating Drugs secretary-general Meas Vyrith
said cultivating or processing marijuana for business or for export was strictly
prohibited. [@1] He said growing two or three marijuana plants at home was not
illegal, but growing large quantities as a drug causing addiction or substance
abuse was an offence. [@1]

Authorities seized more than 4,435 kilogrammes of all drugs in 2021. [@1] They
destroyed more than 52,000 marijuana plants and reported more than 141
kilogrammes of dried marijuana. [@1] A second account of the same 2021 totals
listed 52,214 dried marijuana plants and 228.51 grammes of marijuana within 4
tonnes and 435 kg seized. [@2]

Vyrith said Cambodia would not allow marijuana cultivation even for medicinal
purposes as neighbouring countries do. [@1]
