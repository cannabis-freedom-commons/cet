Police in the Planters district of St Catherine, Jamaica, found 100 packets of
compressed ganja hidden in a parked car during an operation on 17 January 2022.
[@1] The ganja weighed 935 pounds, about 424 kilograms. [@1] No one was arrested
and the car and ganja stayed in police custody. [@2] HaitiLibre put the market
value of the ganja at J$4.5 million, a little under US$50,000. [@1]

Superintendent Hopton Nicholson, acting divisional commander for St Catherine
South, said the shipment was to be delivered to Haiti as part of the illegal
drugs-for-guns trade between groups in the two countries. [@1] He said it was
not the first ganja shipment seized bound for Haiti in that trade. [@1] The
Jamaica Observer reported that St Catherine South police were conducting
operations in the district when they spotted a motor car along a roadway after
2:00 pm. [@2]

Nicholson called the seizure another dent in the illicit drugs-for-guns trade
between Jamaica and Haiti and said his division would keep pursuing those
involved and the spaces used. [@2] Jamaica is the Caribbean's leading producer
of ganja, and traffickers also export the drug to other countries in the region
in exchange for illegal firearms. [@1]
