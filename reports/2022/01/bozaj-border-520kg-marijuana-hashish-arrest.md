Customs officers and police at the Božaj border crossing seized about 500 kg of
marijuana and 20 kg of hashish on the morning of 15 January 2022. [@3] They
arrested a 38-year-old Albanian citizen, identified only as P.Č., on suspicion
of unauthorised production, possession and distribution of narcotics. [@3] The
truck had Albanian licence plates. [@2] It was heading from Albania through
Montenegro to Germany. [@2] Its load was declared as medicinal herbs. [@2]

The truck arrived at the crossing the previous evening, and a police dog trained
to detect narcotics marked the load. [@2] In the early morning, officers checked
the cargo and found 80 sacks of marijuana and hashish weighing about 520 kg in
total. [@4] Albania's State Police said the truck had previously passed through
the Hani i Hotit crossing in Shkodra. [@1]

The Montenegrin police and customs administration said the seizure foiled
another attempt to smuggle narcotics. [@3] Albanian police said an investigative
team was continuing procedural actions in Albania in coordination with the
Montenegrin side. [@1]
