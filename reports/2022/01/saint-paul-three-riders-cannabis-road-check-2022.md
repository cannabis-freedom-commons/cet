On the afternoon of Thursday 13 January 2022, motorcyclists from the Saint-Paul
motorised brigade carried out a road check of three young riders on 125 cm³
motorcycles in the Saint-Paul urban area. [@1] Document checks showed that all
three riders lacked the A1 licence required for light motorcycles. [@1] One of
the three riders was a minor who also had no insurance cover for his motorcycle.
[@1] During the check, the officers noticed apathetic behaviour among the
riders. [@1] Officers therefore carried out a roadside saliva drug screening
test on each of the three riders. [@1]

The screening tests revealed the presence of cannabis in two of the riders,
including the young minor. [@1] Given the offences identified during the check,
officers immediately placed all three motorcycles in the pound. [@1] The riders
were told they would have to answer for these combined road and drug offences
before the Saint-Denis Judicial Court. [@1]

The gendarmerie added that 16 two-wheel riders had died during 2021 on the
island roads in its sector and urged riders to stay careful on the roads. [@1]
