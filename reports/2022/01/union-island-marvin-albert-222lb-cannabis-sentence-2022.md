Marvin Albert, a 25-year-old Grenadian farmer, was caught on Union Island in a
Rapid Response Unit operation on the night of 14 January 2022 and the morning of
15 January. [@1] Officers went to a waterfront area in Ashton, where the smell
of cannabis led one of them to six nylon sacks and a bag. [@1] Albert told the
officers the cannabis was his to take to Grenada. [@1] The coastguard carried
Albert and the drugs from Union Island to the mainland. [@1] He admitted
possessing 100,788 g (222 pounds) of cannabis with intent to supply and
possessing it for the purpose of drug trafficking. [@1]

Chief Magistrate Rechanne Browne sentenced him on 19 January 2022 to one year
and three months for trafficking, and one year and one month for possession, to
run concurrently. [@1] The Chief Magistrate began at 35% of the seven-year
maximum sentence available to her. [@1] The mitigating factors outweighed the
aggravating ones, and nine months were taken off the sentence. [@1] Albert was
given three months on each immigration charge, all to run concurrently. [@1] The
court ordered the marijuana destroyed and Albert removed from the country when
his prison term ends. [@1]

Defence lawyer Roderick Jones said Albert was the eldest child and had been the
breadwinner for his family. [@1] Jones said Albert decided to try to sell
marijuana after his mother told him she and his siblings were homeless. [@1]
Jones said the country licenses medical marijuana exports while jailing poor
people found with the drug. [@1] Prosecutor Sergeant Renrick Cato stressed that
the amount was 222 pounds, not 22 pounds. [@1] Cato asked the court for a
sentence reflecting the gravity of the offence. [@1]

Albert was charged alongside Vincentian Michael Scott, who pleaded not guilty to
drug possession and trafficking charges. [@1] Scott was granted $50,000 bail and
his case was transferred to the Biabou Magistrate's Court for 21 January 2022.
