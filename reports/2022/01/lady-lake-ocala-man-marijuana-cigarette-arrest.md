Lady Lake police arrested a native of Jamaica after a brief chase in the town,
saying he was in possession of marijuana. [@1] The man arrested was Andre Al
Kerk Salter, 34, of Ocala. [@1] The Lady Lake Police Department recorded the
stop in an arrest report. [@1]

An officer caught him on radar travelling at 60 miles per hour in a 45 mph zone
on U.S. Hwy. 27/441. [@1] The traffic stop was made at 3:20 a.m. on Saturday 15
January 2022. [@1] At County Road 25 and Griffin Avenue, an officer detected the
odor of marijuana coming from Salter's vehicle. [@1] A marijuana cigarette was
found between the driver's seat and center console. [@1]

Salter was arrested on charges of fleeing to elude law enforcement and
possession of marijuana. [@1] He was booked at the Lake County Jail and released
after posting $3,000 bond. [@1]
