Samoa Police, Prisons and Corrections Services arrested two people in a raid on
a property at Faatoia, near Apia, on Thursday 6 January 2022. [@2] The two were
held in police custody awaiting remand. [@4] Talamua Online reported that
officers arrested a man and a woman who were at the suspected dealer's place
when they arrived. [@3] Police said the man they describe as one of the
country's top drug dealers evaded the raid on his home. [@3] The acting
commissioner said the place had been raided at least three times before and the
dealer was on the police wanted list. [@3] The woman was known to police and had
served time in prison on drug charges. [@3] More than 20 police officers took
part in the raid. [@3]

Police said they seized marijuana leaves weighing 0.57 grams. [@1] Talamua
described four utensils seized as used for consuming methamphetamine. [@3] The
seizure also included 5,000 tala in cash, four sildenafil 100 mg tablets sold as
Viagra and an electronic scale. [@2] Officers also took two firearms, 22
bullets, two fake number plates reading Regulate and 2PAC, four flash drives and
three hard drives. [@2] The two detainees were held on possession of narcotics
charges. [@3]

The acting commissioner said police had been monitoring Nuu, Vaitele, Alamagoto
and Faatoia for class drugs. [@3] He said police did not want Apia to become a
drug city and would keep raiding on verified information. [@3] Police were
investigating whether the fake number plates had been issued by the Land and
Transport Authority or made elsewhere. [@3]
