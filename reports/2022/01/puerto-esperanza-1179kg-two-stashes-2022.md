Gendarmería Nacional officers of Escuadrón 10 Eldorado found two marijuana
stashes hidden in vegetation in rural Puerto Esperanza, Misiones, while
patrolling dirt roads on the morning of 3 January 2022. [@1] At Paraje Istueta
the officers followed those recent vehicle tracks, combed the area and found 19
bundles holding 586 rectangular packages of cannabis sativa weighing 262 kilos
970 grams. [@1] Primera Edición reported that the Esperanza and Núcleo sections
and the GOIP group of the same squadron carried out the patrol. [@2] The 586
packages were counted in front of witnesses. [@2] Narcotest field tests on them
were positive for cannabis sativa. [@2] Two hours later, near Paraje 5 Arroyos
by National Route 12, the same unit followed more vehicle tracks on foot and
found two similar stashes. [@1] Those stashes held 79 bundles with 916 kilos 328
grams of marijuana packed in 1,616 bricks, the agency said. [@1] The two
procedures together yielded 1,179 kilos 298 grams of marijuana. [@1]

The Federal Court and the Federal Prosecutor's Office in Eldorado intervened in
both procedures and ordered all the drug seized. [@1] Primera Edición reported
that the drug was kept at the Escuadrón 10 Eldorado base. [@2] The Misiones
outlet placed the second search at midday. [@2] Officers found a first stash in
the vegetation and an identical second one about 10 metres away. [@2]
