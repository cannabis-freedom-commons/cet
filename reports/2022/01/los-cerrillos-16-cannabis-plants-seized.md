Officers from Seccional 3 in Los Cerrillos, a police station in the Canelones
department of Uruguay, went to a house at Ramón López and Juan de Dios Cabrera
after a complaint of domestic violence. [@1] The officers detected several
marijuana plants at the address while responding to that report. [@1] In the
first stage of the operation they detained the man accused of the violence, a
33-year-old who was assaulting his partner, a 19-year-old woman. [@1] The man
was taken to a police station after the detention. [@1]

In the operation police seized 16 marijuana plants from the house. [@1] The
house where the plants were found is in Los Cerrillos, in the department of
Canelones. [@1]

The procedure is in the charge of the Primer Turno prosecutor's office in
Canelones. [@1]
