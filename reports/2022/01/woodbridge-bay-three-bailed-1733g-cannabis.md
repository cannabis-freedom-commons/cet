Police charged Jerome Felix of Mahaut and Tyler Matthew and Jaylon Matthew of La
Plaine with importing cannabis, and all three pleaded not guilty. [@1] The three
appeared before the Roseau Magistrate's Court arrested and charged with
importation of drugs, namely cannabis. [@1]

It is alleged that on 7 January 2022, at Woodbridge Bay Port in Fond Cole, Felix
imported 684.8 grams of cannabis into Dominica. [@1] Tyler and Jaylon, who were
jointly charged, were accused of importing 1,048.3 grams of cannabis into
Dominica the same day. [@1] Nature Isle News said Tyler Matthew and her brother
Jaylon were arrested at Woodbridge Bay Port that day for importing 1,048 grams
of cannabis. [@2] Nature Isle News said the drugs were discovered in a barrel.
[@2] Nature Isle News also reported that police arrested and charged Jerome
Felix of Mahaut on 7 January 2022 for importing 684.8 grams of cannabis at the
same port. [@2]

At the court hearing on 10 January 2022, acting Director of Public Prosecutions
Sherma Dalrymple did not object to bail, but Magistrate Michael Laudat adjourned
the bail application to 11 January 2022. [@1] The prosecutor then obtained an
order placing the defendants in the custody of the investigating officers until
the bail hearing. [@1] At the bail hearing, Magistrate Laudat set Felix's bail
at $15,000 with one surety and Tyler and Jaylon's bail at $20,000 with two
sureties. [@1] Bail was granted, the three were released, and they were expected
to return to court on 4 April 2022 for trial. [@1] Felix was represented by
attorney Wayne Norde, while Tyler and Jaylon were represented by Cara
Shillingford-Marsh and Kayla Jean Jacques. [@1] Nature Isle News said Felix was
granted bail of $15,000 with one surety and no reporting conditions. [@2] Nature
Isle News said the matter was adjourned to 4 April 2022. [@2]
