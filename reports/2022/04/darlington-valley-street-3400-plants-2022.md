Officers were called to reports of a grow at an address on Valley Street in Darlington at about 2.15pm on Monday 25 April 2022. [@1,2] Inside the unused office building they found about 3,400 cannabis plants spread over two floors. [@1] The find was described as thought to be the largest grow ever found in County Durham and Darlington. [@1,2]

Four men aged 22, 25, 27 and 27 were arrested at the scene on suspicion of cultivation of cannabis. [@1] They were later charged and were due to appear at Newton Aycliffe Magistrates' Court on 27 April. [@1]
