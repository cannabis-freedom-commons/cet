Anil Kumar Pandi is the main supplier of ganja from Ganjam. [@1] He is
absconding. [@1] A court has issued a non-bailable warrant against him. [@1] The
trafficker is involved in at least 12 narcotics cases in Gujarat and Odisha.
[@1]

On 7 April 2022 the Competent Authority in Kolkata ordered forfeiture of
property worth Rs 2.35 crores in STF case 29/2021. [@2] The forfeited properties
include a palatial three-storey building with a swimming pool, 33 plots and bank
deposits. [@2] In the same STF case officers had seized around 10 quintals of
ganja on 13 August 2021. [@2] All the properties were bought from
drug-trafficking money within the last six years. [@2]

The action was taken under Chapter VA of the NDPS Act, which allows seizure and
forfeiture of property acquired from narcotics business in the last six years.
[@2] The Competent Authority in Kolkata is the quasi-judicial authority that
passes forfeiture orders for such property. [@2] Gujarat Police worked jointly
with Odisha Police's Crime Branch Special Task Force in the seizure. [@1]
Regular ganja supply from Odisha takes place by rail and other modes. [@1]
