The Caldwell County Sheriff’s Department reported that a man was taken into
custody after a short pursuit on 5 April following the discovery of an illegal
homegrown marijuana operation. [@1] The man was identified as 42-year-old Jamal
Ghazai of Lathrop. [@1] Ghazai was charged with felony possession of a
controlled substance and felony manufacture of 35 grams or less of marijuana or
synthetic cannabinoid. [@1] He was also charged with misdemeanor unlawful
possession of drug paraphernalia involving a prior drug offense. [@1] Bond was
set at $25,000 cash only. [@1] Ghazai made an initial court appearance on 7
April and was next scheduled for a bond hearing on 14 April. [@1]

Deputies had executed a search warrant at a residence in the southwest part of
Caldwell County and seized the marijuana operation, which led to a warrant being
issued for Ghazai. [@1] The pursuit that ended in Clay County was carried out by
deputies of Caldwell and Clinton counties. [@1]
