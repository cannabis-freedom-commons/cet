Border police in Mayotte intercepted a kwassa-kwassa, a small open boat used for
crossings from the Comoros, off the south coast of the island on Monday 4 April
2022. [@1] Six passengers and a Comorian passeur, the boatman who brings
migrants across, were on board. [@2] The passeur was prosecuted for aggravated
assistance to irregular entry, irregular entry, smuggling manufactured tobacco
and unauthorised transport and possession of narcotics. [@1] The six passengers
were held at the administrative detention centre pending removal to the Comoros.
[@1]

Officers seized six bales of locally grown cannabis herb, called bangué in
Mayotte, weighing 101.06 kg in total. [@2] The boat also carried 54 packages
holding 2,575 cartons of Le Coelacanthe cigarettes. [@1] Clicanoo estimated that
cigarette consignment at nearly 180,000 euros, at 75 euros a carton. [@1] The
cigarettes and the cannabis were handed to the customs service in Pamandzi to be
destroyed. [@2]

The national police in Mayotte said seizures of goods on kwassa-kwassa boats had
risen since the start of 2022. [@1] By early April they counted 8,431 cartons of
Comorian cigarettes seized, valued at 590,170 euros in customs duty. [@1] They
had also seized 308.52 kg of bangué over the same period. [@2]

The boat was stopped about two kilometres off the village of M'Bouini, in the
commune of Kani-Kéli in the south of Mayotte. [@3]
