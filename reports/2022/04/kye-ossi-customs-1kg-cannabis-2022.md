Cameroon customs seized one kilogram of cannabis at Kyé-Ossi on 5 April 2022,
according to the service's own announcement. [@1] The same customs statement
said the operation also recovered 10,250 tablets of sildenafil, known as Viagra,
which are not counted as cannabis. [@1] Cameroon Customs posted the same figures
on its official account, naming the date and the South region. [@3] The seizure
was made at Kyé-Ossi, where Cameroon, Gabon and Equatorial Guinea share a
border. [@1]

Journal du Cameroun reported that the intercepted cargo also contained one
kilogram of cannabis. [@2] The customs service announced the seizure in a short
message. [@1] The customs service did not reveal the identity of the person who
had tried to import the sildenafil tablets. [@1] Journal du Cameroun said no
origin or destination was given for the goods and the holders of the cargo were
not named. [@2]

Seizures of smuggled medicines are common in Cameroon. [@1] Some buyers obtain
sildenafil on the black market or on the street. [@1]
