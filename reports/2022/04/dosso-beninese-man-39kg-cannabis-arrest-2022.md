Nigerien police detained a man of Beninese nationality in the Dosso region with
45 bricks of cannabis weighing 39.78 kg. [@1] The operation was carried out on
25 April 2022 in the Dosso region by the regional branch of the Office Central
de Répression du Trafic Illicite des Stupéfiants (OCRTIS), Niger's anti-drug
office. [@1] Police described the detained man as an alleged drug trafficker.
[@1]

Nigerien police presented the suspect and the seized cannabis to the local press
on Saturday 30 April 2022. [@2] A second account, citing a statement by the
Direction de la police nigérienne, said the suspect was arrested with 45 bricks
of cannabis totalling 39.78 kg. [@2] A later Bénin Web TV account, attributing
the information to Triomphe Mag, also placed the arrest on 25 April 2022 and the
presentation to the local press on Saturday 30 April. [@3]

At a press conference of Niger's national police, the new governor of the Dosso
region, Albachir Aboubacar, welcomed the OCRTIS officers' success and encouraged
their professionalism in the fight against drug trafficking. [@1]
