Police arrested a female bartender from Whitehouse in Westmoreland in a
secondary operation after she was allegedly held with a large quantity of ganja.
[@2] She was charged with several breaches of the Dangerous Drugs Act. [@2]
Other operations linked to the case were also conducted in St Elizabeth,
according to the Narcotics Police Division. [@2]

On Tuesday, a pre-dawn operation in Coral Gardens, St James led to the
interception of a vehicle in which cocaine weighing 11 kilograms was found. [@1]
Police said a quantity of ganja was also seized during the search for a man
linked to the find. [@1] Area One Police were combing sections of Westmoreland
and St Elizabeth for a man believed linked to the St James drug and money find.
[@1]

Police said the man sought was the subject of an extradition request to the
United States made 17 years earlier. [@1] The person sought reportedly escaped
extradition to the US some years after the request on a technicality. [@1] The
drug seizure led officers to the dwelling where cash was found and to
Westmoreland, where the woman was arrested. [@2]
