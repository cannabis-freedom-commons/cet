Cameroon's national gendarmerie arrested two suspects, the owner of the cargo
and its supplier. [@1] The West Region gendarmerie legion carried out the
operation and the arrests. [@2] Examining the bus led officers to the supplier
and the owner of the chanvre indien. [@2] The suspected traffickers remained
under investigation and were to be presented to the courts. [@2]

Officers seized 25 sacks of narcotics. [@1] The sacks contained chanvre indien
(Indian hemp), 13 packaged and 12 hidden in travel bags. [@1] Actu Cameroun
reported the same count of 13 packaged sacks and 12 concealed in travel bags.
[@2] The gendarmerie put the cargo at 450 kg, worth several million CFA francs,
and said it came from the North-West Region bound for Yaoundé and neighbouring
countries including Chad, Libya and Sudan. [@2] A search of the supplier's home
in Koutaba uncovered 11 more sacks ready for distribution. [@3]

The bus was intercepted on the night of 11–12 December 2022 while running from
Koutaba to Yaoundé. [@2] The seizure was reported at Bafoussam, the main city of
Cameroon's West Region. [@1]
