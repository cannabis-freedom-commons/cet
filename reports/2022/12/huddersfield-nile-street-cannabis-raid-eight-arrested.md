Eight men were arrested and thousands of cannabis plants were found during an
armed police raid in Huddersfield. [@1] West Yorkshire Police said officers had
been at a former commercial premises on Nile Street since 09:00 GMT, with the
operation ongoing. [@1] Nearby Kirklees College said it was placed in lockdown
during the large-scale police search. [@1] St Thomas' Road, near Nile Street,
was closed off by officers so investigations could continue. [@1]

The force said a large number of officers, including armed police, attended
because of the size of the premises. [@1] An underwater search team was also
sent to the scene because of the site's proximity to the River Colne, police
said. [@1] The eight arrested men were in police custody and drugs equipment was
found during the searches, the force said. [@1] Det Ch Insp Ian Thornes of
Kirklees Police said the operation had resulted in the seizure of significant
numbers of cannabis plants. [@1] He said counting the plants would take time,
but thousands were believed to be in the facility. [@1]

Kirklees College said it locked down as a precaution to safeguard its students
and staff. [@1]
