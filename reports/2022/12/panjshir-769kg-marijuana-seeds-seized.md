Afghan counternarcotics forces recovered 769 kilograms of marijuana seeds from a
small truck in Parakh, Panjshir province, and arrested its driver. [@1] Mawlawi
Nasrullah Malikzada, the head of Panjshir's Information and Culture department,
told Pajhwok Afghan News that the seeds were recovered from the truck. [@1] The
seeds were recovered in Parakh, which the provincial official described as
Panjshir's provincial capital. [@1]

Xinhua, citing police, reported the same operation as the seizure of 769 kg of
hemp and the arrest of one man in Panjshir province. [@2] A Bakhtar news agency
report recorded on 14 December said Panjshir's counter-narcotics chief reported
the seizure of 769 kilograms of narcotics. [@3] That account named Bazarak, the
provincial capital, as the place where the narcotics were seized and Hormatullah
as the department head. [@3]

Xinhua reported that the Taliban-run administration had banned poppy cultivation
and vowed to crack down on illicit drugs and their trafficking. [@2]
