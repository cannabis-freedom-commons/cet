Jefferson County drug investigators raided a South Fourth Street property in
Steubenville and described the haul as a grocery store of illicit substances
laid out on tables with prices and a ledger. [@1] Columbus man Louis D. Jordan,
43, was arrested on the Friday before the 6 December 2022 publication without
incident. [@1] Jordan was held at the Jefferson County Justice Center on no bond
facing a major offender status charge for cocaine and a felony charge for
methamphetamine and weapons under disability. [@1]

The task force, assisted by Steubenville police and K-9 Puma, reported seizing a
wide variety of marijuana and marijuana extract alongside a large amount of
United States currency. [@1] The team also seized a very large quantity of
marijuana and marijuana-related products, much of it packaged to appeal to
children. [@1] Some marijuana was repackaged into cartoons and gummies to look
like light sabers in packaging officers described as aimed at children. [@1] In
the same raid officers also reported 423 grams of suspected cocaine, 224 grams
of suspected methamphetamine, 800 suspected ecstasy tablets, 50 suspected
fentanyl pills, two guns and a bulletproof vest. [@1] Officers had so far
counted 14,000 dollars in seized cash, with the total expected to rise. [@1]

The drugs were laid out on tables with pricing and a ledger book recording what
each item was. [@1] The room was basically set up like a storeroom with drugs
laid out by type and price, which investigators said shocked them. [@1] The
search followed reliable information investigators received and followed up,
under a search warrant obtained on that information and investigation. [@1]
Additional charges were described as likely. [@1]
