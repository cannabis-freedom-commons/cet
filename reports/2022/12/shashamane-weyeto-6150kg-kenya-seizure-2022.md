Police in Ethiopia's southern regional state seized 6,150 kilograms of cannabis that
was being smuggled to Kenya, local authorities disclosed. [@1,2] The drug was hidden
inside the trunk of a cargo truck and was seized in Weyeto in the South Omo zone on a
Monday afternoon, in an operation by Southern region special forces personnel. [@1,2]
Police verified that the load had originated in Shashamane, about 250 kilometres
southeast of Addis Ababa. [@1,2] The cargo truck driver and his assistant were arrested
for further investigation. [@1,2]
