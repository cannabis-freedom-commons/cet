Police destroyed illegal cannabis cultivation on 46 bigha and 16 katta of land
in Raksirang and Kailash rural municipalities of Makwanpur district. [@1] The
Makwanpur district police office said officers also destroyed an opium
plantation on three bigha and five katta there. [@1]

Joint police teams destroyed the marijuana and opium over four consecutive days.
[@1] Eighty-three officers from the Narcotics Control Bureau at police
headquarters in Koteshwar took part, with 38 from the Makwanpur district police
office and 36 from the Manhari office. [@1]

Locals said cultivation has not stopped even though police clear plantations
every year. [@1] Superintendent of Police Vamdev Gautam, who heads the Makwanpur
district police office, said the cultivation that has gone on in the district
for many years would be destroyed. [@1] Police said the area's difficult terrain
and lack of transport keep the administration from reaching it, which lets
cultivation continue there. [@1]
