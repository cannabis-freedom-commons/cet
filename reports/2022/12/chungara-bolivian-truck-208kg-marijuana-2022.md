Chilean Customs officers and Carabineros from OS7 intercepted a truck at the
Chungará border complex and found 208.767 kg of Creepy-type marijuana in the
early hours of 27 December 2022. [@2] The truck was entering Chile without cargo
and was first subjected to a documentary review and then a physical inspection.
[@2] Customs officers found anomalies in its structure and moved it to port
facilities, where a scanner truck and a drug-detecting dog revealed the drug in
the walls of the trailer. [@2] Officers found 185 packages of high-quality
Creepy-type marijuana and seized the tractor-trailer. [@2]

The driver, a Bolivian national, was detained for formalization on
drug-trafficking charges. [@2] He was placed in preventive detention with 90
days for the investigation. [@3] Presidential delegate Ricardo Sanzana said the
operation had removed more than 200 kg of marijuana from circulation. [@2] The
acting regional director of the Arica customs, James Alarcón Contreras, said the
detection followed risk profiling and new modus operandi. [@2]

The Chungará border complex lies 202 km east of Arica. [@1]
