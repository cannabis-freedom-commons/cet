Peru's National Police and the Public Prosecutor's Office are investigating five
police officers accused of taking 15 kilograms of marijuana that anti-drug
police had seized in an operation. [@1] The anti-drug operation took place on 15
June 2022, when officers seized 30 kilograms of marijuana from Juan Echevarría.
[@1] Officers opened the sack in the man's car and found dried herbs with the
characteristic smell of cannabis sativa marijuana. [@1] Colonel César
Echevarría, a spokesman for the anti-drug directorate, said the investigation
began after a series of irregularities were reported in the seizure process.
[@1] He said the intent was to illegitimately take half of the drug from the
original provincial intervention. [@1]

The marijuana was presented at a press conference, and 15 kilograms of it were
allegedly taken by five officers of the intelligence team of the Criminal
Investigation Department in Breña, a district of Lima. [@1] The five detained
officers were named as sub-officers Elmer Pizarro Bazán, Raúl Fabián Gómez, José
Dueñas Ruiz, Jesús Roldán Torre and Jairo Huamán Bautista. [@1] Anti-drug
officers, with the support of the police Inspector General, searched the car of
one of the officers and found the 15 kilograms in the boot. [@1]

Investigators also found that the five sub-officers had allegedly tried to
extort the detained Juan Echevarría, whose marijuana had been seized in the
first place. [@1] The five were in the hands of the prosecutor's office, which
with the judiciary was to decide their legal situation. [@1]
