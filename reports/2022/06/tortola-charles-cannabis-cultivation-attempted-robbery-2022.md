Maurice Charles, a man from Old Plantation in East End on Tortola, was arrested
and charged in June 2022 with two counts of attempted robbery and one count of
cultivating cannabis. [@1] He was arrested in the week the charges were
reported, BVI News said. [@1] Police executed a warrant at his home that week
and reportedly found what appeared to be a black firearm and two pots of plants
suspected to be marijuana, according to sources the outlet did not name. [@1]

The alleged supermarket robbery happened late the month before the charges, in
May 2022. [@1] The attempted robbery charges concern an incident at Fine Food
Supermarkets in Fat Hogs Bay, where residents were allegedly held at gunpoint.
[@1] A masked assailant said to have a distinct Vincentian accent approached
people socialising in front of the supermarket. [@1] The gunman tried to rob
them and some of the would-be victims escaped, the report said. [@1] Police took
into custody a man who fitted the description of the armed assailant. [@1]
