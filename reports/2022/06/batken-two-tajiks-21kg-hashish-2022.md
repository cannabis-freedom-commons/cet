Kyrgyz police arrested two Tajik citizens suspected of drug smuggling and
distribution in Kyrgyzstan, the interior ministry's press service said. [@1] The
ministry said the detained group of Tajik citizens included an officer of
neighbouring Tajikistan's interior ministry. [@2] The ministry named one
detainee as I. M., born in 1982, a senior sergeant and inspector in the Tajik
interior ministry, and the other as O. A., born in 1960, described as the
group's organiser. [@2] Azattyk reported that the two were detained in Batken
region, in the south of Kyrgyzstan. [@3] The operation was run by the Kyrgyz
interior ministry's drug control service, SBNON, and the ministry said it had
lasted about a year. [@3]

Officers seized 33 kg 241 g of drugs of Afghan origin, of which 21 kg 942 g was
hashish and 11 kg 299 g opium, the ministry said; only the hashish is counted in
the cannabis figures here. [@2] According to the ministry, the drugs were being
moved from Tajikistan through Kyrgyzstan and on to Kazakhstan. [@2] The
detainees planned to bring 11 kg of opium and 21 kg of hashish into Kyrgyzstan
and then send them to Kazakhstan, the ministry said. [@1]

Police opened a criminal case under article 282 of the Kyrgyz criminal code, on
the illegal manufacture of narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances and their
analogues for sale. [@1] Both detainees were placed in the SIZO-1 pre-trial
detention centre in Bishkek and the investigation continued. [@1] The ministry
said police had reached the Tajik, Kyrgyz and Kazakh cells of the criminal group
and identified its leader, and were still looking for other people involved.
[@2] Tajikistan's interior ministry told RFE/RL's Kyrgyz service on 15 June that
it had heard nothing of the case and could neither confirm nor deny it. [@4]
Azattyk reported that this was the first case in which an officer of a
neighbouring country's law enforcement agencies was among those detained. [@3]
