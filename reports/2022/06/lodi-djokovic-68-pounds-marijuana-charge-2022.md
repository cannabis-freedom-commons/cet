A 37-year-old restaurant owner from Michigan was stopped and arrested in Lodi,
New Jersey, on 28 June 2022, Bergen County Prosecutor Mark Musella said. [@1]
Musella said an investigation had found Djokovic was transporting bulk shipments
of marijuana through Bergen County. [@2]

A search of his vehicle found 68 pounds of marijuana, police said. [@2] Djokovic
was charged with one count of first-degree possession of marijuana over 25
pounds with the intent to distribute. [@2] He remained held in the Bergen County
Jail the following day, awaiting a first appearance in Central Judicial
Processing Court in Hackensack. [@1]

Djokovic, a native Albanian, entered the United States illegally more than two
decades before the arrest, the Daily Voice reported. [@1] The account said his
family brought him out of the former Yugoslavia in 1999, when he was 14. [@1] He
reached the United States through Canada, where smugglers he met helped him
cross, according to court papers cited in the account. [@1] After a long legal
battle with immigration officials, he settled in Washington Township, Michigan.
