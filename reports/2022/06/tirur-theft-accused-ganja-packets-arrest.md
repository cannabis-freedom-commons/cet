Tirur police arrested Parambil Sirajudheen, a 38-year-old native of Kodakkal in
Tirunnavaya who was described as an accused in numerous theft cases. [@1] Before
this arrest, Sirajudheen had already spent time in jail after being implicated
in about ten separate cases registered at different police stations across
Wayanad, Kozhikode and Malappuram districts. [@1] The three districts named for
those earlier cases are Wayanad, Kozhikode and Malappuram. [@1]

The arrest was carried out by a Tirur police team led by station SHO Jijo. [@1]
The team also included Tirur Sub-Inspector Jaleel Karuthedath, Grade
Sub-Inspector Biju Paul and civil police officers Biji and Dhanesh. [@1]
Officers caught the accused around midnight on the previous day on the premises
of the Tirur bus stand. [@1] At the time of the bus-stand arrest, he had packets
of ganja in his hand that had been kept for the purpose of sale. [@1]

After the arrest, the police officers produced Sirajudheen before the Tirur
court, which then ordered his remand. [@1]
