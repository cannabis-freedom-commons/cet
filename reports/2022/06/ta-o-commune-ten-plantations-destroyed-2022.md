The Takeo provincial gendarmerie cracked down on ten marijuana plantations at
Sla village in Ta O commune, Kirivong district, on 23 June 2022, cutting and
destroying the crop. [@1,2] The operation was led by deputy provincial
governor Khan Sokha with coordination from Takeo deputy provincial prosecutor
Nhean Borey. [@1,2]

Law enforcement burned the marijuana crops in the fields. [@1,2] Nobody was
reported arrested; the gendarmerie said investigators were working to identify
the plantation owners and those supplying marijuana seeds so they could be
arrested and brought to justice. [@1,2]
