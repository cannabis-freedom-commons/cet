Zambia's Drug Enforcement Commission arrested three people in Gwembe District of
Southern Province for trafficking in loose cannabis and unlawfully cultivating
fresh cannabis plants. [@1] Gestro Singandu, a 77-year-old man of Chiwangabala
Village in Gwembe District, was arrested for trafficking in 30 kilograms of
loose cannabis and the unlawful cultivation of 1.33 tons of fresh cannabis
plants. [@2] Kenny Sianyanga, 34, of the same village, was arrested for
trafficking in 50.2 kilograms of loose cannabis and the unlawful cultivation of
1.49 tons of fresh cannabis plants. [@2] In a separate incident, Sarafina
Siakumbi, a 45-year-old woman of Siamududa Village in Gwembe District, was
arrested for trafficking in 35 kilograms of loose cannabis and cultivating 2.68
tons of fresh cannabis plants. [@2]

The Commission disclosed the arrests in a statement issued by its public
relations officer Mathias Kamanga on Tuesday 14 June 2022. [@1] The Commission
called on Zambia's traditional leaders to join the fight against cannabis
cultivation in their chiefdoms, saying it contributed to the availability of
such drugs on the streets. [@2]
