Mwanza police held 18-year-old Kingsley Chavula in custody after he was
allegedly found with 511 twists of cannabis sativa, known locally as chamba,
without a permit. [@1] Chavula was arrested during intensive patrols that Mwanza
police conducted in Chikolosa village, in the area of Traditional Authority
Kanduku Maseko in Mwanza District, during the night of Monday this week. [@1]

Mwanza police public relations officer Edwin Kaunda said Chavula was found with
511 twists of cannabis sativa, which he called against the constitution of the
Republic of Malawi. [@1] Kaunda said the suspect was expected to appear in court
soon to answer the charge of possessing cannabis sativa without permit. [@2]

Chavula comes from Chikolosa village, in the area of Traditional Authority
Inkosi Kandukun Maseko in Mwanza District. [@1]
