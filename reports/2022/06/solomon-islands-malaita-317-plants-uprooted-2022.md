Royal Solomon Islands Police Force officers in Auki uprooted 317 cannabis plants
from two communities in the West Kwara'ae electorate of Malaita on 22 June 2022.
[@1] One suspect was arrested during the raid and a second was away at the time.
[@1] Operational Commander Charles Edward Koto confirmed the raid and the
uprooting of the plants. [@1]

Sergeant Robert Alegao, who led the raiding team, said officers pulled more than
100 plants at Buianao from about 10:30 that morning and more than 200 more at
Coral later in the day. [@1] Police put the street value of the 317 plants at
more than 90,000 Solomon Islands dollars. [@1]

Koto said community members had identified those farming the plant and worked
with police. [@1] Buianao and Coral have their own by-laws prohibiting cannabis
cultivation. [@1] Koto said it was the largest cannabis raid Auki police had
carried out that year. [@1]
