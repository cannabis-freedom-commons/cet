Kazakhstan's National Security Committee (KNB) ran more than 30 anti-drug
operations in April and May 2022, some together with the special services and
law enforcement agencies of Kyrgyzstan and Uzbekistan, its press service said.
[@2] Fifteen members of drug structures were detained in the special operations.
[@2]

The committee said the operations eliminated nine international and 14 regional
drug-trafficking channels and closed eight large underground laboratories
producing synthetic drugs. [@2] Officers seized 223 kg of drugs in total; the
cannabis share was 85.2 kg of marijuana and 4 kg of hashish. [@2] The KNB also
said 500 plants of 'Dutch' marijuana were removed from circulation, alongside
800 ecstasy tablets and 4 tonnes of chemical reagents intended for making
psychotropic substances. [@2] Liter.kz reported the same seizure figures from
the KNB statement, including 85.2 kg of marijuana and 4 kg of hashish. [@3]

The KNB estimated the black-market value of all drugs seized in the two months
at more than 2.3 billion tenge. [@1] The committee said its measures against the
international drug business continue. [@2]
