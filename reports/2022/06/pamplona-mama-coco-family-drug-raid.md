Peruvian police raided the home of María Sánchez Gárate, an 85-year-old known as
Mamá Coco, in the Pamplona area of San Juan de Miraflores on suspicion of
leading a family drug-selling operation. [@1] They also detained her partner
Guillermo Villar Santisteban, 70, known as Gepeto, and her granddaughter Rocío
del Pilar Muñoz Sánchez, 25, known as La Sirenita. [@1] All three were detained
for drug trafficking. [@1]

Officers seized 3,000 ketes, small wrapped doses, of coca paste, marijuana and
solid drugs in the house. [@1] Mamá Coco told police she had been in prison for
a year and sold the ketes for one sol each. [@1] The operation involved more
than 40 officers, a door-breaking ram and a crowbar, after the Grupo Terna of
the Escuadrón Verde had filmed the family selling drugs on the street. [@1]

She had been intercepted in 2011, in 2021 and in February 2022, and had an
outstanding warrant for drug trafficking. [@1] The granddaughter had also been
intercepted in February 2022 for drug trafficking. [@1] Neighbors filed an
anonymous complaint because the sales attracted criminals to the neighbourhood,
leading police to investigate and raid the house. [@1] The raid was carried out
under a judicial order. [@2] An undercover police collaborator handed money to
the group and the granddaughter then delivered doses of coca paste outside the
house. [@2] La República reported that police broke in during the early hours
and found the granddaughter, mother of a two-year-old girl, preparing coca paste
packets in her room. [@3] The three detainees were handed to the Public
Ministry. [@1]
