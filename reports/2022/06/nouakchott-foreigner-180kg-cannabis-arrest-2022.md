Mauritanian police announced on 15 June 2022 that a foreign man had been
arrested in Nouakchott and said his nationality was not given. [@1] About 180
kilograms of cannabis were seized in the arrest. [@1] The arrest followed
surveillance by the National Office for the Control of Drugs and Psychotropic
Substances (ONCDSP). [@1] The ONCDSP said its officers carried out the
operation. [@3]

Police placed the suspect in garde à vue, police custody, to allow investigators
to deepen the inquiry. [@1] The office described the arrested man as a drug
dealer. [@2]
