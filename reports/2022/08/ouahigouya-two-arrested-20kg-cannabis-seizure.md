Investigators from the Central Police Station in the town of Ouahigouya arrested
two people who had been actively sought. [@1] The two suspects were themselves
based in Ouahigouya and were moving narcotics between Burkina Faso and a
neighbouring country. [@2] Investigators managed to catch the two traffickers
thanks to cooperation from the population, according to the police. [@3]

Officers found 19 slabs of Indian hemp, a term for cannabis, weighing 20.08 kg
in the pair's possession. [@1] The 20.08 kg total was reported for the 19 slabs
together, not as separate weighings published for each slab. [@2]

The National Police thanked citizens as a whole for the cooperation that ended
the pair's activity and urged everyone to stay vigilant as drug trafficking
grows in towns. [@1] It asked people to keep reporting suspicious cases to the
toll-free numbers 17, 16 and 1010. [@1] The police statement presents the force
as a public force serving citizens and is credited as the source of the account.
[@1]
