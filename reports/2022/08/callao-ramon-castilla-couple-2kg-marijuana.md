Officers of the Departamento Antidrogas del Callao arrested a couple in Callao
after the intelligence area reported a man and a woman using a house in the
asentamiento humano Ramón Castilla, a settlement in the constitutional province
of Callao. [@1] Police said the couple used the house to make and package
illegal drugs that both of them then distributed to residents of the area. [@1]

According to police sources, the two were seen entering and leaving the house
repeatedly to make deliveries before officers stopped them. [@1] A search inside
the property found more than 2 kilograms of marijuana. [@1] The same search
found more than 500 grams of cocaine hydrochloride, which is not counted in the
cannabis figures for this report. [@1]

The detainees were named as César Augusto Caballero Farfán, 30, alias 'Barbón',
and Yannderill Stefanny Ayala, 34, alias 'Teffa'. [@1] Both were taken to the
headquarters of the National Police's anti-drug directorate, Dirandro. [@1]
