A joint team from the Pesisir Selatan narcotics unit's Sapu Jagat operations
team and Sutera sector police arrested a 35-year-old man identified only as S
for alleged cannabis abuse. [@1] Officers arrested S at a house in Kampung Koto
Panjang, Nagari Koto Nan Tigo Selatan Surantih in Sutera district at about 16.00
on Thursday 25 August 2022. [@1] Police said a public tip that S often conducted
drug transactions in the area prompted the operation. [@1] Officers arranged an
undercover buy, agreed a sale, and arrested S when the evidence was handed over
without resistance. [@1]

In a search witnessed by local residents, police found two medium packets and
two small packets of suspected cannabis. [@1] Police also seized a knife and a
bundle of cannabis stalks. [@1] S admitted the seized items were his. [@1]

Narcotics unit chief AKP Hidup Mulia said S will face layered charges under Law
Number 35 of 2009 on Narcotics. [@1] He said the charges carry life
imprisonment, or a minimum of 5 years and a maximum of 20 years, as well as the
death penalty. [@1] He said the quantity together with the tools found strongly
indicated S was both a dealer and a user, subject to intensive professional
examination. [@1] S and the evidence were held at the Pesisir Selatan police
headquarters for further development. [@1]
