Police in Mzimba district, Malawi, arrested a 100-year-old man at his home in
Manyamula on 31 August 2022 on suspicion of possessing cannabis sativa without a
permit. [@1] Mzimba Police Station spokesperson Sub-Inspector Peter Botha
identified the man as Stanley Gondwe and gave the date and place of the arrest.
[@1] Police detectives were carrying out a raid in the area when well-wishers
tipped them off that Gondwe was keeping illicit drugs at his house. [@1]

A search of his home found two pails filled with cannabis sativa. [@1] Officers
detained him on the spot and took him to Mzimba Police Station, where a case of
being found in possession of Indian hemp without a permit was opened. [@1] He
was scheduled to appear in court soon to answer the charge. [@1]

Gondwe comes from Jam Kalua Village in Traditional Authority M'mbelwa, Mzimba
District. [@1] Malawi24 reported the same arrest on 1 September 2022, naming the
same suspect, age, date and place, and calling the drug chamba. [@2] The suspect
was identified as Stanley Gondwe, 100, and was arrested on Wednesday August 31,
2022 at his home. [@2] Africa-Press republished the same account on 6 September
2022. [@3]
