Police arrested 18-year-old Rushawna Cohall of Dempshire Pen, Spanish Town, and
a 15-year-old boy during an operation in Shelter Rock, St Catherine, on 18
August 2022. [@2] Officers searched a premises in the Shelter Rock area at about
4:10 pm during the operation. [@1] Charged are 18-year-old Rushawna Cohall of
Dempshire Pen and the teenage boy. [@1] Cohall and the boy were charged with
illegal possession of a firearm, illegal possession of ammunition, possession of
ganja and dealing in ganja. [@1]

Officers found one Ruger 9mm pistol with a magazine containing 10 rounds of
ammunition in the possession of the accused. [@1] A further search found three
sealed parcels containing ganja. [@2] The constabulary's Corporate
Communications Unit confirmed that the Ruger pistol and 10 rounds were found in
the teenagers' possession. [@2]

The woman and the boy were scheduled to face the court following the firearm and
ganja seizure in Shelter Rock on Thursday, August 18. [@1] Their court dates
were being finalised, with no outcome reported. [@3]
