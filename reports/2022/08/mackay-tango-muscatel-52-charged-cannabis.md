Detectives from the Mackay Tactical Operations Team closed Operation Tango
Muscatel, a major drug investigation, in the week before 16 August 2022. [@2]
During the operation 52 people were charged with a total of 239 offences. [@2]
The operation led to 52 arrests on 239 drug-related charges. [@1] Four people
arrested in the operation could be named; the alleged network spanned Mackay to
Brisbane. [@1]

Operation Tango Muscatel began in November 2021 and focused on trafficking
methylamphetamine, MDMA and cannabis in the Mackay District. [@2] Police said
investigators identified a criminal network reaching from Brisbane to Mackay
involved in supplying and trafficking dangerous drugs. [@2] Investigators seized
2 kg of cannabis and 83 cannabis plants, along with 150 g of methylamphetamine,
13 g of cocaine and quantities of LSD, MDMA and steroids. [@2] Police put the
total street value of all the drugs seized, including the non-cannabis
substances, at $425,000. [@2] Police also seized handguns, rifles and shotguns.
[@2] Investigators seized over $200,000 in cash during the operation. [@2]

On 8, 9 and 10 August officers arrested the operation's main targets in raids
across the Mackay District. [@2] At a Habana address on 10 August police seized
five cannabis plants and equipment for drug production. [@2] A 30-year-old woman
and a 33-year-old man were charged with offences including producing and
possessing dangerous drugs. [@2] The man was due in Mackay Magistrates Court on
12 September and the woman on 24 August. [@2] Of the 239 charges, seven were for
trafficking, 86 for supplying dangerous drugs and six for producing dangerous
drugs. [@3] Police raided properties in the Mackay region between 8 and 10
August. [@3]

Investigators allegedly seized 2 kilograms of cannabis and 83 cannabis plants
alongside 150 grams of methylamphetamine. [@3]

A spokesperson said 52 people were arrested with charges ranging from firearm
possession to trafficking dangerous drugs. [@3]
