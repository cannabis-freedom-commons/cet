Police arrested four people at the Muriqan border crossing in north-western
Albania as they tried to leave the country for Montenegro. [@1] The four men
were aged 24, 23, 22 and 64 and gave home towns of Kurbin, Kuçova, Tirana and
Shkodër. [@4] They arrived at the crossing in a car, with the oldest of the four
driving and the three younger men as passengers. [@3] Border police sent the car
to a second line for a detailed check of the people and the vehicle. [@2]

Physical checks on the four found cannabis, ketamine and ecstasy divided into
packages. [@1] The police said investigative actions showed the drugs were bound
for Montenegro. [@3] Officers seized the drugs, the car, a sum of money and five
mobile phones as material evidence. [@3]

The operation was codenamed Ecstasy and was run by Shkodër's organised crime
directorate (DVKM) with the Shkodër police directorate (DVP). [@2] The case was
referred to the prosecutor's office at Shkodër district court over trafficking
in narcotics committed in cooperation, in an attempted form. [@2]
