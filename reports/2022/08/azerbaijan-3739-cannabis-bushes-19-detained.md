Azerbaijan's Interior Ministry said 19 members of an organised group were
detained during operations in Baku and other regions over the previous 20 days.
[@1] The group was accused of smuggling drugs into the country, cultivating
narcotic plants, organising online sales and involving minors as couriers. [@2]
Special operations in the capital and the regions led to the detention of Agil
Aghazade, Alizade Bakhshiyev and Azim Azimov, the ministry said. [@1] As the
operation continued, 16 more people who organised intensive drug sales were
detained, according to the statement. [@1]

In the cars and homes of the three named men, officers found 40 kg of marijuana
alongside heroin, opium and methamphetamine, electronic scales, cash from sales
and cards used for online sales. [@1] The ministry put the group's overall haul
at 154 kg of narcotic and psychotropic substances, 1,752 psychotropic tablets
and 3,739 cultivated cannabis bushes. [@2] On Azim Azimov's land plot in Sabunçu
district, fitted with irrigation, ventilation and lighting, officers found 3,724
cannabis bushes with a fresh weight of 2,600 kg. [@1]

Criminal cases were opened under relevant articles of the Criminal Code and
every detainee was remanded in custody, Trend reported from the ministry's
statement. [@3] According to the ministry, the group's main aim was to sell
smuggled drugs and recruit more couriers with promises of easy money. [@4]
