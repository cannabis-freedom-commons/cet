Rashon Lewis, 42, of Jacksonville, Florida, and Rhondella Guishard, 25, of
Horsepath, were arrested and charged on August 30, 2022, when police searched
the vehicle they were in at Hodge's Creek. [@2] The Royal Virgin Islands Police
Force Armed Response Team said the charges followed a routine search of the
rental vehicle the couple were in. [@1] Officers approached a vehicle in the
parking lot of Hodge's Creek Marina while making their rounds late Tuesday
night. [@1] After detecting the scent of marijuana, the male driver was
questioned and searched along with the woman and the vehicle. [@1] The
prosecution said police were on patrol on Blackburn Highway near Hodge's Creek
when they noticed a black vehicle parked at the marina. [@3] Both were charged
with carrying a firearm without a licence and unlawful possession of ammunition.
[@1] Lewis was additionally charged with possession of a controlled drug with
intent to supply, unlawful possession of cannabis and landing in the Territory
without leave of an immigration officer. [@1]

A semi-automatic pistol and two loaded magazines with a total of 24 rounds of
ammunition were uncovered. [@1] Police said close to 240 grams of suspected
cannabis and over $4,000 in cash was also found. [@1] The prosecution said the
suspected cannabis weighed 18.17 grams with an estimated street value of
$181.70, and the gun was a Smith and Wesson M&P with 24 rounds of ammunition in
the magazine. [@3] A search warrant at Lewis's home found 220 grams of suspected
marijuana in a suitcase, along with 1,600 grams of MDMA pills described as
ecstasy. [@3]

Guishard and Lewis appeared before Senior Magistrate Tamia Richards on 6
September 2022 on the firearm and ammunition charges. [@3] Lewis was remanded
with no bail application made on his behalf, while Guishard, who had been on
station bail after anxiety attacks, had her bail extended. [@3] The matter was
adjourned to 31 October 2022. [@2]
