Police at Tanger-Med port, coordinating with customs, foiled an international
drug-trafficking attempt on Thursday 25 August 2022, and the suspect was a
Franco-Algerian. [@1] The driver, a 42-year-old Franco-Algerian, was arrested.
[@3] The vehicle he drove was registered in France. [@1] The cargo had been
wrapped and hidden in the roof and seats of the car. [@1]

A DGSN statement put the seized hashish at 840 kg, hidden in a utility vehicle
registered in France. [@2] The find came from a border-control operation using
trained dogs. [@3]

He was placed in police custody at the disposal of an investigation opened by
the judicial police brigade at Tanger-Med port. [@1] The inquiry sought to
establish all the ramifications of the trafficking attempt. [@1] He was about to
take a boat back to France when he was arrested and taken to the police station
for custody. [@4]
