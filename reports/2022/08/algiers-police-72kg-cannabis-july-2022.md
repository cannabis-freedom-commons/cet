Officers of the Sûreté de wilaya d'Alger, the Algiers provincial police, seized
more than 72 kilograms of cannabis during July 2022, according to a communiqué
issued on Saturday 13 August by Algeria's Directorate General of National
Security (DGSN). [@1] The communiqué gave the precise cannabis weight as 72,633
kg. [@2]

The seizures were made across various cases handled by the judicial police
branch of the Algiers provincial police. [@1] Alongside the cannabis, officers
took 21,619 psychotropic tablets, 5.38 grams of cocaine, 38.16 grams of heroin
and seven vials of anaesthetic solution. [@1] The DGSN described the actions as
part of its efforts against different forms of crime, notably the trafficking of
drugs, psychotropics and hard drugs. [@1]

The same communiqué separately reported that the Algiers police had handled
cases of carrying bladed weapons and arrested people who were referred to the
territorial courts after legal procedures, matters distinct from the drug
seizures. [@2]
