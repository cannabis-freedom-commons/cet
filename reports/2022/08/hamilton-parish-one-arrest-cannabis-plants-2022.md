On Thursday 18 August 2022, officers of the Bermuda Police Service Tactical Unit
(PSU) assisted the Government animal wardens with a search warrant under the
Dogs Act 2008 at a residence in Hamilton Parish. [@2] A police spokeswoman said
the search of the property resulted in seven dogs being seized, and that during
the search officers discovered a substantial amount of cannabis plants, some
measuring as tall as 5ft 7in in height. [@1]

One man was arrested on suspicion of cultivation, and the plants were seized.
[@1] The Bermuda Police Service said the man was arrested for the offence of
suspicion of cultivation, that the plants had been seized, and that an
investigation into the matter had commenced. [@2] Anyone with information in
relation to the investigation was asked to call the Police Support Unit on
247-1784 or to email x-PSU@bps.bm. [@2]

The cannabis was discovered at the Hamilton Parish home when police assisted dog
wardens in the seizure of several animals. [@1]
