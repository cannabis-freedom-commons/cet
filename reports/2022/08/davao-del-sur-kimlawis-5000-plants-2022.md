Police seized and destroyed about 5,000 fully grown marijuana plants worth P1
million in an operation in Barangay Kimlawis, Davao del Sur, at past 4 a.m. on
20 August 2022. [@1] The Philippine National Police announced the operation in a
statement that week. [@1] About an hour later, officers uprooted P400,000 worth
of marijuana plants in a second operation at a nearby sitio. [@1] The PNP named
neither the sitio nor a plant count for that second site. [@1]

Together the two operations accounted for an estimated P1.4 million worth of
marijuana plants in several areas of the province. [@1] The PNP described the
two sites as clandestine farms and said their proximity raised the possibility
that the same cultivator or group of cultivators was behind both. [@1] PNP chief
Gen. Rodolfo Azurin Jr. said personnel were working doubly hard to intensify
operations. [@1]
