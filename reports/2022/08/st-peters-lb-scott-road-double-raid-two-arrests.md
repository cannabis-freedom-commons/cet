On August 31, 2022, around 5:45am, officers of the St. Maarten Police Force KPSM
searched two locations, one on L.B. Scott Road and one in St. Peters. [@2] Two
KPSM teams raided the two houses at dawn, searching for narcotics, weapons and
ammunition and seizing several items related to drug trafficking. [@1] Both
searches followed tip-offs, after KPSM had received information from the public
for weeks about locations where people were believed to hold firearms and
narcotics. [@1]

At the St. Peters property, officers found an alarm pistol and confiscated it.
[@1] At the L.B. Scott Road property, officers found and seized items related to
drug trafficking, including narcotics and ammunition. [@1] Officers also found
marijuana growing in the resident's yard and in a marijuana nursery, and seized
all plants and plant objects. [@1] Officers also found and confiscated an amount
of cash and distribution packaging. [@1]

Two suspects were arrested and taken to the police station for further
investigation, with the investigations still ongoing. [@2]
