Police at Kamuzu International Airport in Lilongwe intercepted four kilograms of
cannabis, known locally as chamba, on Thursday 7 January 2016. [@1] The parcel
carried a Harare address as its origin and a London address as its destination.
[@1] It travelled from Zimbabwe through Malawi to the United Kingdom using the
Zimbabwean post office and Kenya Airways, police said. [@2] The cannabis was
pressed into cake shapes, wrapped in carbon paper and covered with hot pepper in
an attempt to kill the smell. [@1]

No arrest had been made because the parcel had been posted from Zimbabwe, police
said. [@2] KIA police said they would contact colleagues in Zimbabwe to trace
the sender using the name and address on the parcel. [@2] The airport police
said they had stepped up scrutiny of every parcel entering or leaving KIA. [@2]

Police said it was the first chamba interception at KIA that year, after similar
cases at the airport between August and November 2015. [@1] Importing dangerous
drugs is illegal under section 11 of the Dangerous Drugs Act read with
regulation 19. [@2]
