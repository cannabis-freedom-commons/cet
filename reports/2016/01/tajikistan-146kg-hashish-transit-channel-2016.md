Tajik operatives in Tajikistan blocked another transit channel used to move
drugs from Afghanistan onward. [@1] The operation lasted about three months and
ended with the detention of five members of an organized criminal group,
including its leader. [@1] All members of the group face more than 20 years in
prison. [@1]

Officers seized more than 146 kilograms of hashish in Tajikistan during the
operation. [@1] In the same seizure, officers also took three kilograms of
heroin described as being of the highest degree of purification. [@1] The
account described the combined haul as the largest drug batch seized that year
in Tajikistan. [@1] The headline figure for the combined seizure was 150
kilograms of drugs. [@1]

The drugs were moving along a transit channel from Afghanistan to Russia, which
the operation closed. [@1] The seized consignment was reported as heading from
Tajikistan onward to Russia. [@1] The cannabis figures count only the hashish,
while the three kilograms of heroin are excluded as a separate non-cannabis
component. [@1]
