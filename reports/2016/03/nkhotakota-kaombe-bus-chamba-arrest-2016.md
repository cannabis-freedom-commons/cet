Nkhotakota police arrested George Kadzuwa, 23, for being found in possession of
cannabis sativa. [@1] Nkhotakota Police Public Relations Officer Sub Inspector
Williams Kaponda said the suspect was arrested at Kaombe roadblock in the
district. [@1]

At the checkpoint, police thoroughly searched a City Tours bus with registration
number B 1600. [@1] Kadzuwa was on the bus and quickly got off as the police
carried out the search. [@1] He began running away, but the police officers
followed and caught him. [@1] Officers then searched his bag and found chamba
concealed under clothes. [@1]

Police said the suspect hails from Laston Village, Traditional Authority
Chikowi, in Zomba. [@1] He was to appear in court to answer the charge of
illegally possessing illicit drugs. [@1]
