Neville Hillary Phagu, identified as a Guyanese national, was one of two men
charged over a multimillion-dollar cocaine find at Carlisle Bay in Barbados.
[@1] Phagu, 39, and Barbadian Jeffrey Harrington Callender, 48, of Coral Land,
Haggatt Hall, St Michael, appeared in the District A Magistrates' Court. [@1]
They were represented by attorneys Satcha Kissoon and Romain Marshall. [@2]

Police seized a boat, 122.9 kilos of cocaine and 94 kilos of cannabis during an
operation the previous Wednesday, and the drugs were valued at over $6 million.
[@1] A Nation report said police charged the two men over Wednesday's seizure of
cocaine and cannabis at Carlisle Bay, St Michael, taking 122.9 kgs of cocaine
and 94 kgs of cannabis valued in excess of $6 million. [@3]

Phagu and Callender were jointly charged with possession of cannabis, possession
with intent to supply cannabis and trafficking cannabis, together with the same
three offences for cocaine. [@1] Phagu was also separately charged with
importation of cocaine and importation of cannabis. [@1]
