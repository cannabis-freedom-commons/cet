Kyrgyz drug-control officers detained two Tajik citizens on suspicion of drug
trafficking, one of them described as a Tajik police major. [@1] The operation
took place on 1 March 2016 and was described as a carefully planned,
long-prepared special operation by officers of the State Drug Control Service.
[@1] Officers stopped an Opel-Vectra in Zhashtyk village in Batken region,
driven by Tajik Interior Ministry employee I.M., born in 1967. [@1] The second
man was O.M., born in 1971 and living in Dushanbe. [@1]

A search of the car trunk found two sports bags holding a dark-brown substance
smelling of wild cannabis. [@1] Specialists identified the substance as hashish
(Afghan charas) weighing 36 kg 300 g, with an estimated value of more than 4
million som. [@1]

The service said officers had thereby cut off a transit channel for Afghan drugs
through Kyrgyzstan and broken up an organised drug group. [@1] Its investigative
department opened a criminal case under Article 247 of the Kyrgyz Criminal Code
on illegal drug manufacture, acquisition, storage, transport, forwarding for
sale, production or sale. [@1] Tajikistan's Interior Ministry told Asia-Plus it
was not aware of the detention and promised to comment later. [@1]
