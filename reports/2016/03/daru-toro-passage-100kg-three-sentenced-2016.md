Three men were each jailed for two years by the National Court in Daru for smuggling marijuana onto the island town. [@1] Police intercepted them at Toro Passage, about four kilometres from the island, during a routine patrol. [@1]

They had nine bags of marijuana weighing more than 100kg, which police said would have been worth more than 500,000 Australian dollars had it reached the Torres Strait islands of northern Queensland. [@1] Police said the drugs had been brought down from the Highlands region through Kikori in Gulf province and then to Daru by dinghy. [@1]

After the convictions the exhibits were destroyed and burnt. [@1] Daru police said they had mapped the trading chain and would crack down on outsiders bringing drugs into the province with locals. [@1]
