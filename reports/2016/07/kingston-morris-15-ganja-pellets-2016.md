Richard Morris, a 41-year-old farmer of Nine Miles in Spaulding, Clarendon, was
arrested on Tuesday and charged with breaches of the Dangerous Drugs Act. [@1]
Police accused him of attempting to smuggle ganja from Jamaica to Barbados. [@1]
He was charged on Tuesday after being held with ganja while attempting to board
a flight at Norman Manley International Airport in Kingston that was destined
for Barbados. [@2]

At about 3:15 p.m. he arrived at the airport and checked in on a flight for
Barbados. [@1] He was stopped and interviewed by the police. [@1] Due to
inconsistencies with his answers, personnel from the Narcotics Office at the
airport asked for secondary checks to be done. [@1] A sample of his urine was
taken, which tested positive for ganja. [@1]

Morris allegedly confessed to ingesting ganja pellets and was taken to hospital,
where he was medically examined. [@1] Fifteen pellets were reportedly found.
[@1] Police said the 15 pellets, weighing 0.4 pounds, were found in his stomach.
[@2]

He was scheduled to appear in the St Andrew Parish Court on Friday. [@1] He was
to appear in the Kingston and St Andrew Parish Court the next day on charges of
breaches of the Dangerous Drugs Act. [@2]
