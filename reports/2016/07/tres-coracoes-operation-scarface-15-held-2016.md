Military police and prosecutors detained suspects in a joint operation in Três
Corações, Minas Gerais, on the morning of Thursday 14 July 2016. [@1] Officers
served 25 search-and-seizure warrants and 18 preventive arrest warrants in the
city, and 15 people were detained. [@1] At least 90 military police officers
from Três Corações, Varginha, Lavras, Cambuquira, Lambari, Campanha and São Tomé
das Letras took part, and warrants were also served in São Tomé das Letras,
Cambuquira and Sorocaba, in São Paulo state. [@1]

The operation, named Scarface after the film, followed eight months of
investigation into a ring that brought drugs from São Paulo state for storage in
Três Corações and distribution across the south of Minas Gerais. [@1] The
prosecution said four of those detained were heads of the drug trade in the
region and had links to a criminal faction. [@1] Prosecutor Igor Serrano Silva
said the group collected the drugs in São Paulo and used Três Corações' central
position near a busy highway to distribute them across the region. [@1]

The cannabis seized was nine parcels, called buchas, of marijuana, 96 grams of
the drug and one marijuana cigarette. [@1] Officers also seized 29 crack rocks,
a precision scale, a grinder, 18 mobile phones, two cars, a motorcycle, a
computer and R$ 1,180 in cash. [@1] The suspects and the material seized,
including restricted-use weapons, marijuana and cocaine, were taken to the
police station in Três Corações. [@1]

Eighteen members of the ring, which also sold drugs inside the regional
penitentiary in Três Corações, were convicted. [@2] All 18 had been arrested in
July 2016 during Operation Scarface, which the account credits to the Gaeco
organised-crime unit in Varginha. [@2] The prosecution said they were sentenced
for drug trafficking, arms sales and possession, and criminal association. [@2]
The longest sentence was 23 years. [@2] The sentences together exceeded 210
years and the fines totalled R$ 400,000. [@2] The 18 were initially to serve
their terms in closed regime. [@2]
