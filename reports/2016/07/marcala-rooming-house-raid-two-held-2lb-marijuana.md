The Public Ministry, together with Military Police of Public Order officers
acting under the national interagency security force FUSINA, raided a rooming
house in the San Miguel neighbourhood of Marcala, in the department of La Paz.
[@1] Officers detained Jose Jamil Carcamo Sanchez, alias Marbella, and Daniel
Enrique Claros Ramos, alias El Negro, who were in one of the rooms where
investigators said drugs were being sold. [@1]

Those detained had about two pounds of suspected marijuana, about 0.91 kg, and
several mobile phones seized from them. [@1] The raid followed investigations
indicating that the premises were being used to sell drugs, with coordination
arranged to detain those responsible for the activity. [@1]

The Marcala local prosecutor was to charge both men with facilitating premises
for unlawful drug trafficking and unlawful association, and they were to be
brought before the local court within hours. [@1]
