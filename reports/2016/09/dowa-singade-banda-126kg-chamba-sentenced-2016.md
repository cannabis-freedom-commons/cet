A Dowa first grade magistrate court in Malawi sentenced Weluzani Singade, 35,
and Kwame Banda, 21, to 30 and 20 months in prison with hard labour
respectively, without the option of a fine, for possessing Indian hemp, locally
known as chamba. [@1] Singade and Banda were arrested on different days but at
the same roadblock in Dowa. [@1]

Presenting facts in court, prosecutor Regina Kumwenda said Singade was found
with two bags of Indian hemp that weighed 98 kg when they were taken to Chitedze
Research Station. [@1] The court heard that Banda, arrested at the same
roadblock, was found with one bag of Indian hemp weighing 28 kg. [@1]

The state asked the court to give stiffer sentences so that others would learn a
lesson. [@1] Passing judgment, First Grade Magistrate Amulan Phiri agreed that
the convicts needed stiff sentences to deter potential offenders and sentenced
Singade to 30 months and Banda to 20 months in prison, both without the option
of a fine. [@1] The second case was prosecuted by Sergeant Triza Kalonjele. [@2]
