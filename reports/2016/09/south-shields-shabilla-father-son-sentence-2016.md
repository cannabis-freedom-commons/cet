Sukdave Shabilla, a former England under-18 hockey player, and his son Manroup were each jailed for two years at Newcastle Crown Court after admitting conspiracy to produce cannabis through their South Shields letting agency, HB Lettings. [@1,2] The pair said they had been intimidated, with Manroup reporting a kidnapping and Sukdave saying live bullets were posted to him and a petrol bomb left at the family home, but the judge said they could have gone to the police. [@1,2]

Police raids found professional cannabis farms at three properties controlled by the Shabillas and tended by Vietnamese gardeners: 216 plants at Beaufront Terrace in January 2014, 250 at Dacre Street, and 268 back at Dean Terrace that August after 12 plants found there that April had drawn no action. [@1,2] The plot was valued at at least 210,000 pounds. [@2] Two gardeners had already been sentenced and police were seeking another man linking the farms to an illegal operation in London. [@2]
