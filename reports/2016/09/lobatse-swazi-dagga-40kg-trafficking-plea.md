Two Swazi men pleaded with magistrate Mareledi Dipate in Lobatse to forgive and
release them from custody while facing trial for allegedly trafficking about 40
kg of dagga into Botswana. [@1] They were Musa ‘Charles’ Mabizela, 40, and
Sibusizo Dlamini, 44, arrested on Saturday at the Pioneer Border Gate in Lobatse
aboard a white Quantum mini bus, where the dagga was allegedly concealed in
various compartments of the car. [@1] In a lengthy arraignment, the duo asked
the court to release them back to Swaziland, where they had left families. [@1]

Mabizela said it was the first time he undertook such a journey into Botswana.
[@1] He said a man in Swaziland had sent them and promised to pay them P6,000 to
transport the dagga into Botswana, which they were supposed to share on success.
[@1] He said he was employed as a taxi driver by a woman whose car was used and
that she did not know where he was. [@1] He said he had four children at home
dependent on him and had intended to use the pay for a child’s school fees. [@1]
