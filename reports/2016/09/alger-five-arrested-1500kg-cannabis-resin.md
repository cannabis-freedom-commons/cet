Algiers wilaya security services said on Monday that they had dismantled an
international drug-trafficking network. [@1] Five members of the network were
apprehended. [@1] The group held more than 15 quintals (1,500 kg) of cannabis
resin. [@1]

Those arrested also held 19 mobile phones, five vehicles used to travel through
the western wilayas, notably Oran and Tlemcen, a shotgun and a large sum of
money. [@1] Police seized 26,600 Subutex tablets, a psychotropic drug that is
outside the cannabis figures. [@1] Special intervention brigades dismantled the
network after three months of research and investigation with scientific police,
and the group had been preparing to move the drugs across the Algeria–Morocco
border. [@1] The investigation began in the commune of El Harrach and expanded
over a long process. [@1]

The network was active on the Algiers–Maghnia–Tlemcen axis and extended to
Europe. [@2] Berrachedi made the announcement at a press conference in Bab
Ezzouar. [@2] He praised cooperation between the security services and citizens,
particularly through the 15.48 hotline. [@1]
