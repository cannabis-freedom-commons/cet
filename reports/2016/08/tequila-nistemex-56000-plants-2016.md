On the afternoon of 12 August 2016, Jalisco state police carried out a ground
surveillance patrol. [@1] The patrol covered a property called Nistemex, about
three kilometres from the town of El Salvador, where the narcotic-plant plots
were found. [@1] Two plots of land planted with green vegetation that appeared
to be marijuana were located and destroyed in Tequila municipality by Fuerza
Única Jalisco. [@1] Each plot covered 7,000 square metres, a combined 1.4
hectares. [@1]

Officers found four marijuana plants per square metre, with an average height of
1.20 metres. [@1] The approximately 56,000 plants were destroyed and
incinerated. [@1] Fuerza Única Jalisco photographed the operation before, during
and after, and took samples of the vegetation. [@1] The samples and the
destruction record were handed to the competent authority. [@1]

The Jalisco prosecutor's office said the operation reaffirmed its commitment to
combat the production and trade of enervantes, narcotic plants. [@1]
