The Palm Beach County Sheriff's Office SWAT team arrested three men in The
Acreage who were living in a house used to grow and distribute marijuana. [@1]
The men were Raul Ruiz-Palau, 38, Yunlet Rivero-Riquene, 30, and Jorge
Rivero-Riquene, 33. [@1] Each faces charges of trafficking, cultivating and
possessing marijuana, as well as possessing drug paraphernalia. [@1] The three
remained in Palm Beach County Jail, with Jorge Rivero-Riquene held on $69,000
bail and Yunlet Rivero-Riquene and Ruiz-Palau held on $59,000 bail. [@1]

Deputies went to the home at 16396 Key Lime Blvd. and found the three men
sleeping in a room that had only two beds. [@1] Officers found one bedroom and a
garage used to grow and package marijuana, fitted with high-intensity lights.
[@1] The rooms also held garbage bags, pots, buckets and drying trays containing
marijuana. [@1] Police put the total at approximately 45 pounds of marijuana
(about 20.4 kg), not including marijuana from a third bedroom or marijuana swept
from the floor. [@1]

The arrests occurred on a Wednesday in The Acreage. [@1]
