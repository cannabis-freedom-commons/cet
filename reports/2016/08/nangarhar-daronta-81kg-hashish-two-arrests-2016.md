Police spokesman Lt. Col. Hazrat Hussain Mashriqiwal said two suspects had been
captured with 81 kilograms of hashish. [@2] The spokesman said the suspects were
smuggling the drugs from Kabul to Torkham and were arrested at the Daronta
police check-post. [@2]

Police recovered 81 kilograms of hashish in eastern Nangarhar. [@1] Police
described the Nangarhar recovery as a large quantity of drugs. [@1] The account
was filed from Kabul and Jalalabad. [@2] Pajhwok Afghan News published the
account on 30 August 2016. [@2]

In the same dispatch, National Directorate of Security personnel reported
seizing four suicide vests and explosives in central Kapisa province. [@2] A
spy-service statement listed 200 kilograms of explosives, nine landmines, 86
mortar shells, 44 warheads and thousands of bullets recovered there. [@2] The
Kapisa arms haul was a separate operation from the hashish arrest and is not
counted in the cannabis figures. [@2]
