Mauritanian authorities stopped a vehicle on Wednesday 24 August 2016 at the
Kouki Zemmal crossing point, located in the far east of Mauritania in the
vicinity of the town of Kobeni. [@1] During the stop, officers seized 35 kg of
hashish that was on board the vehicle. [@1] The passengers travelling aboard the
vehicle were also arrested. [@1] They were detained within the framework of
instructions that were still awaited, intended to throw light on what were
termed the unspoken aspects surrounding this important quantity of drugs. [@1]

The immobilised vehicle had been intercepted in the course of a routine
inspection conducted by the security authorities at that same crossing point.
[@1] That crossing point is located in the Hodh Gharbi wilaya, a region of
Mauritania, on the Mauritania–Mali frontier. [@1] The frontier area around the
crossing is described as a place where the lords of drug trafficking and
cross-border criminality proliferate. [@1]
