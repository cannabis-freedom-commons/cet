The Juzgado de Letras of the Judicial Section of Comayagua sentenced Rafael
Leonardo Petit Luna, 23, and Santos Emilio Guerrero Aguiluz, 21, to four years
and six months in prison plus accessory penalties for facilitating means of
transport for illicit drug trafficking. [@1] Both men had been detained during
routine operations by Preventive National Police officers. [@1]

Petit Luna was detained on 14 July 2016 at about 11:30 p.m. in barrio El Centro
in the city of Comayagua, when officers stopped him for a personal search. [@1]
Officers found 31.84 grams of marijuana in his trouser pocket. [@1] Guerrero
Aguiluz was detained on 3 July 2016 while travelling as a passenger aboard a
taxi intercepted by a police patrol, and officers found him carrying 178 small
bags containing cocaine. [@1]

Both accused accepted their participation and confessed under the abbreviated
procedure in articles 403 and 404 of the Criminal Procedure Code. [@1] The court
also imposed a fine of 37,500 lempiras alongside the prison terms. [@1]
