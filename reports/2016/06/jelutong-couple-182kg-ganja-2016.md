Officers of the Bukit Aman Special Tactical Intelligence Narcotics Group, known as STING, with the Penang narcotics department detained a 37-year-old plumber in the car park of a condominium in Jelutong at 12.30pm on Thursday after finding ganja on him. [@1,2] A follow-up raid on his condominium unit led to the arrest of his 30-year-old wife. [@1,2] Police found nearly 8 kg of ganja hidden in a room of the flat. [@2] The couple then led officers to a car parked in a nearby residential area holding 91 kg, and to a second house in Kampung Dodol used to store drugs holding about 83 kg worth RM240,000. [@2] In total the two raids netted 182 kg worth about RM540,000, acting on a public tip-off and intelligence. [@1,2]

The couple tested positive for drugs and were remanded for investigation under Section 39B of the Dangerous Drugs Act 1952. [@2] Inspector-General of Police Khalid Abu Bakar confirmed the arrests and the seizure on his official Twitter account, congratulating the STING team. [@2]
