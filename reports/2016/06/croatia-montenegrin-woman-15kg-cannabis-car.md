Svetlana Rakočević, a 46-year-old Montenegrin woman, was arrested after she
drove a Fiat Lancia from Bosnia and Herzegovina into Croatia and police searched
the car. [@1] Officers found a specially made hiding place in the car holding
15.25 kg of cannabis. [@1] A joint operation by Montenegrin, Croatian and
Bosnian police resulted in charges against her. [@1]

The Dubrovnik-Neretva County police said the seizure took place on a Monday at
the Nova Sela border crossing. [@3] Officers grew suspicious during the border
check and searched the vehicle. [@3] The specially built compartment in the car
held 21 packages of marijuana weighing 15.25 kg. [@2] Croatian police put the
weight at 15.255 kg in 21 packages hidden in the plastic of the car doors. [@3]
After the criminal investigation she was handed to a detention supervisor on
suspicion of unauthorised production and trafficking of drugs. [@3]

Police said they assumed women were increasingly drawn into the drugs trade
because of a mistaken belief that they would be spared detailed police checks.
