Malawian truck driver Harrison Mlauzi was arrested at Beitbridge border post
after fleeing Nyamapanda border post in April 2016. [@1] Police arrested him on
a Thursday with the help of the truck's owner, Edwin Banda. [@1] He was driving
a Dalitso Logistics truck carrying unprocessed tobacco from Malawi to Zimbabwe.
[@1]

The tobacco was loaded in Malawi on 23 April 2016, and that afternoon Mlauzi
loaded 31 bags of dagga at Biwi Triangle in Lilongwe, the court heard. [@1] The
chamba was hidden among the tobacco stalks, which were to be taken to South
Africa. [@1] He reached Nyamapanda on 26 April 2016. [@1] Zimbabwe Revenue
Authority officers ordered him to park the truck in the searching bay for a
routine inspection. [@1] Mlauzi slipped away before the search, and officers
found the bags concealed in black plastic bags. [@1] Prosecutors said the chamba
weighed 1,440 kg and had a street value of $14,400. [@1]

An earlier report on the discovery put the load at 1,494 kg, valued at $149,400.
[@2] NewsDay reported that the 31 bags of dagga, worth nearly $150,000, were
found together with three bales of second-hand clothes. [@3] He spent the
weekend in custody awaiting a bail hearing on 20 June 2016. [@1]
