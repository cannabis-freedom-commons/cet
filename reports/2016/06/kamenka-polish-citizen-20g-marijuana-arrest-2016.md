On 27 June, 29-year-old Polish citizen Krzysztof Brelanski crossed the PMR state
border without stopping at the Kamenka bridge customs checkpoint. [@1] Officers
of the OGAI traffic unit of the Kamensky district police caught the driver and
returned him to the customs post. [@1] The foreign citizen was detained for
three days. [@1]

During a search of Brelanski's car, customs officers found marijuana with a
total weight of about 20 grams, along with smoking accessories. [@1] The press
service of the Investigative Committee said the man had thereby illegally moved
a narcotic across the PMR customs border. [@1]

Brelanski said he was travelling alone through several European countries,
periodically using the drug on the journey and carrying it unhindered across
several countries' borders. [@1] Under the law in force in Transnistria, the
offence is classed as grave and carries a prison term of 3 to 7 years. [@1] The
investigation therefore intended to ask a court to impose pre-trial detention in
custody as the preventive measure. [@1]
