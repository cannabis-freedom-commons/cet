Four men from Saint Vincent were convicted for transporting several hundred
kilos of cannabis herb. [@1] The four men had been arrested on 20 April in
international waters and appeared before the criminal court. [@1] They remained
silent before the judges and gave no details at the hearing. [@1] The crew were
sent to prison following the conviction. [@1]

More than 700 kilos of cannabis had been seized on 20 April. [@1] On the night
of 19 to 20 April, the surveillance frigate Germinal intervened about 50
kilometres off the Martinique coast to intercept a go-fast suspected of drug
trafficking. [@3] The Germinal acted with a Customs Panther helicopter after
information from the office for the repression of illicit drug trafficking. [@2]
The occupants threw several bales overboard when intercepted, but 48 bales were
seized in total. [@2] The boat was boarded south of Martinique after coming from
Saint Vincent. [@4] The four occupants were arrested and the illicit goods were
seized. [@4] The suspects were handed to agents of the Central Office for the
Repression of Illicit Drug Trafficking. [@4] The seized drugs were destroyed on
the orders of the Fort-de-France specialised prosecutor. [@2]
