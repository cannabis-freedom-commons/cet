Police in Tanzania's northern Arusha region seized 269 bags of marijuana weighing 26 tons in a crackdown across Arumeru district, a notorious marijuana-farming area on the slopes of Mount Meru. [@1] Arusha regional police commander Charles Mkumbo said the haul was the result of a one-month crackdown in the region, after police mapped the area's known marijuana-producing zones. [@1]

Mkumbo called it the biggest seizure of the drug in recent years in the country, and said the bags were destroyed at the Muriet dumpsite a few kilometres from Arusha city centre. [@1,2] Most of the bags had been left unattended by farmers and dealers, some of whom ran away as police moved in, and no suspects were named. [@1]

Arusha is one of Tanzania's key marijuana-producing regions, alongside Morogoro, Iringa, Tabora, Mara, Rukwa, Ruvuma and Tanga, with much of the crop sold in major cities and smuggled to Uganda, Rwanda, Burundi, the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Ethiopia and Sudan. [@1]
