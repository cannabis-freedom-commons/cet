Three men were arrested after police found cocaine and cannabis weed in their
possession with a combined street value of EC$172,000. [@1] Police PRO Acting
Inspector Pellam Jno Baptiste said the Drug Squad Unit of the Police Force led a
successful anti-drug operation at about 10:00 pm on Sunday, 5 June, at Batalie.
[@1] Two of the men were from Layou and one from Kingshill. [@1] They were
arrested on suspicion of possession of cocaine and possession with intent to
supply cocaine, as well as possession of cannabis weed and possession with
intent to supply cannabis weed. [@1]

The search targeted motor vehicle registration HB232. [@1] Its three occupants
had 3,000 grams of alleged cocaine, with an approximate street value of
EC$80,100, according to the police statement. [@1] They also had 92 pounds
(about 41.73 kg) of alleged cannabis weed, which police gave an approximate
street value of EC$92,000. [@1] Police seized motor vehicle HB232 and €800 for
the investigation. [@1] Investigations were ongoing. [@1]
