Police officers at the Gourel Oumar Ly control post arrested Mauritanian
national Bocar Malal Seme on Friday 17 June 2016 at about 19:00. [@2] He had
just landed on the Senegalese bank carrying a travel bag slung over his shoulder
that drew the officers' attention, and he was stopped at once, according to a
National Police public-relations office statement. [@1]

A search of the bag found 20 kilograms of cannabis herb, known locally as
chanvre indien. [@1] The arresting officers belong to the Directorate of Air and
Border Police, whose frontier posts serve as crossing points in both directions.
[@2]

The suspect was handed over to the commander of the Horéfondé Gendarmerie
Brigade, which opened an investigation. [@2] The prosecutor and the Matam
authorities were immediately informed of the arrest. [@3]
