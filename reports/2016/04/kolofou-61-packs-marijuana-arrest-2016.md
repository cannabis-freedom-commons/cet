Tonga police chased and arrested a 40-year-old man from Kolofo'ou on Laifone
Road on Tuesday 12 April 2016. [@2] Officers found 61 small packs of marijuana
on him after a short chase along Laifone Rd in Kolofo'ou. [@1] He was held in
custody following the arrest. [@2]

He was charged with possession of an illicit drug, according to Chief
Superintendent Tevita Fifita. [@1] Fifita said police acted on a tip that the
accused carried the marijuana. [@1] Police believed the detainee intended to
sell the drug to tourists from Pacific Pearl, which arrived in Tonga that
Tuesday. [@1] It was likely that more charges would be laid against the accused.
