Italian police arrested two Montenegrin citizens, Ibrahim Saljaj and Valentina
Krušević, both 30, in Lecce. [@1] The arrest took place on the evening of Friday
22 April 2016. [@3] Officers of Lecce's Squadra Mobile, the city police flying
squad, were running an anti-drug service in the Porta Rudiae area. [@3] Shortly
before 19:00 a patrol noticed a white Fiat Punto parked in Via Manifattura
Tabacchi. [@3] Both occupants were already known to the police. [@2]

The officers noticed that the pair behaved nervously. [@3] They then extended
the inspection to the inside of the vehicle. [@3] In the woman's bag they found
a transparent cellophane wrapper with about 383 grams of marijuana. [@3] The
Montenegrin account of the case put the amount in Krušević's bag at 1,380 grams.
[@1] Police said the Porta Rudiae area had long been used for drug dealing and
that residents had made numerous complaints about addicts and pushers. [@2]

Both were arrested on suspicion of possession of drugs for the purpose of
trafficking. [@3] The two Montenegrins had lived for some time at the Panareo
camp in Lecce. [@2] Judge Giovanni Gallo validated the arrest but released the
pair. [@3] The couple admitted the charge and said they were sorry. [@3]
