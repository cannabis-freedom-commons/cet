Police in Istanbul detained 33 people in a raid against drug sellers across
seven districts of the city. [@1] The operation covered Zeytinburnu, Avcılar,
Bayrampaşa, Esenyurt, Büyükçekmece, Fatih and Ataşehir. [@1] Twenty-one of those
arrested were citizens of Turkmenistan. [@1] The detentions were made by
drug-enforcement units on suspicion of selling drugs. [@2]

Officers seized 1.5 kg of marijuana in the raid. [@1] Police also seized 3 kg of
heroin, 149 grams of methamphetamine and 300 grams of ecstasy, none of which is
counted in the cannabis figures. [@1]

The identities of the arrested Turkmen citizens and their subsequent fate were
not reported. [@1] Radio Azatlyk was unable to obtain any information or comment
from the Turkmen consulate in Istanbul. [@1]
