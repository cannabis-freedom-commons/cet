Two brothers were arrested in Mulanje district in southern Malawi on suspicion
of cultivating cannabis sativa, locally known as chamba. [@1] Wyson Simeon, 58,
and Sankhani Simeon, 53, of Mwanero village under Traditional Authority Nkanda
in the district, were arrested on Monday 18 April 2016. [@1]

Mulanje police public relations officer Gresham Ngwira said the two had planted
the crop in a fence behind their houses. [@1] Ngwira said officers from Chambe
police unit went to both places after a tip from well-wishers and found the
plants. [@1] Wyson was found with 65 plants and Sankhani with 117, 182 plants in
all. [@1] The plants were sent to Vumbwe Research Station for examination. [@1]

The brothers were to be arraigned on a charge of cultivating cannabis sativa
contrary to Section 6 of the Dangerous Drugs Act. [@1] Cultivation or selling of
chamba is prohibited by law, while the government was running trials on
industrial hemp. [@1]
