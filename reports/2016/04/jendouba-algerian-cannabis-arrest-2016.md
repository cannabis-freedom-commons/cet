Jendouba police arrested a man arriving from Algeria on 12 April 2016. [@1] He
had cannabis and Tunisian dinar money in his possession. [@1] He was in a car
and refused to obey a security patrol, which had to chase him. [@1]

Officers forced him to stop and, searching the vehicle, found cannabis (zatla)
and a large sum of money. [@1] The trafficker is Algerian and admitted
contacting Tunisians to whom he sold small quantities of cannabis for their
personal consumption. [@1] Police kept him in detention and opened an
investigation. [@1]
