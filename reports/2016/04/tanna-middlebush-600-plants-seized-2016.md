Police on Tanna seized more than 600 marijuana plants in a raid on cultivation.
[@1] The Vanuatu Police Force said the plants came from the Middlebush area of
the island. [@1] The raid was described as the biggest on marijuana cultivation
by the Tafea Police Patrol. [@2]

The plants covered an area about the size of a football field and stood between
five and eight feet (1.5 to 2.4 metres) tall. [@2] Police seized the plants
earlier in April 2016. [@2] Cannabis cultivation and use had become an
increasing concern in Vanuatu in the years before the raid. [@2] Possession and
use of marijuana is a serious crime in Vanuatu carrying a fine or up to 20 years
in prison. [@2]
