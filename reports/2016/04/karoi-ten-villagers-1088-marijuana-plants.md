Ten villagers in Karoi, Zimbabwe, were arrested for growing marijuana. [@1] One
of the ten was a juvenile; the other nine were identified as Talent Walapa, 23,
Tarisai Chisango, 24, Jane Chitupe, 23, Ellen Changa, 26, Tebes Sirika, Talent
Tambala, 24, Easyman Bikausaru, 40, Tonderai Kapamhare, 38, and Fanuel Karima.
[@1]

National police spokesperson Senior Assistant Commissioner Charity Charamba
confirmed the arrests and said the suspects would soon appear in court. [@2]
Charamba said the ten people were nabbed in Karoi after patrolling members of
the Zimbabwe Republic Police stationed at Karoi Rural Police Station came across
1 088 plants. [@1] The crop was found during the patrol by officers of the
Zimbabwe Republic Police stationed at Karoi Rural Police Station. [@1] The case
was at the stage where the suspects were due to appear in court over the growing
of the plants. [@1]
