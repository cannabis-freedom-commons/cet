Unknown criminals dumped 550 kilograms of mbanje, also called dagga, at
Granville Cemetery in Harare, an area popularly known as Kumbudzi. [@1] Police
said the drug was believed to have been smuggled into the country. [@1] Chief
police spokesperson Senior Assistant Commissioner Charity Charamba confirmed the
discovery and said investigations were still in progress. [@1] The ZRP said it
was investigating a case in which police recovered 11 bags of 50 kg of dagga at
Granville Cemetery in Harare. [@1] Officers acted on a tip-off and went to the
scene, where they found the bags dumped in the area. [@1] Charamba asked anyone
with information that might assist the investigation to contact a nearby police
station or the national complaints desk. [@1]

Police said this was not the first time they had recovered or intercepted large
quantities of mbanje in and around the city. [@1] Most of it was believed to
have been smuggled into Zimbabwe through Mozambique from Malawi, and several
suspects had been arrested and prosecuted in separate incidents in past years.
