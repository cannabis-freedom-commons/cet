Police in Kathmandu district destroyed nearly 4,000 marijuana plants at various
places on Friday 6 May 2016. [@1] Law-enforcement personnel carried out the
destruction in cooperation with local residents. [@1] The published account of
the operation appeared in Kathmandu on the evening of 7 May 2016. [@1] The
figure of nearly 4,000 plants is the only quantity given for the district
operation. [@1]

Part of the clearance took place in Budhanilakantha-5. [@1] Part of the
clearance took place in Kashinkali. [@1] Part of the clearance took place in
Tokha-1. [@1] The remaining clearances took place in Shankarapur-4 and
Shankarapur-5. [@1] Together the named wards and localities span several parts
of Kathmandu district rather than a single field. [@1] The growth cleared at
these places was described as weed plants, meaning marijuana. [@1]

The plants destroyed throughout the operation were identified as marijuana. [@1]
The operation relied on joint action by police and local residents across the
named places. [@1] The operation's various places all lay within Kathmandu
district. [@1]
