Police in the Northern Town arrested two male athletes in Luganville, Santo, on
Saturday 17 December 2016 after confiscating 10 joints of marijuana from their
pockets. [@1] The arrests were made during regular checks at the gate of the
Luganville Soccer City Stadium. [@1] Police confirmed the detentions and
described the two men only as male athletes. [@1]

The joints were taken directly from the athletes' pockets during the
stadium-gate check, rather than in a search of a home or vehicle. [@1] No weight
was given for the 10 joints, rolled marijuana cigarettes, and police published
no estimate of their value. [@1] The check was described as regular, and no
search warrant, targeted operation or prior surveillance was mentioned. [@1]

Nothing was reported about any charge, caution or later court step for the two
athletes. [@1] The two men were not named, and no ages, clubs or teams were
given. [@1]
