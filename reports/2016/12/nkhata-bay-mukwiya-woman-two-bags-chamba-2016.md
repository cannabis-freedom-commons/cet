Police in Nkhata Bay arrested a 34-year-old woman on 2 December 2016 after
officers found two bags of loose cannabis sativa on a Marcopolo bus at the
Mukwiya roadblock. [@1] The bus conductor told the officers that the two bags
belonged to the woman, Angella Banda, who had boarded the bus at Dwangwa on her
way to Mzuzu. [@1]

Police seized the two bags and kept Banda in custody. [@1] She was expected to
appear in court on a charge of being found in possession of cannabis sativa
under section 4a and regulation 19 of Malawi's Dangerous Drugs Act. [@1] Banda
comes from Nsomba village in Traditional Authority Mabuka, Mulanje district.
