Algeria's National Office for the Fight against Drugs and Drug Addiction (ONLDT)
reported that more than 93,052 kg of cannabis resin were seized in the country
in the first ten months of 2016. [@1] Of that total, 68,364 kg, or 73.47
percent, was seized in the west of the country. [@1] The office drew the tally
from the results of three services: the National Gendarmerie, the General
Directorate of National Security (DGSN) and the customs service. [@1] The south
accounted for 19.21 percent of the seized resin, the centre 5.72 percent and the
east 1.60 percent. [@1] A source close to the office said the ten-month resin
total was 9.90 percent lower than a year earlier, mainly because security had
been tightened at the borders. [@1] Algérie360 linked the concentration of
seizures in the west to the region's proximity to the Moroccan border. [@2]

The office counted 31,362 people implicated in drug cases during the ten months,
170 of them foreigners, and said 791 were on the run. [@1] Among those
implicated, 6,295 were cannabis resin traffickers, the office said. [@1] The
office recorded 25,186 drug cases in the period, against 15,393 in the same
months of 2015, a rise of 63.62 percent. [@1] Eleven of the cases concerned the
cultivation of cannabis and opium. [@1]

The same report recorded 946,047 psychotropic tablets seized, nearly double the
498,795 of the same period of 2015; these are not counted as cannabis. [@1]
Cocaine seizures fell 35.73 percent to 54,732.57 g from 85,156.45 g a year
earlier. [@1] Heroin seizures reached 1,370.35 g, down 45.94 percent from
2,534.8 g. [@1]

El Khabar reported the same ten-month total of more than 93 tonnes of cannabis
seized in Algeria. [@3] The office also said 9,650 drug users received medical
and therapeutic care in the first half of 2016. [@1]
