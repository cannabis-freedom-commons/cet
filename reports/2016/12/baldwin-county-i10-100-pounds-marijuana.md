Daniel Oehl, 45, of Fort Pierce, Florida, was arrested on suspicion of
trafficking multiple drugs. [@1] A Drug Task Force and DEA agents made the stop
on Interstate 10 in Baldwin County, Alabama. [@1] K-9 Officer Aron detected the
marijuana, which had been vacuum-packed in triple layers. [@1]

Investigators said Oehl hid more than 100 pounds of marijuana in suitcases,
duffle bags and boxes inside a rented SUV. [@1] Sheriff Huey Mack Jr. identified
the arrested man as Daniel Oehl. [@1] Mack said the shipment was worth almost a
million dollars. [@1] The marijuana was labelled with strains such as "bubble
gum" and "purple mango", names associated with higher-quality marijuana. [@1]
Officers also seized two vials of liquid LSD weighing 28 grams, which is not
counted in the cannabis figures. [@1]

Investigators said Oehl was believed to be a high-level distributor, possibly
linked to an operation out of Arizona. [@1] Mack said all indicators were that
the marijuana was grown domestically in the western United States. [@1] Oehl
faces up to five years in prison, a mandatory sentence if convicted, and may
also face federal charges. [@1]
