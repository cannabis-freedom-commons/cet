Macau's Judiciary Police (PJ) said three local men were arrested on Wednesday in
one of the cases, all of them local residents. [@1] The force announced the
arrests at a special press conference, saying six people were arrested in total
on suspicion of trafficking and taking drugs in three separate cases. [@1] The
case involving the three local men was described by police as cross-border drug
trafficking. [@1]

The spokesperson said police had received a tip-off that members of a
cross-border drug trafficking gang were planning to smuggle a kilogramme of
marijuana into Macau. [@1] After investigating, police identified the buyers of
the marijuana as two local brothers surnamed Kou, aged 27 and 31. [@1] A dealer
surnamed Wong was also identified by police, according to the spokesperson. [@1]

Judiciary Police officers escorted the three local suspects of the cross-border
drug trafficking case to a vehicle from the PJ headquarters in Zape. [@1]
