Police in Sint Maarten arrested a 16-year-old secondary-school student with the
initials E.C. on L.B. Scot road at about noon on 17 February 2016. [@1] Officers
had been called because the student and some friends were smoking drugs in the
area. [@2]

A search of the student's school bag during the arrest found four small plastic
bags, each containing an amount of marihuana, and a brass knuckle. [@2] The
marihuana and the weapon were confiscated for further investigation. [@1]

The student was taken to the Philipsburg police station for questioning. [@1]
The Police Force of Sint Maarten again advised parents to monitor their children
and their activities during and after school hours. [@2] Police urged parents to
watch for changes in behaviour that could indicate that a child was using
marihuana. [@2] The police notice set out physical changes and changes in
actions as signs of marihuana use. [@1]
