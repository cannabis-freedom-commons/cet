Police arrested a McKnight man after a joint operation before dawn on 13
February 2016 found a quantity of marijuana in the area. [@1] The operation was
carried out by the Special Services Unit, the St. Kitts-Nevis Defence Force and
a K-9 team from the Customs and Excise Department. [@1] Officers searched known
locations in Salt Pond Alley, Malone Avenue, Cunningham Street and Upper and
Lower Shaw Avenues and ran foot patrols in McKnight. [@2]

Fifteen abandoned properties were searched during the operation and no illegal
items were found in them. [@1] A quantity of cannabis was found at the Malone
Avenue property of Crispin Crossley. [@1] Crossley was taken into police custody
and charged with possession of cannabis and possession of cannabis with intent
to supply. [@1]

Police said the operation was one of many carried out in recent days on both St.
Kitts and Nevis. [@1] Commissioner of Police Ian Queeley praised the officers
for their vigilance over the preceding weeks. [@1] Queeley said initiatives that
increase police presence in communities result in less criminal activity and
less fear of crime. [@1] He also urged the public to keep supporting the police
in their efforts to make communities safer. [@1]
