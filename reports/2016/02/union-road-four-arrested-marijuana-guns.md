Four men were arrested and held in custody for further investigation on drugs
and firearm charges. [@1] The arrests took place on 18 February 2016 at about
12:00 p.m. during a general control by the Multi Disciplinary team on Union Road
near the border. [@1] Officers stopped a private vehicle with a French licence
plate after smelling a strong odour of marihuana from inside the car. [@1]

A search of the vehicle found two plastic bags each containing a considerable
amount of compressed marihuana, which was confiscated. [@1] The four suspects,
aged 33, 25, 19 and 15, were arrested on the spot and taken to the Philipsburg
Police Station. [@1] The vehicle was also confiscated for further investigation.
[@1]

A later search at the police station found three loaded handguns under the
centre console between the driver and passenger seats, and they were
confiscated. [@1] The youngest suspect was 15 years old, and police urged
parents to pay close attention to their children and their friends. [@1]
