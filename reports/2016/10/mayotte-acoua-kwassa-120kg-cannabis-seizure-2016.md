A radar echo detected a vessel entering Mayotte's territorial waters from
Anjouan early in the morning. [@1] The boat was a kwassa, the craft used for
crossings between Anjouan, in the Comoros, and Mayotte, and police chased it as
it came in. [@1] It came ashore at Acoua, a commune in the north of Mayotte,
with three people aboard. [@1]

Officers seized 120 kilograms of cannabis from the boat. [@1] Comores Infos
called the haul a large seizure, and a rare one for Mayotte. [@1] Kwassas
carrying illicit goods are generally intercepted at night, the outlet said,
which leaves the smugglers time to throw the cargo overboard before they land.
[@1] The seizure was made in waters that the Comorian site describes as those of
the Comorian island of Mayotte. [@1]

The three occupants of the boat reached land and got away, and no arrest was
reported. [@1]
