An Israeli who manages the Overstay guesthouse in Bangkok, a hostel popular with
Israeli backpackers, was arrested on Friday 28 October 2016 on suspicion of drug
possession. [@1] Thai police and soldiers raided the guesthouse, and eight
backpackers staying there were also arrested on suspicion of drug use. [@1] The
manager, from Jerusalem, was arrested with 22 grams of marijuana. [@1]

The raid followed complaints about noise and smoke coming from the building,
where security forces found 25 foreigners from Britain, Germany, Hungary,
Russia, Spain, Israel and China. [@1] All the backpackers were asked for urine
samples; six of those detained tested positive for marijuana and one refused the
test, police said. [@1]

Bangkok police said the Israeli would be charged with drug possession and intent
to distribute. [@1] On his Facebook page the manager wrote that hundreds of
police had arrived at the guesthouse, that they were attempting to charge him
with drug trafficking, and that he hoped to be released on bail shortly. [@1]
Israel's Foreign Ministry said it was aware of the case and that its consul in
Thailand would offer assistance. [@1]
