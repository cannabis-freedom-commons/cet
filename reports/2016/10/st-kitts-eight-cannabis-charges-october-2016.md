The police force published a report of arrests, convictions and other activities
recorded for 3 to 5 October 2016. [@1] Patricia Marsham of Godwin Ghaut was
arrested, formally charged and cautioned for possession of cannabis. [@1]
Jonathan Marsham of Godwin Ghaut was arrested, formally charged and cautioned
for possession of cannabis. [@1] Varon Shane Cole of Brick Kiln was arrested,
formally charged and cautioned for possession of cannabis and possession with
intent to supply. [@1] George Ambrose Duchene of Godwin Ghaut was arrested,
formally charged and cautioned for possession of cannabis, possession with
intent to supply and cultivation of cannabis. [@1] Franklyn Thompson of Keys was
arrested, formally charged and cautioned for possession of cannabis and
possession with intent to supply. [@1] Iwana Bradley of Keys was arrested,
formally charged and cautioned for possession of cannabis and possession with
intent to supply. [@1] Shadique Hendrickson of Keys Village was arrested,
formally charged and cautioned for possession of cannabis and possession with
intent to supply. [@1] Sherianne Govia of Davis Road, Phillips Village was
arrested and charged with possession of cannabis and possession with intent to
supply. [@1]

Franklyn Thompson was granted bail of $5,000 for appearance at Basseterre
Magistrate Court on 7 November 2016. [@1] Iwanja Bradley was granted bail of
$10,000 for appearance at Basseterre Magistrate Court on 7 November 2016. [@1]
George Duchene was granted bail of $25,000 to appear at Dieppe Bay Magistrate
Court on 7 November 2016. [@1] Shadique Hendrickson was granted bail of $5,000
for appearance at Basseterre Magistrate Court on 7 November. [@1] Sherianne
Govia was granted bail of $5,000 to appear at Dieppe Bay Magistrate Court on 7
November 2016. [@1]

Police said they executed a number of search warrants during the period in
Phillip's Bourryeaux Village, Brick Kiln, Godwin Ghaut, New Road Housing and New
Castle. [@1] The force said it continued operations in support of Operation Safe
Streets, a combined law enforcement operation aimed at removing illegal
firearms, ammunition, drugs and instruments that could be used to commit crimes.
[@1] The same report recorded other, non-cannabis matters, including an arrest
for malicious damage. [@1]
