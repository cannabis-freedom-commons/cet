Three people were arrested Saturday evening in Fairfield on suspicion of selling
marijuana and illegally possessing a gun, police said Sunday. [@1] At about 7:30
p.m. two officers walked up to a suspicious vehicle parked in the 2400 block of
Beaufort Drive. [@1] Police said inside were three people who allegedly had
several mason jars containing marijuana. [@1]

23-year-old Suisun City resident Kenny Reed was taken into custody after he
allegedly tried to get away from officers. [@1] Police said they found evidence
of marijuana sales inside the vehicle as well as a loaded 9mm handgun under the
driver's seat. [@1]

Police also arrested two passengers identified as 23-year-old Vallejo resident
Jordan Scott and 22-year-old Fairfield resident Latrail White. [@1] All three
were arrested on suspicion of possessing marijuana for sale and possessing a
loaded and concealed gun. [@1] Reed was also arrested on suspicion of resisting
arrest and Scott for violating his probation. [@1]
