Anti-drug units of the Bolivarian National Police intercepted a speedboat at San
José de Los Cayos, in Píritu municipality, Falcón state. [@1,5,6] The operation took
place on the night of Sunday 29 June 2025, in waters off the Falcón coast. [@2]
The PNB teams came from its Aquatic Spaces Police Directorate and its Anti-Drug
Directorate, within the government's Territorial Defensive System. [@2] The operations involved officers of the Bolivarian National Guard and the Bolivarian National Police. [@6] The crew
escaped and no arrests were reported. [@1,5] Officers recovered a 44-foot
fibreglass launch, four 200-horsepower Yamaha outboards and a phone. [@1,5]

Police seized 107 bales of suspected marijuana weighing 3,161 kilograms. [@1,5] Authorities presented the Apure and Píritu, Falcón seizures together as 3.4 tonnes of marijuana. [@6] In Las Busacas, Puerto Páez, Apure, officers found 300 kilograms of marijuana. [@6]
Falcón's citizen-security secretariat, which supervised the recount and chemical
analysis, put the load at 5,601 panela packages and called it the state's
second-largest seizure in ten years. [@2]

Interior Minister Diosdado Cabello announced the Falcón seizure at a press
conference on 1 July 2025 and put the haul at 3,171 kilograms. [@4] Cabello said
the operation was linked to a “conspiracy” he attributed to the opposition led
by María Corina Machado. [@4,6] He said the seizures came from information taken
from seized phones. [@4] Authorities said investigations continued to identify
those involved in moving the loads. [@3] The seized substances were placed at the order of the Public Prosecutor's Office for analysis and prosecution. [@6]
