The Compagnie des Archipels of the national gendarmerie carried out a
coordinated anti-drug operation across Tahaa, Raiatea, Rangiroa and Raivavae
with its territorial units and support from Flottille 35F. [@2] On Tahaa, troops
were dropped by helicopter into areas that were difficult to access. [@1] At
Rangiroa, boats were deployed to reach isolated motu. [@1] Nine people were
intercepted during the island operations. [@1]

On Tahaa, gendarmes destroyed 199 pakalolo plants, according to gendarmerie
figures. [@1] On Raiatea, the same helicopter system located and destroyed 113
more plants. [@1] On Rangiroa, 183 plants were eradicated. [@2] At Raivavae in
the Austral Islands, 174 plants were located, seized and destroyed. [@3] In
total, 669 plants were destroyed on the four islands. [@1] During a road check
linked to the operation, officers also seized 138 sticks and 5 pouches, already
packaged. [@1]

Judicial proceedings were underway after the seizures. [@1] The gendarmerie said
the actions showed its commitment against illicit crops, even in isolated areas
of French Polynesia. [@1]
