Police destroyed about 600 kilograms of counterfeit cigarettes and cannabis
outside Port Moresby on 25 June 2025. [@1] The exhibits had been confiscated by
the National Drugs and Vice Squad and the Waigani district court. [@1] NBC PNG
put the total at more than 600 kilograms of cannabis and counterfeit cigarette
exhibits. [@2] Director Drugs and Vice Joel Simatab, Deputy Commissioner of
Police Special Operations Donald Yamasombi, squad members, a Waigani Court House
sheriff, the National Censorship Office and the media watched the burning. [@2]

The exhibits had been seized between 2022 and 2025. [@2] They were confiscated
from users and smugglers in the National Capital District, Central and Gulf
provinces. [@2] Detective Sergeant Thomas Moiyang, the officer in charge, said
most of the substances came from the three areas of Goilala in Central province.
[@1] He named Tapini, Woitape and Guari. [@1] Police said the substances were
flown into the city by Air Niugini, Tropic Air and Air Sanga. [@1] Some of the
marijuana destroyed had been left unattended at Jackson International Airport,
and other batches were collected on separate dates. [@1] The cigarettes had been
brought in by people who failed to declare them on arrival. [@1]

Moiyang said most people involved in transporting such drugs were police
officers, Papua New Guinea Defence Force soldiers and Correctional Service
officers in government vehicles. [@1] Yamasombi said the constabulary was
considering a proper incinerator. [@1] He said destroying drugs in an open space
was dangerous for the community and for those carrying out the destruction. [@1]
He said officers facilitating the burning should move to higher ground so they
would not inhale the fumes. [@1] He said synthetic drugs could not be destroyed
in an open place like that. [@1]
