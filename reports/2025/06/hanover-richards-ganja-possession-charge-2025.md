A taxi operator was charged with possession of and dealing in ganja following a
police operation on the Ramble main road in Hanover on Friday. [@2] The person
charged is 51-year-old Orville Richards, of South Sea Park, White House,
Westmoreland. [@1]

Reports from the Lucea police say that about 5:30 pm lawmen were in the area
when they saw Richards driving a Nissan AD wagon along the roadway. [@1] He was
signalled to stop, however, he disobeyed the police and sped off. [@1] Richards
was pursued and the vehicle was intercepted. [@1] He was accosted and searched,
and a search of the vehicle was also conducted, the police said. [@1] During the
search, one plastic bag with ganja was reportedly found in his possession. [@1]

Richards was subsequently taken into custody and charged following an interview
in the presence of his attorney. [@1] His court date has not been finalised.
