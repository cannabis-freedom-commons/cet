Lawmen from the Jamaica Constabulary Force's Firearms and Narcotics
Investigation Division seized and destroyed over 1,500 pounds (about 680 kg) of
ganja in Northampton district, St Elizabeth, on Thursday 5 June 2025. [@2]
Officers carried out the operation between 10 a.m. and 1 p.m. [@1]

Police said the ganja had been cut and left lying in a field to dry. [@1] The
officers destroyed the ganja after seizing it. [@2]

No one was arrested in connection with the seizure. [@1] The constabulary force
said its investigation was continuing. [@2]
