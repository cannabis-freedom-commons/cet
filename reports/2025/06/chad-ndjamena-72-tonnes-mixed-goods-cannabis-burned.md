On 17 June 2025 the N'Djamena city hall, with Customs, the DGRI, the national
police and gendarmerie, incinerated prohibited products at Gassi in the 7th
arrondissement. [@1] APA confirms the operation was led by the N'Djamena
municipality with Customs, the DGRI, the national police and the national
gendarmerie. [@2]

Authorities said 72 tonnes of illegal merchandise with an estimated value of 12
billion FCFA were destroyed. [@1] The destroyed lot included adulterated
alcohol, cannabis, tramadol and dangerous medicines seized in targeted
anti-smuggling and drug-trafficking operations. [@1] APA says the products had
been seized in several operations across the country against smuggling,
pharmaceutical crime and drug trafficking. [@2] Xinhua likewise reports 72
tonnes of narcotics and prohibited medicines valued at 12 billion CFA francs,
seized by customs and security services. [@3]

Security ministry secretary-general Benguela Guindjinga said arrested
traffickers and users would be brought to justice. [@1] Prosecutor Oumar Mahamat
Kedelaye recalled that importing, selling and consuming these products is
strictly prohibited and promised firm prosecution. [@1] N'Djamena mayor Senoussi
Hassana Abdoulaye said the city was fully mobilised to curb the circulation,
sale and consumption of the products. [@1]
