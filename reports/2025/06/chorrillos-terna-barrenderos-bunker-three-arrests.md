Agents of Grupo Terna, a unit of Peru's National Police, raided a drug bunker in
Chorrillos, a district in southern Lima. [@1] Three people were detained in the
operation. [@1] The officers had infiltrated the district dressed as street
sweepers to approach the bunker, which sold coca paste and marijuana. [@1]

Police identified the detainees by the aliases Chitara, Lulú and Garganta de
Lata, aged 34, 54 and 42. [@1] Police linked the group to the criminal band Las
Sonámbulas del Alto Perú. [@1] All three were taken to the Depincri
Barranco-Chorrillos to face drug-trafficking charges. [@1] Supported by drones,
the agents watched buyers reach a house on Girón Santa Rita. [@1] Buyers paid
through a slit in the gate and received the drugs without the traffickers
showing themselves. [@1] When officers stopped one buyer, he confirmed the
location of the sales point. [@1]

Police said the property was an active distribution centre trading day and
night. [@1] The search seized 300 wrappings of marijuana and 1,500 packages of
coca paste, which is not counted in the cannabis figures. [@1] The drugs were
hidden in several places in the building, including the roof where the band's
dogs lived. [@1] Officers also found a firearm, which police cited as a sign of
the band's dangerousness. [@1] Grupo Terna is a specialised unit of the National
Police focused on organised crime, particularly drug trafficking. [@1] It
coordinates with specialised units including Dirandro, the anti-drug
directorate. [@1]
