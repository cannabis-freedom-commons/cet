Obvious Ngwenya, 27, of Emakhandeni, Bulawayo, boarded a pirate taxi from Beitbridge to Bulawayo with a parcel wrapped in a light blue comforter that he paid the driver to carry separately. [@1,2]

At the 129 km peg on the Bulawayo-Beitbridge road, police stopped the vehicle at a routine roadblock and found 33 cobs of dagga weighing 19.01 kilograms hidden in black plastic bags in his parcel. [@1,2]

He appeared before Magistrate Progress Murandu at the Gwanda Provincial Magistrates Court on charges of dealing in dangerous drugs and was sentenced to 10 years in prison. [@1,2]
