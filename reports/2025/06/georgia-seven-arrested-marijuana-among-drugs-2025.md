Officers of the Central Criminal Police Department's main division against
illegal drug trafficking arrested seven people on drug charges. [@3] The
Interior Ministry reported the arrests on 10 June 2025. [@1] The seven, aged 24
to 54, included citizens of Georgia, Ukraine, Israel and Egypt. [@2] They were
accused of illegally buying and keeping large and particularly large quantities
of drugs, preparing drugs for sale, and unlawfully possessing marijuana and a
psychotropic substance. [@1] The offences carry up to 20 years or life in
prison. [@1]

They seized large and particularly large quantities of drugs, including cocaine,
Subutex, methadone, buprenorphine, codeine, Lyrica and marijuana. [@1] Officers
also seized materials for packaging drugs, digital scales, adhesive tape and
polyethylene bags. [@1]

The investigation is under articles 260, 18-260, 261 and 273 prima of Georgia's
Criminal Code. [@2]
