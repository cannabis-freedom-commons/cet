During the previous week, La Araucanía Region was the scene of a series of
police operations led by the Investigative Police (PDI) and Carabineros. [@1]
The PDI dismantled the criminal gang Los Fritz and seized 107 kilos of marijuana
in what the outlet called one of the most important seizures of recent weeks in
the region. [@1]

Regional security chief Israel Campusano, the SEREMI for Public Security,
welcomed the results of the operations. [@1] Campusano said the week showed that
when the State acts there are results, with gangs broken up, thousands of drug
doses taken out of circulation and stolen vehicles recovered. [@1] The Interior
and Public Security Ministry said the operations were part of a regional
intervention strategy centred on police intelligence, timely tactical action and
field work to protect communities in La Araucanía. [@1]
