The Drug Enforcement Administration led a large-scale raid on Wednesday 18 June 2025
against three illegal marijuana grow sites covering nearly 800 acres around Thermal in
the Coachella Valley. [@1] The DEA said it held federal search warrants signed by a
magistrate judge before the operation in the Thermal area, which targeted 787 acres
of illegally grown marijuana. [@2] About 500 officers and troops took part, including
the National Guard, the ATF, Immigration and Customs Enforcement, Customs and Border
Patrol and the FBI. [@2] The DEA said the size and scope of the sites prompted help
from the FBI, the IRS, the US Army, immigration enforcement and the Guard. [@1]

At least 70 undocumented workers were arrested in the raids, and one US citizen was
taken into custody for interfering with law enforcement; during one search officers
found two people locked inside a trailer. [@1] Another account puts the number held
at as many as 75 undocumented immigrants plus the one citizen, with all being
questioned about their possible involvement in the farms. [@2]
