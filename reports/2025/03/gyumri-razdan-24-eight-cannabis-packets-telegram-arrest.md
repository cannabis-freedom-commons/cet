Police detained a 24-year-old resident of Razdan on 26 March 2025 after
information that he was placing drug packets around Gyumri for sale through
Telegram. [@1] Officers detained him at about 17:15 at the junction of Ozanyan
and Sahmanapah streets in Gyumri and took him to the Shirak regional police
department. [@2] In his backpack police found eight packets tied with adhesive
tape. [@1] He said the packets held cannabis that he had collected in Yerevan
through a Telegram channel in order to place it in Gyumri. [@1]

A search on Mayakovsky Street recovered three packets he said he had already
placed, at the spots he indicated. [@1] The backpack also held a yellow-green
mass and a syringe with burnt traces. [@1]

The Shirak regional investigative department opened a criminal case over illegal
circulation of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances for the purpose of
sale. [@1] The 24-year-old was taken to the detainees' holding facility and the
pre-trial investigation continues. [@3]
