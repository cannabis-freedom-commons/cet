The National Police in São Vicente detained eight people on Friday 28 March 2025
during a mega-operation. [@1] Three of the detentions were for possession of a
product presumed to be cannabis, locally known as padjinha. [@1] One person was
detained in flagrante for illegal possession of a firearm. [@1]

Officers seized 67 wraps of cannabis, a firearm, a magazine, five rounds of
ammunition, three knives and a certain amount of money. [@1] Three people were
detained in flagrante for illegal driving and one for driving under the
influence of alcohol. [@1] Police approached 197 people on the public road and
searched all of them. [@1]

Eleven people were taken to police units for identification. [@1] The operation
involved 57 officers from various branches of the São Vicente command, supported
by nine vehicles. [@1] Police said the mega-operation aimed to prevent crimes
and increase citizens’ sense of security. [@1]
