Security services in Aïn Témouchent wilaya dismantled an organised criminal
network specialising in illicit drug trafficking and seized nearly a quintal of
cannabis originating in Morocco. [@1] The Directorate General of National
Security announced the operation in a communiqué published on Saturday. [@1]
Investigators acted on information that the network was about to move a large
drug cargo from a western border wilaya through Aïn Témouchent towards the
northern wilayas. [@1]

Judicial police officers arrested the main suspect in the town of Aïn Sefra, in
Naâma wilaya. [@1] Officers seized 96 kg of cannabis hidden in crates used to
transport and store vegetables. [@1]

The suspect was brought before the public prosecutor at Aïn Témouchent court on
charges of illicit importation of narcotics, transporting narcotics for illicit
trafficking in an organised criminal gang, and smuggling that threatens the
national economy and public health. [@1]
