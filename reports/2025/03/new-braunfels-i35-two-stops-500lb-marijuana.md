The New Braunfels Police Department seized marijuana and THC products in two
traffic stops on Interstate 35 over the weekend of 28–30 March 2025. [@1] In the
first stop, on Friday 28 March, officers arrested four people from Corpus
Christi after finding more than 1,300 grams of THC cartridges. [@1] All four
were taken to the Comal County Jail and each charged with manufacture/delivery
of a controlled substance. [@1] The next day, Saturday 29 March, a second stop
on the same highway led to the discovery of more than 500 pounds (about 227
kilograms) of marijuana. [@2] The driver, a resident of Choctaw, Oklahoma, was
on his way to San Antonio when he was stopped in New Braunfels. [@2] He was
taken to the Comal County Jail and charged with possession of marijuana. [@2]

The stops were made by the department's Street Crimes Unit. [@3] Police said
each case remained under investigation and that additional charges could be
pending. [@2]
