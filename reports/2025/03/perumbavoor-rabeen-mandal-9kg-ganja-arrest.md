Ernakulam Rural police arrested migrant worker Rabeen Mandal with 9 kg of ganja
in Perumbavoor under Operation Clean Perumbavoor. [@2] Rabeen Mandal, 24, of
Raipur in Murshidabad, West Bengal, was apprehended by a team led by Perumbavoor
ASP Shakthi Singh Arya. [@2] In March 2025, police arrested Rabeen Mandal, a
native of West Bengal, with 9kg of ganja in Perumbavoor. [@1]

The raid was conducted in a Bengali colony, where the cannabis was found hidden
in secret compartments in a room. [@2] The arrest came after a college student,
Rabeen’s customer, disclosed details about the supplier. [@2] Operating under
the alias ‘Robin Bhai’, Rabeen allegedly sold ganja in small packets for Rs 500
each. [@2] According to the police, Mandal allegedly went by the name 'Robin
Bhai' and sourced the contraband from Odisha. [@3] The police also seized Rs
10,000, suspected to be proceeds from the sale, during the raid. [@3]

He worked at a hotel before shifting to drug trade. [@2] Having arrived in
Kerala a year ago, he worked in Thiruvananthapuram before moving to Perumbavoor,
where he lived in Bhai Colony. [@2]
