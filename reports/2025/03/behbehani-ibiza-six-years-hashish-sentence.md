Poker player and DJ Salman Behbehani, 37, was convicted of drug smuggling in
Spain and sentenced to six years in prison. [@2] The Balearic Provincial Court
imposed a EUR 30,000 fine alongside the prison term. [@2] He had initially faced
nine years in prison and a EUR 53,000 fine before the court settled at six
years. [@1]

The case began on 16 July 2021, when Behbehani was arrested at Ibiza airport
after flying in from Las Vegas on a private jet carrying around 30 people. [@2]
Officers from the Tax Agency and Guardia Civil Border Patrol scanned the
suitcase at the airport and found drugs and cash. [@2] Investigators listed 705
grams of hashish among the substances, alongside cocaine, Tusi, MDMA, some
cannabis and LSD. [@2] GipsyTeam's account of the sentence also reported 705
grams of hashish and smaller quantities of cannabis. [@3]

A local report on the 2021 seizure from La Voz de Ibiza gave a different
breakdown: 543 grams of cannabis and no hashish, with other substance amounts
also differing. [@4] The whole haul was valued at more than EUR 26,500, a
combined figure that is not specific to the cannabis products. [@1] Police also
found cash in several currencies in the suitcase, raising suspicions that it was
undeclared property. [@1] Behbehani can appeal the conviction to the Civil and
Criminal Chamber of the Superior Court of Justice of the Balearic Islands. [@2]
