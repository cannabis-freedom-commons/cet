Honduras's armed forces, together with the public prosecutor's office, secured a
plantation in the Tocoa area of Colón department in March 2025. [@2] During
long-range patrols the armed forces also seized a marijuana field in Tocoa
covering one manzana of land with 1,000 marijuana bushes. [@1] The armed forces
placed the marijuana field at the village of Las Cruzitas and said it was found
on the same day as the coca plantation. [@2]

The plantations were seized over the weekend before the 24 March 2025 report:
one with 7,000 coca bushes and another with 1,000 marijuana plants in Colón.
[@1] A military patrol on Botaderos mountain found the 7,000 presumed coca
bushes and the 1,000 marijuana bushes at Las Cruzitas. [@2] Special operations
teams continued reconnaissance missions in several parts of Honduras to identify
and secure more crops. [@1] The armed forces described the seizure as part of
the National Solution Against Crime Plan. [@2] They said the case would be
handled according to law and that the corresponding legal proceedings had been
initiated. [@2]
