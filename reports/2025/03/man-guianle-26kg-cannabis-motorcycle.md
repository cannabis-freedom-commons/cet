Anti-drug gendarmes of the Cellule Anti-Drogue (CAD) in Man were running a
routine roadside check at the Guianlé corridor on the Man–Duékoué road during
the night of 10 March 2025. [@1] The officers spotted a motorcycle whose
behaviour they found suspicious. [@1] The rider refused to stop and a chase
began; he abandoned the motorcycle at the roadside and escaped into the village
of Guianlé. [@1] YECLO reported that the chase covered several kilometres. [@2]

On the abandoned motorcycle the gendarmes found 26 blocks of cannabis weighing
about 26 kilograms. [@1] The motorcycle and its load were seized. [@1] YECLO put
the load's value at several million CFA francs on the local market without
giving a precise figure. [@2] The account came from a note by the Gendarmerie
Nationale. [@1]

No one was arrested; an investigation was opened to find the owner of the cargo
and any accomplices. [@1] YECLO said the Man–Duékoué road is a transit route for
drug networks moving between border areas and major cities. [@2]
