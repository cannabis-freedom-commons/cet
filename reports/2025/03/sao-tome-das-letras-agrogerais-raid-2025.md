Military police in São Tomé das Letras, in the south of Minas Gerais, carried
out operation Agrogerais on Tuesday 25 March 2025. [@1] Police said the
operation dismantled a drug trafficking scheme maintained in the town that also
involved criminal association and corruption of minors. [@1]

During the action officers detained a 17-year-old boy and his 23-year-old
partner in a house used to store and divide drugs. [@1] At the house police
seized portions of marijuana, 250 pinos (small vials) of cocaine and 25 crack
rocks. [@1] Officers also seized two-way radios, precision scales and 1,217
reais in cash. [@1] The marijuana portions were among the drugs held for sale
there; the cocaine and crack seized with them are separate substances. [@1]

The investigation began after an anonymous tip and indicated that an
organisation sent drugs into the town and recruited minors to sell them. [@1]
