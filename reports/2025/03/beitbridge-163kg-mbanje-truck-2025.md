The ZIMRA Canine Unit and other border agencies stopped a South African-registered Hino truck at the Beitbridge Border Post during routine inspections on 2 March 2025. [@1,2]

Sniffer dogs found 163 kilograms of mbanje concealed under blankets and plastic wrapping, valued at US$32,600. [@1,2] The truck was in transit through Zimbabwe to the Democratic Republic of Congo. [@2]

ZIMRA spokesperson Francis Chimanda confirmed the seizure in a statement. [@2] The unnamed South African driver fled and was still sought. [@1]
