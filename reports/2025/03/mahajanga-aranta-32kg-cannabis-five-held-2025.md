The national police's Unité d'Intervention Rapide (UIR) in Mahajanga acted on a
tip about a cannabis seller in the Aranta fokontany. [@2] The operation took
place on the morning of Sunday 16 March 2025 at Terrain Mena, in the Aranta
fokontany of Mahajanga. [@3] Officers arrived around 5:30 am and searched a
suspect's home. [@2] L'Express de Madagascar reported that officers from the
central commissariat caught a group of young people selling cannabis leaf in the
fokontany. [@1] Inside the house they found 165 packets of cannabis weighing
32.5 kg, hidden in charcoal sacks and ready for sale, Midi Madagasikara
reported. [@2]

The national police said a man was arrested for keeping cannabis that officers
found in four large boxes in his house. [@3] Investigators then identified
another man and a woman as accomplices, the police statement said. [@3] It said
the three were arrested and held at the commissariat for investigation into
possession and sale of cannabis. [@3] Midi Madagasikara reported that five
people were placed in garde à vue, police custody, at the central commissariat
in Mahajanga. [@2]

The investigators also arrested the transporters who brought the drug from the
Sofia region, the newspaper reported. [@2] L'Express de Madagascar said five
people, including two women, and the transporters were arrested during the
search. [@1] The cannabis was meant for sale in Mahajanga, with some sent on to
Maintirano, L'Express de Madagascar reported. [@1] The dealers and traffickers
were referred to the prosecutor after the investigation, the newspaper reported.
[@1] Police said the investigation continued to identify other network members
and trace the supply chain, Midi Madagasikara reported. [@2]
