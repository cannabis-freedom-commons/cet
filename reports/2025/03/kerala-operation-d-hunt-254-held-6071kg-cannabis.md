Kerala Police said 254 people were arrested in a statewide special drive held on
Sunday as part of Operation D-Hunt. [@1] Officers checked 5,544 people suspected
of selling drugs and registered 243 cases for possession of banned drugs. [@1]

Police seized 6.071 kilograms of cannabis from the suspects. [@1] In the same
drive police also seized 177 cannabis beedis and 29.1 grams of MDMA, which are
excluded from the cannabis figures. [@1]

Police said the two-day statewide Operation D-Hunt is meant to identify people
involved in storing and marketing banned drugs and to take strict legal action
against them. [@1] The drive is led by State Anti-Narcotics Task Force head and
ADGP (Law and Order) Manoj Abraham on the instructions of State Police Chief Dr
Sheikh Darvesh Sahib, together with district police chiefs. [@1] Police said a
data bank of habitual offenders is being monitored continuously and Operation
D-Hunt will continue in the coming days. [@1] A 24-hour Anti-Narcotic Control
Room at 9497927797 collects drug information from the public for action. [@1]
