Airport officers at Sentani Airport in Jayapura Regency, Papua, detained FS, a
51-year-old housewife, on Saturday 22 March 2025 after she was found carrying
141 packets of cannabis in a bag while checking in at the Lion Air counter. [@1]
She was booked on Lion Air flight JT 985 to Timika, and police said she lives in
Timika. [@1] JPNN named her Florida Sambeka, of Timika in Central Papua, and
said the arrest was on the morning of 22 March. [@2]

Iptu Wajedi, chief of the Sentani Airport sector police, said staff grew
suspicious of the bag as she reported for departure, then opened it and found
141 wrapped packets of cannabis. [@1] Kompas, citing airport
stakeholder-relations head Surya Eka, said aviation security stopped her at the
X-ray passenger checkpoint with 100 small packets and 41 large packets of
cannabis she was taking to Timika. [@3] After she said the goods were a friend's
consignment, a further search found a small packet of cannabis in her trousers,
Surya told Kompas. [@3] She told police she was only a courier and would be paid
once the cannabis reached Timika, JPNN reported. [@2] iNews said the 141 packets
of dried cannabis were 41 medium packets and 100 small ones. [@4]

After questioning she was handed to Sentani Airport sector police, and she and
the evidence were then held at Jayapura district police headquarters for the
narcotics criminal investigation unit to process. [@1] Wajedi said this was the
fourth such case uncovered at Sentani Airport. [@1] Aviation security
coordinated with military personnel assigned to the airport and the airport
operations unit before handing her and the evidence to airport police, the
airport statement quoted by Kompas said. [@3]
