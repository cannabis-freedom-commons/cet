Guyana Police Force ranks from Special Branch headquarters searched a blue jumbo
barrel at a shipping wharf in Charlestown, Georgetown, on Thursday 13 March
2025. [@4] The operation took place at the Laparkan Shipping Company's wharf at
the intersection of Lombard and Broad Streets. [@4] The barrel was shipped from
Brooklyn, New York, to a local address in Georgetown. [@1] It had been sent by
Junior Skeete of Brooklyn to Clive James of Durban Street, Lodge. [@2]

Nine sealed transparent plastic bags marked 'California TRACKSTR' were found
among clothing and sneakers, holding leaves, seeds and stems suspected to be
cannabis. [@4] A 32-year-old customs officer witnessed the search. [@3] Officers
took the barrel and its contents to the Crime Laboratory at Criminal
Investigation Department headquarters, where the material was photographed and
weighed at 10.55 pounds (4.79 kg). [@3]

The suspected cannabis was placed in separate evidence bags, sealed and lodged
at the Ruimveldt Police Station for forensic analysis. [@3] The barrel and the
clothing were stored at Ruimveldt police station for safekeeping. [@4] Police
said they had not located Clive James, the intended recipient, and were still
looking for him. [@3] The force said its investigation was continuing. [@4]
