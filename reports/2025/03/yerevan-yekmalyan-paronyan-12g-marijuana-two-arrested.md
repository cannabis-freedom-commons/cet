Officers of the criminal police department for combating illegal drug
trafficking detained a 39-year-old man on Yekmalyan Street in Yerevan on
suspicion of acquiring, storing and selling prohibited drugs. [@1] He was taken
to the Central division of the community police, and a personal search found a
mass resembling methamphetamine. [@1] According to the Interior Ministry, the
man had bought the drugs from a 47-year-old man. [@1]

The 47-year-old was found on Paronyan Street, detained and taken to the central
police directorate together with his Kia car. [@1] In a personal search officers
found packets of crystalline and yellowish-green substances which the man said
were methamphetamine and marijuana. [@1]

A court-authorised search of the 47-year-old's flat found 4.64 g of
methamphetamine and 12.26 g of substances resembling marijuana. [@1] The
criminal proceedings were continuing and no charges or court outcome were
reported. [@1]

The case was announced by the public relations department of Armenia's Interior
Ministry and carried by Armenpress. [@1]
