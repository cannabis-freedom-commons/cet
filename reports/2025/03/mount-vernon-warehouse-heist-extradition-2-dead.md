Marco Tulio Fernandez-Rodriguez was extradited from the Dominican Republic and
arrived in the United States on Wednesday afternoon to face murder, narcotics
and firearms charges. [@1,3,4] The Justice Department's Office of International Affairs worked with law enforcement partners in the Dominican Republic to secure the arrest and extradition. [@4] He appeared the next day in U.S. District Court in White Plains, and Assistant U.S. Attorney Justin Brooke told the magistrate that the government would not seek the death penalty on the murder-through-use-of-a-firearm charge. [@3] He was extradited over an attempted gunpoint robbery
of a Mount Vernon warehouse that federal officials say sold unlicensed marijuana
and nicotine products. [@1,4] The warehouse was at 137 South Fifth Ave. and was used to store and sell illegal cannabis products. [@3] The 24-year-old Dominican Republic citizen faces
charges of murder through use of a firearm, firearms use, carrying and
possession, conspiracy to distribute narcotics and conspiracy to commit Hobbs
Act robbery. [@1,4] Fernandez-Rodriguez was identified as a leader of the robbery group. [@3] Fernandez-Rodriguez was arrested in the Dominican Republic on
December 4. [@1,4] Twelve people have been arrested in connection with the
warehouse robbery. [@2,4] Others named in the most recent superseding indictment are Ryan Rivera, Moises Encarnacion, Cristian Meran, Nazario Rolan and Iberson Jimenez, and Victor Jimenez, Jhoan Diaz-Feliz and Jerpi Diaz-Feliz had been indicted earlier. [@3] One of the robbers, Ilario Contreras, pleaded guilty in January to a robbery conspiracy charge. [@3]

On March 18, 2024, around 10 p.m., two customers arrived at the wholesale
warehouse in Mount Vernon that sold unlicensed marijuana products. [@1,4] Shortly
after midnight, after the order had been prepared, two employees from the
warehouse helped the customers carry the boxes of merchandise from the warehouse
to their vehicle. [@1,4] At that point, at least 11 members of the robbery crew,
including Fernandez-Rodriguez, got out of a large cargo van that had been parked
near the warehouse, with many of them brandishing firearms. [@1,4] Other robbers
ran inside the warehouse to steal the merchandise, including marijuana. [@1,4]
Soon after the robbers entered the warehouse, a gunfight broke out between the
robbers and a warehouse employee. [@1,4] As a result, the employee and one of the
robbers were killed. [@1,4] Employee Salem Rabadi and robber Romano Acosta were fatally shot. [@3] The robbers then fled the scene, without stealing
anything. [@1,4] Law enforcement later found 1,665 pounds of marijuana and 1,107 pounds of cannabis edibles in the warehouse. [@3]

This case is being handled by the Office's White Plains Division, with Assistant
U.S. Attorneys Jared D. Hoffman and Justin L. Brooke in charge of the
prosecution. [@1,4] Podolsky praised the work of the FBI’s Westchester County Safe
Streets Task Force and the Mount Vernon Police Department. [@1,4]
