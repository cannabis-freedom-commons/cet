Police in Dowa District arrested two people for illegal possession of cannabis
sativa at Dzaleka Refugee Camp on 16 March 2025. [@1] The two were Remasi
Mziiki, 22, a Congolese refugee, and Gosta Mvula, 52, a resident of Jeputala
Village under Traditional Authority Msakambewa in Dowa District. [@1] Dowa
police publicist Alice Sitima identified Mziiki as a Congolese national living
in the camp and Mvula as a Malawian. [@2]

Acting on intelligence from an anonymous source, officers from the Dzaleka
Police Unit searched Mziiki’s home in the Lisungwi area of the camp. [@1] The
search found cannabis sativa hidden in food containers, a sack and a plastic
bag. [@1] The Face of Malawi account said officers found volumes of the drug
concealed in food containers, sacks and plastic bags. [@2] Further inquiries led
to Mvula’s arrest near Mziiki’s residence, carrying a laptop bag of cannabis
packed in a sack and prepared for sale. [@1] Neither suspect could produce a
valid licence to possess or sell cannabis. [@1]

Police said Mziiki had an earlier conviction for a similar offence. [@1] The
cannabis was taken for assessment, and both suspects were expected to appear in
court soon to face charges. [@1] The suspects were expected to appear in court
soon to face charges related to their activities. [@1]
