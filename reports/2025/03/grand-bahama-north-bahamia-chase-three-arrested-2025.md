Officers from the Drug Enforcement Unit in Grand Bahama arrested two men aged 34
and 29 and a 31-year-old woman after a high-speed chase on 10 March 2025. [@1]
Acting on intelligence, officers attempted to stop a tan Chevy Malibu in North
Bahamia shortly before 3:30 p.m. [@1]

The driver refused to pull over, prompting a pursuit. [@1] During the chase, the
vehicle's occupants discarded suspected hydroponic marijuana from the window.
[@1] The chase ended when the vehicle stopped, but not before the driver
assaulted one of the officers with the vehicle while attempting to flee. [@1]

An officer discharged his service weapon in fear for his life, and no injuries
were reported. [@1] After arresting all three occupants, officers found a small
quantity of marijuana in the vehicle. [@1] The suspects were charged with
possession of dangerous drugs with intent to supply and assault with a dangerous
instrument. [@1] The investigation was still ongoing. [@1]
