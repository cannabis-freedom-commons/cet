Ivory Coast's drug police seized 20 kg of cannabis at Modeste, on the Port-Bouët
– Modeste road, on the morning of Saturday 8 March 2025. [@1] A team from the
advanced post of the police of narcotics and drugs carried out the seizure under
Operation Épervier 10, a police drive against drug trafficking. [@2] The
national police directorate (DGPN) said officers acted on an anonymous tip about
drug trafficking along the axis. [@2]

Investigators found a parcel of 20 cannabis blocks weighing 20 kg in the brush
around an unfinished house. [@2] Koaci said the parcel was camouflaged in
brushwood around an unfinished construction at Modeste. [@1] It said analyses
confirmed that the seized product was cannabis, in 20 blocks with a total weight
of 20 kg. [@1]

No arrest had been announced, and police said further searches could be carried
out in the coming days. [@1] The authorities presented the seizure as evidence
of intensified efforts to dismantle trafficking networks in the region. [@1]
