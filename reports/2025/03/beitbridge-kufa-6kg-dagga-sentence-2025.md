Detectives acting on a tip-off on 3 March found 6.26 kilograms of dagga hidden in Sphelile Kufa's bag during a search at the Masvingo Turn-off in Beitbridge, where she was looking for transport to Kadoma. [@1,2]

The seized dagga had a street value of US$626. [@2] Kufa, 33, pleaded guilty to unlawful possession of dangerous drugs. [@1,2]

Magistrate Takudzwa Gwazemba sentenced her to 30 months in prison. [@1,2] Prosecutor Tinayeishe Matenga appeared for the state. [@1]
