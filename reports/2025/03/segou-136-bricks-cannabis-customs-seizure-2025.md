Mali's customs service seized 136 bricks of cannabis at the Sékôrô security
checkpoint near Ségou on 23 March 2025. [@4] The seizure took place during an
unannounced check at about 17:45, as the vehicle headed towards Bamako. [@4] The
cannabis was hidden in a compartment built into a personal Mercedes car. [@3]
The vehicle was carrying two men. [@1] Commandant Alassane Sissoko, head of the
Ségou customs research section, said the car was searched because of its
suspicious position. [@4]

The customs service valued the 136 bricks at more than 155 million CFA francs
(about $256,835). [@1] Director-general Amadou Konaté said the interception
showed the work of the service's specialised units. [@3]

The customs service announced the seizure on Monday 24 March, the day after it
was made. [@2] The service described the seizure as one of a series of recent
successes against transnational criminal activity. [@5]
