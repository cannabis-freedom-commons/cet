Twenty-one-year-old American hostess Yannick Nicole Buie arrived at Philip
Goldson International Airport on American Airlines Flight 583 from Miami on
March 18, 2025. [@2] She had come to Belize on vacation to celebrate her
birthday, turning 22 the next day on March 20. [@2]

Authorities at the airport detected a strong smell of cannabis and searched
Buie’s luggage, finding four small, multicolored packages of marijuana. [@1] She
was found carrying 30.51 grams of cannabis in her luggage. [@1] She was detained
and handed over to the Ladyville police, and charged with importation of a
controlled drug. [@2] Customs officers picked up on the strong smell of cannabis
coming from her, prompting the search. [@3]

Appearing unrepresented in Belize City Magistrate’s Court No. 3 on the morning
of March 19, she pleaded guilty to the single charge of importation of a
controlled drug. [@2] The magistrate fined her $800 plus a $5 cost of court, to
be paid immediately or face six months in prison. [@2]
