Anti-narcotics police (SGAIA) of the Civil National Police and the Navy
intercepted a boat carrying suspected contraband at Monterrico beach in Taxisco,
Santa Rosa, with prosecutors from the Public Ministry supporting the operation.
[@1] When naval personnel moved to inspect the boat, those aboard sailed it
toward the beach and fled. [@4] The alleged crew member, 31-year-old Costa Rican
Luis Felipe Abarca Gutiérrez, was detained in flagrante during the procedure.
[@1] Officers also seized from him a .40-calibre Bersa pistol with a magazine
and 33 rounds. [@2]

The boat was taken to the Pacific Naval Command in Puerto San José, Escuintla,
where inspectors found plastic canisters holding 170 packages of suspected
cocaine and a bag of suspected marijuana. [@1] The Interior Ministry later
described the count as 170 packages testing presumptive positive for cocaine,
plus a bag of marijuana and a bag of cocaine under preliminary field tests. [@3]
Field tests on the packages gave a presumptive result for cocaine. [@1] Police
said the boat had come from Costa Rica with the drugs hidden in plastic
canisters. [@2]

Authorities put the estimated value of the seized drugs at Q18.7 million, a
combined figure driven by the cocaine load. [@1] The cargo was moved by land
under heavy security to SGAIA warehouses in zone 6 of the capital for
safekeeping and judicial proceedings. [@1]
