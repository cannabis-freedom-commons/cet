Officers of the Sint Maarten Police Force (KPSM) held a traffic control in the
Welfare Road area at about 4:15 a.m. on a Thursday morning. [@1] During the stop
a 43-year-old man, identified only by the initials B.S., was found in possession
of an undisclosed quantity of marijuana and cocaine. [@1] He was arrested at the
scene and taken to the Philipsburg Police Station for further investigation.
[@1]

KPSM said it continues to carry out regular controls aimed at curbing illegal
activity and maintaining public safety throughout the island. [@1] The 721news
outlet carried the same KPSM account, naming the same 43-year-old suspect. [@2]
The Kiko Ta Pasando news site carried the same statement, including the time and
place of the control. [@3]
