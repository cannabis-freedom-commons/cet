Police seized 23 kg of cannabis in a house in Villeparisis, in the
Seine-et-Marne department, on Friday 18 July 2025. [@1] A neighbour approached
officers from a drug-detection dog unit who were on an unrelated check nearby
and told them about suspicious comings and goings and a strong smell of cannabis
coming from the house. [@1] Three suspects tried to run when officers arrived
but were arrested. [@1] The seizure was made by the local judicial police
service (SLPJ) of Villeparisis. [@2]

Officers found the cannabis stored in two suitcases in the house. [@3] Local
reports described the 23 kilograms as cannabis herb. [@4] Police estimated the
resale value of the cannabis at 225,000 euros. [@1]

The three suspects were two Albanians in their thirties who live in Belgium and
a 23-year-old Spaniard. [@1] The investigation found that the men were importing
narcotics from Belgium, according to the UN1TÉ 77 police union. [@1] The two men
living in Belgium were already known for drug trafficking, and the third was
believed to have been tasked with transporting the drug. [@1]

The three men were referred to the criminal court in Meaux for immediate
appearance. [@2] Their trial was postponed to September, and they were held in
pre-trial detention in the meantime. [@1]
