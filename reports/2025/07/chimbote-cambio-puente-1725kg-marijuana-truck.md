National Police officers in Peru seized 172.5 kg of marijuana from a truck
travelling towards Lima. [@1] The driver, Santos Óscar Castillo Ruiz, 41, from
Cajamarca, was detained at the scene. [@1] Officers of the San Pedro police
station in Chimbote made the stop. [@3]

The truck was stopped at Cambio Puente, a settlement a few kilometres from
Chimbote in Áncash region. [@1] The vehicle, plate COY-854, had been fitted with
a false bottom to conceal the load. [@1] Inside the compartment officers found
more than 120 pressed parcels of marijuana. [@1] Police confirmed the total
weight as 172.5 kg after checking the load at the station. [@1] Diario de
Chimbote described San Pedro police finding more than 170 kilos of marijuana
hidden in a secret compartment of a box truck. [@2] TV Perú reported the load as
180 kilos, a higher figure than the 172.5 kg given by the other accounts. [@3]

Police said the marijuana was bound for Lima's illegal market. [@1] They
suspected the driver was part of a network using road routes to move drugs from
the provinces to the capital. [@1] Colonel Domingo Salazar Durán said
preliminary information placed the drug's origin in the highlands of Áncash and
La Libertad. [@3] He said it was loaded at Pallasca, bypassed checkpoints via
the Evitamiento road and continued up the Panamericana Norte towards Lima. [@3]
TV Perú said the drug could fetch up to 2,000 soles a kilo on the national black
market. [@3] The driver was taken to the San Pedro station with the truck and
the seized load. [@1] The Public Prosecutor's Office said it was coordinating
with police to formalise a complaint against the detainee. [@1] The truck
remained impounded as evidence. [@1]
