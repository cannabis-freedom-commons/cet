Hong Kong Customs announced the cannabis seizure as one of two air-passenger
drug trafficking cases detected at the airport on 15 and 16 July 2025. [@2] A
42-year-old woman and a 22-year-old man arrived in Hong Kong from Bangkok,
Thailand, on 16 July 2025. [@1] During customs clearance at Hong Kong
International Airport, officers found about 12 kg of suspected cannabis buds
concealed in their respective carry-on luggage. [@1]

Customs arrested both passengers. [@1] Customs estimated the market value of the
buds at about HK$2.6 million. [@1] An investigation into the case was still
ongoing. [@1]

Customs said it would step up enforcement against drug trafficking through
intelligence analysis. [@1] It said it would keep selecting passengers from
high-risk regions for clearance checks to combat cross-border drug trafficking.
[@1] The department reminded the public not to accept offers to carry controlled
items into or out of Hong Kong, or to carry unknown items for other people. [@1]
Trafficking in a dangerous drug carries a maximum penalty of a HK$5 million fine
and life imprisonment under the Dangerous Drugs Ordinance. [@1]
