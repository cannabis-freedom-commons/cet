Armenian law enforcement seized more than 7 kg of marijuana smuggled into the
country, according to the Interior Ministry's press service. [@1] Acting on
operational information, officers in Zovuni village, Kotayk region, detained a
29-year-old Yerevan resident and a 39-year-old foreigner and took them and the
foreigner's car to the Nairi municipal police department. [@1] More than three
kilograms of marijuana were found in the car. [@1]

Near a petrol station in Jrvezh village, Kotayk region, police detained a
36-year-old man from Yerevan and a 35-year-old man from Ashtarak, taking them to
the Kotayk police department in the car driven by the latter. [@1] Officers
found 680 grams of marijuana in that car. [@1] On the Gevorg Chaush section of
the Proshyan–Yerevan highway, two foreigners aged 28 and 31 were detained and
540 grams of marijuana were found on the passenger. [@1] On Kamo Street in
Dilijan, a 34-year-old man from Vanadzor and his 33-year-old wife were detained
and taken with their car to the Dilijan police department. [@1] More than two
kilograms of marijuana were found in that car. [@1]

All eight detainees were arrested and the investigation continued. [@1]
