Akhil, a 21-year-old man from Kochuthara Thekkathil in Manappally South,
Karunagappally, was arrested jointly by the Kollam city DANSAF squad and
Karunagappally police. [@1] The arrest followed a search carried out on the
basis of secret information. [@1] The operation was supervised by Karunagappally
ASP Anjali Bhavana, led by Karunagappally police station inspector Biju, and
carried out by sub-inspectors Ashiq and Adarsh together with a DANSAF team under
sub-inspector Anish. [@1]

During the search of his home, officers recovered 63.5 grams of cannabis which
police said was being kept for sale. [@1] The same search turned up 10.56 grams
of MDMA, a different substance that is not counted in the cannabis figures. [@1]
Police said the drugs had been brought for distribution to buyers including
school and college students. [@1]
