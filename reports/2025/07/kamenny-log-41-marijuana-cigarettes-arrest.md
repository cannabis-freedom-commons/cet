Grodno customs officers detained a 41-year-old Belarusian citizen at the Kamenny
Log checkpoint on 20 July 2025 as he returned from Lithuania. [@3] He was
travelling with his wife in an Infiniti car and had chosen the green corridor.
[@2] A service dog indicated by its behaviour that the man might be carrying
drugs. [@2]

Officers searching his bag found a cigarette pack holding two hand-rolled
cigarettes, whose tobacco had bright green flecks of plant matter. [@2] An
expert of the Lida interdistrict department of the State Committee of Forensic
Examinations for the Grodno region concluded that the plant substance was
marijuana. [@2]

Grodno Regional Customs opened a criminal case under part 1 of Article 328-1 of
the Criminal Code over the illegal movement of drugs across the customs border
of the Eurasian Economic Union or Belarus's state border. [@1] The offence
carries up to ten years in prison, a possible penalty rather than a sentence
already imposed. [@3] The Kamenny Log crossing faces Medininkai on the
Lithuanian side. [@1]
