Delhi Police's Crime Branch arrested five foreign nationals accused of working
for an international drug syndicate that operated from Delhi. [@1] Police said
the five were citizens of Nigeria, Cameroon and Côte d'Ivoire and were picked up
at various places in the capital. [@1] The Times of India named Kameni Philipp,
a Cameroonian national, as the syndicate's India operations head. [@4] Police
also arrested three Nigerians named as Godwin John, Kelechi Chikwe and Ibe
Chinedu Austin, and Koulaie Philippe, alias Tall Guy, from Côte d'Ivoire. [@4]

Officers seized 2.7 kg of cocaine, 1 kg of MDMA, 1 kg of cannabis, Rs 2.07 lakh
in cash, a Honda City car and forged Aadhaar cards. [@1] The Times of India
reported the seizure as 2,700 g of Colombian cocaine, 1,000 g of MDMA and over 1
kg of OG ganja, with a sedan and about Rs 2 lakh in cash. [@4] A PTI report said
more than 1 kg of cannabis and Rs 2.7 lakh in cash were seized from the accused.
[@3] Police estimated the total value of the drugs seized at over Rs 100 crore.
[@1]

The case began on 13 June, when police intercepted a parcel at a courier centre
in Moti Nagar and found MDMA concealed in clothing and women's footwear. [@2]
Police said orders were relayed from a command in Nigeria to delivery agents in
Delhi. [@3] Police said the cartel had generated more than Rs 85 crore in India
over the previous six months. [@3]

Police said the investigation was continuing and further arrests were likely.
[@2]
