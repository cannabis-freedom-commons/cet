Honduras's National Police announced that its anti-gang directorate, DIPAMPCO,
captured six MS-13 members with drugs and weapons in Comayagua. [@1] DIPAMPCO
officers detained the six alleged members of the Mara Salvatrucha (MS-13) in a
house raid at dawn on Friday 4 July 2025 in the Piedras Bonitas neighbourhood.
[@2] The raid was carried out under a judicial order on a house in Piedras
Bonitas, in the city of Comayagua. [@3] Police identified the detainees only by
their aliases: “El Crimen”, “Liverpool”, “El Doctor”, “El Teacher”, “El Chele”
and “El Fresa”. [@2] Several had criminal records: “El Crimen” had an
outstanding homicide arrest warrant and a 2024 drug-trafficking detention, while
“Liverpool” had been held in 2019 for extortion and illegal weapons possession.
[@2]

Inside the house officers found a total of 2,976 wraps of dried herb believed to
be marijuana, 2,448 wraps of alleged crack rock, 238 tips of alleged cocaine and
three police uniforms. [@3] Officers also seized five firearms, three
police-uniform trousers and two vehicles. [@2] The five firearms were to undergo
technical analysis to determine whether they had been used in violent acts. [@3]
The police announcement described the haul as almost three thousand marijuana
wraps and a similar quantity of cocaine. [@1]

Police said the six would be accused of drug trafficking in the form of
possession, unlawful possession of police uniforms, unlawful possession of
firearms and criminal association. [@2] The detainees were to be referred to the
Public Prosecutor's Office to face proceedings. [@3] The police directorate said
those captured face drug-trafficking and other charges. [@1]
