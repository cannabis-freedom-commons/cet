Officers of Panama Oeste's drug prosecutor's office, working with the National
Police's National Anti-Drug Directorate, carried out Operation Nómada at several
points in the provinces of Panama and Panama Oeste on Friday 4 July 2025. [@1]
The operation aimed to apprehend people under investigation for the illicit sale
of drugs. [@1] Seven people were arrested and various items linked to the
alleged activity were seized. [@1] At a guarantee hearing five men were
convicted and two citizens were placed under provisional detention after being
charged with drug-related offences. [@2] The prosecutor's office said the
outcome came from a guarantee hearing. [@2]

The seized items included 18 carrizos and 15 small packets of presumed cocaine,
88 envelopes of marijuana, 95 fragments of piedra (crack) and a considerable
amount of cash. [@1] The investigation had begun on 9 February 2025 from
information about suspected illicit drug sales. [@1] The drug dealing was
centred in the community of Coloncito, Chame district, Panama Oeste province.
[@1]

The suspect allegedly used pickup trucks to move the drugs, disguised as loads
of recycling material, iron and cans. [@1] To avoid detection, the suspect
rotated the people selling for him each day. [@1]
