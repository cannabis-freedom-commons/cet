Police of the Direction de la Police des Stupéfiants et des Drogues (DPSD),
Ivory Coast's anti-drug police, carried out a search in the Marley district of
Abobo, Abidjan, on Wednesday 16 July 2025. [@2] The search targeted a house that
an anonymous tip said was being used to store illicit substances. [@3]

Officers found 106 blocks of cannabis packed and hidden inside the building,
weighing 106 kilograms. [@2] Police put the market value of the cannabis at
about 4,770,000 CFA francs. [@1] A quantity that size led investigators to
suspect an organised network operating in the commune, Fraternité Matin
reported. [@2]

No arrest had been announced when the seizure was reported the next day. [@1]
Investigators said they were working to identify and arrest those behind the
traffic. [@2] The seizure was part of Operation Épervier 10, a police operation
aimed at dismantling drug networks in Abidjan and the surrounding areas. [@2]
The authorities restated their zero-tolerance policy and promised more police in
neighbourhoods identified as trafficking hubs. [@1]
