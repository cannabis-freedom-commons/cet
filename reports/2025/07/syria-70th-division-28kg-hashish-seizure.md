A patrol of the 70th Division of the Defense Ministry seized a shipment of
hashish weighing 28 kilograms near the Syrian-Jordanian border. [@1] The 70th
Division is a unit of Syria's Defense Ministry. [@1] The Syrian Ministry of
Defense announced the operation on Saturday 19 July 2025, saying the narcotics
were being prepared for smuggling into Jordan. [@2]

In a statement published on its official Telegram channel, the ministry said a
patrol from Division 70 intercepted the shipment near the border. [@2] The
ministry's Media and Communications Department clarified that the shipment was
intended for smuggling into Jordanian territory. [@1] The ministry said the
confiscated narcotics included 28 kilograms of hashish. [@2] The case, along
with the seized materials, was referred to the Internal Security Department in
the region for further investigation. [@2]
