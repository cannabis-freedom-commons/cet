Agents of Cuba's Interior Ministry seized more than 500 marijuana plants in the
municipality of Tercer Frente, Santiago de Cuba. [@1] Police detained several
citizens who were looking after the crop. [@1] The operation took place in the
Matías area after farmers alerted the Interior Ministry to a marijuana crop
hidden in the undergrowth. [@2]

The operation was reported by the pro-government Facebook profile Héroes del
Moncada, which has links to State Security. [@1] The profile said the detained
caretakers would be judged with all the weight of the law. [@1]
