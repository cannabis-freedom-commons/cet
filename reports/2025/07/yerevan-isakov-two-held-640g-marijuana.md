Officers of the department for combating illegal drug trafficking in Yerevan's
city criminal police directorate detained two men on Isakov Avenue in the
Armenian capital. [@1] Police said the two men were residents of the capital,
one living on Argishti Street and the other on Sharuri Street. [@2]

The find came during a police search of a 40-year-old passenger. [@1] Officers
found a plastic bag that held two smaller bags with zip fastenings. [@2] In one
bag police found more than 640 grams of a substance resembling marijuana. [@1]
In the other they found more than 100 grams of a substance resembling
methamphetamine. [@2] Police described the find as a particularly large quantity
of synthetic and plant-based drugs. [@1]

Officers handed both men and the documents drawn up during the operation to the
investigative department of Yerevan's Malatia-Sebastia administrative district.
[@1] Police said the circumstances of the find were still being established in a
preliminary investigation. [@1]
