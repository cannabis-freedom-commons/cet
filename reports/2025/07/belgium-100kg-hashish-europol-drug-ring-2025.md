Eleven searches were carried out simultaneously in Belgium, the Netherlands and
the United Kingdom on Tuesday 15 July 2025, targeting a gang suspected of moving
drugs through Belgium. [@1] Belgium's Federal Judicial Police of East Flanders
detained seven suspects in the operation. [@1] Five of them were placed under
arrest warrant by the investigating judge in Dendermonde. [@1] The federal
police said twelve suspected traffickers were detained in the operation, seven
of them in Belgium. [@2] In total twelve suspects had been arrested, ten in
Belgium and two in the Netherlands. [@1] Nine of the searches were in Belgium,
at places including Jette, Antwerp, Grâce-Hollogne, Genk, Hasselt and the prison
in Haren. [@1]

Police said they seized 100 kg of hashish over the course of the investigation.
[@1] The same statement put the total drug seizure at more than 600 kg,
including 225 kg of ketamine, 150 kg of amphetamines and 30 kg of heroin. [@1]
Officers also seized 5 million euros in various currencies. [@1] In the final
round of searches they found 150,000 euros in cash and large bags filled with
cannabis. [@1] The Brussels Times reported that the 11 house searches comprised
nine in Belgium and one each in the United Kingdom and the Netherlands. [@3]

The investigation began on 7 September 2024 on the E17 at Waasmunster, when road
police stopped a Lithuanian truck. [@1] That stop produced 225 kg of ketamine
and more than 200 litres of drug-making chemicals. [@1] Belgian postal parcels
containing cocaine were also intercepted. [@1] The Federal Judicial Police of
East Flanders, IRC Netherlands and the UK's National Crime Agency took part
alongside Europol. [@3]
