Georgia's Interior Ministry destroyed a large stock of narcotic and psychotropic
substances seized from illegal circulation across the country. [@1] The drugs
had been seized at different times and in different regions of Georgia during
investigative and procedural operations. [@1] The destruction was carried out
under a court decision in the presence of representatives of the Prosecutor
General's Office, the health ministry and a standing commission. [@1] All the
substances were burned in a special high-temperature furnace in line with modern
environmental safety standards. [@1]

Until destruction, the seized drugs were kept in a specially equipped ministry
store. [@1] They were taken to the destruction site under escort by patrol and
security police units. [@2]

The destroyed lot included 379 kilograms of cannabis. [@1] Alongside the
cannabis, it included heroin, methadone and large numbers of methadone and
pregabalin tablets. [@1] The ministry estimated the black-market value of the
whole destroyed lot at more than 7 million lari. [@2] The ministry said such
destructions are carried out regularly, once every three months. [@2]
