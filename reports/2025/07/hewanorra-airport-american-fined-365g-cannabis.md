Michael Gary Charlemagne, 45, was fined $6,000 for offences involving less than
$2,000 worth of cannabis. [@1] He pleaded guilty to the charges at the Second
District Court at the Bordelaise Correctional Facility on Monday 14 July 2025.
[@1] He would spend three months in prison if he did not pay by the following
Thursday. [@1] The Saint Lucia Daily Post reported the fine as EC$6,000, to be
paid no later than Thursday, July 18. [@2]

Officers found him carrying 13 vacuum-sealed packages of cannabis, pre-rolled
joints, vapes and rolling paper. [@1] The drugs were found in his luggage during
a routine security operation at Hewanorra International Airport on Saturday,
July 12, 2025. [@1] The load weighed 365 grammes, and police estimated its
street value at $1,825. [@1] The Daily Post put the total weight at 365 grams
and the street value at EC$1,825. [@2]

The find was made during a joint operation by the Gangs, Narcotics and Firearms
Unit, the police southern department and the Customs and Excise Department,
targeting passengers arriving on Delta flight 1064 from Atlanta. [@1] He was
arrested and charged the following day with possession of cannabis, possession
with intent to supply and importation of a controlled drug. [@1]
