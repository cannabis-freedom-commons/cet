The Kanyakumari district police seized 30 kg of ganja and detained five people,
including a minor boy, on Wednesday. [@2] The operation was carried out under
the supervision of district superintendent of police R Stalin, acting on a
tip-off. [@2] Those detained were M Vignesh, 22, of Kailasapuram, N Karthikeyan,
23, of Palayamkottai, Manoj Kumar, 23, of Thiruverkadu in Chennai, T Mahendran,
19, of Kalakkadu Bharathipuram, and a 16-year-old boy. [@2]

The team recovered 30 kg of ganja, a substance banned under the NDPS Act. [@2]
The five were caught at a rented house in Kanyakumari with 30 kg of cannabis,
including an underage student. [@1] The accused were remanded. [@1] District
police chief Stalin acted on secret information that ganja was being sold to
students in Kanyakumari, leading to the check. [@1]

Police said a few more suspects were being traced and further investigations
were underway. [@2] Police described the arrests as part of a broader
anti-narcotics operation in the district. [@2]
