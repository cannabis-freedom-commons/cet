Federal police arrested three men at Oostakker after they followed a truck
carrying an imported car. [@1] The men are aged 23, 24 and 37 and were brought
before the investigating judge. [@1] The council chamber extended their
detention on Monday. [@1]

On 26 May at about 19:00, the three men approached a truck driver stopped at
Sinaai in East Flanders. [@1] The men wanted to get access to the car on the
truck. [@1] That car had arrived from Brazil and had just been collected at the
port of Antwerp. [@1] The driver refused and drove on, and the three suspects
followed him. [@1] The interception concerned an imported car transported on a
truck travelling through Belgium. [@1]

Police found 110 blocks of cocaine weighing about 120 kilos inside the car. [@1]
During searches, the authorities found 24,000 euros in cash, cannabis and
several air rifles. [@1] Police said in a press release on Tuesday that the
three occupants had been arrested and placed under arrest warrant. [@1]
