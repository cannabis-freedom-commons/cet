Tourist police received a tip-off on 6 May 2025 that a foreign man was selling
drugs in front of a 7-Eleven in Ban Maduawan, tambon Koh Phangan. [@4] Officers
detained a 36-year-old Russian man after he parked a motorbike in front of a
supermarket in tambon Koh Phangan that evening. [@2] Police said he was
sweating, spoke rapidly and tried to cover his crossbody bag when questioned.
[@2] The tourist police station chief said officers had been observing his
behaviour for some time before the arrest. [@2]

A search of his bag found dark sticky substances wrapped in condoms and dried
magic mushrooms in glass containers. [@1] A search of his person found four bags
of white powder hidden around his waistband, later identified as cocaine. [@1]
Officers seized 415.72 grams of hashish, described as cannabis resin, along with
13.56 grams of magic mushrooms. [@1] Thai police counted 26 blocks of hashish
weighing 415.72 grams in total. [@3]

He told police he had swallowed the hashish in Bhutan to avoid customs checks
and expelled the packages after reaching Koh Phangan. [@1] He planned to sell
the hashish to tourists at high prices for a substantial profit. [@1] Police
charged him with possession of cocaine and magic mushrooms, cocaine use and
possession of a controlled herb above the permitted quantity. [@1] He was handed
with the seized items to Koh Phangan police investigators for prosecution. [@3]
Tourist police said the case was connected to international drug networks
already identified on the island. [@1]
