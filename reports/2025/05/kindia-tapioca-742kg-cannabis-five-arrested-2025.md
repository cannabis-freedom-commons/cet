Defence and security forces in Kindia seized 742 kg of cannabis from a minibus
on Sunday 11 May 2025 and arrested five people. [@1] Traffic gendarmes stopped
the white Mercedes MB100 minibus at Tapioca, in the urban commune of Kindia,
with 14 sacks of cannabis on board; the load came from Bambaya, in the Madina
Oula sub-prefecture. [@1] The arrested were the driver Abdoulaye Camara, three
apprentices named Alseny Sylla, Aboubacar Camara and Ousmane Sylla, and Aïssata
Camara, identified as the owner of the sacks. [@1] The group was presented to
the press in Kindia on Wednesday 14 May 2025. [@2] Guinéenews reported that the
arrest was made by a mixed patrol of gendarmes and police officers. [@2]

The woman told investigators she had bought the cannabis in Sierra Leone for 27
million Guinean francs and intended to resell it in Kamsar. [@1] Guinéenews
quoted her saying she had paid 10 million Guinean francs and received 400 kg, of
which only 300 kg were meant for her, in her second consignment after an earlier
one of 200 kg. [@2] The suspects named a sponsor called Moriké, said to live in
Wondy, and the woman said she had entrusted the transport to a driver called
Moussa; both were still at large. [@1]

The five were to be taken before the Kindia court of first instance on charges
of possession, transport and sale of narcotics and complicity, under articles
19, 831 and 832 of the Guinean penal code. [@1] Colonel Mamadi Condé, commander
of the first military region of Kindia, praised the operation and called for
young people to help dismantle the trafficking networks. [@2]
