Officers from the Yamatagne police post seized eight kilograms of chanvre
indien, Indian hemp, at the control post of the Tobor bridge, at the entrance to
the commune of Ziguinchor. [@1] The seizure took place at about 18:00 on
Wednesday 21 May 2025. [@1] A security source reported the operation. [@1] The
same operation led to the arrest of M. S., a man aged 25. [@1]

Stopped by the police, he admitted that he owned the drugs. [@1] He said he had
bought them in Bignona for 240,000 CFA francs. [@1] He said he intended to
resell them to a contact in Guinea-Bissau. [@1]

The account presented these checks as part of stronger vigilance by security
forces on strategic roads in Ziguinchor in the fight against drug trafficking.
