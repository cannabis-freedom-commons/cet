Prefectura Naval Argentina officers detained three men caught allegedly selling
drugs on a public street during a Saturday dawn operation in Mar del Plata. [@1]
The drugs prosecutor ordered the car's driver to remain detained on suspicion of
illegal possession of a firearm and possession of drugs for sale. [@1] The other
two men in the car were notified for investigation of an offence and released
without restrictions on their freedom. [@1] Three more people found in the
houses, a 33-year-old woman, a 22-year-old man and a 49-year-old woman, were
notified for alleged illegal possession of drugs. [@1]

A search of their vehicle seized a 9 mm Bersa pistol with a magazine and eight
rounds, 478.9 grams of cocaine, an anti-riot tear-gas grenade, 30,401 United
States dollars and 1,176,200 Argentine pesos in cash. [@1] Hours later,
Prefectura and OSMP personnel carried out two raids in the Cerrito Sur and
Juramento neighbourhoods on the order of Guarantees Court No. 4. [@1] In those
houses officers seized about two and a half kilograms of marijuana, seven mobile
phones and documents. [@1] A second account summarised the same weekend
operations in San Martín, Cerrito Sur and Juramento as yielding two and a half
kilos of marijuana and half a kilo of cocaine, with one man detained. [@2]

The case began when personnel on routine Operativo de Seguridad Mar del Plata
patrol noticed a suspected drug transaction in the San Martín neighbourhood.
[@1] Following lines of investigation, the two later raids were carried out with
the approval of Guarantees Court No. 4 and yielded about two and a half kilos of
marijuana, seven phones and documents. [@2] There prosecutors ordered three
people, two women aged 49 and 33 and a 22-year-old man, to be notified of a case
for alleged illegal possession of drugs. [@2]
