Detectives from the Criminal Investigations Department's drugs and narcotics
unit in Manicaland intercepted a Toyota Wish on the Harare-Mutare highway on 22
May 2025, the Zimbabwe Republic Police said. [@1] They arrested the driver,
Erick Manyangadze, 42, and his passenger, Susan Mutasa, 42. [@1]

Police found 22 kilograms of dagga, the local name for cannabis, in the vehicle,
national police spokesperson Commissioner Paul Nyathi said. [@1] Officers also
seized unregistered Broncleer cough syrup and Benylin cough syrup in the same
search. [@1]

Nyathi said the two were arrested in connection with unlawful possession of the
dagga. [@1] The force described them as drug dealers found with dagga and other
substances stashed in their car. [@1]

A New Ziana report the same day said Manyangadze and Mutasa were assisting
police with investigations. [@2]
