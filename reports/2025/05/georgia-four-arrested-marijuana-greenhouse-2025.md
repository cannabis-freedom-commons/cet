Police in Georgia arrested four people on drug charges, including citizens of
Russia and Jordan. [@1] Officers of the Interior Ministry's main division for
combating illicit drug trafficking carried out the operation and detained the
four. [@2] They were accused of illegally acquiring and storing narcotics in
large and particularly large quantities, preparing them for sale, and illegally
cultivating a plant containing narcotics. [@1]

Searches of the suspects' homes and of places they indicated seized large
quantities of cocaine, MDMA, LSD, psychotropic substances and marijuana. [@1] A
particularly large quantity of cannabis plants was seized from a special
greenhouse built for growing drug-containing plants. [@1] Police also seized
equipment for growing drug-containing plants, electronic scales and zip-lock
plastic bags. [@3]

The investigation is proceeding under Criminal Code articles 260, 261, 18-260,
265 and 273, which carry up to 20 years in prison. [@1]
