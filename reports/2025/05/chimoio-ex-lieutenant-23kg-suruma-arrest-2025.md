A 62-year-old former lieutenant in Mozambique's armed forces, the FADM, is held
in the cells of the first police station in Chimoio, Manica province, accused of
drug trafficking. [@2] The former soldier was found with 23 kilograms of Suazi
Gold, a cannabis sativa variety produced in Eswatini and known in Mozambique as
suruma. [@1] The former soldier said the cannabis was found in his room in the
Nhaurir neighbourhood. [@1] The SERNIC spokesman in Manica, Paulo Candeeiro, put
the value of the 23 kilograms at about 175,000 meticais. [@2]

The former lieutenant said the consignment had been ordered from Eswatini and
that he collected it at a truck park in Beira from a driver he named as Timóteo.
[@1] He acknowledged trafficking the drug in Mozambique. [@2] He said he used
truck drivers to transport it. [@2] He said he had been detained two years
earlier at the Alto Maé police station in Maputo for trafficking suruma. [@1]

Candeeiro said the investigation was continuing to establish who else was
involved in the trafficking network. [@1] SERNIC said it was investigating
whether the case involved a trafficking network gaining ground in Manica
province and beyond. [@2] Trafficking suruma is a crime in Mozambique. [@1]
