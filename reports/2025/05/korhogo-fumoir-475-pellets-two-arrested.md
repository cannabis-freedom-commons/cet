Two people were arrested in Korhogo on 19 May 2025 and four blocks and 475
pellets of cannabis were seized in the police operation Épervier 10. [@1] The
raid was carried out by a team from the regional branch of the Direction de la
Police des Stupéfiants et des Drogues (DPSD), Côte d'Ivoire's drug police, in
Korhogo. [@1] Officers acted on information that a fumoir, a den where cannabis
is smoked, had been reactivated in a low-lying area at the junction of the
Ossiéné, Kassirimé and Doclovogo quarters. [@1] The two arrested were identified
only as S.A., 25, and D.R., 28. [@1]

The men were arrested at the site, and a search of the premises followed. [@1]
The search produced the four blocks and 475 pellets of cannabis, according to
the police report. [@2] Abidjan.net took the account from a police note it
consulted on Tuesday 20 May 2025, the day after the raid. [@1] No weight was
given for the four blocks and 475 pellets, and no charge or court step was
reported. [@1]
