Police of the City of Buenos Aires arrested a 34-year-old Peruvian known as
“Jairo” in barrio Rodrigo Bueno, the housing complex beside the Reserva
Ecológica. [@1] A second Peruvian, aged 40, was arrested with him. [@1]

The raid was carried out in a second-floor flat in building 2 at Delia
Degliuomini 877, a few metres from the Costanera Sur nature reserve. [@2] A
narcotics-detection dog named “Capo” took part in the operation. [@1] Officers
seized 18 grams of marijuana buds in the flat. [@3] The same search found 61
grams of cocaine, which is not counted in the cannabis figures. [@3] The cocaine
was distributed in 56 wrappings and a compact piece. [@3] Officers also seized
two mobile phones and 13,000 pesos in cash. [@1] The search also turned up
cutting tools. [@1]

The city prosecutor's office said the case began when neighbours told a police
officer that “Jairo” sold drugs inside the barrio. [@3] Its specialist drug
unit, the Unidad Fiscal Especializada en la Investigación de Delitos vinculados
a Estupefacientes (UFEIDE), led by prosecutor Cecilia Amil Martín, directed the
investigation. [@3] The newspapers dated the inquiry from 11 November, some six
months before the raid. [@1] A city court authorised the raid on the flat. [@3]

Both detainees were charged with commercialising narcotics under national law
23.737. [@3] After a hearing, a court accepted the prosecutor's request to hold
the main suspect in preventive detention. [@3] The 34-year-old was also wanted
on an international warrant issued by the Quinto Juzgado Penal Unipersonal of
the Callao superior court over a simple homicide in Peru. [@1] Interpol Lima
confirmed the warrant. [@1]
