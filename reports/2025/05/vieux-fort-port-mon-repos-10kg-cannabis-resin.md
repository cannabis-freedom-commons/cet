The Royal Saint Lucia Police Force ran a 48-hour operation against drug
trafficking and illegal firearms, using its Gangs, Narcotics and Firearms Unit,
Special Operations Team, K9 Unit, Special Services Unit, Tactical Response Unit
South and Central Intelligence Unit. [@2] The cocaine seizure came on Wednesday
28 May at Dauphin Beach in Monchy, where officers found 175 brick-shaped
packages in a concealed dinghy. [@2] Field tests put the cocaine at 196.56
kilograms, a quantity excluded from the cannabis figures in this report. [@1]

The next day, 29 May, officers executed search warrants in Desruisseaux and Mon
Repos and found a black Colt .357 revolver, two rounds of .357 Magnum ammunition
and 52 grammes of cannabis resin at Kyle Devonte Serieux's house in Mon Repos.
[@2] Serieux was arrested and charged, then taken before the Second District
Court, where he was granted EC$22,000 bail in cash or with a suitable surety.
[@1]

Later on 29 May, after two weeks of surveillance, officers from the Southern
Detachment of the Gangs, Narcotics and Firearms Unit and the K9 Unit found
cannabis resin inside imported food products at the Vieux Fort Port. [@1] The
package came from New York City and was flagged during routine inspections after
the K9 Unit repeatedly indicated it. [@3] Inside, officers found five tampered
food containers holding 25 vacuum-sealed black plastic packages of suspected
cannabis resin. [@3] Police put the resin at about 10.35 kilograms, with an
estimated street value of EC$68,100. [@3] No arrests were made in the port case;
police said the intended consignee was unaccounted for and that investigators
had identified several persons of interest. [@1]

Inspector Shervon Matthieu, head of the Gangs, Narcotics and Firearms Unit, said
the Dauphin Beach seizure demonstrated the force's operational effectiveness and
inter-agency coordination. [@1] Commissioner of Police Verne Garde said the
operations had disrupted the flow of illegal drugs and firearms into Saint
Lucian communities and sent a strong message to those who endanger public
safety. [@2]
