Singapore's Central Narcotics Bureau arrested 139 suspected drug offenders in an
island-wide anti-drug operation from 18 to 30 May 2025. [@1] The youngest person
arrested was a 14-year-old student held on suspicion of drug abuse. [@1] Five
male foreign nationals aged 23 to 34 were arrested near Tuas South Avenue early
on 23 May for suspected drug offences. [@1] Two of them, aged 25 and 34,
struggled to resist arrest and were subdued with necessary force. [@1] A
32-year-old lodger was arrested for possession of a controlled drug after about
27g of cannabis was found in a room in a nearby residential lodging. [@2]
Officers forced entry to a hotel room near Geylang Lorong 10 on 28 May and
arrested a 46-year-old man for suspected drug trafficking. [@1] A 29-year-old
Singaporean man was arrested at the carpark of a condominium near Tampines
Street 86 on the evening of 29 May for suspected drug trafficking. [@1]

Across the operation officers seized 2,744g of cannabis, alongside heroin, Ice,
ketamine, Ecstasy, cocaine, Erimin-5 tablets, LSD stamps and vape devices
suspected to contain THC. [@2] The Tampines man was escorted to his hideout in
the condominium, where officers seized about 2,104g of cannabis along with Ice,
ketamine, Ecstasy, cocaine, LSD stamps, Erimin-5 tablets and 49 vape devices
suspected to contain THC. [@1] At Tuas officers seized about 2g of cannabis that
a 23-year-old man was seen disposing of. [@1] The search of the Geylang hotel
room found about 20g of cannabis along with heroin, Ice, ketamine, Erimin-5
tablets and S$541.61 in cash. [@1] CNB estimated the drugs seized in the
operation, cannabis and other drugs together, at more than S$626,700. [@1]

The operation covered areas including Ang Mo Kio, Boon Keng, Bukit Merah, Jurong
and Sengkang. [@2] Assistant Commissioner Aaron Tang, director of CNB's
Intelligence Division, said officers would hunt drug traffickers down to keep
Singapore drug-free. [@1] Trafficking more than 500g of cannabis carries a
possible mandatory death penalty under the Misuse of Drugs Act 1973. [@1]
Investigations into all arrested suspects were ongoing. [@1]
