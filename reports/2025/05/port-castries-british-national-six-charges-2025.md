Glen Stephens, a 47-year-old British national, was arrested and formally charged
on Thursday. [@1] Initial investigations led to the detention of two persons of
interest. [@1] A British man faced six charges, including importation and
possession of controlled drugs and possession with intent to supply. [@1] He
appeared before the First District Court the same day for a bail hearing and was
granted bail of $22,000 in cash or with suitable surety, with other
court-imposed conditions. [@1]

The Royal Saint Lucia Police Force described the find as a breakthrough in a
transnational drug and firearm trafficking operation after illegal contraband
was discovered in a container at Port Castries. [@1] Police said the operation
was conducted on 30 April by the Gangs, Narcotics and Firearms Unit in
collaboration with the Customs and Excise Department. [@1] Officers responded to
a report of a suspicious container and uncovered packages of cannabis resin,
cannabis-infused products, firearm components and related paraphernalia hidden
inside a vehicle shipped from England. [@1] The RSLPF said the investigation was
ongoing as officers pursued leads. [@1]
