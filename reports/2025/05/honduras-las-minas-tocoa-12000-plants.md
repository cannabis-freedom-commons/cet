Honduras's armed forces said on 21 May 2025 that they had confiscated a
plantation of 12,000 marijuana bushes in the northern department of Colón. [@1]
The military said the plantation was in the mountainous sector of Las Minas
village, Tocoa municipality. [@1] Troops carried out the seizure with the Public
Order Military Police, the armed forces said in a post on X. [@1]

The illegal crop covered two manzanas of land, which the military put at 1.4
hectares. [@1] Officers found 12,000 plants of the drug about one metre tall.
[@1]

The armed forces said that in 2025 up to that point they had secured 11
marijuana plantations with more than 100,000 bushes in Colón and Yoro, both in
the north. [@1] In the same period the security forces reported seizing about 27
coca fields with more than 550,000 coca plants in Colón, Atlántida, Yoro,
Olancho and El Paraíso; those coca plants are not counted in the cannabis
figures. [@1] Army spokesman Mario Rivera said on X that joint work by different
military units was weakening organised crime and protecting Hondurans. [@1]
