Uruguayan and Brazilian police carried out joint operations at the Chuy border
and seized 24 kilograms of marijuana. [@1] Officers seized 14 kilograms on the
Brazilian side and 10 kilograms in Uruguayan territory. [@1] The drug was
carried in suitcases locked with padlocks by Brazilian citizens who were
detained. [@1]

On the night of Thursday 29 May 2025, officers patrolling Chuy saw a man
carrying a suitcase suspiciously at the intersection of General Artigas and
Galicia streets. [@2] The suitcase smelled of marijuana and was locked; the man,
a 20-year-old Brazilian, said he did not know the combination or contents and
was carrying it to settle an obligation with third parties. [@2] The prosecutor
ordered the suitcase opened in the presence of forensic police, who found 11
packages of a substance resembling marijuana weighing 10.5 kilograms, the
Interior Ministry said. [@2] Inforocha reported the weight as 10 kilograms 555
grams. [@3]

The 20-year-old was convicted of supplying narcotic substances under Article 34
of Decree-Law 14.294. [@4] The court sentenced him at a hearing the following
night. [@4] The sentence was two years and six months in prison, to be served in
full. [@4]

Chuy is a border town separated from Brazil by an avenue, with heavy shopping
traffic from Uruguayans. [@1] Subrayado reported rising drug trafficking across
the shared border by land and sea, including boats taking drugs to ships waiting
in the Atlantic. [@1]
