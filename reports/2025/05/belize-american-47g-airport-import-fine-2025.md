Cameron David Rose, a 31-year-old American sales agent from California, was
fined for importing 47 grams of cannabis through Philip Goldson International
Airport for his arrival on Friday, May 23. [@2] He arrived aboard Alaska
Airlines Flight 1334 from Los Angeles and was stopped around 6:45 p.m. after a
vacuum-sealed plastic bag was found in his luggage. [@2] At about 6:45 p.m.,
Customs Officer Dwight Wade found a suspicious package during a routine
inspection and alerted Detective Constable Daymond Rhaburn of the Anti-Narcotics
Unit. [@1]

He was found in possession of 47 grams of cannabis concealed in a vacuum-sealed
plastic bag inside his luggage. [@1] Officer Rhaburn searched and located the
transparent bag inside a brown leather handbag. [@1] When questioned, Rose
admitted the substance was marijuana. [@1] He was arrested and taken before the
Belize City Magistrate’s Court on 26 May, facing a single charge of importation
of a controlled drug. [@1]

Unrepresented, Rose pleaded guilty and was fined $1,500 BZD plus $5 court costs,
to be paid forthwith or serve three months’ imprisonment in default. [@1]
Initially unable to pay the fine, Rose was remanded to the Belize Central
Prison. [@1] He has since paid the fine in full and has been released. [@1]
