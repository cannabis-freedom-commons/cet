NDLEA officers intercepted a 22-year-old British national at the Murtala
Muhammed International Airport in Ikeja, Lagos, on Friday 9 May 2025. [@1] The
agency's Director of Media and Advocacy, Femi Babafemi, named the suspect as
Campell Kaizra Kofi Johannes Slifer. [@2] He had arrived on a Qatar Airways
flight from Thailand through Doha. [@2]

A search of his two suitcases found 35 parcels of Loud, a strong strain of
cannabis, weighing 37.60 kg. [@1] Babafemi said Slifer had been under
surveillance after intelligence reports led officers to his flight. [@3] He was
attempting to smuggle 37.6 kg of Loud into Nigeria hidden in the two suitcases.
[@3]

Babafemi said he claimed he had twice been convicted in Britain for drug
trafficking and robbery and had been recruited in London to travel to Thailand
to pick up the consignment. [@2]
