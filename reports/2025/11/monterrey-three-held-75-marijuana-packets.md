Municipal police detained two men and a woman in central Monterrey after seeing
them argue in public; one of the three was a 22-year-old from Honduras. [@2] The
officers were on a prevention patrol when they saw the three arguing in the
street and stopped to see what was happening. [@2] The three were a 22-year-old
Honduran, a 23-year-old and a 31-year-old woman, detained at the intersection of
Ruperto Martínez and Colegio Civil in the Mesón Estrella area. [@2] When the
patrol approached, the three tried to flee but were finally detained. [@2]

Officers found a handbag-style backpack containing 75 packets of marijuana,
along with crack cocaine and crystal. [@2] Police said the man was found with a
backpack holding 75 packets of marijuana, doses of crystal and cocaine, and was
placed at the disposal of the Ministerio Público. [@1]

The three were taken to Monterrey police facilities and placed at the disposal
of the public prosecutor's office, the Ministerio Público. [@2]
