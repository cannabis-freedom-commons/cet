Prefectura Naval Argentina seized more than 1,350 kilograms of marijuana in
Misiones province. [@2] Officers found the load in an abandoned pickup on a dirt
road at Arroyo Dos Bocas, by kilometre 1,688.8 of the Paraná near Colonia
Gisela. [@2] The pickup's doors were open and no one was inside when officers
reached it. [@1] No one was arrested. [@1]

The marijuana was packed in 1,504 packages. [@2] The Security Ministry estimated
the load's value at more than 3,500 million pesos. [@2]

The operation followed criminal-intelligence work that detected a possible
transport of drugs in the Colonia Gisela area. [@2] The federal prosecutor's
office and the federal court in Oberá took the case and ordered the
corresponding proceedings. [@2] The Colonia Gisela area, on the banks of the
Paraná, is frequently used by drug organisations to bring narcotics into
Argentina. [@1] Misiones, with its long border with Paraguay and its river
routes, is one of Argentina's main entry points for drugs. [@1]
