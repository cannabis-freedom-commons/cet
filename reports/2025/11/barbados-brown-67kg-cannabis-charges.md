The woman charged was Tashana Roselyn Brown, 40, of West Prospect, Bog Walk, St.
Catherine, Jamaica. [@2] Barbados Police Service Narcotics Unit officers
arrested Brown on 1 November 2025 and charged her with possession of cannabis,
trafficking, intent to supply and importation of cannabis. [@1] The four
offences were alleged to have been committed on 1 November 2025. [@2]

Barbados police estimated the total weight of the suspected cannabis at 6.7
kilograms. [@2] They put its estimated street value at 53,600 Barbados dollars.
[@2] The drug bust was at Grantley Adams International Airport in Barbados. [@3]

Brown was scheduled to appear in the District ‘B’ Magistrates’ Court on Monday 3
November 2025. [@2] A 40-year-old Jamaican woman was to appear in a Barbados
court on Monday on four drug-related charges. [@1]
