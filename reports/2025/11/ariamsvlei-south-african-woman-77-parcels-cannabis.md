A 37-year-old South African woman was arrested after drugs worth over N$5.1
million were discovered hidden in her vehicle. [@1] The incident happened at
about 17:00 on 16 November 2025 at the Ariamsvlei Border Post. [@1] According to
Deputy Commissioner Kauna Shikwambi, it is alleged that the female suspect
arrived at the Ariamsvlei Border Checkpoint from South Africa driving a white
Kia mini truck and was directed to take her vehicle to the scanner. [@1] During
scanning, officials from NamRA, Namibia's revenue agency, noticed foreign
objects in the vehicle’s canopy and alerted Ariamsvlei police. [@1] A physical
search found suspected cocaine, cannabis and Mandrax tablets built into the
canopy. [@1]

Officers seized 1,993 Mandrax tablets, 1.05 kg of cocaine and 77 parcels of
cannabis. [@1] Police put the combined estimated street value of all the drugs
at N$5,159,160. [@2]

The drugs were allegedly destined for Okahandja. [@1] Police charged her with
dealing in prohibited drugs under the Abuse of Dependence-Producing Substances
and Rehabilitation Centres Act. [@2] She was expected to appear in court on
Tuesday, 18 November 2025. [@2] She remained in custody as police investigations
continued. [@2]
