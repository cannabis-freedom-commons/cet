GNR officers detained five men and a woman aged 18 to 38 on Tuesday in the
municipality of Olhão for drug trafficking and possession of a prohibited
weapon. [@2] Five of the six detainees still lived with their parents. [@1] The
youngest member of the group was 18. [@1] The arrests followed an investigation
that had lasted about eight months into the extent of a group described as
increasingly violent. [@1] Officers carried out 11 home searches and searches in
two vehicles in the parishes of Quelfes and Olhão. [@1] The detainees were to
appear before the Faro judicial court for coercive measures to be set. [@2]

During the operation the GNR seized 381 doses of hashish, two doses of liamba
and 26.15 grams of cannabis heads. [@1] In the same seizure officers took 445
doses of cocaine, five nitrous-oxide canisters and five digital scales. [@1]
Officers also seized shotguns, a firearm replica, a revolver, an artisanal
pistol and another pistol, air weapons, 84 rounds of ammunition, a hand-grenade
casing, an extendable baton, balaclavas and an axe. [@1] Officers also seized
37,224 euros in cash, a watch, four electric scooters, televisions, a sound
system, three games consoles, clothing, perfumes, two safes and 17 mobile
phones. [@2] GNR Captain António Ramos said pink cocaine and white hashish were
aimed at a higher social stratum because of their price. [@1]

Ramos said the organised group sold to the end consumer in Olhão and
neighbouring municipalities and had criminal records and reports of firearm
violence. [@3] The GNR said it believed it had dismantled the group, but one
suspect remained at large and further arrests were possible. [@1]
