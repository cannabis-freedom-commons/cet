Paulette Emanuel, 62, a businesswoman from North Ruimveldt, Georgetown, was
sentenced to two years in prison and fined $60,000 after she pleaded guilty to
two counts of narcotics possession. [@3] She appeared before Magistrate Tariq
Mohamed at the Bartica Magistrate's Court on Monday, when the charge was read to
her. [@3] Kaieteur News put the fine at $30,000 on each of the two counts. [@2]
Emanuel was arrested on Sunday 2 November 2025 and charged with two counts of
possession of narcotics for the purpose of trafficking. [@3] Cocaine and
cannabis were unearthed in her bag during a search at the Itaballi Police
Checkpoint in Region Seven. [@1]

Police ranks stopped a motor pickup driven by a 38-year-old man of Byderabo
Road, Bartica, and searched the occupants and their belongings. [@1] A
multi-coloured bag in her possession contained several bulky parcels suspected
to be cocaine and a smaller parcel of leaves, seeds and stems suspected to be
cannabis. [@1] The suspected narcotics were weighed in her presence and amounted
to 261 grams of cocaine and 102 grams of cannabis, police reported. [@1] The
exhibits were lodged and the woman was taken into custody. [@1]

The charge was laid under Section 5(i)(a)(i) of the Narcotic Drugs and
Psychotropic Substances (Control) Act 10:10. [@1]
