Police in Escuintla, Guatemala, detained Reynaldo Ismael Navarijo, 27, and
Anderson Marco Tulio Pineda, 23, both Guatemalan nationals. [@1] Officers found
32 nylon-wrapped packets of marijuana hidden among boxes of bananas inside the
pickup truck the two men were driving. [@1] The Interior Ministry identified the
detainees as Reynaldo Ismael N, 27, and Anderson Marco N, 23, and said the 32
packets were being transported in the vehicle. [@2]

The marijuana weighed 39.124 kilograms (86.265 pounds) and police valued it at
27,949 quetzales, almost 4,000 dollars. [@1] The packets carried labels
including 'Chese', 'Diesel', 'Plomo' and 'Oreo', the police report said. [@1]
Officers also confiscated two mobile phones and the pickup truck used to carry
the marijuana. [@1]

The operation followed an anonymous tip to the 1577 anti-narcotics line and a
vehicle search with a canine unit, the PNC said on X. [@1] The Interior Ministry
placed the check at the inter-institutional control post at kilometre 68.3 of
route CA-2 in Santa Lucía Cotzumalguapa, Escuintla. [@2] The Public Prosecutor's
Office said its drug-crimes unit led the operation with the PNC's anti-narcotics
analysis unit and members of the Guatemalan army. [@1] La Hora reported that the
same three bodies made the seizure on Sunday. [@3] After weighing the drug,
authorities repackaged the haul into three parcels for use as evidence in the
criminal case. [@1]
