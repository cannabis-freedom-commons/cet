Zambia's Drug Enforcement Commission arrested and charged six people with
illicit cultivation of over 900 kg of fresh cannabis plants. [@1] DEC Deputy
Public Relations Officer Theresa Mawere said in a statement issued on Wednesday
that the six were apprehended during joint operations conducted in Western and
Luapula provinces. [@1] Those arrested included Harrison Pumulo Afumba. [@1]

The detailed account says the joint operation in Mambalakata Village in Luampa
District in Western Province was carried out by officers from Mongu and Kaoma
Districts. [@1] Following that joint operation, the Drug Enforcement Commission
said it had jointly charged and arrested five suspects in connection with the
stated cultivation and trafficking offences. [@1]

The commission specified the cultivation component as 990 kilogrammes of fresh
cannabis plants allegedly cultivated illicitly by the five. [@1] It specified
the trafficking component as 66 kilogrammes of loose cannabis allegedly
trafficked by the same five. [@1] It further listed 2.5 kilogrammes of cannabis
seeds as part of the same case against the five suspects. [@1]
