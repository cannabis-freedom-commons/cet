Troops of the Army's 28th Brigade seized 813 kilograms of marijuana in the La
Defensa sector of La Primavera, Vichada. [@1] The Army described the site as a
collection point that served as a corridor for moving drugs abroad. [@1] Two
people were captured at the site. [@1] The captured men were placed at the
disposal of the competent authorities. [@1] At the site, the authorities also
found more than 75 million pesos in cash, three pickup trucks, a motorised bongo
boat and communications equipment. [@1] The Army valued the shipment at 12.3
billion pesos. [@2]

Colonel Roberto Contreras Félix, commander of the 28th Brigade, said the
operation formed part of the Ayacucho Plus joint strategic plan. [@1] The
operation was carried out jointly with the Navy, the Air Force, the National
Police and the CTI of the Attorney General's Office. [@1] Military intelligence
linked the load to the finances of the Estructura 53 Édison Romaña, a residual
armed group with a presence in the area. [@1] Contreras said the seizure struck
the group's logistics and finances by stopping a high-value shipment and
breaking a key corridor. [@1]

The route ran from Vichada toward Venezuela, with Europe and Africa as the final
destination, El Tiempo reported. [@3] The Army said its units would keep running
joint operations to deny land and river routes to drug trafficking in the
Colombian Orinoquía. [@1]
