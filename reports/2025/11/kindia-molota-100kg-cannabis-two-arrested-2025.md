Anti-crime police in Kindia prefecture seized 100 kilograms of chanvre indien,
the local term for cannabis, in the rural commune of Molota over the weekend of
8 November 2025. [@1] Officers of the Brigade anti-criminalité (BAC) No. 24
stopped a motorcycle-taxi driver during a routine patrol and found the drug in
his parcel. [@1] Questioned by police, the driver admitted the facts and named a
young woman as the owner of the drug. [@1] The woman was arrested in turn. [@2]

Both suspects were placed in police custody and handed to the central
commissariat in Kindia for further investigation. [@1] Mosaiqueguinee reported
that the 100 kilograms had been carefully hidden in the parcel. [@3]

The national police directorate said the operation showed its determination to
fight drug trafficking, which it said fuels insecurity and affects many young
people. [@3] It urged residents to report suspicious activity on the free
toll-free number 117. [@3]
