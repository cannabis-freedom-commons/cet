Azerbaijan's State Border Service said officers had prevented a large
consignment of drugs being moved from Iran into Azerbaijan by unmanned aerial
vehicle. [@1] The attempt was stopped in the service area of the Goytepe border
detachment of the Border Troops Command. [@1] The English-language account,
citing the Border Service press centre via APA, describes the same drone attempt
from Iran prevented in the Goytapa detachment's service area. [@2]

Officers found and seized 33 kg 345 g of drugs in total, comprising 29 kg 575 g
of marijuana, 3 kg 125 g of methamphetamine, 565 g of opium and 80 g of liquid
methadone, plus 6,758 methadone M-40 tablets. [@1] The second account gives the
identical breakdown of 33 kg 345 g, including 29 kg 575 g of marijuana and the
same quantities of methamphetamine, opium, liquid methadone and tablets. [@2]

Operational-investigative measures in the case were continuing. [@1]
