Police in Man, in western Ivory Coast, arrested one person and seized six
kilograms of cannabis on Thursday 6 November 2025. [@1] The operation was
carried out by the regional branch of the Direction de la police des stupéfiants
et des drogues (DPSD), the drug police, in Man, under Operation Épervier. [@1]
Police identified the man they arrested only by the initials L.M.A and gave his
age as 20. [@1]

According to the Directorate General of the National Police, officers acted on
an anonymous tip about drug trafficking near the municipal cemetery in Man. [@1]
Pouvoirs Magazine reported that the area around the cemetery was often used by
dealers as a place to make transactions. [@2] Officers found the man carrying
several bundles of cannabis. [@2] They arrested him without incident and,
searching the area, found a fumoir, a place where drugs are smoked, hidden
behind a ruined wall. [@2] Police said the place was used both as a space for
consumption and as a point for redistributing drugs to parts of the town. [@2]
The operation also led to the destruction of the fumoir and the seizure of six
kilograms of cannabis. [@1]

Investigators believed the cannabis had been brought in along a corridor often
used by traffickers to move drugs into the country. [@2] The Directorate General
of the National Police said the operation was part of its continuing fight
against organised crime and drug trafficking across Ivory Coast. [@1] A DPSD
official in Man said that each gram seized and each closed sales point meant a
life saved and a youth protected. [@2]
