Afrim Krasniqi, 29, pleaded guilty at the High Court in Edinburgh on 16 October 2025 to being concerned in the supply of cannabis across 12 cultivations, found at homes and business premises in Glasgow, Edinburgh, Penicuik, Aberdeen, Ayrshire and Angus. [@1,2] Police put the street value of the grows at up to 3.8 million pounds. [@1,2]

On 25 November 2025 the High Court in Glasgow jailed him for five years. [@1,2,3] Lord Arthurson cut the term from six years because of the guilty plea, calling it a large-scale and carefully planned criminal enterprise. [@3] Krasniqi, an Albanian illegal entrant who left Albania in 2016, acted as a middleman, using fake Italian identity documents to rent properties while his countrymen directed the grows. [@3]

The charge covered March 2021 to September 2023; residents in Aberdeen tipped off police about several properties, where officers found cannabis farms and tampered electricity meters. [@3] Detective Constable Karen Maxwell said the organised groups behind such grows exploit vulnerable people and damage the properties they use and the communities around them. [@1,2]
