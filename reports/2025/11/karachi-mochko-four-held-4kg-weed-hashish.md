Police in Karachi's Mochko area seized a container loaded with narcotics brought
from Balochistan and arrested four suspects involved in inter-provincial drug
smuggling. [@1] The operation was carried out on a tip-off near Musharraf
Colony, where police intercepted a container transporting drugs from
Balochistan. [@1] The narcotics were being smuggled through rickshaws and
motorcycles to various parts of the city, but the attempt was successfully
foiled. [@1]

Police officials said the four arrested suspects are members of an
inter-provincial drug smuggling gang. [@1] The recovered drugs included 2,600
grams of weed and 1,600 grams of hashish, together 4.2 kilograms of cannabis.
[@1] Officers also recovered 7,200 grams of ice from the same load, and that
weight is not counted in the cannabis total. [@1] The seized narcotics are
estimated to be worth millions of rupees in the market. [@1] A case has been
registered at the Mochko Police Station, and further investigations are
underway. [@1] Police described the weed, the hashish and the ice as the drugs
recovered from the intercepted container. [@1]
