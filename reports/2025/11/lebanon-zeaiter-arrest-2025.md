The army arrested Nouh Zeaiter, a US-sanctioned Captagon and cannabis trafficker, at Kneiseh in Baalbek-Hermel. He had admitted cannabis cultivation and trafficking. On the same day the army searched his home in Baalbeck's Charawneh neighbourhood, seizing cars and military equipment, dismantling surveillance cameras and arresting suspects from the Zeaiter and Jaafar clans. [@4] The 48-year-old, a native of Taalabaya in Zahle district, is described as one of the biggest hashish traffickers in the country. [@4] He was seized in an ambush on the Kneisseh road in Baalbeck after years as a fugitive wanted on warrants issued in absentia for drug and arms trafficking. [@3] He was also involved in hashish and marijuana. [@2] The arrest followed years in which he had immunity from prosecution in the Beqaa, a region where the report says dozens of traffickers operated. [@2] The arrest was one of several against Bekaa networks in 2025. [@1] The court postponed the files concerning drug trafficking, murder and the attempted murder of a soldier to a hearing on 5 May 2026. [@3] In February 2026 a military court sentenced Zeaiter to four months in prison on minor weapons and shooting charges and ordered the confiscation of the seized weapons. [@3]
