Officers of the Anti-Drug Division and the Bolivarian National Police struck
against drug trafficking in Lara by seizing a cargo of suspected marijuana. [@1]
The cargo of suspected marijuana had a total weight of 1,253.2 kilograms, which
is more than one metric ton of the drug. [@1] Venezuela's Interior Minister
Diosdado Cabello disclosed the operation during his participation in the
Congress on Drugs and Juvenile Delinquency held at the Supreme Court of Justice.
[@1]

The security procedure focused on intercepting a Toyota Land Cruiser. [@1] After
inspecting the intercepted vehicle, the authorities discovered 2,250 wrappers of
marijuana of various shapes and sizes hidden inside it. [@1] Besides the
important stash, the agents seized items pointing to sophisticated logistics,
including a Starlink satellite antenna. [@1] They also seized a telephone box
together with a circulation certificate found with the load. [@1]

Authorities said the Prosecutor's Office was notified immediately to open
investigations aimed at dismantling the criminal network behind the shipment.
[@1]
