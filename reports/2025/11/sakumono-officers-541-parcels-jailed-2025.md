The Gbetsile Circuit Court convicted Assistant Superintendent of Police Nasiru
Amadu and Corporal Emmanuel Mintah on 28 November 2025 of unlawful possession of
narcotic drugs for trafficking and conspiracy. [@4] Amadu was sentenced to 10
years in prison and Mintah to five years, with the terms to run concurrently.
[@1] The court also fined each officer 10,000 penalty units, equivalent to
GH¢120,000, with three more years in prison for each if the fine is not paid.
[@1] Their convictions were secured under section 23(1) of the Criminal Offences
Act, 1960 (Act 29) and section 37(2)(b) of the Narcotic Control Commission Act,
2020 (Act 1019). [@1]

At the time, Amadu, 48, was second-in-command at the Sakumono District Police
Headquarters and Mintah, 43, was a service driver under him. [@2] Two Ghana
Police Service officers were arrested at a checkpoint in Sakumono on 19 November
2023 while carrying 541 parcels of narcotic drugs in a police vehicle. [@1] The
vehicle was a Nissan Navara with registration GP 727, travelling from Ho towards
Accra, and it was stopped at about 7:15 p.m. [@2] A search of the pickup found
eight sacks holding the 541 compressed parcels of substances suspected to be
narcotic drugs. [@3] The Forensic Crime Laboratory examined the parcels and
confirmed they were narcotics. [@2]

The prosecution said the case began when an officer at the Afienya District
Police Command reported the two officers' activities. [@2] The prosecution said
Oluman was a relative of Mintah and traded in narcotics at Ashaiman Tulaku. [@4]
Oluman hired Mintah to transport Indian hemp from Peki Tsibu in the Volta Region
to Tulaku, and Mintah then brought Amadu into the arrangement. [@2] The two
officers drove vehicle GP 727 from Sakumono to a forest near Peki Tsibu, where
they collected the drugs from a waiting Benz bus. [@2] Prosecutors said it was
the second time the officers had carried out such an operation. [@2]
