Peru's armed forces and national police seized 210 kilograms of cannabis at a
clandestine collection point in Rumi Rumi, La Mar province, Ayacucho, the
defense ministry said on 21 November 2025. [@1] Rumi Rumi lies in the VRAEM, the
valley of the Apurímac, Ene and Mantaro rivers. [@1] The VRAEM Special Command
carried out the operation through the 2nd Wari Infantry Brigade, working with
Dirandro, the anti-drug directorate of the national police. [@2] The BCT No. 34
combat group entered the storage point. [@1]

The cannabis was packed in eight sacks holding brick- and oval-shaped packages.
[@1] The load was being stored for transport out of the VRAEM. [@3] The material
was secured, identified and handed to the authorities for investigation. [@3]

The defense ministry said the armed forces keep a permanent operational presence
in the VRAEM, with strengthened capabilities against drug-trafficking
organizations. [@1]
