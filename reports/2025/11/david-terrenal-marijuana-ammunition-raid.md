No person linked to this important confiscation was reported captured during an
operation carried out by units of the Dirección Nacional de Inteligencia
Policial (DNIP) and the Ministerio Público. [@1] An arsenal of ammunition,
firearms and suspected drugs was seized during a search of a residence in the
Terrenal sector of David district, in the province of Chiriquí. [@1]

When the items were counted, officers recorded one thousand rounds of 5.56 x 45
calibre ammunition. [@1] The count also listed about five boxes of ammunition
for firearms of calibres .22, .38 and .357 and for a 12-gauge shotgun, one
revolver of calibre .357 whose serial number was not visible, and two replica
firearms described as pellet guns. [@1] In the same search officers found three
plastic bags of vegetable content that was presumed to be marijuana. [@1]

At the end of the procedure the ammunition, the weapons and the drugs were left
at the orders of the Ministerio Público so that an investigation of the case
could begin. [@1]
