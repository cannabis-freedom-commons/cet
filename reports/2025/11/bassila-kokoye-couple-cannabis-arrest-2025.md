Police républicaine officers arrested a couple at their home in the Kokoyè
quarter of Bassila at dawn on Tuesday 18 November 2025. [@1] The operation in
Kokoyè followed investigations that pointed officers to the couple's home. [@1]
Police said the couple, officially shopkeepers, had run a consumption point
inside their own home for a long time. [@1] Le Matinal reported that
investigators said the couple had long run a "ghetto", a drug den, from the
house. [@2]

A search of the home turned up a large slab of chanvre indien, the French term
for cannabis, and several balls already prepared. [@1] Bénin Web TV reported
that the balls were packaged and ready for sale. [@3] Investigators said the
house served as a distribution and consumption point. [@3]

The couple was taken to the police station for the procedures required by law.
[@1] Le Matinal reported that they were held in police custody pending transfer
to Ocertid, Benin's central office against illicit drug trafficking, and trial
at the Criet, the court for economic crimes and terrorism. [@2] The operation
followed the arrest on Saturday 15 November 2025 of a man found with chanvre
indien at Frignon market, a separate case. [@1] The outlet linked both arrests
to a drive to dismantle networks active in the area before the end-of-year
holidays. [@1]
