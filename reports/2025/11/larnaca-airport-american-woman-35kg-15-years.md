A 35-year-old woman from the United States was sentenced to 15 years in prison
after admitting she tried to bring 35 kilograms of cannabis into Cyprus hidden
in two suitcases. [@1] The Larnaca Permanent Criminal Court handed down the
sentence. [@1] The woman was arrested on April 4 after landing at Larnaca
Airport on a flight from Barcelona. [@1]

Customs officers working with the Drug Enforcement Agency checked her luggage
and found 61 packages of dried cannabis tucked inside two bags. [@1] Police said
she confessed on the spot after telling investigators she had been recruited
through a social media app. [@1] She said unknown persons offered to pay for her
vacation and give her 1,000 euros to transport two suitcases to Cyprus while she
was travelling from the United States to France. [@1]

She told authorities she did not know to whom she would deliver the drugs in
Cyprus or how she would receive instructions after arrival. [@1] She arrived at
Larnaca Airport from Barcelona with two suitcases containing the cannabis. [@2]
The court emphasized the need for strict penalties for such drug-related cases.
