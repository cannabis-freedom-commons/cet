The Memphis Police Department together with the Memphis Safe Task Force arrested
eight people as part of Operation All of Boyd Round TWO. [@2] Officers executed
multiple search warrants on Boyd Street near Vance Avenue in South Memphis, an
area police say is known for persistent drug and gang activity. [@2] Larry
Lindsey, 38, faced three counts of possession of marijuana with intent to
manufacture, deliver or sell and paraphernalia counts. [@1] Devandrick Bowie,
37, faced possession of marijuana with intent to manufacture, deliver or sell
and a paraphernalia count. [@1] L. Love, 46, faced a marijuana intent count plus
paraphernalia, convicted-felon handgun possession and firearm-during-felony
counts. [@1] Eric Crawford, 52, faced a marijuana intent count and evading
arrest. [@1] Keosha Griggs, 32, was arrested on a warrant for identity theft.
[@1] Donald McCoy, 41, was arrested on a warrant for theft of property. [@1]
Kelvin Davis, 51, was arrested on a warrant for vandalism. [@1] Coral Price, 51,
received a misdemeanor citation for marijuana possession. [@1]

Officers seized four pounds of marijuana, about 1.81 kg, in the saturation
effort on the South Memphis street. [@1] Officers also seized one gun and $1,000
in cash. [@1]

Police leaders said the Organized Crime Unit and Memphis Safe Task Force
partners worked together to remove drugs, guns and repeat offenders from the
streets. [@2] Boyd Street had been the target of a similar police operation in
June 2024. [@1]
