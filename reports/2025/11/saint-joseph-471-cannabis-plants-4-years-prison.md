A man in Saint-Joseph was sentenced to four years in prison after gendarmes
dismantled his cannabis-trafficking activity. [@1] Gendarmerie personnel said
they had recently ended a large-scale cannabis-trafficking operation in
Saint-Joseph. [@1] The man had been cultivating cannabis for almost ten years.
[@1] The drugs were then resold. [@1]

The operations were carried out by the Saint-Joseph community of brigades
together with the canine-investigations group. [@1] Gendarmes discovered 471
cannabis plants. [@1] They also discovered production equipment. [@1] They also
discovered two firearms. [@1]

The gendarmerie presented the case as illustrating its daily commitment to
fighting trafficking that feeds insecurity and weakens neighbourhoods. [@1]
