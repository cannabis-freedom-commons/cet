The Philippine National Police said a concerned citizen in Palawan turned over a
package of suspected high-grade marijuana found on the seashore at Malinong
Beach Resort. [@2] The package weighed 1.95 kilograms and held what police
called kush, the local name for high-grade marijuana. [@2] Police put the
package's street value at ₱2,340,000. [@2] The police statement was posted on 11
November 2025. [@1] All seized evidence was under police custody pending
investigation and the filing of charges, the PNP said. [@2]

The Palawan find was one item in a PNP statement on simultaneous operations
across the country that day, which also reported seven most wanted arrests, ₱7.9
million worth of drugs and ₱3 million of smuggled cigarettes. [@1] The PNP
valued all the drugs seized that day at ₱7,924,160. [@2] That total includes 520
grams of shabu seized in Davao City. [@2] It also includes 301.2 grams of shabu
seized in Valencia City, Bukidnon. [@2] PNP chief Police Lieutenant General Jose
Melencio Nartatez Jr. said the operations showed the force's efforts to bring
lawbreakers to justice. [@2]
