The Royal Saint Lucia Police Force laid charges against a Castries man after
seizing a significant quantity of illegal drugs during a targeted operation in
the north of the island. [@1] Subsequent investigations led to the arrest of
25-year-old Kesner Henry of Carellie, Castries. [@1] On November 18, Henry was
formally charged with importation of a controlled drug and importation of a
controlled drug with intent to supply. [@1]

According to police, the operation took place on October 30, when officers from
the Gangs, Narcotics and Firearms Unit together with the Customs and Excise
Department intercepted a suspicious package at a popular courier company in the
north of the island. [@1] Police said the parcel allegedly came from the United
States. [@1] Officers found the parcel to contain more than one kilogramme of
cannabis resin. [@1]

The force said the seizure forms part of a broader push to disrupt the flow of
illegal narcotics into the country. [@1] The force noted that inquiries into the
matter remain ongoing as part of a recent series of intensified drug-related
crackdowns by law enforcement. [@1]
