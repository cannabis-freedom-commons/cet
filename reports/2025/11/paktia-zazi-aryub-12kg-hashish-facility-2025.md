The Counter-Narcotics Directorate in Afghanistan’s Paktia province announced
that police forces had identified and dismantled a large hashish production and
processing facility in the Zazi Aryub district. [@1] The directorate said the
site was a large facility for producing and processing hashish. [@1] Police
forces identified the facility and dismantled it, the directorate said. [@1]

According to a statement from the directorate, officials seized 12 kilograms of
raw hashish. [@1] The seized cannabis product was described as raw hashish. [@1]
Officials also seized five sacks of materials used in the production process.
[@1] The directorate said those materials were used in the hashish production
process. [@1] The operation is part of ongoing anti-narcotics efforts in the
province, the directorate said. [@1]

Located in eastern Afghanistan, Paktia is considered one of the insecure regions
with widespread cultivation and trafficking of narcotics. [@1] The Taliban
administration claims to be combating drug production and trafficking. [@1] The
same account said multiple reports suggest that elements within the Taliban are
involved in the drug trade. [@1]
