Holon police station detectives and Border Police troops seized about 70
kilograms of drugs of various kinds in a housing unit on Moshe Sneh Street in
the town of Azor, next to Holon, at midday on Thursday. [@2] The forces raided
the flat as part of a planned enforcement action and under a court-issued search
warrant. [@1] The resident refused to open the door, officers broke in and found
a man in his 30s inside who admitted he was connected to the flat. [@3] The man
was arrested and taken for questioning at the Holon police station. [@3]

Inside, police found the drugs divided into plastic bags, gum containers and
Oreo cookie packages, packaged and ready for immediate distribution. [@1] Ynet
reported the seized substances included 60 kilograms of cannabis and six
kilograms of hashish in various forms, including hash butter, cookies and oil,
alongside hallucinogenic mushrooms. [@2] Israel Hayom reported the haul included
candies, cookies and e-cigarettes containing hashish and cannabis, and said
police suspected the housing unit had served as a centre for dealing in drugs.
[@3] Walla reported police putting the value of the seized drugs at more than
2.5 million shekels. [@4] Ynet reported that police assessed the drugs had
probably been smuggled into Israel from abroad. [@2]

The suspect was due to appear at Tel Aviv Magistrate's Court on Friday for a
remand hearing, and no charges were reported. [@1]
