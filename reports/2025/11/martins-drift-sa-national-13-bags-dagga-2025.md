A South African man en route to Zimbabwe was arrested at Martin’s Drift Border
Post after officers found 13 bags holding substances suspected to be dagga. [@1]
The arrest was made in a stop–question–and–search joint operation run by the
Botswana Police Service, the Department of Immigration and the Botswana Defence
Force. [@1]

Martin’s Drift acting station commander Assistant Superintendent Uyapo Koketso
said the man was driving a vehicle towing a trailer when officers found the
suspected dagga concealed among the luggage. [@1]
