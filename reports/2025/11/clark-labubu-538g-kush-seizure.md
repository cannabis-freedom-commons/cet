The Bureau of Customs said it seized P807,000 worth of kush, or high-grade
marijuana, concealed in a parcel declared as stuffed toy keychains at the Clark
port. [@1] The parcel held 538 grams of contraband, was sent from Hong Kong and
was bound for Biñan, Laguna. [@1] The shipment was declared as keychains and
arrived from Hong Kong on 15 November 2025. [@2]

The BOC’s X-ray Inspection Project flagged the parcel after scanners detected
suspicious images. [@2] Customs said the marijuana was found at the Port of
Clark on 19 November 2025. [@2] On 19 November, operatives of the Philippine
Drug Enforcement Agency and its K-9 unit carried out a follow-up inspection that
confirmed the presence of narcotics. [@2] A physical examination found two boxes
of Labubu toy keychains, each holding two sealed pouches of dried leaves and
fruiting tops suspected to be marijuana. [@2] Chemical analysis by PDEA verified
the substance as tetrahydrocannabinol, the active component of marijuana. [@2]

District Collector Jairus Reyes said the Port of Clark maintains a heightened
watch against drug smuggling. [@1] Customs Commissioner Ariel Nepomuceno said
the agency would keep working with law enforcement partners to protect the
public. [@1]
