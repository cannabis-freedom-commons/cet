Namibian police arrested multiple suspects across the country over the weekend
for dealing in and possession of prohibited drugs, with cannabis and mandrax
among the substances seized. [@1] In Windhoek, two male suspects aged 22 and 41
were arrested around midnight on Friday at the Okahandja checkpoint after a
search of passengers. [@1] In Keetmanshoop, two 34-year-old men were arrested on
Friday around 18:50 about 5km outside town along the Vaalgras–Koes road after
their vehicle was searched. [@1] Also in Keetmanshoop, a 23-year-old Namibian
man was arrested on Saturday at about 08:30 in Trans Namib location,
Tseiblaagte, after being found in possession of cannabis. [@1] In Oranjemund, a
23-year-old man was arrested on Friday around 21:00 at the border post after
cannabis was found hidden under the driver’s seat of his vehicle. [@1] Police
alleged that the Oranjemund driver was entering Namibia from South Africa. [@1]
In Khorixas, a 21-year-old male suspect was arrested around 03:00 on Saturday at
Hotel Sunrise Bar after being found in possession of cannabis. [@1] In Schlip, a
30-year-old male suspect was arrested on Saturday around 19:40 after being found
in possession of mandrax tablets, mandrax powder and cannabis. [@1]

At the Windhoek checkpoint, police found 1,935 grams of cannabis valued at
N$96,750 concealed in sugar and vetkoek flour bags. [@1] Outside Keetmanshoop,
officers discovered 411 grams of cannabis valued at N$20,550 in the searched
vehicle. [@1] In Tseiblaagte, the cannabis found in the man’s possession weighed
21 grams and was valued at N$1,050. [@1] At Oranjemund, the cannabis hidden
under the driver’s seat weighed 20 grams and was valued at N$1,000. [@1] In
Khorixas, the cannabis found was valued at N$450. [@1] In Schlip, the value of
the seized mandrax tablets, mandrax powder and cannabis was yet to be
determined. [@1]

The two Windhoek suspects, aged 22 and 41, were expected to appear in the
Wanaheda Magistrate’s Court soon. [@2] Police investigations continued in all
cases. [@1]
