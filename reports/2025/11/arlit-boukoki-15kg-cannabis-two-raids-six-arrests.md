The Arlit departmental unit of Niger's Office central de répression du trafic
illicite des stupéfiants (OCRTIS), the country's anti-drug office, carried out
two operations in the Boukoki 1 neighbourhood of Arlit on 17 November 2025. [@1]
Officers arrested six people, three of them members of the Forces de défense et
de sécurité (FDS), Niger's defence and security forces. [@1] The first
operation, at a house in Boukoki 1, found four balls of Amnesia-type cannabis
weighing 3.65 kg. [@1] Four people were arrested in that house, three of them
FDS members, for what the accounts call high-risk drug trafficking. [@3]
Officers also seized a handgun whose make and serial number had been filed off,
three 9 mm blank cartridges, an empty pistol magazine and an empty Kalashnikov
magazine. [@1]

A few hours later, still in Boukoki 1, a second operation targeted a goods truck
that had come from Maradi. [@1] Fourteen bricks of cannabis weighing 12.308 kg
in total were found hidden under the chassis. [@1] Two people were arrested and
the truck was impounded for the investigation. [@1] The truck was a TLM-DAF,
according to Les Echos du Niger. [@3] Together the two seizures came to more
than 15 kg of cannabis. [@1]

The account was attributed to a statement by the Nigerien police on Friday 21
November 2025. [@2] The unit said the seizures illustrated the security
challenges of an area between the regions of Agadez, Tahoua and Maradi through
which part of the drug traffic passes on its way north. [@1] Local authorities
called the involvement of security force members worrying, and OCRTIS officials
said they fight such members “with the same firmness as any other offender”.
[@1] The unit called on residents to be more vigilant and to cooperate closely
with the forces fighting drugs. [@1]
