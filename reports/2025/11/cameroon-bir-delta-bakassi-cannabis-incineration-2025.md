The incineration of the drugs took place on Monday at Bakassi, in Cameroon's
Southwest Region. [@1] Cameroon's Rapid Intervention Battalion (Bataillon
d'Intervention Rapide, or BIR) Delta burned the consignment it had seized
earlier in the month, according to military sources. [@2] The destruction was
carried out on 24 November under the supervision of the divisional officer of
Idabato, alongside defence and security forces. [@3]

Officers of the Brigade d'intervention rapide (BIR) seized 200 sacks of
cannabis, 200 packets of compressed cannabis and 300 packets of Tramadol. [@1]
After escorting the vessel to Jabane for a full inspection, authorities
uncovered nearly 200 bags of cannabis, about 200 packets of compressed cannabis
and 300 packets of Tramadol, an opioid painkiller. [@3] The seizure dates to 8
November, when BIR Delta intercepted a suspect vessel off Idabato carrying
irregular migrants from Ghana, Niger, Benin and Nigeria along with a large cargo
of narcotics. [@2] The vessel carried 62 irregular migrants, and the drugs were
concealed in hidden compartments of the boat. [@3]

The sub-prefect of Idabato praised BIR Delta's professionalism, recalling that
the corps remains essential to protecting the Bakassi peninsula. [@2] Security
officials said the shipment was intended for distribution through black-market
networks within and beyond the Bakassi zone. [@3]
