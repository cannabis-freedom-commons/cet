Zambia's Drug Enforcement Commission arrested seven people and seized 120.3 kg
of cannabis in operations in Southern and Western Provinces, it announced on 10
April 2025. [@2] DEC public relations officer Allan Tamba said two men were
arrested and formally charged in Southern Province with trafficking 91 prepacks
of high-grade cannabis weighing 103 kg. [@1] The consignment was being brought
into Zambia from another country through Livingstone District, the commission
said. [@2] The two men were Peter Mkandawire, 31, and Henry Mangisha, 32. [@2]

In Mongu District in Western Province, officers arrested two people in separate
incidents with a combined stated 8.6 kg of loose cannabis. [@2] The commission
named them as Chindele Kalwena, 30, held with 5.2 kg, and Makai Makai, 38, held
with 3.2 kg. [@2] In Kaoma District, Lubinda Liswani, 30, was charged over 250 g
of loose cannabis. [@2] In Sesheke District, Mukendwa Mundia, 24, and Choze
Choze, 19, were jointly charged over 21.3 g of loose cannabis. [@2]

The commission described the operations as part of its ongoing campaign to curb
illicit drug-related activities. [@2] It said it remained committed to promoting
a drug-free Zambia and urged the public to report suspicious drug activity to
the nearest DEC office. [@2]
