A 30-year-old man who worked as a doctor at a medical institution in Cimișlia
was arrested for 30 days on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@1] Special
investigative and prosecution measures established that the man had decided to
organise a small 'business' trading prohibited substances, which he distributed
among a narrow circle of people. [@1] He was detained in Cimișlia with the
support of the Fulger police unit while driving his personal car. [@1]

Police said they seized cocaine and marijuana. [@1] After a search of the car
and the suspect's home, law enforcement officers found and seized the cocaine
and marijuana along with devices for grinding and consuming drugs. [@1] They
also seized bank cards and a mobile phone that had been used to commit the
offences. [@1] Under current legislation a person found guilty of such a crime
faces a prison term of five to ten years. [@1]
