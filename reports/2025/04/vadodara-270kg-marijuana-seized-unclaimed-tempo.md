Harni police seized 270 kg of marijuana hidden in a secret compartment of an
Eicher tempo near the Comfort Inn hotel on the Ahmedabad-Mumbai National Highway
near Vadodara. [@1] The consignment was unclaimed, with no driver or other
person present when police found the tempo. [@1] Police raided the spot after
receiving information while on patrol that a tempo was carrying a large quantity
of marijuana. [@1]

Officers found 45 packets of marijuana in a hidden compartment built behind the
truck's cabin, valued at Rs 27 lakh. [@1] Along with the marijuana, police
seized a mobile phone and the Eicher tempo itself. [@1] Police put the total
value of the seized items, including the marijuana and other materials, at Rs 35
lakh. [@1] The Indian Express corroborated the find as an unattended truck found
150 km from a separate Surat seizure, holding 45 packets with 270 kg of cannabis
worth Rs 27 lakh. [@2]

Harni police started an investigation to establish who sent the consignment and
who was meant to receive it, and began searching for the accused. [@1] Police
Inspector S V Vasava of Harni police station said a process to identify the
owner of the vehicle had been initiated. [@2]
