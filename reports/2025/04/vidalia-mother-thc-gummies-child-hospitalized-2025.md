Vidalia police responded to a report of a child overdose at a local hospital on
11 April 2025. [@2] Officers determined the four-year-old had eaten THC gummies
at the home of 41-year-old Tiffany Martin. [@1] Martin was arrested and charged
with cruelty to children in the second degree and possession of a controlled
substance. [@1] The child was transferred to a Savannah hospital for more
treatment and was expected to make a full recovery. [@1]

The report came from Memorial Health Meadows Hospital in Vidalia. [@3]
Investigators said the THC edibles led to the hospitalization. [@3] Vidalia
police announced the arrest on Tuesday 15 April. [@2] A week later Martin
remained in custody at the Toombs County Jail, with bond set at $8,000,
according to jail records. [@3] Police said the Georgia Bureau of
Investigation's Eastman office helped in the case. [@2]
