Colombia's National Police seized 430 kg of creppy marijuana on the Amazon river
in the El Carbón sector, on the border with Peru. [@1] The operation was led by
specialized units of the Amazonas Police Department (Deama) and supported by the
Navy, after judicial police and intelligence work. [@1] Officers found the load
in an aluminum boat with two large outboard motors, an uncommon type of vessel
in the area. [@1] The boat was near the municipality of Puerto Nariño and the
corregimiento of Santa Sofía, a sub-municipal district. [@1]

A group of people on the riverbank fired at the officers and fled into the
jungle. [@1] No one was arrested. [@1] Police also seized 600 gallons of petrol
and the boat, which they valued at 600 million pesos. [@1]

Colonel Santiago Garavito Aranzazy, commander of the Amazonas police department,
said the marijuana carried three different markings, suggesting it came from
more than one source. [@1] He said the load was probably destined for Brazil's
internal market, where marijuana sells for more than it would on export. [@1]
Investigators are checking whether the load belongs to the Brazilian group
Comando Vermelho or to FARC dissident groups operating in southern Colombia.
[@1] The National Police's statement said investigations continued to establish
where the load came from and where it was going, and to find those responsible.
[@2]
