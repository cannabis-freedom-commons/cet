Belgian public health inspectors and police carried out a joint inspection of a
cannabis shop in central Brussels. [@1] It was the second search of the shop,
which is well known in the city centre. [@1] The case has been passed to the
justice system. [@1]

The search found hundreds of non-compliant products. [@1] Among the items seized
were CBD e-cigarettes, which are banned in Belgium. [@1] In a room upstairs
officers found a large stock of illegal cannabis derivatives. [@1] Police seized
a tear gas canister that was hidden behind the counter. [@1] Over a few months
the two searches at the shop netted 130,000 euros of prohibited goods. [@1] The
shop had already been fined 8,000 euros. [@1]

Since 1 April 2025 Belgian law has banned the visible display of tobacco and its
derivatives in shops. [@1] Even rolling papers must be kept in a closed
cupboard, out of the customer's sight. [@1] The manager said his shop was a
'cannabis museum' where, on his lawyer's advice, he could display whatever he
wanted. [@1] He told the inspectors that the stock upstairs was only 'aromatic
plants'. [@1] Mathieu Capouet of the health ministry's inspection service said
some products carried banned health claims and others had labels that did not
meet the rules, which require French and Dutch. [@1]
