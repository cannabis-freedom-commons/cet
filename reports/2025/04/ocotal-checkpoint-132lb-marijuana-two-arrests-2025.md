Nicaragua's National Police arrested Arlin Francisco Sánchez Reyes and José
David Alvarado Ávila at 11:40 in the morning on Tuesday 22 April 2025. [@1] The
two were passengers on a collective-transport bus on the Jalapa route when
officers searched them at a police checkpoint at kilometre 223.6 of the
Panamericana highway in Ocotal, Nueva Segovia. [@1] Sánchez Reyes was from
Jalapa and Alvarado Ávila from San Carlos, Río San Juan. [@1] Police in Nueva
Segovia announced the arrests at a press conference that Wednesday, through
spokesman Subinspector Walter Gutiérrez. [@1]

During the search officers found four bags holding 12 rectangular packages
wrapped in adhesive tape. [@1] A field test was positive for marijuana, with a
total weight of 132.8 pounds. [@1] Criminalistics experts confirmed the material
was marijuana, and the four bags and the drug were seized as evidence. [@1]

The arrests were carried out under the government's Muro de Contención strategy
against organised crime and drug trafficking. [@1] The two detainees were placed
at the order of the competent authorities for judicial proceedings. [@3] A
second outlet, El Informe Nicaragua, reported the same arrests in Nueva Segovia
with the same 132.8-pound weight. [@2]
