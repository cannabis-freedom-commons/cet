A 19-year-old man was arrested in Tsumeb in the Oshikoto region on Saturday 5
April 2025 after police found him with drugs they valued at N$860. [@1] The
Namibian Police Force's regional commander, commissioner Theopoline
Kalompho-Nashikaku, announced the arrest in a crime update issued on Sunday.
[@2] She said the incident happened at about 07h10 in the Kuvukiland location in
Tsumeb. [@1] Kalompho-Nashikaku said an intelligence-led operation by members of
the police drugs unit resulted in the seizure of the drugs and the apprehension
of the suspect. [@1]

Officers seized a cannabis cigarette roll, four bankies of cannabis, 40 grams of
cannabis seed, one mandrax tablet and three-quarters of a mandrax tablet. [@1]
Police valued the drugs seized from the teenager at N$860. [@1] The suspect was
also found with N$869 in cash, which the police said was discovered alongside
the drugs and is believed to be the proceeds of crime. [@1]

He was arrested and was to appear in the Tsumeb Magistrate's Court on Monday 7
April 2025. [@1]
