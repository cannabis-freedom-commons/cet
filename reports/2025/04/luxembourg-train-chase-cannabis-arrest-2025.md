A man was arrested in Luxembourg on 11 April 2025 during a routine check in a
train, after the canine unit of the country's Customs and Excise Administration
found him with drugs. [@1] The drug-detection dog Chase, part of the customs
canine brigade, led officers to the man during the routine train check. [@4] The
man tried to hide the drugs he was carrying from the officers, but failed. [@3]

The search that followed found about 40 grams of cocaine, eleven ecstasy tablets
and small quantities of hashish and marijuana. [@1] Officers also seized two
mobile phones. [@1]

The prosecutor ordered the man's arrest and he was brought before an
investigating judge, who ordered him held in pre-trial detention. [@1] During
the investigation it emerged that the man was staying illegally in the Schengen
area. [@4] French authorities later confirmed that he was subject to an entry
ban for the Schengen area. [@2]
