Officers from the District A-7 (East Boston) and C-6 (South Boston) drug control
units arrested Anthony Francisco, 34, of Fall River, on drug trafficking charges
at about 1:45 p.m. on Wednesday. [@1]

Officers were carrying out a drug investigation and search warrants in the area
of 34 Westwind Road in Dorchester. [@1] The search warrants turned up about 70
grams of cocaine, 20 grams of crack cocaine, four pounds (about 1.8 kilograms)
of marijuana, $6,062 in cash and $20,550 in Dominican Republic currency. [@1]

Francisco was charged with trafficking of a Class B substance, possession with
intent to distribute a Class B substance and possession with intent to
distribute a Class D substance. [@1] At his arraignment in South Boston court he
pleaded not guilty and was held on $10,000 cash bail, with a hearing scheduled
for May. [@1]
