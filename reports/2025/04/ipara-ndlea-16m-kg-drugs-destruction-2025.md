The National Drug Law Enforcement Agency (NDLEA) publicly destroyed about 1.6 million kilogrammes of seized drugs at Ipara, along the Lagos-Ibadan Expressway in Ogun State, on 12 April 2025, the largest volume it has burned at one time. [@1,2] Cannabis made up the bulk at 1.4 million kilogrammes, alongside 123kg of cocaine, 46.8kg of heroin, 148,000kg of codeine syrup, 3,244.26kg of tramadol, 1,544kg of skuchies and 111kg of methamphetamine. [@1,2]

Agency spokesman Femi Babafemi said the destruction covered seizures by commands under Zone 9 and Zone 11, including Lagos, Ogun, Oyo, the Murtala Muhammed airport, seaports and the Idiroko and Seme border posts. [@1,2] Chairman Brig. Gen. Mohamed Buba Marwa, represented by Director of Assets and Financial Investigation Ibrahim Abdul, said each consignment passed laboratory tests and was destroyed with court approval after its case closed. [@1,2] Marwa warned traffickers there would be no safe haven and noted the agency had convicted 10,572 offenders in four years. [@1,2]
