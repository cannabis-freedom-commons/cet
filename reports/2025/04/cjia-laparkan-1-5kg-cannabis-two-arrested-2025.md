Customs officers of the Guyana Revenue Authority and CANU officers arrested
Machaia Andrews and Lemme Campbell on Wednesday 30 April 2025 after searching
one incoming barrel from the United States and finding several parcels of
foreign cannabis. [@2] The two men were escorted to CANU headquarters with the
cannabis, which the unit said weighed 1.502 kilograms. [@2] Campbell, who is 60,
had gone to the Laparkan bond at Cheddi Jagan International Airport to clear the
barrel when agents arrested him. [@1] Stabroek News, citing the CANU release,
placed the interception at the Laparkan Cargo Shed at Cheddi Jagan International
Airport. [@3]

CANU head James Singh said four pounds of foreign marijuana was found among
cereal in the barrel posted from the United States. [@1] Singh said Campbell was
allegedly trying to clear GY$4 million worth of that foreign marijuana. [@1]

The following Friday, Andrews appeared before Magistrate Fortune at the
Friendship Magistrate's Court, pleaded not guilty to drug trafficking and was
granted $300,000 bail. [@4] He was also to report to CANU every other Friday
from 16 May. [@4] Campbell was denied bail and remanded to prison. [@4] He faced
charges of possession of 1.502 kilograms of cannabis and of aiding Andrews in
trafficking. [@4] Both men were to return to court on 9 June 2025. [@4]

Campbell, his wife and two other people had been convicted in Barbados in 2009
for possession and trafficking of marijuana and cocaine in November 2005. [@1]
They were sentenced to 20 years' imprisonment. [@1] He and his wife were later
ordered released on time served by the Barbados Court of Appeal. [@1]
