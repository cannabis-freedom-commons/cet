Police in Daloa, Côte d'Ivoire, arrested the man who owned a suitcase containing
the drugs, identified only as L.K., aged 30, at the city's main bus station.
[@1] He had been apprehended after quietly leaving the scene during the check.
[@2] The Daloa regional branch of the Direction de la Police des Stupéfiants et
des Drogues (DPSD) identified and arrested the presumed owner of the suitcase.
[@2]

The drugs were found hidden in the suitcase in the luggage compartment of an
intercity coach on the Abidjan–Daloa route during a search on the night of
Thursday 18 to Friday 19 April 2025. [@1] The seizure amounted to 15 blocks of
cannabis with a total weight of 15 kilograms. [@1] Police described the
discovery as a significant quantity of drugs hidden in a suitcase stored in the
hold of a public transport coach. [@2]

The operation was part of Épervier 10, a large-scale anti-drug trafficking
operation by Ivorian security forces. [@1] Police said the case showed how
traffickers keep using public transport networks to carry illicit substances
across Côte d'Ivoire. [@1]
