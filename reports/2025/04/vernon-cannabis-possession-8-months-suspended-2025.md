A 24-year-old Belgian woman living in Vernon appeared before the court in Évreux
on 1 April 2025 charged with possessing drugs. [@1]

A first search of her flat in Vernon on 9 December 2024, after neighbours
complained of suspected domestic violence, found 100 grams of cannabis resin,
which she said was for her own use. [@1] On a later search, a police sniffer dog
stopped at the same flat and officers found 75 grams of heroin, 12 grams of
cocaine, a slab of cannabis resin cut into 19 pieces, ketamine, a knife and a
precision scale. [@1] She told the court the heroin belonged to a friend and
that she had been addicted to cocaine until it became too expensive. [@1]

The court found her guilty as charged and sentenced her to eight months in
prison, suspended, entered on her criminal record. [@1]
