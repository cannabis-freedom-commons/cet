National gendarmes in Cameroon's West Region arrested two people travelling with
a truck carrying 50 sacks of cannabis. [@1] According to Equinoxe Television's
account, the two were arrested aboard the truck, which held 50 sacks each
containing about 100 kg of cannabis. [@1]

The load totalled about 5,000 kg of cannabis. [@1] The cannabis had been
carefully hidden in the truck among food produce. [@1] The shipment had left
Magba in Noun department and was meant to supply Cameroon's main towns. [@1] The
cargo was bound for the national market. [@1]

The national gendarmerie said the swoop succeeded thanks to cooperation from
local people. [@1] Officers displayed the seized load to the press on the parade
ground of the West Gendarmerie Legion in the presence of West Region governor
Augustine Awa Fonka. [@1] Equinoxe Television said this latest load brought the
quantity seized by the gendarmerie in the West Region in one month to nearly
15,000 kg. [@1]
