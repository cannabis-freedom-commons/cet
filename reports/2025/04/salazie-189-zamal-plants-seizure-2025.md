Gendarmes of the autonomous territorial brigade of Salazie discovered a large
cannabis cultivation in Salazie, on Réunion, on 2 April 2025. [@1] They came
across it during a mission de prévention de proximité, a community prevention
patrol. [@1] One man suspected of cultivating the crop was placed in garde à
vue, or police custody, for offences against the legislation on narcotic
products and given a convocation en justice, a summons to appear in court. [@1]

At the site officers seized 189 pieds, or mature plants, and 49 pousses, or
young seedlings, of zamal. [@1] Zamal is a local variety of cannabis
particularly widespread on Réunion. [@2] The plants were described as carefully
tended. [@3]

The plantation was hidden in a difficult-to-access sector of Salazie. [@3] The
operation took place in the Hauts de l'île, the island's mountainous highlands.
[@3] The crop was described as apparently intended to supply local drug
trafficking. [@3] The seized plants were destroyed in accordance with procedure.
[@2]
