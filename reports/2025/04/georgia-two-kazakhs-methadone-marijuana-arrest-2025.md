Two citizens of Kazakhstan were arrested in Georgia on suspicion of illegally
acquiring, storing and attempting to sell narcotic substances. [@1] The Georgian
Interior Ministry reported the detentions as occurring at some point during the
one-month period from 17 March to 16 April 2025. [@1] During searches of the
suspects themselves and of their car, police officers found narcotic substances
that were described as prepared for sale. [@1] The police account listed the
seized drugs prepared for sale as methadone and marijuana. [@1]

Charges in the cases were reported as presented within the framework of the
country-wide anti-drug campaign. [@1] Under current Georgian legislation, the
detainees would face from 20 years of imprisonment to life imprisonment if their
guilt is established. [@1] The two cases were presented as part of a large-scale
police operation in which 65 people were detained in one month, most of them on
suspicion of selling drugs. [@1] Those detained in the wider month-long
operation included Georgian citizens as well as foreign nationals. [@1]
