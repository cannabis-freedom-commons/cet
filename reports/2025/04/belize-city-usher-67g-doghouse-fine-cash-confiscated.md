Christopher Brian Usher, 27, of Belize City, and his two older sisters appeared
in Belize City Magistrate's Court Number Five on 3 April 2025, jointly charged
with possession of 67.9 grams of marijuana with intent to supply. [@1] The
charge followed a police operation at the family's Belize City home the previous
afternoon. [@1]

Usher pleaded guilty to simple possession of the cannabis but not guilty to
intending to supply it, and his sisters pleaded not guilty. [@1] Because the
weight was 7.9 grams over the trafficking threshold and Usher said the cannabis
was for personal use, the prosecutor accepted his plea to the lesser charge.
[@1] The magistrate fined him 300 dollars plus a five-dollar court cost, payable
by March 30 or two weeks in prison in default. [@1] The charge against the
sisters was withdrawn after his plea and they were released. [@1]

Officers searched the family's fenced property, which holds three houses, at
about 4:30 p.m. on 2 April under a warrant for illegal drugs. [@1] Police said
the 67.9 grams of suspected cannabis were in a plastic bag inside a doghouse on
the property. [@1] Inside Usher's locked house, which he opened for the
officers, police found a red school bag under his bed holding 10,738.40 dollars
in Belize and US currency. [@1] The family home was on Amara Avenue in Belize
City, and police put the cannabis find there on 2 April 2025. [@2]

Police seized the unexplained cash and contacted the Financial Intelligence Unit
and the Belize Tax Service. [@1] Usher was detained again by police after the
hearing pending investigation into the money. [@1] The Belize Tax Service then
took half of the 10,738.40 dollars, about 5,369 dollars. [@1] The second outlet
reported that half of the money was taken by the Belize Tax Service after his
conviction. [@2]
