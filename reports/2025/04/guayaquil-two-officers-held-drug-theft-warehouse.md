A National Police lieutenant and a second sergeant were detained for drug
trafficking in Guayas on 10 April 2025. [@1] The capture occurred in south
Guayaquil in an operation planned after a month of investigation. [@1] The
investigation opened after police identified a truck with about 18 kilos of
drugs in March. [@1] Images showed two police officers taking some drug packages
and carrying them away. [@1]

Following a judicial order, raids were carried out and a lieutenant and a second
sergeant were detained. [@1] Both officers were given preventive detention and
transferred to the Penitenciaría del Litoral. [@1] Authorities are investigating
what links the two may have with criminal groups. [@1]

That same Thursday authorities also raided the Las Orquídeas citadel, where they
found a warehouse storing more than 50 kilos of controlled substances, including
cocaine and marijuana, according to police. [@1] Two military-use rifles and
ammunition were also found at the warehouse. [@1] The warehouse also held
National Police vests and State Attorney General's Office jackets. [@1]
According to authorities, criminal-group members divided the drugs there for
retail sale in different parts of the north of the city. [@1]

Police also found counterfeit banknotes at the site. [@1] Pascuales District
chief Ginno Zanzi said the false money is used for scams outside banks. [@1] La
República EC independently repeated the same account, reporting raids in Las
Orquídeas on 10 April and a warehouse holding more than 50 kilos including
marijuana and cocaine. [@2]
