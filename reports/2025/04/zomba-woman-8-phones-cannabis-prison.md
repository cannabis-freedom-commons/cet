On 6 April 2025, Esmey Hamisi visited Zomba Maximum Prison to see her relative
Alli Candida. [@1] Hamisi is 49 and from Makanjila Village in Mangochi District.
[@1] A prison warder searched her food basket as standard procedure and
discovered hidden contraband. [@1] Hamisi was arrested and taken to Zomba Police
Station for questioning. [@1] She has been charged with introducing prohibited
items into a prison and is expected to appear in court soon. [@1]

She brought a food basket containing eight brand-new Itel cellphones, five phone
chargers, five memory cards and eight Itel batteries. [@1] The basket also held
loose cannabis sativa. [@2] The cannabis was described as Indian hemp locally
known as chamba. [@3] The items were hidden inside an empty plastic sugar
packet. [@1]

According to police, Hamisi visited the prison with food for a relative serving
a sentence there. [@3] Candida is a Mozambican national serving an 18-year
sentence for robbery. [@2] Police warned that prison security checks remain
strict and uncompromising. [@3]
