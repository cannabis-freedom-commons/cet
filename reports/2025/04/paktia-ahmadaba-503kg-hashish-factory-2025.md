Three suspects were arrested in Ahmadaba district in connection with the drugs
recovered from the Zar Gul factory. [@1] The arrested suspects will remain under
initial investigation before being handed over to judicial authorities for
further proceedings. [@1] Police headquarters reported that officers uncovered a
drug factory in the Zar Gul area of the district during a raid. [@1] Police said
on Wednesday that the Counter-Narcotics Department carried out the seizure in
southeastern Paktia province. [@1]

The Counter-Narcotics Department seized 503kg of raw hashish in the Ahmadaba
district of southeastern Paktia province. [@1] Officers also recovered 619kg of
cannabis seeds from inside the same factory premises in Ahmadaba district. [@1]
Officers also seized a pick-up vehicle found at the factory alongside the
hashish and cannabis seeds. [@1] Officers also seized a hashish processing table
together with other related drug items from the factory. [@1]

The Zar Gul site operated as a drug factory where raw hashish was kept and
processed with the seized table and equipment. [@1] The raid therefore removed a
working factory stock of more than half a tonne of hashish plus seeds, a vehicle
and processing gear. [@1]
