A judge in Los Santos sentenced four men to 120 months in prison for
international drug trafficking after a plea agreement. [@2] The four were Alex
Toribio Mena, Alexis Varela, Víctor Alfonso León and Eurípides Sinisterra, of
Panamanian, Costa Rican and Colombian nationality. [@2]

The case began on 13 May 2024, when the National Aeronaval Service intercepted a
boat southeast of Punta Mala. [@2] Officers found 1,182 packages of marijuana on
the boat. [@1] The Los Santos drug prosecutor's office brought the plea
agreements that produced the sentences. [@1]

In a second hearing the same prosecutor's office obtained 100-month sentences
for two Colombians, Deyvi Cuero and Edinson Cabeza, for international drug
trafficking. [@2] That case began on 4 March 2024 with the seizure of 22
packages of cocaine hydrochloride southeast of Punta Mala. [@2] The judge
ordered the confiscation of the boat as an accessory penalty in the cocaine
case. [@2] Mi Diario said the two cases showed Panama's coasts being used as a
key route for international drug trafficking. [@1]
