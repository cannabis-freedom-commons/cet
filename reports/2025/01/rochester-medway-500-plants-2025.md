Kent Police executed a warrant at Victory Park in Trident Close on the Medway
City Estate in Rochester on 20 January 2025. [@2] The raid targeted a building
on the business estate. [@2] Officers found about 500 cannabis plants together
with growing equipment at the premises. [@1]

Police gave the plants an estimated street value of about £200,000. [@1]
Officers seized the plants and destroyed the cultivation. [@1]

Police said inquiries were continuing to identify those responsible for the
cultivation, with no arrests reported in either account. [@1] Detective Sergeant
Matthew Sherriff said the large-scale production of cannabis was linked to
violent and serious organised crime, as well as modern-day slavery and the
exploitation of vulnerable people. [@1] He said police would always strive to
stamp out this illegal activity and bring the perpetrators to justice, and both
accounts asked anyone with relevant information to contact Kent Police. [@1]
