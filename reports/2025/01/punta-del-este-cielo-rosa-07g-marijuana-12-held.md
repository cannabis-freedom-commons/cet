The operation began in the early hours of Saturday 4 January 2025, when
Maldonado's Departmental Anti-Drug Brigade stopped an Argentine couple on
Avenida Aparicio Saravia. [@3] Twelve Argentine nationals were detained in the
operation at resorts in the department of Maldonado. [@1] The couple was found
with four nylon wrappings of pink powder known as tusi, a synthetic drug. [@2]
Police then searched rooms at two luxury hotels where the group was staying, the
Barradas Park and the Park Hotel. [@1]

The police report recorded 0.7 grams of marijuana among the items seized in the
searches. [@2] The same haul included 20 ecstasy tablets, 194 grams of tusi,
20.6 grams of MDMA, six grams of a white crystalline substance, two
Argentine-registered vehicles, phones and cash. [@2] Investigators said the
drugs were intended for sale at electronic-music parties in Punta del Este. [@1]

Eight of the detainees, seven men and one woman, were charged with negotiating
in narcotic substances and ordered to serve 120 days of pre-trial detention.
[@3] The group called itself the Young Rich Gang and came mainly from the north
of Buenos Aires province; several members were trap and freestyle musicians.
[@1] Sources to the case said the evidence could bring prison terms of between
two and a half and seven years. [@1]
