Bradford Crown Court jailed Ricky Middleton and Kane Webster on 3 January 2025
for leading a cannabis supply conspiracy. [@1] Kane Webster, 29, of Kingston
Road, Bradford, was jailed for 47 months, while Ricky Middleton, 28, of Duston
Approach, Seacroft, was jailed for 48 months. [@1] Both men "played leading
roles" in an operation to buy and sell cannabis, Judge Christopher Smith told
Bradford Crown Court on Friday. [@1] Four others, Roy Sunderland, Holly Winder,
Thomas Smith and Clare Lockett, were given suspended sentences for the parts
they played. [@1] Sunderland, of Parkwood Rise, Keighley, was given a 19-month
sentence, suspended for 16 months. [@1] Smith, of Wheat Street, Keighley, was
given a 23-month sentence, suspended for 18 months. [@1] Winder, of Hive Street,
Keighley, was given a 15-month sentence, suspended for 15 months. [@1] Lockett,
of Crescent View, Keighley, was given a 19-month sentence, suspended for 18
months - and 25 Rehabilitation Activity Requirement days. [@1] A seventh
defendant, Patrick Wilson, 31, of Highfield Grove, Bradford, faces a trial of
issue in April and will be sentenced at a later date. [@2]

"This case involves supplying hundreds, if not thousands, of users with
cannabis," Judge Smith said. [@1] Judge Smith described the enterprise as a
"long-running and undoubtedly lucrative conspiracy to supply cannabis to
hundreds of users". [@1] Judge Smith said at its peak in August 2020 the phone
was receiving up to 8,000 requests a month. [@1] He added that between 2019 and
2021 the number had been contacted by up to 2,000 different phone numbers. [@1]
When Middleton was arrested police found 1.428kg of cannabis worth £9,812 as
well as drug equipment and weapons. [@3] Police searches discovered around 200g
of cannabis worth just under £2,000 at Sunderland's address, 176g at Smith's
home worth £1,470, and 1.75kg at Winder's home worth £9,630. [@3] The court
heard that cannabis seized by police, valued at £24,000, was just a snapshot of
the stock held by those involved. [@3]

He described Winder, 56-year-old Sunderland and 33-year-old Smith as being
"trusted members" of Webster's and Middleton's gang who "professionally"
packaged and sold the cannabis. [@1] Jailing Ricky Middleton and Kane Webster at
Bradford Crown Court, the judge said they had been responsible for very
significant dealing in cannabis on a commercial scale over a long period of
time. [@2] Judge Smith ordered the forfeiture and destruction of all the drugs
and related equipment seized by police. [@2]
