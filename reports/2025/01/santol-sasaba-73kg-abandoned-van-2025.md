On the morning of Monday 13 January 2025, Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency
officers and La Union police opened a Mitsubishi Delica van abandoned by the
roadside in Barangay Sasaba, Santol, and found 73 rolls of dried cannabis
leaves, each wrapped in transparent plastic and tape, weighing 73 kilograms.
[@1,2] At the Dangerous Drugs Board standard price of P120,000 per kilogram,
the haul was valued at P8.76 million. [@1,2]

PDEA Region 1 director Joel Plaza named the suspects as two Sasaba residents
who had fled a checkpoint the previous Friday when they noticed the
authorities. [@1,2] Operatives kept the abandoned van under surveillance for
several days before securing a search warrant to open it on Monday. [@1,2]
Plaza said the two would be charged under the Comprehensive Dangerous Drugs
Act. [@1,2]
