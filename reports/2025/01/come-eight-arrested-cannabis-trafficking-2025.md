Eight people were arrested in Comè, in Benin's Mono department, on Saturday 18
January 2025, on suspicion of cannabis trafficking, theft and handling stolen
goods. [@4] Police opened an inquiry after reports that a gang was operating in
the arrondissement. [@3] The inquiry located the gang in the Agouèvi Dogba and
Nongo quarters, where officers carried out a raid. [@2] The eight were placed in
police custody and were to be presented to the prosecutor. [@5]

Officers seized an important quantity of chanvre indien, the French term for
cannabis, divided into nine sachets and 97 boulettes (balls). [@2] The same raid
turned up 12 jerrycans of vegetable oil, 12 blister packs of Tramadol tablets,
mobile phones and 80,000 CFA francs in cash. [@1]

The Direction générale de la Police républicaine announced the arrests on 20
January 2025. [@1] The police said the operation showed the force's
effectiveness against crime and called for closer cooperation between residents
and officers. [@2]
