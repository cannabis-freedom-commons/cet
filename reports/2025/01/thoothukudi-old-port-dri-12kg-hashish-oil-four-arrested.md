Officers of the Directorate of Revenue Intelligence seized 12 kg of ganja oil,
also described as hash oil, at the Old Harbour Port in Thoothukudi. [@1] The DRI
arrested four people, among them a Central Industrial Security Force head
constable accused of helping move the oil into the port for illegal transport to
the Maldives. [@1] The agency valued the oil at Rs 30 crore in the grey market.
[@1]

The DRI searched the old port premises on a Wednesday after a tip-off. [@1] The
oil was found in the port premises and was about to be exported to the Maldives.
[@1] The old harbour runs more than a dozen boats ferrying goods to the Maldives
and nearby islands. [@1]

Investigators found that Suthakar, 36, Jesuraj, 34, and Kingsley, 56, had taken
the contraband into the premises. [@1] The suspects said CISF head constable
Marimuthu helped them move the oil into the port. [@1] All four were arrested
and remanded. [@1]

Marimuthu took more than 20 tablets meant for heart ailments in a suicide
attempt and was admitted to Thoothukudi Medical College Hospital. [@1] Further
investigation was under way. [@1]
