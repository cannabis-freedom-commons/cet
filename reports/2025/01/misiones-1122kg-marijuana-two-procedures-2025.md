A coordinated Gendarmería Nacional operation in Misiones province on the night
of 5 January 2025 produced two seizures totalling 1,122 kg 570 g of cannabis
sativa. [@1]

In the first procedure, gendarmes of the Puerto Rico section of Escuadrón 11
“San Ignacio”, patrolling on foot near the paraje of Puerto Rinkel, saw an
apparently abandoned boat on the bank of the Paraná River. [@1] After securing
the scene and searching the area, they found 21 bundles in the scrub holding 516
kg 680 g of cannabis sativa. [@1] The federal court and federal prosecutor's
office in Oberá intervened in that procedure. [@1]

The second procedure was carried out by a motorised patrol of Escuadrón 10
“Eldorado” in dense scrub near the international border river with Paraguay.
[@1] Officers saw fresh trails, searched several metres of the area and found 26
bundles of marijuana buds and two more containing 50 rectangular packets of
compacted plant material. [@1] That second haul weighed 605 kg 890 g. [@1] The
federal court and federal prosecutor's office in Eldorado intervened in the
second procedure. [@1] A Paraguayan outlet reported the same operation, carried
out with the collaboration of several force units in the San Ignacio and
Eldorado areas of Misiones. [@2] It said the drug was distributed in 71
packages. [@2]
