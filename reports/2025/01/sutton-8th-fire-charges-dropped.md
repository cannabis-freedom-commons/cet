York police raided 8th Fire Co, an Indigenous-owned cannabis store at 120 High
Street in Sutton, Georgina, on 7 March 2024, after the shop opened in February
without a provincial licence. [@1] The owner, Jared Big Canoe, said he had been
arrested and intended to defend the case at a court appearance set for 8 May
2024 in Newmarket, invoking Indigenous rights to sell cannabis off-reserve. [@1]
He told YorkRegion.com he was proud to be arrested so his children would not
have to go through the same fight. [@1]

On 29 January 2025 the Crown told him it would withdraw all charges. [@2] Under
the agreement the Crown kept the seized cannabis and $2,000 in cash, and he got
his phone back. [@2]
