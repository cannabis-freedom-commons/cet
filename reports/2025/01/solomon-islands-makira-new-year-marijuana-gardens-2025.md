Kirakira police in Makira-Ulawa Province confiscated tools used for the
production of kwaso, a home-brewed alcohol, in parts of Makira Island on New
Year. [@1] Provincial Police Commander Peter Sitai said police teams were sent
to areas of Makira where people were producing kwaso and also uprooted marijuana
gardens. [@1]

Sitai said police also attended to incidents that were kwaso and alcohol
related. [@1] He said police also attended to reports of an increasing number of
black markets selling alcohol in the New Year period. [@1]

Sitai said that on the whole the Christmas and New Year period in Makira-Ulawa
Province was very peaceful and people were happy. [@1]
