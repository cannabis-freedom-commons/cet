Armenia's Interior Ministry said it had stopped the sale of about 18 kg of drugs
brought in from Iran, part for the Armenian market and part for onward smuggling
into Georgia. [@1] Thirteen people were arrested and a criminal case was opened.
[@2] The overall haul was described as 18 kg, with 13 people detained. [@3] The
operation involved the Interior Ministry's criminal police drug unit and the
State Revenue Committee. [@2]

A 40-year-old Yerevan man was detained on suspicion of smuggling and illegally
selling drugs, with 3.46 kg of a substance resembling marijuana in his car. [@1]
He was due to hand the drugs to a 40-year-old resident of Ararat province, who
was detained the same day on the Yerevan–Yeraskh highway just after taking the
package. [@1] A 51-year-old from Yerevan and a 46-year-old from Kotayk province
were also detained; the latter's car held 142 g of suspected marijuana and 106 g
of a crystalline substance resembling methamphetamine. [@1]

On Mount Lalvar in Lori province, near the Georgian border, a 46-year-old
Armenian holding Georgian citizenship and a 36-year-old Yerevan man were
detained. [@1] The older man retrieved two packages holding 5.5 kg of suspected
marijuana from a cache on the mountain, which the group planned to carry into
Georgia. [@1] On Tigran Mets Avenue in Yerevan, a 38-year-old and a 39-year-old
from Syunik province were detained with 1.15 kg of suspected marijuana, 202 g of
suspected methamphetamine and an automatic rifle with 28 rounds in their car.
[@1] Two men from Shirak province, aged 33 and 32, were detained in Yeghvard,
Kotayk province, with 1.2 kg of suspected marijuana and 85 smaller packages
containing 163 g. [@1]

A 32-year-old Yerevan man stopped on the Yeghvard highway on 20 January had six
packages of suspected marijuana weighing about 6 kg in his car. [@1] The
ministry said all those identified had been arrested and that the investigation
continued, with efforts under way to identify the senders in Iran and their
accomplices in Armenia. [@1]
