Police arrested four men at Beitbridge Total Service Station on 1 January 2025
and said they were found in possession of dagga. [@1] The men were suspected of
smuggling the dagga into Zimbabwe through the Beitbridge Border Post. [@1] The
four were named as Shupikai Maruta (45), Rina Zvokureva (53), Branham Nyamandu
(32) and Anthony Mauchi (53). [@1] Maruta, Zvokureva and Nyamandu are from
Bindura, and Mauchi is from Gutu. [@1]

Matabeleland South acting provincial police spokesperson Assistant Inspector
Stanford Mguni said the men were seen at the service station looking for
transport when officers approached them and asked to search their bags. [@1] The
search found 34 kilograms of dagga in the bags. [@1] Mguni put the street value
of the dagga at about US$3,500. [@1]

Police said they believed the dagga had been smuggled into the country with the
help of an umalaitsha, a cross-border transporter. [@1] Mguni said police would
continue operations to fight smuggling. [@1]
