Four people were arrested in three separate anti-narcotics raids across Cachar
and Karbi Anglong districts of Assam, the state's police said. [@1] In one of
the raids, Cachar Police recovered 73.97 kg of ganja from Dhanehari, a place
under Sonai Police Station in Cachar district. [@2] Police valued the ganja at
Rs 38 lakh. [@1]

Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma said the three operations together seized
drugs worth more than Rs 11 crore. [@1] Cachar Police seized 1.17 kg of
suspected heroin, valued at Rs 5.1 crore, at Dighar Fulertol late on Monday.
[@1] At Dillai Tiniali in Karbi Anglong, officers intercepted a bus from a
neighbouring state and seized 1.22 kg of suspected heroin worth Rs 6 crore. [@1]

Police said investigations were continuing into the origins and intended
destinations of the drugs, and that further arrests were likely. [@3] Sarma
congratulated the Assam Police on social media after the operations. [@2]
