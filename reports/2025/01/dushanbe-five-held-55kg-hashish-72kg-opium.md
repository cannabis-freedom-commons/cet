Officers of the Dushanbe Police Department detained members of an organized
criminal group involved in drug trafficking. [@2] Five people were arrested and
the ringleader was identified as Shamsullo Hukumatov, a Dushanbe resident
previously charged for similar offences. [@2] The group's leader was identified
as 50-year-old Dushanbe resident Shamsullo Hukumatov, who had previously faced
criminal prosecution for similar offences. [@1]

Police said Hukumatov bought the drugs from Afghan dealers and organized sales
through his accomplices. [@1] He told interrogators that he sold one kilogram of
drugs for $1,500. [@1] The operation seized 127 kg of various narcotics,
including hashish and opium. [@1] The operation seized 55 kg of hashish and 72
kg of opium, a reported total of 127 kg of narcotics. [@1]

All five suspects were placed in a pretrial detention facility. [@1] A criminal
case was opened against them under part 4 of Article 200 of Tajikistan's
Criminal Code, covering large-scale drug trafficking for the purpose of sale.
[@1] That offence carries 12 to 20 years' imprisonment. [@1]
