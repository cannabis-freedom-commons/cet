On the evening of Tuesday 14 January 2025, Roxas police manning a Comelec
checkpoint at Barangay Nuesa tried to stop a speeding black SUV, which rammed
through the barricades and a patrol vehicle instead. [@1,2] Officers gave
chase for about two hours before catching the SUV and arresting its two
occupants in Mallig town. [@1,2]

Inside the vehicle police found 222 bricks of dried marijuana leaves valued
at P26.64 million, 19 rolled marijuana leaves worth P2,280, and a hand
grenade. [@1,2] The two men, described as coming from Mandaluyong City in
Metro Manila, were to face charges for violating the Comprehensive Dangerous
Drugs Act and for illegal possession of an explosive. [@2]
