Vice and narcotics detectives of the Jefferson County Sheriff's Office stopped a
vehicle on Interstate 20 East at Kilgore Memorial Drive in Jefferson County.
[@1] Officers recovered 50 pounds (22,679.00 grams) of marijuana in the stop.
[@1] Three people were arrested after the traffic stop led to the discovery of
the marijuana. [@1]

Those arrested were 41-year-old Alexandro Marcos Favela, 42-year-old Dennis
Alexander Marroquin and 41-year-old Yolanda Alessandra Tapia Fevela. [@1] Fevela
was charged with drug trafficking with a bond of $500,000. [@1] Marroquin was
charged with drug trafficking with no bond and Tapia was charged with drug
trafficking with no bond. [@1]

Officers also seized a total of $5,727. [@1] All three suspects were held at the
Jefferson County Jail. [@1]
