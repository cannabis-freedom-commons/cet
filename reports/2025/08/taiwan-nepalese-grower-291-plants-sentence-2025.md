A Nepalese cannabis kingpin, Chhetri Ganesh Roka, was sentenced to 10-and-a-half
years in prison after expanding his growing operation in Taiwan tenfold while on
the run. [@1] The penalty came in an Aug. 26 Shilin District Court ruling, and
the court found Roka with 291 plants worth an estimated NT$20 million
(US$650,852) after police raids in Taoyuan and New Taipei City. [@1] Roka had
been a fugitive after failing to report for a seven-and-a-half-year prison
sentence finalized in July 2020 for possessing 29 plants and five seedlings.
[@2]

In January he was apprehended by the Third Special Police Corps, which raided a
cannabis farm in New Taipei City's Tamsui District run by Mexican man Santiago
Alfonso Balado Yrizar and learned Roka had supplied the grow operation's plants
and equipment. [@1]

The Shilin District Court found Balado Yrizar guilty of manufacturing marijuana
and of using a fake licence plate for his car, giving him 22 months and two
months respectively. [@1] Both sentences were suspended for five years, the
court citing his cooperation with police by giving them information about Roka.
[@1] Taiwanese national Ma Yu-chieh (馬裕傑), identified as a co-conspirator, was
sentenced to 14.5 years for distributing a Category 2 narcotic. [@3] All of the
court's sentences are subject to appeal. [@1]
