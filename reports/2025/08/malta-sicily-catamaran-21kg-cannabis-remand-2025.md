Two 27-year-olds, Osmajic Branko of Montenegro and Nikolina Vranic of Serbia,
were arraigned in Malta on the morning of 5 August 2025 and pleaded not guilty.
[@1] The charges were smuggling cocaine, cannabis and ecstasy pills from Sicily
into Malta. [@1] Their lawyers did not request bail, and the court remanded the
pair in custody. [@2]

Police stopped the pair as they drove off a catamaran from Sicily and found the
drugs in false compartments in the floor and rear of their car. [@2] A court
heard that 21 kg of cannabis grass, 3 kg of cocaine and 750 ecstasy pills were
found in the car. [@1] Police estimated the street value of the three drugs at
€650,000. [@3] Times of Malta, citing the police statement, reported 15 kilos of
suspected cannabis resin. [@3] Newsbook put the cannabis at about 15 kg of a
substance suspected to be cannabis. [@2]

The court was told the pair had no fixed address in Malta, with the woman
holding a seasonal agricultural job and the man unemployed. [@1] Defence lawyer
Roberto Montalto told the court that Vranic is a new mother who is breastfeeding
and wishes to continue. [@1] The court recommended that Child Correctional
Services arrange for her to keep breastfeeding in prison. [@1] Magistrate Lara
Lanfranco presided, and Attorney General lawyer Maria Francesca Spiteri and
Inspector John Leigh Howard prosecuted. [@1]

Members of the Customs Department assisted police, and Magistrate Joe Mifsud is
leading an inquiry. [@2] Serbia's interior ministry said the pair were suspected
members of a criminal group that supplied cocaine, marijuana and synthetic drugs
to buyers in Malta. [@4] A two-year-old girl who was in the car was handed to
social services. [@4]
