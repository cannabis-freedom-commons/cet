Agents of the National Drug Control Directorate (DNCD), National Police officers
and prosecutors raided a house on calle El Proyecto in the El Limón sector of
Bayahíbe, La Altagracia province, on 13 August 2025. [@1] The search was carried
out under warrant 03507-2025. [@2] Officers arrested two Dominican men,
identified as Cornelio Félix and Víctor Melo Santana. [@1] Melo Santana had been
extradited to Puerto Rico in 2012 for conspiring to bring cocaine into the
United States and distribute it there. [@1] Prosecutors in La Altagracia took
both men into custody for an alleged violation of Law 50-88 on drugs and
controlled substances. [@2]

Inside the house officers found 824 packages of presumed cocaine, one bag of the
same substance in bulk, and 22 bales, or pacas, of plant material that appeared
to be marijuana. [@1] The DNCD put the haul at 846 packages, presumably cocaine
and marijuana. [@1] All 846 packages were sent under chain of custody to the
National Institute of Forensic Sciences (INACIF) to establish their exact type
and weight. [@3]

Intelligence reports cited by the DNCD said the house was a collection point for
criminal networks that run drugs by speedboat from the Dominican coast to Puerto
Rico. [@1] A source linked to the investigation told El Día that police had been
following Melo Santana and found the cargo in one of the rooms during a search
for weapons. [@2] Investigators also seized electronic devices that they said
could help identify other suspects. [@1] The Ministerio Público, the DNCD and
the National Police said they were still looking for other members of the
network. [@3]
