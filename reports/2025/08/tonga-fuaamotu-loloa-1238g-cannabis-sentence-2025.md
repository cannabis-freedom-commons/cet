'Oto Langafo'ou Loloa, aged 35, was serving 18 months in prison on two charges.
[@1] One of the charges included possession of 1238.71 grams of cannabis leaves
and seeds. [@1] A Tongan-language account names the man as 'Ofa Langafo'ou
Loloa, aged 35, from Fu'amotu. [@2]

Police had been informed that he was selling from his residence in Fua'amotu.
[@1] Police went to Fu'amotu on 4 March 2025 and noticed Loloa travelling in his
vehicle. [@2]

The Tongan-language account describes the 1238.71 grams of cannabis and cannabis
seeds as his family's livelihood. [@2]
