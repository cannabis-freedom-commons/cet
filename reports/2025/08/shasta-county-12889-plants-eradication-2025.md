The Shasta County Sheriff's Office Marijuana Eradication Team ran a five-day
operation targeting large-scale illegal marijuana grows throughout Shasta
County. [@1] Officers served 23 search warrants on 29 parcels in the county.
[@1] The Anderson Police Department, the Shasta Interagency Narcotics Task
Force, the Shasta County District Attorney's Office and the state Eradication
and Prevention of Illicit Cannabis programme joined the operation. [@1]

The sheriff's office said officers eradicated 12,889 marijuana plants. [@1]
Officers also discovered 1,902.4 pounds of processed marijuana, reported here as
about 862.9 kilograms. [@1] Officers discovered 13 firearms and 50,000 dollars
in cash. [@1]

The operation resulted in numerous complaints filed with the Shasta County
District Attorney's Office. [@1] The sheriff's office said illegal grows harm
people growing legally, harm the environment and attract a criminal element to
the county. [@1]
