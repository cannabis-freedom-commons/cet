In October 2024, Swedish Customs officers raided a farm outside Ängelholm in
Skåne and found about 620 kilograms of hashish hidden in cardboard boxes
together with pomegranates. [@1] Three people were later convicted of extremely
serious narcotics offences and sentenced to prison terms of between five and
eight years. [@1] A fourth person was convicted of money laundering connected
with the case and sentenced to probation. [@1]

The sentences were reported on 11 August 2025. [@1] The account describes the
raid and the convictions without naming the people involved. [@1]
