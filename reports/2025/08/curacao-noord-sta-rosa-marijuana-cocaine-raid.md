On Monday, under the direction of the Public Prosecution Service (OM), police
raided a residence in Noord Sta. Rosa, the Curaçao Police Force (KPC) reported
in a press release. [@1] Officers detained a 47-year-old man and an 18-year-old
woman. [@2] The man remained in custody, while the woman was released after
questioning. [@1]

During the operation, officers found a stash of marijuana and cocaine, packaged
and ready for sale, along with a sum of cash believed to be proceeds from drug
trafficking. [@1] The raid also revealed the theft of water and electricity.
[@1] Utility company Aqualectra intervened on the spot to address the illegal
connections. [@1]

Police said the investigation into the case was ongoing. [@1]
