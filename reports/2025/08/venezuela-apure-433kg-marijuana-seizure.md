Interior Minister Diosdado Cabello announced the seizure of 433 kilograms of
marijuana in the western Venezuelan state of Apure, which borders Colombia. [@1]
He made the announcement during emission 539 of his weekly programme Con el Mazo
Dando, broadcast by the state channel VTV. [@3] The operation involved the
National Anti-Drug Command (CNA), citizen security bodies, the Bolivarian
National Armed Forces and representatives of the Public Prosecutor's Office.
[@3] National Anti-Drug Superintendent Danny Ferrer Sandrea said the drugs were
found in 15 bundles in a house in an abandoned state. [@3]

Experts determined the haul was 737 wrappings of marijuana with a gross weight
of 433 kilograms. [@3] The case was placed at the disposition of the Fifteenth
Prosecutor's Office with competence in drug matters. [@3] Cabello said the find
triggered "Operation Tun Tun" as an immediate response to capture those
responsible. [@2]

Cabello linked the drugs to former Colombian presidents without presenting
evidence. [@1] He named Álvaro Uribe, Iván Duque, Juan Manuel Santos and Andrés
Pastrana. [@1] He said these former presidents "direct Colombian drug
trafficking". [@1] The ministry presented the operation as part of the fight
against drug trafficking and organised crime. [@3]
