Police from several units of the Police Directorate, coordinated by the Higher
State Prosecutor's Office in Podgorica, carried out Operation Trnovo in Trnovo,
in the Bar municipality. [@2] The Police Directorate said it was the largest
marijuana plantation discovered in Montenegro. [@2] Zlatko Maraš, 40, from Zeta
and Dragan Perović, 65, from Bar were arrested at the plantation. [@4] The
Higher State Prosecutor's Office in Podgorica opened an investigation against
both men for unauthorized production, possession and distribution of narcotic
drugs. [@1] The Higher Court in Podgorica ordered that they be held in detention
for up to 30 days. [@1]

Officers found eight marijuana plots forming a single unit, planted with about
13,100 Cannabis sativa seedlings in full cultivation. [@2] The plantation had
irrigation, cultivation and physical and technical security systems, the police
said. [@2] Šćepanović said the plantation was on inaccessible terrain in the
Crmnica area. [@3] Police said the seedlings were seized and cut down after an
on-site investigation. [@2] Officers searched houses and three vehicles at four
locations used by the suspects and people associated with them. [@2]

Acting Police Directorate director Lazar Šćepanović said the seized marijuana
was worth more than five million euros and that its street value could reach ten
million euros. [@1] He said the plants were on eight plots owned by 15 different
owners. [@3] He said police were working to identify everyone who grew the
marijuana. [@1]
