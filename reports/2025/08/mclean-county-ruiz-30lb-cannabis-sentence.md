A woman from Oklahoma was sentenced to eight years in the Illinois Department of
Corrections for possessing more than 5,000 grams of cannabis. [@1] Cynthia M.
Ruiz, 35, of Bixby, Oklahoma, was initially charged in February with cannabis
trafficking, unlawful possession of cannabis with intent to deliver, and
unlawful possession of cannabis. [@1] She pleaded guilty in June to one count of
unlawful possession of cannabis with intent to deliver involving traveling with
more than 30 pounds of cannabis. [@1] The state dismissed her two remaining
charges. [@1] The trafficking and intent-to-deliver counts were charged as Class
X felonies and the possession count as a Class 1 felony. [@1]

Judge William Yoder sentenced her to eight years in prison, to be served at 50%
and followed by 18 months of mandatory supervised release. [@1] The charge
stemmed from an Illinois State Police traffic stop on Interstate 55, during
which troopers found two large trash bags in the bed of a rented truck. [@1]

The bags contained 30 units of cannabis, each weighing slightly more than 1
pound. [@1] Ruiz was represented by Assistant Public Defender Stephanie Wong at
her sentencing. [@1]
