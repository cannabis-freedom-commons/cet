Peru's National Police anti-drug division detained a serving non-commissioned
officer in Castillo Grande district, Leoncio Prado province, Huánuco, on 31
August 2025. [@1] The officer was identified as Ezequiel Rubén Calixto Inga, a
first-class non-commissioned officer assigned to the Tingo María road protection
unit. [@1] He was stopped while driving a white Hyundai Accent, registration
CEU-665, on Avenida Iquitos. [@1] According to the police report, he tried to
throw a blue suitcase from the car window, and a neighbour pointed officers to
where it fell; inside were five sealed packages of marijuana. [@1] A key found
in the car matched the lock of a house in the Picuruyacu Alto sector, and with a
deputy prosecutor present officers found two plastic bins and a sack holding 12
more packages of cannabis. [@1]

In total, 17 packages of marijuana were seized, weighing 18 kilograms 485 grams.
[@1] Officers also seized an iPhone 11, S/400 in cash, a wallet with personal
and financial documents, and the car used to carry the drug, which records
showed was registered to his ex-wife. [@1] Police said the load represented an
economic loss of S/58,417 for the criminal organisation operating in the area.
[@1]

The operation took place on the officer's day off, after anti-drug agents had
been following him on reports that he was involved in moving drugs. [@1] Tu
Diario Huánuco reported that he used his days off to move the drug. [@3] He was
taken to the anti-drug tactical operations department (Depotad) in Tingo María
and held on the Public Ministry's orders under a 15-day preliminary detention
for alleged illicit drug trafficking. [@1] The deputy prosecutor for the Tingo
María specialised drug trafficking prosecutor's office (FETID), Wilmer Jara
Godoy, took part in the operation from the start. [@2] The Aucayacu prosecutor's
office opened a preliminary investigation against him and two other police
officers over drugs in 2020, and the outcome was still unknown. [@1]
