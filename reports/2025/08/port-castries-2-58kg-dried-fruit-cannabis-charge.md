Alston Singh, a Bonneterre native now living in Babonneau, came to Shed 6 at
Port Castries to clear a barrel that officers searched on Friday 8 August 2025.
[@1] The operation was carried out by the police Gangs, Narcotics and Firearms
Unit with Customs and Excise personnel after intelligence about a suspicious
inbound shipment. [@3] Officers found five tins labelled ‘dried fruit’; one was
opened on site and its compressed green plant material tested positive for
cannabis. [@1] The total seizure was 2.58 kilograms of cannabis, which police
valued at EC$51,600. [@1]

Singh was arrested and later charged with four drug and customs offences. [@1]
He appeared before the First District Court on Wednesday, 13 August 2025, and
was granted bail of EC$30,000, cash or suitable surety, with other conditions.
[@2]

Police said Singh was also under investigation over a separate December 2024
case involving marijuana by-products and MDMA in a courier shipment in Castries,
which remained under review. [@1] The RSLPF described the interception as part
of an ongoing effort against illegal drug imports and closer border security
with Customs. [@1]
