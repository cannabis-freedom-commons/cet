Mauritanian police announced on Thursday the arrest of four suspects involved in
the sale and trafficking of drugs and found in possession of a quantity of
Indian hashish. [@1] According to a police communiqué, the Central Office for
the Fight against Narcotics and Psychotropic Substances carried out the
operation. [@1] At dawn on Thursday the office raided a hideout of former
offenders in the Mina neighbourhood and arrested four people in possession of a
quantity of Indian hashish. [@1] Police carried out the arrests in Nouakchott.
[@1]

Part of the seized Indian hashish had been prepared for sale in the form of
packets. [@1] Valium pills intended for sale and for use without medical
supervision were also seized, and are not counted as cannabis. [@1]

The four suspects were placed in police custody for further investigation
pending referral to the prosecutor. [@1] That holding, a garde à vue, was to let
the inquiry continue before the suspects were brought before the parquet, the
public prosecutor's office. [@1]
