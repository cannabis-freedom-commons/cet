In August 2025, Lehigh County District Attorney Gavin Holihan announced that
authorities had arrested 22 people following a three-year grand jury
investigation into an interstate drug ring. [@1] Other arrests took place in
Lehigh, Montgomery and Northampton counties and Philadelphia, as well as New
York City, Chicago and Wisconsin. [@2] Commissioner Zach Cole-Borghi,
Bethlehem's open records officer, was arrested on Thursday afternoon at
Bethlehem City Hall. [@2] Cole-Borghi was charged with possessing a pound of
marijuana and possession with the intent to deliver, while other defendants face
RICO charges. [@1] He was released from the Lehigh County Jail after posting
$50,000 bail. [@2]

Police destroyed two clandestine drug labs and seized $100,000 in cash,
cryptocurrency, at least 25 firearms including ghost guns and semi-automatic
rifles, 2,000 pounds of marijuana, THC liquid, cocaine and MDMA pills. [@1] The
seizure total included 2,000 pounds of marijuana alongside THC liquid, cocaine
and MDMA pills. [@1]

A Lehigh County judge later ordered that dozens of sealed court dockets tied to
the ring be opened to the public, saying most should have been unsealed weeks
earlier. [@1] Following that court order, the media groups were aware of 32
defendants facing charges. [@1] None of the defendants' affidavits of probable
cause had been available before the judge's order. [@1]
