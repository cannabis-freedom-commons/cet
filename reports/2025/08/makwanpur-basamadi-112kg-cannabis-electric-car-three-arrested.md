Police arrested three men and found more than 112 kilograms of cannabis hidden
in an electric car in Makwanpur on the morning of Sunday 31 August 2025. [@1]
The intercepting team was drawn from the Bagmati Province Police Office in
Hetauda and the Basamadi Police Post and was checking vehicles on the East-West
Highway at Basamadi in Hetauda Sub-Metropolitan City-3. [@1] Officers stopped
the car, registration Ba Pra 01-002 J 6549, as it travelled from Newarpani
towards Hetauda. [@1] Mero Patra reported that the check took place at about
4:15 in the morning. [@2]

On inspection, officers discovered 112 kilograms and 790 grams of cannabis
concealed inside the vehicle. [@1] News of Nepal put the interception at about a
quarter past four in the morning. [@3] News of Nepal also gave the quantity as
112 kilograms and 790 grams, found hidden in the car. [@3] Police identified
those arrested as Sudip Tamang, 21, and Sabin Tamang, 20, both residents of
Panchkhal Municipality-8 in Kavre, and Sagar Tamang, 22, of Sailung Rural
Municipality-8 in Dolakha. [@1] Mero Patra reported that Sudip Tamang was
driving the car. [@2] Police took the three suspects into custody together with
the seized cannabis and the vehicle. [@1]

The District Police Office, Makwanpur, said the district court remanded the
three into custody under the Narcotics Control Act while further investigation
continued. [@1]
