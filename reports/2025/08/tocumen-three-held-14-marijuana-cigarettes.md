Panama's specialised drug prosecution service carried out two search warrants on
vehicles at Terminal 2 of Tocumen International Airport together with the
National Police's intelligence directorate. [@1] Officers found illicit
substance in both vehicles and detained three Venezuelan citizens. [@1] Two of
the men were held in one car, where officers found pink residue and a fragment
presumed to be drug remnants. [@1] The third was held in the other car with
drugs stored in small plastic bags as powder, crystals and sheets of various
colours, mostly pink. [@1]

Officers also seized 14 cigarettes containing presumed marijuana and the pink
substance. [@1] The drug prosecution service said the seizures were made over
recent days with the auxiliary arms of the Procuraduría General de la Nación.
[@1]

The same set of proceedings led to the arrest of 13 people in total. [@1] The
Procuraduría said it would continue objective and transparent investigations and
bring cases before the courts where appropriate. [@1]
