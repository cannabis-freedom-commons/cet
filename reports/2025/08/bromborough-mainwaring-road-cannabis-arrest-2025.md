Merseyside Police executed a warrant at a home on Mainwaring Road in Bromborough
as part of an ongoing investigation. [@1] The warrant was carried out on Tuesday
12 August 2025. [@1] A 32-year-old man from Bromborough was arrested on
suspicion of possession with intent to supply Class A and B drugs, theft of a
motor vehicle, possession of an offensive weapon in a private place and
possession of criminal property. [@3] He was released under investigation
pending further enquiries. [@1]

Officers detained the man at the address and seized suspected cocaine, cannabis,
cash, a knuckle duster and two suspected stolen bikes, a Sur-Ron and a KTM. [@2]

Neighbourhood Policing Inspector Nicola Hughes said removing two high-powered
bikes from the streets was good news for communities that should not be put at
risk by reckless riding. [@1] She said Operation Gears was the force's response
to criminals' increasing use of such vehicles, which are often stolen to support
illegal drug trade. [@1]
