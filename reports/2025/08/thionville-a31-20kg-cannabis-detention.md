French customs officers stopped a lorry on the A31 north of Thionville, just
before the Luxembourg border, on Wednesday 27 August 2025. [@1] They found 20
kilograms of cannabis herb hidden among the pallets in the truck, sealed in
heat-sealed bags. [@3] The driver was a 67-year-old Romanian lorry driver who
denied any responsibility. [@2]

The lorry was carrying the drugs from Spain to Germany, with the cannabis
concealed among pallets of merchandise. [@3] The driver denied the charges of
transporting, possessing and importing the drugs, saying he did not know the
cannabis was in his lorry. [@3]

He was presented on 29 August in an immediate-appearance procedure before the
Thionville correctional court. [@1] He told the court that a third party had
loaded the drugs without his knowledge during a stop and that he had noticed the
locking system was damaged and bought a new padlock. [@3] His lawyer requested
further enquiry, arguing the checks were insufficient and the file too thin to
judge a defendant with no criminal record. [@2] The court ordered a
supplementary investigation and adjourned the case to 31 October. [@1] The
driver was kept in pre-trial detention while he awaited trial. [@2]
