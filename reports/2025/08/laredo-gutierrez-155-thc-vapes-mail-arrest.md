Webb County sheriff's deputies arrested Troy Gutierrez, 18, in Laredo after he
collected a mailed package containing THC vapes. [@1] The arrest came on 4
August 2025, when the sheriff's office narcotics unit and the U.S. Postal
Inspection Service ran an operation over a mailed package. [@1] The operation
led investigators to the 4400 block of Peacock Street, where Gutierrez arrived
to collect the package. [@1] He was taken to the Webb County Jail and booked
while awaiting magistration. [@2]

The package contained 155 THC vapes. [@1] El Mañana reported that Gutierrez was
arrested at his home in the Deer Creek neighbourhood, in the 4400 block of
Peacock Street on the road to Las Minas. [@3] El Mañana described the items as
electronic cigarettes or cartridges containing THC, the active principle of
marijuana. [@3] Gutierrez was charged with possession of a controlled substance,
a second-degree felony. [@1]

Sheriff Martin Cuellar said his office would keep working with all law
enforcement partners to improve public safety. [@1] Cuellar urged the public to
report suspicious activity on 956 415-BUST, saying information leading to an
arrest may be eligible for a cash reward. [@1]
