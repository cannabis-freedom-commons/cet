On 1 August 2025, the local branch of OCRTIS, Niger's central office for
repressing illicit drug trafficking, carried out two operations in the
department of Matameye in the Zinder region, seizing nearly 18 kg of cannabis,
hundreds of tramadol tablets and arresting three suspected traffickers. [@1] The
first operation took place at Maimoujiya in the rural commune of Dan Barto. [@1]
Acting on reliable information, OCRTIS agents arrested two Nigerian nationals
already known to the services for similar cases. [@1] The two men had left Machi
in Nigeria to deliver an illicit consignment to Maimoujiya. [@1] Their luggage
held ten balls of cannabis weighing a total of 4.625 kg and 500 tramadol tablets
dosed at 225 mg. [@1] Both suspects were placed in police custody on suspicion
of high-risk international drug trafficking. [@1] The two men were described as
already involved in several drug cases. [@2]

In a second operation at the Gada Dan Barto barrier, OCRTIS agents intercepted a
transport vehicle known as a Sharoon arriving from Nigeria. [@1] Inside a sack
concealing basins, investigators found 35 balls of cannabis weighing a total of
13.680 kg. [@1] A woman involved in the transport was arrested and placed in
police custody. [@1]

The traffickers hid the drugs among ordinary goods to pass road checks, but
increased police surveillance stopped the attempt. [@1] The Matameye
departmental directorate of the national police praised its teams' vigilance in
a statement and urged the public to cooperate more in the fight against drugs.
[@1]
