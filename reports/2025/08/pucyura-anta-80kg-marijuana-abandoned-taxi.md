Past midnight, officers of the Cusco Anti-Drug Division operating on the
Cusco-Abancay national road spotted a grey car with a taxi sign on its roof and
two large multicoloured bags visible on its back seat. [@1] When officers tried
to stop the car, the driver disobeyed the stop order, tried to ram the officers
and fled. [@1] The pursuit lasted three kilometres to a side road, where the car
was left abandoned with its engine running and its doors locked. [@1]

In an intelligence operation in Pucyura district, Anta, Cusco, on the
Cusco-Abancay highway, police seized more than 80 kilos of marijuana apparently
intended for transport. [@1] Inside the Toyota Sprinter XE Vintage Limited with
plate C5J-604, officers found four polyethylene sacks of rectangular packages
wrapped in plastic and packing tape containing cannabis sativa leaves, stems and
seeds. [@1] Officers also found a mini-Uzi-type firearm and, in the glove box, a
wallet holding an identity document. [@1]

Police said the load had left Andahuaylas bound for Cusco and possibly onward to
Puno or Bolivia. [@1] The head of the Cusco Anti-Drug Division, Colonel Walter
Poma, said investigators were applying all investigative techniques to identify
and capture the driver and those responsible for the shipment. [@1]
