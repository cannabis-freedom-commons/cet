A federal judge in Resistencia ordered two men from Chaco province detained for
an offence under Law 23.737, Argentina's narcotics law. [@1] The Federal Court
No. 2 of Resistencia ordered the drug, the vehicle and two mobile phones seized.
[@1]

The operation took place on Monday afternoon, 3 February 2025, near the Costa
Iné locality on National Route 1 in Chaco province. [@3] Gendarmes of Escuadrón
51 'Fontana' were running vehicle checks when a Renault Logan approached at
speed and its driver swerved to evade the checkpoint and rammed it. [@1] The
occupants rammed the force's checkpoint to try to flee, and about eight
kilometres away they lost control and overturned the vehicle. [@2] After
securing the area and assisting those involved, gendarmes inspected the wreck
and found 73 compact bricks in the boot. [@1] Field Narcotest checks confirmed
65 kilograms 814 grams of cannabis sativa. [@1]

The Gendarmerie's Region VIII command described the operation as taking place at
Costa Iné and confirmed two people were detained. [@3]
