Auki police uprooted 300-plus marijuana plants in West Kwaio, Malaita Province,
after information that a suspect was cultivating marijuana. [@3] The suspect was
a 48-year-old father from West Kwaio who had been arrested over a
domestic-violence incident. [@1] He had returned home drunk and chased his
entire family out of their house in a West Kwaio village with a bush knife,
Assistant Commissioner Simpson Pogeava said. [@2] Pogeava said the arrest led
police to information that the suspect also cultivated marijuana. [@1]

The man was taken into custody at Auki Correctional Facility and police applied
for his remand. [@2] Police established two cases against him, one over the act
against his family and the other marijuana-related. [@2]

Pogeava urged community leaders and people of West Kwaio to stop engaging in
illegal and harmful activities and to look at other ways to generate income.
[@1] He said domestic violence was not accepted in the communities, and that
people should seek help and turn to their leaders during difficult times. [@1]
