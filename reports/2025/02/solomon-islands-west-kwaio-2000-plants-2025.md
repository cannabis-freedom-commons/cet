Officers of the Royal Solomon Islands Police Force in Auki uprooted and
destroyed about 2,000 marijuana plants from seven gardens at the Kainafala River
in West Kwaio, Malaita Province, recently. [@1] Assistant Commissioner for
National Capital and Crime Prevention Simpson Pogeava said the plantations were
a main source of marijuana supplied to other parts of Malaita Province. [@1]

The owners of the plantations remained at large and police appealed for them to
surrender. [@2] Pogeava urged residents around the raided location to report the
growers, saying they had escaped when police arrived. [@1] Police could not
verify a street value for the destroyed plants. [@2]

Pogeava said communities around the area welcomed the operation and acknowledged
the support of community leaders. [@1] He reminded residents of the harm
cannabis caused and warned cultivators to stop. [@1] Pogeava said marijuana had
caused problems on Malaita and for the wider public. [@2]
