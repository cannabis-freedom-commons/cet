Ahmad Dhegani, 27, an Iranian national and former mixed martial arts coach, pleaded guilty to two alternative charges of possessing cannabis. [@1] The court heard he held 997.7 g and 860.6 g of cannabis at Jalan Sri Permaisuri 8 in Cheras and Jalan Baba Pudu in Dang Wangi between 3pm and 4.30pm on 16 March 2023. [@1]

On 24 February 2025 Judge Datuk Muhammad Jamil Hussin sentenced him to nine years' jail and 10 strokes of the cane on each charge, with the prison terms running concurrently from his arrest on 16 March 2023. [@1] The charges fell under Section 6 and Section 12(2) of the Dangerous Drugs Act 1952, punishable under Section 39A(2). [@1]
