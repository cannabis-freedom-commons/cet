The Reykjanes District Court sentenced a man and a woman to 22 months in prison for the large-scale importation of marijuana and hashish into Iceland. [@1, 2] The pair had flown into the country on 16 December 2024 with 9.2 kilograms of marijuana and 13 kilograms of hashish in their suitcases, believed to be intended for distribution. [@1, 2]

Both defendants confessed without hesitation and had no known prior criminal record. [@1] The judge suspended 20 of the 22 months, to be waived after three years if they keep to general probation terms, with time already spent in custody since their arrival deducted. [@1, 2] Each was also ordered to pay over one million krónur in legal fees and defence travel costs. [@1]
