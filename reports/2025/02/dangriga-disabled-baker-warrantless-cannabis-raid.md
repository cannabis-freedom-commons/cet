Dylan Ysaguirre, a disabled baker in Dangriga, Belize, said police raided her
home on Thursday night after receiving tips that she was selling marijuana. [@1]
She said the officers walked straight in without a warrant and told her a new
law meant they did not need one. [@1] She said this had happened at least ten
times over the previous year and a half. [@1] She told News 5 that during the
raid she had less than two grams of cannabis on the table and was still accused
of selling weed. [@2]

Ysaguirre is bound to a wheelchair and said her condition is a stress-related
autoimmune disease. [@1] She said she consumes cannabis for medicinal purposes
and administers marijuana oil to her son, who has multiple seizures a day and
congenital malformations. [@1] She said she had been arrested and charged with
drug trafficking the previous year after being found with more than the legal
amount of marijuana. [@1] The broadcast reported that she was charged once in
2023 and had been paying a fine, and she alleged an officer kicked her
wheelchair. [@2]

After the raid she went on Facebook Live and showed baked goods scattered across
her floor, one of her few sources of income. [@1] She demanded compensation for
the lost goods and the emotional distress. [@2] She said the distress made her
physically sick and affected her mental health. [@1] She had not made an
official report but said she was considering one. [@1]
