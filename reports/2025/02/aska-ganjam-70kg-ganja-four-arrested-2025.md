Excise department officials in Odisha seized 70 kg of ganja from a pick-up van
and an escorting car during a vehicle check near Aska in Ganjam district on
Thursday 6 February 2025. [@1] Four men were arrested in connection with the
case. [@1] Officials identified them as Santosh Palai, Titu Palai and Sunil
Swain, all residents of Nabaratnapur in Aska, and Ashok Sahu, who is from
Polasara. [@1] Officials also confiscated six mobile phones from the men. [@1]

Excise department sources said the ganja had been procured from the Daringibadi
area in neighbouring Kandhamal district. [@2] It was to be taken to Mumbai via
Purushottampur, either by train or by car, according to the excise sources. [@2]
The excise sources said the consignment was bound for Mumbai, a route described
in both accounts as running through Purushottampur, from where it would continue
by train or by car. [@1]
