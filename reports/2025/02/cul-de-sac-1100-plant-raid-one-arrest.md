Officers of the Sint Maarten Police Force (KPSM) raided a large marijuana
plantation in the hills near Blyden’s Drive, Cul-de-Sac, at about 5:30 AM on
Tuesday, February 11, 2025. [@1] The raid followed intelligence received by the
police. [@1] A man found at the plantation was arrested and remained in custody
while the investigation continued. [@1]

Officers described the site as an extensive and well-maintained plantation with
a covered area designed for better cultivation. [@1] Police dismantled the whole
setup, seizing over 1,100 marijuana plants and confiscating all related
equipment. [@1] The Daily Herald’s account likewise describes a large-scale
plantation raided early that Tuesday morning, with more than 1,100 plants and
related equipment seized and the setup dismantled. [@2] Its report notes that
some of the plants were growing in bathtubs as well as in the covered area. [@2]

KPSM reminded the public that cultivating and possessing marijuana remains
illegal and punishable by law, saying the law had not changed and that it would
keep enforcing drug rules for community safety. [@1]
