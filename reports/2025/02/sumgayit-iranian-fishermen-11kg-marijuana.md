Azerbaijan's Interior Ministry and State Border Service said a joint special
operation had stopped the illegal import into the country, across the Caspian
Sea, of more than 13 kilograms of narcotics and 4,400 tablets of narcotic
composition. [@1] Police and border guards carried out the operation in Sumgayit
and detained two Iranian citizens on the Caspian shore: 32-year-old Gaffar Gyzyl
and 34-year-old Behzat Garanedjat. [@1] The ministry said the two fishermen,
besides fishing, periodically smuggled narcotics into Azerbaijan. [@1] Trend,
also citing the Interior Ministry, reported that Gyzyl and Garanedjat were main
participants in the smuggling network. [@2] Police continued operational
measures to identify and detain other members of the organised group who had
tried to smuggle narcotics into the country. [@1]

On the boat they were operating, fitted with a special Yamaha engine, officers
found 11 kilograms 680 grams of marijuana, 1 kilogram 510 grams of heroin and
4,400 methadone tablets. [@1] Trend's account of the ministry statement
described the methadone tablets as containing added psychotropic substances.
[@2]

The detainees said they had smuggled the drugs into Azerbaijan from the Iranian
province of Gümushan, through the neutral zone of the Caspian Sea on the border
of Azerbaijan and Turkmenistan, for later sale. [@1]
