On 14 February 2025 a Peruvian police officer dressed as a capybara surprised a
suspected drug seller at his home, who opened the door thinking it was a
Valentine's Day gift. [@2] The house was in Lurín, a district south of Lima, and
officers acted after seeing people approach it to collect suspicious packages.
[@2] The officer was an infiltrator from the Grupo Terna, and Escuadrón Verde
chief Colonel Pedro Riojas Tipto said the disguise was needed to get the door
opened. [@2] The man was led out of the house in handcuffs alongside the
capybara and denied that the drugs belonged to him. [@2] Police took the
detainee to the public prosecutor's office after finding the drugs in the house.
[@3]

The National Police said the search seized 420 grams of marijuana along with
1,774 wrappers of coca paste, metal strainers, a scale and a mobile phone. [@2]
The police's own post said more than 1,700 wrappers of coca paste and marijuana
were found. [@3] Peruvian authorities said 1,700 doses of marijuana and cocaine
were hidden in boxes inside a cooler at the home. [@1]

The Ministry of the Interior said the suspect had previous records for the same
offence. [@1] Riojas said the unit chose the capybara because it is a popular
and well-liked character at present. [@2] The National Police regularly uses
costumes around Halloween and Christmas, including Marvel superheroes and the
Grinch, to surprise suspects. [@2] Video of the costumed officer entering the
house and catching the man went viral on social media. [@2]
