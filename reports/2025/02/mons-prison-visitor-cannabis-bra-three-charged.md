On 2 February 2025 Anna went to visit her companion Mike at Mons prison in
Hainaut. [@1] Prison staff carried out a check and a sniffer dog picked out
Anna. [@1] She admitted placing 5.25 grams of marijuana and 40 grams of cannabis
resin in her bra, along with 1.58 grams of cocaine. [@1] She said she had
brought the drugs at her companion's request. [@1] She was detained along with
Mike and his fellow inmate Steve. [@1] The case brought all three before the
courts. [@1]

Cannabis and cocaine were also found at Anna's home. [@1] The police operation
checked 38 visitors to the prison, of whom three tested positive. [@2] Police
said two of the people detained were deprived of liberty and the visitors would
be summoned before the correctional court. [@3]

During the investigation Mike admitted taking drugs inside the prison and
bringing substances back to his cell after authorised leave. [@1] He said he
supplied fellow inmates in exchange for favours but denied selling; Steve called
himself a simple consumer who worked in prison to buy drugs. [@1] The operation
was run by the Mons-Quévy police drug section with the Mons prosecutor's office
and a federal police dog handler. [@3]
