Emu Park police executed search warrants at two addresses in Zilzie on 6
February 2025. [@1] Two Zilzie men, aged 54 and 38, were charged with 11
drug-related offences between them. [@1] At a San Marino Way address, officers
seized a quantity of cannabis, amphetamine, cash and drug utensils. [@1] A
second warrant at an Amalfi Drive address produced a quantity of
methylamphetamine, cash and numerous utensils. [@1] The local Central Queensland
Today reported that police seized drugs at two Zilzie addresses in searches on
Thursday, 6 February. [@2]

The 54-year-old was charged with two counts of possessing dangerous drugs and
one count each of possessing utensils, possessing tainted property, possessing
anything for use in the commission of crime, and permitting use of place. [@1]
The 38-year-old was charged with possessing dangerous drugs, possessing
utensils, possessing tainted property, obstructing a police officer, and
contravening an order about information needed to access information stored
electronically. [@1] Both men were due to appear before Yeppoon Magistrates
Court on 6 March. [@1]

Emu Park Officer in Charge Sergeant Paul McDonnell said the recent investigative
work had reduced the supply of dangerous drugs in the area. [@1] McDonnell said
police had a zero-tolerance policy when it came to supplying dangerous drugs and
would keep working to curb supply and reduce harm in the community. [@1]
