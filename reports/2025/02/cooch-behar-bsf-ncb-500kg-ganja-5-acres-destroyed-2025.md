Border Security Force troops of the 78th Battalion, under the Guwahati Frontier,
and the Narcotics Control Bureau arrested an Indian smuggler on the
India-Bangladesh border in Cooch Behar district, West Bengal. [@1] The smuggler
was carrying about 5 quintals of ganja, or 500 kilograms. [@1] A BSF release
said the joint operation was launched on specific information. [@1]

In the same operation troops destroyed around 5 acres (about 2 hectares) of
illegal cannabis cultivation, cutting the plants and burning them. [@1] The BSF
said the operation aimed to protect the local border population from illegal
cannabis cultivation and to keep young people in the area away from drug-related
activity. [@1] The force said it also aimed to curb the smuggling of cannabis
across the international border. [@1] The BSF release said such illegal
cultivation in these border areas had become an easy source of income for
outlaws. [@1]

The release said BSF troops remain alert to illegal activities and trans-border
crime on the India-Bangladesh border. [@1]
