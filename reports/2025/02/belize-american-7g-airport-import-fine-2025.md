Nathan Andre Irons, a 22-year-old American delivery driver from Illinois, was
arrested for importing marijuana into Belize and later suffered a seizure while
in police custody. [@1] His arrest occurred on 10 February 2025, when an
Anti-Narcotics Unit officer at the airport was alerted by Customs and
Immigration officials to a strong marijuana odor from an arriving passenger.
[@1] Officers searching his luggage found 7 grams of marijuana, hidden with
Amoxicillin pills inside a white plastic bottle labeled “Levetiracetam” in a red
and black duffle bag, and he was charged with importation of a controlled drug.
[@1]

As Ladyville police prepared to escort Irons to court for arraignment, he
suffered a seizure and was rushed to the Karl Heusner Memorial Hospital for
treatment while relatives waited outside the Belize City Magistrate’s Court.
[@1] After treatment he was brought to Court #2, where he pleaded guilty and the
magistrate imposed a non-custodial sentence of a $1,000 fine plus a $5 court
fee, which he paid immediately. [@1] Failure to pay would have meant a 10-month
prison term, and his prompt payment let him walk free in time for his 23rd
birthday on 12 February. [@1]
