A routine roadside check in Medernach on Saturday night escalated into a foot
chase and led to the arrest of one of the vehicle's passengers. [@1] One of the
vehicle's occupants fled on foot before officers could act. [@1] Several
officers pursued the suspect on foot, and one officer sustained minor injuries
during the chase. [@1] The fugitive was caught and found carrying a bag
containing 350 grams of cannabis. [@1]

The drugs were seized on the orders of the public prosecutor's office, and an
official report was filed. [@1] The suspect was subsequently arrested. [@1]

The incident began just after 12.30am during a drink-driving checkpoint ordered
by the public prosecutor's office. [@1] As officers approached the vehicle to
check its documents, they noticed a strong smell of cannabis coming from inside.
