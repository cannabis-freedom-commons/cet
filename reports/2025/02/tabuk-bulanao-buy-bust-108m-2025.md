Agents of the Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency in Kalinga, aided by policemen,
cornered two alleged drug traders who tried to sell nine bricks of dried
marijuana leaves on Tuesday at Purok 6, Bulanao, Tabuk City, the capital of
Kalinga province. [@1,2] The duo, whose names were withheld by the authorities,
were identified as local residents of Barangay Loccong in Tinglayan town. [@1,2]

The cannabis bricks weighed a total of 9,000 grams and were valued at
P1,080,000. [@1,2] According to PDEA-Cordillera director Derrick Carreon,
firearms and ammunition were also captured from the suspects, to be added to
their drug violation. [@1,2]
