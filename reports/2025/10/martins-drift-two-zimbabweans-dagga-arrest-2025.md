Police stationed at Martin’s Drift Border Post arrested two Zimbabwean nationals
found in possession of substances suspected to be dagga. [@1] The pair, a
50-year-old man and a 26-year-old woman, were arrested on Saturday during a
routine search at the border. [@1]

Martin’s Drift Police Station acting commander Assistant Superintendent Uyapo
Koketso said the suspected dagga was recovered inside their luggage during the
search. [@1]
