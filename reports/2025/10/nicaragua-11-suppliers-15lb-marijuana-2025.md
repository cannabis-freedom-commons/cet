Nicaraguan National Police reported the results of its weekly operation covering
6 to 12 October 2025. [@1] Officers detained 11 alleged drug suppliers in León,
Granada, Rivas and the Triángulo Minero. [@1] Subcomisionada Karen Obando of the
police public-relations co-leadership announced the figures. [@1]

As evidence in Managua, police listed two pounds of marijuana (about 0.91 kg)
alongside 181.75 grams of cocaine, 130.74 grams of crack and cash. [@1] In the
departments, police listed 13 pounds of marijuana (about 5.90 kg) alongside
674.88 grams of crack, 58.2 grams of cocaine, two digital scales, six mobile
phones and cash, plus three revolvers seized in the sweep. [@2]

Police described the citizen-security plans as aimed at guaranteeing the peace,
security and stability of families in Nicaragua through prevention,
investigation and prosecution. [@1] The drug detentions were part of a wider
roundup that also covered homicide and robbery cases. [@2]
