Border guards from the Jelenia Góra post together with officers of the Lower
Silesian Customs and Tax Office carried out routine checks against illicit trade
on the A4 motorway in Bolesławiec county in late October. [@2] They selected for
inspection a truck travelling from Spain. [@2] The truck carried two Belarusian
citizens in their twenties, a driver and a passenger. [@2]

In two structural caches built into the truck floor officers found 10 bags of
plant material identified as marijuana, weighing nearly 5 kg, along with 16
packs of psychotropic medicines. [@2] The two men denied transporting prohibited
substances and were detained after the discovery. [@2] The authorities valued
all the seized intoxicants together at more than 244,000 zloty. [@2]

The Zgorzelec District Court ordered both foreigners held in custody for three
months. [@2] They face a fine and up to 20 years in prison for bringing,
transporting and intra-Community acquisition of a significant quantity of
intoxicants for material or personal benefit. [@2] The investigation continues.
