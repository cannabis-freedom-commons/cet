Guyana Police Force ranks led by a senior officer conducted a four-day Narcotics
Eradication Operation in the Upper Berbice River, launched the previous Friday.
[@1] There were no arrests during any of the busts. [@1]

At the Ebini Savannahs, police found a three-acre marijuana farm with plants
three to six feet tall. [@1] A makeshift camp on that farm was found, and the
camp and marijuana were destroyed. [@1] Using drones, police found another farm
about four miles away from the first farm. [@1] That site held about six acres
prepared for planting cannabis. [@1] Four nursery beds held approximately 5,000
seedlings, and a makeshift camp measuring about 30 by 20 feet was searched. [@1]
In that camp, police found two hammocks, a 12-gauge double-barrel shotgun, a
Stihl chainsaw, a Samsung cellphone, a haversack with about four pounds of
suspected dried cannabis and a box with about 30 pounds of dry cannabis. [@1]
Police lodged the firearm, phone and chainsaw at Kwakwani Police Station and set
the marijuana camp on fire, destroying more than $5 million worth of marijuana.
[@1]

At Wiruni, police searched an abandoned two-storey wooden house and found more
than 90 pounds of marijuana with a .32 pistol, an empty magazine and two
cellphones. [@1] In another Wiruni search, police found a one-storey house with
three 20-gauge shotgun cartridges and an outboard engine, plus more than three
acres of marijuana plants and makeshift camps. [@1]
