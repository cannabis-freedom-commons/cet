Customs Surveillance and the Civil Guard launched Operation Sarcina after detecting a suspicious consignment of recycled cardboard sent from Melilla to the mainland. [@1] Officers tracked the cargo from Melilla to the peninsula, inspected it at the port of Málaga, and finally intercepted it in the province of Granada. [@1]

Despite wrapping and treatment meant to mask the smell, sniffer dogs of the Civil Guard's canine unit found the drugs hidden among the cardboard bales. [@1] Officers seized 3,225 kilos of hashish with a market value of 20 million euros and arrested two people for membership of a criminal organisation and drug trafficking. [@1] The consignment had travelled from the port of Melilla, and the cardboard was bound for recycling on the mainland. [@1] The agency calls it one of the largest seizures of hashish originating from Melilla in recent years. [@1]
