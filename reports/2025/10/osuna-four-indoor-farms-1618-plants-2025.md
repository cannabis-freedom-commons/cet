The Tax Agency's Customs Surveillance service in Seville and the Guardia Civil dismantled four indoor cannabis farms in the town of Osuna during a series of raids carried out in October 2025, after residents complained about strong odours from the properties. [@1,2] Five residents of Osuna were arrested on drug-trafficking and electricity-fraud allegations and handed to the courts with everything seized. [@1,2]

The first raid took place on 8 October at a warehouse holding 150 plants at various growth stages, where the person in charge of the plantation was arrested. [@1] On 16 October officers searched a house and seized 654 plants in advanced flowering along with 10,000 euros in cash and a luxury car, arresting the main suspect. [@1] Finally, on 20 October, four simultaneous raids on two further crops seized 814 plants close to harvest, with three more arrests. [@1]

The operation was run by the Seville Operational Unit of Customs Surveillance with the Osuna Guardia Civil company's judicial police unit, as part of their standing cooperation against drug trafficking in the province. [@1]
