Hillsborough County Sheriff's Office deputies stopped a vehicle near North 12th
Street and East 138th Avenue in Tampa on a Wednesday. [@1] Deputy body camera
video shows law enforcement pulling over George Hall. [@1] Hall was 67 years
old. [@1] After the stop, Hall was asked to step out of the vehicle, and
deputies began to handcuff and detain him, as the body-camera video shows. [@1]

The video continues to show deputies searching through Hall's car, where they
found a green sack that contained small baggies of marijuana. [@1] Deputies
found baggies of cocaine and marijuana packaged for sale in his vehicle. [@1]
Hall was behind bars after deputies found the packaged drugs in his vehicle.
[@1]

Hall was booked on charges of possession of cocaine with intent to sell or
deliver, possession of cannabis with intent to sell, manufacture, or deliver,
and possession of a controlled substance with intent to sell or deliver. [@1]
The agency that stopped the vehicle and made the arrest was the Hillsborough
County Sheriff's Office. [@1] The arrest took place in Tampa, Florida. [@1]
