Officers of the General Administration of Customs and Excise carried out a
random late-morning check of freight from a flight arriving from Qatar at Liège
Airport on Sunday 12 October 2025. [@1] The control followed the unloading of an
aircraft arriving from the Middle East, during which officers discovered about
one tonne of cannabis. [@1] Customs officers found five pallets of merchandise
that contained nearly one tonne of cannabis, according to the prosecutor's
account of the control. [@1] The Liège prosecutor's office confirmed the seizure
through its spokeswoman Rosalie Heinen, who spoke about the customs control and
its result. [@1]

Heinen said the seizure could be described as a very large seizure. [@1] The
investigation into the shipment is now being handled by the Federal Judicial
Police of Liège. [@1] Prosecutors in Liège have opened a judicial inquiry into
the case. [@1] The prosecutor's office presented the operation as an important
narcotics seizure carried out at the airport. [@1]
