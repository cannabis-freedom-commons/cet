Mozambique's National Criminal Investigation Service (SERNIC) in Niassa detained
16 people involved in producing, selling and consuming cannabis sativa, locally
known as suruma or weed. [@1] Those detained included two women and one Malawian
national suspected of belonging to local trafficking and consumption networks.
[@1] A 39-year-old detainee in Lichinga district, presented to the press, denied
owning the drug found in his house and said it must belong to his son-in-law.
[@1]

The detentions resulted from several operations carried out since January 2025
to dismantle drug sale and consumption points. [@1] In the same period, SERNIC
seized 50 kilograms of suruma in different districts of Niassa. [@1] Suruma was
described as the most used drug in the province. [@1]

SERNIC spokesperson in Niassa Moisés Matengue named Lichinga, Mecanhelas, Sanga
and Cuamba as the most critical districts for production, sale and consumption.
[@1] SERNIC in Niassa said it would continue to intensify investigation and
surveillance to reduce drug circulation and consumption in the province. [@1]
