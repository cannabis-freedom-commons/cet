Oklahoma Bureau of Narcotics agents busted a marijuana grow near Stratford,
Oklahoma, in Garvin County. [@1] They served a search warrant at 40 Acres LLC on
a Thursday, arresting one person and taking 15 others into custody. [@1] State
agents seized nearly 1,000 pounds of cannabis during the raid near Stratford.
[@1]

The bureau's Marijuana Enforcement Teams carried out the search on Thursday, 23
October 2025, and seized more than 37,000 plants from a location in Stratford.
[@2] According to OBN, agents recovered 37,509 plants, 986 pounds of processed
marijuana, and two weapons. [@2] Immigration and Customs Enforcement agents
arrested 15 people at the location. [@2]

The teams served the search warrant as part of an investigation into fraud and
the trafficking of marijuana through the black market. [@2]
