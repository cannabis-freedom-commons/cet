During Operation Clean Street in October, the Commonwealth of Dominica Police
Force arrested Merkyle Cooper, a St Lucian national, in Dominica and charged him
with possession of cannabis. [@1] At the time of the arrest he was fined $1,100,
which he paid immediately, the police force said. [@1] Antigua News, reporting
the same arrest, gave the fine as EC$1,100, which he paid at once. [@2]

During the investigation officers found that Cooper was wanted in St Lucia for
serious criminal offences and took him back into custody pending repatriation.
[@1] On Thursday 22 January 2026 he was repatriated and handed over to the St
Lucia Police Force. [@1] Dominica police said the exact charges he faced in St
Lucia were not revealed. [@1]

The Royal St Lucia Police Force later said Cooper, a 20-year-old man of
Desruisseaux, Micoud, had fled after a July 2024 killing and was apprehended in
Dominica for possession of controlled drugs. [@3] He was in St Lucia police
custody from 22 January 2026, was charged with murder on 25 January 2026, and
was remanded the next day at the Bordelais Correctional Facility. [@3]
