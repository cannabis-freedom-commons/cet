The Grenada Star published the Grenada County Jail docket of arrests made from
Friday 26 September through Wednesday 1 October 2025. [@1] Lamarcus K. Goldman
was charged by the Grenada County Sheriff's Office with possession of a
controlled substance and possession of marijuana in a motor vehicle. [@1]

Keonna M. English, listed at an address in Winona, Mississippi, was charged by
Winona Police Department with possession of marijuana in a motor vehicle
alongside a seatbelt violation, careless driving, resisting arrest, DUI first
offence, no insurance, an improperly displayed tag and disorderly conduct. [@1]
The second charge against Goldman, possession of a controlled substance, names
no drug, so only the marijuana charge is counted as a cannabis case. [@1]
Neither marijuana entry gives a weight, a package count or an account of the
traffic stop behind the charge. [@1]

The same docket also lists bookings on murder, DUI, assault and fleeing a law
enforcement officer from the same week. [@1] The Grenada Star publishes every
arrest listed on the county jail docket without exception. [@1] The list states
that an arrest does not mean the accused is guilty and that U.S. citizens are
presumed innocent until proven guilty in court. [@1]
