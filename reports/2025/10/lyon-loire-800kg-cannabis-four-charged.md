Four suspects were placed under formal investigation and remanded in pre-trial
detention after police seized more than 800 kg of cannabis intended for drug
networks in Lyon and Saint-Etienne. [@1] They had been arrested on 20 and 21
October 2025 in a joint operation by officers of the Lyon branch of the
anti-drug office Ofast and of the Loire organised and specialised crime division
DCOS. [@1] The operation followed an investigation opened in autumn 2024 that
led police to three of the suspects: the first stored hundreds of kilograms of
cannabis resin from Spain at the family home near Lyon, while the other two took
an active part in trafficking in the Loire. [@1] A fourth suspect, a
Saint-Etienne trafficker linked to the trade, was arrested at his home the day
after the operation. [@1]

At one suspect's home investigators recovered 342 kg of cannabis resin, a
handgun, 109 rounds of ammunition and empty packaging for Moroccan suitcases,
cardboard parcels sealed with tape. [@1] The search of a lock-up in the Loire
also uncovered 478 kg of cannabis packed in nine Moroccan suitcases and six
large storage bags, as well as a stolen vehicle, and about 17,000 euros in cash
was seized from one suspect's flat. [@1]

At the fourth suspect's home and his mother's home, police found two assault
rifles, two handguns, about 100 rounds of ammunition and a bulletproof vest.
[@1]
