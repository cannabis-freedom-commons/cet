Ahmad Faridz Mohd Fadzil, 46, was arrested at a residence in Taman Menteri, Changkat Jering, Taiping, on 13 April 2022 for cultivating cannabis plants and possessing cannabis and seeds. [@1,2] On 28 June 2024 the Sessions Court sentenced him to life imprisonment of 30 years and six strokes of the cane for cultivation, plus two years' jail each for possessing 13.77 g of cannabis and cannabis seeds, all running concurrently from his arrest. [@1,2]

The High Court upheld the conviction on 7 March 2025. [@1,2] On 30 October 2025 a three-member Court of Appeal bench led by Justice Datuk Azman Abdullah dismissed Faridz's appeal against conviction, finding no error in the lower courts' decisions, while noting his mother's testimony linked the evidence found in his room. [@1,2]
