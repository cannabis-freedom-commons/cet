Authorities in Costa Rica dismantled a drug-trafficking organization with links
to Colombia on Tuesday 21 October 2025. [@1] The OIJ carried out 11 raids in
Osa, Puntarenas, and in Heredia and Alajuela and detained five Colombians and
three Costa Ricans. [@1] AM Prensa reported that nine people were detained and
placed at the disposal of the Public Ministry. [@2] The OIJ said the group was
made up of Colombians and Costa Ricans who coordinated the transport, storage
and distribution of cocaine and marijuana. [@2]

Officers seized 14 packages of marijuana, 194 packages of cocaine and four
packages of synthetic drugs, along with weapons, satellite radios, GPS devices
for boats, vehicles, boat motors and cash. [@1] The OIJ described the marijuana
as 14 packages sealed under vacuum. [@2] Over earlier years the same group had
about seven tonnes of drugs seized and 11 other members detained. [@1]

CRHoy reported that the raids were carried out under the functional direction of
the anti-narcotics prosecutor's office in Alajuela and the southern zone. [@3]
Investigators linked shipments that entered by sea through the Triángulo de Osa,
one of the main drug-entry points in the country. [@3] Part of the group's
logistics was to move the packages overland towards San José. [@3] OIJ director
Randall Zúñiga said the mostly Colombian group brought the drugs from Turbo,
Colombia, for storage in Costa Rica and later export. [@1] The group was also
linked to illegal arms possession and money laundering. [@2] Investigators said
the group bought properties, including a Pacific site being converted into a
tourist development valued at about 500,000 dollars. [@1] The case remained
under judicial investigation. [@2]
