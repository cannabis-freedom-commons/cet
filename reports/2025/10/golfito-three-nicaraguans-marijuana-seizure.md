Costa Rican authorities detained three Nicaraguan citizens aboard a speedboat
carrying more than a tonne of marijuana in the southern Pacific, about 170
nautical miles from Puerto Golfito. [@1] The boat was stopped in the early hours
of Tuesday 14 October 2025. [@4] Those aboard were Tylor, 29, Lincoln, 28, and
Bolaños, 36, all Nicaraguan. [@5]

The initial alert came from US authorities through the joint patrol agreement,
allowing the National Coast Guard Service, the Special Operations Group (GOPES)
and the patrol boat Juan Rafael Mora to locate and intercept the vessel on the
high seas. [@1] The 32-foot boat had no name or registration, two outboard
motors and ten fuel canisters. [@2] After the capture the boat was taken to the
Golfito dock, where the Drug Control Police and the prosecutor's office
inspected it. [@3]

Officers seized 1,249 packages of marijuana, each weighing about a kilogram, for
a total of more than 2,700 pounds (about 1,225 kilograms). [@1] The three men
were placed at the disposal of Costa Rica's public prosecutor's office, which
was to decide their legal situation in the following hours over international
drug trafficking. [@1] The Security Ministry said this was possible thanks to
cooperation with US agencies under the joint Pacific patrol, one of the main
routes used by trafficking organisations moving drugs from South America towards
Central and North America. [@1]
