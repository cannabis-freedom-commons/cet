At Tahaa, three people were identified as being behind illegal crops found in
the Tapuamu and Poutoru sectors. [@1] At Arue, one suspected grower was
identified after a targeted control operation. [@1] At Paea, two brothers were
identified as the alleged growers and sellers. [@1]

At Tahaa, officers destroyed 139 cannabis plants. [@1] At Arue, officers
destroyed 235 cannabis plants. [@1] At Paea, officers found 344 cannabis plants,
479.7 grams of dried herb, 135 branches still drying, 212 cannabis heads and
253,000 francs in cash. [@2] TNTV gave the same Paea seizure as 344 paka plants,
479 grams of dried herb and 253,000 francs in cash. [@3] At Huahine, officers
seized 106 cannabis plants from two plantations hidden in vegetation. [@1] The
outlets summarised the week as more than 800 cannabis plants seized. [@1]

The week’s operations were led by territorial brigades with PSIG support and dog
teams. [@1] At Paea, the home search followed intelligence about two suspected
drug traffickers. [@1] When officers arrived at Paea, occupants were trying to
throw cannabis pots inside the house. [@1] At Huahine, officers returned to a
site first noted during the 8 October operation. [@1] Three sachets with
methamphetamine or ICE residue found at Paea were not counted as cannabis. [@2]
