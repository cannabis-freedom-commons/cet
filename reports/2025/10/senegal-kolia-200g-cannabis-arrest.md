Officers of the Moussala border sector serving at the advanced post of Kolia
stopped a man in possession of drugs during a routine patrol on the Kolia–Gamba
Gamba axis on 27 October 2025, at about 12:20. [@1] The suspect was Daouda
Berté, 38, from Ségou in Mali and living in Kharakhéna, who was arrested while
riding a motorcycle to Kolia. [@1] kedougou7tv placed Kharakhéna in the commune
of Bembou in Saraya department and said he was travelling from Gamba-Gamba to
Kolia. [@3]

The search found a bag holding about 200 grams of chanvre indien, the local term
for cannabis, along with 36 strips of RAKOL-brand Tramadol containing ten
tablets each. [@1] Terangatimes reported the same estimated 200 grams of chanvre
indien and 36 Tramadol tablets. [@2] kedougou7tv called the same drug yamba and
also estimated the bag at 200 grams. [@3]

Berté confessed and said he bought the drugs from a Malian supplier named Yégo
in Mahinamine, Mali, for 130,000 FCFA. [@1] He said he meant to resell them at
traditional gold-panning sites called Dioura, where drug use is often used to
resist fatigue. [@1] After being told of his rights, including the right to a
lawyer, he was presented to the prosecutor at the Saraya district court, which
ordered criminal proceedings opened. [@1] He was referred to the Saraya
prosecutor's office on Tuesday after the Moussala border-sector investigation.
[@1] kedougou7tv dated the referral to the Saraya prosecutor to Tuesday 28
October 2025. [@3]
