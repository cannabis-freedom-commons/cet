Police arrested and charged six people with trafficking marijuana after an
investigation that spanned several months. [@2] One of those charged was Avi
Tal, 52, of Myrtle Beach. [@1] One of those charged was Garrett Johns, 37, of
Myrtle Beach. [@1] One of those charged was Kyle Johnson, 23, of Myrtle Beach.
[@1] One of those charged was Michael Garon, 36, of Myrtle Beach. [@1] One of
those charged was Netta Atar, 37, of Myrtle Beach. [@1] One of those charged was
Jordan Hewett, 21, of Calabash, North Carolina. [@1]

Officers said the investigation began when the Street Crimes Unit intercepted
parcels containing suspected marijuana. [@1] Following a controlled delivery,
Myrtle Beach police, along with the South Carolina Law Enforcement Division,
executed a search warrant at a warehouse on Enterprise Avenue. [@2] During the
search, officers discovered a possible distribution center inside the warehouse.
[@1]

Officers recovered 17,282.32 grams of marijuana (38.1 pounds). [@1] Officers
also recovered 148 loose pre-rolled suspected marijuana cigarettes, 153 boxes or
jars of pre-rolled suspected marijuana, 456 boxes of THC vapes and 232 packages
of THC gummies. [@2] The 456 boxes of THC vapes amounted to approximately 2,280
individual vapes. [@1] Officers also found boxes of mylar bags used for
repackaging, a large quantity of glass jars and locally produced stickers used
to label the contents. [@2] Officers submitted the evidence to SLED for testing,
and the samples were confirmed to contain marijuana. [@1]
