Ecuador's National Police intercepted a truck in the Los Ceibos sector of
Guayaquil in the early hours of Saturday 18 October 2025 during anti-drugs
operation FIAC-2852. [@2] The driver tried to escape, fell and was hurt, and
officers detained him and took him first to a health centre. [@1] Police said
the detained man was an Ecuadorian citizen and was placed at the disposal of the
competent authority. [@2]

The truck was ostensibly carrying bricks and cement blocks, but officers found
369 blocks of marijuana camouflaged among them. [@3] Police seized a total of
378 kilograms of marijuana hidden in the truck. [@1] Officers retained the truck
and a mobile telephone as evidence for the investigation. [@2] Investigators
said the load was to be sent by sea to Chile as its final destination. [@3]

Extra, citing the police, put the load's international-market value at 1.4
million dollars. [@3] The police announcement republished by Vistazo valued the
same load at more than 2 million dollars on the illegal international market.
[@1] The police described the seizure as part of a sustained
anti-drug-trafficking strategy in Guayaquil. [@2]
