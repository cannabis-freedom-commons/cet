Mormugao Police arrested Baina resident Mohammed Rafi for allegedly possessing
hashish oil and ganja during a narcotics raid conducted beneath the Baina
flyover on 17 October 2025. [@1] The raid was led by PSI Saidatta Naik of
Mormugao Police Station, acting on specific and reliable information, and ran
between 10.40 pm and 11.45 pm. [@1]

During the raid, police allegedly caught Rafi red-handed with 4 grams of hashish
oil valued at approximately Rs 8,000. [@1] Officers also allegedly seized 29
grams of suspected ganja, described as greenish-coloured dry leaves, stems and
flowering and fruiting parts of a plant, valued at approximately Rs 2,900. [@1]
The combined alleged seizure was valued at approximately Rs 10,900. [@1] Police
alleged that Rafi possessed the narcotic substances with the intention of
delivering them to his customers. [@1]

Police subsequently arrested the accused in connection with the case. [@1]
Further investigation was being conducted by PSI Suraj Naik of Mormugao Police
Station, with no court outcome reported. [@1]
