Gendarmes from the Paea brigade, with the PSIG of Faa'a and a dog team, searched
two homes in Paea on the morning of Thursday 2 October 2025. [@1] The operation
had been prepared for several weeks as part of the fight against drug
trafficking on Tahiti. [@2] Three men were arrested. [@1] In the first home
officers found 57 cannabis plants, grown by the resident with the help of an
accomplice. [@1]

In the second home they found 254 more plants and 29 branches drying, ready for
resale. [@1] As the officers arrived, the occupant tried to get rid of part of
his crop by throwing plants into the neighbour's garden. [@1] All of the
cannabis was seized. [@1] The two seizures totalled 311 plants. [@2]

Two of the suspects will be dealt with by a composition pénale, a prosecutor-led
alternative measure that avoids a trial. [@1] The third was summoned for a
comparution sur reconnaissance préalable de culpabilité (CRPC), a guilty-plea
procedure before a judge. [@2]
