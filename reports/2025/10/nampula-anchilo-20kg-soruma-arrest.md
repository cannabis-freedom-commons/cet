Mozambique's Republic Police (PRM) detained a 30-year-old man in Nampula who was
accused of producing, consuming and selling soruma, the term used locally for
cannabis. [@1] PRM spokeswoman in Nampula Rosa Nilza Chaúque said the detention
had only been possible because of diligence by the force. [@1]

Officers detained the man on a public road while he was riding a motorbike
carrying at least 20 kg of soruma, and Chaúque placed that detention in the
Anchilo administrative post in Nampula district. [@1] At a press conference, the
detained man admitted being a soruma producer and consumer, saying he had used
the drug to ease social problems and to support his family. [@1]

He said he had cultivated soruma for three years for his own benefit, while also
selling it to various close buyers who asked for it. [@1] He said those buyers
included people close to him and other young people who sought him inside the
Anchilo administrative post and at other points in the city of Nampula. [@1]
