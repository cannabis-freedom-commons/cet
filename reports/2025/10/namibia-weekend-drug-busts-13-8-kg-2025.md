The Namibian Police Force's weekend crime and incident report recorded a weekend
dominated by drug-related arrests and robberies across the country, including a
major drug bust in the capital, Windhoek. [@1] A 39-year-old Tanzanian national
was arrested at Dorado Park, Windhoek, early on Sunday morning after officers
searched the rented room he was staying in. [@1] A second Tanzanian man, aged
51, was detained at the Okahandja-Windhoek police checkpoint on Saturday night
for allegedly concealing cannabis under the bonnet of a car he was driving. [@1]

In the Dorado Park room officers found 13,805 grams (13.805 kg) of cannabis
skunk and 115 grams of cocaine powder, which police valued together at more than
N$842,000. [@1] Several smaller drug-related arrests were also reported at
Stampriet and Otjituuo, where police seized cannabis plants and loose cannabis
with a combined value exceeding N$190,000. [@1] Police spokesperson deputy
commissioner Kauna Shikwambi said investigations into all the cases were
continuing and urged the public to provide information that could lead to
arrests. [@1]
