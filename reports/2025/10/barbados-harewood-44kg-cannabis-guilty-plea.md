A 27-year-old St Michael woman has been charged in connection with the
importation and trafficking of cannabis valued at more than $35,000. [@1] The
Barbados Police Service said that members of its Narcotics Unit arrested and
formally charged Krystal Debra Harewood of 2nd Avenue, Parris Gap, Westbury
Road, St Michael, in relation to the offences committed on October 29, 2025.
[@1] Harewood was charged with possession of cannabis, trafficking of cannabis,
intent to supply, and importation of cannabis. [@1] She appeared before
Magistrate Douglas Frederick in the District ‘F’ Magistrates’ Court on Friday,
October 31, where she pleaded guilty to the offences. [@1]

Police said the drugs weighed approximately 4.4 kilogrammes. [@1] Police gave
the estimated street value of the drugs as $35,200. [@1] The cannabis was valued
at more than $35,000. [@1]

Harewood was granted bail in the sum of $4,000. [@1] The matter was transferred
to the District ‘B’ Magistrates’ Court for February 12, 2026. [@1]
