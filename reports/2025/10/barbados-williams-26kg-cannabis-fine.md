Barbados Police Service Narcotics Unit officers arrested and formally charged
36-year-old Jamaican national Andre O’Shane Williams over multiple drug-related
offences. [@1] Williams was described as being of #131, Howell Content, York
Town, Maypen South West, Clarendon, Jamaica. [@1] Williams appeared on Monday 20
October before Magistrate Douglas Fedricks in the District ‘B’ Magistrates’
Court. [@1] In court he pleaded guilty to possession and trafficking of cannabis
as well as intent to supply and importation of cannabis. [@1] The court fined
him fifteen thousand dollars in Barbados currency, with nine months at the
Barbados Prison Service at Dodds as the alternative. [@1]

He had been formally charged over offences committed on 17 October 2025 after
being found with suspected cannabis. [@1] The total estimated weight of the
suspected cannabis was 2.60 kg. [@1] Police estimated the street value of that
cannabis at $20,800 in Barbados currency. [@1]

Following the case, residents quoted on Facebook asked what background checks,
if any, are used when deciding to grant stays on the island. [@1]
