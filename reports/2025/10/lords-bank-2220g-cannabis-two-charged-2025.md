Police conducted a house search in Lords Bank Village on 3 October 2025. [@1]
Present at the house were 27-year-old Andre Lewis Belisle, an office assistant,
and 21-year-old Monica Olga Bowden, a call centre agent. [@1] Police arrested
two people in the drug bust in Lords Bank Village. [@2] Both were jointly
charged with three counts of possession of controlled drugs with intent to
supply to another. [@2]

Officers found parcels of 1,125 grams, 365 grams and 730 grams of cannabis in
different parts of the residence, adding up to 2,220 grams. [@2] Officers also
found a licensed black Glock pistol with 100 rounds of ammunition and $27,065 in
cash, which were sealed and labelled as exhibits while police continued the
investigation. [@1] Love FM identifies the seized cash as $27,065 in Belize
dollars. [@4]

The pair later pleaded not guilty to the three drug counts. [@4] A magistrate
granted each of them bail of $10,000 plus two sureties of $5,000. [@4] As bail
conditions, they must sign in weekly on Fridays at the Queen Street Police
Station, not leave the country without court permission and not interfere with
prosecution witnesses. [@4] Police said they were not ruling out further charges
as they dug deeper into the case. [@3]
