Barbados police arrested and formally charged 61-year-old Canadian national
Halston Norman Spence over cannabis offences committed on 26 October 2025. [@1]
Spence was charged with possession of cannabis, trafficking of cannabis, intent
to supply cannabis and importation of cannabis. [@1]

Police put the cannabis at about 31.6 kilogrammes with an estimated street value
of BDS$252,800. [@1] The Caribbean Broadcasting Corporation gave the same weight
and value, describing the cannabis as suspected. [@2]

He appeared before Magistrate Douglas Frederick in the District ‘F’ Magistrates’
Court on Friday 31 October 2025 and pleaded not guilty. [@1] Spence was remanded
to the Barbados Prison Service at Dodds and the case was transferred to the
District ‘B’ Magistrates’ Court for 5 November 2025. [@1] CBC gave Spence’s
address as 4180 Brandon Gates Drive, Mississauga, Ontario, Canada. [@2]
