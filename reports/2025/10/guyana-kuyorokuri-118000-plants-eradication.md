Guyana Police Force ranks from the Narcotics Branch and Regional Police Division
#6 carried out a joint narcotics eradication exercise at Kuyorokuri Creek in the
Upper Berbice River. [@3] The operation took place on Wednesday 1 October 2025.
[@1] Police found an open savannah holding six cannabis farms on a total of 21
acres. [@2] The farms held about 118,000 cannabis plants between two and seven
feet tall. [@1]

Officers also found eight camps and ten drying areas containing a combined 2,500
pounds (about 1,134 kg) of dried cannabis. [@2] Police set the plants, dried
cannabis, camps and drying areas on fire, and the exercise ended without
incident. [@1] Officers also seized a tractor, seven tillers, a chainsaw, two
generators, six weeders, three mist blowers, 16 sprayers and nine pumps believed
used on the farms. [@1]

The Guyana Police Force valued the plants at $39,978,400 and the dried cannabis
at $1,020,582,000, a total street value of $1,060,560,400. [@3] Guyana Times
reported that an investigation has been launched. [@3]
