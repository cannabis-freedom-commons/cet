Officers of the Direction territoriale de la Police nationale arrested two
people on the spot at the gare maritime de Papeete during an operation against
inter-island drug trafficking. [@1] Police suspect the two arrested people of
involvement in a network transporting drugs between the islands of French
Polynesia via the regular inter-island maritime links. [@1] According to a DTPN
Facebook post relayed by the outlet, those implicated will soon be summoned
before the courts to answer for their acts. [@1]

In recent days the same DTPN officers seized several hundred grams of dried
cannabis at the Papeete maritime station. [@1] The force presented the seizure
as part of its wider operation against narcotics trafficking through Papeete's
ferry and maritime traffic. [@1]

The National Police says it remains determined to fight actively against all
forms of trafficking across the territory of French Polynesia. [@1] The DTPN is
the territorial directorate of the National Police operating in French
Polynesia, working from Papeete against the inter-island traffic. [@1]
