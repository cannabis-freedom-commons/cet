Taliban authorities said 39 people were arrested across nine provinces of
Afghanistan on charges related to the sale, transport and use of narcotics. [@1]
The Taliban's Ministry of Interior said the arrests were made during separate
operations in Khost, Parwan, Farah, Paktia, Kapisa, Balkh, Baghlan, Laghman and
Faryab provinces. [@1]

Security forces seized six kilograms of hashish during the operations, along
with a quantity of methamphetamine, alcoholic beverages and several intoxicating
tablets. [@1]

The Taliban said it had eradicated the cultivation, production, trafficking and
sale of drugs nationwide over the past four years. [@1] Reports by the United
Nations and other international organizations indicate that drug production and
smuggling continue in parts of Afghanistan despite the Taliban's ban. [@1]
