Nigerien customs officers seized 196 kilograms of chanvre indien, or Indian
hemp, near the village of Guesselbodi in Tillabéry region on 23 October 2025.
[@1] They also took two motorcycles, arrested one rider and failed to catch a
second who escaped. [@1] The raid took place around 7 am on Thursday 23 October
at Guesselbodi on the RN1 highway and was carried out by the Niamey Brigade de
Recherche et d'Intervention (BRI), Niger's customs investigation and
intervention brigade. [@2]

Officers described the drug as carefully packaged and ready to be sold on the
local market. [@2] The operation formed part of a Nigerien customs drive called
Tsaron Kassa. [@2] Niger's national television reported the two seizures
together with a combined estimated value of 95 million CFA francs, about 170,000
dollars. [@1] Les Échos du Niger put the combined market value of the
intercepted goods at nearly 100 million CFA francs. [@2]

The other seizure reported at the same time was made on 19 October at the Kouré
checkpoint, where customs officers found ten canisters of white mercury hidden
in a bus travelling from Gaya in neighbouring Dosso region. [@1] Les Échos du
Niger reported the mercury as about 500 kilograms and the two motorcycles as new
Kasea bikes. [@2]
