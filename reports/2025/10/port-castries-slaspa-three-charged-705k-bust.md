Three employees of the Saint Lucia Air and Sea Ports Authority were arrested and
charged over a major drug bust at Port Castries after an intelligence-led
operation by the Royal Saint Lucia Police Force and the Customs and Excise
Department. [@1] On October 17, 2025, officers intercepted a grey van driven by
Cyril Joseph, a Red Cap assigned to the port. [@1] Police described the
follow-up findings as a coordinated effort among several port employees to
bypass customs procedures. [@1] The inquiry led to the arrest of additional
SLASPA workers, including Ian Moncherry and Nadia Edward. [@1]

Officers found a barrel in the van that had not been cleared for customs
inspection. [@1] The search found cannabis, cannabis resin and MDMA crystals,
which police valued altogether at $705 686.50 on the street. [@1] A search of
Moncherry's residence reportedly yielded an additional quantity of cannabis
resin valued at $4 200. [@1]

All three accused were charged on October 22, 2025 with importation of a
controlled drug, possession with intent to supply, aiding and abetting, and
fraudulent evasion of customs duties and controls. [@1] They appeared before the
First District Court on October 24 and were each granted bail of $45 000 in cash
or with a suitable surety. [@1] The police said the operation was part of
ongoing efforts to safeguard Saint Lucia's borders and root out corruption in
key institutions. [@1] A later regional roundup also described the case as
charges against three SLASPA employees in a $705 000 drug bust. [@2]
