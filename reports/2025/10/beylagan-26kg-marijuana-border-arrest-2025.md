A joint operation by Azerbaijan's State Security Service and State Border
Service stopped an attempt to move a large consignment of drugs into the
country. [@1] Officers detained Asif Huseynov, born in 1989, on the stretch of
the Azerbaijan-Iran state border that runs through Beylagan district as he tried
to take drugs from a hidden cache. [@1] The State Border Service told Axar.az
that the drugs were found and seized in a search of Huseynov. [@3] Minval
described him as 36 years old and said he was collecting marijuana and methadone
tablets that had been hidden in advance on the Iranian side. [@2]

About 26 kilograms of marijuana and 2,520 methadone M-40 tablets were found on
the detained man. [@1] A search of three packages he had collected found nearly
26 kg of dried marijuana and the 2,520 tablets. [@4] The security service and
border service said they prevented the smuggling of drugs from Iran into
Azerbaijan. [@2]

The two agencies said the joint operation targeted the smuggling and illegal
circulation of large quantities of drugs. [@4] An investigation is under way and
further operational measures are continuing. [@1]
