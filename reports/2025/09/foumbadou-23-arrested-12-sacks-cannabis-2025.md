Guinea's defence and security forces arrested 23 people suspected of criminal
activity in a security operation in Foumbadou sub-prefecture, Lola prefecture,
on Friday 26 September 2025. [@1] The 23 suspects were presented to the press in
Lola after being arrested in three villages of the sub-prefecture. [@2] The
authorities accuse them of having slaughtered several oxen after a sweep
operation carried out in the campaign against cross-border transhumance. [@2]
The army announced the arrests in a communiqué read on the national television
channel RTG. [@2]

Officers seized 12 sacks of chanvre indien, Indian hemp, the French term for
cannabis, along with four firearms, nine machetes, 26 calibre-12 cartridges, a
basket of smoked meat and an axe. [@1] The detainees were handed to the
N'Zérékoré research brigade for the investigation. [@1] Investigations continued
to establish the origin of the weapons and of the seized products and to
identify possible accomplices. [@3]

The operation followed incidents over illegal cattle slaughter that came after a
recent mixed clearance operation by the defence and security forces. [@1]
General Abdoulaye Kéita, chief of staff of the army, called on herders and
farmers to respect the pastoral code and show restraint and urged citizens to
cooperate with the security forces. [@1]
