Bondoukou's anti-drug unit, backed by the town's gendarmerie squadron, stopped a
motorcyclist on the Soko-Kabré road in the Gontougo region at 19:30. [@1]
Officers searched the parcel he was carrying and found 53 blocks of cannabis,
two balloons of OG-Kush, described as a particularly potent cannabis variety,
and two cartons of OCB rolling papers holding 100 leaves in total. [@1]
Abidjan.net gave the same haul, reporting 02 ballons of OG-KUSH and 02 cartons
of OCB holding 100 sheets of rolling paper. [@2] Abidjan.net described the
supporting unit as the gendarmerie squadron of Bondoukou. [@2]

The suspect, who presented himself as an ordinary trader, could not justify the
origin or destination of the goods. [@1] He was arrested immediately and his
motorcycle was seized. [@1] He was in police custody and was to be brought
before the competent courts to answer for drug trafficking. [@1] Abidjan.net
said he would be taken before the courts to answer for his actions. [@2]

The interception took place on the road axis between Soko and Kabré in the
Gontougo region. [@1]
