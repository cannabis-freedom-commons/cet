A 21-year-old Bedouin man was arrested on suspicion of holding 14 kilograms of
crystal meth and hashish intended for distribution throughout the Coastal
District. [@1] Police stopped the vehicle and found 13 kilograms of hashish and
approximately half a kilogram of crystal meth. [@1] According to a police
statement, Coastal District officers from Haifa station and Yasam Carmel
fighters stopped the car as it was moving at the entrance to Haifa. [@2]

Mako reported that a search of the car found about 13 kilograms of hashish and
half a kilogram of crystal meth, intended for distribution across Haifa and the
Krayot. [@3] The police statement said the suspected drugs were hashish and
crystal meth meant for distribution throughout the Coastal District. [@2]

The suspect was questioned at Haifa station and then jailed. [@2] A court
granted the police request and extended his remand until Tuesday, 9 September
2025. [@2]
