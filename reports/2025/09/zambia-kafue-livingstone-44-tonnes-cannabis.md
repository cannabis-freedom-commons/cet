Zambia's Drug Enforcement Commission uprooted and destroyed 44 tonnes of
abandoned fresh cannabis plants in Chifwema area of Kafue District. [@1] The
Commission arrested four people in connection with trafficking in cannabis and
possession of prohibited items, deputy public relations officer Theresa Mawere
said. [@1] DEC named the first Kafue suspect as Webster Nyowowo, 35, found with
a 20-litre bucket of 21.3 kilogrammes of compressed cannabis. [@2] The second
suspect, James Tembo, 25, had a 20-litre bucket of 19.8 kilogrammes of
compressed cannabis and four shot-gun shells. [@2]

Officers also seized one muzzleloader firearm, one shot-gun shell and a
slaughtered game animal identified as a common duiker in the Kafue operation.
[@2] In Livingstone, officers intercepted a South African-registered truck
belonging to a named international trucking company. [@2] A search found four
bundles of high-grade cannabis weighing 50 kilogrammes, packaged in 50
pre-packs. [@2] The two suspects held in Livingstone were Patrick Chifita, 40,
of Luanshya Township, and Erick Kaluwa, 33, of Mandevu Compound in Lusaka. [@2]

The commission said it had continued to intensify operations against illicit
drug-related activities, resulting in significant seizures and arrests in Kafue
District in Lusaka Province and Livingstone District in Southern Province. [@1]
Mawere said all the suspects had been detained in lawful custody pending court
appearances. [@2] Mawere said the seizures demonstrated the commission's
commitment to dismantling drug supply chains and protecting communities. [@3]
