Gendarmes from the Compagnie de Gendarmerie de Douala 2 conducted a raid at
about 12:30 on 27 September 2025 in the Tergal neighbourhood of Douala, at a
spot called Pamplemousse. [@1] Five suspected dealers, aged between 25 and 41,
were arrested in the operation. [@1]

Officers seized several balls of Indian hemp intended for sale, a 500-gram
package of cannabis, and material for preparing and consuming psychotropic
products. [@2]

The five suspects were placed at the disposal of the justice system, and the
investigation continued at the Douala 2 gendarmerie company in search of further
accomplices. [@1]
