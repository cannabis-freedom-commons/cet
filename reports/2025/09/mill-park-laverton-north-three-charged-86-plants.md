The Australian Federal Police and Victoria Police dismantled a Melbourne-based
syndicate with alleged organised-crime links, charging three men over cannabis
cultivation and trafficking. [@2] A 35-year-old from Keilor Park, a 28-year-old
from Albion and a 54-year-old from Echuca were charged, while a 39-year-old from
Fraser Rise was arrested and released pending further inquiries. [@2] The
investigation began in November 2024 into a suspected syndicate allegedly
trafficking large quantities of illicit drugs into Australia. [@2] The arrests
followed Friday 19 September 2025 search warrants at homes and commercial sites
across Keilor, Fraser Rise, Albion, Keilor Park, Laverton North, Mill Park,
Sunshine North and Echuca. [@2] Two men appeared in the Melbourne Magistrates
Court on 22 September 2025 and the third was listed before the Echuca
Magistrates Court on 16 December. [@1]

At commercial warehouses in Mill Park and Laverton North, police found cannabis
cultivation facilities inside industrial shipping containers with sophisticated
hydroponic equipment. [@2] Officers seized approximately 86 mature cannabis
plants and an estimated 25kg of dried cannabis across the two sites. [@2] Police
put the potential street value of the mature plants at approximately $129,000
and the dried cannabis at more than $560,000. [@2] The 86 plants and more than
25kg of dried cannabis were together valued at nearly $700,000. [@1]
Investigators also seized more than $200,000 in cash suspected to be proceeds of
crime, small quantities of cocaine in deal bags, an imitation firearm, drug
paraphernalia and encrypted communication devices. [@2]

Further forensic testing will determine the exact weight and composition of the
plants. [@2] Victoria Police Superintendent Matt Volk said police have zero
tolerance for those harming the community by importing and trafficking drugs and
will keep targeting such syndicates. [@2]
