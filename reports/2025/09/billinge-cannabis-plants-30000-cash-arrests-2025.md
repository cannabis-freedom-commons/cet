Officers from Merseyside Police's St Helens Neighbourhood and Targeted Teams,
Matrix Roads Policing and the Dog Unit took part in a coordinated day of action
across the borough on Friday 26 September 2025. [@1] The day formed part of
Operation Gears, a force campaign against illegal use of electric bikes,
e-scooters and off-road motorcycles linked to serious crime and antisocial
behaviour. [@1]

Officers went to a house in Billinge after reports of a stolen motorcycle in its
yard and found a Yamaha motorbike confirmed as stolen from the Stoke area. [@1]
Smelling cannabis from the property, they entered and found a 27-year-old man
hiding in the attic. [@1] A search uncovered cannabis plants, nitrous oxide
canisters and other drug paraphernalia. [@1] A police dog and its handler then
found more than £30,000 in cash hidden under a bathroom sink. [@1]

The 27-year-old man from Billinge was arrested on suspicion of cultivating
cannabis and possessing cannabis with intent to supply, alongside offences
including theft of a motor vehicle and possession of criminal property. [@1] A
62-year-old man from Billinge was arrested on suspicion of the same cannabis
offences, plus handling stolen goods and supplying nitrous oxide for inhalation.
[@1] Both men were conditionally bailed. [@1]

The same day of action also produced arrests over an attempted robbery on
Clipsley Lane in St Helens on 22 September. [@1] Superintendent Chris Ruane, who
leads local policing in St Helens, said officers seized five illegal or
suspected stolen bikes and cannabis plants and recovered more than £30,000 in
cash. [@1] The Liverpool Echo reported that the coordinated raids across St
Helens on 26 September recovered more than £30,000 in cash, drugs and suspected
stolen motorcycles. [@2]
