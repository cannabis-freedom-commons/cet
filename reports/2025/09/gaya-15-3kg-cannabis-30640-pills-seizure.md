The departmental branch of Niger's Central Office for the Repression of Illicit
Drug Trafficking (OCRTIS) in Gaya, working with the canton chief's vigilance
committee, arrested one suspect and handed him to the justice system. [@1]
Officers presented the haul on 15 September 2025 at the Gaya departmental
directorate of the National Police before administrative, judicial and customary
authorities. [@1]

The haul comprised 18 bricks of cannabis weighing 15.3 kg, 20,000 tablets of
Diazepam D5 and 10,640 tablets of Tramadol. [@1] The seizures were carried out
on 11 August and 10 September 2025. [@1]

Departmental police director Chief Commissioner Issaka Djibo called the result
the fruit of joint action between the National Police and the population and
congratulated the OCRTIS officers. [@1] Delegated prosecutor Chaibou Ibrahim
praised the results by the National Police and OCRTIS as proof of the local
anti-trafficking strategy, while canton chief Roumane Elhadje Moussa Gani
thanked the security forces and prefect Moussa Souley urged OCRTIS officers to
stay vigilant. [@1] Local authorities presented the operation as confirming the
role of police-community cooperation against drug trafficking threatening youth
and security in the region. [@1]
