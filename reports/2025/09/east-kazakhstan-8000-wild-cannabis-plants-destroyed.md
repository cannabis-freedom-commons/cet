Officers of the anti-drug crime department raided Ulan and Zaisan districts to
find and eliminate wild cannabis growth as part of the Karasora-2025 preventive
operation. [@3] Police used a drone to detect the wild cannabis plantations from
above in the two districts. [@1]

Police in Ulan and Zaisan districts destroyed more than 8,000 bushes of the
narcotic-containing plant. [@1] More than 8,000 wild cannabis bushes were
destroyed across an area of more than 20 hectares. [@2] All of the bushes found
were cut down and destroyed. [@1]

The regional police department said cannabis serves as the raw material for
drugs such as marijuana and hashish. [@1] The department said its anti-drug
officers systematically eliminate plantations of the narcotic-containing plant
in East Kazakhstan Region. [@1]
