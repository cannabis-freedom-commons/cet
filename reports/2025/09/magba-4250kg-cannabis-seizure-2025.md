The people transporting the cannabis were arrested and placed in detention, the
authorities said. [@3] The seizure and destruction were carried out by officers
of the Magba public-security police station in Cameroon's West Region. [@1] The
cannabis was destroyed on Monday 8 September 2025. [@2]

The load comprised 132 sacks weighing 4 tonnes 250 kilograms (4,250 kg) in
total. [@4] The truck had left the Mbiame area in the North-West Region and was
carrying the cannabis to Tibati in the Adamawa Region when officers intercepted
it at Magba. [@1]

The destruction took place in the presence of the West Region governor,
Augustine Awa Fonka. [@1] The governor was accompanied by the regional delegate
for national security for the West, Moussa Lakreo, and the prosecutor at the
Foumban tribunal, Françis Engolo. [@2] The governor promised an intensification
of action against drug trafficking. [@1]
