Namibian police arrested two men, aged 32 and 33, in Okahandja on Friday 12
September 2025 after finding them in possession of cannabis. [@1] The two were
caught at 19h00 at Ekunde, a location in the town in the Otjozondjupa region.
[@1] Police did not name either man. [@1]

Officers found half a pack of cannabis and four ballies, rolled cannabis
cigarettes, police said. [@1] The men also had a small white scale and N$47 in
cash. [@1] Police said the value of the items was unknown. [@1]

Police said their investigation was ongoing. [@1] The same police weekend report
recorded a separate case at Tses, where a 22-year-old woman was arrested with
ten small bankies of cannabis described as skunk. [@1]
