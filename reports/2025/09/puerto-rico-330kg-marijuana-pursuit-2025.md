Prefectura Naval Argentina detained a man in the city of Puerto Rico, Misiones,
and seized more than 330 kilograms of drugs. [@1] The man was arrested at the
scene after officers identified him as the person who had carried part of the
packages into a house. [@1] He was placed at the disposal of the Federal Court
of Oberá, under Dr Marcos Alejandro Gallandat Luzuriaga, which ordered the
seizure of the material and his transfer. [@1]

Officers of the Puerto Rico Prefectura were on patrol at kilometre 1,739 of the
Paraná River when they spotted a vehicle without a licence plate heading from
the riverbank toward the town. [@1] The vehicle tried to flee when the officers
appeared, and one of its occupants got out with bundles and went into a house.
[@1] With support from other units, the officers closed off the area and
intercepted the load of 17 bundles holding 280 panes of an illegal substance and
one loose package. [@1] The final weighing came to more than 330 kilograms,
which the ministry estimated at more than 1,115 million pesos. [@1]

Misiones Online reported that the 280 panes held marijuana and named the
operation "Con los paquetes en casa". [@2] The outlet also reported the seizure
as more than 330 kilograms of marijuana found after a pursuit in Puerto Rico.
[@2]
