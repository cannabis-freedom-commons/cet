Namibian police arrested 12 people in drug-related cases across the country over
the weekend. [@1] National police spokesperson deputy commissioner Kauna
Shikwambi released the weekend police report on Sunday. [@1] Two men aged 44 and
46 were arrested at Otjomuise in possession of 10 boxes of cannabis worth N$ 9,1
million. [@1] The Otjomuise arrests happened in Windhoek’s Otjomuise residential
area on Friday and involved cannabis skunk valued at over N.dollars 9 million.
[@2]

A 22-year-old woman was caught with 235g of cannabis skunk worth N$11 750 during
a police stop-and-search operation. [@1] A 33-year-old man was arrested at
Rehoboth with a sachet of cannabis skunk valued at N$1 000. [@1] A 27-year-old
man at Tsara Xaibes at Otjiwarongo was found with five ballies and loose
cannabis valued at N$120. [@1] Three suspects aged between 22 to 33 were
arrested at Mariental with skunk and pure cannabis worth N$4 410. [@1] A
52-year-old man and a 51-year-old woman were arrested at farm Condor Pos with 80
bags of cannabis and packs of Yes cigarettes at an estimated value of N$850.
[@1]

A 20-year-old man was arrested at Tsumeb on Friday with mandrax tablets worth
N$840 and N$170 cash. [@1] A 37-year-old man was arrested with eight full
mandrax tablets, one half and two quarters valued at N$1 080. [@1] Police said
the suspects were arrested and investigations continue. [@1]
