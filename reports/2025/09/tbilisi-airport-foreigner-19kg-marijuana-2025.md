A foreign citizen born in 1988 was detained at Tbilisi International Airport
after officers checked his baggage, Georgia's Interior Ministry said. [@1] The
detainee is a 37-year-old foreign citizen, the ministry said. [@2] Officers
seized 19 kilograms and 400 grams of marijuana, divided into 34 packages, as
material evidence. [@1]

Officers of the Interior Ministry's Central Criminal Police Department and the
Customs Department of the Finance Ministry's Revenue Service detained the man in
a joint operation. [@3]

He is accused of illegally acquiring and storing a particularly large quantity
of narcotic drugs. [@1] He is also accused of illegally importing a particularly
large quantity of narcotic drugs into Georgia. [@1] The Interior Ministry said
the offence carries up to 20 years in prison or life imprisonment. [@4]
Investigators are working under Criminal Code Article 260, part 3(a), and
Article 262, part 4(a), covering illegal acquisition and storage of drugs and
their illegal import into Georgia. [@1]
