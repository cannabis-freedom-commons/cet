The Israel Police raided an abandoned kindergarten in Be'er Ya'akov on Monday 8
September 2025 after a covert investigation and arrested two suspects. [@2] The
two suspects are in their 30s and live in Ramla and Beit Shemesh; police suspect
them of setting up and operating the lab. [@3] Ramla police station officers
carried out the raid together with fighters of the National Guard. [@1]

Officers found about 1,000 cannabis plants weighing roughly 100 kilograms. [@4]
They also seized air conditioners, pools, oxygen tanks, irrigation systems,
fertilizers and other tools used to grow the plants. [@1] The building had
housed a kindergarten until five years earlier and was privately owned; footage
showed a "Welcome" sign that once greeted the children. [@1] The findings were
sent to the Israel Police's forensic identification laboratories for evidence.
[@4] The Hebrew-language accounts described a hydroponic laboratory inside the
disused kindergarten, indicating the plants were grown indoors. [@4]

Police held the two suspects for questioning and planned to bring them before a
court to seek an extension of their detention. [@3] Authorities said the
operation was part of their crackdown on drug cultivation and distribution that
endangers public safety. [@1]
