Montenegrin border police filed a misdemeanor charge against DW, a 51-year-old
citizen of Montenegro, after a small amount of marijuana was found in the
Peugeot he was driving. [@1] Officers of the Ulcinj Border Police Station
checked the car at the joint Sukobin–Murićani border crossing at the entrance to
Montenegro, on the second line of border control. [@1] The Police Directorate
said the check had been made the previous day. [@1]

On this occasion, police officers found a small amount of crushed plant matter,
suspected to be the narcotic drug marijuana, in DW's possession, the Police
Directorate said. [@1] A request was filed against DW to initiate misdemeanor
proceedings for violations of the Law on the Prevention of Drug Abuse. [@1]
Police in Montenegro said a smaller amount of marijuana was found in the car
D.W. (51) was driving and a misdemeanor report was filed against him. [@2] A
smaller amount of marijuana was found during the check and a request for
misdemeanor proceedings was filed, according to the police statement. [@3]
