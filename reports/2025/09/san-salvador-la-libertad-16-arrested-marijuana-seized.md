El Salvador's Attorney General's Office (FGR) and National Civil Police (PNC)
arrested 16 people in a pre-dawn anti-narcotics operation in San Salvador and La
Libertad departments on 26 September 2025. [@3] The FGR named Mauricio Antonio
Melado Lara and José Mauricio Melado Mayorga as the alleged leaders of the
organization. [@2] Prosecutors said the 16 would be processed for illicit drug
trafficking and illicit associations. [@1]

In searches of homes and businesses, officers seized 838 grams of marijuana,
cocaine and methamphetamine together, which the FGR valued at $17,529. [@3] They
also seized $730 in cash, telephones, computers and other items linked to the
network. [@3] Two vehicles and sealed safes were seized as well. [@1] La Prensa
Gráfica reported the drugs' market value at $666,443, a total that covers
marijuana, cocaine and methamphetamine together. [@1]

The authorities said the investigation began in January 2024. [@2] The FGR said
the group used international contacts to bring drugs into the country, mainly
cocaine from Guatemala. [@3] The drugs were then distributed in businesses and
directly to consumers, according to the FGR. [@2] The raids took place
simultaneously at homes and businesses in San Salvador and La Libertad,
including a brothel, in what the FGR described as exclusive zones. [@3]
