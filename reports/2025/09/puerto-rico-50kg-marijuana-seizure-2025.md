Prefectura Naval Argentina officers seized a load of about 50 kilograms of
illicit substances valued at more than 168 million pesos in Puerto Rico,
Misiones. [@1] Officers found the load after an extensive search at kilometre
1738 of the Paraná River near Arroyo León. [@1] After several days, officers
detected two bundles holding 64 pressed bricks of marijuana. [@1]

The marijuana had been left to cool hidden among dense riverside vegetation
while traffickers waited to move it into the domestic distribution circuit. [@1]
The Federal Prosecutor's Office in Oberá, headed by Viviana Alejandra Vallejos,
took the case and ordered the merchandise seized and the investigation continued
to find those responsible. [@1]
