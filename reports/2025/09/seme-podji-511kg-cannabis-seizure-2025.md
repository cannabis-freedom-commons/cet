Republican Police officers seized 511 kilograms of chanvre indien, the French
term for Indian hemp or cannabis, at Sèmè-Podji in the night of 6 to 7 September
2025. [@1] Acting on intelligence, officers from the Sèmè-Podji arrondissement
police station moved on makeshift shelters near the town's beach. [@1] There
they found 14 large sacks of psychotropic products packed in balls. [@3] The
suspects fled by sea in pirogues, or canoes, and escaped arrest. [@1]

Investigators said the consignment was intended for local consumption and for
distribution to a neighbouring country. [@1] The 511 kilograms were taken to the
arrondissement police station to be secured and analysed. [@1]

The Republican Police described the seizure as a record for the Sèmè-Podji
arrondissement police station. [@2] The arrondissement commissioner welcomed the
operation and said investigations were continuing to identify and arrest the
fugitives. [@1]
