A man and a woman of Cuban origin were detained in Cancún, Quintana Roo, after
police caught them exchanging packages containing drugs with another person.
[@2] The pair was held on suspicion of offences against health. [@4] They were
identified as Maikel "N" and Ana Idelys "N", both from the island of Cuba. [@2]
They were handed to the Fiscalía Especializada en Combate al Delito de
Narcomenudeo, the state prosecutor's office for narcomenudeo, or small-scale
drug dealing. [@2] The arrest was made at the intersection of calle 43 with
calle 10 Poniente and calle 12 Poniente in supermanzana 62 during a patrol. [@2]

Officers from the Investigation Group saw two men and a woman handling a square
package containing marihuana and several small plastic bags of the same drug;
one man fled and the couple stayed. [@5] Police seized a backpack containing a
square frame of dried green herb resembling marihuana and 77 small bags of the
same plant. [@3] They also took 63 bags of solid fragments resembling "cristal",
a backpack, a mobile phone and a cap. [@2]

14ymedio, citing the prosecutor's office, said the couple was known for selling
marihuana and desoxiefedrina, known as cristal, and that more than 140 doses
were found for sale. [@1] The prosecutor's office said the doses covered both
marihuana and desoxiefedrina, a stimulant known as cristal. [@1] The
prosecutor's office said that if convicted the two could face between four and
eight years in prison. [@1]
