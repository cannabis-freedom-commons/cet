Gendarmes from the anti-drug cell of the 2nd legion in Daloa seized 35.62 kg of
cannabis in the village of Gazibo, near Tapeguia in Issia department, during the
night of 19 to 20 September 2025. [@1] The operation involved the Daloa
gendarmerie squadron and the Issia company and brigade. [@1] They arrested A.J.,
a 31-year-old unemployed man who lives at Gazibouo, while his accomplices fled
before the gendarmes arrived. [@1]

The haul comprised 30 blocks and a quarter of cannabis weighing 30.25 kg, 691
balls weighing 1.036 kg, 55.5 g of loose cannabis, five balloons of OG-Kush
weighing 4.25 kg and 19 OG-Kush balls weighing 28.5 g. [@1] Gendarmes also
seized 193 OCB rolling papers and 12 packets of rubber bands, and gave the total
weight as 35.62 kg. [@2]

Captain Ndouffou Konan Marius, head of the Daloa anti-drug cell, said the
cannabis was meant to supply the local market through resellers. [@1] He warned
that traffickers would keep running into the gendarmerie as long as they carried
on the trade, and linked drug use to crime and insecurity. [@1] Residents of
Gazibo and neighbouring villages said they were relieved by the operation. [@2]
The man was taken before the prosecutor and investigators continued working to
dismantle the rest of the network. [@1]
