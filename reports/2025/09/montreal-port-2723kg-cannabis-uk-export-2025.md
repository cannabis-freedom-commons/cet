Officers from the Montreal Marine and Rail Services made two seizures of
cannabis intended for the UK totalling 2,723 kg on 17 and 26 September 2025.
[@2] The agency delayed releasing information to avoid compromising the
investigation. [@1] The agency turned the cannabis over to the Royal Canadian
Mounted Police. [@1]

On 17 September officers found 2,550 kg hidden in a shipment containing bundles
of lumber. [@2] On 26 September officers found 173 kg in a container with boxes
of paper towel rolls. [@2] In both cases the cannabis was destined for the
United Kingdom. [@1]

Although cannabis is legal in Canada, illicit cross-border smuggling can be
punished by up to five years in prison. [@1] The same offence can bring up to 14
years under the federal Cannabis Act. [@2]
