A 43-year-old Indian national was arrested at Katunayake Airport while trying to
bring 10.75 kilograms of Kush into Sri Lanka. [@3] The arrest was reported on
the morning of Friday 5 September 2025. [@3] He was stopped at the green channel
of Bandaranaike International Airport. [@1] The green channel is the route for
tourists who have nothing to declare. [@2] He worked as a salesman at a shoe
store in New Delhi. [@1] He had bought the drugs in Bangkok, Thailand, and
transported them via India. [@3]

Kush is a variety or strain of the indica subspecies of cannabis plant, known
for high intoxication. [@1] Customs officials seized the consignment in 19
packets. [@3] A senior Customs Department official said the consignment was
detected using scanning machines installed in the green channel. [@1] Officials
said this was the first arrest made using the green channel's newly installed
scanning machine. [@2] Newswire reported the Kush as worth Rs 105 million. [@2]

The suspect and the seized drugs were to be handed to the airport police
narcotics bureau for further investigation. [@3]
