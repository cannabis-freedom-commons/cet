Tunisian customs announced on Monday 29 September 2025 that it had foiled an
attempt to traffic more than half a tonne of cannabis at the port of Radès, in
the northern suburbs of Tunis. [@1] The operation was carried out by officers of
the frontier commercial-operations office at Radès port, who seized 572.5
kilograms of cannabis resin concealed aboard a road trailer bound for a
warehouse. [@1]

When the trailer passed through the X-ray scanner, monitoring officers raised a
first alert, then customs specialists including the canine unit confirmed the
presence of illicit substances with sniffer dogs. [@1] The drug, hidden inside a
trailer bound for a bonded warehouse, was detected by scanner and then confirmed
by a canine-assisted search, according to an official statement. [@2] A thorough
search uncovered a hide in the lower part of the trailer containing 5,380
cannabis plaques with a total weight of 572.5 kg, packaging included. [@2] The
X-ray passage had revealed unusual objects in a suspect trailer bound for a
bonded warehouse, alerting customs officers. [@3] A detailed inspection with the
customs canine unit confirmed illicit material, and investigators found a
compartment fitted under the trailer holding 5,380 cannabis plaques totalling
572.5 kg. [@3]

A seizure report was drawn up and, after consulting the public prosecutor, the
file was sent to the specialised security services. [@1] On the prosecutor’s
orders the file was passed to the competent security services to continue the
investigation and establish who was behind the trafficking attempt and how it
was organised. [@3]
