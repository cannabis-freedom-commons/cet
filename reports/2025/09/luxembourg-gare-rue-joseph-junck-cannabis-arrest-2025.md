Police ran a large-scale control operation on Thursday afternoon in Luxembourg
City's Gare district. [@1] During the checks officers watched a suspected drug
deal on Rue Joseph-Junck between two people. [@1] Officers immediately detained
both the suspected seller and the suspected buyer. [@1] The suspected seller was
arrested on the prosecutor's order. [@1] RTL Today corroborates the Gare arrest,
saying officers arrested the suspected dealer after the Rue Joseph Junck
transaction, found cannabis on him and had prosecutors order his detention. [@2]

Twenty-one officers and a police sniffer dog were deployed for the operation.
[@1] Officers checked 49 people during the operation. [@1] The checks produced
one taxed warning for a drug-law offence and two identification reports. [@1] A
search of the suspected seller's home seized cannabis, with police giving no
weight for the seizure. [@1]

Police said the operation focused on drug and immigration law and on keeping a
visible police presence. [@1] RTL Today's count of two drug arrests that
Thursday also includes a separate Ettelbruck case in which a man was found with
drugs and cash after making threats, which is not counted here. [@2]
