Police intercepted six men in Kadavu waters in the week before 29 September 2025
while they were allegedly transporting marijuana bound for Suva, and all six
were then charged. [@1] The six are Letia Tokoni, Saimoni Mule, Sakiusa Timi,
Apisai Daveta, Tevita Vuki and Timoci Lolohea. [@2] The six have been charged
with one count each of unlawful possession of illicit drugs. [@2] Lolohea also
faces an additional charge of unlawful cultivation of illicit drugs. [@2] They
appeared in the Suva Magistrates Court on the morning of 29 September 2025,
where a bail application was made. [@2] The court denied bail, noting the large
quantity of drugs and the public interest in the matter. [@2]

The consignment was over 26kg of marijuana bound for Suva. [@2] All six were due
to appear at the Suva Magistrates Court that day. [@1] The case was adjourned to
the 13th of October for plea. [@2] The interception happened in Kadavu waters in
the week before the court appearance. [@2]
