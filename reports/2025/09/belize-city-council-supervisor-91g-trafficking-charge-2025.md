A supervisor of the Belize City Council, a 32-year-old man named in the report
as Neal, is out on bail after charges were levied over his arrest in
mid-September 2025. [@1] Police said he was found in possession of 91 grams of
marijuana. [@1] He was arrested in Belize City on Monday 15 September 2025, when
police claimed he tried to conceal the drug by throwing it away after seeing an
officer arriving. [@1]

On 29 September 2025 he appeared at court number six in Belize City before
Magistrate Mannan Dennison. [@1] He was read a single charge of possession of a
controlled drug with intent to supply 91 grams of cannabis, under the offence of
drug trafficking. [@1] The magistrate released him on police bail. [@1]
