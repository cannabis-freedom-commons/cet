At the Božaj border crossing, police found 114 packages of green plant matter
suspected to be marijuana, weighing 131 kilograms in total, in a specially built
bunker with an electronic opening in a truck driven by 46-year-old Bosnian
citizen E.Č. [@1] The vehicle was an empty refrigerated truck and the bunker had
been constructed in its roof. [@1] The Customs Administration said the drugs
were intended for transit through Montenegro to neighboring countries. [@1]

The competent prosecutor was notified and the driver was handed over to the
Police Directorate for further action. [@1] Police said the driver was arrested
on a warrant from the Higher State Prosecutor's Office in Podgorica. [@1] The
drugs were sent to the Forensic Center of the Police Directorate in Danilovgrad
for expert examination. [@1]

Customs described the seizure as further confirmation of the service's
determination in the fight against drug smuggling and organised crime. [@1]
