State and federal forces arrested five men and one woman in eight raids across
Hidalgo, the state public security secretariat said. [@2] The secretariat linked
the detainees to kidnapping, vehicle theft and drug selling. [@1] The detainees
are from Cuba, Mexico City and Hidalgo. [@2] The operations ran for three days,
carried out by the Hidalgo public security secretariat with the defence
secretariat, the National Guard and the state prosecutor's office. [@2]

Eight search warrants were executed in communities of Tizayuca, Singuilucan,
Mixquiahuala de Juárez and Tepehuacán de Guerrero. [@2] Officers seized 9,456
doses of marijuana, 36 marijuana cigarettes and 10 sealed bags of the same drug.
[@2] The haul also included 10 packets of sweets containing THC. [@2] The same
operations produced 155 doses of crystal meth, a replica revolver, two handguns,
six scales and 16 mobile phones, none of which is counted as cannabis. [@2] In
Tula and Tepetitlán officers recovered 10 pickup trucks, six of them reported
stolen, and 1,200 litres of illegally extracted fuel. [@3]

The detainees and the seized drugs, weapons, vehicles and fuel were placed at
the disposal of the competent authorities. [@2]
