Police in Azerbaijan's Jalilabad district detained a 40-year-old man named as
Mahir Atashrus in an operation against drug trafficking. [@3] Officers found
more than 18 kilograms of marijuana and methamphetamine on him. [@1]

The Interior Ministry's press service said officers of the Jalilabad district
police department carried out the operation against the circulation of narcotic
and psychotropic substances. [@1] Investigators said an Iranian citizen, whose
identity is still being established, recruited Atashrus as a drug courier to
deliver the narcotics to addresses in Baku in exchange for money. [@2]

A criminal case was opened and the detained man was placed under arrest as a
preventive measure. [@1] The investigation is continuing. [@3]
