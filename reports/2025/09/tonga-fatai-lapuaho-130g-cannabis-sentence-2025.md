Sione Lapu'aho, 56, was serving 10 months in prison for possession of 130.35
grams of cannabis at Fatai. [@1] The offending took place in February 2025. [@1]
When questioned by police, he admitted that he was intending to supply the
cannabis to others. [@1] Lapu'aho was described as a repeat drug offender. [@1]

The Attorney General's judgment index records the sentence as Rex v Sione Vahai
Lapuaho (CR 77/25) at the Supreme Court on 3 September 2025. [@2] Justice
Garlick KC handed down the sentence. [@2]
