Joint units of the Colombian Navy's Amazon Naval Force and Peru's Amazon
Operational Command carried out 'Operation Bagre' in Puerto Leguízamo, Putumayo,
on the border with Peru. [@1] Troops moved to the Puerto Arturo sector in the
Rosa Panduro district of Peru's Putumayo province, where illegal activity was
concentrated. [@1] There they identified several riverbank houses occupied by
Colombian citizens presumed involved in illicit activity. [@1] Four kilometres
from that first inspection, troops found three boats used to transport drugs,
loaded with 2,137 kilograms of marijuana. [@1] The load is described as more
than two tonnes of marijuana of the 'creepy' variety. [@1]

The operation used four control boats and uniformed personnel, with support from
the Peruvian river gunboat B.A.P. 'Marañón', according to Marine Infantry
Colonel Gustavo Paternina, commander of the Third Marine Infantry Brigade. [@1]
Colonel Paternina said the seizure prevented more than ten million dollars from
entering the traffickers' finances and blocked more than 360,000 doses from
reaching streets around the world. [@1] The military said the marijuana was
meant to supply drug routes connecting Colombia, Peru and Brazil. [@1]

The cargo was taken to the Naval Station 'El Estrecho', where procedures with
the competent authorities continue, and no arrests were reported. [@1] The area
is covered by the ombudsman's Early Alerts 001/25 and 013/25, which rate the
human-rights risk for Puerto Leguízamo's population as 'extreme and imminent'
and call for immediate state action. [@1]
