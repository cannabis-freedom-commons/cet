Acting Police Commissioner Leiataua Samuelu Afamasaga said police used lawful
force at a Tuana'imato home after a person inside fled as officers arrived to
serve a warrant. [@1] A woman had claimed on social media that police forced
their way into her house and broke the windows and the front door. [@1] She said
the police broke in while nobody was at home and without issuing a search
warrant. [@1] Leiataua said the law allowed police to use force when people at a
suspected home refused to cooperate. [@1]

The Tuana'imato house belongs to Stuart Webber, a man previously convicted of
drug-related offences. [@1] The raid was one of seven police raids across Samoa
between 16 and 23 December 2025. [@2] Those raids saw 17 people charged, 15 men
and two women. [@1] The acting commissioner described all 17 as unemployed and
suspected of involvement in drugs. [@1] Police said they seized 1.07 grams of
marijuana and 2.73 grams of methamphetamine in the week's raids. [@2] The 17
suspects, aged between 25 and 50, were held at Tanumalala Prison until 17
February 2026 to appear in court. [@2]

Leiataua said reports from the public and leads from earlier raids helped police
continue the campaign. [@1] He said police would not stop trying to eliminate
drugs from Samoa and that raids continued through the festive season. [@1]
