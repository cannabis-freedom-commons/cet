Khatai district police in Baku detained a previously convicted 42-year-old man,
Orkhan Gurbanov, in an anti-drug operation announced on 25 December 2025. [@1]
Three others — 36-year-old Emin Bayramov, 42-year-old Ilkin Alizade and their
36-year-old acquaintance Amil Ibayev — were held in further operations on
suspicion of illegal drug circulation. [@3]

Officers searching the Land Rover Gurbanov was driving found and seized 17
kilograms 330 grams of marijuana, according to the Interior Ministry's press
service. [@1] From the other three, police seized a total of 21 kilograms of
marijuana and 2 kilograms 809 grams of heroin. [@3]

The four told investigators they had obtained the drugs to sell them in the
capital, Baku. [@1] Criminal cases were opened and the investigation continued.
[@2] The police press service added that Khatai district officers had removed
112 kilograms of narcotics of various kinds from circulation since the beginning
of that year, a separate year-to-date total. [@1]
