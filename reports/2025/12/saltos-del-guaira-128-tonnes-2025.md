Prosecutor Abelino Bareiro Aguirre, who heads the specialised anti-drug unit in
Saltos del Guairá, led the procedures that produced the year's seizures. [@1]
The result is a yearly balance of a series of procedures, run from February to
December 2025, rather than a single seizure. [@2] Those procedures seized
128.668 tonnes of pressed marijuana, about 128,668 kilograms. [@1]

The work was part of investigations into criminal structures dedicated to drug
trafficking and organised crime in the unit's area. [@1] Officers also seized
numerous vehicles, firearms and other equipment of restricted state use during
the procedures. [@1] The unit said the seized vehicles, weapons and equipment
were linked to criminal organisations operating in the zone. [@1] The procedures
were carried out jointly with the National Anti-Drug Secretariat (Senad), the
National Police's anti-narcotics department and other state institutions, in
coordinated and sustained work. [@3]
