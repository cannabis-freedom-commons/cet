Police in eastern Afghanistan's Ghazni province burned thousands of kilograms of
illegal drugs collected over the past year, the Ministry of Interior Affairs
said. [@1] The Ministry of Interior put the total at 8.9 tons. [@4] The public
destruction was watched by officials. [@2]

The destroyed drugs included 464 kg of hashish, 365 kg of opium, 350 kg of
heroin, 15 kg of methamphetamine and 3,500 stimulant tablets known locally as
tablet K. [@2] Counter-narcotics police of the Ghazni police headquarters had
seized the material. [@3] All of it was destroyed by fire in the presence of
relevant officials. [@3]

The ministry said the burning was aimed at preventing the distribution and
consumption of narcotics across the country. [@4] Authorities described the
measure as part of a wider campaign against drug trafficking and the social
harms of drug abuse. [@4] The statement warned that police would not allow the
cultivation, processing or trafficking of drugs anywhere in Afghanistan. [@1]
