Police in Karonga arrested 26-year-old William Kishombe on suspicion of being
found in possession of 10 sachets of cannabis sativa without a permit. [@1]
Karonga Police Station public relations officer Constable Margret Msiska said
detectives arrested him on 30 December at around 10:00 while conducting general
observations within Karonga Township. [@1] A second account said the arrest
happened on a Tuesday during routine police patrols in various parts of Karonga
District. [@2] Kishombe hails from Mwafilaso Village in the area of Traditional
Authority Kyungu, Karonga District. [@1]

Msiska said the suspect was immediately apprehended after failing to produce a
permit or supporting documents for the 10 sachets, which were wrapped in a black
jumbo and found in his possession. [@1] The seized substance was sent to
Lunyangwa Research Station for laboratory analysis. [@1] Face of Malawi
described the allegation as possessing cannabis without a licence. [@2] Kishombe
was expected to appear in court soon on a charge of being found in possession of
cannabis sativa without a permit, contrary to Regulation 4, Section 19(1) of the
Dangerous Drugs Act. [@1]
