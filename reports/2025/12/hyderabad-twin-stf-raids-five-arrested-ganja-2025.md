Five people were arrested in two separate raids by the State Task Force of the
Telangana Prohibition and Excise Department in Hyderabad. [@1] Officers seized
2.34 kg of ganja, 4.2 grams of hash oil, two mobile phones and an autorickshaw
used in the offence. [@1] In the first raid the STF-A team and staff of the
Dhoolpet excise station searched a residence in Dilawar Gunj in the Mangalhat
area of Dhoolpet. [@1] Three people, Jaya Bai, Dilip Singh and Sunita Bai, were
arrested there and 1.220 kg of ganja was seized from them. [@1] A case was
registered against Shivani Singh, Durgeshwari and two others over the offence.
[@1] The three arrested people and the ganja were handed to the Dhoolpet excise
station for further investigation. [@1]

In the second operation the STF-B team arrested K. Suryaprakash and K. Robert
Antony Raj during checks in Madhapur. [@1] Officers seized 1.120 kg of ganja and
4.2 grams of hash oil from the pair. [@1] Two mobile phones and the
auto-rickshaw used in the offence were also confiscated. [@1] The two accused
and the seized contraband were handed to the Alwal excise station. [@1] Further
investigation was under way in both cases. [@1]
