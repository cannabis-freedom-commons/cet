Police arrested two men during a farm raid in the Wepener area of the Free State
on Sunday 21 December 2025. [@2] Free State police spokesperson Mahlomola Kareli
said the two suspects were aged 41 and 52. [@1] The men were taken into custody
without resistance and were due to appear in the Wepener Magistrate's Court.
[@2]

A search of the residence found 78 boxes of dagga and 9 mm live rounds in a
bedroom. [@1] The two face charges of dealing in dagga and unlawful possession
of ammunition. [@1]

The team had received a tip-off that a farm in the Wepener district was being
used to transport and store illicit substances. [@1] A police spokesperson said
the joint effort involved intelligence-led action by several law enforcement
teams. [@2]
