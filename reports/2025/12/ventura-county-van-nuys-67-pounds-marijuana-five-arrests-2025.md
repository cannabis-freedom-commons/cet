Ventura County sheriff's detectives arrested five men accused of belonging to a
drug trafficking organization that distributed cocaine and methamphetamine in
the county. [@2] The sheriff's office said Andrew Baz, 25, stored the
organization's drugs and cash at his home in Van Nuys. [@2] On 4 December 2025
deputies searched Soto's home in the 16000 block of Kalisher Street in Granada
Hills and Baz's home in the 16000 block of Victory Boulevard in Van Nuys. [@2]
Soto and Baz were booked into the Ventura County Pre-Trial Detention Facility on
suspicion of possession of a controlled substance for sale, transportation of a
controlled substance, conspiracy and weapons violations. [@2]

Inside Baz's home detectives seized about 67 pounds of marijuana, along with
fentanyl, cocaine, promethazine, several hundred oxycodone pills, six rifles and
three handguns. [@2] Ventura County deputies put the operation's combined haul
at 92 pounds of drugs, hundreds of oxycodone pills and 14 guns, a figure that
includes the marijuana. [@1]

Narcotics detectives arrested Jose Santoyo and Manuel Sosa on 11 December 2025
for delivering the organization's drugs in Los Angeles and Ventura counties.
[@2] Detectives detained Erik Gutierrez in Oxnard on 5 December 2025. [@2] A
search of Gutierrez's home on Libre Street in Oxnard turned up about one
kilogram of cocaine and methamphetamine. [@2] The Ventura County district
attorney's office filed a criminal complaint against Gutierrez, Soto and Sosa.
[@2] Santoyo's case was under review by the Ventura County district attorney.
[@2] Baz's case was to be presented to the Los Angeles County district attorney
for filing. [@2]

The operation was run by the Ventura County Combined Agency Team, which brings
together the sheriff's office, Oxnard police, the DEA, Homeland Security
Investigations, the FBI, the California National Guard and the district
attorney's office. [@2]
