Officers of the Naval Prefecture of Argentina seized more than 1,200 kilograms
of marijuana in Misiones province, carried in a pickup truck with a theft
warrant from Buenos Aires province. [@1] The operation took place on Monday 29
December near Puerto Tuyaí, at kilometre 1,818 of the Paraná river in the city
of Eldorado. [@1] Prefecture personnel at the riverbank saw a blue Chevrolet S10
pickup with several people loading large bundles at about 11:30. [@3] When they
noticed the officers, the suspects sped off along a dirt road and abandoned the
vehicle with its load. [@1]

A search of the area found the truck with a rear wheel detached and the driver's
door open. [@1] Rectangular plastic-wrapped parcels in the cabin and cargo bed
tested positive for marijuana in field tests. [@1] Officers counted 42 bundles
containing 1,709 compact cakes with a final weight of 1,213.260 kilograms. [@1]
The government's release reported 42 bundles holding 1,213 kilograms of drugs,
valued at more than 4.4 billion pesos. [@2] The official appraisal put the cargo
at 4,473,896,250 pesos and the vehicle at 20 million pesos, for a combined
4,493,896,250 pesos. [@1] Inspection of the pickup established that it had a
theft seizure order and that the cabin and cargo bed held numerous cakes of
marijuana. [@2]

A vehicle records check showed the pickup had a theft seizure order from UFI 11
of La Matanza through the federal police communications system SIFCOP. [@1]
Authorities said the finding supported the hypothesis of an interprovincial
drug-trafficking operation. [@1] The federal prosecutor's office in Eldorado
took the case and Federal Judge Miguel Ángel Guerrero ordered the truck and
drugs moved to the Héctor H. Ligorria municipal sports hall for counting,
weighing and storage under judicial supervision. [@1] Security Minister
Alejandra Monteoliva praised the operation on X, writing that the Naval
Prefecture had delivered another major blow against drug trafficking in
Misiones. [@1]
