In Baranovichi, a 68-year-old pensioner was found with kilograms of cannabis.
[@1] Officers raided her property in September after a tip that she was growing
prohibited plants on her private plot. [@1] Officers found and seized seven
cannabis bushes, and also found prepared drugs stored in boxes, trays and bags
in the house. [@1]

In total almost 4 kg was seized, including almost 3 kg of flowering-stage plants
and more than 800 g of dried material. [@1]

She said she had read an internet recipe for adding dried cannabis to tea as a
medicinal remedy. [@1] She said growing cannabis was motivated by concern for
her health, not a desire for easy money. [@1] She knew the plants were drugs
that could bring criminal liability but paid it no attention. [@1] She was
charged under part 1 of article 328 of the Criminal Code for unlawful
manufacture, processing, acquisition, storage, transportation or shipment of
drugs without intent to sell. [@1] The charge carries up to five years of
restricted freedom or two to five years of imprisonment. [@1]
