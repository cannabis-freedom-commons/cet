Namibian police made several arrests over the weekend in different regions for
drug-related offences, seizing cannabis and mandrax during targeted operations.
[@1] In Noordoewer, a 30-year-old Namibian male was apprehended on Friday after
police found cannabis and N$16,750 at his residence, where he was allegedly
selling drugs. [@1] In Keetmanshoop, a 25-year-old Namibian male was arrested at
the police station on Friday evening for attempting to smuggle a full mandrax
tablet, 10 bankies of cannabis and two ballies of pure cannabis, with a total
value of N$1,010. [@1] In Mariental, a male and female pair, both around 27
years old, were arrested early Friday morning at their home with 60 ballies of
pure cannabis valued at N$11,150. [@1] In the Withuis location, a 24-year-old
man was arrested on Saturday after police seized a cannabis plant worth N$9,500.
[@1]

At Mariental’s Withuis location, police carried out two separate drug-related
arrests early on Saturday. [@2] In Tsumkwe’s Xama village, a 35-year-old
Namibian male was arrested on Friday after being found with 20 bankies of
cannabis/skunk and N$300, with the total estimated value at N$1,500. [@1] At
Klein-Aub, a 70-year-old pensioner was arrested on Friday afternoon after police
found six bankies of skunk cannabis concealed on his body during a
stop-and-search on the C24 Rehoboth-Klein-Aub gravel road at the Slaaipoort
turn. [@2] In Otjiwarongo’s Orwetoveni area, two arrests were made on Saturday,
with a 28-year-old man found with cannabis/skunk valued at N$10,100 and a
23-year-old man arrested with 12 bankies of cannabis worth N$600 and N$70. [@1]
In Warmbad, a 33-year-old Namibian male was arrested on Friday morning after
police discovered half a mandrax tablet concealed in his pockets during a body
search. [@1]

Police investigations into all cases are ongoing. [@1] The Klein-Aub pensioner
was expected to appear in court on Monday. [@2] Both Withuis suspects remained
in custody while investigations continued. [@2]
