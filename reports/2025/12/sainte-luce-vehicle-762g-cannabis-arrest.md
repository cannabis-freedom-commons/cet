On the evening of 17 December 2025, Marin customs officers on a
coastal-surveillance mission were alerted by the armed forces operations centre
to a probable illegal yole landing near Corps de Garde beach at Sainte-Luce.
[@1] Continuing their watch for possible accomplices or other illegally landed
goods, officers checked a stationary vehicle with its lights off and its driver
in the same sector. [@1] The driver, already known to the authorities for
drug-related offences, was arrested and placed in customs detention. [@1] He was
then handed to the Marin gendarmerie and placed in police custody. [@1]

The check found 411 grams of cannabis herb and 351 grams of cannabis resin. [@1]
No proven link was established with the first case from the same evening. [@1]

The landing response was supported by the customs maritime and land operations
centre and carried out in cooperation with the Martinique gendarmerie, whose
approach caused several individuals to flee. [@1] After a pursuit, officers
arrested a Saint Lucian national. [@1] A search of bags apparently landed on the
beach found a loaded semi-automatic handgun with five chambered rounds, 700
rounds of 40 and 9 mm ammunition, two large-capacity rifle magazines in 223
calibre with 40 rounds, and 101 grams of cocaine. [@1] Officers also seized six
cartons of contraband cigarettes. [@1] The two interventions were presented as
part of increased cooperation between customs, coastguards and the gendarmerie.
[@1] They were said to illustrate intensified monitoring of the coastline. [@1]
