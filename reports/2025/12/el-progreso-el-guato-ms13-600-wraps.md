Honduran National Police announced the capture of a man known as alias "El
Guato" in El Progreso. [@1] Police called him a gatillero of the MS-13, a gunman
role in the Mara Salvatrucha. [@1]

He was captured as he was preparing to sell and distribute supposed illicit
substances. [@1] Officers seized more than 600 wrappers containing presumed
cocaine, marijuana and crack. [@1]
