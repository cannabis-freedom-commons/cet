On 19 December 2025 the Brussels correctional court sentenced ten people, mainly
Albanian nationals, to prison terms of twenty months to six years for
involvement in large-scale cannabis cultivation. [@1] The two brothers referred
to as B. and their cousin R., identified as the central figures, received prison
terms of five and six years. [@1] The growers employed at the various sites
received prison terms of between 36 months and four years. [@1] Another
defendant who rented a building on the network's behalf was sentenced to twenty
months in prison. [@1]

The court also ordered confiscation of more than 2.5 million euros,
corresponding to the estimated proceeds of the activity. [@1] The network was
dismantled in June 2024 after several months of investigation by the federal
judicial police of Leuven. [@1] The investigation began in October 2023 after
investigators were informed of several cannabis plantations linked to the
Albanian drug milieu. [@1] Investigators quickly identified two brothers
referred to as B. and their cousin R. as the central figures. [@1]

From the summer of 2020, during the coronavirus restrictions, the organisers
looked for buildings that could discreetly house crops. [@1] The sites retained
included a closed sushi restaurant in Laeken, a dwelling in Heverlee and a
building in Vilvoorde. [@1] The organisers called on compatriots to operate the
plantations and handle their supply. [@1]

After several weeks of surveillance, police installed listening devices in a
vehicle used by different network members. [@1] A first plantation was found and
dismantled at Heverlee in March 2024, and seven searches in June neutralised the
Vilvoorde and Laeken plantations and an installation being fitted out at Muizen.
