Spanish police said they had broken a gang that used helicopters to smuggle
hashish from Morocco into Spain. [@1] Six arrests were made in the operation,
which involved Moroccan, Belgian and Swedish law enforcement. [@1] The six were
detained for drug trafficking, illicit arms possession and membership of a
criminal organisation and placed at the disposal of the courts. [@2] The Civil
Guard said officers found 25 bales of hashish weighing 657 kilograms on an
estate in Níjar, Almeria province. [@2]

Officers seized one helicopter, 657 kilograms (1,448 pounds) of hashish, five
firearms, cash and vehicles during raids in the provinces of Malaga, Almeria and
Murcia. [@1] The helicopters could carry between 500 and 900 kilograms of drugs,
which were stored in rural estates and warehouses in southern Spain before
distribution by road to other European countries. [@1]

Spain's proximity to Morocco makes it a key entry point for drugs into Europe,
but smuggling typically takes place by sea. [@1]
