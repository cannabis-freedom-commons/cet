Customs agents at La Goulette port in Tunis foiled an attempt to bring nearly
100 kilograms of cannabis into the country, hidden in the boot of a car carried
on a ferry from a European country. [@1] Control teams spotted suspicious signs
as the vehicle passed through the scanner, and a thorough inspection uncovered a
large quantity of cannabis resin, packaged and hidden in the rear compartment.
[@1] The suspect vehicle had been noticed when it arrived at the port on Sunday
7 December 2025, with the details released the next day. [@2]

The driver was placed in police custody, known as garde à vue, on the
instruction of the public prosecutor at the court of first instance in Tunis.
[@1] The case was handed to the anti-narcotics sub-directorate of the judicial
police at El Gorjani, which opened a judicial investigation into the cargo's
destination and the members of the network involved. [@3]

Customs said the seizure ranked among the largest made recently at Tunisia's
maritime entry points, reflecting both more attempts to route drug cargoes
through the country and greater vigilance by the specialised units. [@1]
