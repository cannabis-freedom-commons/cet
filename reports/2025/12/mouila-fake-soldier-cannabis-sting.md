Gendarmes in Mouila, Gabon, arrested two men during a routine road check, one of
them posing as a corporal in the Gabonese army. [@1] The man was identified as
Ervan Christ Makanga Moubamba, a 28-year-old Gabonese driver stopped on his
motorcycle while travelling to Ndendé, who was in fact a fake corporal in the
Gabonese armed forces. [@2]

Investigators found messages and images on his phone ordering cannabis and
Tramadol from Congo, and a search of his home found a small quantity of Indian
hemp. [@2] Gendarmerie units from Mouila and Mabanda set a trap with the
prosecutor's approval, and the supplier sent two couriers to deliver the goods.
[@3] The couriers were Kefer Mbina, 22, and a 12-year-old Congolese boy
identified only as I.M., both intercepted on arrival. [@2] Gabon Actu reported
the haul as nearly 50 kilograms of cannabis and 100 blister packs of Tramadol
hidden in a sack. [@1] Gabon Media Time, citing L'Union, gave the delivered
cargo as a sack of 17 kilograms of cannabis and 91 blister packs of Tramadol.
[@3]

The Gabonese man and the Congolese courier were placed in preventive detention.
[@1] Investigators said the presumed Congolese wholesaler was still being
sought. [@1] The Mouila prosecutor ordered the case transferred to the special
tribunal in Libreville, and a remand order was issued against Makanga Moubamba,
described as a repeat offender, and Kefer Mbina on suspicion of buying, selling
and consuming cannabis. [@2] The 12-year-old was handed to an orphanage pending
repatriation formalities. [@2]
