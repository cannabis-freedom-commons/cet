The ministry said the man was suspected of organising the smuggling of a large
batch of narcotics into Azerbaijan. [@1]

Officers seized 41 kg of marijuana, which the ministry described as enriched
with psychotropic substances. [@1] They also found 2,988 methadone tablets on
him; the tablets are not counted as cannabis in this report. [@2] Initial
questioning indicated that the drugs had been smuggled into Azerbaijan from the
Iranian city of Parsabad and were to be placed at addresses in Fuzuli district.
[@2]

A criminal case was opened and a court ordered that the detainee be held in
custody as a preventive measure. [@1] Police said operations were continuing to
identify other members of the group with whom he worked inside the country and
abroad. [@2]
