Royal Saint Lucia Police Force officers from the Gangs, Narcotics and Firearms
Unit and the Choiseul Police Station executed a search warrant in Martin,
Choiseul, on 29 December 2025. [@1] The operation uprooted 5,580 cannabis plants
and seized 7.3 kilograms of cannabis from the residence of 41-year-old Delecus
Alphonse. [@1] Three cannabis plantations were discovered during the operation.
[@2]

Alphonse was arrested and charged with possession of a controlled drug
(cannabis), possession of a controlled drug with intent to supply and
cultivation of a controlled plant (cannabis). [@1] He appeared before the Second
District Court in Vieux Fort on 30 December 2025, when a magistrate set bail at
$3,000 cash or suitable surety, supported by land documents, on each of the two
possession charges and $3,600 on the cultivation charge. [@1] St. Lucia News Now
put the total bail at $9,600 cash or suitable surety. [@2]

As bail conditions he must report to the Choiseul Police Station every Saturday
between 6:00 a.m. and 6:00 p.m., surrender all travel documents and not leave
Saint Lucia without explicit authorisation from the Court. [@1] He is due to
reappear in court on 11 February 2026. [@2]
