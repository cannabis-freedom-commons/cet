Cayman Islands Customs and Border Control arrested two people after intercepting
a shipment arriving from the United States that concealed ganja along with four
firearms and ammunition. [@2] The agency withheld the identities of those
arrested, the type of firearms and the concealment method, saying the details
could not be disclosed without compromising the investigation. [@3]

The ganja weighed three pounds (about 1.36 kg), according to the customs
service. [@2] The interception happened during routine border control processes
on Thursday 18 December 2025. [@3] CBC said the items were secured before
entering circulation and posed no ongoing risk to the public. [@3]

Director Bruce Smith said the interception underscored the role of customs and
border control in safeguarding the islands' borders and communities. [@1] Deputy
Director Marlon Bodden, who heads CBC's Investigations Portfolio, said the
seizure reflected the strength of the service's intelligence-led approach. [@2]
Officials said customs personnel continued to work with on-island and
international agencies to disrupt cross-border crime and stop firearms and
illegal substances from entering Cayman. [@1]
