A 21-year-old Nepalese woman, Nisha Raai, was arrested on 17 December 2025 after
police found drugs in her apartment in Malé. [@2] The apartment was in the
Machangolhi district of Malé City. [@1] The Criminal Court remanded her in
police custody for five days after she asked for time to hire a lawyer. [@1] On
4 January 2026 the court extended her remand by 30 days, saying the state had
presented sufficient evidence linking her to the alleged offence. [@2]

Police recovered 230 grams of cannabis and 141 grams of heroin in the case. [@2]
Her urine tested positive for cannabis. [@3] Police said the equipment suggested
she was involved in selling drugs. [@1] Police put the total weight of drugs
seized in the operation at 377.39 grams. [@4]

Four people were arrested in the operation. [@5] The arrests were made in a
joint operation by the Drug Enforcement Department and Counter Narcotics
Intelligence after a tip-off. [@4] Officers searched a guesthouse and an
apartment in Malé, where they found the drugs. [@4] Three Maldivian men arrested
in the same operation were remanded, two for 16 days and the third for 15 days.
[@2]
