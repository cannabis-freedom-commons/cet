Nathavaram police in Anakapalle district, Andhra Pradesh, arrested eight people,
including a woman software engineer, on suspicion of supplying cannabis from
Narsipatnam to Tamil Nadu and Sri Lanka. [@1] The arrests were made on Friday,
26 December 2025, the New Indian Express reported the following day. [@2]
Officers from the Narsipatnam rural and Nathavaram police stations intercepted
the gang near Srugavaram village. [@2] They seized 74 kg of dry cannabis, a car,
two motorcycles and mobile phones. [@1] Police said it was likely the first time
cannabis from the Andhra-Odisha border had been smuggled into Sri Lanka. [@1]

Police identified the woman as Gade Renuka, 28, a Bengaluru-based software
professional and a native of Santhakaviti in Vizianagaram district. [@1] They
called her the "lady don" for her earlier involvement in the cannabis trade
across Payakaraopeta, Narsipatnam, Saluru and Bengaluru police limits. [@1]
Renuka and her associate Surya Kalidas rented a house in Narsipatnam from which
cannabis bought in Odisha was supplied to Tamil Nadu and Sri Lanka. [@1] With
the middleman Adduri Prasad, she bought cannabis from tribals in Balimela and
Chitrakonda in Odisha at Rs 5,000 per kg. [@1] The New Indian Express named the
suppliers as tribals A Ravi Kumar, O Lalitha Kumari and P Mani Kumari. [@2]

Drivers Madhan Kumar and Naga Muttu carried the consignments to the Rajanagaram
highway junction, from where they were routed to Tamil Nadu and then into Sri
Lanka. [@1] Police said the gang also sold cannabis in small packets across
Tamil Nadu. [@1] The Island reported that authorities were still investigating
the network to identify other people involved. [@3]
