The Judicial Police detained two men aged 22 and 39 in a flagrante delito
operation in Praia, suspecting them of high-risk drug trafficking and of
converting and concealing assets. [@1] The operation was carried out on Saturday
20 December 2025, with home searches in the Achada Santo António (Brasil) and
Fundo Cobom neighbourhoods of Praia. [@1]

Officers seized 254.452 grams of cocaine and 6.352 grams of hashish, along with
plastic sachets used to package drugs. [@1] Police also seized 38,600 escudos in
cash, several mobile phones, a motorcycle and other items of evidentiary
interest. [@1]

The searches were carried out under two search-and-seizure warrants requested by
the Public Prosecutor and issued by the criminal investigation judge of the
Praia court. [@3] Both suspects were brought before the judicial authorities for
a first judicial interrogation and the application of coercive measures. [@2]
The Praia court ordered pretrial detention for both men, who will await further
proceedings in the São Martinho civil prison. [@3]
