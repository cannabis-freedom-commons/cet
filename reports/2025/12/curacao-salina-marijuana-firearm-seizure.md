Police in Curaçao arrested a 50-year-old man on Tuesday over allegations of
relational threats said to have occurred between September and December 2025.
[@1] The suspect, identified only by the initials C.M.V., was taken into custody
in the Saliña area on the orders of the Public Prosecution Service. [@1] A
Dutch-language Curaçao report described the same Tuesday arrest of a 50-year-old
man in connection with alleged relational threats. [@2]

Following the arrest, officers searched the suspect's residence on Kaya
Cruzeiro. [@1] During the search police found a firearm and a quantity of
marijuana, and confiscated both as part of the investigation. [@1] The
Dutch-language account reported the same discovery of a firearm and marijuana at
the Kaya Cruzeiro home and their seizure. [@2]

The man was brought before an assistant public prosecutor, who ordered that he
remain in custody while the investigation into the alleged threats continued.
[@1] Police said the case had been under investigation for several months. [@1]
A Dutch-language report said the man was brought before a deputy public
prosecutor and held in custody pending further investigation into the alleged
threats. [@2]
