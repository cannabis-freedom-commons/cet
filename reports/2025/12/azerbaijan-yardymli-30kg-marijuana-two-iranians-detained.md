Police in Yardymli district in southern Azerbaijan detained two Iranian citizens
accused of organising the smuggling of drugs into the country and their
placement at separate addresses. [@2] The Interior Ministry's Lankaran regional
press group said officers of the Yardymli district police department carried out
the operation. [@2] Officers seized 30 kilograms of marijuana described as
enriched with psychotropic substances from the two men. [@1] The detainees were
23 and 26 years old. [@2]

The Interior Ministry named the two men as Khauri Gadirpuri and Suleyman Khezri.
[@1] A criminal case was opened over the find, and a court ordered that both men
be held in pre-trial detention. [@3] Police said operations were continuing to
identify and detain the other members of the organised group and to establish
their links inside the country. [@4]
