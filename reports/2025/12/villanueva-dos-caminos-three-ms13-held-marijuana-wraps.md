Honduras's Police Investigations Directorate (DPI) arrested three alleged Mara
Salvatrucha (MS-13) members in colonia Gracias a Dios, sector Dos Caminos,
Villanueva, Cortés, on accusations of drug trafficking. [@2] The three, aged 20
to 25, were known in the group as “Pumba”, “El Dientón” and “El Zarco” and
allegedly held the rank of traka. [@2] Honduras's National Police announced the
same arrests on Facebook, naming the aliases as “Pumba”, “El Dienton” and “El
Sarco” and saying drugs were found in their possession. [@1]

At the arrests officers seized two transparent plastic wraps of dried herb
described as apparent marijuana. [@2] The same seizure included 14 pointed
transparent wraps of white powder described as alleged cocaine and seven
transparent wraps of white stone described as alleged crack. [@2]

Investigators said the suspects sold illegal substances in Villanueva and nearby
areas. [@2] Officers placed the accused and the seized evidence at the disposal
of the Public Ministry for due process. [@2]
