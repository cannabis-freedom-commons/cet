Officers from drug-control units of the Nizhny Novgorod regional police and the
Gorodetsky district police detained an unconvicted local man born in 1991 while
following up operational information about drug trafficking. [@1] Police said
the man grew drug-containing plants for later sale. [@1] An investigator of the
Gorodetsky police opened a criminal case for attempted large-scale sale of
narcotic drugs under part 3 of article 30 and point g of part 4 of article 228.1
of the Russian Criminal Code. [@1] The man was remanded in custody. [@1]

Officers inspecting the house in Serkovo in Gorodetsky district where the
detainee lived found a plastic container, a glass, a tape-wrapped bundle and a
box containing plant-origin material. [@1] In a house, garage and outbuilding in
Strochkovo belonging to the suspect, drug police found and seized a plastic
container with seeds, jars, packets and boxes holding plant parts with a
characteristic marijuana smell, and similar ground material was stored on a
hollow bumper part. [@1] All seized material was sent to the regional police
forensic centre, whose examination found it was hemp parts with a total weight
of 20 kg. [@1]

The offence carries up to 20 years' imprisonment under Russian law. [@1]
