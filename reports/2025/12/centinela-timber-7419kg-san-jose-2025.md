Gendarmes of the San José section of Squadron 8 Alto Uruguay stopped a
semi-trailer truck at kilometre 785 of National Route 14 in Misiones. [@1] The
truck carried a bulky load of timber and was bound for Pehuajó in Buenos Aires
province. [@1] La Voz del Interior reported that the operation was carried out
on the previous Saturday, 6 December 2025. [@3] The Federal Court and Federal
Prosecutor's Office in Posadas authorised moving the truck and its load to the
Gendarmería unit for a fuller inspection. [@2] The magistrate ordered the
seizure of the drug and the truck and the detention of the driver, whose details
were not released. [@1]

Officers ran the force's mobile scanner over the vehicle and saw packages of a
density and colour consistent with illegal substances among the beams. [@1] The
anti-narcotics dog Jacki climbed onto the trailer, barked and scratched at a set
of boards, marking the possible presence of drugs. [@1]

Once the timber was unloaded, officers counted 21 wooden crates holding 9,361
packages with a total weight of 7,418.912 kg of marijuana. [@1] Gendarmería
noted that cannabis loads are frequently detected at the same fixed checkpoint.
