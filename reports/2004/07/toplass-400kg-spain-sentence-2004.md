Steven Toplass, a truck driver from Stoke-on-Trent, was taken into custody in
Spain in February 2004 after 400 kg of cannabis was found in the trailer he was
driving. [@3] In July 2004 a Spanish court convicted him of involvement in
transporting the drugs and sentenced him to three and a half years in prison
with a €600,000 fine. [@4,1] He had taken the Spanish delivery as a one-off
while between jobs, his fiancee Diane Downes said. [@3]

Britain's National Crime Squad found Toplass had been used as an unwitting mule
and had no case to answer, and the Crown Prosecutor wrote that there was no
evidential basis to prosecute him as a party to the conspiracy. [@1,4] The
Foreign Office passed a legalised translation of that letter to the Spanish
judge, who refused to acknowledge it; minister Douglas Alexander and Toplass's
MP Robert Flello then discussed the case with Fair Trials Abroad. [@4] Freed in
April 2005 to testify for the Crime Squad against a drug gang, he was kept in
prison after a Spanish judge rejected the prosecution service letter as
possibly inauthentic. [@2]

In June 2006 Toplass returned home after two years in a Spanish jail with a
full pardon, once it was accepted he knew nothing about the 400 kg. [@5]
