Saudi Arabia beheaded two Iraqi nationals by sword on Sunday after convicting them of smuggling drugs into the kingdom. [@1] The Interior Ministry named them as Mohammad bin Ali al-Slami and Faisal bin Karim al-Shiblawi, convicted of smuggling large amounts of hashish and sentenced to death under the kingdom's strict interpretation of Islamic law. [@1]

The ministry's statement put the number of people executed in the kingdom that year at 30, against 52 beheadings the year before, most of them for drug smuggling. [@1]
