Australian Barry Wilfred Hess, 50, walked out of Bali's Kerobokan Prison on 19 December 2007 after serving four months for possessing 14.4 grams of hashish and 2.7 grams of marijuana found at his home in Kuta. [@1,2] Denpasar District Court had cleared him of drug trafficking, which can carry the death penalty, and instead found him guilty of being a drug addict who had not reported himself for rehabilitation. [@1,2]

Hess, a former Ansett executive and general manager of the collapsed Balinese airline Air Paradise, had been arrested in August 2007 after police found the drugs in his Kuta home. [@1,2] He served the sentence alongside members of the Bali Nine and cannabis trafficker Schapelle Corby. [@1]

After his release immigration officials took him to a holding cell near Bali airport while authorities decided whether to deport him. [@1,2] His lawyer said Hess, who runs a furniture export business and has a local partner, hoped to stay in Indonesia. [@1,2]
