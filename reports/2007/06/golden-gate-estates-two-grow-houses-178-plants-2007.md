Collier County sheriff's deputies closed two marijuana growing houses in Golden
Gate Estates and arrested three people. [@1] Adonis Montenegro-Soler, 25, of
Cape Coral, was charged with cultivation of marijuana and possession of
marijuana. [@1] Deputies found Yohan Garcia, 24, of Doral, and Deyanira Valdes,
23, inside the second house, where Valdes's primary residence was listed. [@1]
Garcia and Valdes were charged with trafficking in marijuana and possession of
more than 20 grams of marijuana. [@1]

Deputies obtained a search warrant for a home at 3345 64th Avenue N.E. after an
investigation identified it as a suspected grow house. [@1] Deputies were
watching the house when Montenegro-Soler drove away and stopped him for a
possible traffic infraction; he was driving with a suspended license. [@1] A
search of that house found 98 marijuana plants, which deputies valued at $1,000
each. [@1] Deputies then investigated a second home at 2625 58th Ave. N.E. and
determined it was also a marijuana grow house. [@1] The second search found 80
marijuana plants, also valued at $1,000 each. [@1]
