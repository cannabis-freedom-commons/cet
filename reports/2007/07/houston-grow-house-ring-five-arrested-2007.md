Acting on a tip about suspicious activity at a Montrose home, Houston narcotics
officers arrested Tan Khac Nguyen and Thien Quang Nguyen there on 2 March 2007
and found a sophisticated hydroponic grow with more than 600 marijuana
plants. [@1] A months-long follow-up identified eight more grow houses, and on
Monday 16 July officers found hydroponic operations in each, seizing about
3,100 plants weighing some 1,000 pounds along with about $500,000 in equipment
and six vehicles. [@1] Tuan The Nguyen, Minh Nguyen and Van Dung Nguyen were
arrested the same day at separate locations. [@1]

All five were charged with felony possession of marijuana, the first two in
the 228th State District Court and the other three in the 262nd. [@1]
Reporting on the Monday raids, the Houston Chronicle said officers had hit
seven homes and seized hundreds of high-grade plants after a five-month
investigation into a ring selling marijuana at about $4,000 a pound across the
country. [@2] The Bryan-College Station Eagle added that each house had been
remodelled for growing, with rooms of timer-lit plants capable of yielding
more than four pounds apiece, and that the suspects bypassed electric meters
to power them. [@2,3]

The department published four photographs of one of the raided grow
operations with its release. [@1] Police said the investigation was continuing
and more arrests were expected. [@1,2]
