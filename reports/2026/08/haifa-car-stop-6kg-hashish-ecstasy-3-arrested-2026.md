Police stopped a car at the entrance to Haifa after identifying it as suspicious
and searched it. [@1] In the search they found more than six kilograms (13.2
pounds) of a substance suspected to be hashish. [@1] Officers also confiscated
hundreds of Ecstasy pills from the car. [@1] Police said they suspected the
substances were meant to be sold. [@1]

Three residents of southern Israel were arrested on suspicion of involvement in
the case. [@1] The three were brought before a judge, who extended their
detention until Sunday, 30 August 2026. [@1] Police described the stop as a
“targeted and proactive” drug-busting operation. [@1]

Israel Police said it would keep acting resolutely against parties involved in
drug offences, locating and arresting them to prevent the distribution of drugs
and keep the public safe. [@1]
