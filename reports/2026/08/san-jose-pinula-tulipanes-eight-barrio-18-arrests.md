Eight alleged members of the Barrio 18 gang were arrested in the municipality of
San José Pinula after a public disturbance. [@1] Officers of the National Civil
Police's 13th station seized 27 rifle cartridges and 17 plastic bags containing
what police described as presumed marijuana. [@1] The arrests took place on the
main street of the Tulipanes sector of the Residenciales San José Pinula, where
the group was found fighting in the street. [@1] La Red 106.1 described the
seizure as 27 rifle cartridges and 17 bags of marijuana. [@2]

Publinews reported that police went to the area after several emergency calls
about a fight in the street. [@4] Publinews described the bags as containing dry
herb, presumably marijuana. [@4] The group was made up of three women and five
men, who were taken to the Torre de Tribunales to clarify their legal situation.
[@3]

La Red 106.1 reported that one detainee, known as Zombi, had previous records
for fighting, robbery, drug possession and attempted homicide. [@2] Prensa Libre
reported that one detainee had two previous records for illegal firearms
possession and conspiracy to commit murder, and another for extortion. [@3] The
detainees were handed to the corresponding court to resolve their legal
situation. [@4]
