Operation “Northern Spear” in Bimini resulted in the arrest of two males after
police found suspected Oxycodone pills and marijuana at a Bailey Town residence.
[@1] Police in Bimini conducted the operation on Thursday, August 20, 2026,
carrying out a series of road checks and executing several search warrants for
drug-related offences. [@1] According to the preliminary report, officers
executed the search warrant at the Bailey Town residence shortly after 6:30 a.m.
[@1]

During that search, officers discovered a backpack containing 116 suspected
Oxycodone pills. [@1] A further search of the same residence resulted in the
discovery of a small quantity of suspected marijuana. [@1] The two males
arrested in connection with the discovery were ages 25 and 36. [@1] Police gave
the estimated street value of the 116 suspected Oxycodone pills as $2,300. [@1]

Both the pills and the marijuana were described by police as suspected drugs.
[@1] Police said the investigation into the Bailey Town drug discovery remains
ongoing. [@1]
