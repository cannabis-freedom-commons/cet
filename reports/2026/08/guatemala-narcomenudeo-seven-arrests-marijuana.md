Guatemala's National Civil Police arrested at least seven people and seized
drugs packaged for sale in operations against street-level dealing in four
departments, the force announced on Saturday 29 August 2026. [@1] The operations
began on the night of Friday 28 August 2026, according to the police. [@1] The
departments named were Guatemala, Santa Rosa, Chiquimula and Escuintla. [@1]
Police named those detained in those departments as Carlos Cuz Cucul, 44;
William Franisco Rivera, 50; Jaqueline Francisca Sosa Méndez, 46; Elsa Julia
Corado Rivera, 26; and Dairy Marisol Berganza Estrada, 26. [@1]

Officers reported finding 68 small bags, four wrappers and two bags of suspected
marijuana. [@1] The finds included 245 rocks of suspected crack. [@1] The
operation also produced 52 bags of suspected methamphetamine and 94 colmillos of
suspected cocaine. [@1] Police also seized 20 cocaine capsules, a mobile phone,
a bulletproof vest, Q140.75 in cash and three notebooks with handwriting. [@1]

In a case reported with the same announcement, Gloria, 44, was detained in the
cantón Rayos del Sol, Mazatenango, Suchitepéquez, carrying 72 bags of marijuana
and nine crack rocks. [@1] Investigators of the PNC's DIPANDA unit arrested
Benjamín, 33, in Raxruhá, Alta Verapaz, saying he was carrying nine cocaine
colmillos in his car. [@1]
