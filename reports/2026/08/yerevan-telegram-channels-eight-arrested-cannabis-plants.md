Armenia's police arrested eight people in an operation against an alleged
drug-trafficking network that operated through Telegram channels, the Interior
Ministry said. [@4] Officers of the drug-trafficking division of the Yerevan
city criminal police department had received information that a group of people
were selling drugs through Telegram channels they ran. [@2] One of the channel
owners was found, detained and taken to the department, and the Arabkir
investigative division opened a criminal proceeding. [@1] Police said seven more
people were arrested in the measures that followed, and the preliminary
investigation continues. [@3]

A search at the home of one of the Telegram channel owners found 2 kg 610 g of
methamphetamine. [@2] Searches were also carried out in the flats of a number of
people connected with the channel owners, where officers found and seized
methamphetamine, 8,188,000 drams, 4,500 dollars and cannabis plants. [@1] Police
said round-the-clock surveillance at the suspects' places of residence led to
the arrests and seizures. [@4] Police said they would continue targeted efforts
to combat drug trafficking and to neutralize drug distribution networks. [@4]
