Budva police arrested two Serbian citizens during targeted controls of people of
operational interest and catering establishments, including beach bars, in the
municipality. [@1] M.M., a Serbian citizen from Kragujevac who lives in Budva,
was arrested by order of the state prosecutor at the Higher State Prosecutor's
Office in Podgorica on suspicion of unauthorised production, possession and
distribution of narcotic drugs. [@1] N.Dž., 19, a Serbian citizen, was detained
for an offence under Article 52 of the Law on the Prevention of Drug Abuse after
marijuana was found on him. [@1] A misdemeanour charge was filed against the
third Serbian citizen, V.F., 35, who was also found with marijuana. [@1]

During a targeted check on the beach in Bečići, police found and seized about
200 grams of marijuana, seven packages of cocaine, a scale for precise
measurement and 1,320 euros suspected of coming from drug sales. [@1]

Officers checked 65 people in five catering establishments, three of them of
operational interest, the Police Directorate said. [@3] Two people were deprived
of their liberty and charges were filed against three people for drug abuse
during the operation. [@1]

The cocaine was seized in seven packages of unstated weight and is excluded from
the cannabis figures. [@2]
