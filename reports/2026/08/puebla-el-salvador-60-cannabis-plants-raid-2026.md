The Puebla State Attorney General's Office said it searched and secured a
property that was allegedly used to grow drugs in the state capital, where it
found plants with the characteristics of marijuana. [@1] Agents of the state
prosecutor's high-impact crimes unit carried out the search with soldiers,
National Guard officers and state preventive police. [@1] Anonymous complaints
and field and desk intelligence led investigators to the home in the El Salvador
neighbourhood, and a judge granted a search warrant. [@1]

Inside, officers found 60 plants described as apparent marijuana growing in pots
of various sizes. [@3] The property and the plants were secured and left under
guard with institutional seals. [@3]

No one was arrested during the search, the prosecutor's office said. [@1] The
evidence was handed to a public prosecutor, who would continue building the
investigation file to establish possible legal responsibilities. [@3] The state
prosecutor's office said it would keep working with federal and state forces
against the production, distribution and sale of illegal substances in Puebla.
[@2]
