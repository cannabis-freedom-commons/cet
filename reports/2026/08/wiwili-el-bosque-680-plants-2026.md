Nicaraguan Army soldiers detained Nelson Antonio Sevilla Arróliga, a 30-year-old
Nicaraguan, during an operation against a marijuana plantation in the community
of El Bosque, Wiwilí municipality, Jinotega department, on 21 August 2026. [@1]
The army described him as the person in charge of looking after the plantation.
[@1] Troops of the 6 Comando Militar Regional, the Sixth Regional Military
Command, seized and incinerated 680 marijuana plants at the site. [@3] The army
said the plants, the detained man and the material seized were handed to the
corresponding authorities. [@1]

The army dated its press note in Managua on 21 August 2026. [@1] The note placed
the military's actions under the state strategy Muro de Contención, or
containment wall, within Operation Dignidad Nacional and a permanent security
plan for the countryside. [@1] La Nueva Radio YA reported that the Sixth
Regional Military Command located, seized and burned the 680 plants in El Bosque
on 21 August. [@2] That outlet said the detainee had been in charge of caring
for and maintaining the illegal crop. [@2] Radio La Primerísima reported that
soldiers burned the plants and detained Sevilla Arróliga as the man in charge of
tending the marijuana. [@3]

The same press note reported a separate operation on 18 August, in which the
Pacific Naval District seized 24 rectangular blocks of cocaine from a panga-type
boat about 17 kilometres northeast of Mechapa, El Viejo, Chinandega, after the
crew fled. [@1] Troops there also took two outboard motors and found an
abandoned Costa Rican-registered Toyota pickup truck. [@1]
