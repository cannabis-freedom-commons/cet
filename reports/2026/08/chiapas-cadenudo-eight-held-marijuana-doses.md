Eight people were detained in five operations in Tapachula, Puerto Madero and
Huixtla, Chiapas, the federal security ministry said. [@1] Among those detained
were Cristian Emmanuel, known as “Cadenudo” or “Coca”, who has an extradition
order in Guatemala and is linked to cocaine trafficking, and Roberto Jeremías
Pérez Monzón, who has an arrest warrant in Guatemala. [@1] Members of Los
Cuatetes, described as generators of violence linked to homicides in the region,
were also detained. [@1] The operations were led by Chiapas state police and the
Chiapas state prosecutor's office, with the army, navy, federal prosecutor's
office, security ministry and National Guard taking part. [@1] Harfuch thanked
Chiapas governor Eduardo Ramírez and Guatemala's government for their support.
[@1]

In Puerto Madero, officers detained four men aged 34, 35, 37 and 44, one of them
a foreign national, and seized three long guns, five magazines, 184 cartridges,
30 doses of marijuana and a vehicle. [@1] Two of those men, aged 34 and 35, are
wanted by Guatemala, one under an extradition order and the other under an
arrest warrant, for crimes against health, extortionate obstruction of transit
and illicit association. [@1] In Tapachula de Córdova y Ordóñez, two people, one
of them a minor, were detained with 11 doses of crystal, seven doses of
marijuana and a motorcycle. [@1] In Huixtla, officers detained a minor
identified as a priority target and a generator of violence in the zone, seizing
a short firearm, five cartridges and 22 doses of crystal. [@1] In the Buenos
Aires locality of Tapachula, a 45-year-old man was detained with a firearm, 40
cartridges, 25 doses of drug and a vehicle. [@1] Another minor, detained in
Tapachula, was carrying a short firearm, 10 cartridges, 15 doses of crystal and
12 doses of marijuana. [@1] El Economista reported the same eight detentions in
operations in Tapachula, Puerto Madero and Huixtla, and the same seizures from
the joint statement. [@2] Nación321 reported nine people detained and close to a
hundred doses of drug seized. [@3]

The Puerto Madero detainees were linked to moving cocaine in boats on the high
seas and gathering it around Puerto Madero for distribution in Mexico and
abroad. [@1] The operations formed part of the security strategy to fight
criminal groups and reduce violence in Chiapas's border region, Harfuch said.
[@2] The Security Cabinet said the institutions would keep working with local
authorities to detain generators of violence and strengthen security in strict
respect for human rights. [@1]
