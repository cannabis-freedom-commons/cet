A woman suspected of links to the shipment was arrested at an apartment in the
Residencial Bianco in Santiago de los Caballeros after DNCD agents and
prosecutors traced the cargo. [@2] At the apartment officers took several
portions of presumed marijuana, three small metal boxes of wrapping paper, a
glass container, personal documents, cash, a mobile phone and a vehicle. [@3]
She and the evidence were taken to the Santo Domingo Oeste jurisdiction, where
prosecutors said they would seek coercive measures for an alleged breach of Law
50-88 on drugs and controlled substances. [@2]

DNCD agents and the Ministerio Público seized several packages and portions of
presumed marijuana plus 23 containers filled with the plant during inspection
work at the Haina Oriental port in Santo Domingo Oeste. [@1] A second account
described the port find as three packages of marijuana, several portions and the
23 containers. [@3] DNCD agents and canine units detected the substance while
checking tanks holding clothing and food at the terminal. [@2] The tanks had
been sent from New York, United States, and were checked on arrival at Haina
Oriental. [@2]

Investigators said the case remained open to establish the drug's origin and
identify others involved in the shipment. [@2]
