Pljevlja police have filed a criminal complaint against a man from the town on
suspicion that he committed the criminal offence of unauthorised production,
possession and distribution of narcotic drugs. [@1] The offence covers
producing, possessing and placing narcotic drugs into circulation. [@2] The
Police Directorate said officers of the Intervention Unit of the Pljevlja police
found and seized the drugs in an apartment used by the suspect, M.T. [@1] The
seized drugs were about 27 grams of marijuana and about 14 grams of amphetamine.
[@2]

Police said they suspected the drugs were intended for further distribution on
the street. [@1] The Police Directorate identified the suspect only as M.T., a
33-year-old from Pljevlja. [@2] It described M.T. as a multiple repeat offender
in drug-related crimes. [@2] The state prosecutor at the Higher State
Prosecutor's Office in Bijelo Polje was informed of the case. [@1] The
prosecutor then ordered that a criminal complaint be filed against M.T. over the
two drugs. [@3]
