Police arrested two men and seized 647 kg of marijuana during a vehicle
inspection at Bhedetar in Sangurigadhi Rural Municipality-6, Dhankuta, on the
morning of 6 August 2026. [@1] The two were identified as Umesh Thing, 27, of
Kailash Rural Municipality-3, Makwanpur, the truck driver, and Tularam Rai, 36,
of Shahidbhumi Rural Municipality-1, the co-driver. [@1] Nepal News reported the
same two arrests at Bhedetar Chowk with approximately 647 kilograms of illegal
marijuana. [@2]

A team deployed from the Bhedetar Area Police Office intercepted a truck bearing
the license plate number Na 3 Kha 5095 heading toward Biratnagar for inspection.
[@2] According to police, the contraband was recovered at around 5:30 am from
the truck travelling towards Biratnagar along the Koshi Highway. [@1] Police
said they recovered 48 packets of marijuana, wrapped in black plastic, from
inside the truck. [@1]

The two have been taken into custody. [@1] Further investigation is underway
under the Narcotic Drugs (Control) Act. [@1]
