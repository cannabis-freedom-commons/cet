Police seized nearly 48,000 illegally grown marijuana plants from two Marion
County properties near Hubbard, Oregon, on 7 August 2026. [@1] Oregon State
Police worked with the Marion County Sheriff's Office, the West Side Interagency
Narcotics team and Linn County drug teams to serve separate search warrants at
the two properties in the early morning. [@1]

The agencies seized a combined 47,595 plants, 80 pounds (about 36.3 kilograms)
of processed marijuana, a small amount of cocaine and nine guns from the two
locations. [@1] The two property owners were arrested and charged with
conspiracy to unlawfully manufacture marijuana. [@1] The conspiracy charges were
against the two property owners, and an unlawful manufacture charge against
another adult male was also referred. [@2]

Officers encountered 17 people they identified as potential labor trafficking
victims who were forced to work at the properties. [@1] The people reported poor
living conditions and restrictions on their ability to leave. [@1] Sixteen of
the people were released without charges and one man was cited for possession of
cocaine and unlawful manufacture of marijuana. [@1] Safety Compass, a local
nonprofit that supports victims of human trafficking, provided hygiene kits,
food, interpreters and other resources to the victims. [@1]
