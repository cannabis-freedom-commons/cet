Officers of Georgia's Central Criminal Police Department detained two foreign
nationals in Rustavi on drug charges. [@1] The department's director, Davit
Kiknadze, announced the detention at a briefing on 25 August 2026. [@1] Police
said the two had acted together and planned to sell the drugs in various ways
before officers stopped them. [@1]

Officers seized up to 33 kilograms of hashish, described as
tetrahydrocannabinol, from one of the detainees' flats. [@1] The ministry valued
the hashish at 6 million lari on the black market, about $2.3 million. [@2] Part
of it had been hidden inside car tyres in the same flat. [@1]

Police also seized cocaine, heroin and methadone from a second flat in Rustavi
and from a hotel room. [@1] The investigation is under articles 260, 260¹,
19–260 and 265¹ of the Criminal Code, which carry up to 20 years in prison or
life imprisonment. [@1]
