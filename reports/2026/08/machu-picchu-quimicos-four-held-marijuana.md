National Police officers detained four people in Machupicchu on 26 August 2026,
preliminarily identified as suspected members of a criminal group called Los
Químicos de Machupicchu. [@1] The four were Jhonatan Ayma Sullca, 27, Erick
Davis Quispe Clemente, 26, Jhon Arnold Quispe Huamán, 22, and Lidecsy Isabel
Evarista, 44, a foreign national, and all were taken to the anti-drug unit
(Deandro) in Cusco. [@2] The operation followed intelligence work by the Machu
Picchu police station, with two officers reportedly infiltrating the group as
customers to identify its distribution points. [@2] Officers found the suspects
in a barber shop and then searched a hotel room, according to Colonel Carlos
Guizado of the Cusco police region. [@2]

Police said they seized 270 grams of marijuana and 33 grams of marijuana seeds,
along with 198 grams of coca paste, 160 wrappers of cocaine and a 'kete' of
suspected tusi. [@2] Officers also seized mobile phones, a precision scale and a
gas stove. [@1] A stove, a pot, utensils, a laptop, phones and a notebook
believed to hold records of customers were also found and sent for examination.
[@2] The four were investigated for alleged micro-trafficking and
micro-production of drugs. [@2]

The detainees and seized items were placed at the disposition of the Public
Prosecutor's Office, which took charge of the case. [@1] Police said the
organisation was thought to have six or seven members and did not rule out
further raids and arrests. [@2] The group allegedly exploited Machu Picchu's
tourist traffic, selling drugs to people who frequented nightclubs and
nightspots. [@2]
