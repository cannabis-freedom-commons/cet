Carabinieri arrested and jailed 22 people in Sassari's historic centre in
Operation Replay and placed 44 others under investigation for possessing drugs
for sale. [@1] The investigating judge in Sassari confirmed the custodial
precautionary measures against the 22 in the days before 19 November. [@1]
Prosecutor Dore questioned a justice collaborator before Judge Gian Paolo Piana
who corroborated the accusations against some suspects, with defence
cross-examination set for December. [@1]

During the investigation officers seized more than 2.5 kg of cocaine, another
2.5 kg of heroin and half a kilogram of marijuana. [@2] The Carabinieri
documented several thousand drug sales worth about one million euros. [@2]
Officers carried out 30 house searches in Sassari and nine other municipalities
with helicopter and dog-unit support. [@2]

Those detained were accused of possessing cocaine, heroin and marijuana for
sale, aggravated by the huge quantity and sales near schools. [@2] Prosecutors
say the group, active in Sassari Vecchia, dealt mainly in cocaine and heroin and
to a lesser extent in marijuana. [@1] The inquiry was coordinated by the Sassari
prosecution and deputies Giovanni Porcheddu and Giovanni Dore and run since 2023
by the operations section of the Sassari Carabinieri operations unit. [@2] The
investigation used video surveillance, telephone and online wiretaps,
observation and shadowing. [@2]
