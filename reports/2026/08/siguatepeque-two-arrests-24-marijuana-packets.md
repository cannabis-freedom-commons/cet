Honduran National Police officers captured two people during a drug operation in
Siguatepeque. [@1] The two people were captured at the moment of the operation
and officers linked the seized packets and items directly to them. [@1] Officers
took a motorcycle and two mobile phones from the detained people alongside the
drug packets. [@1]

Police said they seized more than 250 packets of suspected drugs in
Siguatepeque. [@1] The seizure comprised 150 packets of suspected cocaine. [@1]
Officers also reported 120 packets of suspected crack rock and 24 packets of
suspected marijuana. [@1] The two captures and the full list of packets, the
motorcycle and the phones were reported together as the result of the same
Siguatepeque operation. [@1]

The 24 marijuana packets were the cannabis component of a larger seizure
dominated by suspected cocaine and crack, which are not counted as cannabis.
[@1] The reporting force was the Policía Nacional de Honduras, the country's
National Police. [@1] Police described all of the substances as suspected drugs
and the account gives the seizure only as packets, with no weights stated. [@1]
