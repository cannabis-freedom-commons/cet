Nicaragua's National Police said it arrested 109 people linked to what it called
high-danger crimes in a security sweep across the country between 27 July and 2
August 2026. [@1] In Managua 41 were detained, including 13 for robbery with
intimidation, five for robbery with violence and 23 for robbery with force. [@1]
In the departments 68 people were arrested, including 10 linked to homicides in
León, Madriz, Río San Juan and Nueva Guinea. [@1] Commissioner Karen Obando said
15 of the department arrests were for drug trafficking and supply. [@3]

During searches and operations against organised crime and common crime,
officers seized 6 pounds (about 2.72 kg) of marijuana as evidence. [@1] Police
also seized 1 kilogram and 220 grams of cocaine and 844 grams of crack in the
same operation, quantities that are not counted here. [@3] Canal 6 reported that
the arrests took place in various municipalities and departments of the country.
[@2] Officers also took a revolver and a pistol, three digital scales, eight
mobile phones and cash. [@1]

The seized evidence and the detainees were referred to the competent
authorities. [@3] Police said they had strengthened crime prevention through
greater surveillance and motorised patrols in urban and rural areas, and said
they would keep running strategic operations to protect people, families and
communities nationwide. [@1]
