Paraguay's antidrug agents seized about 5.2 tonnes of marijuana and destroyed
five hectares of the crop during an operation in a rural zone of Capitán Bado
municipality, in the department of Amambay in the north, which borders Brazil.
[@1] The operation took place on Wednesday at a site known as Umbu in Capitán
Bado. [@1] Eight camps operating as marijuana production centres were destroyed
there, as detailed in a National Anti-Drug Secretariat statement. [@1] A boy
under 18 who was serving as a custodian was retained in one of the camps. [@1]

Officers found 5,216 kilos of packaged marijuana with distinctive logos in the
clandestine centres. [@1] Investigators believe the logos may identify a
different client or destination within the distribution chain. [@1]

Senad agents destroyed processing tools found at the site, including iron
presses, guillotines, seeders, scales and batteries. [@1] Officers also found a
motorcycle with Brazilian identification at the site. [@1] Senad is Paraguay's
Secretaría Nacional Antidrogas, the national antidrug agency that issued the
statement on the operation. [@1]
