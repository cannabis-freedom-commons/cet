Officers of the Bab Bhar judicial police arrested a man suspected of selling
drugs to young people in several districts of the capital, including Lafayette,
Bab El Khadra and La Goulette. [@1] The arrest followed surveillance and field
monitoring of the suspect's movements in those areas. [@1]

Police laid an ambush and caught the suspect red-handed while he was handing a
quantity of cannabis, locally called zatla, to one of his customers. [@1] The
report identifies zatla in this case as cannabis. [@1] Searches and raids
carried out after the arrest seized quantities of narcotics that had been
divided up and prepared for dealing. [@1] Officers also seized sums of money
alongside the drugs, according to the available information. [@1]

After consulting the public prosecution, the prosecution ordered the suspect
held in custody and an investigation report drawn up in the case. [@1] He was to
remain detained pending referral to the judiciary for further investigation and
whatever legal action was required. [@1]
