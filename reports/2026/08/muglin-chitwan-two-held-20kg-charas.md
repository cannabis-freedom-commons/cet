Chitwan police arrested two men with 20 kg of suspected charas, a cannabis resin
also called hashish, in Ichchhakamana Rural Municipality-4 in Chitwan. [@1] A
police team from the Area Police Office in Muglin stopped and searched the two
while they walked along the road at around 1:00 am on Thursday, acting on a
tip-off, the District Police Office, Chitwan, said. [@1] The arrested men were
Mohan Gole, 42, of Thaha Municipality-11 in Makwanpur, and Bhim Bahadur
Syangtan, 31, of Ramechhap. [@1] Online Khabar placed the arrest in Muglin
bazaar and gave the two men's ages as 41 and 39, both living in Thaha
Municipality-11. [@2]

Each man carried a backpack with 20 packets of material resembling charas
wrapped in yellow tape, weighing 10 kg, for 40 packets and 20 kg in total. [@1]
The two were kept at the Area Police Office in Muglin for further investigation.
[@2]

Police said the two were in custody and that further investigation was under way
under the Narcotic Drugs (Control) Act. [@1]
