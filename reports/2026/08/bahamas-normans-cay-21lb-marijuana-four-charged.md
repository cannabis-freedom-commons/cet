Mike Presume, 39, Jerry Mezidor, 35, Claude Jean, 49, and Rene Joseph, 49, were
found with 26 pounds of cocaine and 21 pounds of marijuana on a white vessel at
sea near Norman's Cay around 8 a.m. on 27 July. [@2] Each was charged with
possession of dangerous drugs with intent to supply, conspiracy to possess
dangerous drugs with intent to supply, importation of dangerous drugs and
conspiracy to import dangerous drugs. [@2] The four accused pleaded not guilty
to all charges before Senior Magistrate Raquel Whyms, with the help of a Creole
interpreter. [@2] Magistrate Whyms denied bail and remanded the men to the
Bahamas Department of Correctional Services. [@2] Their trial is scheduled to
begin on 9 December. [@2]

The interception was a joint maritime operation involving Operation Bahamas
Turks and Caicos (OPBAT) and the Royal Bahamas Defence Force. [@1] Police said
the operation took place on Monday 27 July 2026, after officers acting on
intelligence intercepted a white sailing vessel near Norman's Cay shortly after
8:00 a.m. [@1] Officers boarded the vessel and found 54 Haitian nationals: 47
adult men, six adult women and one juvenile girl. [@1] In a search conducted in
front of the occupants, officers found a backpack of suspected marijuana, a
backpack of suspected cocaine and two more packages of suspected marijuana. [@1]
Four Haitian men aged 50, 49, 39 and 35 were arrested and taken to Coral Harbour
Base, where they were handed to the Drug Enforcement Unit. [@1] The remaining 50
Haitian nationals were turned over to the Bahamas Department of Immigration for
processing. [@1] Police put the marijuana at 21 pounds with an estimated street
value of $21,000. [@1] The cocaine weighed 26 pounds, which police valued at
$520,000. [@1]

The defence force said US partners provided updated intelligence about a vessel
suspected of carrying migrants and illegal drugs near Ship Channel Cay and
Beacon Cay. [@3] The force said its vessel HMBS Maderia and park personnel
searched the area and found the boat early on Monday near Norman's Cay and Wax
Cay. [@3] The defence force account describes two bales of suspected marijuana
and 10 bricks of suspected cocaine. [@3]
