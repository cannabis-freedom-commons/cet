Officers of the Adjara main division of the Interior Ministry's central criminal
police department detained five people in Batumi on charges of illegally buying
and keeping a particularly large quantity of drugs, facilitating their sale and
illegally cultivating a drug-containing plant. [@2] One detainee is a Georgian
citizen and the other four are foreign nationals. [@3] The Interior Ministry
said the detentions took place at different times. [@1]

Searches of the suspects, the flats they were renting, a car and places they
named produced a particularly large quantity of drugs prepared for sale. [@1]
Among the drugs were more than one kilogram of marijuana, up to half a kilogram
of tetrahydrocannabinol and up to eight kilograms of cannabis plant. [@1] The
haul also included 100 grams of methadone packed in 127 packets. [@1] Police
also seized materials used to package drugs. [@1]

Investigators found that one of the accused had fitted out a special greenhouse
in a rented flat in Batumi, where he grew a particularly large quantity of
cannabis plants for sale. [@1] The case is being investigated under Articles
260, 260(4) and 265(1) of the Criminal Code, which provide for up to 20 years in
prison or life imprisonment. [@1]
