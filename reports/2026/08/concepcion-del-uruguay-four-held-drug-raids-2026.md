Four people — three men and one woman — were detained in an anti-drug operation
in Concepción del Uruguay. [@1] Those detained were accused of belonging to a
group dedicated to selling marijuana and cocaine in the city. [@1] The operation
was coordinated between the General Directorate of Dangerous Drugs of the Entre
Ríos police and the National Gendarmerie, with four simultaneous raids on
Thursday night that broke up drug-selling points. [@1] Dangerous Drugs and
Special Guards divisions from the Uruguay, Colón and Gualeguaychú departmental
headquarters took part. [@1] The proceedings were run as a suspected breach of
Provincial Law No. 10.566 on microtrafficking, under Guarantee Court No. 2 under
Judge Gustavo Díaz with Prosecutor's Office No. 5 under Eduardo Santos
intervening. [@1]

Searches in different parts of the city seized cocaine hydrochloride, marijuana
and cannabis flowers, plus cash described as exceeding 4.3 million pesos. [@1]
Officers also seized mobile phones, a vehicle, firearms, ammunition,
documentation and other items of interest to the case. [@1]

The seized phones, documentation and other items were to undergo expert
examination to deepen the investigation and establish the scope of the group
under investigation. [@1]
