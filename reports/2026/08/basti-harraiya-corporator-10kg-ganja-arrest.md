A nominated corporator of Harraiya Nagar Panchayat in Basti was arrested on
Monday for allegedly selling ganja to kanwariyas and other travellers. [@1] The
accused was identified as Shani Kumar alias Ravi, 32, a resident of Hanumangarhi
in Ward 6 of Harraiya, arrested following a tip-off received by police. [@1]
Circle officer Swarnima Singh of Harraiya said police raided his restaurant and
found him selling ganja. [@1]

During the search police recovered two packets containing 10 kg of ganja and Rs
1,200 in cash. [@1] The ganja was recovered from the accused’s restaurant near a
bus stop on the highway. [@1] During questioning, Shani told police he bought
ganja from Nepal, prepared it into small packets and sold it to kanwariyas and
passersby. [@1]

A case was registered against him under Sections 8/20 of the NDPS Act at
Harraiya police station, he was produced before a court, which sent him to
judicial custody, and police described Shani as a listed criminal. [@1] The
arrest triggered political controversy: Shani had been among the people recently
nominated by the state government as councillors of Harraiya Nagar Panchayat,
but Harraiya executive officer Shiv Pratap Singh said he had not yet taken the
oath because he had failed to submit the required documents. [@1] BJP Basti
district president Vivekanand Mishra said Shani had not taken the corporator’s
oath and the party had no connection with him. [@1]
