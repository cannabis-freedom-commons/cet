The suspect was identified as 'La Gorda', 35, an active member of the MS-13
criminal structure. [@2] The Anti-Extortion and Terrorist Associations Division
(DAET), with the Police Intelligence Directorate (DIPOL), carried out the
operation in Pespire, Choluteca, which ended in the arrest of an active Mara
Salvatrucha (MS-13) member. [@2] La Tribuna reported that he was accused of drug
trafficking after officers seized eight pounds of marijuana ready for
distribution. [@3]

At the time of his arrest he was riding a motorcycle carrying eight pounds,
about 3.63 kilograms, of marijuana. [@1] Police said he was riding the
motorcycle with the drugs for distribution and sale and had already made a
larger delivery hours earlier, according to intelligence reports. [@3] Police
presumed that a larger delivery had been made hours earlier. [@1]

DAET spokeswoman Yanisa Moradel said the detainee was considered one of the
largest drug distributors in Pespire and San Antonio de Flores in Choluteca and
San Lorenzo and Nacaome in Valle. [@3] Police described him as the main
marijuana distributor in San Lorenzo, Nacaome, Pespire and San Antonio de Flores
in southern Honduras. [@2] The operation followed surveillance and investigation
that identified drug sales points, and police said the detainee created an
atmosphere of insecurity in the area. [@2] Police said that, according to his
record, he had been arrested in 2019 with more than half a tonne of marijuana.
[@2]
