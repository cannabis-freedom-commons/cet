Brazilian customs officers stopped a Paraguayan van driver for inspection at the
Friendship Bridge customs in Foz do Iguacu during a routine morning check on 28
August 2026. [@3] The van carried Paraguayan plates and was driven by a
Paraguayan national stopped around 6 a.m. [@3]

Inside a false compartment in the van's roof, officers found marijuana tablets
totalling 185 kilograms. [@2] The driver was arrested and taken with the seized
drug to the Federal Police. [@1] The driver, the seized drug and the van were to
be handed to the Federal Police, which would continue the investigation. [@3]

The case was reported as the week's third Paraguayan detained at the same border
customs while trying to enter Brazil with drugs in a vehicle. [@1] Two days
earlier, two Paraguayans had been detained at the same customs post, with 150
kilograms of marijuana found in each of two interventions. [@1] Those two
seizures were made by the K9 team within half an hour through the work of
sniffer dog Daphine. [@1]
