Three people were apprehended in front of the Veraguas Police Zone facilities in
the district of Santiago, where authorities said they caught them in flagrante.
[@1] Those investigated held 1.12 kilograms of marijuana, according to the
Specialized Drug-Related Crimes Prosecutor's Office. [@1]

The procedure occurred on 10 August and ended with two men and one woman
convicted of aggravated possession of illicit substances. [@1] One of the men
and the woman received 84 months in prison, equivalent to seven years, while the
third defendant received 72 months, or six years. [@1]

Like the Chiriqui file, the Veraguas file advanced through a plea agreement,
unlike the Darien case which went to an oral trial. [@1] The court also ordered
confiscation of $7,596.50 found on one of the convicted people at the time of
apprehension. [@1] The Public Ministry did not detail what they were doing there
nor establish whether the seized money was directly related to a drug
transaction. [@1]
