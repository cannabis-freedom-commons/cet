National Police officers in Managua detained Luis Orlando Ramírez Flores, 23,
and Luis Antonio Altamirano Blass, 30. [@1] The operation began at 1:00 p.m. on
Friday 14 August in Barrio 30 de Mayo in District 5 of Managua. [@1] Officers
stopped a black Hyundai car with plate MT34036 carrying both men. [@2] Inside
the vehicle officers found a package containing 890 grams of marijuana. [@1]

Following the seizure, officers searched the home of Ramírez Flores in Barrio
Arnoldo Alemán in District 7 of Managua. [@1] In the house they seized an AK-47
rifle and a .38-calibre revolver, each with its ammunition. [@1] Officers also
seized 49,880 córdobas and 742 dollars in cash, along with the car used to move
the drug and three mobile phones. [@1]

Both detainees and all the recovered evidence were placed at the disposal of the
competent authorities to begin judicial proceedings. [@1] Police presented the
result as a blow against organized crime and drug micro-trafficking in Managua.
