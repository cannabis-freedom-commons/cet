Officers of the DPI and the DNPSC raided an apartment in barrio Las Lajas in San
Marcos de Colón, Choluteca, after several weeks of investigation and
intelligence work. [@1] The raid was carried out under a judicial search warrant
as part of operations against drug-trafficking structures. [@1] The operation
was conducted with the technical-legal accompaniment of the Public Ministry.
[@1] Officers detained a 40-year-old woman living at the address, who
investigators said was in charge of guarding the drugs and weapons found there.
[@1] The detained woman had rented the apartment, which police said was used to
store illicit substances. [@1]

Officers seized 60 pounds of suspected marijuana, 15 tips of suspected cocaine,
a digital scale, drug-processing implements and two mobile phones. [@1] They
also seized a prohibited-use AK-47 rifle, a Mini Uzi-type rifle and a shotgun,
which were to undergo ballistic analysis of their origin and possible use in
violent acts. [@1]

Police said the apartment served as a store of weapons, ammunition, cocaine and
marijuana for local distribution. [@1] Investigators said the suspect used the
short-term rental as a collection and processing centre for drugs. [@1]
