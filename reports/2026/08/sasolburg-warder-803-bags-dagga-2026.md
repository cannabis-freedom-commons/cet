South African Police Service officers in Sasolburg arrested a 52-year-old male
prison warder for alleged possession of and dealing in dagga. [@2] The arrest
followed a complaint received at about 14:00 on Saturday 8 August 2026 that a
warder was in possession of dagga and suspected of dealing in it at the
Sasolburg Correctional Centre. [@2] Police spokesperson Sergeant Josephine Rani
said officers were called to the correctional centre after receiving information
about a prison warder allegedly involved in dealing in dagga. [@1] On arrival
police were directed to a man wearing a Correctional Services uniform, who was
allegedly found in possession of and dealing in dagga on the premises. [@2]

Rani said police found the suspect in his Correctional Services uniform and
seized 803 bags of dagga with an estimated street value of R8,050. [@1] The
police statement described the packages seized as 803 ziplock bags of dagga.
[@2] The warder faced charges of possession of dagga and dealing in dagga and
was expected to appear before the Sasolburg Magistrate’s Court. [@2]
