National Drug Law Enforcement Agency operatives arrested 101-year-old
great-grandmother Esther Ogunmabo in Ilisan, Ogun State, on Saturday 15 August
2026. [@2] Officers found her with retail sachets of skunk, a strain of cannabis
weighing 90 grams, which she said she sells to locals. [@1] NDLEA chief
executive Mohamed Marwa directed that the suspect be granted bail and given
counselling because of her advanced age. [@1] She was released on bail. [@1]

Ogunmabo told investigators she turned to the illicit drug trade after her
provisions shop was destroyed by fire. [@1] One of her daughters, who lives in
Lagos, supplied the cannabis to her every four days, which she then sold in
small quantities. [@2] Her daughter has also been arrested. [@1]

Ogunmabo's bail means she could still be charged. [@1] The authorities described
her as 101, with no independent confirmation of her age in these reports. [@1]
