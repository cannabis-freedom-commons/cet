Police in Tashkent broke up a drug channel and detained two men, born in 1993
and 1992, in the Yakkasaray and Chilanzar districts. [@1] The operation took
place on 3 August 2026. [@1] The older of the two had previous convictions under
articles 228-1, 228-2 and 168 of the Criminal Code. [@1] One of them was
detained while distributing hashish through stash drops in the Yakkasaray
district. [@1] According to the Interior Ministry, the two had worked since 2025
as "storekeepers" for a drug shop on Telegram, distributing up to 10 kilograms
of assorted drugs a month. [@1]

A search of their Nexia-3 car found 20 grams of hashish oil. [@1] Searches of
the suspects' homes seized 2 kilograms of hashish oil, as well as marijuana,
hashish, electrical tape and other items used to distribute drugs. [@1] Under
the "Safe and Healthy Country" programme, officers took 2 kilograms 170 grams of
narcotic drugs out of circulation, about 10,700 doses. [@1] The seized hashish
oil, marijuana and hashish are counted together in that total. [@1]

A criminal case was opened under part 6 of article 273 of the Criminal Code.
