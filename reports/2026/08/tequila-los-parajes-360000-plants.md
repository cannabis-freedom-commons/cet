Jalisco state police found and destroyed a plantation of about 360,000 marijuana
plants in the municipality of Tequila. [@1] The site was a terrain known as Los
Parajes, near the El Salvador delegation, found during patrols of mountain areas
and dirt tracks. [@1] Regional police spotted the plants during a preventive
patrol and got out of their vehicles to inspect the ground. [@2]

The state police described the crop as a plantation of about 360,000 apparent
marijuana plants. [@2] According to the Secretariat of Security, the crop spread
over roughly 300 metres wide by 150 metres long. [@1] The plants reached up to
2.5 metres in height. [@2] Another account gives the plot's area as 45,000
square metres, or 4.5 hectares. [@3]

After confirming the site, police notified a federal public prosecutor, who
ordered the area photographed before, during and after the destruction. [@1]
State officers asked the federal prosecutor to take charge, and the photographic
record covered the incineration and destruction at the site. [@3] The
authorities said they maintain operations in rural areas to detect and combat
illicit crops. [@1]
