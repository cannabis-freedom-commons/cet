Police in Saint-Pierre, Réunion, arrested one man on suspicion of possessing and
trafficking narcotics. [@1] Officers found an indoor cannabis grow in an
apartment while inspecting the common areas of a building. [@3] The operation
was carried out across the Saint-Pierre district on Thursday 20 August 2026.
[@4]

Officers seized 7 kg of cannabis herb and 7,025 euros in cash. [@2] The cannabis
is called zamal locally, and the grow was set up inside the flat. [@5] Six air
pistols, a pellet rifle, hunting ammunition and various valuables were seized as
well. [@4]

Forty police officers took part, backed by the motorcycle and canine brigades,
the border police, Saint-Pierre municipal police, Transdev and the DEETS
directorate. [@2] The day's checks also targeted drug trafficking, road
offences, undeclared work, irregular immigration and carrying weapons. [@5]
