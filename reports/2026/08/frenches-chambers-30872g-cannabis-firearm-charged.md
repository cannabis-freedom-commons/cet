Deryck Chambers, a 41-year-old farmer of Frenches, had his home searched under a
warrant on Saturday 1 August 2026 by tactical officers of the police. [@2]
Officers recovered one firearm, eleven rounds of .38 ammunition, five rounds of
.32 ammunition and 30,872 grammes (30.872 kilograms) of cannabis. [@2]

Chambers was arrested and charged with possession of a firearm without a
licence, two counts of possession of ammunition without a licence, possession of
a controlled drug and drug trafficking. [@2] He appeared before the Serious
Offences Court on Tuesday 4 August 2026 and pleaded not guilty to all charges.
[@1] He was granted bail of EC$15,000 with conditions requiring him to report to
the Central Police Station every Monday and Thursday, surrender his travel
documents and accept stop notices at all ports of entry and exit. [@1] The
matter was adjourned to 24 August 2026. [@2]

The Royal Saint Vincent and the Grenadines Police Force commended the tactical
officers, saying their “vigilance, discipline and operational focus” had removed
another illegal firearm from the streets and calling the seizure a “concrete
gain” for community safety. [@1] The Vincentian reported the drug charge as
possession of 30,872 grams of marijuana with intent to supply. [@3]
