Officers of Venezuela's Bolivarian National Police seized 120 panela bricks of
suspected marijuana hidden in a wooded area of the La Macoya sector, Falcón
state. [@2] The bricks were distributed in four sacks. [@1] The procedure took
place in the La Macoya sector of Los Taques municipality, also called Santa Cruz
de Los Taques. [@1]

The PNB's anti-drug division made the find during field patrols, after a police
dog marked the vegetation and led officers to the sacks. [@2] Últimas Noticias,
citing the National Anti-Drug Superintendency (Sunad) on Instagram, put the
weight at 50 kilograms. [@1] El Paraguanero reported an approximate gross weight
of 60 kilograms for the sacks. [@2] The information was confirmed by Falcón
citizen security secretary Miguel Morales Miranda and ZODI-12 commander Héctor
Echeverría Osorio. [@2] The officers had been patrolling the area after getting
intelligence about illicit drug trafficking. [@1]

Both authorities said security forces were keeping a search cordon around the
area to protect Falcón state from drug-trafficking networks. [@2]
