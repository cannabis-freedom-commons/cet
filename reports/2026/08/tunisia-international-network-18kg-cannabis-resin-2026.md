Units of the drug sub-directorate of Tunisia's judicial police dismantled an
international network trafficking drugs between Tunisia and a neighbouring
country. [@1] The operation took place on Friday 21 August 2026. [@2]

Officers seized 180 slabs of cannabis resin with a total weight of 18 kg. [@4]
The same operation seized 8 kg of raw cocaine, which is not counted in the
cannabis figures. [@1] Réalités described the cocaine as raw and of high purity.
[@4]

La Presse de Tunisie reported that the operation followed extensive intelligence
work. [@3] Investigations were continuing to identify every member of the
network and its branches. [@1]
