A coalition led by the Joint Interagency Task Force South (JIATF-South)
intercepted a 35-foot go-fast boat in the Eastern Pacific on Wednesday 19 August
2026. [@1] The boat was detected 114 nautical miles south of Punta Burica, on
the Panama–Costa Rica border, travelling from Colombia to Costa Rica. [@1] Three
suspects were detained in the interception. [@1]

A U.S. Southern Command maritime patrol aircraft located the boat and maintained
continuous aerial surveillance until surface forces arrived. [@1] Panama's
National Aeronaval Service deployed interceptor boats and, in JIATF-South's
words, pursued the vessel and brought it to a complete stop. [@1] The load
comprised 619 pounds (about 280.77 kg) of marijuana and 2,428 kilograms of
cocaine. [@1] The task force said in a 19 August post on X that it worked with
Homeland Security Investigations, Panama and Colombia to disrupt a “massive
flow” of narcotics. [@3]

JIATF-South, U.S. Homeland Security Investigations (HSI) and authorities from
Colombia and Panama took part. [@1] The operation fell under the American
Coalition Against the Cartels, an 18-nation initiative coordinated with
SOUTHCOM. [@2] The corridor south of Punta Burica is one of the most active
drug-trafficking routes in the Pacific, with shipments leaving Colombia's
Buenaventura and Valle del Cauca for Central America and the United States. [@1]
JIATF-South said the work supported operations to dismantle narcoterrorist
networks in the Western Hemisphere. [@1]
