Azerbaijan's State Border Service detained an Iranian citizen born in 1975 at
the border with Iran as he tried to smuggle narcotics into the country. [@1] The
border service said the detention came during operational-military measures in
the area controlled by its Horadiz border detachment of the Border Troops. [@1]

Officers seized 7 kilograms 490 grams of marijuana from the man. [@1] He was
also carrying 1,810 tablets of methadone M-40 containing psychotropic
substances, which are not counted in the cannabis figures. [@1] The
Azerbaijani-language account, citing the border service, gives the same
quantities: 7 kilograms 490 grams of marijuana and 1,810 methadone M-40 tablets.
[@2]

Operational and investigative measures into the case were continuing, the border
service said. [@1] Naxçıvan Xəbərləri also carried the border service statement,
reporting the same detention in the Horadiz border detachment area and the same
7 kilograms 490 grams of marijuana. [@3]
