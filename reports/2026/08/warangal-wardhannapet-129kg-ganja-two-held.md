Warangal Narcotics Police Station (EAGLE Force) officials seized 129.334 kg of
ganja, valued at Rs 64.66 lakh, and arrested two persons during a vehicle check
at Wardhannapet on Sunday. [@2] The arrested persons were identified as Kerala
natives Sayuj C.S., 33, and Besalel C. Mathew, 38, who were reportedly working
as drivers. [@2] The two men were from Kannur and Pathanamthitta in Kerala. [@4]
Both the accused were booked in ganja cases in Kerala and Karnataka in the past.
[@3]

Police intercepted a car travelling from Khammam towards Warangal at around 5.50
pm and found 61 packets of ganja concealed in the rear portion of the vehicle.
[@2] Police also seized the car, two mobile phones and Rs 2,900 in cash. [@2] In
August, officers conducted checks in the Vardhannapet surrounding areas, seized
129 kilos and arrested two persons. [@1]

Preliminary investigation revealed that the accused had allegedly procured ganja
from the Chitrakonda and Darakonda areas of Odisha and were transporting it
through Rajamahendravaram and Warangal to Bengaluru. [@2] Preliminary
investigation revealed that Vicky of Odisha supplied the ganja, while Ajith
coordinated the transportation. [@3] Two other accused, identified as Ajith and
Vicky, are absconding. [@2] A case has been registered under relevant provisions
of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act. [@2] The arrested persons
were being produced before a court for remand. [@2]
