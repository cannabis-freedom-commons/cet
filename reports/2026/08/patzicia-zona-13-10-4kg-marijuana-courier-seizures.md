Guatemala's National Civil Police said its anti-narcotics unit, the Subdirección
General de Análisis de Información Antinarcótica (SGAIA), seized 10.4 kilograms
of marijuana in two operations on Friday 14 August 2026. [@1,3] At an
interinstitutional checkpoint in Patzicía, Chimaltenango, agents searched a
courier vehicle at kilometre 75.4 of the CA-1 Occidente and found a cardboard
box with 30 nylon bags of marijuana. [@1,3] Each bag weighed about eight ounces,
an estimated 15 pounds or about 6.8 kilograms. [@1,3]

The second seizure came at the imports area of a courier warehouse in zona 13 of
Guatemala City, where a K-9 team found 3.6 kilograms of marijuana. [@2,3]
According to the police, that consignment had come from the United States. [@2,3]

The National Civil Police said it would continue investigating to establish
responsibilities and the final destination of the shipments. [@1] The force
described the two seizures as part of permanent controls meant to intercept
drugs entering and moving through the country. [@2]
