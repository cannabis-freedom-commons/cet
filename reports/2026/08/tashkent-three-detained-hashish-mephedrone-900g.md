Police detained three people in Tashkent and Tashkent region on suspicion of
drug trafficking, seizing about 900 grams of mephedrone and hashish. [@1] The
Interior Ministry reported the detentions. [@1] Officers stopped a BYD car
driven by a 35-year-old man previously convicted of a drug crime in Tashkent
district. [@1] Two other suspects, men aged 34 and 36, one of them previously
convicted of drug trafficking, were detained in Shaykhantahur district in
Tashkent. [@1]

Officers found 200 grams of mephedrone inside the car and another 300 grams of
the same substance at his home. [@1] Officers found 400 grams of mephedrone and
hashish when they inspected the second pair's car. [@1] According to the
ministry, two of those detained had distributed synthetic drugs through a
Telegram shop for a year. [@1] The ministry did not specify how much of the 400
grams was mephedrone and how much was hashish. [@1] Investigators opened a
criminal case against the suspects under point "а" of part six of Article 273 of
the Criminal Code. [@1] The provision covers drug trafficking on an especially
large scale. [@1]
