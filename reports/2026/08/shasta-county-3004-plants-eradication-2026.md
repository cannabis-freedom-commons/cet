The Shasta County Sheriff's Office Marijuana Eradication Team and several allied
agencies carried out a four-day eradication operation against illegal marijuana
grows across Shasta County. [@2] Officers served 12 search warrants at illegal
marijuana grow sites throughout the county. [@1] The warrants were served in
Happy Valley and other parts of the county. [@3] Two people were arrested in
connection with felony weapons charges. [@1]

Officials said the operation eradicated 3,004 marijuana plants and seized 459
pounds (about 208.2 kilograms) of processed marijuana. [@1] A sheriff's handout
showed marijuana trim and stems recovered during the same operation. [@2]
Investigators discovered nine firearms, eight illegal and one stolen. [@1] The
Anderson Police Department, the Shasta Interagency Narcotics Task Force (SINTF)
and the Shasta County District Attorney's Office partnered with the sheriff's
office. [@2]

The sheriff's office described the targeted gardens as unpermitted. [@3] The
sheriff's office said illegal grows harm people growing legally, damage the
environment and attract a criminal element to the county. [@2]
