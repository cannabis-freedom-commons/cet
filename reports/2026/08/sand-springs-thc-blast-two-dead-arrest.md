Ryan Bassham surrendered to authorities after the Tulsa County District
Attorney's Office filed an arrest warrant for him. [@2,4,6] He was booked into the
Tulsa County jail after surrendering to sheriff's deputies. [@3,5] He bonded out
on two counts of felony murder in the first degree in the commission of a crime.
[@2,6] His bail totaled $2 million. [@2,4,6] Employee Stephen Scott died at the scene.
[@3] Michael Newman was critically injured in the explosion and later died from
his injuries. [@3] Newman told a paramedic on the way to the hospital that the area had not been well ventilated and that the explosion happened after he unplugged something. [@6]

The case was listed as an arrest following an explosion at a medical marijuana
extraction business. [@1,6] The explosion happened in the early hours of Aug. 27
at a marijuana extraction business that may have used burnable gases such as
butane or propane to extract THC. [@2] Bassham had taken over the business,
called Solos Extracts LLC, earlier this year. [@3,4] A fire marshal who inspected the facility in January found an outdated fire suppression system and equipment commonly used in marijuana manufacturing, according to court records. [@6]

The city's fire marshal had instructed the business to halt work until
discrepancies were fixed, which it had not done. [@2] After the explosion the marshal found large amounts of liquid propane and other hazardous material that were not there in January, according to court records. [@6] Investigators said Bassham
never applied for a new OBN registration under his name as required by law. [@3] A fire marshal told authorities the business was seeking an updated marijuana license and narcotics registration, according to court records. [@6]
State investigators said they believed he was therefore illegally manufacturing
a controlled and dangerous substance. [@3,6]

No one else was harmed in the explosion, but multiple nearby businesses were damaged. [@4,6]
