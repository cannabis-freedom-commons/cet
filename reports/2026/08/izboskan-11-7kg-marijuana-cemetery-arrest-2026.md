Andijan region's police press service said officers carried out a special
operation in Izboskan district. [@2] They detained a man born in 1989 next to
one of the district's cemeteries. [@2] Officers said he was held at the moment
he was selling 11.72 kilograms of marijuana for 6 million sum. [@2]

A check of his mobile phone showed he had also traded drugs through the Telegram
messenger, sending circle video messages of his stock to prospective buyers.
[@2] Podrobno.uz reported that he had also looked for buyers on social media,
sending them videos of the goods. [@1] Darakchi.uz said the sale was stopped
within Operation Black Poppy 2026, a nationwide operational and preventive
drive. [@3]

Darakchi.uz said the operation was conducted by officers of the
operational-investigative service of the Andijan regional police directorate.
[@3] A criminal case was opened under part 6 of Article 273 of Uzbekistan's
Criminal Code, which covers large-scale illegal drug trafficking. [@1] That
offence carries 15 to 20 years in prison, and the investigation is continuing.
[@1] Darakchi.uz described the stage as a pre-investigation check, with police
still establishing the circumstances and anyone else involved. [@3]
