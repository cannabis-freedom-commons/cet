Security officials in Ghor and Badghis provinces said nine people were arrested
within the previous 24 hours on suspicion of drug trafficking, processing and
sale. [@1] Badghis police said three people were arrested in Jowand district and
two in Dara-i-Bum district on suspicion of collecting materials used in drug
processing and trading hashish. [@1]

The two kilograms of hashish were being sold by traffickers. [@2] Officers
seized 2 kg of hashish along with 1,000 kg of soud, 50 kg of axel and 160 kg of
powder used in narcotics production during the Badghis operations. [@1] The
Badghis cases were referred to judicial and legal authorities for further
investigation. [@1]

In Ghor, four people were arrested on suspicion of trafficking 110 kg of a
narcotic substance known as 'Eif'. [@1] That material was concealed inside four
motorcycles and seized in Mullah Hasar village of Tolak district. [@1] Police
said it was being transported to an undisclosed destination. [@1]
