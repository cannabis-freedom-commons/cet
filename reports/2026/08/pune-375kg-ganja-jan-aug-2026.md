Pune, considered a major centre for education, information technology and young
people, is facing a growing challenge from illegal drug trafficking. [@1] Police
are carrying out continuous raids to stop the sale and trafficking of substances
including ganja, mephedrone (MD), charas and cocaine. [@1] Between January and
August 2026, Pune police registered 504 drug-related cases in just eight months.
[@1]

During the same period the names of 565 suspects came to light. [@1] Police
action seized more than 375 kg of ganja, along with more than five and a half kg
of mephedrone (MD) and about 14 kg of other narcotics. [@1]

Police figures put NDPS Act cases at 111 in 2024, rising to 215 in 2025. [@1] In
those 2025 cases 319 accused were arrested and drugs worth about 8.64 crore
rupees were seized. [@1] Police say they now want to move beyond small sellers
and local smugglers to reach the larger suppliers and ringleaders behind them.
[@1]
