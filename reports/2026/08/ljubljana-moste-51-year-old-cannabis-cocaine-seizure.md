Ljubljana Moste police concluded an extensive criminal investigation into
violent offences, banned drugs, weapons and forged documents. [@1] The
investigation began with a report of violence and threats and continued with
information-gathering and interviews by Moste officers. [@1] After a proposal to
the district prosecution and a court order, officers searched two addresses
together with Ljubljana Center officers and a drug-detection dog handler. [@1]

The searches uncovered a military semi-automatic sniper rifle, a pistol with
removed markings, ammunition and a forged public document. [@1] Officers also
seized almost two kilograms of cocaine and more than one kilogram of cannabis.
[@1] They also seized a vacuum sealer, a precision digital scale and other tools
for packing and preparing drugs for onward sale. [@1] Police said those items,
together with the other findings, pointed to suspected drug resale. [@1]

The 51-year-old Slovenian suspect was detained and, after the investigation,
brought with a criminal complaint to the duty investigating judge, who ordered
remand. [@1]

A second report gives the same seizure figures of almost two kilograms of
cocaine and more than one kilogram of cannabis. [@2]

The public broadcaster likewise reported that the 51-year-old suspect was
detained and brought before the investigating judge, who ordered remand. [@3]
