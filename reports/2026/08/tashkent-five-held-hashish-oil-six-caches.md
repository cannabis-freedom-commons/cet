Police in Tashkent detained five people, including a minor, in an operation
against a transnational criminal network. [@1] The network was suspected of
smuggling drugs from Russia into Uzbekistan and distributing them. [@1] The
pupil, aged 17, was detained in Chilanzar district, where investigators say he
worked as the storekeeper of an online drug shop. [@1] Officers found 180 grams
of alpha-PVP inside his chainsaw. [@1] Two more suspects, who acted as couriers
placing drugs in hiding places, were detained as they collected hidden drugs.
[@1] Before their arrest the two had made six hiding places containing hashish
oil. [@1]

A 34-year-old man who also worked as a storekeeper was detained later. [@1] A
27-year-old man was detained in Yunusabad district as he collected 500 grams of
alpha-PVP at an address given by the group's handler. [@1] The operation was
carried out by the interior ministry together with the State Security Service
and the customs authorities. [@3] Officers watched a suspicious parcel sent from
Russia to Tashkent, and the ministry said the operation used a controlled
delivery to document the crime. [@1] The interior ministry put the alpha-PVP
seized at more than 1.2 kg and said hashish oil was seized as well. [@2] Police
said they located the hiding places and seized the drugs left in them. [@3]

A criminal case was opened under part 6(a) of article 273 of Uzbekistan's
Criminal Code, which covers illegal actions with narcotic drugs and psychotropic
substances for sale. [@1] All five were placed in pre-trial detention. [@1]
Police said they were still identifying other people who may be involved in the
drug trade. [@1]
