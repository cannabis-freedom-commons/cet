DNPA officers, with the DNPSC, located and inspected a car near the Satuyé
checkpoint in La Ceiba. [@2] The driver was detained and the investigative
process continued. [@1] The detainee was described as a 24-year-old man who
would be referred to the competent authorities to continue the legal process.
[@3]

Officers found 44 double packages of marijuana weighing about 88 pounds (about
39.9 kg). [@2] The marijuana was hidden in a false compartment known as a caleta
built into the vehicle, which was also seized as evidence. [@3]

Preliminary investigations linked the detainee to the criminal structure known
as Los Maradiaga, allegedly involved in drug transport and distribution in the
Atlantic region. [@2] He was to be referred to the competent authorities as
allegedly responsible for aggravated drug trafficking. [@4] Further proceedings
continued under coordination with the FESCCO in La Ceiba to follow the procedure
under the law. [@3]
