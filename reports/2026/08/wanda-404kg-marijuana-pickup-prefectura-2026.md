One man was detained and a pickup truck retained when Prefectura Naval
Argentina, Argentina's coast guard, ended an operation near the Paraná River in
Misiones. [@1] The vehicle was intercepted at the access to Colonia Wanda as it
headed toward the town centre. [@1] Inside were 13 large bags wrapped and
secured with packing tape. [@1]

Field tests confirmed the packages held cannabis sativa, split into 535 panes,
or pressed bricks, weighing more than 404 kilograms. [@1] The force put the
market value at 1.496 billion pesos. [@2] By judicial order the driver was
detained, the drug seized and the pickup retained. [@2]

The operation began with intelligence work that took officers to Puerto
Carolina, at kilometre 1,865 of the Paraná River, on the Paraguay border. [@3]
Using a thermal camera, officers watched a motorboat cross the river and unload
large packages on the Argentine bank before returning to Paraguay. [@2] Officers
then deployed to find those who had received the cargo. [@1] The case went to
the Federal Court of Puerto Iguazú under judge Marcelo Alejandro Cardozo, with
the local Federal Prosecutor's Office led by Marcelo José Bernachea. [@1]
