Drug squad officers searched the home of a 35-year-old man in Nicosia district
on a Sunday under a court warrant. [@1] Officers found nine cannabis plants
growing inside two specially adapted tents in the home. [@1] Some plants had
reached 1.9 metres, while the smallest measured 1.15 metres. [@1]

A bedroom in the home had allegedly been turned into a carefully controlled
cannabis farm with indoor greenhouses, irrigation pipes and equipment regulating
temperature and humidity. [@1] Police also seized approximately 1.6 kilograms of
cannabis. [@1] Officers also seized about 90 grams of white powder believed to
be cocaine, which still needs examination to confirm what it is. [@1] A
precision scale was also found, along with machinery controlling growing
conditions, watering pipes and other equipment allegedly connected to
cultivation. [@1]

The 35-year-old was arrested at the scene. [@1] He was expected to appear before
Nicosia District Court on Monday, where police would ask for continued custody
while the investigation continues. [@1] The Morphou unit of the Cyprus Drug Law
Enforcement Unit is investigating the case. [@1]
