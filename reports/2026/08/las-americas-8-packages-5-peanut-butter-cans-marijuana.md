Agents of the National Drug Control Directorate, working with the Public
Ministry, seized eight marijuana packages and five cans full of marijuana during
operations at Las Américas International Airport in Boca Chica, Santo Domingo
province. [@1] A DNCD canine unit alerted officers to controlled substances
inside several boxes during supervision and control work in one of the terminal
warehouses. [@1] On the prosecutor's instructions, officers applied a stricter
inspection protocol and found the eight packages in a cardboard box,
vacuum-packed in black and transparent bags. [@1]

The eight packages had a preliminary weight of nine pounds, about 4.08
kilograms. [@1] Investigators said the box with the eight packages was sent by a
man living on Ridgley Drive in Los Angeles, United States, to a person living on
Salomé Ureña street in Boca Chica. [@1] In a second intervention, officers found
another box with five cans that looked like powdered peanut butter but were full
of marijuana. [@1]

The shipping manifest said that second box was in transit through the country
and addressed to a man living in Northwest, Florida, United States. [@1]
Authorities opened an investigation into both cases to establish criminal
responsibility and any connection between senders and recipients. [@1]
