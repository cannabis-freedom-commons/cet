Two cousins from Mayotte and the Comoros were convicted of drug trafficking in
the Angoulême area. [@1] They had been arrested in Angoulême after a police
check and were tried on Monday 10 August 2026. [@2] The case began with a patrol
in the late afternoon on boulevard de la République, near place Victor-Hugo in
Angoulême. [@1] On 7 August 2026 officers saw several people smoking cannabis at
the foot of a building, among them one of the two cousins. [@2]

In the bag of the 23-year-old they found about 30 grams of hashish and cocaine.
[@2] In the flat where his cousin housed him, officers found about 2,000 euros
in cash and a set of accounts. [@2]

The 23-year-old had left prison in July 2025 and admitted in police custody that
he sold drugs. [@2] His cousin, a former soldier, denied taking part in the
trafficking. [@2] He said he had supplied cannabis to friends for money. [@2]
Investigators found 122 transfers into the former soldier's account. [@2] In
all, 8,200 euros was paid from a bank account held in Paraguay. [@2]

The court sentenced them to two years' imprisonment and 18 months suspended for
participating in a drug-trafficking operation. [@2]
