Officers from Costa Rica's Drug Control Police (PCD), the Criminal Intelligence
and Analysis Directorate (DIAC) and the Public Force dismantled a hydroponic
marijuana laboratory in a home in El Cacao, Tuetal Norte, Alajuela, on 19 August
2026. [@1] The operation began after a complaint about suspicious activity at a
property in Tuetal Norte. [@1] Neighbours had reported that marijuana was being
produced in the house. [@3] The raid took place on Wednesday 19 August. [@2]

Officers seized 321 marijuana plants at different stages of growth and 3,080
grams of processed marijuana ready for possible sale. [@2] Among the equipment
seized were automated irrigation systems and devices to regulate temperature and
humidity. [@1] The house contained a laboratory fully equipped with lighting,
ventilation and fertilisers for hydroponic production, a method that grows
plants in nutrient-rich water without soil. [@1] Hydroponic cultivation develops
plants using nutrient-rich water solutions instead of agricultural soil. [@1]

Authorities said they kept the investigation open to identify those responsible
and establish links to possible distribution networks. [@1] The authorities were
coordinating with the OIJ to gather further evidence in the case. [@3] Costa
Rican law forbids the cultivation, production and trafficking of marijuana, with
sentences of eight to 15 years. [@1]
