Agents of the National Drug Control Directorate (DNCD), with the Public Ministry
and the Specialized Airport Security Corps (CESAC), seized six packages of
marijuana at Las Américas International Airport (AILA-JFPG) in Santo Domingo.
[@1] The DNCD said one man was arrested during the operation after going to
collect the box sent from Miami. [@3] The shipping manifest named that man as
the recipient; he was arrested leaving a shopping plaza in Distrito Nacional
just as he went to collect the box, and officers took other packages of the same
substance from him. [@1] The detainee remained in the custody of the Public
Ministry while judicial proceedings advanced. [@1]

The find was made in a cargo terminal, where anti-drug agents supported by
Customs inspectors detected suspicious images inside a cardboard box from Miami,
United States. [@1] The seizure took place in one of the airport's cargo
terminals as part of reinforced intelligence and interdiction work against drug
trafficking. [@2] Inside the box a plastic tube was hidden in a portaplanos, a
cardboard tube for carrying posters, containing the six packages wrapped in
transparent and black plastic, weighing about seven pounds (3.18 kilograms).
[@1] After the routine inspection raised suspicion, a dog handler passed a dog
over the box and it alerted to controlled substances; the box was then opened on
the acting prosecutor's orders and the drug confirmed. [@1]

The DNCD announced on 26 August that the six concealed packages had been found
during inspection of a cargo terminal at Las Américas airport. [@3] DNCD
spokesman Joel López said the action was part of intensified controls against
organised crime using air and port routes to traffic illicit substances. [@1]
Authorities kept the investigation open to identify and locate others involved
in the failed shipment of marijuana from the United States to the Dominican
Republic. [@1]
