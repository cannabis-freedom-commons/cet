Excise officers found a cannabis plant about ten feet (three metres) tall at the
tourism project site at Venjaramoodu. [@1] Vamanapuram range excise officers
made the find in scrub beside the rock at Madapuram Thampuran Thamburatipara, an
adventure tourism site in Manikkal panchayat. [@1]

Few visitors come to the tourism project because the area is hard to reach, but
residents complain that people using drugs and anti-social elements treat the
spot as a safe hideout. [@1] Police believe the seed had sprouted from cannabis
used at the spot. [@1]

Excise officers reported the find to senior officials and then destroyed the
plant. [@1]
