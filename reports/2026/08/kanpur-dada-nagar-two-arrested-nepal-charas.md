Fazalganj police and a surveillance team arrested two men after recovering a
large quantity of charas from a white Alto car in Kanpur. [@1] The arrested men
were identified as Asmit Gautam, a resident of Yashoda Nagar, and Ayush Tiwari,
a resident of Lal Bangla. [@1] The two were intercepted during a checking
operation near Dada Nagar Dhala, and both occupants were taken into custody.
[@1]

Police said the charas had allegedly been smuggled into Kanpur from Nepal. [@1]
Police put the estimated value of the seized narcotics at around ₹15 crore. [@1]
According to police, the seized narcotics were allegedly meant to be supplied to
several cities in Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra and other states. [@1]

During questioning, the suspects allegedly said charas brought from Nepal was
transported by train and then distributed to other parts of the country. [@1]
Police named Bhopal, Indore, Jabalpur, Raipur, JP Nagar, Nagpur, Mumbai, Nashik,
Valsad and Pune among cities linked to the supply network. [@1] Investigators
were examining whether Asmit and Ayush were directly involved in trafficking or
working as couriers for a larger syndicate. [@1] Police were also examining the
suspects' mobile phones and other digital evidence to identify additional
associates. [@1]
