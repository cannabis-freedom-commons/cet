Nine men were detained in Grecia, Alajuela, on Tuesday 18 August 2026 during 11
raids by the Judicial Investigation Organism (OIJ) on a suspected drug-retailing
network. [@1] The detainees were listed as Solís, 53; Valverde, 25; Alfaro, 26;
Murillo, 36; Picado, 26; Fonseca, 25; Baquedano, 18; Valverde, 23 and Montero,
35. [@1] All nine were treated as suspects of infringing the Psychotropic
Substances Law through retail drug sales. [@1] They were placed at the disposal
of the Public Prosecutor's Office, which will set their legal measures. [@1]

The operation began around 4 a.m. and covered homes in Grecia Centro, Tacares,
San Juan, San Roque, calle El Achiote, Santa Gertrudis Sur, La Arena and Rincón
de Arias. [@1] The 11 raids formed part of Plan Escudo, a strategy run for two
years by the Public Prosecutor's Office and the judicial police against retail
drug sales. [@3] Investigators said the substances on sale included marijuana,
crack and cocaine. [@1] Buyers also phoned in orders and the suspects drove to
different points to make express deliveries. [@2] The raids yielded cash, mobile
phones, marijuana and cocaine. [@1] One home contained a section apparently
fitted out to make, prepare and package the drugs for sale. [@1]

The investigation had begun about a year earlier. [@1] The OIJ said the case
began in 2025 after anonymous alerts about clandestine sales. [@4] La Nación
reported that a prosecutor spoke of 13 detentions, while nine had been
transferred to the Public Prosecutor's Office by midday. [@3] El Observador
counted 14 detentions across Grecia, Santa Cruz and Puriscal, nine of them in
Grecia. [@5]
