Police in Bijelo Polje arrested M.R., 21, on suspicion of selling marijuana to
A.S., three years younger and from the same town, who was also arrested. [@1]
The Higher State Prosecutor's Office in Bijelo Polje ordered M.R.'s arrest on
suspicion of unauthorised production, possession and distribution of narcotic
drugs. [@1] A.S. was arrested for a violation of the Law on Preventing Drug
Abuse. [@1]

Officers from the Kolašin and Bijelo Polje security departments found the
marijuana on A.S. during a check on a train on the Bijelo Polje - Kolašin route.
[@1] The Police Directorate's statement gives A.S.'s age as 18. [@2] A.S. was
taken to the Kolašin Security Department's premises, and further police work
identified M.R. as the person suspected of selling him the drug. [@1] The Police
Directorate described M.R. as an operationally interesting person. [@3]

The Police Directorate said the arrests came in increased activity by its
Northern Regional Security Centre. [@2]
