Kenton Roker Morley, 27, a newly recruited corrections officer, was found in
possession of drugs concealed in his shoes when he reported for work at the
Nassau prison. [@3] He was arrested on 12 August 2026 after police found four
ounces of marijuana stashed in his boots. [@2] The inspection took place at the
Bahamas Department of Correctional Services shortly after 2 p.m., where
officials detected abnormalities in his boots. [@1] A search found four plastic
wraps, two in each boot, each containing suspected hydroponic marijuana. [@1]
Drug Enforcement Unit officers, acting on information received, went to BDOCS
and arrested and cautioned him. [@1] Police estimated the weight at four ounces
(about 0.11 kg) and the street value at $600. [@1]

Morley pleaded guilty to possession of drugs with intent to supply at his
arraignment before Assistant Chief Magistrate Kara Turnquest. [@2] He had
pleaded guilty to trying to smuggle marijuana into the prison. [@2] He was fined
$1,000, with the option to serve a three-month sentence if the fine was not
paid. [@2] Bahamas Press reported that a deeper search of his vehicle uncovered
more drugs and several cell phones. [@3]

The officer was assisting police with the ongoing investigation. [@1]
