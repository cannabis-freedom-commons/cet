Syria's Drug Enforcement Department foiled an attempt to smuggle a large
shipment of narcotics into Jordan using balloons in the eastern countryside of
Sweida province. [@2] Syrian authorities intercepted the attempt on Wednesday
after traffickers allegedly used balloons to transport narcotics from Suwayda
province toward neighboring Jordan. [@1] A SANA photograph of an officer
inspecting the seized narcotics was released on 12 August 2026. [@1]

The department said officers seized 70 kilograms of hashish and 45,000 captagon
pills, along with equipment and tools used in the smuggling. [@2] North Press,
citing the administration's website, said the operation seized 70 kilograms of
hashish, 45,000 Captagon pills and several smuggling tools and devices. [@3] The
administration said its forces thwarted an attempt to smuggle the shipment to
Jordan using balloons in the eastern Suwayda countryside. [@3] Images published
by SANA showed what appeared to be small balloons, apparently filled and laid
out flat in rows. [@1] SANA quoted the drug enforcement department as saying it
thwarted an attempt to smuggle a large shipment of narcotic substances to Jordan
using balloons, and that 70 kilograms of hashish and 45,000 captagon tablets
were seized. [@1]

Parts of Druze-majority Suwayda province, which borders Jordan, are controlled
by Druze armed groups and are outside Damascus's control. [@1] Captagon, a
synthetic stimulant, became Syria's largest export after the civil war began in
2011 and a source of illicit funding for the former government. [@1] Syria's new
authorities, who took power in December 2024, have been working to combat the
captagon trade, and Damascus and Amman have committed to fighting drug smuggling
on their border. [@1]
