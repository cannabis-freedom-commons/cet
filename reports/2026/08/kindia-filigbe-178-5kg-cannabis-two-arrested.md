Gendarmes in Kindia seized 178.5 kilograms of Indian hemp, the name used in
Guinea for cannabis, in the Filigbé neighbourhood on 10 August 2026. [@1] The
operation followed information that a group of people was carrying a large
quantity of drugs in the area. [@1] A team from the Kindia mobile gendarmerie
intervention squadron recovered the drugs and also seized a Mazda car registered
RC 2101 AL. [@1] Officers arrested two men, named as Mamadou Djouma Diallo and
Ibrahima Sory Baldé. [@1]

Baldé said he was not a drug seller and had agreed to keep a parcel without
knowing what it contained. [@1] He said the gendarmes came to his home about two
hours later, searched it and seized the parcel. [@1] Diallo, described as the
driver of the car, said the goods had been loaded into his vehicle without his
knowledge or against his will. [@2] The gendarmerie said the interrogations had
established that the two men took part in transporting and holding the seized
drugs. [@1] Captain Mamadi Konaté, deputy commander of the Kindia intervention
squadron, said the operation was part of the authorities' campaign against drug
trafficking and organised crime. [@2]

The authorities named Alpha Oumar Baldé, known as Bengué, as the suspected
organiser and said he was on the run. [@1] The two suspects and the seized items
were presented at the Kindia gendarmerie on 13 August 2026. [@2] They were
handed to the Kindia investigation brigade at 19:15 that day for further
inquiries. [@2] Guinéenews reported that the two suspects were presented to the
prosecutor at the Kindia court of first instance that day. [@3]
