Police and gendarmerie units announced a run of raids in July and August 2026, breaking up at least ten networks and arresting dozens of people. The largest seizures were 202 kg of cannabis resin hidden in food shipments, 230 kg in a truck trailer at Bassiknou and 210 kg at Nouadhibou, alongside smaller amounts and thousands of psychotropic tablets. In another operation in Nouakchott, gendarmerie units seized a quantity of transformed cannabis resin and 3,316 tablets of psychotropic substances. [@3] At Atar, gendarmerie units broke up two networks and arrested thirteen people, seizing transformed cannabis resin, including pierre noire, and cannabis. [@3] A separate police operation arrested eleven suspects, Mauritanians and foreigners, and seized 9 kilograms of cannabis resin. [@3] In the Nouadhibou police case, seven people were arrested, and officers also seized eight boats used for trafficking and a Toyota Hilux. [@3] In the Bassiknou case, six suspects, Mauritanians and foreigners, were arrested, and officers seized the truck and two off-road vehicles used in the trafficking. [@3] The operations came as the government was preparing a tougher drug law and showed resin moving through the country's eastern and coastal routes. [@1,2]
