Four people were arrested in the district of Paraíso de Osorio, in La Paz
Centro, on suspicion of illicit drug trafficking. [@1] National Civil Police
officers working with the armed forces stopped the vehicle in which the group
was travelling and made the arrests. [@2] The force announced the arrests on
Monday 10 August 2026. [@3]

During the search officers seized marijuana, cash, a scale, a pipe, a metal
grinder, three mobile phones and the car. [@2]

The detainees were named as Nataly Saraí Hernández Mejía, 22, Gustavo Adolfo
Castro Ramos, 18, César Adonay Pérez García, 30, and the minor Steven Ezequiel
A. S., 15. [@2] The group remained in police custody and was to be handed to the
competent authorities for illicit trafficking of substances. [@3] The police
said anyone involved with drugs would go to prison: "Si te involucrás con
drogas, irás a la cárcel". [@1]
