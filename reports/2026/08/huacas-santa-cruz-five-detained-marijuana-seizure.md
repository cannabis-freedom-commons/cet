Fuerza Pública officers detained five people at a road checkpoint in Huacas de
Santa Cruz, Guanacaste, in the early hours of Sunday 9 August 2026. [@1] The
group was made up of four men and one woman. [@1] Four of the five are Costa
Rican and the fifth is a Nicaraguan, according to the Ministry of Public
Security. [@1] The Nicaraguan, identified by the surname Cárdenas, carried no
documents and was in Costa Rica irregularly. [@2] Police said three of the men,
surnamed Villalobos, Cordero and Sotela, had earlier records for international
drug trafficking, offences under the weapons law and the psychotropics law,
assault with a firearm and fraud. [@2] The woman, surnamed Campos, had no police
record. [@2]

Inside the car officers found 16.71 grams of marijuana, divided between a
plastic jar and a clear plastic bag. [@3] They also seized two million colones
(about 3,880 US dollars) of unexplained origin, a pistol with eight rounds of
ammunition and a satellite phone, the Ministry of Public Security said. [@1] The
stop was a routine check of the car in which the five were travelling. [@1]

The five detainees and the seized evidence were left at the disposal of the
Ministerio Público, Costa Rica's public prosecutor's office. [@1] Officers
coordinated the detention and its paperwork with the Fiscalía. [@3]
