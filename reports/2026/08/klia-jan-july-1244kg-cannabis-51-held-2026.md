Bukit Aman narcotics director Comm Datuk Hussein Omar Khan said at an August 11
press conference that 51 people were detained in 41 cases at Kuala Lumpur
International Airport between January and July 2026. [@1] Of the 51 detained, 36
were men and 15 were women, and police put the total value of the drugs seized
at RM96.9 million. [@1]

Cannabis buds were the largest KLIA seizure at 1,244.99 kg, followed by 13.99 kg
of methamphetamine, 7.86 kg of cocaine and 1.32 kg of ketamine. [@1] Of the 51
arrested, 12 were Malaysians and the rest were nationals of the United Kingdom,
Singapore, France, Indonesia, Hong Kong, Japan, Thailand, Ireland, Slovakia,
India, Portugal and Uganda. [@1] A second account of the same press conference
gives the same KLIA enforcement total as 51 individuals arrested and about 1.2
tonnes of drugs worth RM97 million. [@2] British passport holders were the
largest group among the 51 with 14, followed by 12 Malaysians, eight
Singaporeans and five French nationals. [@2]

Hussein said airports remained among the locations being closely monitored to
curb cross-border drug trafficking. [@1]
