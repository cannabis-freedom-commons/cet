DNCD agents intercepted 100 vaper-style electronic cigarettes with a liquid
presumed to be marijuana at the Muelle de Haina Oriental on Monday 10 August
2026, hidden in a shipment of food and used clothing. [@1] Agents of the DNCD
and members of the Ministerio Público made the discovery during an inspection in
the port's cargo consolidation area. [@3] The intercepted cardboard box declared
only used clothing and canned food. [@1] The container holding the parcel had
come from Brooklyn, New York. [@3] The parcel was sent by a woman in a Brooklyn
apartment building and addressed to a man with an alleged residence in Piedra
Blanca, Jarabacoa, La Vega province. [@3]

Officers found a false bottom in the parcel holding the 100 vapers of different
commercial brands, along with several rolls of paper. [@3] The devices were
sealed in security sleeves and sent to the Instituto Nacional de Ciencias
Forenses (INACIF), leaving the exact composition of the liquid pending forensic
confirmation. [@3] Authorities were investigating the circumstances of the
shipment and the possible involvement of those concerned in this form of
controlled-substance trafficking. [@2]

The seizure came amid authorities' checks on consolidated cargo shipments to
detect controlled substances hidden through new transport methods. [@3]
