Eight people were arrested when Costa Rica's Drug Control Police (PCD)
dismantled suspected small-scale drug sales structures in Limón and Guanacaste
after an investigation lasting several months. [@1] In Cariari district, Pococí
canton, Limón, officers raided four houses believed to have been used to sell
and distribute drugs. [@1] The houses were a few metres from El Progreso school,
a situation authorities said raised the risk for students, teachers and
neighbours. [@1] Five people were detained in Cariari, among them two women
surnamed Rojas and Morales. [@1] Two men surnamed Álvarez and Villarreal were
also held, both with police records for drug possession. [@1] A man surnamed
López, with a judicial record including aggravated robbery and drug possession,
was detained as well. [@1]

In Cariari the PCD seized 29 grams and 18 wrappers of marijuana, six grams and
156 doses of crack, ten grams of cocaine and ₡452,000 in cash that authorities
said came from the illegal trade. [@1] A second operation in La Fortuna
district, Bagaces canton, Guanacaste, targeted one house used as a drug sales
point and led to three arrests. [@2] There officers found three doses of
marijuana, 40 doses of crack, 15 doses of cocaine known as 'puntas', a rifle, 45
rounds of ammunition and ₡94,000 in cash. [@1]

The security ministry said the Guanacaste sales point had led to more thefts and
public drug use, affecting residents' peace of mind. [@1] Both investigations
began with reports from residents through the PCD's free, confidential 1-1-7-6
line, the ministry said. [@1] Officers from the Fuerza Pública helped secure the
perimeters during the raids. [@1] All eight detainees were sent to the Public
Prosecutor's Office to face a case for alleged drug sales, an offence punishable
by eight to fifteen years in prison. [@2]
