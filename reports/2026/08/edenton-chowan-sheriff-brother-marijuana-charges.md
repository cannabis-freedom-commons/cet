Chowan County deputies responded on Monday, August 10, 2026, to a home on
Macedonia Road in Edenton for a reported domestic argument. [@3] The sheriff's
office said the call came in the 900 block of Macedonia Road and resulted in
drug-related charges against a local man. [@5] Tommie Wayne Basnight, 69, was
arrested on drug-related charges after he phoned in the domestic complaint. [@6]
Officials identified Basnight as the older brother of Sheriff Edward "Scooter"
Basnight, who lives next door. [@4] He was charged with felony manufacture of
marijuana, felony possession of marijuana with intent to manufacture, sell or
deliver, and misdemeanor possession of marijuana paraphernalia, including
marijuana grinders and scales. [@1]

Basnight told deputies his stepdaughter was at the home in violation of
Department of Social Services directives. [@2] During the investigation,
deputies found marijuana growing inside a shed on his property, along with drug
paraphernalia, according to warrants. [@2] About 10 ounces (about 283 grams) of
marijuana was found at the suspect's home, court documents reported. [@1]

Sheriff Basnight removed himself from any involvement with the investigation and
said he had spoken to the district attorney to ensure transparency. [@1]
According to court records, Basnight was released after posting a $15,000
secured bond. [@6]
