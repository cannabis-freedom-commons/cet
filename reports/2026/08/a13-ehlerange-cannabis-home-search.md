A motorist was slightly injured after losing control of his car on the A13 near
the Ehlerange tunnel in the night from Monday to Tuesday. [@1] Police said the
man was driving towards Pétange and his car struck a retaining wall of the
tunnel. [@1] Luxembourg police said the crash happened at about 1:20am on 25
August 2026. [@2]

Officers smelled cannabis in the car, but the driver tested negative for drugs
while a breathalyser test showed he had been drinking. [@1] A small quantity of
cannabis was found in the vehicle and seized by police. [@4] Police also found
and seized copper cables in the car. [@5] The public prosecutor's office was
informed and ordered a search of the driver's home, which found no further
relevant items. [@2]

The driver was taken to hospital for treatment and had his licence suspended on
the spot. [@3] The investigation continued to establish the full circumstances
of the crash. [@4]
