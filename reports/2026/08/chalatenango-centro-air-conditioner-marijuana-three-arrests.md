Officers of El Salvador's National Civil Police arrested two men and a woman
over drug offences in the Chalatenango Centro district. [@2] The investigation
began with the detention of Juan Alexis Anaya Guardado, 18, in the caserío Plan
de las Mesas, cantón Upatoro, where officers seized a portion of marijuana and a
mobile phone. [@2] From that detention investigators linked other suspects and
obtained a search warrant with prevention of forced entry for a house in the
colonia Las Brisas. [@3] The police said the operation formed part of an
investigation into local drug trafficking and was confirmed through the force's
official channels on the Thursday. [@1]

In the house, agents found marijuana, cash, a mobile phone, a scale, a pipe and
a grinder. [@2] Part of the drug was hidden inside an air conditioner and was
located with the help of the police dog unit. [@2] Ismael Alexander Rivera
Orellana, 24, and Arlin Dinora Rivas Guardado, 24, were detained at the house.
[@2] Both will be referred to the authorities for illicit drug trafficking,
while Anaya Guardado will face a case for possession and holding of drugs. [@2]

The PNC warned that hiding the drugs would not protect those involved, saying:
“No importa dónde escondás los ilícitos, si te involucrás con ellos serás
capturado”. [@1] The force described the operation as part of its permanent
strategy against microtrafficking, small-scale drug dealing, in rural and urban
communities. [@1]
