Philip Antonio King, 45, of Queen Mary Road, Bank Hall, pleaded guilty to
having, trafficking and intending to supply 52.42 grammes of cannabis. [@1] He
was fined $350 after admitting the cannabis offences in a Bridgetown court. [@1]

Prosecutor Sergeant Reginald Blackman told the No. 1 District 'A' Magistrates'
Court that police were carrying out checks in Cheapside and that, on seeing them
arrive, King ran from the area, raising suspicions. [@1] King was pursued and
fell while running, sustaining minor injuries. [@1] He was caught near the
General Post Office and his haversack was searched. [@1] The drugs, which police
estimated at $262, were found in several small transparent plastic bags. [@1]

His attorney Shadia Simpson said the drugs were for his personal use and that he
had offered police cooperation while in custody. [@1] Acting Chief Magistrate
Douglas Frederick fined King $350 immediately for the intent to supply, warning
that failure to pay would mean two weeks in jail. [@1] He was convicted,
reprimanded and discharged on the other two charges. [@1]
