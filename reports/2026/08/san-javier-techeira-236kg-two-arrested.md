A woman and a man were detained while unloading more than 236 kilograms of
marijuana on the Uruguay River coast at San Javier, Misiones. [@1] The two
detainees were a 29-year-old woman and a 32-year-old man. [@3] During
surveillance, officers watched a black pickup advance along a dirt track towards
the shore. [@1] The operation was at the Techeira natural port, around kilometre
1.018.6 of the Uruguay River. [@1] When Prefecture officers approached, the
driver and his companion ignored the order to stop and tried to flee, but were
intercepted a short distance away. [@1]

The bundles were found both inside the pickup and scattered on the riverbank,
indicating the unloading had already begun. [@1] Opening the 17 bundles,
investigators counted 332 pressed pans of marijuana. [@1] Tests confirmed the
substance was marijuana, with a total weight of more than 236 kilograms. [@1]
The authorities estimated the cargo's value at more than 891 million pesos. [@1]
Officers also seized the pickup used to carry the load, two mobile phones,
documentation and other items. [@1]

The operation followed criminal-intelligence work by the force's Criminal
Intelligence and Investigations Delegation, which had information about a
possible drug transfer in the area. [@2] The case went to the federal court and
prosecutor's office in Oberá. [@3] Investigators were still trying to establish
whether the marijuana had come in from the Brazilian bank of the Uruguay River
or was to be shipped from the Argentine shore. [@1]
