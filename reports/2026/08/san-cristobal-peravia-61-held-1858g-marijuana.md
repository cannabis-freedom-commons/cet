The DNCD carried out 680 operations against microtrafficking in San Cristóbal
and Peravia provinces. [@1] The DNCD said it acted in coordination with the
Public Prosecutor's Office and other state institutions. [@2] The operations led
to the detention of 61 people linked to the sale and distribution of narcotics,
according to the official figures. [@1]

The seized drugs included 1,858 grams of marijuana, 13,706 grams of suspected
cocaine and 15 doses of crack. [@1] Officers also seized RD$ 19,155 (about USD
325), a firearm, two motorcycles, eleven mobile phones, three scales and two
radios. [@1]

In San Cristóbal, 350 operations led to 41 detentions and the seizure of 6,901
grams of various substances. [@1] In Peravia, 330 operations led to 20 arrests
and the seizure of 8,668.3 grams of suspected cocaine, marijuana and crack. [@1]

The substances were sent to the National Institute of Forensic Sciences (INACIF)
to determine their exact nature and weight, and the detainees were placed before
the competent judicial jurisdiction. [@1]
