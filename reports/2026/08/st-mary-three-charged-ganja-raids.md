Seven men were taken into custody on Tuesday during a joint security-forces
operation in St Mary. [@1] Three of them were arrested and charged for offences
including possession of ganja and dealing in ganja, as well as possession of
uncustomed goods, praedial larceny and assault occasioning bodily harm. [@1]

The operation ran between 3 a.m. and 1 p.m. and involved simultaneous targeted
raids and coordinated vehicle checkpoints across Oracabessa, Gayle and Annotto
Bay. [@1] Four men were arrested on outstanding warrants. [@1] Sixteen other
people were detained for questioning and processing and were subsequently
released. [@1]

An international manhunt for one of two Dominican Republic nationals, who was
wanted for murder in his country, ended on Tuesday during an intelligence-led
operation in Annotto Bay. [@1] He is 26-year-old Jhovanny De Jesus Metz Cruz.
[@1] He is accused of killing a garbage-truck driver in Santiago on 17 April.
[@1] A police report said the suspect and another Dominican Republic national
were being housed by a resident of the community. [@1] All three men were taken
into custody, and investigations were under way as Jamaican authorities
continued to collaborate with international law-enforcement counterparts. [@1]
St Mary police arrested the two Dominican Republic nationals on suspicion of
involvement in serious and violent crimes in Jamaica and their home country.
[@1] Police also suspected that the two men had entered the island illegally.
