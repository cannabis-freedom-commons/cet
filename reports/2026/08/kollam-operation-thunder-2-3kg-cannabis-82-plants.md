The enforcement drive took place in Kollam district, where checks tightened on
drug sources and distribution networks. [@1] Operation Thunder completed 92
days, with officers filing 307 NDPS cases and arresting 279 people. [@1] The
campaign ran 2,784 raids and 108 joint inspections across the district from 28
May to 27 August 2026. [@1] Officers checked about 22,000 vehicles during the
campaign. [@1]

Excise figures put the cannabis seizure at 2.3 kg, alongside 165 grams of MDMA.
[@1] Officers also seized 6.6 grams of methamphetamine and 26 grams of
nitrazepam tablets. [@1] Officers seized 82 cannabis plants through Operation
Thunder. [@1] Among the cannabis most recently seized was about 5 grams of
hybrid cannabis of the kind seen in metro cities. [@1]

Special Squad, Circle and Range offices led raids and lightning inspections to
trace drug sources and stop inter-state trafficking. [@1] With availability
falling, synthetic drugs including MDMA reportedly cost up to 30 percent more.
[@1] Checks focused on college students and watched accused in earlier drug
cases. [@1] Officers said cases would be filed over the seized plants but
investigation was difficult. [@1]
