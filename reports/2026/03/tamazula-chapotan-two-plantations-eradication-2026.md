Mexico's navy ministry (Semar) said naval personnel destroyed two cannabis
plantations near the village of Chapotán, in the municipality of Tamazula,
Durango. [@2] The operation was announced on 10 March 2026 and, according to
Semar, was carried out with the federal security secretariat (SSPC) and the
attorney general's office (FGR). [@1] Semar said the work was done in
coordination with the Secretaría de Seguridad y Protección Ciudadana (SSPC) and
the Fiscalía General de la República (FGR). [@1]

The first plantation covered about 3,920 square metres and held some 23,520
plants, with an estimated weight of 7,056 kg. [@1] The second covered about
1,000 square metres, with some 10,000 plants and an estimated 3,000 kg. [@1]
Proyecto Puente put the combined haul at more than 10 tonnes of marijuana. [@3]

Naval personnel uprooted and burned the plants where they stood, under federal
prosecutors, who opened an investigation file. [@1] Security sources cited by
Infobae said the plantations belonged to Los Chapitos, the Sinaloa Cartel
faction led by Iván Archivaldo Guzmán Salazar. [@1] The report said the
authorities presume the group controls the area around Chapotán, which it
described as known for drug-trafficking activity. [@1]

Tamazula lies in the Golden Triangle, the border region of Sinaloa, Durango and
Chihuahua where, according to the report, factions of the Sinaloa Cartel have a
historic presence in drug production and planting. [@1]
