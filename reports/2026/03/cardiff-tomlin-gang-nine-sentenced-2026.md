Nine members of an organised crime group led by Danny Tomlin were sentenced
following an investigation led by Tarian, the Regional Organised Crime Unit for
southern Wales. [@2] Sentencing took place at Cardiff Crown Court on 25 March
2026. [@2] Danny Tomlin pleaded guilty to conspiracy to supply cocaine,
conspiracy to supply cannabis and cultivation of cannabis and was sentenced to
10 years and 10 months in prison. [@2] Louis Maxwell pleaded guilty to
conspiracy to supply cocaine and conspiracy to supply cannabis and was sentenced
to six years in prison. [@2] Ryan Battersby was found guilty of conspiracy to
supply cocaine and conspiracy to supply cannabis and was sentenced to six years
in prison. [@2] Ashley Coughlan was found guilty of conspiracy to supply cocaine
and was sentenced to three years and nine months in prison. [@2] David Gorman
pleaded guilty to conspiracy to supply cocaine and was sentenced to four years
and four months in prison. [@2] Christopher Preston pleaded guilty to conspiracy
to supply cocaine and conspiracy to supply cannabis and was sentenced to five
years in prison. [@2] Bradley Williams pleaded guilty to conspiracy to supply
cannabis, offering to supply cocaine, offering to supply ketamine and possession
of criminal property and was sentenced to five years and nine months in prison.
[@2] Jack Griffin pleaded guilty to conspiracy to supply cannabis and was
sentenced to six months in prison, which he had already served on remand. [@2]
Kory Preece changed his plea to guilty to conspiracy to supply cannabis and was
sentenced to 15 months in prison suspended for two years, with 180 hours of
unpaid work and a 10-day rehabilitation activity. [@2]

The group supplied wholesale cocaine and cannabis to customers across southern
Wales and ran two sophisticated cannabis factories in residential properties in
Blackwood and Trebanog. [@2] Tomlin ran two cannabis factories in
Pontllanfraith, Blackwood and in Porth, Rhondda Cynon Taf which police shut down
in summer 2025. [@1] Across 29 and 30 July 2025 Tarian with Gwent Police and
Gloucestershire Constabulary executed warrants to arrest those involved. [@2]
Tomlin, of Valley Road, Wotton-under-Edge, used a garden centre called Kitts
Green Nursery as cover for the operation. [@1]

Tomlin later faced a proceeds of crime hearing at Cardiff Crown Court after his
March jailing. [@1] The prosecution put Tomlin’s criminal benefit at £740,423.51
with only £209.24 in available assets for confiscation, a combined
cocaine-and-cannabis figure. [@1]
