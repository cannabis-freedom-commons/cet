Officers of Georgia's Interior Ministry Central Criminal Police Department and
police units in Adjara, Tbilisi and Samegrelo-Zemo Svaneti detained nine people
on drug charges. [@1] The Interior Ministry said the detentions took place at
different times. [@1] The arrests were announced on Saturday 28 March 2026. [@2]
Those detained included Georgian citizens, foreign nationals and people with
prior convictions. [@1]

Police searched the suspects, their homes and yards, and carried out
investigative experiments at locations the detainees identified. [@2] The
searches recovered drugs prepared for sale in especially large quantities,
including marijuana, mephedrone, methadone, buprenorphine, morphine, codeine,
thebaine, heroin, alpha-PVP and cocaine. [@1] Officers also seized materials
needed to package drugs and money believed to have come from drug sales. [@1]

The Interior Ministry said the investigation was proceeding under articles 260,
261 and 273 of the Criminal Code. [@1] Those articles carry up to 20 years in
prison or life imprisonment. [@1]
