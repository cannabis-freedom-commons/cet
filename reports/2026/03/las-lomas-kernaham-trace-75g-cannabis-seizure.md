Trinidad and Tobago Police Service officers searched a premises in the Las Lomas
district between 6 pm and 11 pm on Tuesday 10 March 2026. [@1] They found about
75 grammes of cannabis in two plastic zip-lock bags hidden in a pile of red
bricks on the western side of a building. [@1] Further searches on the eastern
side of the premises found a black plastic bag concealed in a bushy area,
holding one 9 mm round and five 5.56 mm rounds of ammunition. [@1]

Officers then went to Kernaham Trace, Cunupia, where a search of a forested area
found three pigtail buckets holding three fully grown cannabis trees. [@1]
Police took the cannabis and the ammunition to the Las Lomas Police Post for
safekeeping. [@1]

In a separate intelligence-led operation in the Maloney district between 3 pm
and 6 pm that day, officers seized a firearm, a magazine and fourteen rounds of
.40 calibre ammunition and arrested one suspect. [@1] Officers met the man
outside a shop area on Cleaver Road, Arima, and he ran towards the back of the
house after they told him they would search him under the State of Emergency
Regulations. [@1] He was caught a short distance away with a firearm fitted with
a magazine of fourteen rounds in a multi-coloured bag he was carrying. [@1]
Police identified him as a 43-year-old man of Cleaver Road, Arima, cautioned and
arrested him, and took him to the Maloney Police Station. [@1]

Senior Superintendent Maynard-Wilson and Superintendent McKenzie coordinated the
exercise with officers of the Maloney Police Station. [@1] The police service
said the operations were anti-crime exercises in the Central and North Eastern
Divisions. [@1]
