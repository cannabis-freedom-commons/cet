Haveri Town police arrested two childhood friends for allegedly procuring ganja
from Odisha and selling it in Nagendranamatti. [@1] The two were identified as
Dilleppa Mallappa Alalagi, 28, a shepherd, and Rajasab Babusab Nadaf, 27, a
civil engineer with a private company, both residents of Nagendranamatti in
Haveri. [@1] They were preparing to sell the ganja in Sai Layout near
Hosakerimath Temple in Nagendranamatti. [@1]

Police said the accused had transported the ganja to Haveri by train via
Bengaluru. [@1] They had tightly sealed the bag with tape to prevent the smell
of ganja from spreading. [@1] Acting on a tip-off, the town police conducted a
raid. [@1] Officers seized 10.7 kg of ganja from the two men. [@1] Police valued
the seized ganja at approximately Rs 5.3 lakh and also seized two bags and Rs
360 in cash. [@1]

A complaint was registered at the town police station and further investigation
was under way. [@1] DSP Girimalla Talakatti, town police inspector Motilal
Pawar, DSB inspector Santosh Pawar and sub-inspectors B G Doddamani, M G
Vaggannavar and N N Shishuvinahal took part in the operation. [@1]
