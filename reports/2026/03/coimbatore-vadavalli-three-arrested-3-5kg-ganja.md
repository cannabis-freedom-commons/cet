Vadavalli police in Coimbatore arrested three men on Saturday for possession of
more than 3 kg of ganja for sale. [@1] A police officer identified them as S.
Edison Chakravarthy, 26, and D. Richard Sachin, 26, both from Dindigul district,
and P. Walter William, 21, of Uppilipalayam in Coimbatore city. [@1] The officer
said Edison and Richard were history-sheeters with several cases, including a
murder case, pending against them. [@1]

During a routine patrol near Veerakeralam at Vadavalli, officers stopped the
three on a bike and found 3.5 kg of ganja in a plastic cover, along with a
weighing machine and knives, the officer said. [@1] Vadavalli police seized the
ganja, the bike, the weighing machine and the knives. [@1]

The three were booked under Section 8(c) read with Sections 20(b)(ii)(B), 25 and
29 of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, and under Section 132
of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita. [@1] They were produced before a judicial
magistrate, who remanded them in judicial custody, and they were lodged in
Coimbatore Central Prison. [@1]
