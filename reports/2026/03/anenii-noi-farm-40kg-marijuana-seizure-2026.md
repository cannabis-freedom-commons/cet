Officers of Moldova's National Inspectorate of Investigations (INI) and
prosecutors carried out a large-scale special operation in a village in Anenii
Noi district and shut down a large site for the production and storage of
narcotics. [@1] On a cattle-breeding farm belonging to a local resident,
officers found and seized more than 40 kg of marijuana in various stages of
readiness. [@1]

Police put the consignment's black-market value at more than 14 million lei.
[@1] Investigators say the farm, on land outside the village, was used as cover
for large-scale cannabis cultivation and processing. [@1] Searches took place at
several addresses at once and turned up cultivation equipment, fertiliser and
drying installations as well as the finished product. [@1] The farm's owner had
previously been convicted of other crimes. [@1]

The suspect was detained and police classified his actions as production and
storage of narcotics on an especially large scale for sale, which carries up to
15 years or life imprisonment depending on how it is classified. [@1]
