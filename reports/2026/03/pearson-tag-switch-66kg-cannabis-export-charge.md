On 19 February 2026, CBSA officers at Toronto Pearson airport found about 33
kilograms of cannabis in each checked bag of two passengers scheduled to fly to
Germany. [@2] RCMP investigators found that an Air Canada employee working in
the baggage room had placed luggage tags bearing the unsuspecting passengers'
names onto the suitcases containing the cannabis. [@2] RCMP officers arrested
both passengers, who each denied owning the luggage. [@2] Both passengers
cooperated with police and were released without charges. [@2]

On 12 March 2026, RCMP Border Integrity officers arrested and charged
32-year-old Air Canada employee Atasha Weathley of Mississauga. [@2] She was
charged with possession for the purpose of export under section 11(2) of the
Cannabis Act and conspiracy to commit an indictable offence. [@2] Weathley was
held for bail, released with conditions, and given a 10 April 2026 court date in
Brampton. [@2]

The later W5 account identifies two of the affected travellers as Jan Baumann
and Charlene Ranadheera, who say they were handcuffed and detained at Pearson
for 12 hours and released without charges. [@1] That account says their baggage
tags were switched onto luggage containing 33 kilograms of marijuana on an Air
Canada flight to Germany. [@1] Baumann's own checked luggage was a black hiking
backpack, but officers told him he had an extra large suitcase he did not
recognise. [@3]
