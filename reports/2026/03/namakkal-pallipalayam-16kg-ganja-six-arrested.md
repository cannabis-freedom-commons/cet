The Pallipalayam police in Namakkal district seized 16 kg of ganja, worth around
Rs 2 lakh, that was being smuggled from Bihar, and arrested six men. [@1] Acting
on a tip-off, police checked government and private buses on the Trichy-Erode
National Highway in the early hours of Thursday. [@1] They found two men from
Bihar carrying ganja on a private bus. [@1] The two men from Bihar were carrying
the ganja on a private bus. [@1]

The two, identified as Motilal Shakani, 31, and Vishal Kumar, 26, of Bihar, were
smuggling ganja after being contacted by buyers in Tamil Nadu. [@1] The two men
from Bihar are farmers. [@1] Based on information gathered from them, police
arrested four more men. [@1]

Police identified the four further men as Ramesh, 38, of Dharmapuri district,
Harish, 22, of Konnaiyar near Tiruchengode, Ramesh, 30, of Mettur, and Karthi,
36, of Salem. [@1] All six of the men were remanded in judicial custody. [@1]
