Port police at Tanger Med, working with customs, foiled an attempted
international drug-trafficking operation on the evening of Thursday 19 March
2026. [@2] A joint border-surveillance operation targeting the vehicle detected
the concealment and enabled the seizure. [@2] The truck’s Moroccan driver was
placed in custody under a judicial investigation supervised by the competent
prosecutor. [@1]

Officers seized three tonnes and 932 kilograms of chira. [@1] The drug was
hidden inside cavities built into refrigerated fake fish that had been carefully
prepared and wrapped to mask the smuggling. [@1] The cannabis had been concealed
in fish-shaped forms specially designed and packed to blend into an
international transport load and evade controls. [@2]

Investigators are trying to identify all of the operation’s national and
international ramifications. [@2] The case was described as part of continuing
security efforts against international drug-trafficking networks at Moroccan
border posts. [@2]
