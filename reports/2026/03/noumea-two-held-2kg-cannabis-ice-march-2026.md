Two men will face the Nouméa criminal court on Friday 13 March to answer
drug-trafficking charges. [@1] The first suspect was arrested on Friday 6 March
after a vehicle check on rue Jacques Iekawe in Nouméa. [@1] The second man, a
Nouméa resident originally from French Polynesia, was identified as the first
suspect's supplier. [@1] Both suspects were placed under judicial supervision
pending trial in an immediate-appearance procedure. [@1] Five drug users
identified in the same investigation will face a separate simplified guilty-plea
procedure. [@1]

Customs officers found 1 gram of cocaine, 2 grams of cannabis herb, a straw and
1 gram of ice in the stopped vehicle. [@1] A search at the first suspect's home
in Mont-Dore seized 2 kilos of cannabis with indoor growing equipment, ten
1-gram packets of cocaine and 45,000 francs in cash. [@1] Investigators found 2
grams of ice at the supplier's home. [@1] The prosecutor said that ice had been
brought from French Polynesia by plane hidden in luggage. [@1]

Investigators determined that the first man had been trafficking drugs since
July 2025. [@1] Both suspects admit their involvement, according to prosecutors,
but remain presumed innocent until judgment. [@1] The prosecutor described ice
as rarely found in the territory and extremely dangerous to public health. [@1]
