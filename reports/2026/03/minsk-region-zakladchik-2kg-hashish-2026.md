Belarus's Interior Ministry said drug-control officers, with OMON riot police
providing force support, detained a 19-year-old resident of Minsk red-handed.
[@3] The officers belonged to the drug-control unit of the Mogilev region. [@1]
According to the ministry, the young man had a prior conviction for robbery.
[@2] He had spent about a month working as a zakladchik on a drug market, a term
for a courier who prepares caches from which buyers collect their orders. [@1]
Acting on a handler's instructions, he collected a wholesale batch of the drug
and prepared it for further sale through hiding places in Pristolichye, the area
around Minsk. [@3]

When they detained him, officers found and seized about two kilograms of hashish
on his person. [@2]

Investigators opened a criminal case for illegal drug trafficking. [@3] The
Interior Ministry said he faces a prison term of up to 15 years if convicted.
[@3]
