Ahmedabad Rural Special Operations Group apprehended three people, including a
minor, near Bopal late on Sunday night after a tip-off, and seized hybrid ganja
the police valued at over Rs 9 lakh. [@1] The operation was under Bopal police
station, which registered an FIR on Monday. [@1] A patrol team had been told
that three people would arrive near a showroom close to Bopal bridge on a
two-wheeler to sell narcotics. [@1] Around 1.45am the team reached the spot and
found a scooter parked nearby. [@1] A search of the area led to the detention of
three suspects. [@1]

Police recovered 258.8 grams of hybrid ganja from the suspects and the vehicle.
[@1] Police estimated the seized contraband at Rs 9.05 lakh. [@1] Officers also
seized five mobile phones, Rs 7,730 in cash, three weighing scales, packaging
material and the scooter. [@1] The total value of the seized items was estimated
at around Rs 9.8 lakh. [@1]

Police said the three were acting on the instructions of another person who is
absconding and is believed to have supplied the drugs. [@1] The substance tested
positive for narcotics in an on-site examination, and samples were to be sent
for forensic analysis. [@1] A case was registered under the NDPS Act, and police
were trying to trace the absconding accused. [@1]
