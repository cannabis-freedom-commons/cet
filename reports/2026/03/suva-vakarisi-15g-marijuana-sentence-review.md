Jone Vakarisi, 36, was sentenced by the Suva Magistrate Court to three months'
imprisonment, suspended for two years, for drug possession and damaging a police
uniform. [@2] He was charged with unlawful possession of 15.2 grams of marijuana
and one count of damaging a police uniform. [@2] The charges stemmed from an
incident at the Suva Courthouse cellblock while Vakarisi was in remand. [@2] A
police officer witnessed Vakarisi receiving a brown package from his brother.
[@2] A subsequent search revealed the package contained dried leaves wrapped in
plastic. [@2]

The Office of the Director of Public Prosecutions appealed the suspended
sentence and the Suva High Court took up a review of it. [@3] At an earlier
hearing the matter was adjourned to 30 March for further hearing. [@4] Vakarisi
appeared before Chief Justice Salesi Temo for the review hearing. [@1] He
represented himself and state counsel Hezakaia Noafaga filed and served the
state's submissions. [@1] Justice Temo directed the prosecution to address
whether the lower court's sentence was appropriate and served the interests of
justice. [@1] His bail was extended and the matter was adjourned to 13 April for
Vakarisi to respond to the prosecution's submissions. [@1]

Vakarisi died in military custody on 17 April 2026 while the sentence review was
still pending. [@6] The State made an application for abatement of proceedings
because he had died. [@5] Justice Temo granted the application and the Suva High
Court formally closed the proceedings on 20 April 2026. [@5]
