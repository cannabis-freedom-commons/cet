Prosecutors filed two charges against each of Mohamed Aish Ahmed, 31, and
Aminath Illiyyin Ahmed, 25, of Ma. Mazaage in Male' City, accusing them of
trafficking a Schedule One drug and using cannabis. [@2] The two siblings were
arrested under a court order in September 2025 during a joint operation by
Counter Narcotics Intelligence and Drug Enforcement. [@1] A hearing on the
charges was scheduled at the Criminal Court on Monday 23 March 2026. [@1]

The September operation also detained a third person, Inayath Ali, 34, of
Mariaaz, Haa Dhaalu Kulhudhuffushi. [@3] Inayath Ali was held for seven days and
released on conditional bail on 3 October 2025 pending trial. [@2] Police put
the provisional combined weight of the seized items at 1.246 kilograms. [@1]
Officers seized a hand-rolled cigarette believed to contain narcotics and rubber
packets from two residences in Male'. [@3]

Under the Narcotics Act, trafficking a Schedule One drug carries a penalty of
life imprisonment and a fine between MVR 100,000 and MVR 10 million. [@1]
Possession of more than four grams of a Schedule One drug is legally considered
trafficking. [@1] Cannabis use or possession for personal use is also an
offence, punishable by up to three years in prison. [@1] Possession of more than
14 grams is treated as a trafficking offence. [@1] Drug-dependent offenders may
undergo rehabilitation through the Drug Court, and those who complete treatment
successfully need not serve a prison sentence. [@2]
