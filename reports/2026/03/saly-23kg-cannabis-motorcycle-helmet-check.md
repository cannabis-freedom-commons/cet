Police of the urban police station of Saly-Portudal seized 23 kg of chanvre
indien, the French term for Indian hemp or cannabis, during a routine motorcycle
check at the Saly crossroads on Sunday 15 March 2026. [@1] Two men on a
motorcycle without helmets refused to stop and tried to ride through the
officers before they lost control, fell and fled on foot. [@1] The special
brigade of the Saly-Portudal urban police station carried out the seizure from a
moto-scooter whose rider fled, the Senegalese news agency APS reported from a
police source. [@2]

The men abandoned the motorcycle and a travel bag; officers found 17 blocks of
cannabis in the bag and six more hidden under the motorcycle seat. [@1] The 23
blocks weighed about one kilogram each, Pressafrik reported. [@3] The two
suspects escaped on foot, the motorcycle was immobilised and police said the
investigation to identify them was continuing. [@1]

Actusen reported that the checkpoint was erected in the morning of 15 March
under road-safety instructions from the police hierarchy. [@4] The drugs were
placed under seal and the motorcycle held at the police station, and police said
investigations continued to identify and arrest the fugitives. [@4]
