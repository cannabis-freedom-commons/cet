Charles Adrian Jordan, 47, of 372 Loper Street in Philadelphia, Mississippi, was
arrested and charged by police with a series of drug offences, according to
Philadelphia Police Chief Eric Lyons. [@1] Among the charges were two counts of
possession of marijuana with intent to distribute. [@1] He was also charged with
trafficking cocaine. [@1] Another count alleged possession with intent to
distribute methamphetamine. [@1]

On Friday 27 March 2026, the Philadelphia Police Department received information
that suspected stolen equipment was at a residence on Loper Street and an
adjacent property on Jericho Street. [@1] A search warrant was issued around 5
p.m., and officers recovered two Kubota zero-turn lawn mowers reported stolen
out of Union at Jordan’s Loper Street residence. [@1] When the warrant was
executed Jordan was detained, and officers said they saw him in possession of
cocaine and hydrocodone. [@1] A further search of the residence found stolen
lawn equipment and a host of illicit items. [@1] Lyons said large quantities of
drugs, guns and drug paraphernalia were recovered from the residence. [@1]

Investigators were still sorting through the stolen items, Lyons said further
charges were expected, and Jordan remained in the Leake County Detention Center.
