Q Branch police and Narcotics Intelligence Bureau personnel searched a vehicle
near the Karikuttam bus stop near Mandapam in Ramanathapuram on Sunday morning
after a tip-off. [@2] Officers found the two occupants acting suspiciously and,
after checking cricket kits and plastic sacks in the vehicle, found packed
bundles of drugs. [@1] The two men were identified as Ibrahim Badusha, 29, and
Muhaideen Bayas Khan, 27, both from Mandapam. [@1] After interrogation they were
handed over to NIB, produced before a court and remanded in judicial custody.
[@1]

Officers seized 219 kg of ganja, cannabis, along with 19 kg of methamphetamine
and 10 kg of heroin from the vehicle. [@2] Police said 70 packages were seized,
41 of which contained ganja, 20 heroin and 19 methamphetamine. [@1] Police
valued the whole consignment at around ₹300 crore and described it as the
district's largest narcotics seizure since 2023. [@1]

Investigators said the drugs had been brought from Andhra Pradesh to Mandapam by
road and were to be smuggled to Sri Lanka by sea in a country boat. [@2] A
senior police officer said this was the largest quantity of methamphetamine
seized in Ramanathapuram and the first time heroin had been seized in recent
years. [@1] Police were also searching for a third man, Sheik Rahman, 25, from
Mandapam, said to be involved in the smuggling. [@2]
