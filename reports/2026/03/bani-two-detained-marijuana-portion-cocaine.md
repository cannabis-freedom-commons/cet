Two men were detained when Dominican authorities intercepted a boat several
nautical miles south of Baní, in Peravia province. [@2] Officers found a small
portion of suspected marijuana among the items aboard, alongside 261 packages of
a substance presumed to be cocaine. [@1]

The operation was carried out by the Dirección Nacional de Control de Drogas
(DNCD) with the Dominican Navy and Air Force, with support from the US DEA and
Southern Command. [@1] The cocaine was hidden in nine containers that appeared
to hold fuel, with the packages wrapped in plastic and sealed with tape. [@1]
Nine empty fuel containers and personal documents were also seized. [@2]

The operation followed intelligence reports of a boat sailing suspiciously
toward the Dominican coast. [@1] The packages were sent to the Instituto
Nacional de Ciencias Forenses (Inacif) for analysis of their weight and exact
composition. [@1] The detainees were to be brought to justice for alleged
violations of Law 50-88 on drugs and controlled substances. [@2]
