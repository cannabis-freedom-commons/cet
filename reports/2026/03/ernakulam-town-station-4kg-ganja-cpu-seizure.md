Four kilograms of ganja was seized from Ernakulam Town railway station in Kochi,
Kerala, on Friday during a joint inspection by the crime intelligence unit in
Thiruvananthapuram, the Railway Protection Force at Ernakulam North and the
excise enforcement wing in Ernakulam. [@1] After the Visakhapatnam Weekly
Express departed from the station, officials found 4 kg of cannabis hidden
inside a computer CPU in a bag under a bench on the second platform and seized
it. [@1] RPF officials said the person who brought the cannabis likely abandoned
the bag on seeing police on the platform. [@1]

The cannabis was believed to be worth around Rs two lakh in the market and was
thought to have been brought to sell among migrant workers. [@1] The joint
inspection was being carried out under special instructions from RPF divisional
security commissioner Mohammed Hanif and was to continue rigorously in
connection with the assembly elections in Kerala. [@1]
