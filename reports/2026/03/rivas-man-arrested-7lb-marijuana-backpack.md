Police in the Rivas department presented Geovanny Cuadra Alvarado, 29, from the
Las Piedras neighbourhood, after arresting him on a street in the municipality
of Rivas. [@1] The arrest took place at 3:30 p.m. on Friday 13 March, when
police intercepted him in public and searched him. [@1] The second account says
he was walking along a Rivas street with a backpack on his back when officers
detained him. [@2] Police said he had drawn attention by behaving suspiciously
and that he had a prior criminal record for threatening someone with a firearm.
[@1]

The backpack held eight rectangular 'tacos' of herb, described in the police
report as eight oval packages wrapped in adhesive tape. [@1] A field test on the
herb was positive for marijuana, with a final weight of seven pounds and 14
ounces (about 3.57 kg). [@1] The second outlet reported the same quantity as
seven pounds and 14 ounces of marijuana. [@2]

The detainee and the seized drugs were placed at the disposal of the competent
authorities on suspicion of drug trafficking and organised crime. [@1] The
second account says they were sent to the Public Ministry for prosecution on a
drug-trafficking charge. [@2] Police described the operation as part of their
continuing 'muro de contención' (containment wall) plan against drug trafficking
and organised crime. [@1]
