The Barbados Police Service Narcotics Unit charged a Canadian national with
multiple cannabis offences. [@2] Ronald George Collins, 61, of London, Ontario,
Canada, was charged with offences allegedly committed on 27 March 2026. [@2] The
charges were possession of cannabis, trafficking of cannabis, intent to supply
cannabis and importation of cannabis. [@2] Collins appeared before Magistrate
Douglas Frederick in the District 'B' Magistrates' Court and pleaded guilty to
the offences. [@2]

Barbados Today reported that Ronald George Collins of London, Ontario appeared
before the District 'B' Magistrates' Court on Monday and pleaded guilty to
charges including possession, trafficking, importation and intent to supply.
[@1] Police estimated the suspected cannabis at 26.30 kilogrammes, with a street
value of 210,400 Barbados dollars. [@2] Barbados Today described the seizure as
more than 26 kilogrammes of cannabis, with an estimated street value of
$210,400. [@1]

He was remanded to the Barbados Prison Service at Dodds and was scheduled to
reappear on Thursday, 9 April 2026. [@2] Barbados Today also said he was
remanded to Dodds and was scheduled to reappear on April 9. [@1]
