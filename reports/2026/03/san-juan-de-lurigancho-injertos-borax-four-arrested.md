Peruvian police dismantled a gang known as Los Injertos del Borax in the San
Juan de Lurigancho district of Lima and detained four of its members. [@1] The
four were named as Julio Cusco, Marco Pareja, Brenda Vértiz and Jerry Gómez,
arrested in what the Interior Ministry called a strategic intervention. [@2]
Their arrest was part of a wider state-of-emergency operation across Lima
Metropolitan Area in which police detained 123 people and broke up five criminal
gangs. [@1] Lima police region chief General Francisco Vargas Andonaire
announced the results from the San Martín de Porres police station. [@1]

Officers seized one kilogram of marijuana ready for distribution from Los
Injertos del Borax. [@1] Police said the marijuana was presumably sold in
nightclub areas and through a home-delivery service. [@1] They also confiscated
11 mobile phones believed to have been used to coordinate the gang's financial
transactions. [@1] Police said the phones would be analysed to identify possible
contacts and distribution routes. [@1] The Interior Ministry confirmed the
one-kilogram haul and the 11 phones. [@2]

Across the whole operation, police seized 289 pacos, small packets, of
marijuana, alongside 872 wrappers of cocaine paste (PBC), 14 wrappers of cocaine
hydrochloride and the synthetic drug tusi. [@1] The seizures also included 50
grams and 67 wrappers of tusi. [@3] Officers also seized three firearms and a
stick of dynamite during the interventions. [@1]

General Vargas said the state of emergency gave police the tools to enter
premises when catching offenders in the act. [@2] He said six tusi laboratories
had been detected in northern Lima and that minors were involved in the gangs.
[@1] Police said investigations continue to identify other gang members and
establish the origin of the drugs. [@1]
