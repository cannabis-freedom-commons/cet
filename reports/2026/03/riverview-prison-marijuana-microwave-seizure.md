At Riverview Medium Security Correctional Facility in Ogdensburg, New York,
officers intercepted more than two pounds of marijuana during a contraband
investigation, the union representing corrections officers said. [@1] On March
27, staff responded to a report from the storeroom after an inmate was seen
fleeing the facility. [@1] A civilian employee saw the inmate, who was assigned
to assist her, break open a box holding a microwave, pull a black bag from
inside it and run toward a dormitory, ignoring orders to stop. [@1] At a locked
dormitory door the inmate turned, ripped open the bag and scattered its
contents, but an officer caught him in a body hold and a second officer
handcuffed him despite his resistance. [@1]

Staff recovered a green leafy substance later identified as marijuana weighing
1.3 pounds from the ground; the contraband and the microwave had been shipped by
UPS from a Walmart Distribution Center. [@1] A search of the storeroom aided by
a K9 found an additional vacuum-sealed bag containing 1.15 pounds of marijuana,
which was also placed into evidence. [@1] One officer sustained hand and
shoulder injuries but remained on duty after receiving medical care. [@1]

The inmate, 32, was serving a 10-to-12-year sentence for Criminal Use of a
Firearm in the First Degree after a 2018 conviction in Albany, and after medical
treatment was relocated to the Special Housing Unit. [@1] John Roberts, Northern
Region Vice President of NYSCOPBA, the corrections officers' union, said the
drug seizure and a separate assault at the facility highlighted the dangers
officers face and attempts to smuggle contraband into prisons. [@1]
