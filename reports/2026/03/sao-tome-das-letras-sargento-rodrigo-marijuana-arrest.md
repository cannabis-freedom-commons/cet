Military police arrested a 30-year-old man and seized drugs during an Operation
Sargento Rodrigo action in São Tomé das Letras, Minas Gerais, in the early
hours, with a firearm and more drugs also seized in Campo Belo. [@1] A second
account dates the operation to the early hours of Thursday 12 March 2026. [@2]
Police said that around 00:25 they received information that two men linked to
drug trafficking in Campo Belo were in São Tomé das Letras to sell drugs and
expand the trade in the municipality. [@1] Officers found the suspects in the
city's Municipal Park and, as they tried to flee, one threw a package, was
chased and arrested, while the other, already identified, escaped. [@1]

The package contained 13 portions of a substance resembling marijuana, 44
eppendorfs and 17 plastic pots containing a substance resembling cocaine, all
individually wrapped and ready for sale. [@1] The arrested man told officers
that his home in the Vila Arandutaba neighbourhood of Campo Belo held more drugs
and a firearm, and police searched it with the resident's authorization. [@1]
Inside the home officers found a .32-calibre revolver and 136 buchas (individual
wraps) of a substance resembling marijuana, also packaged for sale. [@1] The
second account likewise reports the revolver and 136 wraps of marijuana at the
Campo Belo home, with all material sent to the district police stations. [@2]
That account reports the same 13 portions of marijuana, 44 pinos and 17 pots of
cocaine from the discarded package. [@2]

The man and all seized material were taken to the Civil Police station for legal
measures. [@1]
