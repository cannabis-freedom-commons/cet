The DNCD and the Public Ministry announced the operations on Saturday 7 March
2026. [@1] In Esperanza, Valverde province, officers carried out simultaneous
raids on drug storage and distribution points in residential areas. [@1] The two
men detained in Esperanza were described as suspected drug distributors. [@2] At
a house on calle Federico Jesús García in the Amina sector, tactical units
arrested Guillermo Minier Mayol, alias “Tony Motora”, a high-value DNCD target
with three previous records for selling and trafficking drugs. [@1]

In the house, officers seized 465 grams of green plant material presumed to be
marijuana. [@1] They also seized 575 grams of a white substance presumed to be
cocaine. [@1] Officers found four precision scales used for weighing and
dividing the drug. [@1] They also took 327,790 Dominican pesos (more than USD
5,000) that they believe came from the day's drug sales. [@1] A second team
raided a house in El Manguito, in the El Palo Damajagua sector, and arrested
Eliazar Domínguez, alias “Romeo”, who was under surveillance over retail drug
sales. [@1] There they found 106 grams of cocaine and 19 portions of crack
weighing about 16 grams, plus 14,800 pesos, two mobile phones and another scale.
[@1] Both men will be prosecuted under Law 50-88 on drugs and controlled
substances. [@1]

In a separate operation in Punta Cana, La Altagracia, DNCD agents intercepted a
46-year-old man on Avenida Ted Kheel. [@1] He abandoned his Mazda CX-5 and tried
to flee on foot before officers caught him a few metres away. [@1] A suitcase in
the boot held 13 packages of suspected cocaine. [@1] Authorities said
intelligence linked him to a network that recruits “mulas” to send drugs to the
United States, Europe and Canada through the country's international airports.
[@1]
