Two suburban men were arrested by police on March 10. [@1] The two men were
Miguel Moctezuma III, 21, of Wheeling, and Brian Ayala Solis, 21, of Palatine.
[@1] They were held in Illinois while awaiting extradition to Indiana. [@1] Both
men were charged with dealing marijuana, possession of an altered firearm and
resisting police. [@1]

About 100 pounds of marijuana and 10,000 THC vape cartridges were seized after
the police chase. [@1] The SUV was loaded with packages of marijuana and THC
products. [@1] Officers also recovered two handguns, one of which had the serial
numbers removed. [@1]

Lake County sheriff's officers began chasing the SUV on the Indiana Toll Road
for traffic violations just before 1 p.m. [@1] The vehicle continued west on
I-90 into Chicago's South Side. [@1] The pursuit ended near 63rd Place and South
Lowe Avenue in Englewood when the SUV reached a dead end. [@1] The driver put
the vehicle in reverse and hit a Lake County police vehicle, trapping an officer
inside. [@1] The officer was evaluated at a hospital and later released. [@1]
The two men allegedly tried to run away but were caught. [@1]
