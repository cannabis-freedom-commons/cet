National police arrested Jordi Yúnior Severiano, 23, and Rangel Merete
Rodríguez, 30, both residents of Puerto Plata, in connection with the killing of
19-year-old Tristan Primeau-Pointras of Sainte-Julienne, Quebec. [@1] The two
were first stopped while riding a motorcycle with a substance presumed to be
marijuana, National Police spokesman Colonel Diego Pesqueira said. [@2] Toronto
Sun, citing the same spokesman, said that on Wednesday they were initially
arrested for narcotics possession and then questioned about the shooting. [@3]

During that stop officers seized a small blue bundle containing more than 90
portions of a green plant material and RD$905 in cash. [@2] Global News, citing
the police release, described the same find as a small blue package with more
than 90 portions of suspected marijuana and $905 in local currency. [@1]

Primeau-Pointras, who was staying at the Riu Bachata hotel, died from a gunshot
wound, police said. [@1] Police said the brothers were intercepted on the Puerto
Plata–Maimón highway for a robbery, and that one of the detainees allegedly
fired the shot that killed him while both suspects were on a motorcycle. [@1]
The victim’s brother picked the two men out of a lineup, and police said they
remained in custody and would be brought to justice while the investigation
continued. [@1] Los Mocanos, quoting Colonel Pesqueira, said the crime the two
are accused of took place on 23 March. [@2]
