City Crime Branch sleuths of Mysuru city police arrested four people for
allegedly selling ganja. [@1] The four taken into custody were Amjad Pasha, Jiya
Khan, Mohammed Saqlain and Syed Baig. [@2] Officers intercepted a Swift car
during a vehicle check and found it was carrying ganja intended for sale and
distribution. [@2]

Police seized 5.32 kg of ganja, which they valued at Rs 8.65 lakh. [@2] A
four-wheeler reportedly used to transport the ganja was also seized. [@1] The
ganja and the vehicle were seized and a case was registered at Udayagiri Police
Station. [@2]

The arrests came under the Vimukthi campaign, Mysuru city police's mission to
make the city drug-free. [@1] The operation was led under City Police
Commissioner Seema Latkar and DCP (Crime and Traffic) K.S. Sundar Raj. [@2] The
same police announcement also reported a separate arrest of a Kerala man on
suspicion of MDMA peddling, a synthetic drug excluded from the cannabis figures.
[@1]
