Two foreign men were detained in Tapachula, Chiapas, on suspicion of belonging
to the Cártel de Chiapas y Guatemala (CCyG). [@1] They were identified as Juan
“N”, a Venezuelan, and Enrique “N”, a Guatemalan. [@1] The arrest was made by
the Guardia Estatal Preventiva, the Fuerza de Reacción Inmediata Pakal (FRIP)
and the Guardia Estatal Fronteriza of the Secretaría de Seguridad del Pueblo
(SSP), in coordination with the Fiscalía del Estado and the armed forces. [@1]
Officers found them during preventive patrols at 20 Avenida Norte and 5ta Calle
Poniente, at the Malecón in Tapachula. [@1]

Officers seized 200 small transparent nylon bags of green and dry herb with
characteristics similar to marijuana. [@2] They also seized 1,000 small nylon
bags of white granular powder resembling crack, known locally as piedra. [@1] A
black revolver-type pistol loaded with one live .44-calibre cartridge was also
confiscated. [@2] The two men told the SSP they belonged to the Cártel de
Chiapas y Guatemala, which maintains operations in the state. [@1]

Both were placed at the disposal of the corresponding Ministerio Público, the
public prosecutor, to determine their legal situation. [@1]
