Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar's city crime branch arrested three more people from
Raigad district in the hydroponic ganja case in which two college students were
earlier caught with contraband worth Rs 28.5 lakh. [@1] Police identified the
newly arrested as a 33-year-old BBA student from Panvel, a 20-year-old LLB
student from Indiabulls in Panvel and a 20-year-old student from Chhatrapati
Sambhajinagar. [@1] The arrests followed the March 10 detention of a 20-year-old
first-year engineering student and a 23-year-old MBA finance student, who were
intercepted near Baba Petrol Pump with 285 grams of hydroponic ganja. [@1]

Police said the operation began around 10.20am on March 10 after a tip-off about
two youths transporting drugs along the Lohandi Pool-Baba Petrol Pump stretch.
[@1] The team recovered eight zip-lock pouches of hydroponic ganja from a
plastic carry bag wrapped in brown tape. [@1] The 285 grams was valued by police
at Rs 28.5 lakh, with the drug fetching nearly Rs 10,000 a gram on the illegal
market. [@1] A digital weighing scale, two cellphones and a motorcycle were also
confiscated, taking the total seizure to Rs 29.51 lakh. [@1]

Police commissioner Pravin Pawar said the investigation revealed financial links
between the initially arrested students and suspects in Panvel. [@1] Pawar said
interrogation showed the ganja was supplied through a contact from Panvel, and
financial transactions with his associates prompted teams to go to Raigad
district. [@1] Acting on technical analysis and leads from questioning, a crime
branch team traced the suspects to housing societies in Kongao, Panvel, where
the three were detained and later confessed their role, officers said. [@1] All
three were brought to Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar and handed over to the Kranti
Chowk police station for further probe. [@1] Police said they were still trying
to identify the main supplier and others involved in the racket, and the
investigation is being led by Sub-inspector Kishor Tanpure of the Kranti Chowk
police station. [@1]
