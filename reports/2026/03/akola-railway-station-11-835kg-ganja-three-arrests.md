Ramdaspeth police in Akola seized 11.835 kg of ganja and arrested three men from
neighbouring Buldhana district near the railway station under the district's
ongoing Operation Prahar. [@1] The three were aged 21, 22 and 23 and lived at
Dongav in Mehkar taluka of Buldhana district. [@1] Police put the total value of
the recovery, including two mobile phones, at 3.21 lakh rupees. [@1]

Officers acted on a tip to police inspector Shirish Khandare that three people
would arrive at the railway station area carrying ganja in two bags. [@1] The
Hindi-language account dates that tip to 27 March 2026. [@2] A police team under
superintendent of police Archit Chandak laid the trap near the chief health
inspector's office at the railway station square. [@1] The operation was
supervised by additional superintendent of police BP Reddy and sub-divisional
police officer Sudarshan Patil, with Anti-Narcotics Task Force (ANTF) staff from
Amravati taking part and the Hindi account putting the recovery at 3,21,875
rupees. [@1]

A case was registered at Ramdaspeth police station under NDPS Act sections 8(c)
and 20(b). [@1] The Hindi-language account gives the crime number as 300/2026.
[@2] Police said further investigation would establish where the ganja came from
and the network it was meant for. [@1]
