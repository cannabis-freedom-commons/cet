Two people were arrested during a search in Barú district, Chiriquí province, by
anti-narcotics agents and the Western Brigade together with the drug
prosecutors' offices for Chiriquí and Bocas del Toro. [@1] One of those detained
was confirmed to have an active arrest warrant issued by a court in Chiriquí
province. [@1]

The Barú raid produced 20 homemade cigarettes containing presumed marijuana,
along with 39 fragments of a white rocky substance presumed to be crack, a
different drug not counted in the cannabis figures. [@1] In Río Madroño, Chepo,
border patrol units conducting verification patrols found an abandoned sack
containing several rectangular packages. [@1] Inspection showed the sack held 14
packages of presumed marijuana, which was sent to Panama's drug prosecutor's
office. [@1]

The operations were reported by units of the Servicio Nacional de Fronteras, or
Senafront, Panama's National Border Service, working in coordination with the
Ministerio Público, the Public Prosecutor's Office. [@1] Authorities said
investigations were continuing and that operations across the country would be
reinforced as part of the action against crime. [@1]
