Police within the Penamaluru station limits in Vijayawada seized banned
substances and arrested two people, including a history-sheeter linked to a gang
war case. [@1] The arrested men were identified as Ananth Kumar and Mahesh. [@1]
Notably, Ananth Kumar is already an accused in a previous gang war case
registered under Patamata police station limits, raising concerns over his
continued involvement in criminal activities. [@1]

Police acted on specific intelligence. [@1] Police recovered 5 kg of ganja from
the accused. [@1] In the same operation, police also recovered 1 gram of cocaine
from the accused. [@1] Officials believe the duo was engaged in illegal drug
distribution in and around Vijayawada. [@1]

Both suspects were produced before court and remanded to judicial custody. [@1]
Authorities are now probing deeper into the network to identify possible links
to larger drug supply chains. [@1] The seizure underscores the police
department's ongoing efforts to curb narcotics and maintain law and order in the
city. [@1]
