Police seized more than 17 kilograms of cannabis during a joint intelligence
operation north of San Pedro Town on Tuesday morning. [@1] San Pedro Police
carried out the intelligence-led operation on Tuesday 24 March 2026 in northern
Ambergris Caye. [@2]

Around 8:30 a.m. officers searching near a seaside mangrove area found a large
black crocus bag holding twenty-nine black plastic-wrapped rectangular bricks.
[@1] The cannabis weighed 17.4 kilograms, about 38.36 pounds. [@1] No one was at
the scene and no arrests were reported. [@2] The cannabis was labelled, sealed
and officially recorded as found property while further investigations
continued. [@1] Officers searched the surrounding area for further contraband or
suspects and found neither. [@2] 7 News Belize, citing a police report, said the
bricks were identified as cannabis. [@3]

Investigators were working to determine where the drugs came from and where they
were meant to go. [@4] Police consider northern Ambergris Caye a hotspot for
drug trafficking because its limited development and low population make it
vulnerable. [@2] Police have said traffickers use "wet drops", releasing
packages at sea so that they drift ashore and are collected, with the drugs then
sold locally or moved on, often towards Mexico. [@2] The San Pedro Sun noted
earlier finds in the same northern area, including a September 2024 cache of
drugs and rifles and a February 2025 operation in which three men were charged
over firearms. [@2]
