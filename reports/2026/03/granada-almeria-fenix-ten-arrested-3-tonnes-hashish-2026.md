Customs Surveillance officers of Spain's Tax Agency and the Guardia Civil broke
up a hashish-trafficking gang in Granada and Almería provinces in a joint
operation named Fénix. [@1] Ten people were arrested in total, and five of them
were remanded in custody after appearing before the Civil and Instruction
Section of the Court of First Instance in Motril. [@2] They were held on
suspicion of drug trafficking, membership of a criminal organisation, illegal
possession of weapons, theft and use of a motor vehicle and falsification of a
public document. [@1] Officers seized seven vehicles, alongside a modified
shotgun, two handguns and an air gun. [@1]

Officers carried out seven searches, five in Granada province and two in Almería
province, at the suspects' homes and in warehouses, farms and storage rooms the
gang used to keep drugs and vehicles. [@1] In those searches officers seized 72
bales of hashish weighing almost 3,000 kilograms and arrested six people. [@1]
In the vehicles they found a modified shotgun loaded with a cartridge in the
chamber and two handguns also loaded, one of them ready to be used. [@1] As part
of a money-laundering investigation, officers asked for the freezing of movable
and immovable assets used by the gang and valued at more than 500,000 euros.
[@1]

The investigation began in December 2025 to identify the people behind the
trafficking. [@1] In mid-March cameras of the SIVE external surveillance system
detected a fast boat approaching the Granada coast, and investigators set up a
joint operation to intercept it. [@2] The boat landed on a beach in the eastern
part of Granada province, where gang members began unloading the bundles into
several vehicles before fleeing when they saw the police. [@1] Officers then
raided a premises the gang used to store the drugs and arrested four members of
the organisation there. [@1] The gang brought the hashish from Morocco in
high-speed boats, hid it for a time on the Granada coast and then moved it to
Europe disguised in different forms of transport for sale. [@1]

The seizure is one of the largest from high-speed boats on the Costa Tropical in
recent years. [@1]
