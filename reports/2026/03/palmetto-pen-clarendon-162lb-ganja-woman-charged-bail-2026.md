Clarendon police arrested a woman after searching a house in Palmetto Pen,
Clarendon on 13 January 2026. [@1] Officers found about 162 pounds (73.5
kilograms) of ganja, uncustomed cigarettes and bulk quantities of rum. [@1]
Police put the cash seized at about $5 million. [@1] The operation took place
sometime after 2:30 p.m. [@1]

The woman was later charged under the Dangerous Drugs Act, along with possession
of criminal property and breaches of customs regulations. [@2] She appeared in
court on 24 March 2026 and was granted $800,000 bail. [@2] The court required
her to report regularly to the May Pen police, surrender all travel documents
and return to court on 28 April 2026. [@2]

The raid was part of ongoing efforts by the Clarendon police to curb drug
trafficking and other criminal activities in the parish. [@1] Police urged
community members to report suspicious activities to help keep the parish safe.
[@1]
