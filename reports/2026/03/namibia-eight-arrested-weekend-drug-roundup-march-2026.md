Namibian police arrested several suspects in separate drug-related incidents
reported in Khorixas, Lüderitz, Windhoek, Rehoboth, Kamanjab and Keetmanshoop
over the weekend. [@1] In Lüderitz, two Namibian men aged between 25 and 35 were
arrested on Friday after being caught attempting to smuggle cannabis hidden in
food into the police station. [@1] Another suspect was arrested that day in
Spokiesdorp after police found a bag of cannabis buried in the ground during a
search. [@1] Two men were arrested at a Lüderitz police station later the same
day after allegedly attempting to smuggle cannabis hidden in food. [@2]

In Khorixas, a 34-year-old Namibian woman was arrested on Friday after police
found a parcel containing cannabis in a vehicle during a search. [@1] In
Windhoek, a 47-year-old Namibian man was arrested on Friday at a roadblock
between Windhoek and Rehoboth after police found him in possession of cannabis
during a search. [@1] In Rehoboth, a 25-year-old Namibian man was found in
possession of cannabis during a search in Block E on Saturday. [@1] In Khorixas,
a 34-year-old woman was arrested after cannabis was allegedly discovered in a
parcel inside a vehicle during a search. [@2]

In Kamanjab, a 25-year-old Namibian man was arrested on Saturday on the
Kamanjab–Outjo C40 road after police found him in possession of cannabis and
mandrax tablets. [@1] Police reported that a vehicle had dropped off three men
on the roadside before the search was conducted. [@1] Another suspect was
arrested along the Kamanjab–Outjo C40 road after police allegedly found cannabis
and mandrax tablets in his possession. [@2] In Keetmanshoop, a 26-year-old
Namibian man was arrested on Saturday after he was found with mandrax tablets
and cannabis that he attempted to throw over a yard wall. [@1] All suspects are
in police custody and are expected to appear in the respective magistrates’
courts. [@1]
