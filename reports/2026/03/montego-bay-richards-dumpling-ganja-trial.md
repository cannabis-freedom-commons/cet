Kenneth Richards is to stand trial over ganja hidden under a dumpling at the
Montego Bay Freeport lock-up. [@3] Richards, 43, appeared before Judge Natiesha
Fairclough-Hylton at the St James Parish Court on 11 March 2026. [@1] The trial
was set for 20 April 2026. [@2] The court clerk said chain-of-custody and escort
statements, plus a report from the investigating officer, were still pending.
[@1] The judge refused a request for an 8 April case-management date because the
case had been before the court since 18 June 2025. [@1] Richards' bail was
extended. [@1]

On 12 June 2025, Richards arrived at the lock-up saying he was delivering a meal
for an inmate. [@1] During a security check, officers found a small plastic bag
with approximately eight ounces of ganja, as well as tobacco, hidden under a
dumpling. [@3] The package was seized before it could reach the cell block. [@3]
When cautioned, Richards allegedly said, “Oh God, a mi uncle put it in deh, but
mi tek responsibility.” [@3] He was arrested and charged. [@1]

He pleaded not guilty on 18 June 2025 to possession of ganja, dealing in ganja
and introducing a prohibited article into a correctional institution. [@3] The
case was adjourned to 30 July 2025 for mention, with bail extended. [@3]
