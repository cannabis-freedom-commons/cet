Federal police carried out two raids in Cipolletti's Anai Mapu area, dismantling
two bunkers used to store and sell drugs. [@5] Two women and two men were
detained and placed at the disposal of the intervening federal court. [@5]
Federal police dismantled two marijuana sale points in the city of Cipolletti,
Río Negro. [@1] One property was in the Anai Mapu neighbourhood, and
surveillance led investigators to a second house in 4 de Agosto. [@1]

Officers seized 188 doses of marijuana ready for sale, seven Cannabis sativa
plants, cash and a precision digital scale. [@1] Officers also secured two
mobile phones to be examined for links to other people in the trade. [@1] The 4
de Agosto house was also used to store marijuana that supplied both sale points.
[@1] With the evidence gathered, the case moved forward and the searches were
ordered. [@3]

The inquiry began in December 2025, when the General Roca federal prosecutor's
office detected suspicious movements in residential areas of Cipolletti. [@1]
Detectives used surveillance and intelligence work to confirm the illegal
operation. [@2] The searches were authorised by the Federal Guarantee Court of
General Roca, headed by judge Hugo Horacio Greca. [@4] The case was led by
federal prosecutor Sebastián Gallardo with assistant prosecutor Francisco E.
Iglesia Frezzini. [@4]

The seized items were placed under judicial custody in a case opened for
infringement of Law 23.737, Argentina's drug law. [@1] The investigation was to
continue to establish whether more people were involved or the network had
branches in other Cipolletti neighbourhoods or nearby towns. [@1]
