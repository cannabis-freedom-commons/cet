Federal police in Rosario detained a 30-year-old Argentine man after a
prosecutor ordered searches of properties linked to him. [@1] Investigators said
the man presented himself publicly as a legal adviser and had no known lawful
job. [@1] The investigation was driven by the specialised micro-trafficking unit
of Santa Fe's public prosecutor's office, led by César Pierantoni. [@1] The man
and the seized items were placed at the disposal of the examining magistrate for
violating drug law 23.737. [@1]

Officers of the Argentine Federal Police's Federal Investigations Department
seized more than 10 kilograms of high-quality cannabis sativa at a sophisticated
indoor nursery in the south of the province. [@1] Police reported 219 cannabis
sativa plants of potent, high-THC varieties known on the illegal market as
“cannabis premium”, along with four complete indoor nurseries, irrigation and
lighting systems, 200 pots, a car, a bill-counting machine, four mobile phones
and documents. [@1] They also seized 7,600 dollars and 914,000 pesos in cash.
[@1] Another account of the same raid reports 6,665 grams of ready-to-sell buds,
3,690 grams of marijuana, 69 flowering plants and 155 seedlings, according to
the Rosario federal police. [@3] That account puts the combined weight of the
seized cannabis at 10,355 grams, or more than 10 kilograms. [@3]

The indoor operation ran from a flat in the Fonavi housing complex in Barrio
Latinoamérica, Rosario. [@2] The flat had been adapted for indoor production
with technology intended to maximise quality and yield. [@1] Once the drug was
ready the man used a delivery system to distribute it to other neighbourhoods in
his own car. [@1] Investigators also found a second home a few blocks away used
for other illicit activity. [@1] The intelligence work began in January after
field work by Rosario's anti-drugs division. [@1]
