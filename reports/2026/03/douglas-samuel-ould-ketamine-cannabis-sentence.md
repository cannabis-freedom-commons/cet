A Liverpool man who attempted to smuggle ketamine to the Isle of Man was jailed
for 22 months. [@1] Douglas Courthouse heard 23-year-old Samuel Ould was a foot
passenger on the morning Manxman sailing to Douglas from Heysham in Lancashire
on 14 December 2025 when he was stopped for a routine search. [@1] Ould pleaded
guilty to importing ketamine, possession of and with intent to supply ketamine,
and importing cannabis. [@1] Ould was also placed under an exclusion order,
banning him from returning to the island within five years of his release from
prison. [@1]

Suspicions grew when he started shaking and changing his story about why he was
travelling to the island. [@1] Officers subsequently found between £20,056 and
£25,070 of ketamine in a plastic bag in his backpack. [@1] The court also heard
Ould, of no fixed address, had £44 of herbal cannabis concealed in his
underwear. [@1] When interviewed by detectives after the drugs were found, he
replied "no comment". [@1]

His lawyer argued that he had been paying off a debt at the time and felt he was
at risk if he did not commit the offence. [@1] Deemster Graeme Cook said he
hoped the "deterrent sentence" would send a message that ketamine was not wanted
on the island. [@1] Deemster Cook said despite there being a "relatively low
uptake" in ketamine on the Isle of Man, the amount reaching the island had
increased year-on-year. [@1] He said organised crime groups operating in the
north-west of England had been taking advantage of higher profit margins on the
Isle of Man. [@1]
