Three people from Ottawa were arrested and each faced charges under the Cannabis
Act, the Controlled Drugs and Substances Act and the Criminal Code. [@1] The
RCMP's Federal Policing Central Region Serious and Organized Crime Unit said it
arrested and charged the three people. [@2] All three were released on an
undertaking and were due to appear in an Ottawa court on 5 May 2026. [@1]

Police searched two homes in Ottawa south on 10 March and seized 485 kilograms
of dried cannabis, 36 kg of hash, 18 kg of psilocybin mushrooms, more than 7,000
edibles, unstamped tobacco and other products worth an estimated $5.5 million.
[@1] Police also seized $115,360 in cash and a vehicle. [@1] The RCMP said the
two search warrants were executed on 10 March 2026 at two Ottawa south
residences. [@2] The RCMP put the approximate street value of everything seized
at over $5.5 million. [@2]

The investigation began when Canada Post found a parcel bound for the Northwest
Territories containing a large quantity of cannabis and other cannabis
byproducts. [@1] The RCMP worked with Canada Post, the Canada Border Services
Agency and FINTRAC to identify a network shipping products through a website
called Culture Cannabis Canada. [@1] Through the website the network shipped
cannabis, cannabis-based products and psilocybin to numerous provinces and
territories across Canada. [@1] Staff Sgt. Martin Gauthier said most of the
edible products seized were marketed for young children. [@1]
