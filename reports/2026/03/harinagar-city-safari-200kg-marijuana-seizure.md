Police in Sunsari seized 200 kg of marijuana at Harinagar Rural Municipality-3
in Sunsari during the night before the 12 March 2026 publication. [@1] Deputy
Superintendent of Police Keshav Kumar Thebe of the District Police Office in
Sunsari provided the information about the seizure. [@1]

The seized marijuana was packed in eight plastic sacks. [@1] Officers recovered
the sacks from a City Safari vehicle bearing registration number Pr 1-02-004 Ha
5639. [@1] The City Safari was travelling from Gautamapur towards Harinagar when
the recovery was made. [@1]
