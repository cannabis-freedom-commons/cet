Across Namibia, police arrested several suspects over the Independence weekend
for possession of illegal drugs including cannabis, mandrax tablets and cocaine,
in a series of stop-and-search operations. [@1] An adult man was arrested at
Osire on Saturday after being found with bankies of cannabis during a search.
[@1] He was held in police custody and expected to appear before the Otjiwarongo
Magistrate's Court on Monday. [@1] At Mururani village in the Kavango West
Region, a 29-year-old Namibian man was arrested on the same day after police
allegedly found cannabis in his possession. [@1] He remained in custody. [@1]

At Aranos, two Namibian men aged between 17 and 19 were arrested on Friday in
the Nuwerus location. [@1] The pair were found with cannabis and mandrax tablets
during a police stop-and-search operation. [@1] In Mondesa, Swakopmund, a
26-year-old Namibian man was arrested on Saturday after police allegedly found
him with cannabis, mandrax tablets and cocaine. [@1] In Mariental, a 20-year-old
Namibian man was arrested on Friday after being found with bankies of cannabis.
[@1] Police said the arrest followed intelligence-led information that led to a
search of a vehicle in which the suspect was an occupant. [@1] Near Karasburg on
the B3 main road, a 63-year-old Namibian man was found with cannabis during a
stop-and-search operation on Friday. [@1] He was expected to appear in the
Karasburg Magistrate's Court. [@1]
