Venezuela's National Police seized 1,122 panela bricks of marijuana from a small
boat at Playa Salina de Jacuque, on the Paraguaná peninsula in Falcón state.
[@1] The load weighed 583 kilograms, according to the official report. [@1] The
four men aboard were detained: Walter Enrique Ojeda Barrandica, a Colombian, and
the Venezuelans Benito Alexander Gonzales Ramos, Elimetec José Torres Urbano and
Michael Misael García. [@1] The seizure was carried out on Saturday 14 March
2026, Falcón citizen security secretary Miguel Morales Miranda said. [@5] The
Falcón citizen security secretariat said the operation was carried out at Punta
Jacuque, in Los Taques municipality on the Paraguaná peninsula. [@4]

Officers also seized the peñero, a small open fishing boat, two outboard motors
of 75 and 40 horsepower, four mobile phones, a GPS device and 180 litres of fuel
in eight 60-litre drums. [@5] Experts from the Bolivarian National Guard's
criminalistics laboratory identified the substance as cannabis sativa, the
security secretary said. [@2] The officers belonged to the National Police's
directorate for aquatic spaces. [@1] The detainees and the seized items were
placed at the disposal of the Public Ministry. [@5]

The Interior Ministry published details of the operation on its Instagram
account. [@3]
