Three residents of Urgut district in Samarkand region, aged 34 and 37, were
detained with 14.7 kilograms of opium and 14.9 kilograms of hashish. [@1] The
three had been under observation and had travelled by bypass routes to
Tajikistan's Pendzhikent district, where they bought the drugs from dealers,
before being caught on their return to Uzbekistan. [@2]

Officers seized 14.9 kilograms of hashish and 14.7 kilograms of opium from the
men. [@1] In a separate case, officers found a hiding place with 9 kilograms of
opium extract among marble stones in a private garage in Samarkand's Orzu
mahalla. [@1] State Security Service officers and internal affairs officers
carried out the garage search. [@4] The service put the two seizures together at
more than 38 kilograms of drugs. [@1] Officers were still working to detain the
owner of the garage. [@3]

Criminal cases were opened over both finds under part 2 of article 246 and part
5 of article 273 of the criminal code. [@2] The three detained men were placed
in custody. [@2] The service said the offences carry up to 20 years in prison.
[@1]
