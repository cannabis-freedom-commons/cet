Officers of the Central Criminal Police Department of Georgia's Interior
Ministry detained five people on drug charges. [@1] The Interior Ministry
announced the detentions in a Facebook post. [@1] The five were detained on
suspicion of illegally acquiring, storing and selling narcotic drugs in large
and especially large quantities. [@1] They were also accused of illegally
planting and cultivating drug-containing plants. [@1]

Searches were carried out in the suspects' flats and at places they pointed to.
[@1] During the searches officers seized a significant amount of drugs,
including heroin, methadone, tetrahydrocannabinol, plant cannabis and raw and
dried marijuana. [@1] Officers also seized materials used to package drugs. [@1]

The investigation is being conducted under articles of the Criminal Code that
provide for up to 20 years in prison or life imprisonment. [@1]
