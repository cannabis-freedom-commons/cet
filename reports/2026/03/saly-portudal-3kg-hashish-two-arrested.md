The urban police station of Saly-Portudal in Senegal broke up a drug-trafficking
network on the night of 14-15 March 2026. [@1] The security operation, carried
out by the Brigade de recherches in the Saly-Tapée sector, seized 3 kilograms of
hashish and detained two people. [@1] The investigation began when police
detained the guard of a villa at the Les Cottages residence, who was found with
a small quantity of drugs he was trying to sell. [@1] At the guard's home
officers found 20 grams of hashish and three cannabis joints. [@2] The guard
admitted he had taken that quantity from the garage he was guarding. [@2]

A search of the villa uncovered 29 blocks of hashish hidden in a refrigerator,
with a total weight estimated at 3 kilograms. [@2] The real owner turned out to
be the son of the villa's owner, who admitted buying the drugs for 5 million CFA
francs at a party in Nguérigne. [@1] The son was arrested after his mother had
initially been questioned and made a full confession. [@2] He said he was the
sole owner of the drugs and that his family knew nothing of his illicit
activities. [@2]

At the end of their custody the guard and the owner's son were referred to the
prosecutor's office at the Mbour High Court. [@1] They were prosecuted for
criminal association and domestic drug trafficking. [@3]
