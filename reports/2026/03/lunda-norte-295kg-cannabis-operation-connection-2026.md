Angola's National Police seized 295 kilograms of a drug of the cannabis type in
Lunda-Norte province during Operation Connection. [@1] Domingos Muanafumo, head
of the national police's institutional communication and press department,
announced the seizure in a press statement. [@1]

Police said the operation's seizures were made in the municipalities of
Xá-Muteba, Xá-Cassau, Lóvua, Lucapa, Mussungue, Chitato, Dundo and Cambulo. [@1]
Officers seized 218 diamond stones of various carats for illegal possession and
trafficking. [@1] They also seized 571,000 kwanzas and $1.5 million in cash.
[@1] Officers confiscated 15,994 litres of petrol and diesel that were being
smuggled to the Democratic Republic of Congo. [@1]

Police detained 782 Congolese nationals for attempting to enter Angola illegally
at border posts in the province. [@1] Eighty-two of those detained were
children. [@2] The detainees were repatriated after legal procedures. [@2]
