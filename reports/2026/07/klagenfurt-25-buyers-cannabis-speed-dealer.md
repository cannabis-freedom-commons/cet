national on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@2] The criminal investigation unit

He had been in the investigators' sights for some time before the arrest. [@4]

[@2] He has been held in Klagenfurt prison since the arrest. [@2]

Investigators kept working on the case after the arrest. [@3] They identified 25
buyers of the 22-year-old and reported them to the prosecutor. [@1]

Police said most of the drug deals were arranged through the messaging service
Snapchat. [@3] The man was suspected of selling several different drugs. [@1]
