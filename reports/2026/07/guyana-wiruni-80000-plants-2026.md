Guyana Police ranks destroyed a cannabis farm at Wiruni Village on the Upper
Berbice River on Tuesday 21 July 2026, burning narcotics worth about G$31.1
million. [@1]

A police headquarters release said one field with a combined area of about six
acres (roughly 2.4 hectares) was found at the site. [@1] About 80,000 suspected
cannabis plants, between two and five feet tall, were growing on the field. [@1]
Officers also found about ten pounds (roughly 4.5 kg) of dried cannabis and a
20-foot by 10-foot makeshift camp. [@1]

The plants were uprooted and burned with the dried cannabis and the camp, and
police put the value of the destroyed narcotics at G$31,186,328. [@1] No arrest
was made, and police said investigations were continuing. [@1]
