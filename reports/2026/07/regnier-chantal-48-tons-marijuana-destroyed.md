On Friday 10 July 2026, agents from the Bureau for Combating Drug Trafficking
(BLTS) and the Coast Guard of the Haitian National Police (PNH) in the South
Department destroyed a stock of previously seized drugs in the town of Regnier
in the commune of Chantal. [@1] The marijuana part of that stock weighed more
than 4.8 tons, stated precisely as 4,842.6964 kg. [@1] Officers destroyed 57.2
kg of cocaine together with the marijuana at the same site. [@1] The item was
headlined as the destruction of 5 tons of marijuana, rounding the precise
marijuana weight. [@1] The units involved were the South Department elements of
the BLTS and the PNH Coast Guard. [@1]

The destroyed drugs had been seized as part of a series of operations conducted
by the PNH since 2017. [@1] Those operations were conducted in the southern part
of the country. [@1] The report therefore presents the 10 July operation as the
disposal in Regnier of drugs accumulated from earlier southern seizures. [@1]
