Portland police arrested Brian Wayne Groves on 15 July 2026 after officers found
him on a property he had been banned from. [@3] Officers had been sent for a
trespass check and recognised him from an earlier trespass warning for the same
property. [@1]

A search of his shirt pocket found two marijuana cigarettes. [@1] His backpack
held about 7.3 ounces of marijuana, 10 unidentified pills believed to be
methamphetamine and five packages of suspected illegal kratom. [@1]

Groves was taken to the Sumner County Jail. [@1] He was charged with criminal
trespass, manufacture, delivery, sale or possession of methamphetamine,
possession of a Schedule VI controlled substance with intent to distribute, and
synthetic derivatives or analogues of methcathinone, the charge the accounts
list for kratom. [@2] WNKY reported that the investigation was ongoing. [@2]
