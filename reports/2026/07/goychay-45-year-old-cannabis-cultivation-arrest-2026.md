Officers of the Goychay District Police Department detained 45-year-old Amil
Vakhidov in Goychay during operational measures on suspicion of cultivating
plants containing narcotic substances. [@1] The press service of Azerbaijan's
Ministry of Internal Affairs reported the detention. [@1] The measures leading
to the detention were carried out by the district police in Goychay. [@1]

At Vakhidov's place of residence, officers discovered cannabis bushes growing on
the property. [@1] Officers also found marijuana at the same residence during
the search. [@1] Vakhidov stated that he had personally provided the
narcotic-containing plants with special care. [@1] He said that this tending had
been performed in accordance with agrotechnical rules for the plants. [@1]

Police opened a criminal case over the alleged cultivation of the
drug-containing plants. [@1] A court subsequently imposed the preventive measure
of arrest on Vakhidov, placing him in pretrial detention. [@1]
