Police officers from the Khazar District Police Department detained Hatam
Sultanov in the Şüvəlan settlement on suspicion of illegal drug trafficking, the
Interior Ministry's press service said. [@1] The operation was carried out by
officers of the Khazar District Police Department in the settlement. [@2] During
a search of the household plot attached to the house where Sultanov lives,
officers found and seized 46 cannabis bushes grown by cultivation, with a total
weight of 232 kilograms. [@1]

A criminal case was opened over the find. [@1] A court ordered that Sultanov be
held in pre-trial detention. [@2] Police were continuing measures against the
illegal circulation of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances and against
the cultivation of drug-containing plants. [@1] The ministry said the
investigation was continuing. [@2]
