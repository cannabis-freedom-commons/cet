Mexico's Secretariat of the Navy destroyed two clandestine marijuana plantations
in an operation it reported in the last week of July 2026. [@1] The two plots
held around 291,000 marijuana plants, which were eradicated by burning. [@3] No
detentions were reported. [@1]

The plantations covered 12,550 square metres in total, about 1.26 hectares. [@2]
The navy estimated the weight of the burned plants at about 44.5 tonnes. [@2]
Sailors collected representative samples and handed them to the authorities for
the investigation file. [@2] The material was to be used as evidence to
establish who owned the land and any possible criminal responsibility. [@4]

The navy said the plots were in the south of Tijuana municipality, in Baja
California. [@2] AGP Noticias, an Ensenada outlet, placed the plantations at
different points in Ensenada municipality instead. [@1] The navy described the
destruction as part of standing operations to locate and disable drug-production
sites and to weaken criminal groups. [@4]
