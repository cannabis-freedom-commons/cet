Argentina's federal police detained a man in San Carlos de Bariloche after
raiding a house that investigators said was used to receive and store drugs sent
by post. [@1] The case began during preventive checks on postal shipments at a
courier company, where a narcotics dog marked two parcels as suspicious. [@1]
With judicial authorisation investigators opened the packages, found wrappings
of marijuana buds and from them identified the addressee and the address under
investigation. [@1]

Officers of the División Unidad Operativa Federal San Carlos de Bariloche
carried out the raid on the order of the city's Juzgado Federal de Primera
Instancia, with the Sede Fiscal Descentralizada taking part. [@3] In the house
they seized more than a kilo and a half of marijuana buds, three mobile phones
and 9,200 US dollars in cash. [@1] The seeds taken from the house came from
different cannabis genetics, among them strains of the 'Jamaicana' type. [@2]

The man detained during the raid, described as the principal suspect, was left
at the disposal of the federal justice on suspicion of an offence under the drug
law. [@4] The investigation remains with the federal justice, which must gather
further evidence on the extent of the operation and the man's responsibility.
[@1]
