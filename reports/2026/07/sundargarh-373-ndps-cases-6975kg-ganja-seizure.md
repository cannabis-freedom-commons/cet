Between 2024 and July 2026, Rourkela Police, Sundargarh Police and Rourkela
Railway Police in Odisha's Sundargarh district registered a total of 373 cases
under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act. [@1] A total of
569 people, including 526 men and 43 women, were arrested in connection with
drug trafficking and peddling and subsequently forwarded to court. [@1]

During the two-and-a-half-year period, enforcement agencies seized 69 quintals,
75 kg and 207 grams of ganja, about 6,975.2 kg, alongside 657.089 grams of brown
sugar that is excluded from the cannabis total. [@1] They also confiscated 44
vehicles allegedly used for transporting narcotics. [@1] In 2024, police
registered 105 cases and arrested 141 people. [@1] Seizures during the year
included 21 quintals, 50 kg and 436 grams of ganja and 20.730 grams of brown
sugar. [@1] The crackdown intensified in 2025, with 181 cases registered and 308
people arrested. [@1] Police seized 29 quintals, 44 kg and 582 grams of ganja
and 279.059 grams of brown sugar during the year. [@1] Between January and July
2026, another 87 cases were registered, leading to the arrest of 120 people.
[@2] During this period, enforcement agencies seized 18 quintals, 80 kg and 189
grams of ganja and 357.300 grams of brown sugar. [@1]

Investigators have identified trains as a major mode of narcotics transportation
into the district. [@1] Drug traffickers are increasingly using railway networks
to ferry ganja and brown sugar. [@1] However, intensified checking by the
Railway Protection Force (RPF) and Government Railway Police (GRP) at railway
stations has led to the detection and arrest of a significant number of drug
carriers. [@1] Despite sustained efforts by the police administration to crack
down on drug mafias and curb the illicit trade, smuggling activities continue
unabated. [@1]
