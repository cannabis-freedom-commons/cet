The Umbutfo Eswatini Defence Force (UEDF), Eswatini's army, seized and recovered
E3.4 million worth of dagga, stolen livestock, a smuggled vehicle and undeclared
goods during cross-border security operations. [@1] The largest item was
2,600.238 kilograms of dagga with an estimated street value of E2,600,238. [@1]
The drugs were handed over to the Royal Eswatini Police Service (REPS) for
further investigation and legal action. [@1]

The figures came from the Ministry of Defence and National Security's First
Quarter Performance Report for the 2026/27 financial year, in which the total
value of controlled items reached E3,411,786.43. [@1] The ministry said the
operations reflected intensified action against cross-border crime along
Eswatini's borders with South Africa and Mozambique. [@1] The report also
recorded the recovery of 185 goats valued at E148,000 and eight cattle worth
E80,000 that were being smuggled. [@1] Soldiers also intercepted one smuggled
vehicle valued at E500,000 and confiscated undeclared goods worth E83,548.43,
including clothing, alcohol and electronic items. [@1]

The Ministry noted that informal border crossings remain higher than the use of
official border posts, driven by factors such as economic hardship, conflict and
the search for better living conditions. [@1] The Ministry said the results
demonstrated the UEDF's continued commitment to protecting the country's borders
by combating drug trafficking, livestock rustling, vehicle smuggling, arms
trafficking, illegal immigration and other forms of transnational crime. [@1]
