Police arrested a youth in Thamel, Kathmandu, with a vape mixed with ganja. [@1]
The arrested person is 38-year-old Kabir Magar, a resident of Lalitpur
Metropolitan City Ward No.-1. [@1] The arrested man is a non-resident Nepali,
and police recovered an Australian passport from him. [@1]

Five vapes recovered from the accused on Ashadh 25 tested positive for THC, an
element found in ganja, SP Durgaraj Regmi of the Narcotics Control Bureau told
Ratopati. [@1] Police tested the recovered vape in the laboratory, and the
accused is being investigated in a drug case. [@1] Online Patrika carried the
same account from SP Durgaraj Regmi of the Narcotics Control Bureau, naming the
five vapes and the THC finding. [@2]
