The truck driver was identified as Rajesh Yadav, 45, a resident of Chhatarpur
police station area in Palamu. [@1] He was booked under relevant sections of the
Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act and sent to judicial custody on
Sunday. [@1] A police team led by Hussainabad sub-divisional police officer
Divyansh Shukla intercepted the truck on the Japla-Chhatarpur main road at
Gamharia. [@1]

Police seized 320 kg of cannabis, estimated to be worth Rs 1.5 crore, from a
Uttar Pradesh-registered truck in Palamu district late Saturday. [@1] The
contraband was found concealed in a specially built wooden compartment inside
the vehicle. [@1] The compartment contained 10 plastic bags carrying a total of
320 kg of cannabis. [@1] A sample tested positive for cannabis using a
drug-detection kit. [@1] The stopped truck bore the number UP-14 FT-6583. [@2]

Palamu SP Kapil Choudhary received specific information on Saturday night that a
truck carrying cannabis was heading towards Chhatarpur from Bihar via
Hussainabad-Japla. [@1] Police are investigating the source and intended
destination of the consignment. [@1] They are also trying to identify others
involved in the suspected interstate drug-trafficking network, including those
responsible for loading and receiving the consignment. [@1] Legal formalities
were completed before samples were sent for FSL examination. [@2]
