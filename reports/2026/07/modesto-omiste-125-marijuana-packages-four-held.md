An anti-drug interdiction operation in the department of Potosí ended with the
seizure of 125 packages of marijuana and the apprehension of four people after
FELCN officers intercepted two vehicles during patrols. [@1] FELCN, Bolivia's
special anti-narcotics force, intercepted two suspect vehicles during an
interdiction patrol in Modesto Omiste province in the department of Potosí. [@2]
The police intervention took place in Modesto Omiste province, where officers
identified suspect vehicles and searched them with representatives of the Public
Ministry present. [@1] As a result of the operation, four people were
apprehended and brought before the Public Ministry, which is to determine their
legal situation within the corresponding process. [@1]

During the inspection of the vehicles, officers found six light-blue yute sacks
containing 125 packages of plant material. [@2] A field test was positive for
marijuana, so the load was seized and held for the corresponding investigations.
[@2]

FELCN said further inquiries would establish the origin and destination of the
controlled substance and the degree of involvement of those held. [@3]
