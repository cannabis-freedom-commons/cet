Nicaraguan soldiers of the Ecological Battalion BOSAWAS detained three
Nicaraguan men in the community of El Romero, 118 kilometres northeast of Siuna
in the North Caribbean Coast Autonomous Region. [@1] The army named the three
men as Elvin Walter Borge Centeno, 30, Jorbin Exequiel Castellón, 19, and
Alexander Joel López Castro, 18. [@1] The army's information note places the
detention within an operation that ran from 5 to 16 July 2026. [@2]

Troops took two firearms, two magazines, 53 cartridges, two hand grenades, seven
pounds (about 3.18 kg) of marijuana and five pounds (about 2.27 kg) of marijuana
seed from the three men. [@1] They also confiscated two portable radios with a
charger and clothing reserved for military and police use. [@1]

The men, the weapons, the supplies and the drugs were handed over to the
relevant authorities for judicial proceedings. [@1] Radio 580, reproducing the
army statement, placed the detentions within actions between 5 and 16 July in El
Romero. [@3] The army dated its information note to 21 July 2026 at the BOSAWAS
battalion headquarters. [@2]
