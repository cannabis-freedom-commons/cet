Azerbaijan's State Border Service said it stopped the smuggling of 78 kilograms
250 grams of narcotics and 1,990 narcotic-containing tablets from Iran into
Azerbaijan. [@1] In the Horadiz border detachment's service area, officers found
6 kilograms 380 grams of narcotics, comprising 5 kilograms 530 grams of
marijuana and 850 grams of opium, plus 1,990 Metadon M-40 psychotropic tablets.
[@1] In the Lankaran border detachment's service area, officers found 71
kilograms 870 grams of marijuana. [@1]

The two marijuana quantities together amount to 77.4 kilograms of cannabis. [@2]
The 850 grams of opium and the 1,990 methadone tablets are not counted in the
cannabis total. [@2] The State Border Service described the operation as
preventing smuggling across the border from Iran. [@2]

The 1news.az account was published at 14:47 on 8 July 2026 and gives no separate
date for the operations. [@1] Operational and investigative measures on the
cases were continuing. [@1] Neither account names a detained person or reports a
court outcome, with the cases described as under ongoing operational and
investigative measures. [@2] The English-language account notes that Azerbaijan
frequently conducts border security operations against narcotics transiting the
region. [@2]
