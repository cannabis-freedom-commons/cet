Trinidad and Tobago police dismantled an indoor cannabis cultivation operation
at an abandoned two-storey house in Gasparillo on Monday 27 July 2026. [@1] The
operation, called Operation Tripod, ran from 3:30 p.m. to 7:30 p.m. and involved
officers from the Gasparillo Police Station and the Central Emergency Response
Patrol (ERP) Unit. [@1] Acting on intelligence, officers searched the house
after smelling marijuana and found a room with LED grow lights, a drying tent
and plants at different stages of growth. [@1]

They seized 30 adult cannabis trees, 31 medium-sized plants, 46 juvenile plants
and 38 seedlings, along with two grow bags of small plants. [@1] Two crocus
bags, the woven sacks used for bulk goods, held dried cannabis, and five sealed
zip-lock bags held processed marijuana. [@1]

A law enforcement source said it was not a casual grow site but a structured
operation designed to produce large quantities of marijuana. [@1] Police said
investigations were continuing as officers worked to identify those responsible
for the farm, and no arrest was reported. [@1] The police service said such
seizures were critical in reducing the supply of illegal narcotics and curbing
related criminal activity. [@1]
