Three people were arrested in Tbilisi by the Central Directorate of Criminal
Police of Georgia's Interior Ministry on suspicion of illegally buying and
storing an especially large quantity of drugs as an organised group,
facilitating their sale and illegally cultivating plants containing narcotics.
[@1] Investigators said some of the defendants had been growing the plants in a
specially equipped greenhouse. [@2]

Searches of the suspects, their homes and places they identified yielded more
than 13 kilograms of cannabis and 7 kilograms of marijuana prepared for
distribution. [@1] Police also seized materials needed to package and grow the
drugs. [@1] The Ministry of Internal Affairs described the total quantity seized
as nearly 21 kilograms of narcotics. [@3]

The investigation was opened under Articles 260, 260¹ and 265¹ of Georgia's
Criminal Code, offences punishable by up to 20 years' imprisonment or life
imprisonment. [@3]
