Officers of the Interior Ministry drug-control service and Severnaia railway
customs carried out joint operations that stopped the criminal network. [@1]
During a special operation in Bishkek post offices, officers detained two
citizens who were collecting parcels containing drugs. [@1] The group had built
a supply channel for cannabis-group drugs from Thailand into Kyrgyzstan through
international mail. [@1]

Officers seized 7 kilograms 560 grams of marijuana that had arrived from
Thailand from the detainees. [@1] Police preliminarily valued the seized drugs
at more than 400,000 US dollars on the black market. [@1] Investigators
established that the consignment was intended for onward transfer to Kazakhstan.
[@1]

A criminal case was opened under Article 282 of the Kyrgyz Criminal Code on
illegal drug production for sale. [@1] Investigative actions are under way. [@1]
Further operations continue to identify other members of the criminal group and
drug-trafficking channels. [@1]
