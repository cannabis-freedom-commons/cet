One man identified only by his first name, Matheus, was arrested in flagrante on
the night of Monday 6 July 2026 in São Tomé de Paripe, a neighbourhood in the
Subúrbio Ferroviário of Salvador, Bahia. [@1] According to the military police,
the arrest took place in a locality known as Alto das Fontes during an operation
by the 19ª CIPM, the 19th Independent Military Police Company. [@1] The suspect
was approached by the teams while the officers were carrying out patrols in the
region. [@1]

During the search, the police found portions of cocaine, crack and marijuana on
the man. [@1] The military police said Matheus also had a prior record for the
crime of homicide. [@1]

Matheus and all the material seized were taken to the Central de Flagrantes in
the Barris district of Salvador, where the occurrence was registered as a
flagrante, meaning an arrest in the act. [@1] He remained at the disposal of the
justice. [@1]
