Police arrested three men in Tobago after seizing 35 cannabis trees during an
intelligence-led anti-crime operation on Monday. [@1] The suspects were a
36-year-old electrician of Pumpmill, Scarborough, a 28-year-old office
assistant, and a 41-year-old self-employed man, the last two of Friends Field
Trace, Calder Hall, Scarborough. [@1] All three were arrested on suspicion of
possession of cannabis. [@1]

Officers attached to the Tobago Divisional Task Force conducted the exercise
under Operation R.E.S.O.L.U.T.E. Shield between 12:30 p.m. and 2:30 p.m. in the
Scarborough district. [@1] Acting on intelligence received under the Emergency
Powers Regulations, 2026, officers went to Friends Field Trace, Calder Hall,
Scarborough, where they discovered and seized the cannabis trees. [@1] Officers
from Task Force Area West carried out the operation with assistance from
personnel attached to Task Force Area North and the Scarborough Police Station.
[@1]

The operation formed part of the TTPS' ongoing efforts to target illegal drug
cultivation and trafficking across the country through intelligence-driven
policing initiatives. [@1] Police did not indicate whether charges had been laid
against the three men up to the time of the media release. [@1] Investigations
into the matter are continuing. [@1]
