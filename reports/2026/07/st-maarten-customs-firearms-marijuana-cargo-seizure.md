St. Maarten Customs officers intercepted concealed firearms and a significant
quantity of suspected marijuana during a recent cargo inspection. [@1] Justice
Minister Nathalie Tackling revealed the seizure during Wednesday's Council of
Ministers press briefing, commending Customs officers for their vigilance and
professionalism. [@1] The firearms had been carefully concealed inside modified
items in an apparent attempt to evade border inspections. [@1]

Officers also discovered what was described as a significant quantity of
suspected marijuana during the same cargo operation. [@1] Both the weapons and
narcotics were immediately confiscated and transferred to the appropriate
investigative authorities for further criminal investigation. [@1] Officials
have not disclosed how many firearms were recovered or where the shipment
originated. [@1]

Tackling said the interception demonstrates the critical role Customs officers
play as the country's first line of defense against organized crime. [@1]
Customs officers conduct routine inspections on incoming cargo to identify
illegal firearms, narcotics and other prohibited goods before they can enter the
community. [@1] Justice officials say the combined efforts of Customs,
Immigration, Police and other law enforcement agencies constitute an integrated
strategy to disrupt criminal networks operating through the island's ports of
entry. [@1]
