Police in Mpumalanga arrested two men aged 42 and 47 for possession of dagga
with an estimated street value of R1.2 million on the N4 near Middelburg. [@1]
Mpumalanga police spokesperson Lieutenant Colonel Jabu Ndubane said the Ermelo
K-9 unit acted on information about a bakkie transporting dagga. [@2] Officers
spotted the vehicle on the N4, stopped it and searched it. [@2] They found about
204 kg of dagga wrapped in sellotape and concealed inside the vehicle. [@2] The
bakkie was confiscated for the investigation. [@2]

The two men were arrested at the scene and charged with possession of dagga.
[@2] They appeared in the Middelburg Magistrate's Court on 22 July 2026. [@3]
Both men were remanded in custody after bail was not granted. [@4] The case was
postponed to 27 July 2026 for further investigation. [@4] Ndubane said the
arrests showed the police's commitment to disrupting the transport and
distribution of illicit drugs in Mpumalanga. [@2]
