Four Israelis were among eight foreign nationals arrested after Greek
authorities found cocaine, MDMA, cannabis, pills, and cash aboard a catamaran
near Lefkada. [@1,2] The other four arrested were citizens of Turkey, Italy, Spain and Latvia. [@3] A search of the boat found more than 55 grams of cannabis, about 10 grams of crystalline methamphetamine or MDMA, more than 8 grams of cocaine and more than a dozen ecstasy pills. [@2] The vessel was described as a party boat carrying illegal
drugs near a Greek island. [@1]

The Hellenic Coast Guard raided the catamaran near the Greek island of Lefkada.
[@1,3] Officers seized cash, passports, suspected drugs, and electronic devices in
the raid. [@1] Authorities also confiscated about 7,000 euros, cash in three currencies including shekels, mobile phones, a laptop and the boat itself. [@2]
