A 19-year-old woman from Chișinău was detained when she came to collect the
parcel of drugs. [@2] TV8 reported that the woman was a native of Bender and was
detained in the act as she collected the parcel. [@1] Officers of the Centru
Investigations Directorate and criminal investigators of the National
Inspectorate of Investigations, working with PCCOCS prosecutors, uncovered a
case of smuggling 1 kg of cocaine and 1 kg of hashish from Spain. [@2] The drugs
were hidden among other parcels in a minibus that arrived in Chișinău from
Spain, inside two metal tins that each held a vacuum-packed bag. [@2]
Prosecutors announced the case on 6 July, saying the parcel held 1 kg of cocaine
and 1 kg of hashish. [@1] NOI.md reported that the operation was carried out by
officers of the Centru Investigations Directorate, the National Inspectorate of
Investigations and PCCOCS prosecutors. [@4]

A court placed her in 30 days' preventive detention at the PCCOCS prosecutors'
request. [@2] Investigators believe she was responsible for receiving the drugs,
after which she divided them into smaller doses for sale through online shops on
Telegram. [@2] TV8 reported that investigators considered her more than a
courier, saying she was to portion the drugs and prepare them for sale through
anonymous Telegram shops. [@1] Esp.md reported that officers detained the
19-year-old Chișinău resident as she collected the parcel. [@3] Police said the
criminal investigation continues and officers are establishing the role of other
possible accomplices. [@1]
