Judiciary Police officers arrested a 26-year-old Malaysian man named Akhmal, who
said he was jobless, for smuggling cannabis at Macau International Airport on
Saturday. [@1] Following a tip-off, they identified him on a flight from
Southeast Asia and, working with the Macao Customs Service, waited at the
airport for his arrival. [@1] On Saturday afternoon, as he collected his
luggage, officers found six bags of cannabis weighing 3.4 kilograms in his
suitcase. [@1]

Judiciary Police spokesman Ho Wai Lok put the estimated street value at about
3.41 million patacas. [@1] Ho said Akhmal was acting for an overseas
drug-trafficking syndicate, had received the drugs abroad, smuggled them into
Macau and was to forward them to neighbouring regions. [@1] Akhmal confessed he
would have been paid 7,000 ringgit, about 14,000 patacas, for the job. [@1]

He was transferred to the Public Prosecutions Office the same day facing a
drug-trafficking charge. [@1] Police said they would continue to investigate
leads in the case and pursue any other suspects who may be involved. [@1] A
second account said he arrived from Southeast Asia with six packages of
marijuana concealed in his suitcase and was preparing to move the drugs to a
neighbouring region. [@2]
