A Bolivian judge ordered 180 days of preventive detention at the Palmasola
rehabilitation centre for Marisol S. C., a sergeant in the Bolivian Police
charged with alleged direct participation in trafficking and commercialising
controlled substances. [@1] The same precautionary measure was applied to the
sergeant's mother and her brother, a first-year student at the police academy.
[@1] The judge ordered preventive detention for all three at Palmasola after
weighing evidence presented by the prosecution and FELCN, citing procedural
risks and sufficient grounds for the investigation. [@2] The decision followed
the prosecution presenting its first evidence, gathered in an operation at a
property where more than 50 kg of marijuana was found. [@3]

FELCN, Bolivia's special anti-drug force, carried out the operation at a home in
the Magisterio Sur neighbourhood, in the Los Lotes area of Santa Cruz de la
Sierra. [@1] The sergeant tried to escape by jumping over her home's perimeter
wall but was captured while hiding in the scrub of an adjoining vacant lot. [@1]
She was initially taken to official premises as a witness; after her statement
the Public Prosecutor's Office widened the investigation and ordered her formal
arrest. [@1] She belonged to the 2023 "Jinetes" promotion, had about three years
of service and was posted to DIDIPI, the police department for internal
investigation. [@1]

Prosecutor Julio César Porras said officers found about 40 packages weighing
around 50 kg of marijuana. [@1] The home was treated as a suspected drug
collection centre, where officers found 40 to 45 brick-shaped packages weighing
roughly 50 to 55 kg hidden in a refrigerator and in jute bags mixed with onion
heads. [@1] Officers also seized police uniforms and identity documents, a
Toyota Tacoma pickup, a Bajaj motorcycle and a mobile phone, and sealed the
property. [@1] Prosecutors said the investigation remained open to establish the
drug's origin, destination and possible distribution network. [@2]

The mother was also placed under preventive detention and investigated for
alleged involvement in storing and commercialising the drug found at the home.
[@3]
