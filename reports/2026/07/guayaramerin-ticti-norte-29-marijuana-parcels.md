Officers of the Fuerza Especial de Lucha Contra el Narcotráfico (Felcn),
Bolivia's special force against drug trafficking, seized cannabis in two
operations in Beni and Cochabamba in early July 2026. [@1] No one was arrested
in either case and investigators continued to identify those responsible for
sending and transporting the drug. [@1]

The first operation was at the bus terminal of the municipality of Guayaramerín,
Beni, as part of the '200 años al servicio de la patria – Vías Seguras'
operations plan. [@2] The detector dog 'Red' marked positively two cardboard
boxes sent from the city of La Paz. [@2] After the alert, Felcn officers opened
the packages in coordination with the Public Prosecutor's Office. [@2] They
found 29 packages of different shapes and sizes containing marijuana; field
tests confirmed the substance and the whole load was seized. [@1]

A second operation was carried out in the Ticti Norte area of Cochabamba, where
Felcn officers found seven abandoned jute bags containing marijuana. [@1] Field
tests by the investigators were positive and the drug was seized under the
procedure established by the Public Prosecutor's Office. [@1] Felcn said both
operations were part of its permanent drug-interdiction work across different
regions of the country. [@2]
