Ervin Lita, aged 28, was found in Compton Road, near Narborough Road, in the
early hours of Sunday, July 12. [@1] Mr Lita, who was also known as Ervin
Axhami, died after being found in Compton Road during the early hours of Sunday
morning (July 12). [@2] Paramedics tried unsuccessfully to resuscitate him, and
he was declared dead at 6.54am. [@1] Two men aged 36 and 27 were arrested on
suspicion of murder and remained in police custody. [@3] The police
investigation was closed on October 27 with no further action. [@1] At an
inquest on November 17, the coroner said no precise cause of death had been
found and police had found no suspicious circumstances or third-party
involvement. [@1]

Friends who had planned to spend the day in Birmingham with Mr Lita forced their
way into his home in Narborough Road South when they could not contact him. [@1]
They told police they found him unresponsive upstairs in the bathroom, lying on
his back with froth coming from his mouth. [@1] They decided to take him to
hospital but struggled to move him because of his size, and after realising he
could be dead they panicked and left him at the side of Compton Road. [@1] Mr
Lita slept in a small bedroom next to two large upstairs bedrooms where cannabis
was being grown, when temperatures that week were between 25C and 35C. [@1]
Officers traced a black Audi seen on CCTV stopping in Compton Road to a few
streets away, where the keeper’s address was empty but a large cannabis farm was
found at the property next door. [@1]

Fertilisers were also sprayed in the bedrooms next to where Mr Lita slept, which
the pathologist said could cause respiratory symptoms and difficulty breathing
if aerosolised in a confined and poorly ventilated space. [@1] Toxicology tests
found no alcohol, illegal drugs or medication in Mr Lita’s system. [@1] The
pathologist concluded the cause of death remains unascertained following a
thorough post-mortem examination supported by laboratory examinations. [@1] A
Leicestershire Police spokesperson confirmed there were no criminal proceedings
in relation to the cannabis farm at the property. [@1]
