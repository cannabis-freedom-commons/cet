Tony Paul, alias “Abra”, of Soufriere pleaded guilty to nine drug-related
offences linked to a seizure at the Vieux Fort Port. [@1] The Second District
Court ordered him to pay a $200 000 fine by 31 December 2026. [@1] The court
ordered that he will spend six years in prison if he fails to pay the fine. [@1]

The seizure followed a joint operation at the Vieux Fort Port on Monday by
officers of the Gangs, Narcotics and Firearms Unit and the Customs and Excise
Department, described in a Royal Saint Lucia Police Force statement issued on
Friday. [@1] Officers recovered controlled drugs from a package concealed in
buckets. [@1] The haul comprised five packages of suspected cannabis resin and
two packages of suspected cannabis. [@1]

Police arrested Paul and a female suspect after investigation. [@1] Both were
jointly charged with three counts each of importation of a controlled drug,
possession with intent to supply and possession of a controlled drug. [@1] After
Paul’s guilty pleas, the prosecution withdrew all charges against the woman.
[@1]
