Paraguay's National Anti-Drug Secretariat (Senad) raided a rural property at
Umbú, in the district of Capitán Bado, Amambay department, with the Public
Prosecutor's Office. [@2] Agents destroyed ten makeshift camps used to store and
process the drug. [@2] The operation was accompanied by prosecutor Celso
Morales. [@2]

Officers cleared 8.5 hectares of marijuana fields by hand. [@3] Agents destroyed
three tonnes of chopped marijuana, 500 kilograms of marijuana that was drying
and 45 kilograms of seeds. [@1] Senad put the total volume taken out of
circulation at almost 30 tonnes. [@2] Two motorcycles used for logistics inside
the property were seized. [@2]

Senad estimated that the cleared 8.5 hectares would have produced 25.5 tonnes of
marijuana. [@2] Senad estimated that the destroyed drug could be worth 4.5
million dollars on the Brazilian market. [@2] Umbú lies in Amambay, the northern
department that borders Brazil. [@1] Paraguay is one of South America's main
marijuana producers, with the crop concentrated in departments that border
Brazil. [@1]
