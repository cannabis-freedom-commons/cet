Drug Enforcement Unit officers arrested six people after seizing suspected
marijuana-infused edible treats during a targeted anti-drug operation in New
Providence. [@1] Shortly after 11:00 a.m. on Wednesday 1 July 2026, officers
acting on information went to Ruby Avenue and West Bay Street and observed a
grey Nissan Cube occupied by a 24-year-old man, a 23-year-old woman and a child.
[@1] Officers intercepted the vehicle and searched the two adults. [@1] Both
adults were arrested and cautioned in connection with the discovery. [@1] The
child was left in the care of a family member. [@1]

In the vehicle, officers found in plain view a white plastic bag containing four
clear Ziploc bags and a blue plastic bag containing fourteen clear Ziploc bags,
each holding a suspected marijuana-infused Rice Krispies treat. [@1] Later that
same day, Drug Enforcement Unit officers executed a search warrant at the
suspects' residence on Graham Drive in Yellow Elder. [@1] On the kitchen table
they found, in plain view, a clear Ziploc bag containing another suspected
marijuana-infused Rice Krispies treat. [@1] Four additional women, aged 18, 18,
27 and 48, were arrested and cautioned in connection with the matter. [@1]

In total, police seized nineteen suspected marijuana-infused edible treats with
an estimated street value of $380. [@1] Investigations were continuing. [@1]
