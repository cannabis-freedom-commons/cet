The Polícia Judiciária's Centre Directorate detained a 35-year-old man at the
Grândola service area in Setúbal district on Saturday 4 July 2026. [@1] The
force said the man was carrying a large quantity of hashish brought from Spain
and intended for Coimbra. [@1] Officers found two concealed compartments built
into the car to hide drugs. [@2]

About 70 kg was in the car and a search of a garage in Figueira da Foz found
another 30 kg. [@4] In total the force seized about 100 kg of hashish, a
precision scale, 1,300 euros in cash, two cars and computer and
telecommunications equipment. [@1]

The operation was named El Camino and the man is suspected of drug trafficking.
[@1] The inquiry is led by the public prosecutor's office in Coimbra and the
investigation continues. [@3] He was taken to court for his first judicial
interrogation and held in pre-trial detention awaiting trial. [@4]
