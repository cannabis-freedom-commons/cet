City police and task force teams arrested seven people in separate operations at
Jubilee Hills and Erragadda and seized about 25 litres of hash oil valued at
₹3.12 crore. [@1] Police commissioner V.C. Sajjanar said five alleged peddlers
and two consumers were taken into custody in two anti-drug operations linked to
an Odisha supply network. [@1]

In Erragadda, task force and Borabanda police arrested Lasangi Yakobu alias
Venkat and Yendrapalli Suri Babu at a rented house and seized 23.706 litres of
hash oil worth ₹2.96 crore and two mobile phones. [@1] The pair allegedly
brought the oil from Odisha suppliers to Hyderabad by train and were preparing
to carry it to Bengaluru when intercepted. [@1] In Jubilee Hills, police
arrested B. Chinnayya Padal, P. Bharath Kumar and Mohd Shoaib and detained
consumers B. Kalyan Teja and T. Uday Kumar at Road No. 10, seizing 1.250 litres
of hash oil worth ₹16 lakh, 75 small containers and a bike. [@1] Officials said
Bharath Kumar and Shoaib repackaged the oil into small containers and sold it
locally for profit. [@1]

The Jubilee Hills operation was carried out by the Secunderabad Zone Task Force
with the Jubilee Hills police, who intercepted the group near Road No. 10. [@2]
Telangana Today put the combined seizure at 24.956 litres and said the
operations took place on Sunday. [@4] The accused were produced before a court
and sent to judicial remand. [@3] A case was registered and further
investigation was under way. [@2]
