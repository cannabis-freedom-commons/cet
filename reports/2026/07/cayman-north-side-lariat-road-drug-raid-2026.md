A man and a woman were arrested after a raid on a house on Lariat Road, North
Side, netted suspected ganja and cocaine, cash and an unlicensed firearm. [@1]
The 33-year-old man and the 36-year-old woman were at the house when police and
customs officials arrived with a search warrant. [@1] The operation took place
on Sunday 5 July, when Royal Cayman Islands Police and Customs and Border
Control officers searched along Lariat Road. [@2] A man, 33, and a woman, 36,
were arrested on suspicion of drug offences and taken into police custody. [@2]

The search warrant was executed at about 3pm under the Firearms Act and the
Misuse of Drugs Act. [@3] Officers recovered quantities of suspected ganja and
cocaine and a quantity of cash at the residence. [@3] A wider search of a nearby
premises and of several vehicles parked on and outside it found separate
quantities of suspected ganja and an unlicensed firearm. [@3]

Police said the items were seized for investigative purposes and the matter
remained under investigation. [@2]
