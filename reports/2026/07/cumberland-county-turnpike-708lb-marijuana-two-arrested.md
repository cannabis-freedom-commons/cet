Pennsylvania State Police pulled a rented Ram 3500 van over on I-76 West in
Lower Mifflin Township around 9 p.m. on 9 July 2026 for going 99 mph in a 70 mph
zone. [@2] The driver was 19-year-old Yackov Bitan of Lakewood, New Jersey, who
was not licensed to drive, and the passenger was 34-year-old Yosef Marchette of
Toms River, New Jersey. [@2] Police said the drugs were seized and Bitan and his
passenger, 34-year-old Yosef Marchette of Toms River, New Jersey, were arrested.
[@2]

Troopers searching the van found it was hauling 708 pounds of marijuana and 200
THC vape cartridges, with a street value of more than $3.3 million. [@3] A later
national account described the same seizure as more than 300 kilograms (660
pounds) of marijuana plus about 200 pills of another illegal narcotic, and put
the estimated street value at more than $3.3 million. [@1]

Both men were charged with possession of a controlled substance with intent to
deliver. [@2] Both were arraigned with bail set at $500,000 each and were held
in the Cumberland County Jail pending a preliminary hearing. [@2]
