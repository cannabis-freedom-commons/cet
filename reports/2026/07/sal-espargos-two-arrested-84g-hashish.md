Two foreign nationals from Guinea-Conakry and Guinea-Bissau were placed in
preventive detention on Sal island, Cabo Verde, after a Judicial Police
operation that seized hashish, money and other materials. [@1] The suspects were
a man and a woman aged 31 and 32, detained in the city of Espargos on Sal
island. [@2] They were detained in flagrante delicto on the night of Friday 10
July 2026. [@2] Police said they were strongly suspected of high-risk drug
trafficking. [@1] After detention they were brought for a first judicial
interrogation and the Sal District Court ordered preventive detention. [@1]

During searches investigators seized 33 doses of a substance with a gross weight
of 8.4 grams that tested positive for hashish in a screening test, plus a blade
fragment with residues of the same drug. [@2] Officers also seized 15,305 Cape
Verdean escudos and 10.40 euros in cash, a knife, three packs of rolling papers
and two mobile phones deemed relevant to the investigation. [@2]

The operation was led by the Sal Criminal Investigation Department through its
drug-trafficking unit BICTE, in coordination with the Public Prosecutor,
executing seven search-and-seizure warrants. [@2] Police appealed for anonymous
public tips through the free hotline 134. [@3]
