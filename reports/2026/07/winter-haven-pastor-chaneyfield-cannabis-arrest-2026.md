Polk County sheriff's deputies arrested 42-year-old Winter Haven pastor Timothy
Chaneyfield on multiple felony charges including sexual offences against a
14-year-old boy. [@1] Chaneyfield was arrested after getting off a plane at
Orlando International Airport on 29 July 2026. [@3] Deputies charged him with
possession of marijuana and five counts of possession of drug paraphernalia
alongside lewd-battery, travel, custody and HIV-disclosure counts. [@3] Sheriff
Grady Judd publicly called Chaneyfield a “dope-smoking pedophile preacher”. [@2]

The investigation began after a cybertip from Instagram alerted the Polk County
Sheriff's Office to illegal activity between an adult and a child. [@1]
Chaneyfield first contacted the 14-year-old on a dating app before moving
communication to Instagram. [@1] He picked up the teen near the boy's
neighbourhood on three occasions and drove him to his Eagle Lake home for sex,
without disclosing his HIV-positive status before unprotected sex. [@1] The
14-year-old was given an HIV treatment cocktail to try to prevent infection,
while investigators found a 26-year-old man who had sex with Chaneyfield and has
since tested positive for HIV. [@1]

Chaneyfield worked for the Polk County school system from 2012 to 2024 as a
substitute teacher, paraeducator and network manager, including time at Crystal
Lake Middle School. [@1] He was also a former employee of Compassionate Care
Hospice, which said it is cooperating fully with law enforcement. [@1]
