Jordan's Anti-Narcotics Department arrested 17 drug dealers and promoters while
dealing with eight cases across the Kingdom over the days before 21 July 2026,
the Public Security Directorate said. [@1]

In the Capital Governorate, two drug promoters were arrested with nine blocks of
hashish. [@1] In the Southern Badia, three dealers were tracked while
transporting narcotics to the capital, intercepted on the road and arrested,
with 13 blocks of hashish, 1,000 narcotic pills and a firearm found inside the
vehicle. [@2] In the Central Badia, officers raided a tent where three dealers
were selling narcotics, arrested all three and seized 36 blocks of hashish. [@1]
In Karak Governorate a dealer was raided and arrested with 35 blocks of hashish.
[@2] In Ma'an, officers tracked and arrested a man after information that he was
cultivating marijuana plants inside his home, and seized the plants. [@1]

A campaign in Madaba Governorate led to three arrests and the seizure of
quantities of narcotics. [@2] In the Capital Governorate three promoters were
arrested after ambushes, with one kilogram of crystal methamphetamine, narcotic
pills and a firearm seized. [@1] A dealer's house in Al-Mafraq was raided and
20,000 narcotic pills found buried in a pit in the courtyard. [@2]
