Agents of the National Police's Anti-Extortion and Terrorist Associations
Division (DAET) and the Metropolitan Prevention Unit UMEP-8 arrested two
suspected Pandilla 18 members in the colonia Gracias a Dios, in the Rivera
Hernández sector of San Pedro Sula. [@1] Police named one of them as Joshua
Enrique Guzmán Alfaro, 20, known as El Terrifer. [@1] The National Police
described him as an alleged leader of the Pandilla 18. [@3] The police report
gave his rank in the gang as Homie. [@1] A 17-year-old known as El Negro was
detained with him. [@1]

Officers seized 574 transparent plastic wrappers of dried plant material
suspected to be marijuana. [@1] They also seized two 9 mm pistols with their
magazines, 18 unfired rounds, 10 rounds of prohibited ammunition and mobile
phones. [@1]

The two were handed to the duty prosecutor's office, the Fiscalía de Turno, on
suspicion of drug trafficking, illegal possession of a permitted firearm,
criminal association and possession of prohibited ammunition. [@1] Both were
held in police custody and then taken to the prosecutor's office while the
investigation continues. [@2] Guzmán had been detained three times before, in La
Lima in 2023 and 2024. [@2] The 17-year-old, described in the police report as a
paisa firme, had been detained in April 2026 for drug trafficking. [@1]
