On 9 July 2026 Mexico's customs agency ANAM, acting through Guadalajara customs
with the National Guard, reported seizing about 2.5 kilos of seeds with cannabis
characteristics hidden in an export shipment. [@1] Customs officers detected
five cardboard boxes holding two voodoo dolls, each stuffed with seeds described
as resembling cannabis, concealed in the export consignment. [@2] The seizure
occurred at Guadalajara customs, located in the municipality of Tlajomulco de
Zuniga in Jalisco near the Miguel Hidalgo international airport. [@1]

The concealment was intended to disguise the seeds as an ordinary shipment. [@1]
Inspection determined the parcel originated in Sinaloa state and was declared
for Santa Cruz, Bolivia. [@1]

After the find the merchandise was handed to National Guard personnel for
referral to the Attorney General's Office, which will pursue the investigation.
[@2] No detentions were reported in connection with the seizure. [@3]
