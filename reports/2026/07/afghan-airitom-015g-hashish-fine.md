A court fined an Afghan citizen for attempting to smuggle hashish into
Uzbekistan after the drug was found as he crossed the state border. [@1] On 10
July he crossed the Uzbek-Afghan border through the Airitom customs post, and
during the inspection customs officers found 0.15 grams of hashish in the pocket
of his vest. [@2]

A forensic chemical examination confirmed the seized substance was a narcotic,
and during the preliminary investigation the accused fully admitted guilt. [@1]
The Termez district court found the foreigner guilty of smuggling narcotic
substances. [@1] In passing sentence the court took into account his admission
of guilt, remorse, lack of previous convictions and his status as a foreign
citizen, and fined him 8.24 million sums. [@1]

The bail he had previously deposited will be used to pay the fine. [@1] The
seized hashish will be destroyed after the judgment takes legal effect. [@1]
