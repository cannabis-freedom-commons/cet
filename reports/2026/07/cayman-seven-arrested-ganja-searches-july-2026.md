Royal Cayman Islands Police Service officers arrested five people on Friday
evening 10 July 2026 and two more on Saturday 11 July, all on suspicion of
drug-related offences. [@1] Shortly after 8pm on 10 July, police stopped a
vehicle travelling in central George Town. [@1] They searched the vehicle and
its four occupants, recovered a quantity of suspected ganja and arrested the
occupants on suspicion of drug-related offences. [@1]

Following enquiries, officers searched a residential premises in Windsor Park
and arrested a wanted man observed in one of the units. [@1] A search of his
residence recovered a quantity of suspected cocaine and he was further arrested
on suspicion of that offence. [@1] An extensive search of the premises and
connected areas recovered a suspected firearm, additional suspected ganja and
drug paraphernalia. [@1] All parties connected to the address were placed under
investigation over possession of the suspected firearm. [@1]

On Saturday 11 July, officers executed another search under the Misuse of Drugs
Act at a residential address in West Bay. [@1] They recovered another quantity
of suspected ganja and arrested two people present at the residence on suspicion
of drug-related offences. [@1] Police said the wanted man was not
attempted-murder suspect Michael Gollab, who remained wanted. [@1] All items
were seized for investigative purposes and the matter remained under police
investigation. [@1]
