Agents of the National Drug Control Directorate (DNCD), the airport security
corps CESAC and the Public Ministry seized almost 30 pounds of suspected
marijuana at Las Américas International Airport in Santo Domingo. [@1] According
to its identification tag, the suitcase belonged to a passenger who arrived in
the country on a flight from New York, United States. [@2] Anti-narcotics
agents, with military and canine-unit support, found the black suitcase while
checking one of the airport's arrivals areas. [@3] On the prosecutor's
instructions, officers opened the luggage and found two bales of marijuana
hidden among clothing. [@1]

The National Institute of Forensic Sciences (INACIF) certified that the plant
material weighed 28.98 pounds, about 13.15 kilograms. [@1] The DNCD said the
luggage had been detected during a routine inspection in the arrivals zone, and
the drug was sent for laboratory tests to confirm its composition and exact
weight. [@1] The seizure was carried out under the supervision of the Public
Ministry, which kept the investigation open to locate and arrest the passenger
responsible for the shipment. [@1] No arrest was reported, and investigators
said they had preliminarily identified the passenger and were continuing efforts
to locate and arrest him. [@4]

The DNCD said controls at airports, ports, borders and coasts had been
reinforced because of an increase in attempts to bring drugs into the country,
mainly from the United States and Europe. [@1] The Public Ministry said the
investigations would continue and asked the public to report suspicious
activity. [@1]
