The National Civil Police (PNC) reported the capture of two men who allegedly
transported drugs in a vehicle during a procedure on 4th Calle Poniente and 3rd
Avenida Norte in San Miguel Centro. [@1] The detainees were identified as Isaac
Nathaniel Benítez Rodríguez, 27, and José Ángel Córdova Márquez, 24. [@1]

During the intervention, agents seized several portions of marijuana and cash.
[@1] According to the PNC, both men will be referred to the appropriate
authorities for the offence of possession and holding of drugs. [@1]
