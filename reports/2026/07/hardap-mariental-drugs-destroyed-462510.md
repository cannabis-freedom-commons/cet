Namibian Police Force officers in the Hardap Region destroyed drugs and other
contraband worth N$462,510 at the Mariental State Hospital incinerator on a
Friday, police said. [@2] The drugs, from cases finalised between January and
December 2025, were laid out for the media in Mariental before being taken to
the incinerator. [@2] Deputy Commissioner Theodore Kotungondo said the drugs
were confiscated in various parts of the region and were linked to cases
finalised in that period. [@1]

The destroyed contraband included 600 Mandrax tablets, 2.670 kg of cannabis
skunk, 11.971 kg of pure cannabis and 400 units of crack cocaine, according to
the Hardap regional office of the Ministry of Information and Communication
Technology. [@1] Kotungondo said the drugs mostly originated from Rehoboth,
Mariental and Kalkrand. [@1] Kotungondo said the drugs destroyed on Friday were
only those linked to cases already finalised by the courts in the region. [@2]
The rest of the drugs seized in the period stay in police custody as exhibits
for pending cases and will be destroyed only once the legal process is
completed. [@2]

The police said exhibits are destroyed only after court cases are finalised and
urged residents to keep reporting drug-related activity in their communities.
[@1] Drugs with an estimated street value of N$2.6 million were confiscated in
Hardap between January and December 2025, among them 2,757 Mandrax tablets,
30.127 kg of skunk cannabis, 12.857 kg of cannabis and 400 units of crack
cocaine. [@2] Police registered 283 drug-related cases and arrested 100 suspects
in the region that year, among them 75 men, 20 women and five juveniles, and 50
of the cases were finalised, producing 44 convictions. [@2]
