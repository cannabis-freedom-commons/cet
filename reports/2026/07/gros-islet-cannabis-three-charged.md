At about 3 a.m. on 29 July 2026, officers from several Royal Saint Lucia Police
Force units carried out a joint operation in Gros Islet town and Degazon. [@3] A
search at a residence on Dauphin Street seized a quantity of suspected cannabis
and led to the arrest of 41-year-old Ronald Charles. [@2] In Degazon, officers
seized suspected cannabis plants and seedlings and arrested 27-year-old Joshua
Thomas. [@2] A firearms warrant at a residence on Notre Dame Street found a
magazine and led to the arrest of 25-year-old Linus Clovis. [@2] The Royal Saint
Lucia Police Force said the operation was part of proactive policing ahead of
the Gros Islet Carnival weekend. [@1]

Charles was charged with possession of a controlled drug and possession with
intent to supply. [@4] Thomas was charged with possession of controlled drugs
and possession with intent to supply. [@4] Thomas appeared before the First
District Court on 30 July and was granted bail of $1,500.00 for each charge,
with the case adjourned to 7 October 2026. [@1] Charles was granted bail of
$2,000.00 on the first charge and $1,000.00 on the second at his 31 July court
appearance. [@1] Charles's case was adjourned to 9 September 2026. [@1] Clovis
was charged with possession of a component part of a firearm and granted
$20,000.00 bail at a 31 July hearing. [@1] Police described the operation as an
ongoing strategy to strengthen public safety and disrupt crime ahead of one of
the island's largest annual celebrations. [@4]
