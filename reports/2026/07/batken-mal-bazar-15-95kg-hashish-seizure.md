Soldiers of the Leilek border detachment saw an unknown man on the Tajik side
throw a large bag into Kyrgyzstan in the Mal-Bazar area of Batken region. [@1]
Kyrgyzstan's State Border Service said the border guards acted on operational
information it had received. [@2] The man did not cross the border himself; he
threw the bag over and fled back into Tajik territory in the dark. [@3] The man
escaped and no arrest was reported. [@3]

Inside the bag the border guards found 1,000 US dollars and 16 packages wrapped
in yellow tape that gave off a strong, distinctive smell. [@1] A preliminary
examination identified the brown substance in the packages as Afghan chars,
described as a high-concentration form of hashish. [@3] The seized substance
weighed 15.95 kg. [@1]

After the paperwork was completed, the money and the drugs were handed to the
competent authorities. [@1] Law enforcement officers were working to identify
the person who ordered the consignment and the person who carried it. [@3]
