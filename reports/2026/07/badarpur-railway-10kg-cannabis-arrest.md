Railway Protection Force and Government Railway Police officers arrested a
20-year-old man aboard the Agartala-Deoghar Weekly Express at Badarpur railway
station in Assam's Barak Valley. [@1] Police identified him as Vishal Debbarma
of Hejamara under Sidhai police station in West Tripura district. [@1] Officers
said he was behaving suspiciously during a routine check in coach D1 of train
No. 15626 and was deboarded at Platform No. 2 of the station. [@1]

Officers recovered 10.180 kg of suspected cannabis from the man's trolley bag.
[@1] The search of his luggage was conducted in accordance with the NDPS Act,
police said. [@1] During preliminary interrogation, Debbarma allegedly said a
man named Addeya Debbarma handed him the bag in Tripura and promised Rs 2,000 to
deliver it to an unidentified person near Guwahati railway station. [@1]

He was unable to give further details about the intended recipient, police
added. [@1] Separate cases were registered under the NDPS Act and investigations
are under way to trace the supply chain and identify others involved. [@1]
