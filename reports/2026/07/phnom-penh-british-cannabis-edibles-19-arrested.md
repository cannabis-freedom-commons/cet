The Anti-Drug Crime Bureau of the Phnom Penh Police Department cracked down on
dealers and traffickers in the capital, arresting 19 people across five drug
operations over the past week. [@1] A British national was among those arrested,
accused of manufacturing cannabis edibles as part of a ring that produced
fruit-flavoured lollies, sweets, pastels and gummies laced with cannabis. [@1]
Police sent the suspects to court for further processing. [@1]

In the operations officers seized marijuana weighing over 10 kilogrammes, as
well as methamphetamine and ketamine weighing about seven kilogrammes. [@1] The
bureau said seven kilogrammes of methamphetamine alone was taken in a separate
raid that formed part of the same week-long crackdown. [@1] The operations were
carried out under the command of Lieutenant General Chuon Narin, Deputy
Commissioner-General of the National Police and Commissioner of Phnom Penh
Police, with Major General Koul Yi, in coordination with the municipal court
prosecutor. [@1]

Police in Phnom Penh arrested the British national accused of producing
cannabis-laced sweets alongside 18 others in a series of anti-drug operations
across the capital. [@2]
