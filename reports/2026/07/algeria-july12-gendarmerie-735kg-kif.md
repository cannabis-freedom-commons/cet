Units of the Gendarmerie nationale in Algiers and Tlemcen dismantled
cross-border criminal networks on Sunday 12 July 2026 and arrested at least 17
suspected drug traffickers. [@1] In a separate operation, detachments of the
People's National Army (ANP) arrested two drug traffickers in the wilaya of
Ouargla. [@1]

The gendarmerie seized six quintals and 35 kilograms of kif traité, the local
term for processed cannabis resin, amounting to 635 kilograms. [@1] Alongside
the resin, officers took 1,350 psychotropic tablets, five utility vehicles and a
motorcycle. [@1] In Ouargla the army seized one quintal of cannabis, or 100
kilograms, as well as a utility vehicle and a sum of money whose amount was not
given. [@1]

The two sets of seizures together amount to 735 kilograms of cannabis, or more
than seven quintals. [@1] The 1,350 psychotropic tablets are a separate category
and are not counted in the cannabis figures. [@1]
