Two men, both appearing to be in their 30s, were arrested in Vaitele, Samoa, on
the morning of Monday 27 July 2026. [@1] Police stopped them around 10am in
front of Lisa's Store, handcuffed them and took them into a police bus. [@1]
Close to twenty officers from TOS, the Tactical Operation Section, were seen in
the area while two police officers walked the men onto the bus. [@1] The arrests
were made in front of bystanders and members of the public who crowded in to
watch, with some filming on their phones. [@1]

Acting Police Commissioner Leiataua Samuelu Afamasaga confirmed that utensils
and at least 50 marijuana sticks wrapped in foil were confiscated from the two
men. [@1] Speaking in Samoan, Leiataua said more than 50 marijuana sticks were
found on the men. [@1] According to Leiataua, police had received reports that
the pair were selling marijuana in that part of Vaitele while disguised as
street vendors. [@1]

Samoa Police said they were continuing their fight against illicit drugs with
the backing of the Prime Minister, who is also Minister of Police. [@1] All
public sector workers, including members of parliament, have been called to take
part in voluntary drug testing. [@1]
