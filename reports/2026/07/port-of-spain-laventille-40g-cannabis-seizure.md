Port-of-Spain Division officers seized a quantity of cannabis and ammunition and
arrested one man during an intelligence-led anti-crime exercise overnight. [@1]
The operation, part of Operation R.E.S.O.L.U.T.E. Shield, ran from 10 p.m. on
Wednesday to 2 a.m. on Thursday across Gonzales, Belmont Valley Road, Snake
Valley, Laventille Road and Perseverance Lane, Plover Street. [@1]

Officers acted on intelligence and searched four houses and one abandoned
structure during the exercise. [@1] A search of the abandoned structure at Snake
Valley, Laventille, led to the discovery of 16 rounds of apparent 9mm ammunition
and 40 grammes of cannabis. [@1]

During the exercise, officers also arrested a man of Rudolph Charles Link Road,
Gonzales, in connection with larceny enquiries being conducted by the Central
Division. [@1] The exercise involved officers from the Belmont and Besson Street
police stations, supported by the Canine Unit. [@1] Investigations into the
seizure of the ammunition and cannabis are continuing, and police have not
indicated whether additional arrests are expected. [@1]
