The Police Force of Sint Maarten, with the Customs Department and the
Immigration and Border Protection Service, held a large multidisciplinary
control on Friday, July 31, 2026. [@1] The controls ran simultaneously at the
Union Road/Bellevue and Belle Plaine (Belvedere) border crossings. [@1] Officers
arrested one person for possession of marijuana. [@1] Several other motorists
were found with small quantities of marijuana, which they surrendered and
officers confiscated and destroyed on the spot. [@1] Immigration officers
checked several people to verify identity and immigration status. [@1]

Officers inspected 125 vehicles to verify that legal documents were in order.
[@1] Officers searched 67 vehicles. [@1] Officers impounded 2 vehicles for lack
of valid road tax. [@1] Officers inspected 17 scooters and found all of them
compliant with required documentation. [@1] Officers issued seven traffic fines
for various offences. [@1] Officers confiscated three prohibited weapons from
separate vehicles. [@1] The three weapons were a machete, a nunchuck and a
knife. [@1]

The operation was conducted with the knowledge of the French authorities as part
of an ongoing cross-border enforcement strategy. [@1] Customs officers with KPSM
carried out inspections under the Customs Ordinance to detect customs offences
and prohibited goods. [@1] The police said the public can expect more joint
operations under the Ministry of Justice strategy. [@1]
