Police arrested a 45-year-old resident of Holon on suspicion of drug trafficking
after raiding a warehouse that the suspect was alleged to use in the city. [@1]
Maariv reported that the 45-year-old city resident was detained for suspected
drug dealing and would be brought for a remand hearing the same day, noting that
he was already known to police in drug matters. [@2]

During the search of the warehouse, officers located about 6.7 kilograms of
material suspected to be cocaine and about 2.5 kilograms of a synthetic
cathinone drug sold under the name Doctor. [@2] In the same search, officers
additionally seized 534 grams of MDMA, about 139 grams of ecstasy, 55 grams of
hashish and small bottles containing suspected liquid drugs with a combined
gross weight of about 852 grams. [@2]

Maariv reported that the raid was carried out in the evening hours under a
court-issued search warrant after intelligence indicated that the suspect was
holding dangerous drugs in a commercial quantity. [@2] The suspect was arrested
at the scene and transferred for questioning at the Galilot police station. [@2]
He was expected to be brought to the Tel Aviv Magistrate's Court, where police
would ask the court to extend his detention. [@2]
