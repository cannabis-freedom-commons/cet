National Civil Police (PNC) officers arrested Arcángel Ernesto Zamora Castañeda,
54, during a search of a hostel in Conchalío, a coastal locality of La Libertad
Costa, on 30 July 2026. [@5] The PNC said the detention was carried out under a
search warrant. [@2] Police accused Zamora Castañeda of illicit drug trafficking
and said he would be placed at the disposal of the judicial authorities while
the investigation continued. [@4]

Officers seized two and a half packages of marijuana, cash, a digital scale and
a mobile phone, which police said would be added to the investigation. [@2] The
PNC described the detainee as possessing marijuana, money, a scale and a mobile
device for trafficking drugs. [@3] The operation took place in the coastal area
of the department of La Libertad. [@1]

Under Salvadoran law, illicit drug trafficking carries 10 to 15 years in prison
depending on the quantity and type of drug and any aggravating factors. [@1] The
authorities said possession, transport, distribution and sale of any drug are
punishable, and that the investigations seek links to wider distribution
networks. [@1]
