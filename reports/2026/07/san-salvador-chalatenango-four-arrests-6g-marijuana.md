El Salvador's National Civil Police arrested four suspected members of a
drug-trafficking structure in coordinated operations in the departments of San
Salvador and Chalatenango on Saturday 18 July 2026. [@1] Security Minister
Gustavo Villatoro said the operations dismantled a network that sought to
finance criminal activity. [@1] The ministry's report named the detainees as
Gerardo José C., Raúl Armando Arteaga V., Diego Alexander O. and Juan José H.
[@1]

Police seized 6 grams of marijuana in the operation, according to the security
ministry's report. [@1] The same report lists 2,028 grams of cocaine, five
mobile phones, two vehicles and USD 700 in cash, none of which is counted in the
cannabis figures. [@1] Villatoro put the value of the cocaine at more than USD
50,000. [@1] The four were said to have operated between San Salvador and
Chalatenango, handling the transport and sale of illegal substances. [@1]

The PNC said the operations relied on police intelligence and cooperation
between specialised units. [@1] Villatoro said the government would not let its
guard down and would keep dismantling these criminal structures and bringing to
justice those who seek to poison Salvadoran society. [@1] He said criminality is
fought not only in the streets but also by controlling the resources such
networks use to expand. [@1] The government presents the operations as part of a
series of sustained actions under its security policy. [@1] A later account,
published on 1 August 2026, also records the 18 July capture of four people in
San Salvador and Chalatenango and the seizure of more than two kilograms of
cocaine, marijuana, phones, vehicles and cash. [@2]
