Ranchi police arrested three men with 320 grams of marijuana during a late-night
anti-narcotics raid near Booty More on the Ranchi-Hazaribag Road on Saturday.
[@1] The arrested men were identified as Parmanand Ray aged 58, Lallan Sah aged
56 and Ritesh Prasad aged 45. [@1]

Police seized 320 grams of marijuana in the raid. [@1] Police also seized Rs
72,500 in cash, a motorcycle, a two-wheeler, one smartphone and two keypad
mobile phones from the men. [@1]

A case was registered under the NDPS Act at Sadar police station. [@1]
