Nikšić police checked five people on the night of 8 July 2026: three from
Nikšić, one from Podgorica and one from Kotor. [@1] A small amount of marijuana
was found on each of them, and one person was caught consuming the drug. [@1]
The five were brought to the Nikšić Security Department for further criminal
processing. [@1]

On the order of the state prosecutor at the Basic State Prosecutor's Office in
Nikšić, criminal charges were filed against DJ and IB on suspicion of
facilitating the consumption of narcotic drugs. [@1] Police said the two had
provided the marijuana to the other people for consumption. [@1] Misdemeanor
charges were to be filed against GJ, O.Đ.L. and JM under Article 26 of the Law
on Public Order and Peace for consuming narcotic drugs in a public place. [@1]

The Police Directorate said the checks took place "last night" across the
municipality of Nikšić. [@2] The statement says one of the five was caught
consuming the drug during the checks. [@2] The checks were part of police
activity aimed at suppressing drug abuse and street distribution, the Police
Directorate said. [@2]
