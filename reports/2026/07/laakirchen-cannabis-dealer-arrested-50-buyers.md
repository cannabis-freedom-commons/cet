Police in Laakirchen arrested a 20-year-old man suspected of trading cannabis,
cocaine and amphetamines in the Gmunden district of Upper Austria. [@2] The
suspect is a Romanian national. [@2] The arrest took place on 3 July 2026 during
a further suspected drug handover. [@3] After the investigation was closed, the
20-year-old was committed to Wels prison. [@1]

Police said the man had sold drugs to at least 50 people. [@1] Police said the
quantity of suspected drugs sold exceeded 25 times the legal Grenzmenge, a
threshold that depends on the substance and its active-ingredient content. [@1]
A house search turned up cash and drugs that had already been portioned and
packaged for resale. [@3]

Police said the trafficking ran from 2021 to July 2026 at various places in the
Gmunden district, mainly in the Laakirchen municipality. [@3] Police said the
investigation into the case was complete. [@2]
