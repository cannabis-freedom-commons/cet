Police in Kendua upazila of Netrokona district arrested two men in an anti-drug
operation and seized 12 yaba tablets and 50 grams of ganja from them. [@1] The
operation was carried out at about 10:30 pm on Sunday 12 July, on a tip-off, by
a team led by Sub-Inspector Md Abdul Awal of Kendua police station. [@1] The two
were detained in the Chengana area of Sandikona union in the upazila. [@1]

Police identified the detainees as Ripon Mia, 22, son of Tahur Mia of Roalbari
(Haripur) in Kendua upazila, and Rukon Mia, 40, son of Nabab Ali of Chengana in
Sandikona. [@1] Sub-Inspector Abdul Awal confirmed by telephone that the 12 yaba
tablets and 50 grams of ganja had been recovered from the two. [@1] The 12 yaba
tablets are not cannabis and are not counted in the cannabis figures for this
event. [@1] Legal action against the two detainees was under way, Awal said.
[@1] Kendua is an upazila, a sub-district, of Netrokona district. [@1]
