Oleg Seliverstov serves as a precinct police officer and juvenile-affairs
officer of the Buinsky district police department. [@1] He is a senior police
lieutenant who has served in the internal-affairs bodies for four years. [@1]
His area of responsibility covers two administrative sectors situated fairly far
from the district centre, with large and populous villages. [@1] During
Operation Mak in the summer of 2026, Seliverstov discovered wild-growing
cannabis on his beat. [@1]

The cannabis occupied thirty sotkas of land, equivalent to 0.3 hectares. [@1]
The account describes the plants as drug-containing and says they were destroyed
without delay. [@1] No suspects, arrests, plant counts or weights were reported
in connection with the find. [@1]

Seliverstov is the first police officer in his family and joined the district
police after graduating from the law department of the Buinsk veterinary college
and serving in the army. [@1] In 2026 he represented the district in the
national People's Precinct Officer competition, passing the republic-level
selection to reach its second stage. [@1]
