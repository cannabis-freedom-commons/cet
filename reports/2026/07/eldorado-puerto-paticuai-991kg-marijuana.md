Several men hurried the packages into a parked pickup on the Argentine bank as
the motorboat returned to Paraguay. [@1] The pickup's occupants ignored a stop
order and led officers on a chase before abandoning the vehicle about a
kilometre from the unloading point and escaping on foot. [@1] A check with the
Triple Frontier joint command showed the pickup had an active theft report,
adding a further offence to the federal inquiry. [@1]

Inside the abandoned pickup officers found 59 bundles containing 1,368 pressed
bricks of marijuana weighing more than 991 kilograms. [@1] Official statements
put the value of the drug at more than 3,700 million pesos. [@1]

The bundles came ashore at Puerto Paticuai, at kilometre 1,811 of the Paraná
River, from a high-power motorboat coming from Paraguay. [@1] The operation
followed criminal-intelligence work by Prefectura and the Security Ministry,
which had placed observers along the Paraná before the crossing. [@1] The
seizure was made under Plan Guacurarí, a Security Ministry programme against
drug trafficking and organised crime on the north-eastern border. [@1] The
Federal Court and Federal Prosecutor's Office in Eldorado, with Judge Miguel
Ángel Guerrero and Prosecutor Pablo Fernando Kulchar, ordered the drug and the
vehicle formally seized. [@1]
