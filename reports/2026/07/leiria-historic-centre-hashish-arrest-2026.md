PSP officers in Leiria arrested a 21-year-old man at about 18:45 on 30 July 2026
on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@1] The arrest was made in the historic
centre of the city by officers of the Leiria criminal investigation squad. [@2]

The suspect was stopped in the late afternoon carrying 4.25 grams of hashish,
which police counted as eight doses. [@3] He also had 1.72 grams of cocaine in
crack form, also counted as eight doses, which is not included in the cannabis
figures here. [@2] Officers seized 123.10 euros in cash on suspicion that the
money was linked to the trafficking. [@1]

The man was summoned to appear before the Public Prosecutor's Office the next
day and was made subject to a term of identity and residence, which requires him
to keep the authorities informed of his address. [@1] The PSP district command
said the detention followed several complaints and that it had stepped up
patrols and prevention work in the historic centre. [@1] The force described
suppressing this kind of offence as a permanent PSP priority, citing public
health and community safety. [@2]
