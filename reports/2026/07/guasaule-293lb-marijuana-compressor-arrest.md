Nicaraguan police arrested Félix Artemio Juárez Tunay, a 34-year-old Guatemalan,
during a vehicle inspection. [@1] At a news conference, police commissioner
Karen Obando said the operation took place at the El Guasaule border control
post in Somotillo, Chinandega, on the morning of Sunday 12 July 2026. [@2] The
truck was a Volvo cab pulling a Manac trailer. [@2]

Officers seized 293 pounds (about 132.9 kg) of marijuana hidden inside an air
compressor. [@1] The compressor held 246 rectangular packages of green herb that
tested positive for marijuana. [@1] The packages were wrapped in adhesive tape,
and field tests applied by experts were positive. [@4] Police said the shipment
was being carried from Mexico to Costa Rica. [@3] Officers also seized a pistol
with 14 rounds of ammunition. [@1]

The detainee and the evidence were handed to the competent authorities for
judicial proceedings. [@1] Police said the seizure formed part of
citizen-security plans and action against local marijuana supply, known as
narcomenudeo. [@2]
