Officers of the Léconi gendarmerie brigade, based in the chief town of the
Plateaux department in Haut-Ogooué province, ended the activities of a
drug-trafficking network operating in the locality. [@2] Security sources
present the operation as the fruit of meticulous intelligence work that led to
the arrest of three Gabonese nationals. [@2] Those arrested were identified as
Van Libaliba Akanga, alias Elpedo, 27, Ivan Mvouloumobena, alias Perturbé, 25,
and Glen Mouyoukou, alias Tukinkin, 24. [@1]

The operation seized 7 kg of compressed cannabis, whose market value
investigators estimated at 11 million FCFA. [@2] On 13 January 2026 the three
men were brought before the investigating magistrate of the Franceville Court of
First Instance, charged and then placed under a committal order in the town’s
central prison to await trial. [@3] Judicial authorities suspect the men
belonged to a structured cartel specialising in the detention and distribution
of narcotics in the region. [@2] A further inquiry is under way to identify
possible accomplices and establish the full extent of the network. [@2]
