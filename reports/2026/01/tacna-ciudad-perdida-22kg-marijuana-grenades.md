At 22:30 on Monday 6 January 2026 Grupo Terna officers detained the Chilean
citizen Leandro Eliecer Araya Plaza, 22, and the Peruvian Maricielo Milagros
Madriaga Pachas, 25, on Jorge Basadre Grohmann avenue at the prolongation of 2
de Mayo in Tacna. [@2] The two were detained during an operation by Grupo Terna
and the anti-drug department Depandro that ran from the night of 6 into the
early hours of 7 January. [@1] Officers found a package of dried marijuana
leaves, stems and seeds on the woman, notified the anti-drug prosecutor and
searched the house where the man lived. [@1] The house was in the Teodoro
Rodríguez Pisco association in the sector known as Ciudad Perdida, Tacna. [@1]

A search of the house found 37 packages wrapped in mustard-coloured and
transparent tape which, checked in the presence of the anti-drug prosecutor,
weighed 22 kilograms and 127 grams of marijuana. [@1] Police valued the drug
preliminarily at about 18,000 soles on the local market, saying its value would
rise sharply if it left Peru. [@1] Officers also found four pineapple-type
defensive hand grenades, classed as war material, which were seized by the
police explosives disposal unit Udex. [@2] The grenades were hidden in a
compartment inside a laundry area, according to General Helberth Luna Velarde,
head of the Tacna police region. [@1]

The woman was intercepted while buying drugs from the man. [@3] Police said the
house was used as a drug collection point, with the presumed aim of moving the
consignment towards Chile through illegal crossings. [@1] The Chilean had an
outstanding arrest warrant in Chile over drug trafficking. [@3] Police said the
Peruvian woman had no criminal record. [@3] Both detainees remained under
investigation for alleged drug trafficking and illegal possession of war
material. [@1] They were taken to the Grupo Terna base in Ciudad Nueva and were
to be handed to Depandro in Gregorio Albarracín for an initial 15 days of
investigation. [@1]
