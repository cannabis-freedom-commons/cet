Four people were detained in a series of police raids in Concordia, in
Argentina's Entre Ríos province, and placed at the disposal of the courts. [@1]
They were accused of illegal possession of a firearm and possession of drugs
with intent to sell. [@1] The raids followed an investigation into repeated
illegal motorbike races, known as picadas, and reckless riding in Benito
Legerén, El Martillo and along the Villa Adela access road. [@1] The prosecutor
handling the case, Mario Figueroa, requested the search warrants under article
193 bis of the penal code, which covers unauthorised speed or skill trials that
put others at risk. [@1] A guarantee court judge, Guerrero, authorised searches
at homes in Benito Legerén and barrio La Bianca. [@1] The investigation was
continuing to establish the detainees' links to the clandestine races and to
possible drug distribution networks. [@3]

At the homes officers seized two wrappings of marijuana and three cannabis
sativa plants between 40 and 80 centimetres tall. [@1] Diario Junio reported
that the marijuana was compacted and prepared for dividing and sale. [@2] The
same searches produced 42 nylon wrappings holding about 50 grams of cocaine,
which is not counted in the cannabis figures. [@1] Officers also seized three
motorcycles — a Motomel C110, a Motomel 150cc and a Corven Energy 110cc — a
12-gauge double-barrelled shotgun with 12 cartridges and a .22 revolver with 29
rounds. [@1] Diario Junio reported that cash was also seized at the homes,
without giving an amount. [@2]
