Nepal Police arrested a man with 60 kilograms of marijuana on an inner road at
Jangal Tol in Lalbandi Municipality-1, Sarlahi. [@1] The operation was carried
out early on Sunday, 4 January 2026. [@1] At around 5:00 AM police tried to stop
a motorcycle heading towards Lalbandi, and the rider was apprehended after he
attempted to flee. [@1] The rider was identified as 32-year-old Nurhasan Sah,
son of Bajul Sah of Nawalpur, Lalbandi-1. [@1]

Acting on Sah's statement, police pursued an Indian-registered car, BR 02 PB
2226, which later crashed into a house in Jangal Tol and whose driver escaped.
[@1] Officers recovered 15 plastic-wrapped packets of marijuana weighing 60
kilograms hidden behind the car seat. [@1] Sah was taken into custody together
with the seized car, motorcycle and marijuana, according to DSP Saroj Rai,
information officer at the District Police Office, Sarlahi. [@1]

The Sarlahi District Court granted an eight-day remand for Sah. [@1] Police said
further investigation was under way. [@1]
