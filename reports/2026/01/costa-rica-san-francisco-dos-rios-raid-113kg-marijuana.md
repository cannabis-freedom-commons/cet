Drug Control Police officers raided a house in San Francisco de Dos Ríos, San
José, on the night of Tuesday 6 January 2026. [@2] Three people were detained in
connection with the operation. [@4]

Inside the house officers seized about 113 kilograms of marijuana. [@1] The same
search turned up 42 kilograms of cocaine and a kilogram of crack, which are not
counted as cannabis. [@3] Officers also found a grenade launcher, two
fragmentation grenades and a tear-gas grenade, 14 quarter-sticks of dynamite,
five emulsion charges with detonators and more than 1,500 rounds of ammunition.
[@3] A .22 pistol and a vehicle were also seized. [@3] Officers counted
30,912,000 colones in cash inside the house. [@2]

On 19 December officers detained a man surnamed Mora in central San José with
more than four million colones and two firearms. [@2]
