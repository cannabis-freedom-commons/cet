Billy Lewis Marina, a 22-year-old roofer from Alfreton, Derbyshire, was stopped
at the Sea Terminal in Douglas at about 18:00 on 24 June after coming off the
ferry from Heysham. [@2] Deemster Graeme Cook jailed Marina for nine years in
prison. [@2] He pleaded guilty to importing cocaine, ketamine and cannabis to
the island and possessing the drugs with intent to supply. [@2]

Officers smelled cannabis coming from his car, detained him and searched his
silver Mercedes A-Class. [@2] Three black vacuum-packed packages containing
cannabis were found inside a duffle bag with a significant amount of cash. [@2]
In total officers found 2.5 kg of cannabis with a potential value of £50,414,
alongside 1 kg of cocaine worth £100,341 and 250 g of ketamine worth between
£10,170 and £12,712, the court heard. [@2]

Analysis of Marina's mobile phone messages showed he was heavily involved in
bringing over the drugs and deciding what type of drugs were carried and how
they were paid for. [@2] Deemster Cook said Marina was not a simple courier or
mule, had been involved in planning and was making a substantial gain. [@2]
Defence advocate Ian Kermode said Marina had made a 'catastrophic error of
judgement'. [@2] Marina wrote to the court accepting full responsibility but
asking for leniency. [@2] He will serve two thirds of the sentence before
automatic release and can apply for parole at the halfway point. [@2] The
deemster said the island was becoming overwhelmed by the amount of drugs coming
in in bulk and that a message needed to get out. [@1]
