Cumberland police arrested three people on 31 January 2026 after stopping a
suspicious vehicle and finding marijuana, fentanyl, methamphetamine and cocaine
on the occupants and inside the vehicle. [@2] The three were Sophie Lemieux, 39,
Jordan Jensen, 35, and Robert Powless, 40, all from the Ashland area, and they
were later charged in Barron County Circuit Court. [@1]

Lemieux was charged with misdemeanor possession of THC, two counts of felony
possession of narcotic drugs, felony possession of methamphetamine and
misdemeanor possession of cocaine. [@1] Police had listed her cannabis count as
possession of THC, a second or subsequent offence, along with probation,
paraphernalia and other drug charges. [@2] Jensen was charged with two counts of
felony possession of narcotic drugs, misdemeanor possession of cocaine and
misdemeanor resisting or obstructing an officer, and he was held on a 2,500
dollar signature bond. [@1] Powless was charged with two counts of felony
possession of narcotic drugs and misdemeanor possession of cocaine. [@1]

On 15 April 2026 Powless appeared by video from Dodge Correctional Institution
and pleaded guilty to one count of felony possession of narcotic drugs. [@1]
Judge James Babler ordered him to serve one year of incarceration, with 74 days
of credit, followed by one year of extended supervision, concurrent with any
other sentence he was already serving, and the remaining charges were dismissed
and read in. [@1] That prison term was imposed for possession of narcotic drugs,
not for a cannabis offence. [@1] Lemieux was bound over after a February 2026
preliminary hearing before Judge Babler and was scheduled to appear again in May
2026. [@1] Jensen was bound over on 8 April 2026 after Judge Babler found
probable cause, and he was scheduled to appear again in June 2026. [@1]
Supervision conditions for Powless required an alcohol and other drug assessment
and any treatment the Department of Corrections recommended. [@1]
