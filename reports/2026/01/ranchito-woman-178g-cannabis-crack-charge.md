Police executed a search warrant at Pineda's residence in Ranchito Village at
around 10 a.m. on 30 January 2026. [@1] Sandra Aracelly Pineda, a 38-year-old
resident of Ranchito village in Corozal District, was arrested during the
search. [@2] Assistant Superintendent Stacy Smith, a police staff officer, gave
the police account of the search. [@1] When officers entered and announced
themselves as police executing the search warrant, the arresting officer saw
Pineda pick up a transparent plastic bag and run through the house. [@1]
Officers pursued Pineda and saw her throw the plastic bag. [@1]

When the bag was opened and inspected, it held several pieces of suspected crack
cocaine totaling 5.8 grams. [@1] A subsequent search of Pineda found an
additional 17.8 grams of cannabis. [@1] Corozal police formally arrested and
charged Pineda with possession of controlled drugs with intent to supply for the
5.8 grams of crack cocaine and possession of controlled drugs for the 17.8 grams
of cannabis. [@3]

No one else in the home was charged because Pineda took full responsibility for
the drugs found on the premises. [@2]
