Royal Eswatini Police Service officers arrested nine suspects and seized 405.99
kg of dagga in a two-day operation across the country. [@1] Mliba police
arrested a 30-year-old man at Zandondo with 117.45 kg of dagga, the largest
single seizure of the operation. [@1]

In Simunye, a 43-year-old man from Siphambanweni was arrested with 60 kg of
dagga. [@1] In the Dumbe area, KaPhunga police found 53 kg of dagga hidden in
the boot of a Honda Fit and took three suspects, aged 20, 27 and 36, into
custody. [@1] At Ngwenya Border Post, Mbabane officers stopped a Ford Everest in
a routine search, found 43 kg and arrested two men. [@1] At Masheleleni, Pigg’s
Peak police stopped a black Honda Fit and seized 47.1 kg, and among those
arrested was a 35-year-old man from Botswana. [@1]

Police said the arrests were part of a broader strategy to protect the country’s
youth and used the hashtag #Sikhona, meaning “We are here”, to say they were
monitoring trafficking routes. [@1] A police spokesperson said the force would
not allow Eswatini to become a “playground for drug lords”. [@1] The nine
suspects were preparing to face the courts, and police asked the public to
report suspicious activity. [@1]

Eswatini Observer reported that police had confiscated more than 300 kilogrammes
of dagga at search points around the country over the previous two days. [@2]
