Police detained two men identified as Candelario Muñoz Becerra and Claudio Mesa
González. [@1] The raid took place at a property in Huachipa, the National
Police said. [@1] Police said the pair belonged to a criminal organisation
called 'Los Mexicanos del Callao', which they said collected and sold drugs.
[@1]

Officers found more than 22 kilograms of marijuana in their possession. [@1]
They also seized 1,600 soles in notes suspected to be counterfeit. [@1] Caretas
described the operation as a key strike against the supply chain for street
dealing in Callao. [@2] General Ricardo Espinoza, head of the Callao police
region, said it was not yet known whether the men put the notes into circulation
or had themselves been deceived. [@1]

Espinoza said the operation was part of a shift towards hitting the large-scale
suppliers who stock street-level dealers. [@1] In a separate operation, police
detained five people from Ayacucho in Ventanilla with 20 kilograms of
high-purity cocaine, a seizure that is not cannabis and is not counted here.
[@1]
