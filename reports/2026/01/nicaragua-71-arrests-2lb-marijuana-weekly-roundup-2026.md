Nicaragua's National Police said it arrested 71 people accused of high-danger
crimes in different parts of the country between Monday 5 and Sunday 11 January
2026. [@1] In the department of Managua and its municipalities, 53 people were
arrested, including 16 for robbery with intimidation, 14 for robbery with
violence, 16 for robbery with force and six for drug supply. [@1] Another 18
people were arrested elsewhere in the country, among them six alleged drug
suppliers in León and two held for drug trafficking in Matagalpa and Nueva
Guinea. [@1]

Police listed two pounds of marijuana, about 0.907 kg, as evidence seized from
those detained in the department of Managua. [@1] The same Managua evidence list
included 130 grams of cocaine and 72 grams of crack, which are not counted in
the cannabis figures. [@1] In the departments, officers listed 3 kg of cocaine,
123 grams of crack, five mobile phones and cash as evidence, with no marijuana.
[@1]

Police presented the roundup as the result of its fight against crime. [@1]
