Gendarmes of the Nioro du Rip territorial brigade searched the home of a
suspected drug trafficker in Dinguiraye village, Paoskoto commune, on 8 January
2026. [@1] They arrested the suspect and his alleged accomplice, both
Senegalese. [@3]

Officers seized four kilograms of chanvre indien, Indian hemp or cannabis,
described as of Senegalese origin. [@1] The suspect was described as an active
trafficker living in a border zone next to the neighbouring country. [@1]

The operation followed a tip about trafficking from a neighbouring country and
was led by Adjudant-chef Souleymane Thior, the Gendarmerie nationale said. [@2]
The two were placed in police custody while investigators worked to establish
the network's size and possible branches. [@2] Dakaractu reported that both had
been handed to the courts to answer for international drug trafficking. [@1]
