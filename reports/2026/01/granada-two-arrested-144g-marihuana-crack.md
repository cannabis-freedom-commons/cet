National Police of the Granada department said two people were arrested for the
offence of alleged drug supply. [@1] Inspector Jorge Antonio Díaz, the police
press officer, said the arrest occurred at 2 p.m. on Friday 16 January 2026.
[@1] The two were arrested at a home in the Países Bajos neighbourhood of
Granada city: Hamilton Yahir Colomer Alemán, 22, and Neykin Alexander Espinoza
Barberena, 23. [@1]

Anti-narcotics agents searching the home found white solid stones hidden in a
plastic container in the sewage pipe that field-tested positive for crack, plus
a green herb that tested as marihuana. [@1] The evidence included 144 grams of
marihuana and 20 grams of crack, plus cash for which no amount was given; the
crack is a separate substance and is not counted in the cannabis figures. [@1]

The detainees and the evidence were handed to the judicial authorities of the
Granada department, who will determine innocence or guilt through due process.
[@1] Police said they would continue surveillance, patrolling, prevention and
education work, including work with young people at risk. [@1]
