Namibian Police (NamPol) are searching for a fellow officer who fled after
allegedly selling cannabis to a trial-awaiting inmate in Walvis Bay. [@1] The
incident happened at about 10:30 on Saturday, 31 January 2026, at Tutaleni
Police Station. [@1] An on-duty male constable and NamPol member allegedly
brought cannabis into the station, took it to the holding cells and handed it to
the inmate, who hid it on his body. [@1]

A complainant noticed the suspects' suspicious movements, approached the inmate
and found 20 small bags and two big plastic bags of cannabis on him. [@1] The
value of the cannabis was still to be determined. [@1] The 35-year-old constable
had fled and was not arrested, while the 25-year-old inmate remained held in the
Tutaleni cells and police investigations continued. [@1]
