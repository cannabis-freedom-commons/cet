Vanuatu Police Force officers arrested a single suspect at Lathe village in East
Santo over alleged marijuana cultivation and supply. [@1] The arrest allegation
treats the suspect as having cultivated marijuana at Lathe village. [@1] The
same arrest allegation treats the suspect as having supplied marijuana. [@1] The
detained person was described only as a suspect, with no name, age or home
village published. [@1]

Officers seized 94 marijuana plants in connection with the Lathe village case.
[@1] The seizure figure for the operation stands at 94 marijuana plants. [@1]
The arrest and the seizure of the 94 plants belong to the same Lathe village
case. [@1] No weight or monetary value was stated for the 94 plants. [@1]

The Vanuatu Police Force (VPF) is the agency that made the arrest. [@1] The
operation is dated to 29 January 2026. [@1] No charge, plea or court outcome was
reported for the suspect. [@1]
