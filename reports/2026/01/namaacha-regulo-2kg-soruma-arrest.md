A 53-year-old community leader (regulo) was detained in flagrante delicto in
Namaacha district, Maputo province, in possession of more than two kilograms of
cannabis sativa known locally as soruma. [@1] The man was intercepted on a
public road while carrying the drug wrapped in a checkered plastic bag. [@1]

The case occurred in Namaacha district in Maputo province. [@1] Questioned about
where the drug came from, he did not reveal its provenance but said it was
intended for sale. [@1] The account was given by Carmínia Leite, spokeswoman for
the Maputo provincial command of the Police of the Republic of Mozambique (PRM).
[@1]

Leite said this was not the first detention this month in Maputo province of
people involved in drug dealing and use, pointing to Boane and Namaacha
districts. [@1]
