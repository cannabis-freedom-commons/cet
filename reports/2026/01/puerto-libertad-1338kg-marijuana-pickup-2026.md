Prefectura Naval Argentina, Argentina's coast guard, seized more than 130
kilograms of marijuana at Puerto Libertad in Misiones during an operation on the
Paraná river. [@1] The operation took place at kilometre 1.871 of the river,
where officers detected a motorboat that had come from Paraguay through an
unauthorised crossing carrying several bundles. [@2] Several people had unloaded
the cargo and returned quickly to the Paraguayan shore before the officers
arrived. [@2] A ground operation was launched and officers noticed a pickup
leaving the riverside area at high speed. [@2]

After a controlled follow, officers found the vehicle with six packages with
rectangular wrappings inside. [@4] Field tests confirmed the packages held 230
'panes', or pressed bricks, of marijuana weighing 133.8 kilograms. [@3] The
force valued the load at more than 492 million Argentine pesos. [@1] Officers
also seized the pickup used to carry the drug. [@1]

The case was placed at the disposal of the Federal Prosecutor's Office in Puerto
Iguazú. [@1] The Federal Prosecutor's Office in Puerto Iguazú and the Federal
Court of the same city ordered the investigation to continue to identify those
responsible. [@2]
