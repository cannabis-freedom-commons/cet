Colombia's National Police Transit and Transport unit seized drugs hidden in a
private car in Valle del Cauca that were bound for Ecuador. [@1] Police paired
that seizure with a similar one in Cauca involving drugs in a private pickup,
putting the combined seizure in both departments at 1,585 kilos. [@1] El Tiempo
likewise pairs the Valle seizure with a similar Cauca seizure in a private
pickup and gives the combined total as 1,585 kilos. [@2] Valle police commander
Brigadier General Sandra Liliana Rodriguez said the operation was carried out
under the Corredores Seguros strategy as a result of strengthened operating
controls. [@1] During registration, control and patrol work, officers noticed a
vehicle parked in the parking area of a lodging in a sector of the municipality
of Bugalagrande. [@1] After neighbourhood inquiries failed to locate the
vehicle's owner, officers searched it and found several packages wrapped in
black vinyl inside. [@1]

Checks established that the packages held plant material with leaves, stems and
seeds whose colour and odour matched marijuana, with an approximate gross weight
of 784.8 kilograms. [@1] Police said the suspect who was driving the vehicle was
placed at the disposal of the Attorney General's Office. [@1]

In Patia, on the Panamericana highway, transit officers seized 800 kilograms of
marijuana being carried in a pickup bound for Ecuador. [@1] During that
procedure the driver noticed the police presence, abandoned the vehicle and
fled, avoiding capture, while authorities opened investigations to identify and
prosecute those responsible. [@1] Cauca police commander Colonel Gerson Bedoya
Piraquive called the result a hard blow to the finances of drug-trafficking
criminal organisations. [@1] A second account confirms the load was moving along
the Panamericana toward Ecuador when Transit and Transport officers intercepted
it. [@4]

Caracol independently reports the Valle vehicle was found in the parking area of
a lodging in the municipality. [@3]
