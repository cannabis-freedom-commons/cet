Police in Zhaksy district, Akmola Region, shut down an illegal storage of
narcotics in an especially large amount. [@1] Officers identified a 34-year-old
resident of Zhaksy village as the suspect. [@2] During a search of the suspect's
home, police found and seized marijuana. [@1] The marijuana weighed 5.6 kg,
which police said was an especially large amount under current law. [@1]

A pre-trial investigation was opened. [@1] Forensic examinations were ordered to
establish where the marijuana came from. [@1] Police were also checking why the
suspect kept the drug at home and who might have supplied it. [@1]

The police statement said illegal circulation of narcotics, including storage in
large and especially large amounts, carries strict criminal liability. [@1]
Police said work to detect and suppress drug crime in the region was continuing.
