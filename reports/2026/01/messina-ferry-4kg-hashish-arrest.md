Finance police of the Messina provincial command, with the collaboration of the
Reggio Calabria provincial command, seized 4 kilograms of hashish and arrested
the courier carrying it. [@1] During routine checks of passengers and vehicles
in transit on the Strait, the Messina group's finance police, aided by canine
units, stopped a car that had just disembarked from the Calabrian coast after
its driver, already known to the police, appeared visibly agitated. [@1]

Officers found 40 blocks of hashish, weighing 50 grams each, hidden in the
cavity between the car's roof and its interior lining. [@1] After verifying the
man's numerous police priors, including for drugs, the financiers asked
colleagues of the territorially competent Melito di Porto Salvo company to
search his home. [@1] At the home, officers found and seized another 2 kilograms
of hashish bearing the same Spanish-flag logo as the blocks intercepted in
Messina. [@1]

The finance police said retail sale of the drugs would have brought about 40,000
euros to the criminal consortium's coffers. [@1]
