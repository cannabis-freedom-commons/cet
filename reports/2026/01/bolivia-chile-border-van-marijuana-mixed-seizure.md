Chilean authorities seized close to 190 kilograms of controlled substances
during an operation on the Bolivia-Chile border as part of an international
drug-trafficking investigation. [@1] According to reports from the Arica
prosecutor's office, the drugs were found inside a white van-type vehicle hidden
in yute sacks. [@1] The seizure included marijuana alongside cocaine paste and
ketamine. [@1]

The drugs were allegedly destined for the Chilean market. [@1] Officers also
seized three other vehicles and cash allegedly linked to a criminal association
of at least seven members. [@1] That group was described as dedicated to
bringing drugs from Bolivian territory into Chile. [@1]

Authorities were continuing investigations to establish responsibility and did
not rule out further arrests. [@1] The account called the case one of the most
significant blows to drug trafficking in the border zone so far this year. [@1]
The published weight of close to 190 kilograms was given for the controlled
substances together. [@1] The investigation was still under way when the account
was published. [@1]
