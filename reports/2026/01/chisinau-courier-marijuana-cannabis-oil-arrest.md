Officers of the INI drug control department, working with Chișinău prosecutors,
stopped a member of a group trafficking drugs in the capital, mainly around
higher education institutions. [@1] For a month the anti-drug service documented
a 34-year-old man working as a courier for an online store through the Telegram
app. [@1] He collected and hid the substances only at night, between midnight
and 3 a.m. [@1] On 15 January he was detained in Chișinău near his home as he
tried to drive off to collect a new batch of drugs. [@1] He was detained and
later, at the prosecutor's request, placed under arrest for 30 days. [@1] The
suspect was caught red-handed by Chișinău police as he prepared to collect a
parcel of drugs. [@2]

He bought drugs wholesale at agreed points, took them home, portioned them into
1-2 gram doses and hid them in caches, especially near universities, for
distribution to end users. [@1] A search of his home found and seized marijuana
and amphetamine, an electronic cigarette containing cannabis oil, a syringe of
heroin, precision electronic scales, zip-lock bags and mobile phones used in the
offence. [@1] Police listed the same haul of marijuana, amphetamine, a
cannabis-oil e-cigarette, a heroin syringe, scales and packaging material. [@3]

If convicted he faces a prison term of seven to 15 years. [@1] The
Romanian-language reports put the possible sentence at up to 15 years. [@2]
