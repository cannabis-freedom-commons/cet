Ecuador's National Police seized 719 blocks of marijuana during a road check on
the E20 between La Concordia and Santo Domingo on 23 January 2026. [@1] The
blocks were hidden in eight bundles inside a red double-cab pickup with plates
from Chimborazo province. [@1] The driver tried to evade the check, and officers
chased the truck and alerted the eastern police district. [@1] The truck was
stopped at about kilometre 21, near the zoo. [@2] Officers arrested the
30-year-old driver, identified as Carlos A. S., who said the marijuana had left
Esmeraldas and was bound for Guayaquil. [@1] A second vehicle that appeared to
be escorting the load escaped. [@1]

The seized marijuana weighed about 715 kg. [@3] The police report estimated the
load's value at 554,000 dollars. [@3] The pickup had been reported stolen, El
Diario reported. [@2] Colonel Eduardo Ricaurte, commander of the Santo Domingo
de los Tsáchilas police subzone, said it was the province's first large-scale
drug seizure of 2026. [@2]

The driver, the pickup and the drug were taken to the Subzona 23 command and
then placed at the disposal of the courts in Santo Domingo de los Tsáchilas.
[@1] Governor Miguel Quezada said it was a new hard blow to drug trafficking.
[@1] The police report said the truck came from San Lorenzo canton and the
marijuana was meant for internal distribution. [@3]
