Derek Kavanagh of 69 Arderrin Way, the Glen, Cork affirmed a signed guilty plea
at Cork Circuit Criminal Court. [@1] He was 31 and had been caught in a car
carrying over €600,000 worth of cannabis. [@1] He was remanded in custody for
sentencing at the February 2027 sessions of Cork Circuit Criminal Court. [@1]
Senior counsel Donal O’Sullivan sought a probation report and Judge Helen Boyle
adjourned the case until February. [@1]

Kavanagh told gardaí he had a €28,000 drugs debt and that more than 30 kg of
cannabis valued at €610,000 had been dropped to him by unnamed parties for him
to store. [@1] The January operation started with a vehicle stop in Cork city on
Thursday 22 January 2026. [@2] Follow-up searches under warrant at several city
locations found a further €610,000 worth of suspected cannabis. [@2]

Detective Garda Rory Fogarty charged Kavanagh with possession of cannabis for
sale or supply at Arderrin Way on 22 January, where the value exceeded €13,000,
the threshold for a possible 10-year minimum sentence. [@1] The men charged in
the January operation appeared at Mallow District Court on 24 January 2026 and
were remanded to Cork District Court on 22 April 2026. [@2] The seized drugs
were sent to Forensic Science Ireland for analysis. [@2]
