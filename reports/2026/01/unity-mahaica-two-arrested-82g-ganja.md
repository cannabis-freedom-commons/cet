A 26-year-old fisherman and a 63-year-old woman were arrested on Thursday night
at their address in Unity, Mahaica, East Coast Demerara. [@1] The two residents
were escorted to the Cove and John Police Station along with the suspected
cannabis. [@1] Both suspects remained in custody while the investigation
continued. [@1]

Police searched the residence at about 20:00 hours during an intelligence-led
operation. [@2] Officers found a black plastic bag concealed in the kitchen area
containing several small zip-lock plastic bags with leaves, seeds and stems
suspected to be cannabis. [@1] The suspected cannabis was weighed in their
presence and amounted to 82.11 grams before it was processed and lodged. [@1]

Police Headquarters said the operation was conducted at 20:00 hours. [@1] The
second account describes the same Thursday-night discovery at a home in Unity,
Mahaica, with the drug later weighed at 82.11 grams as investigations continue.
[@2]
