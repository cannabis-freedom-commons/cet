Mexico City citizen-security officers detained six men aged 30, 39, 41, 46, 48
and 50 and a 46-year-old woman on suspicion of drug possession in the Coyoacán
borough. [@1] The seven were stopped at the intersection of calle Puerto Rico
and calzada de Tlalpan in colonia Parque San Andrés as they handled small bags
and exchanged them for cash aboard two vehicles near a hotel. [@1] The two cars
were parked outside the hotel at that intersection when patrol officers
approached the group. [@2]

Officers seized more than 100 doses of suspected drugs: about one kilogram of
suspected marijuana, 72 plastic bags of the same herb and 39 doses of a solid
substance of different colours, plus cash, a firearm with six live cartridges
and the two vehicles. [@1]

The seven were taken to the Public Ministry, which will determine their legal
situation over possible crimes against health. [@1] Two of those detained,
Patricia Beltrán "N" and Salvador Ruiz "N", were believed to come from Sinaloa.
