Officers of the Hanover main customs office stopped a car with Belgian
registration on the A2 near Peine on the evening of 5 January 2026 after
suspecting forbidden goods. [@2] The driver was a 48-year-old Belgian man
heading toward Berlin. [@1]

German customs found the drug in about ten vacuum-sealed packages hidden under
the floor mats and in the boot of the car. [@1] Officers seized more than 22
kilograms of hashish in the car. [@2] Customs put the street-sale value of the
hashish at about 220,000 euros. [@2]

NDR described the detained man as an alleged drug dealer caught with the
hashish. [@3] After the discovery the man tried to flee on foot but was quickly
caught because of the snow. [@1] Criminal proceedings were opened against him
and he was placed in pre-trial detention. [@2]
