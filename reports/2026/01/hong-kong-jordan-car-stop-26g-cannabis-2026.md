Officers from the West Kowloon regional emergency unit stopped a white Honda
parked at the junction of Parkes Street and Nanking Street in Jordan shortly
before 4 am on 29 January 2026. [@2] Police found a small quantity of suspected
drugs inside the car, and the male driver and a female passenger were arrested
at the scene and taken to a police station. [@1] Police said the car held about
26.2 grams of suspected cannabis and about 1.2 grams of suspected
methamphetamine. [@2] The driver, a 38-year-old man surnamed Law, and the
passenger, a 36-year-old woman surnamed Kong, were held on suspicion of
trafficking in dangerous drugs. [@3]

The 1.2 grams of suspected methamphetamine from the same search is not counted
in the cannabis figure. [@3] Police said the total street value of the drugs had
not yet been calculated. [@3] The case was assigned to the Yau Tsim Mong
district criminal investigation unit, team 4. [@3]
