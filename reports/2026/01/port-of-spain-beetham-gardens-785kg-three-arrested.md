Police arrested two men aged 45 and 32 and a woman after cannabis was found at a
house in Beetham Gardens, Port of Spain. [@2] The operation took place on
Wednesday 14 January 2026. [@2] Officers from the Special Investigations Unit
went to a home at 11th Street, Beetham Gardens, where they carried out a search
under the Emergency Powers Regulations, 2025. [@4] The search was carried out in
the presence of the occupants. [@1] The woman's age was not disclosed. [@1]

In a bedroom, officers found 111 multi-coloured rectangular packages containing
cannabis. [@1] The packages weighed a combined 78.52 kilograms. [@4] Police put
their street value at TT$8,617,334.44. [@1] Officers took the three to the SIU
main office. [@3]

The seizure was announced as part of an anti-crime operation in the Port of
Spain Division. [@2] The Emergency Powers Regulations, 2025, set out the search
powers available during the state of emergency in force in Trinidad and Tobago
at the time. [@1] The three were due to appear in court. [@1]
