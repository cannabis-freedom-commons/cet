The crew of the US Coast Guard cutter Alert intercepted a go-fast boat carrying
illegal drugs in the Windward Passage during a 45-day counter-drug patrol. [@1]
The four suspected smugglers on board were handed to the Bahamas government for
prosecution, together with the drugs and the firearm. [@1] The cutter returned
to its home port in Cape Canaveral, Florida, on Thursday 15 January 2026, the
day the Coast Guard announced the seizure. [@1]

Alongside the cocaine, the crew seized seven pounds of marijuana that the Coast
Guard valued at $7,000. [@2] An account by the EFE agency put the marijuana at
3.1 kilograms, about seven pounds. [@3] The same seizure included 2,250 pounds
(1.02 tonnes) of cocaine valued at $18.4 million, which is not counted in the
cannabis figures. [@1]

The patrol also covered the Caribbean Sea and the eastern Pacific, and the crew
supported Operation Vigilant Sentry, helping to deter unsafe migration near
Haiti. [@2]
