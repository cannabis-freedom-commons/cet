On Wednesday 14 January 2026, the tribunal correctionnel de Fort-de-France, the
criminal court for the Martinique capital, sentenced a 21-year-old man to 18
months in prison. [@1] The conviction was for possession of a weapon and of
drugs, or détention d'arme et de stupéfiants. [@1] The term was imposed as firm
imprisonment, so the 18 months were not suspended. [@1]

The defendant already had two earlier convictions for violence on his record.
[@1] Cannabis was among the substances in the case, which also involved a weapon
fitted with a laser sight and money. [@1]
