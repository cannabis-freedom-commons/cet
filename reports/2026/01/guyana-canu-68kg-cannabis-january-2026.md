The Customs Anti-Narcotic Unit carried out eleven intelligence-led operations
across multiple regions of Guyana in January 2026. [@1] Region Two accounted for
the majority of the drugs seized. [@1] The operations led to the arrest of
eighteen people. [@1] Twelve narcotics-related charges were laid and three
convictions were secured. [@1]

Officers seized 161.09 kg of narcotics in total. [@1] That total included 68.39
kg of cannabis and 92.46 kg of cocaine. [@1] Officers also seized 229 grams of
ecstasy and 84 grams of suspected crystal methamphetamine. [@1] CANU put the
Guyana street value of the cannabis at $20,517,750, with a USA value of
US$102,558.75. [@1] CANU also reported recovering two firearms and confiscating
137 rounds of ammunition. [@1]

The agency said the results reflected sustained enforcement pressure on
trafficking networks operating along roadways, border corridors, residential
areas and international entry points. [@1] It said road transport remains the
primary trafficking method, while airport interdictions are lower in volume but
operationally significant for international trafficking. [@1] The agency said
intelligence-driven operations, inter-agency cooperation and sustained border
vigilance remain critical to disrupting trafficking networks. [@1]
