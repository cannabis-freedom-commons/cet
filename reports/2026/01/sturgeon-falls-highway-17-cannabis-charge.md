Ontario Provincial Police charged a 70-year-old driver with multiple offences
after a traffic stop on Highway 17 west of North Bay earlier this month. [@1]
The motorist, a resident of Sturgeon Falls, was charged with stunt driving and
with driving a vehicle with cannabis readily available. [@2] The traffic stop
took place on 10 January 2026. [@1]

In a social media post, OPP said the vehicle was stopped after it was observed
travelling at 151 km/h in a 90 km/h zone. [@1] During the traffic stop, officers
observed an unsealed THC container on the vehicle's passenger seat. [@1] As a
result, OPP suspended the motorist's driver's licence for 30 days, impounded the
vehicle involved for 14 days and scheduled a future traffic court appearance.
[@1]

Police used the incident to reiterate the dangers of excessive speed. [@1] OPP
said stunt driving puts everyone at risk. [@1] OPP Const. Kyler Brouwer added:
"Slow down." [@1]
