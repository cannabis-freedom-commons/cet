Namibian police arrested suspects across the country over the weekend in a
series of drug operations that recovered cannabis, cocaine, Mandrax tablets and
cash. [@1] Three Namibian men aged 26, 28 and 43 were arrested on Friday at the
Kamanjab–Outjo road, about eight kilometres from Kamanjab. [@1] Police searching
a freight Iveco truck found two parcels of suspected cannabis concealed in a
white box under the passenger seat. [@1] The Kamanjab suspects, including the
driver and alleged owner of the drugs, face charges of dealing in and possession
of dependence-producing substances. [@1] A 51-year-old man was arrested at about
07:00 at Etunda village along the Okahao–Oshakati road in Omusati Region. [@1]
Police said the man had two packs of cannabis skunk weighing 2,108 grams (2.108
kg) and N$46,043 in cash he could not satisfactorily explain. [@1] Police put
the combined value of the drugs and cash at N$110,043. [@1] The man was due in
the Outapi Magistrate's Court on Monday 12 January 2026. [@2]

A 38-year-old man was arrested in Katutura's Damara Location at about 11:00
after police found 30 grams of cocaine valued at N$15,000 in his room. [@1] At
about 12:30 a 20-year-old man was arrested for possession of 35 grams of
cannabis skunk valued at N$1,750. [@1] That 20-year-old was arrested at Mahatma
Gandhi Street in Khomasdal. [@2] In Swakopmund's DRC (Wagdaar) location, a
30-year-old man was arrested at about 14:12 with cannabis skunk, majad cannabis
and Mandrax tablets valued at N$560. [@1] At Perspeka village in Tsumkwe's
Mangetti area, a man of unknown age was arrested at about 01:00 on Saturday with
a medium parcel of cannabis skunk worth N$1,900. [@1] A 24-year-old woman was
arrested at about 16:00 at Vergenoeg in the Gobabis District, where police found
254 sachets of cannabis valued at N$22,750 in her shack. [@1]

In Ehafo Location at Uis, Erongo Region, police arrested two men aged 21 and 23
and a 17-year-old girl at about 20:00 for dealing in drugs. [@1] That operation
recovered two plastic bags and 75 bankies of cannabis skunk, 26 quarters of
Mandrax tablets and N$9,735.80 in cash. [@1] Police records put the weekend at
seven drug cases across the country. [@2] Another account of the same police
report described seven arrests across several regions involving cannabis,
cocaine and Mandrax. [@3] Police said investigations into all the cases were
continuing. [@1]
