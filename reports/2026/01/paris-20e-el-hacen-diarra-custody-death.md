The 35-year-old man was named Hacen Diarra and was originally from Mauritania.
[@1] He was stopped on the evening of Wednesday 14 January by Territorial
Contact Brigade officers who said they had seen him rolling a cannabis joint.
[@1] He was seen at 10:45 pm rolling a cannabis joint by neighbourhood police
officers. [@3]

A police source said he resisted and officers used an electric-shock pistol in
contact mode on his calf to restrain him. [@1] At the station his alcohol test
was negative, and police said he carried false administrative documents and
brown matter resembling cannabis. [@1] Officers notified him of garde a vue for
alleged use of false administrative documents, acquisition, possession and use
of narcotics, and rebellion. [@3] After losing consciousness in the
20th-arrondissement station, the man arrested in possession of cannabis resin
died. [@2] He died during the night from Wednesday to Thursday in the
20th-arrondissement police station. [@3] The death was recorded at 0:20 during
the night of 14 to 15 January in the station on rue des Gatines. [@3]

The Paris prosecutor opened a cause-of-death inquiry entrusted to the National
Police Inspectorate, the IGPN. [@2] His family filed a legal complaint accusing
police of intentional violence that led to his death. [@4] Prosecutors said
Diarra died on Wednesday night in the Paris station after police said they saw
him allegedly rolling a joint. [@4]
