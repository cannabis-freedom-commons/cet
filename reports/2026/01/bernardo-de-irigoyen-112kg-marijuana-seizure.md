Customs forces at the Bernardo de Irigoyen crossing in Misiones seized 112 kg of
marijuana from a car in the early hours of Saturday. [@1] The stop occurred
around 04:45 on Saturday morning when inspectors halted a grey car carrying two
men. [@2] The car was a grey Chevrolet with an Argentine registration, stopped
in the lane for vehicles entering the country. [@1] The two occupants said they
had travelled from Foz do Iguazú to Camboriú, both in Brazil. [@1] Officers said
that account made no sense because the car was entering Argentina rather than
running between two Brazilian cities. [@1] The men said the driver's door would
not open and got out through the passenger side. [@1] When asked again for
identification they ran about 60 metres across the 'frontera seca', the overland
frontier, into Brazil, leaving the car in the customs zone. [@1] The two men
remained at large while authorities worked to identify and locate them. [@1]

A judicial authorisation allowed a full search of the car. [@1] Officers found
188 rectangular cakes hidden in parts of the cabin, and chemical tests confirmed
marijuana. [@1] The final weight was 112 kilograms, which were seized and placed
at the disposal of the court. [@1] The Chevrolet had been subject to a capture
order since 11 November 2025 in a theft case. [@1]

The Northeast Narcotics Investigation Division (DE INAI), a specialist anti-drug
unit, was called in after the flight. [@1] The drugs, car and documents were
seized and placed before the Federal Court of Eldorado in Misiones. [@3]
