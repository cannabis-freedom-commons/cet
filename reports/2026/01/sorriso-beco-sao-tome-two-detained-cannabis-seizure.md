Military police officers were on patrol in the Zona Leste when they entered an
alley known as Beco São Tomé. [@1] They noticed a strong odour of a substance
described as similar to marijuana and smoke coming from a residence in the
alley. [@1] The residence stood in Beco São Tomé itself. [@1] Officers then
approached the residence with the owner's authorisation. [@1] Two people were
then taken to the police station for the applicable procedures. [@1]

The subsequent search of the residence produced the drugs, packaging and a
scale. [@1] Inside, police reported finding 57 wraps of a substance described as
similar to cocaine paste. [@1] They also reported finding one medium-sized piece
of a substance described as similar to marijuana. [@1] They also reported
finding two wraps of a substance described as similar to marijuana. [@1] Police
also reported seizing eight sealed zip-lock bags. [@1] Police also reported
seizing two opened zip-lock bags. [@1] Police also reported seizing one
precision scale that was in use. [@1]

The man responsible for the residence said he was only storing the drugs. [@1]
He claimed the material belonged to his neighbour, known as “Mucamba”. [@1]
