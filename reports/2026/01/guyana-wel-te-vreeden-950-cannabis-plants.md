Ranks of the Guyana Police Force destroyed a cannabis farm measuring about one
acre at Wel te Vreeden, Canje Creek. [@1] The operation was led by a gazetted
officer and ran from 4:00 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. on 14 January 2026. [@2] Officers
found about 950 cannabis plants, between two and five feet tall, on the plot
along with a makeshift camp. [@1]

Police valued the destroyed cannabis at GYD 965,580. [@2] The Guyana Chronicle
described that figure as an estimated street value for nearly 1,000 plants. [@3]
Officers burned the plants and the camp, and no arrests were made. [@1] The
exercise was carried out without incident. [@3]

Police said investigations into the source and cultivation of the plants were
ongoing. [@3] The Guyana Chronicle said the operation formed part of the police
force's efforts against illegal drug cultivation in Berbice. [@3]
