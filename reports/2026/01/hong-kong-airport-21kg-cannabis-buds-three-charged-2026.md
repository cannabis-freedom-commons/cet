Hong Kong Customs arrested a 42-year-old man arriving from Kuala Lumpur via
Singapore at Hong Kong International Airport on 28 January 2026 after finding
suspected cannabis buds in his check-in suitcase. [@1] Officers found about 4 kg
of suspected cannabis buds disguised as food items in the suitcase, and 2 g of
suspected methamphetamine and several nimetazepam tablets were found on him.
[@1] In a follow-up investigation that day, customs officers arrested a
33-year-old woman and a 34-year-old man at the airport and seized about 17 kg
more suspected cannabis buds from the woman's suitcase. [@1]

In total customs seized about 21 kg of suspected cannabis buds, about 2 g of
suspected methamphetamine and several tablets of suspected nimetazepam, with an
estimated market value of about HK$4.2 million. [@1]

The 42-year-old was charged with two counts of trafficking in dangerous drugs,
while the woman and the other man were jointly charged with one count. [@1] All
three were due to appear at West Kowloon Magistrates' Courts on 30 January 2026.
[@1] Trafficking in a dangerous drug carries a maximum penalty of a HK$5 million
fine and life imprisonment. [@1]
