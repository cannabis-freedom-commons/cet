Motorized Police officers on a preventive patrol on calle 31 in Santa Rita de
Cabra, Pacora, spotted two people. [@1] The two people threw a package and fled
through a grassy field after noticing the police from a distance. [@2] Officers
entered the grass to check the contents of the thrown package. [@2] The two
suspects escaped. [@3] Police said they had started steps to identify and locate
the suspects. [@1]

The thrown package held three wrappers containing suspected dried herb
(marijuana). [@2] Police seized the suspected substance and placed it at the
disposal of the judicial authorities to open investigations. [@1] Mi Diario
described the find differently as six black plastic bags sealed with red tape.
[@3]

That outlet said the bags were presumed to hold illicit substances, probably
marijuana, and were handed to the competent authority for analysis. [@3] The
seized material was to undergo analysis to confirm its nature and exact
quantity, while investigation lines remained open. [@3] Patrols in the Santa
Rita area were increased to prevent similar incidents. [@3]
