Police in Dundigal raided a house where ganja hash oil was being made and
arrested three people. [@1] The raid took place within Dundigal police station
limits, officials said. [@1] Officers seized ganja hash oil worth about ₹8 lakh
from the premises during the raid. [@1]

Police said Sunil, a resident of Chintal in Quthbullapur, had rented a house
near Gandi Maisamma Junction. [@1] They said he was manufacturing ganja hash oil
at the house in secrecy with his friends Chintu and Suresh. [@1]

The Medchal Special Operations Team conducted the raid acting on credible
information and took the three into custody. [@1] The team later handed the
three over to Dundigal police. [@1] Police said a case had been registered and
further investigation was under way. [@1] Officials announced the arrests on
Saturday, 3 January 2026. [@1]
