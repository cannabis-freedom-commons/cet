A Delhi Police Special Cell team arrested Tilak Prasad Sharma in Sikkim on a
non-bailable warrant in a drug case in which cocaine and Thai marijuana were
smuggled into Delhi in 2024. [@1] Sharma, 35, is from Padamchey in Pakyong
district and was living at Boomtaar in Namchi. [@1] After an inter-state
operation he was traced to Namchi East, about 150 km from his native village.
[@3] On 10 January a Namchi police team led by its station house officer, with
two sub-inspectors and Pakyong police, made the arrest. [@1] He was produced
before a local court, which granted a transit remand, and then brought before a
Delhi court. [@1]

The case is FIR No. 455/2024, registered on 2 October 2024 under the NDPS Act.
[@1] Police said it concerns the recovery of about 1,290 kg of cocaine and
mephedrone and 40 kg of Thai marijuana. [@1] Sharma is accused of bringing a
consignment of about 40 kg of Thai marijuana from Thailand on the instructions
of supplier Ritik Bajaj. [@2] Police said he flew to Thailand several times and
carried the marijuana in his luggage through airports. [@1]

His arrest is the 20th in the case, and came weeks after Bajaj was detained in
Dubai and brought back to India. [@1] Nepal customs had earlier held Sharma at
Kathmandu airport with Thai marijuana, and police said he escaped from jail
there during unrest and returned to India. [@1] The syndicate's mastermind,
Virendra Basoya, is abroad, the Tribune reported. [@5] A Red Corner Notice has
been issued against his son Rishabh, who is also outside India. [@4] Delhi
Police have filed a chargesheet of more than 10,000 pages in Patiala House Court
naming 19 people, five of whom have been declared proclaimed offenders. [@1]
