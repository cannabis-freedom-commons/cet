Mokpo Police Station arrested rapper D.Ark on 27 October 2025 on suspicion of
violating the Narcotics Control Act. [@2] Police said he had distributed and
smoked marijuana, and he denied the allegations. [@2] His agency said a
preliminary drug screening returned a negative result while a more detailed
analysis was awaited. [@1]

D.Ark was born in 2004 in Yanji, in China's Yanbian Korean Autonomous
Prefecture, and is of Korean Chinese ethnicity. [@1] He rose to fame on Mnet's
hip-hop shows Show Me the Money 777, Show Me the Money 9 and High School Rapper
4. [@1]

Under the Narcotics Control Act, using illegal drugs carries up to 10 years in
prison or a fine of up to 100 million won, while distribution carries life
imprisonment or at least five years. [@1]

On 7 January 2026, D.Ark posted a handwritten letter on social media about the
case. [@4] He said all the detailed tests had come back negative. [@4] Korean
outlets reported that he had been cleared of the marijuana use and distribution
allegations. [@3] He said the distribution allegation had never been part of the
investigation and was an exaggerated report. [@4]
