Nicaragua's National Police arrested José Gregorio Bermúdez, 59, at a police
checkpoint in Kukra Hill on the afternoon of Thursday 29 January 2026. [@1]
Officers stopped the taxi in which he was travelling as a passenger at kilometre
335, on the western side of the Buena Vista farm, in the South Caribbean Coast
Autonomous Region. [@1] Radio La Primerísima, citing a police report, said he
was detained in a taxi in Kukra Hill. [@3]

A body search found a black backpack holding three packages wrapped in adhesive
tape and containing green plant material. [@1] A field test came back positive
for marijuana and police gave the weight as 1 pound 13 ounces, about 0.82 kg.
[@1] Radio La Primerísima, citing a police report, gave the same weight of 1
pound 13 ounces. [@3] Viva Nicaragua Canal 13 also reported the weight as 1
pound 13 ounces. [@2]

Commissioner General Luis Valle, head of the National Police in the South
Caribbean, presented the case at a press conference. [@1] Police said the
operation was carried out under the Plan Muro de Contención, a strategy against
organised crime and drug trafficking. [@1] The detainee and the evidence were to
be sent to the competent court for judicial proceedings. [@1]
