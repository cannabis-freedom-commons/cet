In a cannabis suppression operation, the Trinidad and Tobago Police Service said
it eradicated a large cannabis crop in the Marac, Moruga Forest Reserve. [@1]
The operation was led by the Special Intelligence Unit's Jungle Operations Team
with the Firearms Trafficking Interdiction Unit, Moruga Police, the Southern
Division Task Force, the Air Support Unit and the Trinidad and Tobago Regiment
Special Forces. [@1]

Supervised by Sergeant Lee of the SIU Intelligence Sub-Unit, the team went about
two kilometres into the forest reserve. [@1] There they discovered seven
cannabis fields spanning about eleven lots of land, containing an estimated
16,100 fully grown cannabis plants. [@1] The officers also found a seedling bed
with around 1,500 seedlings and a campsite. [@2]

The TTPS said all the cannabis plants, seedlings and the campsite were
destroyed. [@1] Police put the estimated street value of the destroyed cannabis
at TT$16.1 million. [@1]
