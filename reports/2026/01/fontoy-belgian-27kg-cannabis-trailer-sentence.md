A 55-year-old Belgian man, Nico De Boey, appeared before the Thionville
correctional court on Monday 12 January 2026 and was sentenced to 30 months of
firm imprisonment for transporting more than 27 kilograms of cannabis herb in
his trailer. [@1] On Tuesday 6 January he was stopped by customs for a routine
check on the A30 near Fontoy and initially said he was carrying no goods. [@1]

In the trailer he was towing, concealed beneath an artificial floor, were 22
heat-sealed bags holding 27.248 kilograms of cannabis herb. [@1] A customs
officer told the court the goods came from smuggling and put their value at more
than 130,000 euros. [@1]

The defendant said he believed the load was CBD, telling the court that an
Albanian he had met at a service station asked him to transport it in exchange
for a litre of CBD oil for his horses. [@1] The prosecution sought three years
of firm imprisonment and argued he was not in good faith. [@1] His lawyer said
he had been transparent, was far from being a trafficker or consumer, and had
acted to obtain CBD oil for his horses. [@1] After deliberation the court gave
him 30 months of firm imprisonment with continued detention, ordered him to pay
a 130,700 euro fine and permanently banned him from French territory. [@1]
