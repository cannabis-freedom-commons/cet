On 27 April 2026, Martinique gendarmes carried out a judicial operation in
Rivière-Pilote. [@1] Five people were detained on suspicion of involvement in
drug trafficking in the commune. [@1]

Investigators established that a drug-dealing point operated in the town centre
near the covered market. [@1] The dealing point was active on the public
highway, and the Marin gendarmerie research brigade had followed the trade for
several months. [@2] The operation drew on the Marin gendarmerie company, the
Martinique GIGN detachment and mobile gendarmerie squadrons on the island. [@1]
Searches found 65 grams of cannabis, a 9 mm handgun, precision scales, six
cartons of contraband cigarettes and more than 9,000 euros in cash. [@1]

All five suspects were presented to the judicial court on 30 April 2026. [@1]
Four were due to be tried by immediate procedure on 22 June 2026. [@1] Two were
placed in pre-trial detention and two under judicial supervision pending that
hearing. [@1] The fifth was convicted under an admission-of-guilt procedure and
given two years in prison, 12 months suspended, 140 hours of community service
and an electronic tag. [@1]
