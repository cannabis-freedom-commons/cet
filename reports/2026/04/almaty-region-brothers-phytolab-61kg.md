The Interior Ministry of Kazakhstan announced on 15 April 2026 that it had
dismantled what it called family schemes of drug dealing in Almaty and Almaty
Region. [@1] Kuandyk Alzhanov, deputy chairman of the ministry's Committee for
Combating Drug Crime, said officers had liquidated a phytolaboratory in Almaty
Region organised by two brothers born in 1996 and 1998. [@1] In a rented house
the brothers grew marijuana for further sale in a particularly large amount.
[@1] All the people named in the cases he described were placed in a temporary
detention centre. [@1]

Officers seized 102 pots of marijuana bushes with a total weight of 61
kilograms, together with LED grow lamps, ventilation systems and other
equipment. [@1] The Interior Ministry's Polisia.kz portal published the same
account, giving the seized bushes as 61 kilograms. [@2]

A pre-trial investigation was opened under part 3 of article 297 of the Criminal
Code, which covers illegal acquisition, storage, transport for sale, sending or
sale of narcotics and provides for up to 12 years in prison with confiscation of
property. [@1] Alzhanov also said that in the first 10 days of April about one
tonne of narcotics had been seized, more than 800 kilograms of it synthetic, a
total separate from the marijuana taken at the laboratory. [@1] The same
briefing described two other cases in the city of Almaty, one involving about
one kilogram of the synthetic drug alpha-PVP and another about 500 grams of
mephedrone, which are not counted as cannabis. [@1]
