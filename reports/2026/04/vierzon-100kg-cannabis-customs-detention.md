French customs officers intercepted a 61-year-old man at the Vierzon toll plaza
on Tuesday 7 April 2026 and found nearly 100 kilograms of drugs in his vehicle.
[@1] The seizure was described as 100 kg of cannabis. [@1] The man, a
sexagénaire — a French term for someone in his sixties — spent 96 hours in
police custody after the find. [@1]

On Friday 10 April 2026 he was presented to the tribunal correctionnel de
Bourges, the local criminal court, under a comparution immédiate, a procedure
for immediate arraignment following a short period of custody. [@1] His lawyer
was absent, so he asked the judges for a delay to prepare his defence, as the
law permits. [@1]

The court ordered him placed in provisional detention. [@1] A new hearing was
set for 15 May 2026, and he remained in custody while awaiting it; no verdict
had been returned at the April hearing. [@1]
