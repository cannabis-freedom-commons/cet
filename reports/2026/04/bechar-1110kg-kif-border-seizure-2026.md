Algerian army detachments from the Béchar military sector intercepted a shipment
of treated kif coming from Morocco on 14 April 2026. [@1] The operation led to
the seizure of 11 quintals and 10 kilograms of treated kif, commonly called
cannabis, or 1,110 kilograms. [@1] The Ministry of National Defence announced in
a communiqué that the load came from Moroccan territory and was intended to be
brought illegally into Algeria. [@1]

The ministry said the operation was carried out by combined detachments of the
People's National Army (ANP), Algeria's army, from the Béchar military sector in
the 3rd Military Region. [@3] The defence ministry announced the interception in
a communiqué on Wednesday 15 April, the day after the Tuesday operation. [@2] Le
Courrier d'Algérie quoted the communiqué saying the detachments acted after
making optimal use of intelligence and were targeting smuggling barons and drug
traffickers. [@4] The ministry called the seizure a qualitative operation that
confirmed once more the mobilisation of ANP units in all circumstances to
counter attempts to harm citizens' security and safety. [@1]
