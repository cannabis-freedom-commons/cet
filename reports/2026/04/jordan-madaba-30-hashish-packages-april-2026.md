Jordan's Anti-Narcotics Department arrested a drug dealer in his vehicle in
Madaba Governorate and seized 30 packages of hashish and 1 kilogram of crystal
methamphetamine. [@1] The Public Security Directorate issued the statement in
Amman on 22 April 2026. [@1] Arab News, citing Petra, reported the same Madaba
detention and the same seizure of 1 kg of crystal meth and 30 packages of
hashish. [@2]

Arab News said the Anti-Narcotics Department arrested multiple drug dealers in a
nationwide crackdown that week. [@2] The same announcement described a joint
operation in the Ruwaished district of Mafraq Governorate, near the border with
Iraq, against people suspected of smuggling drugs with airborne balloons, and
reported seizures of narcotic pills, smuggling balloons and firearms. [@2] The
directorate also reported crystal meth dealers arrested in Amman with narcotic
pills. [@1]

In Balqa Governorate the statement said wanted drug traffickers were arrested
and narcotics and firearms were seized. [@1] In Karak Governorate officers
arrested a dealer after a raid and seized narcotic pills and a firearm. [@1]
