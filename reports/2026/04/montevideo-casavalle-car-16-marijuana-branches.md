The Montevideo police headquarters announced on Thursday 23 April 2026 that
officers of the Programa de Alta Dedicación Operativa (PADO) had seized a car
holding 16 marijuana branches near the junction of Pioneros and Ajedrez, north
of the Casavalle neighbourhood. [@1] The operation began after the Equipo Águila
of the National Police's aviation directorate alerted officers to a car carrying
marijuana plants inside. [@1] When officers reached the vehicle it had no
occupants but the branches were still inside. [@1]

A police helicopter from the aviation directorate followed the car and alerted
PADO units to intercept it. [@2] Telemundo reported that the two men inside fled
on foot, leaving the car abandoned. [@2] The car came to a stop near Pioneros
and Ajedrez in the barrio 24 de Enero, where police found it full of freshly cut
marijuana plants. [@2] Neighbours threw stones at the police teams, and officers
withdrew the seized car. [@2]

PADO officers were joined by the Montevideo departmental anti-drug brigade, the
National Republican Guard and forensic police, the Interior Ministry said. [@3]
The car and the marijuana were taken to the departmental anti-drug brigade to
preserve the evidence and continue forensic examinations. [@3] Police
coordinated with the IRCCA, Uruguay's cannabis regulator, to analyse the seized
plants. [@3] The Interior Ministry placed the seizure in Casavalle, in the
jurisdiction of the 17th police station. [@3]
