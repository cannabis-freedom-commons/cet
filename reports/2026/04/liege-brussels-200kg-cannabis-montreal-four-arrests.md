Canadian customs officers in Montreal intercepted a consignment of 200 kilograms
of cannabis bound for Belgium through Zaventem airport. [@2] The Trafics
division of the federal judicial police (PJF) in Liège opened the investigation,
which established that the goods arriving at the airport were to be delivered in
Liège and then sent on to an address in the Brussels region. [@2] The
investigation found that several earlier suspect deliveries had followed the
same route during 2025. [@1]

On Thursday 2 April 2026, more than 100 police officers carried out eight
searches in the Liège and Brussels regions. [@1] The operation was led by the
Liège PJF with support from special units, the Brussels and Halle-Vilvoorde PJF,
a drone team from the Liège DCA and federal police dog handlers. [@2] Four
people were taken in and deprived of their liberty after the searches. [@1]

The searches produced 340,000 euros in cash, 9.5 kilograms of hashish, 2.5
kilograms of cannabis, a 9mm Beretta handgun, six luxury watches and luxury
handbags, clothes, shoes and jewellery. [@1] The watches included Patek
Philippe, Rolex and Cartier pieces, the prosecutor's office said. [@2] Three
suspects were brought before the investigating judge on Wednesday 8 April and
placed under an arrest warrant. [@1] The Trafics division continues the
investigation under the investigating judge's authority. [@2]
