Police in Rankin Borough said a 28-year-old man from the borough would face
multiple charges in connection with the search of a home on Rankin Boulevard.
[@1] The Eastern Regional Mon Valley Police Department said its officers
executed a search warrant at a house in the 100 block of Rankin Boulevard on
Thursday 2 April 2026. [@2] The warrant was issued as part of an investigation
into narcotics violations, the department said. [@1] Officers from the Pitcairn
Police Department and the Swissvale-Braddock Hills Police Department provided
assistance during the search. [@2]

During the search officers seized a quantity of marijuana and a gun that was
later found to have been stolen, police said. [@1] A photo released by the
department shows what appears to be a large amount of marijuana packed into
multiple plastic bags and two large containers. [@1] Police did not give a
specific amount or a value for the marijuana they seized. [@1]

Police have not publicly identified the man. [@2] It was not clear what specific
charges he would face. [@1]
