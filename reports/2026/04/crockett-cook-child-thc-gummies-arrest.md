A Crockett woman was jailed on Thursday after the Houston County Sheriff's
Office said her five-year-old son ate THC gummies. [@1] Kayla Delois Cook, 30,
was arrested and held in the Houston County Jail on a warrant charging her with
endangering a child, according to Sheriff Zak Benge. [@1] The sheriff's office
received a call on Tuesday concerning a lethargic child from the Texas
Department of Family and Protective Services. [@1]

An investigation uncovered that the child had eaten the gummies after gaining
access to the drugs and was taken to a local hospital, Benge said. [@1] The
sheriff's office obtained a warrant for Cook after it was determined there was
enough probable cause for her arrest, and Benge said the child recovered from
their medical issues. [@1] Charged with a state jail felony, Cook potentially
faces between 180 days in jail and two years in prison if convicted. [@1]
