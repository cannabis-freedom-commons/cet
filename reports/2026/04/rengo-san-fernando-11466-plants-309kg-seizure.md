Carabineros’ OS-7 Cachapoal section led the operation in coordination with the
Public Prosecutor’s Office, with support from the Air Police Prefecture and GOPE
under the 2025–2026 Cannabis Sativa crop-eradication plan. [@1] The operation
took place over several days in hard-to-reach foothill sectors between the
communes of Rengo and San Fernando in the O’Higgins region. [@1] No one was
detained during the large operation, and inquiries continued to establish
responsibility and possible links to organized crime. [@1]

Officers focused the searches on the Los Alpes and Aguas Buenas estates, where
they found 11,466 growing plants and 309 kilograms and 100 grams of processed
marijuana, plus bags of drug still drying. [@1] A second account confirms the
operation was directed by the Public Prosecutor’s Office with air-police and
GOPE support and centred on the Los Alpes and Aguas Buenas estates. [@2]

Officers also seized two calibre-12 shotguns with ammunition, supporting the
hypothesis that the plantation had armed protection. [@1] The O’Higgins zone
chief said that together with previous procedures more than 20 tonnes of
marijuana had been seized, equivalent to about 200,000 million pesos, calling it
the largest seizure to date in the region. [@1] The Rengo prosecutor said OS-7
had detected the plantation in a hard-to-reach area during an
illegal-cultivation investigation, describing more than half a tonne of
processed marijuana plus the seized plants. [@1] The second account likewise
reported that no arrests were made in the operation. [@2] It said two calibre-12
shotguns with ammunition were found at the site. [@2]
