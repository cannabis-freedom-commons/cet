Detectives from the Divisional Drugs Unit searched a residential property in the
Dunfanaghy area of County Donegal as part of Operation Tara. [@1] Officers found
quantities of suspected cannabis plants and herb, along with cocaine, MDMA and
tablets including benzodiazepines. [@1] Gardaí put the total value of the drugs
seized at an estimated €40,000. [@1]

A man in his 50s was arrested at the scene. [@1] He was held at a Garda station
under Section 2 of the Criminal Justice (Drug Trafficking) Act 1996. [@1] He was
released without charge pending further investigation. [@1] Donegal Daily
reported that €12,000 in cash was seized along with the drugs. [@2]

The drugs were to be sent to Forensic Science Ireland for analysis. [@1] The
seizure was made under Operation Tara, the national anti-drugs strategy launched
by the Garda Commissioner in July 2021. [@1]
