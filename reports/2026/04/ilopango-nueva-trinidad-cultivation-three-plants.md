Police arrested 45-year-old Cristóbal Humberto Medrano in the comunidad Nueva
Trinidad, in Ilopango, San Salvador Este, on suspicion of sowing and cultivating
marijuana. [@2] The National Civil Police said the detention in the community
formed part of security actions carried out in the area. [@3] The Infobae
account identified the detainee only as Cristóbal H. M., aged 45, and gave the
offence as sowing and cultivation of marijuana. [@1]

Officers seized a large portion of marijuana and three green marijuana plants
that were growing in pots inside the property raided. [@3] The police said the
three plants were taken from the man at the time of his arrest. [@2] No weight
or market value was published for the marijuana portion seized alongside the
plants. [@1]

Medrano was to be sent to the courts to answer under Salvadoran law. [@3]
Salvadoran law punishes the sowing, cultivation and harvesting of plants that
can produce illegal substances such as marijuana with 10 to 15 years in prison,
depending on the quantity seized and the repeat-offence record of the accused.
[@1] The authorities reiterated that possession and cultivation of marijuana,
even in small quantities, is a serious offence pursued under the Plan Control
Territorial, the government's security strategy against drug-related activity
and organised crime. [@1]
