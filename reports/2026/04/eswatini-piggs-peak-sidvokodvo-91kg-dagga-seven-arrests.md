Royal Eswatini Police Service (REPS) officers arrested seven people and seized
more than 90 kilograms of dagga in the Pigg's Peak and Sidvokodvo policing areas
on 6 April 2026. [@1] At about 15:30 on the Nsangwini/Nginamadvolo public road,
Pigg's Peak officers manning a roadblock stopped a vehicle carrying 36.4
kilograms of dagga without the required permits. [@1] The three people arrested
at that stop were a 57-year-old woman and two men aged 37 and 38, from
Luvinjelweni and Meleti, travelling in a Honda Fit. [@2]

Within the hour, Sidvokodvo police stopped two vehicles in the Gundvwini area
and uncovered a further 54.9 kilograms of dagga. [@1] The two vehicles were a VW
Polo carrying 32.5 kilograms, whose occupants aged 31 and 28 were arrested, and
a Mazda 3 carrying 22.4 kilograms, whose occupants aged 32 and 34 were also
charged. [@2]

Police said all the suspects had since been formally charged and that
investigations were continuing. [@1] The police credited community tip-offs and
the "Sikhona" community policing programme for the interceptions, describing the
roadblocks as part of a shift towards preventive policing. [@1]
