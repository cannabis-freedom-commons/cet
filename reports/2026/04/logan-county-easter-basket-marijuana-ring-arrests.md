Adairville Police Chief Aaron Ashby and Auburn Officer Charles Wethington
arrested Jeremy Green, 22, of Glasgow, after seeing him pacing between vehicles
and Casey’s Gas Station on Friday evening. [@1] Ashby said he saw an open
alcoholic beverage in Green's vehicle and that Green was driving on a licence
suspended for a previous DUI. [@1]

A search of Green's vehicle yielded $1,010 and three cellphones. [@1]
Investigators found communication on an encrypted app after obtaining a warrant
for the cellphones. [@1] Messages between Green and 35-year-old Jordan Bailey,
of Auburn, described an alleged sale of a quarter-pound of marijuana for $925.
[@1] Green allegedly admitted delivering marijuana to Bailey at his home on
Howlett Drive in Auburn and said Bailey contacted him weekly to buy marijuana
for resale in Logan County. [@1] A search warrant was executed at Bailey’s home
on April 4, and officers located a “large amount” of suspected marijuana,
suspected cocaine, an unspecified drug, U.S. currency, drug paraphernalia and
multiple firearms. [@1] The Glasgow Police Department searched Green's home in
Glasgow and located “several more pounds” of suspected marijuana and 75 THC vape
pens. [@1] Bailey was arrested and lodged at the Logan County Detention Center.
[@1]

Green was charged with multiple counts of trafficking in marijuana over five
pounds, trafficking in marijuana of eight ounces to less than five pounds,
driving on a DUI-suspended licence, possessing an open alcohol container in a
vehicle and drug paraphernalia. [@1] Bailey was charged with wanton endangerment
relating to three juveniles, possession of cocaine and an unspecified drug,
marijuana trafficking and drug paraphernalia. [@1] Ashby said the operation
continued and additional charges were expected. [@1] Officers from Adairville,
Auburn and Glasgow were participating in the investigation. [@1]
