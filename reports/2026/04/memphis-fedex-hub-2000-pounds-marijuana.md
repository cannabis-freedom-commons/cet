The Memphis Safe Task Force and Shelby County recovered about 2,000 pounds of
marijuana at the FedEx Memphis hub, the task force said. [@1] The Department of
Justice said in a release on Thursday that the task force and Shelby County
worked with FedEx to recover the marijuana. [@1] The U.S. Marshals Service
shared the release on Thursday without disclosing how the drugs were discovered
or where they came from. [@3] The Department of Justice did not say where the
drugs were headed or whether they had all travelled in one shipment. [@1]

Local coverage described the haul as more than 2,000 pounds of packaged
marijuana. [@2] FedEx said it does not tolerate the use of its network for
illegal purposes. [@2] The company said it fully cooperates with authorities in
their investigations. [@2]

The FedEx World Hub inside Memphis International Airport is the company's
largest sorting facility, spanning 940 acres and handling more than 1.5 million
packages a night. [@1]
