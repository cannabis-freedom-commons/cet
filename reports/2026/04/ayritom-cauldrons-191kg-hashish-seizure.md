A second seizure took place on 8 April 2026, when a commercial shipment of metal
cauldrons arrived from Afghanistan for transit through Uzbekistan. [@1] On 8
April, officers intercepted a drug smuggling attempt at the Ayritom border
customs post in the Surkhandarya region. [@2] The interception was made by the
inter-agency border unit, including an officer of the Customs Committee of
Uzbekistan. [@1]

After X-ray screening and risk assessment, officers carried out a detailed
physical inspection of the 750-box cargo. [@1] A cargo truck travelling in
transit from Afghanistan to Kazakhstan, driven by a 1990-born citizen of
Uzbekistan, was carrying 750 Afghan pressure cookers with a documented cargo
weight of 4.1 tons. [@2] Officers found that 210 of the cauldrons had been
tampered with to conceal hashish within their structure. [@1] The hashish was
hidden inside double-walled kazans, traditional cast-iron cauldrons, according
to the State Security Service press office. [@3] That hashish weighed 190.8
kilograms, later confirmed by laboratory analysis. [@1]

The authorities gave the preliminary market value of the seized hashish as more
than US$5 million. [@2] A pre-trial investigation into the seizure was under
way. [@2]
