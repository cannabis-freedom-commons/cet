On Wednesday, agents with the Morgan County Sheriff's Drug Enforcement Unit
responded to Crooked Hill Drive in Falkville in connection with an ongoing
narcotics investigation. [@1] Upon arrival at the Crooked Hill Drive home,
agents made contact with the homeowner, identified as 28-year-old Steven Yeager.
[@1] As a result of the investigation, Yeager was placed under arrest and
charged with possession of dangerous drugs (drug trafficking). [@1] He was
booked into the Morgan County Jail and is currently being held on a $10,000
bond. [@1] Additional charges in this case are pending, including three counts
of unlawful possession of a controlled substance. [@1]

During the investigation, authorities said Yeager was in possession of cocaine,
methamphetamine and MDMA, as well as approximately six pounds of marijuana
(about 2.72 kilograms). [@1] Authorities also said he had approximately 120
grams of THC concentrate and more than 1,000 THC vape cartridges. [@1]

Agents also located drug packaging materials used for distribution, as well as a
large amount of money inside the home. [@1]
