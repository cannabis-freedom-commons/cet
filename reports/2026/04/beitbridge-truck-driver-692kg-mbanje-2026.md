Security agents at the Beitbridge Border Post arrested a Harare-bound truck
driver on suspicion of smuggling marijuana from South Africa on Tuesday 7 April
2026. [@1] Initial investigations found that the driver had loaded 34 bales of
the drug in Musina, Limpopo province. [@1] Zimra officials found the packages
concealed among commercial goods in the trailer of a South African haulage truck
bound for Harare. [@2]

Zimra put the weight of the marijuana at 691.67 kg. [@3] Police valued the
seized consignment at US$69,167. [@1] The driver was arrested at the scene and
the truck was seized as evidence. [@2]

Chief Superintendent Mesuli Ncube, the police commander for Beitbridge District,
said the driver had been charged with illegal possession of dangerous drugs and
would appear in court soon. [@1] Zimra said it handed the driver to the Zimbabwe
Republic Police's CID Drugs and Narcotics division for further investigation.
[@3] The suspect was expected to appear at the Beitbridge Magistrates' Court
later that week. [@3]

Zimbabwe has set up four ZRP Drugs and Narcotics Units at the Beitbridge,
Victoria Falls, Chirundu and Mutare border posts and uses drones for border
surveillance. [@1]
