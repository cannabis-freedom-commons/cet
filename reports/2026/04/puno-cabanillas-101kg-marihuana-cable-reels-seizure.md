Anti-drug police in Puno and the Public Prosecutor's Office seized more than 100
kilograms of marijuana from a cargo vehicle in Cabanillas district, San Román
province, with customs officers taking part. [@1] The load weighed 101.82
kilograms and was hidden inside cable reels. [@2] The operation took place on
the Arequipa-Juliaca road in Cabanillas district. [@2] Officers found the
marijuana packed into five reels of polypropylene rope of different colours,
hidden to avoid detection at checks. [@2]

Field tests on samples drawn through the reels identified the contents as
cannabis sativa, known in Peru as marijuana. [@2] Authorities estimated the
load's illegal-market value at about 33,600 US dollars. [@2]

No arrests were reported, and authorities said they were investigating to
identify the people involved and dismantle networks that move illicit goods
toward the Peru-Bolivia border. [@1] The vehicle and the marijuana were handed
to specialised units, which were to continue inquiries into who sent the
shipment and where it was going. [@2] The operation also involved anti-drug
officers from San Román province based in Juliaca. [@1]
