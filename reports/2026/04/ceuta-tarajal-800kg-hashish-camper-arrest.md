Spanish Civil Guard officers arrested a woman at the Tarajal border post in
Ceuta on 13 April 2026 after intercepting the camper van she was driving during
a routine check. [@3] She was the vehicle's only occupant and was detained on
suspicion of an offence against public health. [@4] The arrest was made by the
First Fiscal and Border Company of the Civil Guard's Ceuta command. [@4]

Officers grew suspicious after detecting structural anomalies in the camper van
during checks on vehicles entering Ceuta from Morocco. [@4] A more thorough
search revealed a hidden compartment built specifically to carry drugs,
containing numerous bales of hashish. [@3] The bales weighed approximately 800
kilograms, according to the Civil Guard. [@4] APS reported that the woman was
about to cross the border separating Ceuta from Morocco with about 800 kg of
hashish hidden in the camper van she was driving. [@1]

The woman, the seized drugs and the camper van were placed at the disposal of
the competent judicial authority. [@4] The Civil Guard said it had opened an
investigation into who was behind the shipment, calling the driver a mere link
in the trafficking chain. [@2]
