Police nationale de La Réunion announced on Monday 4 May 2026 that three people
had been arrested over a drug-trafficking network. [@1] The network, described
by police as a local resale operation, was dismantled after a man was stopped by
the Brigade anti-criminalité territoriale (BAC) in the Chaudron area of
Saint-Denis on 27 April. [@1] The man was found carrying cannabis, cocaine and
MDMA, and investigators opened an inquiry and placed him in police custody. [@2]

A search of his home found 2.3 kilograms of cannabis resin. [@3] The same search
yielded 3,700 MDMA tablets, more than 4,000 euros in cash, several telephones,
two pellet guns and a hunting knife. [@2] Officers seized nearly 70 grams of
cocaine and 2,000 euros in cash in the arrests that followed. [@2]

Auditions of the suspect's circle revealed a drug-trafficking network and led to
several further arrests. [@2] Police said the operation dismantled a local
network selling drugs and that those involved were handed over to the courts.
[@2]
