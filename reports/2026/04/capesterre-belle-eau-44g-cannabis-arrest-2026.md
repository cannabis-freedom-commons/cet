Gendarmes in Capesterre-Belle-Eau, Guadeloupe, arrested a man on the morning of
Saturday 4 April 2026 after he refused to comply with a control. [@1] The local
gendarmerie brigade, backed by the Détachement de surveillance et d'intervention
(DSI), a surveillance and intervention unit, picked him up in the town centre.
[@2] The suspect was placed in police custody after the arrest. [@3]

During the initial search officers found 44 grams of cannabis and 55 grams of
heroin on him. [@1] They also seized 1,717 euros in cash, whose origin
investigators said they questioned. [@1]

A follow-up search at the man's home or in his immediate surroundings turned up
a concealed pistol. [@1] RCI reported that the handgun was fitted with a laser
sight. [@3] The gendarmerie said the intervention illustrated its mobilisation
against crime, trafficking and the circulation of weapons. [@1] Investigators
were continuing to establish the full extent of the man's activities and any
links to drug or weapons trafficking networks. [@1]
