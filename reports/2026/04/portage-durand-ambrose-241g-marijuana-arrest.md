Portage police identified the driver as Durand Ambrose, 49, of Chicago. [@1] A
Portage officer stopped Ambrose's vehicle around 12:14 p.m. on Sunday for
speeding westbound on U.S. 12. [@1] Ambrose was taken to the Porter County Jail.
[@1] He is charged with a felony count of possessing more than 30 grams of
marijuana with a prior drug conviction. [@1]

The officer reported seeing plastic bags from the Bloomery Cannabis dispensary
on the front passenger-side floorboard of the vehicle and smelling a strong odor
of the drug. [@1] Ambrose reportedly told police he was returning from a
Michigan marijuana dispensary and was handcuffed. [@1] He allegedly admitted to
purchasing the marijuana for personal use. [@1] Police said they recovered
various packaged forms of marijuana from the vehicle, weighing a total of 241
grams. [@1]

Ambrose had purchased 241 grams of marijuana from a legal dispensary a few miles
away in Michigan and attempted to transport it into Indiana, where it remains
illegal, according to police. [@1]
