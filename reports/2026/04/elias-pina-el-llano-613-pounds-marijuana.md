Agents of the Dirección Nacional de Control de Drogas (DNCD) and the Specialized
Prosecutor's Office against Illicit Trafficking of Narcotic Substances searched
two houses in the El Llano sector of Elías Piña province. [@1] The houses, on an
unnamed street in the border province, were treated as a storage centre for
controlled substances, and the two searches were simultaneous and carried out
under court orders to dismantle a suspected trafficking network. [@2]
Authorities announced the result on Monday 13 April 2026. [@1]

In the first house officers seized several sacks holding 92 bales of suspected
marijuana, with a preliminary weight of 613 pounds (about 278 kilograms), a
Mitsubishi Montero SUV and documents for the investigation. [@1] In the second
house they seized 10 suspected marijuana plants, a further portion of the plant
weighing about one pound, a Suzuki motorcycle with no plate and no visible
chassis number, and six mobile phones. [@1]

The opening line of the same official note also counted 20 plants, rather than
the 10 listed for the second search. [@1] No suspects were reported in custody:
authorities said the investigation was continuing and that they were still
looking for several men, most of them already identified, and urged them to turn
themselves in. [@1] The marijuana packages were sent under chain of custody to
the National Institute of Forensic Sciences (Inacif) so the exact weight could
be determined. [@1]

Listín Diario, reproducing the press note, placed the houses on an unnamed
street in El Llano and said DNCD agents and the specialised prosecutor's office
led the operation in the border province. [@2]
