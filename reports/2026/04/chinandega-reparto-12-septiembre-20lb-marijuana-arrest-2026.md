Police profiled a motorcycle with plate CH 52460 in Reparto 12 de Septiembre in
the municipality of Chinandega at 6 a.m. on Sunday. [@1] The rider was
identified as 27-year-old German Antonio Sanchez Mairena, the sole person named
for the motorcycle stop. [@1] The detainee, the drugs and the other evidence
were placed at the disposal of the competent authorities for judicial
proceedings. [@1]

Officers searching the motorcycle found a box holding 20 packages of marijuana
weighing 20 pounds 10 ounces, about 9.36 kg. [@1] The National Police said the
23 kilograms of cocaine in the same weekend roundup were seized at the El
Guasaule border post in Somotillo, a separate seizure not counted in the
cannabis figures. [@1]

Police reported the two weekend seizures together as occurring in different
parts of Chinandega department. [@1] The cocaine interception was carried out on
Saturday 25 April 2026 at 11:30 a.m. as part of the Muro de Contencion strategy
against organized crime and drug trafficking. [@1]
