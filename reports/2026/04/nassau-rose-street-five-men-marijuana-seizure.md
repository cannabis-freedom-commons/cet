Five men were arrested and cautioned after officers uncovered suspected
marijuana during a targeted operation on Rose Street in Nassau on Thursday
afternoon. [@1] The five men were aged 26, 30, 41, 48 and 50. [@1]

Officers assigned to Operation Black Scorpion were acting on intelligence
shortly after 12:30 p.m. when they approached a tan-and-brown business
establishment off Marathon Road. [@1] Their attention was drawn to a man seen
kicking a box beneath a gold Jeep. [@1] A search of the man and the box revealed
a significant quantity of suspected marijuana, and officers extended the search
to a white Acura on the property, where additional drugs were reportedly found.
[@1] Police said the drugs weighed approximately 15.2 pounds (about 6.89
kilograms) and had an estimated street value of $30,760. [@1]

The Drug Enforcement Unit said its investigations were continuing. [@1]
