Nepal's Narcotics Control Bureau arrested two Indian nationals on suspicion of
smuggling marijuana from Thailand. [@1] They were Mohsin Salauddin Khan, 39, and
Mohammad Rahil Mohammad Tahir Gangoo, 31. [@1] Republican Daily reported that
bureau officers tracked the two Indian nationals from Tribhuvan International
Airport and detained them at a hotel. [@2] Police took Khan into custody after
he entered a hotel in Gaushala, Kathmandu Metropolitan City Ward No. 9, and
searched it. [@1]

Upon searching the two bags the pair had brought, police discovered seven
kilograms of marijuana. [@1] Bureau Deputy Superintendent of Police Jeevan
Niraula said both men were arrested in the same hotel room and the ganja was
recovered from their bags. [@3] Republican Daily said its press statement
reported that the 39-year-old had arrived from Bangkok via Dubai on Saturday.
[@2] Rahil had arrived in Kathmandu on Chait 26 via a Malaysia Airlines flight
from Bangkok via Kuala Lumpur. [@1] Both were arrested together when Khan
arrived with the marijuana on Chait 28. [@1]

A Kathmandu District Court remand was obtained and both suspects remained under
detailed investigation under the Narcotic Drugs (Control) Act, 2033. [@1]
Republican Daily reported that the bureau produced both men before the Kathmandu
District Court to obtain permission for further investigation. [@2] 18Nepal
noted the high demand for Bangkok ganja in India, which it said drives
trafficking through Nepal. [@3]
