On 14 April 2026 a guarantees judge in Panama's First Judicial Circuit, Kerine
Jaén, validated a plea agreement and sentenced three men aged 20, 22 and 29 for
international drug trafficking. [@3] The 20-year-old was given 100 months in
prison and the other two 110 months, with a further fine of 100 balboas payable
to the national treasury. [@3] The judgment was numbered 651 and issued on 14
April 2026. [@3] The case began when SENAN, Panama's national aeronaval service,
arrested the three southeast of Punta Coco in the Archipiélago de Las Perlas and
seized 1,048 packages of marijuana. [@3] The judge legalised the arrests and
accepted the charge brought by prosecutor Lisbeth Franco, with public defenders
representing the three. [@3]

SENAN reported seizing 1,048 packages of suspected illicit substance, arresting
three people and confiscating an artisanal boat southeast of Punta Coco. [@2]
Officers found sacks inside a suspicious vessel and detained its crew. [@2]
Acting on instructions from Panama's Drug Prosecutor's Office, they took the
boat and the detainees to the Base Naval Capitán de Fragata Noel Antonio
Rodríguez Justavino. [@2] At the base they counted 21 sacks holding 1,048
packages, whose substance was still to be determined. [@2] At the time of the
operation the contents remained under forensic verification to establish the
exact substance. [@1] The operation formed part of stepped-up anti-narcotics
activity at strategic maritime and port points in Panama. [@1]
