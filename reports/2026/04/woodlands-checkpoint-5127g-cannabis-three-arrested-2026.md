Three men were arrested after drugs were found in a Malaysia-registered car
arriving at Woodlands Checkpoint. [@1] The driver, a 23-year-old Malaysian man,
was arrested for attempting to smuggle drugs. [@1] In a follow-up operation near
Woodlands Industrial Park on the same day, CNB officers arrested two Bangladeshi
men aged 42 and 36 for suspected drug offences. [@1]

ICA officers directed the car for enhanced checks on the evening of 14 April
2026 and found two plastic bags believed to contain controlled drugs in the
boot. [@2] CNB officers were immediately alerted to the detection and conducted
further checks. [@1] Officers seized about 5,127g of cannabis and 2,072g of
methamphetamine, known as Ice, from the two bags. [@1] The authorities said the
seized drugs were worth more than $547,000 and could feed the addiction of about
1,900 abusers for a week. [@2]

The Immigration and Checkpoints Authority and the Central Narcotics Bureau
announced the detection in a joint statement on April 23. [@1] Investigations
were ongoing. [@1] Those found guilty of importing into or exporting from
Singapore more than 500g of cannabis or more than 250g of methamphetamine may
face the death penalty. [@1]
