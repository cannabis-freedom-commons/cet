Azerbaijan's Interior Ministry said five people were arrested in Garadagh
district, Baku, on suspicion of involvement in illegal drug trafficking. [@1]
The ministry identified them as Mammadali Mammadov, born 1991, Orkhan Mekteyev,
born 1991, Elmar Mekhtizade, born 1993, Vagif Karimov, born 1989, and Parvin
Orujova, born 1992. [@1] Officers of the Garadagh District Police Department
carried out a series of operations against illegal drug circulation in the
district, the Interior Ministry said. [@3]

The press service of Azerbaijan's Interior Ministry said officers seized a total
of 70.875 kilograms of marijuana with psychotropic substances added. [@1]
Investigators said the suspects had criminal links with citizens of Iran and
bought the drugs in border districts. [@2] The ministry said the drugs were
intended for placement at various addresses and for financial gain. [@3]

Criminal cases were opened and a court ordered detention as a preventive
measure. [@1] The investigation continues. [@1]
