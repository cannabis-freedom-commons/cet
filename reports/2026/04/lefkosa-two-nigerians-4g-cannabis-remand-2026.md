Police in Lefkoşa stopped Jesutimileyin Bolarinwa Adelasa on Mehmet Akif Caddesi
at about 21:30 on 22 April 2026 and found about 3 g of cannabis-type material
wrapped in black nylon in his tracksuit pocket. [@4] A court-ordered search of
the flat in Taşkınköy between 22:20 and 23:00 found about 1 g more cannabis, a
bullet casing, a blue non-explosive training grenade and a plastic grinder. [@4]
Both men are Nigerian nationals who had been living in Cyprus, and police said
the residence held drugs, bullet casings and a hand grenade. [@1] Okoro was
arrested during the search after police found a metal grinder with suspected
drug residue in his room at the same address. [@1]

The two men were brought to court charged with unlawful possession of drugs,
unlawful possession of explosives and living in the country without a permit.
[@4] At the first hearing on 23 April, Judge Jale Ergüden ordered them held for
one day in police custody. [@2] Judge Şevket Gazi then ordered six more days of
detention at a hearing on 24 April. [@3] At a further hearing on 7 May, the same
judge ordered them held for up to 20 days. [@4]

Police told the court that Adelasa had been in northern Cyprus without
permission since 10 January 2022 and Okoro since 29 November 2023. [@4] Okoro
said he had found the grenade on the ground in Tatlısu and taken it home for
decoration, and denied being a drug dealer. [@3] Adelasa said the grinder
belonged to his deceased roommate, whose family never collected his belongings.
[@1] Police said the bullet and grenade contained no explosive material and that
the suspected drugs had been sent for analysis. [@4]
