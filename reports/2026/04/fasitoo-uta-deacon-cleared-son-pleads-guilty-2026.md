Maauga Divine Pasefika Simeti, 20, told Samoa's Supreme Court that the marijuana
and meth utensils police seized in a raid on a home in Fasitoo-Uta belonged to
him, not to his father or Letasi Peter Pesamino. [@1] The court dismissed all
charges against his father, former Catholic deacon Wilson Simeti, 51, and
Pesamino, and released both men. [@1] Wilson Simeti, also known as Willow
Matafeo, had been one of the Catholic deacons suspended by the Archbishop in
2024 while investigations were conducted into illicit drugs. [@1]

Police arrested the three during a drug raid on the home about two weeks before
25 March 2026. [@3] All three were charged with possession of marijuana and drug
utensils. [@2] The son pleaded guilty to the drug-related charges brought
against all three defendants. [@1] After the confession, Prosecutor Maiavatele
Timothy Fesili applied to withdraw the charges against the two older men, and
Justice Fepuleai Ameperosa Roma granted the application. [@1] The Samoa Observer
reported that the prosecution withdrew the charges against Wilson Simeti because
it said there was insufficient evidence to proceed. [@2] In March, Justice Roma
granted Wilson Simeti bail while Pesamino was returned to Tanumalala Prison to
await his next appearance. [@3] Maauga Simeti was on bail and his sentencing was
set for 4 May 2026. [@2]
