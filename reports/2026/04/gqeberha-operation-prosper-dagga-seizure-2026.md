Police and soldiers confiscated mandrax, dagga (cannabis) and an undisclosed
amount of cash on the first day of a crime-fighting operation in the Eastern
Cape. [@1] The police, with the help of the soldiers, raided a number of houses
in Gqeberha that were suspected of criminal activities. [@1]

The soldiers were deployed under Operation Prosper, which aims to disrupt
organised crime including drug trafficking, illegal firearms and extortion
syndicates. [@1] Close to 300 soldiers were deployed to the province. [@1]

One Gqeberha resident, Mavis Dirkse, welcomed the deployment of soldiers. [@1]
Dirkse said the community felt a great sense of peace and freedom, with children
running around, and that the army's arrival was welcome over the Easter weekend
when crime tends to rise. [@1] She said residents were happy to have the army
there and planned to be outside braaiing that night, as they could not do at
other times. [@1]
