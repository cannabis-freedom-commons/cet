Five drug mules were intercepted on arrival at Roland-Garros airport in La
Réunion in the first week of April 2026. [@1] Officers seized 25 kilograms of
cannabis and 1.5 kilograms of cannabis flower pollen from them. [@1] They also
seized 10 kilograms of cocaine, and all the products were confiscated. [@1]
Zinfos974 reported the cannabis weight as 25.5 kilograms and described the five
arrests as operations coordinated between the Douanes, or customs, and the
Police nationale. [@2]

All five were presented to justice and some received prison sentences of up to
four years. [@1] Zinfos974 said all the detainees were prosecuted and that the
first rulings had already been handed down. [@2] The forces of order said the
results came from operational intelligence and cooperation between the customs
service and the national police. [@1] The arrests brought to 19 the number of
mules intercepted on arrival at Gillot since the start of the year. [@1]
