Border guards of the Darkad outpost of Tajikistan's GKNB Border Troops in Mir
Said Ali Hamadoni district intercepted a drug shipment from Afghanistan during
the night of 10 February 2026. [@1] The Khovar account of the official statement
timed the interception at about 01:20 on 10 February 2026 in the Darkad
outpost's service zone. [@2] Four Afghan smugglers illegally crossed the state
border from Afghanistan into Tajikistan. [@1] One smuggler, about 45-year-old
Avalmir valadi Abdulhakim from Maydapata village in Darkad district of
Afghanistan's Takhar province, was detained. [@2]

Officers seized 46 packets of hashish-type drugs from the detained smuggler.
[@2] Two rubber inner tubes the smugglers had used to cross the border illegally
were also seized at the scene. [@2] The three remaining members of the group
used the darkness to withdraw back into Afghanistan. [@2]

Tajikistan's State Committee for National Security opened a criminal case under
articles 200 on drug trafficking with intent to sell, 289 on smuggling and 335
on illegal crossing of Tajikistan's state border, and an investigation is under
way. [@2]
