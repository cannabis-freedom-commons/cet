The Grupo Especial de Control Aeroportuario (GECA) and the Aduana Nacional
intercepted the shipment inside the ALBO customs warehouse at El Alto
International Airport under the Alerta Aeropuerto operation. [@2] The parcel had
come from Miami and had been declared as disposable electronic devices. [@4] A
detector-dog alert led to a physical inspection, and the chemical field test was
positive for marijuana. [@2]

Authorities seized 452 vapes containing 15.6 kilograms gross of liquid
marijuana. [@1] Customs said the case began on 14 January when import
declaration DI-2026-211-2011656 was assigned to red-channel inspection. [@3] The
physical inspection found five packages weighing 45 kilos in total containing
vapes. [@3] The customs laboratory reported on 30 January that the liquid
contained delta-9 and delta-8 THC. [@3]

Customs notified the FELCN on 5 February, and on Thursday 26 February both
agencies carried out the joint examination with a field test and detector dog
positive for THC before the FELCN seized the goods. [@3] The case was handed to
the Public Prosecutor's Office, which took over the investigation, while
shipping documents were seized and inquiries continued to identify the senders
and possible recipients. [@2]
