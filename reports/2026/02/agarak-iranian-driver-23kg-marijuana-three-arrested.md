Customs officers at the Agarak border crossing on Armenia's border with Iran
found 34 transparent bags of marijuana, weighing about 23.4 kilograms, hidden
among the iron supports of a truck trailer. [@1] The truck was driven by an
Iranian citizen, whom the customs statement names only by initials. [@3] He was
detained and taken to the Syunik regional investigative department, and a court
ordered his pre-trial detention. [@3] The State Revenue Committee, Armenia's
customs authority, said the search was carried out jointly by its customs
service and control department and its anti-smuggling department. [@2]

Two Armenian citizens were arrested on the road from Yerevan to Masis on
suspicion of smuggling and selling the drugs, in an operation the customs
service called a controlled delivery and purchase. [@3] Officers from the
customs anti-smuggling department, the police drug-trafficking department, the
Syunik and Vayots Dzor police departments and the patrol service worked out
where the consignment was probably headed. [@3] A criminal case was opened
against both in the Investigative Committee's main department for economic
crimes and smuggling, and both were arrested. [@3]

The criminal proceedings were transferred to that main department from the
Syunik investigators. [@1] ArmenianReport, citing the customs press service,
reported that investigators were still establishing who else was involved in the
drug trafficking. [@2]
