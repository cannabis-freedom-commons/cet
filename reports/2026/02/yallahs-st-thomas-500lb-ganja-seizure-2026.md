Police seized more than 500 pounds of marijuana, about 227 kilograms, in South
Haven, Yallahs, St Thomas, on the afternoon of Friday 20 February 2026. [@1]
Officers said they acted on information and went to a premises in the South
Haven district at about 3 p.m. [@1] There they found several white knitted bags
containing the drugs. [@1] No arrests were made in connection with the seizure.
[@1]

The police put the estimated street value of the ganja at $10 million. [@1]
Investigators suspect the consignment may be linked to the guns-for-drugs trade
and said they were trying to find people connected to it. [@1] Deputy
Superintendent Jovaine Willams, the parish crime officer, said the removal of
over 500 pounds of ganja from circulation was a significant blow to networks
that exchange narcotics for illegal firearms. [@1]

IRIE FM reported that officers acting on intelligence carried out the operation
at about 2:30 p.m. at an abandoned house in the community. [@2] The bags held
compressed vegetable matter resembling ganja. [@2]
