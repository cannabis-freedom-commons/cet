The Phnom Penh municipal court sentenced two British nationals to 14 and 10 years and a total 24 million riel fine for using, possessing, trafficking and transporting cannabis. The verdict was pronounced by presiding judge Tithsothy Borachat and cited Articles 40 and 45 of Cambodia's Law on Drug Control. [@2] Robert Brown, 41, received the 14-year term and Hogg Jordan James, 28, the 10-year term. [@2] They had been arrested at Phnom Penh airport on 17 June 2025 with 19.9 kg of dried cannabis before a UK flight. The marijuana was believed to have come from Thailand. [@2] The two men were arrested by anti-drug police. [@2] The 14-year term exceeds the 5–10 year band for 8–20 kg, suggesting aggravation or a charging issue. [@1] Under Cambodian law the convicts had one month to appeal the verdict. [@2]
