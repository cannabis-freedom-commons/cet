Asheville police arrested three people on gun and drug charges in West Asheville
after repeated complaints about drug activity in the area. [@1] Officers made
the arrests in the Short Michigan Avenue area at about 10:45 p.m. on 17 February
2026. [@1] Police said they first made contact with Jami Marie Lee Tressler, 32,
who was found with fentanyl, methamphetamine and a large amount of cash. [@1] A
residence on Michigan Avenue that Tressler was seen leaving was searched, and
Irvin Franklin Freeman, 55, and Kara Nicole Ervin, 28, were taken into custody
and charged. [@2]

Officers seized 27.6 grams of marijuana. [@1] The same seizure included 53.14
grams of fentanyl and 6.8 grams of methamphetamine. [@1] Four handguns were
found in the residence, one of them reported stolen. [@1] Officers also took
$5,016 in cash. [@2]

Tressler was charged with simple possession of a Schedule VI controlled
substance among other counts. [@2] She was booked into the Buncombe County
Detention Facility under a $250,000 secured bond. [@2] Freeman was charged with
felony possession of a stolen firearm and possession of a firearm by a felon.
[@1] He was booked with no bond. [@2] Ervin was charged with possession of a
Schedule II substance and drug paraphernalia and released after posting bond.
[@2]
