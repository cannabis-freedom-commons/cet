During February 2026 Panama's drug prosecution service with the National Police
intelligence directorate carried out microtrafficking operations in the San
Miguelito district. [@1] The actions focused on Villa Lucre, Los Andes, Torrijos
Carter, San Isidro, Samaria and Brisas del Golf, using controlled buys and
undercover techniques. [@1]

The operations opened 14 case files for alleged sale of illicit substances that
were later brought before the courts. [@1] Officers seized illicit substances
including cannabis sativa (marijuana), cocaine, ecstasy pills and THC
derivatives. [@1] In total 14 people were detained in San Miguelito, including a
foreign citizen, for alleged commercialization and distribution in urban areas.
[@1] Ten people received sentences of up to 84 months in prison through plea
agreements, while other cases remained open in investigation or with a six-month
investigation term. [@1]

The Public Ministry presented these results as its February balance against
microtrafficking, published on 5 March 2026. [@2]
