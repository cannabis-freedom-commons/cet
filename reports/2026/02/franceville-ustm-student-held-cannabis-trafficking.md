Officers of the Haut-Ogooué provincial anti-drug office arrested 34-year-old
Gabonese student Josué Neville Bourébé in Franceville on the morning of 20
February 2026. [@3] Bourébé is enrolled at the University of Sciences and
Techniques of Masuku (USTM) in Franceville. [@1] The officers acted on
information described as reliable and carried out the operation at dawn. [@1]

A search of his home seized a quantity of cannabis and a pot containing cannabis
plants. [@1] He was brought before an investigating magistrate at the
Franceville Court of First Instance and charged with cannabis trafficking. [@1]
The judge ordered his remand and he was transferred to Yéné central prison to
await judgment. [@3]

The operation was presented as part of a broader drive by judicial and security
authorities in Haut-Ogooué to dismantle distribution networks in student circles
and urban centres, with stronger controls announced. [@2]
