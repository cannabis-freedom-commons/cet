At about 4 a.m. on Saturday morning, officers of the Narcotics Brigade
intercepted an unusual convoy on the route. [@1,2,3] The brigade is the Sédhiou regional narcotics brigade, the BRS, which works under OCRTIS, Senegal's central office for the suppression of illicit drug trafficking. [@3] Two individuals travelling
together by bicycle were trying to reach Guinea-Bissau with 56 kilograms of
cannabis carried on board. [@1,2,3] The interception operation was conducted near
the locality of Koussy and brought their onward progression to a halt. [@1,2,3] One
of the two accomplices managed to disappear into the surroundings while the
second was apprehended by the officers. [@1,2,3]

At the scene, police officers seized one bicycle together with several bags
containing the prohibited herb. [@1,2] The bicycle abandoned by the fleeing rider carried three sacks holding 32 blocks of chanvre indien. [@3] In total, the full 56-kg cannabis load was
divided into 32 blocks together with several filled sisal sacks. [@1,2] The courier was carrying three sisal sacks and a backpack of the drug weighing 24 kg, which brought the total seizure to 56 kg. [@3]

During questioning, the arrested courier admitted he had accepted to take this
risk in exchange for the derisory sum of 60,000 FCFA. [@1,2,3] He told the police that the cannabis came from the area and that he was acting as a courier for his boss. [@3] The investigation was
still continuing actively in order to identify the organizer behind this
cross-border trafficking operation. [@1,2] The arrested man was placed in police custody (garde à vue). [@3] Chanvre indien is the French term for
Indian hemp, used in Senegal to refer to cannabis. [@1]
