On 13 February 2026 the Vienna State Criminal Police Office arrested three
suspects operating under the name DROPSHOP during a house search. [@1] The
Federal Criminal Police Office then took over the further investigations. [@1]
No procedural outcome was reported for this case, and the presumption of
innocence applies to the suspects. [@1]

Officers seized about 13.6 kg of various drugs, listed as amphetamine, cocaine,
LSD, cannabis, crystal meth and ketamine. [@1] The Federal Criminal Police
Office confirmed that about 13.6 kg of various narcotics were seized. [@2] It
listed cannabis among the seized substances alongside amphetamine, cocaine, LSD,
methamphetamine and ketamine. [@2]

The case belonged to the Europol-coordinated Operation TorNado, which ran from
May 2025 to October 2026 with more than 350 dealers and buyers arrested
worldwide, including six arrests and six house searches in Austria. [@1]
