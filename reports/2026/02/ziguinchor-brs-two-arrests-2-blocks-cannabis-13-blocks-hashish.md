Ziguinchor's Regional Narcotics Brigade (BRS), a unit of Senegal's central
anti-drug office (OCRTIS), arrested two people on 28 February 2026 with a large
quantity of drugs. [@1] Officers seized two blocks of chanvre indien, the French
term for Indian hemp or cannabis, and thirteen blocks of hashish. [@1] The
operation followed the exploitation of operational intelligence that located the
suspects in Ziguinchor, the capital of Casamance. [@1] Le Soleil reported that
the intervention was carried out « with precision » on Saturday 28 February 2026
and that the two were held with narcotics intended for clandestine circulation.
[@2]

Both suspects were immediately placed in garde à vue, police custody, after
being informed of their rights. [@1] The investigation continues to identify
possible ramifications of the trafficking network in the Ziguinchor region and
beyond. [@1] Le Soleil said investigators were also trying to identify possible
accomplices and the supply and distribution circuits behind the case. [@2]
Dakaractu, citing a note published by the Senegalese national police on its
digital platforms, gave the same count of two blocks of chanvre indien and
thirteen blocks of hashish at the time of arrest. [@3]

Ndarinfo described hashish as a rarer product than chanvre indien in the area.
[@1] Dakaractu said the inquiry was still seeking to determine the origin and
destination of the seized products. [@3]
