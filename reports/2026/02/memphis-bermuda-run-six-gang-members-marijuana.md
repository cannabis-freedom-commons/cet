Memphis police arrested six men they identified as confirmed gang members at a
residence in the 2100 block of Bermuda Run on 18 February 2026. [@1] One of
those arrested was 25-year-old Quentin Burnside, who was out on bond for
attempted second-degree murder at the time and was described by Memphis police
as an Aggravated Assault Prolific Offender linked to multiple violent crimes in
the city. [@1] As officers surrounded the residence they were alerted that one
or more occupants were trying to escape through the attic, made entry and took
the six into custody. [@1]

While securing the house officers saw marijuana and a handgun in plain view in
the living room. [@1] A search warrant at the residence recovered nearly eight
pounds of marijuana, two rifles, two handguns, Alprazolam pills, a digital scale
and a large amount of cash. [@1] Officers also found a Nissan Murano at the
residence whose keys were held by one of the detainees; the car had a fraudulent
VIN and was towed for further investigation. [@1]

The Memphis Police Department said it arrested and charged six gang-affiliated
members following the investigation. [@2] Each was charged with possession of a
controlled substance with intent to manufacture, deliver or sell marijuana,
possession of a legend drug without a prescription, possession of drug
paraphernalia and possession of a firearm during a dangerous felony. [@1]
Mardarius Lee was additionally charged as a convicted felon in possession of a
handgun and had active federal warrants on weapon charges. [@1] Burnside also
had outstanding warrants for attempted second-degree murder, seven counts of
reckless endangerment with a deadly weapon and employment of a firearm during a
dangerous felony. [@1]
