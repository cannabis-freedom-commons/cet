Gendarmería Nacional seized four tonnes of marijuana and detained two people at
a house beside Ruta Provincial 11 in Dos de Mayo, Misiones. [@1] The operation
also ended with the seizure of a Ford EcoSport SUV that was barred from
circulation, a motorcycle, a phone and two pen drives, and with the detention of
a man and a woman. [@1] Inside, gendarmes found 217 bundles holding 4,188
marijuana bricks that weighed 4,166.714 kg. [@1]

The raid was ordered by the Federal Court of Oberá, presided over by judge
Alejandro Gallandat Luzuriaga, after several months of investigation by the
Sección Núcleo of Escuadrón 49 San Vicente. [@1] It was carried out on 15
February 2026. [@3] The search was planned and carried out with support from the
San Vicente traffic section and the El Soberbio and Aurora sections. [@1]

The judge ordered the confiscation of the drug and all the items found, and the
two detainees were placed at the disposal of the federal court. [@2] The case
remained at the investigation stage, with inquiries into the origin and
destination of the shipment and possible links to other drug-trafficking groups
in the region. [@2]
