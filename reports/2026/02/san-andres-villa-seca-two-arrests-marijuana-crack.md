Police carried out a search in San Andrés Villa Seca, Retalhuleu. [@1] Agents of
the Specialized Criminal Investigation Division (DEIC) and prosecutors from the
Public Prosecutor's Office detained two men in the raid. [@1] The men were aged
53 and 24. [@3]

Officers found 53 wrappers of suspected crack, a bag of suspected marijuana and
three "colmillos", the local term for cocaine doses, the Ministry of the
Interior (MINGOB) said. [@2] They also seized a mobile phone and 735 quetzales
in cash, which the ministry said was presumably money from retail drug sales.
[@2]

The evidence was packed and sent for analysis, and the two men were placed at
the disposal of the courts. [@1] MINGOB said the operation was carried out under
the state of prevention in force in the department. [@2] The raid was one of
several anti-drug operations against street-level dealing carried out that day
in Retalhuleu and Guatemala City, República reported. [@3]
