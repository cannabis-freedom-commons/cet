Panama's Public Ministry and the National Aeronaval Service intercepted a boat
south of Punta Mala in Los Santos province on the Pacific coast. [@1] Officers
detained five people believed to be linked to the load. [@1] The maritime
operation south of Punta Mala ended with five men apprehended and a boat seized
carrying 1,163 packages of suspected illicit substance. [@3]

Inside the boat officers found 25 bundles and a backpack holding 1,163 packages
of suspected illicit substance, plus dried herb. [@1] The account explains that
authorities in Panama call dried herb marijuana. [@1] When officers tried to tow
the launch, a crack in its bow kept it from staying afloat. [@2] In coordination
with the Los Santos drug prosecutor, the cargo and the detainees were taken to
the Mensabe Aeronaval Station in Los Santos. [@2]

The reports give no drug type or total weight for the 1,163 packages, noting
only that such packages in Panama usually weigh about a kilo each and that
cocaine is the most trafficked drug. [@1] Panama is described as a transit zone
for drugs produced in the south of the continent and bound for the United States
and Europe. [@1]
