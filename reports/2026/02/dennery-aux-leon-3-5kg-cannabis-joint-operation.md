Royal Saint Lucia Police Force officers arrested two people during an
intelligence-led joint operation that executed six search warrants at premises
in Dennery and La Bourne, Monchy. [@1] The operation was carried out by the
Gangs, Narcotics and Firearms Unit North and South, with support from the
Special Services Unit and the Tactical Response Unit South. [@1] Police said the
warrants targeted premises suspected of storing and distributing firearms,
ammunition and controlled drugs. [@1]

At a residence in Aux Leon, Dennery, officers recovered about 3.5 kilograms of
compressed cannabis. [@1] Police valued the cannabis at EC$35,000. [@1] One
person was arrested on suspicion of possession of controlled drugs and
possession of controlled drugs with intent to supply, and was taken into police
custody. [@1] Police said charges were expected to be preferred within 72 hours
and that the investigation was continuing. [@1]

Further searches at La Bourne, Monchy and at Dauphin Beach formed part of a
fugitive inquiry, and officers seized several electronic devices and documents
for forensic examination. [@1] One person was apprehended under a warrant of
arrest as a fugitive for extradition to the French Republic of Martinique. [@1]
The warrant relates to multiple offences including unauthorised importation and
exportation of narcotics by an organised gang, unauthorised transport,
possession, supply and acquisition of narcotics, and possession and transport of
goods dangerous to public health. [@1] That person remained in custody pending
extradition proceedings. [@1]
