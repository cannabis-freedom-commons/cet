Former Suva lawyer Shazran Abdul Lateef was found guilty of two counts of
unlawful possession of illicit drugs in Suva. [@1] Magistrate Shageeth Somaratne
was satisfied beyond reasonable doubt from the evidence and witness statements
that Lateef was in possession of unlawful drugs. [@1] On 23 February 2026 the
Suva Magistrate Court sentenced Lateef to three years and eight months in jail,
with no parole eligibility for 30 months. [@3] Magistrate Somaratne said
arrangements would be made at the Remand Centre for Lateef’s rehabilitation.
[@2] The court said the sentence reflected the seriousness of drug offences and
the need to deter similar conduct. [@3]

The case concerned 9.5 grams of methamphetamine and 3.5 grams of marijuana
possessed on 9 May 2019. [@1] A key witness met Lateef on 9 May 2019 and
accompanied him to the hotel where the drugs were found. [@3] State witness
Asenaca Balekatuba told the court that Lateef requested to meet her and opened a
briefcase containing methamphetamine in the hotel room. [@1] She testified that
Lateef carried a briefcase, paid for a room, and removed a plastic container
holding a zip-lock bag of methamphetamine. [@3] Police officers entered the room
after knocking, searched the premises, and seized the drugs and related items
from a counter. [@3]

During sentencing Magistrate Somaratne said a significant amount of drugs was
found in Lateef’s possession. [@2] The sentencing reported on 19 February 2026
had initially been scheduled for the next day. [@1]
