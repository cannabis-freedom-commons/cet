Officers from the Brigade régionale des stupéfiants (BRS), Dakar's regional
anti-drug squad, arrested two people on 20 February 2026 while dismantling a
suspected international drug-trafficking network operating through the
Baux-Maraîchers bus station in Pikine. [@1] They seized 2.55 kilograms of
hashish made up into 25 small parcels. [@1] The substance was described by
police as cannabis resin. [@2]

The operation followed a tip about a suspect parcel brought overland from
Ziguinchor and carried towards Dakar in a minibus known locally as a 'Cheikhou
Chérif', after passing through the border of a neighbouring country. [@2] Police
set up surveillance as the vehicle arrived at the station around 20:20 and
detained the driver as he unloaded it, after he said he was carrying a crate of
drinks. [@1] The main suspect was arrested when he came to collect the parcel; a
search of the crate of twelve bottles found that three of them held six 100-gram
and nineteen 50-gram parcels of hashish. [@1]

Investigators also searched the presumed recipient's home at Cité Mbackiyou Faye
in the Mamelles district of Dakar, seizing bank documents, identity papers and a
laptop that they said could support suspicion of money laundering. [@1] The two
suspects were informed of their rights, including the right to a lawyer, and
placed in police custody on suspicion of criminal association, international
drug trafficking, complicity and money laundering. [@3] The drugs and the seized
objects were inventoried and placed under seal. [@1]
