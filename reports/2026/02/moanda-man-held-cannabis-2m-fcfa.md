Freddy Leboudjou Maniassa, a 43-year-old Gabonese man, was placed in detention
at the main Franceville prison. [@1] He is unemployed and had been placed there
recently by the Franceville public prosecutor. [@1] In early February 2026 he
was arrested in the Fumier district of Moanda by officers of the Haut-Ogooué
provincial branch of the Central Anti-Drug Office (OCAD) in possession of a
sachet of bulk cannabis. [@1] He tried to flee on seeing the officers and was
quickly overpowered after a short chase. [@1]

Officers also seized 2.153 million FCFA from him. [@1] The money was described
as likely proceeds from the sale of illicit products. [@1] Taken to OCAD
premises for investigation, he admitted consuming and selling cannabis for about
two years. [@1] He said he acted to provide for his family. [@1]

The public prosecutor ordered his remand under a detention warrant. [@1] Moanda
is the chief town of the Lébombi-Léyou department, and Yéné is the local name
for the Franceville detention facility where he was held. [@2] He was placed
under a detention warrant to await judgment before the court. [@2] He had sold
cannabis, which is prohibited under Gabonese law. [@2]
