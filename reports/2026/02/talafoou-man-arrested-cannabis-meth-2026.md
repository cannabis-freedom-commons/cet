Tonga Police arrested a 34-year-old man from Talafo'ou on 26 February 2026 after
receiving information that he was selling methamphetamine and cannabis in the
Talafo'ou and Navutoka communities. [@1] Officers carried out an operation and
searched the man's residence. [@2]

Officers executing a search warrant at the residence found quantities of drugs,
drug-related utensils and cash believed to be proceeds of crime. [@3] Police
released no details of the quantities of drugs seized. [@3] Methamphetamine,
known locally as ice, was described as a growing concern across Tonga and the
wider Pacific. [@3]

The suspect has been arrested and charged, and investigations remain ongoing.
[@2] Tonga Police said the arrest reflects its ongoing commitment to disrupting
illegal drug activity and protecting community wellbeing. [@2] Police urged the
public to keep providing information to assist enforcement efforts. [@3] The
case is expected to proceed through the courts. [@3]
