Fourteen people were apprehended during Operation Impacto in Panamá Norte, an
operation coordinated between the Procuraduría General de la Nación and the
National Police as part of the #ContraElMicrotráfico strategy. [@1] The people
located during the searches were described as allegedly linked to the offence of
micro-trafficking in the targeted areas. [@1] Those apprehended were placed at
the orders of the Public Ministry for the corresponding procedure while
investigations continue. [@1]

The operation carried out 11 search proceedings across the corregimientos of
Alcalde Díaz, Caimitillo, Las Cumbres, Ernesto Córdoba Campos and Chilibre in
Panamá Norte. [@1] During those searches the authorities seized judicialized
cash, an alleged illicit substance, scales, utensils for packaging drugs and
plants described as alleged marijuana. [@1] The marijuana element of the seizure
was reported as plants, separate from the other alleged substance and the scales
and packaging utensils found alongside them. [@1]

The Procuraduría said these actions form part of the constant fight against
retail drug trafficking in residential communities. [@1]
