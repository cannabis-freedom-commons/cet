State authorities in Chiapas reported the detention of three people accused of
carrying weapons and possessing narcotics who said they belonged to the criminal
group Cartel de Chiapas y Guatemala. [@1] Those detained were two men and a
woman, identified as Carlos “N”, Deymar “N” and Antonia “N”, arrested in the
municipality of Ocozocoautla de Espinosa in an action reported by the Secretaria
de Seguridad del Pueblo on 8 February 2026. [@1] Officers of the Fuerza de
Reaccion Inmediata Pakal and the Guardia Estatal Preventiva made the arrests
during crime-prevention work. [@1] State agents acted in coordination with the
Agencia de Investigacion e Inteligencia Ministerial of the state prosecutor's
office and the federal Secretaria de Seguridad y Proteccion Ciudadana. [@1]

Officers seized 15 small bags of suspected cocaine, ten wrappers of suspected
marijuana, three bladed weapons, nine ponchallantas spikes and cash. [@1] A
second account describes the marijuana as ten wrappers of green dry herb with
the physical characteristics of marijuana, alongside the cash, bladed weapons
and nine welded rods used as ponchallantas. [@2] The authorities' handout photos
showed the detainees had carried mobile phones, banknotes, welded rods, drug
doses and knives. [@1]

The three people were placed at the disposal of the corresponding authorities to
determine their legal situation. [@1] The detainees had told officers they
belonged to the group called Cartel Chiapas y Guatemala, and no charges, pleas
or court outcome were reported beyond that referral. [@1]
