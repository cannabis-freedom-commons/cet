The Mexican army destroyed huge marijuana plantations in Michoacán. [@1]
Soldiers found two marijuana plantations at La Laja, in the municipality of
Turicato. [@6] Personnel of the 21st Military Zone based in Morelia carried out
the destruction on 15 February 2026. [@6] No one was arrested. [@5]

The plantation lay in a hard-to-reach mountain area of the Tierra Caliente. [@4]
Soldiers and aircraft had intensified ground and air reconnaissance in the area.
[@5] The main plantation covered 2.8 hectares, or 28,500 square metres, with
about six plants per square metre and plants averaging 1.3 metres in height.
[@4] The army counted more than 171,000 marijuana plants ready for harvest. [@3]
A second plantation nearby covered about two hectares, and three smaller plots
were found in the same ravine. [@5]

Soldiers found 11 greenhouses and two drying sheds at the site. [@3] The crop
had drip irrigation and artificial light. [@2] They seized sacks holding about
one tonne of marijuana. [@3] The plantation was seized and burned on the spot.
[@3] The operation was part of the Plan Michoacán for Peace and Justice and of
the Paricutín operation. [@6]
