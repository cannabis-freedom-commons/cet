South Korea's Supreme Court upheld an 18-month prison sentence for rapper Lee
Dong-heon, known as Keith Ape, for cannabis use. [@1] Lee was convicted of
violating the Narcotics Control Act. [@1] The top court rejected his claim that
the sentence was excessive. [@1] Prosecutors accused Lee of smoking cannabis and
vaping liquid cannabis five times at music studios in Seoul's Gangnam and Mapo
districts between February 2023 and January 2024. [@1]

Lower courts sentenced him to 18 months in prison, along with 40 hours of
mandatory rehabilitation education and 800,000 won ($556) in forfeiture. [@1]
Lee appealed, arguing that the lower court had failed to fully examine the case
and that the sentence was too heavy. [@1] The Supreme Court dismissed the
appeal, noting that an appeal based solely on the claim that a sentence is
unreasonable is permitted only in cases involving the death penalty, life
imprisonment or a prison term of at least 10 years. [@1]

The Supreme Court's Third Division, with Justice Lee Sook-yeon presiding, said
on 25 February that it had finalized the lower court's ruling. [@2] The
first-instance court said the defendant was highly blameworthy, his narcotics
addiction severe and the risk of recidivism not low. [@2] He had previously
received suspended prison sentences for separate Narcotics Control Act
violations in 2021 and 2023. [@2] The first-instance court cited those prior
suspended sentences, imposed in September 2021 and May 2023, as an aggravating
factor because he smoked cannabis again during them. [@3]
