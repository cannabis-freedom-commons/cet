Oklahoma Bureau of Narcotics agents served a search warrant at Whale Farm Inc.
on US 177 north of Stratford in Garvin County. [@1] The search was carried out
on 10 February 2026. [@2]

Agents seized 10,656 marijuana plants at the site. [@2] They also seized 296
pounds (about 134 kg) of processed marijuana. [@2] KTEN reported that agents
made one arrest. [@1] One person was taken into the custody of U.S. Immigration
and Customs Enforcement after the search. [@2]

The bureau said the search was part of an investigation into illegal cultivation
and a registration obtained by fraud. [@3] OBN said more arrests were expected
as the investigation continued. [@2]
