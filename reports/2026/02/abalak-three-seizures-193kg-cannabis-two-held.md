The departmental OCRTIS branch in Abalak carried out three drug seizures on 22
January and 7 and 9 February 2026 in Abalak department, Tahoua region. [@1] On
22 January at the Abalak exit barrier toward Agadez, officers searching a
transport bus found six cannabis balls weighing 6 kg hidden in a suitcase
belonging to a passenger who had changed buses at Tahoua. [@2] On 7 February,
acting on a tip at the Harouba garage in Abalak's Salama quarter, officers
searched the site and seized 15 bricks of Amnesia-type cannabis weighing 13.30
kg. [@1]

The man found with that cannabis had come from Maiduguri in Nigeria and was to
continue toward Harouba in Mali. [@1] Officers detained the suspect at the
scene. [@1] On 9 February at the same garage, an OCRTIS team supported by DDPN
officers arrested another man from Maiduguri carrying 492 tramadol tablets and
360 rohypnol tablets. [@2] Investigators said those tablets were destined for
the gold-mining site at Bordj El Haouas in Algeria. [@1]

Together the three operations yielded more than 19 kilograms of cannabis and
hundreds of psychotropic tablets. [@1] The seized products were presented on 14
February 2026 to administrative, judicial, customary and religious authorities
at the Abalak police directorate's premises. [@1]
