Five suspects were detained in Baku in a crackdown on illegal drug trafficking.
[@2] The operations were conducted by officers of the Garadagh District Police
Department in Baku's Garadagh district. [@1] Officers detained 43-year-old Akbar
Aliyev. [@1] Officers also detained 39-year-old Baku resident Elkhan Bandaliyev
on suspicion of involvement in illicit drug trafficking. [@1] In Lokbatan
settlement, officers detained 32-year-olds Yunis Mammadov and Nazir Taghiyev and
34-year-old Urfan Mammadli on suspicion of organising illicit drug trafficking
in Baku. [@1]

Aliyev was found with 21.302 kilograms of marijuana. [@1] He was also found with
496 grams of opium and 50 methadone tablets. [@1] Bandaliyev was found with
23.975 kilograms of marijuana. [@1] The three Lokbatan detainees were found with
20.250 kilograms of marijuana that they intended to place at various addresses
in Baku. [@1] Police said more than 66 kilograms of narcotics in total were
removed from illicit circulation in the recent operations. [@1]

Criminal cases were opened over the seizures and the investigation is
continuing. [@1]
