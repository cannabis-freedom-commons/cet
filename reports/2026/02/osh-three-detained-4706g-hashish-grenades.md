Officers of the drug control service of Kyrgyzstan's Interior Ministry stopped
the activity of an organised group selling Afghan-produced drugs in the city of
Osh. [@1] The operation seized more than 4.7 kilograms of hashish, along with
weapons, ammunition and large sums of money, the Interior Ministry's press
service said. [@1] A search of a Daewoo Nexia belonging to citizen S.O.B., born
1995, found 101 grams of hashish and 155,000 som. [@1]

A later search at the home of citizen D.S.S., born 1989, carried out with a
service dog, seized 4 kilograms 600 grams of hashish, two RGD-5 grenades, a
homemade firearm, 51 cartridges, 2,000 dollars and 87,000 som. [@1] The group's
organiser was citizen K.S.S., born 1982, who had earlier had problems with the
law in other countries over drug-related crimes. [@1] Afghan hashish, known
locally as chars, was also found at the organiser's home. [@1] The police said
K.S.S. had repeatedly been held to account for drug crimes in foreign states.
[@2]

S.O.B., D.S.S. and K.S.S. were placed in the temporary detention centre in Osh.
[@1] In total the operation seized 4 kilograms 706 grams of hashish, two
grenades, a firearm, 51 cartridges, 2,000 dollars and 242,000 som. [@1] A
criminal case was opened under Article 282 of the criminal code, unlawful
manufacture of narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances and their analogues for
sale. [@1] Forensic chemical examinations of the seized substances were ordered,
and investigators were still working to identify other members of the group.
[@2]
