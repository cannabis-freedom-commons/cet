Police at the Alfredo Vásquez Cobo International Airport in Leticia, Amazonas,
arrested two women for the alleged offence of trafficking, manufacture or
possession of narcotics. [@1] The National Police, through the Carabineros and
Environmental Protection Section of the Amazonas Police Department, said the
arrests in flagrancy were made under its crime registration, control and
prevention plans. [@2] During baggage inspection a sniffer dog marked two
mattresses being carried by the detainees. [@1]

Officers opening the mattresses found 28 wrapped packages hidden inside, which
tested positive for drugs. [@1] Preliminary tests on the packages were positive
for marijuana and cocaine hydrochloride. [@2] Officials specified that 24
pressed packages tested positive for cocaine hydrochloride and its derivatives
while four others contained marijuana. [@3] The Amazonas police commander
confirmed that the expert PIPH test was positive for cocaine hydrochloride and
its derivatives with four further packages positive for marijuana. [@1]

The women were informed of their rights as detainees and placed at the disposal
of the competent judicial authority for judicial proceedings. [@2] The two women
were placed at the disposal of the Attorney General’s Office, which will
determine their legal situation. [@1]
