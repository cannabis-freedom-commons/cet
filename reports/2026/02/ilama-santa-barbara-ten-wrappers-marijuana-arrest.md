Police in Ilama, Santa Bárbara, arrested a man on suspicion of drug trafficking.
[@1] He is 38 years old and was arrested on Thursday 19 February 2026. [@1] The
arrest was carried out jointly by the Dirección Nacional de Prevención y
Seguridad Comunitaria, the National Police's community prevention directorate,
and the Dirección Policial de Investigaciones (DPI), its investigations
directorate. [@2] The National Police's UDEP-16 unit in Santa Bárbara said it
would continue prevention work in the department. [@2]

Officers seized ten plastic wrappers containing dried plant material believed to
be marijuana, along with two mobile phones. [@2] The man and the evidence were
handed to the competent authorities to continue the legal process. [@2]
Investigators said the seized evidence was a relevant lead in ongoing inquiries,
including into the man's possible links to local drug distribution networks.
[@1]

The National Police described the case as part of its campaign against
microtrafficking, small-scale local drug dealing. [@2] The force said the arrest
was one of a series of operations focused on points its investigators had
identified as vulnerable. [@1]
