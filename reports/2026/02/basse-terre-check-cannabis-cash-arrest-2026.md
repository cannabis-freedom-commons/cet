Police from the Basse-Terre anti-crime squad, the Brigade anti-criminalité
(BAC), carried out a check operation on 26 February 2026 as part of coordinated
action with customs services, supported by a dog team specialised in drug
detection. [@1] Officers arrested one person suspected of involvement in
activities linked to drug trafficking. [@1] Initial findings led to the
discovery and seizure of several hundred grams of cannabis herb. [@1]

Police also seized more than 7,000 euros in cash, placed under seal pending the
coming judicial decisions. [@1]

The Basse-Terre judicial court is to rule in the coming months on what happens
to the money, which can be confiscated by the courts and paid over to the state.
[@1] Investigators from the Basse-Terre local judicial police service continued
the inquiry to establish the scale of the case and any ramifications, and to
determine whether other people were involved. [@1] The inquiry is continuing to
establish responsibility and whether others were implicated in the file. [@1]
