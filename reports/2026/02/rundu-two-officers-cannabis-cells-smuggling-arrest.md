Two police officers from Rundu were arrested after allegedly attempting to
smuggle money, cannabis, cigarettes and mobile phones into police holding cells.
[@1] Kavango East regional police commander commissioner Andreas Haingura
confirmed the arrests on Wednesday. [@1] The suspects are a constable and a
sergeant. [@1] The constable was arrested on Tuesday at the Rundu Police
Station, while the sergeant was apprehended at the Rundu Magistrate’s Court.
[@1] The two appeared in the Rundu Magistrate court after being arrested on
separate days, one on Tuesday and another on Wednesday. [@2] They are charged
with contravening Section 43 of the Anti-Corruption Act, possession of cannabis,
and contraventions of Sections 16 and 17 of the Tobacco Products Control Act of
2010. [@1]

A body search of the sergeant uncovered 19 bankies of cannabis and a N$100
banknote. [@2] The constable was found with 11 bankies of cannabis, three
phones, four batteries, two packets of Yes cigarettes and 20 loose cigarettes,
an FNB ATM card, six SIM cards, a USB charger cable and N$280 cash. [@2]

Intelligence units received a tip-off about a civilian intending to use a police
officer as a mule to bypass security at the court’s holding cells on Wednesday,
and police tailed the member of the public to the court gates where they saw
parcels handed to the officer. [@2] On Tuesday at about 13h00, intelligence was
obtained from a reliable source about a member of the public intending to
facilitate smuggling into Rundu Police holding cells through a police officer.
[@2] Intelligence was shared with members of the Police Conduct Investigation
and Drug Law Enforcement, which instituted a covert controlled delivery
operation. [@2]
