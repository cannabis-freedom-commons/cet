Nicaragua's National Police presented the evidence obtained during an operation
carried out within the framework of the Plan de Seguridad Ciudadana, the
country's citizen-security programme. [@1] Police said the illicit substances
seized in the operation were presumed to have been destined for
commercialization. [@1] Among the material on display were 4 pounds of marijuana
and 56 grams of crack, which had already been properly packaged and weighed as
part of the investigative process. [@1]

According to the preliminary report, the operation was the result of
surveillance and follow-up work by the police. [@1] That work made it possible
to seize the drugs and to detain people linked to the case. [@1] The detainees
were to be remitted to the relevant authorities so that they could face due
judicial process. [@1]

The police also displayed the evidence and reiterated their commitment to
confronting drug trafficking and the dispensing of drugs in neighbourhoods and
communities. [@1]
