US Coast Guard crew from the cutter Richard Etheridge boarded the 45-foot Sea
Ray Lady B in the Atlantic about eight nautical miles northeast of Haulover
Inlet, Florida, for a routine seaworthiness inspection. [@1] The boarding team
found manipulated panels and fresh fiberglass work, then a hidden compartment
under the cabin floor holding 68 bricks of cocaine and 14 bricks of marijuana.
[@1]

Rudalph Hylton, a 33-year-old Jamaican national, was arrested and booked into
the Broward County Jail on behalf of the U.S. Marshals Service on three federal
drug trafficking charges. [@1] Hylton appeared in a Fort Lauderdale federal
court on 23 February 2026. [@1] A second Jamaican national, Aldon Delano Brown,
was on board but was not listed in Broward or federal jail records. [@1] Hylton
remained in custody and was due before a federal judge for a pre-trial detention
hearing on 2 March. [@1]

Hylton told investigators that he and Brown had taken the vessel from Fort
Lauderdale to Jamaica about a month earlier and that he was offered $100,000 to
bring it back knowing illegal narcotics would be hidden on board, the complaint
said. [@1] The complaint did not specify a street value for the drugs. [@2]
Federal prosecutors in the Southern District of Florida are handling the case.
[@3]
