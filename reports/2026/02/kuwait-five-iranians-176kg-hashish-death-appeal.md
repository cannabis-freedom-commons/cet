Kuwait's Court of Appeal upheld the death sentences of five Iranian nationals
convicted of smuggling narcotics into the country by sea. [@1] The appeal panel
was headed by Judge Dr. Fahd Bu Salib. [@1] The Criminal Court had sentenced the
same five men to death in a ruling reported in May 2025 over 176 kilograms of
hashish. [@4] Officers from the Drug Control General Department and the Coast
Guard arrested them as they entered Kuwaiti territorial waters on a Duba boat
carrying 13 bags of the seized drugs. [@4]

The group travelled by boat toward Kuwaiti waters carrying the drugs, according
to the case findings and the defendants' statements. [@1] The men told the court
the shipment was to be delivered to a Kuwaiti citizen at coordinates agreed in
advance. [@2] They said they were drug addicts who had left Iran by boat after
loading the narcotics. [@2] Coast Guard patrols intercepted the vessel shortly
after it entered territorial waters and ordered it to stop. [@1] Security forces
seized the boat, confiscated the narcotics on board and arrested the men at the
scene. [@1] The panel found the men had brought the drugs in solely to supply
the local market, the daily Al-Rai reported. [@3]

Kuwait's recently amended anti-narcotics law provides for capital punishment for
large-scale drug smuggling and trafficking. [@1] The ruling remains subject to
further legal challenge before higher courts. [@1]
