The government withdrew cultivation approvals from six companies after thirteen inspections, citing storage, reporting, documentation and stock discrepancies. The Health Ministry announced checks across 43 licensed entities. According to sources familiar with the inspections, multiple government bodies took part, including health authorities, police units, internal affairs and anti-drug teams. [@2] It also promised stronger legislation after the inspections, rather than announcing that a new law was already in force. [@1,2] Authorities said the names of the companies whose licences were revoked would be made public in the following days. [@2]
