Roberto Feria, 54, of Cape Coral, faced a felony drug trafficking charge. [@1]
The Florida Office of Agricultural Law Enforcement said officers at the
Pensacola Agriculture Inspection Station conducted a regulatory inspection on 16
February 2026. [@2] The driver was identified as Roberto I. Feria and was
arrested and charged with felony drug trafficking, the agency said. [@3]

The semi-truck was hauling oranges from Visalia, California. [@1] During the
inspection officers smelled cannabis and found 17 duffel bags and five trash
bags hidden among the pallets. [@1] Officers detected the odor of cannabis
coming from the trailer while checking the load. [@3] Tests confirmed the
material was 671 pounds (about 304 kg) of THC cannabis. [@1]

Florida authorities said a truck driver faced felony charges after officers
uncovered hundreds of pounds of cannabis within a shipment of oranges. [@2] The
drugs were turned over to the Drug Enforcement Administration (DEA). [@1] Feria
was held in the Escambia County Jail on a $250,000 bond. [@1]
