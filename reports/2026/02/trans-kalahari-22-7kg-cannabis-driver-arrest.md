A Namibian truck driver was arrested at the Trans Kalahari border after
allegedly transporting cannabis and mandrax that police valued together at N$2.9
million. [@1] The suspect was identified as Steven Kandovazu, 32. [@1] Chief
Inspector Fina Sakajengenga said the incident happened on Monday 9 February 2026
at around 21:35. [@2]

Kandovazu was driving a Nam CDS company truck when a scanner detected suspicious
images underneath the vehicle. [@1] A physical inspection found prohibited
illegal substances hidden and tied underneath the truck's axle. [@2]

Officers recovered 22.700 kilograms of skunk cannabis and 14,832 alleged mandrax
tablets. [@1] The N$2.9 million valuation was for the cannabis and mandrax
together, with no cannabis-only value given. [@2]
