CICPC agents detained the driver of a private car in La Grita, Táchira, after
finding drugs inside the vehicle. [@1] The stop followed a pursuit after the car
tried to evade officers conducting a vehicle-checkpoint operation. [@1] The
pursuit ended when the suspect car collided with a flatbed truck. [@1] The
account reports only the driver's detention, with no charges, identity details
or court outcome stated. [@1]

The drugs were organised in 24 sacks containing cocaine and marijuana. [@1] The
account presents the combined haul as half a ton, writing the figure as 567 mil
kilos con 1 gramos, without giving separate cocaine and marijuana weights. [@1]

Officers were reviewing cargo and public-transport vehicles when they spotted
the private car. [@1] The CICPC account was shared in a police report posted on
its Instagram account. [@1] The same article also describes a separate
cocaine-only seizure of 20 panels weighing 23 kilos 214 grams in La Cañonera,
Zulia, which is left out of this report. [@1]
