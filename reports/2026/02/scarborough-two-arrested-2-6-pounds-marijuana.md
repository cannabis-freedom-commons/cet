Two men are facing charges after a drug bust in Scarborough, Maine. [@1] Police
say they searched a vehicle and a home in the Pine Point Beach neighborhood on
Wednesday after getting information about drug trafficking occurring in the
area. [@1] Police then arrested 36-year-old Omar Shir of Westbrook and
35-year-old Mohamed Abdullahi of South Portland. [@1]

Authorities found 395 grams of cocaine, 2.6 pounds (about 1.18 kg) of marijuana,
oxycodone pills and over $1,700 during the searches. [@1] A Scarborough Police
Department handout shows the items recovered from that search. [@1]

Shir was charged with aggravated furnishing of scheduled drugs, aggravated
trafficking of scheduled drugs, refusing to submit to arrest or detention, and
criminal forfeiture of property. [@1] Abdullahi was charged with unlawful
possession of scheduled drugs. [@1]
