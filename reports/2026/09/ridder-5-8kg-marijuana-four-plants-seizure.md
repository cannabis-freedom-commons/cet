In Ridder, police seized four cannabis bushes and more than 5.8 kg of dried
marijuana from a local resident. [@1] Altainews reported that police found
almost 6 kg of marijuana and four cannabis bushes during a search of a garage in
Ridder. [@3] Officers found the plants and a dried plant substance in the
resident's garage, along with a container with residue. [@2] Forensic
examination found the seized bushes belonged to the cannabis genus and
identified the dried substance as marijuana. [@2]

Police put the total weight of the dried marijuana at over 5.8 kg. [@2] About
another 15 grams of narcotic substance was found in the seized plants. [@2] The
drugs were found under the coordination of the regional prosecutor's office
during the Qarasora anti-drug operation. [@2] The search followed a tip about
possible cultivation of narcotic-containing plants received by the East
Kazakhstan anti-drug unit, which acted jointly with Ridder police. [@2]

A criminal case was opened and an investigation was under way. [@2] The regional
police department said illegal cultivation and storage of narcotic substances
entail criminal liability. [@2]
