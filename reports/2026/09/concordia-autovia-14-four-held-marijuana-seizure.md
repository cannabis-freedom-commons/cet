The Entre Ríos police and the Argentine Federal Police ran an inter-force
control at kilometre 270 of the Autovía Gervasio Artigas (Ruta Nacional 14) near
Concordia on the morning of Wednesday 16 September 2026. [@1] Officers checked
72 vehicles, among them 27 buses carrying citrus harvest workers. [@1] Four
adult men were detained because they had outstanding arrest warrants issued by
different judicial authorities. [@1] The detainees were handed to the police
units and authorities that had requested their capture. [@1] Two other people
were taken to the Jefatura Departamental Concordia, the departmental police
headquarters, to establish their identity. [@1]

The dangerous drugs division took part in the operation with a narcotics
detection dog. [@1] The division seized wrappers of cocaine, wrappers of
marijuana and plant material compatible with marijuana buds. [@1] The seizure
was made under Entre Ríos's narcomenudeo law, Provincial Law 10.566, which
covers street-level drug dealing. [@1] No official quantity was given for the
drugs seized, and the police did not report that the marijuana was linked to the
four men held on the warrants. [@2]

Police said the operation was part of the joint preventive controls carried out
at the entrances and on the main road corridors of Concordia. [@1] 7Páginas
reported that the deployment came as a judicial investigation examines suspected
drug dealing among rural citrus workers, including whether drugs were used as
payment. [@3]
