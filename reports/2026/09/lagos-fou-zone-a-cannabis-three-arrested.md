The Nigeria Customs Service Federal Operations Unit Zone A intercepted illicit
drugs and pharmaceutical products worth N3.951 billion and arrested three
suspects in a crackdown on smuggling and trans-border crime. [@1] FOU Zone A
Controller Gambo Aliyu disclosed the seizures on Thursday in Ikeja during the
handover of the seized contraband to other government agencies. [@3]

The seizures included three and a half packs of ground cannabis weighing 3.45
kilogrammes, two packs of granular cannabis weighing one kilogramme, and 11
packs of granular cannabis weighing 0.35 kilogramme. [@4] Officers also
recovered 49 wraps of cannabis weighing 26.1 kilogrammes, alongside one wrap of
crystal meth weighing 0.35 kilogramme. [@4] The bulk consignment of 5,669
parcels and 19 packs weighing 3,116.9 kilogrammes was described as synthetic
cannabis and is excluded from the cannabis figures in this report. [@4] Officers
also intercepted a truck allegedly used to conceal and transport 326 parcels of
cannabis, with two suspects linked to that seizure among those to be transferred
to the NDLEA. [@2]

The items were intercepted along border corridors, highways and other strategic
locations within the unit's area of responsibility. [@1] The three suspects
arrested in connection with the seizures would be handed over to the National
Drug Law Enforcement Agency for further investigation and possible prosecution.
[@2] Customs put the combined approximate street value of the seized narcotics
and mercury at N3.95 billion, a figure covering all the drugs and chemicals
together rather than the natural cannabis alone. [@3]
