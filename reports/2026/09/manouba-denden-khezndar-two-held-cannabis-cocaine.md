Officers of the Manouba judicial police detained two suspected drug dealers
operating in the Denden and Khezndar areas, seizing varying quantities of
cannabis and cocaine along with sums of money described as proceeds of dealing.
[@1] The case began when the head of the Manouba judicial police received
information that a young Denden resident was dealing drugs, and officers raided
his home in coordination with the public prosecution, finding him with a second
young man and seizing an amount of cannabis and a sum of money. [@1]

Preliminary inquiries indicated that the companion found with him was the person
supplying the drugs and that he lived in Khezndar. [@1] In coordination with the
public prosecution at the Tunis Court of First Instance, officers took the
second suspect to his home and searched it, seizing an amount of cocaine, an
electronic scale and other equipment suspected of use in dealing, plus a
significant sum said to be dealing proceeds. [@1]

The two suspects were ordered held in custody while inquiries continue to
identify the remaining members of the network before all those involved are
referred to the judiciary. [@1]
