A team from the Batalhão Especializado de Policiamento do Interior (BEPI), the
Pernambuco Military Police battalion that patrols the interior of the state,
uprooted and burned a cannabis plantation at Sítio Cachoeirinha, a rural
property in the countryside of Cabrobó, in Pernambuco's Sertão, on Wednesday 2
September 2026. [@1] The team reached the site after the Federal Police passed
on information about the plantation, the Military Police said. [@1]

Officers uprooted and incinerated 2,025 cannabis plants. [@1] The plants were
still growing, and about 6.06 kg of cannabis had already been bagged for
consumption. [@4] The plantation and most of the material were burned at the
site. [@3]

A small sample of the material was sent to the Civil Police station in Cabrobó,
where the police said the necessary steps were taken. [@2] The Military Police
did not say whether anyone was found or arrested during the operation. [@3]
Folha de Pernambuco reported that nobody was arrested. [@2]
