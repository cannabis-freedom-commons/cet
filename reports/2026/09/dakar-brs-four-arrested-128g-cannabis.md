Officers of the Brigade régionale des stupéfiants (BRS) of Dakar, a unit of
Senegal's OCRTIS anti-drug office, arrested four people in an operation along
the Rufisque–Zac Mbao–Keur Massar axis in the night of 15 to 16 September 2026.
[@1] The four were placed at the disposal of investigators for the purposes of
the proceedings. [@1]

The operation ran between 23:00 and 02:50 and followed information about an
order for cocaine, police said. [@1] Investigators first set up surveillance and
tracking in the Sipres area of Zac Mbao, leading to the arrest of a first
suspect. [@1] After he refused to give his home address, officers used
operational intelligence to locate it in the HLM district of Rufisque. [@1] A
search at that home and in a Jeep Cherokee led to the arrest of two more people
and the seizure of several items. [@2] Continued surveillance led investigators
to a fourth suspect, arrested at about 02:50 at his home in Keur Massar as he
received a delivery of prohibited goods. [@1]

Officers seized 128 grams of chanvre indien, the French term for Indian hemp or
cannabis. [@1] The same searches found 300 grams of cocaine, 4 grams of MDMA,
ecstasy tablets and 123 grams of a crystalline substance, quantities excluded
from the cannabis figures. [@3] They also seized 725,000 CFA francs in cash,
five mobile phones, equipment and administrative documents, and an imitation
firearm. [@1]
