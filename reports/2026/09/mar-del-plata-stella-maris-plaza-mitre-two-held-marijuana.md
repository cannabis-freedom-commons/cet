Officers of the Argentine Federal Police and the Argentine Naval Prefecture
detained two adult men in separate operations in different parts of Mar del
Plata, seizing wrappers with drugs under drug law 23.737. [@1] The operations
took place between Monday night and the early hours of Tuesday in the Stella
Maris and Plaza Mitre neighbourhoods, where officers seized marijuana wrappers
ready for distribution. [@1] The first incident occurred on Monday night when
Argentine Federal Police officers on preventive patrol in the Stella Maris
neighbourhood stopped a man who looked suspicious. [@1]

After stopping and identifying him, officers found a wrapper containing
marijuana among his belongings. [@1] The Federal Police apprehended the suspect
and formally consulted the local drug prosecutor, which endorsed the police
actions and ordered the seized drug material held. [@1] In a parallel procedure
in the Plaza Mitre neighbourhood, Argentine Naval Prefecture officers conducting
surveillance and prevention tasks intercepted another man. [@1] Noticing his
evasive behaviour, they searched and identified him, finding among his clothing
a wrapper of marijuana divided and prepared for distribution in doses. [@1]

The drug prosecutor immediately endorsed that procedure and ordered the drug
seized and the file transferred to Section Two police station of the Buenos
Aires provincial police for further legal action. [@1] The federal forces
presented the actions as part of reinforced street patrols to discourage
small-scale drug dealing and increase public safety. [@1]
