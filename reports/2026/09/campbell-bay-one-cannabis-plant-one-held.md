Police arrested Manoj Kumar of Joginder Nagar, Campbell Bay, on the spot during
the search. [@2] The raid took place on 8 September 2026. [@1] The raiding team
was headed by Inspector Mithun Kirtania of Campbell Bay police station and
directed by Sub-Divisional Police Officer Samuel Fred. [@1]

Officers found one suspected cannabis plant growing in a pot in the suspect's
residential premises. [@1] The plant was preliminarily tested with a drug
detection kit and described as about 26 inches tall and weighing about 9 grams.
[@3] No contraband was found on Kumar's person and no other incriminating
material was found in the house, and the search was videographed through the
e-Sakshya application. [@3]

Police registered a case against Kumar under Section 20(a)(i) of the Narcotic
Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985. [@2] During preliminary questioning
the accused admitted cultivating the plant at his residence. [@1] Kumar told
police another Joginder Nagar resident whose name he disclosed had provided the
seed, which police said was under verification. [@3] The operation was
supervised by Nicobar District Superintendent of Police Rahul L. Nair, and
police said further investigation was under way to trace the source and any
network. [@1]
