Two Uzbek citizens were detained in the operation, the Customs Committee of
Uzbekistan said. [@1] On 5 September 2026, customs officers at Tashkent
International Airport searched the luggage of a 51-year-old Uzbek citizen
arriving from Thailand and found three wine bottles and packets of spices. [@1]
Laboratory testing identified the contents as hashish oil, a cannabis extract
containing tetrahydrocannabinol (THC), weighing 7.58 kg. [@1]

Investigators said the man was to deliver the drugs to Samarkand for $1,800 on
the instructions of a curator. [@1] In Samarkand, officers detained a
47-year-old man who was to receive the shipment; a search of his home found
hashish oil hidden in wine bottles, a dishwashing-liquid container and syringes,
along with dried mushrooms whose consumption causes intoxication. [@1] Customs
said the man in Samarkand was to distribute the drugs to addresses supplied by a
Telegram curator. [@1]

Customs put the total hashish oil taken out of circulation at 13 kg 80 g. [@2]
The committee estimated the consignment's black-market value at more than $1.7
million and said the quantity could have been enough to involve about 200,000
people in drug use. [@2] The operation was carried out by the Tashkent-Aero
customs complex and the Tashkent International Airport border customs post with
the State Security Service. [@1] The customs authorities opened a criminal case
and said investigative work was continuing. [@2]
