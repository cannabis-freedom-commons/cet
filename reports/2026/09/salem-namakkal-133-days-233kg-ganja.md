In the first 133 days of the Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam-led government in Tamil
Nadu, 125 criminal cases were registered in Salem district and 123 cases in
Namakkal district. [@1] The reporting period ran from 10 May 2026 to 20
September 2026. [@1] Together the two districts recorded 248 criminal cases
spanning murder, burglary, crimes against women and POCSO Act cases. [@2]

Police in Salem district seized 67.23 kg of ganja between 10 May and 20
September 2026. [@1] Of the 125 Salem cases, 34 were in Attur, 31 in Mettur, 26
in Vazhapadi, 17 in Sankari, 10 in Salem rural and seven in Omalur
sub-divisions. [@1] Police in Namakkal district seized 166.54 kg of ganja in the
same 133 days. [@1] The 123 Namakkal cases comprised 14 murders, two burglaries,
36 crimes against women and 71 POCSO Act cases. [@1]

In the corresponding period in 2021, Salem recorded 95 criminal cases and 37.20
kg of ganja seized. [@1] In the corresponding period in 2021, Namakkal recorded
70 criminal cases, including 18 murders, six burglaries, 12 crimes against women
and 34 POCSO cases. [@1]
