The Court of Bosnia and Herzegovina opened the trial of Admir Arnautović, known
as Šmrk, and Adnan Maglić, known as Magla, on 24 September 2026 in a Sky case.
[@4] At the plea hearing that day both men said they were not guilty of the
offences in the confirmed indictment, in case S1 2 K 055062 26 Ko. [@4]

The Prosecutor’s Office of Bosnia and Herzegovina filed the indictment against
the two Sarajevo-born citizens, Arnautović born in 1980 and previously
convicted, and Maglić born in 1985. [@1] The Court of Bosnia and Herzegovina
confirmed the indictment on 11 August 2026. [@3] The confirmed indictment says
that from December 2019 until the end of 2022 the two organised and led a group
that trafficked cocaine and hashish in Bosnia and Herzegovina, European Union
states and other countries, using encrypted Sky ECC phones. [@3] The court
identified the hashish as concentrated resin of Cannabis sativa L. [@3]

The court said the indictment charges them with trafficking at least 10
kilograms of hashish and at least 5.5 kilograms of cocaine, plus at least six
firearms. [@3] The Prosecutor’s Office said hashish sold on the illegal market
for 2,400 to 3,500 euros a kilogram and cocaine for 31,000 to 38,000 euros a
kilogram. [@1] The prosecution said the defendants planned serious physical
injuries to a celebrity living outside Bosnia and Herzegovina. [@2] The
prosecution said confiscation of property bought with the proceeds would be
sought. [@2]
