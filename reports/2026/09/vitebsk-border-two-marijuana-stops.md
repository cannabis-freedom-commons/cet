Vitebsk customs stopped two attempts to bring marijuana into Belarus during special operations on the Belarusian-Russian border, the State Customs Committee told Belta on 26 September 2026. [@1,3] Service dogs indicated the drugs in both cases. [@1,2,3,4]

In the first, a 32-year-old Belarusian was driving a Ford Transit minibus. [@1,2,3,4] Officers found a foil-wrapped package of the substance on the passenger seat. [@1,2,3,4] In the second, a 29-year-old Belarusian driving an Opel Insignia had hidden a package in a deodorant bottle among his personal belongings. [@1,2,3,4]

Specialists of the State Committee of Forensic Examinations in Vitebsk region confirmed that both substances were marijuana. [@1,2,3,4] Vitebsk Customs opened criminal cases against the two men. [@1,2,3,4] The offence allows up to seven years in prison. [@1,2,3,4] The English customs notice of the same stops is dated 28 September. [@2]
