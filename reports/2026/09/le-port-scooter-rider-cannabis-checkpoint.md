One electric scooter rider tested positive for cannabis and was apprehended
during a road-safety checkpoint at Le Port, Réunion, on 29 September 2026. [@1]
Several scooter users were checked during the operation and one of them tested
positive for cannabis. [@2] More than 15 police officers were mobilised at Le
Port to check two-wheelers and motorised personal mobility devices (EDPM),
including electric scooters. [@1] Checks were made at the old bus station in Le
Port and focused on electric scooter users. [@1]

The operation came as 27 people had died on Réunion's roads since the start of
2026, according to the prefecture. [@1] Police said more than 25 bodily
accidents involving scooters were recorded in Le Port in 2025, against two in
2022. [@1] Police commissioner Chloé Courtois said there was a real road-safety
problem in La Réunion. [@1]

Alcohol and drug checks were accompanied by checks on EDPM obligations such as
insurance, the 25 km/h speed limit and the ban on two people riding one scooter.
[@1] Philippe Malizard, the sub-prefect of Saint-Paul, described speed, alcohol
and drugs as a terrible combination. [@1] The Police nationale carried out the
checks in the presence of the sub-prefect of Saint-Paul. [@3] The prefecture
said prevention, awareness and enforcement measures would continue across the
territory. [@1]
