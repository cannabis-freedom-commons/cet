Brazilian security forces seized about 4.5 tonnes of skunk, a potent cannabis
variety, from an armoured speedboat near Codajás, in Brazil's Amazonas state.
[@2] The operation took place on Saturday 5 September 2026, the justice ministry
said. [@2] As officers approached, the suspects fired on them; police returned
fire and the men fled into the forest, where they were not found, and no arrests
were reported. [@3] Police intelligence estimated that at least eight people
could have been aboard the boat, a figure the police did not confirm. [@3]

Officers found the cannabis in 102 sacks weighing 4,568.16 kg during searches of
the riverbank and a nearby creek. [@4] They also seized three AK-47 rifles, two
M60 machine guns, 5,810 rounds of ammunition, 45 magazines and the armoured boat
with six 250 HP outboard motors. [@2] The justice ministry put the value of the
drugs, weapons and other material at about 93 million reais. [@2] Colombia's
navy valued the cannabis alone at more than 47 million dollars. [@1]

The justice ministry said the operation involved the Amazonas military police's
special operations battalion (Bope), the state civil police's narcotics (Denarc)
and organised crime (DRCO) departments, the Ficco/AM task force and Peru's
anti-drug directorate, Dirandro. [@2] Colombia's navy said its naval and air
intelligence located the boat and gave Brazil's federal police the information
for the interception. [@1] The boat was thought to have set out from the
three-country border area where Brazil, Colombia and Peru meet, and the Amazonas
civil police said the case began with an investigation about four months
earlier. [@3] The material was handed to the state narcotics department, Denarc,
for judicial police procedures and further investigation. [@2] The authorities
did not say who owned the drugs and weapons or where the cargo was heading. [@3]
