A serving police constable at the Rivière-des-Anguilles station, Nehemie Ervin
Chulwa, 34, was arrested by colleagues from the Anti Drug and Smuggling Unit
(ADSU) as he was leaving work. [@2] The police report registered the case at
about 18:00 on 14 September 2026 in the compound of the Rivière-des-Anguilles
police station. [@1]

Officers seized 13.02 grams of suspected cannabis, Rs 19,000 in cash and a
working pocket electronic scale bearing suspected cannabis leaf particles. [@1]
L'Express reported that the two packets had gross weights of 10.61 grams and
2.41 grams and that seven almost-empty resealable bags held suspected cannabis
particles. [@3] A white Toyota was also seized as part of the investigation.
[@1] Le Mauricien valued the two packets at Rs 10,000. [@2]

Chulwa was suspected of possession of cannabis for sale with aggravating
circumstances, holding equipment for illegal drug production, and money
laundering. [@1] On the order of ASP Sooknah he was held at the Vacoas Detention
Centre, and his statement had not yet been recorded when the report was written.
[@1] A search of his home at Chemin-Grenier found nothing incriminating. [@2] He
was expected at the Souillac court on 16 September to answer a provisional
charge of drug dealing with aggravating circumstances: possession of cannabis
for the purpose of selling. [@2]
