A 52-year-old resident of Novoye village in Makarovsky District will stand trial
for storing a large batch of drugs. [@2] On the morning of 5 September he went
to the old sawmill area to tie bath brooms and found cannabis growing on the
peat bog. [@1] He put the picked plants in a black bag and hid it under the rear
seat of his car. [@2] After tying one birch broom he returned home, heated the
bathhouse and drank alcohol with a visiting friend. [@1]

After midnight he drove with his friend to pick up another acquaintance but lost
control and the car ended up in a ditch. [@2] Traffic police officers arrived at
the scene of the crash. [@1] Officers inspecting the car found the hidden bag of
cannabis, seized it and sent it for examination, which found the bag held
cannabis plant parts with a total mass of 370.3 grams, a large size. [@1]

The man admitted guilt and said he had planned to dry the plants for personal
use. [@2] The case was investigated under part 2 of Article 228 of the Russian
Criminal Code and the materials will be heard in court for a verdict. [@1] The
criminal case has already been sent to court, according to the investigative
department of the Makarovskoye police. [@2]
