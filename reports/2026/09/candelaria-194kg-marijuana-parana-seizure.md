A cargo of 194 kilograms of marijuana was found during the night on the
Argentine bank of the Paraná River after a boat from Paraguay left bundles there
and returned to the neighbouring country. [@1] The boat's occupants were not
intercepted and escaped in the darkness. [@1] Nine abandoned bundles containing
numerous packages of plant material were recovered in a search of the riverbank.
[@1]

Field checks established that the packages contained marijuana, and weighing put
the total at 194 kilograms across the nine bundles. [@1] A surveillance
operation on the riverbank led to the discovery of 194 kilograms of marijuana
abandoned on the Argentine coast. [@4] The Argentine Naval Prefecture
(Prefectura Naval Argentina) said the operation took place in the coastal area
of Candelaria. [@2] Officers set up observation posts and patrols along the
coast after intelligence indicated a possible drug crossing, and detected the
motorboat from Paraguay at kilometre 1,597 of the Paraná using night-vision
equipment. [@2] Officers estimated the drug's value at more than 747 million
pesos. [@1]

The Posadas fiscal unit, led by prosecutor Gabriela Acasuso, handled the case
and ordered the seizure of the merchandise. [@3] Investigators said they would
seek to identify who took part in the crossing and reconstruct the route the
cargo was to take after entering Argentina. [@1]
