Four people were deprived of their liberty in the Konjic area: S.F., born in
1981, from Konjic; S.D., born in 1986, from Sarajevo; I.M., born in 1992, from
Sinj, a citizen of Croatia; and J.H.H., born in 1983, from Tuzla. [@1] The joint
police and prosecutor statement described them as three men and a woman. [@3]
They were named as Samedin Fišić, 45, from Konjic, Semir Dučić, 40, from
Sarajevo, Ivan Majić, 36, from Sinj, a Croatian citizen, and Jelena Hribar
Hadžić, 43, from Tuzla. [@2] They were suspected of the extended offence of
unauthorised production and distribution of narcotic drugs as co-perpetrators,
and 45-year-old S.F. was also charged with illegal possession of weapons or
explosive substances. [@1] Officers of the Sarajevo Canton interior ministry,
under the Cantonal Prosecutor's Office and with the Intelligence and Security
Agency of Bosnia and Herzegovina and the police of Herzegovina-Neretva and Tuzla
cantons, carried out the operation, codenamed Panther, to document unauthorised
production and trafficking of narcotic drugs. [@1]

In Borci, a village in the municipality of Konjic, officers found a cannabis
plantation and confiscated about 400 stems, with the equipment used to grow the
drug. [@1] The raid was the previous day's operation relative to the 23
September 2026 accounts, placing it on 22 September 2026. [@2]

Searches of premises the suspects used found dozens of packages weighing about
25 kilograms of substances associated with speed, hashish and marijuana, more
than 45 kilograms of material used to bulk out drugs, two pistols with
ammunition, and about 70,000 convertible marks in euros, marks, dollars and
Swiss francs. [@1] Officers also confiscated a Mercedes G. [@1]

The suspects were in criminal processing at the Sarajevo Canton police
headquarters under a joint statement of the police directorate and the cantonal
prosecutor's office. [@1] On 25 September 2026 the Municipal Court in Sarajevo
ordered one month's detention for Fišić, Dučić and Majić, who had been arrested
in operation Panther. [@4] Jelena Hribar Hadžić was released because there was
no legal basis to propose her detention. [@4]
