El Salvador's National Civil Police (PNC) arrested Christopher Orlando Ramírez
Serrano, 18, in San Miguel on suspicion of possession and holding of drugs with
intent to traffic, according to a police statement made public on 17 September
2026. [@1] The detainee was to be referred to the corresponding authorities so
that he could face the legal process for the offence attributed to him. [@1]

During the procedure, the authorities seized several portions of marijuana and
cocaine that were inside a bag, along with cash and a mobile phone. [@1] The PNC
used the case to repeat its warning against involvement in drug-related
activities, saying that anyone found with illegal goods would go to jail. [@1]
