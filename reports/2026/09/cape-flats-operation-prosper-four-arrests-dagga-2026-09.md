Four suspects were arrested across the Cape Flats on drug-related charges. [@1]
Police spokesperson Ndakhe Gwala said members of Operation Prosper conducted
operations in Nyanga, Elsies River, Bellville and Kraaifontein. [@1] The four
were men aged between 36 and 41. [@2] The operations were carried out on Sunday
6 September 2026. [@2]

Officers seized an assortment of drugs, including khat, mandrax tablets, crystal
meth, dagga, the local name for cannabis, and an undisclosed amount of cash.
[@1] The Western Cape government listed the same seizure among the province's
recent drug enforcement successes. [@3] The four suspects face charges of
possession of drugs and dealing in drugs. [@1]

They were expected to appear in court on Monday, 7 September 2026. [@2]
