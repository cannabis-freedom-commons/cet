Police arrested a man in Vuninokonoko, Navua, after a Fiji Police Force detector
dog indicated suspected marijuana during an ongoing drug operation. [@1] Police
spokesperson Ana Naisoro said dried leaves believed to be marijuana were found
in the man's possession, along with more than $1700 in cash. [@1] Officers from
the Fiji Detector Dog Unit made the find as part of the Police Force's ongoing
drug operations. [@1] Police said investigations into the arrest were
continuing. [@1]

Assistant Commissioner of Police Operations Kasiano Vusonilawe said frontline
police teams and a joint task force supported by the Republic of Fiji Military
Forces were making arrests in Nadi and Lautoka. [@1] He said arrests for alleged
unlawful cultivation of illicit drugs had been made in Savusavu and Taveuni two
weeks earlier. [@1] Arrests for alleged unlawful possession of illicit drugs
were also recorded in the other four divisions, and cash believed to be proceeds
of crime was also seized. [@1] Vusonilawe said operations were focused on both
green and white drugs, with some arrests involving repeat offenders. [@1] He
said support capabilities including the Fiji Detector Dog Unit and other law
enforcement stakeholders were being strengthened to curb the illicit drug trade.
