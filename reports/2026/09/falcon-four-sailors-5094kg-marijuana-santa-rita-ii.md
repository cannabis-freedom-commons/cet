Four sailors from Margarita island were detained by the aquatic-space police
division of the Bolivarian National Police after officers found them carrying
more than five kilograms of marijuana on a boat intercepted off Falcón state.
[@1] The detainees were identified as Ángel Jesús Marcano Lugo, 28, Agustín
Ramón León Marín, 53, Manuel José Velázquez Marín, 21, and José Enrique Labori
Rivera, 29. [@1] Falcón's citizen security secretary, General Miguel Morales
Miranda, announced the capture of four people and the seizure of 10 bricks of
marijuana during a maritime operation off the state. [@2] He said the operation
took place on 7 September, about six nautical miles north of the Paraguaná
peninsula, where police intercepted the wooden fishing boat Santa Rita II. [@2]
The security secretary said on 7 September that four crew members had been
detained with 10 bricks of marijuana. [@4]

The boat was inspected at Playa Tierra de Nadie in Carirubana municipality,
where officers checked the Santa Rita II, registration ARSH-PE-0289. [@1] Inside
were 10 panelas — blocks of compressed plant material — holding cannabis remains
and seeds, weighing about 5.094 kg of suspected marijuana in total. [@1] The 10
packages were found hidden in the roof of the command bridge when officers
searched the boat at a police base in the presence of witnesses. [@2] Officers
also seized the Santa Rita II, four mobile phones and the departure papers
issued by the Pampatar port authority. [@1]

The boat had sailed from Pampatar on Margarita island on 10 August. [@1] The
crew were reportedly bound for La Alta Guajira along the Falcón coast when
police intercepted the vessel. [@1] The official account says the boat left Juan
Griego for Isla La Tortuga but diverted towards Colombia to be handed to
criminal groups for drug trafficking. [@2] The four crew members did not appear
on the departure manifest issued by the Pampatar port authority and the boat had
no fishing gear. [@3] The case was placed before the Ministerio Público, which
will determine the criminal responsibility of the four detainees. [@1] The
investigation stayed open into the drug's origin, destination and possible
involvement of others. [@1]
