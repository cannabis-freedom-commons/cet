Police in Ust-Kamenogorsk seized about 3 kg of marijuana hidden in a car. [@1]
Officers found six packages of the banned substance distributed in hiding places
inside the car, in the wheel-arch liners, behind the door trim and the rear
seats. [@1]

The detained man is a 39-year-old resident of another region of the country.
[@2] According to investigators, he acted as a carrier, bringing drugs from
another region of the country to Ust-Kamenogorsk for further sale. [@2] An
examination established that the seized substance was dried marijuana with a
total weight of about three kilograms. [@2]

The man was detained during the police operations Pautina and Karasora. [@2] A
criminal case was opened over the find and the man was placed in a temporary
detention facility. [@2]
