Three people alarmed passengers aboard a train on Thursday by showing a knife to
other passengers and photographing it, causing concern among travellers. [@1]
After passengers present aboard signalled the armed individuals, police carried
out a forced stop at Bettembourg and detained the three suspects. [@2] Officers
searched the suspects at the station and seized a knife and a pepper-spray
canister described as a tear-gas canister. [@2] During the same search officers
seized a small quantity of hashish from the suspects. [@1] The train was
travelling in the south of the country when the intervention and forced stop
took place. [@2]

On the prosecutor's orders, two of the three people were identified and official
reports were drawn up against them. [@3] RTL reported that two of the three were
placed in police custody. [@2] The incident was recounted in the police bulletin
published on Friday, the day after the Thursday intervention. [@3] The three had
been signalled as armed men after displaying the knife to fellow travellers.
[@2]
