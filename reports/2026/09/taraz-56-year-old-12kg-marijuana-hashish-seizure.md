Police in Taraz detained a 56-year-old man on suspicion of illegal acquisition,
storage and transportation of more than 12 kg of marijuana and almost 389 g of
hashish. [@1]

A search of the Opel Vectra he was driving found a travel bag holding 12 kg 30 g
of dried marijuana and 264.58 g of hashish. [@1] Officers seized another 124.38
g of hashish on the balcony of the man's home. [@1] Police put the total seized
at 12 kg 30 g of marijuana and 388.96 g of hashish. [@1]

According to preliminary information, the man had gathered the drugs in Bayzak
district and kept them in storage. [@1] He was detained while driving his own
car toward Zhualy district. [@1]

The man gave confessional testimony during the investigation. [@1] A pre-trial
investigation was opened into illegal acquisition, storage and transportation of
narcotic drugs on an especially large scale for the purpose of sale. [@1]

The 24KZ account reports the same car find of 12 kg 30 g of dried marijuana and
264.58 g of hashish and the same total of 388.96 g of hashish. [@2]
