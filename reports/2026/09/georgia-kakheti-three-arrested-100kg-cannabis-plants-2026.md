Officers from the Kakheti Police Department of Georgia's Ministry of Internal
Affairs arrested three people at different times, the ministry said. [@1] The
three were accused of illegally acquiring and storing a particularly large
quantity of narcotics and of illegally sowing, growing and cultivating plants
that contain a narcotic substance. [@1] Georgia's Interior Ministry said the
three were detained in Kakheti on accusations of illegally sowing
drug-containing plants. [@2]

Searches of the suspects' persons, their homes, their household plots and the
land around a farm produced the drugs as evidence, the ministry said. [@3]
Officers seized up to 100 kilograms of cannabis plants and up to 115 grams of
marijuana in the searches. [@1] They also seized materials that the ministry
said were needed to package the narcotics. [@1]

The investigation is being conducted under Articles 260 and 265¹ of Georgia's
Criminal Code, offences that provide for up to 20 years in prison or life
imprisonment. [@1]
