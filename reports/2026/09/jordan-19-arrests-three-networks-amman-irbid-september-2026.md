Jordan's Public Security Directorate said its Anti-Narcotics Department had
caught three large drug-trafficking networks in the capital and in Irbid
governorate over the preceding period. [@1] Nineteen people were detained in
all, with eleven held in the capital in two separate cases and eight in Irbid.
[@2]

In the first capital case, investigators identified ten members of a network
pushing drugs across Amman, raided all their locations at once and arrested them
with 36 slabs of hashish. [@1]

In the second capital case, officers raided a suspect and searched his home,
finding 41 slabs of hashish, 14,000 narcotic pills and two firearms. [@1]
Between them the two capital cases accounted for 11 detainees, 77 slabs of
hashish and 14,000 narcotic pills, Roya News reported. [@3] In Irbid, a special
investigation team tracked an eight-member trafficking network, then raided
them, arrested all eight and seized 5,000 narcotic pills. [@1]

Al Dustour published the same Public Security Directorate statement on 18
September 2026, with the same two capital cases and the eight-member Irbid
network. [@4]
