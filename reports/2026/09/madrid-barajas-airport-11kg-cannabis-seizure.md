Spain's Guardia Civil and the EU border agency Frontex co-led a week-long
operation called Aerial Routes at Adolfo Suárez Madrid-Barajas Airport against
synthetic drug trafficking through the air. [@2] The operation brought together
Austria, Greece, Ireland, Latvia, the Netherlands, Portugal and Spain. [@1]

Officers seized 11 kilograms of cannabis in five bags at the airport. [@1]
Officers also seized 1,536 grams of ketamine. [@2] They found 15 plastic boxes
holding a substance resembling toxic mushrooms, hidden inside shoe boxes. [@3]
Frontex described the haul as more than 12 kilograms of drugs in total, a figure
that includes the ketamine and the mushroom-like substance but is not counted as
cannabis here. [@2]

Frontex deployed four Standing Corps officers and one operational officer to
support the Spanish authorities with checks on passengers, luggage, cargo and
parcels. [@2] The action covered risk analysis and profiling at airports, canine
units, parcel and postal inspection, mobile scanners, automated baggage handling
and in-person checks on goods passengers carried. [@2]
