Northern Cape police ran a joint cross-border operation with the Botswana Police
Service and other law enforcement agencies along the South Africa–Botswana
border. [@1] Two men were arrested on suspicion of dealing in dagga, the South
African term for cannabis, in the Witdraai and Severn policing areas. [@1]
Police seized dagga with a combined estimated street value of approximately
R5,400, as well as cash believed to be the proceeds of illicit drug sales. [@1]

The operation ran from 13 to 16 September 2026 and also targeted stock theft,
stolen vehicles, illicit firearms and ammunition and the movement of
undocumented people. [@1] Northern Cape police spokesperson Brigadier Mashay
Gamieldien said it involved SAPS Border Policing, Crime Intelligence, Visible
Policing, Public Order Policing, the Vehicle Crime Investigation Unit and the
Stock Theft and Endangered Species Unit. [@1] Gamieldien said the operation was
supported by the South African National Defence Force, the Border Management
Authority, SANParks and SARS Customs. [@1]

Officers issued eight traffic fines worth R4,100, detained three undocumented
people and searched 410 vehicles and 839 people at four vehicle checkpoints.
[@1] During patrols between the Gemsbok and Middelputs ports of entry, officials
found border fence wires untied and broken again and clear truck tracks crossing
between Botswana and South Africa. [@1] The information was handed to the
Botswana Police Service for further investigation because the vehicle and people
involved are believed to be Botswana nationals. [@1] Officials found further
fence damage in sections concealed from the main road, and possible livestock
smuggling was among the lines of inquiry. [@2]
