Officers of the Federal Investigations Department of Argentina's federal police
detained Peruvian citizen “Jhimmy” in the city of Buenos Aires on accusations he
led a drug-distribution structure in the Padre Carlos Mugica barrio of Retiro
and nearby areas. [@1] The capture followed an investigation that produced 12
searches in which five other alleged members of the organisation were also
detained. [@1] The case began in December 2024 after a Buenos Aires City Police
procedure in Padre Mugica in which a woman was detained and more than two
kilograms of high-purity cocaine hydrochloride were seized. [@1]

The National Criminal and Correctional Federal Court No. 1 under Judge María
Romilda Servini intervened, and the investigation was later entrusted to the PFA
Anti-Fraud Division to establish the origin and destination of the drugs. [@1]
Investigators identified “Jhimmy” as the main person under investigation behind
the seized cocaine, and established that he was a fugitive using a false
identity who had entered Argentina through an unauthorised border crossing. [@1]
According to the investigation, he coordinated distribution inside Padre Mugica
and peripheral areas with two Argentine men and three women, one Argentine, one
Peruvian and one Paraguayan, who handled storage, cutting and distribution and
collected sale money for transfer to virtual wallets. [@1] Police identified 12
premises linked to the case, 11 in Padre Mugica and one in the Congreso
neighbourhood, which the court ordered searched in operations run in parallel
with 42 searches against the “Loco César” organisation. [@1]

The PFA detained “Jhimmy” and his five alleged collaborators, leaving all six
adults with the seized items at the disposal of the intervening court in a case
under Drug Law 23.737. [@1] During the searches officers also seized doses of
marijuana, a car, more than 9 million pesos in cash mainly in small bills,
dollars, euros, a notebook, 20 mobile phones and investigation documents. [@1]
