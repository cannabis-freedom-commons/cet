Detainees and cells were checked during a judicial operation at the prison in
Ypres on 27 September 2026. [@1] The action was conducted jointly by prison
staff, the local police and the federal police. [@1] Officers administered a
total of 49 drug screening tests to detainees during that joint operation. [@1]
The local police announced on Monday that searches had produced positive results
in several cells. [@1]

Officers searched a total of 19 cells during the operation. [@1] Eleven of those
19 cells gave a positive result when searched. [@1] Thirty-four detainees
produced a negative result at the end of the operation. [@1] Four prisoners
tested positive for cocaine and eleven tested positive for MDMA. [@1]

Police drew up 12 procès-verbaux, the written police reports, including eight
for drug possession. [@1] Two further reports concerned trafficking in
significant quantities, alongside one report for doping and one for breaching
imposed conditions. [@1] The drugs discovered included hashish, flakka known as
the zombie drug, cocaine, cannabis and mephedrone. [@1] The source describes the
checks as part of a judicial operation rather than a routine inspection. [@1]
