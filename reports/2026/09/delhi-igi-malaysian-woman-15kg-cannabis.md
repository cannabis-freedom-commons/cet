Indian authorities arrested a 22-year-old Malaysian woman from Perak at Indira
Gandhi International Airport in New Delhi on 4 September 2026. [@1] She had
flown from Kuala Lumpur on Batik Air flight OD205, which left Kuala Lumpur
International Airport on the day of her arrest. [@1] Delhi Customs officers
diverted her at the Green Channel and found 30 polythene pouches of suspected
ganja, weighing 15,015.5 grams net, in her trolley bag. [@2] Delhi Customs
valued the haul at about 52.5 million rupees, or about RM2.2 million. [@3]

She was arrested under Section 43(B) of India’s Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic
Substances Act 1985, and the cannabis was seized under Section 43(A). [@2] A
case was registered against her under Sections 8, 20, 23 and 29 of the Act,
which cover illegal possession, transport, import and conspiracy. [@2] Malaysian
police said she was detained by Delhi Customs and would be investigated under
the Act while awaiting trial. [@1]

Malaysia's Bukit Aman police headquarters confirmed the arrest, and its
narcotics department learned of it from India's Narcotics Control Bureau. [@1]
Its director, Comm Datuk Hussein Omar Khan, said the woman had one prior
drug-related offence under Section 15 of Malaysia's Dangerous Drugs Act 1952.
[@1] He said the department would keep in contact with the NCB for further
information on the case. [@1]
