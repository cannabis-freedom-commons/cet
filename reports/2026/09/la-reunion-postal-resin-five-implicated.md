Five people were implicated in an investigation into cannabis resin sent by post
from mainland France to La Réunion. [@1] After the custodies, two suspects were
brought before a magistrate and three other people were summoned to court. [@1]
Investigators sought to establish who was behind the shipments and what role
each person might have played. [@1]

On 2 December 2025 customs at La Réunion intercepted a parcel from mainland
France containing 902 grams of cannabis resin. [@1] Two further parcels
intercepted a few days later held 980 grams and 988 grams of resin. [@1] The
three shipments shared several features, notably their place of dispatch and the
quantities of drugs carried. [@1] The three parcels held nearly 2.9 kilograms of
cannabis resin in total. [@1]

On 13 March 2026 the Saint-Pierre judicial court assigned the Saint-Paul
research brigade to continue the investigation. [@1] Investigators identified
the presumed recipient, who is believed to have had some thirty parcels
containing between 100 grams and one kilogram of resin sent since June 2024.
