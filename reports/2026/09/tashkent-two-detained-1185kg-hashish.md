Two men were detained in Tashkent on suspicion of trying to sell a large batch
of drugs, the State Security Service said. [@3] They had agreed to sell 1 kg 185
g of hashish to a conditional buyer for $26,000. [@3] The suspected seller, born
in 1988 and previously convicted of a drug-related offence, was arrested at a
hotel in Tashkent's Yakkasaray district. [@4] He was detained as he received
$21,000 of the agreed payment, with the drug and other physical evidence. [@1]
The driver of a Cobalt car, born in 1987, was detained near the hotel after
bringing the hashish from Surkhandarya to the capital. [@4]

The joint operation involved officers of the State Security Service's
Karakalpakstan department, customs and internal affairs bodies. [@1] Uzbek
accounts reported that the hashish had been brought into Uzbekistan from a
neighbouring country, which they did not name. [@2] Both men had previous
criminal convictions, one for an offence involving narcotic drugs and the other
for theft. [@1]

A criminal case was opened and investigators said they were establishing all the
circumstances and possible other suspects. [@3]
