Henry Mumba, 56, of Kabushi Township in Ndola was convicted of one count of
trafficking in psychotropic substances and sentenced to 12 months' imprisonment
with hard labour. [@1] The court heard that on 27 May 2026 Mumba trafficked 59.3
grams of cannabis sativa. [@1] After the sentence was passed, Mumba asked God to
bless Ndola Magistrate Brenda Chiganda. [@1]

Senior Public Prosecutor Elvis Silungwe told the court that Assistant
Investigations Officer Rogers Nyanga received information that a man was dealing
in suspected cannabis at Chiwala Compound. [@1] Nyanga and other officers went
to the area, according to the prosecutor. [@1]
