Honduras's National Police said it captured two alleged members of the Pandilla
18 in Comayagüela on 1 September 2026. [@1] Police said the two were detained on
suspicion of illegal possession of firearms and drug trafficking. [@1] The
suspects were identified only by the aliases "El Flaco" and "Chucky". [@1]

Officers said they confiscated two firearms of the pistol type. [@1] They also
seized several packages containing alleged marijuana and cocaine. [@1]
