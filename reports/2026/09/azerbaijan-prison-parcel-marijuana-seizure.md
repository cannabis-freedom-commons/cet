Azerbaijan's penitentiary service seized 0.543 grams of dried marijuana hidden
in a postal parcel sent to a convict at Correctional Institution No. 1, the
Ministry of Justice said. [@1] The marijuana was found in packets specially
concealed in the heel of a shoe in that parcel. [@2]

The same parcel also contained 0.716 grams of heroin and 0.567 grams of
methamphetamine, quantities excluded from the cannabis figures. [@1] In a
separate find at the Baku Investigative Isolator, officers examined a delivery
brought by the wife of a detained man. [@3] That delivery held 0.888 grams of
heroin in four packets sewn into the clothing. [@3] The four packets at the
isolator were stitched into the clothing in a concealed manner. [@3]

Correctional Institution No. 1 is run by the Penitentiary Service of
Azerbaijan's Ministry of Justice, which announced both seizures. [@2] The
ministry said the finds were made during inspections of the parcels. [@1]
Investigations were opened into both incidents. [@1]
