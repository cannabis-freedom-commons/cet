Police in El Salvador announced the arrest of four people on suspicion of
unlawful drug trafficking in procedures in Santa Ana and La Libertad. [@1] In
the first operation, in the Comecayo canton of Santa Ana Centro, officers
detained Álvaro Ernesto Aquino, 67, and Manuel Rigoberto Aquino Salazar, 62, at
a home. [@1] The search was carried out by the La Libertad Oeste anti-narcotics
section with K9 dogs trained to detect illegal substances. [@1] The two men
detained in Comecayo were residents of that canton. [@2] The first operation
took place on Saturday night. [@4]

Officers found portions of marijuana and cocaine in three large packages and
more than 20 small portions whose drug type was not specified, along with two
scales, a laptop, three mobile phones and cash. [@1] The PNC published no
quantities for the drugs or amount for the cash. [@4]

In a second operation, the PNC anti-narcotics division, backed by the Armed
Forces, detained two people in Ciudad Arce, La Libertad Centro. [@1] They were
José Mauricio Martínez Cano, 26, and Nathaly Yessenia Repreza Cano, 38, who were
stopped while riding a motorcycle. [@1] Police seized a large package of
marijuana whose weight was not given, the motorcycle and a mobile phone. [@1]
The motorcycle and phone were described as used to sell the drug. [@3] The
Ciudad Arce pair were intercepted by anti-narcotics agents together with
soldiers. [@5]

The two men from Comecayo were to be referred on a charge of unlawful drug
trafficking. [@1] The cases were to go before the judicial authorities to decide
the detainees' procedural situation. [@1]
