Officers of the Astara Customs Department of Azerbaijan's State Customs
Committee stopped an attempt to move a large consignment of drugs through the
country in transit. [@1] The truck was carrying a load of "lettuce" and "celery"
from Iran to Russia and was stopped on the import control line at the Astara
customs post. [@1]

Officers passed the vehicle through a stationary X-ray scanner and examined it
with a customs service dog, and found that packages were concealed in the cargo.
[@1] In 230 packages made to look like celery and hidden from customs control,
officers found marijuana with a net weight of 79 kilograms and 740 grams. [@2]
The marijuana was packed in 230 "celery-shaped" packages hidden from customs
control. [@3]

The State Customs Committee said an investigation into the case was under way.
[@2]
