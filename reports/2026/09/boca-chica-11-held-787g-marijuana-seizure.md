The National Drug Control Directorate said a joint anti-microtrafficking
operation in Boca Chica on Sunday 6 September ended with 11 people detained and
3,776.5 grams of suspected controlled substances seized. [@1] The deployment
comprised 65 tactical operations and three searches in the municipality and
nearby areas to dismantle drug distribution points in Greater Santo Domingo.
[@1] The agency published the detainees’ abbreviated names as Fausto M. G.,
Walin O. M., Francisco José P., Egrimaldi S. H., Robinson A., Robert Alexander
C., Darwin M., Ixaniel N. M., Anthony Jesús C., Yefri V. de los S. and John S.
R. [@1]

Authorities reported 687 portions of white powder presumed to be cocaine
weighing 1,840.4 grams and 426 portions of rock-like material presumed to be
crack weighing 1,149.4 grams. [@1] They also reported 214 portions of plant
material presumed to be marijuana weighing 786.7 grams. [@1] Officers seized a 9
mm pistol with six cartridges and RD$12,885 in cash, listed as US$217.68. [@1]

The agency added that officers confiscated 16 mobile phones, five laptop
computers, three tablets, nine digital scales, five radios and three metal
chains. [@1]
