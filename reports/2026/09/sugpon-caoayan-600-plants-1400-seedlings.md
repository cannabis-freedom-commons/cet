Police discovered about 600 fully grown marijuana plants and 1,400 seedlings at
three cultivation sites in Sugpon on 5 September 2026. [@1] The eradication
operation took place in Barangay Caoayan and covered an estimated 111 square
metres. [@1]

The operation was led by Lt. Col. David Dulnuan, deputy provincial director for
operations, with the ISPPO drug enforcement and intelligence units, the 2nd
Ilocos Sur Provincial Mobile Force Company, PDEA Region I and Sugpon police.
[@1] Police valued the confiscated plants and seedlings at an estimated
P176,000. [@1]

The operation formed part of the ISPPO continuing campaign to eliminate
marijuana cultivation and curb the illegal drug trade in the province. [@1] A
second account repeats the same 600 grown plants, 1,400 seedlings and three
Sugpon sites on the same Saturday. [@2]
