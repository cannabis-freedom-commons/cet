The second stage of Uzbekistan's nationwide Qoradori-2026 preventive operation
included helicopter overflights in all regions, the Interior Ministry said. [@2,4]
In Andijan, Samarkand and Bukhara regions officers found cannabis grown in
household plots and marijuana stored at homes. [@1] The ministry said 70 kg of
drug-containing plants grown in households were destroyed during the operation.
[@3] UzDaily reported the same ministry statement as 70 kg of narcotic drugs and
plants found. [@1]

In Altynkul district of Andijan region police detained a local resident who kept
24 kg of marijuana at his home. [@1,4] The marijuana was seized in procedural
order and a criminal case was opened under article 273, part 6, clause a. [@3]
In the same district officers detained the manager of a farm who had sown and
grown 520 cannabis bushes on 200 square metres. [@2,4] In Izbaskan district a
resident kept 6 kg 100 g of marijuana in his home. [@2]

In Samarkand district a man grew 41 cannabis bushes on his household plot and
kept 400 g of marijuana. [@2,4] In Bukhara district police detained a married
couple who grew 46 cannabis bushes in the yard of their house. [@1,4] In another
household in the same district family members grew 53 cannabis bushes on 42
square metres. [@2,4]

Criminal cases were opened under parts 1 and 2 of article 270, which covers
cultivating crops prohibited for cultivation, and part 6 of article 273, which
covers sale of narcotic drugs. [@1,4] The ministry said investigative measures
were continuing. [@1,4]
