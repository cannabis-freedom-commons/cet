The Anti-Narcotics Department arrested 14 drug dealers and promoters in eight
cases of trafficking, promotion and smuggling across the Kingdom, the Public
Security Directorate said on Thursday 24 September 2026. [@1] The Public
Security Directorate spokesperson said anti-narcotics officers, dealing with
eight cases of trafficking, promotion and smuggling, arrested 14 dealers and
promoters and seized quantities of narcotics. [@2]

In the Northern Badia, officers raided a drug dealer's home, arrested him and a
companion, and seized 10 blocks of hashish together with one kilogram of crystal
methamphetamine and 4,000 narcotic pills. [@1] In a second Northern Badia case,
officers stopped a dealer who was transporting narcotics toward Zarqa and seized
7,000 narcotic pills. [@1] In a third Northern Badia case, officers raided two
people and seized 4,000 narcotic pills. [@1] In Mafraq Governorate, officers
raided a house used to store narcotics, arrested three people inside, and seized
eight blocks of hashish. [@1]

In Balqa Governorate, officers arrested a suspect who was carrying one kilogram
of heroin. [@1] In Amman, two raids arrested two people classified as dangerous
and wanted in drug cases, and firearms and quantities of narcotics were seized.
[@1] In Karak, two people linked to regional gangs were arrested with 75,000
narcotic pills hidden in a cargo vehicle, which they were preparing to smuggle
to a neighbouring country. [@1] At Queen Alia International Airport, working
with security agencies and Jordan Customs, officers arrested a man who was
trying to receive a package containing half a kilogram of crystal
methamphetamine. [@1]

The cannabis seizures in the roundup are the 10 hashish blocks in the Northern
Badia and the eight blocks in Mafraq. [@1]
