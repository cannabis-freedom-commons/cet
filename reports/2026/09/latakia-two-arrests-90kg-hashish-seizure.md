Latakia's Anti-Drug Branch, the drug-enforcement unit cited as the source of the
account, arrested two people in Latakia governorate. [@1] The branch described
the pair as among the most prominent dealers and promoters of narcotic
substances, identifying them only by the initials (أ.ص) and (أ.س). [@1]

Officers seized 90 kilograms of hashish found in the pair's possession. [@1] In
the same seizure, officers also took 15,000 Captagon pills, which this report
does not count in its cannabis figures. [@1] Officers also seized a quantity of
material described only as crystal, with no amount stated, which is likewise
left out of the cannabis figures. [@1]

The branch's announcement attributes all of these seizures to the same two
detainees in Latakia governorate. [@1] The published account gives no arrest
location more precise than the governorate and no street, town or premises. [@1]
It reports no charges, pleas, detention measures or court outcome for the two
detainees at the time of publication. [@1]
