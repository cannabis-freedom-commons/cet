Police in Winterberg-Langewiese, Germany, discovered a large cannabis plantation
during a search and arrested one suspect. [@1] Criminal police of the
Hochsauerlandkreis district police authority found a 50-year-old man in the
building and took him into temporary custody. [@2] The man was an Albanian
national and was to be brought before an investigating judge in Brilon the
following day. [@2]

The search of the property was carried out on the morning of 17 September 2026.
[@2] It followed a tip from the public that had hardened during further
inquiries. [@2] Officers seized 213 mature cannabis plants, 637 seedlings and
207 already harvested plants, along with the growing facility. [@2] In all, more
than 1,000 plants were recovered. [@3] Police described the site as a
professionally run cannabis growing facility of considerable size. [@2] They
estimated the crop could have produced 60 to 80 kilograms of cannabis. [@2] The
potential street value was put at 600,000 to 800,000 euros. [@3]

Police said inquiries into who was behind the plantation and whether other
people were involved were continuing. [@2]
