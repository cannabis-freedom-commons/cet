Police in Guantánamo detained a man at a house near the intersection of calle
Martí and 13 Norte on Thursday morning, next to a busy bus stop serving the
Reparto Obrero route. [@1] A separate account placed the same Thursday operation
at calle Martí and 13 Norte and said it ended with one man detained and a
substance that tested positive for marijuana. [@2] Neighbors said they had
previously alerted authorities to suspicious activity at the house and that the
detained man was known in the community for an alleged link to drug trafficking.
[@1] When police arrived, the detainee's wife tried to hide part of the
substance, but officers seized it and on-site tests came back positive for
marijuana. [@1] Witnesses quoted by the second account also said the seized
material was tested at the scene and returned positive for marijuana. [@2]

Officers also seized a large sum of cash. [@1] Witnesses described the
intervention as "quick and tense". [@1] The account originated with the local
Facebook page "Guantánamo y su Verdad", which reported several anti-drug
operations around the city that week. [@1]
