City Police officers detained five men and seized almost 20 kg of marijuana and
242 grams of cocaine in raids in Villa 15, known as Ciudad Oculta, in the Buenos
Aires neighbourhood of Villa Lugano. [@1] The investigation began when the
Southern Anti-Drug Investigations Division stopped a Volkswagen Vento at José
León Suárez and Monte. [@1] The driver, a 36-year-old man from Morón, tried to
discard the backpack holding the marijuana. [@1] The backpack held four
marijuana bricks weighing 3.031 kg. [@1] The Unidad de Flagrancia Sur, led by
Agustín Aymerik, ordered the driver's detention and the seizure of the drug.
[@1] The Juzgado Penal, Contravencional y de Faltas n.º 26, led by Agustín
Nicolás Repetto, authorized two raids in Villa 15. [@1] Investigators identified
the alleged suppliers of the drug and their addresses during the inquiry. [@1]

In the first raid, on Calle Hubac, officers detained a 20-year-old man and
seized 15 marijuana bricks weighing 11.808 kg, two mobile phones and cash. [@1]
The second raid led to the detention of three men aged 33, 38 and 68, the eldest
a pensioner, and the seizure of six more bricks weighing 4.645 kg, 242.7 grams
of cocaine, cash and a digital scale. [@1]

In total the operation seized 25 marijuana bricks weighing 19.484 kg and 242.7
grams of cocaine, and the judge ordered all five men held. [@1] The Tactical
Pacification Unit (DUTP) of the Villa 15 neighbourhood police and personnel from
other divisions provided security during the raids. [@1] The operation also
produced other items that investigators linked to drug commercialisation. [@2]
