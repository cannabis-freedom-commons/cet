The public prosecution at the Nabeul Court of First Instance authorised officers
of the Central Anti-Drug Brigade of the National Guard in Ben Arous to hold four
people pending the investigation into a network trafficking drugs and narcotic
tablets in Nabeul. [@1] According to the available account, Nabeul judicial
police received information that a young man had joined drug and narcotic-tablet
trafficking, and intensified inquiries first identified him and showed that he
was wanted for drug trafficking. [@1] Judicial police in Nabeul then raided the
suspect's hideout in coordination with the public prosecution and detained him
together with three other people. [@1]

Searching the premises, officers seized a quantity of Lyrica narcotic pills and
varying quantities of cannabis, described as القنب الهندي. [@1] After the Nabeul
prosecution was notified, it issued a judicial commission to the Central
Anti-Drug Brigade of the National Guard in Ben Arous to continue the
investigation and to keep the four detainees in custody. [@1] The case was at
the investigation stage, with the four held pending further inquiries by the
National Guard anti-drug unit. [@1]
