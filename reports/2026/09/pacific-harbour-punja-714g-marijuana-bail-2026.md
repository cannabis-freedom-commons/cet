Thakor Punja was brought to the Navua Magistrates Court in another case of
alleged marijuana possession, and the court granted him bail this morning. [@1]

Punja was allegedly found with 7.14 grams of marijuana at a checkpoint in
Pacific Harbour on Thursday night. [@1]

He is charged with one count of unlawful possession of illicit drugs. [@1]

Police Prosecutor Nitesh Prasad had strongly objected to bail. [@1] The court
granted Punja bail on strict conditions, including a $1,000 bail bond, two
sureties and a stop departure order. [@1]
