Police seized approximately 1,400 pounds of ganja in two separate incidents in
Westmoreland on Wednesday night during a coordinated operation. [@1] The
operation took place on the night of 23 September 2026, with the two searches
three minutes apart. [@2] Two men were taken into custody in connection with the
seizures. [@1] The matter is being investigated by the Firearms and Narcotics
Investigation Division. [@1]

The first seizure was made about 8:00 pm in Little London, where police found 37
knitted bags containing vegetable matter resembling ganja under a shed, weighing
approximately 1,000 pounds. [@1] About three minutes later, at 8:03 pm, another
team searched the neighbouring community of Bluefields and found a ganja field
and a nearby shed. [@1] Approximately 400 pounds of ganja were found at the
Bluefields location. [@1]

Police attached no street value to the haul, and no charges had been announced
at the time of the report. [@2]
