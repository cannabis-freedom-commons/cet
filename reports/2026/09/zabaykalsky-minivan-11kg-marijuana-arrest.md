In Zabaykalsky Krai, police said they had disrupted the illegal trafficking of a
large batch of drugs by detaining a suspect with harvested wild cannabis. [@1]
The detainee is a 59-year-old previously convicted resident of the village of
Zabaykalsk who is suspected of harvesting and transporting wild-growing
cannabis. [@1] Officers stopped the suspect's minivan in the area of Mirnaya
railway station and inspected its interior and luggage compartment. [@1]

According to the regional Interior Ministry, the man had specifically travelled
about 250 kilometres from his home locality to Olovyanninsky municipal district.
[@1] Inside the minivan, officers found four bags of plant material with the
characteristic smell of hemp, and the total weight of what was seized exceeded
40 kilograms. [@1] A forensic examination established that the seized plant
material was marijuana. [@1]

Police put the weight of the identified narcotic at almost 11 kilograms, well
below the gross weight of more than 40 kilograms of harvested plant material.
[@1] A criminal case was opened following the detention and seizure. [@1]
