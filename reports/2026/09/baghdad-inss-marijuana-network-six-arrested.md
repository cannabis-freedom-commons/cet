Iraq's National Security Service (INSS) dismantled a six-member network
suspected of trafficking and distributing marijuana in Baghdad on Friday 11
September 2026. [@1] The service said its detachments arrested all six members
and seized about 3.5 kilograms of marijuana from one of the network's
storehouses. [@2]

The INSS said the operation ran under the supervision of its head and began with
intelligence on one man suspected of trading in marijuana. [@2] After obtaining
judicial approvals from the service's specialised judge in Baghdad, officers
tracked the first suspect and arrested him in a tight ambush. [@2] Its
detachments then carried out a series of tracking and raid operations, arresting
the remaining members one by one. [@2] The last lead took officers to a
marijuana storehouse hidden inside an abandoned house, which they raided and
where they seized the stored quantity. [@3]

The service said the suspects and the seized material were handed over to the
competent authorities to take the legal measures against them. [@2] Across Iraq,
the Interior Ministry counted 378 drug-trafficking networks dismantled in the
first half of 2026, including 59 international groups, and 1,977 kilograms and
522 grams of narcotics seized. [@1] Courts recorded 3,553 drug-related
convictions during the same period, according to the ministry. [@1]
