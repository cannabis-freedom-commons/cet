Officers working with intelligence-unit staff stopped a shopping-tour bus at a
road check on Ruta Nacional 38 on Tuesday morning. [@1] Officers first checked
the documents of the bus and those of its drivers and passengers, then inspected
the merchandise carried by the people on board. [@1] When they searched the
belongings of an adult woman, they found capybara plush toys of different shapes
and sizes packed together in a bundle. [@1]

The officers recorded anomalies and excessive weight in the toys, and some of
the plush toys gave off a strong smell of cannabis. [@1] After officers were
authorised to open the toys, they pulled bricks containing marijuana and cocaine
from the stuffing. [@1] The Gendarmería Nacional Argentina reported the seizure
of 49 kilos of marijuana and 4 kilos of cocaine from a passenger on a bus
travelling from Salta province to Mendoza. [@1] The passenger was detained and
the drugs were seized. [@1]

Authorities found during the check that the drugs had been hidden inside plush
capybaras. [@1] The stopped bus was travelling from Salta province toward
Mendoza. [@1]
