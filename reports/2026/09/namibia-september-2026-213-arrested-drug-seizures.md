The Namibian Police Force seized drugs and illicit substances valued at
approximately N$7.3 million and arrested 213 suspects, including minors, in
connection with drug-related offences during September 2026. [@1] Of the 213
suspects arrested, 198 were Namibian, eight were Angolan, three were Congolese,
two were Burundian, one was Somali and one was Tanzanian. [@1] Among the
Namibian suspects arrested, 169 were male, 18 were female and 11 were juveniles.
[@1] The Namibian Police Force described the involvement of juveniles in
drug-related offences as a grave concern. [@1]

According to the Namibian Police, the seizures, recorded between 1 and 30
September, included cannabis, tablets of Mandrax, crack cocaine and cocaine
powder. [@1] The total estimated value of the drugs seized was N$7 294 057,
approximately N$7.3 million. [@1]

The police urged members of the public to report drug-related activities,
emphasising that dealing in drugs is illegal and that reporting such activities
can help save lives. [@1]
