Four men from Beemapally and Thiruvallam, Naushad, 40, Saidali, 33, autorickshaw
driver Sulfikar and Shefeek, 51, were arrested at Kovalam with 3.6 kg of
cannabis. [@2] The arrests were made by the city Dansaf team under narcotics ACP
Gopakumar. [@1] The four were intercepted near Porode bridge on the
Kazhakkoottam-Karode bypass on Monday evening with 3.6kg of ganja. [@3]

The cannabis was brought from Odisha by bus to Tamil Nadu and then carried by
autorickshaw. [@1] Police said the group bought it in Odisha for 22,000 rupees.
[@1] The cannabis was packed in two bags. [@1] Officers seized T-shirts bought
in Odisha, three mobile phones and 13,000 rupees, and took the autorickshaw into
custody. [@1] Police also seized paint the group had used on the bus so that the
smell of the cannabis would not be noticed. [@1]

The group was handed to Vizhinjam police and later transferred to Kovalam police
for further investigation. [@3] During verification, police found that Naushad
had been wanted since 2003 in a case registered by Kovalam police for possessing
explosive substances. [@3] During interrogation the accused said they had
procured the ganja in Muniguda, Odisha. [@3] They allegedly brought it to
Thiruvananthapuram to sell it in smaller quantities at a higher price. [@3] The
four were produced before a court and remanded in judicial custody. [@3] Police
said the four had been accused in narcotics cases before. [@1]
