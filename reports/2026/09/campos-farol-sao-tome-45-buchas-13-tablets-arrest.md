Two men aged 19 and 21 were arrested on suspicion of drug trafficking and
association for trafficking in Farol de São Tomé, in Campos dos Goytacazes, on
the night of Tuesday 29 September 2026. [@2] Officers of the Comando de
Policiamento Rodoviário (CPRv), which polices the state's highways, were on
patrol on the RJ-216. [@1] Acting on reports of drug sales in the area, officers
intensified searches near the highway. [@1]

When they entered Rua José Pessanha Neto, officers saw the two men trying to
flee on foot and throwing a plastic bag away; both were caught and approached.
[@1] In the bag police seized 46 pinos, small vials, of cocaine, 45 buchas,
wrapped portions, of marijuana and 13 tablets of the same herb. [@1] Officers
also seized two mobile phones and a portable charger. [@1] The 19-year-old
slipped and fell during the escape, suffering a light cut to his left eyebrow,
and was treated and released at Hospital Ferreira Machado. [@1] The two men were
taken to the 134th Police Station in Centro, where the case was registered, and
remained in flagrante arrest at the disposal of the courts. [@1]
