On Saturday, September 26, officers executed warrants at a property in Corries
Way, Forres. [@1] According to police, cocaine worth an estimated £7,000 and
cannabis worth an estimated £5,500 was found within the property. [@1] STV said
a 24-year-old man was arrested and charged in connection with the seizure and
was due to appear in court that week. [@1] The Northern Scot put the man's age
at 29 and said he was due to appear in court after the weekend raid. [@2] He was
scheduled to appear at Elgin Sheriff Court on September 28, the Northern Scot
said. [@2]

At Elgin Sheriff Court, Nicholas Clark made no plea to two charges of supplying
cocaine and cannabis, the Northern Scot reported. [@3] Both alleged offences
occurred while Clark was already on bail from the same court, following an
earlier appearance on a separate matter on May 26. [@3] During that operation,
officers executed a search warrant at a property on Corries Way and seized the
substantial haul of narcotics. [@3] Clark has been released on bail pending his
next court appearance, scheduled for October 20. [@3]

Constable Mackinnon, of the Moray Community Investigation Unit, said police
remain committed to taking appropriate action against people profiting from the
sale of illegal and harmful substances. [@1] The £5,500 is the police estimate
of the cannabis value, and the £7,000 cocaine estimate is not counted in the
cannabis figures. [@1]
