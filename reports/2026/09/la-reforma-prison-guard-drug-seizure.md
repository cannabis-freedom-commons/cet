A Costa Rica prison police officer surnamed Hernández was detained after
colleagues caught him apparently trying to bring nearly 800 grams of illicit
substances into the Jorge Arturo Montero Castro prison, known as La Reforma, in
San Rafael de Alajuela. [@1] Officers found two packages stuck to his body
during a security check. [@1] The incident happened on the night of Tuesday 22
September 2026, when the officer returned after leaving at about 6:20 p.m. [@1]
The check followed a confidential tip about a possible attempt to bring illicit
substances into the prison. [@1]

The seizure weighed 787.75 grams in total, described as suspected cocaine,
marijuana and wax, a concentrate derived from cannabis. [@1] The packages were
hidden in the groin area, fixed with tape or adhesive material. [@1] The
Alajuela Prosecutor's Office ordered the officer kept in custody while inquiries
continued. [@1] The officer was ordered to serve one month and 22 days of
pre-trial detention in a maximum-security centre. [@2]

Hernández had three years of service and was posted to the prison's 'convivencia
A' living unit. [@1] He belonged to the same police force tasked with guarding
prisoners and keeping prohibited substances out of the country's prisons. [@1]
