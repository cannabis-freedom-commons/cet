New Taipei City police arrested 12 people, including the suspected ringleader,
over a scam ring that recruited teenagers. [@4] The leader is a 34-year-old man
from the Tailian gang, a chapter of the Beisan Alliance based in Beitou, Taipei.
[@1] Police searched several locations in September 2026 and arrested six adults
and six minors. [@2] The Shilin District Prosecutors Office directed the
investigation, the Central News Agency reported. [@4] The Shilin District Court
ordered the leader and three other members held in detention. [@4] The leader
was held incommunicado, with visits barred. [@1]

He rented flats as staff dormitories, putting six or seven members in each
three-bedroom flat. [@1] He gave members cannabis free of charge to smoke and
used the drug to control the teenagers' movements. [@3] He recruited 16- and
17-year-olds through temple festival processions and dating apps. [@1] The group
was referred to prosecutors on suspicion of organised crime, serious injury,
fraud, deprivation of liberty and offences under the Narcotics Hazard Prevention
Act. [@2]

The case came to light when investigators working on another fraud case found a
video of a beating on a seized phone. [@2] In July the leader suspected a member
of keeping scam proceeds and had him taken to a cemetery in the Beitou hills,
where he was beaten for three hours and his arms and legs were broken. [@2]
Police seized the phones used in the scams and Chanel and Gucci bags and shoes
at the scene. [@2] The Beisan Alliance is one of three main gangs in Beitou,
alongside the Beilian and Tianmiao gangs. [@1] The leader had previous drug and
fraud convictions and was sentenced to six years for fraud in 2021. [@1]
