Guardia di Finanza officers from the Latina operational unit spotted a marijuana
plot on land in Via San Francesco, next to the Rio Martino canal, during an
aerial patrol over the city aimed at gang-labour (caporalato) checks. [@1] The
flight was part of an anti-drug check carried out with the support of the corps'
air-and-naval branch. [@3] On the land officers found seven marijuana plants two
metres tall and marijuana inflorescences drying, for a total weight of more than
19 kilograms, as well as precision scales. [@1] The plot lay in an unauthorised
settlement of shacks and caravans between Via San Francesco and Via dei Volsci,
near the Acque Medie canal. [@2] The search extended to two caravans and a
bungalow on the land, and one caravan held marijuana branches drying in a
dehumidifier. [@1] The 175 grams of cocaine were found across the site: 150
grams in four bags in the caravan where he lived and 25 grams in a secret
compartment behind the radio of his Renault Scenic. [@1]

A 55-year-old man who lived at Piccarello was arrested on 29 September 2026 on a
drug-dealing charge and handed to the judicial authorities. [@1] On 30 September
the single judge of the Latina court confirmed the arrest at a direct-trial
hearing (rito direttissimo, the fast-track procedure for suspects caught in the
act), ordered house arrest and adjourned the case. [@1] Only the 175 grams of
cocaine went to that trial: the marijuana charge was separated, leaving a second
case. [@1]

Chemical tests by a court-appointed expert found a high THC level in the
marijuana. [@1] Officers weighed the seven plants at 17.8 kilograms in total.
[@2] The dried marijuana came to 1.2 kilograms. [@1] After that report the
prosecutors issued a notice closing the marijuana investigation, and the man
faces a second criminal case. [@1] On 18 November 2026, in the cocaine case
alone, the judge sentenced him to 3 years and 8 months in prison and a
20,000-euro fine, with five years' disqualification from public office. [@1] The
prosecutor had asked for 3 years and 4 months. [@1]
