Community police officers from the Shengavit and Malatia divisions detained a
34-year-old foreign citizen on Gagarina Street in Yerevan on 1 September 2026 on
suspicion of acquiring, storing and selling drugs and took him to the Malatia
police division. [@1] Police said the man told them he had fitted out a room in
a private house on the street as a greenhouse where he grew cannabis to make and
sell drugs. [@2] The same day officers detained a 40-year-old foreign citizen
near the house on the same suspicion. [@4] Both men were handed to the
preliminary investigation body with the case papers. [@5]

In a search of the house police found 26 glass containers holding a substance
resembling marijuana with a total weight of more than 16 kilograms. [@1]
Officers also seized 34.4 grams of cannabis seeds, dried cannabis, cannabis
plants grown in flower pots and materials for cultivating them. [@1] Sputnik
Armenia reported that the 26 containers held about 16 kilograms of marijuana.
[@3]

Police opened a criminal proceeding over the find. [@1] Police said the
preliminary investigation is continuing. [@1]
