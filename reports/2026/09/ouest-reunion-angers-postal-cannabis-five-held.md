Five people, four men and one woman aged 22 to 47, were implicated in the postal
cannabis case. [@1] All five were placed in police custody. [@1] Three of them
were summoned to appear in court, while the other two were tried and convicted.
[@1] One of the convicted people received two years in prison including one year
suspended, and the other received 18 months including nine months suspended.
[@1]

Investigators found that about thirty packets had been sent from Angers to the
same address in the west of the island. [@1] Each packet contained between 100
grams and one kilogram of cannabis. [@1] Customs officers first intercepted a
parcel from mainland France at La Réunion containing 902 grams of cannabis
resin. [@1] A few days later officers discovered two more parcels containing
nearly two kilograms of cannabis herb and resin. [@1]

In March 2026 the Saint-Pierre judicial court tasked the Saint-Paul research
brigade with identifying those behind the shipments. [@1] The network operated
as ordered drugs delivered directly by post in the west of the island. [@1]
