The Santa Clara County Sheriff's Department said its Covert Investigations Unit
served a search warrant on an illegal grow discovered in a remote area near Mt.
Hamilton Road. [@1] The grow was an illegal creekside grow in a remote part of
Santa Clara County, served earlier in the month. [@1] No suspects were located
at the site. [@1]

Inside a makeshift greenhouse built from tarps and plastic sheeting tied to
trees, officers found about 720 marijuana plants. [@1] The sheriff's office said
the 720 plants were illegally grown, with evidence of illegal water diversion
and pollution. [@2] The plants have since been fully eradicated. [@1]

Deputies first received a tip about the grow in August. [@1] Using the sheriff's
Star 1 helicopter and assistance from the California Department of Fish and
Wildlife, detectives confirmed numerous plants were being cultivated on the
private property. [@1] Detectives also determined that water was being diverted
illegally from a nearby creek. [@1] Marijuana Eradication Team and Fish and
Wildlife officers had to hike several miles through rough terrain to reach the
site. [@1] Detectives completed the investigation and secured a warrant to
remove the grow. [@1]
