Carabineros officers from the OS7 anti-drug department seized 659 kilograms of
marijuana inside a property in the northern Chilean city of Calama, in the
Antofagasta region, according to information published on 16 September 2026.
[@1] In the same operation, officers intercepted a high-end pickup truck in
which packages containing the drug had been hidden. [@1] Officers detained a
Bolivian woman with residence in Chile during that interception. [@1]

According to the police anti-drug unit responsible for combating drug
trafficking and related offences, the substance was intended to supply illegal
points of sale during the Fiestas Patrias holiday celebrations. [@1]

Antofagasta Carabineros zone chief General Cristian Montre said criminal
organisations currently use Starlink antennas to get around silent points in the
desert and to move drug loads toward the centre and south of the country. [@1]
The account notes that these antennas offer high-speed satellite internet in
urban, rural or extreme zones. [@1] In the region, 58 tonnes of drugs had been
seized so far that year, compared with 38 tonnes during all of 2025. [@1]
