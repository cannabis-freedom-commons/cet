Yanis, a Francilien living in the Melun area, was arrested by Bagnols-sur-Cèze
police as he left a cellar in Les Escanaux on the night of 24 September. [@1]
The arrest took place on a deal point in the Escanaux neighbourhood of
Bagnols-sur-Cèze. [@1] A lookout shouting "arrah" and coming out of a cellar
reportedly panicked when officers moved in. [@1]

Yanis had a few grams of cannabis on him. [@1] Officers seized a nearby bag
containing cannabis and cocaine. [@1] He told the court he had come to
Bagnols-sur-Cèze for love, for a girl. [@1] He said that after three weeks with
her, he was told to work for the dealers and slept in a cellar. [@1] He said he
feared reprisals, had been beaten and threatened against his family, denied the
bag was his, and said everyone else had left before police arrived. [@1]

He had previously been implicated in a Paris-region drug case and placed under
electronic monitoring. [@1] He called this his first imprisonment. [@1] He
appeared before the Nîmes criminal court on 13 November. [@1] The prosecutor
requested 18 months of firm prison and a ban from staying in Gard. [@1]
