Portuguese security police, the PSP, detained a 25-year-old foreign citizen at
Humberto Delgado Airport in Lisbon on 10 September 2026 under a European Arrest
Warrant. [@1] He was detained after presenting himself at the arrivals border
control, having travelled from São Vicente in Cape Verde. [@4]

Border guards of the National Unit for Foreigners and Borders detected the
outstanding warrant during checks through the Schengen Information System. [@2]
Police confirmed the warrant's validity and applicability through international
cooperation mechanisms before making the arrest. [@3] The warrant related to an
18-month prison sentence of which 518 days remained to be served, the PSP said.
[@1] The conviction covered several offences, including drug crimes, possession
and transport of a weapon without the required authorisation, and traffic
infractions. [@3] The police said significant quantities of hashish, as well as
cocaine and cannabis, had been seized in the facts behind the conviction. [@1]

After the arrest the man was to be presented to the Lisbon Court of Appeal,
which handles the procedures for executing the European Arrest Warrant. [@4]
