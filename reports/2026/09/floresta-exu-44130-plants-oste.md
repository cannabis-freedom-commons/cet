A joint operation of the 2nd Specialised Infantry Battalion (2º BIEsp), BEPI and
the Federal Police eradicated about 44,130 cannabis plants in the rural zone of
Floresta, in Pernambuco's Sertão, near the Fazenda Exu, during Operação Oste.
[@1]

Armed individuals received the teams with gunfire and fled through the
vegetation; officers returned fire and no one was injured. [@1] Officers located
four cannabis plantings and seized two handmade weapons. [@1] One suspect was
captured and handed over with the seized material to the Federal Police station
in Salgueiro. [@1]

In an earlier campaign, the Federal Police eradicated and destroyed about
286,000 cannabis plants across 49 plantings between 9 September and 21 October
2025 in nine Sertão municipalities, which it said would have produced 95 tonnes
of marijuana. [@1]
