A Peel motorist who drove under the influence of cannabis has been fined £1,000
and banned for two years. [@1] Craig Shaun Wade was arrested after a police
officer on a motorbike smelt cannabis coming from his car. [@1] Prosecuting
advocate Hazel Carroon told the court that the officer was riding a marked
police motorcycle on April 8 at 7.10pm. [@1] He was travelling between St John’s
and Peel, while Wade was coming in the opposite direction in an Isuzu D-Max van.
[@1] As they passed, the officer reported a smell of cannabis, so he turned his
bike around and followed. [@1] He stopped Wade on Archallagan Road and described
him as looking pale and vacant. [@1]

A drug wipe test proved positive for cannabis and the defendant was subsequently
arrested. [@1] At police headquarters, a sample of blood was taken, which was
sent to the UK for analysis. [@1] This later produced a reading of 5.3 for
cannabis, above the legal limit of two. [@1] The 26-year-old has also been
ordered to take an extended driving test at the end of his ban. [@1]

Appearing before Deputy High Bailiff Rachael Braidwood on September 15, he was
represented by advocate Ian Kermode. [@1] Mr Kermode said that Wade had a
stressful job, involving specialist maintenance of commercial vehicles. [@1] He
said that cannabis had helped the defendant deal with stress and pressure, but
said it had been a significant error of judgement. [@1] The advocate said that
the driving ban would impact his client’s family business, as they would now
have to employ someone to drive him to the vehicles he was going to work on.
[@1] Wade was also ordered to pay £125 prosecution costs, and agreed to pay both
amounts within seven days. [@1] The court heard that Wade, of Rowan Avenue, had
no previous convictions. [@1]
