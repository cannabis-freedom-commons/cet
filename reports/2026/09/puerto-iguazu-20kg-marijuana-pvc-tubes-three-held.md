Three men were detained in Puerto Iguazú after federal police found 20 kilograms
of marijuana hidden inside four PVC tubes. [@3] The detainees are two Paraguayan
citizens and one Argentine, who were left at the disposal of the federal courts
while proceedings continue. [@1] One of the detainees, a Paraguayan, had an
outstanding arrest warrant over a case under the national drug law. [@1] The
warrant had been issued by Federal Court No. 2 of Santiago del Estero, which
took over his judicial situation. [@1]

The operation was carried out by officers of the Federal Operational Unit in
Puerto Iguazú, part of the Federal Investigations Department of the Policía
Federal Argentina, supervised by the national Security Ministry. [@2] Officers
were on a preventive patrol on calle Hipólito Yrigoyen al 400 in Puerto Iguazú.
[@1] They saw a car carrying three men and two large bundles heading for a
parcel-dispatch company. [@1] The officers stopped the car and searched it with
the narcotics dog Umma, which marked the possible presence of drugs. [@1] The
search uncovered four completely sealed PVC tubes filled with chopped marijuana.
[@1]

Judge Marcelo Cardozo of the Federal Court of Iguazú and prosecutor Aníbal
Martínez of the decentralized prosecutor's office handled the case. [@1] The
court ordered the detention of the three occupants and the seizure of the 20
kilograms of marijuana, the vehicle and three mobile phones. [@1]
