Five men who supplied drugs in and around Pontefract were sentenced at Leeds
Crown Court. [@1] The sentencing hearing was held on Friday 25 September 2026.
[@1] Kyle Bates, 34, was jailed for nine years and three months. [@1] The court
heard that Kyle Bates was the head of the organised crime group. [@3] Mark
Bates, 38, was jailed for six years and nine months. [@1] Alan Tonks was jailed
for six years and nine months. [@3] Tonks had admitted possession of crack
cocaine, amphetamine, cannabis and ketamine with intent to supply, and being
concerned in the supply of cannabis. [@1] Shane Gray was jailed for five years.
[@1] Reece Britnell, 24, was given a six-month sentence. [@1]

Officers from Wakefield District began investigating after finding evidence of
drug dealing on a mobile phone linked to Kyle Bates in a car belonging to Tonks.
[@1] Officers executed search warrants at premises connected to the group in the
Pontefract area in March 2026. [@1] Five men have been sentenced after drugs and
41 boxes of stolen cornflakes were seized in raids across West Yorkshire. [@2]
Police found cannabis in shipping containers at West End Farm in Darrington.
[@3] The force said the cannabis involved was 26 kilograms with an estimated
street value of £118,000. [@1] Police valued all the drugs in the case at
£420,000, a total that also covered cocaine, crack cocaine, amphetamine and
ketamine. [@1] Court reporting in the Wakefield Express put the seizures at more
than 23 kilograms of cannabis, alongside 1.3 kilograms of cocaine and 9.5
kilograms of ketamine. [@3]

Kyle Bates admitted being concerned in the supply of cocaine, cannabis and
ketamine and possession of cocaine and cannabis with intent to supply. [@1]
Shane Gray admitted being concerned in the supply of cocaine, ketamine and
cannabis and possession of cannabis with intent to supply, among other offences.
[@1] The court heard that Kyle Bates sold kilogrammes of cannabis nationally, to
areas including Liverpool, Scotland and Cheshire. [@3] Detective Sergeant Karl
Heath said the force was committed to tackling those who profit from drug crime
in Pontefract. [@1]
