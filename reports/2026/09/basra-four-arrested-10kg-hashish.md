Iraqi anti-narcotics forces arrested four suspects and seized 10 kilograms of
hashish in Basra on Thursday 17 September 2026, the General Directorate of
Narcotics and Psychotropic Substances Affairs said. [@1] Hashish is a
concentrated cannabis resin. [@1]

Iraqi authorities seized 16.8 tonnes of illicit substances between 2023 and July
2026. [@1] Courts handed down 380 death sentences and 150 life terms in
trafficking cases in the same period and issued 190 international arrest
warrants. [@1] In the first half of 2026 alone, security agencies dismantled 378
networks, including 59 operating internationally, and confiscated more than
1,977 kilograms of drugs. [@1] The national figures cover all illicit substances
and drugs, not only cannabis. [@1]

A day earlier, Iraq's Central Committee for the Destruction of Narcotics
disposed of nearly one tonne of drugs, along with tens of thousands of
psychotropic pills and quantities of liquid substances. [@1]
