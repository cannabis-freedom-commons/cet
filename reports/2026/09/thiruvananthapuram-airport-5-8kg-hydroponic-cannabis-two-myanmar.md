Air Customs officials at Thiruvananthapuram International Airport arrested two
Myanmar nationals in connection with an attempted cannabis smuggling. [@1] The
men were identified as U U Khaing, 32, and Min Zaw Hein, 36. [@1] Customs
officials said the duo arrived at the airport from Singapore on a flight on
Tuesday. [@1] The two arrived at about 10:09 pm on 8 September on a flight from
Singapore. [@2]

The passengers' behaviour raised suspicion during arrival checks, following
which their luggage was subjected to detailed inspection, according to customs.
[@1] The cannabis was allegedly concealed in specially fabricated secret
compartments at the bottom of their check-in bags. [@1] The compartments were
not immediately visible during routine baggage checks, and the customs team
dismantled the bags to recover the contraband packed inside the cavities. [@1]

The seized substance weighed 5.8kg and was identified as hydroponic cannabis, a
high-potency variety cultivated artificially without soil using nutrient-rich
solutions. [@1] Customs officials estimated its value in the international
illicit drug market at around Rs 2.03 crore. [@1] A case has been registered
against the two accused under provisions of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic
Substances Act. [@1] Further investigation is under way. [@1] Customs officials
are examining the travel details and baggage movements of the accused as part of
the investigation. [@1]
