On 6 September 2026, agents of the Division Anti Extorsion y Asociaciones
Terroristas executed a house search in colonia La Garroba, Potrerillos, Cortes,
and detained two members of the Mara Salvatrucha MS-13. [@1] The detainees were
identified as alias La Barra, described as a Compa in charge of drug sale and
distribution as well as contract killing, with 2023 police antecedents for drug
trafficking and illegal weapon carrying, and alias Luce. [@1]

During the inspection, officers seized a .38-calibre revolver, a metal magazine
holding 8 5.56 mm cartridges and 20 7.62 x 39 mm cartridges. [@1] In the same
inspection, officers seized 940 wraps of suspected cocaine, 780 wraps of
suspected crack and 300 wraps of suspected marijuana, plus cash and two mobile
phones. [@1] Police valued the seized drugs at approximately half a million
lempiras. [@1]

The two suspects were referred for alleged illicit drug trafficking, illegal
carrying of a firearm and possession of prohibited-use ammunition. [@1]
