On 19 September 2026, specialists from Honduras's National Anti-Drug Police (DNPA), with other
national police units, inspected a minibus in Puerto Cortés, Cortés department, after surveillance
work. [@1,2,3,4]

They found 28 rectangular bales containing 1,300 pounds of suspected high-grade marijuana. [@1,2,3,4,5]
Police put the preliminary value above 10 million lempiras. [@1,2] The prosecutor's office put the load's market value at 19.8 million lempiras and said the minibus was heading for Guatemala. [@5]

Three men aged 29, 55 and 68 were detained on preliminary aggravated drug-trafficking charges and
handed to the competent authorities. [@1,2,3,5] A court also issued arrest warrants for three Guatemalan citizens linked to the same drug-trafficking case. [@5] Prosecutors named them as Óscar Eduardo Sosa Cruz, Arnold Javier Sosa Gómez and Rubén Gamboa Caballero, all sent to the El Progreso prison in Yoro department. [@5] The Special Prosecutor's Office against Organized Crime (FESCCO) in the northern region filed a charge sheet against the three Hondurans, and a judge ordered them held in judicial detention. [@5] Prosecutors from the Special Prosecutor's Office against
Organized Crime in San Pedro Sula supported the operation. [@1] A court in San Pedro Sula with national competence for organised crime, the environment and corruption ordered formal processing with pre-trial detention on a charge of aggravated drug trafficking. [@7] The three Guatemalans had not been arrested and were still being sought. [@6]

Police said early lines of inquiry pointed to Mara Salvatrucha (MS-13) and distribution in northern
Honduras, but that remained to be proved in court. [@1,2] Investigators continued to trace the
cargo's origin and any other participants. [@1,2]
