Officers intercepted several cases of illegal trafficking of drugs and potent
medicines in the Fergana and Namangan regions. [@1] The operations seized more
than 1.2 kilograms of hashish and hundreds of units of Tropicamide. [@1]

In Kokand, State Security Service officers together with internal-affairs and
customs officers searched the home of a previously convicted 44-year-old local
resident. [@1] In his basement they found 782 units of the potent drug
Tropicamide. [@1] Officers found 758 grams of hashish in another house. [@1]
There they also seized six knives bearing traces of narcotics and several
electronic scales. [@1]

Another operation took place in Namangan district. [@1] Officers stopped a
Lacetti driven by a resident of Altyaryk district on the Fergana-Namangan road.
[@1] Inside the car they found 465 grams of hashish. [@1]

Criminal cases were opened over all three episodes, and the investigation is
continuing. [@1]
