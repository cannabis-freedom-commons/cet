An armed clash between Honduran soldiers and suspected drug traffickers broke
out during an anti-narcotics operation in Limón municipality, Colón. [@1] The
clash took place in the Plan de Flores sector and around the village of Icoteas.
[@1] The suspects were in two pickups when they opened fire on the troops, who
returned fire. [@1] The suspects fled and abandoned the vehicles and other
items. [@5] No one was reported hurt during the exchange of fire. [@1]

The two pickups were taken to a military installation for inspection. [@1] The
vehicles were two white Toyota Hilux without plates, and one of them was
armoured. [@1] The inspection found about a pound (0.45 kg) of suspected
marijuana. [@2] The soldiers also seized 10 kg of suspected cocaine and two
kilos of coca paste, which are not counted in the cannabis figures. [@3]
Packages that may contain chemical precursors were found together with the
marijuana. [@1] Officers also seized three mobile phones, a Starlink satellite
antenna, 33 live 7.62 mm rounds, 100 gallons of Jet-A1 fuel and Colombian
identity documents. [@3]

The Xatruch joint task force, the 15th Special Forces Battalion and other units
stayed deployed in the mountainous area of Limón. [@4] No arrests had been
confirmed, and the army kept searching the area for the suspects. [@4]
