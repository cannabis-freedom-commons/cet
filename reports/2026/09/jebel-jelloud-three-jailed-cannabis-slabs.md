An investigating judge at the Tunis 2 Court of First Instance ordered three
young men, including two brothers, held in prison pending the case after they
were caught with more than ten slabs of cannabis they were preparing to sell in
Jebel Jelloud. [@1] The anti-drug sub-directorate of the El Gorjani judicial
police had received information that a young man from Jebel Jelloud was
suspected of involvement in drug dealing, so officers intensified inquiries into
his movements and placed him under surveillance. [@1] After a tip that the
suspect intended to sell a quantity of drugs, the officers set an ambush and
caught him in a car with two other young men who turned out to be brothers. [@1]

Searching the car, officers seized more than ten slabs of cannabis. [@1] During
questioning the detainees said they had intended to sell the drugs among young
people, and they were kept in custody while other individuals were added to the
wanted list. [@1]

Brought before the investigating judge at the Tunis 2 court, the three were
issued prison detention warrants while the case continues. [@1]
