Nine people suspected of belonging to the Los Popeyes organised crime group were
arrested in Vélez, Santander, in a joint operation by the Army and the Police.
[@1] The Army said the nine suspected members of the GDCO Los Popeyes were
captured by judicial order during eight search and seizure warrants in the
municipality. [@2] Those arrested included men known as Popeye, Rayo McQueen,
Chinche, el Negro, Neider, el Menor, Veneco, Eudomar and Murlon. [@2]

Authorities said the group was dedicated to local drug trafficking and sold
marijuana in parks, sports areas and peripheral sectors of the municipality.
[@1] The operation took place in the urban centre of Vélez under the Ayacucho
Plus campaign plan. [@1] Troops of the Fifth Brigade worked with the Police's
criminal investigation and intelligence units, with support from the Attorney
General's Office, and searched several properties under observation. [@1]
Officers seized doses of marijuana, seven mobile phones, cash and equipment used
to divide the drug into doses before sale. [@1] The cash was to be checked to
establish whether it came from local drug sales. [@1]

Colonel José Darío Rodríguez Gómez, commander of the Fifth Brigade, said the
group was organized common crime involved in the third link of the drug trade.
[@3] Authorities said sales in parks and sports grounds frequented by children
were a reason for prioritising the operation. [@3] The nine detainees were
placed at the disposal of the Attorney General's Office, which will analyse the
evidence before a judge decides on measures against them. [@3]
