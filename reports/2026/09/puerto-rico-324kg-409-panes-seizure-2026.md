Argentina's Naval Prefecture seized more than 324 kilograms of marijuana in an
operation on the bank of the Paraná River at Puerto Rico, Misiones, in September
2026. [@1] The operation began after the force's criminal intelligence unit in
Libertador General San Martín received information about a possible drug entry
from Paraguay and set up an observation post. [@2] Officers watched a rowing
boat cross from the Paraguayan bank with two people aboard and several bundles,
landing near Arroyo León at kilometre 1,738 of the Paraná. [@1]

At the landing point officers found a vehicle with its doors open holding three
bundles of pressed marijuana bricks. [@2] A search of the area uncovered more
drugs on a path leading to the riverbank and in a wooden boat. [@1] In total
officers counted 13 bundles containing 409 bricks of marijuana, weighing more
than 324 kilograms, with a market value the force put at more than 1,242 million
pesos. [@1]

The Federal Prosecutor's Office in Oberá, headed by Viviana Alejandra Vallejos,
ordered the seizure of the drugs, the vehicle and the boat used in the
operation. [@3]
