Four people were arrested in Kétou on 23 September 2026 while police
investigated suspected trafficking in psychotropic products. [@1] The operation
in the Obafèmi quarter was carried out on information supplied by Benin's
Republican Police. [@1]

Officers had targeted the home of a man presented as a presumed distributor.
[@1]

When the officers arrived, three people at the house reportedly tried to hide in
the ceiling of the building before being found. [@1]

The search recovered about 20 kilograms of a product related to chanvre indien,
the French term for cannabis, according to the Republican Police. [@1] Officers
also seized tablets known locally as Atinkinkouin, five scales, several litres
of a drink whose nature has yet to be determined, and cash. [@1] The money
recovered amounted to 5,188,625 CFA francs and 458,000 naira. [@1] The four
people arrested were placed in cells. [@1] Police described the operation as the
seizure of about 20 kg of presumed hemp, tablets, scales and more than 5 million
CFA francs. [@1]
