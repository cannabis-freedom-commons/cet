Jalisco police, working with the Defence Ministry (Sedena), found and destroyed
about 144,000 apparent marijuana plants on two properties in the municipality of
Tequila. [@3] During a surveillance patrol, officers from the regional command
and the Defence Ministry located the two plots in the communities of
Chiquilistán and El Salvador, where they detected plants with the
characteristics of marijuana. [@3] The crops had been sown among corn plants in
an attempt to hide them. [@1]

The two plots covered about 36,000 square metres (3.6 hectares), and the plants
reached up to 1.70 metres in height. [@4] Officers notified an agent of the
federal public prosecutor and asked it to take charge of the investigation; the
prosecutor ordered the plantations destroyed after representative samples were
taken and a photographic record was made. [@3]

The state security ministry said its preventive police maintain patrols across
Jalisco and joint operations with federal authorities to prevent and combat
crimes against health. [@3]

The marijuana-characteristic plants were spread among the corn fields,
apparently to camouflage the production. [@2]
