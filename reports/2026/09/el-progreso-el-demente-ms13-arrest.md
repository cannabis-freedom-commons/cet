Honduran National Police arrested a suspected MS-13 member known as "El Demente"
in El Progreso. [@1] Police described the suspect as holding the rank of
gatillero, a gunman role, within the criminal structure. [@1]

At the time of his detention, officers seized a pistol-type firearm, two
magazines and 11 unfired cartridges from him. [@1] Officers also seized 50 wraps
of suspected cocaine and 11 wraps of suspected marijuana from him. [@1]
