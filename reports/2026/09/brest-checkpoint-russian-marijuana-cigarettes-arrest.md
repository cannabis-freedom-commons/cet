Border guards at the Brest checkpoint detained a 58-year-old Russian citizen who
tried to bring marijuana cigarettes from Poland into Belarus. [@1] The State
Border Committee said the incident happened on 22 September. [@1] A service dog
found several jars holding cigarettes with an unknown substance in his
possession. [@1] The detained man admitted that the substance was marijuana.
[@1] A medical examination confirmed that he had used marijuana. [@1]

Officers also found a grinder, a blister pack with a tablet and a container of
hashish oil in his personal belongings. [@1] The Brest border guards said they
had stopped an attempt to bring drugs from Poland into Belarus. [@2] The man was
detained following the find. [@2] Two criminal cases were opened against him for
illegal drug trafficking and for illegally moving drugs across the border under
part 1 of Article 328 and part 1 of Article 328-1 of the Criminal Code. [@1]
