A 20-year-old resident of Tiraspol was detained in the village of Blizhny Khutor
on 25 September 2026. [@1] Operatives of the Oktyabrsky ROVD found two yellow
wraps of suspected hashish in the pocket of his vest. [@1] His mobile phone
contained photographs of terrain marked with tags and coordinates that were used
to prepare stash sites. [@1] The young man is now remanded in custody, and the
seized substances were sent for examination. [@1]

Within several days he had managed to place about 100 drug stashes. [@1] He
received the assignments from an online shop via Telegram. [@1] He admitted that
he had himself used hashish for six months and claimed that he had found the two
wraps and kept them for personal consumption. [@1]

Unlawful production, sale or forwarding of narcotic drugs carries up to 12
years' imprisonment with confiscation of property. [@1]
