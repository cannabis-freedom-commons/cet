Carabineros of the OS7 Limarí anti-drug unit carried out simultaneous entries
into properties in Ovalle and Sotaquí in Chile's Coquimbo Region and arrested
seven people — four Bolivians, three of them women, and three Chileans — with
GOPE specialists supporting the operation. [@1] Carabineros said the
investigation dismantled an international organization involved in drug
trafficking. [@1] The organization brought women into Chile as tourists through
official border crossings. [@1] Those women carried drugs in swallowed ovoids,
mainly pasta base and cocaine hydrochloride, to Limarí province. [@1] Once in
Ovalle the substances were bulked up to increase their volume, then distributed
in Limarí and Choapa provinces. [@1] Among the detainees were a 23-year-old
Chilean with an outstanding warrant for small-scale drug trafficking issued in
April by the San Felipe Guarantee Court, and a 33-year-old Chilean woman with
earlier drug-trafficking records but no active cases. [@1] Three of the Bolivian
citizens were cautioned under Article 26 of the Criminal Procedure Code, while
the others were placed at the disposal of the justice system on the Ministerio
Público's instructions. [@1]

Carabineros said it seized almost nine kilograms of drugs in total — elaborated
marijuana, cocaine and pasta base — along with about 150 grams of creatine. [@1]
Captain Marcelo González said the operation produced seven detainees and an
important seizure that included marijuana, cocaine and pasta base, along with
vehicles and domestic and foreign cash. [@2] Police also seized more than 3.5
million pesos in Chilean and foreign currency, two vehicles, jewellery,
appliances, two pistols not registered nationally and 12 rounds of ammunition.
[@1]
