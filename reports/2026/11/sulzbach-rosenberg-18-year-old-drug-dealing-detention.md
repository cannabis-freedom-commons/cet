An 18-year-old man was taken into pre-trial detention on suspicion of drug
dealing after youth-protection checks in Sulzbach-Rosenberg. [@1] Police stopped
him at the town's railway station on the evening of Thursday 19 November 2026.
[@2] He had earlier come to police attention in connection with a suspected drug
deal. [@1] Officers found one cocaine dose weighing about 0.4 grams on him. [@1]

The public prosecutor's office ordered a search of the 18-year-old's flat. [@2]
In the flat officers found further cocaine doses and a small amount of a
marijuana-tobacco mixture, which they seized. [@1]

The 18-year-old was provisionally arrested and brought before an investigating
judge the following day. [@2] The judge ordered pre-trial detention. [@2] The
Amberg criminal police inspectorate is leading the further investigation into
the drug-dealing allegation. [@1]

Police used the checks to repeat warnings about the dangers of drug and nicotine
use, especially among young people. [@2] They asked parents to talk to their
children about the health risks and about responsible use of such products. [@2]
