During drug-combat checks in Yozgat city centre, officers stopped the car of a
driver identified only by the initials A.İ.Ö. after deeming his situation
suspicious, and then searched the car. [@1] The suspect was remanded and sent to
prison. [@1] The published account identifies the driver only by initials and
reports no other detained person in this stop. [@1]

In the boot of the car, officers seized 5 kilos 600 grams of esrar. [@1] Esrar
is the everyday Turkish term for cannabis. [@1] The 5 kilos 600 grams equal the
5.6 kilograms of cannabis recorded for this event. [@1] The seizure came from
the boot of that single stopped car, with no home search or second location
reported. [@1]

The teams carrying out the checks belonged to the Asayis public-order branch of
the Yozgat police directorate. [@1] Those checks were aimed at combating drugs
in the city centre. [@1] Police presented the boot discovery as the fruit of
their attention during the search. [@1]
