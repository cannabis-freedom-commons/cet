On the morning of 17 November 2026, Mexico's National Guard carried out a
security operation in the Sábalo Country Club area of Mazatlán, Sinaloa, and
secured a RAM cargo pickup truck that was allegedly transporting drugs. [@1] The
stop took place on Avenida Camarón Sábalo outside the Camino al Mar hotel while
federal officers were patrolling the tourist district. [@1] Officers stopped a
suspicious white RAM pickup to inspect it after noticing it during their patrol.
[@1]

Inside the truck they found several packages whose contents were preliminarily
believed to be marijuana, which led them to secure the vehicle and cordon off
the area. [@1] The secured items were a white RAM cargo pickup and several
suspicious packages that allegedly contained marijuana. [@1] Authorities still
had to analyse the substance to confirm its type and amount and to establish the
circumstances in which it was being carried. [@1]

Two people were reported to have been detained during the operation, but their
identity and legal status had not been officially confirmed. [@1] The truck and
the evidence were handed to the relevant authorities, and the people reportedly
detained were taken away for investigations into any responsibility. [@1]
