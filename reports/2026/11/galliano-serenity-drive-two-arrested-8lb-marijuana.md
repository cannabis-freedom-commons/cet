Lafourche Parish Sheriff Craig Webre announced two people had been arrested as
part of a drug investigation. [@1] Dain Burns, 41, of Galliano and Starr
Cantrelle, 42, of Larose were arrested on Tuesday. [@1] Narcotics agents had
been investigating Burns and Cantrelle for drug distribution in the South
Lafourche area. [@1] Agents obtained arrest warrants and a search warrant for
Burns’ residence on Serenity Drive in Galliano. [@1] On November 24, 2026,
agents located Burns driving in the Cut Off area and conducted a traffic stop.
[@1] Burns was taken into custody and found to have marijuana and
methamphetamine inside his vehicle. [@1] Assisted by patrol deputies, agents
went to Burns’ residence, where Cantrelle was located and taken into custody.
[@1]

Agents searched the home and found multiple types of narcotics and currency
suspected to be linked to drug activity. [@1] In all, agents seized over eight
pounds of marijuana (about 3.63 kilograms) alongside suspected methamphetamine,
cocaine, psilocybin mushrooms, THC, Adderall, Xanax, Valium, Suboxone,
hydrocodone and gabapentin. [@1] Agents also seized more than $5,000 in cash and
digital currency held in a bank account and Cash App. [@1]

Burns was booked into the Lafourche Parish Correctional Complex in Thibodaux on
a warrant for two counts of distribution of marijuana. [@1] He was additionally
booked with possession with intent to distribute methamphetamine, cocaine,
marijuana, psilocybin, Adderall, Suboxone and Xanax, as well as possession of
Valium, a Schedule II drug, a legend drug without prescription and drug
paraphernalia. [@1] Bail for Burns was set at $327,000. [@1] Cantrelle was
booked on a warrant for principal to distribution of marijuana. [@1] She was
also charged with possession with intent to distribute marijuana and psilocybin,
as well as possession of methamphetamine, drug paraphernalia and a legend drug
without a prescription. [@1] Her bail amount had not yet been set. [@1]
