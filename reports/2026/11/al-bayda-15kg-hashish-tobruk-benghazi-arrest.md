Libya's General Department of Security Operations, which belongs to the
government assigned by the House of Representatives, announced the seizure of
about 15 kilograms of hashish in the city of Al-Bayda. [@1] The seizure in
Al-Bayda was made on 16 November 2026. [@1] The drugs were found with two men
from the city of Tobruk who were on their way to deliver the shipment to
Benghazi, the department said. [@1] It said the operation was carried out in
coordination with a branch of the Anti-Narcotics Apparatus, after information
and investigations into a criminal group suspected of moving narcotics and
trading in them from Tobruk towards cities in western Libya. [@1] The department
announced the seizure in a statement on its Facebook page on Monday. [@1]

Investigations established that two men suspected of belonging to the group were
carrying the drug shipment towards Benghazi. [@1] A technical fault in the two
men's vehicle forced them to spend the night in Al-Bayda, which allowed the
security services to locate them, raid them and seize the shipment. [@2] The
department said legal procedures were continuing before the two suspects and the
seized material were referred to the specialised prosecution for anti-narcotics
crimes, to complete the investigations. [@1]
