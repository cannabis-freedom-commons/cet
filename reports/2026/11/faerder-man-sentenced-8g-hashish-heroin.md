Vestfold District Court sentenced a man in his mid-40s to three years and four
months in prison for rape, drug possession and repeated breaches of a
restraining order. [@1] The assault happened in a flat in Færder one night in
August 2024. [@1] Both the woman and the accused were intoxicated, and she had
fallen asleep on a sofa before waking to find the man having intercourse with
her. [@1] He continued the assault and tried to take her into the bedroom, but
another woman who was present intervened and got them out of the flat. [@1]

In addition to the rape, the court convicted him on two counts of drug
possession covering more than 23 grams of heroin and eight grams of hashish,
with a digital scale and cash also seized. [@1] He was also convicted of
repeatedly breaching the restraining order imposed to protect the woman after
seeking her out and threatening her on an open street in central Tønsberg. [@1]
The court set the combined sentence at three years and four months after a
three-month reduction for the time the case had taken. [@1] He was also given a
two-year contact ban against the woman and ordered to pay her 273,098 kroner in
redress. [@1]

The man has ten previous convictions, including for grievous violence, robbery
and drug offences. [@1] His defence lawyer said the man maintains he is innocent
and has decided to appeal the judgment. [@1]
