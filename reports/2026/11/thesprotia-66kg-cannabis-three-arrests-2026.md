Three foreign nationals were arrested over the cannabis load in the Thesprotia
regional unit on 25 November 2026. [@1] One man drove the car carrying the drugs
while the other two went ahead as lookouts, telling him about possible police
checks on the route. [@1] The operation was run at midday by the Igoumenitsa
drug enforcement and investigation sub-directorate together with the Second
Border Guard Department of Filiates. [@2]

Inside the first car officers found 60 packages of raw cannabis weighing 66 kg
and 742 g. [@3] Police seized the two cars used in the operation. [@1] Officers
also seized 1,130 euros in cash and four mobile phones. [@1] The police dogs
Ikarus and Jerry took part in the searches. [@1]

A felony case file was opened against the three and they were taken to the
Thesprotia first-instance prosecutor's office. [@1] The force said the operation
prevented the load from being moved on. [@4]
