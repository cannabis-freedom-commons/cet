Surat railway police and Railway Protection Force staff found 17.115 kg of ganja
in unclaimed bags aboard the Puri–Ahmedabad Express on 14 November. [@1] The
check took place after train number 12843 passed Vyara railway station. [@1]

The cannabis was in a brown trolley bag and a black backpack beneath seats 57 to
62 of reserved coach B/6. [@1] Police valued the seized ganja at ₹855,750 and
registered a case under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act. [@1]
Police were searching for an unidentified person they said had left the bags
behind and fled. [@1]
