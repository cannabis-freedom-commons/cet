Agents of the National Security Centre in Kalaat al-Andalus detained a person
found inside a stable in the Douar Bir al-Arayes area at the beginning of the
week. [@1] The operation produced a quantity of cannabis, the plant referred to
as Indian hemp and locally called zatla. [@1]

The material seized included a slab of the drug, an electronic scale and sharp
implements suspected of being used to cut the drugs, together with sheets of
aluminium foil used for wrapping. [@1] A significant sum of money was also
recovered in the operation. [@1] The same operation also yielded a marble statue
that the security agents suspect has archaeological value. [@1] The marble
statue was awaiting inspection by the competent services, which were to
establish its nature and value. [@1]

The security operation formed part of wider security efforts aimed at combating
drug trafficking and countering various criminal activities. [@1]
