Buniyadganj police in Gaya stopped and arrested a motorcycle rider during
vehicle checking near Shadipur on the Manpur–Khijrasaray main road. [@1,2] The rider turned his motorcycle around and tried to flee when he saw the police team, and officers caught him with the motorcycle with help from an armed force. [@2]
Officers found four kilograms of ganja in the bag he was carrying. [@1] The
arrested man was identified as Purushottam Sharma of Kewla village in the
Hulasganj police-station area of Jehanabad district. [@1,2] Police also seized his
motorcycle and mobile phone. [@1,2]

Station head Dr Narendra Prasad said Sharma admitted during questioning that he
was linked to ganja trafficking and that several earlier cases were registered
against him. [@1] Daroga Deepak Kumar said Buniyadganj police were running a
vehicle-check drive near Shadipur when the rider acted suspiciously, so officers
stopped him and searched the bag on his back, where they found the ganja. [@1] The check was part of a special drive ordered by the Gaya senior superintendent of police against the buying, selling and storage of drugs. [@2] Questioned about the ganja, the arrested man gave no satisfactory answer and produced no valid documents for it. [@2]
SI Deepak Kumar said a first information report was registered on the basis of a
written application and further action was under way. [@1] The case was registered as Buniyadganj police station case number 345/26, dated 19 November 2026, and police were raiding possible locations to arrest other people involved in the trade. [@2]
