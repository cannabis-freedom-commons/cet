Buniyadganj police in Gaya stopped and arrested a motorcycle rider during
vehicle checking near Shadipur on the Manpur–Khijrasaray main road. [@1]
Officers found four kilograms of ganja in the bag he was carrying. [@1] The
arrested man was identified as Purushottam Sharma of Kewla village in the
Hulasganj police-station area of Jehanabad district. [@1] Police also seized his
motorcycle and mobile phone. [@1]

Station head Dr Narendra Prasad said Sharma admitted during questioning that he
was linked to ganja trafficking and that several earlier cases were registered
against him. [@1] Daroga Deepak Kumar said Buniyadganj police were running a
vehicle-check drive near Shadipur when the rider acted suspiciously, so officers
stopped him and searched the bag on his back, where they found the ganja. [@1]
SI Deepak Kumar said a first information report was registered on the basis of a
written application and further action was under way. [@1]
