Losice criminal investigators detained three men for drug possession during a
four-day operation and also detained a man wanted to serve a prison sentence.
[@2] On 12 November in Losice officers detained two men found in a basement,
establishing that one of them was wanted by a court to serve three months in
prison. [@1] Police said all the actions were the result of operative work by
the Losice criminal investigators. [@1]

In the 12 November search of a basement and a flat, officers seized more than 35
grams of mixed amphetamine, marijuana and mephedrone. [@2] On 14 November
officers searched the residence of a 56-year-old Losice resident and found more
than 5 grams of mephedrone in the kitchen. [@1] On 15 November in Zawady
officers checked a Volkswagen, recovered a packet thrown from its window
containing more than 51 grams of mephedrone, and detained the man who admitted
possessing it. [@1] Across the four days officers seized a total of more than 90
grams of mixed amphetamine, marijuana and mephedrone. [@1]

After procedural steps the 56-year-old was released by the prosecutor's
decision. [@2] The man detained in Zawady was placed in the police detention
room, while the other men in the car were released after the necessary steps.
[@2]
