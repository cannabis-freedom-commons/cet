Seoul Main Customs arrested 26-year-olds A and B, who oversaw the crime, for
allegedly bringing a total of 2.5 kg of cannabis from the United States by air
express cargo in July, for violating the Narcotics Control Act. [@1,2,5,7,8,11,12,13,15,16] Seoul Main Customs said it had arrested the two and transferred them to prosecutors on suspicion of violating the Narcotics Control Act. [@9,10] B received
instructions via Telegram from an unidentified upper level, passed them to A and
directed the crime overall. [@1,3,4,5,7,8,10,11,14,16] The pair took part after being offered about 2
million won in virtual assets such as Bitcoin as illegal high-paying part-time
work, dividing roles between them. [@1,2,4,5,6,7,8,10,11,16] Investigators found the two men are friends. [@10,12]

The cannabis was hidden inside toys and board games and disguised as normal
goods to evade X-ray inspection. [@1,3,4,5,6,7,8,10,11,12,13,14,15,16] At the usual 0.5 grams per dose, the customs service said the haul was enough for 5,000 people to use at once. [@4,8,10,14] After analysing recipient information,
clearance records, X-ray images and past delivery history, customs first
identified A as having smuggled cannabis three times by the same method from
September 2025 to January 2026. [@1,2,4,6,7,8,11,12,13,16] The first two parcels passed X-ray screening, but the last was detected because the declaration did not match the X-ray image. [@4,8,9,10] Customs worked with the post office to
monitor deliveries in real time through controlled delivery and, despite a
mid-route change of delivery address to evade investigators, tracked the
suspects and arrested them at the moment the goods were directly received. [@1,2,4,5,6,7,8,10,11,12,13,16]
To avoid tracking, A alternately used the left and right units of an
acquaintance's apartment in Gangwon-do as parcel addresses. [@1,3,4,7,8,9,14,16] From the third
crime, when the drug weight increased, he used an empty house near his workplace
as the receiving address and intended to distribute the cannabis by burying it
in remote hills as instructed via Telegram. [@1,4,7,8,11,16]

Immediately after arrest A tried to destroy evidence by factory-resetting his
smartphone and strongly denied the charges, but a home search recovered a
property contract linked to the crime location and a soil-covered transplanting
trowel hidden in the bedroom. [@1,2,11] Customs continued digital forensics and
persistent tracking and also caught accomplice B, who had directed the crime
from behind. [@1,2,5,11,16] Restoring A's phone revealed the address of an empty house in
Seoul where drugs were hidden, but A avoided detention once at the court's
warrant hearing by claiming he had only received the delivery on another's
behalf and shifting blame to the accomplice. [@1] The team obtained an arrest
warrant for B, located him through tracking and stakeout and arrested him
urgently, and forensics on B's phone proved the trowel found at A's home was
used to bury cannabis and revealed their joint Telegram chain from smuggling to
receipt to transport to distribution. [@1] Yoon Cheong-woon, head of Seoul Customs' first investigation bureau, warned that more young people were being drawn into drug crimes by the lure of high-paying part-time work. [@4,8,9,16]
