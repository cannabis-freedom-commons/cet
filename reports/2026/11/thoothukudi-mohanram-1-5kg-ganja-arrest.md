Thoothukudi police in Tamil Nadu arrested a 23-year-old youth after finding that
he was carrying 1.5 kg of ganja (cannabis) in a bag. [@1] The arrest followed a
patrol by Thoothukudi SIPCOT police inspector Dhanasekaran, assistant inspector
Murugan and other officers in the area. [@1] During that patrol the officers
noticed a youth standing in a suspicious manner in the Madhavan Nagar area of
Sorispuram, Thoothukudi, and took him in for questioning. [@1] He was identified
as Mohanram, 23, son of Paramasivam, of 5th Street, Madhavan Nagar. [@1] The bag
in his possession was found to hold 1.5 kg of ganja. [@1]

Officers seized the ganja, registered a case against him and then arrested him.
[@1] Police said they were also searching for another person connected to the
case. [@1]
