Officers of the Prevention Department of the Ostrów Wielkopolski county police
command detained a couple wanted by the District Court in Ostrów Wielkopolski.
[@1] A prevention patrol had been told that a wanted person might be staying at
one address. [@1] When the officers entered the flat they saw a 40-year-old
woman trying to hide behind a sofa. [@1] After checking her identity they
confirmed she was wanted by the District Court in Ostrów Wielkopolski to serve a
prison sentence, Deputy Commissioner Ewa Golińska-Jurasz, spokeswoman for the
county police command, said. [@1]

The 29-year-old owner of the flat was also there, and officers established that
he too was wanted by the Ostrów court. [@1] His nervous behaviour raised further
suspicion because he tried to cover a chest of drawers in his room. [@1]
Officers decided to check the furniture and found a plastic bag of dried plant
material in one drawer. [@1] Preliminary tests showed that the dried material
was marijuana. [@1] Intoxicants were also found on the 40-year-old woman, and
preliminary tests confirmed that the dried material secured from her was
marijuana as well. [@1]

Both the woman and the man were detained. [@1] After a night spent in police
custody both were taken to a remand prison. [@1]
