A 56-year-old man was arrested and charged over the find and is due to appear in
court on a later date. [@1] Police Scotland executed a warrant at a property on
Netherplace Road around 3pm on Monday, 16 November 2026. [@2]

Police seized 20kg of herbal cannabis and 1.5kg of cocaine at the property. [@2]
Police Scotland estimated the street value of the recovery at £265,000. [@2]
Police said the seizure formed part of ongoing efforts to disrupt serious and
organised crime and the supply of illegal drugs. [@3]

Detective Superintendent Gary Ferguson said the recovery sent a strong message
that the sale and supply of illegal drugs would not be tolerated. [@1] He said
it highlighted Police Scotland's commitment to the Serious Organised Crime
Taskforce and its national strategy. [@1] Police asked anyone with information
about drug activity to contact them on 101 or Crimestoppers, where anonymity can
be maintained. [@1]

Glasgow Live reported the property was on Netherplace Road near Silverburn, in
the south of Glasgow. [@4]
