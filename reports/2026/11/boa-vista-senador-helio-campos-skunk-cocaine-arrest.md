A 28-year-old man was arrested in Boa Vista on suspicion of drug trafficking
after fleeing a Military Police approach and driving at high speed through the
Pintolândia neighbourhood in the west zone on Saturday the 14th, with packets of
cocaine and skunk seized. [@1,2] The patrol had noticed a black car with dark
windows moving at a speed apparently incompatible with the road, and the driver
accelerated and fled on seeing the patrol car. [@1] The patrol was from the 2nd Battalion of the Military Police and the car it spotted was a black Toyota Corolla. [@2] Officers pursued the car and
gave stop orders with sound and light signals which the driver disobeyed. [@1,2]
The stop took place at the crossing of Rosa Oliveira de Araújo and Vereador
Waldemar Gomes streets with support from another team. [@1,2] The pursuit began on Rua Closvaldo Paes Carolino. [@2]

A personal search found nothing illicit, but officers found four packets under
the driver seat in the car, two with skunk and two with cocaine. [@1,2]
Questioned, the man said there were more drugs at the house where he lived in
the Senador Hélio Campos neighbourhood, authorised the officers to enter and
accompanied them there. [@1,2] At the house he pointed to a room at the back where
he lived and showed material hidden under the kitchen sink, where police found a
medium bar and three packets of cocaine plus a tablet and nine packets of skunk.
[@1,2] In total across the car and the house, the Military Police seized one
medium bar and five packets of cocaine plus one tablet bearing the Colombian
flag and 11 packets of skunk. [@1] Officers also seized R$719 in cash, a
precision scale and two mobile phones. [@1,2]

A Military Police canine team searched the residence with a sniffer dog but
found no further illicit material. [@1,2] The man, the car and the seized objects
were taken to the police station, and the case was registered as drug
trafficking and disobedience. [@1]
