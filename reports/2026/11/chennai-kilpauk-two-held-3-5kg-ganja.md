Kilpauk police arrested two men accused of selling ganja near the Kilpauk
Medical College Hospital bus stop and recovered 3.5 kg of ganja from them. [@1]
The men were identified as Raja, 38, of Manali New Town, and Mohammed Javid, 23,
of TP Chathiram. [@1]

Police announced the Kilpauk arrests together with a second operation near
Porur, putting the two at three arrests and 6 kg of ganja. [@1] The city police
said the seizures followed anti-narcotics operations within the Avadi Police
Commissionerate. [@1] Police said surveillance had been stepped up after reports
of drug sales in areas with colleges, IT companies and residential accommodation
for young people. [@1]
