The Department of Narcotics Control in Barguna arrested a man it described as a
drug smuggler and seized 2 kilograms of ganja in a raid in the town. [@1,2] The
raid took place between 12:00 and 12:45 pm on Friday 20 November 2026 on Sadar
Road, in ward 7 of Barguna municipality under Barguna Sadar police station. [@1,2]
The department named the arrested man as Md. Safiqul Islam Nazmul, 27, a
resident of Betagi municipality in Betagi upazila of Barguna district. [@1,2]

Acting on a tip-off, officers stopped him on the paved road on the north side of
Mauban Hotel and Restaurant, in front of the river port on the west side of the
town area. [@1,2] The two kilograms of ganja were wrapped in a blue polythene
packet sealed with adhesive tape inside a market bag. [@1,2] Officers also seized
two mobile phones said to have been used in the drug trade. [@1,2] The department
put the seized ganja's estimated market value at about 100,000 taka. [@1,2]

A case was filed at Barguna Sadar police station against the arrested man under
the relevant sections of the Narcotics Control Act 2018, as amended in 2026.
[@1,2] Md. Hamimur Rashid, the department's deputy director in Barguna, said the
campaign against illegal drugs would continue. [@1,2]
