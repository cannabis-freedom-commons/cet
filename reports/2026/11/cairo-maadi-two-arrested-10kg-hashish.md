Cairo narcotics police arrested two unemployed men in the Maadi district of
Cairo before they could distribute a quantity of hashish. [@1] Major General
Mohamed Youssef, assistant minister for the Cairo security sector, was notified
and referred the two men to the prosecution for investigation. [@1]

The operation began after Lieutenant General Alaa Boshindi, director of Cairo's
general investigation department, received a notification from Lieutenant
General Wa'il al-Shamouti, director of narcotics investigations, about
information and surveillance indicating that two suspects were distributing
drugs in Maadi. [@1] Checkpoints were set up under the supervision of the
department's general director and chaired by the investigation director, with
officers led by Lieutenant General Wa'il Arfan, Colonel Hassan al-Banna and
Colonel Ahmed Samir. [@1] The two men were found in Maadi with 10 kilograms of
hashish and a sum of money. [@1]

Officers took the necessary legal measures after the seizure. [@1]
