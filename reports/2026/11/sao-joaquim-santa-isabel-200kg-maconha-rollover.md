Two men were arrested for drug trafficking on the afternoon of Sunday 22
November 2026 after a car rolled over and they abandoned a vehicle loaded with
drugs in São Joaquim, Santa Catarina. [@1] The operation, conducted by crews of
the 6th Military Police Battalion at about 17:40, seized more than 200 kg of
cannabis in total. [@1] Police came across the overturned car abandoned at the
roadside with no one inside while attending a traffic incident. [@1]

A search of the car found between 10 and 15 kg of cannabis, prompting a search
of the area for those responsible for the load. [@1] In the locality of Santa
Isabel, officers found and stopped the two men who had fled the crash site. [@1]
With the pair, police found the rest of the shipment, bringing the total seized
to more than 200 kg. [@1] The cannabis was found in packages hidden beneath the
car's floor covering and inside its doors, with one account putting the total at
approximately 145 kg. [@2] A police dog unit made the discovery on the SC-114 in
Santa Isabel during Operação Protetor de Divisas. [@2] Police said the drug was
to be collected in Paraguay and taken to Navegantes on the Santa Catarina coast.
[@2]

The two men were arrested in flagrante and taken with all the material to the
Civil Police station. [@1] The two suspects were taken to the Federal Police on
suspicion of international drug trafficking and association for trafficking,
according to the other account. [@2] Alongside the cannabis, police seized the
car used to carry it and two mobile phones. [@2]
