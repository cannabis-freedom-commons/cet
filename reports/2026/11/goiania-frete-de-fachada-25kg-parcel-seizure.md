Goiás Civil Police's anti-narcotics unit Denarc launched Operação Frete de
Fachada on Wednesday 18 November 2026 to investigate people involved in sending
drugs from Cuiabá, in Mato Grosso, to Goiás. [@2] Officers worked with the
Receita Federal, Brazil's federal revenue and customs service, at a freight
company in Goiânia. [@2] With sniffer dogs, teams found a box holding about 25
kilograms of marijuana, packed for distribution. [@2] The parcel was declared as
a household appliance. [@2]

Police said the collection had already been arranged, with a ride-hailing driver
hired to take the supposed appliance to the car park of a shopping centre in
Goiânia. [@1] The driver went to the freight company, but the inspection stopped
the parcel being collected. [@1] Investigators identified two suspects in the
scheme. [@1]

One is accused of hiring the driver and receiving about 1,500 reais for the
intermediation. [@1] The other is accused of buying the drug and distributing it
in Guapó, in Goiás. [@1] The police did not name the two suspects and reported
no arrests. [@1] The operation is part of Operação Narke 7, coordinated by the
Ministry of Justice and Public Safety. [@3]
