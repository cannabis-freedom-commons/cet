On Wednesday 18 November 2026, officers from across South Yorkshire Police took
part in a day of action in Barnsley targeting serious acquisitive crime,
antisocial behaviour, environmental offences and the protection of vulnerable
people. [@1] The operation began with early morning warrants at multiple
addresses. [@1]

In those warrants officers found more than £84,000 worth of Class A and Class B
drugs and several weapons. [@1] Police published a photograph of a suitcase
holding ziplock bags of marijuana from the operation. [@1] Police said officers
acted on intelligence from local communities during the day. [@1]

Inspector Michael Sheedy, who led the operation, said several arrests had been
made for offences related to serious acquisitive crime and that officers had
seized high value Class A and B drugs and weapons from the community. [@1]

Specialist teams, including the Tactical Support Group, the Fortify team, the
Roads Policing Group, the Roads Proactive Policing Team and neighbourhood
officers, took part. [@1]
