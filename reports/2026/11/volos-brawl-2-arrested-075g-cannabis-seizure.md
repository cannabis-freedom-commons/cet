Police took a 19-year-old and a minor into custody, identified an 18-year-old,
and seized cannabis and a cartridge in searches. [@1] Investigators identified
three people, a 19-year-old, a minor and an 18-year-old, with unknown
accomplices also included in the case file. [@2] Officers of the Volos
crime-investigation department, assisted by OPKE, arrested the 19-year-old and
the minor on the afternoon of Sunday 22 November. [@2]

Searches in the homes of those involved found and seized 0.75 grams of processed
cannabis and one cartridge. [@2] The case file covers dangerous bodily harm and
breaches of weapons and drugs legislation, among other offences, and the
investigation is continuing. [@2]

Police made the two arrests over a violent youth brawl in Volos in which two
youths aged 17 to 20 were taken injured to hospital. [@2] The brawl broke out in
the early hours of Sunday 22 November in a park near Volos hospital. [@2] A
group of about 50 youths allegedly carrying planks and clubs attacked a group of
about 10 people there. [@2] Two people were taken to Volos hospital, with one
youth reported to have lost consciousness. [@2]
