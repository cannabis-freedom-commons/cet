A Castres court sentenced a couple to six months in prison without suspension on
Monday for supplying a drug point from an apartment in the Aillot neighbourhood.
[@1] For six months the couple used a tenant's flat on rue des Lilas to supply
about 30 customers a day. [@2] The pair admitted the facts before the
magistrates. [@2] Customers bought cannabis and cocaine at the point. [@1] The
case began with Jennifer, a homeless woman in her thirties who was due to
celebrate her 34th birthday in prison. [@2] Already addicted, she had sold doses
to fund her own consumption before she met the older tenant of the rue des Lilas
flat. [@2]

BAC officers who kept the flat under observation for a morning arrested three
people: two for trafficking and the tenant as the nourrice, the person who hosts
the dealing in her home. [@2] Prosecutor Frédérique Suquet estimated daily
receipts at 1,000 euros, or 180,000 euros over the six months. [@2]

The couple's six-month custodial sentences came with obligations, and breaching
them could add another year in prison. [@2] Only the tenant left free, with a
ten-month term hanging over her for two years and other obligations. [@2] The
sentence was lower than the prosecution had requested. [@2] The couple's lawyer,
Jean-Antoine Escande, said the network and the consumers were the two absentees
from the trial. [@2]
