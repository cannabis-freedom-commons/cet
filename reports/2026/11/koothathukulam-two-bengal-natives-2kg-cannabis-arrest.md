Koothathukulam police arrested two men from West Bengal with two kilograms of
cannabis from a rented house near the municipal stadium. [@1] Those arrested are
Sajidul Islam Mundel and Sanarul S.K., both from Murshidabad, West Bengal. [@1]
The raid was carried out based on a tip-off received by the Aluva Rural SP. [@1]
The raid was led by Puthenkurish DYSP Joy Mathew N.M., CIs Aby M.P. and Tolson
P.J., and Koothathukulam SHO R. Princy. [@1] The suspects were to be produced in
court after a medical examination. [@1]
