Police in Tiruchirappalli surrounded and arrested two women from the Prattiyur
area who they said were selling ganja, the local term for cannabis. [@1] The
arrests were made by the Prohibition Enforcement Wing, a unit of Trichy city
police that monitors drug sales across the city. [@1]

Police said a secret tip had told them that ganja was being sold in the
Prattiyur area. [@1] Acting on the information, officers went to the spot and
carried out an intensive check. [@1] A search of the two women found a total of
1 kilogram 800 grams (1.8 kg) of ganja, which the officers seized. [@1] Police
said they were still searching for another woman believed to be connected to the
case. [@1]

The police said continuous monitoring was under way in Trichy to stop drug sales
aimed at young people and students. [@1] The same district-wide operation
included separate tobacco-product and gravel cases, which are not counted here.
