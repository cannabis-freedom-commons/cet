Seranga police in Gajapati district, Odisha, seized over 500 kg of ganja from a
pickup vehicle in the early hours of 18 November 2026. [@1] The pickup's driver
and the rider of an accompanying motorcycle abandoned both vehicles and fled
into a nearby forest when officers moved in. [@1]

Officers had acted on information that ganja was being transported from Putasing
towards Seranga and laid an ambush near Ragidiputa. [@1] At about 6.15 am they
signalled a white pickup van and the motorcycle to stop. [@1] A search found the
ganja packed in 22 cans in the pickup's loading compartment, and the net weight
of the consignment was 507.9 kg. [@1]

The seizure was made in the presence of the additional tehsildar for Gumma, a
local revenue official, and police also seized the pickup and the motorcycle.
[@1] Seranga police registered a case and said they were investigating to
identify and apprehend those involved in the ganja smuggling network. [@1] The
Gajapati district police chief said in a post that the Seranga team had seized
about 508 kg of ganja along with a Bolero and a Hero motorcycle while
patrolling. [@2]
