Drug-control officers of the Komi Interior Ministry caught a 44-year-old local
resident of Syktyvkar in drug trafficking, detaining him with power support from
SOBR Zarya of the Komi Rosgvardia directorate. [@1] Operatives said he had taken
work for a shadow online shop and ordered wholesale batches that he planned to
divide into small doses and sell in the regional capital. [@1]

Officers searching his home and his garage found and seized containers and
packets of drugs. [@1] Experts identified the seized material as hashish
weighing over 193 grams and amphetamine weighing at least 143 grams, and
officers also seized cutting boards and blades bearing brown and green residue
along with packaging material. [@1] Officers also established that he had sold 4
grams of hashish to another local resident. [@1]

An investigator opened criminal cases against him under drug-sale provisions of
the Criminal Code, which carry up to 20 years' imprisonment, and he was remanded
in custody. [@1] Police said they were continuing operational and investigative
measures to establish all the circumstances of the offence. [@1]
