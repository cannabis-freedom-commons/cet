The defendant, a man from Morlanwelz born in 2001, served a long prison sentence
in 2023, 2024 and 2025 and was caught consuming drugs in his cell at both Ittre
and Nivelles prisons. [@1] Having since been released on parole, he appeared
again in the dock of the criminal court over these acts, with the risk of a
return to prison. [@1] His lawyer said he contested nothing and that there were
six charges at Nivelles prison and two at Ittre. [@1]

He told the court a month earlier that he still smoked and was trying to cut
down sharply. [@1] He had regained his freedom conditionally in July and has a
child. [@1] His lawyer said he had consumed drugs since the age of 15 and
entered prison at 18 after a childhood of institutional placements and conflict
with his father. [@1] He spent several years in Nivelles, where detention
conditions are described as particularly difficult, then two more years at
Ittre. [@1]

Prosecutors stressed his openness but requested a six-month sentence with a
probationary suspension. [@1] The court imposed a work sentence of 80 hours and
an 8,000-euro fine with a total suspension. [@1]
