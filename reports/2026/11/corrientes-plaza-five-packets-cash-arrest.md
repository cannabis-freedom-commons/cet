The Policía de Corrientes reported that officers detained an adult young man and
seized five wrappers of marijuana during a procedure carried out on Friday in
the Corrientes capital. [@1] The operation occurred in the immediate vicinity of
a plaza located in the zone of Cabeza de Vaca and Irala streets, at a time when
uniformed officers were carrying out prevention rounds in the area. [@1] At that
location, officers identified the young man, who was of adult age and was aboard
a 110 cc Honda Wave motorcycle. [@1]

During the subsequent search, officers found 25,000 pesos in cash among the
young man's belongings. [@1] In that same search, officers also found five
wrappers containing a plant-like substance. [@1] After the find, officers
performed the corresponding narcotest on the seized substance. [@1] The officers
confirmed through that test that the substance was marijuana. [@1]

Faced with that result, the officers proceeded to apprehend the young man and to
seize the items that had been found. [@1] Finally, the apprehended young man and
the seized items were transferred to the police station, where the corresponding
procedures continued. [@1]
