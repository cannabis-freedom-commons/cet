Jesse Thomas Norton, 24, of Manhattan, Kansas, was arrested at about 10 p.m. on
multiple drug and weapons allegations. [@2] The Riley County Police Department
said the report was filed at about 8:25 p.m. on 18 November 2026 in the 700
block of Allen Road. [@2]

Norton faces one count of possession of marijuana, two counts of possession of
an opiate, narcotic or certain stimulant, and one count each of criminal use of
weapons and possession of a hallucinogenic drug. [@1] He also faces three drug
paraphernalia counts, including distribution or possession with intent to
distribute, possession with intent to use and possession with intent to
manufacture a controlled substance. [@1]

The Manhattan Mercury's arrest log listed the weapons charge more specifically
as criminal use of weapons with a bludgeon, club or knuckles, and confirmed the
other counts. [@3] Norton was issued a total bond of $13,000 and remained
confined in the Riley County Jail at the time of the report. [@1]
