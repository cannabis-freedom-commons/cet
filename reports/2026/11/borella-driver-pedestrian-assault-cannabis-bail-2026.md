Borella police arrested the 37-year-old suspect on 21 November 2026 over the
assault. [@3] The incident happened at about 3.30 p.m. on 20 November, when a
couple on their way to a funeral tried to cross at the pedestrian crossing near
the Borella cemetery roundabout. [@1] A car travelling from Rajagiriya towards
the roundabout stopped across the crossing while the traffic signal was red, its
driver sounding the horn. [@1] The driver then argued with the couple and
assaulted and threatened one of the pedestrians. [@1] Borella police opened an
investigation after a post about the incident circulated on social media. [@1]

The suspect was produced before the Colombo Chief Judicial Medical Officer,
where medical tests confirmed he was addicted to cannabis. [@1] Deputy Inspector
General of Police W.P.J. Senadheera, who is in charge of traffic control and
road safety, announced the finding. [@1] Daily Mirror reported before the
hearing that the suspect was to be produced before Magistrate's Court No. 02 in
Aluthkade on 22 November. [@2] After his court appearance the suspect was
released on two sureties of Rs 500,000 each. [@1] Newswire reported that the
complainant was a Malabe resident who lodged the complaint with Borella police.
[@3] Police also said they had received information that the complainant shared
an AI-generated image on social media in connection with the incident. [@3] The
vehicle the suspect was driving was taken into police custody. [@3]
