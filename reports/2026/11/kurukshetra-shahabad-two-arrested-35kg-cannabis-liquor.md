Kurukshetra police arrested two men, Sajan and Raghu, who live in Deha Colony in
Shahabad. [@1] Shahabad police raided a shop in Deha Colony after a tip-off that
the two men were selling cannabis and illicit country liquor, and found both
accused inside the shop. [@1] Police registered a case against the two men at
Shahabad police station and seized their car. [@1]

During the check of the shop, police recovered 3.5 kg of cannabis. [@1] From the
same shop, police also recovered over Rs 29,900 in cash, 81 quarters of liquor
and 3 bottles of country-made liquor. [@1] A later search of the men's car found
another 500 quarter-bottles of country liquor. [@1]

The tip-off had said the cannabis and liquor were hidden either in the Deha
Colony shop or inside the men's car. [@1] Police presented the operation as
action against the sale of cannabis and illicit country liquor in Shahabad in
Kurukshetra district, based on information received around November 12. [@1]
