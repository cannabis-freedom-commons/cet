The Urban Security unit of the Mbour central police station seized Indian hemp
in the ONCAD district on 14 November 2026. [@1,2,3,5,6] The operation in the ONCAD
district recovered 79 kilogrammes of drugs and led to the arrest of two people
described as involved in the case. [@1,2,3] Both suspects are taxi drivers who were
referred to the public prosecutor at the Mbour High Court. [@1,2,5,6] Police said the two were suspected of possessing, offering and selling Indian hemp. [@2,7]

The case began with a surveillance patrol in the ONCAD sector. [@1] Officers
spotted a vehicle whose behaviour seemed suspicious to them. [@1] After
following it, officers stopped the vehicle at the district bridge. [@1,7] Its
driver immediately abandoned the car and fled, leaving his mobile phone behind.
[@1,3,4,6] A search of the vehicle uncovered three bags carefully covered in plastic
holding a total of 79 kg of Indian hemp. [@1,3,4,5,6]

After answering the driver’s constantly ringing phone, officers posed as the
driver to arrange a handover at the ONCAD bridge. [@1,2,4,5] The trap led to the
arrest of the people who came to collect the parcel. [@1,4,6] Later technical
examination of their mobile phones allegedly showed regular contacts with the
driver who is still at large. [@1,4,5,7] Both suspects were placed in custody and then
referred to the prosecutor on 16 November 2026. [@1,3,4,6] The main driver involved in
transporting the drugs remains wanted. [@1]
