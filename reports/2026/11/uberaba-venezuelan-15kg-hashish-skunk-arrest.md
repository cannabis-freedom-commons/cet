Federal Highway Police officers detained an 18-year-old Venezuelan man in
Uberaba, in the Triângulo Mineiro region of Minas Gerais, on Sunday 15 November
2026 after finding drugs fastened to his body. [@1] While interviewing
passengers on an intercity bus, officers noticed that one man displayed marked
nervousness, which prompted further checks. [@1] Officers then deepened the
approach and found hashish and skunk concealed on the Venezuelan passenger's
body. [@1] The man was detained and escorted to the Civil Police station in
Uberaba for further procedures. [@1]

Police put the total seized at 1.5 kilograms of hashish and skunk combined, with
no separate weight given for either drug. [@1] The approach took place at about
15:30 at the PRF operational unit in Uberaba during an inspection of a bus
operating on the line between Campo Grande, Mato Grosso do Sul, and Brasília in
the Federal District. [@1]

The published account says the PRF reported no origin for the drugs and no
destination for the load beyond the bus route. [@1]
