Police in Chita detained a 47-year-old local man suspected of illegal storage of
narcotics. [@1] Officers of the drug-control department seized more than 700
grams of a cannabis-group substance from him. [@1] The detention was reported on
16 November through the MAX channel of the Russian Interior Ministry department
for Zabaykalsky Krai. [@1]

Operational inquiries established that he had bought the drugs in a district of
the krai, then transported them to Chita and stored them at his workplace. [@1]
ChitaMedia, citing the regional UMVD press service, likewise reported the
detention of the 47-year-old man on suspicion of illegal acquisition and storage
of narcotics. [@2] According to that account, he bought a plant-based substance
in a district of Zabaykalsky Krai, brought it to Chita and left it where he
worked. [@2] An expert examination put the total mass of the seized material at
more than 700 grams. [@2]

A Chita police investigator opened a criminal case under part 2 of Article 228
of the Russian Criminal Code, and the investigation is continuing. [@1]
