South Sulawesi regional police said that in the Quick Win phase of Operasi
Sarang Narkoba, from 17 October to 17 November 2026, officers recorded 270
police reports with 523 suspects. [@1] The cannabis in the evidence seized that
month was 229.24 grams of ganja. [@1] The same seizure list included 3,602.96
grams of methamphetamine, 14.09 grams of synthetic tobacco and 105 ecstasy
pills, none of which is counted as cannabis. [@1]

Kapolda Sulsel Irjen Pol M. Awal Chairuddin gave the figures at a press
conference on Thursday 19 November 2026. [@2] He said officers carried out 69
enforcement actions in drug-prone areas and night-entertainment venues, held 71
alleged dealers and sent 221 users into rehabilitation. [@1] Three police
officers were also held, two from Polres Pelabuhan Makassar and one from
Polresta Gowa. [@1] Police said all three were still classed as drug users and
were to be processed under the law and to face a police ethics hearing. [@1] The
three officers were caught during Operasi Sarang Narkoba between 17 September
and 17 November 2026. [@2]

Before Quick Win, from 17 September to 16 October 2026, police recorded 311
police reports and 486 suspects. [@1] From those earlier disclosures police
seized 3.59 grams of ganja, which is not included in the Quick Win cannabis
figure. [@1] Police estimated that the wider crackdown had averted potential
economic losses of Rp4,138,674,960. [@1] They also claimed to have prevented the
circulation of about 36,935 drug doses, a combined claim that is not a value for
the ganja. [@1]
