R.K. Nagar police arrested three men for selling intoxicants in Korukkupet,
Chennai. [@1] Acting on a secret tip that intoxicant tablets were being sold
under the Chunnambu Canal flyover in Korukkupet, R.K. Nagar police watched the
area. [@1] Officers detained three men standing suspiciously there and found
ganja and pain-relief tablets hidden in their bag for sale. [@1]

Police identified the three as Seenivasan (25), Sanjay (24) and Amudhavan (24)
of Tondiarpet and seized 1,200 tablets and 1.1 kg of ganja from them. [@1]
Questioning revealed the three had bought the ganja and tablets in Nagpur,
Maharashtra, and sold them in Chennai. [@1]

The three were produced in court and lodged in prison, while police were
searching for absconders. [@1]
