Judges for preliminary hearings in Brescia and Reggio Calabria convicted 40
defendants of criminal association aimed at drug trafficking at first instance.
[@4] The courts imposed prison terms of up to 20 years, totalling 291 years of
imprisonment. [@4] The convictions closed the Twistlock investigation by
Brescia's Squadra Mobile, coordinated by the anti-mafia directorate, which
identified two drug-trafficking organisations. [@3] During the investigation
officers arrested 19 people caught in the act. [@1]

Officers seized 135 kilograms of cocaine, 90 kilograms of hashish and 3
kilograms of marijuana. [@4] The groups operated chiefly in Roncadelle, Torbole
Casaglia, Villa Carcina, Rodengo Saiano and Gussago. [@4] Investigators found 40
kilograms of cocaine hidden in a milk tanker bound for a depot in Roncadelle.
[@4] A search in Villa Carcina also turned up two 45-calibre revolvers with
erased serial numbers. [@4]

The investigation began in 2022 and covered 87 suspects in total. [@2] Police
said further inquiries into the groups' contacts and accomplices were still
under way. [@3]
