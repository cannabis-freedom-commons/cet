The Novovolynsk city court found a miner at the Buzhanska mine guilty of
illegally handling explosives and of storing narcotic drugs without intent to
sell. [@1] The court sentenced him to four years in prison and released him from
serving the term with a two-year probation period. [@2]

Police found the cannabis on 31 July 2026 during authorised searches, at his
flat and at a premises in the village of Stara Lishnia. [@1] He had gathered the
wild cannabis near a lake in Stara Lishnia in 2023, dried it and kept it for his
own use in a household outbuilding in the same village. [@2]

In late May 2025 he came across three electric detonators filled with lead
azide, hexogen and pentrite in coal mined at the mine. [@1] Despite colleagues'
warnings he carried them to his flat without any permit. [@2] At the hearing he
fully admitted his guilt and repented, said he kept the drugs only for himself
and could not explain why he took the detonators home. [@1]
