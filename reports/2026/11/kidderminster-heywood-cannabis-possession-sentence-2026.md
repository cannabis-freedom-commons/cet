James Heywood, 43, of Park Avenue, Worcester, admitted possession of a Class B
drug (cannabis) and two counts of driving with a proportion of a specified drug
above the legal limit at Worcester Magistrates' Court on 16 October. [@2] The
case was adjourned to Kidderminster Magistrates' Court for sentencing on 13
November. [@2] The court listing records a sentencing hearing at Kidderminster
Magistrates' Court on 13 November 2026. [@3]

Magistrates gave Heywood a 12-month community order, banned him from driving for
18 months and fined him £120. [@1] The cannabis was forfeited and destroyed.
[@1]

Police stopped Heywood driving a Vauxhall Astra on the A449 near Hindlip on 4
April and tested his blood for cocaine and cannabis. [@2] His blood showed 271
microgrammes of benzoylecgonine, a breakdown product of cocaine, more than five
times the legal limit of 50. [@2] He also had three microgrammes of THC in his
blood, with the legal limit being two. [@2] Officers found him in possession of
cannabis on the same date. [@1] Magistrates granted him unconditional bail while
a pre-sentencing report was prepared. [@2]
