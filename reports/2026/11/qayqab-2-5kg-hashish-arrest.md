The Qarnada Investigation and Arrest Office under the General Administration of
Criminal Investigation arrested a suspected drug promoter and seized hashish
found in his possession. [@1] The man is a resident of the Ard Quraish area of
Benghazi and admitted that he had been bringing drugs from Qayqab, Abraq and
Qubba to transfer them to Benghazi to promote and trade them. [@1,2]

Officers caught the suspect in an ambush in the Qayqab area near a petrol
station after confirming his movements. [@1,2] Inside his white Hyundai, officers
found about two and a half kilograms of hashish divided into 23 pieces described
as qourza. [@1,2] The Criminal Investigation Agency's photographs of the seized hashish were captioned 20 November 2026. [@2]

The operation followed confirmed information that the man was moving drugs in
his private vehicle from Abraq toward Benghazi, after which the competent
services watched him closely for a full month. [@1] The office said the
operation implemented instructions from deputy armed-forces commander Saddam
Haftar with follow-up from Interior Minister Essam Bouzriba as part of
continuing efforts against prohibited drugs and trafficking. [@1] The
authorities said all necessary legal measures were taken and the suspect and the
seized drugs were referred to the competent authorities. [@1,2]
