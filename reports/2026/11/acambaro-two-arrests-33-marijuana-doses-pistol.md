Two men aged 41 and 25 were detained in the community of San Mateo Tócuaro, in
Acámbaro, Guanajuato, after they were stopped riding a motorcycle without
licence plates. [@2] The check was part of a joint operation by the state's
Fuerzas de Seguridad Pública del Estado (FSPE) and the Secretaría de la Defensa
Nacional, the army. [@2]

Officers found a Colt 9-millimetre pistol, a loaded magazine, 13 rounds of
ammunition and 33 doses of a substance with the characteristics of marijuana
among their belongings, the state authorities reported. [@3]

The two detainees, aged 41 and 25, were left at the disposal of the Fiscalía,
the state prosecutor's office. [@1] The prosecutor's office in Acámbaro will
investigate the case and determine their legal situation. [@2]

The Guanajuato government says its security forces seized 1,009 firearms, 1,257
magazines and 37,446 rounds between the start of the current administration and
31 October 2026. [@3] Authorities say joint operations continue in communities
across the state to detect illegal firearms carrying and prevent crime. [@2]
