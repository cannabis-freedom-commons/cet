Police stopped a 39-year-old motorist on the A8 near Augsburg on Friday evening
after a patrol found him driving at about 21:45. [@1] The check took place at
the Augsburg-Ost rest area on the A8. [@1] During the check officers observed
drug-typical behaviour in the driver. [@1]

A rapid drug test reacted positive to cocaine and cannabis. [@1] Officers also
found marijuana on the 39-year-old during the check. [@2] Officers ordered a
blood sample from the driver and stopped him from continuing his journey. [@1]

Police are now investigating the driver for a possible violation of the Road
Traffic Act. [@1]
