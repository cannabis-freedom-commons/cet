A 38-year-old man from Perpignan was tried at the Nîmes correctional court on
Friday 20 November 2026 for transporting 4.192 kg of cannabis herb that he said
had been handed to him in Perpignan for delivery in Nîmes. [@1] Nîmes customs
stopped him on the A9 at Milhaud on 14 October and he immediately admitted being
a mule, but gave no information about the supplier or buyer. [@1] He was also
prosecuted for driving without a licence. [@1]

The customs lawyer valued the cannabis at €41,920. [@1] The prosecutor requested
two years in prison, continued detention and confiscation of the vehicle. [@2]
The court went further and sentenced him to three years in prison with continued
detention, a €5,000 customs fine and confiscation of the car. [@2]

He had left prison in early October after earlier convictions, mostly for
driving offences. [@2] He told the court he had a wife and three children to
provide for. [@2] The prosecutor pointed to two trips to the Spanish border
shortly before the offence. [@2] The defendant said those trips were to visit
his mother's grave after his release. [@2] His lawyer noted he had never been
convicted of a drug offence. [@2] He asked to be transferred to Béziers or
Perpignan to be closer to his family. [@2]
