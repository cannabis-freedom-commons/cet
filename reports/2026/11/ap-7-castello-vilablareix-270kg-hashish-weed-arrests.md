Spanish police intercepted more than 270 kilos of drugs during two separate
traffic controls on the AP-7 motorway. [@1] At Castelló d’Empúries, one driver
first tried to evade the checkpoint before officers stopped his vehicle. [@1]
That driver was then arrested on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@1] Further
along at Vilablareix, officers stopped a second driver during a second control.
[@1] That second driver was also arrested. [@1]

In the first driver’s vehicle, officers found 204 kilos of hashish. [@1] Police
estimated the street value of that hashish at more than 413,000 euros. [@1] In
the second driver’s car, officers found 66 kilos of weed together with 17 grams
of cocaine. [@1] Together, the two controls were described as yielding more than
270 kilos of drugs. [@1]

The first control took place on the AP-7 at Castelló d’Empúries. [@1] The second
control took place further along at Vilablareix. [@1] Police disclosed no
information about the identities of the suspects, the origin of the drugs or
their suspected destinations. [@1]
