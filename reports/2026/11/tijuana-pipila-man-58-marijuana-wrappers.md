Officers of Tijuana's Secretaría de Seguridad y Protección Ciudadana Municipal
(SSPCM) detained a man for suspected crimes against health during preventive
patrols in the city. [@1] The arrest took place on Thursday 19 November 2026 in
colonia El Pípila. [@1] The man was identified as Jesús "N", aged 42. [@2] A
precautionary search of his belongings found 58 transparent plastic zip-lock
wrappers holding a green, dry herb with characteristics of marijuana. [@1]

He was arrested and the wrappers were secured as evidence for the investigation.
[@1] The detainee and the seized substance were handed to the Fiscalía General
del Estado (FGE), which will continue the investigation and determine his legal
situation. [@2] The SSPCM urged residents to report drug sales anonymously on
the 089 telephone line. [@1]
