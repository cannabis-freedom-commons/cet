An Isle of Wight pair appeared in court accused of drug dealing involving
cocaine, MDMA, ketamine and cannabis, as well as possessing nearly 4,500 pounds
in alleged criminal cash. [@1] Limara Burt, 42, of Pound Lane in Newport, and
Christopher Hall, 31, of Rectory Drive in Wootton, appeared before Isle of Wight
magistrates on Tuesday, November 17. [@1] The pair are charged with being
concerned in the supply of cocaine, MDMA, ketamine, cannabis and cannabis resin.
[@1]

They are also charged with possessing criminal property, namely 4,499.67 pounds
in cash. [@1] Hall alone is further charged with possession of cannabis resin.
[@1] All the charges related to dates between May 23, 2024, and November 28,
2024. [@1]

The pair did not enter pleas to any of the charges. [@1] Their case was
adjourned for a plea and trial preparation hearing at the Isle of Wight Crown
Court on January 4, 2027. [@1] They were both granted unconditional bail. [@1]
