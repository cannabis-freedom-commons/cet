Two men were caught with marijuana in Fraccionamiento Buenos Aires, a
neighbourhood of Monterrey, Nuevo León, and are under investigation for the
theft of vehicle parts and accessories. [@1] The detainees were identified as
Humberto, 51, and Harold Smith, 32. [@1]

Monterrey's C4 surveillance camera network detected the van as it circulated and
police were sent to the area. [@1] The Aerostar van had been used in the theft
of a tyre from a vehicle parked on Avenida Alejandro de Rodas. [@1] During an
inspection the two men were found with two bags of marijuana. [@1]
