A 15-year-old pregnant girl known as Malvadinha was apprehended for the third
time on the night of Tuesday 17 November 2026 in an apparently abandoned
residence on Rua Nossa Senhora de Fátima in the Taquari neighbourhood, Segundo
Distrito of Rio Branco. [@1] A Força Tática team of the 2nd Battalion acted
after an anonymous tip said a couple was using the building to prepare and sell
drugs. [@1] She was apprehended for an infraction analogous to drug trafficking
and taken to the Defla station with the drugs and materials. [@1] At the station
she said she was pregnant, had been involved in drug trafficking since age 12
and had links to a criminal faction. [@1]

Officers found in the living room packages with portions of substances similar
to marijuana and cocaine, already separated for sale. [@2] In the kitchen
officers found stones appearing to be crack, also packaged, plus a marble stone,
knives and a larger package of drugs, which police said showed the house was
used to prepare, portion and pack drugs. [@1] One account listed the seizure as
20 wrappers of skunk, two bars of marijuana, 38 crack stones and 16 packets of
cocaine. [@3]

The girl said her boyfriend identified only as Denis had left shortly before
police arrived and had left his mobile phone behind, which was seized for the
investigation. [@1] Officers noted the street was a dead end with poor lighting
and monitoring cameras nearby. [@1]
