Customs officers at Cairo International Airport's Terminal 3 foiled six attempts
to smuggle assorted narcotics. [@2] The passengers involved had arrived from
Brussels, Athens, Washington, Addis Ababa and New York. [@1] The group included
Egyptians and foreign nationals. [@3] The cases were handled by the general
administration of Terminal 3 customs under its director-general, Hussein Heikal.
[@2]

The seized items included quantities of hashish and marijuana. [@1] Officers
also found about 4.5 kg of khat, a leafy stimulant. [@2] The haul also included
drugged sweets, liquid narcotic preparations and narcotic pills. [@3]

Customs drew up six seizure reports numbered 112 to 117 on the instructions of
four department heads at the terminal. [@2] The Egyptian Customs Authority said
it would keep stepping up inspection and control of passengers and bags arriving
from abroad. [@2] It said the work was meant to protect public security and
support the national economy. [@1]
