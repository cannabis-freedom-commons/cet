Officers of Bahía Blanca's Fourth Police Station arrested Gastón Exequiel Rivas
and his partner, Yoseli Karen Robert, at a home in the city. [@1] The operation
was ordered by Guarantee Court No. 3 at the request of prosecutor Mauricio Del
Cero, who heads Functional Instruction and Judgment Unit No. 19. [@1] The couple
was arrested during a morning search at a home on Tarapacá al 200, in Bahía
Blanca's Villa Rosario area. [@2]

At the property, officers seized marijuana, cocaine and mobile phones to be
added to the case file. [@1] It was at least the third police search at the
house; two earlier raids there had already seized marijuana, cocaine and mobile
devices. [@1]

The investigation had accumulated seven cases opened this year. [@1] It began
early in the year with anonymous complaints that drugs were being sold from the
raided house. [@2] Analysis of the phones led investigators to conclude the
couple sold marijuana, cocaine and LSD at least between 24 November 2024 and 26
May 2026. [@3] The messages referred to drug quantities, prices, bank or
electronic transfers, places to store the goods and other criminal cases. [@3]
Investigators said the couple received buyers referred by Rodolfo Molina, who is
accused in another drug-trafficking case in the city. [@3]
