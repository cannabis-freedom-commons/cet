Chattogram Range police detained 947 people in a 24-hour special operation. [@1]
The operation ran from Wednesday 18 November to the morning of Thursday 19
November 2026. [@1] Police said 62 people were held in drug cases, including
three carriers and 59 users, while 56 more drug traders were held in regular and
other cases for a reported total of 118 drug-linked detentions. [@1] Mobile
courts jailed 20 people during the anti-drug drive, 39 drug cases were filed and
Tk 53,500 in fines was collected. [@1]

Officers seized 8 kg 900 grams of ganja, the cannabis component recorded here.
[@1] The same recovery also included 33,031 yaba tablets, 185 litres of cholai
liquor, 75 bottles of scuff syrup and five tapentadol tablets, which are
excluded from the cannabis figures. [@1]

Officers also detained 394 teenagers in the anti-teen-gang part of the drive,
releasing 230 of them to guardians on bond while legal action was taken against
the remaining 164. [@1] Police also arrested 168 members of an organisation
whose activities were declared banned. [@1] Police said such special operations
would continue in the public interest. [@1]
