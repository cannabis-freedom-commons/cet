Patrols of the General Directorate of Mujahideen in the Riyadh region arrested a
Saudi citizen accused of promoting hashish and tablets subject to
medical-circulation regulation. [@1] The directorate's own statement, posted on
the evening of 17 November 2026, said its Riyadh patrols had arrested a person
for promoting hashish and tablets subject to medical-circulation regulation.
[@2] The hashish, a cannabis resin, is the cannabis element of the case; the
tablets were described only as subject to the regulation of medical circulation.
[@1]

The directorate's account on X lists the Saudi Interior Ministry in its
description. [@2] The suspect was stopped, the statutory procedures were taken
against him and he was referred to the competent authority to complete the
necessary measures. [@1]

Security agencies asked the public to report drug smugglers and dealers on 911
in Mecca, Medina, Riyadh and the Eastern Province and on 999 in the rest of the
kingdom. [@1] They also gave the General Directorate of Narcotics Control's 995
line and the email 995@gdnc.gov.sa, and said every report is handled in complete
confidentiality. [@1]
