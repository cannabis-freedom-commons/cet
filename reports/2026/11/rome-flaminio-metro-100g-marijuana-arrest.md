A 21-year-old worker from Rome, originally from Capena, was arrested on
suspicion of possessing narcotics for the purpose of dealing. [@1] Il Messaggero
described him as a 20-year-old plumber. [@2] Officers stopped him during a
high-impact control operation in Piazzale Flaminio after the drug-detection dog
Odino barked and singled him out as he left the metro station. [@1] The checks
at the Flaminio metro entrance had taken place the day before, according to Il
Messaggero. [@2]

Police found three envelopes of marijuana ready for sale in his pockets. [@1] A
search of his apartment in Capena found a further 100 grams of marijuana, a
precision scale, bags for packaging doses and a knife whose blade was soaked in
THC. [@1] Il Messaggero reported that 200 grams of drugs were found hidden under
a wardrobe at the Capena home. [@2]

The suspect told investigators the cannabis was for personal use. [@2] Officers
pointed to the dose-packaging bags and a scale dirty with narcotic substance.
[@2] He was taken to the Piazzale Clodio court, where a judge validated the
arrest and ordered him held under house arrest until the trial begins. [@2]
