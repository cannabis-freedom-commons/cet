On Friday 20 November 2026, at around 7:40 in the morning, officers of the ADSU,
Mauritius's anti-drug and smuggling unit, searched the home of a 41-year-old
self-employed worker in Coteau-Raffin. [@1]

In the course of that search, police discovered and seized a white sheet of
paper holding 28.5 grams of cannabis. [@1] The 41-year-old man was arrested.
[@1] He was subsequently released on bail. [@1] The investigation continues.
