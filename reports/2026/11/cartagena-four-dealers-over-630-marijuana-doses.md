National Police surveillance patrols captured four alleged drug retailers in
different sectors of Cartagena who allegedly drove local drug dealing in parks
and residential areas. [@1] Officers of the police Special Operations Group
(GOES) caught 18-year-old alias ‘Omar’ in flagrante on Santa Isabel street in
the El Pozón neighbourhood. [@1] Officers caught 35-year-old alias ‘Kike’ in the
El Arca sector on the Mamonal road after a citizen tip. [@1] Patrols caught
alias ‘Mayo’ in the El Hueco sector of the 3 de Junio neighbourhood for
allegedly selling narcotics. [@1] Officers caught 27-year-old alias ‘El Gabi’ on
Carrera 71 in the Santa Mónica neighbourhood while he was allegedly selling
drugs. [@1]

Officers seized more than 600 marijuana doses ready for sale from alias ‘Omar’.
[@1] Officers seized 400 bazuco doses allegedly intended for sale from alias
‘Kike’. [@1] Officers seized more than 40 bazuco doses and 30 marijuana doses
ready for distribution from alias ‘Mayo’. [@1] Officers seized more than 100
cocaine doses from alias ‘El Gabi’. [@1]

The four detainees and the seized substances were placed at the disposal of the
Attorney General’s Office, pending preliminary hearings to decide their judicial
situation. [@1] Cartagena Metropolitan Police commander Colonel Juan Pablo Ruiz
Rodríguez said 2,394 people had been captured this year for drug trafficking and
more than 891 kilograms of hallucinogenic substances had been seized, including
marijuana, coca base, bazuco, cocaine and tusi. [@1]
