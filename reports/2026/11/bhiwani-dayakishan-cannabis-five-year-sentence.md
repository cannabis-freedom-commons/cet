A special NDPS court in Bhiwani convicted Dayakishan, a resident of Kudal, of
possessing cannabis and sentenced him to five years' imprisonment and a
20,000-rupee fine. [@1] Presided over by judge Suruchi Atreja Singh, the court
delivered the verdict on Monday, 23 November 2026. [@1] The court said that
failing to pay the fine would bring six months of additional imprisonment. [@1]

The case dates to 2 May 2023, when Sub-Inspector Krishna Kumar of the detective
staff in Loharu was patrolling the Kudlabas bus stand with a police team and
received a tip that a man was carrying cannabis on a Platina motorcycle. [@1]
Police blocked the bus stand and stopped the suspect, Dayakishan, a resident of
Kudal. [@1] A search of his possession yielded 1 kilogram 286 grams of cannabis.
[@1] Police registered a case under the NDPS Act at the Jui Kalan police
station, arrested Dayakishan and produced him in court. [@1]

During the trial, the court convicted Dayakishan on the basis of police evidence
and witness testimony under section 20 of the NDPS Act. [@1] Superintendent of
police Mayank Mishra has directed district police to take strict action against
illegal narcotics trafficking and possession, arrest suspects and ensure
effective prosecution. [@1]
