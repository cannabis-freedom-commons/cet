Officers arrested two men in Porterville after a traffic stop and a warrant
search on Thursday. [@1] Police stopped a vehicle driven by 23-year-old
Francisco Pantoja-Sanchez after a traffic violation near Highway 65 and
Henderson Avenue. [@1] During the stop officers found a privately made handgun
and marijuana for sale, and police obtained a search warrant for the
23-year-old's home. [@1]

At a home in the 1000 block of W. Tomah Avenue, officers found 21-year-old
Alexander Pantoja-Sanchez. [@1] In the home officers found a short-barreled
shotgun, a loaded handgun, a loaded privately manufactured AR-style rifle,
multiple auto-switches and different types of ammunition. [@1] Officers also
found evidence linking Alexander Pantoja-Sanchez to a street gang. [@1]

Both suspects were taken to the South County Detention Facility on charges
related to weapons and drugs. [@1]
