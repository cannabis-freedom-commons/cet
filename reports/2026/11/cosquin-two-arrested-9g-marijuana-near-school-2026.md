A 24-year-old man and a 17-year-old were arrested on the afternoon of Thursday
26 November 2026 in Cosquín, Córdoba, in a procedure over an alleged breach of
Argentina's national narcotics law. [@1] Officers of the Punilla Norte
departmental police were on preventive patrol when they came across the pair at
the intersection of Palemón Carranza and Ana María Janer streets, in the town's
Centro neighbourhood. [@1] Police said the two tried to hide a backpack behind a
parked vehicle near a school when they noticed the officers. [@1]

The officers identified them and searched their belongings, finding several
wrappings of plant material compatible with marijuana, cash, a digital precision
scale, scissors and two mobile phones. [@1] Personnel of the Fuerza Policial
Antinarcotráfico, the provincial anti-narcotics force, carried out tests at the
scene and confirmed the substance was marijuana, weighing nine grams in total.
[@1] Both were arrested and the items seized, and the case files were placed at
the disposal of the Unidad Judicial de Cosquín. [@1]
