The Cairo Criminal Court sitting at the Abbasiya courts complex sentenced a
29-year-old contractor to five years in prison and a 50,000-pound fine for
trafficking hashish and possessing an unlicensed bladed weapon in the Nozha
police district. [@1] The referral order identified the defendant as Abdel
Rahman E., aged 29, a contractor, who possessed hashish for trafficking on 20
March 2026 in the Nozha district of Cairo outside the cases legally permitted.
[@1] Prosecutors also charged him with possessing a metal-blade white weapon
without a licence. [@1]

An officer of the airport roads and axes security department testified that he
stopped the defendant at a security checkpoint while he was driving a car, found
through a records check that he was wanted for enforcement in another case, then
detained and searched him precautionarily. [@1] The search found pieces of
hashish, a white weapon with drug traces on its blade, money, four mobile phones
and a smart wristwatch in his possession. [@1] The officer attributed
trafficking intent to the drug possession, treating the money as proceeds, the
phones as a means of contacting users, the weapon as defence and protection for
the trade, and the car as a means of promoting the drugs. [@1]

The central chemical laboratories report confirmed the seized material was
schedule-one hashish weighing 396.97 grams. [@1] The report also established
that the seized cutter blade bore traces of a brown substance proved to be
schedule-one hashish. [@1]
