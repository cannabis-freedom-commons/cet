Bornholm Police searched a storage facility and a private home in Ronne on
Friday 13 November 2026. [@1] A 60-year-old man from the town was charged in the
case. [@2] During the searches the officers also found just under 300 grams of
hash. [@2] The man was additionally charged with possessing approximately 300
grams of hashish. [@1]

In the same searches the police seized 3,588 illegal disposable e-cigarettes,
known as puff bars. [@1] He was also charged with possessing more than the ten
puff bars permitted for personal use. [@1] Police said all of the seized puff
bars were fruit-flavoured, while Denmark prohibits flavours other than menthol
or tobacco. [@1]

The investigation began after several parents told the police they suspected a
man of selling the disposable e-cigarettes in the area, including to
schoolchildren. [@1] Deputy police inspector Henrik Schou, the head of
investigations at Bornholm Police, said schools and parents were concerned about
the spread of puff bars among children and young people because the products are
highly addictive. [@1] The police believe the seized puff bars would otherwise
probably have been sold on Bornholm. [@1]
