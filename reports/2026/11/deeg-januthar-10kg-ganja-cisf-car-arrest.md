Januthar police in Rajasthan's Deeg district arrested Chintu, 23, and Monu
Kumar, 26, both residents of Januthar. [@3] Officers seized 10.32 kg of ganja
from the two men. [@3] Police also impounded the Swift car used to carry the
ganja. [@3]

The car carried the letters CISF, and officers seized it as the vehicle used in
the trafficking. [@1] The accused had CISF lettered on the car while trafficking
the ganja, the paper reported. [@2]

The arrests came under Operation Prahar, a special drive against illegal
narcotics trafficking in Deeg district. [@3] The operation was carried out on
the instructions of the Deeg district superintendent of police, Sharan Gopinath.
[@3]
