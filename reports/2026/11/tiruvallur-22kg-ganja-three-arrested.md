Police in Tiruvallur arrested three young men from Tiruttani on 19 November 2026
and had them lodged in jail. [@1] Those held were identified as 27-year-old
Udayakumar, 24-year-old Chandran and 22-year-old Anand Babu. [@1] Officers
produced the three before the Tiruvallur criminal magistrate court, which sent
them to Puzhal prison. [@1]

Tiruvallur Town police were conducting a vehicle check near the tollgate on the
evening of 18 November. [@1] Officers questioned three youths who were near the
spot and looked suspicious. [@1] A search of a bag the men were holding found 22
kg of ganja. [@1] A second account says the district superintendent of police
had received a tip that ganja was being smuggled from Andhra Pradesh to
Tiruvallur, so a special police team kept watch on the Tiruvallur-Uthukottai
road. [@2]

Police said the inquiry found the men had brought the ganja from Andhra Pradesh
to sell it in Tiruvallur. [@2] Officers registered a case against the three
before arresting them. [@1]
