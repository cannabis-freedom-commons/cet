On 21 November 2026 at 11h at Roffiac, a 31-year-old man living in Hérault was
checked while driving under the influence of cannabis. [@1] On 22 November 2026
at 10h at Saint-Mary, a 28-year-old man living in Cantal was checked while
driving under the influence of cannabis. [@1] On 22 November 2026 at 12h20 on
the A75 at Val d’Arcomie, a 36-year-old man living in the Pyrénées-Orientales
was checked at 172 km/h, 163 km/h retained, where the limit was 110 km/h in rain
on a soaked carriageway. [@1]

For the Val d’Arcomie driver, the listed connected offences were driving a
vehicle after having used substances or plants classified as narcotics,
identified as cannabis and cocaine. [@1] The same Val d’Arcomie check also
listed illicit use of narcotics as a connected offence for that driver. [@1]

Drivers stopped in these Cantal controls risk an administrative suspension of
the driving licence that can last up to 12 months. [@1]
