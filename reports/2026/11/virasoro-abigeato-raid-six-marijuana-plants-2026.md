Officers investigating alleged cattle rustling searched a house in Barrio
Co.Vi.Vir in Gobernador Virasoro. [@1] Officers detained one person in the
operation. [@1] The prosecutor's office ordered a related case to be opened over
the cannabis find. [@1]

Officers seized assorted meat cuts, saws, knives and a refrigerator of interest
to the cattle-rustling case. [@1] Officers also found and seized six marijuana
plants during the search. [@1]

The search was carried out by officers of the Unidad Especial de Seguridad Rural
y Ecológica. [@1] The officers opened ex officio proceedings for an alleged
drug-law violation as part of the fight against retail drug dealing. [@1]
