Carabinieri in Pula arrested a 24-year-old local man, unemployed and already
known to police, on suspicion of drug possession with intent to sell. [@1] The
arrest took place in the early hours of 16 November 2026 in Pula. [@1] Officers
had stopped the man in a vehicle during a routine road-traffic check, and his
nervous and suspicious behaviour led them to carry out personal and home
searches. [@1]

The searches recovered four sealed packets holding a total of about five grams
of cocaine and two packets holding about two grams of hashish. [@1] Officers
also seized almost 200 euros in cash in mixed-denomination notes, a precision
scale and material for packaging doses. [@1]

After the formal procedures, the suspect was placed under restriction pending
decisions by the judicial authority, and the drugs, cash and materials were
placed under probative seizure. [@1] A second account reports the same seizure
of cocaine packets, hashish packets, almost 200 euros, a precision scale and
packaging material. [@2] A third account describes the Pula intervention and a
second intervention as two distinct operations by the respective Carabinieri
stations. [@3]
