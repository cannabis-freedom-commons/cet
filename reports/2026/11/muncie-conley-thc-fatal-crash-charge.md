Michael S. Conley, 56, of Muncie, was charged on Wednesday 18 November 2026 with
causing death when driving with a controlled substance in his system, a Level 4
felony carrying up to 12 years in prison. [@1] City police said Conley was
driving a westbound Nissan Rogue on Bunch Boulevard when it pulled into the path
of a southbound SUV on Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. Boulevard on 3 June 2025. [@1]
David M. Flye, 69, a passenger in the SUV, was partially ejected when it
overturned and was pronounced dead by Muncie Fire Department EMS personnel. [@1]
Police said Conley and the driver of the SUV escaped life-threatening injuries.
[@1]

An affidavit says Conley told police he regularly consumed a gummy infused with
THC to help him fall asleep. [@1] The affidavit did not say how much time passed
between the last time Conley ingested THC and the crash. [@1] Conley submitted
to a blood draw at IU Health Ball Memorial Hospital. [@1]

Conley was arrested on Thursday night and was being held in the Delaware County
Jail on Friday under a $10,000 bond. [@1] No trial date had been set in the
Delaware Circuit Court 5 case. [@1] Court records showed no earlier felony
charges against Conley. [@1]
