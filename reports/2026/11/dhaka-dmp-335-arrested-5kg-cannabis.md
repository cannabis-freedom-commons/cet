The Dhaka Metropolitan Police (DMP) arrested 335 people in regular anti-crime
drives across the capital over 24 hours, and filed 36 cases during the same
period. [@1] The raids were conducted across various police station areas in the
capital on Saturday, 21 November 2026, leading to the arrests alongside the
seizure of narcotics and other evidence. [@3]

Mirpur division accounted for 69 of those arrested, the highest number of any
division, followed by Tejgaon with 55, Wari with 53, Motijheel with 37, Gulshan
with 35, Uttara with 33, Ramna with 27 and Lalbagh with 26. [@1] During the
drives, police recovered 5 kg of cannabis. [@1] The same operations recovered
154 yaba tablets, Tk 1,155 and eight mobile phones, and one victim was rescued.
[@2]

The arrests were announced in a DMP press release issued on 22 November. [@2]
Police said legal proceedings against the arrested individuals were under way.
[@1] Further legal action against the arrestees was in process. [@3]
