Four men aged between 19 and 27 were apprehended during a preventive operation
at Mariano Acosta y Garay in the Las Lilas neighbourhood of Mar del Plata. [@1]
The men were intercepted during the preventive patrol and were reported to fall
in the 19-to-27 age range. [@1] The procedure was carried out by personnel of
the Fuerza Barrial de Aproximación XIII. [@1]

Police said the men threw a nylon wrapper containing the substance to the ground
when they noticed the officers. [@1] Police sources told the outlet that
officers seized that wrapper with 4.5 grams of marijuana. [@1]

The Fiscalía de Estupefacientes, the drugs prosecutor's office, intervened in
the case and took charge of the proceedings. [@1] It ordered the apprehensions,
the seizure of the cannabis and notification that proceedings were opened under
article 60 of the Code of Criminal Procedure. [@1]
