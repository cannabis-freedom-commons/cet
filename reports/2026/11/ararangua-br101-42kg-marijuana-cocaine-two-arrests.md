Federal Highway Police officers arrested two men for drug trafficking on the
BR-101 in Araranguá, in southern Santa Catarina, on the night of Wednesday 25
November 2026. [@1] Both occupants of the blue Ford Ka were arrested in the act
and taken with the seized drugs to the Civil Police central station in
Araranguá. [@4] The driver told officers he had collected the drugs in the
Palhoça region, in Greater Florianópolis, and was taking them to Porto Alegre.
[@1]

Officers found 42 tablets of marijuana and eight of cocaine in the Ford Ka's
luggage compartment. [@2] Each tablet weighed about one kilogram, and the PRF
put the total load at about 50 kilograms. [@5] Officers smelled a strong odour
of marijuana during the inspection of the car. [@3] A reagent test confirmed
that the white powder was cocaine. [@1]

In Brazil, drug trafficking carries a prison sentence of five to 15 years. [@3]
