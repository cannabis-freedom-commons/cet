Officers of the Vogošća Police Administration arrested M. L., identified only by
his initials, a 25-year-old from Kamenica who lives in Vogošća, at 13:45 on
Wednesday. [@1] Police said he was suspected of committing the criminal offence
of unauthorised production and placing narcotic drugs into circulation. [@1]

Officers carried out a personal search and searched the house, yard and
auxiliary premises that the 25-year-old uses in the Vogošća area. [@1] Those
searches found spid, the local name for speed, and marihuana, the local name for
cannabis. [@1] Officers also seized a cannabis stalk, a digital scale, a crusher
used to grind drugs, and other objects important for the investigation. [@1]

The duty prosecutor of the Sarajevo Canton Prosecutor's Office in Sarajevo was
notified about the arrest and the drugs seized in Vogošća. [@1] The suspect was
handed over to the detention rooms of the Sarajevo Canton Interior Ministry for
persons deprived of liberty, where officers continued his criminal processing.
