A 28-year-old Beitbridge man was sentenced to five years imprisonment after
being found transporting 21.106 kilogrammes of marijuana along the
Masvingo-Beitbridge Highway. [@1] Decent Mashingaidze, of Dulibadzimu in
Beitbridge, appeared before Resident Magistrate Mr Takudzwa Gwazemba on Thursday
and was convicted of unlawful dealing in dangerous drugs under Section 156(1)(a)
of the Criminal Law (Codification and Reform) Act. [@1]

The magistrate gave him a six-year term but set aside one year conditionally for
five years, leaving five years effective in jail. [@1] Prosecutor Mr Reason
Mutimba told the court that on November 19 at around 1200 hours police of the
National Anti-Smuggling Task Force were manning a roadblock at the 80km peg on
the Masvingo-Beitbridge Highway at Bubi and stopped a silver Toyota Hiace towing
an unregistered trailer that was coming from Beitbridge. [@1] Police instructed
all occupants to disembark with their luggage. [@1] Mashingaidze attempted to
flee but was caught by police officers. [@1] A systematic search of his luggage
led to the recovery of 18 cobs of dagga. [@1] He was subsequently arrested. [@1]
The dagga was weighed at ZIMPOST in the presence of the accused and came to
21.106 kilogrammes. [@1] It had a street value of US$2,110.60. [@1]
