Giza Criminal Court sentenced an unemployed man to three years in prison for
trafficking hashish in the Badrashin police district, with the drugs prepared
for distribution to customers. [@2]

Case papers showed that information reached the security services that the man
was involved in drug trafficking and used the Badrashin police station area as a
base for his activity, promoting hashish among users. [@1] Officers verified the
information, identified the man and monitored his activity before preparing an
operation to arrest him. [@1]

The operation arrested the man; a search found a quantity of hashish prepared
for sale, and officers impounded the drugs, took him to the police station and
filed a report. [@1] After the investigation closed and the man was referred for
trial, the court reviewed the case papers, evidence and charges and imposed
three years' imprisonment for drug trafficking in the Badrashin police district.
[@1] The seizures were impounded and the case passed to the public prosecution
for investigation before reaching the court. [@2]
