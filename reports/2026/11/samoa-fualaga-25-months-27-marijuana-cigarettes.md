Auvaa Fualaga, a 30-year-old father of four from Falelauniu and Vaipu’a on
Savai’i, was sentenced to 25 months’ imprisonment for possession of 27 marijuana
cigarettes while on bail for similar offending. [@1] A 30-year-old man was
sentenced to 25 months in prison after being convicted of possessing 27
marijuana cigarettes at Malaefatu Sogi, the term adding to his existing prison
terms. [@2] Auvaa Fualaga was caught with the drugs at Malaefatu on 10 April
2026. [@1] Motorcycle police found Fualaga at the Malaefatu area of Sogi in
April and charged him over 27 marijuana cigarettes from the prohibited marijuana
plant. [@3] According to the summary of facts, police officers noticed a plastic
packet containing suspected marijuana protruding from Fualaga’s left pocket.
[@1] As police escorted him from the area, Fualaga threw the packet towards a
nearby rubbish area. [@1] An officer retrieved the packet and found 27 marijuana
cigarettes weighing a total of 14.31 grams. [@1]

In delivering his sentence, Justice Leiataualesa Daryl Clarke noted that when
Fualaga was arrested in April, he had been on bail for possession of
methamphetamine. [@1] “This demonstrates a disregard for the law and for the
trust placed in you by the Court when you were granted bail,” Justice Clarke
said. [@1] The Court heard that Fualaga had since been sentenced to six months’
imprisonment in September 2026 for possession of methamphetamine and a drug
utensil. [@1] Justice Clarke identified the high quantity of marijuana, the
offending while on bail and the prevalence of the offending in Samoa as
aggravating factors. [@1] The Court also heard that Fualaga has four children
and a dependent partner. [@1]

Justice Clarke adopted a starting point of two years and six months’
imprisonment and deducted five months for the guilty plea. [@1] The final
sentence of 25 months’ imprisonment is to be served cumulatively with his
existing six-month prison sentence, bringing his combined sentences to 31
months. [@1] Radio Samoa reported that Fualaga was charged and pleaded guilty to
one count. [@3]
