Charlie Highmore, aged 23, of The Scaur, Longtown, was driving an Audi A4 on the
A7 near his home town on 7 July 2026. [@1] The vehicle came to the attention of
a police officer on mobile patrol who noticed the rear brake light was not
working, Suresh Basra told the court. [@1] Highmore's vehicle was pulled over
and he provided a roadside test which gave a positive reading for cannabis. [@1]
After his arrest and transfer to a police station, an evidential specimen was
analysed and showed the level of a psychoactive ingredient of the class B
controlled drug was above the legal driving limit. [@1]

At Carlisle Magistrates' Court Highmore admitted drug-driving. [@1] His defence
solicitor John Smith said he knew he would lose his licence that day and
expected to lose his job. [@1] Smith said Highmore did not expect to find
another job in the Longtown area because of a lack of transport links and would
pay the fine from his savings. [@1] District judge Philip Holden imposed a
12-month disqualification. [@1] Highmore must pay a £400 fine plus a £160
mandatory surcharge and £85 prosecution costs. [@1]
