Four occupants were arrested and lodged in the Bell County Detention Center.
[@2] The four were Morgan, Richard and Steven Allen and Dalton Hobbs, all
charged with trafficking in marijuana alongside carfentanil, fentanyl and
heroin, possession of fentanyl, buying or possessing drug paraphernalia and
engaging in organized crime. [@1] Morgan and Richard Allen have since bonded out
of the Bell County Detention Center, while Steven Allen and Dalton Hobbs remain
lodged there. [@1]

A search of a trailer yielded two baggies of marijuana. [@1] The same search
turned up 109 dosage units of suspected pressed fentanyl, nearly five ounces of
heroin, five containers of carfentanil, eight capsules of suspected fentanyl,
drug paraphernalia and cash. [@1]

Deputies began the investigation after Bell County EMS responded to an overdose
in the Hulen community on 11 November 2026. [@2] The sheriff's department said
Steven Allen had sold the overdose victim fentanyl. [@1] Richard and Morgan
Allen also lived in the trailer and had pooled money from drug sales with Steven
to buy the home after their original residence burned down. [@2] Steven Allen
was additionally charged with wanton endangerment in the first degree. [@2]
Dalton Hobbs was also charged on a bench warrant for contempt of court over
unpaid fines in four previous cases. [@2]
