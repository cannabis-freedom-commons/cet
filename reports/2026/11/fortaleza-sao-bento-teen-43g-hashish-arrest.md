A 19-year-old man was arrested and a 16-year-old boy was apprehended in the São
Bento neighbourhood of Fortaleza on Friday 20 November 2026. [@1] The action was
carried out by the 1º BPRAIO, the Ceará Military Police's intensive patrol
battalion. [@1] The team was on its way to the 30th Police District to present
an earlier drug-seizure case when officers saw four people, two adults and two
adolescents, leaving a car and stopped them. [@1] Officers found a 43-gram
portion of hashish beside the group. [@1] A search of the vehicle found a .38
revolver with six intact rounds, and officers also seized four packets of
marijuana, three mobile phones and the car. [@1]

The case was first presented at the Child and Adolescent Police Station (DCA),
where the police authority individualised each person's conduct. [@1] One
adolescent admitted possession of the drug and was apprehended for an act
analogous to drug trafficking. [@1] The other adolescent was heard as a witness
and handed to his guardian. [@1] The two adults were taken to the 30th Police
District. [@1] There the arrest of the man who claimed the weapon was ratified,
and he was charged with illegal possession of a permitted firearm. [@1] The
other adult was heard as a witness and released. [@1]
