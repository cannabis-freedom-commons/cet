Police raided a field beside Kala Pani lake at Datta Manjari on 17 November 2026
and seized more than 200 cannabis plants. [@1] Police put the seized cannabis at 120.45 kilograms, valued at a market price of 6,022,500 rupees. [@2] The field lies between Datta
Manjari and Shri Datta Shikhar Sansthan in dense forest by the lake and belongs
to Sunil Pawar. [@1] The police registered a case under the NDPS Act against the farmer, Sunil Namdev Pawar, as the accused. [@2] Police said they acted on a confidential tip that cannabis
was being cultivated in the field on a large scale. [@1,2] A team of Inspector
Devidas Chopde and Assistant Inspector Sharad Ghodke carried out the night raid
under the guidance of District Superintendent Nilabh Rohan and Sub-Divisional
Officer Kirankumar Bhondwe. [@1,2]

Officers found more than 200 cannabis plants in the field during the operation.
[@1] The plants were uprooted and seized in the presence of panch witnesses.
[@1] The operation continued until 11 at night. [@1] The preliminary estimate
puts the value of the seized material at more than one crore rupees. [@1]

Residents demanded a special-team inspection drive of suspect farms in the
taluka. [@1] Attention is on further investigation of the case after action on
two consecutive days brought the illegal cultivation issue to the fore in Mahur
taluka. [@1]
