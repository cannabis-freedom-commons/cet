Egypt's Interior Ministry kept up intensive traffic campaigns on main roads and
axes across the country. [@1] The campaigns recorded 107,714 traffic violations
in 24 hours. [@1] The recorded offences included driving without licences,
speeding, parking at random, using a mobile phone at the wheel and breaching
licence conditions. [@1] Officers tested 978 drivers for drug use and 40 were
positive, and legal measures were taken against them. [@1]

A parallel traffic campaign in work zones along the Regional Ring Road recorded
489 violations in 24 hours. [@1] Those violations included carrying passengers
against the law, breaching licence conditions and breaches of vehicle safety
requirements. [@1] Officers tested 154 drivers on the ring road and six were
positive for drug use. [@1]

Seven people wanted under nine court rulings were detained. [@1] Nine vehicles
were held for breaching traffic laws. [@1] The security bodies said legal
measures were taken over the recorded violations and that the campaigns would
continue on the roads and in the ring road work zones. [@1]
