Police from the interdepartmental directorate of the national police for
Haute-Corse (DIPN) arrested three men in Bastia on Thursday 19 November 2026
during a drug transaction on a public street. [@1] The three were caught in the
act and included the seller and the buyer. [@1] The seller was known to police
and his custody was extended. [@1,2] He was due to appear before the Bastia court on Monday 23 November under the comparution immédiate procedure, a fast-track hearing held shortly after arrest. [@2] The 35-year-old alleged seller, arrested in flagrante delicto for drug trafficking on a public street, was brought before the Bastia prosecutor on Saturday 21 November and placed in pre-trial detention. [@2] The buyer was summonsed to the Bastia
criminal court for drug use under an ordonnance pénale. [@1,2] An ordonnance
pénale is a simplified procedure for simple, low-level cases that allows a
defendant to be judged quickly without a hearing. [@1] The third man, who was
with the buyer, was released without charge. [@1,2]

Searches at the seller's home found about 100 grams of cannabis and 175 grams of
cocaine, Bastia public prosecutor Jean-Philippe Navarre said. [@1,2] In all,
nearly 300 grams of drugs were seized in the searches. [@1]

Drug-related cases have been multiplying every week at the Bastia police
station. [@1]
