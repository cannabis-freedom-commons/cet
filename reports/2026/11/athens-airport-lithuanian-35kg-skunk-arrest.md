Officers of the narcotics unit at Athens International Airport arrested a
28-year-old Lithuanian man after a check on his arrival in Greece. [@2,4,5,8,10,11,12,13,14] The operation on Tuesday 17 November 2026 was run by officers of the Drug Enforcement Subdirectorate of the Directorate for Organized Crime. [@7,9] Zougla reported that officers identified him during targeted checks at the airport's narcotics department, after cross-checks, data analysis and profiling. [@6,12] He was
stopped in the airport's arrivals area after checks by the Hellenic Police,
known in Greece as EL.AS. [@1,7] He had landed the previous day, 17 November 2026,
on a flight from Bangkok via Doha. [@3,4,5,8,10,13,14]

His luggage held 23 packages of raw cannabis from hydroponic cultivation, the
drug Greek police call skunk. [@2,4,5,7,8,9,10,12,13,14] He had hidden them in his suitcases. [@1,12] The
packages had a total gross weight of 35 kilograms and 300 grams. [@2,4,5,7,8,9,10,12,13,14] Police put
the quantity at more than 35 kilograms. [@3,7,12] Officers also seized a mobile phone
and the two suitcases used to carry the drug. [@2,4,5,7,8,9,10,12,13,14]

The arrested man was taken to the prosecutor. [@3,4,5,7,8,9,10,12,13,14]
