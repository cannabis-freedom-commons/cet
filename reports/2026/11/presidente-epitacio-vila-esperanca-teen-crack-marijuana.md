A 17-year-old was apprehended for an act analogous to drug trafficking on the
night of Sunday 15 November 2026 in Vila Esperança, Presidente Epitácio, São
Paulo. [@1] Tactical Force officers of the 42nd Interior Military Police
Battalion reached the teenager after receiving information about drug sales in
the area. [@2]

Officers found nothing on the teenager, but in a vacant lot he had jumped into
they found a bag holding several crack portions, a raw crack stone, marijuana
portions and a precision scale. [@1] When questioned, the teenager admitted the
drugs were his and that he was selling them. [@1]

He was taken to the police station, where the apprehension for an act analogous
to drug trafficking was ratified, and he remained at the disposal of the courts.
[@2]
