A 20-year-old man from Neuburg was stopped by police on his e-scooter in
Münchener Straße at about 11.35 on Saturday 21 November 2026. [@2] Officers
noticed signs typical of drug use in the rider during the check. [@1] The police
account says he had apparently consumed drugs before setting off on the ride.
[@1]

A rapid test carried out at the roadside was positive for THC, the psychoactive
substance in cannabis, and police ordered a blood sample to be taken. [@1] The
rider now faces a regulatory fine procedure and a driving ban. [@1]
