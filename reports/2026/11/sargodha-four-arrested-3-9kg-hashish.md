Sargodha police arrested four suspects during separate operations conducted in
different areas of Sargodha, Punjab, on Saturday 14 November 2026. [@1] Teams
from various police stations conducted raids within their respective
jurisdictions to make the arrests. [@1] A police spokesperson said the arrests
followed the separate raids in different areas. [@1] Police identified the four
suspects by single names as Waqas, Amir, Khawar and Ilyas. [@1]

Officers recovered 3.9 kilograms of hashish from the suspects' possession during
the operations. [@1] Officers also recovered a 30-bore pistol, described as an
illegal weapon, from the suspects' possession during the same operations. [@1]

Police registered separate cases against the accused following the arrests and
recoveries. [@1] Further legal proceedings were underway at the time of the
report. [@1]
