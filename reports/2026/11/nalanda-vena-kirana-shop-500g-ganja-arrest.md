Vena police station officers in Nalanda district, Bihar, arrested Awadhesh
Prasad, the operator of a grocery shop in Vena Bazar, after 500 grams of ganja
was found in the shop. [@1] Prasad lives in Vena village. [@1]

The shop stands in the market of Vena village in the Rahui area of Nalanda
district. [@1]

Police were investigating the network linked to the buying and selling of the
ganja. [@1]

A photograph published with the account shows the arrested man in police
custody. [@1]
