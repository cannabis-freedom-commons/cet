Mexico City citizen-security officers detained a man identified as the suspected
perpetrator of abandoning a six-month-old baby girl in the streets of Gustavo A.
Madero borough. [@1,2] Police learned of the abandonment in colonia Villa de
Guadalupe, where the baby was sheltered and received medical care. [@1] The baby
was later transferred to a Public Ministry agency specializing in care for
minors. [@1]

Officers working with Centro de Comando y Control (C2) staff reviewed nearby
surveillance cameras and identified a man who allegedly left the child and
walked away. [@1,2] Image tracking indicated the suspect had moved toward colonia
Centro in Cuauhtémoc borough, so officers set up patrols and surveillance to
find him. [@1] On Calle Parque San Juan officers located a man whose physical
features matched the person seen on the cameras. [@1,2]

When he saw police he tried to escape but was intercepted metres ahead. [@1,2] During the detention in Cuauhtémoc, the man was found with 100 small plastic bags of apparent marijuana and cash. [@2] The
50-year-old man was detained with the seized items and presented to the Public
Ministry agent to determine his legal situation. [@1,2]
