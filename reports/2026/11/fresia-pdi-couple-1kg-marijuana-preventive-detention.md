Detectives of the PDI's anti-narcotics brigade in Puerto Montt arrested a couple
in Fresia on Thursday 19 November 2026 on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@3]
The two sold drugs from their home in Fresia and used a car as a delivery
service for customers in the town. [@2]

Detectives seized 1 kg of marijuana and 1 kg of cocaine at the property. [@1]
Officers also seized a car and a firearm classed as subject to controls. [@1]
Other local reports put the haul at more than 2.3 kg of cannabis and cocaine
hydrochloride together, valued at about 18 million pesos. [@2] Chile's Public
Prosecutor's Office formalised both detainees for offences under Ley 20.000, the
drug law, and for breaking the arms law. [@1] The court granted preventive
detention after finding that their freedom posed a danger to public safety. [@1]

Puerto Varas prosecutor Nathalie Yonsson said the case began with complaints
from residents. [@1] She said the operation was part of an investigative plan to
take drugs and firearms out of the communes of the jurisdiction. [@1] The
prosecutor's office said joint work with the PDI and municipal security teams
would continue and urged residents to report drug trafficking and illegal
firearms. [@1]
