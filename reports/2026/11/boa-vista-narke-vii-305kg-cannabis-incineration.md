The Roraima Civil Police incinerated approximately 315 kilograms of drugs on
Thursday 19 November 2026, in its second destruction action of the year and as
part of the national Operação NARKE VII. [@2] The action took place at the
Cerâmica Aliança and was part of Operação NARKE VII, which ran from 26 October
to 20 November. [@1] Police valued the destroyed material at more than 20
million reais on the illegal market. [@1]

Of the total incinerated, about 10 kilograms was cocaine and 305 kilograms were
cannabis derivatives, including marijuana, skunk and hashish. [@1] Other
presentations destroyed included pasty and oily marijuana. [@1] About 200
kilograms of concentrated cannabis derivatives among the destroyed drugs had
been seized on 10 November during a DRE operation in Cinturão Verde, when three
men were arrested in flagrante. [@1] That load included hashish, dry and ICE,
substances with a high THC concentration. [@1] The rest came from seizures by
DRE, the central police station, capital districts and interior police units.
[@1]

Deputy police chief Jimmy Santana said the incineration followed legal
procedures, with judicial authorization and a favourable opinion from the public
prosecutor's office. [@1] A portion of the drugs was kept for forensic analysis
to confirm their nature before destruction. [@1] Denarc director Herbert de
Amorim Cardoso said the removal represented a financial loss for criminal
organisations. [@1] DRE chief Júlio Cesar da Rocha said the seizures resulted
from investigations by different police teams and had also led to arrests. [@1]
