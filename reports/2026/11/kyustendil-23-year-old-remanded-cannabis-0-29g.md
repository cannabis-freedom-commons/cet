The Kyustendil district prosecutor's office opened pre-trial proceedings for
unlawful possession of a high-risk narcotic substance. [@1] A 23-year-old man
identified only as D.N. was charged with holding cannabis without the required
permit in a Kyustendil home on 19 November 2026. [@1] A second account of the
prosecutor's statement confirmed the 23-year-old was charged over cannabis held
in an apartment in Kyustendil on 19 November 2026. [@3] Investigators carried
out witness interviews, a search and seizure, a field drug test on the substance
and a forensic examination to establish its exact type and composition. [@1]

The prosecutor's office detained D.N. for up to 72 hours and applied to the
court for the strictest restriction measure, detention in custody. [@1] The
Kyustendil district court remanded the 23-year-old in custody, according to the
prosecutor's office. [@2] Prosecutors said the man had been convicted of the
same type of offence in 2025. [@1] Prosecutor's spokesperson Radostina Stoyanova
said the 2025 probation period had not expired, that the offence carries one to
six years' imprisonment and a fine of 2,000 to 10,000 leva, and that the
quantity of the substance was 0.29 grams. [@2]
