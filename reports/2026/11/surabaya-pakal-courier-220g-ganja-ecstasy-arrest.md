ZA, a 24-year-old resident of Pakal in Surabaya, was placed in Polrestabes
Surabaya custody. [@1,2] Officers moved at two locations, a house in the Sidorejo area of Pakal village and a boarding room in the Babat Jerawat area of Pakal district. [@2,3,4] Unit 3 of Satresnarkoba Polrestabes Surabaya carried out the arrest on Wednesday 11 November. [@2,3,4] Narcotics chief AKBP Dodi Pratama said ZA was invited to
become a courier and was paid each time he delivered cannabis and ecstasy. [@1]
ZA said he met the dealer above him, identified only as K, while drinking coffee
with a friend at a stall near his house. [@1] Police said ZA acted on the orders of a person with the initials KBR, who is now on the wanted list. [@2,3,4] Police said ZA had worked as a
courier for two months. [@1,3,4]

Police seized five small packets of cannabis weighing 220.020 grams, plus
fragments of ecstasy pills. [@1,2,3,4] The evidence list also included a partly smoked cannabis joint weighing 0.012 grams. [@3] Police also listed a bundle of empty plastic clip bags and a mobile phone among the seized evidence. [@4] ZA last received 500 grams of cannabis from K
in front of a campus in the Babat Jerawat area of Surabaya, took it home and
repacked it. [@1,2,4] According to the suspect's statement to investigators, the activity began on Friday 30 October at about 20.00. [@2] The cannabis was divided into 10 packets weighing 50 grams
each to await further orders from K. [@1,2,3,4] ZA also collected 200 ecstasy pills by
dead drop in the Dukuh Kupang area and forwarded them immediately. [@1] Another courier collected the ecstasy package the next day, Tuesday 10 November, at about 17.00. [@3,4] ZA collected the ecstasy at Jalan Dukuh Kupang on Monday 9 November. [@3,4] He was
paid Rp 20,000 for each cannabis delivery point and Rp 300,000 for delivering
the 200 ecstasy pills. [@1] In total he received Rp 2.7 million from the dealer,
which police seized along with other evidence. [@1,3,4] ZA told investigators he only carried out KBR's orders and denied dealing directly with buyers. [@3]

Police charged him under Article 114(1) subsidiary Article 111(1) of the 2009
Narcotics Law as amended in 2026, and Article 609(1)(a) of the 2023 Criminal
Code as amended in 2026. [@1,4]
