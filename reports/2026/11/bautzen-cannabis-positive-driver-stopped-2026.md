A 31-year-old German man driving his Toyota was stopped by a patrol from the
Bautzen police station on Thomas-Müntzer-Straße in Bautzen, Saxony, on Saturday
21 November 2026. [@1] During the check the officers carried out a drug test,
which reacted positively for cannabis. [@3] Police banned him from driving on
and ordered a blood sample. [@1] The patrol opened an administrative offence
proceeding (Ordnungswidrigkeitenverfahren) against him. [@2]

The police gave the time of the check as 01:35 to 02:00 on 21 November 2026.
[@1] The fine office (Bußgeldstelle) took over further handling of the case.
[@2] The police account reports the roadside test but gives no result of the
blood sample, no fine amount and no decision date. [@1] No cannabis was found or
weighed at the check, according to the police account. [@1]

The case is being handled as a regulatory traffic offence rather than a criminal
prosecution. [@1] The driver was identified only by his age and German
nationality. [@2]
