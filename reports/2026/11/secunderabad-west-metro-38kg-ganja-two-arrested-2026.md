Excise State Task Force team police arrested two men on Thursday who had been
moving ganja by train from Odisha state to the Secunderabad area. [@1] STF staff
arrested the pair near the Secunderabad West Metro station while the accused
were waiting there for the people who were supposed to take the ganja they had
brought with them. [@1] The accused were named as Nilesh and Tufen, and officers
handed both men together with the ganja to the Malkajgiri excise station. [@1]

Officers seized ganja packets weighing 38 kilograms from the possession of the
two accused men. [@1] The officers said the seized ganja consignment was worth
almost 19 lakh rupees. [@1]

Circle Inspector Venkateshwarlu and Sub-Inspector Manju shared these details of
the operation and the seizure. [@1] Disha reported the case from Tirumalagiri.
[@1] The paper filed it under its Medchal district news. [@1] The account was
published on 19 November 2026, the Thursday on which the arrests were reported
to have taken place. [@1]
