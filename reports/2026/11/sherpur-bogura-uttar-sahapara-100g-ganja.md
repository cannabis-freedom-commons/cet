Police in Sherpur upazila of Bogura detained 20-year-old Joy Mohanta with 100
grams of ganja. [@1] Mohanta is the son of Mithu Mohanta of Uttar Sahapara
village in the upazila. [@1] Officers detained him at 12:20pm on Thursday 19
November 2026 in the Uttar Sahapara area of Sherpur municipality. [@2]

Acting on a secret tip, a team led by Sub-Inspector Md. Tofazzal Hossain raided
the three-road junction in Uttar Sahapara village on the orders of Sherpur
police station officer-in-charge Md. Ekramul Hossain. [@1] They detained Mohanta
in front of a grocery shop belonging to a man named Nikhil and recovered 100
grams of ganja from his possession. [@1]

Police filed a case at Sherpur police station under the relevant sections of the
Narcotics Control Act. [@1] He was later sent to jail at 11am under the
narcotics law. [@2] The officer in charge said the accused was sent to jail
through the court after a case under section 19(a) of the Narcotics Control Act,
2018. [@3]
