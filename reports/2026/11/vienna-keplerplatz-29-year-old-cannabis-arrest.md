Police arrested a 29-year-old suspected drug dealer on Monday evening at
Keplerplatz in Favoriten, Vienna, after allegedly catching him selling drugs in
the act. [@1] The suspect is a 29-year-old Algerian national. [@1]

Police said they found seven bags of cannabis herb in the subsequent search.
[@1] Police said they also found 4.8 grams of cannabis resin. [@1] In the same
search, police found 1.12 grams of cocaine alongside the cannabis. [@1] Officers
also seized 220 euros in cash. [@1]

The man was issued a ban from the Keplerplatz protection zone, a police zone
allowing preventive removal of suspected offenders. [@1] The Vienna State
Criminal Police Office, South branch, took over further investigations. [@1]
