Police arrested a driver from Hura and took him to Rahat station for
questioning. [@1] Balad MK Hassan Nsassa confronted the deputy commander of
Dimona station during the Negev detention. [@1] Nsassa said he was passing by,
saw a young Hura resident lying on the pavement being beaten, stopped his car
and intervened to stop what he described as an assault. [@1] A second account
says the confrontation happened in the morning near Beit Kama after the MK
claimed officers were beating and humiliating a handcuffed detainee by the
roadside. [@2]

According to police, an officer saw the driver weaving between vehicles and
allegedly committing traffic offences and ordered him to stop at the side of the
road. [@1] The officer smelled drugs and a search of the car found cannabis,
according to police. [@1] The car was impounded for 30 days. [@1]

Police say they treat driving under the influence of drugs and alcohol on Negev
roads severely, where many fatal crashes have occurred for that reason. [@1]
Nsassa said the men involved refused to identify themselves and that he plans to
file a complaint over the incident. [@1]
