A 22-year-old man was out in the Ampelokipoi area on the afternoon of last
Thursday, 19 November 2026, an outing the account presents as one he will
remember. [@1] He ran into a patrolling team of DIAS police officers, who
noticed that he visibly changed colour as soon as he saw them. [@1] The officers
decided to carry out a check on him and found a small quantity of cannabis in
his possession. [@1]

Officers then searched his home together with officers of the Ampelokipoi
Department for the Prosecution and Clearance of Crimes. [@1] The home search
produced an additional seizure, on top of the small quantity found on him during
the street check. [@1] In the home, officers found and seized 1 kilogram and 225
grams of raw cannabis. [@1]

During the same home search, officers also seized an electronic precision scale
and the sum of 50 euros in cash. [@1] The Ampelokipoi department drew up a case
file against the arrested man. [@1] He was then brought before the competent
prosecutor, accused of violating the legislation on addictive substances. [@1]
