Police in Kamrej, in Gujarat's Surat district, arrested Satyam alias Pankaj
Dubey during a raid on the outskirts of Umbhel village. [@2] The raid followed a
tip-off received by an assistant sub-inspector of the Kamrej police. [@2]
Officers stopped Dubey on the canal road near Om Industries, behind Mahadev
Hotel, where he was riding a Suzuki Burgman motorcycle without a number plate,
and found ganja on him. [@2]

Police valued the ganja at ₹44,700. [@2] Police put the total value of the
seized goods at ₹74,700, including the motorcycle. [@1] The motorcycle was
valued at ₹30,000. [@2] Questioning showed that Dubey got the ganja from Ranjit
Thakor and sold it loose, police said. [@2]

Police described Thakor as the main supplier, declared him wanted and said they
had begun further investigation to catch him. [@2]
