Excise officers arrested a youth in Varavoor with 1.136 kg of cannabis. [@1,2,3] The accused was produced in court and remanded. [@3] Assistant excise inspectors, a preventive officer and civil excise officers named in the account were also in the search team. [@2] The search team was led by Vadakkanchery Excise Range Inspector A. B. Thomas. [@2,3] The
person held was identified as Sanjay of Ganapathy Parambil, a Varavoor native.
[@1,2,3] Sanjay was 27 years old and his house is at Avasherimukku, Varavoor. [@2]

Excise had kept him under observation for the previous few days after a tip that
he was selling intoxicants on a large scale. [@1] Officers found the cannabis
during a search of his house. [@1,2] The accused said the cannabis had been kept
for sale centred on festivals in the area. [@1] The accused brought cannabis from another state and sold it in Varavoor, Thali and Mullurkkara. [@3]
