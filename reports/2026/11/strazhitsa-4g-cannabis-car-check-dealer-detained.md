Police in Strazhitsa, Veliko Tarnovo Province, stopped a passenger car carrying
three residents of Asenovo village on the afternoon of 25 November 2026. [@1]
The check was carried out by officers of the police station in Strazhitsa. [@1]
Investigators established that the drug found in the car had been bought
immediately before the stop from a 55-year-old man. [@1] The man was a resident
of Strazhitsa. [@2] He was detained for up to 24 hours under the Ministry of
Internal Affairs Act. [@1]

During the check officers found and seized about 4 grams of plant mass. [@1] A
field narcotest reacted to cannabis. [@1] Police described the seized material
as about 4 grams of cannabis. [@2]

Pre-trial proceedings, or досъдебно производство, were opened in the case and
work on them continued. [@1]
