At about 20:15 on Wednesday 18 November 2026, two people were arrested by police
near the maison d'arrêt de Dijon, on rue d'Auxonne in the Faubourg-Sud quarter.
[@1] The arrests were carried out by the brigade anticriminalité (Bac), a police
anti-crime brigade. [@1] The state major of the Direction interdépartementale de
la police nationale for Côte-d'Or (DIPN 21) said the two were men aged 20, one
from Toulon and one from Lyon. [@1]

According to the same statement, the two used two drones to try to deliver drugs
into the prison perimeter, the substance in this case being cannabis resin. [@1]
After they were intercepted by law enforcement agents, both suspects were placed
in garde à vue, police custody. [@1]

The investigation continues and the two young men will subsequently have to
answer for the acts they are accused of before the justice system. [@1]
