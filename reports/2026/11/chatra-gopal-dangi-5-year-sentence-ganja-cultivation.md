A special court in Chatra sentenced a man to five years of rigorous imprisonment
for illegal cultivation of ganja in 2023. [@1,2] The convicting court was the Principal District and Sessions Court-cum-Special Court. [@2] The court also penalised him Rs
25,000 for the offence. [@1,2] If he does not pay the fine, he must serve three more months in prison. [@2] He was tried and convicted under sections of the
NDPS Act. [@1] Special Public Prosecutor Amit Kumar Srivastava identified the
convict as Gopal Dangi, a man in his early thirties. [@1] He is a resident of
the same village. [@1]

Srivastava said Dangi was caught cultivating ganja and arrested on the spot
during a raid at Kadlanga village under Sadar police station limits on 18
December 2023. [@1,2] The raid was led by then-trainee DSP Wasim Raza acting on secret information. [@2] A case was subsequently registered against Dangi and legal
proceedings began. [@1] The case was registered as case number 416/2023 at Sadar police station. [@2] Kadlanga village is under Sadar police station limits.
[@1]

The prosecution examined seven witnesses during the trial, while the defence
produced four witnesses. [@1,2] Srivastava added the witness figures when
describing the trial. [@1] The sentencing was reported on 19 November 2026. [@1] The special court convicted and sentenced Dangi on 19 November 2026 after hearing both sides and examining the evidence. [@2]
Srivastava spoke as special public prosecutor in the case. [@1]
