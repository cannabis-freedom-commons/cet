The Vizianagaram district police invoked the Preventive Detention (PD) Act
against two more people for their involvement in the cannabis trade. [@1] The
two were Vanapalli Balaram (37), also known as Potnuru Balaram or Budda, of
Lankapatnam in Vizianagaram town, and Tangeti Sharath (35), a Vizianagaram
native living in Visakhapatnam. [@1] Police said the two men frequently
transported ganja and took part in anti-social activities. [@2] Superintendent
of police A.R. Damodhar said officers counselled them several times to give up
criminal tendencies, but there was no change. [@1]

Superintendent Damodhar announced the detentions on Saturday 21 November 2026.
[@2] Damodhar said police invoked the PD Act against the two and moved them to
the central prison in Visakhapatnam. [@1] He said the PD Act had been
implemented against 22 people so far for involvement in illegal activities and
the ganja trade, and that those held could not get bail for up to a year. [@1]

Police warned that strict legal action was inevitable against those who
repeatedly committed crimes by illegally transporting and selling cannabis in
the district. [@1]
