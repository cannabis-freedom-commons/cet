Police identified the two people in the stop as 37-year-old Ashley Neels of
Hubert and 35-year-old Denzel Faines of Jacksonville. [@1] Officers with the
Kinston Police Department's D-Squad made the stop around 9:29 p.m. on 23
November 2026 in the 1300 block of West Vernon Avenue. [@1] Both individuals
were taken into custody. [@1] Both were arrested and taken to the Lenoir County
Jail. [@2]

During the stop officers reported discovering fentanyl, marijuana and drug
paraphernalia. [@1] Neels was charged with fentanyl and drug paraphernalia
possession. [@2] Neels was booked under a $30,000 bond. [@2] Faines was charged
with simple possession of a Schedule VI controlled substance and resist,
obstruct and delay. [@2]

Neels remained in custody at the Lenoir County Jail. [@1] Faines was released
after posting a secured bond. [@1] Court records showed Faines tried to pull
away while officers were trying to search the car. [@2] Faines posted a $750
bond. [@2]
