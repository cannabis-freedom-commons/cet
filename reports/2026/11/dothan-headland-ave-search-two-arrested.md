Narcotics investigators executed a search warrant at 601 Headland Ave. on 18
November 2026 as part of an ongoing investigation. [@1] Two people were arrested
after investigators recovered narcotics during a search at a Dothan home on
Wednesday, according to the Houston County Sheriff’s Office. [@1] The sheriff’s
office said various narcotics were recovered upon executing the search warrant.
[@1]

Bryon Keith Ford was charged with manufacturing a controlled substance,
first-degree possession of marijuana and tampering with physical evidence. [@1]
Jeanette Marie Thomas was charged with first-degree possession of marijuana.
[@1] Ford’s listed charges include manufacturing and tampering allegations in
addition to the marijuana possession allegation. [@1]

According to the sheriff’s office, both people arrested were being held at the
Houston County Jail. [@1] The Houston County Sheriff’s Office was the agency
that reported the Dothan search, the narcotics recovery and the arrests. [@1]
