The DANSAF team and the Toofan squad together caught four young men with 150
grams of cannabis in the Karinkallummoozhi area, within Erumeli police station
limits in Kottayam district. [@1] Pathanamthitta Media reported that the four
were arrested after a check in that area. [@2] The inspection followed secret
information, and two of those held are from Karinkallummoozhi. [@1]

Sathyam Online named them as Erumeli natives Aromal Saji Unni, aged 26, Sajan
Johnson, 28, and Niyas Nizar, 28, and Nattakam native Nandu Suresh Kannan, 26.
[@1] Pathanamthitta Media identified their houses as Padinjarethadam in
Karinkallummoozhi, Kadavumkal, Valiyapurackal in Charala, and Parapparambil at
Puthenpalam in Nattakam. [@2] Kanjirappally Reporters described Aromal Saji Unni
and Sajan Johnson as natives of Kanakappalam, and Niyas Nizar as from Charala in
Erumeli. [@3]

With the Sabarimala pilgrimage season approaching, police and anti-drug squads
have continued intensive checks in the Erumeli area. [@1] Police decided to make
checks in the area stricter in the coming days. [@1] Residents allege that large
drug networks are concentrating on Erumeli ahead of the pilgrimage. [@1]
