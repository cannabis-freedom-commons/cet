West Yorkshire Police arrested two men following drugs raids in Keighley. [@1]
Officers executed warrants at three addresses. [@1] Police seized a large
quantity of pre-packaged cannabis, suspected Class A drugs and a significant sum
of cash. [@2]

A spokesperson for West Yorkshire Police said information from the local
community helped make the raids happen. [@1] The spokesperson said every piece
of intelligence matters. [@1] Police said the information people share helps
officers target those suspected of supplying drugs and take action in
neighbourhoods. [@1] The force thanked everyone who continues to speak to
police. [@2]
