Military police teams from Gepar, a unit of the 24th Military Police Battalion,
arrested five people in flagrante on suspicion of drug trafficking in the Jardim
Primavera neighbourhood of Varginha at about 18:25 on Thursday 12 November 2026.
[@1] The detention came during a preventive patrol when officers saw a group try
to flee into a residence on the approach of the patrol car. [@1] During the
approach, one of the suspects discarded a backpack containing drugs. [@1]

Searches at the scene recovered four marijuana bars, additional portions of the
drug and 36 buchas already individually wrapped for sale. [@1] The team also
seized a precision scale, mobile phones and R$20 in cash. [@1] Those detained
were four men aged 18, 20, 23 and 34 and a 35-year-old woman. [@1] After routine
medical care, all five were taken with the seized material to the Varginha Civil
Police station, where the flagrante was formalised. [@1]
