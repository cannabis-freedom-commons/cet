Police in Ulcinj processed three people, two of whom were deprived of their
liberty, in a drive against street selling and drug abuse. [@1] Acting on a
court order, officers searched a residential building in the Štoj neighbourhood
used by a Kosovo citizen and found a bag of marijuana, a precision weighing
scale and other items. [@1] The state prosecutor at the Higher State
Prosecutor's Office in Podgorica ordered criminal charges against the Kosovo
citizen for unauthorised production, possession and distribution of narcotic
drugs. [@1]

During a search of a man from Ulcinj, police found two PVC packages containing a
white powder that was determined to be cocaine. [@1] The prosecutor treated that
case as a misdemeanour under Article 52 of the Law on Prevention of Drug Abuse
and ordered the man brought before the misdemeanour court in Ulcinj. [@1] At
Kodre, officers inspected a Mercedes driven by a man from Ulcinj with a Serbian
citizen from Belgrade in the passenger seat. [@1] A search of the Serbian
citizen revealed marijuana. [@1]

After the misdemeanour proceedings the Serbian citizen was found guilty, and the
foreigner inspector cancelled his residence permit and banned him from entering
Montenegro. [@1]
