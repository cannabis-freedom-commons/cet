An operation in Arauco, in Chile's Biobío Region, left 22 people detained and
more than 20 kilos of drugs seized. [@1] The operation, named Cofradía del
Golfo, was carried out by the Modelo Territorial Cero (MT-0) team of the Lebu
Criminal Investigation Brigade (Bicrim), coordinated with the Arauco local
prosecutor's office, and searched about twenty properties. [@1] It followed a
months-long investigation by the Investigations Police (PDI) and the Public
Prosecutor's Office that identified a criminal organization dedicated to drug
trafficking. [@2]

Officers seized 20 kilos 730 grams of cocaine base, 49.74 grams of cocaine
hydrochloride, 253.54 grams of processed cannabis and 14 cannabis plants, with
all the substances valued at more than 313 million pesos. [@1] They also took
six vehicles, 12 mobile phones and 22 million pesos in cash. [@1] The regional
prosecutor said it was the largest drug seizure ever recorded in the commune.
[@1]

More than 170 officers and over 50 police vehicles took part, said inspector
Carolina Namor, head of the PDI's Biobío police region. [@1] She described the
trafficking groups as operating collaboratively, sharing territory without
hierarchies or disputes. [@1] The 22 detainees' custody was extended to Friday
morning, when they were due to be formally charged before the Arauco Guarantee
Court. [@2]
