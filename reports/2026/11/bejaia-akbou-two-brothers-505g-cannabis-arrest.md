Officers of the Koundouza urban police in Akbou, Bejaia, arrested two young men
aged 31 and 25 on suspicion of involvement in a network selling drugs and
psychotropic substances in the Eucalyptus neighbourhood near a primary school.
[@1] Investigators said the two suspects used their home to store drugs before
distribution. [@1]

Officers searched the home in coordination with the competent judicial
authorities and seized drugs and other items. [@1] The search seized 505 grams
of processed Indian hemp. [@1] Officers also seized 3,992 capsules of
foreign-made pregabalin. [@1] Officers also seized 15 grams of cocaine. [@1]
Officers also seized more than 57 million centimes plus an amount in foreign
currency. [@1]

Officers also seized a precision electronic scale. [@1] Officers also seized a
knife bearing traces of drugs. [@1] Officers also seized a motorcycle used for
trips linked to dealing. [@1] Officers also seized a phone SIM card registered
to another person. [@1] Examination of the main suspect's mobile phone found
photos and videos described as incriminating evidence confirming his alleged
involvement. [@1] After legal procedures the two detainees were presented to the
competent judicial authorities, the main suspect was remanded in temporary
detention and his brother was provisionally released pending trial. [@1]
