A 41-year-old woman was arrested in Carson City on Sunday on a felony warrant
alleging child abuse or neglect, according to a Carson City Sheriff’s Office
booking report. [@1] A Carson City sheriff’s deputy served the warrant. [@1] The
outlet withheld the woman’s name because of the allegation and the victim’s age
to protect the victim’s identity. [@1]

The criminal complaint accuses her of willfully, on or about 22 September 2026,
causing a child under 18 to suffer unjustified physical pain or mental suffering
through abuse or neglect or placing the child in a situation where that could
occur. [@1] The complaint alleges she provided the juvenile with access to
marijuana or otherwise encouraged or enabled his use of the substance at a North
Saliman Road address. [@1]

Bail in the case was set at $20,000. [@1] The account comes from a weekend crime
log based on Carson City Sheriff’s Office probable-cause reports. [@1]
