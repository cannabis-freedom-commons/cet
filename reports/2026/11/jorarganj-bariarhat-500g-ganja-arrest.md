Police in Jorarganj, Mirsharai, Chattogram, arrested a man with 500 grams of
ganja in one of several operations on 17 November 2026. [@1] The operations were
led by the station's officer in charge, Mohammad Abdul Halim. [@1] Acting on a
tip-off, a team led by sub-inspector Md Abdul Alim stopped the man on the paved
road in front of the police box at Imampara, ward 1 of Bariarhat municipality.
[@1] He was identified as Md Riaz Uddin, 36. [@1]

Officers searched him and recovered 500 grams of ganja. [@1] Police filed a case
against him under the Narcotics Control Act. [@1] Riaz Uddin is the son of the
late Abdur Rashid, from Chairman Ghat in Harani union 7 of Hatia upazila,
Noakhali. [@1] The officer in charge said the arrested suspects had been
produced before the court under police guard and that such operations would
continue to keep order in the area. [@1]
