The Korkinsky police department finished the criminal case against a 47-year-old
local resident. [@1] Police said the man found a plot where wild cannabis grew.
[@3] Detectives detained him soon afterwards and took him to the police station.
[@3] The plant-based substance seized from him was sent for testing, which
confirmed it weighed more than 130 grams. [@2]

The Chelyabinsk regional police press service said the man had collected the
cannabis for himself. [@1] The case was opened under part 2 of article 228 of
the Criminal Code. [@2] He had earlier convictions for evading administrative
supervision and for repeatedly failing to obey court-imposed restrictions. [@3]
The case went to Korkinsky city court. [@3]
