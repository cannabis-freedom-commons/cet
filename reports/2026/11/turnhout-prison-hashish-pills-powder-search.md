The Turnhout police zone carried out a targeted drug control inside the local
prison in Turnhout, an operation the force announced on Monday. [@1] The control
was conducted together with prison staff and with the help of a sniffer dog.
[@1] Officers searched several cells in the prison, with the cells chosen for
search by the scent picked up by the dog. [@1]

After completing the searches of the selected cells, officers carried out checks
of 15 detainees held in the prison. [@1] Two of the 15 detainees checked were
found to be carrying drugs directly on their persons during the checks. [@1]
During the cell searches, investigators recovered hashish in loose, bulk form
together with pills and pink powder. [@1]

The hashish represents the cannabis component of this mixed seizure, while the
pills and pink powder stand as separately listed substances. [@1] Finding two
carriers among the detainees checked shows contraband drugs reaching people held
inside the Turnhout prison. [@1]
