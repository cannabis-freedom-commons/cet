Shock teams of the 14th Military Police Battalion inspected an internet-goods
carrier in Foz do Iguaçu on the afternoon of 18 November 2026. [@2] Sniffer dogs
signalled suspect boxes stored in the carrier before officers opened them. [@1]

Officers found an AK-47 rifle, 30 rifle magazines, 21 kg of marijuana, 10 kg of
hashish and 19 slimming pens in the parcels. [@1] The parcels were addressed to
Bahia, the Federal District, Goiás, Minas Gerais and Rio Grande do Sul. [@1]

The carrier had already been subject to other drug seizures, which prompted the
new inspection. [@1] All the seized material was sent to the Federal Police
station in Foz do Iguaçu for further procedures. [@1]
