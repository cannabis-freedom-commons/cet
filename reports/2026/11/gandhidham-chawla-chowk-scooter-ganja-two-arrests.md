Police in Gandhidham carried out an operation against the trafficking of ganja
and foreign liquor. [@1] The ganja side of the operation was handled by officers
of A Division police. [@1] Near the Shiv temple at Chawla Chowk, those officers
found a quantity of ganja being carried on a scooter. [@1] Two people were
arrested in connection with that scooter seizure. [@1]

Separately, officers of B Division police acted near the Good Side bridge, where
290 bottles of foreign liquor were recovered from a car. [@1] The published
account presents the car liquor recovery as distinct from the scooter ganja
seizure, with each attributed to a different police division. [@1] Both reported
locations, Chawla Chowk and the Good Side bridge, are given as places within
Gandhidham. [@1]

Only the scooter seizure and its two arrests form the cannabis account here, and
the liquor bottles are not counted as cannabis. [@1]
