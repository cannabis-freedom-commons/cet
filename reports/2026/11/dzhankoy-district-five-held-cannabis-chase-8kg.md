Police in Dzhankoy district, Crimea, detained five local residents suspected of
attempting to sell marijuana after a road chase. [@1] The van drew the attention
of a traffic-police crew on the highway, its driver ignored the order to stop
and tried to flee, and during the chase the group threw seven bags of cannabis
out of the van before leaving the vehicle in a forest belt and hiding. [@1]
Police later found all the discarded load. [@1] All five were detained at the
41-year-old man's place of residence. [@1]

The group comprised a 41-year-old villager, his 37-year-old partner, two
relatives aged 36 and 50, and a 19-year-old acquaintance, who police say agreed
to gather field cannabis and prepare it for sale on the peninsula. [@1] They
drove to the field at night, cut the bushes, packed them into seven polymer
bags, loaded them into the Gazel van and planned to take them to the 41-year-old
man's yard to dry and divide. [@1] An expert examination confirmed the plant
mass was cannabis weighing more than eight kilograms in dried equivalent. [@2]

The investigative department of the Dzhankoy inter-municipal police division
opened a criminal case on attempted large-scale drug sale under part 3 of
article 30 and part 4 of article 228.1 of the Criminal Code. [@1] Four suspects
were placed in custody and one under house arrest. [@1] An inquiry officer
separately adopted an unspecified procedural decision under part 1 of article
231 on illegal cultivation of drug-containing plants, leaving open who planted
the field cannabis. [@3]
