The Guardia Civil detained one person who was found on the roof of a house in
Poniente almeriense while trying to flee during the operation. [@2] The detainee
was placed at the disposal of the courts as the suspected author of drug
cultivation or production and electricity fraud. [@1]

Officers seized 941 marijuana plants with a total weight of 61.81 kg. [@2]
Officers also seized 160 grams of chopped cannabis. [@2] During the search
officers seized indoor-growing equipment including lamps, reflectors,
air-conditioning units, fans, LED lighting systems, electrical panels,
climate-control equipment, a dehumidifier and air-conditioning compressors. [@2]

The action took place in October as part of operation Ketamar, with officers
from the Roquetas de Mar post supported by the Almeria command's
citizen-security unit. [@1] The Guardia Civil said indoor marijuana plantations
are often linked to illegal electricity connections that increase fire risk and
endanger neighbours. [@2] The force said citizen cooperation is essential to
detect such activity and said information can be reported anonymously by phone,
website or app. [@2]
