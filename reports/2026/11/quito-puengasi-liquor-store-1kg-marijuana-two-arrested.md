Military intelligence identified a liquor store in Loma de Puengasí, in southern
Quito, as a suspected storage and sales point for marijuana and cocaine. [@1]
The store was intervened at 20:00 on 19 November 2026 on suspicion of storing
and selling drugs. [@1] The operation was named Plaga Verde, and a man and a
woman were arrested in it. [@1] Units of the First Army Division Shyris carried
out the intervention. [@1] The army identified the man by the alias Drack and
said he was linked to a Los Lobos cell tied to the structure of alias Colón
Pico. [@1]

Soldiers found one kilogram of marijuana and 12 doses of a whitish substance
described as suspected cocaine. [@1] They also found 48 rounds of ammunition, 19
of them .50 calibre, ten of 7.62 millimetres, thirteen of nine millimetres and
six of .22 calibre, plus a pistol magazine; no firearms were reported among the
seized items. [@1] The troops collected 183.84 dollars in cash, three
surveillance cameras, two surveillance-system recorders, three routers and two
mobile phones. [@1] The military's working hypothesis was that the equipment was
used to watch the surroundings and facilitate illegal activity. [@1]

The inventory also listed an Invicta watch, a vehicle seat cover and five
personal documents belonging to different people, with no provenance given for
the documents. [@1] The two detainees and the evidence were handed to the
National Police for the corresponding procedures. [@1] The military report cited
the shooting death of a woman in Loma de Puengasí on 3 August 2026 as background
but established no link to the detainees. [@1]
