Guarda Municipal officers in Toledo apprehended an adolescent after he was found
with a portion of a substance similar to maconha during an approach in a square
on Rua Carlos Sbaraini in Jardim Panorama. [@1] The occurrence was recorded at
about 19h30 while a Corporation team was patrolling the area. [@1] The team from
patrol car 1.057, made up of the coordinator and the service supervisor,
approached a group of five young people who were in the square. [@1]

During the personal search, officers found in one adolescent's fanny pack two
zip-lock packages containing a substance with characteristics similar to
maconha. [@1] The material was taken to Toledo's 20th Subdivisão Policial, where
it was weighed at a total of 19 grams. [@1] The four other young people who were
approached were released at the scene after no irregularity was found concerning
them. [@1]

The adolescent was taken to the police unit by a Guarda Municipal team from
patrol car 1.133, cooperating with the officers so handcuffs were not needed.
[@1] At the station, the adolescent's legal guardian was asked to attend to
accompany the applicable procedures. [@1]
