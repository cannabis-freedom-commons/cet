Firefighters discovered a cannabis cultivation while fighting a fire in an
apartment in Bairro de Lourdes, Governador Valadares, in the Vale do Rio Doce
region of Minas Gerais. [@1,3] The occurrence was registered on Wednesday 25
November 2026, when firefighters saw a greenhouse with several plants resembling
cannabis. [@1] The Military Fire Brigade of Minas Gerais (CBMMG) found plants,
equipment and materials used in producing the drug. [@1] A 39-year-old resident
was arrested by the Military Police (PMMG) after confirming the cannabis. [@1,3]

The military personnel were alerted through the fire brigade's operations
centre, Copom. [@1] With no response from inside, firefighters broke down the
front door. [@1] The man was found in the building's garage, confirmed the
cultivation when questioned and received a voice of arrest, or voz de prisão.
[@1,3]

A search found seven cannabis-like plants still in their natural state in the
upper stair corridor. [@2,3] Two bags of the same leaves were hidden behind
ceramic pieces nearby. [@2] The occurrence record counted seven plants still
intact and four already burned inside the greenhouse hit by the fire, eleven
plants in total. [@2,3] In a room used by the suspect, officers found fertilisers,
empty pots with plant residue and a tray of dried material resembling cannabis.
[@2,3] Police identified two growing tents, about 1.60 by 0.60 metres and 1.20 by
0.40 metres, one still operating. [@2,3]

The tents and other equipment were photographed and logged, and the suspect and
seized material were taken to the Civil Police station. [@2] The Civil Police
said it had no room to store the equipment and told the brigade to discard the
tents, fertilisers and other cultivation items. [@2,3] A lawyer for the suspect
came to the apartment and followed the searches. [@2,3] The man had no apparent
injuries and refused medical attention. [@2,3]
