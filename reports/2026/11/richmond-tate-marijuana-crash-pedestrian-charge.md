Police in Richmond charged 60-year-old Robert Curtis Tate on 17 November 2026
with causing death while driving with a controlled substance in his system. [@1]
On 8 November 2026 Tate was driving an eastbound GMC Sierra on National Road
when it struck 55-year-old Timothy Scott Pickering as he walked northbound
across the road. [@1] Pickering was taken to Reid Hospital, where he was later
pronounced dead. [@1] Tate was taken into custody after the crash. [@2] He was
taken to the Wayne County Jail and held on a preliminary charge of operating
while intoxicated causing death. [@2]

Police said Tate showed signs of intoxication after the crash, but a breath test
showed he had no alcohol in his system. [@1] During an interview Tate reportedly
acknowledged he had smoked two joints earlier in the day. [@1] The crash
happened at around 7:45 on Sunday night just west of the intersection of South
West 5th by Roots Bistro on National Road West. [@2] A bench trial in Wayne
Superior Court 3, in which a judge rather than a jury will decide the verdict,
is set for 22 January. [@1] According to court records, Tate has prior
convictions including battery, burglary, driving while intoxicated, leaving the
scene of a traffic accident and resisting law enforcement. [@1]
