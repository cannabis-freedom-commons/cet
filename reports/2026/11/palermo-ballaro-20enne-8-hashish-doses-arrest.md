A 20-year-old riding a moped failed to stop during checks at the Ballarò market
in Palermo and struck a police officer in Via Mongitore. [@1] An officer of the
crime prevention unit had ordered the rider to stop, but he accelerated instead
and hit the officer, who was taken to hospital. [@1]

After the impact the young man lost control of the scooter, which ended up on a
pavement, and continued fleeing on foot. [@1] During the pursuit he threw away a
bag containing 8 doses of hashish. [@1]

Once caught, he was arrested on accusations of resistance and intentional
personal injury to a public official, fleeing after a stop order with danger to
the officer, and possession of narcotics for dealing purposes. [@1]
