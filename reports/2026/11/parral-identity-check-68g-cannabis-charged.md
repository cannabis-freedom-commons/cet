Detectives of the PDI's Modelo Territorial Cero (MT-0) team of the Brigada de
Investigación Criminal in Parral arrested a man during an identity check in the
street. [@1] The PDI said the detectives checked three people during
investigative work and found that one of them was carrying cannabis and cocaine
hydrochloride; he was detained in flagrante. [@2]

The procedure yielded 68.58 grams of cannabis and 3.85 grams of cocaine
hydrochloride, as well as cash and a digital scale. [@3]

The PDI said the operation formed part of its MT-0 programme, which it describes
as aimed at combating the sale of drugs in small quantities. [@1]
