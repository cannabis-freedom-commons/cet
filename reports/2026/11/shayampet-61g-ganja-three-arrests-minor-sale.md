Shayampet police arrested three men accused of selling dry ganja and sent them
to judicial remand, Circle Inspector Sudhakar Reddy said. [@1] Officers found
them during patrols at the Bodrai Centre in Shayampet on 19 November 2026. [@2]
One of the three, from Pragati Singaram village in the same mandal, was caught
selling ganja to a minor boy from Shayampet. [@1]

Officers seized 61.47 grams of dry ganja, three mobile phones and a two-wheeler
from the three. [@1] A case was registered over the incident and Sub-Inspector
Jakkula Parameshwar took up the investigation. [@2] The Circle Inspector gave
the details to reporters at his office on Friday, 20 November 2026. [@1] He
warned that those who sell ganja, and anyone who helps them, face strict action
under the NDPS Act. [@2]
