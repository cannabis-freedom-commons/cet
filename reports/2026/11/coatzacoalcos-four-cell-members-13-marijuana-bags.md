In Coatzacoalcos, officers detained three women and one man described as
suspected members of a criminal cell. [@1] Officers seized from them a short
firearm, live cartridges and 13 small bags of green herb with the
characteristics of marijuana. [@1]

The detentions were part of a weekend operation in which the authorities
reported 51 arrests, including five suspected members of criminal groups,
alongside 221 doses of illicit substances and 31 recovered vehicles. [@1] In the
health-offences line of the same weekend, 22 people were held with 52 doses and
32 bags of green herb with the characteristics of marijuana, alongside
crystal-like substance. [@1] In Atoyac, officers detained a suspected cell
member with three doses of green herb with the characteristics of marijuana,
plus crystal-like substance, cartridges, a magazine, a mobile phone and a
motorcycle. [@1] During searches supporting the state prosecutor in Pueblo
Viejo, Tuxpan, Poza Rica, Naolinco, Jalacingo, Medellín and Emiliano Zapata, six
people were detained and officers seized eight doses, four bags, three
containers and one jar of green herb with the characteristics of marijuana. [@1]
In the vehicle-recovery line, one recovered car was carrying seven bags of green
herb with the characteristics of marijuana, and six people including a minor
were detained. [@1]

The operation brought together the state Public Security Secretariat with the
state prosecutor, the Navy and Defence ministries, the National Guard and
municipal police. [@1] The detained people and the seized items, vehicles and
other objects were placed at the disposal of the competent authorities. [@1] The
bulletin carries a Monday 9 November 2026 dateline from Xalapa and describes the
actions as occurring during the weekend. [@1]
