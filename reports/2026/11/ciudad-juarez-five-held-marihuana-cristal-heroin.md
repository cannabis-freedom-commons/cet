Five men aged 25 to 42 were detained in different sectors of Ciudad Juárez after
cristal, marihuana and heroin were found on them. [@1] The Municipal Public
Security Secretariat said the five were arrested in separate incidents on
suspicion of crimes against health in the narcomenudeo form. [@2] After their
rights were read, the five were consigned to the corresponding authority. [@2]

Josué U. A., 32, was arrested at Artículo 123 and Isla Alcatraz in colonia Luis
Echeverría with a bag of marihuana weighing about 61 grams. [@1] Luis David Z.
S., 25, was detained at Zihuatanejo and Tláhuac in colonia Gustavo Díaz Ordaz
with a package of about 80 grams of marihuana. [@2] Iván S. O., 42, was detained
at Felipe Ángeles and Manuel Acuña in colonia Zaragoza with about 16 grams of
cristal. [@1] Óscar Alejandro L. L., 35, was stopped at Circuito Interior and
bulevar Independencia in Parque Industrial Salvarcar with 15 grams of crystal.
[@1] Joan Arturo H. R., 26, was arrested at José María Morelos and Ignacio
Mariscal in Zona Centro with 27 doses of heroin. [@1]
