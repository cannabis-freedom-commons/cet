National Police Reaction Group officers arrested a 21-year-old man in Bello,
Antioquia, caught in the act with a shipment of narcotics made up of marijuana
cigarettes and cocaine hydrochloride, according to the municipality. [@1] The
operation took place in vereda Hato Viejo, a rural sector the authorities have
prioritised because of the presence of criminal structures dedicated to selling
illegal substances. [@1] Officers on patrol saw the man grow suspicious, drop a
black bag and try to escape, but intercepted him metres away. [@2]

Bello's security secretary said the abandoned bag contained 1,040 handmade
cigarettes with a plant substance whose characteristics corresponded to
marijuana. [@1] The same bag also held 116 transparent sealed bags of a
rock-like substance resembling cocaine hydrochloride, which is not counted in
the cannabis figures. [@2] The official put the approximate commercial value of
all the seized drug material at 8,929,000 pesos. [@1]

After being told his rights, the man was placed at the disposal of the competent
judicial authority to answer for the offence of manufacturing, trafficking or
possessing narcotics under article 376 of the Penal Code. [@1] Bello's
municipality and security secretariat said the arrest formed part of permanent
patrol and territorial-control operations against drug selling and the illegal
revenues linked to micro-trafficking. [@2]
