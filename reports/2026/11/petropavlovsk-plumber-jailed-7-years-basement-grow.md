A 51-year-old plumber from Petropavlovsk was sentenced to seven years in a
medium-security facility. [@1] He was taken into custody directly in the
courtroom after the verdict. [@1] The verdict has entered into legal force. [@1]
The court convicted him on three counts: illegal storage of weapons and
ammunition, illegal cultivation of cannabis on an especially large scale, and
illegal manufacture and storage of drugs without intent to sell. [@1] The court
found no evidence that he had sold any of the cannabis. [@1]

Officers searched his hidden basement grow on 19 February 2026. [@1] The search
was carried out by DKNB officers. [@1] He had grown a total of 16 cannabis
plants. [@1] A control weighing found 1.25 kg of dried marijuana, classified as
an especially large amount. [@1] A working TOZ-8 rifle and scattered cartridges
of various calibres were also kept in the hideout. [@1] The weapons bag had been
left with him for temporary storage by an acquaintance in mid-October 2021. [@1]

The grow was hidden in the basement of the apartment building at 48 Buketova
Street in Petropavlovsk. [@1] The windowless hiding room stood behind a hole in
the wall covered by an old sofa, in a part of the basement residents no longer
used. [@1] He fitted the room with lighting, ventilation and fertilisers. [@1]
He ordered seeds and special hydroponics pots online and paid through a banking
app. [@1] He told the court he grew only for his own use to avoid street
dealers, admitted cultivating cannabis and said he repented. [@1]
