Carabinieri of the Siderno station arrested a man and seized drugs and cash
during a search of his home on their own initiative. [@2] The laboratory was
hidden in a tool shed adjoining a private residence and fitted out for storing,
working and packaging narcotics. [@1] After the formalities the man was taken to
the prison in Locri, at the disposal of the judicial authority. [@3] The
Carabinieri said the case was at the preliminary-investigation stage and that
the suspect remained presumed innocent until any final conviction. [@2]

In the shed officers found 2.2 kilograms of marijuana, 850 grams of cocaine and
100 grams of hashish, more than three kilograms in all. [@2] They also found
almost 15,000 euros in cash, considered the probable proceeds of the illegal
activity, along with vacuum-packing machinery and material for preparing and
dividing the drugs into doses. [@1] All the illicit material and the money were
seized. [@1]

The quantity of drugs and the equipment led investigators to believe the lab was
part of an organised operation supplying the local dealing market. [@3] The
Carabinieri said the action formed part of a wider campaign against drug
trafficking aimed not only at street dealers but also at the places and
structures used to store and prepare drugs for the illegal market. [@2]
