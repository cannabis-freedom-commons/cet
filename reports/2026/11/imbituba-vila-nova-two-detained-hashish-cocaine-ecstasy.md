Military police officers from the 34th Military Police Battalion carried out an
anti-drug-trafficking operation in Vila Nova, Imbituba, on Friday 13 November
2026. [@1] The operation followed information about possible drug selling in the
area, which officers checked through inquiries and monitoring. [@3] Officers
detained two people and took them for the applicable legal procedures. [@1] The
case was recorded as drug trafficking and the detainees were taken to the police
station. [@3]

Officers seized 4.79 grams of hashish, along with 10 portions of cocaine and 17
ecstasy tablets. [@1] They also seized nine mobile phones and R$932 in cash.
[@1] One account also lists two precision scales among the seized materials.
[@3] That account places the action on Rua Manoel Pereira Sobrinho near an
ice-cream shop and describes those detained as a couple. [@3]

Imbituba is in the south of Santa Catarina. [@2] The Military Police did not
release the identities of those involved or any booking details. [@2]
