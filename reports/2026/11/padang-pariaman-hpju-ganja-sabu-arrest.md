Police in Padang Pariaman arrested a 35-year-old man identified only as HPJU on
suspicion of distributing methamphetamine and cannabis. [@1] He was detained in
a police operation on the roadside at Korong Kandang Ampek, Nagari Guguak, in
Padang Pariaman's 2×11 Kayu Tanam district, at about 17:30 WIB on Friday 13
November 2026. [@1] The narcotics unit chief, AKP Irhas Murad, said the case
began with a community report about suspected drug transactions in the area.
[@1] The case was recorded in police report
LP/A/66/XI/2026/SPKT/Satresnarkoba/Polres Padang Pariaman/Polda Sumbar. [@1]
Officers from the operational team moved in and arrested him at the roadside
without resistance, Irhas said on 17 November 2026. [@1]

A search found one small packet of methamphetamine in a pink straw hidden in a
cigarette box in the back pocket of his trousers. [@1] At his home officers
found a black cloth bag holding a brown wallet with a medium packet of
methamphetamine in a clear plastic clip bag. [@1] They also found another small
packet of methamphetamine and one small packet of cannabis in clear plastic.
[@1] Police also seized a black cloth bag, a brown wallet, a packet of Qris
cigarettes, a pair of black trousers and two mobile phones, an Oppo and an
Infinix. [@1]

The suspect told investigators and witnesses that all the drug evidence belonged
to him. [@1] The search and seizure were witnessed by local nagari officials and
residents. [@1] Police said the investigation continued, with officers tracing
the origin of the drugs and hunting for the suspected supplier. [@1] HPJU and
all the evidence were being held at the Padang Pariaman police headquarters for
further legal process, and he was suspected of violating Law No. 35 of 2009 on
Narcotics. [@1]
