Special NDPS Judge Nandan Singh's court in Vikasnagar granted bail on Monday to
Rohit Kumar, a resident of Bhagwanpur, Selaqui, in a charas-recovery case. [@1]
This was his second bail application. [@1] He had earlier been on bail, but a
non-bailable warrant was issued after he failed to appear in court. [@1] He was
arrested on 30 October and sent to jail. [@1]

During checking in the area on 27 June 2024, police recovered 254 grams of
charas from Rohit's possession. [@1] A first information report was registered
against him under the NDPS Act. [@1]

The court treated the recovery as below the commercial quantity and noted that
there was no independent witness. [@1] The court ordered his release on a
personal bond of 30,000 rupees with two sureties of the same amount. [@1] The
court made no comment on the merits of the case. [@1]
