Police arrested Dr Arun Muraleedharan, a 35-year-old from Arunodayam Parayil
house in Ettumanoor. [@1] The check took place at Thavalakuzhu at about 12:15 in
the morning, and officers found the drugs inside his car during the vehicle
inspection. [@1] Muraleedharan works as a doctor at a private hospital in
Monipally. [@1] The arrest took place in the early hours of Saturday. [@2] A
court remanded him to custody after the arrest. [@2]

Officers seized 0.890 grams of MDMA, 1.122 grams of cannabis and five diazepam
ampoules from the car. [@2] The team that caught him was led by Ettumanoor
Circle Inspector K.K. Prashobh, with Sub-Inspectors V.L. Binu, Kunhumon Thomas,
Jiji Lukose and V.K. Vinod and Civil Police Officer Sanish. [@1] Police said the
doctor had links with drug gangs. [@1]

He was allegedly transporting the MDMA and cannabis by car when caught. [@2] The
inspection was part of Operation Thoofan, a joint anti-drug check by the
district police chief's squad and Ettumanoor police. [@3]
