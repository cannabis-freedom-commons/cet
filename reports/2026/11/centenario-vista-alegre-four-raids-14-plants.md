Two men were detained in four raids carried out on Friday 20 November 2026 in
Centenario and Vista Alegre, in a case opened over shootings reported earlier in
the month. [@1] The searches covered the Vista Hermosa neighbourhood of
Centenario and addresses in Vista Alegre Norte, in an investigation into an
incident of firearms abuse and injuries reported in November in the area of the
49th police station. [@2] The raids were ordered by the Unidad Fiscal de
Actuación Genérica, the Neuquén prosecutors' general-duty unit handling the
case. [@1] Officers of the Periferia II investigations office and the 5th, 52nd
and 49th police stations of Centenario and Vista Alegre took part, with members
of the UESPO special unit, the DEMOSE and personnel from the Neuquén barracks.
[@1]

Officers found marijuana during one of the searches and called in the
anti-narcotics department, which seized 14 cannabis plants more than 1.70 metres
tall. [@1] A second account gave the same count of 14 Cannabis Sativa plants
above 1.70 metres. [@2] Investigators also seized a Ford Focus III car, which
was taken to a police station for forensic examination. [@1] The other account
adds that a grey Ford Focus, a .22 cartridge and other items of interest to the
case were seized. [@2]

Both detainees were left at the disposal of the courts while their procedural
situation and any measures over the seized items were pending. [@1] Police said
the investigation aims to clarify the shootings reported in the area at the
beginning of November. [@1]
