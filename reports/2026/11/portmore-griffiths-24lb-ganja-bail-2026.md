Marlon Griffiths, a Portmore resident, was granted $200,000 bail with one surety
when he appeared in the St Catherine Parish Court on 25 November 2026. [@2] He
is charged with possession of ganja, dealing in ganja and transporting ganja.
[@1] He appeared before Parish Court Judge Courtney Maxwell on an executed bench
warrant after failing to attend court in October. [@1] Attorney-at-law Javed
Grant, who represented Griffiths, successfully applied for bail, telling the
court that his client was not a flight risk and would comply with the conditions
imposed. [@1] Grant said he was instructed that Griffiths had mixed up his court
dates, resulting in his absence. [@1] Judge Maxwell granted bail in the sum of
$200,000, with one surety, on condition that Griffiths reports to the Portmore
police on Saturdays. [@1] He is scheduled to return to court on January 13,
2027, for a case management hearing, as some documents remain outstanding. [@1]

Griffiths had been arrested and charged, then granted bail, and had made several
court appearances without legal representation. [@1] The allegations are that in
April 2024, the police stopped a motor car being driven by Griffiths. [@1] A
search of the vehicle reportedly uncovered several packages, including one
containing 24 pounds of vegetable matter resembling ganja, about 10.9 kilograms.
[@1]
