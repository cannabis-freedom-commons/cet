Two women were detained and presumed marijuana and 25 cartridges were seized
during a search in Morelia. [@1]

The state prosecutor's office (FGE), with the Defence Ministry, the National
Guard and the Civil Guard, executed the search warrant. [@1] The warrant came
from an investigation into health offences (delitos contra la salud). [@1]
Officers also seized a pipe, a grinder and a Volkswagen Jetta vehicle. [@1]

Agents and a National Guard member guarded the entrance of the property searched
in colonia Leandro Valle. [@1]
