Military police arrested a man in Ouro Fino, in the south of Minas Gerais, on
Tuesday 17 November 2026 during an operation on Rua Humaitá in the Várzea
neighbourhood. [@1] A check of police systems found a preventive arrest warrant
issued by the São Gonçalo district court in Rio de Janeiro. [@1] The warrant
concerned suspected offences under articles 35 and 40 of Brazil's Drug Law, Law
11.343/2006. [@1] Article 35 covers association for drug trafficking, and
article 40 lists circumstances that can increase the penalties under the law.
[@1]

Officers then found a cannabis plant at the man's home and seized it. [@1] The
man was informed of the warrant. [@2] He was taken to the Civil Police station
and placed at the disposal of the court. [@1] The Military Police did not say
how long he had been wanted or give details of the Rio de Janeiro case. [@1]
