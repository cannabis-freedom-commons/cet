Berkeley County sheriff's deputies arrested two Moncks Corner men after a
narcotics investigation that led to the seizure of fentanyl, cocaine,
methamphetamine and eight firearms, including two reported stolen. [@1] The
investigation began in September after a community member reported suspected
drug distribution involving Gregory Anthony Fields Jr., 41, of Smalls Street.
[@2] Detectives with the sheriff's office Narcotics Task Force identified Fields
as a suspected drug distributor operating throughout Berkeley County and said a
second person was believed to be assisting him. [@1]

On 18 November, members of the sheriff's office Narcotics Task Force,
Intelligence Unit and K-9 Unit executed a search warrant at a residence on
Smalls Street, with assistance from the Drug Enforcement Administration's
Charleston office and the South Carolina Law Enforcement Division. [@1]
Investigators reported finding 70.3 grams of marijuana alongside fentanyl,
cocaine, crack cocaine, methamphetamine and MDMA. [@3] Officers also seized
eight firearms, including two reported stolen. [@1]

Several of the firearms were found in a bedroom belonging to Gregory Fields Sr.,
64, the father of Fields Jr. [@1] The sheriff's office did not specify which
charges the two men face or whether both were taken into custody during the 18
November operation. [@1]
