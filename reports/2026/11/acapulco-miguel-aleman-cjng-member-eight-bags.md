Joint forces detained Javier “N”, described as a presumed member of the Cártel
Jalisco Nueva Generación, in the colonia Miguel Alemán of Acapulco de Juárez
after he fled into an abandoned lot where he was captured. [@1] The patrols were
led by the Guerrero state prosecutor’s office with support from the Mexican
Army, the National Guard, the Navy and the state public-security secretariat,
and the capture was confirmed on Monday 16 November. [@1] According to the
prosecutor’s office, Javier “N” allegedly operated in various parts of the
municipality and was described as a possible participant in criminal schemes
affecting several areas of Acapulco. [@1]

At the site officers found three motorcycles with altered serial numbers, 18
doses of methamphetamine known on the street as cristal and eight bags of
marijuana. [@1] They also seized a metal cutter and welding equipment allegedly
linked to dismantling stolen vehicles. [@1] The motorcycles, drugs and tools
were held to be added to the investigation file opened for the case. [@1]
