Faridabad police's Sector-16 crime branch team arrested Vasu Swami, alias Ashu,
near Sector-19 bypass, acting on secret information. [@1] Vasu Swami is a
resident of Raebareli in Uttar Pradesh and currently lives in Sanjay Colony in
Faridabad. [@1] Both operations were carried out on 20 November 2026. [@1]

Faridabad police said its crime branches made two separate arrests in the drive.
[@1] Officers seized 587 grams of ganja (cannabis) from Vasu Swami. [@1] During
questioning he said he had bought the ganja from an unknown person near the old
railway station. [@1]

Police described the operation as action against drug trafficking. [@1] A
separate Border crime branch team arrested Rahul, a resident of Tughlakabad in
Delhi, with 3.88 grams of MDMA in the second case. [@1]
