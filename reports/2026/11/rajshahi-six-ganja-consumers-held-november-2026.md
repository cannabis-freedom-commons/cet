Rajshahi Metropolitan Police's Boalia thana, its Bosapara police outpost,
Rajpara thana and Kashiadanga thana carried out separate anti-drug operations in
Rajshahi city. [@1] The force said 11 people were detained in total and that
equipment used for consuming drugs was recovered from them. [@1]

Boalia thana police arrested Mo. Babu, 38, in Hetermkha Harizan Palli at about
9:45 pm on 17 November 2026 while he was consuming ganja. [@1] At about 8:30 pm
that evening, officers of Boalia thana's Bosapara police outpost arrested Mo.
Saddam Hossen, 32, at his home in Kedur Mor while he was consuming ganja. [@1]
At about 10:15 pm on 18 November, Rajpara thana police arrested Mo. Tajim Ali
Kalu, 37, Mo. Roki, 40, and Mo. Atikul Islam, 35, in the Laxmipur ID Baganpara
area while they were consuming ganja. [@1] Officers recovered implements that
police said were used for consuming ganja from that group. [@1] At about 1 pm
that day, Kashiadanga thana police arrested Mo. Ramjan, 24, at Kashiadanga Mor
while he was consuming ganja. [@1]

On a doctor's opinion, cases were filed against the arrested people at the
relevant thanas under the Narcotics Control Act, and they were sent to jail
through the courts. [@1] The five other arrests came in a separate Rajpara thana
operation at Aliganj Madhyapara at about 9 pm on 18 November, where police said
those detained were consuming yaba tablets; they are not counted in the cannabis
figures. [@1]
