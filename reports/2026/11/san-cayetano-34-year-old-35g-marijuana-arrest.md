A 34-year-old man was apprehended during a house search in San Cayetano as part
of an investigation into alleged drug dealing. [@1] The case had been opened on
the police's own initiative by the Technical Operations Office of the communal
police station against a local resident with a record for similar acts. [@1]
With support from the municipal monitoring centre, investigators used following,
film records and field work to document the suspect's movements. [@1]

The home search was authorised by Guarantees Court No. 1 of the Necochea
judicial district and carried out by communal headquarters and San Cayetano
station personnel. [@1] During the procedure, officers seized a plastic
container holding 35 grams of marijuana buds. [@1] They also seized nylon
cuttings, a precision scale, 80,000 pesos in cash and three mobile phones, a
Motorola E7 and two Samsung phones, models A05 and A04. [@1] Ecos Diarios
likewise describes a plastic pot with 35 grams of marijuana buds, light-green
nylon cuttings, a Time-brand precision scale, cash, notes and three mobile
phones found in the dwelling. [@3] Caynet reports the investigation had started
on the police's own initiative against a city resident with a record for the
same type of acts. [@4]

The judicial authority upheld the investigation and the apprehension, and the
man was notified that he faces a case of possession of drugs for marketing
purposes under Law 23,737. [@1] The case is handled by Prosecutor's Office UFI
No. 30 of Necochea, under prosecutor Carlos Larrarte. [@1] The detained man was
to be taken to the Complex Crimes Prosecutor's Office to give an indagatoria
statement under article 308 of the Criminal Procedure Code, when his procedural
situation will be decided. [@1] TSN Necochea confirms the man was charged under
Law 23,737 and will be brought to the Complex Crimes Prosecutor's Office for his
indagatoria statement. [@2]
