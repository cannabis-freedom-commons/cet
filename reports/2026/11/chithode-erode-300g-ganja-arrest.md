Police at Chithode, a town in Erode district in Tamil Nadu, arrested a
29-year-old man on Wednesday on suspicion of selling ganja, the Tamil word for
cannabis. [@1] The man was Santhosh, 29, son of Appukutti, who lives at
Sandhaikkadai Medu in the Pettai area adjoining Chithode. [@1]

Officers of the Chithode police station stopped and searched him on the
Chithode-Bhavani road, where he was moving about in a suspicious manner. [@1]
The search found 300 grams of ganja that Santhosh was keeping for sale. [@1]
Police seized the ganja, arrested him and sent him to judicial custody, the
remand in prison that follows an arrest while police investigate a case. [@1]
