A court in Khabarovsk found the man guilty of cultivating plants prohibited for
cultivation under article 231 and drug storage under article 228 of Russia's
Criminal Code and fined him 50,000 roubles. [@1] The convicted man is a
42-year-old sales manager at a commercial company in the city. [@2] He said he
had grown the cannabis to dry it and use it himself, with no sale planned. [@1]

Police inspecting his household plot seized 283 cannabis bushes. [@3] In a
gazebo in the private house officers found more than 30 grams of ready marijuana
in a cupboard, along with implements for use. [@3] Officers also found a metal
container with traces of drug use and parts of cannabis plants at the scene.
[@2]

Police opened criminal cases for cultivation and storage, and the suspect was
placed under a travel restriction. [@3] The investigation was reported complete
on 1 October 2026, with the regional UMVD saying the man had grown cannabis on
his own plot and kept marijuana at home. [@2]
