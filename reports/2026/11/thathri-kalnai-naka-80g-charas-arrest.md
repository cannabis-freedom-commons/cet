Police in Doda district arrested a man at Kalnai Naka in Thathri on 26 November
2026 and recovered about 80 grams of a charas-like substance from him. [@1] The
arrest was made during a check at the naka, a roadside checkpoint. [@1] The
party was led by the station house officer of Police Station Thathri. [@1]
Police identified the man as Sandeep Kumar, son of Sanjeet Kumar and a resident
of Dhamota Balgran in Drabshalla tehsil of Kishtwar district. [@1]

The charas-like substance was recovered during a search of the man. [@1] Police
registered FIR No. 87/2026 under sections 8 and 20 of the Narcotic Drugs and
Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act at Police Station Thathri. [@1] The case was
under investigation. [@1]

Kalnai Naka is in Thathri, a town in Doda district. [@1] Police described the 80
grams as a charas-like substance rather than confirmed charas. [@1]
