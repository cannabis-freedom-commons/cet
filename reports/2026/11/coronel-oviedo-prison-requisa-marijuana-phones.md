Paraguay's justice minister ordered a surprise preventive search of Pavilion “B”
Alta at the Coronel Oviedo Regional Penitentiary during an official visit to the
prison. [@1] The search was led by the prison security chief together with duty
superiors and on-duty penitentiary agents. [@1]

Officers searching the facilities seized 27 mobile phones of different brands
and 27 homemade sharp bladed weapons. [@2] They also seized one bag and one
packet containing suspected marijuana. [@1] One account describes the cannabis
as several joints ("tocos") of suspected marijuana. [@3] Officers additionally
seized seven bottles of homemade chicha and other unauthorised items. [@1]

Officials said a brief moment of tension and commotion broke out as inmates
returned to their cells after the search, but it did not escalate and was
quickly brought under control with support from the National Police's GEO unit.
[@1] The Justice Ministry and the prison's new administration said they would
keep working to eradicate such practices and strengthen internal surveillance to
stop prohibited items entering and to keep order and security. [@1]
