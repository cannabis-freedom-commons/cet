Jordan's Southern Military Region said it foiled an attempt at dawn on Sunday 22
November 2026 to smuggle a large quantity of narcotics into the country using
guided balloons. [@1] Border guards detected the balloons while they were trying
to cross the border towards Jordanian territory, the army said. [@1] Units
acting within their area of responsibility spotted the balloons, intercepted
them and brought them down inside Jordanian territory, where the drugs they were
carrying were seized. [@1] The operation was carried out in coordination with
security and military agencies and the Anti-Narcotics Department, and the
confiscated items were handed to the competent authorities for legal action.
[@2]

The Jordan Armed Forces said it would continue its efforts to protect the
kingdom's borders and confront infiltration and smuggling attempts and anything
that threatens national security. [@1] The army said its border units work with
an advanced surveillance system and close, continuous coordination with the
various security agencies to keep the country and its citizens safe. [@1]
