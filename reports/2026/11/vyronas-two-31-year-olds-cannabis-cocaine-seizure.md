Officers from the Security Office of the Central Port Authority of Piraeus
arrested two men, both aged 31, in Vyronas, Attica, in a drug-trafficking
investigation. [@1] The arrests followed multi-day surveillance and a
coordinated operation based on information gathered by coast guard officers.
[@1] Investigators said a specific residence was allegedly used to store and
distribute narcotics. [@1] Officers found the first 31-year-old outside his
residence as he was boarding a motorcycle. [@1]

A check found in his possession a small amount of suspected cocaine, 1,950 euros
and a mobile phone. [@1] Officers then searched his home in the presence of a
judicial officer with the assistance of the drug-detection dog Kelly. [@1] The
search found 85.6 grams of cocaine, some of it divided into 48 packets. [@1]
Officers also found a small amount of cannabis. [@1] They seized two precision
scales. [@1] They seized a metal spoon with cocaine residue. [@1] They seized
400 euros in the house. [@1]

Officers arrested a second 31-year-old who had repeatedly been seen entering and
leaving the first man's residence. [@1] In total the operation seized 2,350
euros believed to come from drug trafficking, two mobile phones and the
motorcycle allegedly used to carry and sell the substances. [@1] The Central
Port Authority of Piraeus is conducting the preliminary investigation and the
seized substances will be sent to the State General Chemistry for examination.
[@1]
