Mindaugas Vaitelis, a 32-year-old Lithuanian national of Kesh Road, Gortnakesh,
Cavan Town, admitted in March 2026 to the sale and supply of cannabis at his
home address. [@1] The drug dealing offence took place at the Gortnakesh address
on 8 June 2024. [@1] Vaitelis and his mother Davia Vaiteliene, 58, each faced
one count of possessing €36,350, reckless to whether it was the proceeds of
criminal conduct, under the Criminal Justice (Money Laundering and Terrorist
Financing) Act 2010. [@1]

Both pleaded not guilty when arraigned before Judge John Aylmer at the November
sittings of Cavan District Court, and a jury of six men and six women was
empanelled for a four-day trial. [@1] The following day State prosecutor Monica
Lawlor told the court the money-laundering charge against Vaitelis would be
taken into account at the end of the case. [@1] The Director of Public
Prosecutions directed that a nolle prosequi be entered on the same
money-laundering charge against his mother. [@1] The State applied to amend the
amount on the charge to €30,000, accepting that €6,350 was Ms Vaiteliene's
legitimate funds to be returned. [@1]

The court requested a probation and welfare report to assess Vaitelis's
suitability for community service. [@1] Judge Aylmer discharged the jury and
remanded Vaitelis on continuing bail to return next term for sentencing. [@1]
His counsel said Vaitelis, a father of one, had lived in Ireland since he was 10
and had no previous convictions. [@1]
