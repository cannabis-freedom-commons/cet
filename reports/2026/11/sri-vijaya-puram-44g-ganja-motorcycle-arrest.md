The Anti-Narcotics Police Station arrested a 24-year-old man after allegedly
recovering 44 grams of ganja beneath the seat of his KTM Duke motorcycle near
the Bajaj Service Centre at Middle Point, Sri Vijaya Puram, on Friday evening.
[@2] The arrested man was Sujay Bala, originally from Subashgram in Diglipur and
residing in Garacharma. [@1] The Anti-Narcotics Police Station said specific
intelligence received at about 5.10 pm on 20 November indicated that Bala would
arrive at Middle Point carrying narcotics to strike a deal with another person.
[@1]

A team led by Sub-Inspector Manoj Lal was constituted after the intelligence was
passed to Deputy Superintendent of Police (Narcotics) Deepender Kumar Singh.
[@1] The team reached Middle Point Road at about 5.40 pm and intercepted Bala's
KTM Duke motorcycle near the Bajaj Service Centre. [@1] In a search conducted
with two independent witnesses, police recovered a polythene packet containing
suspected ganja beneath the motorcycle seat. [@1] A preliminary drug detection
test indicated the substance was ganja weighing 44 grams. [@1] The seized items
were valued at a combined 82,200 rupees, including 2,200 rupees for the ganja
and 80,000 rupees for the motorcycle. [@2] Police seized the contraband and
motorcycle and arrested Bala at about 6.50 pm under Section 20(b)(ii)(A) of the
NDPS Act, which covers offences involving small quantities of cannabis. [@1] A
case was registered as FIR No. 0020/2026 at the Anti-Narcotics Police Station.
[@2] The proceedings were electronically recorded through the e-Sakshya
application. [@1]

Investigators did not disclose the identity of the intended buyer, the source of
the ganja or whether Bala was operating independently or as part of a larger
supply network. [@1] Police have not disclosed whether Bala had undertaken
similar deliveries earlier or identified anyone else connected to the suspected
transaction. [@1]
