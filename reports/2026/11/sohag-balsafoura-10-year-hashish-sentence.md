The Sohag Criminal Court sentenced a 31-year-old man to 10 years of aggravated
imprisonment and a fine of 100,000 Egyptian pounds for possessing hashish with
intent to traffic. [@1] The defendant was identified only by the initials M. A.
A. and lives in Balsafoura village, in the markaz of Sohag, the district around
an Egyptian town. [@1] The ruling was issued by a panel headed by Judge Mohamed
El-Amir Mohamed Abu Kreisha, with judges Ahmed Nabil Talba and Ahmed Mustafa Abu
Sahli, and court secretaries Mohamed El-Arabi and Ali Hussein. [@1]

The case dates to June 2026, when officers of the Sohag Security Directorate
arrested the man with a quantity of hashish during efforts against
drug-trafficking crime. [@1] After his arrest the legal procedures were
completed and he was handed to the investigating authorities, and the case was
then referred to the criminal court for trial. [@1] Cairo24 carried the same
ruling with the same defendant's initials, age and village, the same 10-year
term and the same 100,000-pound fine. [@2]
