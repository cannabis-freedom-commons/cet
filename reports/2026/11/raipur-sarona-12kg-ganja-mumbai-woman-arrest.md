Police in Raipur arrested a 42-year-old woman from Mumbai and seized 12 kg of
ganja that they valued at about Rs 6 lakh. [@1] Police identified her as Sonali
Mote, a resident of Mumbai in Maharashtra. [@1] Officers of the Anti-Crime and
Cyber Unit and D.D. Nagar police station went to the Sarona railway platform
after a tip received on 23 November that a woman there was waiting to leave with
ganja. [@1]

The team identified her from the description the informer gave, caught her, and
found ganja in the separate bags she was carrying. [@1] Officers seized the 12
kg of ganja and arrested her. [@1] Police registered a case against her at D.D.
Nagar police station as crime no. 730/26 under section 20(b) of the Narcotic
Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act. [@2]

Police said they were examining forward and backward links, meaning where the
ganja came from and who it was to be supplied to, and would act against others
connected to the network. [@1] The operation was carried out under the joint
monitoring of Deputy Commissioner of Police (Crime and Cyber) Smritik Rajnala
and Deputy Commissioner of Police (West Zone) Robinson Guria. [@1] The Hitavada
reported that Raipur police were continuing an extensive investigation to
identify other people connected to the alleged smuggling. [@2]
