An anonymous police patrol in the centre of Edegem spotted a suspected drug deal
over the weekend. [@1] The patrol saw two men hand something to a third person.
[@1] When officers moved to check them, one of the men ran off. [@1] During his
flight he lost his shoes, threw away his keys and smashed two mobile phones on
the ground. [@1] The fleeing suspect was quickly caught and was checked along
with the two other men. [@1]

Police searched the homes of the two suspected dealers. [@1] At the first
suspect's home officers found 480 grams of cannabis, 28 grams of cocaine, three
bottles of nitrous oxide, a precision scale, cash and about 50 grip bags. [@1]
At the second suspect's home officers found 34 grams of cannabis, 22 grams of
cocaine and cash. [@1]

The two suspects, aged 19 and 20, were arrested and brought before the
investigating judge. [@1] The judicial investigation is being continued under
the investigating judge. [@2]
