Police in Jamnagar, Gujarat, arrested one man during a raid on a residential
house in the Rabbani Park area on Morkanda Road. [@1] The Special Operations
Group (SOG) of the city police carried out the raid. [@1] The man was identified
as Ghulamhaji Mamdgosh Sheikh, whom police said had been bringing ganja from
outside and selling it from his home. [@1] The operation was part of an ongoing
campaign against the illegal sale of narcotics in Jamnagar city. [@1]

Officers found 576 grams of ganja at the house. [@1] Police valued the ganja at
28,800 rupees and a mobile phone seized with it at 5,000 rupees, putting the
total value of the seized property at 33,800 rupees. [@1]

A case was registered against the man at Jamnagar City 'A' Division Police
Station under the NDPS Act, India's Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances
Act. [@1] Police said they had begun further legal action against him. [@1] The
raid was carried out by an SOG team that included PI V.J. Rathod, PSI L.M. Zer
and PSI A.V. Kher. [@1]
