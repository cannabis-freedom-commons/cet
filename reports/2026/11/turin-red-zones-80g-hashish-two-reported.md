Carabinieri carried out sweeping checks in recent days in the city's so-called
red zones. [@2] Three coordinated services in recent days, alongside two
flagrant arrests by the Radiomobile unit in recent hours, resulted in five
reports at liberty, drug seizures and immediate closure of two businesses. [@4]
In the centre, between Porta Nuova, Porta Susa and Piazza Santa Giulia,
Carabinieri of the Torino San Carlo company checked more than 100 people, half
of whom were of operational interest. [@2] During the centre checks, a
28-year-old found with about 10 grams of hashish was reported to the Turin
Prefecture for possession. [@2]

In Barriera di Milano, Carabinieri of the Torino Oltre Dora company, supported
by NAS and NIL specialists, the Volpiano dog unit and local police, combed the
area of San Giovanni Bosco hospital and the nearby streets. [@2] At the home of
a 37-year-old woman, investigators found more than 66 grams of hashish hidden in
the freezer, and she was reported at liberty for possession for dealing. [@2]
Between Piazza Bengasi and Nizza Millefonti, a 17-year-old resident of the
Chierese area was reported for possession for dealing after being found with 14
grams of hashish and packaging material. [@2] A 23-year-old domiciled in Turin
was reported for unlawful carrying of a weapon after being found with a dagger
21 centimetres long with an 8.5-centimetre blade. [@2]

At two businesses in the area, a minimarket in Via Monterosa and a bar in Via
Cherubini, the owners aged 40 and 45 were reported for serious workplace-safety
violations including missing risk-assessment documents and unauthorised video
surveillance. [@2] Both businesses were immediately closed and given
administrative fines totalling more than 12,300 euros. [@2] The Chinese account
summarises the sweep as five people investigated, two commercial premises
ordered closed and administrative fines exceeding 12,300 euros. [@1] The three
extraordinary control services covered the centre, Barriera di Milano and the
area between Piazza Bengasi and Nizza Millefonti. [@3] The proceedings are in
the preliminary-investigations phase with presumption of innocence until any
final conviction. [@4]
