Police in Zipaquirá, Cundinamarca, carried out new operativos to combat crime in
the municipality. [@1] The procedures included home entries, night patrols and
rural controls. [@1] Police reported four captures linked to different offences.
[@1] One person was detained for theft from a residence, another for aggravated
qualified theft and a third for personal injuries. [@1] Police reported one
capture for drug trafficking and crimes against natural resources. [@1]

One intervention led to the seizure of several substances, including cocaine,
basuco and marijuana. [@1] Authorities also found five abuelote fish, a fish
originating from Mexico, as well as eggs of that species and a bronze-winged
parrot. [@1] Both animals are described as part of protected wildlife. [@1]

A guarantees judge imposed prison detention on one of those captured, citing the
gravity of the alleged offences. [@1] The National Army supported the operativos
with motorized patrols and checkpoints in rural sectors. [@1] Investigations
remain ongoing to determine whether those detained are linked to other acts in
the area. [@1] GAULA agents and the Secretariat of Security and Citizen
Coexistence ran prevention campaigns against extortion and false services in the
Alto del Águila and San Jorge veredas. [@1]
