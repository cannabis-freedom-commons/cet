One woman was detained after police found a package she was allegedly carrying
concealed inside her body during a checkpoint of a long-distance bus in Paclín.
[@1,3] She was taken to Hospital San Juan Bautista in the provincial capital for a
medical examination, where she reportedly tried to flee and then assaulted
police officers. [@1,3] The woman was placed at the disposal of the Federal Court
of Catamarca, presided over by Judge Miguel Ángel Contreras, which issued the
relevant judicial measures. [@2]

With civilian witnesses present, police inspected luggage carried on the bus and
found white powder and herbaceous material. [@2,3] Field tests indicated the
substances were 260.6 grams of marijuana and 409 grams of cocaine. [@1,3] Officers
also seized 839,100 Argentine pesos in cash and a mobile phone. [@2,3]

The operation was carried out by the División Canes Antinarcóticos, the
anti-drug dog unit of the Catamarca Provincial Police's Dangerous Drugs
Directorate, along National Route 38 between La Merced and La Bajada in Paclín
department. [@1,3]
