Polsek Padang Ulak Tanding raided a house in Desa Tanjung Sanai II, Kabupaten
Rejang Lebong, on Friday 13 November 2026. [@1] The house was allegedly often
used as a venue for drug parties and illicit narcotics transactions. [@1] The
operation was led by Kapolsek Padang Ulak Tanding AKP Edi Hermanto Purba and
detained three suspected perpetrators with narcotics evidence. [@1] The case
began with a community tip about suspected sabu party and distribution activity
in Tanjung Sanai II at around 10.00 WIB. [@1]

When officers arrived, the three suspects tried to flee from inside the house to
avoid arrest. [@1] Officers chased the three suspects and caught them. [@1]
Police seized four medium packages and 19 small packages suspected to contain
sabu. [@1] Officers also found one package of dried leaves suspected to be
ganja. [@1] Police also seized two scales, 300 clip-plastic sheets and 10
plastic bottles suspected to be sabu-smoking tools for packing and consuming the
drugs. [@1] Other items taken included 10 lighters, 12 plastic pipettes, two
mobile phones, Rp210,000 in cash, a notebook and a black bag. [@1]

The three suspects and all evidence were taken to Mapolsek Padang Ulak Tanding
for intensive examination and further legal process. [@1] The disclosure
followed direction from Kapolres Rejang Lebong AKBP Syahrul Hariady to crack
down on drug distribution in his jurisdiction. [@1]
