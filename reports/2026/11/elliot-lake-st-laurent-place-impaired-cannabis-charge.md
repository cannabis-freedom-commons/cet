On Nov. 18, 2026, at approximately 6:30 p.m., East Algoma OPP attended an
address on St Laurent Place and conducted a traffic stop. [@1] Police were
responding to a call involving an intoxicated male. [@1] Police spoke to the
driver of a small white car who was displaying signs of drug impairment. [@1]
Officers found suspected cannabis, traces of suspected cocaine and drug
paraphernalia in the car. [@1] The driver was arrested and the vehicle was towed
and impounded. [@1]

The driver was identified as 36-year-old David Jenkins from Elliot Lake. [@1]
Jenkins was charged with operation while impaired by drugs. [@1] He was also
charged with failure or refusal to comply with demand. [@1] He was also charged
with three counts of failing to comply with a probation order. [@1] He was also
charged with having care or control of a vehicle with cannabis readily
available. [@1]

He was scheduled to appear before the Ontario Court of Justice Bail Court in
Blind River on Nov. 19, 2026. [@1] The allegations have not been tested in court
and Jenkins is considered innocent until proven guilty. [@2]
