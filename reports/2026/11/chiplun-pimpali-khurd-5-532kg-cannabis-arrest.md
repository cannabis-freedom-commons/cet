Ratnagiri district police's Local Crime Branch carried out the raid in Chiplun
under 'Mission Phoenix', a drive to eradicate narcotics from the district. [@1]
Officers detained a 20-year-old man who allegedly cultivated and tended cannabis
plants without a licence at the sawmill. [@2] He lived in a room reserved for
mill workers and was originally from Ayodhya district, Uttar Pradesh. [@3] He
was produced at Chiplun police station for further legal proceedings. [@1]

Police seized 5.532 kg of cannabis plants from the Patel Saw Mill premises in
Pimpali Kherdi, within Chiplun police station limits. [@1] Police valued the
seized plants at 138,300 rupees. [@1] Acting on a tip-off at about 9:05 pm on 25
November 2026, the team raided the premises under the NDPS Act. [@2]

Police said their preliminary finding was that the cultivation was for financial
gain, and seized the plants in the presence of witnesses. [@3] The operation was
led by police inspector Nitin Dhere under superintendent Nitin Dattatray Bagate
and additional superintendent B. B. Mahamuni. [@2]
