Jake Saunders, 21, of Cromwell Road, Newport, pleaded guilty to possession with
intent to supply cocaine and cannabis. [@1] The substances were described in the
case as the class A and class B drugs. [@1] Newport Magistrates' Court heard
that the offences occurred on February 22. [@1]

Magistrates granted Saunders unconditional bail ahead of the next hearing. [@1]
The court also ordered the preparation of a pre-sentence report. [@1] No
sentence was imposed at that stage, and the case will go to the crown court
after he admitted cocaine and cannabis trafficking offences. [@1]

The sentencing hearing is due on December 18. [@1]
