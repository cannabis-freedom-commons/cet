Kamal Singh, who is in jail, has accused DRI officers of illegal detention,
assault and preparing a false chargesheet in a ganja seizure case. [@1] The
Nagpur bench of the Bombay High Court issued notices to DRI and six officers of
its Nagpur regional unit. [@1] Justices Urmila Joshi Phalke and Raj Wakode
ordered DRI and the six officers to file replies. [@1] DRI's case is that at
about 6 pm on 22 February 2026 it stopped a Mahindra Bolero pickup driven by
Singh near Bhilai, on the Maharashtra-Chhattisgarh border, and found 729 kg of
ganja. [@1] DRI says it formally arrested Singh at its Nagpur office at 4:30 pm
on 23 February 2026. [@1] A chargesheet has been filed in the case. [@2]

Singh's petition says DRI officers picked him up in Jeypore, Odisha, at about 6
am on 22 February 2026. [@1] It relies on CCTV footage from a shop in Jeypore
showing the officers there at 6:30 am the same morning. [@1] It says the
officers travelled from Nagpur to Jeypore in three cars whose registration
numbers it lists. [@1] It alleges that the officers beat Singh, threatened him
with a gun, planted the ganja in his vehicle and video-recorded him to make the
seizure look genuine. [@1] It says they warned him that his wife would be made a
co-accused if he sought bail or told the court what happened. [@1]

The petition asks the court to order a special investigation team, register an
offence against the officers and award compensation. [@2] It also alleges that
Singh was not produced before a court within 24 hours and that the grounds for
his arrest were not given to his family. [@2]
