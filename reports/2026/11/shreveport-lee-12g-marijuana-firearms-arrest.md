Shreveport police arrested 19-year-old Landon Lee in the 1000 block of Babylon
Drive on 17 November. [@1] The investigation began after officers received a
report that Lee was allegedly possessing firearms while out on bond in a Caddo
Parish domestic violence case. [@1] The Violent Crimes Apprehension Team made
the arrest, assisted by the City Marshal’s Office, the SPD Warrants Unit and the
Louisiana State Police Gator Task Force. [@2]

Lee was booked into the Shreveport City Jail on an outstanding warrant for
possession of marijuana and a charge of violating a protective order. [@2]
Officers found a .223-caliber semiautomatic pistol in Lee’s possession when they
took him into custody. [@1] With the homeowner’s consent, officers searched the
residence and recovered three additional firearms from the attic. [@1] They also
seized ammunition and approximately 12 grams of marijuana. [@1]

The Caddo Parish District Attorney’s Office was notified in connection with a
bond hearing. [@2] The Shreveport Police Department said the investigation
remained ongoing. [@1]
