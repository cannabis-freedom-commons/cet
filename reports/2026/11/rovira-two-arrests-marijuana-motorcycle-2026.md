Two men were arrested in flagrante in the Miraflores neighbourhood of Rovira,
Tolima, at about 9 pm on 24 November 2026, after police found two packages of
plant material that appeared to be marijuana during a check of a motorcycle.
[@1]

Officers of the Rovira police station were carrying out registration and
background checks when they stopped a medium-cylinder motorcycle carrying the
two citizens. [@1] One of the riders was carrying a bag in which officers found
two pressed packages of plant material that, by its characteristics,
corresponded to marijuana. [@1]

Police arrested both men and handed them and the seized material to the Fiscalía
General de la Nación, where they must answer for the alleged crime of
trafficking, manufacturing or possessing narcotics. [@1] According to Tolima
police, the two men were taking part in virtual hearings at which their legal
situation would be decided. [@1]

Colonel Ferney Martín Romero, commander of the Tolima Police Department, said
controls against local drug trafficking would continue in different
municipalities of the department. [@1] The authorities also reminded the public
that security and coexistence matters can be reported to line 123 or the nearest
police patrol. [@1]
