A 19-year-old youth was arrested with 11.8 kg of ganja during a raid on a house
at Chudapudug village in Sambalpur on Friday. [@1] Police dated the raid to 20
November 2026 and said Charmal police station carried out the seizure and
arrest. [@2] The accused was identified as Prakash Patnia, from Kusmel (RR
Colony) under Sadar police station limits in Balangir district. [@1] ASP Sasanka
Beuria said police conducted the raid following a tip-off and recovered the
contraband from the house of Hirendra Karna. [@1]

Charmal police station registered case number 185/2026 under the NDPS Act. [@2]
The case was registered under section 20(b)(ii)(B) of the Narcotic Drugs and
Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act. [@1]

Police said efforts were on to identify and trace others allegedly involved in
the illegal ganja trade, and further investigation was underway. [@1]
