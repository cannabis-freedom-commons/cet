Federal Highway Police officers stopped a car on BR-158 in Cunha Pora, in
western Santa Catarina, and seized 70 kilograms of maconha. [@1,2] The stop
occurred on the night of Saturday 14 November 2026. [@1,2] The car stopped was a Nissan March 16S and the stop was recorded at about 23h. [@2]

Officers inspected a car bearing plates from Araucaria, Parana. [@1,2] Inside the
vehicle officers found dozens of tablets of the drug hidden between the floor
and the mats of the front and rear seats. [@1,2]

Two men were in the car and were arrested in flagrante and taken to the police
station. [@1] Those arrested were taken to the Civil Police station in Maravilha. [@2] Those involved said the drug was bound for Rio Grande do Sul. [@1,2]
