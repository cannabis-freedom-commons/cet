LEAP officers on patrol in Elsies River stopped a vehicle on Owen Road in the
early hours of 16 November 2026 after spotting its windows covered with black
bags. [@1] Officers pulled over the vehicle and found several large bags of
dagga estimated at just more than 1 140 kilograms. [@1] The two occupants were
arrested and taken to Elsies River Police Station. [@1]

The City of Cape Town presented the seizure as part of its enforcement results
for the past week, headlined as more than a ton of dagga. [@1] The same weekly
roundup listed 1 300 Mandrax tablets and 1,55 kilogrammes of crystal
methamphetamine alongside the dagga, which are not counted as cannabis here.
[@1] On 11 November 2026 members of the Metro Police Gang and Drug Task Team
recovered more than 1,5 kilograms of crystal methamphetamine with an estimated
street value of R1 million and nearly 300 Mandrax tablets with an estimated
street value of R14 500. [@1]

The MyCapeTown republication gives the same account of the Owen Road stop, the
estimated 1 140 kilograms and the two arrests. [@2]
