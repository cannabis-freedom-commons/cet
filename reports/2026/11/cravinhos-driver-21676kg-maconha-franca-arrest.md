A man was arrested in flagrante for drug trafficking on the morning of Monday 16
November 2026 after highway officers found 21.676 kilograms of marijuana hidden
in a car bound for Franca. [@1] The Polícia Militar Rodoviária stopped a
Chevrolet Prisma at a fuel station in Cravinhos, in the Ribeirão Preto region of
São Paulo state. [@1] The driver was stopped during a vehicle inspection in
Cravinhos on Monday 16 November with drugs stored in the car he was driving to
Franca. [@3]

Officers on patrol noticed the Chevrolet Prisma parked at the entrance with the
driver inside and, after a preventive circuit, approached it because it had not
moved. [@1] During the search they found two marijuana tablets in the glove
compartment and four more beneath the passenger seat. [@1] Officers then found
signs of more drugs in the dashboard air outlet, dismantled the structure and
recovered another 14 tablets. [@1] In total 20 tablets were seized, weighing
21.676 kilograms after the weigh-in at the police station. [@1] The 20 tablets
were hidden in the glove compartment, under the passenger seat and inside the
dashboard of the car. [@2] Questioned about the car's origin, the driver said he
did not know which city he had received it in and claimed it was already loaded,
his role being to take it to Franca. [@1]

The Chevrolet Prisma was registered to a third person in Rio de Janeiro and had
no criminal or administrative restrictions. [@1] He was taken to the Cravinhos
Civil Police station, where the flagrante arrest was registered, and remained in
custody at the disposal of the courts. [@1] The car was taken with the driver to
the Cravinhos Civil Police station along with the seized drugs. [@2] Alongside
the marijuana, officers seized a mobile phone, cards and cash. [@1] The
Policiamento Rodoviário estimated the seizure had cost organised crime 87,000
reais. [@1]
