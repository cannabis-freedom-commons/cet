A 32-year-old man from the Gard was convicted by the Ales court on Monday 23
November 2026. [@1] He stood trial as a repeat offender for trying to evade a
road check in the north of the department in October. [@1] On 22 October he was
driving fast and crossed a solid line on the road between Les Mages and
Saint-Ambroix. [@1] A gendarmerie car coming from the opposite direction reacted
and finally stopped him on a track near the main road. [@1] He told the court he
had seen the flashing lights but had not thought they were meant for him. [@1]

Officers found a 100-gram cannabis bar in his vehicle and a drug test came back
positive. [@1] He immediately admitted possessing and smoking cannabis. [@1] The
man, a native of Orange, denied trying to evade the check, saying he had turned
off the road because he urgently needed to relieve himself. [@1]

The prosecutor, Quentin Larroque, argued that fleeing showed he knew he was
carrying a cannabis bar. [@1] The court imposed a custodial prison sentence for
the cannabis possession and refusal to comply. [@1]
