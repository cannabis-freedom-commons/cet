Police in Aceh Singkil regency, Aceh, burned ganja held as evidence in the
courtyard of the regency police headquarters on Wednesday 25 November 2026. [@1]
The ganja came from a case opened by a police report and weighed about 320.14
grams in total. [@1] Officers burned about 304.51 grams and set aside 15.63
grams for proof and for the trial. [@1]

Narcotics unit chief IPTU Eska A. Simangunsong said the burned material was the
same evidence taken in an earlier arrest, with nothing substituted or swapped.
[@1] He said the health service had tested the material and defence counsel had
watched, confirming that it was ganja. [@1] Police said the 15.63 grams were
held back because the amount exceeded what is sent for laboratory examination
and because the evidence was still needed in court. [@1] The ganja was burned in
a large open area away from homes. [@1]

The destruction was presented as part of a commitment to transparent,
professional and accountable enforcement, and the unit asked residents to report
drug activity. [@1]
