Eight people were detained in two homes in the Don Antonio neighbourhood of
Piedras Negras, Coahuila, during searches for suspected drug dealing. [@2] The
state prosecutor's office said the searches took place during Thursday night and
the early hours of Friday. [@1] Those detained were Juan José, 62, Sandra
Patricia, 53, Victoria Abigail, 27, Ámbar Yaletzi, 22, Ámbar Guadalupe, 41,
Esperanza, 41, Roberto Manuel, 23, and Carlos Ignacio, 21, with surnames
withheld. [@4] The eight were placed at the disposal of the public prosecutor,
which had 48 hours to decide their legal situation. [@3]

The properties were on calle Antonio Anaya, between Paisanos and San Juan
streets. [@1] Officers seized six black plastic bags containing green and dried
herb resembling marijuana, a plastic bag and 21 Ziploc-style wrappers holding a
substance resembling the synthetic drug known as cristal. [@1] No weight was
published for the marijuana and the chemical analysis and weighing were still
pending. [@2]

The operation followed complaints and an investigation, after which the
prosecutor obtained a judge's warrant to enter the property. [@1] Agents of the
Agencia de Investigación Criminal took part with the state police, the National
Guard and the Secretaría de la Defensa Nacional. [@1] Coordinator of prosecutors
Rogelio Gómez Rodríguez said the operation brought the year's count of searches
to 106. [@3] The state prosecutor's office said it had made more than 100
searches in Region North I, covering Piedras Negras and part of Cinco
Manantiales, against narcomenudeo, or small-scale drug dealing. [@1]
