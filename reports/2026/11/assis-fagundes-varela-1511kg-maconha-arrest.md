A man was arrested in flagrante for drug trafficking on Tuesday 17 November 2026
following a police response on Rua Fagundes Varela in Assis, São Paulo. [@1]
Officers had been called to check a report of possession and drug trafficking at
a residence and, after stopping the man, found nothing illegal in a personal
search. [@1] His mother told police she had found a bag of drugs in his room
after smelling a strong marijuana odour and that, when he realised the Military
Police had been called, he went into the house and came out carrying another bag
of drugs. [@1]

With the resident's authorisation, officers searched the bedroom and found
portions of marijuana, a substance called dry, plastic packaging and a mobile
phone. [@1] Questioned, the man confessed that the drugs were his and said he
had thrown another bag of marijuana onto the roof of a neighbouring property;
officers found that material too. [@1] In total officers seized 1.511 kg of
marijuana, 20 grams of dry and a mobile phone, besides the packaged portions.
[@1]

The man was given a voice of arrest in flagrante and taken to the Central de
Polícia Judiciária (CPJ) in Assis with the seized material. [@1] The police
authority on duty ratified the arrest and the suspect remained at the disposal
of the courts. [@1]
