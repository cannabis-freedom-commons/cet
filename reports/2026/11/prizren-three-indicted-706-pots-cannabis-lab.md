The Basic Prosecution Office in Prizren has filed an indictment against I.M. and
M.M. for jointly cultivating cannabis and against K.S. for helping perpetrators
after the offence. [@1] I.M. and M.M. are accused of jointly cultivating
cannabis in a rented house in Prizren to produce narcotics. [@1] I.M. allegedly
financed the operation by buying equipment and materials, while M.M. installed
the equipment and grew the plants. [@1]

During a search on 2 April 2026 officers found a cannabis cultivation laboratory
in the house with lighting, heating and ventilation equipment, growth
preparations and 706 pots for growing plants. [@1] Of the 706 pots, 450 were
inside the house and 256 were in a vehicle parked in the yard. [@2] Seized
samples tested positive for cannabis. [@1] Burnt remains of narcotic plants were
also found at a landfill in Prizren which M.M. allegedly tried to destroy. [@1]
K.S. is accused of helping M.M. on 31 March and 1 April 2026 to transport
narcotic plants from the house to the landfill in his vehicle to destroy them.
[@1]

Both I.M. and M.M. are also accused of unlawful possession of weapons. [@1]
Twenty-seven cartridges were found at I.M.’s house, while at M.M.’s house, a
pistol converted into a firearm, an empty magazine and two cartridges were
found. [@1] The prosecutor has proposed that the court find the defendants
guilty and sentence them under the law after trial and assessment of the
evidence. [@1]
