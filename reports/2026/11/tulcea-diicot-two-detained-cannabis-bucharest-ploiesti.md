Officers of the Tulcea organised-crime service and prosecutors of DIICOT's
Territorial Bureau in Tulcea documented two people, aged 37 and 47, for
trafficking high-risk drugs. [@1] One of the two was also investigated for
trafficking risk drugs. [@1] On 19 November 2026 the Tulcea DIICOT prosecutors
ordered both people detained. [@2]

Investigators said that from October 2025 to November 2026 the 47-year-old held
and sold cannabis in Bucharest for between 200 and 800 lei. [@1] They said that
from spring 2023 to November 2026 the 37-year-old procured, held and sold
amphetamine, cocaine and crystalline 3-CMC in Bucharest and Ploiești for between
200 and 2,000 lei, depending on the quantity. [@1]

Four searches the previous day in Bucharest and Ploiești found more than 1
kilogram of high-risk drugs, amphetamine and 3-CMC, about 150 ecstasy tablets,
and a quantity of cannabis. [@1] Officers also seized containers and bags of
substances still being tested, items bearing traces of a substance, and sums of
money in lei, euro and pounds. [@2]

The same day prosecutors asked the rights-and-freedoms judge at the Tulcea
Tribunal to place both in preventive arrest for 30 days. [@1] Organised-crime
police in Bucharest and Ploiești and gendarmes from the Special Intervention
Brigade and the Ploiești mobile group supported the searches. [@1]
