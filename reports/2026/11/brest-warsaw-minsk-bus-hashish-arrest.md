Brest border guards stopped a 51-year-old Belarusian citizen travelling on the
Warsaw–Minsk bus at the Brest checkpoint during border control on the night of
17 November. [@1] A service dog reacted to the man during the check. [@2] The
guard repeatedly asked the man whether he was carrying anything prohibited, and
the passenger denied it each time. [@1]

A search of his waist bag found a metal jar containing a piece of pressed
substance. [@2] An expert examination concluded that the seized dark-brown
resinous substance was hashish, a dangerous narcotic drug. [@1]

The man was detained and placed in the temporary detention facility of the Brest
border group. [@2] Criminal cases were opened against him under part 1 of
Article 328-1 and part 1 of Article 328 of the Criminal Code of Belarus, and a
further inquiry is under way. [@1] The reports note that he had previously been
convicted of a similar offence. [@2]
