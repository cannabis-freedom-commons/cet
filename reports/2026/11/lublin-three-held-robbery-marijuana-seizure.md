Three Lublin residents were charged over an armed robbery committed on 11
November 2026. [@1] At the prosecutor's request, a court placed all three
suspects, aged 30, 23 and 23, in temporary arrest. [@1] Investigators say the
men forced their way into a flat occupied by a 24-year-old man and a 21-year-old
woman and attacked the man with telescopic batons and a 35-centimetre metal bar.
[@1]

When officers detained the 30-year-old at a flat on Skautow Street in Lublin,
they secured more than 16 grams of marijuana alongside other drugs. [@1] He
tried in panic to throw some of the drugs out of the window in a plastic bag.
[@1] The next day officers detained the 23-year-old Kacper J. on Bishop Marian
Fulman Street and found more than 1.4 grams of marijuana on him. [@1] Both men
additionally face drug-possession charges, with the younger man treated as a
repeat offender for that charge. [@1]

A second account confirms the court granted the prosecutor's request to remand
all three suspects. [@2] It independently gives the same drug seizures: over 16
grams of marijuana from the Skautow flat and over 1.4 grams from Kacper J. the
next day. [@2] Detectives from the 4th Police Station and the Lublin City Police
handled the case under the Lublin-Poludnie district prosecutor. [@1]
