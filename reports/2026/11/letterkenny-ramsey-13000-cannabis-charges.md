Jake Ramsey, of Foxhills, Letterkenny, was brought before Letterkenny District
Court after being served with a book of evidence that morning. [@1] He was
cautioned by gardaí and made no reply to the charges. [@1] He was 25, and the
book of evidence was served at 11.18am. [@2] He was to stand trial on five
alleged drugs offences. [@2]

He is charged with possession of a controlled drug, namely cannabis, possession
with intent to supply, and possession of a controlled drug valued at €13,000 or
over with intent to supply. [@1] Donegal Daily listed a charge that on 6 March
2025 he, as occupier of land at Foxhills, knowingly permitted the manufacture or
production of a controlled drug there. [@2] A further possession charge relates
to 11 June at the same address. [@2] The section 15A supply charge states that
the market value of the drug amounted to €13,000 or more. [@2]

The drugs were found during a raid at his home on 6 March 2025. [@1] The
Director of Public Prosecutions directed that he be sent forward for trial to
the next sitting of Letterkenny Circuit Court in the New Year. [@1] Donegal
Daily said that sitting begins on 2 February 2027. [@2] Judge Emile Daly
remanded him on continuing bail. [@1] His solicitor requested senior and junior
counsel because of the severity of the charges. [@2] Judge Emile Daly granted
both and gave the alibi warning. [@2]
