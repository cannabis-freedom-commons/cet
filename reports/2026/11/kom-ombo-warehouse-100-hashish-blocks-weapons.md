A security campaign in Kom Ombo, in Aswan governorate, seized a large quantity
of drugs, firearms and ammunition inside a warehouse, the security services
said. [@1] The warehouse is in the Raouf area of the town. [@1] The operation
formed part of the security services' drive against drug trafficking and against
people holding unlicensed weapons. [@1] Inside the targeted warehouse officers
found about 100 blocks of hashish, the pressed blocks in which the resin is sold
in Egypt, and quantities of a drug the article calls hydro. [@1] They also
seized a rifle, a pistol and a quantity of ammunition. [@1]

No suspect was named and no arrest was reported. [@1] The security services said
they had taken the legal measures the case required and were preparing to
present it to the competent investigation authorities. [@1] Investigators were
to establish the circumstances in which the drugs and the weapons were held and
to identify the people responsible for them. [@1]
