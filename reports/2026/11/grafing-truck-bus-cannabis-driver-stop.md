Police in Grafing stopped a truck driver on suspicion of driving under the
influence of cannabis. [@1] Officers opened administrative fine proceedings
against the driver. [@1] The driver had to undergo a blood sample and was
prohibited from driving on. [@1] If the blood analysis confirms the suspicion,
the driver faces a high fine and a driving ban. [@1]

The stop occurred during a control of truck traffic in Grafing between 09:00 and
15:00 on 17 November 2026 as part of the Europe-wide Truck and Bus operation.
[@1] Officers checked compliance with driving and rest times, the technical
condition of vehicles, drivers' fitness to drive, load securing and compliance
with route bans. [@1] Officers recorded three violations in total, each leading
to fine proceedings. [@1]

In a second case officers criticised load securing because a driver had placed
all load parts loosely on his flatbed truck. [@1] In a third case officers found
a truck had ignored the resident-excepted through-traffic ban. [@1]
