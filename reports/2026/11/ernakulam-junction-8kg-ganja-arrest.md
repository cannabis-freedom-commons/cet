A 19-year-old man was arrested with 8.02kg of ganja during a joint inspection by
Railway Police and Railway Protection Force at Ernakulam Junction railway
station around 8.30am on Saturday. [@1] Police found Samim Akhtar of Laskarpur
in Murshidabad, West Bengal, sitting near a catering stall on the northern side
of the platform in suspicious circumstances with a brown shoulder bag. [@1]

A search led to the recovery of dried ganja packed in two covers. [@1] Police
also seized a mobile phone and identity documents from him. [@1] According to
police, the ganja had allegedly been brought for sale. [@1]

The inspection was led by Ernakulam Railway Police SI Sunil G, grade ASI Dinil,
grade SCPO Aneesh and RPF constables Shabu and Ajay Ghosh. [@1] The accused and
the seized contraband will be produced before the Ernakulam special NDPS court.
