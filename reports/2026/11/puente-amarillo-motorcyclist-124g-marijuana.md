A 27-year-old motorcyclist was caught red-handed during a road check on the
highway between Villavicencio and Barranca de Upía, Meta. [@1] He was reportedly
hiding several narcotic substances among his clothing. [@1] During the
inspection, officers of the Meta Police Transit and Transport Section found
about 124 grams of marijuana and five grams of tusi. [@1]

The stop took place at kilometre 6+900, at the Puente Amarillo toll, when police
pulled the motorcyclist over for an inspection. [@2] Police said the seized
substances were worth about 400,000 pesos on the illegal market and amounted to
dozens of doses taken out of circulation. [@1]

The detainee was placed at the disposal of the 40th Local Turn Prosecutor's
Office in Villavicencio to answer for the alleged offence of trafficking,
manufacturing or carrying narcotics, with responsibility to be determined by the
judicial authorities. [@1] Police said they would continue checks on the
department's road corridors and urged people to report drug activity through the
123 emergency line. [@1]
