At about 11:05 on 16 November 2026 a police patrol in Linz tried to stop the
female driver of a car. [@1] Both vehicles had turned from Franckstraße into
Füchselstraße. [@1] In Don-Bosco-Weg officers wanted to subject the 21-year-old
woman from Linz to a check. [@1] The 21-year-old did not follow the patrol car
and fled at excessive speed along Füchselstraße toward Raimundstraße. [@1] At
the intersection with Heizhausstraße she left the road to the left, broke
through a construction barrier and crashed into a truck. [@1] The car was flung
onto the adjacent parking lot. [@1] The driver and her two 20-year-old female
passengers from Linz were injured to an undetermined degree. [@1] All three
received first aid from rescue services and an emergency doctor. [@1] They were
taken to hospitals in Linz for further treatment. [@1] All three injured people
were able to leave the vehicle on their own. [@1] The truck driver was sitting
in the cab and was not injured. [@1] Shortly before, construction workers had
worked in the pit that the driver jumped over with the car. [@1]

The checks found that the 21-year-old had no valid driving licence because it
had been revoked. [@1] A saliva test was positive for THC. [@1] The clinical
examination was refused and the breath-alcohol test was negative. [@1] Officers
also seized a small amount of marijuana in the vehicle. [@1] The crash caused
various damage at the construction site. [@1] Damage to the truck was reported
as unknown. [@1] The truck remained drivable. [@1] The driver's car was a total
loss. [@1]
