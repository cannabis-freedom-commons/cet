Officers from the Rapid Response Unit arrested two Vincentian nationals during a
drug interdiction operation at Levera, St. Patrick. [@1] Police identified one
accused man as 25-year-old farmer Safari Jack of Petit Bordel, St. Vincent. [@1]
Police identified the other accused man as 26-year-old labourer Elton John of
Questelles, St. Vincent. [@1] Following the Levera operation, police jointly
charged the two men on 22 November 2026. [@1] The charge was trafficking in a
controlled drug. [@1] The two accused are men from St. Vincent and the
Grenadines. [@2]

The police interdiction was executed on 21 November 2026, when officers attached
to the Rapid Response Unit carried out an operation in Levera, in the parish of
St. Patrick. [@1] During the search, officers uncovered 122 pounds of cannabis.
[@1] Police estimated the street value of the cannabis at $276,696 ECC. [@1]
Grenada's police force said its Rapid Response Unit seized the cannabis at
Levera. [@3]

Both men were scheduled to appear before the Sauteurs Magistrate’s Court on 23
November 2026. [@1]
