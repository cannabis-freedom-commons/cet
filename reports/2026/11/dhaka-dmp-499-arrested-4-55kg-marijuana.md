Police arrested 499 people in Dhaka over the past 24 hours, including 49 alleged
leaders and activists of the banned Awami League. [@1] The Dhaka Metropolitan
Police said 52 cases were filed during the same period. [@1] The DMP said its
officers carried out the drives in police station areas across the capital on
Wednesday 25 November. [@2]

Mirpur Division recorded the most arrests with 139, followed by Wari with 79,
Tejgaon with 66, Lalbagh with 59, Motijheel with 45, Gulshan with 44, Uttara
with 42 and Ramna with 23, while the Detective Branch arrested two people. [@1]

Officers seized a revolver, a machete, four crude bombs, seven bomb parts, 1,307
yaba pills, 4.55 kg of marijuana, a sewing wrench, seven mobile phones and Tk
32,900 in cash. [@1] One victim was rescued during the operation, police said.
[@1] The DMP said legal action against those arrested was being processed. [@1]
