Officers of the Vienna street-crime unit (EGS) watched a suspected drug sale in
the Degengasse area of Ottakring at about 18:45 on Sunday 22 November 2026. [@1,6,8]
They detained the suspected seller, a 35-year-old Algerian citizen. [@1,3,6,7,8] The
25-year-old buyer, an Afghan citizen, was reported to the authorities. [@1,3,7,8]

A search of the suspect's backpack found 48 containers of cannabis and a
three-figure sum of euros in cash. [@1,4,5,7,8] Investigators then searched an apartment
the 35-year-old had used and found more drugs, cash and a forged identity
document. [@1,5]

In total Vienna police seized about 960 grams of cannabis and about 6,000 euros
in cash. [@1,3,5,6,7,8] The cannabis and the cash came from the man himself and from the
flat he used, according to the police account. [@2]

The 35-year-old remained in police custody while investigators continued to work
on the suspected drug dealing case. [@1,5,8]
