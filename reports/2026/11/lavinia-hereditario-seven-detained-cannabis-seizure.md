Civil police from Lavínia, Mirandópolis and Andradina carried out eight
search-and-seizure warrants during Operation Hereditário in Lavínia, in the
interior of São Paulo state, on 19 November 2026. [@2] The São Paulo Civil
Police arrested six suspects in flagrante and apprehended a teenager during the
operation. [@4] The operation targeted a criminal organisation responsible for
selling drugs in the region. [@2] Police said the investigation examined a
possible family structure suspected of selling drugs, with signs of divided
tasks among those involved. [@3]

Among the drugs recovered, police listed approximately 1.4 kilograms of
marijuana. [@3] They also listed 82 grams of hashish, a cannabis resin. [@3] The
haul included 616 grams of cocaine. [@3] Officers also seized portions of crack,
14 mobile phones, precision scales, packaging used for trafficking and cash.
[@1]

The six adults were taken to the Andradina Judicial Police Centre and held at
the disposal of the courts. [@2] The teenager was taken to a Fundação Casa
facility and later released. [@2] Police had not disclosed the results of phone
examinations or confirmed links to other municipalities. [@3]
