Bathnaha police arrested Aditya Kumar, a resident of Panthpakar village in
Sitamarhi district, Bihar. [@1] Officers seized 1.1 kilograms of ganja at the
spot and took him into custody. [@1] The raid followed a tip-off that ganja was
being kept illegally in Panthpakar and smuggled from the village. [@1]

Bathnaha station house officer Rupesh Kumar confirmed the arrest and the
seizure. [@1] He said the police campaign against drug trafficking and sale was
continuing and that people in the trade would not be spared. [@1]

A first information report was registered at Bathnaha police station under the
relevant sections, and Aditya Kumar was sent to judicial custody after
questioning. [@1] Police said they were trying to establish where the ganja came
from and which areas it was meant to supply, and were examining the role of
other people in the network. [@1]

A Dainik Bhaskar report of the same raid gave the quantity as 1 kilogram 100
grams. [@2]
