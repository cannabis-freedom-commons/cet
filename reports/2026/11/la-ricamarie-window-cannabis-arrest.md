On Thursday morning, police services were conducting an operation in the
communal areas of residential buildings in La Ricamarie at the request of a
social-housing landlord. [@1] During this kind of operation, officers inspect
communal areas such as entrance halls, cellars, letterboxes and electricity
meters with the help of a dog trained to search for drugs. [@1] Enough drugs
were present in one apartment for the canine-team dog to mark in front of its
entrance door. [@1]

As officers approached that apartment, colleagues posted outside saw a man throw
something from his window. [@1] What he threw was about thirty bars of cannabis
resin weighing a little over three kilograms. [@1] When the occupant opened the
door to the officers, he was arrested. [@1] The arrested man is 39 years old and
was already known to the police for other offences. [@1]

The case was taken over by the Saint-Étienne narcotics brigade. [@1] After his
period in police custody ended, the man was summoned to appear before the court
for possession of narcotics. [@1]
