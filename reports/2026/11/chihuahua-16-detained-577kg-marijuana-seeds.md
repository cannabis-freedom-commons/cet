Sixteen people were detained in operations by the National Guard and the Mexican
Army across municipalities of Chihuahua state. [@1] The operations ran from 15
to 21 November 2026 as part of the National Public Security Strategy, according
to Communiqué 51 of the XI Military Region and the 42nd Military Zone. [@2]
During patrols and ground reconnaissance, soldiers located and destroyed an
opium poppy plantation and made the 16 arrests. [@1]

Officers seized 5.77 kilograms of green plant material resembling marijuana and
seven kilograms of dried seeds resembling the same drug. [@1] Security forces
also secured weapons, four vehicles and three properties in the same operation.
[@2] Among the items seized were 940 cartridges, three magazines, 35 grams of a
methamphetamine-like substance and 22 amphetamine-like pills. [@2]

The detainees and seized items were handed to the federal attorneys general's
offices in Ciudad Cuauhtémoc and Hidalgo del Parral for investigation and a
decision on their legal status. [@1] The state prosecutor's office and the
Chihuahua state police accompanied the military and National Guard units. [@1]
