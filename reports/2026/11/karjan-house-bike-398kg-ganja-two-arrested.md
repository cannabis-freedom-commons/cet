District SOG police watching the Karjan-Abhor road stopped Mahendrasinh alias
Lalo Nansinh Jariya, a resident of Abhor village in Padra taluka, as he rode by
bike from Karjan toward Abhor. [@1] In a second raid, Karjan police searched the
home of Kalu Valimahammad Pathan in Hirabag Society in the Nava Bazar area of
Karjan after a tip that he kept ganja at home for retail sale. [@1]

Search of a bag in Mahendrasinh's possession yielded 1 kg 980 g of ganja. [@1]
Checking a domestic flour mill kept beside a safe in Kalu's house revealed a
brown cello-taped packet of ganja. [@1] Together the house seizure of about 2 kg
and the bike seizure of 1.980 kg totalled about 3.98 kg of ganja. [@1]

Police seized the ganja, the bike and a mobile phone from Mahendrasinh,
registered a case against him and continued investigation. [@1] Police detained
Kalu, seizing about 2 kg of ganja and a mobile phone, and questioned him
further. [@1] Police reported two arrests across the two raids, with further
investigation under way and no court outcome yet stated. [@1]
