A man and a woman living in Sallertaine were implicated in a North Vendée
drug-trafficking case, according to the gendarmerie investigation. [@1] The case
came before the Sables-d’Olonne court on 20 October 2026. [@2] When the case was
judged on 15 November, the man denied being a link in the trafficking. [@1] The
court gave the man eight months’ suspended prison with a drug-awareness course
and fined the woman 10,000 euros. [@1]

Investigators found a sports bag holding 22,500 euros in cash and 700 grams of
cannabis resin in their flat. [@1] During a search of their home, officers found
mobile phones, a precision scale and a knife bearing cannabis traces. [@1] The
man did not work but owned two large-engine vehicles estimated at 60,000 euros.
[@1] His bank account showed regular small deposits ranging from 20 to 50 euros.
[@1] The woman, though in low-paid work, held 34,500 euros in her bank account.
[@1]

The 20-year-old man was suspected of cannabis trafficking from early January
2025 until mid-March. [@2] Investigators said he had about fifteen regular
clients. [@2] The main perpetrator in the same North Vendée trafficking case had
been given five years in prison, three of them firm, the previous spring. [@1]
