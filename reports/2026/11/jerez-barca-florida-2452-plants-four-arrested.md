The National Police seized more than 2,400 marijuana plants and arrested four
people in two rural operations in Jerez. [@2,3] The two operations took place on
17 November in La Barca de la Florida, Jerez de la Frontera, over alleged
public-health offences and electricity fraud. [@1,3] The four detainees were
placed at the disposal of the judicial authority in the corresponding police
proceedings. [@1]

Investigators first found signs of a plantation inside a house and, with
power-company technicians, confirmed an allegedly illegal connection and
abnormal consumption before locating an indoor grow. [@1,3] Inside that house
officers found 99 marijuana plants weighing about 21.9 kilograms and arrested
its alleged keeper. [@1,3] In a second court-authorised entry and search on a farm
in the same area, investigators found infrastructure for intensive marijuana
cultivation with rooms fitted out for growing and drying. [@1,3] The farm held air
conditioners, filters, extractors, fans and lamps, with some grow and drying
rooms built underground. [@1,3] Officers seized 2,353 marijuana plants and 60.725
kilograms of buds prepared for distribution at the farm. [@2]

In both operations officers found illegal connections to the power grid
supplying the grows, with consumption far above a normal home. [@1] Police said
such installations cause major economic harm to suppliers and pose a grave
danger to public safety through fire, short-circuit, electric-shock and
supply-cut risks for neighbours. [@1]
