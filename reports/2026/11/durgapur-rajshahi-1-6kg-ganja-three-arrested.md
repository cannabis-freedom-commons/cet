Detectives from the Rajshahi district police arrested three men in Durgapur
upazila on Friday 20 November 2026. [@1] Officers stopped them on the paved road
from Amgachhi to Hat Godagari, in front of a house in Hariyapara village. [@1]
The team seized 1 kg 600 g of ganja, searching two of the men at about 07:30.
[@3]

The three men are from Hariyapara and Palashbari villages in the upazila and are
aged 29, 28 and 50. [@1] A detective sub-inspector led the raid under the
direction of Rajshahi district superintendent of police Mamunur Rashid, after a
tip-off that men were standing on the road to sell drugs. [@3]

The district's additional superintendent of police (DSB), Sabina Yasmin,
confirmed the arrests that night. [@2] A case was filed at Durgapur police
station against the three under the Narcotics Control Act 2018. [@1] Police said
the anti-drug and anti-smuggling drive would continue. [@4]
