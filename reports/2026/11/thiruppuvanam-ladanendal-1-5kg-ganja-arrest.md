Officers of the Thiruppuvanam police station in Sivagangai district, Tamil Nadu,
stopped a young man they found standing suspiciously at Ladanendal, questioned
him about why he was there, and arrested him. [@1] They identified him as
Vimalraj, also known as Ayyannar, a 19-year-old resident of Venkatti village.
[@1]

The inquiry established that he was carrying one and a half kilograms of ganja,
the Tamil word for cannabis, on a two-wheeler, and that he was keeping the drug
for sale. [@1] Besides the ganja, police seized the two-wheeler and two mobile
phones, and said other items connected to the case were also taken. [@1] He was
sent to remand, meaning he was placed in judicial custody after being produced
before a magistrate. [@1]
