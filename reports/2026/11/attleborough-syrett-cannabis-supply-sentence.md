Drew Syrett, 34, of Burgh Common, Attleborough, appeared at Norwich Crown Court
on 13 November 2026 for sentence after pleading guilty to further drug offences.
[@2] He admitted being concerned in the supply of cannabis, possession of
cannabis with intent to supply and money laundering. [@1] Recorder Hooi Low
activated the previous suspended sentence in full and added an additional six
months on top for his new offences. [@1]

In July he had received two years and nine months suspended for two years with
an electronic monitored tag on his movements. [@1] In July, Syrett had received
a suspended sentence of 35 months for prior offences of supplying cocaine and
cannabis. [@3] Prosecutors said the latest dealing had occurred between
September 14 and October 15 and involved more than half a kilo of cannabis. [@1]
On October 15, officers arrested Syrett after messages found on a mobile phone
seized from another suspected drug dealer showed him offering cannabis for sale.
[@2] A subsequent search of his home uncovered a significant quantity of cash
and cannabis. [@2]

Syrett was initially arrested following a violent break-in at Old Hall Farm in
Burgh Common, near Attleborough. [@1] He is also facing proceeds of crime
proceedings, which could see money he made through drug dealing seized. [@2]
