Narcotics investigators of the Périgueux Police nationale carried out a large
anti-drug operation in Périgueux on the Wednesday before the 23 November 2026
publication. [@1] They were supported by the RAID, the Bordeaux dog-handling
brigade and the Périgueux intervention brigade. [@1] The operation led to the
arrest of four men and one woman. [@1] Officers seized 1.5 kg of cannabis. [@1]
After police custody, the five arrested people were brought before the courts.
[@1] All five were then remanded in custody to await trial, scheduled for early
January 2027. [@1]

During the investigation, one suspect admitted that the trafficking had sold 25
kg of cannabis over a two-year period. [@1] The Police nationale said this type
of operation would continue to support public safety and calm. [@1]

As a follow-up to the operation, searches were carried out that same Wednesday
at Coulounieix-Chamiers after the arrests. [@1] Police in Périgueux and Bergerac
said they remained mobilised against all forms of trafficking. [@1]
