South Tangerang police's narcotics unit handled 55 drug cases between September
and November 2026 and detained 71 suspects, police chief AKBP Boy Jumalolo said.
[@1] Boy announced the results at a press conference on Tuesday 24 November
2026, Antara reported. [@3] The cases were handled by Satresnarkoba, the
narcotics unit of Polres Tangerang Selatan. [@2]

Among the evidence seized was 54.09 grams of ganja, the Indonesian word for
cannabis. [@1] DetikNews gave the ganja as 54 grams. [@2] Most of the haul was
other drugs: 2,824 mephedrone pills, 969.195 grams of tembakau sintetis
(synthetic cannabis), 189 etomidate cartridges weighing 450.668 grams, 313.91
grams of methamphetamine, 49.18 ecstasy pills and 25.112 grams of liquid
synthetic drugs. [@1] Police also seized 284 pills of illegal prescription
medicine and 2,143.4 grams of a medicine containing ketamine. [@1]

The suspects were charged under articles 114 and 111 of Indonesia's 2009
narcotics law, which carry the death penalty, life imprisonment or five to 20
years. [@1] The narcotics unit's chief, AKP Pardiman, said drug sales now mostly
take place over social media rather than face to face. [@2]
