Between 14 and 20 November 2026, Kosi range police arrested 66 people in liquor
cases and 4 people in dry-intoxication cases across Saharsa, Supaul and
Madhepura districts, sending all 70 to jail. [@1] Strict legal action was
continuing against those arrested. [@1]

Between 14 and 20 November 2026, police seized 126.157 kilograms of ganja across
Saharsa, Supaul and Madhepura districts. [@1] In the same week, police seized
976.795 litres of illicit liquor. [@1] Officers also seized 17.32 grams of
smack, 25.3 litres of codeine syrup, 4 ml of narcotic injection, ten motorbikes,
two cars, two mobiles, gas cylinders and stoves with other equipment, and 8,760
rupees in cash. [@1]

The drive was part of the Nasha Mukt Koshi Abhiyan operating across the three
districts with public cooperation. [@1] Kosi range Deputy Inspector General Dr
Kumar Ashish issued helpline number 9060384865 for tips, keeping informants'
numbers and identities secret under his direct supervision. [@1] He appealed for
calls, messages or WhatsApp tips with dealers' names, addresses, locations and
photos or videos, promising rewards for major successes. [@1]
