Police arrested three people aged 19 to 23 in the village of Amoriakro on Friday
20 November 2026 and seized 6.30 kg of cannabis. [@1] The operation began with
an anonymous tip that a home in the village was serving as a drug sale point.
[@1] Officers from the Abengourou anti-drug unit GRAD went to the home at about
16:30, where two occupants fled and officers caught one while the other escaped.
[@1] While police were still at the house, two other men arrived and were
arrested on suspicion of coming to obtain drugs, bringing the total detained to
three. [@1]

In the presence of the arrested suspect, officers searched the house and found
six blocks and 19 sachets of cannabis weighing 6.30 kg hidden in dirty laundry.
[@1] The same search also seized 220 Tapentadol tablets dosed at 250 mg, 23
Diazepam tablets and five packs of OCB rolling papers, which are not counted in
the cannabis figures. [@1] Police seized all the products found and placed them
under seal, and the three detainees were taken to the GRAD premises in
Abengourou. [@1]

The operation was carried out under police operations Épervier XII and Épervier
spéciale. [@1] An investigation was opened into the origin, destination and
possible ramifications of the suspected trafficking, and the second occupant who
fled had not been arrested at the time of the report. [@1]
