A mobile court detained 30-year-old Mosa Runa Begum in Namopara village under
Shahmakhdum Thana, Rajshahi, on 26 November 2026 for allegedly keeping cannabis.
[@1] She was identified as the daughter of Rahmat Ali. [@1] She was identified
as the wife of Joynal Ahmmed. [@1] The detention happened during an anti-drug
drive in the Shahmakhdum Thana area of Rajshahi city that ended with a
mobile-court punishment. [@1]

Officers alleged she was keeping 120 grams of ganja. [@1] Ganja is the term the
report uses for cannabis. [@1] The mobile court sentenced her to six months of
rigorous imprisonment for possessing ganja. [@1] It also fined her 5,000 taka
for the same offence. [@1]

An executive magistrate led the drive with a special team and Shahmakhdum Thana
police. [@1] Rajshahi Metropolitan Police said it conducts regular anti-drug
drives to keep the metropolitan city drug-free and safe. [@1] It said such
drives to prevent and suppress drug offences will continue. [@1]
