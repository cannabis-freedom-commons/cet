Gendarmerie officers stopped an intercity passenger bus for a road check in
Bahçe district, Osmaniye, on 10 November 2026. [@1] Two male suspects, K.K. aged
28 and Ş.A. aged 25, faced judicial proceedings over the find. [@1] After
processing at the gendarmerie, both men were brought before a court and remanded
in custody. [@1] The two remanded suspects were delivered to Osmaniye Closed
Prison. [@1]

Officers searching the bus luggage compartment found 2 kg 855 g of skunk hidden
in a backpack and seized it. [@1] The case was processed as manufacture and
trade of narcotic or stimulant substances under Article 188 of the Turkish Penal
Code. [@1]

Osmaniye Provincial Gendarmerie Command said the check on the Osmaniye-Gaziantep
motorway toward Osmaniye followed intelligence work against drug manufacture and
trade. [@1] The command presented the Bahçe road check as one result of its
intelligence and inspection efforts against drug trafficking. [@1]
