The two people arrested were a mother and her son. [@1] A Military Police
operation led to the arrest in flagrante of a 26-year-old man and a 43-year-old
woman in Alto Rio Doce, Minas Gerais, on suspicion of drug trafficking and
association for trafficking. [@1] The action took place on the afternoon of
Wednesday 18 November 2026 while officers carried out a search and seizure
warrant in the Jardim dos Crostas (Parte Baixa) neighbourhood. [@1] According to
the police record, the 26-year-old was caught throwing a bag towards a
neighbouring lot at the moment of the approach. [@1]

Officers searched the property and the adjacent area and found several narcotics
and materials linked to the illegal drug trade. [@1] Officers seized 44 buchas
(wrapped portions) of cocaine, seven more buchas, two tablets, one cigarette and
one cannabis plant, as well as R$ 658 in cash and two mobile phones. [@1]

Once the material was confirmed, both suspects were given a voice of arrest in
flagrante for drug trafficking and association for trafficking. [@1] They were
taken with all the seized material to the Civil Police station in Barbacena,
where the occurrence was presented for the legal steps to follow. [@1]
