Officers of the Motorised Operations Directorate apprehended two men in the
Tucumán capital during a preventive operation. [@1] The procedure took place at
around 21:00 at the corner of Lídoro Quinteros street and pasaje Bazán de
Laguna. [@2] The two men began to run when they noticed the police presence in
an apparent attempt to escape. [@1] Officers intercepted them and subjected them
to a search. [@1]

During the search officers seized 72 wraps of cocaine, 43 small bags of
marijuana and more than 30 Alprazolam tablets. [@2] Officers also found a
digital scale, plastic cuttings used for packaging the substances, a mobile
phone and cash. [@1] Dangerous Drugs Directorate officers for the capital later
carried out the corresponding field tests and confiscated the substances. [@1]

The suspects were taken to the unit's operations base and held at the disposal
of the intervening prosecutor's office. [@1] The investigation continues under
that prosecutor's office for suspected breach of National Law 23.737 on
Narcotics. [@1]
