On Thursday, Harpur police detained a youth at a tea shop near Adapur railway
station on a tip-off. [@1] Sub-divisional police officer Manish Anand said
police had been told a youth was roaming in Adapur with a desi katta at his
waist. [@1] The detained youth was identified as Vipul Giri alias Sanjay Giri,
son of Balmiki Giri of Belwa Giri Tola, and the tea shop was said to belong to
Pradeep Giri. [@1] A search found a loaded desi katta and five live cartridges
on him. [@1]

Acting on his statement, police searched his house and recovered two packets of
a charas-like substance from a hole in the room wall, weighing 1 kg 70 g. [@1]
The house search was carried out on the instructions of station head Priyanka
Kumari in the presence of a magistrate and two independent witnesses, who found
two plastic packets weighing 1 kg 70 g. [@2] A third account likewise reports a
desi katta, five live cartridges and 1 kg 700 g of suspected charas recovered in
the search. [@3]

The youth told police he had come armed near Adapur station to avenge a beating
by some boys three days earlier and would have gone to their homes to kill them
if they did not come to the station. [@1] He also told interrogators he had been
involved in earlier criminal activity, including a firing incident in Mirzapur
in Uttar Pradesh and illegal activities with organisations in Uttar Pradesh.
[@1] Police said a team would be sent to Uttar Pradesh to verify his
confessional statement, and after questioning the youth was sent to judicial
custody while the Harpur team and Adapur station head Priyanka Kumari were
present. [@1]
