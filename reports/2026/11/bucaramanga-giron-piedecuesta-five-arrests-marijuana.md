Five suspected drug sellers were arrested during a series of Bucaramanga
Metropolitan Police operations across Bucaramanga and the municipalities of
Piedecuesta and Girón, in Santander. [@1] One was a 24-year-old woman known as
'La Diabla', caught by officers of the Special Operations Group (GOES) in the
Bosque Norte neighbourhood with a bag holding 2,238 doses of cocaine derivatives
and marijuana. [@1] She was captured in flagrancy on suspicion of trafficking,
manufacturing or carrying narcotics. [@2]

In the San Rafael neighbourhood a 49-year-old man who tried to hide a bag in a
wooded area and flee was intercepted after a preventive follow; the package held
3,410 units of artisanal marijuana cigarettes, coca base and cocaine
derivatives, weighing about 8.5 kilograms. [@1] In Villa Nueva, Piedecuesta,
GOES officers caught a 38-year-old man with two bags containing about two
kilograms of marijuana and 208 doses of cocaine derivatives. [@1] In Ciudadela
Nuevo Girón two men aged 24 and 27 were detained by Police Attention Zone (ZAP)
units carrying about 700 grams of cocaine derivatives, 250 grams of coca base
and 400 further doses of cocaine derivatives. [@1] In its overall balance the
police said the procedures seized 1.5 kilograms of cocaine derivatives, 1.3
kilograms of coca base and 3.5 kilograms of marijuana. [@1] Police estimated the
overall operation had cut more than 31,400 million pesos from criminal
drug-trafficking finances. [@1]

The operations were led by GOES, the Available Force Group and the Police
Attention Zones (ZAP), with patrols, searches and checks aimed at identifying
storage and distribution points. [@1] Brigadier General Miguel Andrés Camelo
Sánchez, commander of the Bucaramanga Metropolitan Police, highlighted
maintaining surveillance in the sectors where the interventions were
concentrated. [@2] The five detainees and the seized substances were placed at
the disposal of the Fiscalía General de la Nación, which will bring them before
a judge to define their legal situation on the suspected offence of trafficking,
manufacturing or carrying narcotics. [@1]
