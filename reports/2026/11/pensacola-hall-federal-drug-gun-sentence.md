EP Hall, 42, of Pensacola was sentenced to three years and one month in federal
prison after pleading guilty to possession of a firearm and ammunition by a
convicted felon and possession of a controlled substance. [@1] U.S. Attorney for
the Northern District of Florida John P. Heekin announced the sentence. [@1]

Court documents show Hall was stopped by law enforcement in March 2026 while
walking on a road in Pensacola. [@2] The encounter was on Market Street in
Pensacola. [@3] Officers found a loaded revolver in his waistband and a fanny
pack containing crack cocaine, marijuana and methamphetamine. [@1] As a
previously convicted felon, Hall was legally prohibited from possessing firearms
or ammunition. [@1]

Heekin said his office remained committed to aggressively prosecuting these
cases until criminals realize there is zero-tolerance for lawlessness in the
Northern District of Florida. [@1] The case was jointly investigated by the
Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms, and Explosives and the Escambia County
Sheriff's Office, and Assistant U.S. Attorney Alicia H. Forbes prosecuted it.
[@1]
