Police in Maków Mazowiecki detained two drivers during weekend road checks, both
suspected of driving under the influence of intoxicating substances. [@1] The
checks were part of an action named Narkotyki – lokalnie (Drugs – locally),
aimed at removing from traffic drivers who might be under the influence of
psychoactive substances. [@1] The first stop came on 13 November at about 19:00
in Szelków commune, where officers pulled over a 24-year-old man driving an
Opel. [@1] The second came the next day, 14 November, at about 15:00 on
Warszawska Street in Maków Mazowiecki. [@2] Traffic officers stopped a
38-year-old man driving a Honda there. [@2] A roadside drug test indicated that
each man might be under the influence of intoxicating substances, and blood
samples were taken for laboratory testing. [@1]

In the Opel, officers found marijuana and mephedrone, a synthetic stimulant.
[@2] They also found e-cigarettes without Polish excise stamps, and seized 20
grams of drugs and 111 e-cigarettes in total. [@1]

The final findings in both cases depend on the laboratory results and the
evidence gathered, and the legal classification of the offences is not yet
settled. [@1] Driving under the influence of an intoxicating substance is an
offence carrying up to three years in prison, while driving after using a
substance that acts like alcohol can be treated as a minor offence, a
wykroczenie. [@1]
