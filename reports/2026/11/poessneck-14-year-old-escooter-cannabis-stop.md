At around 21:20 on Friday 20 November 2026, police in Pößneck, Thuringia,
carried out a general traffic check and stopped a 14-year-old who was riding an
e-scooter. [@1] When the officers checked his fitness to ride, it emerged that
the teenager was driving the e-scooter while under the influence of cannabis.
[@2] During the check the officers also found hashish on the boy and seized it.
[@2]

The police opened an administrative offence proceeding, known in German as an
Ordnungswidrigkeitenverfahren, and took a blood sample from the 14-year-old.
[@2] The boy's guardian was informed about the incident. [@1] After all the
police measures had been concluded, the boy was handed back to the guardian.
[@3] The police body responsible was the Landespolizeiinspektion Saalfeld, the
Thuringian state police inspectorate, which named the Polizeiinspektion
Saale-Orla as its contact unit for the district. [@2]
