Two men arrested in the centre of Douai were tried by the correctional tribunal
on Friday evening and both were incarcerated. [@1] The main suspect was
sentenced to four years in prison. [@1] The main suspect, S.D., 37, was a
'nourrice', a mule recruited to hold a dealer's drugs, who was both in a
precarious situation and a drug addict. [@1]

The operation was carried out on Monday 16 November in the cinéma Majestic
neighbourhood of Douai. [@2] Two men aged 25 and 39 were arrested and placed in
custody, suspected of supplying a cocaine trade. [@2] Officers found the drugs
in two houses on rue d'Ocre. [@2] They seized 4.35 kg of pure cocaine, 608 grams
of cannabis resin, 396 grams of cannabis herb and €8,281 in cash. [@2] The
combined resale value of all the drugs was put at about €400,000. [@2]

Douai's narcotics brigade made the arrests after several weeks of surveillance
in a preliminary investigation. [@3] Investigators also examined seized phones
containing drug photographs, messages and price lists that could confirm the
trafficking. [@2]
