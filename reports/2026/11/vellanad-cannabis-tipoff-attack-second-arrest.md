Aryanad police arrested the second accused in the case of a youth attacked on
the allegation that he had passed information to the police about cannabis
sales. [@1] The arrested man was identified as 28-year-old Kunjumon, who lives
in Puthanveettil house at Thadatharikathu in Changa Charupara, Vellanad. [@1]

The incident behind the case happened at about 8.30 at night on the 15th of the
month. [@1] Sreekanth was standing at Company Mukku Palayam Junction in Vellanad
when the attack took place. [@1] A four-member gang led by Kunjumon attacked
Sreekanth there with deadly weapons. [@1]

Shortly before the attack, shadow police had caught Kunjumon at Parassala while
he had 52 kg of cannabis with him. [@1] Kunjumon and his associates alleged that
Sreekanth had given the shadow police information about cannabis sales, which
was why Kunjumon had been caught, and they attacked him for that reason. [@1]
Aryanad police said Sreekanth is himself an accused in cases that include
cannabis sales. [@1]
