Railway police apprehended two men at Agartala Railway Station on 16 November
2026. [@1] The two were identified as 19-year-old Bishal Singh, son of Sri Bimal
Singh, of Jangalia in Bishalgarh, Sepahijala district. [@1] The second man was
18-year-old Kishan Roy, son of late Krishna Roy, of the same Jangalia address.
[@1] SI Biswajit Das and other Agartala GRPS staff apprehended the two at about
0900 hrs on 16 November 2026. [@1] They were held on Platform No. 01 on a
sitting bench in front of the parking area, between the RPF post and the
pay-and-use toilet. [@1]

Officers recovered a total of 7.315 kg of suspected ganja from a pitthu bag in
their possession. [@1] Police put the black-market value of the seized ganja at
about Rs 350,000. [@1] Police seized the substance and arrested the accused
under Agartala GRPS Case No. 2026GRP083, registered under Sections
20(b)(ii)(B)/29 of the NDPS Act, 1985. [@1]

The joint Government Railway Police and Railway Protection Force operation was
launched on specific information. [@1] Inspector Raju Bhowmik, officer in charge
of Agartala Railway Police Station, gave details of the operation to the media.
[@1] He said such operations against illegal contraband would continue. [@1]
