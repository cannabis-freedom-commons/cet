Officers of the Citizen Security Secretariat inspected a parcel company on
Avenida José López Portillo in Supermanzana 105, Cancún, during surveillance and
prevention work with a canine unit. [@1] During the inspection the K-9 canine
unit gave a positive marking in front of a cardboard box, so with the
establishment staff's authorisation the agents checked its contents. [@3] Inside
they found 10 transparent plastic bags containing green plant material with
characteristics similar to marijuana. [@3]

Officers seized the box and its contents and transferred them to the State
Attorney General's Office, specifically the prosecutor for combating
narcomenudeo. [@1] A second outlet confirms the seized material was placed at
the disposal of the same specialised narcomenudeo prosecutor and that no
detentions were reported. [@2] No detentions were reported, and the
investigation will seek to establish the parcel's origin, its intended recipient
and how it was to be sent or received. [@1]
