Egypt's Interior Ministry security forces detained four people accused of
managing an unlicensed nightclub in Dokki, Giza. [@1,2,3] The raid took place on 25 November 2026. [@3] Two of the four detainees
had prior criminal records. [@1,2,3] Questioned about the seizure, the four admitted
managing the nightclub without the required licences and possessing the seized
items. [@1,2,3] Police completed the legal procedures against the detainees, and the
public prosecution took over the investigation. [@1,2]

The case began when a video circulated on social media in which residents
complained that a nightclub inside a residential building in Giza was disturbing
them. [@1,2,3] Investigators traced the club and found it was operating without a
licence in the Dokki police district of Giza Governorate. [@1,2,3]

Officers searched the detainees and the premises and seized two bladed weapons.
[@1,2] The same search also seized a quantity of hashish, whose weight and
packaging were not stated. [@1,2,3] Officers also seized three bottles of smuggled
alcoholic drinks from the same premises. [@1,2,3]
