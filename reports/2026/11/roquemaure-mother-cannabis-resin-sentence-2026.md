Gendarmes from the Bagnols-sur-Cèze research brigade's anti-drug group arrested
a woman in her forties at her precarious temporary home in Roquemaure on 29
October 2026. [@1] The search found 197 grams of cannabis resin and nearly 70
grams of cannabis herb in the flat. [@2] Officers also seized 1.615 kg of
cocaine, which is not counted in the cannabis figures. [@2] They seized 10,240
euros in cash at the flat. [@2]

She was prosecuted for transport, detention, offer and acquisition of drugs.
[@1] She had accepted parcels containing cocaine and cannabis from traffickers
in return for payment or a little of the drug. [@1] She resold cocaine or
cannabis to a few consumers. [@1] She also collected the day's sale money from
the delivery riders each evening. [@2] She told the court she had taken a credit
and had bills to pay, which drew her back into the trade. [@2] Prosecutor Salomé
Jacob said she had a trusted place in the network. [@2] The prosecution
requested five years in prison and a 5,000-euro fine. [@2] Her lawyer said she
had cooperated fully with the investigators. [@2]

The hearing took place at the correctional court in Nîmes on 25 November 2026.
[@2] The court sentenced her to three years in prison, two of them suspended
with probation. [@2] She was escorted to Nîmes prison to serve the firm year.
[@2] After that year she must work and undergo treatment and is banned from
returning to Roquemaure. [@2]
