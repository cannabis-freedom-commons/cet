Police and the District Detective Branch arrested three people in separate night
raids in Galachipa and Dashmina upazilas of Patuakhali on 22 November 2026. [@1]
In Galachipa, a police team led by Sub-Inspector Sohel Khan raided Ward 3 of
Bara Gabua village in Golkhali Union at about 10:15 pm and arrested 40-year-old
Md Bahadur Khan, son of Md Sekandar Khan. [@1]

Officers recovered 200 grams of ganja from the back veranda of Bahadur Khan's
house. [@1] In Dashmina, a District Detective Branch team raided the paved road
in front of a shop in Chinguria village in Betagi Sankipur Union at about 10:30
pm and arrested Md Jubayer (24) and Md Bashir Mridha (20) with 40 yaba pills.
[@1]

Galachipa police filed case No. 42 against Bahadur Khan on 23 November 2026
under section 36(1), Table 19(a) of the Narcotics Control Act, 2018. [@1] Police
filed a case under the Narcotics Control Act, 2018 against the two men held by
the DB and sent them to court. [@1] Police described the two raids as part of a
continuing anti-drug drive. [@1]
