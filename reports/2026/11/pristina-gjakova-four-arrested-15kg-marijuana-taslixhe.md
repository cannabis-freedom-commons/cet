Kosovo police arrested four people suspected of drug trafficking in an operation
codenamed Taslixhe. [@1,5,6,7,8,9,10,11,20,26] The four suspects, identified only as SB, FB, NI and Gj.Z., were held for 48 hours on a prosecutor's order. [@11,17,21] The state prosecutor asked the court to order detention of the four defendants to ensure their presence in the criminal proceedings. [@21,24,25,27] The Pristina prosecution's department for serious crimes said the arrests followed special investigative measures and searches of several locations. [@25,27] Prosecutors described the suspected offences as unauthorized purchase, possession, distribution and sale of narcotics, unauthorized possession of weapons, and assisting an offender after a crime. [@24,25,27] The four people arrested were citizens of Kosovo. [@13,14,16,19,23] The operation was carried out at different locations in
Pristina and Gjakova, in coordination with the prosecutor's office. [@3,6,7,8,9,10,11,20,21,24] The operation took place at noon in Pristina, Gjakova and at the Mutivoda border crossing. [@13,14,16,19,23,26] The police report dated the operation to 20 November 2026. [@14,19,22,23,27] The police bulletin recorded the case under the category of drug possession. [@22,26] The police report located the Pristina search on Isa Kastrati street. [@14,19,23] The first operation was carried out by investigators from the Pristina and Gjakova units of the Drug Trafficking Investigation Directorate, with the Rapid Response Unit and the Mutivoda Border Police. [@11,18]
Interior Minister Xhelal Sveçla announced the arrests in a Facebook post. [@2,5,8,9]

Officers seized about 15 kilograms of marijuana during the searches. [@4,5,6,7,8,9,10,11,12,20,21,22,26] Four bags of suspected hashish, described as chocolate, were also seized. [@21,24,27] Police put the amount of marijuana seized at 14 kilograms and 815 grams. [@13,15,16,18,19,22,23,24,25,26] The prosecution later put the seized marijuana at 15.443 kilograms. [@25,27] They
also seized an unspecified quantity of cocaine, cash, weapons and ammunition,
mobile phones and two vehicles. [@2,5,6,7,8,9,10,20] The prosecution put the seized cocaine at 1.7 grams. [@21,24,25,27] The police statement also listed two revolvers, 62 cartridges, 9,120 euros, 350 Swiss francs, two cars, four mobile phones and a digital scale. [@11,17,21] The minister gave no quantity for the
cocaine, no cash amount and no type or number of weapons. [@1,8,9] The places
searched and the identities of the four arrested were not published. [@1,9]

No charges had been announced and the prosecutor's office had said nothing
further about the suspected offences or the next steps in the case. [@1,8,9] Sveçla
described the operation as part of the institutions' continuing fight against
organised crime and drug trafficking. [@1,8,9,10,20] He said the security institutions
would keep acting to guarantee the rule of law throughout Kosovo. [@2,9]
