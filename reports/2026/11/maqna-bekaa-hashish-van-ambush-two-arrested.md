A security force ambushed a passenger van in the Bekaa town of Maqna; the van
was carrying a quantity of hashish. [@1] The ambush turned into an arrest
operation. [@1] Two people were arrested. [@1]

They were the van's driver and another person who was with him. [@1] Gunfire
broke out during the operation and the van was hit by several bullets. [@1] The
vehicle, loaded with hashish, was seized. [@1]

Security procedures continued after the operation to establish the circumstances
surrounding it. [@1] Investigations were expected to establish where the seized
material was headed and the background to its transport. [@2]
