Police in Bosnia and Herzegovina arrested a Montenegrin national, B.D., 34, and
A.K., 23, from Sarajevo, in a continuation of an operation called Transporter.
[@1] The two were stopped during a handover on 18 November 2026 in Canton
Sarajevo, under the supervision of the BiH Prosecutor's Office and on an order
of the Court of Bosnia and Herzegovina. [@2] The Federal Police Administration,
or FUP, said the operation was a continuation of the months-long Transporter
action against international drug trafficking. [@3] The two men are suspected of
unauthorised trafficking in narcotic drugs under Article 195 of the Bosnian
Criminal Code. [@2]

Officers searched the two suspects, a MAN truck and a Golf VI car, and said they
found about 50 kilograms of a substance resembling cannabis. [@2] The in4s
account described the haul as about 50 kilograms of cannabis, along with several
mobile phones and more than 17,000 euros. [@1] Both vehicles were temporarily
seized: the MAN truck with Montenegrin plates and the Golf VI with Bosnian
plates. [@4]

The two detainees were to be taken to FUP premises for criminal processing and
then handed to the BiH Prosecutor's Office with a report on the offence. [@1]
