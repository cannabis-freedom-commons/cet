Department of Narcotics Control officers raided drug dealers' dens in Karail
slum in Dhaka. [@1] The operation involved two separate raids in the Jamaibazar
area under Banani police station on Wednesday, 25 November. [@1] The first raid
targeted a rented room in Mannan's house in Unit-1, Block-C, Muktar water-tank
lane in Jamaibazar. [@1] That room had been rented by Ibrahim Khalil, Sujon Mia
and Shahid. [@1] Officers arrested 23-year-old Ibrahim Khalil at the first
house, while 41-year-old Sujon Mia and 37-year-old Shahid fled. [@1] Officers
then raided the house of a man named Badal in the same area and arrested
30-year-old Ramzan Ali, described as the cashier for Sujon Mia and Shahid's drug
business. [@1]

In the first search officers found six kilograms of ganja alongside 2,500 yaba
pills and Tk 188,000 in alleged drug-sale proceeds. [@1] From Ramzan Ali
officers seized 100 yaba pills. [@1] Across both raids the department reported a
total of six kilograms of ganja, 2,600 yaba pills, Tk 188,000 and one button
phone. [@1]

The department said fugitives Sujon Mia and Shahid controlled drug trading in
various parts of Karail slum. [@1] The department said the two men faced
allegations of selling ganja, yaba and heroin. [@1] Two separate cases were
filed at Banani police station under the Narcotics Control Act, 2018 (amended,
2026). [@1]
