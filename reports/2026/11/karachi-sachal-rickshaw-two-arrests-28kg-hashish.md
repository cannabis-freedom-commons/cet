Sachal police arrested two men in Karachi for allegedly supplying narcotics
around the city in a rickshaw. [@1] The suspects were identified as Muhammad
Amin, son of Ramzan, and Idrees, son of Amanat Rehman. [@1] The raid in Madina
Colony, Scheme 33, was conducted on a tip-off, according to an East Zone police
spokesperson. [@1]

Police recovered more than 28 kilograms of hashish from the vehicle. [@1] Police
impounded the rickshaw used for the supply. [@1] Police said the pair had been
using the rickshaw to deliver drugs to different parts of the city. [@1] Mobile
phones and cash believed to be proceeds from drug sales were also seized from
the suspects. [@1]

Karachi Police announced the operation in a post on X. [@1] A case has been
registered against both men under the Narcotics Act while further investigation
is under way. [@1] Police are also gathering details of their previous criminal
records. [@1]
