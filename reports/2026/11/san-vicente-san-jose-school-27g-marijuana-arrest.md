Plainclothes officers watched a suspected exchange of drugs and money in front
of the school in central San Vicente. [@1] A 50-year-old man was detained on the
afternoon of Monday 16 November in San Vicente on suspicion of selling marijuana
in front of the San José school. [@1] Investigators described the 50-year-old as
the alleged seller already under investigation. [@1] The alleged seller was in a
Citroën car, while the other man was identified as the alleged buyer. [@1]
Police said they detected two men apparently exchanging money and nylon bags in
the street. [@1] The intervention happened at 14:00. [@1]

Officers seized four transparent bags holding a total of seven grams of
marijuana and another black bag with 20 grams of the same substance. [@1] In
total, 27 grams of marijuana were seized. [@1] Officers also found 58,000 pesos
in cash and seized the Citroën driven by the suspect. [@1] The vehicle had no
legal impediments, according to informants. [@1]

The operation was carried out by staff of Comisaría 1ª de San Vicente after
surveillance to locate the suspect. [@1] The plainclothes agents worked around
Almirante Brown and 9 de Julio streets and waited undercover to carry out the
operation. [@1] The case was referred to the decentralised San Vicente
prosecutor’s office. [@1] The prosecutor’s office endorsed the police actions
and ordered the man’s apprehension for alleged possession of narcotics for
commercialisation. [@1] The investigation remains with the judiciary while
further steps are carried out. [@1]
