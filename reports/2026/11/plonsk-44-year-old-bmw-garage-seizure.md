The intervention took place on Sunday, 22 November. [@1] Officers from the
patrol and intervention unit of the Płońsk police stopped a BMW because prior
findings indicated its driver might carry drugs. [@1] The driver was a
44-year-old resident of Płońsk. [@1] When asked about banned substances, he
admitted having marijuana and handed officers a plastic container with dried
plant material. [@1] In a backpack on the passenger seat officers found two
sachets of white crystalline substance, which the man said was mephedrone and
which preliminary tests confirmed, with the amount exceeding one kilogram. [@1]
The man was detained. [@1]

Detectives found that the detainee rented a garage where he might store drugs
and searched the premises. [@1] There they found packages with dried plant
material, white clumped substance and crystalline substance, and seized two
electronic scales. [@1] Preliminary tests identified the substances as
marijuana, mephedrone and amphetamine. [@1] Police said a total of 3 kilograms
of drugs were secured in the car and garage, a combined figure for all
substances rather than a cannabis-only weight. [@1]

On the prosecutor's motion, the court ordered the man held in temporary arrest
for three months. [@1] The offence he is accused of carries up to 10 years'
imprisonment. [@1]
