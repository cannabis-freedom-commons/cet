The Grant County Sheriff's Office said a traffic stop on Interstate 75 early on
Friday 20 November 2026 led to the arrest of Brandon Everitt and Brian Sallee.
[@2] Deputies stopped the vehicle at about 12:47 a.m. for reckless driving; it
was driven by Everitt. [@1] The stop was on Interstate 75 in Grant County,
northern Kentucky. [@1]

During the stop, deputies said they smelled marijuana and that Everitt was
showing signs of impairment. [@1] Everitt was arrested at the scene on traffic
violations including DUI and other drug-related charges. [@1] Sallee, who was
also in the vehicle, was arrested at the scene on multiple drug charges. [@1]

The sheriff's office said Sgt. Dalton and Deputy Gillispie made the stop. [@2] A
search of the vehicle uncovered items, the sheriff's office said. [@2] Everitt
and Sallee were lodged in the Grant County Jail. [@1]
