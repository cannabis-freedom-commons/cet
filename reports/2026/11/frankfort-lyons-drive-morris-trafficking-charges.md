Frankfort police executed a search warrant at a Lyons Drive residence at 11:08
a.m. on Sunday morning and located Brandon Morris in the basement. [@1]
Suspected cocaine, marijuana and crack cocaine were in plain view in the
basement where Morris was found. [@1]

Officers found multiple bags and bottles with suspected cocaine, jars and
bottles with suspected marijuana, plus a digital scale, cut straws, rolling
papers, a grinder and other drug paraphernalia. [@1] In the basement where
Morris lived, officers found bundled cash totaling $5,021, more suspected
cocaine in plastic bags inside a magnetic lock box, and suspected crack cocaine
on a porcelain tile on the bedside table. [@1] Morris reportedly admitted that
the narcotics were cocaine. [@1]

He was charged with first-degree trafficking in a controlled substance involving
more than 4 grams of cocaine, trafficking in marijuana involving less than 8
ounces, and possession of drug paraphernalia. [@1] Morris was also served with a
bench warrant out of Fayette District Court for failure to appear. [@1] He was
lodged in the Franklin County Regional Jail on a $10,000 full-cash bond. [@1]
