ROMU officers of Paranaguá's Municipal Civil Guard seized cocaine, marijuana and
a homemade firearm during a routine patrol in Vila Santa Maria on the afternoon
of Friday 20 November 2026. [@1] Three people were arrested. [@2] The patrol car
was moving through the neighbourhood's alleys and narrow streets when officers
saw a young man smoking a marijuana cigarette. [@2] The suspect tried to run
into a shack when he saw the patrol approaching. [@1] The 20-year-old was
chased, caught and arrested. [@2]

As officers approached the first suspect, they caught two men, aged 20 and 28,
preparing portions of cocaine inside the property. [@1] A homemade 12-gauge
shotgun with one intact cartridge was found inside the shack. [@1] At the site
officers found several plastic vials containing cocaine, a portion of marijuana,
zip-lock bags used to package drugs, knives and a precision scale. [@1]

All three and the seized material were taken to the duty desk of the Cidadã
police station. [@2]
