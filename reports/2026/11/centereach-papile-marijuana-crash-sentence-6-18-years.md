Matthew Papile, 18, of Port Jefferson was sentenced on 19 November 2026 to six
to 18 years in prison for a May 2024 crash in Centereach that killed 73-year-old
Marion Napolitano and seriously injured her passenger. [@2,3,4] On 15 September 2026
Papile pleaded guilty to aggravated vehicular homicide, manslaughter, aggravated
vehicular assault, assault, leaving the scene of an incident resulting in death,
criminal possession of stolen property, driving while impaired by drugs and
reckless driving. [@1,3] Papile entered the guilty plea before Acting Supreme
Court Justice Karen Kerr. [@2] Napolitano was taken to Stony Brook University
Hospital where she was later pronounced deceased. [@2,3,4] A front-seat passenger in
the Corolla suffered a traumatic brain injury and remains seriously injured more
than two years later. [@2,3]

Papile was 16 at the time of the crash. [@2,4] On the evening of 4 May 2024 Papile
drove a stolen 2008 Land Rover LR2 while impaired by marijuana. [@2,3] Prosecutors
said Papile was broadcasting on Instagram while driving the stolen Land Rover.
[@1,3,4] Earlier that morning a Coram resident reported his blue 2008 Land Rover LR2
missing from his driveway. [@2] At approximately 9:10 p.m. that evening, Papile
was driving the stolen car eastbound on Middle Country Road when he struck the
side of another vehicle. [@2] He fled and continued to drive recklessly, weaving
across the eastbound lanes, cutting off other vehicles and speeding. [@2] He
then slammed into the rear of the 1994 Toyota Corolla driven by Napolitano,
sending it off the road and into a telephone pole and a tree. [@2,3] The stolen
Land Rover spun out of control and came to rest in the center lane of travel.
[@2] Three Good Samaritans, including an off-duty Suffolk County police officer,
chased Papile and held him until officers arrived. [@2,3,4] Responding police
officers observed signs of marijuana impairment and arrested Papile at the
scene. [@2] Papile admitted smoking marijuana and consented to a blood test.
[@2,4] Toxicology results showed the presence of tetrahydrocannabinol in his
system. [@2,4]

On 19 November 2026 Papile was sentenced to six to 18 years in prison and his
driver’s license was ordered revoked. [@2,3] The case was prosecuted by Assistant
District Attorneys Raymond Varuolo and Jeffrey Rosenheck of the Vehicular Crime
Bureau. [@2]
