A teenager was apprehended on the night of Tuesday 17 November 2026 in the
Magano neighbourhood of Garanhuns, in Pernambuco's Agreste region, on suspicion
of drug trafficking. [@1] Military Police officers of the 9th Military Police
Battalion (9º BPM) caught the teenager negotiating drugs on a street in the
neighbourhood, the Military Police said. [@1] When the officers approached him
they found portions of marijuana and crack in his possession. [@1]

The youth was taken to the 18th Sectional Civil Police Station in Garanhuns,
which covers the municipality, where the applicable measures were taken. [@1] No
weight or number of portions was published for the marijuana, and no other
person was reported arrested. [@1]
