Ontario Provincial Police arrested Joshua Boyer, 39, of Mississauga First
Nation, at a Reduce Impaired Driving Everywhere check on Village Road in the
community. [@1] Officers from the East Algoma detachment were running the check
when a white pickup truck pulled in at about 2:30 p.m. on a Saturday. [@1] While
speaking with the driver they detected an odour of cannabis and saw signs
consistent with drug impairment, the OPP said. [@1]

The driver was arrested and taken to the Espanola OPP detachment for further
testing. [@1] Boyer was charged with operation while impaired by drug, driving
while under suspension and driving a vehicle with cannabis readily available.
[@1] The pickup truck was impounded for seven days. [@1]

None of the allegations have been tested in court. [@1] Boyer is scheduled to
appear before the Ontario Court of Justice in Elliot Lake on 7 January 2027.
[@1]
