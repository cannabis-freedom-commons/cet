Rishikesh Kotwali police arrested the absconding second accused in a
ganja-smuggling case. [@1] Police said the accused, Ramavtar, had ordered ganja
through the previously arrested Naurangi Lal. [@1] Station officer Yashpal Singh
Bisht said Ramavtar faced nine cases from 2015 to 2026, eight of them under the
NDPS Act. [@1]

On 24 July, IDPL outpost police arrested bike rider Naurangi Lal, a resident of
Nagla Bhambu Jat village, Sasni, Hathras, Uttar Pradesh, near Gol Chakkar during
checking. [@1] Officers recovered 3 kg 95 g of illegal ganja from his
possession. [@1] Kotwali police registered an FIR against the accused under the
NDPS Act in that case. [@1]

During investigation, police questioned the accused and analysed his mobile call
detail records and other evidence, which surfaced the name of Ramavtar,
originally of Mayakund, Rishikesh and currently of Lane No. 10, Gujjar Basti,
Shyampur. [@1] The investigation found that Ramavtar had ordered ganja from
Naurangi Lal, after which a police team arrested Ramavtar on Friday evening.
