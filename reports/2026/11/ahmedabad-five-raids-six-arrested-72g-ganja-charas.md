Ahmedabad Police arrested six people after conducting raids at five locations
across the city as part of a crackdown on illegal drug trafficking. [@1] The
raids were conducted in Danilimda, Dariapur, Raipur and Meghaninagar by local
police teams, the Special Operations Group (SOG) and the Anti-Narcotics Wing.
[@1]

Dariapur Police arrested Faisal Mustaqbeg Mirza on a public road near Lunshawad
Tran Batti. [@1] Police recovered 63.170 grams of ganja and 4.560 grams of
charas from him. [@1] Meghaninagar Police arrested Vishal alias Gango Ramesh
Patni from Bhargav Road near lanes 4 and 5 of Amarajinagar. [@1] Police
recovered 4.140 grams of ganja from him. [@1]

The Anti-Narcotics Wing, Sector-1, along with Kagadapith Police, arrested
Firozbhai Malek and Sohel Sheikh outside Raipur Bhajiya House. [@1] The two were
allegedly carrying 6.180 grams of mephedrone. [@1] Police seized 352 bottles of
codeine-based cough syrup, mephedrone, ganja and charas, along with vehicles and
mobile phones. [@1] Six other people allegedly linked to the supply of the
seized substances have been named as wanted accused. [@1]
