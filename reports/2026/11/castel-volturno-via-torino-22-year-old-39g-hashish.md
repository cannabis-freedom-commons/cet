Carabinieri of the local Tenenza reported a 22-year-old at liberty late on 18
November 2026, treating him as seriously suspected of possessing narcotics for
dealing after finding hashish and marijuana on him. [@1] The stop was on via
Torino in Castel Volturno, where officers halted a Renault Modus carrying three
people, including the driver, during a targeted territorial control. [@1] The
occupants' particularly agitated manner and a strong smell of plant matter from
the cabin led the Carabinieri to search the people and the vehicle. [@1]

On the 22-year-old officers found 13 cellophane wraps of hashish weighing 37.3
grams in all, plus another wrap with 1.71 grams of marijuana. [@1] They also
seized 130 euros in cash and a sheet of handwritten notes considered possibly
linked to accounts of the illegal activity. [@1] All of the material was seized
and placed at the disposal of the judicial authority, which had been informed.
[@1]

The case is at the preliminary-investigation stage, and the 22-year-old remains
presumed innocent until any final conviction. [@1] A second account identifies
him as a local man and says the Castel Volturno Tenenza reported him at liberty
for possessing narcotics for dealing. [@2] The check was part of local services
aimed at preventing and countering drug dealing. [@1]
