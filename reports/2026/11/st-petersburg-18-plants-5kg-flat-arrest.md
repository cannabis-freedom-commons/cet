Drug-control officers detained a 41-year-old St Petersburg man in mid-November
near house 15 on Konstantin Zaslonov Street on suspicion of trafficking in
banned substances. [@2] A criminal case for attempted illegal sale of drugs on
an especially large scale was opened against the detainee, who was placed in a
temporary detention facility while the choice of a restraint measure was
pending. [@1] The investigation qualified the case under part 3 of Article 30
and part 4 of Article 228.1 of the Russian Criminal Code. [@2]

During a search of his flat, police found 18 cannabis bushes with roots,
cultivation equipment, electronic scales and packaging materials. [@1] Officers
also seized four packets of crushed plant mass and three bundles prepared for
sale, with a combined weight of more than five kilograms. [@2] An expert
examination has so far confirmed more than one kilogram of cannabis in one of
the packets, while the remaining seized substances are still being examined in
the laboratory. [@1]

According to the preliminary investigation, the man grew cannabis to produce
marijuana for subsequent sale. [@3] Police are working to establish whether he
had accomplices, through which channels he planned to sell and whether he was
involved in other offences. [@1]
