Police in Sidoarjo, East Java, arrested AT alias Acle, a 29-year-old factory
worker from Waru district, on suspicion of distributing cannabis. [@1,7,8,10,11,13,15,16,17,18,20,21,23] Kapolresta Sidoarjo AKBP Bobby A. Condroputra announced the case at a press conference on 20 November 2026. [@15,18,24] Police said the dried cannabis was intended for distribution among workers and students. [@8] He was
detained around 10:00 WIB on Monday 16 November 2026 at the roadside in
Keboansikep village, Gedangan district, after receiving a courier parcel. [@3,10,11,15,17,18,21,23] Police said the fourth parcel had not yet been placed at a drop point when they arrested him. [@2,7,18,19] Police said AT placed the parcels at drop points chosen by the controller, using what they described as a tempel or ranjau system. [@6,7]
The operation began with a public tip-off about a suspected drug transaction in
Gedangan, police said. [@3,4,10,11,13,17,20,21] After surveillance, officers used a disguise to confirm the suspect's whereabouts and arrested him at a warung in the Keboananom area of Gedangan. [@8] Sidoarjo police chief AKBP Bobby Adimas Condroputra announced the arrest on 20 November 2026. [@6,16,22]

Officers seized 3 kilograms of cannabis packed in a cardboard box and one mobile
phone used for the transactions. [@3,6,7,8,10,11,15,17,18,19,20,21] The seized parcel was sent through Lion Parcel under receipt number 11LP1794296872578. [@15] Officers also seized a blue Samsung A07 phone with the dried cannabis. [@13] The parcel seized on 16 November arrived through Lion Parcel under receipt number 11LP1794296872578 and was taken before it could be passed on. [@12] The seized phone was a blue Samsung A07. [@10] Police also seized a blue Samsung A07 mobile phone. [@4] Police said the parcel was the fourth
delivery in a series since September 2026, with an accumulated 11 kilograms
received across the four shipments. [@3,6,7,11,13,15,16,17,18,20,21,24] A second shipment of about 2 kilograms in early October 2026 was left at a different spot in the same Tambak Sawah warehouse area. [@15] Police said the first shipment, in early September 2026, was about 2 kilograms left in roadside bushes at the Tambak Sawah warehouse area in Waru. [@15] On 22 October 2026 he received about 4 kilograms; 1 kilogram was left at a predetermined spot and 3 kilograms were handed to a person identified as IYEK at the suspect's house on the instruction of someone initialled BEN. [@14,23,24] On the first two shipments, in early September and early October 2026, he received about 2 kilograms of cannabis each time and left the parcels in bushes in the Tambak Sawah warehouse area, Waru district. [@14] Police arrested him before the cannabis from that delivery could be placed at another location. [@13] On 22 October 2026 he received about 4 kilograms, left 1 kilogram at Tambak Sawah and gave 3 kilograms to a person identified as Iyek at his house on Bendot's orders. [@13,22] In early October 2026 he again received about 2 kilograms to leave at the Tambak Sawah warehouse area. [@13,22] In early September 2026 he received about 2 kilograms and left it in bushes beside the road at the Tambak Sawah warehouse area in Waru. [@13,18,22] On 22 October 2026 he received 4 kilograms; 1 kilogram was left at a spot set by the controller and the remaining 3 kilograms were handed to a person known as Iyek at the suspect's house on Bendot's orders. [@12,16,17] On the second shipment, in early October 2026, he again received 2 kilograms and left it at a different point around the same warehouse area. [@12,23] On the first shipment, in early September 2026, he received about 2 kilograms of cannabis and left it in roadside bushes at the Tambak Sawah warehouse area in Waru district. [@12,23] AT said he worked as an intermediary who picked up the parcels and left them at set points for others to collect, a method police call the 'sistem ranjau' (dead-drop system). [@11] The 16 November parcel of 3 kilograms arrived through the courier Lion Parcel and was seized before it could be redistributed. [@10,17,22] On 22 October 2026 he received about 4 kilograms, left about 1 kilogram at a set location, and handed about 3 kilograms to a person known as IYEK at his house on Bendot's instruction. [@5,6,7] The first shipment, in early September 2026, was about 2 kilograms and was left in bushes by the road near the Tambak Sawah warehousing area in Waru district. [@5,6,7] AT allegedly acted on orders from a
controller known as Bendot, who is on a wanted list. [@3,6,11,13,15,16,17,18,20,21,22] On 22 October 2026 he received about 4 kilograms, of which about 1 kilogram was left at a designated spot and 3 kilograms were handed to a person known as IYEK at his house on Bendot's instructions. [@15] Police said they were still working to establish his role and to trace the alleged controller and others in the network. [@7,22] Police said they planned to work with courier companies so they would report parcels suspected of containing drugs, and to coordinate with the East Java police IT division to trace the suspect's communications and identify others involved. economic value of the cannabis evidence at 120 million rupiah. [@4,9,10,11,13,17,19,20,21,24] The suspect was held in a cell at the Sidoarjo police headquarters for further examination and legal processing. [@8,16] Police estimated the case had saved about 20,000 people from potential drug abuse. [@4,17,19]

He was charged under Article 114(2) or 111(2) of Indonesia's 2009 Narcotics Law,
as adjusted by a 2026 law. [@2,5,6,10,11,12,13,14,15,17,18,19,20,22,24] He was held at the Polresta Sidoarjo detention centre. [@13] The offence carries a maximum sentence of life
imprisonment or the death penalty. [@2,6,10,12,14,15,17,18,19,21,22,24] AT is being held at the Sidoarjo police detention centre (Rumah Tahanan Polresta Sidoarjo). [@11]
