Police in Sidoarjo, East Java, arrested AT alias Acle, a 29-year-old factory
worker from Waru district, on suspicion of distributing cannabis. [@1] He was
detained around 10:00 WIB on Monday 16 November 2026 at the roadside in
Keboansikep village, Gedangan district, after receiving a courier parcel. [@3] Police said the fourth parcel had not yet been placed at a drop point when they arrested him. [@2] Police said AT placed the parcels at drop points chosen by the controller, using what they described as a tempel or ranjau system. [@6]
The operation began with a public tip-off about a suspected drug transaction in
Gedangan, police said. [@3,4] Sidoarjo police chief AKBP Bobby Adimas Condroputra announced the arrest on 20 November 2026. [@6]

Officers seized 3 kilograms of cannabis packed in a cardboard box and one mobile
phone used for the transactions. [@3,6] Police also seized a blue Samsung A07 mobile phone. [@4] Police said the parcel was the fourth
delivery in a series since September 2026, with an accumulated 11 kilograms
received across the four shipments. [@3,6] The final parcel arrived through Lion Parcel with tracking number 11LP1794296872578. [@5,6] A second delivery of about 2 kilograms followed in early October 2026, and a third on 22 October 2026 brought about 4 kilograms, of which about 1 kilogram was left at a drop point and 3 kilograms handed to a person identified as IYEK at the suspect's home. [@5,6] The first shipment, in early September 2026, was about 2 kilograms and was left in bushes by the road near the Tambak Sawah warehousing area in Waru district. [@5,6] AT allegedly acted on orders from a
controller known as Bendot, who is on a wanted list. [@3,6] Police put the
economic value of the cannabis evidence at 120 million rupiah. [@4] Police estimated the case had saved about 20,000 people from potential drug abuse. [@4]

He was charged under Article 114(2) or 111(2) of Indonesia's 2009 Narcotics Law,
as adjusted by a 2026 law. [@2,5,6] The offence carries a maximum sentence of life
imprisonment or the death penalty. [@2,6]
