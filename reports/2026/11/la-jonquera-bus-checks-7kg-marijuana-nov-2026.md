National Police bus-passenger controls at the La Jonquera border detained four
men of foreign origin in the Alt Empordà over the weekend. [@1] On 21 and 22
November, officers detained a Moroccan man and a Portuguese man as alleged
authors of a public-health offence after finding marijuana among their
belongings. [@1] Officers detained two Senegalese men who were allegedly trying
to leave Catalonia with contraband tobacco. [@1] Apart from those four, officers
detained three more people subject to judicial detention orders and identified
car occupants suspected of curtain-method truck robberies. [@1]

One of the marijuana detainees carried 125 grams while the other carried just
over seven kilograms in a suitcase. [@1] One tobacco detainee carried 1,055
packs in his luggage while the other had 2,661 packs. [@1] Officers also found
two more ownerless suitcases holding 2,000 packs. [@1] Between Saturday and
Sunday officers seized 5,716 packs of tobacco of different brands with a total
value of 16,388 euros. [@1]

The National Police mounts routine controls at the La Jonquera border and checks
the belongings of passengers who mostly travel by bus to combat drug trafficking
and illegal tobacco movement. [@1] Police said the car contained tools for those
thefts such as screwdrivers and knives, used to rob trucks parked in service
areas. [@1]
