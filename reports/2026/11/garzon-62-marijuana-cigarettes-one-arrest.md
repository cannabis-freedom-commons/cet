National Police officers stopped a citizen for a routine inspection in the La
Libertad neighbourhood of Garzón, Huila, while carrying out registration and
control activities. [@1] The National Police reported the arrest in flagrante as
part of action against microtrafficking in Garzón, saying the man was allegedly
transporting narcotic substances. [@1]

During the inspection officers found a transparent bag containing 62 cigarettes
that, by their characteristics, resembled marijuana, inside a bag the man was
carrying. [@1] After the find the man was arrested and taken with the seized
material before the competent authorities for judicial proceedings. [@1]

According to the police, both the detainee and the substance were placed at the
disposal of the Fiscalía General de la Nación, where he must answer for the
alleged offence of trafficking, manufacturing or possession of narcotics. [@1]
The authorities said they would continue control, registration and prevention
operations across the municipality to counter local drug trafficking. [@1]
Police called on the community to report promptly any activity linked to the
sale of illicit substances, saying such information was key to the operations.
