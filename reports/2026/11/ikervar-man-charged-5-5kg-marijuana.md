The Vas County Chief Prosecution Office has charged a multiple repeat-offender
man over marijuana stored in Ikervar. [@2] According to the indictment, the man
stored more than five and a half kilograms of marijuana at his residence in
Ikervar in the summer of 2024 on behalf of an acquaintance, and investigators
seized it. [@1] Investigators seized a significant quantity of drugs at a
property in Ikervar. [@1]

The defendant is currently in custody. [@1] The stored cannabis had a total
active-ingredient content of nearly seven times the lower limit for a
significant quantity. [@2] Prosecutors charged possession of a significant
quantity of narcotics and proposed an unsuspended prison term plus a ban from
public affairs. [@1]

The Szombathely Tribunal will decide on the defendant's criminal liability. [@1]
