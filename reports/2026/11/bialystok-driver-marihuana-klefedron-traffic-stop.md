Police in Białystok said a 23-year-old driver stopped in a traffic check had
more than 130 grams of drugs on him, was sent to custody and faces up to ten
years' imprisonment. [@1] Officers of the Białystok police prevention unit
stopped a passenger Opel for the check. [@1] The driver did not hide that he had
had conflicts with the law in the past. [@1] During the check officers found two
foil bags holding ziplock bags of a white crystallised substance and, in the
man's pouch, a green dried plant material. [@1] A preliminary drug test
identified the secured substances as klefedron and marihuana. [@1]

Police said the combined weight of the drugs was over 130 grams. [@1] The man
was charged with possessing a significant quantity of psychotropic substances
and supplying them for financial gain. [@1]

A court remanded the 23-year-old in custody for three months. [@1]
