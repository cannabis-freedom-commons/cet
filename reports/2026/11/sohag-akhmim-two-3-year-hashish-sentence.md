Sohag Criminal Appeals Court sentenced two men, identified only by the initials
S.M., a worker, and A.A., to three years in prison each and a 50,000 Egyptian
pound fine each on Saturday 21 November 2026. [@1] The first defendant was also
given an additional one-year prison term for possessing a weapon. [@2] Both men
were convicted of trafficking the narcotic hashish and other narcotic substances
within Akhmim markaz, a district in Sohag Governorate. [@1]

The case dates to 2026, when criminal investigation officers in Akhmim markaz
received information that the two men were trafficking narcotic substances and
marketing their goods to their customers. [@1] After the procedures were
legalised and investigations completed, officers confirmed the information
received from a confidential source and arrested both men. [@1] When confronted,
both men confessed to trafficking in narcotic substances, and they were referred
to the criminal court, which went on to issue the sentence. [@1]
