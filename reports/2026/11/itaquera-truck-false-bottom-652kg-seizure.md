Civil Police seized approximately 652 kilograms of marijuana on Wednesday 11
November in a parking lot in Itaquera in the east zone of São Paulo. [@1] The
marijuana was hidden in a secret compartment built into the body of a truck.
[@1] The 35-year-old man driving the truck was arrested in flagrante. [@1]

The operation began as an investigation into possible truck thefts and
robberies, and officers checking the parking lot found two cars with signs of
tampered identification marks. [@1] Using electronic equipment, investigators
recovered the cars' original data and found both had robbery records. [@1]
Officers noticed a strong smell of marijuana coming from the parked truck's body
and carried out a closer inspection. [@1] Inside the false bottom officers found
approximately 652 kilograms of marijuana. [@1] About 142.6 kilograms underwent
initial forensic examination that confirmed THC, and the other approximately 510
kilograms were still to be analysed. [@1]

The truck itself also had a robbery record. [@1] Two men at the site fled toward
a nearby community while the truck driver stayed and was arrested in flagrante
for drug trafficking. [@1] Officers seized the two cars, the truck, the drugs
and a mobile phone to support the investigation. [@1] The case was registered at
the 7th Itaquera sectional police station as drug trafficking, receiving stolen
goods and vehicle identification-mark tampering. [@1] A second account confirms
the hidden compartment under the truck body held approximately 652 kilograms and
that part of the material tested positive for THC. [@2] The second account
confirms the truck had a robbery record, two men fled, the driver was arrested,
and the vehicles and a phone were seized. [@2]
