Kalpetta Excise officers arrested main accused Aikkaran A.K. Subair, 36, from
quarters in Koyilandy, Kozhikode district, where he had been hiding after the
Panthippoyil seizure. [@3] Officers also arrested his 27-year-old friend Shiyas
Mustafa of Thengumunda, Padinjarethara, for allegedly sheltering Subair while he
absconded. [@1] Subair had removed his hair and beard to change his appearance
while hiding. [@1] When he saw the excise team he tried to flee and was chased
down on foot by Civil Excise Officer O. Shafi, described as a long-distance
runner. [@3] Both men were produced before Judicial First Class Magistrate
Court-I at Mananthavady. [@1] The court remanded them to Mananthavady district
jail. [@3]

The case concerns 323 grams of hashish oil and 1.362 grams of methamphetamine
seized from a house at Panthippoyil, Padinjarethara. [@1] The seizure had been
made on 15 October at the Panthippoyil house. [@1] Subair was absent during that
early-morning raid and evaded arrest, leaving drugs stored on a shelf to be
seized. [@2] In the house, 323 grams of hashish oil was stored in 190 plastic
bottles. [@4] The oil was allegedly kept ready for distribution to customers.
[@2] Excise said the seized drugs were worth around ₹30 lakh in the local
market. [@2] Excise suspects Subair was operating as a wholesale dealer in
narcotics including hashish oil. [@2] The October raid followed a tip-off about
hashish oil being supplied to customers at resorts and exclusive establishments
in the district. [@2]

Both accused had earlier drug cases, with Subair accused in a Mananthavady
Excise case over 5.184 grams of methamphetamine. [@1] Mustafa was accused in a
Sultan Bathery police case over 8.25 grams of MDMA. [@1] Excise said
investigation into other links of the drug-sale gang was continuing. [@1]
