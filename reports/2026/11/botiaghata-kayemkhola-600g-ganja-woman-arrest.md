Khulna district detective police (DB) arrested Aléya Begum, 40, on suspicion of
selling cannabis in Botiaghata, Khulna. [@1] Officers raided Kayemkhola Hula
village in Gangarampur Union at about 6:20 pm on Tuesday 17 November 2026. [@1]
The operation was led by Inspector Md Nannu Khan of the Khulna district DB. [@1]
Her husband, Akibur Howladar, 46, fled during the raid. [@1]

Officers recovered 600 grams of ganja. [@1] The Khulna report said she was
caught in the act of selling the drug. [@1] Usharalo, citing Khulna district
police, reported that officers found the cannabis under a bed inside the house
of Akibur Howladar, described as a fugitive accused, based on what Aléya Begum
showed and told them. [@2] Usharalo put the cannabis's estimated market value at
3,000 taka. [@2]

The ganja was entered on a seizure list and Aléya Begum was handed over to
Botiaghata police station. [@1] Usharalo reported that a case was filed against
her at Botiaghata police station under the Narcotics Control Act 2018. [@2] The
Khulna report said the process of filing a case under the narcotics law was
still under way. [@1]
