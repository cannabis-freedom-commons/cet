A 24-year-old man was caught red-handed during a registration and control check
carried out by the Policía Nacional in the municipality of Guamal, Meta. [@1]
The check took place in the Pueblo Viejo neighbourhood, in the area known as El
Hueco, where officers were verifying and searching people. [@1] According to the
police report, the man had tried to hide a black bag under a stone, but the
officers discovered its contents. [@1]

When they checked the package, officers found a plant substance that by its
characteristics corresponded to marijuana. [@1] The drug was divided into about
23 ziploc plastic bags with a total weight of 175 grams. [@1] Police valued the
seized substance at close to 230,000 pesos. [@1]

After the find, the police arrested the man and informed him of his rights. [@1]
He was transferred and placed at the disposal of the Fiscalía 23 Local de
Acacías, where he is to answer for the suspected offence of trafficking,
manufacturing or possessing illegal drugs. [@1] The commander of the Meta police
department, Colonel Nelson Eduardo Zambrano Esguerra, restated his force's
commitment to fighting local drug trafficking and invited residents to report
suspicious activity on the 123 emergency line. [@1]
