Lebanese army units carried out raids and set up temporary checkpoints in
Beirut, Mount Lebanon, the Bekaa, the north and the south, the army said, to
pursue people disturbing security and to combat people smuggling. [@3] In the
Bashara al-Khoury area of Beirut, soldiers detained a citizen and two Syrians
who possessed a quantity of Captagon pills and hashishat al-kif, cannabis resin.
[@1] The army put the sweep's total at 74 people, 10 Lebanese citizens and 64
Syrians. [@3]

Two Syrians were stopped at the Sabtiyeh checkpoint in the Metn area with
narcotic substances the statement does not name, and for moving around without
legal papers. [@1] The largest group, 60 Syrians, was held at the Shadra and
Zaghla checkpoints and in four other areas for entering Lebanon illegally. [@1]
Six citizens were detained over a dispute in Choueifat, in Baabda district, on
12 November 2026, and two military pistols were seized from them. [@1] A citizen
was detained in Qabb Elias, Zahle, over a shooting on the same date. [@1]
Another was held in Bir Hassan for carrying a military pistol. [@1] A man was
also held at the al-Safir checkpoint in Baabda for refusing to obey the soldiers
there. [@1]

The army said the seized items were handed over and questioning of the detainees
began under the supervision of the competent judicial authority. [@2]
