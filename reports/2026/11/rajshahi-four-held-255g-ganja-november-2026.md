Rajshahi Metropolitan Police's Detective Branch and Motihar, Rajpara and Boalia
thanas arrested three alleged drug traders and one drug user in separate
anti-drug operations. [@1] The Detective Branch arrested 57-year-old Md.
Harun-or-Rashid alias Bhutto at Bamonshikhar Uttarpara in Motihar thana at about
8 pm on 22 November 2026. [@1] Motihar thana police raided the home of
22-year-old Md. Ebadot Hossain Amin at Char Rastar Mor in Kajla village at about
1:15 pm the same day. [@1] Rajpara thana police arrested 19-year-old Md. Maruf
Islam at Lakshmipur ID Baganpara at about 10:15 pm on 21 November 2026. [@1]
Boalia thana police arrested 25-year-old Md. Sabbir at Bornali Mor at about 8:45
pm the same night while he was consuming ganja. [@1]

Officers seized 250 grams of ganja kept for sale from Harun-or-Rashid's custody,
and police said he already faced one case under the Narcotics Control Act at
Motihar thana. [@1] Officers seized 5 grams of ganja kept for sale from Ebadot
Hossain Amin's custody. [@1] Officers seized nine tapentadol tablets kept for
sale from Maruf Islam's custody, and police said he already faced one case under
the Narcotics Control Act at Boalia thana. [@1]

Sabbir was examined at the Divisional Police Hospital in Rajshahi, and a case
was filed against him under the Narcotics Control Act on the doctor's opinion.
[@1] Police filed separate cases under the Narcotics Control Act against the
arrested accused at the relevant thanas and sent them to jail through the
courts. [@1]
