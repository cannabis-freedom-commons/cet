A 41-year-old penal officer was arrested in the act on Thursday 19 November at
the Provisional Detention Centre (CDP) in Juína after drugs were found in the
backpack he carried for his shift. [@1] The Civil Police said he was already
under investigation on suspicion of bringing drugs and mobile phones into the
prison and of possible involvement with members of a criminal organisation. [@1]
Investigators had received information that he was responsible for carrying
illicit products into the jail and that a new drug delivery was expected that
Thursday. [@1] The Juína station and the prison management then organised an
inspection of the belongings of staff on shift. [@1]

During the search, fellow penal officers found 10 portions of cocaine base paste
and 16 portions of marijuana hidden in his backpack. [@1] Officers also seized a
mobile phone, a working 9mm Glock pistol and 11 rounds of the same calibre. [@1]
The arrest was part of a joint action by the Civil Police and the State Justice
Secretariat (Sejus) after months of investigation into prohibited items entering
the prison. [@1]

He was taken to the Civil Police station, questioned and charged in the act with
drug trafficking, then held at the disposal of the courts. [@1] Investigations
continue into his possible role in other offences, including bringing prohibited
materials into the prison and possible ties to a criminal organisation. [@1]
