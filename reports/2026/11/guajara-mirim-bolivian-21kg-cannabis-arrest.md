Military Police arrested a 37-year-old Bolivian man for drug trafficking after
catching him carrying about 2.1 kilos of cannabis near the Pérola Negra
clandestine port in Triângulo, Guajará-Mirim. [@1] The stop was carried out by
PATAMO officers and the intelligence nucleus of the 6th BPM during Operation
Madeira-Mamoré II. [@1]

Police said the suspect had just entered Brazilian territory, possibly coming
from Guayaramerín in Bolivia. [@1] When he saw the teams approaching he tried to
discard two packages but was stopped. [@1] A search found two cannabis tablets.
[@1] The man reportedly said he had brought the drug from Bolivia and that
another person would collect the package at the location. [@1]

He was taken to the Federal Police station in Guajará-Mirim, where he was
charged with drug trafficking. [@2] The operation was part of actions against
trafficking and other crimes in the border region. [@1]
