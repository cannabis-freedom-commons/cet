Police from the Metropolitan Police Unit No. 2 (UMEP-2), based in the Belén
neighbourhood, seized 47 packets of marijuana in an operation against
narcomenudeo, the retail sale of small drug quantities. [@1] Twenty-seven of the
packets were of the type sold locally as “crispy”. [@1] The operation took place
on Thursday 26 November 2026 in the Brisas de Olancho neighbourhood of the
capital, Tegucigalpa. [@1]

The UMEP-2 director, police subcommissioner Nelson Mejía, said the seizure was
worth more than 300,000 lempiras. [@1] Police said the seizure dealt a
significant economic blow to the Barrio 18 gang, which they described as a
criminal and terrorist structure operating in that sector of the capital. [@1]

Police said similar operations would continue at other points in the city to
curb the distribution of drugs and weaken criminal groups' finances. [@1]
