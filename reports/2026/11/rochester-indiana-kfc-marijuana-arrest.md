Sheena Steigmeyer, 36, of Bedford, was arrested and booked at the Fulton County
Jail on charges of possession of methamphetamine, a Level 6 felony, and
possession of marijuana, a Class B misdemeanor. [@1]

The arrest came after police were called just before 9:30 p.m. on November 14
about a possible impaired driver who had left the KFC/Taco Bell at 2080 Main St
in Rochester, where an employee had reported that a man and a woman had been
smoking marijuana in the restroom and had left toward Walmart in a blue SUV with
paper plates. [@1] Officers found a light blue Hyundai SUV with a paper Indiana
temporary registration parked unoccupied in front of Walmart, registered to
Steigmeyer. [@1]

After a police K9 alerted to the vehicle, officers found in a suitcase inside a
plastic marijuana dispensary bag holding several packages of green plant-like
material, six packages of THC edibles, seven THC oil cartridges and eleven THC
disposable vapes, with receipts naming Steigmeyer as the purchaser that
afternoon. [@1] A search of Steigmeyer found a rolled $1 bill and a small
container with a white powder that field-tested positive for methamphetamine,
plus additional marijuana in her pants pocket. [@1]

Officers also contacted her 16-year-old son inside Walmart, noticed a strong
smell of burnt marijuana on him, but found no marijuana on him. [@1] Her
boyfriend, who had been with them in Walmart, left the scene on foot and was
never located. [@1]
