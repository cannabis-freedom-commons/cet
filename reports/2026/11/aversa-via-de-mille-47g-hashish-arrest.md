A man was arrested in Aversa on suspicion of drug dealing. [@1] Officers from
the Aversa police station stopped the man in Via De Mille during a control
operation. [@1] On the street, officers found the man carrying 24 doses of crack
weighing about 8 grams in total. [@1]

Officers then searched the man's flat in Aversa and seized another 47 grams of
hashish, plus a small knife and a precision scale allegedly used to prepare
doses. [@1] The man was arrested and placed under house arrest pending a
fast-track trial. [@1]

A second account confirms that Aversa station officers arrested the man stopped
in Via De Mille with 24 doses of crack weighing about 8 grams. [@2]

A third account confirms the follow-up home search seized another 47 grams of
hashish with a small knife and a precision scale, and that the man was placed
under house arrest. [@3]
