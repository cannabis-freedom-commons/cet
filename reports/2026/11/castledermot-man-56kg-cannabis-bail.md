Richard Ellis (37), of Grange View, Kilkea, Castledermot, appeared at Athy
District Court from Garda custody charged with possession and possession with
intent to supply the illicit drug. [@1] After the arraignment formalities, the
investigating officer told the judge there was no objection to bail, prompting a
raised eyebrow from the bench. [@1] Judge Desmond Zaidan said in Athy District
Court this week (10 November) that he found it hard to marry the lack of garda
opposition to bail with the allegation. [@1] The judge asked the investigating
officer whether gardaí would object to bail over a man drunk in the street but
not over something like this. [@1]

Sergeant Ruth Coleman said Ellis had been arrested the day before in possession
of €224,000 worth of cannabis after gardaí intercepted a package addressed to a
technology company in Clane Business Park where he is employed. [@1] The judge
said the man was allegedly found with 5.6 kilos of cannabis. [@1]

With no conditions attached to the bail, Judge Zaidan remanded Ellis on
continuing bail until 13 April to give the National Forensic Lab and the
Director of Public Prosecutions time to rule on testing and jurisdiction. [@1]
