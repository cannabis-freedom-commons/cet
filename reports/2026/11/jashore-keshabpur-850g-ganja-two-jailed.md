On Monday 16 November, the Jashore district office of the Department of
Narcotics Control carried out drives in Jashore Sadar and Keshabpur upazilas.
[@1] Around noon in the Chingra area of Keshabpur, officers arrested 45-year-old
Sajeda Begum and seized 800 grams of ganja from her house. [@1] Her husband Jaso
Morol fled, and a case was filed against both of them at Keshabpur police
station. [@1] In the Tarafdar Nawapara area of Kotwali in the afternoon,
officers seized 18 yaba pills from 40-year-old Monirul Islam and 50 grams of
ganja from 37-year-old Azizul Islam. [@1]

The department said the day's separate drives seized 127.5 litres of rectified
spirit, 18 yaba pills and a total of 850 grams of ganja. [@1] A separate noon
raid at the house of Motiar Rahman in Purba Barandi Nathpara in Jashore town
recovered 37.5 litres of bottled rectified spirit and 90 litres of loose spirit.
[@1] Executive magistrates later ran mobile courts that fined Monirul Islam and
Azizul Islam 200 taka each and sentenced each to 30 days of simple imprisonment,
after which both were sent to jail. [@1]
