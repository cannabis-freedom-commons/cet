Wałbrzych police detained three men suspected of drug offences on Wednesday
evening in Biały Kamień and Podzamcze. [@2] The detainees were two 40-year-olds
and one 36-year-old, holding marijuana, amphetamine and methamphetamine. [@2]
Around 18:00 intelligence officers went to a flat on Dubois Street in Biały
Kamień where a man suspected of holding drugs was expected. [@2] The 40-year-old
there had a wrap of plant material with him. [@1] Tests by a forensic technician
identified the material as marijuana. [@1] He was detained and heard a
drug-possession charge that evening, which carries up to three years in prison.
[@1]

District officers checked the second 40-year-old on Blankowa Street. [@2] The
white powder he carried was confirmed by a forensic technician's test as
amphetamine. [@1] He was charged that evening under the law on counteracting
drug addiction. [@1]

After 23:00 on Wednesday preventive officers stopped a car in which a
36-year-old was travelling. [@1] They found crystals on him which a test showed
were methamphetamine. [@1] He spent the night in a police cell. [@1] The local
rewrite states he was then charged after the road check. [@2] Police said this
drug offence also carries up to three years in prison. [@1]
