A couple sleeping in Mukhiya Chowk, Haripurwa Municipality-9 Basantpur, Sarlahi,
were seriously injured when bricks were thrown at them. [@1] Basudev Sah Teli,
74, also called Sukdev Sah, died during treatment. [@2] Police arrested
19-year-old Shankar Kumar Yadav, a local man, and opened an investigation. [@1]

The couple ran a small grocery store in their house and were attacked around 10
PM on the night of Kartik 27. [@1] Yadav entered the room unnoticed while they
slept and hit Sah and his 73-year-old wife Jivchhi Devi on the head and face
with brick fragments before fleeing. [@1] Their eldest son found them seriously
injured on the morning of Kartik 28 and both were taken to Provincial Hospital
Janakpurdham in Dhanusha. [@1] Police took Yadav into custody on Kartik 28 while
he was hiding in his own house. [@1] The hospital declared Sah dead on Mangsir 3
during treatment, and his wife's treatment continues. [@1]

Deputy Superintendent of Police Ved Prasad Gautam of the Sarlahi District Police
Office said the Area Police Office Kesharaganj is further investigating Yadav on
charges of murder and attempted murder. [@1] Preliminary police investigation
found Yadav was a marijuana user. [@1] Police said Yadav attacked the couple in
revenge over a dispute during a marijuana transaction between him and the
deceased. [@1]
