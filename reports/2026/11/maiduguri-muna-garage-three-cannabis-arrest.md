Troops of the 195 Battalion, Sector 1, together with the National Drug Law
Enforcement Agency, arrested three suspected drug dealers during a joint
security operation in Maiduguri, Borno State. [@1] The three suspects were
identified as Muhammed Yarwama Mustapha, 30, Ibrahim Muhammed, 22, and Abba
Muhammed, 28. [@1] The operation took place at about 6:00 a.m. on November 12,
2026 at Muna Garage, Tashan Kalli and Tashan Kano, along the Maiduguri-Gamboru
Ngala Road. [@1]

The operation was aimed at disrupting the movement of illicit drugs and other
prohibited substances by commuters using the strategic Maiduguri-Gamboru Ngala
route. [@1] Those arrested were found with suspected illicit drugs and cannabis,
with no weight published. [@1] The suspects and the recovered substances were
handed over to the NDLEA Borno State Command for further investigation and
necessary action. [@2]

The operation was described as part of ongoing efforts by security agencies to
curb drug trafficking and stop the circulation of illicit substances that could
undermine security operations in the North-East. [@1]
