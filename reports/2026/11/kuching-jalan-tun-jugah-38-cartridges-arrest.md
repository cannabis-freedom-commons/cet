A 25-year-old man was detained on suspicion of possessing 38 cartridges
containing cannabis liquid in an operation in Jalan Tun Jugah, Kuching. [@1]
Kuching District Police Chief ACP Alexson Naga Chabu said a Narcotics Criminal
Investigation Division team made the arrest at about 3.05pm. [@2] The local
suspect was detained for inspection at around 3.05 pm at a parking area in front
of a shop along Jalan Tun Jugah. [@3]

An inspection found 38 cartridges suspected to contain cannabis-based liquid,
with a total estimated volume of 95 millilitres. [@2] Police estimated the
seized drugs were worth RM17,100 at market prices. [@2] The suspect tested
positive for amphetamine, methamphetamine, ketamine and benzodiazepines in a
preliminary urine test. [@3] Checks found the suspect had no prior criminal
record. [@3]

The suspect was remanded for five days from Nov 15 to 19 to assist the
investigation. [@2] The case is being investigated under Section 39A(2) of the
Dangerous Drugs Act 1952. [@2] On conviction that section carries five years to
life imprisonment and up to 10 strokes of the cane. [@3] Police said the seizure
followed continuous intelligence gathering and public cooperation. [@3]
