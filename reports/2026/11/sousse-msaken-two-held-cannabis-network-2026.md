National security units attached to the national security district in Sousse
dismantled a criminal network specialising in the promotion of narcotic
substances in the Msaken area on Tuesday 24 November 2026. [@1,2] The General
Directorate of National Security reported the Msaken security operation to
Tunisia-Africa Press. [@1] Officers detained two people during the security
operation in the Msaken area. [@1,2] One of the two detainees was wanted under
four search notices for possession and consumption of a narcotic substance
listed in Table B with intent to traffic in it. [@1,2]

The same notices also cited joining gangs in order to commit drug-related
crimes, participation in those crimes and money laundering. [@1] During the
operation officers seized 15 slabs of cannabis described as Indian hemp,
together with five bags containing a quantity of cocaine. [@1,2]

Officers also seized one electronic scale and two mobile telephones during the
operation. [@1,2] A plate bearing traces of cocaine was also seized in the operation. [@2] Investigators said inquiries were continuing after the seizure
in order to arrest the remaining members of the network. [@1,2]
