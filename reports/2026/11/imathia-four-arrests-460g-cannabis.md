Police of the Veroia Drug Prosecution and Investigation Unit arrested four men
in Imathia, northern Greece, on 25 November 2026 for violating drug legislation.
[@1] Two of the men were found in a car carrying one package of raw cannabis
weighing 446 grams. [@1] A third man drove a second vehicle ahead as an escort
for the transport of the drugs, in an operation on Wednesday 25 November. [@3]

A search of a home used by two of the men found 12.24 grams of raw cannabis and
a precision electronic scale. [@1] In a storage space used by the other two men,
officers found five packages of raw cannabis weighing 2.2 grams in total. [@1]
Police described the total seized as over 460 grams of raw cannabis. [@3] Police
also seized the vehicle and four mobile phones found in the men's possession.
[@1]

The four detainees were Greek nationals. [@2]
