A 32-year-old man and his 30-year-old partner were tried by the Le Mans court on
16 November 2026 for drug trafficking at La Ferté-Bernard on 6 July 2026. [@1]
On 6 July 2026 a woman presented herself at the La Ferté-Bernard post office to
deposit a parcel. [@1] The postal clerk noticed a strong smell of cannabis
coming from the parcel and alerted the gendarmes, who intervened quickly. [@1]

The gendarmes established that the woman was the partner of a man who sold drugs
by posting them to clients, who paid by bank transfer. [@1] At his home the
gendarmes found a small quantity of cannabis. [@1]

Before the correctional court the man acknowledged the facts he was accused of.
[@1] He said he sold drugs to help out friends and to fund his own consumption,
remarking that heavy smokers find it expensive. [@1] Unemployed and receiving
RSA benefits, he said he obtained his drugs in Paris. [@1] Asked how he
travelled to Paris, he replied by train, and was already known to the courts.
[@1] He said he defrauded the fare and his criminal record listed nine entries,
including similar offences. [@1] His partner was absent from the hearing and had
until then been unknown to the justice system. [@1]

Following the prosecutor's requisitions, the court found the defendants guilty.
[@1] The absent partner was sentenced to eight months in prison, suspended. [@1]
The man received a mixed 15-month prison term, comprising eight months suspended
and seven months firm. [@1] He will serve the firm part under an electronic
bracelet. [@1]
