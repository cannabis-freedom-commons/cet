Karimnagar Rural police arrested Adapa Vamshi, the absconding second suspect in
the Nagunuru ganja case. [@1] Officers raided Fakeerpet village on Sunday
morning on a tip-off that Vamshi was there and took him into custody. [@1]
Police seized the two-wheeler he had used. [@1] In October, police had acted on
information that two men were preparing ganja into small packets for sale on the
outskirts of Nagunuru village within Karimnagar Rural station limits. [@1] When
police reached the spot both men tried to flee; Sriram was caught while Vamshi
escaped. [@1] Sriram told interrogators that he and Vamshi, of Fakeerpet
village, brought ganja from other areas and sold it in surrounding villages.
[@1] Police seized about 1 kg 100 g of ganja from Sriram and sent him to remand.
[@1]

Police formed special teams to trace Vamshi, who remained absconding after that.
[@1] Questioning established that Vamshi had been selling ganja illegally for
some time. [@1] Police said several cases are registered against him at
Karimnagar Rural and Choppadandi stations and at Sultanabad in Peddapalli
district, among others. [@1] Police said he returned to the same illegal
activity despite going to jail multiple times before. [@1] The circle inspector
said a rowdy sheet has already been opened against him given his criminal
record. [@1] The account is attributed to CI Niranjan Reddy. [@1]
