Federal Highway Police officers stopped a passenger car on the BR-282 in Lages
on the morning of 17 November 2026 during a routine traffic inspection. [@1] A
second account confirms the morning stop by the Federal Highway Police on the
BR-282 in Lages with 37 kilograms of marijuana. [@2]

The team was monitoring traffic flow on the federal highway when it ordered the
car to stop. [@1] The car bore licence plates registered in Criciúma, in
southern Santa Catarina. [@1] It was driven by a 44-year-old woman travelling
with a 24-year-old male passenger. [@1] The second account gives the same driver
and passenger, a 44-year-old woman and a 24-year-old man in the Criciúma-plated
car. [@2] During the inspection officers found a marijuana load inside the
vehicle that totalled 37 kilograms after official weighing. [@1] Police said the
drugs were bound for Criciúma, the city where the car was registered. [@1]

Officers arrested both occupants at the scene and retained the car used to move
the load. [@1] The two suspects were taken with the car and the seized marijuana
to the Civil Police station in Lages for registration and further legal
procedures. [@1] The second account confirms the pair were taken with the car
and the seized drugs to the Civil Police station in Lages. [@2] They face
drug-trafficking charges, an offence punishable by five to 15 years in prison.
