Police in West Columbia arrested the owner of Cloud 9 Vape and Smoke after a
former employee reported he was fired for refusing to sell illegal drugs. [@1]
The shop is at 509 12th Street, and the owner was named in the incident report
as Mulhem Saleh Haj Ali. [@1] The former employee called police on 20 June to
report that Ali had told him to sell "illegal narcotics over the counter",
according to a West Columbia Police Department incident report. [@1] The man
said Ali regularly received deliveries of up to about a pound of what he
believed to be marijuana in vacuum-sealed bags. [@1] He told police that the
deliveries also brought THC vape pens clearly marked with California stickers
and a marijuana leaf. [@1] Some of the pens were on display in the shop and the
others were hidden in a large bag at the back of the store, the employee told
police. [@1]

On 30 October police searched the store with a warrant and confirmed they
confiscated several items; Assistant Police Chief Matt Daes said the
investigation was ongoing and he could not give a full list. [@1]

Ali was charged with possession with intent to distribute a Schedule I
controlled substance and possession with intent to distribute marijuana. [@1] No
other arrests were made in the case. [@1] Ali was not listed among inmates at
the Lexington County Detention Center as of 17 November, and court records
listed a $2,500 surety bond on both charges. [@1] The store did not comment when
reached by The Post and Courier. [@1]
