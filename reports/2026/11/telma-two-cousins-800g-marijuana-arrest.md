Officers of the linear police post at Usolie-Sibirskoye station carried out
operational-search measures at Telma station, where they identified two local
residents aged 38 and 26 who are cousins. [@1] Officers inspected the men's
backpacks and found and seized packets containing plant material. [@1]

An expert examination confirmed that the seized material was marijuana with a
total weight of more than 800 grams. [@1] The detained men said they had
gathered the drug in a nearby field and kept it for personal use, with no intent
to sell it. [@1]

Criminal cases were opened against the two men. [@1] The men were placed under a
travel restriction requiring them not to leave and to behave properly, while the
investigation continues. [@1] The account is attributed to the transport police
of the Angara region. [@1]
