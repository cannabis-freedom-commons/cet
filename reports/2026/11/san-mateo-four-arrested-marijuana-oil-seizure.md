Four people were arrested in a buy-bust operation in San Mateo, Rizal, early on
Friday, 20 November 2026. [@1] The operation was conducted at around 1 a.m. by
local police in coordination with the Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency. [@1]
The four suspects were informed of their constitutional rights and remain in
custody at the San Mateo Municipal Police Station. [@1] Authorities are
preparing criminal complaints against them for alleged violations of Republic
Act 9165, the Comprehensive Dangerous Drugs Act of 2002. [@1]

Police confiscated four small bottles containing approximately 28.32 milliliters
of suspected marijuana oil. [@1] The Rizal Police Provincial Office estimated
the value of the seized oil at P2,832. [@1] Officers also recovered two P500
bills used as buy-bust money. [@1] The search and inventory of the confiscated
items were conducted in the presence of required witnesses. [@1]

The identities of the four suspects were not disclosed in the initial police
report. [@1] The Rizal police said the operation forms part of its continuing
campaign against illegal drug distribution in the province. [@1]
