At about 19:10 on the evening of 17 November 2026, a patrol crew of the
Polizeiinspektion Bingen stopped a 23-year-old man riding an e-scooter in the
area of Bingerbrücker Straße in Bingen and carried out a traffic check. [@1]
During the check the officers found indications that the young man had recently
consumed narcotics, according to the police. [@1] An on-site urine drug rapid
test reacted positively to the substance group cannabis (THC). [@2]

Officers prohibited the man from continuing to ride the electric small vehicle
and ordered a blood sample to be taken to preserve evidence. [@1] The driver was
not allowed to continue riding the electric scooter. [@3] Criminal proceedings
were initiated against the 23-year-old for driving a motor vehicle under the
influence of narcotics. [@1] A criminal case was opened against the 23-year-old
on suspicion of driving a vehicle under the influence of drugs. [@3]
