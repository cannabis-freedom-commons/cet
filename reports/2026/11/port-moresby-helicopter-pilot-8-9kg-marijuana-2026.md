Police in Port Moresby arrested and charged a helicopter pilot with moving 8.9
kilograms of marijuana from Goilala to Port Moresby on a flight that the
operator had not approved. [@2] Commander Benjamin Turi, head of the National
Capital District and Central Provinces command, said the pilot was a 58-year-old
man from Koroba in Hela Province. [@2] He was arrested on Sunday 22 November
2026 at the Air Sanga Terminal of Jacksons International Airport, when he
returned from the second of two flights that day. [@3]

Detectives acted at about 4:30 p.m. after Lama Choppers Limited managers found
the helicopter missing from its landing pad and called Seven Mile Police
Station. [@1] The marijuana was found on board during the arrest. [@3] Turi said
police were interested in the marijuana that formed part of the cargo on the
flight. [@4]

Lama Choppers agreed to withdraw its complaint that the pilot misused the
helicopter. [@2] Turi thanked the company and the arresting officers. [@2] He
said the flight movements were suspicious and the case would be properly
investigated. [@2] Police said the pilot is presumed innocent until proven
guilty in court. [@4]
