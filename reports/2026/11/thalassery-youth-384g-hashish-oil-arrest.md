Thalassery Town police caught a young man with hashish oil during a check in the
Thiruvangad area of Thalassery city in Kerala. [@1] He was identified as Tejas
Praveen, 22, a resident of Kannur. [@1] Police said they stopped him in
suspicious circumstances at about 10 pm while on night patrol. [@1]

Officers recovered 3.84 grams of hashish oil from him, the quantity counted in
the cannabis figures here. [@1] The same check also yielded 2.43 grams of MDMA,
a substance outside this tracker's scope that is not included in the cannabis
figures. [@1] The Kannur City Police's own account named the same 22-year-old
and repeated both seized quantities. [@2]

A police team led by Thalassery police station sub-inspector Neethu
Thachanchery, with senior civil police officers Limnesh and Praveesh and civil
police officer K.P. Sijil, made the arrest and initiated legal proceedings. [@1]
