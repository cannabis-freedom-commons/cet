Egyptian security authorities continued intensive traffic campaigns on roads and
axes across the country to monitor compliance with traffic rules. [@1] The
campaigns recorded 117,728 varied traffic violations in 24 hours. [@1] The main
recorded offences were driving without licences, exceeding speed limits, random
parking, using a mobile phone while driving and breaching licence conditions.
[@1] Officers tested 743 drivers for drug use and 29 were positive, with legal
measures taken against them. [@1]

A parallel traffic and discipline campaign in work zones along the Regional Ring
Road recorded 436 varied traffic violations in 24 hours. [@1] Those violations
included carrying passengers in violation, breaching licence conditions and
lacking vehicle safety and durability requirements. [@1] Officers tested 89
drivers on the Ring Road and two were positive for drug use. [@1] Five people
wanted under a total of seven court rulings were detained, and one vehicle was
held for breaching traffic laws. [@1]
