Mirzapur's Shahganj police arrested accused Sunny Kumar Jaiswal on Friday
evening near BLW in Varanasi. [@1] The accused and his brother are accused of
involvement in ganja trafficking for 12 years. [@1] A reward of 10,000 rupees
had been declared on the accused. [@1] Police had been looking for him for one
and a half months. [@1] The team found him beside the road on the lane from
Mahmoorganj to Chitaipur opposite BLW. [@1] A five-member team including
Ghorawal crime inspector Bhanu Pratap Singh, Shahganj station chief Manvendra
Singh and Shahganj outpost in-charge Rahul Pandey had been sent to Varanasi on
that information. [@1] The team cordoned off the spot and arrested him at about
5 pm. [@1] He was brought to Shahganj for questioning. [@1] He was then
proceeded against under the NDPS Act. [@1] Police are still searching for the
accused's brother in the same case. [@1]

Police link the brothers to a syndicate that brought ganja from Odisha and
supplied it in Varanasi, Bhadohi and nearby districts. [@1] The syndicate came
to light after Shahganj police seized a 625 kg consignment of ganja brought from
Odisha in a secret cabin in a container on 24 September. [@1] In that seizure,
Odisha smugglers Dilip Dehuri and Santosh Kumar Sahu were arrested. [@1] On that
information, Sunny Kumar Jaiswal of Khutahan in Ahraura, Mirzapur, his brother
Surendra Jaiswal and the Odisha supplier were named as accused. [@1] Sunny's
current address was given as Nevada Sunderpur in Varanasi's Lanka area, while
his brother lives at Imliya Chatti in Ahraura. [@1] A reward of 10,000 rupees
was declared on Sunny in view of his role in the gang. [@1]

According to the accused, his brother Surendra worked as a petty contractor at a
government bhang shop near Sheetla temple in Robertsganj in 2014. [@1] During
that time the two brothers began bringing ganja from Odisha and selling it under
the cover of the bhang shop. [@1] Later they began ordering wholesale
consignments and smuggling them into Mirzapur and surrounding districts. [@1]
Special judge Govind Mohan rejected the bail application of Dilip Kumar Dehuri
last month. [@1] The second Odisha accused, Santosh Kumar Sahu, has also filed a
bail application on which a hearing is due on 17 November. [@1]
