Karariya Chauraha police station officers searched a house in Byochi village,
also written Byauchi, in Vidisha district, Madhya Pradesh, and recovered 104.14
kg of ganja from a shed built in front of the house. [@2,4,5,6,7,8,9,12] A joint
team from three police stations and the District Special Branch (DSB) carried
out the raid at 9 p.m. [@10] The first brief on the raid, in Dainik Bhaskar,
dated it to late on Monday night and put the haul at one quintal, about 100 kg.
[@1] The ganja was in five large plastic sacks inside a deep pit dug in an
enclosure and covered with heavy stones and straw. [@10] Police acted on a
tip-off that a man in the village was storing and selling ganja from the shed.
[@2,4,12] Karariya Chauraha station incharge Inspector Seema Rai led the team,
which cordoned off the spot, searched it and weighed the haul before seizing it.
[@2,12]

Police arrested a 26-year-old villager on the spot. [@2,4,10,11,12] They
identified the main accused as Jitendra, 26, son of Halke alias Bharat Singh
Meena. [@10,12] Police estimated the market value of the ganja at about 5.2
million rupees. [@2,4,5,6,7,8,9,10,12] A case was registered against him under
sections 8 and 20 of the NDPS Act, India's Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic
Substances Act, and investigation began. [@3,4,7,9,11,12] The case was recorded
as Crime No. 312/2026. [@11,12]

The operation was part of Operation Clean Sweep against drug trafficking,
ordered by Superintendent of Police Rohit Kashwani. [@3,6,8,12] In the first
brief Kashwani said it would be premature to say anything about the case. [@1]
During questioning, the accused said the consignment had been brought to Vidisha
on the Chhattisgarh Express train. [@10] It had been moved from the railway
station to the Byochi enclosure for distribution across the city through small
peddlers. [@10] Police are investigating where the ganja came from, who it was
meant for and whether the arrested man acted alone or for a wider network.
[@2,10] The reports say further arrests are possible as the supply source,
buyers and others connected to the trade are traced. [@2]
