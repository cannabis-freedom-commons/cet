Military police in Itapema arrested a man wanted by the courts on a
drug-trafficking warrant on the afternoon of Monday 16 November 2026. [@1] The
31st Military Police Battalion (31º BPM), which made the arrest in the Morretes
neighbourhood, said it had received information about the man's location. [@1]
Officers went to the address given, found a person matching the description and,
after checking his identity and confirming the court order, made the arrest.
[@1]

During the approach police seized about 23 grams of a substance resembling
marijuana and 15 grams of seeds from the same plant. [@1] The man said he kept a
quantity of marijuana for personal use and indicated where the material was
stored. [@1] In the container he pointed out, officers found about 23 grams of
the marijuana-like substance and 15 grams of seeds. [@1]

The material was seized and the man was sent to the prison system to serve the
judicial warrant. [@1]
