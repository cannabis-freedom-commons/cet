Bag police arrested Bhangda Bhanwar, who had been absconding for about a month
in the case registered over illegal cultivation of ganja. [@1] Police said a
reward of 10,000 rupees had been announced for his arrest before he was caught.
[@1] Police produced him in court after taking him into custody in Bag. [@1]

The underlying case involved 586 cannabis plants seized from a field linked to
the absconding accused. [@1] On 17 October, teams from Bag police station and
Dehri outpost raided a field in Bakalyapura of Kaneri village on specific
information. [@2] They seized 586 green cannabis plants weighing 446.6 kg that
had been hidden among the cotton crop. [@2]

Police valued the seized cannabis plants at 66.99 lakh rupees, or about 6.699
million rupees. [@2] A case against the accused in the cultivation matter had
been registered at Bag police station. [@2] In Dhar district, the wider drive
was reported as Operation Prahaar led by Superintendent of Police Sachin Sharma.
[@2]
