Military police from the 9th Special Operations Battalion (BAEP) arrested eight
people across São José do Rio Preto on Wednesday 18 November 2026 during an
operation called Saturação. [@1] Four of those arrested were wanted by the
courts and four were caught in drug-trafficking flagrantes, as the operation
swept different parts of the city. [@1] The four wanted people were captured in
the Duas Vendas, Jardim Jéssica, Anchieta and Jockey Club neighbourhoods. [@1]

In the Eldorado neighbourhood, military police patroling Avenida Monte Aprazível
noticed two men at a table who grew nervous and tried to hide their mobile
phones as the patrol car approached. [@1] After smelling cannabis and calling in
the police dog unit, officers found six portions of cocaine weighing 9.3 grams
and one portion of cannabis weighing 110 grams. [@1] They also seized R$534.30
in cash, a mobile phone, two precision scales, a knife and materials used to
wrap drugs. [@1] The suspect said he had worked at the establishment for about
four or five months, sold cocaine portions for extra income, and claimed the
cannabis was for his own use. [@1]

A second flagrante was registered on the Rodovia Washington Luís (SP-310) at
kilometre 436 in the rural area of Rio Preto during an inspection of an
intermunicipal bus. [@1] Officers found a package hidden under the seat occupied
by the passenger, containing one portion of cannabis weighing 108.9 grams. [@1]
The passenger said he lived in Brasília and had brought the drug from Foz do
Iguaçu, and was arrested in flagrante for drug trafficking. [@1]
