A 26-year-old man was detained in the early hours of Monday 16 November in the
city of San Luis after a gender-violence episode at a home in the barrio La
República. [@1] The operation was carried out by officers of the Sección
Movilidad of the Comando Radioeléctrico after an alert from the Centro de
Operaciones Policiales over gender violence at a house in manzana 800 of the
barrio La República. [@1] A 25-year-old woman told the officers her partner had
physically assaulted her inside the house. [@1] With the woman's authorisation
the officers entered the property and found the alleged attacker had left by a
back door. [@1]

During an inspection of the place the officers found cannabis plants, which were
seized by personnel of the Dirección General de Lucha Contra el Narcotráfico
(DG-8) under the Misión Sarmiento actions. [@1]

While the procedure was under way the man returned to the house and tried to
assault the victim again before the officers, who subdued and detained him. [@1]
He was then taken to the Comisaría Seccional 4°, where the proceedings
continued. [@1]
