Anonymous complaints about alleged drug distribution points led the state
prosecutor's office to search two houses in Piedras Negras, where substances
with the characteristics of marijuana and cristal were secured. [@1] No arrests
were made during the searches because nobody was in the houses. [@1]

One search took place at a house on Idelfonso Vázquez street in the Mundo Nuevo
neighbourhood, where agents found several doses of a substance preliminarily
identified as cristal. [@1] The second search took place on Río Lerma street
near the corner with Río Nilo in the Nueva Americana neighbourhood, where
marijuana was reported seized. [@1] The prosecutor's delegate for Region Norte
Uno said at least five kilograms of marijuana were secured in one of the
properties. [@1]

The delegate said the actions form part of investigation strategies and
coordination among agencies of the three levels of government against
street-level drug selling in the city. [@1] About 111 searches have been carried
out so far this year in the North Region of Coahuila. [@1] Investigations into
the two houses continue to establish who could be linked to the reported
activity. [@1]
