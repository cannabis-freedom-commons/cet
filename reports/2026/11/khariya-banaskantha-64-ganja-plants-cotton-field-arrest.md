Thara police raided a cotton field in Khariya village, in Banaskantha district,
Gujarat, after a tip that cannabis plants were growing among the crop. [@1]
Officers arrested Rajubha Kalu Vaghela, the farmer who held the field at
Nekakhad, where he grew cotton as his main crop. [@3] The raid was carried out
in the presence of witnesses, after the usual pre-raid search of the police
party and its vehicles. [@3]

Police seized 64 cannabis plants, both green plants and ones already cut. [@3]
The plants weighed 62.040 kg, which police valued at 3,102,000 rupees. [@4]
Police also took the field's documents, including an Aadhaar card copy and 7-12
and 8-A land extracts, a electricity bill receipt and a mobile phone worth 5,000
rupees. [@3] With the phone, police put the total value of the seized property
at 3,107,000 rupees. [@4]

A case was registered at Thara police station under the NDPS Act, India's main
narcotics law. [@2] Police said they were questioning him about how long the
crop had been grown, who it was meant for and whether anyone else was involved.
[@2]
