In Zavitinsky District police opened a criminal case against a 47-year-old
resident of the village of Novoalekseevka after finding a large stash of drugs
in his possession. [@1] Officers of the Zavitinsk police department, with SOBR
Rosgvardia power support, searched his plot and found dry plant material,
canisters of dark-green liquid and a glass jar of plant material. [@1]

Expert analysis found more than 50 kilograms of hemp plant parts, over 5.5
kilograms of marijuana and 143.38 grams of hashish oil. [@1] Amur.info, citing
the Amur regional UMVD, reported that he stored 50 kilograms of hemp, more than
5.5 kilograms of marijuana and almost 144 grams of hashish oil on his own plot.
[@2] According to police, he had gathered wild cannabis near Novoalekseevka,
brought it to his dacha plot, dried part of it and used the rest to make hashish
oil. [@1] Police said the drugs were intended for his own personal use. [@1]

The case was opened under part 2 of article 228 of the Russian Criminal Code for
illegal acquisition and storage of drugs on a large scale, which carries up to
ten years in a penal colony, and the investigation is continuing. [@2]
