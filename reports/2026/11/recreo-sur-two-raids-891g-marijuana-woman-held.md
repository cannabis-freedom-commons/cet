Unidad Regional I police carried out two raids on Tuesday morning on the same
property near the Quiroga and Roca alleys in Recreo Sur. [@2] The raids were
ordered in an investigation into the alleged theft of water meters. [@1]

In the first house officers seized copper, bronze and aluminium pieces, cutting
tools, grinders, scissors and scales. [@1] Officers also found about 127 grams
of whitish powder that field testing showed was negative for cocaine. [@1] Two
people from the first procedure were taken to police headquarters in an
encubrimiento case. [@1] Both people were later freed. [@1]

In the second house officers seized assorted tools, two brushcutters, six
bicycles, eight mobile phones, a large sum of cash and other items of interest.
[@1] Officers found three bags with about 265 grams of a substance that field
testing showed was positive for cocaine. [@1] Officers seized two bags of plant
material compatible with marijuana weighing 891 grams, along with scales and
items presumed to be used for portioning drugs. [@2] Finally, a woman was
apprehended in the encubrimiento case and placed at the disposal of the judicial
proceedings. [@2]
