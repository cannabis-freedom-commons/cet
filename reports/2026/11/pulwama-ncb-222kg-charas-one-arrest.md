The Narcotics Control Bureau's Srinagar office arrested a drug peddler from
South Kashmir's Shopian district with 2.22 kilograms of charas. [@1] NCB sources
said the man was arrested with the charas in Pulwama district. [@1]

The arrest followed a week of effort; a case numbered 04/2026 was registered and
the investigation continued. [@1] Official sources said the investigation was at
an initial stage and more arrests were expected. [@1] The officials said they
were examining financial linkages, sources and delivery in the case. [@1]
