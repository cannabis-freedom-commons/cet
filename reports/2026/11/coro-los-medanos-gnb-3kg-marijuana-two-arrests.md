Troops of Detachment 132 (D-132) of the Bolivarian National Guard (GNB) arrested
two men in flagrante in Miranda municipality, Falcón state. [@1] The arrest took
place during a preventive patrol on Calle F16 in the Los Médanos housing estate
in Coro, after the soldiers saw the men acting evasively. [@1] The two were
identified as Douglas Jesús Gómez Roque and Douglas Alexander Gómez Guanipa, the
second without identity documents at the time of the arrest. [@1]

The Falcón Citizen Security Secretariat published the report of the operation on
its Instagram account. [@2] In a bag the men were carrying, officers found 13
panela-type wrappings, pressed blocks, of suspected marijuana with a gross
weight of about 3.012 kilograms. [@2] Officers also seized three .38-calibre
firearms: two unmarked revolvers, serials 147750 and 592, and a shotgun of
non-industrial manufacture. [@1] Últimas Noticias reported that the men tried to
flee taking the bag with the 13 panels of marijuana. [@2]

The detainees and the seized evidence were placed at the disposal of the 21st
Prosecutor's Office of the Public Ministry, which was to open the criminal
proceedings. [@1] The information was released by Falcón security chief Miguel
Morales Miranda with the commanders of the ZODI-Falcón and the GNB zone 13
command, Héctor Echeverría Osorio and Jerry Camacaro Salazar. [@3]
