Police in Rethymno arrested a 28-year-old man on Monday 23 November 2026 in an
operation against unlawful possession and trafficking of drugs. [@1] The arrest
was made by officers of the drug enforcement department of the Rethymno crime
investigation subdivision, acting on information in a coordinated operation.
[@1] Officers located the man and searched his home, where they found drugs,
money and other objects. [@1]

The search seized 257 grams of raw cannabis. [@1] Officers also seized a small
quantity of cocaine. [@1] They seized two precision electronic scales. [@1] They
seized 3,510 euros, which police said allegedly came from criminal activity.
[@1] They seized five mobile phones, one folding metal baton and six
firecrackers with fuses. [@1]

A case file was opened against the man for drug-law violations and for
violations of the fireworks and pyrotechnics legislation. [@1] The preliminary
investigation is continuing by the same Rethymno drug enforcement department.
[@1]
