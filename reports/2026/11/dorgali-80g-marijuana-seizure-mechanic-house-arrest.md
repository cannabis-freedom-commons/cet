Carabinieri officers from the Siniscola company conducted an anti-drug operation
in Dorgali on Saturday. [@2,3] The operation resulted in one arrest, one report
and the seizure of several drugs. [@1] Officers arrested a 43-year-old mechanic
found with 80 grams of cocaine and 2,900 euros in cash. [@1,3] Investigators
believe that cash was possible proceeds from earlier drug dealing. [@1] When
officers entered his home, the man allegedly tried to flush some of the cocaine
down the toilet, but the attempt failed. [@1] He was then placed under house
arrest. [@1]

In a separate search, officers found a 35-year-old unemployed man with 80 grams
of marijuana and 500 euros in cash linked to the same activity, and reported him
while at large. [@1,3] In both homes, officers also found materials for packaging
and cutting drugs. [@1,3]

According to investigators, both men were involved in drug dealing in the
Dorgali area. [@1]
