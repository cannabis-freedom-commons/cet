Railway police apprehended 23-year-old Khatowng Tripura on 14 November 2026.
[@1] He was apprehended on platform No. 01 at Agartala Railway Station in front
of the parking area between the RPF post and the pay-and-use toilet. [@1]
Khatowng Tripura, son of Sri Dhan Mohan Tripura, is from Chandi Das Para, Jalaya
under Korbook police station in Gomati district. [@1] Inspector Raju Bhowmik,
officer in charge of Agartala Railway Police Station, gave details of the
operation to the media and said such operations against illegal contraband would
continue. [@1]

Officers recovered 9.985 kg of suspected ganja from his trolley bag. [@1] Police
estimated the black-market value of the seized substance at approximately Rs
500,000. [@1] Police seized the substance and arrested the accused under
Agartala GRPS Case No. 2026GRP081 registered under Sections 20(b)(ii)(C)/29 of
the NDPS Act, 1985. [@1]

The operation was launched on specific information received by the Government
Railway Police together with the Railway Protection Force. [@1] The seized
material was described only as suspected ganja with no laboratory confirmation
reported. [@1]
