A man was arrested in flagrante on suspicion of drug trafficking in the
afternoon of Monday 16 November 2026 in Quadra 7J of Arapoanga, in Planaltina,
in the Federal District. [@2] Police said the operation followed intelligence
work that identified movement typical of drug sales in the area and placed the
address under monitoring. [@3] Officers then searched a residence that,
according to police, was used to store the drugs. [@2]

In the property they found half a bar of marijuana and ten portions of the same
drug, divided into four large, two medium and four small. [@2] They also found a
portion of cocaine, which is not counted in the cannabis figures. [@1] Police
also seized a precision scale, a card machine, a roll of cling film used to wrap
drugs and R$66 in cash. [@1]

A check of police records showed the man already had records for drug
trafficking, drug use and possession and illegal exercise of a profession. [@1]
He was taken with all the seized material to the 16th Police Station, where he
was booked in the act. [@1]
