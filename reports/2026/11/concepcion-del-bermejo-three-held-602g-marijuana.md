Three people were detained during a police operation against suspected drug
retail (narcomenudeo) in Concepción del Bermejo, Chaco, on the night of Tuesday
17 November 2026. [@1] The three were two men, aged 29 and 30, and a woman of
29. [@1] The operation was carried out by officers of the División Microtráfico
Interior, the Chaco police unit for small-scale drug dealing. [@1] It ran
between 20:05 and 22:40 on calle Primera Junta, between Primero de Mayo and
Colectora Sur. [@1] According to the police report, investigators had obtained
information about suspected drug sales in that sector and set up a surveillance
and intervention detail. [@1]

During the operation officers found three people next to two motorcycles, one
blue and one black. [@1] When the suspects noticed the officers they tried to
flee, but were quickly intercepted and detained. [@1] In the presence of a
witness, a search found a grey precision scale in the possession of one of the
suspects. [@1] In the luggage compartment of a black Zanella motorcycle officers
found a rectangular brick-shaped wrapper and two compact pieces of different
sizes containing 602 grams of marijuana. [@1] The black Zanella motorcycle
seized had no licence plate fitted. [@1] A blue Keller Cronos motorcycle was
also taken away as a belonging. [@1]

On the order of the Fiscalía Antidrogas (anti-drug prosecutor's office) of
Presidencia Roque Sáenz Peña, led by prosecutor Laura Soledad Borelli, the three
were apprehended and items linked to the investigation seized. [@1] They were
placed at the disposal of the justice in a case for alleged infringement of
Argentina's National Law 23.737 on narcotics, under Chaco's Provincial Law
2304-N. [@1] The investigation continued with further steps to determine the
responsibilities of those involved. [@1]
