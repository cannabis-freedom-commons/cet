Antioquia police arrested a man in flagrante in Ciudad Bolívar. [@1] The
Departamento de Policía Antioquia, the National Police command for the
department, reported the capture. [@1] The procedure was linked to the alleged
offence of trafficking, manufacture or carrying of narcotics. [@1] The report
describes the detention as in flagrancia and counts the drugs in dosis. [@1]

Officers reported seizing 70 doses of marijuana in the procedure. [@1] In the
same procedure they reported 100 doses of coca base. [@1] They also reported 130
doses of cocaine hydrochloride, which this report excludes from the cannabis
figures. [@1] The 70 marijuana doses were part of a 300-dose seizure across the
three substances. [@1]

Policía Antioquia published the balance on its X account with a photograph of
the detainee and the seized items. [@1] The article carries a 21 November 2026
publication stamp, which is used as the incident date because no separate
operation date was given. [@1]
