On 14 November 2026 a man was arrested in Brest on allegations including breach
of a judicial ban, domestic violence, damage and cannabis use, and he appeared
before the Brest court on Tuesday 17 November 2026. [@1] The defendant appeared
in court on 17 November 2026 with six previous entries in his criminal record.
[@1] In 2025 he had been convicted of domestic violence against his former
partner and their daughter, whom he was banned from approaching or contacting.
[@1] Police found him in the complainant’s flat on the evening of 14 November
after being called for a dispute against a background of alcohol. [@1]

At the time he had 3.38 grams of alcohol per litre of blood and was found in
possession of cannabis. [@1] Before the Brest judicial court he denied
everything except the cannabis. [@1] He denied any physical violence or insults
and said of the damage that he had only taken the kitchen door off its hinges,
which was reinstalled without difficulty. [@1]

Noting his disregard of the bans and lack of explanation, the prosecutor said he
remained unmoved by warnings and sentences and requested thirteen months in
prison, including three months from a previous suspended sentence. [@1] The
defence said the objective evidence was limited to the cannabis found and argued
that the mother and daughter, as civil parties, had constantly contradicted each
other and their words should be treated with caution. [@1] After deliberation
the court retained only drug use and sentenced him to 50 day-fines of 10 euros.
