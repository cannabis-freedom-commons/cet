Police in Dhaka arrested 565 people in a 24-hour drive across the capital,
including 71 members of the Awami League and its student wing, the Bangladesh
Chhatra League. [@1] The Dhaka Metropolitan Police (DMP) disclosed the figures
in a statement on Wednesday. [@1,2] Of those arrested, 50 were from Ramna
Division, 70 from Lalbagh, 91 from Wari, 58 from Motijheel, 68 from Tejgaon, 143
from Mirpur, 29 from Gulshan and 55 from Uttara. [@1,2] Another person was
arrested by the Detective Branch of police. [@1,2]

During the operation police seized two unexploded crude bombs, fragments of six
exploded crude bombs, 6,871 yaba pills, 2.1 kg of cannabis, 117 packets of
heroin, nine bottles of Eskuf syrup, an autorickshaw, 29 torches, 1.75 litres of
kerosene, 11 mobile phones and Tk 3,400 in cash. [@1,2] Police also seized an autorickshaw, 29 torches, 1.75 litres of kerosene, 11 mobile phones and Tk 3,400 in cash. [@2]

The raids were conducted across the jurisdictions of different police stations
in Dhaka on Tuesday, leading to the arrests and recovery of the suspected
explosives and drugs. [@1] The DMP said legal proceedings against those arrested
are under way. [@1,2] Police filed 64 cases in connection with the drive. [@2] The activities of the Awami League remain banned, while the
Chhatra League faces an outright ban. [@1]
