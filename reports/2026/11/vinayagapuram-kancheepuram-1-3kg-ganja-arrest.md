Police received information that ganja, described as banned by the government,
was being sold in the Vinayagapuram area within the Balusettichathiram police
station limits near Kancheepuram. [@1] Following that information, officers
carried out a search in that area. [@1] During the search, officers found that
Palani, aged 20 and a resident of Keezhandai Street in Vinayagapuram, was
keeping ganja for sale. [@1] Police arrested Palani on 14 November 2026, the day
the report refers to as today. [@1]

The arrest took place near Kancheepuram after the search pointed to possession
of ganja meant for sale. [@1] At the arrest, officers seized 1.3 kilograms of
ganja from him. [@1] Police put the value of the seized ganja at 65,000 rupees.
[@1] The seizure was reported as taking place on the same day as the arrest.
[@1]

Vinayagapuram's Keezhandai Street was named both as the suspect's home street
and as the area where the possession for sale came to light. [@1] The account
identifies a single suspect and a single cannabis seizure quantity in this
operation. [@1]
