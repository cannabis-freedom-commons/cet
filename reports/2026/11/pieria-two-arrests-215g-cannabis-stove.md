Police in Pieria arrested two Greek men at midday on Wednesday 25 November 2026
over a case of drug possession and distribution. [@1] The operation was carried
out by officers of the Katerini Sub-Directorate for Crime Investigation and
Detection. [@2] The second man was arrested after police found him with 2 grams
of cannabis that he had bought earlier from the first man. [@1]

In a search of the first man's home, officers found packages of unprocessed
cannabis weighing 215.5 grams hidden inside an electric stove. [@1] A further
2.2 grams of unprocessed cannabis was found on him. [@1] From the house police
seized a precision electronic scale, a metal grinder, 70 euros in cash and two
mobile phones. [@1]

The local Katerini newspaper Olympio Bima reported that a second man was
arrested with 2 grams of cannabis. [@3]
