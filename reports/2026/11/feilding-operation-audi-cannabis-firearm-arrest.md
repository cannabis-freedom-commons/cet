Police arrested a 21-year-old man in Feilding on 18 November 2026, when officers
executed a search warrant in the town. [@1] The arrest was part of Operation
Audi, a coordinated operation against an illegal drug network involving Feilding
Police, the Offender Prevention Team and the Gang Disruption Unit. [@1] Police
said the operation formed part of their efforts to reduce the harm caused by
drugs and unlawful firearms in the community. [@1]

At the search officers found a firearm, ammunition, a significant quantity of
drugs including cannabis and methamphetamine, more than 800 codeine tablets and
a substantial amount of cash. [@1]

Sergeant Mike Linton, officer in charge of Feilding Police, said removing
firearms and significant quantities of drugs from circulation was a great
outcome for the community. [@1] Linton said operations like this showed the
value of police teams working together, sharing intelligence and taking a
coordinated approach to disrupting criminal activity. [@1] He said information
from the public was valuable and helped police build a clearer picture of what
was happening in the area. [@1]

The man faces a number of charges and is due to appear in the Palmerston North
District Court on 19 November. [@1] Police said their enquiries remained
ongoing. [@2] They asked anyone with information about drug dealing or unlawful
firearms to contact police through 105 or Crime Stoppers. [@2]
