On the morning of Friday 13 November 2026, inspectors from the Provincial Road
Transport Inspectorate in Lublin (WITD) stopped a semi-trailer truck on national
road 92 in Woroniec, Biała Podlaska county. [@1] The driver was an entrepreneur
from Podlaskie voivodeship who was carrying out international road transport
from Poland to Belarus. [@1]

While taking the documents the inspector smelled a characteristic odour coming
from the cab and, suspecting the driver was under the influence of a drug,
called police to the scene. [@1] Police carried out a test whose result
indicated the presence of marijuana in the man's body, and the driver was handed
over to police, which took further action in the case. [@1]

The inspector also checked digital records of the driver's activity and found
four occasions on which the truck was driven without the break required by law.
[@1] WITD in Lublin said it was especially concerning that the driver was
himself a transport entrepreneur running international road haulage. [@1]
