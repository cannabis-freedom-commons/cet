Department of Narcotics Control officers in Chatmohar, Pabna, arrested two
suspected drug dealers and seized 5 kg of ganja. [@1] The two were Ismail
Hossain, 25, from Gachhpada area of Pabna Sadar upazila, and Suman, 26, from
Santhia upazila. [@1] The arrests were made on 23 November at Mulgram market on
the Chatmohar–Pabna regional road, in front of Ranjit Mudir's shop. [@1]

The men were carrying the 5 kg of ganja in specially prepared packets and
bundles, intending to sell it, and officers recovered it after acting on a
tip-off. [@1] The Pabna office of the Department of Narcotics Control carried
out the operation in front of the shop at Mulgram market. [@2] A case under the
Narcotics Control Act was being prepared against the two men. [@1]

A different account describes the arrests as made by police and names the two
men as Emon Hossain, 25, of Chhatiani Madhyapara and Ismail Malitha, 35, of
Ramandanpur in Maligachha union. [@3] In that version the 5 kg of dried ganja
was found in two polythene packets inside a black schoolbag, one holding 3 kg
and the other 2 kg. [@3] One account dates the operation to Monday 23 November,
while the others call it Sunday 23 November. [@4] The Officer-in-Charge of
Chatmohar police station, Aliur Rahman, said a regular case under the Narcotics
Control Act was in process and the two would be sent to Pabna district court in
the morning. [@3] In that version an old TVS motorcycle, a button phone and two
smartphones were also seized. [@3]
