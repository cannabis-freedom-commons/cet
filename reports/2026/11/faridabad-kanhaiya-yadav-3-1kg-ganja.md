Faridabad's Crime Branch Sector-65 arrested Kanhaiya Yadav and seized 3.100 kg
of ganja from him on 19 November 2026. [@1] A police spokesperson said the team
received a secret tip-off that day and acted on it to catch him with the ganja.
[@1] Police identified him as a resident of Barhi Patti Nirajana village in
Gopalganj district, Bihar, who had been living in Saraswati Colony, Faridabad.
[@2]

According to police, he brought the ganja from Bihar to Faridabad by train. [@1]
Police said two earlier cases of supplying ganja had been registered against
him. [@1] A case under the NDPS Act, India's main anti-drug law, was registered
against him at Palla police station. [@1]

Officers produced him before a court, which sent him to six days of police
remand. [@1] Police said they were questioning him about other aspects of the
drug trade. [@1] Further questioning in the case was continuing, police said.
[@2]
