Mikey Luke was not a licensed cannabis cultivator when he was arrested for
growing marijuana in 2021. [@1] Virgin Islands Police Department officers went
to his home in Estate Anna’s Hope on 28 September 2021 after a 911 call reported
that he was growing marijuana. [@1] Luke acknowledged that marijuana plants were
growing on the property and signed a consent-to-search form. [@1] Officers
collected approximately 178 plants, and Drug Enforcement Administration agents
later counted 138. [@1] Federal prosecutors charged Luke by information on 26
October 2021, and a superseding indictment was returned in October 2023. [@1] A
jury acquitted Luke of possessing marijuana with the intent to distribute 100 or
more plants but convicted him of maintaining a property for the purpose of
manufacturing marijuana. [@1]

The presentence report calculated an advisory range of 21 to 27 months, and
prosecutors sought 27 months in prison, citing 10 prior felony offences
including a 2010 drug conviction. [@1] The defence asked the judge to sentence
Luke to probation. [@1] The sentencing memorandum described Luke’s Rastafari
faith and belief in marijuana as a sacrament and said he used marijuana tea to
manage pain from bladder stones that went undiagnosed for years. [@1] The
defence challenged the plant count, arguing that only 12 of 26 samples tested
positive for marijuana and that some plants were hemp. [@1] The judge did not
accept the lower plant count but weighed the acquittal on distribution,
cooperation, medical condition and Rastafari upbringing at sentencing. [@1]

Luke was sentenced on a Monday to one day in federal custody, served the same
day, followed by three years of supervised release, with the first six months on
home incarceration with location monitoring and leave only for medical needs or
work. [@1] Luke was also ordered to pay a $100 special assessment. [@1] The U.S.
Attorney’s Office said it follows federal law rather than local law. [@1] The
office said Luke was prosecuted federally because of the number of plants found
at his home. [@1]
