Mayiladuthurai district police jailed two men under the Goondas
preventive-detention law on 26 November 2026. [@1] The ganja accused was
Vignesh, 26, son of Kannan of Thamaraiakulam street in Vaitheeswaran Koil. [@1]
The other man, Mukilan, 26, was accused of threatening the public with a knife
on 25 October in the Manakkudi area. [@1]

Police had received a secret tip that ganja was being sold illegally near
Thamaraiakulam. [@1] Vaitheeswaran Koil police carried out a ganja hunt near
Thamaraiakulam on 19 October 2026. [@1] Officers arrested Vignesh, sent him to
judicial custody and seized the ganja he had hoarded for sale. [@1] Mukilan was
arrested after inspector Sivasenthilkumar investigated a case registered on an
assistant inspector's complaint. [@1]

Superintendent of Police B. Sneha Priya recommended Goondas action against both
men to the district collector. [@1] The district collector ordered Mukilan and
Vignesh jailed under the preventive-detention law. [@1] Police said 89 people
had been jailed under the Goondas Act in Mayiladuthurai district so far in 2026,
including 24 over ganja sale. [@1] Police described the action as part of a
drive to maintain law and order and suppress anti-social activity in the
district. [@1]
