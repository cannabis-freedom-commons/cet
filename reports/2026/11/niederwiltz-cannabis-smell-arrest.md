On Friday evening, officers patrolling on foot in Niederwiltz noticed a group of
several people standing together. [@1] When the officers approached the group,
they detected the smell of cannabis coming from it. [@2] While the officers were
watching, one member of the group walked away toward a parked car and sat down
behind the wheel. [@1] The officers intervened before he could drive off,
stopped him, carried out an identity check and searched both the man and his
vehicle. [@2]

In that search they found drugs, equipment used for consuming or packaging drugs
and a sum of cash. [@1] Police checks then showed that the man was already known
to the police for previous drug-related offences. [@1]

After the prosecutor's office was informed of the incident, it ordered the man's
arrest. [@1] The man was subsequently brought before an investigating judge.
[@1] On the prosecutor's orders, police also searched the man's home, where
investigators seized further quantities of drugs and additional drug-related
equipment and objects. [@2]
