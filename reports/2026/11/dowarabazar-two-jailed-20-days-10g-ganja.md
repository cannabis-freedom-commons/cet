A mobile court in Dowarabazar Upazila sentenced two men to 20 days of simple
imprisonment each for consuming ganja. [@1] The court also fined each of the two
men 100 taka. [@1] A second outlet gives the same outcome of 20 days of simple
imprisonment for each of the two men. [@2] A third outlet gives the same outcome
of 20 days of imprisonment and a 100-taka fine for each man. [@3]

The drive took place on Friday 13 November 2026 across various areas of
Dowarabazar Upazila. [@1] Those detained were 43-year-old Nasir Uddin of
Pratabergaon village and 40-year-old Sadik Ali of Dowargaon village. [@1]
Officers seized 10 grams of ganja from them and destroyed the seized ganja on
the spot. [@1] The two convicts were ordered to be sent to Sunamganj District
Jail. [@1]

The joint drive was led by Assistant Commissioner (Land) and Executive
Magistrate Md Rashedul Islam with a task force of the Department of Narcotics
Control Sunamganj district office and Dowarabazar police. [@1] A sub-inspector
of the district narcotics control office presented the prosecution. [@1] The
district narcotics control deputy director confirmed the operation. [@1]
