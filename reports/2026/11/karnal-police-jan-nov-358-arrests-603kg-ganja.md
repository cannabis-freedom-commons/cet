Karnal police under Superintendent of Police Karan Goyal ran a special campaign
against drug smugglers to protect young people from drugs. [@1] Between 1
January and 13 November 2026, police registered 196 cases under the NDPS Act and
arrested 358 accused people and sent them to jail. [@1]

Police teams caught small suppliers and exposed bigger networks while seizing
large consignments of drugs. [@1] Police records show officers seized 603.460
kilograms of ganja leaf and 508.059 kilograms of poppy husk from the accused
during the period, plus 2.087 kilograms of heroin and 2.015 kilograms of sulfa.
[@1] Raids also seized 51.385 kilograms of opium plants, 8,830 banned pills,
1,596 capsules and one MTP kit. [@1]

Karnal police said they are moving beyond arrests to dismantle the full supply
chain, directing all station areas and CIA teams to closely watch suspicious
activity and habitual offenders. [@1] Superintendent Karan Goyal appealed to
citizens to inform police without fear if they learn of drug sale or smuggling
nearby. [@1] He assured that an informant's name and identity would be kept
fully secret. [@1] He said making Karnal completely drug-free and securing the
future of young people is the police's top priority. [@1]
