Military Police apprehended two teenagers on suspicion of drug trafficking at a
house on Rua dos Jequitibás in the Flor de Pequi neighbourhood of Nova Mutum,
Mato Grosso. [@1] The operation took place at about 22:50 on Tuesday 17 November
2026. [@2] Officers of the 26th Military Police Battalion, part of the 14th
Regional Command, went to the address after an anonymous report of people coming
and going at the property. [@2] Two teenagers ran towards the corridor of the
house, and one of them threw a portion of a substance similar to cocaine paste
next to a washing machine. [@1]

With permission from one teenager's guardians, officers searched inside the
house. [@1] During the search they found a portion of marijuana on a chest of
drawers. [@2] Questioned again, one teenager showed the officers where a large
marijuana bar was hidden. [@2] The officers also noticed a strong smell of the
drug in the corridor of the house. [@2] The two were taken to the Civil Police
station in Nova Mutum together with the drugs found. [@1]

Military Police said both teenagers already had records for acts analogous to
drug trafficking. [@1] The officers also suspected the pair had links to a
criminal faction, a suspicion the report said still depended on investigation by
the competent authorities. [@1] The guardians of one teenager followed the
procedures at the station, while the Conselho Tutelar, the municipal
child-protection council, was called in for the other. [@1] The teenagers were
handed to the Civil Police without apparent injuries. [@2] The Civil Police were
to continue the investigation. [@1]
