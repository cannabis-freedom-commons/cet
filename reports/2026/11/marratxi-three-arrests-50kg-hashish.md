Spain's National Police arrested four men and one woman on Mallorca on suspicion
of drug trafficking and membership of a criminal organisation. [@2,4,5,7] The
investigation was opened several months earlier by Group I of the Narcotics
Division of Udyco, the police's Drugs and Organised Crime Unit, after officers
learned of a hashish parcel addressed to a man living in Palma. [@2,4] Officers
verified that the parcel contained 24 kilos of hashish, located the recipient
and arrested him. [@2,5,6] They also discovered that others were collaborating
with him and that there were two addresses where further drugs might be found.
[@2] The suspects had been collecting hashish sent from the Spanish mainland at
courier companies and keeping it at home for sale through others in the network,
according to case sources quoted by Última Hora. [@1]

## The searches

Última Hora, which first reported the case from its own sources, said at least
three people were arrested on Wednesday 4 November, when officers searched a
home in Marratxí, a municipality east of Palma, and another in a town in es
Raiguer, the district around the town of Inca. [@1] In the Marratxí home police
found a large quantity of hashish in a chest freezer and an air rifle, and
arrested a woman who was released at the police station hours later. [@1] The
police announcement of 14 November said the search of the main suspect's home
yielded 36 kilograms of hashish, an air rifle fitted with a silencer and around
10,000 euros in cash. [@2,4,5,6] Another man and a woman were arrested during
that search. [@2]

Investigators also arrested a suspect who allegedly stored and transported the
drugs. [@2] At his home officers found 2 kilograms of cocaine, further doses of
the drug, cutting agents, a pistol with 9-millimetre cartridges and a bow with
arrows. [@3,4,5] They also found a hydraulic press allegedly used to compress
the cocaine, and suspect the substance was passed to others for sale on a
smaller scale. [@2] A fifth person was arrested over a link to the case. [@5]
Officers from the Prevention and Reaction Unit, Narcotics Group II and canine
guides took part in the searches. [@3,5]

## Two counts

The early account put the hashish at almost 50 kilos and the arrests at no fewer
than three; the announcement a week later gave 60 kilos and five arrests.
[@1,5] All the suspects in the early account are Spanish nationals, and an
investigating court in Palma declared the case secret. [@1] The investigation
remains open and further arrests are not ruled out. [@1,2,4,5,6]
