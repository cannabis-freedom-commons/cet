The narcotics investigation unit of South Sorong district police destroyed
cannabis evidence from seven suspects at the district police headquarters in
Teminabuan on Friday 20 November 2026. [@1] Deputy police chief AKP Yosias Simon
Tatuhey said the destroyed material weighed 772.01 grams across five cases. [@1]
The seven suspects were identified by the initials RHW, SCS, NH, ALS, MFS, OSK
and REY. [@2]

Each suspect was charged under article 114(1) for offering, selling, buying or
receiving narcotics and article 111(1) for possessing or providing them,
offences carrying a threatened sentence of five to twenty years in prison. [@1]
The individual quantities were 3.09 grams for RHW, 1.52 grams in two paper folds
for SCS, 7.17 grams of cannabis for NH, 255.06 grams in 19 packets for ALS,
45.05 grams in three packets for MFS, and 460.12 grams in 21 packets for OSK and
REY. [@1]

The cases came from police reports: RHW and SCS were arrested on Sungai Maruni
road at kilometre 10 towards Sorong city, NH at Pasir Putih village in Fkour
district of South Sorong, ALS and MFS at Sorong city port at different times,
and OSK and REY on Cendrawasih road in Sorong city. [@1] The narcotics unit
chief said that one suspect who ran away during an arrest in Sorong city was
later found again. [@1] The chief said the evidence had passed through
investigation and lawful handling and that the destruction was carried out
transparently before relevant parties. [@1]

The evidence was burned by the deputy police chief, the narcotics unit chief,
Propam and Kasiwas officers and representatives of the Sorong district
prosecutor's office. [@1] Police said the destruction was meant to demonstrate
transparency and accountability in handling evidence and the force's commitment
to fighting drug trafficking. [@1]
