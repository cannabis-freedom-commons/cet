Police stopped a 27-year-old man riding an e-scooter for a traffic check at
about 13:30 on Saturday midday at An der Feuerwache in Eisenach. [@1] A drug
test carried out on the rider was positive for cannabis. [@1]

Officers took the man to hospital for a blood sample to secure evidence. [@2]
Police said a formal report over the offence would follow. [@2] The account is
the Landespolizeiinspektion Gotha police statement for Eisenach-Wartburgkreis
issued on the morning of 15 November 2026. [@2]
