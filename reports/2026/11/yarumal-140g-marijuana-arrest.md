Police in Yarumal, in northern Antioquia, arrested a 26-year-old man on
suspicion of trafficking, manufacturing or possessing narcotics. [@1] The arrest
was made during stop-and-search checks, known in Colombia as registro y control,
that officers carry out on roads and in public spaces. [@1] The Antioquia Police
Department announced the arrest in a post on its X account. [@1]

Officers seized a bag holding about 140 grams of marijuana of the variety known
locally as cripy. [@1] The substance was left in the custody of the authorities
as evidence. [@1] Police did not release the man's identity, and he was placed
at the disposal of the competent authority, which must decide his judicial
situation in the coming hours, including any pre-trial measure. [@1]

Yarumal, about 130 kilometres from Medellín, is one of the largest
municipalities of northern Antioquia and lies on routes linking the subregion
with the Caribbean coast and the country's interior. [@1] The police say the
checks aim to curb drug sales and use in public spaces and to prevent crimes
linked to microtrafficking, and concentrate on parks, transport terminals and
roads into the municipalities. [@1]
