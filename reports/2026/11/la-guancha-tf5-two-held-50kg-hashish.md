At about 04:00 on Friday 13 November 2026, four Guardia Civil traffic officers
from several detachments on the island were running a preventive alcohol and
drugs checkpoint on the TF-5 in the municipality of La Guancha. [@1] When
ordered to stop, the driver of a black car evaded the checkpoint and fled at
high speed. [@1]

The four officers pursued the car for about a kilometre into a zone of side
streets and intercepted it. [@1] The operation ended without endangering other
road users and with the detention of the car's two occupants. [@1]

Officers seized 50 kilos of a substance that by its appearance could be hashish,
along with 17,450 euros in cash. [@1] Proceedings were opened for an alleged
offence against public health and another of driving without a licence. [@1]
