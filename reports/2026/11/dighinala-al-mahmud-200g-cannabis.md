Police in Dighinala, Khagrachhari district, arrested Md Al Mahmud, 22, also
known as Babu, and seized 200 grams of cannabis from him. [@1] The arrest was
made at about 8pm on Wednesday 25 November 2026 on the paved road in front of
Dighinala Degree College. [@3]

Acting on a tip-off, Sub-Inspector Mohammad Ibrahim Khalilullah led a police
team to the area, and the man tried to run when he noticed the officers. [@1]
Officers searched a market bag in his hand and found 200 grams of cannabis
wrapped in green polythene. [@1] Police said he had been waiting for a buyer
with the intention of selling the drug. [@2] The seizure list was prepared at
about 7:45pm under a street lamp in the presence of witnesses. [@2]

Sub-Inspector Khalilullah filed a case at Dighinala police station under the
Narcotics Control Act 2018, registered as FIR No. 04 dated 25 November 2026.
[@3] The investigation was assigned to Sub-Inspector Prantosh Banik and was
still continuing. [@2] Officer in charge Md Iqbal Bahar Chowdhury said anti-drug
operations would continue and no one involved in such crimes would be spared.
