Royal Gendarmerie officers from the Azemmour territorial centre arrested two
people suspected of links to a drug-trafficking network on the evening of 15
November 2026 at douar Bhabha in Haouzia commune, El Jadida province. [@2] The
operation began around 22:30 when a patrol spotted a suspect grey Volkswagen
Tiguan. [@1] The driver resisted the check, tried to force his way out and used
tear-gas spray. [@1] Gendarmes stopped the escape, subdued the driver and
detained his female companion. [@1]

A search of the car found 11.3 kilograms of cannabis resin in 106 slabs and 4.2
grams of cocaine in capsule form. [@1] Also found were two cleaver-type knives,
two tear-gas sprays, a plastic hammer, a black mask, four electronic scales and
about 500 grams of plastic bags for packaging. [@1] Gendarmes also seized 3,353
dirhams hidden in a metal box and the vehicle they said was used to carry the
goods. [@1]

The prosecutor at the El Jadida court of first instance ordered the two placed
in police custody for a judicial inquiry into the network's possible
ramifications and accomplices. [@1] Investigators continued to establish the
exact circumstances and to identify any ramifications of the network. [@3]
Searches of the suspects' homes were ordered, the vehicle was impounded and the
drugs were handed to El Jadida's customs and indirect taxes administration. [@1]
