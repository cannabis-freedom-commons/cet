Saudi Arabia's General Directorate for Drug Control arrested Yemeni nationals in
breach of the border-security system and a Saudi citizen in Jazan region,
accusing them of promoting five kilograms of hashish. [@1,3] Three people were arrested in the Jazan case: two Yemeni border-security violators and one Saudi citizen. [@3] The announcement
identified the detainees only by nationality, calling the Yemenis violators of
the border-security system and describing the other as a citizen. [@1] The
directorate said the suspects were stopped, statutory procedures were taken
against them and they were referred to the Public Prosecution. [@1,3] The arrests
were announced on 24 November 2026. [@1]

Sobra News, reporting the same announcement, said the directorate arrested
Yemeni border-security violators and a citizen in Jazan for promoting five
kilograms of hashish. [@2]
