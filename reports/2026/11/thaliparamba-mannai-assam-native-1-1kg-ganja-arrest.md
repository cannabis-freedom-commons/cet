Excise officers arrested Assam native Ainul Haq at Mannai in Thaliparamba with
1.106 kg of ganja. [@1,2] The arrest was made during a check conducted as part of
Operation Thunder. [@1,2] The team acted on secret information received by Excise
Preventive Officer K. Muhammed Haris of the Kannur Deputy Excise Commissioner
squad. [@1,2] Thaliparamba Excise Range Inspector E. P. Vipin and his team caught
the suspect. [@1,2]

A case was registered against him under the NDPS Act. [@1,2] Excise said Ainul Haq
is a key link among those bringing large quantities of ganja into Kerala from
other states. [@1,2] The accused was to be produced before the Thaliparamba
Judicial First Class Magistrate Court. [@1,2] Further proceedings in the case will
take place in the Vatakara Narcotics Court. [@1,2]
