Military police arrested a man on suspicion of drug trafficking on Monday 16
November 2026 in Santo Antônio de Jesus, in the Recôncavo region of Bahia, after
a report that he was assaulting a woman. [@1] The arrest took place around 10:00
on Rua 21 in the Alto Santo Antônio neighbourhood. [@2] Officers from the Tático
Móvel mobile tactical group of the 14th Military Police Battalion received a
report of an assault taking place inside a residence. [@1] On arrival they said
they heard the man threatening the woman with death, entered the property and
found the couple arguing. [@1] During the approach officers found a .40-calibre
pistol magazine on the suspect and a backpack containing drugs. [@1]

The Military Police list of seized items included 24 portions of a substance
resembling marijuana (maconha). [@1] It also listed 43 portions of a substance
resembling hashish (haxixe). [@1] The same list recorded 1,601 portions of a
crack-like substance, which is not counted as cannabis. [@1] It recorded 278
portions of a cocaine-like substance. [@1] It recorded 18 portions of an
ecstasy-like substance. [@1] Police also collected 13 rounds, a .40-calibre
magazine, two radios, 24 empty plastic tubes and R$ 3, and found two wild birds
in two cages at the property. [@2]

The suspect and all the seized material were taken to the Territorial Police
Station in Santo Antônio de Jesus, and he remained at the disposal of the
courts. [@1] Relatives and neighbours tried to prevent the arrest, and the
police said a less-lethal instrument was needed. [@3] Residents reported
possible excessive force in videos shared on social media, alleging that bombs
were used to disperse onlookers, and no independent confirmation of
disproportionate force was reported. [@2] Another local account described the
operation as seizing more than 2,000 drug portions in total. [@4]
