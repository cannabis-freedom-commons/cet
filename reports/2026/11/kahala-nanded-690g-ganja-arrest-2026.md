Kuntur police raided a cannabis sale at Kahala, in the Barabda police-post area
of Naigaon taluka, Nanded district. [@1] The raid took place on 19 November 2026
at about 8 pm. [@1]

Officers seized 690 grams of ganja, which police valued at about 30,000 rupees.
[@1] Police arrested a 20-year-old man in the case. [@1] The report identified
him as Ratan Bhagwan Giri of Kahala, who police said had been selling cannabis
in front of his home. [@1] The raid was carried out in the presence of the Naib
Tahsildar of the Naigaon tehsil office, two government panchas, police officers
and staff, and a photographer. [@1]

Police registered case 301/2026 at Kuntur police station under sections 8(c) and
20(b)(2)(a) of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985. [@1]
The accused was produced before a court and police asked for his custody for
further investigation. [@1] Inspector Vishal Bhosle is investigating the case.
[@1]
