On Tuesday 24 November 2026, the Federal Police and the Paraná Military Police
seized about 52 kg of marijuana during a joint inspection operation on the banks
of the Paraná River in Foz do Iguaçu. [@1] The joint teams were patrolling the
river margin in Foz do Iguaçu when officers spotted a boat making a crossing
toward the Brazilian side. [@1] During the patrol, officers watched the boat
unload packages on the Brazilian bank before moving in to inspect the landing
site. [@1] As the teams approached, the suspects abandoned the packages and fled
into a nearby wooded area. [@1]

Officers carried out inspections around the landing point and found several
abandoned packages of drugs left on the riverbank. [@1] The volumes recovered at
the scene held about 52 kg of marijuana in total. [@1]

The seized drugs were taken to the Federal Police station in Foz do Iguaçu for
the applicable legal procedures. [@1]
