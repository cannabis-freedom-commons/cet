Police at the Baba Haridas Nagar police station in Delhi's Dwarka district
arrested one man during an action against drug trafficking. [@1] The man was
identified as 30-year-old Naveen, a resident of Indira Market in Najafgarh,
Delhi. [@3] Police said Naveen was a habitual offender with four earlier cases
registered against him between 2019 and 2025 under the NDPS Act and the Delhi
Excise Act. [@1]

During a search officers recovered 3.032 kilograms of ganja from the white
plastic bag the man was carrying on a scooter. [@1] A second bag held silver
ornaments and 59,000 rupees in cash. [@1] Police also impounded the scooter they
said was being used in the trafficking. [@1]

A special team had been formed on the instructions of Dwarka district DCP Kushal
Pal Singh and under ACP Raj Kumar of Najafgarh subdivision, led by Inspector
Ajay Kumar of Baba Haridas Nagar police station. [@1] The team was patrolling in
a lane near the Bengali Clinic by Gupta Market in Najafgarh when it stopped the
man after his activity appeared suspicious. [@1] An FIR was registered at Baba
Haridas Nagar police station and the man was arrested. [@1] Police said they
were questioning Naveen to trace the source of the ganja supply, others involved
in the trade and the origin of the silver ornaments and cash. [@2] Officials
said action against traffickers would continue under the Nasha Mukt Delhi
campaign. [@3]
