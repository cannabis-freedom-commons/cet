The Grenzpolizeigruppe Waldsassen uncovered three drug violations in vehicle
checks on Friday and Saturday, seizing marijuana, hashish and crack. [@2] On
Friday afternoon a patrol checked a VW Crafter with Zwickau registration
carrying three people. [@2] The 31-year-old driver handed over a small amount of
marijuana when asked about prohibited items, which he had previously brought
from Czechia into Germany. [@2]

Officers seized the marijuana and are investigating the 31-year-old for a
violation of the Konsumcannabisgesetz, while police said importing cannabis
across the border remains prohibited even though cannabis is legal in Germany
under certain conditions. [@2] In the early evening on Friday officers checked a
VW Passat entering Germany from Czechia and found a larger amount of hashish in
the mid double-digit gram range attributed to the 19-year-old driver. [@2]
Officers found the 19-year-old was driving under the influence of cannabis, so
she had to undergo a blood test and was not allowed to continue her journey for
the time being. [@2]

At midday on Saturday officers checking a Mercedes found small amounts of crack
in the 56-year-old driver's travel bag on the back seat, seized the drugs and
opened criminal proceedings for a violation of the Narcotics Act. [@1]
