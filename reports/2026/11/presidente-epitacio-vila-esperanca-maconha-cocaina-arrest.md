Military Police arrested a man in flagrante for drug trafficking on Saturday 21
November 2026 in Vila Esperança, Presidente Epitácio, São Paulo. [@1] Officers
on patrol said the team already had information about possible sale of narcotics
by the man near commercial establishments and kept him under observation. [@1]
During the operation officers recorded movements characteristic of possible drug
transactions. [@1] Following that information officers approached the suspect.
[@1]

With the suspect officers found one portion of marijuana and one portion of
cocaine. [@1] Both portions were packaged for sale. [@1] Officers also seized
R$20 with the suspect. [@1] After the flagrante arrest the man remained at the
disposal of the courts. [@1] The Military Police account was reported on Monday
23 November 2026. [@1]
