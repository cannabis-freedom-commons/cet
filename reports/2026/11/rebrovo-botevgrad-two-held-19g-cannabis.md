At about 20:10 on 20 November 2026, officers of the Svoge district police
department checked a man from Zlatitsa in the area of the railway station at
Rebrovo, in Sofia Province. [@1] During the check of his personal luggage the
officers found a parcel wrapped in foil containing 22 grams of amphetamine and
16 grams of marijuana. [@1] The 37-year-old man was detained for up to 24 hours,
and a pre-trial proceeding was opened against him for an offence under Article
354A of the Criminal Code. [@1]

The second case was in Botevgrad, where at about 21:00 on 21 November 2026
officers of the local district department checked an abandoned building in the
Saransk residential complex. [@1] In the room, occupied by a 33-year-old local
resident, the police found three grams of grassy matter that reacted for
cannabis in the test carried out. [@1] That man was also detained for up to 24
hours, and a fast-track proceeding was started against him under Article 354A of
the Criminal Code. [@1] The checks were announced by the Sofia regional
directorate of the Ministry of Internal Affairs. [@1]
