Three men were detained in Cuauhtémoc during a surveillance operation by the
city's Secretariat of Citizen Security. [@3] The arrests happened on 16 November
2026 in colonia Paulino Navarro, at the corner of Topacio and Francisco Javier
Clavijero. [@3] The three were identified as Víctor Alfonso “N”, 42, Brayan
Jesús “N”, 33, and Rafael Rubén “N”, 38. [@3] They tried to escape when they saw
the police and were intercepted metres away. [@3] The three are described as
alleged members of La Unión Tepito, a criminal organization with a presence in
the capital. [@3]

Officers seized 97 bags of suspected marijuana. [@2] The same search found 52
bags of suspected methamphetamine and 28 wrappers of cocaine, which are not
counted as cannabis. [@2] Officers also took a small black backpack, a mobile
phone and cash. [@2] El Universal reported the operation as the seizure of 177
wrappers of suspected drugs in total. [@1]

The three were placed at the disposal of the Public Ministry, which will
determine their legal situation. [@2] A records check reported that Víctor
Alfonso had entered the Mexico City prison system in 2024 for crimes against
health. [@3] Security sources cited by Infobae said the men belonged to a La
Unión Tepito cell involved in extortion, kidnapping, protection payments and
drug sales. [@3]
