Salem divisional anti-narcotics police searched an express train when it arrived
at Jolarpettai railway station during the midnight check. [@1] The train was the
Dhanbad-Alappuzha Express travelling from Dhanbad via Jolarpettai toward
Alappuzha in Kerala around midnight on 14 November 2026. [@1] During the check
officers inspected a bag lying unclaimed in the general compartment and opened
it to examine its contents. [@1]

Police described the consignment as 5 kg of ganja smuggled on the express train
near Jolarpettai. [@1] Officers found 5 kg of ganja inside the unclaimed bag and
seized the contraband on the spot. [@1] The search team handed the seized 5 kg
of ganja to Jolarpettai Railway Police for further legal action. [@1] The bag's
owner was not identified at the scene, with the bag described only as unclaimed
in the general compartment. [@1]

Jolarpettai Railway Police registered a case over the 5 kg seizure and are
continuing investigations to trace those responsible. [@1] The train was passing
through Tiruppathur district via Jolarpettai when the midnight search recovered
the unclaimed ganja. [@1]
