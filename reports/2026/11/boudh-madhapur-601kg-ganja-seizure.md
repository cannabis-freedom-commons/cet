Purunakatak police seized 601.2 kg of ganja from the roadside near Madhapur
Chhak on National Highway 57 in Boudh district, Odisha. [@1] The seizure weighed
601 kg and 200 grams and was made during a night patrol on the previous day.
[@1] Police spotted the consignment near Madhapur Chhak, under the Adenigarh
outpost of Purunakatak police station, before it could be transported. [@1]

Police said the seizure was the largest quantity of ganja recovered in the
Purunakatak police station area so far. [@1] The identity of the person or group
behind the consignment remains unknown. [@1] Police are investigating where the
ganja originated, where it was to be transported and who was involved in the
planned trafficking. [@1] The people behind the attempted trafficking are yet to
be traced, and no arrest was reported. [@1]
