The Força Tática of the 15th Battalion of the Military Police arrested a man and
seized three cannabis plants in Bairro Benedito Rondon, Rio Brilhante, after
receiving reports of possible drug trafficking and cultivation at a residence.
[@1] During patrol, officers spotted a motorcyclist matching the descriptions in
the reports stopping in front of the indicated property, and a portion of a
substance resembling marijuana was found on him. [@1]

The man told officers he was a user and that he cultivated cannabis plants in
the backyard of his residence; with his authorization they searched the property
and found three plants of the cannabis species at the back of the lot, and no
other illicit material. [@1] The portion of the substance found with the man
weighed 7.1 grams. [@2] Maracaju Speed, citing the police incident report, dates
the flagrante to about 14:30 on Monday 16 November 2026 and places the house on
Rua Candelária Sarate, in Vila Planalto, identifying the man as R. M. de C.
(30). [@3]

The man was taken to the Rio Brilhante Civil Police station together with the
seized material and his cell phone, conducted without handcuffs and without
bodily injuries. [@1] Maracaju Speed adds that the man was taken without
handcuffs and without bodily injuries, accompanied by his parents during the
search. [@3]
