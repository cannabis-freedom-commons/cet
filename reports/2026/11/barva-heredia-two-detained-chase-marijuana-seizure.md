Fuerza Pública officers detained two men on Tuesday in Barva de Heredia after
they allegedly tried to flee a police checkpoint and were caught with several
packages of apparent marijuana. [@1] A police chase in Barva de Heredia ended
that afternoon with the detention of two men moving drugs after they tried to
evade a road checkpoint and were involved in a traffic crash while fleeing. [@2]
The detainees were identified only by the surnames Solera and Badilla. [@1,3]

The operation took place at Calle Lajas, where officers manning a road
checkpoint signalled the driver of a vehicle to stop, and the driver allegedly
ignored the order and sped away. [@1] The incident occurred in the Calle Lajas
sector, where officers at a preventive checkpoint signalled the driver to stop
and he sped away, starting a police pursuit. [@2] The fleeing vehicle crashed
into two other vehicles before officers intercepted it and detained its
occupants. [@1] Officers saw one suspect throw a bag onto the sidewalk, and
inside they found several packages of apparent marijuana. [@1,3] Some of the packages bore a photograph of Rafael Caro Quintero and a Colombian flag. [@3]

After securing the scene and seizing the apparent drugs, the Fuerza Pública
coordinated with the prosecution service, which assigned the Drug Control Police
(PCD) to come to the scene and take over the case. [@2] Authorities had not
reported the quantity seized or the cargo's possible destination. [@1] The case
remained under development pending official information on the police and
judicial proceedings. [@1]
