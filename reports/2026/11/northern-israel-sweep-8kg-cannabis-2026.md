Police in northern Israel carried out a series of raids in several towns,
seizing what police said was more than 8.5 kilograms of narcotics including
marijuana, hashish and crystal meth, and arresting several suspects. [@1,3]
Zevulun police investigators arrested a Kiryat Bialik resident after a foot
chase on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@2,3] The suspect tried to flee and
discard a bag holding about 74 grams of suspected crystal meth prepared for
distribution, an electronic scale and tools for hiding drugs. [@1,3] In Nahariya,
officers seized about 60 grams of marijuana and 41,200 shekels believed to be
criminal proceeds, and arrested a suspect for possessing the material. [@1,3]

Kiryat Ata detectives, joined by members of the Yamas Asher intervention unit
and a police dog handler, raided a residential apartment in the city. [@2,3] They
seized more than 5.6 kilograms of marijuana and more than 2.7 kilograms of
hashish in the apartment. [@1,3] The same raid produced 58 biscuits filled with
hashish and eight electronic smoking devices. [@1,3]

Police said all the materials and items found were confiscated and taken for the
collection and examination of evidence within the investigation. [@2,3]
