The Fayoum Criminal Court sentenced defendant M. A. G. A. to life imprisonment
and fined him 100,000 Egyptian pounds. [@1] The ruling was issued in the Itsa
felonies case after the court found the prosecution's charges against him
proven. [@1] The defendant denied the charges during prosecution questioning and
before the court, and his lawyer asked for an acquittal. [@1]

Itsa detectives said the defendant, who was known to the security services,
trafficked drugs and kept firearms and ammunition to protect that activity. [@1]
Acting on a prosecution arrest-and-search warrant, police stopped him near his
home in Al-Siririya village in Itsa after he tried to flee. [@1] Searching his
clothes, officers found a locally made single-barrel 16-gauge shotgun with a
cartridge in its barrel, 15 plastic bags of plant material identified as
hashish, eight more bags of white material identified as methamphetamine, and
160 Egyptian pounds. [@1]

The central chemical laboratory found the 15 hashish bags weighed 19.5 grams in
total and contained the active substance of the hashish plant. [@1] The
laboratory found the other eight bags held 3 grams of methamphetamine, which is
not counted in the cannabis figures. [@1] Prosecutors had charged him with
possessing hashish and methamphetamine for trafficking and with holding the
unlicensed shotgun and cartridge. [@1]
