Coahuila's Fiscalía General del Estado, through its Delegación Norte II, carried
out operations and searches in Ciudad Acuña over the weekend, resulting in about
four kilograms of marijuana seized and two people detained. [@1] The actions
followed anonymous complaints received by the agency. [@1] They were carried out
in coordination with federal forces and local agencies as part of strategies
against narcomenudeo. [@1]

The first operation searched a home at Sierra Nevada 1403-A in the Cuarta
Privada subdivision of Altos de Santa Teresa, where officers detained two
people. [@1] A second search at Heriberto Jara 305 in Colonia Revolución seized
about four kilograms of marijuana. [@1] Both the detainees and the seized drugs
were placed at the disposal of the Public Prosecutor's Office, which will
continue the proceedings. [@1]

Delegation authorities said operations to combat narcomenudeo will continue in
the municipality of Acuña, framing the weekend searches as part of an ongoing
effort. [@1] The account presents the weekend work as separate operations and
searches in the same municipality. [@1]
