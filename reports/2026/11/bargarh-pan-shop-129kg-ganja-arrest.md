Bargarh rural police carried out raids on Saturday in Odisha's Bargarh district,
acting against the illegal transport and sale of ganja, the local name for
cannabis. [@1] During the raid officers checked the pan shop — a small kiosk
selling betel-leaf products — of 20-year-old Prakash Naik at Barahagoda Canal
Chowk. [@1] Police said Prakash Naik was arrested in connection with the find.
[@1]

At the shop officers seized one kilogram 290 grams of ganja, a total of 1.29
kilograms of cannabis flower, the weight given by the police. [@1] They also
took a mobile phone, 1,100 Indian rupees in cash and six cigarette rolls that
had been filled with ganja. [@1]

The six rolls were listed separately from the 1.29 kilograms of ganja, with the
cash and the phone among the other seized property. [@1] The check took place at
a canal-side chowk in Bargarh, and police described the raids as action against
illegal ganja transport and sales. [@1]
