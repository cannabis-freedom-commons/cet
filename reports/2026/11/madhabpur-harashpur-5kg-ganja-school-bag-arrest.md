Police in Madhabpur, Habiganj detained 23-year-old Robin Mia while he was
carrying five kilograms of ganja in a school bag. [@1] The arrest took place at
about 4 pm on Thursday 19 November on the brick-soling road in front of Anwar
Hossain Rubel's pharmacy at Harashpur Bazar in the upazila. [@1] The detained
youth was identified as the son of Md Khokon Mia from Alinagar village in
Dharmaghar Union. [@1]

Police said a team led by Kashimnagar police outpost in-charge Inspector Iktiar
Mia, including ASI Delowar Hossain, carried out the raid on a secret tip-off.
[@1] Officers detained Robin Mia at the scene while he was carrying ganja in the
school bag. [@1] Police later recovered five kilograms of ganja from the bag
held in his possession. [@1]

Madhabpur police station officer-in-charge Md Sohel Rana confirmed the
detention. [@1] He said a case under the Narcotics Control Act was being filed
against the youth and further necessary legal steps were in process. [@1]
