The joint force destroyed approximately 45,000 illegally cultivated cannabis
plants spread across nearly eight acres of land in West Tripura district on
November 16. [@1,2,3,6,7,8,9] The joint operation was carried out in the Taranagar-Noagaon
area under Shidhai Police Station between 4 am and 7 am on November 16. [@1,2,4,5,6,7,8,9] The
drive was led by Mohanpur Sub-Divisional Police Officer Ashish Dasgupta, with
personnel from Shidhai Police Station, TSR and the BSF's 140th Battalion taking
part. [@1,3,6,7,8,9] Personnel from the 9th Battalion of the Tripura State Rifles participated in the coordinated drive. [@9]

Security personnel identified and uprooted illegally cultivated cannabis plants
at 17 different locations across the area. [@1,2,4,6,7,8,9] The destroyed plants were
estimated to number around 45,000. [@1,6] The operation was part of efforts by the
state police and security forces to curb illegal cannabis cultivation in
Tripura. [@1,6]
