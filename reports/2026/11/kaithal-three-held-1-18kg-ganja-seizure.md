Kaithal's Anti-Narcotic Cell made the arrests in two separate cases under its
drive against drug trafficking. [@1] Police arrested Vishal and Sonu of Valmiki
Basti, Dhand on accusations of preparing and selling narcotic drug solution, and
Manohar Sada of Bihar on accusations of selling ganja at Anaj Mandi, Pai. [@1]

Police said they recovered 20 banned Adnok tablets, 456 grams of narcotic drug
solution and 1 kg 180 g of ganja flower-leaves from the three accused. [@1] Only
the 1 kg 180 g of ganja flower-leaves is counted as cannabis; the tablets and
solution are not included in the cannabis weight. [@1]

Police spokesperson Praveen Sheokand said SI Pradeep Kumar's Anti-Narcotic Cell
team caught Vishal and Sonu on Saturday evening under the bridge near the fish
market. [@1]
