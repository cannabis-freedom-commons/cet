Telangana's Prohibition and Excise Department arrested seven people wanted in
multiple ganja-related cases in Dhoolpet, Hyderabad, on Thursday, 26 November
2026. [@3] Those arrested were identified as Jayabai, Shiva Vani Singh, Sneha
Singh, Suman Bhai, Nayana, Devender Singh and Durgesh. [@1] Officials said they
were wanted in cases involving the illegal sale and possession of ganja. [@3]
Earlier that month cases had been registered against Jayabai and Shivani Singh
over a seizure in the Gangabowli area, where both had been absconding. [@3]

Around 30 excise personnel from Dhoolpet, Hyderabad Enforcement, DTF Hyderabad
and the Shamshabad, Charminar, Golconda and Rajendranagar stations took part in
the drive. [@2] The operation was ordered by Enforcement Director Avinash
Mohanty and Greater Hyderabad Deputy Commissioner A. Srinivasa Reddy. [@3] The
raids covered Mangu Basti, Balaram Galli, Gangabowli, Mangal Haat, Lower
Dhoolpet and Jummerat Bazaar. [@1] A 30-member team led by Dhoolpet Excise
Station CI Praveen Kumar carried out the simultaneous searches. [@4] The teams
searched those localities to trace suspects allegedly evading arrest in cases
registered during 2026. [@2]

The arrested people were produced for further legal proceedings. [@3] Officials
said action against the illicit ganja trade in the area would continue. [@3]
