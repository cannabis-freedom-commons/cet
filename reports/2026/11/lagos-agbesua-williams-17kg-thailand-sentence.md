Justice Ibrahim Kala of the Federal High Court in Ikoyi, Lagos, convicted and
sentenced Agbesua Adeolu Williams to 10 years' imprisonment, or a 10 million
naira fine, for trafficking 17 kilograms of cannabis sativa. [@1] The charge
said Williams imported the cannabis on or about 19 October 2026 while passengers
on a Qatar Airways flight from Thailand were cleared at the arrival hall of
Terminal 2 of Murtala Muhammed International Airport in Ikeja, Lagos. [@1] At
the hearing on Wednesday 18 November 2026, NDLEA prosecutor Cyril Osaigbovo told
the court a one-count charge was pending and asked that it be read so Williams
could plead. [@1]

The charge was read and Williams pleaded guilty. [@1] The prosecutor called an
NDLEA operative, who described his investigation and tendered documentary
evidence for the prosecution. [@1] Osaigbovo then asked the court to convict
Williams as charged on his guilty plea and the documentary evidence. [@1]
Justice Kala convicted him. [@1]

Defence counsel Oreofe Ogunleye asked for mercy, saying Williams was a
first-time offender and a young man who had not wasted the court's time by
pleading guilty. [@1] She asked the court to give him another chance with an
option of a fine instead of a custodial sentence. [@1] Justice Kala then
sentenced him to ten years' imprisonment or a fine of 10 million naira. [@1]
