Police arrested a 33-year-old Nigerian man on suspicion of possessing narcotics
for the purpose of dealing. [@1,5] The arrest was made by the state police in
Genoa after a stop in via Bertolotti in the Cornigliano district. [@3,4] The stop
took place around 18:00 on Thursday in via Bertolotti, Cornigliano, while
officers were carrying out a territorial control operation. [@1,5] Officers said
the man tried to move away as soon as he noticed the patrol cars, in an attempt
to avoid the check. [@1,4]

His nervous behaviour led the officers to press their checks, and they found
more than a hectogram of hashish. [@1] A personal search found nine stecche
(hashish sticks) and a panetto (slab) of hashish weighing 108 grams in total,
along with 50 euros in cash. [@1,4] Another local account gave the same totals of
108 grams of hashish and 50 euros. [@3]

He was taken to the Questura police station and held awaiting a rito
direttissimo, a fast-track hearing, scheduled for that morning. [@2,4,5]
