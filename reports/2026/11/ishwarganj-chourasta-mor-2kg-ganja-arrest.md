Bangladesh's Department of Narcotics Control arrested a 47-year-old man with 2
kilograms of ganja in Ishwarganj, an upazila or sub-district of Mymensingh
district. [@1] The department identified him as Md Morom Ali, 47, whose home is
in the Ekratoli area of Chunarughat upazila in Habiganj district, according to
sources in the agency. [@1] A team from the department's Mymensingh divisional
intelligence office carried out the operation at the Chourasta Mor area under
Ishwarganj police station on Friday 20 November 2026. [@1] Officers arrested him
from beside a shop named Sri Krishna Bastraloy on the Kishoreganj–Mymensingh
highway. [@1]

The department said that 2 kilograms of ganja, the Bengali term for cannabis,
were recovered from him. [@1] Sub-inspector Azgar Ali of the Department of
Narcotics Control filed a case as the complainant at Ishwarganj police station
over the arrest. [@1] The Department of Narcotics Control said after the arrest
that its anti-drug operations would continue. [@1]
