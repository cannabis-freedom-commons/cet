A court in Zaragoza that took over the inquiries has archived the separate
money-laundering case opened against nine people for an alleged money-laundering
offence linked to a large hashish seizure. [@1] The court acknowledged a
procedural error because in the legal period of one year no evidence was taken
and none of the nine possible perpetrators was called to testify. [@1]

Officers seized almost a tonne and a half of cannabis in the operation. [@1] The
seized cannabis was valued at more than four million euros. [@1]

The action was carried out as Operation Loren Mau by the Guardia Civil and the
Customs Surveillance Service, which dismantled an international drug-trafficking
network based in Zaragoza. [@2] The network specialised in distributing large
hashish consignments concealed in lorries carrying legal merchandise. [@1] The
drugs were prepared in several warehouses in Zaragoza, where consignments were
loaded for onward transport. [@1] The consignments were dispatched from Zaragoza
to other countries, with Italy named as one destination. [@1]
