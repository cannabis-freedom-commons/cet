A mobile court in Naikhongchhari Upazila of Bandarban sentenced three men to
varying jail terms for consuming yaba and keeping ganja. [@1] The drive took
place on 14 November, a Saturday, in the area by the gate of Saleh Ahmed
Government High School in Ward No. 1 of Sadar Union. [@1] The mobile court was
led by Upazila Nirbahi Officer Enamul Hasan and it sentenced the three men for
drug use and keeping drugs. [@1]

The account identifies the three sentenced men by name, parentage, home area and
age as Rubel Karim aged 20, Touhidul Islam aged 20 and Helal Uddin aged 26. [@1]
Rubel Karim is the son of Ali Hossain from Salamipara in Naikhongchhari Sadar
Union. [@1] Touhidul Islam is the son of Abdur Rahim from Fakrikata in Ramu
Upazila. [@1] Helal Uddin is the son of Hafez Ahmed from Kauwarkhop Union and is
aged 26. [@1]

The drive was presented as part of the local administration's drug-control
effort. [@1]
