Law-enforcement agencies detected 6,792 criminal drug cases in January-October
2026. [@4] Of those cases, 1,975 involved illegal sale, 4,662 involved illegal
acquisition and possession, 153 involved illegal cultivation of
narcotic-containing plants and 2 involved other crimes. [@1] Officers held 4,731
people criminally liable for drug trafficking, including 4,603 men and 128
women. [@1] Among those held liable were 60 foreign nationals, from whom
officers seized a total of 690 kg of drugs, 2,778 tablets and 880 g of cannabis.
[@3]

Seizures in the detected cases totalled 9 tons 49 kg 295.58 g of narcotic drugs
and psychotropic substances. [@1] Marijuana accounted for 7 tons 860 kg 330.386
g of that total and hashish for 253 kg 763.447 g, with heroin, opium, cocaine,
other narcotics and psychotropic substances making up the remainder. [@2]
Officers also seized 21,382 cannabis plants alongside 55,732 methadone tablets.
[@2]

The State Commission's working group valued the combined haul at 265,473,000
manat. [@1] The figures were published by the permanent working group of the
State Commission on Combating Drug Addiction and Illicit Drug Trafficking. [@1]
