Mansurpur police in Muzaffarnagar arrested a 65-year-old man, Pradeep, under the
Operation Savera drive against illegal narcotics and seized 11.45 kg of illegal
ganja from him. [@1] Police identified the arrested man as Pradeep, son of Atra,
of Jadauda village in the Mansurpur station area of Muzaffarnagar district. [@1]

On Sunday the Mansurpur team was checking suspects and vehicles near a tubewell
before the underpass on the road to Sonta when, on an informer's tip, it stopped
and searched the suspect and recovered the ganja from his possession. [@1]
Police registered crime number 341/2026 against him under sections 8 and 20 of
the NDPS Act and began proceedings to produce him before the court. [@1] The
operation was carried out under Senior Superintendent of Police Suraj Kumar Rai,
with SP City Amrit Jain and Khatauli circle officer Rupali Rai Chaudhary
supervising and station in-charge Vipin Kumar Tyagi leading the arrest team of
sub-inspector Shikhar Chaudhary, head constables Rajiv Bhardwaj and Suhail Khan
and constables Mohit Yadav and Monu. [@1]

The same 65-year-old detainee was held by Mansurpur police with 11.45 kg of
ganja and booked under the NDPS Act for production before the court. [@2] The
arrest took place on 15 November 2026, the arrested man was named as Pradeep,
and the action was under Operation Savera. [@3]
