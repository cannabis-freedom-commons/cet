Railway Protection Force officers at Kishanganj railway station in Bihar seized
58.6 kilograms of ganja from the S-3 coach of train 15946, the
Dibrugarh-Lokmanya Tilak Express, in the early hours of Thursday 26 November
2026. [@1] The ganja was in 48 packets left unattended under a seat, and the
coach was searched after the train reached platform 2 at about 3:02 am. [@4]

The search followed a tip from Katihar at about 2:45 am that narcotics were
being carried in the coach. [@4] The load was taken to the RPF post, where the
seizure list was prepared at about 9 am with a team from Katihar present. [@4]
The RPF handed the ganja to the Government Railway Police at Kishanganj, which
registered case 71/2026 under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act
and began looking for unknown smugglers. [@4] Prabhat Khabar reported that a
First Information Report had been registered and that police were working to
identify the smugglers. [@2]

Police estimated the value of the haul at about 29.30 lakh rupees. [@3] The
consignment was hidden for the run from Dibrugarh to Lokmanya Tilak Terminus in
Mumbai. [@3] No arrests were reported, and police said they were trying to
establish who put the packets on the train and where they were to be delivered.
[@3]
