Mauritania's National Gendarmerie battalion in Kaédi arrested two people
suspected of distributing drugs, one in the city of Kaédi and the other in the
village of Jol, after a period of field follow-up and surveillance. [@1] The
arrests were announced on Monday 23 November 2026 by Al-Wiam, Mauritania's
national news agency, citing a gendarmerie statement. [@3] Kaédi is the capital
of the Gorgol region. [@3] In the Kaédi operation, gendarmes also detained five
people who were about to buy drugs from one of the suspects. [@2]

The Kaédi operation yielded a quantity of cannabis that had been cut into
portions and prepared for distribution, together with two motorcycles. [@1] In a
separate operation in Jol, officers seized a sum of more than four million old
ouguiyas, several smartphones, three motorcycles and a batch of valuable
clothing. [@1]

The gendarmerie said the two operations formed part of security efforts to
combat the spread of drugs and curb activities that promote their use. [@1]
