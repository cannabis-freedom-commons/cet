Jack Jones, 28, of Belmont, Hereford, pleaded guilty to one count of driving
while over the drug limit and was sentenced by magistrates in November. [@1] On
May 9, 2026, Jones was driving a Volkswagen Transporter on Belmont Road,
Hereford, when the level of Delta-9-tetrahydrocannabinol, the principal
psychoactive component of cannabis, in his blood exceeded the legal limit. [@1]

A blood sample showed 2.2 micrograms of Delta-9-tetrahydrocannabinol per litre
of blood, above the legal limit. [@1] Magistrates sentenced him on November 4,
2026, taking his guilty plea into account. [@1]

Jones was disqualified from driving for three years and fined £120. [@1] He was
also ordered to pay £85 in prosecution costs and a £48 victim surcharge. [@1]
