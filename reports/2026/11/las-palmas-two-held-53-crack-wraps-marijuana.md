National Police officers arrested two young men in Las Palmas de Gran Canaria as
alleged perpetrators of an offence against public health after seizing more than
50 doses of drugs. [@1] The arrests happened at about 06:30 on 15 November 2026
in an area of the capital known for drug buying and selling. [@1] Officers
intervened after seeing three men arguing, with one insistently demanding that
the other two return 100 euros he said he had given them to buy drugs. [@1]

Officers found two watches and cash among the belongings of one of the young
men. [@1] From the second young man they seized several wraps containing
apparent crack, marijuana and a pink powder that could be tusi, plus a folding
knife. [@1] Under the seat of a moped beside the men, officers found another 44
wraps of a white solid substance, apparently crack. [@1] In total the operation
yielded 53 wraps of presumed crack plus other drugs, 136.50 euros in cash and
two watches whose provenance could not then be established. [@1]

The RTVC account says National Police officers detained two young men after
seizing more than 50 doses of drugs allegedly intended for sale. [@2] Both young
men were detained as alleged perpetrators of an offence against public health.
[@1] Both detainees remained pending presentation to the competent judicial
authority. [@1] The two watches were sent to the Judicial Police Area of the
Centro District police station to establish where they came from. [@1]
