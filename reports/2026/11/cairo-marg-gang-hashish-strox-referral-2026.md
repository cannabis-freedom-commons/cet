The competent investigation authorities referred three defendants to criminal
trial in El-Marg on accusations that they formed a gang specialising in drug
trafficking after being caught holding quantities of hashish and Strox. [@1]
Marg police station investigators had received information that a woman, her two
daughters and her brother had formed a gang specialising in drug trafficking.
[@1]

The group allegedly used their home in the station's district as a base for
their criminal activity and stored the drugs there before selling them. [@1]
After verifying the information and completing legal procedures, a security
force targeted the accused, detained them and found quantities of hashish and
Strox in their possession. [@1] When confronted, the accused admitted holding
the seized drugs to trade and sell to their customers for profit and unlawful
gain. [@1]

The authorities completed the required legal procedures and sent the case to
criminal trial as security forces continued intensive campaigns against
drug-trafficking hotspots and dealers across the districts. [@1] The referral
came within the Interior Ministry's efforts to combat drug trafficking, with
security forces continuing to pursue traffickers in various districts. [@1]
