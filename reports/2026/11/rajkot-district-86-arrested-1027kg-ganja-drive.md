Rajkot district police said they arrested 86 people, including suspected
peddlers, over 11 months in a campaign against the sale of ganja and other
intoxicants and sent them to jail. [@1] The district Special Operation Group
(SOG) carried out raids at 45 places during that period. [@1] Police said the
campaign included intensive day-and-night patrols and special watch to keep
young people from taking drugs. [@1]

Officers registered 45 cases under the NDPS Act: 30 by the SOG, 12 by local
police and three by the Anti Narcotics Wing. [@1] The raids covered areas of the
district including Gondal, Upleta, Metoda, Dhoraji, Jetpur and Vinchiya. [@1]
Among the narcotics seized, police listed 1,027 kg 73 g of ganja, 304.730 mg of
MD powder worth Rs 914,190 and 225 tramadol tablets worth Rs 5.30 lakh. [@1]
Police put the combined value of the drugs and ganja haul at Rs 5.30 crore. [@1]

Police described the campaign as action against drug traders who were ruining
the district's young people. [@1] The arrests and raids took place across the
district over the same 11 months, targeting the sale and trafficking of ganja
and other intoxicants. [@1]
