Four alleged members of the Barrio 18 gang were captured in Guatemala City after
seizing a resident's home to turn it into an operations center. [@1] Those
arrested had forced the owner out under direct threats a month and a half
earlier and had lived since then in the property at lot 8, sector 1, block J, in
the Lomas de Santa Faz neighborhood, in zone 18. [@1] The operation was carried
out by the National Division Against Criminal Development of Gangs (Dipanda) of
the National Civil Police (PNC) together with prosecutors of the Public Ministry
(MP). [@1] Police identified the occupants as Juan “N”, 22, alias “Mono”; Roni
Denilson “N”, 21, alias “Frijol”; Marco “N”, 32, known as “Colocho” or “Maco”;
and Elena “N”, 42, alias “Dalia”. [@1] Institutional reports linked the four
people to the criminal structure clica MDR of Barrio 18, a cell that follows the
orders of a leader known inside the group as the “homie brincado Juan Eliú”.
[@1]

During the judicial search, officers seized a store of weapons that the illegal
occupants kept in different rooms of the residence. [@1] The official inventory
confirmed the seizure of a .223-caliber carbine, three 9-millimeter pistols and
four bulletproof vests. [@1] Investigators also found six magazines and 148
rounds of usable ammunition, 68 for the long gun and 80 for the short arms. [@1]
Besides the weapons, authorities seized 41 individual packages of marijuana and
a motorcycle with an active theft report. [@1] The two-wheeled vehicle, plate
P-646LBZ, had been stolen by armed men on 10 November at Plaza Barrios in
capital zone 1. [@1]

According to initial PNC inquiries, the group used the seized property as a
refuge to shelter illicit goods and to coordinate illegal collections from
delivery workers and traders in the area. [@1] The investigation found that the
four used the house to set extortion quotas under death threats against
neighbors who refused to hand over the demanded sums. [@1] Prison records showed
that alias “Dalia” had a prison entry dated 17 August 2024 for money laundering
or other assets. [@1]
