Military Police officers seized a restricted-use pistol, ammunition and cannabis
tablets on the morning of Monday 23 November 2026 when they checked an
interstate bus on the BR-116 highway in Jamapará, a district of Sapucaia. [@1]
The seizure itself occurred during a routine approach during the bus inspection.
[@1] Officers identified a 19-year-old female passenger whose behaviour they
described as suspicious. [@1] While inspecting the interior of the vehicle,
officers said they smelled drugs. [@1] The bus was operating the interstate line
between Rio de Janeiro and Governador Valadares in Minas Gerais. [@1]

In the hand luggage the passenger was carrying, officers found a 9mm pistol.
[@1] The pistol had a magazine containing 14 intact rounds. [@1] In the same
hand luggage officers also found two large tablets of maconha, the Portuguese
word for cannabis. [@1] Officers took the woman to the police station in
Sapucaia, where she was booked for drug trafficking and illegal possession of a
restricted-use firearm. [@1]
