Police in Russia's Rostov Oblast detained a 51-year-old resident of Dubovsky
District on suspicion of keeping marijuana at home. [@1,3] Officers of Police
Department No. 4 of the Volgodonsk intermunicipal police directorate acted on
operational information and detained the local man. [@1] A second account says
the search took place at his home in the village of Dubovskoye and its
outbuildings. [@2]

The search of the house and outbuildings yielded plant material, which an expert
identified as cannabis (marijuana) with a total mass of 7.9 grams. [@1,3] Police
said the man had picked wild cannabis plants in a forest protection belt and
brought them home for personal use. [@1,3] The second account likewise says he
collected wild cannabis in a shelter belt and kept it at home. [@2]

A criminal case was opened under part 1 of article 228 of the Russian Criminal
Code, covering the illegal acquisition, storage, transport, manufacture and
processing of narcotic drugs. [@1,3] The suspect was placed under an undertaking
not to leave and to behave properly. [@1,3] The second account says the offence
carries a maximum of three years in prison. [@2]
