Military police of the Cavalry of the 14th Regional Command arrested a
38-year-old alleged faction member for drug trafficking on the night of 18
November 2026 in Nortelândia. [@1,2] The team had received complaints that a
residence in the town was being used as a drug storage and distribution point.
[@1] A woman at the address said she did not know about the complaint and
authorised the officers to enter. [@1]

Inside, officers found the suspect showing great nervousness and noticed a
strong characteristic drug odour before searching with the woman's permission.
[@1] In the house search, officers found two tablets of maconha alongside four
tablets of cocaine base paste, 47 lança-perfume bottles and 1,223 pins of
cocaine. [@1,2] Officers also found precision scales, radio communicators and a
notebook, hidden in various rooms of the residence. [@1]

The man confessed he was solely responsible for the drugs and was keeping them
at home on orders of a criminal faction. [@1] He was arrested in flagrante and
taken with all the seized material to the Arenápolis police station for
registration. [@1,2]
