Police arrested Mónica Liliana De La Fuente in Carmen de Patagones on the night
of Thursday 19 November 2026, on an order from Guarantee Court No. 4 requested
by prosecutor Mauricio Del Cero. [@1] Officers of the Dirección Departamental de
Investigaciones carried out the arrest at a house on Julia Dufour at number 600.
[@2] She is accused of selling cocaine and marijuana with one of her
granddaughters, who was 17 at the time of the acts under investigation. [@1] In
that operation officers seized marijuana, cash and mobile phones. [@1] She was
to be questioned the next day by prosecutor Del Cero, who is leading the
investigation. [@1]

The case began on 23 April 2026 after a complaint to the 134 line alleging that
De La Fuente and her grandchildren were selling cocaine and marijuana. [@2]
Prefectura Naval Argentina watched the house and saw hand-to-hand exchanges in
the street and deliveries through a window. [@2] On the afternoon of 4 June 2026
officers searched the same address and seized 39.79 grams of cocaine, 15.25
grams of marijuana, cash, medicines said to be used to cut drugs, nylon cuttings
and mobile phones. [@3] The Federal Police laboratory in Bahía Blanca found the
cocaine samples were between 72.29% and 83.86% pure, and the marijuana was said
to equal about 17 hand-rolled cigarettes. [@2]

Analysis of the seized phones reconstructed alleged sales between 3 January and
4 June 2026. [@1] Investigators said De La Fuente sold cocaine with the
granddaughter through deliveries at agreed points in Carmen de Patagones. [@1]
Messages referred to preparing substances, stock on hand and police patrols
nearby, and others said money was hidden in a child's crib and that items used
in the sales were kept in a child's backpack. [@1] On 25 November 2024
Correctional Court No. 2 gave De La Fuente a suspended sentence of three years
as a co-author of simple drug possession. [@1] That earlier case began with a
search of the same Julia Dufour address on 29 August 2022, which found 46.7
grams of marijuana. [@1]
