On the afternoon of 13 November 2026, officers of the Squadra Mobile of the
Arezzo Questura arrested a 28-year-old Albanian man in Arezzo on suspicion of
drug possession with intent to deal. [@1] The operation began with a check on a
41-year-old Italian man already known to law enforcement, at whose home in
Arezzo officers found the Albanian man staying as a guest. [@1]

A search of the room used exclusively by the Albanian man found about 100 grams
of cocaine and 0.71 grams of hashish. [@1] Officers also found packaging and
weighing material, a mobile telephone and 160 euros in cash in the room. [@1]
Police concluded the drugs were intended for dealing rather than personal use
because of the quantity, the way they were divided and the packaging material.
[@1] After routine formalities the arrested man was taken to the local prison at
the disposal of the judicial authority. [@1]

During the same operation the 41-year-old Italian was found with a small amount
of hashish and received an administrative sanction. [@1] Officers also found 22
electronic vouchers in his possession, 15 Amazon and 7 Google Play vouchers
worth a total of 2,600 euros. [@1] The vouchers were seized and the Italian man
was reported at liberty for receiving stolen goods after he could not explain
their origin. [@1]
