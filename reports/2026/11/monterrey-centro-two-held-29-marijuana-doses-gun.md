Monterrey municipal police arrested two men in the city centre on the night of
25 November 2026. [@1] The detainees were identified as Rubén Patricio, 21, and
Édgar Eduardo, 26. [@1] Officers of the Special Operations unit were on a
prevention patrol when they saw the two arguing in the street. [@3] The arrest
was made at the corner of Julián Villarreal and Arteaga at about 21:05. [@3] A
search of two backpacks found a firearm and 29 bags of green herb with the
characteristics of marijuana. [@3]

Police said both are investigated for extortion against managers of bars in the
first cuadro of Monterrey. [@2] Investigators also link them to the theft of a
DVR recorder from a business's video-surveillance system on 25 September. [@2]
MVS Noticias reported that police consider the two generators of violence in the
metropolitan area. [@4] MVS reported that, besides health offences and carrying
a firearm, they are being investigated over the extortions. [@4] The two were
taken to Monterrey police facilities and placed at the disposal of the
authorities that will determine their legal situation. [@2]

Enfoque Monterrey reported that the authorities did not specify the firearm's
calibre, whether it was loaded, or the weight of the drug seized. [@5]
