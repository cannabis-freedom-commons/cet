Mossos d'Esquadra officers arrested a woman and a man, aged 38 and 48, in Les
Planes d'Hostoles in the Garrotxa comarca of Girona. [@1] The investigation
began after police suspected that cannabis was being grown inside the house.
[@1] Officers carried out an entry and search of the dwelling on 17 November and
arrested the two suspects at the scene. [@1]

The plants were being grown indoors, under artificial lighting. [@1] Police
seized 877 cannabis plants along with growing equipment that included LED
lights, fans, extractors and lamps. [@1] In one room of the property, officers
also found 71 grams of marijuana in a bag. [@2]

The two detainees were accused of drug trafficking and of defrauding the
electricity supply, which had been connected illegally. [@1] The electricity
company estimated the value of the diverted power at about €3,500. [@1] Neither
of the two had a criminal record, and both were brought before the duty court in
Olot. [@1]
