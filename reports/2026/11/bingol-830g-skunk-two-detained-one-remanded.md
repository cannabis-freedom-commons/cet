Bingöl Provincial Police Directorate's Narcotics Crimes Combat Branch carried
out a targeted operation in Bingöl as part of its work against drug trafficking
and drug use. [@2] Officers detained two suspects during the operation in
Bingöl. [@4] Judicial proceedings were started against both suspects on charges
of drug trafficking and of buying, possessing and using narcotic or stimulant
substances for personal use. [@2] After police procedures were completed, both
suspects were referred to the judicial authorities for a court decision on
detention. [@1] The court released one suspect under judicial control while
remanding the other suspect to prison. [@2]

During the searches officers seized 830.75 grams of skunk cannabis in the Bingöl
operation. [@2] In the same searches officers also seized cloth pieces assessed
as having been soaked with methamphetamine and one blank-firing pistol, which
were taken into safekeeping. [@1]

The cloth involved was described as a white cloth measuring 110 by 45
centimetres. [@3] Police said the operation was intended to prevent drug
trafficking in the area where the seizure was made. [@1]
