Kalamata narcotics police, assisted by the Messinia crime-prevention unit,
arrested a 37-year-old man and a 37-year-old woman at their home in Agios
Konstantinos Arfaron on Thursday morning for alleged dealing in heroin, cocaine
and cannabis. [@1] A second account dates the morning arrests to 12 November
2026 in a local community of the municipality of Kalamata. [@2] Police said the
cohabiting pair had been dealing for at least the previous two months, supplying
users who came to the house. [@1] Investigators put the alleged sale prices at
10 euros per gram of heroin, 100 euros for cocaine and 10 euros for cannabis.
[@1]

A search of indoor and outdoor areas of the house found and seized a total of 27
grams of heroin, 11.4 grams of cocaine and 1.10 grams of cannabis, according to
police. [@1] Officers also seized a scale and 495 euros in cash, which police
said was believed to come from the illegal activity. [@1]

Two more people, a 46-year-old man and a 37-year-old woman, were arrested in the
same case for allegedly having obtained drugs from the couple. [@1] The
46-year-old was arrested on 11 November with 0.20 grams of cocaine and the woman
on the next morning with 0.5 grams of heroin, both of which were seized. [@1]
All four detainees were taken before the competent judicial authorities. [@1]
