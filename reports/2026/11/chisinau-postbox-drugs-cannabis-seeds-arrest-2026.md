A 29-year-old man from Chișinău was placed in pre-trial detention for 30 days on
suspicion of trafficking drugs through Telegram. [@1] Police said anti-drug
officers documented his activity for about a month, during which he smuggled
drugs from several European countries. [@2] The court ordered the detention at
the request of the case prosecutor. [@2]

Police said he rented a flat in the capital and installed a post box at the
entrance of the block marked with the number of an apartment that does not exist
in the building. [@1] He ordered drugs almost daily, in amounts of up to 10
grams, from European platforms and collected the parcels sent from abroad from
the improvised box. [@1]

He was detained in mid-November at the entrance of the block just after
collecting a parcel from Germany that contained amphetamine. [@1] A body search
turned up the parcel of narcotics and a mobile phone said to have been used in
the offence. [@1] Officers had earlier taken LSD stamps sent from the
Netherlands and a parcel of hallucinogenic mushrooms and cannabis seeds from the
same post box. [@2]

Investigators said he had been investigated before for similar offences and that
a criminal case over importing narcotics from Europe by post was already before
a court. [@2] If found guilty, he faces between 12 and 20 years in prison. [@1]
