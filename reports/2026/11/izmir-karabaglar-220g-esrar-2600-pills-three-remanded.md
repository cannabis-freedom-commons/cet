The Karabağlar District Police Directorate and Yunus teams carried out three
separate drug operations in Karabağlar, a district of İzmir. [@1] Officers
detained three suspects in the course of those three operations. [@1] Officers
completed procedures for the three detainees and then sent them to the
courthouse. [@1] The court before which the suspects were brought remanded all
three in custody. [@1]

Across the three operations combined, officers seized a total of 220 grams of
kubar esrar. [@1] In the same combined operations, officers seized a total of
2,600 synthetic narcotic pills. [@1] Police records list the narcotic material
seized in the three separate operations as 220 grams of kubar esrar together
with 2,600 synthetic narcotic pills. [@1] The type and quantity of those seized
drugs were recorded as focus evidence in the drug investigation. [@1]

The findings indicate that the operations were planned to target drug
trafficking. [@1] The district police directorate and the Yunus teams ran the
operations together. [@1] The cannabis portion of the seizure was described as
kubar esrar. [@1] The non-cannabis portion was described as synthetic narcotic
pills. [@1]
