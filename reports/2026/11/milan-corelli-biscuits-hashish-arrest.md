Officers at the via Corelli Cpr in Milan, a centre holding people awaiting
repatriation, found narcotics hidden inside a packet of biscuits that a man was
trying to deliver to a person detained there. [@1] The man, a 65-year-old
Italian citizen, was arrested in the act on suspicion of unlawful possession of
drugs for dealing, in concert with two other people who were left under
investigation at liberty. [@1] The arrest was made on Friday 13 November 2026,
around 12:30, when Italian Army personnel flagged a man at the centre’s goods
gate intending to hand over food and clothing for an Egyptian detainee. [@2]

Officers made a summary inspection of the goods because weapons or drugs might
be brought into the centre, and noticed that the seal at the top of a biscuit
packet had been opened and roughly resealed. [@2] Inside, hidden among the
biscuits, they found a wrapper containing 9 grams of hashish, one oxycodone
tablet and two synthetic opioid tablets. [@2] A search of the car he had arrived
in, assisted by a Guardia di Finanza dog unit, found another 4.9 grams of
marijuana in the driver’s-door storage compartment. [@2] Two Egyptian citizens
aged 20 and 22 who were in the car were placed under investigation at liberty on
the same charge. [@1] The substances from both the packet and the car were
seized. [@1]

Investigators said they still had to reconstruct the relationships between the
three people involved and how the drugs came into the recipient’s availability.
[@1] They also had to establish where the various substances came from and who
they were destined for. [@1]
