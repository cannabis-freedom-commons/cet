Marka police in Banda district, Uttar Pradesh, arrested a young man on suspicion
of ganja trafficking under Operation Eagle, a drive against the illegal drug
trade. [@1] Superintendent of Police Palash Bansal said a campaign was under way
against criminals and people involved in drug trafficking. [@3] The patrol took
place on the night between 16 and 17 November near the Yamuna bridge in Asothar
village, where officers stopped and searched a suspicious young man. [@1] The
suspect was identified as Ravendra alias Raju, a resident of Gujeni village in
the Marka police station area. [@1]

Police said the search yielded one kilogram 250 grams (1.25 kg) of illegal dried
ganja, which they seized. [@1] Dainik Bhaskar reported that police took the
accused to the station, registered a case and, after producing him in court,
sent him to jail. [@2]

A case was registered against him at Marka police station under the NDPS Act and
legal action was begun. [@1]
