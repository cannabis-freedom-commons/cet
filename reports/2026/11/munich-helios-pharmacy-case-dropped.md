Leonardo Glowna and Stefan Mahr, the two owners of the Helios medical-cannabis
pharmacy at Königsplatz in Munich, had the criminal case against them dropped
for lack of evidence, according to Glowna. [@1] Prosecutors had investigated the
pair on suspicion of unlawfully producing hashish. [@1] No charge or court
hearing ever took place. [@2]

The Munich I public prosecutor's office searched the pharmacy at Königsplatz on
24 October 2024. [@2] Officers found hashish and equipment used to make it in
the pharmacy and seized them, the prosecutor's office said. [@3] Police and
prosecutors also seized files and about ten kilograms of fresh cannabis flowers.
[@1] The investigation concerned a cannabis medicine for inhalation from which
waxes and fats were mechanically separated from the flower. [@1]

The owners had founded the pharmacy in February 2024 and invested in
freeze-dryers and drying ovens for the project. [@1] The flowers, then still
classed as narcotics, had to be imported deep-frozen and the BfArM permit took
about a year. [@1] The owners' lawyer called the search disproportionate and a
serious infringement of fundamental rights, and described the conduct as
harassment. [@1] Glowna said the medicine is now offered by others, and he and
Mahr remain active in the cannabis business. [@1]
