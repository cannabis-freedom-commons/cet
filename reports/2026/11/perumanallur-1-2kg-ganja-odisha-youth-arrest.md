Perumanallur police arrested a man near Perumanallur on Wednesday after
apprehending him on suspicion of selling ganja in the locality. [@1] During
questioning, the youth was identified as Susantmuki, aged 20, who is a native of
Odisha state. [@1] Police said he was working at a banian knitwear manufacturing
company in New Tiruppur at the time of the arrest. [@1]

According to police, a secret tip had indicated that ganja sales were taking
place on the Perumanallur-Kanakkampalayam road. [@1] Acting on that information,
officers rushed to the location and detained a youth who was standing there
suspiciously carrying a handbag for questioning. [@1] The inquiry established
that he had kept the ganja in his possession specifically for the purpose of
selling it. [@1]

Following the inquiry, Perumanallur police registered a criminal case against
him and formally placed him under arrest. [@1] Police further seized 1 kilo 200
grams of ganja, equivalent to 1.2 kilograms, from his possession during the
operation. [@1]
