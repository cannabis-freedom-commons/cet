On 17 November 2026 the Grasse gendarmerie company carried out multiple controls
at Peymeinade, Saint-Vallier de Thiey, Le Rouret, Roquefort-les-Pins and Vence
on drivers doing school runs while transporting children. [@1] The operation
checked 51 school bus drivers. [@1] Grasse company commander Mathieu Jarnigon
said the objective was primarily preventive, screening all school bus and
shuttle drivers for alcohol and drugs. [@1]

At Saint-Vallier-de-Thiey and Roquefort-les-Pins, two tested drivers were
positive for cannabis. [@1] Those results were confirmed by complementary
analyses. [@1] The two drivers, aged 42 and 58, had their driving licences
immediately retained for up to 72 hours. [@1] A longer-term suspension could
later be ordered by the prefecture. [@1] Schoolgirls were aboard one driver’s
bus when he was controlled, he was asked to leave the vehicle, and a third
person took the wheel to continue the route. [@1]

At Vence another driver screened positive for cocaine, but the follow-up blood
analysis was negative. [@1]
