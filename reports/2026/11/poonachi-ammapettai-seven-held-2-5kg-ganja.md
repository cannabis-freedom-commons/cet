Police received a tip that some people were selling cannabis illegally in the
Poonachi area near Ammapettai, in Erode district near Bhavani. [@1] Officers
went to the spot, surrounded the people there and caught them, and found that
they had cannabis. [@1] Officers searched a rented house beside the Poonachi oil
mill on Wednesday 18 November 2026. [@2] Six of those at the house tried to flee
and were caught: Karthik, 26, Gokul, 20, Gokulraj, 29, Balu, 26, Vijay, 25, and
a 16-year-old boy. [@2] Police arrested seven people, among them a 16-year-old
boy. [@1] The suspects were residents of Chithode, Sengunthapuram, Vellode,
Navapatti near Mettur and Kaveri Bridge, places around Bhavani. [@2]

Officers seized 2.5 kilograms of ganja, two knives, an air gun, a car, four
motorcycles and six mobile phones. [@1] Satheesh, 25, of Karumalaikoodal is also
named among the seven arrested. [@1] The Dinamani report of the raid said police
were still searching for Satheesh of Karumalaikoodal. [@2]
