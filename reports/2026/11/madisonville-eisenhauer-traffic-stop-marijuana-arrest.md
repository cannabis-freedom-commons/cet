Madisonville Police Department officials say a woman is in jail after a traffic
stop led to multiple drug charges. [@1] Officers were dispatched for a vehicle
swerving and unable to stay in its lane, with a taillight out. [@1] Police saw
the vehicle turn left onto North Main Street from West Noel Avenue. [@1] The
driver, later identified as Amanda Eisenhauer, drove on the yellow line several
times while traveling northbound. [@1] A traffic stop was initiated and
Eisenhauer pulled into Advanced Auto. [@1]

Eisenhauer said the truck was difficult to drive and she had just picked up her
child, who was asleep in a booster seat in the front, and was on the way home.
[@1] When she exited the vehicle, officers detected the strong odor of burnt
marijuana. [@1] Asked if any marijuana was inside the vehicle, she said she did
not know if it was inside and she had just smoked. [@1]

During a search, police found a small amount of suspected marijuana, a used
Suboxone strip wrapper and several white pills. [@1] Eisenhauer was arrested and
booked into the Hopkins County Jail on charges of careless driving, operating a
motor vehicle while under the influence, wanton endangerment, possession of
marijuana and possession of a controlled substance. [@1]
