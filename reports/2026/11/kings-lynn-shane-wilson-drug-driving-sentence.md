Prolific Lynn offender Shane Wilson, 32, appeared at Lynn Magistrates' Court on
Thursday and admitted three offences. [@1] Prosecutor Wendy Davidson said a £700
e-bike went missing on July 6 in Norwich despite being locked up. [@1] The
bike's owner traced it by GPS to outside the cathedral gardens in the city,
where Wilson was found with another person. [@1]

On April 4 Wilson was stopped on a motorbike after being seen at 45mph in a
30mph zone and overtaking on a sweeping right-hand bend. [@1] A drug wipe came
back positive and Wilson, of Jarvis Way in Clenchwarton, was arrested and taken
to Lynn's Police Investigation Centre for further tests. [@1] Blood tests showed
127mcg of cocaine per 100ml of blood, against a 50mcg legal driving limit. [@1]
The tests also showed 9.8mcg of THC, described as a cannabis breakdown, per
100ml of blood, against a 2mcg legal driving limit. [@1]

In mitigation, George Sorrell said Wilson wants to work with the court's
probation service to improve his life. [@1] Wilson was given an 18-month
community order requiring 40 hours of unpaid work and a mental health treatment
requirement. [@1] He was also banned from driving for 32 months and ordered to
pay a £114 victim surcharge. [@1]
