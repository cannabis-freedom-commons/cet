A 33-year-old gold miner was taken into police custody after suspected cannabis
was found during a mobile patrol at Sands Backdam, Cuyuni River, on Friday
afternoon. [@1] Police in Regional Division Seven said officers approached a
lorry parked along the roadway for mechanical repairs at about 16:00. [@1] As
the officers approached, they saw the man throw a black plastic bag into nearby
bushes. [@1]

A search of the bushes recovered the bag, which contained five bulky parcels of
leaves, seeds and stems suspected to be cannabis. [@1] The miner was described
as being from the Richmond Housing Scheme, Essequibo Coast, and the location was
given as Sands Backdam, Cuyuni River, in Region Seven. [@2]

The man was arrested and escorted to the Parika Police Station, where the
suspected cannabis was weighed in his presence and amounted to 2,205 grams
before being lodged. [@1] He remained in custody pending charges. [@2]
