The Sohag Criminal Court sentenced a worker identified only by the initials
M.S.Kh. to three years of aggravated imprisonment for trafficking hashish and
narcotic substances in Tama markaz. [@1] The verdict was issued under judge
Mohamed El-Amir Abu Kreisha, with members Ahmed Talaba and Ahmed Mustafa Abu
Sahli. [@1]

Detectives in Tama markaz received information in 2026 that the man was dealing
in hashish, and a confidential source confirmed it. [@1] Officers arrested him,
and he admitted trafficking hashish and narcotic substances before he was
referred to the criminal court. [@1]
