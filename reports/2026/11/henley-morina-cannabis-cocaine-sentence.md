Police caught Edison Morina in Henley-on-Thames on 28 September 2026, where he
was standing behind a black BMW. [@1] He ran off during a drug search and was
arrested after a short chase. [@1] In the vehicle, officers found white powder
later confirmed to be cocaine, along with cannabis and psychedelic chocolate.
[@1] They also seized £1,865 in cash. [@1]

Edison Morina, 24, an Albanian man of no fixed abode, was sentenced at Oxford
Crown Court on 26 November 2026. [@1] He pleaded guilty to possessing cannabis
with intent to supply it, possessing cocaine with intent to supply, possessing
criminal property and driving without third-party insurance. [@1] Prosecutor
Leandra Smith told the court that Morina had four previous convictions for five
offences. [@1] His barrister, Eiran Reilly, said Morina wanted to get back to
Albania as soon as possible. [@1] Morina was an overstayer who was not allowed
to work legitimately in the UK. [@1]

Judge Ian Pringle KC handed him a 32-month prison sentence. [@1] The court also
ordered the forfeiture and destruction of the drugs, paraphernalia, cash and
phones seized. [@1]
