Police in Pulwama district arrested three alleged drug peddlers during a naka
checking operation in the Kakapora area. [@1] A police party from Police Station
Kakapora led by Sub-Inspector Tariq Ahmad was conducting naka checking at
Gundibagh Crossing when it intercepted three persons approaching the main road
towards Pahoo while carrying a nylon bag. [@1] The accused were identified as
Suhail Shafi of Pinglena, Rakeeb Ahmad Dar of Tilangam and Mehraj-ud-Din Mir of
Pahoo. [@1]

During their search, police recovered approximately 6.010 kilograms of a ground
charas-like substance from their possession. [@1]

In connection with the seizure, FIR No. 110/2026 under Sections 8/20/29 of the
NDPS Act was registered at Police Station Kakapora and further investigation was
initiated to trace the source of the contraband and identify others in the
trafficking network. [@1]
