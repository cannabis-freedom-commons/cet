Officers of Kyrgyzstan's Service for Combating Illegal Drug Trafficking (SBKNON)
of the Interior Ministry detained two people in Chuy Region on suspicion of
selling hashish in large quantities. [@1] The service named the detainees only
by initials, E. u. B. and K. A., and said SBKNON officers detained them during
operational and investigative measures. [@1] During a search of the two,
officers found and seized hashish in a large quantity. [@1]

A criminal case was opened under article 282 of the Criminal Code, which covers
the illegal manufacture of narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances and their
analogues with intent to sell. [@1] The service said police were still
identifying other people involved in the crime and the channels of the drug
trade. [@3]

Open.kg reported the same detention and said SBKNON officers carried out a
series of operations before arresting the two. [@2] Open.kg described the
quantity found in the search only as a significant volume of the drug. [@2]
