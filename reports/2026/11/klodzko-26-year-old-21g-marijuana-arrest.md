Police in Bystrzyca Kłodzka detained a 26-year-old man from Kłodzko County. [@1]
He was charged with possession of drugs. [@1] Officers from the local criminal
unit had received information that the man might be keeping illegal substances
at his home and checked the address. [@1]

In a chest of drawers in one room they found bags of dried plant material and
white powder. [@1] Preliminary tests identified the dried material as marijuana
and the white powder as amphetamine. [@1] The marijuana weighed 21.2 grams and
the amphetamine 7.4 grams, according to information supplied by the police. [@2]
Officers also seized bags of tablets of various kinds whose composition is still
to be established in laboratory tests. [@3] They also secured 4,200 zł in cash.
[@2]

The case was described as ongoing. [@1] Police said the criminal unit was
continuing work to clarify all the circumstances, including where the drugs had
come from. [@1] Investigators were also checking whether other people were
involved. [@3]
