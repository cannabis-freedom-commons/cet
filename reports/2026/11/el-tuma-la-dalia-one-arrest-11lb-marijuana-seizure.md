On the afternoon of Wednesday 11 November 2026, at about 4:30 p.m., National
Police officers raided a dwelling in the El Carmen Número 2 district of El
Tuma-La Dalia, Matagalpa. [@1] The raid took place at about 4:30 p.m., according
to Matagalpa police spokeswoman Captain Rosa Amanda Castillo. [@1] Officers
arrested 45-year-old José Santo Sánchez Espinoza during the search. [@1]

In a search of the detainee, officers seized a backpack containing seven pellets
wrapped in adhesive tape and filled with green herb. [@1] A field test of the
herb was positive for marijuana. [@1] Police weighed the marijuana at 11 pounds
(about 4.99 kilograms). [@1]

A second account of the same operation likewise places the capture and seizure
on the afternoon of 11 November in El Carmen Número 2. [@2] It also describes
seven marijuana pellets weighing eleven pounds taken from the 45-year-old
detainee. [@2]
