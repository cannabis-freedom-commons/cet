Central Kalimantan regional police and district units uncovered 698 narcotics
cases with 883 suspects in 2026. [@1] The tally is the accumulation of case
disclosures from 1 January to 19 November 2026. [@1] Central Kalimantan police
chief Inspector General Iwan Kurniawan presented the figures at a press
conference in Palangka Raya on 23 November 2026. [@1]

The narcotics seized in those cases included 120.54 grams of cannabis (ganja),
alongside methamphetamine, ecstasy pills, carisoprodol and etomidate. [@1]
Police estimated the value of all seized evidence at Rp337,045,675,800. [@1]
Under the national police chief's 100-day programme from 17 October to 19
November 2026, police uncovered 93 cases with 108 suspects. [@2] Of those 108
suspects, 11 were alleged dealers (bandar), 96 alleged distributors (pengedar)
and one was linked to unlicensed alcohol production. [@1]

Police intensified Operasi Sarang Narkoba from 17 October 2026. [@1] In 2026 the
provincial and district narcotics units carried out 171 community education
activities, and 133 drug users underwent rehabilitation. [@1]
