Greenville police arrested 20-year-old Edenton resident Jeffrey Cayden Harrison
in Greenville, Pitt County, on Sunday, 22 November 2026. [@1] Arrest records
list Harrison as charged with trafficking MDMA, possession with intent to sell
marijuana, and maintaining a vehicle for keeping or selling controlled
substances. [@1] He also faces charges related to an open container of alcohol,
carrying a concealed gun and possessing drug and marijuana paraphernalia. [@1]

The records allege Harrison had 48.77 grams of marijuana and between 28 and 200
grams of MDMA. [@1] They also allege officers found a concealed firearm, an open
can of Busch Light and plastic bags and glass jars in a vehicle. [@1]

The warrant for the firearm-by-felon charge says Harrison had previously pleaded
guilty to assault on a law enforcement officer with a firearm in Perquimans
County Superior Court. [@1] It says he was sentenced to 36 months of supervised
probation for that prior offence. [@1] Harrison is being held in the Pitt County
jail without bond. [@1] He is scheduled to appear in court Monday, Nov. 23. [@1]
