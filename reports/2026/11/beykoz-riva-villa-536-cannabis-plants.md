Beykoz district police raided a detached three-storey villa in Istanbul's Riva
neighbourhood after receiving information that drugs were being grown there.
[@2,5,6,7] One suspect was caught in the operation. [@2,5,6,8] The raid was carried out on
25 November once the address had been identified. [@3,5,7] The suspect's statement
and link to the case were still to be assessed. [@1]

Officers found 536 Indian hemp plants and 150 grams of esrar, the Turkish term
for cannabis resin, during the search. [@2,5,6,7] The haul included 79 LED lights, 19
fans, six air conditioners, three ventilation fans, a dehumidifier, six boxes of
pesticide, a spraying pump and a tent. [@4,6] Police also took 5,000 Turkish lira
in cash, six mobile phones, a vacuum machine, a steel safe and a money-counting
machine. [@4,6]

Investigators said the seized material showed the greenhouse had been fitted out
professionally with climate-control and lighting systems. [@3] The operation was
part of police work to prevent the manufacture and trafficking of drugs. [@2,6]
The investigation into the case was continuing. [@2,6]
