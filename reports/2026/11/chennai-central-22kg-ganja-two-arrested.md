Washermenpet anti-drug unit police received a secret tip that ganja was being
smuggled from Odisha to Chennai. [@1] On the basis of the tip, officers kept
intensive surveillance in the Chennai Central area on Monday. [@1] They stopped
two men moving in a suspicious manner and questioned them. [@1] Police
identified the pair as 32-year-old Subrata Behera of Nandipada village in Odisha
and 24-year-old Anand Murugan, also called Kulla Anand, of Grace Garden in
Royapuram. [@1] Police arrested both men after the seizure. [@1]

Officers searched a bag held by the pair and found 22 kilograms of ganja inside.
[@1] Police seized the 22 kilograms of ganja, which had been brought by train
from Odisha to Chennai. [@1] The interception took place in the Chennai Central
area during the surveillance operation. [@1]

Royapuram police registered a case, and questioning established that the two had
bought the ganja in Odisha and brought it to Chennai by train. [@1] Police said
further investigation was continuing. [@1]
