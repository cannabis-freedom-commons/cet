Police in Bolzano reported several people during intensified checks over the
weekend. [@1] On Friday evening on Pfarrplatz officers stopped a man who had
dropped a packet and fled at the sight of them. [@1] The man was a previously
convicted asylum seeker and was reported on suspicion of drug dealing. [@1]
Shortly afterwards on Ziller-Platz officers watched a young man throw away two
packets. [@1] The 20-year-old Tunisian was reported on suspicion of drug
dealing. [@1] Officers also found hashish on a 21-year-old Tunisian who was
reported but remained free. [@1] On Saturday afternoon in the old town a woman
stole 60 euros from a closed restaurant till, pushed the employee and fled. [@1]
Police stopped the 56-year-old German woman shortly afterwards and reported her
for attempted robbery. [@1] Three Italian citizens were reported for attempted
thefts in clothing stores and five other foreigners for disobeying expulsion
orders. [@1]

The Pfarrplatz man had about 30 grams of hashish and a dose of cocaine. [@1] The
two packets on Ziller-Platz held about five grams of cocaine in total. [@1] The
21-year-old Tunisian had about 35 grams of hashish. [@1] Officers checked 360
people and 51 vehicles within two days. [@1]

The operation was an extraordinary focus control involving the State Police, the
Carabinieri, the finance police and the municipal police. [@1] It focused on
areas of the city seen by security forces as particularly crime-prone. [@1]
