Federal Police and Paraná Military Police officers seized about 72.9 kg of drugs
beside the Paraná River in Foz do Iguaçu on Tuesday 17 November 2026. [@1] Teams
of the Military Police's Border Police Battalion (BPFRON) and its maritime unit,
NEPOM, watched a boat leave Paraguay and dock on the Brazilian bank to unload
volumes. [@2] The suspects fled into nearby woodland as police approached and
were not found. [@2] No one was arrested; all the boat's occupants escaped. [@3]

Officers found 71.8 kg of capulho marijuana, the unpressed cannabis flower, and
1.1 kg of hashish. [@1] The Paraná Military Police estimated the traffickers'
loss at about 331,200 reais. [@2]

The drugs were taken to the Federal Police station in Foz do Iguaçu for legal
procedures. [@1] The boat had set out from Ciudad del Este, and the interception
took place near Colonia de Pescadores Z-12, a fishing settlement on the
Brazilian bank of the river. [@3] The Federal Police statement put the seizure
in the Rampa do Monsenhor area of Foz do Iguaçu. [@1]
