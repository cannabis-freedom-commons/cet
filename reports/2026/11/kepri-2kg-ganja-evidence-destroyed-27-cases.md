Riau Islands regional police destroyed narcotics evidence from 27 police reports
involving 28 suspects at the Polda Kepri hall in Batam on 24 November 2026. [@2]
The cases were opened between 28 August and 11 November 2026. [@2] The 28
suspects were charged under Articles 111, 114 and 119 of the 2009 narcotics law,
police said. [@2]

The 27 cases yielded 2,013.03 grams of ganja, police said. [@1] Officers burned
1,998.03 grams of that ganja. [@2] The same destruction covered 1,618.0563 grams
of sabu, 1,269 etomidate cartridges and 652 ecstasy pills, none of which is
counted as cannabis. [@3] Eight pieces of MDMB-4EN-PINACA, a synthetic
cannabinoid weighing 83.39 grams, were also destroyed and are excluded from the
cannabis figures. [@3] The rest of the evidence was set aside for trial and
forensic laboratory testing. [@3]

Among the cases was a ganja shipment of about 1.9 kilograms sent through a
parcel service. [@2] Dirresnarkoba Kombes Suyono said the drugs were being sold
in nightlife venues, housing estates, hotels and boarding houses. [@2] Police
estimated that the seizures had saved 26,973 people from drug abuse. [@1]
Prosecutors, the Batam district court, the provincial national narcotics agency
and the anti-narcotics group Granat attended the destruction. [@3]
