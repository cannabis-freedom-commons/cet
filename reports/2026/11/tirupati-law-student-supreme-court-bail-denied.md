The Supreme Court refused anticipatory bail to V. Krishna Kishore Reddy, a law
student who is accused in a ganja trafficking case. [@1] The case was in
Tirupati. [@1] The petition came up for hearing on Wednesday before a two-judge
bench of Justices Ahsanuddin Amanullah and Manmohan. [@1] The bench did not
accept the arguments for granting him anticipatory bail, declined to consider
even interim protection and dismissed the petition. [@1]

The High Court had earlier refused to grant him anticipatory bail in the same
case. [@1] Reddy challenged that High Court order in the Supreme Court on 28
October 2026. [@1] His lawyer, Sanchita Ain, presented his arguments to the
bench. [@1]

The case began when Tirupati police took three students who were consuming ganja
into custody and questioned them. [@1] Acting on the information the students
gave, police searched Reddy's home. [@1] Officers seized 1.1 kg of ganja from
the house and arrested him. [@1]
