Guarda Nacional Republicana soldiers from the Almada territorial detachment
detained a 38-year-old man in the municipality of Almada on 24 November 2026 on
suspicion of drug trafficking. [@1] The detention took place at about 19:30
during an operation inspecting establishments, when soldiers noticed the man
near one of the premises being checked and approached him because his behaviour
seemed suspicious. [@2]

During the police action the soldiers carried out a security search and found
narcotics on him in a quantity consistent with the crime of drug trafficking.
[@3] The operation resulted in the seizure of 140 doses of cannabis resin. [@1]

The man was notified to appear at the Almada judicial court on 25 November for
the application of coercive measures. [@1] The Setúbal territorial command ran
the operation through its Almada territorial detachment and announced the
detention in a press note sent to Central Press. [@3]
