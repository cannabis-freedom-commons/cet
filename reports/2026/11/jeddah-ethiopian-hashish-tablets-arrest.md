Patrols of the General Directorate of Mujahideen in Jeddah arrested a man of
Ethiopian nationality described as a violator of the border security system.
[@1] Officers accused him of promoting hashish and tablets subject to medical
circulation regulation. [@1]

The directorate said the man was stopped, that the statutory procedures were
taken against him and that he was referred to the competent authority to
complete the required measures. [@1] Its announcement describes a single suspect
and gives no quantity of hashish, no count of the tablets, no value and no
neighbourhood where the arrest took place. [@1]

"Violator of the border security system" is the term Saudi authorities use for a
foreigner without valid residency or entry status. [@1] "Tablets subject to
medical circulation regulation" means medicines whose sale is restricted, such
as prescription drugs. [@1] The patrols belong to the General Directorate of
Mujahideen, and the arrest was reported in Jeddah governorate, in Mecca
province. [@1]
