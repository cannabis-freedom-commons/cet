Police arrested the resident of a home on the Hetty Blokstraat in
Amsterdam-Noord on 15 October 2026 and searched the home that day. [@1,4] Officers
found 34,018.50 euros in cash in the resident's vehicle when they arrested them.
[@1,4] When officers went to the home to search it, they saw a person leave with
several bags, stopped them and emptied the bags. [@1,4] The person who came out of the home with the bags was someone other than the resident. [@4] The bags held 487 grams of
hashish, more than 1,100 grams of cannabis, 12,200 euros in cash and a firearm
with a magazine and cartridges. [@1,4]

In the home itself officers found several kinds of hard drugs, including 305
grams of cocaine, and more cartridges for a firearm. [@1,4] Police suspected that
drugs were being dealt from the home. [@1,4]

Mayor Femke Halsema closed the home for three months on 18 November 2026. [@3]
The closure rests on a violation of the Opiumwet, the Dutch drugs law. [@3] In
her closure order Halsema said a commercial quantity of drugs can bring
nuisance, fire risk, rip deals (robberies of drug dealers), burglary and draws
criminals to the home. [@2] She said it is well known that people who deal in
drugs can be violent. [@2] She said the closure was meant to keep public order
and stop criminals coming to the home. [@1,4]
