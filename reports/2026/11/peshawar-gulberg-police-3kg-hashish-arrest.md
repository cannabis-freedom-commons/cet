Gulberg police in Peshawar arrested a suspected drug smuggler who was posing as
a passenger during an operation on Friday, 20 November 2026. [@1] Officers
recovered three kilogrammes of hashish from his possession, the police said.
[@1] According to a police spokesman, the operation was conducted under the
supervision of DSP Cantt Circle Dost Muhammad Khan. [@1] The spokesman said it
was carried out by SHO Gulberg Faheem Shah Khan along with other police
personnel. [@1] The arrested suspect was identified as Muhammad Umar, son of
Shah Muhammad, a resident of Sheikh Abad. [@1]

Police registered a case against the suspect and launched further investigation.
[@1] The spokesman said Peshawar police were continuing operations against drug
smugglers, illegal weapons and criminal elements on the directives of senior
officers. [@1]
