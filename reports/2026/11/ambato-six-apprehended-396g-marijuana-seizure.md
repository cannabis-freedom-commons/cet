Six people were apprehended in different police operations carried out in
Ambato, province of Tungurahua, as part of actions against trafficking of
substances subject to control. [@1] The procedures took place in different
sectors of the city, including Huachi Belén in the Ambato Sur district and San
Bartolomé de Pinllo. [@1] According to police reports, the actions were carried
out through investigative work and operations called Cero Impunidad and
Encuentro. [@1] One of the procedures occurred in Huachi Belén. [@1] In two
interventions, agents of the Anti-Drug Investigation Unit apprehended two men
aged 33 and 21. [@1] Another procedure was carried out in the San Bartolomé de
Pinllo circuit, where a 25-year-old woman was apprehended during operation Cero
Impunidad-36206. [@1] Additional interventions called Encuentro-161 and Cero
Impunidad 36195 were carried out in the Ambato Norte and Sur districts. [@1] In
those actions four men aged between 32 and 56, who had detention warrants, were
apprehended. [@1]

The 33-year-old was found with 49.20 grams of pasta base de cocaína, equal to
492 doses, and 259.60 grams of marijuana, said to represent 519.20 doses. [@1]
In the second procedure officers seized 322.10 grams of pasta base de cocaína,
equal to 3,221 doses, and 136.60 grams of marijuana, corresponding to 273.20
doses. [@1] A digital scale was also found. [@1] Police said the woman carried
1,076 grams of pasta base de cocaína, equal to 10,760 doses, with an estimated
national-market value of 1,210.50 dollars. [@1] A cell phone was also seized
from her. [@1] In those last procedures 226.90 grams of pasta base de cocaína,
equal to 2,269 doses, were seized. [@1] Officers also found four cell phones and
100 dollars in cash. [@1] The interventions together were reported to remove
more than 15,000 doses of drugs from the illegal market, between pasta base de
cocaína and marijuana. [@1]

The 33-year-old had prior records, including one for trafficking of substances
subject to control in 2023. [@1] The 21-year-old apprehended had no prior
records, according to the police report. [@1] Those four men had records
including robbery, theft, illicit association and trafficking of substances
subject to control, among other offences. [@1] The six suspects were placed at
the disposal of the judicial authorities. [@1]
