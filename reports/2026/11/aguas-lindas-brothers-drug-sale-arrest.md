Officers of the Civil Police of Goias arrested two brothers investigated for
commanding drug trafficking in Aguas Lindas de Goias, in the Entorno do Distrito
Federal. [@1] The pair were arrested on Friday 13 November in Jardim Brasilia II
in Aguas Lindas de Goias. [@2] The operation was carried out by civil police
officers of the 1st Police Station of Aguas Lindas de Goias, attached to the
17th Regional Police Station. [@2]

The investigation had identified a possible drug-selling point at the address.
[@2] Officers placed the property under monitoring and observed intense movement
of people considered suspicious. [@2] During the approach officers found
portions of marijuana, cocaine and crack packaged for sale. [@2]

According to the police, one of the brothers already had a criminal record for
the same drug-trafficking offence. [@2] The two were taken to the police station
and booked in the act for drug trafficking. [@2] They remain at the disposal of
the courts pending further judicial steps. [@2]
