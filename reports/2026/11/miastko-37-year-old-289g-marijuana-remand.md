The District Prosecutor's Office in Miastko supervises an investigation against
37-year-old Kamil J.-L., a resident of Miastko. [@1] He was charged with making
criminal threats, twice damaging property and possessing a significant quantity
of drugs. [@1] At the prosecutor's request, a court ordered the suspect held in
temporary detention for three months. [@1] The suspect partly admitted the acts,
confirming possession of 30 grams of marijuana, and denied the remaining
allegations, as relayed by the District Prosecutor's Office in Słupsk. [@1]
Investigators found he had multiple prior court convictions, including for drug
crimes, threats and property destruction. [@1]

The series began on 2 November in a flat in Miastko, when the 37-year-old
threatened his parents with death and tried to force his way into their home
through the balcony. [@1] When that failed, he threw paving stones, breaking
windows in two rooms, then fled before a police patrol arrived. [@1] On the
evening of 3 November he returned to his parents' house, threw a stone at the
balcony window causing further damage, and fled. [@1] After the reports, police
tracked down and detained the suspect. [@1]

During a search of rooms he occupied, officers found and secured 289.47 grams of
marijuana and 125.46 grams of amphetamine. [@1] Criminal threats combined with
property damage carry three months to five years in prison, while possessing a
significant quantity of intoxicants carries one to 10 years, and proceedings
remain ongoing. [@1]
