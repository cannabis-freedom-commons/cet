On Friday evening 20 November, federal police officers found a minor in Essen
main station with a larger quantity of a herbal drug. [@1] At about 22:00, the
officers encountered the 17-year-old in the station. [@1] When asked, the youth
said he had hashish with him. [@1] He handed it to the officers and went
voluntarily with the uniformed officers to the Federal Police station. [@1]

Officers weighed three differently sized sealed bags of the herbal drug and
recorded a total weight of 39 grams. [@1] Officers established the identity of
the Essen resident from his identity card. [@1] The German citizen declined to
comment on the allegations and invoked his right to remain silent. [@1] Police
informed the youth's guardian about the matter. [@1] The minor was allowed to
leave the station shortly afterwards with his father's consent. [@1]

The Federal Police opened an investigation for violation of the Consumer
Cannabis Act (Konsumcannabisgesetz). [@1]
