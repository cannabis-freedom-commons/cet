Military police in Porto Velho arrested a couple and another woman in the Marcos
Freire neighbourhood on the morning of 17 November 2026. [@2] They were detained
at a house on Rua Teixeirinha, near Rua Lumiere, which police said was being
used as a point for selling drugs. [@2] The operation followed complaints about
drug sales at the residence. [@1]

During the approach officers found a man in front of the house with a 9 mm
pistol at his waist, which the occurrence report said he used to threaten
residents. [@2] Inside, police found a brick of marijuana along with portions of
cocaine and crack, ammunition, cash and materials for wrapping drugs. [@1] The
ammunition was of .38 and 9 mm calibre, and officers also seized creatine that
the occurrence report said was used to increase the volume of the drugs. [@2]

The operation was carried out by the 5th Military Police Battalion's Reforço 02
team with support from its sector 11 police. [@2] Sergeant Machado commanded the
team that went to the address. [@1] The three detainees and all seized material
were taken to the Central de Polícia Civil. [@2] The occurrence was still under
way, with further information expected after the police procedures were
concluded. [@1]
