Police detained a 59-year-old unemployed man in Kalininsky District, St
Petersburg, on 18 November 2026 at about 18:30 near house 13 on ulitsa Olgi
Forsh. [@2] Police said the man distributed drugs through in-person meetings.
[@1]

During a search of his flat officers found and seized about 339 grams of
marijuana, scales and packaging materials. [@1] An expert examination confirmed
that the seized plant material was a narcotic drug, police said. [@2] Officers
also found packaging materials and electronic scales that could have been used
to prepare the substances for further distribution, KP reported. [@2]

Police opened a criminal case for attempted illegal sale of narcotic drugs in a
large amount. [@1] KP reported that the case was being investigated under part 3
of article 30 and point g of part 4 of article 228.1 of the Russian criminal
code, the especially large amount provision. [@2] Police were checking whether
he took part in other episodes and whether he had accomplices. [@1]
