Zevulun station detectives operating in the Asher area exposed on Saturday night
an active hydro laboratory for growing drugs inside an apartment in Kiryat
Motzkin, arresting two suspects during the operation. [@1] During the
operational activity the detectives found keys in the possession of one of the
suspects, used those keys to open the apartment, and exposed inside an active
hydro-method drug laboratory that had been erected on the premises. [@1]

In searches that officers carried out in the apartment under a court order, they
seized 289 seedlings of material suspected to be cannabis, together with
documents and additional exhibits, and arrested one more suspect as the activity
continued. [@1] The two suspects were taken to Zevulun station for questioning,
and at the end of questioning they were jailed. [@1]

All of the findings and the seedlings were transferred for continued
investigation of the case. [@1] The transferred evidence was sent for the
exhaustion of evidence in the forensic identification laboratories of the
police. [@1]
