Military police officers arrested a man suspected of drug trafficking during a
police action in the municipality of Capela, in the eastern region of Sergipe.
[@1,2] The operation was carried out by the Batalhão de Polícia de Ações Táticas do Interior (BPATI) on Wednesday (18) in Capela. [@4] The police team was carrying out an ostensive patrol when it received
information about a man suspected of involvement with the sale of narcotics.
[@1,3] The information indicated that the suspect was in front of his own
residence, where military officers identified a man whose physical
characteristics and tattoos matched those described in the complaint. [@1]

During the approach, officers found a bag containing maconha in the man's
pocket. [@1] Officers then located additional portions of the substance inside
the house, already divided into fractions and packaged. [@1] The action resulted
in the seizure of 409 grams of maconha. [@1,3] Together with the drug, officers
seized materials used to condition drugs, a scale, two cell phones and R$150 in
cash. [@1,4]

The man and all of the seized material were taken to the police station for the
adoption of legal measures. [@1,3]
