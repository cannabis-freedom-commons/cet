Guarda Civil Municipal officers arrested a man for drug trafficking in the Neves
neighbourhood of Ponta Grossa in the early hours of Sunday 15 November 2026.
[@1] The occurrence was recorded at about 0h30 during patrol by the Guarda Civil
Municipal, Ponta Grossa's municipal guard force. [@1] According to the municipal
guard, two men fled in opposite directions when they perceived the approach of
the patrol vehicle. [@1] The patrol team carried out the approach and managed to
reach and detain one of the two men. [@1]

During the personal search of the detained man, officers found substances
described as analogous to maconha, the Brazilian term for cannabis, ecstasy and
cocaine. [@1] The second man entered a residence and dropped a packet containing
substances described as similar to crack and cocaine. [@1]

That second suspect then fled the scene and was not located by the patrol team.
[@1] The man who was stopped received voice of arrest and was taken to the
Delegacia de Polícia, the civil police station, for the appropriate further
procedures. [@1]
