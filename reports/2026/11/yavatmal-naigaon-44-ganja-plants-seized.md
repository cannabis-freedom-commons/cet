The Local Crime Branch (LCB) of Yavatmal district police found illegal cannabis
cultivation in a field at Naigaon village in Darwha taluka. [@1] Officers
searched the field, found 44 cannabis plants and seized all of them. [@1] Police
put the value of what they seized at 19,450 rupees. [@1]

The raid followed a tip-off that a man had planted cannabis, known as ganja, in
his own field at Naigaon. [@1] The LCB team was patrolling Pusad subdivision on
Monday night when it received the information. [@1] Officers checked the
information first, then searched the field under the guidance of senior
officers. [@1]

Police registered a case under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances
Act at Darwha police station. [@1] The named accused is Milchand Rathod, a
resident of Naigaon in Darwha taluka. [@1] The action was carried out under
district police chief Kumar Chinta, with additional superintendent Ashok Thorat,
sub-divisional police officer Siddheshwar Bhore and LCB inspector Satish Chavre.
