Police in the village of Sakmara, the centre of Sakmarsky district in Orenburg
Oblast, inspected the outbuildings of a 62-year-old local resident during
operational measures and found a green plant mass, the regional interior
ministry said. [@1] Sakmarsky district police described the man as detained
after the inspection of those outbuildings. [@2]

The householder said he had collected parts of wild hemp growing on his own
yard, supposedly for his own use. [@1] He told officers he had obtained the
substance by gathering parts of the wild hemp plant on the yard before police
found it, and that it was for personal consumption. [@2] An examination
confirmed that what was seized was a narcotic drug weighing 264.87 grams, which
the authorities treat as a large amount. [@1] The local account gives the same
total, 264.87 grams, for the seized plant mass after the study. [@2]

A criminal case was opened over illegal acquisition and storage of the drugs.
[@1] Sakmarsky district police said the case was opened under part 2 of article
228 of the Criminal Code, which covers illegal acquisition and storage of
narcotics in a large amount. [@2] The article carries a maximum of 10 years in
prison. [@2] The regional account said the man faces up to 10 years'
imprisonment. [@1]
