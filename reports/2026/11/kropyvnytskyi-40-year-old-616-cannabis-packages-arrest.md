Police in Kropyvnytskyi detained a 40-year-old man suspected of distributing
narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances across the city, the regional police
said. [@2] During searches, officers found and seized 616 packages of marijuana
weighing about one kilogram, which police classified as a narcotic substance.
[@2] He was detained under Article 208 of Ukraine's Criminal Procedure Code and
charged under part 3 of Article 307 of the Criminal Code with selling narcotics
or psychotropics in especially large quantities. [@1] A court ordered him held
in custody for the duration of the investigation. [@1] If convicted, he faces
nine to 12 years in prison with confiscation of property, and investigators said
the case was continuing. [@1]

The same operation yielded 2,678 packages of the psychotropic PVP weighing about
two kilograms and 590 packages of methamphetamine weighing 85 grams. [@3] Police
put the total seizure at more than 3,800 packages of prohibited substances. [@2]
Zlatopil, citing the regional police communications department, reported 3,884
packages in total. [@4]
