Mamad police said Sub-Inspector Mahesh reported the arrest of two suspects for
illegally trafficking ganja in the town. [@1] The suspects were identified as
Mohammed Ayanuddin, aged 22, and Arun Kumar, aged 21, both described as
residents of Mamad. [@1] Police said both men were arrested after the motorcycle
check and were then sent to remand for further legal proceedings. [@1]

Disha datelined its report from Mamad, the mandal-level town where the police
action took place. [@1] Acting on reliable information received on Monday,
officers raided two people who were allegedly transporting dried ganja on a
motorcycle. [@1] During the raid officers seized packets containing 90 grams of
ganja from the suspects together with the motorcycle used for transport. [@1]

Sub-Inspector Mahesh warned that strict legal action would be taken against
anyone found selling or consuming ganja in the area. [@1] He also urged young
people to stay away from bad habits and intoxicants. [@1]
