Drug-control officers of the Kirovsky district police found a bag holding a
grey-green substance in the possession of a 52-year-old resident of the hamlet
of Kurganny during operational-search activities. [@1] Police of the Kirovsky
district department found and seized more than 1.4 kg of marijuana from a
resident of the hamlet of Kurganny during operational-search activities. [@1]
The regional interior ministry department confirmed the seizure of more than 1.4
kg of marijuana from the 52-year-old Kurganny resident. [@2]

Experts determined that the man had stored almost one and a half kilograms of
marijuana. [@1] The man admitted that he had found wild cannabis while grazing
cattle, picked the plants, dried them and kept them for personal use. [@1]

A criminal case was opened on signs of illegal trafficking of narcotic drugs in
a large amount. [@1] Under the current Criminal Code the man could face up to 10
years of imprisonment for the offence as described. [@1]
