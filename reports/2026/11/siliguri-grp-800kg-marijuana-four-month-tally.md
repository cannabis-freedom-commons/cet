The Siliguri GRP has asked for 500 extra personnel to tighten security along one
of India's most sensitive railway corridors known as the Chicken's Neck. [@1]
The force sent a recruitment proposal to the state police headquarters seeking
400 constables and 100 officers at ASI and SI levels. [@1] The force currently
works with roughly 450 personnel, about 150 ASIs and SIs and 300 constables.
[@1]

Since July the force has seized around 800 kg of marijuana in its railway
operations. [@1] In the same period it also seized about 1,700 litres of
alcohol, 9 kg of brown sugar, 6 kg of Yaba and 1,500 bottles of banned cough
syrup. [@1] The GRP registered 130 cases and arrested 130 people allegedly
linked to these activities. [@1] Of those cases, 65 involved drug trafficking
and 51 were related to liquor smuggling. [@1]

Its jurisdiction stretches from Murshidabad to Alipurduar and covers nearly 10
police stations and outposts between Malda and Alipurduar. [@1] The force also
provides security for 10 trains running from stations such as NJP and
Alipurduar. [@1] Siliguri GRP Superintendent of Police Indravadan Jha said the
force was managing its responsibilities despite the limited manpower. [@1] He
reported that 65 consignments of narcotics and 51 consignments of liquor were
seized over the past four months. [@1]
