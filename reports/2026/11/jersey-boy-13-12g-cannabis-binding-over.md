A 13-year-old boy admitted possessing cannabis among a string of offences dealt
with in Jersey's Youth Court. [@1] The court gave him a six-month binding-over
order and a two-year restraining order. [@1] His advocate said he had no
previous convictions and had turned a corner in recent weeks. [@1]

Police found 1.2 grams of cannabis and a bong in his bag after spotting him
drinking Echo Falls wine with another young person in Victoria Park on 12
September. [@1] Six days later, CCTV captured him smashing a glass light shade
outside the Hotel de Normandie. [@1] He exposed his bottom to a police officer
seven times during a disturbance at Charing Cross on 21 September. [@1] On 8
October, the boy stole three BuzzBallz alcoholic drinks worth £12 from the Co-op
in Colomberie, where other youths had poured baby oil on the floor. [@1]

The boy was ordered to pay £180 compensation to the hotel and £12 to the Co-op.
[@1] The court ordered the drugs to be destroyed and banned him from Millennium
Park for one year. [@1] Magistrate Bridget Shaw acknowledged he faced personal
difficulties and said the answer was not to go out and kick in the door of an
innocent woman near a park. [@1]
