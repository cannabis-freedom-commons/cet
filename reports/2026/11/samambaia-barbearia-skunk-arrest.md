A man serving a semi-open sentence was arrested on Friday 13 November 2026 in
Samambaia, Distrito Federal, suspected of using a barbershop as a drug storage
and sale point. [@1] Military police officers of the Motorized Ostensive
Tactical Patrol Battalion (Rotam) made the arrest after information shared with
the 11th Military Police Battalion. [@1]

A patrol near the shop saw the suspect talking to the driver of a vehicle and
handing over an object, and he tried to flee on foot but fell and was caught.
[@1] After an employee of a neighbouring real-estate agency authorised entry,
officers found about 610 grams of skunk, a precision scale, drug packaging,
suspected trafficking accounts and R$480 in cash. [@1]

Officers found that the mobile phone with the man had a theft or robbery record,
so he was also arrested for receiving stolen property. [@1] He was taken with
all the seized objects to the 26th Police Station. [@2] A second account
confirms the same Friday arrest in Samambaia of a semi-open detainee using the
barbershop to store and sell drugs, with about 610 grams of skunk, a scale and
R$480 seized. [@2]
