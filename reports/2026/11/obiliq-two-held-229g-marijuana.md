Police in Obilić (Obiliq), in Kosovo's Pristina district, arrested two men and
seized 22.9 grams of a substance suspected to be marijuana. [@1] The arrests
were made on 17 November 2026, the day before the account was published. [@1]
Both men were ordered held by the prosecutor's decision. [@2]

The Obilić case was one of three separate drug-possession operations that Kosovo
Police recorded that day, alongside one in Pristina and one at Doganović, near
Kačanik. [@1] Together the three cases produced 27.5 grams of narcotics, the
account says. [@1] The 3.6 grams found at Doganović was suspected cocaine, not
cannabis, and is not counted here. [@1]

Kosovo Police recorded the case as possession of narcotics rather than
trafficking. [@1] According to the Kosovo Police daily report, 22 people were
arrested across Kosovo that day and 17 were detained, figures that cover all
offences, not only drug cases. [@1]
