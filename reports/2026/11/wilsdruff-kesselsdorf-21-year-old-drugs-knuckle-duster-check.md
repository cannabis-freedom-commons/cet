In the night to 15 November 2026, at about 02:35, police officers checked a
21-year-old man on Straße des Friedens in Kesselsdorf, a district of Wilsdruff,
during a night-time control. [@1] The man now faces proceedings including for a
violation of the Narcotics Act. [@1]

During the check the officers found that the man was carrying crystal, hashish
and cannabis on his person. [@1] The Dresden police account of the same check
likewise says officers checked the 21-year-old on Straße des Friedens and that
he had some crystal, hashish and cannabis in his pockets. [@2] Police kept the
narcotics found on him. [@1] The officers also found a knuckle duster on the man
and seized it along with the drugs. [@1]

The Dresden police release times the incident at 02:35 on 15 November 2026 in
Wilsdruff, OT Kesselsdorf, and says officers kept the knuckle duster and the
drugs and filed the corresponding reports. [@2] Reports were filed against the
21-year-old over the finds. [@1]
