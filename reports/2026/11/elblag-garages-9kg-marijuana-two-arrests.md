Elbląg criminal police said they shut down an illegal drug production operation
and secured almost 20 kilograms of narcotics and psychotropic substances. [@1]
Two men, aged 20 and 27, were detained; investigators say they jointly ran the
operation in one of the city's garages, where they produced significant amounts
of amphetamine. [@1] The finished drug was not kept where it was made; the
suspects moved it to a second garage that served as a storehouse, from which the
substances passed into illegal circulation. [@1]

Searches recovered about 20 kilograms of banned substances in total — more than
10 kilograms of amphetamine and 9 kilograms of marijuana. [@1] Officers also
seized equipment used to manufacture the drugs and drug precursors. [@1]

Police press officer Krzysztof Nowacki said both detainees had already heard the
charges. [@1] A court approved a preventive measure and remanded the two men in
temporary custody for three months. [@1] Producing a significant quantity of
psychotropic substances carries a potential sentence of up to 20 years'
imprisonment under Polish law. [@1]
