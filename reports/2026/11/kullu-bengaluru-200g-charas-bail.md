The court of Special Judge Naresh Kumar at the District and Sessions Court in
Kullu granted bail on Friday, 20 November 2026, to a man from Bengaluru,
Karnataka, accused in the recovery of 200 grams of charas. [@1] The court
granted bail on a personal bond of 1 lakh rupees and two sureties of the same
amount. [@1]

According to the prosecution, police set up a checkpoint, or naka, at Dunkhara
Chowk on 25 October 2026. [@1] A taxi coming from the direction of Malana was
stopped for checking. [@1] Police said 200 grams of charas, a hand-rubbed
cannabis resin also called hashish, was found in two transparent pouches in the
bag of the accused, who was sitting on the back seat. [@1] A first information
report was registered at the Manikarna police station under section 20 of the
Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act. [@1]

In court the defence argued that the quantity recovered was below the commercial
quantity, that the investigation was complete and that the accused no longer
needed to be questioned in custody. [@1]
