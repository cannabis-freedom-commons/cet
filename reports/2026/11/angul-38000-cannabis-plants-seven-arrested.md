Police in Angul district, Odisha, arrested seven people over illegal cannabis
cultivation and registered a case against them under the NDPS Act. [@1] Four
suspects were arrested in the Bagadia police station area, where police
destroyed about 10,000 cannabis plants on two acres. [@1] In the Jarapada police
station area, about 28,000 plants on 10 acres were destroyed and three people
were arrested. [@1]

In the joint operation police uprooted and destroyed about 38,000 cannabis
plants growing illegally on about 12 acres across the two police-station areas.
[@1]

The operation was carried out under the direct instruction and supervision of
Angul superintendent of police Rahul Jain, with about three platoons of police
and 20 police officers deployed. [@1] The Angul additional superintendent of
police and the officers in charge of the Jarapada and Bagadia police stations
were present during the search. [@1] Police said they were questioning all seven
detainees to find out who else was involved and what network the cultivation was
linked to. [@1] Superintendent Rahul Jain said police in the district follow a
'zero tolerance' policy against illegal cannabis cultivation and drug
trafficking. [@1]
