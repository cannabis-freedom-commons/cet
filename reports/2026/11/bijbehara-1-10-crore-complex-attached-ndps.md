Jammu and Kashmir Police attached a commercial complex valued at approximately
Rs 1.10 crore in Bijbehara, Anantnag district, on 23 November 2026. [@1] The
property belonged to Ghulam Nabi Allai, son of the late Habibullah Allai, a
resident of Melhoora in Shopian district. [@1] The complex comprised five shops
and five rooms along NH-44 at Hamzehpora, Semthan. [@4] The attached building
was a double-storey concrete shopping complex in Anantnag district. [@4]

Police Station Bijbehara carried out the attachment under the NDPS Act during a
financial investigation into FIR No. 83/2025, registered under Sections 8/20 and
29. [@2] A police spokesperson put the property's assessed current market value
at Rs 1.10 crore. [@3] Police described the action as an investigation into
suspected proceeds of drug trafficking. [@2]

Police said the attachment formed part of efforts to dismantle the financial
networks of narcotics syndicates and seize assets acquired through the illegal
drug trade. [@1] It came a day after Anantnag Police attached a residential
house and eight shops worth Rs 4.17 crore belonging to another peddler in Mattan
under Section 68(F) of the NDPS Act. [@1] Jammu and Kashmir Police said they had
registered more than 1,500 NDPS cases, arrested nearly 2,000 people and frozen
more than 500 bank accounts across the Kashmir Valley during the year. [@1]
