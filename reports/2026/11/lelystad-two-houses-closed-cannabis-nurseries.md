Mayor Mieke Baltus of Lelystad closed three houses in the city over recent
weeks, in which various prohibited goods were found. [@1] The Wederiklaan house
was shut because a hennepkwekerij, a cannabis nursery, was found there. [@2] The
Langezand house was closed on the same ground. [@2]

The Wederiklaan closure ran from Friday 6 November 2026 to Friday 5 February
2027, when the house was due to reopen. [@2] The Langezand closure ran from 16
November 2026 to 16 February 2027. [@2] The three houses lay far apart from one
another and stood in different neighbourhoods. [@1]

The third house, on the Archipel, had been closed on Tuesday 17 November for six
months after illegal weapons, drugs and a large amount of cash were found. [@2]
The municipality said it would examine during those six months whether there was
reason to extend that closure. [@2]
