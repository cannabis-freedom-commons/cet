Federal Highway Police officers seized a marijuana load hidden in false
compartments of a car on the afternoon of Saturday 14 November 2026 during an
inspection on the BR-369 in Cascavel, western Paraná. [@1] The car was driven by
a Paraguayan man accompanied by his Brazilian wife, and officers became
suspicious because of the driver's behaviour and the car's excessive weight.
[@1] Officers said the driver appeared nervous and gave contradictory
information about the trip's destination, prompting a detailed search of the
vehicle. [@1]

Agents found false compartments fitted in the dashboard and the boot, and inside
them found several marijuana tablets. [@1] The couple told the PRF they had
received the car already loaded in Foz do Iguaçu and were taking it to Rua 25 de
Março, a busy shopping area in central São Paulo. [@1] The occupants said they
did not know who was meant to receive the vehicle and claimed they did not know
of the existence or quantity of the drug. [@1]

The car and the drugs were to be sent to the Federal Police for legal
procedures. [@1] The exact quantity seized had not been confirmed when the
information was released, with official weighing still to determine the load's
total volume. [@1] The case was to be investigated by the competent authorities.
