Narcotics Wing officers in Unjha taluka's Unava area arrested a man with 37
grams of ganja after a raid based on a tip. [@1] The man, who lives near Brasta
Colony in the Unava area, was identified as Sheikh Amir Kutubhai. [@1] Police
seized the ganja from the spot and valued it at about 1,850 rupees. [@1]

The Narcotics Wing team was patrolling the Unava and Unjha police station areas
when it received the tip. [@1] The raid was carried out in the presence of two
panchas, or witnesses. [@1] The action was taken against the illegal sale and
storage of intoxicating substances in the area. [@1]

According to police sources, Deputy Inspector General Virendrasinh Yadav and
Mehsana Superintendent of Police Himanshu Solanki had ordered the action. [@1]
Police registered a case against the accused under the NDPS Act and began
further legal proceedings. [@1]
