Four people face drug charges after the Neuse River Narcotics Task Force
searched an apartment on Trent Court in New Bern on 19 November 2026. [@1]
Janice Driggers, 55, and Marvin Ravon Phillips, 52, both of New Bern, were each
charged with felony trafficking in cocaine and felony possession with intent to
sell and manufacture marijuana. [@1] Shawn Tony Cadlett, 52, of New Bern, was
charged with two felony counts of possession with intent to manufacture, sell or
deliver a Schedule II controlled substance, felony possession with intent to
sell and deliver cocaine, felony possession of cocaine, felony conspiracy and
possession of a Schedule VI controlled substance. [@1] Boris Wilford Joyner, 61,
of New Bern, was charged with felony possession of cocaine and possession of
marijuana. [@1]

Investigators seized what the Craven County Sheriff's Office described as
trafficking amounts of cocaine and marijuana. [@1] Shalanda Dickerson, 51, of
Vanceboro, was served with a failure-to-appear warrant involving charges of
resisting a public officer, driving while license revoked, no liability
insurance, possession of a Schedule VI controlled substance and two counts of
possession of drug paraphernalia. [@1] WITN reported that five people were
facing charges after the search, counting Dickerson among them. [@2]

The Neuse River Narcotics Task Force includes the Craven, Carteret and Pamlico
county sheriff's offices, the North Carolina State Bureau of Investigation and
the New Bern Police Department. [@1]
