Police arrested an 18-year-old man in flagrante in barrio Venado de Oro, in the
urban area of San Vicente de Chucurí, Santander, after officers approached him
during a routine patrol because of his suspicious behaviour. [@1] Officers
searched him on the street and found more than 470 doses of marijuana, which the
authorities said were prepared for sale. [@1]

The young man was handed to the competent authority, which will decide his legal
situation. [@1] He will be investigated for the alleged crime of manufacture,
trafficking and carrying of narcotics. [@1]

El Frente reported that officers of the local police station were on
surveillance and patrol duty against drug trafficking in the neighbourhood when
the arrest was made. [@2] El Frente described the arrest as part of an operation
against microtrafficking, the small-scale local drug trade. [@2] Major Oscar
Peña Sánchez, commander of Police District No. 8 in San Vicente de Chucurí, said
officers keep permanent prevention, surveillance and control work in the streets
and that the result came from timely patrolling. [@3] The police reminded
residents that suspicious activity or crime can be reported on the emergency
line 123. [@3]
