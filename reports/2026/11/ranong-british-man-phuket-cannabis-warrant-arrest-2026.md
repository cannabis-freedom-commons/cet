Ranong immigration police arrested a 24-year-old British man on a warrant from
Phuket provincial court. [@1] Officers stopped him as he headed for the Grand
Andaman company pier, where they believed he meant to leave Thailand. [@3] The
operation followed a tip that the man wanted in Phuket was hiding in Ranong and
planned to cross by natural routes or a border pier. [@1]

A biometric check flagged him on the immigration bureau's watchlist and as a
person named in the Phuket court warrant. [@1] The warrant accuses him of
selling a controlled herb, cannabis flower, by keeping it for sale for
commercial purposes without permission. [@1] It also accuses him of running a
business reserved for Thais without permission, trading in local agricultural
products. [@1]

He last entered Thailand at the Khuan Don immigration checkpoint in Satun
province on 7 October 2026 on a 30-day visa exemption valid until 5 November
2026. [@1] Phuket City police confirmed he was the wanted man, and the Ranong
officers informed him of the charges and handed him to Phuket City police
investigators. [@2]
