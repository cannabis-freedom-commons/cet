Araria district police destroyed 2,099 kg of ganja and 178 g of smack seized in
NDPS Act cases, acting on a court order. [@1] The consignment was burned at the
Snerji Waste plant in Bhagalpur under Operation Prahar. [@1] The destruction
took place on Monday 23 November 2026, the day before both reports were
published. [@2]

A special committee constituted as the law requires supervised the transfer of
the ganja and smack from the police storehouse to Bhagalpur in secure vehicles,
where they were burned in the plant's incinerator. [@2] Before burning, officers
weighed the seized drugs and checked the seals on the packets after completing
the required formalities. [@2] Superintendent of Police Jitendra Kumar led the
operation. [@1]

Kumar said the ganja had been seized from different vehicles and smugglers
during raids and checks in border areas and along national highways. [@2] He
said the district police would keep up a zero-tolerance policy against the
illegal drug trade, smuggling and storage, with strict legal action continuing
in such cases. [@2]
