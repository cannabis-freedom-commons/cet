Police carried out two raids on Monday in different sectors of Cipolletti as
part of an investigation into attempted homicide, seizing one cannabis plant and
charging a woman. [@1] The operation was led by officers of Subcomisaría 79 with
the special group COER and personnel of Unidad Regional V. [@1] The measures had
been ordered by the duty prosecutor's office as part of steps to advance the
investigation. [@1]

One raid took place at a home near Antártida Argentina al 1100, where officers
found neither weapons nor the man they sought but detected a cannabis plant
during the inspection. [@1] The Federal Prosecutor's Office of General Roca
ordered the specimen seized and an adult woman found at the address charged.
[@1]

A second raid at a property around Juan Carlos Cobián al 1700 likewise failed to
locate the main suspect or any ballistic evidence. [@1] During that procedure
officers detained a direct relative of the suspect to conduct an interview
linked to the inquiry. [@1] The investigation into the attempted homicide
continued to establish the circumstances and find the main suspect. [@1]
