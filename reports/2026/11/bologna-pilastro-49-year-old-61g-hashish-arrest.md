Carabinieri of the Bologna Pilastro station arrested a 49-year-old Bologna man
for drug dealing after a targeted intelligence operation against drug
trafficking in the Pilastro district. [@2] Officers had put the flat under
observation after gathering evidence of suspected illegal activity there. [@3]
They intercepted the man as he returned to his flat and carried out a search.
[@2]

At the start of the search the man voluntarily handed over a package containing
1.67 grams of cocaine. [@2] Officers then found further doses of cocaine and
61.22 grams of hashish, the cannabis product, along with weighing and packaging
equipment. [@2] They also found a 'jammer', an electronic device used to block
or disrupt radio communications, and seized the whole package. [@1] The flat
also contained bundles of cash. [@1] All the drugs and equipment found in the
flat were placed under criminal seizure. [@4]

A prosecutor ordered the man's arrest and he was taken to a Bologna court for a
validation hearing and an immediate trial. [@2] The judge validated the arrest
and imposed house arrest, postponing the sentence at the defence's request. [@1]
