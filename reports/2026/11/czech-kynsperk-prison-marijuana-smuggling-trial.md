Five defendants went on trial at the Regional Court in Plzeň on 19 November 2026
over an organized group accused of smuggling marijuana and other drugs into
Kynšperk nad Ohří prison. [@1] The indictment named David N., a 41-year-old
prisoner at Kynšperk nad Ohří who had been employed at a DHL logistics centre in
the Cheb area, as the man who devised the scheme and directed it by mobile phone
from behind bars. [@2] Four of the five, Rostislav E., Naděžda H., David N. and
Josef H., pleaded guilty, while Oleh K., a Ukrainian, admitted one package
handover but denied wider involvement. [@1] Rostislav E., a foreman at the
logistics centre who supervised other prisoners, said David N. gradually pressed
him to obtain marijuana and other substances until he agreed and passed the
packages on. [@1] The court accepted the four guilty pleas but gave no verdict.
[@2]

Josef H., also a prisoner, carried the drugs from the workplace into the prison
hidden in his rectum; one case involved ten grams of marijuana split into ten
one-gram bags wrapped in condoms. [@1] Prosecutors counted 13 buyers for the
wider distribution, including three people under 18, and said the marijuana
alone exceeded one kilogram. [@1] In 2024 and 2025 Rostislav E. was accused of
obtaining marijuana, MDMA, cocaine and other drugs at unspecified places in the
Karlovy Vary region and passing them on. [@1] The indictment treated the whole
affair as the work of an organized group and one continuing offence, and the
court considered how a criminal-code amendment in force from 1 January 2026
changed the legal classification of some acts. [@1] Prosecutors proposed prison
terms of between two and a half and five and a half years plus fines; the
statutory maximum is ten years. [@2] Oleh K. admitted buying and using marijuana
illegally while denying any role in the smuggling. [@2] The hearing is to
continue in December. [@1]

The case began in December 2024, when Cheb detectives from the TOXI team
intercepted a parcel containing a narcotic substance at the Cheb post office,
addressed to a prisoner at Kynšperk nad Ohří. [@1] At the end of October 2025
police carried out five searches and seized one kilogram of marijuana, more than
200 ecstasy tablets and cocaine. [@1] Detectives opened criminal proceedings
against eight people aged 21 to 50. [@1] Two of those charged were serving
prisoners who were to carry the drugs into the prison. [@1] David N. also faced
a separate charge over an April 2025 incident in which a bag containing the
synthetic stimulant NEP was lowered from a prison window on a torn sheet and
intercepted by prison staff. [@1] NEP, or N-ethylpentedrone, is a synthetic
stimulant of the cathinone group and is not counted in the cannabis figures.
[@1]
