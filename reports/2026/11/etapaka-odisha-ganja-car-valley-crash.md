People who had bought prohibited ganja in Odisha and were illegally transporting
it in a car were caught by Etapaka police. [@1] The car that was transporting
the ganja struck two-wheelers one after another and finally plunged into a
valley beside the road. [@1] The crash took place in Etapaka, a border area of
Bhadrachalam. [@1] The occupants had purchased that prohibited ganja in Odisha
before carrying it illegally by car through the Bhadrachalam border area. [@1]

On Friday morning the car was travelling fast from Odisha with ganja, through
Bhadrachalam and towards Etapaka. [@1] It first hit two two-wheelers and
continued without stopping. [@1] In Etapaka it then hit another two-wheeler,
lost control and plunged into the valley beside the road. [@1]

Etapaka police, told of the accident, reached the scene at once. [@1] They
inspected the car that had plunged into the valley and found ganja in it. [@1]
Police took the people who had been in the car into custody. [@1]
