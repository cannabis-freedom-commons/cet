Giza criminal investigators arrested the owner of a PlayStation gaming shop in
Emababa and three other young men after finding them with lit hashish
cigarettes. [@1] The shop owner was identified as Amr D. [@1] The arrests were
led by Mohamed Tarek, head of Emababa investigations, and supervised by
inspector Ahmed Essam. [@1]

The operation followed information passed to Alaa Fathy, director of Giza's
criminal investigations directorate, by the head of its northern sector, Mohamed
Rabi. [@1] The information said a young man had opened a PlayStation gaming shop
in the Aziz Ezzat area and met friends from the neighbourhood there to take
drugs. [@1] Investigators said the young men gathered at the shop every day to
consume drugs. [@1] Imad Atef Badran, director of the northern sector, referred
the case to Emababa prosecution for investigation. [@1] The necessary legal
procedures were carried out before the referral. [@1]
