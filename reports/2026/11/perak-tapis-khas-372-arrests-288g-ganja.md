Perak police arrested 372 people, including a 14-year-old girl, in a three-day
statewide Op Tapis Khas operation that began on 23 November 2026. [@1] Perak
police chief Datuk Mohd Alwi Zainal Abidin said the simultaneous statewide
operation was run by the state's Narcotics Criminal Investigation Department
(JSJN) to curb drug distribution and abuse. [@1] The operation targeted places
identified as drug-addiction nests, including Felda and Felcra land schemes,
private plantations, PPRT housing projects, flats and fishing jetties. [@1] Of
the 372 held, 356 were men and 16 were women aged between 14 and 70. [@1]
Thirty-three of those arrested were foreign nationals from Thailand, Indonesia,
Myanmar and Vietnam. [@1] Police said 66 of those held were suspected of drug
distribution, 206 of drug possession, and 100 addicts were being investigated
under Section 15(1)(a) of the Dangerous Drugs Act 1952. [@1]

In the same operation police seized drugs they valued at RM49,648.30, including
288.61 grams of ganja, 251.95 grams of syabu (methamphetamine), 212.98 grams of
heroin and 17.74 grams of ketamine. [@1] Officers also seized 7.46 grams of
Erimin 5 pills, 2.3 kilograms of ketum leaves, 126.10 litres of ketum liquid and
0.18 litre of codeine. [@1] A vehicle and cash worth a total of RM4,700 were
also seized. [@2]

Three people classified as wanted persons were arrested during the operation.
[@1] Police identified two hardcore addicts for action under Section 39C of the
Dangerous Drugs Act 1952. [@1] The operation involved 248 police officers and
members, including 25 senior officers, from the Perak contingent's narcotics
department, district narcotics units, police stations and the marine police.
[@1]
