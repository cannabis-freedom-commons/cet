Deoria district in Uttar Pradesh shares its border with Siwan and Gopalganj
districts in Bihar. [@1] The Uttar Pradesh-Bihar border is becoming a
trafficking corridor, with several liquor, smack and ganja consignments caught
in Siwan and Gopalganj over the past three months. [@1] Police recovered 6.280
kilograms of ganja in the Guthni and Andar areas of Siwan district. [@1] The
account identifies no named suspects, arrests, charges or seizing unit
specifically tied to the ganja recovery. [@1]

Beyond the main roads, vehicles also move through rural link roads across the
border belt. [@1] The repeated recoveries have raised questions about
surveillance and monitoring along the border routes. [@1]

Bihar police and prohibition officers have found cars, pickups, motorcycles and
even boats used to move liquor through the same border belt. [@1] Several of
those liquor cases have confirmed liquor brought into Bihar from Uttar Pradesh.
[@1] Identifying the suppliers behind the repeated smack and liquor consignments
remains the key outstanding question after the arrests in the border belt. [@1]
