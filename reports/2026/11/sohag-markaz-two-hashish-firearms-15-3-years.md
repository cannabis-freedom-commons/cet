The Sohag Criminal Court sentenced two defendants, giving the first 15 years of
aggravated imprisonment and the second 3 years, after convicting them of
trafficking narcotics and possessing unlicensed firearms in the Sohag markaz.
[@1] The ruling was issued by a panel headed by Judge Osama Sabry El-Desouky
with judges Mahmoud Mohamed Berairy and Mostafa Mahmoud Farag. [@1]

The case dates to February 2026, when the two men — identified as B. N., 28, a
driver, and Z. R., 39, a labourer — were charged with possessing the narcotic
hashish with intent to traffic. [@2] The charges also included possessing an
unchambered Turkish-made shotgun and main parts of the same type of firearm
without a licence. [@1]

After the case was referred to the Sohag Criminal Court and its papers were
reviewed, the court sentenced the first defendant to 15 years of aggravated
imprisonment and the second to 3 years. [@2]
