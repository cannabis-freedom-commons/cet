The Comanche County Sheriff's Office announced a marijuana bust at an unlicensed
grow in the week before 25 November 2026. [@1] The sheriff's office's Special
Operations detectives and partner agencies carried out a search warrant at the
grow, at an undisclosed location in Comanche County. [@3] The grow was southwest
of Lake Ellsworth in Comanche County. [@4] Authorities seized around 3,000
illegal marijuana plants at the site. [@2]

Deputies found extensive trash covering the property and several containers of
illegal chemicals used to cultivate the plants. [@4] The sheriff's office said
the chemicals were suspected of originating from China. [@4] The Oklahoma
Department of Agriculture, Food and Forestry removed the chemicals and is
coordinating with the Department of Environmental Quality. [@4] Sheriff Michael
Merritt said officers found possible soil contamination and a dumping site at
the grow. [@2] Officials said the environmental impact of the chemicals was not
yet known. [@2]

Before the raid, detectives and agents from the attorney general's office, U.S.
Customs and Border Protection and U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement
staged at the Comanche Nation Spur Casino on U.S. 62. [@4] Comanche Nation
Chairman Forrest Tahdooahnippah said neither he, the tribal administrator nor
the Comanche Nation Police Department was notified. [@4] Attorney General
Gentner Drummond wrote to the chairman apologising for entering the tribe's land
without permission. [@4] The attorney general's office is reviewing evidence for
potential indictments against suspects. [@3]
