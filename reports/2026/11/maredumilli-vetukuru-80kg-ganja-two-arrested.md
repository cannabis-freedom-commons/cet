Maredumilli sub-inspector K. Srinivas and staff conducted vehicle checks at
Vetukuru Junction on the outskirts of Maredumilli. [@1] They detained two people
moving suspiciously, questioned and searched them, and found 80 kg of ganja in
their possession. [@1]

Maredumilli police seized the 80 kg of ganja. [@1] Police arrested the two
accused and seized from them an auto and a two-wheeler used for the transport
along with the ganja. [@1]

Police identified the accused as Ajay Kumar Sanya of Malkangiri district in
Odisha and Rajendra Harijan of Koraput district. [@1] They were then produced
before the Rampachodavaram court and sent to judicial remand. [@1]

Maredumilli circle inspector B. Narasimhamurthy is investigating the case. [@1]
Police said more accused connected with the illegal ganja transport still had to
be arrested. [@1]
