Around 18h30 on 17 November 2026, Gepar officers on patrol in Esperança saw a
16-year-old apparently handing something to another person. [@1] When he saw the
team approaching, the teenager ran into a residence and then jumped from the
balcony toward a wooded area, and officers searched but could not find him. [@1]

Military police seized crack stones, maconha portions and money on the afternoon
of Tuesday 17 November 2026 in a residence on Rua Mimosa in the Esperança
neighbourhood of Ipatinga. [@1] After the person responsible for the property
authorised entry, officers searched the bedroom used by the teenager and found a
bag holding six crack stones, three maconha portions and R$24. [@1] A Tático
Móvel dog team also took part and searched with a sniffer dog, but no other
illicit material was found. [@1]

Officers sent the drugs and money to the digital duty office of the 1st Regional
Civil Police station, and the teenager was not found but his details were
recorded in the occurrence closed by the military police. [@1]
