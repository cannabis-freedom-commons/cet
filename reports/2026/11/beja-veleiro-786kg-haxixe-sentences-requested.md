Prosecutors at the Beja court asked for prison sentences of six to twelve years
for two Spaniards accused of transporting 786 kg of hashish aboard a sailboat
seized by the GNR at the old mining port of Pomarão, Mértola, in October 2025.
[@1] The prosecutor told the court the case was a drug shipment from
international waters, from Algeciras in Spain, in a criminal operation in which
both defendants took a direct part. [@1] The two defendants are Francisco Rial,
56, of Villagarcia in Pontevedra, and António Contreras, 43, of Madrid; they
were detained on the night of 7 October 2025 inside the Polish-flagged sailboat.
[@2]

Rial's lawyer argued that Contreras had forced his client at sea to make the
journey for a promised 5,000 euros that was never paid. [@1] Contreras's lawyer
said he had been trapped when he travelled from Madrid to Pomarão to change the
boat's oil and filters. [@1] The prosecutor dismissed those accounts as
implausible explanations for the defendants' presence on the boat at the time of
detention. [@1] Both defence lawyers nonetheless joined in asking for the
acquittal of the two men. [@1]

The GNR seized the sailboat at Pomarão on 7 October 2025 with 786 kg of hashish,
which police said would make about 1.5 million doses. [@4] An investigating
judge at Beja had committed the two men for trial on drug-trafficking charges in
September 2026, rejecting defence challenges to the search of the boat and the
phone data. [@3]
