Officers signalled a grey Nissan Sentra with plate MPO890 to stop and
intercepted it metres ahead after the teenage driver allegedly tried to evade
the checkpoint. [@2] The driver, a 16-year-old native of Cali, was apprehended
and placed at the disposal of the Fiscalía in Anserma, Caldas, for alleged
trafficking, manufacture or possession of narcotics. [@2] A youth judge then
ordered his preventive internment at the Los Zagales comprehensive care and
education centre in Manizales. [@2] The decision is reported as a precautionary
measure during the proceedings, not a conviction. [@1] No final ruling on the
teenager's responsibility has been reported. [@1]

Officers found 414 black-plastic-wrapped packages containing plant material
similar to marijuana inside the car. [@2] Weighing set the load at 419.2 kg
gross and 402 kg net. [@1] Police estimated the marijuana's approximate value at
400 million pesos on the illegal market. [@1] Officers also seized the 2013 car,
valued at 35 million pesos, and a mobile phone valued at 200,000 pesos. [@1]

The interception happened on Friday 13 November 2026 at about 12:59 at kilometre
24 of the Cerritos–Cauyá road by the Acapulco service station, in Belalcázar
jurisdiction. [@2] The operation took place in Belalcázar, Caldas, during
registration and control work by Risaralda police units. [@1] The control was
staffed by officers assigned to the Viterbo road section, where the teenager was
driving the grey Nissan Sentra in private service. [@1] The reports do not
establish the shipment's origin, destination or owner, or whether others were
involved. [@1]
