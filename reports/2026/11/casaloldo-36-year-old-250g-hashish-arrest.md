A 36-year-old Moroccan man without a fixed address and in Italy illegally was
arrested by State Police officers from the Desenzano del Garda commissariat on
suspicion of possessing and dealing drugs. [@2] Officers had noticed a car known
to be used by a suspected dealer while investigating in Montichiari and followed
it to Solferino and then to Casaloldo in the Mantua province. [@1] In a car park
next to a bar, police watched a man hand the 36-year-old banknotes in exchange
for a package of cocaine and then stopped both men. [@1]

A search of the man and his car found 25 packets containing 80 grams of cocaine,
three blocks of hashish weighing about 250 grams, a precision scale, a tear-gas
canister, a mobile phone and cash believed to be dealing proceeds. [@1] The
buyer, who said he regularly bought drugs from the 36-year-old, was given an
administrative sanction for possessing drugs for personal use. [@1] The dealer
was arrested and placed at the disposal of the Public Prosecutor, and was also
sanctioned for driving without a licence, with the car impounded. [@1]

Brescia police chief Paolo Sartori issued an order for the man to leave Italian
territory, with a view to expelling him once he is released from custody. [@1]
Sartori said another dealer had been identified and arrested and further
quantities of cocaine and hashish ready for sale had been seized. [@2]
