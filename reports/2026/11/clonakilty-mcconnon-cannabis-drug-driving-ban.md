Thomas McConnon, aged 54, of Parkview, Clonakilty, was convicted at Clonakilty
District Court of drug-driving and driving without insurance. [@1] Gardaí
observed McConnon driving in the car park of Dunnes Stores, Clonakilty, in a
vehicle without tax or insurance. [@1] An oral fluid test returned positive
results for cannabis and cocaine. [@1] A sample taken at the station tested
positive for cannabis at a level above the legal driving limit. [@1]

The vehicle was seized and McConnon was taken to Clonakilty Garda Station. [@1]
McConnon had six previous convictions, most recently for failing to provide
information requested by a peace officer. [@1] He also had previous convictions
for drug possession and possession of drugs for sale or supply, but no previous
motoring convictions. [@1] McConnon said he had got rid of the car and did not
intend to drive again. [@1]

Judge Joanne Carroll convicted him of drug-driving and disqualified him from
driving for 12 months. [@1] He was placed on a probation bond for nine months.
[@1] McConnon was also convicted of driving without insurance and fined €250,
allowing four months to pay. [@1]
