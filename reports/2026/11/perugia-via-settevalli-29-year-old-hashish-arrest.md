Carabinieri from the operational section of the Perugia company, supported by
the Nucleo Radiomobile, arrested a 29-year-old man in flagranza on suspicion of
possessing drugs for sale. [@1] During the territorial control service in via
Settevalli, in Perugia, officers saw the man hand two doses of cocaine to
another man aged 34. [@1] The intervention took place in the afternoon of 16
November 2026, the day before the reports of 17 November. [@1]

Searches of the man's person, his vehicle and his home found 83 wrappers holding
a total of about 122 grams of cocaine, which is not counted in the cannabis
figures here. [@1] In the same searches officers seized two wrappers holding
about 8 grams of hashish. [@1] The cocaine was divided into 83 sachets, and the
officers also seized two precision scales, material for packing doses and 120
euros in cash. [@3]

The 29-year-old was declared under arrest and, on the order of the public
prosecutor in Perugia, taken to the Casa Circondariale di Perugia-Capanne at the
disposal of the judicial authority. [@1] The 34-year-old was not arrested but
reported to the Prefettura of Perugia for the formalities required for personal
use of drugs. [@2] The Carabinieri said the criminal proceedings are still at
the preliminary investigation stage and that the arrested man is presumed
innocent until any final conviction. [@1]
