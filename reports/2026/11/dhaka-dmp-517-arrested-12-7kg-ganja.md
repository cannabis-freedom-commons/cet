Dhaka Metropolitan Police arrested 517 people in regular drives across police
station areas of the capital on Thursday 26 November 2026. [@1] Fifty-one cases
were filed during the same drives. [@2] The DMP's media division announced the
figures in a statement. [@1]

Mirpur division recorded the most arrests with 146, followed by Wari with 80,
Tejgaon with 76, Motijheel with 57, Lalbagh with 47, Gulshan with 45, Uttara
with 41 and Ramna with 21. [@1] The Detective Branch arrested three people and
the SRU one. [@1]

Officers seized 12 kg 700 g of ganja during the drives. [@1] The same operations
recovered 9,158 yaba tablets, a bus, three bank cards, 11 mobile phones and Tk
12,965 in cash. [@1] One victim was rescued during the operation. [@1]

The DMP said legal action against those arrested was being processed. [@1]
