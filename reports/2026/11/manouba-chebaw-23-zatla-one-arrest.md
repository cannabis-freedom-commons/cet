The National Guard's research and inspection brigade in Ariana broke up a
network suspected of drug trafficking in an operation targeting Hay al-Ward in
Chebaw, Manouba Governorate. [@1]

Officers arrested one suspect in the operation. [@1] They seized about 23 slabs
of cannabis, known locally as zatla, which preliminary information said were
intended for sale in Manouba and Ariana. [@1]

After consulting the public prosecution, it ordered the suspect held while
research and investigations continue to identify other possible members and map
the network's extent. [@1]
