On Friday, OBN's Marijuana Enforcement Teams served search warrants in Skiatook.
[@1,2,4] Officials say the search warrants were served simultaneously at Black label
Labs/Organically Grown and Grassfire. [@1,2] Grassfire is at 301 W. 136th St. North. [@4] Black Label Labs/Organically Grown is at 679 W. 136th St. North. [@4] Arrests are pending. [@1,2,4] The operation involved assistance from the Osage County Sheriff's Office, Skiatook Police Department, Oklahoma Department of Agriculture, Oklahoma Medical Marijuana Authority, Osage County District 2 Commissioner Steve Talburt's office, and the State Fire Marshal's Office. [@4]

As a result, agents seized 5,490 marijuana plants and 265 pounds of processed
marijuana. [@1,2,3,4] OBN said its investigation is focusing on evidence of fraud and
straw ownership involving the two Skiatook properties. [@1,4] OBN said it is also
looking into business connections between the two Skiatook grow operations and
the processing facility in Sand Springs where two employees lost their lives.
[@1]

Agents with the Oklahoma Bureau of Narcotics say they are still investigating
marijuana grows as part of an ongoing investigation into a deadly explosion.
[@1] In August, officers with the Sand Springs Police Department responded to an
explosion at the Morrow Gill Business Complex. [@1] As emergency crews were
processing the scene, they found 28-year-old Stephen Scott deceased and
41-year-old Michael Newman injured. [@1] Newman was rushed to a local hospital
but passed away from his injuries. [@1] Investigators learned that both victims
were employees of the business, which was a marijuana processing facility. [@1]
Tulsa County District Attorney Steve Kunzweiler says the explosion was the
result of manufacturing controlled dangerous substances at "Solos Extracts."
[@1] The owner of the business, 35-year-old Ryan Bassham, was arrested and
charged with two counts of first-degree murder. [@1]

Several days later, agents with the Oklahoma Bureau of Narcotics executed a
search warrant on Bassham's property in Creek County. [@1] During the search,
OBN agents seized approximately 1,330 pounds of processed marijuana product,
approximately 140 pounds of marijuana batter, two firearms, and other evidence
of manufacturing outside the regulated market. [@1] Bassham was charged with
aggravated trafficking of marijuana, aggravated manufacturing of marijuana, and
the use of a firearm while in the commission of a felony. [@1] Although the
incident occurred months ago, OBN agents say they are still investigating and
serving search warrants on properties in connection to the explosion. [@1]
