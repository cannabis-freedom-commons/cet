On 25 November 2026, the Tirupati–Coimbatore Intercity Express reached Salem in
Tamil Nadu, one of the stops on its route between the two cities. [@1] Railway
police searched the train at Salem, including the unreserved coach used by
passengers without reserved seats. [@1] The check took place after the train
arrived at the station. [@1]

During the search, officers found a bag lying unclaimed in the unreserved coach.
[@1] The bag held 2 kg of ganja, a form of cannabis. [@1] The railway police
seized the drug from the bag. [@1]

The seized ganja was handed over to the Salem Railway Police for further action.
[@1] Salem Railway Police are investigating who carried the bag on the train and
left it in the coach. [@1] No one was arrested in the case, and no suspect was
named. [@1] No charge or court step was reported, and the case remained under
investigation. [@1]
