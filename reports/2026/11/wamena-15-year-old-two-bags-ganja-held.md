Satuan Samapta Polres Jayawijaya detained a 15-year-old carrying two bags
containing dried and fresh cannabis during raids at several points in Wamena,
Jayawijaya, Papua Pegunungan, on 18 November 2026. [@1] The teenager was taken
to Mapolres Jayawijaya for questioning and further investigation. [@1]

The patrol covered Jalan Bhayangkara, Jalan Trikora, Pertigaan Pelangi, Map
Lima, Pasar Wouma, Jalan Trans Kurima and Pertigaan Yagara. [@1] Deputy
Jayawijaya police chief Kompol Albertus Mabel said officers also seized sharp
weapons, five bottles of liquor and a motorcycle without vehicle documents. [@1]
He said routine patrols would continue to prevent narcotics circulation and keep
Wamena safe and conducive. [@1]

Jayawijaya customary council (LMA) chair Herman Doga welcomed the patrols and
urged residents to help police eradicate cannabis circulation, including
reporting planting in villages. [@1] Herman said cannabis was already being
planted in villages and urged anyone who sees it to report it to police so it
can be destroyed. [@1] He warned that unchecked narcotics would damage children,
increase crime and destabilise Jayawijaya, and called for joint action. [@1]
