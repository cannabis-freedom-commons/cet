Coordinated operations in Quintana Roo detained 254 people between 16 and 22
November 2026 for offences and antisocial conduct. [@1] Of those, 22 were held
for alleged health crimes, 24 for other common-law offences and one adolescent
for juvenile criminal conduct. [@1] Officers carried out 19,493 checks of people
and vehicles during the week. [@1] Felipe Alberto N was detained on 16 November
in Benito Juárez with 145 drug doses, a handgun, a magazine, 13 cartridges and a
vehicle. [@1] He was allegedly linked to an extortion group using the loan-shark
collection method known as gota a gota. [@1] On the same day, Abiut Azarel N and
Iris del Carmen N were detained with 172 drug doses and nine packets of
marijuana weighing about 4.5 kilograms. [@1] The two were allegedly linked to
placing narcomantas in Supermanzana 28 and Region 93 of Cancún. [@1]

In Othón P. Blanco, Francisco N was detained with 15 doses and Ángel Abraham N
with 24 doses and a motorcycle with a theft report. [@1] In Benito Juárez,
Rodrigo N, Francisco Antonio N and Martha Angélica N were detained with 153
doses, and Andrés N, Wilmer N and Fabián N with 113 doses of marijuana. [@1]
Officers seized four firearms, recovered 54 vehicles and confiscated 5,845 doses
of various illicit substances in the coordinated operations. [@1]

Luis Yovani N was detained for robbery without violence with 125 bank cards in
his possession. [@1] Jorge Alberto N was secured for disobedience and
resistance, with an arrest warrant for rape executed against him. [@1] The State
Preventive Police recovered three vehicles and 14 motorcycles, while the Cyber
Police handled 282 cybercrime complaints. [@1]
