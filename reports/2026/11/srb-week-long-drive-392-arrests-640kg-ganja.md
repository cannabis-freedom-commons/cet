Bangladesh's Special Response Battalion (SRB) arrested 392 accused in operations
in various parts of the country over the past week. [@1,2] SRB law and media wing
director Wing Commander M Z M Intekhab Chowdhury gave the figures at a briefing
on Sunday 15 November at the SRB media centre in Karwan Bazar in the capital.
[@1,2]

The 392 arrests comprised 5 in arms cases, 43 in murder cases, 20 in
women-and-children abuse and rape cases, 111 in drug cases, 5 in mugging
incidents, 17 in robbery incidents, 6 in human-trafficking cases, 2 accused in
sabotage incidents and 183 in other cases. [@1,2] Officers seized 8 firearms, 750
grams of heroin, 2,500 bottles of phensedyl, 640 kg of ganja, more than 36,000
yaba tablets and 155 litres of alcohol during the operations. [@1,2]

In a recent operation in the Rasulpur area under Bijaynagar police station in
Brahmanbaria district, SRB recovered 300 kg of ganja, and in two separate
operations in Khulna it arrested one accused with 3 firearms and 14 rounds of
ammunition. [@1,2] In Tangail, SRB also arrested accused fraudster Selim Khan from
a rented house with electronic devices used for fraud. [@1,2]
