A court in Uşak remanded 20 suspects in custody and sent them to prison after
police detained them in a series of drug operations. [@2] After completing
police procedures, officers referred all 20 to the courthouse, where a judge
ordered their detention. [@3]

The governor's office said the operations ran from 1 to 15 November 2026 and
targeted identified addresses across the city under the offence of manufacturing
and trafficking narcotic or stimulant substances. [@3] Searches turned up 50
grams (0.05 kg) of esrar, the everyday Turkish term for cannabis, alongside
36,653 synthetic drug doses, 70 grams of methamphetamine, 23,156 doses of
synthetic bonzai in 59 pieces and 52 grams of cocaine. [@1] Officers also seized
three precision scales and 138,300 Turkish lira that they assessed as proceeds
of crime. [@3]

The operations were carried out by the narcotics branch of the Uşak provincial
police directorate, the governor's office said. [@1]
