Military Police teams responded in Vila Bela, Guarapuava, on the night of Sunday
15 November 2026 after a case involving threats against women, a firearm and
drugs. [@1] The response came at about 20h55 after police received information
that an armed man was threatening women in a public street in front of a
residence. [@1] Shock and dog-operation teams went to the address to check the
report and protect the possible victims. [@2] At the scene officers identified a
33-year-old man who, according to the police report, showed apparent signs of
drunkenness and very aggressive behaviour and resisted the approach. [@1] His
30-year-old wife and 52-year-old mother-in-law told officers that he had
threatened women and had a firearm. [@2]

During searches in the property officers found a Puma .38 carbine near the
residence wall. [@1] The teams also found about 251 grams of a substance
analogous to maconha, plus a precision scale that could be linked to weighing
drugs. [@1] Police arrested the man and took him with the seized materials to
the 14th Police Subdivision in Guarapuava for the applicable procedures. [@2]

The operation was handled by the 16th Military Police Battalion with support
from the specialist teams. [@1]
