A 31-year-old Pole was arrested and taken to Salzburg prison when Austrian
police broke up a drug network in the Pongau region. [@1] The main suspect, a
25-year-old Spaniard, was reported on free foot along with a 27-year-old
Spaniard and a 26-year-old Romanian on a prosecutor's order. [@1] Police said
the 26-year-old Pole was also reported on free foot, while the 31-year-old was
detained on the prosecutor's order. [@2]

The 25-year-old is suspected of dealing between January 2022 and January 2026,
using two instant-messaging services to supply at least 43 buyers. [@2] Police
listed cannabis herb and hashish among the substances, alongside cocaine,
amphetamines and LSD. [@1] The 27-year-old Spaniard and the Romanian woman are
suspected of receiving and storing parcels sent from Spain. [@2] The main
suspect obtained substances by post or by collecting them himself in Spain and
also from dealers in Austria. [@4]

The two Polish men are suspected of importing narcotics and psychoactive
substances from Poland and selling them for profit to numerous buyers in
Austria. [@1] Police described the 25-year-old as cooperative. [@3] Police could
not put a figure on his alleged profit when SALZBURG24 asked on Sunday. [@1]
