Just before midnight on Wednesday 11 November 2026, the 24-year-old driver lost
control of a Chevrolet on ul. Gdańskiej in Zabrze and hit a tree. [@1] Police
found that the driver was subject to an active court ban on driving motor
vehicles. [@2] The 24-year-old driver was detained. [@2]

Officers found marijuana in the Chevrolet on ul. Gdańskiej in Zabrze. [@1] The
25-year-old passenger of the Chevrolet also had drugs on him. [@1] Police fined
the 24-year-old 2,500 złoty for causing the collision. [@2] He will also face
liability for breaching the court-ordered driving ban and for possession of
narcotics. [@2]

Officers took blood from the driver for testing to establish whether he was
under the influence of drugs at the time of the crash. [@2] Breaching a
court-ordered driving ban carries three months to five years in prison under
Article 244 of the Criminal Code. [@1]
