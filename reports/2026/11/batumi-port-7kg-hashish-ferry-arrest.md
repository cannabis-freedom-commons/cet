Officers of Georgia's Interior Ministry and of the customs department of the
Finance Ministry's Revenue Service detained a foreign citizen. [@2] He is
accused of attempting to import, acquire and possess a particularly large
quantity of narcotic drugs. [@2] The Revenue Service said the hashish was found
at the Port Batumi customs checkpoint during checks of vehicles that had arrived
on a ferry from Bulgaria. [@2]

A detailed inspection of the car, registered in the accused man's name, found 7
kg of hashish. [@1] The hashish was divided into 41 packages and hidden in a
specially built stash. [@2] The hashish was seized as material evidence. [@1]

Investigators are working under Articles 19-260 and 19-262 of Georgia's Criminal
Code. [@1] The charge carries up to 20 years in prison or life imprisonment.
