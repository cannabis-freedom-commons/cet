A 36-year-old woman and nine men were arrested in Juaben in Ghana's Ashanti
Region during an unannounced police operation. [@1] The swoop took place on 17
November 2026 in Duampompo, Boama Odumase and neighbouring communities. [@1] The
woman was identified as Yaa Afrakoma; among the male suspects were Darko Asante
Hayford, 25, Prosper Gamajon, 30, Samuel Agyekum, 34 and Ben Klokye, 32. [@1]
The other men were Larbi Daniel, 26, Mohammed Abubakar, 43, Kofi Sampson, 25,
Kwame Sefa, 38 and Samuel Tweneboa, 22. [@1]

The Juaben District Police Command carried out the operation in collaboration
with the Formed Police Unit (FPU) to target known narcotics hideouts, according
to the Juaben District Police Sitrep. [@1] The exercise ran from 5:00 p.m. to
8:00 p.m. [@1] Police said substances suspected to be narcotic drugs were
retrieved and retained during the operation. [@1] The Daily Guide account said
the operation was intended to stop the sale and usage of narcotics in those
communities. [@2]

Police said the suspects were detained to assist with investigations and would
undergo screening and profiling to establish their respective levels of
involvement. [@1] Those found culpable would be put before the court. [@1] Ghana
Guardian reported that the suspects remained in custody while investigations
continued. [@3]
