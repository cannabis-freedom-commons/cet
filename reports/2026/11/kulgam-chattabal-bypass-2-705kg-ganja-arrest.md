Kulgam police arrested Sajad Ahmad Wagay of Goripora, Kulgam, at a checkpoint on
the Chattabal bypass in Kulgam district. [@2] Police announced the arrest in a
statement dated 22 November 2026. [@2] Officers stopped him during a routine
naka, a checkpoint check, after he drew suspicion. [@1] A search found 2.705 kg
of a cannabis-like substance, ganja, in his possession. [@2]

Police described him as a drug peddler. [@2] A case was registered at the Kulgam
police station under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act,
India's main drug law. [@1] Police said the investigation would establish where
the ganja came from and who else was connected to the trade. [@1]

Police said the campaign against drug trafficking would continue. [@1] Police
asked the public to share information about the drug trade to help make society
drug-free. [@1] The Jagran report described the Kulgam stop as part of a wider
police push against drug traffickers in Jammu and Kashmir. [@1]
