Police in Velrampet, Puducherry, stopped four men standing near a private higher
secondary school on the lake road and found 244 grams of ganja on them. [@1]
Mudaliarpet Sub-Inspector Kathiresan led the patrol in the Velrampet area on the
morning of 24 November 2026. [@2] The four young men by the school tried to run
when they saw the officers and were caught and questioned. [@2]

Police said the men were holding the ganja to sell it to school students. [@1]
The men told investigators they had stocked it to sell to young people and
school students in the area for money. [@2] The four were Vignesh, 21, of
Vanarappetai; Charan, 22, of Rodiyarpet; Praveenkumar, 24, of Attuppatti; and
Varadaraj, 20, of Vanarappetai. [@1]

Police seized the ganja, arrested all four and sent them to jail. [@2]
