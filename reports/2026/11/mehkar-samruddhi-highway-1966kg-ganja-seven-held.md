Mehkar police detained seven people, including a woman from Kothrud in Pune,
over a racket selling ganja illegally. [@1] Officers stopped an Ertiga car under
the Samruddhi Mahamarg bridge on the Mehkar-Janephal road near Gotāḷā village
and found 19 kilograms 660 grams of ganja in two suitcases in its boot. [@1]
Police seized the ganja, the car with registration number MH 28 CC 9884, mobile
phones and other material, property valued at 19 lakh 13 thousand rupees. [@1]

The trap followed confidential information that people would come to the Mehkar,
Janephal and Dongaon area in a white Maruti Suzuki Ertiga car to sell ganja.
[@1]

A police sub-inspector filed the complaint, and Mehkar police station registered
a case under sections 8(c), 20(b)(c) and 22 of the Narcotic Drugs and
Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985. [@1] Investigators are establishing whom the
ganja smugglers were dealing with and where the consignment was headed, and said
a larger selling racket in Mehkar taluka was likely to emerge. [@1]
