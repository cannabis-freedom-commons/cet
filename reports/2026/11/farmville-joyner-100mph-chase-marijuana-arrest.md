Farmville Police Department officers arrested 22-year-old Kendrick Joyner in
Farmville on Saturday, 14 November 2026. [@1] Warrants allege Joyner fled a
Farmville officer at more than 100 mph in a 55 mph zone and was involved in a
crash causing more than $1,000 in property damage. [@1] The warrants also allege
he drove left of center on Highway 258 South, failed to stop at a stop sign and
failed to use his turn signal. [@1] Over the chase he faces one felony count of
fleeing or eluding arrest with a motor vehicle, one count of reckless driving to
endanger and multiple traffic violations. [@1]

At the same arrest he was also held on a separate case of multiple drug-related
charges and breaking into a home, which warrants date to May 2025. [@1] In that
case documents allege he possessed under an ounce of marijuana divided into
eight individual packages and kept a home on West Moore Street for storing and
selling marijuana. [@1] Documents allege he broke into the same West Moore
Street home with the intent to commit a felony or larceny. [@1] The listed drug
charges include felony possession with intent to manufacture, sell or deliver,
felony possession, maintaining a dwelling for controlled substances, two
conspiracy counts, breaking or entering, possession of up to half an ounce and
paraphernalia possession. [@2]

He was taken to the Pitt County Detention Center on secured bonds of $300,000 in
the May 2025 case and $75,000 in the fleeing case, totaling $375,000. [@1] He
was scheduled to appear in Pitt County District Court on Monday, 16 November at
2 p.m. [@1]
