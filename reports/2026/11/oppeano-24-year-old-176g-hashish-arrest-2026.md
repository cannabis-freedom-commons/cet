Carabinieri of Oppeano arrested a 24-year-old man from Ronco all'Adige, already
known to the police, on the afternoon of Saturday 21 November 2026. [@1] He was
held on suspicion of possessing narcotic substances for the purpose of dealing.
[@1] The patrol stopped his car after a report of a suspicious vehicle, and the
driver's nervousness and a strong smell of drugs from the cabin led officers to
search it. [@1] The stop took place at Cologna Veneta, and the Oppeano station
is part of the Legnago carabinieri company. [@3]

Searches of the car and then his home found 176 grams of hashish, divided into
five packages and seven individual doses. [@1] Officers also found a cutting
tool and material for packaging and selling drugs, and seized everything found.
[@1]

After formalities at the Oppeano station the man was placed under house arrest
at his home. [@2] On the morning of 23 November he appeared before a judge of
the Verona tribunal, who validated the arrest and, after the defence lawyer
requested time to prepare, adjourned the trial to March 2027. [@2]
