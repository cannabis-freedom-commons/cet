A 21-year-old man was captured in Tarqui, Huila, while transporting
approximately 6.5 kilograms of marijuana. [@1] National Police officers detained
him during registration and control activities at kilometre one of the road
linking Tarqui with the municipality of El Pital. [@1]

During the search, officers found a red bag containing a package wrapped in a
black hermetic bag. [@1] Inside was a vegetable substance with characteristics
similar to marijuana, weighing approximately 6.5 kilograms. [@1] A check of the
motorcycle showed a theft complaint in the municipality of El Tambo, Cauca, with
an active criminal notice. [@1]

The detention was linked to the alleged offences of trafficking, manufacture or
possession of narcotics and receiving stolen goods. [@1] The detained man was
placed at the disposal of Local Prosecutor 25 of Garzón, before whom he must
answer for the acts under investigation. [@1]
