Police in St. Petersburg and Leningrad Oblast detained a criminal group
suspected of illegally selling marijuana on Wednesday 18 November 2026. [@1,7]
Those detained were a 36-year-old supplier and two sellers aged 34 and 32, both
previously convicted. [@1,4,6,7] The operation was part of a criminal case opened in
October 2026. [@1]

In the supplier's flat officers found 203 grams of marijuana, electronic scales
and packaging material. [@1,4,7] At a second address used to cultivate the
drug-containing plant, officers found a further 191 grams of marijuana and
growing equipment. [@1,4,7] In the sellers' flats officers seized a further 126
grams of marijuana, scales and packaging materials, the regional MVD press
service said. [@1,4,7] The regional MVD press service put the combined quantity at
more than 500 grams. [@2,5] Fontanka reported that investigators from the MVD's
criminal investigation unit and Vasileostrovsky district colleagues made the
arrests, that the suspects tried to flush the drugs down a toilet and that
operational footage was shown at police headquarters on 19 November. [@3] In the operational footage the three men confessed to storing and selling marijuana, with bushes of plants and a toilet clogged with grass shown. [@6]
Fontanka reported a greenhouse at the second address used by the supplier. [@3]

KP.RU reported that cases for attempted sale were opened against two of the
suspects. [@2,4] Life.ru reported that the legal basis was article 228.1 of the Criminal Code, covering attempted sale in large and especially large quantities, and that all three men were held under article 91 of the Criminal Procedure Code. [@7] Police said they were conducting measures to identify further
episodes of criminal activity and possible accomplices. [@1,7]
