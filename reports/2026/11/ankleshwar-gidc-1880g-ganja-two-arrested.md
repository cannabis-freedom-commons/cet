Police in Bharuch district arrested two men and seized 1.880 kilograms of ganja,
or cannabis, in a raid in Ankleshwar GIDC. [@1] The raid was carried out by the
Anti-Narcotics Wing of the Bharuch district police. [@1] Police named the two as
Raju Ganesh Gamit and Sachin Pannalal Gupta and said they kept the ganja for
sale for financial gain. [@1] The ganja was found near a paan stall by the
railway crossing in Rachna Nagar. [@1]

Police valued the seized ganja at 94,000 rupees. [@1] Officers also seized a
Bullet motorcycle worth 1 lakh rupees, two mobile phones, a digital weighing
scale and other material. [@1] Police put the total value of the goods seized at
2.15 lakh rupees. [@1]

A case was registered at the Ankleshwar GIDC police station under various
sections of the NDPS Act, India's drug law. [@1] Police said they were
investigating where the ganja came from and who else was part of the sale
network. [@1] The inquiry also brought out the name of another person living at
Bhadkodra. [@1]
