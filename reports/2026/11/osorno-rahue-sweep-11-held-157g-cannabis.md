Carabineros de Osorno deployed across different points of Rahue on 18 November
2026 to reinforce police presence and prevent crime. [@1] The service deployed
60 police officers. [@1] Those officers carried out focused patrols, inspections
and preventive controls that took people wanted by the courts off the streets
and detected crimes caught in flagrante. [@2] The action also sought to increase
neighbours' sense of security in the sector. [@1] Eleven people were detained
during the deployment, which included more than 700 preventive controls. [@3]

Officers carried out more than 700 inspections through vehicle and identity
checks. [@2] Officers issued 22 traffic tickets and seized 157 grams of cannabis
sativa. [@1] A second account described the seized drug as 157 grams of
marijuana. [@3]

Carabineros said these operations would keep being repeated dynamically in
different sectors of Osorno to protect safety and reassure residents. [@2]
