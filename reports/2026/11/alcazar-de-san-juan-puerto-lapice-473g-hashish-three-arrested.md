Guardia Civil officers detained three men in an operation against drug
trafficking in the Alcázar de San Juan area. [@1] The detentions were made in the towns of Tomelloso and Puerto Lápice, in the province of Ciudad Real. [@2,3] The detentions came during
several operations in the first fortnight of November under a police plan
against retail drug trafficking and consumption. [@1,3] Officers first approached
a suspicious vehicle near a fuel service station in Puerto Lápice to identify
the people inside. [@1,2,3] The vehicle fled, joined the A-4 slip road and white
objects were thrown into the ditch during the chase. [@1]

Officers identified the occupants kilometres further on, returned to the spot
where the objects had been thrown and found three white packages containing a
brown substance. [@1] The substance was identified as hashish and weighed 473
grams. [@1,3] In the operation that ended with the three detentions, officers also seized 21 grams of cocaine, 2.82 grams of marijuana and 400 euros in cash. [@2] The Guardia Civil listed the seized items as 508 grams of hashish, 21 grams of cocaine, 82 grams of marijuana and 400 euros in cash. [@3] After collecting the seized substance, officers found one of the
previously identified people at the scene when he came to collect a second
vehicle linked to the incident. [@1] That man was detained as a perpetrator for
a serious crime against public health. [@1] A third man was detained at a vehicle checkpoint after officers found 19 grams of cocaine in a plastic ball in his trouser pocket and 2.82 grams of marijuana beside the handbrake. [@3] A second man was detained in Tomelloso after a customer at a leisure venue threw 2 grams of cocaine and 35 grams of hashish to the floor and was found carrying 360 euros in cash. [@3] The detainees and the case file were sent to the courts of Alcázar de San Juan and Tomelloso. [@2,3]

The service station where officers first acted is in the town of Puerto Lápice.
[@1] The fleeing vehicle joined the slip road of the A-4 motorway. [@1]
