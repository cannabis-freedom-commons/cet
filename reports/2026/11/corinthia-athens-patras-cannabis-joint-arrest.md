A 21-year-old Greek national was arrested on the evening of 20 November 2026 by
officers of the A3 Crime Prevention and Suppression Group (OPKE) of Corinthia.
[@1] The arrest was made on the New National Road Athens–Patras, known by its
Greek abbreviation N.E.O., in Corinthia. [@2] Officers found a cannabis joint in
the man's possession and seized it. [@3]

OPKE is the Greek police's Crime Prevention and Suppression Group, and its A3
team serves Corinthia. [@1] The preliminary investigation is being conducted by
the Sicyon Police Station. [@2]
