Police in Privolzhsky District detained a 45-year-old local woman suspected of
unlawfully storing narcotic substances. [@1] The woman told officers that she
had found wild cannabis on the outskirts of a village, picked it and dried the
drug-containing plants for personal use. [@1]

Investigative actions confirmed her account. [@1] During the preliminary
investigation, inquiry officers ordered a number of comprehensive expert
examinations, collected character material and identified and questioned
witnesses. [@1] The inquiry group of the Russian Interior Ministry department
for Privolzhsky District opened a criminal case against her under part 1 of
article 228 of the Criminal Code for unlawful storage of narcotic drugs,
investigated it and sent it to court with an approved indictment. [@1]

The Privolzhsky District Court found the evidence collected by police sufficient
for a guilty verdict against the local woman. [@1] Taking all the circumstances
into account, the court imposed a fine of 15,000 roubles on her, and the verdict
has entered into legal force. [@1]
