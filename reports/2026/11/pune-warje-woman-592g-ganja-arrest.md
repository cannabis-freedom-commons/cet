Crime Branch Unit 3 of Pune city police arrested a 55-year-old woman for selling
ganja in Warje, Pune. [@1] The woman was identified as Sunita Sikandar Bhat, 55,
a resident of Amar Bharat Society in Warje. [@1] Officers seized 592 grams of
ganja from her, which police valued at about 30,000 rupees. [@1]

A Crime Branch Unit 3 team was patrolling the Warje and Nanded City areas when
Sub-Inspector Pandharinath Shinde and constable Tushar Kindre received
information that a woman was selling ganja in the Amar Bharat Society area. [@1]
The team laid a trap and took Bhat into custody. [@1] A case was registered
against her at Warje Malwadi police station under the Narcotic Drugs and
Psychotropic Substances Act. [@1] Two earlier cases of drug sale had also been
registered against her. [@1]

The operation was carried out under the guidance of Additional Commissioner of
Police Tejaswi Satpute, Deputy Commissioner Gauhar Hasan and Assistant
Commissioner Shailesh Sankhe. [@1] The team also included Senior Inspector
Prashant Annachatre and Sub-Inspectors Santosh Bhandwalkar and Pandharinath
Shinde. [@1]
