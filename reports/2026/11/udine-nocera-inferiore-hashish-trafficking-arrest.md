A 41-year-old man from Nocera Inferiore, identified only as G.C., was jailed in
Udine under a custodial order issued by the judge for preliminary investigations
(gip) of the Udine tribunal in a drug-trafficking investigation. [@1] Three
custodial orders to prison were issued by the Gip of Udine in the Guardia di
Finanza investigation, which dates to 2025. [@4] The other two people jailed
were a Neapolitan man and an Afghan citizen. [@4] A total of 11 people are under
investigation in the case. [@4] The judge described G.C. as a depositor, courier
and distributor who continued the activity after the earlier arrests. [@4] Part
of the drugs was kept at G.C.'s home, and he was already known to the
authorities for prior offences. [@4]

The Guardia di Finanza investigation reconstructed a buying, selling and
distribution network for cocaine and hashish, with significant quantities moved.
[@3] Investigators documented about four kilograms of hashish and other drug
batches, including four deliveries of ten kilograms of hashish each. [@1] G.C.
took part in at least four hashish deliveries of ten kilograms each, handling
the storage and transfer of the drugs. [@4] Those operations allegedly took
place between November and December 2025. [@4] A separate charge concerns
possession of one kilogram of hashish. [@4] Prosecutors estimated the
four-kilogram batch could yield more than 3,000 individual doses, enough to meet
one day's demand in the reference market. [@4] Another charge relates to about
one kilogram of cocaine that G.C. allegedly handled with three others in 2025;
that weight is excluded from the cannabis figures. [@4]

Officers used phone taps, surveillance, tracking and video systems, and
statements from a former drug courier to reconstruct the network. [@1] The
investigation continues to clarify the positions of the 11 suspects and any
further responsibility. [@2] G.C., defended by lawyer Francesco Vicidomini,
faces four charges of possessing and transferring drugs. [@4] The custodial
order covers three suspects, and G.C. may appeal it to the Tribunale del
Riesame. [@4] The custody measure is not a conviction, and all suspects are
presumed innocent until a final judgment. [@4]
