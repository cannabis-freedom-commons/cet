Officers from Gualeguaychú's Comisaría Tercera went to a house at 9 de Julio and
Clavarino in the east of the city at about 09:20 on Saturday 21 November 2026
after a call about a dispute between a couple. [@1] A 34-year-old man at the
house told the officers that his former partner was causing a disturbance and
asked that she leave the property. [@1] With the owner's permission, female
police officers entered the home to ask the woman to leave, and the Gender
Prosecutor's Office ordered that she take her belongings with her. [@2]

Inside the property the officers saw six cannabis sativa plants. [@1] The
prosecutor's office on duty was informed and ordered the plants seized, and the
División Drogas Peligrosas, the city's drug police unit, carried out field tests
on them. [@1] The tests came back positive for marijuana and the plants weighed
more than 3 kg. [@2] In total the police removed six cannabis plants from the
property in the eastern part of Gualeguaychú. [@1]
