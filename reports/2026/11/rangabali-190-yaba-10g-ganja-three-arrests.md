Rangabali Thana police arrested three men in a special operation at Dakshin
Kazikanda (Majhir Hawla) in Ward 3 of Maudubi Union, Rangabali in Patuakhali
district. [@1] The arrested men are 40-year-old Md. Musa Hawlader of Dakshin
Kazikanda, 54-year-old Md. Manik Gazi of Pakkhia in Galachipa who was living in
Dakshin Kazikanda, and 20-year-old Md. Zahid Hang of the same area. [@1]

The raid took place at about 1:30 a.m. on the night of Friday 13 November 2026
at that Kazikanda locality. [@1] An operational team of Rangabali Thana police
carried out the special raid on the instructions of the Deputy Inspector General
of Barishal Range and the Superintendent of Police of Patuakhali. [@1]

Officers recovered 190 yaba tablets and 10 grams of ganja from the possession of
the three men. [@1] Police said the operation was conducted as part of an
ongoing drive to eliminate drugs. [@1] A case was then filed against the three
men under Table 10(a) of Section 36(1) of the Narcotics Control Act, 2018. [@1]
