Sergio Manuel Frías, known as “El Churri”, and Juan Elías Torres Anés were
detained and charged with belonging to an organisation dedicated to drug
dealing. [@2] Prosecutors Nicolás Ayestarán and Pablo Pereira told the court
that both men had taken part in a structure dedicated to selling drugs. [@2]
Investigators allege the organisation involved more than three people, so the
charge is aggravated drug dealing. [@1]

In a raid in Valle Grande investigators seized 350 grams of cocaine. [@1] In
another house search officers found 331,000 pesos in cash, 23 grams of cocaine,
five wraps of that substance, 12 marijuana cigarettes and another 4.8 grams of
cannabis. [@1] Officers also seized two precision scales, nylon cuttings, a grey
Volkswagen Vento car, documents for a motorcycle and four mobile phones already
authorised for forensic examination. [@1]

Judge Eliana Ratta converted both detentions into 41 days of preventive prison
ending on 28 December 2026. [@1] She ordered both men transferred to the
Provincial Penitentiary Service. [@1] She also ordered the bank accounts linked
to both defendants’ tax IDs blocked and set 20 April 2027 as the deadline for
the preparatory criminal investigation. [@1]
