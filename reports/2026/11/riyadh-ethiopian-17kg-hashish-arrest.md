Saudi Arabia's General Directorate of Narcotics Control, the country's dedicated
anti-drug agency, arrested an Ethiopian resident in the Riyadh region for
distributing 17 kilograms of hashish, a cannabis resin. [@1] The man was
detained and administrative measures were taken against him. [@1] He was then
referred to the Public Prosecution. [@1]

The security authorities urged citizens and residents to report any information
about drug smuggling or distribution. [@1] They gave 911 for Mecca, Medina,
Riyadh and the Eastern Province, 999 or 994 elsewhere in the kingdom, and the
directorate's own 995 line and email address. [@1] All reports would be handled
in strict confidence, with no liability for the caller. [@1]
