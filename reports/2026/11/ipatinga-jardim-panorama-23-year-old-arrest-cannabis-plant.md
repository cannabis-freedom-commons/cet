Military Police arrested a 23-year-old man in flagrante for suspected drug
trafficking during an operation in Ipatinga on the afternoon of Sunday 15
November 2026. [@1,3] Police said intelligence indicated the suspect coordinated
drug trafficking in the city centre and advertised sales through social networks
with images of firearms, drugs and money. [@2] The Tactical Command planned the
operation with Tatico Movel, Gepmor and sniffer-dog teams. [@1] Officers located
and stopped the suspect on Rua Serra do Mar in Jardim Panorama and found one
portion of marijuana on him. [@1,3]

Officers then went to his home in Limoeiro, entered with authorisation and found
one cannabis plant, another portion of marijuana and a packet of seeds. [@1,3] At
a commercial establishment in the central area, identified as a possible drug
storage and sale point, officers found crack and cocaine portions. [@1] With
sniffer-dog support, officers seized 20 cocaine wraps, seven crack portions and
two packets of marijuana, plus two precision scales, two intact .32-calibre
cartridges, one spent case and R$806. [@1,3] The man was arrested in flagrante and
taken with all the seized material to the duty office of the 1st Regional Civil
Police station. [@1]
