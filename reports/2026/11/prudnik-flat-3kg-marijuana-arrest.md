Criminal officers of the Prudnik County Police tracked down and detained Dawid
S. from Prudnik using operational methods. [@1] The flat occupant had previously
been under operational surveillance by the police. [@1] At the prosecutor's
office the man was charged with possessing a significant quantity of drugs,
which carries up to 10 years' imprisonment. [@1] He was placed in temporary
arrest for three months, senior constable Monika Łyko of the Prudnik County
Police said. [@1]

In his flat investigators found 3 kilograms of marijuana. [@1] Prudnik police
found marijuana worth 75,000 zloty in a flat in the town. [@1] Police said the
operation kept several thousand portions of drugs worth about 75,000 zloty off
the market. [@1]

Prudnik and the surrounding villages have just over 20,000 inhabitants and are
considered one of the calmer parts of the Opole region. [@1] The article
describes the quantity of drugs being intercepted in the area as alarming,
adding that some still reach buyers. [@1]
