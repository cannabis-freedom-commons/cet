Medan city police's narcotics unit held 1,762 people on drug offences between
January and November 2026. [@1] The detentions came from 1,437 narcotics cases
uncovered in Medan and the surrounding area. [@1]

Officers seized 167 kg of ganja, the Indonesian word for cannabis, in those
cases. [@1] The same haul included 285 kg of sabu, or crystal methamphetamine,
93,000 ecstasy pills, 22,000 happy-five pills and 6,500 vape cartridges, none of
which is counted as cannabis. [@1]
