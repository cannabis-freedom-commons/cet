Municipal officers of the Dirección de Seguridad Pública Municipal seized a
cardboard parcel during a preventive inspection at a parcel company in Torreón.
[@1] The find was recorded at about 11:46 on the previous Wednesday at premises
on bulevar Rodríguez Triana in the Pequeña Zona Industrial of Torreón. [@1] The
Unidad Canina K-9 was conducting surveillance rounds and a preventive review in
the warehouses with support from Noah, a dog specialised in narcotics detection.
[@1] The dog gave a positive marking on a cardboard parcel, leading officers to
open it to determine what substance it carried. [@1] No detentions linked to the
seizure were reported, and no sender or recipient details were released. [@1]

Inside they found a heat-sealed transparent plastic bag holding a white granular
substance resembling crystal, weighing about one ounce. [@1] In the same parcel
they found two black plastic jars labelled “God’s Beard Cult” containing gummies
made with THC extract, the psychoactive compound associated with marijuana. [@1]
Police secured the items to prevent the substances continuing in transit or
reaching their destination. [@1]

The parcel and its contents were placed at the disposal of the Public Ministry
of the Strategic Operations Center, which will establish the shipment's origin
and destination. [@1] The find occurred during preventive checks the municipal
force schedules at courier and parcel firms using specialist canine teams. [@1]
