A woman from Tunkinsky District, Buryatia, was convicted on three episodes of
illegally acquiring and storing a large amount of narcotic drugs. [@1] A second
account reported the same four-year term, suspended with a three-year probation
period. [@2] The court found her guilty on all three episodes, imposed a
four-year prison term and then suspended it after she confessed and showed
remorse. [@1]

According to the court, she gathered wild cannabis three times inside the
enclosure of an abandoned house, carried the raw material home and stored it in
the predbannik, the entry room of a bathhouse. [@1] The seized cannabis weighed
more than 1.3 kilograms, and she had used part of the herb herself. [@1] Police
stopped the activity in September 2026 and seized all the stores and implements.
[@1] An earlier police account identified the woman as 47 years old and said
officers found several plastic bags of plant material in the vestibule of her
private house. [@3] An examination identified the seized material as a narcotic
drug. [@3] The woman told police she had collected wild cannabis in a field near
the settlement for personal use, not for sale. [@3] That earlier police report
put the dried weight of the cannabis at more than one kilogram. [@3]

The court ordered her to register with the criminal-execution inspectorate,
undergo registration, notify authorities before changing residence and consult a
narcologist, with treatment if needed. [@1] The judgment had not yet entered
into legal force. [@1]
