Police Scotland seized a cannabis cultivation with an estimated street value of
over £335,000 at a property in Dunfermline. [@2] Officers made the recovery at
around 10.40am on Monday, 23 November after receiving a report of a possible
cultivation in the Canmore Street area. [@3] A man was found injured and taken
to hospital, where he remained, and police said enquiries were ongoing. [@4]

Detective Inspector David Nairn said the recovery underlined the importance of
information from the public in disrupting the sale and supply of illegal drugs,
which cause harm and misery in communities. [@1] Nairn said it also highlighted
Police Scotland's commitment to the Serious and Organised Crime Taskforce and
its national strategy. [@2] He urged anyone with concerns or information about
drug activity or serious organised crime to contact police on 101 or
Crimestoppers, where anonymity can be maintained. [@2]
