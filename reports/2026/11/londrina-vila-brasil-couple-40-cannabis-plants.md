Civil Police of Paraná (PCPR) arrested a 40-year-old man and a 35-year-old woman
in flagrante for drug trafficking. [@2] The operation took place on the
afternoon of Thursday 26 November in Vila Brasil, in the north of Londrina. [@2]
The couple was taken to the police station and formally recorded for drug
trafficking in the forms of storing drugs and unauthorised cultivation of plants
intended for preparing drugs. [@2] Officers took the couple to Londrina's
Central de Flagrantes, where the occurrence was presented to the police
authority for the applicable procedures. [@1]

Inside the residence the officers found greenhouses and structures adapted for
cultivation. [@2] In total they seized 33 cannabis plants and seedlings at
different stages of growth, seven dried plants, 15 grams of pressed marijuana
and various seeds. [@2] CBN Londrina reported two greenhouses and improvised
equipment inside the house. [@4] The growing structure was not seized because
some equipment was improvised and other pieces were considered too fragile to
transport. [@4]

The investigation began after a tip to the Disque-Denúncia 181 hotline reported
a cannabis plantation in a residence. [@3] Police also found an Instagram
profile with nearly two thousand followers posting about planting and handling
cannabis and its derivatives. [@4] Delegate Magno Roberto Miranda said the team
monitored the property, confirmed plants were present and made the arrest in
flagrante. [@2] The investigation also followed monitoring of an Instagram page
where the suspects displayed cannabis cultivation and drug production. [@1]
Police also received denunciations pointing to the identity of the person
responsible for the profile. [@1] Paiquerê FM reported that the pair had no
legal authorisation to cultivate the plant and were recorded in flagrante for
drug trafficking. [@5]
