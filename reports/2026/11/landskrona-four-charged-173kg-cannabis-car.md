Three people have been charged with gross drug offences after police found
172.68 kilograms of cannabis in a car in Landskrona in June 2026. [@1]

One of the three is also charged, together with a fourth person, with a gross
weapons offence and a gross drug offence after a police search in Malmö in July.
[@1] That brings the number of people charged across the two cases to four. [@1]
All of the accused deny the offences. [@1]
