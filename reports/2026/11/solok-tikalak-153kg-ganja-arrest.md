Police arrested a 20-year-old man identified only as MZA on the roadside at
Dusun Galanggang in Nagari Tikalak, X Koto Singkarak district, Solok regency, on
the afternoon of 17 November 2026. [@1,2] Solok Kota drug squad chief AKP Repaldi
said MZA was carrying ganja from Panyabungan in North Sumatra to West Sumatra.
[@1,2] MZA is a resident of Telaga Biruhun, Simpang Rumbio village, Lubuk Sikarah
district, Solok city. [@1]

Officers acted on a public tip about someone carrying narcotics from North
Sumatra and waited at the roadside from about 17:30. [@1,2] After about half an
hour the suspect rode past on a green Honda Beat motorcycle, registration BA
3481 HAB, and officers arrested him. [@1] A search found 16 packages of dried
ganja wrapped in yellow tape with a total weight of 15.3 kilograms. [@1] Police said the 16 packages, each sealed with yellow tape, were carried in a blue plastic sack on the motorcycle. [@2]

Officers also found a small black wallet in the motorcycle's seat compartment
holding two clear plastic bags of suspected methamphetamine. [@1] Repaldi said
the methamphetamine had not been weighed and its weight could not yet be
estimated. [@1] The suspect, the ganja, the methamphetamine and other evidence
were taken to Solok Kota police headquarters for further legal process. [@1] Police said he was suspected of violating Article 114 and Article 115 of the 2009 Narcotics Law and Article 609 of the 2023 Criminal Code. [@2] The drug squad said the suspect admitted the ganja and methamphetamine were his, that he was taking them from North Sumatra to Solok to be sold, and that he was thought to be a courier ordered by someone who promised a large payment. [@2]
