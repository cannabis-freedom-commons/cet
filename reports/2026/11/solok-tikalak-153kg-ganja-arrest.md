Police arrested a 20-year-old man identified only as MZA on the roadside at
Dusun Galanggang in Nagari Tikalak, X Koto Singkarak district, Solok regency, on
the afternoon of 17 November 2026. [@1] Solok Kota drug squad chief AKP Repaldi
said MZA was carrying ganja from Panyabungan in North Sumatra to West Sumatra.
[@1] MZA is a resident of Telaga Biruhun, Simpang Rumbio village, Lubuk Sikarah
district, Solok city. [@1]

Officers acted on a public tip about someone carrying narcotics from North
Sumatra and waited at the roadside from about 17:30. [@1] After about half an
hour the suspect rode past on a green Honda Beat motorcycle, registration BA
3481 HAB, and officers arrested him. [@1] A search found 16 packages of dried
ganja wrapped in yellow tape with a total weight of 15.3 kilograms. [@1]

Officers also found a small black wallet in the motorcycle's seat compartment
holding two clear plastic bags of suspected methamphetamine. [@1] Repaldi said
the methamphetamine had not been weighed and its weight could not yet be
estimated. [@1] The suspect, the ganja, the methamphetamine and other evidence
were taken to Solok Kota police headquarters for further legal process. [@1]
