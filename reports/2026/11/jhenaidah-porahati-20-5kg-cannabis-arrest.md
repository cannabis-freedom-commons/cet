A 24-year-old man was arrested with 20.5 kg of cannabis during a raid in
Jhenaidah Sadar upazila early Wednesday. [@1] The arrestee was identified as Md
Hridoy Hossain, son of Saheb Hossain, of Porahati area under Jhenaidah Sadar
upazila. [@1] Members of Special Response Battalion (SRB)-6 conducted the raid
at around 12:30 am on Wednesday in Porahati area under Ward No. 9, acting on a
tip-off, according to an SRB-6 press release. [@1]

During the operation, the team arrested Hridoy and recovered 20.5kg of cannabis
from beneath a bed inside his residence. [@1] The seized drugs were confiscated
in the presence of witnesses, the release said. [@1]

Squadron Leader Md Imamim Mubin Sarker Sumon, company commander of SRB-6's CPC-2
Jhenaidah camp, said the arrestee and the seized cannabis had been handed over
to Jhenaidah Sadar Police Station. [@1] A case has been filed against him under
the Narcotics Control Act, he added. [@1]
