Military police of the 4º BPM carried out an operation against drug trafficking
in Bezerros, in the Agreste region of Pernambuco, on Wednesday 25 November 2026.
[@3] The officers were on patrol when they received reports of drug trafficking
in a locality of the municipality. [@1] They went to the address indicated and
stopped one individual there. [@1] When stopped, the man had two wrapped
portions of marijuana. [@2] The suspect was taken, along with all the material
seized, to the Bezerros Civil Police station, where the applicable measures were
adopted. [@1]

As the searches continued, the officers found a further 15 portions of
marijuana, a precision scale and bags used to package the drugs. [@3] The search
also found approximately 1,000 crack rocks. [@1] The police described the scale
and bags as material used in preparing and packaging the drugs. [@1]
