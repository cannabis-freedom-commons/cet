The Fiscalía General del Estado de San Luis Potosí (FGESLP), the state attorney
general's office, secured two vehicles at different points in the metropolitan
area as part of investigative work. [@3] Investigation Police officers found a
black Kia without licence plates parked in circuito San Mateo in the Valle de
Santa Lucía subdivision and inspected it. [@1] Inside they found several metal
artefacts known as ponchallantas, or tyre spikes, and a transparent bag of green
herb with characteristics similar to marijuana. [@1]

The other vehicle was a grey 2019 Volkswagen Golf without plates found on
avenida Constitución in the San Miguelito neighbourhood. [@1] Officers
identified anomalies in the serial number visible on the dashboard. [@1] They
checked other means of identification and located the original number, and a
check on Plataforma México confirmed the Golf had been reported stolen in the
state of Puebla on 13 September 2019. [@2]

The evidence and both vehicles were left with a public prosecutor, who will
follow up and establish any link to criminal acts. [@2]
