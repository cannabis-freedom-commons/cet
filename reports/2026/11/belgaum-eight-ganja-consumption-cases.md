Market police in Belgaum filed separate cases against eight people found moving
unsteadily in public places after consuming drugs, after medical tests confirmed
consumption during a special drive on 18 November. [@1] The account identifies
the substance involved as ganja. [@1]

Working under senior officers, a Market police station team conducted the drive
across the city, taking action against people near the RTO office, on Khadebazar
road, near the Khadebazar police chowki and near Shanivar Koot. [@1] The team
also took action against more people on Khadebazar road, near the central bus
stand and at Ayodhyanagar, bringing the total to eight. [@1] Medical examination
of all eight confirmed drug consumption, after which Market police registered
eight separate crimes numbered 242 to 249 of 2026 under section 27(b) of the
NDPS Act and continued investigation. [@1]

The police commissioner and deputy police commissioner praised the
sub-inspectors, assistant sub-inspectors and staff who took part in the
operation. [@1]
