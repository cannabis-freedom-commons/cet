In Azamgarh, Jahanganj police arrested two men allegedly involved in interstate
ganja smuggling during a drive against the illegal narcotics trade. [@1] Police
said both arrested men are residents of Saran district in Bihar. [@1] Officers
caught the men near Bajha Pulia after a tip-off. [@1] Circle Officer Sadar
Ishwar Lal Gurjar said a police team led by station chief Amit Kumar Mishra was
conducting checks on Sunday. [@1]

Officers seized a total of 11.334 kilograms of illegal ganja from the two men.
[@1] Police found 6.154 kilograms of ganja with Anil Kumar Rai and 5.180
kilograms with Lal Babu Rai. [@1] A second account says officers discovered the
11.334 kilograms of ganja hidden in the suspects' vehicle during a routine
inspection near Bajha Pulia. [@2]

Police registered a case against both men under the NDPS Act and said further
legal proceedings were under way. [@1] The seizure was reported as part of
Operation Trishul, an initiative aimed at stopping the supply of illicit
substances. [@2]
