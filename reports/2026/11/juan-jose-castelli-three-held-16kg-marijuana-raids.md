Argentine Federal Police said it dismantled a drug-trafficking organization
operating in Juan Jose Castelli in Chaco province. [@1] Officers carried out
three simultaneous raids on homes in the town. [@1] Officers detained two adult
men and one adult woman in the raids. [@1] The three detainees were placed at
the disposal of the judiciary for infringement of National Drugs Law No. 23.737.
[@1]

Officers seized more than 16 kilograms of marijuana and one kilogram of cocaine
during the procedures. [@1] Officers also seized a calibre 38 revolver, a
semi-automatic pistol, ammunition, eight mobile phones, three precision scales
and a banknote-counting machine. [@1] Officers also seized 3,297,500 pesos in
cash and documentation relevant to the case. [@1]

The investigation began on 11 August after a complaint linked three people to
drug dealing. [@1] Surveillance established that the suspects used their own
homes to store and portion the drugs for retail sale, known locally as
narcomenudeo. [@1]
