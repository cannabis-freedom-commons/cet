During a narcotics operation in the Alanya district of Antalya province, police
stopped a vehicle at Dinek and detained the three suspects inside it: the driver
M.Ç., 33, H.K., 40, and M.D., 41. [@1] After police processing and court
appearances, H.K. and M.D. were jailed and sent to prison, while driver M.Ç. was
released under adli kontrol, a judicial supervision order. [@3] One of the two
remanded suspects was a woman. [@3]

Officers searched the stopped vehicle and found 20 grams of esrar, the Turkish
name for cannabis resin, along with 10 grams of methamphetamine and a precision
scale. [@1] The scale was assessed as having been used to weigh the drugs. [@3]

Police acted on information that drugs would be brought by car from Antalya to
Alanya and stopped the vehicle at Dinek. [@2] The operation was coordinated by
the Alanya Chief Public Prosecutor's Office and carried out by the narcotics
bureau of the Alanya district police directorate. [@2]
