Officers of Uzbekistan's State Security Service stopped a man in Khorezm region
who had earlier been under surveillance. [@1,2] They detained him as he was
leaving a house in Urgench district where he had been living temporarily. [@1]
At that moment a woman he knew threw a polyethylene bag from a window of the
house. [@1,2]

Officers found 715 grams of hashish in the bag, together with 0.92 grams of
mephedrone and 23 tablets marked PGN 300 containing the potent substance
pregabalin. [@1,2] The State Security Service press office reported the find as
part of action against illegal drug trafficking in several regions of the
country. [@1] Only the hashish is counted as cannabis. [@1] The mephedrone and
the pregabalin tablets found in the same bag are not cannabis and are left out
of the weight. [@1]

Criminal cases were opened on every fact the service identified, under the
relevant articles of the Criminal Code. [@1,2] Investigative actions were still
being carried out after those cases were opened. [@1]
