A mobile court in Noakhali Sadar Upazila sentenced two men to seven days of
simple imprisonment each for consuming cannabis and disturbing public peace.
[@1] The two were Kamruzzaman Biprob, 26, of Lakshminarayanpur in Noakhali
municipality's Ward No. 6, and Sakhawat Ullah, 26. [@2] Each man was also fined
50 taka. [@1]

The operation took place at noon on Thursday, 26 November, in the
Lakshminarayanpur, Harinarayanpur, Adarsh School and Firingir Datterhat Pol
areas of the upazila. [@1] The drive was led by assistant commissioners and
executive magistrates Iqbal Hossen Sohan and Md. Shahabuddin, with the
participation of the Department of Narcotics Control, acting on a tip-off. [@1]

Subrata Sarkar Shubha, deputy director of the Department of Narcotics Control in
Noakhali, confirmed the operation and said such drives would continue to prevent
and eradicate drug abuse. [@1]
