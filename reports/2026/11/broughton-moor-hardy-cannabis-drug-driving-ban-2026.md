Daniel Hardy, 38, of Seaton Road, Broughton Moor, admitted driving a Toyota
Corolla on Seaton Road on 4 September 2026 with 3.4 micrograms per litre of
Delta-9-tetrahydrocannabinol (THC) in his blood, above the legal limit. [@1] He
appeared at West Cumbria Courthouse on 23 November 2026 and pleaded guilty. [@1]

The court disqualified him from driving for 12 months. [@1] His fine was reduced
to £270 from £405 because he admitted the offence. [@1] He was also ordered to
pay £85 in prosecution costs and a £108 victim surcharge. [@1] He must pay the
total in £100 monthly instalments from 21 December 2026, and the court issued a
collection order for the payments. [@1]
