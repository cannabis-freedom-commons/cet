In Piazza Scaravilli, in Bologna's university district, State Police arrested a
20-year-old Tunisian youth on a drug-dealing charge. [@1] Plainclothes officers
were checking the area after residents' complaints when a man asked if they
wanted to buy drugs and led them to the alleged dealer hidden behind a column of
the Via Zamboni arcade. [@1] The man walked to a second person, a Tunisian born
in 2006, who was hiding behind a column of the Via Zamboni portico. [@1]
Officers watched the youth hand over a dose and stepped in to check him, and the
20-year-old in turn offered to sell them drugs. [@2] The youth was arrested for
dealing narcotics jointly with another person who remained unidentified. [@1]

A personal search found about 125 grams of hashish hidden inside the youth's
jacket. [@1] Officers seized the 125 grams of hashish together with 80 euros and
a nail clipper with a pointed blade. [@3] The nail clipper had a pointed blade
bearing traces of narcotic substance. [@1]

The episode happened on the evening of Friday 13 November at about 21:30, during
an extraordinary territorial-control operation by the Due Torri-San Francesco
police station against dealing in the city centre. [@1] Investigators found the
youth was already known to police for specific drug priors and had been arrested
several times for similar offences. [@1] He was also subject to a mandatory
order to leave Bologna with a two-year ban on returning, which police say he had
breached. [@1] As well as the joint-dealing arrest, he was reported at liberty
for disobeying that order. [@2]
