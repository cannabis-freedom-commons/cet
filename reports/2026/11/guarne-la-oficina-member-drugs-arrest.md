Army troops with Antioquia police captured a presumed member of La Oficina
during a check in Guarne, Antioquia. [@1] The operation was run by the army's
Medium Cavalry Group No. 4 Juan del Corral of the Fourth Brigade with police
support, and was announced by the Seventh Division on 15 November. [@1] The
authorities presented the detainee as a presumed member of the organized crime
group La Oficina but released no name and reported no later judicial decision.
[@1]

At the scene troops found sealed bags containing cocaine hydrochloride, bazuco
and marijuana cigarettes. [@1] The army statement gave no weight for the
substances and no count of doses, so no quantified cannabis amount was reported.
[@1] The Seventh Division's account describes a single detained man and the
seizure in Guarne of cocaine, bazuco and marijuana cigarettes. [@1]

The army said the operation hurt the criminal structure's illicit economies and
helped weaken its logistical and financial capacities. [@1] In the army's first
released photo the detainee appears from behind guarded by two soldiers, with
the seized items on a table beside a sign citing article 376 on drug
trafficking, manufacture or possession. [@1] The announcement did not identify
the specific sector of Guarne where the check took place and gave no exact date
for the procedure beyond its disclosure that Sunday. [@1]
