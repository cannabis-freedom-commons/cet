Border Guard Bangladesh seized Indian ganja worth Tk 35,000 in a special
anti-smuggling operation on the Satkhira border. [@1] A patrol team of
Taluigachha border outpost under Satkhira Battalion (33 BGB) carried out the
operation on Sunday, 15 November. [@1]

The ganja was seized from the Charabari area, about 50 yards inside Bangladesh
from border pillar 13/2-S. [@1] BGB said the consignment had been brought
illegally from India while evading customs duty. [@1] Local residents urged that
such operations continue. [@1]
