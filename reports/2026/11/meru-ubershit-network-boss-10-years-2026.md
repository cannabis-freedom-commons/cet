Seven people involved in drug trafficking appeared on Tuesday 17 November 2026
before the criminal court in Beauvais. [@1] The network's alleged boss, still at
large, was tried in his absence. [@1] The boss and six other members of the
Méru-area network were sentenced to firm prison terms. [@1] The absent head
received ten years in prison. [@2] The other custodial terms imposed that night
were reported as ranging from eight months to seven years in prison. [@2]

The network operated by home delivery known as Uber shit through the social
network Snapchat. [@1] The inquiry was run over several months by the Méru
research brigade and the Amiens research section, which identified the main
suspects. [@3] Nine people were arrested on Monday 14 September 2026 in a
judicial operation directed by the Beauvais prosecution. [@4] Searches that day
seized almost seven kilograms of cannabis resin and 500 grams of herbal
cannabis. [@3] Officers also seized 164 grams of cocaine and 7,485 euros in
cash, which are excluded from the cannabis total. [@4]

After four days in custody, one suspect was given a court summons while the
other eight were brought before the prosecution. [@3] Three suspects who
admitted guilt were convicted through guilty-plea proceedings, including one
immediate custodial term. [@4] Five other suspects were placed in pretrial
detention pending the 17 November 2026 hearing. [@4]
