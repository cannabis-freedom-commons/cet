Police arrested 32-year-old Darlington Mandaa at Redwing Mine in Penhalonga
after allegedly recovering 72 kilogrammes of suspected dagga from his house.
[@1] Acting Manicaland provincial police spokesperson Assistant Inspector
Wiseman Chinyoka identified the suspect as Darlington Mandaa. [@1] Detectives
from the Criminal Investigation Department's Drugs and Narcotics section in
Mutare acted on a tip-off alleging that Mandaa was dealing in dangerous drugs.
[@1]

Detectives searched his house and recovered the dagga from several locations,
including a black plastic bag, a 20-litre bucket and 27 sachets found in his
bed. [@1] Police weighed the recovered substance and recorded a total of 72
kilogrammes. [@1] The suspected dagga was found in a black plastic bag, a
20-litre bucket and 27 small sachets hidden in the suspect's bed. [@2] Officers
conducted the search at the suspect's home on Sunday. [@2]

Officers arrested Mandaa and detained him at Mutare Central Police Station as
investigations continued. [@1] Mandaa was transferred to Mutare Central Police
Station, where he remained in custody as investigations continued. [@2]
