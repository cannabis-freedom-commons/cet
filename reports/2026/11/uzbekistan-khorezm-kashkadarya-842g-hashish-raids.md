In Uzbekistan's Khorezm region, police detained a 27-year-old man who had been
under surveillance, and at the moment of detention an acquaintance threw a
package out of a window. [@1,4] The package was found to contain 715 grams of
hashish, along with 0.92 grams of the synthetic drug mephedrone and 23 capsules
of PGN 300 containing pregabalin. [@1,4] The man had been stopped as he left the
house in Urgench district where he was temporarily living. [@2]

At the Umakay road patrol post in Kashkadarya, a Cobalt car travelling from
Surkhandarya to Tashkent was stopped and checked, and its passenger, a resident
of Denau district born in 1996, was found with 327 grams of opium and 127 grams
of hashish. [@2,4] The substances in the car had been illegally brought into
Uzbekistan from Tajikistan, according to the information available to
investigators. [@1,4]

The seizures were part of the 'Safe and Healthy Country' preventive operation
covering Samarkand, Khorezm and Kashkadarya regions. [@3,4] Criminal cases were
opened over the incidents and an investigation was under way, according to the
State Security Service. [@3,4]
