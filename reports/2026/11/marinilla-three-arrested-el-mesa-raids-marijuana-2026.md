A joint operation by the National Police and the Attorney General's Technical
Investigation Corps (CTI) detained three people in Marinilla, in eastern
Antioquia, in proceedings carried out on 14 November 2026 targeting the alleged
organised criminal group known as El Mesa. [@1] The operation was carried out by
the Antioquia Police's Basic Criminal Investigation Unit for Protection and
Special Services, with police intelligence and CTI support, in coordination with
Local Prosecutor's Office 111 EDA of Antioquia. [@1] Officers searched two homes
in the urban centre and arrested three men aged 19, 22 and 67, who were linked
to investigations into alleged weapons and drug offences. [@1]

According to the official report, officers found two traumatic firearms, four
magazines, 28 cartridges for those weapons and ten mobile phones. [@1] They also
seized 16 plastic bags holding a powdery substance with characteristics similar
to coca base and one bag holding marijuana material. [@1] Investigators also
found 26 citizenship identity cards, two driving licences, 20 bank cards and two
notebooks with annotations and accounting records. [@1] Officers also found
material that authorities said had apparently been used to record fingerprints
and signatures, allegedly for identity theft and financial scams and fraud. [@1]

Intelligence and investigative work indicated the raided properties were
allegedly being used to store weapons and to gather inputs for processing
illegal drugs. [@1] Authorities are also investigating the alleged use of minors
in the illegal activities as part of inquiries into how the criminal structure
operates. [@1] Investigations are continuing to establish where the documents
and bank cards came from and whether they are linked to other crimes. [@1]
