Kakinada district police arrested two accused and seized 1.6 kg of ganja from
them, along with cash and silver bars. [@1] Police said they identified the
suspect from fingerprints found at the scene on 17 November. [@1]

The underlying case was a theft at a shop that melts gold and silver jewellery
on Kakinada's Gold Market Street, which district police said they solved within
a week. [@1] Ganja was found with the first accused and, on questioning, he said
the second accused had brought and given it to him, leading to the second man's
detention. [@1] Police said the two men had decided to stop stealing and sell
ganja because surveillance had increased. [@1] One of the men, Loveraju, had
been bringing ganja from Chintapalli and selling it, investigators said. [@1]

Additional SP M. Kishore Kumar disclosed the case details at a press conference
at the DSP office in Kakinada on Tuesday, alongside DSP N.S.V.K. Durga Rao. [@1]
