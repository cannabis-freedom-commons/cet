Security units of the National Security District at Jardins de Carthage
dismantled a drug-trafficking network operating among young people in the
northern suburbs of Tunis and detained seven people, including two women. [@1]
The case dates to 18 November 2026, when information reached the security units
that a network selling drugs to young people in the northern suburbs included a
taxi driver. [@2] After investigations, officers identified a suspect originally
from Bourj El Wazir and set an ambush that caught him supplying two local girls
with cannabis in exchange for 60 dinars. [@1] Investigators found that he
brokered drug sales in coordination with another man in Karm El Gharbi; officers
went to the man's home, arrested him and he confessed to drug trafficking. [@1]

After consulting the public prosecutor, authorities ordered all parties
detained. [@1] Officers seized a taxi, 60 dinars, a quantity of cannabis and
three mobile phones belonging to the suspects. [@3]
