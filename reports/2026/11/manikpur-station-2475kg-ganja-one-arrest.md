The Government Railway Police Crime Wing arrested Indrajeet Singh, described as
a resident of Talgram in Kannauj district, near Manikpur station in Chitrakoot
late on Wednesday night on suspicion of smuggling ganja. [@1] A joint team of
the Government Railway Police and the Railway Protection Force Crime Wing
encircled the accused near the station and took him into custody. [@1]

Officers recovering the consignment seized 24 kilograms 750 grams, or 24.75
kilograms, of ganja from the accused at the time of the arrest. [@1] The seized
ganja was assigned an estimated value of 12 lakh 35 thousand rupees, or
1,235,000 rupees. [@1]

Following the arrest, a first information report was registered against the
accused at GRP police station Karvi. [@1] GRP in-charge Jitendra Kumar said that
information about the wider smuggling network behind the consignment was being
gathered. [@1] Manikpur station, near which the seizure took place, lies in
Chitrakoot district of Uttar Pradesh. [@1]
