A 29-year-old man was apprehended by police in Choele Choel after causing
disturbances in a public place, fleeing in a vehicle and trying to discard items
with suspected prohibited substances. [@1] The operation began mid-morning on
Thursday when Comisaría 8ª staff went to the intersection of Malvinas and San
Martín in Choele Choel after an emergency alert about disturbances there. [@1]
When police arrived, the two men involved left the scene in private vehicles.
[@1] One of them, driving a Ford F-100 pickup truck, fled at high speed along
San Martín street before a police patrol followed and intercepted him at
Avellaneda 600. [@1]

During the apprehension the driver was hostile toward police and tried to throw
a plastic container with a green vegetable substance resembling cannabis and a
metal grinder from the truck bed. [@1] Officers arrested him and transferred him
to the police unit. [@1]

The incident led to summary proceedings for disturbing public order with the
intervention of the local judiciary. [@1] In parallel, the General Roca Fiscal
Unit ordered the formal seizure of the confiscated items to advance an
investigation for suspected possession of prohibited substances. [@1]
