Police captured a 29-year-old man in flagrante during a search-and-seizure raid
by the National Police in a residential complex in central Armenia. [@1] The
detainee, identified by the alias Pipe, is under investigation for alleged
involvement in the production, storage and sale of narcotics. [@1]

Police said the premises allegedly served as a drug storage and distribution
point, where authorities found synthetic substances, marijuana and items for
preparing doses for sale. [@1] During the inspection officers seized several
doses of synthetic substances and marijuana, a precision scale and other
implements said to be used to prepare and dose the drugs. [@1] Police inquiries
indicate he allegedly used his residence to store and distribute mainly
synthetic drugs through home deliveries in different sectors of Armenia. [@1]

The operation was carried out in coordination between the National Police and
Local Prosecutor’s Office 26 of Armenia as part of investigations into drug
distribution points in the city. [@1] The institution said the captured man has
no judicial record. [@1] He must answer for the alleged offence of trafficking,
manufacture or possession of narcotics. [@1]

The man and the seized items were placed at the disposal of the judicial
authorities, which will carry out the procedure to establish his responsibility
for the acts under investigation. [@1]
