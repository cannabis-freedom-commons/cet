Five men were detained by the State Police in Toluca and bound over to trial on
suspicion of offences against health and misuse of official emblems, after being
found with clothing carrying federal agency insignia and patrol-style lights.
[@1] They were identified as Erik “N”, Marco “N”, Aldair “N”, Giovanni “N” and
Mauricio “N”. [@1] Officers found among their possessions a windbreaker labelled
“SSPC México, Secretaría de Seguridad y Protección Ciudadana” and a black
sweatshirt reading “Guardia Nacional, justicia y paz”. [@1]

Police seized a light device known as “códigos”, a backpack, 214 grams and four
milligrams of methamphetamine and 141 grams and four milligrams (141.004 g) of
marijuana. [@1] They also took 15 firearm cartridges and a car that was
circulating without number plates. [@1]

The five men were handed to the Fiscalía General de la República (FGR), whose
prosecutor opened an investigation and presented evidence to a control judge.
[@1] At the initial hearing the judge ruled the detention lawful and ordered the
five bound over to trial on suspicion of the alleged offences. [@1] They were
ordered held on remand and will stay at the Centro de Reclusión Neza-Bordo while
proceedings continue, with two months set for the complementary investigation to
close. [@1] The FGR said the five must be presumed innocent until a competent
court convicts them. [@1]
