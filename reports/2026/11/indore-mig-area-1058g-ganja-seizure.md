Police in the MIG police-station area of Indore seized 1.058 kg of ganja from an
accused near BGM Cafe in Ayodhyapuri Colony. [@1] Police put the value of the
seized ganja at about 20,000 rupees. [@1] Officers registered a case under the
NDPS Act, India's main drug law. [@1]

In the Kanadia police-station area police acted in two separate ganja cases, one
on Nai Sadak in Bhuri Tekri and another on the Sahara City service road at the
AB bypass. [@1] Cases were registered against different accused in both Kanadia
cases under the NDPS Act. [@1] Police registered the cases on complaints and
began investigating. [@1]
