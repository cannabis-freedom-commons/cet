Hayden Craig Nelson, 35, appeared in the Invercargill District Court earlier
this month after admitting drug supply charges. [@1] He admitted seven charges
covering offering to sell cannabis, possession of cannabis for supply and two
charges of offering to sell ecstasy. [@1] The court gave him a sentence focussed
on rehabilitation, described as an opportunity to turn his life around. [@1]
Nelson was described as a Southland drug dealer. [@1]

Police identified 1699 grams of cannabis as cannabis that Nelson had sold. [@1]
Nelson also had further cannabis in his possession beyond the cannabis sold.
[@1] The judge put the combined total of the cannabis sold and possessed at
1.79kg. [@1] Two of the admitted charges concerned offering to sell ecstasy
rather than cannabis. [@1]

The case was dealt with at the Invercargill District Court. [@1] The court
appearance took place on November 6. [@1] The judge stated the cannabis total in
court when the case was addressed. [@1] No drugs besides cannabis and ecstasy
were listed among the admitted charges. [@1]
