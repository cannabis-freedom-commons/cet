Pipariya police in Chhattisgarh's Bemetara district arrested a man who was
selling ganja, the Hindi term for cannabis, to customers in front of his own
house, in a campaign against the illegal drug trade in the district. [@1] The
arrest followed a tip received on Tuesday 17 November that a man in Indauri
village was openly selling ganja, and a police team led by station house officer
Inspector Nitin Tiwari raided the spot. [@2] Police identified him as Rambhajan
Chandravanshi, 40, a resident of Indauri village, and said he was caught
red-handed before witnesses selling ganja in front of his home. [@2]

A search found 356 grams of ganja, which police assessed at a market value of
17,800 rupees, along with 250 rupees in cash from the sales, making 18,050
rupees of seized property in all. [@2]

Police registered a case against him and, after completing the legal
formalities, produced him before a court on Wednesday 18 November. [@2]
