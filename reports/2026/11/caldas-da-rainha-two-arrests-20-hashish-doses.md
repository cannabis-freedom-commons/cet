Public Security Police officers intercepted two men aged 20 and 32 in Caldas da
Rainha after receiving information that they had travelled to the city allegedly
to threaten a resident and might be carrying a firearm. [@1] The Criminal
Investigation Squad of Caldas da Rainha carried out surveillance and stopped the
pair to prevent possible violent acts. [@1] The interception happened on 12
November, and both men were detained on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@2]

Officers found the men carrying 84 doses of cocaine, 20 doses of hashish and
three doses of cocaine in crack form. [@1] Personal searches and a search of the
car they were travelling in found no other objects of criminal relevance. [@3]
Investigators concluded that the trip was linked to a dispute over a debt from
an earlier drug transaction. [@1]

After processing at police premises, both men were released under
identity-and-residence terms and ordered to appear before the Public Prosecution
Service at the Caldas da Rainha judicial court. [@1]
