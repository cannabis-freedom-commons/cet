Chowk Bazar police's surveillance team raided the first floor of plot 261 in
Rail Rahat Colony, near Sulemani Masjid on Bharimata Road in Surat's Phulwadi
area, on 17 November 2026. [@1] They arrested 23-year-old Arbaaz Husain
Nimazuddin Ansari, who lived in the flat. [@1] Police said Ansari is a native of
Panapur in Bihar's Saran district. [@1]

Officers seized 2.065 kilograms of ganja, which police valued at 1,03,250
rupees. [@1] A case was registered at Chowk Bazar police station under sections
8(C), 20(B)(II)(B) and 29 of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act.
[@1]

The raid was part of a Surat city police campaign against the drug trade called
"NO DRUGS IN SURAT". [@1] Police said preliminary investigation indicated he had
entered the illegal ganja trade to earn money easily. [@1] Police said further
investigation would examine where the ganja came from, who it was meant to be
sold to and whether anyone else was part of the network. [@1]
