Spain's National Police arrested four people, aged 17 to 56 and including one
minor, in Orihuela, Alicante, as suspected members of a family clan growing
marijuana. [@2] The National Police dismantled a family clan dedicated to indoor
marijuana cultivation in the Alicante town of Orihuela. [@3] The investigation
began after officers learned of the group's possible activity, which was said to
involve an indoor plantation on a rural property in a hamlet of Orihuela. [@1]
Surveillance and trailing of the suspects confirmed that the property could hold
the plantation, police said. [@1] Officers obtained a court warrant to enter and
search the property and detained the four there on suspicion of drug
trafficking. [@2]

Inside, police found seven rooms used to grow marijuana, with plants in an
advanced stage of flowering. [@1] They seized 900 plants and about 30 kilograms
of marijuana buds, along with documents, cash and mobile phones. [@1] Police
said the property had an advanced indoor growing setup with air conditioning,
LED lighting and carbon filters. [@4] Police described the property as the
centre of the family clan's illegal activity. [@5] Officers dismantled the
installation and electricity company technicians cut off a supply that had been
fraudulently connected to the grid. [@1]

Three of the four detainees were placed at the disposal of the duty
investigating courts in Orihuela after police processing. [@4]
