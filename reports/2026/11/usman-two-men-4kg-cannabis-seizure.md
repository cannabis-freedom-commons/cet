Police in the Usman municipal okrug of Lipetsk Oblast identified two local
residents, aged 38 and 44, as suspected of unlawfully storing plant-derived
narcotics. [@2] The drug-control group of the Usmansky police department carried
out the operation. [@2] Their action formed the third stage of the
interdepartmental crime-prevention operation Mak-2026. [@1] Investigators opened
criminal cases against both men under part 2 of article 228 of the Russian
Criminal Code, covering large-scale acquisition, storage, transport, manufacture
and processing of narcotic drugs. [@1] The two men were detained during the
operation. [@3] Both were placed under a written undertaking not to leave. [@1]
The offence carries a maximum penalty of ten years in prison. [@1]

The 38-year-old kept prohibited substances in an outbuilding garage and in the
boot of a VAZ-2105 car. [@1] Police seized 421 grams of marijuana and more than
one kilogram of parts of a Cannabis plant from him. [@1]

At the home of the 44-year-old, police found and seized more than 887 grams of
the drug and more than 1.7 kilograms of parts of a drug-containing plant. [@1]
The four quantities come to more than four kilograms of cannabis material in
all. [@1]
