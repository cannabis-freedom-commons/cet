The Seventh Oral Criminal Trial Court of Santiago convicted the accused as the
author of illicit drug trafficking committed on 18 June 2024 in the commune of
Macul and imposed six years of effective imprisonment. [@1] In setting the
drug-trafficking penalty, the court recognised the mitigation of substantial
cooperation in clarifying the facts and the aggravation of reoffending from a
previous conviction for small-quantity drug trafficking. [@1] The court also
imposed a 42 UTM fine, payable in twelve monthly instalments of 3.5 UTM to
SENDA, warning that missing one instalment would make the whole balance due.
[@1]

The investigation began after an anonymous complaint about drug sales in two
Macul flats, and during June 2024 surveillance the PDI stopped two buyers
carrying cannabis who said they had bought it in one of the flats, which led to
a judicial entry and search order. [@1] On 18 June 2024 police raided both flats
and arrested the accused. [@1] Officers seized about 1.09 kilograms of cannabis
that he had thrown onto a neighbouring house's roof, plus other quantities of
marijuana and cocaine, a 2.38-metre cannabis plant, a digital scale, drug-dosing
materials and 174,300 pesos in cash. [@1] In his bedroom officers found a
9-millimetre Bruni blank-firing pistol with its magazine and no ammunition. [@1]
Prosecutors charged him with illicit drug trafficking and illegal firearm
possession and initially sought thirteen years in prison for the trafficking
plus five years for the weapon. [@1]

The accused stated that he had sold marijuana for about two and a half years to
fund his own use, at 2,000 pesos per gram and about one kilogram per month. [@1]
He admitted that the drugs thrown onto the neighbouring property were for sale.
[@1] The court found that the accused kept, stored and sold drugs without
authorisation, constituting trafficking under Law 20,000, and rejected that the
substances were only for personal use. [@1] The court acquitted him of illegal
firearm possession because the registration duty for adaptable blank-firing
pistols was still within the regularisation period, which expired on 21 December
2024, after the June 2024 facts. [@1] The court ordered confiscation of the
trafficking items and the 174,300 pesos. [@1]
