In early November, gendarmes arrested 18 people suspected of belonging to a
drug-trafficking network operating around Saint-Loup-sur-Semouse in Haute-Saône
and in Meurthe-et-Moselle. [@1] The 18 arrests took place during the nights of 9
to 10 November and 12 to 13 November, after which those arrested were placed in
police custody. [@2] Seventeen of the arrests were made in Haute-Saône and one
in Meurthe-et-Moselle. [@1] Between 12 and 14 November, 14 of those arrested
were brought before an investigating judge and placed under formal
investigation. [@2] Seven of the 14 were remanded in pre-trial detention and the
other seven were placed under judicial supervision. [@1]

Searches during the week seized 450 grams of cannabis resin, alongside ketamine,
cocaine and heroin making up 1.33 kg of drugs. [@2] Earlier seizures during the
months-long investigation had already taken another 21 grams of cannabis resin
within about 2.75 kg of drugs. [@2] The week's searches also recovered 16
weapons and hundreds of rounds of ammunition of various calibres, six vehicles,
150 nitrous-oxide bottles and about 25,700 euros. [@2] The operation mobilised
190 gendarmes to carry out the arrests. [@1]

The investigation into the trafficking had begun in April 2026. [@1] The alleged
acts ran from 1 January to 12 November 2026 and were committed in Haute-Saône,
the Vosges, Meurthe-et-Moselle and across the national territory. [@2] A
judicial investigation was opened and the inquiry was continuing. [@1]
