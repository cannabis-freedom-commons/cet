Special Response Battalion (SRB-5) detained one young man with a large quantity
of ganja in Chapainawabganj Sadar Upazila. [@1,2] The arrested man was identified as 32-year-old Md. Tariq of Sateroroshia Chamagram in Shibganj, son of Md. Alam and Serina Khatun. [@2] The ganja was recovered by
searching a battery-run auto-rickshaw. [@1]

An SRB-5 team set up a checkpoint in the Amnura Dinagar Mor area of Jhilm Union
and carried out the operation at around 11:45 am on Thursday. [@1,2] The temporary
checkpoint on the Chapainawabganj-Amnura road was set up on secret information
that a drug consignment was being brought from the Rajshahi region. [@1,2] The consignment was said to be coming from the Tanore area of Rajshahi toward the Chapainawabganj railway station area. [@2]
Officers stopped a suspicious auto-rickshaw and questioned the driver about a
plastic sack in the vehicle, and he gave incoherent answers. [@1,2] Opening the
sack found a total of 7 kg of ganja wrapped in scotch tape inside 16 jute-string
bundles, with an estimated market value of about 210,000 taka. [@1,2]

Officers seized the auto-rickshaw used in the incident and a mobile phone, and
handed the arrested person and the seized evidence to Sadar Model Thana. [@1,2] The seized phone was a button mobile phone and the seized vehicle was a battery-run auto-van used for drug transport. [@2]
Law enforcers said a case under the Narcotics Control Act against him was in
process. [@1] A regular case under the Narcotics Control Act has been filed against him. [@2]
