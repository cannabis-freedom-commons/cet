Police arrested four people near Kandachipuram for smuggling cannabis. [@1]
Those arrested were 23-year-old Agasthiyan, son of Anthony of Nallapalayam Road
in Kandachipuram, his 22-year-old brother Alex, 31-year-old Senthilkumar, son of
Sekar, and 28-year-old Sathish, son of Kannan, the latter two described as
friends from the Madavilagam area. [@1] Agasthiyan had arrived in Viluppuram by
train and the four were travelling home together by auto-rickshaw when they were
stopped. [@1]

Inspector Ravichandran and other officers carrying out checks in the
Kandachipuram area stopped the auto-rickshaw and searched it. [@1] The search
found 120 grams of ganja in a bag the group was carrying. [@1] The reported 120
grams equals 0.12 kilograms. [@1] Police registered a case against all four,
arrested them and seized the 120 grams of ganja. [@1]

The interception linked the district town of Viluppuram, where Agasthiyan
arrived by train, with the Kandachipuram area where the road check and arrests
took place. [@1]
