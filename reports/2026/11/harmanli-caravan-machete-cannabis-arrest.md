A 35-year-old Turkish citizen was detained in Harmanli on suspicion of
possessing narcotic substances and resisting police officers. [@1] Officers from
the Harmanli police department acted on operational information at about 18:00
on 19 November 2026 and went to check the man, who lived in a caravan in the
town's industrial zone. [@1] He resisted and threatened the officers with a
machete to stop the check, and was subdued and detained; no officer was injured.
[@2]

A search of the caravan found two hand-rolled cigarettes and plastic bags of
dried green leafy buds with a total weight of about 1.050 kg, which a field drug
test indicated was marijuana. [@1] Officers also found about 340 cigarettes
without excise stamps and one kilogram of cut tobacco. [@1]

The man was held for up to 24 hours and a pre-trial investigation was opened.
[@1] Taran.News reported that police acted on information that the man held and
distributed marijuana. [@3]
