Officers of the Białystok prevention unit arrested two people for possessing
narcotic substances. [@1] While patrolling one of the city's streets, the
officers noticed a young man drinking non-alcoholic beer, with the 23-year-old
woman in his company. [@1] During the conversation the officers smelled a strong
smell of marijuana; on the woman they found two silver packets of green dried
plant material. [@1] The 27-year-old man had two joints containing the same
substance. [@1]

The woman said the marijuana had been prescribed by a psychiatrist, but she had
no prescription with her and could not remember the doctor's name. [@1] A
preliminary drug test confirmed that the secured substances were marijuana. [@1]
Both were detained, and possession of narcotic substances carries up to three
years in prison. [@1]
