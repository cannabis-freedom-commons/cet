Police uprooted and seized eight fully grown cannabis trees during an operation
at the Maloney Housing Development. [@1,2] The trees were growing in a bushy area
near Building 19 and measured between four and six feet tall. [@1,2] Officers
searched common areas and overgrown bush around Buildings 13 to 21. [@1,2]

The operation took place on Friday, 13 November 2026, from 9 am to 1 pm. [@1,2] It
involved officers from the Northern North Division's Gang Unit, Criminal
Investigations Department, Special Investigation Unit and K-9 Unit. [@1] The
Trinidad and Tobago Police Service said Senior Superintendent Frank McKenzie and
ASP Belewa coordinated the operation, which was supervised by Acting Inspector
Caesar and Woman Sergeant Joseph. [@1,2]
