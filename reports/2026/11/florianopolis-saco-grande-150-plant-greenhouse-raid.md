Military Police from Santa Catarina's 21st Battalion found a cannabis greenhouse
in a house in the Saco Grande neighbourhood of Florianópolis on the afternoon of
Thursday 19 November 2026. [@1] Officers went to the address after a tip about a
possible growing structure there and confirmed the cultivation on the spot. [@2]
One man who was in the house was arrested in flagrante. [@1]

In the house officers seized about 150 cannabis plants. [@1] They also seized
765 grams of marijuana and 300 grams of hashish. [@2] The equipment taken
included seven LED lamps, two fans, a humidifier, fertilisers and nutrients used
on the plants, and a mobile phone. [@1]

The growing structure stood inside the residence. [@3] The man and all the
seized material were taken to the Civil Police station for the legal procedures.
[@1] The Military Police did not release the suspect's identity. [@2] Saco
Grande is in the north of Florianópolis. [@3]
