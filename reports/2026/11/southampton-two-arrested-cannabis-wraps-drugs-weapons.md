Two men have been arrested after police found a significant quantity of drugs
and weapons at a Southampton address. [@1] Hampshire police carried out
plain-clothes patrols in the city centre in recent days to target drug-related
activity, after concerns were raised by residents. [@1] During the patrols,
officers saw a man known to police leaving a house suspected of being linked to
drug supply. [@1] The man was detained under Section 23 of the Misuse of Drugs
Act a short distance away from the property and was found to be in possession of
multiple wraps of cannabis. [@1] He was arrested on suspicion of possession with
intent to supply cannabis. [@1]

Following the arrest, officers found a "significant" quantity of Class A and
Class B drugs, as well as a number of weapons during a search of the property.
[@1] A second man was then arrested on suspicion of being concerned in the
supply of Class A and Class B drugs and possession of an offensive weapon in a
private place. [@1]

A Hampshire police spokesperson said drug-related crime has a significant impact
on communities and the Bargate Neighbourhoods Team remains committed to tackling
those involved in the supply of illegal drugs. [@1] Police said they will
continue high-visibility and plain-clothes patrols across the city using foot,
cycle and targeted vehicle operations. [@1]
