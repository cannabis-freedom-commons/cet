Dhaka Metropolitan Police Special Response Unit officers arrested three people
in separate operations in Jatrabari and Tejgaon while they were allegedly
attempting muggings. [@1] Police identified those arrested as 26-year-old
Jahidul Islam Tamal, 38-year-old Md Habib and 31-year-old Md Shamim Ahmed. [@1]
The Intelligence Analysis Division detained Tamal and Habib at about 10:30 pm on
Saturday 14 November under the Hanif Flyover in the Jatrabari Chowrasta area
while they were allegedly attempting a mugging. [@1]

Officers recovered 100 grams of ganja, the local term for cannabis, from their
custody. [@1] The two men were handed over to Jatrabari police station for
further legal action. [@1]

In the separate Tejgaon operation, a Transnational Crime Division team arrested
Shamim Ahmed in the Karwan Bazar area at about 8:15 pm the same evening while he
was allegedly consuming drugs. [@1] Legal proceedings against those arrested
were reported to be under way, with no court outcome stated. [@1]
