Police in Aceh Tengah regency, Aceh, destroyed 24,043.28 grams (24.04 kg) of
dried ganja at the regency police headquarters in Takengon on Thursday 26
November 2026. [@1] The ganja came from four police reports with four suspects,
disclosed by the narcotics unit in August and September 2026. [@1] Regency
police chief AKBP Teguh Siswoyo led the destruction at a press conference
attended by the deputy regent, TNI and DPRK representatives, the Takengon
district court and the Aceh Tengah prosecutor's office. [@2]

The destroyed lots came from four districts: 450 grams from Bintang, 1,469.28
grams from Silih Nara, 21,254 grams from Atu Lintang and 870 grams from
Pegasing. [@2] Police valued the dried ganja at about 241 million rupiah and
said its destruction could prevent potential abuse by 48,087 people. [@2] A
third account confirmed that the 24,043.28 grams were destroyed at Mapolres Aceh
Tengah on 26 November 2026, led by Teguh Siswoyo. [@3]

The Aceh Tengah destruction was held alongside a simultaneous national
narcotics-evidence destruction directed by President Prabowo Subianto and
national police chief Suyudi Ario Seto. [@2] Teguh said police would keep
pursuing drug networks in Aceh Tengah, including ganja, methamphetamine and
ecstasy. [@1]
