Police in Mecheri in Salem district seized 4.4 kilograms of ganja kept for sale
and arrested two men in connection with it. [@1] A police team led by Mecheri
Inspector Angappan was conducting vehicle checks on the Pirivu road in Mecheri
on Saturday. [@1] The team stopped a motorcycle that was speeding in a
suspicious manner and caught its rider, 32-year-old Srinivasan from Kuttapatti,
for a search. [@1] The search of the motorcycle found 1.4 kilograms of ganja
concealed on that vehicle. [@1]

During questioning, Srinivasan told police that he had bought the ganja from
Santhoshkumar from Settikarichiyur. [@1] Acting on that statement, police
surrounded and arrested Santhoshkumar as he stood with a motorcycle in a sorghum
field near Emkalipatti. [@1] Police seized 3 kilograms of ganja that he had
hidden in the sorghum field and in a cattle shed. [@1] Along with that stored
ganja, police also seized a motorcycle and a weighing machine from his
possession. [@1]

After arresting both young men, police produced them before the Mettur court,
which remanded them to Salem Central Prison. [@1]
