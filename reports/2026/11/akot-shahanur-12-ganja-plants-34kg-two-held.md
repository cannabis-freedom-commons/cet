Akot Rural police went to the house of Sachin Jambhale at Shahanur on a
confidential tip that ganja was being grown behind it, among a tur (pigeon pea)
crop. [@1] Officers raided the house on Monday 16 November 2026, with the
superintendent of police's permission. [@1] They seized 12 cannabis plants,
small and large, with leaves, flowers, seeds, buds, stems and roots, weighing 34
kg 60 g in total. [@1] Police estimated the value of the seized plants at about
Rs 6.80 lakh. [@1] Police took two men into custody: Sachin Laxman Jambhale, 40,
of Shahanur, and Anilal Lalji Dhandekar, 39, of Somthana in Chikhaldara taluka,
who was living at Shahanur. [@1] A case was registered against both men at Akot
Rural police station. [@1]

The case was registered under sections 8(c) and 20(b) of the Narcotic Drugs and
Psychotropic Substances Act 1985, which make cultivating, possessing and selling
ganja without a licence a serious, non-bailable offence. [@1] The report noted
that more than 20 kg of ganja carries at least 10 to 20 years' rigorous
imprisonment and a fine of Rs 1 lakh to Rs 2 lakh. [@1]

Akola district police described the raid as a success for Operation Prahar, its
campaign against the illegal narcotics trade. [@2] Police said the operation was
carried out on the orders of superintendent of police Archit Chandak, under
additional superintendent Nilesh Pandey and Akot subdivision assistant
superintendent Nikhil Patil. [@2]
