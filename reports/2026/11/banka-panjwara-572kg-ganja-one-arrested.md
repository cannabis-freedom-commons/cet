Panjwara police in Banka, Bihar, arrested Laudhiya village resident Phantus Sah
in the Panjwara police station area after accusing him of smuggling ganja. [@1]
Police identified the detained man by name as Phantus Sah and gave his home as
Laudhiya village. [@1]

Officers reported a total seizure of 5 kg 720 g of ganja in connection with the
accused. [@1] During a search of his house, police recovered about half a
kilogram of ganja along with 40 chilams, small clay smoking pipes. [@1]

From the same house officers also seized a taraju-batkhara balance-and-weights
set and a pair of scissors found with the drug. [@1] Officers also seized Rs
6,100 in cash and one mobile phone from the house during the same search, and
the available account reports no charge or court decision yet. [@1]
