Carabinieri from the Terzigno station arrested a 65-year-old local man on
suspicion of drug dealing on 20 November 2026. [@1] Officers had noticed a car
parked under the man's home with another man waiting inside, and followed it
when the 65-year-old got in. [@1] The car stopped near a farmhouse in a rural
area, and the man went inside briefly before officers blocked both men and
searched the scene. [@1]

The search found a jar holding 15 doses of cocaine and crack. [@1] One dose of
hashish was found in the driver's possession. [@3] The 65-year-old was arrested
on suspicion of possessing drugs for the purpose of dealing. [@3]

The driver was not arrested; he was reported to the Prefettura, the provincial
interior ministry office, as a drug user. [@2] The operation was part of the
Carabinieri's anti-drug work across the Vesuvian area around Naples. [@1]
