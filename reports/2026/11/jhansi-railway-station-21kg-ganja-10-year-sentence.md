A special NDPS court in Jhansi sentenced a ganja smuggler to 10 years of
rigorous imprisonment. [@1,2] The case was heard by Special Judge (NDPS) / Additional Sessions Judge Om Pal Singh, with Deepak Tiwari as special public prosecutor. [@2] The convicted smuggler was identified as Inkar alias Amir Khan, son of Beche Hasan, aged 20, originally from Kanha Nagla in Binawar, Badaun, and then living in North East Delhi. [@2] The court also fined the convicted smuggler 100,000
rupees for the same cannabis offence. [@1,2] The sentencing order additionally
requires three months of additional jail if the fine is not paid. [@1,2] The court
acted on a Saturday under the NDPS Act. [@1]

The case arose after the smuggler was caught at Jhansi railway station with 21
kilograms and 300 grams of ganja. [@1] Officers found 8.470 kilograms of ganja in one jute bag and 12.860 kilograms in the second bag. [@2] The seized quantity equals 21.3 kilograms
of cannabis flower in total. [@1] The arrest at the railway station took place
about four years before the November 2026 sentencing. [@1] After the arrest a case was registered, the man was produced and sent to jail, and a charge sheet was filed after investigation. [@2] After the tip, a sub-inspector informed senior officers, the railway circle officer arrived, and the police party encircled and caught the man on platforms 4/5. [@2] On 6 April 2022, acting on an informer's tip during patrol, officers found the man with jute bags near the under-construction lift on platforms 4/5 toward the Bhopal end. [@2] The reported seizure
quantity for this case is 21 kilograms and 300 grams. [@1]

The prosecution was brought under India’s Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic
Substances Act legislation. [@1] He was convicted under sections 8(C) read with 20(B)(ii)(C) of the NDPS Act. [@2] The railway station named is Jhansi railway
station in Jhansi city, Uttar Pradesh. [@1] The substance in the case is
described as ganja. [@1]
