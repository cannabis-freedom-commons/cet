The head of a drug-trafficking network operating between France and Germany ran
the business from prison, with orders and deliveries organised notably via
Snapchat, police said after a long investigation. [@1] In a first operation with
Raid officers, police in France arrested 13 people. [@1] At the same time German
police arrested nine people suspected of taking part in the traffic. [@1] In
France the 13 suspects were brought before the authorities, of whom six were
jailed and five placed under judicial supervision. [@1] Of the 13 suspects
arrested on 3 November in the French case, 11 were brought before an
investigating judge with a view to being charged, the Nancy prosecutor's office
said. [@2]

Searches in France seized more than 35.5 kg of cannabis, 1.8 kg of heroin,
cocaine, ketamine, two firearms and 22,590 euros in cash. [@1] In Germany
officers uncovered five plantations and seized 2,349 cannabis plants, alongside
nearly 25 kg of amphetamines, two firearms and 21,000 euros in cash. [@1] The
Nancy deputy prosecutor separately put the French cannabis seizure at 31.4 kilos
plus ketamine, cocaine and a few grams of heroin. [@2]

The network operating between Germany and Meurthe-et-Moselle was dismantled on
Tuesday 3 November in a vast simultaneous raid in both countries. [@3]
