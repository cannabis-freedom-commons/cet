Teams of the Força Tática, the Military Police's tactical unit in the 9th
Battalion, found about 53.335 kilograms of marijuana at a freight company in
Cachoeiro de Itapemirim on Wednesday 18 November 2026. [@1] No one was arrested
in the operation. [@3] Police had received information that people were using
freight companies to send drugs from southern Brazil to Cachoeiro de Itapemirim.
[@1]

The marijuana was pressed into 67 tablets and hidden in three boxes sent through
a transportadora, a freight and parcel company. [@1] The sniffer dog Kyara, of
the battalion's K9 team, indicated the three suspect boxes. [@1] With the
manager's authorisation the officers opened the parcels and found the 67
tablets, which weighed about 53.335 kilograms. [@1] The company's name was not
released, at the company's own request. [@1]

Officers then went to the address given on the parcels to try to identify the
recipients. [@1] A resident there said he did not know the material and that the
people named as addressees were not at the property. [@1] The marijuana was
taken to the 7th Regional Police Station in Cachoeiro de Itapemirim. [@1]

The 9th Battalion said the seizure was among the most significant it had made
this year. [@1] The commander of the 9th Battalion, lieutenant-colonel Nério,
said tackling drug trafficking remains one of the unit's operational priorities.
[@2] The battalion command said the seizure dealt a financial blow to the
criminal structure behind the drugs and kept the load from being distributed and
sold in the region. [@4] Police said they keep up operations against drug sales
in neighbourhoods and against large consignments supplying criminal
organisations. [@1]
