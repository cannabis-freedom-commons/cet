Police in Raipur, Dehradun, arrested a youth on suspicion of ganja smuggling.
[@1] The accused was identified as 27-year-old Parwana Nath, a resident of
Sapera Basti, Mothrowala. [@1] Officers caught him near Ghoda Factory Ground in
the Balawala chowki area. [@1]

On the night of 15 November, a Balawala police team was conducting checking in
the area. [@1] During the checking, an informer told the team that a youth near
Ghoda Factory Ground was involved in ganja smuggling. [@1] A search of his
possession recovered 1 kg 42 grams of ganja, or 1.042 kg. [@1] Police then took
him to the police station. [@1]

Police registered crime No. 478/2026 against him at Raipur kotwali under
sections 8/20 of the NDPS Act. [@1] The action team comprised Balawala chowki
in-charge Sub-Inspector Praveen Pundir, Head Constable Vinod Kumar and Constable
Rajesh Rawat. [@1]
