The Ernakulam excise enforcement and anti-narcotic special squad arrested two
students and a robotics engineer in two separate drug cases on Thursday. [@1] In
the cannabis case, officers arrested Akshay, an 18-year-old BCom student from
Kathrikadavu, at a lodge on MG Road in Kochi. [@1] The officers seized 1.08 kg
of ganja from him. [@1] They alleged that he sold ganja targeting students in
Kochi city. [@1] The raids were conducted as part of Operation Thunder by a team
led by inspector Kannan K S. [@1]

The other case involved Sayooj, a 21-year-old third-year LLB student from
Maradu. [@1] He and his associate Aromal, a 28-year-old robotics engineer from
Kovoor in Varkala, were arrested from a lodge in Tripunithura. [@1] The team
seized nearly 5 g of MDMA from them, valued at around Rs 30,000. [@1] Officers
alleged that the two were members of a group selling MDMA in and around
Tripunithura. [@1]
