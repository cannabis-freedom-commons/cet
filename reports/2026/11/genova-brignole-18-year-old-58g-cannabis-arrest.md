Genoa Mobile Squad officers arrested an 18-year-old of Tunisian origin on 16
November 2026 on suspicion of possessing drugs for dealing in the area in front
of Brignole station. [@1,2]

Officers staked out the building where the young man lived, already suspected of
selling drugs in the area, before entering the flat to search it. [@1,3] The officers were in plain clothes and rode motorcycles during the surveillance. [@3] On
entering, officers found a precision scale and material for packaging doses on a
chair. [@1,3] Police dog units then found the rest of the drugs hidden in several
parts of the flat and in the bathroom ceiling. [@1,3] Officers seized 58 grams of
cannabis, 42 grams of cocaine already divided into doses, and the cash. [@1,2,3] The
cash amounted to 3,000 euros, hidden among the crevices of the flat and in the
bathroom ceiling. [@1]

He was tried on the morning of 17 November with the direct procedure (rito
direttissimo), Italy's fast-track hearing for arrests in flagrante. [@1]
