Two youths were arrested on charges of attempt to murder after they allegedly
rammed their motorcycle into an Excise Inspector during a routine vehicle
inspection in Idukki district. [@1] The two were Tence, 23, of Kurishankal
House, Punnamattam, Muvattupuzha, and Rohit, 22, of Thuruthumalil House,
Mutharamkunnu, Kumaramangalam. [@1] Thodupuzha Excise Inspector Lijo Oommen
suffered swelling and a ligament injury to his leg and was treated at a private
hospital in Thodupuzha. [@1]

The collision happened at about 6.30 pm on Friday near Mattuppara in
Kumaramangalam, where Lijo Oommen and his team were checking vehicles. [@1]
According to the complaint, the riders were travelling from Para towards
Mylacomb, ignored signals to stop and rammed their motorcycle into the officer,
throwing him and both riders onto the road. [@1] Inspector V R Rajesh and Civil
Excise Officer A R Arun, on duty with Lijo Oommen, immediately took the two into
custody. [@1] The excise team recovered seven grams of ganja from their
possession. [@1] The two were initially released on station bail. [@1]

On a formal complaint lodged by Lijo Oommen, Thodupuzha police intercepted and
arrested the two as they returned after securing bail, and registered a case of
attempt to murder against them. [@1] The two were produced before the court on
Saturday night. [@1]
