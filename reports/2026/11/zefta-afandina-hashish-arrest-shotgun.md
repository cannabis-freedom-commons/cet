Detectives at Zefta police station in Gharbia Governorate arrested a man known
as "Afandina" whom they suspected of dealing ice, hydro and hashish across the
town and neighbouring villages. [@1] Gharbia security director Rashad Farouk
received a report from the Zefta police chief that intelligence had identified a
suspect trading ice, hydro and hashish to young people in villages, on their
roads and in Zefta itself. [@1] The suspect, identified as Fathy A. and known as
"Afandina", lived in the al-Dawran area of Zefta and was said to have an earlier
criminal record in several cases. [@1]

Officers set up fixed and mobile ambushes in the Izbat al-Ma'in area and stopped
the motorcycle he was riding. [@1] In a bag he was carrying, officers found a
large quantity of assorted drugs, including ice, hydro and hashish, prepared for
sale. [@1] They also seized cash, mobile phones suspected of being used to
contact clients, a shotgun with cartridges and the motorcycle used in the trade.
[@1]

According to the arrest report, he admitted holding the drugs for trafficking,
that the money came from sales, that the phones were for contacting clients and
that he carried the weapon for protection. [@1] He was taken to the Zefta police
station and the Public Prosecution ordered him detained pending investigation,
holding the seized items and the motorcycle. [@1]
