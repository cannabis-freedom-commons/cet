Richard Calvert, 26, of Stenson Park, Farranshone, appeared before Judge Eamonn
Carroll at the District Court from Limerick Prison custody. [@1] He pleaded
guilty to three drugs charges arising from a large cannabis seizure in March
2026. [@1] He pleaded guilty to Section 15, Section 15A and Section 3 of the
Misuse of Drugs Act 1977. [@1] The DPP had directed trial on indictment at the
Circuit Court, and Calvert was sent forward for sentencing after the guilty
pleas. [@1]

On 5 March at approximately 6.30pm, Gardaí searched a vehicle in Limerick city.
[@1] A significant six-figure sum of cannabis was discovered, according to
Inspector Gearoid Thompson. [@1] Gardaí seized around €100,000 worth of cannabis
during a search on a car in Limerick City. [@2] A man in his 20s was arrested in
connection with the seizure of around €100,000 worth of cannabis in Limerick
City. [@2] The cannabis herb seizure resulted from a search on a car in the city
area at around 6.30pm on March 5. [@2] The arrested man was detained under
Section 2 of the Criminal Justice (Drug Trafficking) Act 1996, and the drugs
were to be sent to Forensic Science Ireland for analysis. [@2]

The seizure and arrest were carried out under Operation Tara, a nationwide
anti-drugs strategy. [@2] Gardaí said investigations were ongoing following the
search and seizure. [@2]
