Officers from the Gonesse anti-crime brigade (Brigade anticriminalité, BAC)
stopped a 17-year-old driver in Gonesse, Val-d'Oise, on 12 November 2026 after
seeing him commit a traffic offence. [@1] The check showed he was driving
without a licence. [@1] Alerted by a suspicious smell, officers searched him and
found a pouch containing narcotics. [@1] He was placed in police custody (garde
à vue) and, after custody, was brought before a magistrate of the Pontoise
prosecutor's office before being released under judicial supervision (contrôle
judiciaire) to await judgment while the investigation continues. [@1]

A judicial police officer ordered a search of his home in Gonesse, where
officers found drugs packaged ready for sale. [@1] They seized 2 kg of cannabis
resin, eight pouches of cannabis resin weighing about 18 g and 18 pouches of
cannabis herb weighing about 41 g, a cannabis total of about 2.059 kg. [@1]
Officers also seized four doses of cocaine weighing about 4 g, which is not
counted in the cannabis figures. [@1]

The youth, who was not previously known to police, may have acted as a nourrice,
someone storing drugs at home, according to a source close to the case. [@1] The
police union Alliance Police Nationale 95 publicly congratulated the Gonesse BAC
officers for the seizure and their daily work against drug trafficking. [@1]
