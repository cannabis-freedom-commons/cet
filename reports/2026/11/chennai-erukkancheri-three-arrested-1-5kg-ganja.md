Police in the Erukkancheri area of Chennai arrested three men from the same
neighbourhood for allegedly selling cannabis, which is locally known as ganja.
[@1] The three men were identified as Sarathkumar, aged 29, Bharath, aged 26,
and Gnani, aged 28, all described as residents of the Erukkancheri area. [@1]
According to police, the three had been engaged in selling ganja within
Erukkancheri before officers detained them there the previous day. [@1]

During the detention, officers seized 1.5 kilograms of ganja from the men and
recorded it as the quantity recovered in the Erukkancheri operation. [@1] The
1.5 kg of ganja was the cannabis quantity police attributed to the three men
together in this Erukkancheri enforcement action. [@1]

After the arrests, the three accused sellers remained in police custody while
the ganja-sale matter stood at the investigation stage of the case. [@1] At that
point the case against the three men had not reached trial, conviction or
sentence. [@1]
