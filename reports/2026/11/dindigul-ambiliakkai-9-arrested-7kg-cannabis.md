Police in Dindigul district, Tamil Nadu, arrested nine people near Ambiliakkai
on suspicion of trafficking cannabis. [@1] Nine men standing suspiciously in the
Ellaiparai Kulam area tried to flee when they saw the police. [@1] Officers
surrounded and caught them. [@1]

Those detained included men from Uttar Pradesh and Odisha, aged 21 to 32. [@1]
Police seized 7 kg of cannabis, 200,000 rupees in cash, two two-wheelers and two
cars from them. [@1] The nine were detained in the Chinnaiyagoundanvalasu and
Ellaiparai Kulam area while engaged in cannabis trafficking and sale. [@3]

The operation was ordered by Dindigul district superintendent of police S.
Jayakumar after a secret tip that cannabis was being stored and trafficked near
Ambiliakkai. [@2] A team led by Oddanchatram deputy superintendent of police
Devaraj, with inspector Lingapandian and sub-inspector Savadamuthu, carried out
the search on the afternoon of 22 November. [@2] The nine were taken to
Ambiliakkai police station, where a case was registered and questioning
continued. [@2] Police were preparing to produce them in court for remand. [@2]
