Two suspects escaped on the night of 17 November 2026 after noticing a Military
Police patrol in a wooded area between Parque Jacinta and Santa Clara in
Teresina's South Zone, and officers did not locate them. [@1] The patrol was
conducted by a team from the 22nd Battalion of the Military Police in an area
described as known for drug-selling points called “bocas de fumo”. [@1] Officers
said they saw two men walking along a dark road, one of them already known to
the authorities and carrying a purple bag. [@1]

When the patrol moved closer, both suspects turned and fled on foot, abandoning
the purple bag and its contents in the wooded area. [@1] Officers recovered the
abandoned material, identified as skunk and cannabis seeds, during a search of
the spot where the pair had been seen walking. [@1]

The team seized the drugs found after the flight and delivered them to the
Central de Flagrantes in Teresina. [@1] Neither of the two men was located or
arrested during the operation. [@1]
