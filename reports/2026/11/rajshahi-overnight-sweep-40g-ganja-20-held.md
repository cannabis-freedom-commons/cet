Rajshahi Metropolitan Police arrested 20 people in overnight drives across
Rajshahi city on Monday 16 November 2026. [@1] Officers seized yaba, tapentadol
tablets and ganja during the overnight operation. [@1] City police spokesperson
Deputy Commissioner (Sadar) Md Gaziur Rahman confirmed the account on Tuesday
afternoon. [@1]

Damkura thana police arrested 35-year-old Md Firoz Hossain with 40 grams of
ganja in the Bindarampur area at about 7am. [@1] Police filed separate cases
under the Narcotics Control Act at the concerned thanas, and the accused in the
Paba and Damkura cases were sent to jail through the court. [@1]

In the same sweep police held Selim with 20 pieces of yaba in the Barabongram
Sufinagar area, and a court gave him one year's imprisonment after he admitted
the offence in a summary trial. [@1] Shahmakhdum thana police arrested Md
Shahin, 30, and Md Jamal alias Jamu, 33, with 17 pieces of yaba in the Paba
Natunpara area. [@1] Paba thana police arrested 19-year-old Md Bijoy with 75
tapentadol tablets in the Parila area, while his associate Majnu, 35, fled. [@1]
