Guardia Civil officers carried out six searches in Iznalloz and seized
approximately 1,000 marijuana plants. [@1,2] Several Guardia Civil units deployed
on the ground carried out the six searches. [@1] Officers had sufficient
evidence to travel to Iznalloz. [@1] Officers went with the clear purpose of
finding drugs. [@1] After a morning in which many neighbours noticed the police
presence, approximately 1,000 marijuana plants were seized. [@1] Five people
were placed at the disposal of the courts for drug trafficking. [@1,2] Eight other
people were investigated for defrauding the electricity supply. [@1,2]

The Iznalloz operation was carried out in the previous week. [@1] The action
formed part of the Guardia Civil's Alto Impacto service in Granada province,
which was launched on 15 September to strike criminal networks linked to drug
trafficking and marijuana cultivation and to remove weapons from circulation.
[@1]

The special service was expected to remain active until 15 December. [@1] Under
the service the Guardia Civil has been carrying out numerous searches every
week, seizing large quantities of marijuana plants and other narcotics. [@1]
