Police from the Iron station set up a checkpoint on road 6353 by Harish as part
of a proactive operation against drug sale, possession and distribution. [@1] In
one of the cars stopped for inspection officers carried out a search and found
dozens of packages of material suspected to be cocaine, ketamine, MDMA and
cannabis, alongside stamp bags and dozens of ecstasy pills. [@1] The suspect, a
35-year-old resident of Yehud, was arrested and taken for questioning at the
police station. [@1]

Police said the suspect was held on suspicion of possessing and dealing drugs.
[@4] He was brought before a court, which granted the police request to extend
his detention so that the investigation could continue. [@2] A court granted the
police request and extended his detention until 25 November 2026. [@1] Police
said they would continue to act against drug sale and distribution. [@3]
