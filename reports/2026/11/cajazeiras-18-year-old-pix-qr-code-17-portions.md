An 18-year-old man was arrested in flagrante on Monday 16 November 2026 in the
São Francisco neighbourhood of Cajazeiras, Paraíba, on suspicion of drug
trafficking. [@2] The arrest took place in the São Francisco neighbourhood of
Cajazeiras, in the Sertão da Paraíba region. [@1] Military police made the
arrest during joint patrols with the Águia 04, Força Tática 02 and Rádio
Patrulha teams on rua Vitória Bezerra, an area known for drug sales. [@2] When
officers spotted the man acting suspiciously next to a recycling depot, he threw
a blue bag to the ground and tried to run but was quickly caught. [@2]

Police seized 17 portions of maconha, R$37 in small notes, various empty
wrappings used to store drugs, a notebook with the trafficking accounts and a
printed QR Code for receiving Pix payments. [@2] Officers also found packaging,
a notebook with notes and money, and the QR Code drew attention because police
suspected buyers used it to make transfers. [@1]

The use of digital payments is one aspect the investigation will examine to
clarify the financial movements linked to the suspected activity. [@1] The
suspect was taken to the Civil Police station's Special Tactical Group (GTE) in
Cajazeiras for the applicable measures. [@2] The young man was detained and the
seized material was to undergo the procedures required for the investigation.
[@1] Authorities said they would check the contents of the notebook and whether
the records were linked to drug sales. [@1]
