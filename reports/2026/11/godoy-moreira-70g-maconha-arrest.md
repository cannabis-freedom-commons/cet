On the night of Friday 13 November, at about 19:00, Military Police arrested a
resident on Rua Cornélio Procópio in Godoy Moreira in an approach that seized 70
grams of maconha. [@1] According to the Military Police, the suspect was already
the target of complaints about drug trafficking in the municipality. [@1]

Officers on patrol spotted the man, who discarded an object and ran inside a
residence to try to avoid being approached when he saw the patrol car coming,
according to the occurrence bulletin. [@1] The team caught the suspect, who was
with his wife, and during searches of the grounds found about 70 grams of
maconha hidden under some roof tiles. [@1]

The man was arrested and taken with the seized drug to the police station in São
João do Ivaí for the appropriate procedures. [@1]
