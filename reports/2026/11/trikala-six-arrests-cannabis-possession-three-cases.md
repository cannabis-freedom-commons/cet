Six people were arrested in areas of the city of Trikala for alleged violations
of the legislation on narcotic drugs. [@1,2] The arrests followed control checks
carried out in Trikala by officers serving with the DIAS police team. [@1,2] The
detentions took place during the evening hours of Monday 16 November 2026 and
Tuesday 17 November 2026. [@1,2] Police handled the six detentions as three
different cases detected during those DIAS checks. [@1,2] All three cases were
recorded as cases of cannabis possession in Trikala. [@1]

The six people taken into custody were Greek nationals detained in the Trikala
areas covered by the checks. [@1,2] According to the police account, officers
found small quantities of unprocessed cannabis in the possession of the six
detainees. [@1,2] According to the same police account, the small cannabis
quantities found during the three cases were seized by the officers. [@1,2] The
six arrests in Trikala were therefore spread across three different possession
cases detected during DIAS checks. [@1]
