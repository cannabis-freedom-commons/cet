Military and Civil police carried out a joint operation on the morning of
Saturday 14 November in Teixeiras in the Zona da Mata. [@1] Around 11h50
officers executed a search-and-seizure warrant at number 107 Rua Antônio
Floresta Medina in the Alencar neighbourhood. [@1] Officers arrested a
21-year-old man during the operation, which also recorded an occurrence of
resistance. [@1] The detained man was identified by the initials E. M. F. and
was taken in for an occurrence recorded as drug trafficking and resistance. [@1]
The action involved officers of the 4th Platoon of the Military Police linked to
the 10th Independent Military Police Company, with Civil Police support. [@1]

Officers seized seven pins of a cocaine-like substance, five portions of
marijuana-like material and seven portions of a crack-like substance. [@1]
Officers also found a portion of about 250 grams of a cocaine-like substance.
[@1] The team also collected R$1,522 in cash, a precision scale and an iPhone
mobile phone. [@1]

The investigation is to clarify the circumstances of the seizure and the alleged
drug trafficking. [@1]
