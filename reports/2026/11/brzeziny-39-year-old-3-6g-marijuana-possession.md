Police in Brzeziny detained a 39-year-old resident of Brzeziny County during a
check on Modrzewskiego Street at about 20:00 on Saturday 14 November 2026. [@1]
During the check officers found a bag of dried plant material on him. [@1] A
preliminary examination identified the secured substance as marijuana weighing
3.6 grams. [@1] The man was detained and taken to the police command. [@1]

After he had sobered up, the 39-year-old heard a charge of possessing narcotic
drugs in the form of cannabis herb other than fibrous hemp, the Polish legal
term for marijuana as distinct from industrial hemp. [@2] Under Poland's
Anti-Drug Law the offence carries a maximum sentence of three years'
imprisonment. [@2] The arrest was one of two interventions carried out by
Brzeziny police over the preceding weekend, in which two men were detained. [@1]
