On 10 June 2026, detectives from the New Castle County Division of Police Active
Crime Trend Team tried to stop an Acura on Maryland Avenue for a traffic
violation. [@1] The Acura fled at high speed along Maryland Avenue, running
multiple red lights. [@1] A bag containing approximately 605 grams of marijuana
was thrown from the fleeing vehicle and recovered by officers from a sidewalk in
Wilmington. [@1] The driver was identified as a 24-year-old man from Wilmington,
DE. [@1]

The investigation led detectives to a residence in the 200 block of Portland
Avenue in the Woodcrest community. [@1] On 13 November 2026, detectives from the
Active Crime Trend Team executed a search warrant at that residence and took the
suspect into custody. [@1] During the search, officers recovered approximately
4,205 grams of packaged marijuana and 177 grams of cocaine, plus additional drug
paraphernalia. [@1] Officers also recovered a Delaware registration plate used
during the 10 June incident. [@1]

The suspect was also identified in connection with a separate vehicle pursuit in
Lower Merion Township, Pennsylvania, where police held an active warrant for
him. [@1] He was charged with felony counts including possession of a controlled
substance and possession with intent to deliver a controlled substance. [@1] He
was arraigned in Justice of the Peace Court 2 and committed to the Howard R.
Young Correctional Institution after failing to post $16,107 in unsecured bail
and $117,001 in cash bail. [@1] Police did not release the suspect's name or
photograph, citing legal limitations under House Bill 59. [@1]
