Five people were charged after police raided a house in the Peñi Trapum
neighbourhood of Cutral Co. [@2] The operation took place on the afternoon of
Friday 20 November 2026 and was carried out by the Comarca anti-narcotics
division of the Policía del Neuquén. [@3] The two men were aged 17 and 39 and
the three women 18, 44 and 58, and the police did not specify what role each was
alleged to have played. [@1] The investigation had begun on 11 November after
officers received information that a house was being used as a drug sales point.
[@1]

Police reported seizing 22 doses of cannabis sativa flowers. [@4] The same
search also produced about 670 doses of cocaine hydrochloride, a separate
substance not counted in the cannabis figure. [@1] Officers also found two
digital scales, two firearms and around 42 rounds of ammunition. [@1] They
additionally seized nine mobile phones, nylon cuttings used to portion the drugs
and two sedan cars, all incorporated into the case. [@1]

The raided property consisted of two independent apartments with separate
entrances, in an area where episodes of firearm violence had been recorded. [@1]
A specialised prosecutor's office authorised the search. [@4] The operation
falls under Neuquén's microtrafficking regime, established by provincial Law
3.488 in February 2025. [@1] The seized material was added to the case file
while the microtrafficking prosecutor's investigation continued. [@3]
