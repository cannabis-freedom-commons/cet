Officers from the drug-control unit of the Interior Ministry police department
in Armavir detained a 54-year-old local resident on Yamskaya Street. [@1] The
man was taken to the police department, where officers found a bag of
plant-based narcotic substance in his rucksack. [@1] The bag held more than 120
grams of marijuana. [@1]

The suspect told officers that he had acquired the drugs and had kept them for
personal use. [@1] The press service of the Armavir police department said a
criminal case had been opened under Article 228 of the Russian Criminal Code.
[@1] The police said the man faces up to 10 years in prison if convicted. [@1]
