The Federal Police Directorate Hannover ran a priority safety operation from 13
to 15 November 2026 at the central stations in Bremen, Hamburg and Hannover.
[@1] At Hannover station, officers stopped a 19-year-old and found narcotics and
an animal-defence spray on him. [@1] A search of his jacket pockets found a
consumption unit of marijuana weighing just under 30 grams and a foil-wrapped
portion of cocaine. [@1] Officers seized the items found on him. [@1] Police are
investigating him on suspicion of violating the Narcotics Act, section 29 BtMG.
[@1]

At Hamburg station on Friday evening, officers stopped a youth and secured
several prohibited items and narcotics. [@1] The check showed the 14-year-old
had violated an existing ban on going out. [@1] A search found four knives and
four category F3 fireworks, whose possession and use are prohibited for minors
and without a permit. [@1] He was also carrying cannabis and another substance
suspected to be a narcotic. [@1] He now faces investigations for violations of
the Weapons Act, the Explosives Act and the Narcotics Act. [@1]

The checks took place overnight from Friday to Saturday and Saturday to Sunday,
in each case from 18:00 to 03:00 at the three central stations. [@1]
