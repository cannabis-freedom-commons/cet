Officers arrested Mustakali Mehboobali Saiyad, who lives on the Rajarani Lake
embankment behind the GEB office in Panigate, after finding 83.40 grams of
charas valued by police at Rs 20,850 in his possession. [@1] Police declared
Mohammadhusen alias Mohsin Janmahmmad Sheikh, who lives behind the GEB office at
Rajarani Lake in Panigate, wanted in connection with the ganja seizure. [@1] His
mother, who was present at the house, told police that Mohammadhusen had gone to
Rajpipla. [@1]

In a second case in the same Rajarani Lake area, officers seized 29.39 grams of
ganja valued by police at Rs 1,470. [@1] Police valued the 83.40 grams of charas
at Rs 20,850 and the 29.39 grams of ganja at Rs 1,470. [@1] Panigate police
raided the area after receiving a tip that drugs were being sold there. [@1]

The Rajarani Lake area is described as notorious for the sale of intoxicating
substances, and Panigate police raided it yesterday. [@1] The raid was carried
out the day before the 23 November 2026 publication, placing the operation on 22
November 2026. [@1]
