Manjeshwaram police arrested three men after their car crashed into a
supermarket at Uppala Kaikamba during a police chase. [@1] A joint team of the
district police chief's special DANSAF squad and Manjeshwaram police made the
arrests. [@1] Police said the three were accused in several cases including drug
trafficking and had been under surveillance for days. [@1]

The crash happened at about 10:30 am on Wednesday 25 November 2026 on the Uppala
Kaikamba Bayar road. [@3] Police said the squad followed the car after a tip
that a large quantity of drugs was being smuggled from Karnataka into the Uppala
area. [@2] The car lost control and hit the front veranda of a roadside shop
called MR Traders at Kaikamba, police said. [@3] A search of the car found six
grams of ganja and four grams of MDMA. [@1]

The three told police they had brought the drugs from Bengaluru for sale and
use. [@3] Police also took two glass tubes kept for drug use and four mobile
phones from the accused. [@3] Manjeshwaram police registered a case under the
NDPS Act. [@3]

The crash and arrests were part of Operation Toofan, the district's continuing
anti-drug drive. [@1] Police said they were questioning the three about where
the drugs came from, for whom they were brought and whether others were
involved. [@1]
