Three men were arrested in Himmatnagar, Sabarkantha district, Gujarat, after the
Anti-Narcotics Task Force (ANTF) acted on a tip-off and found them with ganja
and charas. [@1] Police valued the seized ganja (dried cannabis) and charas
(cannabis resin) at about Rs 21,815. [@1] The ANTF's Gandhinagar zone carried
out the raid on suspicion of drug sales. [@2] A case was registered against four
people: the three held at the scene and another man linked to the network. [@1]

Officers seized 67.340 grams of ganja, 13.410 grams of charas and 9.920 grams of
ganja. [@2] The four accused were booked under sections 8(C), 20(B)(ii)(A),
21(A) and 29 of the NDPS Act. [@2] The operation was carried out by a team led
by ANTF Gandhinagar zone police inspector N.R. Patel. [@2] Police said the ganja
and charas being sold had been obtained from a man in Visnagar, Mehsana
district. [@2]

The detainees were questioned about where the consignment came from and who was
to receive it. [@1] Police said checks against drug trafficking in the district
would continue. [@1]
