Lincoln Parish Sheriff's Office deputies arrested Damion McConnell, 32, of Hodge
at 5:01 p.m. on 15 November 2026. [@1] Ruston police had gone to the residence
to check for gunshot victims and entry or exit holes while investigating a
reported drive-by shooting. [@1]

In the kitchen they saw digital scales and a clear plastic baggie of marijuana
by the microwave, and a partially opened drawer beneath it held suspected
marijuana and U.S. currency. [@1] McConnell told an agent the marijuana belonged
to him, and that “Tabs” inside the home were his as well. [@1] A search warrant
obtained after the interview turned up a large amount of marijuana and 10
hydrocodone 10 mg units in an unlabeled pill bottle in the kitchen, along with a
large amount of U.S. currency. [@1] Inside a safe in a utility room, officers
found more baggies of marijuana and more currency. [@1] Digital scales in the
kitchen had marijuana residue. [@1]

The agent wrote that the amount of marijuana and hydrocodone and the
paraphernalia were believed conducive to drug trafficking, and the residence was
within 2,000 feet of a drug-free zone, a government housing property. [@1] He
was taken to the Lincoln Parish Narcotics Enforcement Team office and then to
Lincoln Parish Detention Center. [@1] He was booked on possession with intent to
distribute a Schedule I drug (marijuana), possession with intent to distribute a
Schedule II drug (hydrocodone), possession of drug paraphernalia and violation
of the uniform controlled dangerous substances law in a drug-free zone. [@1]
