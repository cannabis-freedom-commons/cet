Carabineros officers from the OS7 unit detained seven people in an anti-drug
operation in central Coquimbo. [@1] The operation included searches of three
properties and was carried out in coordination with the Local Prosecutor's
Office as part of an investigation into drug-trafficking offences. [@1] Those
detained were five women and two men aged between 18 and 60. [@1] Carabineros
linked the arrests to small-quantity drug trafficking, illegal possession of
firearms and an outstanding arrest warrant, and said the group had 14 prior
police records between them. [@1]

During the searches officers found 440 wrappers of cocaine paste weighing more
than 109 grams and another 43.6 grams of marijuana. [@1] Officers also seized a
revolver without a serial number, $1,563,800 in cash and a mobile phone
connected to the investigation. [@1]

Among those detained were five Chilean citizens, one Colombian citizen and one
Venezuelan woman, with police saying the two foreign nationals had irregular
migration status in Chile. [@1] A 60-year-old Chilean woman among those detained
had an outstanding arrest warrant from the Coquimbo Guarantee Court for
small-quantity drug trafficking. [@1] Six of the seven people arrested were
brought before the court on Thursday for a detention-control hearing, while the
procedural situation of the seventh person was not specified. [@1]
