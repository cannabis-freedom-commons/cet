Jhalokati district's Detective Branch arrested four people and seized 90 grams
of ganja in an anti-drug drive on the night of Saturday 21 November 2026. [@1]
Officers stopped them at about 9:50 pm on the paved road in front of Badsha
Mia's tea shop at CNB Bazar in the Sreemantakathi area of Shekherhat union,
Jhalokati Sadar upazila. [@1] Those held were named as Md Sajib Majhi and Md
Rajib Hossain, also known as Zahirul, of Baidharapur in Jhalokati Sadar upazila,
and Rafat Howlader and Md Bappi of the City Park area of Jhalokati municipality.
[@1] Police also named Md Raihan Talukder of Sreemantakathi, son of the late
Hanif Talukder, as an absconding accused on a charge of helping to sell the
drug. [@1]

Detective Branch Sub-Inspector Md Humayun Kabir led the drive with other
officers acting on a tip-off, and filed the case as complainant at Jhalokati
Sadar police station. [@1] The four detained face charges under section 19(ka)
of the schedule to section 36(1) of the Narcotics Control Act 2018, and the
absconding accused under the relevant section of the same law. [@1]

A second outlet, Sheersha News, carried the same operation, reporting that the
district DB detained four men with 90 grams of ganja at Sreemantakathi and named
another man as an absconding accused. [@2]
