A special NDPS court in Jhansi, presided over by Special Judge Ompal Singh,
sentenced four people convicted of cannabis trafficking to the time they had
already spent in jail. [@1] About three years earlier all four had been caught
smuggling ganja at different places. [@1]

Prosecutors said Lahchura police station arrested Bharat Singh Yadav on 8 April
2023, catching him on Garotha road with 400 grams of ganja. [@1] He spent six
days in jail, admitted his guilt, and was sentenced to the time served plus a
10,000-rupee fine. [@1] Arvind Rajput was arrested with 300 grams of ganja,
spent 21 days in jail and was also fined 10,000 rupees. [@1] In the same police
station area, Praveen Mishra was caught with 300 grams of ganja and received a
time-served sentence and a 10,000-rupee fine. [@1] Mahadev Rai was arrested with
1.1 kilograms of ganja, spent one month and ten days in jail and was fined
10,000 rupees. [@1]
