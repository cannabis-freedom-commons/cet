Officers of the General Trias Component City Police Station arrested a resident
identified only as alias Mel after recovering a caliber .38 revolver, four live
rounds of ammunition and suspected illegal drugs. [@1] The operation in General
Trias City on Wednesday evening, 18 November targeted an allegedly illegal
firearm and led to the discovery of suspected marijuana and kush. [@1] The
search was conducted at approximately 7:30 p.m. under a warrant issued by
Regional Trial Court Branch 23 in Trece Martires City to recover an unlicensed
firearm. [@1] Intelligence operatives and Sector 3 personnel of the city police
station conducted the operation under the supervision of acting police chief Lt.
Col. Archimedes Baking. [@1]

The seized substances consisted of two sachets of suspected marijuana weighing
approximately 28.40 grams, valued at ₱3,408, and two sachets of suspected kush
weighing 22.90 grams, valued at ₱68,250. [@1] The combined estimated value of
the suspected drugs reached ₱71,658. [@1] Investigators found the suspected
drugs beside the firearm inside a sling bag during implementation of the search
warrant. [@1]

The warrant issued on 12 November covered an alleged violation of Republic Act
10591, the Comprehensive Firearms and Ammunition Regulation Act. [@1]
Authorities marked, inventoried and photographed the evidence in the presence of
required witnesses, including a barangay official and a media representative,
under procedures of Republic Act 9165. [@1] Police are preparing complaints
against the suspect for alleged violations of RA 10591 and Section 11, Article
II of RA 9165, which penalizes illegal possession of dangerous drugs. [@1] The
suspect and confiscated evidence remain under the custody of the General Trias
Component City Police Station pending documentation and proceedings. [@1]
Authorities said the return of the search warrant would also be submitted to the
issuing court. [@1]
