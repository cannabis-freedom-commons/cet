Customers came to buy drugs at the home of Guillaume Romeuf, 42, in the centre
of Tallende, in the Puy-de-Dôme, a trade that soon attracted attention. [@1] The
Clermont-Ferrand criminal court tried him in an immediate-appearance hearing
(comparution immédiate, a fast-track trial) on Monday 16 November 2026 and
sentenced him to five years in prison. [@1] He was sentenced as a legal
reoffender (récidive légale), meaning he already had a previous conviction. [@1]
At the hearing he answered almost none of the presiding judge's questions,
though he had largely admitted the facts during police custody (garde à vue) in
September. [@1] His lawyer, Jean-Hubert Portejoie, was taken aback by his
client's attitude at the hearing. [@1]

In a gendarmerie operation, officers found 4.7 kg of cannabis herb and 1.5 kg of
cannabis resin at his home. [@1] The same search turned up 8.5 kg of
amphetamines, 5 kg of ecstasy and 123 LSD tablets. [@1] Officers also found
15,410 euros in cash and weapons and ammunition hidden at the house. [@1] The
prosecutor said the search had opened Pandora's box. [@1]
