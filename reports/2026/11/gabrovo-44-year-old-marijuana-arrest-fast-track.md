Police in Gabrovo detained a 44-year-old local man during a drugs operation in
the city on 25 November 2026. [@1] Officers from the criminal police sector of
the Gabrovo district police directorate checked the man, who had a previous
conviction for theft. [@2]

A dry green leafy mass found on him reacted to marijuana in a field drug test.
[@2] In the procedural actions that followed, officers found and seized more
marijuana at his home. [@2]

He was detained for up to 24 hours under the Interior Ministry Act, and police
opened fast-track proceedings against him. [@2] The Gabrovo regional directorate
of the Interior Ministry reported the case in its information bulletin of 26
November 2026. [@2] A second Gabrovo outlet, Gabrovo Dnes, reported the same
case and described the material found on the man as reacting to marijuana in a
field test. [@3]
