Local police in La Spezia arrested a 29-year-old Moroccan man who lives in the
city and regularly resides in Italy. [@1] Plainclothes officers stopped him near
the 2 Giugno school complex as part of anti-dealing checks focused on areas
frequented by students. [@1] The stop took place on the afternoon before the 14
November report. [@1]

Officers found several doses of cocaine in a cigarette container in his jacket.
[@1] Officers then extended the check to the place where he habitually stayed,
which was different from his registered address. [@1] With support from Jazz,
the command’s anti-drug dog, the search found more cocaine and hashish weighing
just under 100 grams in total. [@1] Officers also recovered a precision scale
and material used to package individual doses. [@1]

Preliminary chemical-reagent tests were positive for both substances. [@1] The
man was arrested on suspicion of unlawful possession of drugs for dealing and
was referred to the judicial authority for a fast-track trial. [@1] He was
placed at the disposal of the judicial authority for a giudizio direttissimo.
