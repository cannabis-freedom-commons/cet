Police in Saladas, Corrientes, raided a house during an investigation into a
reported theft and recovered part of the property denounced as stolen. [@1] A
man was left subject to the case on the orders of the prosecutor's office that
directed the investigation. [@1] The operation was carried out by officers of
the local Comisaría Segunda under an order from judge César Daniel Romero. [@2]

Officers seized a dinner set of 16 white porcelain plates with orange floral
centres, 14 medium and two small, matching the objects reported stolen. [@1] In
the same house police found three plants that, from their characteristics and
smell, were believed to be cannabis sativa, and seized them preventively. [@1]
They also seized a disassembled 5.5-calibre rifle, presumed to be an air rifle,
with its metal mechanism separated from the wooden stock. [@1]

The case continued in order to establish the circumstances of the theft and
determine responsibility. [@3]
