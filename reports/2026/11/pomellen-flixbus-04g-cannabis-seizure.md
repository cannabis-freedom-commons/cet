Federal police checked a 50-year-old Polish citizen in a Flixbus at the Pomellen
border checkpoint on Saturday morning. [@1] A warrant from the Kleve prosecution
was outstanding against him for unauthorised import of narcotics combined with
driving without a licence and handling stolen goods. [@1] He was to pay a 7200
euro fine and 396.31 euros in procedural costs. [@1] Ninety days of imprisonment
in lieu of payment had been set for non-payment. [@1] He was arrested and taken
to the Pomellen federal police station. [@1]

Officers searching him seized 3.8 grams of amphetamine and 0.4 grams of
cannabis. [@1] Reports were filed under the Narcotics Act and the Consumer
Cannabis Act for the finds. [@1]

After paying the fine, the 50-year-old was released from the measures at 04:25.
[@1] Federal police spokesperson Igor Weber announced the checks on Monday. [@1]
