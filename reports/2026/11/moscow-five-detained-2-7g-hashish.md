Moscow police detained five young people suspected of attempting to distribute
drugs in the capital. [@1] The press service of the Internal Affairs Directorate
for the North-Eastern Administrative District reported the operation. [@1]
Officers initially detained two citizens aged 20 and 17 and, in a personal
search, seized a packet holding 2.7 grams of hashish. [@1] Three more
participants in the scheme, aged 18 to 22 and including one of the organisers,
were identified later. [@1]

The young men acted in an organised group whose organiser bought large batches
of drugs, recruited distributors, taught them to make stash caches and divided
the proceeds. [@1] A search of an apartment on proezd Serebryakova, rented for
the activity, found more than 460 grams of mephedrone. [@1]

A criminal case was opened for attempted crime and for illegal production, sale
or shipment of narcotic drugs under parts 3 of Article 30 and 5 of Article 228.1
of the Russian Criminal Code. [@1] Two of the suspects were remanded in custody
while a preventive measure for the others was still being decided. [@1]
