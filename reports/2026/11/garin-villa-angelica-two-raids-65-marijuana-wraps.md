Two court-authorised searches of homes in barrio Villa Angélica, in Garín,
seized 77 wraps of cocaine and 65 wraps of marijuana during an investigation
into alleged drug dealing. [@1] The investigation began with an anonymous
complaint to the municipal 0800 Escobar Sin Drogas line. [@1] The Escobar
drug-trafficking investigations sub-unit led the inquiry, gathering evidence and
identifying two addresses allegedly linked to drug sales. [@1]

Officers also seized a scale bearing cocaine traces, nylon cuttings, documents
of interest to the case and five shotgun cartridges from the two properties.
[@1] A Municipal Police K-9 dog named Yandel located items of interest for the
investigation in the boot of a vehicle. [@1] Municipal Police held the perimeter
and gave operational support through Security Secretariat areas, joined by the
COE, the GOE, the Motorised Division and the Scanner Mobile plus Buenos Aires
provincial police units, with a municipal ambulance deployed. [@1]

On the basis of the seized material, investigators requested the arrest of the
people under investigation for alleged links to drug dealing. [@1] The case
involves the Ayudantía Fiscal de Estupefacientes No. 2 and was authorised by
Guarantee Court No. 4 of the Zárate-Campana judicial district. [@1]
