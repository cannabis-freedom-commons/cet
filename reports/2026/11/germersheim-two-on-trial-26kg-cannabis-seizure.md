Two men accused after a large drug find in Germersheim stood trial as defendants
before the Landgericht Landau, the regional court in Landau. [@1] A co-defendant
who had already been convicted appeared as a witness in their trial and made
statements exonerating the two accused. [@1]

In August 2024 police stormed a flat in Germersheim and arrested the two men who
were present in the flat at the time. [@1] In that raid officers secured more
than 3,000 ecstasy tablets, more than one kilogram of amphetamine, two kilograms
of marijuana, 600 grams of hashish and several small tubes containing
testosterone. [@1] The cannabis in the seizure was two kilograms of marijuana
and 600 grams of hashish, together 2.6 kilograms. [@1]

The August 2024 raid, the seizure of the drugs and the arrests in the
Germersheim flat are the case now being tried before the Landau court. [@1]
Germersheim is the town where the raided flat is located and Landau is the city
whose regional court is hearing the case against the two accused. [@1]
