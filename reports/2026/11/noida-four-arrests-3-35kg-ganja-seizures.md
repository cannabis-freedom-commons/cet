Police in Noida arrested four people, including a woman, in an action against
the illegal drug trade. [@1] Sector-113, Phase-1, Phase-2 and Phase-3 police
caught the accused at separate places during checking. [@1] ACP Diksha Singh
said FIRs were registered against all four accused and they were sent to jail.
[@1]

Officers recovered a total of 3.35 kg of ganja from the possession of the
accused. [@1] Sector-113 police arrested 39-year-old Banu near the cremation
ground by the Sector-71 underpass and found 750 grams of ganja with her. [@1]
During questioning she said she brought ganja from Delhi and sold it in Noida.
[@1] Phase-2 police arrested 20-year-old Rajkumar alias Katappa on the drain
road near the cremation ground in Bhangel with 900 grams of ganja. [@1]

Phase-1 police arrested 32-year-old Puneet of Haraula on the dirty-drain track
in Sector-14 with 1.1 kg of ganja, and police said he met his expenses by
selling ganja. [@1] Phase-3 police caught 26-year-old Dhiraj Pathak near the
Mamura power-house cut with 600 grams of ganja. [@1] Police said the accused
brought ganja from Delhi and other places and sold it to passers-by and local
people in Noida at high prices. [@1]
