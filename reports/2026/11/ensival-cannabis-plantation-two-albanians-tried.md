Two men were found at a cannabis plantation in Ensival, Verviers, one of them
trying to flee across the roofs before officers caught him. [@1] Residents had
noticed a smell from a hangar behind the Ensival sports hall in May, and a
police check of its electricity and water use found consumption up to 420% of
normal for electricity. [@1] The raid took place on the morning of 9 June 2026.
[@2] It was carried out in a warehouse on rue Auguste Dupont, behind the old
Avenière warehouses. [@2] About 20 officers, including special forces, chased
and caught the two men when they tried to run. [@2]

The search uncovered three crates of already harvested cannabis weighing 22 kg
and three growing rooms of cannabis plants that weighed 220 kg once cut. [@1]
The two men were Albanian nationals who were held in custody and appeared before
the criminal court. [@1] Ilia, 30, said he was recruited in Albania for small
jobs and offered 5,000 euros for five months. [@1] Shefik, 39, said he was
recruited in Brussels after trying for a month in France to reach England, and
was promised 5,000 euros a month to keep a compatriot company. [@1]

Both said they were hired as simple gardeners, but prosecutor Mme Troisfontaines
of the ministère public told the court they were low-level members of a
trafficking network who clearly knew why they were there. [@1] She said Shefik's
phone showed involvement with cocaine since 2022 and that he had already been
imprisoned in Britain. [@1] She requested 18 months of firm imprisonment and an
8,000-euro fine for each man. [@1]
