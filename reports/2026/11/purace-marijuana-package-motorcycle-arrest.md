Police in Puracé, a municipality in Cauca department, arrested a man in
flagrante, caught in the act, during a stop-and-search operation in the barrio
Centro. [@1] Officers were checking the backgrounds of people and vehicles in
the area. [@1] They stopped a man riding a motorcycle and inspected the vehicle.
[@1]

Under the rider's seat they found a large package wrapped in a plastic bag. [@1]
According to the police procedure, the package contained a plant substance that
was marijuana. [@1] The authorities accused him of trafficking, manufacturing
and carrying narcotics. [@1] The police described the package under the seat as
the main known element of the procedure. [@1] The arrest was made at the same
place where the officers were carrying out the registration and verification
work. [@1]

The police said the judicial authorities must establish the conditions of the
transport and the man's eventual responsibility. [@1] They said they maintain
these checks in Puracé as part of their surveillance and control work. [@1]
