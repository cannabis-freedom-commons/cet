Ansar members detained a young man carrying ganja at Dhaka Medical College
Hospital in Dhaka in the early hours of Thursday 26 November 2026. [@1] The man,
Nawab Ali, was stopped at about 5:00 am as Ansar members patrolled a ward in the
hospital's new building. [@3] He could not give a satisfactory reason for being
in the hospital. [@1]

Ansar members searched him and recovered ganja wrapped in paper and 17 boxes of
Gastrocal medicine. [@2] They handed him to the hospital authorities for legal
action. [@1]

Ansar, the Bangladesh Ansar and Village Defence Party, keeps members on duty at
the hospital day and night to protect patients and their relatives and to keep
order. [@1] The Ansar statement called hospitals sensitive public places, where
someone carrying drugs or posing as a doctor puts patients and their relatives
at risk. [@1] Hospital authorities and Ansar members said patrols and
surveillance at the hospital would continue. [@3]
