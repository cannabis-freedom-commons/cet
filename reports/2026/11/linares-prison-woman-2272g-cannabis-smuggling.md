A joint procedure by the Investigative Police (PDI) and Gendarmería stopped
illicit substances from entering the Linares prison. [@1] Gendarmería officers
spotted a woman trying to bring drugs hidden among her clothing into the
facility. [@1] They called in detectives from the Linares BRIANT anti-narcotics
brigade. [@1]

Officers seized 139.94 grams of cocaine base, 22.72 grams of cannabis sativa and
13.28 grams of a substance whose composition had not yet been determined. [@1]
The total seizure was 175.94 grams of substances. [@1]

The woman, aged 55 and of Chilean nationality, was detained for alleged
responsibility for violating article 4 of Law 20,000. [@1] The coordinated work
kept the substances from entering the prison in the commune. [@1]
