On 17 November 2026, State Police in Ravenna arrested two Italian citizens who
were holding narcotic substances, packaging material and cash at their homes.
[@2] Police seized about 180 grams of hashish in total, with modest quantities
of cocaine and heroin, packaging material and more than €2,200 in cash. [@1] The
operation was carried out on Tuesday by Squadra Mobile agents executing personal
and home search warrants issued by the Ravenna public prosecutor's office. [@1]
The searches extended across the province and the cities of Milan, Ferrara and
Arezzo, supported by local mobile squads, crime prevention officers and dog
units from the Bologna police headquarters. [@3]

In the Ravenna home of a 20-year-old, officers found about 80 grams of hashish,
modest quantities of cocaine and heroin, packaging material and more than €500
in cash. [@1] The young man was already known to police for previous
drug-related offences and was arrested. [@1] In a second Ravenna home, linked to
a 30-year-old, officers seized 100 grams of hashish divided into several
packets, packaging material and more than €1,700 in cash. [@1] The 30-year-old
also had previous reports to the judicial authorities for drug offences. [@1]

Both were arrested and brought before a judge for an expedited hearing. [@1]
After validating the arrests, the judicial authority imposed a residence
obligation on the 20-year-old and house arrest on the 30-year-old. [@1] Police
said the proceedings remained at the preliminary investigation stage and the
suspects were presumed innocent until a final conviction. [@2]
