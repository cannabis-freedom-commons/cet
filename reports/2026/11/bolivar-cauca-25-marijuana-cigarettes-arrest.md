Police in the municipality of Bolívar, in Cauca department, arrested a man
identified as Fredy Delgado on suspicion of possessing several doses of
narcotics. [@1] The operation took place in the barrio Venecia, at calle 2 with
carrera 5, where officers were carrying out registration, control and
surveillance work against drug trafficking. [@1] Officers questioned the man and
searched him, finding among his belongings 20 packages of a substance that, by
its characteristics, would be coca base, a separate drug not counted in the
cannabis figures. [@1] The police also found 25 cigarettes that would contain
suspected marijuana, which were seized and placed at the authorities' disposal
as part of the procedure. [@1]

Delgado was arrested and handed to the competent authorities to answer for the
suspected crime of trafficking, manufacturing or carrying narcotics. [@1] The
case was left to the courts to carry out the investigation, establish where the
substances came from and decide his legal position. [@1]
