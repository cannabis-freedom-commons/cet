Twin Lakes police, the Kenosha and Walworth county drug units and the US Postal
Inspection Service searched a home on East Lakeshore Drive in Twin Lakes,
Wisconsin, on 13 November 2026. [@1] Police said the investigation targeted a
suspected kilogram-level methamphetamine distributor who they described as a
convicted felon with a history of drug-related arrests. [@1] Police said his
earlier arrests included misdemeanour marijuana possession, felony marijuana
possession and felony possession of amphetamines with intent to distribute. [@1]
Authorities had not publicly identified the person under investigation or
announced criminal charges from the search. [@1]

Officers also seized 30 THC vapes, 59.5 grams of marijuana, two digital scales
and nine firearms, including eight handguns and one rifle. [@1] Investigators
also recovered an undisclosed amount of cash during the investigation. [@1]
Police said the money may be subject to civil asset forfeiture. [@1]

The same operation recovered about one kilogram of suspected methamphetamine, in
roughly 2,500 pills. [@1] Police said the wider investigation took about four
more kilograms of methamphetamine, taking the total to about five kilograms, or
roughly 11 pounds. [@1] The involvement of the Postal Inspection Service
suggested investigators were examining possible links to the mail system. [@1]
Police said the investigation remained active and that no further information
was available for release. [@1] Police had not said how long the investigation
had been running or whether other suspects were being investigated. [@1] Twin
Lakes is a village in western Kenosha County, near the Illinois border. [@1]
