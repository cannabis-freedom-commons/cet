Carabinieri of the Petilia Policastro station, Italy's gendarmerie, arrested a
30-year-old local man already known to police on the evening of 22 November
2026. [@1] Officers found a homemade greenhouse set up for indoor cannabis
cultivation inside a garage at the man's home. [@2] The man was accused of
cultivation and unlawful possession of narcotic substances. [@3]

Officers seized 19 Indian hemp plants, between 10 and 15 centimetres tall, and
10 seedlings of the same species each kept in its own pot. [@1] They also seized
45 grams of marijuana and about 13 grams of hashish. [@3] An electronic
precision scale and material for packaging doses were taken as well. [@2]

The man was placed under house arrest at his home by order of the judicial
authority, pending a validation hearing before a judge. [@1] The operation was
coordinated by the Crotone public prosecutor's office, led by Domenico
Guarascio. [@1] The case remains in the preliminary investigation phase and the
suspect's responsibility has yet to be established in court. [@3]
