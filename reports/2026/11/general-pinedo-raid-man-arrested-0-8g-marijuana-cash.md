Police in General Pinedo arrested one man and notified a second of the case
while leaving him free during a raid on Saturday 21 November 2026. [@1] The
search ran from 10:15 to 13:30 at a house on Calle 1, between calles 10 and 12,
in General Pinedo. [@1] The operation was carried out by the División
Operaciones Drogas Interior Charata under warrant 843/2026 from the Juzgado de
Garantías of the Cuarta Circunscripción Judicial. [@2] Sixteen people were
identified inside the house. [@1]

Officers seized 0.8 grams of marijuana and 14.4 grams of cocaine, plus 353,000
pesos in cash. [@1] They also took two mobile phones, a precision scale, an
envelope of bicarbonate, nylon cuttings, plastic cards, a spoon and a marijuana
grinder. [@1] The items were added to the investigation to establish whether
they were linked to drug sales. [@1]

The anti-drug prosecutor ordered the arrest of Daniel Iván Gastón Oviedo, known
as Colino, while Matías Agustín Tévez was notified of the case and remained
free. [@1] Officers also found 13 long .38-calibre cartridges, and the Fiscalía
Penal N.º 3 ordered their seizure. [@1] No further procedural measures were
ordered against the people present over the ammunition. [@1]
