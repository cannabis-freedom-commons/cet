Nepal Police arrested 27 people on suspicion of possessing illegal drugs during
routine patrols and security operations across the country. [@1] The Central
Police Newsroom said the arrests were made in Kathmandu, Sunsari, Jhapa, Bara,
Ilam, Tanahun and Dang districts. [@1] Ten people were arrested in Kathmandu,
seven in Sunsari, six in Jhapa and Bara combined, two in Tanahun and one each in
Ilam and Dang. [@1] The arrests took place on Tuesday and Wednesday, police
said. [@1] The arrests were made by officers on regular patrol, according to the
Central Police News Room. [@2]

Police seized various prohibited substances, including brown heroin, marijuana
and tramadol tablets, from those arrested. [@1] Cash was also recovered from
some of the suspects. [@1] Brown heroin, marijuana, Tramadol tablets and other
banned substances were recovered from the accused. [@3]

The seven districts stretch from the capital to Koshi Province in the east,
Madhesh Province in the south, Gandaki Province and Lumbini Province in the
west. [@2] In operations in the seven districts, police arrested suspects along
with various banned substances. [@4]
