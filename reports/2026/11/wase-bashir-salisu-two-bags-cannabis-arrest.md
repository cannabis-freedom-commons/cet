Troops of Sector 2, Operation Enduring Peace intercepted a 30-year-old suspected
drug dealer with two bags of substances suspected to be cannabis sativa, known
as Indian hemp, in Wase Local Government Area of Plateau State. [@2] The suspect
was identified as Bashir Salisu and was intercepted at about 10:30 a.m. on
November 14 while riding a motorcycle along the Bashar–Zak–Kampani Road. [@2]
Troops deployed at Zak stopped him during a routine patrol and discovered the
two bags of suspected cannabis in his possession. [@2]

The Daily Post account said the development was contained in a post shared on X
on Sunday night by security analyst Zagazola Makama on his verified handle. [@1]
The suspect and the recovered substances were taken into military custody for
further investigation and necessary action. [@1]
