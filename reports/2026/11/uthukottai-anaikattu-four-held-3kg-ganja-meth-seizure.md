Police said they had received information that ganja and intoxicant tablets were
being smuggled through the Anaikattu area on the Periyapalayam-Avadi highway
near Uthukottai. [@1] Acting on that information, Vengal police mounted
intensive vehicle checks in the Anaikattu area during the day on 16 November.
[@1] During those checks, officers intercepted a two-wheeler and a car
travelling through that stretch of highway and searched both vehicles. [@1]
Police arrested four men travelling in those vehicles, all described as being
from Bengaluru and identified as Ajay, 26, Manoj, 28, Harish, 21, and Karthik,
27. [@1]

Officers said the search of the two vehicles yielded 3 kg of ganja. [@1] In the
same search officers also found 15 grams of methamphetamine tablets, which are
not counted in the cannabis figures. [@1] Police seized both the car and the
bike used by the four men. [@1]

Police in Uthukottai said the four were held for alleged smuggling of ganja and
methamphetamine tablets and were being questioned. [@1]
