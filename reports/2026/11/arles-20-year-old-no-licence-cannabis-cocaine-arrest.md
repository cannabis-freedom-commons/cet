A young man of twenty was arrested in Arles for dangerous driving. [@1] The
Arles anti-crime brigade stopped him in the night from Tuesday to Wednesday at
around midnight. [@1] He had been spotted at the wheel of a vehicle driving
hazardously in the Camargue town. [@1] The check showed the driver had no valid
driving licence. [@1] He was arrested after officers noticed his hazardous
driving in the streets of Arles. [@1]

Officers found him in possession of cocaine and cannabis. [@1] The outlet's
summary says he was carrying cocaine and cannabis and admitted selling drugs to
fund his personal consumption. [@1] He admitted selling drugs to pay for his own
drug use. [@1]

He was deferred to the Tarascon prosecutor on Thursday. [@1] He is to be tried
later in a CRPC hearing, comparution sur reconnaissance préalable de
culpabilité, the French guilty-plea procedure. [@1]
