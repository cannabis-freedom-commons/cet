The Adamawa State Police Command arrested a suspected drug dealer during a
routine patrol in Bille Village, Demsa Local Government Area. [@1] The suspect
was identified as Samaila Idris, a resident of Mayo-Lope in Lau Local Government
Area of Taraba State. [@2] Officers attached to the Demsa Divisional Police
Headquarters intercepted him during the patrol. [@1] A search of him produced a
large sack of dried leaves suspected to be Indian hemp, other suspected hard
drugs and four jerricans of a liquid popularly known as "Suck and Die". [@1] The
operatives also recovered ₦597,000 in cash. [@1]

The state police public relations officer, CSP Suleiman Nguroje, said the
suspect and the exhibits remained in custody while the investigation continued.
[@2] Nguroje said Idris would be charged in court when the investigation
concluded. [@2] The command disclosed the arrest in a statement issued on Sunday
by Nguroje on behalf of Commissioner of Police Kabir Umar Hassan. [@1]

Commissioner of Police Kabir Umar Hassan reiterated the command's determination
to intensify efforts against drug-related offences. [@1] He urged residents to
give police timely and credible information that could help identify criminal
activity. [@1]
