Police in Turin arrested four people after an investigation into a system used
to store and move drugs across the metropolitan area. [@1] The four were taken
to the Lorusso e Cutugno prison in Turin and placed at the disposal of the Ivrea
prosecutor's office, which coordinated the investigation. [@3] The drugs came
from a storage and distribution network between Turin and its first belt of
towns. [@2]

Officers found and seized about 320 kilograms of hashish, 27 kilograms of
marijuana and 13 kilograms of cocaine. [@1] The cocaine was to be turned into
crack and sold around Parco Sempione in Turin. [@3] Investigators estimated that
the cocaine could have yielded 40,000 doses, or 80,000 doses of crack once
processed. [@2] GPS trackers and hidden cameras were found inside the packaging,
used to monitor the storage points remotely. [@2]

The investigation began with surveillance of people suspected of handling large
quantities of drugs. [@1] Officers focused on suspicious movements near a
company in Leinì, where boxes were loaded into cars and taken to garages in the
area. [@1] After reconstructing the movements and identifying the storage
points, police carried out the raid and searches. [@2]
