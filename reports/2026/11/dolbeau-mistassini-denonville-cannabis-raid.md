Sûreté du Québec officers, working alongside officers from the Sécurité publique
de Mashteuiatsh, carried out a drug search at a residence on rue Denonville in
Dolbeau-Mistassini on the morning of Thursday 26 November 2026. [@1] The
resident, a 60-year-old man, was arrested and interrogated at the scene when the
operation ended. [@2] He was released for the continuation of the proceedings.
[@2] He was expected to return to court at a later date. [@1] Police said he
could face charges linked to drug trafficking. [@1]

The items seized at the residence included nearly 75 grams of cocaine, illicit
cannabis, more than 1,000 methamphetamine tablets and a firearm. [@1] Officers
also recovered nearly $6,000 in cash and equipment used for the sale of drugs.
[@1]

Le Lac-St-Jean said police interventions over drug trafficking have been
succeeding one another in the Saguenay–Lac-Saint-Jean region for several days.
[@3] The rue Denonville search came after an operation in the La Baie district
of Saguenay two days earlier. [@3]
