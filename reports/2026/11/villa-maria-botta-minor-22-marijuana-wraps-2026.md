On Saturday afternoon police detained a minor in barrio Felipe Botta, Villa
María, for carrying 22 nylon wraps containing marijuana. [@1] Officers stopped
him on Calle Int. La Colina al 700. [@1] A preventive frisk found the wraps
inside a backpack he was carrying. [@1]

The Fuerza Policial Antinarcotráfico assisted, with operational cover from
G.E.O.T. and S.E.O.M. officers. [@1] Tests confirmed the material was 30 grams
of cannabis (THC). [@1] Officers also seized a cell phone, a wallet and the
backpack. [@1]

The minor and the seized items were placed at the disposal of the intervening
Fiscalía de Instrucción and the Fiscalía de Lucha contra el Narcotráfico. [@1]
