Officers of the Transit and Transport Section found 5,950 grams of marijuana
during road controls in the municipality of La Pintada, in southwestern
Antioquia. [@1] The marijuana was being moved as a parcel shipment inside a
vehicle. [@1] The procedure took place during registration and control
activities on Saturday 14 November, and the drug was placed at the disposal of
the Fiscalía. [@1]

The police report was published at 11:00 a.m. on the Antioquia Police
Department's official account on X, which said Transit and Transport officers
found the drug during road controls. [@1] The report mentioned no arrests and no
person identified with the shipment. [@1] Police did not say which company or
vehicle carried the parcel, nor where it came from or where it was going. [@1]

The seizure was publicised with a photograph showing two Transit and Transport
officers alongside the seized marijuana packages. [@1] The outlet distinguishes
this case from recent arrests in Santa Bárbara and Guarne, noting that in La
Pintada police only reported the roadside seizure without identifying anyone.
