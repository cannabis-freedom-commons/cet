Two men aged 31 and 44 were arrested on Friday 13 November 2026 in an area of
Ierapetra municipality in Lasithi on charges of possessing and trafficking
narcotics. [@1,3,4,5,6,7] Officers of the Lasithi Police Operations Department said they
saw the 44-year-old supplying the 31-year-old with drugs in exchange for money
during targeted anti-trafficking checks. [@1,3,4,5,6,7] A second account identifies the
two men as foreign nationals and gives the same ages, place and date of arrest.
[@2]

Officers then checked the two men and searched the rural area where they were
found. [@1,3,4,7] The account says the immediate intervention included physical control of the two men. [@4] In that search officers found 156.2 grams of raw cannabis carefully
hidden and seized it. [@1,3,4,5,6,7] They also seized 20 euros, one precision scale and
one mobile phone. [@1,4,6,7] The second account confirms the same seizure of 156.2
grams of raw cannabis, 20 euros, a precision scale and a mobile phone. [@2]

The preliminary investigation was taken over by the Ierapetra Crime Prosecution
and Investigation Department. [@1,3,4,5,6,7]
