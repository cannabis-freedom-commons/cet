Judge Deni Mathews made a confiscation order at Shrewsbury Crown Court on
Wednesday 25 November 2026 after a proceeds-of-crime hearing. [@2] A court
ordered Stefan Farmer to repay £69,889.99 after finding that his
cannabis-dealing operation had generated £326,132.55. [@2] Farmer, 35, was
jailed for two years in January after admitting possession of a class B drug
with intent to supply and acquiring criminal property. [@2] The hearing came
after Farmer's arrest in 2025. [@1] Officers arrested the 34-year-old in
February 2025 after gaining a warrant for his arrest. [@3]

A search of his address found a quantity of cannabis buds in an outbuilding, all
vacuum packed and sealed, ready to be sold. [@3] Officers also discovered
scales, zip-bags and £50,000 in cash, all linked to drugs supply. [@3] Police
found cannabis, packaging equipment and £50,000 in cash when they searched his
home. [@2] Detective Sergeant Andy Pond said the custodial sentence had taken a
drug dealer off the streets of Telford and protected the community. [@3]
