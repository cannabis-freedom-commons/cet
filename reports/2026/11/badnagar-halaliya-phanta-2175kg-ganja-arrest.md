Police in Badnagar in Ujjain district arrested a 22-year-old youth who had
arrived carrying ganja from Odisha. [@1,2] The arrest was made under Operation Prahar, an ongoing drive against illegal drug trafficking and sale. [@2] Officers spotted him on foot at
Halaliya Phanta during vehicle checking, and when he tried to flee on seeing the
police they surrounded and stopped him. [@1,2] The interception took place on 15 November 2026. [@2] Questioning identified him as
Junesh Malik, son of Makhija Malik, a resident of Pithiaguda under Kiria police
station in Kenjhamal district, Odisha. [@1,2]

A search of his bag found leaf-like material wrapped in brown tape, which
examination identified as ganja. [@1,2] Weighing showed the quantity to be 2 kg
175 g. [@1,2] Police put its value at about 75,000 rupees. [@1,2] Police seized the
ganja and registered a case against him at Badnagar police station under Section
8/20 of the NDPS Act. [@1,2] He was produced in court on Monday, which granted two
days of police remand. [@1]

According to police, he said he had brought the ganja from Odisha and was
preparing to sell it in Badnagar. [@1,2] During the remand police will question
him about the source of the ganja and its intended buyers in Badnagar, and are
tracing other people in the network and purchasers. [@1]
