Drug-control officers in Dzhankoy found cannabis plant parts in a greenhouse
while inspecting the property of a 42-year-old resident of Dzhankoy district.
[@1] The man told police he had planted seeds on his plot and cultivated the
plants in order to sell marijuana. [@2]

An expert examination found the seized plant material, 7,104.5 grams calculated
as dried substance, to be cannabis containing a narcotic. [@1] An investigator
opened a criminal case for attempted illegal sale of narcotic drugs in a large
amount, an offence that carries a maximum of 20 years in prison. [@1] The
regional interior ministry's press service reported the case, which police
described as stopping illegal storage and preparation for sale of
drug-containing plants. [@1]
