Ho Chi Minh City's People's Court said on 21 November 2026 that it would soon
bring 38 defendants to trial in a drug ring case. [@1] The defendants are
charged with illegal trading, organising illegal use and illegal possession of
narcotics. [@1] Thirteen of the 38 were charged under a sentencing frame of 20
years in prison, life imprisonment or death. [@1]

Investigators held Lý Bích Ngọc and Lâm Hoàng Tiến liable for 128,407.7466 grams
of drugs of various kinds. [@1] The case's drugs are of several types:
methamphetamine, ketamine, nimetazepam, MDMA and cannabis. [@1] Lý Hùng Cường
was held liable for 17,240.7766 grams, Ngô Ngọc Yến Nhi and Nguyễn Quốc Thái for
3,724.7866 grams and Hứa Hoàng Long for 1,783.0393 grams. [@1]

From February 2025 to the end of April 2025, Ngọc and Tiến took instructions
from an unidentified person called Su Su over Signal, Telegram, Zangi and
Messenger. [@1] Their task was to receive drugs, hide them at their home, divide
them into small packets and arrange delivery to customers. [@1] When booking
app-based couriers they entered one wrong digit in the recipient's phone number
and wrote the real number on the parcel. [@1] Drugs were disguised in coffee
bags, sweets packets, shoeboxes, foam fruit boxes and rice boxes. [@1] At about
15:00 on 25 April 2025, Ho Chi Minh City police searched the flat of Nguyễn Quốc
Thái and Ngô Ngọc Yến Nhi in Long Bình ward and found a large quantity of drugs,
electronic scales and a vacuum packing machine. [@1] That evening police caught
Lý Hùng Cường, Ngọc's younger brother, carrying two cartons of drugs for
delivery to Nhi. [@1] A search of Ngọc and Tiến's home found dozens of cardboard
boxes and foam boxes holding large quantities of drugs. [@1]
