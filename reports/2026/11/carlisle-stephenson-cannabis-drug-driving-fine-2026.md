Paul Stephenson, 62, who had never been in trouble before, appeared at the
Rickergate court in Carlisle. [@1] He admitted driving while over the limit for
the cannabis ingredient THC, and possessing the Class B drug. [@1] He began
using cannabis to ease the emotional pressure of becoming a full-time carer for
his wife. [@1] He had given up his own business when his wife's mental health
deteriorated. [@1] The defence gave magistrates a letter of support from his
wife's doctor and character references. [@2]

Police stopped him at Todhills on the southbound M6 on 12 July, and he had a
small amount of cannabis. [@1] A test confirmed that he had eight times the
legal limit for the active ingredient of cannabis in his system. [@1] Adele
Graham, defending, said that while the THC reading was high, there was no
suggestion his driving was impaired. [@1]

Magistrates accepted that there was no concern about his driving and that his
guilty plea made the case relatively straightforward. [@1] They fined him £120,
with £85 costs and a £48 victim surcharge. [@1]
