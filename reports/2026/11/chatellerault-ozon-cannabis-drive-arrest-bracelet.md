Police in Châtellerault dismantled a cannabis 'drive', a street sale point, in
the Ozon neighbourhood and arrested a 24-year-old man caught in the act on
Wednesday 25 November 2026. [@1] The suspect, a 24-year-old from Châtellerault,
was wearing an electronic ankle bracelet because he was serving a home-detention
sentence for another case. [@1] After several weeks of investigation, officers
of the Service local de police judiciaire (SLPJ) arrested the man on suspicion
of selling cannabis near the former Amor factory. [@1] Customers came to supply
themselves directly on the public road. [@1] Officers intervened in flagrante
delicto, at the moment of a transaction. [@1]

During the search investigators seized a little cash, cannabis resin and
material for packaging and resale. [@1]

Placed in police custody, he reportedly admitted the facts, and several regular
customers of the 'drive stups' were also questioned. [@1] He was brought before
the Poitiers prosecutor on 25 November 2026 and placed in pre-trial detention by
the juge des libertés et de la détention, a judge ruling on detention, awaiting
trial by comparution à délai différé on 18 January 2027. [@1]
