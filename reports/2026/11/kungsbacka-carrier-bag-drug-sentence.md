In February last year, plainclothes police in Kungsbacka recognised a suspected
woman in her 30s and watched a mint-green IKEA-type carrier bag being handed to
her by a person who arrived by car. [@1] Officers followed the woman to her
home, where she was arrested after first trying to break free before cooperating
and handing over the key so the home could be searched. [@1] The search found
more drugs and bags of various kinds for packaging, and the woman admitted the
offence in questioning. [@1]

The bag held almost a kilo of 87-percent cocaine, enough for 2,271 doses and
worth SEK 1.9 million at consumer level, plus several kilos of cannabis and hash
cakes. [@1] Possession of the drugs was assessed as an aggravated narcotics
crime. [@1] Her home had long served as a staging post for large amounts of
drugs, and the total sales value of what was seized at the arrest was estimated
at SEK 2.7 million. [@1]

The woman, who repackaged drugs in her home, has now been convicted of
aggravated narcotics crime. [@1] Her sentence is four years in prison. [@1]
