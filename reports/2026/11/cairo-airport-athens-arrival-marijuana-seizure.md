Security forces at Cairo International Airport seized five smuggling attempts
involving marijuana, human medicines, dental medical supplies and mobile phones
in violation of the law. [@1,2,5] The marijuana attempt took place in the fourth
department of Passenger Terminal 3 during inspection of passengers arriving from
Athens on a Greek airline flight. [@1,2] Officers suspected an Egyptian passenger
as he left the customs committee gate, X-rayed his bags and found two cream
containers holding four bags containing marijuana. [@1,3,5]

In a second attempt, officers stopped a foreign passenger arriving from Baghdad
on an Iraqi airline flight and found 40 ampoules for treating cancerous tumours
hidden among clothes in his bags. [@1,5] In a third attempt, detectives stopped an
Egyptian passenger arriving from Guangzhou on an EgyptAir flight and found
dental medicine and surgery supplies in his bags. [@1,5] In a fourth attempt in
Terminal 2, customs officers stopped a foreign passenger arriving from China and
found 38 mobile phones hidden among clothes. [@1,5]

In a fifth attempt, officers stopped another foreign passenger arriving from
China and found 34 mobile phones hidden among clothes. [@1,5] Authorities took
legal measures and drew up five customs seizure reports. [@1,4,5]
