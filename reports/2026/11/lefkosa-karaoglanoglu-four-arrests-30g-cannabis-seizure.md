Police arrested four people in two separate drug operations in Lefkoşa and
Karaoğlanoğlu. [@1] In Lefkoşa, officers searched Y.Y. and the house he shared
with R.K. [@1] Y.Y. and R.K. were arrested by police. [@1] In Karaoğlanoğlu,
officers tried to stop a car driven by C.S. [@1] The driver threw a bag he was
carrying out of the car and fled the scene. [@1] Police later found the car in
Girne and arrested the driver C.S. and the passenger Ö.Ç. [@1]

The Lefkoşa searches found a total of 30 grams of a substance believed to be
cannabis in sale-ready packets, plus a precision scale believed to carry drug
residue, all taken as evidence. [@1] The bag thrown from the car held about 5
grams of a substance believed to be cocaine, taken as evidence. [@1] The Police
Press Office said Narcotics and Anti-Smuggling Directorate teams carried out the
searches and seized substances believed to be Indian hemp and cocaine. [@1]
