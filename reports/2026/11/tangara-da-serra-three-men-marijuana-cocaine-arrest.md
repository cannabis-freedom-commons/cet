Three men were arrested on suspicion of drug trafficking during an operation by
the Força Tática of the Military Police on the night of Saturday 21 November
2026 in Tangará da Serra, 242 km from Cuiabá. [@1] The Military Police said the
approach took place in the vicinity of the bus station on Avenida Brasília after
the team received information about a possible sale of narcotics in the area.
[@1] During the checks, officers stopped two men and found portions of cocaine
and marijuana with them. [@1] Acting on information from the two men approached
first, the team went to an indicated residence where officers found a third man
and additional drugs. [@1]

Inside that residence, officers seized five portions of marijuana, one larger
portion of the same substance and 10 portions of cocaine. [@1] Officers also
collected two mobile phones and a bag together with the drugs. [@1]

The three men were taken to the police station together with all the seized
material for further legal measures. [@1] The operation was conducted by the
Força Tática of the Military Police. [@1]
