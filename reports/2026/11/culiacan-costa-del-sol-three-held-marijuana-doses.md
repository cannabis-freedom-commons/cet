Elements of the Policía Estatal Preventiva, the preventive police of Sinaloa's
Secretariat of Public Security, detained two women and a man in the Costa del
Sol residential area of Culiacán on 14 November 2026. [@4] The arrests were made
in coordination with the Grupo Interinstitucional, an inter-agency security
group. [@3] During patrols the officers saw the three people near an empty lot,
and when the three noticed the authorities they tried to flee and abandoned a
backpack. [@1] The officers followed them and caught up, and a search of the
backpack found substances that appeared to be illicit drugs. [@3]

In the backpack the officers found 28 doses of presumed marijuana, 63 doses of a
substance with the characteristics of cocaine and 17 doses of apparent
methamphetamine. [@4] The backpack held 108 doses of different drugs, the state
account said. [@5]

The two women and the man, with the seized substances, were placed at the
disposal of the competent authority, which is to determine their legal
situation. [@2] The authorities had not disclosed the identities of the
detainees or specified the crimes they could be charged with. [@4] The
secretariat said the operation was part of coordinated work between security
agencies to combat crime and strengthen surveillance in sectors of Culiacán.
[@2]
