Carabinieri from the Albizzate station arrested a 22-year-old unemployed man in
Besnate, Varese, on suspicion of possessing drugs for dealing. [@1,4] The arrest
followed a road traffic check in via Libertà in Besnate on 7 November 2026. [@3]
After formalities the man was taken to prison in Busto Arsizio, at the disposal
of the judicial authority. [@2,4] His 27-year-old passenger was found with 0.37
grams of hashish for personal use and reported to the prefecture as a drug user.
[@2,4]

Police described the man's home as a 'bazar' of drugs. [@1] There they found a
25.8-gram block of hashish and eight ready-prepared hashish doses weighing 12.58
grams in total. [@2] They also found two doses of marijuana and one
already-rolled joint. [@2] Seized with them were cannabis sweets in promotional
packaging: a THC tart of 111.3 grams and a THC biscuit of 30.9 grams. [@1,4]
Officers also seized 5,910 euros in cash, which they said was proceeds of the
dealing. [@1]

The car search had found 2.013 kilograms of ketamine, 59 grams of cocaine, seven
grams of MDMA and one stamp of LSD hidden in the front seat linings. [@1,4] The
home search also yielded 105.2 grams of MDMA, three ecstasy pills and one dose
of cocaine. [@1] The drugs, the cash and the car used to transport them were all
seized. [@3] Investigators said they were reconstructing the client network, the
scale of the trade and any accomplices. [@3]
