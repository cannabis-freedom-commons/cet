On Thursday, Nurpur district police were conducting vehicle checks near Nadoli
on the 32 Mile-Lanj road in the Jawali area. [@1] During the operation, officers
stopped a car at the checkpoint and searched the vehicle. [@1] The search
recovered 4 kilograms and 72 grams of charas, a cannabis resin also known as
hashish. [@1]

Police registered a case in connection with the recovery. [@1] Both accused
persons associated with the car were arrested after the seizure. [@1]

The action was taken by the Nurpur district police district, which covers
Jawali, during routine vehicle checking on the 32 Mile-Lanj route. [@1]
