Two men from Blomberg were each sentenced to two years and three months in
prison on 20 November 2026 for their part in an illegal cannabis plantation in a
house in the town. [@2] A court spokesman said the two had looked after about
100 cannabis plants as a kind of gardening team. [@2] The owner of the house
also stood trial and came away with a suspended sentence. [@2]

Four men, most of them from Blomberg, went on trial over large-scale illegal
cannabis cultivation on 20 November 2026. [@1] The case was heard at the
Landgericht Detmold, the regional court in Detmold. [@1] Prosecutors accused
them of running a plantation of about 100 plants in a house in Blomberg since
September 2025. [@1] According to the indictment the men sold a harvest at the
end of 2025 and replanted in January 2026. [@1] The court had said it might
deliver a verdict the same day. [@1]
