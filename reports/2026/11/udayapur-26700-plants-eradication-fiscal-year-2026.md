Police in Udayapur destroyed 26,700 marijuana plants grown illegally during
fiscal year 2026/27, the District Police Office said. [@1] The eradications took
place in Katari and Belaka municipalities and in Tapli and Limchungbung rural
municipalities, where police said marijuana was being cultivated on a commercial
scale. [@1]

The district police office released the details on 25 November 2026, describing
campaigns at nine locations across the four local levels. [@2] The
Nepali-language account gives the fiscal year as 2083/84 and credits the
destruction to the district police office and its subordinate units. [@3] By
local unit, police counted 14,520 plants destroyed in Katari, 9,450 in Belaka,
1,950 in Limchungbung and 750 in Tapli. [@2]

In Katari Municipality, plants were destroyed in Marjepur, Balaute, Ahale,
Tatari, Seurini, Babala, Bhantabari and Maruwakhani. [@1] In Belaka, operations
covered Pabla, Tilitle, Khamare, Myungtar, Pitlas and Kekra. [@1] Plantations
were also destroyed in Madale, Nigalabas and Shikharpur in Tapli Rural
Municipality and in Jimigaun, Baraha, Majhkharka and Katahare in Limchungbung
Rural Municipality. [@1]

Police said western Udayapur has remained a hotspot for illegal cannabis
cultivation, with much of the produce allegedly smuggled across the border into
India. [@1] Authorities pledged to continue eradication campaigns and monitoring
to discourage large-scale marijuana production in remote parts of the district.
[@1]
