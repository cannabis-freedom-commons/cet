Eight people aged between 22 and 42 are under investigation by Bonn police on
suspicion of illegally trading in cocaine and cannabis in not insignificant
quantities. [@2] On 19 November 2026 police searched five residential properties
in Bonn's Tannenbusch district and one apartment in Bad Godesberg. [@2] The
operation followed anonymous tips about suspected drug dealing in southern
Tannenbusch, especially around a site known locally as the "Düne". [@2]

Police seized cocaine, marijuana and weapons during the searches in Bonn. [@1] A
drug-sniffing dog was used and officers also secured cash, a telescopic baton
and a machete. [@1] Six properties were searched in all. [@1] The seized items
are now being evaluated as evidence. [@2]

The operation was run by the Tannenbusch investigation group of Criminal
Investigation Department 25, the Bonn police unit specialising in drug crime.
[@2] The investigation against the eight suspects continues. [@2]
