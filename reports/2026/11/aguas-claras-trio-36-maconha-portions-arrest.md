The Military Police of the Federal District arrested three suspects for drug
trafficking in Aguas Claras, Federal District, on the night of Monday 16
November 2026. [@1,2] The operation was run by teams of the 17th Military Police Battalion with tactical air support from the PMDF's Águia 37 aircraft, in Quadra 203 of Aguas Claras. [@2] The arrest occurred during a tactical patrol while teams
were carrying out inquiries in the area. [@1] Officers of the 17th Military
Police Battalion spotted a previously monitored vehicle as it was leaving the
garage of a residential building. [@1,2] The car was a white Volkswagen Polo. [@2] Officers approached the vehicle and found
the three suspects inside it. [@1]

The trio was caught with more than 30 dry-type marijuana portions hidden inside
the glove compartment of the car. [@1,2] During the search officers found 34
fractioned and packaged dry marijuana portions in the glove compartment, plus
two larger portions. [@1] One of the three men told officers the drugs were his. [@2] Officers also found a precision scale with the trio in
the same search. [@1,2] Police seized the car along with the drugs. [@2]

The three suspects were arrested in flagrante and taken to the 21st Police
Station in Taguatinga Sul, where they remain at the disposal of the courts. [@1,2]
