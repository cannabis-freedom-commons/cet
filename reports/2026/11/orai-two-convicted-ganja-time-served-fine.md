In Orai, Additional District Judge Bharatendra Singh's court found both accused
guilty after trial in two ganja-smuggling cases. [@1] The court sentenced each
of them to the jail period already undergone and a fine of 5,000 rupees. [@1]

On 31 August 2026, Dakor Kotwali police arrested Kuthaunda resident Angad Rajput
and recovered 1 kg 50 g of ganja from his possession. [@1] On 15 September 2026,
Kailia police arrested Dinesh Raikwar of Kanshiram Colony in Rath town of
Hamirpur district with ganja. [@1]

Police completed the investigation in both cases and filed charge sheets in
court. [@1] On Tuesday, on the basis of witness statements and the available
evidence, the court convicted both accused and punished them with the jail
period already undergone and a fine of 5,000 rupees each. [@1]
