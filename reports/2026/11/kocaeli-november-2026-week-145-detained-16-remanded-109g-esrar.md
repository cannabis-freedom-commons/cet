Narcotics officers of the Kocaeli provincial police directorate ran the week's
operations to prevent the manufacture and trade of drugs. [@1] During the week
the teams detained 121 people on suspicion of using or holding drugs and 24 on
suspicion of trafficking. [@1] The week's work came to 145 people detained, of
whom 16 were held in custody on a court order. [@1] The 16 were remanded on the
charge of drug trafficking. [@2]

Searches seized 109 grams of hashish, called esrar in Turkey, together with 931
grams of synthetic drugs, 727 synthetic pills and a precision scale. [@1]
Officers also seized 18,120 Turkish lira judged to be proceeds of crime. [@1]
İhlas Haber Ajansı's account breaks the 931 grams down as 829 grams of synthetic
cannabinoid, 102 grams of methamphetamine and 155 grams of assorted drugs. [@2]
The Anadolu Agency report carried by Hürriyet gives the synthetic total as 931
grams and the cannabis haul as 109 grams of esrar. [@3]

In Turkish practice the 145 were held in police detention, gözaltı, while the 16
were remanded in custody by a court, tutuklama, which is why the two numbers
differ. [@1]
