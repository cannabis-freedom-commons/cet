Seven members of a Dnipropetrovsk drug group were notified of suspicion for
illegally growing cannabis and trafficking drugs. [@1] One suspect serves as an
inspector at a Dnipro correctional colony. [@1] A court ordered detention for
three suspects, allowing bail of 2 to 4 million hryvnias each. [@1] Pretrial
measures for the four other suspects were still being decided. [@1] The
Dnipropetrovsk Regional Prosecutor's Office oversees the proceedings and the
pre-trial investigation is continuing. [@1]

Officers carried out 44 searches and seized more than 82 kg of cannabis. [@1]
They also seized 860 cannabis plants and five firearms. [@1] They also seized
24,000 euros in cash, along with vehicles and equipment for growing and
packaging cannabis. [@2] Prosecutors estimated the seized drugs could have been
sold for almost 50 million hryvnias. [@1]

At the beginning of 2026 the suspects established three hidden cannabis
plantations in Kryvyi Rih and Kamianske districts. [@1] They hid the plantations
among agricultural crops, dense vegetation and in shelterbelts. [@1] Members
tended the plants, harvested and dried the crop, and produced cannabis for sale.
[@1] They sold the cannabis in wholesale and retail quantities across the region
through acquaintances. [@1]
