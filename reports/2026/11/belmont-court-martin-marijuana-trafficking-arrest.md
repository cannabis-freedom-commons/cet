Detectives with the Daviess County Sheriff's Office and Kentucky State Police
executed a search warrant in the 5300 block of Belmont Court in eastern Daviess
County on Monday, 23 November 2026, as part of an ongoing drug trafficking
investigation. [@3] Investigators located Charles Edward Martin Jr., 64, at the
residence and arrested him on an active bench warrant. [@2] Martin admitted
owning the guns found in the home, and a prior felony conviction for possession
of a controlled substance barred him from legally possessing firearms. [@1] He
was charged with possession of a handgun by a convicted felon, trafficking in
marijuana, possession of a controlled substance and possession of drug
paraphernalia, and was booked into the Daviess County Jail. [@1]

Officers seized three loaded handguns, ballistic body armor, marijuana packaged
for distribution, suspected methamphetamine, digital scales and glass pipes
containing suspected methamphetamine residue. [@1] Martin was booked into the
Daviess County Detention Center. [@4]

Martin admitted owning and possessing the firearms after being advised of his
Miranda rights. [@2] Investigators determined he had a prior Daviess Circuit
Court felony conviction for first-degree possession of a controlled substance
involving methamphetamine. [@2]
