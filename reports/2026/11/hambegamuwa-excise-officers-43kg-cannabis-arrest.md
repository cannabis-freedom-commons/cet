Police in Hambegamuwa, Thanamalwila, arrested three suspects, including two
Excise Department officers, on suspicion of transporting dried cannabis in a
car. [@1] The arrests were made during a round-up carried out by officers of the
Hambegamuwa Police Station in the area. [@1] When officers stopped and searched
a car, they seized 43 kilograms and 200 grams of dried cannabis from it. [@1]

The Excise officers in the car told police that the cannabis was the stock they
had seized during a raid of their own. [@2] Further police investigations found
no information that such a raid had taken place or that legal authorisation had
been obtained to transport the cannabis. [@2] Police arrested the two Excise
officers and the other suspect after those checks. [@2]

The suspects were to be produced before the Wellawaya Magistrate's Court on 23
November 2026. [@3] Hambegamuwa Police are continuing their investigations into
the incident. [@3]
