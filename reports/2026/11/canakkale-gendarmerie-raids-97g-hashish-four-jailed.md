Gendarmerie anti-narcotics teams detained seven suspects in operations in the
Çanakkale gendarmerie area from 10 to 15 November 2026, coordinated by the
Çanakkale, Lapseki and Biga chief public prosecutor's offices. [@1] The seven
had been identified over alleged drug manufacturing and trafficking, unlawful
cannabis cultivation and drug use, and three addresses were searched under
court-issued search orders. [@3] Proceedings were opened against all seven for
manufacturing and trafficking drugs and for purchasing, possessing or using
drugs for personal use. [@1] After gendarmerie questioning the suspects were
brought before a court, which remanded four to prison while three were released
under judicial-control measures. [@2]

The searches seized 97 grams of kubar esrar, a pressed cannabis resin, and 3
grams of cannabis seeds, along with three precision scales and two drug-grinding
tools. [@1] The same searches also found 254 grams of synthetic cannabinoid, 55
grams of methamphetamine and methamphetamine-impregnated tissues, which are not
counted in the cannabis total. [@3] Officers also seized an unlicensed
blank-firing pistol, 89 blank cartridges and 3,000 Turkish lira assessed as
suspected crime proceeds. [@3]

The operation was run by the Çanakkale Provincial Gendarmerie Command's
narcotics branch in its area of responsibility as part of anti-drug work. [@3]
