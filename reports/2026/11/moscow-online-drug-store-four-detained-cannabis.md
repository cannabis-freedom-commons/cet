Moscow police shut down an online store they say was used to distribute drugs
and detained four suspected members of the group. [@1] The first suspect, a
29-year-old man, was detained at Dubrovka metro station with more than 130 paper
strips containing a substance with LSD. [@1,3] A search of his apartment also
turned up MDMA, hashish and LSD. [@1,3]

Police later detained three more suspected group members, a grower, an
administrator and the organiser of the online store. [@1,3] Searches at their
places of residence found marijuana, hashish, cannabis and psilocybin mushrooms.
[@1,3] The operation was carried out by the Moscow police drug-control directorate
with support from SOBR of the Russian National Guard's Moscow directorate, the
Moscow MVD press service said. [@2,3]

A criminal case was opened for attempted illegal sale of narcotics on a
particularly large scale, under part 3 of Article 30 and part 5 of Article 228.1
of the Russian Criminal Code. [@1,3] All four were remanded in custody. [@1,3]
