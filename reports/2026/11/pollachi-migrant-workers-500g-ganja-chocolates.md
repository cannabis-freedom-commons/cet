Police in Coimbatore district, Tamil Nadu, searched a residence where North
Indian migrant workers were staying near Pollachi on the afternoon of 21
November 2026. [@1] Four of the migrant workers were being questioned by police
in connection with what was found there. [@1] Officers seized 500 grams of ganja
and 28 ganja chocolates from the places where the workers had been staying. [@1]

Negamam police had acted on a tip that ganja was circulating among North Indian
workers in the Negamam and Kattampatti areas near Pollachi. [@1] The search
formed part of a drug-prevention operation carried out in the name of the
Coimbatore district superintendent of police. [@1] Police said they had been
checking grocery stores and small shops in the area to stop ganja and gutka from
circulating. [@1] The station inspector said surveillance had been increased
because many North Indian workers work in the area, and that the seizure
followed a secret tip-off. [@1]
