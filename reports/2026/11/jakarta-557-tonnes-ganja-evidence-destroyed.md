President Prabowo Subianto and national police chief General Suyudi Ario Seto
oversaw the destruction of 601.7 tonnes of drug evidence at Lapangan
Bhayangkara, the national police headquarters in Kebayoran Baru, South Jakarta,
on Thursday 26 November 2026. [@1] The cannabis in the batch was 557,424,488
grams (557.42 tonnes) of ganja, according to the list of evidence police
displayed at the site. [@1] The same list included 432 grams of hashish. [@1]
Police said the evidence came from drug cases uncovered over the period from
November 2025 to November 2026. [@3]

The rest of the batch was other drugs, including 11,250,595 grams of
methamphetamine, 3,066,304 ecstasy pills, 3,454,982 grams of ketamine and 82,173
grams of tembakau gorila, a tobacco-based drug; none of these is counted in the
cannabis figures. [@1] Police estimated the value of the whole 601.7-tonne batch
at 61.6 trillion rupiah, a figure that covers every drug in it rather than
cannabis alone. [@2] The destructions were divided between the headquarters
grounds and PT Wastec International, a hazardous-waste plant in Tangerang. [@3]
Three incinerators were prepared at the site for the burning, with forensic
laboratory officers standing by. [@1]

Syahganda Nainggolan, head of the government's communication agency Bakom, said
the president had declared war on narcotics. [@1] He said foreign powers had
long used drugs as an instrument with which to destroy a state. [@1] Syahganda
said the campaign needed public involvement, particularly to protect children
and young people from drugs. [@1]
