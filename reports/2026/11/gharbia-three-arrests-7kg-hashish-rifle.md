Security services uncovered the circumstances of a post supported by a video
clip that had been circulated on social media. [@1,3,4,6,8,9,12,13,14] The poster complained that
the occupants of a private car were promoting narcotic substances in a village
in Gharbia Governorate. [@1,2,3,4,5,8,9,10,11,12,13,14] Investigators identified the car appearing in the
video and arrested those responsible for the incident. [@1,4,7,9,12,14] The three are
unemployed men, two of whom have criminal records, and they live in Gharbia and
Kafr El Sheikh governorates. [@1,2,3,4,5,6,8,9,10,11,12,13,14]

After legal procedures were completed, officers arrested the suspects while they
held more than 7 kilograms of hashish. [@1,2,3,4,5,7,9,11,13,14] They also held a quantity of ice
drug and an automatic rifle alongside the hashish. [@1,2,3,4,5,6,8,9,10,11,12,13,14] When confronted, the men
admitted committing the act as described and possessing the narcotics for the
purpose of trafficking. [@1,2,3,4,5,6,8,9,10,11,12,13,14] The suspects said they kept the firearms to protect their criminal activity. [@11]

Authorities took the necessary legal measures against the suspects, and the
Public Prosecution took over the investigation. [@1,2,3,4,5,7,8,9,11,12,13,14]
