Dorchester County sheriff's deputies arrested a North Charleston man on Tuesday
after reports of break-ins at empty townhomes. [@1] The man was identified as
Jaquan Frasier, 24, of North Charleston. [@2] Frasier was charged with
second-degree burglary, vandalism, resisting arrest and simple possession of
marijuana, second offense. [@1]

Deputies were patrolling the Planters Knoll area that Tuesday after property
management complained that people were entering unoccupied homes, the sheriff's
office said. [@2] Deputies said the front door of a townhome burst open and an
individual began to flee. [@1] The sheriff's office said the man, later
identified as Frasier, ran out of one of the units and fled down Hunters Ridge
Lane. [@2] Frasier ignored verbal commands to stop and deputies caught him after
a pursuit. [@1] Sheriff Sam Richardson said the arrest showed deputies taking
the initiative to address community concerns and holding individuals accountable
for their actions. [@1] Frasier was booked at the Dorchester County Detention
Center. [@1]
