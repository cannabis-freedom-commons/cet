Sadar Ratia police arrested the alleged main supplier, Vikas, in the case built
on the recovery of 7 kg of doda post and 4 kg of ganja. [@1] The arrested man
was identified as Vikas, son of Ballu, a resident of Dhamtan Sahib village in
Jind district. [@1] With Vikas's arrest, three accused have now been held in the
case, after two accused were arrested earlier. [@1]

The first accused, Chhindrapal Singh alias Chhindra of Nagpur village, was
caught on 13 October 2026 by an Anti-Narcotic Cell Fatehabad team led by
Assistant Sub-Inspector Bhupendra Singh on the Nagpur-Hanspur road. [@1]
Officers recovered 7 kg of doda post and 4 kg of ganja, including the sacks,
from his possession. [@1] Sadar Ratia police registered case number 222 on 13
October 2026 under sections 15(B), 20(B), 61 and 85 of the NDPS Act. [@1]

Police said the arrested accused will be produced before the court. [@1] Police
said investigation into other aspects linked to drug trafficking is continuing.
