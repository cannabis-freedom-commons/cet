Venice state police arrested an Italian citizen in Mestre on the afternoon of
Monday 16 November 2026 on suspicion of producing, trafficking and illegally
possessing narcotics for sale. [@1] The operation began during a patrol in via
Einaudi, where officers of the flying squad stopped a car and, suspicious of the
driver's agitation, checked his backpack and found a first quantity of hashish.
[@2]

A search of the man's home found more of the drug, kept in blocks and hidden in
the bedroom, along with cash in small banknotes, material for preparing and
packaging doses and containers with drug residues, all seized. [@2] Police
described the cash as proceeds of the illegal activity. [@1]

The man was arrested and placed under house arrest at his residence, at the
disposal of the judicial authority and to be tried under rito direttissimo, an
expedited trial procedure. [@1]
