Police on routine patrol in barrio Parque Industrial in La Banda stopped a
46-year-old man and placed him at the disposal of the courts. [@1] The stop took
place on calle Eva Perón, between Huaicurú and Mocovíes, in barrio Parque
Industrial. [@1] El Liberal places the same procedure at the intersection of Eva
Perón and Huaiquiru and says it occurred on the Wednesday in a public street.
[@2] Officers said the man was walking, works as a day labourer and lives in the
same Parque Industrial neighbourhood. [@2]

In his backpack he carried a glass jar and an aluminium-foil wrapper holding
brownish-green plant material, plus a serrated-type knife and other personal
items. [@1] Dangerous-drugs officers field-tested the plant material, which
tested positive for marijuana and weighed 58.81 grams in total. [@1] La Banda
Diario described the marijuana as packaged for retail sale. [@1]

La Banda Diario attributes the initial procedure to the Prevention Division of
Citizen Security Department 4. [@1] El Liberal instead attributes the procedure
to the Motorized Unit. [@2] The duty prosecutor ordered the seizure of the
substances and the knife and the apprehension of the 46-year-old suspect. [@1]
Prosecutor Flavia Torres took charge of the case, which proceeds as an alleged
breach of National Law 23.737 on narcotics. [@2]
