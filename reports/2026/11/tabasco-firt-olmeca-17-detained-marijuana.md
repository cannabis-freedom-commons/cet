Tabasco's security cabinet reported the results of operations carried out
between 16 and 20 November 2026 as part of a permanent security strategy. [@1]
The coordinated FIRT Olmeca actions led to 17 detentions and the seizure of
eight motor vehicles, narcotics and firearms. [@1] La Verdad del Sureste
reported the same 17 detentions and eight vehicles across Nacajuca, Centro and
Huimanguillo. [@2]

In the Pomoca neighbourhood of Nacajuca, state security officers detained six
people on suspicion of retail drug dealing and firearms offences. [@1] Officers
there seized wrappers resembling marijuana along with methamphetamine and crack,
a short firearm, ammunition, radio equipment and two motorcycles with altered
identification numbers. [@1] In the José María Pino Suárez area of Centro
municipality, SSPC officers detained four people. [@1] Those four had various
doses of suspected marijuana, methamphetamine and crack, a long firearm, a
magazine, ammunition, radio equipment and a vehicle. [@1]

In Huimanguillo, one man was detained with a 9 mm firearm, six cartridges, drug
doses and radio equipment, and two minors were detained in possession of drugs.
[@1] A property containing drugs, a car and radio equipment was also secured
under a technical investigation order, and federal ministerial police executed
an arrest warrant for torture. [@1] FIRT Olmeca also carried out community
policing checks, inspecting vehicles and reviewing and interviewing residents in
Centro and four other municipalities. [@1]
