Ghurna police arrested 48-year-old Amirul near the India-Nepal border in Araria
district with 30 kilograms of ganja carried in a sack. [@1] Station house
officer Suman Kumari said police received a tip during evening patrol at about 6
pm on 18 November that a man was carrying ganja across the border toward the
Susar river embankment, informed senior officers and formed a raiding team. [@1]
Narpatganj circle officer Sanjeev Kumar joined the team as magistrate for the
search, and the team reached the road leading to the Jama Masjid from the
Ghurna-Achra road where the carrier dropped the sack on seeing police, tried to
flee and was caught. [@1]

Police identified him on questioning as Amirul, son of late Ejaz and resident of
Pathraha ward number 12, and searched the sack before independent witnesses,
weighing 30 kilograms of ganja on an electronic scale. [@1] Police seized the
ganja, arrested Amirul and registered Ghurna police station case number 111 of
2026 on 18 November 2026 under sections 8 and 20(b)(ii)(C) of the Narcotic Drugs
and Psychotropic Substances Act. [@1] After questioning and legal formalities
the accused was sent to Araria in judicial custody on Thursday while police
continued the investigation. [@1]
