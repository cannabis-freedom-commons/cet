Police in Faridabad arrested Munna Yadav, a resident of Jeevan Nagar Part-2, on
18 November 2026 and said they seized 340 grams of ganja from him. [@1] The
arrest was made by the Crime Branch Sector-56 team while acting against illegal
drugs. [@1] Ganja is the local term for cannabis. [@1]

According to a police spokesperson, the team acted on secret information, caught
Munna in the Gauchi area and recovered 340 grams of ganja. [@1] Police
registered a case against him under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic
Substances (NDPS) Act, India's main anti-drug law, at Mujesar police station.
[@1]

During questioning, Munna told police he had brought 400 grams of ganja from
Delhi's Sadar Bazar for 4,000 rupees. [@1] He was produced in court and action
was taken under the rules. [@1]
