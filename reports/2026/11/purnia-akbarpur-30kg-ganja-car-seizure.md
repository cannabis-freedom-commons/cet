The driver of a Hyundai i20 abandoned the car and fled when police appeared at a
vehicle check near Tirasi crossing in the Akbarpur police station area of Purnia
district, Bihar, on Saturday 21 November 2026. [@2] Police, working with armed
forces, were checking vehicles on a tip that a liquor consignment was passing
through the area. [@2] Officers tried to stop the car near Tirasi village, but
the driver got out and ran. [@3]

A search of the car found a stitched plastic bag of polythene-wrapped ganja
weighing about 30 kilograms. [@2] The ganja had been hidden in the car's boot.
[@3] Police seized the ganja and the car. [@3] Akbarpur station head Abhishek
Kumar Singh said a case was registered under the relevant sections. [@2]

Singh said officers were raiding likely hideouts and using human and technical
intelligence to identify and arrest the fleeing driver. [@2] He said police were
tracing where the ganja had come from and where it was to be delivered, and
whether others were involved in the trafficking. [@3] Live Hindustan reported
that the ganja's source, buyers and trafficking network were under
investigation. [@1]
