Police in Gaziantep detained 285 suspects during operations against drug
trafficking in the city, and a court remanded 52 of them. [@1] The operations
ran over the preceding week and were carried out by teams from the
narcotics-crimes branch of the Gaziantep provincial police directorate. [@2]
Fifty-two of the detained suspects were remanded by the judicial authorities,
the police statement said. [@3]

Searches at the suspects' addresses yielded 12 kilograms 984 grams of esrar, the
cannabis preparation known in Turkey. [@1] The same searches found 420 grams of
synthetic cannabinoid, 190 grams of methamphetamine, 47 drug pills, 30 grams of
cocaine and three precision scales. [@2] The police listed the esrar first among
the seized items, ahead of the synthetic cannabinoid, methamphetamine, pills,
cocaine and scales. [@3]

The detentions and the seizures were published together as the combined results
of the last week's coordinated operations. [@2] The account of the operation was
published on 17 November 2026. [@1]
