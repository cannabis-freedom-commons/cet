A special team of STF North Range Kangra (Dharamshala) was on regular patrol and
surveillance in the area on Friday evening. [@1] At about 7:30 pm the STF
personnel were near Tissa bridge on the Tissa-Chamba main road when a passerby's
suspicious behaviour drew their attention. [@1] The personnel cordoned off the
suspect and stopped him on the spot. [@1] A search of his person recovered 765
grams of charas from his possession. [@1] The arrested man was identified as
36-year-old Hari Lal, son of Durga, a resident of Kandog village (post office
Jungra) in Churah tehsil of Chamba district. [@1]

Police seized the drug and took the accused into custody at once. [@1] The STF
arrested him on the spot and registered a case against him under the NDPS Act at
the STF police station North Range Kangra (Dharamshala). [@1]

The action took place in the Churah valley of Chamba district as part of the
STF's continuing drive against the drug trade. [@1] Shortly before this action
the STF team had recovered a consignment of more than 9 kg of charas in the same
Tissa area. [@1] STF officers said patrolling and intelligence surveillance in
the area would be further intensified to end the trafficking network. [@1]
