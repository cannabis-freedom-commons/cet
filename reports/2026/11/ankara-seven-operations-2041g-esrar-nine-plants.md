The narcotics branch of the Ankara Provincial Gendarmerie Command coordinated
operations across Ankara targeting people who trafficked, transported and used
drugs. [@1] Seven separate incidents were recorded and legal action was taken
against eight suspects. [@1] A DHA account of the same operation said the eight
suspects were detained. [@3]

Officers seized 2,041 grams of esrar, the Turkish term for cannabis resin, and
nine skunk plants. [@1] The same operations also yielded 192 synthetic drug
pills, 23 kilograms of mescaline, 1,055 ecstasy tablets, 120 synthetic
cannabinoid units and 45 grams of synthetic cannabinoid precursor, which are
excluded from the cannabis figures. [@2] The seized materials were confiscated.
[@1]

Judicial proceedings were opened in connection with the incidents. [@3]
