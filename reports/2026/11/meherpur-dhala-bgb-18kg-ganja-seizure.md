In one drive in Daulatpur upazila's Mahishkundi Ghonapara area, BGB arrested a
man named Moh. Lalu, 45, and seized 29 Indian yaba tablets and 10 tapentadol
tablets from him. [@1] Border Guard Bangladesh said it carried out three
separate anti-drug and anti-smuggling drives along the Kushtia border,
recovering a large quantity of Indian drugs and smuggled goods. [@1] Kushtia
Battalion commander Lieutenant Colonel Rashed Kamal Roni confirmed the
information in a press release at noon on 17 November 2026. [@1]

In a separate drive at Dhala field, in the area under Dhala BOP, at about 00:40
on 17 November, officers found 18 kg of Indian ganja and eight bottles of Indian
liquor abandoned. [@1] Asia Post locates that recovery at Dhala field in Gangni
upazila, Meherpur, under the Kushtia Battalion (47 BGB). [@2] BGB said the
materials were found abandoned on the basis of specific and reliable
intelligence. [@2] Earlier the same day, six Indian mobile phones were recovered
unclaimed in the Buri Khal area under Chilmari BOP. [@1]

BGB gave the recovered drugs and smuggled goods a combined estimated seizure
value of 272,199 taka and said legal action against the arrested man was in
process. [@1] The battalion said it was stepping up intelligence surveillance
and would keep conducting regular drives to prevent drug smuggling and secure a
drug-free border. [@1]
