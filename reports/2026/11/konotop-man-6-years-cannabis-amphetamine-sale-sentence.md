A court in Sumy Oblast sentenced a 48-year-old Konotop resident to 6 years and 1
month in prison with confiscation of property for repeated distribution of
narcotic and psychotropic substances. [@2] The verdict was issued under a
guilty-plea agreement concluded with a prosecutor of the Konotop District
Prosecutor's Office. [@2] The convicted man will remain in custody until the
sentence takes legal effect. [@3]

In September 2023 he tried to pass cannabis and amphetamine into a pre-trial
detention centre hidden in a toothpaste tube. [@1] Officers intercepted the
parcel containing the narcotic and psychotropic substances. [@2] In spring 2024
officers documented two further instances of amphetamine sale by the man. [@2]
In one of those instances he enlisted an acquaintance who is mentally
incompetent because of illness to distribute the drugs. [@3] During a search of
his home officers found amphetamine, as well as containers and electronic scales
bearing traces of psychotropic substances. [@2] The court convicted him under
Articles 307(2) and 309(1 and 2) of Ukraine's Criminal Code. [@2]
