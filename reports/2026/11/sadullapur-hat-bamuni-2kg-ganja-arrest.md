District detective branch police detained 42-year-old Abdur Rashid during a
special late-night operation in Sadullapur upazila of Gaibandha district. [@1]
Officers seized 2 kg of dried ganja, the local word for cannabis, in the same
operation. [@1]

The DB team raided Hat Bamuni village in Kamarpara Union No. 10 at 1:40 a.m. on
Tuesday 24 November. [@1] Acting on secret information, the officers recovered
the 2 kg of dried ganja from inside Rashid's own dwelling house. [@1] Police
also alleged that a second man, 35-year-old Mehedi Hasan Rahul, was involved in
the matter. [@1]

Following the seizure a case was filed at Sadullapur police station on 24
November under the Narcotics Control Act. [@1] The raid village of Hat Bamuni
lies in Kamarpara Union No. 10 of Sadullapur upazila, the administrative area
named for the whole operation. [@1]
