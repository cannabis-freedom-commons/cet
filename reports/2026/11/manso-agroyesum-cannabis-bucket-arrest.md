The suspect was identified as 45-year-old Kwaku Ayitey, arrested on 15 November
2026 after police received intelligence that a bus passenger was conveying
suspected narcotics from Kumasi to Manso Datano. [@1] The intelligence came from
an informant who alerted officers to the suspected transportation of the
prohibited substance. [@1] Acting on the tip-off, police intercepted a
commercial Sprinter bus with registration number GT 5144-16 at Manso Agroyesum
while it was travelling with passengers on board. [@1] The driver identified
Ayitey as the owner of the bucket and its contents, leading to his immediate
arrest and transfer to the police station for interrogation. [@1]

A search of the vehicle found a white plastic bucket holding three parcels of
dried leaves suspected to be cannabis, covered with fresh tomatoes. [@1] The
police account recorded the tip at about 1230 hours on 15 November 2026,
describing a male adult transporting substances suspected to be Indian hemp from
Kumasi towards Manso Datano in a Sprinter mini bus. [@1]

During questioning Ayitey admitted owning the suspected narcotics but did not
disclose their source. [@1] He told investigators he was travelling to Manso
Datano to look for a buyer. [@1] The suspect and the seized exhibits were
subsequently transferred to the Manso Nkwanta Divisional Headquarters for
further investigations. [@1] Police said preparations were under way to bring
Ayitey before the court. [@1]
