State police detained Sergio “N”, 38, in Colonia La Estación, Tenango del Valle,
on suspicion of crimes against health. [@1] The arresting officers were from the
Secretaría de Seguridad del Estado de México (SSEM), the State of Mexico police.
[@2] The intervention followed a citizen report of suspected drug selling and
took place during Operativo Argos. [@1]

When the man saw the officers he became evasive, so they asked him for a
preventive inspection. [@1] During the search officers found a transparent
plastic bag of green, dry herb with characteristics similar to marijuana. [@1]
Officers also seized 24 small sealed bags of a white granular substance
resembling the drug known as crystal. [@2] Once the items were found, police
proceeded with the detention and the seizure of the substances. [@1]

After being told his rights, Sergio N was taken with the seized items to the
Centro de Justicia de Tenango del Valle. [@1] He remained at the disposal of the
competent authority, which will determine his legal situation and continue the
investigation. [@2]
