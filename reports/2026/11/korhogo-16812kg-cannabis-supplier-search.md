Officers of the Korhogo anti-drug unit (GRAD) were patrolling the low ground
between the Ossiéné and Kassirimé quarters at about 10:15 on 17 November 2026
when they stopped a man. [@1] He was carrying sachets and balls of cannabis and
Tramadol tablets, and was identified by the initials C.I., 32, living in the
Petit-Paris quarter, before being taken to the police station. [@1] InfoRelayeur
reported that the suspect then gave investigators indications about his alleged
supplier's identity. [@3]

Investigators turned to a man the suspect called his usual supplier, and
officers went to his home, but he was absent. [@1] With authorisation from the
prosecutor at the Korhogo court of first instance, police searched the house
anyway. [@1] Hidden under clothes in a large bag known locally as a bôrô they
found cannabis packed as 13 blocks and 3 ballons (balloon-shaped packages), 392
boulettes (balls) and 42 sachets of OG-Kush-type cannabis, 16.812 kg in total.
[@1] Akwabanews gave the total as 16 kg 812.33 g. [@2] Akwabanews placed the
search at the suspect's home in the Ossiéné quarter. [@2] Officers also seized
111 Tramadol tablets dosed at 100 and 250 mg, 11 packets of OCB rolling paper,
39 lots of plastic film, a grinder and a pair of scissors. [@1] Akwabanews put
the weight of the 111 Tramadol tablets at 55.44 g. [@2] Police estimated the
market value of all the seized products at 1,438,500 CFA francs. [@1] The
operation formed part of Épervier XII and Épervier spéciale, police drives
against drug distribution networks in Korhogo. [@1]

One person was arrested, and the man named as the alleged supplier remained at
large. [@1] The prosecutor was informed and searches continue for the supplier
and for anyone else in the distribution circuit. [@1]
