Police in Morrinhos apprehended an adolescent on Monday 16 November 2026 on
suspicion of an offence analogous to drug trafficking. [@1] The youth was taken
to the Civil Police together with the seized material. [@1] The Guardianship
Council was called and followed the case. [@1]

Officers found about 2 kg of maconha during the stop of a ride-hailing car. [@1]
The team found pieces of maconha and several portions of the same drug that the
force said were ready for sale. [@1] Officers of the Specialized Policing
Company of Caldas Novas stopped the vehicle. [@1]

Intelligence officers of the 36th Military Police Battalion had been monitoring
the case after receiving trafficking complaints. [@1] During the monitoring, the
team learned that a ride-hailing trip would be used to deliver drugs in the
city. [@1]
