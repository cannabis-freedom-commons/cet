Two brothers aged 21 and 22 were tried in immediate proceedings at the judicial
court in Nantes on Monday 16 November 2026, charged with acquiring, possessing,
transporting and supplying narcotic products. [@1] They admitted the facts but
disputed the scale alleged by the prosecution. [@1] The presiding judge, Pierre
Dupire, commented on how close the two appeared, and the younger, more talkative
brother answered that they were "the best of friends". [@1] He added that they
were "not bad people, just people short of money". [@1]

The court sentenced the brothers to firm prison terms of 18 and 42 months. [@1]
The case followed the discovery of a clandestine drug laboratory, an illegal
workshop, at Grandchamp-des-Fontaines, north of Nantes. [@1] According to an
accounting notebook found on the scene, the workshop was used to move several
kilos of cocaine and cannabis. [@1] The two brothers were suspected of having
organised the activity in Nantes. [@1]
