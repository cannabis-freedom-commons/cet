Personnel from Carabineros' OS-7 anti-drug section surprised the traffickers on
Ruta Cinco Sur. [@1,2] The police operation began in Curicó province and ended in
Talca province during the early hours of Thursday 19 November 2026. [@1] The car was intercepted at kilometre 224 of Ruta 5 Sur in the commune of Río Claro with support from other police units. [@2,3] The two
detained car occupants are aged 29 and 18, and the older man has previous
convictions. [@1,2] One detainee lives in the O’Higgins Region and the other in the Biobío Region. [@2] Both defendants were to appear before the Curicó Guarantee
Court to face criminal drug-trafficking charges, where the prosecution would
seek pretrial detention. [@1] The court ordered pretrial detention for both men and set a three-month period for the investigation. [@2,3] Both men were placed at the disposition of the Curicó Guarantee Court and formalised for drug trafficking. [@2,3]

Inside the car officers found 25 packages wrapped in adhesive tape containing
processed cannabis sativa, totalling 25 kilos. [@1] Police valued the seized
drugs at 25 million pesos, equal to just over 45,000 doses removed from
circulation. [@1]

OS-7 officers were carrying out random checks on the road when they tried to
stop a vehicle travelling from north to south with two occupants inside. [@1] Carabineros said the road-check operation was part of a Ruta 5 Sur enforcement plan that would extend to some parallel routes. [@3]
The occupants ignored police orders and accelerated to evade the preventive
inspection. [@1,2] OS-7 agents then pursued the vehicle at a distance until they
detained it, according to Maule zone chief General Carlos Contreras. [@1] The pursuit covered about 30 kilometres before the car was stopped in Río Claro. [@3] During the pursuit the occupants allegedly tried to get rid of some containers holding illicit substances. [@2,3]
