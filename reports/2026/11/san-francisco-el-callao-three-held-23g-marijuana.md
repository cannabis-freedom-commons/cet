The Special Response Team of the Bolivarian Police of Zulia state carried out
two procedures in barrio El Callao, Domitila Flores parish, San Francisco
municipality, detaining three people with prohibited substances in their
possession. [@1] One man was apprehended for microtrafficking after officers on
patrol spotted him on foot acting suspiciously on seeing the patrol approach
past 12:45 in the afternoon. [@1] Officers relying on article 91 of the Organic
Code of Criminal Procedure conducted a body search and found in one trouser
pocket a white synthetic wrapper holding brownish-green lumpy plant remains that
tested positive for marijuana weighing about 13 grams. [@1] The man was
identified as Ángel Navarro and was kept under police custody for possession of
13 grams of marijuana, with the evidence retained for processing under a file
sent to the Public Ministry prosecutor. [@1]

In the same barrio, Antony Antonio Diaz Guilles and Anthony José Uzcátegui
Espinoza were detained with narcotic and psychotropic substances after being
searched at 4:30 in the morning on 19 November during patrol work. [@1] Officers
of the Special Response Team saw the two walking on the main avenue of the
community diagonal to Sumaque on the public road and said they quickened their
pace and tried to enter a dwelling inside the barrio on seeing the patrol
approach. [@1] After officers ordered them to stop, a search recovered synthetic
wrappers with plant remains positive for marijuana weighing ten grams. [@1] Both
men had their constitutional rights read to them, were placed under police
custody, and the collected evidence was processed under a file sent to the
Public Ministry prosecutor. [@1]
