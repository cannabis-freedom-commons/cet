Three men were arrested and charged following a police operation in Trelawny,
Jamaica, in which officers seized US$50,980 (about J$8 million) in cash and
about eight ounces of ganja. [@1] The Jamaica Constabulary Force said the
operation was carried out by members of the Trelawny Division, the Area Fugitive
Apprehension Team, Specialized Operations and the Public Safety and Traffic
Enforcement Branch. [@1]

The operation was conducted under the supervision of Trelawny Divisional
Commander Superintendent Velonique Campbell. [@1] The three men were charged
with offences relating to possession of ganja and an offensive weapon. [@1]
Police said the operation formed part of ongoing efforts to clamp down on
criminal activities and enhance public safety throughout the parish. [@1]
