Municipal officers on patrol on Rua Olaria in the Topolândia neighbourhood
detained a 28-year-old man and three 16-year-old adolescents on the afternoon of
Tuesday 17 November 2026. [@3] Officers said they saw four people with backpacks
and bags, one apparently buying drugs from another, who fled toward a wooded
area when approached but were caught. [@1]

Officers seized portions of cocaine, crack, marijuana and a substance recorded
as K2, part in hundreds of portions ready for sale, with the police report
putting the combined total at about 2.6 kg. [@1] Officers also collected R$195
in cash and three mobile phones. [@2]

All four were taken to the 1st Police District of São Sebastião with the seized
material. [@2] The delegate ordered the 28-year-old man's flagrante detention
for drug trafficking, association for trafficking and corruption of minors. [@1]
The three 16-year-olds were held for acts analogous to drug trafficking and
association for trafficking. [@1] One adolescent resisted and fought with an
officer, and the officer cut his hand on glass in the area, suffering severe
bleeding, and was taken to the UPA emergency unit. [@2] The case remains under
Civil Police investigation. [@2]
