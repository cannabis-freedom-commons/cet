Police registered a case against two people after seizing 10 grams of ganja from
students at the Abode Valley apartments in Potheri on 14 November 2026. [@1] The
search involved more than 200 police officers acting under the Tambaram police
commissionerate. [@1]

Officers divided into eight groups and searched homes at Abode Valley, VGN and
Aishwarya apartment complexes between 6 a.m. and 9 a.m. [@1] A contemporaneous
account described officers searching rooms at Abode Valley, where private
college students lived. [@2] The searches followed repeated complaints to police
about ganja and other drugs being sold to residents of the apartment buildings.
[@1] Residents called for regular patrols and action against people storing and
selling drugs in the area. [@1]
