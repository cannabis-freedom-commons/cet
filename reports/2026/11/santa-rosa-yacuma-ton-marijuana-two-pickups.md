Officers of Bolivia's Special Force to Fight Drug Trafficking on patrol and
interdiction duty under the Frontera Segura 2.0 plan intercepted two pickups
found abandoned beside the road leading to the municipality of Santa Rosa de
Yacuma in Beni. [@1] Those aboard the vehicles had apparently fled when they
noticed the police presence. [@1]

Officers searched both of the abandoned vehicles and found 36 yute bags holding
several packages containing a greenish substance. [@1] A field test of the
substance was positive for marijuana, which officers reported as weighing more
than one tonne in total. [@1] As a result of the operation, officers seized the
two pickups together with three shotguns, a rifle and ammunition. [@1]

FELCN officers then combed the surrounding area to try to find the people who
had been aboard the two abandoned vehicles. [@1] That follow-up search by FELCN
officers ended without success. [@1] The two pickups had been found abandoned at
the side of the road to Santa Rosa de Yacuma when officers arrived to inspect
them. [@1]
