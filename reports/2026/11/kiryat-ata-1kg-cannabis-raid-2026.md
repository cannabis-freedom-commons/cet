One suspect was arrested after detectives from the Kiryat Ata police station
searched a building in the city and seized more than one kilogram of material
suspected to be cannabis. [@1] The suspect was taken to the station for
questioning. [@1]

The raid was one of several operational activities carried out by the station in
the Asher area at the end of the previous week against drug trafficking and
illegal-residency offences. [@1] Police said the activity had led to arrests and
significant seizures as part of their continuing effort to maintain public
security in the area. [@1]

The seized substance was described as suspected cannabis. [@1] The operation was
presented as part of the police campaign against the illicit drug trade in the
area. [@1] The material was found at the searched premises and the arrest was
made there. [@1]

Israel Police said it would continue acting firmly against drug traffickers,
illegal residents and their helpers to preserve public order and security. [@1]
