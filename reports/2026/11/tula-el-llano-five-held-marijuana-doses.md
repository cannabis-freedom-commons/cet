Five men were detained in the colonia El Llano, a neighbourhood of Tula de
Allende, Hidalgo, and placed at the disposal of the Ministerio Público after an
operation in which officers seized three firearms and several doses of
substances with characteristics of marijuana, cocaine and crystal. [@1] The
Procuraduría General de Justicia del Estado de Hidalgo (PGJEH) said the
detentions came from an investigation into narcomenudeo, or retail drug dealing,
in the Tula region, with intelligence work by the Agencia de Investigación
Criminal (AIC). [@1] The five were identified only by the initials C. D. R. H.,
U. A. R. L., C. C. C., I. R. C. and F. J. C. B. [@1]

According to the prosecutor's report, the agents found two long firearms and one
short firearm. [@1] The substances had characteristics of marijuana, cocaine and
crystal, but the available report gave no quantities and no results of forensic
analysis. [@1]

At the end of the intervention the agents placed the five men at the disposal of
the ministerial authority and handed over the weapons and substances as
evidence. [@1]
