As a result of the two procedures, officers detained three men aged 27, 29 and
44. [@1] After investigative work and the collection of evidence, officers
carried out two raids on properties in the San Antonio and Santa Rosa de Lima
neighbourhoods of that town. [@1] Officers of the Narcocriminal Investigation
Division of Joaquin V. Gonzalez carried out an investigation into alleged retail
selling of narcotics that began from information received through the Denuncias
Web site. [@1]

In the same procedures, officers seized more than 400 doses of narcotics,
described as including marijuana, pasta base and cocaine. [@1] Officers also
seized cash, mobile telephones, a gram scale, scissors and other elements
described as being of interest to the case. [@1]

The procedure was directed by the Criminal Prosecutor Office and the Guarantees
Court of Joaquin Victor Gonzalez, and the detained people and the seized
elements were placed at the disposal of the judiciary. [@1]
