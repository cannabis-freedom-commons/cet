Police in Tiruppur city carried out searches on Friday and arrested six people
who were keeping ganja, gutka, liquor bottles and lottery tickets for sale. [@1]
Near Iduvai within Mangalam police station limits, officers arrested Chinnan
aged 27, Hariprasad aged 18 and Jayanth aged 18 who were holding 3.100 kg of
ganja and put them in jail. [@1] Dinamalar places the same three ganja arrests
near the Iduvai TASMAC shop, identifies them as residents of
Bharathipuram-Annamalai Garden in Iduvai and Thirunagar in Parapalayam, and says
3 kg of ganja was seized from them. [@2]

In a separate check near the Gandhinagar bus stop, Anupparpalayam police
arrested Ramu aged 60 who was holding 5.340 kg of banned gutka and Balraj aged
39 who was selling lottery tickets. [@1] Near Sevanthampalayam, Nallur police
arrested Samir Mandal aged 32 for illegally selling liquor and are continuing to
question him. [@1] The Mangalam, Anupparpalayam and Nallur police actions were
reported as parts of the same Friday search operation across Tiruppur city, with
each station reporting arrests in its own area. [@1]
