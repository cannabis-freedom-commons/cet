SRB members arrested a father and son at Dhunat Mor in Bogura on Friday 20
November 2026 and seized 3 kg 900 g (3.9 kg) of ganja from them. [@1] The two
were Sharitullah, 65, and his son Nurunnabi, 45, residents of the Kursha Feriya
area of Fulbari in Kurigram district. [@1] The operation was mounted around
quarter past twelve on the day the arrests were reported. [@1]

The account came from SRB-12, the force's Bogura base. [@1] The ganja arrest was
one of three separate operations that SRB members carried out in different
places. [@1] In one, carried out the previous night in Bogura city's Shibvati
Kalitala area, officers arrested a 48-year-old wanted in a murder case. [@1] In
another, in Tangail the same night, they arrested a 35-year-old wanted in an
attempt-to-murder case. [@1]
