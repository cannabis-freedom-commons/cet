Law enforcement officers in Khmelnytskyi Oblast received information that
cannabis was being grown at a private household in the village of Reshnivka,
Khmelnytskyi district, and detained a 28-year-old man there. [@1] The detainee
was a serviceman who had left his military unit without permission in autumn
2025 and had been on the wanted list since then, police said. [@1] He lived in
the region without registering at his actual place of residence. [@3]

Officers of the Khmelnytskyi district police department of the Main Directorate
of the National Police planned the operation with fighters from the rapid
response unit of the Khmelnytskyi zonal department of the Military Law
Enforcement Service, known as VSP. [@1] After the detention the serviceman was
handed to police for further procedural action. [@2]

Criminal proceedings were opened over the possible unlawful cultivation of
cannabis under part 1 of Article 310 of the Criminal Code of Ukraine, and a
pre-trial investigation is continuing. [@1]
