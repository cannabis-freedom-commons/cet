Guardia Civil officers arrested twelve people, nine in Cantabria and three in
Asturias, in operation HELES against a suspected organisation dedicated to drug
trafficking and insurance fraud. [@1] The network supplied cocaine delivered in
the car parks of public establishments and moved under heavy security, including
shuttle vehicles, to hiding places in Cantabria and Asturias. [@1] The twelve
detainees were nine men and three women aged between 31 and 68. [@1] Four of
them were remanded in provisional prison after appearing before the court. [@3]

On 17 November officers searched seven addresses in Cantabria, in Val de San
Vicente, Torrelavega, Suances, Potes and Cillorigo de Liébana, and four in
Asturias, in Llanes and Peñamellera Baja. [@1] The searches produced 266 grams
of hashish and small quantities of marijuana, along with nearly a kilo of
cutting agent, 80 pills of various substances and more than four kilos of
cocaine. [@1] Most of the cocaine was found in the searches at Val de San
Vicente, in Cantabria, and Llanes, in Asturias. [@2] Officers also seized
documents and scales, a loaded pistol prepared to fire, prohibited weapons, 21
mobile phones, seven vehicles and almost 80,000 euros in cash. [@1]

One of the two leaders, who lives in Torrelavega, is accused of arranging
simulated traffic accidents in Cantabria, photographing the damage and reporting
it to insurers without alerting the Guardia Civil, defrauding about 15,000 euros
so far. [@1] The fraud organiser used people who owed him money from buying
drugs from the network. [@1] The same man is accused of laundering the proceeds
of drug sales by buying vehicles and property registered in the name of a third
person who was also arrested. [@1] The investigation is directed by the civil
and instruction section of the court at San Vicente de la Barquera, coordinated
with Cantabria's special anti-drug prosecutor's office and carried out by the
Guardia Civil's judicial police unit in Cantabria. [@1] The Guardia Civil says
the operation remains open and further arrests are not ruled out. [@4]
