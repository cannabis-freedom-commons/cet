Avdhootwadi police in Yavatmal arrested a woman red-handed on 17 November 2026
on suspicion of illegally selling ganja. [@1] The operation took place at Amba
Nagar, within the Avdhootwadi police station area, after police received
information that a woman there was keeping and selling ganja without permission.
[@1] Officers acted on the tip by laying a trap and raiding the spot. [@1]

Police seized a total of 2 kilograms 528 grams of ganja from the woman, which
they valued at an estimated 50,000 rupees. [@1] The report's lead describes the
haul as two and a half kilograms of ganja and 50 thousand rupees' worth of
seized material. [@1]

A case was registered against the woman under India's Narcotic Drugs and
Psychotropic Substances Act, or NDPS Act, and further legal proceedings were
under way. [@1] The action was carried out by assistant police inspectors of the
Avdhootwadi crime-detection branch and the local crime branch under the Yavatmal
superintendent of police and other senior officers. [@1]
