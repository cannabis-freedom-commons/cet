Montenegro's Supreme Court extended the detention of 27-year-old M.S. from Zeta
by another three months. [@1] M.S. is suspected of attempting to smuggle around
20 kg of marijuana across the Scepan Polje border crossing in August. [@1]
Before the Higher State Prosecutor's Office, he said he had agreed to transport
the drugs to Bosnia-Herzegovina because of gambling debts totalling €24,500.
[@1] He claimed the people he owed money to had promised to reduce his debt in
return. [@1]

Police found 20 packages of marijuana hidden between the rear seat backrest and
the luggage compartment of the Mercedes driven by M.S. [@1] M.S. said he had
been instructed to leave the vehicle behind his house with the key on the wheel
so others could place the drugs inside. [@1] He was then instructed to drive
toward Foca and await further instructions. [@1] He claimed the organisers
contacted him through protected numbers, WhatsApp and Zangi. [@1]

DNA analysis did not link M.S. to biological material found on several items
examined in the investigation. [@1] Despite that result, the Supreme Court
extended his detention by another three months. [@1]
