Thiruvallur Town police stopped people standing suspiciously near the
Thiruvallur-Uthukottai toll gate within the town limits and questioned them,
during which they spoke nervously. [@1] Police arrested three men from
Tiruttani, identified as 27-year-old Udayakumar, 24-year-old Chandran and
22-year-old Anandbabu alias Omkar Anandbabu. [@1] The three men were later
produced before a court and lodged in Puzhal Central Prison. [@1]

Officers searching their bag found 21 kg 70 g of ganja in their possession. [@1]
Police seized 21 kg 70 g of ganja from the three men. [@1] Police said the ganja
had been brought from Andhra Pradesh via Tiruttani and Thiruvallur toward
Chennai for sale to school and college students and young people. [@1]

The checks were part of intensive vehicle inspections ordered by Thiruvallur
district Superintendent of Police Sai Praneeth against people smuggling and
selling ganja. [@1]
