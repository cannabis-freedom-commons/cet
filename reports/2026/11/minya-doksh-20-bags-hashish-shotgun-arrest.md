Minya Directorate of Security officers arrested a man wanted in a
drug-trafficking case, known by the nickname al-Doksh, in the jurisdiction of
the first Minya police district, finding him with 20 bags of hashish and a
shotgun. [@1] The arrest was led by Major Amr Faisal, head of investigations at
the first Minya police station, in an operation against drug trafficking and
possession of unlicensed firearms. [@1] The arrest was made during a security
campaign supervised by the Minya security director, the directorate's head of
criminal investigations and its head of investigations, with Major Amr Faisal
leading the operation on the ground. [@3]

Officers seized 20 bags of hashish and a shotgun from the suspect and impounded
the seizures. [@3] After the arrest, police took the necessary legal steps,
filed a report on the incident and prepared to refer the case to the competent
investigating authorities. [@2]
