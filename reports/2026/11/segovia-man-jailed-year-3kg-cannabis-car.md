The Audiencia Provincial de Segovia dismissed the appeal by a man convicted of
drug trafficking by criminal courtroom No. 2 of Segovia's Tribunal de Instancia.
[@1] That court had imposed one year in prison and a 22,000-euro fine for drug
trafficking. [@1] The same judgment acquitted him of the misappropriation charge
over the rental car on which he had also stood trial. [@1]

On 8 July 2023 the man parked the rental car he had taken over from a friend
around midnight in the Plaza de la Universidad in Segovia to go to a bar. [@1]
National Police officers watching the car because it was reported for
misappropriation detained him when he came out of the bar. [@1] Local Police
officers called to move the vehicle noticed a strong smell of hashish and, on
opening the bonnet, found packages hidden under the ventilation grille. [@1]

Hidden there were 17 packages of cannabis resin, including ten tablets marked
'Gold Ketama', two marked 'FatimamAeur' and marijuana paste packages marked
'Zkittles'. [@1] The courts found the 3.136 kilos, valued at 21,420 euros, were
intended for sale to third parties. [@1] The appeal court rejected his claims
that others had used the street-parked car and that fingerprints were not taken,
citing his exclusive use of the car, the hidden compartment and the obvious
smell. [@1]
