A court in Kharkiv Oblast sentenced a 31-year-old resident of Izium to 10 years
in prison with confiscation of all her property except her home. [@1] The court
found her guilty of illegally acquiring and storing narcotic drugs and
psychotropic substances for sale on an especially large scale under Article
307(3) of Ukraine's Criminal Code. [@1] Prosecutors proved that she kept
cannabis and the psychotropic substance PVP at her home, preparing them for
sale. [@1]

During a search in April 2025 officers seized 234 zip packets of PVP weighing
almost 50 grams, along with cannabis, cannabis extract, electronic scales and
mobile phones. [@1] At trial, witnesses confirmed that they had bought the
psychotropic substance from the woman. [@1] The buyers said they transferred
money to a bank card and then collected the drugs from so-called zakladky dead
drops. [@1] The case was reported by the Kharkiv Oblast prosecutor's office, as
carried by Dumka. [@1]

The defence insisted that she had kept the seized substances for her own use.
[@1] The court instead relied on the quantity, the packaging, the scales and the
witness testimony as confirming an intent to sell. [@1] She will remain in
custody until the verdict takes legal effect. [@1]
