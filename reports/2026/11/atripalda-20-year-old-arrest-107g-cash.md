Carabinieri from the Atripalda station arrested a 20-year-old local man in
flagrante for alleged possession of drugs for dealing. [@2] Officers tried to
check him during an evening territorial patrol, and he fled on foot, pushing and
shoving the officers to force his way through. [@1] Officers blocked him safely
after a few metres. [@1] After the formalities, he was taken to his home under
house arrest at the disposal of the judicial authority. [@1]

The personal and home search seized about 90.1 grams of hashish and 17.5 grams
of marijuana. [@1] Officers also seized assorted precision-weighing and
dose-packaging material. [@1] They also seized 8,701.60 euros in various
banknotes, considered proceeds of dealing. [@1]

The operation was part of prevention and repression of drug use and dealing
ordered by the Avellino provincial Carabinieri command. [@1] The suspect is
presumed innocent until a final conviction establishes guilt. [@1]
