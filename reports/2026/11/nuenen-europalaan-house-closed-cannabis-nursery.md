Mayor Freek van Genugten closed a house on the Europalaan in Nuenen on Tuesday
10 November 2026. [@1] The municipality gives the closure date as 10 November
2026 and the duration as a period of 3 months. [@2] Police found a cannabis
nursery in the house during a check. [@2]

The outlet likewise says a cannabis nursery was found in the house during a
check. [@1] The municipality closed the premises under Article 13b of the Opium
Act and the municipal Damocles policy. [@2] The municipality says drug
criminality has a major impact on the surroundings through fire risk and
unsafety around the premises. [@2]
