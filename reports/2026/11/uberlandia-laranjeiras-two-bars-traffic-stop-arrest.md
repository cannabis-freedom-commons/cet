A 30-year-old man was arrested on the night of Wednesday 18 November 2026, at
about 19:50, on suspicion of drug trafficking during a traffic inspection on
Avenida Vereador Carlito Cordeiro in the Laranjeiras neighbourhood of
Uberlândia, in the Brazilian state of Minas Gerais. [@1]

The Military Police said the man was driving a white Chevrolet Onix Plus along
the avenue when officers on the traffic operation approached and stopped him.
[@1] When officers inspected the car they found two bars of marijuana inside the
Chevrolet Onix Plus. [@2] The operation was intended to verify documentation,
vehicle conditions and possible irregularities. [@3]

The man was arrested in flagrante and taken to the on-duty station of the Civil
Police, together with the suspected drugs and two mobile phones collected during
the approach. [@1] The Chevrolet Onix Plus was removed from the scene and taken
to a licensed impound lot. [@1]
