Cocaine and cannabis were found on the passengers. [@1] The pair were a
38-year-old man and a 20-year-old woman. [@1] On Tuesday 17 November 2026 they
were referred to the prosecutor's office and tried by CRPC (comparution sur
reconnaissance préalable de culpabilité), the French procedure in which an
accused person acknowledges guilt and the prosecutor proposes a sentence that a
judge then approves instead of holding a full trial. [@1] The charge was
détention et usage de produits stupéfiants, possession and use of narcotic
products. [@1] Both were convicted. [@1]

The driver, who was not among the two convicted passengers, will be tried in
April 2027. [@1] The case follows a refusal to comply on the evening of Sunday
15 November 2026 on avenue Thiers in Brive-la-Gaillarde, in the Corrèze
department of south-central France, an incident that ended with shots fired by
the national police. [@1]
