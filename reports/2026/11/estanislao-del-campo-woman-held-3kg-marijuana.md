Officers of the Comisaría Estanislao del Campo detained a 49-year-old woman
during a preventive patrol in Estanislao del Campo. [@1] The woman had just got
off a long-distance bus coming from the city of Formosa when officers
intercepted her. [@1] After the find she was detained and notified of her
constitutional rights and guarantees. [@1] She was later transferred to
Provincial Penitentiary Unit No. 3, where she remains held at the disposal of
the courts. [@1]

The operation took place during the early hours of Monday while officers were on
preventive patrols. [@1] The incident occurred at about 3:10 when police saw the
long-distance bus from Formosa arrive. [@1] She was carrying two burlap bags
containing clothing. [@1] Officers inspected the bags in the presence of
witnesses and found inside five packages containing plant material. [@1]
Officers from the Las Lomitas Dangerous Drugs unit then carried out chemical
orientation tests on the seized substance. [@1] The analysis was positive for
marijuana. [@1] Officers seized more than three kilograms of marijuana in five
wrappers, plus cash and items of interest for the investigation. [@1]

The case is under the intervention of Court of Instruction and Correction No. 3
of the First Judicial District. [@1] Judicial proceedings were opened to advance
the investigation and establish the circumstances in which the substance was
being transported. [@1]
