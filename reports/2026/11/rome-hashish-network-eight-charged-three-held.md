Carabinieri of the Bracciano company placed eight people under search decrees on
charges that included possession and trafficking of narcotics. [@2] On the same
order, issued by the investigating judge at Tivoli court on the prosecutor's
request, three other people were placed under precautionary measures, two in
prison and one under house arrest, as the suspected arsonists. [@3]

The investigation began with an arson attack on the night of 27-28 October 2025
at Castelnuovo di Porto, where flames destroyed nine garbage compactors
belonging to Ekorec, the company contracted to collect the town's waste. [@1] It
later established evidence of a flourishing cocaine and hashish trafficking
network rooted in Rome's Porta Portese, Monti Tiburtini, Jonio and Monte Sacro
neighbourhoods, run in part by the same suspects. [@3] Carabinieri documented
numerous systematic drug sales and reconstructed how the illicit proceeds were
divided. [@1]

The same investigation linked one of the arson suspects to a bomb attack on the
night of 31 December 2025 in Rome's Via Monte Cervialto, where a homemade bomb
was placed against a tobacconist's vending machine. [@1] All the proceedings
remain at the preliminary investigation stage. [@4] For the suspects, the
presumption of innocence applies until a final conviction. [@1]
