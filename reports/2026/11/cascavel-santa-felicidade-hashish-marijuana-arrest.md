A 31-year-old man was arrested for drug trafficking on the afternoon of Thursday
19 November 2026 in Cascavel, in western Paraná. [@1] Police acted on
information about a suspect making drug deliveries in the city centre. [@1]

An officer on patrol identified a motorcycle matching the description given in
the tip-off. [@1] The rider was stopped in front of a residential condominium
and nothing illegal was found in the personal search. [@1] Officers noticed the
man was nervous and gave inconsistent answers, and nearby, in a drain, they
found two packets of cocaine. [@1] Police records showed earlier
drug-trafficking entries, and the man first gave a false address before pointing
officers to a house in the Santa Felicidade neighbourhood. [@1]

With the man's permission officers entered the house and found different types
of drugs, a precision scale, R$1,305 in cash, a notebook, a mobile phone and a
pack of zip-lock bags. [@1] In all, police seized 9.89 grams of cocaine, 85
grams of hashish, 6 grams of ecstasy, 0.17 milligram of LSD and 220 grams of
marijuana. [@1] The man, the drugs and the other seized items, including a
motorcycle, were taken to the police station for the formal procedures. [@1]
