Police in Junín carried out two preventive operations that ended with two people
apprehended for marijuana possession and a motorcycle seized for lack of
documentation. [@1] In the first, officers of the Unidad Policía de Prevención
Local (UPPL) stopped an adult man near San Martín and Rivadavia avenues during a
preventive patrol. [@1]

A search of his belongings found a dose of marijuana, which an orientative test
indicated was cannabis sativa. [@1] The Junín drug prosecutor's office opened
proceedings for an offence under article 14, second paragraph, of law 23.737.
[@1] In the second, agents of the Sección Apoyo de Seguridad Urbana (SASU)
detained two young people at the corner of República and Soria, in the Foetra
neighbourhood. [@1] One of them, aged 16, was carrying marijuana, and the drug
prosecutor's office and the corresponding judicial and youth assistance bodies
are handling the case. [@1]

In the same check officers seized a 110cc Gilera motorcycle ridden by an
18-year-old who lacked the papers required to ride it. [@2] Proceedings were
opened under the national traffic law 24.449 with the local misdemeanour court.
[@1]
