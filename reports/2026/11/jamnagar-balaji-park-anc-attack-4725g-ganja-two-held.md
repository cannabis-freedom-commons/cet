Constables Divyarajsinh Jadeja and Harvijaysinh of the Anti-Narcotics Cell were
on patrol to gather information. [@1] A police anti-narcotics team moved into
Balaji Park in Jamnagar on Sunday night. [@2] The officers found Thakor Kalam
Damor, Dharmendra Kalam Damor, Radha Kalam Damor and Meera Sunil Bilwa standing
with bags resembling cement sacks in suspicious circumstances. [@1] When the
officers identified themselves as police and asked to open a bag, all four
became agitated and attacked the police. [@1] Meera Damor and Radha Damor held
Constable Divyarajsinh Jadeja while Thakor Damor and Dharmendra Damor pelted
stones. [@1] Divyarajsinh suffered injuries to the head, ear and shoulder and a
fracture to a finger on his right hand. [@1] Constable Harvijaysinh was also
beaten. [@1] Both officers were injured in the attack. [@1,3] A 108 ambulance crew
shifted the injured constable to GG Hospital for treatment. [@2,3] Reinforcements
from the Local Crime Branch and City-C Division police rushed to the spot. [@2]

Police checked the bags and found 4 kg 725 g of a substance that appeared to be
ganja, which police valued at Rs 2,36,250. [@1,3] Police detained Radha Damor and
Meera Bilwa for questioning, while Thakor Damor and Dharmendra Damor fled the
scene. [@1,3] A preliminary inquiry found no prior criminal history for the four
accused. [@1]

City-C Division police registered a case against all four for obstructing duty
and assault under BNS sections 221, 121(1), 121(2), 109(1) and 54 and GP Act
section 135(1). [@1] Police also registered a trafficking case under NDPS Act
sections 8(C), 20(B)(2) and 29, India's drug law, and started moves to catch the
absconders. [@1] The raid was conducted by Jamnagar police's anti-narcotics wing
within City-C Division limits after tip-offs about narcotics activity. [@2]
Further investigation into the attack is under way. [@2,3]
