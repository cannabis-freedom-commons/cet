Kadi police arrested Sahbaj Yasinbhai Sheikh, 30, a resident of Parmarwada, on
suspicion of selling ganja packed in hot-masala sachets from his peanut cart.
[@1] Officers searching the cart found a saffron-coloured bag under a spread
cloth holding 12 small plastic packets marked 'Osm hot masala'. [@1] A
scientific officer from the Mehsana Forensic Science Laboratory's mobile van was
called to the scene. [@1] A narcotics test kit confirmed that the packets held
ganja. [@1] The ganja weighed 36.180 grams on an electric scale and police
valued it at ₹1,809. [@1]

Police said a tip-off told them that Sheikh kept ganja in his peanut cart near
Ashrafi tea stall, in front of the Kadi court, and sold it there. [@1] A team
led by PSI R.R. Parmar, on the instructions of PI A.S. Rabari, reached the stall
in the evening and found the accused selling peanuts. [@1] Police also seized a
Vivo mobile phone worth ₹5,000 from the accused. [@1] The total property seized,
including the ganja, the 12 packets, the plastic bag and the phone, was valued
at ₹6,809. [@1]

The accused had no legal licence to keep or sell ganja, police said. [@1] On a
complaint by PSI Parmar, Kadi police station registered a case under sections
8(c) and 20(b)(2)(a) of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act. [@1]
