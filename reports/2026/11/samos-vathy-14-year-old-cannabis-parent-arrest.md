Police arrested a 14-year-old girl in the Vathy area of Samos on Friday 20
November 2026, after a police check found a small quantity of cannabis in her
possession. [@1] A parent of the minor was arrested in the same case, accused of
neglecting the supervision of a minor. [@1,2]

According to the police, officers of the Crime Prevention and Suppression Team,
known by its Greek acronym O.P.K.E., of the Samos Subdirectorate for the
Prosecution and Investigation of Crimes carried out the check. [@1,2] During the
check, the officers found a plastic grinder containing a small quantity of raw,
unprocessed cannabis in the 14-year-old's possession. [@1,2] Officers seized both
the grinder and the narcotic substance. [@1]

A case file for possession of narcotic substances was opened against the minor,
while her parent was arrested for neglect of supervision of a minor. [@1] The
preliminary investigation was conducted by the Drug Prosecution Department of
the Samos Subdirectorate for the Prosecution and Investigation of Crimes. [@1]
