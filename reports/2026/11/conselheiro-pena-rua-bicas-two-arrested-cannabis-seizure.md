Military Police arrested two men aged 22 and 25 in flagrante in Conselheiro Pena
on Tuesday 17 November 2026 on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@1] Officers went
to a house on Rua Bicas after anonymous reports and residents’ information about
possible drug trafficking at the location. [@1] The house and a shed at the back
of the plot were said to be used to store, prepare and distribute drugs. [@1] At
the address officers saw a man near the gate making a movement consistent with
discarding an object, stopped him and found nothing illicit on his person. [@1]
In the yard officers found a container with individually wrapped substances
resembling crack. [@1] Another man found inside first said he did not live there
and later said he had come to buy a portion of maconha. [@1]

Searches of the house and shed found 121 crack rocks plus other portions of the
same substance, three maconha bars and one maconha tablet, one bar with skank
characteristics, a portion of cocaine and 121 ecstasy tablets. [@1] Officers
also seized three digital precision scales, 44 .380-calibre rounds, 16 with
percussion marks, and 45 .22-calibre rounds. [@1] Officers also collected five
mobile phones and R$314 in cash. [@1]

Both men were arrested in flagrante on suspicion of drug trafficking and
association for trafficking. [@1] The two were taken to Conselheiro Pena
Hospital for medical assessment and then to the on-duty Civil Police station in
Governador Valadares with the seized materials. [@1] The Military Police
registered the case and handed the materials to the Civil Police for legal
measures and continuation of the investigation. [@1]
