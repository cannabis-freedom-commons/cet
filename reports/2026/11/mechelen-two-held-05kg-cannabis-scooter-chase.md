Two men were noticed at about 17:20 on Saturday travelling together on a single
shared e-scooter in Mechelen city centre. [@1] Riding together on one shared
scooter is not permitted, so police tried to pull them over for a check. [@1]
The two men instead fled from the police, sparking a pursuit that began on the
shared scooter. [@1] The pursuit then continued on foot after the pair left the
scooter. [@1]

While fleeing, the men threw away a bag containing half a kilogram of cannabis.
[@1] Officers eventually stopped and arrested the pair. [@1] The arrested men
were taken to the police station for further investigation. [@1] Police seized
the discarded drugs. [@1]

The arrests were carried out by Police Zone Rivierenland last weekend. [@1]
