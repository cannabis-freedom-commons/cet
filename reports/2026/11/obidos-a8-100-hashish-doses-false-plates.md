GNR detained an 18-year-old man on 21 November 2026 at a service area of the A8
motorway in the municipality of Óbidos, on suspicion of forging licence plates.
[@2] The arrest followed a road patrol during which soldiers of the Caldas da
Rainha territorial post noticed suspicious behaviour by the driver of a car
refuelling at the A8 service area. [@2] During the stop the young man said at
once that he had no driving licence and did not know where the car's documents
were. [@2]

A strong smell of narcotic product led the soldiers to search the driver and two
passengers and to search the car. [@1] The search seized 100 doses of hashish,
110 euros in cash and a vehicle key. [@3] Investigators found that the car was
subject to a seizure order issued by Spanish police and was circulating with
stolen false plates. [@1] The seized key belonged to a BYD vehicle subject to a
seizure order for abuse of confidence. [@2] Inside the BYD a second key led to a
BMW that also had a seizure order and stolen plates. [@1] The BMW had already
been recovered in Caldas da Rainha on 20 November. [@1] In all, officers seized
three vehicles, four licence plates and a mobile phone, besides the drugs and
money found in the initial search. [@1]

The young man was made an arguido, a formal suspect, and the facts were
communicated to the Caldas da Rainha judicial court. [@1] The operation was run
by the Caldas da Rainha territorial post with support from its criminal
investigation unit (NIC) and the Óbidos territorial post. [@2]
