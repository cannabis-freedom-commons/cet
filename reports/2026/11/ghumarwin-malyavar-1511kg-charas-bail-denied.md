The court of Special Judge-II Navish Bhardwaj rejected the accused's regular
bail petition. [@1] The court said cases involving commercial quantities of
drugs must satisfy the twin conditions under Section 37 of the NDPS Act. [@1]
The court said bail cannot be granted merely because the investigation is
complete, the challan has been filed and the accused has remained in custody for
a long time. [@1]

The case dates to 20 October 2024. [@1] Police were checking vehicles at a
checkpoint near the NH-205 four-lane at Malyavar that day. [@1] At about 8:10 in
the morning officers stopped a taxi coming from the Mandi side for checking.
[@1] The occupant of the front seat was seen trying to hide the bag on his lap
near his feet, and those in the taxi appeared nervous. [@1] The vehicle was then
searched in the presence of an independent witness. [@1] Inside the bag police
found a blue carry bag under khaki-coloured cloth, holding small black round
pieces in transparent polythene. [@1] The substance was found to be charas
weighing 1.511 kg on an electronic scale. [@1] Police seized the charas and
registered a case. [@1]

The defence said the accused is innocent and was falsely implicated. [@1] The
defence said the charas was not recovered from his personal search. [@1] The
defence said the independent witness did not support the prosecution and denied
the recovery happened in his presence. [@1] The defence said the investigation
is complete and the challan has been filed, the accused has no criminal history
and is the family's sole earner. [@1] The prosecution opposed bail, saying the
charas is a commercial quantity attracting special restrictions under Section
37. [@1] Citing Supreme Court rulings, the court said bail requires prima facie
satisfaction that the accused is not guilty and is unlikely to reoffend on bail.
[@1] The court said a hostile independent witness alone does not disprove the
case and the witness's statement will be assessed during trial. [@1]
