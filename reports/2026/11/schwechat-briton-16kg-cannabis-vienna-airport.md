Police detained a 23-year-old British man at Vienna Airport in Schwechat after
cannabis was found in his suitcase during weekend checks of transit baggage.
[@1,2,3,4,6,7,8,9] Criminal investigators from the Schwechat city police command checked transit baggage from a Bangkok flight at Vienna Airport on 14 November 2026. [@4] He was taken to Korneuburg prison after denying an allegation of drug
trafficking. [@1,2,3,5,6,7,8]

The seizure totalled 16 kilograms of cannabis. [@1,2,5,6,7,8,9] The cannabis was packed in
16 airtight packages. [@1,2,3,5,6,7,8,9]

The man had been travelling from Bangkok in Thailand to London. [@1,2,3,5,6,7,8,9] Police
announced the case on Monday in a dispatch. [@1,2,3]
