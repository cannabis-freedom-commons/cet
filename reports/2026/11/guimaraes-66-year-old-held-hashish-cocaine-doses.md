In the city of Guimarães, the PSP detained a 66-year-old man on suspicion of
drug trafficking. [@1] The detention occurred during a policing operation, and
police said the suspect had cocaine equal to about 13 doses and hashish enough
for about 11 doses. [@1] Police also seized a sum of cash whose value was not
disclosed. [@1] The man was to appear before the Judicial Court of Vila Nova de
Famalicão. [@1]

The four detentions reported in Braga district on 12 November 2026 covered two
distinct occurrences linked to theft and drug trafficking. [@1] The other
occurrence involved three men aged 34, 42 and 50 detained on suspicion of theft
inside a building under construction. [@1] Those three theft suspects were
notified to appear before the Judicial Court of the Braga district. [@1]
