South Bruce OPP officers were dispatched to a traffic complaint in the
Municipality of Kincardine before 3:30 p.m. on November 17, 2026 and stopped a
vehicle, police said in a news release the next day. [@1] Police said the
officers saw signs of impairment in the driver and arrested her. [@1]

Angela Ball, 61, of Kincardine is charged under the Criminal Code with operation
while impaired by alcohol and drugs and with dangerous operation. [@1] She is
also charged under Ontario's Cannabis Control Act with having care or control of
a vehicle with cannabis readily available. [@1] None of the allegations has been
proven in court. [@1]

Police released her from custody and scheduled her to appear at the Ontario
Court of Justice in Walkerton on January 6, 2027. [@1] The vehicle was towed and
impounded for seven days and her driver’s licence was suspended for 90 days,
police said. [@1] Police did not say what the traffic complaint involved, where
officers stopped the vehicle or whether anyone was injured. [@1]
