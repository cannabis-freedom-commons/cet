Two men aged 18 and 21 were taken to the police station on suspicion of drug
trafficking on the night of Monday 23 November 2026 in Nova Brasília, Imbituba.
[@1] The Military Police handled the incident at about 21:20 during a traffic
inspection. [@1] The 18-year-old suspect was riding a motorcycle and said he
held no Carteira Nacional de Habilitação (CNH). [@1] During the stop officers
found marijuana remnants in a delivery box. [@1] When questioned he said he
delivered drugs and had just completed a sale. [@1] He also told police he kept
more drugs and a stolen motorcycle at home. [@1]

With permission to enter the dwelling, officers found a 21-year-old man who said
he had lived there for about a week and also sold drugs. [@1] The searches
seized 300 grams of hashish, two ecstasy tablets, 25 grams of marijuana and 43
electronic cigarettes containing THC. [@1] Officers also found two motorcycles
with theft records, two card machines, two mobile phones, a watch, a vehicle
number plate and two rounds of ammunition. [@1]

Officers also seized R$2,255 in cash. [@1] Both men underwent forensic
examinations and were taken to the Laguna police station for legal procedures.
