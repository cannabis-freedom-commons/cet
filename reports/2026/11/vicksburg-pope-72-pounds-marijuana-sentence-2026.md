Tayvion Pope was sentenced Thursday by Judge Toni Terrett after pleading guilty
to a lesser charge of possession with intent to distribute. [@1] Pope received a
20-year sentence, with eight years suspended and 12 years to serve. [@1]
Assistant District Attorney Branan Southerland represented the state in the
case. [@1] Pope took full responsibility for the marijuana found inside the
vehicle and did not place any blame on his co-defendant. [@1] The two men
arrested were identified as 22-year-old Tayvion Pope and 24-year-old Robert
Lamont Ward, both of Vicksburg, and were charged with trafficking of a
controlled substance. [@2] Judge Toni Terrett sentenced Tayvion Pope on Thursday
to 20 years, with eight years suspended and 12 years to serve. [@3]

A stop for illegal tint on Indiana Avenue led to the seizure of nearly 70 pounds
of marijuana, two weapons and two arrests, according to the Vicksburg Police
Department. [@2] At about 9:45 p.m. on Sunday, June 8, Lt. Donnie Heggins of the
Vicksburg Police Department Narcotics Division stopped a vehicle on Indiana
Avenue for illegal tint. [@2] A drug dog indicated the odor of illegal
narcotics, giving officers probable cause to search the vehicle. [@2] In total,
officers confiscated nearly 70 pounds of marijuana with a street value of
$100,000 and two firearms. [@2] Both the driver and the passenger were arrested.
[@2] Pope was arrested in June 2025 during a traffic stop in which he was
identified as the driver of a vehicle carrying approximately 72 pounds of
marijuana. [@1] The Vicksburg Police Department used a drug-sniffing dog to
assist with the search. [@1] Two handguns were also seized during the
investigation. [@1]

Pope was out on bond for felony possession of marijuana in a March 11 armed
robbery case that left 19-year-old Quincy Peoples dead and two more people
hospitalized. [@2] After the stop, Pope received a $300,000 bond and Ward
received a $200,000 bond for trafficking of a controlled substance at an initial
hearing before Judge Penny Lawson. [@2]
