Police in Rudrapur arrested two men who had come from the Mukteshwar area to
sell charas in the town. [@1] A police team with the Anti Narcotics Task Force
was patrolling on Monday night near Roadways when two youths turned back on
seeing the police and were chased and caught. [@1] Police identified the two as
Gopal Chand of village Dino, Paharpani and Bhuvan Chandra of village Malla Dino,
Paharpani in the Mukteshwar area of Nainital district. [@1]

Police seized 1.152 kg of charas from their possession. [@1] Police said the men
planned to contact truck drivers in the SIDCUL area and shopkeepers around
Roadways to sell the charas. [@1]

The men told police they are friends from a rural area who use charas themselves
and had grown bhang at home to prepare the charas. [@1] They also said they had
bought small quantities of charas from local villagers and had come to Rudrapur
together to sell it. [@1] Police registered an FIR under the NDPS Act against
both men and were producing them in court to send them to jail. [@1]
