Tanza police arrested a suspected illegal drug seller in Barangay Bagtas, Tanza,
Cavite, during a buy-bust early on Saturday 14 November 2026. [@1] The suspect,
identified only as alias CJ, was arrested following the operation. [@1] Police
described the seized material as suspected high-grade marijuana commonly known
as kush. [@1]

According to the Tanza Municipal Police Station, the operation was conducted at
approximately 4 a.m. by its Station Drug Enforcement Team in coordination with
the Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency. [@1] Police seized two heat-sealed
sachets holding about 1.03 grams of dried leaves suspected to be kush, with an
estimated standard drug price of P1,545. [@1]

Officers also recovered a P500 bill used as buy-bust money. [@1] The suspect was
being processed for inquest proceedings over alleged violations of Sections 5
and 11, Article II of Republic Act 9165. [@1]
