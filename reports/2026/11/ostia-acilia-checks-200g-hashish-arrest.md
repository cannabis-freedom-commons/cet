Carabinieri from the Rome Ostia company made five arrests and reported seven
people to prosecutors during a check service on the coast. [@1] The checks
concentrated on Ostia Ponente, Casal Palocco, Acilia and nearby areas. [@1] The
operation was an extraordinary service carried out in the evening. [@3] Two
homeless Roman men aged 52 and 48, both already known to police, were arrested
as they handed drug doses to five buyers, who were reported to the Prefecture as
users. [@1] A 23-year-old Bangladeshi man was arrested with about 200 grams of
hashish. [@1] Two of the five arrests executed restrictive measures issued by
the courts rather than new drug charges. [@2] A 49-year-old Sri Lankan was taken
to Rebibbia prison to serve a final sentence of 3 years, 3 months and 28 days.
[@1] A 41-year-old Roman already under house arrest was moved to Civitavecchia
prison after an order tightening his measure. [@1]

A search of the 23-year-old's home in Conca D'Oro found another 5 grams of
hashish and 2 grams of marijuana, plus precision scales and packaging material.
[@1] The cannabis seized in the operation came to about 205 grams of hashish and
2 grams of marijuana. [@1] Officers also seized seven doses of crack and cocaine
weighing about 3 grams, which are not counted as cannabis. [@1] Another 11
people were reported to the Prefecture as personal users, with small amounts of
hashish, marijuana, crack and cocaine seized. [@1]

Three people, two Romans aged 21 and 26 and a 56-year-old from Monterotondo,
were reported for possessing crack and cocaine for sale. [@1] Three men aged 55,
46 and 32 were reported for carrying offensive weapons after road checks, and a
26-year-old Roman for drunk driving after refusing a breath test. [@1] Officers
identified 137 people, checked 73 vehicles and issued 24 traffic fines worth
more than 11,000 euros. [@1]
