South Australia Police's Serious and Organised Crime Branch arrested four people
after an investigation into alleged drug trafficking across metropolitan
Adelaide. [@1,3] On 12 November 2026, officers arrested a 21-year-old Flinders
Park man after stopping him in Adelaide's western suburbs. [@2,3] A search of a
Flinders Park property allegedly found cocaine, cannabis, cash and an
unregistered firearm. [@1,3] The man was charged with trafficking in cannabis and
cocaine and with firearm-related offences. [@1]

A 52-year-old Flinders Park woman was also arrested and charged with drug
trafficking and firearm possession offences. [@1] Police restrained vehicles and
a boat as part of proceeds-of-crime investigations. [@1,3] Both were refused
police bail and appeared in the Adelaide Magistrates Court on 13 November 2026.
[@2]

On 13 November, investigators arrested a 58-year-old Norwood man and charged him
with multiple counts of trafficking a controlled drug. [@1,3] An 18-year-old
Brooklyn Park man was arrested the same day and charged over alleged firearm
trafficking offences. [@1,3] Both men were bailed to appear in the Adelaide
Magistrates Court on 6 January. [@1,3]
