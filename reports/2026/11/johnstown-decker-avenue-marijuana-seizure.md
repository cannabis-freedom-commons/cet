Authorities in Cambria County say two men are behind bars after a crash in
Johnstown led to the discovery of drugs. [@1] The county district attorney
identified the men as Gerardo Enrique Cubas and Walter Amaya and said they were
arrested on Tuesday after a Johnstown police investigation into a single-vehicle
crash near Decker Avenue and Stone Street. [@1] The district attorney's office
dates the crash to 24 November 2026, when Johnstown police responded to a
reported vehicle accident at that intersection. [@2]

Officers applied for and executed a search warrant on the vehicle, which was
driven by Cubas and carried Amaya as a passenger. [@2] The search turned up
psilocybin mushrooms, MDMA, marijuana, methamphetamine, scales and other
packaging material. [@2] Police put the combined suspected street value of all
the seized drugs at $250,000. [@1] A WTAJ account citing the criminal complaint
says officers smelled marijuana from inside the car and reported seeing a
marijuana cigarette. [@3] That account lists marijuana and a concentrated
cannabis extract among the suspected drugs, along with $394 in cash. [@3]

Both men were charged with multiple counts of possession with intent to deliver,
possession of a controlled substance and possession of drug paraphernalia. [@2]
A magisterial district judge arraigned them and set bond at 10% of $1 million.
[@2] Preliminary hearings are scheduled for 3 December 2026. [@2] The
investigation was conducted by the Johnstown police with assistance from the
state Attorney General's Bureau of Narcotics Investigations and the county
district attorney's office. [@2]
