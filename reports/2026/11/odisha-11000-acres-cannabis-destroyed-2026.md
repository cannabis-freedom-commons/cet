The STF is the Special Task Force of Odisha Police. [@1] The STF and the
Anti-Narcotics Task Force (ANTF) recorded a major milestone against illegal
cannabis under the Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyan and Nasha Mukt Odisha initiatives.
[@1] Through sustained, coordinated operations with district police, enforcement
teams destroyed illegal cannabis cultivation spanning approximately 11,000
acres, about 4,451.54 hectares, across the state. [@1] The 11,000-acre figure is
given as an approximate statewide total for the continuing eradication campaign.
[@1]

The ongoing eradication drive is heavily concentrated in six identified hotspot
districts. [@1] Those districts are Boudh, Kandhamala, Gajapati, Rayagada,
Koraput and Malkangiri. [@1] The stated purpose of the drive is to choke off
narcotics supply chains at the source and cripple the financial networks of drug
syndicates. [@1]
