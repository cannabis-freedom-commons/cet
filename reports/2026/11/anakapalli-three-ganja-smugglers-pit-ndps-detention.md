Anakapalli district police invoked the PIT NDPS Act against three habitual ganja
smugglers with repeated narcotics involvement. [@1] Superintendent of Police
Tuhin Sinha warned that police would keep constant surveillance over people
involved in ganja activities and that habitual offenders would face laws
including the PIT NDPS Act. [@1] The three accused were detained and lodged in
Visakhapatnam Central Prison. [@1]

R. Bodakonda alias Nani of Narsipatnam was allegedly involved in inter-State
ganja smuggling. [@1] He reportedly moved large ganja consignments from the
Agency areas of Alluri Sitharama Raju district to Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada and
Hyderabad. [@1] Bodakonda had six previous ganja cases registered against him at
different police stations. [@1] K. Sattibabu of Golugonda mandal had three ganja
cases against him. [@1] Paila Suresh of Makavarapalem mandal was involved in two
previous ganja cases at Makavarapalem police station. [@1]

Narsipatnam police said the three had allegedly continued the illegal trade
despite earlier arrests and imprisonment, threatening public order and the
future of youth. [@1] Police invoked the preventive detention provisions of the
PIT NDPS Act to stop them continuing their alleged activities. [@1] A team
including Narsipatnam Rural Circle Inspector L. Revatamma took the three into
custody in separate cases before shifting them to Visakhapatnam Central Prison.
