Three men were arrested during Operação Narcos, a joint Civil and Military
Police action, in Jequié on the morning of Tuesday 24 November 2026. [@1] Two
suspects aged 40 and 38 were found in the Amaralina and Joaquim Romão
neighbourhoods. [@1] The third man, aged 39, had his arrest warrant served at
the 9th regional interior police headquarters in Jequié, where he was already in
custody in another case. [@1] One of the suspects was also booked in flagrante.
[@1] The men were taken to the police unit where they completed the legal
procedures and remain in custody at the disposal of the courts. [@1] Work
continues to find a fourth suspect who remains at large. [@1]

Officers also executed three search-and-seizure warrants against the men, who
are described as responsible for a drug depot used by a criminal organisation
operating in Jequié and nearby cities. [@1] Officers seized 26 kg of cannabis
alongside a revolver, ammunition, a hydraulic press, a vehicle, a mobile phone,
a tablet and a notebook. [@1] The men will answer for drug trafficking, illegal
possession of restricted-use ammunition and criminal organisation. [@1]

Operação Narcos stems from an investigation that began after officers seized
more than 500 kg of drugs and 2,645 rounds, including rifle calibres, from a
property in São Luiz, Jequié, on 5 November. [@1] As that inquiry advanced,
investigators identified those responsible for the property used as the drug
depot. [@1] The action was carried out by the 11th drug-trafficking station of
Jequié, linked to Denarc, together with Rondesp-MRC and Cipe/Central teams of
the Bahia Military Police. [@1] The operation also forms part of the Bahia Civil
Police Narke VII and Malhas da Lei actions. [@1]
