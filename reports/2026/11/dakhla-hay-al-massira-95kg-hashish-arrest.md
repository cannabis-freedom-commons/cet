On the evening of Friday 13 November 2026, the anti-gang brigade of Dakhla's
regional judicial police arrested two men aged 33 and 39 on suspicion of
possessing and trafficking chira, acting on information from the General
Directorate for Territorial Surveillance. [@1,2] One of the two was described as
having a criminal record and being wanted nationally by Dakhla police for drug
trafficking. [@1] Both suspects were placed in police custody while the
investigation continues under the supervision of the competent public
prosecution. [@1]

Officers stopped the first suspect in the Hay Al Massira neighbourhood of Dakhla
while he was in a light car of doubtful origin with chira ready for sale. [@1] A
search of the car found 60 chira plates weighing about five kilograms, plus a
significant amount of Moroccan cash and three foreign currencies suspected to be
trafficking proceeds. [@1,2] His accomplice was stopped in the same neighbourhood
with about four and a half kilograms of chira and Moroccan cash also suspected
to be proceeds. [@1,2] Police said inquiries are continuing to establish each
suspect's alleged acts and to reach any possible extensions of the activity.
