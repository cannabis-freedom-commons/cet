Military Police in Jaru, Rondônia, arrested a man on the morning of Wednesday 18
November 2026 on Rua Goiás in Setor 03, on suspicion of involvement in drug
trafficking. [@1] Officers patrolling the area saw him riding a bicycle and
stopped him after he appeared nervous at the approach of the police car and
suddenly changed direction. [@1] A search of his backpack found about 60 grams
of a substance resembling marijuana, a drug grinder, packaging and tools
including pliers and keys. [@1]

Police said the tools raised suspicions that they were used in thefts of
electric wires and cables, a crime reported in the municipality, but found no
direct link between the tools and any specific theft. [@1] The man said he was a
drug user and confirmed he had bought the substance but did not say from whom.
[@1] Officers also seized a mobile phone, the bicycle, the backpack and the
other objects found. [@1]

He was arrested and taken with all the material to the Unidade Integrada de
Segurança Pública (UNISP), the city's integrated public security unit. [@1] The
case was registered for investigation and analysis by the police authority. [@1]
