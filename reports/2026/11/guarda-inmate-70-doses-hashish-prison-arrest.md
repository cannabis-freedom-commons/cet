A 57-year-old man was caught in the act by Portugal's Judiciary Police as he
returned from a temporary release and tried to bring drugs into the Guarda
prison. [@1] The man was an inmate at the prison, the PJ said in a statement
sent to Lusa. [@2] When officers approached him he promptly handed over about 50
doses of hashish pollen, or cannabis resin, hidden inside his body. [@1] After
being taken to a hospital unit he had about 20 more doses of the same drug
removed. [@1] Correio da Manhã reported that the resin was intended for sale
inside the prison. [@3]

The arrest was made by the PJ's Guarda Criminal Investigation Department with
the collaboration of the prison guard corps and the prison directorate. [@1] The
detainee was to be brought before a first judicial interrogation to decide on
coercive measures. [@1] The inquiry is being handled by the Guarda Department of
Criminal Investigation and Action (DIAP). [@1] Correio da Manhã reported that
the interrogation was expected on Thursday 26 November. [@3]
