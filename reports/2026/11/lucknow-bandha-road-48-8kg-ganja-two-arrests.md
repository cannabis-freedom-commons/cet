Police arrested two men on 19 November 2026 near the municipal corporation
office on Bandha Road. [@3,7] Police said they acted on a tip-off that two men who had brought ganja from Odisha were waiting in a red car near the municipal corporation office on Bandha Road. [@8,9,12] The arrests were made by the Crime Branch of Lucknow
Police. [@2,6,7,9,10,11,12] The operation was directed by the Aliganj ACP and the Aliganj station house officer, with the Lucknow crime branch in-charge and the Hazratganj station house officer also involved. [@5] Officers seized 48.80 kg of ganja from the men. [@1,6,7,8,9,10,11]

The cannabis was packed in 43 packets. [@1,6,7,8,9,10,11] The two men were aged 49 and 45,
police said. [@2,7,8,9,12] Police identified the two as Prakash Dubey, a Prayagraj resident, and Jay Anand Mishra, who lives in the Awadh Vihar Yojana in Lucknow. [@6,7,9,11] Police said the men brought ganja from Odisha for supply in
Lucknow and adjoining districts. [@2,6,7,8,9,10,11,12] Officers also found two mobile phones and
540 rupees in cash. [@3,7,8,9] Police seized the red car used to transport the
consignment. [@2,7,8,9,10] The car they were using carried a Jharkhand registration number. [@6,11] The car's registration number was JH01CC9687. [@8,9] Police recovered the ganja from a Brezza car and took the vehicle into custody. [@4]

An FIR was registered at Aliganj police station under Case No. 227/2026,
invoking Sections 8 and 20 of the NDPS Act. [@2,7,8,9,10,12] The arrests are part of
Operation Narco Hunt, launched by the Lucknow Police Commissionerate under the
Union Home Ministry's three-year Vision Document for 2026–2029. [@2,9,10] ADCP Crime
Kiran Yadav said the Crime Branch was investigating the source of the cannabis,
the transport route, intended buyers and other members of the network. [@2,9,10,12] The
campaign aims to trace narcotics from source through transport routes to local
distribution and dismantle the syndicates behind it. [@2]
