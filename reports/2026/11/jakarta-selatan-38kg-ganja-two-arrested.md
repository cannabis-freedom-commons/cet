South Jakarta Metro Police's narcotics unit, Satresnarkoba, arrested two
suspects identified only as AD and SAM. [@1] The two were detained in the
Ciputat area of South Tangerang on Monday 23 November 2026. [@2] The case began
with information from the public, which police followed up through
investigation. [@1]

Investigators were led to a rented house in Palmerah, West Jakarta, believed to
be used to store the cannabis. [@1] At that house officers found 37 large
packages of cannabis. [@1] Other evidence was found at AD's home in Sawah Besar,
Central Jakarta. [@1] After the arrests police searched two different locations
for further evidence. [@3] In total police seized 38 large and two small
packages of cannabis with a gross weight of 38,783 grams, about 38.7 kg, plus a
digital scale and two mobile phones. [@1]

Preliminary checks suggested the cannabis had been sent from West Sumatra for
distribution in and around Jakarta. [@1] The suspected sender, identified as S
or Y, was placed on the wanted list while investigators examined links between
those involved. [@1] Both suspects were undergoing legal process and police were
still tracing the network and other possible participants. [@1]
