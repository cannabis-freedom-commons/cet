Troops of the Army's Thirteenth Brigade carried out two operations under the
Ayacucho Plus campaign in Fusagasuga, Cundinamarca, and Flandes, Tolima. [@1] In
Fusagasuga, the operation was a search in the Monteverde neighbourhood, a sector
north of the urban perimeter, launched on military information. [@1] In Flandes,
soldiers of Airborne Infantry Battalion No. 28 Colombia found a clandestine
depot holding two pistols, two magazines, a changon shotgun and thirteen
cartridges of various calibres. [@1]

In Monteverde, troops of Infantry Battalion No. 39 Sumapaz found three
revolvers, sixteen .38 cartridges, 195 grams of marijuana and a scale. [@1]
Across the two operations the Army reported six firearms, 29 cartridges of
different calibres and about 195 grams of marijuana taken out of circulation.
[@2] The Army said the two results were achieved in recent hours under the
Ayacucho Plus campaign plan. [@2]

The seized material was placed at the disposal of the competent authorities for
analysis and investigation. [@2] Both procedures were carried out with no
reported harm to civilians or troops. [@1]
