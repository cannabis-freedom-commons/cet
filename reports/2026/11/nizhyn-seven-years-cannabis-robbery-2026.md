The Nizhyn city-district court in Chernihiv Oblast convicted a local man of
openly taking goods from a shop and of illegally making and storing drugs, and
sentenced him to seven years and one month in prison. [@1] The verdict was
issued on 13 November 2026 in case No 740/1612/26. [@2]

In summer 2025 the man picked wild cannabis plants in a tree belt near the Nifar
plant in Nizhyn, took them home, dried them and ground them up for his own use.
[@2] On 2 March 2026 police found a newspaper wrap holding 13.976 grams of
cannabis on him. [@2] On 6 March 2026, near the Oster river, officers seized a
coffee packet containing 16.877 grams of cannabis. [@2] He had been convicted of
a drug offence in July 2025 and given two years of probation, which made the new
storage episodes a repeat offence under part 2 of article 309 of the Criminal
Code. [@2]

The court imposed seven years for the robbery and one year for the drug offence,
then absorbed the shorter term into the longer one. [@2] Because he had not
served the earlier probation, part of that unserved term was added, and the
sentence runs from his detention on 10 March 2026. [@2] The robbery happened at
about 16:20 on 10 March 2026 in a shop on Vozdvizhenska Street in Nizhyn. [@2]
The goods he carried out, with the bag, were worth 590 hryvnias 78 kopiykas.
[@2] At trial he denied the robbery, saying he believed the payment had gone
through. [@2] In sentencing the court took into account his remorse over one of
the offences, his help in solving the crime and his repayment of the damage, and
found no aggravating circumstances. [@1] The verdict can be appealed to the
Chernihiv appellate court within 30 days of its pronouncement. [@1]
