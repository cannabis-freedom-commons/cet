Prohibition Enforcement Wing police were on surveillance at the Thiruverkadu
model village junction near Poonamallee, Tamil Nadu, the day before publication.
[@1] They stopped two men behaving suspiciously, identified as Suresh, 36, and
Nagaraj, 26, both residents of Thandaiyarpet. [@1]

A search found that the two men were carrying 11 kilograms of ganja, the Tamil
word for cannabis. [@1] Police seized the ganja, arrested both men and jailed
them. [@1]
