Military police officers of the 24th Companhia Interativa Comunitária arrested a
man identified as Thiago, known by the nickname “Galo Cego”, on suspicion of
drug trafficking at Praça Tenreiro Aranha in the Centro district of Manaus on
Wednesday 18 November 2026. [@1]

During the operation officers seized 85 marijuana cigarettes and 39 small wraps
of cocaine. [@1] Officers also found a precision scale, a mobile phone, US$16
and R$4 in cash, as well as other objects that were presented at the police
station. [@1] After the arrest Thiago was taken to the 1st Distrito Integrado de
Polícia, where the case was presented for the completion of the legal
procedures. [@1]

The stop took place at Praça Tenreiro Aranha, described as one of the busy
high-circulation areas in the Centro of the Amazonian capital. [@1] The case was
to remain under investigation to clarify the origin of the seized material and
to check whether other people were involved in its possible sale in the area.
[@1] The suspect remained at the disposal of the authorities. [@1]
