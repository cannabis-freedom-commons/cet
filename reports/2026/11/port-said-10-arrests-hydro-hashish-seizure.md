The Port Said Security Directorate carried out a large-scale security campaign
across the Al-Zohour and Al-Dawahi districts to enforce security and pursue
outlaws. [@1] The campaign was led by Assistant Interior Minister for Port Said
security Maj. Gen. Wael Roshdy, assisted by Criminal Investigation director Maj.
Gen. Diaa Zamel and overseen by Public Security inspector Maj. Gen. Tamer Selim.
[@1] The operation detained 10 outlaws, including 3 described as major drug
dealers, in what was presented as a strike against drug-trafficking hubs. [@1]

Officers seized 5 kilograms of hydro and 1.5 kilograms of hashish found in the
suspects' possession. [@1] The same seizure also included 300 narcotic pills and
quantities of powder, which are not counted in the cannabis total. [@1]

Legal procedures were being completed for the suspects, who were to be presented
to the investigative authorities. [@1]
