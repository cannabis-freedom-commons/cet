Customs officers at Martinique Aimé-Césaire airport intercepted four drug
couriers between 12 and 16 November 2026. [@1] Three of the passengers checked
were minors. [@1] The first cannabis seizure came from a flight arriving from
Paris on 12 November. [@1] Officers found 61 packets of cannabis resin weighing
6.250 kg in the luggage of a 20-year-old passenger and handed him to the Office
anti-stupéfiants (Ofast), France's anti-drug office. [@1] On 16 November,
officers stopped a passenger flagged as a suspected drug carrier, found 2.025 kg
of cannabis resin taped to his thighs and handed him to Ofast. [@1]

The two cannabis seizures together accounted for 8.275 kg of cannabis resin,
according to customs. [@1] The other two people intercepted were carrying
cocaine, which is not included in the cannabis figures. [@1] Three of those
apprehended were handed to Ofast, while the 17-year-old girl was entrusted to
the territorial police directorate in Fort-de-France on the prosecutor's
instruction. [@2]

The concealment methods varied, with drugs hidden in luggage, shoe soles,
plastic bottles or directly against the body. [@1] The Martinique regional
customs directorate said the four interceptions showed constant vigilance over
passenger flows at departure and arrival. [@3] Customs said its controls cover
passengers both arriving in Martinique and leaving the island. [@1]
