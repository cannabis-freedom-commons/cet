The Dise, the Civil Police's drug-investigations unit, investigated for about 30
days a scheme that stored and distributed drugs by delivery in the Fraternidade
neighbourhood of São José do Rio Preto. [@1] Police said the woman used a white
Fiat Palio to make deliveries, mainly at night, and that officers found about 50
grams of cocaine with her when they approached her. [@1]

Questioned, she said she kept no other drugs at home and gave a false address,
but further inquiries identified the real property, also in Fraternidade. [@1]
At the house officers seized 25 bricks and several large pieces of marijuana,
three further portions of marijuana, six bricks of crack and nine portions of
cocaine. [@1] They also took about 1,000 reais in cash, a large scale, a knife
with drug residue and a vacuum sealer. [@1] The Fiat Palio used for the
deliveries was seized. [@1]

The woman, identified only by the initials K.S.M.S., works as an administrative
assistant. [@1] She was arrested in the act, charged with drug trafficking and
remained at the disposal of the court. [@1]
