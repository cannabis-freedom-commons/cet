Military police of Rio de Janeiro's 33rd Battalion carried out operations in the
Belém, Bracuí and Campo Belo communities of Angra dos Reis in the early hours of
Sunday 22 November 2026. [@1] The operations ended with two arrests and four
minors taken into custody, with a pistol and drugs. [@1] All the occurrences
were registered at the 166th Police Station and those detained remained at the
disposal of the courts. [@1]

In Belém, two adolescents were apprehended with a replica pistol, a magazine,
221 cocaine pins and 203 crack rocks. [@1] On Rua Santa Catarina in Bracuí,
Serviço Reservado officers seized 41 strips and six tablets of marijuana along
with 69 cocaine pins, 14 crack rocks, a radio and R$30 in cash. [@1] Four people
were taken to the police station in Bracuí, two of them minors who were
apprehended. [@2] On Avenida Boungavile in Campo Belo, officers collected 17
small wraps, nine pieces of marchinha and three strips of marijuana, with 35
cocaine pins and 16 crack rocks. [@1] Those suspects fled into a wooded area
leaving the drugs behind, and nobody was arrested there. [@2] At an address in
the Promorar area, Belém UPP officers acting on a tip found five tablets, five
strips and 11 small wraps of marijuana with 95 cocaine pins, 65 crack rocks and
12 scales, and arrested one suspect. [@1]

The operation's overall tally was more than 300 cocaine pins, 35 marijuana
strips, more than 270 crack rocks and about R$17,000 in cash, in addition to a
weapon. [@1] The Disque Denúncia Angra tip line (0300 253-1177) was the source
of the reports that triggered at least three of the occurrences, according to
the anti-drug programme. [@2] A Cidade reported that the operations took place
in areas where trafficking is controlled by the Comando Vermelho criminal group.
[@3]
