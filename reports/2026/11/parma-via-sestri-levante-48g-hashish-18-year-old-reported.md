The operation, coordinated by the Parma Provincial Carabinieri Command, used
numerous patrols on checkpoints, patrols and plainclothes duties in Parma's
historic centre and province. [@1] Carabinieri of the Radiomobile section
reported an 18-year-old Italian for possession of drugs for dealing purposes.
[@1] In via Sestri Levante, officers stopped a group of young people after
noticing their nervousness and a strong smell of hashish coming from an
18-year-old. [@1]

The 18-year-old first handed over one gram of the substance, saying it was for
personal use, but officers continued the check after noticing a visible bulge in
his trouser pocket. [@1] A personal search then found a whole hashish slab
weighing about 48 grams hidden in his clothing, which officers seized
immediately. [@3] Carabinieri said the overnight extraordinary crime-prevention
service seized a total of about 50 grams of hashish. [@1]

Separately, a 27-year-old foreigner living in another province was found with a
small quantity of marijuana for personal use and was reported to the
administrative authority as a drug user. [@1] The marijuana was assessed as
strictly for the 27-year-old's personal use. [@2]
