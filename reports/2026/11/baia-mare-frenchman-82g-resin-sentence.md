The Maramureș Tribunal sentenced a 38-year-old Frenchman to a combined 4 years
and 5 months in prison on 16 November 2026 for possessing drugs for his own use
and bringing cannabis resin into Romania. [@1] The sentence was for possessing
drugs for personal use and bringing over 82 grams of cannabis resin into
Romania, combined with an earlier sentence for driving under the influence of
psychoactive substances. [@1] The incident happened on the evening of 13 August
2025 at about 20:09. [@1] Police were told a car had entered the pedestrian zone
in Piața Libertății and the driver had tried to flee but was stopped by people
nearby. [@1] When police arrived the man was being held by two security guards.
[@1] The vehicle's owner, also a French citizen, said his friend had consumed
banned substances before they drove to the city centre that morning. [@1] He was
taken to the psychiatric hospital in Sighetu Marmației and admitted, and the
judgment says he was under the influence of banned substances and not coherent.
[@1]

Officers found tablets, powders, a stamp-style dose and packages of brown solid
matter in a cooler box in the car. [@1] Laboratory analysis identified MDMA,
LSD, cocaine, ketamine and cannabis resin, and measured 82.42 grams of cannabis
resin, 4.38 grams of a cocaine-containing substance and 0.69 grams of a
ketamine-containing substance. [@1] The man admitted buying the cannabis resin
in France and bringing it into Romania for his own use. [@1] By a sentence of 16
November 2026 the tribunal gave 9 months for possessing risk and high-risk drugs
for personal use and 3 years and 6 months for bringing risk drugs into the
country. [@1] He had earlier received 2 years' suspended imprisonment for
drug-impaired driving, final on 16 April 2026. [@1] The tribunal revoked the
suspension, merged the three terms and added a mandatory 11-month increase to
the heaviest term of 3 years and 6 months to reach 4 years and 5 months in
detention. [@1] The period he spent detained, under preventive arrest and under
house arrest from 14 August 2025 to 8 January 2026 was deducted. [@1]

The judge found the drugs were for personal use only and that there was no
evidence of intent to sell or involvement in wider international trafficking.
[@1] The tribunal treated driving into a pedestrian area frequented by the
public, up to a terrace, as aggravating the conduct. [@1] The man admitted all
the facts and asked for the simplified procedure, benefiting from reduced
sentencing limits. [@1] The court ordered confiscation of the drugs left after
analysis, biological samples for DNA profiling and payment of 3,211.94 lei in
court costs. [@1] The first-instance sentence can be appealed within 10 days of
communication. [@1]
