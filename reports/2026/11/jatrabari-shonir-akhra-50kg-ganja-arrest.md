Detectives of the Dhaka Metropolitan Police's Detective Motijheel division
arrested a man with 50 kg of ganja in Jatrabari, Dhaka. [@1] The DMP statement
named the arrested man as Md Sakib Ahmed, 24. [@2]

The arrest took place at about 9:20 am on Sunday 22 November 2026 at the Shanir
Akhra bus stand under Jatrabari police station. [@2] Officers recovered the 50
kg of ganja, a mobile phone and a jeep used to carry the drugs. [@2] The VOD
Bangla account also reports the seizure of the jeep used to transport the drugs
and a mobile phone. [@1]

Police filed a case under the Narcotics Control Act at Jatrabari police station.
[@2] He was produced before a court. [@2] The DMP Media and Public Relations
division announced the arrest in a press release on Monday 23 November, the day
after the raid. [@1]
