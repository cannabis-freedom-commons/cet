A joint operation destroyed illegally cultivated cannabis crops spread over
approximately 30 acres in different areas of Chaman. [@1] The operation was
conducted in Turtangi, Hassan Zai, Bostan Karez and Nida Karez under the
leadership of the Assistant Commissioner City and the Excise and Taxation
Officer Chaman. [@1] Personnel from the district administration, Excise
Department, Chaman Police, 40 Frontier Force, Frontier Corps, Anti-Narcotics
Force and Agriculture Department took part. [@1]

During the operation, the Revenue Department took the land used for illegal
cannabis cultivation into government custody, while the Excise Department
registered a formal case. [@1] According to Excise officials, the operation was
completed peacefully and successfully. [@1]

The authorities said efforts against illegal drug cultivation would continue to
protect society from narcotics. [@1] Officials identified limited financial
resources as a challenge to making future operations more effective. [@1]
