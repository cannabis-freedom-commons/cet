In a major action against the narcotics trade, the Anand Special Operations
Group seized a consignment of ganja near Sarasa Patiya by Vasad in Anand
district. [@1] In connection with the seizure, police detained two accused,
including one woman, and began questioning them about the consignment and its
movement. [@1]

Officers recovered about 51 kilograms of ganja from vehicles at the spot, a
quantity that defines the scale of this roadside seizure. [@1] Police put the
estimated value of the seized ganja at 29.68 lakh rupees, or about 2,968,000
rupees, in their initial assessment. [@1] Along with the ganja, police seized
the vehicles used to carry it as well as cash and mobile phones found with the
consignment. [@1]

According to preliminary details, the consignment was being transported by road
from Surat toward Rajkot when it was intercepted near Vasad. [@1] Vasad police
station registered an offence under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic
Substances Act, the law cited for the narcotics case. [@1] Investigators then
opened further inquiries to trace the wider supply network behind the
Surat-to-Rajkot movement. [@1]
