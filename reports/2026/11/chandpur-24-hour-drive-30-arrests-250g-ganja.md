Chandpur district police, with the district Detective Branch (DB), jointly ran a
special operation across the district's police stations on Saturday 14 November
2026. [@1] Police arrested a total of 30 people, including six leaders and
activists of a banned organisation, nine drug dealers and fugitive accused in
various cases. [@1] The six banned-organisation arrestees were two from Chandpur
Sadar Model police station and one each from Faridganj, Matlab Dakshin, Haziganj
and Shahrasti. [@1] Various station police also arrested 15 more accused who
were wanted under warrant or on bail documents in regular and other cases. [@1]
As part of the operation police detained 21 teenage gang members from various
police station areas of the district. [@1] After verification the detained
teenagers were released to their guardians on surety. [@1]

Officers recovered a total of 250 grams of ganja (cannabis) and 132 pieces of
yaba from the accused, the yaba being a methamphetamine tablet not counted here
as cannabis. [@1] Station-wise, yaba tablets were recovered from one accused in
Chandpur Sadar Model (17 pieces), two in Matlab Uttar (76), one in Haziganj (5)
and one in Shahrasti (4), and the 250 grams of ganja was seized from one accused
in the Kachua police station area. [@1] In a separate operation the Chandpur
Detective Branch arrested three accused and recovered 30 pieces of yaba from
them. [@1]

Legal action was taken against the other arrested accused under existing law at
the concerned police stations. [@1] The district police said such operations in
the public interest to suppress crime would continue regularly. [@1]
