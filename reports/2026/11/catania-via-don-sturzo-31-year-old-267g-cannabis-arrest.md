State police arrested a 31-year-old man from Catania during an anti-drug
operation in the historic centre. [@1] Police dog Briska found a drug hideout
between the living room and the bedroom of a flat in a building on Via Don
Sturzo. [@1] The dog unit of the General Prevention and Public Rescue Office was
on patrol against drug dealing in the city’s neighbourhoods when Briska led
officers into the flat, which was then thoroughly searched. [@1]

In the man’s bedroom officers found 14 grams of hashish, 15 grams of marijuana,
4 grams of methamphetamine and 4 grams of hallucinogenic mushrooms. [@1] Briska
then signalled more drugs in a living-room cabinet, where officers recovered two
tubs holding a total of 238 grams of marijuana. [@1] All the drugs were seized
and the man was arrested for possession of drugs for dealing, with the
presumption of innocence applying until a final conviction. [@1]

After the formalities the man was placed under house arrest awaiting a
fast-track trial, as ordered by the duty prosecutor at the Catania court. [@1]
