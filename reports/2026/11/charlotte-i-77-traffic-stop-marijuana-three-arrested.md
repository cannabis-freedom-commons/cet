Charlotte-Mecklenburg police stopped a car travelling north on I-77 shortly
after 9 p.m. on 5 November 2026 because it displayed a fake registration tag.
[@1] During the stop officers saw a firearm in the car and found that one of the
people in it was wanted on an outstanding arrest warrant. [@1] The driver,
25-year-old Lajuan Leach Jr., was arrested on that warrant. [@2] A 20-year-old
passenger, Cameron Rouse, was charged with possession of a firearm by a
convicted felon, possession of a weapon of mass destruction, possession with
intent to sell or deliver marijuana and possession of a machine gun or machine
gun part. [@2] A second 20-year-old passenger, Jaqualon Davis, was charged with
possession with intent to sell or deliver marijuana. [@2]

The search produced three firearms, one modified with a switch device, a second
switch device that had not been fitted, about one pound (0.454 kg) of marijuana
and drug paraphernalia. [@1] The stop was made by CMPD's Westover officers. [@2]

A switch device is a part that illegally converts a semi-automatic pistol into a
fully automatic weapon. [@3] CMPD said the enforcement action recovered multiple
firearms, including an illegally modified weapon, and removed a wanted person
from the community. [@1]
