A former trainee pharmaceutical commercial assistant (PKA) was convicted at the
Amtsgericht Hamburg of intentionally trafficking medical cannabis without
permission. [@1] The court imposed a fine of 80 daily rates of 40 euros. [@1] A
court spokesman said the fine came to 3,200 euros. [@1] The judgment is not yet
final. [@1] The defendant did not comment himself. [@1]

Prosecutors accused him of stealing a bag of 19.86 grams of cannabis from a
storage room at the pharmacy in St. Pauli where he trained. [@1] They said he
sold the bag without a prescription and asked 160 euros for it. [@1] The
prosecution argued that he wanted to create a lasting source of income by
selling cannabis from the pharmacy. [@1]

The court could not establish how he obtained the medical cannabis, so no theft
conviction was added to the trafficking offence, a spokesman said. [@1] The
pharmacy's stock records did not reliably prove a shortfall either. [@1]
