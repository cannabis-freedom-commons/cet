Detectives from the Evansville Police Department and the Vanderburgh County
Sheriff's Office Street Crimes Unit served a search warrant at a home on East
Tennessee Street around 1 p.m. on Thursday 19 November 2026. [@1] Tyson Hooks
Jr. was the only person inside and was taken into custody, according to a police
report. [@1] A second warrant was served at a home in Newburgh, Indiana, where
Christian Thomas was detained as he left. [@1]

Detectives found bags of marijuana in two bedrooms, some of them in suitcases
and a storage bin, along with a handgun, a vacuum sealer, empty bags and a scale
with residue. [@1] In total they seized more than 30 pounds (about 13.6
kilograms) of marijuana from the East Tennessee Street home. [@2] At the
Newburgh home they found packaged marijuana, a money counter, a vacuum sealer
and vacuum-sealed bags. [@1]

Police said Hooks told detectives he lived at the home and acknowledged the
marijuana was there, and that he had an active warrant out of Wabash County,
Indiana. [@1] Investigators said messages on Hooks' phone appeared to show the
two men working together to sell drugs, while Thomas denied knowing about the
East Tennessee Street home. [@1] Both were booked into the Vanderburgh County
Jail on marijuana dealing charges. [@2]
