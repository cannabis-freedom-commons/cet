Carabinieri of the Roma Casal Bertone station arrested in flagrante a
31-year-old Roman man with previous convictions on suspicion of stalking and
possession of drugs for dealing. [@3,5] On the morning of 2 November at about
8:45, while on patrol, the Carabinieri heard shouting from the street and caught
the man railing at and threatening to kill a 58-year-old local woman. [@2,5] When
he saw the Carabinieri, the 31-year-old tried to flee on foot but was safely
stopped. [@2,5]

The woman had already filed a stalking complaint against the same man on 25
September. [@3,5] She also described earlier unreported episodes of harassment and
threats linked to a small debt her son owed the 31-year-old. [@4,5] A later
personal and home search led to the seizure of 17 grams of hashish, already
divided into small doses ready for dealing. [@1,5]

The suspect was taken to Regina Coeli prison, where his arrest was validated.
[@2,5] The case remains in the preliminary-investigation phase, with the suspect
presumed innocent until any final conviction. [@3,5]
