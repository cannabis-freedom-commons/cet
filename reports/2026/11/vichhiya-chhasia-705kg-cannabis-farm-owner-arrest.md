Rajkot Special Operation Group officers raided a field in Chhasia village,
Vinchiya taluka, Rajkot district, and seized about 705 kilograms of green
cannabis plants. [@1,2,3,4,6,7,8,9] Officers were patrolling the Jasdan and Vinchiya areas when they received a tip-off that the field was under cannabis cultivation. [@9] The cannabis plants had been left along the edge of the field to dry, police said. [@3,5,7,8,9] The cannabis plants had been grown among pigeon pea and maize in the same field, according to the police account. [@5] Rajkot district superintendent of police Vijaysinh Gurjar told reporters that Mithapara had sown the cannabis on five bigha of his own land. [@9] One person was arrested in connection with the
cultivation. [@1,2,3,4,6,7,8,9] The arrested man was the owner of the field, Savshibhai Bachubhai Mithapara, aged 55. [@3,5,7,8] The farmer told police he had cultivated cannabis for the first time. [@7,8] Police said Mithapara told them he took to the crop to make money quickly because he was in financial difficulty. [@9]

The seized cannabis was estimated to be worth 3.52 crore rupees. [@1,2,3,5,6,7,8,9] Police
acted against the accused under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances
(NDPS) Act. [@1,2,3,5,6,7,8,9] The search was carried out under the guidance of the Rajkot Range inspector general of police and the district police chief. [@3] The search team was led by police inspector F. A. Pargi and sub-inspector G. J. Zala. [@9]

Investigators were examining whether a wider network was involved in the illegal
cultivation. [@1,2,3,5] Police were questioning him about where he obtained the seed and to whom he intended to sell the cannabis. [@9]
