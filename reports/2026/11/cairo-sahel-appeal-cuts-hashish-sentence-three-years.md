The Cairo criminal appeals court accepted an unemployed man's appeal against a
10-year aggravated-prison sentence and 10,000-pound fine and reduced the prison
term to three years of aggravated imprisonment. [@1] The case concerned drug
trafficking and resisting the authorities in the Sahel police district in Cairo.
[@1]

Cairo security officers identified the suspect after receiving information that
he possessed drugs and trafficked in them and carrying out investigations. [@1]
Officers arrested the man, described as unemployed with a criminal record, and
found a black plastic bag holding 300 grams of hashish plus a firearm with
cartridges and ammunition. [@1]

When confronted, he admitted the offence and the authorities took the required
legal measures against him. [@1]
