Tarik Gochi, a 42-year-old Iraqi national, appeared before the judicial court in
Senlis on Monday 16 November 2026 on a charge of illicit use of narcotics, after
police arrested him in Creil. [@1] When officers arrested him they found three
pouches containing cannabis resin and 1,200 euros in cash on him. [@1]

He told investigators in custody that the money came from his work on building
sites and that he sometimes sold contraband cigarettes, an account he repeated
to the judges. [@1] Analysis of the banknotes found a heavy concentration of
cannabis and cocaine traces, and examination of his phone found messages that
left no doubt about the organisation of drug transactions, the court heard. [@1]
His criminal record carries 18 entries for aggravated theft, drugs and insults
to holders of public authority, committed under several aliases. [@1]

Prosecutor Loïc Abrial told the court the defendant was the linchpin of a
structured trafficking operation and asked for 12 months in prison with him kept
in detention and a permanent ban on residing in France. [@1] Defence lawyer Marc
Sadoudi said his client, who left Iraq aged 16 and has spent most of his life in
France, could not regularise his status alone and had several addictions,
smoking six to seven joints a day plus cocaine. [@1]

The court sentenced him to one year in prison and ordered him to leave France
when released. [@1]
