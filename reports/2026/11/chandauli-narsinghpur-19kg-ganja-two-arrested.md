A joint team of Sadar Kotwali police and the Ghazipur ANTF arrested a man and a
woman on the highway near Narsinghpur village after midnight on Sunday. [@1] The ANTF team included sub-inspector Shami Ashraf Sheikh and head constable Sanjay Singh Rajawat. [@3] Two accused were arrested from Chandauli while eight others were arrested from Kaimur. [@3] The Anti-Narcotics Task Force arrested 10 alleged smugglers from Chandauli and Kaimur in the same smuggling network. [@3] The
arrested man was identified as 19-year-old Neeraj Sahni. [@2,3] The arrested woman
was identified as 21-year-old Preeti Kumari. [@2,3] Both are residents of Amrud
Mandi in the Rajghat police station area of Gorakhpur district. [@2] The eight accused arrested from Kaimur were identified as Siddharth Shukla, Ravi Kumar Bharti, Vandan Nishad, Vandana Nishad, Kanhaiya Sahni, Pawan Sahni, Suraj Sahni and Vicky Kumar, all residents of Gorakhpur. [@3] Officers
had set up checks around Majhwar railway station on NH-19 toward Narsinghpur
Road. [@2] An informant pointed out the pair during checking, and officers
surrounded them as they tried to flee. [@2]

A search of their bags yielded 19.26 kg of ganja. [@1] The contraband had been concealed inside a vehicle seized by police. [@3] Police recovered 85.59 kg of illegal marijuana worth around Rs 50 lakh, along with 10 mobile phones, two four-wheelers and Rs 200 in cash. [@3] The consignment had been
brought from Odisha and was being taken via Varanasi toward the Gorakhpur
market. [@2] The accused bought marijuana cheaply from Odisha and Ranchi and sold it in small packets in Gorakhpur, nearby districts and Bihar border areas. [@3] Police registered crime number 541/2026 at Chandauli Kotwali and
booked the pair under sections 8/20/29 of the NDPS Act, with proceedings under
way to trace the wider network. [@2] The accused arrested in both cases were connected to the same smuggling network. [@3] A case was registered at Durgawati police station in Kaimur under sections 8(c), 20(B)iiC, 27(a), 25, 35 and 29 of the NDPS Act. [@3] Interrogation of the two Chandauli accused led police to arrest their eight alleged accomplices from Kaimur. [@3]
