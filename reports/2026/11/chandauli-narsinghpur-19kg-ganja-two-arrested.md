A joint team of Sadar Kotwali police and the Ghazipur ANTF arrested a man and a
woman on the highway near Narsinghpur village after midnight on Sunday. [@1] The
arrested man was identified as 19-year-old Neeraj Sahni. [@2] The arrested woman
was identified as 21-year-old Preeti Kumari. [@2] Both are residents of Amrud
Mandi in the Rajghat police station area of Gorakhpur district. [@2] Officers
had set up checks around Majhwar railway station on NH-19 toward Narsinghpur
Road. [@2] An informant pointed out the pair during checking, and officers
surrounded them as they tried to flee. [@2]

A search of their bags yielded 19.26 kg of ganja. [@1] The consignment had been
brought from Odisha and was being taken via Varanasi toward the Gorakhpur
market. [@2] Police registered crime number 541/2026 at Chandauli Kotwali and
booked the pair under sections 8/20/29 of the NDPS Act, with proceedings under
way to trace the wider network. [@2]
