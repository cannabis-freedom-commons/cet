Military police of the 34th Interior Military Police Battalion arrested a man in
flagrante for drug trafficking in Bragança Paulista on the afternoon of Friday
20 November 2026. [@1] While patrolling the Parque dos Estados neighbourhood on
Rua Antônio Ribeiro, officers saw the man leave a residence carrying a backpack
and hurry into a nearby bar when he noticed them. [@1] During the stop he
confessed that he was carrying marijuana in the backpack and said the drug
belonged to him. [@1]

A personal search found a mobile phone, and inside the backpack officers found a
brick of marijuana weighing 482.41 grams, a scale and various packets used to
divide the drug. [@1] He told officers he would receive R$100 to store the drug
and was taking it to another person near a market in the same neighbourhood.
[@1]

He was formally arrested in flagrante for drug trafficking and taken to the
police station, where he remained at the disposal of the courts. [@1]
