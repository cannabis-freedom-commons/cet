Jamie Costello, 42, of Drummond Crescent, South Shields, was pulled over by
police who saw him driving at high speed in October 2023 and found bags of
cannabis, valued at between £750 and £2,290, and £700 cash in the vehicle. [@1]
He was arrested and police searched his home, where the plants were being
cultivated with lighting, ventilation and feed. [@1] Prosecutor Stuart Graham
said officers found a large tent in a bedroom containing three quite tall,
five-foot-five cannabis plants. [@1] The court heard that messages on his mobile
phone indicated supply to others. [@1] The prosecution said messages on his
phone included requests to grab a quarter or grab half a q. [@2] Five separate
bags of the class B drug were found in his vehicle. [@2]

The offences occurred in October 2023. [@2] His lawyer Brian Hegarty said
Costello had been a long-standing cannabis user but that was now in the past and
he was in a responsible job. [@1] Mr Hegarty said it was very much a small-scale
operation with only about a dozen text messages over five months. [@1]

Costello, who had been in no trouble since, was sentenced to nine months,
suspended for 12 months. [@1] Recorder Christine Egerton said Costello was a
hard worker who had put drug-taking behind him. [@1] The judge said there was a
reasonable prospect of rehabilitation when imposing the suspended sentence. [@3]
