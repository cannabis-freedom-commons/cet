Two men were arrested on the night of Thursday 19 November 2026 in Bairro
Coqueiral, in the north zone of Aracaju, on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@1]
Officers saw a car stopped in the middle of the road, with one man outside
talking to the driver. [@1] When the officers approached, one of the men tried
to run but was caught. [@2]

During the approach officers found two wraps of cocaine and another with
flower-type marijuana. [@1] Searching the car, they found three more tablets of
marijuana. [@1] One of the men said the other had hired him for 1,000 reais to
carry the drugs from Bairro Marcos Freire to Porto Dantas. [@1]

In total police seized about 3.07 kilograms of marijuana and 27 grams of
cocaine. [@2] The car used to carry the drugs was also seized. [@1]
