The Chernogorsk City Court sentenced a 54-year-old man to 3 years 6 months in a
general-regime correctional colony. [@1] The judgment has taken legal effect.
[@1] He was previously convicted of a similar offence. [@1] He told police he
had gathered the cannabis on a wasteland near Kholmistaya Street and stored it
for personal use. [@1] He was convicted of unlawful acquisition and possession
of narcotics under part 2 of Article 228 of the Criminal Code. [@2]

In July 2026 police received information that a Chernogorsk resident might be
storing drugs, and the check led them to a dormitory on Oktyabrskaya Street.
[@2] Officers brought in a cynologist with a service dog, which indicated one
room and then the exact place where a packet of plant material was hidden. [@1]
Forensic examination confirmed the packet contained 183.7 grams of marijuana and
0.98 grams of hashish oil. [@1]
