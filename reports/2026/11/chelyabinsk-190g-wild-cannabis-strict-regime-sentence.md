The Sovetsky District Court in Chelyabinsk sentenced a previously convicted
local man to three years and six months in a strict-regime penal colony for
keeping 190 grams of cannabis. [@1] The court found that on 18 September 2026
the man picked wild cannabis, put the plant parts in a bag and carried them for
his own use, and that police detained him the same day. [@2] An examination
showed the seized plants weighed 190 grams in dried form, a large amount under
Russian law. [@1]

He was convicted under part 2 of article 228 of the Criminal Code, which covers
illegal acquisition and storage without intent to sell of parts of plants
containing narcotic drugs on a large scale. [@1] The man had an earlier
conviction under part 2 of the same article. [@2] The court counted his guilty
plea, remorse, active help in the investigation and poor health as mitigating
circumstances, and dangerous recidivism as an aggravating one. [@1] The maximum
penalty under the charge is 10 years in prison with a fine of up to 500,000
roubles. [@2]

The sentence had not entered into legal force and could be appealed to the
criminal panel of the Chelyabinsk Oblast Court. [@2]
