Egypt's Public Prosecution referred a worker to the Criminal Court on charges of
possessing hashish for personal use and endangering his young daughter's life
through negligence. [@1] Investigators found that the father had left a piece of
hashish in his bedroom without securing it or keeping it out of his daughter's
reach. [@1] The little girl reached the piece without understanding what it was
and ate part of it believing it was candy or a piece of chocolate. [@1] After
eating the drug, she developed severe illness that required emergency transfer
to hospital for medical care amid serious risk to her health. [@1]

Police inquiries said she was taken to hospital after symptoms of illness
appeared and that doctors carried out the necessary medical examinations. [@1]
Laboratory analysis of her sample tested positive for hashish. [@1]
Investigations confirmed the father possessed the drug for personal use and that
leaving it unsecured in the room caused the danger to his daughter. [@1] After
completing the investigations, First Attorney General Ahmed Qayed of the South
Giza prosecution ordered the worker's referral to the Criminal Court on the same
two charges. [@1]
