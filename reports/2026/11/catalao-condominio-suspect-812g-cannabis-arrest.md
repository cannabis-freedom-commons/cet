Military police CPE Alpha officers arrested a suspected drug trafficker on the
night of Wednesday 18 November 2026 in a residential condominium in Catalão,
Goiás. [@1] The team said it acted on information received through the
Disque-Denúncia hotline, reports from residents and data shared by the Regional
Intelligence Agency. [@1] That information indicated the suspect was using his
own apartment to sell illegal drugs. [@1]

After checks at the location, officers found the man and seized 590 grams of
skank, 100 grams of hashish and 122 grams of marijuana, a total of 812 grams of
cannabis. [@1] Officers also seized three precision scales and plastic bags for
packaging the drugs. [@1] The same action also seized 30 grams of cocaine
hydrochloride and 100 grams of crack, which are not counted in the cannabis
total. [@1]

The suspect and all the seized material were taken to the competent police
authority for legal procedures. [@1]
