Two alleged ganja smugglers who were waiting for a bus in Kondagaon were
arrested. [@1,2,4] The arrests were made by Keshkal police, working under the supervision of Additional Superintendent of Police Kapil Chandra and Keshkal Sub-Divisional Officer Arun Kumar Netam on the directions of Superintendent of Police Pankaj Chandra. [@3] The accused were identified as Siddharth Singh (25) of Bhimnagar, Mainpuri district, and Ashok (46) of Abdul Navipur, Mathura district in Uttar Pradesh. [@2,3,5] Police said an informant tip on 13 November reported two suspects with blue and black backpacks waiting for a bus near Batarali Chowk on NH-30. [@2,3,5] Sixteen kilograms of ganja, along with SIM cards and ATM cards,
were recovered from the accused. [@1,2,4] Siddharth was carrying seven Fino Payments Bank and two NSDL Payments Bank ATM cards. [@2] Alongside the ganja, police also found mobile phones, SIM cards of different companies, several ATM cards, earphones and cash. [@2] Police also seized mobile phones, SIM cards, ATM cards, earbuds and cash from the accused. [@3] Two packets were found in Siddharth's blue bag and sack and one packet in Ashok's black bag, together weighing 16 kilograms. [@2,3,5]

The seized ganja was valued at 8 lakh rupees (Rs 800,000). [@1,2,3,4,5] They were produced in court and sent to judicial custody. [@2,3,5] Both were booked under Section 20(b) of the NDPS Act. [@2,3,5]
