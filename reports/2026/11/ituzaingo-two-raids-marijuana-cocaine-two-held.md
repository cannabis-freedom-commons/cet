Police carried out two raids on Friday and Saturday in Ituzaingó, ending with
drugs, weapons and two people detained. [@2] The first raid was carried out on
Friday by officers of Comisaría Tercera of Ituzaingó, seizing cocaine, marijuana
and firearm replicas, among other items. [@1] The first order was executed at a
house on Santiago del Estero and Mitre streets, where officers did not find the
items named in the order but seized other substances. [@1] Officers in the first
raid seized marijuana and cocaine, along with mobile phones, bladed weapons, two
replica firearms, two precision scales and cutting and distribution items. [@1]
Officers apprehended an adult man at the first house as allegedly linked, and
placed him and the seized items at the disposal of the courts. [@1]

The second raid was carried out on Saturday by officers of Comisaría Primera in
the Asentamiento 420 neighbourhood, described as a drug kiosk. [@1] Officers in
the second raid found cocaine and marijuana, along with cash, mobile phones,
firearms, scales and cutting items. [@2] The Asentamiento 420 kiosk held cocaine
and marijuana together with cash, phones, firearms, scales and cutting items.
[@1] One person was detained in the second raid and placed with the seized items
at the disposal of the judicial authority. [@1]

Both cases were investigated as alleged breaches of National Law 23.737 on
narcotics, with the intervention of the prosecution unit. [@1] During the
Asentamiento 420 procedure an adult man was detained at the scene and taken to
the police station. [@3]
