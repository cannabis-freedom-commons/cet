The incident occurred on 16 November 2026 at around 15:15 at Esenboğa Airport.
[@1] German nationals Justin Weikum and Zoe Ingrid Ackerschott arrived from
Frankfurt on flight XQ0793 and customs officers checked their luggage at
Esenboğa Airport. [@1]

A detailed search of the two passengers’ suitcases found a total of 28 kilos 800
grams of cannabis hidden in vacuum-sealed bags. [@1] Police put the market value
of the seized 28 kilos 800 grams of cannabis at 25 million 920 thousand lira.
[@1]

The two couriers were taken to the Esenboğa Customs Anti-Smuggling and
Intelligence office for processing. [@1] In a preliminary interview the two
suspects said the people who would take delivery of the cannabis were waiting
for them at Esenboğa Airport. [@1] On the instruction of the Çubuk Chief Public
Prosecutor’s Office, officers of the Ankara Police Anti-Narcotics Branch Border
Office were informed. [@1]

Officers detained Banu Ergün, Oğuzhan Sert and Faruk Baran, who were in the car
kept under surveillance. [@1] The three were assessed to have waited at the
airport to take delivery of the cannabis brought in suitcases from Germany. [@1]
All five suspects were sent to court with a request for remand and were remanded
to prison. [@1]

The waiting car was a Mercedes with German plate MK OG 1312 that had waited a
long time at the airport. [@2]
