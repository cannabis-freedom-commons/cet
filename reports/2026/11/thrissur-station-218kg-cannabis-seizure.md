Railway Protection Force officers inspecting two trains at Thrissur railway
station in Kerala seized 2.180 kilograms of cannabis and 62 grams of heroin
found in the trains. [@1] Officers said the seized narcotic drugs would fetch
13.50 lakh rupees in the market. [@1] During an inspection of the
Dhanbad–Alappuzha Express at Thrissur railway station, officers found 2.180 kg
of cannabis kept in an unclaimed state in the general coach. [@1] During a
subsequent inspection of the Dibrugarh–Kanyakumari Express at Thrissur station,
officers found 62 grams of heroin hidden in the general coach inside five soap
boxes. [@1]

The inspections were carried out by a team comprising assistant sub-inspector
Vijayakumar, Rajeev and Manoj. [@1] The team acted under Senior Divisional
Security Commissioner Muhammad Haneef and Crime Intelligence Branch inspector
Jipin A.J. of the Railway Protection Force Thiruvananthapuram team. [@1] Both
seizures were made during the same round of train inspections at Thrissur
railway station. [@1]
