As part of Operativo Sur, officers of the State Security Secretariat of the
State of Mexico (SSEM) detained a man in Joquicingo on 17 November 2026 on
suspicion of offences against health. [@1] The man was identified as Jesús “N”,
28 years old, and was stopped while the officers were carrying out surveillance
and crime-prevention work in the Segundo Barrio locality. [@1]

During the intervention the officers found among his belongings wrappings of
green and dried herb with characteristics similar to marijuana. [@1]

The detainee said he probably belonged to a criminal group originating in
Michoacán and carried out surveillance activities for that organisation. [@1] He
was transferred and presented before the Public Prosecutor's Office, where his
legal situation will be determined and the relevant investigation carried out.
[@1] SSEM said it maintained operational deployments in the south of the state
to combat crime and contribute to the tranquillity of families in the State of
Mexico. [@1]
