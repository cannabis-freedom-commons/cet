Tirur police arrested a 26-year-old man accused of bringing cannabis from Odisha
to Kerala by train to sell in the Tirur area. [@1,2] Police identified him as
Thekkarath Abdul Aseeb, 26, of Kuzhippuram junction in Kottakkal. [@1,2] The
arrest was made by a team led by Tirur police station inspector Jaleel
Karuthedath. [@1]

Police said the man bought the cannabis in Odisha, packed it in a bag and
travelled by train to Tirur. [@1,2] Officers caught him in the parking ground
outside Tirur railway station as he tried to leave through the path towards the
rear bus stand rather than the station's front gate. [@1,2]

Police said he brought the cannabis to sell in small packets to students and
young people. [@1,2] Police said they were checking whether anyone else was involved in bringing the cannabis from Odisha and whether the man had a local sales network. [@2] Police said a check of his phone turned up the details of
students and young people who were customers. [@1] The investigation team
included sub inspectors and civil police officers from Tirur police station.
[@1]

He was produced before the Tirur court, which remanded him to the sub jail in
Perinthalmanna. [@1] Tirur police said they would keep taking strong action
against drug gangs. [@1]
