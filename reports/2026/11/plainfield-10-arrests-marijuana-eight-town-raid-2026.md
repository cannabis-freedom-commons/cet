Ten people were arrested in a drug operation spanning eight New Jersey towns,
authorities announced on 20 November 2026. [@1,7] Union County Prosecutor William
A. Daniel said the arrests followed a long-term investigation into a drug
distribution network operating mainly in Plainfield. [@1,3,5,7] On Wednesday 18
November, officers executed 11 search warrants and multiple arrest warrants in
Plainfield, Roselle, Rahway, Berkeley Heights, New Brunswick, Somerset, Hillside
and Dunellen. [@1,3,4,5,6,7] One search warrant was for a home on Riverbend Road in Berkeley Heights. [@7] The investigation was led by the Union County Prosecutor's
Office Intelligence and Narcotics Division, the FBI Newark Field Office, the DEA
New York Enforcement Division and the Plainfield Police Department Narcotics
Unit. [@1,3,4,5,6,7] The DEA unit named in the investigation was its New York Enforcement Division D-43. [@4] More than 20 law enforcement agencies and specialized units assisted,
including SWAT teams, K-9 units and federal task forces. [@1] Among the many law enforcement bodies involved were police departments in Plainfield, Roselle, Rahway, Elizabeth, Berkeley Heights and Springfield, sheriff's and prosecutors' offices, the New Jersey State Police and Homeland Security investigations. [@3] Agencies assisting included the Somerset County Prosecutor's Office, the Somerset County Sheriff's Office K-9 Unit, the Somerset County Tactical Narcotics Team and Somerset County SWAT. [@5]

Authorities seized more than 10 pounds of suspected marijuana alongside more
than 1.5 kilograms of suspected powder cocaine, more than 1,000 folds of
suspected heroin, more than $40,000 in cash and three handguns. [@2,3,4,5,6,7] The raids
also turned up equipment used to package drugs and operate a drug mill. [@1,4,5]

Luis Camino, 44, of Plainfield, faced first-degree charges of leading a
narcotics trafficking network, maintaining a drug production facility and
possession with intent to distribute, plus weapons, conspiracy, drug possession
and child endangerment counts. [@1,3,4,5,6,7] Julian Molina, 32, of New Brunswick, was
charged with second-degree conspiracy, possession with intent to distribute
drugs and marijuana distribution. [@1,3,4,5,7] Molina also faced a fourth-degree charge of possessing more than 6 ounces of marijuana. [@3] Molina was also charged with a second-degree count of possessing more than 5 pounds of marijuana with intent to distribute. [@5] Jesus Vega, 44, of Somerset, faced
first-degree possession with intent to distribute, second-degree conspiracy and
additional drug and marijuana offences. [@1,5,7] Vega was charged with third-degree possession of more than an ounce of marijuana with intent to distribute and fourth-degree possession of more than six ounces of marijuana. [@5] Jose Dilone, 51, of Hillside, and Maribel Zamudio, 39, of Dunellen, each faced a second-degree charge of conspiracy to distribute a controlled dangerous substance. [@3,4,5,7] Aneesha Rosado, 40, Jose Tirado, 41, and William Camino, 48, all of Plainfield, each faced a second-degree charge of conspiracy to distribute a controlled dangerous substance. [@3,5,6,7] Maritza Brackman, 65, of Berkeley Heights, and Michael Retamar-Torres, 51, of Rahway, each faced third-degree charges of conspiracy to possess and possession of controlled dangerous substances. [@3,5,7] The charges against the group
ranged from conspiracy and possession offences to first-degree narcotics
distribution counts. [@2] Daniel said the operation showed what agencies could
achieve by pooling their resources and intelligence. [@1,4,5] Plainfield Police Director James Abney commended the Plainfield Police Narcotics Unit and thanked the other agencies that took part in the operation. [@6]
