In Russia's Krasnodar Krai, law enforcement destroyed more than four kilograms
of cannabis plant parts during the interdepartmental operation Mak-2026,
regional police said. [@1] The campaign took place in the region from late June
to early September 2026. [@1] It was run in three stages by police officers,
Cossacks and regional officials. [@1] Agencies involved included the regional
Interior Ministry directorate, the FSB, the border service, the National Guard,
customs, the military commandant's office and Kuban's Cossack society. [@1]

Police said the four-kilogram cannabis figure was separate from a combined total
of more than 24 kilograms of narcotics and raw material, which also included
opium poppy straw, marijuana, hashish and cannabis oil and cannot be counted as
cannabis alone. [@1] Officers liquidated 19 illegal plantations, among them
plots of opium poppy and cannabis and a planting of drug-containing mushrooms.
[@1] They filed 41 administrative reports for illegal cultivation. [@1]

Police said 121 foci of wild drug-containing plants covering more than 85,000
square metres were found, but the area was not given for cannabis alone. [@1]
Investigators registered 238 offences linked to plant-based drug trafficking and
opened 173 criminal cases for the sale and storage of banned substances. [@1]
The operation identified more than 420 people over administrative violations and
227 citizens connected to drug crimes, and detained 62 people who were wanted.
[@1]

Specialists found 188 websites and network addresses with illegal content and
blocked 168 of them. [@1] The regional police said Mak-2026 had ended but that
work to find drug crops, stop illegal trafficking and block related websites
would continue. [@1]
