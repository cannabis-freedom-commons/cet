Tiruppur North police on patrol in the Tiruppur railway station area questioned
a young man from a northern state who came out of the station in a suspicious
manner. [@1] The man was identified as Kundankumar, 22, from Bihar, and police
found that he had brought ganja by train. [@1]

Police arrested him and seized a 900-gram ganja packet from him. [@1] In Arivoli
Nagar, police arrested Rahul, 25, from Jharkhand, and seized 50 grams of ganja
from him. [@1] In total across the two checks, police seized 950 grams of ganja
and arrested two people. [@1]
