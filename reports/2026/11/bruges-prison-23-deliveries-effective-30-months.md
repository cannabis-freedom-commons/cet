A woman from Blankenberge delivered drugs and alcohol inside the walls of Bruges
prison on at least 23 occasions. [@1] The woman was identified as 47-year-old
I.A. [@1] She delivered cannabis, cocaine and medication to her boyfriend held
in the prison on at least 23 occasions. [@1] To finance her addiction she began
selling cocaine for a dealer in October 2020 and began selling on her own
account in the summer of 2021. [@1]

The Bruges criminal court sentenced her on 3 June 2024 to thirty months in
prison, wholly suspended. [@1] She failed to appear before her justice
assistant, after which the prosecutor asked for the suspension to be revoked.
[@1] The prosecutor said she had also tested positive for cocaine in April 2025.
[@1]

The thirty-month sentence is now being made effective because she used cocaine
again. [@1] The defence unsuccessfully asked for one last chance, saying she was
now completely clean. [@1] Her lawyer said the positive urine test followed a
trip with two friends to Ibiza, where someone may have put something in her
drink. [@1]
