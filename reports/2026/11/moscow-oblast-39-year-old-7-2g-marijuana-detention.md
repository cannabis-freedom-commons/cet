Russian National Guard officers in Moscow Oblast detained a man on suspicion of
illegal drug trafficking. [@1] The regional National Guard department reported
the detention to the Moskva city news agency. [@1] A detention-group crew on a
patrol route noticed people drinking alcohol in a public place on the street.
[@1] When officers approached, one man grew visibly nervous, answered questions
incoherently and admitted that he was carrying a narcotic substance. [@1]

Officers summoned an investigative-operational group to the scene. [@1] During a
personal search, officers seized eight foil-wrapped packages from the
39-year-old man. [@1] An expert examination found that the packages contained
marijuana with a total weight of 7.2 grams. [@1]

The detainee was handed over to police officers, and a criminal case was opened.
