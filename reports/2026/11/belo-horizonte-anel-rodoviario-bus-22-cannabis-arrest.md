A man was arrested in flagrante on Sunday 15 November 2026 on suspicion of
transporting drugs on an interstate bus on the Anel Rodoviário de Belo
Horizonte, near Shopping Del Rey in the north-west of the capital. [@1,2] The passenger was 27 years old. [@2]

Military Police of Minas Gerais found 20 bars of marijuana, 15 of cocaine and
two portions of skunk in the passenger's luggage. [@1] The drugs were marked with a poster for the series Escobar: o patrão do mal, in which Pablo Escobar was played by actor Andrés Parra. [@2] Police found 13 bars of cocaine in the backpack the passenger carried by his feet. [@2] Police found 20 bars of marijuana, two packets of skunk and two bars of cocaine in the suitcase in the luggage compartment. [@2] The suspect's mobile
phone was also seized. [@1]

The man told police he would take the drugs to the municipality of Tucano, in
Bahia, and would receive R$10,000 for the transport. [@1,2] He said he had collected the luggage in São Paulo and refused to say who would receive the drugs. [@2] The seizure came
during Operation Última Parada, when officers stopped a bus travelling between
Santos, in São Paulo state, and Itabaiana, in Bahia. [@1,2] During the inspection
officers found the suitcase containing the cocaine, and the suspect carried a
baggage claim for a second suitcase holding the other drugs. [@1]
