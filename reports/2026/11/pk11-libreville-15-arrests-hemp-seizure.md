Gabon's mutualised intelligence services intercepted 15 suspected drug
traffickers at the closed telecommunications-barrier site at PK11 in Libreville.
[@1] The raid was carried out during a violent storm. [@2] Hooded agents armed
with Kalashnikovs and travelling in all-terrain vehicles sealed off the
perimeter, a red zone where construction is formally forbidden. [@2]

Officers seized several illicit products, notably chanvre indien, or Indian
hemp, along with cocaine and tramadol, according to information reported by
Gabon24 on Thursday 19 November 2026. [@1] The site had become a sales and
circulation point for narcotic products, exposing local people, especially the
young, to the substances. [@1]

Several suspects were surprised by the speed of the operation and reportedly had
no time to flee, and some gave investigators details about their activities and
supply sources in initial interviews. [@1] One told investigators he had bought
his goods at the Barça from a man called Moussa. [@1] The mission's leaders said
the operations would continue across Libreville and the rest of the country,
destroying shelters used as meeting points and intercepting those involved. [@2]
The operation forms part of a wider offensive by the Gabonese authorities
against drug trafficking. [@1]
