A man was reported for driving under the influence of cannabis after a family
dispute in Selbitz, Bavaria, kept police busy several times on one afternoon.
[@2] A family dispute took place at midday on Wednesday 25 November 2026 in
Rothenbürg, a district of Selbitz. [@1] Police called to the dispute said
several beer bottles were smashed during the argument. [@1] Officers were called
again in the afternoon and told that one of those involved had an addiction
problem and regularly consumed cannabis and alcohol. [@1]

While police were speaking with him, the suspect drove up in his car and
officers noticed signs typical of drug use. [@1] He agreed to a voluntary test,
according to police, which reacted positively to THC, the psychoactive substance
in cannabis. [@1] For further investigations, officers ordered a blood sample to
be taken. [@1]

A report was filed against the man. [@1] The Naila police inspection said on
Thursday that the matter ended with a charge for driving under the influence of
cannabis. [@2]
