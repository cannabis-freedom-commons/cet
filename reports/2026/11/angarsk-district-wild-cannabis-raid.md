A raid was carried out in the Angarsk urban okrug of Irkutsk Oblast after an
operational signal about possible growth of banned crops. [@1] Specialists were
working through the information to establish the owners of the land plot. [@1]

Secretary of the Anti-Narcotics Commission Anna Petruk said the work to detect
and destroy wild cannabis in the area is systematic and continues year-round.
[@1] She said particular attention is paid to monitoring between seasons,
because seeds of narcotic plants survive the winter and sprout as soon as the
ice melts, before the main agricultural work begins. [@1] The commission's main
task is to identify the owners quickly so they can be held responsible and to
organise the destruction of remaining growth. [@1]

Under the law, destroying wild cannabis is the landowner's direct duty, and
administrative liability is provided for failing to eliminate places where it
grows. [@1] Residents who know where banned crops grow were invited to report
them through the Anti-Drug Guide of Irkutsk Oblast chat bot. [@1]
