Dhaka Metropolitan Police's Special Response Unit arrested 20-year-old Tarek and
24-year-old Azmir in East Dholairpar under Shyampur police station in Dhaka at
about 11:40 on 22 November 2026. [@1] Officers seized 10 grams of ganja from the
two men during the search in East Dholairpar on 22 November. [@1] Police said
both Tarek and Azmir were previously convicted accused in drug cases before this
arrest. [@1]

The arrests were made by a Cyber Crime Investigation Division team conducting a
special drive across DMP police-station areas in Dhaka to recover illegal drugs
and arrest snatchers. [@1] In a separate operation in the same drive, two men
were arrested for allegedly trying to snatch valuables from bus passengers. [@1]
Police named the four arrested men as 24-year-old Md Arif, 18-year-old Abdul
Kadir, 20-year-old Tarek and 24-year-old Azmir. [@1]

Police said further legal proceedings were initiated against all those arrested
in the operations. [@1] The Shyampur police-station area actions were separate
operations within the same SRU special drive. [@1]
