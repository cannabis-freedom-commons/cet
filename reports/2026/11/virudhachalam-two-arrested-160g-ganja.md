Police near Virudhachalam in Cuddalore district, Tamil Nadu, arrested two local
men for selling ganja, the local term for cannabis. [@1] Sub-Inspector Bhagyaraj
of Karuveppilangurichi and other officers were on patrol on 18 November 2026.
[@1] Near the Ambedkar statue in Sathukudal Melpathi they arrested Sasi, 26, son
of Senthilkumar, who was selling ganja. [@1] Officers seized from him 30 packets
of ganja weighing 5 grams each, 150 grams in all. [@1]

In A. William village they arrested Arumugam, 28, son of Sellamperumal, also
from the same area, for selling ganja. [@1] From him they seized 10 grams of
ganja, bringing the two seizures to 160 grams in total. [@1]
