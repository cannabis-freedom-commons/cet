Miskolc police are investigating a 57-year-old local man for drug trafficking,
suspecting that he both grew and sold the drug. [@2] Officers searched his
property on Magashegy, in Miskolc, on 19 November 2026. [@2] The man was taken
into custody and police initiated his letartóztatás, pre-trial detention. [@1]

In the goat shed, a building on the property used for keeping animals, officers
found 50 cannabis plants. [@1] Police found further suspected drugs in various
quantities in different hiding places on the property. [@2] Officers seized cash
and equipment that could be used to distribute the drugs. [@3]

The case began with a traffic stop, when the Miskolc police detection unit
checked a car and its occupants in the days before the search. [@2] A passenger
got out of the car and tried to get rid of a jar he was carrying by throwing it
under the vehicle. [@1] Officers noticed the movement and found drugs in the
container. [@1] Police said the trail from consumers led them to the dealer on
Miskolc's Magashegy. [@2] Police did not say how long the man had been selling,
how many buyers he had reached or what the seized drugs were worth. [@1] The
Borsod-Abaúj-Zemplén County Police Headquarters published a video of the
operation. [@1]
