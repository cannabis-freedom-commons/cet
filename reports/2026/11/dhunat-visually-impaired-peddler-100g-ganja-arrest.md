Police in Dhunat upazila of Bogura arrested 65-year-old visually impaired
peddler Shahjahan Ali while he was standing on an empty road selling ganja. [@1]
Officers seized 100 grams of ganja from him at the time. [@1]

The arrest took place on Thursday night on the road in front of the Chithulia
cemetery, where he was standing and selling ganja. [@1] On Friday 13 November at
noon he was sent from Dhunat police station through the court to Bogura district
jail. [@1]

Shahjahan Ali is the son of Abdul Kuddus of Chithulia village in Gosaibari Union
of the upazila. [@1] Police describe him as a listed drug trader with multiple
prior cases under the Narcotics Control Act. [@1] A new case under the Narcotics
Control Act was filed against him at the police station over this incident. [@1]
Dhunat police station officer-in-charge Atiqul Islam said he was arrested with
ganja as part of a special anti-drug drive and sent to jail. [@1]
