Songadh police in Bhavnagar district received a specific tip that cannabis was
being cultivated secretly in the wada, the enclosed yard, of a residential house
in Ambla village. [@1] A police team then carried out a planned sudden raid on
the house yard in the village. [@1] Police arrested Jitugiri Goswami in
connection with the cultivation. [@1] Jitugiri Goswami was the only accused
named in connection with the cultivation. [@1]

Officers found a total of 54 green cannabis plants at the site. [@1] Police
recorded the total weight of the plants as 2.470 kilograms. [@1] Police seized
the plants and other material together worth Rs 61,750. [@1] Police sought help
from the Forensic Science Laboratory to verify scientifically whether the seized
plants were cannabis. [@1] Forensic examination officially confirmed that the
seized plants were cannabis. [@1]

Ganja is the local term used in this case for cannabis. [@1] After the
confirmation, police registered a case against the accused under the Narcotic
Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act and continued further investigation. [@1]
The NDPS Act is India's narcotics control law. [@1]
