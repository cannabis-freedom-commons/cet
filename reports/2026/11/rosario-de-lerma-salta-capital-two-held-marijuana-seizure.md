Salta police raided a home in barrio San Bernardo in Rosario de Lerma and placed
a 27-year-old woman at the disposal of the judiciary. [@1] During preventive
patrols near the Astillero river by the Circunvalación Oeste in Salta Capital,
motorbike officers stopped a 21-year-old youth who was carrying marijuana in his
clothing and he was detained. [@1] Across the two procedures two people were
placed at the disposal of the judiciary. [@1]

In the Rosario de Lerma raid officers seized cocaine, cash, a mobile phone and
other items of interest to the case. [@2] In the Salta Capital check officers
called in specialist drug investigators who seized the marijuana. [@1]

The Rosario de Lerma investigation into alleged retail selling began from a
report on the Denuncias Web platform and led to the search warrant for the
property. [@1] The two procedures were carried out by different Salta police
units through investigative work and preventive action. [@1] The Unidad Fiscal
contra la Narcocriminalidad took intervention in both cases. [@2]
