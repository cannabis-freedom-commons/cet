Officers of the Gotse Delchev police department detained a 29-year-old man from
the village of Valkosel under a detention order for up to 24 hours. [@1] The man
was held after officers established that he had been driving after using a
narcotic substance. [@2] The roadside check took place at about 23:25 on 18
November 2026. [@2] Officers stopped the passenger car driven by the man. [@2]
The stop for a check took place in the village of Ablanitsa. [@2]

A technical device used at the roadside indicated that the driver had driven
after the use of a drug. [@2] During the check officers found about 0.4 grams of
dry herbaceous material on the man that reacted positively to cannabis in the
reported field test. [@1] The reported amount was given as approximate, at about
0.4 grams. [@2]

Police opened pre-trial proceedings in the case following the stop, the
drug-driving finding and the seizure. [@1] The detention was imposed as a police
measure for up to 24 hours, with no court outcome reported at this stage. [@2]
