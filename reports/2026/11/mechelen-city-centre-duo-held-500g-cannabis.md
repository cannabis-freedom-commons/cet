On Saturday evening at around 17:20, a driver and a passenger travelling
together on a shared scooter drew police attention in the city centre. [@1] When
officers tried to check them, the two fled, first riding away on the scooter and
then continuing their flight on foot. [@1] The officers arrested the duo and
took them to the police station, where they were held for further investigation.
[@1]

During their flight on the way, the pair threw away a bag containing 500 grams
of cannabis. [@1] The reported 500 grams corresponds to half a kilogram of
cannabis in total. [@1]

Three striking police checks were reported in Mechelen during that same weekend.
[@1] The checks were handled by the traffic police, who also reported two other
incidents that weekend. [@1] Van de Sande gave the account of the duo being
picked up and transferred to the station. [@1] The report carrying these police
details was published on 23 November 2026 at 09:18. [@1]
