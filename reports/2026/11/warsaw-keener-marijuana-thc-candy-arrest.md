at a residence on 12 November 2026. [@1] Officers knocked on the door, met
Keener there and brought him outside the residence. [@1] Brandon Lee Keener, 41,
lives at 2709 Glad St. in Warsaw. [@1] Keener was arrested and has since been
released on bond. [@1] He was charged with possession of marijuana with a prior
conviction and possession of marijuana, alongside methamphetamine,
controlled-substance, nuisance and paraphernalia counts. [@1]

Officers searched the residence and a vehicle belonging to Keener. [@1] In the
residence they found a bag of THC-infused candy and a bag containing marijuana.
[@1] In the vehicle they found a smoking device with burnt plant material
identified as marijuana. [@1] The residence search also found methamphetamine
and a plastic bag containing psilocybin mushrooms weighing 4.4 grams, which are
excluded from the cannabis figures. [@1] Keener admitted being a methamphetamine
and marijuana user and knowing about the smoking devices in the vehicle and
residence. [@1]

Keener has a previous conviction for possession of methamphetamine from Allen
County in 2015. [@1]
