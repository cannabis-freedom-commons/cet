The main suspect was travelling in a private vehicle when police arrested him.
[@4] Four balls of Indian hemp, the local term for cannabis, weighing about four
kilograms, were found in his possession and seized. [@1] The National Police
announced the seizure in a written statement on 26 November 2026. [@3]

The regional anti-drug unit (AR-UAD) of Pô and the security control post at
Tambolo/Pô carried out control, search and observation missions that led to
arrests and the seizures. [@1] Seneweb described it as a separate intervention
in which officers intercepted an individual transporting Indian hemp in a
vehicle. [@2] The same statement also announced a separate seizure of Tapentadol
and Tramaking from traffickers who used motorcycles on rural tracks. [@1]
Seneweb reported that several suspects were arrested in the pharmaceutical
operation and put the value of the medicines seized at more than 20 million CFA
francs. [@2]

The police statement put the value of the seized merchandise at more than 20
million CFA francs. [@1] They thanked residents for their cooperation and urged
them to report suspected cases on the numbers 17, 16 and 1010. [@1] Pô is in the
Centre-Sud region of Burkina Faso. [@3]
