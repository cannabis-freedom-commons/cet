Officers from the drug-crime unit of the Łódź city police detained a 46-year-old
man who cultivated and possessed intoxicants in his flat. [@2] On 9 November
2026 they checked a flat on Bydgoska Street in Łódź after receiving information
about criminal activity there. [@2] The suspect was charged with possessing
significant quantities of drugs, manufacturing them and cultivating cannabis.
[@2] By decision of the Łódź-Bałuty district prosecutor he was placed under
police supervision. [@2]

Investigators established that he ran the illegal grow in two premises, one on
Bydgoska Street in Łódź and the second in Zgierz. [@1] In a specially prepared
grow tent officers found six cannabis plants. [@2] Officers seized a total of
almost 1,900 grams gross of the intoxicant, almost 1.9 kg of marijuana, along
with growing accessories and equipment. [@2]

The flat owner, found at the scene, admitted cultivating the prohibited
substance, saying he did it for his own use. [@2] The offences carry a penalty
of up to 10 years of imprisonment. [@2]
