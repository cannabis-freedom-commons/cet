Police in Barranquilla arrested three men aged 25, 32 and 27 in flagrante on
suspicion of trafficking, manufacturing or possession of narcotics. [@1] The
operation was carried out by officers of the Riomar Police Station in the vía 40
sector of the Siape neighbourhood. [@1] The arrest was made under the 'Sin
Tregua al Delito' strategy. [@2]

Patrols on surveillance and control duty surprised the three men travelling in a
red Hyundai Accent, in which they were carrying 483 grams of marijuana. [@1]
Police valued the marijuana at approximately 1.2 million pesos. [@1] The three
were placed at the disposal of the Fiscalía General de la Nación for the
corresponding judicial process. [@1]

Brigadier General Sandra Liliana Rodríguez Castro, commander of the Barranquilla
Metropolitan Police, said such procedures are part of permanent actions to
counter drug trafficking and strengthen security in the city's different
sectors. [@1] The National Police said it would continue operational and
preventive action against illicit drug trafficking and invited residents to
report criminal activity through the 123 emergency line or the 317 896 5523
Crime Against line. [@1]
