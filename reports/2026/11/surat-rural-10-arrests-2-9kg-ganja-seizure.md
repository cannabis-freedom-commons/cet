Surat Rural Police registered 10 cases under the NDPS Act in a single day in
Surat district. [@1] Police arrested 10 accused in connection with the drug
trade in the same single-day drive. [@1]

Officers seized a total of 2.9 kilograms of ganja in the drive. [@1]

Police said they had adopted an aggressive strategy against the drug trade
harming youth in the district. [@1] The drive was part of intensive work running
for several days to break the drug network at its roots, during which district
teams recorded 10 serious cases under the NDPS Act in a single day. [@1] The
operation was carried out jointly by the Local Crime Branch, the Special
Operation Group and the Anti-Narcotics Wing of the Surat Rural Police. [@1] The
teams conducted intensive checking and raids with precise planning, teamwork and
coordination in Palod, Kosamba, Kamrej, Kadodara, Bardoli and Palsana and other
key areas. [@1]
