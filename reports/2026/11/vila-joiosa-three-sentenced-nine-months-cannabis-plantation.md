A criminal court in Alicante sentenced three people to nine months in prison and
a 14,400-euro fine over a marijuana plantation at a country house in La Vila
Joiosa. [@1] The court applied an attenuating circumstance for undue delays
because the case took six years to reach trial. [@1] The sentence is not final
and can be appealed before the Audiencia Provincial. [@1]

National Police officers searched the property in the partida Almisera, a rural
area of La Vila Joiosa, on 4 September 2020. [@1] The search followed a
complaint by the former partner of one of the accused, who told officers at the
police station there were marijuana plants near the chicken coop. [@1] The
defence argued the search was void because it began with a complaint made out of
revenge and without enough evidence for a judge to order it. [@1] The court
found that officers did not act blindly on a private grudge but set up external
surveillance after the tip, which confirmed the indications. [@1] It noted that
the woman had lived on the property for more than 17 years and that the accused
did not report her entry that day. [@1] Neighbours had complained about the
strong smell of cannabis in the area. [@1] Police obtained the warrant two days
after the woman's complaint. [@1]

Officers found an underground room covered with corrugated cement panels, with
air extraction, fans and drip irrigation, holding 80 pots and an illegal
electricity connection. [@1] There was also a small plantation outdoors, hidden
among fruit trees. [@1] The count of the operation was eight plants weighing
more than 800 grams, another that had not flowered, eleven cuttings, more than
half a kilo of leaves and four precision scales. [@1] A hashish tablet was also
seized from another of those involved, who arrived at the property at speed
during the search. [@1]

The judge did not believe their accounts, saying that although small quantities
were seized, the infrastructure on the plot was that of a large plantation, with
pots ready to be planted. [@1] She found it proven that one accused took part in
preparing the plants with the aim of distributing them. [@1] The case spent two
years under investigation and more than four years stalled in the criminal court
in Alicante. [@1]
