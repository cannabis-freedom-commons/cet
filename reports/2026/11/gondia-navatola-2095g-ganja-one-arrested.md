Deori police took action against illegal cannabis transport and seized a
ganja-like substance. [@1] The action took place near the Navatola forest
department checkpost on National Highway 53 on 15 November. [@1] Police arrested
a 28-year-old youth in the case. [@1] The arrested accused was named as
28-year-old Vishal Niranjan Patil. [@1] Police said the accused is originally
from Bhandara district and was currently living in Nagpur. [@1]

Officers searched the suspect's bluish travel bag in the checkpost area on the
Raipur-Nagpur route in the presence of a gazetted officer and panch witnesses.
[@1] The bag held two bundles wrapped in khaki-coloured polythene. [@1] The
bundles contained a greenish, pungent-smelling, moist ganja-like substance in
the form of sticks, seeds, leaves and crushed material. [@1] Police said the
substance weighed 2 kg 95 g in total. [@1] Police put the substance's value at
Rs 104,750 and the bag's value at Rs 2,000. [@1]

Police registered a case at Deori police station. [@1] Further investigation was
being carried out by Sub-Inspector Milind Gedam under the guidance of Inspector
Ganesh Wanare. [@1]
