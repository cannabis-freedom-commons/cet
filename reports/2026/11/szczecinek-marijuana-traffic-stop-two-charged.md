Police in Szczecinek stopped a Volkswagen for a check because its lighting was
faulty. [@1,2] Three residents of Barwice were in the car. [@1,3] During the check
officers smelled a characteristic odour from inside the car and noticed the men
behaving unusually, which made them suspect drugs were in the vehicle. [@1,2]

A 41-year-old passenger tried to hide substances under the seat. [@1,3] Officers
noticed this and secured small amounts of mephedrone, amphetamine and marijuana.
[@1,3]

The 20-year-old driver told police he was under the influence of marijuana. [@1,2]
Officers seized the driver's licence and ordered a blood sample at a medical
facility, with a laboratory test to determine the concentration in his body.
[@1,3] Police said the 41-year-old will be prosecuted for possessing drugs and the
driver for driving under their influence, and both face up to three years in
prison. [@1,2]
