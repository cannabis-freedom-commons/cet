Sibiu Tribunal convicted the driver for possessing cannabis, driving with a
suspended licence, driving under the influence of psychoactive substances and
using number plates assigned to another car. [@1] The court found the acts took
place on the evening of 15 November 2025, when the man drove a BMW on public
roads although his driving right was suspended. [@1] The BMW carried plates
assigned to a Peugeot, while the BMW's own plates were found in the boot. [@1]

Toxicology tests indicated 3-CMC and THC in his blood and mephedrone and THC in
his urine. [@1] During the check police found 0.72 grams of cannabis in the car,
which the court said was held for his own consumption. [@1] The court noted his
licence had been suspended in July 2025 after he was caught driving with 0.31
mg/l pure alcohol in exhaled air, extended by 30 days because he did not take or
pass the traffic-rules test. [@1]

For the four offences the tribunal imposed individual terms of between 3 months
and 1 year and 6 months in prison. [@1] After merger the resulting punishment
was set at 2 years and 1 month, with execution suspended under supervision for 3
years. [@1] The man must follow a social reintegration programme and perform 90
days of unpaid community work. [@1] He was also banned from driving for 2 years
and ordered to pay 3,500 lei in court costs. [@1] The ruling was pronounced on
12 November 2026 and can be appealed within 10 days of notification. [@1]
