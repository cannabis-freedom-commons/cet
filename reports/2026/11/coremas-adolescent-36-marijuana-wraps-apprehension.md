Military police in Coremas, Paraíba, apprehended an adolescent on the night of
Friday 20 November 2026 after finding him with portions of substances resembling
marijuana and cocaine. [@1] Patrols began after residents reported possible drug
sales in the city centre and social media posts also pointed to suspected
trafficking there. [@1] Officers saw two men on a sidewalk, one of whom fled and
was not found, while the other, an adolescent, was stopped. [@1] Diamante Online
says the patrol was commanded by Lieutenant Colonel C. Lima. [@2] The youth's
mother told officers at the scene that he had previously tried to assault her.
[@2]

In the personal search officers found 36 trouxinhas, or small wraps, of a
substance resembling marijuana. [@1] Repórter PB reported 19 wraps of a
cocaine-like material alongside the marijuana-like wraps. [@1] Diamante Online
instead reported 16 packages of a cocaine-like substance and only one precision
scale. [@2] Repórter PB said officers also took a Motorola phone, two precision
scales and a fish knife. [@1]

Asked where the drugs came from, the adolescent said they belonged to a man
known as Nego Bila, who he said was held in the Pombal public jail. [@1] Police
said the claim had not been independently confirmed. [@1] The youth was taken to
the civil police station at Piancó, accompanied by his mother, for legal
procedures, and all the seized material was handed to the police authority. [@1]
The military police did not disclose the adolescent's age, his identity or the
whereabouts of the second man. [@1]
