Military Police officers from the 23rd Battalion (23º BPM) in Pernambuco seized
about half a tonne of marijuana in the rural zone of Tuparetama, in the Sertão,
on the afternoon of Friday 20 November 2026. [@2] The operation followed
information shared by the battalion's intelligence teams with the officers
taking part. [@1]

Officers found 474 kilograms of marijuana, packed in 27 sacks and described by
the police as ready for consumption. [@1] With that information, the 23rd
Battalion contingent carried out checks in the indicated area while drones
helped in the search for the material. [@1] The drones helped the officers
locate where the drug was stored in the rural area of the municipality. [@1] The
drug was already prepared for consumption, according to the information released
about the operation. [@1]

After the seizure, the whole load was collected and presented at the Civil
Police station in Afogados da Ingazeira, the municipality that received the
material for the necessary procedures. [@1] The material stayed under the Civil
Police's responsibility for the follow-up required. [@1]
