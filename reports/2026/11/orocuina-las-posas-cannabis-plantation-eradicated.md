The Anti-Drug Trafficking Directorate (DLCN), working with the Public Order
Military Police (PMOP), located and completely eradicated an illegal marijuana
plantation in the hamlet of Las Posas, Orocuina municipality, Choluteca
department, on Tuesday 17 November 2026. [@1] The operation formed part of
investigative work in the south of the country against cannabis cultivation and
local drug distribution. [@1]

Detectives and soldiers found bushes of suspected Cannabis sativa averaging
between 1 and 1.5 metres tall, at an advanced stage of growth and ready for
harvest and later sale. [@1] After photographing and documenting the find and
taking samples for toxicological analysis, the units uprooted and burned all the
plants at the site. [@1]

The authorities said the action was part of investigations aimed at dismantling
microtrafficking networks operating in the municipalities of the southern
corridor. [@1] The Public Prosecutor's Office and the Armed Forces said they
would keep permanent tracing and eradication operations in rural areas. [@1]
