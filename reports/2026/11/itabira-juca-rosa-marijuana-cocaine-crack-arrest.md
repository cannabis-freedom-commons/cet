Military Police arrested one individual in the Juca Rosa neighbourhood of
Itabira on the night of 14 November 2026 on suspicion of involvement with drug
trafficking. [@1] Officers recorded the occurrence at about 19h15 on Rua Ipê
after checking a tip received through the Disque-Denúncia Unificado that pointed
to drug trafficking at a residence in the area. [@1] Teams from the 83rd
Tactical Mobile Company together with the Operations Command carried out the
police operation at the location. [@1] The individual named in the tip was
located and approached in an alley in the neighbourhood. [@1]

Officers found drugs on him, then used sniffer dog Loki to search the area and
help locate other materials linked to the occurrence. [@1] In total, officers
seized one packet of cocaine, 28 crack rocks, two portions of marijuana, R$22 in
change and two mobile phones of doubtful origin. [@1]

The individual was given voice of arrest and taken with the seized materials to
the Civil Police station in Itabira for the applicable legal measures. [@1] The
report presents the occurrence as showing the importance of population tips in
confronting drug trafficking and the crime-fighting work of the security forces.
