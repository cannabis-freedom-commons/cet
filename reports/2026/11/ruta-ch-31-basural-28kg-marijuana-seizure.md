Specialized S7 Carabineros personnel, together with officials of the Canine
Training School, carried out an operation in the Atacama region. [@1] The
operation seized 28 kilos and 83 grams of marijuana. [@1] The procedure took
place at kilometre 36 of Ruta CH-31. [@1] Officers found the drug hidden among
waste in an informal dump in the sector. [@1]

According to Carabineros, the operation began after a call received through
emergency line 133. [@1] Specialized personnel deployed with drug-detector dogs
to carry out the inquiries. [@1]

Carabineros counted the haul as more than 56,000 doses of marijuana worth about
$281 million pesos. [@1] Carabineros said the procedure was part of preventive
and operational work to keep illicit substances from reaching Atacama
communities. [@1]
