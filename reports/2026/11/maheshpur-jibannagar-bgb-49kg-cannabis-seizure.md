Border Guard Bangladesh's Maheshpur 58 Battalion arrested 175 people in separate
operations along the Maheshpur and Jibannagar border areas of Jhenaidah and
Chuadanga over two and a half months. [@1] The operations ran from 1 September
to 20 November to prevent illegal infiltration and smuggling, according to a BGB
press release. [@1]

The seized drugs included 49.072 kg of cannabis, alongside 1,252 bottles of
foreign liquor, 5,957 bottles of banned Indian syrup, 6,743 yaba pills and 8,361
intoxicating tablets. [@1] BGB also recovered two firearms, four rounds of live
ammunition and 575.03 grams of gold during the period. [@1] BGB put the worth of
the seized smuggled goods, drugs, gold and firearms at Tk 4.38 crore. [@1]

Several cases were filed with Maheshpur and Jibannagar police stations in
connection with the incidents. [@1] Maheshpur 58 BGB Battalion Commander Lt Col
Md Rafiqul Alam said patrols and intelligence surveillance had been strengthened
in vulnerable areas to prevent push-ins and smuggling. [@1] He said joint
operations were being conducted with police and the administration, alongside
awareness meetings for border-area residents. [@1]
