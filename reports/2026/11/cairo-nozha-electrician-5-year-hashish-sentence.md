The Cairo Criminal Court sentenced an electrician to five years of rigorous
imprisonment for drug trafficking in the Nozha area. [@1] The prosecution
referred the defendant, Farghali S., aged 33 and an electrician, to criminal
trial for possessing hashish with intent to traffic outside the legally
permitted cases. [@1]

Investigations found that a Nozha police investigations officer spotted the
defendant during a security patrol, and that he was wanted for enforcement of a
Mallawi appeal misdemeanour judgment sentencing him to one month in jail with 50
pounds bail. [@1] The officer arrested him and found pieces of hashish, money
and a mobile phone on him, and the defendant admitted possessing the drugs for
trafficking when confronted. [@1]

The chemical laboratory report confirmed the seizure was 10 pieces of
schedule-one hashish weighing 12 grams and 56 centigrams. [@1]
