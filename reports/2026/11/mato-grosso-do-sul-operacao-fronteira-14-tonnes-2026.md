Brazil's Federal Revenue Service reported R$234.4 million in seizures in Mato
Grosso do Sul during Operação Fronteira Receita Federal 2026, an enforcement
drive across several municipalities in the state. [@1] The service's balance
sheet put the statewide haul at more than 16 tonnes of drugs removed from
circulation, R$41.3 million in irregular goods and 35 people arrested. [@1]

About 14 tonnes of that haul were marijuana-like substances, including a single
seizure of almost eight tonnes. [@1] The deputy superintendent for the 1st
Fiscal Region highlighted large volumes of marijuana and skunk seized around
Mundo Novo, alongside cocaine loads intercepted in Corumbá and Campo Grande.
[@1]

The actions took place in Campo Grande, Corumbá, Dourados, Mundo Novo and Ponta
Porã, as well as Brasília in the Federal District, with about 200 Revenue
officers supported by hundreds of federal, state and municipal agents. [@1]
Counting the Federal District actions, the 1st Fiscal Region reported more than
R$236 million over the operation's nine days, beating the previous edition and
setting a new regional record. [@1] The Revenue Service said the Fronteira
operation began in Mato Grosso do Sul in 2021 and was expanded nationwide in
2025. [@1]
