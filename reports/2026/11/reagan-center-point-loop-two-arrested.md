Deputies from the Henderson County Sheriff's Office and agents from the
Tennessee Bureau of Investigation carried out a search warrant in Reagan,
Tennessee, on Tuesday 24 November 2026. [@1] The warrant was served on Center
Point Loop in Henderson County. [@1] Two people were arrested in the search.
[@1]

Stanley Brunt, 53, was charged with possession with intent to resell a Schedule
II drug and with possession with intent to resell a Schedule IV drug. [@1] He
was also charged with unlawful drug paraphernalia. [@1] Angela Hodnett, 25, was
arrested and charged with unlawful drug paraphernalia. [@1]

Investigators found felony amounts of methamphetamine, marijuana and drug
paraphernalia in the search, the sheriff's office said. [@1] The sheriff's
office quoted Sheriff Atkinson on the value of the cooperation and teamwork
between the two agencies. [@1]
