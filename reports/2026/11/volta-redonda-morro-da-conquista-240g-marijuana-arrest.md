Military Police officers in Volta Redonda arrested a 19-year-old woman on
suspicion of drug trafficking on the night of Thursday 19 November 2026. [@1]
The arrest was made during a patrol in the area of Morro da Conquista, a hill
neighbourhood in the city. [@1] Officers said they were looking for a motorcycle
and its rider, who might have been involved in a homicide in the Nova Primavera
neighbourhood. [@2]

The Military Police said she threw three small bags on the ground when she saw
the patrol car, and that officers found marijuana in them. [@2] During the
search officers noticed an object in her pocket, and she told them she was also
carrying two more portions of marijuana. [@2] Forensic examination confirmed the
material was marijuana and put the total at 240 grams in 48 packets. [@1]

She was taken to the Volta Redonda police station, where she remained in custody
suspected of drug trafficking. [@2] The Military Police did not say whether
further steps were taken over the suspect or over the homicide under
investigation in Nova Primavera. [@1]
