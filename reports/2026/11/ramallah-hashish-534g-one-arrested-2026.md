Palestinian police arrested a man suspected of trading in and promoting drugs in
an operation west of Ramallah. [@1] The anti-drug branch of Ramallah and
Al-Bireh governorate police carried out field surveillance before moving in,
acting on information about the man's activity. [@1] Police said the drugs were
found in the possession of a dealer suspected of promoting them in the
governorate. [@2]

Officers seized five slabs of suspected hashish weighing 533.7 grams. [@1] They
also seized two containers of green material suspected to be marijuana. [@1] The
same operation produced 376 pills suspected to be ecstasy. [@1] Officers seized
ten small bags of white material suspected to be crystal, weighing about 160
grams, which are not counted in the cannabis figures. [@1]

Police held the suspect and the seized material to complete legal procedures
against him. [@1] Police said they would keep pursuing drug dealers and
promoters. [@1]
