Police in Minusinsk detained a 45-year-old local man suspected of illegally
storing narcotics. [@1] The man had previously been convicted of a similar
offence. [@1]

Officers found the marijuana on his household plot beside the banya, the plot's
bath house. [@1] Forensic examination confirmed the plant material was marijuana
weighing more than 480 grams. [@1] The find was made by the narcotics control
unit of the Minusinsk police department during the federal operation Clean
Generation 2026. [@3] The Krasnoyarsk regional police headquarters reported the
case. [@2]

Investigators opened a criminal case under part 2 of article 228 of the Russian
Criminal Code, illegal storage of narcotic drugs without intent to sell in a
large amount. [@1] The charge carries a maximum penalty of 10 years in prison.
[@1] The man was placed under a written undertaking not to leave, the standard
Russian pre-trial measure for a suspect not held in custody. [@3]
