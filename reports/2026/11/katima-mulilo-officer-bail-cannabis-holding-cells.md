A 36-year-old Namibian police officer was arrested on 12 November 2026 over an
attempt to smuggle cannabis into the holding cells at Katima Mulilo police
station. [@2] The officer, Mudengi Lukato, was off duty when fellow officers
caught him behind the police station holding cells with packets of cannabis,
cigarettes and cellphone batteries. [@1]

Police valued the cannabis at N$3 000. [@1] A case was opened against him for
possession of cannabis. [@2] Lukato made his first appearance in the Katima
Mulilo Magistrate's Court on Monday 16 November, where he was granted bail of
N$2 000. [@1]

Zambezi regional police commander commissioner Julia Sakuwa-Neo told The
Namibian that the officer was arrested immediately by the officers who caught
him. [@1] Sakuwa-Neo warned all police officers to refrain from smuggling
contraband into the cells and said those found would be dealt with accordingly.
[@1] She said officers caught smuggling prohibited items into holding cells
would face disciplinary and legal action. [@2]
