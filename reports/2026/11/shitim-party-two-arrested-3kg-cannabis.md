Police searched a car in the parking area of a party at Shitim, near Eilat, and
arrested two young people who had arrived in it. [@1] The two were arrested at
09:06 on 20 November 2026. [@1] A police representative told the court the stop
was random, with no prior intelligence or suspicion towards the pair. [@1] The
woman, a 20-year-old Tel Aviv resident, had known the man for only about two
weeks and her lawyer said she did not drive the car and did not know about the
drugs. [@1]

Police said about 3 kg of suspected cannabis was found in the car, including
about 2.1 kg in medical cannabis packaging. [@2] The court account gave the
amount as about 2 kg of cannabis, with additional bags and seven small
containers of a substance suspected to be cocaine. [@1] The police
representative said the seven containers' contents would be sent for laboratory
testing and no result confirming the substance had been presented in court. [@1]
Police opened an investigation on suspicion of possessing drugs other than for
personal use and of drug trafficking. [@1] At the Nur festival in Shitim,
security guards had repeatedly reported a man trying to enter the venue without
permission. [@3] After he tried to evade officers, they found small bottles
containing residue of a suspected drug on him. [@4]

At a hearing at the Eilat Magistrate's Court on 20 November 2026, Judge Gil
Edelman found reasonable suspicion against both but said the suspicion against
the woman was weaker. [@1] He extended the man's detention until 25 November
2026 at 10:00. [@1] The woman was placed under full house arrest at her mother's
home in Tel Aviv until 26 November 2026 at 10:00, with a 2,500-shekel
third-party guarantee and a 3,000-shekel personal commitment. [@1] Police asked
for a 48-hour delay to consider an appeal and the court postponed her release
until 22 November 2026 at 12:00. [@1] Police then filed an appeal, with a
hearing on her release set for the next day at the Beersheba District Court.
