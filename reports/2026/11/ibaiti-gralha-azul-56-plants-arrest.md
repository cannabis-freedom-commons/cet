A joint Paraná Military Police and Civil Police operation dismantled a structure
for cultivating, processing and distributing marijuana derivatives in Ibaiti on
the morning of Wednesday 25 November 2026. [@1] Officers executing a
search-and-seizure warrant in the Gralha Azul neighbourhood arrested a
42-year-old man in flagrante. [@1] The operation took place at about 8 a.m. with
teams from the 2nd Military Police Battalion and agents of the 37th Regional
Police Station. [@1] The suspect tried to flee inside the residence to reach and
hide mobile phones, disobeyed police orders and offered physical resistance
before being contained and handcuffed. [@1] Two military police officers
suffered light abrasions during the intervention. [@1]

In an outbuilding behind the property, police found an air-conditioned structure
covered with shade cloth and set up for cultivating, drying, processing,
portioning and storing the drug. [@1] The space held refrigerated compartments
for keeping dehydrated cannabis flowers and a large quantity of seeds of
different varieties. [@1] Officers found a running computer with cryptocurrency
transactions including Bitcoin and records of shipment orders to several regions
of the country linked to the Growers Brasil platform. [@1] A Copel technical
team confirmed that the greenhouse equipment was powered by a clandestine
connection straight into the electricity grid. [@1]

The man was arrested in flagrante for drug trafficking, qualified theft of
electricity, resistance and bodily injury. [@1] He and the seized materials were
taken to the 37th Regional Police Station in Ibaiti. [@1] The operation seized
56 potted marijuana plants, 615.14 grams of a marijuana-like substance and 16.51
grams of a hashish-like substance. [@1] Officers also collected 94 containers
and 122 cards or envelopes holding seeds of different varieties, plus a box
ready for dispatch to São Paulo containing two seed tubes. [@1] The seized
equipment included two notebooks, a 4 TB external hard drive, two iPhones, three
precision scales and a pack of one thousand microtubes with ziplock packaging,
stickers and customised boxes. [@1]
