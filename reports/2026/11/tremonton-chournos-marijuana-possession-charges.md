A Tremonton man is facing eight criminal charges, including four felonies, after
an investigation into an alleged assault led authorities to LSD, psilocybin,
methamphetamine and marijuana inside his home. [@1] Dugan Johnnie Chournos, 46,
was charged with one count of possession of marijuana with prior convictions, a
third-degree felony. [@1] He also faces one class A misdemeanor count of assault
resulting in substantial bodily injury and three class B misdemeanor counts of
possession or use of drug paraphernalia. [@1] Charges are allegations, and
Chournos is presumed innocent unless proven guilty. [@1]

According to charging documents, the investigation began on 9 November 2026,
after Chournos allegedly struck another person during an argument. [@1] Court
documents state the victim suffered a “deep black eye and swelling”. [@1]
Chournos allowed Tremonton-Garland Police officers into his home to explain what
had happened. [@1] Inside, officers reported smelling marijuana and seeing
marijuana cigarette remnants in plain view. [@1] Asked about additional drugs,
he allegedly told them the drugs were in his nightstand and let them collect the
substances. [@1] Beyond more marijuana, officers recovered makeshift bongs and
pipes, a grinder, LSD, psilocybin and a clear substance that a NIK test
indicated was methamphetamine, the charging documents state. [@1] A NIK test is
a presumptive field test used by police to identify suspected controlled
substances. [@1]

The severity of the drug charges reflects Chournos's prior criminal history.
[@1] According to court documents, he has at least four qualifying convictions
within the past seven years, including two felony convictions. [@1] One of those
convictions was for possession with intent to distribute a controlled substance.
[@1] Chournos was taken into custody and is currently being held without bail.
