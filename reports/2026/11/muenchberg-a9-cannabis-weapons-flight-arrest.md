Police stopped the 39-year-old driver in the early hours on the A9 near
Münchberg in the district of Hof. [@1] The man from Thuringia escaped the check
and fled on foot. [@1] Officers searching his Audi found cannabis in a
prohibited quantity, a non-negligible amount of crystal methamphetamine,
drug-use utensils and items banned under the weapons law. [@2] The number plates
on the Audi had not been issued for that vehicle and the driver was under an
administrative ban on driving motor vehicles. [@2]

Dozens of officers searched the area around Münchberg for hours, with the
federal police helping at railway stations and a police helicopter, drones and
search dogs used. [@2] Officers found the man at about 13:50 in a wooded area
west of Münchberg and arrested him without resistance. [@2] The criminal police
took over the investigation. [@3]

On the application of the Hof public prosecutor the man was brought before an
investigating judge on Wednesday 25 November. [@1] The judge ordered pre-trial
detention on suspicion of trafficking in narcotics in a non-negligible quantity
while carrying weapons. [@2] He is now held in a prison. [@2]
