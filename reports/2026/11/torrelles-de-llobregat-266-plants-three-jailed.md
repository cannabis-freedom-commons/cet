Mossos d'Esquadra arrested three men aged 30, 34 and 36 for alleged drug
trafficking and electricity fraud. [@1] Two of the arrests were made in
Torrelles de Llobregat, while police located the alleged manager of the
plantation in Sant Joan Despí. [@1] All three men were sent to prison after
their arrests, according to sources close to the investigation. [@1] One of
those arrested already had a criminal record for the same offences. [@1]

Officers dismantled a marijuana plantation with 266 plants in Torrelles de
Llobregat. [@1] At the mountain residential site, officers also found 312
cuttings and 41 kilos of marijuana buds, the part of the plant usually sold.
[@1] During the search, officers found material indicating previous grows at the
same site, including rubbish bags with plant substrate and broken ventilation
equipment. [@1]

The Mossos carried out the entry and search of a building and made the arrests
on 17 November. [@1] The Local Police of Torrelles had detected signs of the
plantation in early April. [@1]
