Anti-drug investigators of Ecuador's National Police arrested two Ecuadorian
citizens in Santo Domingo de los Tsáchilas on suspicion of illicit trafficking
of controlled substances. [@1] The intervention took place at kilometre 7 of the
Vía Quevedo, between Callejón 6 and Calle E, under the state of exception. [@1]
Officers carried out an operation called Cero Impunidad 37509 to shut down local
retail drug points. [@1] Both detainees had outstanding judicial warrants when
they were intercepted, the authorities said. [@1]

The first detainee, Gabriel E., is a 22-year-old Ecuadorian. [@1] Police records
showed a prior case against him dated 18 June 2026 for unauthorised possession
of firearms, ammunition and explosives. [@1] He also had an active wanted notice
coded 12283-2026-02206. [@1] The second detainee, Jair A., is a 26-year-old
Ecuadorian. [@1] Although he had no prior criminal record, an arrest warrant was
active against him, according to the judicial system. [@1]

Officers found a green plant substance on the suspects and preliminary chemical
tests identified it as marijuana with a net weight of 185.00 grams. [@1] The
anti-drug unit estimated that quantity at 370 doses removed from the illegal
market. [@1] Police put the value of the seized marijuana at about 140 dollars
on the national market. [@1] Officers seized two mobile phones, a turquoise
Infinix and a black Honor, as evidence. [@1]

The detainees and the seized items were placed under chain of custody at the
disposal of the competent judicial authority. [@1]
