Special Response Battalion-10 arrested 25-year-old Nazmul Hossain Nur with five
kilograms of ganja in Dhaka's Jatrabari. [@1] The arrest took place at about
9:45 pm on Monday 16 November on the Chattogram-to-Dhaka highway in the Matuail
area under Jatrabari Police Station. [@1]

Officers recovered five kilograms of ganja from Nazmul at the scene. [@1] SRB-10
Assistant Director (Media) Senior Assistant Police Superintendent Tapan Sarkar
gave the information in a press release. [@1] Nazmul is a resident of the
Atgharia area under Palashbari Police Station in Gaibandha. [@1] The release
described Nazmul as a professional drug dealer who had long collected ganja and
other drugs from border areas and supplied them in Jatrabari and other parts of
the country. [@1]

A case under the Narcotics Control Act was to be filed against him, and he was
handed over to the relevant police station with the seized drugs. [@1]
