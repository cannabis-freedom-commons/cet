Police arrested ten people in the morning in a large operation in the provinces
of Limburg and Flemish Brabant. [@1] Officers searched eighteen locations in the
operation. [@1,2] They found two active cannabis plantations, in Wellen and
Houthalen, with 1,300 plants between them. [@1,3] Police also seized 300,000 euros
in cash and eleven kilograms of cannabis. [@1,3]

The arrests were of eight men and two women from Hasselt, Alken, Herk-de-Stad
and Wellen. [@1] Nine of the ten were placed under arrest warrant, meaning an
investigating judge ordered them held. [@1] Another 24-year-old suspect from Hasselt also remains in custody following a recent ruling by the Antwerp Indictments Chamber. [@2] Three other suspects in the case had already been released under conditions earlier in the month, a 23-year-old woman from Hasselt, a 44-year-old man from Wellen and a 30-year-old woman from Alken. [@3] On Tuesday the Hasselt council chamber ordered five men in the drug-network case to remain in custody, four aged 24, 26, 29 and 46 from Hasselt and a 28-year-old living in Houthalen. [@3] The local police force LRH, which
covers the Limburg regional capital Hasselt, said at a press conference on
Thursday that this was its largest operation, on the Tuesday before the
article's 24 September publication. [@1]
