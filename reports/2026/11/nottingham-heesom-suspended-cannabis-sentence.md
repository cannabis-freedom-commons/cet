Devante Heesom, 32, a former soldier now of Ellwood Crescent, Wollaton, pleaded
guilty to possession with intent to supply cannabis. [@1] Judge Mark Watson at
Nottingham Crown Court handed him a two-year jail term, suspended for 18 months,
with 200 hours unpaid work. [@1] The judge told Heesom that had it not taken the
police over two years to charge him he would be spending time behind bars. [@1]
During the long delay between arrest and court Heesom, who had never troubled
the law before, had found and excelled in a job with Nottingham City Council.
[@1] He was medically discharged from the army. [@1] He provided glowing
references from a former army corporal, an ex-police officer and his employer.
[@1]

Police executed a warrant at Heesom’s then home in Mountfield Drive on September
4, 2023. [@1] The search found vast quantities of cannabis leaf, cannabis
sweets, cannabis oil and syrup in various locations around the property. [@1] In
total there was 1.67kg of cannabis plus large amounts of cannabis sweets. [@1]
More than 1,000 bags of cannabis-infused sweets were found during the raid. [@2]
The prosecution valued the sweets at between £2,695 and £10,780 and the cannabis
leaf, oil and syrup at between £21,000 and £71,000 depending on how it could
have been sold. [@1] Officers also recovered £20,063.82 in cash and messages on
his mobile phone related to him selling cannabis. [@1] The judge described what
police found as a thriving business. [@1]

A 29-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of possessing a Class B drug with
intent to supply and later released on bail. [@2] A Proceeds of Crime hearing,
which could see Heesom stripped of cash and assets, will take place on a future
date to be decided. [@1]
