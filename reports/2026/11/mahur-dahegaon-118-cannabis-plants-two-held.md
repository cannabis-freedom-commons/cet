On 16 November 2026 a Nanded Local Crime Branch team patrolling the Kinwat and
Mahur subdivisions received a tip that cannabis was being illegally grown and
tended in a farm field at Dahegaon. [@1] The team then raided field group number
85 in Dahegaon shivar. [@1] In the field the officers found cannabis grown as an
intercrop among cotton and pigeon pea. [@1]

The police team seized 118 small and large green cannabis plants from the field.
[@1] The seized plants weighed about 83 kg in total. [@1] Police put the total
value of the seized cannabis at 4,150,000 rupees (41.5 lakh rupees). [@1]

Two farm owners, aged 75 and 70, were taken into custody in the case. [@1] A
case against the accused was registered at Mahur police station on the complaint
of sub-inspector Milind Sonkamble. [@1]
