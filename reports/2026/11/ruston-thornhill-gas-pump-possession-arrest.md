Caleb Thornhill, 38, of Haughton, was arrested at 11:58 p.m. on Nov. 10 by the
Ruston Police Department. [@1] Police checked on a suspicious vehicle that had
been parked at a Ruston gas pump for several hours. [@1] An officer dispatched
to a Farmerville Highway convenience store in Ruston found Thornhill moving
around inside his gray Ford F-150. [@1] The officer ordered him to step out, and
Thornhill was visibly nervous and showed signs of impairment. [@1]

Officers saw marijuana in plain view on the back driver-side seat of the truck
and noted an odor of burnt marijuana during the search. [@1] In the truck
officers found a blunt and a mostly burnt blunt containing suspected marijuana,
along with several hard white substances suspected to be methamphetamine. [@1] A
pipe suspected to be used for smoking methamphetamine was found on Thornhill.
[@1]

Thornhill was booked on charges of possession of a Schedule I drug, possession
of a Schedule II drug and possession of drug paraphernalia. [@1] He has not been
convicted of any criminal offense and is presumed innocent until proven guilty
in a court of law. [@1]
