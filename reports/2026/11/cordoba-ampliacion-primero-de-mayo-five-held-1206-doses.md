Five adults, three men and two women, were detained in three raids by the FPA,
Córdoba's anti-narcotics force, in the city of Córdoba. [@2] The prosecutors'
office said the group sold drugs in the south-east of the city. [@2] Four of the
five were a father, a mother and their two children, who had earlier
drug-trafficking records and were free on bail. [@2] The three searched homes
were in barrio Ampliación Primero de Mayo, two of them, and Ciudad Evita, one.
[@2] When tactical teams entered one home the father resisted and tried to block
the officers, and his wife tried to get rid of the drugs, which were seized.
[@2]

The haul from the three homes was 1,206 doses of marijuana and 86 doses of
cocaine. [@2] Officers also seized 969,700 pesos in cash, a digital scale, a car
and other items of interest to the case. [@2]

The third-turn anti-narcotics prosecutor charged all five with comercialización
de estupefacientes, drug sales, under law 23.737. [@2] All five and the seized
items were sent to the courts while the inquiry continues. [@1] The inquiry was
directed by the Ministerio Público Fiscal, the provincial prosecution service,
and carried out by the FPA. [@1]
