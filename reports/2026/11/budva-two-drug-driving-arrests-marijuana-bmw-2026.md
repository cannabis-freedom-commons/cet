Budva traffic police arrested two men on 26 November 2026 for driving under the
influence of drugs. [@2] The Budva police confirmed the detentions to Vijesti
unofficially. [@1] At about ten in the morning, traffic officers arrested a
39-year-old man from Budva who was found riding an electric scooter under the
influence of cocaine. [@2] At about 16:45 they arrested a 23-year-old man from
Podgorica for driving under the influence of marijuana. [@2]

Police described both men as persons of operational interest (OIL), a
designation Montenegrin officers use for people they keep under observation.
[@2] Officers seized the unregistered BMW X3 the Podgorica man was driving and
said it was taken from him for further checks. [@1]
