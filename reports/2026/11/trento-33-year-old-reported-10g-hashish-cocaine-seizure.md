In services ordered for Piazza Portella and the immediately surrounding areas in
Trento, State Police reported a foreign citizen for possession of drugs with
intent to deal. [@1] Volanti patrol officers stopped the man during a dedicated
control service in Piazza Leonardo da Vinci in Trento. [@1] The man was later
identified as a 33-year-old foreigner with specific prior offences on record.
[@1]

He was found in possession of drugs with a total weight of about 10 grams,
described as hashish with only a minimal part of cocaine, all of which police
seized. [@1] Police recorded the whole quantity found on him as seized. [@1] The
man was then referred to the judicial authority while remaining at liberty
rather than being held in custody. [@1]

The check belonged to the ordered services covering Piazza Portella and the
immediately adjacent areas of the city. [@1] Criminal responsibility will be
established only at the end of proceedings with an irrevocable judgment, with
the presumption of innocence applying until that sentence. [@1]
