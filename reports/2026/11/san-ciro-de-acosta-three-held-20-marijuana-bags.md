Guardia Civil Estatal officers detained three suspected drug sellers in San Ciro
de Acosta during security and surveillance operations in the Zona Media. [@1]
The detained men were Hilario N, aged 44, Juan N, aged 44, and Joel N, aged 26,
all natives of San Ciro de Acosta. [@2]

Officers seized about 20 bags of marijuana in the intervention. [@1] Officers
also seized about 25 doses of cristal and cash believed to be proceeds of drug
sales. [@1] The detained men and the seized items were placed at the disposition
of the competent authority for investigation of health offences. [@3]

The Guardia Civil Estatal counted the three San Ciro arrests within its daily
total of 34 detentions across the state. [@1] A further account likewise
described the three men as detained in San Ciro de Acosta over alleged sale and
distribution of narcotics. [@4]
