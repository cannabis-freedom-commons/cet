Border police in Cascavel, Paraná, arrested a woman on the morning of 26
November 2026 after finding her carrying 3.24 kg of Moroccan hashish, valued at
about R$ 240,000, in a ride-hailing car. [@1]

The stop was made by teams of the Batalhão de Polícia de Fronteira (BPFRON), the
Border Police Battalion, on Avenida Tancredo Neves near the Havan store. [@1]
The car had left Foz do Iguaçu and was heading to Curitiba, and was occupied by
the driver and three other passengers. [@1] During the check officers grew
suspicious of one woman's nervousness and searched her. [@3] They found a
package by her leg and identified the material as Moroccan hashish. [@1] The
drug was wrapped in coffee powder, possibly to disguise its smell and make
detection harder. [@2]

She told police she would deliver the drugs to another person in Curitiba, then
continue to São Paulo, where she lives. [@1] She received a voice of arrest and
was taken to the Cascavel Civil Police station together with the seized drugs.
[@1]
