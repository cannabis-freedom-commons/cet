Border Guard land patrols in the Al-Raboah sector of Asir region intercepted an
attempt to smuggle hashish. [@1] The patrols arrested a man who was in the
kingdom in violation of the border security law, an Ethiopian national. [@1] He
was carrying 41 kilograms of the drug, the Border Guard said. [@1]

The Border Guard said initial legal procedures had been completed against the
arrested man and that he was referred with the seizures to the competent
authority. [@1] The Saudi news site Ajel reported the same Al-Raboah arrest of
an Ethiopian national with 41 kilograms of hashish and said he and the seizures
were handed to the competent authority. [@2]

The Border Guard asked the public to report drug smuggling or promotion on the
911 line in Mecca, Medina, Riyadh and the Eastern Province, the 999 line
elsewhere in the kingdom, its own 994 line and the anti-narcotics directorate's
995 line and email address. [@1] It said all reports are handled in strict
confidence and that the informant bears no liability. [@1]
