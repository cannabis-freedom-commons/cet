Fernando Antonio "N" was detained in Cancún's Paseos Kabah neighbourhood on
suspicion of crimes against health in the narcomenudeo, or small-scale drug
dealing, modality. [@1] The intervention was carried out by Grupo Orión, Zona
Norte, of the state Citizen Security Secretariat, while patrolling Supermanzana
222. [@1] When he noticed the police he tried to leave in a white Renault Kwid,
but officers stopped him on calle Juan Escutia. [@1]

A preventive inspection found 18 small bags of green plant material described as
resembling marijuana. [@1] Officers also found 15 bags of white powder
apparently cocaine, which are excluded from the cannabis figures. [@2] They also
seized three mobile phones of different brands and a black fanny pack
(cangurera). [@1] The white Renault Kwid with Quintana Roo plates USP524P that
he had tried to drive away in was also secured. [@3]

Fernando Antonio "N", the seized items and the vehicle were placed at the
disposal of the state prosecutor's office specialising in narcomenudeo, which
will investigate and determine his legal situation. [@1] He is presumed innocent
unless a court declares his responsibility. [@1]
