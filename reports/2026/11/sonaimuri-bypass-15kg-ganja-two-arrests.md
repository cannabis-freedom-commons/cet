A joint operation by the Department of Narcotics Control (DNC) and SRB-11 in
Begumganj and Sonaimuri of Noakhali arrested two alleged drug traders. [@1] The
two men were arrested in separate raids on the afternoon of Sunday 15 November
2026. [@1] The arrested men were identified as 53-year-old Nurul Alam of
Naikhongchhari in Bandarban and 28-year-old Md Noman Hossain of Faridganj in
Chandpur. [@1] Nurul Alam was arrested with yaba at the Chowmuhani Chowrasta
area, while Noman Hossain was arrested with ganja at the Sonaimuri Bypass area.
[@1]

The joint drive seized a total of 3,950 yaba pills and 15 kg of ganja. [@1]
Officers also seized a button mobile phone at the time of the arrests. [@1]
Preliminary questioning indicated that Nurul Alam collected drugs from Cox's
Bazar and Naikhongchhari and was involved in trafficking them to different parts
of the country. [@1]

Authorities are taking legal action against both men under the relevant sections
of the Narcotics Control Act, 2018. [@1]
