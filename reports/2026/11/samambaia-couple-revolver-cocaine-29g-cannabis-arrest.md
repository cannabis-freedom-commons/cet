Military police arrested a man and a woman in Samambaia, Distrito Federal, on
suspicion of drug trafficking and illegal possession of a firearm. [@1] The
operation began after the team received information about a man firing shots in
the area. [@1] Police went to the location and stopped a man matching the
description near the indicated residence. [@1] The incident was recorded on
Friday the 13th during an operation by military police of the GTAM group of the
Rotam battalion. [@1]

After obtaining authorisation to enter the property, officers found a
.38-calibre revolver, three fired cartridges and four live rounds of the same
calibre, plus ten .22-calibre rounds. [@1] Officers also seized 142 grams of
cocaine, 29 grams of marijuana, one ecstasy tablet and 148 grams of a substance
police said was used to mix with drugs. [@1] Three precision scales and a press
used to prepare drugs were also found. [@1] Police said the inspected house held
different types of drugs and equipment used to prepare them. [@1]

Faced with the seized material, the man and the woman were arrested and taken to
the 26th Police Station. [@1]
