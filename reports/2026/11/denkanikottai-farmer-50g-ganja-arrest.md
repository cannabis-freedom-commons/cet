Sub-inspector Mohan and officers of the Denkanikottai police in Krishnagiri
district, Tamil Nadu, were on patrol near Thippasandram lake when they
questioned a man standing there suspiciously. [@1] They identified him as
Muthukrishnan, a 46-year-old farmer from the same area, and found that he had
been selling ganja. [@1] The officers arrested him and seized 50 grams of ganja
from him. [@1]

Dinamalar reported the same patrol and arrest, saying the officers went to
Thippasandram lake the day before its 27 November report, which places the stop
on 25 November. [@2] It gave the farmer's age as 38 and said he was trying to
sell ganja when police stopped him. [@2] Both reports put the quantity seized at
50 grams. [@2]
