In the department of Putumayo, officers found ammunition and narcotic substances
during patrol, registration and control work in the El Trébol sector on the
Variante road of Puerto Asís. [@2] The operation was carried out by the National
Police in coordination with troops of the National Army. [@1] During the
procedure a man noticed the authorities, fled and abandoned a bag he was
carrying. [@1]

Officers seized 29 cartridges of calibre 5.56 mm. [@1] They also seized 593
grams of marijuana. [@1] A third item was an assault bag that was army supply
material. [@1] The seized elements were valued at approximately 1,150,000 pesos.
[@3]

The material was left at the disposal of the Fiscalía 12 Local URI for the
corresponding judicial procedures. [@1]
