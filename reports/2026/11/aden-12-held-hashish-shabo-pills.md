Aden's anti-drug police and National Security Forces, led by Colonel Mayas
Al-Ja'dani, arrested 12 suspects caught in the act in several districts of the
capital on 26 November 2026. [@1] They were held in cases of possession,
trafficking and use of prohibited substances including hashish, shabo and pills.
[@2]

Colonel Mayas Haydara Al-Ja'dani, the head of the anti-drug administration, said
the operations extended a comprehensive security plan to dry up drug sources and
catch dealers. [@1] His team carried out a series of raids and ambushes that led
to the arrests, and he said the work would continue. [@2] Al-Ja'dani said
growing public awareness and residents' urgent reports about drug dens and
suspicious sites were decisive in locating and catching the suspects. [@3] He
praised the role of citizens, saying the success came from close cooperation
between the community, the security forces and the judiciary. [@2]

All the cases were handed to the Security and Investigation Prosecution so that
the files could be handled under the law and the suspects referred to the
competent authorities. [@1]
