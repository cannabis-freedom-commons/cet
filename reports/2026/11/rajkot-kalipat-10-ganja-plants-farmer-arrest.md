Rajkot's Anti-Narcotics Wing arrested farmer Teja Govind Sakariya in a raid on
an orchard beside Kalipat village, on Vadali Road, where ganja was growing among
a sugarcane crop. [@1] The team acted on a tip that a man named Teja Sakariya
had planted ganja in Govindbhai's orchard near Kalipat. [@1] Police seized 10
green ganja plants weighing 9.690 kg and material valued at Rs 4,94,620, and
began legal action against the farmer. [@1]

Police said the farmer was himself addicted to marijuana and had raised the crop
about a month and a half earlier for his own use and for sale. [@1] During
questioning he said he had planted the ganja among his sugarcane crop about a
month and a half earlier and sold it to others. [@1] Police are investigating
whether he had grown ganja before and to whom else he sold it. [@1]

Rajkot police had been arresting drug and ganja suspects for three days running,
which both accounts link to an instruction from the Union home minister. [@1]
