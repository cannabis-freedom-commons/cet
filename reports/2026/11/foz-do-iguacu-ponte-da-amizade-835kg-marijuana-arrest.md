Federal revenue officers stopped a Paraguayan-plated car at the Friendship
Bridge customs in Foz do Iguacu at about 5 a.m. on 16 November 2026. [@1] The
Paraguayan driver was arrested in flagrante after the drugs were found. [@1] The
driver told the officers that he had previously been involved in drug
trafficking. [@1]

Officers seized a total of 835.1 kilograms of marijuana-like substances. [@1]
The load comprised 832.9 kilograms of pressed marijuana-like substance and 2.2
kilograms of capulho-like cannabis plant material. [@2] The drugs were stored in
the trunk and around the front and rear seats of the car. [@1]

The driver, the vehicle and all the seized drugs were taken to the Federal
Police. [@1] The case was passed to the Federal Police, which is responsible for
continuing the legal proceedings. [@1]
