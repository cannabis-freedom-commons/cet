On 14 November 2026 police stopped three car drivers in the city of Salzburg and
in Golling after officers noted signs of impairment. [@1] Drug tests in all
three cases reacted positively to cannabis and doctors subsequently found the
drivers unfit to drive. [@1]

At about 14:30 on 14 November police stopped a 24-year-old Salzburg car driver
in Johannes-Brunauer-Straße in Salzburg city, and the official doctor found him
unfit to drive after the positive cannabis test. [@1] On the afternoon of 14
November police noticed a conspicuously driven car on the A10 Tauern motorway in
Golling and stopped its 24-year-old driver from Pongau, whose cannabis test was
positive and who was found unfit to drive by a doctor. [@1] That evening in
Müllner Hauptstraße in Salzburg city police stopped a 19-year-old Salzburg
probationary-licence holder whose car stood out for excessively loud music. [@1]
The 19-year-old's alcohol test was negative but his drug test reacted positively
to cannabis, and the official doctor found him unfit to drive in the clinical
examination. [@2]

All three drivers had their driving licences seized on the spot and were
forbidden from continuing their journeys. [@1] All three were reported to the
competent authorities for the violations found. [@2]
