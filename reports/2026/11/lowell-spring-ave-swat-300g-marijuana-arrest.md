Lowell police detectives and members of the Northeastern Massachusetts Law
Enforcement Council SWAT team executed court-authorized search warrants at
Joseph Luna's apartment at 50 Spring Ave. [@1] Officers approached the apartment
shortly after 6 a.m. and announced the warrant before entering. [@1] Luna, his
significant other and two small children were inside the apartment when the
warrant was executed. [@1] The predawn operation at the Lowell apartment on
Tuesday ended with an arrest on a drug trafficking charge. [@1]

During the search, police allegedly found a zippered container hidden on top of
kitchen cabinets containing 88.5 grams of crack cocaine and 16.8 grams of
cocaine. [@1] Investigators also allegedly recovered a digital scale from a
kitchen drawer and approximately 300 grams of marijuana stored in five mason
jars inside a bedroom closet. [@1] Luna denied having any cocaine inside the
home and told investigators he only had a pound of marijuana in the residence.
[@1] Police said the quantity of drugs, the digital scale and the absence of
drug-use paraphernalia led investigators to conclude the narcotics were intended
for distribution rather than personal use. [@1] A search of Luna's pickup truck,
which was also included in the warrant, yielded no evidence. [@1]

Luna was charged with trafficking in cocaine, 100 grams or more but less than
200 grams, and possession with intent to distribute a Class D drug. [@1] Court
documents state the cocaine trafficking charge carries a minimum of eight years
and a maximum of 20 years in prison. [@1] He was arraigned in Lowell District
Court before Judge Mary Phillips, who ordered him held on $5,000 cash bail. [@1]
Court documents show Luna posted bail the same day and is slated to return to
court on Dec. 8. [@1]
