The Policía de Investigaciones carried out four raids on homes in Avellaneda in
a drug-retailing investigation and apprehended three women. [@2] The raids took
place in the Belgrano and Cooperación Norte neighbourhoods. [@2] Those
apprehended were aged 28 and 26, with a third woman identified only by her
initials. [@2] On the orders of prosecutor Sebastián Galleano, the three women
were transferred and placed at the disposal of the justice system. [@2]

Investigators seized 26.2 grams of marijuana alongside 75.4 grams of cocaine and
253,320 pesos in cash. [@2] They also seized five mobile phones, two tablets,
two digital scales, nylon cuttings, scissors and a Honda Tornado XR 250
motorcycle. [@2] In the Belgrano search, officers found about 24.6 grams of
cannabis in 24 wrappers inside a backpack with 202,400 pesos, scissors and nylon
cuttings, plus more substances and a precision scale elsewhere in the same
property. [@1] At another Avellaneda address they reported about 75 grams of
cocaine with a scale, cash, phones and a motorcycle, while the flat linked to
the 26-year-old yielded only electronic devices and no drugs. [@1]

The case grew out of criminal-analysis work by the Fiscalia General
micro-trafficking team under the prosecution service's persecution plan. [@2]
Prosecutors are testing whether retail selling resumed through the entourage of
Sandra Viviana Gómez, known as Kika, who has been detained since a charging
hearing on 20 August. [@1]
