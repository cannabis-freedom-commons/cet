Papua Police are holding 16 people, 14 Indonesians and two Papua New Guinean
nationals, over cannabis and liquor seized in Jayapura. [@1] Police said they
included dealers, narcotics couriers and owners of local liquor production
sites. [@1] The evidence was destroyed at the Ditpolairud pier in Jayapura on
the afternoon of 26 November 2026. [@1]

Officers destroyed 31,036.85 grams (about 31 kg) of ganja, 3.3891 grams of
methamphetamine and 650 litres of local liquor. [@1] The ganja came from 13
hotspots in Jayapura City and Jayapura Regency. [@1] Papua officials joined a
national destruction ceremony held online by the criminal investigation unit,
Bareskrim, before destroying the evidence in Jayapura. [@3]

Operasi Sarang Narkoba and Operasi Minuman Lokal had run since 9 October 2026,
targeting drug-dealing hotspots and home liquor production. [@3] The operation
was part of the national police chief's 100-day work programme. [@3] Deputy
Papua police chief Brigadier General Muhajir said enforcement could not rely on
police action alone and needed public involvement. [@2] Papua Police said it
would keep working with the National Narcotics Agency (BNN) on prevention and
public information. [@1]
