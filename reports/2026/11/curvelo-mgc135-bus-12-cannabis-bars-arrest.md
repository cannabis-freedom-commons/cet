Military Highway Police officers arrested a man in flagrante on a bus at km 634
of the MGC-135 in Curvelo, in central Minas Gerais. [@1] Officers stopped the
bus at about 9h on Sunday 22 November during road patrol and inspected the
vehicle and passengers' luggage. [@1] One suspect was identified as responsible
for the material, detained in flagrante and taken to the Civil Police station in
Curvelo with the seized drugs. [@1] The Highway Police did not publish the man's
name or age. [@1]

Officers found a total of 1,000 wraps of cocaine and 12 bars of maconha in
suspect suitcases. [@1] The police put the estimated loss to drug trafficking
from removing both substances at about R$206,000. [@1]

The bus had left São Paulo and was travelling toward Bahia when it was stopped,
on a highway used for travel between states and also designated BR-135. [@1]
Police concentrated the check on bags considered suspicious and found the wraps
and bars inside them. [@1] After the occurrence was recorded, the case was
passed to the authorities responsible for investigating the facts. [@1]
