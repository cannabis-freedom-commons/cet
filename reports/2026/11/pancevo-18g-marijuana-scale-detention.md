Police in Pančevo arrested I. A., 27, on suspicion of the unauthorised
production and sale of narcotic drugs, on the order of the Higher Public
Prosecutor's Office in Pančevo. [@1] A criminal complaint was filed in the
regular procedure against his fellow townsman V. K., 27, on suspicion of
unauthorised possession of narcotic drugs. [@1] I. A. was held for up to 48
hours and was to be brought to the competent prosecutor with a criminal
complaint. [@2] The prosecutor then opened an investigation against both men,
born in 1999, over the production and sale of drugs and over possession. [@3]
The Higher Court in Pančevo ordered 30-day detention for the man accused of
selling, on the prosecutor's proposal, over the risk of influencing witnesses
and of repeating the offence. [@3]

Criminal police searched the flat and other premises I. A. used and found about
18 grams of plant material suspected to be marijuana. [@1] They also found a
precision scale with traces of the material. [@1] At V. K.'s they found about
1.2 grams of plant material resembling marijuana. [@1] The prosecutor's account
says the seller held 17 grams of marijuana and a digital scale in his flat on 25
November for sale, and sold one gram to the other suspect. [@3] The other
suspect is accused of holding the purchased drug for his own use. [@3] The
investigation also covers an allegation that the seller held 485 grams of
marijuana and 575 grams of amphetamine in a parked car in Pančevo on 13
February, also for sale. [@3]
