The Clean Green programme was under way in the Nandapur block, with police and
excise staff destroying large numbers of ganja plants over the previous four
days. [@1] Acting on information about illegal ganja cultivation in the forest
area near Putambo village in the Chatua gram panchayat, excise and police
officials jointly raided the spot on Saturday. [@1]

The teams destroyed 12,500 ganja plants spread over 8 acres, about 3.24
hectares, of forest land near Putambo. [@1] The operation was led by Padwa
station officer Thunna Pidika, with additional tehsildar Madhav Pangi and excise
staff taking part. [@1]

The account was published on 14 November 2026, the same Saturday as the reported
raid. [@1]
