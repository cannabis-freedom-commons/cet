Security forces at Zefta centre in Gharbia Governorate arrested two people on
suspicion of drug trafficking during a security campaign targeting the Kafr Anan
area. [@1] The two were identified only as A. A. and his partner M. S., known as
Adud, suspected of trafficking and promoting drugs in Kafr Anan under Zefta
centre. [@1]

Police acted on the tip, carried out inquiries into the suspects' activity and
completed legal procedures before launching the campaign to arrest them. [@1]
The campaign seized a quantity of hashish from the pair, along with cash and two
mobile phones. [@1] Officers also seized a tok-tok vehicle suspected of being
used to facilitate their movements and drug promotion. [@1]
