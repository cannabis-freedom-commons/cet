A 26-year-old resident of Zoppola, identified only as C.N., was finally
convicted of drug dealing and ordered to serve the remainder of his sentence
under house arrest. [@1] On the morning of 14 November 2026, Carabinieri of the
Fiume Veneto station executed the substitute home-detention order issued by the
judicial authority. [@1] After an abbreviated trial, the
preliminary-investigations judge of the Pordenone court sentenced him to 2 years
and 2 months in prison plus a 3,334 euro fine for possession of drugs for
dealing. [@1] The sentence became final on 14 October 2026. [@1] After deducting
time already spent in pre-trial custody, the Udine surveillance judge set the
remaining term of 1 year, 2 months and 10 days to be served under house arrest.
[@1]

The investigation began on 27 February 2026 in Via Nazionale in Zoppola, near a
fuel distributor. [@1] Officers had intercepted a suspicious shipment and
stopped the young man immediately after he collected a parcel from an automated
locker. [@1] Inside the parcel were three cellophane wraps containing about 905
grams of marijuana. [@1] He tried to flee on foot into nearby fields, but the
Carabinieri immediately caught and stopped him. [@1] Officers then searched his
car and home and found another 53 grams of hashish hidden in his bedroom in two
modified tins with screw-on false bottoms, together with precision scales and
packaging material. [@1] Analysis by the drug laboratory of the Pordenone
investigative unit confirmed the drugs' high active-drug content. [@1] Police
estimated that the seized quantity could have yielded more than 2,200 marijuana
doses and over 320 hashish doses. [@1]

The Fiume Veneto Carabinieri, responsible for supervision, withdrew his travel
documents and accompanied him home. [@1] He must remain available for checks,
may not associate with people with criminal records, and may leave home for at
most four hours per day in agreed time slots and only for essential needs. [@1]
