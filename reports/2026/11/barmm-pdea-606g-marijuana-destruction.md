The Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency's Bangsamoro office (PDEA-BARMM) and
partner agencies destroyed more than P51 million worth of illegal drugs and
expired medicines in Maguindanao del Norte on 18 November 2026. [@1] PDEA-BARMM
director George Paul Alcovindas said the drugs were destroyed at the compound of
private corn miller Lamsan, Inc. in Barangay Simuay, Sultan Kudarat town. [@1]

The destroyed items included 7,443 grams of methamphetamine hydrochloride
(shabu) and 605.8562 grams of marijuana, with a combined estimated value of
P51,202,784.0040. [@1] Expired medicines valued at P515,987.38 were also
destroyed. [@1] The seized drugs and medicines underwent thermal destruction
using Lamsan's 5.5-megawatt boiler to ensure their complete destruction and
proper disposal. [@1]

Presiding Judge Bai Ashrafia Aymee Biruar of the Regional Trial Court Branch 14
in Cotabato City served as the event's keynote speaker. [@1] The activity was
witnessed by representatives from the Cotabato City and Maguindanao del Norte
prosecutor's offices, the National Intelligence Coordinating Agency, the Public
Attorney's Office, the Philippine National Police–Police Regional Office
Bangsamoro Autonomous Region, and the PNP Regional Forensic Unit-BAR. [@1]
Alcovindas said the participation of the agencies and local officials
highlighted inter-agency cooperation and independent witnessing in upholding
transparency and accountability in the proper disposition of seized dangerous
drugs. [@1]
