Coordinated Command police detained 21-year-old Gonzalo N., known as “El Chalo,”
in the community of Villa Juárez in the municipality of Asientos after finding
three packets of suspected marijuana in his possession. [@1] The suspect, who
identified himself as Gonzalo N., was 21 years old and was detained after
officers found the presumed drug among his belongings. [@1]

The seizure occurred on Olmos street, where uniformed officers were carrying out
surveillance duties when they detected a man who adopted an evasive attitude on
noticing the police presence. [@1] Because of that behaviour, officers decided
to intercept the man and subject him to a preventive review, during which they
found three plastic wrappers containing green plant material with
characteristics similar to marijuana. [@1] The three wrappers together held
approximately 60 grams of green herb, apparently marijuana. [@1]

The young man and the seized wrappers were then placed at the disposal of the
Public Ministry agent of the Attorney General’s Office of Aguascalientes, the
authority charged with determining his legal situation. [@1]
