Military police from Igaratinga arrested two men aged 20 and 24 and apprehended
a 16-year-old adolescent during an anti-drug-trafficking operation on the night
of Thursday 12 November 2026 in São Gonçalo do Pará. [@1] The action took place
at a residence on Rua Bonfim in the Residencial Luís Nogueira neighbourhood,
where police said drug sales had been moved to another house on the same street
and the occupants used cameras to watch for patrol cars. [@1] When officers
arrived, the 20-year-old suspect tried to flee across the roof of a neighbouring
house, discarding drugs and a mobile phone during the escape, but was caught,
while the other two were also detained. [@1]

Searches of the residence and its surroundings found a .22-calibre revolver
loaded with seven intact rounds, one bar and nine tablets of maconha plus three
other larger portions of the drug. [@1] Officers also seized a considerable
portion of cocaine, 60 paper wraps and 56 pins of the same drug, 15 crack
stones, a portion of hashish and portions of skunk. [@1] Police collected a
precision scale, three mobile phones, R$500 in cash, a 10-dollar note and 35
euros. [@1]

The 24-year-old was arrested for drug trafficking, illegal possession of a
firearm and corruption of minors, the 20-year-old for association for
trafficking, and the adolescent for acts analogous to trafficking and
association for trafficking. [@1] The adolescent was accompanied by a Child
Protection Council representative, and all three were taken with the drugs, the
gun and the other seized materials to the Pará de Minas Civil Police station.
[@1] A second account confirms that two men aged 20 and 24 were arrested and a
16-year-old apprehended on the night of Thursday 12 November in Residencial Luís
Nogueira in São Gonçalo do Pará, with a .22 revolver, maconha, cocaine, crack,
hashish and skunk seized alongside phones, a scale and money in reais, dollars
and euros. [@2]
