Four men were due to make first appearances before Warrington Magistrates' Court
on Tuesday November 17 over drug supply allegations linked to Widnes. [@1] Tyler
Peel, aged 24 and of Ridsdale in Widnes, was charged with being concerned in the
supply of cocaine and possession with intent to supply cannabis. [@1] The
cocaine allegation against Peel covers supplying cocaine in Widnes over a week
in September 2025. [@1] His cannabis allegation covers possessing cannabis with
intent to supply in Widnes between September and December 2025. [@1]

Benjamin Ankrah, aged 43 and of Charlton Close in Runcorn, was charged with
possession with intent to supply cannabis and possessing criminal property. [@1]
Both charges against Ankrah were allegedly committed in Widnes on March 24,
2025, with the criminal property said to be £2,205.38 in cash. [@1] Elliot
Hughes, aged 19 and of Delamere Avenue in Widnes, was charged with possession
with intent to supply cannabis. [@1] Hughes allegedly possessed cannabis with
intent to supply in Widnes on December 19, 2025. [@1]

Malcolm Beaulieu, aged 28 and of Tedder Square in Widnes, was charged with
possession with intent to supply cannabis and possession of amphetamine. [@1]
Both charges against Beaulieu were allegedly committed in Widnes on January 3
this year. [@1] The four cases followed separate police investigations into
illegal substance supply in Widnes. [@1]
