Neighbourhood officers arrested two people during early-morning warrants at
three addresses in Caerphilly county on Wednesday 25 November. [@1] Gwent Police
officers arrested a 29-year-old man and a 22-year-old woman, both from
Caerphilly county. [@2] The 29-year-old man and 22-year-old woman, both from
Caerphilly county, were arrested on suspicion of possession with intent to
supply a controlled drug of class A (cocaine), being concerned in the supply of
a class B drug, and acquiring, using or possessing criminal property. [@1] The
arrests took place at an address on Hilary Road, Pantside. [@1] Officers
recovered mobile phones, a large quantity of cash and three cars at that
address. [@1] Both remained in police custody as enquiries continued. [@1]

At a unit at Bowen Industrial Estate, Aberbargoed, officers seized a quantity of
suspected cocaine, a quantity of suspected cannabis, weighing scales, drug
paraphernalia, a van and a quad bike. [@1] A warrant was also carried out at an
address on Birch Crescent, Cefn Hengoed. [@1]

A Gwent Police spokesperson later said the 29-year-old man and 22-year-old woman
had been released on conditional bail as the investigation continued. [@3]
Sergeant Tom Brookes, of Gwent Police’s Caerphilly North neighbourhood team,
said community information played a key role in the operation. [@2] Brookes
said: “We hope this morning’s simultaneous warrants show that input from our
community really does make a difference, so please continue to report your
concerns and information to us.” [@1]
