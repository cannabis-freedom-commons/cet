A Bloomington man was detained on weapons and cannabis charges after police said
he ran from a traffic stop in October. [@1] The detained man was Tyshon L.
Fanning. [@2] Officers pulled Fanning over on 25 October 2025 while he was
driving through Normal as the sole occupant. [@2] He ran south across Pine
Street and was chased by multiple officers. [@2] Officers later found an uncased
loaded 9 mm pistol under the driver's seat that had been reported stolen in
September 2024. [@2] Alongside the pistol, officers found 61.5 grams of cannabis
in plastic baggies. [@2]

Fanning pleaded guilty to possession of a stolen firearm and was sentenced to
six years in prison by Judge Jason Chambers. [@2] He also pleaded guilty to
obstructing a peace officer and received one year, with both terms to be served
concurrently. [@2] He received credit for 278 days already served. [@2]

A Bloomington resident had reported the pistol stolen in September 2024. [@2]
Fanning accepted the plea and was sentenced on 3 November 2026. [@2]
