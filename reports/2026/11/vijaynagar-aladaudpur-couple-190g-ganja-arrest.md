Department of Narcotics Control officers arrested a husband and wife in an
anti-drug operation in Vijaynagar that recovered yaba, ganja and cash. [@1] The
operation took place in the Aladaudpur area of Chandura Union in Vijaynagar
Upazila on Monday 23 November 2026. [@1] Those held were Harun Mia, 50, son of
the late Suruj Ali of Aladaudpur village, and his wife Firoza Begum, 45. [@1]

Acting on a secret tip, an operational team of the department's Brahmanbaria
district office searched Harun Mia's two dwelling houses under his own
possession in Aladaudpur. [@1] Inside the two houses officers found 337 yaba
tablets, 66 grams of yaba powder, 190 grams of ganja and Tk 151,800 in cash.
[@1] Both Harun Mia and his wife Firoza Begum were arrested at the scene after
the recovery. [@1]

Sub-Inspector Foyez Ahammad of the department filed a regular case against the
two at Vijaynagar police station. [@1] Deputy Director Sajedul Hasan of the
department's Brahmanbaria office confirmed the anti-drug operation. [@1]
