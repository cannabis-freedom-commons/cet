A prison guard at Villeneuve-lès-Maguelone grew suspicious after hearing
conversation coming from a cell occupied by a single inmate, according to the
UFAP UNSa Justice union. [@1] Watching through the cell peephole, the guard saw
the inmate using a phone while talking to the occupant of the neighbouring cell
and, the union said, guiding a drone carrying prohibited items. [@1] The guard
kept watching until the delivery arrived, then disclosed his presence and
ordered the inmate to put the objects on a table and move to the back of the
cell. [@1]

Management was then alerted and staff opened the cell and seized the products
and objects. [@1] The union listed 50 grams of cannabis resin among the items
seized, alongside 10 grams of cocaine, a bottle of water containing alcohol, an
Apple phone with an Orange SIM and two packets of cigarettes. [@1] The discovery
was in the prison's Structure d'accompagnement vers la sortie (SAS), a
pre-release support unit, and the union said the drone delivery was intercepted
during the night; its communiqué is dated Tuesday 17 November. [@1]

The union said it had warned for months about trafficking, evolving methods of
introducing prohibited goods and security flaws at the institution. [@1] It
asked that the SAS be given appropriate technological equipment to prevent
illicit deliveries, as the main prison has. [@1]
