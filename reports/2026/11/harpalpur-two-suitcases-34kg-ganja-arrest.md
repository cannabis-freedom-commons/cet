Police in Harpalpur, in Madhya Pradesh's Chhatarpur district, seized about 34
kilograms of ganja that was being carried in two suitcases. [@1,2,3] According to SHO Nitin Sharma, 10 packets weighed 1 kg each while the remaining 10 weighed 2 kg each. [@2] Police recovered two suitcases containing 20 packets of cannabis. [@2] Officers
arrested a 19-year-old youth at the scene in connection with the two-suitcase
consignment. [@1,2] The action was taken as part of an ongoing campaign against drug smuggling in Madhya Pradesh under the directions of Superintendent of Police Rajat Saklecha. [@2] Acting on a tip-off that three people carrying the consignment would arrive on Rath Road, a team led by Station House Officer Nitin Sharma and Kakunpura Outpost in-charge Sub-Inspector Girjesh Raja Bundela set up a blockade near the Rath Road railway crossing. [@2] Two other youths who were accompanying him evaded the police
and fled from the spot. [@1,2] Police are searching for the two absconding accused. [@2] The two men who escaped were identified as Kuldeep and Shani Rai. [@2]

The arrested youth is from Orchha. [@1,2] The arrested youth was identified as Shivam Ahirwar (19), son of Dharmendra Ahirwar and a resident of Orchha in Niwari district. [@2,3] Shivam Ahirwar's address was given as Ward No. 10, Orchha in Niwari district. [@3] According to the police account, the
group brought the ganja from Odisha by train. [@1,2] Police are examining the phone's contents to identify other people linked to the network. [@2] Police also seized the mobile phone of the arrested accused. [@2] Police suspect that a larger interstate cannabis smuggling network is involved in the illegal trade. [@2] The consignment was allegedly meant for further supply to Uttar Pradesh through Madhya Pradesh. [@2] Police estimated the value of
the seized ganja at about Rs 10,20,000, described in the headline as ganja worth
about Rs 10 lakh. [@1,2] A case was registered under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act. [@2,3] The arrested accused was produced before the court. [@2,3]

The seizure links an Odisha-origin train supply route to a suitcase consignment
intercepted at Harpalpur, with one carrier arrested and two companions fleeing
the scene. [@1]
