Police in Harpalpur, in Madhya Pradesh's Chhatarpur district, seized about 34
kilograms of ganja that was being carried in two suitcases. [@1] Officers
arrested a 19-year-old youth at the scene in connection with the two-suitcase
consignment. [@1] Two other youths who were accompanying him evaded the police
and fled from the spot. [@1]

The arrested youth is from Orchha. [@1] According to the police account, the
group brought the ganja from Odisha by train. [@1] Police estimated the value of
the seized ganja at about Rs 10,20,000, described in the headline as ganja worth
about Rs 10 lakh. [@1]

The seizure links an Odisha-origin train supply route to a suitcase consignment
intercepted at Harpalpur, with one carrier arrested and two companions fleeing
the scene. [@1]
