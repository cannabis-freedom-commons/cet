The Suceava Court of Appeal gave a final ruling in a DIICOT case over drug
trafficking and possession in which six young people aged 21 to 27 were tried.
[@1] None of the six will serve a prison sentence under the ruling. [@1] The
appeal court partly changed the Suceava Tribunal's sentence of 6 October 2023 in
a decision dated 3 November 2026. [@1]

Andrei-Marcel Gavreliuc was finally sentenced to two years and six months in
prison, suspended, for continued trafficking in risk drugs. [@1]
Alexandru-George Păstrăv, first sentenced to four years and two months in
prison, received three years suspended on appeal, with three years' supervision.
[@1] Păstrăv must also complete 100 days of unpaid community work for the
Suceava city hall or the county social services directorate. [@1]
Alexandru-Iulian B. kept a one-year suspended sentence for possessing risk and
high-risk drugs for his own use, with two years' supervision. [@1]
Iulian-Cristian R. kept six months in prison, suspended, for the same possession
offence, with two years' supervision. [@1] Claudiu-Mihăiță P. and
Alexandru-Ștefan P. had six-month prison sentences for cannabis possession for
personal use deferred, with two years' supervision. [@1]

Investigators documented Gavreliuc's activity in cultivating and selling
cannabis. [@1] Prosecutors said part of the drugs Păstrăv bought in Suceava were
to be sold to people at the Beach, Please! festival in Costinești and part given
to acquaintances to consume. [@1] On 19 October 2022 anti-drug police searched
two Suceava properties Gavreliuc used and found an indoor cannabis grow in one
of them, on Narciselor street. [@1] The two searches turned up 839.07 grams of
cannabis and many items used to grow, process and pack the drug. [@1] Among the
items were three grow tents, lighting and ventilation systems, timers, pots,
nutrients, electronic scales, grinders and 87 self-sealing bags. [@1]

DIICOT argued that Gavreliuc's three-year suspended sentence was too lenient and
asked for a longer term to be served in prison. [@1] The appeal court held that
the two documented sales involved small quantities and that the 839.07 grams
found in the searches had never been sold. [@1] The appeal hearing was held on
12 June 2024 but the ruling was repeatedly postponed until 3 November 2026. [@1]
The case number is 319/86/2023. [@1]
