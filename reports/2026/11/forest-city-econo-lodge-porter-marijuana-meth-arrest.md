Rutherford County sheriff's deputies were carrying out a saturation patrol at
the Econo Lodge motel in Forest City on Saturday 21 November 2026 when they
developed probable cause to suspect drug activity or sales in one of the rooms.
[@1] Deputies obtained a search warrant for the room and identified its occupant
as Archie Orlando Porter. [@1] Porter was arrested and taken to the Rutherford
County Detention Center. [@1] The sheriff's office said he was held without bond
because he was already out on pretrial release for previous charges. [@1]

Deputies reported finding 6.9 grams of marijuana in the room. [@1] They also
reported 17.98 grams of methamphetamine, which is not counted in the cannabis
figures. [@1] Officers found drug paraphernalia including a digital scale and
glass smoking pipes, and seized $1,118 in cash. [@1]

Porter, 45, faces five new drug-related charges in Rutherford County case
26CR458494-800, court records show. [@2] The charges are possession with intent
to manufacture, sell or deliver Schedule II and Schedule VI controlled
substances, felony maintaining a place for controlled substances, and possession
of drug and marijuana paraphernalia. [@2] It is his third pending Rutherford
County drug case filed since 30 October 2026. [@2] Porter is scheduled to appear
before a District Court judge in Rutherford County on Monday 23 November. [@1]
