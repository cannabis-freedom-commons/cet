A 16-year-old from the Saale-Orla district was driving his Leichtkraftfahrzeug,
a light motor vehicle, on the Hauptstraße in Langenwolschendorf at about 22:30
on 20 November 2026. [@1] Officers of the Greiz police inspection (PI Greiz)
stopped him and carried out a traffic check. [@1] When they spoke to the youth,
officers noticed a strong smell of cannabis in the vehicle. [@1]

The teenager said he had consumed cannabis a few days earlier. [@1] A drug test
reacted positive to the substance. [@1] Officers then took a blood sample and
opened a traffic administrative offence proceeding
(Verkehrsordnungswidrigkeitenverfahren) against him. [@2]

The case is being handled as a regulatory traffic offence rather than a criminal
prosecution. [@1]
