Prohibition and Excise officials apprehended 34-year-old Hatgade Karan at
Shivalal Nagar in Hyderabad on Monday 23 November 2026 while he was allegedly
selling ganja from an autorickshaw. [@1] Karan was arrested and produced before
a court. [@1] Excise Circle Inspector Praveen Kumar said cases had also been
registered against Karan's family members. [@2] Officials gave no number, roles
or specific charges for those family members. [@2]

The team seized 1.23 kg of ganja and the autorickshaw allegedly used to
transport and sell it. [@1] The seizure took place in Shivla Nagar within the
Dhoolpet Excise Police Station limits. [@2] The operation was led by Excise
Circle Inspectors Praveen Kumar and Viplav Reddy. [@2]

Sub-Inspectors Venkateswara Reddy and Mogal Appa and other staff took part in
the operation. [@2] Officials said the investigation would establish whether the
alleged activity was limited to Karan or involved other people. [@2]
