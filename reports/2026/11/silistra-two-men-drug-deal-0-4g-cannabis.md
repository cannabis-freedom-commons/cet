Police in Silistra detained two men aged 47 and 32 during an operation against
drug distribution, catching them in the act of a methamphetamine deal, according
to the regional directorate of the interior ministry. [@1] Detectives detained
the two men at the moment of a deal involving 1.4 grams of methamphetamine. [@1]

After the detention, officers searched the 47-year-old man's flat and found a
further 1.7 grams of methamphetamine, 0.4 grams of cannabis and an electronic
scale. [@1]

Police said both detainees had previous criminal involvement connected with
drugs and that the older man had served a custodial sentence for a similar
offence. [@1] An investigation was opened to clarify all the circumstances of
the suspected illegal drug deal. [@1]
