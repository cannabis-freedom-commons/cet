Investigator Fábio Cristiano Luchetti, 51, of the São Paulo Denarc anti-drug
department was held on suspicion of drug trafficking and said he may have been
framed. [@1] Luchetti was arrested on Monday 16 November with officers Fábio
Luiz Tessare, 53, and Francisco Paulo de Almeida Souza, 46, and all three were
indicted for drug trafficking and association for trafficking. [@1] The three
are investigators of the São Paulo Denarc, including the chief of its 3rd Dise
drug-investigation unit, and were arrested in flagrante on suspicion of
trafficking and criminal association. [@2] A São Paulo court converted the
flagrante arrests into preventive detention on Tuesday 17 November after a
custody hearing. [@3]

The Corregedoria found 11 kg of drugs in three official vehicles used against
traffickers, comprising nine kilograms of marijuana and two of cocaine. [@2]
Searches found drugs in vehicles linked to the investigators, including a van
holding cocaine packets, trays, a hammer, bags and cash. [@1]

The case began after an anonymous report sent through the São Paulo prosecution
service's Gaeco about diversion of cocaine seized by the team. [@1] The
Corregedoria found 2.11 kilograms of cocaine missing from the 3rd Dise safe,
material that had been weighed, photographed and sealed. [@1] Luchetti suggested
the marijuana found in a car under his responsibility could have been placed by
whoever filed the complaint, saying the vehicle was broken down at the
department and not used in operations. [@1] Luchetti denied resisting, while the
Corregedoria said he and Tessare disobeyed orders and pushed another officer.
[@1]
