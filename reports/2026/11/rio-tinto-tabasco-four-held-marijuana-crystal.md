Tabasco's Security and Citizen Protection Ministry (SSPC) detained four people
on suspicion of offences against health during an operation in the municipality
of Centro. [@1] Among those held was Marco Antonio "N", alias "Tintán", "Rai" or
"Raidolito", a man originally from Chiapas state. [@1] The others were Pablo del
Carmen "N" ("El Palomino"), Valeria "N" ("La Vale") and Oliver Antonio "N" ("El
Bacali"). [@3]

Officers intercepted three men and a woman on the Reforma–Villahermosa highway
at the Río Tinto 1.ª sección ranchería. [@1] XEVT reported that the detentions
took place during a security patrol in Río Tinto Primera Sección. [@2] During a
preventive search officers seized various packages resembling marijuana and
methamphetamine known as "cristal", along with backpacks, a mobile phone and
cash. [@1]

After registration in the National Detention Registry (RND), the four people and
the seized items were handed to the relevant prosecution authority to open an
investigation file and determine their legal situation. [@1] The SSPC said the
detainees are presumed innocent and will be treated as such at every stage until
a court rules on their responsibility. [@3]
