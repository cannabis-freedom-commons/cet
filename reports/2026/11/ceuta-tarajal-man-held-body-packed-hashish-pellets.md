Guardia Civil officers detained a man of about 45 on Monday afternoon at the
Tarajal border in Ceuta after discovering he was carrying hashish inside his
body. [@1] The arrest took place as the man was crossing the border post from
Morocco toward the autonomous city. [@1] The case began during a border check
when a Guardia Civil canine-unit dog quickly marked the man, raising the
officers' suspicions. [@1] Officers then told him to step aside for a more
thorough inspection. [@1]

During the first search, officers found several hashish pellets in the man's
pockets. [@1] Suspecting he could be carrying more drugs, officers decided to
transfer him to the University Hospital of Ceuta for an X-ray. [@1] At the
hospital, the X-ray confirmed the officers' suspicions: the man was also
carrying narcotics inside his body. [@1] He remains in custody and must expel
all the drugs he is carrying before being brought before a judge. [@1] He is
under investigation for an alleged offence against public health. [@1]

The report says he was loaded with pellets, having swallowed a near-limit
quantity that endangered his life if any pellet ruptured. [@1] Internal drug
transport carries a serious health risk because a ruptured pellet or wrapper
containing hashish can cause extremely serious, potentially fatal poisoning.
[@1] The previous week, the Guardia Civil had detained 12 people at the port of
Ceuta for carrying hashish inside their bodies. [@1]
