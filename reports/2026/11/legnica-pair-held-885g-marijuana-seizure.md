Officers from the Legnica municipal police detained a 40-year-old woman and a
53-year-old man in Legnica on suspicion of possessing significant quantities of
marijuana, methamphetamine, cocaine and MDMA. [@1] Officers acted after
operational work indicated that the woman and man could hold significant
quantities of intoxicants, going to a previously identified address in Legnica
where they found both suspects. [@2]

Searching their flat and vehicle, officers seized 885 grams of marijuana
together with 58 grams of methamphetamine, 14 grams of cocaine and 315 MDMA
tablets. [@3] The pair also held equipment for portioning the substances,
indicating the drugs were being prepared for further sale. [@1]

After detention in police custody, both were brought to the prosecutor's office,
where they were charged with possessing a significant quantity of drugs and
preparing to introduce a significant quantity of drugs into circulation. [@2]
The prosecutor imposed police supervision and financial surety on the suspects,
who face up to 10 years' imprisonment. [@1] Police said the seized substances
would not reach potential buyers. [@3]
