Civil Police in Bombinhas arrested a 44-year-old man in flagrante on the morning
of Monday 16 November 2026 on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@1] The warrant
was served at a residence in the Zimbros neighbourhood of Bombinhas. [@2] After
the search the man was taken to the Bombinhas police station, booked in
flagrante for drug trafficking and remained at the disposal of the courts. [@3]

Police seized approximately 1 kg of marijuana. [@1] Officers were assisted by
the Itapema municipal guard's dog kennel. [@1] A sniffer dog named Duna
identified where marijuana was hidden inside the house. [@1] The marijuana was
hidden in a bag in the bathroom, in the wardrobe, at the head of the bed and in
the bathroom ceiling. [@1] Officers also seized two precision scales, a notebook
with records of drug sales, a mobile phone and two laptops. [@1]

The arrest came while officers executed a search and seizure warrant issued by
the Vara Regional de Garantias of the Balneário Camboriú district court. [@1]
According to the Civil Police, the warrant followed indications that the
residence might be used for selling drugs. [@1] The operation was led by the
Bombinhas police station with support from the Itapema municipal guard. [@1]
