Officers executed a warrant at a property in Liverpool Street, Luton. [@1] The
Luton Town Centre Community Team carried out the warrant on Friday, November 20,
following reports from the public and intelligence gathered by officers. [@1]
After entering the property, officers discovered what appeared to be a
substantial cannabis cultivation, with six rooms dedicated to growing the
plants. [@1] Officers seized 63 adult-sized cannabis plants. [@2]

Police said cannabis cultivations are often linked to wider criminality and can
pose risks to neighbouring properties and residents. [@1] They warned that
illegal modifications to electrical systems at growing operations can create
serious fire hazards and put lives at risk. [@1] The force credited information
from the public and proactive police work for helping identify and disrupt
criminal activity in the area. [@1]

Enquiries remain ongoing. [@1]
