Military Police officers seized a large quantity of maconha from a forested area
in Resende, Rio de Janeiro state, on Sunday 15 November 2026. [@1] The seizure
took place on Rua Oito in the Jardim Aliança neighbourhood. [@1]

According to the Military Police, officers were searching a location already
known for drug trafficking. [@1] In total, officers seized 190 small packets of
maconha. [@1]

The seized material was taken to the Resende police station, where the case was
registered. [@1] No one had been arrested as of publication. [@1]
