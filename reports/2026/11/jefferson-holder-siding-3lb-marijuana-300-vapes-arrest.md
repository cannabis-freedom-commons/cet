Jackson County Sheriff's Office Special Operations Division deputies executed a
search warrant on Friday at a home on Holder Siding Road in Jefferson. [@1] Four
men from Jefferson were taken into custody following the search. [@1] Terry Dean
Strickland, 24, was charged with two counts of possession of a controlled
substance with intent to distribute, possession of a firearm during the
commission of a crime, tampering with evidence and possession of a firearm by a
convicted felon. [@1] Connor James Pierce, 23, was charged with violation of the
Georgia Controlled Substance Act, party to a crime and tampering with evidence.
[@1] Christopher Thomas Szanto, 25, was charged with party to a crime. [@1]
Bishop Tyler Hassler, 23, was charged with party to a crime. [@1]

Investigators recovered one firearm, about three pounds of marijuana and 30
grams of THC wax. [@1] Deputies also seized 300 THC vaping devices and 225 THC
edibles during the raid. [@1] The raid occurred on November 20, 2026. [@1] The
sheriff's office described the haul as suspected THC products and
narcotics-related evidence recovered at the Holder Siding Road location. [@2]

The Jackson County Sheriff's Office said it appreciates citizen-community
partnerships. [@1]
