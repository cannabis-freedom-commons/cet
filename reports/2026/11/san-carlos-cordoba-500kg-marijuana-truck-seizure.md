A joint operation by army troops of the Gaula Militar Sinú, under tactical
control of the 11th Brigade and with support from the 7th Urban Special Forces
Battalion, together with the Criminal Investigation and Judicial Section of the
National Police, located and seized about 500 kilograms of marijuana on a road
in San Carlos, Córdoba. [@1,3,4] According to the investigation, the cargo allegedly
belonged to the Clan del Golfo. [@1,3,4] The operation made no arrests but did seize
a truck that had been abandoned with packages of the drug. [@1] The immobilized vehicle was an NPR-type truck that authorities said was presumably used to transport the drugs. [@5]

Officers found the vehicle and signalled it to stop, but the driver ignored the
order and fled; minutes later the vehicle was found abandoned several kilometres
further on. [@1,5] Inside, investigators found 497 packages of a narcotic
substance, apparently marijuana, hidden in compartments fitted inside the truck.
[@1,5] The authorities put the value of the packages at about 1,800 million pesos.
[@1,3,4]

The force said the operation directly affected the illegal fundraising and
financing capacity of the Javier Yepes Cantero substructure of the Clan del
Golfo. [@1,3,4] Preliminary inquiries indicated the cargo came from Risaralda
department and was bound for various retail points in Córdoba, where it would be
sold illegally. [@1,5] The police statement placed the operation in vereda
Cantarrana, in the jurisdiction of San Carlos, and said it followed intelligence
work and information from human sources. [@2,5] The police said officers of the
Criminal Investigation Section of the Metropolitan Police of San Jerónimo de
Montería acted in coordination with the GAULA Militar. [@2,5] The police said the
substance was suspected of being one of the main sources of financing for a Clan
del Golfo substructure operating in the region. [@2,5]
