Troops of the Army's Ninth Brigade, working with the National Police, arrested a
man in flagrante who was carrying about 40 kilograms of marijuana on a public
bus. [@1] The check took place at the El Cable military checkpoint in the vereda
El Cedro, in the corregimiento of Bruselas, in rural Pitalito. [@1] During the
search, officers found the drugs hidden among the items the man was carrying.
[@1]

Authorities said the seizure equalled about 80,000 doses taken out of illegal
distribution and sales circuits. [@1] Preliminary information linked the load to
the illicit economies of the residual organized armed group Comandos de
Frontera. [@1] The seizure was estimated to cost that group 60 million pesos by
blocking income for its criminal activities. [@1]

The arrested man and the seized material were handed to the competent
authorities for proceedings for alleged drug trafficking, manufacture or
possession. [@1] The Army said the result helps reduce drug circulation, protect
the population and strengthen controls on strategic road corridors in the south
of the department. [@1] The Ninth Brigade said it would continue
territorial-control and interdiction operations with the National Police and
other authorities against illicit economies in Huila. [@1]
