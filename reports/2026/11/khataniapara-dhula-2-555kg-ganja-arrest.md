Dhula Police arrested a ganja smuggler from Khataniapara village in Darrang
district, Assam. [@1] Acting on a tip-off, a police team raided the dwelling
house of Abbas Ali in Khataniapara village on Saturday and recovered 2.555 kg of
ganja, which was seized on the spot. [@1] The smuggler was arrested under the
NDPS Act and further investigation began. [@1] He was produced before a court
and sent to jail in judicial custody. [@1]

Local residents welcomed the police action, saying the smuggling of ganja and
other intoxicants was increasing in the area and ruining the younger generation.
[@1] Residents said the action had created panic among drug smugglers. [@1]
Darrang police appealed to the public to report any information about drug
trafficking and promised to keep informers' identities secret. [@1]
