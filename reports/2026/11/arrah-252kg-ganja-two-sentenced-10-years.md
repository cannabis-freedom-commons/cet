A court in Arrah sentenced Vikas Kumar and Prakash Kumar to ten years of
rigorous imprisonment each in a ganja smuggling case. [@1] Additional District
and Sessions Judge Aditya Suman also fined each convict one lakh rupees. [@1]
The two convicts are residents of Garhni in the Charpokhari police station area.
[@1]

The case began on 26 September 2024, when excise assistant sub-inspector Ajay
Kumar of the Jagdishpur excise station acted on a secret tip and had a car
searched near Paliya Mor, close to Baligaon. [@1] Officers recovered 252.04
kilograms of ganja from the car. [@1] They arrested the car's driver, Vikas
Kumar, and Prakash Kumar at the scene. [@1]

Three prosecution witnesses testified at the trial. [@1] After the hearing on
sentence, the court convicted both accused under the relevant sections of the
NDPS Act, the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act. [@1] Public
prosecutor Rana Pratap Singh conducted the prosecution, and the court sentenced
both convicts on the evidence and witness statements he presented. [@1]
