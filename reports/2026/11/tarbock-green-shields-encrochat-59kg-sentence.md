Gerard Shields, 45, of Garnetts Lane in Tarbock Green, was sentenced to 11 years
and three months at Liverpool Crown Court on Monday 23 November 2026. [@1] He
lived in Tarbock Green in Knowsley. [@2] He pleaded guilty to conspiracy to
supply cocaine and heroin and to possession with intent to supply a Class B
drug, cannabis. [@1]

Investigators linked Shields to the Encrochat handles 'wasplake' and
'fadedwelder'. [@1] Encrypted messages revealed his involvement in the supply of
around 59 kg of cannabis, 2 kg of heroin and 1 kg of cocaine in 2020. [@1] He
was identified through messages that referred to his home address, family,
birthday and his background as an electrician. [@1] Messages showed Shields
claimed he could source cannabis from Spain and discussed testing the smuggling
network with a dummy run. [@1]

The case formed part of Operation Aquarium, Merseyside Police's response to the
national Operation Venetic, which targets criminals using the Encrochat
platform. [@1] Since 2020, Merseyside Police and partner agencies had secured
convictions against 238 criminals, resulting in more than 3,000 years of
combined jail time. [@1] Detective Inspector Jay Boardman said Shields's
sentencing took the force past the 3,000-year mark for jail terms. [@1] Nearly
£2.9 million in cash had also been seized over the previous six years as part of
these investigations. [@1]
