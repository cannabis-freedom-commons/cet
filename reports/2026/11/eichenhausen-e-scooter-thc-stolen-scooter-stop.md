A patrol of the Neustadt police stopped an 18-year-old Czech woman on an
e-scooter in the Ortsstraße in Eichenhausen at about 06:05 on Thursday 19
November 2026. [@2] Eichenhausen is a village in the municipality of
Wülfershausen an der Saale in the Rhön-Grabfeld district of Lower Franconia.
[@2] According to the police account, the check turned up several offences at
once. [@1] The scooter had no insurance plate. [@1] The woman showed
drug-typical signs of impairment, and a rapid drug test confirmed the suspicion
and reacted positive for THC. [@1]

Further checks showed that the scooter had been stolen in the city of
Schweinfurt in May 2025. [@3] Police seized the vehicle. [@2] The 18-year-old
was released after a blood sample was taken. [@3] She now faces proceedings for
driving under the influence of intoxicants, a particularly serious case of theft
and an offence under the compulsory insurance law. [@3] Bad Neustadt police have
taken up the investigation. [@2]
