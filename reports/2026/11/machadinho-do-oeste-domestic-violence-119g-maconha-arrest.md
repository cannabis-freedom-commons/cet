Military Police in Machadinho do Oeste were called after a 20-year-old woman
reported being assaulted by her 19-year-old partner. [@1] During the response
she told officers that the man and another 18-year-old resident were involved in
selling drugs. [@1] The two suspects were stopped when they returned to the
house. [@1] Both men were arrested on suspicion of drug trafficking, and the
19-year-old was also held over the domestic-violence complaint. [@1] Both were
taken to the UNISP with the seized drugs. [@1]

Searches found portions of marijuana buried in the backyard and cocaine and
crack hidden under the stove. [@1] Officers seized 119.7 grams of marijuana, 7.3
grams of cocaine and 2.9 grams of crack, plus additional fractioned portions.
[@1]

The case began as a domestic-violence call on the morning of Sunday 15 November
in Machadinho do Oeste, Rondônia. [@1] Part of the drugs was buried in the yard
of a residence and another quantity was hidden inside the house. [@1] The two
men held were aged 19 and 18. [@1]
