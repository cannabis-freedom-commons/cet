Criminalists from the Sandanski district police station detained a 27-year-old
local man after a police check in which a narcotic substance was found. [@1] The
check took place on 20 November 2026 at an apartment in the city of Sandanski
occupied by the man. [@2] In one of the rooms the police officers found a paper
envelope containing about 3.09 grams of dry herbaceous mass. [@1] The regional
police bulletin described the flat as occupied by a 27-year-old man from the
city and the envelope as found in one of its rooms. [@3]

A field narcotic test performed at the scene reacted positively to cannabis.
[@1] The police described the material as dry herbaceous mass that tested
positive for cannabis. [@2] After the substance was established, the 27-year-old
was detained under an order for up to 24 hours. [@1]

A pre-trial proceeding was opened in the case. [@3] Work on documenting and
clarifying all the circumstances continued. [@1]
