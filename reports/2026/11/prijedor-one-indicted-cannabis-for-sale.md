A previously convicted Prijedor man identified only by the initials Z.L. was
indicted on drug charges. [@1] The District Public Prosecutor's Office in
Prijedor announced the indictments in this case. [@1] The indictment alleges
that on the night between 17 and 18 December 2022, in the house he used in
Prijedor, he gave cocaine to S.J. and Đ.D. for their use and also held cannabis
there for sale. [@2]

It further alleges that on 18 December 2022 he handed drugs to a man identified
as M.M., who had previously paid for them, and voluntarily handed the remaining
narcotics to officers during a police check. [@1] The announcement notes that
the law provides two to 12 years in prison for enabling drug use by several
persons and one to 10 years for unauthorised production and trafficking of
narcotics. [@2] The two people who allegedly received cocaine for their use were
identified only by their initials S.J. and Đ.D. [@2]
