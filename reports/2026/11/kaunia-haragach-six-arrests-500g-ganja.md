Police in Kaunia upazila of Rangpur arrested six people, one woman and five men,
and recovered ganja from them in a raid. [@1] The raid took place in the Araji
Bircharan area of Haragach police station late on the night of Saturday 21
November, at around 11:30 pm. [@2]

A team from Rangpur Metropolitan Police's Haragach thana, led by Sub-Inspector
Ruhul Amin, carried out the raid on a tip-off. [@1] Officers recovered 500 grams
of ganja from the arrested people, which police estimated to be worth about
15,000 taka on the market. [@2] The six were arrested on the charge of keeping
ganja for sale. [@1]

The arrested were named as Jaheda Khatun, 36, Rashedul Islam, 35, Bellal Mia,
42, Azihar Rahman, 40, Khalek Mia, 42, and Rashidul Islam, 33. [@1] A case was
filed against the six under the Narcotics Control Act, and they were sent to
Rangpur court on Sunday 22 November. [@1] Haragach Metropolitan police station's
officer-in-charge, Ashok Chouhan, said the raid was part of regular operations
to build a drug-free society. [@2]
