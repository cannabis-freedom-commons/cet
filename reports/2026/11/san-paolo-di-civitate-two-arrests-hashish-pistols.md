Carabinieri arrested two people from San Paolo di Civitate in the province of
Foggia for possessing clandestine pistols. [@1] During a search, officers found
a 20-year-old in possession of a loaded 6.35-calibre pistol with an abraded
serial number and a round in the chamber. [@1] Officers also found several grams
of hashish already divided into doses ready for dealing. [@1]

In the same operation, officers arrested a 68-year-old found in possession of
two pistols with abraded serial numbers in calibres 22 and 7.65. [@1] Officers
also seized a total of 241 cartridges held without authorisation and described
as compatible with the weapons found. [@1]

After validation of the arrest, the preliminary investigations judge imposed on
the 20-year-old an obligation to remain in his place of residence and to report
to the judicial police. [@1] The 68-year-old was instead arrested and taken to
prison to await validation by the preliminary investigations judge. [@1]
