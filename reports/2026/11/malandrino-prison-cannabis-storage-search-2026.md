Greece's Subdivision for Combating Organised Crime of Northern Greece carried
out a targeted police operation at the Malandrino prison in Fokida. [@1,2] The
operation took place on Friday 20 November 2026. [@1,3] The raid was carried out at midday. [@5] The searches were carried
out in the presence of a judicial officer. [@1,4]

Three mobile phones were found carefully hidden in three of the searched cells,
and a case file was opened against three Greek prisoners for holding them. [@1]

In a shared storage room in the same wing, officers found and seized 36 grams of
raw cannabis. [@1,5] The same search yielded 25 grams of processed cannabis of the
“chocolate” type. [@1,5] Officers seized 1.5 grams of heroin. [@1] They seized 1.5
grams of cocaine. [@1] Police also took 28 narcotic tablets. [@1] Two improvised
knives were seized as well. [@1] A mobile phone that did not work was taken too.
[@1] In total four mobile phones were found in the operation, one of them non-functional. [@5]

Police opened a case file against unknown perpetrators for violations of the
drug legislation, the weapons legislation and the rule against supplying a
prisoner with a mobile phone. [@1] Possession of the drugs and improvised knives had not been attributed to any of the people searched. [@5] The files were to be submitted to the
competent prosecutor's office. [@1]
