Deoli police station's team acted on illegal ganja cultivation in Deoli taluka,
Wardha district, booking Baliraam Daulat Shivarkar, 55, of Pachgaon
(resettlement). [@1] Officers seized 5.704 kg of wet ganja, which police valued
at 85,560 rupees. [@1] Deoli police registered case no. 880/2026 under sections
20(a) and 8(b) of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act and said
further investigation was under way. [@1]

Wardha district police said they were running a continuing special campaign
against the illegal cultivation, production, sale and trafficking of drugs. [@1]
The operation was carried out under the guidance of district police chief
Saurabh Kumar Agarwal, additional superintendent Sadashiv Badhmare and Pulgaon
subdivision police officer Vandana Karkhele, and led by Deoli station officer
Sandeep Kapade. [@1]

Police asked residents to report illegal ganja cultivation or other drug
activity in their area, and urged parents to watch their children's friends,
changes in behaviour and habits. [@1]
