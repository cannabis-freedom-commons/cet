Military police arrested two men and a woman, and took a 14-year-old girl into
custody, on suspicion of involvement in drug trafficking in Rio do Sul. [@1] The
operation was at a house in the Taboão neighbourhood on the night of Thursday 19
November 2026, after complaints about drug sales there. [@1] RBA TV, citing the
Military Police, placed the action at about 21:00 that Thursday. [@2]

Officers said their suspicions were strengthened after they stopped a driver who
had just left the house and found a portion of marijuana on him. [@1] The man
confirmed that he had bought the drug at the property, and officers then entered
the house. [@1] RBA TV said a tactical team watched the driver enter the garage
and leave shortly afterwards before stopping him. [@2] During the search police
found 310 ecstasy tablets, about 800 grams of marijuana and about 60 grams of
cocaine. [@1] Officers also seized 785 reais in cash, a precision scale, six
mobile phones and notebooks with notes related to drug sales. [@1] According to
the Military Police, one of the women tried to throw some of the drugs out of a
window when she saw the officers. [@1] RBA TV said officers recovered the
material she threw. [@2]

On the lower floor of the house police found a bicycle recorded as stolen. [@1]
Two men and a woman were arrested, and the 14-year-old girl was taken into
custody; all were taken to the Civil Police station in Rio do Sul. [@1] The case
involves suspicions of drug trafficking, association for trafficking, corruption
of minors and receiving stolen goods. [@1] Sintonia FM said the Civil Police
would handle the legal procedures and continue the investigation. [@3]
