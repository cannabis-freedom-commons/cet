Military Police officers in Ituiutaba arrested a man who was travelling in a
ride-hailing car with about 10 kilograms of marijuana. [@1] According to the
Military Police, the drug was being transported from Uberlândia to Ituiutaba.
[@1] A passenger in the car smelled the drug and called the police. [@1]

The Ituiutaba news outlet Canal Janela Aberta reported that the man arrested was
27 years old and was stopped on the morning of Tuesday 17 November 2026. [@2] In
its account the driver of the app car noticed a strong smell of marijuana during
the trip and alerted the Military Police. [@2] Officers set up a siege and
blockade on BR-365 near the Clube Beira Rio, intercepted the vehicle and
arrested the suspect. [@2]

The two accounts differ on who alerted the police: G1 says a passenger in the
car smelled the drug, while Canal Janela Aberta says the driver did. [@1]
