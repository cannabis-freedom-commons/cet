A 22-year-old man ended up in jail accused of possession of drugs for dealing,
while a 55-year-old woman and a 20-year-old youth were reported for the same
offence. [@1] Carabinieri of the operational unit of the Crema company carried
out the operation on the afternoon of 18 November in a village in the Cremasco
area. [@1] The investigation began after reports of suspicious movements near a
house with frequent coming and going, including people from outside the village.
[@1] Officers then organised plainclothes observation and monitored movements in
the area. [@1] During the checks officers stopped several people who had walked
away after brief meetings near the house. [@1]

A first man found with 10.6 grams of hashish was referred to the Prefecture and
had his driving licence withdrawn. [@1] A 20-year-old stopped after another
quick exchange had over 51 grams of hashish with him and was reported. [@1] A
third person found with about 5.5 grams of the same substance was referred to
the Prefecture and had his licence withdrawn. [@1] Posing as couriers, the
officers had the 22-year-old open the door and blocked him after identifying
themselves. [@1] Inside the house and the cellar the officers found and seized a
total of 6 kilograms and 700 grams of hashish, some of it kept in the freezer
drawer, plus 5 grams of cocaine. [@1] The search also recovered two precision
scales, packaging material, cash just under 32,000 euros and ten cartridges of
various calibres for long arms. [@1]

The 22-year-old was arrested for possession of a large quantity of drugs for
dealing and reported for unlawful possession of ammunition. [@1] The 55-year-old
woman, his relative, was instead reported for complicity in possession for
dealing. [@1] The young man was taken to Cremona prison. [@1] On 21 November the
judge validated the arrest and ordered precautionary custody in prison against
him. [@1]
