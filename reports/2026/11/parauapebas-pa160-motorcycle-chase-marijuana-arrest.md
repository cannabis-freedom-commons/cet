Raylan Barbosa Gonçalves was taken to the Civil Police station in Parauapebas on
the afternoon of Friday 20 November 2026, suspected of involvement in drug
trafficking. [@1] Military Police said the incident was recorded during a patrol
by a team of the Grupamento de Prevenção Ativa, an active-prevention unit, on
the PA-160 highway between the Alto Bonito and Popular neighbourhoods. [@1]
Officers saw him riding a Honda CG 150 Fan motorcycle without a licence plate
and moved in to approach him. [@1] Police said he disobeyed orders to stop,
accelerated and fled, ignoring sirens and flashing lights. [@1]

According to the police report, one of the officers saw the rider throw a
package to the ground during the pursuit. [@1] After stopping him, officers
found a mobile phone, a wallet and 100 reais in cash with him. [@1] When they
checked the discarded package, the officers found marijuana. [@1] Questioned
about the material, he said he had picked up the package to deliver it to the
place where he worked. [@1] The motorcycle was also seized; there was no theft
or robbery report against it, but it was being ridden without a plate. [@1]
