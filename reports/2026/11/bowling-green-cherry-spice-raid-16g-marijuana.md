Bowling Green police executed a search warrant at a home in the 400 block of
Wade Avenue in Bowling Green at about 2:11 p.m. on 18 November 2026. [@1]
Detectives seized about 16 grams of marijuana packaged for resale. [@1,3] James
Cherry, 28, of Bowling Green was taken into custody at the home. [@2] He was
taken to the Warren County Regional Jail and later released on bond. [@1] Cherry was released Thursday from Warren County Regional Jail on a $7,500 cash bond, and his case is pending in Warren District Court. [@3] Cherry
was charged with trafficking in synthetic drugs, second or subsequent offence,
and tampering with physical evidence. [@2,3] Officers found a damaged mobile phone
in a toilet at the home, according to the uniform citation. [@2]

The search followed an investigation the Bowling Green–Warren County Drug Task
Force opened in June 2026 into spice trafficking. [@1,3] Detectives used
surveillance and covert drug purchases from the suspect to establish the scale
of the operation. [@1] The same search turned up about 3 pounds of synthetic
marijuana, known as spice. [@1] Officers also seized about 25 pounds of Damiana,
a plant material used in making synthetic marijuana. [@1] Alongside the
marijuana, officers took $7,777 in US currency, 22 pairs of high-value
basketball shoes, a Cartier watch, a Gucci wallet and two vehicles believed used
in drug sales. [@2,3]

Detectives with the drug task force led the investigation, assisted by the
Kentucky State Police. [@1] The case was to be presented to the Warren County
Commonwealth's Attorney for the grand jury. [@1] Additional charges are
anticipated as the investigation continues. [@1] Cherry has previously been
convicted of trafficking in synthetic drugs, according to court documents. [@1]
