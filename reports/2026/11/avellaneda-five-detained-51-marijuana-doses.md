Five people were detained during a mega-operation against drug sales in
Avellaneda, a district of Buenos Aires province. [@1] The detainees were four
men and one woman, all adults. [@1] The six raids were at four properties on
Avenida Hipólito Yrigoyen, one in Dock Sud and one near La Saladita in Sarandí.
[@3]

Officers found 794 doses of cocaine and 51 of marijuana. [@1] Police described
the combined seizure as more than 840 doses of cocaine and marijuana, along with
firearms and ammunition. [@1] They also seized a .380 pistol, two revolvers of
.22 and .32 calibre, 9mm magazines, ammunition and shotgun cartridges. [@1]
Officers also seized a military-type transport vest, precision scales and items
used, according to the investigation, to cut and portion the drugs. [@1]

The raids were carried out by Buenos Aires province police with the Avellaneda
municipality's security secretariat, the UFIyJ No. 4 and the Guarantees Court
No. 1 of the Avellaneda-Lanús judicial department. [@1] The case was handled by
UFI No. 4 under prosecutor Mariano Zitto. [@2] Municipal security secretary
Alejo Chornobroff announced the operation's results on social media. [@3] All
five were charged with illegal possession of narcotics with intent to
commercialize, in real concurrence with illegal possession of a weapon and war
ammunition and possession of a civil-use weapon. [@1] Investigators sought to
establish the extent of the drug sales and each detainee's role. [@1]
