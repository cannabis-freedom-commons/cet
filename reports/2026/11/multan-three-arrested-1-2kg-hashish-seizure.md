The Narcotics Control Station Multan arrested three alleged members of an
organised drug-trafficking network, including two women. [@1] NCS Multan officer
Nusrat Ali led strike teams that raided suspected locations in Multan city and
its suburbs. [@1] Near Head Muhammad Wala on the Northern Bypass, officers
arrested two women identified as Saira Bibi and Mansab Bibi. [@1] In a separate
raid near Shershah Toll Plaza, officers arrested Saif Abbas. [@1]

Officers recovered 1.2kg of hashish and 300 grammes of Ice from Saif Abbas. [@1]
Around 1.2kg of Ice was recovered from Saira Bibi, while 450 grammes of heroin
was seized from Mansab Bibi. [@1] Official sources put the total recovery at
approximately 1.5kg of Ice, 450 grammes of heroin and 1.2kg of hashish. [@1]

Cases have been registered against all three accused under relevant sections of
the law, with further investigations under way to trace other members of the
alleged network. [@1] Officials said NCS Multan would continue its crackdown on
narcotics traffickers to protect the younger generation from the harmful effects
of drugs. [@1]
