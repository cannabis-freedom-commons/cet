The Public Security Police (PSP) dismantled a drug trafficking network that had
acted in Madeira for at least two years, in an operation carried out on 12 and
13 November 2026 in the municipalities of Funchal and Santa Cruz. [@4] The
operation, named Colombiana, ran on Thursday and Friday in Funchal and Santa
Cruz, the PSP's Regional Command said in a statement. [@3] Five people were
detained, men aged 21 to 41 from Portugal and South American countries, and
seven other suspects were identified. [@1] The operation served 31 search and
seizure warrants, 15 of them at homes, the police said. [@4]

Seizures included 505.46 grams of hashish, 400 ecstasy pills weighing 216.13
grams and 352.75 grams of an unidentified product that two suspects allegedly
sold as cocaine. [@2] Police also confiscated four firearms, three firearm
replicas, assorted ammunition, 3,000 euros in cash and 11 mobile phones, plus
equipment linked to drug preparation and sale. [@4] During one home search
officers found a military-calibre anti-aircraft round, which the bomb disposal
team assessed and destroyed in a controlled explosion. [@2] RTP's account gives
the hashish as 500 grams and describes 350 grams of the haul as cocaine. [@1]

According to the police, the group imported recreational drugs from other
European countries and sold them mainly at night in the region's nightlife
venues. [@1] Two of the five detainees were released on an identity and
residence term (TIR) while the other three remain detained pending their first
judicial questioning. [@2]
