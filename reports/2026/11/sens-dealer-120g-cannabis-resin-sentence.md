A 32-year-old man was sentenced to 18 months in prison, suspended, by the Sens
correctional court on Thursday 5 November 2026 after being accused of possessing
and selling drugs in Brienon-sur-Armançon, according to the Yonne gendarmerie.
[@1] On 1 November a heroin user went to his dealer to buy €20 worth of
products, but another man beat him and stole his money, the gendarmerie said.
[@1]

The victim went to the gendarmerie brigade in Migennes to complain about his
attacker and also denounced his dealer and the drug supplier, leading to three
arrests and searches of the dealer's home. [@1] Officers found 37 vials of 15 ml
of methadone, 120 g of cannabis resin, 65 g of cocaine, €380, a mobile phone and
scales. [@1]

The investigation involved the Migennes and Sens research brigades, gendarmerie
surveillance units in Sens and Joigny and a dog unit from Dijon, and the dealer,
who admitted the facts, was sentenced a few days later. [@1]
