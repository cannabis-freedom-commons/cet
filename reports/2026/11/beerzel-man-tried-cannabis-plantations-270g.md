On 19 February, a Rivierenland police patrol stopped 27-year-old Albanian man
S.S. in Mechelen for speeding. [@1] Prosecutors accused him of having a
coordinating role in one or more cannabis plantations. [@1] He has spent nine
months in pre-trial detention. [@1] The prosecution asked for a three-year
prison sentence and a combined 13,000 euro fine. [@1] The court is due to rule
in the case on 14 December. [@1]

At the stop he looked very nervous, could give no address for the friend he said
he was visiting, carried more than 2,000 euro in cash and had a drainpipe and
ventilator for building cannabis plantations in his car. [@1] Police found a
Waze destination on his phone to an address on the Aarschotbaan in Beerzel,
Putte, where number-plate data showed his car appeared regularly. [@1] A house
search there found no cannabis plantation but did find a bag with 270 grams of
cannabis. [@1] Officers also found 8 grams of cocaine and a handgun with a
magazine and six cartridges in the flat. [@1] Police also found invoices for
material that could be used to build cannabis plantations. [@1] Although S.S.
refused to give his phone codes, investigators cracked the device and found
photos of plantations and harvests confirming his involvement. [@1]

The prosecution acknowledged it never established where the plantation or
plantations stood. [@1] His lawyer said police came up empty three times
searching for plantations and argued S.S. was not the big fish. [@1] The defence
said the gun was a converted alarm pistol that was not ready for use, with the
cartridges stored separately in a box. [@1] S.S. told the court he had become a
father in prison without yet seeing his child and would travel immediately to
Albania to see the child if released. [@1]
