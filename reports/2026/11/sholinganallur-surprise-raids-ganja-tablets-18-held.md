During surprise raids across several places in Sholinganallur, police arrested
three history-sheeters and 15 persons found in possession of narcotic items.
[@1,2] In a separate crackdown on rowdy elements during the same drive, police registered three cases and secured four people, including history-sheeters. [@3] Police personnel of Pallikaranai district under the Tambaram Police
Commissionerate organised the surprise enforcement operation in which ganja and
narcotic tablets were seized. [@1,2] The Tambaram police said more than 160 police
personnel split into 47 teams took part in the surprise raids. [@1,2,3] The 47 teams
were led by T. Eswaran, the Deputy Commissioner of Police for Pallikaranai
district. [@1,3] Tambaram City Police Commissioner Najmul Hoda ordered the searches after receiving information that drugs were being used at lodgings in the southern Chennai suburbs. [@2]

The teams searched more than 90 houses in various places, including the housing
tenements in Kannagi Nagar of Sholinganallur. [@1,2] The named localities included Pallikaranai, Sholinganallur, Perumbakkam, Medavakkam, Kannagi Nagar and Semmancheri. [@2] Kannagi Nagar's housing
tenements were specifically named among the various places where the more than
90 houses were searched. [@1] Officers seized quantities of ganja and narcotic
tablets during the Sholinganallur house searches. [@1,2] Police seized 4.25 kg of ganja and 119 narcotic tablets during the searches and registered nine cases in connection with the raid. [@2] Of the 18 people, 17 were remanded in judicial custody and one was released on station bail. [@3] Police registered six cases under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act and arrested 14 people during the drive. [@3] Officers also seized
two-wheelers and four-wheelers from the people found with the narcotic items in
the same operation. [@1]
