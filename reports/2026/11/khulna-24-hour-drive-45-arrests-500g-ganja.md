Khulna district police arrested 45 people in various cases during a special
24-hour operation conducted at various places across the district. [@1] The
operation's figures were announced in a district police press release on
Thursday 19 November 2026. [@1] Officers searched 511 vehicles at various
checkpoints during the same 24-hour period. [@1]

The anti-drug part of the drive recovered 147 yaba tablets and 500 grams of
ganja. [@1] Police filed 17 cases for traffic-law violations and detained nine
vehicles during the operation. [@1] The 147 yaba tablets were recovered
alongside the ganja and are not counted in the cannabis total of 500 grams. [@1]

Police said regular operations and vehicle searches are continuing to keep the
district's law-and-order situation normal, control crime and strengthen
anti-drug activities. [@1] Police said such operations and searches will
continue to control drugs and crime and build a safe Khulna, and they sought the
cooperation of the district's respected residents. [@1] The press release
presented the drive as part of ongoing efforts for a safe district. [@1]
