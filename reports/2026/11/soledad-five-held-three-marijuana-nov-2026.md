Over the previous 24 hours, officers of the Guardia Civil Municipal, the
municipal police of Soledad de Graciano Sánchez in San Luis Potosí, detained
five men on suspicion of offences against health. [@1] Those held were Jesús
'N', 19, Armando 'N', 31, Pedro 'N', 19, Diego 'N', 24, and Noé 'N', 29,
detained in the Hacienda San Miguel, Cerrada de las Huertas, Rancho Pavón, San
José and Privada Continental neighbourhoods. [@2]

During separate interventions Jesús, Armando and Pedro were found with bags of
suspected marijuana, while Diego and Noé were found with doses of suspected
methamphetamine. [@1] The three were found with bags of green herb described as
having characteristics similar to marijuana, and the other two with doses of a
substance described as having characteristics of methamphetamine. [@2]

After being told why they were detained, the five men were taken to the
municipal command for medical certification and then placed at the disposal of
the Fiscalía General del Estado, the state prosecutor's office, with the seized
substances. [@1]
