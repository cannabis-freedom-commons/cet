Personnel from the OS7 unit of Carabineros in San Antonio detained two people on
Route 78 for alleged participation in an offence covered by Law 20.000 after a
procedure carried out on the highway. [@1] Carabineros said the two people were
detained for drug trafficking during a preventive inspection of an
interprovincial bus on Route 78 and that both were placed at the disposal of the
Public Prosecutor's Office. [@2]

The operation was carried out at kilometre 87 in the commune of San Antonio as
part of the preventive and inspection services performed by the specialised
unit, during which officers controlled an interprovincial bus circulating on the
road. [@1] During the review of the luggage a specialised sniffer dog gave a
positive alert to the possible presence of illicit substances, which allowed
officers to direct their inquiries and detect the drugs transported in the
vehicle. [@1] As a result of the procedure Carabineros seized almost 500 wraps
of coca paste, together with prepared marijuana and cocaine hydrochloride. [@1]
The two people were detained for alleged participation in drug trafficking in a
procedure that removed different illicit substances being transported along an
important regional connection route. [@1]
