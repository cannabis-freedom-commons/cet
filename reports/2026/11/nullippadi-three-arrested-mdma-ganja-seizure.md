Acting on a tip received by the District DANSAF squad, a Vidyanagar police team
searched a house near PMS Road in Nullippadi at about 6:40 pm on Monday. [@1]
Those arrested were N.H. Rabiyath (39), A.I. Farhan (30) and Muhammad Sadiq
(32), all within Kasaragod police station limits. [@1] The drugs were found kept
under the bed in the bedroom of the first accused Rabiyath's house. [@1]

Police seized 38.760 grams of MDMA and 6.650 grams of ganja from them. [@1]
Police's preliminary finding was that the accused kept the drugs for sale and
for their own use. [@1]

Those arrested were taken into custody along with the recovered drugs. [@1]
Kasaragod police registered case No. 714/2026 under NDPS Act sections 22(c), 29
and 20(b)(ii)(A) and assigned further investigation to Inspector Melbin Jose.
