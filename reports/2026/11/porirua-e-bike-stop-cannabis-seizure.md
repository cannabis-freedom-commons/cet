New Zealand Police said a 40-year-old man was found riding an electric motorbike
in Cannons Creek, Porirua, on 13 November 2026 while disqualified from driving
and breaching bail. [@2] The man tried to evade police and attempted to hand
items to an associate who came out of a nearby address before officers took him
into custody. [@1]

Officers searching the man found 29.5 grams of cannabis and nearly NZ$20,000 in
cash alongside methamphetamine. [@1] Police valued the methamphetamine at about
NZ$100,000. [@1] Police executed a search warrant at the associate's address,
found more cannabis there and, with a bailiff's help, seized two motorcycles for
unpaid reparation. [@4]

The man was remanded in custody and will reappear in Porirua District Court in
December, facing charges including possession of methamphetamine for supply.
[@3] Police did not release the man's name and said the case remained before the
courts, with the defendant presumed innocent until a court ruling. [@1]
