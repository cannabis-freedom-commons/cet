A Rajkot A.N.T.F. CID Crime team raided a farm on the outskirts of Rampara
Chobari village in Chotila, Surendranagar, where illegal ganja cultivation had
been detected. [@1,2] The operation uncovered illegal ganja cultivation in the
Chotila area of Surendranagar district, where a large quantity of green plants
was found in a single farm. [@1] Officers seized 130 green ganja plants, the
local name for cannabis, from the farm during the search. [@1,3] Police recorded
the total weight of the 130 seized plants as 118 kilograms. [@1,3]

Police estimated the value of the 118 kilograms of seized ganja at Rs 5.9
million, reported as Rs 59 lakh. [@1,2] The seized consignment was found in the
farm belonging to Bharatbhai Bavaliya. [@1] The case was presented as action
against the owner of the farm where the plants were found. [@1] The accused, Bharat Mavjibhai Bawaliya, was reported to be absconding, with police checking his mobile location and other contacts to arrest him. [@2] Police
registered a case under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act at
Anandpur police station in connection with the farm seizure. [@1,2] The ganja plants had been grown hidden among another crop so that no one would suspect them. [@2] The case cited NDPS Act sections 8(b), 8(c), 20(a)(1) and 20(b)(2)(c) against farm owner Bharat Mavjibhai Bawaliya, with Rajkot Zone ANTF Police Inspector J.J. Chauhan named as the complainant. [@3]
