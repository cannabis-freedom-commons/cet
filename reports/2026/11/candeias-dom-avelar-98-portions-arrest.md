Military police took a 60-year-old man, identified as Giovanes dos Santos Sena,
to the police station after finding drugs and trafficking materials at a house
in the Dom Avelar neighbourhood of Candeias, in the Salvador metropolitan area.
[@1] The incident was registered on the afternoon of Wednesday 18 November 2026.
[@1] Officers of the 26th Military Police Battalion went to the house on
Travessa Terceira after reports that the address was being used to sell drugs.
[@1]

Police found three men at the house; two of them fled and the 60-year-old
stayed. [@1] He reportedly told the officers that the drugs were inside the
fridge and that precision scales were nearby. [@1] Officers seized 98 portions
and one tablet of marijuana, two precision scales and a Motorola mobile phone.
[@1]

The man and the material were taken to the 20th Territorial Police Station in
Candeias, where the incident was registered and legal measures were adopted.
[@1]
