Cengkareng police arrested a 28-year-old man identified as A alias B over
alleged cannabis distribution in Palmerah, West Jakarta. [@1] Officers detained
him on Tuesday 10 November 2026 at about 13.30 in Gang Y, RT 006/RW 015,
Kelurahan Palmerah. [@1] Cengkareng police chief AKP Rahis Fadhillah said the
operation began after residents reported the spot was often used for drug
transactions. [@1]

An operational narcotics team under Iptu Lasaro Atmaja went to the scene to
investigate after receiving the residents report. [@1] During the search police
seized six brown-paper packages containing dried cannabis leaves. [@1] Police
said the net weight of the cannabis was 21.29 grams. [@1] Officers also seized a
red Oppo A5S phone and a black plastic bag as supporting evidence. [@1]

The man and all the evidence were taken to the Cengkareng police command for
further investigation. [@1] He was charged under Article 114 paragraph (1),
subsidiary Article 111 paragraph (1), of Law Number 35 of 2009 on Narcotics.
