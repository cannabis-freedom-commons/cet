Officers of the Department of Narcotics Control's Moulvibazar district office
arrested Santu Malaha, 35, after finding 210 grams of ganja at his home in the
Hingazia tea garden area of Kulaura. [@1] The ganja raid was the second of two
carried out that morning, between 11:20 and 11:50 am in the Nichu line area of
Hingazia tea garden, whose post office is listed as Kajaldhara. [@1]

The department said the seizures also included 12 litres of chola liquor and 60
litres of wash, a liquor-making ingredient, from a separate raid the same
morning. [@1] The first raid, from 10:10 to 10:50 am on secret information,
searched the home of Riday Das, 24, in the Durga Mandap line area of Lowaini tea
garden and seized the liquor. [@1]

Deputy director Mohammad Rasheduzzaman of the district office said two separate
regular cases were filed at Kulaura police station under the Narcotics Control
Act against the two men. [@1] The department said it would continue such
operations against drugs. [@1]
