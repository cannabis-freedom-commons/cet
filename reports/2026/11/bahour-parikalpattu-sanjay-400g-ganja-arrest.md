Bahour police officers led by Sub-Inspector Sivakumar were on midnight patrol on
17 November 2026 when they received information that ganja was being sold in the
Parikalpattu area of the Villupuram–Nagappattinam bypass road. [@1] During that
patrol they received information that ganja was being sold in the Parikalpattu
area of the Villupuram–Nagappattinam bypass road. [@1] On the basis of that
information, officers went to the spot on the Villupuram–Nagappattinam bypass
road at Parikalpattu. [@1] There they detained a youth who was standing in a
suspicious manner and searched him. [@1] It was found that he had stocked ganja
and that he was Sanjay, aged 22, of Mariamman Koil Street in Melparikalpattu.
[@1]

Police arrested Sanjay after finding that he had stocked ganja. [@1] Officers
seized 400 grams of ganja from his possession. [@1] Officers also seized a
mobile phone found with Sanjay at the time of the arrest. [@1] Officers also
seized a bike found with Sanjay at the time of the arrest. [@1]

He was produced before a court and lodged in jail. [@1]
