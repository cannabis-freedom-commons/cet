Police in Roermond arrested the resident of a home on the Koningin
Regentesselaan after a search of the property and a garage box on the
Scheidingsweg on Tuesday afternoon. [@2] The suspect was due to appear before
the examining magistrate, or rechter-commissaris, on Friday. [@1] Police said
they entered the home following information they had received. [@1]

Officers found a few kilograms of amphetamine, a batch of hemp (hennep) and
various other narcotics. [@1] Weapons seized included firearms, gas and alarm
pistols, stabbing weapons and a ploertendoder, a type of baton. [@1] Officers
also found chemicals for processing narcotics, equipment for setting up a
cannabis nursery (hennepkwekerij) and cash. [@1] All the items were seized. [@1]

The police carried out the searches on Tuesday and announced them the following
day. [@3]
