Trenčín regional police investigators charged four men aged 38, 40, 40 and 41
with illegal production and trafficking of a narcotic and psychotropic substance
as co-perpetrators. [@1] The four were detained on 9 November 2026. [@3] All
four were placed in police custody cells. [@1] The investigator sought their
pre-trial detention, and the court agreed and ordered all four held in custody.
[@1]

Police said the men had acquired marijuana since at least the start of 2025 and
kept, divided and distributed it to other people. [@1] The group was suspected
of operating in Dubnica nad Váhom. [@3] Police searched homes and found a large
amount of narcotic and psychotropic substances. [@2] Police said the men acted
for financial gain. [@4] Police did not publish the exact weight or an estimated
value of the seized substances. [@4]

The Trenčín regional police spokeswoman Petra Klenková announced the charges.
[@5] The regional outlet Trenčiansky kraj reported the police action under the
name QUATTRO. [@4]
