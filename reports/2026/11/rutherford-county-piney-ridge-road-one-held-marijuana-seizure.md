The Rutherford County Sheriff's Office said deputies from its Road Patrol
Division had been investigating complaints and tips about suspected drug
activity at an apartment on Piney Ridge Road. [@1] On 12 November 2026 the
sheriff's office said deputies applied for a search warrant for the apartment
and were granted it after an investigation that included surveillance and
traffic stops. [@1]

While executing the search warrant, deputies found and seized multiple illegal
narcotics, including marijuana alongside MDMA, also called ecstasy,
methamphetamine and fentanyl. [@1] The renter was not in the apartment during
the search, and deputies later found Archie Orlando Porter and arrested him.
[@1]

The sheriff's office said Porter was charged with possession with intent to
manufacture, sell or deliver marijuana. [@1] Porter was also charged with
maintaining a dwelling for the purpose of keeping or selling controlled
substances. [@1] Porter was also charged with possession of drug paraphernalia.
[@1] Porter was held in custody without bond after his arrest. [@1] The
sheriff's office said the investigation was ongoing and additional charges and
arrests were anticipated. [@1]
