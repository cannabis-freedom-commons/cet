Police padlocked an unlicensed cannabis shop on Bell Boulevard in Bayside,
Queens, for the second time this year, the Queens district attorney's office
said. [@1] A court-authorized search warrant was executed at the 501 MORE shop
at 40-13 Bell Blvd. on 5 November 2026, covering the shop, an apartment above it
and an apartment in an adjacent building. [@1,2] Two people were arrested during
the search. [@1,2] The two arrested were issued desk appearance tickets and were
not the people arrested on felony charges in the earlier raid. [@1,2]

Officers seized 1 pound (about 0.45 kg) of cannabis flower, half a pound (about
0.23 kg) of THC vapes and more than 225 pounds of illegal flavored vapes. [@1,2]
Officers found a chute and pulley system from the store to the apartment above
it and a concealed compartment. [@1,2] Queens District Attorney Melinda Katz
inspected the delivery system. [@1,2]

The shop had been padlocked on 14 May 2026, when an estimated $10 million worth
of products were seized. [@1,2] In the May raid officers seized 526 pounds of cannabis flower, 417 pounds of THC vapes, 284 pounds of edibles, 17 pounds of THC concentrate, 1,635 pounds of flavored tobacco vapes and 101 pounds of other tobacco products. [@2] Saif Ajmal Khan, 27, and Haroon Al Nomiri, 23,
were arrested in the May raid and arraigned on a charge of criminal possession
of cannabis in the first degree, facing up to seven years in prison. [@1] Their
cases are proceeding, the district attorney's office said. [@1,2]
