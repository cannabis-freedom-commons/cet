The drug unit of Jember police in East Java raided the house of a suspected
cannabis dealer. [@1] The raided house is in Perumahan Panji, Panji district,
Jember, and the search was witnessed by the local neighbourhood head. [@1]

Officers found 26 cannabis plants growing in the backyard of the house. [@1]
Police seized the 26 homegrown plants. [@1] Officers also seized 15 grams of
dried cannabis already packed in several packages. [@1]

The suspect is a repeat offender in a similar case who allegedly resumed dealing
cannabis over the past few months using seeds said to have come from Aceh. [@1]
