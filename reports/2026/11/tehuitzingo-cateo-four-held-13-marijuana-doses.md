Puebla state prosecutors carried out a search warrant at a property in the
Primera Sección of Tehuitzingo. [@1] Officers detained four men on suspicion of
crimes against health. [@1] The search took place on 19 November 2026 after
intelligence work, field investigation, checks on anonymous complaints and
surveillance. [@1] The FGE carried it out with the Secretariat of National
Defence, the National Guard and the state police. [@1]

Inside the property officers seized 13 plastic bags of dried green herb with
characteristics similar to marijuana. [@2] In the same search they seized 81
ziploc bags of a crystalline substance similar to crystal, a gram scale, a brown
shotgun and six cartridges. [@1] The FGE said the house had been used as a
discreet distribution and sales point with activity reported around the clock.
[@1]

Officers secured the property and handed the four detainees to the Public
Ministry, which will decide their legal situation. [@1] In total, the
intervention produced 94 bags of suspected illicit substances, plus the weapon
and other items. [@3]
