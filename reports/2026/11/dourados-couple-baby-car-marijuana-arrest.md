A couple was arrested on suspicion of drug trafficking in Dourados, Mato Grosso
do Sul, on the night of Thursday 19 November 2026, after being caught
transporting marijuana and cocaine in a car that also carried their son, aged
one year and eight months. [@1] The suspects, aged 22 and 25, were in the
vehicle when they drew the officers' attention during patrols in the Izidro
Pedroso neighbourhood. [@1] That stop also led the Guarda Municipal, Dourados's
municipal guard, to the family's residence, where more drugs were found. [@1]

After the stop, the woman asked to call at the residence before continuing with
the team, saying she needed to take some clothes that were in the car. [@1]
Inside the house, guards found four more portions of marijuana on top of a piece
of furniture. [@2] The man admitted he owned the drugs but denied that his
partner had any involvement. [@1]

Both were taken to Depac (Delegacia de Pronto Atendimento Comunitário, a
community police station) and booked in flagrante for drug trafficking. [@1]
