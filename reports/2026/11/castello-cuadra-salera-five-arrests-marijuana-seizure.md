Spain's National Police detained five people in an anti-drug operation in the
Cuadra Salera area of Castelló. [@1] The detained people were reported to be
three Spanish citizens, one Colombian and one Moroccan. [@1]

Officers had searched a house on Calle Algarrobo in the previous days and seized
a significant quantity of marijuana. [@1]
