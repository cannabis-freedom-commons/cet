A 21-year-old man was stopped by police near the junction of Venezuela and
Lambramani avenues in Arequipa on 25 November 2026. [@1] The intervention took
place during a preventive patrol. [@1] Police said the man, identified only by
the initials G.G.C., appeared nervous when he noticed the officers and was
searched. [@1] Among his belongings officers found a ziploc bag holding greenish
leaves, stems and seeds that they took to be cannabis sativa, marijuana. [@1] He
was carrying what looked like a pistol at waist height. [@1]

Officers found a Glock pistol grip with serial number BHTN628 and a magazine.
[@1] A forensic examination determined that the object was a replica, not an
authentic pistol. [@1] The young man was taken to a police station for further
procedures, and the case was reported to the Public Ministry, which is to assess
the facts and establish responsibility. [@1]
