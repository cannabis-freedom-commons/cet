Quintana Roo ministerial authorities searched two homes, one in Playa del Carmen
and one in Cancún, as part of investigations into alleged small-scale drug
dealing. [@1] State prosecutor's personnel carried out both operations with
support from the Defence Ministry, the Navy, the National Guard and municipal
police. [@1] After the searches, both properties were secured under ministerial
custody and the seized objects and substances were added to the investigation
files. [@1]

The first search took place at a property in the Palmas 2 subdivision of Playa
del Carmen, on Circuito Palma Chit. [@1] There, agents found dry herb with
characteristics similar to marijuana, a crystalline substance that could be
crystal, blue pills, pipes and a gram scale. [@1] A GM pickup truck was also
seized at that address. [@1]

The second search took place at a property on Chumayel street, between 117
street and Industrial avenue in Supermanzana 97 in Cancún, where dry green herb
presumed to be marijuana was found. [@1] The searches were authorised by court
orders and were linked to suspected drug dealing. [@2]
