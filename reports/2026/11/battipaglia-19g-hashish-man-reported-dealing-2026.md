Police in Battipaglia stopped a foreign man in the town centre on the evening of
20 November 2026 after he tried to get away from the officers' checks. [@1] The
stop was part of a territory-control patrol carried out by the investigative
squad of the local public security police station, the Commissariato di Pubblica
Sicurezza di Battipaglia. [@1] A personal search found 25 pieces of a substance
that a police narcotics test identified as hashish, with a total weight of 19.50
grams. [@1] The officers seized the hashish and placed it at the disposal of the
judicial authority. [@1]

The man, who had no identity documents and no fixed abode, was identified
through fingerprint checks. [@1] Police reported him at liberty for possession
of a narcotic substance for the purpose of dealing, and he was not arrested.
[@1] They then made him available to the immigration office of the Questura di
Salerno, the provincial police headquarters, to check whether his stay in Italy
was lawful. [@1]
