Guardia di Finanza officers in Catanzaro arrested a 50-year-old Italian man in
flagrante during territorial checks aimed at countering drug dealing in urban
areas. [@1,5] The man was already known to police for earlier drug-related
judicial matters. [@1,5] After the arrest he was taken to the Ugo Caridi prison in
Catanzaro. [@1,5] The investigating judge at the Tribunale di Catanzaro validated
the arrest and ordered pretrial custody in prison. [@1,5]

During a search of the suspect's home, officers found about 500 grams of
hashish, already divided into blocks (panetti). [@1,5] The home searched was in
the Isonzo neighbourhood of Catanzaro. [@4,5] He was found in possession of the
drug during that domiciliary search. [@3] Investigators estimated the seized
substance could have generated about 10,000 euros in illicit proceeds. [@1,5] That
estimate was reported in the prosecutor's statement. [@2]

The operation was carried out by the Baschi Verdi of the Guardia di Finanza
group in Catanzaro, with support from the canine unit of the rapid-deployment
company in Lamezia Terme. [@1] The case is at the preliminary investigation
stage, with the suspect's responsibility to be established in court. [@1]
