A 33-year-old man was stopped by military police in the early hours of Sunday 22
November as he tried to leave a house in Bugalho, Campos dos Goytacazes, with a
cannabis tablet hidden at his waist. [@1] Officers had been called to the
address after a report that men linked to drug trafficking were using the
residence to store drugs. [@1] At the house the man told police his sister and
brother-in-law were inside, and both initially authorised entry while saying the
property belonged to his mother. [@1]

While waiting for the owner, officers saw the shirtless man go inside and return
in a dark shirt, then try to leave with a bulge at his waist, and stopped him.
[@1] A personal search found one tablet of dried pressed herb appearing to be
cannabis, weighing about one kilogram. [@1] A search inside the house found 29
more tablets of the same substance. [@1] In total, 30 tablets of the drug were
seized. [@1] The 30 tablets amounted to about 30 kg of the drug. [@1]

The man, his sister and his brother-in-law were taken to the 134th Police
Station in central Campos with all the seized material. [@1] At the station the
occurrence was recorded and the 30 tablets seized; the man was questioned and
then released, while the sister and brother-in-law were heard as witnesses and
also released. [@1]
