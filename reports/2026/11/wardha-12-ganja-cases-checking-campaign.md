Wardha district police carried out a special anti-drug checking campaign across
the district between 10 and 14 November 2026. [@1] The campaign was intended to
check secluded and sensitive places where drug use was suspected and to verify
people with earlier cases under anti-drug laws. [@1] Police registered 11 of the
cases after finding people consuming ganja and one after finding a person
possessing it, making 12 cases in all. [@1]

Officers checked 236 secluded locations across all 19 police stations in the
district. [@1] They also checked 106 people who had cases registered against
them under anti-drug laws. [@1]

The campaign involved sub-divisional police officers from Wardha, Hinganghat,
Arvi and Pulgaon, the in-charges of all 19 police stations, other officers,
constables and local crime branch staff. [@1] It was carried out under the
guidance of Saurabh Kumar Agrawal, superintendent of police in Wardha, and
Sadashiv Waghmare, additional superintendent of police. [@1] Police said the
special campaign created a deterrent effect on those committing drug-related
offences and would help curb such crime in future. [@1]
