The Brigada Militar arrested two people for drug trafficking in Torres during
Operação Olhos da Lei actions on Friday 13 November, in the Getúlio Vargas
neighbourhood. [@1,3] The two detainees were taken for medical evaluation and then presented at the Torres Delegacia de Polícia de Pronto Atendimento with the seized materials for the legal procedures. [@3] During a check in a commercial establishment, a woman was
arrested after officers found 11 portions of crack and one portion of marijuana
in a backpack. [@2,3] A 37-year-old woman tried to move away toward an inner room and identified a pink backpack on a snooker table as hers. [@3] About 20 minutes later, at about 14:50, Tactical Force officers carried out the second approach in an establishment on Rua Coronel Pacheco. [@3] In another action in the same neighbourhood, a man was
arrested after officers found seven portions of cocaine and one portion of
hashish on him. [@1,3] According to the Brigada Militar, video-monitoring images showed the moment the material was thrown inside the establishment. [@3] The first approach happened at about 14:30 near a bar on Rua Osvaldo Aranha, where the 25-year-old man was stopped at the entrance. [@3]

The Brigada Militar balance reported two people arrested and 20 portions of
substances identified as crack, cocaine, marijuana and hashish. [@1] The woman
had five police records for drug trafficking and five for contempt, plus records
for theft and receiving stolen goods. [@2] The man also had a prior police
record for drug trafficking. [@1]

The actions were part of Operação Olhos da Lei, which aims to intensify
ostensive policing, carry out area-saturation actions and combat criminality.
[@2]
