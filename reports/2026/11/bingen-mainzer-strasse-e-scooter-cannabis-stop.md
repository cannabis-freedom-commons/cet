During a patrol, police in Bingen stopped a man riding an e-scooter on Mainzer
Straße at about 17:55. [@1] The rider was a 26-year-old who at the start of the
traffic check handed officers a small weapons permit, a loaded starter pistol
and an animal-defence spray to avoid misunderstandings. [@2]

The subsequent roadworthiness check produced clear indications that the man was
under the influence of drugs. [@1] The driver told officers that he grew
cannabis himself and consumed it regularly. [@1] A voluntarily offered rapid
drug test could not be carried out despite several attempts. [@2] Police barred
him from riding on and ordered a blood sample. [@1]

The starter pistol, weapons permit and animal-defence spray were seized
preventively and sent to the weapons authority, because the suspected drug
influence raised serious doubts about his suitability and reliability under the
weapons law. [@2] Officers opened a proceeding against the driver on suspicion
of driving under the influence of drugs. [@1]
