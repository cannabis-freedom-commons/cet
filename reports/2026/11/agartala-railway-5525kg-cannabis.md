A joint Government Railway Police and Railway Protection Force operation was
launched at Agartala Railway Station on 13 November 2026 on specific
information. [@1] Officers apprehended 19-year-old Niraj Kumar on Platform No. 1
on a sitting bench in front of the parking area, between the RPF post and the
pay-and-use toilet, at about 0900 hrs. [@1] Kumar was described as a resident of
Kumarditola, Malahbigha in the Makhdumpur police-station area of Jehanabad
district, Bihar. [@1]

Officers recovered 05.525 kg of suspected ganja from a pithu bag in his
possession. [@1] Police put the black-market value of the seized ganja at
approximately Rs 2.5 lakh. [@1] Railway police arrested him and registered
Agartala GRPS Case No. 2026GRP080 under Sections 20(b)(ii)(B)/29 of the NDPS
Act, 1985. [@1]

Inspector Raju Bhowmik, officer in charge of Agartala Railway Police Station,
gave details of the operation to the media. [@1] He said such operations against
illegal contraband would continue. [@1]
