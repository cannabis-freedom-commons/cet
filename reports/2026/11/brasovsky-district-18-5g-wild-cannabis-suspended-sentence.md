A 43-year-old man from Brasovsky District in Bryansk Oblast was convicted of
illegally acquiring and storing narcotic drugs in a significant amount without
intent to sell. [@1] The Prosecutor's Office of Brasovsky District supported the
prosecution at his trial. [@1] A court in the region found him guilty and
sentenced him to one year in prison suspended, with a two-year probation period.
[@1]

The verdict had not entered into legal force. [@1]

He had gone into a forest and collected wild cannabis there. [@1] The regional
prosecutor's office said the plant was collected in early August 2026. [@2]
Officers found 18.5 grams of the drug on him during a check. [@1] He kept the
cannabis at his home for his own use. [@2]

The charge treated the amount as significant, the category that raises the
offence above simple possession without intent to sell. [@1] Officers removed
more than 18 grams of the banned substance from circulation. [@2]
