PC Roberts of Thames Valley Police's Rural Crime Taskforce assisted local
officers following reports of two men poaching pheasants at Winchbottom Lane,
Marlow, on the evening of November 13. [@1] Officers seized catapults and
cannabis during the incident and dealt with the two men for the offences. [@1]

Further checks showed one of the two suspects was wanted by the Immigration
Agency. [@1] That suspect was arrested and handed over to Immigration
Enforcement officers. [@1]

Thames Valley Police encouraged residents to keep reporting suspicious activity
to officers. [@1]
