A resident of the Albarine neighbourhood in Ambérieu-en-Bugey alerted the
gendarmes on 21 November 2026 to a dealing point set up at the foot of a
building. [@1] The dealing point was spoiling life for local residents. [@1]
Young people had placed chairs in front of the building entrance and were
dealing openly. [@1] Gendarmes arrived in the early afternoon, the situation
became tense, and they arrested four young men. [@1]

Searches confirmed the gendarmes' suspicions. [@1] Some of those arrested had
300 to 400 euros on them, as well as cannabis kept in small make-up boxes, a box
cutter and a knife. [@1] On 26 November 2026 three of the four arrested and a
fourth man, accused of supplying the dealing point, were convicted. [@1] Three
of the four convicted were jailed after the hearing. [@1]
