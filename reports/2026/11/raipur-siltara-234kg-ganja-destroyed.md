Raipur Rural Police destroyed 234 kg of ganja, 326 banned tablets and 12 grams
of MDMA seized in separate cases. [@1] The drugs were burned in the furnace of
the Jaiswal Nico plant at Siltara. [@1] The action was carried out under the
provisions of the NDPS Act and court orders. [@1]

Police said the drugs had been seized in NDPS cases registered at different
police stations in the Raipur rural area. [@1] A district-level drug disposal
committee had been formed for destruction of the seized material. [@1] The
destruction was carried out under the committee's supervision following
government guidelines and environmental standards. [@1] Superintendent of Police
Shweta Srivastava Sinha, Additional Superintendent Sandeep Mittal, City
Superintendent Virendra Srivastava, Deputy Commissioner of Excise Praveen Verma
and the concerned station incharges were present during the destruction. [@1]
