Ronda no Bairro agents arrested a 19-year-old youth for possession and
consumption of drugs late in the afternoon of Sunday 15 November 2026 in the
Marco dos Corais area of Ponta Verde in Maceio. [@1] The patrol was sent by the
Military Police Operations Centre at about 17h00 after the state Public Security
Secretariat's video-monitoring system flagged suspected drug use at the spot.
[@1] During the search officers found substances described as similar to
marijuana and crack together with a table knife, and arrested the youth. [@2]

With support from a Military Police patrol the youth was taken first to the
Oplit at Marco dos Corais and then to the Central de Flagrantes in Tabuleiro do
Martins for police procedures. [@1] The arrest took place in the Marco dos
Corais area in the Ponta Verde neighbourhood of Maceio. [@3]
