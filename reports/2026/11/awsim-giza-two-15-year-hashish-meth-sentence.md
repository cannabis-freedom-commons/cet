The Giza Criminal Court, sitting at the Zeinhom courts complex, sentenced two
defendants to 15 years of rigorous imprisonment. [@1,3,4] The court found they had
been caught in the Awsim centre district with hashish and methamphetamine plus
blades used to assault people. [@1] The referral order in case 1900 of 2026
Awsim felonies, registered as 2279 of 2026 North Giza, accused them of
possessing hashish and methamphetamine for trafficking. [@1,3,4] Prosecutors also
charged them with possessing blades for assaulting people without legal
justification or professional need. [@1,4]

An Awsim police investigations officer said a secret source reported that the
two were trafficking drugs from their residence. [@1,4] After verifying the
information and obtaining prosecution permission, he went with a security force,
arrested the two and searched them and their home, finding the drugs and tools.
[@1,4]

The chemical laboratory report found the seizure included 5.80 grams of hashish.
[@1,3,4] The same report listed separate methamphetamine samples weighing 5.50 grams
and 89.89 grams plus five bags totalling 4.90 grams, which are not counted as
cannabis. [@1,4] A second account confirms the same Giza court imposed the same
15-year term for trafficking hashish and methamphetamine and possessing the
blades in Awsim, Giza Governorate. [@2]
