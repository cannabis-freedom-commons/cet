Mollie Geary, 30, of St James Road, Brentwood, was sentenced at Southend Crown
Court on November 12. [@1] She had pleaded guilty to being concerned in the
supply of Class A and Class B drugs. [@1] The judge found she had been
vulnerable and exploited and had a lesser role in the operation. [@1] She wept
and hyperventilated in the dock until the judge reassured her he would not send
her to prison. [@1] He sentenced her to 20 months in prison, suspended for two
years, with 12 mental health treatment sessions and an alcohol treatment
requirement. [@1]

Police stopped her boyfriend's car in February 2025 and found drugs and money
under his seat while Geary sat beside him in the passenger seat. [@1] Essex
Police then raided the address the pair shared and found cannabis and both pill
and powder forms of MDMA. [@1] Officers also recovered digital scales and
zip-lock bags. [@1] Analysis of Geary's phone showed messages offering to supply
cannabis and MDMA and taking updates from a drug runner, largely acting on
behalf of another. [@1]

Her former partner had already been sentenced by the time she was charged and
brought before magistrates to plead in summer 2026. [@1] She was seeking support
for her own drug use, having cut out Class A drugs but still using cannabis, and
her father attended court to support her. [@1]
