On 1 November 2026, an Armed Police Force team acting on a tip-off intercepted a
truck on an inner road at Mainamaini in Belka Municipality-8, Udayapur. [@1] The
truck was a 12-wheeled vehicle with registration Province 1-02-002 Kha 0206
carrying marijuana hidden in 246 plastic bags. [@2] The driver abandoned the
truck on the narrow road and fled with the men escorting it, and the truck was
removed the next morning using an excavator. [@2] The truck seized in the
evening was brought to the Area Police Office in Belka only the next morning.
[@2]

Police seized 7,826.81 kg of marijuana from the truck. [@3] The marijuana had
been packed into 246 plastic bags and covered with black polythene. [@1]
Investigators believe the marijuana had been produced in Bhojpur and was being
transported through Udayapur to India. [@3]

Unnamed sources claimed the truck had actually carried about 13,000 kg of
marijuana, leaving a gap of about five tonnes with the officially reported
amount. [@2] Officials gave conflicting accounts of the delay in moving the
truck, with the Armed Police Force citing mud and Nepal Police citing the narrow
road requiring an excavator. [@2]
