Gauri Chak police station in Patna raided a kirana, a small neighbourhood
grocery shop, in Khushhalchak village on 26 November 2026 after a tip-off that
ganja was being bought and sold there. [@2] Three people were arrested at the
shop. [@1] The police said the raid was part of a continuing drive against
intoxicants and illegal liquor ordered by the Patna senior superintendent of
police and the city superintendent of police (east). [@2]

Bus tickets were also recovered during the search, the police statement said.
[@2] A case was registered at the police station and further legal action was
under way, the police said. [@2] The police account was given by Ranjan Kumar,
sub-divisional police officer for Sadar-02 in Patna. [@2] Dainik Bhaskar
reported the seizure as 12 kilos 540 grams of ganja recovered from the grocery
shop. [@1]
