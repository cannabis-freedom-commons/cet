Bilal F., Mounir H. and Chakib M. lived together in a house in Caspe from which
they sold drugs to local buyers. [@1] For home deliveries the three used a car
in which the housing for the passenger airbag had been specially prepared to
conceal their merchandise. [@1] While the three devoted themselves to that
illicit business they managed to capture an important portion of the drug market
in the surrounding comarca. [@1]

According to the published summary, the accused trafficked in marijuana, cocaine
and hashish. [@1] The Guardia Civil regarded the flat they operated as a very
active drug sales point in the comarca. [@1]

Guardia Civil officers detained all three men on 13 March. [@1] Zaragoza's
provincial court sentenced the three men to three years in prison on that
Monday. [@1] The concealment in the passenger airbag compartment and the regular
home-delivery runs by car were central to how the group held its significant
local market share. [@1]
