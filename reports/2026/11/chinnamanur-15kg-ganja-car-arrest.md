Chinnamanur police conducted a vehicle check on the bypass road at Chinnamanur
on 17 November 2026. [@1] During that roadside check, officers stopped a car
that was travelling along the bypass road and searched the vehicle. [@1] They
arrested 44-year-old Vimalraj, who was travelling in the car and was described
as hailing from Aduthurai in Perambalur district. [@1]

During the search of the car, officers found 15 kg 179 g of ganja divided into
eight separate packets. [@1] Police seized the eight packets containing the 15
kg 179 g of ganja discovered inside the vehicle. [@1] Officers also seized the
car itself, which had been used to transport the cannabis along the bypass road.
[@1] The total quantity seized was recorded as 15 kg 179 g, just over 15 kg of
ganja in eight packets. [@1]

Chinnamanur police are investigating the case following the seizure of the
cannabis and the arrest of the suspect. [@1] The investigation is being carried
out by Chinnamanur police, who seized the ganja and the car and detained the
suspect. [@1]
