Military Police arrested two men aged 21 and 24 in flagrante late on the
afternoon of 18 November 2026 in the Rochedo neighbourhood of Conselheiro
Lafaiete. [@1] Police said they had received information that a property was
being used to store drugs. [@1] Officers surprised the two suspects as they were
leaving the property, and the men tried to flee but were caught and arrested.
[@1]

Inside the property officers found 26 bars of a substance resembling maconha,
nine larger portions and 34 smaller already packaged portions. [@1] Officers
also found three pills resembling ecstasy alongside the cannabis. [@1] Officers
also seized a .32-calibre pistol with four rounds, two precision scales,
materials used to package and label drugs, five mobile phones and R$100 in cash.
[@1]

Three motorcycles were collected, with police saying some had irregular
identifier markings and one had allegedly been used in trafficking. [@1] The two
detainees were taken for medical care and then presented to the Civil Police
with the seized materials. [@1]
