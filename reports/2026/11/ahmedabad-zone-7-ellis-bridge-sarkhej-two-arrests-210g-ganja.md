Zone-7 police carried out a drive in the Ellis Bridge and Sarkhej areas of
Ahmedabad. [@1] The drive was part of the 'No Drugs in Ahmedabad City' campaign

cases, and a case was registered under the NDPS Act. [@1]

auto-rickshaw and the mobile phones, at Rs 2,26,500 in all. [@1]
