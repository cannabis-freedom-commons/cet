A Bowling Green man was jailed on several charges, including fleeing police.
[@1] Bowling Green police stopped a vehicle on Veterans Memorial Highway with
only one headlight on shortly before midnight on Wednesday. [@1] The driver was
26-year-old Tavarus Holloway of Bowling Green. [@1] Holloway was taken into
custody. [@1]

Police said they could smell marijuana coming from the vehicle, leading to a
search. [@1] Officers said they found an open container of alcohol and a rifle,
and that Holloway had multiple prior felony charges. [@1] When officers said
they were detaining Holloway, he ran from the scene. [@1] Police said that when
they caught him he resisted arrest and refused commands. [@1]

Holloway said he had difficulty breathing, prompting an EMS response, and police
said they found suspected marijuana in his possession. [@1] He was taken to The
Medical Center at Bowling Green for clearance and then to the Warren County
Regional Jail. [@1] At the jail he remained on charges including possession of
marijuana (enhancement), third-degree fleeing or evading police and resisting
arrest. [@1]
