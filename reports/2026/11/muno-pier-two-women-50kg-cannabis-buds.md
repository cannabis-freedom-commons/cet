Two Thai women, aged 32 and 26, were detained during a joint security operation
in Narathiwat province. [@2] The operation took place on 21 November 2026. [@2]
Officers stopped them on the access road to Ton Chomphu pier at Muno market, Moo
1, Muno subdistrict, Su-ngai Kolok district, where the Golok River marks the
Thai–Malaysia border. [@2]

Officers seized 50 kilograms of cannabis flower buds that the women were
preparing to carry across to the neighbouring country. [@2] A joint team of
Border Protection Company 8 under the Suriyothai control command, Border
Surveillance Unit 4412 and the Su-ngai Kolok district administration carried out
the stop. [@2] The seized material was described as cannabis flower buds
intended for an illegal export across the border in Narathiwat. [@1] The two
women were intercepted as they prepared to move the 50 kg of cannabis buds
across the Thai–Malaysia border in Su-ngai Kolok. [@3]

The two women and all the seized evidence were handed to investigators at the
Su-ngai Kolok police station for legal action. [@2] Investigators were asked to
trace the network and the source of this batch of cannabis buds. [@2] The
operation followed tighter patrols along the Thai–Malaysia border during
November 2026, particularly around Muno and toward Tak Bai. [@2] Cannabis buds
are classed as a controlled herb under a Ministry of Public Health announcement
even though they were removed from the narcotics list. [@2] Possessing large
quantities for trade, transporting them or exporting them without permission is
an offence under the Protection and Promotion of Thai Traditional Medicine
Wisdom Act B.E. 2542 (1999), as well as customs law. [@2]
