Military Police officers arrested a man during an operation against drug
trafficking on Rua Vinte e Quatro de Junho, between Morro Santana and Morro São
João, in Ouro Preto, Minas Gerais. [@1] The operation took place on Saturday 14
November after military personnel received information that a residential
property on the street was being used for the commercial sale of narcotics. [@1]
During the follow-up checks at the scene, officers identified movements around
the property that they described as consistent with the practice of drug
trafficking. [@1]

In the backyard of the targeted residence, officers found 12 marijuana plants
growing on the premises. [@1] In the same search, officers also seized a pot
containing 65 crack stones. [@1] Officers also seized R$300 in cash during the
search. [@1]

Officers found the suspect inside the residence when the seizure was made. [@1]
He was then taken to the UPA emergency care unit in Ouro Preto to receive
medical attention before being taken on to the police station. [@1]
