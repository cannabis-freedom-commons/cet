Narsipatnam Town police arrested three people on Wednesday and produced them in
court. [@2] The three are contract stenographer Sampadarao Ravikumar alias Ravi
of the Additional Civil Judge court, Visakhapatnam District Court advocate
Mandal Anuradha and her husband Rikki Viplavamurthy alias Akash. [@2] Police
traced their involvement through call data, bank transactions and other
technical evidence. [@2]

On 9 September, Kotavuratla police caught Naresh Raju Sridharan (27) of
Kailasapuram, Tiruchirappalli, while he was transporting 10 kg of ganja. [@2]
Police booked him under the NDPS Act and sent him to Visakhapatnam Central Jail
on remand. [@2] Others prepared release warrants, bonds and bail warrants to
free Sridharan on bail. [@1] The judge’s signature and court seal on those
papers were forged and sent to the central jail authorities. [@2]

Suspicious jail officials sent the papers to the Narsipatnam Civil Judge (Junior
Division) court. [@2] Court officials examining them confirmed the papers were
fake. [@2] The court superintendent complained to Narsipatnam Town police on the
13th. [@2] The forgery case remains under investigation. [@1]
