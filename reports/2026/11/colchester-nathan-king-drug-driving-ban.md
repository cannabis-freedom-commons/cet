Nathan King, 27, was stopped in Colchester in April after officers noticed a
problem with his car's suspension. [@1] After he pulled over, officers could see
packets of white powder and a cannabis bud in the car. [@1] Police found a bong
in the driver's-side door well and a cannabis grinder in the central console.
[@1] Officers found a single bud of cannabis and 30 packets of white-powder
residue. [@1] A test later found cocaine, cannabis and MDMA in his system. [@1]

King, of Harwood Close in Colchester, admitted three counts of drug driving.
[@1] In mitigation, his lawyer said he had taken cannabis and cocaine on the
Friday or Saturday before the stop. [@1] The lawyer said the MDMA must have been
cut into the cocaine because King had not taken it knowingly. [@1]

Deputy District Judge Christopher Maynard banned King from driving for two years
and ordered him to carry out 100 hours of unpaid work in the next 18 months.
[@1] He was also ordered to pay £85 in costs and a £114 victim surcharge. [@1]
