Benin's Republican Police seized 375 kilograms of cannabis in Parakou, in the
Borgou department, on the night of 25 to 26 November 2026. [@1] The driver of
the vehicle carrying the drugs fled. [@1] Officers from the second
arrondissement police station found the cargo after searching the car, and the
vehicle and drugs were seized for the investigation. [@1]

The operation took place around 2 a.m. near the carrefour Trois Banques, after a
patrol team led by its unit commander was alerted by intelligence about
suspicious movement around the vehicle. [@2] The driver was picked up by a
motorcyclist who was following him and escaped; the abandoned vehicle was
immobilised and handed to investigators. [@2] The search found 375 slabs, or
plaquettes, of chanvre indien of one kilogramme each, hidden in jute sacks, for
a total of 375 kg. [@2] Investigators also found administrative documents in the
vehicle, including a release notice from Lokossa prison dated 15 January 2026,
which could provide decisive leads in the investigation. [@3]

Searches continued to find the fleeing driver and the motorcyclist described as
his suspected accomplice. [@2]
