Bilpank police arrested one accused person for allegedly cultivating cannabis
illegally. [@1] The arrested man is Dayaram Godar, aged 40 and resident of
Pankheda village. [@1] Police said he had no valid document or permission for
cultivating the plants. [@1] Police were questioning him on remand to find where
he obtained the cannabis seeds and who else was involved. [@1]

Police searched the field in the presence of witnesses and found a total of 28
green cannabis plants. [@1] Police seized the 28 plants, weighing 18 kg 670 g,
at an estimated value of about 72,000 rupees. [@1] Police registered crime
number 739/2026 under Sections 8/20 of the NDPS Act. [@1]

Police said an informant had reported on Friday that Dayaram, son of Mansingh
Godar of Pankheda, had grown cannabis plants around his house and in the field
behind it. [@1] Acting on the tip, a police team from the Birmaval outpost
reached the spot. [@1]
