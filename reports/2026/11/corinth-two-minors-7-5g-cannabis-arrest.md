Two minors were arrested in Corinth on the evening of Monday 16 November 2026 by
officers of the A2 team of the Corinthia Crime Prevention and Suppression Group,
a police unit known by its Greek initials OPKE. [@1] Police said the two jointly
possessed cannabis. [@1] The cannabis the two held weighed 7.5 grams in total
and was seized. [@1] The preliminary investigation into the Corinth case is
being conducted by the Corinth Crime Investigation and Detection Subdirectorate.
[@1]

Less than 24 hours later, on the afternoon of Tuesday 17 November, a separate
case saw officers of OPKE Epirus arrest a 35-year-old woman on the Athens–Patras
national road, where they found and seized 29 narcotic tablets. [@1] The tablets
were described only as narcotic tablets, not as cannabis. [@1] The preliminary
investigation in that case is being conducted by the Sikyon police station. [@1]
