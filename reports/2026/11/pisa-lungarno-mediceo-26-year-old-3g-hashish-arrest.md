Carabinieri arrested a 26-year-old foreign citizen on Lungarno Mediceo in Pisa
for alleged possession of drugs for dealing. [@1,4,5] Radiomobile officers with
colleagues from the Pisa station stopped the man while he was loitering in the
area with a suspicious manner. [@2,4,5] The stop took place on the evening of Monday
16 November 2026 at about 20:40 during territorial controls against dealing in
the historic centre. [@2,4,5]

A personal search found just over 3 grams of hashish and 200 euro in cash, which
investigators considered proceeds of dealing. [@1,4,5] Officers seized both the drug
and the money. [@2,4,5]

On the duty prosecutor's order the man was held in the command's security cells
awaiting a fast-track hearing. [@2,4,5] On the morning of Tuesday 17 November the
arrest was validated. [@2,4,5] He was ordered to report to the judicial police three
times a week. [@3,4,5]
