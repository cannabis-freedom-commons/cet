Police arrested a 43-year-old man known as alias 'Palomo' in flagrante during a
raid on a home in San Sebastián de Mariquita, northern Tolima. [@1] The house
was in the La Concordia neighbourhood. [@1] The operation began at about 6 a.m.
on Sunday 22 November 2026. [@3] Officers seized 80.5 grams of marijuana and a
mobile phone at the house. [@1]

Units of the Seccional de Investigación Criminal (SIJIN), intelligence officers
and the Mariquita police station took part, coordinated by the Cuerpo Técnico de
Investigación (CTI) and supported by the National Army. [@1] Authorities
recorded eight prior judicial annotations for the detainee. [@1] Four
annotations were for drug trafficking, manufacturing or possession, one for
illegal possession of firearms and three for conspiracy to commit crimes for
drug-trafficking purposes. [@1] Those annotations were prior records and were
not, on their own, criminal convictions. [@4]

He was placed at the disposal of the Fiscalía General de la Nación to answer for
alleged trafficking, manufacturing or possession of narcotics. [@1] At the time
he was taking part in a virtual hearing in which a control-of-guarantees judge
would decide his legal situation. [@1] Policía Tolima commander Colonel Ferney
Martín Romero said the force would continue investigations against local drug
trafficking. [@2]
