Two Ashford men were arrested after investigators seized marijuana and recovered
a firearm in Ashford, according to the Houston County Sheriff’s Office. [@1]
Investigators detained Bruce Henry Iverson at the apartment and later located
and detained Marlon Durand Williams. [@1] Both men were arrested and are being
held at the Houston County Jail. [@1]

Investigators with the sheriff’s office’s Vice Division served a search warrant
at 508 Oak St., Apt. 37, with help from Dothan and Ashford police. [@1]
Investigators seized about 9 pounds of marijuana, about 4.08 kg, and recovered a
firearm. [@1] The sheriff’s office estimated the marijuana’s street value at
$18,000. [@1]

The investigation began Friday, Nov. 13, into a shipment of marijuana to an
Ashford apartment. [@1] The sheriff’s office did not specify the charges in its
Monday release. [@1] The sheriff’s office said the investigation is still
ongoing and more arrests are expected. [@1]
