Pelalawan police said they detained 31 suspects in 31 drug cases from October
through the second week of November 2026. [@1] The total included 29 adult men,
one woman and a 17-year-old. [@1] Police reported seizing 16.54 grams of
cannabis during October, alongside methamphetamine and ecstasy. [@2]

The police chief presented the figures at a press conference on 13 November.
[@1] The October cases covered several districts, including Ukui, Pangkalan
Kuras and Pangkalan Kerinci. [@1] The nine suspects detained in the first part
of November included eight adult men and the teenager. [@2]
