The San Luis Potosi state prosecution obtained a binding-over order against Jose
“N” for an offence against health. [@1] State Civil Guard officers detained him
in colonia San Vicente in the municipality of Moctezuma. [@1] A Control judge
found the detention lawful after prosecutors brought him before the court. [@1]
After reviewing the evidence presented at the hearing, the judge ordered him
bound over for trial and imposed justified pretrial detention. [@1]

Officers said he was carrying eleven wrappers containing dry green herb with
characteristics similar to marijuana. [@1] Officers also seized 43 plastic bags
containing a white granular substance similar to the drug known as crystal, and
he was placed at the disposal of the prosecution's Second Delegation. [@1] A
second account confirms the detention in colonia San Vicente with eleven
wrappers of herb similar to marijuana and 43 bags of presumptive crystal, and
the binding-over with lawful detention and justified pretrial detention. [@2]
