The action took place on Saturday at Sonpur station of the East Central Railway,
where the RPF raided an AC coach of the Up Maurya Express and seized 15.497 kg
of ganja. [@1] The RPF inspector said the control room had reported two
unclaimed bags in coach B-5 of Up Maurya Express train number 15027. [@1] When
the train reached platform number 4 at Sonpur station, the RPF team examined the
coach. [@1] The team found one black and one blue rucksack bag near the electric
panel by the western door. [@1] None of the passengers questioned claimed either
bag. [@1]

In the presence of senior officers the team opened both bags and found 15.497 kg
of ganja packed in eight bundles wrapped in brown tape. [@1] The smuggler fled
during the raid. [@1] The RPF estimated the value of the seized ganja at Rs
774,850. [@1]

The RPF handed the seized ganja to the Government Railway Police for further
action. [@1] The GRP station head said a first information report was registered
under the NDPS Act as case number 74/26 and an investigation is under way. [@1]
