Carabinieri arrested a 31-year-old Italian man on Friday evening after a chase
that ended in Cavaria con Premezzo. [@1] The man was driving a Fiat Panda in via
Libertà in Besnate and did not stop at a Carabinieri road checkpoint despite
driving with a revoked licence. [@1] The pursuit crossed Besnate and the Cajello
district of Gallarate before ending in Cavaria con Premezzo. [@1] Officers
stopped the vehicle and blocked the driver, who reportedly resisted. [@1]

A personal and car search found a 103-gram block of hashish, a knife and cash of
just under 32,000 euros. [@1] The man, described as unemployed, was held
overnight in security cells. [@1] A second account confirms the search found a
103-gram hashish block, a knife and about 32,000 euros in cash, and that the
driver’s licence had been revoked. [@2]

On Saturday morning he appeared before the Busto Arsizio court for a fast-track
direttissimo hearing. [@1] After the hearing the judge imposed a residence ban
from Varese province and a daily reporting obligation to judicial police in a
municipality in Milan province. [@1] A third account also says the man spent the
night in a security cell and then appeared before the Busto Arsizio judge for a
direttissimo hearing. [@3]
