Police arrested three people at Weldaad, West Coast Berbice, on Saturday night
after seizing suspected cannabis. [@1] The driver was a 38-year-old taxi
operator from Glasgow Housing Scheme in New Amsterdam, held with two female
passengers aged 43 and 25. [@1] Police described the women as a cook from
Glasgow Housing Scheme and a salesgirl from Cumberland, Canje. [@1]

The Guyana Police Force said the operation took place at about 11:50 pm on
Weldaad Public Road. [@1] Police said the Toyota Allion was heading toward
Georgetown and its driver failed to stop when signalled, prompting a pursuit.
[@1] Officers intercepted the car on Foulis Public Road and had the driver
return it to Weldaad Police Station for a search of the car and its occupants.
[@1] The search found 24 bulky parcels of suspected cannabis in handbags on the
rear seat and in the trunk. [@1]

Police weighed the suspected cannabis in the suspects' presence and recorded
94.90 pounds, about 43.05 kilograms. [@1] Police lodged the suspected cannabis
and the car as investigations continued. [@1] A second account says three people
including a taxi driver were arrested after police seized 94.90 pounds of
suspected marijuana from a car after a brief pursuit on the West Coast Berbice
roadway on Saturday night. [@2]
