Madhya Pradesh police ran a statewide anti-drug drive and reported seizures in
different districts in the three days before 20 November 2026. [@2] Cases were
registered in nine districts: Bhopal, Indore, Jabalpur, Harda, Chhatarpur,
Satna, Guna, Mauganj and Panna. [@2] The drive was carried out on the
instructions of Chief Minister Mohan Yadav and the guidance of Director General
of Police Kailash Makwana. [@2]

In Jabalpur, Bhedaghat police found 136 kg 84 g of ganja in 12 packets wrapped
in khaki tape in the boot of an unregistered Scorpio parked beside a canal near
Chaukital. [@1] The Scorpio was seized and a case was registered against its
driver under the NDPS Act; police were looking for him. [@2] The two Jabalpur
operations were carried out on 17 November 2026, the district police said. [@3]
Ghamapur police in the same city arrested a woman after seizing 1 kg 7 g of
ganja kept in packets in a bag in her cupboard. [@1] In Chhatarpur, Alipura
police arrested two ganja smugglers during a night patrol at the RTO barrier and
seized 20 kg 500 g of ganja from two trolley bags. [@1] Mauganj police found 24
kg 620 g of ganja in a sack lying by the road in the Hanumana police station
area after a Dial-112 call. [@2] No one was arrested in the Mauganj case, in
which police registered a case against an unknown accused. [@2] In Panna's
Ajaygarh area, police found cannabis plants in the accused's courtyard, seized
27 kg 350 g of ganja and arrested him under the NDPS Act. [@2]

Police put the value of drugs and vehicles seized in the drive at about 1.27
crore rupees, a figure that includes MD drugs, smack and brown sugar as well as
ganja. [@2] Police said action against drug trafficking and illegal cultivation
would continue. [@2] They asked people to report any suspicious drug-related
activity to the nearest police station. [@2]
