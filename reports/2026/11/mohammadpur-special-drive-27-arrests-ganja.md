Police in Mohammadpur in the capital Dhaka arrested 27 people in a day-long
special operation across the police station area. [@1] The arrests were made on
Tuesday 17 November 2026 at different places in the thana area, and those held
were later sent to court. [@1] Of the 27, two were held in drug cases, 12 under
anti-terrorism and explosives cases, one over preparation for robbery, one on an
arrest warrant and 11 under the Dhaka Metropolitan Police ordinance. [@1]

Officers seized 54 yaba tablets weighing 5.4 grams, six puria of ganja weighing
12 grams and two unexploded crude bombs, known locally as cocktails. [@1]
Another account listed the same 54 yaba tablets, six puria of ganja and two
unexploded cocktails but gave no weights. [@3]

The deputy commissioner of the Dhaka Metropolitan Police's Tejgaon division
confirmed the arrests on Wednesday 18 November. [@3] The Mohammadpur police
station officer-in-charge confirmed the operation and the arrests. [@2] The
arrests were made by the thana's regular patrol and operation team under the
direction of the division's deputy commissioner and the supervision of its
additional and assistant commissioners and the officer-in-charge. [@3]
