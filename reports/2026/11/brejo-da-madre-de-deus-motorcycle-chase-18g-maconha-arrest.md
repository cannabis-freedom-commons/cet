Military Police officers of the 24th Battalion were on patrol near the first
stage of the Casas da Caixa in the São Domingos district of Brejo da Madre de
Deus at about 21h10 on the night of Monday 16 November 2026. [@1] Officers
spotted two men on a motorcycle who tried to flee on seeing the patrol car,
starting a tactical follow-up. [@1] Police identified the rider carrying the
drugs as José Ramon Fernando da Rocha. [@1] Police identified the other
motorcycle occupant as Macelin Bernardo da Silva. [@1] Both men were taken with
the seized material to the Civil Police station in Santa Cruz do Capibaribe.
[@2]

Officers found about 36 grams of cocaine-like substance in nine portions on José
Ramon Fernando da Rocha. [@2] Officers also seized about 18 grams of
maconha-like substance in four portions. [@2] José Ramon told police the drugs
belonged to a person held in the Santa Cruz do Capibaribe prison system who was
responsible for drug sales in São Domingos district. [@2]

José Ramon gave information about another address where drugs were stored, and
officers went to the indicated home. [@1] At that home a teenager discarded a
quantity of substance in the toilet on seeing officers, so it could not be
recovered, but packaging linked to drug storage was found. [@1] Civil Police
will investigate possible participation by other people in drug sales, and
responsibilities still depend on inquiry. [@1]
