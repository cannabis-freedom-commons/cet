Sonbhadra police announced a reward of 20,000 rupees for the arrest of
absconding ganja smuggler Siba Shankar Behera, named in a September ganja
seizure case. [@1] Superintendent of Police Abhishek Verma made the announcement
for Behera, described as a ganja smuggler from Bhubaneswar in Odisha's Khurdha
district. [@1] Behera has remained absconding since his name surfaced in the
ganja seizure and has not surrendered before a court. [@1]

The reward is attached to a case registered at Shahganj police station under the
NDPS Act against Behera, son of Simanchal Behera of Bhubaneswar. [@2] Police say
Behera is wanted in that case and has not been caught. [@2] The case stems from
the seizure of 625 kg of ganja from a container near the Gauri Shankar temple in
the Shahganj police station area. [@1] On the night of 23 September, Shahganj
police and the SOG stopped a container near a culvert past the temple. [@1]

Dilip Kumar and Santosh Kumar Sahu were arrested as they jumped from the
container, while the other accused fled. [@1] Police had registered the case
against Dilip Kumar, Santosh Kumar Sahu, Shankar of Cuttack, Surendra Jaiswal of
Mirzapur and Sunny Jaiswal of Chunar. [@1] Verma announced the reward to secure
Behera's arrest and directed police teams to gather information about his likely
hideouts and contacts. [@2] The SP asked the public for information about Behera
and promised that the informant's name and address would be kept confidential.
[@1]
