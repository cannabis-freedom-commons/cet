Police arrested two men in La Pampa, Fusagasugá, after they allegedly tried to
avoid a checkpoint. [@1] Officers from GOES Sumapaz, a special operations group,
said the men, aged 25 and 23, abruptly changed direction on seeing police and
were intercepted after a short pursuit. [@1]

During the search, officers found and seized about one Colombian pound, or
roughly half a kilogram, of marijuana described as creepy in the men's
belongings. [@1] According to official sources, the men were detained on
suspicion of offences involving the trafficking, manufacture or illegal
possession of drugs. [@1] The men and the seized material were placed in the
custody of the judicial authorities. [@1]

The operation formed part of a local intervention plan targeting offences
associated with the sale of psychoactive substances. [@1]
