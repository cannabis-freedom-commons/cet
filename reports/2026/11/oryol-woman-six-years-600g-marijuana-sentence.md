On 13 November 2026 the 2nd Western District Military Court found a woman from
Oryol Oblast guilty on all counts. [@2,3] The counts were public justification of
terrorism and illegal storage of narcotics on a large scale. [@2,3] The court
sentenced her to six years in a general-regime penal colony, a fine of 400,000
roubles and a three-year ban on administering websites and using internet
networks. [@1,3] The sentence has not yet entered into force and may be appealed.
[@2,3]

The case was investigated by the Oryol regional department of the Federal
Security Service, which reported it through its press service. [@2,3] During a
search of her home officers found and seized more than 600 grams of marijuana,
which she had grown herself at home. [@1,3] Officers described the find as a home
drug plantation where she cultivated prohibited plants herself. [@2]

Investigators found that she used her Telegram account to publish messages
accessible to a wide circle of users, and an expert examination found signs of
justification of terrorist activity in one statement. [@2] The criminal case was
pursued under Article 205.2(2) for public justification of terrorism using the
internet and Article 228(2) for illegal storage of narcotics on a large scale.
[@1,3]
