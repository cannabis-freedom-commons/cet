Members of the Balarhat and Kashipur border outposts of Border Guard
Bangladesh's Lalmonirhat Battalion (15 BGB) carried out the raids on the night
of Friday 20 November 2026. [@1,3,5] At 8:50 pm the outpost members seized 10 kg 500
g of Indian ganja at Paschim Balatari, in the area of the Balarhat outpost in
Fulbari upazila, Kurigram. [@1,3,5] At 12:40 am a second raid at Dharmapur, in the
area of the Kashipur outpost, recovered 110 yaba tablets and a button mobile
phone. [@1,3,5] In the first of the three drives, at about 3:45 pm on Friday afternoon, a patrol from the Bagbhandar outpost stopped a man at Khayrar Mor in Bhurungamari and found 21 Indian yaba tablets on him. [@3]

BGB put the estimated value of the seized drugs at Tk 77,450. [@1,4] No one was
arrested in the two raids. [@2] The only person arrested in the three drives was the man held with the yaba tablets at Khayrar Mor. [@3] BGB said it was collecting information to
identify those involved in smuggling the drugs and that legal proceedings were
under way. [@2,5] A case was filed against the man at Bhurungamari police station under the Narcotics Control Act. [@4]

Dharla Post reported that the raids were part of the battalion's ongoing
Operation Narcotics Strike. [@2,3] Lt Col Mehedi Imam, commander of 15 BGB, said
intelligence surveillance and patrols had been stepped up against drug
trafficking and smuggling across the border and that anti-drug operations would
continue. [@1,3,5]
