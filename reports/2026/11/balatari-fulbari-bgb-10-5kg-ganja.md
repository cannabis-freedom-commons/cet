Members of the Balarhat and Kashipur border outposts of Border Guard
Bangladesh's Lalmonirhat Battalion (15 BGB) carried out the raids on the night
of Friday 20 November 2026. [@1] At 8:50 pm the outpost members seized 10 kg 500
g of Indian ganja at Paschim Balatari, in the area of the Balarhat outpost in
Fulbari upazila, Kurigram. [@1] At 12:40 am a second raid at Dharmapur, in the
area of the Kashipur outpost, recovered 110 yaba tablets and a button mobile
phone. [@1]

BGB put the estimated value of the seized drugs at Tk 77,450. [@1] No one was
arrested in the two raids. [@2] BGB said it was collecting information to
identify those involved in smuggling the drugs and that legal proceedings were
under way. [@2]

Dharla Post reported that the raids were part of the battalion's ongoing
Operation Narcotics Strike. [@2] Lt Col Mehedi Imam, commander of 15 BGB, said
intelligence surveillance and patrols had been stepped up against drug
trafficking and smuggling across the border and that anti-drug operations would
continue. [@1]
