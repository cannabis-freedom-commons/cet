The Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency's Central Visayas office and police in
Central Visayas burned more than ₱431 million worth of illegal drugs and expired
medicines in Cebu City on 26 November 2026. [@2] The destruction was carried out
at the thermal facility of Cosmopolitan Funeral Homes on Junquera Street. [@3]
Authorities destroyed 1.05 kilograms of marijuana among the confiscated
substances. [@2] The same disposal burned 63.44 kilograms of shabu, which is not
counted in the cannabis figures. [@2] The shabu included 41 kilograms seized in
a joint PDEA and PNP operation in Barangay Mabolo, Cebu City, on 10 October
2026. [@3] The destroyed marijuana and other drugs had been seized in PNP and
PDEA operations across Cebu or turned over from Bohol. [@1] PDEA-7 put the total
value of the destroyed contraband at ₱431,725,191.61. [@2]

The burning went ahead after the courts handling the cases authorized it, with
only samples retained as evidence for ongoing proceedings. [@3] PDEA-7 chemists
collected samples and field-tested the substances before they were moved to the
thermal facility. [@3] PDEA Director General Isagani Nerez and Police Regional
Office 7 director Brigadier General Isagani Fetizanan led the activity. [@3]
Representatives of the Department of Justice, the Public Attorney's Office,
civil society groups and the media witnessed the destruction. [@3]

PDEA said it was the second-largest drug destruction by value since July 2017,
when ₱600 million worth was burned. [@1] PDEA-7 said it was its second
destruction this year, after one at the same facility on 28 July 2026 involving
₱676,659,213.84 worth of drugs. [@3] The disposal was carried out under Republic
Act 9165, the Comprehensive Dangerous Drugs Act of 2002. [@1]
