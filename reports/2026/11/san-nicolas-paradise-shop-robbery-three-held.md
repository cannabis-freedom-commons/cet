Municipal police in San Nicolás de los Garza detained three youths, including a
15-year-old, after allegedly catching them robbing merchandise from a shop
selling cannabis products. [@1,2] Police identified one of the adults as Jorge
Jahir, 20. [@1,2] Police identified the other adult as Daniel Alberto, 18. [@1]
The third detainee is a 15-year-old whose identity was withheld because he is a
minor. [@1] Police said all three are originally from Monterrey. [@1]

During the operation authorities recovered 1,400 marijuana cigarettes, more than
300 jars with cannabis, vaporizers, edibles and accessories. [@1,2] Officers saw
several youths allegedly carrying boxes and bags with merchandise taken from the
Paradise establishment. [@1]

The robbery occurred during the early morning at the Paradise shop at Avenida
Universidad 100 in colonia Anáhuac, where the suspects allegedly damaged a
window to enter. [@1,2] The police action began around 3:00 in the morning after
an alert about a blue Honda CR-V with a theft report. [@1] Officers located the
van inside a nearby underground parking lot. [@1] When they noticed the
officers, the youths tried to flee on foot toward José Manuel Othón street. [@1]
The youths were intercepted after allegedly trying to flee the parking lot where
the van with a theft report was. [@1] The detainees were placed at the disposal
of the Public Ministry with the secured products and the van with a theft
report. [@1,2] Their responsibility must be determined through the corresponding
legal procedure, so detention is not a sentence. [@1] No economic value for the
secured products or the damage to the shop has been reported. [@1]
