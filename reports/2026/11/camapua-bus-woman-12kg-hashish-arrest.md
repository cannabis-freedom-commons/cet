Military Police arrested a woman on the night of 15 November 2026 in Camapuã
after she was caught carrying about 12 kilograms of hashish on an interstate
bus. [@1] Officers singled her out during passenger checks because she behaved
suspiciously and gave contradictory information about her travel destination and
where she would stay. [@1] A female military police officer then carried out the
personal search and found several packages of a substance analogous to hashish
totalling about 12 kilograms. [@1]

The drug, of the DRY type, was hidden against the passenger's body and fixed
with adhesive tape. [@1] The seizure was made by the Military Police during
ostensive patrolling and inspection of a bus travelling from Campo Grande to
Goiânia. [@1] Questioned, the passenger said she had been hired by an individual
to transport the drug to Goiânia bus terminal for delivery to another person.
[@1] She said she would receive R$9,000 for the job after the delivery was
completed. [@1]

The woman was given voice of arrest. [@1] She was first taken for medical care
and then presented with the seized drug at the Civil Police station for the
applicable legal measures. [@1]
