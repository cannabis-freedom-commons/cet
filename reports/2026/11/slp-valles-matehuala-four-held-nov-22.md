The Guardia Civil Estatal, San Luis Potosí's state civil guard, detained four
people in two operations in Ciudad Valles and Matehuala on suspicion of selling
drugs. [@1] In Ciudad Valles officers detained a 20-year-old man and a
24-year-old woman suspected of distributing drugs at sales points in the
municipality's eastern zone. [@2]

Officers there seized bags of marijuana and doses of cristal, a street term for
a methamphetamine-like stimulant, plus a sheet with notes of suspected sales
codes and two handbags. [@2] Pulso reported that the Valles pair were carrying
ten bags of marijuana and at least four doses of cristal. [@3] In Matehuala
officers detained two men aged 27 and 44 who had police records, and seized bags
of marijuana and doses of cristal from them. [@2]

All four detainees and the seized substances and other items were placed at the
disposal of the competent authorities for investigation. [@1] Potosinoticias
reported the operations formed part of surveillance and prevention patrols in
the Huasteca and Altiplano regions. [@2]
