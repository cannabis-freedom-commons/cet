Military police arrested a man and apprehended an adolescent on the morning of
18 November 2026 in Jardim Gramado, São Gabriel do Oeste, on suspicion of drug
trafficking. [@1] The incident was recorded at about 8h during Military Police
patrols. [@1] Officers saw a man enter a residence quickly as the patrol car
approached and found a marijuana-like portion on him during the search. [@1]
During searches in the house the man pointed to another container where officers
found more marijuana-like and skunk-like portions and a precision scale. [@1]
The man said part of the drugs belonged to an adolescent. [@1] Officers went to
the indicated address where the adolescent presented himself and said he owned
the stored drugs. [@1]

Officers seized marijuana and skunk portions, one precision scale and two mobile
phones, and all the material was taken to the Civil Police. [@2] The man was
formally arrested while the adolescent was apprehended on suspicion of a
trafficking-equivalent offence. [@2] Both underwent forensic medical
examinations showing no injuries and were then taken to the São Gabriel do Oeste
Civil Police station with the seized materials. [@1]
