Police in Huraidah district, Hadramout Governorate, detained a 26-year-old man
on suspicion of possessing narcotic substances during a security operation in
the district. [@1]

The police said the detainee was found with about 125 grams of hashish. [@2] The
operation followed intelligence that a man was coming to the district on a
motorcycle, and a patrol set up a checkpoint at the district's general entrance.
[@3] Officers at the checkpoint stopped the man as he arrived and, searching
him, found the hashish, which they estimated at about 125 grams. [@3]

The man was placed in detention and the seized material secured, with the case
to be completed and sent to the Public Prosecution. [@3] Legal measures were
taken against the detainee and the case file was being completed for referral to
the competent authorities. [@1]
