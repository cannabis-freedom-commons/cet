Hampshire police were called at 11.20am on 21 November by the fire service to a
fire at a residential property on Athelstan Road in Southampton. [@1] Officers
helped with a road closure and traffic management while firefighters dealt with
the fire. [@1] After searching the property, officers found a number of cannabis
plants inside the address. [@1] No arrests had been made and officers stayed at
the property to carry out enquiries and keep it secured and safe. [@1]

Athelstan Road was later reopened but a police scene remained at the address.
[@1] The fire badly damaged the house, including a partial collapse of the roof.
[@1] Six fire engines and an aerial ladder platform attended the incident. [@1]
The cause had not been determined and there were no reported injuries. [@1]

More than 20 firefighters dealt with the fire at the scene. [@2] Neighbour
Diljit Singh said the fire had left him really frightened. [@2]
