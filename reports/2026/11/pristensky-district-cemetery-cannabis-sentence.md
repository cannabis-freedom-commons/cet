The Pristensky District Court in Kursk Oblast convicted a 48-year-old local man
on a drug charge. [@1] He fully admitted his guilt in court and repented for
what he had done. [@1] The court sentenced him under Part 1 of Article 228 of
the Russian Criminal Code, applying Article 64, to one year of restriction of
freedom with additional restrictions. [@1]

In August, the man was at a cemetery in Pristensky District when he noticed
seven bushes of wild-growing cannabis on the burial plots of the dead. [@1] He
pulled the bushes from the ground, put them into a cardboard box he had found,
and carried them to his shed. [@1] He kept the cannabis in the shed for his own
consumption. [@1]

An expert examination identified the material as the narcotic drug cannabis
(marijuana) weighing 49.5 grams. [@1] That weight qualifies as a significant
size. [@1]
