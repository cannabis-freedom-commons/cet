Villagers in Outadoura, a village in Koraput district, Odisha, blocked officials
who came to destroy cannabis grown in their paddy field and threw stones at the
tahsildar's car. [@1] The crop was burned after the confrontation ended. [@1]

The village is in the Murkar panchayat of Dasmantpur block. [@1] The team went
on information that cannabis was being cultivated there, led by excise officer
Kush Jal and including police inspector-in-charge Charan Singh, tahsildar Priya
Pushpanjali Behera and revenue inspector Amit Mahanti. [@1] Ganja, the name used
for the crop, is cannabis. [@1]

The officials found the plants growing in the middle of a paddy crop and began
pulling them up and destroying them. [@1] Villagers intervened, blocked the work
and staged a protest. [@1] Stones were thrown at the tahsildar's car while she
was travelling in it. [@1] Staff of the two departments calmed the villagers.
[@1] The officials then set fire to the plants and destroyed them. [@1]
