Two men were arrested on suspicion of drug trafficking inside the Centro de
Abastecimento de Pernambuco (Ceasa), the state's wholesale supply centre, in the
Curado neighbourhood of West Zone Recife on Monday 16 November 2026. [@1]
Officers of the 12th Military Police Battalion (12º BPM), acting on information
from the force's operations centre (Copom) that drugs were being sold at the
site, searched the area, set up a cordon and detained the two men. [@2]

Police said they seized 104 wrappers of marijuana, 24 of cocaine, 15 portions
and four grams of crack and nine wrappers of hashish. [@1] They also collected a
precision scale, R$ 308 in cash and two cell phones. [@1] The suspects and all
the material were taken to the Ceplanc, the capital's police duty centre, where
the appropriate measures were adopted. [@2]

The arrests followed other recent incidents at Ceasa — a man held there on 5
November for breaking into a car and stealing a cell phone, and a double
homicide on 11 November — which the police account describes as distinct and
unconnected. [@1]
