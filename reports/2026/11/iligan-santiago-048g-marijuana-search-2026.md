Police in Iligan City arrested a 37-year-old man, known only as alias "Bem", in
a search-warrant operation in Purok 5, Barangay Santiago at about 2:40 a.m. on
21 November 2026. [@1] He was already on the police list of high-value
individuals. [@1] In the search officers seized 0.48 grams of suspected dried
marijuana leaves. [@1] Police also recovered 56.84 grams of suspected shabu
valued at ₱386,512. [@1] A weighing scale and surgical forceps were confiscated
as drug paraphernalia. [@1]

Intelligence operatives of Iligan City Police Station 1 led the operation, with
the Iligan City Mobile Force Company in support and in coordination with the
Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency (PDEA). [@2]

The Santiago search was one of three anti-drug operations Iligan City police
reported in different barangays on 20 and 21 November 2026. [@1] Together the
three operations yielded an estimated 162.30 grams of suspected shabu worth
₱1,103,640, plus a small quantity of suspected marijuana. [@1]

The man was held in police custody for documentation and the filing of
appropriate charges, and the seized substances were sent for forensic
examination. [@1] Police Regional Office 10 director PBGEN Nestor C. Babagay Jr.
said intelligence-driven operations against illegal drugs would continue. [@1]
