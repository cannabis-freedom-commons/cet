Joyceville Institution lies north of Kingston. [@1] Correctional Service Canada
says the seizure took place on November 23 at the multi-level security federal
institution near Kingston. [@2] It was the second such seizure at the
institution in three days. [@2]

The seized items included tobacco, cannabis concentrate, edged weapons and a
cell phone with accessories. [@1] Items recovered included tobacco, cannabis
concentrate, edged weapons, a cell phone and related accessories. [@2]
Correctional Service Canada put the total estimated institutional value of the
seizure at $159,823. [@2]

CSC says it uses a variety of measures to prevent drugs and other prohibited
items from entering federal institutions, including ion scanners and
drug-detector dogs. [@2] These tools are used to search buildings, personal
property, inmates and visitors. [@2] The agency says preventing contraband from
entering correctional facilities remains a priority in maintaining a safe and
secure environment for inmates, staff and visitors. [@2] CSC also works closely
with police agencies to investigate and prosecute those responsible for
introducing contraband into institutions. [@2]
