Two men were placed under investigation after raids in La Paz's Feria
neighbourhood on suspicion of illegal firearm possession and growing plants for
drug production. [@1] The suspected offences are illegal firearm possession
under article 189 bis of the Argentine Penal Code and cultivation of plants for
drug production under article 5(a) of National Law 23,737. [@1] The two men were
adults and were left subject to the judicial proceedings. [@2] The four raids
were carried out at different homes in the neighbourhood on the morning of
Monday 23 November 2026. [@1]

Officers seized four Cannabis Sativa plants, a .22-calibre pistol and ammunition
of various calibres. [@1] The raids were carried out by the criminal
investigation and intelligence division of the La Paz departmental police
headquarters. [@2]

The operation was carried out in the framework of an investigation into a
property crime. [@2] Police said the measures followed earlier work including
analysis of information, field surveys and security-camera footage. [@1]
Officers from the minors and family-violence station, the radio-command unit and
the special guard also took part, all under the La Paz departmental police
headquarters. [@1] The seized items were placed at the disposal of the judicial
authority and the proceedings continued. [@1]
