Police in Estonia detained a 42-year-old woman near Narva in mid-November 2026
on suspicion of illegally handling 1.2 kg of narcotic substances. [@2] Police
found 1.2 kilograms of narcotic substance on the woman, and a court ordered her
held during the pre-trial investigation. [@4] The woman was stopped in Olgina on
12 November after collecting a large quantity of narcotic substances in Tallinn,
and was detained as a suspect. [@1]

At the detention officers found a substance containing alpha-PVP and amphetamine
on the woman, and a search of her Narva apartment also turned up cannabis and
other narcotic substances. [@2] A Põhjarannik report said a large quantity of
cannabis was among the items confiscated from the woman and published a police
photograph of the seizure. [@3] Police said the exact composition of the
substances would be established by a forensic examination. [@2]

Police said they believed the woman obtained the substances through the Telegram
app and that drug sales were her main source of income. [@1] The case is being
investigated by the Eastern prefecture and led by the Viru district prosecutor's
office. [@2]
