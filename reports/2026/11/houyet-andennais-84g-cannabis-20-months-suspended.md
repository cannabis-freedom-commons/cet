A 33-year-old man from Andenne was sentenced to 20 months in prison with a
four-year probationary suspension by the criminal court. [@1] The suspension was
granted with strict conditions to keep a fixed address, answer court summons,
prove abstinence from alcohol and drugs, avoid drug-related people and places,
follow therapeutic support for addiction and commit no new offence. [@1]

On 30 June 2026 police tried to stop a suspect vehicle on the N911 at Houyet,
but the driver fled at high speed. [@2] He reached 212 km/h in an uninsured car,
without a licence and under the influence of cannabis. [@1] A search of his car
found 33 ecstasy pills, 3.50 grams of cocaine and 8.40 grams of cannabis. [@2]
He told officers the cannabis was for himself, the ecstasy belonged to a friend
and he was to deliver the cocaine from Namur to Givet. [@2]

Investigators found 54 pages of drug-related Snapchat conversations and he was
also prosecuted for acting in association. [@2] The prosecution had requested 40
months in prison. [@1] He had been held in custody since the June stop and
appeared on 22 October on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@2]
