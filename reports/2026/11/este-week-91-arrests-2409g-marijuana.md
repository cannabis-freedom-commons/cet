The DNCD and the National Police detained 91 people in operations in the east of
the country. [@1,2] The interventions ran during the previous week across La
Romana, La Altagracia, Verón and El Seibo. [@1,2] They hit dozens of barrios and
vulnerable sectors where drug sale and distribution points operated. [@1] In La
Altagracia, six searches and 348 operations left 37 people detained. [@1,2] In La
Romana, one search and 418 operations led to 26 arrests. [@1] In Verón, 68
operations and six direct interventions at drug points left 16 people detained.
[@1] In El Seibo, 112 operations and several interventions led to 12 detentions.
[@1]

The week yielded 20,946.4 grams of suspected controlled substances. [@1,2] Of
that, 2,409.3 grams was vegetation presumed to be marijuana, with 17,849.9 grams
of suspected cocaine and 687.2 grams of crack-like rock. [@1,2] La Altagracia
yielded 10,313.8 grams of drugs including 560.4 grams of marijuana. [@1] La
Romana yielded 8,294.3 grams including 803.7 grams of marijuana. [@1] Verón
yielded 1,898.7 grams including 946.9 grams of marijuana. [@1] El Seibo yielded
439.6 grams including 98.3 grams of marijuana. [@1] Officers also took four
motorcycles, 10 scales, 14 cell phones, RD$50,655 and US$10 as
trafficking-related evidence. [@1,2]

Agents, prosecutors and police carried out the seizures. [@1] Authorities said
interdiction operations will stay active in the eastern provinces with close
inter-institutional coordination. [@1]
