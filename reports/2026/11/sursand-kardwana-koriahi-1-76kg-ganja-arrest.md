Sursand police in Sitamarhi district, Bihar, searched a house in Kardwana
Koriahi village and seized 1.76 kg of ganja. [@1] A joint team of Sursand police
and a district intelligence unit (DIU) team from Sitamarhi carried out the raid
on Thursday evening after a tip that ganja was kept and sold from a house in
ward seven. [@2] Police arrested Sanjeet Kumar, the householder, who they said
had allegedly been selling ganja for several years. [@1]

The arrested householder was identified as Sanjeet Kumar, son of the late
Rambhajan Singh. [@2]

Police said the seized ganja had an international market value of about 50,000
rupees. [@2]

Station house officer Lalkishor Gupta said the accused had been in the drug
trade for a long time and that police had found leads about his network and
possible sources, which were being investigated. [@2] Sub-inspector Rakesh
Kumar, the investigating officer, said the seizure was carried out by a team led
by sub-inspector Raghuwar Sah. [@2]

The ganja was sealed, a case was registered under the relevant sections, and
Kumar was sent to judicial custody. [@2]
