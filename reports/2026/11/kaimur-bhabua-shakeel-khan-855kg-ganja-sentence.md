A special NDPS court in Bhabua convicted Shakeel Khan, son of late Vakil Khan of
Biyur village in the Chainpur police-station area, under Section 20(b)(ii)(B) of
the NDPS Act. [@1] The court sentenced him to three years of rigorous
imprisonment. [@1] It also imposed a fine of Rs 25,000. [@1] It ordered an
additional term if he does not pay the fine. [@1]

The case began on 11 April 2012, when Chand station-house officer Inspector Ram
Rahan Singh received information that a man was moving in suspicious
circumstances with ganja. [@1] Police searched the accused and recovered a total
of 8 kg 550 g of ganja from him. [@1] Police registered case number 171/2012 at
the concerned station on 11 April 2012. [@1]

Special public prosecutor for NDPS cases Mithlesh Kumar Singh presented the
prosecution account. [@1] The court found the accused guilty on prosecution
evidence and witness testimony and pronounced the sentence on Thursday, 19
November 2026. [@1]
