Texas Department of Public Safety troopers executed a narcotics search warrant
at an Amarillo residence on Nov. 23, 2026, with 31-year-old Zacharia Jerome
Robertson the only person inside. [@1] Robertson was arrested and was being held
at the Potter County Jail. [@2] Robertson was charged with unlawful possession
of a firearm by a felon. [@1] Robertson was charged with possession of 4 ounces
to 5 pounds of marijuana. [@1] Robertson was charged with possession of a
controlled substance in penalty group 3 weighing 28 to less than 200 grams. [@1]
Robertson was charged with money laundering involving $2,500 to less than
$30,000. [@1] Robertson had been convicted of felony assault, was released from
prison in August 2025 and was discharged from parole on Sept. 19, 2026. [@1]

Troopers found a black shotgun and a black handgun in the residence. [@1]
Troopers found about 3 pounds of marijuana, reported as the cannabis seizure.
[@1] In the same search troopers also found roughly 39 grams of hydrocodone
pills and U.S. currency totaling more than $2,500 but less than $30,000, which
are not counted in the cannabis figures. [@1]

The search was at an apartment near Southeast 16th Avenue and South Dallas
Street in Amarillo. [@2] The arrest report says the residence is about 577 feet
from Humphrey’s Highland Elementary School. [@1]
