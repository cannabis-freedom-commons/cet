Police in Nantes arrested an 18-year-old man late on Friday 13 November 2026 as
he was allegedly about to deliver to inmates of the Nantes remand prison by
drone. [@1] He was presented as a school pupil who said he had agreed to the
delivery to pay off debts after others talked of going after his brother. [@1]
His trial was set for Monday 16 November 2026. [@1] At about 23:30 that Friday a
brigade anticriminalité patrol noticed a stopped Clio on Rue de la Contrie in
the Dervallières district of Nantes in Loire-Atlantique. [@1] The 18-year-old
driver had just obtained his driving licence and was accompanied by two female
friends. [@1]

A smell of cannabis led the officers to search the vehicle. [@1] Inside they
seized 340 grams of cannabis, 880 euros in cash and four telephones, all packed
in two large socks. [@1]

The presence of a drone in the car suggested the trio was preparing a delivery
to the remand prison. [@1] The driver disputed that interpretation and was the
only one of the three placed in police custody. [@1]
