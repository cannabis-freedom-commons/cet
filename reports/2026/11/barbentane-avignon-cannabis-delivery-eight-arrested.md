Gendarmes arrested eight people on 13 November 2026 and dismantled a cannabis
home-delivery network organised from Barbentane. [@1] The operation mobilised
105 gendarmes. [@1] The arrests were carried out by the Graveson territorial
brigade and the Atlas anti-drug cell of the Arles gendarmerie company. [@1] The
network operated in northern Bouches-du-Rhône and southern Vaucluse, reaching as
far as Avignon. [@1]

The investigation began on 30 May 2026, when gendarmes in Graveson received
information about trafficking organised in Barbentane for several months. [@1]
Two men presented as the network's managers bought from suppliers and took
orders through a social network. [@1] Cannabis resin was the main drug sold; it
was stored at the home of a nourrice, a stash keeper, before delivery to
customers' homes. [@1]

Investigators worked for nearly six months under the direction of the Tarascon
public prosecutor's office. [@1] A gendarmerie photograph published with the
account shows the several kilos of cannabis resin the gendarmes took hold of.
