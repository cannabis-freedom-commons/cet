The operation was carried out by military police officers of the ROCAM
motorcycle patrol, attached to the 1st Highway Policing Battalion. [@1] The team
intercepted a rider carrying cannabis tablets on the Rodovia dos Imigrantes at
km 32.5. [@1] The interception occurred on the afternoon of Friday the 13th.
[@1] The team had spotted a Yamaha Ténéré motorcycle travelling on the highway.
[@1] On noticing the officers, the rider swerved sharply, changed lanes and
accelerated to get away, prompting the stop. [@1]

A search of the rider's backpack found half a tablet of a substance analogous to
cannabis. [@1] Police found nine further tablets of the same drug hidden in
compartments of the motorcycle, bringing the total seized to 10.5 kg of
cannabis. [@1]

The rider was arrested in flagrante for drug trafficking. [@1] He and all the
seized material were taken to the 4th Police District in São Bernardo do Campo.
[@1] The case was formally registered there, and the suspect remained in custody
at the disposal of the courts. [@1]
