Kandhamal police in Odisha said they have arrested 271 people so far this year
in a crackdown on inter-state ganja syndicates and seized more than 82 tonnes of
the drug. [@1] Superintendent of Police Ramendra Prasad said 50 cultivators were
also arrested as police destroyed ganja cultivation in the district. [@1]

Of the 271 arrests, 196 were natives of Odisha, while 12 were from Madhya
Pradesh, 11 each from Uttar Pradesh and West Bengal, nine from Bihar, eight from
Maharashtra, seven from Kerala, five each from Chhattisgarh and Telangana, two
each from Punjab, Karnataka and Rajasthan, and one from Tamil Nadu, sources
said. [@1] Police valued the seized ganja at a minimum of ₹82 crore (about 820
million rupees). [@1]

Kandhamal is one of Odisha's primary hotspots for illicit ganja cultivation,
where remote forest cover and hilly terrain conceal crops and temperatures of
around 20–30°C suit plant growth, sources said. [@1] Police said high returns
and outside financing drew local farmers to cultivate ganja on thousands of
acres, with the drug smuggled to states across western, northern, central and
southern India. [@1] So far this year, ganja cultivation has been destroyed on
more than 4,110 acres (about 1,663 hectares), the Superintendent of Police said.
[@1] Police said they are carrying out drone surveillance and aerial
reconnaissance to identify concealed plantations in inaccessible locations. [@1]
The SP said awareness programmes had been held in 52 panchayats to encourage
farmers to switch to crops such as turmeric, millets and mustard, and that
enforcement and the campaign would continue. [@1]
