A 22-year-old man appeared before the Alençon court in Orne on Monday 23
November 2026, facing a list of driving offences. [@1] The court condemned him
for multiple driving offences and also for attempted fraud, with some of the
facts dating from earlier years. [@1] He was given ten months of imprisonment,
four with a suspended probation term, and must serve the firm part wearing an
electronic bracelet. [@1]

On 11 March 2023 police stopped him in Alençon on a scooter without insurance
and he tested positive for cannabis. [@1] Another check on 8 November 2024
produced the same finding and a further one on 16 December 2025 was again
positive. [@1] Police also said he did not hold a brevet de sécurité routière
(BSR), the French road-safety certificate. [@1]

His lawyer defended him as a courageous young man who was doing everything he
could to work. [@1]
