Police from Behara police station in Dobhi, Gaya district, Bihar, raided the
house of Vijay Tanti in Kothwara village and seized 7 kilograms 400 grams of
ganja. [@1] Officers arrested Fulwanti Devi, the wife of Vijay Tanti, at the
house. [@1] The arrest was on the accusation that ganja had been found in the
residence. [@1]

Vijay Tanti, described as the accused, and one of his relatives escaped from the
scene. [@1]
