Police raided Unit 31 of the Buenos Aires Penitentiary Service in southern
Greater Buenos Aires after discovering that four inmates were selling cocaine
and marijuana inside the establishment. [@1] The four inmates implicated are
aged 44, 51, 37 and 42. [@1] Police identified outside accomplices who allegedly
brought the drugs in during visits to the inmates under investigation, using
hiding places and a bunker installed in an emergency neighbourhood under armed
guard. [@1]

Quilmes Departmental Investigations officers seized 189 doses of cocaine and 152
doses of marijuana, plus cannabis-sativa buds and four mobile phones, in
Pavilion No. 11 and the search sector of the prison grounds. [@1] The prison
grounds are located at kilometre 15.5 of Provincial Route 53 in southern Greater
Buenos Aires. [@1]

The case file was opened on 30 September. [@1] The case remains provisionally
classified as criminal association and breach of drugs law No. 23,737, under
prosecutor Roxana Karina Giménez of Decentralised Functional Unit No. 9 for
prison crimes of the local courts. [@1]
