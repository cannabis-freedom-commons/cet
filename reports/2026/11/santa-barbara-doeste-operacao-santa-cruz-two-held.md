Civil Police arrested a 39-year-old man and detained a 17-year-old during an
operation against drug trafficking on the morning of 25 November in the Santa
Cruz neighbourhood of Santa Bárbara d'Oeste. [@1] The action, named Operação
Santa Cruz, was carried out by the Dise drug investigations unit of Americana
with support from the Romu municipal guard unit and a sniffer dog. [@1] Dise de
Americana launched the operation on the morning of 25 November at a residence on
Rua Pedro Álvares Cabral. [@2] Officers also detained a 17-year-old in the same
operation. [@4] A third suspect, 25, was identified as the man who stored the
drugs and supplied another sales point known as 'biqueira do Vale' in the same
neighbourhood, but he was not at the house when the warrant was served. [@1]
Police also indicted the absent 25-year-old in the police inquiry. [@1]

Police searched a house on Rua Pedro Álvares Cabral, in front of the Escola
Municipal Professora Rosa Lee Carr Conti, under a search and seizure warrant.
[@1] A bag of Colombia-type marijuana was found under the mattress where the
adolescent slept, and a sniffer dog located a sealed container buried in the
yard. [@1] Police seized 81 portions of hashish, seven portions of Colombia-type
marijuana and one brick of marijuana, alongside 105 crack rocks, 39 portions of
cocaine and bags of drugs not yet divided for sale. [@1] Officers also collected
about 700 empty wrappers, two precision scales, two mobile phones, handwritten
trafficking accounts and utensils for dividing and packaging the drugs. [@1]

The investigation began after complaints about heavy movement at the house, and
officers monitored the property on different days and times. [@1] Drug orders
were received through a mobile app and deliveries were made on the street in
front of the house. [@1] The case was recorded as drug trafficking, association
for trafficking and corruption of minors, with an aggravating factor for selling
near a school. [@3] The adult was to be sent to the Sumaré public jail and the
adolescent to a Fundação Casa unit in Campinas. [@1]
