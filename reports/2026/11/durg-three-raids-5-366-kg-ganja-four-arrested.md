Durg police arrested four adults and also took two juveniles into custody in the
raids. [@3] The raids were made on 22 November 2026 at separate places in the
Newai, Mohan Nagar and Padmanabhpur police-station areas after informers
reported ganja sales. [@2] In Newai, police found Gopi Rao alias Vicky, 36,
selling ganja from the courtyard of his house near Hamar Clinic in Mauhari
Maroda. [@2] He was sent to the central jail in Durg on judicial remand. [@3] At
the Dhamdha Road vegetable market in Mohan Nagar, the main suspect Rahul Gada
fled when police arrived, while Dhaneshwar Manikpuri, 19, and two juveniles were
caught with 2.028 kg of ganja, ₹2,690 and a mobile phone. [@2] Padmanabhpur
police raided a store room of a house behind the Hanoda petrol pump in
Mansarovar Colony, where ganja was being kept and sold. [@2] Chaitanya Bagh
alias Shivam, 19, and Harish Mugri, 20, were arrested there. [@2]

Officers seized 2.016 kg of ganja, ₹1,300, two mobile phones and a
button-operated sharp-edged weapon from them. [@2] Across the three raids police
seized a total of 5.366 kg of ganja. [@3] They also seized ₹3,990 in cash, three
mobile phones, a sharp weapon and other material. [@3] Police put the value of
the Newai seizure at about ₹65,000. [@2]

The cases were registered under NDPS Act sections 20(b) and 27(a), and the
Padmanabhpur case also under section 25 of the Arms Act. [@3] The two juveniles
are being dealt with under the Juvenile Justice Act. [@2] Dainik Bhaskar
reported that the ganja was kept and sold in a house courtyard, at a vegetable
market and in a house store room. [@1]
