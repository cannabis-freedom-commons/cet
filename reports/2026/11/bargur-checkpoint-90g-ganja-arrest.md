Police at the Bargur police station checkpoint near Anthiyur, in Erode district
of Tamil Nadu, stopped Naveenkumar, 27, on 17 November 2026. [@1] He was the son
of a resident of Mevani, a village near Aththani in the same district, and had
reached Tamil Nadu from Karnataka state the day before. [@1] He was riding a
Hero Pulsar motorcycle when officers checked him at the checkpoint. [@1]

Officers found 90 grams of ganja, the local name for cannabis, on the
motorcycle. [@1] They seized the drug together with the motorcycle and arrested
the rider. [@1]

Naveenkumar was taken to Bhavani, another town in Erode district, and produced
before the judicial magistrate's court there, called the JM court. [@1] The
court remanded him to a branch jail, a sub-jail serving the district, where he
was held pending the case. [@1]
