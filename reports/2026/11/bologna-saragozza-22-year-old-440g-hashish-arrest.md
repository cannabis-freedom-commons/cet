Bologna State Police arrested a 22-year-old Italian man on suspicion of drug
dealing. [@1] The man, born in 2004, was placed at the disposal of the judicial
authorities. [@3] The operation began with a report through YouPol, the police
app that lets citizens report drug dealing anonymously. [@1] The judicial police
squad of the Due Torri San Francesco station ran a surveillance operation around
via Saragozza after the report. [@2] Officers noticed the man walking barefoot
through the streets, an unusual sight that led them to follow him. [@1] He
reached the gardens at the end of via Battaglia and, according to police, handed
a small package to one of two people waiting on scooters. [@1]

Checks on the two suspected buyers were negative, while the 22-year-old had
hallucinogenic mushrooms, a wrapper containing 0.66 grams of hashish and 120
euros in cash. [@1] The Bologna Questura said a search of the man's home found
over 440 grams of hashish, further hallucinogenic mushrooms and equipment for
preparing and packaging doses. [@2] The equipment included a precision scale,
packaging bags, three grinders and a folding knife with a blade stained with a
narcotic substance. [@1] Officers recovered a further 350 euros in cash at the
home, in addition to the 120 euros found at the first stop. [@1]
