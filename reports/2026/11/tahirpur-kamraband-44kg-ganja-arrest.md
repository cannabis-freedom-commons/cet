Police arrested drug dealer Islam Uddin with 44 kg of ganja in Kamraband village
in Tahirpur Upazila, Sunamganj. [@1,2,3] The arrest took place on Sunday night in
Kamraband village in Badaghat Union. [@1,2] Tahirpur Thana Sub-Inspector Sadek
Hossain and accompanying force raided the village on secret information,
detained him with ganja and brought him to Tahirpur Thana. [@1,2,3] When the officers arrived, an associate named Sattar, son of the late Hasim Miah of Sonapur village, and two unidentified others fled. [@4] Police
identified the arrested man as the son of the late Iskandar Fakir of Kamraband
village, North Badaghat Union, and as a member of a local ganja and Indian Nasir
Uddin bidi syndicate. [@1,2,3]

Police filed a case at Tahirpur Thana over the seizure and sent the accused to
court at noon on Monday. [@1,2] Police filed the case under the Narcotics Control Act naming Islam Uddin, the fleeing Sattar and two unidentified others as accused. [@4] Tahirpur Thana officer-in-charge Faruk Ahmed
confirmed the case filing and the court referral. [@1,2,3]

Local residents said Islam Uddin and an influential syndicate of five to seven
people had sold liquor, ganja and yaba wholesale and retail across Tahirpur
Upazila for four to five months under cover of the Indian Nasir Uddin bidi
business. [@1,2,3]
