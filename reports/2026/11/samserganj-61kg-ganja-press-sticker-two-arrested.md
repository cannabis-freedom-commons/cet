Police in Samserganj, in Murshidabad district, stopped a car on national highway
12 at the Jaikrishnapur water-tank crossing on the afternoon of Saturday 21
November 2026. [@1] Sacks recovered from the car held about 61 kg of ganja, and
officers arrested the two men travelling in it. [@1] The car had a ‘press’
sticker fixed to its front. [@1] Investigators believe the sticker was used to
keep police from stopping the vehicle. [@1] The two arrested men were named as
Rasidul Sheikh, 23, of Rasikpur village in Gazole police station area, Malda,
and Nur Hossain, 18, of the Haroa police station area of North 24 Parganas. [@1]

Police said the car used to carry the ganja had also been seized. [@1] A
preliminary police estimate put the market value of the ganja at about Rs 3
lakh. [@2] The search was carried out in the presence of Samserganj block
development officer Samiran Krishna Mandal. [@1] Neither the driver nor his
companion could show a valid press identity card. [@2]

A case was registered against the two under the narcotics law. [@1] Police
suspect the ganja had been brought from Odisha and was being carried to Malda
with several changes of route, to be handed to an unidentified man there. [@1]
Farakka sub-divisional police officer Dipendra Tamang gave the details at a
press conference at Samserganj police station on Saturday night. [@2] Tamang
said police had begun investigating who else was part of the trafficking
network. [@1]

The two were produced in court on Sunday with a police request for seven days of
custody. [@2]
