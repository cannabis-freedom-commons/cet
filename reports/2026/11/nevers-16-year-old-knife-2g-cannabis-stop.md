Police in Nevers stopped a 16-year-old boy on Tuesday 17 November 2026 and said
they found him with a kitchen knife and a small quantity of cannabis. [@1] A
patrol from the brigade anticriminalité (BAC), an anti-crime police unit,
decided to stop the teenager as he was walking along boulevard
Maréchal-de-Lattre-de-Tassigny at about 22:45. [@1] At the time, the streets of
Nevers were calm. [@1]

The police said the 16-year-old was carrying a kitchen knife at his waist. [@1]
He was also in possession of a small pouch containing 2 grams of cannabis. [@1]

After the stop, the teenager was brought to the police station. [@1] He was then
handed over to his legal guardian. [@1] He will be reconvened in the near future
to be interrogated about these facts. [@1]
