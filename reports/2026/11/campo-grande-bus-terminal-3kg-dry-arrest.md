Military police Shock Battalion officers inspected an interstate passenger bus
at the Campo Grande bus terminal on the night of 14 November 2026. [@1] The
canine team used police dog Kinay, which is specialised in detecting narcotic
substances. [@1] After the initial luggage check, officers interviewed
passengers and identified inconsistencies in the account given by one traveller.
[@1] A search of the passenger's hand luggage found six packages containing the
drug. [@1] The woman was arrested and taken to DEPAC/CEPOL, the community
emergency police station, for legal measures. [@1]

Officers seized 3.066 kg of dry marijuana from the passenger's hand luggage.
[@1] The drug was presented to Denar, the specialised narcotics-repression
station. [@1] The woman said she had travelled from Sao Paulo to Dourados to
collect the drug and would carry it onward to Belo Horizonte, Minas Gerais. [@1]
She said she would receive R$1,000 for transporting the drug. [@1]

The case was registered as drug trafficking, with possible interstate
trafficking. [@1] The report describes dry marijuana as dry sift or cannabis
resin concentrate with a much higher market value than conventional marijuana
because of its THC concentration and extraction process. [@1]
