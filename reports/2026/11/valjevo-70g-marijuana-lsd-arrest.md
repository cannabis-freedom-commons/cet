Police in Valjevo, acting on an order from the duty prosecutor at the Higher
Public Prosecutor's Office in Valjevo, arrested L. G., a man born in 1994 from
the city, on suspicion of unauthorised production and putting narcotic drugs
into circulation. [@1] The operation was carried out by officers of Serbia's
Ministry of Internal Affairs in Valjevo. [@2]

A search of the apartment the suspect used found several packages with about 70
grams of plant material suspected to be marijuana, 50 sheets suspected to be LSD
and an electronic scale for precise weighing. [@3] In its own account of the
operation, the Serbian Ministry of Internal Affairs said the search of the
apartment produced several small packages holding about 70 grams of suspected
marijuana, 50 sheets suspected to be LSD and an electronic scale for precise
weighing. [@2]

The man was ordered held for up to 48 hours and was to be brought before the
competent prosecutor with a criminal complaint. [@1]
