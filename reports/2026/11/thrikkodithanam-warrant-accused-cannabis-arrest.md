Thrikkodithanam police together with the district police chief's anti-drug squad
arrested 28-year-old Jithin Mohan of Pallikkamattam house at Mamood in
Changanassery in Kottayam after catching him with cannabis while he was
absconding in a warrant case. [@1] The examination that led to the arrest was
conducted under the leadership of Thrikkodithanam station house officer
Inspector M. J. Arun. [@1]

Police said Jithin was an accused in several criminal cases, including a 2023
case at Mamood in which he was accused of trying to kill youths by striking
their heads with a soda bottle and of failing to appear in court after coming
out on bail. [@1] Before his capture he had been hiding in the Thiruvalla area
while remaining absent from the court proceedings in that earlier case. [@1]
Officers caught him during a joint search carried out after receiving secret
information about his whereabouts in that hiding place. [@1]

After the arrest police produced him in court, which ordered his remand in
custody pending further proceedings in the pending cases. [@1]
