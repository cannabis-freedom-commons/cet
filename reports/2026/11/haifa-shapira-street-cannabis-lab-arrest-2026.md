Police from the Zevulun station in Haifa arrested Daniel Romanov, a 24-year-old
from Kiryat Yam, on suspicion of involvement in a drugs laboratory at 11 Shapira
Street, Haifa, on 5 November. [@1] The later account described the same case as involving growing more than 120 kg of cannabis. [@2] Police accuse him of cultivating and
producing drugs, possessing drugs not for personal use, conspiracy to commit a
crime and obstructing a police officer in the performance of his duty. [@1]

The police asked Haifa Magistrate's Court to extend his detention by eight days,
and a second suspect is involved in the case. [@1] His lawyer, Gal Dahan, agreed
with the police representative on an extension of four days only. [@1]

Judge Or Lerner gave the agreement effect and extended his detention until 9
November. [@1] She clarified that the feasibility check would not bind the court when it later considers detention until the end of proceedings. [@2] She extended his detention until 24 November, and electronic-monitoring officers had already visited the grandmother's house. [@2] Judge Kantor decided not to wait for an indictment and ordered that electronic monitoring at his grandmother's home be examined now, although the time to order release had not yet come. [@2] His lawyer Gal Dahan proposed house arrest at his grandmother's house under family supervision, noting that he is a young man with no prior convictions. [@2] On 19 November the Krayot Magistrate's Court held a further detention hearing before Judge Orit Kantor after several extensions since his arrest early in the month. [@2]
