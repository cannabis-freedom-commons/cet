Police in Russia's Primorsky Krai reported the results of the 2026 'Mak'
operation, the region's annual campaign against drug crops. [@1] Officers found
111 illegally grown cannabis plantations and seized 48,766 cannabis plants from
them. [@2] The largest single plantation was at Kiparisovo-2 in Nadezhdinsky
district, where police found 21,814 cannabis plants that a local man was
growing. [@2]

Officers destroyed 86 patches of wild Manchurian cannabis covering more than
42.2 hectares. [@2] In Khankaisky district, several wild cannabis patches
covering 14 hectares were found near the villages of Novoselishche, Melgunovka
and Pervomayskoye. [@2] The operation took 513 kg of natural drugs out of
circulation, including marijuana, hash oil, heroin and poppy straw. [@2] It also
produced 459 drug-related crimes, covering cultivation, sale, storage and
transport of drugs. [@2] Officers wrote 262 administrative protocols against
people involved in the drug trade. [@1]

The regional department for coordination of law-enforcement activity said the
operation targeted illegal plantations, the people who grow them, transport
channels and drug caches. [@2] VladNews reported the same 111 cannabis
plantations and 86 wild patches destroyed, and more than 500 kg of banned
substances taken out of circulation. [@1]
