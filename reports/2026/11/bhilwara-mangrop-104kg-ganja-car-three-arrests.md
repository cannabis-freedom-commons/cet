Three people were arrested and 104 kilograms of illegal ganja were seized from a
car near the Guwari nala, or drain, in the Mangrop police station area of
Bhilwara district, Rajasthan. [@1] The operation was carried out jointly by the
Mangrop police station and the district special team, or DST, which detained the
three suspects found in the car. [@1] The haul was described as doda chura, a
cannabis preparation also called ganja. [@1]

A secret chamber had been built into the car's dicky, the Hindi word for its
boot, to carry the drug for trafficking. [@1] The headline says the illegal
substance was found in two kattas in the car. [@1]
