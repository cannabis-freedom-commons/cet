The Guardia Civil Estatal, San Luis Potosí's state civil guard, detained four
people described as priority targets in the metropolitan area on suspicion of
crimes against health and robberies of commercial establishments. [@1] In
intelligence operations in Villa de Pozos, officers detained Noé "N", 27, whom
they suspected of retail drug dealing in neighbourhoods of the eastern sector.
[@1] During the intervention they secured 30 bags of dried herb with
characteristics similar to marijuana and 36 doses of a granular substance
similar to crystal. [@1]

In colonia General I. Martínez, José "N", 40, was detained in possession of
three doses of marijuana. [@1] Police also identified José as a priority target
over suspected violent robberies and intrusions at convenience stores in the
southern part of the capital, and intelligence linked him to four robberies
committed during the previous month. [@1]

In colonia El Mezquital, Alan "N", 21, and María "N", 31, were detained as
suspected repeat offenders in shoplifting robberies of pharmacies, convenience
stores and department stores in the northern capital and the eastern
metropolitan area. [@1] Officers secured goods valued at about 700 pesos from
them, along with a 2020 Nissan Versa used as a taxi. [@1] The detainees and the
seized items were placed at the disposal of the competent authorities for the
corresponding investigations. [@1] The state security secretariat said it would
maintain surveillance and prevention operations in the state's four regions to
combat crime. [@1]
