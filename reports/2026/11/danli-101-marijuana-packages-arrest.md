Officers from the Departmental Police Unit No. 7 (UDEP-7) detained a man in
Danlí, El Paraíso, on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@2] The national police's
Facebook post said the patrols led to the detention of a 51-year-old man
suspected of drug trafficking. [@1] The operation was carried out in the barrio
Abajo district of Danlí by officers patrolling the area. [@2] The man was 51
years old, originally from Tegucigalpa in Francisco Morazán and living in the
barrio El Carmelo. [@2]

Officers seized 101 transparent plastic wrappings containing dried herb
suspected to be marijuana and 320 lempiras in various denominations. [@2] The
Facebook announcement described the haul as 101 packages of marijuana. [@1]

The police statement said the arrest involved the community prevention and
security directorate, the border police services directorate, the investigation
police, the tactical special operations group and the anti-extortion division,
with support from the armed forces. [@2] The investigation police said its
agents carried out the operation through tracking and surveillance. [@3] After
his rights were read, the man and the seized evidence were to be handed to the
relevant authorities to determine his responsibility. [@2] The national police
repeated the same announcement on its X account. [@4]
