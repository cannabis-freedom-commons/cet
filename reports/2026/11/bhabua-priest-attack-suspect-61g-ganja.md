Bhabua police arrested Imran Alam, also known as Raju Miya, a Ward 15 resident
of Bhabua and the son of Ghulam Ashraf, in the case of an attack on a priest.
[@1] Kaimur police announced the arrest and seizure on Friday 20 November 2026.
[@1] Station house officer Awadhesh Kumar said Alam had made a life-threatening
attack and beaten a priest near the Khaki Gosain temple, and that police had
registered an FIR and were searching for him when a tip led to the arrest that
Friday. [@1]

A search after the arrest recovered 61.41 grams of ganja, 600 rupees in cash and
a mobile phone from him, police said. [@1] After completing the required legal
steps, police began the process of sending him to judicial custody. [@1]

That account said the 61.41 grams of ganja, 600 rupees and a mobile phone were
found in a search of his house. [@2]
