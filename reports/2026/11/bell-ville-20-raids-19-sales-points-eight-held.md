Officers of the Fuerza Policial Antinarcotráfico, Córdoba province's
anti-narcotics police force, carried out 20 raids in the city of Bell Ville in
an investigation directed by the Ministerio Público Fiscal. [@2] The searches
were concentrated in the south-east of the city and dismantled 19 drug sales
points and one place used to store drugs. [@2] Eight people were detained, six
men and two women, all described as suspected narcomenudistas, small-scale drug
dealers. [@1]

Investigators and narcotics detector dogs searched the homes. [@2] Officers
seized 21 cannabis sativa plants and one dose of marijuana, while the 946 doses
of cocaine found in the same raids are excluded from the cannabis figures here.
[@1] They also seized 2,006,400 pesos and 200 dollars in cash, a car and two
motorcycles. [@1]

The Fiscalía de Lucha contra el Narcotráfico de Bell Ville ordered the eight
detainees transferred to judicial headquarters for infringement of the National
Narcotics Law. [@1]
