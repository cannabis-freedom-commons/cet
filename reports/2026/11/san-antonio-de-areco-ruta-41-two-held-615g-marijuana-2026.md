Road safety police in San Antonio de Areco stopped a black Fiat Cronos carrying
two people during a night vehicle interception on Provincial Route 41 at
kilometre 278 on 16 November 2026. [@1] Inside a backpack in the car officers
found 231 grams of marijuana, 192 grams of cocaine hydrochloride and two mobile
phones. [@1] The phones were seized as elements of interest for the drug case.
[@2] Both occupants were immediately detained for alleged breach of Narcotics
Law 23.737 and placed at the disposal of the Federal Court of Mercedes. [@2]

On 17 November investigators carried out simultaneous searches at the detainees’
homes based on the roadside evidence and the seized phones. [@1] In the homes
officers seized another 367 grams of marijuana in plant state among leaves and
stems, plus 17 grams divided into nine dose packets. [@1] The nine packets were
described as allegedly ready for retail sale. [@2] Both detainees were then
transferred under custody to the Mercedes federal court for a first indagatoria
hearing while the federal case continued. [@2]
