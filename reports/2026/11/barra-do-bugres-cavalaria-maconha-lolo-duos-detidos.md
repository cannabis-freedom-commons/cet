Military police in Barra do Bugres, Mato Grosso, arrested a 27-year-old man and
apprehended a 15-year-old adolescent on the night of 16 November 2026 on
suspicion of association for drug trafficking. [@1] The man was spotted during a
motorised patrol of the Alvorecer neighbourhood and tried to flee when he saw
the Cavalry unit approaching. [@1] Officers intercepted him and found portions
of cocaine in his pockets. [@1] He confessed to selling and delivering drugs in
the area and indicated that the rest was hidden at his residence. [@1]

At the house, officers found a large portion of cannabis, other portions of
cocaine, five bottles of loló, a precision scale and material for storing the
drugs. [@1] The man said a sixth bottle of loló had been handed to a 15-year-old
and took officers to the adolescent's address. [@1] The adolescent confirmed
receiving a bottle of loló and other drug portions meant for sale. [@3] With the
adolescent's mother's authorisation, officers searched the home and found the
bottle of loló and portions of cocaine scattered in different parts of the
house. [@1] In total, the operation seized six litres of loló divided into six
bottles, along with cocaine, cannabis, a precision scale and storage material.
[@2]

Both were taken to the police station in Barra do Bugres to register the
incident. [@3] The case, registered as suspected association for drug
trafficking, is to be investigated by the Civil Police. [@2] Because one suspect
is a minor, the proceedings must follow the rules of the Statute of the Child
and Adolescent (ECA). [@3]
