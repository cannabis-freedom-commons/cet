Police in Vilyuysky district detained three suspects and placed them in custody
in a criminal case over drug sales by an organised group. [@1] The three are
local residents of the district, Ulus.Media reported. [@4] The Vilyuysky
district prosecutor's office ruled that the opening of the case by the district
police investigation department was lawful. [@2] Investigators say a resident of
Vilyuysk set up the organised group, which had been selling drugs in the
district for a long time. [@1]

Law enforcement in the district stopped the group's activity, the Yakutia
edition of Komsomolskaya Pravda reported. [@3] Officers seized 110 grams of
hashish during operational measures. [@1] The case was opened under part 4 of
article 228.1 of the Russian Criminal Code, which covers illegal sale of
narcotic drugs by an organised group in a large amount. [@1]

The prosecutor's office of the Sakha Republic (Yakutia) announced the case
through its press service. [@1] Investigative measures are continuing, and the
district prosecutor's office is supervising the investigation. [@4]
