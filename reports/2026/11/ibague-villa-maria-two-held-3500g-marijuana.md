Two men, aged 25 and 30, were arrested in the barrio Villa María, in comuna 11
of Ibagué, while they were travelling on a motorcycle with 3,500 grams of
marijuana, the police said. [@2] Officers of the CAI Variante, a neighbourhood
police post, stopped them during a patrol after they judged the men's attitude
suspicious and searched the occupants. [@1] The Metropolitan Police of Ibagué
said the men were moving through that sector of the Tolima capital on the
motorcycle when the officers decided to search them. [@4]

The marijuana was in a basket on the rear of the motorcycle. [@1] During the
same procedure officers also seized a mobile phone and 1,802,000 pesos in cash.
[@1] Major Yuleixy Andrade Fandiño, commander of the Centro police station, said
police controls against drug trafficking would continue in different parts of
the city. [@2] The commander said the arrest confirmed the reach of those
controls across sectors of Ibagué. [@3]

The two men and the seized items were left with the Attorney General's Office,
which will take the judicial case forward. [@1] Police did not say what charges
would be brought. [@1]
