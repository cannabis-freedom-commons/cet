Police in Ennore, in north Chennai, arrested Ganesan, 25, after ganja was found
hidden under a mattress in a flat in the Ennore tsunami housing colony. [@1]
More than 50 officers led by Ennore Assistant Commissioner Venkat Kumar carried
out the surprise search at noon on 20 November 2026. [@1]

Officers found 4.2 kilograms of ganja in a flat on the fourth floor of the 79th
block, hidden under the mattress and inside a toy. [@1] Police seized the 4.2
kilograms of ganja and put its value at 45,000 rupees. [@1]

In another flat, in the 133rd block, officers found 20 packets of Hans and
arrested a 49-year-old woman, Padmavathi. [@1] Both were produced before a court
after questioning and sent to prison. [@1]
