In Fuengirola, a 33-year-old man sought by the German authorities for armed
robberies, kidnappings and drug offences was remanded in custody pending
extradition. [@1] National Police officers arrested a 33-year-old man with
German and Turkish nationality in Fuengirola as a fugitive wanted by the German
authorities. [@2] Police described him as the suspected leader of a gang
involved in drug trafficking, mainly cocaine and cannabis, with a base of
operations in Spain. [@2] Police said his role was to link suppliers, buyers and
couriers, handling the delivery of drugs and cash and supplying cars fitted with
hidden compartments. [@2]

German authorities had issued three European arrest warrants against him in
October over two armed robberies, seven kidnappings and four drug-trafficking
offences. [@3] According to the German authorities, he fled Germany in autumn
2025 to avoid serving a sentence. [@2] At the arrest he carried a Swedish
passport and driving licence bearing his photograph but a different identity,
although police say he has no link to Sweden. [@3] He was separately detained on
suspicion of document forgery over the Swedish documents. [@2]

Officers from the National Police Costa del Sol drugs and organised crime unit
tracked him down in Fuengirola last weekend after extensive surveillance and
European police cooperation. [@3] He appeared before Spain’s National Court on
the German warrants and separately before a Fuengirola court on the suspected
forgery. [@3] A court ordered him remanded in custody pending extradition to
Germany, where he faces outstanding sentences exceeding 25 years. [@2]
