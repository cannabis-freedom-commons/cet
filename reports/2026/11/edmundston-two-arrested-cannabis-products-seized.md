The Edmundston police force arrested a 40-year-old man and a 27-year-old woman
in connection with an investigation into drug trafficking. [@1] Members of the
force's patrol section carried out the arrests, the force said in a statement
announcing the operation. [@2]

During the intervention officers searched a vehicle and seized a significant
quantity of cannabis products. [@2] They also seized other illicit substances,
prescription medications, equipment associated with the sale and consumption of
drugs, weapons and imitation weapons, ammunition, a sum of money and contraband
cigarettes. [@1]

The two could face several charges under the Controlled Drugs and Substances Act
and the Criminal Code, and the investigation continues. [@2] The force said it
was continuing its efforts against drug trafficking and illegal possession of
weapons in the community, that public safety remained a priority and that it
worked closely with citizens and law-enforcement partners. [@2] Police asked
anyone with information about criminal activity to contact the Edmundston police
force. [@1]
