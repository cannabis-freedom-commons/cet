A man was arrested for drug trafficking on the morning of Sunday 15 November
2026 on PRC-280 in Palmas in southern Paraná. [@1,2] The Military Police seized 22
kilograms of marijuana that was being transported in the automobile stopped in
the operation. [@1]

The Rádio Patrulha team had stopped the same car on an earlier date, when the
driver said informally that he was heading to a border region to acquire a load
of drugs. [@1,2] After further inquiries indicated the car would be returning on
Sunday morning, officers patrolling PRC-280 saw it travelling at high speed and
stopped it at about 9:10 a.m. [@1,2]

During the vehicle inspection, officers located a large quantity of marijuana on
the floor behind the driver's seat. [@1,2] Police said the load found in the car
totalled approximately 22 kilograms of marijuana. [@1,2] Police estimated the seizure represented a loss of R$50,000 to organised crime. [@2] The driver received voz
de prisão and was taken with the seized drug to the 12ª Central Regional de
Flagrantes. [@1] The driver was taken with the seized drug, the seized vehicle and a seized cell phone to the 12ª Central Regional de Flagrantes. [@2]
