State investigators detained Alan ‘N’ and Uriel ‘N’ in the first of two searched
houses in San Pablo Xochimehuacan, in the city of Puebla. [@1] The Puebla State
Prosecutor's Office said it executed two search warrants at properties allegedly
used for drug sale and distribution in that neighbourhood. [@1] The operations
were carried out with the Defence Ministry, the National Guard and the State
Police, and led to the detention of two men and the seizure of suspected
narcotics, a firearm and other items. [@1] Both searches took place in homes on
calle Mexicano identified as possible drug-selling points, where State
Investigation Agency staff executed the judicial orders with support from the
participating institutions. [@1]

In the first house officers found 63 plastic bags holding granular substance
resembling crystal and 29 bags holding dried green herb resembling marijuana.
[@1] In the same house officers also secured a .22-calibre pistol, five live
cartridges of the same calibre and a gram scale, handed to the ministerial
authority for analysis and case-file integration. [@1] In the second house on
the same street officers found another 50 bags of crystal-like granular
substance, 21 bags of dried green herb resembling marijuana and a gram scale.
[@1]

Together the two actions secured 113 bags of possible crystal, 50 bags of
probable marijuana, two gram scales, a firearm and five cartridges. [@1] Both
properties were secured and the two detainees were placed at the disposal of the
Public Ministry agent, who will determine their legal situation. [@1]
