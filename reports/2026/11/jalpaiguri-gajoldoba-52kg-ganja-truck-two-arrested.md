A joint action by the Special Task Force and police recovered 52 kg of ganja
from a truck and two people were arrested. [@1] The team comprised the Special
Task Force and the Gajoldoba outpost police under Bhorer Alo police station.
[@2] Two people were arrested in the case. [@2] The arrested men were named as
Shubhajit Das and Mangaldeep Bhadra. [@2] Both men are residents of Tufanganj in
Cooch Behar district. [@2] Police said the two would be produced in Jalpaiguri
district court on Friday. [@2]

The joint operation took place on Thursday evening in the Gajoldoba area,
targeting a four-wheeler goods truck. [@2] Officers stopped a suspicious goods
truck and questioned its driver. [@2] Finding contradictions in the driver's
answers, officers searched the truck. [@2] Inside the truck they found packet
after packet containing ganja. [@2] Police said the total seizure was about 52
kg of ganja. [@2]

Police had begun monitoring the area after information that a large consignment
of narcotics would be smuggled via Gajoldoba. [@2] Police initially assessed
that the consignment was being smuggled from Cooch Behar toward Siliguri. [@2]
