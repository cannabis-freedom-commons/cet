Police destroyed illegally cultivated ganja plants in the Dumbriguda-Nuvvuphut
forest area belonging to Hatbari panchayat of Nandapur samiti. [@1] The details
were attributed to the Padua police station officer. [@1] After receiving
information about illegal ganja cultivation in that forest area, police and
excise officials jointly carried out inspections. [@1]

During the inspection, police identified 31,500 ganja plants being cultivated
over 20 acres. [@1] The reported 20 acres correspond to about 8.09 hectares.
[@1] The plants were destroyed under the leadership of Excise Inspector Sudhir
Kumar Sahoo. [@1]

Officials said search operations had been launched for the accused. [@1] The
account was published on 17 November 2026. [@1]
