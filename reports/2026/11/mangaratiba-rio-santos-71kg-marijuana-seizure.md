Federal highway police seized 71 kilograms of marijuana from a car during a
check on the Rio-Santos highway (BR-101) in Mangaratiba, Rio de Janeiro, on the
morning of 25 November 2026. [@3] The team stopped the car while checking the
mandatory equipment of vehicles. [@2] Inside the vehicle officers found 51
tabletes, or tablets, of marijuana that weighed 71 kilograms in total. [@2]

The driver was detained at the scene and stayed silent when asked where the
drugs came from and where they were going. [@2] He was taken first to the PRF
post and then to the 165ª Delegacia de Polícia in Mangaratiba, where the
occurrence was recorded. [@1] Police said they did not rule out that the drugs
were meant for communities in the Costa Verde region. [@1] The PRF had not
confirmed by publication whether the drugs were to be distributed in
municipalities in the region or sent to other states. [@1] The man remained at
the disposal of the courts. [@1]

The seizure took place on BR-101, one of the main road links on the fluminense
coast, which crosses municipalities of the Costa Verde. [@1] The stretch between
Mangaratiba and Paraty is described as a route used by criminals to supply the
Costa Verde, other states and municipalities in southern Rio de Janeiro state.
[@1]
