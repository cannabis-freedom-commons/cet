A Klaipėda District Court convicted a 46-year-old Klaipėda man, identified only
as Ž. Ž., of setting up an organized criminal group to systematically move very
large quantities of narcotics between European Union states. [@1] The court
announced the guilty verdict on 13 November 2026 and sentenced him to 13 years
in prison. [@1]

Lithuania's Criminal Police Bureau began the investigation in spring 2021 after
receiving information from Sweden about a lorry stopped with a large drug cargo.
[@1] Foreign customs officers found more than 308 kg of cannabis resin (hashish)
in hidden compartments in the lorry and its semi-trailer. [@2] The group, which
the convicted man had assembled with accomplices in other countries at the end
of 2019, was to produce and repeatedly traffic large quantities of cocaine and
hashish between EU states. [@1] Separately, between late 2020 and March 2021 he
organized at least 14 shipments of about 700 kg of cocaine from the Netherlands
to France. [@1] French officers detained him in March 2021 during the last of
those shipments, 70 kg of cocaine, for which more than 1.2 million euros were
handed over. [@1]

Charges in the same case were also brought against a Polish citizen living in
Klaipėda, but the proceedings against him were closed after his death. [@1] The
state prosecution was presented in court by a prosecutor from the Klaipėda
District Prosecutor's Office's Organized Crime and Corruption Investigation
Division. [@1] The pre-trial investigation was carried out by Lithuanian
Criminal Police Bureau officers and supervised by a prosecutor of the General
Prosecutor's Office's Organized Crime and Corruption Investigation Department.
[@1] Investigations were also opened in France and Sweden, and some members of
the group have already been sentenced. [@1] The case was detected through
cooperation between Lithuanian prosecutors and police, Europol, Eurojust and law
enforcement in France, the Netherlands, Sweden and elsewhere. [@1] The verdict
can be appealed to the Lithuanian Court of Appeal within 20 days of its
announcement. [@1]
