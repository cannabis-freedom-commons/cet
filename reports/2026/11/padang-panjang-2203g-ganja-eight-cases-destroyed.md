The Padang Panjang prosecutor's office destroyed evidence from cases with final
legal force on Thursday 19 November 2026. [@1] The destruction was held in the
yard of the Padang Panjang prosecutor's office and covered eight cases whose
evidence was ordered forfeited and destroyed by court decision. [@1] Among the
evidence was 22.03 grams gross of ganja from one narcotics case, ordered
destroyed under a November 2026 court decision. [@1] Chief prosecutor H. Adhi
Setyo Prabowo said both kinds of narcotics case involved violations of Article
114 of the 2009 Narcotics Law. [@1]

He said 3.68 grams gross of sabu, or methamphetamine, from three narcotics cases
was destroyed in the same exercise. [@1] Other items destroyed included
clothing, scales and bongs. [@1] Head of asset recovery and evidence management
Mega Nanda Beniv Fitria said this was the office's fourth destruction exercise
of 2026 and closed the year, covering decisions from September to November 2026.
[@1] The ceremony was attended by prosecutors, the city health office, the
Padang Panjang prison, police narcotics and criminal units and the district
court. [@1]
