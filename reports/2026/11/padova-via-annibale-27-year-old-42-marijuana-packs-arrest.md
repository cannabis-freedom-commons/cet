State Police patrols arrested a 27-year-old Nigerian man in via Annibale da
Bassano in Padova at about 20:15 on Sunday 15 November 2026. [@1] The man was
riding a bicycle and sped up when he saw the police car, fleeing along the
pavement toward via Dalmazia before officers caught him after a short chase.
[@2]

Officers said they noticed a strong smell of marijuana and found two cellophane
wraps of green herbal substance in his jacket. [@1] A search found a shoulder
bag hidden under his clothing containing another 40 wraps of the same substance.
[@1] Police said tests confirmed the substance was marijuana, with 42 packages
seized weighing 45.63 grams in total, split as 2.05 grams in the first two wraps
and 43.58 grams in the other 40. [@2] Officers also found 55 euros in cash in
the same bag, in mixed notes whose origin the man could not explain. [@2]

The 27-year-old, described as already known for drug dealing, was arrested for
possession of drugs for dealing and placed at the disposal of the courts pending
a fast-track hearing. [@2] Padova police chief Marco Odorisio referred his case
to the immigration office for assessment of whether his refugee status and
international-protection residence permit should be revoked. [@2] The arrest was
one of two that evening during extraordinary weekend checks in Padova and the
Arcella district that police said led to 94 people identified and three reported
for other offences. [@3]
