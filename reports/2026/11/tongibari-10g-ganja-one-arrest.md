Police arrested 43-year-old Kefayet Ullah Khan Tuhin Khan in a special operation
in Tongibari, Munshiganj on 16 November 2026. [@1] Members of the Dighirpar
police investigation centre of Tongibari police station conducted the operation
on Monday 16 November. [@1]

He was arrested on the paved road on the west side of Hanif Malot's Doctor
Pharmacy near Dighirpar bus stand. [@1] Officers recovered 34 yaba pills and 10
grams of ganja from him. [@1] The arrested man is a resident of Uttar Murchor in
Dighirpar Union of Tongibari upazila. [@1] Police said a CDMS check found three
previous drug cases against him. [@1]

A regular case under the Narcotics Control Act was filed against him at
Tongibari police station. [@1] Police said he was sent to court later on Monday.
