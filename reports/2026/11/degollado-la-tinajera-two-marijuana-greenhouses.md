Under Mexico's National Public Security Strategy, the National Guard and the
Mexican Army, together with FEMDO of the Attorney General's Office and the
Criminal Investigation Agency of the FGR, executed a technical investigation
order at a property in the La Tinajera locality of the municipality of
Degollado, Jalisco. [@1,4] On 13 November 2026 the Mexican Army, the National Guard and the FGR disabled two laboratories for producing synthetic drugs and two marijuana greenhouses in Degollado, Jalisco. [@7,9,12,13,14,17,18] The property was operated by a criminal group led by
Heraclio 'N', alias Tío Laco, identified as the regional boss of the Jalisco New
Generation Cartel in the area. [@1,4,8,9,10,12,17,18] The installations were operated by a group led by Heraclio Guerrero Martínez, alias Tío Lako, described as a regional CJNG chief with operations in Jalisco and Michoacán. [@6,11]

At the site the authorities seized firearms, cartridges, two synthetic-drug
production centers and two marijuana greenhouses. [@1,4,5,8,9,11,12] One marijuana greenhouse measured about 3,250 square metres and the other about 8,400 square metres. [@6,10,13,14,15,16,17,18] Machinery, tools and
various items recovered on the property were used to make methamphetamine and to
process and extract high-purity cannabidiol (CBD) and tetrahydrocannabinol (THC)
from marijuana. [@2,4,6,9,10,17,18]

Everything seized was handed over to the Federal Attorney General's Office,
which is continuing its investigations and forensic work to determine the legal
situation arising from the operation. [@1,4] Everything seized was placed at the disposal of the FGR at its headquarters in Mexico City to continue investigations and expert work. [@6,10,13,14,15,16,17,18] No arrests or charges were reported
in this operation; the authorities said the legal situation of the people
involved is still to be determined. [@3,18]
