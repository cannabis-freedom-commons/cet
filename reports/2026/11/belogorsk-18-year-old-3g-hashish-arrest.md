Transport police in Belogorsk detained an 18-year-old resident of the village of
Vozzhaevka. [@1] A search of the young man found three packages containing
drugs. [@1] The Transbaikal Linear Directorate of the Interior Ministry for
Transport said the dark-green substance was hashish with a total mass of about 3
grams. [@1]

A medical examination showed he was in a state of drug intoxication. [@1] Police
opened a criminal case under Part 1 of Article 228 of the Russian Criminal Code,
for illegal acquisition and storage of narcotic drugs without intent to sell.
[@1] The investigation was continuing, the police said. [@1]
