K. K. Sukruth, a 26-year-old resident of Chelakkara, was caught at Thrissur
railway station in Kerala with 5.840 kilograms of cannabis. [@1] The seizure was
made during a check on platform number one of the station. [@1] Officers noticed
the young man on the platform without a ticket and carrying a blue backpack.
[@1] The cannabis was found when the officers searched his bag. [@1]

Officers valued the cannabis at 2.92 lakh rupees. [@1] The operation was carried
out by a joint team of railway police, the Railway Protection Force (RPF) and
the Crime Investigation Branch (CIB), under railway police sub-inspector Biju.
[@1] The team included senior constables Pradeep, Sushanth and Prashanth,
constable Amal Shankar, RPF officers K. S. Rajeev and R. Vijayakumar, and CIB
officer M. R. Manoj. [@1]
