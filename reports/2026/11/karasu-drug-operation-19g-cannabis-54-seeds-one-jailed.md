Officers from the Sakarya provincial police anti-narcotics branch and the Karasu
anti-narcotics bureau carried out the operation under the Karasu Chief Public
Prosecutor's Office. [@1] Police detained a 45-year-old man identified as M.B.
after information that he was trading drugs in Karasu district. [@1]

Searches in the operation found 18.62 grams of esrar, the Turkish word for
cannabis, and 0.8 grams of ready-to-smoke esrar, about 19.4 grams in all. [@1]
Officers also seized 54 cannabis seeds, a cannabis grinder and 84 rolling papers
used for drug consumption. [@1] The operation also found one unlicensed pistol,
eight cartridges and two mobile phones. [@1] After processing at the police
station M.B. was taken to court and remanded in custody. [@1]
