Police seized 85 kilograms of ganja hidden inside a house in Veliyangode,
Malappuram district, and arrested the man accused of storing it. [@1,2] The cannabis was kept in four sacks in a locked room in the house. [@2] The cannabis was found packed in sacks kept in the accused person's house. [@2] The
arrested man was identified as 23-year-old Mohammed Azhar. [@1,2] Police said the
ganja stored in the house had been smuggled from Rajasthan for later
distribution. [@1,2] The cannabis was brought from Rajasthan in a vehicle used for transporting cattle for the Christmas-New Year market. [@2] Investigators said questioning of Azhar showed he had been
selling ganja across Kerala state. [@1]

The Veliyangode house therefore served as the storage point linking an
interstate supply route from Rajasthan to sale in Kerala. [@1] The reported
quantity for the house seizure was the full 85 kilograms, described as cannabis
stored for sale across the state. [@1]

The storage and seizure setting was a residential house in Veliyangode, in
Malappuram district of Kerala. [@1] The case stood at the investigation stage
after the arrest and questioning, with the distribution account based on Azhar's
statements. [@1] Officials said this was the first seizure on this scale in the area and that they were checking whether more accused were involved. [@2] The seized cannabis and the arrested person were to be produced before the Ponnani court. [@2] Kerala police were the acting authority named for the seizure,
arrest and questioning in Veliyangode. [@1] The arrest was made by a Ponnani Range excise team led by Excise Inspector V. Noushad. [@2]
