Udaipur police raided fields at Sadi village in the Mandwa police area on 22
November 2026 after receiving intelligence about illegal ganja cultivation. [@2]
Police identified the grower as Sadi resident Ramia, son of Gujra, who allegedly
grew cannabis on his own land. [@2] Ramia fled when the police team arrived and
remained absconding while searches for him continued. [@1,3] Mandwa police
registered a case under the NDPS Act against Ramia over the unlicensed
cultivation. [@1,3]

Officers searching the field found green cannabis plants concealed among the
cotton crop to avoid detection. [@1,3] They uprooted the whole crop and seized
415.4 kilograms of fresh plants on the spot. [@2] Police estimated the market
value of the haul at more than two crore rupees. [@1] The uprooting and seizure
operation ran from early on Sunday morning until late evening. [@1,3]

Police said the tip about the village came under the Ganja Mukt Bhomat campaign,
run within the wider Trinetra anti-drug drive. [@1] The operation was conducted
under the guidance of Superintendent of Police Dr Amrita Duhan. [@2] The raid
was led by Additional Superintendent of Police Mukesh Sharma with the Kotra
circle officer and Mandwa station house officer Gulabram. [@2] Police said they
were investigating whether the farm was linked to a wider supply chain or other
traffickers. [@2] Police said the Ganja Mukt Bhomat campaign would continue with
further raids and strict action. [@2]
