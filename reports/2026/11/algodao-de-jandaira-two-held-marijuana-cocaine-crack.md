Civil police arrested two men in flagrante on 17 November 2026 during an
operation in Algodão de Jandaíra, in the Agreste region of Paraíba, on suspicion
of drug trafficking. [@1] The Paraíba Civil Police said the operation was
carried out by the Special Tactical Group (GTE) of the 12th regional civil
police station in Esperança and resulted in the two arrests. [@2] It followed
anonymous reports of drug sales at the residence, sent through the
Disque-Denúncia 197 hotline, after which investigators began inquiries in the
area. [@1]

During monitoring, officers saw movement they considered consistent with drug
sales involving the two men and, on approaching them, found portions of
marijuana, cocaine and crack. [@1] They also seized a precision scale that
police said was used to weigh drugs intended for sale, and the total quantity of
drugs collected was not disclosed. [@1]

The two suspects were taken to the Civil Police station in Esperança, booked in
flagrante for drug trafficking and left at the disposal of the justice system.
[@1] Investigators found that the property was the same one where a homicide
linked to conflict between criminal factions over control of the local drug
trade had been recorded in June 2026, but the police reported no direct link
between the two men and that killing. [@1] The Civil Police said it would keep
conducting operations against drug trafficking and organised crime in the region
and asked residents to use the anonymous Disque-Denúncia 197 line. [@1]
