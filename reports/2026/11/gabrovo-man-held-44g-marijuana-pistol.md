Police in Gabrovo detained a 46-year-old man after police found drugs, a firearm
and ammunition in the private property he inhabited. [@1,3] The information about
the detention and the seized items was attributed to the Regional Directorate of
the Interior Ministry in the city of Gabrovo. [@1,3] Police officers carried out
the search of the inhabited private property of the man on 15 November. [@2,4]

During that search of the private property, officers found nearly 44 grams of
marijuana. [@1,3] The plant material reacted to marijuana in a field drug test. [@3] The published marijuana figure was described as nearly 44 grams
rather than as an exact laboratory-confirmed weight. [@1] In the course of the
same check of the property, officers also discovered a pistol. [@1,3] Officers
additionally recovered three cartridges together with the pistol from the same
property. [@2,4] The man did not have the permit required for the pistol and the three cartridges. [@3]

Once the drugs, pistol and cartridges had been established, officers detained
the 46-year-old man. [@1,3] He was detained under the procedure of the Interior Ministry Act. [@3] An investigation into the case was opened and remains
under way after the detention. [@2,4] At the time of publication, police had
released no further details about the origin of the weapon or the drugs. [@1]
