Officers of the Policía Nacional Civil (PNC) arrested 29-year-old Juan José
Jiménez Menéndez in La Libertad Oeste for alleged unlawful drug trafficking.
[@1] According to the institution's official report shared on its social network
X, the detention was carried out following a citizen complaint. [@1] The police
account places the capture in the municipality of La Libertad Oeste and
identifies the detainee by his full name and age. [@3]

During the procedure, officers found marijuana that Jiménez Menéndez was
transporting in a bag attached to his body. [@1] Officers also found
methamphetamine in the same bag attached to his body. [@1] The police account
described the seizure as portions of marijuana and methamphetamine transported
inside a bag attached to his body. [@2]

After the detention, he was to be placed at the disposal of the competent court,
where he would face justice for unlawful drug trafficking. [@1] Police
reiterated the importance of citizen complaints and security procedures for
identifying drug-trafficking activities and bringing suspects to justice. [@2]
