Officers from Campo Grande's metropolitan civil guard and the state integrated
anti-organised-crime force FICCO-MS stopped a 25-year-old motorcycle delivery
driver carrying a parcel that hid drugs inside a vacuum cleaner. [@1] He said he
had collected the parcel at a transport company and was trying to find the
recipient's address when officers stopped him. [@1] Atual News located the stop
on Avenida Uraçá, where the driver was intercepted while carrying the parcel.
[@3]

During the inspection agents found several packages of a substance resembling
narcotics hidden inside the vacuum. [@1] The material was taken to Denar, the
specialised anti-narcotics police station, for weighing. [@1] The weighing gave
6.468 kilograms of marijuana and 764 grams of cocaine, a combined 7.232
kilograms. [@1] RCN67 said the packages in the vacuum held 6.4 kilograms of
marijuana in four tablets and two portions, alongside 764 grams of cocaine. [@2]

The motorcycle used for the delivery and a mobile phone were also presented to
the civil police. [@1] The case was recorded as drug trafficking and will be
investigated. [@1] Atual News said the investigation was continuing to identify
others involved in the distribution network. [@3]
