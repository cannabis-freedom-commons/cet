Guardia Civil officers carried out another anti-drug operation in Granada
province on Thursday 26 November 2026, this time in Iznalloz. [@1] Officers
seized nearly 400 marijuana plants in two searches of homes. [@1] In both homes
they found the electricity supply had been connected illegally. [@1] Electricity
fraud was also detected at three other locations. [@1] About five people were
investigated. [@1]

More than ten officers deployed at the entrance to the town. [@1] Four officers
guarded the entry and exit and checked that nobody crossed the police cordon.
[@1] The operation began a few hours earlier and ended around one in the
afternoon. [@1] Investigation officers, the Unidad de Seguridad Ciudadana
(Usecic) and public security patrols took part. [@1] Endesa technicians removed
fraudulent connections to the electricity network during the morning. [@1]

The raid formed part of the Guardia Civil's Servicio de Alto Impacto in Granada,
which began in mid-September. [@1] The service was expected to end on 15
December. [@1] It was the second raid in Iznalloz in days; the previous week
officers seized about 1,000 marijuana plants. [@1] In that earlier operation
five people were handed to the courts for drug trafficking and eight were
investigated for electricity fraud. [@1]
