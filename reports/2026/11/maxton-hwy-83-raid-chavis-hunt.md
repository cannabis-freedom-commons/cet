The Border Belt Drug Task Force said the arrests came in two separate narcotics
investigations. [@1] Robeson County Sheriff’s Office narcotics investigators and
SWAT operators searched a property in the 70 block of Hwy 83, south of Maxton,
on Thursday 19 November 2026. [@1] Mitchell Kelly Chavis, 47, and Toria Lynn
Hunt, 37, both of Maxton, were arrested and charged. [@1]

Investigators seized cocaine, marijuana, pills, firearms, U.S. currency and drug
paraphernalia in the search. [@1] The charges include two counts of trafficking
cocaine and possession with intent to sell or deliver marijuana. [@1] Both were
also charged with two counts of exposing a child to a controlled substance. [@1]

Chavis is being held at the Robeson County Detention Center on a $1,300,000
secured bond. [@1] Hunt is held in the same jail on a $1,050,000 secured bond.
[@1] The sheriff’s office said bond amounts are set by a judicial official, such
as a judge or magistrate, not by the office. [@1] The release also said every
person is presumed to be innocent unless proven guilty in a court of law. [@1]
