A three-judge court in Villa La Angostura sentenced Pastor Muñoz Álvarez to four
years and three months of effective prison for selling cannabis. [@1] The court
convicted him of possession of narcotics for commercial purposes. [@2] The
sentence followed an agreement between the prosecution, the defence and the
defendant, which the court approved unanimously. [@3] The panel was made up of
judges Juan Valderrama, Ignacio Pombo and Maximiliano Bagnat. [@1] Chief
prosecutor Gastón Ávila and legal assistant Federico Sura asked for four years
in prison, a fine of 45 fixed units and the confiscation of the goods used in
the trade. [@1] They also asked the court to revoke a six-month suspended
sentence imposed in Río Negro in August 2023 for another offence and to set a
single penalty of four years and six months. [@1] The judges revoked the
conditional sentence but set a unified term below the prosecution's request, at
four years and three months. [@4]

Police caught him in daylight on 13 March 2026 in a public street of Villa La
Angostura as he sold cannabis sativa from inside a car. [@1] Officers seized
wrappers holding almost 200 grams of marijuana, jars of cannabis oil and
tincture, seeds, two precision scales, nylon bags for portioning, notes, mobile
phones and cash. [@1]

The court ordered the definitive confiscation of 569,860 pesos in cash and the
Hyundai car used to sell the drugs. [@1] It ordered the seized substances and
the portioning equipment destroyed. [@1] Until the sentence becomes final he
must report weekly at Police Station No. 12 in El Bolsón and may not leave the
country. [@1] The case was investigated by the Ministerio Público Fiscal,
Argentina's provincial prosecution service, with the provincial police. [@1]
