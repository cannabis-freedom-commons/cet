Two men went on trial at the Schöffengericht of the Amtsgericht Fritzlar over a
professional cannabis plantation in Spangenberg. [@2] The defendants are
Albanian citizens aged 49 and 35 and remain in pre-trial detention. [@1] They
are charged with producing and cultivating cannabis in large quantities and with
aiding gang-related trafficking in two cases. [@1] The court set further public
hearings for 12 December at 14:30 and 16 December at 13:00. [@1]

In April 2026 police found 1,127 cannabis plants more than a metre tall on more
than 160 square metres inside a house in Spangenberg. [@1] Officers also seized
almost eight kilograms of harvested cannabis, packed in vacuum bags. [@1] The
search took place on 15 April 2026 at a property in Spangenberg-Metzebach. [@3]
Police put the number of plants at about 1,100 at the time. [@3] Detectives from
the Homberg criminal police carried out the search on a warrant issued at the
request of the Kassel prosecutor's office. [@3] When officers arrived, the two
men hid under a table and were arrested in the attic. [@1]

Both defence lawyers admitted their clients' involvement, with very different
explanations. [@1] The younger man's lawyer said his client knew the work was
illegal, had spent three months in the house watering the plants and had planned
to return home two weeks after the arrest. [@1] The older man's lawyer said his
client had refused to do the work and was waiting to leave. [@1] According to
the prosecution, the 49-year-old entered Germany in April 2026 and the
35-year-old in November 2025. [@1] The prosecution says they belonged to a gang
that intended to run the plantation permanently and sell several kilograms of
cannabis to third parties. [@1] A police officer told the court the plants were
in full flower and that there was a typical cannabis smell. [@1]
