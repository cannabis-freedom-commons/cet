Police and a Special Task Force (STF) raided a house in the Mokambari area under
Lefunga police station in Tripura and seized about 260 kilograms of dried ganja
(cannabis). [@2] The dried ganja was found wrapped in polythene packets during
the search. [@1] The joint team acted on a tip-off about suspected cannabis
trafficking and was led by Rajkumar Jamatia, officer in charge of Lefunga police
station, with STF personnel from Agartala. [@2] The team carried out the raid on
the morning of 20 November 2026. [@3]

Jagarantripura reported that the house owner and those involved had not yet been
identified and that police had begun an investigation. [@1] Tripura Info,
however, said the team nabbed the house owner, whose name was not disclosed.
[@3] Tripura Info said the entire consignment was kept in a large number of
packets for sale in the black market. [@3]

Police put the black-market value of the ganja at an estimated 25 to 26 lakh
rupees (about 2.6 million rupees). [@2] Speaking to the media, Jamatia said the
raid followed secret information about a possible cannabis trafficking operation
in Mokambari. [@2] Tripura Info said the area falls under Mohanpur subdivision,
West Tripura. [@3]
