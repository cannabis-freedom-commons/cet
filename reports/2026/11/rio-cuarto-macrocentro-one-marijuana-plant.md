A 30-year-old man was detained on calle 25 de Mayo al 800 in the macrocentro of
Río Cuarto, Córdoba, while carrying a single marijuana plant inside a bag. [@1]
A second account confirmed the man was 30, was stopped in the city's macrocentro
and was carrying the plant in a bag. [@2]

The intervention was carried out by officers of the Fuerza Policial
Antinarcotráfico (FPA), Córdoba's anti-narcotics police force. [@2] The FPA
seized the plant on an order issued by the corresponding prosecutor's office.
[@1] The procedure took place in that sector of the city and, by disposition of
the prosecutor's office, the plant was seized. [@2]

The plant seizure was reported as one of several detentions and seizures in the
macrocentro and the 70 Viviendas and Santa Rita neighbourhoods. [@1] In the same
roundup a man in barrio Santa Rita discarded a .32-calibre revolver with six
cartridges, which was seized and handed to the judicial unit. [@1]
