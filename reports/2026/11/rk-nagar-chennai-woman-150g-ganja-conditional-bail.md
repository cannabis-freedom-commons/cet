Chennai's POCSO special court granted conditional bail to the woman accused in
the case over attempting to sell cannabis to children in R.K. Nagar. [@1] The
accused is Nithya, a 39-year-old resident of R.K. Nagar in Chennai. [@1] Police
had received information that she was selling ganja to minors in that area. [@1]

During a check, R.K. Nagar police seized 150 grams of ganja from Nithya. [@1]
She was then arrested under the Juvenile Justice Act and the
narcotics-prevention law. [@1] She was remanded to jail on Oct. 17 following
that arrest. [@1] Her subsequent bail petition came up for hearing before Judge
S. Padma of the Chennai POCSO special court. [@1]

After hearing the petition, the judge granted bail on the ground that
investigations other than the scientific investigation had been completed. [@1]
As one condition of bail, Nithya must appear daily at the R.K. Nagar police
station by 5 p.m. [@1] She was further ordered not to abscond while on bail.
[@1] She was also ordered not to tamper with witnesses in the case. [@1]
