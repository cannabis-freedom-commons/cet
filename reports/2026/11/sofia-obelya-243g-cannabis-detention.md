A 41-year-old man was detained during a police operation against drug
distribution in the Obeleya neighbourhood of Sofia. [@1] The operation was
carried out on 18 November by officers of the counter-crime unit at the Ninth
Regional Directorate of the Sofia police. [@1] Police said they acted on
operational information about a man distributing narcotic substances from an
address in the neighbourhood. [@2]

At the address, criminalists found and seized 243 grams of cannabis and a small
quantity of amphetamine, according to the Interior Ministry. [@1] The ministry
described the haul as close to 240 grams of narcotics in the Obeleya operation.
[@2] According to police, the 41-year-old was already known to the Interior
Ministry and had been convicted of driving after using narcotic substances. [@1]

The case materials were reported to the Sofia City Prosecutor's Office. [@2] He
was charged with an offence involving the unlawful production, possession or
distribution of narcotic substances. [@1] A prosecutor's order placed him under
detention for up to 72 hours. [@2] He had first been detained for up to 24
hours. [@2]
