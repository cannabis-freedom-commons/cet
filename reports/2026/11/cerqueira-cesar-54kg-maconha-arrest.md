A 34-year-old woman was arrested in flagrante on suspicion of drug trafficking
on the morning of 25 November 2026 in Cerqueira César, São Paulo. [@1] Civil and
Military police made the arrest while executing a search-and-seizure warrant at
a residence in the city centre, the Polícia Civil said. [@1] The state Military
Police said the warrant was for a house on Rua Djalma Dutra. [@2] The joint
operation was named Fronteira Oculta. [@3]

In a construction at the back of the house, officers found 67 whole tablets of
marijuana and one partially fractioned portion, totalling just over 54 kg. [@1]
They also seized seven mobile phones, a precision scale and material used to
fraction and package the drugs. [@1] The marijuana was sent to the Instituto de
Criminalística de Avaré, where preliminary forensic examinations confirmed the
substance was cannabis. [@1] Police reported 67 tablets and a fractioned portion
weighing more than 54 kg in a rear room of the property. [@2]

The woman was taken to the Cerqueira César police station, booked in flagrante
and formally named in the trafficking inquiry. [@3] The delegate in charge asked
the court to convert the arrest into preventive detention. [@1] Investigators
said the drugs came from the country's border region and were brought to
Cerqueira César for sale. [@3] The inquiry continued into the origin of the
load, its suppliers, those who transported it and other suspected group members.
[@1] The woman remained at the disposal of the courts and was due to appear at a
custody hearing. [@1]
