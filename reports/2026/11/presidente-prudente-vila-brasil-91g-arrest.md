A 25-year-old man was arrested in flagrante for drug trafficking on 13 November
2026 in Vila Brasil, in Presidente Prudente. [@1] A Cavalry team of the 8th
Special Police Actions Battalion was conducting inquiries at an address subject
to recurrent drug-trafficking complaints when it approached a suspect leaving
the property. [@1]

The personal search found 24 portions of marijuana and 13 crack rocks, already
portioned, plus R$20 and two mobile phones. [@1] During the check inside the
property, the owner handed police an additional quantity of marijuana that had
been stored in the refrigerator, which was also seized. [@1] Officers seized a
total of 91 grams of marijuana and 7 grams of crack in the operation. [@1] A
second outlet gave the same totals of 91 grams of marijuana and 7 grams of crack
for the operation. [@2]

The suspect was taken to the Civil Police Participative Station, which confirmed
the flagrante arrest for drug trafficking. [@1] The arrested man remained at the
disposal of the courts. [@1]
