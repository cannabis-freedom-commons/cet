Officers from the OS7 Talca unit of Carabineros, supported by the Maule special
operations group (GOPE), found an illegal cannabis plantation in Potrero Grande,
in the pre-cordillera of the commune of Curicó. [@1] The plantation held 411
cannabis plants in the growth phase inside an improvised pen. [@1] The plants
were hidden among vegetation and ravines in an area of difficult access, and
officers used special entry manoeuvres to reach them, according to Carabineros
in Curicó. [@2] The seizure was one of the first of the season, with the
2026-2027 eradication work in Potrero Grande practically beginning with it,
Radio Condell reported. [@2]

The operation formed part of the 2026-2027 Cannabis Sativa Cultivation
Eradication Plan. [@1] On the instruction of the Public Prosecutor's Office, all
the seized plants were sent to the Maule Health Service, which was to receive,
analyse and later destroy them. [@2] No one was arrested or detained in the
operation. [@3]

Carabineros' Maule zone command said the OS7 operation had seized 411 plants in
Potrero Grande. [@4] Carabineros said it would continue investigative and
preventive work against illegal cannabis cultivation in the region and asked the
public to report plantations, including anonymously. [@2]
