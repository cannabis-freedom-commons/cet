Gefron officers arrested a truck driver on the night of Sunday 22 November 2026
on BR-364 at the exit of Cuiabá, Mato Grosso, after finding 383 tablets of skunk
hidden in his load. [@1] The stop came at about 20:00 after a tip to the
Disque-Denúncia line about suspicious movements by a bitrem truck at a fuel
station in the city, and officers found the vehicle on the BR-364 heading
towards Rondonópolis. [@1] The driver told officers he had received the drug in
Várzea Grande, in greater Cuiabá, and was to take it to Uberlândia, in Minas
Gerais. [@1] He was taken to the Central de Flagrantes in Cuiabá with the drug
and the seized vehicles, and the record of the case showed no previous
convictions. [@1]

The drug was hidden among a load of zinc and manganese ore. [@1] A closer
inspection found 11 large sacks holding the 383 packages of material resembling
cannabis. [@1] Besides the cannabis, officers seized the Scania tractor unit and
two semi-trailers. [@1] The state security secretariat valued the drug at
1,149,000 reais and put the total loss to criminal groups at 2,264,336 reais,
including 855,336 reais for the Scania tractor and 260,000 reais for the two
semi-trailers. [@2]

Gefron commander Airton Feitosa attributed the seizure to the unit's
intelligence work, which identified the transport of the drug. [@2] The stop was
part of the Protetor das Fronteiras and Olhos da Fronteira operations, which
target drug trafficking and other crimes linked to illicit goods. [@3] Skunk is
a street name for a potent form of cannabis that Brazilian police call
supermaconha. [@1]
