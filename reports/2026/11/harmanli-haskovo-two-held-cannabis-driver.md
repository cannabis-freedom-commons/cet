Two men were detained on suspicion of drug possession in Harmanli and Haskovo,
the police said. [@1] On 21 November 2026 at about 11:40, officers in the yard
of the Harmanli municipal hospital checked an 18-year-old resident of the town.
[@1] In his hand they found a paper fold containing about 2 grams of a
brown-green dry leafy mass. [@1] The youth said the substance was for his own
use and handed it over with a voluntary surrender record. [@1] He was detained
for up to 24 hours and a police file was opened on the case. [@1]

The same day at about 12:35 officers on Georgi Benkovski Street in Haskovo
stopped a Mercedes driven by a 38-year-old local man. [@1] A roadside test for
drug use returned a positive result for cannabis. [@1] The driver gave a blood
sample for chemical analysis and was detained for up to 24 hours, the car was
seized and pre-trial proceedings were opened. [@1] HaskovoNews, citing the
Haskovo regional police directorate, reported that an 18-year-old in Harmanli
was held for possessing 2 grams of drug mass and a 38-year-old in Haskovo for
possessing cannabis. [@2]

The Haskovo regional police directorate announced the detentions in a bulletin
on 23 November. [@2]
