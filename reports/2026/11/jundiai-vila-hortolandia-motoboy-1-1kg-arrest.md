Military Police officers arrested a delivery rider for suspected drug
trafficking during the night of Saturday 21 November 2026 in Vila Hortolândia,
in Jundiaí. [@1] According to the Military Police, the patrol team was covering
the neighbourhood when it saw the motorcycle rider run a red traffic light. [@1]
The rider tried to flee on the motorcycle when he noticed the attempted police
stop, but the pursuing officers caught him. [@1] At the scene, the detained
suspect was formally given the voice of arrest. [@1] Because of that earlier
escape attempt, the officers placed handcuffs on the suspect to restrain him.
[@1]

During the subsequent search, officers found about 1.1 kg of marijuana inside a
backpack the rider used for deliveries. [@1] Inside that same backpack, the
search recovered 62 tubes, five eppendorfs and a single marijuana tablet. [@1]

Officers then took the detained man, the seized drugs and the motorcycle to the
on-duty police station in Jundiaí. [@1] After arrival at the station, he
remained at the disposal of justice. [@1]
