Kerala police carried out a 'mega combing operation' across the state. [@1]
Officers arrested 157 people under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic
Substances Act, India's central drug law. [@1] They registered 259 cases under
the Act. [@1]

The checks ran in two phases, from 4 p.m. to 10 p.m. on Saturday 21 November and
from midnight to 6 a.m. on Sunday 22 November. [@1] Police inspected 34,180
vehicles during the operation. [@1] Officers registered cases against 1,708
people for drunken driving. [@1] Sixty-six cases concerned under-age driving and
five concerned fake number plates. [@1] Officers seized 12.78 grams of ganja, a
dried cannabis preparation, during the inspections. [@1] The same inspections
yielded 55.186 grams of MDMA, a non-cannabis drug that is excluded from the
figures here. [@1]

ADGP (Law and Order) P. Vijayan led the inspections. [@1]
