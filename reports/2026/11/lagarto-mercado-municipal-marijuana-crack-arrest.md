Military police in Sergipe took two people to the police station after a search
at the Municipal Market of Lagarto on the afternoon of Thursday 19 November
2026. [@2] The Getam, the military police's Special Tactical Motorcycle Group,
was called out after a tip-off that drugs were being sold at the market. [@1]
Officers at the market identified two people whose appearance matched the
description in the tip. [@1] The two showed signs of nervousness as officers
approached and tried to walk away quickly before the search. [@2]

Personal searches found portions of crack, cocaine and marijuana. [@1] Officers
also seized 110 reais in cash. [@1] The Lagartense portal described the material
as substances analogous to marijuana, cocaine and crack. [@2]

The two people and the seized material were taken to the Regional Police Station
in Lagarto. [@1] The A8SE portal reported that the two were men, while the
Lagartense portal described a couple taken to the station. [@1]
