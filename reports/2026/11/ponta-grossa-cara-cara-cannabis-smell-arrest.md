Military police arrested a man for drug trafficking in Cara-Cará, Ponta Grossa,
on the night of Friday 13 November 2026. [@1] The arrest was made by officers of
the Choque Platoon of the 1st Military Police Battalion during patrol on Rua
Padre Antonio Cavanis at about 21:04. [@1] Officers noticed a strong smell
characteristic of drugs and identified a man acting suspiciously near a vehicle.
[@1] When they approached, they saw fragments resembling marijuana on the car
seat and then detained the suspect. [@1]

After the initial approach, the person responsible for a property voluntarily
allowed the team to check the room used by the suspect. [@1] Inside they found a
pressed marijuana tablet plus other marijuana portions stored in glass
containers. [@1] They also found packets of flowering marijuana known as buds
and a portion of a hashish-like substance. [@1] Military police also seized a
precision scale, zip-lock plastic packaging and other objects police said could
be used to divide and pack drugs. [@1]

Officers took the man with the seized drugs and objects to the 13th Police
Subdivision in Ponta Grossa, which houses the flagrante custody centre. [@1]
There the case was presented to the police authority for legal procedures and
continuation of the investigation. [@1]
