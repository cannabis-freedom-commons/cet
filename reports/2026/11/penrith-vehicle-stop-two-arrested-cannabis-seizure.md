Officers from Eden's Neighbourhood Policing Team and roads policing colleagues
stopped a vehicle just outside Penrith. [@1] Cumbria Crack placed the stop on
Tuesday 24 November 2026. [@2]

A search of the vehicle under the Misuse of Drugs Act found a "substantial
quantity" of cannabis in the boot, according to police. [@1] The two occupants
were arrested on suspicion of drug supply offences and remained in custody
overnight. [@1] A second Cumbria account said a large quantity of suspected
cannabis had been found in the boot and that both occupants were arrested by
Eden Neighbourhood Policing Team. [@2]

Eden Police said: "We will always do everything we can to target drug supply in
our community, whether those involved stop here or think they can travel through
our area." [@1] The stop involved roads policing officer PC Morgan, with
neighbourhood officers PC Cook and PC Evans making the arrests. [@1] They were
supported at the scene by PC Clark and PC Maughan. [@1]
