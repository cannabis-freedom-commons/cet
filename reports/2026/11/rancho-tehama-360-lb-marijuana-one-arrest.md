A Corning man was arrested on 4 November 2026 after investigators searched a
home in Rancho Tehama where they reported finding marijuana, firearms and
cockfighting equipment. [@2] He was identified as David Huerta Valdovinos, 44,
the subject of a two-month investigation by Major Crimes detectives. [@3] He was
booked into the Tehama County Jail on suspicion of marijuana cultivation,
possession of marijuana for sale, possession of an assault weapon and possession
of fighting bird equipment. [@2]

Investigators said the grow operation involved more than 300 marijuana plants,
80 of which were still on the property when the warrant was served. [@1] The
rest of the crop had been harvested, and officers reported finding about 360
pounds of processed marijuana in stages of drying, trimming and packaging. [@2]
Investigators also confiscated seven firearms from the residence: three AR-style
assault weapons, two shotguns and two handguns. [@1]

The Tehama County Sheriff's Office said its Major Crimes Unit served the warrant
in the 16200 block of Rainbow Court after a two-month investigation into
suspected illegal cultivation. [@2] The sheriff's office said the investigation
remained active and that additional arrests were anticipated. [@1]
