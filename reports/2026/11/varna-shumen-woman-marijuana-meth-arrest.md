Police in Varna detained a 30-year-old woman from Shumen for up to 24 hours for
possession of narcotic substances. [@3] The woman was already known to the
Interior Ministry and was checked by officers from the First District
Department. [@3] During the check officers found a white crystalline substance
and dry herbal matter in her possession. [@2]

An expert report identified the two substances as methamphetamine and marijuana.
[@3] Police opened fast-track proceedings under Article 354a of the Penal Code
and handed the case materials to the prosecutor's office. [@1] The Varna
regional police directorate is named as the source of the announcement. [@3]
