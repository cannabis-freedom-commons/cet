Officers at the Comisaría Undécima intercepted a 30-year-old man at midday as he
tried to bring drugs hidden in clothing to a detained relative. [@2] The man had
presented himself with clothing for his detained relative during the reception
of food and supplies for inmates. [@1] Officers inspecting each garment found
abnormal bulges inside the seams of a football shirt. [@1] They opened the seams
and found hand-rolled marijuana cigarettes and pills hidden inside. [@1]

Agents of the Departamento de Consumos Problemáticos arrived and tested the
material with a narcotest. [@1] The test confirmed the plant material was
marijuana weighing 1.7 grams. [@2] Officers drew up the seizure record for
everything found. [@1] The pills were seized with the cannabis, while the only
weight given was the 1.7 grams of marijuana. [@1] The man was notified of
proceedings for alleged breach of Narcotics Law No. 23.737 and placed at the
disposal of the duty Fiscalía Antidrogas, with no detention reported beyond the
notification. [@1]
