home during a raid on 20 November 2026. [@1] Konak district police opened the
operation after identifying a suspect they had determined was selling drugs.

preparations and was carried out that day at the suspect's residence in Konak.

cannabis strain, alongside 55 grams of cocaine. [@1] The police operation
produced one detention and the seizure of 10 kilograms 550 grams of skunk and 55
grams of cocaine. [@2]

after the Konak operation. [@1] The detained suspect's procedures at the police
