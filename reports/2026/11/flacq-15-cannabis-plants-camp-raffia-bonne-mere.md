Police in the Flacq district uprooted 15 cannabis plants in two operations on
Sunday 15 November 2026. [@1] The first operation took place around midday on
scrubland beside a river at Camp Raffia, in the Poste-de-Flacq area, where
officers uprooted four plants. [@1] The four plants measured 36 to 70
centimetres and police valued them at Rs 20,000. [@1]

A second operation at 12:55 at Bonne-Mère, also on scrubland along a river,
uprooted 11 plants measuring 10 to 30 centimetres. [@1] Police valued that
seizure at Rs 55,000 and put the market value of all the plants seized at Rs
75,000. [@1] An investigation was opened after the operations. [@1] No arrests
were reported. [@2]

Both sites were overgrown ground beside a river, which the MauritiusNews account
said is used to hide crops because it is hard to monitor and away from housing.
[@2] Cannabis cultivation is illegal in Mauritius under the Dangerous Drugs Act.
