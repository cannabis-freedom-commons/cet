Egyptian Interior Ministry security officers arrested two men accused of running
a social-media page that promoted the sale of drugs for money. [@1,5] The General
Directorate for Combating Information Technology Crimes, coordinating with the
General Security Sector, detected the page. [@2,5] The man running the page was a
driver living in the Beba police-station district of Beni Suef governorate, and
officers found a mobile phone holding evidence of his activity. [@1,5] He
confessed to running the page and said he obtained the drugs from another man,
who was identified and arrested; the second suspect was an unemployed resident
of the Second Shubra El Kheima police-station district. [@1,5] The page operator
told investigators he had created a Facebook page specifically to promote drug
sales. [@3]

Officers found a quantity of heroin and hashish with the second suspect. [@1,5]
The second suspect supported the first suspect's account and admitted holding
the seized drugs with intent to traffic. [@2,5] The Interior Ministry said legal
measures were taken against the two suspects. [@4,5]

The public prosecution took over the investigation and ordered the two men
detained for four days pending inquiries. [@3] The arrests were part of the
ministry's efforts to combat online drug trafficking. [@1]
