Vicenza's local police found just over 800 grams of marijuana hidden in a
shopping bag among the plants of the Tecchio youth centre in viale San Lazzaro
112. [@1] Officers of the force's Nucleo operativo speciale, its special
operations unit, made the find during routine patrols on the morning of
Wednesday 18 November 2026. [@1] Inside the bag were bulky clear plastic
packages holding two wrappers full of marijuana. [@1] No one was at the youth
centre when officers checked. [@1]

The two wrappers weighed a little more than 800 grams in total, eight etti in
the paper's measure of 100-gram units. [@1] Il Giornale di Vicenza put the
marijuana's value on the illegal market at about 8,000 euros. [@1] Officers
seized the drug and informed the judicial authorities, opening a case for
possession for sale against unknown persons; no arrest was reported. [@1]

Guido Guglielmi, commissioner of the local police's anti-degradation squad, said
the find was the fruit of the special operations unit's constant checks in the
historic centre and the city's neighbourhoods. [@1] He said dealers hide their
stock in holes in the ground or among the leaves of trees, bushes and hedges.
