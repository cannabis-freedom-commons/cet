DIICOT prosecutors at the Maramureș territorial office detained five defendants
on 17 November 2026 on suspicion of high-risk drug trafficking. [@1] A judge for
rights and freedoms at the Maramureș Tribunal ordered three of them held in
preventive arrest for 30 days on 18 November, and the other two were placed
under judicial control. [@1] Judicial control leaves the two at liberty with
conditions set by prosecutors, including not leaving the locality without
approval. [@1]

Prosecutors say that between June and November 2026 a 37-year-old repeat
offender bought, kept and sold 3-CMC, known as "crystal", cannabis and cannabis
resin in Baia Mare. [@1] They say he supplied "crystal" to a 35-year-old, who
sold it to a 33-year-old who bought it to resell. [@1] Two other defendants
allegedly obtained, kept and sold doses of the same drug over the same period.
[@1] Only one defendant also faces a risk-drug trafficking charge alongside the
high-risk trafficking charge. [@1]

Seven home searches on Tuesday 17 November found quantities of 3-CMC and
cannabis, grinders, and containers and envelopes of substances still to be
analysed. [@2] Officers also seized sums of money in lei and euros and a
rubber-projectile pistol. [@2] The drugs the prosecutors say were traded were
seized during the investigation. [@1]

The investigation was carried out with police from the Maramureș organised-crime
service. [@1] Forensic specialists, the police special operations service and
Maramureș gendarmes supported the operation. [@1] The case is at the criminal
investigation stage and the defendants have the presumption of innocence;
prosecutors did not publish their names or the case number. [@1]
