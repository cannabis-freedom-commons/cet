CRS officers cleared 15 to 20 young people who had been blocking access from
inside the Lycée Jean Monnet on the Alco site in Montpellier on 19 November
2026. [@1] About 200 students seeking to enter the school that morning found a
blockade first formed by seven pupils in a cordon from 7am and joined by others
around 7:30am, and none could get through. [@1] The education authority and the
departmental education director, alerted by the headteacher, asked the
prefecture for police intervention to break the blockade. [@1]

A CRS company with 12 trucks and national police officers from DIPN 34 removed
the barrier amid jeers and insults while also facing projectiles. [@1] One
officer was slightly injured and one outspoken student was arrested during the
operation. [@1] The arrested student was found carrying a few grams of cannabis
and was handed to officers at the central police station for judicial
proceedings. [@1]

Calm returned afterwards and pupils, teachers and staff were able to enter the
school. [@1] Facing blockades, violence and damage since the start of the
student mobilisation, the Hérault prefect banned all gatherings from 6am to 6pm
on 20 November 2026 near the Jules Guesde, Jean Monnet and Joffre schools in
Montpellier. [@1]
