A 41-year-old resident of Chernogorsk was the defendant in a criminal case
handled by the city police investigation department. [@1] He was accused under
part 2 of Article 228 of the Russian Criminal Code, covering unlawful
acquisition, storage, manufacture, processing and transport of drug-containing
plants. [@1] The Chernogorsk City Court sentenced him to three years of
imprisonment, suspended, with a four-year probation period and a fine of 50,000
rubles. [@2] The verdict has taken legal effect. [@1]

In June 2026, officers of the drug-control unit of the Chernogorsk police
detained the previously convicted local resident, born in 1985, near a house on
Respublikanskaya Street and seized a packet of plant material. [@1] Police noted
that the man appeared to be under the influence of drugs when they detained him
in late June. [@3] A forensic examination established that the packet contained
155 grams of marijuana. [@1] He told police he had gathered the drug-containing
plant for personal use. [@1]

He refused a medical examination for drug intoxication, and police drew up a
separate administrative protocol for consuming narcotics without a doctor's
prescription. [@2] For that offence the court gave him two days of
administrative arrest. [@2] Police also made a representation to the Chernogorsk
city administration to destroy a site where wild cannabis was growing. [@4]
