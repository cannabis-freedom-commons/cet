Police carried out a house search in the Verbandsgemeinde Oberes Glantal on
Tuesday and seized a larger quantity of cannabis during that search. [@1]
Officers had originally been deployed to the address because of a suspected
theft offence. [@1] While deployed there, officers found about 2.4 kilograms of
the drug in the cellar of a 43-year-old man. [@1] The officers who made the
seizure are identified in the account as the police. [@1]

The seized material is described as cannabis buds and plant remains. [@1]
Officers seized all of the buds and remains because the find clearly exceeded
the permitted amount of 50 grams. [@1]

The 43-year-old now faces criminal proceedings because of the seizure. [@1]
Police described the proceedings as a case of suspected unlawful possession of
drugs. [@1] Under the Cannabis Consumption Act, unlawful possession of this kind
can be punished with a fine or up to three years in prison. [@1]
