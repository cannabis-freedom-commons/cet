Federal Highway Police officers arrested a 25-year-old man in flagrante on
suspicion of drug trafficking on 25 November 2026 during an inspection on the
BR-230 at the Galante district in Campina Grande, Paraiba. [@1,2] The stop took place near km 135 of the highway. [@2,3] The driver
disobeyed an order to stop and fled along the highway, prompting a tactical
pursuit. [@1,2] In addition to the drug-trafficking suspicion, he was cited for disobedience for failing to obey the stop order and fleeing. [@2,3] The driver had no driving licence and was caught about 500 metres after fleeing. [@2,3] After a period of pursuit, the driver stopped the car and
surrendered to the officers. [@1]

Inside the vehicle, officers found several wheat-flour bags placed on the back
seat. [@1,2,3] The marijuana was packed in four wheat-flour bags. [@3] Inside the bags, officers found several marijuana tablets hidden in
the product, totalling 98.8 kg. [@1,2] The tablets were covered with brown adhesive tape. [@2,3]

The suspect said he had received the load in Joao Pessoa and was to deliver it
to a person in Campina Grande. [@1,2] He said the car was borrowed from a friend and did not say who had supplied the drug. [@2,3] He said he would telephone after arriving to learn where to make the delivery. [@2,3] The arrested man and the seized marijuana
were taken to the Civil Police complex in Campina Grande for recording of the
flagrant arrest and further legal measures. [@1,2]
