Six young men from villages around Palani in Dindigul district were ordered held
under the Goondas Act in a ganja case on Friday, 20 November 2026. [@1] District
Superintendent of Police S. Jeyakumar recommended the detention of all six. [@1]
District Collector Durga issued the order that Friday. [@1] The six were lodged
in Madurai Central Prison under the order. [@2]

The case began on 21 October 2026, when Palani police seized 23 kg of ganja
smuggled from Odisha from behind a private mill at Pettanayakanpatti. [@2]
Palani taluk police arrested the six over ganja sale in the Pettanayakanpatti
area. [@1]

Dinamani names them as Hilsan, 20, Darshan, 20, Johnson, 20, Vinothkumar, 29,
Sunil Surya, 21, and Thalapathi, 25. [@1] The six were aged between 20 and 29.
[@1] Dinamalar gives the same first names and ages but places Hilsan in Palani's
Nagoor section and Sunil Surya in Begampur. [@2] Dinamani places Hilsan in
Pettanayakanpatti and Sunil Surya in Mettupatti, both in Dindigul district. [@1]
