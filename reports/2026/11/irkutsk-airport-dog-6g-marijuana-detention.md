At the entrance control of Irkutsk airport's domestic terminal, the service dog
Leti scented a banned substance and indicated a 42-year-old man to police with a
signal pose. [@1] Transport police detained the man, a 42-year-old resident of
Ust-Ilimsk, as he attempted to carry drugs through the airport. [@2]

During a personal search, officers of the Irkutsk air-transport police unit
found and seized a wrap later certified by an expert as marijuana weighing more
than 6 grams. [@2] The man said he had made the drug himself for personal use
with no intent to sell it. [@2] He had travelled to Irkutsk by public transport
to depart on the Irkutsk–Talakan flight. [@2]

A criminal case under Article 228 of the Russian Criminal Code was opened
against him. [@1]
