Police backed by the RAID, the national police's tactical unit, arrested six
people in Saint-Lô on 16 November 2026. [@1] Officers from the town's police
station had watched two flats in the rue Saint-Georges where a cannabis and,
above all, cocaine trade was run. [@2] The searches found 0.60 grams of cannabis
resin, 744 grams of cocaine and two handguns, one of them ready to use. [@1] In
total, 116,180 euros in cash was seized, hidden in a padlocked bag. [@1]

The two men at the head of the network, aged 42 and 29, appeared before the
Coutances court on 20 November 2026 in an immediate-appearance hearing. [@1] The
42-year-old was sentenced to seven years in prison and a 15,000 euro fine, with
a 15-year ban on holding a weapon and a five-year ban from the Manche
department. [@1] The 29-year-old was given three years, twelve months of them
suspended with probation, and must seek treatment and work or train. [@1] Both
men went straight to prison after the hearing, the committal warrants having
been issued. [@3]

The investigation began on 24 August 2026 with an anonymous tip about a trade in
the town. [@1] The prosecutor of Coutances said about 70 customers were
identified, coming to buy at any hour of the day or night. [@4] The prosecutor's
office described a pyramid, with one man at the head of the network and a second
buying directly from him. [@5] Four other people implicated, notably for
complicity in offering or supplying drugs, will be summoned later under the
guilty-plea procedure. [@1]
