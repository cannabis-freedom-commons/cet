Police in Adamdighi upazila of Bogura detained one man, Saiful Islam, aged 28,
the son of Rupchan Pramanik of the Kochpukuria area of Murail Bazar, with 405
grams of ganja. [@1] Officers held him on the night of Wednesday 18 November
2026 in front of Jiten Chandra's shop at Murail Bazar in Adamdighi. [@1]

Adamdighi police station said a secret tip about drug sales at Murail Bazar
reached officers at about 9 pm on Wednesday 18 November 2026. [@1] On that
information officers carried out a raid at the spot and detained Saiful Islam
with 405 grams of dried ganja, dried cannabis the station said it had seized
there. [@1] Inspector (Investigation) Shahidul Islam of Adamdighi police station
confirmed that the incident was true. [@1]

After a case was filed under the Narcotics Control Act, Adamdighi police sent
Saiful Islam to court on Thursday 19 November 2026, the day after the night-time
stop at Murail Bazar. [@1]
