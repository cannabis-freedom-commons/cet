Naikhongchhari Thana police arrested three men who were smoking ganja during an
anti-drug drive in Naikhongchhari, in Bandarban district. [@1] Officers detained
them at about 6am on Friday 27 November 2026 at a junction beside Shafiullah's
shop in the Baganghona area of Ward 2, Naikhongchhari Sadar Union. [@1] The
three were named as Imam Hossain, 30, son of Nurul Islam; Shahidul Islam, alias
Moyna, 22, son of Abdur Rashid; and Abdullah, alias Biden, 18, son of Md Ala
Uddin, all from Baganghona. [@1]

A mobile court sat at about 6:30am the same morning in the presence of
Naikhongchhari upazila executive officer and executive magistrate Md Enamul
Hasan. [@1] The court gave Imam Hossain 3 days, Shahidul Islam 10 days and
Abdullah 7 days. [@1]

Police said the three were picked up during a drive against illegal weapons,
drugs and youth gangs in the thana area. [@1] The operation was led by
Naikhongchhari police second officer Habibur Rahman Khan. [@1] Officer-in-charge
Mohammad Mozammel Haque said no offender would be spared, whoever they were.
[@1] He said police would keep up regular drives to wipe out drugs and keep law
and order normal in the area. [@1]
