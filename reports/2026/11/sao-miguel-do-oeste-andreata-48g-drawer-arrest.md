Military Police arrested a 27-year-old man for drug trafficking on the night of
Tuesday 17 November 2026 in Bairro Andreata, São Miguel do Oeste, in western
Santa Catarina. [@1] Officers went to a house at about 22:00 during a routine
patrol and approached its owner, who was with other people. [@1]

With the help of the sniffer dog Ayla, officers searched the property and the
dog found two large portions of marijuana wrapped in white plastic inside a
drawer of a chest of drawers. [@1] After weighing, the drug totalled
approximately 48 grams, the police account said. [@1]

Police established that the man was already serving a sentence for drug
trafficking in a semi-open regime and was wearing an electronic ankle monitor.
[@1] He received voz de prisão, Brazil's in-flagrante arrest, and was taken with
the seized drug to the Civil Police station of São Miguel do Oeste for the legal
procedures. [@1]
