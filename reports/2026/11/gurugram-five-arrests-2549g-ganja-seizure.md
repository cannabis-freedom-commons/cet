Five people accused of illegal narcotics trade were arrested at different
locations in the Gurugram district, police said on Tuesday. [@1] Police
identified them as Tayyub (32), Ram Singh (34), Nitesh alias Neetu (22),
Devender Kumar alias Hawa Singh (26) and Bharat Bhushan alias Vicky alias
Lankesh (44), all residents of Gurugram. [@1] Teams from the Gurugram police
Crime Branch and its Anti-Narcotics Cell carried out the arrests. [@2] In total
officers seized 2.549 kilograms of ganja, 10.73 grams of smack and 15.99 grams
of chitta (heroin), along with a scooter, a bag and a mobile phone. [@1]

Tayyub was arrested on 16 November near Harari village on the Pataudi-Rewari
bypass highway with 536 grams of ganja and 10.73 grams of smack. [@2] Ram Singh
was held near the pond in Basai village with 1.215 kilograms of ganja and the
Activa scooter used in the offence. [@2] Devender Kumar was arrested near Dhani
Mor on the Sohna-Gurugram road with 530 grams of ganja. [@2] Bharat Bhushan was
arrested near a Shiva statue on Basai Road with 268 grams of ganja. [@2]

Cases were registered against all five at the relevant police stations. [@2] A
senior police officer said the accused were being questioned. [@1] Tayyub told
police he had bought the drugs for 5,000 rupees and wanted to sell them in small
quantities at a profit. [@2] Ram Singh told police he had bought the ganja in
Najafgarh, Delhi, for 12,000 rupees to sell in Gurugram. [@2] Ram Singh already
had two criminal cases registered against him in Gurugram. [@2] Bharat Bhushan
had an earlier case under the NDPS Act, the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic
Substances Act. [@2]
