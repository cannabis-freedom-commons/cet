Police in Kubrat detained two young men from the village of Duhovets in Ispperih
municipality after stopping their BMW on Vitosha Street at about 15:15 on 18
November 2026. [@1] The 19-year-old was driving and the 16-year-old sat in the
front passenger seat. [@1] Razgrad's regional directorate of the Ministry of
Internal Affairs announced the case. [@2]

A search of the car found a men's bag under the front bonnet, in the filter box,
holding nine polyethylene wrappers known as 'jumpers' with nearly 12 grams of
dry plant material. [@1] A field test of the contents reacted positively for
cannabis. [@3] Officers also searched two properties in Duhovets under urgent
procedures and found no banned substances. [@1]

Pre-trial proceedings were opened under Article 354a, paragraph 3, item 1 of the
Criminal Code. [@1] That provision carries criminal liability for the unlawful
acquisition or possession of narcotic substances. [@4]
