After drug traffickers were stopped at the entrance to the city of El Kef the
previous week, police monitored and identified the residences of the remaining
suspects in a drug network operating between Tunis and El Kef. [@1] Judicial
police officers of the Kef National Security District, in coordination with
officers of the Bab Bhar National Security District in Tunis, raided a rented
house in Tunis. [@1]

In the raid officers arrested the remaining suspects, five young men and a girl,
for trafficking drugs between Tunis and El Kef. [@1] Officers seized a quantity
of cannabis and cocaine in the same operation, with no weight or separate amount
reported for either drug. [@1]

The case was referred to the Tunis territorial public prosecution, which ordered
the six detained suspects held for completion of the remaining legal procedures.
[@1] No charges, court decision or further outcome for the six detainees had
been reported at the time of publication. [@1]
