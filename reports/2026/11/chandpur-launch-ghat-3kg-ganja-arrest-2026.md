Chandpur Sadar Naval Police Station arrested a 30-year-old man with 3 kilograms
of ganja at Chandpur launch ghat on Wednesday 25 November 2026. [@1] Police
named him as Md. Rubel Bepari, a resident of Mithapur village under Patuakhali
Sadar police station, and gave his father's name as Md. Azahar Bepari and his
mother's as Mosa. Rani Begum. [@1] The raid followed a tip-off and began at
12:10 pm at the launch terminal. [@1] A naval police team led by sub-inspector
Suphal Chandra Singha recovered the ganja from him and made the arrest, the
station said. [@1] Chandpur naval police's officer in charge, Md. Azamgir
Hossain, confirmed the arrest that afternoon, Dhaka Mail reported. [@2]

Police said Bepari told them in preliminary questioning that he had collected
the ganja at Cumilla bus stand and was carrying it for delivery in Patuakhali
Sadar. [@1] The station said legal proceedings against him under the Narcotics
Control Act were under way. [@3]
