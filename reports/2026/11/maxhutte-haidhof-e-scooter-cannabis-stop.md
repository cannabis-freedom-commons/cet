Police in Maxhütte-Haidhof checked a 50-year-old man riding an e-scooter in
Bürgermeister-Gierl-Straße on Friday at around 10 a.m. [@1] Officers said the
man showed signs indicating cannabis consumption. [@1] As the check went on, the
man became increasingly aggressive. [@1]

Even after a blood sample had been taken, he did not accept officers stopping
his ride and announced that he would ride on. [@1] To prevent him from
continuing, officers seized his e-scooter on the spot. [@1] Police opened a
report against the 50-year-old under road traffic legislation. [@1]

The Burglengenfeld police inspection reported the stop as one of several
incidents in Burglengenfeld, Teublitz and Maxhütte-Haidhof. [@1]
