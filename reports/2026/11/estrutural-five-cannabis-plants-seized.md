Military police of the 15th Battalion seized five cannabis plants from a house
in Setor Santa Luzia, in the Estrutural community of Brasília, Brazil's Federal
District. [@1,4] The plants stood between 2.5 and 3 metres tall, with one about
2.5 metres in front of the house and four of 2.5 to 3 metres at the back. [@1,4]
Nobody was arrested despite the size of the home-grown crop. [@1,4]

The patrol was approached by passers-by who reported a strong odour
characteristic of drugs from the property and a cannabis-like plant visible
above the wall. [@1,4] Officers went to the address, confirmed the smell outside
and saw the vegetation over the low wall, then entered through the partly open
wooden gate because the situation was a flagrante, an offence caught in
progress. [@2,4]

All the seized plants were collected and taken to the police station for the
area for the legal procedures. [@2,4] The plants were taken to the responsible
police station and no one was arrested during the occurrence. [@3,4]
