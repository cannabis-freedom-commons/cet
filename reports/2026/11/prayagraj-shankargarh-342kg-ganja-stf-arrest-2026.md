The Uttar Pradesh STF's Prayagraj field unit seized 342 kg of ganja from a
Mahindra Bolero pickup in the Shankargarh police station area of Prayagraj. [@1]
Officers arrested the driver, Ramesh Kumar Yadav, a Prayagraj resident. [@1] The
arrest was made at about 9:45 pm on 20 November 2026. [@1] The STF acted on
information that a Bolero pickup was bringing a large load of ganja from Raipur
in Chhattisgarh to Prayagraj. [@1] The load was hidden under empty plastic
crates so that the pickup looked like an ordinary goods vehicle. [@1] Haribhoomi
reported that the pickup, a Mahindra Bolero, carried the registration UP70QT8721
and that a mobile phone was seized with it. [@2]

Police put the value of the consignment at about 85.50 lakh rupees. [@1]
Haribhoomi described the 85.50 lakh figure as the estimated international market
price. [@2] Police seized the pickup used for the smuggling and a mobile phone.
[@1] Initial questioning indicated the ganja had been brought from Raipur in
Chhattisgarh to Prayagraj. [@1] Yadav told the STF he belonged to a gang that
brought ganja from Chhattisgarh and sold it in Prayagraj and nearby districts.
[@1] He said he had been promised 20,000 rupees for delivering the consignment
to its destination. [@1] He said he drove the pickup to Raipur on the owner's
instructions. [@1] Haribhoomi gave his address as Pure Kanta village under
Handia police station in Prayagraj. [@2]

The STF registered case number 266/2026 at Shankargarh police station against
Yadav. [@1] He was booked under sections 8, 20 and 27 of the NDPS Act. [@1]
Further legal proceedings were left to the local police. [@1] Police said Yadav
had two cases registered at Jhunsi police station in Prayagraj in 2021 over
keeping stolen property. [@1] Police said the pickup's owner was Shyam Bahadur,
a resident of Holapur under Handia police station. [@1] Police said they would
also examine the roles of Shyam Bahadur and Mukesh Yadav, which rested so far on
Yadav's statement. [@1]
