Security patrols of the Afwaj Amniya in Saudi Arabia's Asir region arrested a
Saudi citizen in Al Farsha governorate for allegedly promoting hashish. [@1] The
patrols said officers seized 49 kilograms of narcotic hashish from the suspect
during the operation in the governorate. [@1]

Officers detained the man immediately after the seizure and completed the
statutory procedures required against him. [@1] The patrols then referred him to
the competent authority to face further investigation and prosecution. [@1]

After the operation, the security authorities renewed a general appeal for
citizens and residents to report any information about drug smuggling or
promotion. [@1] They asked people in Makkah, Madinah, Riyadh and the Eastern
Province to call 911 and people elsewhere in the Kingdom to call 999, as well as
using the narcotics-control number 995 and email 995@gdnc.gov.sa. [@1] The
authorities said all such reports are handled in complete confidentiality and
involve no liability for the person reporting. [@1]
