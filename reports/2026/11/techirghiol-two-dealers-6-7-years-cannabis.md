Two Techirghiol men were definitively convicted when the Constanța Court of
Appeal rejected their appeals on 20 November 2026. [@1] The dealer who handled
street transactions was sentenced at first instance to six years in prison for
high-risk drug trafficking and unauthorised operations with psychoactive
substances. [@1] The second man, who had a previous high-risk drug trafficking
conviction, was sentenced to seven years. [@1]

The case began on 3 March 2025, when a protected-identity person told DIICOT
that a man was holding and selling large quantities of risk and high-risk drugs
in Techirghiol, Constanța County. [@1] Criminal proceedings opened on 28 April
2025 and investigators used three undercover officers and one collaborator with
a real identity. [@1] The two men were detained on 21 January 2026, with
preventive arrest proposed to a Constanța tribunal judge the next day. [@2]

The dealer met customers near Stadionul Sparta, took their money, then bought
the substances from the second man and delivered them. [@1] Searches at both
homes followed on 21 January 2026, and at the dealer's home officers found
several quantities of cannabis and objects bearing traces of cannabis. [@1] An
earlier police account said four search warrants were executed in Techirghiol
and that cannabis (a risk drug), 2CMC (a high-risk drug) and 4BMC (a
psychoactive substance), along with grinders and precision scales, were
discovered and removed. [@2] The younger man, 22, was reported to have bought
2MMC from the other man and sold it in Constanța County for 800 to 1,500 lei.
[@3]

During the search of his home the second man handed over incriminating material
including quantities of prohibited risk and high-risk substances and equipment
for measuring and cutting drugs. [@1] Questioned on 21 January, the first man
denied the offences and the second declined to make a statement, but both later
confessed during the judicial investigation. [@1]
