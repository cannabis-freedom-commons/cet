Special Response Battalion-14 arrested 28-year-old Md Raihan Ali, described as a
drug dealer, with 5 kg of ganja at Ashekpur Bypass Mor on the Dhaka-Tangail
highway around noon on Saturday, 21 November 2026. [@1] Raihan Ali, a resident
of Rajshahi district, was carrying the drugs disguised as an ordinary passenger
on a Grameen Travels bus travelling from Dhaka to Rajshahi. [@1] A CPC-3
operational team of SRB-14 in Tangail acted on a secret tip about drug smuggling
on that bus during a routine anti-drug patrol, set up a checkpoint in the
Ashekpur Bypass area and searched the bus before detaining Raihan. [@1]

In front of witnesses, officers recovered 5 kg of ganja hidden in a specially
concealed way inside a carton kept in the bus luggage box, as pointed out by
him. [@1] Officers also seized a mobile phone allegedly used for buying and
selling drugs. [@1] SRB said Raihan had long collected ganja from border areas
and other parts of the country and sold it in different areas of the country.
[@1]

He was handed over to Tangail Sadar Police Station along with the recovered
drugs and the seized phone for legal action. [@1]
