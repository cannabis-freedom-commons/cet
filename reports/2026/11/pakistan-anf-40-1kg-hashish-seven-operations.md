Pakistan's Anti-Narcotics Force carried out seven operations against drug
trafficking in cities across the country, recovering 44.296 kilograms of
narcotics and arresting six suspects, including two women. [@1] At a courier
office near the Pakistan Employees Housing Authority in Karachi, ANF recovered
500 grams of hashish from a parcel destined for the United Arab Emirates,
concealed inside a cardboard carton. [@1]

Under its domestic drug interdiction operations, ANF recovered 21.6 kilograms of
hashish from a woman's shoulder bag at Nasapa Stop on Charsadda Road in Peshawar
and arrested the female suspect. [@1] In another Peshawar operation on Warsak
Road, 18 kilograms of hashish were recovered from a vehicle, concealed inside
its CNG cylinder. [@1] ANF also recovered 1.370 kilograms of heroin from the
trolley bags of a man and a woman travelling to Jeddah at Multan International
Airport, 2.4 kilograms of opium from a suspect on public transport in Islamabad,
400 grams of hashish from a suspect near Government Postgraduate College in
Haripur, and 26 grams of cocaine from a motorcyclist near a cricket academy in
Johar Town, Lahore. [@1]

Cases have been registered against the arrested suspects under the Control of
Narcotic Substances Act, 1997, and further investigations are under way. [@1]
