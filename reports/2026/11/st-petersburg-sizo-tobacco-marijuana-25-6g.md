Staff at remand prison SIZO-1 in St Petersburg intercepted an unusual parcel
intended for a 24-year-old detainee, sent by his girlfriend. [@1] She had
disguised 25.6 grams of marijuana as ordinary tobacco in the parcel. [@1] The
penitentiary service said the sender was a St Petersburg resident and the food
and tobacco were meant for a defendant born in 2002 who was held in custody.
[@4]

The parcel failed the mandatory inspection: officers noticed suspicious contents
in the tobacco packaging and sent them for examination. [@1] An examination
confirmed the packet held marijuana, which was seized before it reached the
detainee. [@1] Experts found that the tobacco contained a prohibited narcotic
substance weighing 25.6 grams. [@4] The regional penitentiary service said the
25.6-gram impurity was inside the tobacco pack; the drugs were seized, examined
and a check was ordered. [@2] Specialists at the expert-criminalistic centre
identified the material as a narcotic substance weighing 25.6 grams. [@3]

A check was ordered into the attempted delivery, and the circumstances of how
the drug entered the parcel are being established. [@4]
