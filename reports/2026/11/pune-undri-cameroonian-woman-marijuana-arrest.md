Kalepadal police in Pune arrested a 56-year-old Cameroonian woman at Wadachiwadi
near Undri at about 5.15 pm on Friday, following a tip-off. [@1] Officers seized
packets of marijuana that police valued at Rs 68,250. [@2] Senior inspector
Girisha Nimbalkar of Kalepadal police said the woman was found sitting near
Wadachiwadi lake with the packets and that the team seized the drug. [@1] Police
said she allegedly intended to sell the marijuana to people known to her. [@1]

Nimbalkar said the woman has a valid passport, arrived in India a few months ago
on a tourist visa, and that her visa expired many years ago. [@1] Police said
they were investigating how the woman became involved in drug trafficking and
who supplied her with the marijuana. [@1]
