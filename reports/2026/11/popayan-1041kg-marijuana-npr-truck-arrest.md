Troops of Anti-Narcotics Battalion No. 2 carried out a ground interdiction
against a motor vehicle in a rural area of Popayán, Cauca. [@1,7] Officers found
1,041 kilograms of marijuana hidden inside an NPR-type truck during the check.
[@1,3,4,5,6,7] The driver ignored the stop signal and fled, prompting a motorized pursuit until troops intercepted the vehicle. [@6] Troops at a 29th Brigade road checkpoint detected the suspicious van-type truck and signalled it to stop. [@6] Officers arrested the driver of the vehicle at the scene. [@1,3,4,5,6,7] The seized
load, the immobilized vehicle and the detained man were placed at the disposal
of the judicial authorities for prosecution for alleged drug trafficking,
manufacture or possession. [@2,3]

Military intelligence linked the load to the Dagoberto Ramos structure of the
FARC dissidents. [@2,3,4,5,6,7] Intelligence indicated the drug was first bound for Puerto
Asís, Putumayo. [@1,3,4,5,6,7] From Puerto Asís the load was to be moved by river launches
toward Brazil. [@1,3,4,5,6,7]

Authorities estimated the seizure equalled about 52,050 doses for illegal
markets. [@1,4,5,7] The Army put the economic impact on that structure at about 1,050
million pesos. [@2,7]
