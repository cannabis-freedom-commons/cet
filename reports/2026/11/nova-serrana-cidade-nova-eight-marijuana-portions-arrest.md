Military Police in Nova Serrana, Minas Gerais, arrested a 20-year-old man on
suspicion of drug trafficking in the Cidade Nova neighbourhood. [@1] During a
patrol the officers noticed the young man trying to hide as soon as he saw the
team approaching. [@1] He threw an object into the vegetation, and the first 10
wrappers of a substance resembling cocaine were found there. [@1]

Officers continued the search at a second address that the suspect himself had
indicated, where they found the remaining drugs and the materials related to
drug trafficking. [@1] In total officers seized 233 packets of a substance
resembling cocaine and eight portions of material resembling marijuana. [@1]
They also seized a digital scale, an amount of money and a mobile phone. [@1]

He was arrested in flagrante delicto and taken, along with all the seized
material, to the Delegacia de Polícia Civil, the local Civil Police station.
[@1] The occurrence was registered on Sunday 15 November 2026. [@1]
