Rajshahi Metropolitan Police arrested seven people, including a woman, in
separate anti-drug operations. [@1] The operations took place on 18 November
2026. [@1] They were carried out by Kashiyadanga, Belpukur, Shahmakhdam and
Matihar thana police, the metropolitan Detective Branch and the Mirzapur police
outpost. [@1] The metropolitan Detective Branch arrested Osirun Begam, 50, at
her home in Kharkhari (Bhatapara) village and seized 40 grams of ganja and 40
tapentadol tablets from her custody. [@1]

Belpukur thana police arrested Mizanur Rahman, 41, at Belpukur Golchattar and
seized 105 grams of ganja from his custody. [@1] Mizanur already faced a drug
case at Belpukur thana. [@1] The two seizures recovered 40 grams and 105 grams
of ganja, the only cannabis reported in the sweep. [@1] Kashiyadanga police
arrested Julekha Begam, also called Putli, 50, with heroin and yaba tablets.
[@1] Belpukur police arrested Masum Ali, 22, with yaba tablets. [@1] Shahmakhdam
police arrested Paritos Pahari, 29, and Shariful Islam, 37, with country-made
chollai alcohol. [@1] Matihar's Mirzapur outpost arrested Faisul, 21, with
tapentadol tablets. [@1]

Police filed separate cases under the narcotics control law at the relevant
thanas and sent the suspects to jail through the courts. [@1]
