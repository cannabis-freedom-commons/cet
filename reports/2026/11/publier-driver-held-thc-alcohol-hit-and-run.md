A 29-year-old man living in Publier was arrested on Saturday 14 November 2026
after causing a property-damage crash and trying to flee. [@1] After hitting
another vehicle, he left the scene without stopping. [@1] A motorist who
witnessed the collision chased him and managed to immobilise him before police
officers intervened. [@1] The driver was placed in police custody. [@1] At the
end of custody he was released. [@1] Prosecution continues in a preliminary
inquiry. [@1]

The events happened at about 13:00 in rue des Châtaigniers. [@1] Checks found
his driving licence was suspended and his vehicle was uninsured. [@1] A breath
test showed 1.14 mg of alcohol per litre of exhaled air. [@1] A saliva screening
was also positive for THC, the psychoactive substance in cannabis. [@1] The
victim’s vehicle was heavily damaged at the rear. [@1] The victim filed a
complaint for hit-and-run. [@1]

Police said he combined several offences: drink-driving, driving after drug use,
a suspended licence and lack of insurance. [@1] He was already subject to
several proceedings for drink-driving and drug-driving. [@1]
