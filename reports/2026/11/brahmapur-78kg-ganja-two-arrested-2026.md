Officials of Odisha's excise intelligence wing seized 78 kilograms of ganja
being transported illegally in a car through Brahmapur, in Ganjam district, on
Monday. [@1] The wing is the state's abkari nigha vibhagam, or excise
intelligence department. [@1]

Deputy Superintendent Shrutikant Raut of the wing said two people belonging to
Uttar Pradesh were arrested. [@1] He said an investigation had been taken up
into the case. [@1]
