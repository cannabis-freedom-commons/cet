At about 8:45 on Saturday 14 November, ADSU officers searched the Union-Park
home shared by a 33-year-old aide-ouvrier and his brother. [@1] The two brothers
were arrested and detained in a police cell. [@1]

Officers found 27 cannabis plants growing in containers, measuring between 7 cm
and 97 cm tall. [@1] They also seized a transparent plastic sachet containing
113 cannabis seeds. [@1]

The investigation was continuing. [@1]
