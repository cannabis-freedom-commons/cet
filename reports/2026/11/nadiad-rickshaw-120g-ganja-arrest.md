The Kheda-Nadiad Anti Narcotics Wing caught a 31-year-old man trafficking ganja
near Kabir Complex in Nadiad, on the road from Kabristan Chowkdi toward
Ahmedabadi Darwaja, on the night of Thursday 19 November 2026. [@1] The team set
up a watch near the complex on specific information and stopped the man as he
arrived in a CNG auto-rickshaw that matched the tip-off. [@2]

A search of the man and the rickshaw found plant-origin ganja in two white
plastic bags at his waist and in 19 small zip-lock pouches. [@2] Weighed in the
presence of a Forensic Science Laboratory officer, the ganja came to 120 grams,
which police valued at about Rs 6,000. [@2] Officers seized the auto-rickshaw,
valued at Rs 50,000, two mobile phones worth Rs 5,500, Rs 4,150 in cash, a
colour copy of a driving licence and the empty bags and pouches, property police
valued at Rs 65,650 in all. [@2]

Police said the man admitted in questioning that he had brought the ganja for
retail sale from a man known as Rafuchacha, who lives near Arjunshababa's dargah
near Kalal Pipal in Petlad. [@2] Nadiad Town police station registered a case
under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act on the complaint of
wing police inspector B.D. Vasava. [@2]
