Chennai police arrested a gang that had tried to move foreign currency and
hawala money out of the country. [@1] The Ennore assistant police commissioner,
Venkata Kumar, had received information that a five-member gang was carrying
foreign currency, hawala money and ganja in a car from Sengundram via Bangalore,
to be flown onward. [@1] Acting on that tip, officers stopped and searched the
car at Sengundram on Wednesday as it travelled from Gandhi Nagar to Bangalore.
[@1]

In the car they found and seized 6.42 lakh rupees in hawala cash, foreign
currency notes worth 12 lakh rupees, 600 grams of ganja valued at 6 lakh rupees,
and silk sarees. [@1] Those arrested were Ashish Ali, Abubakkar Siddique and
Usama from Sivaganga, Ismail from Trichy and Shaik Abdullah from Thanjavur. [@1]

During the investigation police found that Abubakkar, Ismail and Usama had tried
to move the consignment to Malaysia. [@1]
