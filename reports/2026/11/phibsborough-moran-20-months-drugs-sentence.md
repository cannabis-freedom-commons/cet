Angelo Moran, 28, of Fortlawn Park, Blanchardstown, pleaded guilty at Dublin
Circuit Criminal Court to possession of ketamine, cocaine and cannabis on
Phibsborough Road, Dublin on 20 November 2023. [@1] He has no previous
convictions. [@1] Judge Martin Nolan imposed a 20-month sentence and refused to
defer its start until after Moran completes exams next June. [@1]

Gardaí secured a warrant for a flat on Phibsborough Road after a tip-off and
found drugs and paraphernalia scattered about, with nobody present. [@1] The
drugs included just over 200 grammes of cocaine valued at €15,049, ketamine
valued at €47,640 and cannabis valued at €536. [@1] The total value of the drugs
was €63,219. [@1] Officers also found €800 in cash, bagging material, a scales
and a blender. [@1] Gardaí established that Moran lived there and arrested and
interviewed him the following month. [@1] He admitted holding, mixing and
bagging the drugs. [@1] He said he had agreed to do so to pay off a drug debt of
about €2,000-€3,000. [@1]

Garda Wilson agreed Moran had been intimidated into involvement because of his
drug debt and had since turned his life around. [@1] His counsel said he had a
difficult childhood after being kidnapped by his father and taken to Romania
before returning to live with his mother. [@1] Counsel said Moran had since
rehabilitated, is drug-free, is in full-time study and hopes to qualify as a
physiotherapist. [@1] Judge Nolan noted Moran had an addiction and owed a debt
to third parties who sought to take advantage of the situation. [@1] The judge
called it an unusual type of case and said the court could depart from the
presumptive 10-year minimum but Moran’s rehabilitation did not meet the
threshold for a non-custodial sentence. [@1]
