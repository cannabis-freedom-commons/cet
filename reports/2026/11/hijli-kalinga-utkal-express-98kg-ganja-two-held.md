Railway Protection Force officers searched the Kalinga Utkal Express, train
18477, at Hijli station in Kharagpur after a ticket examiner passed on a tip
that a large quantity of ganja was being smuggled on the train. [@1] Officers
who began the search found two suspicious passengers in the H-1 coach. [@1] A
search of their bags found six trolley bags holding 98 packets of ganja weighing
slightly more than 98 kg. [@1] Both passengers were arrested, one a man and one
a woman, and two mobile phones were seized from them. [@1] The two told officers
that a man had given them the bags at Sakshigopal station and that people in
Morena and Delhi were to collect them. [@1]

The RPF, which announced the Hijli recovery together with a second one at
Narayangarh station, put the market value of the ganja from the two trains at
about 50 lakh rupees. [@1] Railway police said they were checking how the two
obtained the ganja, where and to whom they sold it, and who else was part of the
network. [@1]
