Yamoussoukro anti-drug police dismantled two drug dens at M’Baoucessou and Andé
in Bongouanou department. [@1] Officers acted after an anonymous tip reported
several drug sale points in both villages. [@1] The operation began on Wednesday
18 November 2026 at about 17:00. [@1] At M’Baoucessou, occupants of a den fled
when they saw police, abandoning drugs they had been handling. [@1] Police
seized the products and destroyed the den. [@1] At Andé, officers found an
open-air den where several people were gathered. [@1] Eight people were arrested
there. [@1]

The eight detainees were taken to the GRAD premises in Yamoussoukro with the
seized products and the abandoned vehicle. [@1] The detainees were aged 22 to 50
and worked in commerce, farming, mechanics, welding and building. [@1] The
alleged supplier escaped, abandoning a black BMW E46 registered 567 FB 01. [@1]
Police immediately seized the vehicle. [@1] The seizure totalled 8.21 kg of
cannabis. [@1] Officers also seized 197 heroin doses weighing 172 grams and 90
cocaine doses weighing 86 grams, which are not counted in the cannabis total.
[@1]

The prosecutor at the Bongouanou court of first instance and the police
hierarchy were informed. [@1] The operation was part of Operation Épervier XII,
ordered by the Director General of the National Police to fight trafficking and
use. [@1]
