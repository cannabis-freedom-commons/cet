Korar police in Kanker district raided the garden plot beside the house of Renu
Ram Darro, 66, at Koreta Mavlipara and arrested him. [@2] Police said they acted
on information that a resident of the Mavlipara hamlet was illegally growing
ganja plants in his bari, the fenced plot attached to a rural home. [@1]
Officers searched the plot, found the plants and seized them. [@1]

Police said the 35 green ganja plants weighed 1.690 kilograms and estimated
their market value at about 76,000 rupees. [@1]

A case was registered against him under section 20(a) of the Narcotic Drugs and
Psychotropic Substances Act, which covers illegal cultivation of cannabis. [@3]
He was produced before a court, which sent him to judicial remand. [@1] The raid
was carried out under Ujiyara, the Kanker district campaign against the illegal
drug trade and drug cultivation run on the instructions of Superintendent of
Police Nikhil Rakhecha. [@1] Kanker Additional Superintendent of Police Yogesh
Sahu said that under section 20(a) cultivation carries up to 10 years of
rigorous imprisonment and a fine of up to 100,000 rupees. [@2]
