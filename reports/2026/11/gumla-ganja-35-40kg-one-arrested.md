Police in Gumla district, Jharkhand, arrested one man suspected of ganja
trafficking and recovered about 35 to 40 kilograms of the drug from his
possession. [@1] The operation followed a tip-off to the district superintendent
of police, Harris Bin Zaman, on whose instructions police from Gumla and the
Toto police station carried out a joint action. [@1] Officers watching for
suspicious activity chased the suspect and caught him, and a search found the
ganja packed in a sack with a capacity of 50 kilograms. [@1]

Police said the ganja had not yet been weighed and that the quantity could
increase after weighing. [@1] The arrested man's identity was withheld; he was
held at Gumla Sadar police station for questioning, and officers were trying to
establish where the ganja had come from and who it was to be supplied to. [@1]

Police said preliminary questioning suggested other people were involved in the
trade, suspected that an organised network might be behind the smuggling, and
said further arrests could follow the interrogation. [@1]
