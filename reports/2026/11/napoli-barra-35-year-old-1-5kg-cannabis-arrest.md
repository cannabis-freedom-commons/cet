State Police arrested a 35-year-old man in eastern Naples for unlawful
possession of drugs for dealing. [@1] After the arrest he felt ill and was treated by 118 emergency staff before being taken to Poggioreale prison. [@4] The arrested man was identified in press accounts as 35-year-old Tommaso Buo, a TikTok creator reported as having almost 300,000 followers. [@6] Officers of the Campania Crime Prevention
Unit spotted the man in a car in Barra and stopped him after he tried to evade
the check. [@1,4] He was driving a rented Fiat Panda when stopped at the checkpoint in via Stigliano in Barra. [@4] The man tried to elude the patrol on seeing it but officers
blocked him quickly. [@1]

Searches of both his car and his home found numerous ready-divided sachets and a
large quantity of drugs. [@1,5] Officers seized over 700 grams of marijuana and
800 grams of hashish, which form the 1.5 kg cannabis total used here. [@2] In
the same seizure officers also took 16 grams of ecstasy/MDMA and 2 grams of
cocaine, which are excluded from the cannabis figures. [@2,5] Press accounts describe part of the non-cannabis seizure as pink cocaine, presented as a synthetic mixture linked to MDMA or ecstasy. [@7] Officers also seized
a knife with its blade soaked in drugs. [@1] Officers also seized a precision
scale and material for packaging doses. [@1,4]

The arrest came during an extraordinary territorial control on 13 November in
the San Giovanni a Teduccio and Barra districts under the Naples police
prevention plan. [@1] During the same deployment officers identified 61 people,
14 of them with police records, and checked 45 vehicles. [@2] The wider control
was run by San Giovanni-Barra station officers supported by the Campania Crime
Prevention Unit. [@3]
