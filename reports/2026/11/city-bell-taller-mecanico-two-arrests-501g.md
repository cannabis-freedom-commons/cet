Buenos Aires province police detained a 39-year-old man and a 35-year-old woman
during a search of a mechanic workshop in City Bell that investigators said was
a front for retail drug sales. [@2] The two were identified as Rubén C., 39,
alias Cucha, and Sabrina C., 35, and both were placed at the disposal of the
justice on a charge of illegal possession of narcotics with intent to sell. [@1]

Officers seized 501.8 grams of marijuana in buds (cogollos) and 17.5 grams of
cocaine divided into 50 wrappers, along with a precision scale, cutting
substances, 36,500 pesos in cash and four mobile phones. [@1] The operation,
named Cucha, took place at a property in the El Molino neighbourhood after more
than five months of investigation that began on 2 June 2026 following a
complaint about drug dealing at the address. [@1]

The operation was carried out by officers of the La Plata delegation of the
province's Superintendency of Investigations of Illicit Drug Trafficking. [@2]
The search was authorised by Guarantee Court No. 4, headed by Juan Pablo Masi.
[@1] The case is handled by UFI No. 18 of the La Plata judicial district, led by
prosecutor Hugo Tesón. [@1]
