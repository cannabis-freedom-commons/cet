Bibb County sheriff's investigators served a narcotics search warrant at a home
on Sherbrooke Drive in Macon on 23 November 2026. [@1] Deputies found more than
four pounds (about 1.81 kg) of marijuana at the home, along with packaging
materials and digital scales. [@1] They also seized a SportArms .38 Special
revolver, a quantity of fenethylline and thousands of dollars in US currency.
[@1]

Anthony Skyler Ward, described as a convicted felon, was arrested and charged
with possession of marijuana with intent to distribute. [@1] He was also charged
with possession of a Schedule I controlled substance, fenethylline, possession
of a firearm during the commission of a felony and possession of a firearm by a
convicted felon. [@1] The sheriff's office Drug Unit led the investigation, with
the Gang Unit, Property Unit and Patrol Division assisting. [@2] The charges are
accusations, and Ward is presumed innocent unless convicted in court. [@2]
