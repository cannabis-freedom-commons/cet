Three people were arrested with ganja, painkiller tablets and syringes at
Thoppampalayam Crusher Medu in Erode in the early hours of Sunday. [@1]
Bhavanisagar police identified the arrested men as D Kutti alias Veerasamy, 28,
U Manjunathan, 28, and S Sheik Dawood, 25, all from Sirumugai in Coimbatore
district. [@1] A team from Bhavanisagar police station led by special
sub-inspector M Jageer intercepted and searched the men. [@1]

During the search police seized 50 grams of ganja valued at approximately Rs
2,500. [@1] In the same search police seized 10 Tapentadol 100 mg tablets valued
at approximately Rs 5,000 and a syringe. [@1] Police also confiscated a
two-wheeler that had been used for smuggling the narcotics. [@1]

Police said the primary accused, Kutti alias Veerasamy, is a history-sheeter
under Sirumugai police station limits with 13 criminal cases pending against
him. [@1] Bhavanisagar police registered a case under relevant sections of the
NDPS Act, arrested all three men, and said further investigation is on. [@1]
