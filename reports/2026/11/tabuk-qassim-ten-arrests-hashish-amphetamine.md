Saudi Arabia's General Directorate of Narcotics Control, the country's
anti-narcotics agency, arrested 10 people in the Tabuk and Qassim regions for
allegedly promoting narcotic substances and tablets subject to medical
regulation in two separate security operations. [@1] In the Tabuk operation,
officers arrested a Saudi citizen and six residents of Pakistani nationality who
were accused of promoting amphetamine, hashish and tablets subject to medical
regulation. [@1] A second Saudi outlet also reported the same Tabuk arrests,
describing a citizen and six Pakistani residents held for promoting amphetamine,
hashish and regulated tablets. [@2]

In the Qassim operation, anti-narcotics officers detained three Saudi citizens
accused of promoting hashish and tablets subject to medical regulation. [@1] The
authorities said all 10 suspects were detained, had the required legal
procedures completed against them and were then referred to the Public
Prosecution for further action. [@1]

After the arrests, the directorate urged citizens and residents to report drug
smugglers and dealers by calling 995, by calling 911 in Mecca, Medina, Riyadh
and the Eastern Province and 999 in the rest of the Kingdom, or by email to
995@gdnc.gov.sa, saying all reports are handled in strict confidence without any
liability for the informant. [@1]
