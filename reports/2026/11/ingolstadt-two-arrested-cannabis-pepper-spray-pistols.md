Shortly after midnight, emergency callers reported two males with firearms in
front of a bank branch on Theresienstraße in Ingolstadt. [@1] Officers found the
two suspects, aged 21 and 19, in the foyer of the branch. [@1] The 21-year-old
is German and the 19-year-old is Cameroonian. [@2]

During the check officers found two pepper-spray pistols, more than a small
amount of cannabis and a total of about 1,500 euros in cash. [@1] The suspects
were arrested and taken to the police station on suspicion of violating the
Narcotics Act, with Ingolstadt criminal police conducting further
investigations. [@2] They resisted the officers during the arrest, and no one
was injured. [@1]

At the prosecution's request both men were brought before a detention judge and
then taken to prisons. [@1]
