The Basic Prosecution in Gjakova has indicted four people, identified only by
the initials MK, ZZ, MK and VK, over drug dealing. [@1] All four were charged as
co-perpetrators with unauthorised purchase, possession, distribution and sale of
narcotics, psychotropic substances and analogues. [@1] The first defendant, MK,
was additionally charged with unauthorised possession of weapons. [@1] The
prosecutor asked the court to convict the defendants and punish them under the
law after a trial with evidence. [@1] The four had been arrested on 5 June 2026
after police searched multiple locations in Gjakova on suspicion of drug
trafficking. [@1] By the prosecutor's order the suspects were detained for up to
48 hours after arrest. [@1]

Searches of the defendants' homes seized 270.45 grams of cocaine, 146.79 grams
of heroin mixed with paracetamol and caffeine, and 10.97 grams of marijuana.
[@1] The prosecution said the drugs had been sorted into plastic bags ready for
sale. [@1] Police documented the sales through secret investigative measures
including simulated drug purchases. [@1] Alongside the drugs police seized a
short gun, a gas pistol, 90 rounds of assorted ammunition, 6,536 euros, 474
dollars, 60 Swiss francs and five mobile phones. [@1]

The indictment alleges the group acted together in Gjakova up to 5 June 2026 to
hold and distribute various drugs for sale to others. [@1] The Serious Crimes
Department announced that it had filed the case. [@1]
