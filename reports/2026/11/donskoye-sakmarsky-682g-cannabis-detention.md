A resident of Sakmarsky District was detained with a large batch of cannabis.
[@1] Police officers working under Operation Kanal-Kaspiysky Mayak inspected the
outbuildings of a resident of the village of Donskoye and discovered stored
plant material. [@1] The press service of the regional Interior Ministry
reported the case and said the detained man faces a prison term. [@1]

An expert examination identified the seized plant material as a narcotic drug
consisting of parts of wild-growing cannabis. [@1] Police put the total weight
of the seized cannabis parts at 682.51 grams. [@1] The cannabis came from wild
growth rather than cultivation. [@1] The 50-year-old man told police that he had
gathered the cannabis himself and had been storing it for personal consumption.
[@1]

Investigators opened a criminal case over the seizure. [@1] The case concerns
illegal acquisition and storage of narcotic drugs on a large scale. [@1] The
offence as described carries up to 10 years' imprisonment. [@1]
