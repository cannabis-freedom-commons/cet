Officers of the Polizia Penitenziaria found almost 700 grams of hashish, divided
into about ten blocks, hidden in a cell of Bassone prison in Como during routine
checks of the spaces destined for detainees. [@1] The prison police command
attributed the result to the attention and scrupulousness of the officers,
stressing the importance of their daily check work. [@2]

The substance, of a hashish type, was seized pending checks to establish its
active ingredient. [@1] An investigation into the find is under way. [@1]
Another account described the find as 700 grams of hashish divided into blocks
hidden in a cell. [@3]

The Polizia Penitenziaria, in making the operation public, said it testified to
the importance of the control and surveillance activity carried out daily by the
corps. [@1] It remained to be understood how the substance had come to be inside
the prison. [@4]
