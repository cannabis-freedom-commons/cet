Ashuganj thana police arrested two suspected drug dealers and seized 15 kg of
ganja (cannabis) and a pickup truck they said was used to transport drugs in
Brahmanbaria district on 22 November 2026. [@1] A team from the station acted on
secret information and stopped a suspicious pickup near the Ashuganj toll plaza
at about 7:30 in the morning, then searched it. [@1] Officers recovered 15 kg of
ganja from the vehicle and arrested the two men. [@1]

The two were Mohammad Rakibul Islam, son of Anwar Hossain, from the Kumarakhali
area of Kushtia district, and Mohammad Nurul Islam Mia, son of the late Azgar
Ali, from the Badarganj area of Rangpur district. [@1] Police said the recovered
ganja and pickup were seized in accordance with the rules. [@1]

A case was filed against the two at Ashuganj police station under the Narcotics
Control Act. [@1] Police said their operations against drugs and legal action
would continue. [@1]
