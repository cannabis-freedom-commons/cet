Carabinieri in Piombino Dese arrested a 19-year-old local man shortly after
midnight on 16 November 2026 after he swerved into viale della Stazione when he
saw a patrol in via Roma. [@1] The driver was already known to police for
offences against the person and had never obtained a driving licence, drawing an
administrative fine of several thousand euros. [@1] A patrol of the Piombino
Dese carabinieri station on a territory-control duty was driving through via
Roma when it saw the driver turn suddenly right into viale della Stazione. [@2]

His shoulder bag contained 30 doses of cocaine weighing 16.53 grams in total and
26.42 grams of hashish, along with 1,123 euros in cash. [@1] A search of the
man's home turned up a further 650 euros in small notes. [@4] Officers seized
all the drugs and cash found in the car and the home. [@1] He was arrested on
suspicion of unlawful possession of narcotic substances and held in the
Cittadella carabinieri company's security cells on the Padua prosecutor's order
pending a validation hearing. [@1]

At the validation hearing on the morning of 16 November the judge upheld the
arrest and imposed a daily signing obligation at the Piombino Dese carabinieri
station. [@1] TgPadova said the case was at the preliminary investigation stage
and the suspect's responsibility would have to be established in court. [@3]
TgPadova reported the charge as possession of narcotic substances for the
purpose of dealing. [@3]
