Customs officers stopped a Moroccan lorry driver who lives in Belgium at the
Vergèze rest area on the A9 in the Gard and arrested him during a check on 17
November 2026. [@1] Dogs found a hiding place under the floor of his lorry,
where 322.4 kg of cannabis pollen was packed in ice-cream tubs. [@1] The lorry
also carried mattresses and cushions that he said were his own. [@1] Prosecutors
valued the load at nearly 3 million euros and said he was to take it to Denmark.
[@1]

The driver, a man in his fifties, was also prosecuted for allegedly trying to
flee when he was stopped. [@1] Through an interpreter he told the hearing he had
gone to urinate and moved aside because women were nearby, and that the officers
thought he was running away. [@1]

He appeared before the Nîmes criminal court on Monday 23 November 2026. [@1] His
lawyer, Me Hugo Ferri, obtained a more lenient penalty for his client. [@1]
