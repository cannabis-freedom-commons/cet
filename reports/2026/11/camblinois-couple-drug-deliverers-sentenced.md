A couple described as Camblinois was convicted for working as deliverers in a
trafficking operation involving cocaine, ketamine and cannabis. [@1] The court
gave the woman a prison sentence to be served under an electronic bracelet. [@1]
Her partner was ordered to serve his own prison time behind bars. [@1] The case
against the couple centred on their role as couriers for the network. [@1]

Police dismantled the wider trafficking operation at the end of August. [@1] A
man living in the Bruaysis stored the merchandise for the network. [@1] He also
prepared the merchandise before it was handed to deliverers. [@1] He passed the
drugs to about fifteen deliverers working for the network. [@1] Police
investigators suspected that the Camblinois couple were among those deliverers.
[@1]

The three substances named for the network were cocaine, ketamine and cannabis.
[@1] The investigation established that drugs worth EUR 158,000 passed through
the stash keeper. [@1] That EUR 158,000 total was given for drugs in general
rather than for cannabis alone. [@1]
