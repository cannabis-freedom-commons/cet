Military troops arrested Lidia Elena Muñoz in an operation against the sale of
narcotics, alleging she belonged to a common-crime group linked to drug
distribution in Cauca department. [@1] The intervention took place in the Las
Cruces sector on the road linking Popayán with the municipality of Timbío, where
the troops carried out control and registration checks against drug trafficking.
[@1]

During the search the soldiers found several wrappers containing substances
believed to be intended for distribution. [@1] According to the authorities,
they seized 11 wrappers holding approximately 80 grams of marijuana and 13
others with about 60 grams of cocaine. [@2] The soldiers also found cash, which
was seized and referred for verification by the judicial authorities. [@1]

Muñoz was captured and linked to the proceedings to clarify her alleged
participation in these activities. [@1] The case was handed to the competent
authorities, who must investigate and determine her legal situation. [@1]
