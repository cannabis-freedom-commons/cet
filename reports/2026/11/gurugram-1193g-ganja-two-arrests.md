Gurugram police arrested two young men from Bihar who were moving around the
city to sell ganja. [@1] The arrested men were identified as Santosh Kumar of
Pratapgarh village and Gangaram Mandal of Lalpur village in Supaul district,
Bihar. [@1] A case under the NDPS Act was registered against both men at
Sector-10 police station in Gurugram. [@1]

On 13 November, a Crime Branch Farrukhnagar team patrolling the Pataudi-Gurugram
road detained the two near the Salig ki Dhani turn on the basis of suspicious
activity. [@1] A search recovered 1 kg 193 grams of ganja from their possession.
[@1] The men told police during initial questioning that they had bought the
ganja in Nepal for Rs 8,000 and brought it to Gurugram. [@1] Their plan was to
sell it in Gurugram, but police arrested them before any sale. [@1]

Police found that Gangaram Mandal already faces an earlier NDPS case in
Gurugram. [@1] Both accused were being questioned while police investigated
other aspects of the trafficking and pursued further legal action. [@1]
