Police in Asunción arrested a 23-year-old man at his home in Barrio Chiquito
Santa Lucía before dawn on 26 November 2026. [@1] Officers carried out the raid
at 05:00 at a house on an unnamed passage beside the Mburicaó stream. [@1] The
detained man was named as Juan Gabriel Vallejos Insfrán, a Paraguayan who lived
at the address. [@1]

Officers seized 10 grams of suspected marijuana in a single portion. [@1] The
marijuana was described as ready for sale. [@2] The same search found 36 doses
of crack cocaine weighing 10 grams, a Samsung A17 phone and a red Motostar
Star200 motorcycle with plate 701ABXF. [@1] Police put the estimated loss at 6
million guaraníes. [@1]

The operation was run by the Asunción urban trafficking division of the National
Police's specialised anti-drug department, part of the organised crime
directorate and the SUMAR drug-abuse action plan. [@1] Prosecutor Samara Lesme
of Asunción's specialised anti-narcotics unit intervened in the case. [@1] The
detainee was held at the prosecutor's disposal, with no charge or court decision
reported. [@2]
