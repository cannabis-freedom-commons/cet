On 13 November 2026, officers of the Grand-Yoff district police station arrested
three individuals in three sectors of the commune: Senzela, Cité Sonatel and the
Arafat neighbourhood. [@1,2,3] The three men are suspected of possession, packaging
and trafficking of cannabis (chanvre indien). [@1,2,3] The third suspect was identified as 23-year-old tiler A. Diallo, living in Grand Yoff. [@3] The second suspect was identified as 23-year-old trader W. Seck, living in Cité Aliou Sow. [@3] The first suspect was identified as 46-year-old trader M. Kounta, living in Arafat. [@3] Officers seized one kilogram
of cannabis, several cones of the drug, a quantity of cannabis in bulk,
packaging equipment, a knife and a total of 89,500 FCFA. [@1,2,3] Diallo admitted owning the seized drugs, saying he received them in bulk from an individual called « Pabi – Thieré », repackaged them into cones for resale, and did so to cover medical expenses. [@3] Kounta denied owning the seized kilogram, said the bulk cannabis on him was for his personal use, and said he bought it for 5,000 francs CFA from an individual called S. [@3] Officers found eleven cannabis cones and a knife hidden in a plastic barrel and under newspapers at Diallo's home. [@3] Officers seized five cannabis cones hidden in envelopes, three pairs of scissors and 7,000 francs CFA from Seck. [@3] Officers then found one kilogram of cannabis hidden near the entrance door of the home Kounta had left. [@3] Officers found bulk cannabis mixed with tobacco and 82,500 francs CFA on Kounta. [@3]

The three suspects were placed in police custody and the seized items were
sealed; the investigation continues to identify any suppliers or accomplices.
[@1,2,3]
