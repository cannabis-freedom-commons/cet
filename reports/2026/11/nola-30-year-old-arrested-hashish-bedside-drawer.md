State police officers from the investigative squad of the Nola police station
stopped a 30-year-old man during what investigators described as a cocaine sale.
[@1] A search of the man and his vehicle found 40 doses of cocaine already
packaged for sale. [@1] Officers also seized 350 euros in cash, which
investigators believe came from drug dealing. [@1]

Officers then searched his home on the outskirts of Nola, going through the
bedroom. [@1] A brown solid substance found inside a bedside drawer turned out
to be hashish. [@1] The operation was part of police efforts against drug
dealing in the Agro Nolano, the area around Nola. [@1]

The 30-year-old was arrested and placed at the disposal of the judicial
authorities. [@1]
