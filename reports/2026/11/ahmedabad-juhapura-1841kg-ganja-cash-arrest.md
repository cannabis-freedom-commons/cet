Acting on a tip-off, officers raided a house in the Noor-e-Ilahi Society in
Juhapura on Sunday. [@1] The operation was carried out by the Ahmedabad city
crime branch's Special Operations Group (SOG). [@1] A 46-year-old man from the
society, Farooq Sheikh, was arrested after 1.841 kg of ganja and Rs 2.6 lakh in
cash were recovered from his house. [@2] Police alleged he was involved in
selling the contraband. [@2]

Officials valued the ganja at about Rs 92,050. [@1] Police said the cash was
suspected to be proceeds from selling the drug. [@1] The SOG team also seized
two digital weighing scales, a mobile phone, 155 black pouches and six packets
of plastic zip-lock bags used for packaging. [@1] Police put the total value of
the seized property, including cash and other articles, at about Rs 3.7 lakh.
[@2] Sandesh put the total value of the seized property at Rs 3,65,400. [@3]

Police booked him under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS)
Act. [@1] Investigators were still tracing the source of the ganja and the
extent of the alleged distribution network. [@1] Police were also questioning
him about others linked to the network, Sandesh reported. [@3]
