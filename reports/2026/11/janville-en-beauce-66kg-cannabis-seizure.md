Two residents of Essonne, one born in 2001 and the other in 1999, were
travelling in a Citroën DS3 when customs officers stopped the car at about 17:00
on a Sunday evening in November 2026. [@1] The stop was at the exit of the
Allaines toll booth, situated in the commune of Janville-en-Beauce, in the
Eure-et-Loir department. [@1] The officers looked in the boot of the car and
found cannabis there. [@1] At about 22:00 that same Sunday, gendarmes from the
Lucé company were contacted by the Essonne customs officers, after the seizure
of narcotic products had already been made. [@1]

The cannabis seized from the vehicle amounted to a total of 66 kg and comprised
both resin and herb. [@1] It followed an earlier seizure of narcotic products in
the same department, Eure-et-Loir, which Essonne customs officers had made on 17
November, making this their second there. [@1]
