The Mexican Army and Air Force carried out a joint operation on Tuesday 17
November 2026 in Chihuahua, Durango and Sinaloa. [@1,4,5,6] The operation was carried out within the framework of Mexico's National Public Security Strategy. [@5] Soldiers destroyed 25 drug
plantations in all, of which 16 were marijuana and nine were poppy. [@3,4,5,6] The
operations combined ground patrols with aerial reconnaissance to find and
eradicate the crops. [@1,4,5,6] The plantations were destroyed by incineration and aerial fumigation. [@5] No detentions linked to the operation were reported, and no information was given on the possible destination of the drugs that could have been produced on the plots. [@6] The plantations were located in mountainous areas of the three states. [@4] For these tasks the Air Force used Bell 407GX helicopters. [@5]

The Secretariat of National Defence (Sedena) reported the results. [@1,4] The 25
plantations covered about 3.06 hectares in total. [@2,4] That area is the combined
surface of the poppy and marijuana plots. [@1] The nine poppy plots covered 1.10 hectares and the 16 marijuana plots covered 1.96 hectares. [@5,6] Sedena estimated the damage to
the organisations growing the crops at 8.74 million pesos. [@1,4] The ministry
gave the estimate for the destruction of all 25 plantations. [@3]

The ministry said the destruction prevented the drugs from reaching illegal
markets. [@1] Sedena presented the operation as part of its campaign against the
sowing and sale of drugs. [@1] Sedena said the loss weakened the operating and
logistics capacity of the criminal groups that grew the crops. [@3] Sedena said
the operation contributed to reducing drug trafficking in the region. [@1]
