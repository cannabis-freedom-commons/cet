Saudi Border Guard land patrols in the Al-Raboah sector of Asir region arrested
two Ethiopian nationals who were in the kingdom in violation of the border
security law. [@1] The patrols caught the two while they were smuggling 100
kilograms of hashish, the Border Guard said. [@1] The site Slaati carried the
same account, reporting that the two Ethiopian nationals were arrested with 100
kilograms of hashish in the Al-Raboah sector. [@2]

The Border Guard said it had completed the initial legal procedures against the
two and handed them, along with the seized material, to the competent authority.
[@1]

An account of the same arrest by Riyadh 24 described the 100 kilograms as qat, a
stimulant plant that is not cannabis, rather than hashish. [@3] The security
authorities asked people to report drug smugglers and dealers on the 911 line in
Mecca, Medina, Riyadh and the Eastern Province, and on 999 elsewhere in the
kingdom. [@1] They also gave the Border Guard's 994 line, the anti-narcotics
directorate's 995 line and its email address, and said reports would be handled
in strict confidence with no liability for the caller. [@1]
