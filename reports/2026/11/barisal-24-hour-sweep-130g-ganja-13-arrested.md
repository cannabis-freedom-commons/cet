Barisal Metropolitan Police detained two people in a drug case and arrested 11
others in separate cases during a 24-hour operation, a total of 13. [@1] Four
police stations and the detective branch carried out the separate drives in
different parts of the city on 18 November 2026, with the figures published the
next day. [@2]

Officers seized 300 yaba tablets and 130 grams of ganja and detained two people
in the drug case. [@1]

The other arrests comprised one on an arrest warrant, five in regular cases, two
under the Barisal Metropolitan Ordinance and three under other laws. [@1] Police
said 13 people were arrested in all for drug recovery and involvement in various
crimes. [@1] Police said such operations would continue to keep law and order
normal and control crime. [@1]
