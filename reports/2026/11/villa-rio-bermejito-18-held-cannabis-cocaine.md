At about 1:40 on the morning of 21 November 2026, officers of the Villa Río
Bermejito police station were on a prevention patrol along Avenida 9 de Julio.
[@1] The 18-year-old was intercepted with the drugs inside the grounds of an
educational establishment. [@3] The officers tried to identify the three young
people, but they ran, and one was caught a few metres away. [@1] He tried to
discard a fanny pack, which the officers recovered. [@2] Inside the pack they
found 15 wrappers with a whitish substance and two other packages containing
cannabis. [@1] The whitish substance was described as presumably cocaine. [@2]

The Anti-Drug Investigation Prosecutor's Office in Presidencia Roque Sáenz Peña
took over the case. [@1] Proceedings were opened for an alleged infringement of
Argentina's national narcotics law 23.737, under Chaco's provincial law 2304-N
on narcomenudeo, small-scale drug dealing. [@1] Police continued searching for
the other two young people who fled. [@2]
