At 13.12 on Tuesday, Greenland Police received a report from customs officers at
Nuuk Airport. [@1] Customs officers had found no less than 5.4 kg of hash in a
suitcase. [@1] The suitcase had arrived in Nuuk by plane from Denmark. [@1]

The suitcase owner was in detention on Wednesday morning. [@1] Watch chief
Aqqalu Petersen said the owner was waiting to be brought for a preliminary
hearing later that Wednesday. [@1]
