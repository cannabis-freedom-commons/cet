At about 21:00 on 21 November 2026, officers of the district police department
in Botevgrad carried out a late-evening check inside an abandoned building in
the town's Saransk residential complex. [@1] The officers entered a single room
inside that abandoned building in the Saransk complex during the check. [@1]
That room was occupied by a local resident already known to the police for prior
criminal activity. [@1]

During the same check, a 33-year-old man with a criminal record was detained for
possession of narcotic substances. [@1] Inside the occupied room, the police
officers found three grams of herbal matter suspected to be a narcotic
substance. [@1] A police field test applied to that herbal matter at the scene
reacted to cannabis. [@1]

Following the discovery, the 33-year-old man was detained for a period of up to
24 hours. [@1] Police then opened fast-track proceedings in the case for an
offence under Article 354a of the Bulgarian Penal Code. [@1]
