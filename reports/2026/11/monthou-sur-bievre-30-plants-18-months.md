A 37-year-old man living in Monthou-sur-Bièvre was sentenced on 17 November 2026
after a cannabis plantation whose smell drew a gendarmerie patrol was discovered
near the village. [@1]

The case began on 14 October 2026, when gendarmes from the Controis-en-Sologne
community of brigades on a foot patrol near Monthou-sur-Bièvre noticed a strong
smell of cannabis coming from a private property, leading to a judicial
investigation. [@1] On 16 November 2026, a judicial police operation by the
Blois research brigade found a cannabis cultivation with 30 plants and 2.5 kg of
flowers ready for sale, as well as cash and weapons. [@1] The 37-year-old was
placed in police custody and admitted during questioning that he produced and
sold narcotic drugs. [@1]

He appeared the next day before the Blois prosecutor's magistrate under a
plaider-coupable guilty-plea procedure and was sentenced to 18 months'
imprisonment, 10 of them convertible to an electronic bracelet and eight
suspended with two years' probation. [@1] The seized cannabis was incinerated.
[@1] The court also imposed a 2,500 euro fine, a five-year ban on possessing a
weapon and an order to undergo treatment. [@1]
