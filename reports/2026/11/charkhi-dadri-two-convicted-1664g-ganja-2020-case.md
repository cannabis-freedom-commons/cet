Additional Sessions Judge Satish Kumar's court in Charkhi Dadri convicted two
young men over selling ganja in the city. [@1] The court convicted Vikas alias
Vicky and Rahul under the NDPS Act after completing the hearing in the nearly
six-year-old case. [@1] Police had arrested both men on 26 October 2020 in front
of the closed petrol pump near the Dhani railway gate. [@1]

At that time, police recovered 1 kg 622 g of ganja from Vikas and 42 g of ganja
from Rahul, a total of 1.664 kg. [@1] On 26 October 2020, ASI Surendra was
patrolling at Samsapur Chowk with a police team when an informer said Vikas
alias Vicky and his associate Rahul were selling ganja in front of the closed
petrol pump near the Dhani railway gate. [@1] Police reached the spot and caught
both men. [@1] A search recovered 1 kg 622 g of ganja from the black polythene
in Vikas's hand. [@1] A search of Rahul found 42 g of ganja in seven packets in
his trouser pocket, and police also recovered 430 rupees from Vikas's pocket.
[@1]

Police had registered the case against both men at City police station Dadri
under the NDPS Act and filed a charge sheet after completing the investigation.
[@1] The prosecution recorded statements of 11 witnesses in the case. [@1] The
defence questioned the police action, arguing that no independent witness was
joined, and raising the search and recovery procedure and delay in sending
samples to the FSL. [@1] After considering both sides' arguments and the
evidence, the court held that police had proved the recovery and legal-procedure
facts. [@1] The FSL report identified both samples as ganja. [@1] The court held
that both accused had jointly procured ganja and kept it in their possession for
the purpose of selling it. [@1] The court then convicted both under NDPS Act
provisions and ordered that they be taken into custody. [@1]
