A driver was detained and a vehicle seized after a police officer became
suspicious. [@1] The officer noticed the silver Citroën being driven on Ash
Drive in Eye and decided to investigate further. [@1] The driver attempted to
evade police by turning into a busy public car park and parking between other
vehicles. [@1] The officer boxed in the vehicle and the driver was detained.
[@1]

Checks revealed that the driver did not hold a valid driving licence. [@1] The
same checks showed the driver didn't have the right insurance. [@1] The driver
was issued with a Traffic Offence Report. [@1] The vehicle was seized before the
driver was further detained for a stop and search. [@1] Officers found a small
amount of herbal cannabis, police said. [@1]

The driver was then interviewed and reported for that offence as well. [@1]
Suffolk Constabulary was the force behind the Eye stop. [@1]
