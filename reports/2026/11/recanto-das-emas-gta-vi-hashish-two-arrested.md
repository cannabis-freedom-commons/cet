Two people were arrested in flagrante during a drug delivery in Recanto das
Emas, Distrito Federal, on Tuesday 24 November 2026, by civil police of the Drug
Repression Section (SRD) of the 27th Police Station. [@3] The pair were
described as two 21-year-olds. [@1] One of the suspects had already been
detained for the same crime in March 2025. [@1]

The investigation began about a week earlier after an anonymous tip about
suspicious movement at a property in Conjunto 26. [@2] Officers watched the pair
enter the house, collect portions and make two sales through the passenger
window, to a pedestrian in Conjunto 51 and a motorcyclist in Conjunto 9, then
stopped them on their third return. [@3] With the passenger officers found a
portion of cocaine, another of skunk and the house keys. [@1] Inside the house
they found 18 plates of hashish, some stamped with GTA VI and Viva México
stickers, three large tablets and smaller portions of skunk, and several
portions of cocaine hydrochloride. [@1] Police also seized R$ 7,637 in cash,
five precision scales, two knives with drug traces, cling film, ziplock bags,
four mobile phones and bank cards. [@1] The car used for deliveries and a black
Honda CG 160 Titan motorcycle were also collected. [@1]

The driver told officers he had rented the house about a month earlier for R$
1,800 and hired the car at an airport agency specifically to transport the
drugs. [@1] At the 27th Police Station the two were booked for drug trafficking
and association for trafficking, both non-bailable offences, and after a
forensic medical examination both were placed in the Civil Police lock-up at the
disposal of the courts. [@1]
