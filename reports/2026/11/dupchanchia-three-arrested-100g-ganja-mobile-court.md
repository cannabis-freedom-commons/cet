Officers of the Department of Narcotics Control arrested three men with ganja
and tapentadol tablets in an anti-narcotics drive in Dupchanchia upazila of
Bogura district. [@1,2] The drive was carried out in the evening of Tuesday 17
November 2026 in different areas of the upazila. [@1,2] The three arrested were
named as Naim, Aslam Munshi and Mahbub. [@1,2] Their homes were given as Sanjaypur, Baghopara and Khanjoypur villages in the upazila and their ages as 25, 28 and 36. [@2] The team was from DNC Bogura's
'Kha' circle and was led by Dupchanchia assistant commissioner (land) and
executive magistrate Md. Touhidul Islam. [@1,2]

Officers recovered 100 grams of ganja and 10 tapentadol tablets from the three
men. [@1,2]

A mobile court sentenced two of them to six months' imprisonment each and the
third to 25 days of simple imprisonment. [@1,2] The court fined one of the three
100 taka and the other two 1,000 taka each. [@1,2] The drugs seized in the drive
were destroyed at the scene on the executive magistrate's order. [@1,2]
