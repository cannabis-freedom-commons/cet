The Hyderabad Commissioner’s Task Force Charminar Zone team jointly with Bhavani
Nagar police arrested a 44-year-old rowdy-sheeter in Old City on Wednesday,
November 18, who had been absconding under a preventive detention order. [@1]
The man was identified as Mohd Naseer alias Chotu alias Chaku Naseer, who lives
at Talabkatta and works in real estate. [@1]

A detention order under the Telangana Prevention of Dangerous Activities (PD)
Act dated November 10 had been issued against him, but he had gone into hiding.
[@1] The pending detention proceedings were recorded as order SB(1) No.
644/CIU-4/PD/HYD/2026 dated November 10, 2026. [@3] Police said Naseer faces 21
cases, mainly property offences and offences under the Narcotic Drugs and
Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act, at police stations across the Charminar
Zone, and described him as a habitual ganja peddler. [@1]

According to the police, he kept travelling to Nagpur to buy ganja and sold it
in Hyderabad for quick money. [@1] Police said he continued offending despite
earlier cases and had become a threat to public order, prompting detention
proceedings to prevent further activities prejudicial to maintenance of public
order. [@2]

In July he had been arrested with Mohd Mehraj Ali after about 2.3 kg of ganja
was seized near the railway track at Talabkatta, which police said the pair
bought from Nagpur suppliers to sell in small packets in Old City. [@1]
