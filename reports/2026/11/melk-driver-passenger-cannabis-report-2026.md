On the evening of 22 November 2026 at about 21:15, officers of the St. Poelten
aliens and border police inspection stopped a 41-year-old Hungarian driver and
his car on Wachbergstrasse in Melk. [@1] Inside the car during that same
roadside stop, the officers noticed a strong smell of cannabis. [@1]

When officers searched the stopped car on Wachbergstrasse, they found small
amounts of cannabis herb together with cannabis utensils inside it. [@1] Police
seized both the cannabis herb and the utensils found in the car. [@1] A
subsequent official medical examination of the driver found that the 41-year-old
was impaired by intoxicants at the time of the control. [@1] Officers therefore
provisionally took away his driving licence on the spot and forbade him from
continuing his journey by car. [@1]

Separately from the drug report, the driver was reported to the competent
district administrative authority in connection with the impaired driving. [@1]
The driver and his 46-year-old Hungarian passenger were then both reported to
the St. Poelten public prosecution office. [@1] Those two reports were for
offences under Austria's Narcotic Substances Act, the Suchtmittelgesetz. [@1]
