Ciudad Juárez municipal police arrested three people aged 19 to 23 in separate
incidents across the city, reporting about 158 grams of marihuana and 13 grams
of crystal. [@2] Jesús Manuel F. C., 19, was detained at the crossing of Cerro
de la Silla and Sierra Madre del Sur streets in colonia El Jarudo with two
plastic wrappers of marihuana weighing about 118 grams. [@1] Ángel L. H., 23,
was arrested at the crossing of Urique and La Cruz streets in colonia Anáhuac
with a plastic wrapper of crystal weighing about 13 grams. [@1] Jorge C. A., 23,
was detained at the crossing of avenida Miguel de la Madrid and Garambullo
street in colonia Héroes de la Revolución with a wrapper of about 40 grams of
marihuana. [@1]

The Municipal Public Security Secretariat said the three were arrested in
separate incidents on suspicion of possessing marihuana and crystal
methamphetamine. [@1] The city force reported about 158 grams of marihuana in
all, with the 13 grams of crystal counted separately. [@2] The stops took place
in different sectors of the city, and those detained were placed at the disposal
of the corresponding authority. [@3]

After their rights were read, the three were consigned to the corresponding
authority for their presumed responsibility in the offence. [@1] The cases were
referred as alleged crimes against health in the narcomenudeo form, the Mexican
term for street-level drug dealing. [@2]
