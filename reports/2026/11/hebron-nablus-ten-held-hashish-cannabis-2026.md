Palestinian police arrested a suspected drug trader in Hebron on Monday 23
November 2026, after months of surveillance. [@2] The Hebron suspect had escaped
during an earlier seizure operation in which nine slabs of hashish were
confiscated. [@1]

In Nablus the same day, anti-narcotics police arrested two people suspected of
dealing drugs and detained seven people described as users. [@1] The material
seized in Nablus included hashish, hybridised cannabis, Larica pills and
ecstasy, none of it with a published weight. [@1]

Police said the suspects and the seized items were held to complete the legal
procedures against them. [@1] The General Secretariat of the Council of Arab
Interior Ministers carried the statement on 24 November 2026. [@3]
