Fatehpur police in Saharanpur, Uttar Pradesh, arrested a man they described as a
seasoned drug smuggler, identified as Sakib, during a checking campaign near
Bhatpura Kalasia Road and recovered 90 grams of illegal charas, a cannabis
resin, from his possession. [@1] Sakib, the son of Taslim, lives in Muslim
Colony in the town of Chutmalpur, within the Fatehpur police station area of
Saharanpur district. [@1] Police stopped Sakib near Bhatpura Kalasia Road,
searched him and arrested him immediately after recovering the charas. [@1]

The checking campaign in the Fatehpur police area ran under the supervision of
the area's circle officer near Sadar and was led by Station House Officer
Narendra Bhadana. [@1]

A case, number 464/26, was registered against Sakib at Fatehpur police station
under section 8/20 of the NDPS Act, India's main anti-drug law, and police said
they were completing legal action and producing him before a court in time. [@1]
