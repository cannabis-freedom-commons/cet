Rosselkhoznadzor, Russia's federal agricultural and phytosanitary watchdog,
carried out two field surveys of farmland in Kyzyl district, in the Republic of
Tyva. [@1] Its inspectors checked more than 169 hectares of pasture and hayland
during the two surveys. [@1] They used drones and the Inspector mobile app to
carry out the checks. [@1]

One plot of 53.3 hectares, leased by the head of a peasant farm, a small family
farm, was 24% overgrown with weeds. [@1] The neighbouring plot of 50 hectares,
leased by a local resident, was 43% overgrown with wild hemp. [@1]

After the surveys, Rosselkhoznadzor filed representations against the
responsible persons, requiring them to destroy the dry weed stalks and the hemp
on the two plots. [@1] The watchdog reminded land users of their obligation to
protect their land in good time from overgrowth by trees, bushes and weeds. [@1]
The results were reported by the directorate of Rosselkhoznadzor for the
republics of Khakassia and Tyva. [@1]
