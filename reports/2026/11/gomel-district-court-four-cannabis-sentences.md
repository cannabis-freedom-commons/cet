The Gomel district court sentenced four defendants in a case of illegal drug
circulation. [@1] One of the defendants grew cannabis in the basement of his
house and made more than 250 grams of marijuana from it, according to Gomel
prosecutor's assistant Alexander Shapovalov. [@1] The events took place from 31
March to 1 April 2026. [@1]

The grower sold part of the marijuana to an acquaintance for 220 dollars and
gave part away free; the court gave him seven years in a reinforced-regime
colony and a fine of 5,400 Belarusian rubles. [@1] His friend sold marijuana to
his brother for 500 dollars and later took another batch for himself and to
resell; he received seven and a half years in a reinforced-regime colony and a
fine of 6,750 rubles. [@1] An intermediary who took money from the buyer, bought
prohibited substances from the seller and was detained at the moment of transfer
received three years in a reinforced-regime colony and a fine of 2,250 rubles.
[@1] The buyer, who wanted marijuana for himself, received one year and six
months of restricted freedom and compulsory treatment for drug addiction. [@1]

The judgment has not entered into legal force and may be appealed and protested.
