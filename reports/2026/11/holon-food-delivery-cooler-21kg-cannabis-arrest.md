Detectives from Glilot station in the Yarkon area arrested a 25-year-old Holon
resident on Sunday 15 November 2026 after spotting him riding a scooter carrying
a food-delivery cooler. [@1] A search of the cooler found suspected drugs, some
disguised as sweets and allegedly intended for distribution to customers. [@1]
Police suspect the man acted as a courier in a distribution network that
supplied customers through the Telegram app, and that his flat served as the
network's warehouse. [@1] Following the arrest, officers searched his flat in
Holon and found additional suspected drugs already divided and packed for
immediate distribution. [@1] The suspect was taken for questioning at Glilot
station, and police said they would bring him to court on Monday to request an
extension of his detention. [@1]

Police said officers seized a total of more than 20 kilograms gross of suspected
drugs from the cooler and the flat, including cookies, gummy-bear sweets and
cannabis products. [@1] The seizure included about 7 kilograms gross of
suspected marijuana, about 3.5 kilograms of vapes containing suspected cannabis
oil, about 3 kilograms of cookies suspected of containing drugs and about 2.3
kilograms of gummy-bear sweets suspected of containing drugs. [@1] Officers also
seized about 4.6 kilograms of suspected cannabis oil, about 450 grams of
suspected Moroccan hashish and about 340 grams of additional suspected hashish.
[@1] A Channel 14 account of the same seizure describes the hashish together as
about 790 grams of suspected hashish. [@3]

All of the published weights are gross weights, and every item was described
only as suspected pending laboratory checks. [@1] A second local account
confirms that the flat was suspected of serving as a Telegram network warehouse
holding material already divided and packed for immediate sale. [@2]
