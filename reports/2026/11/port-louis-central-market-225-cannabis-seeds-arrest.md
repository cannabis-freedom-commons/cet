A 32-year-old man identified as Jeremie H, a Roche-Bois resident, was searched
by Tornado Squad officers near Port Louis's central market on Tuesday 24
November 2026. [@1] The officers who searched him were constables Kownden and
Appavoo. [@1] Their search turned up 225 cannabis seeds concealed in a plastic
bag. [@1] The man asked the officers for mercy, saying 'Donn-mwa enn sans', but
was placed under arrest. [@1]

The arrest was the second of two Tornado Squad operations in Port Louis reported
for 22 and 24 November. [@1] The first operation, on Sunday 22 November, was at
the Deschartes avenue bus stop. [@1] There a man identified as Jiovanny M
discarded a pencil case when he saw the officers, led by sergeants Mohun and
Hatteea. [@1] Officers found 50 doses of synthetic drugs and 10 doses of heroin
in the pencil case. [@1]
