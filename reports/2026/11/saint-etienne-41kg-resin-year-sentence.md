A 31-year-old man was sentenced to twelve months' firm imprisonment by the
Saint-Étienne tribunal on 24 November 2026, after cannabis and cocaine were
found on him and at his home. [@1] Le Progrès said his arrest came at the end of
an investigation involving several national police units. [@1]

The case began on 11 October, when neighbours found drugs in the basement
stairwell of the building where he lived. [@1] Police seized 2.5 kg of cannabis
resin, 400 g of cocaine and 90 g of cannabis herb. [@1] Fingerprint analysis
linked the drugs to the man, a resident of the building. [@1] Le Progrès places
the building in the cours Fauriel neighbourhood and says residents called police
after the discovery. [@2] Officers placed the suspect under surveillance and
stopped him on rue Gambetta on Sunday 22 November. [@2] The paper put the total
resin seized in the case at 4.1 kg. [@2]

At the stop he carried three bars of cannabis resin, €1,500 in cash and a
precision scale. [@1] A search of the room he occupied at his parents' home
found fourteen more bars, bringing the resin seized that day to 1.6 kg, along
with €100 in cash and 1 g of cocaine. [@1] He was presented to a magistrate the
day after his arrest. [@1] Le Progrès says he appeared before the Saint-Étienne
judicial court the next day in a comparution immédiate, an immediate court
appearance, and received twelve months' firm imprisonment with a committal
warrant. [@2]
