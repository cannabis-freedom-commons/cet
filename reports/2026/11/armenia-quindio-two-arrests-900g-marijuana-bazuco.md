In two separate procedures in Armenia, the National Police in Quindío captured
two people found with illicit substances in their possession. [@1] The first
procedure was carried out by officers of the Armenia Police Station at Calle 30
with Carrera 31 in barrio Quindío, where they intercepted a 30-year-old man
during registration and control patrols. [@1] After officers found a bag
containing bazuco among his belongings during the personal search, the man was
immediately captured for trafficking, manufacture or possession of narcotics.
[@1] He was then transferred to the Fiscalía General de la Nación, where the
judicialization process began. [@1]

The second operation took place at Carrera 17 with Calle 35 in barrio Santa Fe
in Armenia, where officers carrying out surveillance saw a 28-year-old man who
acted evasively on noticing the police presence. [@1] During the personal search
officers found that he was carrying a bag with about 900 grams of marijuana.
[@1] He was captured for trafficking, manufacture or possession of narcotics and
placed at the disposal of the competent judicial authority for proceedings
against him. [@1]

The patrol, control and surveillance actions yielded the seizure of bazuco and
approximately 900 grams of marijuana as part of the institutional strategy to
prevent and contain local drug trafficking. [@1] National Police in Quindío said
the actions are part of a continuous operational deployment focused on combating
micro-trafficking and restoring calm in Armenia's neighbourhoods. [@1]
