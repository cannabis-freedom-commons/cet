Police in Dharampasha Upazila, Sunamganj district, arrested two men during a
special drive on Tuesday, 17 November 2026. [@1] The two were held as they
smoked ganja, the Bengali term for cannabis, during the operation. [@1] Police
identified them as Babul Mia, 32, son of Nurul Islam of Noaband Ukilpara
village, and Rubel, 35, son of the late Fazal Ali of Abuarachor village. [@1]

Officers seized 10 grams of ganja, a wooden pipe about six inches long and a red
Platina motorcycle from them. [@1] The drive covered several areas of the
upazila on the instructions of Dharampasha police station officer-in-charge
Mohammad Anwar Hossain. [@1]

Produced before the Dharampasha court, the two were tried in a summary trial and
the magistrate sentenced each of them to one month of simple imprisonment and a
2,000-taka fine. [@1] In default of the fine each was ordered to serve seven
more days of simple imprisonment. [@1] Anwar Hossain said police operations
against drugs, gambling and online gambling were continuing. [@1]
