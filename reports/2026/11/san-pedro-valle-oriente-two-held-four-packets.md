San Pedro police detained two men in Colonia Valle Oriente for their probable
participation in delitos contra la salud after officers found four wrappers of
dry herb with characteristics similar to marijuana. [@1] Officers on prevention
and surveillance patrols on Avenida Lázaro Cárdenas, near the crossing with
María Izquierdo street, spotted the two arguing in the street. [@1] When
officers approached to check the situation, both men reacted aggressively and
ignored the authority's instructions. [@1]

A preventive inspection found two transparent plastic bags of dry herb with
characteristics similar to marijuana in each man's belongings, four wrappers in
total. [@1] The detained men were identified as Moisés “N”, aged 51, and Jordan
“N”, aged 35, held for their probable participation in delitos contra la salud.
[@2] Officers told the men they would be detained for disturbing public order,
then found two transparent plastic bags of dry herb resembling marijuana on each
of them during the preventive inspection. [@2] Both men were taken to the C2
facilities in San Pedro with the seized items to be placed at the disposal of
the Ministerio Público. [@1] That authority is to determine their legal
situation and continue the corresponding investigations. [@1]
