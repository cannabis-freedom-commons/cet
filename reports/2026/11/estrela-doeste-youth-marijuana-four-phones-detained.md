A 20-year-old identified by the initials T. F. S. was detained by the Military
Police on the morning of Friday 13 November after trying to flee an approach in
central Estrela d’Oeste. [@1] The youth took off his flip-flops to run faster
after seeing a patrol car, and an officer chased him on foot for about two
blocks before catching him after he tripped and fell. [@1]

He told police he had fled because he was carrying a marijuana cigarette. [@1]
Officers then searched his home and found another portion of the same drug and
four smartphones whose lawful origin he could not explain. [@1]

All of the material and the electronics were taken to the Estrela d’Oeste police
station. [@1] After giving a statement to the duty delegate, the youth was
released and will answer in freedom for drug possession, while the four phones
remained seized for investigation into whether they came from theft or robbery.
[@1]
