Officers of the ADSU arrested a 65-year-old retiree who resides in Upper
Dagotière during an afternoon operation on the parking lot of a hypermarket in
Ébène on Friday 13 November 2026 at about 16:55. [@1] The officers intercepted
the sexagenarian on the parking lot in Ébène and took him into custody at the
scene. [@1]

When they searched him, they found nine separate packets wrapped in transparent
plastic, each containing a significant quantity of cannabis. [@1] The cannabis
in the nine packets weighed 460 grams in total, which the officers seized. [@1]

After the parking-lot arrest, officers carried out a follow-up search of the
suspect's home in Upper Dagotière to check for any additional drugs or
incriminating objects kept there. [@1] The home search turned up nothing, with
neither another narcotic product nor any incriminating object discovered at the
house. [@1] The suspect was placed in police detention, and the investigation
was continuing. [@1]
