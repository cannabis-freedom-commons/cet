Indore police arrested eight people in seven separate thana-area actions against
buying, selling and smuggling of narcotics. [@1,2] Tejaji Nagar police detained
Bharat Senani on the service road near Sej University bridge in Indore. [@1]
Gandhinagar police caught Ritesh alias Rahul Sharma on open ground near Vinod
Multi in Gandhinagar, Indore. [@1] Dwarkapuri police caught Abhishek in front of
the Akash Nagar water tank on Ahir-Khedi Kakad Road in Indore. [@1] The seven thana areas were Chandan Nagar, Tejaji Nagar, Khajrana, Hiranagar, Bhanwarkuan, Gandhinagar and Dwarkapuri. [@2]

A search of Bharat Senani found 1 kg 703 g of illegal ganja. [@1] A search of
Ritesh alias Rahul Sharma found 837 g of illegal ganja. [@1] A search of
Abhishek found 1 kg 499 g of illegal ganja. [@1] Police totalled the drive at 4
kg 39 g of illegal ganja, alongside 35.02 g of MD drugs and 18.90 g of brown
sugar held by the other five accused. [@1] Police also seized one motorcycle
allegedly used in the trafficking. [@1,2]

Police registered cases under the NDPS Act, India's main drug law. [@1] Police
said all eight were being questioned in detail about buying and selling,
sources, suppliers and the trafficking network. [@1,2] Police said further
investigation and legal action were continuing in all cases. [@1] Additional DCP Rajesh Dandotiya said action against people involved in the drug trade would continue. [@2]
