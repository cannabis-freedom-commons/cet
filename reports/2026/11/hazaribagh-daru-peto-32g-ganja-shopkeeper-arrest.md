Daru police in Hazaribagh district, Jharkhand, seized about 32 grams of ganja
from a shop in Peto and arrested the shopkeeper. [@1] The raid was carried out
on Friday 20 November 2026 by a team led by Daru station house officer
Mrityunjay Kumar. [@1] The shop, Prakash General Store, stands beside the NH-522
road in Peto. [@1]

Officers recovered about 32 grams of ganja from the shop during the search. [@1]
Kumar said police had received information that illegal narcotics were being
kept and sold at the shop, and acted on it. [@1] The shopkeeper, Jaiprakash
Kushwaha, was arrested and sent to judicial custody. [@1]

Kumar said Daru police were running a continuing series of raids against the
drug trade and drug use in the police-station area. [@1] He asked residents to
give police information about people connected with the drug trade, saying the
informant's name and address would be kept confidential. [@1]
