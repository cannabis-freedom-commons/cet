Polrestabes Medan, the city police force in Medan, North Sumatra, reported 123
drug cases and 306 suspects during the 37 days of the national police chief's
Quick Win programme. [@1] The force said the programme ran from 15 October to 20
November 2026. [@1] Medan police chief Kombes Pol Jean Calvijn Simanjuntak gave
the figures at a press conference at Polrestabes Medan on Saturday 21 November
2026. [@1]

The cannabis in the haul was 20 kilograms of ganja. [@1] The same haul included
60 kilograms of sabu, or crystal methamphetamine, more than 10,000 ecstasy pills
and 4,898 vape cartridges, none of which is counted as cannabis. [@1] A second
Medan account of the same press conference described 80 kilograms of seized
drugs, 60 of them sabu and 20 ganja. [@2] Analisadaily said the vape cartridges
contained etomidate, a sedative, rather than cannabis. [@2] Analisadaily also
reported that the force held 306 suspects in the same 37-day period. [@2]

Officers carried out 360 raids on drug dens during the 37 days, far more than
the 196 recorded in the previous year. [@1] Police named seven areas with the
heaviest enforcement, including Jermal, Sei Mencirim, the Tembung railway line,
Klambir 5, Jalan Denai Gang Jati and Sibolangit. [@1] Calvijn said recorded
crime in Jermal fell 15 percent once officers added patrols and talks with
residents to enforcement. [@1]
