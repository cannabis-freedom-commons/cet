Luxor police arrested a registered criminal found with a range of drugs and a
firearm in the Al-Awamiya area of Luxor. [@1] The suspect was a 40-year-old
unemployed man with a previous criminal record, wanted on two arrest warrants
over earlier shooting and drug incidents and other court judgments. [@2]

After legal procedures, officers led by Major Ahmed Nassef seized about half a
kilogram of hashish, roughly 240 grams of ice ("shabu"), 150 grams of "hydro"
and 15 grams of heroin, along with a shotgun, four rounds, cash and a phone;
only the hashish is counted as cannabis here. [@1] Confronted with the haul, he
admitted possessing the drugs for trafficking, the gun and ammunition for
self-defence, the cash as proceeds of sales and the phone for contacting
customers. [@3]

Police drew up a report and referred the case to the prosecution, which ordered
the suspect held for four days pending investigation. [@1]
