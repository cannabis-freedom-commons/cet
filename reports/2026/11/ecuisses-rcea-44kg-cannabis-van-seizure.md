Customs officers from the Chalon-sur-Saône brigade stopped a light utility van
on the RCEA at Écuisses, in Saône-et-Loire. [@1] The interception took place on
Monday 16 November 2026. [@1] The van was driven by a man of Polish nationality.
[@1]

In the vehicle the officers found 44 kilograms of cannabis. [@1] The same van
was carrying seven kilograms of cocaine. [@1] The cocaine is not a cannabis
product and is left out of the cannabis figures recorded for this seizure. [@1]
