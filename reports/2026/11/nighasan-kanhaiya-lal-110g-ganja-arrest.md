Nighasan police in Lakhimpur Kheri district, Uttar Pradesh, arrested Kanhaiya
Lal, a resident of Rakehti, on Sunday 22 November 2026 with 110 grams of ganja
(cannabis). [@1] Acting on an informant's tip, officers stopped him at the
Paragipurwa turn as he cycled from Rakehti towards Nighasan with the drug. [@1]
CO Shamsher Bahadur Singh said a patrol including Jhandi checkpoint in-charge
Rajveer Singh was checking suspects in the area that day. [@1]

A search found 110 grams of ganja wrapped in yellow and black foil in the right
pocket of his trousers; police sealed it and impounded his bicycle. [@1]
Nighasan police station registered the case as FIR No. 418/2026 under sections
8/20(b)(11)(a) of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act.
[@3] He was presented in court on Monday. [@2] Police identified him as Kanhaiya
Lal, 24, son of Santosh Gupta. [@2] Police said he already had a case registered
against him in 2022 under sections 323/504 of the Indian Penal Code and the
SC/ST Act. [@3] Officers said they were investigating where he obtained the
ganja and to whom he sold it. [@3] The arresting team included sub-inspector
Rajveer Singh and constables Rishabhdev Pipariya and Ankit Soni. [@4]

CO Singh said the drive to stop the trade in intoxicants and maintain law and
order would continue. [@1]
