Two prisoners were executed at Bandar Abbas Prison on Thursday 19 November 2026.
[@1] The two men were Mostafa Yousefian, about 38, of Aran and Bidgol in Isfahan
Province, and Peyman Sedaghat, about 52, a father of two from Bandar Abbas. [@2]

Sedaghat was arrested in 2021 on charges of selling and distributing 75
kilograms of hashish and was sentenced to death by the Revolutionary Court. [@1]
The other man, Yousefian, was arrested in 2021 on charges of murder and armed
robbery and subsequently sentenced to death by a judicial authority. [@1]

Prison authorities and other relevant institutions had not officially announced
the executions at the time of reporting. [@1]
