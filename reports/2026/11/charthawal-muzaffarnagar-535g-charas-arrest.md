Police in Charthawal, a town in Muzaffarnagar district of western Uttar Pradesh,
arrested a man described by officers as a history-sheeter during a check. [@1]
The man was carrying 535 grams of charas, a hand-pressed cannabis resin, when
officers stopped him. [@1]

The police said the charas was worth about 1.5 lakh rupees, or 150,000 rupees.
[@1] Officers also recovered a motorcycle from the man. [@1] An electric
weighing scale was recovered along with the motorcycle. [@1]
