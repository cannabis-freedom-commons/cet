Acting on information that ganja, gutka and other intoxicants were being
smuggled into Tamil Nadu from other states on trains passing through
Jolarpettai, Salem railway anti-narcotics police were carrying out monitoring
and checks. [@1] Early on Monday morning, police searched the Sabari Express
running from Hyderabad station in Andhra Pradesh via Jolarpettai to
Thiruvananthapuram in Kerala as it travelled between Vaniyambadi and Jolarpettai
stations. [@1] In the train's general compartment, officers recovered an
unclaimed bag left in the coach and, on searching it during the check, found
that it contained 2 kg of ganja. [@1]

Salem division anti-narcotics police seized the 2 kg of ganja smuggled on the
Sabari Express via Jolarpettai. [@1] Because the people who had smuggled the
ganja could not be identified, the anti-narcotics police handed the seized ganja
to Jolarpettai Railway Police for further action. [@1] Jolarpettai Railway
Police registered a case over the seizure and are investigating to trace the
unidentified people who had carried the ganja on the train. [@1]
