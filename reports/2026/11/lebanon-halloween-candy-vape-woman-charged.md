Lebanon police were called on 1 November 2026 by a father who reported finding a
THC vape filled with marijuana in his children's Halloween candy bag the night
before. [@1] The children told officers that Hali Maxwell, 26, put the device in
their bag while they were trick-or-treating. [@1] Police later tracked Maxwell
to her workplace in Lebanon. [@1]

Asked whether she was missing a vape, Maxwell pulled out a nicotine vape from
her pocket and said no, according to court documents. [@1] Shown the device, she
denied it was hers, then told an officer 'fine it is mine'. [@1] She told
officers the device was a 'Delta' vape bought at a local smoke shop and that it
must have fallen out of her fanny pack while the children were near her on
Halloween. [@1]

Police arrested Maxwell and booked her into the Boone County Jail. [@1] She was
charged on 25 November 2026 with felony neglect of a dependent and misdemeanor
possession of paraphernalia. [@1] Court records show the incident was also
reported to the Department of Child Services. [@1]
