Military motorcycle-patrol teams went to a house on Rua Ezequiel Ferreira Lima
in the Aero Rancho neighbourhood of Campo Grande at about 21:00 on Sunday 15
November 2026 after an anonymous resident reported that a man wanted by the
courts was using it to store drugs. [@1] At the address officers found the
suspect at the gate, where he threw away a bag holding a substance similar to
marijuana and tried to run back into the house but was caught. [@1] During the
stop the teams smelled a strong odour of marijuana from the property, and the
man admitted storing drugs there and showed where they were hidden. [@1]

The search found 76 portions of marijuana packed in suitcases and hidden in
different rooms, including under a washing machine, inside a wardrobe and in the
bathroom, totalling 58.8 kilograms according to the police record. [@1] A check
of the national prison-monitoring database confirmed a pending arrest warrant
against the man, and he was taken to the Cepol specialist-police centre with two
seized precision scales. [@1]

The seized marijuana was presented to Denar, the specialist narcotics-repression
station, in an operation supported by a Military Police Tactical Force team.
