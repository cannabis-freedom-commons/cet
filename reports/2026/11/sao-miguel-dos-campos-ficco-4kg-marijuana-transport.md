Officers from the Força Integrada de Combate ao Crime Organizado em Alagoas
(FICCO/AL) seized about four kilograms of marijuana from a vehicle used in
alternative passenger transport in São Miguel dos Campos on Wednesday 25
November 2026. [@1] The seizure was made during an inspection of vehicles used
in alternative passenger transport, the privately run vans and minibuses that
supplement scheduled bus services, in the municipality. [@1] The operation was
carried out jointly with the Military Police's intelligence directorate
(DINT/PMAL) and the special operations group GOPES of the 1ª Companhia
Independente (GOPES – 1ª CPM/I). [@1]

FICCO/AL is made up of the Federal Police, the Military Police of Alagoas and
the state prison police. [@1] FICCO/AL described the inspection as part of a
strategy to strengthen its operations in the interior of the state and to
intensify prevention and repression of organised crime. [@1] The drug was sent
for judicial police procedures and forensic examination to confirm the
material's nature and to identify those involved. [@1]

The Federal Police's own statement said FICCO/AL seized about four kilograms of
narcotic substances on 25 November during the inspection in the municipality.
[@2] The same statement described the material as a substance with
characteristics of marijuana. [@2]
