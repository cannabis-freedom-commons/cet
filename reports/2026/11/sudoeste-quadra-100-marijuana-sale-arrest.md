A man was arrested in flagrante on the night of 25 November 2026 after selling
marijuana in a community exercise area in Quadra 100 of Sudoeste, Brasília. [@1]
Officers of the Military Police of the Federal District (PMDF) seized two
portions of the drug, marijuana seeds and a precision scale. [@1] Three other
people were also approached and cited for use and possession of narcotics. [@1]

Police said officers were patrolling the area at about 20:40 when they saw the
man change direction on spotting the patrol car, and that once stopped he was
carrying narcotics. [@1] Moments earlier the suspect had been talking with three
other people in the community space, and officers stopped the group as well.
[@1] One of the women had a portion of marijuana, which she said she had bought
from the man; he confessed to selling narcotics at the site. [@1]

Those involved were taken to the 5th Police Station, where the occurrence was
recorded. [@1]
