Surendranagar SOG and Dhajala police raided two sites in Sayla taluka in a
single day on tip-offs. [@1] District police said they had tightened action
against the illegal drug trade in Sayla taluka. [@1] Cultivation cases had
surfaced in the rural areas of Sayla taluka before these raids. [@1] At Sokhda
village, an SOG team raided a farm on the village outskirts and found two
illegally grown green ganja plants. [@1] Police booked Sokhda farm grower Dana
Devabhai Sheikh under the NDPS Act and took legal action. [@1]

The Sokhda seizure weighed 4 kg 370 g, estimated by police at about Rs 218,500.
[@1] Single-day raids in Sokhda and Navagam yielded 10 kg 520 g of green ganja
worth Rs 5.26 lakh. [@1] Police named two accused in NDPS cases over the
seizures. [@1]
