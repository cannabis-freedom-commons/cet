The Special Response Battalion arrested 45-year-old Asadul Molla in an anti-drug
operation in Mirpur, Kushtia, seizing yaba tablets and ganja alongside cash and
a mobile phone. [@1] The operation was conducted at 5:35 pm on Thursday 19
November in Koborbariya village, Ward No. 5 of Barui Para Union under Mirpur
Upazila in Kushtia. [@1] An operational team of SRB-12, CPC-1, Kushtia camp, led
by its camp commander and acting on secret information, arrested him on the
paved road on the north side of Abdar member's house. [@1] The arrested man was
identified as the son of the late Ansar Molla of Koborbariya village. [@1]

Citing SRB sources, the account said the team recovered 33 pieces of yaba
tablets and 28 grams of ganja from his possession. [@1] The team also recovered
7,580 taka in cash described as proceeds from drug sales and one old Android
mobile phone. [@1]

The process of filing a case against the arrested man at Mirpur Police Station
in Kushtia with the seized drugs and other seized items was under way. [@1]
