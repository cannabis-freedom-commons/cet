A man who was guarding and distributing a stock of drugs was arrested in
flagrante, taken to the 151st Civil Police station and booked there, where he
remained at the disposal of the courts. [@1] The arrest was for drug
trafficking. [@2] The operation involved the civil police's 151st precinct and
the reserve service of the 11th Military Police Battalion. [@1]

The operation took place on Wednesday 18 November after the battalion's
intelligence unit identified a property used to store drugs destined for
trafficking in the Cordoeira neighbourhood. [@2] At the property officers seized
400 tablets of marijuana and 75 sachets of hashish. [@2] The cannabis formed
part of 3,424 drug units that also included 2,949 packets of cocaine, according
to the military police. [@1] The military police put the estimated loss to local
trafficking from the whole seizure at 50,000 reais. [@2]

The drugs were hidden in a residential property on Rua Augusto Severo in Nova
Friburgo's city centre, in an area described as 'asfalto' outside the community,
and were attributed to the Comando Vermelho faction. [@1]
