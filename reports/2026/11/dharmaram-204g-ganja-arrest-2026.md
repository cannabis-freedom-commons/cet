Police in Dharmaram mandal, Telangana, detained a man at Sheetala Cheruvu, a
pond beside the mandal centre, who they said had brought dried ganja there to
sell. [@1] Officers took Vemula Srinivas, 53, son of Guruvulu and a resident of
Jawaharnagar in Sultanabad village, into custody at the spot. [@1] They seized
204 grams of dried ganja, the local name for cannabis, from him. [@1]

Dharmaram Sub-Inspector M. B. Srikanth said a case had been registered over the
incident. [@1] The account names no other suspect and says nothing about where
the ganja was grown or whom Srinivas meant to sell it to. [@1]
