National Police officers in Ibiza arrested two Spanish men as alleged drug
traffickers after stopping their car for driving the wrong way on Avenida Ignasi
Wallis. [@1] Officers from the Citizen Attention Group on crime-prevention
duties spotted the car with two occupants going the wrong way early on Thursday
morning, creating a risk for other road users. [@1]

During the search of the vehicle officers found in a shoulder bag a plastic bag
containing about 50 grams of cocaine. [@1] In the same bag they found another
bag with a wad of 500, 200, 100 and 50 euro notes totalling 5,000 euros. [@1] In
another compartment of the same shoulder bag officers found about 250 euros in
small-denomination notes and hashish. [@1] Officers also seized a computer and a
tablet whose origin the two men said they did not know. [@1]

During the identification officers noted the driver showed possible signs of
drunkenness or drug use and asked the Local Police to administer alcohol and
drug tests. [@1] The driver refused to take the alcohol and drug tests despite
repeated warnings that refusal constituted an offence. [@1] Officers finally
detained both vehicle occupants as alleged drug traffickers, with the driver
also accused of a road-safety offence. [@1]
