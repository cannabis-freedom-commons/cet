A 19-year-old was arrested for drug trafficking on Friday 20 November 2026 in
Campo Grande. [@1] Officers of the Batalhão de Choque carried out the operation.
[@1] The youth was found in the Dom Antônio neighbourhood and showed officers a
property where the drugs and weapons were kept with help from another person.
[@1]

Officers seized about 1.4 kg of maconha, a 9 mm pistol and 16 rounds of
ammunition. [@1] At the storage address officers also found an imitation
firearm, a digital scale, a mobile phone and a notebook with notes. [@1]

The investigation began after police received reports of drug sales in Jardim
Pênfigo and use of a motorcycle to support the activity. [@1] During the
inquiries officers also received information about a suspect's possible
involvement in vehicle and goods robberies in the capital. [@1] The youth
confessed to taking part in earlier robberies in Campo Grande and gave
information about other people involved and places where stolen goods were
stored. [@1] He was taken with the seized material to Depac Cepol. [@1] Teams
also searched for the other suspects. [@1]
