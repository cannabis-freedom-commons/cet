Kriminalpolizei Passau, backed by the Zentrale Einsatzdienste Passau, executed
search warrants in the southern part of Landkreis Passau on 24 November 2026 on
suspicion of unlawful drug dealing in non-trivial quantities. [@2] The operation
consisted of two searches. [@1] The searches covered residential and business
premises. [@2] They were directed at a 27-year-old Syrian man. [@2] He was
suspected of possessing and dealing in drugs in non-trivial quantities. [@3]

The 27-year-old was brought before an investigating judge at the Amtsgericht
Passau on 25 November 2026. [@2] The judge executed an arrest warrant applied
for by the Passau public prosecutor's office. [@2] The man, described as a
27-year-old Syrian, remained in pretrial detention. [@4] Officers found about
150 grams of marijuana and about 13 grams of cocaine. [@1] They also seized just
under 7,000 euros in cash, several mobile phones and prescription medicines.
[@2]

The Passau criminal police investigation was continuing under the direction of
the Passau public prosecutor's office. [@2]
