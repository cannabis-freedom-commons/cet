Two women, aged 27 and 30, were searched at a police station in Guarapuava,
where officers found cash in their clothing. [@1] A man was also taken to the
station, making three people in all. [@2]

Police pursued a Volkswagen Gol in the Conradinho neighbourhood of Guarapuava
after its driver fled an attempted stop. [@1] The incident happened on the
afternoon of Tuesday 17 November 2026. [@2] During the chase officers saw the
occupants throwing objects from the car and, after stopping it, found nothing
illegal in the vehicle or on those inside. [@1] They returned to where the
objects had been dropped and found two mobile phones and a substance resembling
hashish. [@1]

Police had been told the car was being used to carry illicit goods and that it
was driving along Avenida Manoel Ribas towards the highway. [@1] The occurrence
was registered around 16:30 and classified as acquiring, selling, supplying
and/or producing drugs. [@1] The case remains under investigation. [@1]
