Police arrested a man on suspicion of drug trafficking on the night of Thursday
19 November 2026 during a police operation in the Ernesto Kuhl neighborhood of
Limeira, a city in the state of São Paulo. [@1] According to the occurrence
report, officers were patrolling Rua Canudos when they saw the man handling an
object next to the water meter of a residence. [@1]

During the approach, officers found five microtubos, small tubes, of marijuana
and seven portions of cocaine on the man. [@1] A search of the point where the
man had been standing led the officers to locate more drugs there. [@1] In
total, police seized 57 microtubos and 15 ziplock-style packages of marijuana,
37 microtubos of cocaine and one microtubo of crack. [@1] The quantities
recorded for the seizure amounted to 233 grams of marijuana in all. [@1]

The man was taken to the Plantão Policial, the police station in Limeira, where
the occurrence was registered, and he remained in custody at the disposal of the
courts. [@1]
