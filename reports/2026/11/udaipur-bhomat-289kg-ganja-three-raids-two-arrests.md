Udaipur police seized 289.2 kilograms of illegal ganja and cannabis crop and
arrested two people in three joint operations under the Ganja Free Bhomat
campaign. [@1] The campaign is run on the directions of Udaipur Superintendent
of Police Dr Amrita Duhan and targets the cultivation, storage and trafficking
of narcotics. [@1]

On 20 November a Kotra police patrol reached Chak Sandmariya village, where a
large quantity of dried and wet ganja was found laid out to dry in the courtyard
of a house. [@1] The suspects fled when they saw the police, and officers seized
212.2 kilograms of ganja, registered a case under the NDPS Act and continued
searching for them. [@1]

On 19 November TD police station and the Udaipur District Special Team, or DST,
ran a search operation in the Saru Phala Daban area, seized ganja plants and
dried ganja from outside a house and arrested Nana Meena, 60. [@1] That evening
police surrounded and caught Deva Meena, 65, who was carrying ganja in plastic
sacks along a footpath; he dropped the sacks and ran toward the forest, but the
team caught him. [@1] The two 19 November actions yielded a total of 77
kilograms of illegal dried ganja and cannabis crop, and separate NDPS cases were
registered against both men. [@1]

Dainik Bhaskar reported that TD police station and the Udaipur DST seized 77
kilograms of ganja and caught two accused in the same action. [@2] Bhaskar said
officers on patrol saw illegal ganja plants outside a man's house on the route
toward the forest of Saru Phala Daban village. [@2]
