A special police team arrested two men in Triplicane, Chennai, on suspicion of
supplying narcotics. [@1] The Tamil-language account named the two as Mohammed
Moideen, 23, and Abdul Mazar Ullah, 30, and said Ice House police arrested them.
[@2] Police named the two as Mohammed Maideen and Mohammed Azhar and said they
procured narcotics from Bengaluru and supplied them in Chennai. [@1] Another
police account said the pair sourced the narcotics from Bengaluru before
transporting them to Chennai for local supply. [@3]

Officers seized 24 kg of ganja from the two men. [@1] The Tamil report also
recorded 24 kg of ganja. [@2] Alongside the ganja, police seized 50 grams of
methamphetamine, which is not counted in the cannabis figures. [@1] The Tamil
account put the methamphetamine at 51 grams. [@2] Another account put the
seizure at 24 kilograms of ganja and 50 grams of methamphetamine. [@3]

The two men were handed over to Ice House police station for further
investigation. [@1] Police were questioning them to establish the source of the
drug and distribution network. [@1] Investigators said they were tracing the
origin of the drugs and looking for others connected to the supply chain. [@3]
