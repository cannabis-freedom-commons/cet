A teenage boy was arrested after police on foot patrol detected the smell of
cannabis on Market Street in Portadown, County Armagh. [@1] The officers
belonged to the Brownlow Neighbourhood Police Team of the Police Service of
Northern Ireland (PSNI). [@2] When officers approached and told him they could
smell cannabis, the boy tried to run away. [@1] He was quickly apprehended and
detained so that a search could be carried out. [@1]

Officers found 14 bags of suspected herbal cannabis during the search. [@1] He
was arrested for possession of Class B controlled drugs and possession of Class
B controlled drugs with intent to supply. [@1] Police took the teenager into
custody on suspicion of possession of a Class B controlled drug and possession
with intent to supply. [@3] The PSNI refused to give the boy’s age, saying only
that he is in his teens. [@1] The substance was described only as suspected
cannabis, with no confirmation of what the bags contained. [@2]

A youth diversion officer will decide whether the boy is reported to the Public
Prosecution Service (PPS) or an out-of-court diversion is used. [@1] The PSNI
said it was committed to tackling the possession and supply of controlled drugs
in communities. [@1] Police asked anyone with information to report it on 101 or
through the online reporting tool. [@1]
