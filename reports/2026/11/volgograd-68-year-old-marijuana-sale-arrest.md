Police in Volgograd detained a 68-year-old local resident. [@1] Police said the
man intended to sell a narcotic substance in the city. [@1] The seizure account
was attributed to the press service of the regional headquarters of the Interior
Ministry. [@1]

Officers found and seized marijuana during a personal search of the detained
man. [@1] Officers also found and seized cannabis at his place of residence.
[@1] The detained pensioner said he had bought a plant-based drug from an
unidentified person. [@1] He said he bought it over the Internet. [@1] He said
the purchase was for further sale. [@1]

A criminal case was opened against the man under part 4 of article 228.1 of the
Russian Criminal Code. [@1] The sanction of that article provides for punishment
of up to 20 years of imprisonment. [@1] The case was treated as preparation for
the sale of marijuana. [@1]
