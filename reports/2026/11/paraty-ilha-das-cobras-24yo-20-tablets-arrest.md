Military Police officers arrested a 24-year-old man on suspicion of drug
trafficking on the night of Thursday 19 November 2026 in Paraty, in Rio de
Janeiro state. [@1] The arrest took place on Rua Angra dos Reis, in the Ilha das
Cobras neighbourhood. [@1] Officers went to the address to check information
about drug sales, the Military Police said. [@1] They saw the suspect sitting on
a motorcycle with a bag and handing items to people who approached him, movement
they described as resembling drug dealing. [@1]

In the bag the officers found drugs and seized a mobile phone, 12 packets of
hashish, 20 tablets of marijuana, 79 portions of cocaine and 20 reais in cash.
[@1] O Dia reported the marijuana tablets valued at 50 reais each and the
hashish packets at an estimated 20 reais each. [@2] The motorcycle, a Honda CB
300F Twister, was also seized and was to be examined to check whether its
chassis had been tampered with, O Dia reported. [@2]

The man was taken to the Paraty police station, where he remained in custody on
suspicion of drug trafficking, and the motorcycle was seized. [@1] The material
was registered at the 167th police station, which is handling the investigation,
O Dia reported. [@2] The operation was part of routine patrols by the 2nd
Independent Military Police Company (2ª CIPM) in Paraty, O Dia reported. [@2]
The Military Police did not release the man's identity or say whether anyone
else was identified. [@2]
