Katghar police arrested 33-year-old Parvez during a patrol in Karula, Moradabad.
[@1] Sub-Inspector Deepak Kumar, posted at the station, was on patrol at about
12:50 on the intervening night of Saturday and Sunday when he spotted a youth by
the Chandausi railway line about 50 to 60 steps from the Bhadora Chamunda temple
in Karula. [@1] The youth tried to flee on seeing the police, but the team
caught him. [@1] On questioning, he gave his name as Parvez, son of Laik, and
his address as Karbala Lane No. 01 near the Taron Wali Masjid in Bhadora under
Katghar police station. [@1]

Police found 340 grams of charas on him. [@1] Police seized the charas and
registered Crime No. 666/2026 against Parvez at Katghar police station under
Sections 8/20 of the NDPS Act. [@1]

Police said they are checking his criminal record and questioning him about
where he brought the charas from and to whom he was going to deliver it. [@1]
