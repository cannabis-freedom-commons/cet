Detectives from the PDI's Lo Barnechea Criminal Investigation Brigade (Bicrim)
detained seven people during an investigation into violations of Law 20.000,
Chile's drug law. [@1] The operation was carried out in the Cerro 18 Norte
sector in Lo Barnechea and in La Legua in San Joaquín, as part of an inquiry
related to the sale of illicit substances. [@1] Those arrested were five women
and two men, all adults of Chilean nationality. [@1] Lo Barnechea brigade chief
Subprefect Rossana Mosquera said all seven detainees had prior police records
for violations of the drug law, Law 20.000. [@1]

In the procedure officers seized 2,135.9 grams of cocaine base, 33.85 grams of
cocaine hydrochloride, 3.73 grams of cannabis and 63.43 grams of ketamine. [@1]
Officers also seized 316,000 pesos in cash during the procedure. [@2]

La Tercera reported that the family clan was led by a woman who maintained a
monopoly over the commercialization and sale of different types of drugs within
the commune. [@2] According to the police account, the leader maintained
relationships with people living in the sector, who passed her information about
police presence in the area. [@1]
