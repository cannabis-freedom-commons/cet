Police searching the homes of history-sheeters in the Sengundram police station
limits found five kilograms of ganja and 50 narcotic pills at a house in
Padiyanallur and arrested two men. [@1] The two are Veeraraghavan, alias Veera,
31, and Narendran, alias Paravai, 23, both residents of Padiyanallur near
Sengundram. [@1] A second Tamil report names the same two men as residents of
Padiyanallur near Sengundram and gives the same seizure of five kilograms of
ganja and 50 pills. [@2] Police list Veeraraghavan in 15 criminal cases and
Narendran in nine. [@1]

The checks were carried out in several localities under the Sengundram police
station on 20 November 2026. [@1] Sengundram inspector Krishnamoorthy led the
police team, which registered a case covering drug trafficking and the hoarding
of drugs for sale. [@1] Both men were produced before a court and sent to
prison. [@1]
