Egypt's Interior Ministry kept up intensive traffic campaigns on roads and main
axes across the country, as part of efforts to achieve traffic discipline and
confront offences that threaten the safety of citizens. [@1] The campaigns
recorded 100,141 traffic violations in 24 hours, including driving without
licences, speeding, parking at random, using a mobile phone at the wheel and
breaching licence conditions. [@1,2] Officers tested 1,111 drivers and 41 were
positive for drug use. [@1,2]

A parallel campaign in work zones along the Regional Ring Road recorded 469
traffic violations in 24 hours. [@1,2] Those violations included carrying
passengers against the rules, breaching licence conditions and failing to meet
vehicle security and durability requirements. [@1,2] Officers tested 154 drivers
on the Regional Ring Road and four were positive for drug use. [@1,2] Police also
detained eight people wanted under 11 court rulings. [@1,2] The ministry said the
necessary legal measures were taken over the recorded violations and cases. [@1,2]
