A man who lives at Gullhaug in Holmestrand was sentenced to two years and ten
months in prison after extensive involvement with drugs, alcohol, weapons and
impaired driving. [@1] The prosecution was described as a comprehensive
indictment in which the drug finds in his home were one part of several other
extensive matters behind the prison term. [@1]

Officers found more than 2.5 kilos of cannabis in the man's home at Gullhaug,
alongside other drugs kept at the same address. [@1] Officers also found around
1.2 kilos of amphetamine in the same home, which is not counted in the cannabis
figures. [@1] The case involved stored weapons alongside the several kilos of
drugs and the alcohol. [@1] The case also involved 2,700 litres of alcohol kept
alongside the weapons and the drugs. [@1]

The prison term of two years and ten months was imposed for the cannabis and
other drug findings together with the other extensive matters, not for cannabis
alone. [@1] Impaired driving was among the additional offences covered by the
sentence alongside the drug, alcohol and weapons matters. [@1]
