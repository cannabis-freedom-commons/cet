Morocco's Second Anti-Gang Brigade, attached to the Marrakech security
prefecture, arrested a person suspected of drug trafficking in Douar El Fakhara,
Marrakech. [@1] Officers searching the suspect seized one plate of chira, the
pressed cannabis resin known in Morocco as shira and elsewhere as hashish, from
his possession. [@1]

Follow-up inquiries then led officers to raid the home of his main supplier in
the same neighbourhood. [@1] The house search seized 44 additional plates of the
same drug, with a reported total weight of about 4,500 grams, along with two
electronic scales used to weigh drugs. [@1] A second Marrakech outlet gives the
same account of the raid, putting the 44 additional plates at 4 kilograms and
500 grams and likewise reporting two electronic scales. [@2]

The suspect was placed under police custody in coordination with the competent
public prosecution service pending presentation before the courts. [@1]
Investigations were continuing to arrest the main supplier at large and to
identify and detain any other possible accomplices in the case. [@1] The second
outlet likewise reports custody under the competent prosecution while inquiries
continue to arrest the main supplier and everyone linked to the case. [@2]
