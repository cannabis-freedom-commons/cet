Uttarakhand Police arrested 76 alleged drug peddlers and seized narcotic
substances worth Rs 1.35 crore during a two-day statewide crackdown at 273
locations, officials said on 26 November 2026. [@1] About 181 police teams
carried out simultaneous raids across the state on 24 and 25 November 2026. [@1]
The operation, named Operation Track-7, was aimed at breaking drug trafficking
networks and targeting hotspots used for the illegal trade, inspector general of
police (Cyber and STF) Nilesh Anand Bharne said. [@1] Raids were carried out at
hotspots including Sapera Basti in Dehradun, Gumaniwala in Rishikesh, Gularbhoj
in Nainital and Muni Ki Reti in Tehri Garhwal. [@2]

Police seized 4.9 kg of charas and 45 kg of ganja in the raids. [@1] Officers
also seized 450 grams of heroin, 1,510 prescription tablets and capsules and 40
cartons of Indian-made foreign liquor. [@1] Police put the estimated value of
the seized drugs and liquor together at about Rs 1.35 crore. [@3] Police
destroyed illegal cannabis cultivation spread over 1.3 acres, about 0.53
hectares, in Uttarkashi district. [@1]

Police verified 968 suspects and registered 69 cases under the Narcotic Drugs
and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act. [@1] Police prepared dossiers on 473
accused and opened 61 new history sheets to monitor suspected drug traffickers.
[@1] Police arrested five absconding accused through the execution of
non-bailable warrants and registered Gangster Act cases against two accused.
[@1] Bharne said the operation was conducted on the direction of director
general of police Deepam Seth. [@1]
