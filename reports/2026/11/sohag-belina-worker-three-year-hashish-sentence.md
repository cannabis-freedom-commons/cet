The Sohag Criminal Court sentenced a worker identified only by the initials M.
A. M. to three years of aggravated imprisonment. [@1] The court also imposed a
fine of 50,000 Egyptian pounds. [@1] He was convicted of trafficking hashish and
other narcotic substances in Belina markaz, an administrative district in Sohag
Governorate. [@1]

The case dates to 2026, when information reached detective officers that the man
was dealing hashish and other narcotic substances in Belina markaz. [@1] After
the required legal procedures and investigations, information from a
confidential source was confirmed. [@1] Officers arrested the man and, when
confronted, he admitted trafficking hashish and other narcotic substances. [@1]
He was referred to the criminal court, which issued the sentence. [@1]
