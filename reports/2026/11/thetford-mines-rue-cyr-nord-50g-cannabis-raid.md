Sûreté du Québec officers from the MRC des Appalaches, joined by the force’s
tactical intervention group, searched a multiplex on rue Cyr Nord in Thetford
Mines on 12 November 2026. [@3] The search followed an investigation opened in
summer 2026 which found that a man living in one of the building’s apartments
was trafficking drugs from that address. [@1] The man, described as being in his
forties, was arrested on site on accusations including substance trafficking,
weapons manufacturing, possession of a prohibited weapon and possession of a
weapon for a dangerous purpose. [@3] He was then released pending laboratory
results and the continuation of the investigation. [@1]

Officers seized nearly 50 grams of dried cannabis. [@1] In the same search
officers also seized about 180 methamphetamine tablets, about 5 grams of
psilocybin, more than 70 prescription tablets and more than 400 Canadian
dollars. [@2] Officers also seized a cell phone, 14 air guns ranging from
pistols to long guns, a prohibited knife, a baseball bat modified with makeshift
spikes, three 3D printers and 3D-printed weapon parts. [@2]

Police said the operation fell under the ACCES Cannabis programme, a government
initiative against contraband cannabis. [@1]
