Special Response Battalion 9 arrested two men in Habiganj with 15 kilograms of
cannabis and a motorcycle. [@1,2] Officers detained them at about 11 pm on
Thursday 19 November in West Fandrail village of Lokra Union, under Habiganj
Sadar Model Police Station. [@1,2] The two were identified as Sanju Mia, aged 24,
from the Kalishiri area of Chunarughat, and Md Abdur Rauf, also known as Rubel,
aged 26. [@1]

SRB-9's media officer, an additional superintendent of police, said in a notice
that a team from the battalion's CPC-3 Shayestaganj Camp set up a checkpoint in
the village and detained the two during a search. [@1] The press notice was signed by SRB-9's media officer and additional superintendent of police K. M. Shahidul Islam Sohag. [@2] Officers later recovered
15 kilograms of cannabis, wrapped in Scotch tape, from a black school bag the
men were carrying. [@1]

On questioning, the two said they had collected the cannabis together from a
border area of Habiganj and were holding it in order to sell it. [@1] A case was
filed under the narcotics control law, and the accused and the seized evidence
were handed to Habiganj Sadar Model Police Station. [@1]
