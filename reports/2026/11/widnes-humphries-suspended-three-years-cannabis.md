Callum Humphries, 24, of no fixed abode but of Widnes, pleaded guilty to being
concerned in the supply of class A and class B drugs, namely cocaine and
cannabis, and to possessing criminal property. [@1] Humphries was sentenced to
three years in prison, suspended for two years. [@1] He was also ordered to
complete a drug rehabilitation course. [@1]

Earlier this year, Cheshire Police became aware of a mobile phone number being
used in the Widnes area to sell illegal drugs. [@1] Officers identified
Humphries as the controller of the graft phone, sending out flare messages
advertising illegal substances for sale. [@1] At around 12.35pm on Wednesday 30
September, he was spotted acting suspiciously on Coronation Drive with two other
men. [@1] Officers followed the group and arrested Humphries on Deansway. [@1]

A search found a backpack containing a large quantity of cannabis and the mobile
phone used to sell the drugs. [@1] He was also found with a large quantity of
cash and a blister pack of Naproxen. [@1] After questioning he was charged with
being concerned in the supply of cocaine and cannabis and with possession of
criminal property. [@1]

When he was arrested, Callum Humphries was 23. [@2] He appeared at Chester
Magistrates' Court and was remanded in custody. [@2] He was listed to appear at
Liverpool Crown Court on 12 November. [@2] Cheshire Police said the sentence
would limit what career he could take and where he would be able to travel. [@1]
