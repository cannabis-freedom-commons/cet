Nimbokrang sector police arrested a 34-year-old man identified by the initials
AK in a raid on a house in Kampung Bunyom, Nimbokrang district, Jayapura
Regency, early on 27 November 2026. [@1] Officers seized 25 large packages of
dried cannabis ready for distribution. [@1] The Nimbokrang police chief, Iptu
Ignatius Sepbiantoro, said the operation followed a public report at about 00:20
WIT of a suspected cannabis transaction at the house. [@1] Officers reached the
scene at about 00:35 WIT. [@1] Several people fled towards the back of the house
when officers raided it. [@1] AK, described as a private-sector employee living
in Jalan Sulawesi, Dok 9 Kali, North Jayapura, was the only suspect detained.
[@1] A search of the house also turned up three bottles of vodka, a mobile phone
and two motorcycles, a Yamaha Jupiter Z without a number plate and a Yamaha
X-Ride. [@1] Officers also took a long parang, a folding knife, a cassowary
bone, a catapult, a bow with three arrows, and personal documents. [@1] AK and
the evidence were taken to the Nimbokrang police station at about 02:00 WIT for
further examination. [@1]

Police said initial questioning indicated the two men who fled were Papua New
Guinea nationals. [@1] AK told officers the cannabis had been brought into
Nimbokrang the previous day, 26 November, by two men from Papua New Guinea. [@2]
Police said the cannabis was intended for distribution in Nimbokrang, Nimboran,
Namblong, Kemtuk Gresi and Unurum Guay districts. [@3] Searches covered Kampung
Bunyom, Kampung Rhepang Muaif, Kampung Benyom Jaya 1 and Kampung Benek in Unurum
Guay district. [@2]
