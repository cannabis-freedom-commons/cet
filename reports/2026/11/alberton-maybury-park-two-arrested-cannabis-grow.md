Two people were arrested after police uncovered a suspected cannabis cultivation
and dealing operation at a residential property in Maybury Park, Alberton, on 19
November 2026. [@1,2,3,4] The suspects, aged 42 and 43, were arrested during an
intelligence-driven operation by the Ekurhuleni Metro Police, working with the
South African Police Service West Rand K9 Unit and the Directorate for Priority
Crime Investigation. [@1,3,4] The operation began at around 6am after police
received information that the household was allegedly cultivating and selling
cannabis. [@1,3] Officers obtained a search warrant before entering the property
in Lepelhout Street. [@1,3,4]

In the yard, officers found numerous cannabis plants growing in growing bags
alongside large quantities of dried cannabis stored in plastic containers and
tubs. [@1,3,4] Officers also found several pre-rolled and packaged cannabis products
bearing different strain labels and various bottles and jars containing
cannabis-related products on the premises. [@1,2,3,4] The cultivation and equipment were valued at over R3 million. [@2] Further searches inside the
property led to the discovery of suspected hallucinogenic mushroom substances,
which police said would be subjected to further investigation. [@1,2,3,4] Forensic experts from the head office laboratory and the Germiston Local Criminal Record Centre were called to the scene to collect evidence and test the suspected drugs. [@4] Two suspects believed to be the owners were investigated for allegedly selling psychedelic mushrooms. [@2]

The two suspects were detained at Alberton Police Station. [@1,3,4] Both were facing charges of dealing in dagga and the illegal possession or dealing in controlled substances. [@4] Investigations
were ongoing. [@1,3]
