Police in Cartagena arrested a man known as alias 'Bachi' in the Olaya Herrera
neighbourhood during a search and seizure operation. [@2] Investigators said he
had been selling narcotics since 2023 and was a distributor of marijuana in
various sectors of Olaya Herrera. [@1]

The seizure was carried out by SIJIN, the criminal investigation unit, with
SIPOL, the police intelligence unit, of the Metropolitan Police of Cartagena
(Mecar). [@3] Officers seized 9,500 grams (9.5 kg) of marijuana ready for
distribution, two scales used for weighing and dosing, and a mobile phone used
to coordinate sales. [@2]

Colonel Juan Pablo Ruiz Rodríguez, commander of the Metropolitan Police of
Cartagena, said the operation affected criminal structures dedicated to drug
trafficking and kept the 9,500 grams of marijuana off the streets. [@2] The
suspect and the seized items were placed at the disposal of the prosecutor's
office for alleged manufacturing, trafficking and possession of narcotics. [@1]
