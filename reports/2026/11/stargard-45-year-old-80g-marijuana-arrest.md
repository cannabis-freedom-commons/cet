On the previous Saturday in Stargard, a police dog handler on duty with his
service dog stopped a man whose behaviour aroused the officer's suspicion that
the 45-year-old might be carrying drugs. [@1] Asked to voluntarily hand over any
drugs he had, the 45-year-old produced a paper packet containing plant material
and was detained. [@1] Officers then searched the man's home, where they found
and secured a total of more than 80 grams of the substance. [@1]

A police forensics technician weighed the seized material and tested it,
confirming that the dried plant matter was marijuana. [@1] The 45-year-old was
charged with possession of narcotic substances and will answer for the offence
before a court. [@1] The Stargard county police account describes the same
Saturday intervention by the dog handler against the suspected 45-year-old. [@2]
