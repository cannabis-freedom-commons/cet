Military police on patrol stopped a Paraná-plated Fiat Strada pickup at km 185
of the Rodovia Castello Branco in Bofete at about 9h30 on Sunday 15 November
2026. [@1] He was arrested in flagrante and the pickup was seized. [@1] The
civil police delegate ratified the arrest and requested its conversion into
preventive detention. [@1]

The rental contract for the vehicle was in another person's name, different from
the one the driver had given. [@1] A detailed search found dry-type maconha in
portions wrapped in colourful balloons hidden in the pickup's right-side air
box. [@1] Officers counted 81 portions totalling about 33 kg, weighed as two
lots of 15.98 kg and 17.14 kg. [@1] Officers also seized a mobile phone and
several empty balloons. [@1]

The driver had said he had come from Campo Mourão in Paraná and was heading to
São Paulo for health treatment. [@1] Questioned, the man said he had rented the
car in Campo Mourão, received it already loaded, and was to take it to Sorocaba
for R$5,000 for the transport. [@1]
