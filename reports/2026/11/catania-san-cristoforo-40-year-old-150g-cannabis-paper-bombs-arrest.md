Catania police traced the flat to a 40-year-old man from Mauritius already known
for previous drug offences. [@1] He was arrested for possession of drugs for
dealing and illegal possession of explosive material and was taken to jail on
the duty prosecutor’s order. [@1] He was placed in jail pending a decision by
the preliminary-investigations judge. [@2]

On the balcony officers found 142 grams of marijuana and 8 grams of hashish,
plus a knife still coated with drugs and a precision scale used to weigh doses.
[@2] In the kitchen officers found two paper bombs without identification marks
on top of the refrigerator. [@1] Bomb-disposal specialists recovered the devices
safely and detonated them in a suitable place because even accidental ignition
could have harmed residents. [@1]

Flying Squad and dog-unit officers of the Catania Questura were patrolling San
Cristoforo when the drug dog Rex signalled drugs near a building in a side
street between Via Garibaldi and Via Plebiscito. [@1] The man had denied holding
drugs, but the dog led officers to the balcony. [@3]
