Santa Catarina Military Police arrested one man for drug trafficking and illegal
possession of ammunition on the BR-282 highway in Lages in the early hours of 15
November 2026. [@1] The stop was registered at about 4h24 on the Rodovia Ulysses
Guimarães in the Chapada neighbourhood after police received information that a
vehicle was being used to transport drugs. [@1]

Inside the vehicle officers found about 6 kg of marijuana, 792 grams of hashish,
71 grams of skunk and 37 ecstasy tablets. [@1] Officers also seized 70 rounds of
ammunition of different calibres, a precision scale, two mobile phones and R$86
in cash. [@2] A second Lages outlet confirms the same vehicle search and the
same seizure of about 6 kg of marijuana, 792 grams of hashish, 71 grams of skunk
and 37 ecstasy tablets. [@3]

The driver told police he was transporting the drugs for a payment of R$1,500.
[@2] The man was taken with the vehicle and all the seized material to the
Central de Polícia for the applicable legal procedures. [@3]
