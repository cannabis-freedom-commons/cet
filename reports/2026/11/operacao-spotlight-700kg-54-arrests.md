The first phase of Operation Spotlight ended with 54 preventive arrests, four of
them in Santa Catarina. [@1] Police also seized 700 kilograms of marijuana in
Maravilha, in western Santa Catarina, with support from the Border Air Police
Service. [@1] A Civil Police helicopter that works on the border supported the
Maravilha seizure. [@3] Criciúma and Cocal do Sul received warrants in the
second phase of Operation Spotlight, against drug trafficking and money
laundering, launched on Thursday 19 November by the Civil Police. [@1]

The second phase carried out 31 search-and-seizure warrants, 13 preventive
arrest warrants and 28 judicial orders blocking bank accounts in Rio Grande do
Sul, Santa Catarina and Paraná. [@2] In Santa Catarina the warrants were served
in Criciúma, Cocal do Sul and Florianópolis, with one address in each city; in
Rio Grande do Sul in Porto Alegre, Canoas, Cachoeirinha, Esteio, Sapucaia do
Sul, São Leopoldo, Novo Hamburgo and Passo Fundo; and in Guaíra, Paraná. [@1]
The action was run through the Drug Repression Unit of the State Directorate of
Criminal Investigations (DRD/DEIC) and coordinated by the State Department of
Narcotics Investigations (Denarc). [@2] The operation was still under way, and
the Civil Police said the tally would be published once the teams finished. [@1]
