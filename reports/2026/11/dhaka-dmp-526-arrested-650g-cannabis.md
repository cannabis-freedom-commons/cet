Dhaka Metropolitan Police arrested 526 people across Dhaka in the 24 hours
leading up to the morning of 17 November 2026. [@2] The 526 included 50 members
of the Awami League and its student wing, the Bangladesh Chhatra League, who
were allegedly preparing for a torchlit procession involving crude bomb
detonations. [@1]

In a Tuesday statement, the Dhaka Metropolitan Police said officers conducted
routine drives from Monday morning to Tuesday morning and filed 45 cases. [@1]
Mirpur Division recorded the most arrests at 150, followed by Wari with 89,
Tejgaon with 73, Motijheel with 71, Uttara with 42, Lalbagh with 41, Gulshan
with 37 and Ramna with 21, while the Detective Branch detained two. [@1]

During the raids police recovered a machete, a local cleaver, three unexploded
crude bombs and fragments of two detonated bombs. [@2] Officers also seized 650g
of cannabis and 3,499 Yaba pills, plus two motorcycles, a CNG-run autorickshaw
and two mobile phones. [@1] Activities of the Awami League remain banned, while
the Chhatra League faces an outright ban. [@1] The crackdown follows 480 arrests
reported across the capital in the preceding 24 hours, which also included Awami
League and Chhatra League activists. [@1]
