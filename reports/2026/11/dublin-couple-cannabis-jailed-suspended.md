A couple was caught with nearly €300,000 worth of cannabis in their home in
Dublin. [@1,2] Crowe Cully, a plumber, and McGaughran, a healthcare worker, were arrested in their places of work and interviewed separately. [@3] CCTV footage outside the apartment showed a van delivering the drugs some days earlier, with the couple both carrying the bags inside. [@3] Gda Burke agreed that the value of cannabis had fallen and the amount found in the couple's apartment would now be worth about €210,000. [@3] Garda Dane Burke told the court that the cannabis was found in two black bags in the bedroom with a street value of €298,000, along with just over €1,000 of cocaine and €6,110 cash in one of McGaughran's handbags. [@3] Crowe Cully (39) and McGaughran (28), of Iveagh Trust Buildings, Bride Street, Dublin 8, pleaded guilty at Dublin Circuit Criminal Court to two counts of possessing cannabis for sale or supply in their home on April 15, 2025 and one count of possessing proceeds of crime. [@3] The couple were Thomas Crowe Cully and Donna McGaughran. [@3]

The pair cried and embraced in the dock. [@1,2] The man was jailed and the woman
was given a suspended sentence. [@1,2] McGaughran was given a two-year sentence which was suspended in full. [@3] Judge Martin Nolan set a headline sentence of six years for Crowe Cully, which he reduced to two years and eight months. [@3]
