The Veszprém District Prosecutor's Office charged a 53-year-old man with drug
possession committed through cultivation for growing cannabis at his house in
Várpalota. [@1] The indictment says he ordered seeds over the internet from
Austria and planted them in the yard of his house in the summer of 2024. [@1] He
grew cannabis plants containing a psychoactive substance classified as a
narcotic drug for his own consumption. [@1]

He kept the plants in plastic flower pots in the yard, using plant protection
products and lamps to maintain ideal growing conditions. [@1] In June 2025 the
investigating authority searched his residence and seized 29 cannabis plants,
together with the pesticides, lamps and other equipment used to tend and consume
them. [@1]

The prosecution asked the Veszprém District Court to impose a suspended prison
sentence without holding a trial. [@1] It also sought confiscation of the seized
plants, remaining plant material and the tools used to commit the offence, as
announced by the Veszprém County Chief Prosecutor's Office. [@1]
