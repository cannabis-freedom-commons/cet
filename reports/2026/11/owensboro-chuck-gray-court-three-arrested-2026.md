Three people were arrested on the morning of 26 November 2026 after Owensboro
police searched a residence in the 600 block of Chuck Gray Court. [@1] The
department's Emergency Response Team and officers from the Patrol Division
executed the search warrant. [@2] Police said officers seized what they
described as trafficking amounts of methamphetamine and marijuana, a handgun and
a large amount of money. [@3] Police named the three arrested as Thomas Coomes,
Roger Hill and Kieara Aubrey. [@2]

Coomes was charged with first-degree trafficking in a controlled substance, a
second or subsequent offence involving less than 2 grams of methamphetamine and
enhanced. [@1] He was also charged with trafficking in marijuana, less than 8
ounces, second or subsequent offence, enhanced. [@1] His other charges included
possession of a handgun by a convicted felon. [@1] He was also charged with drug
paraphernalia, delivery or manufacture. [@1]

Hill was charged with trafficking in marijuana, 8 ounces to less than 5 pounds,
first offence. [@1] He also faced a charge of first-degree possession of a
controlled substance, second offence, involving methamphetamine. [@1] A further
possession charge, a first offence, involved opiates. [@1] He was also charged
with tampering with physical evidence. [@1] Hill also faced a drug paraphernalia
charge of delivery or manufacture. [@1]

Aubrey was arrested on a capias warrant, a court order requiring a person to be
brought before a court, and police did not identify the underlying case. [@1]
