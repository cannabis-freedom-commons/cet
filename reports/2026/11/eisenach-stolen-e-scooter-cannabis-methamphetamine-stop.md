Police in Eisenach stopped a 44-year-old local man who was riding an e-scooter
in a traffic control at about 02:10 on Sunday morning. [@1] A drug test carried
out during the control was positive for cannabis and for methamphetamine. [@1]
Because of the suspected driving under the influence of intoxicants, officers
ordered a blood sample to be taken from the rider. [@1] Police said a formal
report over the intoxicated driving would follow. [@1]

Officers found that the e-scooter was not insured. [@1] Police said the
44-year-old had stolen the e-scooter from the inner city shortly beforehand.
[@1] Police opened criminal proceedings for theft of the e-scooter and for
violation of the compulsory insurance law. [@1]

The account is the Landespolizeiinspektion Gotha police statement for
Eisenach-Wartburgkreis, issued on the morning of 15 November 2026. [@2] The
police statement gives the same sequence of a 02:10 e-scooter control, a drug
test positive for cannabis and methamphetamine, an ordered blood sample and
proceedings for theft and the insurance violation. [@2]
