Police and gendarmerie teams ran anti-trafficking operations across Mersin
province between 14 and 20 November 2026, coordinated by the Mersin Chief Public
Prosecutor's Office. [@1] The work covered 18 separate incidents and 24 suspects
were remanded in custody. [@1] After processing at the police station the 24
were taken to court and remanded by the magistrate. [@2]

Searches seized 431 grams of esrar-skunk and a further 5 grams, 436 grams in
total of the herbal cannabis preparation Turkish police call skunk. [@1] The
same searches produced 29,549 synthetic pharmaceutical pills, 230 ecstasy
tablets, 565 grams of synthetic cannabinoid (bonzai), 40 grams of
methamphetamine, 14 grams of the bonzai precursor A-M and 7 grams of cocaine,
none of which is counted as cannabis here. [@1] Officers also confiscated four
precision scales and 125,330 Turkish lira plus 500 US dollars that police
assessed as proceeds of drug trafficking. [@1]

The figures are a week's tally of anti-trafficking work by both forces across
the province, and cannabis made up a small part of the haul. [@2]
