Prison authorities released in error a Dubliner who had been charged over a
€4.2m drugs haul outside Shannon in May. [@1] The Dubliner was charged again
over the €4.2m drugs haul. [@1] At Ennis District Court, counsel Barry O’Farrell
told Judge Adrian Harris that his client James Lawlor had been held on remand
over the drug charges and had been released in error for a period before
returning to prison when asked. [@1] Lawlor spent from May until late September
on remand apart from the period he was released in error. [@1] Lawlor, of
Killininny House in Firhouse, Dublin, was first brought before Ennis District
Court charged with two offences. [@3] Judge Harris remanded him in custody to
appear by video-link at Ennis District Court on 3 June. [@3] A judge later
struck out all charges against the Dublin man over the May Shannon suspected
cannabis seizure. [@4] With no charges remaining, James Lawlor was free to leave
Limerick prison. [@4]

Revenue officers seized 210 kilograms of suspected cannabis herb worth about
€4,200,000 in County Clare on 26 May 2026. [@2] The 210 kg of cannabis was
seized on the N19 at Smithstown on the outskirts of Shannon. [@3] The seizure
resulted from a joint operation by the Garda National Drugs and Organised Crime
Bureau, the Clare Divisional Drug Unit and Revenue’s Customs Service. [@2] One
man in his 40s was arrested and detained in County Clare under Section 2 of the
Criminal Justice (Drug Trafficking) Act 1996. [@2] The seized drugs were sent to
Forensic Science Ireland for analysis. [@2]

He was charged with having cannabis at Smithstown on 26 May for sale or supply
under Section 15 of the Misuse of Drugs Act 1977. [@5] He was also charged with
possessing cannabis at the same place and date under Section 3 of the same Act.
[@5] He had been on remand since May after gardaí stopped the van he was driving
on the N19 at Smithstown on 26 May. [@4] He was brought back before Ennis
District Court by prison officers on 2 September. [@5] The Prison Service
confirmed a prisoner was released in error from court and returned to custody on
4 September. [@5]
