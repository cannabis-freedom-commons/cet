Police carried out a special operation in Pernik and the Radomir village of
Dolna Dikanya. [@3] The operation was run by officers of the criminal-police and
special-tactics sectors. [@1] Officers checked a 37-year-old resident of Pernik.
[@1] The man was detained for up to 24 hours under a police measure. [@1]

They seized 116 grams of dry green leafy mass from him that reacted to marijuana
in a field drug test. [@1] A search of a property he lives in in Dolna Dikanya
found another 502 grams of the same substance. [@1] The total amount of plant
mass seized was 618 grams. [@1]

The Interior Ministry's national bulletin for 20 November 2026 listed the
seizure under Pernik. [@2] A pre-trial proceeding was opened and work on the
case continues under the supervision of the prosecutor's office. [@1]
