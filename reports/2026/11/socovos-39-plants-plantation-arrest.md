The Guardia Civil in Albacete arrested a 55-year-old resident of Socovos on
suspicion of a crime against public health after dismantling a marijuana
plantation hidden in woodland in the municipality. [@2,4,6,7] Investigators identified
the man as the person who cultivated the crop, and he was detained on suspicion
of cultivating and processing drugs. [@2,4,7] The operation was carried out by the
Guardia Civil's investigation team in Hellín, supported by officers from the
Letur post, as part of the force's permanent work against cannabis cultivation
and trafficking in Albacete province. [@1,5,6,7]

The investigation began after officers learned of a possible illegal marijuana
crop in a rural area of the Socovos municipal district. [@1] A search operation
confirmed the reports and led officers to an isolated, hard-to-reach spot hidden
among pine woods and dense forest. [@1,5] The crop was spread over three separate
areas and held 39 large cannabis sativa plants. [@1,5,6,7] An automatic irrigation
system fed by several water tanks watered the plants. [@2,4,7] Officers also found
plant-care products at the site. [@3,4,7]

Officers removed the plants and stripped and weighed the buds, and the force
said analysis showed the material could have produced about 18,000 doses of
marijuana once dried and prepared for sale. [@2,4,6,7] The Guardia Civil said the
operation kept the cannabis out of illegal distribution channels. [@2] The force
said the man could face three to six years in prison and substantial financial
penalties, depending on the court's assessment of the case. [@2,4,6,7] The case file
was sent to the civil and instruction section of the Tribunal de Instancia of
Hellín, court number 3. [@3,4,6,7]
