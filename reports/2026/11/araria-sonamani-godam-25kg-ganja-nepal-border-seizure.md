Police and Sashastra Seema Bal troops seized 25 kilograms of ganja in a joint
operation on the India-Nepal border in Araria district, Bihar. [@1] Sonamani
Godam police station in Kursakanta block and a border outpost of the SSB's 56th
Battalion carried out the operation late on the night of Friday 20 November
2026, and the traffickers used the darkness and fog to escape into Nepal. [@2]
Station house officer Raja Babu Paswan said police had received a tip that a
large quantity of ganja was being brought into Indian territory from Nepal. [@2]

The joint team went to the India-Nepal border near pillar number 174 after
informing senior officers. [@2] People carrying loads on their heads came from
the Nepal side, and they threw down the sacks and ran back into Nepal as soon as
they spotted the police force. [@2] A search of the abandoned sacks found a
total of 25 kilograms of ganja in separate bags. [@2] The station house officer
said a first information report had been registered on the basis of a written
statement. [@2] No one was arrested, and police said they were working to
identify and arrest the traffickers who fled. [@2]

The news agency Hindusthan Samachar reported the action was taken on Saturday by
the SSB 56th Battalion's B Samvay Dubatola team with Sonamani Godam police, at a
checkpoint about 300 metres inside Indian territory from border pillar 174/1.
[@3] That account says one suspect came into Indian territory with a sack on his
head and, about 15 metres in, threw it down and ran back towards Nepal when he
noticed the checkpoint party. [@3] The seized material was handed over to
Sonamani Godam police station. [@3]
