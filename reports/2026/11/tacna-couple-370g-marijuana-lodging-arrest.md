Grupo Terna officers detained 32-year-old Harley Luis Velázquez Santillán and
23-year-old Tracy Charaña Castillo inside a room at Hospedaje Residencial Molina
in Tacna. [@1] The hotel stands on Modesto Molina street, half a block from
Varela avenue in central Tacna. [@2] The operation took place on the night of
Tuesday 17 November with DEPANDRO specialists and the duty prosecutor present.
[@1]

Police said the pair were staying at the establishment and the operation
followed prior surveillance and investigation work. [@2] Officers searching the
premises found several bags of marijuana hidden in the bathrooms and on the
bedroom beds. [@1] Police reported 370 grams of marijuana in the lodging
operation. [@1]

After the intervention the man and woman were taken to the Grupo Terna base in
Ciudad Nueva for identification and weighing of the seized substance. [@2] The
drug was later sealed and taken to the specialized unit for legal proceedings,
while both detainees remained at the authorities' disposal under investigation
for alleged drug microcommercialization. [@1]
