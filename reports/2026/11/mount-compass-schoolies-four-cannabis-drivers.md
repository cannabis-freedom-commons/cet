Police have seized a stash of illicit drugs during a crackdown on Fleurieu roads
as Schoolies celebrations kicked off on Friday. [@2] South Australia Police said
the operation was on Victor Harbor Road at Mount Compass on Friday 20 November,
screening drivers for alcohol and conducting roadside drug tests. [@1] Eight
drivers returned positive drug tests, including four for cannabis, three for
methamphetamine and one for cocaine, and all were given immediate
loss-of-licence notices. [@1]

A 47-year-old Mannum man tested positive for cannabis after being stopped in a
sedan. [@1] He was issued with a three-month immediate loss of licence notice.
[@1] A search of the car allegedly found cannabis, a pipe and capsicum spray.
[@1] He was fined for the drug offences and reported for possessing a prohibited
weapon, and police said further charges may follow pending forensic analysis.
[@1]

Police reported five drivers, issued eight expiations and four defect notices,
and seized cocaine, cannabis, MDMA, ketamine and fireworks during the operation.
[@1] Police said they would keep a visible presence on Fleurieu roads throughout
the Schoolies period, with motorists liable to be stopped for alcohol and drug
testing at any time. [@1]
