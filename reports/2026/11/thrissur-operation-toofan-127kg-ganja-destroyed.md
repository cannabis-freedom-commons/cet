The Thrissur Rural police on Wednesday destroyed a large haul of narcotic
substances seized in various cases registered across five police station limits
as part of Operation Toofan, Kerala's state-wide anti-drug drive. [@1] The haul
comprised 127.297 kg of ganja and 229.640 grams of methamphetamine, seized in
five police station limits. [@1] The cases were registered at Puthukkad,
Koratty, Anthikad, Aloor and Kodungalloor police stations. [@1]

Chemical analysis reports on the seized contraband had been received before the
destruction. [@1] The contraband was incinerated around noon on Wednesday in the
kiln of Om Shankar Tile Factory at Chittissery, within the Puthukkad police
station limits. [@1] The narcotics were transported to the tile factory under
tight security. [@1]

The destruction was carried out in accordance with legal procedures on the
orders of the District Drug Disposal Committee, chaired by Thrissur Rural
District Police Chief Mohammed Nadeem. [@1]
