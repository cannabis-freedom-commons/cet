A Worcestershire businessman appeared in court as one of 10 defendants charged
with modern day slavery and conspiracy to produce cannabis. [@1] John Bruce of
Ryden Lane, Charlton, near Pershore, was among the 10 who denied conspiring to
hold a person in slavery and conspiracy to produce cannabis when he appeared
before Judge Nicolas Cartwright at Worcester Crown Court on Tuesday. [@1] All 10
denied conspiracy to hold a person in slavery or servitude between May 31, 2021
and June 30, 2022 at Stourbridge and conspiracy to produce cannabis between the
same dates, at a plea and trial preparation hearing. [@1,2,3] The 10 appeared
together in the dock and were arraigned in court two on Tuesday morning, each
denying the two identical counts on the indictment. [@1,3]

The defendants were John Bruce, 54; Cuong Nguyen, 29; Minh Nguyen, 40; Manh Ho,
37; Dean Prater, 39; Tuan Nguyen, 64; Henry Price, 50; Harry Price, 52; James
Price, 37; and Brett Stanfield, 39, with addresses in Charlton, Birmingham,
Bathgate, West Lothian, Brackley Hatch and the West Midlands. [@1,3] Four of the 10 defendants were from the Black Country. [@3] A Vietnamese
interpreter appeared over videolink to assist some defendants. [@1,2,3] The case was
prosecuted by Timothy Harrington. [@1,2,3] The trial was expected to last eight
weeks. [@1,2,3]

The trial date was set for November 6, 2028, with a case management hearing on
Friday, June 9, 2028 given a one-hour time estimate. [@1,2,3] All defendants were
asked to complete a defence statement by January 26 the following year. [@1,2,3]
Judge Cartwright extended unconditional bail for all defendants. [@1,2,3] He warned
that if any failed to attend, the trial would almost inevitably continue in
their absence. [@1,2,3]
