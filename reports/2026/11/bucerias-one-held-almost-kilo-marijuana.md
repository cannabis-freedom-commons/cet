In a joint operation between the Mexican Army, the National Guard and the
municipal police of Bahía de Banderas, officers detained a man suspected of
being a small-time drug dealer in the town of Bucerías, Nayarit. [@1] He was
identified only as Serafín "N" and was placed at the disposal of the public
prosecutor's office over a suspected crime against health. [@1]

During the intervention officers found a black backpack holding 30 envelopes of
a substance possibly known as crystal, along with nine packages of marijuana and
four additional bags of the same plant, with a total weight of almost a
kilogram. [@1] Officers also seized cash in banknotes amounting to almost 2,000
pesos, which they presumed could be the proceeds of drug sales on the streets of
the municipality. [@1]

The detention occurred during a joint operation in Bucerías, as part of actions
by the three levels of government to combat retail drug dealing in Bahía de
Banderas. [@1]
