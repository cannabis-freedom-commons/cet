Military police in Foz do Iguaçu, in western Paraná, arrested a man and seized
more than a tonne of marijuana hidden in a load of oranges on the night of
Wednesday 18 November 2026. [@1] An ROCAM patrol of the 14th Military Police
Battalion was in the Jardim Guaíra area at about 20:00 when it saw a van with
Paraguayan plates. [@1] The van was heading from Argentina towards Brazil. [@2]
Seeing the police, the driver made a sharp U-turn and drove on the wrong side of
the road to try to escape. [@1] The officers followed briefly and intercepted
the vehicle. [@1]

In the search they found a large quantity of marijuana tablets hidden among the
oranges the van was carrying. [@1] Weighing gave a total of 1,004.3 kilograms of
marijuana. [@1]

The driver was arrested and taken with the van and the whole seized load to the
Federal Police station in Foz do Iguaçu. [@1] The Federal Police were to handle
the legal procedures in the case. [@1]
