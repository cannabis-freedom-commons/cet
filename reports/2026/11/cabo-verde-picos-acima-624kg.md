On Thursday, 12 November 2026, an operation against cannabis cultivation was
carried out at Picos Acima by the Departamento de Investigação Criminal de
Assomada in collaboration with the Procuradoria da Comarca de Santa Catarina.
[@1] According to a statement from the Polícia Judiciária cited by Inforpress,
the intervention targeted three agricultural parcels that were being used to
grow cannabis. [@1] The cannabis plants on those parcels were destroyed on the
spot. [@1]

In total, 624 kilograms of plants in an advanced state of maturity were seized
and destroyed in the operation. [@1] The operation formed part of the
authorities' wider actions to combat the cultivation and trafficking of narcotic
drugs. [@1]
