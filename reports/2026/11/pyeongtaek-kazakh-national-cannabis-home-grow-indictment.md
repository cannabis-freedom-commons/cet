A Kazakh national identified by the surname Kim has been sent to trial in South
Korea over cannabis grown in his home. [@1,2] He was offered the work by someone
above him in the operation whom he had met through gambling, who promised to buy
cannabis at 40,000 won for every two grams. [@1] He began growing cannabis at
the house in November last year, fitting a grow tent, LED lights and air
circulators to remove the smell of the plants. [@1,2] The house stood in the
middle of a neighbourhood of low-rise rental homes, where a neighbour said they
had not known about the grow. [@1]

In May he dried the cannabis from 18 plants he had grown and hid it on a road in
Cheongju. [@1,2] Using a drop-off method, in which the drugs are left at a hiding
place for the buyer to collect, he sold 7.6 million won worth of cannabis and
was paid entirely in cryptocurrency. [@1,2] In September he grew a further 21
plants for sale and was sent to trial. [@1,2]
