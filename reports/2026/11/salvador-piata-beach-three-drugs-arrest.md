Military police from CETO, part of the 32nd Military Police Battalion, arrested
a man on the afternoon of Thursday 19 November 2026, the eve of a holiday, at
Praia de Piatã in Salvador, Bahia. [@1] According to the police, the suspect was
with a group of men on the beach when he noticed the patrols approaching and
tried to run off to avoid the stop, but the officers caught up with him. [@1]

During the search the officers found crack stones, cocaine portions and portions
of marijuana on the man, and all the material was seized. [@1] The suspect
received a voice of arrest and was taken to the Central de Flagrantes, where he
was presented to the Polícia Civil for the appropriate measures. [@1] The man
remains at the disposal of the courts, and the circumstances of the case are
being investigated by the Polícia Civil. [@1]
