Kotwali Nagar police arrested a suspect youth near Hind Nagar jungle in
Ghaziabad during patrol and checking. [@1] The arrested youth identified himself
as Harsh, a resident of Daulatpura. [@1]

The arrest took place at about 11:35 pm on 14 November 2026 while Sub-Inspector
Prashant Kumar Gautam and his team were on patrol and public-order duty. [@1]
Officers surrounded and caught the man near the under-construction bridge as he
headed toward the railway line and turned back nervously on seeing police. [@1]
Police recovered a total of 1 kg 600 g of illegal ganja from his possession.
[@1]

Police registered a case against the accused under the NDPS Act. [@1] Police are
investigating other people involved in the illegal trade. [@1]
