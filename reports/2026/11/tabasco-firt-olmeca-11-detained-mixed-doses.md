Tabasco's security cabinet said FIRT Olmeca detained 11 people and seized five
firearms, more than 200 narcotics doses, four properties and five vehicles in
three days of operations from 20 to 22 November 2026. [@1] The actions covered
Jalpa de Méndez, Teapa, Centro and Cárdenas and included detentions for health
offences and unlawful weapons carrying plus execution of search warrants. [@1]
In Jalpa de Méndez and Teapa officers detained five men. [@1]

Those operations seized 50 doses of marijuana, cocaine and crystal, a long gun,
a magazine, 10 cartridges, a replica short weapon and three motorcycles. [@1] In
Centro municipality four operations for health offences, unlawful weapons
carrying and searches led to detention of three people including a minor. [@1]
Officers there seized two properties, two firearms, a replica, five magazines,
25 cartridges, 144 drug doses of marijuana, crystal and cocaine, two vehicles
and radio equipment. [@1] In Cárdenas two women were detained for health
offences with 29 doses of cocaine and marijuana, two cell phones and cash. [@1]
In additional search-warrant operations in Cárdenas a man was detained and two
properties allegedly used for clandestine alcohol sale were secured. [@1]

In Centro FIRT Olmeca inspected 715 vehicles and 735 motorcycles, checked 1,335
people and interviewed 1,345 more. [@1] In Comalcalco, Cunduacán, Huimanguillo
and Jalpa de Méndez teams inspected 410 vehicles, 460 motorcycles, checked 520
people and interviewed 565. [@1] Through Operativo Cárcel Segura 345
incarcerated people were searched and prohibited objects were seized without the
cabinet specifying which. [@1]
