Officers of the drug-control department of the Central District police in St
Petersburg stopped the activity of a 30-year-old man from Novorossiysk suspected
of drug trafficking. [@1] In an apartment on Chernyshevsky Prospekt they found a
home bakery making pastries filled with an illegal substance, including cakes,
rolls, bars and cookies shaped like a marijuana leaf. [@1] The baked goods
weighed about 20 kg. [@1] Officers also seized a plastic container, jars of
plant mass, dozens of capsules of a potent medicine, three electronic scales and
a vacuum sealer. [@1]

A police forensic laboratory found that the seized substance was marijuana and
that its mass exceeded 2.3 kg. [@1] The man said he distributed the pastries
among his friends and acquaintances. [@2] The St Petersburg police press service
said the 30-year-old from Novorossiysk admitted producing and distributing
drugs. [@3]

The Central District investigative department opened a criminal case for
attempted large-scale sale of narcotic drugs under part 3 of article 30 and
point g of part 4 of article 228.1 of the Criminal Code. [@1] The suspect was
detained under article 91 of the Criminal Procedure Code, the provision for
holding a suspect in custody. [@1] The investigation continues. [@1] Besides the
pastries, officers seized more than 2.3 kg of marijuana, the plant mass, dozens
of capsules of a potent drug, electronic scales and a vacuum sealer, RBC
reported. [@4]
