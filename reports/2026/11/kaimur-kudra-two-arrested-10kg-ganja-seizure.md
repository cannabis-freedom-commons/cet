In Kanadihara village under Kudra police station in Kaimur district, police
raided on Wednesday night and arrested two alleged smugglers with about 10
kilograms of ganja. [@1,2,4,5] The two had alighted at Kudra railway station and were caught at the bus stand under the Lalapur overbridge while preparing to take a bus to Kanadihara village. [@2,4,6] Police said the action was taken on the basis of secret
information and the two arrested accused are being questioned. [@1,2,5] Station house officer Priyesh Priyadarshi formed a police team and cordoned off the area under the overbridge after receiving the tip. [@6] Police cordoned off the area under a magistrate's supervision and detained the two suspects as they began to move. [@5] The two accused tried to flee on seeing the police but were caught by the personnel. [@6] The arrested men were identified as Manowar Siddiqui, about 40, and Jai Prakash Gupta, about 28, both residents of Kanadihara under Kudra police station in Kaimur district. [@2,6]

Officers recovered about 10 kilograms of ganja during the search in Kanadihara
village. [@1] The seized 9.435 kilograms was reported to have a market value of 500,000 to 600,000 rupees and to have been brought from Raigada in Odisha. [@3,6] Police said detailed information about the case will be shared
through a press briefing. [@1] Police said the two had brought the consignment by train from Odisha, and Mohania SDPO Gopal Krishna told a Thursday evening press briefing that Kudra case number 544/26 was registered under the NDPS Act. [@2,5,6] Police said both accused had no prior criminal history and had become involved in ganja smuggling for the first time. [@6]

The seizure was described as part of an ongoing drive against narcotics
smuggling and the illicit liquor trade being carried out on the instructions of
Kaimur Superintendent of Police Shikhar Chaudhary. [@1,5] Police said action is
being taken against people linked to the illegal heroin, ganja and liquor trade,
noting that a large amount of cash had earlier been recovered with heroin in
Kudra police station area. [@1] Police are now investigating whether the
arrested accused have any contact with other people involved in narcotics
smuggling, an inquiry that follows continuous action that has caused concern
among those in the illegal trade. [@1,5]
