In Kanadihara village under Kudra police station in Kaimur district, police
raided on Wednesday night and arrested two alleged smugglers with about 10
kilograms of ganja. [@1] Police said the action was taken on the basis of secret
information and the two arrested accused are being questioned. [@1]

Officers recovered about 10 kilograms of ganja during the search in Kanadihara
village. [@1] Police said detailed information about the case will be shared
through a press briefing. [@1]

The seizure was described as part of an ongoing drive against narcotics
smuggling and the illicit liquor trade being carried out on the instructions of
Kaimur Superintendent of Police Shikhar Chaudhary. [@1] Police said action is
being taken against people linked to the illegal heroin, ganja and liquor trade,
noting that a large amount of cash had earlier been recovered with heroin in
Kudra police station area. [@1] Police are now investigating whether the
arrested accused have any contact with other people involved in narcotics
smuggling, an inquiry that follows continuous action that has caused concern
among those in the illegal trade. [@1]
