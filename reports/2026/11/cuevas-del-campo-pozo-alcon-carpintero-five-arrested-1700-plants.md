Policía Nacional officers from the Baza commissariat in Granada arrested five
people and dismantled four cannabis plantations inside homes in Pozo Alcón, in
Jaén province, and Cuevas del Campo, in Granada province. [@1] The five detained
were three men and two women aged between 32 and 56, all with previous police
records. [@1] Officers carried out nine searches in the early hours of Thursday
19 November under an operation named Operación Carpintero. [@1]

In four of the homes searched police found rooms fitted out for cultivation with
lighting, aeration and ventilation systems. [@1] They seized more than 1,700
cannabis plants at different stages of growth, some of them in advanced
flowering, distributed across the rooms. [@1] The same searches produced 800
grams of hashish, 50 grams of cocaine, jewellery, 1,500 euros in cash, three
high-end vehicles, knives and air guns, ammunition, mobile phones, precision
scales, vacuum-packing machines and body armour. [@1] Some of the homes had
high-security doors that made it harder for officers to get in. [@1] During the
searches police also found illegal connections to the electricity supply. [@1]

The five are being investigated for drug trafficking, membership of a criminal
group and electricity fraud. [@1] The operation remained open and further
arrests were not ruled out. [@3] Police had identified other people they had yet
to detain, Hora Jaén reported. [@2] Officers from the local commissariat in Baza
and the provincial commissariat in Granada, from judicial police and public
security units, took part. [@1]
