North Sumatra police said they uncovered 981 drug cases involving 1,311 suspects
and seized 30.34 tons of various drugs and 23,868 milliliters of
narcotics-containing liquid over 46 days. [@1,2,4,5] Police said the enforcement
operations ran from 1 October to 15 November 2026. [@1,2,3,5,6] In 1,019 operations officers uncovered 301 cases and detained 397 suspects. [@3] Polda Sumut also intensified Operation Sarang Narkoba during the same period. [@3] North Sumatra police
chief Inspector General Sonny Irawan announced the results on 16 November 2026
at the North Sumatra police headquarters in Medan. [@1,4,5,6]

From those operations, police seized 172 kilograms of methamphetamine, 157
kilograms of cannabis, 150,012 cannabis plants, 30,510 ecstasy pills and 23,868
milliliters of narcotics-containing liquid. [@1,2,3,4,5,6] Those operations seized 84,685.11 grams of methamphetamine, 57,284.08 grams of cannabis, 14 cannabis plants, 321 ecstasy pills, 3,673 etomidate vapes and Rp645 million in cash. [@3] Police also seized Rp1.2
billion in cash and detained seven suspects alleged to be dealers. [@1,3,4,5,6] The Rp1.2 billion seized is part of three money-laundering cases that police said remain under investigation. [@4] Officers also dismantled and burned 119 barracks or huts used for drug abuse and distribution. [@3] Officers also took a rehabilitative approach to 535 people who tested positive for drugs on urine tests but were found carrying no drug evidence. [@3] Police
said the seized evidence was estimated to have saved 93,469,666 lives and was
valued at Rp3.36 trillion. [@1,4]

Sonny said the enforcement reflects the National Police commitment to
eradicating drug abuse and illicit distribution while maintaining public
security and order. [@1] He said drugs are not only a law-enforcement issue but
also a threat to youth, security, public health and the future of the nation.
[@1] Polda Sumut asked for support from all elements of society to help
eradicate narcotics in the region. [@1]
