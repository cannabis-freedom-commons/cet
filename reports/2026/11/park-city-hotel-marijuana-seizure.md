A tiny amount of a substance believed to be marijuana was discovered in a Park
City hotel last weekend, the Police Department said. [@1] The agency received
the report a bit before 4 p.m. on Saturday, and public police logs indicated
that someone had left weed in one of the rooms of the hotel. [@1] A
representative of the Main Street hotel indicated to the police that it is the
hotel's policy to report that sort of case, the logs said. [@1]

A Police Department narrative released in response to a Park Record request
under state open records laws said two jars were found. [@1] One jar contained
0.1 gram of bud marijuana, while the other, described as a prescription bottle,
contained a glass pipe and earbuds, the narrative said. [@1]

The police said the owner of the marijuana was unknown and that the substance
was booked into evidence and would be destroyed. [@1]
