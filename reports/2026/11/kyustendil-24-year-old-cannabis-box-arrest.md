Police in Kyustendil detained a 24-year-old man after a box of cannabis was
found in the dwelling where he lives. [@1] Criminal investigators found and
seized the box of cannabis during procedural actions in a dwelling in the city
occupied by the 24-year-old man. [@2] The substance was found the previous day
during procedural actions in the property occupied by the man. [@1]

A field narcotics test identified the substance as cannabis. [@1] The occupant
of the dwelling was detained under a police order. [@1] The Kyustendil district
police opened fast-track police proceedings for possession of narcotic
substances. [@2] A duty prosecutor was notified of the case. [@1]
