Felipe 'El Militar' Sosa is accused of the femicide of Érika Antonella Álvarez
in Tucumán. [@1] Federal justice was already investigating Sosa for cultivating
more than 300 cannabis plants. [@1] The federal file was opened after a judicial
procedure in 2024 found more than 300 cannabis plants linked to him. [@2] A
January 2026 account of Sosa's cases said the federal cultivation investigation
followed a search of his home in which cannabis plants were found. [@3]

That account said Sosa was dismissed in the federal cultivation case after
proving an authorisation for medicinal use. [@3] The same account said it had
transpired that the quantity seized exceeded what was permitted and that the
Public Prosecutor's Office opposed closing the case. [@3]

The link to Sosa surfaced in a separate case over synthetic drugs sold at
electronic parties, where investigators found two phone contacts associated with
him. [@1] The narcomenudeo prosecutor José Sanjuán sent the finding to federal
justice and federal prosecutor Agustín Chit sought to have Sosa charged with
selling synthetic drugs. [@1] That accusation was added to the federal file that
already investigated him for producing marijuana. [@2]

The femicide trial is a separate proceeding: the drugs material does not form
part of the hearings but could prompt new investigations into drug selling in
Tucumán. [@1] Suspicions of drug selling do not prove Sosa's part in the killing
or change his position in the femicide case. [@1]
