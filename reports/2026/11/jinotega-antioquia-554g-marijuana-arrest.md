National Police officers captured Jonny Ramón Dormuz Montenegro, 34, during a
citizen-security operation (operativo de seguridad ciudadana) in comarca
Antioquia, in the department of Jinotega. [@1] Officers searched the suspect and
found inside his backpack a plastic bag containing green herb, which weighed
554.9 grams of marijuana, more than half a pound. [@1]

The detainee, together with the seized evidence, was taken to the police
delegation and will be remitted to the competent authorities in Jinotega to face
due process for the alleged crime of possession of narcotics. [@1] The article
attributes no plants or other drugs to the arrest and gives no value for the
marijuana. [@1]
