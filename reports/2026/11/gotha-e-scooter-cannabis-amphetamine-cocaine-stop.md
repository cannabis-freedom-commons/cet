On Monday evening, 16 November 2026, a 37-year-old woman was riding an e-scooter
on the Erfurter Landstraße in Gotha when officers stopped her in a traffic
control. [@2] A drug pre-test carried out during the stop was positive for
amphetamine/methamphetamine, cannabis and cocaine, the Landespolizeiinspektion
Gotha said. [@1] Officers prevented the woman from riding on, ordered a blood
sample and opened a regulatory offence procedure (Ordnungswidrigkeitenverfahren)
against her. [@1]

The 37-year-old had been stopped after riding the e-scooter on the Erfurter
Landstraße, and the roadside test returned positive results for amphetamine,
methamphetamine, cannabis and cocaine. [@3]
