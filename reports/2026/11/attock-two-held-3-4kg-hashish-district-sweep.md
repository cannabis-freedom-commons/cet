Attock police arrested 30 suspects across Attock district on Monday in separate
drug and gambling operations. [@1] Sixteen of those held were described as
alleged drug peddlers and 14 as alleged gamblers. [@1] Jand police arrested
Faheem Khan, son of Hakeem Khan, during a checking operation. [@1] Hassan Abdal
Sadar police, under the supervision of Sub-Inspector Kamran, apprehended Waqas,
son of Mohibullah and a resident of Battagram. [@1]

Officers recovered two kilograms of hashish from Faheem Khan's possession. [@1]
Officers recovered another 1.4 kilograms of hashish from Waqas. [@1] Police put
the total hashish recovered in the district-wide operations at 3.4 kilograms.
[@1]

Fateh Jang police formed a special team on a gambling tip-off, raided the
identified location and arrested 14 alleged gamblers. [@1] Police seized Rs7.2
million allegedly placed as gambling stakes in that raid. [@1] Separate cases
were registered against the arrested suspects at the respective police stations
while further investigations and legal proceedings were under way. [@1]
