The Força Tática of the 4th Military Police Battalion arrested a man wanted over
a homicide on Thursday 19 November 2026. [@1] The arrest took place on a rural
property in the CUT area of the Nova Itamarati district in Ponta Porã. [@2] Nova
Itamarati lies in Ponta Porã, on Brazil's border with Paraguay. [@3] The warrant
had been issued by the court of the São José do Rio Preto judicial district in
São Paulo. [@2]

The operation followed a request for support from the homicide unit of São
Paulo's state criminal investigations department, the Deic. [@1] Comando Força
Patrulha and Força Tática teams worked with investigators from the Ponta Porã
Civil Police's general investigations unit, the SIG, to confirm the wanted man's
location. [@1]

In searches at the property, officers seized 333 grams of marijuana. [@1] They
also found a .38 revolver loaded with six rounds and seven more intact rounds of
the same calibre. [@1] Officers collected three mobile phones, to be sent to São
Paulo's Civil Police to support the homicide inquiry. [@1] Two people were taken
to the 1st Civil Police Station in Ponta Porã with the weapon, ammunition, drug
and phones. [@2]
