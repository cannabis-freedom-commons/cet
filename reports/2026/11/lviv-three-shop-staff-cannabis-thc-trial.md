Three employees of a Lviv chain of shops, a 41-year-old manager and two
salespeople aged 20 and 21, have been sent to trial. [@1] All three are Lviv
residents; the salespeople are a 20-year-old man and a 21-year-old woman. [@2]
The indictment has been sent to court, and the three face charges under part 2
of article 307 of the Criminal Code, which covers illegal production,
acquisition, storage, transport or sale of narcotic drugs, psychotropic
substances and their analogues. [@2] The offence carries six to ten years in
prison with confiscation of property. [@2]

Police say the shops traded under the signboards of a well-known all-Ukrainian
chain, alongside legal products. [@2] They stood at seven locations in Lviv.
[@3] The manager handled the purchase and storage of the goods, logistics,
advertising, staff recruitment and training and the division of the proceeds,
and sometimes served customers herself. [@2] Products sold as dietary
supplements, cigarettes, roll-your-own tobacco, souvenirs, jelly bears and
sweets contained delta-9-THC, delta-8-THC, ADB-4en-PINACA and PVP. [@2] One
accused salesman bought a batch of cannabis from a specialist online shop and
stored it for sale; officers seized it before he could sell it. [@2]

In April 2026 officers searched the homes of the manager and the salespeople,
the manager's car and the shops. [@4] They seized 7,122 bottles of drinks, 6,207
sweets, 1,438 joints, 784 bars of chocolate and 649 tubs of jelly bears. [@4]
The seized substances were to be sent for expert testing. [@4] Officers also
took over 650,000 hryvnia and 650 US dollars in cash, along with phones,
packaging equipment, smoking devices and shop documents. [@4] The three were
notified of suspicion under part 2 of article 307 in April. [@4] The chain then
had 12 shops in Lviv, nine of them in the city centre. [@4]

Officers of the drug crime department and Lviv Oblast police investigators
worked the case under the procedural supervision of the Lviv Oblast Prosecutor's
Office. [@2]
