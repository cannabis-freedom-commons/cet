Ahmedabad City Police arrested three people, including a woman, in three
separate single-day Zone-7 operations under the ‘No Drugs in Ahmedabad City’
campaign. [@2] A joint Zone-7 Local Crime Branch and Sarkhej Police team
arrested 26-year-old Sajidshah Habibshah Diwan, a Decent Avenue resident
originally from Varahi in Patan district. [@2] Anandnagar Police arrested
25-year-old Navghanbhai Laljibhai Vaghela and seized 663 grams of cannabis
valued at Rs 33,150. [@2] Vasna Police arrested 27-year-old Urmilaben Parmar and
seized 35.760 grams of cannabis, a mobile phone and Rs 400 in cash. [@2]

The three operations together yielded 48.740 grams of MD drugs and 698.760 grams
of cannabis, along with cash, mobile phones and other materials. [@2] In the
biggest seizure the joint Zone-7 team recovered 48.740 grams of MD drugs valued
at Rs 2,94,320. [@2] The total value of the Anandnagar seized property was Rs
37,751, including a mobile phone worth Rs 4,000 and Rs 601 in cash. [@2] Police
put the total value of the Vasna seizure at Rs 2,688. [@2]

Police registered three cases under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic
Substances (NDPS) Act at Sarkhej, Anandnagar and Vasna stations. [@2] Police
said other members of the supply chain were involved in the Sarkhej, Anandnagar
and Vasna cases. [@2] The three cases were registered on 24 November. [@1]
Hitesh of Dhanera in Banaskantha district was reported as absconding in the
Sarkhej case. [@2] Bhurabhai Valmiki of the Jamalpur Riverfront flower market
was yet to be arrested. [@2] Nita Bhati of Gulbai Tekra was also being sought.
[@2] The operations followed senior officials’ directions to intensify action
against the sale and purchase of narcotics under the campaign. [@2]
