Cairo Criminal Court closed a gang case over drug trafficking and unlicensed
firearms and ammunition in Al-Muthallath in Helwan. [@1] The court sentenced the
first and second defendants to ten years of rigorous imprisonment each and the
third defendant to three years' imprisonment. [@1] The case headline identifies
the first two defendants as a father and his son. [@1]

The case arose from an incident at Espeeko housing in Al-Muthallath in Helwan
that led to the arrest of three defendants from one family. [@1] A Helwan police
investigations force led by Ahmed El-Daly under Mohamed Magdy carried out the
arrest operation under a prosecution arrest order. [@1] The prosecution said the
three formed a gang led by the first defendant, joined by the second and third,
to traffic hashish. [@1] Prosecutors recorded about 837 grams of drugs, two
shotgun rifles, a single shotgun, live rounds and empty cases. [@1]

The charges also covered possessing drugs for trafficking, holding unlicensed
smoothbore firearms and ammunition, and resisting the arrest force. [@1] The
arrest report said the force came under gunfire and was pelted with stones and
empty bottles around Espeeko housing. [@1] During the investigation prosecutors
confronted the first defendant with accusations of attempting to kill Mohamed
Mohsen and of using force against officer Ahmed El-Daly. [@1] The first
defendant denied both accusations, answering “it didn't happen” to each. [@1]
