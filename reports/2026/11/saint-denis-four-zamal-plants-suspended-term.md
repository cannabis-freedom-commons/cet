The tribunal correctionnel de Saint-Denis heard the case on 20 November 2026.
[@1] The court sentenced the third defendant, the young Réunionese, to four
months' suspended prison. [@1] Police had found four cannabis plants, known as
zamal, at his home. [@1] The prosecutor had asked for six months' prison for
him. [@1]

The case began on 21 May 2025, when a BAC anti-crime squad crew checked a
Peugeot 308 in a street in Saint-Denis. [@1] One occupant threw a bag of white
powder under the passenger seat during the check. [@1] Officers recovered 84.36
g of cocaine and seized 1,350 euros from Jules and 3,470 euros from Garry
Pierre. [@1]

Garry Pierre, a native of Kourou, had made about 20 trips between Guyane,
Fort-de-France and Paris in recent months. [@1] The prosecutor called Garry
Pierre a central figure in the cocaine traffic despite his young age and clean
record. [@1] The court sentenced Garry Pierre to three years' prison and Jules
to 18 months. [@1] Jules had already been given four years' prison in July for
similar offences. [@1] Daril, who travelled with Garry Pierre, is to be tried
later. [@1]

Videos found on Garry Pierre's phone showed trainers being taken apart with
cocaine hidden inside. [@1] Another video showed people with suitcases as a
voice called out "Bonjour les mules!" [@1] Garry Pierre told the court he did
not know where the images came from and that they had probably been shared in a
Snapchat group. [@1]
