Police from Mexico City's Secretariat of Citizen Security (SSC) arrested two
men, aged 30 and 32, in the Agrícola Oriental neighbourhood of Iztacalco
borough. [@1] Officers on a patrol saw the pair handling plastic bags like those
used to distribute narcotics at the corner of Ignacio Zaragoza and Oriente 257.
[@1] Both men ran when they noticed the police and were caught a few metres
away. [@1] A preventive search found 105 plastic baggies holding dry green herb
with the characteristics of marijuana, along with cash. [@3]

The two were informed of their constitutional rights and handed to the Public
Prosecutor's Office with the seized items, and that authority will decide their
legal situation. [@1]

The SSC said a records cross-check showed the 30-year-old had previously been
brought before a Mexico City prosecutor over criminal damage (daño en propiedad
ajena) recorded in 2021. [@1] The authorities did not say whether that earlier
record is connected to any investigation now under way. [@2] The arrest came
during the SSC's security and surveillance patrols in Iztacalco borough. [@3]
