Railway police arrested a woman at Santragachi station in West Bengal on
suspicion of trafficking ganja, the Bengali term for cannabis. [@1] The arrested woman was identified as 41-year-old Lakshmi Antal, a resident of Kondel. [@2] Officers
recovered about 13 kilograms of cannabis from two bags she was carrying. [@1,2] Police put the combined net weight of the ganja at 12.940 kg, with 6.495 kg from a maroon-and-red first bag and 6.445 kg from the second, against gross weights of 7.185 kg and 7.035 kg. [@2]
Kharagpur railway superintendent of police Jayanta Mukhopadhyay said the
recovery took place at Santragachi station on Monday. [@1]

The woman was spotted that morning between platforms 6 and 7 of the station.
[@1,2] Police were patrolling in white clothing when they noticed her behaving
suspiciously. [@1] Sub-inspector Shyamal Sarkar acted on information about a suspicious woman during crime-control surveillance, and she was questioned with the help of woman constable Suparna Singh. [@2] Officers questioned her and found inconsistencies in her
account. [@1] The woman admitted that the bags contained ganja, and the search was carried out in the presence of gazetted officer Arunoday Chakraborty, who found two brown packets. [@2]
