Railway police arrested a woman at Santragachi station in West Bengal on
suspicion of trafficking ganja, the Bengali term for cannabis. [@1] Officers
recovered about 13 kilograms of cannabis from two bags she was carrying. [@1]
Kharagpur railway superintendent of police Jayanta Mukhopadhyay said the
recovery took place at Santragachi station on Monday. [@1]

The woman was spotted that morning between platforms 6 and 7 of the station.
[@1] Police were patrolling in white clothing when they noticed her behaving
suspiciously. [@1] Officers questioned her and found inconsistencies in her
account. [@1]
