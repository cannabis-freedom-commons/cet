Alès police arrested four young men, including two minors, over the weekend in
the Cévennes district. [@1] The arrests of the four young men were made during
an operation against drug trafficking in the Cévennes district of Alès. [@1]
Members of the force intervened in the last hours at a point de deal, a street
dealing point, in the Cévennes district. [@1]

Officers seized about 400 grams of drugs in total during the intervention,
including both cannabis resin and cocaine. [@1] The drugs were discovered in the
suspects' possession by police during the same operation against drug
trafficking. [@1]

All four suspects were referred to the courts for a hearing on Monday 16
November. [@1] The referral included a fast-track immediate-appearance procedure
for the adult suspects among the four. [@1] Police described the intervention as
a fruitful weekend in the fight against drug trafficking in Alès. [@1] The
intervention was presented as part of the mobilisation of Alès police against
street drug-selling points across the city's neighbourhoods. [@1]
