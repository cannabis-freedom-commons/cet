Prosecutors in Karangasem destroyed 303 items of evidence from 40 criminal cases
that had become final and binding. [@1] The destruction was carried out on
Wednesday 18 November 2026 and covered cases closed between June and November
2026. [@1] Among the destroyed evidence was cannabis weighing 6.28 grams gross
and 5.70 grams net. [@1] The same narcotics batch also included methamphetamine,
known locally as sabu, and synthetic tobacco, which is not counted as cannabis.
[@1] The 40 cases also covered theft, sexual offences, child abduction and
abuse, illegal sand mining, subsidised LPG decanting and unlicensed alcohol
sales. [@1]

The cannabis, methamphetamine and synthetic tobacco were destroyed by blending.
[@2] Officers also destroyed bongs, glass tubes, modified lighters and straws,
scales and plastic clips used in drug use. [@1] The destruction took place in
the back yard of the Karangasem prosecutor's office with the district's regional
leadership forum, Forkopimda. [@2]

Karangasem district attorney Kusufi Esti Ridliani said the destruction was the
prosecutors' duty as executors of court decisions that had become final. [@1]
She said the aim was to finish evidence handling in an orderly, transparent and
accountable way and to avoid a pile-up of evidence in the office's storage. [@1]
The event was attended by the regional secretary representing the regent, the
district council chair, the Karangasem police chief, the district military
commander, the head of the Amlapura district court, the local narcotics agency
head and the head of Karangasem prison. [@3]
