Police found a door on the back wall and arrested Dang alongside 1118 cannabis
plants. [@4] Dang was allegedly the only person found on site when police
cleared the warehouse. [@3] He pleaded guilty last week to possession of a
commercial quantity of cannabis and dealing with the proceeds of crime and will
be sentenced in Newcastle District Court next year. [@4] Bankstown man Van
Nguyen, 39, found hiding in the shed at Birmingham Gardens, and Austral
grandfather Quang Toan Dang, 51, arrested behind the "false room" at
Broadmeadow, had both taken the job of crop sitter, a miserable existence that
so many other Vietnamese nationals have found themselves living in the Hunter
over the past decade. [@4]

About 1.45pm on Thursday 26 February 2026, police attended a warehouse on Newton
Street, New Lambton, to execute a search warrant. [@2] Officers attached to the
Hunter Region Enforcement Squad and the Northern Operations Support Group
allegedly located a large hydroponic cannabis set-up inside the building. [@2]
The large warehouse at Broadmeadow had what police called a "false room" near
its entrance, concealing the sprawling $2.2 million hydroponic cannabis
operation behind. [@1] Police alleged a total of 1,118 cannabis plants were
located and seized during the search, with an estimated potential street value
of $2,236,000. [@2] Police allegedly discovered 1118 cannabis plants, ranging
from 20 centimetres to two metres tall, with an estimated potential street value
of $2,236,000. Officers also allegedly seized $1170 cash. [@3]

A man was arrested at the scene and taken to Waratah Police Station, where he
was charged with knowingly taking part in the cultivation of a prohibited plant
of a large commercial quantity, diverting or using electricity for a generating
system without authority, and dealing with property suspected of being proceeds
of crime (less than $100,000). [@2] The court heard electricity had been
diverted with a fictitious name and business used. [@3] Mr Overall said Dang
claimed his attendance at the warehouse on Newton Street where more than 1100
cannabis plants were allegedly being grown was because he was a cleaner and
"deliverer of materials". [@3] The police prosecutor argued the case against
Dang was strong as he was seen tending to the maintenance of the cannabis
plants. [@3]
