Melanie McKamie and Robin McKamie of San Antonio were arrested on a Saturday
after a traffic stop in Hallettsville led to the seizure of drugs, a handgun and
other contraband. [@1] The stop took place on 14 November 2026, when a Lavaca
County Sheriff's Office deputy assigned to Operation Lone Star pulled over a
vehicle in the 100 block of US Highway 77S. [@2] The deputy stopped the vehicle
for disregarding a traffic control device at the intersection of North Texana
Street and West Fourth Street. [@2] Both occupants were arrested and charged
over what the search found. [@2]

A probable-cause search of the vehicle found THC vapes, gummies and wax along
with marijuana. [@2] Officers also seized a handgun, prescription medications
and other contraband. [@2] Both face a first-degree felony count of possessing a
controlled substance in Penalty Group 2 of 400 grams or more. [@2] They also
face three misdemeanor charges. [@2]
