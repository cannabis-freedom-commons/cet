Officers of the judicial police brigade in Ariana city, Tunisia, dismantled what
they described as a dangerous criminal network specialising in the trafficking
of narcotic substances and money laundering, active across the Hay Ennazha, Hay
El Mostakbal and Ariana Supérieure neighbourhoods. [@1] The operation came after
a series of close monitoring and intensive field investigations that identified
the network's main members and the places where they hid, leading to
simultaneous surprise raids in those neighbourhoods and the arrest of the people
involved in the illegal activity. [@1]

The searches turned up a quantity of cannabis sheets prepared for trafficking,
described as Indian hemp and known locally as zatla, together with a significant
quantity of cocaine. [@1] Officers also recovered equipment including cutting
tools and a precise electronic scale used to weigh and traffic the drugs, as
well as significant sums of money suspected of being proceeds of drug
trafficking. [@1]

After consulting the public prosecutor at the Court of First Instance in Ariana,
the authorities were authorised to retain the suspects in custody. [@1]
