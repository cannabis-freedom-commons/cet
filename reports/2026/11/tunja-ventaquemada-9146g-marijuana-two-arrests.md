The Metropolitan Police of Tunja reported two operations against drug
trafficking, supported by dogs trained to detect narcotics, through its
Carabineros and Environmental Protection Section and its Carabineros and Canine
Guides Group (GRUK9). [@1] At a courier company in the Parque Industrial in
Tunja, the dog 'Matt' alerted officers to a cardboard box holding 2,146 grams of
plant material resembling marijuana and 62 grams of a rock-like substance
resembling bazuco, a cocaine paste. [@1] Those substances were seized and left
with the 47th Sectional Prosecutor's Office (URI) in Tunja. [@1]

On the Bogotá–Tunja national highway at the Pailitas sector, in the municipality
of Ventaquemada, officers carrying out registration and control checks with a
narcotics dog found a suitcase carried by two passengers of a public-service
bus. [@1] The suitcase held approximately 7 kilograms of plant material
resembling marijuana. [@1] Two men aged 28 and 27 were arrested for trafficking,
manufacturing or possession of narcotics and handed to the judicial authorities.
[@1]

The National Police put the two seizures at approximately 9,208 grams of
narcotic substances, a total that includes the 62 grams of bazuco found in the
parcel. [@1]
