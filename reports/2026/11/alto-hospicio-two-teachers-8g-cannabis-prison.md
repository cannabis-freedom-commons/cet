Two teachers working at the Complejo Penitenciario de Alto Hospicio in Tarapacá
were detained by the Investigations Police (PDI) and Gendarmería on Tuesday
morning as they brought drugs into the prison. [@1] PDI anti-narcotics
detectives made the arrest at the prison's access controls and found the drugs
hidden among the teachers' clothing. [@2] The drugs were not for personal use
but were meant to be sold among the prison population of Alto Hospicio. [@3]

Police said the operation seized more than 21 grams of cocaine hydrochloride, 8
grams of cannabis and more than 80 controlled-medication tablets. [@1]
Preliminary examinations by PDI detectives and Gendarmería staff established
that all the drugs were meant to be supplied inside the prison, particularly to
inmates. [@1]

Both were handed to the Ministerio Público: one teacher was formally charged
with micro-trafficking and sent to the Tribunal de Garantía de Alto Hospicio for
a detention-control hearing, while the second was cautioned pending further
inquiries and a summons from the local prosecutor's office. [@1] The court
imposed arraigo, a residence order, monthly signing and a ban on approaching the
prison, setting a 120-day investigation period. [@2]
