Excise officers in Aluva, Kerala, arrested Alikunju, a 43-year-old man known as
Ali Bhai, after finding 8.635 kg of cannabis being carried in an auto-rickshaw.
[@1] The check was made by an excise circle party led by Circle Inspector Jose
Prathap. [@2] The interception took place on Edathala Central Warehouse Road,
and the accused's home was in Chunangamvely. [@3] The check formed part of
Operation Thunder in the Aluva and Edathala areas. [@3]

Excise authorities said he routinely bought large quantities of cannabis from
migrant cannabis traders from other states and sold it on to small-scale dealers
in Aluva and Perumbavoor. [@2] Officers seized the auto-rickshaw, registration
KL-40Q-0968, used to carry the cannabis and 3,370 rupees in proceeds from its
sale. [@1]

The searching party also included excise inspectors Shyamkumar, Johnson and Arun
Kumar, shadow-team members Jibinas, Vishnu and Salahudeen, women's civil excise
officer Nija Joy and civil excise officer-driver Pradeep. [@1]
