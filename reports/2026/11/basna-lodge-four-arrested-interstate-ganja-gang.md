Police at Basna, in Chhattisgarh's Mahasamund district, raided a lodge in the
town on 19 November 2026 after a tip that suspects from Madhya Pradesh and
Odisha were planning to buy, sell and move ganja between the two states. [@1]
Four men from the two states were in the lodge, with a Hero Passion Pro
motorcycle registered CG-06/GB-9277. [@1] Police said Mangal Singh Meena and
Hemraj Meena had built an organised gang with associates in Madhya Pradesh and
an Odisha supplier who lives in Rudkidhadar village, in Bargarh district's
Jharbandh police station area. [@1] The other two arrested, Lingraj Guru and
Udeept Pradhan, brought the ganja to Basna on the motorcycle and acted as
intermediaries and pilots for the deliveries, police said. [@1] All four were
arrested and presented in court on 20 November 2026, which sent them to judicial
custody. [@1]

Officers seized four Android mobile phones worth about 40,000 rupees and the
motorcycle, which police said was used to carry the ganja and to pilot
consignments. [@1] No ganja was delivered at the lodge: police said a bank
transfer to buy about 25 kilograms from the main supplier, Suresh Nayak, was due
on 19 November but the payment was not made in time and the raid stopped the
delivery. [@1] Police said PhonePe records on Mangal Singh Meena's Oppo phone
showed the gang had given him 15 kilograms of ganja in two bags about 15 days
earlier, for which 70,000 rupees was paid digitally. [@1]

A case was registered at Basna police station, crime number 652/2026, under
sections 29(1) and 30 of the NDPS Act and section 61(2) of the Bharatiya Nyaya
Sanhita. [@1] Police said Lingraj Guru took consignments on to Bhopal with the
Madhya Pradesh gang. [@1] Police said they were still tracing the main supplier,
who was at large, and other associates. [@1]
