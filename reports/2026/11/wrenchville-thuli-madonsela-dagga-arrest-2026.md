Members of the JTG District Highway Patrol followed up on a tip-off about
suspected dagga at a residence in Thuli Madonsela Street, Wrenchville, at
approximately 5:30pm on 16 November 2026. [@1,2] They arrested a 31-year-old man
for possession of dagga during the intelligence-driven operation in Wrenchville
on 16 November 2026. [@1,2]

Northern Cape police spokesperson Captain Nomthandazo Mnisi said officers
introduced themselves to the occupant and explained the purpose of their visit.
[@1] After presenting a search warrant, officers were granted permission to
search the premises. [@1,2] During the search, police discovered and confiscated
dagga with an estimated street value of R220 000. [@1,2]

The suspect is expected to appear in the Kuruman Magistrate’s Court soon. [@1,2]
Northern Cape Police Commissioner Lieutenant General Koliswa Otola commended the
members for their continued commitment to combating drug-related crimes. [@1]
Otola said drugs remain a serious threat to the safety and well-being of
communities, particularly to young people. [@1] Otola said police would continue
intensified intelligence-driven operations and called on community members to
report suspected drug activities. [@1]
