Two men were arrested at the Itaballi Police Checkpoint in Region Seven on
Thursday 19 November 2026 after police found a 9mm Taurus pistol, five rounds of
ammunition and 1,036 grams of suspected cannabis. [@1] Ranks stopped a Honda XR
motorcycle carrying two men who were travelling to Puruni Landing. [@1] The
rider was behaving suspiciously, so the officers searched both men, their
belongings and the motorcycle. [@1]

A parcel of leaves, seeds and stems suspected to be cannabis was found inside a
pressure washer the men were carrying. [@1] Further searches of the motorcycle
found another parcel of suspected cannabis, the pistol and a magazine holding
five matching rounds. [@1]

The rider is a 32-year-old from Charity on the Essequibo Coast, and the pillion
rider is a 22-year-old excavator operator from La Parfaite Harmonie, West Bank
Demerara. [@1] The two were arrested and escorted to the Bartica Police Station
with the suspected cannabis, firearm and ammunition. [@1] The cannabis was
weighed in their presence at the station and amounted to 1,036 grams. [@1] The
cannabis, firearm and ammunition were lodged pending further investigation. [@1]
Both men remained in police custody as investigations continued. [@1]
