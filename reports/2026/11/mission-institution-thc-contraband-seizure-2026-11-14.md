Staff at Mission Institution seized a package containing methamphetamine, THC, a
cellphone and a charging cable on 14 November 2026. [@1] The package was seized
in the prison's medium-security unit on Saturday 14 November. [@2] The prison is
a medium-security facility in B.C.'s Fraser Valley. [@1]

The Correctional Service of Canada put the total estimated institutional value
of the seizure at $434,300. [@2] The agency says institutional value is its
estimate of what contraband is worth inside a given prison, generally higher
than the same items outside prison walls. [@1] The seizure mixed methamphetamine
and THC with a phone and charging cable, and no separate weight for the THC was
published. [@2]

The agency credited the vigilance of staff members for the seizure. [@1] Police
were notified and the institution is investigating. [@2] The agency says it uses
ion scanners and drug-detector dogs to search buildings, personal property,
inmates and visitors. [@1] It says it works with police against attempts to
introduce contraband and runs a telephone tip line for security information.
