A joint anti-drug operation in Keraniganj, Dhaka, led to ten people receiving
prison terms and fines on 17 November 2026. [@1] The sweep ran throughout the
day and detentions were made in several areas of the upazila. [@1] In the first
phase, led by the Assistant Commissioner (Land) for Keraniganj, Md. Adnan
Zulfikar, five people were detained. [@1] Among those detained, Tafsir Ahmed and
Nazrul Islam of Borhanibagh were arrested separately with ganja and each
sentenced to 20 days in jail and a 200-taka fine. [@1] Two other men, Mohammad
Khairul of Kalatia Bazar and Mohammad Rajib of Bounakandi, had yaba recovered
from them and were each given 30 days and a 200-taka fine, a non-cannabis
element excluded from these figures. [@1]

In a second phase, led by Assistant Commissioner (Land) Md. Sajid Hossain,
officers raided the Ambagicha area of Agangan Union in the southern part of
Keraniganj. [@1] Five more people were caught red-handed consuming ganja; four
received five days in jail each and one received 15 days of rigorous
imprisonment, all with 50-taka fines. [@1]

In total, ten people were given prison terms and fines in the operation, seven
of them over ganja. [@1] The administration said the operation again reflected
its firm stance against drug abuse and drug use. [@1] It said such operations
would continue to stop the spread of drugs, protect young people and keep law
and order normal. [@1]
