Manamunda Police seized more than 8 quintals of ganja from an Ashok Leyland
truck and arrested two alleged interstate drug traffickers in Odisha's Boudh
district. [@1] A patrol team conducting routine surveillance in the
Gudvelipadar-Bilaspur area intercepted an Ashok Leyland truck bearing
registration number WB-11AF-7357 near the Meheruni Bridge. [@1] Upon searching
the vehicle, police discovered several sacks filled with ganja. [@1]

The truck's occupants were identified as Hardev Chauhan (57) of Revati in Ballia
district, Uttar Pradesh, and Aditya Chaurasia (20) of Alam Bazar under Balanagar
police limits in West Bengal. [@1] When asked to produce valid documents or
authorisation for transporting the contraband, the accused failed to do so. [@1]
In the presence of an Executive Magistrate and independent witnesses,
authorities weighed the seized substance and found it to be 810 kilograms (8
quintals and 10 kg) packed in 18 sacks. [@1]

Police seized the ganja along with the truck and arrested both accused. [@1] A
case was registered under relevant provisions of the NDPS Act, with further
investigation under way to ascertain the source and destination of the
consignment. [@1]
