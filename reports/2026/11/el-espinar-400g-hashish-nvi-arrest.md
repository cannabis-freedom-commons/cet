The Guardia Civil arrested a 26-year-old man from Valladolid in El Espinar after
a checkpoint stop on the N-VI. [@1] He was held on suspicion of an offence
against public health, the Spanish charge for drug trafficking. [@1] Officers of
the Citizen Security Unit of the Segovia Command were running a preventive
citizen-security and road check at about 23:45 on 21 November 2026. [@1] The
driver of a high-end car made an evasive manoeuvre when he saw them before
reaching the checkpoint. [@1]

Guards stopped the car at once and searched the area until they recovered the
wrapper, which held a substance that official weighing put at almost 400 grams
of hashish. [@1] The man was taken to the Guardia Civil post in El Espinar for
the case file. [@1] He was then brought before the duty court in Segovia. [@2]
