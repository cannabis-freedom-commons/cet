Police stopped a car at the Bhaise police checkpoint in Bhimphedi Rural
Municipality-2 at about 1:40 on Wednesday night during a routine check. [@2,4] The
car, registration Ba.A.Ba. 4431, was travelling from Palung toward Bhaise when
police stopped it. [@2] The car was heading toward Hetauda via the Tribhuvan Highway when it was stopped. [@3] As officers began checking the car, the driver said
“nothing, sir” and fled, abandoning the vehicle. [@2,4] District Police Office spokesperson Deputy Superintendent Madhav Prasad Kafle said the driver abandoned the vehicle and fled. [@5]

Police in Bhimphedi, Makwanpur, seized the abandoned car, and its driver was
reported as absconding. [@1,3] Officers opened the rear dickey of the car and
found marijuana packed in nine small and large packets. [@2,4] Police said that,
on weighing, the marijuana came to 117 kilograms and 910 grams. [@2] The seizure
therefore totalled 117 kilograms and 910 grams of marijuana carried in the car.
[@1]

Police took the car and the marijuana to the District Police Office in
Makwanpur. [@2,4] Police said the absconding driver was being sought and further
investigation into the incident was under way. [@2]
