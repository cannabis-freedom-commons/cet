A joint team of Tarana police and the Crime Branch arrested two alleged
smugglers who were carrying ganja on a Yamaha motorcycle. [@1,2] Police officers posed as customers to reach the traffickers, who tried to flee; two companions escaped while the other two were surrounded with the ganja. [@2] Police had
received information that smugglers were bringing ganja from Bagoda toward
Tarana on a Yamaha motorcycle. [@1] Police set up a checkpoint at Natakhedi
crossing on the Tarana-Bagoda road and stopped two youths passing on an
unnumbered Yamaha motorcycle. [@1] The arrested men's motorcycle carried a police sticker to avoid the police and had no number plate. [@2] On questioning, the two gave their names as
Ravi son of Prahlad and Chetan son of Vikram Parmar. [@1,2] Ravi was 24 years old and Chetan 26. [@2] Both men are residents
of Golva in Tarana. [@1,2]

A search of the vehicle recovered ganja. [@1] The drug was hidden inside a blue
plastic bag under the seat and weighed 5.312 kilograms. [@1,2] Police also seized both accused men's motorcycles. [@2]

Police registered an NDPS case against the accused. [@1] The accused were handed over to Tarana TI Ramnarayan Singh Bhadauria for questioning. [@2] The action was carried
out under Operation Prahar by the Tarana police and the Crime Branch. [@1,2] During questioning the traffickers said they supplied ganja from Ujjain to Dewas and Shajapur, and police were still seeking two main accused. [@2]
