A joint team of Tarana police and the Crime Branch arrested two alleged
smugglers who were carrying ganja on a Yamaha motorcycle. [@1] Police had
received information that smugglers were bringing ganja from Bagoda toward
Tarana on a Yamaha motorcycle. [@1] Police set up a checkpoint at Natakhedi
crossing on the Tarana-Bagoda road and stopped two youths passing on an
unnumbered Yamaha motorcycle. [@1] On questioning, the two gave their names as
Ravi son of Prahlad and Chetan son of Vikram Parmar. [@1] Both men are residents
of Golva in Tarana. [@1]

A search of the vehicle recovered ganja. [@1] The drug was hidden inside a blue
plastic bag under the seat and weighed 5.312 kilograms. [@1]

Police registered an NDPS case against the accused. [@1] The action was carried
out under Operation Prahar by the Tarana police and the Crime Branch. [@1]
