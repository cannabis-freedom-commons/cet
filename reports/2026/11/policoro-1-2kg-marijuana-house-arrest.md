Carabinieri carried out an anti-drug operation in the Metapontino area. [@1] In
Policoro, a man already known to law enforcement was arrested on suspicion of
possessing narcotic substances for the purpose of dealing. [@1] He was stopped
while driving his own car and subjected to checks. [@1]

Follow-up searches were extended to his home. [@1] Officers seized one kilogram
and 200 grams of marijuana. [@1] They also seized over 36 grams of cocaine,
material for weighing and packaging doses, and a mobile phone. [@1]

The suspect was placed under house arrest, at the disposal of the judicial
authority. [@1]
