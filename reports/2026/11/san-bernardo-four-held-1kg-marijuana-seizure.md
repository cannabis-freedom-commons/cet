Personnel from the 62nd San Bernardo police station, on preventive patrols,
surprised a group allegedly carrying out a drug transaction outside a housing
block on Rocallosas street. [@1] Officers checked one of those involved and then
entered the building, where they found various illicit substances. [@1]
Carabineros detained four people, one man and three women, all Chilean adults.
[@1] Those involved had criminal records, according to the information provided.
[@1] The four detainees were placed at the disposal of the Public Ministry,
which is to determine the next steps in the investigation. [@1]

Officers seized 1 kilo and 75 grams of bulk marijuana and 38 grams of dosed
marijuana, alongside 41 wraps of coca paste and 22 grams of cocaine
hydrochloride. [@2] Carabineros also seized three digital scales, cash, a
blender and two mobile phones. [@1] Two graters used to process the drug were
also found among the items. [@1]

All those detained were Chilean nationals with police records for various
offences. [@3]
