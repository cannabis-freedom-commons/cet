St. Bernard Parish deputies arrested Eugene Dejean, 21, and John Max Jr., 20, in
a large-scale drugs and weapons bust near Chalmette. [@1] Dejean lives in the
2500 block of Delille Street in Chalmette. [@2] Max lives in the 3300 block of
Sinclair Street in Chalmette. [@2] Dejean was booked with conspiracy to
distribute marijuana. [@2] Dejean was also booked with transactions involving
proceeds from drug offenses. [@1] Max was booked with possession with intent to
distribute marijuana. [@1] Max was booked with possession with intent to
distribute THC vapes. [@1] Max was booked with possession with intent to
distribute THC edibles. [@1] Max was booked with possession of a firearm in the
presence of a controlled dangerous substance. [@1] Both men had a fugitive NOPD
warrant for conspiracy to distribute marijuana. [@1] Max's bond was set at
$210,500, while Dejean is held without bond on a probation hold. [@1]

Agents recovered 174 pounds of marijuana. [@1] Agents recovered 12 firearms in
the same investigation. [@1] Agents recovered $57,328 in cash in the same
investigation. [@2] Agents recovered 579 THC vape cartridges. [@1] Agents also
recovered packaging materials and a cash counting device. [@2]

The St. Bernard Sheriff's Office ran the investigation with the Jefferson Parish
Sheriff's Office and the New Orleans Police Department. [@2] Narcotics agents, a
K-9 unit, Jefferson deputies and New Orleans police contributed to the
investigation. [@1] A background check showed Dejean is on probation for prior
drug charges. [@2] Sheriff Baumy called taking this marijuana off the streets a
significant step in disrupting drug activity in the parish. [@1]
