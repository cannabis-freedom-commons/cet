District police officers in Lwówek Śląski detained three men during the previous
weekend on suspicion of possessing banned substances. [@1] The first detainee
was a 54-year-old resident of Wleń commune who had 9.24 grams of marijuana on
him. [@1] The second was a 29-year-old driver in Lwówek Śląski commune who was
driving under the influence of intoxicants and possessed 7.52 grams of
marijuana. [@1]

The third detainee was a 44-year-old resident of Lwówek Śląski commune found
with 4.63 grams of methamphetamine, which is excluded from the cannabis figures.
[@1] The two marijuana amounts of 9.24 grams and 7.52 grams total 16.76 grams.
[@1] All three men are to answer for drug possession, and the youngest will
additionally answer for driving under the influence of intoxicants. [@1]
