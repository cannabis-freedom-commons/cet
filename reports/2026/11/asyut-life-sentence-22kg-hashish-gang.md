The Seventh Circuit of the Asyut Criminal Court sentenced defendants in a
drug-trafficking gang case to life imprisonment and acquitted the others, in a
case in which about 22 kilograms of hashish were seized. [@1] The bench was
chaired by Counselor Mohamed Abdel Hamid Qutb, with judges Mohamed Hassan
Salama, Ehab Ahmed Deheis and Amr Abdel Aziz Qarni, and secretaries Sayed Ali
Bakr and Nasser Fouad. [@1]

The case dates to March 2026, when the public prosecution charged four people
with forming a criminal gang specialising in bringing in and selling narcotics,
particularly hashish, inside the country. [@1] Investigations by the
Anti-Narcotics General Administration for the Central Upper Egypt region found
the defendants ran an organised network led by the first defendant that traded
hashish wholesale and semi-wholesale. [@1] The first defendant's son stored and
guarded the drugs, the third defendant carried them and sold them to customers,
and the group worked from the El-Fath, Abnoub and Asyut centres. [@1]

After a prosecution warrant, security forces set an ambush on the Eastern Desert
Road in Asyut governorate and caught the second and third defendants as they
prepared to hand over a drug shipment in a private car. [@1] A search of one
defendant found a locally made shotgun, two 16 mm cartridges and a sum of money.
[@1] The car held a plastic sack and a bag containing 166 blocks of hashish and
434 pieces of different sizes of the same drug. [@1] The chemical laboratory
report said all of it was hashish, with the fully wrapped blocks weighing more
than 16 kilograms and the cut pieces about 6.4 kilograms, more than 22 kilograms
in total. [@1]

Two defendants admitted in questioning that they held the drugs for sale, used
the gun to protect the trade and used the car to move and distribute the drugs.
[@1] The general attorney of the North Asyut prosecutions referred the
defendants to the criminal court on charges of trafficking narcotics and
possessing weapons and ammunition without a licence. [@1]
