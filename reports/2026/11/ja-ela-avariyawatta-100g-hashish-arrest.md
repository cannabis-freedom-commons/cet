Officers of the Western Province North Crime Division arrested a 36-year-old man
in Avariyawatta, Ja-Ela, for possession of hashish. [@1] The arrested man is a
resident of Wellawaya. [@1] Police said the arrest was made during a raid
conducted by officers of the Western Province North Crime Division. [@1]

During the raid, officers found the suspect in possession of 100 grams and 500
milligrams of hashish, or 100.5 grams in total. [@1]

Ja-Ela, the place of arrest, is in Gampaha District, north of Colombo. [@2]
Wellawaya, where the suspect lives, is in Monaragala District in Uva Province.
