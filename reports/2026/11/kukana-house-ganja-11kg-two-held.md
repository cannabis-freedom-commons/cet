Ahilyanagar's Local Crime Branch raided a house in Kukana, Nevasa taluka, and
took Shahmad Mehbub Sheikh, 48, into custody on suspicion of selling cannabis
from it. [@1] Officers carried out the raid on 19 November 2026. [@2] Sheikh
told police he had bought the ganja from Malhari Mohan Borude of Majaleshahar in
Shevgaon taluka, and Borude was also taken into custody on a supply charge. [@1]

The search of the house found 11.182 kilograms of ganja (cannabis) packed in
three sacks. [@3] Police seized the ganja with weighing scales, weights, steel
vessels and plastic bags, putting the value of the entire haul at 560,700
rupees. [@1] The ganja on its own was valued at 559,100 rupees. [@4]

The special team had been formed on the superintendent of police's orders to act
against illegal businesses in the district, and received a confidential tip that
Sheikh kept a stock of ganja at home and sold it. [@1] Both suspects were
produced at Nevasa police station with the seized material, and a case was
registered there under sections 8(c), 20(b) and 20(2)(b) of the Narcotic Drugs
and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985. [@3] The operation was carried out under
the guidance of Ahilyanagar superintendent of police Mumma Sudarshan. [@1]
