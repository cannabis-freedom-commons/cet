Four people were arrested in Natchez, Mississippi, on 25 November 2026 after
Adams County deputies searched an apartment following complaints from residents.
[@1,3] The four were Derrick Coleman, Wilneisha Fleming, Jaheim Johnson and
Derrick Washington. [@2,3] All four were taken to the Adams County Jail for
processing. [@1,3]

The Adams County Sheriff's Office Special Operations Group obtained a search
warrant for an apartment at 601 Old Washington Road, with the Mississippi Bureau
of Narcotics assisting. [@1,3] Deputies said they found 8.8 grams of marijuana, 36
dosage units of oxycodone, about 5 grams of crack cocaine and 1.2 grams of
fentanyl. [@1,3] Agents also found digital scales, razor blades and plastic bags
containing drug residue. [@1,3]

The charges listed included possession of crack cocaine, fentanyl, oxycodone and
marijuana, and possession of drug paraphernalia. [@1,3] The arrest report also
alleged intent to distribute for all four and identified a possible church-zone
enhancement because the apartment was within 1,500 feet of BibleWay Pentecostal
Church of the Apostolic Faith. [@1,3] WLBT reported the same four names and that
the arrest report included a potential church-zone enhancement. [@2] Fleming
also had two outstanding contempt of court warrants and two affidavits involving
larceny and simple assault. [@1] Sheriff Travis L. Patten said the investigation
began with neighbors who spoke up. [@1,3] All people charged are presumed innocent
unless proven guilty in court. [@1,3]
