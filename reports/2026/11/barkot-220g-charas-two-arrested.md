Barkot police arrested two young men with 220 grams of illegal charas in an
action against drug trafficking. [@1] On the evening of 13 November, Kotwali
Barkot police were conducting a checking drive near Krishna Khad when they
stopped two riders on an Apache motorcycle bearing number UK 07 DS 2148 and
searched them. [@1] The two men were identified as 23-year-old Pravesh Singh of
Panigaon, Purola, and 21-year-old Shivam Singh of Binai, Purola. [@2]

The search recovered 220 grams of illegal charas from their possession. [@1]
Police also seized the Apache motorcycle used in the smuggling. [@1] Police put
the estimated value of the seized charas at about 40,000 rupees. [@3]

Police registered a case against both men at Kotwali Barkot under sections
8/20/60 of the NDPS Act. [@3] The operation was carried out under the direction
of Superintendent of Police Kamlesh Upadhyay, supervision of Deputy
Superintendent Janak Singh Panwar and leadership of Barkot station in-charge
Subhash Chandra. [@1]
