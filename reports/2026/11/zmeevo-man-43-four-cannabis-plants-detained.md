Officers from the Balchik district police department checked a private property
in Zmeevo, a village in Balchik municipality in Dobrich Province, on 22 November
2026. [@1] The property was lived in by a 43-year-old man, the Dobrich regional
directorate of the Interior Ministry said. [@2] In the property officers found
an equipped greenhouse holding four plants at different stages of development.
[@2] A field drug test made at the site indicated that the plants were cannabis.
[@1]

During the investigative actions that followed, police found a jar in one of the
rooms of the house containing dry plant mass that also reacted to cannabis. [@3]
The 43-year-old man was detained for up to 24 hours. [@2] The regional
directorate announced the find, which local outlets reported as a broken-up
greenhouse with four cannabis plants in the village. [@4] Police said work to
clarify the case is continuing. [@2]
