The Kortrijk criminal court convicted a 31-year-old man from Halluin of drug
dealing and sentenced him to 14 months in prison. [@1] Only the five months he
had already spent in pre-trial detention were pronounced as an effective
sentence. [@1] His co-defendant Lahcen H. did not appear for his trial and was
given a fully effective one-year prison sentence. [@1] Both were ordered to pay
a fine of 8,000 euros each, and the money found on them was confiscated. [@1]
Both had earlier convictions for drug offences. [@1]

Michko B. was first seen in suspicious circumstances in Menen on 11 October 2023
but escaped, and the next day he had to leave his car in the town. [@1]
Fingerprints, DNA traces and documents led investigators to him. [@1] Police saw
him again on 28 May 2024 together with Lahcen H. (45) from Menen. [@1]

B. was carrying 16 grams of heroin, 120 euros in cash and cannabis, and H. had
two mobile phones and 20 euros. [@1] A house search yielded 21 grams of heroin
and cocaine. [@1] B. spent five months in pre-trial detention and was then
released on bail with conditions. [@1]

His lawyer Machteld Ryckeboer asked the court for a suspended sentence. [@1] She
said he was working, had moved to Wattrelos, had become a father and wore an
electronic tag after earlier convictions in France. [@1]
