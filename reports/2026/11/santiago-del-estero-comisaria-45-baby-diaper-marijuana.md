Two women were held as prevenidas, a police holding status for identification,
after marijuana was seized inside a Santiago del Estero police station. [@1] The
episode happened during visiting hours on Thursday 19 November 2026 at Comisaría
Comunitaria Nº 45. [@1] Info del Estero places the station in the city's barrio
Vinalar. [@2] Police said they saw detainee Kevin Acosta, 20, hand a wrapper to
another detainee, Facundo Roldán. [@1] Roldán refused to hand the packet over
and went back into the cells. [@1] Officers then reviewed security-camera
footage and reconstructed how the substance had entered. [@1]

The images showed Rita Acosta, 20, Kevin's sister, taking the wrapper from the
diaper of a baby she was carrying and giving it to the detainee. [@1] She had
come with her cousin, Dahiana Ibarra, 30. [@1] A nylon wrapper was seized and a
field test was positive for marijuana, with a total weight of 1.6 grams. [@1]
When the two detainees who had received the wrapper were briefly taken aside,
they said they would cause disturbances and damage once back in the cells,
according to the police report. [@1]

Prosecutor Celia Mussi ordered the first measures and, once the substance was
confirmed as prohibited, the narcomenudeo prosecutor Natalia Saavedra took over.
[@1] Saavedra ordered that Rita Acosta and Dahiana Ibarra be held as prevenidas
until that night, awaiting further judicial instructions. [@1]
