A Warangal Urban district task force team checked the Konark Express in the
early hours of Saturday 21 November 2026. [@2] Officers found the drug in three
bogies between Mahabubabad and Kazipet and seized a total of 6 kg of dry ganja.
[@2] The joint operation involved Warangal's enforcement assistant commissioner
and station house officers from Kazipet, Warangal Urban, Hasanparthy,
Hanumakonda and Khila Warangal police stations. [@2] Officials estimated the
value of the seized ganja at about 600,000 rupees. [@3] The people connected to
the bags were reported to have absconded. [@3]

A follow-up account described the find as 3 kg of dried ganja worth 600,000
rupees, seized while every bogie was checked between Mahabubabad and Kazipet.
[@1] Police then searched railway stations at Dornakal, Mahabubabad, Kesamudram,
Kazipet and Warangal for narcotics, on the platforms and in the surrounding
areas. [@1]

Smugglers pose as passengers, keep their bags in view but disclaim them, and get
off before their destination if they hear a check is ahead. [@1]
