A 46-year-old Spanish lorry driver was arrested at the Saint-Jean-de-Védas toll
booth near Montpellier on 12 November 2026. [@1] He was sentenced to three
years' firm imprisonment and ordered to remain in detention. [@1] He was also
ordered to pay a 783,550-euro customs fine. [@1]

French customs said he had brought 391.7 kg of cannabis resin into France. [@1]
The resin was packed into 149 parcels hidden in a cache in the double bottom of
his trailer. [@1] Customs valued the resin at 783,550 euros. [@1] Officers
noticed marks from a cutting disc, welding and putty on one corner of the lorry.
[@1] Behind 33 pallets of supermarket goods bound for Rome they found a 30 cm
trapdoor opened by a cable. [@1]

The road check was run by French customs at the toll booth on the night of 11 to
12 November 2026. [@1] Five lorry drivers were stopped for possession, transport
and importation of narcotics, with more than 500 kg of drugs seized in total.
[@1] The driver had been arrested eight years earlier with migrants in his
lorry. [@1]
