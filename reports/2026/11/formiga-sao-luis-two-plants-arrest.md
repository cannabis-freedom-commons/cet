Military Police of Minas Gerais ran an anti-drug-trafficking operation on
Saturday 14 November 2026 in the São Luís neighbourhood of Formiga. [@1] Teams
of the 63rd Military Police Battalion, linked to the 7th Military Police Region,
carried out the action. [@1] The operation followed anonymous reports pointing
to drug trafficking at an address on rua 2. [@1] Tático Móvel teams and the
Specialized Motorized Policing Group (GEPMOR) checked the indicated property.
[@1] Police arrested one person in flagrante delicto. [@1]

Officers seized two cultivated cannabis plants. [@1] They also seized a maconha
portion ready for consumption. [@1] Police seized R$732 in banknotes of various
denominations, described as proceeds of the illicit activity. [@1] The arrested
person and all seized material were taken to the Civil Police station for the
applicable legal procedures. [@1]
