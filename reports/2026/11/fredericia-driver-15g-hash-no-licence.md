In the early hours of Tuesday 24 November 2026, at 02.35 during the night, a
police patrol stopped a car on Sanddal Bakke in Fredericia Kommune in order to
carry out a check of the driver. [@1] The driver was a 29-year-old man who comes
from Fredericia Kommune. [@1] The patrol found that the man behind the wheel
appeared to be under the influence of euphoric substances, and that he was at
the same time driving without holding a driving licence. [@1]

He was also carrying two lumps of hashish with him, one weighing 14.61 grams and
the other weighing 0.58 grams, which together make 15.19 grams in total. [@1] He
has now been charged with three separate offences, namely driving while under
the influence, driving without a licence and possession of hashish. [@1] The
roadside check that found the hash began as a driver control. [@1]

South East Jutland Police recorded the roadside stop in its daily report, which
was presented on the morning of 24 November by Police Commissioner Arno Rindahl
Pedersen. [@1]
