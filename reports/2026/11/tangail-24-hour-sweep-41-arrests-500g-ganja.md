Tangail district police arrested 41 people in a special 24-hour operation across
the district. [@1] The arrests were made in the 24 hours before 26 November
2026. [@1] Seven of them were accused of involvement in the drug trade. [@1] The
operation was carried out under the instruction of the superintendent of police
and led by the officers in charge of every police station and police unit in the
district. [@1] The arrests covered preventive measures, regular cases, drug
recoveries, other recovery incidents and court warrants. [@1]

Officers seized 500 grams of ganja, 225 yaba tablets and 160 tapentadol tablets.
[@1] The ganja was the only cannabis among the three drugs seized. [@1] Police
said the seven people arrested over the drug recovery were drug traders. [@1]

The district police said such regular operations to eliminate drugs and arrest
offenders would continue. [@1] The arrested people were sent to the relevant
court after legal action was taken against them. [@2]
