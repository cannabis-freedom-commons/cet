Officers of the ADSU arrested a mason at Mont-Choisy on Tuesday 17 November.
[@1] The suspect is 24 years old, lives in Dagotiere and was held for possession
of drugs with intent to resell. [@1]

Officers found a plastic bag in his possession containing 300.30 grams of
cannabis. [@1] Officers then searched his home in Dagotiere but found nothing
incriminating. [@1]

A police photographer attended the scene to carry out the usual examinations.
[@1] The suspect was placed in police detention. [@1] The investigation was
continuing. [@1]
