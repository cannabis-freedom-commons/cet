On Wednesday 18 November 2026, gendarmes from Le Soler intervened at a private
home in the Pyrénées-Orientales department and found a large quantity of drugs.
[@1] A man at the home was arrested and placed in police custody after the
discovery. [@1] According to L'Indépendant, a characteristic smell of cannabis
is what led the officers to the house. [@1] The gendarmes of Le Soler were the
officers who carried out the intervention. [@1]

In the basement of the house the gendarmes found a plantation with 80 cannabis
plants in pots and 150 plants in the process of drying. [@1] The two batches
together amounted to 230 cannabis plants. [@1] The suspect had all the equipment
needed to grow cannabis. [@1] The gendarmes seized the whole lot of growing
equipment, including humidifiers, heaters and air extractors. [@2]

When questioned, the suspect was already known to the justice system, having
been placed under an electronic bracelet for similar offences committed a few
years earlier. [@2] He has since been presented to a magistrate and is jailed
pending trial. [@2]
