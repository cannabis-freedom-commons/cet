Carabinieri from the Barcellona Pozzo di Gotto station intervened on Saturday
evening in Piazza Convento and arrested a boy born in 2009 on suspicion of
possessing and dealing narcotics. [@1] Officers said they caught the boy as he
was in the act of handing over drugs. [@1] A second account of the same
intervention places it on Sunday evening and describes the boy as arrested on
suspicion of drug dealing. [@2]

During checks at the scene, officers recovered 15 packets containing marijuana.
[@1] The packets had been hidden inside a bush in the vegetation, apparently to
keep them from being found during checks. [@2] The boy had already been stopped
before during checks over possession of drugs. [@1] He was therefore already
known to the police. [@1]

After the arrest, the juvenile-justice measures provided for by law were applied
to him. [@1] He was transferred to the Juvenile Penal Institute in Catania and
placed at the disposal of the juvenile judicial authority. [@1] Further
inquiries were under way to reconstruct the episode in detail and to check for
any other responsibility. [@2] The episode drew attention to drug dealing in the
town's public areas and gathering places also frequented by young people. [@1]
