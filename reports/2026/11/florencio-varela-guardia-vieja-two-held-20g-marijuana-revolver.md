Officers of the Florencio Varela Patrol Command identified a 20-year-old and a
21-year-old on 12 November while patrolling the Guardia Vieja and Finochietto
area. [@1] The two young people, aged 20 and 21, were apprehended in Florencio
Varela in a drug-law and illegal-firearm investigation. [@1] The second account
identifies the pair as a 20-year-old man and a 21-year-old woman stopped in the
San Eduardo neighbourhood. [@2] Both young people were left at the disposal of
the intervening prosecutor's office. [@1]

Police found 11 nylon packets of marijuana totalling 20.55 grams among their
belongings. [@1] Officers also seized a Doberman .32-calibre revolver with seven
intact rounds in its drum and two Motorola mobile phones. [@1] The case was
classified as breach of Law 23.737, simple drug possession and unlawful carrying
of a civilian firearm. [@1]

At the request of UFI N° 1, the court ordered two searches of homes linked to
the detainees. [@1] The searches covered a property on Pedro Morán between
Finochietto and Calle 22 and another at Federico Rauch and General Simón. [@1]
The second account dates the two searches to 17 November and says they were led
by the 2nd-station tactical unit with GAD, 5th- and 6th-station units, UPPL and
GPM support. [@2] Residents were identified at both homes but no further items
of interest were found. [@1]
