Military Police officers arrested two young men, aged 19 and 21, on the night of
Sunday 15 November 2026 during an operation against drug trafficking in Novo
Mundo, Mato Grosso. [@1] The occurrence was registered around 18:30 on Avenida
Brilhante in northern Mato Grosso, where officers seized portions of marijuana,
skank, cocaine and coca paste. [@2] Police said a patrol stopped the two men
because their behaviour was suspicious and a personal search first turned up
seven portions of a substance analogous to marijuana. [@2]

Questioned, the men said they had recently moved into a property on Avenida
Brilhante, an address police said had already appeared in earlier intelligence
as a possible drug-trafficking support point. [@1] One suspect then indicated a
vacant lot behind the residence where officers found more drugs hidden under a
tile and among vegetation. [@2] The material included marijuana, skank (a potent
cannabis strain), cocaine and coca paste, all divided and prepared for sale,
according to the police record. [@2]

One of the young men told police he had travelled from Cuiabá at the behest of a
woman who, in his account, was responsible for a criminal faction's activity in
the municipality and for sending people to sell drugs. [@1] The police record
also mentioned a possible link between those involved and the Comando Vermelho
criminal faction. [@1] The two suspects were then taken to the Civil Police
station with all the seized material. [@3]
