A mobile court in Keshabpur upazila, Jashore, sentenced Ruhul Biswas, 65, to 15
days' simple imprisonment and fined him 100 taka. [@1] Department of Narcotics
Control officers had seized 50 grams of ganja, the local term for cannabis, from
him in Majidpur at about 3pm. [@1] The three raids took place on the afternoon
of Thursday 26 November 2026 in Khatiakhali, Majidpur and Aruya Biswaspara. [@1]
Officers detained three men in the raids. [@1] Executive Magistrate Anwar
Hossain ran the mobile court on the prosecution of Sub-Inspector Shahriar
Hossain of the department. [@1]

Sub-Inspector Shahriar said the separate raids seized 4,005 yaba tablets and 50
grams of ganja. [@1] Yaba is not a cannabis product, so the tablets are left out
of the cannabis figures. [@1] Four thousand of them were found in 20 blue zipped
packets in a box-bed drawer at a house in Khatiakhali. [@1] A regular case was
filed at Keshabpur police station under the Narcotics Control Act over that
raid. [@1] The remaining five tablets were seized in Aruya Biswaspara. [@1]
