Postojna police searching the home of a 22-year-old man in the vicinity of
Postojna found a space set aside for growing cannabis. [@1,3] The house search was
carried out on 19 November on the basis of a court order. [@1,2,3] The search order was issued by the District Court in Postojna. [@2]

In one of the residential rooms the man had set up a special tent, and five
cannabis seedlings were growing inside it. [@1,2,3] Officers also found a smaller
amount of marijuana at the property. [@1,2,3] The seized plants and substances were
sent for forensic analysis. [@1,2,3] Police said that once the results arrived they
would open a fast-track misdemeanor procedure against the man. [@1,2,3] The competent state prosecutor ordered the case to be handled as a misdemeanour, with the fast-track decision based on Article 33(1) of the Illicit Drugs Production and Trafficking Act. [@2]

During the visit officers also found that the 22-year-old was living in
unsuitable conditions. [@1,2] They said they would inform the social work centre
about his living conditions. [@1,2,3]
