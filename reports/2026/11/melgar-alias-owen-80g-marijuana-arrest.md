Police in Melgar, Tolima, arrested a 20-year-old man known as alias 'Owen' in
the Galán neighbourhood after he ran from a registration and background check.
[@1] The arrest was made by officers of the Melgar Police Station on suspicion
of manufacturing, trafficking, carrying or possessing firearms, accessories,
parts or ammunition, and of trafficking, manufacturing or carrying narcotics.
[@1] Colonel Ferney Martín Romero, commander of the Tolima Police Department,
said the procedure took place around 10:00 on a public road in Galán while
officers were checking people and vehicles. [@1] The officers were carrying out
registration, control and background checks across several sectors of the
municipality that morning. [@2] The operation was recorded at about 10:00 on the
morning of 18 November during street registration and control work. [@3]

During the personal search officers found a pistol-type traumatic weapon
believed to have been modified, a magazine, three cartridges for it and about 80
grams of marijuana, which were seized. [@1] The detainee and the seized items
were placed at the disposal of the Attorney General's Office on the weapons and
drug offences. [@4]

A check of police records showed an outstanding arrest warrant issued by the
Seventh Court for Execution of Penalties and Security Measures in Ibagué for
prisoner escape. [@1] He was attending virtual hearings at which his legal
situation would be decided. [@1] One account said about 20 officers took part in
a search and pursuit after an exchange of gunfire near a bridge in the Galán
neighbourhood. [@5]
