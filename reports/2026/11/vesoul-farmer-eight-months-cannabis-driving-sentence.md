On Thursday 12 November 2026 the judicial court in Vesoul tried a 23-year-old
farmer from Haute-Saône in his absence in a case centred on drugs, road offences
and an alleged trafficking charge. [@1] The case went back to a police control
on 10 June 2025, when officers stopped the 23-year-old as he was driving a
Partner van through Vesoul at about midnight. [@1] The prosecution said he had
been driving under the influence of cannabis and without a valid licence, and it
also pursued a drug-trafficking charge. [@1]

The Partner was a stolen vehicle, and the 2025 stop was carried out by the
police of Vesoul. [@1] The court convicted the farmer of the road offences but
acquitted him of the drug-trafficking counts. [@1] It sentenced him to eight
months of firm imprisonment for the road offences after the trafficking
acquittal. [@1]
