Four people were arrested on suspicion of drug trafficking and association for
trafficking after Federal Highway Police linked them to a Fiat Pulse that left
the road at km 7 of the BR-153 in Araporã, in the Triângulo Mineiro region of
Minas Gerais, on the morning of 25 November 2026. [@2] Around 10:55, officers
stopped a Nissan Versa near km 633 of the BR-365 in Uberlândia carrying four
people. [@2] One occupant drew attention by having soil on his or her clothes
and body, and the four gave contradictory accounts of the journey. [@2]

At about 08:05 the PRF was called to a car off the road at km 7 of the BR-153,
with no victims reported but suspicious packages near the vehicle. [@2] When
officers arrived, staff of the Way-153 concession were removing the Fiat Pulse.
[@2] In the roadside vegetation they found three large bags and a fourth volume
submerged in a flooded area. [@2] The bags held 139 tablets of maconha, the
Brazilian Portuguese word for marijuana. [@1] The PRF counted 137 large and two
small tablets, and put the haul at 137 kg. [@2] The seizure was reported as 137
kilograms of marijuana. [@3]

A search of the Nissan turned up a portion of marijuana and a Fiat car key in a
bag belonging to one of the occupants. [@2] The passenger confirmed the bag and
its contents were hers but said another occupant had handed her the key. [@1]
Officers tested the key and found it started the Fiat Pulse that had crashed
near where the tablets were found. [@1] All four occupants were formally told
they were under arrest on suspicion of drug trafficking and association for
trafficking. [@2] Both vehicles were removed to licensed impound yards, and the
detainees were taken to the Judicial Police station in Araguari with the drugs
and other seized material. [@2]
