Army, Navy, SSPC and state police officers seized weapons, drugs, vehicles and
tactical equipment during an operation in ejido El Alamito, in the municipality
of Hidalgo, Tamaulipas. [@2] The federal Security Cabinet lists the seizure
among its relevant actions of 17 November 2026. [@2] The Security Cabinet said
the Tamaulipas seizure formed part of coordinated detentions, searches and
seizures of firearms and drugs in six states. [@2] Milenio reported the same
seizure as weapons, drugs and 101 spike strips in an ejido in Hidalgo,
Tamaulipas. [@1]

Officers seized one kilogram of marijuana. [@2] The same intervention also
yielded one kilogram of methamphetamine, which is excluded from the cannabis
total. [@2] Milenio detailed six long guns, 16 magazines and 297 cartridges plus
four tactical vests among the items seized. [@1] The haul also included 101
spike strips known as ponchallantas. [@2] Authorities found two vehicles, one of
them with a theft report. [@2]
