Lugo's provincial prosecutor is seeking four to six years in prison for six
people accused of selling cocaine and cannabis resin in Chantada. [@1]
Prosecutors say the sales took place between 8 October 2021 and 8 April 2022.
[@1] Most of the accused are Colombian nationals with no significant criminal
record, except one, J.A.Q.M., for whom the prosecutor asks that an earlier
suspended sentence be revoked. [@1]

The accused are said to have made repeated small sales to different buyers,
mainly from a flat at number 1 Rúa Ramón y Cajal. [@1] The prosecution alleges
that two of the accused women used two underage girls, their respective daughter
and granddaughter, to distribute the drugs. [@1] Later searches of their homes
seized cash, high-purity cocaine and a precision scale. [@1]

On 2 February 2022 police stopped M.L.S. with more than 34 grams of high-purity
cocaine bought from two other accused, J.A.Q.M. and E.J.H.G., hidden in two
wrappers in a black sock inside her clothing. [@1] Those two men are accused of
supplying cocaine to the others and selling it to end users, and were carrying
more than 1.2 kilograms when they were arrested. [@1]

Alongside prison the prosecutor seeks fines of 11,000 to 200,000 euros depending
on the accused, disqualification from standing for office for the length of the
sentences, confiscation and destruction of the seized substances, and a ten-year
ban on foreign nationals re-entering Spain after their sentences. [@1] Several
of the accused were held in pre-trial detention and later released on different
dates. [@1]
