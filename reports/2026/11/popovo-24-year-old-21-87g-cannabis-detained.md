The Targovishte Regional Directorate of the Ministry of Internal Affairs (ОДМВР
– Търговище), which announced the case, said a young man had been detained at
the Popovo district police station for possessing a narcotic substance. [@1] The
man was 24 years old and came from Popovo itself; police checked him at the
town's railway station at about 18:20 on 22 November 2026. [@2] Officers
established that he was carrying 21.87 grams of what they described as a
high-risk narcotic substance, which reacted to cannabis in a field drug test.
[@1] He was taken to the Popovo district police station (РУ-Попово) in the same
town and held there. [@2]

The substance was seized by officers under the established procedure. [@1] His
detention at the station was ordered under Article 72, paragraph 1, item 1 of
the Ministry of Internal Affairs Act. [@2] Police also opened a fast police
proceeding against him under Article 354a, paragraph 3, item 1 of the Criminal
Code. [@1] The case remained at the police-investigation stage. [@2]
