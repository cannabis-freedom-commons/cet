Officers of the Larisa Crime Prevention and Suppression Team arrested a
22-year-old man shortly after midnight on Thursday, 19 November 2026, in an area
of Larisa, on suspicion of possessing a small quantity of cannabis. [@1] The
Hellenic Police, Greece's national police, said the 22-year-old was detained for
a suspected breach of the country's legislation on narcotics and that the arrest
took place in the early, after-midnight hours. [@1] The arresting officers came
from the Larisa unit of the Crime Prevention and Suppression Team, a police
formation known by its Greek acronym ΟΠΚΕ. [@1]

According to the Hellenic Police, those officers found the young man in
possession of a single nylon package containing 2.2 grams of raw, unprocessed
cannabis. [@1] The 2.2 grams of raw cannabis were confiscated by the officers,
and the young man's arrest followed. [@1] The package seized at the scene was
described as holding raw cannabis. [@1]
