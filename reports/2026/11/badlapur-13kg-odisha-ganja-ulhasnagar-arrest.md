Officers from the Ulhasnagar Crime Branch took into custody a man accused of
bringing ganja from the state of Odisha and storing it for sale in and around
Badlapur, in Thane district, Maharashtra. [@1] During questioning Vishal told
police he had obtained the ganja from his associate Rajat. [@1] Police said he
had hidden the stock in the store room of the housing society below his home.
[@1]

Officers seized 13 kg 497 g of ganja, which police valued at 3,37,425 rupees at
market rates. [@1]

The information reached police constable Rajendra Thorve through an informant on
9 November 2026. [@1] The search was carried out under the guidance of senior
police inspector Rajesh Gajjal, with assistant inspector Shirang Gosavi among
the team. [@1] The team searched Badlapur East and West, found him, took him
into custody and questioned him. [@1] A case was registered against him at
Badlapur West police station. [@1]
