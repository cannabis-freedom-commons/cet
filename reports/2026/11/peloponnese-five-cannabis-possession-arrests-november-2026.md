A police bulletin reported a barrage of police operations for drug cases across
the Peloponnese region in early November 2026. [@1] In Kiato, Corinthia, a
39-year-old man was arrested on the evening of 4 November 2026 for holding a
small quantity of cannabis, which officers seized. [@1] In Loutraki, two men
aged 23 and 27 were arrested the same evening for jointly holding a small
quantity of cannabis and a cannabis cigarette. [@1] In Sparta, Laconia, a
21-year-old was arrested that evening after a vehicle search found a small
quantity of cannabis and a cannabis cigarette. [@1] In Kalamata, Messenia, a
32-year-old foreigner was arrested on the evening of 3 November 2026 with
cannabis weighing 54.1 grams. [@1]

The Sikyona police station was assigned the preliminary investigation in the
Kiato case. [@1] The Loutraki case went to the Loutraki police station for
preliminary investigation. [@1] The Kalamata drug prosecution unit handled the
preliminary investigation in the 54.1-gram case. [@1] The Sparta crime detection
unit handled the preliminary investigation in the Laconia case. [@1]
