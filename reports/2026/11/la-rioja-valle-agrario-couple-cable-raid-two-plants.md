An adult couple, one man and one woman, was detained during a raid in an
investigation into the theft of cables belonging to the Telecom company. [@1,2,3] The two were a 25-year-old woman and a 30-year-old man. [@2]
Police identified the alleged culprits and carried out the raid at the home
linked to both suspects. [@1,2] The home was in the Virgen de Andacollo neighbourhood of La Rioja capital. [@2,3] The raid was carried out at about 07:00 on the Tuesday morning. [@3] Both suspects remained detained and at the
disposal of the judicial authorities. [@1,2]

Officers seized clothing that had allegedly been used at the time of the
offence. [@1,2] Officers also seized the motorcycle in which the pair had
travelled. [@1,2] The motorcycle was a Yamaha Crypton, and officers also seized a helmet, a handsaw, about 20 metres of cable sheathing and 15 metres of electrical cable. [@3] Officers found part of the stolen cable at the same place, while
another quantity had reportedly already been sold. [@1,2] Officers also seized
some grams of marijuana and cocaine, along with two cannabis plants. [@1,2,3] The Directorate of Narcotics said it would seek the corresponding measures before the Federal justice over this finding. [@2,3] Field tests by the Directorate of Narcotics returned a positive result for 18.2 grams of marijuana and 2.5 grams of cocaine, and officers confirmed the two plants were Cannabis sativa. [@2,3] Local officers, the Special Corps and the Technical Criminalistics Unit took part in the operation. [@3] A minor daughter of the two detainees was in the house; officers of the Directorate of Juvenile Affairs took charge of her and handed her to a direct relative. [@3]

Commissioner Ariel Rojas said the investigation began after a company
representative reported the theft of 20 to 30 metres of cable in the Valle
Agrario area, on Route 5. [@1,2,3] The investigation continues in order to establish
the circumstances of the robbery and the destination of the stolen material.
