An adult couple, one man and one woman, was detained during a raid in an
investigation into the theft of cables belonging to the Telecom company. [@1]
Police identified the alleged culprits and carried out the raid at the home
linked to both suspects. [@1] Both suspects remained detained and at the
disposal of the judicial authorities. [@1]

Officers seized clothing that had allegedly been used at the time of the
offence. [@1] Officers also seized the motorcycle in which the pair had
travelled. [@1] Officers found part of the stolen cable at the same place, while
another quantity had reportedly already been sold. [@1] Officers also seized
some grams of marijuana and cocaine, along with two cannabis plants. [@1]

Commissioner Ariel Rojas said the investigation began after a company
representative reported the theft of 20 to 30 metres of cable in the Valle
Agrario area, on Route 5. [@1] The investigation continues in order to establish
the circumstances of the robbery and the destination of the stolen material.
