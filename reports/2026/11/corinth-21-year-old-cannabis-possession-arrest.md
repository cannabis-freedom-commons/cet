Officers of the B team of the Corinthia Crime Prevention and Suppression Group,
known by its Greek initials OPKE, arrested a person in Corinth on the evening of
Saturday 14 November 2026. [@1] Police said the detainee was a 21-year-old Greek
national. [@1]

He was found carrying a small amount of cannabis during the OPKE check, and the
cannabis was seized. [@1] The cannabis was discovered in the course of the
police check in Corinth. [@1]

The preliminary investigation is being conducted by the Corinth crime
investigation subdirectorate. [@1]
