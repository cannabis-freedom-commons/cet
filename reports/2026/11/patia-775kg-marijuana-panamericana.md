Police and soldiers seized 775 kilograms of marijuana in two operations on the
Pan-American highway in Patía, southern Cauca, Colombia. [@1,3,4] Both seizures took place on the Mojarras–Popayán stretch of the Pan-American highway. [@3,4] The first find, at
kilometre 15 in the El Estrecho corregimiento, was about 250 kilograms of
marijuana inside an abandoned vehicle. [@1,3,4]

A second vehicle at kilometre 20 concealed 525 kilograms of marijuana in its
front and rear sections. [@1,3,4] Authorities said the cargo was being carried from
Caloto in northern Cauca to Ipiales in Nariño department. [@1,3,4] They valued the
haul at about 447 million 302 thousand pesos. [@1,3,4]

Both vehicles were immobilized and, together with the drugs, placed at the
disposal of the competent judicial authorities. [@1,3,4] The commander of the Cauca
police department, Colonel Gerson Bedoya Piraquive, highlighted the joint work
between police and the army in strengthening controls on the department's main
road corridors. [@2,3,4] The authorities said they would keep conducting joint
operations to curb the transport of illicit substances on Cauca's roads. [@2]
