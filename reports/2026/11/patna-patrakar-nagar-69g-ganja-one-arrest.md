Patrakar Nagar police in Patna arrested Kankarbagh resident Ranjit Paswan near
Metro Pillar No. 44 on the New Bypass on Thursday 19 November 2026. [@1] The
arrest followed intelligence collection and a raid against narcotics trafficking
in the Patrakar Nagar station area. [@2] Police said further lawful proceedings
were under way, leaving the case at the investigation stage. [@2]

Officers seized 69 grams of ganja from the accused in the raid. [@1] The station
in-charge said Paswan had previously gone to jail in a ganja-smuggling case.
[@1] Patna Police confirmed the same figures in its own statement, reporting one
accused held with 69 grams of ganja. [@2]

The press account presented the ganja case alongside an attempted-murder arrest
in Rajiv Nagar and a POCSO detention in Shrikishunapuri, describing them as
separate actions in different station areas. [@1] In Rajiv Nagar, police
arrested Roshan Kumar over an assault that seriously injured an auto driver in
Jaiprakash Nagar. [@1] In Shrikishunapuri, police apprehended a minor in a POCSO
case in Anandpuri and sent the minor to a reform home. [@1]
