Rawalpindi police arrested 15 alleged drug suppliers, including five women, on
23 November 2026 in a crackdown ordered by City Police Officer Dr Sardar Ghias
Gul. [@1]

Rawat Police held two female drug suppliers and recovered 2.3 kg of drugs. [@1]
Race Course Police arrested two alleged drug suppliers, including a woman, and
seized 3.1 kg of drugs. [@1] Saddar Bairooni Police arrested two suspects,
including a female supplier, and recovered 2.8 kg of drugs. [@1] Morgah Police
arrested a female drug supplier and recovered 1.4 kg of hashish. [@1] Waris Khan
Police arrested two suspects and recovered 3.2 kg of hashish. [@1] Jatli Police
arrested one supplier and seized 2.6 kg of drugs, while Dhamial Police recovered
2.1 kg after arresting another suspect. [@1] City Police arrested an alleged
supplier and recovered 1.7 kg of hashish. [@1] Civil Lines Police seized 1.6 kg,
Taxila Police 1.3 kg and Saddar Wah Police 1.2 kg after separate arrests. [@1]

An Urdu-language account of the same operation called the seized material
charas, or hashish, and put the total above 23 kilograms. [@3] Police registered
separate cases against the arrested suspects under relevant laws. [@1] CPO Dr
Sardar Ghias Gul said all possible measures were being taken to eliminate the
menace of drugs from society. [@2] He said the crackdown would continue to
protect citizens, particularly the younger generation, from narcotics. [@1]
