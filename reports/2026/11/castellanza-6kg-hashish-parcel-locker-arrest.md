Officers of the Guardia di Finanza's Busto Arsizio group arrested an Italian
citizen in Castellanza as he collected a parcel sent from Spain at an automated
locker. [@2] The parcel was addressed to a false name and held at an automated
pickup point in the town. [@1] Officers had found the shipment during checks on
goods moved by private couriers. [@1] They watched the pickup point and stopped
the man as he was about to take the parcel. [@1]

A search found more than six kilograms of hashish in blocks, about 3,000 euros
in cash and a knife with a knuckle-duster, which is illegal to carry. [@1]

He was arrested in the act on suspicion of possession and illicit trafficking of
narcotics and reported to the Busto Arsizio prosecutor's office for carrying
offensive objects. [@2] The Guardia di Finanza estimated the hashish would have
brought in more than 60,000 euros if sold at retail. [@1] Investigators said the
case had intercepted one of the new channels used to move drug shipments,
through automated parcel lockers. [@2]
