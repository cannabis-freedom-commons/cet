Guardia Civil officers arrested two people in Ceuta on 26 November 2026 during
an operation against facilitating irregular immigration. [@1] The operation
included searches of several homes in the El Sarchal and El Príncipe
neighbourhoods. [@2] Officers found ten irregular migrants in the homes who were
ready to leave for mainland Spain. [@2] The ten migrants were identified and the
procedures set out in extranjería law were activated. [@1]

During the searches officers seized 2.5 kg of a substance that, pending
confirmation, was believed to be hashish resin. [@1] Ceuta Ahora described the
same haul as 2,500 grams of hashish resin. [@2] El Faro de Ceuta reported that
the hashish was found in the house in El Príncipe, along with psychotropic
pills. [@3] The Guardia Civil said it had identified several properties used as
hiding places and logistical support for the clandestine transfer of migrants to
the Peninsula. [@1]

Both detainees were held on suspicion of offences against the rights of foreign
citizens and against public health. [@1] They were to be placed at the disposal
of the Ceuta investigating court with the Guardia Civil's case papers. [@1] El
Faro de Ceuta reported that other migrants escaped before officers arrived. [@3]
That outlet said the two detainees would be brought before a court on Friday.
[@3] The Guardia Civil released video footage of the entries and searches in the
two houses. [@3]
