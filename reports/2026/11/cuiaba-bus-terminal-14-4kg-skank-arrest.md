Gefron, a state special border group, arrested a man with prior criminal records
at the Cuiabá bus terminal on the night of Monday 16 November 2026 with about
14.4 kg of skank, a potent cannabis variety known as supermaconha. [@1] The man
had previous records for drug trafficking, possessing or carrying a restricted
firearm, robbery and theft. [@1]

Officers acted on an anonymous tip to the Disque-Denúncia line that a passenger
on an intermunicipal bus travelling from Mirassol d'Oeste to Cuiabá was carrying
drugs, then went to the terminal and stopped the suspect. [@1] During the
baggage check they found three bags holding 28 wrappers of a substance
resembling skank, weighing about 14.4 kg in total. [@1] Questioned, the man said
he would receive R$ 2,000 for transporting the drugs. [@1]

He was arrested in flagrante and taken with the seized drugs to the Central de
Flagrantes in Cuiabá for the case to be registered. [@1] Gefron estimated the
seizure had cost criminal gangs R$ 43,200. [@1]
