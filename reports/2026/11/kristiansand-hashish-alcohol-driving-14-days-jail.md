A man in his 30s was sentenced to 14 days in prison for driving a car in
Kristiansand while impaired by both hashish and alcohol. [@1] He was also
ordered to pay a fine of 61,000 kroner and lost his driving licence for two
years. [@1]

The driving took place in Møllevannsveien in Kristiansand on the evening of 18
February this year. [@1] According to the judgment, he was impaired by alcohol
and by THC, the active substance in hashish, with an impairment equivalent to
more than 1.2 per mille. [@1] The court wrote that the THC impairment alone was
well above the Road Traffic Act's fixed limit corresponding to 1.2 per mille.
[@1] He also had a blood-alcohol level of 0.61 per mille. [@1]

The judgment referred to road safety and the potential for harm from impaired
driving. [@1] The court said the starting point was 18 days' imprisonment, but
his unconditional confession earned a sentencing discount so the sentence was
set at 14 days. [@1] The court noted that the fine for this type of driving
should as a starting point be set at one-and-a-half times gross monthly income.
[@1] The man said he earns around 27,000 kroner net per month, corresponding to
a gross income of just over 40,000 kroner. [@1]
