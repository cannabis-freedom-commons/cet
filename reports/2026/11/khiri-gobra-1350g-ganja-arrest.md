Khiri police arrested Ajay Kol in Prayagraj district. [@1] Kol, the son of
Hinchhalal, is about 45 years old and lives in Gobra village under Khiri police
station. [@1] Sub-Inspector Manoj Kumar and his team carried out the action on
13 November 2026 and arrested Gobra resident Ajay Kol, son of Hinchhalal, during
it. [@2] Khiri station house officer Vir Pratap Singh said the accused was
arrested with ganja in a raid conducted on an informant's tip. [@2] Police
registered case number 202/2026 under NDPS Act section 8/20 at Khiri police
station and sent him to jail after further legal proceedings. [@1] Police said
Kol already faced case number 160/2021 under the same section at Khiri in 2021.
[@1] Police said this is now his second case under the Act at Khiri. [@2]

A search of Kol yielded 1,350 grams of ganja and 150 rupees described as
proceeds from sales. [@2] The arrest and seizure team included Sub-Inspector
Manoj Kumar, constables Akash Pandey, Ashish Kumar, Manoj Kumar Yadav, Pankaj
Kumar Pratham, Deepak Kumar and Ankit Singh, along with woman constable Palak
Mishra. [@1]
