Carabineros from the Diumén post, part of Río Bueno's Fourth Commissary,
detained six people in two anti-drug procedures on the Ruta T-85. [@1,3,4] The six detainees were four men and two women, all of Chilean nationality. [@4] The first
check took place at about 01:30 at the Cachillahue junction, where officers
found 1 kg 59 g of marijuana in a vehicle and detained its two occupants. [@1,3]
An hour later in the Pindaco sector, four occupants of a car were arrested after
officers found 1 kg 10 g of the same substance. [@1,3] One of the detainees, a
17-year-old girl, handed over a revolver and three unspent .38-calibre rounds
and was also detained for illegal possession of a firearm. [@1,3] The prosecution ordered the Carabineros criminalistics laboratory, Labocar, to examine the seized firearm and ammunition to establish whether the weapon was capable of firing. [@4] Specialist OS7
officers confirmed by field test that the marijuana seized came to 2 kg 69 g.
[@1,3] The flagrancy unit of the Southern Austral Macrozona of the prosecution service directed the investigative steps. [@4] On the duty prosecutor's instruction the six accused were taken to a
detention-control hearing that morning, and the seizure of the vehicles, the
weapon and the ammunition was ordered. [@1,3] The six detainees were to be placed at the disposal of the Juzgado de Letras y Garantía de Río Bueno during that day. [@4] The Río Bueno commissioner, Major
Juan Cáceres, said Carabineros would keep reinforcing preventive patrols and
operations on the commune's roads. [@1]

The local outlet Noticias Río Bueno reported that two people were detained on
the Ruta T-85 at Cachillahue and four in Pindaco, for offences under the Ley de
Drogas and the Ley de Control de Armas. [@2] The checks were made during
preventive patrols and each vehicle carried a kilo and a fraction, with the
firearm found in one of them. [@2] The Public Prosecutor's Office called in the
OS7 unit from Valdivia to carry out the field test on the drug. [@2] Cáceres
said Carabineros would work day and night to stop people trafficking illicit
substances in the commune. [@2]
