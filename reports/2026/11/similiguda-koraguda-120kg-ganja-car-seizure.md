Similiguda police, while patrolling on Friday night, seized 120 kg of ganja from
a car without a number plate at Koraguda junction. [@1] Police noticed a car
that appeared to have met with an accident and found 17 ganja packets on
searching it. [@1]

Police took the car and the ganja to the station, where weighing showed 120
kilos. [@1] Koraput Police said its Semiliguda station detected an NDPS case and
seized 120 kg of contraband ganja along with one Maruti Suzuki Brezza car. [@2]
No one had been arrested in connection with the seizure. [@1]

Police said they were investigating where the ganja was being transported from
and where it was being taken. [@1] Police said some valuable documents were
found in the car. [@1] Searches were under way for the smugglers. [@1]
