The Sabari Express, running from Secunderabad to Thiruvananthapuram, reached
Salem on 20 November 2026. [@1,4] The checks covered four trains: the Secundrabad-Thiruvananthapuram Express, the New Delhi-Thiruvananthapuram Express, the Dibrugarh-Kanyakumari Express and the Patna-Ernakulam Express. [@4] Salem railway police's narcotics prevention
section searched its general compartment. [@1] Officers Vijayakumar and
Prabhakaran of the railway narcotics prevention section carried out the check.
[@3] The train was passing through Salem that morning. [@2] Two bags were lying
unclaimed in the luggage space of the rear unreserved coach. [@1] One was a
trolley bag containing 14 kg of ganja. [@1,4] Officers also seized 1.9 kg of ganja chocolates from the bags. [@4] A second bag lying in the same place
held 13 kg of gutka, a chewing-tobacco product. [@3]

Police seized both bags and handed the ganja and gutka to Salem Junction railway
police. [@3] Salem Junction railway police registered a case and are
investigating who carried the bags on the train, where they came from and who
they were meant for. [@3,4]

Railway police have been checking trains passing through Salem and seizing ganja
and tobacco products carried on them. [@2] The 13 kg of gutka is not cannabis
and is not counted in the cannabis figure here. [@3]
