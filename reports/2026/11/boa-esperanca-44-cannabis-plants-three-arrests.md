Military Police carried out Operação Batida Policial on the night of Friday 13
November 2026 in Boa Esperança, arresting three adults and apprehending one
adolescent. [@1] The operation involved teams from the 172nd Company and the
207th Tactical Mobile Company. [@1] Police said the effort aimed to repress
offences and reinforce policing in parts of the town identified as gathering
points for offenders. [@1] During the checks officers recorded cases of drug
trafficking, drug use and consumption, and enforcement of court orders. [@1] Two
people were detained under open arrest warrants. [@1]

Officers collected 44 cannabis plants: 26 in small cups, 14 in large cups, three
in pots and one in a large vase, each container holding one plant. [@1] Police
also seized a container with a dried plant, two containers of cannabis seeds, a
portion of pressed marijuana, loose marijuana leaves and a packet of cocaine.
[@1]

In traffic checks during the same night officers removed six motorcycles and
issued 13 traffic citations. [@1] Those detained were taken to the Civil Police
station with the seized materials, and police did not disclose the
neighbourhoods where the stops happened or the ages of those involved. [@1]
