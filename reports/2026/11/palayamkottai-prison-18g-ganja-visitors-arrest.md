Pichaipandi, 27, and Joshua Devapriyan, 25, both from Malumichampatti in
Coimbatore, and Gopi, from Kanyakumari, came to Palayamkottai Central Prison on
20 November 2026 to visit a life convict. [@2] The prisoner, Gautham, a life
convict earlier lodged in Coimbatore prison, had been shifted to Palayamkottai a
few months before on security grounds. [@3]

While the three waited in the visitors' area, a police personnel of the
Manimutharu battalion searched them and found ganja concealed in the folded
sleeve of Joshua Devapriyan's shirt. [@4] Officers seized 18 grams of ganja and
arrested all three men. [@1]

Prison officer Muniyandi held an inquiry and handed all three men over to
Perumalpuram police. [@2] Perumalpuram police registered a case against the
three men under three sections and are investigating. [@2] Police said further
investigation was under way to establish how the ganja was procured and whether
it was intended to be handed to the convict. [@3]
