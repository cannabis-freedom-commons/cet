The Bandung city prosecutor's office destroyed evidence from hundreds of
criminal cases with final court decisions on 26 November 2026. [@1] The
destruction took place in the yard of the prosecutor's office on Jalan Jakarta
No. 42–44 in Bandung. [@1] Prosecutor section heads attended, with
representatives of the Bandung city police, the Bandung district court and the
city health office as witnesses. [@1]

The evidence from 99 narcotics cases included 1,822.74 grams of ganja
(cannabis), 283.07 grams of synthetic tobacco and 3,095 grams of sabu-sabu
(crystal methamphetamine). [@1] The ganja and sabu-sabu were burned, while the
other evidence was shredded, cut or crushed until unusable. [@2] Officers also
destroyed 97 mobile phones, 54 scales and 249 other items such as plastic bags,
duct tape, bongs, bags, foam and straws. [@1]

Evidence from 77 cases under the 1951 Emergency Law, including sharp weapons,
keys and clothing, was destroyed in the same exercise. [@1] Banned medicines
such as tramadol, heximer and dextro from 34 Health Law cases were also
destroyed. [@1] Corruption-case exhibits destroyed included a Mercedes-Benz car
key, two wallets, a 2011 MacBook Air laptop and hard disks. [@1] Head of the
evidence and asset recovery section Arie Prasetyo said the aim was to stop the
evidence being misused and to give legal certainty. [@1] Bandung chief
prosecutor Abun Hasbulloh Syambas said the destruction was a routine execution
of final court decisions and urged residents to report drug distribution and
other crimes. [@3]
