Eight suspected drug dealers and consumers, including an 18-year-old girl, were
arrested by the Jigawa State Police Command during a crackdown. [@1] The six men
and two women were arrested in operations across Jigawa State. [@2] The teenager
was Nusaiba Ibrahim, from Sandamu in Katsina State, alongside 20-year-old
Firdausi Sani. [@1] Six men aged between 20 and 25 were also arrested during the
intelligence-led operations. [@1] The men were named as Garba Sabo, Kamal
Ibrahim, Muhammad Ibrahim, Muhammad Nura, Muhammad Bako and Habu Usman, also
known as Makwa. [@1]

The police said 8,739 assorted drug-related substances, including dry leaves
suspected to be Indian hemp, were recovered during the operation. [@2] The
cannabis recovered included three and a half blocks of suspected Indian hemp and
1,199 wraps of marijuana. [@1] Police listed 4,035 pieces of Exol, 1,384 pieces
of D5, 634 capsules of pregabalin, 896 capsules of tramadol and 248 tablets of
diazepam among the recovered substances. [@1] Officers also seized whisky,
shisha, syrup, Ermo, Angur, snuff and Daga. [@1]

Police Public Relations Officer Shiisu Lawan Adam said the operation was part of
efforts to dismantle drug distribution networks and curb criminal activities
associated with substance abuse. [@1] Commissioner of Police Tijjani Murtala
said the command would sustain the crackdown on drug trafficking and warned
suspected dealers and their collaborators to desist from the illegal trade. [@1]
The police did not say whether the eight suspects had been charged to court, and
they remained suspects. [@3]
