Operativo Otomí has been deployed across the State of Mexico since 23 September
2026 as a joint federal-state security strategy. [@1] The authorities reported
2,778 people detained for probable participation in various offences during the
operation's first 50 days. [@1] The joint operation brought together the
Secretaría de Seguridad del Estado de México (SSEM), the Secretaría de la
Defensa Nacional (Defensa), the Guardia Nacional and the Fiscalía General de
Justicia del Estado de México (FGJEM). [@1] El Universal Edomex described the
same inter-institutional coordination between SSEM, Defensa, the Guardia
Nacional and the FGJEM for the period from 23 September to 12 November. [@2]

The reported results cover the period from 23 September to 12 November 2026.
[@1] Of those detained, 1,314 were held for offences against health, alongside
216 for concealment by receiving, 190 for carrying weapons, 140 for robbery and
92 for attacks on means of communication. [@1] Officers seized 65 short arms and
17 long arms, for a reported total of 82 firearms. [@1] Officers also seized
111.6 kilograms of green and dry herb with characteristics similar to marijuana.
[@1]

In the same operation officers also seized 6.9 kilograms of a granulated
substance with characteristics similar to crystal and 5.4 kilograms of white
powder with characteristics apparent to cocaine, which are not counted in the
cannabis figure. [@1] As part of the ministerial and operational actions,
officers executed 557 arrest warrants, carried out 176 technical investigation
orders and conducted 243 inspections. [@1] A second outlet gave the same seizure
balance of 65 short arms and 17 long arms and 111.6 kilograms of herb with
characteristics similar to marijuana. [@3] The operation period from 23
September to 12 November included 1,314 detentions for offences against health.
[@3]
