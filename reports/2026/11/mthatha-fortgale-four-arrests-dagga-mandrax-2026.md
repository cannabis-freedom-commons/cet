Four suspects, aged between 24 and 30, were arrested in Mthatha. [@1] The
arrests were made in Fortgale on Friday 20 November during an
intelligence-driven operation involving the Mthatha K9 and Mounted units, the
Flying Squad and the KuGompo K9 Unit. [@1]

Eastern Cape police said provincial and Mthatha crime intelligence members,
together with the other units, executed a search warrant at premises in Nduli
Crescent, Fortgale at 06:55. [@1] During the search police confiscated 588
mandrax tablets, as well as tik and dagga, the South African term for cannabis,
worth more than R118,000 in total. [@1] Four laptops suspected to be stolen and
an undisclosed amount of cash were also seized. [@1]

The four suspects were charged with possession and dealing in mandrax, tik and
dagga, as well as possession of suspected stolen property. [@1] The four
suspects remained in custody and were due to appear in the Mthatha Magistrate's
Court on Monday 23 November. [@1] Acting provincial police commissioner Major
General Ngangema Xakavu commended the members involved for their collaboration
and dedication in combating drug-related crime. [@1] He said intelligence-driven
operations would continue to target those involved in the distribution and sale
of illegal substances. [@1]
