A suspected drug dealer and asylum seeker with links to Preston is fighting
extradition to Albania. [@1] Gezim Cela applied for bail while he battles being
sent home to serve a ten-year prison sentence imposed in December 2018 for
exporting 50 kg of cannabis from Albania to Kosovo. [@1]

His legal team said Cela, who is claiming asylum in the UK, had kept out of
trouble since arriving in the country eight years earlier. [@1] The High Court
heard he has family in Britain and is in a five-year relationship with a
UK-based partner. [@1] His lawyers said he would accept bail conditions
including an electronic tag, a nightly curfew and regular reporting to a police
station. [@1] Three friends offered to post a £24,000 surety. [@1]

Mr Justice Fordham refused bail, saying the overall picture of risk was very
clear cut and rested on the assessed risk of absconding. [@1] The judge said the
proposed bail conditions, and any other conditions he could impose, did not
allay his concerns that Cela would fail to surrender. [@1] He said the Albanian
authorities had described Cela as a ‘fugitive from justice’. [@1] Evidence was
given that in Albania Cela had received a remand order banning him from leaving
the country. [@1] Cela said he was not aware of the prohibition and believed the
charges against him had been dropped for lack of evidence. [@1] The judge said
there was evidence of Cela's links to London, Preston and Peterborough and to a
wide network of associates in the UK. [@1] He asked how the three friends could
afford £9,000, £7,000 and £8,000 and from what sources. [@1]

Before his arrest for extradition in 2025 the National Crime Agency had been on
his trail for some time. [@1] At his arrest he had an unexplained £800 in cash,
no bank cards and no ID or membership cards, and no passport or identity
document had been surrendered or seized. [@1] In 2022 he was arrested in the UK
in a car with a knife and 520 wraps of cocaine, and gave false details using a
bank card in someone else's name. [@1]
