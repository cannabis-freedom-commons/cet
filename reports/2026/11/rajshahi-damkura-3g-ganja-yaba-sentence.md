An executive magistrate-led mobile court carried out an anti-drug drive in the
Damkura thana area of Rajshahi with the cooperation of Damkura thana police.
[@1] During the operation on 22 November 2026 officers detained Md Belal Uddin,
aged 29 and son of Md Abdul Mannan, of Jotrabon village under Damkura thana.
[@1] He was held on the allegation of keeping two yaba tablets and three grams
of ganja, the local term for cannabis. [@1]

Officers listed the items in his possession as two yaba tablets and three grams
of ganja. [@1] For keeping the yaba tablets the mobile court sentenced him to
one year's rigorous imprisonment and fined him 2,000 Taka. [@1] For keeping the
ganja the same court sentenced him to six months' rigorous imprisonment and
fined him another 2,000 Taka. [@1] The two penalties were reported as separate
sentences for the yaba and ganja offences. [@1]

Rajshahi Metropolitan Police said it conducts regular anti-drug drives to keep
the metropolitan city drug-free and safe and that such drives to prevent and
suppress drug offences will continue. [@1]
