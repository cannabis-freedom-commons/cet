Tamil Nadu police said 15,478 people connected to 10,559 cases were arrested in
a six-month anti-drug drive. [@2] Government data records 10,559 cases under the
NDPS Act between May 1 and November 15, 2026, against 6,057 in the same period
in 2025. [@4]

Tamil Nadu police seized 18,190 kg of ganja in six months. [@1] The seizure
total covers May 1 to November 15. [@2] In the same period police seized 485,000
drug tablets, against 145,315 tablets in the corresponding period, up 234 per
cent. [@3] Police linked 1,400 kg of ganja to an attempted smuggling to Sri
Lanka and said assets worth Rs 2.63 crore linked to smuggling were frozen. [@2]

Director General of Police Mahesh Kumar Agarwal presented the figures on
November 18 while launching an online e-CSR complaint facility. [@3] The DGP
said cases rose 74 per cent, ganja seizures 25 per cent, offender arrests 73 per
cent and freezing of accused bank accounts 64 per cent compared with the
corresponding period. [@3] Police said an Anti-Narcotics Task Force was set up
because the state's coastline could be used as a transit point, to work toward a
drug-free Tamil Nadu. [@3]
