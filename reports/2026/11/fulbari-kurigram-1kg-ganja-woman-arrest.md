In a special anti-drug operation in Fulbari of Kurigram district, thana police
detained 40-year-old Jamena Begum after finding one kilogram of ganja in her
possession. [@1] The detention took place in front of the upazila sadar hospital
in Fulbari at about 3:30 in the afternoon on Sunday, 22 November 2026. [@1] The
detained woman was identified as Jamena Begum, the 40-year-old wife of Abdur
Rouf from Bera Melia village, which falls under Nawabganj police station in
Dinajpur district. [@1]

Police said the operation was launched on the basis of secret information, and
officers recovered one kilogram of ganja from the woman's possession during the
search. [@1]

Fulbari police station's officer-in-charge, Kamal Hossain, confirmed the
incident and the recovery of the ganja from the detained woman. [@1] He said
police filed a case against the detained woman at Fulbari police station under
the Narcotics Control Act in connection with the seized one kilogram of ganja.
[@1] Police later sent the detained woman to court. [@1]
