Konak İlçe Emniyet Müdürlüğü Narko Alan ekipleri targeted an address in Konak
over suspected drug trafficking and unlicensed weapons and raided it. [@1]
Officers searched the home of suspect B.K. and detained him during the
operation. [@2] The suspect was identified as 24-year-old B.K. [@2]

The search found 8.95 grams of skunk in 10 pieces and one cannabis plant root
alongside 12.23 grams of cocaine in 9 pieces and two pills. [@1] Officers also
seized two unlicensed pistols and nine cartridges, two of them loaded in a
magazine. [@1] Police also found 9,750 Turkish lira, 40 US dollars, 45 euros and
10 Canadian dollars assessed as proceeds of drug trafficking. [@1]

Judicial proceedings were started against B.K. for drug trafficking and
unlicensed possession of weapons, and his police procedures were continuing.
[@1]
