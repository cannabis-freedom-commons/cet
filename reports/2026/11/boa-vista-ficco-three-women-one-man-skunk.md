FICCO officers arrested three women and one man in Boa Vista on Tuesday 17
November 2026. [@2] The Integrated Force to Combat Organised Crime in Roraima
brings together the Federal, Civil, Military and Penal police and the state
public security secretariat. [@2] Police said the group was investigated for
supplying drugs to others and for using properties to store them. [@1]

Officers identified a house used as a drug depot and watched it, and one of the
women under investigation left, went to a parking lot and got into a car with a
couple inside. [@2] When officers approached, the driver tried to flee and hit a
police vehicle, and searches of the car found about 1 kg of skunk and portions
of cocaine. [@2] At the monitored house police found another suspect and seized
the rest of the drugs, a firearm, ammunition, two precision scales and money.
[@1]

In total FICCO seized 3.31 kg of skunk, 3.20 kg of cocaine powder and 3.12 kg of
crack, 9.63 kg of drugs altogether. [@2] Officers also seized a .38 revolver
with its serial number erased, six rounds, two precision scales and about 18,600
reais in cash. [@2]

The four suspects were taken to the Federal Police regional superintendency in
Roraima and booked in flagrante for drug trafficking, association for
trafficking, illegal possession of a firearm with an erased serial number and
damage to property. [@2] Police said the scheme was made up mainly of women, who
did the transport and the deliveries. [@2]
