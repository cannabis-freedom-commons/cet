Four men were detained by officers of the Guardia Civil Municipal of Soledad de
Graciano Sánchez during security patrols over alleged health offences. [@1] Two
of them were detained in separate actions in the Quintas de la Hacienda
neighbourhood and the municipal centre for possessing a bag of suspected
marijuana. [@1]

The other two were detained in the Fracción Rivera and Rinconada del Palmar
neighbourhoods for possessing doses of suspected methamphetamine. [@1] After
officers informed the detainees of the reason for their detention, they were
transferred to the State Prosecutor's Office with the seized substances. [@1]
