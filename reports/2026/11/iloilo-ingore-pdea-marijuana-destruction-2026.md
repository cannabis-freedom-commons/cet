The Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency destroyed seized dangerous drugs and
expired regulated medicines at a thermal facility in Barangay Ingore, La Paz, on
19 November 2026. [@1] The destroyed items included 19.9058 grams of marijuana,
which PDEA valued at PHP2,388.70. [@2] Assistant Secretary Renato Gumban, PDEA
deputy director general for operations, led the incineration at the Panay Energy
Development Corp. thermal facility in Barangay Ingore, La Paz. [@2]

Of the marijuana, 10.6303 grams came from Iloilo City and 9.2755 grams from
Bacolod City. [@2] Bacolod City accounted for 9.2755 grams of marijuana in the
same disposal. [@2] The seized evidence was turned over by 11 Regional Trial
Court branches in Iloilo City, three branches in Barotac Viejo, Iloilo, and five
branches in Bacolod City. [@2] Turnovers from Philippine National Police
regional forensic units in Western Visayas and the Negros Island Region were
also included. [@2]

The same disposal burned 21,789.7536 grams of methamphetamine hydrochloride, or
shabu, valued at PHP148,170,324.48, and expired regulated medicines valued at
PHP215,172.30, which are not counted as cannabis. [@2] Daily Tribune reported
that the thermal destruction also included marijuana and put the combined value
of the incinerated contraband at PHP148.39 million. [@3] Daily Guardian put the
combined value of the destroyed dangerous drugs and expired medicines at
approximately PHP148.387 million. [@1]
