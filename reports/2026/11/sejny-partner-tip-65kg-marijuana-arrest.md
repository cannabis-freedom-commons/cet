Sejny police detained a 37-year-old man after his partner called them to their
home over a quarrel. [@1] Officers had been called to a domestic intervention
after a report that the partner was causing trouble at home. [@1] The woman told
officers that the man did nothing and spent his time drinking alcohol and
smoking marijuana. [@1] When asked where her partner would get drugs, she
answered without hesitation that he grew them himself on the upper floor of the
building. [@1] The upset woman then led the officers upstairs, with the
37-year-old going along, and inside one room stems with leaves were drying on
hung strings. [@1]

Officers secured a total of almost 6.5 kilograms of plants and dried material,
including 30 drying branches weighing over 4.5 kilograms net, 6 jars holding
over 700 grams and a can holding over 1 kilogram hidden in a freezer. [@1] A
preliminary narcotest indicated that the secured dried material was marijuana.
[@1]

The man first admitted everything, telling officers that he had bought the seeds
online and grew the crop only for his own use. [@1] A few minutes later he
changed his account and tried to convince the officers that the plants belonged
to his partner. [@1] He was detained and charged with possession of a
significant quantity of narcotic drugs. [@1] A court ordered him held in custody
for one month, and he faces up to 10 years of imprisonment under the applicable
law. [@1]
