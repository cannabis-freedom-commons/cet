Veracruz state police and partner agencies detained 19 people in coordinated
operations on Tuesday and Wednesday before a bulletin dated 19 November 2026.
[@1] Three of the detentions came during searches in Tuxpan, Poza Rica and
Tierra Blanca carried out with the state prosecutor's office (FGE). [@1] The SSP
led the operation with the federal and state prosecutors' offices, the Navy, the
Defence Ministry, the National Guard and municipal police. [@2]

In those three searches officers seized 460 grams (0.46 kg) of green herb they
said had the characteristics of marijuana, plus three separate doses of the same
herb. [@1] A van was also seized containing green herb described as resembling
marijuana, along with tyre puncturers, grab rings, a retractable baton and two
motorcycles not reported stolen. [@2] The same searches produced one handmade
firearm, two replicas, live cartridges, mobile phones, computers, tablets,
drones, a tactical vest and two motorcycles. [@1]

Elsewhere in the operation, 11 people were arrested on suspicion of health
crimes, with 100 doses and three small bags of a crystal-like substance seized.
[@2] A man suspected of belonging to a criminal cell was arrested in Córdoba
with eight doses of a crystal-like substance and a motorcycle. [@1] In Medellín,
officers supporting an FGR search seized eight casino-style slot machines. [@2]
The detainees and seized items were handed to the competent authorities. [@1]
