Officers in Corfu arrested a 31-year-old Greek man in the early morning hours of
Thursday 12 November 2026 for drug trafficking. [@1] The arrest was made by
officers of the Corfu Narcotics Prosecution Department of the Subdirectorate for
Prosecution and Investigation of Crimes, after they located the man and searched
his home in the presence of a judicial officer. [@1]

During the home search, officers found and seized 873 grams of raw cannabis.
[@2] In the same search, officers also seized 150 grams of cocaine, which is not
counted in the cannabis figure. [@3] Officers also seized a precision electronic
scale and a preparation used to cut cocaine with other substances, which police
linked to the preparation and trafficking of the drugs. [@2]

A felony case file for drug trafficking was formed against the 31-year-old. [@2]
He was taken to the Corfu Misdemeanour Prosecutor and then referred to the
investigating judge. [@2] After his plea before the investigating judge, his
pretrial detention was ordered. [@1]
