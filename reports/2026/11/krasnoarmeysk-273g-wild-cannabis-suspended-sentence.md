The Krasnoarmeysk inter-district prosecutor's office supported the prosecution
of a 34-year-old local man. [@2] The case was heard in Krasnoarmeysk, where the
man lives. [@2] A court in the town found him guilty of acquiring and storing a
narcotic drug without intent to sell, in a large amount, under part 2 of article
228 of the Russian Criminal Code. [@2]

Between 4 and 5 October 2026 he gathered the tops of wild-growing cannabis on a
vacant lot behind one of Krasnoarmeysk's microdistricts. [@2] Police seized
273.9 grams of cannabis from him when they detained him. [@1] He had kept the
drug at home until it was seized. [@2] The prosecutor's office said the 273.9
grams is a large amount. [@2]

The court sentenced him to three years' imprisonment suspended, with a
three-year probation period. [@2] At the prosecutor's request the court issued a
private ruling to the administration of the Krasnoarmeysky municipal district to
destroy the patches where wild drug-containing plants grow. [@2]
