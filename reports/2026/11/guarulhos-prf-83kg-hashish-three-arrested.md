Federal Highway Police officers arrested three people during an operation on the
Presidente Dutra highway (BR-116) in the Guarulhos region on the night of
Thursday 19 November 2026. [@1] Officers intercepted the suspect truck and
followed it to its destination carrier, where the company and the driver
cooperated with a detailed inspection of the goods. [@1] While officers were
processing the cargo, a man arrived in a utility vehicle demanding to collect
the parcel and was detained. [@1] Based on information from that stop, officers
located and arrested the two other members of the group in a bar nearby. [@1]
The case was taken to the 73rd Civil Police precinct. [@1]

Officers seized 82.95 kilos of hashish. [@1] The truck had come from Mato Grosso
do Sul after officers received intelligence pointing to its cargo. [@1] In the
warehouse, sniffer dogs flagged narcotics inside nine bales of plastic bags.
[@1] Inside officers found 82 hidden packages, each containing ten tablets of
the drug. [@1]

The PRF describes intelligence work and sniffer dogs as strategically important
in intercepting interstate drug loads before they reach urban distribution
centres. [@1]
