A large cannabis farm was uncovered in Darwen following a police raid. [@1]
Officers from Lancashire Police's Urban Task Force entered a property on Bank
Street. [@1] They discovered 270 cannabis plants of various sizes growing in
multiple rooms. [@1] Lancashire Police described the site as a cannabis
cultivation operation. [@3] The entire setup was dismantled and the plants were
seized. [@1]

A police spokesperson said: "Your help is invaluable in disrupting criminals,
reducing the antisocial behaviour and risks that come with drug cultivation, and
in bringing offenders to justice." [@2] The spokesperson added: "Thank you as
ever for your continued support." [@2] The force said it relies on public
intelligence to identify and target such sites. [@3] Enquiries into the
operation remain ongoing. [@3]
