Costa Rican units intercepted a fast drug boat in the eastern Pacific carrying
just over 2,642 kilos of cannabis and 300 kilos of cocaine. [@1] Two people on
board were detained. [@1] A United States maritime patrol aircraft spotted the
vessel and alerted the Costa Rican authorities, who sent interceptor boats that
brought the go-fast under control after a sustained action and towed it to a
harbour for further examination. [@1]

There were 75 bales of drugs on board. [@1] The interception was part of an
international anti-smuggling operation supported by the American Joint
Interagency Task Force South, which tracks suspect transports in the Caribbean
and the eastern Pacific to disrupt smuggling routes and criminal networks. [@1]
The identities of those arrested and the origin and destination of the drugs had
not been published. [@1]

A United States task force account described the same maritime interdiction as
roughly 5,826 pounds of marijuana and 661 pounds of cocaine. [@2] That account
said Costa Rican authorities launched interceptors, intercepted the vessel and
towed it to a port, as reported by the Joint Interagency Task Force South in a
12 November post. [@2]
