The Landgericht Tübingen sentenced a now 48-year-old man to eight years in
prison after finding him guilty in seven cases of gang-scale cannabis
trafficking. [@1]

The court found the man proved to have procured large quantities of cannabis
between 2020 and 2021, delivering hundreds of kilograms to Spain, from where
other gang members brought the goods into Germany. [@1] The court held the man
provably involved as procurer in seven cannabis deliveries from Spain to
Germany, five of them destined for Münsingen in the Reutlingen district. [@1]
Two deliveries of up to 500 kilograms of cannabis each went to Halle (Saale) and
were moved on from there to Russia. [@1]

Lithuanian investigators tipped off German authorities after an international
police operation broke up a messenger service enabling encrypted communications.
[@1] The man was arrested in Spain in early 2026, extradited to Germany at the
end of March and has been in remand custody in Stuttgart since April. [@1] The
verdict is not yet final and the convicted man may appeal; the judge said he
could also serve his sentence in Spain, otherwise he would first be imprisoned
in Germany and deported to Lithuania after serving half of it. [@1]

In July the Landgericht had already convicted a recipient of the Münsingen
deliveries of gang-scale cannabis trafficking in five cases with a total prison
sentence of four years. [@1]
