Three men were arrested on suspicion of drug trafficking during an approach by
the Military Police of Piauí at Povoado Soinho, in the rural zone of Teresina,
on Thursday 19 November 2026. [@2] A Força Tática team of the 29th Military
Police Battalion was patrolling the area and spotted a vehicle matching the
description of a car named in earlier information about armed men. [@2] On
approaching, the officers found the three men at the back of a residence,
apparently handling drugs. [@2] One of the three, identified only as S.A.B., is
described by the Military Police as one of the leaders of a criminal
organisation operating in Povoado Soinho. [@2] S.A.B. already had a record for
homicide and drug trafficking and was the subject of an open arrest warrant, the
police said. [@1]

Officers seized one large wrapper of a substance resembling marijuana, two large
wrappers and two smaller portions of a substance resembling cocaine, a precision
scale and three mobile phones. [@2] A check of the National Arrest Warrant
Database showed that two of the three, identified as J.H.C.S. and S.A.B., had
open arrest warrants. [@2] The three were arrested and taken to the Central de
Flagrantes in Teresina for the legal procedures. [@2]
