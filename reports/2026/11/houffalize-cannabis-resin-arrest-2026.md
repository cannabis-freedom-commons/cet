Police arrested a man at his home in Houffalize on 13 November on suspicion of
dealing drugs. [@1] The man, born in 1975, was detained and interviewed the
following day. [@1] A search of his home found 62.9 grams of cannabis and 81.9
grams of cannabis resin, a total of 144.8 grams. [@1] Police also found €3,700,
mostly in small notes, and seized the drugs, the suspect's mobile phone and his
computer equipment. [@1]

Duty magistrate Loïc Richard said the arrest formed part of a Famenne-Ardenne
police plan against drugs. [@1] The plan aims to increase checks in the
municipality and combat drug trafficking. [@1] Prosecutors decided to summon the
suspect to an accelerated court hearing on 4 December. [@1]
