Sri Lanka Police arrested 324,780 suspects in 325,616 islandwide raids under the
‘Ratama Ekata’ national anti-drug operation between 30 October 2025 and 19
November 2026. [@1,2] Police said the operation was launched on the Inspector
General of Police's instructions to eradicate drug trafficking by targeting both
supply and demand for narcotics. [@1] The programme focused on disrupting drug
supply networks, reducing demand, expanding law enforcement raids,
rehabilitating drug addicts and strengthening community participation. [@1]

Officers seized 11,274 kilogrammes and 137 grammes of cannabis (ganja), along
with 17,302,791 cannabis plants, and a further 439 kilogrammes and 601 grammes
of hashish. [@1,2] The same seizures included 1,768 kilogrammes and 521 grammes of
Kush. [@1] Other seizures in the operation included 2,979 kilogrammes and 984
grammes of heroin, 3,753 kilogrammes and 508 grammes of crystal methamphetamine
(Ice) and 303 kilogrammes and 886 grammes of cocaine. [@1] Police said 69,973
raids resulted in the detection of cannabis and cannabis plants. [@1,2]

Detention orders were obtained against 3,896 suspects, investigations into the
illegal assets of 459 suspects were initiated and 2,714 people were referred for
rehabilitation. [@1,2]
