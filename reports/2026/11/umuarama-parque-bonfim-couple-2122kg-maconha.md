A domestic-violence call on the night of Friday 20 November 2026 ended with a
couple arrested and more than two kilograms of marijuana seized in Parque
Bonfim, Umuarama, in an operation recorded around 21:50. [@1] Officers were
first called to a report of aggression in Jardim Caroline, where a woman said
her 25-year-old son had argued with and assaulted his 41-year-old partner inside
their residence. [@1] Told the man had returned to the couple's home on Rua
Estados Unidos and resumed the assault, the officers went there with a second
team. [@1] At the house the man was stopped, the woman had recent lip injuries
and facial swelling, and her phone had been damaged. [@1] The man received a voz
de prisão, an arrest in the act, for bodily injury in the context of domestic
violence. [@1] During the arrest he told officers that drugs were kept in the
couple's bedroom. [@1]

A search of the room found three and a half tablets of marijuana weighing 2.122
kg in total, plus a precision scale. [@1] Tribuna Hoje described the tablets as
pressed marijuana and reported the same 2.122 kg and a precision scale. [@3]
According to the Military Police, the woman said she owned the drug and that it
was intended for commercial sale, so she was also arrested in the act, for drug
trafficking. [@1] Both were taken separately to Umuarama's 7th Police
Subdivision with the drug and the scale. [@1]

The Military Police estimated the marijuana at R$ 4,244 and the scale at R$ 30,
putting the total estimated loss to trafficking at R$ 4,274. [@1] OBemdito
reported that the drugs and the scale were also handed to the Civil Police in
packaging meant to preserve the chain of custody. [@2]
