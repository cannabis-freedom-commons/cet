Carabinieri arrested a 27-year-old man living in the province on Sunday on
suspicion of possessing drugs for dealing. [@1] A patrol of the Radiomobile
section of the Ferrara Carabinieri company spotted him acting suspiciously in
front of a bar in the IV Novembre area during an afternoon patrol. [@1] When he
saw the patrol car he shouted to warn the people inside and hurried into the bar
to try to avoid the check. [@1] Officers stopped him in the doorway, saw him
throw a package into an inner room of the bar, and saw two more doses fall from
his hand. [@1]

With the help of Aaron, the Local Police drug dog, officers recovered a 19-gram
block of hashish from the bar games area along with another dose of hashish and
one dose of marijuana, already packaged and ready to be sold. [@1] All the drugs
he had tried to get rid of were found and seized with the help of the Local
Police dog unit. [@1] The 27-year-old, already known to police for similar
offences, was taken to the offices, placed under arrest and held in the
provincial command cells pending the validation hearing. [@1] On the morning of
16 November the Ferrara court validated the arrest and banned him from residing
in the municipality of Ferrara. [@1]
