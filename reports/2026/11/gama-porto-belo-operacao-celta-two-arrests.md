The Civil Police of the Federal District executed eight search-and-seizure
warrants on 19 November 2026 during Operation Celta, an inquiry into a theft at
a home in Condomínio Porto Belo in Gama. [@1] Two people found with drugs during
the searches were arrested in flagrante for drug trafficking. [@2]

The theft under investigation involved the theft of three firearms, ammunition,
jewellery, money, documents and electronic devices from inside the home. [@2]
The operation was led by the Coordination for the Repression of Property Crimes
to identify those responsible, recover the objects and discover the whereabouts
of the weapons taken. [@1] Investigators are still working to clarify the
involvement of those concerned and to locate the stolen goods, especially the
three firearms. [@1]

In one searched address officers seized a large quantity of skunk. [@1] In
another property they found hashish and a liquid stored in a container applied
like eye drops that is suspected to contain a THC derivative, and its
composition will still be analysed by forensic examination. [@1]
