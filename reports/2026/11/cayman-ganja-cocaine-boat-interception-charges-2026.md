Two 49-year-old men from Jamaica were arrested when the Cayman Islands Coast
Guard and an RCIPS helicopter intercepted a vessel at sea on Sunday, 22 November
2026. [@2] The coast guard and the RCIPS Air Operations Unit recovered
suspicious packages from the boat. [@2] The packages were found to contain
cocaine and ganja, in quantities the authorities did not disclose. [@1]

Each man was charged with one count of unlawful importation of a controlled drug
for the cocaine, one for the ganja, possession of cocaine with intent to supply,
possession of ganja with intent to supply, and illegal landing. [@2] Both men
were remanded in custody after appearing in court on 24 November 2026, pending a
further court appearance. [@2] Cayman News Service reported that the two men had
appeared in court the day before its 26 November report. [@1]
