National Police officers arrested a man in flagrante during a patrol in barrio
El Olimpo, in the sector known as El Callejon, in the municipality of Ciudad
Bolivar. [@1] The procedure took place on Tuesday 10 November at about noon,
when Police Station officers found the man holding several bags of narcotic
substances. [@1]

Police reported finding 62 sealed bags with marijuana, 39 bags with cocaine
hydrochloride and 118 bags with bazuco on the man. [@1] Officers found 219 bags
in total containing the three reported narcotic substances. [@1]

Police arrested him in flagrante for trafficking, manufacture or possession of
narcotics under article 376 of the Colombian Penal Code. [@1] The arrested man
and the substances found were placed at the disposal of the competent judicial
authority to continue the judicial process. [@1] At the time of publication, the
legal situation of the person involved was still to be decided by the judicial
authority under the presumption of innocence. [@1]
