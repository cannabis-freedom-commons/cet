Satresnarkoba Polres Pelabuhan Belawan arrested 20-year-old AP for alleged dried
ganja distribution in Pasar 4, Dusun 7, Desa Helvetia, Labuhan Deli, Deli
Serdang. [@1,2] The arrest took place on Tuesday 24 November 2026 in Labuhan Deli. [@2] AP is now detained at Belawan Port police headquarters for further
investigation. [@1,2]

Police seized 25 large plastics and two white paper wraps containing dried ganja
leaves. [@1,2] Officers also seized one Android phone, one Yamaha Mio motorcycle
and Rp41,000 in cash allegedly from ganja sales. [@1,2] Officers also seized a black bag, and the seized Yamaha Mio motorcycle was red. [@2] The seized evidence
including dried ganja leaves was held at Belawan Port police headquarters on 26
November 2026 after being taken from the suspect in Helvetia village. [@1]

Belawan Port Police chief AKBP Aditya Sembiring said on 26 November 2026 that
the arrest followed residents' concern about suspected narcotics distribution in
their neighbourhood. [@1] AP admitted to selling dried ganja for about nine
months for profit of hundreds of thousands of rupiah. [@1,2] Police are still
working to identify the supplier who provided the ganja to AP. [@1]
