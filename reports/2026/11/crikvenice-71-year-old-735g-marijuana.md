Primorje-Gorski Kotar police arrested a 71-year-old man from the Vinodol
municipality area near Crikvenice on suspicion of unauthorised production and
drug trafficking. [@1] Officers from the drug crime service of the
Primorje-Gorski Kotar Police Directorate, working with Crikvenica police,
carried out the investigation against the 71-year-old Croatian citizen. [@4]

Police found 735 grams of marijuana and 73.5 grams of cocaine when they searched
his home and vehicle, according to the Primorje-Gorski Kotar police statement.
[@2] Officers also seized three digital scales, money and other items that
police said pointed to the drugs being meant for further resale. [@3] The
searches of his home and the vehicle he used took place in the Vinodol
municipality under a warrant from an investigating judge of the County Court in
Rijeka. [@4]

Police concluded that the drugs were intended for further resale. [@4] After the
investigation police filed a criminal report with the competent prosecution for
six offences of unauthorised production and trafficking of drugs and handed the
man to a detention supervisor. [@1]
