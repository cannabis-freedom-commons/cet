Drug-control officers of the Tver Oblast Interior Ministry stopped the activity
of a 47-year-old man in Tver Oblast. [@1] The man ran a full production cycle in
the basement of his own house, from growing cannabis to drying and packing it.
[@1] He sold the finished product without meeting buyers, through hidden stash
points across the region. [@1] The Tver-24 account places the house in
Kalininsky District. [@2]

During the search of his home officers found polymer bags with parts of plants,
containers and jars with plant matter, and a bag of ground material, weighing
about 10 kg in all. [@1] Forensic examination identified part of the samples as
cannabis (marijuana), with a mass of 1,973.2 grams after drying to constant
weight. [@1]

A criminal case was opened against the 47-year-old owner of the house under part
4 of article 228.1 of the Russian Criminal Code. [@1] The suspect was detained
under articles 91 and 92 of the Criminal Procedure Code and placed in a
temporary detention facility. [@1]
