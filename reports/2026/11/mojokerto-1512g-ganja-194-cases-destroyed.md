On 17 November 2026 the Mojokerto district prosecutor's office destroyed
evidence from 194 criminal cases that had obtained final and binding court
decisions. [@1,2,3] The destruction took place at the State Confiscated Goods
Storage House (Rupbasan) and was led by district prosecutor Fauzi. [@1,3]
Representatives of the Mojokerto district court, the Mojokerto Class II prison,
the district health office and the Mojokerto district police attended. [@1]

The evidence came from 194 general criminal cases handled between August and
October 2026. [@1,2] Narcotics cases were the largest group, with 69 cases. [@1]
The remainder comprised 50 theft cases, 20 health-offence cases, 11 gambling
cases and other crimes. [@1] The narcotics destroyed included 105,671 double-L
pills, 333.776 grams of sabu and 1,512 grams of ganja, the Indonesian term for
cannabis. [@1,2,3] Only the ganja is counted as cannabis here; the pills and sabu
are excluded. [@1]

Fauzi said the destruction carried out court decisions that had become final and
declared the evidence confiscated for destruction. [@1] Different types of
evidence were destroyed by crushing, striking, burning, soaking in water and
burying in soil. [@1] The seized narcotics were destroyed by blending, burning, burying and mixing with soap so that no further effects or risks would arise. [@3] Fauzi said the seized evidence had been larger because
most had already been destroyed during investigation with court permission over
misuse risks, so the day's stock was the remainder set aside from police
seizures. [@1,3]
