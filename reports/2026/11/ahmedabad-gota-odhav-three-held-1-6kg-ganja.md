Ahmedabad Police arrested three men and seized around 1.6 kg of ganja, along
with cash and other material, in two separate anti-drug operations in Gota and
Odhav. [@1] Sola High Court police, acting on a tip-off, arrested Ravi Makwana,
36, from a residential complex in Gota. [@1] The Special Operations Group (SOG)
of Ahmedabad Crime Branch raided a house on Odhav Ring Road and arrested Sanjay
Bajpai, who lived there, and Dharamveer Singh Rajput, 26, originally from
Fatehpur district in Uttar Pradesh and staying there. [@1]

At his residence in Vasantnagar Township, officers recovered 474.1 grams of
ganja that police valued at Rs 23,705, along with a weighing scale. [@1] In the
Odhav raid police seized 1.1 kg of ganja, cash, a mobile phone, a digital
weighing scale and packaging material. [@1] A second account puts the Gota
seizure at 474 grams of ganja taken from Ravi Makwana in Gota's Vasanth Nagar
Township. [@2]

Cases were registered under relevant provisions of the Narcotic Drugs and
Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act at Sola High Court and Ahmedabad Detection of
Crime Branch police stations. [@1] Police said they were investigating the
source of the contraband and whether the accused supplied drugs to others. [@1]
