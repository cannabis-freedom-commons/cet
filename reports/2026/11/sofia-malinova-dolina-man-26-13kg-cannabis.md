A 26-year-old man was detained in a police operation in Sofia's Malinova Dolina
quarter on 19 November 2026. [@1,3,5,6,7] The operation followed operational information
about a man distributing drugs in Sofia. [@1,3,5,6,7] Criminal investigators detained
him at about 15:30 on ulitsa Akademik Yordan Trifonov. [@1,3,5,6,7] A personal search
found a bag of marijuana and a small sum of money on him. [@1,3,5,6,7] The operation was
run by officers of the Seventh district police department of the Sofia city
police directorate (SDVR). [@2,3,6,7]

In the flat he lived in, officers found six bags of loose plant matter, a bag
with 10 tablets, a substance in lumps and powder, and a bag of resinous mass.
[@1,3,5,6] They also seized an electronic scale, many empty sachets and nearly 9,800
euros. [@1,3,5,6,7] In a cellar he used at the same address, investigators found more
drugs, including 20 tablets, pressed plant mass and bags with plant matter and
traces of it. [@1,3,5,6]

An expert report put the seized marijuana at more than 1,333 grams. [@1,3,5,6,7] It put
the hashish at more than 125 grams. [@1,4,5,6,7] The same report found 20.92 grams of
cocaine. [@1,3,5,6,7] It also found more than 10 grams of ecstasy. [@1,3,5,6,7]

A pre-trial case was opened under article 354a of the Penal Code. [@1,3,5,6] The files
were reported to the Sofia City Prosecution, which charged the 26-year-old under
the same article. [@1,3,5,6,7] He was ordered held for up to 72 hours. [@1,3,5,6,7]
