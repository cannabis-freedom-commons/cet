Carabinieri in Voghera arrested a 38-year-old man on suspicion of possessing
narcotics for the purpose of dealing. [@1] The arrest followed a targeted
operation by the Voghera company of the Carabinieri at an apartment in via
Amendola. [@2]

The blitz took place in a Via Amendola apartment, where officers seized 350
grams of hashish and 1.3 kilograms of cocaine. [@1] Officers also recovered
equipment for weighing and packaging doses and 3,490 euros in cash, which they
considered proceeds of the dealing. [@1]

Unusual coming and going of people near the building prompted the officers to
look into the situation. [@1] After stakeouts and observation services to check
what was happening inside the building, the Carabinieri decided to intervene.
[@1] In the searched apartment officers found the 38-year-old and the drugs,
which were immediately seized. [@1] The man was arrested and placed at the
disposal of the judicial authority. [@3]
