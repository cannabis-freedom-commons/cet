Videovigilance staff in Paraná spotted a man allegedly using drugs on Dr Raúl
Alfonsín street in the Sala Mayo area and police detained him near the wharf.
[@1] The police operation was part of prevention and security tasks, with
videovigilance staff alerting officers who went to the sector and located the
man by the wharf bordering Sala Mayo. [@1]

Officers searched a backpack that held various belongings, a blister pack with
pills of different colours and wrappers containing suspect substances. [@2] At
the prosecutor's order, the General Directorate of Dangerous Drugs took part to
carry out field tests on the pills and wrappers. [@3] Specialists' field tests
were positive for MDMA (ecstasy) in pink pills, amphetamine in violet pills,
cocaine in a white substance and marijuana in vegetal material. [@1]

After being informed of the results, the duty prosecutor ordered the substances
and the man's mobile phone seized and his transfer to the Alcaidía. [@4] The
reports published no quantities for the seized pills, wrappers or vegetal
material and gave no identity for the man. [@1]
