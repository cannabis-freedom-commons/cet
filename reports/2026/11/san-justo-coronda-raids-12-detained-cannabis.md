Santa Fe's Investigations Police (PDI) carried out 20 raids in San Justo and at
Coronda prison under a case for an alleged breach of Argentina's narcotics law,
Law 23.737. [@1] Twelve people were detained, while six others were notified of
the investigation and left in freedom. [@1]

Officers seized 39.69 grams of marijuana and one cannabis plant, along with
69.43 grams of cocaine. [@1] They also took 31 mobile phones, two digital
scales, a laptop and 1,114,750.50 Argentine pesos in cash. [@1] The searches
also turned up cutting agents, notes of interest to the investigation and
objects bearing traces of cocaine. [@1] In the search of cellblock D, where the
alleged organiser was held, investigators seized notes with phone numbers, bank
accounts and other data linked to the activity. [@1]

The measures were carried out between the night of 18 November and the early
hours of 19 November, at various homes in San Justo and in two cellblocks of
Coronda prison. [@1] Investigators said the network was allegedly run by a man
identified only as M.S.G., who coordinated activities from the prison. [@1] His
partner, identified as M.L.E., was alleged to have played a central role in
supplying drugs and coordinating sales points. [@1] The inquiry began with
reports to the 911 emergency centre about alleged drug sales in different parts
of San Justo. [@1] The case falls under the priorities of the Public Prosecution
Service (MPA) in its criminal prosecution plan, with prosecutor Luciana Escobar
Cello of the Special Microtrafficking Prosecution Unit (Ufemi) involved. [@1] By
court order the alleged organiser was also detained in this case although he was
already serving a sentence in another case, and the search of cellblock C
returned nothing. [@1] The operation involved several PDI units, provincial
police, tactical groups and Federal Penitentiary Service staff. [@1]
