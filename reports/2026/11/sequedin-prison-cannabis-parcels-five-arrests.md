Five men from Lens, three of them minors, were arrested as they tried to throw
parcels of cannabis and heroin into the prison at Sequedin. [@1] Three minors
and two adults from the Lens area were arrested while trying to get parcels of
drugs into the grounds of the maison d’arrêt at Sequedin. [@2]

The attempt came in the middle of the afternoon, and the prison administration’s
intervention officers alerted the police to attempts to hand parcels over. [@2]
The two adults were tried at Lille on the Friday after the arrests. [@2]
