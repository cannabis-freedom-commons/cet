Doda police arrested two people at two checkpoints on 22 November 2026,
recovering approximately six grams of a heroin-like substance from one and 192
grams of a charas-like substance from the other. [@2] The man held at the
Khellani checkpoint was Ankit Raina, son of Khushi Ram, a resident of Bun, Doda.
[@2] Police said he had already been booked in an earlier case at Doda police
station. [@2] The other suspect was Farooq Ahmed, son of Ghulam Ahmed, a
resident of Sarsi, Doda. [@2]

Officers of Police Post Pul Doda intercepted a vehicle registered JK06C-4519
travelling from Khellani towards Sarsi at Jangalwar, Paryote. [@2] The driver
allegedly tried to evade the checkpoint before being detained. [@2] About 192
grams of a charas-like substance was recovered from the driver. [@2] The vehicle
was also seized. [@2] Police registered FIR No. 309/2026 under sections 8 and 20
of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act at Doda police station
over the charas recovery. [@2] The six grams of heroin-like substance led to FIR
No. 308/2026 under sections 8, 21 and 22 at the same station. [@2]

The two stops came as Doda police intensified a campaign against drug
trafficking. [@1] Police said investigation into both cases was continuing. [@2]
