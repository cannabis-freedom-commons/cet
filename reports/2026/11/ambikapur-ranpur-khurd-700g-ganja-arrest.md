In Ambikapur, Kotwali police arrested one accused in a ganja-smuggling case as
part of continuing action against narcotics trafficking in Surguja district.
[@1] On 18 November 2026, Kotwali police received an informant's tip that Ranpur
Khurd resident Mohd Zafar Shah was waiting with ganja near the Ranpur Khurd
water tank for a customer. [@1] A police team reached the spot and cordoned off
the area and caught the suspect. [@1]

Questioning identified the man as 42-year-old Zafar Shah, son of Noor Mohammad,
a resident of Ranpur Khurd Parsa under Kotwali police station, Ambikapur. [@1]
Officers searched the white bag he was carrying and recovered 700 grams of
ganja. [@1] Police said the seized ganja was illegal and worth about 35,000
rupees. [@1]

Police registered crime No. 809/26 against him at Kotwali police station under
Section 20(A) of the NDPS Act. [@1] He was arrested and was being produced
before the court. [@1] Kotwali inspector Sumesh Singh, trainee sub-inspectors
Mahesh Netam and Aashi Jha, woman constable Poonam Paikra and constables Vivek
Rai and Nitin Sinha took part in the operation. [@1]
