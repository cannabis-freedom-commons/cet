The Kalininsky District Court in Novosibirsk convicted a 40-year-old local man
of drug sale and attempted sale. [@1] The court sentenced him to 8 years 6
months in a strict-regime penal colony. [@1] He had sold marijuana to an
acquaintance and then tried to sell mephedrone on a large scale. [@1]

The offences took place in August 2026. [@1] He used the internet to sell
cannabis and to arrange other illegal deals. [@1] Police detained him before he
could carry out all his plans. [@1]

The court qualified the conduct as illegal drug sale using the internet and
attempted sale of narcotic drugs and a psychotropic substance on a large scale.
[@1] The regional prosecutor's office said the verdict followed the position of
state prosecutor Vitaly Dymolazov, deputy regional prosecutor, under the cited
articles of the Russian Criminal Code. [@1] The court confiscated the mobile
phones he had used to commit the offences. [@1] The sentence had not yet entered
into legal force. [@1]
