A 42-year-old woman was arrested at Larnaca airport on 26 November 2026 after
cannabis with a total gross weight of 38.4kg was found in her luggage. [@1]
Police said the seizure was part of cooperation between the drug squad (Ykan)
and the customs department to identify people trying to import drugs into the
Republic. [@1] Acting on information assessed by Ykan, a customs officer stopped
the woman for inspection at about 6pm after her arrival from abroad. [@1]

A search of her two suitcases found four plastic packages believed to hold
cannabis, with a total gross weight of about 38.4kg. [@1] She was arrested for a
flagrant offence and taken into custody to help the investigation. [@3]
Philenews reported that she was a Greek national who had arrived in Cyprus on a
flight from Milan. [@2]

On 27 November 2026 the Larnaca district court remanded her in custody for eight
days. [@3] The Larnaca drug squad is continuing its investigation. [@1]
