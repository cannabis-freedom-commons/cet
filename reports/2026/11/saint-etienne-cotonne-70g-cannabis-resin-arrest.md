Police stopped a Peugeot Partner driving at high speed in the Cotonne area of
Saint-Étienne at about 21:30 on 25 November 2026. [@1] The driver, a 19-year-old
Saint-Étienne resident, was arrested and placed in garde à vue (police custody).
[@1]

Officers had noticed a pouch wedged between the two front seats during the stop.
[@1] Because an officer of the judicial police was present, the officers could
search it. [@1] Inside they found nearly 70 grams of cannabis resin, packaging
sachets, an electronic scale and 100 euros in cash. [@1]

After custody, he was summoned to appear before the Saint-Étienne judicial court
in the coming weeks. [@1] He is to answer a charge of supplying and offering
narcotic products to a person for personal consumption. [@1]
