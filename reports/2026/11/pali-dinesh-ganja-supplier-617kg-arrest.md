Pali district police made two arrests in separate drug-trafficking cases in an
operation directed by superintendent of police Monica Sen. [@1] Both suspects
had been wanted by police for about six months, and one had stopped using a
mobile phone for fear of being caught. [@2] Dinesh, a 30-year-old son of
Khetaram from Janguon ki Dhani in Dhandhiya, was arrested as the ganja supplier
by a team formed by Kapooraram Chaudhary, station house officer of Sadar police
station, in the Luni police station area of Jodhpur commissionerate. [@2] Police
said Dinesh was accused of supplying ganja and was being questioned. [@2]

The case goes back to 3 May 2026, when officers at Rohat police station caught
40-year-old Madanlal, son of Kaluram, riding a motorbike with 6.170 kg of ganja
and began searching for the supplier he named in questioning. [@2]

The other arrest was Suresh Kumar, 34, son of Babulal, of Nehara in the Jaitpur
police station area, who had fled when police tried to stop his car. [@2] When
officers searched the abandoned car they found 1 kg 940 g of doda-posta, a
poppy-husk product, and handed that case to Hanuwant Singh Sisodia, station
house officer of Transport Nagar police station. [@2]
