The Cherkasy District Court found a man guilty under Part 2 of Article 309 of
Ukraine's Criminal Code and sentenced him to one year in prison. [@1] Under
Article 75 of the Criminal Code the court released him from serving the prison
term and set a probation period of one year. [@1] The man had acquired the drugs
in an unspecified way, the verdict says. [@1] In court he fully admitted his
guilt and repented. [@1]

According to the verdict, police searched the man's home on 17 September 2026
and found several packets of cannabis with a total weight of 576.214 grams. [@1]
They also seized a homemade device on which they found 0.784 grams of cannabis
extract. [@1]

Article 309 of the Criminal Code covers the illegal storage of narcotic drugs
without intent to sell, the offence he was convicted of. [@1] Article 75, which
the court applied, lets a court release a convicted person from serving the
sentence and set a probation period instead. [@1]
