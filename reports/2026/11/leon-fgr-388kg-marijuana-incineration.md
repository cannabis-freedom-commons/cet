Mexico's federal prosecutor's office (FGR) destroyed almost half a tonne of
drugs seized during different federal investigations in Guanajuato. [@1] The
incineration took place at facilities in the Club Cinegético del Bajío, in the
municipality of León. [@1] The substances had been seized through coordinated
actions with the National Guard and the Defence Ministry. [@1] EstoEsGto
datelines its account of the incineration León, Gto. 13 November 2026. [@3]

The largest share by volume was marijuana, at 388 kilos 712 grams. [@1] The FGR
statement also listed 96 grams four milligrams of hashish and 23
tetrahydrocannabinol vapes among the destroyed drugs. [@2] Alongside the
cannabis, the destroyed load included 95 kilos 587 grams of methamphetamine
hydrochloride and one kilo 533 grams of cocaine, which are not counted in the
cannabis figures. [@1] The FGR also destroyed 2,126 diverse objects, including
backpacks, bags, vests, ballistic plates, helmets, mobile phones and replica
firearms. [@1]

The procedure was carried out with Federal Ministerial Police and Criminal
Investigation Agency staff, supervised by the FGR's internal control body. [@2]
The FGR did not specify which criminal groups or municipalities the seizures
related to. [@1] It gave no number of case files or of people detained in the
operations that produced the seizures. [@1]
