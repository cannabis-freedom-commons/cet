Uniformed officers of the police Traffic and Transport Section seized 1,551.8
grams of creepy-type marijuana on the Medellín–Llanos de Cuivá road in
jurisdiction of Santa Rosa de Osos, Antioquia. [@1] The report describes the
seizure as a find that occurred on Monday 23 November 2026. [@1] Officers left
the seized substance at the disposal of the Santa Rosa de Osos section of the
Attorney General's Office, which will carry out the corresponding proceedings.
[@1]

The seizure took place during registration and control activities in the El
Pinal sector of that road. [@1] Police presented the checkpoint as part of
operations against trafficking, manufacture or possession of narcotics. [@1] The
1,551.8 grams correspond to just over 1.5 kilograms of marijuana identified as
creepy type. [@1]

The report states that this conduct is established as an offence in article 376
of the Penal Code. [@1] Santa Rosa de Osos lies in Antioquia, and the controlled
road connects Medellín with Llanos de Cuivá. [@1]
