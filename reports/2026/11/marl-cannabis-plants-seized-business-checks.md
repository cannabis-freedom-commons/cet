Thomas Richter told Marl's city council that cannabis plants and untaxed tobacco
were seized during joint inspections and that food which had not been kept
properly refrigerated was destroyed. [@1] About 20 officers from various
authorities took part, checking three businesses in the city area. [@2]

The inspections were carried out under the 'Sicher in Marl' (Safe in Marl)
working group. [@1] The bodies involved were the Kommunaler Ordnungsdienst
(KOM), the city's trade office, customs' transport-route and undeclared-work
branches, food monitoring, the Recklinghausen district waste authority and the
police. [@2] Three shops and kiosks were checked. [@2] The operation found
several breaches of legal requirements: some goods were taken off sale, other
products were seized and measures were ordered at the businesses where faults
were found. [@1]

The city did not disclose which businesses were inspected. [@1]
Ordnungsdezernent Thomas Richter, the city's order deputy, said close
cooperation between the authorities allowed violations to be detected and acted
on. [@1]
