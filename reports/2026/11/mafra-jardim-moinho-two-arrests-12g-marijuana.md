Military Police arrested a 21-year-old man and a 19-year-old woman on suspicion
of drug trafficking in Mafra. [@1] The arrests followed a vehicle stop on the
afternoon of 13 November 2026 on Rua José Cassias Pereira in Jardim Moinho. [@1]
Police stopped the Fiat Uno during traffic patrols, with the incident recorded
at about 5:35 pm. [@2]

Officers found a portion of marijuana, six rocks of crack and cash inside the
car, according to the Military Police. [@1] Police said the occupants authorised
a search of a house linked to them, where officers found cocaine, more crack,
cash and phones. [@1] The total seizure included 12.9 grams of marijuana and
R$4,441.90 in cash, as well as cocaine, crack and four phones. [@1]

The man and woman were taken with the seized material to the Civil Police
station for legal procedures. [@2] The driver had no licence and was not wearing
a seat belt, and police recorded traffic violations and removed the car to an
impound lot. [@1]
