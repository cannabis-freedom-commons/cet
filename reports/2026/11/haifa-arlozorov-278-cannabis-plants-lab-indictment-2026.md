The Haifa District Prosecution filed an indictment against a 22-year-old from
Haifa for allegedly running a large-scale drug lab in a rented flat in the city.
[@1] The defendant is Alexander Sherman, a 22-year-old from Haifa, who rented a
flat on Arlozorov Street and turned it into a cannabis growing lab over the
course of a year. [@1] All three rooms of the flat were converted for cannabis
growing and equipped with lighting, ventilation, air-conditioning and irrigation
systems. [@1] The indictment was filed in the Haifa District Court against the
single defendant. [@1]

Police seized 278 cannabis seedlings with a combined weight of 127.6 kg in the
flat, according to the indictment. [@1] A police raid on 5 November exposed the
drugs alongside 38 LED lamps, nine fans, portable air conditioners, blowers, air
purifiers and other equipment. [@1] In questioning, Sherman said he alone had
rented the flat and that the seized equipment and seedlings were his. [@1] He
said he bought the equipment and seedlings for thousands of shekels in cash and
hired an electrician to install the bypass electricity connection. [@1] The
detention request notes that he confessed and helped officers find the flat key
and led them to the premises. [@1]

The lab allegedly ran on electricity drawn through a connection that bypassed
the meter, consuming power worth more than 50,000 shekels. [@1] Prosecutors also
asked to detain him until the end of proceedings, saying the lab operated in an
occupied building of ten flats where the improvised wiring endangered residents.
[@1] The indictment charges growing, producing, preparing and extracting a
dangerous drug, possessing drugs not for personal consumption, possessing
drug-preparation instruments and taking electricity. [@1] He was later released
to house arrest with electronic monitoring, subject to financial guarantees from
appointed supervisors. [@1]
