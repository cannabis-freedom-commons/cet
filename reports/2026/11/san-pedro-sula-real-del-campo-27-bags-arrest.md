National Police officers detained a suspect during a motorized patrol in
Residencial Real del Campo. [@1] The police described the detained suspect as a
member of the Mara Salvatrucha (MS-13) captured with drug wrappers in San Pedro
Sula. [@1] Police said the suspect allegedly distributed drugs in that
residential sector. [@1]

During the operation officers seized 27 small bags of suspected marijuana. [@1]
In the same operation they also seized 38 wrappers of suspected crack and 14
wrappers of suspected cocaine, 79 packages in total. [@1]
