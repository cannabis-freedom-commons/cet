Hillsborough County deputies carried out a search warrant on 12 November 2026 at
a Tampa residence after receiving information about a large amount of marijuana
inside the home. [@1] The Hillsborough County Sheriff's Office said the Florida
Department of Corrections had indicated that Delray Thomas potentially had a
large quantity of marijuana in the home. [@2] Deputies found a laundry room set
up as a sales and distribution location with scales and various types of
marijuana. [@1] In the laundry room deputies found commercial-style bags, and
the room had an exterior door designed to serve as the point of sale. [@2]

The two men inside the home, Delray Thomas, 27, and Darrell Thomas, 50, were
arrested and face several drug-related and firearm-related charges. [@1]
Deputies also found several firearms, ammunition, fentanyl, crack cocaine,
oxycodone, MDMA and $4,315 in cash. [@1] Delray Thomas was on parole for weapons
charges at the time of the arrest. [@2]
