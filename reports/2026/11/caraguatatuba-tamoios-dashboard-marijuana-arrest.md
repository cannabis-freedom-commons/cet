Military highway officers stopped a car on the Rodovia dos Tamoios (SP-099) in
Caraguatatuba, on São Paulo's north coast, during a Tático Ostensivo Rodoviário
operation on Saturday 14 November 2026. [@1] Officers approached the vehicle
after noticing a strong smell of marijuana from the ventilation ducts and
deformation under the dashboard suggesting a hidden compartment. [@1] The driver
was arrested and the case was presented to the police authorities. [@1]

During the inspection, officers found a space prepared to store drugs inside the
dashboard. [@1] There officers seized 18 bricks of marijuana. [@1] They also
seized eight large packs of marijuana flower. [@1] They also seized 300 portions
of marijuana already divided for sale. [@1] Officers also seized 40 portions of
ICE. [@1]

The driver said he had collected the drugs in Itatiba and would take them to
Ilhabela, on the north coast. [@1] He said he would receive R$2,000 for
transporting the material. [@1]
