A Chester drug dealer who told police about selling cupcakes was jailed by a
crown court judge on Monday. [@1] Isaac Moutionguissa, 20 and of Stratford Road
in Blacon, was handed a 12-month sentence for offering to deal cannabis and
cocaine. [@1] Judge Patrick Thompson, sitting at Chester Crown Court, heard the
case. [@1]

Between March 2 and June 22 this year, Moutionguissa used Snapchat to send out
messages offering drugs. [@1] He advertised the drugs via Snapchat from his home
in Blacon, using a variety of emojis and pictures of Chester. [@1] He told
police he was just selling cupcakes and was running three phones. [@1] He
answered not guilty to all questions in interview and had previous convictions
for selling Class A drugs. [@1]

He pleaded guilty to breaching his nine-month suspended sentence order, imposed
in January 2026, for a second time. [@1] The prosecution said it was his second
breach of the order and his compliance with probation was poor. [@1] His lawyer
said he was a young man who entered guilty pleas at the earliest opportunity.
[@1] His lawyer said he was close to an ADHD diagnosis. [@1] He had completed 44
hours of unpaid work while continuing to commit offences. [@1] He had breached
the order in May, when 20 more hours were added to his unpaid work. [@1] The
judge imposed six months of the nine-month suspended sentence. [@1] The judge
imposed a consecutive 12-month sentence for the drugs, making 18 months in
total. [@1] The judge revoked the suspended order, took no further action on it,
and said Moutionguissa would face licence conditions after release from a young
offender institution. [@1]
