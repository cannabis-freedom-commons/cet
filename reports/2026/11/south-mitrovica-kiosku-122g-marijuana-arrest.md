Kosovo Police arrested one person in South Mitrovica on suspicion of selling and
distributing narcotics. [@1] The arrest was made during a tactical operation
codenamed KIOSKU in South Mitrovica. [@1]

Officers first stopped the suspect and found 4.5 grams of marijuana on him. [@1]
On the prosecutor’s order, police then searched a premises and an apartment
owned by the suspect. [@1] During that search officers found and seized a
further 117.9 grams of marijuana, three digital scales, a mobile telephone and
435 euros in cash. [@1] A second outlet confirmed that police arrested one
person and seized 117.9 grams of marijuana in South Mitrovica. [@2]

By decision of the prosecutor, the suspect was taken into custody. [@1] The
narcotics had been divided into small packages suspected of being prepared for
street-level distribution. [@2] Interior Minister Xhelal Sveçla said the fight
against narcotics would continue, describing it as protecting the health and
safety of children and young people. [@1]
