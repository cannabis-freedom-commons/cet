Officers stopped a Honda in Biała Rządowa just before midnight on 20 November
2026. [@1] Officers had noticed the Honda driver having clear problems keeping a
proper course. [@1] The driver was a 40-year-old resident of Wieruszów County
who behaved nervously during the intervention. [@1] The Wieruszów County
resident was detained and placed in police custody. [@1] The next day he was
brought to the District Prosecutor's Office in Wieluń, where he was charged with
possessing significant quantities of psychotropic substances and narcotics. [@1]

Officers searching the car found more than 45 grams of white powder and 1.8
grams of plant material. [@1] Preliminary drug tests indicated the seized
substances were amphetamine and marijuana. [@1] Officers also took blood from
the driver for laboratory tests to establish whether he drove under the
influence of drugs. [@1]

A records check showed the man had no required driving entitlement. [@1] The
charged offence carries up to 10 years in prison. [@1] Separately, the
40-year-old will face court for driving without the required entitlement. [@1]
The investigation is conducted by the District Prosecutor's Office in Wieluń.
