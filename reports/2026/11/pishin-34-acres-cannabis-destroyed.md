A joint operation by the district administration, the Excise and Narcotics
Department, the Intelligence Bureau and district police destroyed cannabis crops
grown on about 34 acres in different areas of Tehsil Huramzai. [@1] The
operation was carried out on the instructions of the Deputy Commissioner Pishin
and under the supervision of the Assistant Commissioner Pishin. [@1] The Pishin
authorities said the operation followed the provincial government's policy to
eliminate illegal drug cultivation. [@1]

Teams destroyed cannabis covering about 11 acres in Ghwas Killi Manzari. [@1]
Teams cleared another 23 acres across two separate locations in Killi Bala
Manzari. [@1] Together the clearances in the two village areas totalled about 34
acres, or about 13.76 hectares. [@1]

Officials said the drive was aimed at preventing illegal drug cultivation,
protecting young people from the harmful effects of drugs and supporting
government efforts against narcotics. [@1] The district administration said
action against illegal cultivation and drug-related activities would continue in
Pishin district. [@1]
