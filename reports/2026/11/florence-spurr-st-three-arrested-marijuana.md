Three people are in jail after a drug bust in Florence, Alabama, in which drug
agents seized three pounds of marijuana from a home on Spurr Street. [@1] Agents
with the Lauderdale County Drug Task Force executed a search warrant at the
residence at 116 N Spurr St. [@3] Investigators arrested Donte Neely, Brandy
Hurley and Marcus Bizzelle on trafficking charges. [@2] The task force
identified them as Donte Brady Neely, 45, Brandy Patrice Hurley, 43, and Marcus
Paul Bizzelle, 32, all of Florence. [@3] Each was charged with trafficking in
illegal drugs for ecstasy and synthetic marijuana, unlawful possession of a
controlled substance for THC vapes and clonazepam, unlawful possession of
marijuana in the first degree and unlawful possession of drug paraphernalia.
[@3]

The warrant was sought because of the drug dealing activities of the residence's
occupants. [@3] The Florence/Lauderdale Special Operations Team made entry and
found multiple people inside. [@3] During the search agents located over 200
grams of synthetic marijuana (Spice), over 150 grams of ecstasy pills, three
pounds (about 1.36 kg) of marijuana, clonazepam pills, over 40 THC vapes and a
handgun. [@3]

The Florence Police Department, Lauderdale County Sheriff's Office,
Florence/Lauderdale Special Operations Team and the Lauderdale County District
Attorney's Office assisted the task force. [@3] Task force officials said
additional charges and suspects are expected as the investigation continues.
[@3]
