A suspended electricity board employee was arrested on Saturday for smuggling
ganja on his two-wheeler in Mettur. [@1] Police identified the arrested man as M
Joseph, 47, of the Matha Kovil area of Mettur. [@1] According to an
investigation officer, Joseph was stopped during a routine vehicle check along
the right bank of Mettur dam on Kolathur Road. [@1]

The officer said Joseph was riding the two-wheeler in a suspicious manner and
that inspecting it revealed 900 grams of ganja concealed inside the seat cover.
[@1] Officers seized the ganja and took Joseph to the police station for
interrogation. [@1] Investigations revealed Joseph was already facing a ganja
smuggling case, which had led to his suspension from the electricity board. [@1]

Police registered a case, arrested Joseph and produced him before the Mettur
court. [@1] The Mettur court remanded him in judicial custody and he was
subsequently lodged in Salem Central Prison. [@1]
