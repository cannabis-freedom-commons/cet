Officers of Guelmim's second security district arrested a man with a criminal
record on Monday 16 November 2026, suspecting him of possessing and trafficking
chira, Morocco's name for cannabis resin. [@1] The arrest was made in the
afternoon, according to Mamlakapress, which reported it the same day. [@2]

A search after the arrest found a significant quantity of chira intended for
sale. [@1] Officers also seized a sum of money suspected to be proceeds of the
activity and a motorcycle they said the suspect used to move around while
dealing. [@1] Darb24 described the seizure as significant quantities of chira in
the suspect's possession, plus a sum of money and a motorcycle believed to have
served his activity. [@3] Darb24 reported that the arrest came during an
inspection and monitoring campaign in the city. [@3]

The suspect was held in police custody, known in Morocco as garde à vue, for a
judicial investigation supervised by the public prosecutor. [@1] Police said the
investigation would establish the circumstances of the case, any further reach
of the activity and any other people involved. [@1]
