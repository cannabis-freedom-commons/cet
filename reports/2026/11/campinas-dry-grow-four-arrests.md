Police in Campinas, Sumaré and Hortolândia arrested four people in flagrante
during Operação Dry Grow, an operation against the production, distribution and
sale of cannabis derivatives with a high concentration of THC, the compound
responsible for the drug's psychoactive effects. [@1,3,4] The operation's final balance put the number of people arrested in flagrante at five, up from the four reported earlier the same day. [@5] The four arrests were made on Tuesday 17 November 2026. [@4] The two residents of the Sumaré house, a couple, were arrested there, a second couple was detained in Campinas and a fifth suspect in Hortolândia, all at addresses where officers found drugs. [@5] Investigators said the
high-potency cannabis was not only manufactured but also sold and supplied to
drug-selling points. [@2,4] Investigators described a division of tasks in which one suspect supplied the drug, a couple processed the marijuana and made the dry, and a second couple was to sell the product. [@5] They suspected that properties and companies formed
part of the criminal group's structure. [@1,4]

Officers served seven search and seizure warrants, four in Sumaré, two in
Campinas and one in Hortolândia. [@1,3,4,5] Officers seized about 10 kilograms of drugs, including marijuana and derivatives at different stages of processing, at three of the seven addresses. [@5] The operation aimed to establish how the
organisation worked and identify others involved. [@1,4] The laboratory that processed the marijuana and produced the dry concentrate operated in a couple's home in Sumaré. [@5]

The action was carried out by Ficco, the integrated force against organised
crime, together with the Military Police's 1st Baep special-actions battalion
and the Civil Police's Deinter 2. [@1,4,5] Ficco Campinas comprises the Federal
Police, the Federal Highway Police, the Civil Police, the Military Police and
Paulínia's municipal guard, with support from the prosecutors' Gaeco unit. [@1,3,4,5]
A Federal Police handout showed seized packaging bearing the image of Colombian
trafficker Pablo Escobar. [@1,6]
