Daejeon Metropolitan Police Agency's drug crime squad said it arrested 25
people, including the ringleader in his 30s, for operating a Telegram drug
distribution platform with about 3,000 members for about six years starting in
2020. [@1] Police sent 12 of them to prosecutors under detention on suspicion of
violating the Narcotics Control Act and applied criminal-organization charges to
the ringleader and other core operators. [@1] Officers built the case over one
year and seven months of real-time monitoring of the Telegram channels, combined
with CCTV analysis, telecom investigation and international cooperation, before
identifying and arresting the ringleader at the top of the organization. [@1]

Investigators found the group on-boarded individual drug sellers onto the
platform, brokered sales with quality-guarantee advertising, and recruited drug
couriers to build the distribution network. [@1] The group allegedly collected
about 30 million won worth of virtual assets per month from the sellers as
advertising fees. [@1] Police said they traced tens of thousands of transactions
to uncover a mixing route used for money laundering and obtained a
pre-indictment preservation order for about 700 million won in virtual assets
hidden on an overseas exchange. [@1]

A police-provided video still published with the report shows cannabis growing
inside an indoor cultivation tent. [@1] A second account likewise describes
about 19 months of real-time platform monitoring to gather evidence. [@2] A
third account likewise reports 12 sent under detention on Narcotics Control Act
charges, with criminal-organization charges applied to the core operators. [@3]
