The Dirección de Seguridad de Patagones told NoticiasNet that no one was
detained, although the search was positive. [@1] The search was carried out by
personnel of the Drogas Ilícitas unit of the Buenos Aires province police,
belonging to the Grupo Operativo Patagones, in an investigation into a suspected
breach of Law 23.737. [@1] The court-ordered search took place on Thursday 12
November 2026 at a home on calle San Martín al 700, ordered by Guarantee Court
No. 4 and the Functional Instruction and Judgment Unit No. 19 under prosecutor
Mauricio Del Cero. [@1]

During the search officers seized doses of cocaine and marijuana, elements used
for splitting and weighing drugs, mobile phones and cash. [@1] The police
statement also listed the seized material as a white substance and herbs, money
and scales. [@1]

The case remains at the investigative stage and continues under the Functional
Instruction and Judgment Unit No. 19. [@1]
