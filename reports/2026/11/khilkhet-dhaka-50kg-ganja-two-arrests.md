Dhaka Metropolitan Detective Police's Ramna division arrested two alleged drug
dealers in Dhaka's Khilkhet with 50 kg of ganja and 24 bottles of Indian-made
intoxicating cough syrup. [@1] The arrested accused were identified as
24-year-old Mahbub Alam Shipon of Araihazar in Narayanganj and 49-year-old Md.
Hazrat Ali. [@1] A DB team arrested them in a drive in the Kuril Mor area under
Khilkhet Police Station on Saturday and recovered a motorcycle used to transport
the drugs at the time of arrest. [@1]

Acting on a secret tip, a Ramna zonal team conducted a special anti-drug drive
and stopped the two men in front of the Haji Sahab Uddin Baitul Falah Jame
Mosque on the south side of the road from Kuril Mor toward 300 Feet. [@1] A
search of the sacks with them yielded 50 kg of ganja and 24 bottles of
Indian-made WINCEREX cough syrup. [@1] Citing DB, the report put the estimated
value of the seized drugs at 1,072,000 taka, comprising 1,000,000 taka for the
50 kg of ganja and 72,000 taka for the cough syrup. [@1]

Officers also seized an unnumbered Yamaha R15 V4 motorcycle used by Mahbub Alam
Shipon. [@1] A case was filed against the two arrested men at Khilkhet Police
Station under the relevant sections of the Narcotics Control Act, 2018. [@1]
Assistant Police Commissioner Md. Ali Ashraf of the DB Ramna zonal team
confirmed the information at noon. [@1]
