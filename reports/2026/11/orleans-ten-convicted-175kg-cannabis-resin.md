Ten people were tried from Tuesday 24 November 2026 for their alleged
involvement, direct or indirect, in a large drug-trafficking operation. [@1] The
defendants were nine men and one woman aged 25 to 43. [@1] The court examined
what role each played — driver, couriers, an organiser, delivery men or
go-betweens — and some defendants contested the accusations. [@1] The Orléans
criminal court found all ten guilty and delivered its verdict on the evening of
Wednesday 25 November 2026. [@1]

The two-day hearing at the Orléans criminal court concerned seizures of up to
175 kg of cannabis resin, 40 kg of heroin and more than 200,000 euros. [@1]
Judges handed down sentences ranging from suspended terms to ten years in
prison. [@1] They also imposed large fines, two of them exceeding one million
euros. [@1] The defendants had ten days to appeal. [@1]
