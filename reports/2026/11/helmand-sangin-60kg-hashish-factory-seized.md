Security forces of the Helmand Police Command seized 60 kilograms of hashish and
uncovered a drug manufacturing facility in Helmand province in an enforcement
action announced by the Ministry of Interior Affairs. [@1] The ministry said the
drugs were seized and the drug processing factory was discovered in Sangin
District during several separate operations conducted by the security forces.
[@1] Officers acted on a tip-off and raided a site on the outskirts of Sangin district. [@2] The ministry communicated the account of the Sangin District seizure and
factory discovery in a post on its X account. [@1]

The total quantity reported seized in the Helmand operations was 60 kilograms of
hashish. [@1,2] The uncovered site was described as a drug manufacturing facility
in the province. [@1,3] The same site was also described as a drug processing
factory in Sangin District. [@1,2] The enforcement action comprised several
separate operations rather than a single raid. [@1]

The ministry did not provide further details about how the facility operated.
[@1] The ministry also did not provide further details about the individuals
linked to the seized hashish or to the facility. [@1]
