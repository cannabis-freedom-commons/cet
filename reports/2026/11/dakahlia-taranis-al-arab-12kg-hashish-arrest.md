Officers from the General Directorate for Drug Control's East Delta region
arrested a man in Taranis al-Arab village, in the SanbLawain district of Egypt's
Dakahlia Governorate, catching him with drugs before he could distribute them to
customers. [@2] Police identified him only by initials, gave his age as 34 and
said his nickname was Tarek al-Saadawi; they said he had previously been caught
in a drug-trafficking felony and had resumed his criminal activity in the area.
[@1]

After prosecutors issued permission, a team of drug-control inspectors raided
his home and found 12 kilograms of hashish and one kilogram of the drug called
ice, along with a sum of money and a mobile phone. [@1] Police said that, when
confronted with what the search produced, he admitted holding the drugs for
sale, said the money was the proceeds of his criminal activity and said the
phone was for contacting his customers. [@1]

The necessary report was prepared and the Public Prosecution was notified to
take up the investigation. [@1]
