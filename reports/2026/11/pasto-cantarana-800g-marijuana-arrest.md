In Barrio Cantarana, officers of Colombia's National Police acting through the
Special Operations Group captured a 33-year-old man in flagrante during control
and patrol operations. [@1] Officers on routine surveillance saw the man walking
on a public road and said he adopted a suspicious attitude when he noticed them.
[@1]

The man tried to evade the check by throwing a bag to the ground, which officers
then picked up to verify its contents. [@1] Inside the bag officers found
several transparent hermetic-type bags containing plant material that was
preliminarily identified as marijuana by its colour, smell and appearance. [@1]
Officers seized approximately 800 grams of the substance and placed the man at
the disposal of the competent authority to answer for the imputed offence. [@1]

Authorities presented the result as part of strategies to combat
micro-trafficking in urban sectors. [@1] Police reiterated a call for residents
to keep reporting crime through the enabled lines, saying citizen cooperation is
key to fighting crime. [@2]
