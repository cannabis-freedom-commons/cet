Police in Karditsa arrested a 29-year-old Greek man on the evening of 17
November 2026. [@2] The officers were from the Karditsa unit of DI.AS. (Omada
Dikykis Astynomefsis), the Greek police's motorcycle patrol. [@1]

During a check they found him carrying a small quantity of raw, unprocessed
cannabis, and seized it. [@1] The Karditsa police directorate's daily crime
bulletin for 18 November 2026 lists the case as an arrest for a violation of the
drug legislation. [@2] Police released only his age and nationality. [@2]

The arrest was one of three that police in Karditsa and Sofades made that
evening, in as many separate cases. [@1] A 55-year-old man was arrested in
Sofades for playing music at high volume at home and disturbing his neighbours.
[@2] A 20-year-old in Karditsa was arrested after dropping a knife when officers
approached him. [@2]
