Acting on information received by Sri Lanka Army Intelligence operatives,
officers carried out a series of raids at three separate locations in the
Hambegamuwa area of Monaragala on 14 November 2026 in coordination with officers
of the Special Task Force based in Ambalantota. [@2] During the operation
officers arrested one suspect in the Hambegamuwa area with cannabis plants
valued at more than Rs 2.1 million. [@1] The Army said the suspect was arrested
in a special cordon operation mounted jointly with the police Special Task Force
after secret information was received. [@1]

The Tamil account places the three-site operation in Hambegamuwa on Saturday 14
November and describes the crop as cannabis plants under cultivation on 175
perches of land. [@1] Security forces seized 32,310 cultivated cannabis plants
at the raided sites, grown on 175 perches of land equivalent to about 0.44
hectares. [@2] The Army estimated the value of the seized cultivation at more
than Rs 2,100,000. [@2]
