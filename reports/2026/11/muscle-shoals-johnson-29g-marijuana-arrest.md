The Muscle Shoals Police Department said its narcotics unit and tactical team
executed a search warrant on Tyler Avenue in Muscle Shoals, Alabama, around 5:30
a.m. on 25 November 2026. [@1] Officers found Bryan Earl Johnson, 40, inside the
home, police said. [@1]

During the search, police said agents found 31 grams of cocaine, 13 grams of
hydrocodone, 29 grams of marijuana and 3 grams of alprazolam, along with a
handgun and an AR-style rifle. [@1] The 29 grams of marijuana is the only
cannabis quantity in the case; the cocaine, hydrocodone and alprazolam are
separate substances and are not counted in the cannabis figures. [@1]

Johnson was arrested and charged with several offences, police said. [@1] The
charges were trafficking illegal drugs: cocaine, trafficking in a controlled
substance: hydrocodone, unlawful possession of a controlled substance,
first-degree unlawful possession of marijuana, certain persons forbidden to
possess a pistol and endangering the welfare of a child. [@1] Police said
Johnson is being held on a $300,000 bond and is presumed innocent unless proven
guilty in court. [@1]
