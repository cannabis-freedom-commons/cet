Police in Heraklion identified two Greek men aged 30 and 34 during an inquiry
into organising and taking part in improvised boxing bouts in a public space.
[@1] The bouts were in a public park in Therisso in the Municipality of
Heraklion. [@3] Police said the 34-year-old, described as a boxing instructor,
organised an improvised bout in which the 30-year-old took part without the
required permit for holding a sports meeting. [@1] The bout was reportedly held
in an unsuitable venue without the prescribed medical cover, endangering the
participants. [@1] A case file was formed against both men for violations of
amateur and professional sports legislation. [@1]

The 30-year-old was additionally arrested for drug-law violations. [@1] A search
of the 30-year-old's home found and seized a small amount of cannabis and a
precision scale. [@2] The First Police Department of Heraklion is conducting the
preliminary inquiry. [@1]
