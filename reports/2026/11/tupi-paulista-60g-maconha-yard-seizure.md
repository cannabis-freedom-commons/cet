A woman in Tupi Paulista, in São Paulo state, called the Military Police on the
morning of Tuesday 17 November 2026 after finding suspicious objects in the yard
of her home. [@1] She told the police that on the previous evening a precision
scale and a package of drugs had been thrown from the neighbouring house into
her yard. [@1] The following morning she found a second wrapper and a bunch of
keys, and contacted the police. [@1]

At the scene officers of the Military Police seized 60 grams of marijuana and 8
grams of crack, together with a precision scale and two keys. [@1] The Military
Police confirmed the findings at the address and asked for support from the
Civil Police and the Scientific Police, who carried out the inquiries at the
property. [@1]

The occurrence was registered with the Civil Police, which is responsible for
investigating the case. [@1] A second local outlet reported the same seizure of
60 grams of marijuana and 8 grams of crack at the Tupi Paulista residence. [@2]
