Police in the Imamganj police-station area of Bihar's Gaya district arrested
Sanjay Kumar, a Raniganj resident, on allegations of selling ganja, the local
name for cannabis. [@1] The operation was led by circle officer Sukesh Kumar,
who said police had received information for several days that Kumar was making
small packets of ganja at his home and selling them. [@2]

Officers in plain clothes kept watch near the house in Raniganj Niche Bazar and
caught Kumar as he handed ganja to a customer who had come to buy. [@2] A search
of the house found ganja packed in small slips, and police seized 844 grams in
total and prepared a seizure list. [@2]

Station house officer Amit Kumar said the arrested man was questioned, produced
before a court and sent to jail. [@2] The circle officer said the drive to keep
young people away from drugs would continue in the area. [@2]

Dainik Bhaskar reported that the 844 grams of ganja were recovered from his
home. [@1]
