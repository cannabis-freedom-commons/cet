Kumhari police in Chhattisgarh's Durg district arrested a man carrying ganja in
an operation under the district's Sankalp campaign against the illegal drug
trade. [@1] Dainik Bhaskar reported that the district Anti-Crime and Corruption
Unit (ACCU) and Kumhari police made the arrest. [@1] Officers seized 1 kilogram
850 grams of ganja from him. [@1]

CG Awaaz reported that district police had been running a series of operations
against the buying, selling and trafficking of illegal drugs. [@2] CG Awaaz
named the arrested man as Shyam Tandi, a resident of Kumhari. [@2] Police caught
him near a pond on Mahamaya Road in the Station Chowk area of Kumhari. [@2]
Officers found the ganja in a blue bag when they searched him. [@2]

Police put the estimated value of the ganja at 87,500 rupees. [@2] He was
arrested and legal proceedings were begun in the case. [@2] Police said
operations against people involved in the ganja trade in the district would
continue and asked residents to pass on information about drug dealing. [@2]
