A 48-year-old woman reported her cargo bike stolen at about 20:00 on Sunday 15
November 2026, saying she had locked it on Warschauer Straße and could locate it
in Volkspark Hasenheide. [@2] Officers found the suspected thief, a 33-year-old
man, leaving the park with the bike at about 20:50 and arrested him. [@2] He
resisted arrest, hitting and kicking the officers and biting one of them in the
hand, according to the police account. [@2] All the injured officers were able
to continue their duty. [@2] The suspect suffered facial injuries in the
struggle and was treated in a hospital under police escort. [@2]

A search of the man found several zip-lock bags of cannabis. [@2] A look at his
mobile phone strengthened the suspicion that he traded in drugs without
authorisation. [@1]

Police are investigating assault on law enforcement officers, unauthorised trade
in cannabis and serious theft of a bicycle, and said the investigation
continues. [@2] The man was handed to the responsible section commissariat,
which is leading the investigation. [@3]
