Police in Kataram mandal launched special checks focused on ganja consumption,
targeting young people suspected of using the drug in the area. [@1] During
raids in Damerakunta, Mallaram and Kataram villages, officers detained 11 youths
on suspicion of ganja use for further examination. [@1] Police took all 11
detained youths to Bhupalpally government hospital, where doctors conducted
medical tests to check for recent ganja consumption. [@1] The hospital tests
confirmed that nine of the examined youths had consumed ganja, establishing
recent use in those nine cases. [@1] The same round of hospital examinations
returned negative results for the other two detained youths, clearing them of
recent ganja use. [@1]

Police said they were preparing to take action under the narcotic-substances
prohibition law against the nine youths who had consumed ganja. [@1] Police also
said they had placed under special surveillance people selling ganja within the
mandal to curb further supply and consumption. [@1] Officers urged young people
to stay away from intoxicating substances and asked parents to closely monitor
their children's movements and activities. [@1]
