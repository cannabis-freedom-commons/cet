Aaron Ashton Gibson, 34, of Rome, Georgia, was arrested on a dozen drug and
traffic charges after a traffic stop. [@1] Georgia State Troopers arrested him
on multiple charges including possession of a Schedule I controlled substance
and possession of marijuana with the intent to distribute. [@2] The stop took
place on Highway 101 at East 12th Street after officers saw the vehicle fail to
maintain its lane. [@2] Officers said they detected the smell of alcohol while
speaking with Gibson. [@1]

After determining that Gibson was reportedly driving under the influence of
alcohol, officers found numerous drugs in his possession. [@1] Police said
Gibson was found with marijuana, pills and a digital scale. [@1] Gibson was
charged with felony possession of a schedule I or narcotic schedule II
substance, possession of marijuana, possession of tools for the commission of a
crime, felony possession or distribution of marijuana, drugs not in their
original container, possession of drug-related objects, driving on a suspended
licence, driving without insurance, failure to maintain lane, DUI and two counts
of probation violation. [@1]

He was additionally charged with possession of less than an ounce of marijuana,
driving while unlicensed and driving with suspended registration. [@2]
