Juvenile prosecutor Pedro De León filed serious charges for violation of the
Weapons Law against a teenager detained with a rifle and marijuana in Arecibo.
[@1] The accused is a 15-year-old boy charged over possession of a long weapon,
a magazine, ammunition and marijuana. [@1] The boy was stopped at building 24 of
the Trina Padilla de Sanz residential complex in Arecibo. [@1]

Officers seized a rifle, a magazine containing 28 rounds of ammunition and
marijuana from him during that intervention. [@1] A police handout photograph
published with the report shows the seized rifle, bullets, magazine and
marijuana. [@1] The evidence was presented before Judge Alba I. Calderón Cestero
of the Arecibo court, who found probable cause on the charges. [@1] The judge
ordered the boy to be admitted to the Juvenile Institution in Ponce pending
further proceedings in the juvenile case. [@1]

The adjudicatory hearing in the case was scheduled for 23 November. [@1] The
Family Department was notified as required and investigators are checking
whether the boy attended school. [@1]
