Four men were convicted in San Patricio del Chañar for taking part in a
drug-selling network with several selling points in the town. [@1] One received
an effective prison term, while the other three received terms to serve in
freedom under conditions. [@1] Prosecutor María Eugenia Titanti and assistant
Luciano Vidal agreed the sentences through abbreviated procedures. [@1] The four
accused admitted responsibility and accepted the penalties proposed by the
Public Prosecution Service. [@1] Judge Juan Manuel Kees approved the agreements
at two hearings and imposed the sentences. [@1] The parties waived the appeal
deadlines, so the sentences became final in the same act. [@1]

Ángel Xavier Toscano received the highest penalty, four years of effective
prison for drug commerce. [@1] Toscano must also pay a $7,200,000 fine. [@1]
According to the investigation, Toscano sold cocaine and marijuana from his
home. [@1] Investigators recorded 1,710 bank transfers to him between January
and July for about $143 million. [@1] A search of his home seized more than 500
grams of cocaine, marijuana, scales, cash and mobile phones. [@1] Walter Eugenio
Barrera was sentenced to three years of conditional prison and a $3,600,000
fine. [@1] Barrera received drugs from a supplier, split them and distributed
them by home delivery. [@1] Diego Armando Muñoz Arancibia received two years of
conditional prison and a $2,700,000 fine as a secondary participant. [@1] He
moved drugs from Centenario to supply Toscano and others under investigation.
[@1] Néstor Jorge Gatica received three years of conditional prison and a
$3,600,000 fine. [@1] Gatica sold drugs from a home and also made home
deliveries. [@1] A procedure at that property seized 132 grams of cocaine,
marijuana, five scales and other related items. [@1] Together the four sentences
totalled $17,100,000 in fines. [@1]

The case began with 12 anonymous reports to the Public Prosecution Service
through a QR code and the Neuquén Te Cuida app. [@1] Antinarcotics Department
staff then began observation and tracking. [@1] On 24 July, searches were
carried out at several homes in the town. [@1] Those procedures seized drugs,
cash, scales, mobile phones and other items of interest. [@1] Prosecutors later
dropped the aggravating circumstance of organised participation by three or more
people. [@1]
