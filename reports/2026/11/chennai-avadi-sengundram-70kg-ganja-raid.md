Twenty-seven people were arrested for alleged cannabis sale in a special police
check across Chennai's northern suburbs. [@1] The check covered areas under 30
police stations in the Avadi and Sengundram police districts and was carried out
the day before the report was published, on 20 November 2026. [@1]

Officers seized 70.39 kilograms of cannabis and 260 narcotic tablets from the
suspects. [@1] The drive was run by the Avadi Police Commissionerate across its
Avadi and Sengundram police districts. [@2] Avadi Police Commissioner
Senthilkumar ordered the operation, which was carried out by special teams led
by assistant commissioners. [@2]

In the same drive, police registered 25 cases over the sale of banned gutka,
arrested 27 people and seized 37.78 kilograms of it. [@2] The prohibition
enforcement wing separately arrested seven people over illegal liquor sales and
seized 22.32 litres of alcohol. [@2] The combined number of arrests across the
cannabis, gutka and liquor cases was 61. [@2] Police said similar checks would
continue to curb the movement of drugs and stop young people from taking them.
[@2]
