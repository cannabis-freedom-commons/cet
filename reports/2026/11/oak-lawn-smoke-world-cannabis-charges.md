Community members told Oak Lawn police that a local retail tobacco shop might be
selling cannabis products to minors, and that tip led to the arrest of a
30-year-old Oak Lawn man. [@1] Police identified the shop as Smoke World at
10041 S. Ridgeland Avenue and said it was reportedly selling cannabis products
to minors. [@1,3] Mohammad Tarawneh, an employee of the business, was arrested on
Monday. [@1,3] Officers executed a search warrant at the store that Monday and
seized a large quantity of cannabis, THC-containing products, nitrous oxide and
approximately $19,000 in cash. [@1,3]

Tarawneh was charged with two misdemeanor counts of manufacture or delivery of
cannabis in the 2.5-to-10-gram range and one class 3 felony count of manufacture
or delivery of nitrous oxide. [@1,3] The two cannabis counts were Class A misdemeanors. [@3] He appeared for a pre-detention hearing on
Wednesday, where he was cited and released while the case was pending. [@1,3] Police said Tarawneh made his first court appearance at the Bridgeview Courthouse. [@3] The
Oak Lawn Police Department thanked community members who reported the suspected
illegal activity and said their information was instrumental in initiating the
investigation. [@1] Police said the investigation remained ongoing. [@1,3]

Illinois law restricts cannabis sales to adults 21 and older. [@2]
