State police in Torreón detained a delivery-app driver who, they said, was
transporting marijuana in the colonia Villas Universidad, in the east of the
city. [@1] The outlet reported that the young man was allegedly delivering
marijuana in eastern Torreón. [@1] The detention happened on the morning of
Monday 16 November 2026, when officers flagged down a motorcyclist riding on
Universidad street. [@1] The rider ignored the officers' instructions and
accelerated to try to escape. [@1] Officers chased him along the prolongación
Juárez. [@1] There the state officers caught up with him and detained him. [@1]

During a search of his belongings the officers found green, dry herb with
characteristics similar to marijuana. [@1] He was secured together with the
substance and taken before the corresponding authorities, who are to carry out
the investigation. [@1] The young man was placed at the disposal of those
authorities so that his legal situation could be determined. [@1]
