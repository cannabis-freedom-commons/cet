Military Police in Marabá, Pará, arrested four men on the night of Thursday 19
November 2026: Elzemir Felix do Nascimento, Josiel de Sousa Alves, Luís
Guilherme Silvino Oliveira and Samuel Lima Araújo. [@1] The operation was
carried out by a crew of the 34th Military Police Battalion during patrol, after
reports of a possible drug-selling point in the region. [@1]

Officers spotted the group acting suspiciously outside a residence; when the
patrol car approached, the men went inside, locked the door and tried to escape
out the back using a ladder to climb the yard wall. [@1] Police surrounded the
house, got inside and intercepted all four. [@1] The search turned up 35
portions of marijuana already packaged for sale, along with loose cash, a
precision scale and mobile phones. [@1]

The other detainees named the homeowner as responsible for the drug sales, and
one said more drugs had been thrown towards nearby woodland, but searches found
nothing. [@1] The four men and the seized material were taken to the Civil
Police station, where the case was registered; police did not disclose the
measure adopted. [@1]
