One alleged drug dealer was killed in an exchange of gunfire near Phalina Dam
while three of his accomplices escaped. [@2] Police on patrol near Phalina Dam
stopped two unregistered motorcycles carrying four men at about 12:50 am and
said the riders opened fire before fleeing towards Mandra. [@2] The dead man was
identified as Iftikhar Ali, a resident of Darkali Sher Shahi in Kallar Syedan
who was reported to be a former police employee aged about 62. [@2] Police found
Iftikhar wounded in a field beside the road and said he told them he had been
shot by his own companions. [@2] He died while being shifted to hospital. [@1] A
post-mortem was carried out at THQ Hospital Kallar Syedan and his body was
handed to his family. [@2] A murder case was registered against unidentified
people. [@2]

Officers recovered 1,500 grams of charas and a 30-bore pistol from Iftikhar's
possession. [@1] In two other operations that night, sub-inspector Qamar Shahzad
arrested Tauseef and Naheed, both residents of Dhok Marzian, Bhalakhar. [@1]
From both the accused police recovered 3,400 grams of charas. [@1] The three
operations produced 4.9 kilograms of charas in total. [@1]

Some police sources said Iftikhar, reported as 60 years old, was a former police
constable who had been acquitted by a court in a narcotics case in February
2026. [@3] The Kallar Bar Association protested what it called his extrajudicial
killing by police and demanded a judicial inquiry. [@4]
