Patrols of the Security Regiments in Jazan region foiled an attempt to smuggle
18 kilograms of hashish in Al-Dayer governorate. [@1] The intercepted material
was described as hashish narcotic substance. [@1] The seized consignment was
handed over to the competent authority. [@1]

The security authorities urged citizens and residents to report any information
they hold about activities linked to drug smuggling or promotion. [@1] They gave
the numbers 911 for the Mecca, Medina, Riyadh and Eastern regions and 999 for
the rest of the kingdom. [@1] Reports can also be made to the General
Directorate of Narcotics Control on 995 or by email at 995@gdnc.gov.sa. [@1] The
authorities said every report would be handled in strict confidence and the
person reporting would bear no liability. [@1]
