Officers from the Bielany criminal division detained the 16-year-old after a tip
that he was renting a Warsaw flat to store prohibited substances. [@1] After
about two hours of surveillance a Toyota pulled into a courtyard and the boy got
out, prompting officers to stop and search him. [@1] Foil packets of plant
material were found on the car floor and a search of the flat uncovered more
packages, some hidden in a cupboard beneath the refrigerator. [@2]

Laboratory tests confirmed the packages included more than 380 grams of
marijuana, the cannabis seized in the case. [@2] The same haul also held more
than 525 grams of mephedrone, a synthetic stimulant excluded from the cannabis
figures, and the combined weight of all the drugs exceeded 900 grams. [@1] After
his detention the boy was taken to a police juvenile holding room. [@1]

Police said the teenager would answer for possessing a significant quantity of
drugs, supplying prohibited substances to others and driving without a licence.
[@1] The case went to a family court, which ordered the boy to spend three
months in a juvenile shelter as a temporary measure. [@3] The family court will
later determine guilt and set any penalty, and there is a real possibility the
boy will be tried as an adult. [@1]
