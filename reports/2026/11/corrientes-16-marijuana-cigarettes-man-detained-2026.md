Officers of the Dirección General de Drogas Peligrosas y Crimen Organizado
detained an adult man in Corrientes on the night of 24 November 2026 and seized
marijuana found among his belongings. [@1] The officers acted after watching two
people making what appeared to be an exchange in a dark area and then
approaching them to establish their identities. [@1] One of the two proved to be
an adult man carrying a transparent bag that held 16 hand-rolled cigarettes
containing plant material. [@1] Officers carried out the corresponding
indicative test on that plant material, which returned a positive result for
marijuana. [@1]

Officers also seized a pair of scissors and plastic paper described as material
used for rolling the cigarettes. [@1] Following the seizure, the officers
notified the prosecutor's unit on duty, which took intervention in the case.
[@1] The detained man and everything seized were transferred to a police station
while the required further procedures continued. [@1] No second detention was
reported at the scene. [@1]
