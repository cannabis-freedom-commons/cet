Carabinieri of the Montesilvano company arrested a 44-year-old man from Pescara
who lived in Montesilvano, caught in the act of dealing in via Nilo near a
public park on the afternoon of 19 November 2026. [@1] The operation followed
reports from passers-by about suspected drug dealing in the area. [@1] When
officers from the operational and mobile radio unit arrived the man tried to
move away, but he was stopped and searched. [@1]

The personal search found about 8 grams of cocaine, two doses of heroin and one
dose of hashish. [@1] His waist pouch also held an electronic precision scale, a
folding knife about 15 centimetres long and 670 euros in cash in notes of
various denominations. [@1] A subsequent search of his home found pre-cut
plastic sheeting, apparently for packaging individual doses, and another wrapper
with about 4 grams of heroin. [@1] In total officers seized over 8 grams of
cocaine, 4 grams of heroin and one dose of hashish, from which investigators
said more than forty doses could have been prepared. [@1]

He was arrested on suspicion of possessing narcotic substances for the purpose
of dealing. [@3] A judge ordered him held under house arrest at his home pending
a validation hearing. [@1] The case is at the preliminary investigation stage
and the suspect is to be considered innocent until a final conviction. [@2]
