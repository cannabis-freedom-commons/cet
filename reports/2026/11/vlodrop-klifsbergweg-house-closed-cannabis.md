Mayor Jeffrey van Agtmaal closed a house on the Klifsbergweg in Vlodrop for half
a year on Tuesday. [@1] He is the mayor of Roerdalen and the closure was set at
six months. [@2]

About a month earlier, police found several drugs in the house, including
hennep, the Dutch name for cannabis, as well as XTC, MDMA powder and
amphetamine. [@1] The find was made on 15 October. [@2] Officers also found
stilettos, a machete and a jerrycan containing raw material for hard-drug
production in the house and the adjoining yard. [@2] A pistol was among the
items found. [@1]

The house was occupied without the owner's permission. [@1] The closure was
imposed under the Opiumwet and the house stays shut until 17 May. [@2]
