Fouh investigation officers arrested four people, including a woman, accused of
trafficking drugs in Fouh district. [@1] The three men, aged 36, 16 and 28, are
unemployed and live in Abu Ghunayma village in Sidi Salem. [@1] The 27-year-old
woman lives in Manshaat Abbas village in Sidi Salem and was detained with the
men in the Fouh district. [@1] Police said the first suspect had 16 prior theft
and drug cases, the third had a prior drug case and the woman had four prior
drug cases. [@1]

Officers seized 6 bags of hashish for sale, plus a bladed weapon and a sensitive
scale. [@2] The same search also found 240 heroin tickets and 150 ice tickets,
which are mentioned only to explain the mixed seizure. [@1] Case 9085 of 2026
was filed at Fouh police centre and the prosecution ordered all four held for
four days pending investigation. [@2]

Detectives acted after a tip to Fouh investigation chief Ahmed Metwally that the
group was selling drugs to users and dealers in Fouh and nearby villages. [@1]
After legal procedures were completed, a large security force led by Metwally
raided the suspects' location and detained them. [@1]
