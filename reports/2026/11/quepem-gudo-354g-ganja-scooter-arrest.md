Quepem Police arrested Reinbert Dias, a resident of Quepem, in connection with
the alleged possession of the contraband. [@1] The second accused was traced, interrogated and arrested, and his Royal Enfield Himalayan motorcycle was seized. [@3] Further investigation found the contraband was allegedly meant to be delivered to Adesh s/o Sambaji Patil, 26, a resident of Xelvona, Curchorem. [@3] Another account names the accused
as Rainbert Dias of Cotta, Amona, Quepem. [@2] The first accused is Rainbert s/o Thomas Dias, 24, a resident of Amona, Quepem. [@3] That account describes him as a
24-year-old. [@2]

Police seized 354 grams of a greenish leafy substance with flowering and
fruiting tops, suspected to be ganja, valued by police at approximately ₹35,400.
[@2] The seizure happened during a late-night operation near Gudo, within the
jurisdiction of the Avedem Village Panchayat, around 11:30 pm. [@1] Officials
allegedly recovered the substance from a Jupiter scooter during the raid. [@1] A
second account places the incident behind Santadurga Chamundeshwari Temple at
Gudo-Avedem, between 10:39 pm on 20 November and 12:35 am on 21 November 2026.
[@2] Police also seized ₹11,500 in cash, two mobile phones and one Jupiter
scooter, and attached the property under a panchanama. [@2] The total value of
the seized property was estimated at approximately ₹1,76,900. [@2]

Authorities registered an offence under relevant narcotics laws. [@1] Section 29 of the NDPS Act was subsequently added to the case. [@3] Quepem
Police Station registered the case on the complaint of PSI Siddesh Naik. [@2] PSI Siddesh Naik is conducting the investigation under the supervision of PI Prashal P. N. Dessai, SDPO Nilesh Rane and SP Akshat Aayush. [@3] Quepem Police Station registered the case as Crime No. 56/2026 on 21 November 2026 under Section 20(b)(ii)(A) of the NDPS Act, 1985. [@3]
Further investigation was under way to trace the source and intended
distribution of the drugs. [@1] Both accused were produced before the Judicial Magistrate First Class, Quepem, and remanded to three days of police custody. [@3]
