Police arrested two men for selling ganja near Guduvanchery. [@1] Police
received information on 14 November that unidentified persons were selling ganja
targeting college students near Nandivaram Malaimedu next to Guduvanchery. [@1]

A police team led by Guduvanchery Sub-Inspector Angusamy acted on the tip. [@1]
Those arrested were 42-year-old Muthuramalingam of Sivaganga district and
34-year-old Karuppusamy of Ramanathapuram. [@1] Officers seized one kilogram of
ganja from the two men. [@1]
