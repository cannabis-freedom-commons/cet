Police in Sherpur upazila of Bogura arrested 40-year-old Sobahan Mondal with
yaba tablets and ganja. [@1] Mondal, the son of Jamat Ali Mondal, is from the
Banmoricha (Bastipara) area. [@1] Officers raided his dwelling house in
Banmoricha (Bastipara) on the afternoon of Wednesday 18 November and arrested
him there. [@1]

Acting on secret information, a police team led by Sherpur Police Station
Sub-Inspector Kamrul Hasan carried out the raid at about 3:30pm on the
instructions of Officer-in-Charge Ekramul Hossain. [@1] Officers recovered 10
pieces of yaba tablets and 200 grams of ganja from Mondal's custody. [@1]

Police filed case No. 21 dated 18 November 2026 against him at Sherpur Police
Station under sections 10(a) and 19(a) of the Narcotics Control Act, 2018. [@1]
Police said he would be sent to the court after completion of legal process.
