Detectives from the PDI Criminal Investigation Brigade detained four people in
Papudo, Valparaíso region, in an operation targeting house robberies. [@1] Three
men were held as suspects in the robberies, while a woman was caught in
flagrante with marijuana and ketamine. [@1] After formalization, three of the
accused were ordered into preventive prison with a 120-day investigation period.
[@3]

The operation carried out three judicial entry and search orders at properties
in Papudo. [@2] Officers seized a vehicle allegedly used in the crimes and
clothing worn during their commission. [@1] Officers also seized 87.4 grams of
marijuana alongside 0.77 grams of ketamine. [@2] Police said the three men were
linked to night-time robberies inside homes and the woman to drug
microtrafficking. [@1]

During the searches officers found a war-mortar munition, prompting intervention
by the PDI explosives unit TEDAX. [@1] Specialists wearing anti-explosive suits
determined it was a US-made M90 mortar munition stripped of its explosive charge
and initiation mechanisms. [@1] On the prosecution's instructions the detainees
were placed at the disposal of the La Ligua Guarantee Court for detention
hearings. [@1]
