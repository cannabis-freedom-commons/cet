Civil and Military Police arrested two men during an anti-drug-trafficking
operation on Wednesday 25 November 2026 in Bom Conselho, in the Agreste region
of Pernambuco. [@1] The operation targeted members of a criminal organisation.
[@1] During the searches, José Pedro da Silva Cirilo, known as “Pedrinho”,
showed officers a place where drugs were hidden. [@1] Officers seized 59 grams
of maconha linked to him. [@1] The two detained men and all the seized material
were taken to the Civil Police station, where they were booked for drug
trafficking and criminal association for trafficking. [@1]

At the home of Douglas Vinicius de Oliveira Viana, officers found 3.117 kg of
maconha buried in the backyard. [@1] At another address linked to a third
suspect in the Vavá neighbourhood, officers found a further 1.010 kg of pressed
maconha. [@1] That man was not at the scene when the teams arrived and was not
found. [@1] Police put the total seizure at approximately 4.186 kg of maconha.
[@1]

The operation was coordinated by delegate James Karlos. [@1] It forms part of
the actions against drug trafficking and criminal organisations operating in the
municipality. [@1]
