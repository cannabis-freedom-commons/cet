Police arrested 12 alleged drug suppliers, including a woman, during a crackdown
in Rawalpindi. [@1] The arrests, including that of a woman, came in operations
across several areas of Rawalpindi. [@1] The crackdown was launched on the
directives of City Police Officer Dr Sardar Ghias Gul. [@1] A second account
describes the raids as recovering hashish from one woman and 11 men. [@3]

Gujar Khan Police arrested a female drug supplier and recovered 1.3kg of hashish
from her possession. [@1] Warish Khan Police held two suspects and recovered
3.26kg of hashish, while Race Course Police apprehended two drug suppliers and
seized 3.24kg. [@1] Pirwadhai Police arrested two suspects and recovered 2.45kg
of hashish, while Kallar Syedan Police arrested one drug supplier and seized
1.7kg and Mandra Police recovered 1.63kg from another suspect. [@1] Saddar Wah
Police recovered 1.4kg, Saddar Barooni Police seized 1.28kg, and Wah Cantt
Police recovered 1.2kg of hashish following the arrest of one suspect each. [@1]
The seizure was headlined as more than 17kg of hashish, with the station-level
amounts adding to about 17.46kg. [@1]

CPO Dr Sardar Ghias Gul said all possible measures were being taken to eliminate
the menace of drugs from society. [@2]
