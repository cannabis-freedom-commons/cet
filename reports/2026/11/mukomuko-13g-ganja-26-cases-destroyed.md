The Mukomuko District Attorney's Office destroyed evidence from 26 criminal
cases with final and binding court judgments on Wednesday 18 November 2026. [@1]
The destruction took place in the yard of the Kejari Mukomuko office and was
witnessed by related parties. [@1] It was witnessed by Mukomuko District Court
judge Gracia Wulandari, Mukomuko police narcotics unit head Iptu M. Setya Yuli
and a district government representative through health office secretary M. Zum.
[@1]

The evidence came from various cases, including narcotics misuse, gambling,
subsidised-fuel misuse and asusila cases. [@1] Among the destroyed evidence were
seven packages of sabu with a total weight of 14.9 grams and three packages of
ganja weighing 13.51 grams. [@1] Alongside the drugs, officers destroyed
clothes, carrier baskets, chicken cages, wallets, identity cards, buckets, a car
exhaust part and other items. [@1] A second account describes the same 26 files
as narcotics-misuse, theft, gambling and asusila cases. [@2] Flammable items
were destroyed by burning, while hard objects were crushed with a grinding tool.
[@2]

The prosecutor's office said it as executor was obliged to destroy evidence
designated for destruction in judgments with final legal force. [@1] The
exercise included the principal and students of SMAN 1 Mukomuko to give them a
direct understanding of criminal acts, especially narcotics. [@1]
