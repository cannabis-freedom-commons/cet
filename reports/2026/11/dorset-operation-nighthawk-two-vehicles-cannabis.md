Dorset Police officers took part in Operation Nighthawk as part of the Stay Safe
season. [@1] The patrols took place in Swanage, Wimborne and Weymouth last
weekend. [@1] A second local outlet reported that patrols were stepped up in
Weymouth, Swanage and Wimborne ahead of Christmas. [@2]

Two vehicles were stopped and searched and cannabis was located in them. [@1]
The matter was dealt with through community resolution. [@1] The AIR account
likewise reported that cannabis was found during searches of two vehicles. [@2]

Officers provided a visible presence in the three towns and spoke with staff at
licensed premises and people enjoying nights out. [@1] Teams responded to a
number of incidents during the patrols. [@1] Officers gave a man who was abusive
to door staff a section 35 dispersal order. [@1] He was told to leave the area.
[@1] Police also attended a concern for welfare. [@1] Patrols will continue in
towns across the county during night-time economy and in the build-up to the
festive season. [@1]
