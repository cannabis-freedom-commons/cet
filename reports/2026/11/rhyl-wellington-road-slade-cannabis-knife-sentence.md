Reece Slade, 28, of Vale Street, Stoke-on-Trent, was sentenced to 30 weeks’
imprisonment at Newcastle-under-Lyme Magistrates Court on Tuesday, November 10,
2026. [@1] He had previously admitted cannabis possession and was found guilty
in his absence of possessing a bladed article in public. [@1]

Police stopped him on Wellington Road in Rhyl on Monday, June 15 with a quantity
of cannabis and a knife. [@1] Magistrates jailed him for the two offences, and
the cannabis was ordered to be forfeited and destroyed while the knife was
confiscated from him. [@1]

The court described Slade as showing a “flagrant disregard for people and their
property”. [@1] It also cited his “poor compliance, a previous history of
offending, and previous convictions”. [@1] He will also pay a statutory
surcharge of £187, to be paid in full by July 1, 2027. [@1]
