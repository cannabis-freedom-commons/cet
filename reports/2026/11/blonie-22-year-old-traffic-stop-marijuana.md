Officers from the patrol-and-intervention unit at the police station in Błonie
stopped a Toyota driver for a road check during a patrol of the municipality.
[@2] The stop took place shortly before 16:00, when the driver's nervous
behaviour drew the officers' attention. [@1] The detained driver was a
22-year-old man. [@1] During the intervention the officers found dried plant
material, susz roślinny, on him, and a narcotics test confirmed that the
material was marijuana. [@1] Inspector Ewelina Gromek-Oćwieja, the police press
officer, said the patrol officers noticed the driver's behaviour and that the
intervention led to the discovery of marijuana. [@1]

The 22-year-old is to answer in court for possessing intoxicating substances.
[@1] Poland's drug-prevention law, the ustawa o przeciwdziałaniu narkomanii,
provides for up to three years in prison for possessing intoxicating or
psychotropic substances. [@1] The district prosecutor's office in Grodzisk
Mazowiecki is supervising the proceedings. [@1]
