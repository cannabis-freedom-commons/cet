A joint operation by the Support Group of the 2nd Military Police Battalion and
the Federal Police in Barra do Garças arrested a 20-year-old man in the Serrinha
neighbourhood on Friday 13 November 2026 on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@1]
The suspect was given arrest orders during the operation and police said he
already had a prior record linked to article 28 of the Drugs Law. [@1]

During the searches officers found five large tablets and one medium tablet of a
substance described as analogous to marijuana, plus a Samsung mobile phone. [@1]
Police put the total seizure at approximately 6 kg of the marijuana-like
substance. [@1] Investigators said the drug had been transported from Mato
Grosso do Sul to Barra do Garças, where it was to be distributed to supply the
local illegal drug trade. [@1]

The operation followed information-sharing between the security forces, with
support from the Local Intelligence Agency of the 2nd BPM and the Regional
Intelligence Agency of the 5th Regional Command. [@1] Police described the
seizure and arrest as another result of integration between the Military Police
and the Federal Police in combating drug trafficking through intelligence work
and institutional cooperation. [@1]
