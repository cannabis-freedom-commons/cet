Military police seized 74.55 kilograms of marijuana in four suitcases at the
Ponta Porã bus terminal on the afternoon of Monday 16 November 2026. [@1] The
suitcases were to be carried on an interstate bus running the Ponta Porã–São
Paulo route and were stopped before the vehicle left the terminal. [@1] Two
people had left the four bags with a bus-company employee to be loaded onto the
bus. [@1] Those responsible for the bags did not return to board the bus. [@1]
Ponta Porã is a municipality on the Mato Grosso do Sul border with Paraguay.
[@1]

The bus driver became suspicious because of the weight and smell of the bags,
took them off the vehicle and reported the matter to a company employee. [@1]
Military police were called on the 190 emergency line and, at about 17h50, a
team from the Sanga Puitã district was sent to the terminal to check the report.
[@1] At the terminal officers opened and inspected the bags and found various
volumes of marijuana inside. [@1] After weighing, the load totalled 74 kilos and
550 grams, distributed among the four bags. [@1]

The seizure took place before the bus departed, so the load was taken out of
circulation while still in Ponta Porã. [@1] All the marijuana was sent to the
First Civil Police Station of Ponta Porã, which is handling the legal
procedures. [@1] No arrest was recorded in the incident, and the two people who
left the bags did not return to the terminal. [@1] Military police took
statements from witnesses at the terminal, including the employees involved in
identifying the bags. [@1] The identification of the people responsible for the
load and the circumstances of the shipment still depend on investigation. [@1]
