Thirunagar police were carrying out a patrol in the Balaji Nagar area, along the
bank of the Thenkal irrigation tank, when the incident occurred. [@1] Two men
who were in that area ran off as soon as they saw the officers. [@1] The police
caught and questioned them and established that they were Bharath, 21, of Rajiv
Gandhi Nagar in Thirupparankundram, and Hariharan, 19, of Thenparankundram. [@1]

The two-wheeler they had arrived on was found to contain 8 kilograms of ganja,
the local name for cannabis. [@1] Officers seized the ganja and the two-wheeler,
arrested both men and began questioning them. [@1]
