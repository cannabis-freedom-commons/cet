Rajkot Range police arrested seven people in six cases during a three-day
anti-narcotics drive across four districts of Saurashtra. [@1] Officers checked
519 people, covering the Rajkot Rural, Jamnagar, Surendranagar and Devbhumi
Dwarka police districts. [@3] The drive ran on the instructions of Rajkot Range
deputy inspector general Nirlipt Rai, with separate teams from the Local Crime
Branch, the Special Operation Group, the Anti-Narcotics Wing and local police.
[@3]

In Devbhumi Dwarka police checked 156 people, found one case and seized 402
grams of ganja valued at Rs 20,100. [@2] Jamnagar police checked 238 people and
found two cases, seizing 4 kg 725 g of ganja worth Rs 2,36,250 along with 4.19
grams of MD powder worth Rs 41,900. [@1] Surendranagar police checked 90 people
and recorded three cases, seizing nine green ganja plants weighing 12 kg 16 g
and valued at Rs 6,08,000. [@2] Rajkot Rural police checked 35 people during the
same drive. [@5] Police put the total value of the property seized in the drive,
including the MD powder and other items, at Rs 14,32,220. [@2]

The six cases were registered under the NDPS Act, India's drug law, and all
seven arrested were booked in them. [@4] Cases were registered at the police
stations concerned, and police said legal action was under way. [@5] Police said
the drive targeted the illegal sale, storage, transport and consumption of
narcotics. [@2]
