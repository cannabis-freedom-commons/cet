A 32-year-old truck driver was arrested in flagrante, caught in the act, during
a check in the early hours of Monday 23 November 2026. [@1] The Polícia Militar
Rodoviária, São Paulo's highway military police, made the stop on the Rodovia
Presidente Castello Branco in Santana de Parnaíba. [@1] According to the
Secretaria da Segurança Pública (SSP), officers of the 5º Batalhão Rodoviário
decided to approach after seeing the truck change lanes constantly. [@1]

The driver said he had left Umuarama in Paraná and was carrying a load of
furniture. [@1] During the inspection officers found a black package hidden at
the rear of the cargo compartment. [@1] At an operational base they unloaded the
truck and found 88 fardos, or bales, of marijuana and another partially opened
package. [@1] Weighing put the drug at more than 1.2 tonnes, and the highway
police said the cargo was destined for Rio de Janeiro. [@1] The force estimated
the seizure represented a loss of more than 3 million reais to drug trafficking.
[@1]

The case was registered as drug trafficking at the Santana de Parnaíba police
station and the driver remained in custody at the disposal of the courts. [@1]
