Pakistan's Anti-Narcotics Force arrested seven suspects, including a woman,
during eight operations in cities across the country. [@1] UrduPoint gave the
same ANF action an Urdu headline about operations against drug smuggling with
seven suspects including a woman arrested. [@2] Cases were registered against
those held under the Control of Narcotic Substances Act, 1997, and further
investigation was under way. [@1] The woman held at the Sialkot bus terminal had
previously been convicted in a narcotics case, the ANF said. [@1]

In Nawaz Chali, Jiwani, in Gwadar district, officers recovered 246 kg of hashish
and 165 kg of crystal methamphetamine from a pickup vehicle, concealed in jerry
cans. [@1] At the Motorway Toll Plaza in Peshawar, officers recovered 120 kg of
hashish concealed in specially built hidden compartments of a truck. [@1] In
Islamabad, officers recovered 6 kg of hashish from a bus passenger and 9.6 kg of
hashish from hidden compartments in a vehicle, both at the Motorway Toll Plaza,
and in Sialkot recovered 1.34 kg of hashish from a man and a woman at a bus
terminal. [@1] The five separately stated hashish seizures of 246 kg, 6 kg, 120
kg, 9.6 kg and 1.34 kg total 382.94 kg of cannabis. [@1]

The Gwadar consignment was reportedly intended to be smuggled to Yemen through
speedboats and small vessels. [@1] The remaining seizures were non-cannabis
drugs excluded from the cannabis figure, including ice, opium, 23,030 narcotic
pills weighing 3.15 kg and 1.1 kg of ecstasy tablets hidden in speakers in a
Lahore courier parcel. [@1]
