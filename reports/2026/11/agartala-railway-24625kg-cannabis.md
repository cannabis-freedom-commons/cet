A joint Government Railway Police and Railway Protection Force operation was
launched at Agartala Railway Station on 13 November 2026 on specific
information. [@1] Officers apprehended the two men on Platform No. 1 on a
sitting bench in front of the parking area, between the RPF post and the
pay-and-use toilet. [@1] The two men were identified as 19-year-old Md Abul
Fazal of Karimganj district and 44-year-old Arabindu Das, latterly of Bishalgarh
in Sepahijala district. [@1]

Officers recovered a total of 24.625 kg of suspected ganja from a trolley bag
and a pithu bag in the men's possession. [@1] Police put the black-market value
of the seized ganja at about Rs 12.5 lakh. [@1] The seizure and arrests were
recorded under Agartala GRPS Case No. 2026GRP081 under Sections 20(b)(ii)(C)/29
of the NDPS Act, 1985. [@1]

Inspector Raju Bhowmik, officer in charge of Agartala Railway Police Station,
gave details of the operation to the media. [@1] The officer in charge said such
operations against illegal contraband would continue. [@1]
