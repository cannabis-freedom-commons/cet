Officers of the Volos traffic police department arrested a minor on the morning
of Friday 20 November 2026, after a police check established that he was
carrying narcotic substances. [@1] The arrested youth, whom police described as
a Greek national, had a single nylon bag in his possession containing 26 grams
of unprocessed cannabis. [@2] The officers seized the drug and arrested him for
violating the drug legislation. [@1]

The police announcement did not specify the minor's exact age, the place in
Volos where the check took place or the circumstances in which the cannabis was
found. [@1] It also made no reference to any evidence linking the case to
trafficking in narcotics. [@1] The case is being handled under the procedures
set out in the drug legislation and under the special provisions that apply to
minors. [@1]
