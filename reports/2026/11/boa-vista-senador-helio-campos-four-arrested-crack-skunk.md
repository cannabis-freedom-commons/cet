The Integrated Force to Combat Organized Crime in Roraima arrested four men in
an operation in the Senador Hélio Campos neighbourhood of Boa Vista on Monday 16
November 2026. [@1] The Federal Police said its FICCO unit arrested the four men
in flagrante that Monday during an anti-trafficking action in Boa Vista. [@2]
They were taken to the Regional Superintendence of the Federal Police in Roraima
and booked in flagrante for drug trafficking and association for trafficking,
with two also booked for resistance. [@1]

The operation followed weeks of investigation and monitoring of a property
suspected of serving as a drug sales point, with officers identifying
drug-trafficking movements and observing new transactions and two buyers at the
time of the action. [@2] During the operation officers caught new transactions
and stopped two people who had just bought drugs at the premises. [@3] During
the intervention some suspects tried to flee over walls and fences of
neighbouring homes, and two resisted arrest and discarded portions of drugs
while fleeing before being caught. [@1] Investigators said the property was used
habitually and shared by different traffickers selling at the same time. [@1]

Searches seized portions of crack and skunk, about R$800 in cash and four mobile
phones, and the four suspects were sent to the Federal Police and booked in
flagrante for trafficking and association. [@2] FICCO in Roraima brings together
the Federal, Civil, Military and Penal polices and the state Public Security
Secretariat. [@2]
