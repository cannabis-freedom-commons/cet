Officers from the district police headquarters in Minsk Mazowiecki and the
police station in Halinow detained two people who were carrying intoxicants and
psychotropic substances, with both interventions ending in drug seizures. [@2]
The first detention took place on a street in Minsk Mazowiecki, where
patrol-and-intervention officers found drugs on a 27-year-old woman. [@2]

Officers seized 4.89 grams of marijuana from the woman, together with 1.47 grams
of amphetamine and 0.97 grams of mephedrone. [@1] The same day, Halinow officers
detained a 29-year-old Ukrainian man who also had prohibited substances on him,
seizing 0.67 grams of amphetamine from him. [@1]

Police said the seized substances would undergo further procedural steps and
that both detainees would answer for their conduct before a court. [@3] Police
reminded that possessing intoxicants or psychotropic substances is prohibited in
Poland and carries criminal liability, with even a small amount able to bring
serious legal consequences. [@2]
