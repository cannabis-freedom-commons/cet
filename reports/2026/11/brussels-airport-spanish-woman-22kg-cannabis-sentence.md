A Spanish woman was sentenced to 40 months in prison for smuggling almost 22
kilograms of cannabis in her luggage through the airport at Zaventem. [@1]
Customs officers at Zaventem airport found almost 22 kilograms of cannabis in
her suitcase on 2 August. [@1] She had just arrived on a flight from Cambodia
when officers checked the suitcase. [@1]

The suitcase was registered in her own name, so she was unable to distance
herself from its contents. [@1] She told officers immediately that she would not
name the person who had commissioned her to carry the cannabis. [@1] Her defence
tried to secure a more lenient sentence by pointing to her personal
circumstances. [@1] Her defence said she had been a victim of partner violence
while living in Spain. [@1] Her defence said she wanted to start a new life with
her daughter but needed money for that, which was why she had accepted the
courier job. [@1]

The court nevertheless imposed the standard sentence of 40 months in prison.
