Police in Bakcharsky District, in Russia's Tomsk Oblast, detained a previously
unconvicted local man born in 1990 on suspicion of storing a narcotic. [@1]
Officers of the criminal investigation unit of the district police department
made the detention, the regional police press service said. [@1] The Tomsk
regional police reported the detention on 20 November. [@2]

The man, 36, had collected the tops and leaves of wild cannabis growing on land
behind his house in the district. [@3] In his garage he then boiled the plant
material, making a drug that he kept for his own use. [@4]

Officers seized more than 460 grams of the prohibited substance from him. [@1]
An examination showed that the seized drug was marijuana. [@2] The regional
police said a criminal case was opened over storage of narcotics in a large
amount. [@3] An investigator of the Bakcharsky district police opened the case
under part 2 of article 228 of the Russian Criminal Code. [@1] The offence
carries up to ten years in prison. [@1]
