Officers from the Directorate of Criminal Investigations in Kilifi South went to
the residence of Martin Katana Charo, alias Ndume, in Kuruwitu, Shariani area,
to arrest him over alleged malicious damage and grievous harm. [@1] Charo
spotted the detectives approaching his house and fled through a window into
nearby bushland, escaping arrest. [@1] Officers detected a strong smell of
cannabis from the premises, cordoned off the area and broke into the house with
a multi-agency team. [@1]

Inside the house the team found two-and-a-half sacks of cannabis rolls and
four-and-a-half sacks of loose and rolled cannabis, weighing about 315.5
kilogrammes in total. [@1] Police valued the recovered cannabis at Sh9,465,000.
[@1] Officers also recovered three empty sacks, the suspect's identity card, a
solar table lamp and materials believed to have been used in packaging the
drugs. [@1] Crime scene personnel documented the scene and the exhibits were
secured for further investigation. [@1] The suspect remains at large while
detectives continue investigations. [@1]

The DCI said its Kilifi South officers had traced the individual to his
residence in Kuruwitu, Shariani area, to arrest him over claims of malicious
damage and grievous harm. [@2] The DCI account says the search uncovered two and
a half sacks of large cannabis rolls and another four and a half sacks of loose
and rolled cannabis, weighing 315.5 kilogrammes and valued at Ksh9,465,000. [@2]
The DCI account says the premises may have been used to store and package
cannabis before distribution. [@2]
