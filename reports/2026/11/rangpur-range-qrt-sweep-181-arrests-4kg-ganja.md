Police in Rangpur Range arrested at least 181 people in a special operation of
the Quick Response Team carried out across all eight districts of the range in
north-western Bangladesh. [@1] The Rangpur Range DIG's media cell gave the
results to reporters on Sunday afternoon, saying the arrests were made in the
previous 24 hours. [@1] Those held were listed as suspected drug dealers, wanted
and absconding accused, thieves and members of theft gangs, robbers, fraudsters
and members of banned organisations. [@1] The total covers every offence in the
sweep, not drug cases alone. [@1]

During the operation officers seized 4 kg of marijuana. [@1] The same recovery
included 175 yaba tablets and 68 tapentadol tablets, neither of them a cannabis
product, and both are left out of the cannabis figures. [@1] The operation ran
under the overall supervision of Rangpur Range DIG Mohammad Abdul Mabud. [@1]
