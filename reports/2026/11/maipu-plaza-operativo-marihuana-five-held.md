Four people were detained during the operation after bags of marijuana were
found in the vehicle they were travelling in. [@1] Carabineros' tally identified
four of those held as detained over breaches of the drugs law, with the
remaining person the operator of the clandestine casino. [@2] In one procedure
Carabineros checked a vehicle carrying three men, and the OS-7 drug-detection
dog Oso found drugs inside it, after which the occupants were detained. [@2]

The inspection operation was carried out in the main plaza, on Avenida 5 de
Abril, by Carabineros' OS-7 unit and municipal officials. [@1] Public Security
Minister Martín Arrau led the operation, which ended with five detentions, a
clandestine casino dismantled and about twenty vehicles taken off the road. [@1]
The operation took place amid criticism from the municipality over the
ministry's communication about the schedule of these procedures. [@3]

Officers also seized 49 gaming machines from the clandestine casino, valued at
about 80 million pesos. [@1] Twenty vehicles, motorcycles and cars, were taken
off the road for not meeting the rules for circulating in public. [@1] Those
detained were to appear at a detention-control hearing on Thursday before
Santiago's 9th Guarantee Court. [@2]
