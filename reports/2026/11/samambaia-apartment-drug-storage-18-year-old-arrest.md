Military police of the Distrito Federal shock battalion arrested an 18-year-old
on suspicion of drug trafficking on 15 November 2026 in Samambaia. [@1]
Intelligence information had indicated that a property in the area was being
used to store narcotics. [@1] While monitoring the building, officers identified
a youth in a pink shirt and black shorts leaving the premises and called in the
ostensive team to approach him. [@1] The suspect was carrying drug portions and
a key matching one of the building's residences and initially denied any link to
the property. [@1]

With a resident's authorisation, police entered the building, smelled a strong
odour of marijuana and found four tablets and two smaller portions of skunk,
nine portions of hashish, five of marijuana and a small quantity of cocaine.
[@1] Officers also seized a mobile phone during the operation. [@1]

The youth had no criminal record and was arrested in flagrante and taken with
all the collected material to the 26th Police Station. [@1] The case was
presented to the Distrito Federal Civil Police for further legal measures. [@1]
