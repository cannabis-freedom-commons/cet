Gendarmes arrested two men, a minor and an adult living in the Vienne, on 17
November 2026 on suspicion of running a drug trafficking operation between the
Vienne and the Deux-Sèvres. [@1] The Poitiers public prosecutor's office set out
the case on 20 November 2026. [@2]

The case began on 3 October 2026 when a driver refused to stop at
Chasseneuil-du-Poitou, ended up in a ditch and fled, leaving two mobile phones
and a bank card behind. [@1] The account holder for the abandoned card was ruled
out, but the phone line used to open the account was tied to drug trafficking,
the prosecutor's office said. [@2] Interviews with users and the bank movements
established deliveries of several tens of thousands of euros of drugs, mainly
cocaine, sold through social media across the two departments. [@2] Searches of
their homes found cocaine, cannabis resin and herbal cannabis, mobile phones and
cash at the minor's home. [@1]

The prosecutor's office said the minor, already known to the courts, played a
leading role in the trafficking, while the adult admitted helping by collecting
payments from buyers. [@2] The adult was charged with money laundering and drug
use and summoned to a plea-bargain hearing on 4 February 2027. [@1] The minor
was charged with moving, holding, offering or supplying, buying and using drugs,
refusing to give the unlock code for his phone, driving without a licence and
failing to stop. [@3] The prosecutor asked for the minor to be held in pre-trial
detention. [@1] The minor is to be tried by the children's court on 8 December
2026. [@1]
