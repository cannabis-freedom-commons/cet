Navy personnel inspecting two cargo vehicles at the port of Mazatlán, Sinaloa,
detained one man and seized presumed drugs, six containers of vape liquid and
540 glass pipes, the Secretaría de Marina said. [@1] The first finding came
during a routine check of a vehicle that had entered the port to carry out cargo
handling. [@1] Inside, naval personnel found a bag of approximately 24 grams of
a granular substance with characteristics similar to methamphetamine, along with
a pipe bearing residue of a presumably illicit substance. [@1] The driver was
detained for that reason and the evidence was placed under the authorities'
guard. [@1]

During a second inspection of another cargo vehicle, personnel of the Naval Unit
for Port Protection (Unaprop) found a box containing 11 packages of dry herb and
two more packages of presumed marijuana. [@1] Officers also seized six
containers of liquid for vapes and 540 glass pipes that could be used to consume
narcotics. [@1]

Both findings were recorded at the Mazatlán port facility during screening
filters maintained by the Mexican Navy in its coast-guard role. [@1] The Navy
coordinated with the Fiscalía General de la República (FGR) to place the
detainee, the vehicles and the seized objects before the Federal Public
Prosecutor. [@1] That authority will assemble the investigation files and
determine the corresponding responsibilities. [@1]
