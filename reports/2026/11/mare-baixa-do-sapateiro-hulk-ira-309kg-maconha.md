Police sniffer dogs Hulk and Ira helped officers locate another drug hideout in
the Complexo da Maré, in Rio's North Zone, on Tuesday 17 November 2026. [@1] The
two Belgian Malinois sniffer dogs took part in a Batalhão de Ações com Cães
(BAC) operation in the Baixa do Sapateiro community, where the drugs were kept
in a bunker used for storage. [@1] Officers seized approximately 309 kilograms
of marijuana at the site. [@1] The case was sent to the 21st police station in
Bonsucesso. [@2]

Officers also seized skank and ice at the hideout, but no quantities for those
two substances were reported. [@1] The seizure was part of an integrated
operation in the Maré complex that marked the second consecutive day of actions
against the Terceiro Comando Puro (TCP) there and affected classes in 43 schools
in the area. [@2] During the same Tuesday operation, the Batalhão de Operações
Especiais (Bope) also found a workshop with a machine capable of producing up to
3,000 rifles per year. [@2]

Hulk had already helped locate almost 48 tonnes of marijuana in an improvised
bunker in Maré in April, which the Military Police described as the largest
seizure in the country in a single operation. [@1] Twelve days before the new
find, on 5 November, Hulk was honoured with a statue unveiled during the Medalha
de Mérito Cinófilo ceremony at the Comando de Operações Especiais headquarters
in Ramos. [@1]
