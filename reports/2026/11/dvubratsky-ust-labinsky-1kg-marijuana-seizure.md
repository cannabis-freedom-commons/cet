Police searched the apartment of a 55-year-old resident of Dvubratsky settlement
in Ust-Labinsky District, Krasnodar Krai. [@1] The search targeted the man's own
flat, where he was keeping the seized material in his apartment. [@1]

Inside the flat, officers found a large plastic container holding glass jars
previously used for caviar and pickles. [@1] Officers found six such jars that
the man had packed with plant material, along with a packet containing the same
material. [@1] The jars and the packet were seized during the search of the
apartment. [@1] According to preliminary data, the material was marijuana with a
total weight of about 1 kg. [@1]

The seized substance was sent for forensic examination, so the preliminary
identification and weight were still to be confirmed. [@1] Police said the man's
actions showed signs of the criminal offence of illegal acquisition, storage and
manufacture of narcotic drugs. [@1] The account of the search and the seizure
was attributed to the press service of the Krasnodar Krai Main Directorate of
the Interior Ministry. [@1]
