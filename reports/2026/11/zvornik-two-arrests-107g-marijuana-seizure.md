Police in Zvornik arrested two people from the town, identified only as D. K.
and M. K., on suspicion of unauthorised production and trafficking of narcotic
drugs. [@1] The Zvornik Police Administration said the arrest was carried out at
about 23:00 on the night before the 14 November 2026 publication, placing the
operation on 13 November. [@1]

During the search officers seized three PVC bags of green plant material
resembling marijuana, with a total weight of 107.1 grams, plus other objects
linked to the offence. [@1] In the same search officers also seized two PVC bags
of white powder suspected to be amphetamine, weighing 1,809.23 grams, which is
not counted in the cannabis figures. [@1]

Officers searched the suspects, a flat and a vehicle they use under a Basic
Court in Zvornik order and with the prior approval of the competent prosecutor.
[@1] The second account identifies the approving authority as the District
Public Prosecutor's Office in Bijeljina. [@2] After criminal processing, the
suspects were to be handed over to the competent prosecutor's office with a
report on the offence. [@2]
