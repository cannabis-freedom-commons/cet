Ten people were detained during a security operation in the municipality of
Piedras Negras. [@1,2] Soldiers of the Mexican Army and the National Guard carried
out the intervention together with state and municipal forces. [@1,2] The commands
of the XI Military Region and the 47th Military Zone said the actions took place
on 20 November 2026 under the National Public Security Strategy, in coordination
with the State Prosecutor's Office and state and municipal police. [@1,2]

The detentions were made while executing an investigation order, and officers
also secured a property that was placed at the disposal of the authorities. [@1,2]
Officers found 32 doses of a substance with characteristics similar to
methamphetamine and 14 doses of green herb with characteristics similar to
marijuana, for a total of 46 doses of possible narcotics. [@1,2] Authorities said
the seized substances must undergo expert analysis to determine their
composition while investigations into the detainees continue. [@1,2]

After the intervention, the ten detainees, the property and the seized
substances were placed at the disposal of the State Prosecutor's Office in
Piedras Negras for further proceedings and to determine the legal situation of
those involved. [@1,2] The military commands said such operations are part of
coordinated work among security institutions at the three levels of government
to combat crime and strengthen security in the border region. [@1]
