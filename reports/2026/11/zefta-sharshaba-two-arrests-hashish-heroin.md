Police from the investigations unit of Zifta police station in Gharbia
Governorate arrested two alleged drug dealers in a security campaign across the
villages of the markaz. [@1] Officers said the two men ran a wide trade in ice,
hydro and hashish on village roads and inside the villages, selling to young
people in Sharshaba, in neighbouring villages and in the town. [@1]

The first man, from Sharshaba and known to police, was carrying a large quantity
of hashish prepared for sale, cash from the trade, a mobile phone and a
motorcycle used for selling it. [@1] El-Dostor reported that the hashish suspect
was taken to the Zifta police station with the seized items and that the public
prosecution took over the investigation. [@2]

The second man, also from Sharshaba and wanted in several cases, was carrying
heroin and ice, cash from the trade, two mobile phones and a knife. [@3]
Vetogate, covering the same arrests, said both men held large quantities of ice
and hydro, described as a newer drug, for sale inside Sharshaba. [@4]
