In Noida, police carried out an enforcement action against ganja smuggling that
covered two separate police-station areas and recovered ganja in both places.
[@1] During that action, police took into custody a young man and a juvenile
delinquent aged about 17 years. [@1] Phase-1 station police caught 23-year-old
Suraj in Sector-8 with 1.15 kg of ganja. [@1]

According to police, Suraj bought the ganja from a man named Sukhbir and used to
make small packets for sale in slum settlements and the factory area. [@1]
Separately, Sector-24 station police arrested a juvenile delinquent in Sector-11
with 1.12 kg of ganja. [@1] Police said that teenager used to sell ganja at high
prices to daily-wage labourers and people who use drugs. [@1]

Because the juvenile was aged about 17 years, police called a child welfare
officer to the spot at the time of the seizure. [@1] Assistant Commissioner of
Police Mayank Tiwari said further legal proceedings were under way in both
cases. [@1]
