Two more arrests were made in Gisborne over the weekend after police carried out
six more searches under the current Gang Conflict Warrant. [@2,3] Police laid four
charges against the two arrested people, aged 17 and 53. [@2,3] The pair appeared
in Gisborne District Court and Youth Court on charges of possession of utensils,
possession for supply and possession of an offensive weapon. [@2,3]

Police seized small amounts of methamphetamine and cannabis, along with a knife.
[@2,3]

Police said they are committed to holding those responsible for these offences
to account. [@1] Police said officers are out in the communities ready to
respond to any unlawful behaviour by gangs and anyone who believes they are
above the law. [@1] Police said the public will continue to see an increased
police presence in and around Gisborne. [@1] Police asked anyone who witnesses
anything to ring 111, or 105 after the fact. [@1] Police said anonymous
information can be provided through Crime Stoppers on 0800 555 111. [@2]
