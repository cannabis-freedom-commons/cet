Gendarmes from Merdrignac ended a drug trafficking operation in Trémorel, a
commune of about 1,150 people in the south of Côtes-d'Armor, after a five-month
investigation. [@1] The brigade had been investigating reports of an active
trafficking operation on the voie verte in Trémorel, a former railway path,
since July 2026. [@2] Investigators identified a 30-year-old man living in the
area as the supplier, delivering several types of drugs by electric scooter
along the voie verte, the Saint-Brieuc gendarmerie company said. [@1] The man
was absent when gendarmes searched his home on Monday 16 November 2026 and he
turned himself in at the brigade two days later. [@1] He was held in police
custody, brought before the Saint-Brieuc prosecutor on 20 November 2026 and
placed in pre-trial detention. [@1] Judged in immediate appearance on Monday 23
November 2026, he was sentenced to 24 months in prison, with a four-month
suspended probationary sentence revoked, and remained in detention. [@1]

At the house, gendarmes seized 170 g of cannabis resin and 50 g of cocaine, a
haul the gendarmerie said was worth several thousand euros at resale. [@2]
Officers also found equipment used to cut and prepare the drugs and a scooter
that could have been the one used for deliveries. [@1] They seized several
bladed weapons, a tear-gas canister, a telescopic baton and an air rifle. [@1]
The search also turned up luxury items, including two watches valued at a total
of 75,000 euros and perfume sets valued at more than 4,000 euros. [@1] With a
dog unit specialised in drug searches, gendarmes searched the homes of four of
the dealer's customers, where a small quantity of cannabis resin was found. [@1]

The man was already known to the courts for offences including road traffic
offences and carrying prohibited weapons. [@1]
