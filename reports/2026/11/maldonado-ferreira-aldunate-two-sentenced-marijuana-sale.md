The two occupants of the house, identified by the initials C.N.M.P., aged 20,
and T.T.P., aged 18, with no criminal record, were detained at the property.
[@1] After appearing before the court on Friday 13 November, both were convicted
as criminally responsible for a continued offence of supplying narcotic
substances and given a sentence of two years in prison. [@1]

The case was investigated over several weeks by officers of the Investigations
Area of Operational Zone II under the direction of the 5th Turn Prosecutor's
Office, as reported by the Maldonado Police Headquarters to FM Gente. [@1]
Intelligence work confirmed that drugs were being sold from a house on Avenida
Ferreira Aldunate in the northern zone of the departmental capital. [@1]

With the evidence gathered, the Prosecutor's Office obtained a search warrant
that was executed on 12 November. [@1] During the inspection police seized a jar
and two hermetic-seal bags containing marijuana, although no weight for the
seized substance was reported. [@1] Officers also seized two precision scales, a
mobile phone and a compressed-air pistol. [@1]
