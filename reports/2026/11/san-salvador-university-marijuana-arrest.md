Agents of the Policía Nacional Civil, El Salvador's national civil police,
arrested César Danilo López Hernández, 34, inside a higher-education centre in
San Salvador on Friday 6 November 2026. [@1] César Danilo López Hernández, 34, will stay in detention after an initial hearing in the case over marijuana sales inside a San Salvador university. [@4] Police said he was carrying
marijuana and cash hidden in a backpack. [@1] A field test was positive for marijuana, which weighed 76.5 grams and was valued at $270.39 on the illegal market. [@4]

According to the police report, officers located him while he was acting in a
way they called suspicious, and he tried to flee when he noticed them but was
detained. [@1] According to the official report, he was with other men when the university's private security intervened, he tried to flee and resisted a search, and the security staff called the National Civil Police, who found nine small portions of marijuana on him, on 5 November 2026. [@4]

The man will be referred for the alleged offence of possession and holding with
intent to traffic, the initial stage of the case. [@1] He is being prosecuted for drug trafficking under article 33 of the Ley Reguladora para las Actividades Relativas a la Droga. [@4] The institution did not
publicly identify the university, nor state how much marijuana or money was
seized. [@1] El Mundo reported, on unofficial information, that he was detained inside the Universidad de El Salvador (UES). [@4] Police did not say whether the detainee is a student, employee or
visitor of the institution. [@1] He also carried $624.05 in cash and a mobile phone. [@4]

El Mundo reported that police also seized a mobile phone from him. [@2] The
arrest was made inside the grounds of a university in the San Salvador district,
the police said. [@2] The detainee was to be placed at the disposal of the
courts for possession and holding with intent to traffic. [@2]

According to El Mundo, the university arrest came earlier the same morning as two other marijuana arrests in the capital, at a home in Santa Tecla and in the case of the couple accused of using minors. [@3]
