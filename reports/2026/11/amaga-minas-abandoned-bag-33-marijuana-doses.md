No one was arrested after police in Amagá found an abandoned bag holding drug
doses in the corregimiento of Minas, Villa Diana sector, on Friday 13 November
2026. [@1] Inside were 47 doses of bazuco, 40 doses of cocaine and 33 doses of
marijuana. [@1] Officers found the bag during surveillance and patrol work in
that sector of the municipality. [@1]

The Antioquia Police Department said its officers found and seized 120 doses in
the Minas corregimiento, listing 47 of bazuco, 40 of cocaine hydrochloride and
33 of marijuana. [@2] The substances were seized and left at the disposal of the
competent authority for the judicial process. [@1] The account reports no
arrests and does not say who owned the bag. [@1] The quantities were given as
doses of each substance, with no weight stated. [@1]
