Officers of the Drug Control Department of the Interior Ministry for the
Republic of Adygea, working with state traffic inspectors, detained a
62-year-old local resident while following up operational information, the
ministry's press service said. [@1] The officers identified a Maykop resident
who had been illegally storing banned substances, and the suspect was detained.
[@2] On a street in Maykop police stopped a domestically produced car driven by
the suspect. [@1] During a personal search they found a packet of dried plant
matter on the 62-year-old man. [@2] Officers later seized a similar substance at
the detained man's home. [@1]

Forensic experts of the republican Interior Ministry concluded that the seized
substance was marijuana and that its total mass exceeded 43 grams. [@1] A
criminal case was opened against him under Part 1 of Article 228 of the Criminal
Code of Russia, which covers illegal acquisition, storage, transportation,
manufacture or processing of narcotics, psychotropic substances or their
analogues. [@2] The suspect is held in a detention centre, and the article
provides for punishment of up to three years' imprisonment. [@1] The press
service of the Interior Ministry for the Republic of Adygea said he faces up to
three years' loss of liberty. [@2]
