Customs officers at Kempegowda International Airport in Devanhalli, Bengaluru
arrested five passengers who arrived on flights from abroad. [@1] The
interceptions took place at Kempegowda International Airport near Devanhalli,
outside Bengaluru. [@1] Cases were registered against the arrested passengers
under the drug prevention law and investigation is continuing. [@1]

From a passenger who arrived from Malaysia, officers seized 10 kg 180 g of
high-grade cannabis, which customs valued at Rs 3.56 crore. [@1] From another
passenger who arrived from Malaysia, officers seized 1 kg 400 g valued at Rs 51
lakh, and from a passenger who arrived from Singapore, 5 kg 300 g valued at Rs
1.85 crore. [@1] From another passenger who arrived from Malaysia, officers
seized 6 kg 900 g valued at Rs 2.41 crore. [@1] Customs officers also arrested a
passenger who had smuggled high-grade cannabis from Thailand. [@1] Altogether
officers seized high-grade cannabis valued at about Rs 10 crore from the five
passengers. [@1]

The first Malaysia arrival aroused suspicion and a search of his belongings
found the high-grade cannabis. [@1]
