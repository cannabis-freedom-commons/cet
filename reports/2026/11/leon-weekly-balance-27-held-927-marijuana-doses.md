León police counted 27 detentions for possession of marijuana in the week's
tally, alongside 263 for crystal possession and nine for robbery of shops. [@1]
The weekly report, given at the corps' parade, counted 1,671 people detained in
the municipality. [@1] Of the rest, 1,325 people were referred to civil courts
for administrative faults, including 179 for consuming narcotics in public. [@1]

The balance covered 15 to 21 November 2026, when authorities seized 2,072 doses
of illicit substances in the municipality. [@1] Of those, 927 doses were
marijuana, while 1,112 were crystal and 33 were cocaine and piedra base. [@1]
Officers also seized eight firearms, including a long weapon reserved for the
army, with five magazines and 106 live rounds. [@1] The force reported
recovering 18 vehicles with active theft reports: 14 motorcycles, three cars and
vans and one tractor-trailer. [@1]

Meganoticias reported the same weekly figures, attributing them to the León
municipal police and the security secretariat. [@2]
