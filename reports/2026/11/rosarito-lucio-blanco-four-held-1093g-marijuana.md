Officers detained four men in colonia Lucio Blanco, Playas de Rosarito, as part
of the Rosarito Seguro operation. [@1,3] The stop happened at about 22:58 on 22
November on Valentín Ruíz Pimentel street, where Fuerza Estatal de Seguridad
Ciudadana agents with federal authorities held four occupants of a white
Chevrolet Tahoe. [@1] They were identified as Cristian Ulises N, 31, José María
N, 28, both from Tijuana, Hirán Armando N, 36, from Obregón, Sinaloa, and Adán
N, 46, from Durango. [@1] Cristian Ulises N and Adán N are former members of the
Playas de Rosarito municipal police. [@1,3] One of the two former officers was suspended in 2012 and the other left the force the previous year after failing the examinations of the Centro de Control y Confianza (C3), the police trust-control centre. [@3] A second account identifies the four
as alleged hitmen of the Los Rusos cell affiliated with the Sinaloa Cartel. [@2,3] State Citizen Security Secretary Laureano Carrillo Rodríguez said the detainees formed part of a cell linked to brothers René Arzate García, alias El Rana, and Alfonso Arzate García, alias El Aquiles. [@3]

In the vehicle officers found four short firearms, a UZI-type calibre .35, a
revolver and two pistols, one calibre .380 and one 9mm, plus a semi-automatic
long gun with telescopic sight, retractable stock and laser, and a shotgun. [@1]
Officers also found eight magazines, five of them holding 28 usable cartridges
in total, and two ballistic vests. [@1] They also seized a package of green, dry
herb with characteristics similar to marijuana weighing about 1,093 grams. [@1,3]

Preliminary information said the detainees could belong to a criminal cell
active in the area. [@1] The four people, with the weapons, magazines,
cartridges, vests, suspected drugs and vehicle, were placed at the disposal of
the Attorney General of the Republic, which will investigate and determine their
legal situation. [@1,3] The operation was supported by the Defence Ministry, the
Navy and the federal Citizen Security Ministry. [@1]
