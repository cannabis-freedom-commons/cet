Police in Pokhara arrested four people, including 21-year-old Yamray Chhetri, on
suspicion of possessing about 700 grams of ganja, the Nepali word for marijuana,
on the evening of 19 November 2026. [@2] Officers from the Shishuwa area police
station searched Chhetri's room in Pokhara Metropolitan-26 and seized the drug.
[@2] The four were among 17 people arrested across Nepal that Thursday in a
roundup covering Jhapa, Kaski, Lalitpur, Saptari, Sunsari and Rupandehi
districts. [@1]

Ten of those arrested were in Kaski district. [@1] A separate arrest in
Pokhara-15 involved two people with 4.2 grams of a heroin-like substance and 11
tramadol tablets. [@1] The roundup also turned up brown heroin and brown sugar
alongside prescription medicines. [@2] In Rupandehi two Indian nationals were
arrested with 329 ampoules of diazepam, 336 of buprenorphine and 337 of
promethazine after being stopped on a motorcycle entering Nepal from India. [@1]

Two people were arrested in Damak-8, Jhapa, with 1.47 grams of a heroin-like
substance and 27,000 rupees. [@1] Three were held in Mahalakshmi-5, Lalitpur,
with 7.31 grams of a heroin-like substance and 18,950 rupees. [@1] Police said
investigations into the cases were ongoing. [@1]
