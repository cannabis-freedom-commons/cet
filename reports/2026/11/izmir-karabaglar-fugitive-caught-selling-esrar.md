Police teams of the Karabaglar district security directorate located the hiding
address of 25-year-old fugitive M.B., who was wanted on a final theft sentence
of 21 years and 7 months. [@3] Officers caught M.B. selling drugs at that
address, inside a house in Karabaglar, Izmir. [@1] Police moved after learning
the address where the theft convict was hiding, according to the DHA account.
[@1]

A search of the address seized 1,225 synthetic pharmaceutical tablets, some
esrar and a precision scale. [@2] The Arabic account describes the tablets as
synthetic medicines used for abuse, the cannabis as hashish and the scale as the
kind usually used to weigh narcotics. [@1] Officers also seized 4,645 Turkish
lira assessed as proceeds of the sales. [@2]

Police opened a separate drug-trafficking case against M.B., and his custody
procedures were continuing. [@2]
