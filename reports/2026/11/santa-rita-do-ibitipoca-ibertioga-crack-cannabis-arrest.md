Military Police officers on patrol approached an 18-year-old acting suspiciously
in the centre of Santa Rita do Ibitipoca on the afternoon of Friday 13 November
2026. [@1] A personal search of the youth found nothing illicit. [@1] The youth
said he was from Ibertioga and had arrived by app-based car with a 16-year-old
friend. [@1]

Military Police officers in Ibertioga told the patrol about reports linking both
youths to drug trafficking. [@1] Officers sweeping the area around the stop
found six plastic wrappers holding 57 crack rocks near a wall. [@1] When
questioned, the youth confessed he had bought the drugs to resell them in
Ibertioga with his friend. [@1]

Officers then went to the author's residence in Ibertioga and seized one bucha,
a lump, of maconha, the Brazilian Portuguese word for marijuana, along with the
mobile phone he was carrying. [@1] Officers arrested the author in flagrante for
drug trafficking and took him with the seized materials to the on-duty Civil
Police station in Barbacena. [@1]
