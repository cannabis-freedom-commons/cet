Military Police arrested three men aged 19, 33 and 53 for drug trafficking on
Tuesday 17 November 2026 in the Cantinho do Céu neighbourhood of Mutum in
eastern Minas Gerais. [@1] Officers launched an operation on Rua Domingos
Barcelos after receiving information about the sale of drugs in the area. [@1]
During a search of one residence officers found cannabis tablets, money and
mobile phones. [@1]

Officers then went to a second property in the same operation and located more
portions of the drug. [@1] In total police seized 79 pressed tablets and three
small lumps of maconha, the Portuguese word for cannabis. [@2] Police said they
also seized R$867 in cash and three mobile phones. [@3]

Officers also seized a red Honda Pop 110 motorcycle during the operation. [@3]
The three men were arrested in the act and taken with all the seized materials
to the Civil Police station in Manhuaçu for further legal proceedings. [@1] Both
searches took place in two houses in the same Cantinho do Céu neighbourhood.
