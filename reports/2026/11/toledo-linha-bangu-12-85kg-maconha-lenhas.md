A ROTAM team of the Military Police's 19th Battalion in Toledo, Paraná, seized
the drug on the morning of 17 November 2026 at a rural property in Linha Bangu,
in the district of Concórdia do Oeste, in the interior of Toledo. [@1] Police
were told that a man doing maintenance work on the property had found a quantity
of a substance analogous to marijuana hidden among firewood. [@1] The property
was rented to a third party. [@1] After weighing, the seizure totalled 12.85 kg
of marijuana and nobody was arrested. [@1]

During the search officers also found three mobile phones. [@1] The drug and the
phones were seized and taken to the 20th Police Subdivision in Toledo, where
they were presented to the competent authorities for the applicable procedures.
[@1] Rádio Educadora reported that the phones might be analysed and that, with
the tenant identified, the Civil Police saw a possibility of identifying those
responsible for the drug. [@2]
