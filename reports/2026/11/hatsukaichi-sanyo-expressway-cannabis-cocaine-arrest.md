Police arrested a 20-year-old unemployed man with no fixed address on suspicion
of possessing drugs for sale. [@1] Police say the man allegedly possessed
cannabis and cocaine for sale inside a car on the inbound Sanyo Expressway in
Hatsukaichi City in March this year. [@1]

The man allegedly collided with the car in front and then left his car on the
expressway and fled. [@1] The drugs were found inside the car he had abandoned
on the expressway. [@2]

Police put the street value of the seized drugs at more than 3.4 million yen.
[@1] The man has remained silent during police questioning while police
investigate the supply route and other possible offences. [@1]
