Washim district police carried out a midnight special operation across the
district against illegal trade, drug trafficking, illegal weapons and absconding
suspects. [@1] The drive ran from 17 to 18 November 2026. [@1] Teams from police
stations across the district and from the district's local crime branch worked
together in the operation. [@1] Officers seized 7 kg 250 g of a substance
resembling ganja, made up of leaves, flowers and seeds, the police said. [@1] A
case was registered against one suspect over that seizure. [@2]

In the same drive police carried out two separate actions under the arms law
against people keeping illegal weapons. [@1] Officers arrested people named in
non-bailable warrants issued by courts and arrested suspects wanted in pending
cases. [@1] Pudhari reported that 25 officers and 110 constables took part in
the drive. [@1] Tarun Bharat, citing information from the superintendent of
police's office, put the force at 15 officers and 110 constables. [@2] The
operation was carried out under Superintendent of Police Nikesh Khatmode-Patil
and Additional Superintendent of Police Lata Phad. [@1]

Police said similar special drives would continue against illegal trade, drug
trafficking, illegal weapons and wanted suspects. [@1]
