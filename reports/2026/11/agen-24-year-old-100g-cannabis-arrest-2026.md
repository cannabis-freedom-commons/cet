On Saturday 14 November, during the daytime, Agen police arrested a 24-year-old
man in possession. [@1] The man arrested was a holder carrying 100 grams of
cannabis. [@1]

Separately, a 46-year-old man seized a large kitchen knife and struck a man who
had been his friend moments earlier. [@1] The victim suffered head and hand
wounds described as not serious. [@1] That attacker was placed in police custody
in a separate action from the cannabis arrest. [@1]
