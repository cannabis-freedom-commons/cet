Officers searched the bathhouse and the attic of the shed belonging to a
villager in Torbeyevsky District, Mordovia. [@2] Inside they found a dried
cannabis bush and loose plant material on cardboard, the Interior Ministry for
Mordovia said of the 36-year-old local man's outbuildings. [@1] The seized
material was marijuana with a total mass of 291.4 grams. [@1] The man was
charged under part 2 of article 228 of the Criminal Code, which covers illegal
storage of narcotic drugs. [@1] Investigators finished the investigation and
sent the case file to court. [@1]

The man told police he found a growing cannabis plant in August on an abandoned
plot in a neighbouring village. [@1] He said he picked the plant for his own
use, took it home, dried it and kept it on the grounds of his house. [@1] Police
said officers acted on operational information when they carried out the search.
[@3] He was also held administratively liable under part 1 of article 6.9 of the
Administrative Code for consuming drugs without a doctor's prescription. [@1]

The regional Interior Ministry first reported the case in October, when the man
was a suspect and a criminal case had been opened. [@4]
