Sergipe Civil Police seized cocaine and skunk marijuana in Tobias Barreto, in
the south-central region of Sergipe, on the afternoon of Friday 13 November
2026. [@1] The operation was built on prior work by the Regional Delegation of
Tobias Barreto and followed an earlier flagrant drug-trafficking arrest in the
same town. [@1] Police said intelligence work and cross-checking of data from
the first stage of the inquiry produced new information about the local
distribution of the drugs. [@1] During the Friday-afternoon inquiries officers
located two people and took them to the town police station to give
clarification about the case. [@1]

Officers located and seized the cocaine and skunk, which were together valued at
about R$80,000. [@2] All of the seized material was catalogued and sent for
technical forensic examination. [@1] Police said investigations remain under way
to identify other possible participants and map the supply chain in the region.
