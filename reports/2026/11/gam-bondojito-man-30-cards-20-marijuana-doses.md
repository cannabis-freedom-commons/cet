Officers of the Secretaría de Seguridad Ciudadana (SSC) of Mexico City detained
a 36-year-old man in possession of 30 bank cards from various financial
institutions, a high-end vehicle and 20 doses of marijuana in streets of the
Gustavo A. Madero borough. [@1]

The arrest was made during security patrols in the colonia Bondojito, at the
crossing of Norte 62-A and Oriente 106 streets, where officers detected unusual
behaviour inside a parked white high-end car. [@1]

According to the agency's official report, the officers observed the occupant
handling various bags of presumed marijuana. [@1]

The preventive search secured 20 small bags with the apparent narcotic, a cell
phone and a lot of 30 bank cards from different banks, whose legal ownership and
provenance the man could not prove on the spot. [@1]

El Independiente likewise reports that the inspection found 20 small bags with
the apparent narcotic, a cell phone and 30 bank cards of different institutions
whose legal ownership the man could not prove. [@2]

The man was detained and, together with the secured objects and the vehicle,
presented before the agent of the Ministerio Público, who will determine his
legal situation. [@1]
