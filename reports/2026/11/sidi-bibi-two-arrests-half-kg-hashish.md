Royal Gendarmerie officers in the commune of Sidi Bibi, Chtouka Ait Baha
province, arrested a man with a previous criminal record on Saturday 21 November
2026, on suspicion of involvement in the possession and trafficking of drugs and
alcoholic drinks without a licence. [@1] According to a security source quoted
by Chtouka Press, gendarmes from the Sidi Bibi territorial centre identified the
suspect after extensive field inquiries and caught him red-handed with 22
bottles of wine, a significant quantity of chira, cannabis resin known as
hashish, and a sum of money believed to come from the activity. [@1] In the same
operation and within the same Sidi Bibi jurisdiction, gendarmes from the same
centre detained a second man, described as having special needs, who was found
possessing drugs at Douar Drayd. [@1]

The man used his home at Douar Drayd as a den for trafficking drugs, and the
search of the house seized about half a kilogram of chira. [@1]

Following instructions from the competent prosecution at the Biyokri primary
court, both detainees were placed under garde à vue, Morocco's police-custody
measure, pending a judicial investigation; they were to be brought before the
public prosecutor and then the courts once the inquiries were complete. [@1]
