Antioquia police arrested a man in flagrante in Parque La Cruz in Venecia and
reported finding doses of bazuco, coca base and marijuana, plus a cellphone, in
his possession. [@1] The police announced the result on Sunday 15 November
through its X account. [@1] The intervention happened while officers were
carrying out patrols and preventive plans in that part of the municipality. [@1]
Police released neither the man's age nor his identity. [@1]

The published tally lists three substances, bazuco, coca base and marijuana, but
gives no weight for any of them and no number of doses found. [@1] The man was
arrested for alleged trafficking, manufacture or carrying of narcotics. [@1]

The official publication reports no judicial decision, charge filing or custody
measure. [@1] Police said the results seek to protect young people and families'
peace, and announced no further arrests linked to this procedure. [@1]
