Police in Tendukheda of Damoh district arrested two accused with illegal ganja.
[@1] The two accused were a woman and a man held by the Tendukheda police
station team. [@1] Police moved after an informer reported that two people with
illegal ganja were near Vaishnav Dhaba before the 27 Mile crossing. [@1] The
police team reached the spot, cordoned it off and caught both the woman and the
man. [@1]

Officers recovered 13 kg 990 g of illegal ganja from the possession of the two
accused. [@1] Police valued the seized ganja at about Rs 280,000. [@1] Police
registered Crime No. 482/2026 against both accused under Section 8/20 of the
NDPS Act. [@1] Both accused were arrested and produced in court on judicial
remand. [@1]

Police said they were questioning the pair to trace the purchase, sale and
supply network behind the ganja. [@1] Police were also investigating where the
ganja had been brought from and how many others were involved. [@1] Police
presented the action as part of a continuing drive against illegal narcotics,
liquor, gambling and betting. [@1]
