Military police arrested a 40-year-old man in flagrante on Monday 16 November
2026 in the Santo Agostinho neighbourhood of Monte Carmelo, in Alto Paranaíba.
[@1] Police had identified two properties linked to drug sales after several
days of monitoring. [@1] The suspect tried to discard a bag of drugs when
officers arrived, and officers recovered the bag and found a key in the first
property giving access to the second address. [@1] Police said the man used
cameras to watch street movement and anticipate the arrival of patrol cars. [@1]

In the searches officers seized 11 bars and 14 portions of a substance
resembling maconha. [@1] In the same operation they seized 20 portions of
flake-type cocaine plus 75 paper wraps and 152 pins of suspected cocaine. [@1]
The second property was an abandoned house that police said was used to store
and prepare drugs for sale in the town. [@1] Officers also seized two precision
scales, two mobile phones, four monitoring cameras and a digital video recorder,
plus empty pins and packing material. [@1]

The search also recovered four monitoring cameras and a digital video recorder
with the scales and packing material. [@1]
