PSP officers in Almada detained a 24-year-old man on 10 November 2026 on
suspicion of drug trafficking. [@1] The detention took place during a preventive
operation against drug trafficking in an area known for recurrent drug use and
trafficking, following complaints from residents. [@1] During the operation
officers approached a vehicle carrying three people and found drugs and objects
linked to packaging and use. [@1]

The search resulted in the seizure of 28.1 doses of hashish, nine plastic
packages used for packaging drugs, and a kitchen knife with hashish residue.
[@1] Officers also seized five bottles of nitrous oxide, known as the laughing
drug, and 105 euros in cash. [@1]

The detained man was notified to appear before the competent judicial authority
for a first judicial interrogation and possible coercive measures. [@1]

The Almada Police Division said the operation was carried out by its
intervention and inspection squad. [@2]
