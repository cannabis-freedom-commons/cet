Carabinieri from the Fiumicino station and the Civitavecchia prosecutor's office
arrested two men aged 45 and 51, both already known to police and resident in
via Forni, Ostia. [@1] The men were held under a custodial order signed by the
investigations judge (Gip) and face detention for dealing, with one also facing
receiving stolen goods and illegal weapons possession. [@1] The two men are now
in Civitavecchia prison. [@1]

In February, officers found about 400 grams of hashish, equal to over 5,000
doses, and two pistols inside a camper parked in Isola Sacra, including a
revolver stolen in 2007 and a semi-automatic pistol. [@1] Investigators linked
the camper load to the two suspects, who had already been arrested in February
aboard a Renault Clio with 22 doses of cocaine, 16 of crack, two phones
dedicated to dealing and 2,590 euros in cash. [@1] The camper was described as a
mobile operational base parked on Isola Sacra streets to store drugs and
firearms and to feed illicit traffic between Ostia and Fiumicino. [@1]

The investigation is now checking whether the link between via Forni and Isola
Sacra reflects a wider criminal expansion strategy. [@1]
