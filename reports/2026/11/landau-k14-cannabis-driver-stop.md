Police in Landau set up a stationary check at about 13:00 on Tuesday 17 November
2026 on farmland tracks around the K14, which was closed for renovation, after
complaints that drivers were ignoring the transit ban. [@2] By 13:15 officers
had already found two unauthorised vehicles on the closed tracks. [@2] The first
driver was let off with a warning. [@1]

The second driver, aged 47, was stopped from driving on because he was under the
influence of narcotics, identified as cannabis. [@2] Officers ordered a blood
sample and opened criminal proceedings against him for driving while
intoxicated. [@2] Police said the K14 renovation was expected to last until
December and that further checks of the bypass tracks would follow. [@1] A
second outlet likewise reported that the 47-year-old was barred from continuing
because he was under the influence of narcotics (cannabis) and that a blood
sample was ordered with proceedings for driving while intoxicated. [@3]
