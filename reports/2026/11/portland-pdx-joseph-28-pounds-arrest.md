Transportation Security Administration (TSA) screeners at Portland International
Airport found 28 pounds (about 12.7 kilograms) of marijuana in a checked
suitcase on Sunday 22 November 2026. [@1] The suitcase failed to scan and was
found glued shut when agents opened it by hand. [@1] Inside were a large
vacuum-sealed bag, scented dryer sheets and a second vacuum-sealed bag holding a
green, leafy substance that an officer confirmed was marijuana. [@1]

Officers seized all 28 pounds from the suitcase and traced the bag to
26-year-old Jomo Kenyatta Joseph, arresting him at his departure gate. [@1] He
was booked into jail for illegal import or export of marijuana items, unlawful
delivery of a marijuana item and unlawful possession of marijuana by a person 21
years of age or older. [@1] A second checked bag belonging to Joseph was also
seized and is to be searched once investigators obtain a warrant. [@1] Hoodline
reported that it was unclear whether formal charges had been filed or when the
second bag would be opened. [@2]

Port of Portland figures cited by KPTV show just six arrests tied to attempted
marijuana smuggling of two pounds or more in 2022 and five in 2023. [@2] By
September 2026 the station had counted 19 such arrests at PDX for the year. [@2]
