Acting on information from an informant, a police team searched the house of
Haider Khan alias Ayan Khan in Kantaliya village, Pali district, Rajasthan, on
16 November 2026. [@1] Police said the information they acted on concerned a drug trade in Kantaliya. [@3] Khan, the son of Salim Khan, is an 18-year-old labourer
from Kantaliya in the Bagdina Nagar police station area of Pali district. [@1]
Khan was arrested on suspicion of illegal ganja possession. [@1,2,3] The operation
was ordered by Pali district superintendent of police Monica Sen and led by
sub-inspector Prahlad Singh, station house officer of Bagdina Nagar, under
additional superintendent Om Prakash Chaudhary and Sojat circle officer Prateek
Meel. [@1]

During the search officers found ganja hidden in the house with a total weight
of 894 grams (0.894 kg), police said. [@1,2,3] Police said the ganja was found in
Khan's possession and, because it was classed as a small quantity, he was
eligible for police bail; after presenting a surety and signing a bail bond at
the scene he was released. [@1] Police registered case number 189 dated 16
November 2026 under sections 8 and 20 of India's Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic
Substances Act and said the investigation was continuing. [@1,2]
