Police arrested four men in Perumbakkam, Chennai, in the early hours of 24
November 2026 and seized about 85 kg of ganja carried in an autorickshaw in
bundles. [@5] The men were identified as Saranraj, 21, Hemathkumar, 32, Gautham,
21, and Sesuraj, 35. [@3] Police said the ganja had been smuggled from Wayanad
in Kerala. [@4]

It emerged that a prisoner in Puzhal Central Prison and his wife had been
running a ganja smuggling gang from inside the prison. [@1] Police named them as
Senthilkumar, alias Kullakanda Senthil, a repeat offender, and his wife
Kalaivani, alias Kalai, both held in the prison. [@3] During interrogation
police found that Saranraj and his associates had visited the couple in prison
the previous Thursday. [@3] Senthilkumar told them about the incoming
consignment and instructed them to deliver it to designated buyers and collect
the payment. [@3] Residents of the Tamil Nadu Urban Habitat Development Board
tenements in Perumbakkam had complained about ganja, narcotic tablets and banned
gutka being sold in the locality. [@3] Acting on the tip, a special police team
seized about 85 kg of ganja in 40 parcels during the overnight check and
arrested the four. [@2]

Police said the consignment arrived from Kerala on Senthilkumar’s instructions
and was kept ready for distribution on Tuesday. [@3] Preliminary investigation
found Perumbakkam was a wholesale ganja distribution hub supplying peddlers
along East Coast Road, Old Mahabalipuram Road and Tambaram. [@3] Police also
seized the autorickshaw and the two-wheeler used in the operation. [@4]
