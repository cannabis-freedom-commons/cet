Rishabhdeo police in Udaipur district took action against alleged illegal
cultivation of narcotic plants at Athmana Kotda village. [@1] Officers recovered
illegal ganja plants from the field of its owner, 65-year-old Jiva Meena, son of
Sega Meena and a resident of the same village. [@1] Police registered case
number 266/26 against him under sections 8 and 20(a) of the NDPS Act, India's
main drug law, and opened an investigation. [@1]

The field operation was carried out on 12 November 2026 by a team led by
Rishabhdeo station house officer Hemant Ahari. [@1] The investigation of the
case was handed to Kalyanpur station house officer Sohanlal Bamniya, with police
saying further inquiry was under way. [@1]

Police said the focus of the action had now shifted to investigation and legal
process after the plants were found in the field. [@1] No plant count, weight,
seizure quantity or value was published, and the account does not say whether
the plants were destroyed on the spot. [@1]
