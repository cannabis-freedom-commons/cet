At the international express supervision site of Xi'an Xianyang Airport Customs,
officers seized suspected marijuana from multiple inbound express parcels mailed
from the United States. [@1] The seizure totalled 24 kilograms of marijuana
concealed in courier parcels sent from abroad. [@1] The case was described after
verification as the largest marijuana-smuggling case uncovered in Shaanxi's
postal and express delivery channel to date. [@1]

Officers had been screening cross-border express parcels through machines with
intelligent image-review equipment at the airport's international express
supervision and inspection site. [@1] They found the X-ray images of several
parcels sent from the United States to be abnormal because the declared product
names clearly did not match the internal imaging structure. [@1] Officers then
opened those parcels for manual unboxing and re-inspection. [@1]

Chinese law clearly defines marijuana as a narcotic drug that greatly harms the
human nervous system, with long-term smoking liable to cause mental disorders,
reduced immunity and various physical and mental illnesses. [@1] The seized
goods have been handed over to the anti-smuggling department, and the case
remains under further investigation. [@1]
