Amnaur police began checking vehicles at the Amnaur bazaar chowk in Saran
district, Bihar, after a state-level tip from the Prohibition and State
Narcotics Control Bureau in Patna and the Excise and Prohibition Department.
[@3,6,7] The ganja seizure was one of several actions in a 24-hour special drive ordered across Saran district by Senior Superintendent of Police Roshan Kumar on 19 November 2026. [@5] The vehicle check was carried out in the presence of a magistrate. [@4] Officers signalled a white Nexon to stop; its driver tried to speed away
but was caught because of a traffic jam in the market. [@2,6] The car was coming from the Sonho side when officers signalled it to stop at about 4:35 pm. [@4] The driver was
arrested, and after his disclosure police raided a shop in Jhakhri village and
arrested a second man. [@2,4,5,6] Police registered a first information report against
four people, including the car's registered owner, under NDPS Act sections for a
commercial quantity. [@3]

Officers found 21 packets of ganja packed in sacks and plastic in a hidden
compartment built into the boot and under the rear seat. [@2,6] Weighed on a
digital scale in the presence of the block development officer, the ganja, the
local name for cannabis, came to 115 kg 930 g. [@2,4,5,6] The driver could produce no
documents to show the ganja was legal. [@2] Police also seized a Samsung mobile
phone, 2,900 rupees in cash, an Aadhaar card and the car's registration
certificate from him. [@1,5]

The driver told police the car's owner had bought the ganja in Motihari and
given it to him to deliver. [@1] He said he was to hand it to a man in Vaishali
district and to another in Jhakhri, in the Amnaur police station area. [@1] He
told police he had delivered several earlier consignments of ganja to the two
men. [@1] The station house officer said the car looked ordinary from outside
but the rear seat and boot were unusually thick with fresh welding, which led
officers to the hidden compartment. [@3] He said the investigation's first aim
was to break the inter-district syndicate's main supplier and its chain of local
buyers. [@3]
