Rosenheim police stopped four impaired road users in checks on Friday 13
November and Saturday 14 November 2026. [@2] Three of those stopped were
e-scooter riders aged 14, 35 and 26, and each rider's drug test was positive for
cannabis. [@2] Officers stopped the 14-year-old in the Aising district around 8
am on Friday, the 35-year-old in Kufsteiner Straße around 7 pm on Friday, and
the 26-year-old in Münchener Straße around 6 pm on Saturday. [@2]

The fourth person stopped was a 46-year-old car driver in Brianconstraße on
Friday around 1 pm whose breath-alcohol test was over two per mille. [@1] Police
took blood samples from the three e-scooter riders and opened fine proceedings
carrying points in Flensburg and a driving ban. [@1] Police took a blood sample
from the alcohol-impaired driver, secured his driving licence and opened an
investigation for drunk driving. [@2]
