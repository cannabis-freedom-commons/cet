Police in Saharsa district arrested two motorcycle riders carrying ganja on
NH-107. [@1] The operation took place near Block Mor on NH-107 in the
Sonvarsaraj police station area. [@1] Police received a tip on the morning of 25
November 2026 that two men were carrying ganja from Viratpur through Sonvarsaraj
towards Khagaria. [@1] The riders tried to turn and flee when signalled to stop,
and the Pulsar motorcycle skidded before police and armed-force personnel caught
them. [@1] The two were identified as Nirmal Kumar and Rahul Kumar alias Jugnu.
[@1] Hindustan named them Nirmal Yadav and Jugnu Kumar, both residents of
Khagaria district. [@3]

Police recovered a total of 24.57 kilograms of ganja. [@1] Officers also seized
two mobile phones and the Pulsar motorcycle. [@1] A separate Prabhat Khabar
account said the search found 24.57 kg of ganja hidden in two bags on the
motorcycle. [@2] The estimated value of the seized ganja was about 25 lakh
rupees, according to the police briefing reported by Hindustan Samachar. [@5]

A case was registered at Sonvarsaraj police station as case 232/2026 dated 25
November 2026, and questioning continued. [@1] Superintendent of Police Vikram
Sihag said a special campaign was under way in the district against the illegal
trade, trafficking and use of dry drugs. [@1] Dainik Bhaskar quoted the SP as
saying the initial inquiry suggested links to Jharkhand, West Bengal and
northeastern states. [@4] Police said they had not yet confirmed any larger
trafficking network or the role of other accomplices. [@1]
