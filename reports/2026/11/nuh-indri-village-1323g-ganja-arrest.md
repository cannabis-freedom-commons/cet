Haryana State Narcotics Control Bureau officers from the Faridabad unit arrested
Vikram in Indri village in Nuh district for allegedly selling ganja outside his
house. [@1] Officers said he was sitting outside the house with the bag at the
time, waiting for a customer to arrive for a handover. [@1] Vikram was the
person taken into custody in the operation. [@1] He was allegedly acting as a
smuggler supplying the drug to customers. [@1]

The team searched the black bag at the spot and recovered the ganja kept inside
it. [@1] The bureau weighed the recovered ganja at 1 kilo 323 grams, or 1.323
kilograms. [@1]

Indri is a village in Nuh district in the state of Haryana, where the arrest
took place. [@1] The Haryana State Narcotics Control Bureau is the state's
narcotics enforcement agency, and its Faridabad unit carried out this seizure.
[@1] Ganja is the local term for cannabis flower, and the 1.323 kilos counted
here are cannabis. [@1]
