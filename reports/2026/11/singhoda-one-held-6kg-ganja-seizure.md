The Anti Narcotic Task Force said it had directions to act against ganja
trafficking through end-to-end and financial investigation of source and
destination points. [@1] On 15 November 2026 an informant told police that a man
was bringing ganja from Odisha toward Chhattisgarh on NH-53 in a black backpack.
[@1] Officers cordoned off Rehtikhol Chowk on NH-53 and caught the man with the
backpack. [@1] The man identified himself as 24-year-old Jaiprakash Patel of
Bara Gadoli, Uchehara, Satna district in Madhya Pradesh. [@1]

Asked about the backpack, he first gave evasive answers and then admitted on
strict questioning that it contained ganja. [@1] Officers seized 6.14 kilograms
of ganja from the backpack, which police valued at 307,000 rupees. [@1] They
also seized a Redmi mobile phone worth 7,000 rupees, putting the total seized
property at 314,000 rupees. [@1] Police said he was taking the ganja from
Phulbani in Kandhamal district, Odisha, to sell in Satna, Madhya Pradesh. [@1]

Police registered Crime No. 141/26 at Singhoda police station under Section
20(B), ii(B) of the NDPS Act and sent the accused to judicial remand. [@1]
