At about 20:10 on 15 November 2026, a DIAS police crew on patrol at a central
point in Xanthi spotted a group of young people it deemed suspicious and stopped
them for a check. [@1] Officers first found and seized a small quantity of
cannabis that had been thrown to the ground as they approached. [@1]

Body searches then found additional cannabis weighing about 7 grams, 270 euros
in cash and a precision electronic scale with cannabis residue, all of which was
seized. [@1] A thorough search of the wider area found three carefully hidden
packages of cannabis weighing over 115 grams near where the checked people had
been, plus another precision electronic scale. [@1]

Police said the total cannabis seized exceeded 122 grams and that two precision
scales were seized. [@1] Four Greek nationals were arrested and taken to the
Xanthi first-instance prosecutor. [@1]
