Police investigators in Novopokrovsky District in Russia's Krasnodar Krai
completed the criminal investigation into a 55-year-old man from the rural
stanitsa of Novopokrovskaya who is accused of keeping marijuana at home. [@1] He
is charged under part 2 of article 228 of the Russian Criminal Code. [@1] The
investigation was conducted by the investigative department of the Russian
Interior Ministry department for Novopokrovsky District. [@1] The case with the
approved indictment has been transferred to court for consideration on the
merits. [@1]

According to investigators, the man found wild cannabis bushes, picked them and
made marijuana for his own personal use. [@1] He then stored the resulting drug
in outbuildings on the grounds of his own household. [@2] During operational
measures, police seized more than 330 grams of the prohibited substance. [@1]

The Krasnodar regional police press service is the named source for the account
of the discovery, home production and storage in the outbuildings. [@2] The
punishment under the charged part carries a maximum of up to ten years of
imprisonment. [@1]
