Nanded district police took strict steps to curb illegal drug use and addiction
in the city and district. [@1] The action was part of a special drive against
drug consumers organised under district police chief Nilabh Rohan. [@1] On 15
and 16 November 2026, officers raided six station areas and filed cases against
18 people for consuming ganja in public places. [@1] The raids covered Itwara,
Bhokar, Tamsa, Nanded Rural, Deglur and Vazirabad, where officers acted at
various public places. [@1] Those caught red-handed consuming ganja in public
were booked under sections 8(c) and 27 of the NDPS Act. [@1]

Officers from Itwara, Tamsa, Nanded Rural, Bhokar and Vazirabad seized chillums,
tobacco-mixed ganja-like material, matchboxes and burnt sticks from those held.
[@1] Police burned and destroyed all the seized material before witnesses
following legal procedure. [@1]

Police said a zero-tolerance policy against trafficking, sale and public
consumption remains in place and the drive will be intensified. [@1] Police
appealed to residents to contact the nearest police station with information
about drug use or sale in their area. [@1]
