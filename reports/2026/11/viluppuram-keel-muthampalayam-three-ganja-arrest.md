Viluppuram taluk police arrested three people for selling ganja in Viluppuram.
[@1] Sub-inspector Manikandan and other officers of the Viluppuram taluk police
were on patrol near the Keel Muthampalayam government higher secondary school.
[@1]

Police found that three people had been selling ganja: Akilan, 19, son of
Moorthy from Nadumuthampalayam, an 18-year-old youth, and a 17-year-old youth
from Koliyanur. [@1] Viluppuram taluk police registered a case against the
three, arrested them and are questioning them. [@1]
