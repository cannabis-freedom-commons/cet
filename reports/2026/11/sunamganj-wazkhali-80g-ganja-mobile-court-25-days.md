Detectives from the Sunamganj district police arrested a young man with ganja
during a special drive at Wazkhali Bazar in Sunamganj Sadar upazila. [@2] The
man was Nur Ahmed, 23. [@2] He is the son of Abul Bashar of Wazkhali village.
[@2] Officers seized 80 grams of ganja from him. [@1] The raid took place at
about 11am on 26 November 2026 on the Sunamganj–Sylhet road beside Wazkhali
Bazar. [@2] The district detective branch said the team acted on a tip-off and
was led by sub-inspector Masud Alam Bhuiyan. [@2] The special drive covered the
Sadar police station area. [@2]

An executive magistrate held a mobile court at the scene and sentenced him under
section 36(5) of the Narcotics Control Act. [@1] The sentence was 25 days of
simple imprisonment and a 500-taka fine. [@1] The magistrate passed it after
consulting senior authorities at the scene. [@1] Nur Ahmed was then taken to
Sunamganj district jail under police escort. [@2]
