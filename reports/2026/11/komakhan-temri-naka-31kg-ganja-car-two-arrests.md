Komakhan police and the Anti-Narcotics Task Force stopped a grey car at Temri
naka after an informant said two people were bringing ganja from Khariar Road in
Odisha toward Mahasamund. [@1] The car held a driver and one other occupant, who
identified themselves as 34-year-old Jitendra Das and 39-year-old Samaru Sahu,
both residents of Kesinga in Kalahandi district, Odisha. [@1] Police registered
crime number 209/2026 under sections 20(B)(II)(C) and 29 of the NDPS Act at
Komakhan and sent both accused to judicial remand. [@1]

Officers searching the car's boot recovered 31.370 kilograms of ganja in two
bundles, which police valued at Rs 15,68,500. [@1,2] Police said the pair were
carrying the consignment from Kesinga in Kalahandi district to Raipur in
Chhattisgarh. [@1,2] Along with the ganja, police seized the car used for
transport and two mobile phones, placing the total seized property at Rs
21,79,000. [@1] The seizure list prices the two mobile phones at Rs 10,500. [@1]
The seizure list prices the Maruti car at Rs 6,00,000. [@1]

The action was carried out by the Anti-Narcotics Task Force with Komakhan
police. [@1] The force said it was acting under directions for enforcement
against ganja trafficking, transport and sale, including follow-up and financial
investigation. [@1]
