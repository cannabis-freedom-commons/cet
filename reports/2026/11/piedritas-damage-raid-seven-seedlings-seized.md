Police carried out a raid on Wednesday 18 November 2026 at a home in the sección
quintas area of Piedritas as part of an investigation into damage that occurred
over the weekend. [@1] The incident that started the case happened on Saturday
night at a home in the town, where window glass and a door were broken. [@1] The
complainant is a man of about 26 and the accused is about 40, and both are
neighbours in the town who were reportedly friends. [@1] The account was
attributed to Subteniente Cristian Rodríguez, the officer in charge of the
Piedritas police detachment. [@1]

The raid at the house in the sección quintas was carried out by officers of the
local police detachment together with members of the GAD police support group
and personnel from other district police units. [@1] During the procedure
officers seized spent shell casings. [@1] In an urgent action at the same place
officers also seized seven marijuana seedlings found there. [@1] No one was
detained in the operation. [@1]

Following the complaint, an investigation was opened under the heading Damages,
with the intervention of prosecutor Fabio Arcomano of the Trenque Lauquen
judicial department. [@1] The investigation continued under the prosecutor's
office in charge of Fabio Arcomano. [@1]
