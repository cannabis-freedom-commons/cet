The Deurne-Noord neighbourhood team of Antwerp police held a coordinated
operation against several forms of nuisance on Sunday 22 November 2026. [@1]
During the operation officers checked a group of young people in
Bisschoppenhofpark. [@1] Police found a quantity of marijuana on the group,
seized the drugs and drew up an official report. [@1] Police spokesperson Wouter
Bruyns said a quantity of marijuana was found and the drugs were seized with a
report to follow. [@2]

After several complaints the team also checked a newspaper shop in
Tweemontstraat, near the AFAS Dome. [@1] In the shop officers found five vapes,
42 pouches of snus and 29 cones, and seized all of the products. [@1] The team
drew up five official reports over the shop for various offences, including
inciting drug use. [@1] Police also made an administrative file on the shop for
the city of Antwerp, which handles further follow-up and possible sanctions.
[@1]

In Van Heystveltstraat officers seized a moped that reached 43 kilometres per
hour where 25 is the maximum. [@2] A second moped rider tested positive in a
saliva test and had no driving licence. [@2] Someone caught dumping waste was
identified and given a municipal fine report. [@2]
