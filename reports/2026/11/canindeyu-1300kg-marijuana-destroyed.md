Anti-drug agents carried out an operation in Canindeyú department that located
and destroyed almost 1,300 kilograms of marijuana. [@2] The intervention located
and eliminated an important cannabis load in a rural area of the department.
[@1] The aim was to remove a large quantity of drugs from circulation and affect
criminal organisations operating in the area. [@2]

The department is considered one of Paraguay's main marijuana production
territories because of its strategic location and proximity to the Brazilian
border. [@2]

Interventions in the region seek not only to seize shipments but also to
dismantle spaces used for cultivation, processing, storage and later transfer to
other markets. [@2] Senad and other security forces maintain incursion
operations in rural zones to identify and destroy production and storage centres
linked to international trafficking. [@2]
