Military Police found a car that had crashed after fleeing a police stop in
Lages in the locality of Itararé, on the BR-282 in São José do Cerrito, Santa
Catarina, on the afternoon of 18 November 2026, and seized about 25 kg of
marijuana from it. [@1,3] Nearby they found a man with facial injuries and pain in
his right leg who was being helped by a resident. [@1,3] He told police he had
been in the car when it crashed and that another occupant had fled into a wooded
area. [@1,3] Rescue teams took the injured man to hospital in Lages. [@1,3] Officers
with ROCAM and other units searched the region for the second man. [@1,3] His
location was still unconfirmed when the information was released. [@1]

The car had fled a police stop in Lages on the previous night and headed towards
São José do Cerrito. [@1] Searches and roadblocks in the region failed to locate
it. [@1] Around 13:00 on 18 November, during further patrols with intelligence
support, police found the vehicle in a ravine about five metres deep after it
left the road. [@1] Officers found 30 bars of marijuana totalling 25.05 kg,
according to Notícia no Ato. [@1,3] Oeste Mais reported that the drug was divided
into 30 bars. [@2] Oeste Mais put the amount at approximately 25 kg. [@2]

The marijuana was seized and sent for the applicable legal procedures. [@1]
Oeste Mais said the suspect found at the scene and the seized drug were taken to
the Civil Police station. [@2]
