SRB-10 officers arrested two men in Pani Chhatra area of Madaripur Sadar upazila
at about 20:05 on 17 November 2026 and seized 25 kg of ganja. [@1] The Special
Response Battalion (SRB) is a police unit that runs law-and-order and anti-drug
operations across Bangladesh. [@1] One of the two was Md Lalon Sardar, 35, of
North Jhikarhati in Madaripur Sadar. [@1] The other was Md Shipon Bepari, 40, of
South Pakullah in Rajoir upazila, also in Madaripur district. [@1]

A team of SRB-10, CPC-1, Jatrabari camp, acted on intelligence that drugs were
being taken from Dhaka's Jatrabari area to Madaripur and set up a temporary
checkpost at Postagola bridge in Dhaka at about 17:35. [@1] When the traders
crossed the bridge, the team followed them with technical support and stopped
them in Madaripur that night. [@1] SRB-10 put the ganja's market value at
750,000 taka. [@1]

The battalion said the two were professional drug traders who had long collected
ganja and other drugs from border areas and supplied them to Jatrabari,
Madaripur district and other parts of the country. [@1] The two were handed to
the relevant police station with the seized drugs so a case could be filed
against them under the Narcotics Control Act. [@1] SRB-10 said its operations
against drugs, weapons, terrorism and extortion would continue. [@1]
