Narcotics Control Bureau officers arrested eight persons for smuggling drugs in
Choolaimedu on Monday. [@1] The NCB had received a tip-off about the sale of
heroin in Choolaimedu. [@1] A special police team apprehended a man acting on
that information. [@1] A search found he was in possession of half a gram of
heroin. [@1] He was identified as 26-year-old salon worker Nihal Thapa from West
Bengal. [@1] Based on his information, police arrested Amrit Pradhan, 29, of
West Bengal, Sarvakumar, 20, of Bihar, and Rakesh Tamang Thing, 29, of Nepal.
[@1]

Police seized 85 grams of heroin, 50 grams of ganja and 50 narcotic pills from
the gang. [@1] All four were handed over to the Choolaimedu police. [@1] After
further information, the Choolaimedu police apprehended Anmol Tham, 43, from
West Bengal, Shal alias Manraj, 35, from Nepal, Sawan Gurung, 23, from Sikkim,
and Rahul Pratap, 23, from Darjeeling. [@1] A second account of the same arrests
says the Choolaimedu police seized 100 grams of methamphetamine. [@2]

That account identifies the group as Rakesh and Sail from Nepal, Saurav Kumar
from Bihar, Amrit Pradhan, Anmel, Mikhal and Rahul Pratap from West Bengal, and
Shawan from Sikkim. [@2] Investigators were tracing the supply chain used to
transport the drugs into Chennai. [@2]
