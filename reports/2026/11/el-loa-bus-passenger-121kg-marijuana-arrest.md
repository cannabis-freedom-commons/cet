Carabineros' OS7 unit in El Loa found more than 12 kilograms of marijuana being
transported inside an interurban bus on Ruta B25, in El Loa province. [@1] The
check was carried out at kilometre 74 of Ruta B25. [@1] A 22-year-old foreign
passenger in an irregular migration situation was carrying 11 packages of
processed marijuana in a suitcase. [@1] The packages weighed 12 kilograms 100
grams in total. [@1] The woman was detained on suspicion of drug trafficking.
[@1] Officers seized 334,000 pesos in cash that police said was linked to the
offence. [@2]

A drug-detection dog, the Sección de Investigación Policial of Calama's 1st
police station and the Sección de Intervención Centauro took part in the check.
[@3] The procedure was part of the El Loa prefecture's focused and specialised
police services, aimed at detecting illicit substances moved along the
province's main routes. [@4] Prefect of El Loa Colonel Oliver Matteo said the
controls were not random and responded to planning that focused the services on
strategic points of the routes. [@1] Matteo said roadside controls let police
act before the drugs were distributed, reinforcing preventive work on the
province's routes and in its urban areas. [@1]
