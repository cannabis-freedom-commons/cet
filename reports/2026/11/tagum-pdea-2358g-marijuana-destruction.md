PDEA-Davao destroyed more than P10.9 million worth of confiscated illegal drugs
in Tagum City, Davao del Norte, on 23 November 2026, marking its second reported
destruction activity in the region within five days. [@1] The marijuana in the
disposal weighed 2,358.1840 grams and was worth P282,982.08. [@1] The disposal
also included 1,562.2884 grams of shabu valued at P10,623,561.12, bringing the
combined street value of the destroyed substances to P10,906,543.20. [@1] The
substances were destroyed by thermal incineration at Woodhaven Chapels and
Crematorium as part of Drug Abuse Prevention and Control Week 2026. [@1]

It was the third drug destruction conducted by PDEA Regional Office 11 this year
and was carried out with the Davao del Norte Provincial Anti-Drug Abuse Council,
headed by Governor Edwin Jubahib. [@1] PDEA-Davao regional director Benjamin
Gaspi said the agency had to ensure confiscated drugs were properly accounted
for and permanently destroyed rather than returning to circulation. [@1] Tagum
City Regional Trial Court executive judge Sharon Rose Saracin witnessed the
destruction along with representatives of the Department of Justice, the Public
Attorney's Office, civil society groups, an elected official and the media. [@1]

The activity followed a joint PDEA-Davao and PDEA Region 12 disposal in Davao
City on 18 November 2026 that destroyed more than P14 million worth of drugs
from the two regions. [@1]
