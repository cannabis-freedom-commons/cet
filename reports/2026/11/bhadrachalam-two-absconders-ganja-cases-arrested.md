Bhadrachalam police arrested two absconding accused wanted in two old cases of
ganja trafficking in an operation on Friday night. [@1] The two men taken into
custody were identified as Sheikh Jilani and Charan Raj, with each man wanted in
a different one of the two cases. [@1] Jilani was wanted in a ganja-trafficking
case that police said had been registered on 21 March 2025. [@1] Charan Raj was
wanted in a separate ganja-trafficking case that police said had been registered
on 4 April 2024. [@1]

The April 2024 case was one in which 3.980 kilograms of ganja had been seized at
the time that case was registered. [@1] Police said several other accused
persons in these two cases had already been arrested before the latest
operation. [@1] The latest arrests took place in Bhadrachalam in Khammam
district in Telangana. [@1]
