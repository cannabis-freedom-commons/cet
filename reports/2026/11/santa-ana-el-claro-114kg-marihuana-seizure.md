National Guard personnel and soldiers seized 114 kilograms of green herb with
marijuana characteristics during patrols and ground reconnaissance in ejido El
Claro, in the municipality of Santa Ana, Sonora. [@1] The troops found the herb
at a camp they detected in the ejido, according to the 45th Military Zone's
account. [@2] The commands of the II Military Region and the 45th Military Zone
announced the seizure. [@3]

The commands said the seizure took place on 23 November 2026 as part of the
National Public Security Strategy. [@1] They estimated the economic impact on
criminal groups at 397,631 pesos, saying the blow weakened their operating
capacity and sources of financing. [@1] The 45th Military Zone said the seizure
weakened the financing and operating capacity of the region's criminal cell.
[@2] The marijuana was destroyed at the scene under supervision, with personnel
of the Attorney General's Office (FGR) present, while investigations and
forensic examinations continue. [@1]

The commands said the army, air force and National Guard have seized 140,740
kilograms of marijuana nationwide under the current administration. [@1]
