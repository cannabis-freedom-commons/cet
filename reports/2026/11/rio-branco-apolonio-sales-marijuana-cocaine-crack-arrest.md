Military police in Rio Branco arrested a 21-year-old man, identified only by the
initials R. C. A., on suspicion of selling drugs in an irregular occupation area
of the Apolônio Sales neighbourhood. [@1] Officers from the Tactical 01 and
Tactical 02 teams of the 2nd Military Police Battalion, backed by the tactical
team of the 3rd Battalion, went to a house after an anonymous tip about drug
dealing nearby. [@1] Residents had reported a growing circulation of drug users
in the area, the police account said. [@1] Police said the man was nervous when
approached, told them he kept drugs inside the property and allowed the officers
in. [@1] The operation by the tactical teams of the 2nd and 3rd Military Police
battalions was mounted after the anonymous tip about sales at the residence.
[@3]

In the search the officers found portions of marijuana, a substance with the
characteristics of cocaine and rocks resembling crack. [@1] The man told the
officers that this was all the illegal material he had, according to the police
account. [@1] Police said he already had records for drug trafficking, O Alto
Acre reported. [@2]

He was arrested and taken with the seized drugs to the Central Flagrantes Police
Station (Defla) in Rio Branco for processing. [@1]
