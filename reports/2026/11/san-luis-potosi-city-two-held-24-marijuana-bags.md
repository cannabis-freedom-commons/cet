Two men allegedly distributing drugs in the capital were detained in recent
hours in the Julian Carrillo and Progreso neighbourhoods. [@1] Guardia Civil
Estatal officers carried out both detentions for alleged participation in
narcomenudeo, the small-scale sale of drugs, seizing drug doses and a van
carrying ponchallantas. [@1] In Julian Carrillo officers detained Andres 49,
originally from Veracruz, seizing several crystal-like doses, at least 24 bags
of green herb with marijuana characteristics and about 32 cocaine-like doses
from him. [@1]

In Progreso officers detained Moises 19, finding 10 doses of a crystal-like
substance on him during the same operation. [@1] During surveillance patrols in
Julian Carrillo officers found a silver 2007 Dodge Caravan blocking traffic and
seized it after finding about 15 ponchallantas, metal tyre spikes, inside. [@1]

The two detainees, the substances and the seized items were placed at the
disposal of the competent authorities for further investigation. [@2] The State
Secretariat of Security and Citizen Protection said it maintains preventive and
surveillance operations to combat criminal activity in the state. [@1]
