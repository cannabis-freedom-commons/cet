A police action in Vitória da Conquista resulted in the detention of one suspect
identified only by the nickname “Boca de Tomate” and his transfer to the city’s
Integrated Public Security District (DISEP). [@1] According to the account
broadcast on the Conquista Agora programme on Rádio Brasil on Tuesday the 17th,
the man was caught during routine patrol carrying portions of marijuana and
paper wraps of cocaine. [@1]

Military personnel who made the approach seized all of the narcotics found in
his possession during that routine patrol stop in the city. [@1] The officers
then presented the detainee to the Civil Police Central Duty Office in the Joia
do Sertão Baiano so that the applicable legal measures could be adopted. [@1]

The receiving authority is the Distrito Integrado de Segurança Pública (DISEP)
in Vitória da Conquista. [@1] The haul comprised portions of marijuana together
with paper wraps of cocaine seized at the same stop. [@1] That central duty
office serves the city referred to as the Joia do Sertão Baiano. [@1]
