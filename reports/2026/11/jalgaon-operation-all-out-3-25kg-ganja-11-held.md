Jalgaon police ran a district-wide ‘Operation All Out’ on 16 November to curb
illegal businesses and crime. [@1,5] The special drive was led by Superintendent
of Police Shrikant Dhivare and targeted the liquor mafia, gamblers, ganja
smugglers, habitual offenders and traffic violators. [@1,5] Superintendent of Police Shrikant Dhivare had ordered action across the district against the sale, transport and use of drugs under the Nashamukt Bharat campaign. [@6] The Raver action was led by police inspector Sandeep Patil. [@2] Inspector Sandeep Patil had told his team to gather information on people who sell and store illegal drugs, the instructions behind the Pal raid. [@6] Teams from every
police station and the Local Crime Branch started raids at various places from
early morning. [@1,5] In the anti-drug part of the operation, police took action
against 11 people. [@1,5,7] Ten of the 11 people were found smoking ganja. [@7] A case was registered at Raver police station and sub-inspector Manoj Mahajan was conducting the further investigation. [@2,3,6] Raver police arrested Meharban Jahangir Tadvi, a 36-year-old resident of Pal, Raver. [@2,3,4,6]

Officers seized 3 kg 250 g of ganja. [@1,2,3,4,5,6] Raver police acted on a confidential tip-off that ganja was stored for sale in a two-storey house, verified it and raided the house, finding the ganja near the staircase at the entrance. [@2,3,6] The confidential tip-off that led to the raid reached Raver police on 16 November 2026. [@3] The raid took place at 2.20 pm on Monday 16 November 2026. [@6] Police gave the seized ganja an
approximate value of Rs 1.62 lakh. [@1,3,5,6,7] Police said the single-day drive seized
muddemal worth Rs 35,72,935 and also collected fines. [@1,5]

The operation was carried out under the guidance of Superintendent Shrikant
Dhivare and Additional Superintendent Ashok Nakhate by sub-divisional officers
and the crime, traffic and station staff. [@1,2] Jalgaon district police warned
that such strong drives would continue so illegal businesses and criminal
tendencies get no shelter. [@1]
