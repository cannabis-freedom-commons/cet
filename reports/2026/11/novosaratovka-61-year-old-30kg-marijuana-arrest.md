In Novosaratovka in Vsevolozhsky District of Leningrad Oblast, police broke up a
drug plantation run by a 61-year-old previously convicted St Petersburg man.
[@1] Officers detained the man at about 6 p.m. on 11 November as he was leaving
his country plot. [@1,4] A second account says officers came in the evening of 11
November and found the 61-year-old leaving the plot, confirming he was
previously convicted. [@3]

During the search police found three containers and 14 polymer wraps of crushed
green plant material with a total weight of more than 30 kg. [@1,4] Police
described the seizure as more than 30 kg of marijuana taken during the search.
[@1] An examination of the contents of one container confirmed marijuana
weighing more than 1 kg. [@1,4]

According to police, the man had earlier grown cannabis (marijuana) at that same
address in order to sell it later by contact hand-to-hand sale. [@1,4] The
Vsevolozhsky district investigative department opened a criminal case on
attempted large-scale drug sale. [@2] Police identified the case as part 3 of
article 30 and point g of part 4 of article 228.1 of the Russian Criminal Code.
[@1] The suspect was remanded in custody, and police said they were now looking
for possible accomplices and distribution channels. [@1,4]
