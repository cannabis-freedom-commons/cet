Teams from the Narcotics and Anti-Smuggling Directorate and the Crime Prevention
Branch of the Nicosia Police Directorate carried out the operation in Hamitköy
at about 17:30 on 17 November 2026. [@1] Police arrested two men aged 37 and 32,
identified in the police statement as C.I.A. and C.S.O. [@1] The arrests
followed a search of the men's home in Hamitköy after police received
information that drugs were kept there. [@2] A court ordered both men held in
police custody for two days while the investigation continues. [@3]

Officers seized 360 grams of a substance believed to be cannabis-type drug,
which the Turkish-language reports call hint keneviri (Indian hemp). [@1] The
court account itemised 311 grams found in the living room, 20 grams in one man's
room and 28 grams described as ready for sale in the other's room. [@2] Police
also seized 34 pills believed to contain drugs, a precision scale with drug
residue and 3,850 euros in cash believed to come from drug sales. [@1]

Police told the court the two had admitted buying 390 grams of cannabis and 40
pills through WhatsApp earlier in November. [@2] They said they broke 30 grams
into small pieces and left them at locations in Nicosia for people they did not
know to collect, and that they distributed six of the pills the same way. [@2]
The men said the 3,850 euros had been sent to them by the person they worked
for, the court was told. [@2] They were arrested on suspicion of illegally
acquiring, possessing and supplying cannabis and of laundering the proceeds of
crime. [@2] Police said the investigation had just begun and that statements
were still to be taken and exhibits sent for analysis. [@2]
