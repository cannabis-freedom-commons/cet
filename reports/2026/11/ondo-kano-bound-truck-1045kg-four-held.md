Operatives of the National Drug Law Enforcement Agency intercepted a Kano-bound
truck in neighbouring Ondo State after intelligence gathering and surveillance
of the vehicle. [@1] Four suspects were arrested in connection with the seizure.
[@1] The announcement was issued in a statement by NDLEA spokesman Femi
Babafemi. [@1] The agency described the interception as part of separate
intelligence-led operations across the country. [@1]

The truck was loaded with 93 bags of cannabis. [@1] Those bags weighed 1,045
kilograms. [@1] The agency said the separate operations together yielded more
than 5.6 tonnes of cannabis. [@1]

The same operations included 160 explosives being transported to Zamfara State,
which are excluded from the cannabis figures. [@1] They also included two
locally made firearms and five live cartridges recovered from a drug joint in
Abeokuta, Ogun State, also excluded here. [@1] The agency said it continued its
War Against Drug Abuse sensitisation campaign in schools, communities and
traditional institutions alongside the enforcement operations. [@1]
