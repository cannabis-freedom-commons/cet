Military Police arrested a 36-year-old man in flagrante on suspicion of drug
trafficking at an abandoned property on Avenida Salomão Abrahão in the Santa
Mônica neighbourhood of Uberlândia, Minas Gerais. [@1] The occurrence was
recorded on Friday 13 November 2026 at the abandoned property on that avenue.
[@2] According to the Military Police, officers had started work after an
anonymous tip about possible drug sales at the site. [@1] During monitoring they
identified an intense movement of people with characteristics they described as
typical of drug trafficking. [@1] Teams then set a strategic cordon around the
perimeter and approached the suspect, arresting him in flagrante. [@2]

During the searches officers found a bag containing portions of substances
resembling marijuana, cocaine and crack. [@2] In total they seized three medium
tablets and six buchas of a marijuana-like substance. [@1] They also seized two
paper wraps of a cocaine-like substance and six medium crack stones. [@1]
Alongside the drugs, police collected R$325 in cash, a precision scale and a
white iPhone XR phone. [@2]

The man and all the seized material were taken to the Civil Police station and
presented for the applicable procedures. [@1]
