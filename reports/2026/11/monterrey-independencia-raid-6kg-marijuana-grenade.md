A search warrant was executed at a home in colonia Independencia, Monterrey,
Nuevo León. [@1] The searched property is on calle Arroyo in that colonia. [@1]
The case began with an anonymous call to the 089 line warning that two armed men
were entering and leaving the house. [@1] A Fuerza Civil police report had
earlier recorded a cream-coloured handgun, two magazines and a package of green,
dry herb. [@1]

Inside the property officers seized two long arms with the characteristics of
rifles, a handgun with various markings, two firearm magazines and cartridges
whose count had not been completed. [@1] They also seized an object of military
material resembling a grenade. [@1] Authorities seized two blocks of green, dry
plant matter with the appearance of marijuana, weighing approximately six
kilograms, and the house itself. [@1]

The items and the property were placed under the custody of the federal
prosecutor's office (FGR), the Nuevo León state prosecutor's office and the
state police force Fuerza Civil. [@1] The investigation covers delitos contra la
salud, Mexico's drug-related health offences, and violations of the federal
firearms and explosives law. [@1] The prosecutors are building the case against
whoever is responsible. [@1]
