Prague city police stopped a 25-year-old driver in V Kolkovně street in Prague
after a traffic offence. [@3] When officers checked his identity, they found
that his driving licence had been withdrawn, and the reason given was driving
under the influence of drugs. [@1] His licence had been withheld, so he was not
allowed to drive at the time. [@1] He told the officers he had last used drugs a
week earlier. [@1]

A drug test was positive for THC, as well as amphetamine and methamphetamine,
the city police said. [@2] During the check officers also found a gas pistol and
a flick knife on him. [@1]

The case was handed to the national Police of the Czech Republic for further
action. [@1] The Prague City Police reported the stop as one of several
drug-driving cases. [@1] The force said a drug-impaired driver can change or end
an innocent person's life within seconds. [@1]
