Federal Highway Police officers arrested a 19-year-old Venezuelan youth for drug
trafficking on the afternoon of Wednesday 18 November 2026 after finding more
than 300 kg of drugs in a vehicle involved in a traffic accident on BR-116 in
São Lourenço do Sul. [@1] The team had initially been called to attend the
accident and found a large quantity of drugs inside one of the vehicles while
inspecting those involved. [@1] After the flagrant arrest, the suspect was taken
to the Civil Police station in São Lourenço do Sul together with the seized
drugs. [@1]

The occurrence was recorded at kilometre 448 of the highway. [@1] Officers
seized 305.5 kg of marijuana and 10 kg of hashish, as well as R$1,700 in cash.
[@1]

The driver told officers that the load had been collected in Joinville in Santa
Catarina and was to be delivered in the south of the state. [@1] During the
vehicle check, officers found that the car was circulating with adulterated
plates. [@1] The Federal Highway Police said the vehicle was cloned and its
chassis number corresponded to another car with a judicial restriction, and a
second false plate was also found inside the car. [@1]
