Binpur police stopped a car during a checkpoint check near Amainagar More on the
Dahijuri–Lalgarh road in Jhargram district, West Bengal. [@1] The vehicle was a
Maruti van, local reporting said. [@3] The search recovered about 22 kg of
cannabis. [@1] Police arrested three residents of the Goaltore police station
area in Paschim Medinipur district. [@1] The three arrested men were aged 30, 25
and 27, police said. [@2] Police said the men were bringing the cannabis from
Odisha. [@2]

Jhargram district police said the seizure followed secret information. [@1]
Investigators were checking whether anyone else was part of the trafficking
ring. [@2] The three were being questioned about where the cannabis came from
and where it was to be taken. [@2] Binpur police station began investigating the
case. [@2]
