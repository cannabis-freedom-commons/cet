Civil Lines police in Moradabad arrested 28-year-old Shubham in the Civil Lines
police station area and said he was carrying 1.094 kilograms of charas, a
cannabis resin. [@1] The operation was led by the station's in-charge inspector,
Harish Vardhan Singh. [@1] Police said the charas was recovered from Shubham's
possession. [@1]

Immediately after the recovery, police registered a case against Shubham under
sections 8/20 of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act and began
further legal proceedings. [@1] Police said Shubham had a previous criminal
history and were questioning him about other aspects of the case. [@1] Police
said detailed information had not yet emerged about where the charas was
supplied from or who it was meant to reach. [@1]

The police described the arrest as part of their continuing action against the
illegal narcotics trade in the district. [@1] Officers said their investigation
would try to establish whether Shubham was linked to a larger network. [@1]
