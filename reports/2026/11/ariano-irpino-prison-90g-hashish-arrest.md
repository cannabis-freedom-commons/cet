Penitentiary police in the visits area of the Tricolle prison in Ariano Irpino
intercepted two women who allegedly tried to introduce drugs concealed to evade
checks on 18 November 2026. [@1] The woman found with the larger amount was
arrested and taken to the Benevento prison. [@3] The second woman was reported
at liberty. [@4]

Officers seized about 90 grams of hashish from the woman carrying the larger
quantity. [@2] The visits unit that foiled the attempts is led by a coordinating
inspector. [@4] The account was announced by the Ariano Irpino branch of the GAU
UIL FP penitentiary-police union, which praised the professionalism of the
officers on duty in the visits area. [@1]

The union said officers continued checks with the same attention after the first
find and thus foiled the second attempt. [@1] The union said long-denounced
staff shortages force daily overtime, yet officers continue to defend legality
and block drugs and wrongdoing inside prisons. [@2]
