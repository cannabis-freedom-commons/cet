Drug-control officers of the Votkinsk department of the Russian Interior
Ministry, acting on operational information, exposed a 46-year-old local man
suspected of attempting to sell a large batch of marijuana. [@1,2] The
investigative department of the same Votkinsk police department opened a
criminal case under part 3 of article 30 and part 4 of article 228.1 of the
Russian Criminal Code, covering attempted illegal sale of narcotics. [@1,3] The maximum penalty under the charge is 20 years in prison. [@5] For
the period of the investigation, the suspect was remanded in custody. [@1,2]

With power support from Rosgvardia fighters, police stopped the grower's
activity and searched the premises, finding three containers as well as 240
small packets containing marijuana. [@1,3] Police said the 240 packets had been prepared for sale. [@4] Police put the total weight of the
seized marijuana at more than three kilograms. [@1,3]

The operation took place in Votkinsk in the Udmurt Republic. [@1] According to
operatives, the man used premises rented for furniture production and had fitted
out one of its rooms for cultivating cannabis. [@1,2] He collected and dried the
mature harvest and then sold the finished drug to people he knew. [@1,2]
