In Nachol upazila of Chapainawabganj, a team of the Chapainawabganj district
office of the Department of Narcotics Control arrested 28-year-old Md. Shah
Jamal @ Nahid, described as a drug dealer, with 1 kg 200 grams of ganja. [@1]
The operation was carried out on the morning of Sunday, 15 November 2026. [@1]
The special operation ran from 6:20 am to 7:00 am in the Kholshi (Natuntola)
village area under Fatepur Union in Nachol police station area, where Jamal was
detained with the 1.2 kg of ganja. [@1]

The detained man is from Ward No. 1 of Kholshi (Natuntola) village in Nachol
upazila, son of Md. Monirul Islam and Mst. Parvin Begum. [@1] The details were
given in a press release signed by Chowdhury Imrul Hasan, deputy director of the
Department of Narcotics Control district office in Chapainawabganj. [@1]
Sub-inspector Md. Mamunur Rashid of the Department of Narcotics Control filed a
regular drug case at Nachol police station over the seizure. [@1]
