On 15 November 2026, police officers from the Skopje Police Department detained
four people at various locations in Skopje. [@1] Those detained were M.S. (30),
A.K. (24) and a 17-year-old, all from Skopje, and D.P. (22) from the village of
Tabanovce in the Kumanovo region. [@1]

Marijuana and a white, lump-like substance were found and seized from them. [@1]
Police said appropriate charges will be filed against them after complete
documentation of the cases. [@1] A second account gives the same date, places,
ages and seizure of marijuana and white lumpy substance for the four detained in
Skopje. [@2]
