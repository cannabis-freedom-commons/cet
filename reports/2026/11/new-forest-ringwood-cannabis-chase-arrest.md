A driver was arrested after a police chase in the New Forest, Hampshire. [@1]
Officers from Ringwood Neighbourhood Policing Team were alerted by a member of
the public to a suspicious vehicle in the area. [@1] A police spokesperson said
a short pursuit followed. [@1] The driver eventually realised he was not going
to get away, the spokesperson said. [@1] He was arrested after officers
established that he was disqualified from driving, had no insurance, and failed
a roadside drugs test. [@1]

Cannabis was also located in the vehicle, the spokesperson said. [@1] Police
said they were awaiting laboratory results to confirm the level of cannabis in
the driver's system. [@1] The arrest was among a number of incidents dealt with
by officers in the New Forest over recent weeks. [@1]
