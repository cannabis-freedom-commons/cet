Three students of the CETis 60 technical school in Ramos Arizpe were detained by
municipal police after being reportedly caught consuming marijuana on a sports
court opposite the campus. [@1] CETis 60 is a Centro de Estudios Tecnológicos
Industrial y de Servicios, the public technical school where the three were
enrolled. [@3] Students alerted the authorities to classmates who were
reportedly consuming drugs in one of the passages of the sports facilities. [@1]
Two police units went to the site, located the youths and searched them. [@1]
One of the three was carrying a bag of a substance with the characteristics of a
drug. [@1] Zócalo, the Saltillo newspaper, reported that the three were caught
smoking marijuana in a passage of the sports court in daylight. [@2] All three
were taken to the municipal public security headquarters and left at the
disposal of the authorities to determine their situation. [@1]

The detentions came on the first day that a surveillance booth installed outside
the school was operating. [@1] Info7 reported that the booth was installed at
the request of school authorities to strengthen security around the institution.
[@3] Two days before the detentions the school's director had signed a
cooperation agreement with the social work faculty of the Autonomous University
of Coahuila on psychosocial support for students. [@3] The three detentions were
the first intervention reported after the booth was installed, amid school and
municipal efforts to address drug use. [@3]
