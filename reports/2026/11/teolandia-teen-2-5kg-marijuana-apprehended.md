A 15-year-old girl was apprehended in Teolândia on Tuesday 17 November 2026 in a
joint Civil and Military Police operation, for acts analogous to drug
trafficking and association for trafficking. [@1] Officers seized 2.5 kilograms
of marijuana, 934 grams of cocaine and 243 grams of crack in the operation. [@1]
They also seized two precision scales, materials for packaging drugs, two mobile
phones and portions of drugs ready for sale. [@1] Four wild birds kept illegally
in captivity were also found. [@1]

A 32-year-old man fled the police approach and remained at large, investigated
for drug trafficking, association for trafficking, rape of a vulnerable person,
corruption of minors and crimes against wildlife. [@1] The teenager was taken to
the police unit and remained at the disposal of the courts. [@1]

The operation was carried out by teams from the Territorial Police Stations of
Teolândia and Wenceslau Guimarães, linked to the 5th Regional Coordination of
Interior Police in Valença, together with the 60th Independent Military Police
Company. [@1] Police said investigations continue to locate the suspect and
identify other possible participants. [@1]
