Police on control and security duties in the Protecho Salado neighbourhood in
northern Ibagué arrested a 29-year-old woman after finding drug doses in her
handbag. [@1] The procedure took place in the Protecho Salado barrio, where
uniformed officers were carrying out control and security actions. [@1] The
detainee was placed at the disposal of the Fiscalía General de la Nación, which
is to continue the case and determine her legal situation. [@1]

During the personal search officers found 103 marijuana cigarettes weighing 200
grams. [@1] Officers also found 10 bags containing cocaine with a total weight
of 78 grams, which is excluded from the cannabis figures. [@1] Officers seized
the substances and captured the woman in flagrancia, transferring her for the
corresponding judicial procedures. [@1]

The procedure formed part of security actions led by Colonel Jeison Giraldo
Pineda, commander of the Metropolitan Police of Ibagué, aimed at combating drug
trafficking and strengthening controls in different sectors of the city. [@1]
Authorities said such operations seek to identify people allegedly dedicated to
transporting and distributing illegal substances, especially in residential
areas. [@1]
