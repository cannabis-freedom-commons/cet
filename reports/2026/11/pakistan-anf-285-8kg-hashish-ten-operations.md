Pakistan's Anti-Narcotics Force arrested seven suspects across the country on 19
November 2026 and said it had seized 335.309 kilogrammes of narcotics worth
about Rs34.1 million in 10 operations. [@1] Cases were registered against the
suspects under the Control of Narcotic Substances Act, 1997, and further
investigations were under way. [@3]

Near a hotel at Committee Chowk in Rawalpindi, officers recovered 4.5 kg of
hashish and 364 grams of ecstasy tablets from a suspect, concealed inside an MP3
player. [@1] In an inter-provincial operation on the M-3 Motorway near Pindi
Bhattian, 265.2 kg of hashish and 34.8 kg of opium were recovered from two
vehicles, with the case still under investigation. [@1] At the M-1 Motorway Toll
Plaza in Islamabad, 9.6 kg of hashish was seized from a vehicle and two suspects
were arrested, the drugs hidden in the vehicle's doors. [@2] On the Daud
Khel–Kalabagh Road in Mianwali, a motorcyclist was arrested with 4.8 kg of
hashish during an intelligence-based operation. [@1] At Adda 1-Chak on Sargodha
Road in Faisalabad, another suspect was taken into custody with 1.7 kg of
hashish and 300 grams of ice. [@3]

The five separately stated hashish seizures total 285.8 kilograms. [@1] At a
courier office in Chaklala Garrison, Rawalpindi, 10 grams of cannabis was
recovered from a jacket in a parcel destined for the United Kingdom. [@1]
Officers also recovered 1.175 kg of heroin from the trolley bag of a passenger
travelling to Jeddah for Umrah at Islamabad International Airport and arrested
the passenger. [@1] In Aabpara, Islamabad, 960 grams of heroin were recovered
from two books in a parcel destined for Spain, soaked into the pages. [@1] A
courier office in Karachi's Federal B Area yielded 3.9 kg of ice in a parcel
bound for Australia, concealed inside wooden tools. [@1] Near the Painsara
Motorway Toll Plaza in Faisalabad, 8 kg of ice was recovered from a suspect.
[@2]
