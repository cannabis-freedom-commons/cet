A registration-and-control operation in Santander de Quilichao, in the north of
Cauca, ended with the arrest of Alexander Mera after National Police officers
found narcotic substances during a search. [@1] The operation was carried out in
the El Libertador neighbourhood, specifically at carrera 15 con calle 5 number
05-101, where police were patrolling, identifying people and verifying records.
[@1]

According to information provided by the authorities, officers carried out a
personal search of Mera and found in his possession 65 grams of marijuana and
six grams of a substance believed to be coca base. [@1] On the basis of the
find, police arrested Mera in the act on suspicion of the offence of tráfico,
fabricación o porte de estupefacientes, or trafficking, manufacturing and
possession of narcotics. [@2]

Mera was then transferred and placed at the disposal of the competent authority
together with the seized substances, so that the corresponding judicial
procedures could proceed. [@1] The case was left with the authorities, who must
establish the circumstances of what happened and continue the corresponding
process. [@1]
