A joint investigation led by prosecutors Luis Lagioa of the Regional
Micro-Trafficking Prosecutor's Office and Ana Belén Stampella of the Firmat
Prosecutor's Office led to a series of raids in Firmat on Friday 13 November.
[@1] The raids followed fieldwork and observation by the Firmat Operational
District of the Police of Investigations under both prosecutors, which gathered
the indications needed to request court orders. [@1] Four men and three women
were detained in a case of alleged possession of drugs for sale. [@1]

Officers seized 47 grams of cocaine divided into one bocha and 25 wraps. [@1]
Officers seized approximately 445 grams of marijuana and one cannabis seedling.
[@1] Officers seized 27 mobile phones and more than one and a half million pesos
in cash. [@1] Officers also seized two microSD memory cards, one compressed-air
rifle, two homemade bronze pipes and other items of interest for the case file.
[@1]

The prosecutors ordered the detention and incommunicado holding of the seven
people and their later transfer to the Melincué Departmental Jail while the
investigation continues. [@1] The operation was led by the Firmat PDI
Operational District with help from the Micro-Trafficking Division and the
Melincué Operational District of Region III, plus Rosario PDI detector-dog
units, the D3 Drones air division and entry teams from Casilda, San Lorenzo and
Coronda. [@1]
