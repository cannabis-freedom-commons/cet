Nuevo León state prosecutors searched a home in the Cerradas de Cumbres
neighbourhood of Monterrey during the morning of 26 November 2026. [@2] Officers
seized marijuana inside the property. [@2] The house is on calle Manzanares
inside a gated subdivision, and the operation also reached avenida Monte
Everest, where a canine unit of the state prosecutor's office looked for
evidence. [@2] No one was detained during the search. [@2] The authorities did
not disclose how much marijuana was seized. [@2]

The search was one of three the Fiscalía General de Justicia carried out that
day, in Monterrey, Santa Catarina and Ciénega de Flores, from investigations
into drug sales, extortion and a violent robbery. [@1] At the Cerradas de
Cumbres property, besides the marijuana, officers seized other evidence that was
not described. [@1]

The other two searches that day, in Santa Catarina and Ciénega de Flores,
belonged to extortion and violent robbery cases rather than the drug case. [@1]
The Monterrey case is being handled as delitos contra la salud, Mexico's
drug-related health offences, in their narcomenudeo form, the term for retail
drug dealing. [@2]
