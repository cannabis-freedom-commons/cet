Satara taluka police in Maharashtra caught two men who had come to sell ganja
from a four-wheeler near Shendre village. [@1] They were Sahil Tanaji
Chandanshive, 25, of Mande in Khatav taluka, and Vaibhav Ramling Gadkar, 34, of
Uplai Budruk in Madha taluka, Solapur district. [@1] Officers stopped their Tata
Nexon, number MH 11 DH 9371, on a service road beside a quarry on the
Satara-Shendre road at about 5:30 pm on Saturday 21 November 2026. [@1]

A search in the presence of two witnesses found 24 kg 960 g (24.96 kg) of ganja
in the car. [@1] Police put the ganja's value at 6 lakh 24 thousand rupees, the
two mobile phones at 14 thousand and the car at about 10 lakh. [@1] The total
value of the seized property was 16 lakh 38 thousand rupees, according to
police. [@1]

A confidential tip that two men were coming to sell ganja from a vehicle in the
Shendre area prompted the taluka police's crime-detection squad to lay the trap.
[@1] The squad worked under Superintendent of Police Nikhil Pingale, Additional
SP Suraj Gurav and subdivisional police officer Vipul Patil, with Inspector
Nilesh Tambe in charge. [@1] The two gave evasive answers and obstructed the
vehicle search, which deepened the officers' suspicion. [@1] Police
sub-inspector Abhijeet Gurav is continuing the investigation. [@1]
Superintendent of Police Nikhil Pingale had ordered action against the sale and
trafficking of narcotics across the district. [@2]
