Police arrested five people at a Hartswater roadblock on 13 November 2026 after
finding drugs in their vehicle. [@1] The suspects were aged between 22 and 41
and were charged with drug possession, according to a Northern Cape police
spokesperson. [@2] They were expected to appear in court soon. [@1]

Officers from Hartswater police, the highway patrol and a dog unit stopped a
white Isuzu double-cab bakkie, or pickup truck. [@2] A search of the vehicle and
its occupants found dagga, a South African term for cannabis, along with Mandrax
tablets and crystal methamphetamine, also known as tik. [@1] Police estimated
the combined street value of the drugs at R5,000. [@2] Officers also confiscated
the vehicle because it was allegedly used in a crime. [@1] The roadblock formed
part of Operation Shanela II's high-visibility festive-season policing effort.
[@1]
