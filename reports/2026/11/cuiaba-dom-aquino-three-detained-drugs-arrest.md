Military police from the traffic battalion's motor-patrol group stopped a car in
the Dom Aquino neighbourhood of Cuiabá on the night of Tuesday 17 November 2026
and took three people into custody, seizing a variety of drugs. [@1] Those
involved were two teenagers, aged 14 and 17, and a 28-year-old man. [@1]
According to the incident report, officers stopped a vehicle carrying three men
and found various narcotic substances inside it during the check. [@1] The
17-year-old was reported to have a previous record related to drug trafficking,
and the 14-year-old was said to have taken responsibility during the incident
for the drugs found in the car. [@1]

During the searches police found portions of maconha (marijuana), pasta base de
cocaína (cocaine paste), haxixe (hashish) and synthetic drugs popularly known as
ecstasy, also called balas. [@1] Police said the variety of substances drew
their attention, with the hashish, maconha and cocaine paste all found inside
the vehicle occupied by the three. [@1]

The two teenagers were taken to Cuiabá's central de flagrantes, where they are
expected to answer for the corresponding atos infracionais, the juvenile
offences applied to minors in Brazil. [@1] The 28-year-old man was arrested and
taken to a police station, suspected of involvement in drug trafficking. [@1]
The case will be followed by the Polícia Judiciária Civil, the civilian
investigative police, which will continue the proceedings and investigations to
establish each person's participation. [@1]
