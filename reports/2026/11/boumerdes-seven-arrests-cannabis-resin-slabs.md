Boumerdès judicial police arrested seven people suspected of running a
vehicle-theft network that also included a document-forgery workshop. [@1] The
DGSN said the seven-person network was also involved in the illicit trafficking
of narcotics. [@2] The operation was carried out during the week before the
announcement. [@1]

Searches conducted under the supervision of the prosecutor seized two slabs of
processed kif. [@1] The DGSN described the same items as two small slabs of
cannabis resin. [@2] Police also recovered a stolen truck and motorcycle and
seized a pistol with live ammunition of the fourth category and an alarm gun.
[@1] Three other vehicles were immobilised during the searches. [@1]

The suspected offences included theft, forgery and use of forged official
documents, drug trafficking and illegal possession of weapons. [@1] The
network's members altered the paperwork of stolen vehicles so they could be
resold and circulate apparently legally. [@2] The seven suspects were referred
to the prosecutor of the Republic at the Boumerdès court. [@2]
