Delhi Police arrested two women in the Bawana area and recovered 12.205 kg of
suspected ganja from them. [@1] The arrests were made on 24 November 2026 during
a routine evening patrol in the jurisdiction of the Narela Industrial Area
police station. [@1] Officers noticed the two women carrying bags near
Rajasthani Mandir Wali Gali in B-Block, JJ Colony, Bawana, and found the ganja
when they searched the bags. [@1]

The accused were identified as Marjina, 35, and Krishma, 19, both residents of
JJ Colony, Bawana. [@1] Officers recovered 6.106 kg from Marjina and 6.099 kg
from Krishma. [@1] Two mobile phones were seized from the two women. [@1]

A case was registered at Narela Industrial Area police station as FIR No.
674/2026, dated 25 November 2026, under sections 20(b)(ii)(B) and 29 of the
Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act. [@1] Police arrested both
women after obtaining prior permission from the Duty Judicial Magistrate First
Class under section 43(5) of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, and seized
the contraband. [@2] Marjina told interrogators that she procured and supplied
ganja on the directions of a person identified as Kamlesh alias Raju alias Baba
and his associates, a disclosure police said was being verified. [@1] Police
said efforts were under way to identify and arrest the suppliers and other
people involved in the network. [@1]
