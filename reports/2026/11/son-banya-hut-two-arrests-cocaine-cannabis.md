On Saturday afternoon, National Police Citizen Attention Group patrols mounted
an operation against small-scale drug trafficking in the Son Banya settlement.
[@2] Officers went to a hut at the entrance to Son Banya, a type of precarious
construction used as a drug-sales point, and saw two men run from it into the
settlement as police approached. [@1] The two men, of Romanian and Paraguayan
origin, tried to flee when they noticed police and were intercepted after a
chase through the settlement. [@2]

During the chase, one suspect threw a package onto a house roof and tried to do
the same with a fanny pack before being stopped, while a patrol stayed by the
house to prevent anyone removing the roof package. [@1] Once both men were
stopped, officers entered the hut and found narcotic substances scattered on a
table, laid out for sale. [@2] In total officers seized more than fifty grams of
cocaine, marijuana and hashish combined, plus erectile-dysfunction drugs and
items for selling and distributing drugs. [@2]

A search of the two men found more than 3,000 euros in mixed notes and coins
hidden in their clothing and in the seized fanny pack. [@2] Both men were
arrested as alleged perpetrators of drug trafficking and taken to police
premises. [@2] Police identified the two detainees as a Romanian man and a
Paraguayan man held for drug trafficking. [@1]
