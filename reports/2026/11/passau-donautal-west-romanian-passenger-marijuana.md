Border police investigators from the Passau border police inspectorate checked
an Opel car with Austrian registration on the A3 at the Donautal West rest area
at 01:20 on 22 November 2026. [@1] The car was travelling in the direction of
Austria. [@1] A 33-year-old Romanian woman was in the car as a passenger. [@1]

Officers searched her handbag and found and seized a small amount of marijuana.
[@1] According to the police account, she had bought the marijuana in England
and brought it into Germany. [@1] The woman was reported for an offence under
the Consumer Cannabis Act and released after the police had finished processing
the case. [@1] The account was issued by the Passau border police inspectorate.
[@1]
