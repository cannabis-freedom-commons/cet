Officers of the Secretaría de Seguridad Ciudadana (SSC) detained a 24-year-old
man in Colonia San Felipe de Jesús, in the Gustavo A. Madero alcaldía of Mexico
City. [@1] Officers on surveillance rounds saw the man handling wrappers with
characteristics similar to those used for narcotics distribution. [@2] The
detention occurred at the crossing of Acámbaro and Apatzingán streets, where
officers carried out a preventive review and also secured cash. [@1] Officers
approached the man, asked him to stop, and searched him in line with police
action protocol. [@1] The 24-year-old was informed of his constitutional rights.
[@1]

The search found 40 wrappers containing dried green herb with characteristics
apparently of marijuana, plus cash. [@2]

He and the seized objects were taken before the corresponding Ministerio Público
agent, the prosecutor's office that will continue the investigation and
determine his legal situation. [@1] San Felipe de Jesús is a colonia
(neighbourhood) in Gustavo A. Madero, one of Mexico City's alcaldías (boroughs).
[@1] The Secretaría de Seguridad Ciudadana (SSC) is the capital's
citizen-security police force. [@1] The arrest took place during a police
operation in the colonia. [@2]
