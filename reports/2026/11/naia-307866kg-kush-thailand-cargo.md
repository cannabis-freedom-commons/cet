The Bureau of Customs–Ninoy Aquino International Airport (BOC-NAIA) seized
307.866 kilograms of high-grade marijuana, known as kush, from two shipments
from Thailand on 23 November 2026. [@1] Customs valued the seized marijuana at
an estimated 369.44 million pesos. [@1] Both shipments were bound for consignees
in Bulacan and Caloocan City. [@1]

BOC-NAIA District Collector Yasmin Mapa reported to Customs Commissioner
Charlito Martin Mendoza that personnel flagged both shipments based on
information from Thailand Customs and cargo profiling. [@1] X-ray screening
revealed suspicious images, after which the Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency
(PDEA) conducted a K9 inspection of the shipments. [@1] During the physical
examination, Customs personnel found kush in 164 boxes. [@1] The cargo
declarations listed truck side mirrors and assorted children's plastic toys.
