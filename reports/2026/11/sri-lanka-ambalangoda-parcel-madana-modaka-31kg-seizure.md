Officers of the Galle District Crime Prevention Division seized 31 kg 470 g of
cannabis-mixed madana modaka from a suspicious parcel sent to a parcel delivery
company in Ambalangoda. [@1] The parcel had been sent from the Embilipitiya area
labelled as medicines, and police searched it in the afternoon of 25 November
after receiving a complaint. [@1] A team from the division inspected the parcel
with the cooperation of officials of the Southern Province Ayurvedic Department.
[@1] Inside the parcel, 2,125 packets of the cannabis-mixed preparation were
found hidden and seized. [@1]

No arrests were reported; police said they were carrying out further
investigations with Ayurvedic Department officials to arrest suspects connected
with the parcel. [@1]
