Adonay Humberto Constantino Portillo, 24, was arrested by agents of the National
Civil Police (PNC) on Thursday 19 November 2026 after officers found several
portions of marijuana inside the backpack he was carrying. [@1,2] The detention
occurred in the district of Atiquizaya, which belongs to the municipality of
Ahuachapán Norte, and the PNC announced it through a post on its account on the
social network X. [@1,2]

In the photographs shared by the police, at least 33 portions of marijuana could
be counted, along with a mobile phone and the backpack in which the marijuana
was being transported. [@1] The PNC said Portillo would be referred to the
judicial authorities for the offence of unlawful drug trafficking, or tráfico
ilícito de drogas. [@1,2]
