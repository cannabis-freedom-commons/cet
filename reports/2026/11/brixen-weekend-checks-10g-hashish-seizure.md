Several police forces carried out a special weekend operation across Brixen,
systematically checking parks, commercial businesses and accommodation
facilities. [@1,2] The operation was mounted under decisions of the provincial committee for public order and security as part of measures to strengthen public safety and combat illegality. [@2] The municipal-area checks brought together the State Police,
the Carabinieri, the financial police with its drug-detection dog unit and the
city police. [@1,2] The deployment concentrated on particularly sensitive areas in
and around Brixen. [@1,2] Across the operation, officers checked 48 people, among
them 23 foreign nationals. [@1,2] Officers also checked six commercial businesses
during the same deployment. [@1,2] At the Station Center near the railway station,
officers encountered a 58-year-old foreign national who had no valid residence
permit. [@1,2] The area had repeatedly been the subject of residents' reports of neglect and suspected drug dealing. [@2] An expulsion order was then enforced against him. [@1,2]

In Romstraße, officers seized 10 grams of hashish together with 1.5 grams of
cocaine. [@1,2] Both drugs were already packed in sale-ready doses. [@1,2] During
the same checks, officers ordered the confiscation of a rifle that was in the
possession of the person concerned. [@1] The confiscation was ordered under Article 39 of the consolidated text of the public-security laws (T.U.L.P.S.). [@2] Taken together, the weekend operation
secured hashish, cocaine and a weapon while identifying dozens of people and
several businesses. [@1]
