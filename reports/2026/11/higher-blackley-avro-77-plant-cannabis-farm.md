Greater Manchester Police arrested 116 people and executed 30 warrants during a
24-hour Operation AVRO day of action across Manchester on 20 November 2026. [@1]
The operation began shortly after midnight, with officers executing warrants at
addresses linked to criminality following intelligence gathered through policing
and information provided by members of the public. [@1] The operation returned
to GMP's City of Manchester district and produced the highest number of arrests
since Operation AVRO began. [@2]

Early-morning warrants across Manchester turned up drugs, weapons and cash. [@1]
At an address on Blackley New Road in Higher Blackley, officers found a cannabis
farm containing 77 plants. [@2] Police estimated the plants' street value at
around £64,000. [@1]

Officers also seized £12,500 worth of nitrous oxide from a business on Fog Lane
in Didsbury and arrested three people on suspicion of concern to supply. [@1]
Officers worked with Trading Standards and seized approximately £25,000 of
illicit goods. [@1] Chief Superintendent David Meeney, from GMP's City of
Manchester district, said Operation AVRO brought specialist and neighbourhood
policing resources together to tackle the issues that mattered most to
communities. [@1]
