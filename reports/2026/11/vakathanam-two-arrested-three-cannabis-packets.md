Vakathanam police in Kottayam district arrested two Vakathanam residents, Vinith
V. Nair and Abhijith R. Nair. [@1] The pair was caught after a motorcycle was
ridden at police during a vehicle check. [@1] The check was in the Thottakadu
area of Vakathanam and was part of Operation Thoofan. [@2] The incident happened
the previous day. [@2]

Officers signalled the two men on the motorcycle to stop. [@1] They tried to
ride past at speed instead. [@1] The handlebar struck a police officer's hand
and the motorcycle lost control and overturned. [@1] A police team led by the
sub-inspector then subdued both men using force. [@1]

A search of the two men found three packets of cannabis. [@1] The three packets
were recovered in a body search. [@2] The men told police the cannabis had come
from Kochi for friends to use at a local temple festival. [@1] It was meant for
distribution to friends. [@2]
