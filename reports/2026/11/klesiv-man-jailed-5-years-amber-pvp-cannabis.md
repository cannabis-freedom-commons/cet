A court sentenced a 33-year-old resident of Klesiv in Sarny district to five
years in prison with confiscation of property for four crimes. [@1] The Rivne
regional police reported the case. [@1]

In May 2023, the man broke into the house of a fellow female villager and stole
a mobile phone, a charging cable and a wristwatch worth almost 4,000 hryvnias.
[@1] For that theft he had been given five years in prison with a probation
period. [@1] In 2026 he committed another theft. [@1] During investigative
actions at his place of residence, police seized raw amber, a homemade cold
weapon in the form of a Japanese katana sword, the psychotropic substance PVP,
cannabis and devices for using cannabis. [@1]

Investigators collected the necessary evidence and notified the man that he was
suspected after expert examinations. [@1] The court found him guilty of four
crimes and imposed five years in prison with confiscation of property. [@1] He
was also ordered to pay more than 20,000 hryvnias toward the costs of the
forensic examinations. [@1]
