Three suspected members of a gang the Interior Ministry calls Los Alarakos del
Verde were arrested when an operation that began at a home in Santa Anita ended
hours later at a warehouse in Ate. [@1] All three were detained at the first
location by officers of the National Police's Emergency Division. [@1] The
intervention began at about 14:15 on Wednesday 25 November 2026. [@1] Police
searched two properties, a home in the Virgen de las Nieves sector of Santa
Anita and a premises of the Asociación Industrial Los Artesanos in Ate. [@1,2]

Officers found about 20 kilograms of marijuana in two sacks in Santa Anita,
along with a scale, ammunition and packaging supplies. [@1] A further 50
kilograms was found in Ate, part in packages wrapped in plastic film and part in
small bags that police said were ready for sale. [@1] They also seized a loaded
9 mm pistol with ten rounds, two boxes containing 100 cartridges and another 15
loose cartridges. [@1] Two digital scales and a mobile phone were taken at the
Ate warehouse. [@1] The Interior Ministry's summary said 80 kilograms, while its
own breakdown and RPP reported about 70. [@1,2] The official summary also gave 115
rounds of ammunition against 125 in the itemised breakdown, with final figures
to depend on weighing and inventory. [@1]

A phone message linked one detainee to the supply of the drugs and led officers
to the Ate depot, with the intelligence directorate Dirin and drones supporting
the search. [@1] The three detainees and everything seized were handed to the
anti-drug directorate Dirandro, which will continue the investigation as part of
the Unified Command's operations in Lima. [@1]
