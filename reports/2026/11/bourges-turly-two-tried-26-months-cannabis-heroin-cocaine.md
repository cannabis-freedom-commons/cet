Two young men went on trial at the Bourges criminal court on Monday 23 November
2026 over a drug trafficking operation in Turly, in the northern quarters of
Bourges. [@1] The 19-year-old defendant, Kamel Benkaddouri, disrupted the
hearing, and the presiding judge had him removed from the courtroom by his
escort. [@1] Both men, aged 19 and 22, had been arrested and taken into police
custody on Saturday 10 October 2026. [@2] After being brought before the Bourges
prosecutor, one was held in pre-trial detention and the other placed under
judicial supervision pending trial. [@2]

At a hearing on 14 October 2026 the case was postponed to 23 November 2026. [@2]
