Charles Hamilton, 20, of Salisbury Road, Totton, pleaded guilty to possessing a
class B drug with intent to supply and to possessing criminal property. [@1] He
was sentenced at Southampton Crown Court. [@1] Judge William Mousley KC handed
him a two-year community order including 180 hours of unpaid community work.
[@1] The judge said his young age and the positive steps he had taken since
weighed in his favour. [@1] The judge told him a community order was the best
way to deal with the offences. [@1] Hamilton must forfeit the cash and pay £150
in costs. [@1]

Police searching his home in Salisbury Road found cannabis in his bedroom and an
outbuilding. [@1] Officers also seized vapes containing THC, the active
ingredient in cannabis, in a search on 4 November 2024. [@1] More than £4,000 in
cash was found at his home. [@1]

Hamilton gave a no-comment interview to police. [@1] His counsel, Jamie Gammon,
said Hamilton, who was 18 when arrested, had had a very long time to think about
it. [@1] The court heard he was not charged with the offences until July 2026.
[@1] Judge Mousley said it was quite clear Hamilton had been involved with
drug-related transactions for a period of time. [@1]
