Police in Dupnitsa detained a 32-year-old local man during a specialised
operation against drug-related crime, police reported. [@3] Dupnitsa criminal
investigators carried out procedural steps at the man's house in the town and
found and seized five packages of dry leaf mass there; the material was dried
leaf, not growing plants. [@2] A field test carried out at the scene reacted to
cannabis. [@4] One report describes the detained man as the owner of the
searched house. [@4]

The operation was carried out on 24 November 2026, the day before the reports
were published. [@2] The Dupnitsa district police department opened a fast-track
police procedure, a shortened pre-trial process, known in Bulgarian as бързо
полицейско производство, that police use for straightforward offences. [@2] The
duty prosecutor was notified of the case. [@1]

The arrest was one of a series of crimes and incidents that police recorded in
Kyustendil province in the previous 24 hours, according to the regional
bulletin. [@1]
