Bingol provincial gendarmerie said officers caught three people with finalized
prison sentences and handed them to the prison service. [@1] Two had finalized
terms of 6 years 3 months and 4 years 3 months for theft of property kept inside
a building, while one had 4 months 11 days for driving under the influence of
alcohol or drugs. [@1] The three were remanded by the judicial authorities and
transferred to the penal institution. [@1] Nineteen suspects were processed in
connection with the incidents. [@1]

Searches in the same work seized 16 grams of cannabis, called esrar. [@1] The
same searches seized 42 synthetic pharmacy pills and two drug-use apparatus.
[@1] Teams also seized 400 kilograms of chopped tobacco, three hunting rifles
and 18 cartridges. [@1]

The gendarmerie said its teams continued province-wide work to ensure public
peace and to catch wanted persons. [@1] In the same period, public-order,
counterterrorism, anti-smuggling, narcotics and cyber branches together with
district gendarmerie commands intervened in 17 incidents. [@1]
