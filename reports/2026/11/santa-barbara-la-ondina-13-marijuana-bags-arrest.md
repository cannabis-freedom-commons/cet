National Police officers arrested a man in flagrante during a surveillance and
control procedure in vereda Los Naranjos, in the La Ondina stream sector of
rural Santa Bárbara, Antioquia. [@1] The Antioquia police commander, Colonel
Luis Fernando Serna Zapata, reported the case and said the arrested man and the
seized substances were placed at the disposal of Fiscalía 27 Seccional. [@1]

During the intervention officers found 23 sealed bags with bazuco, 13 plastic
containers with coca base and 13 sealed bags with marijuana in the man's
possession. [@1] Authorities seized 49 packages and containers with narcotic
substances in total across the three drug types. [@1] The report gives no weight
for the substances and no identity or age for the detainee. [@1]

The man was arrested in flagrante for alleged trafficking, manufacturing or
carrying narcotics. [@1] At publication time his legal situation was still
pending before the competent judicial authority under the presumption of
innocence. [@1] The article presents the information as the objective account
supplied by the National Police and Attorney General's Office officials
conducting the criminal investigations. [@1]
