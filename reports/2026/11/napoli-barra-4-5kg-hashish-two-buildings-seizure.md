State Police officers seized about 4.5 kilograms of hashish found hidden in the
common areas of two apartment buildings in Barra, a district of eastern Naples,
and no one was arrested. [@2] Officers of the San Giovanni-Barra police station,
with a drug-detection dog unit from the general prevention and public rescue
office, searched a building on corso Sirena and found 40 packets of hashish
weighing about 4 kilograms in all inside a storage box. [@1] That hashish was
seized against unknown persons, and no suspect was identified in either
building. [@1]

In a second building on via Mastellone the dog Evan led officers to five more
packets of hashish, weighing about half a kilogram in all, hidden inside a drain
near the cellar rooms. [@3] In the corso Sirena building officers also stopped a
man found carrying five doses of marijuana and fined him administratively for
unlawful possession for personal use rather than charging him. [@1]

The Naples police headquarters said the checks were carried out the day before
its 21 November statement, placing them on 20 November 2026, as part of
extraordinary territorial controls against drug possession and trafficking. [@1]
The operation was run by the San Giovanni-Barra police station with a
drug-detection dog unit of the Ufficio Prevenzione Generale e Soccorso Pubblico.
[@2]
