In Voronezh Oblast a 35-year-old Voronezh resident will stand trial accused of
attempted drug sale on an especially large scale and illegal storage of banned
substances. [@1] Deputy regional prosecutor Denis Sazin approved the indictment
in the criminal case against him. [@2] He is accused of attempted illegal sale
of narcotics via the internet by an organised group on an especially large
scale. [@1] He is also accused of illegal acquisition and storage of narcotics
on a large scale without intent to sell. [@1] The criminal case was sent to
Ramonsky District Court for consideration on the merits. [@1]

According to investigators, in August 2026 he received information about a
hidden amphetamine cache in Moscow Oblast and travelled there to collect it for
later distribution in Voronezh Oblast. [@2] While transporting the substances he
was detained in Ramonsky District. [@2] The regional FSB department stopped his
activity and seized more than 1.2 kg of banned substances from circulation. [@2]
During a later search of his home officers found hashish weighing more than 29
kg, which was also seized. [@2] The prosecution reported the court referral on
19 November 2026. [@3]
