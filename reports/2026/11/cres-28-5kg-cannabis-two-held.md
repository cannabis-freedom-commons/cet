Police on the Croatian island of Cres filed criminal reports against a
39-year-old Croatian and a 32-year-old Nepali on suspicion of cultivating and
dealing drugs. [@1] The investigation was carried out by officers of the Mali
Lošinj Police Station, which has a branch on Cres, against the two men on
suspicion of unauthorised production and trafficking of drugs. [@2] After the
criminal investigation both suspects were handed to a detention supervisor with
a criminal report sent to the competent state prosecution. [@3]

Officers from the Mali Lošinj station with a Cres branch seized about 28.5
kilograms of cannabis and around 36 grams of cannabis resin on the island,
according to the Primorje-Gorski Kotar Police Directorate, which announced the
result on Thursday 19 November. [@1] The drugs were found during searches of the
home, other rooms and areas of the family agricultural holding on Cres used by
the 39-year-old. [@1] Alongside the cannabis and resin, police seized two fans
and a lighting lamp suspected of being linked to the offence. [@1] Two
unregistered air rifles were found during a separate search of the 39-year-old's
home on the previous day, 18 November. [@4]

A months-long investigation established that the 39-year-old owner of the family
agricultural holding had abused a permit for growing organic fruit and
vegetables to cultivate cannabis on the plot. [@5] Police suspect he engaged the
32-year-old Nepali man, who had no regulated residence in Croatia and whose
previous work permit had expired, to help with the cultivation. [@1]
Investigators say the two men hid and repackaged cannabis in the house for
further resale and illicit gain. [@1] Besides the criminal case, the Croatian
man faced administrative proceedings under the weapons law and the Nepali under
the foreigners law. [@1]
