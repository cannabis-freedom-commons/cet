Sultanabad police took two men into custody in Garrepally village of Sultanabad
mandal after finding them moving around with ganja. [@1] Sub-Inspector
Chandrakumar said police noticed two men looking suspicious near the Pochamma
temple on the road leading to the Maneru stream on the outskirts of Garrepally
on Monday and searched them. [@1]

During the search, police found 30 grams of ganja on 19-year-old Nyathari Srikar
alias Don and 10 grams on 23-year-old Kommu Sai, making 40 grams in total. [@1]
Police seized the 40 grams of ganja. [@1]

Questioning indicated that Srikar had purchased the ganja from Macherla Raviteja
alias Bittu of Bhupalapatnam village and was reselling it to others at higher
prices. [@1] Police registered a case against both men under the Narcotic Drugs
and Psychotropic Substances Act. [@1] The sub-inspector urged people to stay
away from the purchase, sale, use and transport of ganja and other drugs and to
inform police immediately if they learn of such illegal activity. [@1]
