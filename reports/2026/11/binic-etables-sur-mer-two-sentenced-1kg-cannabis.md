The two main protagonists of a drug-trafficking case in Côtes-d'Armor were
sentenced to two years in prison on Sunday 22 November 2026. [@1] The
investigation had begun four days earlier, during a check by gendarmes of the
communauté de brigades de Binic-Étables-sur-Mer, a grouping of local gendarmerie
brigades. [@1] It started on Wednesday 18 November 2026, when gendarmes on
patrol went to the home of a woman in her forties because her vehicle was badly
parked. [@1] When the door was opened, the officers noticed a very strong smell
of cannabis coming from inside. [@1]

In the home they found a bag containing 400 grams of cannabis resin and 600
grams of cannabis herb, a total of one kilogram of cannabis. [@1] The same bag
held 300 grams of cocaine. [@1] The gendarmes also found drug-packaging material
at the property. [@1]

The case was described as a trafficking network dismantled near Saint-Brieuc,
with three weapons and a car among the items seized. [@1]
