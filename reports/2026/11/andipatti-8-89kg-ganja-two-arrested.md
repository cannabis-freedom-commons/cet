Police in Theni district arrested two men on Sunday 22 November 2026 on
suspicion of possessing ganja near Andipatti. [@1] The arrests followed
intensified vehicle checks across the district after police received information
that ganja was being smuggled through Theni. [@1] Officers stopped two men
walking with a bag near the S. Ranganathapuram bus stop outside Andipatti and
searched them. [@1] The bag held 8 kilograms 890 grams of ganja, which police
seized. [@1]

Officers took the two men to Andipatti police station, where they were
questioned. [@1] There they were identified as Eswaran and Manoharan, and the
inquiry found they had brought the ganja from Andhra Pradesh. [@1] Police
arrested both. [@1] The older man, Manoharan, is 61 and from Varushanadu
Singarajapuram, while Eswaran is 23 and from Gandhipuram in Viluppuram district.
[@2] The ganja was packed in four packets, and the men told police they had
bought it in Andhra Pradesh to sell. [@2]
