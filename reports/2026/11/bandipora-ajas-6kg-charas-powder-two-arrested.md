Police in Bandipora district, Jammu and Kashmir, arrested two alleged drug
peddlers and recovered approximately six kilograms of charas powder during a
naka checking (police checkpoint) operation in the Ajas area, officials said on
Thursday. [@1,3] The two were identified as Altaf Ahmad Rather, son of Abdul Gani
Rather, and Asif Ahmad Rather, son of Mohammad Shafi Rather, both residents of
Ajas. [@1,2,3]

The naka party of Police Post Ajas intercepted two people moving suspiciously
with bags from Sadunara towards Ajas at Pariwan. [@1,3] On noticing the police
personnel, the two allegedly attempted to flee but were apprehended by the
police party. [@1,3] During a search, approximately six kilograms of contraband
substance identified as charas powder was recovered from their possession,
officials said. [@1,3]

Police registered FIR No. 246/2026 under Sections 8/20 of the NDPS Act at Police
Station Sumbal and arrested both accused. [@1,2,3] Further investigation was under
way, police said. [@1,3]
