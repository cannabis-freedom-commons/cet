Rajshahi Metropolitan Police said its Rajpara thana and the Shiroil police
outpost of Boalia thana carried out separate anti-drug drives in Rajshahi city,
arresting one drug trader and one drug user and recovering ganja. [@1] Rajpara
thana police arrested Md. Kamrul Hasan, 47, of Thakurmara Colony village in
Kashiadanga thana, at Dingadoba Mozammel's Mor at about 6 pm on 21 November
2026. [@1] Police recovered 220 grams of ganja, the local term for cannabis,
from his custody and said it was kept for sale. [@1] Police said Kamrul Hasan
already faced one case under the Narcotics Control Act at Kashiadanga thana.
[@1]

At about 5 am the same day the Shiroil police outpost of Boalia thana carried
out a drive at Barnali Mor in the city. [@1] There officers arrested Md. Shuvo
Islam Joy, 24, of Shoshthitola village on suspicion of behaving disorderly after
using ganja and yaba. [@1] He was examined at the Divisional Police Hospital in
Rajshahi. [@1] A case was filed against him under the Narcotics Control Act on
the doctor's opinion. [@1]

Police filed separate cases under the Narcotics Control Act at the relevant
thanas and sent both arrested men to jail through the court. [@1]
