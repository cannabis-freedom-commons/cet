Palakkad Railway Police arrested Sameen Seikh, 27, in West Bengal as the alleged
mastermind of a ganja smuggling network operating in Kerala. [@1,3,5] A special
investigation team under Railway Superintendent P. B. Krishnakumar, led by
Deputy Superintendent Manoj Parayatt and Inspector Prathap A, travelled to West
Bengal and arrested him after discreet surveillance. [@1,4,5] Officers located his
hideout and house at Hameedpur in Purbasthali, Purba Bardhaman, and detained him
around midnight after surrounding the place. [@2] Police said they had watched
him for days, found the hideout and house, and subdued him around midnight by
surrounding the place. [@2]

He is the first person charged in a case registered after police arrested two
men with 22.403 kilograms of ganja at Palakkad Junction railway station on 19
September 2026. [@1,4,5] Asim Seikh, 23, and Sahidul Mallik, 28, were allegedly
transporting the ganja from West Bengal to Kerala by train via Chennai. [@1,4,5]
Masood Seikh, 28, another link in the network, had earlier been arrested in
Kozhikode. [@1,4,5] Questioning of those suspects identified Sameen, a native of
Purba Bardhaman district, as the organiser, police said. [@1,4,5] Police said he would be brought to Kerala and produced before the Palakkad Judicial First Class Magistrate Court after the required procedures. [@4,5]

Police said Sameen had come to Kerala about 12 years earlier for construction
work and had lived in Mahe and Thalassery. [@1,4,5] Police said he also has a previous case under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act registered at the Vazhakkad police station in Malappuram district. [@4,5] Police said he later used migrant workers to move ganja by train to Palakkad, Tirur and Kozhikode. [@4,5]
