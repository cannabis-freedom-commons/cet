Police carried out four searches in barrio La Tosquera in San Pedro in an
investigation into the theft of animals and items from a rural dwelling. [@2]
The searches had been ordered by Guarantees Court 3 of the San Nicolás judicial
department, with the San Pedro UFI 11 intervening, after investigative work.
[@1] The four procedures took place on Friday 13 November in four dwellings,
three on Laprida street and one in the Cuartel Tercero area near the La Tosquera
chapel. [@2] The three Laprida targets yielded negative results for the items
sought. [@1]

Officers did not find the items that prompted the warrants in the fourth house
either, but detected weapons, cartridges and drugs there. [@1] They seized a
Tejano .32-long revolver, a sawn-off single-barrel 36-calibre shotgun,
cartridges of different calibres and 505 grams of marijuana. [@1] The procedures
were carried out by the Tactical Operations Group with the San Pedro communal
police station, the Río Tala and La Tosquera outposts, the DDI, the Rural
Prevention Command and the Departmental Support Group. [@1]

A 20-year-old woman was apprehended at the house and a 25-year-old man was
identified in the case. [@2] The 20-year-old resident was notified of the
formation of proceedings against her. [@1] Prosecutor's Office 11 ordered the
seizure of all the items and the corresponding steps under article 60 of the
Criminal Procedure Code. [@1] UFI 11 ordered the seizure, the corresponding
notifications and then the release of both people, while the judicial
proceedings continue. [@2] The investigation continues under judicial
supervision to determine the circumstances of the finds, which could lead to
further action. [@1]
