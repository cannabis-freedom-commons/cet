Four people — two men and two women — who had been held by residents of
Tlacotepec de Benito Juárez, Puebla, over an alleged vehicle theft were detained
by the Secretaría de Marina (the Mexican Navy) in an operation on Camino Real a
la Tepetatera. [@1] Residents accused them of taking part in the theft of a
vehicle and threatened to lynch them before security forces arrived. [@1] The
intervention took place in Tlacotepec de Benito Juárez municipality on 19
November 2026. [@3]

During the intervention, in which municipal and state police also took part,
officers seized three long firearms, a replica gun, 15 cartridges, 39 doses of
marihuana and 96 doses of cristal. [@1] The Navy said the operation yielded 135
doses of suspected drugs, 39 of green herb resembling marihuana and 96 wraps of
a substance resembling cristal. [@2] The 39 doses of presumed marihuana and 96
doses of a cristal-like substance were found alongside three long firearms, a
replica firearm and 15 cartridges. [@4]

The four detainees and the seized items were handed to the Fiscalía General de
la República (FGR), which will decide their legal status; their identities were
not released and the theft allegation was not confirmed. [@1] The FGR was to
continue the investigation and define the legal situation of the four. [@2]
