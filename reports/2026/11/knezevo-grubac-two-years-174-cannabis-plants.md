Danijel Grubač, 33, from Kneževo was sentenced in the Basic Court in Kotor Varoš
to two years in prison and a 3,000 convertible mark fine for growing marijuana,
illegally keeping weapons and endangering his uncle's safety. [@1] The court
accepted a plea agreement Grubač reached with the Banja Luka District Public
Prosecutor's Office. [@1] He received a single two-year prison term for
unauthorised production and trade of narcotic drugs and for illegal possession
of weapons or explosives, while the fine was imposed for endangering safety.
[@1] The fine is due within 30 days of the judgment becoming final, and if it is
not paid each 100 marks owed can be converted into one day in prison. [@1]

According to the judgment, on 21 July 2026 in Imljani near Kneževo Grubač
threatened his uncle Zoran Grubač in the yard of the family house the younger
man uses. [@1] He called his uncle over and told him, holding him by the arm,
that he had called him because he wanted to kill him. [@1] He then took an
automatic rifle without a magazine and asked a woman identified only as M.M. to
bring him ammunition. [@1] Holding his uncle's arm, he threatened to kill him
and his brothers and pressed a pistol to his chest, saying "Look me in the eyes"
and "I will kill you". [@1] The uncle asked to be let into the toilet and used
the chance to run out of the yard. [@1]

Police searched the house and yard Grubač uses in Imljani the next day, on 22
July. [@1] Officers found an automatic rifle, a converted nine-millimetre gas
signal pistol with a magazine and 182 rounds of ammunition of various calibres.
[@1] In the yard police found a small marijuana plantation, where Grubač had
planted Indian hemp stalks in 174 plastic cups filled with soil, between 1.5 and
13 centimetres tall. [@1] He was convicted of illegal weapons possession and
unauthorised production and trade of narcotic drugs on the basis of the weapons
and the hemp plot. [@2]

Grubač had been convicted before in Serbia and Austria, receiving a one-year
term in Zaječar in 2020 for serious bodily injury. [@1] A Vienna criminal court
sentenced him to two years in prison in 2022 under the narcotics law. [@1]
