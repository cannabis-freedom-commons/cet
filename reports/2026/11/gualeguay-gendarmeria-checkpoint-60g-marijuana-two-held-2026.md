Gendarmería Nacional detained two adults at a preventive vehicle check in
Gualeguay, Entre Ríos. [@1] The check was run by the force's Gualeguay section
of Squadron 56 Gualeguaychú on provincial route 11 on Thursday 19 November 2026.
[@2] Officers saw one of the vehicle's occupants throw a bag into vegetation
beside the road. [@1]

Searching the spot in front of witnesses, the officers found a bag of plant
material and a piece of clothing holding several wrappings of white powder. [@2]
Field tests identified the plant material as cannabis sativa weighing 60 grams,
and the powder as cocaine in 83 wrappings weighing 15 grams. [@2]

The Federal Prosecutor's Office of Gualeguaychú ordered the judicial measures in
the case. [@2] Officers seized the drugs, the vehicle and several mobile phones.
[@1] Both detainees remained at the disposal of the court in a case for
suspected infringement of National Narcotics Law 23.737. [@1]
