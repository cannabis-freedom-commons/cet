Police Regional Office 4A recorded 27 anti-illegal drug operations that resulted
in the arrest of 36 suspects and the confiscation of 128.16 grams of suspected
shabu and 3.58 grams of suspected marijuana kush. [@1] The operations were
recorded from 6 a.m. on 19 November to 6 a.m. on 20 November 2026, as regional
police intensified action against wanted individuals, illegal drugs, gambling
and loose firearms. [@1]

PRO 4A put the combined estimated value of the seized illegal drugs at 876,858
pesos, based on the Dangerous Drugs Board valuation cited in the police report.
[@1] In the same 24-hour series of operations police arrested 48 wanted persons,
including 17 classified as most wanted. [@1] Police conducted 58 operations
against illegal gambling, resulting in the apprehension of 119 individuals and
the confiscation of 15,538 pesos in alleged gambling proceeds. [@1] The account
was filed from Camp Vicente Lim in Laguna. [@1]

The regional police office said its continuing anti-criminality campaign aims to
strengthen public safety and maintain peace and order throughout Cavite, Laguna,
Batangas, Rizal and Quezon. [@1] PRO 4A Regional Director Police Brigadier
General Dominic L. Baccay has been overseeing the B.E.S.T. policing framework,
which emphasizes police visibility, immediate community engagement, coordinated
service and public trust. [@1]
