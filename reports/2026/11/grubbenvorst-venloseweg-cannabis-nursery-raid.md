Police found a cannabis nursery, called a hennepkwekerij in Dutch, on Tuesday on
Venloseweg in Grubbenvorst. [@1] The discovery was made as part of an ongoing
criminal investigation. [@1] The operation was carried out together with several
chain partners, called ketenpartners in Dutch. [@1]

During the operation officers traced the nursery and then dismantled it. [@1]
Police are continuing to investigate the involvement of suspects and the
circumstances surrounding the nursery. [@1]

Cannabis cultivation is prohibited and is often accompanied by risks such as
fire hazard, electricity fraud and undermining crime. [@1] Police emphasise the
importance of reports of suspicious situations for tracing such locations. [@1]
No further details about any arrests or the size of the nursery had been
published at the time. [@1]
