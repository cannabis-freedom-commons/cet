Ingolstadt traffic police stopped a 30-year-old driver under the influence of
drugs on the motorway at about 14:15 on Wednesday, checking him at the
Ingolstadt-Süd junction as he drove toward Nuremberg. [@1] Officers found
drug-typical abnormalities, a test reacted to cannabis and a blood draw was
ordered; if the suspicion is confirmed the man faces a fine and a driving ban,
and he had to leave his car. [@1]

Police in Ingolstadt's Nördliche Ringstraße stopped a 26-year-old German woman
riding an e-scooter at about 21:15 on Wednesday evening after she showed signs
of drug influence, and a rapid test reacted positively to cannabis. [@2] A
27-year-old German car driver checked at about 22:30 at Brückenkopf and a
40-year-old Hungarian car driver checked shortly before 23:00 in Eriagstraße
were also under the influence of cannabis. [@2] All three evening road users
underwent blood draws and administrative offence proceedings were initiated
under the road traffic law. [@2]
