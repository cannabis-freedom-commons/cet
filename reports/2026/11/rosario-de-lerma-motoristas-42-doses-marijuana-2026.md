Motorcycle officers of the Valle de Lerma emergency police division were on a
preventive patrol near finca Las Peras in the locality of Rosario de Lerma,
Salta. [@1] They identified a 37-year-old man and checked what he was carrying,
finding that he was handling a wrapper of plant material. [@1]

Personnel of the División Investigación Narcocriminal 3 carried out field tests
and seized 42 doses of marijuana. [@1] The seizure of the 42 doses was made
during the preventive patrol near finca Las Peras, in the locality of Rosario de
Lerma. [@2] The 37-year-old man was cited for an offence under the Narcotics Act
23.737. [@1] The flagrancy prosecutor's unit took the case. [@3]
