Rajkot Range police team raided a field on the outskirts of Nana Matra village
in Vinchhiya taluka and arrested the farmer cultivating cannabis there. [@2] The
arrested farmer was identified as Lakshman Danabhai Garambhadiya, owner of the
'Khodiyar vala' field on the village's eastern boundary. [@1] Police registered
a case against him at Vinchhiya police station under the NDPS Act and began
investigating where he obtained the seed and to whom he intended to sell the
crop. [@1]

Officers seized a total of 39 small and large green cannabis plants with roots
from the field. [@1] The seized plants weighed 15 kg 400 g in total. [@1] Police
valued the seized cannabis plants at Rs 7.70 lakh. [@3] The accused had grown
and tended the cannabis illegally among the main cotton crop to hide it from
police. [@1]

The raid followed a tip-off and was carried out under the guidance of PI Apurva
Patel on the instructions of Rajkot Range IG Nirlipat Rai to act against
narcotics in the range's districts. [@1] Vinchhiya police station staff joined
the Range team, including PSI B.C. Miyatra and others, in the operation. [@1]
Police were seeking the accused's remand to trace the seed source and the
intended buyers and receivers in the chain. [@3] The operation was reported as a
further cannabis-cultivation catch in the Vinchhiya area after finds at Chhasia
and Kharchiya villages. [@4]
