Gulshan-e-Maymar police in Karachi acted near the Afghan Camp and broke up an
attempt to supply charas worth crores of rupees, recovering the drugs from a
Vigo vehicle. [@2] Aaj News likewise reported the seizure as 216 kg of charas
recovered from a double-cabin vehicle near the Afghan Camp. [@1] According to
police, the suspect at the scene managed to flee. [@2]

Officers recovered 180 packets holding 216 kg of charas from a hidden
compartment of the Vigo pickup. [@2] Each packet held 1,200 grams, making
216,000 grams in total. [@3] SSP West said the seized charas was worth crores of
rupees on the international market. [@2]

Police registered a case against the absconding suspect, while efforts to arrest
him continued. [@3] Other recoveries described alongside this one, including
more than 28 kg of charas taken from a rickshaw in a Sachal police action, are
separate events and are not counted here. [@2]
