A pensioner in Vyazniki was sentenced by the Vyaznikovsky City Court to one year
and three months in a general-regime colony. [@1] The court found that she
planted cannabis seeds in May 2026 and tended the plants through the summer.
[@1] After harvesting, she moved the dried plant parts into a garage. [@1]

On 13 August police found and seized more than four kilograms of cannabis. [@1]
At trial she said she grew and stored the plant for treatment rather than sale,
but this did not exempt her from responsibility. [@1] The verdict had not yet
entered into legal force. [@1]
