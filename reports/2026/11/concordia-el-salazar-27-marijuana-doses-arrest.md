National Police officers arrested a man in flagrante in the El Salazar
neighbourhood of Concordia, Antioquia, after finding drugs in his possession.
[@1] The police procedure took place on Sunday 15 November 2026. [@1] The arrest
was made for the alleged offence of trafficking, manufacture or possession of
narcotics under article 376 of Colombia's Penal Code. [@1] The man and the
seized substances were placed at the disposal of Fiscalia 034 Local de
Concordia, the local prosecutor's office, through a criminal report for the
judicial process. [@1]

During the operation uniformed officers reported finding three types of
substances in the man's possession. [@1] The institutional report listed 27
doses of marijuana with a gross weight of 140 grams. [@1] Officers also recorded
29 doses of cocaine hydrochloride at 50 grams gross and 101 doses of coca base
at 180 grams gross, bringing the combined total to 157 doses. [@1] The
institution's weights are reported as gross weights as presented in the
procedure bulletin. [@1]

The report gave no age for the detainee. [@1] His alleged responsibility must
still be determined by the competent authorities, and arrest does not equal
conviction. [@1]
