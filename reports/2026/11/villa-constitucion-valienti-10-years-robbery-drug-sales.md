Joel Maximiliano Valienti was sentenced to 10 years in effective prison on 18
November 2026 by judge Marisol Usandizaga, who approved an abbreviated procedure
agreed by the prosecution and the defence. [@1] The prosecution, led by
prosecutor Ramiro Martínez, charged him with robbery doubly qualified by the use
of a firearm fit to fire and by being committed in a populated place and as a
gang, unlawful carrying of a war firearm and commercialization of narcotics.
[@1]

One of the crimes behind the conviction was a robbery on 5 September 2024 at
about 23:00 at a home on calle Bolivia al 200 in Pavón, where the attackers
kicked in the door and, armed with a 9 mm Bersa Thunder Pro pistol, beat one
victim on the head with the butt of the gun and held another by the neck while
demanding money. [@1] The attackers took three mobile phones and a cap and fired
at least one shot as they fled. [@1] Valienti was detained after a police chase
at the junction of Almafuerte and Pública 2 in Empalme Villa Constitución. [@1]

A separate investigation accused him of selling cocaine and marijuana as a
street seller from at least 27 August 2024 to 25 April 2025, alongside others in
the same drug-selling structure. [@1] In raids on various homes, investigators
seized mobile phones, cash, digital scales and substances that tested positive
for marijuana and cocaine. [@1] The items included jars of marijuana, a plant
about one metre tall, dried branches and wrappers of fractionated plant
material, as well as equipment used to divide substances into doses. [@1] At
Valienti's home investigators found four mobile phones, 151,000 pesos in cash, a
small quantity of marijuana and a scale that tested positive for cocaine. [@1]

The 10-year sentence covers both the Pavón robbery and the commercialization of
narcotics. [@1]
