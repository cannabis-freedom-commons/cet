Police detained two young men with mephedrone and hybrid ganja on the road
opposite the Rukmani Chainani maternity home at SSG Hospital in Vadodara city.
[@1] Officers caught the pair around midnight while they were travelling in a
Thar vehicle to sell drugs. [@1] The operation was carried out jointly by the
LCB Zone-2 team together with Raopura police. [@1]

Police said they seized 6 grams of MD and 8 grams of hybrid ganja near SSG
Hospital. [@1] The cannabis portion was 8 grams of hybrid ganja, recorded here
as 0.008 kilograms. [@1] The same stop also yielded 6 grams of MD, and that
weight is not counted in the cannabis total. [@1] Police linked the seizure to
the midnight movement of the Thar on that road. [@1]

SSG Hospital stands in Vadodara city, and the stop took place on the road facing
its maternity block. [@1] The men used a Thar vehicle, and officers said they
had set out in it to sell the drugs. [@1] The two detained men were accused of
taking drugs in the Thar for sale. [@1]
