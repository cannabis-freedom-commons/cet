Police detained two young men with mephedrone and hybrid ganja on the road
opposite the Rukmani Chainani maternity home at SSG Hospital in Vadodara city.
[@1,2] Police identified the two accused as Aman Shahid Memon (23) and Shafin Raza Rizwanali Sarani (21), both residents of Vadodara city. [@2] Officers caught the pair around midnight while they were travelling in a
Thar vehicle to sell drugs. [@1,2] The LCB team and Raopura police laid a trap and intercepted the vehicle at around 2.45 am on Monday. [@2] The operation was carried out jointly by the
LCB Zone-2 team together with Raopura police. [@1,2] Police identified two alleged suppliers who were yet to be apprehended, Mehtab alias Mama for the mephedrone and a man known as Gatto for the hybrid ganja. [@2] A case was registered at Raopura police station under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985, with further investigation underway into the source and supply network. [@2]

Police said they seized 6 grams of MD and 8 grams of hybrid ganja near SSG
Hospital. [@1,2] The Thar vehicle was also seized, taking the total value of the seized goods to Rs 16.005 lakh. [@2] Police valued the eight grams of hybrid ganja at about Rs 28,000. [@2] Police also recovered a small digital weighing scale, two iPhones and Rs 19,400 in cash, as well as empty plastic pouches allegedly used for packaging. [@2] The cannabis portion was 8 grams of hybrid ganja, recorded here
as 0.008 kilograms. [@1] The same stop also yielded 6 grams of MD, and that
weight is not counted in the cannabis total. [@1] Police linked the seizure to
the midnight movement of the Thar on that road. [@1]

SSG Hospital stands in Vadodara city, and the stop took place on the road facing
its maternity block. [@1] The men used a Thar vehicle, and officers said they
had set out in it to sell the drugs. [@1] The two detained men were accused of
taking drugs in the Thar for sale. [@1]
