Ankara police detained Aras Tan, son of Istanbul MP Namık Tan of the Yeni Parti
(New Party), in a security operation over drug and prostitution crimes. [@1] The
operation was carried out two days before the report, under the supervision of
the Ankara Chief Public Prosecutor's Office and by the anti-narcotics crimes
branch of the regional security directorate. [@1] The investigation covered
promoting the drug trade and drug use, as well as inciting, arranging or forcing
people into prostitution. [@1] Hürriyet reported that Aras Tan was among the
suspects detained in the Ankara drug and prostitution investigation. [@2]

Officers searched the addresses of the detained suspects, and reports said Tan's
residence contained hashish and a tool for grinding drugs. [@1] Hürriyet, citing
judicial sources, said a search of Aras Tan's home found esrar (cannabis resin)
and a drug grinder. [@2] Judicial sources said the hashish and grinding tool
seized at the address remained under examination. [@1]

Tan's legal status had not been announced and would be settled after the
judicial procedures were completed, with the case still at the investigation
stage. [@1]
