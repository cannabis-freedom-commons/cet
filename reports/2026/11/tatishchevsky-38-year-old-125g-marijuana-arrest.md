Police in Tatishchevsky District, Saratov Oblast, detained a 38-year-old local
man on suspicion of illegal drug trafficking, acting on information from the
region's Interior Ministry directorate. [@1] The regional Interior Ministry said
the man was a resident of one of the district's villages and gave the details of
the operation. [@3]

During a search of his house and outbuildings, officers seized packets of
marijuana with a total weight of about 125 grams and more than a kilogram of
parts of cannabis plants. [@1] A separate account said the substances had been
hidden in caches on the detainee's household property before the search. [@4]

A criminal case was opened under part 2 of Article 228 of the Russian Criminal
Code. [@2] The suspect was placed under an undertaking not to leave and to
behave properly (подписка о невыезде и надлежащем поведении). [@1]
