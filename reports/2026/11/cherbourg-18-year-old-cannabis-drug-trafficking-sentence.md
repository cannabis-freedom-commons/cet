A Cherbourg court sentenced an 18-year-old man to 30 months in prison, 10 of
them suspended with probation for two years, leaving 20 months to serve. [@2] He
was tried by comparution immédiate, France's immediate-appearance procedure, for
drug trafficking, refusal to comply and hit-and-run, and was kept in detention.
[@1] Investigators described his role in a cocaine, heroin and cannabis
trafficking operation in the north Cotentin as greater than he admitted. [@1]
Domiciled in Lison, Calvados, he appeared before the judicial court of Cherbourg
on 26 November 2026 over acts committed between 1 and 16 October. [@2] He was
charged as a repeat offender with holding, transporting, offering, transferring
and acquiring cocaine, heroin and cannabis. [@2]

Four days after the 12 October chase, police saw him driving a Cupra and
arrested him when he tried to escape. [@2] Investigators found 1.3 g of cannabis
on him, along with 16 bags of cocaine totalling 12.6 g, 17.8 g of heroin and 580
euros in cash. [@2] The tribunal also referred to a 22 September search at an
Airbnb in Tourlaville rented by the defendant. [@2] That search seized 23 g of
cannabis, 340 g of cocaine and 4,031 euros. [@2] The sentence included an
obligation to find work and follow treatment. [@2] He was banned from appearing
in Cherbourg for three years and the seized exhibits were confiscated. [@2] He
was ordered to pay the accident victim 5,000 euros in material damages and 2,500
euros for moral damage. [@2]

On 12 October police pursued a dark Toyota at 100 km/h along rue de l'Abbaye
towards Querqueville. [@2] During the pursuit the driver braked sharply, ran a
stop sign and struck another car before fleeing on foot. [@2] The other car was
written off and its driver injured. [@2] Inside the Toyota rented from a Caen
agency, investigators found numerous coloured 'cocottes'. [@2] Each was marked
with a gram weight and held cocaine or heroin. [@2] He described himself as a
courier, but prosecutors said he had an organiser's role in contact with
suppliers. [@2] His latest of three prior convictions was a firm six months from
the Caen court on 6 October 2026 for refusal to comply, damage, driving without
a licence and drug use. [@2]
