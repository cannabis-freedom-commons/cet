Border police from the Passau inspectorate checked a Citroën with Austrian
registration parked at the Donautal-Ost motorway rest area at about 15:00 on
Wednesday 25 November 2026. [@1] The car had entered Germany from Austria on the
A3 shortly before. [@1] During a search of the vehicle, officers found a small
amount of cannabis in a storage compartment in the passenger door. [@1] They
seized the cannabis. [@1]

The passenger, a 24-year-old Austrian, is under investigation for importing
cannabis. [@1] The investigation concerns import, meaning bringing the drug into
Germany across the border. [@1]
