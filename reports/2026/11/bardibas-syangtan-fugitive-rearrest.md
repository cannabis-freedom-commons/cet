Ranglal Syangtan, a resident of Bardibas Municipality-11 in Mahottari district,
was arrested on Friday as a fugitive defendant in a narcotics case. [@1] A
police team dispatched from the Narcotics Control Bureau branch office in
Bardibas carried out the arrest. [@1] The bureau's Koteshwor office said
officers arrested him on Friday in Bardibas Municipality-1. [@1] The arrest was
reported from Kathmandu on 4 Mangsir as the capture of a fugitive defendant in a
narcotics case in Mahottari. [@3] Syangtan is a resident of Bardibas
Municipality-11, and the Bardibas branch team is the unit that arrested him.
[@2]

Police had arrested Syangtan once before, on 26 Jestha 2082, when he was with an
electric van and 40 kilograms of marijuana. [@2] He was afterwards held at
Mahottari District Jail. [@2] He absconded from that jail during the Gen Z
movement. [@2] He was still a fugitive defendant in the narcotics case when
officers arrested him again on Friday in Bardibas Municipality-1. [@2]
