Border Guard Bangladesh's Lalmonirhat Battalion (15 BGB) seized 7 kg 500 g of
Indian ganja, a motorcycle and 78 bottles of Iscap syrup in two separate raids
on the Fulbari border in Kurigram. [@1] The battalion announced the seizures in
a press release on Tuesday 17 November. [@1] BGB said the two raids were carried
out on the night of Monday 16 November in areas held by its Gangarhat and
Anantpur border outposts, as part of its Operation Narcotics Strike. [@1]

Acting on secret information, the Gangarhat outpost team raided the North
Vidyabagis area at about 8:30 pm and seized 7 kg 500 g of Indian ganja and a
motorcycle. [@1] A second report of the raids describes a checkpoint set up on
intelligence at North Vidyabagis under the Gangarhat outpost, where BGB members
seized about 7.5 kg of ganja and a motorcycle. [@2]

Shortly afterwards, at 11:50 pm, the Anantpur outpost team raided the Nagraj
area and seized 78 bottles of Iscap syrup, which is not a cannabis product and
is left out of the cannabis figures. [@1]

BGB estimated the market value of the drugs seized in the two raids at Tk
182,450, a combined figure covering both the ganja and the syrup. [@1] No
arrests were reported; BGB said intelligence work was continuing to identify
those involved and that legal action against them was being prepared. [@1]
Lieutenant Colonel Mehedi Imam, commander of the Lalmonirhat Battalion, said
surveillance and patrols had been stepped up in sensitive border areas and that
anti-drug and anti-smuggling operations would continue. [@1]
