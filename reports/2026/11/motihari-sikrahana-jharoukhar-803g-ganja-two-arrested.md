Police in Motihari, Bihar, arrested two people on suspicion of selling ganja.
[@1] In Sikrahana, station head Gopal Kumar was told ganja was being sold at a
kiosk near Sundaram Devi Chowk. [@2] A search found 375 grams of ganja. [@2] The
shopkeeper, Chandrika Yadav, was arrested. [@2] The second action was in the
Jharoukhar police station area. [@2] Police arrested Deepak Kumar with 428 grams
of ganja. [@2]

The two seizures totalled 803 grams of ganja, police said. [@1] Cases were
registered against both men under the NDPS Act. [@1] The separate account of the
Jharoukhar raid reported 428 grams of suspected ganja and 64 pieces of 'gogo'
rolling paper seized from a pan-gutkha kiosk near Kaiya village. [@3]

Police said the two accused had been selling ganja in the area for a long time.
[@2] Police were questioning Deepak Kumar about who supplied the ganja and how.
[@2] Superintendent of police Swarn Prabhat said action against ganja would
continue. [@2] He instructed station heads to run campaigns against drugs in
their areas. [@2] Police began the process of sending both accused to jail. [@2]
