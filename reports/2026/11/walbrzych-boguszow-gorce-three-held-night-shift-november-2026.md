Boguszów-Gorce and Wałbrzych officers detained three people during the overnight
shift for possessing marijuana or methamphetamine. [@1] Late on Tuesday evening
after 10 p.m., officers from the Boguszów-Gorce police station spotted two young
men while on patrol. [@1] The patrol stopped the men on Wałbrzyska Street to
check whether they held drugs. [@1]

The 17-year-old carried a wrap of plant material and his companion, three years
older, carried a container filled with the same intoxicant. [@1] A forensic
technician's tester confirmed both samples were marijuana. [@1] The two men
spent the night in police cells and on Wednesday morning were questioned and
charged under the Law on Counteracting Drug Addiction. [@1] Police said the two
younger men could now face up to three years in prison. [@1]

After 2 a.m., preventive officers from the first station checked a 39-year-old
on Wrocławska Street in Piaskowa Góra on suspicion of drug possession. [@1]
During the personal check officers found a zip bag with a small amount of
crystals on him. [@1] A forensic technician's test confirmed the crystals were
methamphetamine. [@1] The suspect also spent the night in a police cell and on
Wednesday morning was charged with drug possession. [@1] A court will decide his
punishment in criminal proceedings. [@1] He faces a three-year prison term like
the two younger men. [@1]
