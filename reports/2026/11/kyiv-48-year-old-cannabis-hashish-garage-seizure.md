Kyiv police detained a 48-year-old man on suspicion of unlawful acquisition and
possession of narcotic drugs and explosives. [@3,5] The Darnytskyi district
prosecutor's office in Kyiv notified him of suspicion of those offences. [@1] The most serious charge carries up to 10 years in prison with confiscation of property. [@6,7] Investigators notified him of suspicion under part 2 of Article 307 and part 1 of Article 263 of Ukraine's Criminal Code. [@6,7]
Investigators said he bought the cocaine for retail sale. [@2] Police said he sold the cocaine through postal shipments to customers in different regions of the country. [@6,7] He was caught
near a parcel locker carrying a cardboard box. [@2] The cocaine was hidden in
jars of face cream. [@3,5,7]

During a search of his garage, officers found and seized packages containing
cannabis and hashish along with weapons and explosives. [@4,7] The seized items
included a block of TNT, a grenade fuse, grenade bodies and large numbers of
rounds of various calibres. [@1] All of them were sent for examination. [@1] At
the arrest he smashed his own phone, which investigators said was an attempt to
conceal his contacts. [@1]

A court ordered him held in custody with the right to post bail. [@1] The Kyiv
City Prosecutor's Office said the pretrial investigation is being conducted by
Darnytskyi district police with operational support from the capital's
drug-crime unit. [@1]
