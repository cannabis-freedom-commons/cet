Civil police from the 1st DIG, the São Paulo state DEIC investigations unit,
seized skunk and hashish in the city on the afternoon of Thursday 19 November
2026. [@1] Officers went to a property in the Cecap neighbourhood after
receiving information that the resident was selling skunk. [@1] On arrival they
noticed a strong smell of the drug and the suspect tried to flee by throwing
objects into the neighbouring house. [@1]

The officers forced entry, detained the 38-year-old man and found five tubs of
skunk, 28 assorted packets, three portions of hashish and 59 packets of seeds,
plus equipment for packing the drug. [@1] The seized material also included a
vacuum-packing machine, a mobile phone and a roll of plastic film. [@1] He
confessed to selling the drug and was taken to DEIC headquarters, where an
arrest in flagrante was recorded. [@1]

The man remains at the disposal of the courts and will answer for drug
trafficking. [@1]
