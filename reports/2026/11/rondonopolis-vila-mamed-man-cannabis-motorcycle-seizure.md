Military Police stopped a man carrying a cannabis cigarette and a marijuana-like
portion in Vila Mamed, Rondonópolis, on the afternoon of Monday 23 November
2026. [@1] According to the Military Police, the suspect had attracted officers'
attention during patrols in the area. [@1] He was riding a motorcycle and
carrying a delivery bag on his back when officers approached him. [@1] During
the search, officers found a pouch fastened at his waist containing the
cigarette and the marijuana-like portion kept in zip-lock-type plastic. [@1]

When questioned, the suspect said he had bought the drug for his own
consumption. [@1] He was taken to the Military Police Operations Centre for the
appropriate measures. [@1] The motorcycle he was riding, a red Honda Fan without
a plate, was also seized. [@1] The motorcycle was removed and taken to a
tow-truck yard. [@1] Police recorded the case as illicit drug use. [@1]
