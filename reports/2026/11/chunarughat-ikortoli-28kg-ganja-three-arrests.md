An SRB-9 team raided a house in Ikortoli village, Gazipur Union, Chunarughat,
Habiganj, at 1:40 at night after Monday 23 November. [@1] Four people tried to
flee when officers reached the house, and three were caught while one escaped.
[@1] The three detained men were identified as Md Shahin Mia, 25, Md Roni Mia,
24, and Md Iqbal Hossain, 21, all from the same village. [@1]

The CPC-3 Shayestaganj camp team of SRB-9 acted on a tip received at 1:20 at
night that drugs were being bought and sold in a house in Ikortoli village. [@1]
Officers found the drugs inside the dwelling in two white plastic sacks, wrapped
in blue polythene and khaki scotch tape. [@1] SRB-9 seized 28.3 kg of ganja and
one bottle of foreign liquor in the house raid. [@1]

SRB-9 Sylhet media officer and Additional Superintendent of Police K M Shahidul
Islam Sohag gave the account in a press statement. [@1] In initial questioning
the detainees said they and the fugitive had collected the ganja and foreign
liquor from a border area and kept it to sell. [@1] SRB-9 said a case was filed
under the Narcotics Control Act against the three detainees and the fugitive,
and the detainees and seized drugs were handed to Chunarughat police station.
