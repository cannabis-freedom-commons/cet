The Sumlar interception was reported alongside a separate operation in Ajas as
part of Bandipora Police's intensified anti-drug drive. [@1] Police Post Sumlar
apprehended Mohd Zamana Turk alias Pappu, son of Khan Zaman Turk and a resident
of Kudara, at Tota Mod, Gujarpatti, Chuntimulla. [@1] He was intercepted while
travelling on foot from Chuntimulla towards Sumlar. [@1]

The interception took place at Tota Mod in the Gujarpatti-Chuntimulla area
covered by Police Post Sumlar. [@1] During his search, police recovered
approximately 2.330 kilograms of charas-like substance in crushed form concealed
in a polythene bag. [@1] Police registered FIR No. 189/2026 under Sections 8/20
of the NDPS Act at Police Station Bandipora and arrested the accused after
completing legal formalities. [@1]

Further investigation in both cases is underway. [@1] Bandipora Police
reiterated its commitment to intensifying action against drug trafficking and
appealed to the public to share information about drug-related activities and
cooperate with law enforcement agencies in the fight against substance abuse.
