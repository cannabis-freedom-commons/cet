On 17 November 2026 in Chambly, the driver refused a new order to stop issued by
law enforcement officers. [@1] He fled, accelerated, came close to knocking down
a pedestrian and struck four cars driven by other motorists. [@1] This time he
was intercepted and placed in police custody. [@1] He was subsequently placed in
pre-trial detention. [@2] The driver was 38 years old. [@1] The prosecutor did
not disclose the charges retained against him. [@2]

A test showed that he was positive for cannabis. [@1] His driving licence had
been invalid since 2017. [@1]

A similar incident had been reported on 21 September 2023 in Bornel, where a
driver refused to stop despite police orders, led officers on a chase, drove
onto the railway line, abandoned his vehicle and escaped on foot without being
caught. [@1] The Bornel driver was reported to be the same man. [@2] Under
French law, a refusal to obey that directly exposes another person to a risk of
death or serious injury, as narrowly missing a pedestrian does, carries a
maximum of five years in prison. [@2]
