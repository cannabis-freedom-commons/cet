Police in the Cep district of Genoa arrested a woman and a 30-year-old man after
entering a home allegedly used as a drug-dealing base. [@1] Investigators say
the woman had hosted the 30-year-old man in her home and he had allegedly used
the flat for dealing. [@2]

When officers entered the property, the 30-year-old allegedly tried to get rid
of a pouch by throwing it from the window, but officers stationed below the
opening recovered its full contents. [@1] Officers seized about 200 grams of
cocaine and 100 grams of hashish in the home, alongside almost 4,000 euros in
cash investigators believe came from dealing. [@1]

The operation was carried out by officers of the General Prevention and Public
Rescue Office (UPGSP) after days of investigations in the Cep area. [@1] The
second account describes the home as a reference point for drug dealing in the
Cep neighbourhood during a State Police operation. [@2] Both suspects were
arrested while inquiries continue to establish their exact roles and the scale
of the alleged dealing. [@2]
