Carabineros detained a 28-year-old Chilean man who lives in Vallenar after a
roadside drug test came back positive for THC, the substance found in marijuana.
[@1] The check was run by officers of the Tenencia Carreteras Atacama Sur with
SENDA Atacama under the Tolerancia Cero programme, which targets driving under
the influence of alcohol and other drugs. [@1] Officers stopped a car travelling
from south to north whose driver had no police record. [@1]

The driver's licence and the vehicle's documents were up to date, and an alcohol
test read 0.0 grams per litre of blood. [@1] The Narcotest device returned a
positive result for THC and the driver was detained for driving a motor vehicle
under the influence of psychotropic substances. [@2] Carabineros referred the
case to the Fiscalía Local de Vallenar, the local public prosecutor's office.
[@1]

Carabineros de Atacama repeated its call for responsible driving and respect for
traffic rules to prevent crashes on the region's roads. [@1]
