The Policía de Investigaciones carried out two raids in San Antonio de Obligado
in a microtrafficking investigation. [@1] Officers detained one man and seized
marijuana, cannabis plants, fractioning materials and cash. [@1]

Officers seized 447 grams of marijuana, six cannabis plants, a marijuana
cigarette, a grinder and a precision scale with cocaine traces. [@1] Officers
also seized a revolver, a .44 cartridge, a bullet tip, mobile phones, cuttings
and 312,650 pesos. [@1]

The investigation grew out of criminal-analysis work by the Fiscalía General
microtrafficking team. [@1] A detention order was issued for another man who
could not be located. [@1] On prosecutor Sebastián Galleano's orders, the
detainee was transferred to the Regional Unit XIX lock-up in Vera at the
disposal of justice. [@1]
