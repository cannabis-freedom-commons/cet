A garrison of the 77th Independent Company of the Military Police identified two
men in a situation deemed suspicious during patrols in the Renato Magalhães
neighbourhood of Vitória da Conquista and stopped them for a search. [@1] The
two men, together with all the material collected during the searches, were
taken to the Integrated Public Security District, DISEP, where the legal
procedures related to the incident were adopted. [@1]

The initial personal search found 32 portions of a substance with
characteristics similar to cocaine, as well as a quantity of marijuana. [@1] The
officers then continued the steps at a property where, after authorised entry,
they located a cocaine tablet, a .32-calibre revolver, a scale used for weighing
and a mobile telephone. [@1]

The Military Police action that produced the drugs, firearm and other materials
occurred on the night of Thursday 12 November in the Renato Magalhães
neighbourhood of Vitória da Conquista. [@1] The account says the occurrence
began during rounds by the 77a CIPM garrison, which identified the two
individuals as suspicious and carried out the approach. [@1]
