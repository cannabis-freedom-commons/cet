Four people were detained in security operations in the San Luis Potosí
metropolitan area and the municipalities of Cárdenas and Tampacán, on suspicion
of health offences. [@1] The Guardia Civil Estatal said the detentions resulted
from security and surveillance deployments in the state's four regions. [@2]

In Colonia San Felipe, east of the metropolitan area, officers arrested César
“N”, 25, and José “N”, 53. [@1] From the two men officers seized doses of a
substance with characteristics similar to crystal, bags of marijuana and
wrappers with cocaine. [@1] In Cárdenas, Guardia Civil Estatal officers detained
Julio “N”, 34, who allegedly had bags of marijuana and doses of a crystal-like
substance in his possession. [@1] In Tampacán, Pascual “N”, 55, was arrested
with several bags of marijuana found during patrol work. [@1]

The four detainees and the seized substances were placed at the disposal of the
competent authority, which will carry out the corresponding investigations. [@1]
The State Secretariat of Security and Citizen Protection said the Guardia Civil
Estatal keeps prevention and surveillance operations running in San Luis
Potosí's four regions to combat small-scale drug dealing. [@2]
