Cristian Nahuel Cabral Leguizamón, a 31-year-old Paraguayan citizen, accepted a
four-year prison sentence for drug transport in an abbreviated trial, a
procedure in which the accused admits the facts and agrees the penalty with
prosecutors, at the Federal Oral Court of Posadas. [@1] Federal prosecutor Pablo
Di Loreto and official defender Luis Errecaborde signed the agreement on Friday
20 November 2026. [@2] The court also imposed a fine alongside the prison term.
[@2]

On the afternoon of 28 August 2025, Gendarmería Nacional officers of Squadron 8
Alto Uruguay stopped a Correo Argentino truck on Route 14 at Paraje Centinela in
San José, Misiones. [@1] A narcotics detector dog marked a parcel of four boxes
dispatched from Puerto Piray and addressed to him. [@1] The parcel held 81
packages of marijuana weighing 60.040 kilograms in total. [@1]

He was arrested on 3 September 2025 when he came to collect the parcel at a
Correo Argentino branch in Wilde, Avellaneda partido, Buenos Aires province.
[@1] The arrest was made by Gendarmería's Villa Bonita investigations unit based
in Villa Soldati. [@2] The case began in the Federal Court of Posadas, which
declared itself incompetent and sent it to the Federal Court of Eldorado. [@1]
