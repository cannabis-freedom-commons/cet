Military Police in Ponte Nova arrested a 27-year-old man on suspicion of drug
trafficking in the Bairro Triângulo neighbourhood on the night of Friday 13
November 2026. [@1] The operation was handled by a team from the 21st
Independent Military Police Company. [@1] Officers began monitoring the house
after a report that drugs were being sold there. [@1]

Officers saw the suspect leave the house towards a vehicle. [@2] When he noticed
the police he tried to flee but was caught and stopped. [@1] A portion of
marijuana was found with him in the stop. [@1] A search of the house found more
drugs and material that the Military Police said was used to divide and sell
drugs. [@1]

Officers seized a pressed bar of marijuana, a tablet of the same substance, six
buchas (wrapped portions) of marijuana and several fragments of the drug. [@1]
They also found a fragment of cocaine, two digital precision scales and a mobile
phone. [@1]

The man was arrested in the act on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@1] He was
taken with the seized material to the Civil Police station, where the case was
presented for the appropriate procedures. [@1] The Military Police's
communications office released the account. [@1]
