Rajshahi Metropolitan Police Motihar, Kashiadanga, Damkura, Boalia and Paba
thana officers arrested four alleged drug traders and five alleged drug users in
separate drives. [@1] Motihar police arrested Md. Ikbar Hossain (29) and Md.
Jakarul Islam (36) in the Khojapur area at about 5 pm on 23 November 2026. [@1]
Kashiadanga police arrested Md. Sujan Ali (33) near Guripara lake at about 4 pm
the same day. [@1] Damkura police arrested Sabbir Hossain (26) in its thana area
at about 7:15 pm the same day. [@1] Boalia police arrested Md. Istiak Ahmed
Pallab (35) while he was consuming ganja in Uposhohor Sector 1 at about 3:30 at
night. [@1] Paba police with local help arrested four men while they were
consuming ganja at Nagar Parila CD crossing at about 9:30 pm. [@1] They were Md.
Abdur Razzak (42), Md. Ashikul Islam (25), Md. Shimul (32) and Md. Limon (25).
[@1] Police filed separate cases under the Narcotics Control Act against those
arrested and sent them to jail through the courts. [@1]

The drives together recovered 50 grams of ganja, 2 grams of heroin and 30 yaba
tablets. [@1] Officers recovered 20 grams of ganja said to have been kept for
sale from the two Motihar detainees. [@1] From Sujan Ali officers recovered 30
grams of ganja, 2 grams of heroin and 10 yaba tablets said to have been kept for
sale. [@1] From Sabbir Hossain officers recovered 20 yaba tablets said to have
been kept for sale. [@1]

Pallab was examined at Rajshahi Divisional Police Hospital and a case was filed
on the doctor's opinion. [@1] The four Paba detainees were examined at Paba
Upazila Health Complex and cases were filed on the doctors' opinions. [@1] Sujan
Ali was living at Terkhadia Notun Bilsimla in the Rajpara thana area. [@1]
