Officers of the Criminal Investigation Department of the Jabal al Akhdar
Security Directorate arrested two suspects in criminal cases in the city of Al
Bayda in northeastern Libya. [@1] In the first case, officers arrested a person
in the Old Zawiya neighbourhood of Al Bayda after finding him in possession of
narcotic pills. [@1] During questioning, that suspect admitted that he had been
involved in 18 theft incidents concerning public property and other stolen
goods. [@1]

Investigators said one of those theft cases dated back to 2017 and that they had
recovered part of the stolen property. [@1] In a separate operation, officers of
the same department arrested another person who was accused of trafficking in
hashish. [@1] The department stated that it had completed all the legal
procedures in these cases and referred both suspects to the Public Prosecution
for further action. [@1]
