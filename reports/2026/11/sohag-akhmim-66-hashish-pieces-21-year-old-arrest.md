Sohag security forces detained a 21-year-old man in Akhmim district on suspicion
of dealing in narcotics. [@1,2] The suspect was identified only as Sh. N. Dh., a
resident of the Ezba and Arab area of Akhmim city with another residence in
Al-Mazalwa village. [@1,2] Officers of the Akhmim police investigation unit acted
after receiving information that he was promoting drugs to users in the
district. [@1]

After verifying the information and completing legal authorisation,
investigators tracked his movements and fixed his whereabouts before detaining
him. [@1] Capt. Mostafa Nasser, an Akhmim investigation officer, reached the suspect's location using a tuk-tuk and a taxi to track him down and arrest him. [@2] Officers found 119 wraps of shabu and 66 pieces of hashish in his
possession, 185 items in total. [@1,2] Officers also seized a shotgun and four
cartridges said to have been held for use in his criminal activity. [@1,2]

The operation was supervised by the Sohag security director and criminal
investigation chiefs and led in the field by Akhmim investigation officers. [@1]
The account named Maj. Mohamed Ali Fahmy, Maj. Mahmoud Dahi and Capt. Mostafa
Nasser among the Akhmim officers. [@1] Authorities completed the legal
procedures, impounded the seized items and referred the suspect to the competent
investigation authorities. [@1,2]
