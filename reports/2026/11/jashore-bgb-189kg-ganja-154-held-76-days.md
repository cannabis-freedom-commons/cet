Border Guard Bangladesh said its Jessore region operational teams arrested 154
suspects during border drives from September to 15 November 2026. [@1] The
commander of the 49 BGB battalion in Jessore, Lieutenant Colonel Golam Mohammad
Saiful Alam Khan, announced the figures in a press statement. [@1]

Officers seized 189.59 kg of ganja, the Bengali term for cannabis, during the
76-day drive. [@1] The haul also included 2.428 kg of heroin, 3,192 bottles of
foreign liquor, 77,311 yaba tablets, 7.097 kg of crystal meth, 21,062 bottles of
narcotic syrup and 9.349 kg of gold, which are not counted in the cannabis
figures. [@1] The force valued the seized drugs and smuggled goods together at
85 crore 41 lakh 71 thousand 473 taka. [@1]

The Jessore region's area of responsibility stretches about 600 kilometres from
Kushtia to the Sundarbans in Satkhira. [@1] In the Sharsha and Chougacha border
areas of Jessore, the force made eight arrests and seized goods worth 6 crore 20
lakh 37 thousand 187 taka over the same period. [@1] That haul included 27.3 kg
of ganja along with syrup, liquor and yaba tablets. [@1]
