Police in Thoothukudi arrested three young men after finding 7 kg of ganja, six
sickles and a knife during a vehicle check on Beach Road. [@1,2] The check and the arrests took place on Wednesday 18 November 2026. [@2] Officers of the
Thoothukudi South police station, led by inspector in charge Thirumurugan and
sub-inspector Subburaj, were checking vehicles on Beach Road. [@1,2] The three
were riding three motorbikes when police stopped and searched them on suspicion.
[@1,2] The men had 7 kg of ganja in a jute sack, and officers seized it with six
sickles, a knife, three mobile phones and the motorbike they had ridden. [@1] Police also seized the three two-wheelers the men had ridden. [@2]

The three were taken to the station and questioned, and were identified as
Selvam alias Selvasathish, 25, Sivagnanamurthy, 25, and Vinoth, 23. [@1,2] Police
said all three had several cases registered at the station and were listed in
its history sheet as offenders. [@1,2]

Police registered a case over the seizure and were continuing the investigation.
