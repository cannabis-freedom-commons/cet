Officers were on a drug saturation detail on 18 November 2026 when they watched
a Chevrolet Silverado supposedly driven by 46-year-old Brandon French of
Yeagertown leave a home on Logan Street in Lewistown that police had been
monitoring for possible drug activity. [@1] A Mifflin County officer later
stopped the truck at a Wawa in Derry Township with 44-year-old Amanda French of
Yeagertown and 45-year-old Robert Wheeler of Lewistown inside. [@1] Wheeler was
already under investigation by the police task force over suspected narcotic
sales. [@1] Two people were arrested during the stop, according to the Mifflin
County Regional Police Department. [@1]

While searching the occupants, officers found Brandon French with a baggie of
possible crack cocaine, a crack pipe and more than $1,000 in cash, and found two
more baggies of crack cocaine in the floorboard after he exited. [@1] Police
said Amanda French was also found with a crack pipe. [@1] A search of the truck
found about 19 grams of suspected crack cocaine packaged for sale, about 3 grams
of possible fentanyl and three bags of marijuana, plus paraphernalia linked to
sales and a loaded 9mm handgun. [@1] Police confirmed none of the three in the
vehicle had a concealed carry license. [@1] After being read his Miranda rights,
Wheeler took ownership of the items in the truck except the gun. [@1]

Wheeler was taken to the Mifflin County Correctional Facility and charged with
possession with intent to deliver a controlled substance, possession of a
controlled substance, possession of a small amount of marijuana and possession
of drug paraphernalia. [@1] Amanda French was charged with possession of drug
paraphernalia, while charges against Brandon French Sr. were still pending. [@1]
