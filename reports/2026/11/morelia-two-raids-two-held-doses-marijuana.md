Two men were detained and drugs seized during searches of homes in the Vasco de
Quiroga and Primo Tapia Oriente neighbourhoods of Morelia on 23 November 2026.
[@3] The Michoacán state prosecutor's office said the detentions followed two
search warrants executed at different properties in the state capital. [@1] The
detainees were Carlos Alberto “N” and Cristian “N”, both taken at the second
property in Primo Tapia Oriente. [@3]

At the Vasco de Quiroga property officers seized methamphetamine equivalent to
697.5 doses and marijuana corresponding to 37.36 doses. [@1] At the Primo Tapia
Oriente property they seized methamphetamine equivalent to 880 doses and
marijuana corresponding to 8.145 doses. [@1] The methamphetamine from both
properties together was described as more than 1,500 doses, apart from the
marijuana. [@3]

The operation was carried out by the state prosecutor's specialised unit for
property crimes and street-level dealing. [@2] Investigative Police officers
from the street-dealing investigation and prosecution unit conducted both
searches, while Morelia police provided perimeter security. [@1] The drugs, the
detainees and the properties were placed at the disposal of the competent
authority, which will continue the investigation. [@1]
