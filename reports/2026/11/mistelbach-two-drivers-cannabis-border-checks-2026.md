Police in Lower Austria's Mistelbach district stopped two drivers during
border-area checks on 23 November 2026. [@1] The checks were carried out by
officers of the Laa an der Thaya police inspection's aliens and border police
unit. [@2] At about 11:00 they stopped a 46-year-old Czech citizen in Reintal
for a driver and vehicle check. [@2]

The officers smelled cannabis coming from his car and searched it, seizing small
amounts of cannabis herb and cannabis utensils. [@2] A drug test and a medical
examination found him impaired by intoxicants. [@2] Police provisionally took
away his driving licence and barred him from continuing his journey. [@2] He is
being reported to the Korneuburg public prosecutor's office and the Mistelbach
district authority. [@2]

At about 20:25 the same day, officers stopped a second car in Pottenhofen. [@2]
Its 42-year-old Czech driver had no valid driving licence and also showed signs
of drug impairment. [@3] He admitted having consumed drugs but refused a medical
examination. [@2] Officers found and seized a small amount of a brown substance
in his car. [@2] He was also barred from driving on and is being reported to the
competent district authority and the Korneuburg prosecutor. [@2]
