The Uppal Zone Special Operations Team (SOT) and Nacharam police mounted an
operation at IDA Nacharam in Hyderabad on 16 November 2026. [@1] They arrested
three people and seized 3.18 kg of hashish oil worth ₹40 lakh. [@1] The three,
aged 25, 23 and 21, include two degree students and a fabrication supervisor
from Jeedimetla. [@1] Officers intercepted them on a TVS Jupiter scooter and
found three packets weighing 1.070 kg, 1.080 kg and 1.030 kg. [@1] Police also
seized three mobile phones and the scooter used to carry the oil. [@1] A case
was registered at Nacharam police station under the NDPS Act. [@2]

Police suspect the trio planned to sell the oil to college students near
Maisammaguda. [@1] According to police, the men bought hashish oil from a
supplier named Venkat in Annavaram, Andhra Pradesh, at ₹1 lakh a litre and had
sold it in Hyderabad for about ₹2 lakh a litre on two earlier occasions. [@1]
Each of the three contributed ₹1 lakh, pooling ₹3 lakh for a consignment of
about three litres. [@1] Two of them travelled by train to Annavaram on 11
November and collected the oil from Venkat near the railway station the next
day. [@1] They returned to Secunderabad on 13 November and kept the consignment
at a house in Kalavathi Nagar, Jeedimetla, until they set out to deliver it on
16 November. [@1] The accused told investigators they had brought the hashish
oil from the Andhra Pradesh-Odisha border, the Telugu daily Andhrajyothy
reported. [@3] Police are trying to trace Venkat, who is absconding, along with
the source of the contraband and the distribution network. [@1]

Malkajgiri police commissioner B. Sumathi supervised the operation, with Uppal
Zone DCP K. Suresh Kumar and SOT DCP K. Manohar. [@1]
