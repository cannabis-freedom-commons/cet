A 29-year-old man was convicted at the Dieppe criminal court over cannabis resin
found in his car. [@1] He appeared on 20 November 2026 in immediate appearance
for 13 offences committed between 30 September and 1 October 2026 while in
recidivism, including driving with a cancelled licence and drug-related
offences. [@1] A prior case from 4 June was joined to the hearing. [@1] The
court acquitted him of drug trafficking. [@1] He had been suspected of
trafficking after bars of cannabis resin were found in his vehicle. [@1]

Gendarmes arrested him on 30 September 2026 at Bertrimont, where he was driving
without a licence. [@2] In his car they found 215 g of cannabis resin, 0.41 g of
cocaine and 2,490 euros in cash. [@2] He refused to take a drug test that day.
[@2]

About four months earlier, at Crosville-sur-Scie, he had fled a police check at
high speed in an uninsured Twingo, passing close to pedestrians. [@2] He was
placed under judicial supervision with a ban on driving while awaiting judgment.
[@2] The prosecutor joined the two cases at the 2 October 2026 hearing, when he
asked for time to prepare his defence. [@2] The court kept him in pre-trial
detention until the 20 November hearing. [@2]
