Two men were arrested in flagrante on suspicion of drug trafficking on the night
of Thursday 19 November 2026 at km 398 of the Raposo Tavares highway (SP-270) in
Salto Grande, São Paulo state. [@1] The detainees were aged 25 and 43 and both
were from Ibirarema. [@1] The Military Police operations centre had reported
that two people left Ibirarema by motorcycle for Salto Grande to buy drugs that
would later be sold. [@1] Officers positioned themselves under a viaduct near
the entrance to Salto Grande to watch for the suspects. [@1] The team identified
a blue Honda CG 150 Titan EX motorcycle carrying two men. [@1] The motorcycle
was followed and stopped at km 398. [@1] During the stop the passenger threw
away two portions of a substance resembling marijuana, which the officers
recovered. [@1] A personal search of the driver found R$92 in cash and an
iPhone. [@1]

The men denied keeping drugs at home but authorised searches, which police
recorded on video. [@1] Officers searched homes in the Maria Martins Ziglio and
Vila Feliz neighbourhoods of Ibirarema. [@1] At the younger man's home they
found a portion of a substance resembling marijuana. [@1] At the older man's
home they found a portion initially identified as crack. [@1] Police also seized
the cash, the phone and the motorcycle used by the suspects. [@1] The motorcycle
was taken to the Grupo Carvalho yard. [@1]

Forensic tests confirmed the presence of marijuana and cocaine. [@1] One sample
was positive for marijuana with a net weight of 0.93 g. [@1] A second sample was
positive for marijuana with a net weight of 39.40 g. [@1] A third sample was
positive for cocaine with a net weight of 1.15 g. [@1] The police report said
the laboratory weights differed from the preliminary amounts recorded at the
seizure. [@1] The police authority ordered the two held in flagrante for drug
trafficking under article 33 of Law 11.343/2006. [@1] They were taken to the
Central de Polícia Judiciária in Ourinhos and stayed in custody awaiting a
custody hearing. [@1] The Civil Police will handle the case. [@1]
