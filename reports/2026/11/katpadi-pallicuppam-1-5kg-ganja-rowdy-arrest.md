Katpadi police arrested Manikandan, a 33-year-old described locally as a rowdy
(a known troublemaker), on suspicion of selling ganja at the foot of a hill near
Katpadi. [@1] The arrest followed a secret tip-off that ganja was being sold
illegally in the Pallicuppam hill-foot areas next to Katpadi in Vellore
district. [@1]

Police kept the area under close surveillance and checks, then caught a man
red-handed who had hidden 1.5 kilograms of ganja there for sale. [@1]
Questioning established the detainee as the Pallicuppam rowdy Manikandan;
officers seized the 1.5 kilograms of ganja, registered a case against him and
made the arrest. [@1]
