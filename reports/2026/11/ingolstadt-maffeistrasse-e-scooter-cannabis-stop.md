Police in Ingolstadt stopped a man riding an e-scooter under the influence of
drugs on Wednesday evening. [@1] The rider was checked at about 22:30 in the
Maffeistraße. [@1] According to the police report he showed signs of previous
drug use, and a rapid test reacted positively to cannabis. [@1]

The man, aged 28, was not allowed to continue riding. [@1] Police ordered a
blood sample and opened administrative offence proceedings under the Road
Traffic Act. [@1]
