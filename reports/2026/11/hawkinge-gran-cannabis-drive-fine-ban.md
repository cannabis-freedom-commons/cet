Michaela Cruickshank got in her car and drove her “missing” grandson to
Folkestone Police Station after he turned up at her home in Hawkinge. [@1]
Cruickshank, of Pannell Drive, Hawkinge, drove her grandson to the station in
Bouverie Road West, but on the way back was spotted driving erratically by
officers on patrol who pulled her over. [@1] She was arrested and later charged
with driving while unfit through drugs and possession of cannabis. [@1]
Cruickshank appeared at Folkestone Magistrates’ Court on November 5, where she
pleaded guilty to both charges. [@1]

She had not only taken prescription drugs for her depression, but she had also
smoked two cannabis joints before she got behind the wheel. [@1] She then failed
a drug swipe after testing positive for cannabis, a small amount of which was
found in her purse. [@1] District Judge William Nelson heard that Cruickshank
was pulled over shortly before 1.20am on February 8. [@1] Prosecutors said she
was seen driving in Canterbury Road, Hawkinge, in an erratic manner. [@1] Judge
Nelson also heard Cruickshank admitted at the time to having smoked two cannabis
joints the day before, one at 2pm and the other at 7pm, as she was depressed at
the death of her mother. [@1] They found cannabis in her purse and there was
impairment, but her blood was not over the limit for THC. [@1]

Her 10-year-old grandson was staying with her and her 16-year-old grandson
arrived and smashed the door; he had been reported missing in Rochester. [@1]
She dialled 999 and, worried, got him away from the house and took him to the
police station. [@1] The judge fined her £133 and banned her from driving for 12
months for the driving offence. [@1] Cruickshank received no separate penalty
for the drug possession offence, but was ordered to pay a £53 victim surcharge.
[@1] The judge also ordered the cannabis to be destroyed. [@1] Judge Nelson said
he had some sympathy with Cruickshank regarding the situation she had found
herself in that night and that it was difficult to know how anyone would have
reacted with the same set of circumstances. [@1]
