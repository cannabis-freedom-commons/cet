Soldiers of the Fuerza de Tarea Conjunta Guayas arrested a man during a military
control and surveillance patrol in Vergeles, in northern Guayaquil. [@1] A
search found packages of narcotics among his clothes, and a preliminary count
confirmed 17 doses of suspected marijuana and two doses of a substance known as
PLOP. [@1] The armed forces said the doses were divided into small wrappers
ready for retail sale in the neighbourhood. [@1] The military detained him in
flagrante delicto and withheld his identity, saying it did not want to prejudice
the case or his presumption of innocence. [@1]

The man and the 19 wrappers were taken under chain of custody to the judicial
facilities in Guayaquil and left at the disposal of the Fiscalía General del
Estado for a flagrancy hearing and the filing of charges. [@1] The evidence was
to undergo preliminary chemical identification tests (PIPH) and official
weighing at the Criminalística laboratories, which the report said were needed
to support a prosecution for illicit trafficking of controlled substances. [@1]

The patrol was part of the Bloque de Seguridad's preventive patrols and its
operations against weapons, ammunition and explosives (Camex) in districts of
Zone 8, meant to contain retail drug sales and related crime. [@1] Vergeles, in
the Pascuales district, is one of the priority areas for the security forces in
northern Guayaquil because of local drug selling and illegal carrying of
weapons. [@1]
