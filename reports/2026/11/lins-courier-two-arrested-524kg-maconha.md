Military Police officers in Lins stopped a ride-hailing car and found two bricks
of cannabis inside. [@2] The driver denied knowing about the drug and said he
was only carrying out a ride requested by a passenger. [@1] Investigators
established that the app driver had no involvement in the trafficking and did
not know what the parcel contained. [@2] Two men were arrested in flagrante on
drug-trafficking charges. [@1]

Officers then searched a property in the Chácara Flora neighbourhood, where they
found more drugs, precision scales and materials used to prepare and pack the
narcotics. [@2] A suspect escaped during the searches. [@2] In the Junqueira
neighbourhood, officers found another man with a parcel containing two bricks of
cannabis. [@2] He told police he would receive R$100 to receive the drug and
pass it on to another person. [@1]

In total, 5.24 kg of cannabis was seized, in bricks and dried flowers, along
with mobile phones and precision scales. [@2] The two detainees were charged in
flagrante with drug trafficking and taken to a penitentiary in the region. [@1]
The offence carries a maximum of 15 years in prison in closed regime. [@1] The
Civil Police are investigating the origin of the parcels and the possible
involvement of other people in the scheme. [@2]
