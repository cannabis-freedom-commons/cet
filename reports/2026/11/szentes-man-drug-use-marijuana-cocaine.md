Police in Szentes, Csongrád-Csanád County, were called on 15 November 2026 after
a relative reported a man lying on the ground and convulsing, fearing he had
consumed drugs. [@1] At the scene officers established that the man's girlfriend
was also present. [@1] She told police she and her partner had consumed drugs
together and admitted she had herself used an illegal substance during the early
hours. [@1]

Because of his condition the man could not be tested immediately and stayed
under medical observation until it improved. [@1] The next day officers took a
urine sample and ran a rapid test. [@1] The test came back positive for
marijuana and cocaine and the man admitted drug use to the officers. [@1] A
rapid test on the girlfriend was also positive for marijuana use. [@2]

Officers brought the man to the Szentes police station and started criminal
proceedings against him on suspicion of committing the misdemeanour of drug use.
[@4] The county police headquarters said the man was questioned as a suspect.
[@3] It described the suspicion of drug-use misdemeanour as well-founded. [@3]
