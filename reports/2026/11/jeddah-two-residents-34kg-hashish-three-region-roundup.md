Saudi Arabia's General Directorate of Narcotics Control announced the arrest of
drug dealers in Jeddah, Jazan and Riyadh in one operation. [@1] In Jeddah, the
directorate arrested two residents, of Guinean and Ethiopian nationality,
accused of promoting 34 kilograms of hashish. [@1] The same two were accused of
promoting 48,065 tablets subject to medical controls, which are not a cannabis
product and are excluded from the hashish figures. [@1] A second Saudi outlet
reports the Jeddah arrests with the same two nationalities, the same 34
kilograms of hashish and the same 48,065 tablets. [@2]

The announcement also covered Jazan, where officers seized 13,336 of the same
medical-control tablets. [@1] In Riyadh, the directorate reported 1.9 kilograms
of methamphetamine, known locally as shabu, a different drug left out of the
cannabis figures. [@1] In all, the three cases held 61,401 tablets subject to
medical controls, alongside the hashish and the shabu, and none of the tablets
is counted as cannabis. [@2] The Jeddah pair were stopped, the regulatory
procedures were completed against them, and they were referred to the Public
Prosecution. [@1]

The directorate asked the public to report smugglers and drug dealers on its 995
hotline or by email, and through 911 in Mecca, Medina, Riyadh and the Eastern
Province and 999 elsewhere. [@1]
