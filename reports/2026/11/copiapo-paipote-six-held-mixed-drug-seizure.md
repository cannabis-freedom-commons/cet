Detectives of the PDI Copiapó MT-0 group of the Bicrim carried out an operation
in the locality of Paipote in Copiapó. [@1] The operation detained six people,
four men and two women, all Chilean adults, for small-quantity drug trafficking.
[@1] The detainees were placed at the disposal of the Copiapó Guarantee Court,
where the prosecution had them formally charged, with two held in preventive
prison and the rest under precautionary measures while the investigation
continues. [@1]

Officers intervened in five homes during the operation. [@1] Officers seized
1,309 doses of cocaína base, cannabis, cocaine hydrochloride and ketamine, a
combined total whose cannabis share was not separated. [@1] Officers also seized
more than one million pesos in cash. [@1] Investigators found the accused used
their homes to sell drugs in Paipote, simulating a handshake to carry out each
transaction. [@1]

The MT-0 officers acted in coordination with the SAC unit of the Atacama
prosecution after establishing illegal drug sales at a home in Paipote. [@1]
Police said the operation dismantled an important drug sales point in Paipote
under the MT-0 model focused on recovering public spaces for the community. [@1]
