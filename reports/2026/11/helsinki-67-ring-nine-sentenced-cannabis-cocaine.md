The Helsinki District Court has passed sentence in the so-called 67 case. [@1]
Hashim Quasem Ibrahim, aged 21, received 12 years for nine aggravated drug
offences and a firearms offence. [@1] Dieudonne Croiyance, aged 23, received 8
years and 10 months for two aggravated drug offences and a firearms offence.
[@1] Hasen Mohamed Habib Al-Bakaa, aged 27, received 8 years and 9 months for
two aggravated drug offences. [@1] Omar Mohamed Aly Tawfik Elgbaly, aged 25,
received 8 years and 6 months for two aggravated drug offences. [@1] Hussein
Mohamed Habib Al-Bakaa, aged 21, received 7 years and 11 months for aggravated
drug offences, firearms offences, drunk driving and a drug-use offence. [@1]
Niko Kalevi Kristian Autio, aged 25, received 6 years and 4 months for two
aggravated drug offences and three firearms offences. [@1] Abdisamad Ahmed
Mohamed, aged 26, received 5 years and 1 month for an aggravated drug offence
and escaping from prison. [@1] Lassi Johannes Sabelo Kokkonen, aged 21, received
5 years for an aggravated drug offence. [@1] Aba Alhassan Ali Abdulameer, aged
20, received 3 years and 8 months for one aggravated and two basic drug
offences. [@1]

The court called the case an exceptionally broad and professional drug-crime
complex with related firearms offences in Helsinki, Vantaa and Kerava in
2024-2025. [@1] The group was found to have distributed tens of kilos of cocaine
in batches of up to 20 kilos. [@1] The group also distributed more than ten
kilos of amphetamine and several kilos of cannabis. [@1] The operation was
organised wholesale and street-level distribution, with drugs stored and packed
in short-term rented flats and cocaine bricks stamped with logos including 67.
[@1]

The court held the offending was traditional aggravated drug crime, not
street-gang activity as defined by law. [@1] The court said it stayed unclear
what grouping used the number codes, and that stamping the 67 logo on cocaine
alone did not prove an offence committed for a group. [@1] The court described
Al-Bakaa as a central leading figure who obtained the drugs and instructed
others in distributing them. [@1] The court said no evidence about Ridu's
identity or activities was presented, leaving his participation unclear. [@1]
Ridu fled the country after a separate shooting and was not charged as part of
this drug case. [@1]
