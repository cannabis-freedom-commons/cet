Officers of the Saale-Holzland police inspection stopped a 19-year-old car
driver in Stadtroda during a Friday evening traffic control. [@1] The check
found that the young driver had no valid driving licence and was under the
influence of cannabis. [@1] The car had number plates fitted that had previously
been stolen in Saxony. [@1] The officers ordered a blood sample, prohibited any
further driving and secured the vehicle key. [@1]

The 19-year-old later collected the key from the police together with an
acquaintance. [@1] He then drove himself again and crashed into a lamp post near
Meissen in Saxony. [@1] The second drive ended for good when the car sustained
an economic total loss in the collision. [@1] At the time of the crash the
driver was still under the influence of cannabis. [@1] Saxon colleagues are now
investigating the case for endangering road traffic among other offences. [@1]

The Jena state police inspection issued the underlying police message on the
morning of 22 November 2026. [@1]
