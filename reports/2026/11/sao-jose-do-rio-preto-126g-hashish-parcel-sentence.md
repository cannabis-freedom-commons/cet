The 3rd Criminal Court of São José do Rio Preto convicted marketing manager
Adirson Xavier Júnior of privileged interstate drug trafficking and sentenced
him to 1 year, 11 months and 10 days of confinement in an initial open regime
plus 194 day-fines. [@1] Judge Carolina Marchiori Bueno Cocenzo signed the
decision on Thursday the 12th and replaced the custodial term with community
service and payment of one minimum wage to a social entity. [@1] He was allowed
to appeal the sentence in liberty. [@1]

The investigation began in February 2025 after managers of a Correios branch
alerted the Civil Police to a parcel posted in Curitiba whose addressee was not
found at an apartment on Avenida Fortunato Ernesto Vetorazzo in Jardim Santa
Lúcia. [@1] Police opened the parcel and found 126 grams of hashish
vacuum-sealed inside an electric kettle. [@1] Inquiries found the defendant
lived in the neighbouring apartment and used third-party names, including a
former employer's, to disguise drug purchases made over social media. [@1]
During a search of his home in January 2026 officers seized three pots holding
cannabis plants and two portions of the drug. [@1]

He claimed the hashish was for personal use and health treatment, but the judge
rejected that defence given the large quantity, the high value of the substance
and the lack of legal authorisation for import or cultivation. [@1] Because he
was a first-time offender who did not belong to a criminal organisation, he
received the penalty-reduction benefit. [@1]
