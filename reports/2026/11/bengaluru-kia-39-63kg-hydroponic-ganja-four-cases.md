Bengaluru Customs seized 39.63 kg of hydroponic ganja valued at Rs 13.9 crore in
four separate cases at Kempegowda International Airport between 20 and 23
November 2026. [@1] The accused in all four cases were arrested under the
Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985. [@1]

The latest seizure was made on Monday, when officials intercepted a passenger
arriving from Kuala Lumpur and recovered 11.7 kg of hydroponic ganja worth Rs
4.11 crore. [@1] On November 21, officials intercepted two passengers arriving
from Bangkok in separate cases. [@1] While 15 kg of the contraband, valued at Rs
5.25 crore, was seized from one passenger, another was found carrying 8.03 kg
worth Rs 2.81 crore, concealed in checked-in baggage. [@1]

On November 20, Customs officials seized 4.9 kg of hydroponic ganja, valued at
Rs 1.73 crore, from a passenger arriving from Bangkok. [@1] Kempegowda
International Airport, abbreviated KIA, is the Bengaluru airport where officials
said all four interceptions took place. [@1]
