The Derna branch of Libya's General Administration for Combating Drugs and
Psychotropic Substances carried out a security operation south of Gate 200 in
the city of Derna. [@1,4,13,18,22,25,26] The seized drugs were pictured and dated to Tuesday 17
November 2026 in Derna, in material credited to the anti-drugs administration.
[@1,11] Officers seized 18 kilograms of hashish in the operation. [@1,4,13,18,21,22,26] Photos published by the administration with its statement showed quantities of hashish alongside boxes and clear bags holding white pills. [@3,5,7,8,9,10,11,12,15,16,17,19,20,23,24]

The same haul also included 4,162,682 narcotic pills described as Captagon,
ecstasy, tramadol and Tamol. [@1,4,13,18,21,22,25,26] Officers also seized 11 kilograms of cocaine
alongside the hashish and pills, which is not counted in the cannabis figures.
[@1,4,13,22,26] A Reuters account of the seizure put the haul at more than four million narcotic pills and 29 kilograms of cocaine and hashish together. [@3,5,9,10,11,12,14,15,16,20,23,24] The head of the anti-drugs administration, Maj. Gen. Ahmed Al-Shamikh, gave
the same breakdown of 4,162,682 pills plus 18 kilograms of hashish and 11
kilograms of cocaine in the second account. [@2] The same account quoted the administration's director, Maj. Gen. Ahmed Al-Shamikh, saying in a video statement released late on 17 November that the authorities would not be lenient with anyone who sought to tamper with the security of young Libyans or the country's future. [@3,5,6,7,8,10,12,14,15,19,20,23,24,26] The first account said the
operation implemented instructions from the administration's director to
intensify anti-drug efforts and pursue dealers and promoters. [@1,13] The second
account instead described the operation as implementing instructions from the
deputy commander-in-chief of the armed forces and the interior minister to
redouble efforts against drugs and pursue their traders and promoters. [@2] The statement named the officials whose instructions it cited: Lt Gen Saddam Haftar, the deputy commander-in-chief, interior minister Maj Gen Essam Abu Zariba and administration director Maj Gen Ahmed Al-Shamikh. [@22]

The seized materials were sealed as evidence and the necessary legal measures
were taken. [@1,4,13,22] Inquiries were continuing under Public Prosecution supervision
to identify the members of the gang involved. [@1,4,13,21,22] The authorities were questioning the detained network members to establish its foreign links and who was behind its financing. [@6] The administration's statement also said members of the criminal network had been detained in the city. [@3,5,6,7,8,9,10,11,12,14,15,16,17,19,20,23,24,25,26] The statement said the detainees were involved in possessing and trading the seized substances. [@26]
