Puducherry's anti-narcotics division said it had caught a gang that carried
cannabis in a luxury car near Villianur to supply retail vendors. [@1] After a
tip-off that a gang was passing cannabis to retail sellers, the anti-narcotics
division kept several spots in the Villianur area under covert watch. [@1]
Officers surrounded and searched an Innova car that had been standing for a long
time on the road near the Villianur bypass and Ariyapalayam. [@1] Five men in
the car were arrested, and the search found cannabis inside it. [@1]

Police seized one kilogram of cannabis, 200 kilograms of tobacco products, five
mobile phones, a weighing machine and the Innova car, items they put at 15 lakh
rupees in value, and handed them to Villianur police. [@1] The five were
identified as Maitreyan, 21, Vasanthakumar, 19, and Sabari, 18, from the
Ariyapalayam area, Suman, 31, from Koonimudakkuppam in Tamil Nadu, and Abdul
Rasheed, 48, from Cuddalore district. [@1]

Investigators said the group sold cannabis wholesale to vendors around Villianur
and also supplied college students, young people and shops. [@1] A case was
registered against all five, who were arrested, produced in court and sent to
jail. [@1]
