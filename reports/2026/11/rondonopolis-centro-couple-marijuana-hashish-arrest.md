Police in Rondonópolis arrested a couple in a grey Volkswagen T-Cross in the
city centre on the night of Tuesday 17 November 2026 on suspicion of drug
trafficking. [@1] Officers of the 5th Battalion were on visible patrol in the
centre when they saw the car, and stopped it because its occupants appeared
nervous at the sight of the patrol vehicle. [@1] A search of the two people and
the car found 13 portions of a substance similar to cocaine inside the vehicle
and two portions of material similar to marijuana on the back seat. [@1] The two
were arrested and taken to the police station. [@1]

In the station yard, a more thorough search of the car found more drugs hidden
in a compartment below the steering wheel: another 14 wrappers of material
similar to marijuana and four wrappers of material similar to hashish. [@1] In
total the military police seized 13 portions of cocaine, 16 portions of
marijuana and four wrappers of hashish. [@1] Police said one of the two suspects
has a criminal record for threat, in 2026. [@1]
