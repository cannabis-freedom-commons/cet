A joint operation by the POP teams of the 14th Military Police Battalion, BPFRON
and FICCO/PR arrested a man in Foz do Iguaçu on the morning of Monday 16
November 2026, seizing a rifle, drugs and irregular medicines. [@3] The
operation continued inquiries begun on 13 November into a criminal group
suspected of moving drugs, medicines and other illicit products from Paraguay to
several Brazilian states. [@3] Officers identified the suspect at a commercial
business in the Universitário neighbourhood as the man responsible for
dispatching suspicious parcels. [@3] In a simultaneous check at a carrier,
officers intercepted a parcel previously sent by the same sender. [@1]

Inside the parcel officers found a Colt M4 Carbine rifle in calibre 5.56 with
two magazines. [@1] At the company officers also found slimming medicines,
anabolics, electronic cigarettes containing THC and dry hashish hidden in shoe
boxes. [@1] The suspect told officers more illicit materials were at his home,
where teams found drugs stored inside an outdoor barbecue grill along with more
anabolics and slimming medicines. [@1] After weighing, officers counted 2.854 kg
of dry hashish and 4.739 kg of premium marijuana known as Gorilla Roxo. [@1] The
Federal Police account confirms one man arrested with a rifle, more than 7.5 kg
of drugs and 80 units of medicines and anabolics in Foz do Iguaçu. [@2] In total
officers seized the rifle, two magazines, the drugs, THC electronic cigarettes,
anabolics, medicines, an HD, a notebook and two mobile phones. [@1]

Officers also seized two firearm magazines, two mobile phones, a notebook, a
storage device and a vehicle. [@2] The man was arrested and taken with all the
seized material to the Federal Police for the applicable procedures. [@3]
Security forces put the estimated loss to crime at R$337,226. [@1]
