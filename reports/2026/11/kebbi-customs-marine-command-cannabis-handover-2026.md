The Nigeria Customs Service's North Western Marine Command handed seized
suspected cannabis sativa to the NDLEA in Kebbi State on Friday 20 November
2026. [@1,4] The cannabis was in 14 van-sized and four laptop-sized bundles. [@1,4] The pharmaceutical products handed to NAFDAC comprised 128 cards of Timaking tablets (250mg), two cartons of Dynewell tablets, three bottles of Dynewell syrup and 60 rolls of Paracetamol tablets. [@4] The consignment handed to the NDLEA also included 95 packs of Tapentadol Royal tablets (100mg) and 10 packs of Tropisto tablets (250mg). [@4]
Acting Area Commander Nnamdi Festus Agwu said officers intercepted the items
during enforcement operations along the Dole-Kaina waterways. [@2,4]

The handover was announced at a press conference at the Customs Kebbi Area
Command office in Birnin Kebbi. [@3] Agwu said the cannabis and the
pharmaceutical products had a combined duty-paid value of N18.29 million. [@1]
The Sun reported the certificates of value as putting the combined figure at
N18,243,960. [@3] The whole handover was valued at N22.52 million. [@1,4] The
command said it would destroy 195 bottles of seized CSP Codeine Syrup worth
N4.27 million, which had deteriorated and was unfit for consumption. [@1,4] The destruction would be conducted in the presence of NAFDAC and other relevant agencies, following approval by the Comptroller-General of Customs. [@4] A long
gun and a short pistol with 170 rounds of ammunition seized by the command were
to be handed to the National Centre for the Control of Small Arms and Light
Weapons. [@3] Captain Dauda Iliya (rtd.), representing the National Centre for the Control of Small Arms and Light Weapons' zonal director Jamil Mustafar, assured the Customs Service that the centre would take full custody of the items in line with operational procedure. [@4]

The NAFDAC state director, Muyideen Suleiman, called for the arrest and
prosecution of those behind the trade, recalling that a similar earlier seizure
produced no arrest. [@1,4] Kebbi Area Command acting controller Olufemi Egunjobi
said the exercise showed the agencies' shared commitment to public safety and to
fighting illicit trade. [@1,4]
