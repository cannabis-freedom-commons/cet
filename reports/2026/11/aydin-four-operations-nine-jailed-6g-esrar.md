Aydın provincial police's narcotics crimes branch said it carried out four
separate operations simultaneously in the Nazilli and Efeler districts on the
two days before 26 November 2026. [@1] The operations were aimed at preventing
and tracking drug sales, the police said. [@1] Officers detained nine suspects
in the four operations. [@1] All nine were jailed after being taken to court
following their processing at the police station. [@1]

Searches of the suspects' homes, workplaces and persons found 6 grams of esrar,
the Turkish word for cannabis. [@1] The same searches seized 1,315 synthetic
pills, 10 grams of methamphetamine, 12 grams of bonzai, 5 grams of cocaine and 1
gram of heroin, none of which is counted in the cannabis figures. [@1] Police
also seized 108,200 Turkish lira in cash that they assessed as proceeds of drug
sales. [@2]
