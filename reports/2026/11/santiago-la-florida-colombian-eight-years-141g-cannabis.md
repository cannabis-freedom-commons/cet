Santiago's Sixth Oral Criminal Court sentenced a Colombian man to eight years in
effective prison for drug trafficking committed on 25 April 2025 in Santiago and
La Florida. [@1] The court found he coordinated the receipt, transfer and
storage of more than 41 kilos of cocaine base while also keeping cannabis and a
large amount of cash in two properties in the capital. [@1] Carabineros officers
watching the properties saw him carry two sacks from a vehicle to a flat on
Santo Domingo Street and then go to a home in La Florida. [@1]

Under judicial authorisation they searched both properties and seized 41 kilos
and 673 grams of cocaine base and $35,626,000 in cash in Santiago and 141 grams
of cannabis, five phones and $20,350,000 in La Florida. [@1]

The prosecutors charged him as the author of illicit drug trafficking under Law
20,000 and sought seven years in prison and a 100 UTM fine. [@1] At trial the
defence did not dispute the facts or their classification as drug trafficking
and sought recognition of his cooperation during the proceedings. [@1] The court
recognised his clean record in Chile and Colombia as a mitigating factor but
rejected substantial cooperation because his identification and detention
resulted solely from police work. [@1] It imposed eight years in effective
prison, a 100 UTM fine, and confiscation of the drugs, five phones and
$55,976,000 in cash. [@1] Time in custody since 25 April 2025 counts toward the
sentence, alongside disqualifications from public office, political rights and
certain professions, costs, DNA registry entry and communications to SENDA and
the National Migration Service once the judgment is final. [@1]
