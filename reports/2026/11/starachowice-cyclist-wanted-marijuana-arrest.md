Traffic police in Starachowice stopped a 34-year-old local man who was riding a
bicycle for a road check on Thursday 19 November 2026. [@1,4] A sobriety test
showed alcohol in his body. [@1,4] He was fined 1,000 złoty on the spot for riding
under the influence. [@3] A records check showed he was wanted on an arrest
warrant to serve 182 days of substitute imprisonment. [@1,4]

During the check officers found 1.8 grams of dried plant material and nearly 12
grams of white powder on him. [@1,4] Preliminary tests showed the substances were
marijuana and amphetamine. [@1,4] The seized substances were sent for further
testing. [@3]

He was detained and placed in a penal institution, where he will serve the
182-day substitute sentence. [@2,4] Police said further legal consequences await
him beyond the term he was taken in to serve. [@2,4] Possession of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances carries up to three years in prison. [@4]
