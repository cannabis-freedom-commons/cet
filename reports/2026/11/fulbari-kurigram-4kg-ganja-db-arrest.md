Kurigram district's Detective Branch (DB) police arrested Toffazzal Hossain,
alias Tofa, 52, in Fulbari and seized 4 kg of ganja at his home. [@1] The
district police statement named the arrested man as Toffazzal Hossain Tofa, 52,
son of the late Abdur Razzak of Kurusa Ferusa. [@3] The raid was at Kurusa
Ferusa village in Nawdanga Union, a rural administrative unit of Fulbari
Upazila, at about 3 a.m. on Saturday 21 November 2026. [@1] That statement put
the operation at about 3:05 a.m. on 21 November. [@3] Acting on a tip-off, a DB
team searched the house, recovered the ganja and arrested him at the scene. [@1]

Kurigram district DB officer in charge Md Rezaul Karim confirmed the arrest and
said police were running regular anti-drug operations in the district. [@1] He
said a case was being filed against the suspect at Fulbari police station under
the Narcotics Control Act. [@1]

Later accounts reported that the case was filed and the suspect was sent to jail
custody through a court. [@2] The search was of the suspect's own home in Kurusa
Ferusa, and he was caught red-handed with the ganja. [@4]
