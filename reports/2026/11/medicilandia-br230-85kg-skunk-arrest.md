In a joint patrol on the Transamazônica highway, Federal Highway Police and
Military Police officers stopped a silver SUV at kilometre 735 of the BR-230 in
Medicilândia, in southwestern Pará. [@1] A couple were in the car when officers
approached it. [@1] The 39-year-old driver, a native of Santarém, was arrested
in flagrante on suspicion of drug trafficking under article 33 of Law
11.343/2006. [@1]

The drug was hidden in an improvised structure behind the panel of the silver
SUV. [@1] On inspection, officers found a hidden compartment holding 74 tablets
of the substance. [@1] Police weighed the load at 85.05 kilograms of skunk-type
marijuana. [@1]

The stop took place on the Transamazônica highway in Medicilândia in
southwestern Pará on Monday 16 November 2026. [@1] The man, the vehicle and all
the seized material were taken to the Medicilândia Civil Police station, where
the legal procedures were carried out. [@1]
