Oleśnica police arrested a 21-year-old man who kept marijuana in his flat. [@1]
The arrest was made by officers of the station's criminal division in the course
of their work. [@2] Police said the man had the banned substances at his place
of residence. [@2]

The marijuana seized from him was enough, the police said, to prepare several
dozen portions. [@1] The 21-year-old has heard a charge of possession of
intoxicating substances. [@2] Under the Act on Counteracting Drug Addiction, the
charge carries up to three years in prison. [@1]

The Oleśnica police said their officers run prevention work with children and
young people, teaching them how dangerous intoxicating substances can be. [@2]
The police appealed to young people to act sensibly and take responsibility for
their decisions, saying that curiosity or a wish to impress peers was not worth
risking health and the future. [@2]
