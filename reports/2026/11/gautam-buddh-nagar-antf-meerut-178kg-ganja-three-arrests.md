The Anti-Narcotics Task Force team from Meerut arrested three accused in Gautam
Buddh Nagar during an operation against the drug trade in connection with the
recovery of a large consignment of ganja, in what was described as a major
success for the team. [@1] The team seized 178 kilograms of ganja in Gautam
Buddh Nagar during the same operation, a recovery the report also describes as
made from Noida. [@1] According to preliminary information, the quantity of the
seized ganja was 178 kilograms and its estimated market value was about one
crore rupees. [@1]

Police arrested the three accused at the time of the ganja recovery and took
them into custody together with the seizure. [@1] After the arrests, police sent
the three accused to jail through judicial process. [@1] The available account
places both the seizure and the three arrests in Gautam Buddh Nagar district as
parts of the same reported ANTF Meerut team action. [@1]
