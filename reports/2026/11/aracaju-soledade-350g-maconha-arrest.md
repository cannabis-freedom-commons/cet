A man was arrested in flagrante on suspicion of drug trafficking on the
afternoon of Monday 23 November 2026 in Bairro Soledade, in the north zone of
Aracaju. [@1] The case was recorded by military officers of the Shock Police
Battalion, who were patrolling the area when they saw the man leave a residence
and get on the back of a motorcycle. [@1] During the resulting stop, officers
found a crack rock in the suspect's possession. [@1]

In the property where he had been, officers seized 350 grams of maconha, 33
grams of cocaine and 17 grams of crack. [@1] Police described the haul as about
400 grams of drugs in total. [@1] With his partner's authorisation, the team
searched the residence and found further portions hidden in different parts of
the house, including inside a cushion and shoes. [@1] Alongside the drugs,
officers seized a precision scale, zip-lock packaging, two mobile phones and a
small amount of cash. [@1]

The man was formally arrested and taken to the police station with the seized
material. [@1]
