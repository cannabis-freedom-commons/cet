Preston Crown Court convicted Bradley Taylor on 11 November 2026 following a
Wyre Task Force investigation and sentenced him to five years and six months'
imprisonment. [@1] He had pleaded guilty to three charges of drug possession and
supply at Preston Crown Court. [@1]

One count was being concerned in the supply of crack cocaine and heroin. [@1] A
second count was possession with intent to supply crack cocaine. [@1] The third
count was possession with intent to supply cannabis, a Class B drug. [@1] He
admitted being concerned in the supply of Class A drugs, including crack cocaine
and heroin, as well as possession with intent to supply both Class A and Class B
drugs. [@2]

Lancashire Police said the sentence resulted from proactive work by Wyre Task
Force officers to disrupt illegal drug supply. [@1] Police said the supply of
controlled drugs is often linked to violence, exploitation, acquisitive crime
and anti-social behaviour. [@1] Lancashire Constabulary said it continues to
pursue those involved in drug-related offending and work to safeguard vulnerable
people. [@1]
