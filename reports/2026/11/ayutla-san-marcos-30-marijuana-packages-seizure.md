Guatemala's National Civil Police reported that agents of its anti-narcotics
analysis unit SGAIA, working with Guatemalan Army soldiers, seized 30 packages
of marijuana on the Suchiate River street in Ayutla, San Marcos. [@1] The police
identified the participating unit by its full name, the Subdirección General de
Análisis de Información Antinarcótica, known as SGAIA. [@1] The soldiers were
described as troops of the Army of Guatemala acting in coordination with those
police agents. [@1] The anti-narcotics agents and soldiers located the 30
packages hidden in a black bag on that street in Ayutla. [@1]

According to the police report, the packages were concealed inside a black bag.
[@1] The stated location was the calle del Río Suchiate, in the municipality of
Ayutla in the department of San Marcos. [@1] Authorities did not state the
weight of the seized marijuana. [@1] Authorities reported no arrests linked to
the seizure. [@1]

The seizure was reported on 21 November 2026, and no separate date for the
operation was given. [@1] No charges, custody decisions or court outcomes were
reported in connection with the seizure. [@1]
