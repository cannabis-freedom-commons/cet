A dog from the canine brigade flagged a young man who was then checked by
Tergnier police on Tuesday 17 November 2026 in the underpass of the station.
[@1] The check was part of a wider law-enforcement awareness operation requested
by the sub-prefecture. [@1] Forty grams of cannabis resin were found on him,
leading to his placement in police custody and a search of his home. [@1]
Investigators found two handguns, cash and a further quantity of cannabis resin
at the home. [@1]

Officers also searched the home of a relative the next day, as the detainee held
the key to it. [@1] A few grams of cannabis resin were found at the relative's
home. [@1]

Presented to the justice system on Thursday 19 November under a reconnaissance
préalable de culpabilité (a procedure in which the accused admits the facts),
the 24-year-old man was sentenced to six months in prison with the sentence
suspended. [@1] He was also ordered to undergo treatment for his addiction and
to find employment. [@1]
