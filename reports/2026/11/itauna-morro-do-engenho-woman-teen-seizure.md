Military police arrested an 18-year-old woman in flagrante for drug trafficking
and apprehended a 17-year-old adolescent at a residence in Morro do Engenho,
Itaúna. [@1] Both were taken to the Civil Police station for the legal
procedures. [@1]

During the intervention at the house officers seized cannabis bars, smaller
portions of the same substance, large portions of cocaine, Cannabis sativa
plants and a precision scale. [@1]

Tático Móvel officers, who work in the Homicide Prevention Patrol, went to the
house to check an anonymous Disque-Denúncia 181 tip that Cannabis sativa was
being cultivated there. [@1] The operation took place on the night of Monday 23
November 2026. [@1]
