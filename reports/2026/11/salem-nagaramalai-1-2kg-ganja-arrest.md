Alagapuram police in Salem were on patrol duty at the Nagaramalai foothills the
day before yesterday when the encounter took place. [@1] During the patrol,
officers noticed a man standing in a suspicious manner and detained him for
questioning. [@1] Officers searched the bag the detained man was carrying to
check its contents. [@1] Questioning after the search identified the man as
21-year-old Ramprasad of Pallikalani in Namakkal district. [@1]

The search of the bag found 1.2 kg of ganja in his possession. [@1] Ganja is the
Tamil term used in this report for cannabis. [@1] Police seized the 1.2 kg of
ganja recovered from the bag. [@1] Police said the inquiry found that Ramprasad
had tried to sell the ganja. [@1]

Police then arrested Ramprasad in connection with the seized ganja and took him
into custody. [@1] Only one person was detained and arrested in this operation.
[@1] Salem is the city where Alagapuram police and the Nagaramalai foothills are
located. [@1] The 1.2 kg figure is the police search figure for the ganja in the
bag. [@1]
