Domodedovo customs officers stopped a 19-year-old foreign traveller arriving
from Bangkok via Dubai and found nearly 10 kilograms of banned substances in his
suitcase. [@1] Officers stopped him in the 'green' corridor during selective
control and, searching the case, found two plastic jars containing hair masks,
seven packets of sauce holding a brown liquid, and one glass jar of massage oil
with a light-yellow liquid. [@1]

Examination of the samples showed that the brown liquids were cannabis oil
(hashish oil) weighing more than 9.3 kg, while the light-yellow liquid contained
421.5 grams of tetrahydrocannabinol. [@1] Customs said the luggage held more
than 9.7 kg of narcotic substances in total. [@1]

The passenger said he did not know what was in the case and had been asked to
carry it for a reward, while acknowledging that he knew the customs rules. [@1]
A criminal case was opened for smuggling narcotics in an especially large
quantity under paragraph 'b' of part 4 of Article 229.1 of Russia's criminal
code. [@1] The provision carries a sentence of 15 to 20 years' imprisonment with
a fine of up to one million roubles, or life imprisonment. [@1]
