Police in Manuguru, Telangana, searched a house at Gandhi Bomma Center on the
morning of 22 November 2026 after receiving information that ganja was being
stored there illegally. [@1] The house was the residence of a man named Pasha,
who lived at Gandhi Bomma Center within the limits of the Manuguru police
station. [@1]

Officers seized 1.5 kilograms of ganja during the search. [@1] They took three
people into custody and arrested them on suspicion of illegally possessing the
ganja. [@1] The police summary of the operation also recorded the 1.5 kg
seizure, the arrest of three people and their removal to remand. [@1]

A case was registered against the three suspects under the NDPS Act, the Indian
law covering narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances. [@1] As part of the
next legal steps, the suspects were produced in a court and sent to judicial
remand, according to the police. [@1]
