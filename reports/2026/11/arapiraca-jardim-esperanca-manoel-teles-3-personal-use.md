Three distinct Military Police actions resulted in seizures of cocaine and
maconha during Friday 20 November 2026 in Arapiraca. [@1] The occurrences were
handled by teams of the 3rd Military Police Battalion and involved two adults
and a minor, all released after legal procedures for personal consumption were
recorded. [@1] Around 17h in Jardim Esperanca, a Canil patrol approached a youth
walking on the pavement who abruptly changed direction, quickened his pace and
tried to enter a home on seeing the patrol car. [@1] Officers found a small
portion of maconha known as a balinha in the pocket of his shorts. [@1] A check
showed the suspect was under 18. [@1] He said the drug was for his own
consumption and that he had received it from his brother. [@1] His mother
attended the scene and accompanied the detailed occurrence bulletin, signing a
commitment to present him at a future hearing. [@1]

About half an hour earlier at 16h30 the same Canil team had made another
approach in Jardim Esperanca, an area described as known for trafficking. [@1] A
man in front of a property there showed nervousness and quickened his steps on
noticing the police approach. [@1] Officers seized a bombinha of maconha from
him, and he said he was a user, signed a detailed term and was released. [@1]

That morning around 10h another stop occurred on Rua Abel Monteiro de Maia in
Manoel Teles. [@1] Radio Patrol 01 officers became suspicious of a parked Fiat
Argo with closed windows after its occupant made an abrupt movement to hide
something inside the car. [@1] A personal search found ten pins of a
cocaine-like substance in the suspect's left pocket, and officers seized the
material and drew up a detailed occurrence term. [@1]
