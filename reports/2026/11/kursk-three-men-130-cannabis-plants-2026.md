One of them found a patch of wild-growing cannabis in Medvensky district, Kursk
Oblast, and decided to turn it into a business. [@1] He tended the plants
carefully as they grew. [@1] He harvested 130 plants, moved them to Kursk and
planned to process the raw material for sale. [@1]

He brought in an acquaintance who was to help find buyers. [@1] The men had
already sold a first portion when police detained all three red-handed as they
tried to sell the next one. [@1] A search of one suspect's home also found 4
grams of synthetic drugs, which are not counted in the cannabis figures. [@1]
The investigative unit of the Seimsky police division in Kursk handled the case.
[@2]

All three face trial under the article on illegal circulation of prohibited
substances, and the case was due to be considered in court shortly. [@1]
