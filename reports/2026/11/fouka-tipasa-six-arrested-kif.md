The operation led to the arrest of six people, including one woman, who police
said belonged to an organized network active from a southern wilaya to the north
of Algeria. [@1,2] The arrests were announced by the General Directorate of
National Security (DGSN) after an operation by the mobile brigade of Fouka's
judicial police in Tipasa wilaya. [@1,2] Investigators intercepted two passenger
vehicles in Tipasa wilaya that were being used to carry the illicit substances.
[@1,2]

The inquiry, supervised by the competent prosecution, also seized a quantity of
kif traité, or processed cannabis, along with a sum of money in national
currency and prohibited bladed weapons. [@1,2] The same operation seized nearly 29
kg of cocaine and 3,669 capsules of the psychotrope pregabalin, which are not
counted as cannabis. [@1] Officers found the cocaine hidden in a suitcase in the
first vehicle and the pregabalin capsules inside the fuel tank of the second.
[@1]

The six suspects were presented before the prosecutor at the tribunal in Koléa.
[@1,2]
