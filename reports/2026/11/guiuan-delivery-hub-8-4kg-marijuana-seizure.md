Police and PDEA agents found more than P1.2 million worth of dried marijuana in
a parcel at a delivery hub in Guiuan, Eastern Samar, on the evening of 21
November 2026. [@1] The interception was a joint operation by the Guiuan
Municipal Police Station and the Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency's Eastern
Samar provincial office, acting on an anonymous tip about a suspicious item at
the hub. [@1] A K9 team helped the anti-narcotics operatives carry out a search
and seizure operation through random inspection. [@1]

Officers found one medium-sized box wrapped in bubble wrap holding eight
transparent plastic sachets of dried leaves suspected to be marijuana. [@1] The
leaves had a total estimated weight of 8,400 grams, or 8.4 kilograms. [@1] The
Dangerous Drugs Board's estimation matrix put their value at P1,260,000. [@1]

The seized items were marked and inventoried on site before required witnesses,
including elected barangay officials — the smallest unit of local government —
as required by Republic Act No. 9165. [@1] Officers took them to the PDEA-8
Chemist Section for qualitative and quantitative laboratory examination. [@1]
