A 30-year-old woman was detained by National Police officers after she was found
allegedly transporting 30 kilograms of marijuana. [@1] The operation took place
at kilometre 25 of the road from Trujillo into the Andean part of La Libertad,
near the Menocucho toll booth in Laredo district. [@1]

The intervention was carried out by officers of the Criminal Investigation
Division (Divincri) Trujillo. [@1] While checking two cardboard boxes, the
officers found pressed marijuana inside. [@1] Officers also seized two mobile
phones and 160 soles in cash. [@1]

The woman and the seized material were handed to the competent authorities. [@1]
