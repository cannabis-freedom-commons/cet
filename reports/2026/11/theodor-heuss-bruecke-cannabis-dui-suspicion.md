Police from Wiesbaden and Mainz carried out a joint, cross-state traffic check
on the Theodor-Heuss-Brücke over the Rhine, checking 115 vehicles in both
directions between 18:00 and 00:00. [@1] In one of four drug tests given during
the evening, the suspicion arose that a person had driven under the influence of
cannabis. [@1] Officers ordered a blood sample, which was taken by a doctor.
[@1]

The same check produced two fines for failing to wear a seat belt and a defect
notice for missing mandatory warning equipment. [@1] Police also opened an
investigation into a man suspected of illegal entry or stay. [@1]

The stated aim of the cross-state checks was to noticeably improve road safety
and detect possible violations early. [@1] Police said the operation was marked
by good, close cooperation between the Wiesbaden and Mainz crews and that the
format would be continued in future. [@1]
