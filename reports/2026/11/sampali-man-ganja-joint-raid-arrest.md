Polrestabes Medan, the city police force, and Polda Sumut, the North Sumatra
provincial police, held a joint raid on Jalan H Anif in Sampali on the night of
Thursday 26 November 2026. [@1] Officers detained a 44-year-old man identified
only by the initials MS, who was carrying one package of ganja. [@1] The package
was found in the left pocket of his trousers as he rode a black Honda Vario
motorcycle. [@1] MS told officers he had obtained the ganja from an acquaintance
in the area of the Ilak café. [@1] He and the package were handed to the
Satresnarkoba, the city police narcotics unit, for further examination. [@1]

The raid followed a readiness roll call led by Medan city police chief Kombes
Pol Jean Calvijn Simanjuntak at Jalan H Anif in Medan Estate. [@1] Medan city
police intelligence chief Kompol Lengkap Suherman Siregar said the raid targeted
illegal street racing, motorcycle gangs, street crime, drug abuse, thuggery,
weapons and non-standard exhausts. [@1]

Officers also held the driver of a white Toyota Avanza carrying 13 empty
35-litre jerrycans that had held diesel, suspected of fuel stockpiling. [@1] The
driver and the vehicle were handed to the city police criminal investigation
unit for further inquiry. [@1] Seven motorcycles were also seized for traffic
violations including missing plates, non-standard exhausts and missing vehicle
documents. [@1]
