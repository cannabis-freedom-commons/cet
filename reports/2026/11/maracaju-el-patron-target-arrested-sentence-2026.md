Military Police arrested a 47-year-old man convicted of drug trafficking in
Maracaju, about 160 kilometres from Campo Grande, on Wednesday 25 November 2026.
[@3] Officers of the Força Tática, the Military Police's tactical unit, of the
15th Battalion stopped him in a vehicle on Rua Almirante Barroso in the Alto
Maracaju neighbourhood. [@1] The stop executed a final arrest warrant that the
Criminal Court of Maracaju issued the previous night, and the man had six years
of closed-regime prison left to serve under a conviction that had become final,
with no further appeals possible. [@1] A check of the National Arrest Warrant
Bank of the National Council of Justice confirmed the open order, and in the
vehicle officers found R$4,530 in cash, two knives, two whetstones, rods used to
sharpen knives and a mobile phone. [@1] He was accompanied by his lawyer and
taken to the city's Civil Police station, in an action with Civil Police
support. [@1]

The Military Police said the man was linked to the 33.3 tonnes of marijuana that
the Border Operations Department (DOF) seized on 26 August 2020 from a bitrem
truck on a rural road off the MS-166 in Maracaju, then the largest seizure in
Brazil. [@1] In December 2021 the Civil Police launched Operation El Patrón from
that load and served seven preventive arrest warrants, and the Military Police
said the man arrested now was one of its main targets. [@1]

Campo Grande News named him as Rondineli Amarila Herrera, 47, whom the Civil
Police identified as the owner of the 2020 load. [@2] The warrant he was
arrested on came from another case, an investigation into the sale of cocaine
and coca paste in Maracaju. [@2] In September 2025 the second criminal chamber
of the Mato Grosso do Sul state court of justice (TJMS) unanimously rejected the
appeals of the defence and the prosecution service, making the sentence final.
[@2] The El Patrón case itself is separate and was still under appeal and seal,
although the TJMS confirmed his conviction in it in 2025. [@2] He was to appear
at a custody hearing. [@2]
