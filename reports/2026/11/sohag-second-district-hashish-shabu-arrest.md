Security forces at the Second Sohag police station, in Sohag Governorate,
arrested a man accused of dealing in narcotics. [@1,2,3] The suspect, identified as
Emad H., is 25 and lives in Akhmim markaz. [@1,2,3] He was stopped while frequenting
the police district to carry out illegal activity. [@1,2,3] Officers found 30
tickets of shabu and pieces of hashish in his possession. [@1,2,3] The arrest was
part of a security campaign aimed at catching drug dealers. [@1,2,3]

The campaign followed directions from Sohag security director Major General
Ashraf Abdel Malek and criminal investigation chief Major General Mohamed
El-Khouly. [@1,2,3] Brigadier General Tarek Abu Sedira, head of investigations at
the Sohag security directorate, was named among those directing the campaign.
[@1,2,3] The campaign was led in the field by Colonel Islam Agag, the investigations inspector, Lieutenant Colonel Sherif Abdel Mohsen, head of the Second Sohag investigations unit, and two other station officers. [@3] Their directives called for intensified security campaigns against
drug-trafficking crimes. [@1,3] After the legal procedures were completed, the
suspect and the seized items were referred to the competent authorities. [@1,2,3]
