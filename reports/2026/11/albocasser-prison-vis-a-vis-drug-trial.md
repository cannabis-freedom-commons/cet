The Second Section of the Castellón provincial court tried former partners over
an alleged drug handover during a vis-à-vis in Albocàsser prison. [@1] The woman
denied supplying drugs to the inmate, her former partner. [@1]

Prison officers found hashish, high-purity cocaine and ketamine inside the
inmate's body after the family visit ended. [@1] The inmate, who said he had
spent 24 years behind bars, claimed he was already carrying the drugs before the
meeting and cleared the woman of responsibility. [@1] He told the court he had
brought the drugs from the prison wing. [@1]

Albocàsser officers testified that a sniffer dog marked the prisoner when the
vis-à-vis ended. [@1] One officer said the inmate first claimed he had smoked a
joint and later admitted he was carrying drugs. [@1] Another officer said the
inmate described the drugs as for personal use while admitting his wife had
brought them, while a third mentioned an alleged payment to an inmate in
exchange for drugs. [@1]

The prosecutor requested four years in prison for each defendant and the trial
was left ready for judgment on Monday. [@1]
