Military police in Breves, on Marajó Island in Pará, arrested a man caught in
the act during an operation that seized 32 kilograms of skunk cannabis. [@1]
Officers from the Grupamento Fluvial de Segurança (Gflu) had been monitoring a
wharf on the city waterfront where the illicit load was to be shipped. [@1] When
they noticed the police approach, those already aboard a boat escaped by river.
[@1] A suspect who had remained at the wharf tried to run into dense woodland
nearby but was reached and arrested by the officers. [@1]

During the searches in the vegetation the sniffer dog Jay-Z found two black bags
hidden among the trees containing dozens of skunk tablets, described as a more
concentrated and valuable form of marijuana. [@1]

The state security secretariat Segup said Marajó is strategic and one of the
main drug-trafficking routes in the state, its river network attracting criminal
organisations. [@1] The man and all the seized material were taken to the Civil
Police station in Breves; police said the drug would undergo technical
examination to establish its origin and then be destroyed by incineration. [@1]
Police said the investigation continues in order to identify and locate the
suspects who fled by river. [@1]
