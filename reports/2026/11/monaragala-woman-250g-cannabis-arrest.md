Police in Monaragala arrested a woman after discovering a consignment of
narcotics worth more than Rs 1.5 million, allegedly transported from Colombo to
a residence on Dutugemunu Road in a travel bag by a courier service. [@1] The
raid was conducted at the residence, which lies in the Monaragala Police
Division, acting on confidential information received by the Monaragala
Divisional Anti-Corruption Unit about a suspected drug trafficking operation.
[@1]

In a search of the premises, police recovered 150 grams of Kush cannabis, 100
grams of hashish and more than 40 narcotic tablets, all concealed inside a
travel bag. [@1]

Police estimated the street value of the seized drugs at more than Rs 1.5
million. [@1] The arrested suspect was produced before the Monaragala
Magistrate's Court on 16 November 2026. [@1] Police said further investigations
would determine how long the alleged drug trafficking operation had been running
and identify those believed to be behind it. [@1]
