Lalpur police in Natore arrested Kalam Hossain, known as Titu, 40, a drug
trader, and seized 1.5 kg of cannabis from him. [@1] Officers detained him at
about 4 am on Monday 23 November 2026 in Bormahati village, in Lalpur upazila.
[@1] He is the son of Abdul Jalil of the same village. [@1]

Shafiqul Islam, the officer-in-charge of Lalpur police station, said a police
team raided the village on a tip-off. [@1] Officers found the cannabis in a
date-palm grove in the village and arrested Titu there. [@1] Police filed a case
against him under the Narcotics Control Act at the station. [@1]

He was produced before a court in the afternoon of 23 November. [@1]
