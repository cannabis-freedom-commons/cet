On 23 November 2026, officers of the Customs Intelligence Unit of Trichy Customs
(Preventive) carried out an operation on specific intelligence about suspected
narcotics smuggling through the coastal region. [@1] The operation led to the
arrest of two persons allegedly connected with the smuggling activity. [@1]

Officers seized 75.700 kg of ganja valued at ₹56,77,500 allegedly smuggled
through the Ramnad coastal area. [@1] In addition to the drugs, officers seized
a Vallam, a traditional fishing boat suspected to have been used in connection
with transportation or smuggling. [@1] The seized boat was valued at
approximately ₹15 lakh. [@1]

The Ramnad coastal belt, with its fishing activity and maritime connectivity,
requires sustained surveillance to prevent misuse of traditional vessels for
illicit transportation. [@1] Further investigation is expected to establish the
source of the contraband, its intended destination and the possible involvement
of other persons in the network. [@1] The seizure highlights continued vigilance
by Customs authorities over vulnerable coastal routes that may be exploited for
illegal narcotics transportation. [@1]
