Personnel of the National Guard and the Mexican Army operated in the
municipality of Jala, Nayarit, on 13 and 14 November, according to the commands
of the V Military Region and the 13th Military Zone. [@1] During surveillance
patrols and ground reconnaissance on the Potrerillos-Flor road section, military
personnel were attacked by armed civilians and repelled the attack. [@1]
Everything seized was placed at the disposal of the federal Attorney General's
Office in Tepic to continue investigations and expert procedures. [@1]

In the town of Jomulco, working with the Nayarit state prosecutor's office while
executing an investigative order, the forces seized a building, 10 long guns, 93
magazines, 3,807 cartridges, 2.100 kilograms (2.1 kg) of marijuana and tactical
equipment. [@1] On the Potrerillos-Flor road section they seized 6 long guns, 47
magazines, 1,300 cartridges, 2 vehicles and tactical equipment. [@1]

The commands said the actions were carried out in compliance with the National
Public Security Strategy. [@1] The commands said results of this kind weaken
criminal structures, citing much larger administration-wide seizure totals that
are not counted in this report's figures. [@1]
