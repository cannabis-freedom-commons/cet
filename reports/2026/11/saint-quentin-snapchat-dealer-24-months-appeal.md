The Amiens appeal court on 18 November 2026 sentenced a man who sold drugs on
Snapchat and delivered them across the Saint-Quentinois to 24 months in prison.
[@1] The defendant is a 29-year-old from Saint-Quentin who did not dispute the
facts and was retried at his request on 21 October 2026 by the Amiens appeal
court. [@2] His earlier penalty had been 36 months in prison plus a six-month
revocation of a suspended sentence for drug trafficking. [@2] He told the court
he started dealing in December 2025 and continued until his arrest in May. [@2]
He appeared in custody at the appeal hearing. [@2]

Between November 2025 and May 2026 he offered cocaine, heroin and cannabis resin
on Snapchat. [@1] He delivered the drugs directly to clients’ homes in
Saint-Quentin, Vermand, Mennevret and Bohain-en-Vermandois. [@1]

He said money problems and paying for his son’s private school led him to deal
before he fell into a vicious circle. [@1] He said an unnamed supplier persuaded
him to set up a small business. [@2]
