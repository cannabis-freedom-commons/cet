Komakhan police arrested a 22-year-old man from Udhampur district, Jammu and
Kashmir, after stopping his grey scooter at Temri naka on the Khariar
Road-Bagbahara route. [@1] The rider identified himself as Kalluram Agrawal of
Ramnagar tehsil, and officers linked him to ganja being carried from Odisha
toward Bagbahara. [@1] The interception was part of vehicle checks by the
Anti-Narcotics Task Force and Komakhan police. [@2]

Searching the scooter's storage compartment, police recovered 6.210 kilograms of
ganja, which they valued at Rs 310,500. [@1] Police said Agrawal had bought the
consignment at Raja Khariar in Odisha and was taking it to Bhilai-Durg in
Chhattisgarh to sell. [@1] The compartment also contained a spare number plate
bearing Durg registration. [@2]

Police seized the ganja along with the scooter and a mobile phone, placing the
combined seized property at Rs 415,500. [@2] Officers arrested Agrawal and
registered crime number 206/2026 under section 20(B)(II)(B) of the NDPS Act at
Komakhan police station, where the case was placed under investigation. [@2]
