Boudh Police stepped up its drive against illegal cannabis cultivation under the
'Sankalp: Nasha Mukt Boudh' campaign during the 2026–27 crop season. [@1] In a
single enforcement operation over 24 hours, police destroyed illegal cannabis
cultivation spread over 375.78 acres (about 152 hectares) in identified hotspot
areas of the district. [@1]

With that operation, the total area of illegal cannabis cultivation destroyed
across Boudh district in the ongoing crop season reached 5,386.70 acres, police
said. [@1] Of that season total, 4,474.69 acres of illegal cultivation had been
destroyed in identified hotspot areas. [@1] Boudh Police had identified
12,892.52 acres as hotspot areas requiring focused surveillance and enforcement
against illegal cannabis cultivation. [@1] Police said those areas were being
closely monitored to prevent further cultivation and curb narcotics-related
activities. [@1]

Under the Superintendent of Police, Boudh, coordinated operations were under way
to find illegal cannabis plantations, destroy the crops and take legal action
against those involved. [@1] Boudh Police said the campaign reflected a
commitment to eliminating illegal cannabis cultivation, preventing drug
trafficking and safeguarding younger people. [@1] Police appealed to the public
to share information about illegal cannabis cultivation and drug-related
activities. [@1]
