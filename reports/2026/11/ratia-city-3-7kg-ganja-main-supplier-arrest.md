Ratia City police in Fatehabad district, Haryana, arrested Amarjeet alias
Bhagat, described as the main supplier in a case built on 3 kg 700 g of ganja.
[@1] The suspect is from Palsar village in Fatehabad district. [@1] He was
produced before a court, which sent him to judicial custody. [@1]

The case began on 2 October 2026, when a Crime Investigation Branch team led by
Assistant Sub-Inspector Ramkumar was patrolling near Ramnagar Colony in Ratia.
[@1] Officers stopped Ramkumar of Bosti village after he turned back with a
plastic sack on seeing them. [@1] They found 3 kg 700 g of ganja on him. [@1]
Ratia City police station registered case 211 on 2 October 2026 under sections
20B, 27A, 61 and 85 of the NDPS Act, India's main drug law. [@1]

Police said the arrested man was being questioned about the network that
supplied the drug. [@1] Fatehabad police announced the arrest in their own
statement, saying the investigation into other aspects of the drug supply was
continuing. [@3] Dainik Bhaskar reported the arrest as one of three made by
Ratia City police in three separate cases that day. [@2]
