Two residents of Lubin County were stopped for road checks by police officers
from Chocianow and Przemkow. [@1] Officers from the Chocianow police station
stopped a Volkswagen driven by a 41-year-old resident of Lubin County. [@1] The
officers checked whether the man was under the influence of intoxicants after
his behaviour aroused their suspicion. [@1] The Przemkow officers noticed a
woman driver who swerved sharply on seeing the patrol car, apparently trying to
avoid the check. [@1] Officers stopped the vehicle driven by the 22-year-old
resident of Lubin County. [@1]

A roadside drug test indicated the presence of marijuana, amphetamine and
methamphetamine in the 41-year-old man. [@1] The 22-year-old woman's test
indicated the presence of amphetamine and methamphetamine. [@1] A check of the
woman's data showed that she was subject to a court ban on driving motor
vehicles. [@1] In both cases officers prevented the drivers from continuing to
drive. [@1] Blood was taken from the 41-year-old and the 22-year-old for
detailed laboratory tests to determine whether they had driven under the
influence of intoxicants. [@1]

Driving a motor vehicle under the influence of intoxicants carries up to three
years of imprisonment. [@1] Breaching a court driving ban can lead to
imprisonment from three months to five years. [@1] A court will decide what
happens next to both Lubin County residents. [@1]
