Excise officers in Pathanapuram, in Kerala's Kollam district, arrested an
18-year-old from Assam on suspicion of keeping cannabis for sale. [@1] Officers
seized 1.050 kilograms of cannabis from him. [@1] The excise named him as
Rahmath Ali, a native of Baladanga in Narayanpur, Dekhajuli, in Assam's Sonitpur
district. [@1]

The excise department said he had been selling cannabis among migrant workers
from other states. [@1] A shadow excise team, working with the help of the
Kollam cyber cell, helped catch him. [@1] Excise inspector S. Amrutha led the
check, with assistant excise inspector S. Sajeev Kumar, preventive officer Saji
John and civil excise officers S. Vineeth, S.S. Arun Kumar and Latheesh Chandran
on the team. [@1]

He was produced before a court, which remanded him to custody. [@1]
