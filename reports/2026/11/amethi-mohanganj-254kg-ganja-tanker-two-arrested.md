Amethi police arrested two men and seized 254 kg of ganja being moved in a
petroleum tanker and an accompanying Scorpio SUV. [@1] Police identified them as
Vimal Singh, 35, and Amar Singh, 45, both residents of Dusauti village in Rae
Bareli district. [@1] Three other suspects fled from the spot, Dainik Bhaskar
reported. [@3]

Mohanganj police stopped the tanker as it came from the Jagdishpur side during a
checking drive made on specific informer input. [@1] Officers found 234 kg of
ganja concealed in a compartment of the tanker and another 20 kg in the Scorpio.
[@1] Police put the market value of the consignment at about Rs 1.30 crore. [@1]
They seized the tanker, a black Scorpio-N and a motorcycle. [@3] Dainik Bhaskar
identified the seized motorcycle as a Splendor. [@2]

The interception was part of Operation Chakravyuh, the Amethi district police's
crackdown on narcotics trafficking. [@1] Superintendent of Police Saravanan T
said the accused told interrogators the ganja had been procured from Odisha.
[@1] Police described the movement as a partnership-based trafficking operation
involving an organised interstate network. [@1] The superintendent formed four
teams to search for the suspects who had fled. [@3] Police said a case had been
registered under the relevant sections and further action was under way. [@3]
Investigators said they were probing the source of the consignment in Odisha,
its intended destination, the financial arrangements and other network members.
