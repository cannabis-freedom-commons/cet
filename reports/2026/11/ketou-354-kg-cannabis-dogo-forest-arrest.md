Police in Benin arrested a presumed cannabis grower known by the nickname
'Petit'. [@2] Investigations after the discovery led officers to the suspect's
home in the Olorun Shogo neighbourhood of Kétou. [@2] He was handed to OCERTID,
Benin's central office for repressing illicit drug and precursor trafficking,
for further investigation. [@1]

The operation was carried out at dawn on Saturday 21 November 2026 by the Idigny
district police station in the hamlet of Agbédo, village of Awaya. [@2] La
Nouvelle Tribune, citing the Republican Police's Facebook page, said the raid
targeted Agbédo hamlet in Awaya village, about 30 km east of the Idigny station.
[@3] Officers found 18 sacks of chanvre indien (Indian hemp) in an earth-walled
room and seized a total of 354.35 kg. [@4] Police said the quantity was the
harvest of the presumed owner. [@1] The sacks were stored near what the police
described as a presumed cultivation area. [@3]

Dunia News reported that the seized cannabis was believed to come from a
plantation set up around Awaya village. [@4]
