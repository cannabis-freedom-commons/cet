Theni district authorities jailed three men under the Goondas
preventive-detention law. [@1] The three are Yogaraj of the Kambam area, Giri,
son of Kannan of Kambam, and Hari Ranjith Kumar, son of Asaithambi of the same
area, all aged 21 except Yogaraj. [@1] Yogaraj had already been arrested in a
ganja case, while Giri and Hari Ranjith Kumar were jailed in assault and
attempted-murder cases. [@1]

Allinagaram police arrested Yogaraj on 18 October with 5 kg of ganja. [@2]
Kambam North police had arrested Giri and Hari Ranjith Kumar the previous month
in a murder case near a cinema theatre in Kambam. [@2]

District Superintendent of Police K. S. Praveen Gautham recommended Goondas
detention for all three, and District Collector V. Sivakrishnamurthy ordered it.
[@1] Prison staff served copies of the detention orders on the three men on
Monday. [@1]
