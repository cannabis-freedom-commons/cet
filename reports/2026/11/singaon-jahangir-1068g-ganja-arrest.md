Deulgaon Raja police raided a house in Singaon Jahangir, in Deulgaon Raja
taluka, after a confidential tip that narcotics were being sold there. [@1] The
search was carried out before dawn on 16 November 2026, from 1:15 to 2:30 a.m.,
with a police team, a gazetted revenue officer, a photographer, a person with a
weighing scale and two official panch witnesses present. [@1] Police seized the
material and took the accused into custody. [@2]

In a brown bag inside the house, officers found 1 kilogram 68 grams of a
substance resembling ganja. [@1] Police valued the seized ganja at about 30,000
rupees and said it had been kept for sale. [@1]

A case was registered against Ramesh Roduba Sanap, aged 65 and a resident of
Singaon Jahangir, under the NDPS Act. [@1] The offence was recorded under
sections 20(b)(ii) and 8(c) of the NDPS Act as crime number 512/2026. [@1]
Police sub-inspector Nitin Ganeshrao Mind filed the complaint, and further
investigation was assigned to assistant police inspector Savita Kuware. [@1] The
operation was carried out under subdivisional police officer Santosh Khade and
police inspector Ashish Ingale, both holding charge of Deulgaon Raja. [@1]
