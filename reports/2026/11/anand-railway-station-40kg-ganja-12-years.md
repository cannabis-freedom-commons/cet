A court in Anand convicted two men from Odisha, Ashok Krishnachandra Nahak and
Radhakrishna Udaynath Sindha, and sentenced each of them to 12 years of rigorous
imprisonment and a fine of one lakh rupees. [@1] The court ordered that each of
them serve a further year of simple imprisonment if the fine is not paid. [@1]
The verdict came from the special NDPS court of Additional Sessions Judge R.B.
Gadhavi in Anand. [@1]

The case began on 11 July 2021, when the two men got off the Puri-Gandhidham
Express at Anand railway station with 40 kg and 110 g of ganja in red, navy-blue
and orange-checked bags, without a permit. [@1] They had boarded the train at
Brahmapur in Odisha and were stopped on platform 3 by Narcotics Department
intelligence officer Amitkumar Shartakumar Khara and his team. [@1] Police said
a woman living in Juhapura, Ahmedabad, had ordered the consignment. [@1] A case
was registered against all three under NDPS Act sections 8(c), 20(b)(2)(c) and
29, and the third accused was arrested on 9 November 2021. [@1]

The investigating team filed a chargesheet in Anand's special court. [@1] At the
trial the prosecution examined eight oral witnesses and produced 75 documentary
exhibits. [@1]

The court found no sufficient evidence against the woman accused of ordering the
ganja, who was in jail when the seizure happened and whose call detail records
were not found, and released her on benefit of doubt. [@1]
