Cenkay Toparlak, 29, of Mississauga, was arrested and charged after an Ontario
Provincial Police traffic stop in West Nipissing. [@2] He was charged with
speeding, driving a vehicle or boat with cannabis readily available, possession
of unmarked cigarettes and possession of an imitation weapon for a dangerous
purpose. [@1] Police said the speeding was 1-49 km/h over the posted limit. [@2]
He was released from custody and is scheduled to appear before the Ontario Court
of Justice in North Bay on 4 February 2027. [@2]

The stop happened at about 3:20 p.m. on 13 November on Golf Course Road in West
Nipissing after officers saw a vehicle travelling at excessive speed. [@1]
During the investigation officers saw an open bag containing suspected cannabis
on the passenger seat. [@1] A subsequent search of the vehicle produced unmarked
cigarettes and a replica handgun. [@1] The Nipissing West detachment of the OPP
said the items were seized after the speeding stop. [@3]
