Carabineros detained two adult men during an anti-drug operation in the plan,
the low-lying central area, of Valparaíso, Chile, where officers also seized
illicit substances and a motorcycle with an active warrant for theft. [@1] Both
detainees were adults with prior criminal records and were placed at the
disposal of the Public Prosecutor's Office, the Fiscalía. [@1]

During the search of the property, officers seized doses of marijuana, cocaine
hydrochloride and clonazepam tablets, along with mobile phones and other items
linked to the investigation. [@1] They also found a motorcycle registered with
an active theft warrant. [@1]

The operation was the result of an investigation by the OS7 unit of Carabineros,
which detected the suspected sale of drugs at a property located beside a
hospital facility in the commune. [@1] It was supported by officers from the
Special Police Operations Group (GOPE), Public Order Control (COP) and Centauro
personnel from Valparaíso. [@1]
