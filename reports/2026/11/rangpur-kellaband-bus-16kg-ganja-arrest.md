On the morning of 14 November 2026, an SRB-13 team from Rangpur carried out an
operation at Kellaband CO Bazar under Kotwali police station in Rangpur city.
[@1] Officers searched a Rangpur-to-Saidpur bus named Rakib Express and seized
16 kg of ganja. [@1,3] They arrested a passenger, 35-year-old Md Suman Mia of
Bhatera in Cumilla Sadar Dakshin. [@1,3]

SRB-13 announced the operation in a press release signed by Senior Assistant
Director (Media) Biplab Kumar Goswami on the evening of Saturday 14 November.
[@1] The same statement, also published by Banglar Alo News, identifies the bus
as Rakib Express, registration Rangpur Metro-Ba 11-0029, and says the 16 kg of
ganja was seized at 8:30 am on the paved Rangpur-to-Saidpur road. [@2] It says
Suman Mia, son of Md Zakir Hossain, of Bhatera (Jangalpur) in Cumilla Sadar
Dakshin, was arrested for keeping the drug in his own custody and transporting
it. [@2]

Biplab Kumar Goswami said the arrested accused and the seized exhibits were
handed to the relevant police station for further legal action. [@1] SRB filed a
case under the Narcotics Control Act as complainant against the accused. [@2]
