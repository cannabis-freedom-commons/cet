Police in Limassol arrested a 53-year-old man under a court warrant after
searches at his home on 18 November 2026. [@1] The 53-year-old remained in
custody and was expected to be brought before Limassol District Court on 19
November 2026 for police to seek a remand order. [@1] A 25-year-old man found in
a room in the same home was arrested for unlawful possession of a controlled
drug after six grinders containing cannabis traces were found there, but he was
later released without being charged. [@1]

Officers found three plastic containers holding approximately 24 grams of
cannabis along with 16 boxes of a pharmaceutical preparation in another part of
the house. [@1] Following directions from the 53-year-old, officers recovered a
small bag concealed in a tree containing seven cannabis packages with a combined
gross weight of approximately 170 grams, together with a set of precision
scales. [@1] Police put the total cannabis at approximately 194 grams, alongside
precision scales and 16 boxes of a pharmaceutical product. [@2]

Police said the search followed assessment of information as part of an
anti-drug-trafficking operation by the DAOE-YKAN units. [@2] The Limassol branch
of the Cyprus Police Drug Law Enforcement Unit (YKAN) is continuing its
investigation. [@3]
