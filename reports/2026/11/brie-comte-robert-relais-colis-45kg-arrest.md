A 20-year-old man was arrested on 24 November 2026 at 12:30 on rue du
Général-Leclerc in Brie-Comte-Robert, Seine-et-Marne, as he left a parcel relay
carrying a package of drugs. [@1] Customs had intercepted a parcel containing
more than 4,500 grams of cannabis herb from Spain, and with the Melun
prosecutor's agreement police watched the relay point where the goods were
delivered. [@1]

The man, who lives in Val-de-Marne, said in questioning that he had collected
the parcel for a third party and was to deliver it, but stayed silent about who
ordered it and the delivery address. [@1]

He admitted most of the facts and said he knew the parcel's contents were
illegal, thinking "that they were narcotics". [@1] He was brought before the
Melun judicial court on 26 November 2026 for immediate trial for attempted
transport and possession of narcotics, complicity in importation, and refusal to
hand over the unlock codes of his phones. [@1] The court sentenced him to 18
months in prison, and he was remanded in custody. [@1]
