Officers from the General Directorate for Drug Control's East Delta region
arrested a man in Barqin village, in the Senbellawein district of Dakahlia
Governorate, and said they caught him with hashish and ice before he could sell
them to his customers. [@1] Police named him as Muhammad R. A. A., known by the
nickname Hamouksha al-Shabrawi, gave his age as 40 and said he was unemployed
and had been arrested before in seven criminal cases, among them kidnapping,
drug, weapons and fighting cases. [@1]

He was trading drugs and had made his home the base for the activity, according
to information that reached the head of the East Delta drug-control region. [@1]
After prosecutors issued permission, a team of drug-control inspectors raided
the home and found quantities of hashish and ice, a sum of money and a mobile
phone. [@1] The hashish and the ice together weighed 25 kilograms, a combined
figure that the accounts did not break down by drug. [@2]

Confronted with the seizure, he admitted holding the drugs for sale, said the
money came from his criminal activity and said the phone was for contacting
customers. [@1] The necessary report was prepared and the Public Prosecution was
notified to begin its investigation. [@1]
