The case arose during a district-wide traffic focus that Upper Austrian police
ran through the night to 21 November 2026. [@1] The district police command for
Grieskirchen and Eferding carried out the checks across the two Upper Austrian
districts. [@2] At one checkpoint officers smelled cannabis and found two young
adults consuming it on a nearby parking lot. [@1] Both will be reported under
the Suchtmittelgesetz, Austria's narcotics law. [@1]

During the operation police provisionally took the driving licences of two
drivers, one for alcohol and one for drugs. [@1] A third driver was reported for
drunk driving after being stopped during the same operation. [@1] Officers also
filed twelve other traffic reports and issued 31 organ mandates, the on-the-spot
fines Austrian police can collect for minor traffic offences. [@1] The district
command announced the results in a statement on the morning of 21 November 2026.
[@2]

Austria sets no fixed blood level for drug driving; a doctor's examination
decides whether a driver is impaired. [@1]
