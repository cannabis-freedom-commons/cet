Lebanese army units carried out raids and set up temporary checkpoints in the
Bekaa, the north and the south to pursue people disturbing security and to
combat drug trafficking. [@2] The army said the raids and checkpoints detained
six Lebanese citizens, 182 Syrians and one Palestinian. [@1] The same statement
reported the arrest of a citizen at Haysha in Akkar for smuggling military
weapons from Syria, with arms, ammunition and military equipment seized. [@3]
The army announced the arrest of 190 people in the operation. [@4] Of those
held, 181 Syrians were arrested in Talia in Baalbek, in Wadi Khaled and Aboudieh
in Akkar and at checkpoints in the north and the Bekaa for entering Lebanon
illegally. [@3]

In Sidon, soldiers arrested a Palestinian man accused of firing weapons on
several dates and wounding a person on 17 August 2025, and seized a military
pistol and a quantity of hashish, called hashishat al-kif, from him. [@3] In
Adloun, in the Sidon area, soldiers arrested a citizen accused of using and
promoting drugs and seized a quantity of narcotics. [@3]

The army said the seized items were handed over and questioning of the detainees
began under the competent judicial authority, and that it was continuing to
pursue others involved. [@3]
