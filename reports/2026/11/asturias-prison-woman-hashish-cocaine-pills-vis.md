The Guardia Civil detained a woman on 14 November 2026 as she tried to bring
hashish, cocaine and pills into the Asturias prison during a vis-a-vis visit.
[@1] Prison officers had first run an internal investigation into possible drug
introductions through Saturday family visits and called in the Guardia Civil
canine unit to cover checks at the prison. [@1] When the visit took place, the
trained dog gave a positive alert on the woman, letting officers intervene and
seize a large quantity of drugs meant for inside the prison. [@1]

The drugs were reportedly intended for distribution among inmates. [@1]
Proceedings were also opened against the inmate the drugs were addressed to.
[@1]

The prison-staff union Tu abandono me puede matar praised the prison officers
for their professionalism and thoroughness in daily surveillance and prevention
work. [@1] The union said that from 2027 the Asturias prison will remain one of
the few Spanish prisons without its own penitentiary canine unit and demanded
one operating seven days a week. [@1]
