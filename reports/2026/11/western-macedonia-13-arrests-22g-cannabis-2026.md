Police in Western Macedonia arrested 13 Greek nationals in ten separate cases
during targeted checks over the last ten days. [@2] The arrests covered drug
possession, driving under the influence of alcohol, driving without a licence
and selling tobacco products to a minor. [@2] The arrests were made by officers
from police departments in Kastoria, Eordea and Voio, traffic units in Kozani,
Eordea, Kastoria and Florina, and crime investigation units. [@1] The drug squad
of the Kastoria security directorate and motorcycle, crime-prevention and
road-accident prevention units took part in the checks. [@1]

Officers seized 22.3 grams of raw cannabis, three cannabis grinders and one
hand-rolled cannabis cigarette. [@1] The police described the drug offences as
possession of narcotic substances. [@2]

The case files against those arrested were to be submitted to the competent
prosecutors. [@3] The regional police directorate said the checks would
continue, with combating drugs, preventing traffic accidents and protecting
minors from tobacco and alcohol as priorities. [@2]
