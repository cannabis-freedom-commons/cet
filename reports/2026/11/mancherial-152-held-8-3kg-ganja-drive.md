Collector Kumar Deepak said everyone should work to completely curb drug use and
transport and build a drug-free society. [@1] He spoke at a district-level Narco
Coordination Committee meeting held on Monday at the Collectorate with concerned
officials. [@1] He said youth should be informed about drugs through posters,
rallies and awareness seminars in schools, colleges, villages and towns. [@1]

Officials said 8.3 kg of ganja had been seized and 152 people arrested so far in
the district. [@1] DCP Bhaskar said 20 ganja live-plant cases had been
registered and a list of 156 suspects prepared. [@1]

Counselling and rehabilitation services are being provided to those addicted to
drugs. [@1] The Collector afterwards released 'Say No to Drugs Yes to Life'
posters. [@1]
