Chithode police acted after receiving information that some people were selling
ganja on the road leading from Chithode to Erode. [@1] Officers detained a
person who was standing suspiciously on that stretch of road and questioned him
about the reported sale. [@1] The questioning identified the first of the three
as Kannan, aged 20, of the Veerappanchatram area in Erode. [@1] The second was
identified as Naveen, aged 19, of Thattankuttai near Tiruchengode in Namakkal
district. [@1] The third was a 17-year-old youth of the Tiruchengode area in the
same district. [@1]

Chithode police arrested all three as the suspects in the roadside sale and took
them into custody. [@1] Officers seized 50 grams of ganja from the three and
took the drug into custody. [@1]

The 50 grams, equivalent to 0.05 kilograms, was the only cannabis quantity
reported in the case, and ganja is the Tamil word for cannabis. [@1] With only
arrests reported and no charges or court outcome stated, the case remained at
the investigation stage. [@1]
