A couple was detained by agents of the Direccion Policial de Investigaciones
(DPI) while transporting 40 packages of suspected marijuana in a pickup truck.
[@1,2] The two detainees were a 35-year-old man known by the alias “El Humilde” and a 32-year-old woman. [@2] The operation took place in the village of Villa Vieja in the capital's Distrito Central. [@3] The load was carried inside sealed boxes, a method that would have allowed
the drugs to be hidden while being moved. [@1] Police seized the gray Honda CR-V and a gray Samsung mobile phone as evidence. [@3] Police valued the suspected marijuana at about 200,000 lempiras on the illicit market. [@2] Officers found 39 wraps of dry plant material, suspected to be marijuana, in two cardboard boxes in the vehicle's trunk. [@2]

During the procedure investigators located the packages containing the suspected
illicit substance, so both occupants of the vehicle were taken into custody by
the police authorities. [@1] The couple was placed at the disposal of the
corresponding authorities to continue the legal process. [@1,3] Investigators alleged that the pair were part of an independent criminal band selling and distributing drugs in eastern Honduras. [@3]

No details were provided on the identity of the detainees or the exact place
where the capture was carried out. [@1] The report was published on 14 November
2026. [@1]
