Panevėžys County police detained two men on the evening of 18 November 2026
after suspected narcotic substances were found in the cars they were using, and
opened pre-trial investigations against both men. [@1] According to the
Panevėžys County Police Headquarters, both vehicle checks were recorded on 18
November. [@1]

In Gitėnai village in Panevėžys district, at about 19:10, officers checked an
Opel car and found suspected narcotic substances, detaining a man born in 2006
on suspicion of the offence. [@1]

In Panevėžys on Velžio kelio street, at about 22:35, officers checking a BMW
found a sealed packet containing suspected narcotic material identified as
marijuana. [@1] In that BMW case, a suspect born in 1990 was detained on
suspicion of the offence. [@1] Both men face pre-trial investigations for
unlawful possession of narcotic or psychotropic substances without intent to
distribute, under Article 259 of the Lithuanian Criminal Code. [@1]
