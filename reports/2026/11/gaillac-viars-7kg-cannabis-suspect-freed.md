The story had started with a minor burglary, but the haul found was anything but
ordinary. [@1] In a Peugeot 207 parked in the Viars neighbourhood of Gaillac,
gendarmes, members of France's gendarmerie, had discovered several kilos of
cannabis resin. [@1] The same car also held handguns, ammunition and packaging
material. [@1] The find brought to light a drug traffic that supplied the Viars
neighbourhood and in which several young Gaillacois, residents of Gaillac, had
been involved. [@1]

Oussama B., identified only by first name and initial, was presented as one cog
in a drug traffic dismantled in the Viars neighbourhood of Gaillac. [@1]
Suspected of being linked to the seven kilos of cannabis discovered in a Peugeot
207, Oussama B. nevertheless escaped the main prosecutions. [@1]

He was tried on Friday 13 November 2026 before the tribunal correctionnel, the
correctional court, in Albi, and left free that same evening. [@1] He was
convicted for possession of narcotic drugs. [@1]
