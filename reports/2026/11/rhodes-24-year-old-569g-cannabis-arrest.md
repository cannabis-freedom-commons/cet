A 24-year-old Greek man was arrested in Rhodes on 19 November 2026 after a
search of his home and charged with possessing cannabis with intent to
distribute it, violence against officers and unlawful possession of weapons and
ammunition. [@1] The operation was carried out by the drug enforcement unit of
the Rhodes Sub-Directorate for Combating and Investigating Crimes. [@3]

Under the bed in the bedroom officers found two homemade plastic packages
holding 390 and 143 grams of unprocessed cannabis, along with an electronic
scale that weighs up to 10,000 grams. [@1] Two more packages weighing 18.2 and
18.4 grams and two precision scales were found in the same place. [@1] The total
gross weight of the cannabis was 569.6 grams. [@1] Police said the man was
systematically cultivating, processing and distributing quantities of cannabis.
[@2] Officers also seized a 20-centimetre sword, a blank-firing pistol with four
cartridges in its cylinder and 36 more blank cartridges. [@1] A mobile phone and
1,025 euros were found on the man. [@1]

The search took about 35 minutes in the presence of a judicial officer. [@1]
Police said he became argumentative, freed his left hand from the handcuffs and
tried to strike the officers with his hands and feet. [@1] His 27-year-old
brother gave a different account, saying that when one handcuff was removed all
three fell to the ground as officers tried to restrain him. [@1] The man did not
dispute that the seized substances were narcotic but refused to sign any
document, saying he would speak in court. [@1] On 20 November 2026 he was taken
to the prosecutor, who ordered a main investigation, and then to the
investigating judge, from whom he obtained a deadline to present his defence on
23 November. [@1]
