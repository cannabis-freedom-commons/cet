25 marijuana plants were found growing amid thorny bushes on Sabanakere, a
government-owned lake in a forested area near Shrimantagada village in
Shirahatti taluk, about 42 km from Gadag town. [@1]

The growers, who are yet to be identified, had installed a water tank, an
irrigation pump and a pipeline on the lake bund, and local residents said they
appeared to have visited the spot regularly to water and care for the plants.
[@1]

A team of police officials and the tahsildar visited the site, and the marijuana
plants and the equipment were seized. [@1]

A Shirahatti resident said the growers might have started the cultivation about
six months earlier, but it was not clear whether the space had been used for
ganja before. [@1]
