A 51-year-old man was detained in Lamego for possession of drugs after a
complaint reported alleged drug selling beside a school in the city. [@1] The
Lamego detention occurred the day before the 18 November 2026 publication,
placing it on 17 November 2026. [@1] After receiving the information, officers
of the Criminal Investigation Squad carried out inquiries at the site to confirm
the reported facts, according to the PSP Viseu District Command. [@1] When he
noticed the officers, the suspect tried to flee on foot but was intercepted
moments later. [@1]

After being stopped, the man admitted he was in possession of drugs. [@1]
Officers seized about 41 doses of hashish from him, along with a penknife and a
mobile phone. [@1]

The PSP presented the Lamego and Viseu detentions as part of inspection and
crime-fighting actions for drug possession and driving under the influence of
alcohol. [@1] In Viseu, officers separately detained a 51-year-old woman at
01:40 for driving under the influence of alcohol, a non-cannabis case not
counted in the cannabis figures. [@1]
