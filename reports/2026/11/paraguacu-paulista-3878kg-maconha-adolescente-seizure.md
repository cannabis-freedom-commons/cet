Civil police in Paraguaçu Paulista seized 3.878 kilograms of marijuana during an
anti-drug-trafficking operation on Tuesday 17 November 2026 and detained a
15-year-old girl who was in the house where the drugs were found. [@1] The
operation followed an anonymous tip that a residence in the municipality was
being used to store, distribute and sell drugs. [@1]

Investigators from the Paraguaçu Paulista police station went to the indicated
address and, during the check, saw a person behaving in a way they considered
suspicious, possibly linked to buying drugs. [@1] The person left quickly when
he noticed the officers and was not located. [@1] Officers entered the residence
and found 3.878 kilograms of marijuana, a precision scale, materials used to
portion and package drugs and a sum of money. [@1] The 15-year-old who was in
the residence at the time was taken to the police station along with the seized
materials. [@1]

After police procedures, the girl was handed over to her legal guardian, as
provided for under article 174 of the Statute of the Child and Adolescent (ECA).
[@1] The occurrence was recorded and referred for the appropriate legal
measures. [@1] The seizure formed part of the Civil Police's drug-trafficking
work in Paraguaçu Paulista and the surrounding region. [@1]
