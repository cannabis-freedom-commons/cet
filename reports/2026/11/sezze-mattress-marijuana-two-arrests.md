Carabinieri in Sezze arrested a 43-year-old man and an 18-year-old on the
morning of 18 November 2026, suspecting them of jointly possessing drugs for
sale. [@2,5,6] The 43-year-old was already under house arrest for drug offences and
the officers had come to his home for a routine compliance check. [@3,5,6]

Inside the apartment they saw marijuana residue on the living-room table and a
glass jar near the fireplace holding about three grams of the same substance.
[@1,5,6] The 18-year-old, found in a bedroom, had five grams of hashish and one gram
of marijuana. [@1,5,6] Under the mattress were about 75 grams of marijuana in 27
small bags the carabinieri believed were meant for sale. [@1,5,6] A backpack beside
the bed held material for cutting and packaging the doses, including a
vacuum-packing machine. [@3,5,6]

Both were arrested and placed under house arrest at their homes pending an
immediate trial, a giudizio direttissimo. [@4,5,6]
