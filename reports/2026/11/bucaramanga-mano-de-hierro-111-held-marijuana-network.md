Police detained 111 people in Operation Mano de Hierro, an offensive by the
Metropolitan Police of Bucaramanga against eight criminal structures operating
in the city and its metropolitan area. [@1] Of those detained, 45 were held
under court orders and 66 were caught in flagrante. [@1] Fifteen of the
detentions dismantled the Dominio structure, subordinate to Los Claverianos,
which investigators said sold about 96 kilograms of marijuana and 40 kilograms
of cocaine a month. [@1] In the Acapulco sector of Girón, officers captured
three more people with 1,695 doses of drugs described as cocaine base and
marijuana, plus a home-made firearm and dosing equipment. [@1]

More than 1,430 officers took part and carried out 36 search and raid
procedures. [@1] The sweep ran from midnight on 11 November into the early hours
of 12 November across northern Bucaramanga, with special units and air support.
[@2] Police said the targeted organisations were also investigated for homicide,
feminicide, kidnapping, extortion and theft, not only drug trafficking. [@1]

A presidency-linked balance put the same offensive at 136 captured and 70 raids,
with more than 18,837 grams of narcotics seized. [@3] The Metropolitan Police
commander said the results came from intelligence, criminal investigation and
deployment work against groups affecting public safety. [@1]
