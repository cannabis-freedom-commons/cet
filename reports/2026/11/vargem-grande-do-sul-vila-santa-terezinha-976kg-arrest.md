Military Police arrested a man in flagrante for drug trafficking on the night of
Tuesday 17 November 2026 in Vargem Grande do Sul, São Paulo. [@1] The occurrence
was recorded at about 21:10 on Rua Betina Mazuco Sipola in the Vila Santa
Terezinha neighbourhood. [@1] The team had received a tip that someone had
acquired a large quantity of maconha and passed it to another person to store.
[@1] The person keeping the drugs was to receive R$500 per month for the
service. [@1]

Officers spotted a man leaving a house in an attitude they considered suspicious
and stopped him. [@1] A search found nothing illicit on him, but officers found
R$47 in various notes. [@1] Questioned about the tip, the man confessed that he
was storing the drugs and said he had accepted the work because he needed money
to pay debts to loan sharks. [@1] He showed officers where the drugs were
hidden, and in a bedroom inside a backpack officers found nine bricks of
maconha, a smaller portion of the same drug and a precision scale. [@1] In
total, police seized 9.76 kilograms of maconha, R$47 in cash and a precision
scale. [@1]

The man was arrested in flagrante for drug trafficking, underwent a forensic
medical examination at the emergency unit and was then taken to the police
standby station in São João da Boa Vista. [@1] Duty delegate Rebeca Bravo de
Oliveira Gomes confirmed the flagrante arrest, and the man was sent to the
public jail where he remained at the disposal of the courts. [@1]
