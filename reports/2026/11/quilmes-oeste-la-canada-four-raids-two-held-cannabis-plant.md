Two men aged 28 and 32 were detained in the La Cañada area of Quilmes Oeste, in
Buenos Aires province, in four simultaneous searches. [@1] A judge issued the
four search warrants for homes in the La Cañada urban area during an
investigation into retail drug sales. [@1] Police identified the two main
suspects as Claudio S., 28, and Rodrigo G., 32. [@3]

In the homes officers seized more than 20 grams of marijuana buds and one
cannabis sativa plant. [@4] The same searches turned up more than 200 grams of
cocaine in wrappings, which is excluded from the cannabis figures. [@2] Officers
also took two precision scales used to weigh and prepare doses. [@2] They seized
a .45 ACP Ballester Molina pistol and a .38 SPL Rubí Extra revolver, both with
their serial numbers removed. [@3] Officers took two .45 magazines, 31 .45 ACP
rounds, 46 .38 SPL projectiles, two mobile phones and two handheld two-way
radios. [@3]

The Quilmes delegation of the Superintendencia de Investigaciones del Tráfico de
Drogas Ilícitas, the Buenos Aires provincial police drug-investigation unit,
carried out the raids. [@2] The case is before Functional Instruction Unit No.
20 of the Quilmes judicial district, headed by prosecutor María de los Ángeles
Attarian Mena with assistant prosecutor Leonardo Valli, and Guarantee Court No.
2 under judge Martín Nolfi. [@1] The prosecution validated the searches and kept
both men detained, formally charging them with possession of drugs with intent
to sell under Law 23,737. [@2] Both were taken to the courthouse to give a
statement before the investigating judge. [@1]
