Acting on a denunciation, military police officers went to a smallholding on the
GO-174 highway in rural Rio Verde at about 10:50 on the morning of Tuesday 24
November 2026 to check a reported marijuana plantation. [@1] At the address, the
officers approached the property's caretaker. [@1] The caretaker was arrested in
flagrante for drug trafficking, an offence under Article 33 of Brazil's Drugs
Law (Law 11,343/2006). [@1] He was taken with the seized plants, the drying
leaves and the seized cash to the 8th Regional Police Station, where the police
authority confirmed the flagrante arrest. [@1]

A search of the bedroom occupied by the suspect found marijuana leaves already
drying and R$348.35 in small change. [@1] A detailed sweep of the smallholding's
outside area found several marijuana plants growing at different points of the
grounds. [@1] When asked about the source of the money in his bedroom, the man
gave no plausible explanation. [@1]

The 8th Regional Police Station is a unit linked to the Civil Police of the
State of Goias. [@1]
