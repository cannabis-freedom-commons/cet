Dindigul district police said that so far this year a total of 386 cases have
been registered and 612 persons have been arrested in the district in connection
with the smuggling, hoarding and selling of ganja. [@1] The police said around
275 kg of ganja had been seized from the accused persons across the district
during the same period. [@1] They said 10 of the accused had been detained under
the Goondas Act. [@1]

In the latest case reported with the figures, a 46 year old man was arrested on
Wednesday in the Batlagundu police station limits in Dindigul district for the
possession of two kg of ganja. [@1] Following a tip-off that ganja was being
sold near Kattakamanpatti kanmai in the Batlagundu police station limit, the
police conducted intensive surveillance in the area. [@1]

They arrested M. Stalin of Palani Chettipatti in Theni district while he
attempted to flee the spot. [@1] The police seized two kg of ganja from him.
