Brigada Militar officers in São Leopoldo arrested two people on the night of 22
November 2026. [@1] The action was carried out by Força Tática officers of the
25th Military Police Battalion during an ostensive patrol in the municipality.
[@1]

During the patrol, officers spotted a speeding vehicle and suspicious movement
between two cars. [@1] The driver disobeyed an order to stop and fled, but was
caught. [@1] A search of the vehicle found more than 19 tablets of maconha
totalling 14.4 kg. [@1]

A woman was also arrested during the incident when she tried to take one of the
seized mobile phones. [@1] Officers also seized two mobile phones and the
vehicle used to transport the drug. [@1] Those involved were taken for medical
care and then presented at the pronta-atendimento police station for legal
procedures. [@1]
