National Civil Police officers arrested two people who were riding a motorcycle
in the village of Montecillo, in Cuilapa in the department of Santa Rosa, after
finding drugs in their possession. [@1] The two detained men were identified as
Alejandro N and Maynor N, and police gave both men's ages as 20 years old. [@1]
The arrests were reported by the National Civil Police, the Guatemalan
institution in charge of the security of Guatemalans, as a marijuana-possession
case in Cuilapa. [@1]

During the search, officers found 15 small bags containing marijuana in the
pair's possession. [@1] In the same search, officers also found a single fang of
cocaine alongside the marijuana. [@1] The two men were detained while travelling
together on the same motorcycle at Montecillo, and police linked the 15
marijuana bags directly to the pair. [@1] Montecillo is the village where the
motorcycle was stopped, Cuilapa is the place named in the police report, and
Santa Rosa is the department where both are located. [@1]
