Excise officers in Thalassery, in Kerala's Kannur district, arrested a man from
West Bengal and seized 1.720 kilograms of ganja from him. [@1] Officers said the
ganja had been brought to Thalassery to be handed over. [@1] Local reports named
him as Chabir Mulla, a native of Bengal. [@3]

The seizure was made by the roadside on the road leading from the Thalassery
railway station goods shed road towards the new bus stand. [@1] The search
followed a tip-off from the Kannur Excise Intelligence and Investigation Bureau
and was led by Thalassery Range Excise Inspector Jijin K T and his party. [@1]
The excise party included assistant excise inspectors, preventive officers and
civil excise officers, among them a woman civil excise officer. [@1] An
inspector of the Kannur intelligence bureau and two of its assistant excise
inspectors were also part of the party. [@1]

Excise registered a case under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances
(NDPS) Act. [@1] The Thalassery Judicial First Class Magistrate Court remanded
the accused to custody. [@2]
