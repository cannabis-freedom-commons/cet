Hasthampatti police in Salem were on patrol at Ana-medu the day before yesterday
when they detained two people standing suspiciously and searched them. [@1] The
search found the two in possession of 1.15 kg of ganja. [@1]

Questioning identified them as Suresh, 21, of Ambal Lake Road, Thathagapatti,
and Sundaram, 32, of Velu Street, Annadanapatti. [@1] The inquiry found they had
kept the ganja for sale. [@1] Police seized the ganja and arrested both men.
