A magistrate in Kimovsky district, Tula Oblast, gave a local man five days of
administrative arrest for using drugs without a doctor's prescription. [@2,3] The
ruling was made on 25 November 2026 by the magistrate of judicial precinct No.
81 of the Kimovsky judicial district. [@2] The joint press service of the Tula
Oblast courts reported the case. [@1,3]

The court found that on the evening of 9 November 2026 the man found wild
cannabis in a forest plantation and took it home. [@2,3] He dried and ground the
plant and then consumed it. [@2,3] Police stopped him near his home the next
morning, and he agreed to a medical examination and admitted using the drug.
[@2,3] Specialists at the Novomoskovsk branch of the Tula regional drug-treatment
dispensary confirmed drug intoxication. [@2,3]

He admitted his guilt at the hearing, and the court also ordered him to undergo
diagnostics at a medical institution. [@1,3] The ruling had not yet entered into
force and could be appealed. [@2,3]
