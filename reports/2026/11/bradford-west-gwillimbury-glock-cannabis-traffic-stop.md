South Simcoe Police arrested two men after a traffic stop in Bradford West
Gwillimbury. [@1] Officers stopped a car in the area of Line 5 and Coffey Road
in Bradford on 12 November 2026 at 3:25 a.m. after seeing it travelling too
fast. [@1] Both occupants were arrested and charged. [@1] The two men together
face 16 charges. [@1] A 21-year-old Bradford man was among those charged. [@1] A
20-year-old Vaughan man was among those charged. [@1]

Officers saw cannabis readily accessible inside the vehicle after pulling the
car over. [@1] Officers also found a loaded Glock .40-calibre handgun in the
vehicle. [@1] The firearm contained a magazine capable of holding 13 rounds of
ammunition, exceeding the legal capacity limit. [@1]

The Bradford man was charged with driving a vehicle with cannabis readily
available. [@1] The Vaughan man was charged with failure to comply with a
probation order. [@1] Both men were charged with possession of a weapon for a
dangerous purpose. [@1]
