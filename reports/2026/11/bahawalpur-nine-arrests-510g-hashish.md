Bahawalpur district police arrested nine suspects in separate operations over
two days, a police spokesperson said. [@1] The operations recovered 510 grams of
hashish, 200 grams of crystal methamphetamine, 95 litres of illicit liquor and
two illegal pistols. [@1]

Teams from the Cantt, Abbas Nagar and Khairpur Tamewali police stations arrested
three suspected drug traffickers and seized 200 grams of crystal
methamphetamine, known in Pakistan as ice, and 510 grams of hashish. [@1]

Teams from the Saddar Bahawalpur, Dera Nawab and Uch police stations arrested
four suspects allegedly involved in liquor trafficking and recovered 95 litres
of illicit liquor. [@1] City Hasilpur police arrested two suspects for allegedly
possessing illegal firearms and recovered two 30-bore pistols. [@1] Police said
investigation into the registered cases was underway. [@1]
