Excise police carried out checks against the illegal storage of ganja and
arrested a man found with the drug. [@1] Excise officers in Telangana took
Prashanth, a man from Madhya Pradesh, into custody in the Phase-1 Agricultural
Colony area of Patancheru after reliable information that he was illegally in
possession of ganja. [@1] The officers seized the 1.2 kg of dried ganja that he
was carrying. [@1]

Excise police registered a case over the incident, arrested the suspect and
produced him before a court in Sangareddy. [@1] He was then moved to judicial
remand on the court's orders. [@1]

Excise circle inspector R. Sravan Kumar said the department kept continuous
surveillance on the illegal transport, sale and use of ganja and would take
strict action against anyone involved. [@1] He asked the public to cooperate in
building a society free of ganja. [@1] The raid involved excise circle inspector
Sravan Kumar, sub-inspector Fayaz, head constable Sridhar and constables Nehru
and Suresh. [@1]
