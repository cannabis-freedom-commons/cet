Police carried out a control operation on 24 November 2026 in the Four-à-Chaux
quarter of Le Lamentin, Martinique. [@1] Sixteen officers, accompanied by two
dogs from the canine team, checked 25 people and two vehicles. [@1] The presence
of the dogs helped uncover several caches of narcotic products. [@1]

The officers seized 130 grams of cannabis resin and 133 grams of cannabis herb.
[@1] In the same search they seized 9 grams of crack, which is not counted in
the cannabis figures. [@1] The control was described as yielding several hundred
grams of narcotics that day. [@1]
