Perumanallur police arrested a man on Sunday 22 November 2026 on suspicion of
selling ganja in Kanakkampalayam, near Perumanallur in Tiruppur district. [@1]
The arrest came during a patrol on the road from Perumanallur to
Kanakkampalayam. [@1] Near Andipalayam, officers noticed a young man standing
with a bag and found him suspicious. [@1] They stopped him there and questioned
him about the bag. [@1]

The handbag he was carrying held cannabis that he was keeping for sale. [@1] He
was identified as Rakesh Nag, a 35-year-old native of Sambalpur in Odisha state.
[@1] He had been staying in the Andipalayam area and working at a banian
knitwear company. [@1]

Perumanallur police registered a case over the ganja sale and arrested him. [@1]
