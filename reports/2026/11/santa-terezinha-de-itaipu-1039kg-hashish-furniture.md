Federal highway police seized 10.39 kilograms of hashish hidden inside bedside
tables being carried on a truck on the BR-277 in Santa Terezinha de Itaipu,
western Paraná, on the night of 16 November 2026. [@1] The seizure took place at
about 20:00 during a routine inspection by the federal highway police. [@1]
During the inspection officers smelled marijuana coming from boxes containing
modular furniture. [@2] When the packages were opened they found that the frames
of some bedside tables had been tampered with and discovered packages of hashish
in internal compartments. [@1]

Once removed and weighed, the drug totalled 10.39 kilograms. [@1] The furniture
was to be delivered in Piracicaba in São Paulo state and in Palhoça in Santa
Catarina. [@3] The hashish was hidden in the structure of furniture shipped as
parcels to make it harder to identify in transit. [@1] The driver said he worked
for the transport company and knew nothing about the drugs in the load. [@1]

The Federal Police was notified once the drug was found. [@1] The drug, the
bedside tables used to hide it and the fiscal documents were seized and taken to
the Federal Police station in Foz do Iguaçu. [@1] The driver was released after
guidance from the Federal Police. [@1] The PRF did not disclose the exact origin
of the parcels or the identity of those responsible for sending the load. [@1]
The occurrence was passed to the Federal Police for legal procedures over the
transport of the drug. [@1]
