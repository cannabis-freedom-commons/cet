Belo Horizonte mayor Álvaro Damião published a video on 23 November 2026 showing
the approach to a man caught smoking cannabis in a public space in the capital.
[@1] He said he was from Paraíba and did not know that smoking cannabis on
public streets was prohibited. [@2] Damião called agents of the Municipal Civil
Guard (GCMBH), who arrested the man in flagrante. [@1] The video ends with the
man being searched by a municipal guard officer and the mayor stating that he
had been arrested. [@3] The encounter took place on a street in the Centro
district, where the mayor ordered the municipal guard to make the arrest. [@3]

The man also said the substance had been given to him. [@2] In the post caption
Damião said Belo Horizonte was 'not lawless' and that anyone who kept
disrespecting the rules would have to answer for their actions. [@1]

The arrest came after Municipal Law 12.102/2026 entered into force, setting a
R$1,500 fine for people caught carrying or consuming illegal drugs in public
places in Belo Horizonte. [@1] The law was published in the municipal gazette on
12 August after the city council overrode the mayor's veto. [@1] The penalty may
be suspended if a person voluntarily undergoes treatment for drug dependence.
[@1] In June 2024 Brazil's Supreme Court established that possession of up to 40
grams of cannabis or up to six female plants is presumed to be for personal use.
[@1] That ruling did not legalise consumption or possession of cannabis in
public places. [@1]
