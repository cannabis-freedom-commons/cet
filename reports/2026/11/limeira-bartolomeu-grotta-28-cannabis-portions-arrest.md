Military Police arrested a man in flagrante for drug trafficking on the morning
of Sunday 15 November 2026 on Avenida Claudemiro Roland in Jardim Residencial
Bartolomeu Grotta in Limeira, Sao Paulo. [@1] According to the police occurrence
report, officers on patrol saw the man walking toward their vehicle, then
suddenly change direction and appear nervous near a wooded area. [@1]

Officers noticed a bulge at his waistline and approached him, finding a bag
holding 112 eppendorfs of a substance identified as cocaine and 28 portions of
pressed maconha. [@1] The 112 cocaine tubes are noted only to explain the mixed
seizure and are excluded from the cannabis account, for which no weight was
published. [@1]

The man was arrested, taken to Limeira Santa Casa for a precautionary
examination, and then presented at the police station. [@1] Police said the
suspect confessed to drug trafficking, saying he acted because of financial
difficulties, including to give financial help to his daughter. [@1] The police
authority ratified the flagrante arrest for drug trafficking, set no bail, and
the man remained in custody. [@1]
