The Jabal al-Akhdar Security Directorate announced that it had detained one man
accused of using drugs and assaulting his wife. [@1] On Tuesday 17 November
2026, investigators at the Susa police station brought a man and his wife,
accompanied by their children, to the station after a complaint that the husband
had beaten his wife near the military-police gate at the entrance to Susa. [@1]
When questioned, the wife said her husband had beaten her and accused him of
using hashish and narcotic pills, and she was examined by the duty doctor for a
medical report on her injuries. [@1] On completion of the questioning, the
husband admitted beating his wife and confessed to using hashish and tramadol
pills. [@1]

The directorate said legal procedures were completed and the case papers were
referred to the Public Prosecution for jurisdiction and action. [@1] The other
substance in the same detention was tramadol pills, a non-cannabis drug
mentioned here only to explain the case, while only the hashish allegation is
counted as the cannabis component. [@1]
