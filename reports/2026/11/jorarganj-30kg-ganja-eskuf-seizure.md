Border Guard Bangladesh seized Indian narcotics and smuggled goods in drives on
the Feni and Chattogram border. [@1] On 14 November 2026, a special patrol team
from Alinagar BOP under Feni Battalion (4 BGB) carried out a drive in the
Jorarganj border area of Chattogram on confidential information. [@1] According
to BGB sources, the drive was carried out as part of regular patrolling by the
Alinagar BOP team. [@2]

The patrol recovered 30 kg of ganja (cannabis) and 83 bottles of Indian Eskuf
syrup in an ownerless state. [@1] No one involved in smuggling or drug dealing
could be detained in the operation. [@1] BGB put the combined seizure value of
the recovered narcotics at 138,200 taka. [@1]

Legal procedures were under way to hand the seized narcotics to the local police
station. [@1] Battalion commander Lieutenant Colonel Hasibul Islam said BGB
remains vigilant and active to curb drugs and border crime and ensure border
security. [@1]
