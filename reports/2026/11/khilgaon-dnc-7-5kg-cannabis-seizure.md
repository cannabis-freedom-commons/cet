The Department of Narcotics Control's Dhaka Metro (South) office said Khilgaon
Circle arrested Rina Begum, 30, during an operation in the Meradia Rajab Ali
Badsha Road area of Dhaka. [@1] Two other suspects named in the case, Sharmin
Akter, 31, and Md. Jinnatul Islam, 35, remained absconding. [@1]

Officers recovered 7.5 kilograms of cannabis in the Khilgaon Circle operation.
[@1] They also recovered 550 yaba tablets and Tk 10,500 in suspected drug
proceeds. [@1] The DNC said the wider drive also seized 200 grams of crystal
methamphetamine (Ice), 2,950 yaba tablets, 100 grams of yaba powder and 2 grams
of heroin. [@1]

The Khilgaon operation was one of several carried out by separate teams from the
DNC's Motijheel, Demra and Khilgaon Circles under Deputy Director Md. Anwar
Hossain. [@1] Motijheel Circle raided an apartment on the eighth floor of
Chairman Bhaban in South Kajla, Nayanagar, and arrested Md. Atikur Rahman, 32,
with 200 grams of crystal meth and 400 yaba tablets. [@1] Demra Circle arrested
Amulya Kumar Roy, 40, in front of Amin Cafe and Restaurant on Simpson Road in
Sadarghat with 2,000 yaba tablets and 100 grams of yaba powder. [@1] Another
operation in the North Mugda Para Jhilpar area under Mugda Police Station led to
the arrest of Md. Rohan, 20, with 2 grams of heroin. [@1] The DNC said separate
cases were filed against the arrested individuals under relevant sections of the
Narcotics Control Act. [@1]
