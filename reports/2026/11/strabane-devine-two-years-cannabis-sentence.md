Paul Devine, 20, of Railway Street, was sentenced at Dungannon Crown Court. [@1]
He was charged with being concerned in the supply and possession of cannabis and
ecstasy, possessing criminal cash, attempted criminal damage of a police car,
resisting arrest and driving without insurance. [@1] The offending took place
within a seven-month window between June and December 2025. [@1]

The offending began during a police operation in the Ballycoleman Estate on 13
June 2025, when Devine ran away after seeing police and dumped a bag over a
fence. [@1] The bag was recovered and contained eight bags of cannabis. [@1] A
search of his property found a further 21 grams of cannabis, numerous deal bags
and a further 118 grams, along with £3,300 in cash. [@1] Examination of his
phone showed he was involved in dealing a quantity of drugs over a social media
app. [@1] On 28 September police saw Devine on a scrambler motorbike on the
Strabane Rugby Pitches and, as they approached, he tried to make off but was
apprehended. [@1] He was searched and 15 deal bags of cannabis and one bag of
ecstasy were found along with cash and two phones. [@1] Devine resisted arrest
and kicked the window of the police car, and his phone yielded further evidence
of class A and B drug dealing. [@1]

On 28 November owners of an accommodation block reported that Devine had been
seen on CCTV supplying drugs to another resident, footage showing him hand a man
a cigarette box from which the man later took a bag of cannabis. [@1] Judge Mark
McGarrity said there was a high risk of Devine reoffending but said his place
was last in the supply line. [@1] The judge said he needed to mark the community
rejection of drug dealing and that the custody threshold was unavoidable, noting
Devine's repeat offending while on bail. [@1] Devine was sentenced to two years
in custody, half to be served in prison and half on licence. [@1] He was also
fined £100 for resisting police and disqualified from driving for six months for
not having insurance on his scrambler. [@1]
