Five men were detained after 21 simultaneous raids carried out on Friday in
Entre Ríos and Buenos Aires provinces. [@1,3] The raids took place in Gualeguay,
General Galarza, Gualeguaychú and Pueblo Belgrano in Entre Ríos and in Boulogne,
Buenos Aires province, between 6 am and 4 pm. [@1,3] The proceedings were part of
an investigation into suspected infringement of Argentina's National Narcotics
Law 23.737. [@1,3] The five detainees were placed at the disposal of the judicial
authority, and officials did not release their identities or alleged roles. [@1]

Officers seized almost four kilograms of marijuana, among wrappers and plant
material. [@1,3] They also reported more than one and a half kilograms of cocaine
in 26 wrappers, a quantity excluded from the cannabis figures. [@1,3] The money
seized amounted to 40,388,200 Argentine pesos and US$43,924. [@1,3] Police removed
three cars and a motorcycle. [@1,3] Officers also seized a .22-calibre revolver, a .22-calibre carbine and an air rifle converted to .22 calibre, along with magazines and ammunition. [@3] Officers also found precision and digital
scales, cutting tools and packaging materials, and objects with traces of
cocaine. [@1]

The investigation was run by the Dangerous Drugs Directorate through its
Gualeguay division, on orders from the Federal Court of Victoria headed by
Federico Ángel Martín. [@1] Some 51 mobile phones, two notebooks, a USB drive
and a SIM card were among the items seized for analysis. [@1,3] During the 21
raids, 54 people, 27 men and 27 women, were identified and remained subject to
the proceedings, with no further arrests reported. [@1,3] The raids involved
Dangerous Drugs staff from several departmental commands, special guards, the
Special Operations Company and the trained Infantry Guard. [@1] A second account
described the operation as a major anti-drugs strike in southern Entre Ríos and
independently reported the five arrests, nearly four kilos of marijuana, the
cocaine and both cash sums. [@2]
