Police in Honda, in northern Tolima, arrested a 23-year-old man in flagrante
during four search warrants. [@1] One of the searches was carried out at a
property in the barrio Obrero, where the man was detained. [@3] Officers found
36 grams of marijuana there together with plastic bags that police said were
used to pack and dose the drug for local distribution. [@2]

Units of the Seccional de Investigación Criminal (SIJIN), intelligence officers,
the Honda police station and troops of the National Army took part, with
operations starting at about 6 a.m. [@1] The detainee and the seized material
were placed at the disposal of the Fiscalía General de la Nación for alleged
trafficking, manufacturing or possession of narcotics. [@1] At the close of the
police report he was taking part in a virtual hearing before a judge who would
decide his legal situation, and he retained the presumption of innocence. [@1]
Tolima police commander Colonel Ferney Martín Romero said investigations and
controls would continue in the north of the department against local drug sales.
[@1] Tolima7Días, which did not name the other three locations, dated the
procedures to 24 November. [@3]
