On the evening of Saturday 14 November 2026, at around 19:40, officers of the
ADSU posted at Plaine-Corail airport in Rodrigues arrested two people. [@1] The
first is a 25-year-old services coordinator residing at Camp Pintade; the second
is a 27-year-old tele-agent living at Montagne Cabris Est. [@1] Police deployed at Plaine-Corail airport were watching the disembarkation of several targeted passengers before the two travellers were given a thorough check. [@3]

Both were stopped after officers found them in possession of one kilogram of
cannabis. [@1,2,3] Both kept silent when questioned after being informed of their rights. [@2] The cannabis was hidden in four pairs of Marina Rossi sandals, each packed in its own box, and a scanner showed suspect material under the soles. [@2] Acting on information, officers intercepted the pair at about 19:25 as they stood with their luggage and took them to ADSU premises for a search after they declined to give their identity. [@2] The two suspects were arrested and placed in cells. [@1,3]

The investigation is continuing. [@1,2] The anti-drug brigade confirmed the existence of a cannabis supply channel from Mauritius to Rodrigues. [@3] In a separate money-laundering procedure, Rs 725 found on one of the two was seized as suspected proceeds of drug trafficking. [@2] An investigator said traffickers bring in varieties available on the Mauritian market, mainly from Thailand, because cannabis grown locally has a low THC level. [@3]
