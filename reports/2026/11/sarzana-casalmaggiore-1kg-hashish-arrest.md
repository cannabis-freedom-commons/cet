A 23-year-old man from Casalmaggiore was arrested by Carabinieri during a road
check in Sarzana, in the province of La Spezia, on 20 November 2026. [@1] He was
driving along via del Corso, a street perpendicular to the Variante Aurelia,
when he was stopped. [@1] His nervousness before the officers prompted them to
extend the check into a search. [@1] The Carabinieri said the man was smoking a
hashish joint in his car at the roadside. [@2] The arrests came from a targeted
territory-control service against drug use and dealing. [@2]

A search of the car found 15 blocks of hashish in a backpack, weighing about one
kilogram in total. [@2] The quantity led to his immediate arrest. [@1] He spent
the night in the safety cell of the barracks. [@1] He appeared the next morning
before judge Mario De Bellis at the La Spezia tribunal, who validated the
arrest. [@1] His lawyer Serena Corsi obtained his release, and he was placed
under house arrest pending a trial set for 1 December. [@1]

The Carabinieri said the case remained at the preliminary-investigation stage
and that guilt would be established only by a final conviction. [@2] A separate
arrest in the same operation was made at Castelnuovo Magra, where a 23-year-old
was found with cocaine. [@3]
