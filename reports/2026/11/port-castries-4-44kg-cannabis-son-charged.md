Joshua Son, a resident of Boguis in Babonneau, was apprehended on 17 November at
Rodney Bay Marina while attempting to leave Saint Lucia for Martinique. [@1]
Police said Son was in possession of an American passport bearing his name at
the time of his arrest. [@1] Son was formally charged on 20 November with
importation of controlled drugs, possession of controlled drugs and intent to
supply controlled drugs. [@1] He appeared before the Magistrate’s Court and was
granted bail of $60 000 in cash or by suitable surety. [@1]

The case stems from a Customs inspection at Shed 7, Port Castries, on 10
November 2025. [@1] Customs officers found a brown box concealed at the bottom
of a barrel belonging to Son. [@1] A later examination found eight parcels
containing a green plant-like substance suspected to be cannabis. [@1] The drugs
weighed 4.44 kilogrammes and had an estimated street value of $88 800. [@1]

Police said warrants were issued for Son’s arrest on the three drug-related
offences. [@1] Officers spent approximately a year trying to locate Son before
his apprehension. [@1]
