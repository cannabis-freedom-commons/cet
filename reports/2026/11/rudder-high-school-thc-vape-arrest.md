A student from Rudder High School was arrested after being found with a THC vape
at the school. [@1] School resource officers were called to the assistant
principal’s office around 12:45pm on Monday for the bathroom incident. [@1]
Assistant Principal Davis told officers that 18-year-old Xochitl Moreno was the
student found in the girls’ Rotunda bathroom after marijuana was smelled coming
from the restroom. [@1]

Arrest records said Moreno had several earlier instances of being found with
vape products, specifically products containing THC. [@1] Those same records
noted that Moreno had no adult arrest record in Brazos County before this school
arrest. [@1]

Moreno was charged with possession of a controlled substance in a drug-free zone
over the THC vape found at the school. [@1] She was free on $10,000 bond after
being charged in the Rudder High School case. [@1] The vape products cited in
the arrest records were specifically described as THC products found on Moreno.
