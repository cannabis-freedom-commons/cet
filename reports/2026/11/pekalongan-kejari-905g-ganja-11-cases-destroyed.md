The Pekalongan regency prosecutor's office destroyed evidence from 11 general
criminal cases that had final and binding court decisions on 17 November 2026.
[@1] The destruction took place in the front yard of the district prosecutor's
office from 09.00 to 10.00, attended by about 25 people from the prosecution,
police, BNNK Batang and the health office. [@1] District prosecutor chief Conny
Novita Sahetapy Engel watched the destruction directly with her staff and
representatives of the related agencies. [@1]

The destroyed evidence came from 11 cases from the October to November 2026
period, ranging from crime tools, clothing and bags to pills and the narcotics
sabu and ganja. [@1] The ganja destroyed had a gross weight of 905 grams. [@1]
The same exercise also destroyed sabu packets of varying gross weights and
hundreds to thousands of pills including Yarindo, Hexymer, Dextromethorphan,
Tramadol and Alprazolam, which are not counted in the cannabis figure. [@1]

Asset-recovery and evidence-management section head Janu Widono said the
destruction was a routine exercise carried out in execution of court decisions.
[@1] Officers destroyed the evidence by burning, blending and damaging it with a
grinding wheel so it could not be reused. [@1] The activity closed with the
signing of minutes of the destruction as accountability for executing the court
decisions. [@1]
