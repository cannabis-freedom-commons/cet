Kediri district police in East Java recorded 25 drug cases with 27 suspects
under the national police's 100-day programme. [@1] The cases were uncovered
between 7 October and 18 November 2026. [@1] Fifteen cases were narcotics
offences with 17 suspects, and 10 involved okerbaya, a category of dangerous
medicines, with 10 suspects. [@1] Twenty-one of the 27 suspects were described
as dealers and six as users. [@1] Users who met the conditions were sent for
assessment and rehabilitation, while alleged dealers remained the target of
enforcement. [@1] Kediri police chief AKBP Muh. Wahyudin Latif presented the
results at a press conference at the district police headquarters on 19 November
2026. [@1]

Officers seized 1.26 grams of ganja during the period. [@1] The same haul
included 64.36 grams of methamphetamine and 5,720 LL pills, neither of which is
counted as cannabis. [@1] Supporting evidence listed for the drug cases included
21 mobile phones, eight digital scales, six glass pipes, four methamphetamine
pipes, packaging materials and 1,175,000 rupiah in cash. [@1] Police also took
434 bottles of illegal liquor, one jerrycan of ciu and two jerrycans of
bekonang, local spirits. [@1]

Kras, Kandangan, Ngasem and Pare were named as districts of particular concern
for narcotics and dangerous-drug sales. [@1] The district police ranked sixth
among East Java police units for drug cases uncovered. [@1] Latif said the
number of cases had risen from the previous year and that most people caught
were aged 20 to 40. [@1]
