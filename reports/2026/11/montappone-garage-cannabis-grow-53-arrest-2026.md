Carabinieri arrested a 53-year-old man at Montappone, in the province of Fermo,
on suspicion of cultivating cannabis and possessing it for sale. [@1] The search
was carried out by carabinieri of the Falerone station during an operation in
the Valtenna area. [@2] A Guardia di Finanza dog unit assisted the carabinieri.
[@1]

During a search of his home, officers found that the man had fitted out his
garage as an indoor greenhouse. [@1] They seized eleven dried cannabis plant
parts and eight whole plants. [@1] Officers also found some grams of hashish,
seeds and packaging material. [@1]

On the basis of that evidence the public prosecutor's office in Fermo issued a
pre-trial custody order. [@1] The carabinieri took the man to Fermo prison. [@1]
The prosecutor's office ordered the custody after weighing the evidence gathered
in the search. [@3] Investigations continue into a possible dealing activity and
the origin of the substances and materials seized. [@3]
