Officers from the Uşak Provincial Gendarmerie Command searched the home of a
suspect in the central district of Uşak, working under the coordination of the
Uşak Chief Public Prosecutor's Office. [@1] The gendarmerie had assessed the man
as involved in the manufacture and trade of drugs. [@1] He was detained and
referred to the courthouse on a charge of manufacturing and trading narcotic or
stimulant substances. [@1] A judge remanded him in custody and he was delivered
to a penal institution, the gendarmerie said. [@4]

The search produced 70 grams of esrar, the Turkish term for cannabis resin,
alongside 7,220 doses of paper-absorbed synthetic cannabinoid (bonzai) in two
pieces. [@1] Officers also found a cannabis grinder and a mobile phone at the
address. [@3] The gendarmerie said all the material found at the address was
confiscated. [@4]

The command said it was continuing work against people involved in drug
manufacturing and trade in Uşak. [@2]
