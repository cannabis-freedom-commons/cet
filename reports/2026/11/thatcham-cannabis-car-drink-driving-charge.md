Thames Valley Police officers found a man smoking cannabis in a car in Thatcham.
[@1] PCs Dollery and Jenkins spotted the man in the vehicle on the evening of
Friday, November 13. [@1] A search found that the man had also consumed alcohol.
[@1] He was arrested on suspicion of being in charge of a motor vehicle while
unfit through drink or drugs and possession of a Class B drug. [@1]

In custody he gave a breath test reading above the legal drink-drive limit. [@1]
He has since been charged and is due to appear in court. [@1] A Thames Valley
Police spokesman said the man blew over the drink-drive limit in custody and was
subsequently charged to court. [@1]
