Police in Hyderabad, Sindh, arrested two alleged drug suppliers in separate
incidents as part of a continued drive against drug peddlers. [@1] A City Police
Station team led by SHO Inspector Tahir Mughal arrested Shahbaz alias Kala
Pathan during patrolling and recovered 563 grams of marijuana from his
possession. [@1] In another drive the same team arrested Nasif alias Nasir
Arain, from whom police reported recovering a quantity of marijuana given in the
report as 1104 kilo gram, an unclear figure. [@1]

Police said marijuana was recovered from each man's possession. [@1] Police
registered a case against Shahbaz under the law the report names as the
substance of the Narcotics Control Act. [@1] Police registered a separate case
against Nasif under the relevant act. [@1]
