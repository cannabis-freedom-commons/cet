Border Guard Bangladesh detained 19-year-old Narayan Chandra in an anti-drug
operation in Fulbari, Kurigram. [@1] The operation took place at about 7:30am on
Wednesday 25 November in the Gorokomondol border area of the upazila. [@1] The
detained youth is the son of Bishwanath Chandra from Majhipara village in
Naodanga Union of the upazila. [@1] BGB filed a case against him at Fulbari
police station and handed him over to police with the seized drugs and
motorcycle. [@1]

A sudden checkpoint under the Gorokomondol border outpost stopped the suspect
and a search recovered 1.5 kg of Indian ganja and one motorcycle from him. [@1]
BGB put the estimated market value of the seized ganja and motorcycle at Tk
85,250. [@1]

The checkpoint was set up on intelligence information as part of the Lalmonirhat
Battalion (15 BGB)'s ongoing Operation Narcotics Strike. [@1] Battalion
commander Lieutenant Colonel Mehedi Imam said intelligence surveillance and
patrols have been intensified in sensitive border areas to protect young people
from drugs and anti-drug operations will continue. [@1] BGB said work is under
way to collect information to identify other members of the smuggling ring. [@1]
