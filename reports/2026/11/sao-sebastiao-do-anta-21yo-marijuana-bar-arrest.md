A 21-year-old man was arrested by the Military Police of Minas Gerais (PMMG) on
Friday 13 November 2026 in Córrego do Pronaf, in São Sebastião do Anta, eastern
Minas Gerais, on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@1] The 62nd Military Police
Battalion said a patrol team spotted the suspect's motorcycle and began
following it. [@1] During the pursuit, officers noticed that the driver
repeatedly touched the waist area, which led them to stop him. [@1]

In the personal search, officers found a bar of a substance resembling marijuana
hidden in the man's waist. [@1] Questioned during the stop, the man said there
were other illicit materials at his residence. [@2] Officers went to the home he
indicated and found two rocks of a substance resembling crack hidden inside a
sneaker. [@1] In all, the seizure was one bar of a marijuana-like substance, two
rocks of a crack-like substance, a mobile phone and the motorcycle the suspect
had been riding. [@1]

The man received voz de prisão and was taken to the Civil Police station with
the items for the legal procedures. [@1] The Military Police did not say whether
others were involved in the occurrence and published no details of the outcome
of the procedures at the police station. [@1]
