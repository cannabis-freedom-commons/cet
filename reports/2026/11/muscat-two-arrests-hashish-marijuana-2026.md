The Royal Oman Police said its Drug and Psychotropic Substances Control
Administration, led by Muscat Governorate police, arrested two people of Asian
nationality in Muscat. [@1] According to the police statement, the pair were in
possession of quantities of crystal methamphetamine, hashish, marijuana,
morphine and psychotropic tablets, and police said they intended to traffic in
the drugs. [@1]

In its own account of the operation on X, the Royal Oman Police listed hashish
and marijuana among the substances seized, alongside crystal methamphetamine,
morphine and psychotropic pills. [@2] Police said through their official account
that the two had dissolved part of the crystal methamphetamine to disguise and
smuggle it, placing the material in barrels and carrying it in a truck. [@1]

Police said legal measures were taken against the two suspects. [@1] The Royal
Oman Police post said the legal procedures against the two were still being
completed. [@2]
