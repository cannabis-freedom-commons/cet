Police in Taraganj, Rangpur arrested Md Sabbir Hossen, 29, also known as Sabbir
Sadhu, in an anti-drug operation, finding him with yaba tablets and ganja. [@1]
The operation was carried out around 11:20 pm on Wednesday 25 November on the
east side of the Shial Danga bridge at Ghoniram in Kursa union, Taraganj
upazila. [@1] Hossen was a resident of Puthia upazila in Rajshahi district and
worked during the day as a sales representative for a pesticide company in
Taraganj. [@1]

A police team led by sub-inspector Samsul Alam of Taraganj police station was on
routine night mobile duty and a drug-recovery drive when a tip-off came at about
11:05 pm near Beltoli Medical More that a man was selling drugs by the bridge.
[@1] Police said the man was detained after he tried to run off when officers
arrived. [@1] Officers searched him at about 11:30 pm in front of witnesses
using electric light and a digital weighing machine. [@1] From his right trouser
pocket they recovered two pink yaba tablets wrapped in white polythene, weighing
0.2 grams in total and valued by police at about 600 taka. [@1] From his left
pocket they seized 10 grams of dried ganja wrapped in old newspaper, which
police valued at about 200 taka. [@1]

Police said the suspect admitted to selling drugs during questioning. [@1]
Taraganj police station's officer in charge, Md Ejar Uddin, said a regular case,
number 13, was filed under the Narcotics Control Act and the suspect was sent to
court with the seized drugs. [@1] Local residents said they had been worried
about drug dealing around the bridge at night and were relieved by the police
operation. [@1]
