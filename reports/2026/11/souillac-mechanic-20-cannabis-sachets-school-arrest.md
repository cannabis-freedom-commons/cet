Anti-Drug and Smuggling Unit officers arrested a 20-year-old mechanic who lives
at Morcellement, Souillac, at about 9:30 on Thursday 12 November 2026. [@1] The
young man was stopped on Rue Lady Barkly in Souillac, directly in front of a
school. [@1]

Officers found him carrying 20 plastic sachets each containing cannabis, with a
combined weight of 13.68 grams. [@1] Police also seized Rs 1,500 in cash from
him. [@1]

Investigators then searched the young man's home, but the search found no other
illicit product or incriminating object. [@1] The investigation was continuing.
