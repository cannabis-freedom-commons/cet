Nabiganj police detained one man, Sanaullah, described as a drug trader, at a
special checkpoint in the Rustampur toll plaza area. [@1] The operation was led
by Sub-Inspector Jayanta Talukdar with accompanying force under the direction of
Nabiganj police station officer-in-charge Mohammad Abul Hossain. [@1] The
detained man is the son of Hanifa Mia from Sahebnagar village under Raipur
police station in Narsingdi district. [@1] Police said he admitted in
preliminary questioning that he had long been involved in the drug trade. [@1]

Police said a Nabiganj thana team was searching suspicious vehicles and
pedestrians in the Rustampur toll plaza area as part of routine law-and-order
and drug-seizure work. [@1] During the search, officers detained Sanaullah after
noticing suspicious movements. [@1] Officers then searched the sack he was
carrying and his body and recovered two kilograms of ganja. [@1]

Police said legal action has been taken against him under the Narcotics Control
Act and that he was being produced before the court with the seized drugs. [@1]
Police said such anti-drug operations and patrols by Nabiganj thana police would
continue in the future. [@1]
