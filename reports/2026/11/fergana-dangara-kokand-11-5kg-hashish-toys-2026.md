Police in Fergana region broke up a group suspected of distributing hashish in
several regions of Uzbekistan. [@1] Officers detained one suspect on 17 November
in Dangara district as he sold 11 kilograms of hashish. [@1] The hashish was in
a cache in a field, and the man had sent its coordinates to the buyer in
advance. [@1] A second man was detained in Baghdad district, where a search of
his home found 50 grams of hashish and electronic scales. [@1] Investigators
found he had bought the drug from a woman who was later stopped in Kokand. [@1]

In a bag of clothes carried by the woman, among children's toys, officers found
a package with 503 grams of hashish. [@1] In total officers seized more than
11.5 kilograms of hashish in the operation. [@1] The Interior Ministry said the
amount was enough for about 26,200 single doses. [@1] The Interior Ministry said
the Dangara suspect had trafficked drugs for years, receiving them from
accomplices in neighbouring republics and selling to clients in several regions.
[@2] Repost.uz, citing the ministry, said police had kept about 12 kilograms of
hashish from reaching users. [@2] The Interior Ministry described the detained
suspects as members of an international criminal group. [@2]

Criminal cases were opened under article 273, on drug offences with intent to
sell, and article 246, on smuggling. [@1] Repost.uz reported that the cases were
opened under part 5(a) of article 273 and part 2 of article 246 of the criminal
code. [@2]
