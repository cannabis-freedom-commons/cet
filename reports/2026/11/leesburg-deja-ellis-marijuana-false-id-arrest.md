Officers stopped 26-year-old Deja Shakya Ellis and a female passenger in a white
2015 Nissan at the intersection of U.S. Hwy. 441 and Michigan Avenue in Leesburg
at 12:15 a.m. on Tuesday, Nov. 17. [@1] An officer had seen the occupants get
out of the vehicle and switch positions before the stop. [@1] Officers observed
an open container of alcohol in the back seat directly behind where Ellis was
sitting in the front passenger seat. [@1] Both Ellis and the driver were then
detained for investigation. [@1] Ellis said she did not have identification with
her and identified herself as her sister. [@1] Dispatch then advised that the
name given had an active warrant out of Sumter County. [@1] The arrest report
noted that officers had initially believed Ellis was herself, wanted on a
Pinellas County warrant after successfully fleeing police. [@1] When questioned,
Ellis maintained that she was her sister and had never fled from officers. [@1]
She was subsequently arrested and transported to the Lake County Jail. [@1]

The patrol vehicle had no smell before the encounter but had an odor of
marijuana when the officer opened the trunk to secure his weapons. [@1] The
officer retrieved Ellis and found a small clear plastic baggie ripped open on
the floorboard directly under her. [@1] Small fresh green leafy buds of
marijuana lay in pieces of the plastic baggie and on the seat. [@1] The officer
asked Ellis what she had put in the patrol vehicle and believed she may have
swallowed something because she was coughing. [@1]

Ellis said the marijuana was not hers and that she had not swallowed anything.
[@1] Detention deputies searched her person and found nothing else. [@1] Jail
fingerprinting later revealed her true identity and confirmed the Pinellas
County warrant. [@1] Ellis was charged with possession of marijuana (less than
20 grams), giving false identification to a law enforcement officer and
tampering with evidence. [@1] She was booked at the Lake County Jail and
released after posting $6,000 bond. [@1]
