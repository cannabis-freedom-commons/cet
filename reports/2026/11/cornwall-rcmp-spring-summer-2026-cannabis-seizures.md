Between 1 April and 1 October 2026, the RCMP Border Integrity Team in Cornwall,
Ontario, and its regional partners laid multiple charges and seized contraband
and proceeds of crime valued at $452,940. [@1] The RCMP alleges several
individuals tried to smuggle unstamped cigarettes, nicotine pouches, dried
cannabis, cannabis edibles, cannabis plants and THC vape products. [@1] On 19
June 2026, the Cornwall Regional Task Force identified two vehicles believed to
be carrying illegal cannabis products and stopped and searched them under the
Excise Act, 2001. [@1] Officers found 63 cartons of unstamped edible cannabis
products, 1.5 kg of dried cannabis, five tins of nicotine pouches and $22,850 in
Canadian currency. [@1] Daryl Robson, 41, of Peterborough, Ontario, and Quinten
Round Point, 27, of Akwesasne, Ontario, were charged with possession of
unstamped cannabis products, possession of proceeds of crime, possession of
cannabis for the purpose of selling and importing and exporting cannabis. [@1]
The charges from that stop remain pending before the courts. [@1]

On 22 August 2026, CBSA officers searched a vehicle at the port of entry under
the Customs Act. [@1] They found 560 cannabis plants, 40 g of dried cannabis and
$8,000 in Canadian currency. [@1] The RCMP arrested the driver, Peter Mei, 58,
of Lake of Bays Township, Ontario, and charged him with possession of unstamped
cannabis, possession of more than four cannabis plants and possession for the
purpose of distribution. [@1]

Across the whole period the team reported 105.04 kg of dried cannabis. [@1] It
reported 547 cannabis plants. [@1] It also reported 78 packages of THC edibles.
[@1] It reported $30,850 in proceeds of crime. [@1] The RCMP put the total value
of all contraband and proceeds at $452,940. [@1] The Cornwall Regional Task
Force combines the RCMP, the Ontario Provincial Police, the Canada Border
Services Agency and the Ontario Ministry of Finance, and works with the
Akwesasne Mohawk Police Service and US partners. [@1]
