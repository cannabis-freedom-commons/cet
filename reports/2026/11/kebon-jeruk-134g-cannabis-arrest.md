Polsek Cengkareng uncovered an alleged cannabis distribution case at Jalan Ratu
Melati, Duri Kepa, Kebon Jeruk, West Jakarta. [@1] Officers arrested a
20-year-old youth identified as M with five brown-paper packs containing dried
cannabis leaves. [@1] Cengkareng police chief AKP Rahis Fadhillah said the
action followed residents' reports of suspected drug transactions in the area.
[@1] An operational narcotics team under investigator IPTU Lasaro Atmaja
monitored the location and made the arrest on Tuesday 10 November 2026. [@1]

The five packs together weighed 134.17 grams. [@1] Police also seized an orange
tote bag, a digital scale, a pack of brown wrapping paper, a mobile phone and a
black Honda PCX motorcycle. [@1]

The suspect was named a suspect for violating Article 114 paragraph (1)
subsidiary Article 111 paragraph (1) of Law Number 35 of 2009 on Narcotics. [@1]
