Ren Charles Ambrose, 41, of Orlando was driving westbound on State Road 44 near
County Road 231 in Wildwood at about 3:03 a.m. on Friday 20 November 2026 when a
Sumter County sheriff's deputy stopped him because his Chrysler 300 had
inoperable tag lights. [@1] The deputy smelled the odor of marijuana inside the
car and asked Ambrose about a blunt next to him in the centre console. [@1]
Ambrose first said he did not know what was in the blunt, then said it appeared
to be marijuana, and a dispatch check confirmed he did not have a valid medical
marijuana card. [@1] The stop was on State Road 44 just outside The Villages,
the Sumter County retirement community. [@1]

A probable cause search of the car found a plastic bag under the driver's seat
holding a small amount of ground-up marijuana, and both the blunt and the loose
substance field-tested positive for marijuana. [@1] Ambrose was arrested on a
misdemeanor charge of possession of marijuana of not more than 20 grams, booked
at the Sumter County Detention Center and released after posting $500 bond. [@1]
