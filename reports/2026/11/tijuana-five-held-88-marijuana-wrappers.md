Municipal officers of Tijuana's Secretaría de Seguridad y Protección Ciudadana
Municipal (SSPCM) detained five people for suspected crimes against health
during preventive surveillance patrols carried out on 14 November 2026 in
different parts of the city, seizing quantities of substances resembling
marijuana and cristal. [@2] In colonia Las Torres, officers detained Harry N,
37, after an administrative offence. [@2] During that stop officers found one
wrapper holding 10 cristal-like doses plus a knife of about 10 centimetres. [@2]
Police described that man as a suspected priority target for his possible
connection to 12 robberies at eight OXXO stores. [@2]

In colonia La Mesa, officers stopped Jesús N, 21, for an administrative offence
and found 30 wrappers of green, dry herb resembling marijuana. [@2] In colonia
Mariano Matamoros, officers stopped Isaías N, 39, for an administrative offence
and found 58 wrappers resembling marijuana. [@2] In Rancho Las Flores, officers
detained two men, both recorded as José N, aged 28 and 37, seizing two bags of
cristal-like substance with a combined weight of 1 kilo 600 grams. [@2]

Punto Norte places the Rancho Las Flores interception on the afternoon of
Saturday 14 November at rampa Orquídeas and avenida Laureles in the Playas de
Tijuana delegation. [@3] Punto Norte reports each bag held 800 grams of
metamphetamine, making 1 kilo 600 grams of the drug also known as cristal. [@3]
The five detainees were put at the disposal of the FGE for the corresponding
investigations and the determination of their legal situation. [@1]
