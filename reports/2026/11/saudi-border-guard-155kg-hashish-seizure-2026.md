Saudi Arabia's Border Guard patrols foiled several attempts to smuggle narcotic
substances and regulated medicines, acting to protect the borders and confront
smuggling operations. [@1] Border Guard officers arrested the smugglers involved
in those smuggling attempts. [@1] The force presented the interceptions as part
of its continuous efforts to guard the borders and counter smuggling. [@1]

The combined seizures comprised 155 kilograms of narcotic hashish, according to
the Border Guard account. [@1] Officers also seized 25,560 tablets of narcotic
amphetamine alongside the hashish. [@1] Officers also seized a further 110,800
tablets subject to medical-circulation controls alongside the hashish and
amphetamine tablets. [@1]

The Border Guard described the intercepted consignments as large quantities of
narcotics and regulated medicines. [@1] The Border Guard said the operations
form part of sustained efforts to secure border crossings, safeguard the
security of Saudi Arabia, and firmly confront smuggling attempts. [@1] The force
said its patrols remain engaged in continuous border-security work to block
narcotics and regulated medicines from being smuggled. [@1]
