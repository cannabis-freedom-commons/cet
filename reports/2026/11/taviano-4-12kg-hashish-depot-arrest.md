Carabinieri arrested 42-year-old local building contractor Simone Mauramati in
flagrante during an anti-drug operation between Taviano and Gallipoli. [@1] A
42-year-old man from Taviano was arrested on charges of possessing narcotics for
dealing and illegally holding ammunition. [@2] The carabinieri charged him with
possessing narcotic substances for the purpose of dealing. [@1] On a judicial
order he was placed under house arrest. [@1] Prosecutor Alessandro Protera
ordered the house arrest after the formalities were completed. [@2]

In a building-materials depot in his possession, carabinieri found and seized
4.120 kilograms of hashish divided into several blocks. [@1] The same depot also
held about 10 grams of marijuana and a cartridge belt with hunting ammunition.
[@2]

Officers from the local carabinieri station, the Casarano company mobile unit
and the Modugno dog unit carried out home and workplace searches linked to the
man. [@1] His 33-year-old cohabitant was referred on a separate charge of
possessing a counterfeit electronic identity card without its chip. [@1]
