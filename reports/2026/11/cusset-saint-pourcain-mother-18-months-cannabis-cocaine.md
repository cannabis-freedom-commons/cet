The criminal court in Cusset sentenced a 37-year-old mother from
Saint-Pourçain-sur-Sioule to 18 months in prison and a 41-year-old man from
Vichy to 12 months in prison for drug trafficking involving cannabis and
cocaine. [@1] The woman stored cannabis and cocaine at her home and resold them.
[@1]

Both defendants had been arrested on 10 November and were tried by immediate
appearance on Monday 16 November 2026. [@1] The inquiry began after a gathering
in rue Michelet in Vichy aroused police attention. [@1] On 10 October 2026
neighbours discussed repeated car-to-car transactions and had noted the
registration number of one of the cars. [@1]

Vichy police officers then investigated the owner of that vehicle, a 37-year-old
woman living in Saint-Pourçain-sur-Sioule. [@1] Surveillance and tailing
repeatedly showed a 41-year-old Vichy man in the woman's car. [@1]
