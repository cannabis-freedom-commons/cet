Criminal officers of the Wadowice county police headquarters searched a flat in
Kalwaria Zebrzydowska after operational intelligence indicated that drugs were
being kept there. [@3] The search took place on Tuesday 24 November 2026. [@1]
Officers detained a 29-year-old man and a 30-year-old woman in the flat. [@1]

The 29-year-old was charged at the Wadowice district prosecutor's office on
Wednesday 25 November with possession of significant quantities of narcotic
drugs and psychotropic substances. [@1] The same day the Wadowice court ordered
him held in pre-trial detention for three months. [@1] The 30-year-old woman was
questioned as a witness and released. [@1]

In the flat officers seized more than 970 grams of marijuana, more than 576
grams of mephedrone and more than 88 grams of methamphetamine. [@2] Police put
the total weight at over 1.6 kilograms and the black-market value of the whole
haul at about 90,000 złoty. [@1]

Under the Polish anti-drug law, possession of significant quantities of narcotic
drugs or psychotropic substances carries one to 10 years in prison. [@4]
