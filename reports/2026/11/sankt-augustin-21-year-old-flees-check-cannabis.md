On the evening of 13 November 2026 a 21-year-old driver in Sankt Augustin tried
to evade a police check, ignoring stop signals and driving on in his BMW. [@1]
Officers on Marie-Curie-Strasse turned to check him after seeing the oncoming
car turn into Einsteinstrasse with squealing tyres. [@1] During the pursuit he
repeatedly exceeded the speed limit, overtook other vehicles, ran a red light
and forced an oncoming driver to brake hard to avoid a collision. [@1]

After officers lost sight of him on Mendener Strasse, a witness who saw a car
stop on the pavement and the driver get out informed police. [@1] Officers met a
man at a nearby stop who matched the BMW driver, and the 21-year-old admitted he
had driven the car. [@1] The check showed he had no driving licence and he said
he had consumed cannabis, which a drug pre-test confirmed. [@1]

A doctor took a blood sample from the man and police seized the BMW. [@1] He
must now answer on suspicion of a prohibited motor vehicle race and for driving
without a licence and under the influence of drugs. [@1]

The Rhein-Sieg district police stated that the 21-year-old tried to evade the
check and endangered other road users during the flight. [@2]

The police statement confirms he said he had consumed cannabis and that officers
seized the BMW and had a blood sample taken. [@2]

The General-Anzeiger confirms the Sankt Augustin driver had no licence and had
consumed cannabis. [@3]
