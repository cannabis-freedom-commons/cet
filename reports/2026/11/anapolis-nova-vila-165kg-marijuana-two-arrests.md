Rotam officers stopped a pickup truck on the BR-153 in Anápolis after
intelligence work identified it as a vehicle suspected of carrying and
distributing drugs. [@2] The driver gave officers the address of a house in
Setor Nova Vila where he said he had left a quantity of drugs. [@2] At the house
police found a second man, who confirmed that he stored marijuana there in
exchange for a motorcycle and a sum of money. [@2]

With his permission the officers searched the property and found several boxes
containing about 165 kilograms of marijuana. [@2] Officers also seized three
precision scales, three mobile phones, a notebook with notes and the pickup
truck. [@2]

Both men were arrested and taken with the drug, the vehicle and the other
material to the Genarc headquarters in Anápolis for the legal procedures. [@2]
Police put the value of the seizure at about 500,000 reais, the loss caused to
organised crime. [@1] The operation was carried out by Rotam teams with the
Civil Police's Genarc unit in Anápolis, the integrated force FICCO-GO and the
COINT intelligence service. [@2]
