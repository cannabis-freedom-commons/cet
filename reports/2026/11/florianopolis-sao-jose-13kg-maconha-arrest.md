Santa Catarina military police arrested a man and seized 14.3 kilograms of drugs
and over R$59,000 in cash during an anti-trafficking operation in Greater
Florianópolis. [@1] The operation took place on Monday 16 November 2026 in
Florianópolis and São José, and the seized drugs included 13 kilograms of
marijuana and 1.3 kilograms of crack. [@1] Officers of the 22nd Military Police
Battalion acted on information that a vehicle was carrying drugs from São José
to the Chico Mendes community in continental Florianópolis, and stopped a Honda
HR-V near the viaduct giving access to that community. [@1]

During the vehicle search officers found crack portions ready for sale and cell
phones inside the car. [@1] Officers then went to a residence linked to the
suspect in São José, where they found a large quantity of marijuana and crack,
part of it already divided into portions and packed for sale, plus cash,
precision scales and drug-trade accounting notes. [@2] In total police listed 13
kilograms of marijuana, 1.3 kilograms of crack and R$59,286 in cash, plus three
precision scales, four cell phones, the Honda HR-V and trafficking records. [@1]

The detained man and all seized materials were taken to the Civil Police station
in the capital, which took over the case procedures. [@1] Jornal Razão named the
arrested man as Renato Barcellos de Souza, 34, a Florianópolis native detained
at the viaduct giving access to the community. [@3] Police described him as the
person responsible for trafficking around the Chico Mendes community. [@3]
According to the outlet, he had prior criminal records. [@3]
