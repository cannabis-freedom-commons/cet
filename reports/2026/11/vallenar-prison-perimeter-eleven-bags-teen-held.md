The Vallenar local prosecution office opened a formal investigation into a
suspect caught by Carabineros near the Vallenar prison carrying bags of drugs,
two pendrives and mobile-phone chips suspected to be destined for throwing into
the prison as “pelotazos”. [@1] Prison staff alerted Carabineros on Saturday at
about 10:00 to a person on land adjoining the prison, and SIP officers went to
the scene. [@1] Officers entered the plot with its owner's permission, found the
suspect hidden under a non-working vehicle, detained him and collected the drug
bags and other items for analysis. [@1]

In total he carried eleven plastic bags, some containing marijuana and others
coca paste, cocaine hydrochloride and ketamine, plus two mobile-company chips
and two pendrives. [@1] The 18-year-old detainee was told he is under
investigation for completed trespass for entering the plot without permission,
as well as for illicit drug trafficking. [@1]

Prosecutor Zárate requested and obtained preventive detention after the detainee
was deemed a danger to society, and the duty judge ordered him into the local
prison. [@1]
