Civil Police arrested a man in flagrante in Gravatai during an operation to
combat drug trafficking. [@1] He was taken to the police department and booked
in flagrante for drug trafficking. [@1] The action took place on Thursday 12
November 2026. [@1]

Inside a house in the Morada do Vale III neighbourhood, officers found 315
marijuana bricks totalling 270 kilos of the drug. [@1] The Civil Police account
gives the same count of 315 bricks totalling 270 kilos of marijuana in the
residence. [@2] Police said the investigation began after information that a
white Renault Duster was being used to transport and distribute marijuana at
different points in the city. [@1] Officers located the vehicle, monitored its
movements and saw it enter the residence in Morada do Vale III, where they then
searched the premises. [@1]

The seizure was carried out by officers of the 3rd Investigation Division of the
State Anti-Narcotics Department (Denarc), coordinated by delegate Fernando
Siqueira, within Operation Narke. [@1] The Civil Police account places the
flagrante arrest and seizure in Morada do Vale III in Gravatai under Operation
Narke. [@2]
