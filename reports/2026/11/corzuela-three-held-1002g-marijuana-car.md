Officers detained three adult men and seized more than a kilogram of marijuana
in a rural area of Corzuela on the late afternoon and evening of Tuesday 18
November 2026. [@1] The operation was carried out by officers of the División
Operaciones Drogas Interior Charata during a preventive vehicle check on a rural
road about one kilometre from the town centre, near the railway tracks. [@1] The
check took place on 18 November at about 19:30 on that rural road by the
railway. [@2] Officers stopped a white Toyota Etios carrying the three men as it
travelled from Corzuela toward National Route 89. [@2]

The driver said he had neither the vehicle registration nor a driving licence,
and after the occupants got out officers saw in plain view on the floor behind
the driver's seat a heat-sealed bag of brownish plant material. [@2] Field tests
and weighing established that the bag held marijuana buds weighing 1.002
kilograms. [@1] Officers also seized the car, two mobile phones and cash. [@1]

The Federal Prosecutor's Office of Presidencia Roque Sáenz Peña took charge of
the case and ordered the detention of the three occupants and the seizure of the
substance, the car and the other items. [@2] The case was opened as a suspected
breach of National Law 23.737 on narcotics, with the federal courts intervening.
[@1]
