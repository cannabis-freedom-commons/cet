A 45-year-old man was apprehended in the barrio Mosconi of Santiago del Estero
city on Friday 20 November 2026 after being stopped in the street with more than
30 doses of marijuana. [@1] The operation took place at about 13:50 at the
crossing of calles 15 and 17. [@1] Officers of Comisaría Comunitaria N° 4, part
of Departamental N° 2 Centro, were on preventive patrol when they noticed a man
carrying a white bag. [@1] Inside the bag they found 33 black nylon wrappers
holding a brownish-green substance. [@1]

Specialists from the Dirección General de Drogas Peligrosas ran field tests on
the material, which were positive for marijuana and gave a total weight of 46.71
grams. [@1] After the procedure at the scene he was taken to the police station
as a demorado, held for identification. [@1] Duty prosecutor Natalia Saavedra
ordered his formal apprehension and the preventive seizure of all the drug,
leaving the case at the investigation stage. [@1]
