Peru's anti-drug police directorate, Dirandro, detained four people during an
operation in Desaguadero, a border district in the Puno region. [@1] Three of
the detainees are Bolivian citizens and one is Peruvian. [@1] The operation was
carried out in a building in the district, in a space that would have functioned
as an alleged farm. [@1] There, officers found the illicit substance and seized
43 boxes containing packages of compressed marijuana. [@1]

Preliminary information described the detainees as suspected members of a
transnational criminal organization called Peruano-Boliche. [@1] Their
participation and responsibilities were to be determined during the
investigation. [@1] Dirandro is the Dirección Antidrogas of the Policía Nacional
del Perú, the national police force. [@1]
