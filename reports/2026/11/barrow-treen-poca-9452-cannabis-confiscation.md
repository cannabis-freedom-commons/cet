At Preston Crown Court on 20 November 2026, Daniel Treen was made the subject of
a confiscation order under the Proceeds of Crime Act. [@1] Her Honour Judge
Kirsty-Ann Gunn found that Treen received a total benefit of £312,826.75 from
his criminal activity of being concerned in the supply of cannabis between 1 May
2022 and 11 July 2023. [@1] Just £9,452.70 was available to him. [@1] Judge Gunn
ordered him to pay the full available amount within three months and set a
default prison term of six months if he did not pay before 19 February 2027.
[@1]

Treen, of Greengate Street, Barrow, was jailed for 27 months in May 2025 after
admitting counts of being concerned in the supply of cannabis and possessing
criminal property. [@1] Prosecutor Kiera Shaw told Preston Crown Court that the
then 35-year-old was caught with 664.53 grams of cannabis on 11 July 2023 after
officers executed a warrant at an address on Cloisters Avenue, Barrow, at
approximately 7:30am. [@1] During the search officers recovered a 5kg “Serious
Gainz” bucket from the kitchen containing 576g of cannabis, and a further 88.53g
was found in a wooden box. [@1] Two sets of digital scales and three mobile
phones were also seized, and officers said messages recovered from the phones
were consistent with the supply of cannabis. [@1]

A financial investigation found that Treen's account received 692 third-party
credits between 2 May 2022 and 2 May 2023, totalling £58,268.26, after removing
payments considered legitimate including benefits and company funds. [@1] In
police interview Treen said he received ESA and PIP worth about £1,500 a month
and smoked half an ounce of cannabis a day. [@1] Forfeiture and destruction of
the drugs and drug paraphernalia were sought as well as deprivation of a knuckle
duster and three mobile phones. [@2]
