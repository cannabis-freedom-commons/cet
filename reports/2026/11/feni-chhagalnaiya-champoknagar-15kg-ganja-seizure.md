A special patrol team of Champoknagar border outpost recovered 15 kg of Indian
ganja left ownerless in the Champoknagar area on the Chhagalnaiya border in Feni
on the morning of 16 November 2026. [@1] The team carried out the operation at
about 10:40 am on Monday 16 November in the Champoknagar area. [@1] No one was
detained with the cannabis because it was found in an ownerless condition. [@1]

Border Guard Bangladesh valued the seized ganja at Tk 52,500. [@1] In a separate
operation the previous night, a Jashpur border outpost team of Feni Battalion (4
BGB) seized 48 bottles of Indian liquor in the border area at about 8:50 pm on
15 November, acting on secret information. [@1] The seized liquor was valued at
Tk 72,000, putting the combined estimated market value of the liquor and ganja
at Tk 124,500. [@1]

BGB said work was under way to hand over the seized liquor and ganja to the
concerned police station for further legal action. [@1] The Feni Battalion (4
BGB) commander, Lieutenant Colonel Md. Hasibul Islam, confirmed the operations.
