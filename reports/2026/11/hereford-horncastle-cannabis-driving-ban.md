Daniel Horncastle, 26, of Hereford, pleaded guilty before magistrates to driving
a Volkswagen Polo on Bartonsham Road with cannabis in his blood over the legal
limit. [@1] Magistrates banned him from driving for 12 months and fined him
£450. [@1]

On 28 April 2026 police officers spotted Horncastle driving along Hampton Park
Road believing he was exceeding the 30mph speed limit. [@1] After stopping the
vehicle, officers became suspicious when they observed his pupils and carried
out a roadside drugs test. [@1] The test returned a positive result for
cannabis, leading to his arrest and transportation to a police station for
further investigation. [@1] Subsequent analysis found 5.3 micrograms of cannabis
per litre of blood in his system, more than double the legal limit. [@1]

The court was told Horncastle had no previous convictions and was regarded as
being of good character. [@1] The court heard he had been travelling to work
when he was stopped by police. [@1] His solicitor said he had been experiencing
difficult family emotional situations and had been using cannabis as a sleeping
aid. [@1] Horncastle fully cooperated with police and entered an early guilty
plea. [@1] In addition to the ban he was ordered to pay £85 prosecution costs
and a £180 surcharge. [@1]
