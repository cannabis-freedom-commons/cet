Five suspected drug and illicit cigarette dealers were arrested at Shongoane One
and Two outside Lephalale in Limpopo. [@1] The five suspects, aged between 19
and 70, were expected to appear before the Phalala Magistrate Court on 16
November 2026 on charges of dealing and possession of drugs and illicit
cigarettes. [@1] Police said investigations were continuing. [@1]

Police confiscated drugs and cartons of illicit cigarettes in the operation.
[@1] The suspects face charges of illegal possession of and dealing in drugs, as
well as illicit cigarettes. [@1] The operations were conducted between 12
November 2026 and 13 November 2026. [@1]

Police in the Waterberg District made the arrests in intelligence-driven
operations in the Villa Nora policing area targeting dealing in dagga, crystal
meth and illicit cigarettes. [@1] Dagga is the South African term for cannabis.
[@1]
