Mexico's Security Cabinet reported 20 detentions plus seizures of arms, drugs
and vehicles in operations carried out on 20-22 November 2026 in 11 states as
part of the National Security Strategy. [@1] In Monterrey, Nuevo León, a search
detained eight people and seized six kilos of marijuana along with electronic
cigarettes and various objects. [@1] In Nacajuca, Tabasco, six people were
captured and officers seized doses of cocaine, marijuana and methamphetamine.
[@1] In Michoacan, two men with provisional detention orders for extradition to
the United States were detained. [@1] In Sinaloa, arms were seized with two
detentions, while in Durango 27 vehicles were found, 11 of them with theft
reports. [@1]

In Queretaro, authorities seized 35 kilos of methamphetamine, four firearms, 180
cartridges, two vehicles and one property. [@1] In Tierra Blanca, Veracruz, Army
and National Guard personnel found 38 kilos of methamphetamine. [@1] The actions
also included operations in Guerrero, Nayarit and Sonora, plus another arms
seizure in Veracruz. [@1]

The Security Cabinet said these interventions are part of the strategy to
strengthen intelligence, investigation and coordination between federal and
state authorities. [@1]
