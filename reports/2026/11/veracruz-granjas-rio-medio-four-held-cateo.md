Four men were detained during a search of a home in the Granjas del Río Medio
neighbourhood of Veracruz city. [@1] The Veracruz state prosecutor's office
(FGE) carried out the search through its regional office and its 'investigative
trilogy', a team of prosecutors, forensic experts and ministerial police. [@1]
Navy (Semar), National Guard and Veracruz public security secretariat (SSP)
personnel backed the operation. [@2] The four were held in flagrancy, identified
by the prosecutor's office only as José Manuel 'N', Osvaldo 'N', Raymundo 'N'
and Uriel 'N'. [@1] Two of them, Raymundo 'N' and Uriel 'N', were suspected of
house burglaries recorded in the region. [@1]

A judge authorised the search, which formed part of the prosecutor's
investigation and field work. [@3] Officers seized bags of dried green herb with
characteristics similar to marijuana, to be added to the investigation file.
[@1] The prosecutor's office did not say whether the four men face other
allegations linked to criminal activity. [@2]

The prosecutor's office said investigations continue to establish legal
responsibilities and the origin of the seized items. [@1]
