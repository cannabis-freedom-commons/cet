Senafront, Panama's national border service, arrested seven people in Chiriquí
province in an operation named Bugaba Seguro, aimed at the presumed sale of
drugs. [@1] The actions were part of Plan Firmeza and were carried out in
coordination with the Specialised Prosecutor's Office for Drug-Related Offences.
[@1] The seven detainees and the evidence found during the proceedings were
placed at the disposal of the competent authorities. [@1]

Officers seized eight packets and eight handmade cigarettes containing a
substance presumed to be marijuana. [@1] The same operation yielded 18 packets
of white powder presumed to be cocaine, which are not counted in the cannabis
figures. [@1] Reten Chiriquí, a local news outlet, reported the same seven
detentions and the same 18 packets of suspected cocaine, eight packets and eight
handmade cigarettes of suspected marijuana. [@2]

Senafront described the operation as part of its work against microtrafficking
in Chiriquí, which it says it carries out with the Public Ministry. [@1] The
institution said it would keep staging operations to curb the sale of illegal
substances and reinforce security in communities. [@1] The identities of the
detainees and the specific places of the arrests were not released. [@1]
