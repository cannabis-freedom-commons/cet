On the afternoon of Saturday 14 November 2026 military police stopped a driver
carrying portions of drugs in a vehicle on highway SP-369 near Bairro Muricoca
in rural Pirassununga during an operation. [@1] The stop happened at about 13h55
during a blockade to inspect vehicles and drivers. [@1] During the search inside
the vehicle officers found portions of maconha and dry, described as a cannabis
concentrate, after initially finding no irregularities. [@1]

Officers seized 12 grams of maconha and 10 grams of dry, totalling 22 grams of
drugs. [@1] The driver was taken to the Distrito Policial of Pirassununga, the
local civil police station, for questioning. [@1]

At the station the case was registered as porte de drogas para consumo pessoal,
possession for personal use. [@1] After giving a statement to the police
authority the man was released. [@1] He underwent precautionary medical
examinations that found no injuries. [@1] The seized drugs remained held for
legal procedures. [@1] The occurrence was presented to the Policia Civil, the
civil police responsible for further measures. [@1]
