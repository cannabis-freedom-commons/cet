Federal highway police seized 490 kilograms of marijuana on the BR-386 in
Sarandi, in northern Rio Grande do Sul, on the afternoon of Thursday 26 November
2026. [@1] Officers ordered the driver of a car with Santa Catarina plates to
stop; he disobeyed and fled at high speed. [@1] After a pursuit the driver
abandoned the vehicle in a field beside the highway and ran away on foot. [@1]
Searches of the area did not locate the suspect. [@2]

Inside the car officers found hundreds of marijuana tablets hidden in the boot,
under the floor and on the back seat, covered by a black cloth. [@1] After
weighing, the drug totalled 490 kilograms of marijuana. [@1]

The vehicle and the drugs were seized and sent to the Civil Police in Sarandi,
which registered the case and is continuing the investigation. [@1]
