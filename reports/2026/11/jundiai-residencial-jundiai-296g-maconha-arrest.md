Guarda Municipal officers arrested a 22-year-old man in flagrante at about 11:50
on Friday 13 November 2026 in Parque Residencial Jundiaí, in Jundiaí. [@1] The
team was on preventive patrol and heading to UPA Novo Horizonte when it spotted
a group acting suspiciously on the avenue. [@1] One of the men carried a
backpack and a transparent plastic bag in which eppendorf-type microtubes and
portions of a greenish substance apparently marijuana could be seen. [@1] When
the patrol car stopped for the approach, the individuals fled on foot. [@1]
During the pursuit the suspect entered an alley, tripped and fell, and was
caught still holding the bag. [@1]

A personal search found the drugs and money in the backpack and, in the bag,
more marijuana portions and a cell phone. [@1] Officers seized 296 grams of
marijuana in 46 portions, alongside 44 grams of cocaine in 16 portions, 16 grams
of crack in 24 portions and 22 grams of another substance in 24 portions known
as K2. [@1] They also collected R$89 in cash, a Positivo-brand cell phone and
material with a Pix QR code. [@1]

Officers gave the order of arrest and read him his constitutional rights,
including the right to remain silent. [@1] Handcuffs were used because officers
feared another escape. [@1] Because of abrasions from the fall he was offered
medical care, which he refused. [@1] The case was taken to the Jundiaí Police
Station, where delegate Gabriela T. de Siqueira Peres confirmed the flagrante
arrest for drug trafficking under article 33 of Law 11.343/2006. [@1] Bail was
denied because it is a non-bailable crime. [@1] The detainee was sent to the
Campo Limpo Paulista screening centre, where he awaited the custody hearing.
[@1] An expert examination of the seized substances was requested. [@1]
