A suspected drug dealer was arrested after a short police pursuit ended with a
car stopping on a grass verge in a rural village near Ashford. [@1] The detained
man was 30 years old and from the local area. [@1] He was taken into police
custody and later released on bail while enquiries continue. [@1] No charges
have been announced. [@1]

Searches led officers to recover suspected cocaine, cannabis, deal bags, cash
and a mobile phone. [@1] The man was arrested on suspicion of possession with
intent to supply Class A and Class B drugs, as well as multiple driving
offences. [@1] The recovered substances were described by police only as
suspected drugs. [@1]

Kent Police officers patrolling the Romney Marsh area spotted a silver Peugeot
suspected of being linked to offences shortly after 2pm on Wednesday, 11
November. [@1] Officers signalled for the driver to stop before pursuing the
vehicle for a short distance. [@1] The vehicle came to a halt on a grass verge
in Ivychurch Road, near Brenzett. [@1] Kent Police confirmed details of the
arrest on Monday, 16 November. [@1]
