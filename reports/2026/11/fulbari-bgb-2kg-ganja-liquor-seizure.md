Border Guard Bangladesh ran two separate special operations on the Phulbari
border in Kurigram on the night of 15 November as part of its ongoing Operation
Narcotics Strike, acting on specific intelligence. [@1,2] At 8:35 pm a Balarhat
border outpost patrol seized 8 bottles of Indian Happy Gold liquor in the
Chowratari area. [@1,2] At 12:50 am a Gorkomondol border outpost patrol seized 2
kg of ganja in the Gorkomondol housing area. [@1,2]

The Lalmonirhat Battalion (15 BGB) confirmed the seizures in a press release on
16 November. [@1] BGB put the total seizure value of the drugs at Tk 19,000,
covering the liquor and the ganja together. [@1,2] No smugglers were detained; BGB
said it was collecting information to identify those involved and that legal
action was under way. [@1,2]
