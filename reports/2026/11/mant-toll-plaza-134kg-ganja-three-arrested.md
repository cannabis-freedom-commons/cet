The Anti-Narcotics Task Force's Agra zone and Mant police station carried out a
joint operation against drug trafficking in Mathura, Uttar Pradesh. [@1] The
operation took place on the Yamuna Expressway on 23 November 2026 after ANTF's
Agra zone received a tip-off about a large ganja consignment in two cars. [@1]
Police arrested three men at the scene: Anil Kumar alias Chhote, Sachin Devidas
Taywade and Somesh Sahebrao Manekar. [@1]

Officers stopped a Volkswagen and a Hyundai i20 before the Mant toll plaza and
found 67 packets holding about two kilograms of ganja each, or 134 kg in total.
[@1] Police put the estimated value of the ganja at about 70 lakh rupees. [@1]
Officers also seized four mobile phones, two debit cards and 3,200 rupees in
cash. [@1] A second account confirmed that ANTF Agra Zone and Mant police
intercepted the cars near the toll plaza after a tip-off. [@2]

During initial questioning the men said the ganja came from a person named Anna
in Rajahmundry, Andhra Pradesh, and was to be delivered in Delhi. [@1] Anil
Kumar drove the car carrying the ganja while Somesh Sahebrao Manekar went ahead
in the Hyundai i20 to watch for police and send location updates. [@1] Police
said Somesh brought in a man from his village, Sachin, promising him 25,000
rupees and giving him two mobile phones. [@1] A second account said the accused
told investigators the consignment came from a person identified as Anna in
Rajahmundry and was bound for Delhi. [@2] Police said they were tracing Anna and
the person meant to receive the consignment in Delhi, along with others
involved. [@1] A second account said police were still trying to trace Anna and
identify the network expected to receive the cannabis in Delhi. [@2]
