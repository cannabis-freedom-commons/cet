The checks took place on 16 November 2026. [@1] At about 09:50 in Dobrich,
officers checked a 43-year-old man and found dry plant mass that reacted to
cannabis. [@1] The regional directorate of Bulgaria's Interior Ministry said the
men were detained for up to 24 hours. [@1] Expedited police proceedings, a
fast-track procedure, were opened in the cases. [@1]

The operation was reported as a single set of checks in which cannabis,
amphetamine and methamphetamine were seized. [@1] In Kavarna at about 09:30, a
check of a 33-year-old man found a crystalline substance reacting to
amphetamine. [@1] At about 13:50 in Dobrich, a check of a 36-year-old man found
a crystalline substance reacting to methamphetamine. [@1]
