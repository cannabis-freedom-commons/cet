Military police arrested a 32-year-old man in Bodocongó, Campina Grande, on the
night of Saturday 14 November 2026. [@1] The action was carried out by the
Polícia Militar. [@1] The occurrence was recorded during Operação Cidade Segura.
[@1]

Officers were patrolling the area when they saw two individuals acting
suspiciously. [@1] The two men separated when they noticed the patrol approach.
[@1] One of them threw an object and tried to flee but was caught and approached
by military police. [@1] During a check of the discarded material, police found
a bag containing substances similar to drugs. [@1] Police seized eight wraps of
a marijuana-like substance, 18 crack rocks and five wraps of a crack-like
substance, 37 containers of a cocaine-like substance and a mobile phone. [@1]

The man was given voice of arrest and taken to the Polícia Civil station, where
the legal procedures were adopted. [@1]
