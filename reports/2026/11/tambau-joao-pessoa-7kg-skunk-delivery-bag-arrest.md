Military Police in Tambaú, a neighbourhood of João Pessoa in Paraíba, arrested a
man suspected of drug trafficking after finding him with more than seven
kilograms of skunk cannabis. [@1,2] The arrest took place on Monday, 16 November 2026. [@2] The stop followed a report from residents
about suspicious movement in the area. [@1,2] Officers located the man as he rode
a motorcycle and carried a bag similar to those used by food-delivery app
workers. [@1,2]

During the check, police found the drug packed inside the bag. [@1] The seized
material totalled more than seven kilograms of skunk cannabis, a variety
described as having a high concentration of psychoactive substances. [@1,2]

Questioned about the drug, the man said he had been hired only to transport the
package, an account that authorities responsible for the investigation will
analyse. [@1,2] The suspect said he had no previous police record. [@2] He was detained and the seized material was placed under the
authorities' responsibility for legal procedures. [@1] Investigators are to
establish the drug's origin, the delivery destination and whether other people
were involved. [@1] The suspect's identity and any other people involved had not
been disclosed at the time of publication. [@1]
