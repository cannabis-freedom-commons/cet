Police were called to a fire on Branch Road at about 9.59pm on Saturday 14
November 2026 after the fire service raised the alarm. [@1] Investigators
believe the fire started because of a suspected cannabis farm. [@1] A
32-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of producing controlled drugs. [@1] He
was released on bail while enquiries continue. [@1]

The fire badly damaged the My Ngan Chinese takeaway on Branch Road in Wortley on
Saturday evening. [@2] Large numbers of police, fire and ambulance crews
attended the scene. [@2] No casualties were reported, but nearby properties
suffered smoke damage and some residents had to leave their homes. [@2] The
takeaway was later boarded up. [@2]

A neighbour said people thought the fryer had caused the fire. [@2] Commenters
on the community post disputed the cannabis account and said the fire department
had called it a cooking fire. [@1]
