Police in the Limburg Regio Hoofdstad (LRH) police zone, the local force
covering Hasselt, said they held a proactive ANPR (automatic number-plate
recognition) operation on Wednesday. [@1] In Kuringen, a village in the Hasselt
municipality, officers found a driver who was in possession of cannabis. [@1]
The driver also delivered a positive saliva test and did not have a driving
licence on him, police said. [@1]

From that same driver, officers seized more than 1,000 euros in cash, according
to the police account. [@1]

In the same operation, a driver in Runkst also returned a positive saliva test.
[@1] Police issued four on-the-spot fines for expired vehicle inspections and
for failing to wear a seatbelt or helmet. [@1] They also drew up three official
reports covering provisional licences, entrusting a vehicle and excessive speed.
[@1] A driver who used a phone at the wheel had to hand over his licence. [@1]
