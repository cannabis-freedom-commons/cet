A 29-year-old man who worked as a window cleaner was detained by patrol officers
near Pasaje Castro and the pedestrian street Absalón Rojas in downtown Santiago
del Estero. [@1] Diario Panorama reported the same 29-year-old window cleaner
detained in the city centre after officers found wrappers of cocaine and
marijuana among his belongings. [@2] Info del Estero reported that police
spotted the suspect at Pasaje Castro and the pedestrian Absalón Rojas and
identified him. [@3]

Officers found several wrappers of suspicious substances and asked the General
Directorate of Dangerous Drugs to run field tests. [@1] The tests and weighing
established four wrappers of cocaine weighing 1.07 grams in total and two
wrappers of marijuana weighing 1.85 grams in total. [@1] Info del Estero
likewise reported 1.85 grams of marijuana, but said the 1.07 grams of cocaine
was in two plastic wrappers. [@3]

The prosecutor in the case ordered the substances seized and the man held as an
aprehendido, a detainee at the disposal of the courts. [@1]
