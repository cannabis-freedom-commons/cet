Municipal preventive police detained two men in the invasión Altares, in the
south of Hermosillo, after they allegedly assaulted officers during a police
intervention. [@1] The men were identified as Rodrigo Manuel 'N', aged 40, and
Rubén Gadiel 'N', aged 30, who were intercepted at 17:15 on Sunday at the
crossing of calles 19 and López Riesgo. [@1] Officers had signalled them to stop
for a check after they behaved evasively on seeing the patrol. [@2] The two men
reacted aggressively toward the preventive officers, throwing kicks and punches
as the officers tried to work, before the officers secured and controlled the
scene. [@1]

During the search officers found eight units of green, dry herb, apparently
marijuana, among their belongings, which were secured. [@1] Meganoticias, citing
the police report, described the seizure as eight wrappers holding marijuana
portions that were ready for sale. [@2]

The two men, who resisted arrest by throwing slaps and kicks, were placed at the
disposal of the investigating authority to answer for delitos contra la salud
and related liability. [@2] N+ reported they were placed at the disposal of the
corresponding authorities for the investigations and legal procedures that will
determine their legal situation. [@1]
