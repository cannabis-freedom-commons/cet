A man was detained in a narcomenudeo, or retail drug dealing, search of a home
in San Antonio on Isla Apipé Grande, Corrientes. [@1] The search was carried out
by Prefectura Naval Argentina, Argentina's naval prefecture. [@2] It followed a
judicial investigation that included observation, surveillance and analysis of
information by the force's Delegación Inteligencia Criminal e Investigaciones.
[@2] The operation was ordered by the Juzgado de Garantías under substitute
judge Claudio Darío Garay. [@2]

Officers seized cocaine, marijuana and cannabis sativa seedlings, along with
cash in Argentine pesos and guaraníes. [@1] They also seized three mobile
phones, a pendrive, eight phone chips, a precision scale, two motorcycles, a
modified rifle and ammunition. [@1] The rifle was an air rifle modified for .22
calibre, with ten cartridges of that calibre. [@2] Authorities valued all the
seized items at 9,746,877 pesos. [@1]

The detainee was taken to the Comisaría Primera in Ituzaingó and remained held
there. [@1] The seized items were secured and left at the disposal of the court
handling the case. [@2]
