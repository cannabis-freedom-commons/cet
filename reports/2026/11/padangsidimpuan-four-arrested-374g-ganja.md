Padangsidimpuan's narcotics investigation unit arrested four men suspected of
dealing ganja at three locations in the city. [@1] The arrests were made on
Tuesday 17 November 2026. [@2] The four were identified as IMS (51), HS (35),
IAS (37) and INS (53). [@2] Police said three of them, HS, IAS and INS, were
repeat offenders. [@1]

The case began with a public tip about a suspected ganja transaction in a cocoa
plantation in Desa Partihaman Saroha, Kelurahan Sirappak, Padangsidimpuan
Hutaimbaru. [@2] Around 11:00 WIB officers arrested IMS in the plantation with
53.69 grams of ganja in a red plastic bag and a white Oppo phone. [@2] Under
initial questioning IMS said he obtained the ganja from HS. [@1] Police arrested
HS around 12:00 WIB in Desa Partihaman Saroha and seized 27.76 grams from him,
along with a phone and a trail motorcycle without a number plate. [@2] The
investigation then led to the house of IAS in Kelurahan Losung Batu,
Padangsidimpuan Utara, where officers secured IAS and INS around 14:00 WIB. [@1]
At that house police found 291.03 grams of ganja in a black plastic bag in the
kitchen and a 0.06-gram packet in front of INS, together with phones, a black
bag and a scale. [@2]

Police gave the total weight of the seized ganja as 374.45 grams. [@1] Another
account reported the seized total as 372.54 grams. [@3] Narcotics chief AKP Juli
Purwono said the arrests followed a public tip and came over about three hours.
[@3] All four suspects and the seized evidence were taken to the Padangsidimpuan
police station for further legal processing. [@1] Purwono urged residents to
report suspected drug activity at a nearby police station or through the 110
call centre. [@1]
