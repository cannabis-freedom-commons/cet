Three suspects found in the flat were arrested at the scene and taken to Acre
police station for questioning. [@2] Detectives from Acre police station in the
'Asher' area uncovered a large hydroponic drug-growing lab in an apartment in
the city. [@2]

During a covert operation officers raided the apartment and found an active,
sophisticated drug lab spread over three growing rooms. [@1] Police said the
rooms held about 246 plants and material suspected of being cannabis with a
total weight of about 47 kg. [@2] Investigators found that an illegal electrical
connection and stolen electricity had been used to run the lab. [@1] Officers
also seized M-16 rifle parts, including assemblies suspected of having been
stolen from the Israel Defense Forces, from one suspect's car. [@2]

All the items seized were transferred to Israel Police forensic identification
laboratories for evidence processing. [@2] Israel Police said it would keep
acting to uncover drug labs and stop military equipment reaching criminal
groups. [@1]
