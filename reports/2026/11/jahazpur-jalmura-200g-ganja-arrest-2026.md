Jahazpur police arrested a 50-year-old man and seized 200 grams of ganja from
him at Jalmura crossing in Jahazpur, Bhilwara district, Rajasthan. [@1] Police
said the ganja was being sold under the cover of a gutkha and bidi shop. [@1]

The arrest was made on suspicion at the crossing during a patrol on the night of
20 November 2026. [@1] Officers on the patrol were led by Jahazpur station
in-charge Dayaram Chaudhary. [@2] The arrested man was Baboolal, 50, son of
Narayan Keer, a resident of Keer Mohalla in Jahazpur. [@1]

Police arrested him after the ganja was recovered and registered a case under
the NDPS Act. [@2] Further investigation of the case was handed to the in-charge
of Pander police station. [@1] The team with the station in-charge included head
constable Kasiram and constables Yogesh and Mukesh. [@1] The second account
states that the case was registered under the NDPS Act and sent for
investigation. [@2]
