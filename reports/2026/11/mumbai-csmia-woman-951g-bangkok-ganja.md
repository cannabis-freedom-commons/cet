Customs officers arrested a woman arriving from Bangkok at Mumbai's Chhatrapati
Shivaji Maharaj International Airport and seized ganja worth an estimated 33.25
lakh rupees. [@1] The Air Intelligence Unit (AIU) of Mumbai Customs identified
the woman as Radhika Selvam, 36. [@1] Selvam, a resident of Karimedu in Tamil
Nadu, had a case registered against her under the NDPS Act. [@1] She was later
remanded in judicial custody, the public prosecutor said. [@2]

Selvam arrived on a Thai Airways flight early on Sunday morning. [@1] Officers
say she was intercepted on the basis of spot profiling after arriving at the
airport from Bangkok. [@2] A personal search found nothing, but officers spotted
two packets hidden in her handbag. [@1] Officers described the packets as
containing fruiting and flowering tops of the cannabis plant. [@2] A field
testing kit indicated the substance was hydroponic ganja. [@1] Officers seized
951 grams of the high-quality ganja, which an officer valued at about 33.25 lakh
rupees (3,325,000 rupees). [@1]

Preliminary questioning found that Selvam knew the packets contained ganja but
had become involved in trafficking for quick money. [@1] Her recorded statement
admitted that she knew the strict punishment but was drawn by the promise of
quick money. [@2] Customs said the investigation was at an early stage and other
people involved had yet to be traced. [@2] Investigators were trying to
establish who she was supposed to deliver the packets to. [@1]
