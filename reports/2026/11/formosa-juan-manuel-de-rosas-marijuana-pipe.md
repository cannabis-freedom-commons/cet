The cannabis-related intervention took place at about 02:40 on Saturday during a
preventive patrol by the Comisaría Seccional Cuarta in the Juan Manuel de Rosas
neighbourhood of Formosa, the provincial capital. [@2] Officers identified a man
carrying packages of cocaine and marijuana along with various items including a
homemade pipe. [@1] Narcotics personnel ran orientation tests that were positive
for cocaine hydrochloride and for tetrahydrocannabinol, the active principle of
cannabis. [@1]

In both interventions the substances were seized and the subjects were notified
of their legal situation and placed at the disposal of the Juzgado de
Instrucción y Correccional Contra el Narcocrimen, the investigating court
against narcotics crime. [@3] The other intervention took place on the preceding
Wednesday night near the bus terminal on Avenida Gobernador Luis Gutnisky and
Antártida Argentina. [@1] The police statement says the two interventions
together resulted in two men being apprehended and cocaine and marijuana being
seized in Formosa city. [@1]
