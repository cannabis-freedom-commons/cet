Military police arrested a man and a woman in Faxinal on the night of 21
November 2026 after a pursuit on the rural road to Faxinalzinho. [@1] A ROTAM
patrol spotted a blue Fiat Uno driven by a man already facing drug-trafficking
complaints and ordered it to stop. [@1] When ordered to stop, the driver fled
and drove the vehicle at two police officers. [@1] Officers fired to stop the
attack, and the driver continued at high speed on two flat tyres before
abandoning the car and entering woods with the passenger. [@1] Police later
found the woman at the couple's home, where she resisted, injured an officer and
was arrested. [@1] The man was later found on the roof of his mother's house in
the Mutirão neighbourhood and arrested. [@1]

Officers found a 369-gram tablet of a substance described as similar to maconha
in the abandoned car. [@1] At the house they seized another 381 grams of
maconha, a precision scale, a tray, a knife with drug residue and three mobile
phones. [@1] In total the operation seized 750 grams of maconha. [@1]

Both detainees were taken for bodily-injury examinations and then to the 53rd
Regional Civil Police Station with the seized material. [@1]
