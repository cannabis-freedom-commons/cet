A 34-year-old man was sentenced by the Quimper court on 20 November 2026 for
driving without a licence, driving under the influence of drugs, and selling and
transporting narcotics. [@1] He was tried in immediate appearance, the
fast-track procedure for defendants brought straight to court. [@1] Before the
court he said he was very afraid of prison because of the drugs circulating
inside. [@1]

On 27 October 2026 gendarmes from the Quimperlé barracks spotted a car with a
faulty rear light and stopped it. [@1] The driver tested positive for cocaine
and no longer had a licence. [@1] In the car, which belongs to his brother,
officers found drugs and 1,250 euros in cash. [@1] He later said he was in the
middle of delivering cocaine, heroin and cannabis after answering an
advertisement on a social network. [@1] Several grams of those substances and of
ketamine were also found at his home. [@1]
