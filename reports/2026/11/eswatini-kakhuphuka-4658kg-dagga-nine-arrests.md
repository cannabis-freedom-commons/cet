A routine police operation to find a suspect wanted in a domestic violence case
ended with the discovery of 465.8 kg of dagga, the local name for cannabis, at
kaKhuphuka in Eswatini. [@1] Royal Eswatini Police Service officers from Mliba
Police Station made the discovery at about 12:30pm on 16 November 2026. [@1] The
officers came across the dagga inside a house and in its yard. [@1] Police said
the nine suspects were aged 25, 26, 28, 33, 36, 42, 46, 63 and 64 and were found
in possession of the dagga without a valid licence or permit. [@1]

All nine were arrested for allegedly contravening the Pharmacy Act. [@1] They
appeared before the Manzini Magistrate's Court on Tuesday, 17 November 2026.
[@1]

According to the police report, only the 36-year-old woman was convicted and
ordered to pay a fine of E7,000. [@1] She subsequently paid the fine. [@1] The
police statement did not explain why the remaining eight suspects were not
convicted or whether the charges against them were withdrawn, dismissed or
remained pending. [@1] It was also not immediately clear whether the convicted
woman had admitted ownership of the dagga or whether the court had established
that she was solely responsible for its possession. [@1]
