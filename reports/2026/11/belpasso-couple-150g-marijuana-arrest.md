State Police in Catania arrested a 53-year-old man and his 49-year-old wife,
both from the city, who had nearly 150 grams of marijuana at their home in
Belpasso. [@2] The couple were identified by officers of the dog unit of the
Catania police headquarters during a routine roadside check near via Vescovo
Maurizio in Catania. [@1] The drug dog Ares signalled insistently at the work
van driven by the 53-year-old, which he used for his job. [@4] When the man
opened the van's rear doors, officers found a package containing more than 6
grams of marijuana. [@2]

When the 49-year-old saw the officers with her husband, she went onto the
balcony and threw a plastic container outside. [@4] An officer recovered the
container and a narco-test confirmed it contained marijuana. [@4] After the
officers entered the flat, Ares found a further 140 grams of marijuana. [@1] All
the marijuana found in Catania and Belpasso was seized and both spouses were
arrested for possession of drugs for sale, with the presumption of innocence
applying until a final conviction. [@2]

The dog signalled for drugs in both the van and the house, about 150 grams were
recovered, and the couple were placed under house arrest. [@3] The spouses were
placed under house arrest on the order of the duty prosecutor, pending trial
under the direttissimo fast-track procedure. [@2]
