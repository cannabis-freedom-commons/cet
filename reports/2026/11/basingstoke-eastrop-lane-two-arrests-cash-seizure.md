Two men have been arrested after a late-night police stop in Basingstoke led to
the seizure of more than £24,000 in cash, cannabis and nitrous oxide canisters.
[@1] A 21-year-old man from Old Basing was arrested on suspicion of possession
with intent to supply a controlled drug of Class C and acquiring / using /
possessing criminal property. [@1] A 20-year-old man from Basingstoke was
arrested on suspicion of possession with intent to supply a controlled drug of
Class B and Class C. [@1]

Officers on patrol stopped a white Audi A3 on Eastrop Lane around 11.30pm on
Friday, November 13, after noticing suspicious activity. [@1] A search of the
vehicle uncovered 40 cannabis vape pens, 26 nitrous oxide canisters, cannabis,
scales and more than £1,000 in cash. [@1] A further search of a property in Old
Basing led to the discovery of more than £23,000 in cash. [@1]

Both men have been released under investigation. [@1]
