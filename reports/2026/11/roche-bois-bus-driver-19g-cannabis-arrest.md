Officers of the Anti-Drug and Smuggling Unit (ADSU) mounted an operation at
Roche-Bois on Sunday 15 November 2026, and made an arrest there. [@1] The person
arrested during that operation is a 50-year-old man who works as a bus driver.
[@1] The driver, described as a quinquagénaire, is domiciled on Cluny Road at
Union Park. [@1] After his arrest, the suspect was placed in detention at the
Line Barracks centre, where he was being held. [@1]

In the course of the operation, the police discovered in the driver's possession
a small plastic bag containing 19 grams of cannabis. [@1] Alongside the
suspected cannabis, the officers also seized a sum of Rs 19,700 in cash from the
suspect. [@1]

The driver is now the subject of an investigation for possession of cannabis
with intent to sell, rather than simple possession. [@1] In parallel, a separate
and distinct money-laundering inquiry was opened with the aim of determining the
origin of the seized sum of money. [@1]
