A joint team of Jwalamukhi police and the Special Detection Team raided a
residential house in Ward No-4, Jwalamukhi, after a tip-off that charas was
hidden there. [@1] Police arrested two local youths, identified as Karan and
Rahul, at the spot. [@1] The same raid and arrests are independently described
as a joint action in which police and the Special Detection Team raided the Ward
No-4 house and arrested Karan and Rahul. [@2]

The search of the house yielded 1 kg 890 grams of charas, which police seized.
[@1] A second outlet gives the same quantity as 1 kg 890 grams recovered in the
joint action at Jwalamukhi. [@2] A third account also reports a search of the
Ward No-4 house yielding 1 kg 890 grams of charas. [@3] A fourth account reports
1.890 kg of charas recovered from the house in the joint action by police and
the SDT team. [@4]

Police registered a case against both accused under the NDPS Act and took the
seized charas into custody. [@1] One of the arrested youths already faces two
NDPS cases, while the other faces one old case. [@1] Dehra Superintendent of
Police Sandeep Dhawal confirmed the case and said police are interrogating the
accused to trace the source and other members of the supply network. [@1]
