Police from the criminal division of the Dąbrowa Tarnowska county police
headquarters conducted the operation in Bochnia and Brzesko counties. [@1,2] The
operation led to the detention of three men and the seizure of a large amount of
dried plant material and an illegal cannabis cultivation. [@1,2]

The first detentions took place on 17 November in Bochnia county, at the homes
of two men aged 29 and 31. [@1,2] In the living quarters officers found almost 900
grams of dried plant material preliminarily identified as marijuana. [@1,2] In the
attic of the building they found 39 non-fibre cannabis shrubs, the Polish term
for drug-type cannabis, together with equipment needed to grow plants. [@1,2] A
further detention took place on 23 November in Brzesko county, where officers
detained a 23-year-old man. [@1,2] At his residence they found almost 23 grams of
dried plant material and one gram of a crystalline substance. [@1,2] Preliminary
tests showed the secured substances were marijuana and mephedrone. [@1,2]

Charges were brought for drug offences including possession of a significant
quantity of narcotics and illegal cultivation of non-fibre cannabis. [@1,2] The
court ordered the first two suspects held in pre-trial detention for three
months, while the third was granted a property surety. [@1,2]
