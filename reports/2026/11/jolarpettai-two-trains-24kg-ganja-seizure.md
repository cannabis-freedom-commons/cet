Salem subdivision anti-narcotics police seized 24 kilograms of ganja being
carried in two trains near Jolarpettai railway station in Tirupattur district,
Tamil Nadu. [@1] The Salem subdivision Narcotics Control Division police had
been checking trains passing Jolarpettai from northern states for ganja, gutka
and other intoxicants. [@1] After searching trains from Katpadi to Salem at
night, police checked the run from Katpadi to Jolarpettai in the early hours of
Tuesday and examined a train travelling from West Bengal to Bengaluru. [@1] In
the rear general compartment of that train, officers found an unattended bag
under a passenger's seat containing 20 kilograms of ganja tied up in bundles.
[@1]

In a second train, travelling from Secunderabad to Thiruvananthapuram, an
unattended bag near the toilet in the general compartment held four kilograms of
ganja. [@1] Because the people who had carried the ganja could not be
identified, police handed the 24 kilograms they seized over to Jolarpettai
railway police. [@1]
