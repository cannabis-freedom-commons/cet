Vadodara rural SOG arrested wanted ganja supplier Sanjay Subedar Giri near
Chhani jakatnaka in Vadodara city. [@2] The SOG team acted on a tip that Giri,
wanted for supplying ganja in a Bhadarwa police station NDPS case, was near the
Mahadev temple at Chhani jakatnaka. [@2] After legal procedure, police handed
him over to Bhadarwa police station. [@2]

Giri was described as living at Bhavani Hotel in Ranoli and originally from
Kaserva in Jaunpur district, Uttar Pradesh. [@1] In August, district police had
caught Ishwar Haribhai Thakor of Sankarda with ganja at Moxi village in Savli
taluka, and Thakor admitted during questioning that he had brought the ganja
from Sanjay Giri, after which Sanjay absconded. [@1] Police said preliminary
questioning indicated he had gone to Maharashtra while evading arrest. [@1]

Police said he is himself a ganja addict and had previously been caught in a
total of seven cases in Savli, Chhani, Jawaharnagar and Sayajiganj. [@1] He had
obtained retail ganja stock from Khodiyarnagar and sold it for profit. [@1] The
arrest was made during Vadodara rural police's Operation Parakram special drive
from 12 to 26 November, part of a 15-day Anti-Narcotics Task Force drive against
absconding NDPS accused. [@2]
