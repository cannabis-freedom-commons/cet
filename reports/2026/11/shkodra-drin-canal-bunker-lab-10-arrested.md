A secret cannabis cultivation and processing laboratory was found in an
underground space in Shkodra. [@1] Police arrested 10 people during the
intervention. [@1] The operation, codenamed Labyrinth, searched two buildings in
the area known as the Drin Canal. [@1] Those held came from Gjirokaster, Cerrik,
Tirana, Polican, Ishull-Lezha and Mirdita. [@1]

Officers seized around 2,600 suspected cannabis plants grown in pots. [@1]
Officers also seized 170 kilograms of processed cannabis packed in 10 plastic
bags. [@1] The underground space was organized into two separate sectors. [@1]
One sector grew cannabis in pots under lamps while the other processed and
prepared the drug. [@1] Inside a warehouse, officers found a camouflaged
entrance leading to the underground area. [@1]

The operation was carried out by the Narcotics Investigation Section with the
Shkodra Police Commissariat and the Eagle Special Force. [@1]
