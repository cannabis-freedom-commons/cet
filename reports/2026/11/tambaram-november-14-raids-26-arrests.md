Police arrested 26 people in 15 cases during searches across the Tambaram
commissionerate area that yielded cannabis and other goods. [@1] Dinamalar
reported that 20 of those arrested were jailed. [@1]

Dinamani reported that police searched private lodgings, commercial complexes
and public gathering places after receiving information about drug use in
lodgings in suburban Chennai. [@2] The paper reported that 89 special teams and
450 police personnel took part in the searches. [@2] Both accounts reported the
seizure of cannabis and 160 tablets, but gave different cannabis weights. [@2]
Dinamalar also reported the seizure of tobacco products and a three-wheeled
vehicle. [@1] Tambaram police said surveillance had been intensified around
places where students and members of the public gather. [@1]
