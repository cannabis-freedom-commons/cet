Antioquia's traffic and transport police seized 500 grams of creepy-type
marijuana from a public-service vehicle during prevention and control checks on
the department's road corridors. [@1] The Departamento de Policía Antioquia said
the seizure took place during those road-corridor checks and that the drug was
left at the disposal of the Fiscalía General de la Nación. [@2] Police did not
say in which municipality or on which road the procedure took place, or whether
anyone was captured or identified. [@1]

Creepy, also written 'cripa' or 'cripy', is a high-potency cannabis variety.
[@1] A 2019 InSight Crime report cited in the account puts its THC content at 15
to 25 percent, against single digits in traditional marijuana. [@1] That report
places the main growing areas for the variety in northern Cauca and says
criminal groups handle its production and transport. [@1]

Colombia's personal-use dose of marijuana is capped at 20 grams under Law 30 of
1986; a 2012 Constitutional Court ruling says holding a dose solely for personal
use is not punished, and the 500 grams seized is 25 times that limit. [@1]
Exceeding the personal dose alone does not establish a crime: under article 376
of the Penal Code prosecutors must show the substance was intended for
distribution or sale to make a trafficking case. [@1]
