Officers of the Guardia di Finanza's Lecce group followed the three young men as
they drove away from Campo Rom Panareo and stopped their speeding car near the
industrial zone. [@1] The 23-year-old at the wheel had never held a driving
licence. [@1] The three, aged 23, 19 and 18, were arrested by the Guardia di
Finanza and placed under house arrest. [@1]

During the road check officers noticed a strong smell of drugs. [@1] After
initial reluctance, one of the youths handed over two hashish blocks of about
104 grams each hidden in his clothing. [@1] A search of the car recovered more
of the same substance, bringing the hashish total to more than 370 grams. [@1]
Just over half a gram of cocaine was also found in the driver's wallet. [@1]

A later search at the 23-year-old's home in Monteroni found two pistols in the
garage, one with a magazine holding six rounds. [@1] On the duty prosecutor's
order the three were placed under house arrest and were to appear before the
preliminary investigations judge for a validation hearing. [@1]
