Cairo police arrested Brandon Williams, 41, at his home in the 300 block of 15th
Street SW on 17 November 2026, serving arrest warrants at about 11 a.m. with
Grady County sheriff's deputies. [@2] The arrest followed a months-long
investigation into drug activity in the area of his home. [@1]

After the arrest, officers obtained and executed a search warrant at the house,
where they collected additional cocaine, marijuana and other evidence. [@1]
Williams is charged with possession of marijuana with intent to distribute. [@1]
The charges also include two counts of trafficking cocaine, two counts of sale
of cocaine, possession of cocaine with intent to distribute and possession of
cocaine. [@2] He also faces two counts under the Georgia Racketeer Influenced
and Corrupt Organizations Act, the state's racketeering law. [@1]

Williams was taken to the Grady County Jail. [@1] Police said more arrests are
expected as the investigation continues. [@2]
