On 13 November 2026, at about 1:35 p.m., ADSU officers searched the home of a
33-year-old painter living in Roche-Bois. [@1]

During the search, officers found and seized 12 square-folded aluminium papers,
each containing cannabis, with a total weight of 1.8 grams. [@1] Investigators
also seized a plastic bag containing 21 grams of cannabis. [@1] They also seized
Rs 14,700 in cash suspected to come from drug sales. [@1]

Following the seizure, the suspect was arrested. [@1] The investigation was
continuing. [@1]
