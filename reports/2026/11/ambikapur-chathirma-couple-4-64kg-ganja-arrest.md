A joint team of Gandhinagar police and the cyber cell in Ambikapur arrested a
husband and wife with 4.640 kg of illegal ganja, which police valued at 232,000
rupees. [@1] Police said an informant told Gandhinagar police on 20 November
2026 that Ripun Bala, 53, and his wife hid ganja in a garden behind their home
on Banaras Road, Chathirma, and sold it in paper and plastic packets for 50 and
100 rupees. [@1]

The joint team cordoned off the spot and raided it, detaining Ripun Bala and his
wife. [@1] A search of a white plastic bag hidden near the garden behind the
house found a total of 4.640 kg of ganja. [@1] Police seized the ganja and
registered case number 750/2026 at Gandhinagar police station under section
20(b) of the NDPS Act. [@1] Both accused were arrested and were being presented
before a court. [@1]

Navoday Chhattisgarh identified the couple as residents of Banaras Road,
Chathirma, Ambikapur, and said the ganja was kept in a white bag in the garden
behind their house. [@2] Ghatati-Ghatna reported that both were arrested and
produced in court. [@3] Janta Ki Awaz also reported the case number 750/2026
registered under section 20(b) of the NDPS Act. [@4]
