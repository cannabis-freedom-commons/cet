At about 04:30 on 22 November 2026, officers of the Passau border police
inspectorate checked a long-distance coach with Albanian registration on the A3
at the Passau Mitte junction, travelling in the direction of the border. [@1]
During the check officers searched a 22-year-old Bulgarian passenger and found a
small amount of marijuana in his jacket pocket. [@1]

According to the police account, the man had bought the marijuana in Belgium and
brought it into Germany. [@1] Officers seized the marijuana and reported the man
for an offence under the Consumption Cannabis Act (Konsumcannabisgesetz), the
law governing personal cannabis use. [@1]

He was allowed to continue his journey after the police had finished processing
the case. [@1]
