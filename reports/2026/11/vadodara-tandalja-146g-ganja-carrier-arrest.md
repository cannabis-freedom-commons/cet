Police in Vadodara, Gujarat, said they had exposed a ganja-selling operation in
the Tandalja area and caught one man with 146 grams of the drug. [@1] The raid
was made in an open space near the gate of Vadivala School, where officers found
ganja concealed in the dickey of an Activa scooter. [@1]

Officers seized the ganja, the Activa, a mobile phone and other material, the
police said. [@1] A man named Jaim Kaji was named in the case, and the person
caught was described as a carrier in the ganja trade. [@1]
