Police in Vadodara, Gujarat, said they had exposed a ganja-selling operation in
the Tandalja area and caught one man with 146 grams of the drug. [@1,2] The raid
was made in an open space near the gate of Vadivala School, where officers found
ganja concealed in the dickey of an Activa scooter. [@1] The man caught was named in the Gujarat Samachar account as Sarfaraj Pathan. [@2] Officers raided the spot after a tip that ganja was being sold in the area near Vadiwala School. [@2] The raid was carried out by a team sent by Gotri police inspector P.B. Lakkad. [@2]

Officers seized the ganja, the Activa, a mobile phone and other material, the
police said. [@1,2] Police valued the 146 grams of ganja at Rs 7,300. [@2] A man named Jaim Kaji was named in the case, and the person
caught was described as a carrier in the ganja trade. [@1] During questioning the man said Jaim Kazi had supplied him the ganja, and police said they were searching for Kazi and had seized a motorcycle. [@2]
