A joint operation by the Police and the Army uncovered a suspected storage and
distribution centre for narcotics operating in a sublet room in the Coovip
neighbourhood of San Gil. [@1] The investigation by the Sijín of the Santander
Police Department led officers to a property in that sector, where they carried
out a search with Army troops. [@1] Officers captured in flagrante delicto the
man who was at the premises. [@1] The detainee had no prior entries in the
Fiscalía system. [@1]

Officers found more than 696.6 grams of cocaine and more than 1,339.8 grams of
coca base. [@1] They also found several grams of marijuana and more than 2,235.3
grams of caffeine during the inspection of the property. [@1] The seized
inventory also included 27 cartridges for short weapons, a precision scale and a
cell phone. [@1]

Investigators say the man had obtained the room as a sublease through a
friendship with the tenant of the property. [@1] The room was allegedly used to
store different drugs and to facilitate their dosing and sale. [@1]
Investigators hypothesise the space had become a collection and distribution
point for narcotics. [@1] The detainee and all seized material were placed at
the disposal of the competent authority to determine criminal responsibility and
advance the judicial process. [@1] Santander Police commander Luis Fernando
Atuesta Zárate said the authorities would keep targeting points used to store
and sell drugs. [@1]
