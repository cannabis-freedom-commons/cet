On the night of 12 October 2026, Dhumakot police signalled a Swift Desire car
with registration UK 13-3322 to stop during intensive checking. [@2] The driver
broke through the barrier and sped away on seeing the police, and the occupants
later abandoned the car a short distance away and fled after a chase. [@2]
Police searched the abandoned car and recovered 81 kg 630 g of illegal ganja,
registering case 12/2026 under NDPS Act sections 8/20/29/60 at Dhumakot police
station. [@2] Police estimated the seized ganja's international-market value at
about 2.5 million rupees. [@2] Special teams arrested four accused from
Moradabad on 16 October using surveillance and other evidence. [@2] The four
were produced before a local court and sent to jail, while police said they were
tracing the source and full supply chain of the smuggling. [@2]

On 16 November 2026, the Dhumakot team summoned wanted ganja seller Jagat Singh
of Thalisain under warrant-B in the same 81.63 kg transport case. [@1] The court
approved the accused's remand and sent him to jail in judicial custody. [@1] The
court issued non-bailable warrants against other absconding wanted accused, and
police teams were raiding possible hideouts to arrest them. [@1]
