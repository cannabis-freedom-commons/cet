Simri police raided the house of 60-year-old Mustafa Ansari, a resident of
Balihar village, on a tip-off that he bought and sold ganja and other narcotics
from his home. [@1] Ansari was identified as the son of the late Sultan Ansari.
[@1]

Police received the tip at about 2 pm on Saturday afternoon and raided the house
after verifying it. [@1] Magistrate Rahul Kumar accompanied the police team with
armed force during the house search. [@1] Officers found a ganja-like narcotic
substance kept wrapped in plastic and paper in the house. [@1] Police seized a
total of 2.597 kilograms of ganja and 25,640 rupees in cash from the house. [@1]

Police registered a first information report against Ansari and sent him to jail
after completing legal formalities. [@1]
