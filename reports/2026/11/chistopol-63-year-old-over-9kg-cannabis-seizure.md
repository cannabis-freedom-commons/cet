Police in Chistopol detained a 63-year-old local resident after seizing more
than 9 kilograms of drugs from him. [@1] Officers who examined the man's private
house found plant material along with electronic scales and packaging material.
[@1] During questioning, the detained man admitted that he had cultivated
cannabis in a ravine not far from his home. [@1]

He said that he had stored and dried the cannabis in a specially equipped
outbuilding in his yard. [@1] He told police that he had grown and kept the drug
because he wanted to earn money by selling it. [@1] Forensic experts established
that part of the seizure was marijuana with a total mass of 282.5 grams. [@1]
Officers separately seized numerous cannabis plant parts from his yard
outbuildings which in total contained more than 9 kg of narcotic substance. [@1]

Police opened a criminal case over the seizure and cultivation. [@1] The man now
faces a prison term of 10 to 20 years. [@1]
