Wahdat Colony police arrested Waris alias Bhola, described by police as an
alleged drug dealer with a criminal record, during an intelligence-based
operation near Shah Kamal Shrine in Lahore on Monday 23 November 2026. [@1]
According to police, the suspect was identified as Waris alias Bhola and was
arrested following a tip-off. [@1] Police said the suspect had a criminal
record, with multiple cases already registered against him at Sanda,
Gulshan-e-Ravi, Lytton Road and Islampura police stations. [@2]

During the operation, officers recovered 1.4 kilograms of hashish from his
possession. [@1] In the same search, officers also recovered 200 grams of
crystal methamphetamine, known as ice, from his possession. [@1] Police reported
the two seized substances together as the narcotics recovered in the operation
near the shrine. [@1]

A case was registered against the arrested suspect following the seizure. [@1]
He was then handed over to the investigation wing for further legal proceedings.
[@2] SP Iqbal Town Sidra Khan appreciated the performance of the SHO Wahdat
Colony and the police team for their efforts in the operation. [@2]
