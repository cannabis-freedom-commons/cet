A plainclothes patrol of the Bamberg police checked a 17-year-old at the city's
railway station on the evening of 20 November 2026. [@1] During the check the
officers found a larger quantity of cannabis, divided among several containers
and labelled. [@1] The youth was also carrying other drugs. [@1]

Police seized the cannabis and handed the 17-year-old over to his mother. [@1]

He faces proceedings for trafficking in cannabis and for a general offence under
the Narcotics Act (Betäubungsmittelgesetz), Germany's drug law. [@1]
