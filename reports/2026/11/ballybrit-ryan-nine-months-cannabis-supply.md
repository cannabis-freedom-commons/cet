Dylan Ryan, 19, of The Paddocks, Ballybrit, pleaded guilty to possession and
sale or supply of cannabis before Judge Fiona Lydon at Galway District Court.
[@1] Judge Lydon imposed a nine-month sentence on the sale or supply charge and
took the other charges into account. [@1] Ryan was taken into custody after
sentencing and had to provide his own €500 bond and one from an independent
surety, approved by Gardaí as bail conditions, before he could be released. [@1]

Drug Unit Gardaí found the cannabis in a rucksack in Ryan's bedroom during a
search of his home on 14 March 2025. [@1] He was charged with possession and
sale or supply of cannabis. [@1] Gardaí valued the cannabis at €4,214, but Ryan
maintained it was worth only €2,000. [@1]

Defending solicitor Brian Gilmartin said a probation report found Ryan suitable
for community service and at low risk of re-offending, and put the cannabis's
value at about €2,000 rather than the Garda estimate of over €4,000. [@1] Judge
Lydon said a significant amount of cannabis was involved and noted a report that
Ryan had used cocaine up to last August and had not arranged hair-follicle
testing to show he was off drugs. [@1]

Ryan had three previous convictions, including a 2022 possession of MDMA
conviction at Gort District Court and a 2021 driving without insurance
conviction at Ballinasloe District Court. [@1] Mr Gilmartin asked for
recognisances to be fixed for an appeal against the severity of the sentence,
and these were agreed and the matter was adjourned. [@1]
