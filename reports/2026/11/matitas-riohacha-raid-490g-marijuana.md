Authorities raided a property in the Matitas district of rural Riohacha in the
early hours of 19 November 2026 and were met with gunfire. [@1] The operation
involved troops of the Mechanized Infantry Battalion No. 6 Cartagena and members
of the Criminal Investigation Section (Sijín), according to the military source.
[@1] One of the presumed suspects was wounded in the exchange, received first
aid from military nurses and was later taken to a medical centre. [@1] One man
and two women were captured, believed to be members of the Autodefensas
Conquistadoras de la Sierra Nevada (ACSN). [@1] A minor was rescued and their
rights were restored. [@1]

Officers seized 490.5 grams of marijuana and two mobile phones during the raid.
[@1] They also seized an M4 rifle, a 12-gauge shotgun of the guacharaca type, a
.38 revolver, a pistol with a magazine, six revolver cartridges, two rifle
rounds, two 9 mm cartridges and one .22 cartridge. [@1] The presumed members of
the criminal group were said to have come to the property intending to steal
cattle, pigs, chickens, air conditioners, tools, animal feed, kitchen utensils,
a stove and other items. [@1] Authorities also immobilised two dump trucks, two
Toyota TXL vehicles and four motorcycles. [@1]

The three captives, the seized items and the vehicles were placed at the
disposal of the competent authorities in Riohacha for judicial proceedings. [@1]
