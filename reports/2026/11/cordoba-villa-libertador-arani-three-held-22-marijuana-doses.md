Three adult men were detained after four raids closed two alleged drug sale
points in Barrio Villa El Libertador in Córdoba city. [@1] Córdoba's Fuerza
Policial Antinarcotráfico carried out the operation as part of an investigation
into alleged drug dealing. [@1] The procedures focused on premises on Calle
Arani in the south of the provincial capital. [@1] The suspects allegedly handed
over substances from the pavement outside the raided homes. [@1] One member of
the group had a previous criminal record for drug trafficking, according to the
force. [@1] The Second-Turn anti-narcotics prosecutor supervised the FPA actions
and ordered the three detainees and seized items transferred to judicial
headquarters. [@1]

Officers seized 233 doses of cocaine and 22 doses of marijuana during the
inspections. [@1] Officers also seized 134,300 pesos, a digital scale, a car and
items used to portion illicit substances. [@1] The official information gave no
model or owner for the retained vehicle. [@1]

The case is being handled as an alleged breach of National Drug Law No. 23,737.
[@1] The case will continue in court, where the evidence will be assessed and
the detainees' procedural situation defined. [@1]
