Bilecik provincial narcotics police's Bozüyük bureau carried out an anti-drug
operation in Bozüyük district. [@1] Officers searched one vehicle and two people
during the operation. [@1] Officers detained two suspects in the operation. [@1]

The searches recovered skunk in an unspecified quantity described as muhtelif
miktarda. [@1] Police started proceedings against both suspects on suspicion of
buying, accepting or possessing drugs. [@1]
