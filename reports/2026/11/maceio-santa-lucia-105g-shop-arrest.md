Military police arrested a man on suspicion of drug trafficking on the afternoon
of Friday 13 November 2026 in Santa Lucia, Maceio. [@1] A second account places
the same arrest on the afternoon of Friday 13 November in Santa Lucia, Maceio.
[@2] The team had been called to check a report of drug trafficking at a
commercial establishment. [@1] Officers stopped a man matching the description
in the report and found marijuana in his pocket. [@1]

A search inside the premises found further portions of the same drug, bringing
the total to 105 grams of marijuana. [@1] Officers also seized a precision
scale, a mobile phone, R$117 in cash and one pack of the medicine Rohypnol. [@1]

The suspect admitted the seized items were his, was taken to the Central de
Flagrantes and was formally charged with drug trafficking. [@1] The case was
logged at 16h31. [@1]
