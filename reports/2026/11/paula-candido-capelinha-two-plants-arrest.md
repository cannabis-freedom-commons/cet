Military Police arrested a man for drug trafficking in the Capelinha
neighbourhood of Paula Cândido on the night of Sunday 15 November 2026. [@1]
Teams from the Tactical Mobile Platoon and local policing were on joint patrol
on Rua José Oliveira Duarte when they spotted suspicious movement at a property
already reported for selling drugs. [@1] During the approach officers located
drugs and illicit materials, part of which had been discarded into a pipe in the
residence. [@1]

The seizure comprised five pinos of cocaine, seven ecstasy tablets, six crack
rocks and two marijuana plants. [@1] Officers also seized three cameras that had
been used to monitor the arrival of police and people deemed suspicious,
together with notes linked to drug trafficking. [@1] Officers also seized a
damaged mobile phone and R$245 in cash alongside the drugs and monitoring
equipment. [@1]

The suspect was detained at the scene and taken to the competent authorities for
further legal procedures. [@1]
