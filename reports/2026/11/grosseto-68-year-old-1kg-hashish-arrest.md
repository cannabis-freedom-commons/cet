Carabinieri arrested a 68-year-old man resident in the province of Grosseto
after finding him in possession of one kilogram of hashish. [@1] The carabinieri
described the quantity as significant and arrested the man on suspicion of
detenzione di sostanze stupefacenti ai fini di spaccio, possessing narcotics for
the purpose of dealing. [@1] The carabinieri found the hashish hidden in the man's home during a search of his residence. [@2]

The operation was carried out by carabinieri, who seized the substance and
arrested the man, born in 1958. [@1] After the arrest the man was transferred to Siena prison. [@2] A hearing to validate the arrest was set
for Friday 20 November 2026 before the judge for preliminary investigations, the
giudice per le indagini preliminari. [@1] The validation hearing was held at the Grosseto tribunal on Thursday 19 November 2026. [@2] The 68-year-old was assisted in the
case by the lawyer Elena Pellegrini. [@1] Preliminary investigations judge Cecilia Balsamo reserved her decision on the request. [@2] Lawyer Elena Pellegrini asked the judge to replace prison custody with house arrest. [@2] He took part in the hearing by video link from Siena prison. [@2]
