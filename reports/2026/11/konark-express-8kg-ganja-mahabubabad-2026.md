Mahabubabad excise and task-force officials caught dry ganja being transported
on the Konark Express train during a joint morning inspection. [@1] Joint excise
and task-force staff carried out the checks on the Konark Express train at about
6.30 on Sunday morning. [@1] The operation proceeded under the supervision of
District Prohibition and Excise Officer Karanchand. [@1] Ramprasad provided the
details of the operation. [@1]

The inspection of the Konark Express yielded 8 kilograms of dry ganja. [@1]
Officials valued the seized 8 kilograms of dry ganja at 400,000 rupees. [@1]

The officials said it was not yet known to whom the seized ganja belonged. [@1]
The officials also said it was not yet known from where to where the ganja was
being transported. [@1] A case was registered over the seizure and further
investigation was under way, according to the excise inspector. [@1] Mahabubabad
excise Sub-Inspector Santosh, Head Constable Lalitha Rani and other accompanying
staff participated in the checks. [@1]
