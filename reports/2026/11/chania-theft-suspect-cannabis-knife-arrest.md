The Chania crime-investigation unit solved a case involving a foreign man who
appeared under five different names, four birth dates and three nationalities.
[@1] He alternately claimed Afghan, Algerian or French nationality, which made
verifying his real details difficult. [@1] His real identity remained unknown.
[@1] A case file was opened against him for three vehicle theft-burglaries,
while he was arrested for drug- and weapons-legislation violations. [@1]

Investigators allege he committed three vehicle thefts in Chania between 29
October and 12 November 2026. [@2] From those thefts he took a total of 150
euros, along with various documents and objects. [@2] Officers searched his
residence and seized burglary tools, a knife and a small quantity of cannabis.
[@2] Another account describes the burglary tools, a knife and a small quantity
of cannabis as found in his possession during a police check. [@3]

For the three cases a file for aggravated theft was opened, alongside the arrest
for drugs and weapons. [@1] The Chania Subdirectorate for the Prosecution and
Investigation of Crimes is conducting the preliminary inquiry. [@1]
