A 30-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of drug trafficking and illegal
possession of firearms on the night of Saturday 21 November 2026 in the Novo
Horizonte neighbourhood of Itajubá, in southern Minas Gerais. [@1] The 56th
Military Police Battalion said the operation began after anonymous tips reported
that a man was using a motorcycle to deliver marijuana tablets and kept drugs
and weapons stored. [@1] Police intelligence work located the suspect's home;
officers stopped him as he rode the reported motorcycle and obtained permission
to search the property. [@2] A detection dog assisted the search, and a further
tip pointed to an uninhabited house nearby. [@2]

In total officers seized 30 tabletes — bricks of compressed marijuana — hidden
in a buried drum, a plastic bag in a wooded area near a fence and behind a water
tank, and inside the empty house. [@2] Alongside the drugs, police found two
firearms — a .32 calibre revolver and a 7.65 mm pistol — a magazine and six
intact 7.65 mm rounds. [@1] Officers also collected a precision scale, a card
machine and three mobile phones. [@1] The motorcycle used for the deliveries was
impounded and removed to a licensed yard. [@1]

After the arrest the man was taken for medical attention and then presented at
the Itajubá Civil Police station with all the seized material. [@2] The case was
registered as suspected illicit drug trafficking and illegal possession of a
firearm of permitted use. [@1] The Military Police said the man had previous
records for trafficking and bodily harm. [@1]
