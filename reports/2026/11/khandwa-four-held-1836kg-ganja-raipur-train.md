Kotwali police in Khandwa arrested four people, including three women, in a
ganja-smuggling case. [@2] Officers caught them on Anand Nagar Road near the
Imleshwar Mahadev temple after a late-Saturday-night tip that some people were
looking to sell ganja there. [@2] The Kotwali team reached the spot with
independent witnesses, cordoned off the area and took a man and three women
found in suspicious circumstances into custody. [@2]

Police found nine packets holding a total of 18 kg 360 g of ganja on them and
seized the consignment. [@2] Police valued the seized ganja at about Rs 4 lakh.
[@2] The accused had brought the consignment from Raipur by train and, after
reaching Khandwa, were preparing to travel onward by bus. [@2] The consignment
had been brought from Raipur by train. [@1]

All four live at Bhopal Naka in the Ashta area of Sehore district. [@2]
According to preliminary information, they were to supply the consignment in
Ashta, Bhopal and nearby areas, and police are working to establish where it was
bought and for whom. [@2] Police registered a case against all four at Kotwali
police station under Sections 8/20 of the NDPS Act and are questioning them
about the smuggling network and others involved. [@2]
