The PSP detained three men aged between 20 and 24 in Guimarães on suspicion of
drug trafficking. [@2,4] The three detainees were to be brought before a court for
a first interrogation and the setting of coercive measures. [@1] The detainees were to appear at the Tribunal Judicial de Vila Nova de Famalicão. [@4,5,6] The arrests
followed an investigation that had run for more than two years and the execution
of four home search warrants. [@3,4,5,6]

During the operation officers seized hashish enough for about 712 doses, cocaine
enough for about 720 doses and 97 grams of liamba, making a total of more than
half a kilo of drugs. [@1,4,5,6] Officers also seized a taser, three mobile phones and
4,700 euros, along with other material used in drug trafficking. [@1,4,5,6]

The cocaine, enough for about 720 doses, is not cannabis and is excluded from
the cannabis figures. [@1] The hashish was reported only as a dose count with no
weight in grams, so only the 97 grams of liamba is recorded as seized cannabis.
[@2] The PSP announced the operation in a statement on Tuesday 17 November 2026.
