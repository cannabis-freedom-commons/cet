On Thursday 19 November 2026, military police seized about 50 kg of marijuana in
an uninhabited property in the Santo Agostinho neighbourhood of Santa Cruz do
Capibaribe, in the Agreste region of Pernambuco. [@1] Officers of the 24th
Military Police Battalion made the seizure during patrols in the locality. [@1]
They said they saw a man who disobeyed a stop order and entered the apparently
uninhabited property. [@1] During the search they found the drug stored in a
bathroom, along with a precision scale and materials used to package drugs. [@2]

Two motorcycles were also found at the property. [@1] One had a theft or fraud
restriction and the other had a suppressed chassis number, which prevents normal
identification through that number. [@1] The suspect confessed that he was
responsible for guarding the property. [@1]

One suspect was referred to the duty police station. [@1] After receiving
medical attention, he was taken to the duty police station with all the material
seized during the operation. [@1] The case was handed to the Polícia Civil for
the applicable legal measures. [@3]
