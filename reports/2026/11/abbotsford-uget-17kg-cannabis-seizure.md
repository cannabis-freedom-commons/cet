A Uniform Gang Enforcement Team (UGET) officer arrested two men after observing
suspicious activity near isolated railway tracks in Abbotsford, British
Columbia, on 9 November 2026. [@1] The officer had reason to believe the two
individuals were carrying illegal cannabis. [@1] Both men were arrested under
the Cannabis Act. [@1]

A subsequent search of the two vehicles uncovered two large garbage bags inside
a trunk. [@1] The bags held approximately 70 freezer bags of bulk illicit
cannabis, with a combined weight exceeding 17 kilograms. [@1] Authorities seized
the cannabis following the discovery. [@1]

UGET operates under the Combined Forces Special Enforcement Unit-British
Columbia (CFSEU-BC), which is responsible for addressing gang-related criminal
activity and supporting investigations into organized crime. [@1] CFSEU-BC is
described as British Columbia's provincial anti-gang law enforcement agency and
Canada's largest integrated police unit, involving more than 500 sworn officers
and civilian staff from 12 policing agencies. [@1] The investigation remains
ongoing. [@2]
