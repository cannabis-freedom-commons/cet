Narcotics officers of the Pematangsiantar city police arrested AOS alias M, 31,
a man earlier convicted in a 2019 narcotics case, on suspicion of selling ganja
in Gang Pulo Kumba. [@1] The arrest took place at about 14:50 WIB on Friday 20
November 2026 on Jalan Rakutta Sembiring, Gang Pulo Kumba, Kelurahan Nagapita,
Kecamatan Siantar Martoba. [@3] Police named him a suspect and detained him to
face prosecution after a case review, a police spokesperson said. [@1]

Officers found a clear plastic bag holding 25 ganja packets wrapped in black
plastic in the front right pocket of his trousers. [@1] Police put the gross
weight of the 25 packets at 33.60 grams. [@1] Officers also seized four sheets
of tiktak paper and Rp20,000 in cash. [@3] The suspect told police he had
obtained the ganja from a man in the Parluasan area whose identity officers had
established. [@1] Police said the man had not been found and the search for him
was still under investigation. [@1]

The operation began with public information that a man was selling ganja in the
Gang Pulo Kumba area. [@2] He was charged with selling narcotics under Article
114(1), subsidiary to Article 111(1), of the 2009 narcotics law, as adjusted by
Law No. 1 of 2026 on sentencing. [@2]
