An enforcement team in Hyderabad seized 1.1 kg of dry ganja during raids in the
Dhoolpet area on Thursday, 19 November 2026, and arrested a woman allegedly
involved in its sale. [@1] The arrested woman was identified as Anitha Bai. [@1]
Officials said she was allegedly involved in the regular sale of ganja, the
local name for cannabis, along with her associates. [@1] Those associates were
named as Aruna Bai, alias Anguri Bai, and Surendra Singh. [@1] Surendra Singh is
absconding. [@1] The woman was arrested on suspicion of involvement in the sale
of the seized ganja. [@1]

During the raid the team detected the ganja possession and seized 1.1 kg of dry
ganja from Anitha Bai. [@1] The seizure proceedings were documented through a
video-recorded panchanama, a formal record of what was found. [@1] The arrested
woman, along with the case papers, was handed over to the Station House Officer,
Dhoolpet police station, for further legal action. [@1] Officials are making
efforts to arrest the absconding suspect. [@1]
