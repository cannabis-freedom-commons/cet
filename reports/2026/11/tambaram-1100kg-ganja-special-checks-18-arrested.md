Tambaram city police sent 59 special teams, with 191 officers and personnel in
all, to check areas across the city police limits on 26 November 2026. [@1] The
checks were carried out on the orders of Tambaram city police commissioner
Najmul Hoda to keep public peace and law and order. [@1] Police checked 204
people with criminal records to see whether they were still involved in
offences. [@1]

Police arrested 18 people, nine with criminal records and nine without. [@1] The
arrested people were produced before a court and sent to judicial custody. [@1]
Officers seized 1,100 kg of ganja during the operation. [@1]

A police officer said such special raids would continue to prevent crime,
monitor offenders and protect public safety. [@1]
