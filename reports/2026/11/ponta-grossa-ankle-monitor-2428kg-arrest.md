Military police Shock Platoon officers arrested a man monitored by an electronic
ankle bracelet for drug trafficking in Ponta Grossa. [@1] The team was on
preventive patrol when it spotted the man, who fled into a residence on seeing
the patrol car. [@1]

During the stop, the man admitted he had run because he kept drugs stored in the
bedroom. [@1] With the resident's permission, officers searched the room and
found three bricks and two portions of maconha totalling 2.428 kg. [@1] The
2.428 kg of maconha, about 2.4 kg, was hidden in a wardrobe inside the
residence. [@1] Officers also seized two knives with drug residue and a mobile
phone. [@2]

The man was arrested and taken with the seized materials to the Judicial Police
station for further procedures. [@2]
