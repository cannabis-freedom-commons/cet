Civil police in Campo Grande arrested three men — Joilson Pereira Leite, Igor
Oliveira Pereira and Mateus Silva Alves — at the end of an operation by the
Denar narcotics unit. [@1,2,6,9] The operation ran from the night of Monday 16
November into the early afternoon of Tuesday 17 November 2026. [@1,2,3,4,5,6,8,9] The three
were taken to Denar for judicial police procedures. [@1,4,5,6,7,8,9]

The investigation began after police noticed a Volkswagen Gol with apparently
false number plates and signs of involvement in drug transport. [@1,2,3,7,9] The Gol was
found abandoned in vegetation at the side of a highway after running out of
fuel, still carrying 488.8 kg of cannabis in 493 tablets. [@1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8,9] Police stopped a
Chevrolet Astra carrying two men, one of them Igor Oliveira Pereira. [@1,2,3,8] Igor
admitted taking part in the criminal activity and gave police information about
other people and vehicles involved. [@1,2,9] The information led police to Mateus
Silva Alves, identified as responsible for a Chevrolet Onix used to support the
drug transport. [@1,2,3,8,9] At a residence linked to Mateus, police found 166 grams of
cannabis and the keys to a property he had rented. [@1,2,3,6,9] Mateus had rented a
property in the Universitário neighbourhood of Campo Grande. [@1,2,6,7,8,9] At that
address officers found a further 652.8 kg of stored cannabis. [@1,2,5,6,7,8,9] The two loads
took the total seizure past 1.1 tonnes of cannabis. [@1,2,4,5,7,8,9] Besides the cannabis, investigators collected vehicles and other objects considered relevant to the continuing investigation. [@9]

The action formed part of Operation Protetor de Fronteiras e Divisas, run by
Brazil's Ministry of Justice, and targeted a structure suspected of transporting
and storing drugs from the region bordering Paraguay. [@1,3,5,6,7,8,9] Other narcotic
substances found at the property appeared to investigators to relate to a
separate occurrence. [@1,2,4,6,8,9] Police said the work was not finished and that the
origin and destination of the drugs remained unclear. [@1,6]
