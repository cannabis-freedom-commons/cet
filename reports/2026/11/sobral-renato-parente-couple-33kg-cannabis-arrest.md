Military police arrested a 30-year-old man and an 18-year-old woman in the
Renato Parente neighbourhood of Sobral on 14 November 2026. [@1] Teams from the
Tactical Force of the 3rd Military Police Battalion with support from two Shock
Police Battalion units carried out the operation. [@1] The operation followed
intelligence that a house on Rua Maria Glória Chaves Lima Verde was being used
to store drugs, weapons and other illicit material for a criminal group active
in the region. [@1]

When the suspect returned, he surrendered with a backpack in which officers
found a portion of maconha, and he indicated further illicit material inside the
house. [@1] Officers seized 31.830 kilograms of maconha and 1.188 kilograms of
skunk, a cannabis total of 33.018 kilograms, alongside crack and cocaine paste
that are not counted as cannabis. [@1] In the house officers seized an unbranded
5.56 rifle with no serial number, a .40 pistol and a .380 pistol, plus 1,207
rounds of assorted calibres and seven magazines. [@1]

Both suspects were taken to the Civil Police station and charged in flagrante
with drug trafficking and illegal possession of a restricted firearm, and the
man already had a criminal record for drug trafficking. [@1]
