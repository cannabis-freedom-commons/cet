Novario Foto, a 25-year-old Surinamese man, appeared before the justice on
Thursday 19 November 2026. [@1] He was sentenced to five years in prison without
suspension for exporting cannabis. [@1] He was convicted of taking part in drug
trafficking between Limoges, French Guiana and Suriname. [@1]

The case was the latest step in the continuing dismantling of a drug-trafficking
network in Limoges's Surinamese and Guyanese community. [@1] Another 25-year-old
Surinamese man, Donovan Linga, had been sentenced on 5 November to eight years
in prison after being stopped while driving between Rouen and Limoges with nine
kilograms of cocaine. [@1]
