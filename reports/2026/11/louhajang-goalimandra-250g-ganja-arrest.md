In the Goalimandra Bazar area of Louhajang Upazila, Munshiganj, District
Detective Police arrested 34-year-old Md Roni with yaba and ganja. [@1] Officers
arrested him from the paved road in front of Joy Mishtanna Bhandar in
Goalimandra Bazar at about 2:15pm on Saturday. [@1] Roni is the son of Samsul
Sheikh of Satgaon village in Sreenagar Upazila, Munshiganj. [@1]

The raid was conducted by Sub-Inspector Mohammad Khayrul with a DB force as part
of the district police's anti-drug, anti-extortion and anti-terrorism operation.
[@1] Officers recovered 100 yaba pills and 250 grams of ganja from Roni. [@1]

District Police Superintendent Md Badruddoza confirmed the information. [@1]
Legal action against him was pending in connection with the seizure. [@1]
