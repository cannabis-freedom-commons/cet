An Anti-Drug and Smuggling Unit operation uncovered 2.216 kg of cannabis hidden
in speakers on Tuesday 17 November 2026 at the container park in Port-Mathurin,
Rodrigues. [@1] The seizure led to the arrest of 41-year-old mason Joseph Jean
Alex Ravina, who lives in Papayes. [@1] A 31-year-old fisherman living in
Eau-Clair, who allegedly shipped the goods and paid the freight cost to
Mauritius Shipping, was questioned and then released. [@1] Ravina alone was
arrested for possession of cannabis intended for distribution on suspicion of
drug trafficking and was held at Grande-Montagne police station. [@1]

Both men were first asked to accompany investigators to the container park to
witness the search of their goods, which they identified and which were then
passed through a scanner. [@1] Two pairs of speakers were dismantled in the
presence of both men. [@1] Investigators found two transparent plastic parcels,
each holding four hermetically sealed sachets. [@1] Inside was a greenish plant
substance suspected to be cannabis. [@1] The total seized drug was estimated at
2.216 kg. [@1] Eight packets holding 12,800 rolling papers were also found in
the same container, prompting a separate case for illegal possession of
prohibited articles. [@1]
