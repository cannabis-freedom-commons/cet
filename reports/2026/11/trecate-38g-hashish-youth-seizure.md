In via Sorelle Ministre della Carità in Trecate, local police focused a patrol
check at about 11:30 on an adult Italian youth. [@1] Drug-detection dogs led
officers to find and recover a packet with about 38 grams of suspected substance
immediately available to the youth. [@1]

The material seized in the street was taken to the command offices for standard
technical checks. [@1] A preliminary narcotest was positive for cannabis
derivatives, confirming the material was hashish. [@1]

Officers carried out an administrative seizure and alleged detention for
personal use under Article 75 of Presidential Decree 309/1990. [@1] The youth
was formally reported to the Prefecture of Novara, which will decide further
measures. [@1]
