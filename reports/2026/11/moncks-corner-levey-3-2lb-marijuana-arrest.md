Berkeley County sheriff's deputies arrested Gerod Xavier Levey, 50, of Coaxum
Road in Moncks Corner, after searching his home on Wednesday 18 November 2026.
[@3] He was charged with possession with intent to distribute marijuana, second
offense; possession with intent to distribute crack cocaine; and possession of a
firearm or ammunition by a prohibited person. [@1] The sheriff’s office said its
Narcotics Task Force began investigating him in October after a tip about
suspected drug distribution in Berkeley County. [@1]

Task force members, with sheriff’s office intelligence and K-9 units and DEA and
SLED agents, executed a search warrant at the residence. [@1] Investigators
seized 1,471.85 grams (about 3.2 pounds) of marijuana, 1.11 grams of crack
cocaine, $2,947 in cash and a Daisy .22-caliber rifle, the sheriff’s office
said. [@2]

During the search investigators also found seven pit bulls tied to trees on the
property. [@1] Berkeley County Animal Control officers observed bruises, bite
marks and scratches and seized all seven dogs. [@1] The sheriff’s office said
the injuries raised suspicions of possible dogfighting, and Animal Control
opened a separate investigation with animal cruelty charges pending. [@1]
