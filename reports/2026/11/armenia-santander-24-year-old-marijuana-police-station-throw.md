Police in Armenia detained a 24-year-old man during a surveillance and control
operation in the barrio Santander. [@2] Officers said he was about to throw a
black bag holding drugs into the police facilities where detainees are kept.
[@2] The detention took place at calle 35 con carrera 21 in the barrio Santander
of Armenia, the capital of Quindío. [@3] Police said they went to the spot after
an alert about a man loitering suspiciously with a black plastic wrapper in his
hands. [@2] A search of the man found undosed marijuana, according to the
police. [@3]

Police told him his rights as a person detained for the suspected crime of
tráfico, fabricación o porte de estupefacientes. [@2] He was taken to the
Fiscalía General de la Nación, the prosecutor's office, for the judicialisation
procedure and urgent proceedings. [@2] La Patria reported that the police left
him at the disposal of the Fiscalía. [@1] The police said they would keep
strengthening controls to prevent illicit substances from entering detention
centres. [@2]
