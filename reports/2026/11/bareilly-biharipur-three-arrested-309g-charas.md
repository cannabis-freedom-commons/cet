Shahi police station police in Bareilly arrested three men on suspicion of
narcotics trafficking and seized 309.19 grams of charas, a cannabis resin, from
them. [@1] Police said the operation took place around 8:10 pm on 19 November
near a road leading under a river bridge in village Biharipur. [@1] Those
detained were Ram Murthi, 65, Dev Dutt alias Kallu, 23, and Sanjeev, 24, all
residents of village Dungerpur in the Shahi police station area. [@1] The three
were standing near the bridge intending to sell the charas, police said. [@1]

During questioning the men said they earned a living as labourers until an
unknown person offered them a large profit to sell charas. [@1] They said they
took the charas from that person and went to Biharipur to sell it, where police
arrested them. [@1]

Police said Sanjeev already faced cases registered in 2022 under sections 376D
and 506 of the Indian Penal Code and in 2024 under sections 303(2) and 317(2) of
the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, India's penal code. [@1] The operation was led by
station in-charge Ashutosh Dwivedi and a case was registered against the three
under sections 8 and 20 of India's Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances
(NDPS) Act, as case number 424/2026. [@1] Police were taking the three to court.
