The Special Response Battalion's SRB-7 unit in Chattogram arrested three alleged
drug traders with 20 kg of ganja at Feni railway station. [@1] Acting on secret
information received on 16 November 2026 that traders disguised as passengers
were waiting at the station to smuggle drugs to Dhaka, an SRB-7 team raided
platform No. 1 at about 1:30 at night. [@1] The three were caught red-handed
while trying to flee. [@1] Those held were identified as 19-year-old Sohan Islam
and 30-year-old Jesmin Ara, both from Dinajpur, and 56-year-old Nurnabi from
Feni. [@1]

A search of their shoulder bags and trolley bags recovered 20 kg of ganja
wrapped in scotch tape in a concealed manner, with an estimated market value of
about 200,000 taka. [@1] In initial questioning they admitted collecting the
ganja from a border area and supplying it to retail sellers in Dhaka and other
parts of the country. [@1] SRB-7 said the accused and the seized drugs were
handed over to the relevant police station for further legal action. [@1]
