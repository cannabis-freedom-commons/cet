Ivanovo police detained a 24-year-old resident of the regional centre as he was
handing over a narcotic substance. [@1]

A personal search of the suspect turned up 1.95 grams of marijuana. [@1]
Officers searched his flat the same day and found further banned substances,
including marijuana and hashish. [@1] They also seized electronic scales,
packaging bags and a vacuum packer, which police said pointed to systematic drug
sales. [@1]

Investigators opened a criminal case under Article 228.1 of the Russian Criminal
Code. [@1] Police said they were still establishing how the drugs were supplied
and the full circumstances of the case. [@1]
