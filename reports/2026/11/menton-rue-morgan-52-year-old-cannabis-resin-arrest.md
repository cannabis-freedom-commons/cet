Repeated comings and goings at a building in rue Morgan drew residents'
attention, and several residents reported suspicious visits to the police. [@1]
Those reports led police to set up targeted surveillance before moving in. [@1]
On 18 November, a 52-year-old man was arrested in rue Morgan in Menton while
carrying out a drug transaction with two other people. [@1]

A search of his nearby home found cannabis resin alongside about ten grams of
heroin and about ten grams of cocaine. [@1] Officers also seized 510 euros in
cash at the home. [@1] Questioned by investigators, the man admitted taking part
in the drug dealing. [@1] He told police he resold drugs to fund his own drug
use. [@1] According to his account, the activity had begun about six months
earlier. [@1] Police inquiries suggested the dealing had been going on for
longer. [@1]

On 20 November he was brought before the Nice judicial court under the
guilty-plea procedure, comparution sur reconnaissance préalable de culpabilité
(CRPC). [@1]
