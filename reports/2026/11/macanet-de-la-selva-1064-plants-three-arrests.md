Policía Nacional officers arrested three people after dismantling a marijuana
plantation of 1,064 plants in a house in Maçanet de la Selva, in the province of
Girona. [@2] The three detained were the occupants of the house and the main
suspects for offences against public health and electricity fraud, the force
said in a statement. [@2] La Vanguardia reported that police linked the three to
a criminal organisation dedicated to drug trafficking. [@3]

The inquiry began in June 2026 after unusually high electricity readings at the
property, which officers checked with a second measurement. [@2] Officers kept
watch on the chalet and confirmed the plantation from the strong smell of
marijuana and the running air conditioning. [@1] With judicial authorisation
they entered the house and found 1,064 marijuana plants inside. [@1] In the rest
of the chalet officers seized four air pistols, .50-calibre ammunition, two
large machetes and 32,620 euros in cash. [@1] La Vanguardia also listed a
telescopic sight among the items seized. [@3]

The three men were brought before the duty judge, who ordered provisional prison
without bail for one of them. [@1] The amount the growers are accused of
defrauding from the electricity company was not disclosed. [@1] Police released
no ages, nationalities or previous records for the three detainees. [@1]
