Four men are on trial in Hordaland District Court in a major drug case that
includes 137 kilos of cocaine. [@1] A student in his twenties at the Norwegian
School of Economics (NHH) is accused of holding a central leadership role. [@1]
He has been held on remand since his arrest in December last year. [@1] He
allegedly coordinated sales in several places using cryptocurrency and encrypted
communications services. [@1] Another man in his thirties allegedly held a
second leadership position. [@1]

According to the prosecution, a total of 137 kilos of cocaine, 25 kilos of
hashish and three kilos of amphetamine were procured, stored and sold over a
one-year period until December last year. [@1] The two alleged leaders have
partially admitted guilt, including laundering 240,000 kroner through a
construction company. [@1] The two other defendants allegedly acted on
instructions from the NHH student and are charged over a smaller quantity of
drugs, and they too have partially admitted guilt. [@1]

The hearing is scheduled to last eight weeks. [@1] The two alleged leaders
previously served prison terms at the same time. [@1]
