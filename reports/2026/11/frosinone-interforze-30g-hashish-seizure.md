State Police, Guardia di Finanza and Local Police jointly carried out an
extraordinary territorial control in Frosinone on the evening of 17 November
2026. [@1] The operation was ordered by the Questore to guard areas most exposed
to decay and dealing and involved General Prevention teams, two Lazio Crime
Prevention patrols, a State Police dog unit, Guardia di Finanza officers and
Local Police officers. [@1] Outside the minimarket in via Verdi officers
identified ten foreign citizens who were all already known to police. [@1] In
the Don Minzoni and Sacra Famiglia area officers administratively reported three
people for personal possession, a 20-year-old resident in Alatri and a
30-year-old resident in Rome each holding about 0,6 grams and an undocumented
teenager holding 0,3 grams who was later entrusted to his guardians. [@1] In the
viale Spagna area known as Casermone officers found about 15 grams of hashish
belonging to a local man who was reported to the Prefecture with seizure of the
drug. [@1]

The operation seized about 30 grams of hashish in total. [@1] That total
included 0,80 grams of hashish found abandoned on the ground and seized against
unknown persons outside the via Verdi shop. [@1] It also included four abandoned
wraps found between via Don Minzoni and via Sacra Famiglia containing about 13
grams of hashish seized against unknown persons. [@1] The minimarket in via
Verdi was found without a fiscal measurer installed and without separation
between food and non-food products. [@1] A second minimarket in Corso della
Repubblica was sanctioned for lacking the obligatory telematic recorder. [@2]

The evening ended with joint checkpoints along the main roads of the town to
ensure circulation safety and compliance with the Highway Code. [@1] Further
services were announced for the coming days. [@1] A second account put the
seizure toll at about 29 grams of hashish with three people reported for
personal possession and fiscal irregularities in two minimarkets. [@2] Police
announced that further extraordinary services will be carried out in the coming
days. [@3]
