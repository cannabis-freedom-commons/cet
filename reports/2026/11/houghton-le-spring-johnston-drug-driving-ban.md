A drug-driver caught with cocaine and cannabis above the legal limit in a North
East town has been banned for two years. [@1] Kyle Johnston, 22, of Rose Avenue,
Houghton-le-Spring, admitted two drug-driving offences at Peterlee Magistrates'
Court on November 13. [@1] He pleaded guilty to driving with both controlled
drugs above their specified limits. [@1] His guilty pleas were taken into
account by the court. [@1]

The court heard Johnston was driving a Benelli motorcycle on Black Boy Road,
Houghton-le-Spring, on March 20. [@1] Tests showed he had no less than 135
micrograms of cocaine per litre of blood and 6.6 micrograms of cannabis per
litre of blood. [@1]

Magistrates disqualified Johnston from driving for 24 months and fined him £200
for the cocaine offence. [@1] No separate penalty was imposed for the cannabis
offence. [@1] Johnston was also ordered to pay £85 prosecution costs and an £80
surcharge. [@1]
