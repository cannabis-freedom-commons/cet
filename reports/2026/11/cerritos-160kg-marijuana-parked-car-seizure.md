Officers found 22 packages containing 160 kilograms of marijuana in the car.
[@1] No one was arrested in this procedure. [@1] The police did not say who had
driven the car before it was found parked beside the road, or how long it had
been there. [@1] The checks were carried out by the traffic and transport
section of the Risaralda police. [@1]

The car was found on the corridor linking Andalucía with La Ye de Cerritos, one
of the access routes to the Pereira metropolitan area from Valle del Cauca. [@1]
Officers inspected the vehicle after identifying possible changes to the numbers
used to establish its legal identity. [@1] A police report said the load had
probably left Cauca department for Pereira, but that route was only an initial
hypothesis and did not establish who owned the substance. [@1] Police put the
marijuana's value at more than 160 million pesos and its equivalent at about
160,000 doses. [@1] The two operations took place within a span of hours and
were estimated to represent a loss of more than 170 million pesos to criminal
organisations. [@3] Both results came from road controls by the Risaralda
traffic police to detect the movement of drugs. [@2]

In a separate procedure during the same controls, officers found cocaine in a
bus passenger's luggage and arrested the woman. [@1] Later judicial information
placed that bus procedure on 18 November 2026, during a check on a Cali–Medellín
bus. [@1] Although both cargoes were found on the same corridor, the account
says there is no confirmed information linking them to one network or to the
people involved. [@1]
