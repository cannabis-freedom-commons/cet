Kollam city's District Anti-Narcotic Special Action Force (DANSAF) arrested a
31-year-old repeat offender, identified as Tom, who ran a men's hostel at Vikas
Nagar near TKM College in Karicode. [@2,3] The DANSAF squad raided the hostel on
Saturday afternoon, 21 November 2026, and arrested him. [@2]

Officers seized 5.21 grams of MDMA and charas, a cannabis resin, from his
possession. [@2] They also found various tools used for drug consumption. [@2]
Police valued the MDMA and charas together at up to ₹10,000. [@2] The Malayalam
report put the seized MDMA and charas at 5.20 grams. [@1,3]

Police said the synthetic drug was stocked for distribution mainly to college
students in the Karicode area. [@2] Investigators said Tom had been selling MDMA
at ₹6,000 to ₹7,000 a gram. [@2] He had been under DANSAF surveillance for
several days after a confidential tip to Kollam city police chief Aswathy Gigi.
[@2,3] Police said Tom had been arrested by Kollam East police in 2022 with 58
grams of MDMA. [@2] He was handed over to Kilikolloor police, where a case was
registered under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act. [@2]
