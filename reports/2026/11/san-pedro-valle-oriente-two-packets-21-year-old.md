A 21-year-old man was detained by the Policía Vial de San Pedro Garza García
after officers found two envelopes of herb resembling marijuana in the Valle
Oriente neighbourhood. [@1] The man was identified as Martí "N", 21 years old.
[@1] The encounter took place on Avenida Lázaro Cárdenas near Reyes Ferreira
street, where the officer saw the man crossing the avenue without precautions
and stepping into the path of vehicles, putting himself and other drivers at
risk. [@2] According to the municipal police report, the man began insulting the
officer and was told he would be detained for a suspected breach of the
municipal Civic Justice Regulations. [@1]

During the intervention the officer detected a characteristic smell of cannabis,
which led to an inspection of the young man's belongings. [@1] The police force
said the search found two transparent plastic bags of green and dried herb with
characteristics similar to marijuana. [@1] The man was suspected of probable
involvement in offences against health, and the envelopes were secured for the
corresponding inquiries. [@1]

The municipal Public Security Secretariat did not give the quantity or weight of
the substance and did not say whether it had been tested to confirm its
composition. [@1] After the seizure, municipal authorities began the
corresponding procedure to determine the man's legal situation. [@1] The
investigation is to establish the nature of the substance and whether there is
sufficient evidence of criminal conduct. [@1]
