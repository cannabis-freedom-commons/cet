A man identified only as A was sentenced to 1 year 6 months in prison by the
Chuncheon District Court's 2nd Criminal Division, presided over by Kim Sung-rae,
for violating the Narcotics Control Act and fraud. [@1] The court ordered him to
complete 40 hours of drug-addiction rehabilitation education and to forfeit
16.35 million won (1635만원). [@1]

The judgment said that in April 2025 A took delivery of liquid cannabis smuggled
from Thailand and distributed it on the instructions of a drug distribution
organisation. [@1] The organisation had divided the work between a boss
supplying cannabis in Thailand, a middle manager, an overseas carrier and a
domestic carrier. [@1] On 15 April the overseas carrier received about 200 ml of
liquid cannabis in shampoo bottles in Phuket, Thailand, and concealed them with
toiletries in a suitcase. [@1] The next day A arrived at Incheon International
Airport and collected the shampoo bottles and toiletries in the arrivals hall
men's restroom on the middle manager's instructions. [@1] On 25 April A divided
the liquid cannabis among three plastic bottles of 50 ml and 100 ml and wrapped
them in black insulation tape. [@1] He hid them in a villa parking lot and
flower beds in Seoul's Gangseo District for buyers to find in a so-called
'throw' handover, and about 200 ml of liquid cannabis was distributed. [@1]

A was also charged with fraud over 3.86 million won (386만원) obtained from a
junior soldier in six payments in July 2024 while serving in the Army. [@1] In a
separate strand, he bought about 1 g of methamphetamine from a Telegram seller
on 16 April 2025 using 350,000 won worth of cryptocurrency. [@1] The next day he
injected about 0.3 g of the methamphetamine himself, injected the same amount
into an acquaintance and gave the acquaintance another 0.4 g. [@1] Separately, A
had already been sentenced to 5 years by the Seoul High Court's Chuncheon
division for cannabis importation; that judgment became final on 27 August and
he is serving it. [@1]
