Carabinieri arrested four people and reported two others after an operation at
the Parco Monaco housing estate in Melito, along corso Europa. [@1] The four
arrested were Angelo Sarno, 50, of Melito; Vincenzo Russo, 36, of Afragola;
Domenico D'Ausilio, 51, of Giugliano; and Francesco Maiorano, 25, of Melito.
[@2] The four were accused of possessing drugs for sale. [@4] After processing
they were taken to Poggioreale prison. [@3] Carabinieri from the Marano
company's operational section, supported by the local Melito tenenza, carried
out the operation. [@2]

Officers watched a steady flow of people around the apartment blocks before
moving in, judging it consistent with retail drug sales. [@1] Officers seized
more than 400 grams of marijuana and more than 40 grams of cocaine, already
divided into doses for sale. [@1] They also recovered 1,200 euros in cash, which
investigators believe may be proceeds of the drug trade. [@1] Three people
identified as buyers were referred to the Prefecture as drug users. [@4]

A 31-year-old identified as the alleged 'palo', a lookout, was reported at
liberty after escaping during the operation, and a 54-year-old woman was
reported on suspicion of helping one of the suspects. [@2] The Parco Monaco site
along corso Europa is described as a long-standing drug market linked to the
Amato-Pagano clan. [@1]
