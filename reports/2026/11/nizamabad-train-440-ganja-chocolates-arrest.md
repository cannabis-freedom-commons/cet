Excise officials caught a man moving ganja chocolates from Odisha to Nizamabad
district. [@1] Officers carried out special Saturday checks on the
Sambalpur-to-Nanded Nanded-Valsad Express. [@1] Officers searching the bag of
passenger Fakir Badai found 440 ganja chocolates. [@2]

Officials said inquiry showed he had brought the chocolates from Sambalpur in
Odisha to the Kammarapalli mandal village in Nizamabad district where he worked.
[@1] Eenadu reported he had lived for more than two years in Kammarapalli mandal
and worked as a daily-wage labourer. [@2]

The accused was handed to the Nizamabad-2 police station SHO for further action.
[@1] Officials said a case was to be registered and investigation taken up. [@1]
Inspectors said the accused was sent for remand. [@2] Eenadu described the check
as an 11 a.m. Saturday passenger check when the Nagavali Express arrived at the
railway station. [@2]
