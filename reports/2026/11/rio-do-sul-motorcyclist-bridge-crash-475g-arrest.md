A 22-year-old motorcyclist was arrested on suspicion of drug trafficking in the
afternoon of Thursday 19 November 2026 in Rio do Sul after fleeing a Guarda
Municipal approach and crashing into the side of the Ponte Ingomar Bachmann.
[@1] The pursuit began on Avenida Oscar Barcelos, where guards saw a motorcycle
without rear-view mirrors and the rider ignored an order to stop, accelerating
away. [@1] Over about five kilometres he ran a red light, made dangerous
overtakes and passed near schools, the Guarda Municipal said. [@1]

In Bairro Bremer he entered the Ponte Ingomar Bachmann against the traffic flow,
lost control and hit the side of the structure. [@1] He suffered abrasions and a
bleeding wound on one arm and was given first aid before being taken to Hospital
Regional Alto Vale. [@1] After the fall he threw a package toward the riverbank
and tried to run, but guards caught him. [@1]

During a search of the riverbank guards found the package, which held a 475-gram
tablet of a substance similar to marijuana. [@2] After medical treatment he was
presented to the police authority for a flagrante arrest on suspicion of drug
trafficking. [@1] The motorcycle was removed to the yard because of the traffic
offences committed during the pursuit. [@3]
