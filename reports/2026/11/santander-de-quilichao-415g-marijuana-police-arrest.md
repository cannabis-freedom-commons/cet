Police in Santander de Quilichao, Cauca, caught a man in the act during patrol
work at calle 3 with carrera 12 and held him on suspicion of trafficking,
manufacturing and possession of narcotics. [@1] The Cauca Police Department said
the force was the Policía Nacional and that the man was held over the suspected
offence of tráfico, fabricación o porte de estupefacientes. [@2]

Officers searched him and found a transparent plastic bag and nine paper
wrappers holding what the news account described as a plant substance resembling
marijuana. [@1] Police put the weight at 415 grams of marijuana found inside a
bag. [@2]

The man and the seized substance were handed to the Fiscalía General de la
Nación, Colombia's attorney general's office, to answer for the alleged offence.
[@1] The police statement said the man and the seized substance were placed at
the disposal of the competent authorities. [@2] Police said they would keep
strengthening surveillance, searches and checks across Santander de Quilichao to
counter drug trafficking. [@1]
