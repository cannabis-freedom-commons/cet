Nine alleged members of 'Los Box' were captured in Oiba and Socorro, Santander.
[@1] The nine detainees were three women and six men. [@2] Six of them were
detained on a judicial order and three were caught in flagrante delicto. [@1]
Among those captured was alias 'El Buchón', whom investigators identified as the
group's presumed leader. [@1] Alias 'El Buchón' had two prior judicial records,
for drug trafficking in 2022 and theft in 2001. [@2]

Officers carried out six search and inspection warrants, making the nine arrests
on suspicion of trafficking, manufacturing or possession of narcotics. [@1] The
operation followed almost a year of intelligence work coordinated by Colombia's
National Police, the Attorney General's Office and the National Army. [@1]
Investigators used telephone interception, an undercover agent and analysis of
audiovisual material. [@1] Officers seized narcotics including marijuana and
cocaine, plus cash that authorities said came from the sales. [@1] Police put
the group's criminal income near seven million pesos a week. [@1] The group's
income was also reported as up to one million pesos a day, about 30 million
pesos a month. [@2]

The group sold marijuana and cocaine directly hand to hand and by motorcycle
delivery in Oiba and Socorro. [@1] Colonel Luis Fernando Atuesta Zárate,
commander of the Santander Police Department, said the operation struck drug
distribution networks in the south of the department. [@1] Police reported the
network dismantled and said the detainees would answer to the courts as
proceedings continue. [@1]
