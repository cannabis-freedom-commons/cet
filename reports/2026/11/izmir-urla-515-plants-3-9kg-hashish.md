One suspect was caught in the act and taken into custody during a
drone-supported gendarmerie search for cannabis in Urla, in İzmir province. [@1]
The anti-narcotics branch of the İzmir Provincial Gendarmerie Command acted on
information that cannabis was being grown at a spot in Zeytinler Mahallesi, a
neighbourhood of the Urla district. [@1] The search was carried out on 26
November, the day before the account of it was published. [@1]

The officers seized 515 cannabis plants and 3,942 grams of kubar esrar, a
pressed cannabis resin sold as hashish. [@1] The Anadolu Agency's account gave
the same totals: 515 plants and 3 kilograms 942 grams of resin. [@2] That agency
described the site as rural land in Zeytinler Mahallesi where hemp was being
grown. [@2]

The detained suspect's procedures were still under way, the Anadolu Agency
reported. [@2]
