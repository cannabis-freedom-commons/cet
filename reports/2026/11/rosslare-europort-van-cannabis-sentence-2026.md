Wexford Circuit Court Judge Cormac Quinn said Rosslare Europort is at the heart
of drug smuggling into Ireland after jailing another importer. [@1] The man
presiding over Wexford Circuit Court made his remark after sending yet another
importer of illegal substances to jail. [@1] Polish national Andrzej Klimczak,
aged 29, admitted having more than a quarter of a million euro worth of cannabis
in his Renault Master van. [@1] When the case came to court he pleaded guilty
after making admissions in his sixth interview by gardai. [@1] Judge Quinn
handed him a three and a half year prison sentence with the final nine months
suspended. [@1]

The offence was brought to light by Customs and Excise officers on 17 February
2025. [@1] The light goods vehicle was examined after being chosen at random for
a search after arriving at the port on the DFDS ferry from Dunkirk. [@1] The 26
bags of cannabis were concealed in boxes which Klimczak had collected in the
Netherlands. [@1] He at first told investigators that he believed the boxes
contained electronics or car parts. [@1]

While on bail the accused had been living in Westmeath. [@1] Because he was
unable to leave the country as a condition of bail, his wife and two young
children had joined him in Ireland. [@1] Detective Garda Colm Dunne accepted
that the defendant stood to earn just 1,500 euro for the delivery run. [@1]
