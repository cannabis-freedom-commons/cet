Papua regional police's narcotics directorate arrested 32-year-old MSD in Apo
Bengkel, Bhayangkara ward, North Jayapura district, Jayapura City, at about
21:30 WIT on Thursday 19 November 2026. [@1] Police acted on a public tip that a
man held ganja he planned to carry to Sorong regency by sea on a PT Pelni ship.
[@1] Officers raided and searched the boarding house where MSD lived in Apo
Bengkel RT/RW 002/002, Bhayangkara ward. [@1]

On the mattress they found a black bag marked POLO GS holding 10 large clear
plastic packets of suspected ganja. [@2] On the front balcony of the second
floor they found a ROBONGHOLO cardboard box wrapped in a large red plastic bag
containing 78 more large clear plastic packets also believed to hold ganja. [@1]
Papua narcotics director Kombes Pol Alfian said the suspect and all the evidence
had been secured at the Ditresnarkoba office for further legal process. [@1]

Officers also seized one rolled ganja cigarette. [@1] A purple Vivo Y17s phone
was seized as evidence. [@1] A black Samsung Galaxy A07 phone was also seized.
[@1] MSD was accused under Article 114(1) and/or Article 111(1) of Law 35/2009
on Narcotics, as adjusted by Law 1/2023 and Law 1/2026. [@1]
