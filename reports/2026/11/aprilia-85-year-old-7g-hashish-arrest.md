Carabinieri of the Reparto Territoriale di Aprilia, the carabinieri's
territorial unit, arrested an 85-year-old man on suspicion of possessing
narcotics for the purpose of dealing. [@1,4] The man was an Italian citizen
already known to the forces of law and order for specific prior offences. [@1,4]
The episode took place on the evening of 19 November, when men of the
Radiomobile section of the Aprilia territorial unit stopped him in via della
Meccanica while he was driving his own car. [@2,4]

His evident agitation prompted the carabinieri to carry out a personal and
vehicle search. [@1,4] Officers found a package containing about 80 grams of
cocaine during the check. [@1,4] The checks continued at the man's home, where
carabinieri found a further 7 grams of hashish. [@3,4] All the substances were
seized and were to undergo laboratory tests ordered by the Latina prosecutor's
office in the coming days. [@1,4]

After the formalities a coercive measure was applied pending the rito
direttissimo, a fast-track hearing, as ordered by the duty prosecutor of the
Pontine prosecutor's office. [@2,4] The proceedings remained at the preliminary
investigation stage, with the presumption of innocence until any final
conviction. [@2,4]
