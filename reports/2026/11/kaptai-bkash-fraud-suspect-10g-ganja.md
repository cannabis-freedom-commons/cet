Border Guard Bangladesh soldiers detained a man named Suman at the 41 BGB Wagga
checkpoint in Kaptai, Rangamati district, on Saturday 21 November 2026. [@1,3]
They recognised him while searching a Dhaka-bound vehicle, matching him to a
photograph the owner of a local bKash shop had circulated after a man took Tk
35,000 there without paying. [@1,3] The bKash shop was Manchitra Mobile Telecom at Kaptai New Market. [@3] The soldiers held him and handed him to Kaptai
police station. [@1,3] Dainik Azadi named him as Motaleb Hossain Suman, 35, of the
Kaptai BFIDC industrial area, and reported that police sent him to Rangamati
district jail with the ganja the same day. [@2]

Police found 10 grams of ganja when they searched his body after taking him into
custody. [@1,3] Police filed a case against him under the Narcotics Control Act
over the ganja and sent him to court. [@1,3] The shopkeeper and the man settled
the bKash money, so no complaint was filed over it. [@1,3]

Kaptai police station chief Sheikh Mahmudul Hasan Rubel confirmed the case and
said no complaint had been received over the bKash transaction. [@1,3] Local
sources told the outlet that Suman had been arrested before in a
counterfeit-currency case and was on bail in it. [@1,3]
