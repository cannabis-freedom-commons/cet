Four people detained during a search of a home in the Luis Donaldo Colosio
neighbourhood of Playa del Carmen were bound over for trial on suspicion of drug
offences. [@1] They were identified as Juana "N", Gerardo "N", Jesús "N" and
Arturo "N" and charged with possessing narcotics for commercial purposes, in the
modality of sale, specifically methamphetamine, cocaine and cannabis. [@1]

The investigation began after an anonymous email complaint reached the guard of
the Federal Ministerial Police (PFM), warning of presumed drug sales at a home
in Playa del Carmen. [@1] The Federal Public Prosecutor attached to the Regional
Control Specialised Prosecutor's Office (FECOR) requested a judicial search
order, executed with support from PFM officers. [@1] During the search officers
detained the four people and seized 32 grams 9 milligrams of methamphetamine, 1
gram 26 milligrams of cocaine and 195 grams 4 milligrams of marihuana. [@1] The
detainees and the seized substances were placed at the disposal of the federal
authority for the investigation to continue. [@1]

The federal prosecutor presented evidence to the control judge, who bound the
four over for trial, and the judicial proceedings continue. [@1] The FGR
recalled that under the presumption of innocence the accused remain innocent
until a competent court issues a conviction. [@1]
