Troops of 34 Artillery Brigade intercepted a vehicle carrying about 169 kg of
suspected cannabis in Ihitte Uboma Local Government Area, Imo State. [@1] The
interception followed intelligence received on 21 November 2026, after which
troops mounted a snap checkpoint in the area. [@1] Troops tried to stop the
vehicle for a routine check, but the driver refused to halt. [@1] Soldiers fired
at the vehicle's tyres and pursued it until it was forced to stop about 10
kilometres ahead of their initial position. [@1]

About four occupants got out of the vehicle and opened fire on the troops, who
returned fire before the suspects fled. [@1] A search of the vehicle recovered
15 bags containing approximately 169 kg of suspected cannabis. [@1] The vehicle
and substance were handed to the Imo State Command of the National Drug Law
Enforcement Agency (NDLEA) on 22 November for further investigation. [@1] The
suspects remained at large, and the recovered exhibits were placed in the
custody of the anti-narcotics agency. [@1]
