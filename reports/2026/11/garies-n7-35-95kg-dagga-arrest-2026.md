Garies while he was travelling toward Cape Town. [@1] He was stopped on a farm
road near Garies while en route to Cape Town after a tip-off to local police.
[@1] Thompson was the sole occupant of the vehicle. [@1] Thompson appeared
briefly in the Springbok magistrate’s court on Friday, 20 November. [@1] He was
identified in court as Lee Thompson (37). [@2] The case was postponed to 26
