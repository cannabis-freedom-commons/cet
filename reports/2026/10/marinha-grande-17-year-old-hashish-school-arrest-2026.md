A 17-year-old student was arrested beside the school he attends in Marinha
Grande on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@1] The arrest took place on 26
October 2026, the PSP's Leiria district command said. [@2] Officers were
patrolling around schools in the town after several alerts from the school
community. [@1] They found a group of people consuming drugs in a public street
and stopped them for identification. [@1]

One of the two 17-year-olds identified had 1.32 grams of hashish, and the other
had 11.88 grams of the same substance in six bags. [@3] The youth with the six
bags was arrested in flagrante delito on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@2] The
other youth, also 17, was identified on suspicion of drug consumption. [@2]

The arrested youth was made an arguido, the formal suspect status in Portugal,
and placed under a termo de identidade e residência (TIR), an
identity-and-residence order requiring him to keep his address and appear when
summoned. [@2] The case was reported to the judicial authorities and continues
at the Marinha Grande DIAP, the public prosecutor's criminal investigation
department. [@1]

The PSP said alerts from citizens and institutions through official channels
help direct policing. [@1] It stressed cooperation between police, schools,
families and the local community in identifying and preventing risks to young
people. [@1]
