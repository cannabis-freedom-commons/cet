The case concerned a resident of the village of Kudara-Somon in Kyakhtinsky
district. [@2] He was found guilty of illegal acquisition, storage and transport
of narcotics without intent to sell on a large scale under part 2 of Article 228
of the Russian Criminal Code. [@2] The court sentenced him to three years'
imprisonment, suspended with a two-year probation period. [@2]

In September 2026 he drove his Lada Kalina to a field, where he gathered 419
grams of marijuana and kept it in the car's cabin. [@2] The material was
described as parts of wild cannabis he had collected in the field. [@1] The next
day, on the road toward Bichursky district, police stopped him and found and
seized the substance during an inspection of the car. [@2]

The court rejected the defence argument that the car was needed for housekeeping
and ordered it confiscated to the state. [@2] The confiscated vehicle was
identified as his Lada Kalina. [@3] The verdict had not entered into legal
force. [@2]
