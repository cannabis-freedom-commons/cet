Drug-control officers in the Republic of Adygea received operative information
that a 56-year-old resident of a settlement in Krasnogvardeysky District was
producing prohibited substances. [@1] The tip was confirmed when police found a
large batch of cannabis herb at the man's place of residence. [@1] The man was
detained. [@1]

In a woodshed on the household property, officers found drying cannabis bushes
and plant parts, while inside the dwelling they found ready-to-use marijuana.
[@1] Specialists of the regional police expert centre established the total mass
of the seized cannabis at about 12 kg. [@1] He explained that he had grown the
prohibited plants on his household plot, mowed them in the autumn and put them
in the shed to dry in order to later make a narcotic from the harvest for
personal use. [@1]

Investigators of the Investigative Department of the MVD for the Republic of
Adygea opened a criminal case against him under part 2 of article 228 of the
Criminal Code of the Russian Federation. [@1] He was placed in a detention
isolator. [@1] The investigation into his unlawful activity is continuing. [@1]
