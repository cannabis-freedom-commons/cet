A Moberly man, Michael Wolf, faces a delivery charge after troopers said he
drove the wrong way on Highway 63 with marijuana in his vehicle. [@1] Just after
midnight on Sunday 25 October 2026, highway patrol troopers were called out to
find a driver going the wrong way on Highway 63 north of Moberly. [@1] Randolph
County deputies found the driver south of the junction with Business 63. [@1] In
court documents, a trooper said the driver, Michael Wolf, seemed intoxicated.
[@1] The trooper arrested Wolf, and the documents state that his blood alcohol
content was 0.10%. [@1]

Searching the vehicle, the trooper said they found 521 grams of pot, $2,103 in
cash, a marijuana pipe and alcoholic beverages. [@1] Prosecutors charged Wolf
with Deliver Controlled Substance. [@1]
