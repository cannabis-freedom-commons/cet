Officers of Uzbekistan's State Security Service (SGB) stopped a Kokand resident
during an operation in the city and seized 200 grams of hashish. [@1] The
resident, born in 1976, was carrying the hashish and 1,092 tablets of Regapen, a
strong-acting medicine, in a bag. [@2]

The SGB press service said it had identified people involved in the illegal
circulation of strong-acting medicines. [@2] It said a criminal case had been
opened over the Kokand find and that investigative actions were continuing. [@1]

The SGB reported three raids in the two regions, in which Regapen, Tropicamide
and hashish were seized and criminal cases were opened in all three. [@3] In
Izbaskan district, Andijan region, officers stopped a Chevrolet Lacetti heading
for Tashkent and found 1,400 Regapen tablets. [@1] In Uchkuprik district,
Fergana region, they found 99 units of Tropicamide in a VAZ-21017 travelling
from Margilan to Kokand. [@1] Only the 200 grams of hashish was cannabis; the
other two seizures were of strong-acting medicines. [@1]
