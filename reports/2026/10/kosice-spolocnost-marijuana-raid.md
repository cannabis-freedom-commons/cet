Slovak police launched a large anti-drug operation codenamed Spoločnosť in
Košice and its surroundings from the morning of Tuesday 27 October 2026, aimed
at organised drug crime allegedly involving several people. [@2] Three suspects
were detained at that stage, the police Presidium spokesman said. [@3] The
operation was carried out by the National Anti-Drug Unit of the Presidium of the
Police Force and targeted organised drug crime in which several people were
suspected of taking part. [@1] An investigator later charged four men aged 34 to
44, and one of them faces up to life imprisonment if convicted. [@1]

During the raid officers made five house searches, seven searches of other
premises and three personal searches. [@1] Alongside a larger quantity of
methamphetamine, officers seized marijuana, €100,000 in cash, investment gold
and two cars, which police said might be linked to proceeds of crime. [@1]

The National Anti-Drug Unit said the action confirmed that drug crime was no
longer just street dealing but involved organised groups importing, storing and
distributing drugs and laundering the proceeds. [@1] Police said the
investigation was continuing and that they would not comment further for now.
[@1] The spokesman said the action concerned drug crime allegedly committed by
several people within Košice city and its vicinity. [@4]
