Military Police in Flórida Paulista, in inland São Paulo state, apprehended two
boys aged 14 and 16 on Monday 26 October 2026 over an infraction analogous to
drug trafficking. [@1] The case began when the mother of the 14-year-old found
portions of marijuana with her son and reported it to the Conselho Tutelar, the
municipal child-protection council. [@1] The 25th Interior Military Police
Battalion was sent through the Copom operations centre to support the council.
[@3]

During the inquiries officers received information about the possible
involvement of the 16-year-old. [@1] At an abandoned house on Rua das Araras in
the Bela Vista neighbourhood, the team found two black fanny packs containing 92
portions of marijuana weighing 114.7 grams in total. [@2] Police said the
portions were packed for commercial sale. [@1] A Samsung mobile phone was also
seized at the property. [@2]

The two boys were taken to the Civil Police station in Flórida Paulista, where
the case was presented to the police authority. [@1] The case was referred for
the steps set out in the Statute of the Child and Adolescent (ECA), Brazil's
framework for proceedings against minors. [@3]
