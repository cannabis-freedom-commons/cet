Mons-Quévy police detained five people over two days in mid-October 2026 during
patrols by its Team ProA unit. [@1] The first intervention was on Wednesday 14
October, when officers recognised a man who was the subject of a police alert.
[@1] He fled but was caught, and officers found several pellets of cocaine on
him. [@1] Police searched his home and, because he was staying in Belgium
illegally, deprived him of liberty and transferred him to a closed centre. [@2]

On Thursday 15 October officers saw a drug sale taking place and detained four
suspects, all of whom were deprived of liberty. [@1] Two searches that day
seized 32.39 grams of cannabis and about €2,035 in cash. [@1] Officers also
seized four mobile phones, a precision scale and packaging material. [@1] Three
of the four suspects detained on 15 October received a written charge for an
offence against the drugs law. [@1] The fourth, who was staying in the country
illegally, was transferred to a closed centre. [@1] Across the two days the
operation involved three searches, counting the home search on 14 October. [@3]

The Mons-Quévy police zone and the Mons prosecutor's office jointly announced
the operation and said drug trafficking remains one of their priorities. [@1]
They said they rely on patrol-led interventions and targeted checks against
street crime. [@1]
