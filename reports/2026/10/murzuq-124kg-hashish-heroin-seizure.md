Officers of the Criminal Investigation Department of the Murzuq Security
Directorate intercepted a drug shipment in the Al-Arbaeen area that was on its
way to Sebha. [@2] The vehicle was a Honda driven by a Libyan national who lives
in Ghat, and he was detained with the seized goods for legal procedures. [@2]

Inspectors searching the vehicle found 124.5 kilograms of hashish hidden in the
vehicle's cargo box. [@2] The same search also found 11 kilograms of heroin
hidden with the hashish, which is not counted in the cannabis figure. [@1]

A second account dates the operation to 1 October and describes the vehicle as a
Hyundai pickup carrying a Libyan man, cannabis resin and heroin en route to
Sebha. [@3] The authorities held the suspect and the drugs pending legal
procedures, and no court outcome has been reported. [@2]
