The Audiencia Provincial de Cuenca confirmed a two-year-and-two-month prison
sentence for a man found with cannabis and weapons at his home in the city of
Cuenca. [@1] The first-instance court had sentenced him to eight months in
prison and a 2,000-euro fine for a public-health offence, applying an analogous
drug-addiction mitigation. [@1] It added one year and six months in prison for
unlawful possession of weapons, making two years and two months in total. [@1]
The appeal ruling dated 15 October is not final. [@1] Cassation before the
Second Chamber of the Supreme Court for infringement of law remains available
within five days of the last notification. [@1]

The case began on the afternoon of 1 November 2023, when National Police
officers came to the accused's Cuenca home after his then-partner reported being
a victim of gender violence. [@1] Inside the bag he had been carrying, officers
found four jars with vegetable matter, three glass and one plastic, plus a
wooden box. [@1] Officers also found two weapons: an Astra 1921 pistol with an
unloaded magazine and a Gamo PX-107 compressed-air pistol. [@1] The next day,
after judicial authorisation, officers searched the home and found more
substances, two 9-millimetre cartridges, handwritten sheets and two agendas with
names and quantities in the living room. [@1] One note expressly read «última
venta». [@1] In the bedroom officers found a Trankimazin bottle, another
container with 50 pills, a powder substance and another notebook with names and
references. [@1] Analysis found 370.4 grams of cannabis, another 9.01 grams of
cannabis, 5.06 grams of cannabis resin and 25.99 grams of alprazolam. [@1] The
judgment valued all the substances together at 2,758.71 euros and found they
were for sale to third persons. [@1]

An expert report found the seized Astra pistol in working order and capable of
firing, with a magazine holding eight cartridges and two 9x23-millimetre
projectiles in usable condition. [@1] The court rejected a personal-use
explanation, finding trafficking proven not only by quantity but by the
name-and-quantity sheets and agendas, the «última venta» reference and
psychotropics above self-consumption without prescription. [@1] With those
arguments, the court dismissed the appeal in full and confirmed the conviction
imposed on 7 April 2026. [@1] The ruling rejected both extending the
drug-addiction mitigation to the weapons offence and the undue-delay claim. [@1]
