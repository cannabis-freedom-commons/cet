Campbell County Sheriff's Office deputies arrested 20-year-old Khristian Lee
Anderson of Lynchburg on outstanding warrants on Saturday, October 17. [@1] At
the time of his arrest he was suffering from a gunshot wound to his leg that he
reportedly sustained days earlier after accidentally discharging a firearm. [@1]
Deputies executed search warrants on October 18, 2026, at a home and a vehicle
associated with Anderson, the day after his arrest. [@2]

The sheriff's office said the investigation led to the seizure of suspected
marijuana products, guns and more than $10,000. [@1] Among the seized items were
two firearms, a Draco-style firearm and a Glock handgun equipped with a switch
designed to enable automatic fire, along with ammunition. [@1] Deputies seized
more than $10,000 in U.S. currency. [@1] They also seized a large quantity of
suspected counterfeit U.S. currency. [@1] The suspected marijuana was described
as a substantial quantity of plant material, vaping products and edible
products. [@1]

Anderson was charged with possession with intent to distribute marijuana. [@1]
The sheriff's office said the investigation was still ongoing and additional
charges might be added. [@1]
