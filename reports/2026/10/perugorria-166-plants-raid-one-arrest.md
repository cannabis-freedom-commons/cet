Corrientes police raided a rural property at Paraje Rincón Quiroz, outside
Perugorría, on the afternoon of Thursday 15 October 2026 and detained one man.
[@2] The raid followed about six weeks of intelligence work and was carried out
under Argentina's federal drug law 23.737 and the provincial narcomenudeo law
6.725. [@2] The Criminal Investigation Division of Curuzú Cuatiá carried out the
raid, with prosecutor Oscar Cañete and the head of Regional Unit III present at
the property. [@3] The narcotics case and a parallel inquiry into wildlife
remains were still in progress. [@5]

Officers seized 166 marijuana plants being grown for sale, with a gross weight
of 38 kg. [@3] They also took 1.915 kg of cannabis seeds and 13 wrappings of
marijuana that weighed 287 grams in total. [@2] Six long firearms, ammunition, 9
mm magazines, two precision scales, 120,000 pesos in cash and digital cameras
were seized in the house. [@3] Investigators also seized a Toyota Hilux pickup
that police said had been bought with drug proceeds and used to distribute
cannabis through narcodelivery. [@3] Corrientes Hoy put the value of what was
seized at close to 218 million pesos. [@1] El Diario de Curuzú reported a police
valuation of 180,840,000 pesos for the same seizure. [@2]

Police described the raid as the dismantling of a kiosco narco, the local term
for a small drug-selling point, with a large Cannabis sativa plantation on the
property. [@2] The sweep of the property confirmed the plantation, and
investigators concluded the site was used to grow and prepare marijuana for
sale. [@2] A separate case was opened over about 32 kg of deer antlers, two
freezers and cuts of wild animal meat found during the same search. [@4]
