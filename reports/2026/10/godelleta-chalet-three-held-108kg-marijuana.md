A fugitive with three outstanding judicial claims and two orders for immediate
imprisonment was arrested at a chalet in Godelleta, where police say he had set
up a point for producing and selling drugs. [@1] Officers also arrested his
partner and another man who lived with them. [@1] The alleged ringleader was
detained on suspicion of drug trafficking, document falsification and the
outstanding judicial claims, and a judge ordered him imprisoned. [@1]

Investigators from the UDYCO drugs and organised crime group in Valencia found
5.6 kilograms of heroin, 4.8 of cocaine and 10.8 of marijuana, along with two
rooms used for indoor cultivation, seven large plants outside and areas for
drying and packaging drugs. [@1] They also seized two machetes, an axe, a
high-capacity magazine, 20 rounds of 9mm ammunition and a Romanian identity card
in another person's name but bearing the principal suspect's photograph. [@1]

The house stood in a hard-to-reach mountainous area reached by dirt roads and
was guarded by cameras that triggered alarms when anyone approached and by four
large dogs. [@1] Because of these security measures, the search on 27 October
was led by the GOES special security operational group with support from the
dog-handling unit. [@1]
