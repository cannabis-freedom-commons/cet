A 24-year-old man with no criminal record was sent to trial for repeated
high-risk drug trafficking in cocaine and for holding cannabis for sale. [@1]
The Bucharest Tribunal sentenced him to 5 years in prison for continued
trafficking in risk and high-risk drugs in four material acts. [@1] Before the
court he admitted the acts as described in the indictment and asked for the
simplified procedure. [@1]

On 19 June 2025 he sold a protected collaborator two doses of cocaine totalling
0.88 grams for 800 lei. [@1] On 25 September 2025 he sold the collaborator one
dose of cocaine of 0.35 grams for 400 lei. [@1] On 31 October 2025 in the 23
district of Bucharest on Hășmașului street he sold the collaborator one dose of
0.50 grams for 800 lei. [@1] On 19 November 2025 a home search at the dwelling
he used found 0.76 grams of cannabis, a precision scale, a grinder and a metal
pipe. [@1] The court rejected his claim that the plant matter was for personal
use and held it was held for sale. [@1] A computer search supported that
conclusion by showing frequent drug-trafficking activity, mainly involving
cannabis. [@1] Laboratory analyses confirmed THC in the grinder and cocaine in
the pipe. [@1]

The investigation had started after a 14 April 2025 tip that he was selling
cocaine and cannabis in Bucharest. [@1] An undercover investigator and a
protected-identity collaborator were introduced and authorized drug purchases
were organized. [@1] An additional authorized purchase on 22 July 2025, paid
with 800 lei, was excluded from the four acts after laboratory analysis found no
psychoactive substance. [@1] The Bucharest Tribunal's First Criminal Section
gave judgment 1427/2026 on 30 October 2026. [@1] The court held that despite his
sincere attitude before the judge, the repetition and organization of
procurement and sale justified a prison term. [@1]
