Railway Protection Force officers seized 11 kilograms of cannabis at Ernakulam
South railway station in Kochi. [@1] The cannabis was found under the footbridge
on the south side of platform 6, after the train from Shalimar in West Bengal to
Thiruvananthapuram had left the station. [@1] It was packed in six packets
inside two shoulder bags. [@1] The check was led by inspector A.J. Jipin of the
RPF Crime Intelligence Branch. [@1] Officers indicated that whoever carried the
bags abandoned them and left when they saw the check. [@1]

Assistant sub-inspectors J.P. Anilkumar, Sijo Xavier and Vijayakumar, head
constable Vipin and constables Manoj, K. Sunish and Kalaiselvan were also part
of the team. [@1] The cannabis was handed over to the railway police. [@1]

RPF Thiruvananthapuram senior divisional security commissioner Muhammed Haneef
said the force had seized 150 kilograms of cannabis carried on trains in the
previous 30 days. [@1] He said 10 accused had been arrested in that period. [@1]
He said surveillance and checks would continue at the same intensity. [@1]
