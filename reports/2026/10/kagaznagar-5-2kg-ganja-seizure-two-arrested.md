Police checks at the Kagaznagar X-road in Kumuram Bheem Asifabad district on 19
October 2026 led to the seizure of 5.2 kg of ganja. [@1] Two people were
arrested in the case. [@1]

Ganja reaches the district from Chandrapur and Rajura in neighbouring
Maharashtra. [@1] Ganja is transported along routes through Bejjur,
Penchikalpet, Kagaznagar, Vankidi and Kerameri on the Maharashtra border. [@1]
The consignments are moved at night along these routes. [@1] Allegations say
ganja is moved to various centres in the district, including wards of Kagaznagar
town and camps in the mandal. [@1]

The district police said they have placed strict surveillance on the routes used
for the illegal transport. [@1] Awareness sessions for students were held in
colleges under the district Superintendent of Police. [@1] Kagaznagar DSP
Wahiduddin said strict action would be taken against those transporting ganja
and that surveillance had been tightened on the routes from Maharashtra. [@1]
Doctors quoted in the article say taking ganja has a major effect on the brain,
including memory loss and mental problems. [@1]
