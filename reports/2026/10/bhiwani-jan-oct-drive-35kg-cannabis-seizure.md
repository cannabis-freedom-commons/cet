Bhiwani district police said officers acted from January through October 2026 in
a campaign against illegal liquor, drug trafficking, illegal weapons and
gambling on the directions of Superintendent of Police Mayank Mishra. [@1]
Police registered 101 cases under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances
Act and arrested 232 accused persons. [@1] In those NDPS cases officers seized
2.978 kilograms of charas-sulfa and 32.516 kilograms of ganja, together 35.494
kilograms of cannabis. [@1]

The same NDPS seizures also comprised 1.680 kilograms of opium, 15.079 kilograms
of doda-post poppy husk, 40.061 grams of smack and 10 kilograms 201 grams of
heroin, plus 573 narcotic pills and six injections, none of which is counted in
the cannabis figure. [@1] Superintendent Mishra said action is being taken
seriously against people involved in illegal liquor, drugs, weapons and betting
to curb crime and intoxication and give residents a safe environment. [@1] He
appealed to residents to inform the local police station, post or district
police about such activity so action can be taken in time. [@1]
