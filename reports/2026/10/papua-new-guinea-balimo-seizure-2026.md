A police team travelled by dinghy to Gaima village in Balimo, Western Province, and seized 500 kg of marijuana in a dawn raid on 8 October 2026. Post Courier placed the raid at Gaima village near the Fly River in Delta Fly District, Western Province, and described the haul as believed to be more than 500 kilograms. [@2] Five suspects from Nipa-Kutubu in the Southern Highlands and their spouses were arrested; police said locals had harboured them. The suspects and exhibits were being taken to Daru for charges.
