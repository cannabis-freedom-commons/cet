Two men received suspended sentences for charges that included possessing 1.96
grams of methamphetamine and 0.51 grams of cannabis seized in a police drugs
bust. [@1] Dwight ‘Aisea To’a, 28, and ‘Alo He, 19, were sentenced by Justice
Garlick KC at the Supreme Court in Nuku’alofa. [@2] To'a pleaded guilty to four
charges that included possession of 0.51 grams of cannabis, another 0.02 grams
of cannabis, 250 empty packets used for packing illicit drugs and one smoking
pipe. [@2] 'Alo He pleaded guilty to possession of the 1.96 grams of
methamphetamine. [@2] To'a was sentenced to eight months' imprisonment. [@2] He
was sentenced to 16 months' imprisonment. [@2] Both sentences were fully
suspended for two years on conditions. [@2]

Police acted on information received on 20 May 2025 that To'a was using a black
Subaru to distribute illicit drugs. [@2] Officers found the car outside a
Chinese shop in Ha'alalo and searched both men, finding a black bag with a
smoking pipe, a packet of suspected methamphetamine and empty packets. [@2] A
search of To'a's residence and workshop in 'Utulau found two packets of plant
material suspected to be cannabis and about 250 empty packets. [@2] A further
search at his Ha'ateiho home, where he lived with his wife, found a packet of
green plant material he said was left over from smoking the previous night. [@2]

The Crown cited the prevalence of illicit drugs, the defendants' failure to
cooperate with police and the quantity of empty packets as aggravating factors.
[@2] In mitigation both defendants pleaded guilty early and To'a, who had no
previous conviction, was still young. [@2] The judge said there was no direct
evidence that the drugs were being supplied on a commercial scale. [@2] The
starting sentences were reduced by one-third to recognise the early guilty
pleas. [@2] The court ordered the seized items destroyed and the $85 pa'anga
forfeited to the Crown. [@2]
