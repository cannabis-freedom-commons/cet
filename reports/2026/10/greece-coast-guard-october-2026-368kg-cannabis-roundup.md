The headquarters of the Hellenic Coast Guard published its detailed operational
tally for October 2026 through its directorates for maritime-border security and
drug and contraband enforcement. [@1] In drug enforcement, Coast Guard units
handled 23 cases and arrested 23 people during October 2026. [@1]

Officers seized 367,883.9 grams of raw cannabis and 462.4 grams of processed
cannabis. [@1] They also seized 79 cannabis seedlings, along with 10 grams of
seeds and three hand-rolled cigarettes. [@1] A second account of the same
monthly Coast Guard figures reports about 368 kilograms of raw cannabis, 462.4
grams of processed cannabis and 79 seedlings. [@2]

The same drug operation also seized 20.4264 kilograms of cocaine, plus 44.4
grams of pink cocaine and 86.7 grams of methamphetamine with 3 grams of crystal
methamphetamine. [@1] It additionally seized 392 grams of codeine, 166.8 grams
of MDMA with 95 Ecstasy tablets, 159.8 grams of ketamine with sedative tablets,
and LSD with hallucinogenic mushrooms. [@1] The tally covers only incidents
handled exclusively by the local port authorities. [@1]
