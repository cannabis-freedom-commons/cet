Portugal's Maritime Police said they seized about 464 kilograms of drugs,
presumed to be hashish, packed in 10 bales on the coastal strip between Cabo
Espichel in Sesimbra and Sines in Setúbal District. [@1] The seizure was
announced on Friday 9 October 2026. [@1]

The seizure resulted from the Maritime Police's operational activity on the
Alentejo coast through maritime and coastal patrol and surveillance actions
aimed at detecting illicit activities. [@1] The 10 bales were handed to the
Judicial Police on Thursday. [@1] After the handover the Maritime Police said it
reinforced and extended its patrol presence in the area by sea and land. [@1]

The police statement said the drugs had been seized in recent days. [@1]
