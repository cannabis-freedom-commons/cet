Porto PSP officers detained two men aged 27 and 42 and seized 10,653 doses of
drugs in an operation against trafficking in the cities of Porto and Valongo.
[@2] The operation took place on 30 October 2026 and was run by the Criminal
Investigation Division of the PSP's Porto Metropolitan Command. [@1] Both
suspects were taken to the Porto judicial criminal investigation court and,
after a first judicial interrogation, were remanded in pre-trial detention. [@3]

Officers seized 10,250 doses of cannabis resin, known in Portugal as haxixe, and
403 doses of cannabis leaf and flowering tops. [@1] Police also seized 1,460
euros in cash, two motorcycles, three mobile phones and a digital scale. [@1]
Officers collected a motorcycle helmet, a pair of motorcyclist gloves and other
objects said to relate to the activity under investigation. [@1] Three home
searches and one non-home search were carried out under judicial warrants. [@1]

The investigation focused on Bairro de São Tomé in the Paranhos parish, close to
Porto's university campuses. [@1] The PSP highlighted the impact of the
intervention in a zone used daily by thousands of students because of the
proximity to the university campuses. [@1] The PSP said the dismantling of the
network was another hard blow against drug trafficking in the Porto metropolitan
area, with particular impact in Paranhos. [@3]
