Brest customs opened a criminal case against a 49-year-old passenger of an
international bus who tried to bring drugs into the EAEU. [@1] She was
travelling through the Brest checkpoint on the scheduled Wrocław–Gomel bus. [@1]
A sniffer dog indicated the passenger seat she occupied, but a visual inspection
found nothing. [@1] In the customs clearance hall the dog again indicated her,
and she denied carrying prohibited substances when asked directly. [@1]

A personal search found a plastic container wrapped in a sock in the inner
pocket of her jacket. [@1] A forensic examination by the Brest regional forensic
unit confirmed the container held marijuana and hashish with a combined weight
of 3.46 grams. [@1] The case was opened under part 1 of Article 328-1 of
Belarus's Criminal Code, which provides for a sentence of up to seven years.
[@1] Belarus's State Customs Committee, which announced the case, said the woman
denied carrying prohibited substances when questioned. [@2] The sock-wrapped
container was found in a concealed pocket of her jacket during the search,
BGmedia reported. [@3]
