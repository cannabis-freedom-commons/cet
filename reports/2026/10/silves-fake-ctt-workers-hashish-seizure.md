GNR officers arrested two men, aged 19 and 26, in the municipality of Silves on
1 October 2026 on suspicion of fraud by collecting CTT parcels while posing as
postal workers. [@1] The GNR said the 26-year-old had worked for CTT before,
which let him know how parcels were collected and distributed. [@1] The two were
intercepted near a CTT branch with parcels worth about 3,500 euros, one of them
wearing a CTT vest. [@3] According to the GNR, they picked up parcels for
supposed deliveries without making the required payment. [@1]

After the arrests, officers searched the suspects' homes in the municipality of
Albufeira. [@1] There they seized 10 doses of hashish and one dose of liamba,
the Portuguese word for cannabis. [@2] The same searches found 0.89 grams of
MDMA, which is not counted in the cannabis figures. [@2] Officers also seized
four parcels, bags, shoes, watches and clothing, two mobile phones, a laptop and
a CTT vest. [@2] A precision scale was seized along with the drugs. [@2]

The two men were brought before Silves court for a first judicial interrogation
and ordered to report regularly to the police station in their home area while
the investigation continues. [@1] The operation was run by the Silves criminal
investigation unit of the GNR's Faro territorial command, with support from the
Armação de Pêra territorial post. [@1]
