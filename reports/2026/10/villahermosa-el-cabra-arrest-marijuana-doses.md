Óscar Ariel “N”, known as “El Cabra”, was arrested in Villahermosa, Tabasco, and
the federal security secretary, Omar García Harfuch, confirmed the detention on
Monday 12 October 2026. [@1] The federal security cabinet said he is one of the
leaders of the Mara 18 gang, which El Salvador's authorities link to at least 15
homicides, sexual offences and small-scale drug dealing, known in Mexico as
narcomenudeo. [@1] A Salvadoran court warrant for illicit association and
aggravated homicide was in force against him, issued by the Second Court against
Organised Crime of the Santa Ana department. [@1]

During the operation officers seized doses of marijuana, and no quantity was
given. [@1] The arrest was made during security and surveillance patrols, with
personnel from the Navy, the Army, the National Guard, the federal Security and
Citizen Protection Secretariat and the Tabasco state prosecutor's office. [@1]
He and the seized drug were handed to the public prosecutor's office for the
legal steps toward his delivery to the authorities seeking him. [@1] He was
informed of his rights and presented to the public prosecutor together with the
seized substances. [@2]

Local media had reported the capture from Friday 9 October, identifying him as a
Salvadoran citizen. [@1] García Harfuch thanked El Salvador's government for the
exchange of information and Tabasco's governor, Javier May, and the state
authorities for their collaboration in the arrest. [@3]
