Jordan Warner, of Ashgrove Lawn, Carrigaline, was charged with possession of
cannabis and cocaine at Carthage Place, Ringaskiddy, on 7 October 2026. [@1]
Gardaí put the cannabis at €22,000 and the cocaine at a street value of €3,500,
a total of €25,500. [@1] He was also charged with having both drugs for the
purpose of sale or supply. [@1]

Sergeant Gary Brennan objected to bail at Cork District Court. [@1] Judge David
Waters said that, on the valuations given, the case would not be a matter for
the district court. [@1] The judge refused bail and remanded Warner in custody
until 15 October 2026. [@1]

Judge Waters said the allegation was “this is serious dealing, this is serious
involvement in the drugs business” and that he had real concerns Warner would
continue his involvement in the drugs trade. [@1] Sergeant Gearóid Davis said
directions would be sought from the Director of Public Prosecutions. [@1]
