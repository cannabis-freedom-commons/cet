On 20 October 2026, detectives from CID Drugs and Narcotics in Mashonaland West
arrested four people in connection with unlawful possession of dagga and
unregistered medicines. [@4] Those arrested were truck driver Biemba Daniel
Malesu (41), his assistant Geoffrey Makumbe (53), Sydney Shamanga (41) and
Sandra Makovere (40). [@1] Police said a tip-off that a haulage truck travelling
along the Chinhoyi-Chegutu Road was carrying drugs led officers to intercept the
truck and arrest driver Malesu and assistant Makumbe. [@2] A subsequent
controlled delivery led to the arrest of the other suspects at Mapako Bus Stop
in Chinhoyi while they were allegedly loading the dagga into a Nissan Note
vehicle. [@2]

Police recovered 502 kilogrammes of dagga, 96 bottles of Benylin cough syrup and
250 bottles of Broncleer cough syrup. [@2] Police gave the seized dagga and
cough syrup a combined estimated street value of US$50,768 in Mashonaland West
Province. [@4] The four face charges of unlawful possession of dagga and
unregistered medicines. [@3]

Two other suspects, Kingstone Matsveruka and Clifford Dozva, are still at large,
and police say investigations are ongoing. [@2] Authorities have not disclosed
the origin or intended destination of the seized drugs. [@4]
