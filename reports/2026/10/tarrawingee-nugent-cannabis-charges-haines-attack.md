Federal independent MP Helen Haines was allegedly struck by a vehicle outside
her electorate office in Murphy Street, Wangaratta. [@3] She lost both legs as a
result of the alleged attack. [@3] Victoria Police arrested 43-year-old
Tarrawingee resident Brendan Nugent over the alleged attack and charged him with
dangerous driving causing serious injury, intentionally causing serious injury
and causing harm to a Commonwealth public official. [@1]

Court documents released by the Wangaratta Magistrates Court showed police had
laid extra charges of cultivating cannabis and possessing cannabis. [@2] The
cannabis charges relate to his rural property in Tarrawingee and are dated 20
October, the day after the alleged attack. [@3] After holding Nugent in custody
for about a day, Victoria Police charged him with dangerous driving causing
serious injury, intentionally causing serious injury and causing harm to a
Commonwealth public official. [@4]

No application for bail was made. [@2] He is due back at Wangaratta Magistrates’
Court for a committal mention on 4 February 2027. [@4] On 16 November Haines was
discharged from hospital to a rehabilitation hospital as her treatment entered a
rehabilitation stage. [@1]
