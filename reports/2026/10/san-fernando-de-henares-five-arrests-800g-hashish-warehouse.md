National Police dismantled an alleged retail drug sale point in an occupied
warehouse in San Fernando de Henares, arresting five men. [@1,2] The five men
lived in two shacks set up as dwellings inside the warehouse. [@1,2] The
investigation and search were carried out by officers of the Torrejon de Ardoz
National Police station on 29 October. [@1,2] The five arrested men were accused
of an alleged offence against public health and were placed at the disposal of
the judicial authority. [@1,2] The five detainees were aged between 35 and 50, were of several nationalities and had known police records. [@3] The operation was led by the Torrejon de Ardoz
police station although the searched building lies in the municipality of San
Fernando de Henares. [@1]

During the entry and search officers found nearly 800 grams of hashish, part of
it hidden inside a portable air-conditioning unit in a room used as a dwelling.
[@1,2] Inside the unit were several pieces of hashish of about 100 grams each.
[@1] Some pieces were prepared for distribution and others were open and ready
to be divided into smaller amounts. [@1] Officers also found transparent
wrapping film with the hashish pieces inside a refrigerator near the warehouse
entrance. [@1] Under a mattress in one room police found a dagger with a
25-centimetre blade and a folding knife with a blade of about 10 centimetres.
[@1] Officers seized assorted jewellery and costume jewellery including
earrings, necklaces and rings. [@1] Officers also seized two GPS geolocation
devices with their SIM cards plus additional telephone cards. [@1,2]

Police treated the arrangement of the drugs and preparation material as evidence
of alleged retail sale from the building. [@1] The operation was part of police
inquiries aimed at finding and dismantling drug distribution points around the
Corredor del Henares. [@1]
