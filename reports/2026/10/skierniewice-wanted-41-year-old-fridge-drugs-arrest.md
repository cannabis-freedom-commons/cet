Skierniewice criminal police detained a 41-year-old local resident who was
wanted to serve more than two years in prison for drug-possession offences. [@2]
The detention took place on 20 October 2026. [@2] The man was taken to the
Skierniewice city police headquarters. [@1] The next day he was charged with
drug possession, the prosecutor applied for pretrial detention and the court
granted it. [@3]

During the operation at his home officers uncovered a total of 159 grams of
intoxicating and psychotropic substances, described as amphetamine and
marijuana. [@2] Most of the drugs were hidden in the refrigerator. [@2]

Police said the man had already been sought over earlier drug-possession
offences. [@2] For the new possession offence he faces up to 10 years in prison.
[@2] The case remains under prosecutorial supervision. [@1] Police press officer
Aneta Placek said effective detective work both detains wanted persons and
secures prohibited substances so they do not reach further circulation. [@2]
