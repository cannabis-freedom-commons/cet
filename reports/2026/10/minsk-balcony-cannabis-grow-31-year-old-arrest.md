Officers in Minsk detained a 31-year-old city resident after establishing that
he had fitted out a room in his own flat for cultivating drug-containing plants.
[@1] The GUVD of the Minsk City Executive Committee, the capital city's police
directorate, was named as the source of the account. [@1] The detained man had
not previously been brought to criminal responsibility. [@1]

During the search of his residence, operatives found and seized a growbox, an
exhaust vent, a thermometer and fertilisers together with three pots holding
soil and cannabis plants. [@1] In the same search officers also seized about 20
single doses of marijuana described as ready for consumption. [@1] The search
thus linked cultivation equipment, live cannabis plants and ready marijuana at
the same flat. [@1]

The man had bought the seeds through an online drug market and cultivated the
drug-containing plants on the balcony of his flat for his own consumption. [@1]
A criminal case for illegal drug trafficking was opened following the detention.
[@1]
