The Directorate of Revenue Intelligence (DRI), in coordination with the Railway
Protection Force (RPF), arrested two alleged drug traffickers during an
operation near Lucknow on Monday night. [@1] The two suspects were intercepted
while travelling in the general compartment of the Sapt Kranti Express, and a
search of their luggage led to the recovery of 3.931 kg of Nepal-origin charas,
according to the DRI. [@1]

The DRI valued the seized charas at Rs 3.93 crore. [@1] During questioning, the
two suspects allegedly admitted that the contraband had been smuggled into India
from Nepal and was being transported to New Delhi for delivery. [@1] The
narcotic substance was taken into custody and both suspects were arrested under
the provisions of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act,
1985. [@1]

According to the DRI, Nepal-origin charas, particularly hand-rubbed Himalayan
charas, commands a premium in illegal markets because of its potency and
distinctive characteristics. [@1]
