Police in Faridpur upazila, Pabna district, detained Md Momin Prang, 45, with
one puriya, a small packet, of ganja, the local term for cannabis. [@1] The
detention was made in a special operation by a Faridpur thana police team on
Monday 5 October 2026. [@1] Momin Prang is the son of the late Mozahar Prang of
Demra Charpara village in Faridpur upazila. [@1]

Police said the operation was carried out on secret information by a team of SI
Zahidul Rahman Badal and ASI Shahanur Rahman, under the direction of Faridpur
police station chief Monjurul Hasan Masud. [@1] The detained man was taken to
the office of the Faridpur upazila executive officer, who served as executive
magistrate, and a magistrate convicted him under section 36(5) of the 2018
Narcotics Control Act. [@1] The magistrate sentenced him to six months' simple
imprisonment and a fine of 50 taka. [@1] Masud said the convicted man had been
sent to court under police guard and that anti-drug operations would continue.
[@1]

Monjurul Hasan Masud was chosen as Pabna district's best officer in charge for
August, and the Rajshahi Range deputy inspector general and the district police
superintendent handed him a crest at the superintendent's conference room. [@2]
The station's account of that record lists the 5 October case, with Momin Prang
detained with one puriya of ganja and given six months and a fine by mobile
court. [@2]
