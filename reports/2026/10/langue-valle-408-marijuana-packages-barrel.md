Anti-drug police found a buried barrel packed with thousands of packages of
suspected drugs in a wooded area of El Basurero, in barrio Nuevo, municipality
of Langue, Valle. [@1] The find was made during an operation on Friday 9 October
2026 in the department of Valle. [@3] Officers of the National Anti-Drug Police
Directorate (DNPA) worked with the Police Intelligence Directorate (Dipol) and
the National Directorate of Border Police Services (DNSPF) after intelligence,
surveillance and follow-up work. [@2]

Inside the barrel, officers counted 408 transparent bags of dried herb suspected
to be marijuana. [@1] In the same stash they counted 4,350 puntas, or doses, of
white powder said to be cocaine and 350 stones of crack. [@2] In total, the
authorities counted 5,108 wrappers and bags holding the three suspected
substances. [@2]

Police attributed the stash to the Mara Salvatrucha (MS-13). [@1] No detentions
in connection with the find were reported. [@3] The case continues in
coordination with the Valle regional prosecutor's office for legal and
investigative steps. [@2] Investigators are also trying to establish whether
other people took part in hiding the drugs and whether the load was meant for
distribution in communities of the area. [@1] The authorities described the
substances as suspected and said investigations must confirm their nature. [@1]
