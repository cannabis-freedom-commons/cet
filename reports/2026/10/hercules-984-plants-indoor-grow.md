Hercules police detectives served a search warrant at a residence in the city
and found about 984 mature and immature marijuana plants. [@1] The plants were
growing inside the home, and officers removed the crop, police said. [@2] Police
described the home as an illegal grow house. [@3] No arrests were announced, and
the investigation remained open. [@2] Police did not release details on how many
arrests were made or on potential charges. [@3]

The department announced the discovery in a social media post on Tuesday 27
October 2026. [@3] Police said illegal indoor grows can create fire hazards,
electrical dangers and unsafe structural conditions. [@1] The department asked
residents to report suspicious activity by calling 911 in an emergency or its
dispatch line at 510-724-1111, and warned them not to approach. [@2] Police did
not say what brought them to the residence. [@3]
