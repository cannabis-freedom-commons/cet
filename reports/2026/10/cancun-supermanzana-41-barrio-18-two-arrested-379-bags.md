Quintana Roo's Citizen Security Secretariat said its investigators arrested two
men, identified as Rudy “N” and Lester “N”, on suspicion of drug and firearms
offences. [@2] The secretariat said the two were suspected leaders of the
transnational gang Barrio 18, also called Calle 18, and held Interpol red
notices after escaping a Guatemalan prison in October 2025. [@2] Officers
stopped them in Supermanzana 41 in Cancún while, they said, the two were
exchanging small transparent bags of dry herb. [@1] The arrests took place on 4
October 2026, the date the secretariat's account gave to Guatemalan newspaper
Prensa Libre. [@5] Guatemala's interior ministry said the two had escaped from
the Fraijanes II prison on 12 October 2025 and were wanted on Interpol notices.
[@4]

During a preventive search officers seized 379 small transparent bags of dried
herb resembling marijuana. [@3] They also seized a Taurus G3 9x19 mm pistol, two
magazines, 18 rounds, two black backpacks and three mobile phones. [@2] The men
also carried a regional visitor card issued by Mexico's National Migration
Institute, an ordinary passport and a green notebook with notes. [@2]

The two were held on suspicion of offences against health and of violating the
Federal Firearms and Explosives Act. [@3] They and the seized items were handed
to the Quintana Roo attorney general's office, in its unit specialising in
drug-dealing offences, known as narcomenudeo. [@2] The state secretariat linked
the two men to the killing of a woman in Supermanzana 38 on 3 October, Prensa
Libre reported. [@5] Guatemala's interior ministry said both would be extradited
once legal proceedings in Mexico were finished. [@4]
