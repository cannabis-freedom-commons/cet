Three passengers arriving from Bangkok were held at Bengaluru's Kempegowda
International Airport after Customs recovered hydroponic ganja valued at about
Rs 8.38 crore. [@1] The three arrests were made on 22 October under the NDPS
Act. [@3] Bengaluru Customs said the seizures came in three separate cases and
that each passenger concealed the ganja to evade detection. [@3]

In the first case Customs seized 12.010 kg of hydroponic ganja valued at Rs 4.20
crore from a Bangkok passenger, according to a Customs post on X. [@3] In the
second case officers intercepted another Bangkok arrival and seized 8.940 kg
valued at Rs 3.13 crore. [@2] In the third case 3.010 kg valued at Rs 1.05 crore
was seized from another passenger from Bangkok. [@3]
