A 33-year-old Bolivian man was arrested on Tuesday 27 October 2026 with 12
tablets of skunk on a bus stopped at km 576 of the Rodovia Raposo Tavares
(SP-270) in Álvares Machado, São Paulo. [@1] The Military Highway Police said a
Tático Ostensivo Rodoviário (TOR) team stopped the bus, which carried 21
passengers and ran from Campo Grande in Mato Grosso do Sul to Maringá in Paraná.
[@2] The bus bore Maringá (Paraná) plates. [@4]

Officers searching the bus's internal luggage compartment found 12 tablets of
skunk distributed across two labelled bags. [@2] The estimated weight was 12,820
kilograms (12.82 kg in Brazilian decimal notation) and the material was valued
at 130,000 reais. [@1] Police said the bags belonged to the passenger in seat
five. [@2]

The arrest happened in the late afternoon during a routine highway check by the
TOR team as part of Operação Impacto (Operation Impact). [@3] The man was
charged in flagrante with drug trafficking and remained detained at the federal
police unit. [@3] The occurrence was presented at the Federal Police
headquarters in Presidente Prudente, and the man remained at the disposal of the
courts. [@1]
