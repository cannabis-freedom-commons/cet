Three people were arrested in a black market marijuana investigation. [@1] The
three were taken into custody following an investigation by the Oklahoma Bureau
of Narcotics. [@2] On Oct. 7, OBN's Marijuana Enforcement Teams and Special
Investigations Unit served multiple search warrants as part of the ongoing
investigation. [@2] Agents simultaneously executed search warrants at locations
in Edmond, Wewoka and Seminole. [@2]

Agents seized 5,274 pounds of processed marijuana during the searches. [@2]
Agents also seized 145 pounds of THC wax. [@2] Agents seized 290 THC vape
cartridges. [@2] Agents seized 922 packages of mushroom edibles. [@2] Agents
seized 2,211 packages of mushroom gummies. [@2] Agents seized two pistols and
one suppressor. [@2]

Officials said three people were taken into custody and more arrests are
anticipated as the investigation continues. [@2] OBN Director Donnie Anderson
said the investigation targeted and dismantled a significant transnational
criminal enterprise shipping thousands of pounds of Oklahoma-grown marijuana to
the out-of-state black market. [@2] The operation involved cooperation from the
DEA's Oklahoma City and Dallas field offices, U.S. Immigration and Customs
Enforcement, the Oklahoma agriculture department, Edmond and Mesquite police,
the Seminole County Sheriff's Office and the Seminole County District 3
Commissioner's Office. [@3]
