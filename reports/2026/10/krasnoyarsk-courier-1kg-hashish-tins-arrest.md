Krasnoyarsk drug police detained a 24-year-old courier who worked for an online
drug shop. [@1] Officers searched the flat he rented in the Sovetsky district of
Krasnoyarsk. [@1] He was placed in pre-trial detention. [@1]

In the flat police found packaging materials, electronic scales, mobile phones
and parcels of drugs ready for sale, some of them kept in the freezer. [@1]
Police seized two sealed metal tins of meat stew from the man. [@1] Inside the
tins were polymer bags of a pressed brown substance. [@1] The tins had been
collected from a stash on the instructions of an anonymous curator and brought
to the rented flat. [@1] Forensic examination identified the substance as
hashish weighing almost one kilogram. [@1] Police said the hashish was intended
for contactless sale in the city. [@1] The man was to divide the load into small
doses for sale in Krasnoyarsk and received instructions from an anonymous
operator through a messenger. [@2]

A criminal case was opened over attempted sale of narcotic drugs on a large
scale. [@3] The case was opened under part 3 of article 30 and part 4 of article
228.1 of the Criminal Code. [@1] The investigation continues. [@1]
