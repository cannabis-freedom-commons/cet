DINT detectives jailed a man on 5 October 2026 whom they had sought since the
August grow was found. [@1] On 4 August 2026 DINT served a search warrant in the
1000 block of Tunnel Road in Glendale and dismantled a growing operation there.
[@2] As officers entered the property, 46-year-old Adam Paul Stewart fled on
foot with a suspected firearm and was not found despite a search. [@2]

During that search DINT seized and destroyed 527 marijuana plants and seized
more than 300 pounds of processed marijuana. [@2] DINT also seized multiple
firearms, residue amounts of methamphetamine, a stolen U-Haul vehicle being
dismantled and a commercial welder believed stolen from a work site. [@2]

On 5 October 2026 Stewart was found at an unrelated hearing at the Douglas
County Courthouse, taken into custody and transported to corrections. [@2]
Stewart was charged with unlawful manufacture and delivery of a marijuana item,
two counts of unlawful possession by a person over 21, unlawful possession of
methamphetamine and possession of a stolen vehicle. [@2] Bail was set at $20,000
and Stewart was released the next day. [@2]
