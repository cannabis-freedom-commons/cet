On 21 and 22 October 2026, gendarmes of the COMGENDPF and the Leeward Islands
company, supported by the PSIG and a dog team, carried out two judicial
operations in the communes of Faa'a and Punaauia. [@1] After the investigations,
the people implicated were summoned before the Papeete judicial court to answer
for their acts. [@1]

The operations seized 3 kg of cannabis herb. [@1] Officers seized 794 cannabis
plants. [@1] Officers seized 136 cannabis branches. [@1] Officers seized 161,000
CFP francs in cash. [@1]

The gendarmerie presented the operations as action against drug trafficking.
