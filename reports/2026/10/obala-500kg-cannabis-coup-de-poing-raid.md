Gendarmes arrested 16 people in an overnight operation in Obala, in Lékié
department, according to the national gendarmerie. [@3] The raid, code-named
'Coup de poing', ran from 20:00 on 1 October to 02:30 on 2 October 2026. [@3]
Cameroun Actuel reported five suspects and about 500 kg seized. [@1] The
operation was conducted by Captain Malick Baourou under the coordination of
Colonel Désiré Joseph Nguele. [@1] The Centre Region gendarmerie legion carried
out the arrests and found five 100-kg bags of Indian hemp. [@2] Officers
simultaneously entered the Mboa, Haoussa and Chauffeur neighborhoods. [@1]

In Mboa, five 100-kg bags of cannabis, about 500 kg in all, were found in a room
fitted out for sale. [@1] Cameroon Tribune said the room served as both a store
and a sales point. [@2] The main suspect was described as a known repeat
offender. [@2] Gendarmes valued five packages of the seized drugs at about 2
million CFA francs. [@3] In Chauffeur they dismantled a smoking room, or fumoir,
and seized consumption accessories. [@1] A hookah containing suspected drugs and
two pipes used for smoking hard drugs were also recovered. [@3] Officers also
seized five mobile phones, a tablet and eight motorcycles. [@3]

The raid followed intelligence about a suspected large supply of drugs to Obala,
in Lékié. [@2] The 16 detainees were placed at the disposal of investigators,
who were still establishing each person's role. [@3] The five suspects named by
Cameroun Actuel were expected to be presented to justice in Monatélé. [@1]
