Bexar County sheriff's deputies searched a home in the 140 block of Kinder Drive
on the North Side of San Antonio, Texas, on 30 October 2026 under a warrant in a
weeks-long organized crime investigation. [@1] Deputies found Ivette Aleia
Claire, 24, and Timothy Ray Mendoza, 23, in the home and arrested both at the
scene. [@1]

Officers seized 344.6 grams of marijuana. [@1] The search also turned up 310
grams of THC cartridges. [@1] Deputies also recovered 2,860 grams of
methamphetamine. [@1] The seizure also included 45 grams of cocaine and 25 grams
of psilocybin mushrooms. [@2] In all, the sheriff's office said deputies took
more than 3,500 grams of narcotics, 15 firearms and more than $28,000 in cash.
[@2]

Both were charged with possession of marijuana in the 2-to-4-ounce range. [@1]
Claire was charged with two counts of manufacture or delivery of a controlled
substance in Penalty Group 1 and with possession of a controlled substance in
Penalty Group 2. [@2] Mendoza faced manufacture or delivery of a controlled
substance in Penalty Group 1, two counts of possession in Penalty Group 2 and
unlawful possession of a firearm by a felon. [@2]

The sheriff's office said its Specialized Organized Crime Unit executed the
warrant. [@2] Investigators were continuing to pursue leads, the sheriff's
office said. [@2]
