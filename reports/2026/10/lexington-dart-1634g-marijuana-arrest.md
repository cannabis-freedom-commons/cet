A search warrant executed at a home in Lexington, North Carolina, on Wednesday,
Oct. 28, 2026, led investigators to seize more than 1,600 grams of marijuana and
arrest a woman on multiple drug-related charges, authorities said. [@1] The
woman arrested was Carrie Fancy Carlile. [@1] The narcotics investigation was
carried out by investigators with the Davidson Anti-Drug Regional Task Force, a
regional drug unit known as DART, at a residence in the 100 block of Foxfire
Circle. [@1]

During the search of the residence, the investigators seized about 1,634 grams
of marijuana and marijuana paraphernalia, according to the authorities. [@1] The
approximately 1.6 kilograms of marijuana and the paraphernalia were recovered
from the home named in the warrant. [@1]

After her arrest, Carlile was taken before a Davidson County magistrate, where
she was formally charged and given a $25,000 unsecured bond. [@1] She is
scheduled to appear in Davidson County District Court on Feb. 18, 2027, to
answer the charges. [@1]
