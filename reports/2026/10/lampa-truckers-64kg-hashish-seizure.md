Two truck drivers were detained for alleged involvement in trafficking nearly
740 kilograms of drugs that included cocaine hydrochloride, cocaine pasta base
and hashish. [@1] The interception took place on Ruta 5 at Lampa, where two
Chilean citizens aged 66 and 46 were captured. [@1]

Officers seized 6 kilograms 400 grams of hashish, a cannabis resin, along with
480 kg 850 g of cocaine hydrochloride, 244 kg 950 g of cocaine pasta base, 5 kg
950 g of ketamine and 1 kg 100 g of powdered THC. [@2] The operation was carried
out by the PDI's Southern Anti-Narcotics Brigade (Briang) in collaboration with
the Metropolitan South Prosecutor's Office. [@2] The two drivers were due to
appear at a detention control hearing on 22 October. [@2]

Most of the cocaine hydrochloride was believed to be destined for southern
Santiago. [@1] Metropolitan South prosecutor Héctor Barros said the seizure
suggested a possible change in the substances reaching southern Santiago. [@1]
PDI director general Eduardo Cerna said the force had not seen hashish in
seizures, especially in this quantity, and called for deeper inquiry into its
origin and the routes used. [@1] Authorities valued the entire mixed haul at
13,500 million pesos. [@2]
