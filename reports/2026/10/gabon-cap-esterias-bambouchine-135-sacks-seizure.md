Gabonese security forces detained 15 people during two operations on 5 and 12
October 2026 in maritime and forest zones north of Libreville targeting drug
networks and clandestine immigration. [@1] In the night of 5 October at La
Sablière, officers detained 13 people. [@1] On 12 October in a forest area near
Nendé island, officers detained two people described as being in clandestine
immigration. [@1]

The 5 October operation seized 64 sacks of cannabis and almost 700 tablets. [@1]
The 12 October operation seized 71 additional sacks. [@1] The combined total was
135 sacks of cannabis, about 700 Tramadol tablets and 15 detained people. [@1]

The intervention ran between Cap Estérias and the Bambouchine landing and used
aerial, maritime and land surveillance. [@1] Several intelligence and security
services took part, including the DGCES, OCLAD, DGDI, DGR and DGCIM with support
from the Republican Guard nautical brigade. [@1] The operations targeted
maritime routes used by networks operating from abroad to bring people and goods
to the Gabonese coast. [@1] Those detained were to answer for the alleged acts
before the competent judicial authorities, whose specific charges and procedural
status were not reported. [@1]
