The interception occurred at the corregimiento of Mondomo, on the stretch
linking Santander de Quilichao with Popayan. [@3] The cargo truck was travelling
from Cali in Valle del Cauca toward Neiva in Huila. [@3] The drug was concealed
in a truck hauling molasses to move it undetected along the southwestern
highway. [@2] Officers immobilised the cargo vehicle and arrested its driver for
drug trafficking. [@3]

Officers found 1,000 packages of plant material inside the vehicle, placed under
official custody for legal analysis. [@3] The find was reported as 1,000
packages of plant material whose contents awaited confirmation by the competent
authorities. [@1] Police preliminarily valued the load at about 800 million
pesos. [@2] The detainee, the seized marijuana and the vehicle were handed to
the competent authority for judicial proceedings. [@2]

President Abelardo de la Espriella announced the blow and wrote that criminals
would be given no truce as routes were closed and illicit businesses hit. [@1]
Police linked the blow to the finances of the Bloque Occidental Jacobo Arenas,
which seeks to consolidate illicit routes in the west. [@3]
