National Police officers raided a house in the El Naranjo community of El
Tuma-La Dalia, Matagalpa, at 4:20 p.m. on Friday 23 October 2026. [@1] They
arrested Harold Dávila Mendoza, 48, and Kevin Joel Castillo, 19, inside the
house. [@1] Captain Cristian Meneses of the Matagalpa National Police announced
the operation. [@1]

Police described the operation as an anti-narcotics raid that left two people
detained and more than six pounds of marijuana seized. [@1] During the search of
the dwelling, officers found four pellets wrapped in adhesive tape and filled
with green herb. [@1] A field test was positive for marijuana, and police
weighed the contents at six pounds eight ounces, about 2.95 kilograms. [@1] La
Nueva Radio YA reported the weight as six and a half pounds. [@2]

The two detainees and the evidence were handed to the authorities for the
judicial process. [@2]
