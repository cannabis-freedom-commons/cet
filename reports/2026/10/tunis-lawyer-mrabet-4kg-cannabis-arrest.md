The Tunis public prosecutor authorised the anti-drugs sub-directorate of the
judicial police to hold lawyer Ghazi Mrabet and a wanted drug dealer, already
serving a 40-year prison sentence, on drug-dealing charges. [@1,2,4] The dealer appeared in detention before the chamber, which after deliberation confirmed the 40-year sentence with confiscation of all property as an additional penalty. [@4] The case goes back to 2024, when a huge quantity of drugs in the form of hundreds of sheets hidden tightly inside car wheels imported from Morocco for a car sales agency in Tunis was discovered. [@4] The Fourth Criminal Chamber of the Tunis First Instance Court confirmed the in-absentia sentence of 40 years and confiscation of all movable and immovable property. [@4]

The two were caught in the act at the searched dealer's home, where the search
seized more than 8 kg of cocaine, 4 kg of Indian hemp and 394,000 dinars in
cash. [@1,2] After his arrest the dealer objected to the in-absentia judgment against him. [@4] The lawyer allegedly tried to obstruct the officers so the dealer
could flee before both were caught.
