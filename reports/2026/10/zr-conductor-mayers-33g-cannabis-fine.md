Corey Andre Roderick Mayers, a ZR route taxi conductor of Bank Hall, St Michael,
was fined in the District 'A' Traffic Court after admitting several offences,
including cannabis possession, that he had committed three years earlier. [@1]
Corey Andre Roderick Mayers, of Bank Hall, St Michael, had been charged with
driving a motor car along Bush Hall Yard Gap without a driver's licence, driving
without insurance, fraudulently using number plates, driving an unregistered
vehicle and failing to produce government-issued identification when asked by a
police officer. [@1] He appeared before Magistrate Jared Richards last week and
changed his pleas, admitting to the offences, which took place on September 13,
2023. [@1] He told the court that he had just purchased the car a few days
earlier when he was pulled over by police and only had his permit. [@1]

He was also charged with possessing 33 grammes of cannabis, possessing cannabis
with intent to supply and possessing a traffickable quantity of cannabis. [@1]
He had denied the charges before he changed his pleas. [@1]

He was ordered to pay $300 for driving without a licence or face two months'
imprisonment, $500 for having no insurance or face three months' imprisonment,
$100 for the fraudulent use of number plates or face one month's imprisonment,
and $400 for driving an unregistered vehicle or face three months' imprisonment.
[@1] Mayers was also fined $100 for possessing cannabis. [@1] He was convicted,
reprimanded and discharged on the charges of possession with intent to supply,
possession of a traffickable quantity and failure to produce identification.
[@1] The fines are all due within six months, and failure to pay will result in
the applicable alternative prison terms. [@1]
