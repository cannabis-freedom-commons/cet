Bogotá's Metropolitan Police broke up the Hera group, which sold drugs in La
Estanzuela while posing as a vehicle-accessories business, and arrested 19
people. [@1] Fifteen of the detainees were held under judicial arrest warrants
and four were caught in flagrante. [@1] The operation included 17 searches in
the localities of Los Mártires, Antonio Nariño and Puente Aranda. [@1] All 19
face charges of concierto para delinquir, conspiracy to commit crime, and of
manufacturing, trafficking or carrying narcotics. [@1] A judge of control of
guarantees ordered pre-trial detention in prison for 10 of the accused. [@1] The
remaining nine were given house arrest. [@1]

The group sold marijuana, bazuco, a cheap cocaine paste, and coca base in La
Estanzuela, in the centre of Bogotá. [@1] It worked from vehicle-parts premises
and stamped its doses with the Batman symbol to identify them. [@1] Officers
seized more than 9,000 doses of marijuana, bazuco and coca base. [@1] Alias
Marta was identified as the group's leader, controlling the dealers and the
security of the sales points. [@1] Her sister, alias La Mona, was the
administrator and provider, responsible for collecting the money from the drug
sales. [@1] Alias Carolina, a Venezuelan national, was the visible face of the
front, running the vehicle-accessories shop while storing and distributing the
drugs. [@1] Police attributed sales roles to 11 other people. [@2]

The investigation lasted more than a year and included the work of an undercover
agent. [@1] The authorities estimated the impact on the group's finances at 1.2
billion pesos. [@1] El Espectador reported that the break-up of the structure,
which also sold drugs in the La Leona sector, was announced on the morning of
Wednesday 7 October 2026. [@2] Mayor Carlos Fernando Galán confirmed the
operation on social media. [@1] Several of the detainees already had judicial
records for theft and similar offences. [@1] The district security office said
the group had answered earlier police controls with violence, injuring officers
and damaging official vehicles. [@3]
