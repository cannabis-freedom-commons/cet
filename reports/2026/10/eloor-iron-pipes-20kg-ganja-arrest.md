A West Bengal native was arrested with cannabis. [@1] Police seized more than 20
kg of ganja from him in Eloor, on the Kalamassery side of Kochi. [@2] The Kochi
City police's District Anti-Narcotics Special Action Force, known as DANSAF,
made the arrest. [@3] The Ernakulam narcotics ASP Ananth Lal led the operation.
[@3] The arrested man was Sukhchand Sheikh, a native of Murshidabad district in
West Bengal. [@3]

Police had watched him for about a week after a tip that he was selling ganja in
the area. [@2] The ganja had been packed into three iron pipes with a hydraulic
machine and the pipes welded shut with JCB parts and rods so the smell would not
escape. [@2] Police and the fire force worked for hours to break the iron open
and take the ganja out. [@2] According to one account he reached Aluva from
Tamil Nadu on a luxury bus and was travelling on to Kochi by autorickshaw when
he was caught. [@5] He told police in a preliminary statement that the ganja was
brought to be handed to one person. [@5]

The narcotics team said he bought the ganja cheaply in Odisha, shifted it to
Karnataka and packed it into the iron compartments before bringing it to Kerala.
[@4] Police said he sold the drug mainly to migrant workers from other states.
[@4] Police said they had begun a detailed investigation into an interstate
trafficking network centred on him. [@6] MediaOne described it as the third
large ganja seizure in Kochi within a week. [@2]
