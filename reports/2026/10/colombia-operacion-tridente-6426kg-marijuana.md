Colombia's navy carried out Operation Tridente in Colombian Pacific waters
between 8 and 29 October 2026, President Abelardo de la Espriella announced.
[@1] The operation was executed by the Pacific Naval Force in coordination with
the Joint Interagency Task Force South of the U.S. Southern Command, as part of
the Escudo de las Américas strategy. [@1] Twenty people believed to be linked to
drug trafficking were captured. [@1]

According to the official balance, officers seized 16,420 kilograms of drugs, of
which 6,426.7 kilograms was marijuana and 9,993 kilograms was cocaine
hydrochloride. [@1] Authorities reported destroying 37 laboratories used to
process coca base and finding 13,082 gallons of liquid inputs and 1,876
kilograms of solid inputs there. [@1] The balance also listed six maritime
interdictions, six immobilized vessels, three collection points intervened and
three offensive actions by the Marine Infantry. [@1]

The president said the operation also affected more than 530 million dollars of
criminal organisations' finances. [@1] Authorities estimated the seizures kept
about 26 million doses off illegal markets. [@1] They said the results would
also prevent roughly 100 overdose deaths, 10,000 medical emergencies and 5,000
new addiction cases, and save close to 6.5 million dollars for health systems.
[@1] The government said the results showed the importance of cooperation with
the United States under the Escudo de las Américas strategy against
transnational criminal organisations. [@1]
