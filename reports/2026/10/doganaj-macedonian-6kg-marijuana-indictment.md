The Basic Prosecutor's Office in Uroševac filed an indictment on 9 October 2026
against EA, a citizen of North Macedonia, over the transport of more than six
kilograms of marijuana on 30 January. [@1] EA has remained in custody since the
January transport. [@1] He is charged with unauthorized purchase, possession,
distribution and sale of narcotics, psychotropic and analogous substances. [@1]

Prosecutors say that at about 20:30 on 30 January EA, acting on instructions
from others, drove his black VW Passat to the entrance to Podujevo and stopped
at the Hib Petrol station. [@1] He then followed another vehicle to a
supermarket parking lot, where several people arrived in two Mercedes cars and a
Volkswagen Transporter van. [@1] The indictment says EA was not allowed out of
the car while other people opened the boot and put a bag and a plastic bag
inside. [@1] They followed him until he joined the highway towards Đeneral
Jankovići. [@1] Police stopped him at the exit to Doganaj and found in the boot
marijuana divided into 20 packages with a total weight of 6,121.08 grams
(6.12108 kg). [@1] The drugs were confiscated and the prosecution asked the
court to find EA guilty and punish him in accordance with the law. [@2]

Police had announced on 31 January that two citizens of North Macedonia were
arrested on the Doganaj-Kačanik highway and that marijuana and heroin were
seized. [@1] Police then said they found 20 packages of a substance suspected to
be marijuana weighing 6.4 kg and six packages of a substance suspected to be
heroin weighing 3.2 kg. [@1] The indictment refers only to 6,121.08 grams of
marijuana and does not mention the heroin or the other person arrested at the
time. [@1]
