Border Guard Bangladesh arrested 615 people in anti-smuggling drives along the
border under its Northwest Region, Rangpur, in the first 10 months of the year.
[@1] Four sectors and 15 battalions under the region conducted the operations
from January 1 to October 30. [@1] The information was disclosed in a press
release issued by Panchagarh Battalion (18 BGB) on Saturday. [@1] The Rangpur
region is responsible for 1,668.854 kilometres of the country's 4,427-kilometre
border. [@1]

The seized drugs included 1,001.92 kg of cannabis, alongside foreign liquor,
local liquor, heroin, yaba tablets, phensedyl, Fairdil pills and crystal meth.
[@1] A second account rounded the cannabis amount to one thousand kilograms.
[@2] Among the seized items were 1.653 kg of gold, 14 firearms and 41 rounds of
ammunition. [@1] The list also included 238,807 tapentadol tablets, 146,302
alcohol-making tablets, 123,853 bottles of intoxicating syrup, 23,410
intoxicating injections and 215,316 Indian-made intoxicating tablets of various
types. [@1]

The release put the total worth of the seized drugs and smuggled goods at Tk
221.45 crore. [@1] BGB said it was working to ensure border security and prevent
smuggling, drug trafficking, illegal entry and human trafficking. [@1] It was
also conducting operations to prevent illegal stockpiling of fuel and edible oil
and attempts to create artificial shortages. [@1] BGB also strengthened patrols
and surveillance along the border to prevent illegal crossings and push-ins.
[@2]
