Police seized 30 kg of ganja packed in four suitcases and arrested four people
at Ponkunnam in Kottayam district. [@1] The accused were identified as Nandu
Renosh, Sharath Kumar, Shahrukh Khan and Adarsh P Reji, all natives of Erumely.
[@1]

Acting on a tip-off, police intercepted an interstate bus from Bengaluru around
8 a.m. and recovered the contraband concealed in the accused's baggage. [@1] A
second account describes the vehicle as a sleeper bus that arrived from
Bengaluru and says the 30 kg was found in four suitcases. [@2] Police said the
four had allegedly procured the ganja from Odisha and transported it via
Bengaluru to Erumely for sale. [@1]

They were produced before a magistrate. [@1]
