A repeatedly convicted prisoner was given three more years in prison after
keeping cannabis in his cell. [@1] He had been keeping about 560 grams of the
drug in his cell for a third party. [@1] He said he knew what he was doing and
expected to be caught so that he could stay in prison. [@1]

Prison officers found the cannabis during an unannounced sector search on 16
October 2026. [@1] He did not appear worried by the discovery. [@1] At his
immediate appearance, comparution immédiate, he said he had played and lost and
told the court to give him whatever it wanted. [@1]

Unannounced sector searches are common in French prisons, and finding drugs
there is not unusual either. [@1] His lawyer tried to speak about his client's
daily life in prison, referring to the absence of a financial mandate and of
visits. [@1]
