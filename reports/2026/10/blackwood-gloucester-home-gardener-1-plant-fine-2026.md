Phil DeAngelis is a home cannabis gardener whose house in Blackwood in
Gloucester Township was raided by local police over a single growing plant. [@2]
A SWAT team entered the house during the early-morning raid. [@2] DeAngelis said
officers body-slammed him and his wife and held the whole family, including
young children, at gunpoint. [@2] He was jailed for 9 days away from his family.
[@1] He resolved the case on 13 October 2026, a year after his arrest on 17
October 2025. [@1] The distribution charge was dropped and he pleaded to
breaking a local ordinance of disturbing the peace due to possession of
psilocybin, paying $533. [@1]

Officers found one live cannabis plant along with 5 pounds of cannabis flower
and some psilocybin mushrooms. [@1] In an earlier account he put the
non-cannabis haul at half an ounce of mushrooms in a bag with the plant and 5
pounds. [@2] A detective had claimed to see 30 plants over the fence, but only
one plant was found. [@1] Police weighed the single plant at 4 pounds including
its roots and stalk. [@2] No weapons were found despite the warrant also naming
weapons and distribution. [@2] His case bounced between Gloucester Township in
Camden County and the State of New Jersey. [@1] The State kept sending the case
back for lack of evidence. [@1]

DeAngelis said his children were traumatized by the raid. [@1] He said getting a
job at EV Cultivation helped him after the case. [@1]
