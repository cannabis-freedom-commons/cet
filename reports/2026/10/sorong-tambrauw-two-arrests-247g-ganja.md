Satresnarkoba Polres Tambrauw uncovered two ganja distribution cases in Kota
Sorong. [@1] The cases were presented at a press conference at Mapolres Sorong
on 26 November 2026. [@1] D.S., 25, a student, was the first suspect. [@1] He
was arrested at 23:00 WIT on 19 October 2026 on Jalan Basuki Rahmat Km 8 in Kota
Sorong during Operasi Pekat. [@1] Officers found eight white paper wraps of
ganja in a small bag in the seat compartment of his Yamaha Fino motorcycle. [@1]
The ganja from D.S. weighed 6.99 grams net. [@1]

W.A.P., 19, was the second suspect. [@1] He was arrested at 19:00 WIT on 24
October 2026 on Jalan Tanjung Rimoni in front of Lokalisasi Malanu in Kota
Sorong. [@1] Officers found seven large clear plastic bags of ganja on W.A.P.
[@1] A search of his home found eight more large clear plastic bags, on top of a
cupboard and in a black bag. [@1] The ganja from W.A.P. weighed 240.23 grams
net. [@1] Police put the total seized from the two men at 247.22 grams. [@2]
Officers seized D.S.'s red Yamaha Fino, which had no number plate. [@1] They
seized W.A.P.'s red-maroon Honda Beat Deluxe, also without a number plate. [@1]

Both men were held at the Sorong police detention centre. [@1] Police charged
them under Article 114(1) of Indonesia's 2009 Narcotics Law, subsidiarily
Article 111(1), for distribution and possession of class I narcotics. [@1]
Police said D.S. admitted distributing ganja for profit and for personal use.
[@1] W.A.P. said he acted to make a profit and to expand his friendships. [@1]
Iptu Ahmad said Polres Tambrauw would keep combating narcotics in its
jurisdiction, especially in Kota Sorong. [@1]
