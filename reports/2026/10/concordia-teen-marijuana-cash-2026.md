Comisaría Segunda officers detained a 15-year-old in Concordia who was carrying
several wrappings of suspected marijuana and cash. [@1] They stopped him during
a pedestrian patrol near José Larocca and Ramírez streets. [@1] A pat-down
revealed wrappings with a characteristic cannabis smell. [@1] A drug-detection
dog marked the presence of narcotics before a search warrant was obtained. [@1]
Police seized the wrappings of marijuana and cash made up of low-denomination
bills. [@1] The marijuana was divided into portions described as ready for sale.
[@2] Prosecutor Macarena Mondragón ordered him held on a charge of possession of
narcotics with intent to sell. [@1]

Comisaría Segunda and Comisaría Primera handled two separate procedures in the
operation. [@2] In the second, near Libertad and Padre Servín, officers moved to
stop another 15-year-old suspected of carrying a gun. [@1] He ran and threw away
a Smith & Wesson 'Trade Mark' .38 revolver with a five-round cylinder. [@1] The
gun was seized and the scientific police unit was called to examine it. [@1] The
duty prosecutor ordered the second boy held while the circumstances of the gun
possession were investigated. [@1]
