Varanasi Range police registered 138 cases under the NDPS Act in their drive
against drug trafficking. [@2] Officers recovered 2,849 kilograms of ganja in
Chandauli district in those cases. [@3] The same cases also yielded 1.9
kilograms of heroin and 540 grams of opium, which are not counted as cannabis.
[@3] Deputy Inspector General of Police Vaibhav Krishna, who heads the Varanasi
Range, gave the figures. [@3]

The campaign ran from 1 January to 31 October 2026 across Jaunpur, Ghazipur and
Chandauli. [@2] Police attached 24.59 crore rupees of illegal property under the
Gangster Act in Ghazipur. [@2] The attachments included 3.60 crore rupees of
property belonging to a man police described as a drug trafficker. [@3] They
booked 558 people under the Goonda Act across the three districts. [@3] Officers
opened 301 new history sheets in the same drive. [@1] Police also registered 648
liquor-smuggling cases and freed 687 cattle from smugglers. [@2]

Chandauli police moved against 164 of the 199 criminals on the reward list. [@3]
Krishna said the drive to attach crime-earned property, watch offenders and
arrest absconding accused would continue. [@3]
