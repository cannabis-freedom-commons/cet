National Civil Police officers arrested three men on the old Pan-American
highway in the El Carmen district of Cuscatlán Sur on the afternoon of Tuesday
27 October 2026. [@1] The detainees were identified as Julio Steven Sánchez
Galán and Edwin Omar Ramos Vásquez, both 22, and Erick Daniel Ramos Munguía, 25.
[@1] Ramos Munguía also had an outstanding arrest warrant for other aggravated
sexual assault, according to the police report. [@1]

The arrests were made during a preventive patrol at kilometre 35½ of the old
Pan-American highway. [@1] Officers saw a vehicle with suspicious occupants and
found marijuana portions hidden in two cloth bags and a paper container inside.
[@1] Diario El Salvador reported that the marijuana was divided into portions.
[@2] Police also seized cash and mobile phones. [@1] The National Civil Police
announced the arrests on its social media accounts that Tuesday afternoon. [@3]

The three were to be referred for the offence of possession and holding with
intent to traffic, the police institution said. [@1] Diario El Salvador reported
that they would be presented before the competent authorities to be charged with
possession and holding of drugs with intent to traffic. [@2]
