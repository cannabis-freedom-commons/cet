A woman accused of trafficking controlled substances was ordered held for three
months of preventive detention (detención preventiva) by Bolivia's justice
system after authorities discovered an illicit shipment whose destination was
the town of Macha, in Oruro. [@1] Prosecutor Fernando Pardo said the measure was
imposed after the public prosecutor's office (Ministerio Público) presented
evidence gathered in an operation on the night of 3 October 2026. [@1]

During a routine check at the Vichuloma checkpoint, police stopped a
public-service bus in which the suspect was carrying four bundles wrapped in
jute. [@1] After a search and field tests by specialised personnel, the bundles
were confirmed to contain 171 kilograms of marijuana, and the woman was arrested
in flagrante. [@1]

The prosecutor's office assumed the legal actions needed to keep the defendant
present while the investigation continued. [@1] The inquiry sought to establish
the links and reach of the network involved in transporting controlled
substances. [@1] The case remained under investigation. [@2]
