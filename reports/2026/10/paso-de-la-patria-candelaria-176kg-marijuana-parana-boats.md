Prefectura Naval Argentina seized almost 177 kilograms of marijuana in two
operations on the Paraná in Paso de la Patria, Corrientes, and Candelaria,
Misiones, with an estimated value above 675 million pesos. [@1] The force
announced the results on Thursday 1 October 2026. [@1] In both operations
motorboats came from the Paraguayan bank, left bundles on the Argentine side and
returned to Paraguay. [@1]

In Paso de la Patria a thermal camera picked up the crossing at kilometre 1.239
of the Paraná, near the Huajó stream. [@2] Officers found several men carrying
burlap sacks on motorcycles; the men fled into vegetation, abandoning the load
and a motorcycle. [@1] Ten bundles held 17 sealed bags of cannabis buds weighing
32 kilograms and 84 blocks of pressed marijuana weighing another 62 kilograms.
[@2] Prefectura valued the Paso de la Patria load at almost 360 million pesos.
[@1] The abandoned motorcycle was seized as well. [@1]

In Candelaria, Misiones, a second motorboat with several people aboard landed at
kilometre 1.604 of the Paraná, left bundles and returned to Paraguay. [@2] Seven
abandoned bundles there held 82 kilograms of bulk marijuana flowers. [@1] The
second load was valued at more than 316 million pesos. [@1] The published
partial weights add up to 176 kilograms, while the official balance gave almost
177 kilograms for the two procedures. [@1]

The cases went to the Federal Prosecutor's Office of Corrientes, Área de
Atención Inicial, led by Melina Beatriz Perborell, and to the Posadas
prosecutors' unit led by Débora Fores. [@1] The statement reported no
detentions. [@1]
