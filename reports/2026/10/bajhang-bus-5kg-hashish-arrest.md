Police in Bajhang arrested a man with a substance suspected to be hashish and
identified him as 30-year-old Katak Japrel of Durgathali Rural Municipality-5.
[@1] According to police, he was arrested along the Jaipathivi Highway at
Bagthala Bazaar in Bitthadchir Rural Municipality-8. [@1]

Police said the suspected hashish was discovered during a search of a bus
travelling from Chainpur to Dhangadhi on Sunday evening at around 5:30 pm. [@1]
The substance had been concealed in a black bag inside the bus’s rear luggage
compartment. [@1] The bus, bearing registration number Sudurpashchim Province
01-001 Kha 4211, was stopped for inspection when police found the substance.
[@1] According to police, the substance weighed 5.450 kilograms including the
plastic packaging and 5.195 kilograms net. [@1]

Japrel has been taken into custody and kept at the temporary police post in
Bagthala. [@1] Police said further investigation into the incident is underway.
