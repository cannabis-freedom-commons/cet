On 24 June 2026 a court acquitted a man prosecuted for attempted possession of
drugs but convicted his 28-year-old wife. [@1] Her sentence was eight months in
prison with a suspended term. [@1] The case arose from her visit to her husband
at Ittre prison on 2 February 2023, when she carried a small packet containing
32 grams of cannabis and 8 grams of cocaine. [@1] The husband was under pressure
from fellow inmates who demanded drugs and threatened the family, saying they
knew where the children went to school. [@1]

The husband's acquittal was based on attempted possession not being an offence
under the law and on the impossibility of recasting such a charge as a sale.
[@1] Both defendants were represented by the same lawyer. [@1] The husband also
told the court his wife had previously brought him cannabis "une ou deux fois"
before February 2003. [@1]

The woman filed opposition to the judgment despite the suspended sentence. [@1]
Her case was heard at the correctional hearing on 21 October, where she appeared
in person, said she lived on CPAS social welfare and expressed regret. [@1] The
prosecution asked the court to grant her the benefit of suspension of
pronouncement. [@1] The court followed that recommendation and ordered the drugs
and her mobile phone confiscated. [@1]
