A 19-year-old man was arrested in a buy-bust operation in Barangay 3, San
Nicolas, Ilocos Norte, on Sunday, 18 October 2026. [@1] He is a resident of
Barangay 11, San Fernando, Ilocos Norte, and was arrested at around 11:40 a.m.
[@1] The San Nicolas Municipal Police Station led the operation, with provincial
drug enforcement and intelligence units, the Regional Intelligence Division of
Police Regional Office 1, the Ilocos Norte Maritime Police Station and the 101st
Maneuver Company of the Regional Mobile Force Battalion 1, in coordination with
PDEA Region 1. [@1] Police said he faced alleged violations of the Comprehensive
Dangerous Drugs Act of 2002 and the Comprehensive Firearms and Ammunition
Regulation Act. [@1]

Police said the suspect allegedly sold a sachet of suspected marijuana to an
officer posing as a buyer. [@2] Two plastic sachets of suspected marijuana
leaves, stems and roots weighed about 20.28 grams; one of 10.35 grams was the
buy-bust item and the other, of 9.93 grams, was recovered from the suspect. [@1]
Police put the estimated standard drug price of the marijuana at P3,000. [@1]
Officers seized a North American Arms Black Widow .22-caliber revolver with no
serial number, two rounds of .22 Magnum ammunition and three rounds of .22 Long
Rifle ammunition. [@1] Police also recovered a P1,000 bill used as buy-bust
money, a gray pouch bag and P52 in cash they believed was the suspect's personal
money. [@3]

The items were inventoried and marked at the scene in the presence of the
suspect and the required witnesses, with two alternative recording devices used.
[@1] The suspect and the evidence were brought to the San Nicolas Municipal
Police Station, and police said he was taken there for follow-up investigation.
[@3] The police report did not specify whether formal charges had already been
filed. [@1]
