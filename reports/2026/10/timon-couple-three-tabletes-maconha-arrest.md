Military police in Timon, in the Brazilian state of Maranhão, detained a couple,
Douglas George de Carvalho Assunção and Isaura Evelyn Miranda Rabelo, after a
police approach in the Guarita neighbourhood on 19 October. [@1] The Military
Police said the approach took place near the Beira da Linha locality during
patrols in the region, and that officers found the illicit material in the
possession of the vehicle's occupants. [@1]

Officers seized three bars of a substance described as analogous to marijuana,
known locally as 'tabletes', along with two mobile phones, two sets of car keys
and a JBL speaker. [@1]

The suspects were identified as having illicit material in their possession
during the police operation. [@1] The couple and the seized objects were
presented at the Central de Flagrantes, the police station handling arrests in
flagrante, for legal procedures. [@1]
