A Department of Narcotics Control team led by Assistant Director Mostak Ahmed
raided a flat in Dhaka’s Bhatara area and arrested 19-year-old Saim Islam with
300 grams of cannabis. [@2] Saim, who is from Bakerganj in Barishal, lived in a
flat in the Lichubagan area of Joar Sahara in Bhatara. [@2] The first
information report says officers searched the flat on a tip-off and recovered
300 grams of cannabis wrapped in white polythene, and also seized a used mobile
phone. [@2]

After recovering the 300 grams of cannabis in Bhatara on 16 October, the DNC
said it uncovered an online network centred on university students selling kush
and synthetic cannabis through websites and private Telegram groups linked to
two private universities in Bashundhara. [@1] Saim told officials he was a
private-university student who had created a website two months earlier to sell
drugs online and then formed a secret Telegram group that had about 250 students
from two private universities as members. [@2] The DNC said the network operated
around two private universities in the capital’s Bashundhara Residential Area
through a website and a secret Telegram group where kush and synthetic cannabis
were readily available. [@2]

Following Saim’s arrest, DNC Inspector Mohammad Khorshed Alam filed a case with
Bhatara police station. [@2]
