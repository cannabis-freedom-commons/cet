Border Guard Bangladesh seized smuggled goods worth Tk 273.37 crore in
operations in border areas and other parts of the country during October 2026.
[@1] The Business Standard likewise reported that BGB seized smuggled goods,
arms and narcotics worth Tk273.37 crore during nationwide October drives
including border areas. [@2] BGB public relations officer Md Shariful Islam
announced the figures on Saturday 14 November. [@1]

The drugs recovered included 1,564 kg of ganja, alongside yaba tablets, heroin,
Phensedyl, crystal meth and foreign liquor. [@1] The Business Standard reported
1,564kg of cannabis among the month's narcotics. [@2]

BGB arrested 219 alleged smugglers for drug trafficking and other cross-border
smuggling. [@2] BGB separately detained 471 people for illegal border crossing,
comprising 350 Myanmar nationals, 114 Bangladeshis and seven Indians, and said
legal action was taken against those detained. [@1] The Business Standard
likewise reported legal action against the 114 Bangladeshis, seven Indians and
350 Myanmar nationals held for illegal crossing. [@2]
