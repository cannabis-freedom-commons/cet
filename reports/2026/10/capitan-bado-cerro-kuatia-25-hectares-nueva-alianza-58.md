On the fifth day of Operation Nueva Alianza 58, agents of Paraguay's National
Anti-Drug Secretariat (SENAD) and troops of the internal defence command (CODI)
destroyed 25 hectares of illegal marijuana crops at Cerro Kuatiá, in the
district of Capitán Bado, Amambay department. [@1] CODI reported that 34 tonnes
of marijuana ready for sale were found in the plantation and in collection
centres and destroyed. [@1] Officers also destroyed a large quantity of seeds
and precarious camps in the operational area. [@1]

Operation Nueva Alianza 58 began on Tuesday 13 October with SENAD agents and
CODI personnel in the locality of Menta, Capitán Bado. [@1] The operation was
part of coordinated action by the security institutions against drug trafficking
and organised crime in the border zone. [@1]

CODI said the work of locating and destroying marijuana plantations in Capitán
Bado would continue. [@1]
