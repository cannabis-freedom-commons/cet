City police arrested three men at East Point Colony in Chinnawaltair, within the
limits of the III Town police station in Visakhapatnam. [@2] East division
Assistant Commissioner of Police Lakshmana Murthy announced the arrests at a
press conference. [@2] Police said they seized 1 kg of hashish oil, a car,
₹10,000 in cash and two mobile phones from one of the men. [@2] From a second
man they recovered 20 grams of MDMA, ₹10,000 in cash and a mobile phone. [@2]

Eenadu reported the arrests as a seizure of 50 grams of MDMA and a litre of
liquid ganja from three gang members who had brought the drugs to sell to
college students. [@1] Udayam Digital reported that Task Force police took the
three into custody at East Point Colony as they came to sell drugs. [@3]

Gang members approach young people who come to bars to drink. [@1] They suggest
smoking ganja for a stronger high than alcohol. [@1] They invite the young
people to parties at resorts on the city's outskirts, where they get them used
to ganja and then to other drugs. [@1] Recruits are added to a WhatsApp group.
[@1] The gang takes payment online for the drugs it sells. [@1]

Police said they were continuing to investigate the source of the narcotics and
whether others were involved. [@2]
