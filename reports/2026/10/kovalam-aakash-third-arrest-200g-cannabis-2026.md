Kovalam police arrested R.L. Aakash, 26, of Kulathoor, as one more suspect in a
case involving MDMA, hashish oil and cannabis. [@1] Police said financial
transactions for the drugs were carried out through Aakash's bank account. [@1]
Police said they also found many photographs on his mobile phone. [@1] Aakash
was produced before a court and remanded in custody. [@1]

The case began on 30 June 2026, when police arrested Sachu, 27, of Thumba
Karimanal, known as Chicken, and Akhil Krishna, 29, of Pangappara, Sreekaryam.
[@1] Officers recovered from them more than 120 g of MDMA, 4.11 g of hashish oil
and 200 g of cannabis. [@1] Mathrubhumi reported that the city Dansaf team, a
police anti-drug unit, caught them at Kovalam junction on the national highway.
[@2] Questioning after the pair were handed to Kovalam police led investigators
to Aakash, the gang's main link, Mathrubhumi reported. [@2] Malayalam Press
reported that the gang had bought the drugs in Bengaluru for 150,000 rupees and
brought them to Thiruvananthapuram through Tamil Nadu. [@3]

The case is part of Operation Toofan, Kerala's statewide anti-drug campaign.
[@1] Sachu and his brother Sanju faced earlier drug cases, police said. [@1]
