Municipal police detained three people in colonia Margaritas in Ciudad Juárez on
13 October 2026. [@2] The detainees were Enrique G. C., 33, Diana R. T., 33, and
Daniel M. G., 39. [@1] Officers went to the address after a 911 call reported a
person held against his will at the crossing of avenida de las Américas and José
Martí in colonia Margaritas. [@1] A Salvadoran man told them he had been kept at
a house metres from that crossing by three people. [@1] He said the detainees
had offered to help him cross illegally into the United States. [@1] The
municipal force said the three were held on suspicion of crimes against the
migration law as well as crimes against health. [@1]

At the house officers found about 27 kilograms of marijuana in plastic boxes and
bags of different sizes. [@1] The federal Attorney General's Office (FGR) later
put the amount at 26 kilograms 605 grams. [@2] The FGR said the officers
arrested them in flagrante as they carried two plastic boxes and 33 ziploc bags
holding marijuana. [@2]

A federal judge bound the three over for trial on a charge of possessing
marijuana for sale, in a ruling reported on 25 October 2026. [@2] The judge also
imposed mandatory pre-trial detention. [@2] The three were held in state prisons
number 2 and 3. [@2] The ruling does not amount to a conviction and the three
are presumed innocent, the FGR statement said. [@2]
