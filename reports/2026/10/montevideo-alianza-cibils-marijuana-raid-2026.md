Montevideo police detained a 14-year-old during a raid at a home in the camino
Alianza and camino Cibils area, seizing arms, ammunition, marijuana and various
objects. [@1] The search was carried out by the Investigations Area of the
Operational Zone IV headquarters as part of an investigation into an offence
involving a gunshot, according to the police report reviewed by Montevideo
Portal. [@1] Officers saw the teenager leave the home with a bag and what
appeared to be a long weapon; after a halt call he tried to escape towards a
nearby farm but was subdued. [@1] The operation took place on Saturday 3 October
in western Montevideo. [@2]

Among the seized items officers found a package of pressed marijuana and a
precision scale. [@1] Police also seized a 9 mm pistol with its serial digits
removed, an unnumbered carbine-type submachine gun of the same calibre and a
compressed-air pistol, along with an extended magazine holding 40 rounds, a
cartridge belt and cartridges of different calibres. [@1] Other seized property
included a motorcycle, a bicycle, a pressure washer, two lawnmowers, a wood
cutter, a concrete mixer, a welding machine, a grinder, aluminium windows,
ceramics and construction materials. [@1]

The Flagrancy and Adolescents prosecutor's offices were informed of the
operation while the investigation continued. [@1]
