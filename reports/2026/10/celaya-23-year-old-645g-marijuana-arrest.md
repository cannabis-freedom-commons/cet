State police officers detained 23-year-old Guillermo “N” in the community of San
Martín de Camargo, in Celaya, after locating suspected marijuana in the backpack
he was carrying. [@1] He is originally from Neutla, in the municipality of
Comonfort, Guanajuato. [@1] The encounter occurred on 29 October on Morelos
street, where officers of the Fuerzas de Seguridad Pública del Estado (FSPE),
Guanajuato’s state police, were conducting preventive patrols and approached him
for a check to which he agreed voluntarily. [@1]

A personal inspection found no illicit objects on him, but a search of his
backpack uncovered a bag containing green herb with characteristics similar to
marijuana. [@1] Police weighed the herb at 645 grams of suspected marijuana and
described that amount as equivalent to 645 doses, or about one gram per dose.
[@1]

Officers placed the detained man and the seized substance at the disposal of the
Fiscalía General del Estado, the state prosecutor’s office, in Celaya for
further investigation. [@1]
