Navy and army personnel in La Yesca, Nayarit, eradicated three marijuana plots
holding about 51,600 plants. [@1] Federal and state authorities presented the
results at the state security meeting chaired by Governor Miguel Ángel Navarro
Quintero, covering operations carried out on Tuesday 21 October 2026. [@2] In
Agua Caliente, in the municipality of Acaponeta, army, National Guard and state
police detained a man on suspicion of crimes against health and seized a truck,
two packages of marijuana, mobile phones and cash. [@1]

Officers in the same locality also seized a motorcycle and 220 live cartridges,
which were handed to the federal prosecutor's office. [@1] In Huajicori, forces
destroyed a camp and seized six magazines, cartridges and two improvised
explosive devices, which the army destroyed. [@1] In Tepic, the state
prosecutor's office, navy and army seized a property and 20 slot machines, while
marijuana was seized during patrols in the Los Sauces neighbourhood. [@1]

The National Guard reported reviewing 16 fuel tankers as part of action against
oil theft and inspecting 763 people and 326 pieces of luggage at Amado Nervo
Riviera Nayarit International Airport. [@1]
