A guarantee judge on 3 October 2026 ordered provisional detention for a
19-year-old man over the alleged sale of brownies containing marijuana around a
school in La Chorrera, Panamá Oeste. [@1] The precautionary measure followed an
investigation by the Specialised Prosecutor for Drug-Related Offences of Panamá
Oeste with agents of the National Police Intelligence Directorate. [@1]

The case stems from 30 September 2026, when the authorities carried out three
operations as part of Operation Loky to check alleged dealing in illicit
substances. [@1] Officers apprehended the youth during those actions, alleging
he sold brownies prepared with marijuana. [@1] The three Loky operations were
carried out around the school in La Chorrera district in coordination with the
National Police Intelligence Directorate. [@2]

The prosecutor put the investigation's elements before the judge, who ordered
the accused held provisionally while inquiries continue. [@1] The Public
Ministry said it will keep investigating this type of drug sale near schools.
[@1] Authorities have reported only that the brownies contained marijuana,
giving no amount seized and saying nothing on whether students bought or ate
them. [@2]
