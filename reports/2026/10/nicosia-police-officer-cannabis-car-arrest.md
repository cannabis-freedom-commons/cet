A 42-year-old female police officer was arrested after officers allegedly found
cannabis and cannabis cigarettes in her car during a roadside check in Nicosia.
[@1] Members of the drug squad, YKAN, stopped her car at around 3 p.m. on
Monday. [@1] She was arrested on suspicion of illegal drug possession and taken
into custody. [@1] She was expected to appear before the Nicosia District Court
on Tuesday, where a case was expected to be filed against her. [@1] The chief of
police instructed that procedures be launched immediately to suspend her. [@1]

During the search officers found and seized 3.4 grams of cannabis, four handmade
cigarettes containing cannabis and two partially smoked cigarettes also
containing cannabis. [@1]

Police said the force has zero tolerance for conduct by its members that goes
against the law, its regulations or its principles. [@1] Police added that all
cases are investigated transparently and handled without exceptions. [@1] The
drug squad is investigating the case. [@1]
