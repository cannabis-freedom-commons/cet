Police arrested two foreign nationals in the case. [@4] The arrests took place
in Banodzha, Tsqaltubo municipality. [@4] Rustavi 2 reported that both were
Ukrainian citizens. [@4] Both lived in a rented flat in Kutaisi. [@4] After the
arrest both refused to testify, exercising their right to silence. [@4]

Officers searched the suspects and the locations they identified and seized
almost 28 kg of hashish in 267 packages. [@3] The same searches yielded almost
12 kg of methadone in 117 packages, which is excluded from the cannabis figures.
[@2] According to the investigation, the group supplied Imereti and Adjara with
wholesale batches and hid large consignments in forest areas, where couriers
repackaged them for sale. [@4] The Interior Ministry estimated the black-market
value of the whole haul at more than 14 million lari. [@1]

Prosecutors charged both with illegal acquisition, storage and facilitating
large-scale drug sales by an organised group under articles 260 and 260⁴. [@4] A
court ordered both held in custody for the investigation. [@4] The offences
carry up to 20 years in prison or life imprisonment. [@5]
