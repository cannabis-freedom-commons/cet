The criminal court of appeal attached to the Oran justice tribunal sentenced a
63-year-old retiree identified by the initials S-K to 20 years of firm
imprisonment. [@1] He was prosecuted for belonging to a criminal network
specialising in trafficking and illicit import of narcotics. [@1] The public
prosecutor had requested life imprisonment. [@1]

He had been caught red-handed trying to transport more than 6 quintals and 75
kilograms of cannabis. [@1] The investigation began after the investigation
section of the Oran National Gendarmerie command received information about a
drug load coming from the Maghnia border area and bound for eastern wilayas.
[@1] Officers intercepted him on the East-West Highway while he drove a Renault
Master with the cannabis hidden under a load of fruit and vegetables, and a
search of the vehicle uncovered the load. [@1]

At the hearing he admitted working for a Moroccan baron who had him carry drug
loads for 20 million centimes per operation. [@1] He had previously worked in a
public establishment in Tlemcen wilaya before retiring, experience that had
given him good knowledge of border routes. [@1] He lives in a wilaya in the east
of the country. [@1]
