Officers of CBŚP's Elbląg division detained a 41-year-old man on a recreational
plot in Ostróda County as he packed cartons into a passenger car. [@1,3,4] The man lived in Elbląg. [@4,5,7] Inside
the car were 20 vacuum-packed packages of marihuana. [@1,3,4,5,7] A further search of a
recreational building, a garage and another vehicle found 14 more packages of
the drug. [@1,3,4,5,7] Police took the man to the District Prosecutor's Office in
Ostróda, where he was formally charged. [@1,3,4] He was charged with participation in the trafficking of a significant quantity of intoxicating substances. [@5,6,7] He faces two to twelve years in prison if convicted. [@4,5,7] On the prosecution's application
the District Court in Ostróda ordered his temporary arrest. [@1,3,4] The court ordered him held in temporary custody for three months. [@4,5,7]

In total the officers secured 34 kilograms of ready dried marihuana. [@1,3,4,5,6,7] They
also recovered items indicating the use of camouflage methods and preparation of
the drugs for distribution on a wide scale. [@1,7]

Information gathered by officers indicated the property could be used to store
drugs before they were distributed further. [@1,4,5,7] Officers learned of the plot while investigating a group dealing in wholesale drug trafficking in Elbląg County. [@6,7] The plot was in Miłomłyn commune, Ostróda County. [@4,5,7] Officers began verifying the
information in the second half of October. [@1] Officers entered the property on 23 October 2026. [@4,6] The information indicated that
the drugs were stored on the property before further distribution. [@2] The man
was known to have contacts with the football hooligan milieu of an Elbląg sports
club. [@1,4,5,7] The case remains open as investigators seek to establish all the
circumstances and identify any others involved. [@1,4,5,7] On 3 November 2026 the regional prosecutor's office in Elbląg took over the investigation. [@6]
