Allan Nisbett of Ramsbury, Nevis, pleaded guilty to possession of cannabis with
intent to supply and cultivation of cannabis when he appeared before District
"C" Magistrate Reynold Benjamin on Tuesday, October 13. [@1] Magistrate Benjamin
fined him $7,000 for possession with intent to supply, with four months allowed
to pay, and said failure to pay would bring eight months' imprisonment; on the
separate cultivation count he was convicted and cautioned. [@1]

The case arose from a search of Nisbett's residence on May 21, 2025, by officers
of the Task Force Unit accompanied by members of the St. Kitts and Nevis Defence
Force. [@1] Asked whether he had anything to declare, he replied that he had "a
little weed" and pointed out the cannabis, which officers confiscated. [@1] A
further search uncovered approximately 200 cannabis seedlings and 100 plants of
varying heights growing in the yard; Nisbett admitted the plants were cannabis,
was taken into police custody and was charged with both offences. [@1] The
Government Analyst confirmed the plant material was cannabis weighing 925
grammes, with an estimated street value of $7,013. [@1]

Nisbett told the court he was a heavy cannabis user who grew the plants for
personal consumption after his mother died of cancer, mixing them with ginger
for tea, and insisted he did not sell cannabis. [@1] He also claimed a
politician had misled him into believing he could grow as many cannabis plants
as he wanted on his property, and asked for leniency. [@1] Magistrate Benjamin
acknowledged the claim, suggesting the politician may have been seeking votes,
but reminded Nisbett that the law limited how much cannabis he could possess and
indicated the guilty plea would be considered. [@1]
