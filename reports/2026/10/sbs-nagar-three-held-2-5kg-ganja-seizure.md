Police teams from different police stations in Shaheed Bhagat Singh Nagar
district detained three alleged accused in raids against people engaged in the
drug trade. [@1] Senior Superintendent of Police Tushar Gupta said the raids
were carried out daily under the Yudh Nashian Virudh campaign against drug
traffickers. [@1]

Officers recovered 2 kg 500 g of ganja and 35 g of heroin from the three
detainees. [@1] Only the 2.5 kg of ganja is counted as cannabis in this report,
while the 35 g of heroin from the same operation is a separate non-cannabis
seizure. [@1] The published photograph shows the detained accused with police
personnel. [@1]

Police registered cases against the detainees and began further investigation.
[@1] Gupta said the district police campaign against drug traffickers would
continue. [@1]
