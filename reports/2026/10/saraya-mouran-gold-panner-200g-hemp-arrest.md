A 36-year-old Senegalese gold panner was arrested on Thursday 29 October 2026 at
the Mouran border control post in Saraya department, south-east Senegal. [@1]
Officers stopped him at about 19:22 during a patrol of the frontier zone with
Mali. [@1] He was coming from Bountou in Mali and heading for Dalato, and lived
at Balakonko. [@2]

A search found two plastic bags of loose Indian hemp (cannabis) weighing 200
grams in total, 100 grams in each bag. [@1] He admitted the drug was his and
said he had bought it in Mali to supply a resale network in Senegal. [@1]
Officers also seized a Bravo motorcycle, two mobile phones of the Samsung and
Infinix brands and 46,500 CFA francs, and placed everything under seal. [@1]

The Moussala border sector, which runs the Mouran post, announced the arrest as
one of two interpellations it had made. [@3] The inquiry was opened for
trafficking of Indian hemp. [@1] He was handed to the Saraya prosecutor's office
on Friday 30 October 2026. [@1]
