Anti-Drug and Smuggling Unit officers searched a villa on Victoria Road at
Trou-d'Eau-Douce on Friday 23 October 2026. [@2] They found a white cloth bag
holding a black plastic bag with 792 grams of cannabis, which police valued at
Rs 1,425,600. [@2] Two occupants, a 38-year-old woman and a 30-year-old man,
were arrested on suspicion of possessing cannabis for delivery, with an
allegation of trafficking. [@2] A search of the man's home at Palmar found no
other drugs or incriminating items. [@2] Three mobile phones were seized during
the initial investigation. [@1]

The 30-year-old man was provisionally charged with drug trafficking before the
Flacq court and kept in detention. [@1] The ADSU's East division first sent the
file to the Director of Public Prosecutions, who returned it for further
investigation. [@1] The case was then transferred to the Central Criminal
Investigation Department, which must take up several aspects of it. [@1]

A lawyer is suspected of giving a mobile phone to his detained client during a
private interview at the police station over a weekend. [@1] CCTV images from
the police station are said to have revealed the handover. [@1] A policewoman
present recorded the facts in the diary book and alerted her superiors, and was
later questioned by ADSU investigators. [@1] Investigators are also examining
possible links between trafficking networks in the region and the Dreamer yacht
case, in which a large cannabis cargo was found off Mauritius. [@1]
