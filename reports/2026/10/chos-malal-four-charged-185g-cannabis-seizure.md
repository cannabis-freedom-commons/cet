Four people were charged in Chos Malal with alleged joint sale of cocaine and
cannabis after searches of homes and business premises. [@1] Prosecutors alleged
the four acted with divided tasks to sell cocaine and cannabis at least between
16 July and 8 October. [@1] The prosecution said A.A.A. supplied the substances
and directed weighing, cutting and sale, while I.M.N., P.K.S. and L.A.F. handled
holding, sale and virtual-wallet money movements. [@1] All four were charged as
co-authors with possession of drugs for sale, and A.A.A. was additionally
charged with illegal possession of a civilian firearm. [@2]

Officers searched homes and shops in Chos Malal, finding 185 grams of cannabis
in one house along with other drug quantities and 81.6 grams of compacted
cocaine in another suspect's home. [@1] A second account likewise reported 81.6
grams of cocaine in one home and 185 grams of cannabis in another, plus other
drug amounts. [@2] Officers also seized precision scales, cash, firearms,
ammunition and other items investigators linked to alleged sale. [@1] A
provincial police account of the Thursday-night operation said four adults were
detained and two others were held for identification. [@3] That preliminary
police account reported 270 doses of cocaine hydrochloride and more than 300
grams of cannabis, plus scales, a .22 pistol, phones and a car. [@3]

Prosecutor Natalia Rivera sought pretrial detention, but guarantees judge
Eduardo Egea freed the four with four-month no-approach and no-contact orders
toward two witnesses. [@1] The prosecution requested review of that refusal
before a three-judge guarantees panel. [@1] The judge extended the closure of
A.A.A.'s home for two months as an alleged sale site. [@1] The court also froze
the accused's virtual-wallet funds except for L.A.F., while the sale
investigation continues pending the review hearing. [@1]
