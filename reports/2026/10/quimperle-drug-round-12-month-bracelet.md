On 27 October 2026 gendarmes in Quimperlé, in Finistère, stopped a man in his
thirties because a rear light on his car was not working; he was at the time
finishing a round of seven or eight clients. [@1] The client round took him
between Finistère and Morbihan. [@1]

He was a consumer of drugs himself and sold heroin, cocaine, ketamine and
cannabis to the clients on his round. [@1] Before the court he admitted
everything, including having driven again under the influence of cocaine despite
his driving licence having been cancelled. [@1] He acknowledged that above all
it was a drug-trafficking operation between Morbihan and Finistère. [@1] He had
been convicted eight times before and, at the bar of the Quimper court, trembled
and bit his nails. [@1]

The Quimper court sentenced him to twelve months under an electronic bracelet.
