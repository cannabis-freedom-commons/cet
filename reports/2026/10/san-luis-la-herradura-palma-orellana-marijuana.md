Nelson Alfredo Palma Orellana, 25, was detained on Thursday 29 October 2026 by
the Policía Nacional Civil (PNC) and the Fuerza Armada de El Salvador (FAES)
because he was carrying several portions of marijuana, El Salvador's national
civil police and armed forces said. [@2] The arrest took place in the colonia
Bella Mar 2 of the barrio Guadalupe, in the jurisdiction of San Luis La
Herradura, municipality of La Paz Centro, according to the police. [@2] The PNC
reported that the 25-year-old had several doses of marijuana confiscated in
pasaje Bella Mar 2, barrio Guadalupe, in the district of San Luis La Herradura,
La Paz Centro. [@1]

The armed forces published photographs of at least 12 portions of marijuana and
confirmed that a mobile phone was also seized from him. [@2] Diario1 reported
that police teams carried out the intervention in coordination with elements of
the armed forces. [@3]

After the arrest, the security authorities said he would be referred to the
courts for possession and holding of drugs. [@2] Diario1 said the PNC would
place him at the disposal of the Fiscalía General de la República, which would
present him to the corresponding court to face the possession and holding
charge. [@3]
