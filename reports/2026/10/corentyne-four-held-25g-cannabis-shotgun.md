Four 19-year-old men from Albion, Corentyne, were taken into police custody
after an operation at Number One Road, Corentyne, Berbice. [@1] Police said they
went to the area at about 10:40 am after receiving information and saw the men
with two motorcycles, one bearing registration CS 7869 and the other
unregistered. [@1] The arrests were made on 12 October 2026. [@2]

Officers searched the men and found a 12-gauge shotgun and a black haversack
holding 18 12-gauge cartridges and 15 small zip-lock bags of leaves, seeds and
stems suspected to be cannabis. [@1] Police weighed the suspected cannabis at
25.3 grammes. [@1] Two of the men were identified as David Ramphal, called
Tampoo, of Belvedere, and Dedesh Naris of Albion Front. [@2]

They faced charges of possession of narcotics for the purpose of trafficking,
possession of a firearm without a licence and possession of ammunition without a
licence. [@2] They appeared before Magistrate Matthias at the New Amsterdam
Magistrate's Court on Thursday. [@3] Both were remanded to prison and are
expected to return to court for further proceedings. [@2]
