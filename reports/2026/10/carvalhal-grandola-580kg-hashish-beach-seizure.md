Portugal's Maritime Police seized 12 bales holding about 580 kilograms of
hashish near Carvalhal, in the municipality of Grândola, Setúbal district. [@2]
The seizure was announced on Friday 2 October 2026. [@3] Officers from the
Setúbal local command of the Maritime Police found several bales in a beach area
while on coastal patrol and surveillance. [@2] A search-and-recognition
operation along the coastline was set up to find other volumes or items of
interest to the investigation. [@2] The search area was progressively widened,
and officers from the Maritime Police's Sines local command supported the
operation. [@2] Tests on the seized product confirmed that it was hashish, a
Maritime Police source told the Lusa news agency. [@2]

The facts were reported to the competent judicial authority, which took over
direction of the investigation. [@4] Diário de Notícias first described the 12
bales, together weighing about 580 kilograms, as suspected narcotics before the
tests confirmed hashish. [@4] The National Maritime Authority said searches
along the coast were continuing, with Maritime Police units staying in the area
to detect material that might have washed ashore or be scattered there. [@1] TVI
Notícias reported that searches were continuing and that several hypotheses were
being considered to explain how the drug reached the beach. [@5]
