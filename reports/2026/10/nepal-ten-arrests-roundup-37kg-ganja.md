Nepal Police arrested 10 people in separate drug cases across several districts.
[@1] The arrests were made on Monday and Monday night, 26 October 2026. [@3] At
Tribhuvan International Airport, police arrested two women with about 14 kg of
ganja outside the arrival area on Monday night. [@2] They were Sheikh
Shabanabanu Yar Mahammad, 46, and Ansari Sharifa Abbas, 35, both from Gujarat,
who arrived on a Malaysian Batik Air flight. [@1]

In Siraha, police arrested 41-year-old Shiva Shankar Sah with 6 kg 270 g of
ganja at Dagar Tol in Dhangadhimai Municipality-10 on Monday afternoon as he
rode a motorcycle. [@2] In the same municipality, a search of 56-year-old Jhanak
Bahadur Thakuri's house in Dhangadhimai-14 turned up 16 kg of material suspected
to be ganja on Monday evening. [@2] In Kawasoti Municipality-15 in Nawalparasi
East, police arrested 48-year-old Buddhiram Gurau with 1 kg 138 g of ganja. [@2]

In Kathmandu, two men were arrested with 90 tramadol tablets at Danchhi Chowk in
Kageshwori Manohara-4 the night before the statement. [@2] The remaining four
arrests were for 2.62 g of suspected brown heroin in Bhimdatta-4 in Kanchanpur
and 4.78 g of suspected brown sugar in Inaruwa-4 in Sunsari, which are not
counted in the cannabis figures. [@1] Police said the necessary investigation
was under way in all the cases; no charges or court dates were reported. [@2]
