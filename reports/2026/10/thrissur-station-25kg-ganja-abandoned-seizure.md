Railway Police and the Railway Protection Force found 2.5 kg of ganja abandoned
on the south side of platform 3 at Thrissur railway station during a joint check
of the platforms. [@1] Police said the carrier had probably abandoned the ganja
on the platform on seeing the inspection and fled. [@1] Police registered a case
over the find and began an investigation. [@1]

The check was led by Railway Police sub-inspectors T. D. Biju and Jayakumar and
civil police officers Smilesh and Prasanth, together with an RPF team including
Shinoj Kumar, sub-inspector Geethu Krishnan, head constable M. B. Binu and
constable M. Najeem. [@1] Police said the seized ganja would be produced before
the court. [@1] Police said further investigation was under way to identify the
accused because there were no CCTV cameras in the part of the station where the
ganja was found. [@1] No arrest was reported at the scene, with police saying
work to trace the person who left the ganja was continuing. [@1]
