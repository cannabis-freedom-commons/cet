Police officers from the Le Port commissariat went to a home in Saint-Paul,
Réunion, on Tuesday 13 October 2026 as part of an investigation into a theft.
[@1] They found drugs there and called in gendarmes from the Saint-Paul brigade.
[@1] The gendarmes seized 1.7 kg of cannabis herb, 100 g of cannabis resin and
16 g of cocaine. [@1] They also found cash and an airsoft revolver replica. [@1]
One person was arrested and placed in police custody. [@1]

During questioning the man admitted he had organised drug trafficking in the
commune. [@1] He was brought before the court on Thursday 15 October 2026 and
sentenced to 20 months in prison, 10 of them suspended. [@1] The 10 firm months
are to be served under an electronic bracelet as a sentence adjustment. [@1]
