Anti-Narcotics Department officers arrested a suspect in downtown Amman who was
cultivating marijuana seedlings inside his home. [@1] Officers seized 2,300
marijuana seedlings at the home. [@1]

The arrest was one of five drug smuggling and trafficking operations in recent
days that the Public Security Directorate announced on 24 June 2026. [@1] Nine
suspects were arrested across the five operations. [@1] At Queen Alia
International Airport, officers arrested an Arab national arriving with six
litres of liquid cocaine concealed inside containers of various products in his
luggage. [@1] At the Jaber border crossing, security personnel seized 150,000
narcotic pills hidden inside the spare tire of a cargo vehicle and arrested
three people inside the Kingdom who were allegedly preparing to receive the
shipment. [@1]

In Mafraq Governorate, officers arrested an armed wanted suspect linked to
regional drug smuggling networks and seized narcotic pills during the raid. [@1]
In Balqa, two alleged drug traffickers were arrested and 9,000 narcotic pills
were confiscated. [@1] The Public Security Directorate said investigations were
continuing and that all suspects had been referred to the relevant judicial
authorities. [@1]
