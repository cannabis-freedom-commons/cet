Iraq's Interior Ministry said on Monday that anti-narcotics forces arrested a
suspected drug dealer and seized 29 kilograms of hashish, a concentrated
cannabis resin. [@1] The General Directorate for Narcotics and Psychotropic
Substances Affairs described the man as one of the most dangerous traffickers in
Anbar province, known for his ability to disguise himself and change his
smuggling and distribution methods. [@3] The directorate said the hashish had
been prepared for distribution. [@3]

The directorate said its Special Investigations and Covert Surveillance
Departments carried out the operation after obtaining judicial warrants from the
investigating judge of the Central Criminal Court that handles drug cases. [@2]
Officers tracked the suspect's movements and arrested him in the act using GPS
technology as he went to receive the drug shipment. [@2]

The Interior Ministry said it had dismantled 230 drug trafficking networks,
including 27 international rings, over the past three years. [@1] The
directorate said it would keep carrying out pre-emptive operations against
trafficking and distribution networks. [@4]
