Nearly 37 kilograms of cannabis resin were found in the luggage of a passenger
arriving from Paris-Roissy Charles-de-Gaulle at Marcel-Henry international
airport in Mayotte on Sunday 21 June 2026. [@1] After customs retention, the
passenger was placed in garde à vue (police custody) at the gendarmerie’s
research section and presented to the judicial court of Mamoudzou for a
comparution immédiate (immediate appearance). [@1]

During passenger checks a customs detection dog marked two suitcases, and a
search found the resin packed in plastic-wrapped savonnettes, bars of about 100
grams each. [@1] Each of the two suitcases contained more than 18 kilograms of
the drug. [@1] State services called it one of the largest cannabis resin
seizures made in Mayotte in recent years. [@1]

The operation coincided with a visit by a delegation of MILDECA, the
interministerial mission against drugs and addictive behaviours, whose officers
were at the airport and watched the customs team at work. [@1] The prefect of
Mayotte, Frédéric Poisot, congratulated the customs officers for their vigilance
and effectiveness, saying that the fight against drug trafficking remains a
major security and public-health issue on the territory. [@1]
