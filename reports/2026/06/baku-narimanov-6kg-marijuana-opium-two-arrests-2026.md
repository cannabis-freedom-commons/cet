Officers of the Narimanov district police department in Baku carried out an
operation against illegal drug trafficking. [@1] They detained 27-year-old Pasha
Bayramov and his 46-year-old female accomplice. [@1]

The drugs were found during a search of the two detainees. [@4] Officers seized
more than six kilograms of narcotics, described only as marijuana and opium.
[@1] The Interior Ministry told Report that the seized drugs were more than six
kilograms of marijuana and opium. [@2]

Investigators said the two planned to organise drug sales by making 'stash
placements' at various addresses across the capital. [@1] A criminal case was
opened over the seizure. [@1] A court ordered both suspects held in custody.
[@1] A preventive measure of detention was chosen for both individuals by court
decision. [@3] Investigative actions continue. [@4]
