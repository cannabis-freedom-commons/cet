Three people were arrested at a road check in Cayenne, French Guiana, on 23 June
2026, after police found a large quantity of drugs in the vehicle they were
travelling in. [@1] Officers from the anti-crime squad, the Brigade
anticriminalité (BAC), seized more than 10 kilograms of cannabis resin and more
than 2,500 euros in cash. [@1]

The BAC crew stopped the vehicle and carried out checks inside, discovering the
drugs and a sum of money that investigators suspected came from trafficking.
[@1] The three occupants were arrested and placed in police custody. [@1] After
interviews and investigations by detectives, two of the three were brought
before the Cayenne judicial court on facts related to drug trafficking. [@1]

Both were remanded in custody under a committal order to await judgment. [@1]
