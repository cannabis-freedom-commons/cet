Law-enforcement officers in Armenia acted on operational information they had
received. [@1] The press service of the Police of the Ministry of Internal
Affairs of the Republic of Armenia told NEWS.am about the operation. [@1] The
detention took place on 23 June on the Jrazvej–Arinj road. [@1] Two residents of
Yerevan, aged 33 and 34, were detained on justified suspicion. [@1] The
suspicion concerned acquisition, storage and sale of a narcotic substance that
had been brought into the country by contraband. [@1] The two men were taken
together with the Honda car operated by one of them. [@1] They were delivered to
the Nor Nork department of the Community Police. [@1]

During the personal search of the 34-year-old man, officers found about 9
kilograms of marijuana. [@1] According to preliminary data, the 34-year-old man
was to sell the marijuana. [@1] Both men were presented to the preliminary
investigation body. [@1] The preliminary investigation is continuing. [@1]
