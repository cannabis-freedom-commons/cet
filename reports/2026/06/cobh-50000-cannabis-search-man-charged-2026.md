Officers from the Cork North East Divisional Drugs Unit searched a residential
property in the Cobh area of Cork on Saturday 6 June 2026. [@3] A man in his 30s
and a woman in her 20s were arrested at the scene and detained at Garda stations
in the Southern Region. [@1] The man has since been charged. [@1] He was due
before Midleton District Court on Monday 8 June 2026. [@3] The woman has since
been released without charge, and a file will be prepared for the Director of
Public Prosecutions. [@1]

Gardaí recovered cannabis with an estimated value of approximately €50,000
during the search. [@2] Gardaí also recovered a small quantity of cocaine during
the same search. [@1] Officers also found utensils and equipment linked to the
mixing and preparation of drugs at the premises. [@3] The seized substances were
sent to Forensic Science Ireland for further analysis. [@1]

The search was carried out as part of Operation Tara, the nationwide crackdown
on the sale and distribution of controlled drugs. [@1] Gardaí said
investigations were ongoing. [@1]
