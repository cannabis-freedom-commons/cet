Police carried out a check on a 37-year-old man in Galabnik village, Radomir
municipality, during a specialised operation against drug offences. [@1] The man
was already known to police and had been convicted several times. [@1] He was
detained for up to 24 hours under the Interior Ministry Law. [@1]

Officers found a packet of about 55 grams of dry green leaf material in the
engine compartment of the Audi he was driving, and a field test reacted to
marijuana. [@1] A search of a property he occupied in Galabnik turned up a
further quantity of over 26 grams of marijuana. [@3] Police said the total
marijuana found exceeded 80 grams. [@2] The same search also found a press of
white crystalline substance weighing about one kilogram that reacted to
methamphetamine, a separate drug excluded from the cannabis figures. [@3]
Officers also seized an electronic scale and numerous small plastic bags
intended for portioning drugs. [@2]

The check was carried out by criminalists from the Radomir police department and
officers from the special tactical actions unit of the Pernik regional police
directorate. [@1] A pre-trial case was opened under Article 354a, paragraph 1 of
the Criminal Code, the prosecutor was informed and the investigation continued
under her supervision. [@1]
