Officers of the research and intervention brigade (BRI) of the Naâma wilaya
police in western Algeria dismantled a cross-border organised criminal network,
the Directorate General of National Security (DGSN) said. [@2] The network
specialised in drug trafficking, the DGSN statement said. [@1] A raid on a cache
fitted out to conceal the prohibited goods led to the arrest of four members of
the network. [@2]

Operational investigations by the brigade's officers identified the members of
the network in the context of the fight against drug-trafficking crimes. [@2]
Officers seized more than 98 kg of kif traité, treated cannabis resin, that was
said to come from Morocco. [@3] The quantity was described as nearly a quintal.
[@1] Officers also recovered a utility vehicle and a motorcycle used in the
trafficking. [@2]

The operation was conducted under the supervision of the competent prosecutor.
[@2] The suspects were brought before the public prosecutor at the court of Aïn
Sefra. [@2]
