The marijuana was found in a hand bag and a backpack thrown by a man after he
detected a Gendarmería patrol near the international border pass with Bolivia.
[@1] Gendarmes of the SITEVIF border-surveillance system of Escuadrón 21 "La
Quiaca" were patrolling the border zone of La Quiaca, in Jujuy province, when
they observed a man in the area. [@1] On seeing the uniformed officers, the man
threw away the bags and fled towards the neighbouring country; no arrest was
reported and his identity was not published. [@1]

The Federal Prosecutor's Office of Jujuy ordered the area guarded, witnesses
summoned and the abandoned bundles taken to the unit's headquarters for a
search. [@1] Inside the bag and the backpack the gendarmes found 10 amorphous
packages holding a compact vegetable substance. [@1] Forensic staff of the
Force's criminalistics group ran a Narcotest field test, which came back
positive for marijuana, with a total weight of 10 kilos 772 grams (10.772 kg).
[@1]

El Tribuno de Jujuy reported the same seizure of 10 kilos 772 grams of marijuana
in the border zone and the same flight towards the neighbouring country. [@2] By
order of the intervening judge, the drugs were seized. [@1]
