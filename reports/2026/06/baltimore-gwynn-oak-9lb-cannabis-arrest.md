The Baltimore City Sheriff's Office carried out an enforcement action on 1 June
2026 at a business in the 4700 block of Gwynn Oak Avenue in Baltimore. [@1] The
investigation began when an undercover sheriff's deputy made a controlled
purchase of suspected cannabis from an employee inside the business. [@1] Marvin
Morales, 25, was taken into custody and charged with possession of cannabis with
intent to distribute. [@1] A second person was arrested on an outstanding
warrant issued in June 2020 for first-degree rape. [@1]

Investigators executed a search warrant at the business and seized nine pounds
(about 4.08 kg) of what was believed to be cannabis, along with 17,794 tobacco
products. [@1] The Sheriff's Office said the action was part of an ongoing
initiative targeting businesses that unlawfully sell these products. [@1]

Sheriff Sam Cogen said smoke shops were marketing to children, selling
unlicensed tobacco and harming the community. [@1] Baltimore City State's
Attorney Ivan J. Bates said residents had inundated his office with complaints
about illegal smoke shops and businesses that openly violate Maryland law. [@1]
Mayor Brandon M. Scott said the city would not tolerate individuals and
businesses breaking the law and causing harm in its communities. [@1] Officials
said undercover deputies would continue targeting illegal sales throughout the
summer. [@1]
