Spain's National Police dismantled the network at the end of June 2026,
arresting 14 people across Granada, Málaga and Almería provinces. [@1] Seven of
the detainees were arrested in Granada province, six in Málaga and one in
Almería, and all 14 were accused of drug trafficking and membership of a
criminal organisation. [@2] A court remanded seven of them, including the
network's main suspects, in custody. [@1] Officers carried out 19 searches: 13
in Granada province, three in Benalmádena, one in Fuengirola and two in Almería.
[@2]

Police seized 118 kilograms of marijuana and five kilograms of hashish in the
searches. [@2] Officers also took weapons including an expandable baton, two
sets of brass knuckles and a pepper-spray gun, a GPS beacon detector, packaging
material and gas canisters, alongside nearly 21,200 euros in cash. [@1] In
Granada province officers seized eight vehicles, almost 10,000 euros, jammers,
presses, more than 200 mobile phones, a drone and a taser, while in Almería they
took two vehicles, about 1,200 euros and 186 butane gas cylinders. [@2]

The network grew the marijuana in Granada and Almería, stored it in industrial
units and homes, and hid the proceeds in the Granada town of Baza. [@4] The
operation dismantled several indoor cannabis plantations across Granada, Málaga
and Almería provinces. [@5] The drug was taken to Málaga province in trucks
under heavy security, with the network using escort vehicles and prohibited
weapons to hinder police action. [@2] In a house in Fuengirola used as a stash
house, the drug was packaged into boxes and prepared for shipment to other
European countries, mainly Germany and Greece, where higher sale prices
multiplied the profits. [@3] The police said they had completely dismantled the
facilities used for cultivating, processing and packaging the drugs. [@1] The
investigation began in September 2025, and in April 2026 officers intercepted
the suspected leader and two other members as they left the stash house having
prepared a 36-kilogram marijuana shipment. [@4]
