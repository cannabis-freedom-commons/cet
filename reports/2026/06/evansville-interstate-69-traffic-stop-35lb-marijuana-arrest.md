The Evansville Vanderburgh County Drug Task Force arrested 32-year-old Jordan W.
Rushing during a traffic stop on Tuesday 2 June 2026. [@1] Detectives stopped
his vehicle on Interstate 69. [@2] During the stop, officers found more than 25
pounds (about 11.3 kg) of what they described as marijuana in the car. [@1]

According to the Evansville Police Department, investigators then obtained a
search warrant for Rushing's home, where they found 10 more pounds (about 4.5
kg) of marijuana, two handguns and a large amount of cash. [@1] In all, police
said they had confiscated more than 35 pounds (about 15.9 kg) of marijuana. [@2]

Police said multiple dogs were also removed from the home. [@1] Animal Control
had received multiple previous complaints about the property but had been unable
to make contact with any of the residents, police said. [@1] Rushing faces
preliminary charges of driving while suspended and of dealing marijuana greater
than 10 lbs. [@1]
