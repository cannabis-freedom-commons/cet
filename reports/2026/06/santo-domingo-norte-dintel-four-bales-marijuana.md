National Police officers in the Dominican Republic, acting through the Central
Intelligence Directorate (DINTEL), carried out an operation in Santo Domingo
Norte. [@1] The operation was coordinated with representatives of the Public
Prosecutor's Office. [@1] The raids were carried out in distinct sectors of the
municipality of Santo Domingo Norte. [@2] Officers detained three people for
investigation purposes. [@1] The detainees were placed at the disposal of the
Public Prosecutor's Office. [@1]

Police said the operation concluded with the seizure of four bales of marijuana
and four packages of a white powder presumed to be cocaine. [@1] The cash seized
amounted to RD$5,021,050, according to the police statement. [@3] Infobae
described the cash as more than five million Dominican pesos, the equivalent of
more than US$85,973.85. [@1] Officers also seized three pistols, a revolver, two
scales believed used to weigh drugs, gold-coloured clothing and a blue Honda
CR-V. [@3] They also seized eight slot machines that were operating irregularly
at the properties searched. [@3]

Police said the evidence was collected and would be sent to the relevant bodies
for analysis and legal proceedings. [@1] The operation was described as part of
a series of anti-drug actions carried out during the year by Dominican
authorities. [@1]
