Zambia's Drug Enforcement Commission arrested Jesper Chirwa, 59, of Chipyola
Compound in Lundazi District, for trafficking in 135 kilogrammes of cannabis.
[@1] Officers recovered 90 kilogrammes concealed in a polythene sack hidden in
the bedroom and another 45 kilogrammes in a black plastic sack during a search
of his residence, deputy public relations officer Theresa Mawere said. [@1]

Mawere said preliminary investigations indicated Chirwa was believed to have
been sourcing the cannabis from Malawi for distribution within Lundazi and other
parts of Zambia. [@1] The commission said Chirwa had been on its watch list.
[@1] Mawere confirmed the arrests in a statement issued in Lusaka on Sunday.
[@2] The commission said the suspects were in lawful custody and would appear in
court soon. [@1]

The 135 kilogrammes was the only cannabis in a statement that also announced
arrests in Lusaka over pethidine and crack cocaine, which are excluded from the
figures here. [@1]
