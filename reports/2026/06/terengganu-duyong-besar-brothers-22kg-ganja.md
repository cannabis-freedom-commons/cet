Terengganu police arrested two brothers suspected of using their house in
Kampung Duyong Besar as a storage facility for drugs worth RM1.76 million. [@1]
The brothers, aged 39 and 50, were arrested with about 22 kilogrammes of
suspected ganja buds during a raid at about 3.30 pm on June 14 under Ops Tapis
Khas. [@1] Initial investigations found that both suspects acted as storekeepers
for a syndicate believed to have been active since March this year. [@1] Police
said the drug supply was believed to have been obtained from an international
syndicate that smuggled it into the country via sea routes. [@1]

Both suspects tested negative for drugs, and the 50-year-old had 13 previous
drug-related offences while his younger brother had no criminal record. [@1]
Mohd Khairi said the drug supply, estimated to be sufficient for 44,000 addicts,
was believed to be intended for the international market. [@1] Police said the
ganja buds were meant to be sent overseas and would be processed to extract a
liquid believed to be used as a flavouring for electronic cigarettes that can
induce intoxication. [@1] Officers also seized assets worth a total of RM149,000
during the raid, comprising a Hyundai Tucson, three motorcycles and RM6,000 in
cash. [@1]

The remand period for the two brothers, which began on June 15 and ended on June
21, was extended by another seven days to facilitate further investigations
under Section 39B of the Dangerous Drugs Act 1952. [@1] The wider Ops Tapis Khas
operation, conducted from June 12 to 14, saw the arrest of 243 individuals for
various drug-related offences and a total of 22.35 kg of drugs worth RM1.79
million seized. [@1]
