Sédhiou's regional drugs squad seized 91 kg of chanvre indien, the name used in
Senegal for cannabis, near a clandestine landing stage in the commune of Bona
during the night of 11 June 2026. [@1] Acting on intelligence, officers set a
six-hour ambush and at about 3:30 spotted two suspected couriers, who escaped
into the darkness. [@1] A sweep of the mangrove then found an abandoned
motorcycle and bicycle carrying five sisal sacks. [@1]

The haul amounted to 91 kg of cannabis of the so-called green variety, 61 kg on
the motorcycle and 30 kg on the bicycle. [@1] The drugs squad said the
motorcycle carried three sacks of 61 kg in all, two of 15 kg and one of 31 kg.
[@2] It said the bicycle carried two sacks of 15 kg each, 30 kg in all. [@2] The
cannabis and the two two-wheelers were placed under seal, and police said the
investigation was continuing in order to find the fugitives. [@1]

The national police said the stakeout followed intelligence that couriers were
about to bring a consignment through the clandestine landing stage. [@3] SENTV
reported that investigators were also trying to identify the owners of the
abandoned motorcycle and bicycle. [@4]

The seizure came after an earlier drugs squad operation on 15 March 2026, when a
suspect was arrested and 179 kg of cannabis was seized as it was cycled between
North Sindian and Bafatá in Guinea-Bissau. [@1]
