Two young men were tried by immediate appearance for trying to deliver illicit
objects and substances by drone over the Baie-Mahault prison in Guadeloupe. [@1]
The hearing took place on Friday 5 June 2026, after the pair were surprised in
the act of making a drop near the prison on Monday evening. [@2] The owner of
the drone was sentenced to 15 months of firm imprisonment with continued
detention. [@2] His companion received a 10-month suspended sentence with
community service. [@2]

Using a drone, the two defendants were preparing to drop over the prison wall a
bag containing 15 packets of cigarettes and cannabis herb. [@1] A municipal
police patrol spotted an unidentified flying object on Monday evening and went
to the prison car park, where two men fled; one escaped and the other was caught
with a bag in his hand. [@2] Inside the bag were several packets of cigarettes
and cannabis herb, and the 20-year-old caught with it claimed a third man had
told him to pick it up. [@2]

After questioning, the accomplice was identified as a 28-year-old Petit-Bourg
resident at whose home officers found drones, narcotics and a long gun with
ammunition. [@2] The older man accused a Dominican known as Boxy, said to be
widely feared, of organising the night delivery. [@2] The prosecution doubted
whether Boxy existed and asked for firm sentences. [@2]
