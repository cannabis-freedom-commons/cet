Cape Verde's Judicial Police detained five people in the city of Praia on 10
June 2026 during an operation against drug trafficking. [@1] The suspects were
indicted for high-risk drug trafficking and for converting and concealing
assets, in joint authorship. [@1] Four of them, three men and one woman aged
between 19 and 33, were caught in the act in the Bela Vista and Calabaceira
neighbourhoods. [@1] A fifth man was detained that night at Nelson Mandela
International Airport as he prepared to fly to Lisbon, suspected of belonging to
the same group. [@1]

Officers searching the premises seized cannabis and its derivative hashish
together with cocaine and methamphetamine, in quantities enough to prepare 225
individual doses, along with cutting products, drug-use materials and two
precision scales. [@1] They also seized 21,245 Cape Verdean escudos and 10
dollars, silver artefacts and mobile phones. [@1] The man held at the airport
had 3,055 euros, equivalent to 336,050 escudos, as well as a mobile phone and
personal documents. [@1] The operation was coordinated by the judicial police's
central criminal investigation directorate through its specialist
drug-trafficking and organised-crime section, under four home search warrants
issued by the criminal investigation court of the Praia judicial district. [@2]

All five detainees were brought before the Judicial District Court of Praia for
a first judicial interrogation. [@1] Four men were ordered held in preventive
detention, while the woman was required to report periodically to the
authorities and was barred from leaving the country. [@1]
