Costa Rica's Judicial Investigation Agency (OIJ) carried out 12 simultaneous
raids across Cartago, Puriscal, Ciudad Colón, Heredia and La Fortuna de San
Carlos in the early hours of Tuesday 30 June 2026. [@1] The operation resulted
in nine arrests, along with the seizure of a large arsenal and thousands of
doses of drugs ready for sale. [@1] At least two of the suspects were already in
prison and were suspected of continuing to coordinate parts of the criminal
network from custody. [@1] All nine were placed at the disposal of the Public
Prosecutor's Office, which was to decide on precautionary measures and pursue
the criminal case. [@1]

Among the items seized were about 50 pounds (about 22.7 kg) of marijuana, the
cannabis component of a haul that also included synthetic pills, apparent
cocaine and crystal drug. [@1] A second account listed the same 50 pounds of
marijuana alongside an AR-15-type rifle, two pistols, a revolver, a shotgun and
a tablet press. [@2]

The investigation ran for about two years and used surveillance and special
investigative techniques to identify the network and its members. [@1] Interim
OIJ director Michael Soto said the growth of the synthetic drug market was one
of the main challenges for Costa Rican authorities. [@1]
