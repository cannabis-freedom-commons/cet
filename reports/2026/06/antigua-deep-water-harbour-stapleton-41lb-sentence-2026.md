Thirty-seven-year-old Damar Stapleton of Bendals was taken into custody after
officers uncovered 41 pounds of cannabis at Deep Water Harbour. [@1] He was
later sentenced to two and a half years in prison after admitting drug
trafficking charges linked to the harbour seizure. [@3] Justice John Spencer
imposed the sentence in the High Court after Stapleton pleaded guilty on May 12
to trafficking cannabis. [@3]

Police and Customs officers found the 41 pounds of cannabis concealed in two
boxes at the port during a March 8, 2024 operation. [@3] An earlier police
account said the joint operation at the main seaport found two boxes holding 41
packages of the controlled substance. [@2] Officers put the estimated value of
the haul at $246,000. [@2]

Stapleton was formally charged with drug trafficking and importation of 41
pounds of cannabis, possession of the controlled drug, possession with intent to
transfer, and being involved in supplying the drug. [@1] He appeared before a
magistrate, was remanded to prison, and his committal hearing was set for July
9. [@1] He is also before the court on separate charges over the alleged
importation of 20 pounds of cannabis through Deep Water Harbour on March 13,
2024. [@3]
