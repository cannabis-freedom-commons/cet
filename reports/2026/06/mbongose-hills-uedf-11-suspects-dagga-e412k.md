Eleven suspects, including Eswatini and South African nationals, were arrested
during the operation. [@1] The operation was carried out late on Friday, 26 June
2026 at Mbongose Hills in the northern Hhohho region. [@1] The Umbutfo Eswatini
Defence Force said it had dismantled a suspected cross-border criminal network
involving illegal gold mining and dagga trafficking. [@1] The 11 accused
appeared before the Pigg's Peak Magistrate's Court and were remanded in custody
until 8 July 2026, when a trial date was expected to be set. [@2]

At the same location soldiers intercepted a dagga consignment with an estimated
street value of E412 000. [@1] The seized dagga material included processed
dagga, seeds and waste products packaged in different forms, indicating
large-scale production and distribution activity. [@1] During the raid, soldiers
uncovered a large consignment of gold-bearing sand weighing approximately 7.82
tons. [@1] Authorities also recovered a Taurus 92 pistol loaded with 11 rounds
of live ammunition. [@1] The combined value of all exhibits recovered during the
operation exceeded E584 000. [@2]

All suspects, along with the recovered materials and equipment, were handed over
to law enforcement authorities for further investigation and possible
prosecution. [@1] Officials said investigations into the wider network were
ongoing and might lead to further arrests. [@1] Army Commander Lieutenant
General Moses Mashikilisane Fakudze described the operation as a major
breakthrough in disrupting organised criminal syndicates operating within and
across borders. [@1]
