A 22-year-old Delhi University student was arrested for allegedly peddling drugs
and supplying contraband to students of his college in northwest Delhi's Ashok
Vihar. [@1] Police named him as Jaseem Siyadul Farsan, an eighth-semester BA
student who was nabbed in a raid. [@1] He lives in Vijay Nagar and comes from
Kozhikode in Kerala, police said. [@1] Police had acted on a tip-off about
illicit sale of the drug in a park near Vijay Nagar. [@1]

Officers seized hydroponic ganja, cannabis grown without soil, from his
possession. [@1] Police said the drug had an international market value of over
Rs 20 lakh (about 2 million rupees). [@1] DCP (northwest) Akanksha Yadav said
sustained interrogation showed he had been peddling drugs and supplying
contraband to clients on demand. [@1] Police said he told them he supplied the
contraband mainly to students and other customers in the area to earn quick
money. [@1]

Investigators were trying to establish the source of the narcotics and identify
others linked to the supply network. [@1] A case was registered under sections
of the NDPS Act at Model Town police station. [@1]
