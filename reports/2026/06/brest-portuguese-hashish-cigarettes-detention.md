Brest customs officers detained a 37-year-old Portuguese citizen on 6 June 2026
for trying to bring drugs into Belarus, the State Customs Committee said. [@1,2]
He was crossing at the Brest border checkpoint as a passenger on a scheduled
bus. [@1,2] The man chose the green corridor and declared he carried no prohibited
or restricted goods. [@1,2] A sniffer dog singled him out in the customs clearance
hall, and he again denied carrying banned substances when an officer asked him
directly. [@1,2]

A search of his backpack and suitcase found two opened packets of cigarettes
containing a suspicious substance. [@1,2] Experts who examined 40 of the
cigarettes found hashish in 20 and marijuana in two more. [@1,2]

Brest customs opened a criminal case under part 1 of Article 328-1 of the
Criminal Code. [@1,2] That article provides for up to 10 years' imprisonment, and
the man was placed under a custodial remand measure. [@1]
