The two people arrested were described as men, one aged 26 and the other aged
50. [@1] The arrests occurred during a targeted operation conducted by officers
assigned to Operation Trident on Friday, 12 June 2026. [@1] Police said officers
assisted by the Canine Support Unit executed a search warrant shortly after 7:00
p.m. at a blue residence in Pinder’s Point. [@1]

During the search inside the home, officers discovered a glass jar containing a
quantity of suspected marijuana. [@1] Officers then searched the exterior of the
same property and found four potted marijuana plants on the southern side of the
residence. [@1] The four potted plants measured between four and seven feet in
height. [@1]

Police gave the estimated street value of the four plants as $4,000. [@1] Police
said the marijuana recovered from the glass jar weighed approximately 17 grams.
[@1] Police gave the estimated street value of that jarred marijuana as $85.
[@1] The investigation remains ongoing. [@1]
