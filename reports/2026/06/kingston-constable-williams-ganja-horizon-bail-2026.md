A district constable accused of introducing prohibited items into a correctional
institution was granted $300,000 bail when he appeared in the Kingston and St
Andrew Parish Court on Monday 15 June 2026. [@1] The accused is Shemar Williams,
who is charged with possession of ganja, dealing in ganja and introducing
prohibited items into a correctional facility. [@1] Attorney-at-law Donahue
Martin made the bail application before Parish Court Judge Peter Wilson. [@1]
Bail was granted under conditions. [@1] Williams is scheduled to return to court
on 9 October 2026. [@1]

The allegations are that on 8 June 2026 Williams was at the Horizon Correctional
Centre, where he was searched and a quantity of ganja was allegedly found on his
person. [@1] He was subsequently charged. [@1]
