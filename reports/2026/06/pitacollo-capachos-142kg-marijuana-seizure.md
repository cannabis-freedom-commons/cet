FELCN officers intercepted a vehicle outside Pitacollo, near the Chilean border,
during an interdiction operation in the early hours of Sunday 7 June 2026. [@1]
After a detailed inspection, officers found several jute sacks containing
marijuana hidden under a load of pasankalla that the driver was using as cover.
[@1] A field test at the scene was positive for marijuana, and officers arrested
the driver at once and opened a drug-trafficking investigation. [@1]

On Monday 8 June 2026, the Public Ministry and FELCN destroyed and incinerated
142 kg and 546 g of the seized marijuana. [@1] Acting on a prosecutor's order,
officials carried out the incineration on Monday afternoon near Capachos. [@1]
Officers of FELCN, representatives of the Public Ministry and Bolivian Police
inspectorate staff attended and verified the total destruction of the load. [@1]

Officials estimated that the seizure cost drug-trafficking networks 195,430 US
dollars. [@1] The detained driver remained under legal process while
investigators worked to establish further links and additional criminal
liability. [@1]
