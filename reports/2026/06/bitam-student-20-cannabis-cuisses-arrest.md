A 20-year-old pupil from Oyem was arrested in Bitam, in Gabon's Woleu-Ntem
province, by the Office central de lutte antidrogue (OCLAD), the central
anti-drug office, with 20 cannabis cuisses hidden in his bag. [@1] Officers
stopped him at the police post at Mengang, in the commune of Bitam, during a
targeted check coordinated by Captain Simplice Yambangoy. [@2] OCLAD said the
check was carried out by its local unit based in Bitam, and identified the
suspect only by the initials A.A.I. [@2] According to the first accounts, he had
crossed the border into Cameroon, bought the cannabis there and was bringing it
back into Gabon when he was stopped. [@1]

During the check officers found the 20 cuisses of cannabis in his possession.
[@2] He was placed at the disposal of the competent judicial authorities and the
procedure was continuing under Gabon's anti-drug law. [@2]

Gabon Mail Infos presented the arrest as a warning about vulnerable teenagers
drawn by easy money into organised networks that use them as mules or resellers.
[@1] The outlet called for more awareness campaigns in schools and open dialogue
between generations, saying that arrests alone would not be enough. [@1]
