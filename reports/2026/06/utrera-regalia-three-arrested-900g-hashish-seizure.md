The Guardia Civil in Utrera dismantled an alleged criminal group selling drugs
with home delivery, leaving three people detained and a minor investigated. [@1]
Operation Regalia, carried out by Investigation Area officers, began after
indications of drug trafficking in a house in the town. [@1] The group used the
investigated house as a fixed sales point and at the same time made rapid
deliveries by the method known as tele-coca to different points in Utrera and
nearby municipalities. [@1] A minor was directly and actively involved in
distributing the drugs, being used to make arranged deliveries to buyers by
electric scooter to speed travel and hinder police detection. [@1]

Officers sought judicial authorisation to enter and search the investigated
house in the presence of a court administration officer. [@1] The search located
and seized a large quantity of narcotic substances and material used to prepare
and distribute them. [@1] Officers seized about 900 grams of hashish in tablets,
80 hashish doses and 20 marijuana doses prepared for sale, alongside cocaine,
MDMA, cash and five vehicles. [@1] The Guardia Civil detained three people for
alleged crimes against public health and membership of a criminal group and
investigated a minor linked to the facts. [@1] Police put the market price of
all the seized drugs at about 70,000 euros and said about 4,000 doses were
prevented from reaching the street. [@1]
