Agents with the West Central Illinois Task Force arrested 25-year-old Jaret T.
Shear and 21-year-old Israel N. L. Gordon, both of Quincy, on multiple drug
charges. [@1] Special agents took Shear and Gordon into custody during the
search at one residence in the 1500 block of Zellerman Avenue. [@2] Shear was
charged with cannabis delivery of more than 5,000 grams, cannabis production of
50 to 200 plants, cocaine possession, controlled-substance possession and
psilocybin-mushroom possession. [@1] Gordon faced possession of 10 to 30 grams
of cannabis and unlawful use of a weapon. [@1] Both men went to the Adams County
Jail, and after Wednesday detention hearings Gordon was released on pretrial
conditions while Shear was ordered held. [@1]

Agents executed warrants at two homes in the 1500 block of Zellerman Avenue, one
home in the 5300 block of Lyter Road and the KB Co. business in the 600 block of
Maine Street. [@1] The Maine Street business was The KB Co. at 611 Maine Street,
and police described the case as an active investigation. [@3] In the four
searches agents found more than 20 pounds of black-market cannabis, more than 80
grams of cocaine, more than 200 grams of psilocybin mushrooms, firearms
ammunition and a large amount of United States currency. [@1] WGEM reported the
owner of The KB Co. in Quincy had been charged over growing marijuana. [@3]

The task force draws officers from the Illinois State Police, the Quincy Police
Department and the Adams County Sheriff's Department. [@1] The operation was
assisted by ISP Troop 6 and the Illinois Department of Revenue. [@1] The KB Co.
was not a state-licensed dispensary, according to Illinois regulators. [@3]
Court records showed Shear was charged Tuesday with manufacturing more than
5,000 grams of marijuana, producing 50 to 200 cannabis plants and two counts of
possession of a controlled substance. [@3] Shear made his first court appearance
on Wednesday, when prosecutors sought denial of pretrial release. [@3]
