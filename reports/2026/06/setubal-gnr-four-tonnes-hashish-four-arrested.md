The GNR's Coastal and Border Control Unit (UCCF) arrested four men aged between
26 and 66 in the early hours of 27 June 2026, all of them on the two boats it
seized. [@3] One of the four was already in the process of being expelled from
Portugal, the GNR said. [@1] The men were brought before the Setúbal Judicial
Court for a first judicial interrogation, and the investigating judge ordered
preventive detention. [@5] All four were then taken to the Setúbal prison. [@4]

Officers seized 101 bales of hashish, two fibreglass boats and ten jerrycans,
four of them still holding fuel. [@2] The GNR estimated each bale at about 40
kilograms, putting the consignment at about four tonnes. [@5]

The operation began on Friday night, when the Integrated Surveillance, Command
and Control System (SIVICC) detected a high-speed vessel heading for the
entrance to the Port of Setúbal. [@1] Maritime, land and air units intercepted
the first boat, arrested its two occupants and seized the drugs and fuel on
board. [@1] The second boat fled but was caught near the Tróia peninsula, where
two more crew members were arrested. [@1] The high-speed launch believed to have
carried the hashish from North Africa to the Portuguese coast was not
intercepted. [@2] The investigation was continuing to identify the wider
criminal network behind the shipment. [@1]
