Chris Haleigh Philbert, a 41-year-old man from Dominica who lives in Miami, was
arrested in St. Lucie County and charged with drug possession after officials
found marijuana, cocaine and methamphetamine, among other drugs, in his car.
[@1] He was arrested on 22 June 2026 and charged with cocaine possession, two
counts of possession of more than 20 grams of marijuana, three counts of having
a controlled substance without a prescription and two counts of drug-equipment
possession. [@1]

Florida Highway Patrol troopers first detected the smell of cannabis coming from
his car during a traffic stop by the 165-mile marker of Florida's Turnpike at
about 2:01 p.m. that day, near the northern section of Fort Pierce. [@1]
Philbert and another passenger in the black Honda sedan denied having a medical
marijuana card or drugs in the car. [@1]

Officers found a black container in the trunk with what the affidavit called a
large amount of green leafy marijuana, and small amounts of cocaine,
methamphetamine, MDMA, also known as Ecstasy, and a single dose of LSD. [@1]
Troopers also found an electronic pipe with marijuana residue and a THC vape in
the car. [@1]

Philbert was booked in the St. Lucie County Jail and released on 23 June on a
$16,000 bond. [@1]
