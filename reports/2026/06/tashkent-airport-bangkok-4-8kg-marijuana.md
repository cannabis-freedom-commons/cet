Officers of the Tashkent-Aero specialised customs complex, working with other
law-enforcement agencies, stopped two attempts to bring drugs into Uzbekistan
through Tashkent International Airport. [@1] A 44-year-old passenger who arrived
on a flight from Bangkok and walked through the green corridor was detained at
the border customs post. [@1] The inspection of his carry-on luggage and checked
baggage found more than 4.8 kg of marijuana. [@1] Customs officers detected that
first case on 30 June 2026. [@2]

In a second case a 19-year-old citizen of a neighbouring state who flew in from
Phuket was inspected, and packages labelled as powdered milk drew officers'
attention. [@1] The Central Customs Laboratory found that ten of those packages
held mephedrone weighing 7 kg 180 g, a synthetic drug not counted in the
cannabis figures. [@1] The 19-year-old said he had met an unknown person through
Telegram and agreed, on that person's instruction, to deliver the drugs to
Tashkent for a payment of 1,000 US dollars. [@1] Together the two seizures
weighed about 12 kilograms, the customs committee said. [@1]

Criminal cases were opened over both cases and investigative measures were
continuing. [@1] The customs committee said the detained 44-year-old passenger
could face up to 15 years in prison. [@3]
