Acting on specific information and secret reports, a Crime Prevention Special
Company team of RAB-13, Rangpur raided a dwelling in Kurusha Ferusha village,
Naodanga Union, under Phulbari police station in Kurigram district at 07:45 on
10 June 2026. [@1] Officers arrested three alleged drug traders during the raid
in the village. [@1] All three detainees were recorded as residents of the same
Kurusha Ferusha village address in Naodanga Union, Phulbari, Kurigram. [@1]

Inside the dwelling, officers found 15 kg of ganja kept in a plastic sack hidden
among maize stalks, while 4.5 kg of ganja was in the possession of the two other
detainees, making a total seizure of 19.5 kg of ganja. [@1] The detainees
together with the seized ganja were handed to the concerned police station for
further action. [@1]

RAB-13 said its elite force regularly conducts operations against all-pervasive
drugs and that every member works to make its responsible area drug-free. [@1]
It added that its intelligence monitoring and ongoing operations against drugs
would continue in implementation of a zero-tolerance policy. [@1]
