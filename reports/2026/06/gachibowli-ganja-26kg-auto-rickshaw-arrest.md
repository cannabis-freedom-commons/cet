A 26-year-old man was arrested during a joint operation by the Gachibowli police
and the Serilingampally Special Operations Team (SOT) after 26 kilograms of
ganja was seized during a vehicle interception near Nanakramguda on Saturday,
June 13, 2026. [@1] The accused was identified as Mohd Nayeem, a resident of
Mangalhat, who was arrested after police intercepted an auto-rickshaw near
Sumadhura Acropolis in Nanakramguda. [@1]

A search of the vehicle recovered six packets of ganja concealed in three bags,
collectively weighing 26 kilograms. [@1] Investigators said the contraband was
being transported for delivery to local peddlers operating in the city. [@1] The
accused was taken into custody and later remanded to judicial custody. [@1]

Police said three other accused — Goutham Naik alias Bipin Naik, a supplier from
Odisha, and Kalapathi Nithu Bai and Kalapathi Goutham Singh, both local peddlers
— remained at large. [@1] A case was booked and further investigation was
underway. [@1]
