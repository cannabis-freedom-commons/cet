Customs officers from the mobile brigade in Mbouda seized the load at the mixed
control post of Bafounda, in Cameroon's West Region, during the night of 13–14
June 2026. [@1] The officers found the cargo while searching the hold of a
public-transport bus. [@2] They seized more than 200 kilograms of chanvre indien
(Indian hemp). [@1] The load was hidden in the hold luggage of a bus running
from Bamenda to Yaoundé. [@2]

The customs directorate described the seizure as an important quantity of
narcotics intercepted before it reached its destination. [@3] It estimated the
cannabis at about 1.5 million CFA francs. [@1] It presented the operation as
part of its surveillance at borders and inside the country against illicit
trafficking. [@2]

No arrests were reported, and the sources said the circumstances were under
investigation to identify the owners of the cargo and any accomplices. [@3]
