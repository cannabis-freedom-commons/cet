The Royal Bahamas Defence Force recovered 12 suspected bales of hydroponic
marijuana during a routine maritime patrol near Fresh Creek, Andros, on Sunday
14 June 2026. [@2] Interceptor P-51 recovered the jettisoned bales at about
11:41 a.m. while conducting routine patrol, boarding and search operations in
the general vicinity of Fresh Creek. [@2] Eye Witness News, citing the defence
force, reported the same Sunday recovery of 12 suspected bales near Fresh Creek
and an estimated street value of $718,000. [@1]

The suspected narcotics consisted of 12 crocus sacks containing 146 packages.
[@1] The sacks had a total weight of about 359 pounds, about 163 kilograms. [@1]
The defence force estimated the street value at $718,000. [@1]

P-51 transported the suspected narcotics to HMBS Coral Harbour, arriving at
about 5:20 p.m. [@1] The items were handed over to the Royal Bahamas Police
Force Drug Enforcement Unit. [@1] The defence force said investigations remain
ongoing. [@1] The force described the recovery as its second drug recovery
within the last two months. [@2]
