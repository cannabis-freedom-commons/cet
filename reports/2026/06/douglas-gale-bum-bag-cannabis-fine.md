A visitor from Berkshire was fined £700 for importing cannabis to the Isle of
Man on the ferry. [@1] Ryan Ralph Gale, of Ambassador, Bracknell, was told to
pay the fine immediately, plus £125 costs, or face up to 50 days in prison. [@1]
Gale was arrested on 11 May while travelling as a passenger in a car
disembarking the ferry from Liverpool. [@1] The arrest was at the Sea Terminal
in Douglas. [@1]

A police dog indicated that there were drugs in a bum bag belonging to Gale.
[@1] Officers searched the bag and found 13.5 grams of cannabis, which police
valued at £263. [@1]

Defence advocate Paul Rodgers said Gale used the drug to combat stress and
anxiety and that the cannabis found had been for personal use only; his age was
not given in court. [@1]
