The Santa Catarina judicial district court ordered preventive detention on the
morning of 22 June 2026 for a suspect from Guinea-Bissau living in the Cumbém
area of Assomada, on suspicion of high-risk drug trafficking. [@1] The defendant was 43 years old. [@2] The Judicial
Police (Polícia Judiciária, PJ) said the man was detained on Friday 19 June,
outside flagrante delicto, during a home-search operation requested by the
Assomada Criminal Investigation Department from the Public Prosecutor and
authorised by the district's Criminal Instruction Court. [@1]

During the searches officers seized one package of cannabis herbs with a gross
weight of 2.28 grams. [@1,2] The same search also found 19 individual doses of
cocaine with a gross weight of 10.46 grams, which is not cannabis and is
excluded from the cannabis figures. [@1,2] The Judicial Police also seized 66,330
Cape Verdean escudos and 1,000 CFA francs in cash. [@1,2]

The Judicial Police said the operation followed ongoing investigations by the
Assomada department into drug trafficking and associated criminal activity in
the municipality. [@1] The defendant was to await the further proceedings
subject to that coercive measure. [@1,2]
