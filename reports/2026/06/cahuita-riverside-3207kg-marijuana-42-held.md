Costa Rica's Judicial Investigation Agency (OIJ) carried out what it called the
largest anti-drug operation in the country's history, codenamed Operativo
Riverside, against a network involved in national and international drug
trafficking and money laundering with links to Colombia and the United States.
[@1] The operation took place on a Tuesday in Limón, with its epicentre in
Cahuita, and extended to Cartago, Heredia, Turrialba, Tres Ríos and La Reforma
prison. [@1] Officers detained 42 people during the operation. [@1] Those
arrested were placed at the disposal of the Public Ministry to determine their
legal status. [@1]

During the investigation, judicial agents seized 3,207 one-kilo packages of
marijuana (3,207 kg), alongside 960 kilos of cocaine. [@1] The OIJ valued the
network's movable and immovable assets at about 2,600 million colones (USD 5.7
million). [@1] An independent English-language account gave the same
investigation figures of 960 kilos of cocaine and 3,207 one-kilo packages of
marijuana. [@2]

Investigators said the group was led by a man surnamed López, alias Pecho de
Rata, who was extradited to the United States, and had direct links to the
Colombian criminal organisation Los Costeños. [@1] The joint action involved the
Fuerza Pública, OIJ special units, municipal, fiscal and migration police,
coordinated with the Public Ministry and the Fiscalía. [@1]
