The Edo State Command of the NDLEA destroyed 73,463.21 kilograms of assorted
illicit drugs seized across the state. [@1] The agency publicly destroyed
73,463.21 kilogrammes of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances in Benin
City, Edo State. [@3] The destruction exercise, held on Thursday at Idogbo
bypass in Ikpoba-Okha Local Government Area, was carried out pursuant to an
order of the Federal High Court in Benin. [@3] Cannabis sativa accounted for the
largest share of the destroyed exhibits, with 73,210.23 kilogrammes. [@2]

Other destroyed substances included 246.36 kilogrammes of psychotropic drugs,
including tramadol, diazepam and codeine cough syrup. [@2] Also destroyed were
2.66 kilogrammes of methamphetamine, 3.74 kilogrammes of cocaine and 0.22
kilogrammes of heroin. [@2] The agency said the destroyed drugs were valued at
N2.8 billion in Edo State. [@2] NDLEA Chairman Mohamed Buba Marwa described the
exercise as a demonstration of the agency's commitment to combating drug
trafficking and substance abuse. [@2]

Edo commander Mitchell Ofoyeju said the exhibits were recovered through
sustained intelligence-led operations targeting drug trafficking syndicates
operating within and outside Edo State. [@3] Ofoyeju said operatives had
intercepted vehicles and articulated trucks conveying drugs and uncovered
narcotics concealed in unusual locations, including shrines. [@2]
