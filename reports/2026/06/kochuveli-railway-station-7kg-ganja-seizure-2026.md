Excise officials seized 7 kg of ganja from the Kochuveli (Thiruvananthapuram
North) railway station late Sunday. [@1] The contraband was seized from the
railway premises during a raid conducted on the basis of a tip-off. [@1]

Efforts are under way to nab the person responsible for smuggling the
contraband. [@1] The excise team also destroyed a bunch of ganja plants that
were found from the locality. [@1]
