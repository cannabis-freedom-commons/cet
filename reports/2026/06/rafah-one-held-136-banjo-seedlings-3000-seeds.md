In Rafah, the anti-narcotics department working with the Rafah police centre
detained a suspect as part of continued efforts to combat drug trafficking and
limit its spread. [@1] The operation followed intensive investigation and field
surveillance, and the department named the detainee only by the initials R.K.
[@2]

Officers searching the suspect found 136 seedlings of the narcotic banjo plant,
the cannabis term used in the reports, in his possession. [@1] They also found
about half a kilogram of banjo ready for use, alongside an additional quantity
of dried banjo for which no weight was given. [@1] Officers also seized 3,000
banjo seeds that the department described as prepared for planting. [@1]

The department said all the seized materials were secured under legal procedures
and that legal measures were taken against the suspect. [@1] He was to be
referred to the competent authorities to complete the required judicial
procedures. [@1] The department said it would continue pursuing those involved
in trafficking, promotion and use of drugs. [@2]
