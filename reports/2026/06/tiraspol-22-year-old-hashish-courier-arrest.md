Police in Tiraspol detained a 22-year-old man described as a zakladchik, a
courier who hides drug stashes for customers to collect. [@1] The young man was
a drug user. [@1] He had taken work as a courier for an online drug shop to pay
for prohibited substances. [@1] The Interior Ministry press centre said he had
lasted only two weeks in the trade. [@1] He came to the attention of criminal
investigation officers soon after starting. [@1]

The courier was detained in the afternoon of 25 June. [@1] He was under the
influence of drugs at the time. [@1] Officers found photographs of drug stashes
with coordinates on his phone. [@1] Officers seized ten laid-out stashes
containing hashish. [@1] They also found a master stash, a package holding 22
wraps that the man planned to distribute. [@1]

He faces up to 20 years in prison for the unlawful sale of narcotics. [@1] He
was placed in pre-trial detention until sentencing. [@1]
