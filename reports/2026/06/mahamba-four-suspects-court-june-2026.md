Four suspects appeared before the Piet Retief Magistrate's Court in Mpumalanga
on charges linked to dealing in dagga after SAPS operations in the Mahamba
policing precinct. [@1] Phelelisiwe Siphiwe Zwane, 47, was arrested on 22 June
2026 at the Mahamba Port of Entry after police found 12 rolls of dagga in a
Mercedes-Benz travelling from Eswatini. [@1] Zwane appeared before the Piet
Retief Magistrate's Court and remained in custody. [@2] Vezi Robert Mabaso, 50,
Mpendulo Shabalala, 30, and Nkosinathi Khumalo, 39, appeared in court after a
joint SANDF and SAPS operation on 20 June 2026. [@1] The three men appeared
before the Piet Retief Magistrate's Court on 22 June 2026 after being arrested
by SANDF and Mahamba SAPS members. [@2]

The dagga in the Mercedes-Benz weighed about 29.90 kg and police valued it at an
estimated R180,000. [@1] The Mercedes-Benz was confiscated and Zwane's case was
postponed to 1 July for further investigation. [@1] Officers stopped a
Volkswagen sedan on the N2 Road and recovered 16.25 kg of dagga valued at more
than R100,000. [@1] The three men's matter was postponed to 8 July 2026. [@1]

SAPS said it remained committed to combating drug trafficking and related crimes
across the region. [@1]
