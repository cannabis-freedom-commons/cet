Two men aged 20 and 27 were charged in flagrante in Salvador on Monday 8 June
2026 with kidnapping, false imprisonment, drug trafficking and illegal
possession of a firearm. [@1] Military police from the 23rd Independent Military
Police Company were patrolling Rua São Tomé when they spotted two men suspected
of drug trafficking, one of them armed. [@2] The pair fled toward Travessa José
Sampaio, forced their way into a house and held two residents hostage. [@2] The
hostages were a 56-year-old woman and a 23-year-old man, according to the police
account. [@1] The suspects surrendered during negotiations, before the special
operations battalion (Bope) reached the scene, and the two hostages were
released unharmed. [@2]

Officers seized a .40-calibre pistol, seven magazines and 84 rounds of
ammunition. [@2] They also seized 92 portions of crack, 42 vials of cocaine and
29 portions of hashish, two mobile phones and an unspecified amount of cash.
[@2] The Public Security Secretariat disclosed the operation on 9 June, the day
after the afternoon arrest. [@3]

Both men were taken to the Central de Flagrantes, where the case was registered
and they remained in custody at the disposal of the courts. [@1]
