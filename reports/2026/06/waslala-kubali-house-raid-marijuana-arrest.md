National Police officers raided a residential house in the Kubalí area of
Waslala at 6:00 a.m. on Thursday 4 June 2026 and arrested 39-year-old Ebil
Antonio Chavarría. [@1] Police presented the Waslala operation as the second
case, carried out through a house raid against a person holding marijuana. [@1]
The two captures were presented as the result of National Police security and
anti-drug-trafficking plans in the department of Matagalpa. [@1]

During a search of the dwelling, officers seized a backpack holding green herb.
[@1] A field test was positive for marijuana, and police put the weight at 1
pound 12 ounces (about 0.79 kilograms). [@1]

Police said the drugs seized from the detainees were allegedly intended for
sale. [@1] Police said Chavarría had prior records for stalking and threats with
a bladed weapon. [@1] Police said the operations were part of continued
preventive actions to guarantee the security of Nicaraguan families and confront
drug trafficking across the country. [@1]
