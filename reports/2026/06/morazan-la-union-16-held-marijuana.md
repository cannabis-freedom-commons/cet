At least 16 people were arrested before dawn on Wednesday 3 June 2026 by El
Salvador's Attorney General's Office (FGR) and the National Civil Police (PNC)
in the departments of La Unión and Morazán. [@1] Prosecutors said the detainees
belonged to a narcomenudeo structure, a street-dealing network. [@1] The FGR
said the structure was made up of three cells. [@1] The FGR said the group
stored, distributed and sold crack, cocaine and marijuana in the Morazán
districts of San Francisco Gotera, Lolotiquillo, Cacaopera, Chilanga and El
Divisadero, and in Santa Rosa de Lima and La Unión. [@1] Among those held was
Irma del Rosario Villatoro Hernández, identified as the main distributor of
drugs for two structures that sold crack and marijuana. [@1] The 16 were said to
have worked as transporters, intermediaries, distributors and sellers of drugs.
[@2]

In 22 searches officers seized mobile phones, cash, an electronic scale,
portions of marijuana and a vehicle. [@1] Mario Enrique Hernández Coca, one of
those held, was accused of using his painting-frame shop to sell marijuana. [@2]
Diario El Mundo said the marijuana seized amounted to various smaller
quantities. [@2] Hoy.com.sv reported that the vehicle seized in the searches
showed traces of cocaine. [@3]

Those arrested were to face charges of planting and cultivation, illicit
association, illicit trafficking, and preparatory acts, proposal, conspiracy and
criminal associations. [@1] The FGR said the investigation of the structure
began in May 2025, a period in which officers made 18 arrests in flagrante and
documented about 453 drug transactions. [@1] Diario El Mundo reported that the
inquiry ran from 26 May 2025 to 20 May 2026. [@2]
