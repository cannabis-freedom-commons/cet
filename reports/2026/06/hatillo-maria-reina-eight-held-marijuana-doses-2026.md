Costa Rica's Drug Control Police (PCD) dismantled a suspected retail drug
network operating in the María Reina community of Hatillo, San José. [@1] Eight
men were detained and more than 5,000 doses of different drugs were seized in
the operation. [@1] The detainees were suspected of selling, distributing and
supplying drugs in the area. [@1]

The police action comprised 11 simultaneous raids on homes in the southern part
of the capital. [@1] The operation was coordinated with the Narco-trafficking
Prosecutor's Office. [@1] Officers seized 1,269 doses of marijuana distributed
among cigarettes and wrappers, and a further 14 grams of marijuana. [@1]
Officers also seized 22 doses of suspected methamphetamine, 21 of tusi, 76 of
ecstasy, four of ketamine, one of LSD and 43 wrappers of unidentified
substances. [@1] Police also seized 288 nicotine e-cigarettes, five boxes of
liquor, 636,800 colones (about USD 1,200) in cash and 28 calibre .40 rounds.
[@1]

Authorities described María Reina as one of the Greater Metropolitan Area's main
drug-selling points, where territorial disputes have led to shootings and
killings. [@2] The PCD said it had carried out at least two other operations in
María Reina the previous year. [@1] Police said six of the eight detainees had
prior police records. [@3] The eight detainees were placed at the disposal of
the Public Prosecutor's Office, which was to decide on precautionary measures.
[@4]
