Honduras's National Police said its anti-drug directorate seized more than 500
packets of drugs from a drug dealer in Siguatepeque. [@1] The haul included 48
packets of suspected marijuana. [@1] Police listed 400 packets of suspected
cocaine and 144 packets of suspected crack from the same seizure, neither of
which is counted in the cannabis figures. [@1] Marijuana was the smallest part
of the listed seizure, at 48 packets against 400 of cocaine and 144 of crack.
[@1] A local Instagram news page that reproduced the same statement said the
drugs and a vehicle were seized from the suspect. [@3]

The anti-drug directorate, the Dirección Nacional Policial Antidrogas, announced
the case in a short video on the National Police's Facebook page. [@1] Police
described every packet as suspected and counted the seizure in envoltorios, the
wraps used for retail drug sales, without weights. [@1]

The police post was published on Saturday 13 June 2026. [@2]
