Customs officers at Noi Bai International Airport checked Portuguese passenger
Ivo Miguel Dos Santos Silva after a risk screening of his Bangkok-Noi Bai flight
on 2 June 2026. [@1] He was booked from Bangkok through Noi Bai on to Dubai and
Zurich. [@1] In one black checked suitcase officers found 12 vacuum-sealed white
nylon bags of dried cannabis weighing about 15.47 kg. [@1]

Customs handed him, the drugs and the case file to the Hanoi police drug
investigation office, PC04. [@1] On 3 November 2026 the Hanoi investigation
police charged him with illegal transport of narcotics under Article 250(2) of
the Penal Code. [@1]

The Customs Department announced the two airport cases together on 26 November
2026, saying both foreign passengers had been charged with illegal transport of
narcotics. [@2] It said Hanoi investigation police had issued prosecution
decisions against two foreign nationals over the more than 40 kg of dried
cannabis. [@3] The two cases together involved more than 40 kg of dried
cannabis, customs said. [@1]
