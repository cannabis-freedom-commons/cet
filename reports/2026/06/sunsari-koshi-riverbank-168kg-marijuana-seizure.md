Police seized 168 kilograms of marijuana from the bank of the Koshi River in
Barahakshetra Municipality-1, Sunsari. [@1] The load was found abandoned at a
location known as Section No. 17 along the riverbank. [@1]

The cannabis was packed in nine plastic sacks and had been placed using tubes
and bamboo supports. [@1] The District Police Office Sunsari said the ganja was
recovered in an unclaimed state from the Koshi bank at Satra Number,
Barahakshetra Municipality-1. [@2]

Police said further investigation into the incident was under way, and no
arrests were reported in either account. [@1]
