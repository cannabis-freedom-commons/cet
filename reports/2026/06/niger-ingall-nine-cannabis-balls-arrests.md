The National Guard squadron in Ingall arrested two suspects and seized nine
balls of cannabis, the guard announced. [@1] The operation also netted 810
tablets of tramadol, branded Tima King 250, and a motorcycle used to transport
the products. [@1] Officers acted on information judged reliable and the guard
described the quantity of drugs as significant. [@1] The action came during
reinforced patrols and road checks on local routes. [@1]

At the end of the intervention the force arrested two individuals suspected of
involvement in trafficking. [@1] It handed them and all the seized items to the
guard's Brigade des Pistes in Ingall for judicial follow-up and further
investigation. [@1] That inquiry is to establish the suspects' responsibility,
the exact origin of the products and whether wider distribution networks operate
in the region or beyond its borders. [@1]

The National Guard gave no market value for the seized drugs. [@1] It said
tramadol, an opioid medicine often diverted into illicit circuits, is regularly
found in security operations in the Sahel. [@1] It said the nine cannabis balls
showed that flows of chanvre indien, or Indian hemp, were persisting along
lightly controlled routes. [@1]

Squadron commander Captain Abdoulaye Toudou praised the officers and urged
continued pressure on traffickers. [@1] He called for closer cooperation between
the guard and Ingall residents, saying information from citizens greatly helps
preventive and enforcement action. [@1] The authorities promised increased
vigilance and regular patrols on sensitive routes, with judicial follow-up so
that the arrests lead to prosecutions. [@1]
