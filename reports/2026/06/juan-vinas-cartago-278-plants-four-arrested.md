A joint police operation located and eradicated a suspected marijuana plantation
in Juan Viñas, a district of Jiménez canton in Cartago province. [@2] Four men
were detained during the operation as suspects of maintaining the plantation.
[@2] Three of the suspects were to serve three months of preventive detention,
while the fourth had to sign in every 15 days. [@1]

Officers found a plantation of 278 plants of possible marijuana, between 28
centimetres and 2.3 metres tall. [@3] A separate account in La Nación put the
number of plants at 236. [@1] Besides the plants, officers seized a .22-calibre
carbine without make or serial number, a magazine with six rounds and a box of
28 cartridges. [@2] They also seized cultivation supplies, fertilisers,
agrochemicals and documents. [@2]

The operation involved the Fuerza Pública, the Policía de Control de Drogas
(PCD, the drug police), the Grupo de Apoyo Operacional (GAO) and the Dirección
de Inteligencia y Análisis Criminal (DIAC). [@2] The operation followed a series
of violent incidents in the canton this year, which police cited as the reason
for reinforced security actions in the area. [@2] The case passed to judicial
authorities for further investigation. [@2]
