Raigad police booked Percept chief Harindra Singh for allegedly smoking cannabis
at a party in Alibag on 14 June 2026. [@1] Police raided a gated colony in Saral
village and booked Singh and another person for allegedly consuming cannabis.
[@1] The other person was identified as Sunil Baliram Chawla, 69, a resident of
Inner Circle Society, Saral. [@1] A Ukrainian national was also booked for
allegedly overstaying her visa. [@1]

Officers seized cannabis-filled paper rolls and a partially burnt cannabis
cigarette. [@1] Police recovered approximately three grams of cannabis from the
spot. [@1] The seized quantity falls under personal consumption. [@1] Cases were
registered at Mandwa Coastal Police Station under the NDPS Act, alongside an
immigration charge against the woman. [@1] The initial case was registered for
consumption and notices were issued, with an investigation into the supply chain
under way. [@1] The initial case was for consumption, notices to that effect

Harindra Singh. [@1] PI Somnath Lande, part of the investigating team, told
