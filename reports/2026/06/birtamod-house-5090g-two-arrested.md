A police team from the Area Police Office in Anarmani, acting on a special
tip-off, recovered 5 kilograms and 90 grams of marijuana from a house in
Birtamod Municipality-4. [@1] Suman Baniya, Acting Information Officer for the
District Police Office, Jhapa, gave the account of the house search. [@1] During
the raid police arrested 30-year-old Binod Magar of Arjundhara Municipality-7
and 20-year-old Sagar Rai of Hatuwagadhi Rural Municipality-4, Bhojpur, for
alleged involvement in the illicit drug trade. [@1] A team from the Area Police
Office Anarmani seized 5 kg 90 grams of cannabis from a house in Ward No. 4 of
Birtamod Municipality. [@2] Police arrested Binod Magar and Sagar Rai on charges
of involvement in drug trafficking. [@2]

In a separate incident, police arrested two other men with brown sugar in the
Taghandubba area of Jhapa Rural Municipality-4. [@1] A team from the Taghandubba
Police Post grew suspicious of people walking from India towards Nepal and
recovered 6 grams and 1 milligram of brown sugar from 32-year-old Suraj Rai of
Damak Municipality-10. [@1] Police recovered 8 grams and 70 milligrams of a
substance resembling brown sugar from 42-year-old Baburam Darjee of Biratnagar
Metropolitan City-3, Morang. [@1] Both of those men were brought to the District
Police Office, Jhapa, for further investigation. [@1] The brown-sugar arrests
were a separate incident in the Tagnadubba area of Jhapa Rural Municipality-4.
[@2]
