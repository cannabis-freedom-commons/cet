Moses Chauke, 50, appeared before Harare regional magistrate Francis Mapfumo
facing a charge of unlawful possession of dangerous drugs. [@1] He was remanded
in custody to the following day for a bail hearing. [@1] The haulage truck was
driven by Thapson Ndou and was destined for Harare. [@1] Ndou had already been
arrested and was on remand facing related charges. [@1] Everjoy Chipfiko,
described as the intended recipient of the consignment, was also on remand
facing a similar charge over collection arrangements in Harare. [@1] Three other
suspected accomplices, Prosper Jena, Moses Chiverengera and a woman known only
as Victoria, remained at large. [@1]

The State alleges that on 1 June 2026 Chauke illegally imported 1,094.20 kg of
compressed dagga from South Africa into Zimbabwe through an unauthorised Limpopo
River crossing east of the Beitbridge Border Post. [@1] After the crossing,
Chauke allegedly coordinated transportation of the consignment to Harare. [@1]
The contraband was loaded into a 30-tonne haulage truck concealed inside a metal
container. [@1] Police, acting on information received, intercepted the truck
the same day at Ligi Restaurant Truck Stop in Southerton, Harare, opposite Swift
Company. [@1] A search of the vehicle led to the recovery of the dagga. [@1] On
2 June the recovered drugs were weighed at the Zimpost Harare Main Post Office
at a total of 1,094.20 kg. [@1] The court heard the recovered dagga had an
estimated street value of US$1.2 million. [@1]

Investigations subsequently led police to arrest Chauke at around 2am on 15 June
in Dulivhadzimu, Beitbridge. [@1] His mobile phone was recovered during that
arrest. [@1] Examination of the phone allegedly uncovered messages, chats, voice
notes and call logs linking Chauke to his co-accused and the smuggling
operation. [@1]
