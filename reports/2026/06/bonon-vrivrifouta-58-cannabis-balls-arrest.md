Police in Bonon arrested a 29-year-old man suspected of drug trafficking on
Sunday 21 June 2026, with a large stock of cannabis and tramadol tablets, in the
town's Vrivrifouta quarter. [@1] The arrest came during a patrol breaking up
drug-smoking dens in the Vrivrifouta and Fracroh sub-quarters, where several
people caught dealing and consuming fled at the sight of officers. [@1] The man,
identified only by the initials O.B.J., unemployed and living in Bonon's
Jacqueville quarter, was the only person the officers restrained. [@1]

The operation was carried out at about 1 pm under the first-class police
commissioner Zuli Bi Boua, as part of the national Operation Épervier XI, a
drive launched by the Directorate General of the National Police against drug
trafficking and use. [@1] In questioning the suspect admitted the seized
products were his and said they were meant for resale. [@1] He named a supplier
known by the nickname "T" and living in the town, and an investigation was
opened to arrest him. [@1]

The search seized 58 balls of cannabis, 63 tramadol tablets and three pairs of
scissors used to package the drugs. [@1] The prosecutor at the Bouaflé court of
first instance ordered his committal to detention under order number 1305,
pending his appearance in court. [@1]
