Prison officers arrested a woman at the Centro de Corrección y Rehabilitación
Las Parras in San Antonio de Guerra after catching her trying to bring a
prohibited substance into the prison during a family visit on Saturday. [@3] The
woman, accompanied by an 11-year-old girl, was surprised in flagrante delicto
trying to hand a package to an inmate, was handed to the Public Prosecutor's
office and had her name withheld to protect the girl. [@1]

The package was found on an inmate who was trying to leave the children's
visiting area with a cylindrical parcel hidden in his intimate parts. [@2]
Guards seized the parcel, which on its physical characteristics, smell and
texture was presumed to contain marijuana pending laboratory analysis. [@2]

After the seizure, inmates in some modules tried to disturb the order of the
prison in response to the control measures and the arrest of their fellow
inmate, but prison security officers restored order. [@1] The episode prompted
internal reviews by the prison and the Dirección General de Servicios
Penitenciarios y Correccionales (DGSPC), the country's prison service
directorate, to determine responsibilities. [@1] The DGSPC said visits other
than conjugal visits and those for delivering medicine were suspended. [@1]
