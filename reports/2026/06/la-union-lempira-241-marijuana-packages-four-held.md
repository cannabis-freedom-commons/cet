Agents of Honduras's National Anti-Drug Police Directorate (DNPA), assigned to
Regional Anti-Drug Unit No. 4 in Copán and working with the Special Prosecutor's
Office Against Organized Crime (FESCCO), detained four people in western
Honduras. [@1] Three men and one woman were detained and placed at the disposal
of judicial authorities on suspicion of aggravated drug trafficking. [@2] The
operation was carried out on the road from La Unión, Lempira, toward San
Nicolás, Santa Bárbara, where officers stopped a pick-up that had been kept
under surveillance on suspicion of transporting illicit substances. [@1]

Officers found handmade false compartments in the pick-up holding 77 double
packages and 21 single packages of suspected marijuana, hidden to evade police
checkpoints. [@1] A further search turned up 29 double and eight single packages
hidden inside one of the vehicle's tires. [@1] The official tally was 106 double
and 29 single packages, equivalent to 241 packages of suspected marijuana. [@3]

All the seized evidence was sent under chain of custody to the Public Ministry
for analysis and legal proceedings. [@2]
