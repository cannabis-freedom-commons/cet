Officers of the National Drug Control Directorate (DNCD), soldiers of the Joint
Interagency Force and prosecutors seized the marijuana at a military checkpoint
at Los Pilones in Azua province. [@1] The operation led to the arrest of a man
and a woman. [@1] They were the driver of the vehicle and a woman who was with
him. [@3] The identities of the two detainees were not released. [@1]

The marijuana was found hidden in the spare-wheel compartment of an SUV after
officers stopped the vehicle on an intelligence tip. [@1] A search ordered by
prosecutors uncovered 13 bales in a hidden compartment at the back of the
vehicle. [@3] The National Institute of Forensic Sciences put the total weight
at 129.62 pounds, about 58.8 kilograms. [@2] Officers also took three mobile
phones and cash. [@1]

The two were to be handed over to the Public Prosecutor's Office to answer for
an alleged violation of Law 50-88 on drugs and controlled substances. [@1]
