Police in Dushanbe detained a man suspected of illegally trafficking narcotics
and seized more than 17 kilograms of hashish from him. [@1] According to
investigators, he distributed drugs in the capital through social networks and,
after receiving payment, sent buyers the addresses of places where the drugs
were hidden. [@1] The police called the method «закладки» (stash drops), in
which drugs are left hidden at an address passed to the buyer. [@1]

During a search, officers found and seized more than 17 kilograms of hashish
from the suspect. [@1] A criminal case was opened against him under part 4 of
Article 200 of Tajikistan's Criminal Code, which covers illegal trafficking of
narcotic drugs. [@1] The suspect is in pre-trial detention while the
investigation continues. [@2]
