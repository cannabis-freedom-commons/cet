Drug Enforcement Unit officers seized a significant quantity of suspected
marijuana in Grand Bahama on Friday, June 12, 2026, following an
intelligence-led operation. [@1] According to preliminary reports, officers
acted on information received and proceeded along a dirt road off Wild Cat Alley
to investigate. [@1] Officers reached the area shortly after 1:00 p.m. and
conducted checks along the dirt road. [@1] While conducting checks in the area,
officers found and searched a derelict tan Chevrolet vehicle concealed in nearby
bushes. [@1]

Inside the Chevrolet, officers discovered a blue Igloo cooler containing
multiple packages of suspected marijuana. [@1] The seizure involved multiple
packages stored together in a single cooler hidden inside the concealed vehicle.
[@1] The suspected drugs weighed approximately 19.5 pounds, about 8.85
kilograms. [@1] Police estimated the street value of the suspected drugs at
$19,500. [@1]

Officers seized the suspected marijuana and took it into police custody for
further investigation, which was continuing. [@1]
