In Chiriquí province, two Colombian citizens were sentenced to 150 months in
prison after accepting responsibility in an international drug-trafficking case.
[@1] The prosecutor asked a Guarantees Tribunal to validate the plea agreement,
and the judge verified it met the legal requirements before issuing the
conviction. [@2] The 150-month term equals twelve years and six months. [@5]

The case dates to 17 December 2025, when SENAN units on maritime patrol
intercepted a suspect boat at Isla Montuosa in the Gulf of Chiriquí. [@2] The
two people aboard tried to flee with evasive manoeuvres, setting off a pursuit
before officers secured the speedboat. [@3] Officers counted 74 sacks holding
2,851 packages and moved the detainees, the boat and the packages to the SENAN
base at Quebrada de Piedra. [@3] The police action took place south of Isla
Montuosa, where two men travelling in the launch were apprehended. [@4]
