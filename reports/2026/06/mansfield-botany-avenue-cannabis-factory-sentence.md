Nottingham Crown Court jailed six men for running a sophisticated cannabis
factory in Mansfield. [@1] Lanti Avapi, Artu Ruqu, Eldis Beneri, Elton Salihasi
and Robert Ruko, all of no fixed address, and Ardit Gjonaj, of Crocketts Lane in
Smethwick, all pleaded guilty to being concerned in the production of a Class B
drug. [@1] Sentencing the men, Judge James Sampson called the factory an
extremely large and sophisticated commercial operation inside fortified premises
with bricked-up entrances and CCTV. [@1] Avapi, 45, received 24 months, Ruqu,
44, received 36 months, Beneri, 34, received 22 months, Salihasi, 41, received
24 months, Ruko, 48, received 24 months, and Gjonaj, 37, received 32 months.
[@1] The judge accepted each defendant played a lesser role and was unlikely to
have had a managerial or directing function. [@1]

Officers forced their way into a fortified warehouse in Botany Avenue,
Mansfield, on 3 May after acting on a strong smell of cannabis and hearing
people inside. [@1] They found almost 5,000 plants with an estimated street
value of between £1 million and £3 million. [@1] The court heard 3,928 plants
were in three growing areas with a potential yield of between 110kg and 330kg
worth between £1 million and £3 million. [@1] A further 935 plants were in a
drying area and were not valued by officers. [@1] Nottinghamshire Police said
the factory was its largest ever cannabis seizure, surpassing 4,049 plants found
in Basford in 2017. [@1]

Officers had been alerted to the farm after getting a strong smell of cannabis
while patrolling in nearby Sutton Road. [@1] Officers returned the next day and
found a building with sealed entrances. [@1] Officers noticed movement inside
and could hear people, called for back-up units and arrested the defendants in
the building. [@1] The court heard the men lived in cramped and unsanitary
conditions with no personal hygiene beyond a sink. [@1] The court heard the
plants were healthy and well-maintained and the operation showed a high level of
expertise and planning. [@1]
