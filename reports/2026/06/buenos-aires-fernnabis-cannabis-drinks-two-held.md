Federal police raided a house in the city of Buenos Aires where cannabis-infused
alcoholic drinks were made and sold, detaining two people. [@1] The operation
was carried out by the Division for Chemical Precursors and Emerging Drugs of
the Policía Federal Argentina. [@1] La Nación identified the production site as
a property on Piedras al 600 in the Monserrat neighbourhood and said a couple
was behind the operation. [@2] The two detainees were placed at the disposal of
the federal justice while the case continued over suspected offences under drug
law 23.737. [@1] Both suspects were later released because they had no prior
criminal records and will continue the case at liberty. [@2]

Officers found three marijuana plants about 60 centimetres tall. [@2] They also
seized two bags of marijuana leaves weighing 1,225 grams in total and a jar
containing 195 grams of buds. [@1] The haul included 12 twenty-litre drums,
about 66 litres of an alcohol and cannabis preparation, 30 bottles of fernet and
10 bottles of cannabis liqueur and Fernnabis. [@1] La Nación itemised two
bottles of cannabis liqueur, 30 bottles of Fernnabis and 19 plastic diffuser
jars containing alcohol with cannabis. [@2]

The case began when a bottle labelled "FERNNABIS – Un Fernet con Extractos de
Cannabis" was found near nightclubs in Ramos Mejía. [@1] Investigators used
intelligence work, social media monitoring, open-source analysis and a
court-approved controlled purchase. [@1] A chemical test on a sample from the
controlled purchase returned positive for THC. [@1] The drinks were promoted on
social media and messaging apps as having psychoactive effects, with delivery
offered across Argentina. [@1] The investigation was led by Federal Prosecutor's
Office No. 1 of Morón under federal judge Jorge Ernesto Rodríguez. [@1] El1
reported that the investigation began when the chemical precursors division
identified the drink being sold through social media and digital platforms. [@3]
