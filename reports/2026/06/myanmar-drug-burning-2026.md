On the UN International Day against Drug Abuse and Illicit Trafficking, the regime burned more than 50 tonnes of heroin, opium, ketamine, methamphetamine, marijuana and crystal meth, valued at about US$600 million, in Yangon, Mandalay and Taunggyi. Official figures put the destroyed narcotics and precursors at 603.77 million U.S. dollars nationwide. [@3] In Yangon alone, some $321 million worth of 31 different types of drugs were set ablaze. [@2] The destroyed narcotics included 31 kinds worth more than 321.32 million dollars in Yangon, 27 kinds worth 113.68 million dollars in Mandalay and 93 kinds worth 168.77 million dollars in Taunggyi. [@3] Events were also held in Mandalay and in Taunggyi to mark the United Nations’ International Day Against Drug Abuse and Illicit Trafficking. [@2] Authorities burned more than 50 tons of heroin, opium, ketamine, methamphetamine, marijuana and crystal meth — some $600 million of confiscated illegal drugs destroyed nationwide. [@2] The total was more than double the 2025 figure. No marijuana weight was given. [@1]
