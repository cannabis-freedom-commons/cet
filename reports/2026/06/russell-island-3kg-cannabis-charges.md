After Queensland police executed a search warrant at a Russell Island residence
last week, they charged a local 60-year-old Russell Island man with drug
offences. [@1] Police issued the man with a formal notice to appear at Cleveland
Magistrates Court on 7 July. [@1]

The man faces four counts in total. [@1] On the first of the four counts, police
allege the 60-year-old man possessed a schedule 2 drug in a quantity exceeding
schedule 3 possession limits. [@1] On the second of the four counts, police
allege the 60-year-old man supplied dangerous drugs. [@1] On the third count,
police allege the 60-year-old man possessed property suspected of having been
used in connection with the commission of a drug offence. [@1] On the fourth
count, police allege the 60-year-old man contravened an order about information
necessary to access information stored electronically. [@1]

It will be alleged that officers located three kilograms of cannabis inside the
searched Russell Island property on 11 June. [@1]
