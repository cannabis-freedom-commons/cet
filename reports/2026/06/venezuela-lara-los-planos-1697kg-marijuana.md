Officers of the Lara state police force, known as Polilara, seized 1,697.45
kilograms of marijuana packed in 2,237 wrappers and hidden inside two pickups.
[@2] The seizure was made in the caserío Los Planos, in the rural Torres
municipality of Lara state. [@2] The interior ministry said the load had been
carried in more than 2,200 wrappers inside two pickups that the occupants
abandoned when they noticed the police. [@1] Officers had chased the two pickups
through the rural area, and the suspects fled into woodland as they were
cornered. [@3] The interior ministry said a saturation operation was deployed in
the area to capture those who fled. [@2]

The seizure was announced by the interior ministry, the EFE news agency
reported. [@4]

One pickup, a Toyota Autana, carried 35 bundles holding 1,125 wrappers weighing
899.05 kilograms, while a Toyota Samuray carried 31 bundles with 1,112 wrappers
weighing 798.40 kilograms. [@2] The interior ministry said the load made up 66
bundles placed at the disposal of the competent institutions. [@2] National
Guard officers joined the operation to carry out the technical testing of the
marijuana blocks. [@3] The bundles were handed to the judicial authorities to
begin the investigations. [@1]

Venezuela's national anti-drug superintendency, Sunad, published the operation's
details on Instagram and described the seizure as a heavy blow to the criminal
gangs dedicated to illicit drug trafficking. [@3]
