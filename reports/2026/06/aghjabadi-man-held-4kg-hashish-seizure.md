In Aghjabadi, officers of the Aghjabadi District Police detained Mahir
Magerramov on suspicion of illegal trafficking in narcotic drugs following
operational measures conducted by the district police. [@1] The Barda regional
group of the Interior Ministry press service announced the detention and named
the detainee as Mahir Magerramov. [@1]

During the operation, officers discovered and seized 2 kilograms of heroin and
more than 4 kilograms of hashish from the detainee. [@1] The overall seizure was
described as 6 kilograms of drugs taken out of circulation in Aghjabadi. [@1] In
his testimony, the detainee stated that he had purchased the drugs for the
purpose of sale. [@1] The cannabis component of the seizure was the more than 4
kilograms of hashish, with the remaining 2 kilograms reported as heroin. [@1]

Following the seizure, the authorities opened a criminal case over the alleged
illegal drug trafficking. [@1] A court then selected detention as the preventive
measure against M. Magerramov, keeping him in custody. [@1]
