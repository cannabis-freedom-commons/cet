Trinidad and Tobago police arrested six people while seizing three firearms,
ammunition, cannabis and contraband during coordinated State of Emergency
operations. [@1] Task Force officers searched the home of a male suspect in
Tunapuna and found 11 fully grown cannabis trees, 1.256 kilogrammes of cannabis
and four pieces of camouflage clothing. [@1] Police arrested the man in
connection with the finds. [@1] In a separate North Central Division exercise,
officers arrested a Macoya man for possession of cannabis. [@1]

The exercises were carried out by the Trinidad and Tobago Police Service across
the Northern, Northern North, North Central and North Eastern Divisions on
Thursday. [@1] The Tunapuna search ran between 5.30 pm and 9 pm. [@1] Acting on
intelligence, officers later searched a bushy area in Maloney and found a pistol
fitted with a magazine containing 12 rounds of ammunition. [@1] Officers
arrested four suspects during a separate operation. [@1] That North Central
Division exercise ran between 6 pm and 11 pm and involved CID units, the Guard
and Emergency Branch and the Canine Unit. [@1] Police arrested an El Dorado man
found with a Beretta pistol loaded with 14 rounds, a Trinity man over malicious
damage and threats to kill, and a Tunapuna man over housebreaking and larceny.
[@1]

In another North Central Division operation, officers searched a warehouse at
Garden Village, Arouca, and found quantities of illegal cigarettes and alcohol.
[@1] Police arrested a 60-year-old Arouca man in connection with that seizure.
[@1] Task Force officers in the North Eastern Division searched an abandoned
wooden structure in Morvant between 2.30 pm and 5.30 pm and found an AR-15 rifle
with a magazine containing 29 rounds of 5.56 ammunition. [@1] Police said
investigations into all of the matters were continuing. [@1]
