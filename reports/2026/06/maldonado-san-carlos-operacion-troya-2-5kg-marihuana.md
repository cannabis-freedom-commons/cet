The Maldonado Departmental Anti-Drug Brigade carried out raids on 23 and 24 June
2026 that led to the detention of three people. [@4] The first raid, on the
afternoon of 23 June at a property on Dr. José Ramón Cortés street in Maldonado,
detained a 30-year-old man with a criminal record whom police linked to drug
sales there. [@2] Hours later, at a house on Rafael Urbin street in San Carlos,
officers detained two more men, aged 26 and 48. [@2] Police said the operation
dismantled two sites linked to micro-trafficking of narcotics in Maldonado and
San Carlos. [@1]

In the San Carlos house police seized about 2.5 kilograms of marijuana buds, 93
paper wrappers of cocaine hydrochloride, cash, precision scales and mobile
phones. [@2] At the Maldonado property they seized 70 grams of cocaine
hydrochloride, precision scales and a mobile phone. [@2] Officers also recovered
a 110cc motorcycle that had been reported stolen in San Carlos in March. [@3]

The investigation was led by Maldonado's 4th Turn prosecutor's office, headed by
prosecutor Ana Carolina Dean, with support from the PADO II programme and
Investigaciones III. [@3] Two of the detainees were convicted, and the
26-year-old was sentenced to 2 years in prison for supplying narcotics. [@4] The
48-year-old was sentenced to 2 years and 2 months for supplying narcotics and
handling stolen goods. [@4] The 30-year-old was sentenced on 25 June to 2 years
and 3 months for aggravated assault, personal injuries and continued supply of
narcotics. [@4]
