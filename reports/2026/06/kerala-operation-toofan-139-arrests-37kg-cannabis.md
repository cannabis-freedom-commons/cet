Police in Kerala arrested 139 people across the state on anti-drug day as part
of Operation Toofan. [@1] Officers registered 127 cases during that one-day
drive. [@1] Total arrests under Operation Toofan had reached 4,340 at that
point. [@1]

Officers seized 37.016 kilograms of cannabis from those held. [@1] Officers also
seized 2.24 grams of hashish oil in the same drive. [@1] The same seizures also
yielded 270.5974 grams of MDMA and 0.4 grams of heroin, which are excluded from
the cannabis total. [@1]

The drive was a one-day statewide operation held on anti-drug day. [@1]
