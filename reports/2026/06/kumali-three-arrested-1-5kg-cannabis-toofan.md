Kumali police arrested three young men with 1.5 kg of cannabis under Operation
Toofan. [@1] Those held were M.J. Ebin, 19, Santhosh, 23, and Arjun, 22. [@1]
The cannabis was found at Ebin's home. [@1]

Police said they had been monitoring the three on a tip that they had been
selling cannabis in the area for some time. [@1] According to police, the
cannabis had been brought from Perumbavoor to Kumali and stored at Ebin's house
for sale. [@1] Officers questioned the three and learned how the cannabis had
been supplied. [@1]

The operation was led by Toofan special officer Mariappan Shino Mon, Circle
Inspector Abhilash Kumar and Sub-Inspector Kannan. [@1] The three were produced
before Peerumade court and remanded. [@1]
