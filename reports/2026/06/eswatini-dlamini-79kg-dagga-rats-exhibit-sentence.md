Rats destroyed part of a dagga exhibit in a drug possession case while it was
stored at Pigg's Peak Police Station, a police officer told Pigg's Peak
Magistrate Thabiso Fakudze on Wednesday. [@1] John Dlamini, 44, of Lugaganeni,
faced a charge of contravening the Pharmacy Act over dagga possession. [@2] The
charge was contravening Section 12(1)(a)(i) of the Pharmacy Act No. 38 of 1929.
[@2] The charge sheet said that on 3 June 2025 at about 8am, at Nginamadvolo in
the Hhohho region, Dlamini wrongfully and unlawfully possessed 79.2 kilogrammes
of dagga while driving a black Toyota Hilux. [@2] Dlamini pleaded guilty and his
lawyer confirmed the plea. [@2] The Crown nevertheless called a police officer
to prove the offence. [@2]

The officer said he was patrolling along the Mlambongwenya Bridge on 3 June 2025
when he stopped the vehicle. [@2] The search found eight black refuse bags
containing dagga. [@2] The accused was arrested and the exhibits were taken to
Pigg's Peak Police Station for processing. [@2] The dagga was weighed in the
accused's presence, each bag about 10 kg, making about 80 kg in total, and part
was sent for forensic analysis. [@2] The officer said rats damaged the plastic
bags and some of their contents while the dagga was stored at the police
station. [@2] Because the bags were destroyed, some dagga was later stored in a
blue container that was not part of the original exhibit. [@2] The officer said
the rat damage and the transfer to another container may have affected the
exhibit's weight. [@2]

The court found Dlamini guilty in accordance with his plea. [@2] In mitigation
his lawyer said he was a first-time offender with family responsibilities who
had pleaded guilty at the earliest opportunity. [@2] Magistrate Fakudze
sentenced him to five years' imprisonment with an option of a E5,000 fine. [@2]
