The Anakapalli district police arrested five people, including a man police
described as a drug smuggler with links to Sri Lanka, and seized 213 kg of
ganja. [@1] The arrested were Shaikh Ajmal Khan, 30, of Ramanathapuram district
in Tamil Nadu; his brother Shaikh Abdul Rahman of Narsipatnam; T. Durga Prasad
of Narsipatnam; K. Lova Prasad; and Ch. Ravi Kumar of Pedabayalu. [@1]

Superintendent of Police Tuhin Sinha told a press conference on 17 June 2026
that a special team of Parawada police checked vehicles at Lankelapalem Junction
on 16 June and stopped a car carrying 213 kg of ganja. [@1] Officers also seized
about Rs 95,000 in cash. [@1] Sinha said Abdul Rahman and his associates had
procured the ganja in Odisha and brought it into Andhra Pradesh. [@1] The
contraband was moved through Anakapalli and Narsipatnam before being routed to
Vijayawada, with plans to transport it to Chennai and Ramanathapuram in Tamil
Nadu. [@1]

Police said they had identified the network's forward and backward links and
were trying to arrest six more people. [@1] Sinha said Ajmal Khan had been
arrested by the Sri Lankan Narcotics Bureau in April 2022 in a case involving
495 grams of methamphetamine and 8.5 kg of hashish oil, and was held in prisons
in Colombo and Galle. [@1] After his release on bail in October 2025, Ajmal Khan
entered India illegally by sea and began smuggling narcotics with his brother
and other associates, police said. [@1] The district police said they would
coordinate with central investigating agencies, the Sri Lankan Embassy, and the
Sri Lankan police and prison authorities. [@1]
