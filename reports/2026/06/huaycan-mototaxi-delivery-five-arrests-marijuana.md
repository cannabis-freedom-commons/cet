Police in Huaycán dismantled a band dedicated to selling drugs by delivery with
mototaxis in the Horacio Ceballos zone, where members carried out packing and
distribution from four in the afternoon. [@1] The intervention was carried out
in Horacio Ceballos and allowed officers to detain the members at their base of
operations. [@1] The operation relied on two drones to locate the suspects
inside a six-storey building where direct surveillance was limited for the
officers. [@1] Police said the band was detained in its entirety, four men and
one woman, with the woman identified as the ringleader's partner in a support
role. [@1]

Police reported seizing three motorcycle-taxis, five mobile phones, 287 packets,
11 packets of marijuana, five watches and packing utensils including a strainer,
a grater, spoons and paper used for wrapping. [@1] The group did the packing at
its base and then went out to deliver from four in the afternoon in three torito
motorcycle-taxis in circuit rounds, carrying the drugs to homes. [@1] The
vehicles were not their own but rented to reduce any direct trace to those
involved. [@1] RPP identified the alleged leader as 19-year-old Jefferson Bullón
Calle, known as Flaco Yepes. [@2] Officers said Bullón Calle and his partner
Nicole Andrea Peña, alias Gordita, kept a base house where they gathered the
illicit material for distribution in eastern Lima. [@2]

Intelligence work also led to the flagrant capture of Víctor Encarnación
Briceño, alias Gato Víctor, and Carlos Huangal Rivas, alias Carlos, while
allegedly distributing drugs in the area. [@2] The detainees remained under a
fifteen-day investigation before being placed at the disposal of the Public
Ministry and then the judiciary. [@1]
