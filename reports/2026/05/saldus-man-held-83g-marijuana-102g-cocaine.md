On 14 May 2026 at about 21:10, officers of the Dienvidkurzeme precinct of the
Kurzeme regional administration of the State Police detained a man in Saldus on
suspicion of illegal circulation of intoxicating substances and found substances
resembling drugs on him. [@1] The man is a Saldus resident who had not
previously come to police attention. [@1] He was given suspect status with a
security measure not involving deprivation of liberty. [@2]

Later the same day officers with a police dog handler searched the man's
residence and properties belonging to him. [@3] The search recovered cocaine and
marijuana together with cash amounting to several tens of thousands of euros.
[@1] The cash was placed under arrest on suspicion that it was obtained
criminally, possibly by selling narcotics. [@1] Completed forensic examinations
established that 102.02 grams of cocaine and 83.17 grams of marijuana were
removed from illegal circulation in Saldus. [@1]

The case remains under investigation for unauthorised storage and transport for
sale and unauthorised sale, offences punishable under the Criminal Law by two to
eight years' imprisonment, with or without confiscation, and up to three years'
probation supervision. [@1]
