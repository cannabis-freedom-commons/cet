On 4 May 2026, Dakar's Brigade Régionale des Stupéfiants (BRS) dismantled a
drug-trafficking network in Thiaroye-sur-Mer and arrested three people. [@3]
Investigators first arrested the main recipient and an accomplice in a targeted
house search, then arrested the pirogue captain at his nearby home. [@2]

The BRS said the operation seized 41,600 kg (41.6 kilograms) of chanvre indien,
or Indian hemp (cannabis). [@1] The search of the house found the drugs packed
as two parcels and seven "camlottes", or wrapped cones. [@1] Officers also
seized three mobile phones, 45,000 CFA francs and packaging material. [@1]

The operation followed a tip about a clandestine nighttime landing of chanvre
indien by pirogue, with the drugs then stored at a coastal home with the
captain's complicity. [@3] During questioning the main suspect admitted owning
the drugs while the captain admitted transporting them, with a dispute over the
quantity delivered. [@1] The three were placed in police custody for criminal
association, drug possession and trafficking, complicity and money laundering,
and the investigation continued. [@1]
