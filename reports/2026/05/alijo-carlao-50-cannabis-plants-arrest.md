Portugal's National Republican Guard (GNR) detained a 39-year-old man in
flagrante on 25 May 2026 on suspicion of drug trafficking in the municipality of
Alijó. [@2] The GNR said a denunciation and investigation led officers to catch
the suspect in the middle of a cannabis plantation while he was watering the
plants. [@2] The plantation was in Carlão, in the municipality of Alijó in Vila
Real district. [@1]

GNR military personnel from the criminal investigation unit at Peso da Régua
searched the man, his car and his residence. [@1] Officers seized 50 cannabis
sativa plants at various stages of flowering and development. [@2] They also
seized 40.9 grams of liamba, a cannabis product, equivalent to 16 doses. [@2]
Among the seized items were a shovel, hoes, a fork, pruning shears, thirteen
pots, half a bag of fertiliser and several metres of hose. [@2]

The man was constituted an arguido, formally named a suspect, then placed under
a Termo de Identidade e Residência (TIR), an identity and residence obligation;
he was released and notified to appear at the Alijó Judicial Court. [@2]
