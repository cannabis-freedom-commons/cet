In May 2026, officers of the ONC detained a 38-year-old resident of the
settlement of Smirnovka in the Solnechnogorsk urban district of Moscow Oblast.
[@1] Officers found about 4 kilograms of plant material in his dacha house. [@1]
A forensic examination confirmed that the material was cannabis (marijuana).
[@1] A criminal case was opened against the man under article 228.1 of the
Russian Criminal Code. [@1]

The Solnechnogorsk interior ministry department said 123 drug-related crimes had
been recorded in the district since the start of the year, 104 of them classed
as grave or especially grave. [@1] It listed hashish, marijuana,
N-methylephedrone, mephedrone and cocaine among the substances seized across the
period, with a combined weight of more than 48 kilograms. [@1] The department
said the main method of sale was contactless stashes left in residential
districts and forest strips. [@1] Of the crimes recorded, 98 episodes were sales
and 23 were storage of narcotic drugs. [@1]
