A check of the Mangalapuram-Coimbatore Express after it reached Shoranur railway
station led officers to seize 14 kilograms of cannabis that had been carried on
that train standing at the station at the time. [@1] Officers carried out the
examination of the train standing at the station at about 10:30 in the morning
on the previous day. [@1] The people who had brought the consignment are
believed to have slipped away from the spot as soon as they saw the police, and
the account names no one as arrested or detained. [@1]

Officers found the cannabis inside a trolley bag discovered during the train
check, where it had been divided and stored as seven separate packets. [@1] The
search inside the train that located the cannabis was led by Shoranur Railway
Sub-Inspector Anil Mathew together with RPF Sub-Inspector Balasubramaniam. [@1]
The report presents this recovery as yet another cannabis seizure at Shoranur
railway station, pointing to repeated seizures at the same station. [@1]
