A joint team of the police and the 42nd Battalion of the Sashastra Seema Bal
arrested a Nepalese national, Mohammad Jabir Halwai, near the Indo-Nepal border
in Uttar Pradesh's Bahraich district on Wednesday. [@1] Superintendent of Police
Vishwajeet Srivastava said the station house officer of Rupaideha had received
information that a consignment of charas was being smuggled into India from the
neighbouring country. [@1] Halwai, a resident of Eklaini village under the Bada
Prahri police station area in Nepal's Banke district, was arrested in the raid
that followed the tip-off. [@1]

According to the superintendent, 4.66 kilograms of charas, stated to be worth Rs
2.5 crore in the international market, was recovered from him. [@1] During
questioning, he allegedly told investigators that he had brought the contraband
from Nepal and was planning to sell it in India. [@1]

A case was registered against him under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic
Substances Act, and further legal proceedings were under way. [@1] Srivastava
said that, from the preliminary interrogation, efforts were being made to trace
the arrested smuggler's forward and backward linkages. [@1] A second account
says a joint effort between local police and the Sashastra Seema Bal's 42nd
Battalion led to the arrest of a Nepalese national near the Indo-Nepal border.
[@2] That account identifies the suspect as Mohammad Jabir Halwai and says he
was caught with 4.66 kilograms of illegal charas valued at approximately Rs 2.5
crore. [@2]
