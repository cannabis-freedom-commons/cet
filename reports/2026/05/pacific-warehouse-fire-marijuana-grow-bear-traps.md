Valley Regional Fire Authority crews responded to a commercial fire at a
warehouse in the 100 block of Stewart Road Southwest in Pacific, Washington, at
about 6:34 p.m. on 23 May 2026. [@3] Firefighters extinguished the blaze, found
no victims but discovered evidence of a marijuana grow operation and asked
police for help. [@1] A more thorough search of the property led to the
discovery of active bear traps. [@1]

Pacific police secured the scene and obtained a search warrant, which a judge
signed on 26 May and officers executed the following day. [@3] Officers seized
more than 1,000 marijuana plants and what officials described as extensive
hazardous chemicals. [@1] Police said four bear traps were placed outside the
entrances of the warehouse, posing a danger to first responders and others
entering the property. [@2] Investigators reported numerous building and fire
code violations and indications of suspected electrical power theft. [@1]

A second unit in the same warehouse complex was also being used for an illegal
grow, and a second warrant led to the seizure of several hundred additional
marijuana plants. [@1] The investigation remains ongoing and no arrests have
been announced; police have not said who is suspected of running the grows or
setting the traps. [@3] The Pacific Police Department is working with the fire
authority, Puget Sound Energy, city code enforcement and property management to
assess the safety and habitability of the building. [@1]
