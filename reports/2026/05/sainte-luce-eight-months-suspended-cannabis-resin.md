Gendarmes in Sainte-Luce opened an investigation after a tip-off to the brigade
numérique, the gendarmerie's online reporting service, that a resident was
probably growing cannabis at his home. [@1] The case was handled by the
gendarmerie company of Le Marin, which covers the south of Martinique. [@2] The
gendarmes went to his home in an operation and arrested him there. [@3] A search
of the home seized 1.151 kg of cannabis resin and 615 grams of cannabis herb.
[@3] Officers also seized all the equipment needed for cultivation, 4,005 euros
in cash and 37 cartons of contraband cigarettes. [@3]

In custody the man admitted that he sold drugs and contraband tobacco. [@1] He
was brought before the Fort-de-France judicial court on Friday 29 May 2026 under
the reconnaissance préalable de culpabilité, France's guilty-plea procedure.
[@3] The court sentenced him to eight months' imprisonment, suspended, with six
months under electronic monitoring. [@2] The brigade numérique, which received
the tip, lets members of the public exchange messages with a gendarme seven days
a week, around the clock. [@3]
