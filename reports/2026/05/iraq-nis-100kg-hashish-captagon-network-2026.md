Iraq's National Intelligence Service announced on Monday 4 May 2026 that it had
foiled an attempt to smuggle a large shipment of narcotics through Iraqi
territory to neighbouring countries. [@2] The service said the operation seized
100 kilograms of hashish and more than one million Captagon pills and arrested
all members of the network. [@1] The Captagon pills are a separate substance,
and only the 100 kilograms of hashish is a cannabis product counted here. [@1]

The network included Iraqi and foreign members identified on what the service
called precise intelligence, with arrests made across several provinces. [@1]
The operation was carried out with the Sulaimani-based Zanyari Intelligence
Service and the province's anti-narcotics directorate. [@1] It was conducted in
coordination with the Supreme Judicial Council and the head of the Karkh First
Investigation Court. [@3] Sabq reported that the intelligence operation had
lasted several months. [@3]

Iraq's narcotics laws impose death or life sentences for drug-related offences.
