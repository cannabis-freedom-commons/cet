Zambia's Drug Enforcement Commission jointly charged and arrested three men over
an organized transnational drug trafficking scheme at Kazungula Border Post in
Southern Province. [@1] The suspects were Tody Siakumbila, 33, a truck driver
from Monze District employed by a South African trucking company, Luka Phiri,
25, a shopkeeper, and John Kabaso, 23, a taxi driver, the last two from
Livingstone. [@3] Acting on intelligence, officers intercepted the truck driver
and the two other men. [@3]

The consignment weighed 150 kilogrammes of high-grade cannabis. [@1] DEC said it
was packed in 10 bales containing 141 pre-packed parcels. [@3] Deputy Public
Relations Officer Theresa Mawere said it was concealed beneath sacks of sulphur
aboard a truck from South Africa to evade detection. [@1] The commission seized
the truck carrying the consignment, along with a Toyota Vitz. [@2] DEC said the
Toyota Vitz had allegedly been hired by the prime suspect to ferry the cannabis
after it was offloaded. [@3]

The three suspects were remanded in lawful custody pending a court appearance
and further investigations. [@3] DEC described the operation as intelligence-led
and warned the public against drug trafficking and other transnational organized
crime. [@3]
