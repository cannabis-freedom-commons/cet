Eight people were charged following a joint police operation in Wilton's Yard,
Belair and Dierre Fort, three Castries communities, that seized suspected
cannabis and cannabis plants. [@1] The operation was carried out on 28 May by
officers of the Gangs, Narcotics and Firearms Unit, with support from the
Criminal Investigations Department, Central Intelligence Unit and Tactical
Response Unit. [@1] A search warrant at a residence in Wilton's Yard seized a
quantity of suspected cannabis and cannabis plants, and Ishon Joseph, 21, was
arrested. [@1] In Dierre Fort, searches seized more suspected cannabis and
several cannabis plants, leading to the arrest of six people: Naheem Samuel, 21,
Thecla Joseph, 45, Marcus Joseph, 46, Leon James, 21, Ryan Louis, 36, and a
17-year-old. [@2] A separate warrant in Belair led to the arrest of Kerius
Polius, 31, who was wanted over an armed robbery on Darling Road, Castries, on 7
May. [@1]

On 29 May, Ishon Joseph, Samuel, Thecla Joseph, Marcus Joseph, James, Louis and
the juvenile were charged with cultivation of cannabis, possession of a
controlled drug, and possession with intent to supply. [@3] Samuel, Thecla
Joseph, Marcus Joseph, James, Louis and the juvenile appeared before the First
District Court and were each granted bail of EC$1,500 on their own recognisance.
[@3] Ishon Joseph pleaded guilty at the First District Court on 1 June, was
fined EC$4,200 and placed on probation for one year, with six months to pay the
fine or face six months' imprisonment. [@3] Polius was charged on 2 June with
robbery and possession of ammunition without a valid licence and was granted
bail of $5,000 and $10,000 on the two charges. [@1]
