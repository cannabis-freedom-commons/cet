Agents of the Dirección Nacional de Control de Drogas (DNCD), the Dominican
drug-control agency, and members of the Public Ministry arrested two men in Boca
Chica. [@2] The two detainees were Claudio Ignacio González and Juan Ramón Pérez
de Oleo, located through intelligence and interdiction work. [@1] They were
found on calle Juan Pablo Duarte in the Los Coquitos sector of Boca Chica
municipality. [@1]

Officers seized 270 grams of marijuana, 348 grams of a white powder presumed to
be cocaine and more than 150 doses of crack. [@1] They also seized a scale, a
bulletproof vest, a communication radio, a mobile phone and a significant amount
of cash. [@1]

The DNCD said both men were linked to Los San Juaneros, a microtrafficking
structure under police surveillance. [@1] The network's alleged leader, alias
Antolín, remained at large, and authorities asked him to surrender while the
investigation continued. [@1] The DNCD's own press note said the operation was
led by the Joint Task Force (Fuerza de Tarea Conjunta, FTC). [@3]
