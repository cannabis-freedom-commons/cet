The DNCD and the Public Ministry seized 133 packages of presumed marijuana at
the Puerto de Haina Oriental in Santo Domingo Oeste during inspection
operations. [@1] The packages were found in two containers that had arrived from
Brooklyn and New York in the United States. [@1] The DNCD and the Public
Ministry said the seizures were part of reinforced controls at maritime
terminals within the national anti-drug strategy. [@1]

The first inspection followed the arrival of a container from Brooklyn. [@1] On
a prosecutor's instructions, agents searched inside and found seven plastic
boxes hiding 103 packages of presumed marijuana. [@1] Those packages were
wrapped in transparent and black plastic bags, as reported by the Public
Ministry. [@1] The find resulted from a routine verification procedure
reinforced in recent weeks because of more attempted maritime drug entries. [@1]
In the second inspection, DNCD teams with canine-unit support checked another
container from New York. [@1] During that search they found 30 packages of
suspected marijuana hidden inside tanks that also held canned food and used
clothing. [@1]

After both operations, the DNCD and the Public Ministry opened an investigation
to establish the shipments' exact origin and those responsible. [@1] Authorities
said they remain on alert and keep reinforcing surveillance at ports, airports
and other strategic points to detect and prevent new trafficking methods. [@1]
