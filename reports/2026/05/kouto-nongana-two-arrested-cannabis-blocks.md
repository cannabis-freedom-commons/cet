Two men were arrested in possession of drugs at the end of a police operation.
[@1] They were identified only as C.S, aged 31, and Y.M, aged 50. [@1] The
arrests took place in Nongana, in the department of Kouto in northern Côte
d'Ivoire. [@1]

The operation followed an anonymous tip, reported by security sources, that
individuals were selling drugs and prohibited medicines from their homes in
Nongana. [@1] A crew from the Korhogo regional unit of the Directorate of the
Narcotics and Drugs Police (DPSD), Côte d'Ivoire's national drug police, went
there to verify the information and make any arrests. [@1] The seizure comprised
two blocks of cannabis and 337 capsules and tablets of Tramadol in 100 mg, 120
mg and 250 mg strengths. [@1] The arrests were made under an operation called
'épervier XI'. [@1]

The two detainees were to answer for their actions before the competent
authorities. [@1]
