GNR officers arrested a 21-year-old man on 6 May 2026 during a traffic check on
the A2 motorway at Alcácer do Sal. [@1] Portuguese reports said the 21-year-old
was detained on suspicion of drug trafficking after a chase on the A2 at Alcácer
do Sal. [@2] Officers ordered a southbound car to stop, but the driver sped away
and endangered other road users. [@1] After several kilometres the fleeing car
collided with another vehicle. [@1] The driver then lost control and crashed on
the roadside. [@1] The occupants abandoned the car and fled on foot, and one
suspect was intercepted at the scene. [@1]

The GNR's criminal investigation unit (NIC) in Palmela confirmed drugs inside
the car. [@1] Officers seized 72.163 kilograms of hashish, the equivalent of
144,000 doses, along with a passenger car, two mobile phones and €245 in cash.
[@4] The GNR said the investigation was continuing and the facts had been
reported to the competent court. [@1]

A police source told Lusa the detainee was presented for a first judicial
interrogation at the Setúbal court, where coercive measures were to be
considered. [@1] The Setúbal Judicial Court ordered pre-trial detention after
that first judicial interrogation. [@3] The suspect was then taken to Setúbal
prison. [@3]
