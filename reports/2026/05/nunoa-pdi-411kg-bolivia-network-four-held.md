Detectives of Chile's Investigative Police dismantled a criminal structure
dedicated to drug trafficking after an investigation led by the Los Vilos
Antinarcotics Brigade. [@1] The operation detained four people, three Colombian
citizens and one Venezuelan. [@1] The accused were detained at different points
in Ñuñoa, with further procedures in Estación Central, Quinta Normal and in the
north of the country. [@1] The eastern Fiscalía formalized the four on Wednesday
and all four were placed in preventive prison. [@2] The court set a 90-day
investigation period while the investigation continues. [@2]

Officers seized 411 kilos of cannabis. [@2] The marijuana was contained in 405
cling-film wrappers, and officers seized two vehicles and more than five cell
phones. [@2] Police put the haul at more than 400,000 doses with a value of more
than 2 billion pesos. [@1]

The organization moved the drugs from Bolivia into Chile through unauthorized
border crossings in the Antofagasta region. [@1] The drugs entered
clandestinely, were stored in Calama and then transported by high-tonnage trucks
to eastern Santiago. [@2] That drug was stored in the commune of Ñuñoa, the
stash location identified on 24 May after PDI inquiries. [@2] Subprefect Augusto
Frías said the investigation of the transnational Colombian-Venezuelan
organization had run for about two months. [@2] The operation followed
collaboration with Carabineros in Tarapacá over a drug seizure made about three
weeks earlier in that region. [@2]
