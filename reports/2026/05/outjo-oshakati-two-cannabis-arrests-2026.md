Police arrested two suspects in separate drug-related incidents in Outjo and
Oshakati on Friday after cannabis worth tens of thousands of dollars was seized.
[@1] In Outjo's Soweto, Etoshapoort, a 27-year-old Namibian woman was arrested
after police discovered skunk cannabis in the boot of a VW Polo during a
roadblock search. [@1] Police valued the skunk cannabis at over N$50,000. [@1]
The drugs were allegedly intended for delivery by the suspect. [@1] The woman
remained in police custody and was expected to appear before the Outjo
Magistrate's Court on charges of dealing in drugs. [@1]

In a separate incident in Oshakati's Uupindi Location, a 34-year-old Namibian
man was arrested after police searched his residence. [@1] Cannabis was
allegedly found in his possession. [@1] He was also taken into custody, and
police investigations into the matter continue. [@1] Police said investigations
into the Outjo case were ongoing. [@1]
