A consignment in the name of Hayasher Gilbert of Au Leon, Dennery, was searched
at the Vieux Fort seaport. [@1] Officers recovered 4.70 kilogrammes of cannabis
resin from the consignment. [@1] Gilbert was charged with possession of a
controlled drug, possession of a controlled drug with intent to supply and
importation of a controlled drug. [@1] He appeared before the Second District
Court on Thursday 28 May 2026 and was granted bail of EC$55,000 cash or with a
suitable surety. [@2]

The search was carried out by the Gangs, Narcotics and Firearms Unit and the
Customs & Excise Department. [@3] It came during joint operations on Wednesday
27 May 2026, when teams also patrolled Castries streets and ran traffic checks.
[@3] Sweeps covered Wiltons Yard, Grass Street, Jeremie Street, Lewis Street and
Faux A Chaux in Castries, with traffic checks at Deglos, Bexon, Sarrot, Ti
Rocher, Chaussee, Bagatelle, Marchand, Union, Vigie and Calvary Road. [@1]

Police said 438 vehicles were stopped, 27 vehicles and 107 people were searched,
50 traffic tickets were issued and one arrest was made for a traffic offence.
[@1] Officers also recovered six knives, 15 scissors and 25 grammes of cannabis.
[@1] The force said the operations were part of ongoing efforts to combat
illicit trafficking, strengthen border security and improve public safety. [@1]
