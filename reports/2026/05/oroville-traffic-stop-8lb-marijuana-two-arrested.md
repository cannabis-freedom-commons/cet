Butte County sheriff's deputies began investigating a burglary at a business in
Palermo in late April 2026 and had surveillance footage of the suspect and the
suspect vehicle. [@1] Deputies stopped the suspect vehicle near Lower Wyandotte
Road and Ophir Road in Oroville on Monday 4 May 2026. [@1] The driver was
Richard Cintas, 33, of Oroville, and the passenger was Mariah Inman, 31, of
Chico. [@1] Deputies saw drug paraphernalia in plain view inside the vehicle and
searched it. [@1]

The search found 8 pounds (about 3.6 kilograms) of marijuana, along with 5.8
grams of methamphetamine, more paraphernalia, suspected burglary tools and
documents belonging to other people. [@1] Two people were arrested in the
burglary investigation. [@1]

Cintas was arrested on charges including burglary, possession of drug
paraphernalia, possession of personal identifying information with intent to
defraud and possession of marijuana over 28.5 grams. [@1] Inman was arrested for
possession of methamphetamine and possession of drug paraphernalia. [@1]
