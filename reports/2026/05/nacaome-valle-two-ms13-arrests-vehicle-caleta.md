Police announced the capture of two MS-13 members in Valle with a large quantity
of drugs in a vehicle hide. [@1] Police captured two alleged members of the Mara
MS-13 in the department of Valle after finding a hide holding a large quantity
of drugs inside a vehicle. [@2] The capture occurred in Nacaome, Valle, in a
strategic interception and intelligence operation against MS-13 distribution in
the south. [@3] Agents tracking Valle roadways profiled a suspicious vehicle and
found an artisan-built hidden compartment known as a caleta during a detailed
scan. [@3]

During the seizure officers took cocaine, marijuana and crack, plus more than
120,000 lempiras in cash described as proceeds of illicit activity. [@2] The
marijuana and crack portion was described as dozens of plastic wraps dosed for
immediate sale in outlying southern barrios and colonias. [@3] Police said the
drugs were hidden strategically in the vehicle hide for transport by the
criminal structure. [@2]

The two detainees were placed at the disposal of the Public Ministry to face
drug-trafficking and alleged membership of a criminal structure. [@2] They were
then referred to the Special Prosecutor Against Organized Crime to face
aggravated illicit drug trafficking against public health. [@3]
