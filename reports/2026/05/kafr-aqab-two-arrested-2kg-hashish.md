Palestinian police arrested two suspected drug dealers early on Friday 22 May
2026 and seized a quantity of suspected narcotics in Kafr Aqab, north of
Jerusalem. [@1] The police said the anti-drug branch had received precise
information that a quantity of narcotics was being brought from what the
statement called 'the occupied interior' (الداخل المحتل), the Palestinian term
for Israel inside the Green Line, to be delivered to one dealer in the Jerusalem
suburbs. [@2] An anti-drug force, backed by units from several departments of
the governorate's police, set up what the police described as a tight ambush and
arrested the two men. [@3]

In their possession officers found 18 blocks (بلاطة, slabs) suspected of being
hashish, with a combined weight of about two kilograms, according to the police
statement. [@1] The two suspects were detained and the material seized so that
legal procedures could be completed, PNN reported. [@3]

Police said they were pressing ahead with firm measures against drug dealers.
[@2]
