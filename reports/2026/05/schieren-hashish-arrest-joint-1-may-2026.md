During a patrol on the cycle path in Rue de la Gare in Schieren at about 09:30
on 1 May, officers saw a person smoking a joint and then found that the person
was carrying a knife and a small quantity of hashish. [@2] Because of existing
knowledge of the person's links to drug dealers and an earlier conviction for
drug trafficking, officers obtained a search warrant from the public prosecutor
for the home, where they found cash and a quantity of hashish. [@2]

On the basis of those findings the public prosecutor ordered the suspect's
arrest, and the person was brought before an investigating judge on 2 May 2026.
