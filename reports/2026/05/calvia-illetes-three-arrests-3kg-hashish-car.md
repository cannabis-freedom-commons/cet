Officers of the Calvià municipal police stopped a car in Illetes, Mallorca, and
detained three Peruvian immigrants who were carrying drugs and cash hidden
inside. [@1] Calvià town hall said the stop was made on the afternoon of "last
Monday" by officers from the Traffic and Multi-purpose divisions. [@1] The
Guardia Civil's own account instead placed the stop in the early hours of
Tuesday 19 May, after officers saw the car make a suspicious manoeuvre in
Illetes. [@3]

A small flap at the driver's feet, almost imperceptible and fitted as a false
bottom, prompted a fuller search. [@1] In the compartment officers found 15,000
euros in cash and drugs including 3.1 kilograms of hashish, cocaine, tusi and
ecstasy. [@1] They also seized five high-end watches and a pearl necklace of
doubtful origin. [@1] Última Hora, citing judicial sources, reported a lower
cash figure of 12,000 euros and about 40 grams of tusi. [@4]

The three suspects were arrested on suspicion of a crime against public health
and handed to the Guardia Civil. [@2] The Guardia Civil said the men were
investigated for drug trafficking and belonging to a criminal group. [@3]
Investigators said the drugs had been loaded into the false bottom in Barcelona
province, from where the men boarded a ferry on 14 May. [@3] They had stayed in
hotels in Magaluf while distributing the substances to others. [@3] A judge
ordered all three held in prison and the investigation was left open. [@3]
