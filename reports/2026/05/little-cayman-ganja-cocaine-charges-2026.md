On 6 May 2026 police and Customs and Border Control (CBC) officers ran a joint
operation on Little Cayman and stopped and searched a vehicle in the early
hours. [@1] They found a quantity of ganja in the vehicle and arrested the
28-year-old driver, a Little Cayman resident, on suspicion of possession. [@2]
Officers then searched his home under the Misuse of Drugs Act and found more
ganja, packages believed to be cocaine and drug paraphernalia. [@2]

The man was taken to Grand Cayman and booked into custody. [@1] He was charged
with possessing cocaine with intent to supply, possessing cocaine, possessing
ganja with intent to supply, possessing ganja, and possessing utensils used in
preparing or consuming a controlled drug. [@1] He appeared in court on Friday 8
May and was remanded in custody. [@1]

At a Summary Court hearing on 18 August 2026 he was named as Keniel Barrington
Braham and pleaded guilty to the cocaine supply and ganja possession counts.
[@3] The ganja was found in five places around the property: 3.12 ounces in two
mason jars in a container, 0.24 of an ounce in a bedroom chest of drawers, 1.95
ounces in a container marked GLAD, 0.69 of an ounce in a glass jar on a chest of
drawers, and 7.90 ounces in two bags in the kitchen fridge, 13.9 ounces (about
0.39 kg) in all. [@3] The Chief Magistrate declined to proceed with the drug
utensils count because it was not sufficiently particularised. [@3] Sentencing
was scheduled for 21 October 2026. [@3]
