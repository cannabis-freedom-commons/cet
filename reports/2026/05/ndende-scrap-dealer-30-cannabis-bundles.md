Philippe Mayana, a 33-year-old scrap dealer, was arrested in Ndendé, in Gabon's
Ngounié province, for possession and trafficking of cannabis. [@1] Agents of the
Central Anti-Drug Office carried out the arrest in a recent operation. [@1] He
worked for a construction company on the Ndendé-Doussala road and, according to
the account, did the trafficking in his spare time. [@1] Tipped off,
investigators searched his home in the Mindanda neighbourhood and found him
asleep with his girlfriend. [@1]

Investigators seized 30 cuisses of cannabis hidden in tall grass at Bel-Air,
another neighbourhood of the town. [@1] The cannabis was in two plastic bags.
[@1] Taken to the station for the inquiry, Mayana admitted the offences. [@1] He
named an alleged supplier, Davis Mouloungui, alias Psychopathe. [@1] Mouloungui
was said to live in Mabanda, the chief town of Doutsila department, on the
border with Congo-Brazzaville. [@1] Mayana said the bag of cannabis had been
sold to him for 60,000 CFA francs. [@1]

Brought before the public prosecutor, he was placed in pre-trial detention at
the central prison in Mouila. [@1] Under Gabon's penal code, unauthorised
possession of drugs is punishable by two to ten years in prison and a fine of
250,000 to 50 million CFA francs, and illicit trafficking or supply carries the
same penalties. [@1]
