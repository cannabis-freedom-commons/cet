Three people were arrested and charged in Antigua and Barbuda after police
searches in the Red Hill community seized a firearm, ammunition and several
quantities of cannabis. [@2] Embert St. Ange of Red Hill was charged with
unlawful possession of a firearm, ammunition and cannabis after officers found a
Ruger .380 auto pistol along with ammunition and cannabis. [@1] Gael St. Ange,
38, was charged separately with unlawful possession of 113 grams of cannabis.
[@1] Gael St. Ange was jointly charged with 28-year-old Tamisha Cyrille with
unlawful possession of 3.8 pounds of cannabis and unlawful possession of
cannabis with intent to transfer. [@1]

The searches were carried out in the early hours of 18 May 2026 by officers of
the Criminal Investigations Department Task Force, the Gang and Guns Suppression
Unit, the Special Services Unit and the K-9 Unit. [@2] Investigators said the
seizure from Mr St Ange included fourteen rounds of .380 ammunition, one round
of 9mm ammunition and approximately one pound of cannabis. [@1]

The three accused were expected to appear before the court at a later date. [@1]
Police said the arrests formed part of ongoing efforts to crack down on illegal
firearms and drug activity across Antigua and Barbuda. [@1]
