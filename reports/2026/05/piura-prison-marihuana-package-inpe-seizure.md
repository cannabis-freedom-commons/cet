Twenty-nine agents of the Instituto Nacional Penitenciario (INPE) searched the
left-hand sixth pavilion of the Piura prison in Peru and seized drugs and
prohibited items. [@2] A package wrapped in packing tape containing marihuana
was seized during the search. [@2] The intervention was coordinated with the
Peruvian National Police and representatives of the Public Ministry under a
'zero tolerance' policy on illicit goods entering prisons. [@1]

In a white bucket, officers found a bag of breads concealing small packets of
coca paste, a different drug from the marihuana package. [@2] The search of
pavilion 6 recovered coca paste, marihuana and various prohibited objects. [@3]
Alongside the drugs, officers seized electrical cables, light bulbs, handmade
pipes and a gram scale, which INPE said could have served small-scale drug
dealing inside the prison. [@1] Bottles of toxic substances including lacquer
and terokal and other banned material were found hidden in rubbish bins around
the pavilion. [@2]

During a body check in the prison yard, officers found a micro SD adapter and a
32 GB card on an inmate, devices banned in prisons. [@1] The inmate was a
25-year-old Venezuelan serving a 35-year sentence for sexual assault. [@1] INPE
notified the on-duty prosecutor's office in Castilla and the Tacalá police
station so that investigations could begin. [@1] The Public Ministry ordered
evidence to be lifted and said it would continue inquiries into possible links
with internal small-scale distribution networks. [@1]
