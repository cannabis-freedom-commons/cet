Four men were arrested in a joint Dominican operation against two boats. [@2]
Three Dominicans and one Venezuelan were arrested during the two operations.
[@3] The boats were intercepted off the coast of Pedernales province in the
southwest of the Dominican Republic. [@3] Two men were arrested aboard the first
boat, which held 314 packages of cocaine and 89 of marijuana. [@2] A Dominican
and a Venezuelan were arrested aboard the second boat with 836 packages of
marijuana and 56 of cocaine. [@2]

The two boats carried 925 packages of suspected marijuana and 370 of suspected
cocaine. [@3] All 1,295 packages were sent to the national forensic institute to
determine their type and exact weight. [@2] The DNCD carried out the operation
with the navy, air force, state intelligence bodies and prosecutors, with
support from the US DEA and JIATFS. [@2]

The DNCD and the public prosecutor opened an investigation into whether the two
seizures were linked to the same international criminal structure. [@1] One
account reported two detainees, a Venezuelan and a Dominican, in the boat
interceptions. [@1]
