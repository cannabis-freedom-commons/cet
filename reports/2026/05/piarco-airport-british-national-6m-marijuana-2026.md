Customs and Excise Division officers arrested a 22-year-old British national at
Piarco International Airport on Sunday 17 May 2026 after finding marijuana
concealed in luggage. [@1] Customs officers referred the passenger to the Red
Line for a closer examination of the luggage during routine clearance
operations. [@1] Officers found a quantity of plant-like material hidden in the
baggage, called in the Preventive Branch, and field tests confirmed the
substance was marijuana. [@1] The Customs and Excise Division put the street
value of the seizure at TT$5,996,295.80. [@2]

The matter was being pursued under the Customs Act, Chapter 78:01, and charges
relating to the importation of prohibited goods were expected to follow. [@1]
Customs said investigations were continuing with partner agencies within the
National Security Network. [@1] The division said the interception reflected
intelligence-led border enforcement aimed at preventing the importation of
illegal narcotics. [@1] The British national remained in custody pending further
investigations and the formal laying of charges. [@3]
