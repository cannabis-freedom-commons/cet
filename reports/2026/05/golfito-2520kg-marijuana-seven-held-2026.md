Costa Rica's National Coast Guard intercepted a boat about 255 kilometres off
Golfito in Puntarenas province on Sunday. [@1] The boat was brought to port with
its seven crew: five Costa Ricans, one Colombian and one Nicaraguan. [@3]

Officers found 2,520 kilograms of marijuana and 10 kilograms of cocaine aboard,
according to the authorities. [@1] The coast guard carried out the operation
jointly with United States authorities. [@2] The Ministry of Public Security
announced the seizures on Monday 11 May. [@2]

The interception was the larger of two operations that weekend, which together
removed 4,784 kilograms of cocaine and marijuana and led to nine detentions.
[@1] Authorities say Costa Rica has become a collection and redistribution
centre for South American drug shipments sent on to the United States and
Europe, mainly in containers. [@1] In March Costa Rica joined the regional
'Escudo de las Américas' initiative promoted by President Donald Trump to combat
drug cartels. [@1]
