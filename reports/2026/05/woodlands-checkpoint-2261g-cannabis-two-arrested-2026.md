Two Malaysians were arrested at Woodlands Checkpoint on 4 May 2026 for allegedly
trying to smuggle drugs into Singapore. [@1] The Immigration and Checkpoints
Authority and the Central Narcotics Bureau identified them as a 29-year-old
Malaysian woman and a 26-year-old Malaysian man who arrived in a
Malaysia-registered car. [@3] ICA officers referred the car for enhanced checks
after information from the agency’s Integrated Targeting Centre. [@4] Officers
found a backpack belonging to the pair in the boot of the car, with a plastic
bag and two black bundles believed to contain controlled drugs hidden by a pair
of jeans. [@3]

The joint release said the plastic bag held about 261 g of Ice, or
methamphetamine, and the black bundles about 2,261 g of cannabis. [@2] The
authorities estimated the seized drugs at more than S$71,000 and said they could
feed the addiction of about 470 abusers for a week. [@1] Under the Misuse of
Drugs Act, importing more than 500 g of cannabis or more than 250 g of
methamphetamine into Singapore may carry the death penalty. [@2]

The agencies said investigations were ongoing and reported no charge. [@3]
