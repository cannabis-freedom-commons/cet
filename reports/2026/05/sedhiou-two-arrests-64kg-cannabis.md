Police in Sédhiou, Senegal, arrested two men on 20 May 2026 and seized 64
kilograms of chanvre indien (cannabis), according to the Sûreté urbaine of the
city's central police station. [@1] The pair were stopped at the village of
Mandina Findifé in the commune of Diendé while riding a TVS motorcycle. [@2] A
second account identifies them as B. Candé, 45, a seller of maïga (a type of
sausage), and O. Q. Quade, 39, a scrap metal dealer. [@3] That account describes
both men as Bissau-Guinean nationals. [@3]

A search of the motorcycle's luggage found the cannabis, Seneweb reports. [@3]
Brief questioning led the two men to confess to the offences they were accused
of. [@1] They said they had bought the drugs from an unidentified man in Balla
Bassens, in the north-Sindian area of Bignona department. [@1] They told
investigators the shipment was to be taken onwards to Guinea-Bissau. [@1]
Seneweb says the men were taken before the public prosecutor at the Sédhiou High
Court and remanded in custody. [@3] Police opened an investigation to identify
the presumed supplier and possible accomplices. [@1]
