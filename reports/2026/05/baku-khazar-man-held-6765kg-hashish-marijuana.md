Officers of the Khazar District Police Department detained 31-year-old Syanan
Mukhtarov in Baku on suspicion of unlawful drug trafficking. [@1] The detention
took place in the Khazar district during operational measures conducted by the
district police. [@1] The Ministry of Internal Affairs reported the operation to
1news.az. [@1]

During a search of the detainee, officers discovered and seized a combined total
of 6 kilograms 765 grams of hashish and marijuana. [@1] The ministry described
the result as more than 6 kilograms of narcotic substances taken out of
circulation in the Khazar district. [@1] The seizure total names hashish and
marijuana together, without a separate weight for each substance. [@1] Police
established that the detainee had acquired the narcotics for the purpose of
sale. [@1]

A criminal case was opened on the fact of the alleged trafficking. [@1] A court
chose arrest as the measure of restraint for S. Mukhtarov, placing him in
custody. [@1] The case was reported as a detention in Baku involving a large
batch of drugs. [@1]
