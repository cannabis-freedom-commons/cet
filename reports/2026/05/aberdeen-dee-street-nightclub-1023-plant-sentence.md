Albanians Thodlhori Llambro, 31, Sandri Hodaj, 36, and Amarildo Gockaj, 36,
admitted producing cannabis at Dee Street in Aberdeen in August 2025. [@1]
Aberdeen Sheriff Court jailed each of the three men for two years. [@1] Two of
the men were found in growing rooms emptying pots of soil and the third was
asleep, and they appeared to be living in the building. [@1] Gockaj had come to
the United Kingdom illegally by small boat hoping for work to help pay his
mother's medical bills. [@1] Hodaj had been in debt to those who transported him
to the United Kingdom in a lorry on a ferry. [@1] Llambro had been used as cheap
labour by those behind the cannabis operation. [@1]

Police went to the building after receiving intelligence that it was being used
to grow cannabis. [@1] Officers noticed a strong smell of cannabis and a
constant droning noise consistent with fans used in production. [@1] Officers
found a large-scale cultivation spread over two floors. [@1] The grow was inside
the former Pearl Lounge nightclub building on Dee Street. [@1] Prosecutors said
1,023 cannabis plants were found, with a maximum value of £859,320. [@1] Police
Scotland said officers attended the Dee Street premises on 22 August 2025. [@2]
Police initially estimated the Dee Street cultivation's street value at
£1,304,000. [@2] Four men aged 30, 35, 35 and 40 were arrested and charged over
the Dee Street find and were due at Aberdeen Sheriff Court on 25 August 2025.
[@2]

Sheriff Morag McLaughlin said only a custodial sentence was possible. [@1] She
described the grow as a very substantial commercial operation. [@1] She said the
sentence would have been three years but for the guilty pleas, which reduced it
to two years. [@1]
