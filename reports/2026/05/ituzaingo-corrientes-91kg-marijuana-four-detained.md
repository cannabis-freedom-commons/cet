Four people were detained in the operation. [@2] The four were three men and a
woman loading bundles into a pickup when patrol officers approached and they
tried to flee. [@2] The chase ended on Ruta Nacional 120 at kilometre 1.458, in
the sector called Loro Cuarto. [@1]

Officers found 335 wrappers containing cannabis buds in the vehicle's boot. [@2]
The cargo weighed more than 91 kilograms of cannabis buds. [@1] Officers also
seized the pickup, three mobile phones and cash. [@2] Authorities valued all the
seized items at almost 339 million pesos. [@2]

The operation began after an anonymous call to nautical emergency line 106
reported suspicious movements on the Paraná riverbank at kilometre 1.462, in an
area known as Chancharía. [@2] The case is handled by the Federal Prosecutor's
Office of Corrientes, led by Melina Beatriz Perborell with assistant prosecutor
Juan Martín García. [@2] The four detained were placed at the disposal of the
federal justice. [@3]
