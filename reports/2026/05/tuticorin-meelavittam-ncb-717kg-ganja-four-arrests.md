The Narcotics Control Bureau's Chennai zonal unit seized 717 kg of ganja from a
container truck on the Madurai-Tuticorin national highway near Meelavittam in
Tuticorin. [@1] The Tamil-language account dated the vehicle check to 1 May
2026. [@2] That account said officers arrested four people who had been in the
lorry, from the Thoothukudi and Ilayangudi areas. [@2] The Times of India named
three of them, S Manikandan, K Siva Perumal and S Krishnamoorthy, and said they
had prior murder convictions and pending NDPS Act cases. [@1] Police News Plus
likewise reported that four accused had been arrested. [@3] The bureau described
the trafficking network as having suspected links to Sri Lanka without giving
details of them. [@1]

The NCB valued the ganja at about 3.58 crore rupees. [@3] Officers found the
ganja concealed in a false ceiling and specially designed hidden cavities. [@1]
The Tamil report said the container itself held nothing and that officers
removed a tin sheet fitted at the top inside the lorry, finding the ganja in
bundles. [@2] Police News Plus identified the vehicle as a Bharat Benz container
truck. [@3]

The Times of India said the consignment came from Odisha and passed through
Andhra Pradesh, specifically Anakapalli, before being routed into Tamil Nadu.
[@1] ETV Bharat instead reported that the 717 kg had been brought to Tamil Nadu
from Paderu in Visakhapatnam district, Andhra Pradesh. [@2] The Times of India
said the network's mastermind and the lorry owner were from Ratnagiri in
Maharashtra and that the load was meant for a receiver in Tuticorin. [@1] The
bureau said officers were still identifying other members of the syndicate and
tracing the money behind it. [@1]

The NCB said its Chennai unit had seized 2,427.35 kg of ganja, 87.64 kg of
hashish oil and 1.045 kg of amphetamine in 2026, made 14 arrests and seized
drugs worth about 18.75 crore rupees. [@1]
