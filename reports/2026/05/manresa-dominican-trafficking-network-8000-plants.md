Spanish National Police officers arrested six people in Manresa, near Barcelona,
in two raids and searches in early May 2026. [@1] Among those arrested was the
network's main leader in Spain, who was remanded in pretrial detention. [@4]

In one of the properties searched, officers found an indoor marijuana plantation
of more than 8,000 plants at different stages of growth and flowering. [@1] The
plantation had lighting and ventilation equipment for intensive cannabis
cultivation. [@4] Officers also seized €325 in cash and three vehicles, and
froze nine bank accounts belonging to the main suspect. [@1]

The operation broke up a network accused of bringing Dominican citizens into the
Schengen Area with Colombian documents obtained fraudulently. [@1] The
investigation began in August 2025 from information supplied by Ameripol's
centre on human trafficking. [@3] The network charged each migrant about €5,000
for the documents and the journey. [@3] Police said the investigation clarified
nearly 30 migration episodes linked to the network. [@2]
