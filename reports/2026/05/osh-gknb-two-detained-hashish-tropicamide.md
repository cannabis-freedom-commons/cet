The State Committee for National Security of Kyrgyzstan detained two Kyrgyz
citizens in Osh on 12 May 2026 while they were selling narcotics. [@1] The two
were identified only as K. M. M., born in 1964, and K. B. M., born in 1993, and
were caught in the act during the sale. [@2] The committee said it had shut down
a branched sales network in Osh Region that had been selling hashish and the
potent substance tropicamide. [@1] The network was described as an active
channel for hashish and tropicamide across Osh Region. [@2]

The case was opened under part 1 of Article 282 of the Criminal Code, on illegal
manufacture of narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances and their analogues for
sale. [@2] Both detainees were placed in the committee's remand centre, where
they remained in custody. [@1] The committee said investigative and operational
work was continuing. [@3]
