Portugal's Judicial Police (PJ) detained two men and a woman suspected of drug
trafficking on Flores island in the Azores. [@1] The detainees were a
32-year-old couple and a 35-year-old man, caught in flagrante delicto during the
operation. [@1] The operation was run through the PJ's Department of Criminal
Investigation of the Azores, which described the three as strongly suspected of
narcotics trafficking. [@1]

The investigation began after the National Republican Guard (GNR) flagged a
suspicious parcel during an inspection of a freight forwarder on Flores. [@1]
Inside the goods received by the suspects, inspectors found about 8.3 kilograms
of hashish. [@1] The PJ said the quantity was enough to prepare about 42,000
individual doses. [@1]

The PJ described it as the largest hashish seizure ever made in the Western
Group of the Azores archipelago. [@1] The three detainees were to be brought
before the competent judicial authorities for coercive measures to be imposed.
