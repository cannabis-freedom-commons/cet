Two adult men were detained and five other people were imputed under Argentina's
Narcotics Law after Río Negro police broke up a drug distribution network. [@2]
The operation, named Acción Unificada contra el Delito (AUDI), was carried out
on 30 May 2026 after a 13-month investigation. [@1] Nine homes were searched at
the same time, eight in Allen and one in Bariloche. [@2] The operation used
about 120 officers from special police units. [@2]

Officers seized about 1.1 kg of marijuana and about 1.6 kg of cocaine in the
searches. [@2] The marijuana was packed in airtight bags coated with grease to
make it harder to detect during transport. [@1] Police said the organisation was
dedicated to transporting, distributing and selling drugs within the province.
[@3] Investigators said the network moved drugs from the Alto Valle to Bariloche
for distribution and sale. [@2]

Officers also seized 2,486,250 pesos, 16,400 dollars and 205 euros in cash, as
well as phones, precision scales and money-counting machines. [@1] They also
seized three firearms and three vehicles believed to have been used in the
operation. [@1] The case stayed with the federal justice system, which continued
analysing the seized material. [@1]
