Salvadoran authorities captured a man in flagrante after catching him allegedly
about to transport a considerable portion of marijuana valued at nearly two
thousand dollars. [@1] The detainee was identified as Franklin Enrique R. I.,
who was carrying a marijuana package valued at $1,941.50. [@1] The intervention
took place during a vehicle inspection in which canine teams detected the
presence of illicit substances. [@1] Security and Justice Minister Gustavo
Villatoro announced the case on Sunday and highlighted the work of the Division
Antinarcotics K9 dogs in detecting the drugs. [@1]

Villatoro said the case would be remitted to the corresponding judicial
authorities and the suspect would have to answer for illicit trafficking as part
of the institutional commitment against drug trafficking nationwide. [@1] The
arrest prompted judicial proceedings against the suspect after specialized
anti-narcotics teams identified the prohibited-substance transport during the
vehicle inspection. [@1]

Authorities have not confirmed the exact spot where the drugs were seized, while
Villatoro warned that the country would not allow drugs to keep staining society
and that officers would pursue offenders. [@1] The minister said the K9 actions
form part of a comprehensive strategy to curb the criminal economy, protect the
population from the influence of drugs, and maintain zero tolerance toward these
crimes. [@1]
