The DGSS intercepted a vehicle in Libreville on 9 May 2026 containing a large
quantity of narcotics. [@1] The investigation was continuing to identify and
arrest the driver and accomplices. [@1]

Citing Gabon 24, the account says the search of the vehicle uncovered an arsenal
of drugs with a market value of several tens of millions of CFA francs. [@1] The
cannabis portion was 23 bags valued at more than 13.8 million FCFA. [@1] Skunk
accounted for 16 bundles and 25 balls totalling about 13 million FCFA. [@1] Two
balls of OG, described as a potent cannabis variant, were valued at nearly 30
million FCFA. [@1] Cocaine, crack and cannabis sweets were also seized. [@1] The
outlet put the total value of the seizure at 57 million FCFA. [@1]

Investigators presented the seizure as a sign of heightened vigilance against
criminal networks, saying Gabon would never be a favoured drug destination. [@1]
