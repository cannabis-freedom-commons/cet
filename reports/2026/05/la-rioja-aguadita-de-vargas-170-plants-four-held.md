Argentina's federal police carried out simultaneous raids on a rural property at
Aguadita de Vargas, a few kilometres outside La Rioja's urban area. [@1] Four
people were detained and placed at the disposal of the federal justice, which is
handling the case through the intervening judge and secretary. [@1] The second
account described all four detainees as Argentine adults. [@2]

Officers seized about 7 kilograms of marijuana in the form of buds and about 170
plants at different stages of development. [@1] According to the portal Nueva
Rioja, officers also seized 21 firearms and a significant quantity of
ammunition. [@1] Three vehicles were also seized, which investigators said were
used to distribute the drug at different points across the province. [@1]

The case began with an anonymous complaint that led the federal justice to open
an investigation and order the searches. [@1] The rural property was guarded and
used both to produce and process marijuana destined for sale in the provincial
capital. [@1] El Sol reported that the site served as the group's base for
cultivation, production and processing, with the drug distributed around the
capital. [@3] The four detainees and the seized material were left at the
disposal of the federal court for infringement of the drug law. [@2] Police said
further people could be charged after examining the seized phones and electronic
devices. [@1] Officers examined the weapons and vehicles to see whether they
were linked to other open cases in the province or neighbouring jurisdictions.
[@1]
