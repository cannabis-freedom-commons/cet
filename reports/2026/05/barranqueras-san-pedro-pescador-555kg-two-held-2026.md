Prefectura Naval Argentina, Argentina's coast guard, seized cannabis in Chaco
under Plan Paraná and detained two adult men. [@1] The operation took place in
the San Pedro Pescador neighbourhood on the Paraná riverbank at kilometre 1206.
[@1] Criminal-intelligence officers from Prefectura Barranqueras spotted a
Toyota Hilux speeding along the riverbank. [@1] The Toyota Hilux had no licence
plate. [@4] On intercepting the truck, agents saw rectangular bundles in the
cargo bed and rear seat consistent with packages of marijuana. [@1] With support
from other Prefectura units and local police, officers identified and detained
the two occupants, both adult Argentine citizens. [@1]

A narcotest confirmed the transported substance was Cannabis Sativa. [@1] At
Prefectura Barranqueras, officers counted 164 packs of cannabis buds weighing
168.643 kilograms and 343 compressed cannabis blocks weighing 386.776 kilograms.
[@1] The two loads totalled 555.419 kilograms, which authorities valued at
1,957,851,975 pesos. [@1] Authorities put the total value of everything seized
at 1,998,161,975 pesos. [@1] The Toyota Hilux was valued at about 40 million
pesos and had an active theft report in Córdoba province. [@1] Officers also
took two mobile phones and 10,000 pesos in cash. [@3]

The Federal Court of Resistencia, presided by judge Zunilda Niremperger, ordered
a deeper search of the vehicle and those involved. [@1] The Federal Prosecutor's
Office of Resistencia, led by Marcelo Burella, ordered the truck and detainees
taken to Prefectura Barranqueras for the load to be opened, counted, weighed and
valued. [@1] The drugs were placed in judicial custody at Prefectura while
investigators sought the load's origin and destination. [@1] Both occupants
remained in Chaco police custody at the disposal of the federal courts. [@2] The
two men were held incommunicado for an initial 72 hours while federal
investigators examined possible links to drug-trafficking organisations in the
region. [@1]
