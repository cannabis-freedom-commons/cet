Gendarmes intercepted the landing of two yoles from Saint Lucia at Anse Poirier
in Rivière-Pilote during the night of 14 to 15 May 2026. [@1] Officers arrested
four people, one Martinican and three Saint Lucians in an irregular situation.
[@1] The four were held in custody in an inquiry entrusted to the Marin research
brigade. [@1] Presented for immediate trial on Monday 18 May before the
Fort-de-France judicial court, the accused obtained a postponement to prepare
their defence. [@1] They were remanded to the Ducos detention centre pending
judgment set for Friday 10 July 2026. [@1]

On the beach officers seized an unregistered yole, a handgun with 58 rounds of 9
mm ammunition, and cash in euros and Caribbean dollars. [@1] The drug seizure
totalled 3.5 kg of cannabis herb, alongside 1.5 kg of cocaine, cannabis resin
and synthetic cannabinoid. [@1] This was the first time officers had intercepted
synthetic cannabinoids on a landing. [@1]

The interception followed intelligence from the Armed Forces in the Antilles.
[@1] The interception was the 41st in 15 months under the Scotopelia coastal
surveillance operation. [@1]
