Customs officers at Roland-Garros airport in La Réunion arrested two British
women arriving from Thailand on 15 May 2026. [@2] The two women were placed in
police custody and referred to the Saint-Denis court on 19 May for an immediate
appearance. [@3] One of them, Crystal, is a 19-year-old London resident now held
at Domenjod prison in Saint-Denis. [@1] She had arrived on a flight from Bangkok
via Chennai, India, with the cannabis in heat-sealed plastic bags. [@1]

Officers found 15 packages containing a total of 17 kilograms of cannabis herb
in the luggage. [@2] They also seized 5,000 pounds sterling in cash. [@2] The
cannabis was Thai skunk, a variety sought for its quality and high THC content.
[@1]

The Saint-Denis correctional court sentenced both women to two years in prison.
[@1] The judgment also imposed a customs fine of 220,400 euros. [@1] Sixteen
travellers had been stopped on the Thailand–La Réunion route since October 2025,
each carrying between 15 and 19 kilograms, and the island was used as a stopover
before Europe. [@1]
