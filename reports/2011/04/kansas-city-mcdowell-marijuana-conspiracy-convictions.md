A federal jury in Kansas City, Kansas, convicted eight defendants on 28 April
2011 of belonging to a drug trafficking organization that prosecutors said did
more than $20 million worth of business distributing marijuana in metropolitan
Kansas City. [@1] The trial began on 8 April in U.S. District Court in Kansas
City, Kansas. [@1] Prosecutors presented evidence that Gladstone McDowell, 60,
led the organization, which had outposts in Phoenix, Las Vegas, Los Angeles,
both Kansas Cities, Nashville, Paterson, N.J., and Pittsburgh, as well as other
cities in Florida and Jamaica. [@1] Gladstone McDowell was found guilty of
conspiracy to distribute and possess with intent to distribute more than 1,000
kilograms of marijuana, engaging in a continuing criminal enterprise, conspiracy
to commit money laundering, money laundering and attempted possession with
intent to distribute more than 50 kilograms of marijuana. [@1] Dwight Rhone, 40,
was convicted of one count of conspiracy to distribute and possess with intent
to distribute more than 1,000 kilograms of marijuana. [@1] Delroy Bucklely was
convicted of money laundering and distributing marijuana. [@1] Co-defendant
Michelle Midgyet was acquitted. [@1]

The traffickers acquired marijuana from suppliers in Arizona and shipped it to
cells across the country through parcel services, using the same services to
send bulk currency back to Phoenix. [@1] Prosecutors said the organization
laundered money through members' bank accounts in the United States and Jamaica.
[@1] Investigators identified Global Cable Company as a business used to launder
drug funds after tracing large Bank of America transfers in July 2007. [@1] In
May 2007 five members of the operation were arrested in Maricopa County,
Arizona, where investigators seized more than $200,000 in cash and more than 600
pounds (about 272 kg) of marijuana. [@1] Further seizures included more than
$139,000 hidden in a vehicle in Oklahoma in November 2007, more than $366,000
from boxes at a UPS store in Las Vegas in February 2008 and $150,000 from three
FedEx packages in Marietta, California, in May 2008. [@1]

Curtis Pitter had pleaded guilty and was awaiting sentencing, with other
defendants given sentencing dates through July. [@1] Sentencing for the eight
convicted defendants was set for 12 September. [@1] U.S. Attorney Barry Grissom
commended the Drug Enforcement Administration, the Internal Revenue Service, the
U.S. Postal Inspection Service and the Kansas City, Missouri, Police Department
for their work on the case. [@1]
