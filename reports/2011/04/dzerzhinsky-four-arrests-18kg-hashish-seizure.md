Officers of the Moscow region branch of Russia's Federal Drug Control Service
broke up an organised group selling drugs wholesale in Dzerzhinsky, the
service's regional press office told Interfax. [@1] Several people were
arrested, including an active member of the group, a Tajik citizen born in 1979
who was in Russia without registration, was not employed, and had a common-law
wife and child. [@1] Three of his accomplices, Russian citizens, were also
detained. [@1]

About 3 kg of heroin and 18 kg of hashish were taken out of illegal circulation
in the operation, the press office said. [@1]

A criminal case was opened, and investigators were establishing how the drugs
reached Moscow Oblast and identifying other members of the group. [@1]
