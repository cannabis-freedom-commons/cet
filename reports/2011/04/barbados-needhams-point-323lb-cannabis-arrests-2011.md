The men, Carson Aaron Thomas, 29, of Clare Valley, Rodney Dennis Deroche, 26, of
Questelles and Samuel Alphonso James, 39, of Park Hill, were charged with
importing 323 pounds of marijuana to Barbados on April 18. [@1] The men were
allegedly apprehended in Barbados territorial waters. [@1] On Monday April 18,
2011, about 4.40pm, an operation carried out by the Drug Squad, and assisted by
members of the Barbados Coast Guard and the Regional Security System, led to the
seizure 10 polyphene bags and 1 package of cannabis, weighing 323 lbs, valued
approximately $650,000. [@2] This seizure occurred some 8 nautical miles off
Needhams Point, Christ Church. [@2] One boat was also seized. [@2]

They were also charged with the possession of the drug, and having a trafficable
quantity of the drug and possession with intent to supply the drug, which was
estimated to have a street value of BDS $646,000. [@1] The men were not required
to plead to the charges. [@1] They were remanded to Her Majestyâs Prison Dodds
by Acting Magistrate Manila Renee, after prosecutor Sergeant Wendell Greenidge
objected to bail, citing the seriousness of the charges and the menâs
nationality. [@1] Three Vincentian men were expected to return to a Barbadian
court on May 18 to answer charges of marijuana possession. [@1] One of the men,

more than 19 years and had status there, which prompted the magistrate to
