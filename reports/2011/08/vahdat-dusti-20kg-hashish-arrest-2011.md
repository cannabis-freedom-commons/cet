Officers of Tajikistan's Drug Control Agency detained 22-year-old I.N. and
placed him in the agency's temporary detention facility. [@1] He was the nephew
of the 29-year-old owner of a house in the Dusti jamoat of Vahdat district and
lived in that house. [@1] He had no prior convictions and was temporarily
unemployed. [@1] The agency's investigation department opened a criminal case
against him under article 200 part 4 of the Penal Code for especially
large-scale drug trafficking for sale. [@1]

Acting on operational information that drugs were stored in the house, officers
searched it with a service dog named Richi and found a plastic bag of pressed
dark substance inside an iron chest buried in the hayloft. [@1] The agency's
forensic laboratory identified the substance as hashish weighing about 20
kilograms. [@1] The state news agency Khovar likewise reported that the agency's
officers seized about 20 kg of drugs in the house. [@3] The English-language
account dates the discovery in the house to 1 August 2011. [@2]

Investigators said he had bought the drug from a citizen of Afghanistan and kept
it hidden for some time. [@1] He planned to move it by vehicle through the
border district of Jirgital into Kyrgyzstan. [@1] Vahdat district lies about 20
kilometres east of Dushanbe. [@2]
