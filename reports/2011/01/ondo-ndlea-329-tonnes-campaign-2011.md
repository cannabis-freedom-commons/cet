NDLEA state commandant Walter Nicholas told journalists in Akure on 27 January
2011 that his command had seized 32.9 tonnes of Indian hemp worth 3 billion
naira in the previous eight months. [@1] He said the command had seized 6.7
tonnes of cannabis in January alone and destroyed 357 hectares of hemp
plantation on identified farms. [@1] At least 18 people charged over drug
offences had been convicted, and Nicholas said the command had lost two
officers to growers' activities in the past year. [@1]

Nicholas said the command was working with the police, the State Security
Service and the civil defence corps, and that he had visited rulers including
the Olowo of Owo, the Alayede of Ogbese and the Deji of Akure to press them
not to give land to hemp growers. [@1] The command had also opened a
counselling and rehabilitation centre. [@1]
