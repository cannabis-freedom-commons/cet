Three Vietnamese nationals arrested in a raid on a cannabis factory at Melville House on the Hurdsfield industrial estate were jailed after admitting production of a class B drug at Chester Crown Court on 17 August. [@1] Anh Cong Hoai, 19, Minh Viet Dinh, 34, and Cong Khac Nguyen, 18, each received two years in prison, and all three faced deportation after serving their sentences. [@1]

Officers had found the factory, holding 2,803 cannabis plants with a potential street value of £868,571, when they raided the Queen's Avenue building in September 2010 and detained five Vietnamese men trying to escape through the roof, calling in a police helicopter and fire engines. [@1] Mac Van Do, 34, and a 16-year-old detained with them denied the charge and were found not guilty after a three-day trial at the same court, telling the jury they had paid agents for passage to Britain and believed the gardening work would be ordinary farm labour. [@1]
