The Interior Ministry told Asia-Plus that officers detained 45-year-old A.I., a
resident of Khatlon's administrative centre, on the Sarband-Kurgan-Tyube road on
Tuesday, 10 May 2011. [@1] During a personal search, officers found and seized a
packet containing a dark plasticine-like substance with a distinctive smell.
[@1] A criminal case was opened over the seizure and an investigation was under
way. [@1]

An expert examination identified the seized substance as hashish weighing 5 kg
284 grams. [@1] The account described the seizure as a large consignment of
Afghan hashish recovered in southern Tajikistan. [@1] The account cited UN
drug-and-crime experts as forecasting increased output of cannabis-group drugs
in Afghanistan, which it said produces 90% of all drugs, above all Afghan
hashish. [@1] Tajik anti-drug officials said smuggling of that drug would
therefore grow along all routes, including the northern route for which
Tajikistan is one of the transit countries. [@1]

First-quarter statistics cited in the same account said 1 tonne 210 kg of almost
1.5 tonnes seized by Tajik agencies were cannabis-group drugs, namely hashish
and marijuana. [@1]
