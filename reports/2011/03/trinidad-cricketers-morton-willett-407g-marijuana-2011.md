Regional cricketers Runako Morton and Tonito Willett were charged with
possession of marijuana by Morvant police in March 2011. [@1] Morton was playing
for Trinidad and Tobago and Willett for the Leeward Islands in a regional
first-class match at Queen's Park Oval when they were arrested. [@1] Both men
were from Nevis. [@2] North Eastern Division Task Force officers stopped the car
they were in on Lady Young Road, Morvant, early on Saturday 12 March. [@4] They
were charged alongside two women, aged 37 and 28. [@4]

Police alleged that they were found with 407 grams of marijuana, which was given
an estimated street value of TT$4,070. [@4] Both men were absent from the rest
of the match, and the teams decided they would take no further part in it. [@1]
Team manager Omar Khan said Morton had broken the team's 11.30 pm curfew and was
now on his own. [@1] They spent a night in the Remand Yard prison when they
could not post bail, and were released on the Tuesday, 15 March. [@3] Both
pleaded not guilty at the Port of Spain Tenth Court on 17 March, and the case
was adjourned to 17 June. [@3]

The West Indies Players Association posted bail and appointed a lawyer for both
players. [@5] On 5 February 2014 the Director of Public Prosecutions
discontinued the proceedings against Willett, and a notice of discontinuance was
signed and filed with the court. [@5] Morton, a former West Indies batsman, had
died by the time of the 2014 hearing. [@6] Willett, who had always proclaimed
his innocence, said the dropping of the charges was a tremendous relief. [@5]
His lawyer told the court that the case had been delayed by the frequent absence
of the arresting officer at hearings. [@5]
