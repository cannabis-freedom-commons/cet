An 18-year-old youth was detained on 23 March 2011 inside the Municipal Park of
São Tomé das Letras. [@1] Police found a portion of marijuana on the youth when
he was detained in the park. [@1]

A check in the police computer system showed existing complaints alleging that
the youth sold weapons and ammunition. [@1] A police team then went to his house
and searched it with his mother's authorization. [@1] Officers seized seven
calibre 28 rounds during the house search. [@1]
