Law-enforcement bodies carried out a special operation in northern Tajikistan
from 25 October to 3 November 2011, seizing about 39 kg of drugs. [@1] Following
the operation, police detained B.M., a 45-year-old resident of Nurabad district
who was temporarily living in Chkalovsk and had previously been convicted for
drug trafficking. [@1] On 25 October, near the Prints shop in Chkalovsk, he
handed a controlled buyer a plastic bag containing nine packages of a pressed
brown substance with a distinctive smell, weighing more than 5 kg. [@1]

An expert examination identified the substance as hashish. [@1] The next day, at
the same place, he handed the controlled buyer almost 11 kg of hashish. [@1] On
3 November, B.M. handed the controlled buyer two plastic bags containing 42
packages of hashish weighing 22 kg 718 g near the same shop. [@1] He was
detained after that transaction. [@1]

Police opened a criminal case against B.M. under Article 200, part 4 of
Tajikistan's Criminal Code for illegal trafficking of narcotics, psychotropic
substances or precursors with intent to sell, and the investigation was
continuing. [@1] The regional drug-control agency said law-enforcement bodies in
northern Tajikistan had seized more than 531 kg of drugs in the first ten months
of 2011, including 131 kg of heroin. [@1]
