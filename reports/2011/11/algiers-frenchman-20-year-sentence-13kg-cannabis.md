Sami Dekhil was 24. [@1] He is a young man from Besançon. [@1] The criminal
court in Algiers sentenced him to 20 years in prison for cannabis trafficking.
[@1] The court tried him for exporting narcotics to France. [@2] He had been
arrested in 2010 at the port of Algiers while about to travel to Marseille. [@2]

Officers found 13 kilograms of carefully wrapped cannabis hidden in his car's
fuel tank during the customs check. [@1] Prosecutors said he admitted during
questioning that a man in France, identified only by a first name, had offered
him 10,000 euros to obtain a large quantity of cannabis in Algeria. [@2] He
first refused to speak, then admitted knowing about the unlawful transport
before retracting confessions made under particularly harsh conditions. [@1] His
mother said he does not speak Arabic yet signed his statement in Arabic after
seven days in custody without a translator or a lawyer. [@1]

He stood trial alone with no co-defendants and appealed the verdict. [@1] His
mother enlisted a Besançon lawyer to alert the French authorities and seek at
least a transfer to France if his guilt were confirmed. [@1]
