Eleven men, mostly from the West Midlands, were convicted after a five-month
Staffordshire Police investigation into what officers believed was being set
up as one of the country's largest cannabis factories. [@2] They were found
guilty by a jury at Stafford Crown Court in the week of 11 November 2011.
[@1]

Police had raided the farm at Castle Bytham, Lincolnshire, in 2010 and found
more than 800 plants alongside 1,600 empty buckets prepared for cultivation.
[@1,2] The gang had spent about £1.4m adapting huge barns with generators,
fans, lighting and ventilation for an operation police said could yield about
£2.75m of cannabis a year. [@1,2]

Desmond Bayliss, 40, a former nightclub doorman from Toxteth who had acted in
the Channel 4 drama-documentary Shooters, was found working at the farm during
the raid and was convicted alongside the farm's owner, Iain Brown, 60, of
Chichester, and nine others. [@1] All eleven were to be sentenced at a later
date. [@1] A Staffordshire Police detective said the group had criminal links
across the country and that officers had seized significant amounts of cash
for action under proceeds-of-crime law. [@1]
