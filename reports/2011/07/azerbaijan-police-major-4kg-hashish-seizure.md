In Azerbaijan, a seizure of more than 4 kilograms of hashish resin, described as
a narcotic drug, was taken from a serving police major and his accomplices in
the same enforcement action. [@1] The serving officer was identified as Police
Major Rafael Mirzoyev, whose reported post was inquirer in the Dashsalahly
police department in Gazakh district, linking him to that district police
structure. [@1] The accomplice named alongside him in the same seizure was Vahid
Abdullayev, who was described as a resident of Syaryak village in Astara
district. [@1]

The precise quantity reported as taken from Mirzoyev and Abdullayev in the
seizure was 4 kg 125 g of resin, equivalent to 4.125 kilograms of hashish resin.
[@1] The seized material was identified as hashish resin constituting a narcotic
drug, with the rounded total given as more than 4 kilograms before the precise
weight was stated. [@1]

The enforcement action is recorded here with an incident date of 26 July 2011,
reflecting the date of the seizure account. [@1]
