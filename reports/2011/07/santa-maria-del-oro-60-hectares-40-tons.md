Soldiers on patrol found 23 marijuana fields covering 60 hectares near San José de Ramos, in the
municipality of El Oro, Durango, on 23 July 2011. [@2] The fields lay in Mexico's "golden
triangle" region, and the patrol also found 40 metric tons of harvested marijuana, a processing
lab and five camps apparently used by workers or guards. [@1,2]

## Valuation

The Defence Department said the planted and harvested drugs together could have amounted to 100
tons of marijuana worth 1.55 billion pesos, about US$133 million. [@1] The find came almost two
weeks after soldiers uncovered a 120-hectare plantation in Baja California, described at the time
as the largest ever found in Mexico; that earlier operation is a separate event. [@1]
