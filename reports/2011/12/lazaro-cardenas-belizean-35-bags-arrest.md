In the municipality of Cardenas in Quintana Roo, Mexico, Belizean national Luis
Herlindo Leyva was arrested by Mexican police after allegedly trafficking 35
packages of marijuana. [@1] The second account places the bust in the city of
Lazaro Cardenas in the state of Quintana Roo and describes Leyva as a well-known
Belizean drug dealer. [@2] Leyva was arrested a few minutes after he allegedly
sold two small plastic bags of weed to Mexican national Eulogio Coba Cupul in
the Colony of Miguel Borge Martin. [@1] The second account likewise says Leyva
was arrested just minutes after selling two bags of marijuana to Eulogio Coba
Cupul. [@2]

Leyva was allegedly found with a shipment of 35 bags of marijuana, blood
pressure pills, packaging bags, paper to produce cigarettes, a cell phone and
cash. [@1] The second account lists the same seizure as 35 bags of marijuana,
pressure pills, packaging bags, cigarette wrapping paper, a cell phone and an
undisclosed amount of cash. [@2] Cupul told police he was only a user, not a
trafficker, and that he had bought each plastic bag of marijuana for 50 pesos
from Leyva. [@1]

Leyva had so far been dealt with only by the local jurisdiction's Attorney
General Ministry. [@1] He was expected to be brought before the federal
jurisdiction in Cancun on the drug trafficking charge. [@1] The second account
says Leyva had a prior Mexican arrest record for drug peddling and had served a
short sentence before, and that this time he was headed for the federal prison
in Cancun. [@2] Leyva, who is married to a Mexican national, was known locally
in Mexico as "The Belizean". [@1]
