Hundreds of kilograms of hashish were seized by security officials after an
hour-long firefight with traffickers in eastern Nangarhar province on Tuesday,
14 June 2011. [@1] The 1,000 kilograms of charas, a local name for hashish, were
loaded on 12 mules and packed in 30 sacks for smuggling to Pakistan. [@1] Police
intercepted them in the Gorgorai area of Haska Mena district. [@1] The first
border police zone commander, Brig. Gen. Aminullah Amarkhel, told a news
conference in Jalalabad about the seizure. [@1]

Police escaped unhurt from the clash, but blood spots at the site indicated the
traffickers had suffered casualties, the official said. [@1] The official added
that the men wanted to smuggle the drugs to Pakistan for processing through the
Spin Ghar area. [@1] The commander said they had received prior reports that
narcotics were being smuggled into the neighbouring country. [@1] Smugglers
often succeeded in their attempts because border police lacked helicopters to
chase them, the commander said. [@1]
