Anti-narcotics police in Rafah governorate seized nine rolls of banjo, weighing
nine kilograms, beside a tunnel on the Palestinian-Egyptian border during an
inspection tour. [@1] Police said the owner of the border banjo had not been
found and search and investigation for him continued. [@2]

In a separate house search, officers arrested a hashish dealer caught with 20
small pieces ready for distribution and held the material. [@1] In another house
search, officers seized a man identified only as M.A. with rolls of banjo and
referred the case to complete the legal proceedings. [@1]

The Interior Ministry account confirms the hashish pieces were seized during a
search and the accused was arrested with the case referred for legal completion.
[@2]
