Police in Philipsburg arrested three women as they attempted to smuggle
marijuana into the Pointe Blanche Prison facility on Sunday. [@1] Police
spokesman Ricardo Henson identified the three suspects as E.D.C. from the
Dominican Republic and S.U.N.G. and K.J.P. from St. Maarten. [@1] Two of the
women were described as being from St. Maarten, while the third was described as
being from the Dominican Republic. [@1]

The commander on duty told detectives who reached the scene that the three women
had come to the prison to visit prisoners. [@1] The women were found to have
marijuana in their possession when they were searched at the prison. [@1]
Detectives found three separate packs of marijuana on the women and confiscated
them. [@1]

The three women were arrested and held pending further investigation. [@1] The
arrests took place at the Pointe Blanche Prison facility where the women had
arrived as visitors. [@1] The police account gave the suspects names as
initials. [@1]
