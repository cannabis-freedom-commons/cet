Nine military police officers went to Ilha do Aracapa in Oroco after an
anonymous tip that the island was being used to grow cannabis. [@1] The
eradication happened on Thursday 2 October 2014 on the island as part of the
Military Police Operation Crack. [@1] The operation began in the early hours of
the morning. [@1] No one was arrested, but officers found signs that people had
been tending the crop. [@1] On the island officers found ranchos, makeshift
growers camps, with pots, mats and meat laid out to dry, according to Lieutenant
Wander Savio de Sa of the 2nd Company. [@1] Police said the growers had probably
fled when the team arrived and that officers used a boat to reach the island.
[@1]

The military police account counted 11,400 pits totalling about 34,200 cannabis
plants. [@2] G1 rounded the seizure to almost 35,000 cannabis plants. [@1]
Police also found three sacks of processed cannabis weighing 30 kilograms at the
site. [@1] Officers incinerated the plantation and sent the seized drug to the
Civil Police station. [@1] The lieutenant said cannabis produced in the region
was historically distributed to northeastern state capitals. [@1]
