A January 2014 account published by Alwihda Info reported that 73 kilograms of
cannabis had been seized in an ambulance, giving no calendar date, no location
and no agency for the operation. [@1] The material was described only as
cannabis, without saying whether it was herb or resin, and the account named no
ambulance, owner, driver or officer involved. [@1] It placed the ambulance
seizure about two weeks before a separate incident in which two kilograms of
cocaine were seized at N'Djamena airport. [@1] The account said agents of the
Ministry of Public Health were believed to be involved in drug trafficking. [@1]

The ambulance seizure was cited in a list of several trafficking episodes used
to accuse the Ministry of Public Health of sheltering a vast network of drug
traffickers. [@1] No person was arrested in connection with the ambulance
seizure, and no charge, conviction, sentence or other court outcome was
reported. [@1]
