Gendarmes of the Taravao brigade, with a dog-handling team, searched five sites
at Vairao and Toahotu on the morning of 31 January 2014. [@1] Six suspects were
identified and questioned by the Taravao gendarmes, and all were summoned to
appear in court. [@1] La 1ère reported that six people were detained by the
gendarmes and would be heard by the courts. [@2]

The gendarmerie gave the plant, stick and branch counts as the total for the
operation across all five sites. [@1] Officers seized and destroyed 1,440
cannabis plants, 500 of which reached two metres in height. [@1] They also
seized 27 sticks and 45 dried branches of cannabis. [@1] La 1ère put the haul at
more than 1,400 plants found across five sites between Vairao and Toahotu. [@2]

Cannabis is known locally in French Polynesia as paka, the term the gendarmerie
used in its Tahitian account of the operation on the peninsula, Tahiti iti. [@1]
