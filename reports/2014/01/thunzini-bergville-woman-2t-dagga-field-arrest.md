The Upper Tugela Borderline Control unit discovered a field of dagga during a
crime-prevention operation in the Emmaus area of Bergville, KwaZulu-Natal. [@1]
Police arrested a woman at her Thunzini homestead for allegedly cultivating the
field of dagga. [@1] The suspect is 44 years old and has been charged with
cultivating dagga. [@1]

KwaZulu-Natal police spokesperson Captain Thulani Zwane said the plantation was
estimated to yield about two tonnes of dagga, which would be worth about R500
000 when dried. [@1] Provincial commissioner Lieutenant-General Mmamonnye
Ngobeni praised the officers, saying the arrest and the seizure of dagga plants
was another successful stride in fighting drug use and the illicit drug trade.
[@1]

The mountainous areas along the Lesotho border are described as notorious for
dagga cultivation and smuggling, with Basotho smugglers said to use donkey
trains to bring crops down mountain passes into South Africa for sale. [@1] Some
of those smugglers are rumoured to also be involved in livestock thefts on the
South Africa-Lesotho border. [@1]
