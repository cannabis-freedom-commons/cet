Police arrested ten people on the night of 15 November 2012 during a special
operation on Hondaidhoo island, searching it with a court warrant and
reporting large amounts of suspected drugs and alcohol. [@1] Those held
included Jumhooree Party MP Abdulla Jabir, Maldivian Democratic Party MP
Hamid Abdul Ghafoor, former SAARC secretary general Ibrahim Hussain Zaki,
former press secretary Mohamed Zuhair with his wife Mariam Faiz, resort
manager Jadhulla Jaleel, Zaki's son Hamdan Zaki, two Sri Lankans and a
Bangladeshi. [@1] A police sub-inspector told Haveeru the suspects were held
with alcohol and hash oil, and all but Hamdan Zaki were later released by the
Kulhudhufushi magistrate court. [@1]

Seven suspects including both MPs were charged, and on 20 February 2014 the
Criminal Court sentenced Jabir to twelve months under the Drug Act for
refusing to provide a urine sample for a drug test. [@2,3] A week later the
same court acquitted Jabir of possessing cannabis on the island, ruling there
was not enough evidence that he held cannabis when detained. [@3] Jabir
remained in Male jail on the refusal conviction and was still facing the
alcohol trial linked to the raid. [@3]

## Pardon

In July 2014 President Abdulla Yameen pardoned Jabir under the Clemency Act
while he served the one-year term. [@4] By then Jabir had run for his
Kaashidhoo seat from jail and lost, spent three months in Malaysia on medical
release, and been re-jailed on 10 July, the day he and Hamid were acquitted
of the alcohol charges. [@4]
