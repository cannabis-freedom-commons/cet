Senegalese police seized 96 kg of Indian hemp (chanvre indien, cannabis) at
Dakar's 'Mali' railway station on the evening of Wednesday 26 February 2014,
calling it a record haul. [@1] The national police public relations office said
the consignment was 48 carefully packaged blocks of two kilograms each. [@1]
Transrail, the company operating the Bamako–Dakar railway, had its security
agents telephone the Bel Air district police station around 22:00 to report
drugs in a wagon of train 6214 arriving from Bamako. [@2]

The drugs were found while the train ran on the Thiès–Dakar section near Mbao.
[@2] The carriers waited for the train to reach a slowdown zone to try to unload
the consignment, but Transrail security agents spotted them and they fled,
leaving the drugs. [@1] Police said the owners of the drugs were unknown to the
railway security staff and remained at large. [@1]

The drugs were taken to the Bel Air district police station and sealed, and
Transrail employees were questioned on official record; an investigation was
opened. [@1] A police communiqué distributed to PANA described the 96 kg as
Indian hemp seized in a freight train arriving from Bamako. [@3]
