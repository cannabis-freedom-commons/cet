The seizure was made in Bodoyel, a village in sector 61 of Médina Wandifa in the
Sédhiou region of Senegal, about ten kilometres from the Gambian border. [@1]
Soldiers of sector 61 based at Médina Wandifa in the Sédhiou region carried out
the seizure. [@2] It took place on Saturday 8 February 2014. [@2] They took five
sacks of Indian hemp that the commander said weighed 100 kilograms each, or 500
kilograms in total. [@1] Koita described the motorcycle as a Korbon Best
registered TC 7044A. [@1]

The officer was Lieutenant Colonel Boubacar Koita, commander of sector 61 and
the 26e Bataillon de Reconnaissance et d’Appui, the 26th reconnaissance and
support battalion. [@1] He said the trafficker was still at large and that an
earlier, smaller seizure had already been made in the area. [@1] Koita called
that earlier haul proof that trafficking of the herb was intense in the zone.
[@1] He appealed to local people to collaborate more with the armed forces to
instil fear among those he called bandits. [@1]
