Gardaí from Kildare and Newbridge carried out a planned search of a house in School Lane, Suncroft, at about 9pm on 20 February 2014 and found a grow house with cannabis plants at various stages of growth. [@1,2] The crop had an estimated street value of up to €164,000, pending analysis. [@1]

Two males, aged 27 and 29, were arrested at the scene and held at Newbridge Garda Station while the scene was preserved for technical examination. [@1,2] Both were charged on the evening of 21 February and put before a special sitting of Naas District Court the next morning. [@1]
