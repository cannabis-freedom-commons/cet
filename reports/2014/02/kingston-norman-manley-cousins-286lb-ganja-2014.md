Detectives from the Transnational Crime and Narcotics Division arrested four men
and a woman for breaches under the Dangerous Drugs Act on 27 February and 3
March 2014. [@1] Forty-eight-year-old Gilbert Cousins of Hamstead in St
Catherine was arrested on 27 February 2014 after attempting to leave Norman
Manley International Airport in Kingston for Curacao with 2.86 pounds of ganja
he had ingested. [@1]

Police reported that the 2.86 pounds, about 1.30 kg, was ganja Cousins had
ingested. [@1] On the same day, 24-year-old Kemar Taylor of 19 Miles, Sandy Bay
in Clarendon was arrested after arriving at the airport on a flight from
Curacao. [@1] After being interviewed by the TCND, Taylor was taken to Kingston
Public Hospital where Taylor expelled 32 pellets containing cocaine weighing
half-a-pound. [@1]

On 3 March detectives took three men into custody after they attempted to enter
the island with cocaine. [@1] The three men had arrived on a flight that
originated in Curacao and expelled a total of 174 cocaine pellets weighing
approximately 3.47 pounds at the hospital. [@1] Police said all the accused
individuals would be placed before the Half Way Tree Resident Magistrate’s Court
the next week. [@1]

Stabroek News republished the same Jamaica Observer account of Cousins
attempting to leave the Norman Manley International Airport headed for Curacao
with ingested ganja. [@2]
