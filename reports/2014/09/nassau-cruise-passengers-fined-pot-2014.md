Bahamian police arrested four young men in their early 20s from several cruise
ships in Nassau for small amounts of marijuana. [@1] The first two men were from
Georgia and had disembarked from an unidentified cruise ship. [@1] Police
arrested the two Georgians on the cruise wharf with one gram of marijuana
between them. [@1]

The magistrate told the two men they could have faced up to seven years in Her
Majesty’s Prison. [@1] He fined each of the two men $300 to be paid by 5 p.m. or
they would spend a month in prison. [@1] A passenger from Miami was arrested
aboard Royal Caribbean’s Majesty of the Seas for possessing three grams of
marijuana after customs officers boarded the ship. [@1] The magistrate
threatened that passenger with sitting in a prison cell for seven years and
fined him $400 or two months in jail. [@1]

Another young man from Miami was arrested aboard Norwegian Cruise Line’s
Norwegian Sky after the ship’s security chief invited Bahamian police aboard and
led them to his cabin, where officers found 12 joints. [@1] The magistrate fined
the Norwegian Sky passenger $500 or two months in prison. [@1]
