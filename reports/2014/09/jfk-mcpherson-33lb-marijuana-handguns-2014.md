A 24-year-old woman was arrested at Kennedy International Airport on Sunday
morning with two handguns, ammunition and 33 pounds of marijuana concealed in
her luggage. [@1] She was Nyesha McPherson, of Scarborough, Ontario, travelling
on a Canadian passport with a ticket to Barbados, and was taken into custody at
about 6:30 a.m. at Terminal 5. [@1] Port Authority police said the arrest was
made without incident and McPherson remained in custody. [@2]

Transportation Security Administration officers first became suspicious when
McPherson's checked luggage set off an alarm on a baggage-screening machine.
[@1] When an agent picked up the bags they seemed strangely heavy, according to
the authorities. [@1] The bags were stuffed with boxes and cans labelled as baby
wipes, coffee, floor-dusting sheets, lemonade mix, iced tea mix and cat litter.
[@1] The T.S.A. said none of those boxes or cans contained the products shown on
their labels. [@1] The T.S.A. statement said officers detected two disassembled
.40-caliber handguns, 350 rounds of ammunition, four magazines and 58 bricks of
marijuana totaling 33 pounds (about 14.97 kg), concealed in the boxes, tubs and
cans. [@1] The New York Post put the weight at about 38 pounds in two suitcases.
[@3]

The agents alerted Port Authority police, who found McPherson in a passenger
screening area and took her into custody. [@1] She was charged with first-degree
criminal possession of marijuana, third-degree criminal possession of a weapon,
criminal possession of a firearm, possession of high-capacity magazines and
possession of ammunition. [@1] The New York Post said she was due to be
arraigned in Queens Criminal Court. [@3] McPherson was awaiting arraignment on
Monday afternoon and it was unclear whether she had a lawyer. [@1]
