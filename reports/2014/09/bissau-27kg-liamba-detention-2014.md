The Judicial Police of Guinea-Bissau detained a young Guinean man in possession
of 27 kilograms of liamba, cannabis herb. [@2] Folha 8 reported that the man was
held that week in the capital, with the herb packed in bales and displayed on 26
September 2014. [@1] The deputy director of the Judicial Police said the young
man was from the south of the country and was living in Bissau. [@2] Folha 8
said that, besides dealing in liamba, the same man was taking cocaine toward a
residential area. [@1]

On 26 September 2014 the Judicial Police presented about two kilograms of
cocaine and 27 kilograms of liamba, which ANG said had been seized on 21
September at Bissau International Airport. [@2] ANG said that at the same time
police detained the young man with the 27 kilograms of liamba. [@2] On the night
of 23 September 2014, after a plane landed at Bissau International Airport,
police found three men, two Guinean and one Angolan of Guinean origin, who had
each swallowed a little more than 80 cocaine capsules. [@2] Folha 8 said the
Judicial Police that day showed more than one kilogram of cocaine taken from
three young people who had entered through Bissau airport on Tuesday, one of
whom had swallowed 29 plastic capsules while the other two carried about 80 each
on their bodies. [@1]

Folha 8 said the three were subject to preventive detention. [@1]
