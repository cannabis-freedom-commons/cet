On Saturday 7 June 2014, customs officers at the Koumpentoum control post
stopped a Mali-registered truck bound for Dakar for the usual search. [@2] The
vehicle looked empty, and it was around 16:00 when it stopped at the post for
the usual inspection. [@1] After a glance at its two empty containers, the
officers moved to the driver's cab. [@2] They found 40 slabs of yamba, 80 kg of
top-quality Indian hemp (chanvre indien, meaning cannabis), beside the driver's
seat. [@2] The driver and his apprentice ran off, but residents helped the
officers catch them, the driver about 500 metres away and the apprentice about
100 metres away. [@2] Both were handed to the Tambacounda prosecutor the same
day. [@1]

Customs officers in Senegal are known as "soldats de l'économie", soldiers of
the economy. [@1] The arrest renewed debate about the customs service's shortage
of staff and equipment; without the residents' help, the two men would have
escaped, Dakaractu reported. [@2]
