Police seized about 42.7 tonnes of cannabis off Sicily aboard a cargo ship
flying the Togolese flag. [@1] All 15 crew members were handed over to the
Italian judicial authorities. [@2] The 110-metre ship, the Aberdeen, carried a
Lebanese supervisory crew and Indian and Syrian sailors. [@2]

The cargo was 42,672 kilograms (42.672 tonnes) of cannabis resin, which
Sauvalère said was the equivalent of two full semi-trailers. [@2] He put its
semi-wholesale resale value at about 120 million euros. [@2] The interception
happened on Monday 23 June 2014 off Sicily. [@3]

The ship was stopped between Sicily and Tunisia and towed to the Sicilian port
of Trapani. [@2] The resin was believed to originate in Morocco, and the ship
was due to call at an eastern Mediterranean port before the drugs reached
western Europe. [@2] The operation was based on information supplied by French
customs. [@1]
