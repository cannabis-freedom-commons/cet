A resident of Baie-Mahault aged in his forties was arrested on Monday and placed
in police custody. [@1] His custody ended on Thursday and he was released. [@1]
He was to be summoned before the criminal court in April for detention and
consumption of narcotics. [@1]

Seventy-three cannabis plants were found at his home. [@1] The 73 cannabis
plants were visible from the road. [@1]
