Eight people were detained after Panama's National Border Service (Senafront)
seized more than half a tonne of drugs in Darién province. [@1] Frank Ábrego was
the director general of Senafront at the time. [@1] He made the announcement at
a press conference on Tuesday 9 December 2014. [@1] He said the operations were
carried out in distinct communities. [@1] Puerto Obaldía and Yaviza were among
the communities named. [@1] The eight detainees were Panamanian nationals. [@2]
They allegedly carried out the offence with the collaboration of foreigners.
[@2]

Official reports recorded the seizure of 4,848 packages of cocaine and 360
packages of marijuana. [@1] Telemetro reported that Ábrego announced 599
packages of cocaine found inside a boat in a sector of Darién. [@2] Three plates
of cocaine were also seized from a Panamanian tourist in Colombia. [@2]
