Luxembourg police arrested three people on suspicion of using marijuana on
Saturday evening after finding 49 grams of the drug in the car. [@1] The plants
were found in the home of one of the three suspects. [@1] Several cannabis
plants were found at that property. [@1]

The mother of the suspect whose home was searched said the plants were kept
there because of their fragrance. [@1] Her son had been arrested on suspicion of
possessing marijuana. [@1] Police were not satisfied with the explanation and
seized all of the plants. [@1]
