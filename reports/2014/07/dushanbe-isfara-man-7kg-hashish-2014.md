Officers of the internal affairs department for Dushanbe's Firdavsi district
detained 40-year-old Qosimkhon Qodirov, a resident of the northern city of
Isfara in Sughd region, during special operations. [@1] Asia-Plus reported that
Qodirov was detained in Dushanbe on suspicion of drug pushing, citing the
Interior Ministry. [@3]

Officers searching his car found a batch of hashish weighing over 7 kg hidden
under the driver's seat and confiscated it, according to the Interior Ministry's
press centre. [@1] A second account identifies the car as an unregistered
Hyundai SVR driven by Qodirov, with slightly more than 7 kg of hashish found
under the driver's seat. [@2] That account dates the detention to 14 July and
gives Qodirov's birth year as 1974, consistent with the reported age of 40. [@2]

A criminal case was opened over the seizure and an investigation was under way,
with no court outcome reported. [@1]
