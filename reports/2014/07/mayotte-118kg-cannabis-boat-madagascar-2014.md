Mayotte gendarmes intercepted a boat arriving from Madagascar and seized 118.5
kg of cannabis. [@1] The island's authorities described it as one of the largest
seizures they had made since the start of the year. [@1] Two presumed smugglers
among the passengers and a third man involved in the trafficking were placed in
pre-trial detention at Majicavo. [@1] They were held at the Maison d'arrêt de
Majicavo after being referred to the examining magistrate and the judge for
liberty and custody. [@2] After 48 hours in police custody the case was referred
to the prosecutor, who opened a judicial investigation. [@1]

A radar echo had alerted the Mayotte gendarmerie in the night of Thursday 24
July to a vessel entering the island's territorial waters from Madagascar. [@1]
The interception took place in the night of Thursday 24 July. [@2] The vessel,
which resembled a pleasure boat more than a kwassa, was intercepted by the
helicopter of the aerial section, whose crew had noticed undocumented migrants
on board. [@1] Fifteen people were aboard, and the gendarmerie seized a large
package containing 118.5 kg of cannabis. [@1]

The 13 undocumented migrants, who were not connected to the trafficking, were
returned to the border after a health check. [@1] The gendarmerie said the
previous trafficking interception had been in October 2013, when 160 kg of
cannabis was seized. [@1]
