Seven people accused of running a drug-trafficking ring from inside a prison in
Salvador, Bahia, were arrested in an operation by the state civil police's
narcotics department, Denarc. [@1,2] Police said the group operated in the Paripe
and Periperi neighbourhoods of the city's railway suburb. [@1,2] The gang was
allegedly led by Joseilton de Jesus Moura, known as Jó, who was already held at
the Mata Escura prison complex and was not among those arrested. [@1,2] Officers
seized two firearms, including a .40 pistol restricted to military use, and five
kilograms of marijuana, cocaine and crack in total. [@1,2]

Five of the detainees were caught at a property in São Tomé de Paripe, where the
drugs and a .38 revolver were hidden. [@1,2] Police said one of them, known as
Camarão, was carrying the seized pistol. [@1] The five were booked in the act
for illegal possession of a firearm, drug trafficking and association with drug
trafficking, and were to be sent to the prison system. [@1,2]

All seven had their arrests ordered by the 2nd Specialist Drugs Court, the 2ª
Vara Privativa de Tóxicos. [@1,2] Operation Orla II involved 100 civil police
officers. [@1] Police were still investigating the group's involvement in
drug-related killings in the suburb. [@1,2] The detainees were presented by the
heads of Denarc and the 3rd Homicide Division on the morning of Monday 28 July
2014. [@1]
