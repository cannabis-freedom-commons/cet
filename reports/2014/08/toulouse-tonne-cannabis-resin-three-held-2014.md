Three Frenchmen, two in their twenties and one aged 69, were arrested on Friday
8 August 2014 at a hotel in the suburbs of Toulouse. [@1] The three couriers
were brought before the Paris investigating judge handling the case on an arrest
warrant, indicted and jailed. [@2] The investigation was run by the Office
central pour la répression du trafic illégal de stupéfiants (OCRTIS), France's
central office against illegal drug trafficking. [@1]

They were carrying 370 kg of cannabis from Spain in a go-fast, a convoy of two
scout cars and a drug-carrying vehicle, with the resin hidden in caches built
into a pick-up. [@1] Police said about one tonne of cannabis resin had been
seized in the case. [@1] French police officers based in Malaga traced the
traffic to its source in Morocco and seized nearly 400 kg of cannabis. [@1] A
second branch of the network led investigators to seize nearly 300 kg of resin
in Belgium in a separate operation. [@2] Several people were arrested in the
Spanish and Belgian operations, without a total being given. [@2]
