Spanish Customs Surveillance intercepted the merchant vessel Mayak in the Alboran Sea south-east of Málaga in the early hours of Sunday 16 March 2014 and found bales of the type commonly used to transport hashish, estimated at about 12,000 kilos. [@1] Air and sea surveillance assets of the Customs Surveillance Service and French Customs had flagged the vessel for unusual manoeuvres in an area where a vessel of that type is not usually found, and the patrol vessel Fulmar boarded it with the flag state's authorisation. [@1]

All eight crew members, of Syrian nationality, were arrested, and the vessel and its cargo were seized for transfer to the port of Málaga and handed to the competent court. [@1] The agency presented the seizure, under Operation Oriente, as the sixth large hashish interception on the eastern Mediterranean route since the previous June, bringing that route's running total past 75 tonnes. [@1] Its 2014 annual report lists the Mayak boarding among the year's key Customs Surveillance actions. [@2]
