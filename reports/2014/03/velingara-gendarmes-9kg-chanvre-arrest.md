Gendarmes in Vélingara arrested Saliou Ba, a 25-year-old man from Guinea-Bissau,
for possession of Indian hemp, the local name for cannabis. [@1] He had left
Guinea-Bissau for Dakar in order to sell the cannabis. [@1] The gendarmes seized
9 kilograms of Indian hemp. [@1]

At Diaobé he boarded a bus after packing the drug carefully in his suitcase.
[@1] Apprentices on the bus smelled the yamba coming from the suitcase and told
the driver, who went on as if nothing had happened. [@1] He was not stopped from
Diaobé through Kounkané to Saré Coly Sallé. [@1]

At the entrance to Vélingara the driver stopped beside the gendarmes on duty and
told them about a suspicious suitcase, and the officers checked the passengers'
luggage. [@1] The smell of cannabis drew the officers' attention. [@1]
Questioned as the owner of the suitcase, Ba had no time to deny the find, and he
was taken by force by the men of Adjudant-Chef Diallo. [@1]

He was held in custody at the Vélingara gendarmerie brigade and was to be
referred to the prosecutor's office in Kolda. [@1]
