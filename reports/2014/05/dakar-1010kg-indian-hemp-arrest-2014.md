Senegalese police arrested a Malian man living in Bamako during an Ocrtis
seizure near Dakar on Monday 12 May 2014, according to the state news agency.
[@1] The man told officers that he had imported the drug from Mali. [@1] He was
placed in police custody on suspicion of possession and international
trafficking of Indian hemp, the agency said. [@1] The seizure was made near
Dakar by Ocrtis agents on the Monday. [@1]

Ocrtis, the Office central de répression du trafic illicite de stupéfiants, is
the anti-drug section of the Senegalese police. [@1] The drug was described as
chanvre indien, or Indian hemp. [@1] Ocrtis said the Indian hemp had been
meticulously concealed inside the sisal bags. [@1] The 21 sisal bags together
weighed one tonne ten kilograms, which is 1,010 kg, according to figures Ocrtis
gave to the Senegalese Press Agency. [@1]

The Senegalese Press Agency, a public news agency, said the arrest and seizure
took place earlier in the same week. [@1]
