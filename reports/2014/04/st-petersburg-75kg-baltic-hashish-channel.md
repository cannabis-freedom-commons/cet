St Petersburg drug-control officers seized about 75 kg of hashish that had
arrived in the city from the Baltic states. [@1] Drug police identified a group
that transported large consignments of the drug from the Baltic states. [@1]

Four members of the group were caught red-handed at an auto service at 4
Marshala Blyukhera while removing 40 kg of the drug from a specially equipped
cache under a car's boot. [@1] Police established that the group kept several
rented flats as warehouses holding hashish in the city's Kirovsky and
Krasnoselsky districts. [@1] Searches of those flats recovered about 35 kg more
of the banned substance. [@1]

The criminal group comprised three Russian citizens and one Ukrainian citizen.
[@1] According to operational information, the group sold hashish across Russia
at a rate that could have reached up to 200 kg per month. [@1] A criminal case
was opened under part 4 of article 228.1 of the Russian Criminal Code, and those
detained were placed under arrest. [@1]
