Six Algerian traffickers aged 30 to 45 were arrested in Oran. [@1] Police chief
Salah Nouasri told AFP that one of the traffickers was considered the baron.
[@1]

Algerian police seized eight tonnes of cannabis resin brought from Morocco in
Oran on a Thursday. [@1] Nouasri said the quantity seized was a record for
Algeria's second-largest city. [@1] The haul was more than eight tonnes of
cannabis intended for sale in Europe and the Middle East. [@2] Police also
seized seven vehicles and 15 mobile phones at a warehouse in Sid Benyebka, about
30 kilometres east of Oran. [@1]

The network was dismantled after a two-month investigation, and Nouasri said the
drugs came from the western border with Morocco. [@3] Nouasri said the
traffickers were supplied through the country's western borders and meant to
send the goods to the south, then Libya, the Middle East and Europe. [@1]
