Five men were charged on 10 April 2014 over two bales of compressed marijuana
found aboard a vessel at the Port Zante marina the previous afternoon. [@1]
Police named them as Wasim Richards of Ramsbury, Nevis; Damion Jeffers of Pond’s
Pasture, St. Kitts; Jermaine Pemberton of New Castle, Nevis; Kemroy Simmonds of
Hermitage Village, Nevis; and Robert Gregory Roberts of Stoney Grove, Nevis.
[@1] They were formally arrested and charged with possession of cannabis and
possession of cannabis with intent to supply, offences dated to 9 April 2014.
[@1]

At about 1:30 p.m. on 9 April 2014, officers acting on intelligence found the
two bales aboard a vessel moored at the marina. [@1] Police had received
information that a vehicle had arrived at the marina and dropped off a package
on the vessel. [@1] The driver of the vehicle was unable to leave the area after
the delivery and was intercepted and arrested. [@1] The operation was conducted
by the Royal St. Christopher and Nevis Police Force, the St. Kitts-Nevis Customs
and Excise Department and the K9 Unit. [@2] A number of people were taken into
custody for questioning on the day of the discovery. [@2]

Police did not disclose how much the bales weighed, their street value or who
owned the vessel. [@1] Commissioner of Police Celvin ‘GG’ Walwyn thanked
everyone involved in the operation and urged the public to keep reporting
suspicious activity to the authorities. [@1]
