Three registered sex offenders were arrested for marijuana possession during
CNMI Sex Offender Task Force compliance checks on Rota, Saipan and Tinian,
according to the Department of Public Safety. [@1] Angel Palacios, 45, was
arrested and charged with possession of an illegal controlled substance after
officers found two marijuana plants in plain sight at his Dandan residence on
April 17. [@1] On April 19, Rota officers arrested 69-year-old Crispin Taitano
at his residence in Songsong, where DPS said he possessed 111 marijuana plants.
[@1] Later that afternoon, Rota officers arrested Stephen Albright, 47, after
six marijuana plants were found growing in his Sinapalo residence. [@1] Taitano
and Albright were charged with possession of an illegal controlled substance.
[@1]

The task force began the registered sex offender compliance checks for the three
islands on April 17. [@1] The four-day operation located and verified 30
offenders on Saipan, nine on Tinian and six on Rota, DPS said. [@1] The checks
are carried out quarterly to confirm the offenders' locations, verify compliance
and obtain updated photos. [@1] DPS said all plants discovered during the
compliance checks were seized and taken as evidence. [@1]
