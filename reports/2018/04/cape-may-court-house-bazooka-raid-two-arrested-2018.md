On 27 April 2018 members of the Middle Township Police Department Street Crimes
Unit executed a court-authorized search warrant with the Cape May County SWAT
Team and the Cape May County Prosecutor's Office. [@2] They arrested the two men
who lived at the residence, Craig Byrd and Matthew Lindholm, both 40. [@2] The
house was at 3 Lacivita Drive in Cape May Court House. [@4]

Inside, officers found about three pounds of marijuana, 20 grams of cocaine and
more than $12,500 in cash. [@1] The cash was later given as $12,521, and
officers also seized a .45 caliber handgun, two shotguns, a military bazooka and
drug paraphernalia. [@2] Police believe Lindholm took the bazooka from Iraq
while he was in the military. [@1] Officials said they did not believe he had
permission from the U.S. military to bring it home. [@1]

Byrd was charged with possession of cocaine, possession of cocaine with intent
to distribute more than half an ounce, and possession of drug paraphernalia.
[@2] Lindholm was charged with possessing more than 50 grams of marijuana,
possession with intent to distribute more than one ounce of marijuana, handgun
and shotgun offences, possessing a handgun during a drug offence, endangering
the welfare of a child and possession of drug paraphernalia. [@3] Both men were
lodged in the Cape May County Correctional Center. [@3] Police said further
charges were pending an investigation into the bazooka's status and whether it
could fire a missile. [@3]
