The Guam Police Department announced the drug-related arrests of Julius Ike
Aguigui Nedlic and Jessie Lynn Underwood in early May 2018, saying they were
linked to the arrest of the governor's former spokesman. [@1] The Mandaña Drug
Task Force made a series of arrests the week before, including the arrest of
Enrique Harris Guerrero of Agana Heights on Thursday 26 April. [@1]

Nedlic, 33, of Mangilao, was arrested on 26 April 2018 by Superior Court
marshals on an outstanding warrant, police spokesman Sgt. Paul Tapao said. [@1]
The marshals found drugs and drug paraphernalia and called in the Mandaña Drug
Task Force. [@1] Task force officers found four grams of marijuana, two grams of
crystal methamphetamine, money, drug paraphernalia and crystalline residue. [@1]
He was charged with possession of a Schedule II controlled substance with intent
to deliver, possession of a Schedule II controlled substance and possession of
less than an ounce of marijuana. [@1]

Guerrero was arrested after investigators searched his home and car on 25 April,
according to a magistrate's complaint filed at the Superior Court of Guam that
describes firearms, marijuana, LSD, methamphetamine and a substance Guerrero
called the drug ecstasy. [@1] The complaint says Guerrero was on pre-trial
release on other drug and gun charges at the time. [@1] Guerrero told
investigators Underwood had asked him to allow packages to be delivered to his
home on her behalf, and that he received methamphetamine for his services. [@1]
Task force members obtained a search warrant for her home in the Tumon View
Condos. [@1] A woman at the condominium who identified herself as Underwood's
partner, Kala Joe Taitague, was arrested and later charged with drug offences.
[@1]

Underwood, 35, of Tumon, was arrested on Monday 30 April after drugs were found
in her home, Tapao said. [@1] Underwood was charged with possession of a
Schedule II controlled substance with intent to deliver, possession of a
Schedule I controlled substance with intent to deliver, possession of a Schedule
II controlled substance and possession of less than an ounce of marijuana. [@1]
Troy Torres, Gov. Eddie Calvo's former spokesman, was arrested on Friday 27
April on drug charges, but no charges were filed against him and the attorney
general's office said the case was under investigation. [@1] Torres said at a
press conference broadcast by KUAM that Nedlic was his roommate and Underwood
was his friend. [@1]
