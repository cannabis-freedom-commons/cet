Minsk police detained a 29-year-old soloist of a Krasnoyarsk music group after
drugs were found on him. [@1] The musician had come to Minsk with his band on a
concert tour. [@2] Officers stopped him on 23 April as he tried to enter an
event marking the founding of a Minsk football club. [@2]

The drug-control officer believed the young man was under the influence of
drugs. [@2] A search of his bag found a film canister holding two foil wraps
containing a substance. [@1] Forensic experts identified the brown pressed
substance in the wraps as hashish. [@1] He was detained and placed in a
temporary detention facility. [@3]

He admitted that he had brought the drugs with him from Russia. [@2] A criminal
case was opened against him under part 1 of Article 328 of Belarus's Criminal
Code on illegal drug trafficking. [@1] The charge carries a maximum penalty of
up to five years of imprisonment. [@1]
