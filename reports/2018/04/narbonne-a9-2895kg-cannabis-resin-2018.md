On the evening of 2 April 2018, Narbonne customs officers stopped a lorry coming
from Spain on the A9 motorway and found 2,895 kilograms of cannabis hidden in
the load. [@1] The refrigerated trailer was supposed to hold 15 tonnes of
lettuce and oranges and was heading toward Paris. [@1] Customs called the find
the largest cannabis seizure in France by the customs service since the start of
the year. [@1]

Officers took the lorry to a rest area for a mobile-scanner check, which showed
density differences in the trailer, and a sniffer dog marked the front of the
cargo. [@1] They cut a trench into the trailer and found numerous Moroccan
suitcases holding 2,895 kilograms of cannabis in all. [@1] The Public Accounts
Ministry said the suitcases held cannabis resin, found under two boxes of
lettuce and empty crates at the third pallet. [@2] The detection dog, named
Geff, marked the front of the refrigerated trailer while scanner technicians
noted the density differences. [@2]

The Spanish driver was arrested and handed to the judicial police in Perpignan
on the instructions of the Narbonne court. [@1] That court passed the file to
the specialised interregional court in Marseille. [@1] Customs also seized the
tractor, its trailer and the 15 tonnes of fruit and vegetables. [@2] Public
Accounts Minister Gérald Darmanin congratulated the Narbonne customs officers on
the morning the seizure was announced. [@1]
