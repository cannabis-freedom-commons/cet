Michael Ashby of Main Street was arrested and charged on 4 April 2018 with
unlawful possession of cannabis and several driving offences. [@1] He was
granted $5,000 bail and was due in the Magistrate’s Court on 18 May. [@1]

Carlos Emanuel Chinnery of Greenland was arrested and charged on 5 April with
unlawful possession of cannabis and a tinted-windows traffic offence. [@1] He
was granted $2,500 bail for an appearance in the Magistrate’s Court on 18 May.
[@1] Charles Rochester, 23, of Baughers Bay was arrested and charged on 6 April
with unlawful possession of cannabis and granted police bail for the same court
date. [@1] Malik Maduro of Lower Estate was arrested and charged on 7 April with
unlawful possession of cannabis. [@1]

The force said it recorded seven arrests between 1 and 7 April 2018. [@1] The
blotter also listed drug possession, traffic offences and other charges such as
using insulting language. [@1]
