Police in Mzimba arrested a father and his two sons for allegedly cultivating
chamba at their home in Nthapangwa Lunda Village. [@1] The three were Lyton
Lunda, 91, and his sons Nervason, 74, and Alufeyo, 61. [@3] They were arrested
on Thursday 19 April 2018 after a tip from a villager. [@3] They were held at
Mzimba Police Station pending a court appearance. [@2]

Police uprooted about 300 cannabis plants found in the family's garden. [@3] The
plants were taken to Lunyangwa Research Station, where they were tested and
weighed 456 kilogrammes including stems, seeds and roots. [@3] Police had first
put the weight at 500 kilogrammes. [@1]

The three pleaded guilty to cultivating Indian hemp under the Dangerous Drugs
Act and were convicted. [@3] Police prosecutor Sergeant Snowden Chimombo told
the court there was need for a stiffer punishment to serve as a lesson to other
ganja farmers. [@3] He said the first accused was a village headman who should
have acted responsibly and been exemplary to young people. [@3] First Grade
Magistrate George Longwe said the offence was very serious but noted that the
three were first offenders and elderly. [@3] The court discharged them on
condition that they commit no offence within 12 months. [@3]
