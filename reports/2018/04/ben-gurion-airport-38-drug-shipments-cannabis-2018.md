Israeli police and Customs agents intercepted 38 shipments of illegal drugs on
their way into Israel at Ben-Gurion Airport on Sunday 22 April 2018, in what was
described as the largest single-day seizure of illicit substances to take place
at the airport. [@2] The drugs were sent to Israel via international mailing
services. [@2] They were discovered during an operation by the Customs Authority
Canine Unit. [@2] The Israeli Customs Administration said its officials seized
LSD, cocaine, ecstasy and other drugs in packages mailed from Europe. [@1]

Cannabis seeds, cannabis oil and cannabis tea were also seized. [@2] The
packages seized included cocaine, MDMA, LSD and mescaline, along with various
methamphetamines and other designer drugs. [@2] The substances originated mostly
in Spain and the Netherlands. [@2]

A photograph published with the announcement shows illicit drugs seized at
Ben-Gurion Airport on 22 April 2018, the day of the operation. [@1]
