Officers of Tajikistan's State Committee for National Security (SCNS) carried
out an operation in Dushanbe that led to the seizure of 14 kilograms of Afghan
hashish. [@1] Twelve people were arrested on suspicion of being involved in drug
trafficking. [@1] Five of those arrested were residents of Dushanbe: Tojiddin
Khabirov, 33, Dilovar Sherov, 29, Nasijon Rajabov, 33, Firdavs Zevarshoyev, 39,
and Halim Ahmadbekov, 53. [@1] The security service said the 12 detained people
formed a group involved in drug trafficking. [@2]

The SCNS press center said the 14 kilograms of hashish were seized in the home
of one Dushanbe resident and had been brought from Khorog for retail sale in the
capital. [@2] A separate account said the traffickers had brought the drugs from
Afghanistan to Khorog before delivering them to Dushanbe for retail sale. [@3]

Criminal proceedings were instituted against the alleged traffickers under
Article 200 (3) of Tajikistan's Penal Code for large-scale drug trafficking, and
an investigation was under way. [@1]
