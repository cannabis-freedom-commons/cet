Brest border guards prevented a large consignment of hashish from being brought
into Belarus at the Domachevo checkpoint on 11 April 2018. [@1] A resident of
the Brest district returning from a trip abroad had chosen the green channel.
[@1] During border control, a service dog reacted to his car. [@1] Border guards
then ordered a special inspection of the car jointly with customs officers. [@1]

In the luggage compartment of his Volkswagen, the border detail and customs
officers found a cache holding 12 rectangular briquettes of plant material with
a total weight of 92 kg. [@1] Experts concluded the substance was hashish, a
dangerous narcotic drug. [@1] The drugs were seized by border service officers.
[@1]

A criminal case was opened against the man under part 2 of article 328-1 of the
Criminal Code, covering illegal movement of narcotic drugs, psychotropic
substances, their precursors or analogues across the state border of Belarus.
[@1]
