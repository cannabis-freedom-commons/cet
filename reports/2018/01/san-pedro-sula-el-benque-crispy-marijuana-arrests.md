Anti-drug police arrested Antonia Sofía Casiano Ponce, 29, and Karla Patricia
Serrano, 30, in a parking lot in barrio El Benque, San Pedro Sula. [@1] The
Directorate for the Fight Against Drug Trafficking (DLCN) carried out the
operation there the previous day after receiving information that women working
in the area distributed drugs. [@1] The raided parking lot stands on 7 Avenida
between 5 and 6 Calle. [@1]

Officers seized eight wrappers of the drug crispy, which they described as a
product of mixing cannabis and cocaine. [@1] The seizure of eight wrappers of
crispy raised alarms among the Honduran authorities. [@1] In the same action
officers also seized 15 crack rocks, eight marijuana joints, flavoured paper
strips for making such joints and 5,000 lempiras. [@1] Officers said crispy
differs from a traditional marijuana joint because it uses higher-quality herb.
[@1]

About 15 DLCN agents with drug dogs inspected the site at 11:00 a.m. and closed
it to cars. [@1] DLCN agents said consumption of crispy was increasing and in
high demand, especially among young people. [@1] Police described the place
where the drugs were seized as territory where the MS gang operates. [@1]
