Vincentians Kenrick Kevin John and Keron Andre Walters faced criminal charges in
neighbouring Barbados alongside a Barbadian co-accused. [@1] John is 25, Walters
is 32, and the Barbadian accused is 36-year-old Delon Covelle Asgill of 14th
Avenue, West Terrace, St James. [@1]

Prosecutors allege that on Sunday, January 14, all three men had cannabis while
inside the territorial waters of Barbados. [@1] The prosecution further alleges
that they possessed that cannabis with intent to supply other people. [@1] They
were also charged with trafficking cannabis within those same territorial waters
and with importing the illegal substance into Barbados. [@1] Police allege that
the trio was found with 15 polythene bags and two taped packages containing
768.24 pounds of cannabis about 11.8 nautical miles off the Cement Plant at
Checker Hall, St Lucy. [@1] That 768.24-pound amount converts to about 348.47
kilograms. [@1]

The men appeared at the Holetown Magistrate's Court on Wednesday following the
weekend allegations. [@1] Magistrate Wanda Blair remanded all three men to
prison. [@1]
