Two men and a woman were arrested and charged with ganja offences after police
seized five pounds of the drug during an operation in Boscobel, St Mary. [@1]
They were Jerome Jackson, 51, a fisherman of Boscobel; Errol Watson, 65, of
Highgate; and Rosemarie Addison, 60, a cleaning attendant of Highgate, all in St
Mary. [@1] The ganja seizure in Boscobel, St Mary, took place on a Wednesday,
and one of the three people held was a woman. [@2]

Reports from Narcotics police said that at about 7:00 am lawmen were in the area
when a shack was searched and the drug was found. [@1] The illicit drug was
found in the possession of the three people, according to the police reports.
[@1] All three persons were arrested and charged with illegal possession of
ganja and dealing in ganja. [@2] The police found five pounds of ganja during
the operation. [@2]

They were scheduled to appear before the St Mary parish court on February 15.
