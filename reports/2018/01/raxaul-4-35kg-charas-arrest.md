Acting on a tip-off, a Sashastra Seema Bal team arrested Intezar Alam near the
Indo-Nepal border in East Champaran district. [@1] Alam, a resident of the
Raxaul police station area, was described as someone who supplied drugs in the
area. [@1] After preliminary interrogation, Alam was handed over to the
Narcotics Control Bureau at Patna. [@1]

Officers recovered nine packets of charas weighing 4.35 kg from his possession.
[@1] Alam had obtained the contraband from Nepal and was travelling in an
auto-rickshaw with the nine packets when he was caught. [@1] SSB Deputy
Commandant Amit Kumar valued the seized charas at about Rs 87 lakh in the
international market. [@1]

The arrest took place on the night of 30 January 2018 after a tip-off and was
announced from Motihari on 31 January 2018. [@1] India.com carried the same PTI
account, saying one person was arrested with more than four kilogrammes of
charas worth over Rs 80 lakh. [@2]
