State prosecutors in Jalisco said officers found and destroyed a marijuana
plantation in Magdalena municipality. [@1] The plot, known as Las Barranquitas,
lies about 4.5 kilometres southeast of the Santa María delegation in Magdalena.
[@1] Officers counted about 36,000 marijuana plants about 1.10 metres tall. [@1]
The cultivated ground covered 9,200 square metres, about 0.92 hectares. [@1]

Officers cut and burned the plantation. [@1] They handed destruction records and
plant samples to the federal Public Ministry to begin an investigation. [@1] The
federal Attorney General's Office said its Jalisco delegation opened a case file
against whoever is responsible for crimes against health. [@2] The federal
account put the destruction at 36,000 plants with an average height of 1.10
metres. [@2] Representative samples were placed at the disposal of the federal
Public Ministry, which continued building the case file. [@2]
