Ricardo Bryan, a 47-year-old butler of Brittons Hill, St Michael in Barbados,
was arrested and charged on Friday 19 January 2018 after compressed ganja was
found in suitcases he had checked at Norman Manley International Airport in
Kingston. [@2] He was identified as 47-year-old Ricardo Bryan, a butler of
Brittons Hill, St Michael in Barbados, and was in police custody after a little
more than 60 pounds of marijuana was discovered in the suitcases. [@1] Police
charged him with possession of ganja, dealing in ganja and taking preparatory
steps to export ganja. [@1] He was scheduled to appear in the Kingston and St
Andrew Parish Court on Thursday. [@1]

At about 4:10 p.m. Bryan checked in to board a flight destined for Barbados.
[@1] During security screening, irregularities were discovered in a suitcase he
had checked in. [@2] Narcotics detectives intervened and an investigation
revealed that a second suitcase had also been checked in. [@1] Both suitcases
were checked and found to contain false compartments with compressed ganja
weighing approximately 60 pounds, about 27.2 kilograms. [@1]
