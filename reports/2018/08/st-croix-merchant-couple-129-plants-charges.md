Colin Merchant and Delia James-Merchant, a couple in St. Croix, were arrested on
a Friday and charged with possession of marijuana with intent to distribute.
[@1,2] They were scheduled to appear before U.S. Magistrate Judge James Cannon on
Thursday. [@1]

The arrests followed a V.I. Police officer noticing their home in Estate Grove
Place surrounded by blue tarps and apparent marijuana plants and suspecting a
grow operation, according to the arrest affidavit. [@1] The officer returned
three days later with a search warrant, and officers found and seized 107
marijuana plants in different stages of growth in pots outside the house. [@1]
Officers also seized a quantity of drying and processed marijuana from the home
and 22 marijuana plants being grown inside the house. [@1]

The couple told police they cultivated marijuana to supplement their income
because both were unemployed and for personal use. [@1,2] Colin Merchant said he
had a little over 100 plants and had started growing marijuana after the
hurricane. [@1] Delia James-Merchant said she liked to keep the number under 50
and thought there were only about 40 plants at the home. [@1] The couple told
police they believed marijuana was decriminalized in the territory and that
their religious beliefs treated it as a medicinal plant. [@1] Colin Merchant
also acknowledged that he knew marijuana was still illegal. [@1]
