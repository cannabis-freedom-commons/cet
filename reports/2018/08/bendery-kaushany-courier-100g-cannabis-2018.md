Transnistrian customs officers detained an offender at the Bendery (Kaushany)
checkpoint after a routine inspection of a vehicle and questioning of its
passengers. [@1] The man was travelling from Moldova in a Chadyr-Lunga–Tiraspol
shuttle taxi. [@1] The owner of the waist bag holding the drug packet was acting
as a drug courier. [@1] He said he had been told by phone where to collect the
parcel so he could carry it through the Transnistrian checkpoint to Tiraspol.
[@1] Further instructions on what to do with the packet and whom to pass it to
were also to arrive by phone. [@1]

Officers seized more than 100 grams of dried cannabis plants from the passenger
at the checkpoint. [@1]

Officers from the Bendery customs anti-smuggling unit and a Bendery police
investigative team documented the illegal movement of the drug across the PMR
border. [@1] The report describes the episode as an internationally planned
group trafficking run that failed at the checkpoint. [@1] The full circumstances
and the intended recipient were still to be established by the competent
authorities. [@1] Interior Ministry experts were to give the final
classification of the seized substance, after which a decision would be taken on
opening a smuggling case. [@1]
