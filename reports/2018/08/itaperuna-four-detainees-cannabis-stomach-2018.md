Four detainees from Itaperuna who had temporary release for Father's Day were
caught with drugs inside their stomachs as they returned to the Presídio
Diomedes Vinhosa Muniz. [@1] Police said the ingested drugs were detected by the
prison scanner. [@1] The incident happened on Friday the 17th and was disclosed
by the Civil Police on Sunday the 19th. [@1]

Police said one detainee was carrying 75 wraps of maconha and five of cocaine.
[@1] Another man aged 38 had swallowed 27 wraps of maconha. [@1] A 27-year-old
prisoner was caught with 20 wraps of maconha, and the fourth detainee aged 29
had ingested ten wraps of maconha and five of cocaine. [@1] The men were taken
to the emergency care unit in Itaperuna and underwent stomach washing to expel
the material. [@1]

After the procedure the four detainees returned to the prison. [@1] The case was
registered at the 140th police station in Natividade, which was the duty station
that weekend. [@1]
