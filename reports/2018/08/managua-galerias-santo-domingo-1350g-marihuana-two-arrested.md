Nicaragua's National Police arrested two men in the car park of the Galerías
Santo Domingo shopping centre in Managua after finding them carrying marihuana.
[@1] The detention was carried out on the west side of the centre's car park.
[@1] The two detainees were named as Lorenzo Delgado Gómez and Kevin Delgado
Méndez. [@1] Police described the pair as drug suppliers. [@2]

Officers said the men were transporting 1,350.28 grams of marihuana, just over
1.35 kilograms. [@1] The variety was known as 'Creepy' and described as 'hierba
de calidad', meaning quality herb. [@1] A second police account rounded the
quantity to three pounds, about 1.36 kilograms, and said the pickup truck in
which the drug was carried was seized. [@2]

The National Police's Managua delegation announced the arrests as part of a week
of operations in the capital following 460 complaints from the previous week.
[@2]
