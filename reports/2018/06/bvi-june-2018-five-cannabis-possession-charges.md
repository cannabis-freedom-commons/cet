The Royal Virgin Islands Police Force reported eight arrests between 17 and 23
June 2018. [@1] On 20 June, police arrested and charged 19-year-old Kiara
Huggins and a 14-year-old male, both of Hodges Creek, with unlawful possession
of cannabis. [@1] Police also arrested 20-year-old Bradley Bell of Frenchman's
Cay that day for the same offence. [@1]

Two days later, police arrested and charged Charles Rochester Jr of Baughers Bay
with unlawful possession of cannabis, driving when not secured by a seatbelt and
tinted windows. [@1] Rochester was granted bail for a Magistrate's Court
appearance on 3 September. [@1] On the same day police arrested and charged
Tiejie Henley of Little Hill with unlawful possession of cannabis and riding as
a passenger without a seatbelt. [@1] Henley was granted bail for a Magistrate's
Court appearance on 3 September. [@1]

The eight people in the blotter faced charges ranging from drug offences to
robbery. [@1]
