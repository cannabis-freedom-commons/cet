Police in Namibia's //Karas region arrested a 35-year-old Namibian man on
charges of drug dealing after intercepting a truck carrying dagga. [@1] Regional
police commander Rudolf Isaak said officers from the customs and excise
directorate and the police searched the truck and found the dagga. [@1] The
truck's driver was expected to appear in court on the charge on 25 June 2018,
the day before the seizure was reported. [@1]

Officers found 644 kilogrammes of dagga in the truck. [@1] Police put the drug's
estimated street value at N$644 000. [@1] The boxes in which the dagga was
hidden indicated shoes and clothes destined for the Katutura, Wanaheda and
Khomasdal suburbs of Windhoek. [@1] The truck belonged to Trans Desert Logistics
and was travelling to Windhoek from Johannesburg in South Africa. [@1]

Isaak said the seizure was the second dagga bust at the border town in less than
two months. [@1]
