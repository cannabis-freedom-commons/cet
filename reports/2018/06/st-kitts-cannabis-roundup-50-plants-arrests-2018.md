The Royal St Christopher and Nevis Police Force recorded the cases in its report
of arrests, convictions and other activities for 19 and 20 June 2018. [@1] Kevin
Gaskin of New Road, St Peters, was arrested, formally charged and cautioned for
possession of cannabis and possession with intent to supply, and bailed at
$7,000 to appear at the Basseterre Magistrate Court on 2 July 2018. [@1]
Kevaughn Browne of Salt Pond Alley, Basseterre, faced the same two charges and
the same $7,000 bail and court date. [@1] Zeron Carey of Conaree, Basseterre,
was also charged with possession of cannabis and possession with intent to
supply and bailed at $7,000. [@1] Leon Isaac of Conaree Village faced the same
two charges but was granted bail of $10,000. [@1] Enslie Lewis of Prickly Pear
Alley, Basseterre, was arrested, formally charged and cautioned for the two
offences and bailed at $7,000. [@1] Akeem Chumney of Fort Thomas Road,
Basseterre, was arrested, formally charged and cautioned for the two offences
and bailed at $7,000. [@1] Marlo Martin of Stapleton Village was charged with
possession of cannabis, possession with intent to supply and importation of
cannabis, and bailed at $7,000 for the same court date. [@1]

At the Basseterre Magistrate Court, Wasim Howe of Conaree Village pleaded guilty
to possession of cannabis and was ordered to pay $6,000 within one month or
serve six months in prison if the fine went unpaid. [@1] Anathalia Francis of
Pitcairn Street, Newtown, pleaded guilty to the same charge and was given three
months to pay $6,000, with six months in default. [@1] Both convictions were
among the matters at the Basseterre Magistrate Court, presided over by His
Honour Mr. Reynold Benjamin. [@1]

Officers of District B uprooted 50 mature marijuana plants in the Ottleys area.
