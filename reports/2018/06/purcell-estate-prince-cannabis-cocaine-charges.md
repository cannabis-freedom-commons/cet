Junior Bradford Prince of Purcell Estate was charged with two counts of unlawful
possession of a controlled drug with intent to supply. [@1] A 56-year-old man
was taken into custody on cocaine and marijuana charges. [@1] Police said Prince
was held with more than 12 grams of cocaine during a stop-and-search operation
the previous week, according to the Royal Virgin Islands Police Force. [@1] A
search warrant at his premises then seized over a kilo-and-a-half of cocaine and
a quantity of marijuana, the force's information unit said on 18 June. [@1]
Prince was scheduled to appear in the Magistrate's Court that day. [@1]
Investigations were still ongoing. [@1]

At Prince's trial the court heard that the residence search had produced
1,644.36 grams of cocaine and 21.58 grams of cannabis. [@2] Officers found four
bundles of cash bound by rubber bands at the residence, each amounting to
$1,000. [@2] Prince was charged with two counts of possession of a controlled
drug with intent to supply to another and possession of proceeds of criminal
conduct. [@2] At the stop-and-search police found 12.8 grams of cocaine in the
vehicle and $1,666 in Prince's pocket. [@2]

Prince's lawyer Stephen Daniels asked Officer Richard Francis whether police had
tested the money for cocaine or marijuana residue. [@2] The officer replied that
no such test had been carried out. [@2] Daniels also asked whether officers had
investigated Prince's salary as a Public Works Department employee. [@2] The
trial was adjourned and scheduled to resume on 8 October 2018. [@2]
