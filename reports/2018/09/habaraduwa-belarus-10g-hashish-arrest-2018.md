Police in Habaraduwa, Unawatuna, in southern Sri Lanka, arrested a citizen of
Belarus found in possession of hashish. [@2] Officers recovered 10 grams of
hashish, according to the police statement carried by Hiru News. [@2]

The Belarusian-language outlet belmir.by reported the same detention in the
southern Sri Lankan town of Habaraduwa, with the same 10-gram quantity of
hashish, attributing the information to Hiru News and the police. [@1]
