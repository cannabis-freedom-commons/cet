On 5 September 2018, Inyo National Forest staff working with sheriff's deputies, wildlife officers and federal agents eradicated 8,000 marijuana plants growing illegally on forest land in a remote area of the Inyo Mountains. [@1] Officers also seized 100 pounds of processed marijuana at the site. [@1]

US Forest Service law enforcement, the Inyo County Sheriff's Office, the Mono County District Attorney's Office and California Fish and Wildlife secured the site and destroyed the plants in coordination with the Drug Enforcement Administration. [@1] The agencies warned residents that illegal grows damage ecosystems and that anyone stumbling on a cultivation site should leave at once and report its location. [@1]
