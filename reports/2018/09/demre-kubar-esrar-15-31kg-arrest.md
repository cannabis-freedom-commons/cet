Acting on information that a man identified only by the initials Y.D. was making
a drug shipment out of Demre district, gendarmerie teams in Antalya mounted an
operation. [@1] The work was carried out jointly by the Antalya provincial
gendarmerie command and the Demre district gendarmerie command. [@1] Officers
stopped the pickup truck that the suspect was driving in the Sura area of
Karabucak neighbourhood, at the fifth kilometre of the Demre–Kaş highway. [@1]
After questioning and other procedures at the gendarmerie, Y.D. was referred to
the courthouse on the instruction of the Public Prosecutor. [@1] The charge
against him was 'drug trafficking', and after being brought before a court he
was remanded in custody. [@1]

Officers searched the pickup truck and inside it found 15 kilograms 310 grams of
kubar esrar, the cannabis named in the account. [@1] The seizure took place in
Demre district of Antalya province. [@1]
