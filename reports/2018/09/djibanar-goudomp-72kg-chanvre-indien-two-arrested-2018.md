Gendarmes of the Goudomp brigade seized 72 kg of chanvre indien, the French term
for Indian hemp used in Senegal, at Djibanar in the Goudomp department of
Sédhiou region, during an operation carried out in two stages. [@1] Two men, a
Bissau-Guinean and a Senegalese, were arrested during the operation, according
to the TFM correspondent cited by Dakarposte. [@1] The owner of the house where
the seizures were made and a Bissau-Guinean soldier fled towards Guinea-Bissau
on Jakarta motorcycles. [@1] The soldier left his national identity card at the
house and a 12-gauge weapon was found there. [@1] Gendarmes under Commander
Léonard Sambou kept patrolling the area and detained the house owner's wife.
[@1]

Officers seized 61 kg during the night of Monday and 11 kg the following
Tuesday. [@1] Pressafrik put the value of the haul at 4 million CFA francs. [@2]
Pressafrik reported that two of the group were arrested and three escaped on
motorbikes. [@2] Pressafrik said the detainees would be presented to the
prosecutor in Kolda within days. [@2]

Pressafrik, citing Zik FM in Ziguinchor, said the Bissau-Guinean soldier was
also implicated in the theft of about 30 head of cattle and several armed
robberies in the area. [@2]
