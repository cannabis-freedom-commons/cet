Gendarmería Nacional carried out two procedures in the Flores neighbourhood of
Buenos Aires as part of the national anti-drug effort. [@1] Officers of
Prevention Unit 1-11-14 on foot patrol in Sector 3 reached Pasaje Oceanía
between Pasajes San Juan and El Sol and saw a group of men passing black bags
and removing small packets. [@1] The men fled into the passageways when they
noticed the officers. [@1] In Sector 1 of the same neighbourhood officers saw a
woman leaving a house carrying a handbag. [@1] She dropped her belongings and
tried to flee when she saw the officers, and they immediately detained her. [@1]

Officers searching the first spot found two black nylon bags holding 298 packets
with 1 kilo 904 grams of marijuana and 53 packets with 28.8 grams of cocaine,
plus 2,300 Argentine pesos. [@1] Opening the woman's bag before witnesses,
officers found 258.7 grams of white powdery substance and 61.7 grams of compact
yellowish substance that tested positive for cocaine, plus 200 Argentine pesos,
three precision scales and other items of interest. [@1] Across both procedures
officers seized a total of 1 kilo 904 grams of marijuana, 349.2 grams of cocaine
and 2,500 Argentine pesos, with one woman detained. [@1]

Federal Criminal and Correctional Court No. 7 intervened in both cases and
ordered confiscation of all the drugs and detention of the woman involved. [@1]
