A 49-year-old man who arrived in Hong Kong from Delhi, India, on the morning
of 24 September 2018 was stopped at Hong Kong International Airport, where
Customs officers found about 13 kg of suspected cannabis resin concealed
inside the false compartments of his check-in suitcase. [@1] Customs estimated
the market value at about HK$1.1 million. [@1] The man was arrested and
charged with one count of trafficking in a dangerous drug, and was due to
appear at West Kowloon Magistrates' Courts on 26 September. [@1]

Under Hong Kong's Dangerous Drugs Ordinance, trafficking carries a maximum
penalty of a HK$5 million fine and life imprisonment. [@1]
