The Jamaica Defence Force Coast Guard stopped and boarded a blue motorised boat acting suspiciously off the Westmoreland coast on 3 May 2018. [@1,2] Coastguardsmen found eleven packages of compressed marijuana still on the vessel and retrieved five more parcels the crew had thrown overboard, about 765 lb (347 kg) in total. [@1,2]

Aboard were two Jamaicans, 39-year-old fisherman Orlando Sangster of Barbary Hall and 21-year-old labourer Renaldo Seaton of Middle Quarters, both from St Elizabeth, along with 39-year-old Haitian farmer Guy Robert Joseph. [@1] All three were charged with possession of ganja, dealing in ganja, taking steps preparatory to export ganja, use of conveyance and conspiracy. [@1]

Sangster and Seaton were each offered bail of $500,000 when they appeared in the Westmoreland Parish Court, on condition they report to the Black River Police Station on Mondays and Wednesdays, while Joseph was remanded. [@1] Tuck Magazine placed the run in the Jamaica–Haiti guns-for-drugs pattern, in which Jamaican ganja is traded for firearms. [@2]
