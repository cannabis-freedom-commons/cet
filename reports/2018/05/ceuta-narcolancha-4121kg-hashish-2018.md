A Guardia Civil patrol boat spotted a fast inflatable boat of the kind used for
drug trafficking in the Strait of Gibraltar, about ten nautical miles off the
entrance to Ceuta's port, at around 14:00 on 21 May 2018. [@3] The patrol
followed it, and a few miles on saw it join two similar boats, one of them
loaded with sacks. [@3] When the patrol closed in to identify those aboard, the
boats took evasive manoeuvres, and as the interception neared one of the empty
boats came alongside and the crew jumped into it and fled. [@3] The abandoned
boat, 14 metres long with three 350 horsepower engines, was towed with its load
to the Guardia Civil base in Ceuta's marina. [@3] The Guardia Civil put the load
at 4,121 kilograms of hashish resin once the sacks were counted in Ceuta. [@3]
It sent a report to the duty investigating court in Ceuta and left the hashish
in the government delegation's health service. [@3] The Guardia Civil described
the interception as taking place between the Moroccan coast and the Andalusian
coast. [@2]

Kifache, a Moroccan news site, reported the interception on 22 May as the
stopping of a fast boat carrying two tonnes of hashish. [@1] The smugglers
intended to take the load to southern Spain, specifically the coast of Cádiz
province, Kifache reported. [@1] Kifache also reported that two smugglers on
board had been arrested and the drug seized. [@1]
