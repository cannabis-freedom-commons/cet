A magistrate's complaint filed in the Superior Court of Guam charged brothers
Sun Koo Chu, 34, and Rick Ho Chu, 36, over marijuana mailed from California and
plants growing in Rick Chu's Tumon apartment. [@1] A package of about one and a
half pounds of marijuana sent in the mail led authorities to the Tumon apartment
where they found a marijuana grow operation. [@1] Authorities intercepted a UPS
package on 4 March 2018 that had been sent from "Minho Kim" in Los Angeles,
California to "Sung Kong" in Tamuning and held 702 gross grams of a green, leafy
substance that tested presumptive positive for marijuana. [@1]

Sun Chu contacted UPS the following Monday and asked for the package to be
delivered to his work, after which officers took out the suspected marijuana and
replaced it with a sham material and a location-tracking device before resealing
it. [@1] An undercover officer posing as a UPS employee delivered the package,
and Sun Chu was later detained with the opened package near a Tumon apartment,
while the tracking device was found in a nearby jungle. [@1] Sun Chu told
authorities he contacted a person identified as "Kubu" and wired Kubu $3,600 in
exchange for one and a half pounds of marijuana to be mailed to him in Guam.
[@1]

A search of the Tumon apartment found the sham material from the intercepted
package, seven marijuana plants with lights, soil and other equipment in one
bedroom, and several smoking devices with residue that tested presumptive
positive for amphetamines in another bedroom. [@1] Digital scales and a handgun
reported stolen in 2010 were also found, and Rick Chu told police he found the
firearm and decided to keep it despite not having a firearms ID. [@1] Rick Chu
also said he had been using marijuana for some time and that it was his first
time growing marijuana. [@1]

Sun Chu was charged with importation of a Schedule I controlled substance as a
first-degree felony, conspiracy to import a Schedule I controlled substance as a
first-degree felony, money laundering as a first-degree felony and conspiracy to
possess with intent to deliver a Schedule I controlled substance as a
first-degree felony. [@1] Rick Chu was charged with manufacture and possession
of a Schedule I controlled substance as first-degree felonies, possessing a
firearm without a Guam firearms identification card, possessing an unregistered
firearm and theft by receiving a stolen firearm as third-degree felonies,
possession of a Schedule II controlled substance as a third-degree felony and
possession of a Schedule I controlled substance as a petty misdemeanor. [@1]
