Military Police arrested a 22-year-old man at his home in the Caminho das Águas
2 neighbourhood of Lavras, Minas Gerais, on the morning of Monday 26 March 2018,
after he posted a photo of himself holding a 9mm submachine gun on social media.
[@1] In the caption he wrote "O pente tá carregado pronto pra cuspir", or "the
magazine is loaded, ready to spit". [@1] The Military Police said the weapon, a
model restricted to the armed forces, had been used in the robbery of a bus
carrying sacoleiros, street traders who travel to buy stock, at the Carmo da
Cachoeira junction the previous Friday. [@1] The gun contained 47 intact rounds
and was ready for use. [@1] At the house officers also seized three mobile
phones and R$ 2,500 in assorted notes. [@1] The man confessed to the robbery and
said he acted with two accomplices from the same neighbourhood and with others
from outside the city. [@1]

He told police the money was to buy drugs for trafficking in Lavras and that he
was negotiating to buy marijuana from a resident of the Fonte Verde
neighbourhood. [@1] Police went to Fonte Verde and found a .38 revolver with its
serial number ground off at the home of a 25-year-old man. [@1] They then found
a 20-year-old suspect carrying 1 kg of marijuana and, with a sniffer dog, 11
more tablets of the drug, G1 reported. [@1] Lavras24Horas reported that the
marijuana, 12 kg pressed into 12 tablets, was found buried in a lot at the
address the man gave. [@2] Three people were arrested and taken to the police
station with the material seized. [@1]

The robbers had used a car stolen in São Tomé das Letras, which they abandoned
after dividing R$ 50,000 among themselves. [@1]
