Hank Weech, 50, and his sons Brandon, 26, and Brad, 21, were arraigned in a
magistrate's court and charged after a Grand Bahama police operation earlier
that week. [@1] Hank Weech and his sons, 26-year-old Brandon and 21-year-old
Brad Weech, stood before Deputy Chief Magistrate Subusola Swain charged with two
counts each of possession of the proceeds of criminal conduct and possession of
dangerous drugs with intent to supply. [@1] They were three of four people
arraigned in connection with the seizure of over $1 million in cash, over
$45,000 worth of marijuana and $20,000 of hashish after an operation by local
and international officers in Freeport on Monday. [@1]

Prosecutors alleged that on 19 March, at Freeport, Grand Bahama, the three
together possessed $1,351,162 in US currency, knowing or suspecting that it
represented proceeds of criminal conduct. [@1] It was also alleged that on the
same date the Hampshire Drive, South Bahamia residents were found in possession
of marijuana with intent to supply. [@1] Shortly after midnight on 19 March
2018, Drug Enforcement Unit and Rapid Response Unit officers with a search
warrant went to a residence on Hampshire Drive, South Bahamia, found over one
million dollars in US currency, and made no arrest there. [@2] Shortly after 3
a.m. the same morning, officers of the same units searched an apartment on
Hampshire Drive, South Bahamia, and found a duffle bag hidden in the ceiling
containing suspected marijuana and suspected hashish. [@2] Two adult Bahamian
men were arrested and taken into custody. [@2]

Police estimated the hashish at 9.80 pounds, with a street value of $20,000, and
the suspected marijuana at 45.3 pounds, with a street value of over $45,000.
[@2] The Tribune gave the marijuana weight as 45.3 pounds with a street value of
$45,000 and the hashish as 9.80 pounds with a street value of $20,000. [@1] Each
of the Weech family pleaded not guilty, and the case was adjourned to 20 June
for trial. [@1] The three were remanded to the Bahamas Department of
Correctional Services to await trial, with the right to apply to the Supreme
Court for bail. [@1] A fourth man, 38-year-old Marvin Clarke of Canaby Terrace,
was arraigned on firearm and ammunition charges from the same operation. [@1]
