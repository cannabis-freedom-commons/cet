Gendarmes arrested two alleged cannabis dealers in Kani, a town in north-western
Côte d'Ivoire, on Saturday 24 March 2018. [@1] The arrests were made in
Mamouroula, a neighbourhood of Kani. [@1]

The two were caught with 26 balls of cannabis weighing 100 grams in total. [@1]
They hid the seized drug in a black bag. [@1] One of those arrested was a
28-year-old Ivorian and the other a 16-year-old Malian. [@1]

Anonymous information led the gendarmes to the two, described as alleged
dealers. [@1] Questioned by the officers, they told them where the cannabis they
sell comes from. [@1]
