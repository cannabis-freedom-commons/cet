Nicaragua's National Police seized 278 pounds (about 126 kg) of marijuana at the
El Guasaule border post in Chinandega on Monday 10 December 2018. [@1] The head
of the National Police's judicial aid directorate (DAJ), commissioner general
Luis Alberto Pérez Olivas, gave the details at a press conference on Tuesday 11
December. [@1] Officers found 62 rectangular packets of green plant material in
concealed compartments in the truck's fuel tanks, and a field test was positive
for marijuana. [@1] The driver, Edwin Hernández Valenzuela, 40, fled when he was
discovered and was to be investigated for organised crime and international drug
transport. [@1]

Police seized the green tractor unit, plate C187VNW, and its Guatemalan trailer,
plate TC038VVM, whose right and left fuel tanks held the compartments. [@1]
Pérez Olivas said the case was international trafficking because the load came
concealed in a freight truck from Mexico. [@1]

Police presented the seizure as part of the 'muro de contención' (containment
wall) plan against drug trafficking and organised crime. [@1]
