Morocco's National Brigade of Judicial Police arrested a truck driver near El Jadida, south of Casablanca, after catching him with 400 bags of hashish weighing 10 tons on the nights of 4 and 5 December 2018. [@1,2] The operation, based on intelligence from the General Directorate of Territorial Surveillance (DGST), led to three further arrests, including the truck's owner, one of them a woman. [@1,2]

Police seized 10,000 euros from the gang, which is suspected of belonging to an international drug-trafficking network. [@1] The packaging and wrapping indicated the drugs were to be smuggled by sea. [@1,2]

The four suspects were placed in custody while the judicial investigation continued under the supervision of the competent prosecutor's office, to establish the network's links inside Morocco and abroad. [@1,2]
