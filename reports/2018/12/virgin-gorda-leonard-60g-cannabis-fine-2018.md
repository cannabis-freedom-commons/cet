Hakeem Leonard, a 29-year-old US Virgin Islands native, pleaded guilty at the
Magistrate's Court to possession of a controlled drug and possession of a
controlled drug with intent to supply. [@1] Magistrate Shawn Innocent fined him
$5,000 on Thursday 6 December 2018, after he was held with 60 grams of cannabis
in the Valley on Virgin Gorda. [@1] The $5,000 fine related to the
intent-to-supply charge; Magistrate Innocent gave Leonard 30 days to pay and
said he would spend six months at Her Majesty's Prison in Balsam Ghut if he
failed to do so. [@1] On the simple possession charge he was cautioned,
reprimanded and discharged. [@1]

The court heard that on 30 September 2017 Leonard was in police custody for an
unrelated matter when officers searched him. [@1] Police found 58 dime bags
containing the cannabis tucked in his pant pockets. [@1] When questioned by
police, Leonard said: "You all catch me with everything, officer." [@1] He told
law enforcers that he had received the drugs 'for someone' and was subsequently
charged. [@1]

At an earlier appearance before Senior Magistrate Tamia Richards, Leonard,
described as a 29-year-old construction worker from St Thomas, did not enter a
plea to the two charges. [@2] That appearance was adjourned to 6 December 2018,
when the case was concluded. [@2] Ahead of sentencing his attorney, Leroy Jones,
asked the court to impose a fine or a suspended custodial sentence, saying
Leonard was a first-time offender who was sorry and ashamed of his actions. [@1]
