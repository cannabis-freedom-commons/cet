Police searched a residence in Lords Bank Village, Belize District, on the
morning of 19 December 2018. [@1,2] At the house officers found a .22 K-62 PAT-Ten
brand handgun with a black handle and a serial number, and one suspected
cannabis plant. [@1] The marijuana plant was three inches tall and was growing on the verandah. [@2] Edward Ferguson and Erwin Lanza were present during the
search. [@1] The men were cousins: 20-year-old Erwin Lanza and 21-year-old Edward Ferguson. [@2] Both men were arrested and charged with keeping a firearm without a
gun licence and with cultivation of cannabis. [@1,2] Lanza also pleaded guilty to the gun charge and received an additional fine of $2,000 due by 30 May 2019, with two years in jail if he defaulted. [@2] The Senior Magistrate sentenced each man to pay a fine of $2,500 by 30 March 2019, with two years in jail if they defaulted. [@2] They were arraigned before Senior Magistrate Aretha Ford and both pleaded guilty to the marijuana cultivation charge. [@2]
