Uşak narcotics police detained three people travelling in a car on the
Uşak-Ankara road: a couple said to have married 15 days earlier, identified as
E.Ç. and H.Ç., and a third person, E.Ç., travelling with them. [@1] After
procedures at the police station the three suspects were sent to the courthouse,
where the judgeship remanded E.Ç. and E.Ç. in custody and released the woman
suspect H.Ç. under judicial control. [@1]

The provincial narcotics branch had acted on a tip that a large quantity of
drugs would be brought to Uşak from Diyarbakır. [@2] Officers stopped the car
they had been following near the Uşak Sugar Factory and searched it with the
narcotics dog Tora. [@2] Inside pillows among dowry items officers found 10
kilos 350 grams of kubar esrar. [@1] A second account describes the haul as
10.35 kilograms of cannabis hidden in pillows, a sleeping set and suitcases that
were part of the dowry. [@3] The three were referred to the judicial authorities
on suspicion of manufacturing and trafficking drugs. [@3]
