Gendarmerie officers, after investigation work, set up checkpoints on National
Routes 12, 14 and 120 to stop a large vehicle believed to be carrying drugs from
Misiones to San Juan. [@1] Officers intercepted a Chevrolet Onix acting as scout
for a Fiat Iveco truck, which tried to flee, and apprehended one Argentine man
near the city of Gobernador Virasoro. [@1] Officers separately stopped the truck
at kilometre 745 of National Route 14, where two men of the same nationality
were travelling. [@1] The Federal Court No. 1 of Corrientes ordered the seizure
of the drugs and the detention of the three people, described as members of a
criminal organisation supplying drugs to Cordoba, San Juan and presumably Chile.
[@1]

Inspection of the truck, in front of witnesses, found that 497 packages of plant
material had been packed into the auxiliary fuel tank behind the cab. [@1]
Forensic officers' Narcotest field tests were positive for cannabis sativa, with
a total weight of 345 kilos 741 grams. [@1] The El Litoral account of the same
operation was published on 28 November 2018. [@2]

Court-ordered searches of six homes in the Misiones city of Posadas and in
Garupa were later carried out by judicial-investigation units and Squadrons 50
Posadas and 47 Ituzaingo. [@1] Those follow-up operations seized a vehicle,
mobile phones, cash and other items of interest to the case. [@1] The San Juan
outlet likewise reports that the marijuana was being carried in a truck's
auxiliary fuel tank from Misiones with San Juan as its final destination, ending
with three detainees. [@3] The Corrientes outlet NOVA Corrientes likewise
reports 345 kilos 741 grams of marijuana transported in a truck's auxiliary fuel
tank in Corrientes. [@4]
