Police arrested five people at a traffic checkpoint on Hihifo Road in Tongatapu
on Thursday 15 November 2018 after finding illicit drugs and firearms in a
vehicle. [@1] The 47-year-old driver from Fangaloto and his 35-year-old
passenger from Popua were arrested at the checkpoint and taken into police
custody. [@1] Officers found methamphetamine, cannabis and a pistol with live
bullets inside the vehicle. [@1] A follow-up search at the driver's property led
to three more arrests: a 36-year-old man from Mataika, a 28-year-old man from
Ngele'ia and a 24-year-old man from 'Utulau, found with more drugs and a bullet
shell. [@1]

Police seized 2.06 grams of cannabis and 13.42 grams of methamphetamine. [@1]

Police charged all five suspects with possession of illicit drugs and remanded
them in police custody until 26 November 2018. [@1] Acting Chief Superintendent
Tevita Vailea said police were committed to disrupting the supply of harmful
drugs and holding those responsible to account. [@1] He asked anyone with
information about drug dealing to contact police confidentially on 23417 or 922.
