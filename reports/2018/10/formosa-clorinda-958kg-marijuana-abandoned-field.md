On 28 October 2018, personnel of Squadron 16 “Clorinda” stationed at the
“Gendarme Fermín Rolón” checkpoint on National Route 11 were alerted to
suspected vehicles in a rural zone with signs of unlawful activity. [@1] The
gendarmes moved to the area and combed the perimeter near an estancia, where
they spotted 25 large black nylon bags lying among the grass a few metres away.
[@1]

The abandoned load lay in a field about 35 kilometres south of the city of
Clorinda, in plots bordering National Route 11, hidden among weeds. [@1] Before
witnesses, the officers carried the load to their unit and opened the bundles,
finding 1,151 rectangular packages holding cannabis sativa. [@1] The haul
weighed 957 kilos 985 grams of marijuana, which officers of the National
Gendarmerie seized as part of the anti-drug effort led by the National Security
Ministry. [@1]

The Federal Court and the Federal Prosecutor’s Office of Formosa intervened in
the case and ordered the seizure of the drug. [@1] No suspects, arrests or
vehicles were named in the account of the recovery of the abandoned bags. [@1]
