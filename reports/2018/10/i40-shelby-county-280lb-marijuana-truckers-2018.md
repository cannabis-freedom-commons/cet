Members of the Multi-Agency Gang Unit and the West Tennessee Drug Task Force
arrested two men on Interstate 40 in Shelby County. [@1] Corey Dajuan Walden,
31, of Antioch, Tennessee, and Jonathan Tarell Carter, 43, of Cane Ridge,
Tennessee, were charged with possession of a controlled substance with intent to
manufacture, deliver or sell. [@1] Carter was also charged with failure to
exercise due care. [@1] Both men were held in the Shelby County Jail. [@1]

The tractor trailer was pulled over for a traffic violation shortly after 8 p.m.
on Sunday in eastbound I-40 near Canada Road in northeast Shelby County. [@1]
After a K-9 officer alerted to narcotics, officers found five large duffel bags
of marijuana in the trailer, which was loaded with washing machines. [@1] The
load contained 280 pounds of marijuana. [@1] West Tennessee Drug Task Force
Director Tim Helldorfer put the street value of the marijuana at approximately
$840,000. [@1] The trailer carried a load of washing machines along with 280
pounds of marijuana. [@2] A K-9 alerted police to the $840,000 worth of drugs.
[@3]

Shelby County District Attorney General Amy Weirich and Sheriff Floyd Bonner Jr.
called the seizure an example of coordinated work among agencies including the
Tennessee Highway Patrol. [@1] The Multi-Agency Gang Unit included members of
the Shelby County Sheriff's Office, the Memphis Police Department Organized
Crime Unit, the ATF, the FBI and the U.S. Marshals. [@1] The West Tennessee Drug
Task Force draws more than 40 officers from agencies in three West Tennessee
judicial districts and is administered by the Shelby County District Attorney's
Office. [@1]
