Officers found nine crocus bags of suspected marijuana in bushes on Florin
Drive, South Bahamia, a Freeport subdivision on Grand Bahama, shortly before 4
p.m. on Tuesday 9 October 2018, and made no arrests. [@2] Police said the bust
followed information from an anonymous tipster. [@1] Central Detective Unit
officers, the K-9 Unit and Bahamas Customs officers went to the bushy area on
Florin Drive and found the nine bags. [@1] Assistant Superintendent Terecita
Pinder said immigration officers, customs officers and the K-9 unit went with
Central Detective Unit officers to Florin Drive. [@3]

The suspected marijuana weighed 226 pounds, about 102.5 kilograms, and police
put the street value at $226,000. [@1] Another police account put the estimated
street value at $260,000 for nine sacks weighing 226 pounds found in South
Bahamia shortly before 4 p.m. on Tuesday. [@3] That account gave the weight as
226 pounds and the street value as $226,000, and said no arrest was made while
the investigation continued. [@2]

Grand Bahama police said no arrest was made after the drugs were found in bushes
in a Freeport subdivision. [@3] The investigation was ongoing. [@3]
