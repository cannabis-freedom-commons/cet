An American cruise ship passenger was fined $4,000 after admitting possession of
cannabis and drug equipment. [@1] The passenger was Meghan George, 41, from
Pennsylvania, heard in Magistrates’ Court. [@1] Customs officers caught her with
two bags of plantlike material in her purse as she tried to board the Anthem of
the Seas in Dockyard. [@1] Officers searched her cabin and found three marijuana
pipes, a grinder and an electronic cigarette cartridge holding cannabis oil in
her suitcase. [@1] She was arrested the next morning and remanded in custody.
[@1]

Local police on the island arrested the woman and fined her $4,000 for
possession. [@2] The offence happened on Monday night. [@1] George pleaded
guilty to four counts of drug possession. [@1] Bermuda Magistrate Juan Wolffe
ordered her to pay the fine before she left the island. [@2] Her defence lawyer
said she put the drugs in her bag to avoid smoke from a neighbour’s house fire.
[@1]

Magistrate Juan Wolffe heard that possession of cannabis has been decriminalised
in Pennsylvania but is still illegal. [@1] He reminded George that the legal
