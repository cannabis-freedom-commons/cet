Ken Stanisclaus, a 39-year-old mason, and Angela Pierre, a 32-year-old cook,
were jointly charged with possession of cocaine, possession of cannabis,
trafficking in a controlled drug, illegal possession of a firearm and illegal
possession of ammunition. [@1] Stanisclaus, of Woburn, and Pierre, of Mont
Toute, were the two people charged. [@2]

Police took them into custody on Wednesday, 10 October 2018, after searching a
house at Woodlands, St George, where they were the occupants. [@2] The search of
the house and its immediate surroundings turned up cocaine, Creepy cannabis, one
Israeli BUL-M5 9 mm pistol, 50 rounds of 9 mm ammunition and 10 rounds of .38
hollow-point ammunition. [@2] The Jamaica Observer account describes the same
find as cocaine, creepy marijuana, a gun and ammunition. [@1]

Police described Cripa or Creepy as a powerful marijuana strain grown from
plants native to Europe and the United States. [@1] An internet search cited by
police reporting says the strain has up to 20% higher levels of THC, the active
psychoactive ingredient, than other marijuana strains. [@2] The two people
caught with the drug were due in court on Friday, 12 October 2018. [@2]
