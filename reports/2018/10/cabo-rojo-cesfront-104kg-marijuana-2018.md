On Saturday 20 October 2018, members of the Specialized Border Security Corps
(CESFRONT), following work by Dominican intelligence services, seized marijuana
in Cabo Rojo on the south coast of Pedernales province. [@1] The operation was
reported on Saturday 20 October 2018, the day of the seizure. [@2] Officers took
23 packages, described as marijuana bullets, weighing 229 pounds (103.8
kilograms). [@1] The packages were concealed in a cache in a truck carrying
clothing from Port-au-Prince. [@1] Colonel José Manuel Durán Infante, director
general of CESFRONT, said the drug was wrapped in used clothes that the group
intended to move along the Pedernales-Barahona road. [@2]

Three people who were transporting the merchandise were arrested during the
seizure. [@1] They were identified as Juan Herasme, Michael Cuevas and Jessi
Reyes. [@1] Juan Herasme was driving the vehicle and was accompanied by Michael
Cuevas and Hessix Reyes, who were detained. [@2] Members of CESFRONT and of the
C-2 intelligence unit carried out the seizure. [@2]

Colonel Durán Infante handed the drugs and the three detainees to the National
Directorate of Drug Control (DNCD). [@1] The vehicle and the detainees were sent
to the police, while the drugs were delivered to the Public Ministry and the
DNCD in Pedernales. [@2]
