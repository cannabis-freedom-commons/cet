Aaron Joseph, a 25-year-old United States citizen living on Virgin Gorda, was
sentenced to 36 months in prison for his burglary and drug offences by
Magistrate Shawn Innocent. [@2] Joseph is a United States citizen who worked as
a construction worker and is a father of four. [@1] The sentence was made up of
12 months for the burglary, 12 months for two counts of cannabis possession and
a further 12 months for two counts of possession with intent to supply. [@2]
Magistrate Innocent ordered the three terms to run concurrently, so Joseph will
serve no more than 12 months. [@2] The sentence runs from 13 February 2018, the
date he was first remanded. [@2]

On 19 August 2017, police found 80 grams of cannabis at Joseph's home, in a
clear plastic bag in the refrigerator and in other parts of the house. [@1] On
19 February 2018, officers executing a search warrant at the home found 749
grams of cannabis scattered in the wooden flooring, a closet, two bedrooms, the
porch and his car. [@1] He pleaded guilty to possessing the 80 grams and to
possessing it with intent to supply. [@1] He also pleaded guilty to possessing
the 749 grams and to supplying those drugs. [@1] Joseph admitted ownership of
all the drugs, telling officers “I just smoke”. [@1]

He had also pleaded guilty to burglarizing a Virgin Gorda grocery store in late
November 2017, taking food items after finding the cash register empty. [@1]
Defence attorney Patrick Thompson told the court the burglary was not
premeditated but done on impulse. [@1] Thompson said Joseph was remorseful and
had been on remand for at least nine months. [@1] He said Joseph had cooperated
with police and had no previous convictions. [@1] Magistrate Innocent said the
aggravating and mitigating factors seemed evenly weighed. [@2] The court heard
that Joseph was found with a relatively substantial quantity of drugs within a
short period. [@2] The magistrate also pointed to the seriousness and prevalence
of the offences in the British Virgin Islands. [@2]
