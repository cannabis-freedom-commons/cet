The Benton County Sheriff's Office arrested three people on drug charges after
an investigation by Arkansas and Oklahoma authorities into a marijuana delivery
to Garfield, Arkansas. [@1] Jeffrey Card, 30, of Tijeras, New Mexico,
Christopher Ramirez, 29, of Garfield, and Megan Wingett, 34, of Garfield were
arrested on charges of conspiracy to deliver a controlled substance. [@2]
Ramirez faced additional charges of possession of a firearm by a certain person
and possession of a controlled substance with purpose to deliver, and Wingett
faced a further charge of possession of a controlled substance with purpose to
deliver. [@1] All three were released on bonds of $10,000, $5,000 and $2,500 and
had hearings set for 13 November in Benton County Circuit Court. [@2]

Authorities seized 55 pounds (about 25 kilograms) of marijuana, one and a half
pounds of hash, four ounces of mushrooms and three ounces of kratom, along with
two guns. [@1] The mushrooms and kratom are not cannabis and are left out of the
cannabis figures here. [@1]

The Benton County Sheriff's Office Narcotics Unit worked with the Oklahoma and
Fayetteville offices of the Drug Enforcement Administration and the Oklahoma
Bureau of Narcotics on a controlled delivery of 50 pounds of marijuana to
Garfield. [@1] Card was stopped in Oklahoma on Tuesday 2 October 2018 by an
Oklahoma Bureau of Narcotics agent who found about 50 pounds of marijuana in his
car, according to a probable cause affidavit. [@2] Deputies had him complete the
delivery to Wingett's house in Garfield, where they arrested her, and a search
of the home found three pounds of marijuana underneath her trailer. [@2]
Deputies then arrested Ramirez at his home, where a search under his probation
search waiver found more drugs and two guns. [@2] The Democrat-Gazette reported
that the three were arrested on the Tuesday in a bust that included agencies
from two states and turned up more than 50 pounds of marijuana. [@3]

Sgt. Shannon Jenkins, a sheriff's office spokeswoman, said the arrests were a
joint effort between multiple agencies in two states. [@2]
