Peru's navy and National Police, with support from the Colombian Navy, seized a
cargo of 1,200 kilograms of marijuana in a river-control operation in the upper
Putumayo river sector of Loreto. [@1] Authorities valued the seized cargo at
more than 5 million dollars. [@1]

The marijuana was described as the "cripy" variety, and the operation also
netted weapons and boats fitted with double bottoms. [@1] The intervention took
place at the locality of La Libertad, 24 kilometres northwest of Soplín Vargas
district. [@1] The action involved representatives of the prosecutor's office,
personnel of the BAP Castilla, the Amazon River Control Unit and the Gueppí
detachment of the Marine Infantry. [@1]

The Peruvian Navy said it was promoting cooperative security in the Amazon
through meetings between commanders of bordering countries, the armed forces and
the national police. [@1] The institutions reiterated their commitment to
continue control and surveillance operations against illicit activity, in
coordination with the competent authorities. [@1]
