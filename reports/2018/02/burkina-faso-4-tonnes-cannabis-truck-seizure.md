Burkinabè customs seized more than four tonnes of cannabis hidden in a truck on
17 February 2018. [@1] The cannabis was in a truck trailer that had come from
Ghana and was bound for Mali. [@1] Laurent Blaise Kaboré, the director of
customs investigations, explained the traffickers' method at a press conference
in Ouagadougou on Friday 23 February 2018. [@1] RFI reported the same operation
as four tonnes of cannabis hidden in a truck from Ghana. [@2]

The truck looked empty, but a compartment had been specially fitted with a floor
made of several strips of plywood and the drugs were stored at the bottom of the
trailer. [@1] Officers found the hidden load and seized more than four tonnes of
cannabis concealed in the vehicle. [@1] The truck made two rotations a month
between Bamako and other regional capitals. [@2] It was being sought by Malian
security forces. [@2] All the documents found in the truck were false. [@2]

At the same briefing customs reported seizing 15 380 kg of cannabis across the
country between November 2017 and 17 February 2018. [@1] Kaboré said Burkina
Faso was now a drug-consuming country rather than only a transit country. [@1]
