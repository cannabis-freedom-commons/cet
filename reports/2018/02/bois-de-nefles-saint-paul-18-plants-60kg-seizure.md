On 14 February 2018, gendarmes from Bois-de-Nèfles went to the Hauts de
Saint-Paul to deliver a summons and spotted zamal plants sticking up above a
fence. [@1] After searching that home, they again saw zamal plants sticking up
above a neighbour's fence. [@1] A further search then led them to spot zamal
plants above yet another neighbour's fence. [@1]

After counting and weighing, officers seized 18 zamal plants about 4 metres tall
with a total weight of 60 kilos. [@1] The 18 plants were spread across three
different homes in Bois-de-Nèfles, in the heights of Saint-Paul. [@2] The
seizure was described as unexpected, made while the officers had come for
something else. [@2]
