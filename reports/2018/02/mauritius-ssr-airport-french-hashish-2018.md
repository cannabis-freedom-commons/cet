A French national was arrested at Plaisance airport, the SSR International
Airport, on 4 February 2018 after hashish was found in his bag. [@1] He had
arrived that afternoon on Air France flight AF 460 from Paris. [@1] He was due
to stay in a villa at Flic-en-Flac during his visit. [@1]

Police put the value of the hashish at about Rs 3,000. [@1] Officers also seized
77 cigarette rolling papers. [@1]

He was questioned over the find and then released on parole. [@1] His release
was conditional on his appearing at the Mahébourg court on 5 February 2018 for a
provisional charge. [@1]
