Police in Surkhrod district of eastern Nangarhar province seized 404 kilograms
of hashish and detained four people, provincial police spokesman Col. Hazrat
Hussain Mashriqiwal said on Tuesday. [@1]

Mashriqiwal said 360 kilograms of hashish were recovered at a checkpoint in the
Dusaraka locality. [@1] The contraband was being smuggled from Kabul to Torkham,
the spokesman said. [@1] It had been skilfully placed in a vehicle. [@1] Three
individuals were arrested in connection with that seizure. [@1]

Separately, police seized 44 kilograms of hashish and detained a man from a
Fielder car in the Daronta area of the same district. [@2]

The four detainees were introduced to the provincial attorney's office for
investigations. [@2]
