Police in Pocrí, in Panama's Los Santos province, apprehended four foreigners
who were found close to the coast of Pocrí during the Carnival security
operation. [@1] National Police personnel in Pocrí detained the four men, whose
nationalities were still to be confirmed because they were carrying no
documentation of any kind. [@1]

Officers seized 788 rectangular packages of the drug known as marijuana from a
boat at La Calendaría beach in the Pocrí sector on the afternoon of Carnival
Sunday. [@1] A field test carried out on the seized packages at the scene
returned a positive result for marijuana. [@1] Los Santos National Police chief
Frank Pinilla said specialists in amphibious operations and officers of the
Anti-Drug Operations Directorate located the drugs during the operations then
under way. [@1]

The Delegated Drug Prosecutor's Office for the provinces of Herrera and Los
Santos opened inquiries into the case. [@1] Pinilla added that police maintained
full Carnival operability throughout the country while also responding to any
additional illicit activity that might occur. [@1]
