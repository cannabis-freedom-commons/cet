The special prosecution against organised crime (FESCCO) and the Juzgado de
Letras incinerated 80 packages of marijuana seized from Carlos Humberto Andrade
Figueroa, 29. [@1] The drugs were burned on the beaches of Perú before health
authorities, the national police, forensic toxicology staff, prosecutors and
members of the judiciary. [@1]

FESCCO carried out the burning in a case for possession and illicit drug
trafficking, after the seizure of two cardboard boxes. [@1] Andrade Figueroa was
detained in an operation at the López Bonito post while travelling on an
Emtraiol bus on the Tocoa to San Pedro Sula route. [@1] The boxes of what the
prosecution called hierba alucinógena were in the boot of a blue and white
Mercedes Benz with plates AAJ-6744. [@1]

La Prensa reported that preventive police arrested Andrade Figueroa in a routine
operation at the López Bonito police post on 26 January 2017, finding several
pounds of dried plant material suspected to be marijuana. [@2] Officers counted
79 packages of one pound each, 79 pounds in total, which La Prensa valued at
about 300,000 lempiras. [@2] La Prensa described him as 28 years old, from Colón
and living in the El Tamarindo neighbourhood of Tocoa, travelling as a passenger
on a bus between Olanchito and San Pedro Sula. [@2]
