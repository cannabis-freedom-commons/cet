Police in Thyolo district, in southern Malawi, arrested village headman Gumbi
for allegedly cultivating cannabis, which is known locally as chamba. [@1] A
second account identified him as Group Village headman Ghumbi and said he was
arrested on 8 July 2018. [@2] The arrest followed a tip-off: some well-wishers
informed the police about his cannabis growing, Face of Malawi reported. [@2]

Officers found the plants in a garden behind his house and uprooted 278 of them.
[@1] A statement from Thyolo police public relations office said officers had
uprooted the Indian hemp he was cultivating behind his house. [@1] Face of
Malawi reported that 278 plants of Cannabis sativa were uprooted by the law
enforcers. [@2] Police sent the plants for tests and reminded the public that
cultivating Indian hemp is a crime. [@1]

Face of Malawi said the suspect was expected to appear in court soon to answer
charges. [@2] Face of Malawi described Thyolo as a tea-growing district. [@2]
