Lhokseumawe police chief AKBP Ari Lasta led the destruction of four hectares of ready-to-harvest cannabis fields at Cot Rawa Thoe, Jurong village, in Sawang district on 19 July 2018. [@1,2] Soldiers of the Indonesian National Armed Forces (TNI) assisted the police, and the crop was uprooted and burned on the spot while thousands of stems were carried off as evidence. [@1]

Officers detained two growers at the scene: 73-year-old HU, who said the plot behind his house was only half a hectare and that he had already harvested three times, and his 28-year-old son MU. [@1,2] HU told reporters he sold harvested marijuana to visiting buyers at 125,000 rupiah a kilogram and claimed not to have known the plant was prohibited. [@2]
