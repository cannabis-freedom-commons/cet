More than 1.3 tonnes of material believed to be marijuana was seized on 13 July
2018 in Montenegrin waters near the border with Albania. [@1] Fishermen spotted
the packages around ten in the morning and alerted the police and the Maritime
Safety Administration. [@1] Police said officers found 62 packages of dark green
plant material weighing 1,332.3 kilograms about 11 nautical miles south-west of
the mouth of the Bojana River. [@3]

The search involved the Regional Border Police Centre South, the Ulcinj border
security department, patrol boats of the Bar and Budva border police units, an
electronic surveillance unit and the Maritime Safety Administration. [@1] The
packages were loaded onto the border police centre's vessels and handed to the
drug squad. [@2] No smugglers were found or identified. [@1]

An investigator told Vijesti that the smugglers' speedboat may have broken down,
prompting them to dump the cargo and leave Montenegrin waters. [@1] The state
prosecutor in the Higher State Prosecutor's Office in Podgorica ordered the drug
seized. [@1] Police said they were taking intensive measures to identify the
people who threw the drug into the sea. [@3]
