Judiciary Police arrested five university students for smoking cannabis and
announced the case at a regular press conference in Zape. [@1] The five suspects
were local men aged 21 to 23 who study at two universities on the Macau
peninsula. [@1] Wu, 21, went to a friend's home in Areia Preta and smoked
marijuana on Thursday evening, then felt unwell, called an ambulance and was
taken to Conde de Sao Januario Hospital. [@1] At the hospital Wu admitted
smoking marijuana earlier that evening and a drug test confirmed it. [@1] The
hospital afterwards notified the Judiciary Police about the incident. [@1] Under
questioning Wu said he had smoked weed with three fellow students that evening
using cannabis bought from a student surnamed Tam. [@1] Police located the four
other suspects and confirmed through drug tests that they had all smoked the
drug. [@1]

Tam said he got the drug for free from a stranger at a night entertainment venue
in Zape and later sold three joints to the other four for 900 patacas. [@1]
Police seized a total of 4.53 grammes of cannabis with a street value of 2,300
patacas along with drug paraphernalia in the operation. [@1]

All five were arrested and transferred to the Public Prosecution Office for
further investigation. [@1] The Social Welfare Bureau said it was greatly
concerned about the case, had contacted the related tertiary education
institutions and would provide counselling and support to the drug-takers. [@1]
