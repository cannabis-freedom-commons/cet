The Criminal Court sentenced 19-year-old Siyan Haadhee to three years in
prison for using cannabis. [@1,2] Haadhee, of Ma. Maajehivilla, was arrested
at 1:18 am on 2 April 2013 near the Mahchangolhi cemetery in Male on
suspicion of having used drugs. [@1] He confessed in court that he was on
drugs at the time of his arrest, and tests conducted during the investigation
came back positive for cannabis. [@1,2]
