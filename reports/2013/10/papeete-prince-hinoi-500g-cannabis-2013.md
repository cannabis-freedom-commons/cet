National police in Papeete seized 500 grams of cannabis during a routine vehicle
check at about 17:00 on Tuesday, on avenue du Prince Hinoi. [@1] The two
occupants of the car were held in police custody until their judgment at an
immediate court appearance on Thursday morning. [@1] The two men under
investigation are aged 45 and 50. [@1]

They are brothers, and the older one lives in Maupiti, in the Leeward Islands,
where he admitted that he cultivates cannabis. [@1] He had been in Tahiti since
the previous weekend in order to sell his harvest. [@1] During the check,
officers of the national police noticed a strong smell of pakalolo. [@1] When
asked whether they were carrying drugs, the occupants produced a glass pipe and
two sticks. [@1] The officers remained doubtful because a strong smell of
cannabis was still coming from the vehicle. [@1]

A judicial police officer was sent to the scene and carried out a full search of
the vehicle. [@1] He quickly found two ice-cream boxes full of cannabis and the
bottom of an expanded-polystyrene cooler. [@1] The older of the two was also
carrying three newly acquired mobile phones and 31,000 CFP francs in cash. [@1]
The Public Security Directorate said this was its fourth seizure since the start
of the year of cannabis goods coming from Maupiti. [@1]
