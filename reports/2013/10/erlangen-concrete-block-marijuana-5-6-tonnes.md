Nine suspects were arrested, according to the customs investigation office in
Munich. [@3] The Landgericht Halle sentenced ten people involved in the case to
a total of more than 50 years in prison, a customs investigator said. [@1]
Investigators said a gang moved about 5.6 tonnes of marijuana into Germany, the
Netherlands and Italy over several years. [@1]

The case began with a routine check on 30 October 2013 at a motorway rest area
near Erlangen. [@4] A contemporaneous account said the lorry carried 686
kilograms of marijuana built into a concrete block, with an estimated market
value of up to three million euros. [@2] A drug-sniffing dog alerted to a block
listed on the papers as a 17-tonne granite stone, and experts who broke it open
found 374 packages containing 645 kilograms of marijuana. [@4] The smugglers had
the hollow blocks cast for the purpose in Albania, sealed them with concrete and
worked the surfaces to look like raw granite. [@4]

After months of surveillance and phone tapping, officers moved in at a factory
site south of Halle. [@4] The suspects were caught removing 150 kilograms of
marijuana from a hiding place in a lorry. [@4] Investigators estimated the
black-market value of the smuggled quantity at about 50 million euros. [@4] The
case is still described as a record in Bavaria. [@1]
