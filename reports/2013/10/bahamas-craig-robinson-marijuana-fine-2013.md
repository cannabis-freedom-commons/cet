American actor Craig Robinson was arrested in the Bahamas on 9 October 2013
while trying to board a plane. [@1] The drugs were found in his luggage at
Lynden Pindling International Airport in Nassau. [@3] Prosecutors told the court
he had half a gram of marijuana and 18 Ecstasy pills. [@2] Robinson was in the
Bahamas for a comedy show at the Atlantis hotel. [@1]

He was taken into custody and appeared before a magistrate without a lawyer.
[@2] He pleaded guilty to two counts of drug possession in a magistrate's court.
[@3] When Magistrate Andrew Forbes asked where he got the drugs, Robinson said
he had brought them from the United States. [@3] Robinson told the magistrate he
did not know marijuana was illegal in the Bahamas. [@3] The minimum sentence for
both counts was four years in prison. [@3]

Magistrate Andrew Forbes imposed a fine of $1,000, with six months in prison as
the alternative. [@3] Forbes said Robinson would be handed to immigration
officials for deportation once the fine was paid. [@3] He was not handcuffed
when escorted in or out of court. [@3] A Bahamian law-enforcement official told
reporters the incident was nothing major. [@1]
