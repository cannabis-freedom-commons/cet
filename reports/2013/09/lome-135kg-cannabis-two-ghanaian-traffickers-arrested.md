Prosper Forson and Rafael Forson, two Ghanaian men, were arrested on 3 September
2013 at the Hollando bus station at Lomé's Grand marché, in possession of bags
containing 47.4 kg of cannabis as they tried to set off by road for Cotonou.
[@2] The two were presented to journalists at the offices of OCRTIDB on Friday 6
September, and the seized drugs were displayed to the media. [@2] OCRTIDB is
Togo's central office for repressing the illicit traffic in drugs and
money-laundering. [@1]

Officers seized 135.4 kg of cannabis in the operation, which ran from 29 August
to 3 September. [@2] Searches on 29 August uncovered another 88 kg of cannabis
hidden in shoe bags at Hedzranawoé, also in Lomé, which was to be carried to
Sémè in Benin. [@2] The cannabis had been received at two bus stations in Lomé
and near the capital's Grand marché. [@1]

According to deputy head of operations Yélénéké Kodjo Katanga, the two men
belonged to a trafficking network operating between Ghana, Benin, Nigeria and
Togo. [@1] Investigators said they were still seeking the owners of the drugs,
who remained at large. [@2]
