Gardaí from Bailieborough carried out a planned search of a warehouse on the outskirts of Virginia on the morning of 11 February 2013 and discovered a major indoor cultivation operation. [@1,3] About 800 cannabis plants were growing there, with an estimated street value of up to €700,000 pending analysis. [@3,1]

Two males in their twenties were arrested at the scene and detained at Bailieborough Garda Station while the scene was preserved for technical examination. [@1,3] Both appeared charged before Ballyconnell District Court at 10.30am the next morning. [@2]
