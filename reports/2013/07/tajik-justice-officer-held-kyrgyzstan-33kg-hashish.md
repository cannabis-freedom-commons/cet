Kyrgyzstan's State Service for Drug Control stopped a Daimler-Benz car in
Karabak village, Batken district, at 6:30 am on 12 July 2013, with servicemen of
border unit No. 2022 assisting. [@1] The driver was a Tajik Justice Ministry
officer with the rank of senior lieutenant of internal service, born in 1982.
[@1] The suspect was a resident of Khujand in Tajikistan. [@3]

Officers found 52 bricks of hashish, wrapped in tape, in a specially built
hiding place in the car's fuel tank. [@1] The hashish weighed 32 kg 950 g. [@1]
GSKN officers had been investigating the Tajik citizen for two months over
smuggling of Afghan-origin drugs. [@2]

GSKN said it had cut off a channel for smuggling a particularly large
consignment of Afghan-origin drugs through Kyrgyzstan. [@1] The service's
investigative unit opened a criminal case and was working to identify others
involved. [@1]
