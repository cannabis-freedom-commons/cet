Three men were arrested during an anti-drug operation in a suburb of
Antananarivo, the Malagasy capital, one of them believed to be a repeat
offender. [@1] They had been unloading suspicious merchandise at Antanimenakely
at around midnight when their behaviour caught the attention of officers from
the service central des stupéfiants, Madagascar's central narcotics service.
[@1]

Madagascar's L'Express newspaper reported that the brigade antidrogue de
Tananarive, the capital's anti-drug brigade, seized more than one tonne of
cannabis in the outskirts of the capital on Saturday 13 July 2013. [@1] The
drugs were being carried in a utility vehicle in 36 sacks. [@2] The first
findings of the investigation suggested the consignment probably came from the
Tuléar region. [@2]
