A female administrative worker at Pointe Blanche Prison in Philipsburg was
arrested on Tuesday morning for having illegal drugs in her possession. [@1] The
worker was identified as Mercedes Wilson, and she was arrested after the
substance was found on her. [@1] Wilson had worked at the prison for 17 years as
a financial assistant. [@1]

When Wilson passed through the scanner twice it beeped, and a hand scanner
passed over her also went off. [@1] She was then strip-searched, and a good
quantity of marijuana was found taped on her back. [@1] The account describes
the load as a large amount of marijuana strapped to her back. [@1]

Deputy Attorney General Taco Stein confirmed that a prison worker was arrested
for having marijuana in her possession while going into work. [@1] Stein could
not confirm the worker's identity or the amount of marijuana found on her. [@1]
Several attempts to reach Prison Director Edward Rohan for confirmation on
Tuesday and Wednesday proved futile. [@1] SMN News also could not reach Chief
Prosecutor Hans Mos for comment on Tuesday evening. [@1]
