Police in Jawzjan province recovered 74 kilograms of hashish from a passenger
vehicle, Deputy Police Chief Col. Syed Zamanudin Hussaini said on Sunday. [@1]
The hashish was packed in cartons and confiscated during a cordon in Shibarghan,
the provincial capital, Hussaini told Pajhwok Afghan News. [@1] One suspect was
arrested in connection with the seizure, which the deputy police chief said came
in cooperation with local people. [@1]

The suspect was being referred to the prosecutor's office, Hussaini said. [@1]
Security forces had seized nearly 300 kilograms of drugs in Jawzjan since the
start of the Afghan calendar year, including heroin, opium and hashish,
according to the account. [@1]
