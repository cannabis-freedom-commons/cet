Two roommates renting a home in Lenoir, Jordan Francois Holder, 23, and
Elizabeth Marie Roden, 23, both students at ASU, were arrested and charged over
an indoor marijuana grow operation found by the Caldwell County Sheriff's
Office. [@1] Holder was arrested in Boone on Monday and Roden turned herself in
on Wednesday, according to the sheriff's narcotics secretary. [@2] Holder was
arrested in Watauga County and Roden in Caldwell County. [@1] Both were charged
with felonious possession with intent to sell/deliver marijuana, felonious
manufacture marijuana, and felonious maintain a dwelling to keep/store a
controlled substance. [@1] Each was issued a $5,000 secured bond. [@1]

A concerned citizen reported that the door to the students' rental home at 5959
Waterfalls Road in Lenoir had been left open for two days with no sign of the
tenants. [@2] Deputies entered the home on March 14 and found numerous marijuana
plants growing in plain view. [@2] Inside, plants were growing in the basement
and in an upstairs bedroom, and drug paraphernalia was found throughout the
home. [@1] Agents with the ICE Unit seized 103 marijuana plants, $1,000 in cash,
growing equipment and supplies, and drug paraphernalia. [@1] In the basement,
deputies also found 50 grams of packaged marijuana. [@2]

The sheriff's office valued the plants at over $275,000 under state guidelines.
[@1] The student newspaper, citing the sheriff's narcotics secretary, put the
total value of the marijuana at $257,500. [@2] Roden made her first appearance
in court on Thursday. [@2]
