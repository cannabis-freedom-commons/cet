On 30 March, police stopped an Opel Astra in Dushanbe during an operation and
detained the two people inside. [@2] The driver was a 40-year-old resident of
Farkhor and the passenger was a 55-year-old resident of Kurgan-Tyube. [@3]

A search of the car's boot uncovered 73 kg of hashish, described as an
especially large consignment. [@1] Police said the two detained men were
suspected of trafficking Afghan drugs to Russia and other CIS countries. [@1]
Investigators established that the two had bought the consignment from a
32-year-old Afghan citizen in order to sell it. [@2] Two other alleged group
members were identified as a 55-year-old Kurgan-Tyube resident and a 48-year-old
resident of Khuroson district who sold drugs. [@2]

The operation was carried out by the Transport Police Directorate of
Tajikistan's Interior Ministry acting on operational information. [@3] A
criminal case was opened and investigative measures were under way. [@3]
Tajikistan lies on a transit route for Afghan drugs moving to Russia and onward
to Eastern and Western Europe. [@1]
