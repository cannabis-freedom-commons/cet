Two individuals were arrested during patrols in the Petecuy neighbourhood of
Cali, Valle, after police found them in possession of bags of marijuana,
according to the National Police. [@1] The operation was carried out by the
National Police Intervention and Anti-Terrorism Unit, known as UNIPOL, in
compliance with the Strategy Against Small-Scale Drug Trafficking. [@1] Police
said the arrest was for the crime of manufacturing, trafficking and possession
of narcotics. [@1]

The 23 white plastic bags recovered at the scene contained 11 kilograms of
marijuana that was ready for sale, police said. [@1]

The two individuals, together with the seized narcotics, were turned over to the
Prosecutor's Office for the aforementioned crime. [@1] Authorities said they
would continue carrying out operations against drug trafficking to strengthen
community safety and security. [@1]
