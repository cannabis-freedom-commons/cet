New Zealand police recovered 10,224 cannabis plants across the Central District
in their annual Cannabis and Crime operation, up from 10,134 the year before.
[@1] Of the total, 5,918 plants came from the aerial operation and 4,306 from
search warrants. [@1] The operation combined intelligence-gathering with an
aerial phase using Air Force Iroquois helicopters to winch officers into plots,
plus a ground phase of 80 search warrants against dealers and cultivators. [@1]

Cannabis grows were found at 108 plots, and police said 122 offenders had been
identified, resulting in 178 charges. [@1] Officers also found two clan labs
and recovered firearms, vehicles, cannabis seeds, ecstasy tablets and
methamphetamine. [@1] Taranaki yielded 1,052 plants from the air, Ruapehu
2,344, Whanganui 1,737 and Manawatu 785. [@1] Operation coordinator Detective
Senior Sergeant Keith Borrell credited public tip-offs, including Crimestoppers
calls, for the result. [@1]
