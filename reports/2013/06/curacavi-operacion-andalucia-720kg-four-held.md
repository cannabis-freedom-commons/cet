After a two-month investigation dubbed Operacion Andalucia, detectives of the
PDI's Metropolitan Anti-Narcotics Brigade working with the Curacavi prosecutor's
office seized the shipment. [@2] Officers intercepted two cars travelling from
Curacavi toward Santiago at kilometre 37 of Route 68 on Thursday 13 June 2013.
[@1] Four Chilean men were arrested in the coordinated operation with the
Mendoza police: Hernan Escobar Vega, Enrique Vargas Paillavil, Claudio Vargas
Vivallos and alleged ringleader Richard Vargas Vivallos. [@2]

The two cars carried 720 kilos of pressed Paraguayan marijuana. [@1] The load
had come from the Argentine city of Mendoza. [@2] The PDI said all of the drugs
were bound for sale in the southern zone of Santiago. [@2] Raids on the
detainees' homes then turned up cocaine, pasta base, 83 ecstasy pills, three
vehicles, three firearms and 23 mobile phones. [@1] Police valued the marijuana
at 3,600 million pesos. [@2]

Mendoza anti-narcotics officers attended the Chilean arrests as observers, with
the Curacavi guarantee court and Mendoza's Federal Court No. 1 collaborating.
[@1] The alleged leader, Richard Vargas Vivallos, had already been arrested in
2004 for bringing in a tonne of pressed Paraguayan marijuana. [@2]
