Vinroy Hylton, a 23-year-old Jamaican national, was arrested at Grantley Adams
International Airport in Barbados on 9 December 2013 and charged with possession
of cannabis, possession with intent to supply, trafficking and importation. [@2]
He had flown in from Kingston, Jamaica, cleared Immigration and Customs and was
suspected of having ingested a controlled drug. [@2] He was referred to the
Queen Elizabeth Hospital, where he passed 33 wrapped packages containing
cannabis he had ingested. [@2]

The cannabis named in the possession charge weighed 1 pound, about 454 grams.
[@2] Hylton was formally charged three days later, on 12 December 2013. [@2]

He appeared before Chief Magistrate Beckles at the Oistins Magistrates Court on
13 December 2013 and pleaded guilty to the charges. [@2] He was fined BDS$5,000
or 18 months' imprisonment. [@1]
