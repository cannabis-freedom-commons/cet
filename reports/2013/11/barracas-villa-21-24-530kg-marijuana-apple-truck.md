The Federal Police's Barrial Prevention corps seized 530 kg of marijuana hidden
in a van carrying apples in Villa 21-24 in the Buenos Aires neighbourhood of
Barracas. [@1] Officers intercepted the van near the settlement after seeing
that its cargo bed carried crates of apples. [@2] When they searched the load,
officers found bricks of chopped marijuana hidden among the fruit so they would
pass unnoticed and reach their destination. [@2]

The morning operation was carried out by the Federal Police's Barrial Prevention
corps, whose officers were pelted with stones by residents during the seizure.
[@3] Spokespeople said one person was detained and the drug was hidden inside a
truck loaded with crates of apples. [@3] National Security Secretary Sergio
Berni confirmed the seizure of 530 kilos of marijuana hidden among apple crates
in an anti-drug operation in Villa Zavaleta in Barracas. [@2] Berni praised the
three officers who took part in the operation at a midday press conference at
Comisaria 32 on Avenida Caseros 2724. [@3]

The 530 kilos were loaded outside Comisaria 32 in Parque Patricios under the
secretary's supervision and taken in a police van to the Federal Police
investigations superintendency for chemical testing and final weighing. [@1] The
same government release presented the Barracas seizure alongside a separate
reported drug-cooking operation in Comodoro Rivadavia, whose drugs and arrests
are not counted in this cannabis report. [@1]
