SLNS Udara, on routine patrol for the Northern Naval Command, stopped a
suspicious Indian dhow north-east of Kachchatheevu Island on 25 April 2013
and found four bags of cannabis hidden inside. [@1] The navy put the haul at
about 100 kg of "Kerala Ganja". [@1,2]

Four Indians were arrested aboard the GPS-fitted dhow, which the navy said
was heading for a pre-arranged handover point. [@1] Two Sri Lankans who
arrived at the spot in a fibreglass dinghy to take the drugs away were
arrested as well. [@1]

The six arrested people, the cannabis and the craft involved were handed over
to Delft Police for further investigation. [@1]
