Police assigned to the Potrerillo station in Pasto arrested a 38-year-old man in
the Santa Clara neighbourhood. [@1] The arrest followed a citizen tip that the
man appeared to be selling a suspicious substance at one of the entrances to the
Potrerillo market. [@1] Officers went to the indicated location and found the
man described by the complainant. [@1] After a personal search, officers found a
black bag inside his jacket containing a plant substance identified as
marijuana. [@1]

Officers seized 275 grams of marijuana in total, an amount police said was
enough to arrest him for trafficking, manufacturing or possession of narcotics.
[@1] A background check showed the man was under house arrest and was violating
it by being outside his residence. [@1] In addition to the drug charge, he was
also charged with escape for breaching that measure. [@1]

In the operation reported from San Juan de Pasto, police captured the
38-year-old man for drug trafficking and prison escape. [@1] Police presented
the arrest as part of their work against micro-trafficking and enforcement of
judicial measures to protect public safety. [@1]
