Police in Yerevan detained a 34-year-old resident of Abovyan on 25 November 2024
on suspicion of keeping, using and selling illegal drugs. [@1] Officers picked
him up on Aram Khachatryan Street in Yerevan at 13:50 and took him to the
Arabkir police division, the Investigative Committee said. [@2] A search of his
pockets found 23 packages wrapped in coloured adhesive tape with a total weight
of 42.88 grams. [@2]

He told officers the packages held marijuana he had bought through a Telegram
channel in order to sell it. [@2] Forensic examination found marijuana in
samples taken from the packages, with testing of the remaining material
continuing. [@2] A criminal case was opened under article 393, part 2, point 3,
and article 396, part 2, of the Armenian Criminal Code. [@2] He was charged
under article 393, part 3, point 2, of the Criminal Code. [@2] He did not admit
guilt and, using his procedural status, refused to testify. [@1]

A motion was filed with the Yerevan court of first instance of general
jurisdiction to hold him in custody for two months. [@1] The court instead
placed him under house arrest for two months combined with bail of 500,000
drams. [@1] The preliminary investigation was continuing. [@1]
