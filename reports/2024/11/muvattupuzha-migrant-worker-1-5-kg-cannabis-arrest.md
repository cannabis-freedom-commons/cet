A worker from another state was arrested with one and a half kilograms of
cannabis that had been brought for sale to Kalachantha Road, near the
Muvattupuzha market. [@1] Muvattupuzha police made the arrest. [@1] The arrested
man is 47 years old and comes from Merampur in Murshidabad district, West
Bengal. [@1] He had lived at the Muvattupuzha market for 12 years and worked at
an onion wholesale shop there. [@1]

He had been under the surveillance of the police crime squad. [@1] The arrest
was carried out by an investigation team led by Inspector S. Jayakrishnan. [@1]
The team included sub-inspectors Vishnu Raju, C. Jayakumar and M.M. Ubaise,
senior civil police officers C.K. Meeran, Bibil Mohan, K.A. Anas and Shan
Muhammed, and civil police officer Faisal. [@1]

Police said the investigation was continuing. [@1]
