One man was arrested in Ambalanomby, Morondava, after an anonymous tip exposed a
large cannabis trafficking operation. [@1] The operation was carried out by the
gendarmerie group of Madagascar's Menabe region. [@1] The owner was identified
as a trafficker heading a network involved in producing, transporting and
selling drugs, with his product sold mainly on Morondava's black market. [@1]
Officers took him by surprise before he could leave the scene. [@1]

Inside a wooden cabin, officers seized fifteen woven polyethylene bags holding
hundreds of kilograms of Indian hemp (chanvre indien). [@1] The bags were filled
to the brim and the cannabis was already prepared and packaged, awaiting
shipment. [@1] The entire cargo was loaded into a 4x4 pickup to be taken to
Morondava. [@1] A separate account described the substance as cannabis indica
and reported that gendarmes seized a significant quantity and arrested one man.
[@2]

The gendarmerie said it acted on intelligence and had launched the operation
after being put on the trafficker's trail. [@1] The man was being questioned in
preliminary investigations, with his referral to the prosecutor expected in the
coming days. [@1]
