Drug-control officers of the Berezovsky interior ministry department in
Krasnoyarsk Krai detained an Armenian citizen born in 1983 on suspicion of
cultivating and storing narcotic substances. [@1] The detainee was a 41-year-old
Armenian national. [@2] He was legally in Russia and had converted a garage box
in Berezovka into a small cannabis plantation, creating all the conditions
needed to grow the plants. [@2] Police said the plantation came to their
attention and officers arrived at the garage without warning. [@1]

Officers seized four cannabis bushes and 900 grams of marijuana from the garage,
the regional interior ministry said. [@1] The regional press service gave the
same figures of four bushes and 900 grams of marijuana. [@3] The man confessed,
and a criminal case was opened under part 2 of article 228 of the Russian
Criminal Code for unlawful possession of plants containing narcotic substances
on a particularly large scale. [@1] His measure of restraint was a written
undertaking not to leave and behave properly, and he faces up to three years in
prison. [@1] He was also ordered to pay a 4,000-ruble fine for illegally
cultivating plants containing narcotic substances. [@1]
