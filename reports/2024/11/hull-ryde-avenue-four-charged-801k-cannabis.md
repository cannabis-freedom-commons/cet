Humberside Police officers executed a warrant at a residential property in Ryde
Avenue, off Clough Road in Hull, on Friday 15 November 2024. [@2] Pellumb
Cunhaji, 30, Glerdi Koceku, 19, Zani Plaka, 22, and Sajmir Spahiu, 33, all
described as of no fixed abode, were charged at Hull Magistrates' Court on
Saturday with cultivating cannabis. [@1] Spahiu was also charged with possession
of a knife in a public place. [@1]

Officers found cannabis with an estimated street value of £801,000 at the Hull
property. [@1] Police found the drugs together with two machetes when they
searched the Ryde Avenue premises during the warrant. [@1] Hull Live described
officers discovering an estimated £801,000 worth of cannabis throughout the
property along with the two machetes. [@2]

A plea hearing for the case was set for 18 December at Hull Crown Court. [@1]
Hull Live reported that three people had appeared in court in connection with
the same find. [@2] All three individuals in its account were remanded into
custody to appear at court on Saturday 16 November. [@2]
