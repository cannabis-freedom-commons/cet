Operative Customs officers working with the drug-control unit of the Partizansky
district criminal police in Minsk seized 3.5 kg of marijuana from a 47-year-old
previously convicted resident of Minsk District. [@1] The man was detained
during the operation. [@1]

During questioning the man said he had grown the marijuana himself on his
household plot and kept it at home for his own consumption. [@1] Customs
officers established during operative-search measures that he had bought the
cannabis seeds for growing through a foreign website. [@1] Experts of the State
Committee of Forensic Examinations confirmed that the seized batch was
marijuana. [@1]

The detainee had previously been convicted of illegal sale of narcotics under
part 2 of Article 328 of the Criminal Code of Belarus and had served two years
in prison. [@1] On the basis of the Operative Customs materials, the Minsk
District department of the Investigative Committee opened a criminal case
against him under part 1 of Article 328 for unlawful manufacture, processing,
acquisition, storage, transportation or transfer of narcotics without intent to
sell. [@1] The charge carries a penalty of two to five years of imprisonment.
