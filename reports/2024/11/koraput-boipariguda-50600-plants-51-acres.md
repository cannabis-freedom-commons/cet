Law enforcement officers destroyed over 50,600 hemp plants across 51 acres of
land in the Boipariguda area of Koraput district, Odisha, on Tuesday. [@1] A
joint team of police and excise officers, supported by district voluntary forces
and local labourers, carried out the operation in Raginiguda, Tal Tikarpada and
Upper Tikarpada under G Majhiguda panchayat. [@1]

Ankit Verma, the sub-divisional police officer at Jeypore, said most of the
destroyed plants were fully grown and more than five feet high. [@1] Reports
suggest tribal communities cultivated the hemp with support from traders in
neighbouring Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, West Bengal and Chhattisgarh, who
supplied hybrid seeds and funding. [@1]

Cannabis cultivation typically takes place between October and November and
between January and February, mainly in Maoist-affected villages in Lamataput,
Nandapur and Boipariguda blocks. [@1] Verma said earlier actions had been
sporadic, that the authorities now aimed to conduct operations over a longer
period and that raids would intensify in the coming days. [@1]
