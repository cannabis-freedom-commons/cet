Officers from the Nuneaton & Bedworth Safer Neighbourhood Team carried out a
warrant on a property on Camp Hill Road, Nuneaton, on 6 November 2024. [@1]
Inside, they found a number of cannabis products, including edibles, resin and
herbal cannabis. [@1] Officers also found £7,000 in cash, which police suspected
was connected to the supply of drugs. [@1] A 24-year-old man from Nuneaton was
arrested on suspicion of possession with intent to supply and money laundering.
[@1] He was taken into custody for questioning. [@3]

PC Jonny Walker of Nuneaton SNT said intelligence-led action was the best way to
keep illegal drug sales out of communities, along with the violence, gangs and
misery that come with them. [@1] He urged residents worried about a nearby
property being used by criminals to report it through the police website. [@1]

The BBC put the cash at more than £7,000. [@2] Warwickshire Police images of the
raid showed bags of cannabis, scales, cash and packaging. [@2]
