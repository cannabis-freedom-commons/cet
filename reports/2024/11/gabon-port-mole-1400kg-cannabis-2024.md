Gabonese customs officers searching a ship from Togo at Libreville's Port-Môle
found 1,561 cannabis tablets weighing 1.4 tonnes on 4 November 2024. [@1,5] The tablets were hidden in shoes packed into sealed cartons that arrived from Lomé, in Togo. [@5] The
ministry valued the cargo at 140 million CFA francs. [@1]

Customs seized the boat along with its passengers and captain, who were placed
under investigation. [@3,5] No one came forward to collect the consignment, though a customs declaration had been filed. [@5]

Mays Mouissi said a judicial investigation had been opened to identify everyone
involved in the trafficking. [@2] Mays Mouissi said the cannabis would be destroyed within 24 hours. [@5] On 13 November police circulated a wanted
notice for the suspected owner of the load. [@4] The minister announced tighter
controls at maritime borders to deter traffickers and protect the population.
[@2] The load was intended for consumption in snack bars, nightclubs, hotels and schools. [@5]
