Algeria's National People's Army (ANP), with the security services, intercepted
more than nine quintaux of kif traité, treated cannabis, between 6 and 12
November 2024. [@1] That is more than 900 kilograms of treated cannabis, taking
one quintal as 100 kilograms. [@1] The operations led to the arrest of 32
narcotrafiquants, or drug traffickers. [@1]

The seizures came in a week of operations along the border with Morocco,
described as more than 1,500 kilometres long and a nerve centre of drug
trafficking. [@1] Algerian authorities acknowledge that the border cannot be
made fully impermeable. [@1] Traffickers use clandestine routes and even drones
to bring loads in from Morocco. [@1]

In Algeria the Moroccan product is known in popular slang as “marocaïne”. [@1]
The 2022 UNODC World Drug Report ranked Morocco first among world cannabis
producers, with more than 700 tonnes a year. [@1] Experts put THC levels in
recent seizures at up to 50%, a rise attributed to Moroccan growers' use of
high-yield hybrid varieties. [@1]
