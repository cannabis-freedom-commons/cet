PSP officers detained two unemployed men, aged 24 and 26 and living in Porto, in
Bairro de São Tomé, a neighbourhood in Porto, on suspicion of drug trafficking.
[@1] Both men were remanded in preventive detention, the most serious coercive
measure, after a first judicial interrogation. [@1] Police said the operation
took place at about 14:30, when officers of the Criminal Investigation Division
acted on complaints of drug trafficking in the area and located the suspects,
seeing several users walking up to them. [@1]

The PSP said the two suspects were working together to sell narcotics and that
officers arrested them in the act. [@1] Officers seized about 300 doses of
hashish and cocaine together, plus about 1,850 euros that police said came from
trafficking. [@1]

Both suspects had already been indicated for the same crime in separate
proceedings. [@1]
