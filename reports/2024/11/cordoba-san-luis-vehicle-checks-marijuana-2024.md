Gendarmerie officers of the Escuadrón Seguridad Vial Santa Catalina stopped a
car coming from Villa Dolores at kilometre 72 of Ruta Nacional 30 near Santa
Catalina, Córdoba. [@1] The car was heading toward Río Cuarto, and officers
found the occupant carrying a backpack holding 55,000 US dollars without
supporting documents. [@1] The Federal Court of Río Cuarto ordered the foreign
currency seized and kept the person subject to the case for alleged breach of
article 303 of Argentina's Penal Code. [@1]

In a second check, officers inspected a vehicle travelling from Buenos Aires to
San Luis. [@1] In an inner compartment of the vehicle behind the driver's seat,
they found a jar and a bag holding plant material. [@1] Field Narcotest checks
on the substance came back chromatically positive for 18.6 grams of marijuana.
[@1] Through the provincial anti-narcotics prosecution office, the FPA directed
that proceedings be carried out and the substances seized. [@1]

At the La Punilla toll on Provincial Route 1 in San Luis, La Punta road-safety
officers seized 4 grams of cannabis sativa being carried among a couple's
luggage. [@1] The San Luis federal prosecution unit ordered the substance
confiscated, proceedings drawn up, and the people to remain free but subject to
the case if they had no restrictive measures. [@1]
