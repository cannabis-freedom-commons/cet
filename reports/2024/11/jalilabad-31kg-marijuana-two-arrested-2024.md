Azerbaijan's Interior Ministry said officers of its Main Directorate for
Combating Drugs detained two members of an organised group, Jeyhun Abbasguliyev
and Rahshan Mamedov. [@1] Officers seized 31 kilograms of marijuana from the two
men, which the ministry described as enriched with psychotropic substances. [@1]

According to the ministry, the pair planned to take the large consignment from a
cache in Jalilabad district. [@1] The supplier was an Iranian citizen whose
identity investigators were still establishing. [@1] The detainees were couriers
for the Iranian citizen, police said. [@3] The group intended to sell the
marijuana at various addresses, the ministry said. [@1]

A criminal case was opened over the seizure and a court ordered the two
detainees held in pre-trial detention. [@2] Police operations to identify and
prosecute other members of the group were continuing. [@1]
