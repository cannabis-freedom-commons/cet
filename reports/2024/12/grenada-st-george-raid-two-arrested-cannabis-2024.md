Royal Grenada Police Force officers arrested two men in St George's early on 4
December 2024 during an operation that executed about 12 search warrants at
private homes. [@1] Jahbarry Gilbert, a 44-year-old businessman from Calliste,
St George, was taken into custody after 5.14 pounds (about 2.33 kg) of cannabis,
EC$6,042.74 and US$12 were found in his possession. [@1] Gilbert was arrested
for trafficking in a controlled drug and money laundering. [@1] At the home of
36-year-old Devol Phillip of Mt Toute, officers found 11 grams of cocaine with a
street value of EC$1,100. [@1] The same search recovered 103 grams of cannabis,
which police valued at EC$515, and EC$10,300 in cash. [@1] Phillip was detained
for trafficking in a controlled drug and money laundering. [@1]

In Grand Anse Valley, officers uprooted six cannabis trees between 1 and 3 feet
tall and found 35 grams of cannabis, and no one was arrested. [@1] Another 7.50
grams of cannabis were found in Marian, St George, with no arrest. [@1] In
Woburn, South St George, police found 67 grams of cannabis and made no arrest.
[@1] The newspaper reported that warrants at homes in Grand Anse Valley,
Calliste and Marian found nothing illegal. [@1]

The operation lasted nearly four hours and involved officers from the Criminal
Investigation Department, the Drug Squad and uniformed officers from stations
across the island. [@1] The newspaper linked the operation to the return to
active duty of Assistant Superintendent Raymond "Beast" Matthew after illness,
which it said had brought stepped-up operations by the force. [@1] Matthew said
he would target areas known for illegal activity, especially the sale of drugs
such as crack cocaine. [@1]
