Oklahoma Bureau of Narcotics Marijuana Enforcement Teams served a search warrant
this week at a marijuana growing operation on County Road 1560 in Stonewall,
Pontotoc County, about 11 miles east of Ada. [@1] The agency said the operation
was part of an investigation into cultivation of marijuana without the required
OBN registration. [@1] The enforcement targeted a single grower facility in the
Stonewall area of Pontotoc County, Oklahoma. [@1]

Agents seized 1,638 marijuana plants from the grow operation on County Road
1560. [@2] They also seized seven pounds, about 3.18 kilograms, of processed
marijuana at the same operation. [@2] Personnel from the Oklahoma Highway Patrol
and the Pontotoc County Sheriff's Office assisted OBN agents in the search and
seizure operation. [@1]

OBN said arrests were pending as the investigation moved forward. [@1] No
suspects were named and no charges had been announced in the accounts of the
operation. [@2] The agency said the investigation was continuing. [@2]
