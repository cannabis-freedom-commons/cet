Kentucky State Police arrested University of Kentucky football player Jordan
Lovett in Nelson County on Dec. 30, 2024, and charged him with reckless driving
and possession of marijuana after the citation said he had been racing another
vehicle. [@1] The arrest citation identified Lovett as a junior defensive back
for UK. [@3]

The citation detailed that troopers caught two vehicles on the parkway near mile
marker 26 traveling at over 110 mph. [@1] One of the vehicles slowed down and
was stopped, and its driver was identified in the citation as Lovett, while
troopers attempted to locate the other vehicle he was allegedly racing. [@1] A
trooper clocked both vehicles at over 110 mph and said Lovett at one point
exceeded 120 mph. [@2] When the trooper pulled Lovett over, he said he smelled
marijuana and found some in the car. [@2]

Lovett was released on Dec. 31, and his arraignment was scheduled for Jan. 7.
