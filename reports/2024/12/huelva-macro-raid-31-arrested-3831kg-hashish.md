A Guardia Civil macro-operation in Huelva province left up to 31 people
detained. [@1] A later account put the 31 detentions at 25 in Huelva, two in
Seville, two in Algeciras, one in Granada and one in Malaga, for alleged
drug-trafficking, criminal-organisation, weapons, money-laundering, smuggling,
forgery, receiving and vehicle-theft offences. [@2] The operation aimed to
dismantle a major criminal organisation active in cocaine and hashish
trafficking. [@1]

About 300 Guardia Civil officers took part, alongside Colombian and Panamanian
police officers through the GDIN intelligence-sharing project. [@1] Officers
carried out 18 searches for drugs and people connected with trafficking. [@1]
The searches covered Huelva city, Moguer, Trigueros, Gibraleon, Beas and San
Juan del Puerto. [@1] Officials later said the 18 searches spanned ten
municipalities in four provinces, with cooperation from the US DEA, Colombia,
Europol, France and Portugal, Guardia Civil units and Customs Surveillance. [@1]

Across the investigation's actions officers seized 278 kilograms of cocaine,
3,831 kilograms of hashish and 180 cannabis plants, plus more than 500,000 euros
in cash and several weapons of war. [@2] The first-day account reported 29 kilos
of cocaine and hashish combined, plus properties, more than ten vehicles and
firearms. [@1] The proceedings were declared secret and are directed by Huelva's
Instruction Court No. 5 and the Huelva anti-drugs prosecutor. [@1]
