Police arrested six men on Christmas Day 2024 across Fiji for possession of
illicit drugs. [@1] FBC News confirms six people were arrested on Christmas Day
across various locations in Fiji after being found with illicit drugs. [@2]

A 38-year-old unemployed man from Nabua was found with seven sachets of dried
leaves wrapped in aluminum foil, believed to be marijuana. [@1] A 24-year-old
unemployed man from Qauia, Lami, was found with a branch of dried leaves
believed to be marijuana after being searched by officers. [@1] A 52-year-old
man from Nakelo, Tailevu, was found with 20 sachets of dried leaves wrapped in
aluminum foil, believed to be marijuana. [@1] A 50-year-old unemployed man from
Tomuka, Lautoka, was arrested for possessing five sachets of dried leaves
wrapped in aluminum foil, believed to be marijuana. [@1] Two men aged 44 and 40
from Vusuya, Nausori, were arrested in Valelevu with two zip-lock plastic bags
of a crystal-like substance believed to be methamphetamine, which is not counted
in the cannabis account. [@1]

Assistant Commissioner of Police Operations Livai Driu said police conducted
drug checks throughout Christmas Day and would continue monitoring picnic spots
and communities. [@1] All six people were in police custody awaiting drug
analysis as investigations continued. [@1]
