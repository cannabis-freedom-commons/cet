Police in Dharan arrested 26-year-old Shyam Kumar Bishwakarma from Sundar
Haraicha Municipality-11, Morang, while the driver of the second vehicle fled.
[@1] The arrested suspect Bishwakarma is being charged under drug trafficking
laws, with preparations under way for a court extension of his remand. [@1]
Police said a manhunt was under way to locate the driver who abandoned his
vehicle and escaped. [@1]

Police recovered the cannabis from two Scorpio SUVs with registration numbers Ba
1 Jha 5127 and Ba 1 Jha 4605. [@1] From the vehicle Ba 1 Jha 5127 police seized
365 kilograms of cannabis found in plastic-wrapped packages. [@1] From the
vehicle Ba 1 Jha 4605 police seized 335 kg of contraband in plastic packaging.
[@1] Police gave the total weight of the seized cannabis, including plastic
packaging, as 700 kilograms. [@1] The vehicles were impounded on Tuesday evening
near Amarhat in Dharan-13 while en route from Dhankuta to Dharan. [@1]

To bring the situation under control, police fired four rounds of gunshots in
the air. [@1] One round struck the rear of the fleeing vehicle before police
surrounded and seized both vehicles. [@1] Dharan Area Police Office officers
said they were compelled to open fire when the fleeing vehicle posed a threat to
security authorities. [@1] Police were investigating whether the seized vehicles
were genuine government property or were using fake government number plates.
[@1]
