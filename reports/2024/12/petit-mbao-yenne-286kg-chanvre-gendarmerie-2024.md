Gendarmerie brigades from Yenne and the Zone Franche Industrielle carried out
interception operations on the nights of 17 and 18 December 2024. [@1] Officers
intercepted 286 kilograms of chanvre indien, the Senegalese term for cannabis,
packed in plastic sacks. [@1] The Yenne brigade recovered 216 kg and the Zone
Franche Industrielle brigade 70 kg. [@1] The cannabis was intercepted along the
beaches of Petit Mbao and Yenne, where the gendarmerie had stepped up
surveillance. [@1]

Officers also seized two vehicles used in the transport, a Rav4 registered in
Dakar and a Peugeot 307 registered in Thiès. [@1] The gendarmerie said
investigations were continuing to identify and dismantle the network behind the
operation. [@1] Enquête Plus attributed the account to a communiqué from the
gendarmerie's communication division. [@3]

The Dakaractu account, published on 20 December 2024, gave the same total and
split it between the two brigades. [@2] Le Dakarois named the two impounded
vehicles as a Rav4 registered in Dakar and a Peugeot 307 registered in Thiès.
[@4] The gendarmerie high command presented the seizures as part of a series of
operations to secure Senegal's coastline and disrupt criminal networks. [@1]
