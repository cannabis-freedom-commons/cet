Police in San Bartolo, a beach district on Lima's southern coast, arrested three
siblings accused of selling drugs from a house on Calle La Ermita. [@3] The
operation took place on Wednesday 18 December 2024 and was carried out by the
Escuadrón Verde, the Grupo Terna AntiDrogas unit of the National Police and San
Bartolo's municipal security service. [@3] It was supervised by the provincial
criminal prosecutor's office of Lurín. [@3] An officer of the Grupo Terna
dressed as the Grinch broke down the door of the house with a hammer so the
other officers could enter. [@1] Police named the alleged ringleader as Eva
Espinoza Maza, known as 'La Reina del Sur', and said she had been caught three
times before for selling drugs in San Bartolo. [@2] Her siblings Norma and Fidel
Espinoza Maza were detained in the same raid, the National Police said. [@2]

Inside the house officers seized what the National Police described as a large
quantity of marijuana. [@1] El Telégrafo reported that doses of coca paste and
marijuana were seized in the house. [@4] The San Bartolo district government's
account of the search lists 1,280 wrappers of coca paste and 16 small bags of
cocaine hydrochloride, which are not counted as cannabis. [@3]

The National Police said the Escuadrón Verde had broken up a criminal band known
as 'La mafia de San Bartolo', which sold drugs across the southern beach
resorts. [@1] Colonel Carlos López, head of the Escuadrón Verde, said the unit
had learned of a place where drugs were sold 24 hours a day. [@2] The three were
handed to the police and were to be investigated for drug trafficking. [@2] The
Grinch is one of the disguises the Grupo Terna uses at Christmas; its officers
have also dressed as Santa Claus, Disney characters and Marvel superheroes to
make arrests. [@1]
