Argentine Naval Prefecture officers detained three people and seized a shipment
of more than 3,600 kilograms of marijuana in Puerto Rico, Misiones. [@1] The
three detained were the driver of the pickup truck that had tried to flee, the
man guarding the warehouse and a third man who came to the scene and lives in
the adjoining house. [@1]

The operation began when maritime authority patrols spotted a pickup truck
coming from the riverbank at kilometre 1739 of the Paraná River, by Puerto Río y
Sol, and the vehicle fled on seeing police. [@1] After a controlled pursuit
through the town and a cordon operation, officers intercepted the vehicle as it
tried to enter a warehouse. [@1] Inside the truck and stored on the premises
officers found 121 bundles. [@1] The search produced more than 3,600 kilograms
of marijuana divided into 5,443 panes, or bricks, which the authorities valued
at more than 9,500 million pesos. [@1]

Another account said the operation was coordinated by the national Ministry of
Security. [@2] The operation was named 'Gordo de Navidad'. [@2] The Federal
Court of Oberá took charge of the investigation and will determine the suspects'
criminal responsibility. [@3]
