Gendarmes of Escuadrón 11 “San Ignacio”, in its Puerto Rico section, were
patrolling on foot with a dog team at the port called Rinkel when they saw a
small boat on the Paraná River moving away towards Paraguay. [@1] The canoe was
uncrewed and held three black bundles which, when brought near the dog Oma
accompanying the patrol, drew the animal's reaction indicating marijuana. [@1]

Counting and weighing established that the bundles held 112 panes, or pressed
blocks, of marijuana weighing 73.020 kg. [@1] The Gendarmería valued the load at
$172,318,475. [@1] The prosecutor's office and the court in Oberá intervened in
the case. [@1] The seizure was made by the Gendarmería Nacional Argentina within
the anti-drug-trafficking effort promoted by the national Ministry of Security.
[@1]
