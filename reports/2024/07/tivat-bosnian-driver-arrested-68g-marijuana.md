Police in Tivat arrested a citizen of Bosnia and Herzegovina identified only as
N.Š., who has temporary residence in the town, during a traffic check late on 26
July 2024. [@1] Officers of the Tivat Security Department, part of the Regional
Security Centre South, stopped his vehicle at about 22:00. [@2] The Police
Administration said the control was carried out under enhanced measures aimed at
people of operational interest and traffic participants. [@1]

Officers found 68 grams of a substance suspected to be marijuana. [@2] A breath
test showed that N.Š. had been driving with 1.75 g/kg of alcohol in his body.
[@2] He was arrested on suspicion of unauthorised production, possession and
distribution of narcotic drugs. [@1] The drug suspicion was handled as a
criminal offence, while a misdemeanour report was filed against him under the
Road Traffic Safety Law. [@1] The Police Administration identified the arrested
man only by his initials, N.Š. [@2]
