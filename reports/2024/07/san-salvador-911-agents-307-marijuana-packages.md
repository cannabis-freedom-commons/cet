El Salvador's Attorney General's Office (FGR) said on 24 July 2024 that it had
searched a home in San Salvador and found 307 packages of marijuana linked to
two police agents. [@1] The officers were César Edgardo Vásquez and Romeo Vargas
Rivera, both assigned to the 911 emergency service in San Salvador. [@2] They
had been stopped on the night of 22 July on the road to Quezaltepeque after a
pursuit from western El Salvador. [@1] The National Civil Police said it
detained the two agents in La Libertad department after finding a package of
drug inside their vehicle. [@3] The arrest was made near the El Jabalí
racetrack, in the caserío Milagro de la Roca of the cantón Primavera, according
to El Mundo. [@6] Police seized the two vehicles the agents were travelling in.
[@5] La Prensa Gráfica reported that the drug in the car was not identified at
the time of the arrest. [@1]

The FGR carried out the search under a house-search warrant on properties of the
two officers. [@4] The 307 packages weighed 56 kilograms, according to the FGR's
account in El Mundo. [@6] The FGR said the two distributed marijuana at
different points in the capital. [@1]

The FGR presented its charge sheet against both officers before the Sixth Court
against Organized Crime in San Salvador. [@6] They are accused of illicit
trafficking and illicit association, and Vásquez also faces a charge of
aggravated resistance. [@6] The FGR asked the court for pre-trial detention.
[@6] La Página reported that the charge sheet was presented on the afternoon of
5 August 2024. [@7] Preliminary information indicated that the arrest was joint
work by the PNC's Anti-Narcotics Division (DAN) and the US Drug Enforcement
Administration (DEA). [@1] The PNC said it would not tolerate an officer who
stains the uniform and betrays the public's trust. [@2] La Prensa Gráfica asked
the PNC for more information and had received no reply by publication. [@1]
