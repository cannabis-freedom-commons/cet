Jordan's Public Security Department said its Anti-Narcotics Department arrested
18 people involved in drug trafficking and smuggling across the Kingdom in the
days before 8 July 2024. [@1] Roya News reported that the arrests came in seven
drug cases across various regions of Jordan. [@4] A joint security campaign in
Mafraq Governorate and the Northern Badia arrested nine people, some described
as dangerous, and seized narcotics and firearms. [@1] In Mafraq, officers
stopped an attempt to smuggle 50,000 pills and arrested two suspects with a
vehicle fitted with a hidden compartment. [@1] In Ramtha, a resident linked by
police to regional drug dealers was arrested with 8 kg of crystal
methamphetamine and 14,000 pills. [@5] Two other people were detained in Ramtha
with half a kilogram of crystal methamphetamine. [@3]

In Madaba Governorate, a suspect was found with 10 blocks of hashish and 3,000
pills. [@2] In southern Amman, a raid led to the arrest of two dealers and the
seizure of narcotics and live ammunition. [@1] In Karak Governorate, a dealer
was arrested with 5,000 pills after a raid. [@1]

The Public Security Department said all the suspects were referred to the State
Security Court prosecutor for further questioning and indictment. [@5]
