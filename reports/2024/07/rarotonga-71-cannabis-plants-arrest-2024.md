Cook Islands police arrested a 49-year-old man caught with 71 cannabis plants.
[@1,2] Police investigators searched his residence in Arorangi before the arrest on 19 July 2024. [@2] Police strategic and media advisor Trevor Pitt said the man was arrested
and charged on Friday over what he described as a well-constructed and organised
crop of cannabis seedlings. [@1] He faced a charge of cultivating cannabis. [@2] He was remanded into prison on Saturday after
appearing in court. [@1,2]

He was to appear in court again the following week. [@1] Pitt said the bench warrant would remain in effect until he returns to the Cook Islands. [@2] The case was adjourned to 25 October 2024. [@2] A bench warrant has been in effect since 4 September 2024 for the defendant, who left the country sometime in late August. [@2] Police were advised by defence counsel that he had left the country for either New Zealand or Australia. [@2] The Chief Justice granted bail, ordering him to surrender his passport and not to leave Rarotonga, and the case was called again on 30 August 2024. [@2]

The same police roundup reported three rental-car crashes on Rarotonga roads
over the past weekend. [@1] It also reported the separate arrest of a man
charged with assaulting a child and a woman after a domestic incident on
Saturday night. [@1]
