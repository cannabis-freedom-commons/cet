Spanish police broke up an international drug-trafficking group that produced
and sold drugs in Spain and included citizens of Serbia, Croatia, Armenia and
other countries. [@1] The group operated in at least three Spanish provinces.
[@1] Police arrested 10 members of the group. [@1]

Officers seized more than 3,000 cannabis plants. [@1] They also seized 85
kilograms of finished cannabis product ready for sale. [@1] Officers also seized
weapons and ammunition. [@1]

The group used agricultural and livestock buildings to grow large quantities of
marijuana. [@1]
