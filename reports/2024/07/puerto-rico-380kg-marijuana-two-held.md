Prefectura Naval Argentina officers carrying out control operations in Puerto
Rico, Misiones, spotted several people handling bundles along a path. [@1] The
officers approached the suspects, gave the order to stop and immediately
detained two men. [@1]

Officers then found a boat moored on the coast holding multiple packages, which
suspects had been loading via a leafy path. [@1] They inspected the boat and
found 380 kilograms of marijuana divided into 478 bricks packed in 19 boxes.
[@1] Official sources put the load's value at 900 million pesos. [@1]

The Federal Court in Oberá ordered the seizure of the goods and the detention of
those involved. [@1] Argentina's official Prefectura account confirms two men
were detained and more than 380 kilograms of marijuana were seized in Puerto
Rico. [@2] That account says the load was 478 bricks in 19 boxes worth more than
900 million pesos, under the Oberá court of Miguel Ángel Guerrero and María
Virginia Guida. [@2] A third account repeats that the coastal boat held 478
panes in 19 boxes and the same Oberá court ordered the seizure and detentions.
[@3]
