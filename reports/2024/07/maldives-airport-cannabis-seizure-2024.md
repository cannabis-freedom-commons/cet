Maldives Customs officers found over 15 kg of cannabis in the luggage of a 44-year-old Malaysian national who arrived at Velana International Airport on Emirates flight EK656 from Dubai on the morning of 27 July 2024. [@1,3] Police valued the seizure at 12.3 million rufiyaa, and initial tests confirmed the substance was cannabis. [@1,3]

The police Drug Enforcement team then ran a controlled delivery, which led to the arrest of a second suspect, a 41-year-old Malaysian man, that night. [@1,3] Both men were remanded for five days by the Criminal Court while the investigation continued. [@1,3]

Police said the investigation pointed to an international trafficking network with links to people from four countries, and called the haul the largest cannabis seizure in the Maldives that year. [@1,3] Maldives Customs later put the cannabis seized in this case at 15.38 kg. [@2]
