Police officers from the Międzyrzec Podlaski station detained a 41-year-old man
living in Gmina Drelów, in the Lublin region, after a member of his family
called them to a domestic incident, and charged him with possessing marijuana
and cultivating cannabis. [@1] When officers reached the property they found
cannabis plants other than fibre hemp growing in the vegetable garden, and the
man told them he had cultivated them for his own use. [@2] Before the patrol
arrived the man had tried to conceal part of the crop in a cornfield, a detail
the police press officer confirmed. [@3] The plants that remained were secured
by criminal investigators and the man was taken to a police detention cell. [@1]

The initial call concerned a family quarrel in which the resident was reported
to be aggressive. [@3] During the search officers also seized dried plant
material at the man's home, and a preliminary examination confirmed it was
marijuana. [@2] The police said the man now faces a maximum sentence of three
years' imprisonment. [@4]
