Free State police officers arrested two men who were allegedly driving a bakkie
full of dagga in Bloemfontein on Monday. [@1] The Bloemfontein Highway Patrol
apprehended the two men early on Monday morning, 1 July 2024, while they were
transporting dagga on the N1 highway. [@2] Provincial spokesperson Sergeant
Mahlomola Kareli said the suspects, aged 23 and 25, were arrested during a
highway patrol on the N1. [@1]

While patrolling and conducting stop and search, Bloemfontein highway patrollers
spotted a white Toyota Hilux bakkie with a canopy and GP registration heading
towards Colesburg. [@1] Police followed the vehicle and safely stopped it near a
farm on the N1. [@1] Two males alighted from the bakkie to be searched and,
while searching the vehicle, members found 12 compressed dagga packages sealed
with brown cellotape, which police valued at an estimated R1.7 million. [@1]

Sergeant Kareli said the men have been charged with dealing in dagga, and the
driver faced an additional charge of driving without a driver's license. [@1]
They are expected to appear in the Springfontein Magistrate's Court soon. [@1]
