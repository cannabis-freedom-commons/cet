Judiciary Police spokesman Ho Chan Nam told a special press conference that a
local couple were arrested on Tuesday after one of them collected a parcel
containing marijuana buds from a pickup point in Nam Van. [@1] The two are
surnamed Leong and Lou, aged 31 and 28 respectively. [@1] Leong is a clerk at an
insurance company, while Lou said she works in the marketing department of a
local company. [@1]

The Judiciary Police had recently received a notification from Hong Kong customs
about suspicious parcels addressed to Leong that were being forwarded to Macau
from North America. [@1] When Leong tried to collect the parcel in Nam Van, PJ
officers intercepted him and found six packages of marijuana buds wrapped in
double-layered foil and disguised as fruit tea. [@1] At the apartment police
found drug-taking paraphernalia including a water pipe and arrested Leong's
girlfriend, Lou. [@1] Police seized a total of 117.4 grams of marijuana buds,
which they valued at an estimated street price of 117,000 patacas. [@1]

Under questioning both admitted a history of drug use, but subsequent drug tests
were negative. [@1] The couple were transferred to the Public Prosecutions
Office (MP), both facing drug-related charges. [@1]
