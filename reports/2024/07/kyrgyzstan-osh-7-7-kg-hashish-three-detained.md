Kyrgyzstan's State Committee for National Security (GKNB) detained the organiser
of an international drug channel, identified only as U.D.U. and born in 1960,
during a transaction caught in the act on 8 July 2024. [@1] The buyer U.B.Ch.,
who meant to carry the drugs onward to Uzbekistan, was detained in the same
operation. [@2] A third man, J.Kh.T., born in 1961 and previously convicted of
drug sales, weighed and packaged the consignments at his home. [@1] All three
confessed to storing narcotics with intent to sell and were placed in the GKNB's
temporary detention facility. [@1] vb.kg reported that the three were detained
as suspects under Article 282 part 3 of Kyrgyzstan's Criminal Code. [@3]

A brick of cannabis resin (hashish) weighing 250.77 grams was found on U.D.U.
[@1] A second brick weighing 555.34 grams was found on U.B.Ch. [@1] 24.kg
reported the two bricks together as 806.11 grams. [@2] A search of J.Kh.T.'s
house, used as a stash, found seven bricks of hashish weighing 6 kg 905.47
grams. [@1] The seizure was reported as a 7.7 kg haul of drugs. [@3]

The GKNB said U.D.U. had set up a channel bringing drugs from Tajikistan for
sale in Osh Region and Uzbekistan. [@1] The group distributed drugs to residents
of Osh Region along a Tajikistan–Kyrgyzstan–Uzbekistan route. [@1] The
investigation remained open, with investigators working to establish all
circumstances and identify other people involved. [@1]
