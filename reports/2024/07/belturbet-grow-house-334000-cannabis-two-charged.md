Gardaí searched a grow house in Belturbet, County Cavan, on the night of Wednesday 17 July 2024 and seized cannabis worth €334,000. [@1,3] Officers found €44,000 worth of cannabis plants and €290,000 of cannabis herb at the property. [@1,3] The drugs were sent to Forensic Science Ireland for analysis. [@1,3]

Two men in their thirties were arrested and detained under Section 2 of the Criminal Justice (Drug Trafficking) Act 1996 at a Garda station in the north-western region. [@1,3] Both men were subsequently charged and brought before Cavan District Court on the morning of Friday 19 July 2024. [@2]
