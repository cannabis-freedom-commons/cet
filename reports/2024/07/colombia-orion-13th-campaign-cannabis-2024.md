Colombia's navy announced the results of the 13th edition of its multinational
Orion drug campaign. [@2] Authorities detained 744 members of criminal
organisations during the campaign and handed them to the authorities. [@2] The
campaign ran for 45 days with nine international organisations, 125 institutions
and 62 countries from five continents taking part. [@1] Twenty more countries
joined than in the previous edition. [@1]

Participating forces seized 196 tonnes of cocaine, more than 164 tonnes of
marijuana and 41 tonnes of hashish. [@1] The drugs were found in oceans, coastal
waters, rivers, island areas and port facilities on five continents. [@1] In
Brazil and Paraguay, authorities eradicated crops and destroyed production and
storage centres, interrupting 1,689 tonnes of marijuana. [@1] The campaign
destroyed 302 illegal structures used to extract and process alkaloids. [@1]
Forces intercepted 112 boats, 211 vehicles, nine semisubmersibles and five
aircraft. [@1] Two semisubmersibles were seized in Guyana, the first in the
campaign's history, on intelligence from the Colombian navy. [@1] The navy said
the results stopped criminal organisations from receiving more than $18.7
billion. [@1]

The campaign has run since 2018 and is led by the Colombian navy. [@3] This
edition consolidated the International Judicial Network Orion (REJIO), developed
with the UN Office on Drugs and Crime. [@1] Prosecutors in Colombia, Panama and
Costa Rica shared information on four judicial cases through the network. [@1]
