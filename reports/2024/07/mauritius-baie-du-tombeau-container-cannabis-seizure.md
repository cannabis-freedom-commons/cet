Officers of Mauritius's Anti Drug and Smuggling Unit raided a logistics
warehouse in Baie-du-Tombeau on Monday 29 July 2024 and uncovered the network.
[@2] The operation was led by Inspector Luckmun of the St-Pierre anti-drug
brigade. [@1] Officers seized 22 packets of cannabis weighing 11.5 kg in total.
[@2] No suspect had been arrested at that point. [@1] A St-Pierre ADSU team
searched the containers held at a private company's warehouse in
Baie-du-Tombeau. [@4]

The 22 parcels were concealed inside a washing machine, two speakers and a
subwoofer. [@1] The value of the seized drugs was estimated at more than 13.8
million rupees. [@4] The container had arrived from South Africa the previous
Friday, was in transit in Mauritius and was due to leave for Madagascar. [@1]
Police believed the 11.5 kg of cannabis was intended for the Mauritian market.
[@3]

The discrepancy prompted suspicion that the drugs could be removed in Mauritius
before their final shipment to the port of Tamatave, Madagascar. [@2] From 30
July the St-Pierre ADSU tried to identify the network and to establish whether
employees of the logistics company were involved. [@2] The drugs were sent to
the Forensic Science Laboratory, where DNA analysis of the packaging was under
way. [@2]
