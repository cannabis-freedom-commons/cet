Republican police in Tchatchou, an arrondissement of Tchaourou commune in
Benin's Borgou department, arrested a man suspected of producing psychotropic
substances. [@1] The man had a maize field with chanvre indien, the French term
for cannabis, growing in its midst. [@1] Acting on information from an
informant, officers raided the vast maize field on Monday 9 September 2024 and
found a cannabis plantation stretching as far as the eye could see. [@1]

After making the usual observations and reporting to the relevant authorities,
police seized and destroyed the plants. [@1] The suspected producer was placed
under garde à vue, or police custody, for the needs of the investigation,
according to the police directorate. [@1] Bénin Web TV reported that he would
soon be presented to the public prosecutor for the continuation of the
proceedings. [@2]

The arrest was announced on 12 September as part of a campaign against the
proliferation of ghettos, with the Tchatchou station revitalising its
intelligence network to cut off their supply. [@1]
