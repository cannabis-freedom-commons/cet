Temple police arrested two Killeen Independent School District administrators,
Jennifer Washington and Finni Johnson, on a marijuana charge on Saturday night.
[@1,4] The Herald described the two as apparently sisters and said the charge was
a low-level marijuana offence after police pulled them over. [@1] Washington was
the district's executive director of administrative services. [@1] Johnson was the district's coordinator for special education campus operations. [@4]

Temple police said that at about 11:10 p.m. on 21 September 2024 officers near
Interstate 35 and Exit 299 stopped a vehicle whose driver-side headlight was
malfunctioning. [@2,4] Officers identified Washington as the driver and said a
strong odor of marijuana was coming from the vehicle. [@2] They asked all
occupants to step out of the vehicle and then searched it. [@2,4] They located 1.9
grams of marijuana inside the vehicle and arrested Washington without incident.
[@2,4] KXXV reported that both people inside the vehicle were taken to the Bell
County Jail on misdemeanor possession charges. [@3]

Killeen ISD confirmed to KWTX that Washington was placed on administrative leave
after the marijuana possession arrest. [@2] Killeen ISD placed both Washington and Johnson on administrative leave pending an investigation. [@4] KXXV said Washington had served more
than 30 years in education and was appointed to her current role in 2023. [@3]
