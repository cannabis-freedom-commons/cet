Nottinghamshire Police officers executed a warrant at the Town Mill, in Bridge
Street, Mansfield, shortly before 5pm on Friday 20 September 2024. [@2] They
found more than 2,000 cannabis plants at various stages of growth in multiple
rooms of the former mill. [@1] The Grade II listed building dates from the
mid-19th century, when its water-powered milling gear produced flour for the
town's bakers, and was most recently a pub. [@1]

Police put the street value of the plants at more than £1 million. [@1] The West
Bridgford Wire reported a potential street value of more than £2 million once
the plants had fully matured. [@4] Officers detained a man a short time after
the raid near St Peter and St Paul Church. [@3]

Fatmir Khculi, 28, of Bridge Street, Mansfield, was charged with cultivating
cannabis. [@2] He appeared at Nottingham Magistrates' Court on Monday 23
September and was remanded into custody. [@3] He was due to appear at Nottingham
Crown Court on 22 October. [@1]

The plants were taken away and the growing equipment was put beyond use. [@2]
Emergency work on the building's electrical system was needed to make it safe
after the raid. [@1] Insp Kylie Davies, Nottinghamshire Police's district
commander for Mansfield, said the listed building was placed at significant risk
by the illegal activities inside. [@1]
