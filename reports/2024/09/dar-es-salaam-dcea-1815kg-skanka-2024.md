Tanzania's Drug Control and Enforcement Authority (DCEA) seized 1,815 kg of high-THC cannabis known locally as skanka in the Luguruni Mbezi and Magomeni areas of Dar es Salaam, in an operation running from late August to 2 September 2024. [@1,2] Five suspects were arrested, including 47-year-old businessman Richard Henry Mwanri of Mbezi Makonde, whom the DCEA commissioner described as a key distributor who received drugs from abroad concealed among other goods. [@1,2]

Also arrested were Felista Henry Mwanri, 70, who owned the house where the drugs were found in Luguruni Mbezi; Athumani Koja Mohamed, 58, a businessman from Tanga; Omary Chande Mohamed, 32, a bajaji driver from Buza; and Juma Abdallah Chapa, 36, of Kiwalani. [@1,2] Officers also seized a Mitsubishi Pajero and a bajaji motorcycle taxi used in the trade. [@2]

DCEA Commissioner General Aretas Lyimo announced the haul at a press conference on 10 September and urged border officers to stay vigilant, saying traffickers moved the drug into the country through crossings hidden in vehicles and goods. [@1,2]
