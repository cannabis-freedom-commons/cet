The Mfoundi territorial gendarmerie group in Cameroon carried out a significant
seizure. [@1] The gendarmerie's own video said its Mfoundi territorial regiment
had nabbed alleged cannabis dealers. [@2] The traffickers placed under arrest
were to answer before the justice system and were presented to the prosecutor.
[@1]

Officers found a truck carrying 16 sacks of about 30 kilograms each, filled with
cannabis, or roughly 480 kilograms in all. [@1] The headline described the
seizure as nearly 500 kilograms of cannabis. [@1] The gendarmerie described the
operation as of capital importance in the security forces' policy against drug
trafficking. [@1]

The truck had left a locality in the Loum department, in Cameroon's West Region.
[@1] The traffickers were trying to ship the illicit cargo to the Mfoundi
department, particularly the city of Yaoundé. [@1] The large quantity of drugs
was to be destroyed, as at a recent destruction ceremony presided over by the
minister of territorial administration a few weeks earlier. [@1]
