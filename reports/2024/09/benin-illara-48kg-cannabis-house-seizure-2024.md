The Illara border police station in Plateau department, Benin, carried out the
operation on Saturday 28 September 2024. [@2] Officers went to search a house
after intelligence reported suspicious activity and an important quantity of
chanvre indien, the French term for cannabis, stored there. [@2] According to
the information received, the owner of the cargo was preparing to move the
products to a neighbouring country. [@2] The search led to the seizure of two
bags of chanvre indien with a total weight estimated at 48 kg. [@2]

Technicians of Ocertid, Benin's central office against illicit drug trafficking,
were called in to investigate the case and any links to other illegal
operations. [@2] The police said investigations were continuing to establish the
scale of the trafficking network and to take the measures needed to stop it.
[@2] The Illara police also called on residents to report any suspicious or
illegal activity in their neighbourhoods. [@1]
