Nearly 800,000 cannabis plants and saplings were destroyed in the Boxanagar area
of Sepahijala district, Tripura, on 18 September 2024. [@1] The raid was led by
Naru Gopal Deb, the officer in charge of Kalamchoura Police Station. [@1]
Sub-inspectors Biswajit Debbarma and Shanti Tripura and Tripura State Rifles
personnel took part in the raid. [@1]

The operation covered nearly four acres of land used for illegal cannabis
cultivation. [@1] Police also destroyed cannabis nurseries north of Baba Bricks
Industry in the Veluarchar area of Sepahijala district. [@1]

A police officer said such operations would continue in the coming days. [@1]
