Officers arrested two men on suspicion of drug trafficking in the urbanización
Ceres, Ate Vitarte, Lima. [@2] The two were named as Luis Diego Fernández
Pariona, 25, alias “Timón”, and Christopher Dios David Ore Cóndor, 23, alias
“Pumba”. [@1] The men were stopped in a pickup truck, licence plate BYL-052,
parked suspiciously in front of a house. [@2] Police described them as
street-level marijuana sellers. [@3]

A search of the vehicle found a box with nine sealed bags of dried stems, leaves
and seeds that appeared to be Cannabis sativa. [@2] A second box in the boot
held two more bags of the same plant material, about five kilograms in total.
[@2] TVPerú Noticias reported the same weight and said the marijuana was stacked
in boxes inside the vehicle. [@1]

The detained men, the drug, the pickup and other items of interest were placed
at the disposal of the Santa Anita criminal investigation department. [@2] That
unit was to continue the proceedings and investigations with the duty
prosecutor. [@1]
