Police in the Negev uncovered an underground cannabis greenhouse containing
approximately 400 large marijuana plants, each about a metre tall. [@1] The
greenhouse was uncovered on 22 September 2024. [@1] The plants were growing in a
50-metre tunnel dug below the ground, which officers found by chance while they
were securing the demolition of nearby buildings. [@3] The tunnel lay in the
Bedouin dispersion, a term for the scattered Bedouin communities of the Negev.
[@2]

The greenhouse was partly covered with a fabric sheet. [@1] An irrigation system
had been installed inside. [@2] Police said the greenhouse and the plants in it
would be destroyed. [@2]

Earlier in September, police had announced a separate underground cannabis lab
near Arad, with over 600 plants intended for public distribution. [@3] That
earlier lab's drugs were valued by police at over one million shekels. [@3] The
tunnel was one of many such operations uncovered in southern Israel. [@4]
