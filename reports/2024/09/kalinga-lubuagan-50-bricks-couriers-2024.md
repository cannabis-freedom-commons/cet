Two alleged couriers trying to move marijuana out of Kalinga were flagged down
on Sunday 22 September 2024 during a checkpoint operation in Lubuagan, Kalinga.
[@1,2] Officers stopped the vehicle during regular peace-and-order operations
and noticed suspicious bags on its passenger side. [@1,2] Inside they found 50
marijuana bricks plus a tubular pack of dried marijuana leaves and stalks
wrapped in transparent plastic, weighing 51 kilos altogether and valued at
P6,120,000. [@1,2]

The pair were arrested and will face illegal drug charges, but the accounts do
not name them. [@1,2]
