The National Police drone squad carried out aerial surveillance that detected
and documented illicit activity in a bunker on block nine of Jiron Ancash in
Bellavista, Callao. [@1] The bunker operated 24 hours a day and was known
locally as La Botica for its constant drug sales. [@1] Four alleged gang members
were detained: Yair Moreno Villanueva (26), alias Berraco, Lucio Lopez Fernandez
(45), known as Loco, Nelson Gallo Castro (27), alias Negro, and Ronald Garcia
Collado (47), nicknamed Perro Flaco. [@1] The detainees face charges of illicit
drug trafficking and illegal possession of firearms. [@1]

Officers seized 1 kg of marijuana along with 400 PVC packets of cocaine
hydrochloride, 100 packets of marijuana and 160 smaller packets known as tipo
Paco. [@1] Officers also seized two backpacks, one containing a firearm and the
other containing drugs. [@1] Police drones captured detailed images of drug
transactions occurring at all hours, even as police patrols passed by. [@1]

Colonel Pedro Rojas, head of Divincri Callao, said the group used an evasion
strategy based on recognising regular buyers. [@1] Buyers entered through a
small window in a restricted-access door while lookouts ensured only authorised
customers got in. [@1] Police identified those captured as Los Bellacos del
Alamo and called the bunker intervention a win against drug trafficking and
organised crime. [@1] A second account of the same La Botica bunker reported 400
packets of coca paste and 100 packets of cannabis sativa found at the scene.
[@2] That second account named the same three men aged 26, 27 and 45 but called
the leader Rico, gave a different full name for the 47-year-old Perro Flaco and
added a fifth detainee aged 24. [@2]
