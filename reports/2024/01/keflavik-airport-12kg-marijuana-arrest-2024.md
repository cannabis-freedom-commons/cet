A foreign national was stopped on arrival in Iceland on 9 January 2024 with two kilograms of cocaine and 12 kilograms of marijuana in his suitcase. [@1] The Reykjavík Grapevine, reporting RÚV's account, notes that his baggage contained nothing but drugs. [@1]

The man has been held in custody since the seizure, and on 14 March a court extended his detention after police argued he was a flight risk with no ties to Iceland. [@1] Police were examining possible accomplices in Iceland and abroad, and whether the case connected to other smuggling attempts stopped at the airport. [@1]
