The Fresno City Attorney's Office and two state agencies served a search warrant
at AOA Private Golf Club in Fresno. [@2] The club was raided on the morning of
Thursday 25 January 2024. [@4] It was not a traditional country club but a
golf-simulator venue that hosted events and a full-service bar. [@3] Fresno City
Attorney Andrew Janz said the business had clearly been operating illegal sales
for a significant amount of time. [@2] The search was carried out by multiple
agencies at 1817 N. Helm Ave., near Fresno Yosemite International Airport. [@1]

Investigators seized thousands of dollars of marijuana, edibles and
paraphernalia from the club. [@2] No arrests were made, but a criminal
investigation as part of a California state crackdown on illegal cannabis
businesses was opened. [@1] Janz said the point of going after illegal grow
operations and sales was to support the legal cannabis industry, which is
regulated and safe. [@2]

The city also seized property belonging to AOA, which after the investigation
would be eligible for state or local asset forfeiture. [@3] The raid was part of
the Cannabis Administrative Prosecution Program targeting illegal cannabis
operations in Fresno. [@4] Janz said the raid showed that illegal activity was
occurring in places that appeared reputable at first glance. [@3]
