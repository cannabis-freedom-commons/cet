On 15 January 2024 the Colombian Navy seized its first drug load of the year in
the Colombian Pacific. [@1] During integral maritime control and security
operations, the authorities intercepted a boat crewed by three people carrying
1,548 kilograms of narcotics in the Colombian Pacific. [@1]

A Coast Guard unit moved to the area and carried out the interdiction after Navy
units spotted the moving boat. [@2] The small boat later sank because of
difficult sea conditions and multiple water entries. [@1] The people and
material were taken to Buenaventura in Valle del Cauca and handed to the
Attorney General's Technical Investigation Corps, whose preliminary test found
1,518 kilograms of marijuana and 30 kilograms of cocaine hydrochloride. [@1]

The three people aboard were to face proceedings for trafficking, manufacture
and possession of narcotics. [@2] The Navy said the seizure kept about 11
million dollars from trafficking networks and kept more than 300,000 doses off
the streets, figures given for the combined load. [@2]
