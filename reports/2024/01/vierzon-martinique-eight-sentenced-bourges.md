Eight defendants aged 21 to 45 were tried in November 2023 before the Bourges
correctional court over trafficking in cannabis resin and cocaine in Vierzon and
money laundering in Martinique. [@1] Only five of the eight defendants were
present in the dock. [@1]

The correctional court announced its verdict on Wednesday afternoon, 17 January
2024. [@1] The tribunal handed down sentences of five and four years of firm
imprisonment, a customs fine of 150,000 euros and suspended prison terms. [@1]
The sentences ranged from five years of firm imprisonment to one year suspended.
[@1] Dimitri César, 34, was sentenced to five years of firm imprisonment with an
arrest warrant and fined 10,000 euros. [@1]

Part of the drugs were sent by parcel through metropolitan France to Vierzon,
the second city of the Cher department, between January 2019 and January 2021.
[@1] The case also involved rental of villas, purchase of vehicles and a luxury
hotel in Dubai. [@1]
