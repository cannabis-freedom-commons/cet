Police arrested four men at Yarrowkabra, off the Soesdyke-Linden Highway, on
Monday 15 January 2024 after finding eight fields of marijuana plants. [@2]
Those in custody were Fayon Reeves, 25, a farmer of Circuit Road, Timehri;
Kishore Ram, 35, and Munesh Ram, 29, labourers of Cummings Lodge. [@2] The
fourth was Miguel Gomes, 21, a painter of Hyde Park, Timehri. [@1] Gomes was
found in a camp and led police to the field where he was planting narcotics; the
narcotics and the camp were destroyed. [@1]

Police Headquarters said the discovery was made between 04:00 and 11:30 hrs
during an intelligence-led operation at Yarrowkabra backdam. [@2] A police
release said eight fields of 100 by 200 feet each held cultivated cannabis
plants two to three feet tall, and eight camps were seen. [@1] Police said a
nursery of seedlings amounting to about 1,000 plants was found and destroyed.
[@2] The plants amounted to about 5,000, police said. [@1] Several plants were
uprooted and taken as exhibits. [@1] Dried cannabis found in the camp with
Reeves, Kishore Ram and Munesh Ram weighed 1,700 grams (1.7 kg). [@1]

The operation was led by an Assistant Superintendent from Special Branch and
comprised several ranks from Regional Division 4B. [@1] The suspects remained in
custody pending charges. [@2]
