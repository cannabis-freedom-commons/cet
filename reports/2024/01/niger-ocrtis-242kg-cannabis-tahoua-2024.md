Niger's Central Office for the Suppression of Illicit Drug Trafficking (OCRTIS)
presented seized drugs and other goods in Tahoua on 9 January 2024. [@1] The
presentation took place at the regional headquarters of the national police in
Tahoua, at a ceremony attended by the region's governor. [@2] The head of the
OCRTIS office in Tahoua, Ali Akilou, said the haul came from three operations
carried out over one week in Tahoua, Tchintabaraden and Guidan Takoussara. [@2]
Officers seized 242 bricks of cannabis together with 500 tramadol tablets and
2,000 diazepam tablets. [@1]

The three operations also turned up 12 sacks of dynamite, 60 pairs of military
shoes and 30 camouflage uniforms. [@1] The goods were recovered in Tahoua,
Tchintabaraden and Guidan Takoussara. [@1] The authorities estimated the whole
haul at more than 70 million CFA francs. [@1]

Governor Oumarou Tawayé called it a record drug seizure for the Tahoua region.
[@3] He said the fight against drug trafficking and terrorism requires the
commitment of all the defence and security forces. [@1] He said drug trafficking
finances terrorism and called for cooperation between the security forces and
the population. [@1]
