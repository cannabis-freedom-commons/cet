After almost a month in pretrial detention, the three defendants were tried on
16 January by the Nouméa correctional court. [@1] The two brothers were
sentenced to 30 months and 18 months in prison, with continued detention. [@1]
Their mother received a one-year prison term to be served as home detention with
an electronic bracelet. [@1]

On 19 December, after a five-month gendarmerie investigation, the two brothers
aged 23 and 25 and their mother were arrested and placed in custody over a large
cannabis trafficking operation in Nouméa. [@1] Searches found more than a kilo
of cannabis and 72 already packaged sachets, as well as a pouch holding 196,500
francs in cash. [@1] One of the brothers rented six flats in Nouméa used for
indoor cannabis cultivation and used several people to tend the crops, make up
packets and sell them. [@1]

The family trafficking network had been dismantled after phone taps and
surveillance by the gendarmerie research section. [@1] The mother handled
packing of the cannabis and resale of the sachets as well as laundering of the
money. [@1]
