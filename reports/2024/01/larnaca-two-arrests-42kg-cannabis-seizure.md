On the afternoon of 4 January 2024 police in Larnaca district stopped a car
driven by a 21-year-old Larnaca resident in a village in the district. [@1] The
YKAN Larnaca branch ran a separate operation around 5:30 PM the same day. [@1]
Officers intercepted a vehicle driven by a 57-year-old Larnaca resident and
found a syringe containing cannabis oil. [@1] Both suspects were re-arrested on
court warrants and remained in custody. [@1]

A search of the 21-year-old's vehicle uncovered 20.669 kilograms of cannabis
hidden inside. [@1] A follow-up search of his home seized another 21.303
kilograms of cannabis. [@1] Officers also seized a pistol with its magazine, 11
casings and an unfired cartridge from the house. [@2] A search of the
57-year-old's home found items that were sent for further examination. [@1]

Famagusta CID joined the YKAN drug squad in the Larnaca operation targeting the
two men. [@2] Police spokesman Christos Andreou said the 21-year-old was stopped
on a Larnaca village road at about 5pm with over 20 kg of cannabis in his
vehicle. [@2] Both men were due before a court the following Monday while the
investigation into the network continued. [@2]
