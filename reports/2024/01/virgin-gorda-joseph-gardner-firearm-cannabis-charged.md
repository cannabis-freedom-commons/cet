Royal Virgin Islands Police Force officers stopped a car carrying three men on
Virgin Gorda on Thursday 18 January 2024 during a stop-and-search operation.
[@3] In the search they found two firearms, a rifle and a hand pistol, multiple
rounds of ammunition and a substantial quantity of drugs believed to be
cannabis. [@2]

Akeem Joseph, 37, of St Croix in the US Virgin Islands, and Kyreek Gardner, 22,
of The Valley, Virgin Gorda, were jointly charged with possession of a
prohibited firearm. [@2] They were also charged with keeping an unlicensed
firearm, two counts of unlawful possession of explosives, unlawful possession of
cannabis and unlawful possession of cannabis with intent to supply. [@1] Both
men remained in custody until the next available sitting of the Magistrate's
Court. [@1]

A third occupant of the car escaped on foot and remained at large. [@1] Police
described him as five feet tall, with a dark complexion and plaited hair, and
said he wore a red top, blue jeans and a gold chain when he ran. [@1] Police
asked anyone with information to call Crime Stoppers on 800-8477 or the RVIPF
intelligence unit on 368-9339. [@1]
