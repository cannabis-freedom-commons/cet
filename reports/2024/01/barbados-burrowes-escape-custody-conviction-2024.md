A jury took an hour and a half to find Herwin Llewellyn Burrowes guilty of
escaping police custody. [@1] Burrowes, of Taylor’s Gap, Delamere Land,
Martindales Road, St Michael, had been arrested for unlawful possession of
cannabis and escaped lawful custody by force at Temple Yard on 9 June 2018. [@1]

On that date Constables Jabarry Mayers and Wayne Marcellin were on duty in
Temple Yard when they saw Burrowes standing between two stalls carrying a green
and black haversack. [@1] Constable Mayers arrested Burrowes after noticing a
strong scent of marijuana and suspecting that he had cannabis. [@1] While the
officer held Burrowes’ right hand, Burrowes struggled, pushed Mayers in the
chest and caused him to stumble and lose his grip. [@1] Burrowes ran away while
Constable Mayers pursued him but could not apprehend him. [@1] Burrowes dropped
a haversack containing marijuana and a utility bill with his name and address on
it. [@1]

Senior State Counsel Romario Straker and State Counsel Treann Knight prosecuted
the case while attorney Rhea Layne represented Burrowes. [@1] Madam Justice
Laurie-Ann Smith-Bovell allowed Burrowes to remain on bail, ordered a
pre-sentence report and adjourned sentencing submissions to 8 March. [@1]
Burrowes has five previous convictions and was warned that remaining on bail did
not mean he would avoid a custodial sentence. [@1] Justice Smith-Bovell warned
him that he could go to Dodds. [@1]
