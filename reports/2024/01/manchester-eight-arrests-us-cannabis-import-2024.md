A Greater Manchester Police operation targeting a suspected organised crime
group that imported high-strength cannabis into the United Kingdom resulted in
eight arrests. [@1] In the early hours of 4 January 2024, the Serious Organised
Crime Group coordinated 16 simultaneous warrants across Manchester, Cheshire,
West Yorkshire and Lancashire against a group suspected of facilitating cannabis
importation from the United States. [@1] The eight people, aged between 18 and
52, were arrested on suspicion of cannabis importation and remained in custody
for questioning. [@1]

Police and Border Force had intercepted over 100 kilos of cannabis during the
investigation. [@1] Searches of the properties recovered £500,000 in cash and a
BMW M4. [@2] Police said the cannabis seized so far was worth over £3 million.
[@1]

Each person was believed to have played a different role in the conspiracy, from
orchestrating importation and arranging payment to selling drugs to criminals
across the United Kingdom. [@1] Detective Inspector Claire Rawcliffe thanked
Border Force and neighbouring forces for supporting the ongoing operation and
investigation. [@1] She said police would continue working with partners to
identify and prevent new drug-smuggling methods. [@1]
