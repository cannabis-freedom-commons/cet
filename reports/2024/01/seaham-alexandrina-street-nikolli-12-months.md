Bladar Nikolli, a 31-year-old Albanian national with no previous criminal record
in the UK, was sentenced over a cannabis grow in County Durham. [@2] Nikolli
appeared at Durham Crown Court via video link from HMP Durham to be sentenced
for production of cannabis. [@1] He had pleaded guilty and admitted producing a
class B drug. [@2] Judge Jo Kidd imposed a 12-month prison sentence and said he
would serve half before being eligible for release. [@2] The judge told Nikolli
he faces possible deportation after serving the sentence, a matter for the Home
Office. [@1]

Police visited the property in Alexandrina Street, Seaham, with a search warrant
and found what was considered a sophisticated cannabis grow on 3 November 2023.
[@2] The court was told 64 plants were being grown in the house while Nikolli,
the only person present, was in a small rear bedroom. [@2] He handed over the
property keys to police and was found with £64 and a mobile phone. [@2]
Examination of the phone showed pots of cannabis growing from 28 October and
video of him in a moving car on 1 November, showing he could come and go. [@2]

The judge said police video showed the sophisticated operation with lighting and
other equipment and a bypassed electricity meter. [@2] The court ordered
forfeiture and destruction of the plants and paraphernalia seized from the
house. [@2]
