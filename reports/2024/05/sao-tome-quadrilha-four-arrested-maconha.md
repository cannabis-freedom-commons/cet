On the morning of Friday 3 May 2024, military police of the 9ª Independent
Military Police Company (CIPM) of São Tomé, working with civil police, carried
out an operation to detain people involved in extortion of local merchants and
drug trafficking. [@1] The teams went to a residence that was under
investigation and arrested three men and one woman. [@1] One of the men had an
outstanding arrest warrant. [@1]

During the approach officers seized a substantial quantity of drugs, including
maconha (marijuana) and cocaine, divided into portions. [@1] They also seized a
.12-calibre shotgun with ammunition. [@1] A .38-calibre revolver with ammunition
was taken as well. [@1] Five mobile phones were confiscated. [@1] Two
motorcycles were also confiscated, along with a balaclava and other material.
[@1]

All four detainees and the seized items were taken to the police station for the
appropriate legal procedures. [@1]
