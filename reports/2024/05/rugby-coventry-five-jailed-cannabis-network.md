Five men were jailed for their part in a cannabis-growing enterprise in Rugby
and Coventry. [@4] They were sentenced at Warwick Crown Court after pleading
guilty. [@1] Miklovan Myrtaj, 33, was jailed for 58 weeks after pleading guilty
to production of cannabis. [@4] Rexhino Myrtaj, 27, was jailed for a year after
pleading guilty to production of cannabis and possession of cocaine. [@4]
Laurenc Mingaj, 30, was jailed for a year after pleading guilty to production of
cannabis. [@4] Klajdi Agalliu, 23, was jailed for three months after pleading
guilty to production of cannabis. [@4] Martin Myrtaj, 25, was jailed for a year
after pleading guilty to possession of criminal property. [@4]

In September 2023, detectives went to a house in Grosvenor Road, Rugby, after
intelligence that cannabis was being produced there. [@4] They forced entry
after smelling cannabis, hearing fans and seeing the blinds and curtains shut.
[@4] Officers found 42 plants in the front bedroom and 40 in the loft, 82 in
all, along with used soil bags and stripped plants that suggested a recent
harvest. [@4] Three men were arrested in the house, and all five were arrested
in the operation. [@3] Phone analysis led police to a flat in Bedford Street,
Coventry, where they found Martin Myrtaj and seized more than £15,000 in cash.
[@4] Klajdi Agalliu arrived at the flat and was detained; a receipt for growing
equipment and a utility bill for a house in Torrington Avenue were found in his
car. [@4] At Torrington Avenue, officers found 32 mature cannabis plants drying
in the loft with growing equipment. [@1] Mingaj's phone showed 83 visits to
Richmond Street, where a search found 119 cannabis plants. [@4] Police valued
the seized cannabis at £162,000. [@1] The network used four properties,
including the flat where the cash was found. [@2]

Detective Sergeant Tom Quinlan said the investigation was 'the gift that kept on
giving' with a trail of evidence leading to four properties used for criminal
enterprise. [@4]
