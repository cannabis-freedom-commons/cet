Customs officers in Dubossary, Transnistria, stopped a car with Moldovan plates
traveling from Chișinău to a village in the Dubossary district and questioned
its driver and passenger. [@1] The post inspector said both men behaved
nervously, repeatedly interrupting the conversation and drinking water, and both
denied carrying prohibited items. [@1] Suspecting they were under the influence
of drugs, the inspector searched the car. [@1]

Inside, officers found homemade smoking devices and, in a paper bag of tobacco,
a polyethylene bag of dark-green plant material. [@1] The press service of the
State Customs Committee of the PMR said an operative-investigative group was
called to the scene. [@1] The men said the bag was not theirs and had been left
by a friend to whom they had lent the car. [@1] An examination confirmed the
plant material was marijuana. [@1]

Transnistria's State Customs Committee said a criminal case was opened for the
smuggling of narcotic substances and that an investigation was under way. [@1]
