Eight men from Scunthorpe were jailed at Hull Crown Court after admitting
offences. [@1] Seven of the gang were sentenced on 9 May 2024 and the eighth on
10 May 2024. [@2]

Charlie Rhodes, 27, was jailed for 14 years and four months after pleading
guilty to conspiracy to supply class B controlled drugs and conspiracy to commit
aggravated burglaries. [@1] Liam Pollock, 22, was jailed for 12 years and two
months after pleading guilty to conspiracy to supply class B controlled drugs
and conspiracy to commit aggravated burglaries. [@1] Tyler Gallagher, 22, was
jailed for 11 years and eight months after pleading guilty to conspiracy to
supply class B controlled drugs and conspiracy to commit aggravated burglaries.
[@1] Joel Haines, 29, was jailed for 10 years and nine months after pleading
guilty to conspiracy to supply class B controlled drugs and conspiracy to commit
aggravated burglaries. [@1] Michael Lowther, 19, was sentenced to nine years in
a young offenders institution after pleading guilty to conspiracy to supply
class B controlled drugs and conspiracy to commit aggravated burglaries. [@1]
Bert Keeton, 22, was jailed for eight years and six months after pleading guilty
to conspiracy to supply class B controlled drugs and conspiracy to commit
aggravated burglaries. [@1] Josh Cooling, 29, was jailed for two years and nine
months after pleading guilty to being concerned in the supply of class B
controlled drugs. [@1] Jamie Hemsworth, 29, was jailed for 12 months after
pleading guilty to being concerned in the supply of class B controlled drugs.
[@1]

The gang made an estimated £1m over a four-month period. [@1] The gang divided
Scunthorpe into three areas, Town, Ashby and Westcliff, with a dedicated drug
line serving each area. [@1] They targeted cannabis grows set up by other
organised crime groups armed with baseball bats. [@1] They used thermal imaging
devices to find heat sources and posed as police officers to carry out raids.
[@1] They broke into premises armed with baseball bats, making threats and
assaulting the occupants. [@2]

Humberside Police launched an investigation in January 2023 after reports of car
thefts and burglaries across Grimsby and Scunthorpe. [@1] Analysis of Tyler
Gallagher's mobile phone showed the group used Snapchat to document extensive
serious and organised criminality. [@1] The eight gang members were arrested in
May 2023 and subsequently charged. [@1]
