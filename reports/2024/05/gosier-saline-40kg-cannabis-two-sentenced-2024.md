Nicolas Paul and Sadian Copol, 27, were sentenced to three and two years in
prison by the Pointe-à-Pitre correctional court. [@1] The prosecution presented
Paul as the lookout, and a Taurus-type pistol found at his home aggravated his
case. [@1] Copol was suspected of being the driver. [@1] Both men were arrested
four months after the landing. [@1] They were tried in immediate appearance, a
fast-track procedure, before the correctional court. [@1]

Traffickers were about to receive the shipment on the Saline beach at Le Gosier.
[@1] In November 2023, 40 kilograms of cannabis herb packed in bales were
unloaded from a boat believed to have come from Dominica. [@1] Gendarmes who had
set up surveillance caught the receivers in the act and they fled. [@1] They
drove off the beach with the cargo in a car preceded by a lead car. [@1] Near
Carénage the car carrying the drugs was overtaken by the gendarmes and its two
occupants abandoned the drugs and the vehicle. [@1] Investigators traced the two
through their phones' location data on the beach that night and the plate of the
abandoned car. [@1]

The prosecutor had asked for four years for Paul and three for Copol. [@1] Their
lawyers argued that they had played minor roles and that the requests were
excessive. [@1] Paul's lawyer said he had come from the Cour Zamia area, had
found work and was supporting his seriously ill mother and brother. [@1] She
asked that he be judged only for what she called his minor participation on
three occasions. [@1] After deliberation the three judges reduced each requested
term by one year. [@1] The account says more people were probably present at the
landing given the quantity involved. [@1]
