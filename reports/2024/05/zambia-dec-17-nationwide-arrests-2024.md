Zambia's Drug Enforcement Commission arrested 17 suspects across the country for
various drug-related offences, it said in a statement on Friday 10 May 2024.
[@1] In Choma, Southern Province, officers arrested Andrew Kabamba, described by
the commission as a notorious drug supplier. [@1] The Choma arrest followed a
raid on a local nightclub as part of the countrywide targeted operations. [@2]

Kabamba was found with 43 sachets of heroin weighing 2.58 grams and 18 sachets
of dry loose cannabis weighing 32.4 grams in a Starlet vehicle. [@2] In
Chinsali, Muchinga Province, officers jointly charged and arrested James
Kanyika, 38, and Bridget Nangogo, 42, for trafficking in 40.1 kg of dry loose
cannabis. [@2] On the Copperbelt, operations led to the arrest of eight people
for trafficking in more than 15 kg of dry loose cannabis. [@2]

In Luapula Province, an operation along Tuta Road in Samfya District led to the
arrest of a juvenile and two other people for trafficking in 2.8 kg of dry loose
cannabis. [@2] In Serenje District, Central Province, three people were arrested
for trafficking in more than 2 kg of dry loose cannabis. [@2]

Assistant public relations officer Theresa Mawere said the commission had
launched a series of targeted operations countrywide. [@1] The commission said
it had executed strategic interventions to dismantle drug syndicates and disrupt
the flow of narcotics. [@1]
