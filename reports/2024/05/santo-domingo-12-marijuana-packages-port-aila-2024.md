Anti-drug agents and Public Ministry prosecutors in the Dominican Republic
seized 12 packages of marijuana in operations at the port of Santo Domingo and
Las Américas International Airport (AILA). [@1] The same operations produced 15
containers and 18 envelopes of a substance presumed to be liquid cocaine, found
at Inposdom, the Dominican postal service, which are not counted in the cannabis
figures. [@1]

Nine of the 12 packages were found in a box at the Santo Domingo port that was
to be shipped to Puerto Rico. [@1] The box was supposedly sent by a person with
an address on the marginal of the Autopista Las Américas and was to be received
by a woman at a residential estate in Guaynabo, Puerto Rico. [@1] The other
three packages, hidden in items of clothing, were seized at Las Américas airport
after being sent from Middleton, United States, to a man living in an apartment
tower on Ramón de Lara street in Santiago. [@1]

In a simultaneous Inposdom operation, officers seized 11 more containers of a
liquid presumed to be cocaine, sent according to the manifest from Baní to a man
in Morovis, Puerto Rico. [@1] The Public Ministry and the DNCD said they were
deepening their investigations into the seized substances in each case and
remained alert to drug trafficking by any means through the country's airports
and ports. [@2]
