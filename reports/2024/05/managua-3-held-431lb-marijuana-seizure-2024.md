Nicaraguan police detained Marvin de Jesús Vargas Velásquez, Yesenia Oneida
Vanegas Ruiz and Daniela de Jesús Barberena Porras in Managua on suspicion of
drug trafficking. [@1] Commissioner General Victoriano Ruíz, head of Auxilio
Judicial, said the seizures came from a surveillance and patrol plan carried out
on Thursday 23 May 2024. [@1] Officers stopped a grey Toyota Corolla at about
3:40 pm at a police checkpoint in front of the Carlos Roberto Huembes hospital
and found a tape-wrapped package of green herb in the boot that tested positive
for marijuana. [@3] Police then searched two homes, the first in Ciudad Sandino
and the second in the Mirna Ugarte neighbourhood of Managua. [@1]

The total seized across districts III and X of Managua was 431.89 pounds (about
195.9 kg) of marijuana and 3 kg 37 g of cocaine. [@1] At the Ciudad Sandino home
officers found 435 tape-wrapped oval packages containing 411.68 pounds of
marijuana and 1 kg 24 g of cocaine. [@1] At the Mirna Ugarte home they seized
20.21 pounds of marijuana and 2 kg 13 g of cocaine. [@1] Officers also took two
motorcycles, a Toyota Corolla and five mobile phones. [@1] Police put the cash
seized at 84,151 córdobas and US$511. [@2]

The police technical team photographed the evidence and used a Scintrex trace
detector on the vehicles, clothing, money and the detainees, which tested
positive for cocaine and marijuana particles. [@1] The results were announced
under the Muro de Contención strategy against organised crime and drug
trafficking. [@4]
