Gendarmes of the anti-drug cell in Bouaké seized 33 blocks of cannabis in a bus
coming from Abidjan during a routine check on the southern corridor of the city.
[@1] The seizure was made on Sunday 26 May 2024. [@2]

The gendarmerie opened an investigation immediately after the seizure to find
the owner of the parcel. [@1] The inquiry led to the arrest of a man identified
only as S.Y. [@1] The owner of the cannabis was arrested the day after the
discovery. [@2] He was to answer for his acts before the competent authorities.
[@1]
