The load was seized on 4 May by Bolivarian National Guard officers at the El
Carira citizen-attention point in Garcia de Hevia municipality, Tachira state.
[@4] The two occupants of the vehicle were detained and identified as Jose
Alirio Rivas Fernandez and Roberto Jose Berroteran Suarez, who were coming from
Colombia and heading to Caracas. [@3] One of those detained was reported to be
the private chauffeur of Spanish diplomat Victoria Garcia Ojeda, who was outside
Venezuela at the time of the police action. [@1] Spanish diplomatic sources said
the case was in all probability a fraudulent use of that vehicle. [@1]

Inside the vehicle officers found six suitcases holding 363 panels of marijuana
weighing 185 kilos 50 grams and 16 panels of cocaine weighing 8 kilos 950 grams,
according to the GNB Detachment 213 report. [@2] The Public Ministry later
described the same six suitcases as holding 163 marijuana panels and 16 cocaine
wrappers, with gross weights of 185.5 kilos and 8 kilos 950 grams respectively.
[@4] Rivas Fernandez and Berroteran were travelling through that checkpoint in a
vehicle with Spanish Embassy diplomatic plates when they were stopped for a
routine inspection. [@4] The driver and the other passenger surrendered
immediately to the Venezuelan authorities with raised arms, and inspection then
uncovered just over 185 kilos of marijuana and almost nine kilos of cocaine in
more than 300 packages. [@5]

At the Public Ministry's request, driver Jose Alirio Rivas Fernandez and
co-driver Roberto Jose Berroteran were remanded in custody for alleged
responsibility for trafficking the marijuana and cocaine panels. [@4] In the
same case Jenny Doralba Vargas Castellanos and Publio Argenis Rojas Garrido were
also remanded in custody for alleged links to the facts. [@4] At presentation
hearings the 29th Tachira prosecution office charged Rivas Fernandez and
Berroteran with alleged illicit trafficking of narcotic and psychotropic
substances as transport, plus association. [@4] Investigators found Rivas
Fernandez had been contacted to move the load from San Antonio del Tachira for
10,000 US dollars. [@4]
