The Algiers wilaya police said its operational services seized 6.145 kg of
cannabis during April 2024, along with 1.314 kg of cocaine, 4.12 g of heroin and
53,980 psychotropic tablets. [@1] The opening line of the statement put the
cannabis haul at more than 6 kg, and the same communiqué gave the figure as
6,145 kg, with 14 bottles of sedative. [@1] The force said it had handled 4,958
cases during the month, 3,265 of them linked to drugs and psychotropic
substances. [@1] It presented the results as part of its fight against crime in
all its forms. [@1]

The monthly bulletin also counted 787 cases of offences against people, funds
and property, 60 cybercrime cases, 38 family cases and 521 cases against public
order. [@1] It ended by reminding residents of the green number 1548, the
emergency number 17 and the Allo Chorta app for reporting crime. [@1]

The figures were announced in a statement issued on Sunday 19 May 2024. [@1]
Ouest Tribune published the same statement and figures the following day. [@2]
