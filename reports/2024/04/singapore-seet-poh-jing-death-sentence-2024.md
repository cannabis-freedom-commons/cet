Seet Poh Jing, a former property sales agent with Huttons Asia, was sentenced
to the mandatory death penalty for trafficking cannabis in Singapore. [@1,2]
In reasons issued on 2 April 2024, Justice See Kee Oon of the Appellate
Division convicted him on one charge of trafficking no less than 4,509.2
grams of cannabis mixture. [@1,2] Justice See held that Seet did not qualify for life imprisonment as an alternative because he never claimed to be a mere courier. [@3]

Central Narcotics Bureau officers arrested Seet, then 28, on 28 June 2018
after he led them to a car holding five blocks of vegetable matter that
proved to be cannabis mixture he called weed. [@1,2] Seet was 28 years old when he was arrested on 28 June 2018, together with his girlfriend, in Tampines. [@3] His home in Hougang was also searched. [@3] Forensic examination of
two of his mobile phones found mass-sent messages advertising the drug. [@1,2]

Seet claimed the cannabis was for research and development toward a
cannabidiol business, and relied on a psychiatric report of a drug-induced
hypomanic episode behind grandiose plans for a cannabis venture. [@1,2] His trial began in September 2022. [@3] A psychiatric assessment by Dr Kenneth Koh of the Institute of Mental Health concluded that Seet had a substance use disorder from his consumption of methamphetamine and cannabis. [@3] The
judge rejected both, calling the research claim a concocted afterthought at
odds with Seet's post-arrest statements, which spoke of helping friends and
set out a weed pricing strategy without mentioning research. [@1,2] The judge found that Seet did not have the know-how to extract cannabidiol from the drugs. [@3] Seet said in a statement on 3 July 2018 that the drugs were for sharing, his own consumption and sale, with each block priced at between $2,500 and $4,500. [@3] In a later statement he said the drugs were for his own consumption and that he took 25g each day. [@3]
