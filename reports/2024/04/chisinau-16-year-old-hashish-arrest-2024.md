Officers of Moldova's National Inspectorate of Investigations detained a
16-year-old boy in Chișinău in the night of 30 April 2024 with 1 kg of hashish.
[@1] The teenager was placed under house arrest. [@1] NewsMaker reported that
the house arrest was ordered for 30 days and that police were looking for his
accomplices. [@3]

A search of the rented flat where the boy lived found a large quantity of
marijuana, synthetic drugs and tablets, plus packaging for portioning small
batches and scales. [@3] According to police, the seized substances were worth
about 1 million lei in total on the black market. [@3] The boy lives in the
north of Moldova. [@3] The drugs were meant to be portioned and sold to
customers. [@2]

He came to the capital every 10 days to collect a batch of drugs, then took a
taxi to districts of Moldova and left caches of 30 doses each for other
couriers. [@1] The investigation continued in order to identify all members of
the group. [@1]
