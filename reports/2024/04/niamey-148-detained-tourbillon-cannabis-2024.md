Nigerien officers arrested at least 148 people in Niamey during a raid codenamed
'Tourbillon' on the night of 26 to 27 April 2024. [@1] The detainees were
counted as 134 men, 14 women and 14 minors. [@1] Twenty-one of them, comprising
16 men, five women and one minor, were placed at the disposal of OCRTIS, Niger's
central office for repressing illicit drug trafficking; 19 were held over drug
offences and three because an inquiry was needed. [@1] The other 127, comprising
118 men, nine women and 13 minors, were handed to other investigation units for
offences within their competence. [@1]

Officers seized quantities of cannabis along with crack, tramadol, shisha
devices, counterfeit pharmaceutical products, vehicles, motorcycles and bladed
weapons. [@4] OCRTIS led the raid with the support of Niger's defence and
security forces. [@3]

The operation covered five areas of Niamey and targeted places where drugs were
sold and consumed. [@1] Its stated aim was to detect and neutralise existing and
emerging drug sales and consumption networks, focusing on dealers operating at
those places. [@1] The supporting units came from the Niamey regional security
council, notably the national police, national gendarmerie, national guard and
national customs. [@2]
