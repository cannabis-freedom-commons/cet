Vanuatu police arrested a suspect after executing a marijuana search warrant at
Eton village on Efate. [@1] During the search officers uprooted 14 marijuana
plants at the village. [@2] Police gave no calendar date, saying the warrant was
executed several weeks before the 18 April 2024 brief, which places the search
in March or early April 2024. [@1] The operation was carried out under a search
warrant, and the arrest followed it. [@1]

The arrested person was identified only as a suspect; no name, age or home
village was published. [@1] No charge, plea, court date or sentence was
reported, and no weight or value was given for the 14 plants. [@1] The only
cannabis reported in the operation was the 14 plants uprooted; no dried
cannabis, cash or equipment was listed. [@2]
