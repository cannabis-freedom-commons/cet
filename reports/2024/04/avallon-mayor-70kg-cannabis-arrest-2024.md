French police detained seven people, including Avallon mayor Jamilah Habsaoui
and two of her brothers. [@1] The raids followed an investigation into drug
trafficking in the eastern town that had lasted several weeks. [@1] Habsaoui has
been mayor of Avallon since 2021 and also serves as a regional councillor. [@1]
Officers searched her home, the town hall and the pharmacy where she worked.
[@1]

Investigators found at least 70 kilogrammes (150 pounds) of cannabis resin in
her home. [@1] The prosecutor gave no amount for the drugs found at her home,
while an investigation source said 70 kg of cannabis was seized. [@1] Other
parts of the same investigation uncovered 983 grammes of cocaine, 7,000 euros in
cash and about 20 gold ingots. [@1]

Prosecutor Hugues de Phily gave the account of the raids to AFP. [@1] The
operation was part of a nationwide campaign against the growing drugs trade in
French cities. [@1]
