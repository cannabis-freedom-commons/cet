Andrej Krstikjevikj, a 40-year-old man from Montenegro, was jailed for eight
years and fined €30,000 after admitting possessing cannabis and ecstasy at the
Imsida primary school. [@1] Rather than proceed to trial, the prosecution and
defence agreed on the eight-year prison sentence and a fine of €30,000. [@1]
Madame Justice Consuelo Scerri Herrera approved the agreed sentence and ordered
the man to pay €1,713 in court expenses. [@1]

The case began in August 2019, when police went to the primary school after
reports that people were living there at night while it was closed for works.
[@1] Officers found him inside and seized two kilograms of cannabis, which they
valued at between €20,000 and €56,000, along with 160 ecstasy pills valued at
between €1,000 and €1,600. [@1] He was hospitalised and tested positive for
ecstasy. [@1]

At the time he was a homeless man found sleeping at the school, and he was
remanded in custody after his arrest. [@2] The Msida Primary School had been
closed for two years pending refurbishment works. [@2] He was arraigned before
magistrate Audrey Demicoli, then aged 35, accused of possession of cannabis and
ecstasy in circumstances indicating the drugs were not solely for personal use.
[@2] He was also accused of committing the offences within 100 metres of a
school. [@2] A second outlet reported the arrest as an accusation of selling
drugs near the school. [@3] That account put the combined value of all the drugs
at more than €32,000. [@3]
