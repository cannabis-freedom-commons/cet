The Drug Control and Enforcement Authority said it had intercepted a substantial
quantity of enhanced marijuana at the Zambia-Tanzania border. [@1] DCEA
Commissioner General Aretas James Lyimo said the drugs, known as Skanka or super
weed, were disguised as innocuous goods resembling tea leaves or snacks and had
come from Eswatini, Malawi, Zambia and South Africa. [@1]

Lyimo said officers confiscated over 600 kilogrammes of packaged enhanced
semi-processed marijuana, a significant haul given how light dried marijuana
leaves are. [@1] A second account places the seizure of more than 600 kilograms
of the packaged enhanced marijuana at Tunduma on the Zambia-Tanzania border.
[@2] Smugglers concealed the drugs inside legitimate shipments such as apple
crates imported from South Africa, tucking the marijuana packets alongside the
fruit boxes to evade routine inspection. [@1]

Unlike locally grown marijuana with around 10 percent tetrahydrocannabinol, the
seized chemically enhanced cannabis exceeded 40 percent THC, making it four
times more potent. [@1] Official reports linked increased smuggling across
eastern African borders including Tanzania to an unprecedented surplus of
marijuana production in those southern African countries last year. [@1]
