The Royal Oman Police arrested an expatriate in South Al Batinah Governorate after finding more than 130 blocks of hashish in his possession. [@1, 2] The force's anti-narcotics department, led by the governorate's police, detained the man, described as being of Asian nationality, and said legal procedures against him were being completed. [@1, 2]
