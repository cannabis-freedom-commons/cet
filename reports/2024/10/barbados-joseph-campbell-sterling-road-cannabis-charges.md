Officers of the Tactical Response Unit of the Barbados Police Service arrested
and jointly charged two people over several drug offences. [@2] They were
Timothy Nicholas Joseph, 51, of Maxwell Hill, Christ Church, and Jamaican
national Adieka Kelly-ann Campbell, 35, of St Barnabas, St Michael. [@1] The
offences were committed on Saturday 26 October 2024 at Sterling Road, St Philip.
[@2]

Police said the cannabis weighed 0.5 pounds (about 0.227 kg) and had a street
value of $4 000. [@1] The cannabis was valued at 4,000 Barbados dollars. [@3]
Police said the cocaine weighed 1 kilo and had a street value of $50 000. [@1]
The pair appeared before Chief Magistrate Ian Weekes at the District 'A'
Magistrate's Court on Thursday 31 October 2024. [@2]

Joseph pleaded guilty to all charges and was fined $10 000, payable in 12 months
or nine months in prison. [@1] Campbell pleaded not guilty to all charges and
was granted bail of $10 000 with one surety. [@1] She was due to reappear in the
same court on Tuesday 14 January 2025. [@1]
