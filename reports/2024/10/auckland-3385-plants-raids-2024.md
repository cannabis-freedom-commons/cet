Police across the Counties Manukau, Auckland City and Waitemata districts executed over 30 search warrants in the week before Labour Weekend, targeting an organised crime group using rented homes as indoor growing operations. [@1,2]

They seized and destroyed 3,385 cannabis plants at various growth stages and 48 kilograms of dried cannabis, with an estimated total value of $18 million based on estimated plant yield. [@1,2]

Eleven people were arrested. [@1,2] Police said most of those involved were in New Zealand illegally, and 11 Vietnamese nationals were deported to Vietnam. [@1,2] Officers warned landlords about tampered electricity meters and deliveries of growing nutrients at rental properties. [@2]
