Salto police carried out 28 simultaneous raids on the morning of Tuesday 22
October 2024. [@2] Police detained 31 people in the raids and took another 10 in
for questioning. [@2] The operation was carried out by the Salto police with the
Republican Guard's GEO unit, dog handlers and drone unit, and officers from the
Artigas, Tacuarembó and Flores departments. [@2] El Observador reported that 33
people were detained in the operation. [@1]

The raids followed an investigation by Salto's departmental anti-drug brigade
and the third-shift departmental prosecutor's office into a group selling and
stockpiling drugs in three neighbourhoods of the city. [@2] Salto police chief
Carlos Ayuto said the operation closed 25 drug sales points, known in Uruguay as
bocas de venta, and two drug storage sites. [@2] Most of the sales points raided
were run by the same family, with others linked commercially to some of its
members, El Observador reported. [@1]

Officers seized cannabis plants, marijuana, a .38 revolver with 11 cartridges, a
homemade crossbow-type weapon, 14 mobile phones, nine vehicles, cash and goods
reported stolen. [@2] Cocaine base paste was seized in the same raids and is not
counted in the cannabis figures. [@1]

In all, police said they intervened with 41 people: 31 detained and 10 taken in
for questioning. [@2] Those detained were handed to the Salto departmental
prosecutor's office, third shift, which is handling the case. [@2]
