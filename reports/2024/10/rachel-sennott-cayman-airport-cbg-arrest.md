United States actress Rachel Sennott said she was arrested at the start of a
Cayman Islands break when border patrol officers found a cannabis product called
Mom Grass in her hand luggage. [@1,2] Islands authorities found a prerolled joint in her carry-on, and she said she had not realized it was there. [@2] The 29-year-old from Connecticut described
the arrest on the 9 October edition of The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon.
[@1,2] She said she had not looked into international travel laws before the trip
and was pulled aside for a bag search at the airport on arrival. [@1] Sennott
said officers took her phone and passport and that the single phone call she was
allowed had to be to someone within the Cayman Islands. [@1,2] Her custody was
short-lived after her assistant, alarmed when she stopped answering text
messages, called her hotel, learned she had probably been detained and contacted
her lawyers, who reached the authorities. [@1]

The Mom Grass product was sold legally in the United States but was illegal in
the Cayman Islands. [@1] Sennott described it as CBD made to look like weed.
[@1] The brand actually contained CBG, or cannabigerol, rather than CBD, or
cannabidiol. [@1] It was a Mom Grass hemp CBG joint produced by Dad Grass, which she bought at Erewhon. [@2] CBG, like CBD, is derived from cannabis but is
non-psychoactive and does not cause a high like THC. [@1]

Sennott said she was let off with an adult caution and was refused a copy of her
mug shot despite asking for one. [@1,2] Customs and Border Control did not answer
questions about the incident before publication. [@1]
