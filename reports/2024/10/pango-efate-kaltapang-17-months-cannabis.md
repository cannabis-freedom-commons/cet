Mekana Kaltapang was sentenced to 17 months in jail after pleading guilty to
unlawful cultivation and possession of cannabis, contrary to the Dangerous Drugs
Act. [@1] The Supreme Court heard the case. [@1]

Kaltapang was arrested and cautioned on 5 July 2022 after a search by the Port
Vila Drugs Unit and Platoon 2 of the Vanuatu Mobile Force (VMF). [@1] The search
uncovered a pot plant outside his home in Pango village on Efate, where he had
been cultivating cannabis seedlings. [@1] The substances were tested and
confirmed to be cannabis, weighing 0.1 grams. [@1]

He pleaded guilty to both charges, unlawful cultivation of cannabis and
possession of cannabis, which fall under Vanuatu's Dangerous Drugs Act. [@1] The
case began with the police search at his home in Pango village in July 2022.
[@1] The 17 months in jail is the sentence for the two cannabis offences he
admitted. [@1]
