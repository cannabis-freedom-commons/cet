Pacific Region RCMP Federal Policing investigators arrested six people on 3
October 2024 when they executed search warrants at two dispensaries and five
homes on Vancouver Island. [@1] The dispensaries were Green Coast Dispensary in
Port Alberni and Coastal Storm Dispensary in Lantzville. [@2] Police linked the
warrants to an organized crime group allegedly involved in producing and
distributing illicit drugs, unregulated cannabis and contraband tobacco in Port
Alberni and Nanaimo. [@2] Officers also searched a suspected stash site in Port
Alberni and a storage and production facility adjacent to the Coastal Storm
dispensary. [@1] The facility included two modular trailers where cannabis
edibles were being produced, stored and distributed. [@1] The operation brought
together RCMP Federal Policing units with the Combined Forces Special
Enforcement Unit-BC, Criminal Intelligence Service BC and the Nanaimo and Port
Alberni RCMP detachments, plus other partner agencies. [@1]

Officers seized more than 120,000 cannabis edibles in packaging resembling
popular chocolate bars, potato chips, nacho chips, honey and other candies. [@1]
They also seized over 500 pounds of cannabis bud. [@1] They seized 2.2 pounds of
pressed cannabis resin. [@1] They seized over 19 pounds of shatter. [@1] They
seized more than 5,000 cannabis vape cartridges. [@1] Police also listed over 3
kg of psilocybin mushrooms, 1,740 psilocybin capsules, over 400 psilocybin
chocolates and candies and other psilocybin products, which are left out of the
cannabis totals. [@1] Officers seized five vehicles and two ATM machines
containing cash. [@1] Officers seized more than 164 master cases of contraband
tobacco, equating to 82,000 packs of cigarettes. [@1] Officers seized over
$400,000 in cash. [@1] Officers seized a shotgun and other paraphernalia. [@1]

Police said the edibles were produced in highly contaminated and unsanitary
modular trailers with unknown THC amounts and likely cross-contamination with
other drugs present there, posing serious health risks. [@1] RCMP Chief
Superintendent Stephen Lee urged extreme caution for anyone who already had or
came across such products, pointing to Halloween being just around the corner.
[@1]
