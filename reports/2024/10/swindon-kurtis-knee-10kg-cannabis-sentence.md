Kurtis Knee, 23, of Keswick Road, Swindon, pleaded guilty to possession with
intent to supply cannabis and possessing criminal property. [@1] He was jailed
for two years. [@1] Knee was sentenced at Swindon Crown Court on Monday. [@1]

Police found around 10kg of cannabis during a planned warrant search of the
property on 31 July. [@1] The cannabis and other controlled drugs seized from
the property were together worth about £100,000. [@1] More than £4,500 in cash
was also seized. [@1] Clothing and trainers valued at £5,689 were seized as
criminal property. [@1]

Insp David Tippetts said this was a 'significant seizure of drugs' from a person
who had 'continued to supply, despite being previously convicted'. [@1] He said
the Neighbourhood Policing Teams in Swindon would always respond to any
information received to disrupt this type of activity. [@1]
