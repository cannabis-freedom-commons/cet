Mpumalanga police arrested a 30-year-old man and recovered a large dagga haul
during a bust on the N12 in Delmas. [@1] A second account identifies the
arrested man as a 30-year-old from the Kingdom of Eswatini. [@2] Police
spokesperson Colonel Donald Mdhluli said the truck was travelling from Eswatini
towards Gauteng and that the operation followed intelligence and the Trilateral
Planning Cell. [@1] Delmas SAPS, Middelburg K9, the Middelburg Flying Squad Unit
and Mpumalanga Covert Intelligence intercepted the truck on the N12 freeway just
before the Dryden off-ramp. [@1]

During the first search officers smelled a strong dagga odour but could not find
the drugs. [@1] The truck was escorted to Delmas SAPS, where a K9 narcotic dog
conducted another search. [@1] Officers found a hidden compartment at the back
holding 63 bales of dagga wrapped in brown sellotape. [@1] Police said the dagga
weighed 1,180 kg and had a street value of at least R4.7 million. [@1] The
second account gives the same 1,180 kg weight and puts the street value at about
R4.7 million. [@2]

Mdhluli said the bales were marked and were suspected to have been packaged for
distribution. [@1] The second account says the truck was carrying the drugs
towards Johannesburg. [@2] The suspect was expected to appear in court soon.
[@2] Acting provincial commissioner Major General (Dr) Zeph Mkhwanazi lauded the
joint operation. [@2]
