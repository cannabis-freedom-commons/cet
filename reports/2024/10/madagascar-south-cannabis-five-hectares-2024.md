Madagascar's armed forces announced on 15 October 2024 that a wide military
operation against banditry in the south of the country had destroyed more than
five hectares of cannabis and eight sacks of the same substance. [@1] The
operation was run by a regional mixed operational staff bringing together the
military, the gendarmerie and the police. [@2]

Five bandits were killed, eight people were arrested and three weapons were
seized in the operation. [@2] Nearly a thousand stolen zebu were recovered and
were to be returned to their owners. [@1]

Four ministers, including the armed forces minister, went to the field on 14
October 2024 to supervise and reinforce the state's security strategy in the
south. [@3] The ministry said the strategy aimed to ensure peace in the south of
the country. [@1]
