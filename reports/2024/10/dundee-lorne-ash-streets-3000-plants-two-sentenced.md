Dundee Sheriff Court sentenced Indrit Saraci, 27, to three years and nine months
and Adiol Hila, 37, to three years and four months on 1 October 2024. [@2] The
two men had pleaded guilty to drugs offences linked to the cannabis cultivation.
[@3] Four men had initially been arrested and charged after the January raids.
[@4]

Police Scotland officers executed search warrants in Lorne Street and Ash Street
in Dundee on 8 January 2024. [@1] Officers found approximately 3,000 cannabis
plants across the two properties. [@1] Police estimated the value of the
cultivation linked to the two men at £1.7 million. [@2] Police had initially
estimated the same haul of about 3,000 plants at over £2.25 million. [@4]

Detective Constable Luke Doogan said the case showed police determination to
remove harmful substances from communities and hold suppliers accountable. [@1]
He said the case highlighted continued commitment to the Serious Organised Crime
Taskforce and the national serious and organised crime strategy. [@1] He said
public support was vital and urged people to look out for signs of cannabis
cultivations and report concerns. [@1]
