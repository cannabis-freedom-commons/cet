The Police Regional Office Cordillera Administrative Region announced on Tuesday
that Benguet police had discovered and destroyed marijuana plants worth P1.73
million. [@1] A screengrab of a Benguet PNP video showed agents destroying the
marijuana plants. [@1]

Operatives found 2,650 fully grown marijuana plants valued at P530,000 on the
Monday before that announcement. [@1] In the same find, operatives recovered
10,000 grams of dried marijuana stalks valued at P1.2 million. [@1] The Benguet
Police Provincial Office reported that the stalks and plants came from four
plantation sites covering 530 square metres along a communal forest in Barangay
Tacadang in Kibungan town. [@1]

Authorities documented, uprooted and burned the stalks and plants on site. [@1]
Samples from the seizure were turned over to the Regional Forensic Unit CAR.
