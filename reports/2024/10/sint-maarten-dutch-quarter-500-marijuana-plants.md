The suspected person was not present when officers searched the property. [@1]
The search took place on 5 October 2024 at a property on Gibbs Drive in Dutch
Quarter. [@2] Police said they received information from people who had
witnessed the illegal pot farm. [@1] Acting on information from several sources,
officers initiated an investigation and went to the location. [@1] Officers
encountered a substantial number of marijuana plants being cultivated on the
lot. [@1]

The Sint Maarten Police Force confiscated more than 500 marijuana plants of
different sizes. [@2] Police described the property as a hot spot for illegal
pot-farming in a residential area. [@1] The site was described in a second
account as a large illicit marijuana plantation. [@2]

KPSM reminded the public that the laws governing the cultivation, distribution
and sale of illegal substances, including marijuana, are strict and clear. [@1]
“Our officers are committed to upholding these laws and ensuring the safety of
the community,” police management said. [@1] The investigation was ongoing, and
further updates were promised as new information became available. [@1]
