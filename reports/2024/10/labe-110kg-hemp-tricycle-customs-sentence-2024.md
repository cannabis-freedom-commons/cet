Customs officers in Labé stopped a moto-tricycle carrying 110 kg of Indian hemp
in the town on Thursday 10 October 2024. [@1] They arrested one suspect and took
him, with the seized drug, before the public prosecutor at the court of first
instance in Labé. [@1] The Guinean Press Agency reported that the parcels were
shown at the regional customs directorate on 17 October 2024 in the presence of
administrative, security and judicial authorities. [@2]

Colonel Aly Nestor Fancinadouno, the regional customs inspector, said the drug
was packed in six parcels and that the interception took place on the road
leading to the Elhadj Oumar Tall camp. [@1] He said the consignment had passed
through Madina Woula, Mamou, Dalaba and Pita before reaching Labé, and that its
final destination was Koundara. [@1] The intended recipient, he said, was a man
known as Elhadj in the Daka district of Labé, where the parcels were to be
delivered. [@1] The Guinean Press Agency reported that the tricycle's rider
fled. [@2] The agency named the man held as Djibril Bangoura, a professional
driver, who said the parcel was not his and that he had come to represent the
brother of a friend. [@2]

The court of first instance in Labé convicted Djibril Bangoura of detention of
narcotics, Indian hemp, and sentenced him on Wednesday 29 October 2024 to one
year in prison and a fine of 5 million Guinean francs. [@3] The presiding judge,
Boubacar 3 Barry, announced the ruling after the court deliberated in open
session. [@3] Prosecutor Maurice Onivogui said drug consumption was growing in
Labé and that those responsible were being tried and convicted. [@1]
