A young man was arrested on Thursday 17 October 2024 in Sandéou village, in the
commune of Savè in Benin's Collines department, for illegally growing cannabis,
known in French as chanvre indien. [@2] Officers of the Monka border police
station carried out the operation on precise intelligence received by the local
police. [@2] His file was to be transferred to the central office for the
repression of illegal drug trafficking for the procedure to continue. [@2]

He was placed in police custody. [@1] La Nouvelle Tribune reported that he would
answer for his acts before the competent judicial authorities in the coming
days. [@1] Le béninois libéré reported that he would soon be presented to the
special prosecutor of the CRIET, Benin's court for economic crimes and
terrorism. [@4]

Bénin Web TV reported that he was found growing the plant on a vast area of
land. [@3] An account in Le béninois libéré said the arrest formed part of a
broader fight against drug trafficking in the commune of Savè. [@4] Local
administrative authorities welcomed the operation as a significant step in the
fight against illicit activity in the region. [@4]
