Two men were arrested after officers seized parcels of dried leaves believed to
be marijuana at Cunningham Old Road in Nasinu. [@1] The raid was conducted at
the home of the two suspects and recovered more than 40 large parcels, more than
400 sachets and a bottle of seeds, all believed to be marijuana. [@1] The
operation was within the Fiji Police Southern Division area, and ACP Livai Driu
said the substances were believed intended for sale. [@1] The two men remained
in custody while investigations continued. [@1]

Police said they would investigate the source of the substances and possible
proceeds of crime. [@1] ACP Driu said the sale and distribution of drugs often
increased in the lead-up to the festive season. [@1] He said divisional
operations with stakeholders would work together to disrupt and dismantle the
networks involved in drug sales. [@1] He added that police were seeking the
support of the Vanua (traditional Fijian community groups), religious
organisations, civil society and other stakeholders for work to reduce the
supply of illicit drugs. [@1]

The seized substances were sent for analysis. [@1]
