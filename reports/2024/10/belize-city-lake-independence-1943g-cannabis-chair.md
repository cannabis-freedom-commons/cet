Police searched a residence in Belize City on the basis of intelligence on 30
October 2024. [@1] The operation took place in the Lake Independence area on a
Wednesday night. [@2] It brought together the Special Patrol Unit, the Quick
Response Team and the K-9 Unit. [@2] Belize Live News described the target of
the raid as a well-known gang figure associated with the PIV gang. [@3] The
primary suspect was not at the property, and police issued a warrant for his
arrest. [@3]

The K-9, Axel, indicated the presence of drugs in a chair on the property. [@1]
Officers inspected the chair and uncovered several parcels of suspected cannabis
wrapped in transparent plastic. [@1] The cannabis weighed 1,943 grams, just
under two kilograms. [@1] A man present at the residence was cautioned, arrested
and taken to the Raccoon Street Police Station with the seized drugs. [@1] He
was named as 40-year-old Chester Noralez, of Fern Lane. [@2] He faced a charge
of possession of controlled drugs with intent to supply. [@2]

Police said they planned to find and arrest two more people connected to the
case. [@1] Shortly after nine o'clock that night, officers went on to an
abandoned cement house on Marigold Lane. [@2]
