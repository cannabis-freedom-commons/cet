Qatar's Land Customs Department thwarted an attempt to smuggle hashish through
customs. [@1] Inspectors became suspicious of one of the vehicles entering the
country. [@1] After the passengers were referred for a self-inspection, two
pieces of hashish were found hidden under their clothes. [@1]

The seized substance weighed approximately 80 grams. [@1]
