Police in Honduras seized 70 cannabis plants from a property in the village of
Luquigüe, in the municipality of Olanchito, Yoro department, under a search
warrant. [@1] The operation was carried out by the National Police's
Departmental Prevention Unit No. 18 (UDEP-18), which targeted a marijuana
plantation spread across a plant nursery and a small plot of land. [@1] No one
was in the dwelling when officers entered it. [@1]

Officers seized 20 plants about 30 centimetres tall inside the house and another
50 in the nursery. [@1] The plantations were eradicated and placed under
investigation to establish their origin. [@1] Police said they had begun
inquiries to identify those responsible and bring them before the courts. [@1]
The seizure was described as a significant blow against drug trafficking in a
region that has evolved from a transit route into an area of illicit-drug
production. [@1]
