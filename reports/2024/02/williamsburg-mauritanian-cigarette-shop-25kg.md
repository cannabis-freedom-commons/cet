A Mauritanian man was arrested in Williamsburg, a neighbourhood of New York City
in the borough of Brooklyn, for the sale and possession of cannabis. [@1]
Alakhbar reported the arrest on Tuesday 6 February. [@1]

The arrest followed a police search of a cigarette shop where the man worked.
[@1] During the search, police found 2.5 kilograms of cannabis. [@1]
