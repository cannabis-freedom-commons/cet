Oklahoma Bureau of Narcotics agents served search warrants at ML Growing LLC and
Ian Green LLC in Lahoma, Oklahoma, on Wednesday 7 February 2024. [@1] The bureau
said both operations were tied to an ongoing investigation into illegal
marijuana distribution. [@1]

Agents with the bureau's Marijuana Enforcement Team seized several thousand
marijuana plants and processed marijuana during the searches. [@1] No one was
arrested at the time; OBN said arrests were pending as the case moved forward.
[@1]

The Garfield County Sheriff's Department, the Major County Sheriff's Department
and the North Enid Police Department assisted in the operation. [@1] The bureau
said its marijuana teams had shut down more than 2,500 grows, arrested over 250
people and seized over 975,000 pounds of marijuana since 2021, in investigations
into fraudulently licensed grows and out-of-state black-market supply. [@1]
