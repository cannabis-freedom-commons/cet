The two people held were Gwendoline Olochi Okoro, a 46-year-old Nigerian woman,
and Nelson Itong, a 45-year-old Cameroonian man. [@1] Police in Libreville
picked them up at their respective homes after two days of surveillance aided by
informants. [@1] The operation followed police tracking with the help of
informants. [@1]

The seizure took place on 22 February in the Nkembo and Lalala areas of
Libreville. [@1] Officers of the Ntoum branch of the Office central de lutte
anti-drogue (Oclad), Gabon's central anti-drug office, made the seizure. [@1] A
thorough search found 10 bales of Indian hemp concealed among the suspects'
belongings. [@1] The investigation report said Olochi, believed to have pursued
the trade for a long time, had travelled to Cameroon about a month earlier to
restock. [@1] Itong was to distribute the product once it reached Libreville.
[@1]

At the station both suspects quickly admitted the facts they were accused of.
[@1] They were expected to be presented before the Libreville prosecutor's
office soon. [@1]
