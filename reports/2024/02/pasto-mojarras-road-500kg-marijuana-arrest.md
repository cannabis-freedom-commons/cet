Officers of the Nariño Transit and Transport Section arrested a man in flagrante
delicto at kilometre 85 of the Pasto–Mojarras road for trafficking,
manufacturing and/or carrying narcotics. [@1] The driver tried to escape in a
gray van when stopped by police and was intercepted metres later. [@1]

Searching the driver and the vehicle, officers found 113 packages wrapped in
black plastic containing a plant substance identified as marijuana, weighing 500
kilos. [@1] Police seized 500 kilograms of marijuana together with two mobile
devices and a gray truck in the roadside operation. [@1] The arrested person,
the drug, two cell phones and the vehicle were placed at the disposal of the
Attorney General's Office. [@1]

Semana reported that Nariño authorities intercepted a man with at least 500
kilograms of marijuana on the road from Pasto to Mojarras. [@2] TuBarco reported
that the capture took place during National Police registration and control work
on the Pasto–Mojarras road and that the man was carrying 500 kilograms of
marijuana. [@3]
