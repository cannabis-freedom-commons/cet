Royanson Arrington, 32, of Purcell Estate, was charged with two counts of
unlawful possession of controlled drugs. [@2] Acting on information received,
Royal Virgin Islands Police Force officers arrested multiple people after
intercepting a vehicle in Fish Bay, Tortola, on Wednesday 14 February 2024 and
uncovering illegal drugs. [@2] Arrington remained in custody pending his
appearance at the Magistrate’s Court. [@1] He had been arrested on identical
charges just over four years earlier. [@1] In addition to that arrest, Arrington
was sentenced to 36 months in prison for theft in 2015 and had two prior theft
convictions from 2007 and 2013. [@1]

Police did not disclose the quantity of drugs that was discovered but suggested
it included cocaine and marijuana. [@1] The Royal Virgin Islands Police Force
press release of 15 February 2024 did not disclose what the illegal drugs found
were. [@2] The arrest came amid heightened tension over an apparent escalation
of serious crimes in the territory. [@1]
