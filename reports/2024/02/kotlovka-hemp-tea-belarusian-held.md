On 8 February 2024, border guards detained a Belarusian man who was returning by
car from Lithuania through the Kotlovka checkpoint, opposite Lavoriskes, over a
package of factory-made tea containing hemp. [@1] The man was travelling to
Belarus from Lithuania by car when the incident occurred on 8 February at
Kotlovka, adjacent to Lavoriskes. [@2] During the inspection of the boot, a
service dog reacted to the tea package. [@2] The man explained that he had
bought the herbal tea in Lithuania. [@2]

He said he bought it in a grocery store. [@3] He said he had been told there
would be questions at the border. [@3] Another account described the package as
herbal tea with marijuana carried in the car. [@3]

The substance was seized for forensic examination, with further proceedings
pending. [@2] The border committee warned that illegal cross-border movement of
narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances, their precursors and analogues carries
restriction of liberty for up to four years or imprisonment for three to seven
years. [@2]
