Gendarmes of the Cuay Grande control post, part of Escuadrón 57 “Santo Tomé”,
intercepted two parcels on a courier lorry travelling from Puerto Iguazú,
Misiones, to Villa Lugano, Buenos Aires, on 30 January 2024. [@1] Opening the
parcels revealed 22 kilos 440 grams of cannabis sativa. [@1] A drug-detection
dog named “Astra” had marked the two parcels during the check of a general cargo
lorry. [@4]

The Federal Court and the Federal Prosecutor’s Office of Paso de los Libres
ordered a controlled delivery of the parcels. [@1] On 7 February 2024 officers
of the Cinturón Sur complex-crimes unit kept a parcel centre in Buenos Aires
under discreet watch. [@1] Two men were arrested when they came to collect the
parcels. [@3] Officers seized the car the men arrived in, three mobile phones
and cash. [@4] The two detained men were held incommunicado. [@2]

The court ordered the detention of those involved and the confiscation of the
drug and other items of interest. [@1] The force put the value of the parcels’
marijuana at 47,432,550 pesos. [@2] The arrests took place in Villa Lugano,
Comuna 8 of the city of Buenos Aires. [@3]
