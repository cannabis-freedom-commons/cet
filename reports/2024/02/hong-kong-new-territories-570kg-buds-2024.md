Hong Kong Customs raided a logistics warehouse in the New Territories on 26
February 2024 and seized about 570 kilograms of suspected cannabis buds from
about 145 carton boxes. [@2] Customs estimated the market value at about HK$115
million and called it the largest cannabis-buds case the department had detected
in town. [@2] The buds were hidden in 145 boxes mixed with another 387 boxes of
electronic products. [@1]

Officers found the cannabis in four of 20 pallets, 145 of 532 boxes declared as
household electrical appliances. [@3]

After a follow-up investigation, customs officers on 8 March arrested a
53-year-old man who claimed to be a logistics company owner and was suspected of
connection with the case. [@2] He was a local man, arrested at his home in the
New Territories and released on bail pending investigation. [@3] Customs said
the investigation was continuing and that further arrests were not ruled out.
[@2]

The department said enforcement since January had brought the amount of
suspected cannabis seized from January to February to about 790 kilograms, a
figure that includes this warehouse case and other operations. [@2] Under the
Dangerous Drugs Ordinance, trafficking in a dangerous drug carries a maximum
penalty of a HK$5 million fine and life imprisonment. [@2]
