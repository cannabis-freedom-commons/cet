On Friday 9 February 2024, Customs Anti-Narcotics Unit officers conducted an
operation at Eccles Housing Scheme, East Bank Demerara, and intercepted a motor
vehicle driven by one man. [@2] The Customs Anti-Narcotics Unit arrested Rohan
“Sauce” Rambarran of Eccles Housing Scheme, East Bank Demerara, allegedly with
250 brick-like parcels of foreign marijuana known as Creepy. [@1]

A search of the vehicle revealed five large bales wrapped in black plastic,
containing 250 brick-like parcels suspected to be cannabis. [@1] CANU said the
suspected cannabis was tested and determined to be foreign marijuana known as
Creepy, and officers escorted Rambarran to CANU headquarters with the cannabis
and ammunition. [@2]

A further search of the residence found seven live 9mm rounds of ammunition in a
magazine. [@2] CANU said investigations were ongoing. [@2]

In December 2009, Rambarran and four others were convicted of importing,
possessing and trafficking 91.3 kilograms of cannabis and 119.4 kilograms of
cocaine concealed in logs found on 29 November 2005. [@1] He was sentenced
concurrently to 25 years for trafficking cannabis and 30 years for trafficking
cocaine. [@1] On 29 August 2019 the Barbados Court of Appeal ordered his release
on time served, and he was deported to Guyana. [@1]
