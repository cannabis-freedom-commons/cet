DNCD agents seized 19 packages of marijuana during inspection work at Puerto de
Haina Oriental, in Santo Domingo Oeste municipality. [@1] Agents and military
personnel were checking dozens of tanks full of food that had reached the
terminal from the United States when a canine unit alerted to strange substances
in one of them. [@1] On the acting prosecutor's instructions a stricter
inspection found 19 packages of marijuana inside the tank, hidden among
clothing, tinned food and several boxes of cereal. [@1]

The packages, wrapped in plastic film and vacuum-packed, weighed 23 pounds
(about 10.43 kg), according to an analysis by the national forensic institute
INACIF. [@1] Authorities established preliminarily that the tank was sent by a
man living on Avenida Audubon in New York and was to be received by a woman
living in Montellano municipality, Puerto Plata province. [@1]

The Ministerio Público and the DNCD continued to investigate the case while
intensifying interdiction operations against drug trafficking at airports, ports
and across the country. [@1]
