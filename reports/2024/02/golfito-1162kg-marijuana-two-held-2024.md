Costa Rica's National Coast Guard Service intercepted a boat carrying marijuana
and cocaine in the Pacific off the country's southern coast. [@1] A Colombian
and a Panamanian were arrested and handed to the prosecutor's office. [@1]

The boat was found at sea with more than 2,000 bales of drugs on the Thursday,
after a United States aircraft on a joint patrol flight alerted the authorities.
[@1] It was 116 km off the coast of Golfito when coast guard officers stopped it
and took it to port for inspection, a count of the drugs and the detention of
its crew. [@1]

The Drug Control Police's final count was 2,056 packages of marijuana, 1,787 of
about 500 grams each and 269 of about a kilo each. [@4] The marijuana weighed
1,162 kg in total. [@2] Officers also seized 200 packages of cocaine of about a
kilo each, which are not included in the cannabis figures. [@2]

The boat was 36 feet long, had no name or registration and carried three
outboard motors. [@2] Coast guard officers coordinated with the deputy
prosecutor's office against drug trafficking and related crimes to take the boat
to the dock at Golfito. [@4] The boat was left in judicial custody at the Puerto
Golfito coast guard station on the prosecutor's order. [@3]
