Law enforcement officers said they seized pounds of drugs from a home in Camden
County. [@1] On Wednesday 14 February 2024, deputies, Highway Patrol troopers
and task force officers from the Lake Area Narcotics Enforcement Group served a
search warrant at a home just off Bittersweet Road. [@1] The Camden County
Sheriff's Office issued a press release on the seizure. [@1] The release did not
state whether anyone was arrested. [@1]

During the search of the home, investigators found ten pounds (about 4.54 kg) of
marijuana. [@1] They also found four pounds of psilocybin mushrooms and 1,383
psilocybin mushroom edible products infused with nine pounds of psilocybin
mushrooms. [@1] The haul further included one pound of THC wax, 5,351 THC
products infused with 18 pounds of THC, and oxycodone and alprazolam pills. [@1]
The mushrooms, THC products and pills were part of the same seizure but were
reported as substances separate from the marijuana. [@1]

Investigators said the drugs had a street value of $900,000, an estimate
covering all the items seized rather than the marijuana alone. [@1] They also
seized $25,000 in cash. [@1]
