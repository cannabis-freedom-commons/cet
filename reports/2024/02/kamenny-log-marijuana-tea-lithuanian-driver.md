Customs officers at the Kamenny Log checkpoint found tea in a car driven by a
Lithuanian citizen entering Belarus. [@1] Grodno Regional Customs seized the tea
and an examination found a drug in it. [@1] The packages were sent to the Grodno
regional office of the State Committee of Forensic Examinations for testing for
narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances, their analogues and precursors. [@1]
The seized packages carried the labels "RELAX SU KANAPIU LAPAIS", "ZOLYNELIS"
and "KANAPIU IR PIPIRMECIU LAPAI". [@2] The forensic experts concluded that the
mixtures submitted to them contained parts of marijuana. [@1]

Grodno Regional Customs is conducting a check into the incident. [@1] The
committee said tea, chocolate, alcohol and other products containing parts of
Cannabis plants are legally sold in some European countries but may not be
imported into the customs territory of the Eurasian Economic Union. [@1] Moving
narcotic drugs, psychotropic substances or their analogues and precursors across
the border carries criminal liability in Belarus. [@1]
