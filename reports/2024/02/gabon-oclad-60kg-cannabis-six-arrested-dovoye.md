Officers of Gabon's Central Anti-Drug Office (Oclad) arrested six suspected
cannabis traffickers on 26 February 2024 at Dovoye village near Moulengui-Binza,
the capital of Mongo department in Nyanga province. [@1] All six were Gabonese,
and the operation was led by Captain Judicaël Ditsoga and his men against a
dealing network said to have branches across Nyanga province. [@1] Those named
were Evert Chely Mombo, Will Smith Moutsinga, known as le Sénateur and aged 26,
Jerry Moussotsi, aged 30, Ludger Tate Koumbe, Nel Garcia Mouvengui and Gilles
Mounguengui Mabika. [@1]

Investigators said Evert Chely Mombo, a final-year pupil at Lycée Général
Nazaire-Boulingui, was the main seller of cannabis to pupils at Tchibanga's
secondary schools, and that Will Smith Moutsinga, known as le Sénateur, was the
gang's leader in Nyanga and beyond. [@1] Ludger Tate Koumbe was described as the
network's courier, whose role was to deliver the cannabis to resellers and
clients. [@2] Officers seized 60 kg of cannabis at Dovoye village near
Moulengui-Binza. [@3] A source familiar with the operation put the market value
of the seized cannabis at 6 million FCFA. [@1]

The group was reported to have sold cannabis throughout Nyanga province. [@2] At
the time of the March reporting the six suspects were awaiting presentation
before the prosecutor's office in Tchibanga. [@2]
