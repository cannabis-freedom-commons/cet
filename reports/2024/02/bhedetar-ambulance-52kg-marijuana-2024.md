Police seized 52 kg of marijuana from an ambulance at Bhedetar in Dhankuta
district. [@1] The ambulance, registration Me 1 Cha 2032, was travelling from
Sangurigadhi toward Biratnagar. [@2] The marijuana was packed in 52 plastic
packets of one kilogram each. [@2]

The ambulance is registered under Haldibari Sewa Samaj, a charity in Jhapa. [@1]
Kantipur reported that the ambulance had brought a patient from Nobel Hospital
in Biratnagar to Sangurigadhi Rural Municipality-3 and was returning when it
carried the load. [@2] The chief of the Area Police Office, Jiban Karki, gave
that account. [@2] Hindustan reported that patrol police of the Area Police
Office Bhedetar stopped the ambulance as it came from Dhankuta toward Dharan on
Saturday evening. [@3]

Police detained the driver, Jayadev Chel, 48, from ward 8 of Biratnagar
Metropolitan City, for investigation. [@1] The Hindi account said the driver was
being questioned in the custody of the District Police Office. [@3] Police said
detailed investigation into the incident was under way. [@1]
