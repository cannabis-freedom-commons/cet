Four people were arrested in the Tazara area of Kasama, in Zambia's Northern
Province, for unlawfully cultivating fresh cannabis plants. [@1] The Drug
Enforcement Commission, Zambia's drug enforcement agency, announced the arrests.
[@1] The commission put the weight of the fresh cannabis plants at 208.6
kilograms. [@1] Its Northern Province office carried out the operation, which
the commission called successful. [@1]

The commission said the operation took place on Thursday 15 February 2024. [@1]
Public relations officer Mwenge Mulenga announced the arrests in a statement on
Saturday 17 February 2024. [@1] The statement named the first of the four as
Kasonde George, a 47-year-old man, and said the four were jointly charged. [@1]

In the same statement, the commission announced the arrest of a 39-year-old
American national at Harry Mwanga Nkumbula International Airport in Livingstone
for trafficking in marijuana. [@1] The Livingstone airport arrest is a separate
case from the Kasama cultivation arrests. [@1]
