Anti-narcotics agents of the Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency, along with police
from the Kalinga Police Provincial Office, found the cannabis inside an abandoned
silver and green Mitsubishi Pajero with plate number UST-711 in Mosimos, Tabuk
City, at about 4:45 a.m. on Tuesday. [@1,2] Police said they were responding to a
tip about the transport of marijuana from Tinglayan to Tabuk City. [@1,2]

The haul comprised 205 marijuana bricks, 45 tubular forms and 25 vacuum-sealed
packages of dried marijuana leaves, stalks and fruiting tops, which Philstar valued
at P28,050,000 and BusinessWorld rounded to P28 million. [@1,2] Operatives also
found six grams of suspected shabu valued at P40,800, a non-cannabis drug left out
of the figures above. [@1,2]

The inventory was witnessed by Department of Justice officials, members of the
media and barangay representatives, and the drugs and other evidence were secured
by the police for further legal proceedings. [@1,2]
