The Royal Saint Lucia Police Force conducted a joint operation within the
Northern and Central Divisions on Thursday 1 February 2024. [@1] It involved
police officers from the Criminal Investigations Department Castries, Gros Islet
Patrol Team, Special Patrol Team, and the Bicycle Patrol Unit. [@1] Officers
executed five search warrants and apprehended eight individuals. [@1] The RSLPF
said the ongoing operations were aimed at dismantling criminal networks and
addressing public safety. [@1]

A house in Ferrand Cul De Sac, Castries, was searched and yielded one pistol and
eight rounds of .45 calibre ammunition; two people were arrested for possession
of a firearm and ammunition. [@1] A marijuana plantation was discovered within
100 yards of that residence and, as a result, 800 marijuana plants were
destroyed. [@2]

A house in Dugard, Micoud, yielded one illegal immigrant from Venezuela; two
Saint Lucians were arrested for harbouring him and he was arrested for illegal
entry. [@2] At an establishment in the south, a Jamaican national who had
overstayed his permit and two Colombian nationals were arrested. [@2] The
operation ended with a strong police presence in known crime hotspots in
Vieux-Fort, with targeted sweeps. [@1]
