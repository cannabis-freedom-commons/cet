Four people were detained after an investigation into a suspected drug
trafficking network and the discovery of cannabis in a container at Bizerte
port. [@1] Tunisia's Interior Ministry said the investigation led to the arrest
of an initial suspect and three alleged accomplices; three others were wanted.
[@1] The ministry said officers found 1,000 slabs weighing 100 kilograms among
other goods in the container during a search on 8 February 2024. [@1]

Directinfo described the material as approximately 100 kilograms of hashish in
about 1,000 slabs. [@2] According to the ministry, the first suspect confessed
that he and an accomplice were to open a container holding cannabis at the port.
[@1] After prosecutors authorised the search, customs officers, anti-drug
officers and forensic police inspected the container in the presence of the
transport company's legal representative. [@1] Investigators also seized cash,
luxury watches, bank cheques and documents, according to Directinfo. [@2]
