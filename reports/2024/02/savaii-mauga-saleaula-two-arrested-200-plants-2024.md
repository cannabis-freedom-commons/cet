Samoa Police, Prisons and Corrections Services arrested two men, aged 43 and 40,
in a drug raid at Mauga in Saleaula, on the island of Savai'i. [@1] The search
was carried out on the morning of 16 February 2024 at a plantation and a house
in Mauga. [@2] The 43-year-old is a man of Saleaula and Mauga, and the
40-year-old is a man of Samalaeulu. [@2] Both men were held in police custody.
[@2]

The search found more than 200 marijuana plants, along with a firearm and
ammunition. [@2] The English-language release said fresh marijuana plants were
seized from the premises, as well as a firearm and ammunition. [@1] The police
statement gave no value for the drugs confiscated. [@1]

Samoa Newshub described the operation as targeting areas suspected of drug
trafficking and said it yielded what it called substantial evidence implicating
the suspects. [@3] Samoa Newshub said the suspects were detained pending further
investigation and legal proceedings. [@3] The police service thanked the public
for its consistent support and for promptly reporting criminal activity in the
community. [@1]
