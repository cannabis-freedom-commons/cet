On Wednesday 14 August 2024, NDLEA operatives supported by soldiers, other security agencies and community youths raided the Ise forest reserve at Saalaja camp in Ise-Ekiti. [@1,2] They destroyed 21,800 kilograms of cannabis growing on 20 hectares of farmland. [@1,2] Agency spokesman Femi Babafemi announced the raid in Abuja on Sunday 18 August as part of a week of operations across several states. [@1,2] No arrests in the Ise raid were reported. [@1,2]
