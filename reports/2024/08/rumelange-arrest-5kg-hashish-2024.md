A police patrol in the Rue du Parc in Rumelange approached a man who was visibly
uneasy at the officers' presence on the evening of Friday 2 August 2024. [@1] He
tried to walk away but was stopped shortly afterwards and checked because of his
suspicious behaviour. [@1] Officers found about 100 grams of hashish in his bag.
[@1]

Police then searched his home, suspecting he held a larger quantity of drugs.
[@1] The search seized about 5 kg of hashish, a small quantity of marijuana and
some mushrooms. [@1] Officers also found a knife, three mobile phones and a
large sum of money. [@1] RTL reported that the home search turned up about 5 kg
of hashish, an illegal knife, three phones and a large sum of cash. [@2]

On the prosecutor's order the suspect was arrested and brought before an
examining magistrate on the morning of Saturday 3 August 2024. [@3] Police
described the man as a suspected drug dealer. [@3]
