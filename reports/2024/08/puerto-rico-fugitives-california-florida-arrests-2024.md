The United States Marshals Violent Offenders Task Force captured two fugitives
wanted in Puerto Rico, as announced by federal prosecutor W. Stephen Muldrow.
[@1] Luis Crespo-Cumba, 48, faced a federal arrest order for money laundering
and conspiracy to possess cocaine and marijuana with intent to distribute in
Puerto Rico and the United States. [@1] Crespo-Cumba was arrested on 20 August
in the Central District of California. [@1] Crespo-Cumba was described as one of
the most wanted fugitives from Mayaguez and was accused of leading a massacre in
2020. [@1] In January 2023 a Mayaguez judge had issued an arrest order against
him for murder and firearms-law violations with bail set at 2.5 million dollars.
[@1] Jose Pabon-Custodio, 35, was accused of conspiracy to distribute controlled
substances and firearms-law violations. [@1] Pabon-Custodio was arrested without
incident in Florida on 21 August. [@1]

Since 2013 the drug-trafficking organization Pabon-Custodio belonged to had
distributed heroin, crack, cocaine and marijuana in southern Puerto Rico,
including the Jose N. Gandara and Hogares de Portugues public housing projects
in Ponce. [@1] A federal grand jury in Puerto Rico returned a 60-count
superseding indictment charging 18 individuals with importing cocaine and
marijuana into Puerto Rico and the continental United States and with money
laundering. [@2] According to the indictment, the defendants conspired to
possess cocaine and marijuana with intent to distribute. [@2] The organization
also received multi-kilogram shipments of marijuana from the continental United
States for distribution throughout Puerto Rico. [@2]
