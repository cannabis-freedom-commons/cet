Nepal police seized 100 kg of cannabis from a car with an Indian number plate in
Koshi Province on Tuesday 13 August 2024. [@1] Nepal police said in a statement
that the car carried a number plate from West Bengal. [@1] The cannabis was
seized from the car at a bridge in Dharan Municipality-5 in eastern Nepal. [@1]
Police identified the interception site as Panamari bridge in Dharan
Municipality-5, where officers encountered the car on the road. [@1]

As police reached Panamari bridge, the driver left the car on the road and ran
toward the nearby forest. [@1] Officers searching the vehicle recovered the
cannabis packed in a plastic bag inside the car. [@1] No suspect was detained at
the scene because the driver had fled toward the nearby forest, leaving the car
behind. [@1]

Police launched a hunt for the car driver, who remained on the run. [@1] Nepal
Police said further investigation into the matter was under way. [@1] The 100 kg
figure is the police account of the load found in the abandoned car at the
bridge. [@1]
