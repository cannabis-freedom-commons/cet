Namibian Police and Namibia Revenue Agency (Namra) officials stopped a truck
driver between the Ariamsvlei and Nakop borders, close to the Ariamsvlei Border
Post, at around 00h15 on Thursday morning, after an intelligence-led operation.
[@1] A search of the truck found 100.4 kg of cannabis worth N$500 000, the
police and Namra stated. [@1] The Namibian Sun also reported the bust, saying a
search found 100.40 kilograms of cannabis valued at N$500 000 and that the
55-year-old South African suspect was arrested. [@2] The Windhoek Observer
reported the same stop, saying officials intercepted another truck driver on the
road between the South African and Namibian entry points near the Ariamsvlei
Border Post at around 00h15 on Thursday. [@3]

A 55-year-old South African man was taken into custody and was scheduled to
appear in the Karasburg Magistrate's Court on Friday. [@1] The Windhoek Observer
says the suspect was set to appear in the Karasburg Magistrate Court on
Thursday, a day earlier than The Namibian reports. [@3]

The seizure was announced in a joint statement by police spokesperson deputy
commissioner Kauna Shikwambi and Namra spokesperson Yarukeekuro Ndorokaze, which
also reported a separate 120.6 kg cannabis seizure in Windhoek the day before.
[@1] Police and Namra said they remain dedicated to stopping the illegal
importation and distribution of controlled substances. [@1]
