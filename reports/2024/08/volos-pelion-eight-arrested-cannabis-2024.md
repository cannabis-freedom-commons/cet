Eight people were arrested in a joint Greek and Israeli police operation that
seized drugs in Volos. [@2] The operation ran from Monday 12 August 2024 into
the early hours of Tuesday 13 August. [@2] The Israel Police said four Israelis
in their 30s and 40s were arrested early Tuesday and that four non-Israeli
suspects were also arrested. [@1] The suspects were brought before a prosecutor,
and police said they were still looking for other members of the network. [@2] A
44-year-old was described as the leader who coordinated the operation and the
movement of the drugs. [@2]

Hellenic Police said the group imported drugs from western Europe into Greece
and then transshipped them to Israel. [@2] Greek police said the network
smuggled the drugs by sea using yachts. [@2] Officers arrested suspects at a
roadblock in Agria as they left Volos and searched motorhomes in the Kala Nera
area of Pelion. [@3] Police found evidence that drugs supplied from European
countries, potentially the Netherlands and Germany, were hidden in trailers that
travelled through Bulgaria to Greece. [@1]

Greek police said about 130 kilograms of MDMA, ketamine and cannabis were
confiscated in the raid. [@1] A detailed inventory of the confiscated drugs
listed 7 grams of cannabis. [@3] The same inventory listed 53 kilograms and 240
grams of MDMA, which is not counted as cannabis. [@3] It also listed 50
kilograms and 250 grams of ketamine, which is not counted as cannabis. [@3]
Officers also seized 2,450 euros, 3,460 Israeli shekels, a vehicle, two
caravans, 13 mobile phones and 14 canisters. [@2] Police estimated the network's
illegal profits at more than 2.5 million euros. [@2]
