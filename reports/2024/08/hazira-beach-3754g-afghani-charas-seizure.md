Surat police's Special Operations Group seized Afghan-origin charas, which the
police described as cannabis resin, on Hazira beach. [@1] The drug was in
Starbucks Coffee packaging that had washed up on the beach, and police reached
the beach after fishermen noticed the packets. [@1] Three packets of charas were
found in an uninhabited area along the seashore, approximately 500 meters from
tank numbers 1001 and 1002 behind the Shell company located in the Hazira area.
[@2] The Fishermen's Watch Group at Hazira had informed the SOG that three
suspicious packets in Starbucks Coffee packaging had washed up on the shore.
[@2] The packets contained 3.754 kg of Afghani charas, with a total value
exceeding Rs 1.87 crore. [@2] A separate police account of the same recovery put
the charas at 3.5 kg and worth Rs 1.8 crore. [@1]

SOG sources said the charas was of the highest purity and costs Rs 50 lakh a
kilogram in the international market. [@1] Police suspected that drug
traffickers had discarded the packets into the sea to avoid detection by the
authorities. [@2] An SOG source said it was the same quality of charas found the
previous year at Suvali beach, whose packets bore the words Afghan Product, and
that FSL officials had confirmed the match. [@1]
