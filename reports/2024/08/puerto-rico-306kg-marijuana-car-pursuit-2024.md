Prefectura Naval Argentina officers seized more than 306 kilograms of marijuana
in an operation in Puerto Rico, Misiones. [@1] The procedure took place near
kilometre 1,733 of the Paraná River, at a spot known as Puerto Rinke. [@1]
Officers on control duty detected a car entering National Route 12 at high speed
that had an outstanding arrest warrant for robbery, and chased it. [@1]

Officers found the car on a side road and saw several rectangular packages
inside. [@1] A second Prefectura Naval Argentina notice of the seizure said the
load was distributed in 16 bundles holding 337 marijuana bricks valued at more
than 700 million pesos. [@2] Misiones Opina reported the operation took place in
the border zone of Puerto Rico, at a spot known as Puerto Rinke, and that the
700 million peso value was Prefectura's own calculation. [@3]

The Federal Prosecutor's Office in Oberá (Misiones), headed by Viviana Alejandra
Vallejos, intervened in the case. [@1]
