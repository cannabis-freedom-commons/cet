The Paktia security command said it had destroyed 138 jeribs of cannabis fields,
about 27.6 hectares, in Aryub Zazi district. [@1] Spokesman Munib Zadran told
Pajhwok that security forces destroyed the 138 jeribs over the previous three
days in Bilut Sufla, Bilut Ulya and some other areas. [@1] Zadran said the
campaign to destroy the cannabis fields was still under way in the district.
[@1] An official said on Tuesday that cannabis had been eradicated on 67.5 acres
of land in Zazi Aryub district of southeastern Paktia province. [@2]

Some residents of Zazi Aryub welcomed the start of the clearing but asked for an
alternative crop. [@1] Jannat Gul, a resident of Bilut Ulya, said local people
grew cannabis on their land out of necessity. [@1] He said they would stop
growing cannabis if the government and aid agencies supplied seed, fertiliser
and irrigation water. [@1] Tribal elder Anwar Siddiqi asked aid agencies and the
agriculture department to help farmers grow crops other than hashish. [@1]

Paktia security officials said they were making serious efforts to stop the
cultivation, trafficking and sale of any narcotics. [@1]
