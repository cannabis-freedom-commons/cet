Iraq's Interior Ministry spokesman and Security Media Cell chief, Major General
Miqdad Miri, announced on Friday that a drug trafficking network had been
dismantled and its members arrested. [@1] Miri said the Babil Drug Affairs
Directorate, working with the Najaf Directorate, arrested five members of the
network in a series of targeted security and intelligence operations. [@1]

Officers seized six kilograms of marijuana from the group in Najaf governorate.
[@1] The five were held under a judicial order under Article 28 of the Drug Law
and were to face justice. [@1]

The Interior Ministry said that between the start of 2024 and July it had
recorded 7,000 drug arrests, seizures of two tonnes of drugs and ten tonnes of
psychotropic substances, and 100 death sentences or life terms for trafficking.
[@1] Grand Ayatollah Ali al-Sistani has condemned drug use and trafficking as
strictly forbidden in Islam and urged Iraqis to report drug crimes to protect
families. [@1]
