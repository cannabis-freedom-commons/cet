Two men were stopped by Luxembourg customs officers during a routine check of
their Luxembourg-registered car, and both denied transporting illegal goods.
[@1] Sniffer dogs Gwen and Balto signalled two suspicious boxes in which the
cannabis was carefully wrapped in cellophane. [@1]

Helped by the dogs, the officers discovered 3.66 kg of cannabis hidden in the
two suspect boxes. [@1] After the discovery the officers searched the suspects'
home, deploying dogs trained to detect drugs and explosives. [@1] That search
seized a further 800 g of cannabis, two firearms with ammunition and packaging
material generally used in drug trafficking. [@1]

The two suspects were immediately presented to an investigating judge, who
ordered their arrest. [@1] The seized drugs and weapons were handed over to the
prosecutor's office. [@1] The operation was carried out in August 2024 and made
public only in January 2025 for investigative reasons. [@2]
