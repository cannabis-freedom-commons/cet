Tangipahoa Parish Sheriff's Office narcotics detectives executed a search
warrant at Jordan Recatto's residence in Hammond following months of
investigation. [@1] Deputies arrested 30-year-old Hammond man Jordan Recatto
following the narcotics investigation in Tangipahoa Parish. [@1] Authorities
said Recatto faces multiple drug-related charges in connection with a
sophisticated drug operation involving the sale of high-grade marijuana,
marijuana variations, and other drugs. [@1]

During the search, investigators said they found evidence of a highly developed
production and sales operation that included on hand marijuana, psilocybin
mushrooms, cocaine, Ecstasy, Molly, Xanax and Tramadol pills, THC cartridges,
pens and edibles, and several doses of LSD. [@1] Recatto was booked into the
Tangipahoa Parish Jail on Thursday, August 1. [@1]
