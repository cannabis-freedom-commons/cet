Georgia's Interior Ministry said on 20 August 2024 that 19 people, both Georgian
and foreign nationals, had been arrested on drug-related charges, including six
alleged drug dealers. [@1] Police made several controlled purchases of drugs
from those held on sale charges under a judge's order and secretly audio- and
video-recorded the purchases. [@1]

Personal searches and searches of the detainees' homes recovered a large
quantity of drugs, including marijuana, alongside heroin, cocaine, MDMA,
pregabalin, methadone, alpha-PVP, buprenorphine, methamphetamine and lyrica.
[@1] Money obtained from drug sales was seized by police as physical evidence.
[@1] Adhesive tape and electric scales were also found during the investigation.
[@2]

The investigation was opened over illegal acquisition and storage of large and
especially large quantities of narcotic and psychotropic substances and the
illegal sowing and cultivation of marijuana, under articles 260, 261, 265,
18-260, 19-260 and 273 of Georgia's Criminal Code. [@1] The offences carry up to
20 years in prison or life imprisonment because of the large and especially
large quantities involved. [@1]
