Multinational Joint Task Force troops deployed against Boko Haram and ISWAP in
the Lake Chad region arrested two key operatives who supplied the groups with
illicit drugs and material for uniforms and flags. [@1] The New Telegraph placed
both arrests on the Kekeno-Cross Kauwa Road and the Gubio-Damasak Road in Borno
State, Nigeria. [@3] One of those arrested, Yahaya Muhammad, was stopped on the
Kekeno-Cross Kauwa Road. [@1]

He was found with 198 wraps of Indian hemp (cannabis) alongside other drugs.
[@1] The MNJTF statement listed Metro, Doxycycline and Discloppxac among the
drugs, as well as sachets of Kpomix, Coldtime, Nopami, Coughter, Nexcape and
Ostanac. [@2] The statement said Muhammad was not a licensed pharmacist. [@1] In
a separate operation, the second operative was arrested on the Gubio-Damasak
Road driving a blue Toyota pickup and carrying two sacks of 19 bundles of
clothing material meant for uniforms and flags. [@1]

In a third operation troops rescued three women and a baby suspected of being
relatives of the terrorists, who said they had fled the Jubillaram enclave in
the southern Lake Chad island in Marte local government area, and they were
undergoing medical assessment. [@1]
