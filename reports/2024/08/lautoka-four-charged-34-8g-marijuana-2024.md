Police raided a home in Lautoka on Friday 30 August 2024. [@3] The man arrested
was a 29-year-old believed to be a key player in the local illicit drug trade.
[@1] The raid was a joint operation by the Fiji Police Force's Serious Organised
Crime Unit and officers in the Western Division. [@2] Two other men and a
20-year-old woman were arrested with him, making four arrests. [@2] The suspect
allegedly released his dog and tried to flee during the execution of the search
warrant, but was pursued and arrested by officers. [@1]

Police said the raid seized white crystals confirmed to be methamphetamine,
white powder confirmed to be cocaine and dried leaves confirmed to be marijuana.
[@2] Officers also seized smoking apparatuses, a digital weighing machine, a
packing machine, and local and foreign currencies believed to be proceeds of
crime. [@2] The four were charged with unlawful possession of illicit drugs
covering 31.129 grams of methamphetamine, 5.52 grams of cocaine and 34.8 grams
of marijuana. [@4] Kunal Prasad, Alex Chandra and Trisha Reddy were charged
alongside the 29-year-old. [@4] The 29-year-old, identified in court reporting
as Avneel Chand, was released on bail by the Lautoka Magistrates Court and was
to appear on 13 October 2024. [@4]

SOCU is the Fiji Police's Narcotics Bureau, which was renamed after the Counter
Narcotics Bureau was formed. [@2] Assistant Commissioner of Police Mesake Waqa
said the force was committed to bringing key players in the illicit drug trade
to justice and asked for patience. [@2]
