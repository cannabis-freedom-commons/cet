The Williamson County Sheriff's Office Criminal Interdiction Unit stopped a 2022
Ford F-150 for a traffic violation on 27 August 2024. [@2] The vehicle carried
Texas plates. [@1] The driver, a 41-year-old man from Garden Ridge, was
arrested, while his passenger, a 19-year-old woman from San Antonio, was
released. [@2] Authorities did not release the names of the people involved.
[@1]

A deputy observed suspicious factors and asked for consent to search the truck,
which was denied. [@1] A K-9 unit was then called to the scene. [@1] The K-9
unit alerted to the vehicle, leading to a probable cause search. [@2] After the
dog alerted to the presence of drugs, deputies carried out a probable cause
search and found 32.2 pounds (14.61 kg) of marijuana. [@1] The marijuana was
seized. [@2]

The stop and seizure took place in Williamson County, Texas. [@1] The sheriff's
office described it as the second such marijuana bust in Williamson County in
recent weeks. [@2] The case remained under investigation by the Williamson
County Sheriff's Office. [@1]
