The Nicaraguan Army said it detained Francisco Javier Pastrán Alaniz, 38,
describing him as the person responsible for caring for the drug, on 28 August
2024. [@2] EFE reported the detainee's name as Francisco Javier Pastrán Alanis
and said he was handed over with the processed marijuana. [@1] The army said the
detained man and the processed marijuana were handed to the corresponding
authorities. [@2]

Troops of the army's 6 Comando Militar Regional, the Sixth Regional Military
Command, seized and incinerated 90,000 marijuana plants in the community of
Oculi No. 2, in the Microrregión de Ayapal, San José de Bocay municipality,
Jinotega department. [@2] Officers also seized 25 pounds of marihuana that had
already been processed, about 11.34 kg. [@2]

The army said the operation was carried out under the state strategy called
'Muro de Contención', or Wall of Containment. [@2] Nicaragua runs that permanent
strategy to stop drugs circulating in population centres, with cooperation from
other Central American and Caribbean countries, the United States, Mexico and
Russia. [@1] EFE said Nicaragua lies in a high-traffic drug corridor, with drugs
produced in South America and moved towards North America. [@1]
