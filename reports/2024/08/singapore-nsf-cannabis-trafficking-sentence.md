Min Soe Htat, a 26-year-old Myanmar national and Singapore permanent resident
serving as a full-time national serviceman, was sentenced on 12 August 2024 to
five years and one month's jail and 10 strokes of the cane for cannabis
trafficking. [@1] He pleaded guilty to two charges under the Misuse of Drugs Act
for trafficking 3.3g of cannabis and possessing 21.87g of cannabis for the
purposes of trafficking, with two further charges taken into consideration. [@1]

The court heard that Min Soe Htat had bought 50g of cannabis from another seller
for S$420 on 6 October 2023. [@1] He split the cannabis into six packets for
sale and had eight regular customers, spending the profits on personal expenses.
[@1] At about 3pm on 10 October 2023, CNB officers arrested his customer Nyi Thu
San, then went to a military camp and arrested Min Soe Htat about one-and-a-half
hours later. [@1] A search of his home seized exhibits including six packets
containing at least 21.87g of cannabis in total. [@2] Investigations showed Nyi
Thu San had begun buying cannabis from him after meeting him in July 2023, and
bought 15g for S$300 in October 2023. [@1] On 7 October 2023 the package was
left behind a water pipe at a staircase landing of a Housing Board block in
Bukit Batok, where Nyi Thu San retrieved and consumed the cannabis. [@1]

Cannabis is a Class A controlled drug under the Misuse of Drugs Act. [@2]
Trafficking a Class A controlled drug carries between five and 20 years' jail
and between five and 15 strokes of the cane. [@1] The prosecutor sought between
five years and five years and one month's jail with 10 strokes of the cane. [@1]
The prosecutor said he had regular clientele and was "gradually expanding his
offerings to include methamphetamine on top of cannabis". [@1] Defence lawyer
Ashvin Hariharan asked for the mandatory minimum of five years' jail and five
strokes of the cane per charge. [@1] He argued his client was not involved in
directing or organising commercial-scale drug trade or a syndicate, had not
involved others and had no prior convictions. [@1]
