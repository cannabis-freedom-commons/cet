Officers of Azerbaijan's State Security Service and State Border Service found
and seized a large batch of drugs smuggled into the country by Iranian citizens
in a joint operation. [@1] Four men were detained red-handed while trying to
remove the drug parcels hidden by Iranian citizens in the settlement's green
area. [@3]

The cache held 74 kg 163 g of dried marijuana found in Pirsagi settlement in
Baku's Sabuncu district. [@3] The detained men are three Azerbaijani citizens
born in 1982, 1988 and 1993 and one stateless man born in 1982. [@1] The four
were caught as they collected the cache hidden by the road in Pirsagi in Baku's
Sabuncu district. [@1]

A criminal case was opened under articles 206.3.2, 234.4.1 and 234.4.3 of
Azerbaijan's Criminal Code and the detainees were brought to criminal
responsibility. [@1] The drugs had been smuggled into Azerbaijan by citizens of
Iran. [@2] The investigation was continuing. [@4]
