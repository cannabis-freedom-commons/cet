The Fimela river-maritime customs brigade, part of the South Coast subdivision
and the maritime units directorate, seized 845 kg of cannabis in an operation at
sea. [@1] The operation took place during the night of 25 to 26 August 2024 off
Djifere. [@2] Officers chased and boarded two pirogues during the operation.
[@1]

Officers found 28 parcels of cannabis weighing 845 kg in total aboard the two
pirogues. [@2] Four people, the two pirogue captains and their two assistants,
were arrested during the operation. [@2] Customs put the combined value of the
drugs and the two boats at nearly 69 million CFA francs. [@2]

Customs credited fast patrol boats supplied under its PROMAD modernisation
programme for making the interception possible. [@1] The customs administration
said it remained determined to fight drug trafficking in all its forms. [@1]
