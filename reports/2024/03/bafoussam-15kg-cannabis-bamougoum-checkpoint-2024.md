Cameroon's mobile customs brigade in Bafoussam, the capital of the West Region,
announced on Tuesday 12 March 2024 that it had seized 15 kg of cannabis. [@1]
The public broadcaster CRTV reported the seizure. [@1] The cannabis was
concealed inside a vehicle. [@1] Officers intercepted the vehicle at the
checkpoint at the Bamougoum crossroads. [@1]

Xinhua's dispatch from Yaoundé was dated 12 March 2024, the day of the
announcement. [@2]
