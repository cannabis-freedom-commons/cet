Judiciary Police arrested three Filipino non-resident workers in Taipa on 12
March 2024 on suspicion of selling and using drugs. [@2] Police named them as
Botones, 34, a manual worker; Ronquillo, 31, a waiter; and Agustin, 41, a
hairdresser. [@2] After a tip-off that Filipinos were selling drugs in Taipa,
officers began watching Botones's flat at about 7 am and stopped him when he
left the building about 45 minutes later. [@2] He was carrying six packets of
ice and one packet of cannabis, and a search of his flat found two more cannabis
packets in a bookcase and drug-taking equipment. [@2] Ronquillo was found in the
flat with equipment for smoking ice on his bed. [@2] Officers arrested Agustin
in a Taipa street at about 1 pm and found drug-taking equipment and an
electronic scale in his backpack. [@2] In all they seized 1.56 grams of ice and
1.11 grams of cannabis, which police valued at 6,300 patacas. [@1]

Urine tests on all three were positive and they admitted having a drug habit.
[@1] The Macau Post Daily reported that Botones admitted selling drugs. [@1] The
Macao Daily News reported instead that all three denied trafficking and that
Botones said he had bought drugs from Agustin two or three times, including
about half a gram of ice for 1,000 patacas. [@2] Police said their investigation
indicated that someone had been selling drugs for at least two weeks. [@2]

Prosecutors charged all three with drug use and possession of drug-taking
equipment and added trafficking charges against Botones and Agustin. [@2] The
three were sent to the Public Prosecutions Office on the afternoon of 13 March.
[@2] Police said they were tracing the source of the drugs and looking for other
people involved. [@2]
