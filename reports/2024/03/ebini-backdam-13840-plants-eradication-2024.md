Police in Regional Division 10 held a narcotics eradication exercise at Ebini
Backdam along the Berbice River on Tuesday 5 March 2024. [@1,2] On the first
farm, covering about two acres, officers counted 130 beds with 68 plants each,
or 8,840 plants between two and six feet tall, and destroyed them. [@1,2]
Police put the weight of those plants at 19,448 kg and their street value at
about GY$2.9 million. [@1,2]

About 200 metres away, officers found a second farm of about two acres holding
some 5,000 plants between one and four feet tall. [@1,2] They destroyed those
plants as well, putting their weight at 5,500 kg and their street value at
about GY$847,000. [@1,2] Officers also found a makeshift camp with two
hammocks, a mattress, kitchen utensils and groceries, and destroyed it. [@1,2]
No one was arrested. [@1,2]
