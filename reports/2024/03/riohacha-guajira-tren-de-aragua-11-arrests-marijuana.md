Police announced on 9 March 2024 that they had dismantled a Venezuelan drug
network operating in La Guajira, where it kept a marijuana collection centre for
moving the drug clandestinely into Venezuela. [@1] The National Police and the
Attorney General's Office's CTI arrested 11 Venezuelan nationals in the raid in
rural Riohacha, La Guajira. [@1] Among the 11 was Carlos Antonio López Centeno,
alias El Pilo, whom police described as the leader of Los Negritos del Monte, a
group dedicated to arms trafficking, narcotics and extortion and working with
the Tren de Aragua. [@1] The detainees were linked to the Tren de Aragua, the
Venezuelan gang police say has been extending its drug trafficking into
Colombia. [@4]

Officers seized 24 pounds of marijuana, about 10.9 kilograms, in the operation.
[@1] Brigadier General Nicolás Alejandro Zapata Restrepo said the collection
centre could receive at least three tonnes of narcotics, which were dosed and
sent to Venezuela. [@1] InSight Crime reported that investigators found the
group planned to collect three tonnes of marijuana for trafficking across the
border to Venezuela. [@3] El Tiempo reported that the 11 were arrested in
flagrante in rural Riohacha and that officers seized five rifles, war material
and the 24 pounds of marijuana. [@2] InSight Crime reported that police also
seized multiple weapons, grenades, an armoured vehicle and 10 kilograms of
marijuana. [@3]

The 11 were being processed for trafficking, manufacture or possession of
narcotics and for manufacturing, trafficking and carrying firearms or
ammunition. [@1] Police said the group ran microtrafficking in Riohacha and set
up drug collection centres for distribution to Central American countries. [@1]
El Pilo was one of Venezuela's most wanted and faced two Venezuelan arrest
warrants, one for homicide and one for drug trafficking. [@2] He identified
himself with two false identity cards when arrested, El Tiempo reported. [@2]
Police said the marijuana came from central Colombia, passed through Cesar to La
Guajira and then crossed into Venezuela. [@1]
