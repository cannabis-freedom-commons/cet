Officers of the Ndende gendarmerie brigade, in Dola department of Ngounie
province, seized several bundles of cannabis during an operation in the week
before the 19 March 2024 publication. [@1] The cannabis was concealed inside six
sacks each said to hold 50 kg of dried cassava. [@1] The brigade's officers
seized the cannabis bundles during the Ndende operation. [@1]

The suspected trafficker had tried to send the consignment to the Gabonese
capital through a transport agency. [@1] He had reportedly bought the cassava in
Congo-Brazzaville, dried it, and then hidden cannabis inside pieces of the
tubers. [@1]

After the seizure, the court of first instance in Mouila, the provincial capital
of Ngounie, reportedly opened an investigation to shed further light on the
case. [@1]
