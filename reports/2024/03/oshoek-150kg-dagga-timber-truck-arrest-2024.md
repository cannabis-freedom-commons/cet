A 43-year-old truck driver from the Kingdom of Eswatini was arrested at the
Oshoek Port of Entry for allegedly trying to smuggle dagga into South Africa.
[@1] The seizure took place at the border post on 21 March 2024. [@2] At about
00:30, police searched the man on the arrivals side of the port of entry. [@1]
Officers found the dagga concealed under a load of timber. [@1] The driver was
detained and charged. [@1]

A total of 150 kg of dagga was seized, with an estimated street value of
R630,840. [@1] The truck, which had Eswatini number plates, was confiscated
together with the dagga for further investigation. [@1]

The driver was expected to appear at the Elukwatini Magistrate's Court on 22
March 2024, the day the police statement was issued. [@1] Mpumalanga provincial
police commissioner Lieutenant General Semakaleng Daphney Manamela praised the
border policing members for their dedication. [@1] She said the arrests and
successes at the borders showed the members were working diligently. [@1]
