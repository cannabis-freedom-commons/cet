Border police arrested seven persons along with 73 kilograms of drugs in Dur
Baba district of eastern Nangarhar province, as reported from Jalalabad. [@1]
Officials said the detainees wanted to smuggle those drugs from Nangarhar to
Pakistan. [@1] Wahidullah Mohammadi, identified as spokesman for the 201 Khalid
Bin Walid Corps, told Pajhwok Afghan News that security forces arrested the
suspects near the Durand Line and recovered the hashish from them. [@1]

The drugs recovered from the suspects were identified as 73 kilograms of
hashish. [@1] According to Mohammadi's information, the suspects were arrested
at the time when they wanted to smuggle the hashish across the Durand Line
toward Pakistan. [@1] Mohammadi provided the details of the operation to Pajhwok
Afghan News as the corps spokesman. [@1]

The account added that the operation came days after security forces arrested 12
people over drug smuggling in the same area. [@1] The report described the
incident as a foiled bid to smuggle 73kg of hashish to Pakistan. [@1]
