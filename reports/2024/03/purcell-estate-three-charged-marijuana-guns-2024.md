Royal Virgin Islands Police Force officers found two firearms, several rounds of
ammunition and a large quantity of marijuana near the basketball court in
Purcell Estate during a planned search on Friday 1 March 2024. [@3] The search
also turned up a large quantity of crack cocaine. [@1] Four individuals were
held in custody at the Road Town Police Station for questioning about the seized
items. [@2]

Seantroy Hanley, 36, and Jahkeema Charles, 32, both of Fat Hog's Bay, were
jointly charged with two counts each of keeping a firearm without a licence,
unlawful possession of explosives, possession of a controlled drug and
possession of a controlled drug with intent to supply. [@1] A third man, Quentin
Noel, 19, of Purcell Estate, who was arrested at the scene, was charged with
possession of a controlled drug. [@1]

Charles was granted $40,000 bail to appear at the Magistrate's Court on 18 April
2024, while Hanley and Noel remained in custody until the next sitting. [@1] The
force described the raid as part of a series of actions against a surge in gun
crime and drug trafficking, after a daylight robbery at the Aladdin jewellery
store in which four masked assailants escaped with valuables. [@1]
