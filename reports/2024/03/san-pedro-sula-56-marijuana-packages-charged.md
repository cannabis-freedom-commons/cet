Honduran prosecutors filed a criminal complaint over 56 packages of suspected
marijuana seized in San Pedro Sula. [@1] The accused was Kevin Ariel Torres
Martínez, 29, charged with aggravated drug trafficking and association to commit
crimes. [@1] He was remanded with judicial detention to the maximum-security
prison known as Siria in El Provenir, Francisco Morazán. [@1] Torres Martínez
was detained on 10 March 2024 in possession of 56 packages of suspected
marijuana. [@2]

He was detained during an operation by the micro-trafficking unit and officers
of the DIPAMPCO anti-gang police directorate in the Las Anonas area of San Pedro
Sula. [@1] The 56 packages were found inside a red Pontiac parked outside his
home. [@1] Officers also seized a mobile phone and the Pontiac vehicle. [@2]

Police described Torres Martínez, known as alias 'Benny', as an alleged MS-13
member who moved drugs to different parts of the country. [@2]
