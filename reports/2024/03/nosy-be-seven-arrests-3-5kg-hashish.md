Malagasy national gendarmerie officers arrested seven people, one of them a
Mauritian national, in the operation at Nosy Be. [@4] The gendarmerie described
the seven as suspected members of a hashish trafficking network. [@2] Newsmada
reported that the network comprised the Mauritian and six Malagasy suspects.
[@3] The operation began when the Dzamandzar brigade, acting on information,
arrested two people near Madirokely, one of them a Mauritian already known to
the gendarmerie for fraud. [@2] Interrogation of the two led to five more
arrests and the seizure of 20 bars of hashish weighing 3.5 kg in total. [@2]
Newsmada placed the Mauritian's arrest at Diégo-Hely in Dzamandjary district on
a Thursday morning, with the six Malagasy suspects detained afterwards. [@3] The
seven suspects were placed in preventive detention, the gendarmerie said. [@2]
Newsmada reported that the suspects were to be brought that day before the
prosecutor's office in Nosy Be. [@3]

The national gendarmerie seized 3.5 kilos of hashish, which it classes as a hard
drug, at Nosy Be in northern Madagascar, the commander of the national
gendarmerie in Nosy Be, Amad Zakaria Rachid, said. [@1] After searching the
suspects' homes, the gendarmes found the 3.5 kg of cannabis resin, Newsmada
reported. [@3] Newsmada said the network was spreading the cannabis resin, sold
in the form of chocolate biscuits, in Dzamandjary district. [@3] The company
commander put the purchase price of a kilo of hashish at about 3,000 to 4,000
euros and said it was resold for up to 90,000 ariary a gram. [@2]

The gendarmerie said the investigation was continuing in order to dismantle the
whole distribution network. [@1] In the same roundup the gendarmes arrested a
woman who planned to resell 250 grams of cocaine at the port of Nosy Be, a
separate case not counted in the cannabis figures here. [@2]
