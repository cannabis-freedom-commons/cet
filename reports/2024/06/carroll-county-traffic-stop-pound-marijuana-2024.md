Carroll County deputies stopped a vehicle on Highway 35 North near Carrollton on
Tuesday 4 June 2024. [@1] Deputies arrested Venessa Dawn Horne, 40, of
Carrollton. [@1] They also arrested Casey Turner Sanders, 45. [@1]

The vehicle held approximately 115 grams of methamphetamine and one pound (0.45
kg) of marijuana. [@1] Horne was charged with trafficking methamphetamine and
felony possession of marijuana with intent to distribute, and was booked into
the Grenada County Jail. [@1] Sanders was charged with possession of
paraphernalia and possession of a controlled substance, and was booked into the
Leflore County Jail. [@1]

Deputies said the investigation continued and that more arrests were possible.
