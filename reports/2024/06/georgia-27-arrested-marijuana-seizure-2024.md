Georgia's Interior Ministry reported on 19 June 2024 that 27 Georgian and
foreign nationals were arrested for drug crimes, including 15 alleged drug
dealers. [@2] The arrests were made following extensive operational measures and
investigative actions by various police departments and in coordination with the
General Prosecutor's Office. [@3] The Central Criminal Police, Patrol Police,
and regional police departments in Tbilisi, Adjara, Samegrelo-Zemo Svaneti,
Guria, Kakheti and Imereti were all involved in the recent operations. [@3]
These coordinated efforts led to the arrests of both Georgian and foreign
nationals involved in drug crimes. [@3] On the basis of the judge’s decision,
the police made several control purchases of drugs and secretly audio-videotaped
these purchases. [@2]

Personal searches and searches of the detainees' homes recovered large
quantities of drugs as evidence, including heroin, cocaine, buprenorphine,
morphine, thebaine, codeine, methadone, alpha-PVP, mephedrone, clephedrone,
methamphetamine, pregabalin (Lyrica) and marijuana. [@1] Police also seized a
large sum of money and electronic scales as physical evidence. [@1]

The cases were investigated under articles 260, 18-260, 19-260, 261, 262, 265,
273 and 19-273 of Georgia's Criminal Code. [@1] Those detained face accusations
of illegal storage of narcotics in large and especially large amounts, including
marijuana and psychotropic substances, as well as preparation for sale and
attempted sale. [@1] The crimes are punishable by up to 20 years or life
imprisonment. [@3]
