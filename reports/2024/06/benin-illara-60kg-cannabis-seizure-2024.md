Officers serving at the Illara arrondissement police station achieved a
successful operation in the early morning of Wednesday 19 June 2024, dismantling
a network that trafficked in psychotropic products. [@1] Illara, where the
operation was conducted, is a locality on Benin's border with Nigeria in which
the Republican Police said a large quantity of cannabis was seized. [@1]

Acting meticulously, the officers laid hands on one individual together with
three jute sacks containing what was described as apparent cannabis, called
chanvre indien in the local French usage. [@1] The quantity of the seized
prohibited product was estimated at 60.38 kilograms. [@1] The detained person
stands suspected of detention, commercialisation and use of cannabis,
accusations that the same account says could expose him to severe judicial
prosecution. [@1]

He was kept in police custody while awaiting presentation to the prosecutor.
[@1] To carry out its mission successfully, the police encouraged members of the
public to denounce any suspicious activity and to cooperate with security
officers. [@1]
