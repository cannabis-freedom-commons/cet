Kuwait's Court of Cassation, presided over by Counselor Sultan Bouresli, upheld death sentences by hanging for two Iranian nationals convicted of attempting to smuggle 189 kg of hashish and psychotropic substances by sea. [@1,2] The verdict followed their interception by the Coast Guard and the Drug Control Department after they sailed from the Abadan area toward Kuwait from an agreed point at sea. [@1,2]

The defendants admitted the charges and told the court they were heroin users. [@1,2]
