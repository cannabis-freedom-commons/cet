Officers of the 45th Independent Military Police Company (45ª CIPM), acting in
Operation Terra Limpa, eradicated a planting of more than 31,000 cannabis sativa
plants in Curaca in northern Bahia on Saturday 29 June 2024. [@1] The Bahia.ba
account identifies the unit by its full name, the 45ª Companhia Independente de
Polícia Militar. [@2] A Tarde reports that a plantation of more than 31,000
marijuana plants was incinerated on Saturday 29 in Curaca in northern Bahia.
[@3] The operation targets cannabis planting and drug trafficking on the islands
of the Sao Francisco River. [@1]

Officers found about 26,000 plants at the locality known as Ilha Redonda. [@3]
Another 5,200 plants were found near the water-catchment source used for the
illegal crop, bringing the total to more than 31,000. [@1] All the plants were
incinerated except for one sample collected for registration of the case with
the competent authority. [@2] No one was found at the site, and no arrest was
reported. [@4]

The 45ª CIPM commander, Major Leonel Ribeiro, said almost 160,000 marijuana
plants had been eradicated that year during Operation Terra Limpa. [@1]
