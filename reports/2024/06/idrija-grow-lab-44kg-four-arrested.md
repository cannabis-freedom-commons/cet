Officers and criminal investigators of the Nova Gorica Police Directorate found one of Slovenia's largest cannabis cultivation laboratories in the Idrija region during a court-ordered search on 20 June 2024, following international police cooperation. [@1] The laboratory comprised four specially adapted rooms with pumps, heaters, ventilation, data devices and vacuum sealers. [@1]

Instead of growing plants, officers found cannabis vacuum-packed in 0.5 kg bags and packages of plant parts intended for resale, totalling about 44 kg. [@1] Police estimated the suspects could have grown 2,500 to 3,000 plants in the laboratory and would have made about €130,000 from one harvest, with four harvests a year possible. [@1]

Four men were arrested: a 54-year-old Slovenian and a 48-year-old Croatian were released after questioning, while a 39-year-old Serbian and a 51-year-old Croatian were brought before the investigating judge of the District Court in Nova Gorica, who remanded them in custody. [@1]
