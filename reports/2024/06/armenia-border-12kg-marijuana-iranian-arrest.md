An Iranian citizen was arrested for attempting to smuggle marijuana and cocaine
across the Armenia-Iran state border, according to the State Revenue Committee
of Armenia. [@1] The Iranian citizen, identified only as A.A., was arrested
under criminal proceedings initiated under Article 399 of the Criminal Code.
[@1] The materials related to the incident were transferred to the Investigative
Committee of Armenia. [@1]

During further customs control conducted jointly with employees of the SRC
anti-smuggling department, the vehicle underwent a thorough inspection. [@1]
Officers found more than 12.1 kg of marijuana in the vehicle. [@1] Officers also
found approximately 4.42 grams of cocaine-like substances in the same vehicle.
[@1]

The report notes that everyone charged is presumed innocent until proven guilty
by a court decision that has entered into legal force. [@1]
