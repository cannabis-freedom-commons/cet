Customs officers were carrying out checks at the Perthus toll barrier on the A9
on the afternoon of Saturday 1 June 2024. [@1] They stopped a rented van
registered in France that had come from Spain. [@1] Two Belgian nationals on
board told them they had nothing to declare and were simply going home after a
holiday. [@1] France 3 Occitanie reported that the pair told officers they were
going home to Germany after a tourist stay in Spain. [@2] Actu Perpignan
reported that the two men lived in Germany. [@3]

Officers opened the rear doors and found bin bags and shopping bags giving off a
strong smell of cannabis. [@1] In total the two men were carrying 109 kg of
cannabis resin and 26 kg of cannabis pollen. [@1] Actu Perpignan put the value
of the 135 kg at 450,000 euros on the parallel market. [@3]

Both men were placed in customs detention before being handed to the Perpignan
judicial police. [@1] They appeared before the Perpignan judicial court on
Wednesday 5 June 2024. [@1] The court sentenced them to three years in prison, a
10-year ban from French territory and a customs fine of 450,750 euros. [@1]
France 3 Occitanie reported that the two were convicted of drug trafficking.
[@2]
