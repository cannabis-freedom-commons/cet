The court in Papeete acquitted a father prosecuted for two years over about a hundred plants grown in Taravao to make oil for his son; the prosecutor had asked it to waive punishment. Without the treatment he lost about ten kilos and convulsed heavily. [@2] The couple refused the proposal, so the next step could be before the correctional court. [@2] The magistrate proposed six months of suspended prison. [@2] He again appeared before the Papeete prosecutor under a guilty-plea procedure. [@2] Without cannabis oil, the boy suffered seizures every two minutes for four days and nearly died. [@2] After two years of respite, gendarmes again came to their home in May 2022. [@2] The father had previously been sentenced to two months of suspended prison and a 200,000 Fcfp fine for use and possession of narcotics. [@2] After starting cannabis oil, he went from 20 seizures to three and then had a month with no seizures. [@2] As a baby he convulsed up to 20 times a day. [@2] The father planted different varieties at home. [@2] Growing for therapy remained prohibited. [@1,2]
