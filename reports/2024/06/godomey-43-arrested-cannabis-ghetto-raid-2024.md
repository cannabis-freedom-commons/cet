Police from the Godomey arrondissement station arrested 43 people, one of them a
woman, for possessing, using and trafficking chanvre indien, the French term for
cannabis. [@2] The operation was carried out on 15 June 2024 by the Godomey
arrondissement police station. [@4] Officers raided a large ghetto near the
Hêvié Liklan junction in Godomey, in Abomey-Calavi commune, after complaints
from residents about pickpocketing and extortion. [@2]

During the raid officers seized sachet liquors, a non-negligible quantity of
chanvre indien and smoking accessories. [@3] The Abomey-Calavi prosecutor
ordered that the investigation continue. [@2] The prosecutor also asked OCERTID,
the central anti-drug office, to test the detainees and analyse the seized
products. [@2]

The raid formed part of a wider operation by the Atlantique departmental police
directorate against crime hotspots. [@3] Police described the sweep as part of
their drive against the proliferation of ghettos, the term used in Benin for
drug-use spots. [@1] The police statement said the operation showed the
commitment of police and local authorities to fighting drug trafficking and the
crime that threatens residents' safety. [@2]
