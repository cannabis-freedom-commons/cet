Merseyside Police arrested a 61-year-old man from Liverpool on 26 June 2024 on
suspicion of importing a Class B drug. [@1] He was released under investigation.
[@1]

Border Force intercepted the container on 22 April 2024 as it arrived at the
Royal Seaforth Dock in Liverpool, seizing 365.41kg of cannabis. [@1] Police
valued the cannabis at between £1.28m and £1.92m. [@1] Warrants at an address
and a business premises in Kirkby seized devices, documentation and £5,000 in
cash. [@1] A police cordon remained at the Kirkby premises while officers
continued to search the site. [@1]

Detective Inspector Steve Byrom said the operation had disrupted and removed a
large quantity of drugs bound for the streets and further afield. [@1] A police
image showed the cannabis laid out in a warehouse on wooden crates, in cardboard
boxes stamped with the Canadian flag. [@2]
