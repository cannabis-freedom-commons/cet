Arkansas State Police stopped a Ford Expedition towing a trailer on eastbound
Interstate 40 in Conway County, near Morrilton, on 6 July 2022 after the vehicle
crossed out of its lane past the fog line. [@1] The stop was on Interstate 40
near Morrilton on 6 July 2022, the Arkansas State Police reported. [@1] The
driver, Alrick Perry, 37, of Bermuda Run, North Carolina, and the passenger,
Kristoper Morgan, 40, of Virginia Beach, Virginia, were arrested and taken to
the Conway County Detention Center. [@1] Police said both were charged with
trafficking a controlled substance and possession of felony drug paraphernalia.
[@1]

While questioning the driver, police said a request was made and granted to
search the vehicle and trailer. [@1] An officer with the Morrilton Police
Department brought a dog that alerted on the trailer. [@1] Authorities said they
found 1,632 pounds of marijuana, about 740 kilograms. [@1] Arkansas State Police
later identified Trooper Deston Linkous's largest marijuana seizure of 2022 as
1,632 pounds in one July seizure. [@2]

In the Eastern District of Arkansas, case United States v. Perry, 4:23-cr-00099,
a superseding indictment later named Alrick Perry and Kristopher Morgan. [@3]
Judge Lee P. Rudofsky sentenced Perry to 36 months in prison, three years of
supervised release and a 100-dollar special assessment, in a judgment signed on
11 June 2024. [@3] The same court sentenced Kristopher Morgan to 12 months and
one day in prison, two years of supervised release and a 100-dollar special
assessment, in a judgment signed on 14 May 2024. [@4]
