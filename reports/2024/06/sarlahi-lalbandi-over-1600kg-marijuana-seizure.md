A joint team of the Nepal Police and the Armed Police Force seized more than 16
quintals of marijuana in Sarlahi district, which police described as a large
haul of illegal marijuana. [@1] Officers found the marijuana during a search
operation concealed in a tin drum and plastic sacks inside a house at Langur
Danda in Lalbandi municipality. [@1] Police also recovered marijuana-processing
equipment about 100 metres from that house, indicating on-site preparation or
packaging of the drug. [@1]

Police said this was the first seizure of such a large quantity of marijuana in
Madhesh Province, underscoring the scale of the haul for the region. [@1] A
125-member joint team, comprising 80 Nepal Police personnel and 45 Armed Police
Force personnel, was deployed to recover the contraband from the site. [@1]

The District Police Office in Sarlahi said the exact location of the house was
not disclosed and an investigation was under way, naming no suspects or arrests
in this account. [@1]
