Namibian police arrested 112 suspects in drug-related matters during June 2024.
[@1] Police spokesperson deputy commissioner Kauna Shikwambi announced the
figure in a media release. [@2] Of those arrested, 106 were Namibian nationals,
one Angolan, two Tanzanian, one Zambian and two Zimbabwean. [@1]

Police recorded the month's cannabis seizures as 191 837,97 kilograms. [@1] The
same tally lists 2 285 tablets of mandrax and five units of crack cocaine, which
are not counted as cannabis. [@1]

Shikwambi called on the public to report drug dealing, saying: Let us stand
together against illicit drugs, report it and save lives. [@1]
