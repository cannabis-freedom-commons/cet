Côte d'Ivoire's national police directorate announced the arrest of one man and
the seizure of 25 kilograms of cannabis in Divo on Tuesday 11 June 2024, during
a patrol in the operation Épervier Renforcé (Reinforced Sparrowhawk). [@1] The
officers came from the Divo regional unit of the Direction de la police des
stupéfiants et des drogues (DPSD), the national drug police. [@2] They detained
a 38-year-old man, named in the accounts as S. Daouda and describing himself as
a planter, at about 11:50 on a minibus arriving from Abidjan at the corridor at
the entrance to the town. [@2] The cannabis was in 25 one-kilogram blocks, in a
backpack he was carrying and in a second bag under his seat. [@2] The anti-drug
officers went to the corridor after being told that a suspicious passenger was
arriving, and found him sitting among the other passengers. [@2] Koaci reported
that the tip was anonymous and came around midday, saying that a passenger on a
bus from Abidjan was carrying a suspicious parcel. [@3]

The national police note valued the 25 kilograms at 1,250,000 CFA francs. [@1]
After questioning he said he was a planter from Niokoblognoa, a village in
Lakota department, and admitted trafficking. [@2] He said he had been buying his
supplies in Abidjan, specifically in Abobo, for more than three years. [@2]
Koaci reported that he was taking the cannabis to sell at Niokoblognoa, in the
Lakota region. [@3]

He was to be brought before the Divo public prosecutor's office for the case to
continue. [@2]
