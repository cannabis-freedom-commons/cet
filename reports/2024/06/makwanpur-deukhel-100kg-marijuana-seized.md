Police on patrol from the Area Police Office in Palung checked two motorcycles
on the road in Deukhel of Thaha Municipality-7. [@1] Officers seized around 100
kilos and 420 grams of marijuana during the checks. [@1]

The motorcycles checked bore registration numbers 20 P 1867 and Province
3-02-012 P 1592 respectively. [@1] The riders absconded from the scene. [@1]
Police said they were searching for the fugitives and carrying out necessary
investigation. [@1]
