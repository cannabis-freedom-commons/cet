The stop took place under the National Security Ministry's measures against drug
trafficking on the country's borders, while the federal force was carrying out
vehicle checks on National Route 12. [@2] Officers of the Puerto Rico section of
Squadron 11 San Ignacio, with the Misiones complex-crimes unit, saw a car
stopped at the roadside at kilometre 1,469. [@2] The two occupants fled into the
adjoining mountainous area carrying two large packages and abandoned the
vehicle. [@2]

A search of the area found the drugs, and in front of witnesses officers opened
both wrappings and found 58 rectangular packages of compact plant material. [@2]
Field Narcotest tests were positive for Cannabis sativa, and the force put the
total weight at 58 kilograms and 20 grams. [@1]

The Federal Court and the Federal Prosecutor's Office in Oberá ordered the
seizure of the drug for a breach of Law 23.737. [@2] Infobae's account of the
same patrol said the two people abandoned their belongings and ran into the
hills when they saw the force's vehicle. [@1] Infobae said the search then found
two large packages of the kind used to transport drugs. [@1]
