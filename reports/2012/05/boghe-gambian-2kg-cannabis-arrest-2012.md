Officers serving under Commissioner Isselmou O. Abdallahi arrested Diani Oumar,
a Gambian born in 1981, at the Carrefour bus station in Boghé at about 18:00 on
a Wednesday, catching him in flagrante delicto with 2 kg of cannabis. [@1]

He had crossed into Mauritania at the unofficial Lopel crossing, 4 km west of
Boghé, before going to Carrefour and intending to continue to Nouakchott. [@1]
He immediately paid for two bus tickets on a Mercedes although he carried only a
10-litre oil can wrapped in a bag. [@1] That drew the attention of brigadier
Moustapha, who took him to the police station. [@1] There he was placed in
custody before being taken before the public prosecutor in Aleg. [@1]

He told police, who noted his Gambian passport, that this was his first attempt
on Mauritanian territory. [@1] The arrest took place in a border area where drug
trafficking has become commonplace. [@1]
