Basham Julien was arrested and charged on May 1st, 2012 at Bath Estate where he
was chased by members of the police Drug Squad. [@1] A 23-year-old Kingshill
resident was fined $1928.90 by Magistrate Candia George for possession of
cocaine, possession of cocaine with intent to supply and possession of cannabis.
[@2]

According to police, he had 27 grams of cocaine, estimated at about EC$500,
which he threw over a galvanised covering when officers approached him. [@1] The
drug was later recovered and brought in to the police headquarters. [@1] Also in
his possession was 5 grams of marijuana and $426.90. [@1]

Julien’s attorney, Bernadette Lambert pleaded to the court for leniency for her
client. [@1] She informed the court that he was the owner of a small business
and is currently pursuing a diploma in business. [@1] The magistrate in her
ruling warned the young man from appearing in court again and urged him to stay
away from anything illegal. [@1]
