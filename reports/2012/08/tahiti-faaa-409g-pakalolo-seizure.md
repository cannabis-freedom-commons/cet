Tahiti-Faa'a customs seized 409 grams of pakalolo, the local name for cannabis,
from a passenger arriving on domestic flight VT 923 from Rimatara-Rurutu in the
Austral Islands on Friday 24 August 2012. [@1] The traveler was already known to
law enforcement as a producer and dealer of pakalolo, and was due to appear at a
mobile court session in Rurutu on 6 November 2012. [@1] A search of his home
uncovered a further 37 grams of cannabis, so the two seizures together amount to
446 grams. [@1]

A customs sniffer dog named Benji detected the narcotics during a routine check
on the airport's checked-baggage carousel. [@1] Officers searched the suspect
suitcase in its owner's presence and, among baby diapers and clothes, found a
carefully wrapped package resembling a gift that contained 30 sachets of
cannabis weighing 409 grams. [@1] Questioned by customs agents, the traveler
admitted that the pakalolo was his and came from his own plantation, hidden
inside a taro field at Rurutu. [@1]
