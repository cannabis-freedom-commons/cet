Preet Bharara, the U.S. Attorney for the Southern District of New York,
announced the extradition of Orlando Rodriguez from the Dominican Republic on 15
November 2012. [@1] Rodriguez, 36, arrived in New York on 14 November 2012, was
arraigned the next day before U.S. Magistrate Judge James C. Francis IV, and
remains in federal custody. [@1] He had been arrested in the Dominican Republic
on 16 August 2012. [@2]

Rodriguez was charged in August 2012 in a 40-count racketeering indictment with
eight other members of the Rodriguez Enterprise. [@1] Prosecutors say the
enterprise's members sold large quantities of marijuana, carried out murders and
other violent acts, and transported and laundered millions of dollars. [@1] The
indictment charges Rodriguez with the 2000 murder of a man known by the alias
Carlos Valentin, an attempted murder in 1996, and multiple racketeering and
marijuana trafficking crimes. [@1] He is also charged with laundering the
enterprise's marijuana proceeds in the Dominican Republic. [@1]

The indictment was filed in connection with Operation Green Venom, a
multi-agency investigation led by ICE Homeland Security Investigations that was
first announced in October 2010. [@1] Bharara praised the investigative work of
HSI and the NYPD. [@1] He thanked the Drug Enforcement Administration, the U.S.
Marshals Service, the Bergen County prosecutor's office and other agencies for
their help. [@1] Bharara said the investigation is ongoing. [@1] The charges in
the indictment are accusations, and the defendants are presumed innocent unless
and until proven guilty. [@1]
