2nd Platoon, B Company, 1st Battalion, 5th Infantry Regiment, 1st Stryker
Brigade Combat Team, 25th Infantry Division, took part in a patrol led by Afghan
partners near Combat Outpost Sperwan Ghar in southern Kandahar. [@1] The
patrol's stated purpose was to get to know the people in the village of
Shengazi. [@1] The platoon had never been to Shengazi before and did not even
know what the village was called. [@1] On the morning of Jan. 3, the platoon and
its Afghan National Army partners marched out of Sperwan Ghar with rifles, mine
sweepers and a Military Working Dog. [@1]

During the patrol, the Afghan soldiers found and destroyed more than 2,000 lbs
of marijuana. [@1] Counternarcotics had become a focus for Afghan security
forces operations in the area. [@1] Growing and using marijuana is both against
Afghan law and Islam. [@1] For the first time since 2002, the Afghan government
was putting emphasis on drug eradication. [@1] The platoon leader said the
Taliban would no longer have the income that marijuana brings them to use
against the Americans and the Afghan army. [@1]

The 2011 fighting season was over, and U.S. forces were adjusting their mission
down to platoon-level operations to reflect the changed focus. [@1] The patrol
also carried radios to hand to heads of households that receive the Afghan
government's official broadcasts, plus newsletters with the same information for
literate family members. [@1]
