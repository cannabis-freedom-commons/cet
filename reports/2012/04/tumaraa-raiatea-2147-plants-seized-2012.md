Gendarmes stationed on Raiatea carried out an important seizure of cannabis on
Tuesday 3 April 2012. [@1] The seizure was made on the island of Raiatea, at
kilometre marker 35.5 in the commune of Tumaraa. [@1] Two brothers, one aged 42
and the other 34, were arrested in connection with the seizure. [@1] Both men
were being questioned under garde à vue, France's short-term police custody.
[@1]

In the Vaiau sector of Raiatea, officers seized two plantations. [@1] The
plantations most likely belonged to the two arrested men. [@1] The first
plantation held 1,109 cannabis plants spread over 70 square metres. [@1] Those
plants stood between 20 centimetres and 3 metres high. [@1] The second
plantation, on a neighbouring parcel of 90 square metres, held 1,038 cannabis
plants. [@1] None of the plants on that second parcel was taller than 2 metres.
[@1] Counted together, officers seized 2,147 cannabis plants on 160 square
metres, about 0.016 hectares. [@1]

The cannabis seized at Tumaraa was described as pakalolo, the local name for the
plant. [@1]
