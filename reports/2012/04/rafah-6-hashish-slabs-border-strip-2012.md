Palestinian anti-narcotics police in Rafah governorate seized six farsh of
hashish. [@1] Officers seized the hashish during an attempted smuggling through
the border-strip area. [@1]

Rafah anti-narcotics director Major Anwar Zaroub said the operation was part of
continuous monitoring of the border-strip area. [@1] He warned that the
anti-narcotics police would stand watch and strike with an iron fist against
anyone trying to bring drugs in. [@1]

Anti-narcotics public-relations and media director Captain Saadi al-Sheikh
Khalil praised the department members' efforts. [@1] He said its members draw
closer to God by fighting this dangerous scourge that leads to the loss of
society. [@1]
