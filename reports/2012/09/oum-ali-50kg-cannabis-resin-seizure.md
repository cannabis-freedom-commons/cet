Algerian anti-drug officers arrested two men, a Tunisian in his forties and a
34-year-old Algerian, and placed them in custody. [@1] The two men were
travelling in a utility vehicle and were about to hand the drugs to two
smugglers who were waiting for them. [@2] The smugglers fled after learning that
the two men had been arrested. [@2] After the two confessed, officers took steps
to arrest the fugitives inside or outside Algeria through Interpol. [@3]

More than 50 kg of cannabis resin on its way to Tunisia was seized and an
international trafficking network was dismantled. [@1] Security sources said 50
kg of cannabis resin was seized in a stakeout on the RN16 near Oum Ali. [@2]
Tébessa police described the haul as a 'demi quintal', or 50 kg, of drugs bound
for Europe. [@3] A search found the drugs hidden in the boot of the car. [@1]

The operation was carried out by the Bir El Ater district anti-drug brigade
together with the Annaba regional anti-drug service. [@1] The car was a Peugeot
406 registered in Oum El Bouaghi province and was stopped at the Oum Ali
junction during the night from Saturday to Sunday. [@1] Information reaching
Annaba police pointed to an attempt to smuggle a significant quantity of drugs
from Bir El Ater to Tunisia and then Italy. [@3] The two detainees were to be
presented to the courts once the investigation was complete. [@2]
