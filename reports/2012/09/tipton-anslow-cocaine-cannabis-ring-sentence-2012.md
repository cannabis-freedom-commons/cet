John Anslow, 33, from Tipton, was sentenced in September 2012 to 22 years in
prison for conspiracy to supply cocaine and cannabis, convicted in his absence
while on the run. [@1,5,6,7,8,9] He led a UK-wide gang distributing large quantities of
cocaine and cannabis. [@1,5,6,7] The network distributed drugs across the country from
a Tipton base. [@1] He was sentenced alongside seven others for large-scale drug
supply. [@4] His later appeal described concurrent terms for cocaine supply and
for cannabis supply. [@3]

Police in Liverpool uncovered the operation in February 2011 when officers found
cannabis resin worth £1.1 million at a lock-up in Formby. [@2,9] Police had
uncovered the network after bugging his Bradley flat in December 2010 and
January 2011. [@1] Two days later, on 11 February 2011, West Midlands Police officers found half a kilo of pure cocaine, cutting agents and a drugs press at a Tipton business premises belonging to one of Anslow's associates. [@9] When they raided the flat they found a Rolex watch and
evidence of a luxury lifestyle. [@1] Police found £107,900 in cash hidden inside two safes at his home. [@9] A joint Staffordshire and West Midlands
police investigation uncovered the extensive dealing network based in the West
Midlands but spread further afield. [@4] Police found Anslow lying low at a caravan park in Bewdley on 12 August 2011. [@9]

On 23 January 2012 Anslow was sprung from a prison van while held over a murder
case. [@1] A gang of three masked men stopped the prison van, smashed its windows with sledgehammers and punched its driver. [@9] The gang that freed him used sledgehammers as he was driven from HMP Hewell to Stafford Crown Court. [@8] The breakout made Anslow Britain's most wanted man at the time. [@7,8] The breakout made Anslow the first category A prisoner to escape custody in more than 17 years. [@9] He spent 13 months on the run in northern Cyprus, held over
immigration offences before deportation to the UK. [@1] He fled to Cyprus and
was on the run until March of last year, when he was arrested on immigration
offences and deported back to the UK. [@4,7,8] After 439 days on the run he was tracked to the Alsancak district of Northern Cyprus and returned to the UK in March 2013. [@9] Reporting restrictions were then
lifted, revealing that he was serving a 22-year drug-trafficking sentence
alongside a further term for the escape. [@4] He was found guilty of escaping from custody, adding another seven years to his original sentence. [@7,8,9]
