Two occupants and several accomplices began unloading burlap bags from two boats
at San Gotardo, a natural port on the Paraná in Puerto Rico, Misiones. [@1] When
Prefectura Naval Argentina officers intervened, the traffickers fled into the
darkness and hid in dense vegetation, and investigators believed they escaped
toward Paraguay. [@1] The cargo came from Paraguay and was found on the
riverbank about 175 kilometres north of Posadas. [@1] A Prefectura patrol had
watched the area for several days on unofficial information about the landing of
a marijuana shipment. [@1]

Officers found more than 200 burlap bags holding 4,489 "bricks" of marijuana,
with a total weight of 4,098 kilos. [@1] The Prefectura's initial investigation
valued the load at 19 million pesos. [@1] The operation was conducted under
Escudo Norte, a national Security Ministry programme against crime. [@2] The
Federal Court of Eldorado, with Judge José Luis Casals, took charge of the case.
[@2] Officers were still combing the area for those responsible. [@2] The
seizure had taken place by 8 September 2012, when the first reports were
published. [@3]

Governor Maurice Closs called on the Prefecture and the courts to burn the
seized drugs immediately under controlled conditions. [@1] He said keeping
seized drugs for so long encouraged corruption and made no sense. [@1]
