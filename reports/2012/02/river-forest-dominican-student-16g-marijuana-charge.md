Police were dispatched to the Power Hall dorm room of 20-year-old Marcin Sip of
Chicago on Feb. 6, according to River Forest Deputy Police Chief Craig Rutz.
[@1] University staff called police after they smelled marijuana coming from
Sip’s room. [@1] Security called River Forest police, and Sip admitted to them
that he threw a box out of the window, which they recovered on the ground. [@1]

Police opened the box and found a substance that appeared to be marijuana and
small bags containing pills, powder and syringes. [@1] The powder was identified
as heroin and methamphetamine, Rutz said. [@1] There were other prescription
pills and 16 grams of marijuana. [@1] Police said Sip confessed to having
methamphetamine, heroin, Xanax and Klonopin in his possession. [@2]

Sip was charged with four felonies: possession of less than five grams of
methamphetamine, manufacture or delivery of less than 10 grams of heroin, and
two charges of possession of a controlled substance. [@1] He was also charged
with misdemeanor possession of marijuana. [@2] Campus safety said it was the
first time either methamphetamine or heroin had been reported at the school.
[@1] Police said they did not think the incident was part of a larger drug
operation at the school. [@1]
