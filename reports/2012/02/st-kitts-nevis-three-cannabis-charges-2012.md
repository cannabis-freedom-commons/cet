Officers of the Gingerland Police Station charged Kevin Merchant with possession
of controlled drugs (cannabis) after searching him in the Old Manor area on
Tuesday 21 February. [@1] Dean Daniel was arrested on the same day in the same
area during a police stop and search operation and charged after being found
with a quantity of cannabis. [@1] On St. Kitts, Eugene ‘Killer Cat’ Davis was
taken into custody and charged with possession of cannabis and possession of
cannabis with intent to supply another. [@1]

Drug Unit officers executed a search warrant at the Newton Ground premises of
Nakisha Francis and Eugene Davis, seeking controlled drugs, arms and ammunition.
[@1] The search revealed 972 grams of cannabis wrapped in a shower curtain, with
some on a barrel in the kitchen. [@1]

The charges were announced by the Police Press and Public Relations Office in a
release covering criminal cases across St. Kitts and Nevis over a two-week
period. [@1] Police said drug-related charges were preferred against three
people, two on Nevis and one on St. Kitts. [@1]
