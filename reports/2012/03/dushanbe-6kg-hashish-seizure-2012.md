Officers from the Drug Control Agency under the President of Tajikistan detained
a 25-year-old resident of Khatlon province during operational-search activities
in Dushanbe on suspicion of illegal drug trafficking. [@1] The agency's
English-language report dates the operation to 13 March 2012. [@4] The same
report says a package confiscated from the man tested positive as hashish
weighing some six kilograms. [@4]

Officers found and seized a cellophane bag holding a dark-green plant substance
pressed into book shape. [@1] Avesta, citing the agency's press centre, reports
the bag contained six such pressed substances with a specific smell. [@2] The
agency's forensic laboratory identified the seized substance as hashish weighing
about 6 kilograms. [@1]

A criminal case was opened against the detained man under Article 200, part 4 of
the Criminal Code of Tajikistan, covering illegal trafficking of narcotic
substances or psychotropic substances with intent to sell in especially large
quantities. [@1] Khovar describes the suspicion against him as drug smuggling
rather than illegal trafficking. [@3] The agency said its investigation and
operational group was taking measures to identify the man's accomplices. [@2]
