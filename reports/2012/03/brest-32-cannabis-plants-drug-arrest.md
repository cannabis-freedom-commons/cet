The case began with a routine traffic check in Brest, where the anti-crime
brigade stopped a car carrying three people on the Thursday night and found 11
grams of heroin on the driver. [@1] The drug squad identified the driver's
presumed supplier as a 30-year-old man originally from the Central African
Republic who lived in Penn-ar-C'hleuz. [@1] After surveillance was set up,
officers arrested him at 3pm on Monday in the act of reselling drugs at his
home. [@1]

In the search of the home, investigators found 32 cannabis plants spread across
two rooms used solely to grow them. [@1] The same search also turned up 404
grams of pure heroin, a handgun and twelve shotgun cartridges hidden in a bag
under a sofa; those items are not counted as cannabis. [@1] He had just supplied
4 grams of heroin to a 32-year-old woman who had come from Landivisiau, and was
charging 20 to 40 euros a gram against a market price of 60 to 70 euros. [@1]

In police custody the man, already known to the courts for drug trafficking
around Nantes and Orléans, reportedly admitted dealing in Brest since September
2010 to about ten customers. [@1] He was referred to the prosecutor's office on
Wednesday, placed in pre-trial detention and was due to appear at an
immediate-appearance hearing on Thursday. [@1]
