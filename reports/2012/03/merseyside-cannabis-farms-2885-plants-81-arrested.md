Police in Merseyside arrested 81 people on suspicion of offences connected with
cannabis cultivation in the first two weeks of a month-long regional operation
against cannabis farming. [@1] At Newport Court in Vauxhall, officers raided a
house and found the components for a cannabis farm in an upstairs bedroom. [@2]
A 57-year-old man and a 41-year-old woman were arrested at Newport Court on
suspicion of cannabis cultivation. [@2] The man was released on police bail
after being interviewed while the woman faced no further action. [@2]

The month-long regional operation launched on 5 March and, in its first two
weeks, 68 warrants were executed across Merseyside. [@1] The warrants led to the
discovery of 36 cannabis farms and the seizure of 2,885 plants. [@1] The plants
had an estimated street value of around £1 million. [@1] Officers also seized
£12,750 in cash. [@1]

A Merseyside Police superintendent said the operation reflected a commitment to
ridding communities of cannabis farms. [@1] The superintendent said those
involved in farming cannabis were often linked to firearms and other serious
criminality funded by the trade. [@1] A Merseyside Police statement said
organised crime groups were growing cannabis to fund gun crime and serious
violence, sometimes stealing rival groups' crops. [@2]
