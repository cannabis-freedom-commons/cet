Border police officers of the Trebinje unit stopped a Renault with Montenegrin
registration plates at the Deleuša international border crossing, on the way
into Bosnia and Herzegovina, at 06:15 on 13 March 2012. [@1] Officers found
packages hidden at the left and right rear fenders holding a plant substance
that looked like marijuana. [@2] A preliminary expert examination found 17
packages with a total weight of 12.6 kilograms of marijuana. [@3]

The driver was Dejan Stešević, 27, and the passenger was the former policeman
Željko Goranović, 29, from Nikšić; both were arrested at the crossing. [@1] They
were deprived of liberty the same day, and the vehicle and the marijuana were
temporarily seized. [@3] The Trebinje border police unit said it would file a
report with the Prosecutor's Office of Bosnia and Herzegovina against both
Montenegrin citizens for the criminal offence of unauthorised traffic in
narcotic drugs. [@3]

Goranović had been one of the police officers arrested in the "Kapitol"
operation against car smuggling on 25 November 2009. [@1] That operation
detained 17 people, among them three police officers and a customs official.
[@1] A few days later the High Court ended his custody because the deputy
special state prosecutor did not request an investigation. [@1] Montenegro's
police directorate did not answer whether it had information about the arrest of
Goranović and Stešević. [@1] The head of its anti-narcotics department, Miša
Laković, said he had received no information about the arrest of Montenegrin
citizens for smuggling marijuana. [@1]
