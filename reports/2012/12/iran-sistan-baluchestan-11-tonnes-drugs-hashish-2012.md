Iran's border patrol police said they seized more than 11 tonnes of drugs in
what they called the biggest bust since the 1979 Islamic revolution. [@1] The
haul was seized in the Jakigour area of Sistan-Baluchestan province, which
borders Pakistan, border police chief Brigadier General Hossein Zolfaqari said.
[@1] Zolfaqari said 10 tonnes of the load were opium and the rest hashish. [@1]
One drug trafficker was killed and a number of others were wounded but escaped
across the border, the general said. [@1] The seizure came at the end of a
36-hour operation. [@1]

Iran lies on a key transit route for narcotics headed for Europe and the Middle
East, and official figures put its drug seizures at more than 350 tonnes, mostly
opium, since the start of 2012. [@1]
