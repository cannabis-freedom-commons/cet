A fight among three men brought police to a home on A.T. Illidge Road in
Philipsburg on Monday afternoon, 2 July 2012. [@1] The fight ended at a home on
Bermuda Drive on A.T. Illidge Road, where police found several pots of marijuana
plants. [@1]

The acting prosecutor and a K-9 dog were at the scene assisting the police. [@1]
Police confiscated the marijuana plants and arrested three people. [@1] The
police narcotics department was investigating the case. [@1]
