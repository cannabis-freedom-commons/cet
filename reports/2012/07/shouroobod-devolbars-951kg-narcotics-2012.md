Police officers seized 951 kilograms of narcotics, primarily cannabis, in an
operation called Devolbars in the Devolbars area of Shouroobod district in
Khatlon, Abdulvahob Iskandarov, deputy chief of the Khatlon police directorate
for the Kulob region, told Asia-Plus on 20 July 2012. [@1] During the same
operation officers found an arms cache containing firearms, camouflage uniform,
bayonets and more than 1,000 bullets of different calibre. [@1]

On 15 June police took one bayonet and 20 bags of a drug that tested positive
for cannabis, weighing nearly 419 kilograms, from a hide in Devolbars that
reportedly belonged to Afghan smugglers. [@1] On 10 June more than 100 kilograms
of cannabis were seized in the same area after an armed clash with Afghan
traffickers, who then hid on Afghan territory, and a TOZ-16 rifle was found at
the scene. [@1] Asia-Plus's chronicle of June put that 10 June seizure at 100.6
kilograms of cannabis-group drugs, together with a TOZ-16 rifle. [@3] On 6 June
more than 90 kilograms of drugs were seized in the Devolbars area during a
special operation. [@1] The Interior Ministry's website, as cited by Asia-Plus
on 18 June, reported the same 15 June cache: one bayonet and 20 bags of a drug
that tested positive for cannabis, weighing nearly 419 kilograms. [@2]

Iskandarov said a section of the Tajik-Afghan border in Shouroobod, about 35,000
hectares, had never been controlled by border guards, and described Shouroobod
as the most vulnerable part of that border. [@1]
