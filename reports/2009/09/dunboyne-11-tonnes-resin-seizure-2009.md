Gardaí and Customs officers found 1.1 tonnes of cannabis resin at an industrial
premises in Dunboyne, County Meath, on the evening of 17 September 2009. [@1,2,3]
The resin had an estimated street value of €6.6 million, subject to forensic
analysis. [@1,2,3]

A man in his 30s arrested close to the scene was held at Blanchardstown Garda
station under Section 2 of the Drug Trafficking Act. [@1,2,3] He appeared in
court on 19 September and was remanded to appear before Cloverhill District
Court on 24 September. [@1] A second man arrested in Carlow was charged and was
due before Blanchardstown District Court on 21 September. [@1]
