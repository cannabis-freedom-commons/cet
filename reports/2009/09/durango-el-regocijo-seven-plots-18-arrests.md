On 21 September 2009, soldiers under the 10th Military Zone found seven
marijuana plots of different sizes and a camp used for drying and packing the
drug near El Regocijo in the municipality of Durango. [@1] The operation led to
the detention of 18 people, including three minors. [@1]

Troops seized 700 kilograms of marijuana. [@1] They also seized three long guns,
seven handguns, nine magazines and 85 cartridges of various calibres. [@1] They
seized a pick-up truck, a quad bike and a vacuum-packing machine. [@1] The plots
and the camp were destroyed on the spot by manual labour and incineration, with
the corresponding authorities informed. [@1]

The detainees and the seized items were placed at the disposal of the
corresponding authorities. [@1] The army said the action formed part of the
Permanent Campaign against Drug Trafficking and enforcement of the Federal
Firearms and Explosives Law. [@1]
