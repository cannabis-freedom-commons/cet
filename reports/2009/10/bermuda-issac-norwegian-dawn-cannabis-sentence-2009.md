Anthony Issac was jailed for seven years at Supreme Court in Bermuda after
smuggling marijuana aboard a cruise ship. [@2] He had admitted guilt in
conspiracy to import cannabis and possession of cannabis with intent to supply.
[@2] The 34-year-old truck driver from the Bronx apologized to the court. [@1]
He said a friend had paid him $10,000 to go on vacation and drop the suitcase in
Bermuda. [@1]

CSET (Combined Seaport Enforcement Team) officers saw Issac disembark the cruise
liner at Dockyard. [@2] Officers opened a black suitcase on his bed and found 30
brown paper packages of plant material. [@2] Norwegian Dawn passenger Issac
brought the drugs in a black suitcase but was caught before they reached the
street. [@2] The case concerned 5,048 grams of marijuana, which prosecutors
valued at $252,000. [@1]

Issac was sentenced to seven years' imprisonment on both counts, to run
concurrently. [@2]
