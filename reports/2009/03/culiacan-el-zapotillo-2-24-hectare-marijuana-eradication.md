On 10 March 2009, personnel of the 24th Motorized Cavalry Regiment found a
marijuana plantation during ground reconnaissance near the village of El
Zapotillo, in the Melchor Ocampo district of Culiacan, Sinaloa. [@1] The
plantation covered 2.24 hectares. [@1] The Defence Ministry announced the find
from Lomas de Sotelo on 13 March 2009 as part of its Permanent Campaign against
Drug Trafficking and enforcement of the Federal Firearms and Explosives Law.
[@1]

No one was detained in the operation. [@1] Soldiers destroyed the crop where it
stood by manual labour and incineration, and the corresponding authorities were
informed of the actions. [@1]
