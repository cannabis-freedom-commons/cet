Officers from the Gorno-Badakhshan counternarcotics directorate of the Drug
Control Agency detained Afghan national Iskandar waladi Shoniyoz as he illegally
crossed the Panj River near the village of Niwodak in Shughnon district late on
Saturday. [@1] A sack of narcotic that tested positive for hashish, weighing
18.4 kilograms, was confiscated from him. [@1] Asia-Plus reported the detention
from Dushanbe on 20 July 2009, citing the Drug Control Agency press service.
[@1]

Criminal proceedings were instituted against the Afghan trafficker, and an
investigation was under way. [@1] A US Embassy Dushanbe cable sent on 17 August
2009, summarizing July law-enforcement developments, reported the same detention
of Iskandar waladi Shoniyoz by drug control officers from the Badakhshan
department of the Drug Control Agency. [@2] The embassy account dated the arrest
to 20 July and said he was apprehended while illegally crossing the Panj River
near Niwodak in the Shugnan district. [@2] The cable said officers confiscated a
sack of hashish weighing 18.4 kilograms on his arrest. [@2] The embassy summary
also said criminal proceedings had been instituted and that an investigation was
under way. [@2]
