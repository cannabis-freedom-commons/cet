Military personnel destroyed 60 marijuana plots in the highlands of Sinaloa,
Mexico's Defence Secretariat said. [@1] The plots covered 67,110 square metres,
or 6.711 hectares. [@1] The military also reported destroying 84 kilograms of
loose marijuana in the eradication operation. [@1]

The work formed part of the Sierra Madre eradication operations, during searches
for drug crops in the state's mountainous area. [@1] The plots were destroyed by
hand and burned where they were found, according to the secretariat. [@1] The
military said it also destroyed two poppy plots covering 550 square metres. [@1]
