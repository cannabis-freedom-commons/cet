Two gangs operating in Cianorte, in the northwest of Paraná, were arrested
during a police operation on Thursday 21 May 2009. [@1] The 17 detainees were
accused of drug trafficking and robbery. [@1] The leaders of the two groups,
Anderson Domingos de Oliveira and Thiago Camargo, were among those arrested.
[@1]

Officers carried out 21 search and seizure warrants and 17 arrest warrants. [@1]
The court-ordered warrants began to be served simultaneously at 6 a.m. in
several neighbourhoods of the city. [@1] With the groups, police found cocaine,
marijuana and firearms. [@2] The operation involved 80 police officers from
Cianorte, Curitiba, Maringá and Campo Mourão. [@2] Among the weapons seized were
a 12-gauge shotgun, a revolver and toy guns used by the gang in robberies. [@1]

Delegate Ítalo Sêga of Cianorte said the gangs had robbed businesses in the city
and nearby towns in recent months and also trafficked drugs in the region. [@1]
The 17 prisoners were taken to the police station and joined 114 others,
bringing the total to 131. [@1] A search of the cells turned up piercing
instruments made by prisoners, mobile phones, a little marijuana and a hole in
the wall used to communicate between cells. [@1]
