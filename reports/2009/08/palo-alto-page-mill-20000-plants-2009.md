Officers destroyed about 20,000 marijuana plants, which they valued at about
$60 million, in raids on two foothill gardens near Page Mill Road in Palo Alto
and on Midpeninsula open space on 5 August 2009. [@1] Palo Alto police SWAT
officers joined the Santa Clara County sheriff's marijuana-eradication team and
state fish and game agents to sweep both gardens and remove the plants. [@1]
No one was arrested; officers found several unoccupied camps, signs of
unauthorised hunting, two bags of ammunition and a Taser-style weapon. [@1]

A later account of the county's summer campaign described a July eradication
off Page Mill Road at the same size and street value, and said the July raid
had turned up a loaded 12-gauge shotgun. [@2] The sheriff's office had already
destroyed about 220,000 plants that season, up from 190,000 the year before. [@2]
