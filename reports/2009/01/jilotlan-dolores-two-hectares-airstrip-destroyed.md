On 24 January 2009, soldiers of the 62nd Infantry Battalion, exploiting
information provided through a citizen complaint, carried out ground
reconnaissance around the hamlets of La Palma de Coco and Las Cruces in the
municipality of Jilotlán de los Dolores, Jalisco. [@1] No one was detained
during the operation, which ended with the plantation and the landing strip
destroyed on site. [@1]

Near those hamlets the troops located a marijuana plantation covering two
hectares. [@1] They also found a clandestine landing strip lying beside that
plantation. [@1] Soldiers destroyed both the two-hectare marijuana plantation
and the clandestine landing strip beside it by manual labour and incineration
directly at the place where they had been found. [@1] The competent authorities
attested to the facts at the scene. [@1]

The Defence Ministry said the reconnaissance, destruction and attestation were
carried out within the Permanent Campaign against Drug Trafficking and under the
enforcement of the Federal Firearms and Explosives Act. [@1]
