On a tip-off, a joint team led by Deputy Commandant Murlidharan and Inspector
Sawyambir of the 195th battalion BSF with excise department officials raided
Warabari under Sedhai Police Station. [@1] The team recovered 200 kilograms of
dry ganja worth Rs 10 lakh from an abandoned place in Warabari. [@1] The raid
ran from about 10:30 am to 2 pm. [@1] Nobody was arrested in connection with the
seizure. [@1]

A BSF official said the force would keep conducting such raids across the state
to curb smuggling of ganja, phensedyl and other contraband items to Bangladesh.
[@1] Officials said illegal ganja cultivation had become an alarming problem in
the state. [@1]

Officials said security agencies had destroyed 12,10,925 ganja plants worth Rs
11.34 crore in 2014. [@1] Plantations of cannabis are done on a large scale in
the interior and inaccessible areas of the border region. [@1] Most of these
plantations are tucked away in hills and forests, which government agencies have
a tough time detecting and destroying. [@1] According to officials, a large
number of villagers in Tripura cultivate cannabis and smuggle it to Bangladesh
to earn money. [@1]
