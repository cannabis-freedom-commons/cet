In the night of 12 to 13 February 2015, the French Navy surveillance frigate
Ventôse pursued a go-fast boat heading toward Guadeloupe. [@3] The boat,
suspected of drug trafficking, had been flagged by the OCRTIS via the UK
National Crime Agency and the Saint Lucia coast guard. [@3] Four to five
suspects were placed in police custody after initially fleeing and being caught
the next day with help from anglophone authorities. [@1] At least four of those
arrested were reported to be of Saint-Vincent origin. [@2]

The boat was carrying more than one tonne of marijuana. [@1] During the
helicopter pursuit the five people aboard threw bales of drugs overboard, and
the Ventôse crew recovered nearly forty bales, about one tonne of marijuana, by
daybreak. [@3] The crew had come from the north of the Lesser Antilles arc and
appeared to be heading for the Guadeloupe coast. [@1]

The OCRTIS, the central office for the repression of unlawful drug trafficking,
took charge of the investigation. [@1] According to the OCRTIS in Guadeloupe,
the cannabis herb was intended mainly for the local market, unlike cocaine which
was reserved for export to Europe. [@2]
