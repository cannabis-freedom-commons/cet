Malawi Revenue Authority officers seized 29.5 kg of Indian hemp at Mwanza Border
on 21 January 2015, during inspections of an Intercape bus coming from South
Africa. [@1] The incident took place on Wednesday, 21 January 2015, during
inspections conducted on the bus from South Africa. [@1] Police immediately
arrested the carton's owner, Prisca Musesa. [@1]

The hemp was disguised in well packaged bricks, sealed in a carton, and was
suspected to be destined to another country. [@1] The hemp remained in police
custody while the case continued. [@1]

Wilma Chalulu, the authority's marketing communications manager, said the carton
containing the prohibited drug belonged to Musesa. [@2] The revenue authority
appealed to the public to tip it off about smuggling through toll-free numbers
847 for Airtel and TNM subscribers and 80000847 for MTL subscribers. [@1]
