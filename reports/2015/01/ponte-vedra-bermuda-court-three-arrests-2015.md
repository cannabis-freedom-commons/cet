Three Ponte Vedra Beach men were arrested on drug charges after detectives
executed a search warrant Friday morning at a home at 125 Bermuda Court in
Sawgrass. [@1] Detectives with the St. Johns County Sheriff's Office and the
S.W.A.T. team executed the warrant at 7:30 at 125 Bermuda Court. [@1]

The warrant followed a two-month undercover investigation into suspected illegal
narcotics activity at the home, begun after neighborhood complaints. [@1]
Detectives seized a large amount of suspected cannabis, equipment allegedly used
to extract THC from the cannabis plant, other drug paraphernalia and a small
amount of cocaine. [@1]

The men arrested were Jordan Alexander Grossholz, 20, Zachary C. Redman, 21, and
Travis James Ables, 24, all of 125 Bermuda Court. [@1]

All three were charged with maintaining a drug dwelling, a third-degree felony,
and possession of drug paraphernalia, a misdemeanor. [@1] Grossholz was also
charged with possession of cannabis with intent to sell, manufacture, or deliver
and possession of more than 20 grams of cannabis. [@1] Redman was also charged
with possession of cocaine and possession of less than 20 grams of cannabis.
[@1] Ables was also charged with possession of less than 20 grams of cannabis.
[@1] All three were booked into the St. Johns County Jail. [@2] Deputies said
more arrests were pending further investigation. [@2]
