Officers of the interdistrict department for the Kulyab group of districts of
the Khatlon department of Tajikistan's Drug Control Agency detained a local
resident in the city of Kulyab. [@1] The account was given by the press centre
of the country's anti-drug agency. [@1] The detention took place on 7 January
2015. [@2]

During a personal search, officers seized a cellophane wrap from the man's
pocket containing 50 pressed briquettes. [@1] A forensic examination established
that the seized substance was the narcotic drug hashish. [@1]

A criminal case was opened under Article 200, part 2, on illegal trafficking of
narcotic drugs with intent to sell on a large scale. [@1] The detained man was
placed in the temporary detention facility. [@1]
