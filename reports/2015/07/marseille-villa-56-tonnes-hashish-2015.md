French police discovered nearly six tonnes of cannabis resin, known as hashish, in a villa in Marseille and arrested three men, the interior ministry said on Monday 27 July 2015. [@1] The find came after months of investigation and ranks among the biggest cannabis seizures in France in recent years. [@1]

Interior Minister Bernard Cazeneuve said police had tracked the three men, believed to belong to a vast drug-trafficking network, to Marseille and the nearby town of Vitrolles on Sunday. [@1] Officers found 5.6 tonnes of hashish in the villa and another 150 kilos of the drug in a car. [@1] Cazeneuve congratulated the officers on what he called a major success. [@1]
