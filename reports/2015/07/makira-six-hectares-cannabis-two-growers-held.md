Gendarmes of the Antalaha territorial company carried out a special operation in
the Makira protected reserve in north-east Madagascar. [@1] They destroyed a
cannabis plantation covering six hectares. [@1] The operation was carried out
jointly with agents of the conservation group WCS and lasted ten days, according
to the Malagasy daily Les Nouvelles. [@1] The party left Antalaha on 18 July
2015 and travelled more than 80 km to reach the area. [@1]

Two cannabis growers were arrested, one of them a 52-year-old traditional
healer. [@1] Two hectares of the plantation belonged to the healer and four to
the other grower. [@1] Captain Pierre Ramonjanirina said the growers practised
slash-and-burn cultivation and had escaped WCS patrols, so the company made a
long detour to avoid alerting them. [@1] Gendarmes seized 57 kg of cannabis as
evidence. [@1]

The two growers were brought before the prosecutor the previous Monday and
placed in pre-trial detention. [@1] Ramonjanirina said another operation would
follow in the coming days to dismantle other cannabis plantations. [@1]
