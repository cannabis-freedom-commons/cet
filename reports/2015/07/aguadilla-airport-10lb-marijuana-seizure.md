Personnel assigned to the Drugs Division at Rafael Hernández Airport in
Aguadilla seized a package containing 10.25 pounds (about 4.65 kg) of marijuana
at about 6:46 p.m. on Friday 10 July 2015. [@1] No arrests were made in the
seizure. [@1]

The seizure resulted from a routine inspection at the cargo terminal of an
international flight from California to Barbados. [@1]

Agent Ariel Vera of the Drugs Division made the seizure and consulted prosecutor
Pier Montaperto. [@1] The prosecutor ordered the substance confiscated and the
corresponding investigation to continue. [@1]
