A 28-year-old man who tended the cannabis plants at a disused school in Skarstad
outside Lysekil was sentenced to two years and six months in prison for an
aggravated drug offence. [@2] The court also ordered that he be expelled from
Sweden. [@2] The 28-year-old had been arrested when police raided the old school
on 2 December 2014. [@3] He was convicted of tending a cannabis cultivation in
an old school at Skarstad outside Lysekil. [@2]

Police found 570 cannabis plants at the school, and a large number of people
were said to have taken part in the production. [@2] Officers seized 4,508.6
grams of dried cannabis in the operation. [@3] Another 497 grams of dried
cannabis were found inside the cultivation. [@3] The cultivation could have
produced about 31 kilograms of marijuana at harvest. [@3] The men who worked the
sites watered and fertilised the plants, then trimmed, harvested and dried them,
the prosecutor said. [@4] The building had been fitted out inside as a
greenhouse, with an insulated shell and a climate made optimal for growing
cannabis. [@4]

The court said the man had an important role in keeping the cultivation
productive but was subordinate in the organisation and did not share in its
profits. [@3] He admitted knowing about 337 of the plants, those present while
he worked there between 18 October and 27 November 2014, and denied knowledge of
the other 233. [@3] He left the farm after about a month because he was afraid
of being arrested, which the court did not treat as a mitigating circumstance.
[@3]

The police investigation led to the questioning of 13 other people over other
suspected cannabis farms in the area. [@1] The wider case was handled by the
international public prosecutor's office in Gothenburg because offences had also
been committed in Denmark. [@3]
