Tajik border guards on routine patrol confronted a group of 10 to 13 armed
trespassers illegally crossing the border river in the area of the Hamadoni
border unit late on Sunday 6 July 2015. [@1] An exchange of gunfire followed,
leaving two of the trespassers dead while the others were forced back onto the
contiguous territory, with no casualties among the Tajik border guards. [@1] The
two dead were described as Afghan drug traffickers killed in Khatlon in the
night of 6 July. [@1]

Officers found more than 270 kilograms of hashish at the scene. [@1] A second
account says the border guards found more than 270 kilograms of hashish and the
two bodies of Afghan drug couriers during the inspection of the scene. [@2] The
group of 10 to 13 Afghan smugglers had tried to cross the border illegally in
the night from Monday to Tuesday to carry the drugs onto Tajik territory. [@2]

On a border-post section of the Hamadoni detachment on the Tajik-Afghan border,
an operational group of the SCNS Khatlon directorate together with a border
detail stopped the attempted illegal crossing and drug smuggling. [@3] The SCNS
investigative body was conducting an investigation at the scene. [@3]
