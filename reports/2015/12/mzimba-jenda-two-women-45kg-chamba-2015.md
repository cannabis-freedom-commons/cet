Police in Mzimba district arrested two women and accused them of being found in
possession of about 45 kilograms of chamba, the local name for cannabis. [@1]
The district's police publicist, Constable Kanjauso, named them as Esther
Kumwenda, 34, and Rose Misi, 19. [@1] Kanjauso said the two were travelling by
bus from Mzuzu to Lilongwe, where police suspected they had a market for the
drug. [@1]

Officers at the Jenda road block searched the bus and found the bag of chamba
the women had hidden. [@1] The women were held in custody awaiting their first
court appearance on charges of being found in possession of chamba. [@1]

Police said both women come from Traditional Authority Khosolo in Mzimba
district. [@1] Police warned that they would prosecute everyone involved in the
illegal trade. [@1]
