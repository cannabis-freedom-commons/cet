A scooter rider was stopped for a control at a general checkpoint the Multi
Disciplinary Team had set up at the East Roundabout, by the Causeway Bridge, in
Philipsburg. [@1] The rider ignored the officer's stop sign and drove through
the checkpoint. [@1] Officers made no attempt to chase him. [@1]

Shortly afterwards the team saw the same scooter rider coming towards the same
checkpoint again. [@1] Officers noticed that he had changed his shirt in order
to disguise himself so that they would not recognise him. [@1] He was stopped
near the RBTT bank on Union Road. [@1] During the control the suspect could not
properly identify himself. [@1] Officers searched him and arrested him after
finding a considerable amount of marihuana. [@1]

The marihuana and the scooter were confiscated for further investigation. [@1]
The suspect was taken to the police station for further questioning. [@1]
