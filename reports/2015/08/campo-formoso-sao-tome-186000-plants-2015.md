Military police officers acting on an anonymous tip located a large cannabis
plantation on the border between Campo Formoso and Mirangaba in northern Bahia.
[@1] The people tending the crop fled when the detachment arrived, and no
arrests were reported. [@1] The plantation's owner had not been located. [@1]

The blog places the crop at São Tomé. [@1] G1's account of the same 54th CIPM
operation places it at Canabrava. [@2] Police counted about 186,000 standing
cannabis plants at the site. [@1] Officers also seized about 10 kg of cannabis
seeds and 7 kg of marijuana ready for consumption. [@1] Police burned the crop
and drugs at the site, drew up a burning record and kept a sample to present at
the Campo Formoso police station. [@2] The military police described it as the
largest drug seizure in the region that year. [@1]

The operation brought together military police from the 54th and 24th CIPMs and
the specialised Caatinga company (CIPE-Caatinga). [@2] The eradication began on
Monday 17 August 2015 and resumed on the morning of Tuesday 18 August because of
the size of the planted area. [@2] Officers mapped the site so that further
judicial steps could be taken. [@1]
