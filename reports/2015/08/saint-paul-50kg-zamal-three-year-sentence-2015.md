Gendarmes in Saint-Paul, Réunion, seized 50 kilograms of zamal, the local name
for cannabis, from a man in his sixties. [@1] The dealer was 64 years old and
was tried by immediate appearance. [@2] He was sentenced on Tuesday 11 August
2015 to three years of firm prison. [@1]

Officers of the Possession gendarmerie brigade made the seizure at the
supplier's home. [@1] The gendarmerie of La Réunion announced the operation on
13 August 2015 as a record seizure of narcotics. [@2] The 50 kilograms of drug,
packed in cartons and rolls, were destroyed. [@2]

According to Journal de l'Île as cited by LINFO.re, the man told investigators
he had bought 8,000 euros of cannabis in order to resell it. [@1] He hoped to
make 20,000 euros by selling rolls at 20 euros each. [@1] He denied being a
trafficker and said he wanted to earn enough money to exile himself to Mayotte.
[@1]
