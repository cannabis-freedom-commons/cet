Jamaican narcotics agents seized a load of marijuana hidden in two shipping
containers at wharves in Kingston, the Caribbean island's capital. [@2] No one
was arrested in the incident. [@3]

In a Sunday statement, Jamaican police said investigators found 10 bags filled
with more than 453 kilograms (1,000 pounds) of compressed marijuana in a
container whose final destination was Suriname. [@1] Another container bound for
Holland held almost 34 kilograms (75 pounds) of the same drug. [@1] A second
container was bound for the Netherlands with nearly 75 pounds of marijuana. [@2]

Jamaica is believed to be the Caribbean's largest producer and exporter of
marijuana, with plants grown mostly on mountainsides, in swampy areas or hidden
among other crops. [@2] Drug-law reforms earlier in 2015 partially
decriminalised possession of small amounts of marijuana in Jamaica, but
authorities said they had not softened their stance on transnational trafficking
or illegal cultivation. [@1]
