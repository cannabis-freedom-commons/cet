Armenian police carrying out the nationwide Konoplya-mak-2015 anti-drug
operation discovered 4,612 kilograms of cannabis on the territory of the
republic on 18 August 2015 and destroyed it. [@1] The Armenian police
information and public relations department provided the destruction total and
the additional seizure figures on the anti-drug operation to Armenpress. [@1]
The 4,612-kilogram amount is the quantity of cannabis found across Armenia
during the 18 August phase of the 2015 operation and destroyed as part of that
phase. [@1]

In addition to the destroyed cannabis, officers seized 100 grams of wet cannabis
mass during the same operation. [@1] Officers also seized 1.5 grams of marijuana
and one homemade drum-type pistol, which police listed alongside the wet
cannabis mass as separate seizures. [@1] The pistol was reported together with
the cannabis and marijuana seizures in the police account of the 18 August
operation day. [@1]
