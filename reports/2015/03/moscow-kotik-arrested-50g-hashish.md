Moscow police detained a 32-year-old native of Georgia described as a leader of
the criminal underworld and a so-called "thief in law" known as "Kotik" in the
city's Central District. [@1] Other reporting identified the man as Georgy
Kalashyan, known in criminal circles by the same nickname. [@3]

During a search officers found and seized a packet of hashish weighing more than
50 grams, according to the Interior Ministry's press service. [@1] The police
statement quoted by the New Day news agency said the brown packet of pressed
plant material was identified as hashish by testing. [@4] Lenta, citing the
Interior Ministry's website, reported that officers found a packet containing 50
grams of hashish during a personal search. [@2]

A criminal case was opened on the basis of "illegal drug trafficking". [@1]
Police said they were establishing whether the detainee was involved in other
crimes. [@1]
