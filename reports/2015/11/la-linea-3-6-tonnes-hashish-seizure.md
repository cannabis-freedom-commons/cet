Spanish security services seized 3.6 tonnes of hashish in La Línea de la
Concepción, in the Cádiz area of southern Spain, as announced by Spanish police
on Monday. [@1] The operation remained open so investigators could locate the
suspected members of the trafficking network. [@1]

The seized hashish was hidden inside four stolen off-road vehicles parked in a
warehouse in the Andalusian town. [@1] The four high-end vehicles held a total
cargo of 3.6 tonnes of hashish divided into 116 crates. [@1] That 3.6-tonne
cargo equals 3,600 kilograms of hashish. [@1]

The operation leading to the seizure was launched on the basis of information
that a drug-trafficking network had succeeded in bringing a large quantity of
hashish into Spanish territory. [@1] A subsequent inquiry then led investigators
to the loaded vehicles in the warehouse. [@1] Police gave the account of the
warehouse, the vehicles and the quantity in a communiqué. [@1] No arrests,
charges or court outcomes were reported in the account. [@1]
