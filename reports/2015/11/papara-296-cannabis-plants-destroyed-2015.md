Gendarmes in Papara, on Tahiti, searched the home of a 23-year-old man after
detaining him drunk in public. [@1] The man admitted drinking home-made
fermented alcohol at his brother's home. [@1] At the house the gendarmes found a
barrel holding 100 litres of Komo Puaka, a fermented drink made locally. [@1]

In the same search they seized a cannabis plantation on the premises. [@1] Forty
of the plants were 50 centimetres tall. [@1] Twelve others were 1.5 metres tall,
and the plantation held 244 young shoots. [@1] All the plants and shoots seized
were destroyed. [@1] The three counts come to 296 cannabis plants and young
shoots. [@1]

The man detained was questioned about the facts. [@1]
