After receiving information that opened an investigation, gendarmes from the
Papara brigade uncovered an indoor pakalolo growing operation in the Faurahi
neighbourhood of Teva I Uta. [@2] The 38-year-old suspect sold the drug only in
the form of cuttings or whole dried branches. [@1] Placed in custody, he
admitted that his trafficking had earned him 3,300,000 CFP francs since February
2015. [@1]

Officers seized 525 cannabis plants at different stages of growth, along with
the equipment used to cultivate them. [@2] That equipment included neon lighting
blocks, rooting foam packs and growth hormone. [@1] Officers also discovered a
dryer where numerous branches corresponding to 60 plants that had reached
maturity had been put to dry ahead of their planned sale. [@2]

The operation was grown indoors and relied on artificial lighting for
cultivation. [@1] He was expected to appear before the criminal court in May of
the following year, with TNTV reporting the hearing date as 17 May. [@1]
