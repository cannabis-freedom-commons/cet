Spain's Guardia Civil and National Police dismantled an alleged criminal
organization dedicated to transporting and distributing cocaine and hashish
pollen in an operation code-named Zorrito. [@2] Investigators said the network
supplied cocaine to outlets in the Sierra de Cádiz and Jerez area while
financing itself by moving high-quality hashish to Galicia, Madrid and Toledo.
[@2]

Officers made a first seizure of 8.5 kilos of hashish that suspects had planned
to take to Arcos de la Frontera after they tried to flee a Guardia Civil
checkpoint. [@2] The group later gave young members three kilos of hashish
pollen in pellet form to take and sell in Madrid. [@2] Investigators recovered
1,100 grams of that pollen from a suitcase the youths left at their lodging when
they feared arrest. [@2]

After the final seizure in the case, officers detained the members still at
large, reaching 15 detainees in total, alongside two people already in prison
over the related Tobera case. [@2] Investigators also seized 20 kilos of liquid
cocaine hidden in tropical-juice cans from a shipment the group expected from
Cuba via Madrid to Seville. [@3] The submitted account identifies that final
cocaine seizure as the last step before the arrests of the remaining members of
the gang distributing hashish and cocaine. [@1]
