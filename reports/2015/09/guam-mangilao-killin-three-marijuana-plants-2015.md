A Mangilao man who police said was growing marijuana was arrested and admitted
owning the plant but denied ever using the drug. [@1] Steve Killin reportedly
told police he was "just cultivating the plants" because he liked gardening.
[@1] Killin, 30, was charged Thursday with manufacture of a Schedule I
controlled substance as a first-degree felony and possession of a Schedule I
controlled substance as a petty misdemeanor. [@1]

Tuesday evening, police arrived at a home in Mangilao looking for Killin, who
was noted to be a suspect in a separate investigation. [@1] When they found him,
police allegedly found three marijuana plants outside the home. [@1] When
officers confronted him, Killin allegedly said he "owned and cultivated the
plants" but did not use the drug himself, court documents state. [@1] Documents
state he "was just cultivating the plants because he was a general enthusiast of
botany and gardening." [@1] He also reportedly told officers his friends
occasionally took pieces of the plants to smoke them. [@1]
