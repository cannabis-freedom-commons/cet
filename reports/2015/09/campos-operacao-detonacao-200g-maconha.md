Operação Detonação led to 13 arrests, with a court issuing 14 arrest warrants of
which 12 were carried out and one person was caught in the act. [@1] The
warrants targeted suspects of robbing automated teller machines in Campos, São
Francisco de Itabapoana, São João da Barra and Quissamã. [@1] At the Jóquei Club
on Rua José Bonaparte in Campos, two men were arrested. [@1] G1 reported that of
14 arrest warrants issued by the court, 11 were carried out and one person was
arrested in flagrante. [@2]

At that location police seized 200 grams of maconha, along with 10 rounds of
.380 and three of .38 calibre ammunition. [@1] The operation as a whole also
seized a large quantity of cocaine, many weapons, three luxury cars and four
motorcycles. [@1] Delegado Geraldo Rangel said it was clear the gang also worked
in drug trafficking. [@1] G1 reported that 11 weapons and explosive material
intended for the crimes were seized in addition to drugs. [@2] A G1 report said
arms, ammunition and drugs were seized during the searches. [@3]

The operation mobilised the civil, military and federal highway police together
with the state public prosecutor's office. [@1] G1 reported that 130 military,
civil and federal highway police took part. [@2] The investigation was opened
after an automated teller machine was blown up in the Goitacazes district. [@2]
Delegado Geraldo Rangel said the involvement of a military police officer with
the gang was under investigation. [@1]
