Police raided a former asparagus farm on the Kantonsweg at Neeroeteren, near
Maaseik, on Wednesday 30 September 2015 and found a professional cannabis
plantation and a working drug laboratory. [@1] Seven people were arrested,
including the owner of the asparagus farm. [@1] The Limburg prosecutor's office
said the plantation and the laboratory were of large scale. [@1] VRT NWS placed
the farm in Dilsen-Stokkem and reported that the police found the plantation and
the laboratory there the previous evening. [@2]

Officers seized 9,500 cannabis cuttings and 500 mother plants, about 10,000
plants in all. [@1] The Limburg prosecutor's office put the number of cannabis
plants seized at about 10,000. [@2] The laboratory was fully operational for the
production of synthetic drugs. [@1] One of the seven suspects was injured during
his arrest on Wednesday. [@1]

The Limburg prosecutor's office decided to bring the six suspects and the
50-year-old woman before the investigating judge in Tongeren, who would decide
who was to be held in custody. [@1] The local police of Maasland had started the
investigation into the large cannabis plantation. [@1] The investigation was
then transferred to the federal judicial police of Limburg. [@1] Researchers
from the National Institute of Criminalistics and Criminology came to the site
for an extensive investigation. [@1] Civil protection officers dismantled the
plantation and the laboratory and removed all the materials, products and
chemicals. [@1] Several vehicles were seized and towed away. [@1]
