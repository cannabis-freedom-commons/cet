On Tuesday 2 June, several women were detained at the Baie-Mahault prison
centre. [@1] The women had come to visit prisoners. [@1] In total, nine women
were detained. [@1] In total, 275 grams of cannabis herb were found. [@1]

The cannabis was concealed in the women’s cleavage. [@1] The women were carrying
herb on their persons. [@1] About fifteen soldiers and a dog handler checked the
visiting room to prevent illegal drug imports into the prison. [@1] A dog
alerted officers by scenting the women’s cleavage. [@1]
