Police in Blantyre arrested a 28-year-old woman after finding five bags of
cannabis sativa, known locally as chamba, in her house. [@1] She was arrested on
Wednesday 3 June 2015 at her house in Machinjiri Area 10 in Blantyre. [@1] She
was being held at Blantyre Police Station. [@1] The woman comes from Chiradzulu
district. [@1]

Officers acted on a tip-off that the woman was keeping the drug at the house and
found the five bags and three plastic carriers containing twists of cannabis,
according to Blantyre Police spokesperson Elizabeth Divala. [@1] Divala said the
bags were found in her house and that the woman had contravened Regulation 4(a)
as read with Section 19 of the Dangerous Drugs Act. [@1]

The arrest followed calls in Parliament by Malawi Congress Party MP for Ntchisi
North Boniface Kadzamira for marijuana to be legalised. [@1] Kadzamira told
Parliament that chamba could be a viable alternative that could help drive
foreign exchange into Malawi. [@1]
