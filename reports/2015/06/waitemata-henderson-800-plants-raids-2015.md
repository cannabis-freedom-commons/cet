Forty-six Waitemata police officers executed ten search warrants across Auckland
on the morning of 16 June 2015, targeting large-scale cannabis production and
supply. [@1] At an industrial unit on View Road, Henderson, they found a
commercial growing operation of 800 plants, 600 of them mature, with rooms for
each growth stage including a cloning nursery, two shipping containers converted
to grow rooms, and a shredder for stalks. [@1] Officers also opened two fish
bins holding thousands of dollars of cannabis head. [@1]

Across town in Beach Haven, a further 246 plants were found growing in a house
basement, while police also searched a yacht at Westhaven Marina, a commercial
unit in Albany and homes in Sunnyvale, Massey and central Auckland. [@1] Eight
people were arrested and all were expected to face supply or cultivation
charges; police said buyers visited one address alone more than a hundred times
a day. [@1] The next day police confirmed all eight had been charged with
cultivation and supply offences, two also with theft of power, and listed their
North Shore and Waitakere court dates. [@2]
