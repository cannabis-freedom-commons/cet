Gendarmes in Raiatea, one of the Society Islands in French Polynesia, seized 190
cannabis plants in the commune of Taputapuatea on the morning of 3 June 2015.
[@1] Paka is the local name for cannabis, and the authorities described the
seizure as a continuation of their fight against the drug on Raiatea. [@1] The
suspected offender, a man in his thirties, was identified. [@1]

The operation that led to the seizure was carried out through close cooperation
between the Raiatea gendarmerie, France's military-status rural police force,
and the municipal police of Taputapuatea. [@1] In the week before the 3 June
seizure, law enforcement officers had dismantled three cannabis plantations in
the area. [@1] In total, 602 paka plants were uprooted and then destroyed in the
commune of Taputapuatea alone. [@1]
