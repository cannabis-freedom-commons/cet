Interior Ministry officers detained members of a criminal group that distributed
drugs in Krasnoyarsk. [@1] Those detained were a 63-year-old local man who leads
the Roma diaspora and a 45-year-old man born in Tajikistan. [@1] Officers found
and seized about 47 kg of heroin and 18 kg of hashish in the detainees' car.
[@1] The Krasnoyarsk region police press service said the arrests had been made
the day before its 21 April 2015 statement. [@3] The detention was carried out
by the Interior Ministry's Main Criminal Investigation Directorate with
Krasnoyarsk investigators and armed support from SOBR officers. [@2]

Police suspect the two were part of an organised group running a channel to
supply drugs to Krasnoyarsk region. [@2] A criminal case was opened over illegal
circulation of narcotic drugs on an especially large scale. [@1] The police
press centre said the detainees' accomplices were still being identified. [@2]
Officers were working to establish how the heroin and hashish had been brought
into the region. [@4]

The Interior Ministry's press centre said the seized drugs had an average
black-market value of about 40 million roubles. [@1]
