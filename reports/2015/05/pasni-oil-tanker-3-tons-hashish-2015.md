Pakistan's Coast Guard seized three tonnes of hashish bound for the Middle East in the southwestern coastal town of Pasni in Balochistan province, in one of its biggest drug hauls. [@1,3] The naval force raided an oil tanker in which the hashish had been hidden in secret chambers, and took custody of two motor vehicles along with the tanker. [@1]

The raiding party had to cut into the tanker with power tools to retrieve the 3,010 kg of drugs. [@1] Guardsmen acted on a tip after the tanker set sail from the port of Karachi, and arrested three crew members from Balochistan province. [@2] One of the men arrested was high on the list of the country's most wanted smugglers, though the force declined to give the suspects' names. [@1]

Spokesman Major Zafar Ahmed said the haul was the biggest ever scored in the history of the Coast Guard, worth about $50 million in the Middle Eastern countries where it was bound. [@1,2] Pakistan has been a key smuggling route for hashish and heroin cultivated and processed in its tribal regions and neighbouring Afghanistan. [@1]
