A Federal High Court sitting in Lagos sentenced Sule Ibrahim, of Mopo Alayo village in Okun Ajah, to life imprisonment on 20 October 2015 for dealing in 11.8 kilograms of cannabis sativa. [@1,2] The charge said he committed the offence on 9 November 2012, and after an initial not-guilty plea in March 2013 he changed his plea to guilty in April 2014. [@1]

At the July 2015 review of facts, the NDLEA prosecutor tendered Ibrahim's confessional statement, a forensic report confirming the substance was cannabis, and the bulk 11.8 kilograms itself. [@1] His counsel urged mercy as a first offender, but the judge imposed the maximum life term. [@1]

Ibrahim was one of 149 drug dealers convicted in Lagos in 2015 on NDLEA arrests, and one of three given life sentences that year. [@2] The NDLEA's Lagos command said it seized 7,345.87 kilograms of mostly cannabis and arrested 531 suspects over the same year. [@2]
