Criminal charges were filed on 22 October 2015 against 18 people, among them
Duško Martinović, Dejan Jablan, Igor Vušurović and Jovo Martinović. [@1] Ten of
them were arrested that morning – four in Podgorica, four in Budva, one in Kotor
and one in Cetinje. [@1] Four of those prosecuted were already held at the
Institute for Enforcement of Criminal Sanctions, while arrest warrants were to
be issued for two believed to be outside Montenegro. [@1] The raids took place
in several Montenegrin cities, including Podgorica and Budva, and inside the
Spuž prison. [@2] Police spokesman Dejan Knežević said the arrests followed
searches of 11 premises. [@2]

Police in Montenegro and Croatia seized 21 kg of marijuana in Montenegro and 0.5
kg of hashish in Croatia, alongside cocaine, heroin, ecstasy and amphetamines.
[@1] Knežević described the cannabis as skank, a potent cannabis strain, and
gave the same seizure quantities for both countries. [@2] He said the joint
operation was codenamed Mak in Montenegro and Hunt in Croatia. [@2] Thirteen of
the accused faced charges of drug production, possession and distribution plus
creating a criminal organisation, four the drug charges alone and one the
organisation charge. [@1]

The charges were filed on the order of the Special Public Prosecutor. [@1]
Police named Duško Martinović as the organiser of the group and noted his 2005
conviction in France for an armed robbery by the Pink Panther gang. [@1] Those
arrested were to be brought before the competent prosecutor within the legal
deadline. [@1]
