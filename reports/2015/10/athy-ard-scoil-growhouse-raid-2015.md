Gardaí from the Kildare Drugs and Crime Unit searched a premises in the Ard Scoil area of Athy shortly after 11pm on Monday 5 October 2015 and found it to be a fully functioning grow house. [@1] Cannabis plants and herb were seized, with an estimated value of €700,000 pending analysis, along with cultivation paraphernalia. [@1,3]

A 43-year-old man was arrested in the Athy area early the next morning and detained at Kildare Town Garda Station. [@1,2] He was charged on 7 October and was due before Naas District Court. [@3]
