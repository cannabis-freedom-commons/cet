Two men were convicted and sentenced at the Nkhunga First Grade Magistrate Court
on Thursday 29 October 2015 for being found in possession of cannabis sativa at
the Kaombe roadblock in Nkhotakota. [@1] They were Gilbert Sichali, 32, and John
Gunde. [@1] Sichali and Gunde admitted the charge. [@1] Magistrate Buleya
ordered Sichali to pay a K350,000 fine or, in default, serve three years'
imprisonment with hard labour. [@1] Gunde was ordered to pay K80,000 or, in
default, serve one year and eight months' imprisonment with hard labour. [@1]
Sichali and Gunde had not paid their fines when the report was published and
were being held at Nkhotakota prison. [@1]

Police found Sichali with 365 kilograms and Gunde with 170 kilograms of cannabis
sativa, a total of 535 kilograms. [@1] Sichali was arrested on 16 October 2015
and Gunde on 22 October 2015, with the drug concealed in cartons and woven
baskets. [@1] A Malawi24 report of 14 October 2015 said police in Nkhotakota had
arrested a 35-year-old man the previous day for allegedly possessing 65
kilograms of Indian hemp. [@2] The report named the man as John Gunde and said
he was arrested at the Kaombe roadblock after police searched the bus he was
travelling in. [@2] The hemp was hidden in a woven basket used to carry tomatoes
and in a green sack bag. [@2]

Sichali, 32, comes from Kamtekete village in Senior Chief Kanyenda's area of
Nkhotakota district, while Gunde comes from Chibweya village in Traditional
Authority Mpando's area in Ntcheu district. [@1] State prosecutor Sub-Inspector
James Ngupasye Muyira told the court that Indian hemp cases are rampant in the
district. [@1]
