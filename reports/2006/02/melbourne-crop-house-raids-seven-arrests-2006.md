Victoria Police raided hydroponics shops in Melton and Werribee, houses across
Melbourne's western and northern suburbs and a farm near Bacchus Marsh on
Thursday 9 February 2006, arresting seven people in a crackdown on hydroponic
cannabis crop houses. [@1,2] Three of the men appeared in the Melbourne
Magistrates Court the next day. [@1]

A 50-year-old man arrested at the farm was remanded in custody charged with
cultivating and trafficking commercial quantities of cannabis after police
found 102 plants growing hydroponically. [@1] A 42-year-old man arrested at
the Melton shop was released on bail after being charged with trafficking
commercial quantities of cannabis, ecstasy and cocaine; police said they
seized half a kilogram of cannabis worth $3,000 alongside about 100 grams of
cocaine and 50 ecstasy tablets. [@1] A 24-year-old man was bailed on an
ecstasy trafficking charge and four other men were to be charged on summons.
[@1]

The raids, led by Acting Detective Senior Sergeant Jason Kelly, ended a
six-month operation by the cannabis unit of the Major Drug Investigation
Division, and police said more arrests would follow. [@1,2] Police said they
had arrested 51 people over the previous year and seized 2,086 plants and
about 130 kilograms of cannabis head worth more than $6 million in 2005. [@1]
