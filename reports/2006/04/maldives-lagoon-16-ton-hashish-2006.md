Lobster fishermen diving in a Maldives lagoon found hashish sunk in about 10
metres of water in waterproof one-kilo packages and contacted the
authorities. [@3] Maldives Police seized the 1.6-ton haul on 22 April 2006 in
territorial waters about two hours north-east of Malé, with the Coast Guard
and security agencies taking custody. [@3] The Haveeru news agency reported
the find the next day, saying the fishermen had found the hashish packed in
1,697 plastic bags on the ocean bed, about 1,700 kilos in total. [@1]

Police, customs and defence officials confirmed it as the country's biggest
illegal drug find. [@1] The United States Embassy cable called it by far the
largest seizure ever reported in the Maldives, far beyond local consumption
and a possible early sign of transshipment through the islands. [@3]
Maldives Police and Customs investigated and asked the United States Drug
Enforcement Administration for help. [@3]

## Aftermath

No arrests were made, and police destroyed the stash two years later. [@2]
A 2018 retrospective places the lagoon off an island in Alif Alif Atoll,
while the 2006 cable puts the find north-east of the capital. [@2,3]
