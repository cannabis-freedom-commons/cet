The year-long Project Woolwich investigation targeted a network that rerouted cannabis grown under Health Canada's medical regime into the illegal market. [@1,2,3] Police across the country worked the case with United States border officials. [@1] On 13 August 2020 officers executed 26 raids throughout the Greater Toronto Area and Niagara Region, in Leamington and in British Columbia, arresting eight people. [@1,3,4] They seized 101,049 plants and 2.5 million Canadian dollars. [@1]

Police listed 1,921 pounds of cannabis bud (about 871 kilograms), 21 pounds of shatter, 22 pounds of oil and 3 pounds of hash, plus hundreds of vape pens. [@1] They also took two ounces of cocaine, six firearms, four vehicles, grow equipment worth more than $1 million and large cash sums in Canadian, US, Chinese and South Korean currencies; only the cannabis counts toward this report's figures. [@1,3]

## Cross-border shipments and charges

Investigators said the group moved large volumes of illegal cannabis from British Columbia to Ontario and seized shipments bound for the United States alongside large shipments of United States currency headed for Canada. [@1,2] In all, 15 people were charged with 135 offences under the Cannabis Act, the Controlled Drugs and Substances Act and the Criminal Code. [@2] Detectives described the accused as a sophisticated criminal enterprise rather than members of a known organised-crime group. [@1,3]

OPP Inspector Jim Walker said the network spanned from British Columbia into Ontario and into the United States. [@4] He said criminal groups were exploiting Health Canada's personal and designated production registrations, which allow up to 400 plants per licence and four licences at one address, to hide large-scale farms behind paperwork. [@3,4] Neighbours near the raided Northend Gardens greenhouse at 4030 Fifteenth Street in Jordan Station welcomed the raid after months of odour complaints, and Lincoln town officials said they were preparing a public-nuisance by-law against rule-breaking growers. [@4]
