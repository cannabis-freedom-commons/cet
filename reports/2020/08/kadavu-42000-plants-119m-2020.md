Police uprooted marijuana plants on Kadavu over the two months from 22 June to
Saturday 22 August 2020. [@1] Police Chief of Operations ACP Abdul Khan said
42,000 plants had been uprooted so far. [@1] The plants taken in that period
were valued at more than $119 million. [@1] More raids were continuing, police
said. [@1]

Khan said police were also monitoring other areas, including Navosa, Ra, Ba and
Cakaudrove. [@1] He said there were indications that people had started to move
to those areas again to plant marijuana. [@1]
