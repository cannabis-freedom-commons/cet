A land patrol of the Prefectura Naval Argentina, Argentina's coast guard,
watched a man cross in a boat from the Paraguayan bank of the Paraná River to
the Argentine bank at kilometre 1,726 with a bag on board. [@1] When the
officers reached the landing point on the coast at Puerto Rico, Misiones, they
found the bag abandoned on the riverbank. [@1]

Inside the bag officers found 32 pressed bricks of marijuana weighing more than
17 kilograms. [@1] The force said the load was intended to be brought into the
country through the coast of the Misiones city of Puerto Rico. [@1] The
Prefectura valued the seized marijuana at more than 1,200,000 pesos. [@1]

The Federal Prosecutor's Office in Oberá, headed by Viviana Vallejos with legal
secretary Arlindo Otto Kurtz, took charge of the case. [@1] The crossing linked
the Paraguayan and Argentine banks of the Paraná at Puerto Rico, so the case was
treated as a cross-border river smuggling attempt. [@1]
