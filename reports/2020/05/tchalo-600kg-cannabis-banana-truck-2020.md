The driver of a banana-carrying minibus escaped from Togolese customs agents at
Tchalo, using a traffic jam to slip away while they checked his vehicle. [@1]
Nabiyou Hayimdètè, head of the division for combating fraud and illicit
trafficking, said officers had stopped the minibus, which was making a journey
from the south towards the north. [@1]

Customs officers seized 600 kilograms of cannabis from the vehicle, counted as
1,042 loaves. [@1] The check showed that, alongside the banana load, the minibus
carried bags containing the cannabis. [@1] The operation took place on the night
of 17 to 18 May 2020. [@1] The Office Togolais des Recettes, Togo's revenue
office, revealed the seizure to the press on Wednesday. [@1] Tchalo is a
locality in the Centrale Region of Togo. [@1]

Cannabis, also called hemp, is a plant originating in equatorial regions whose
consumption can aggravate some chronic illnesses. [@1] In Togo, the production,
trade and distribution of cannabis are prohibited. [@1]
