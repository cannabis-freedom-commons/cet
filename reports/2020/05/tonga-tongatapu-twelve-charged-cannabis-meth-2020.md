Tonga Police arrested 12 people in a series of drug raids on Tongatapu. [@1] All
12 were charged with methamphetamine and cannabis-related offences. [@1] Nine of
them were men and three were women, aged between 20 and 41, and they were due to
appear in court. [@1]

Police seized 273 grams of cannabis in the raids. [@1] Officers also seized 20
grams of methamphetamine and a range of drug utensils. [@1] Acting commissioner
Tevita Vailea described the seizure as significant, with the potential to cause
serious harm in the community. [@1]

The drugs were seized in the week before 11 May 2020, when the arrests and
charges were announced. [@1]
