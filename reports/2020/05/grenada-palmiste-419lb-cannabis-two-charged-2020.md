Royal Grenada Police Force officers recovered 419 pounds of compressed cannabis,
about 190 kg, and seized a fishing vessel. [@1] The cannabis was given an
estimated street value of EC$950,292. [@1] Two men were arrested and charged
over the haul: Kenton Roberts, 32, a mason of Grenville, St Andrew, and Rennie
Cruickshank, 39, of Ashton, Union Island in St Vincent and the Grenadines. [@2]

The drug suspects were arrested in an early morning police operation on Saturday
at Palmiste, St John. [@1] Officers of the Drug Squad and the Gouyave police
station acted on intelligence, saw a boat in the water and found bags of
compressed cannabis while searching the beach. [@3] The boat was left in the
possession of the state. [@3]

Both men appeared at the Gouyave Magistrate's Court on Monday 18 May 2020,
jointly charged with trafficking a controlled drug. [@1] Roberts was granted
EC$200,000 bail with two sureties and ordered to surrender all travel documents
and report to the Gouyave police station every Monday, Wednesday and Friday.
[@1] Cruickshank, a St Vincent national, was remanded to the Richmond Hill
Prison. [@1] NOW Grenada reported that Cruickshank was described as a diver by
profession and was deemed a flight risk. [@3] The two men were due back in court
on 5 June 2020. [@1] Police said their investigations were continuing. [@2]
