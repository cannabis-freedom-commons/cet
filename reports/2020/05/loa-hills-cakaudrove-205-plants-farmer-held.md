Police officers based at Tukavesi conducted a raid in the interior of the Loa
Hills in Cakaudrove in Fiji's Northern Division. [@1] Acting on a tip-off, the
raiding team targeted a farm that allegedly belonged to a 47-year-old farmer.
[@1] The suspect from the Loa Hills farm remained in police custody after the
plants were seized. [@1]

During the search of the farm, officers uprooted 205 plants believed to be
marijuana. [@1] Police described the Loa Hills operation as the latest in
continuing drug seizures and arrests in the Northern Division. [@2] Police said
the latest raid alone resulted in the discovery of more than 200 plants believed
to be marijuana. [@2] Police said the uprooted material will be sent for
analysis. [@1]

Police said they were encouraged by the show of public support for ongoing
efforts to curb the illegal drug trade. [@2] Police said information continues
to pour in about the involvement of suspected individuals in the drug trade.
[@1] Police released a photograph showing plants believed to be marijuana
uprooted after the Cakaudrove raid. [@1]
