National Guard members in Mexico destroyed five opium-poppy plantations and one
field of marijuana by incineration in Ímuris, a town in the north-centre of
Sonora state, after following up an anonymous complaint. [@1] The incineration
was the second operation the National Guard had conducted in seven days and
concluded with the destruction of extensive areas used to cultivate drug crops.
[@1]

The force's report said the plants destroyed in this operation covered the
equivalent of 54,000 square metres, about 5.4 hectares, a combined figure for
the poppy and marijuana fields. [@1] Officers also secured seeds for cultivating
the drug, which the account calls an enervante, the Spanish term for a narcotic
crop. [@1]

A week earlier, in the same seven-day stretch, National Guard and army personnel
had destroyed two poppy fields and one marijuana field in Ímuris and in the
municipality of Trincheras during joint investigative work. [@1]
