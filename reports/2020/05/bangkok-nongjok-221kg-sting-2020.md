Police seized bricks of dried marijuana weighing 221 kg at a house in Lam Toi Ting subdistrict in Bangkok's Nong Chok district on Friday evening. [@1,2] The raid followed a sting operation earlier that day in which 46-year-old Manop Buakareem delivered 1 kg of marijuana to undercover police on Suwintawong Road and was arrested. [@1,2] Officers of the Lam Phak She police station then raided the house where the stock was stored, tying the 221 kg hoard directly to the street arrest made hours earlier. [@1,2]

Manop told police he stored the drugs for another man known only as Naf, who was supposed to collect them and pay him a storing fee. [@1,2] He was charged with possessing a category-5 narcotic substance, marijuana, with the intention to sell and was taken into custody. [@1] Police displayed the accused, pictured with a blurred face, alongside the confiscated drugs at the station on Friday evening. [@1] The seizure was announced on Saturday. [@1,2]
