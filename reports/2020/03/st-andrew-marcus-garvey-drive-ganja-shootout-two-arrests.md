A police team working in the St Andrew South state of public emergency arrested
two of three men who tried to pass through a vehicular checkpoint with hundreds
of pounds of ganja. [@1] One of those arrested was treated at hospital for
gunshot wounds and released into police custody. [@1] The Gleaner reported that
the injured man was taken to hospital, treated and released into police custody.
[@2]

About 5:30 am, officers on a checkpoint operation along Marcus Garvey Drive saw
two motor cars that aroused their suspicion. [@1] The officers reportedly
signalled the drivers of both motorcars to stop. [@1] The occupants of the
vehicles instead opened gunfire at the police team. [@1] The gunfire was
returned, the police said, and two of the men were arrested, while a third
escaped. [@1] A subsequent search of the cars, a Nissan Latio and a Toyota
Probox, revealed approximately 500 pounds (about 226.8 kg) of compressed ganja.
[@1]

The police Corporate Communication Unit said officers signalled two vehicles to
stop along Marcus Garvey Drive. [@2] The unit said the vehicles did not comply
and officers were fired on. [@2] The unit said police searched the cars and
found a large quantity of compressed ganja in one of them. [@2] The Gleaner
placed the incident at the intersection with Fourth Street around 5:45 a.m. and
reported hours of traffic congestion while investigators processed the scene.
[@2] The Gleaner said a third man believed to have been in the other car
escaped. [@2]
