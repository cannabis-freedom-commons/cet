Inoke Siva of Kadavu was sentenced to seven years in prison for possessing 15
kilograms of marijuana. [@1] Siva was 49 years old. [@2] He is a subsistence
farmer and the sale of the cannabis was meant to supplement his income. [@1]
Justice Salesi Temo handed down the sentence at the High Court in Suva on Monday
9 March 2020. [@2] Siva was charged and convicted on one count of possession of
illicit drugs. [@1]

The court found that on 20 July 2018, at Suva, he possessed 15 kg of cannabis
sativa without lawful authority. [@1] Siva brought the cannabis to Suva in a
white and red striped bag to sell in the capital. [@2] Police in Suva had been
forewarned about his activities and arrested him on arrival, discovering the
drugs he had brought. [@1] The 15 kg of cannabis sativa was tendered as evidence
in his trial. [@2]

Justice Temo ordered that the drugs be destroyed by the Chief Registrar's Office
with the help of police as soon as possible. [@1] He also ordered that the drugs
be photographed before destruction, with the photographs kept as part of the
record. [@1] Siva was given 30 days to appeal to the Court of Appeal. [@1]
