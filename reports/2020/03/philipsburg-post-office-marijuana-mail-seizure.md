St. Maarten Customs seized an undisclosed quantity of cocaine and marijuana
hidden in two packages at the Post Office in Philipsburg on Tuesday, March 10.
[@1]

The marijuana package had arrived in St. Maarten from Canada. [@1] The marijuana
was hidden inside a can of laxative powder. [@1] In the same check, cocaine was
concealed in the hollowed-out portion of a book intended to be sent to the
Netherlands. [@1]

Customs confiscated the drugs. [@1] Customs said the confiscated drugs will be
destroyed. [@1] In a Facebook post on Tuesday night, Customs said that besides
routine controls at the airport and the port, it also regularly carries out
checks at the Post Office and other places. [@1]
