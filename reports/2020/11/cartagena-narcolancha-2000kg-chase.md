A National Police helicopter spotted the 14-metre narcolancha, fitted with two 300 hp engines, sailing about 80 miles off Cartagena with bales of drugs visible on board. [@1,3] A Tax Agency Customs Surveillance patrol boat gave chase for more than five hours over 100 nautical miles as the five crew threw the bales overboard to get rid of them. [@1,2,3] The helicopter crew filmed masked smugglers photographing and waving at the officers during the pursuit. [@3]

## Boarded and seized

Joint action on the high seas recovered the jettisoned bales and boarded the speedboat, arresting all five occupants. [@1] Officers seized 66 bales totalling 2,000 kilos of hashish, plus the boat, satellite phones and computer equipment. [@1,2,3] Police said the gang had picked the load up in Morocco and was heading for the beaches of Valencia to unload it. [@3]
