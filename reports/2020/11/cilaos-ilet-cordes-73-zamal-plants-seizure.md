On Saturday 28 November 2020, a Cilaos gendarmerie patrol found a zamal plant on
land at the place called Terre Fine in Ilet à Cordes. [@1] The woman who owned
the land became angry and pulled up the plant found by the officers herself.
[@1] Officers quickly linked her to a man who had hurriedly left the spot just
before they arrived. [@1] The man turned out to be the landowner's partner and
came back to the scene a short time later. [@1] The man was placed in custody
for questioning. [@1]

Zamal is the name used on Réunion for the cannabis plant. [@1] The first plant
led officers to several other plants growing on the same land. [@1] A search at
the man's home found eight more plants and a bag of dry branches. [@1] Officers
seized 73 zamal plants in total. [@1]

The man had to answer questions in custody while gendarmes said inquiries were
continuing. [@1] Gendarmes said further patrols were already planned in the
sector that week. [@1]
