More than three tonnes of pressed marijuana were seized in two National
Gendarmerie operations in Misiones province in late November 2020. [@1] Officers
of Escuadrón 10 'Eldorado' raided a house in Colonia Delicia on the night of 28
November. [@2] They found 68 bundles holding 2,037 packets of Cannabis sativa
with a total weight of 1,603 kg. [@1] Eldorado federal judge Miguel Ángel
Guerrero ordered the raid and federal prosecutor Liliam Edith Delgado took part.
[@1]

A second operation by the Gendarmerie's Agrupación 4 'Misiones' seized almost
1,700 kg of marijuana. [@1] It followed seven days of fieldwork and an intense
search through dense vegetation in Colonia Luján, where officers found 70
bundles of plant material shaped like bricks. [@1] Narcotests confirmed the
material was marijuana and the judge ordered it seized and the exact location of
the find recorded. [@1]

A later Gendarmerie account put the combined haul from the two operations at
3,290 kg. [@3] It reported 1,693 kg weighed from the vegetation in Colonia
Luján. [@3] It reported 1,603 kg 402 g taken from the house at Delicia 4. [@3]
The house at Delicia 4 had no residents when officers entered. [@3]
