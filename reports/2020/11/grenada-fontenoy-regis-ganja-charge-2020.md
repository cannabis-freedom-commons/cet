Chris 'Santa' Regis, a former national swimmer, was chased and shot by police in
a ganja-related incident in the village of Fontenoy in St George. [@2] Regis was
shot on 21 October 2020 after police saw him smoking a ganja spliff. [@1] A
relative told The New Today that a police party visited him in hospital and told
him he had been charged with possession of ganja. [@1] The relative said three
officers, two men and a woman, came with a plastic bag allegedly holding ganja
and a bail form for him to sign. [@1] The officers said the bag held the ganja
found at his home on the day of the incident. [@1] On a lawyer's advice, he
signed only the bail form, not the bag. [@1]

The New Today said it got no response from Superintendent Vannie Curwen, head of
the force's community relations department, on whether police had visited Regis
in hospital and charged him. [@1] The relative said Regis was not told the
amount of the bail or the date of the court hearing. [@1] Curwen said no
instructions had been given to anyone in the force to visit the injured man in
hospital, and promised a full investigation into the allegation. [@1] The
Grenadian Voice said on 5 November 2020 that it was still not clear whether
Regis had been charged. [@3]

Days later, Regis's father, Trevor Regis, attacked three police officers and was
shot dead. [@1] A relative said the hospital visit and the charge were what
prompted the father to attack the police. [@1] The New Today later reported that
police visited Regis in hospital as part of a criminal investigation against him
over the single joint. [@2]
