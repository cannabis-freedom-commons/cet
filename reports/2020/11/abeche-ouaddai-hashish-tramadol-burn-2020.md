Ouaddaï governor General Brahim Seid Mahamat burned seized drugs on Saturday
morning at the southern exit of Abéché on the N'Djamena-Oum Hadjer road. [@1]
Accompanied by close aides, he carried out the incineration of drugs seized by
the Ouaddaï defence and security forces. [@1] The burning took place in the
presence of the public prosecutor, the mayor of Abéché and other civilian and
military figures. [@1]

The destroyed lot was described as a significant quantity of tramadol and
hashish. [@1] Abéché appeals-court prosecutor Hassan Djamous Hachim recalled Law
No. 22 of 28 September 1995 authorising the incineration of drugs. [@1] He said
the rules require prohibited products to be destroyed by incineration. [@1] He
said the products are incinerated so people cannot consume or sell them. [@1]

Governor Mahamat said consumption of the drugs endangers the population and
especially young people, whom he called the spearhead of the country. [@1] He
congratulated the defence and security forces for work that led to the seizure
of the large quantity of drugs. [@1] He said officers spare no effort to track
drug traffickers, whom he personally considers terrorists who must be fought
with full energy. [@1]
