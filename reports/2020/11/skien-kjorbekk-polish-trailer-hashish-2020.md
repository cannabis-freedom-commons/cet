Police arrested a Norwegian and a Lithuanian man at a warehouse at Kjørbekk in
Skien on Saturday 21 November 2020. [@1] Between 700 and 800 kilograms of
hashish were found in a Polish-registered tractor with a trailer, which the
Southeast Police District called one of the largest single hashish seizures
in Norway. [@1] Police put the probable turnover value at about 140 million
kroner and said the drugs were likely intended for much of the country. [@1]

A third Norwegian man was arrested afterwards, and police said all the men
faced serious drug crime allegations, with more arrests possible. [@1] One
police hypothesis was that the hashish had arrived with the Polish trailer.
