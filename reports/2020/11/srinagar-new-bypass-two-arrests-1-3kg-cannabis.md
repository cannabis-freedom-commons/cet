Srinagar police set up a naka checkpoint at New Bypass near Peaks automobiles in
Srinagar after receiving a specific input. [@2] Officers at the checkpoint
stopped a vehicle bearing registration number JK06-4852 with two men on board.
[@1] The two men were identified as Imran Ahmad Shah and Bilal Ahmad Shah, both
residents of Chandergee in Kulgam. [@1]

During the search, officers recovered 1.3 kg of cannabis from the men's
possession. [@1] The cannabis was a charas-like substance wrapped in corn husks.
[@2] Both men were arrested on the spot and shifted to Police Station Nowgam,
where they remained in custody. [@1] Officers also seized the vehicle used in
the commission of the offence. [@1]

Police registered FIR No. 148/2020 under sections 8 and 20 of the NDPS Act at
Police Station Nowgam. [@2] The investigation was continuing in order to trace
further culprits. [@2] Local residents welcomed the police action and expected
similar action against the drug menace to continue. [@2]
