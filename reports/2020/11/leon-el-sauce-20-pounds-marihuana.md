Policía Nacional officers stopped a black Pulsar motorcycle in comarca Agua
Fría, El Sauce municipality, León department, at about 8 a.m. on Wednesday 25
November 2020 and detained its 23-year-old rider, a man surnamed Martínez
Zepeda. [@1] Officers found a sack containing 20 packets ('óvulos') of green
herb wrapped in adhesive tape. [@1]

A field test on the herb was positive for marihuana and police weighed the
seizure at 20 pounds (about 9.07 kg). [@1] Commissioner Fidel Domínguez said the
stop resulted from the 'muro de contención' strategy against drug trafficking
and organised crime. [@1] Police technical investigators examined the scene and
a chemical test of the sample was positive for marihuana. [@3] The evidence
comprised the detainee, the drug and the motorcycle. [@1]

TN8 said a complaint would be filed in the department's judicial complex in the
coming hours to start proceedings against him. [@1] Canal 2 reported that the
man and the seized items would be placed at the disposal of the Ministerio
Público, Nicaragua's public prosecution service. [@3] La Nueva Radio YA quoted
the León police command as saying the suspect and evidence had been placed at
the disposal of the competent authorities for prosecution. [@2]
