Cristian Valencia-Mendoza of Colombia was sentenced after a federal jury found
him guilty of conspiracy to possess and possession of a controlled substance
with intent to distribute on board a vessel under US jurisdiction. [@1] The
vessel's captain, Centeno A. Hansel-Andres of the Dominican Republic, had
previously pleaded guilty and was sentenced to 37 months in prison. [@1]

A U.S. Coast Guard cutter first sighted the go-fast vessel on Oct. 18, 2019,
approximately 45 nautical miles north of Malpelo Island, Columbia, and it
displayed no indication of nationality. [@1] Officers boarded the vessel, seized
21 bales of marijuana weighing approximately 1,355 pounds (about 614.6 kg) and
arrested the two individuals on board. [@1] Valencia-Mendoza confessed he had
been hired to assist the boat captain in delivering the drugs. [@1] A federal
jury returned guilty verdicts on Jan. 22. [@1]

U.S. District Court Judge Robert A. Molloy sentenced Valencia-Mendoza to 33
months’ imprisonment followed by two years of supervised release. [@1] The case
was investigated by the United States Coast Guard and the Drug Enforcement
Administration. [@2]
