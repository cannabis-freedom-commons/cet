Gendarmes seized 60 kg of cannabis in Loum, in Cameroon's Littoral Region, on 18
November 2020. [@1] The load was found during a search of the home of a man in
his forties, carried out on the public prosecutor's order. [@1] Gendarmes
arrested the man at Loum with the 60 kg of cannabis. [@1] The gendarmerie
learned of the case after a violent fight between the suspect and his brother.
[@1] The brother was detained after being seriously injured in the fight. [@1]

Questioning of the brother led gendarmes to cannabis at his home and to his
account that he bought his supply in Loum. [@1] The cannabis came from
Balessing, in the Menoua department of the West Region. [@1] It was said to have
been grown on the suspect's own plantation, which he cultivated discreetly. [@1]

The suspect was described as part of a large organised-crime network operating
in the Littoral, North-West and South-West Regions. [@1] The gendarmerie said
the investigation was continuing in an attempt to dismantle the trafficking
network. [@1]
