A couple in their thirties was arrested at the house. [@1] Officers from the
Villeneuve-sur-Lot gendarmerie company searched a house at Boudy-de-Beauregard,
a commune near Cancon, on Thursday morning. [@1] The house is on the commune of
Boudy-de-Beauregard in the north of Lot-et-Garonne. [@1] The woman was released
later that morning, while the 27-year-old man remained in custody at the
Villeneuve-sur-Lot gendarmerie that afternoon. [@1]

Inside the property, officers found 19 cannabis plants. [@1] Officers also
seized 2 kg of herbal cannabis at the same house, described as ready for
consumption. [@1] According to initial interviews after the arrests, the large
quantity of drugs was intended for the couple's personal consumption. [@1]

The search was presented as part of an intensifying fight against narcotics in
Lot-et-Garonne. [@1] The anti-drugs operation was carried out jointly by the
Monflanquin community of brigades and the Villeneuve research brigade. [@1] Both
people were ordered to appear before the court in February to answer for the
cannabis plantation. [@1]
