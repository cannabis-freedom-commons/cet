Nicaragua's National Police presented weekly anti-delinquency results that
included five cleared homicides, three dismantled criminal gangs, six jailed
drug suppliers and 35 solved robberies with intimidation. [@1] The roundup was
reported in Managua, where police headlined the dismantling of criminal gangs
and the seizure of 99 pounds of marijuana. [@1]

Police arrested six drug suppliers and seized a total of 99 pounds of marijuana,
about 44.9 kg. [@1] In the same capital sweep, police detained 12 people for
robbery with intimidation. [@1] Police listed among the items seized in the
sweep two pistols, one revolver, one .22-calibre rifle and two homemade firearms
capable of being fired. [@1]

Police said the group La Mona committed intimidation robberies in Ciudad
Sandino. [@1] Police said five named members of the group Los Mojica would be
processed for robbery with force and intimidation. [@1] Police also reported the
capture of two men described as the authors of the homicide of Darwin Junior
Rivera Cárcamo, committed on 16 November in Villa Venezuela. [@1]
