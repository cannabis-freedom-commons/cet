Four men were jailed for a combined 25 years at Southampton Crown Court on 24
November 2020. [@2] They had pleaded guilty to conspiracy to commit burglary and
possession of a firearm with intent. [@1] Robert Johnston, 25, of Tennyson
Avenue, Southampton, was jailed for five years and four months. [@1] Fardeen
Hamidy, 26, of Bursledon Road, Southampton, was sentenced to eight years and
eight months. [@1] Christy Flaherty, 27, of Malmesbury Road, Southampton, was
jailed for eight years. [@1] Ken Mulangala, 18, of East Street, Barking, Essex,
received a three-year sentence. [@1] Flaherty had also admitted being concerned
in the supply of cocaine. [@2]

Police were called to River Walk, Southampton, after reports of gunfire early on
5 September 2019. [@1] The group went there to burgle a house being used as a
cannabis factory. [@2] The group had decided to try to steal a large amount of
cannabis for their own gain, police said. [@2] Police later found a cannabis
factory in a residential property with more than 100 plants and a wholesale
value of more than £25,000. [@1] A large amount of cannabis was also found
abandoned in waste bags in the street. [@1] Two cars arrived quickly and a
standoff with shouting and shots fired began. [@1] The court heard the raiders
found the gun inside the cannabis factory and decided to use it to defend
themselves. [@1]

Sol Hemy, 20, was later found drowned in the nearby River Itchen. [@1] The court
heard it was still not known how he died, and the police investigation was
continuing. [@1] Judge Henry called it an "audacious" plan to steal from a rival
drugs gang. [@1] He said the defendants were "prepared to use extreme and
dangerous methods". [@1] Det Insp Andy Symes said the raid had been "terrifying"
for neighbouring residents. [@1]
