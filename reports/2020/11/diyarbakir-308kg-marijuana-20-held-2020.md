Twenty suspects were also held in countrywide anti-narcotic operations. [@1]
Turkish police seized 308 kilograms (approximately 679 pounds) of marijuana in
an anti-narcotics operation in Turkey's southeastern Diyarbakir province, the
provincial governorate said on Tuesday. [@2] Other illegal items including
pistols were also seized in the operation carried out in the countryside of the
Lice district. [@2] Two suspected drug traffickers were also held. [@2]

The raid was part of Operation Yildirim-7, which also focused on narco-terrorism
and was launched on 26 August to eliminate the PKK. [@2] Separately, 978 grams
of drugs, two pistols and 109 cartridges were seized in simultaneous operations
in five provinces, according to local police. [@2] At least 13 suspects were
detained during the raids in Erzurum, Ankara, Istanbul, Izmir and Kocaeli
provinces. [@2] Turkish gendarmerie forces also seized more than 47 grams of
powder hashish, 628 cannabis roots and other illegal items including four
pistols in Borcka district of northeastern Artvin province. [@2] Security teams
also rounded up five suspected drug traffickers during the action. [@3]

The PKK is listed as a terrorist group by Turkey, the US and the EU and was
blamed for nearly 40,000 deaths over more than 30 years. [@2]
