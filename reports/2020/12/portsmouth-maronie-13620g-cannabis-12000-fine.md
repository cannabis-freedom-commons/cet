Leon Maronie, a 26-year-old man from Portsmouth, was ordered to pay EC$12,000
after pleading guilty to possessing cannabis with intent to supply. [@1] He
appeared before Magistrate Pearl Williams, where the facts presented in court
said that on Tuesday 1 December 2020 he had 13,620 grams of cannabis with intent
to supply it to another. [@1] The facts presented in court put a street value of
EC$30,000 on the drug. [@1]

Police obtained a warrant to search a business place and executed it, and on the
compound officers saw an old dilapidated shed. [@1] As officers entered the
shed, they saw Maronie running toward an open window in an attempt to escape,
and chased and apprehended him. [@1] A search found cannabis spread out on a
green tarpaulin and hanging from a wire in the rooftop. [@1] He was arrested on
suspicion of possessing cannabis and taken with the drug to the Roseau Criminal
Investigations Department, where he was questioned. [@1] Samples of the
substance tested positive for cannabis. [@1]

The fine is to be paid in monthly instalments of EC$2,000 from 30 December 2020
until 31 May 2021, with six months' imprisonment in default. [@1]
