Two drug traffickers were arrested and placed in detention by police of Bamako's
7th arrondissement. [@1] Officers seized 10,800 briquettes of hashish, described
by police as Moroccan, from the truck. [@3] One other occupant of the truck fled
on foot and was still being sought. [@1]

The interception took place on the night of Friday 4 December 2020, at around 8
pm. [@2] A patrol of the 7th arrondissement's investigation brigade stopped a
semi-trailer truck on the slip road from the Komoguel hotel to the Sogoniko
technical inspection centre in Bamako's Commune VI. [@3] The truck was filled to
the brim with charcoal. [@3] The driver said the briquettes were bound for
northern Mali, mainly Timbuktu. [@3] Police also arrested another truck driver
waiting nearby with a second truck loaded with 20 tonnes of cement to carry the
load onward to Timbuktu. [@3]

Le360 Afrique put the value of the seizure at about 216 million CFA francs, some
330,000 euros. [@1] It described the haul as one of the largest single seizures
of this type of drug ever made by Malian security forces. [@1] The investigation
continued in an effort to identify the other accomplices. [@3] Police were still
trying to establish the exact origin and destination of the hashish. [@1]
