National Guard units in the Thala area of Kasserine Governorate seized 10 kg of
cannabis on Monday 14 December 2020 in an ambush laid for drug traffickers in
the Bouderies region, on the Tunisia–Algeria border. [@1] A highway police unit
working with the National Guard's research brigade in Thala carried out the
operation against two drug dealers that day. [@2] The two dealers were due to
carry the drugs from Algeria into Tunisia through the Bouderies border zone but
took flight. [@2]

The traffickers had moved the drugs from Algeria into Tunisia using paths
through the mountains. [@1] The prosecutor of the Kasserine court of first
instance authorised arrest warrants against the two fugitives after they escaped
the ambush in which the drugs were seized. [@1]
