Costa Rica's Public Security Ministry said the police work began on Sunday 27
December and ended on Monday 28 December 2020, when four men were detained and a
drug consignment seized after a launch and a vehicle were intercepted in
Puntarenas. [@3] The ministry said the interception took place at the entrance
to the estuary in Puntarenas province, on the central Pacific, where National
Coast Guard officers stopped a suspicious boat named Nene. [@1] When the coast
guard intercepted the launch, one of its two crew members jumped into the sea to
try to escape, and he was detained together with two other people who were in a
vehicle apparently waiting for the boat. [@1] The four detainees were three
Costa Ricans aged 37, 44 and 33 and a 33-year-old Nicaraguan, the man who tried
to flee. [@1]

The Drug Control Police (PCD) said the load was 513 packages: 507 kilograms in
packages plus six packages of 500 grams each, for a total of 510 kilograms of
marijuana. [@2] Security Minister Michael Soto said the coast guard spotted the
suspicious boat with two male crew and that, once the load was counted, it
proved to be about 510 kilograms of marijuana. [@1] Officers also seized 162,000
colones, 250 dollars and the suspicious boat with a 75-horsepower motor. [@2]

The four were to be presented to the Public Ministry as suspects of
international drug trafficking, an offence punished in Costa Rica with up to 20
years in prison. [@1] Costa Rica's anti-drug authorities consider the country a
transit and storage site for drugs, in operations in which Costa Ricans often
work with foreign groups. [@1]
