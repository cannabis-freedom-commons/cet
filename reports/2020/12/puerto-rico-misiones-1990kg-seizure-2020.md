Officers of the Prefectura Naval Argentina, Argentina's coast guard, seized a
cargo of about two tonnes of marijuana in an operation in the town of Puerto
Rico, Misiones. [@1] The force said the operation followed guidelines set by the
national Security Ministry against drug trafficking. [@1]

The seizure took place during a routine patrol at kilometre 1767 of the Paraná
River, at a spot known as Puerto Giral. [@1] Accompanied by the drug-detection
dog Rango, the officers found 25 bundles of rectangular bricks hidden in brush
400 metres from the shore and 60 more on the shore. [@1] The count gave 2,650
panes, or pressed bricks, of marijuana with a total weight of 1,990 kilograms.
[@2] The force valued the load at more than 172,614,369 pesos. [@1]

After Dr. Arlindo Otto Kurtz of the Federal Prosecutor's Office in Oberá was
informed, the drugs were seized and counted. [@1] At publication officers were
still combing the area, joined by a second dog, Fiona. [@1]
