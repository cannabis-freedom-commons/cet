Vietnamese authorities seized 665 kilograms of cannabis from a container that had been shipped to Hai Phong City, Singapore's Central Narcotics Bureau said on 29 December 2020. [@1] The container, declared as animal-feed raw materials for a Hanoi trading company, arrived on 11 December and was moved to an industrial park for repackaging, where the cannabis packages were discovered. [@1]

Early reports said the container had been shipped from Singapore, but CNB checks showed it had departed from a European country, passed through another port of call, and never passed through Singapore. [@1] CNB said it would keep working with foreign counterparts against transnational drug trafficking. [@1]
