Border troops of Uzbekistan's State Security Service detained a Tajik citizen,
Kurbonzoda Sheroz, in Bekabad district, Tashkent region, as he crossed the
Uzbek-Tajik border illegally. [@2] A search of the two sacks he was carrying
found 24 packages. [@2]

Forensic examination found 5 kg 9 g of heroin, 17 kg 259 g of opium and almost 2
kg of hashish in the packages. [@1] Only the hashish is counted as cannabis; the
heroin and opium are not. [@1]

A criminal case was opened under the relevant articles of Uzbekistan's Criminal
Code. [@3] Two local residents suspected of involvement in the crime were put on
a wanted list. [@3] No arrest of the two wanted local residents was reported.
[@3]
