People linked to the trafficking fled when army troops arrived, so no arrests
were made at the scene. [@1] Forces and means of the 1st Regional Military
Command of the Nicaraguan Army were used in the seizure. [@1]

Troops seized six sacks containing 301 packages of marijuana. [@1] The seizure
took place on 30 October 2020 during an exploratory patrol in the comarca Buena
Vista Chiquita, 7 kilometres northwest of Jalapa municipality in Nueva Segovia.
[@1]

The Army said the patrol was carried out under the State Border Protection Plan
and the Muro de Contención state strategy. [@1] The seized drug was handed over
to the corresponding authorities. [@1] The account was issued as Information
Note No. 035/2020 from the headquarters of the 1st Regional Military Command.
[@1]
