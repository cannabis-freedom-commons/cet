Three people were arrested in Carvin, Pas-de-Calais, on 15 October 2020 on
suspicion of drug trafficking. [@1] The three suspects were two men and a woman
who had been under investigation since June 2020. [@1]

Officers seized 50 kg of cannabis, herb and resin, an automatic pistol, 3,000
euros in cash, encrypted phones, a banknote counter and a vehicle. [@1] La Voix
du Nord reported the cannabis as 30 kg of herb and 20 kg of resin and identified
the pistol as a loaded Glock 26. [@2] The woman was cleared, while the two
Carvin men were charged with organised drug trafficking and possession of a
weapon and placed in pre-trial detention. [@1]

The operation came from a Lille inter-regional specialised jurisdiction (Jirs)
inquiry that began with data intercepted from the encrypted EncroChat network,
recently dismantled by French and Dutch authorities. [@1] Investigators
suspected the group of importing cocaine and cannabis from Belgium and the
Netherlands. [@1] Surveillance in mid-October suggested a fresh importation of
drugs from the Netherlands. [@1] The inquiry drew on the gendarmerie's C3N
digital-crime centre and the Ofast anti-drug office of Lille's judicial police.
[@1]
