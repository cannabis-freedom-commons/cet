Samoa police raided a location at Faleatiu village in the week before 23 October
2020 and arrested three people. [@1] The three arrested people face meth-related
charges. [@1]

Officers confiscated methamphetamine, cash, weapons, marijuana plants and a
rental car at the village. [@1] Police impounded a Moana Rentals vehicle found
at the location. [@1]

Police are treating the investigation as organised transnational criminal
activity. [@1] The commissioner said investigations are now focused on how the
methamphetamine is being brought into Samoa. [@1] He said information leads
police to believe the methamphetamine is not manufactured in Samoa and is coming
from somewhere. [@1] He stressed that border agencies must work together if the
war against drugs is to be won in Samoa and across the Pacific region. [@1]
Commissioner Keil said the high volumes of cannabis and level of activity in
recent drug raids places it in the category of transnational crime. [@1] He said
these narcotics and weapons are somehow coming into Samoa as organised
transnational crime type activity. [@1]
