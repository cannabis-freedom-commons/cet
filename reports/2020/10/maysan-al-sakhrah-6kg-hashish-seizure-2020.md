Iraq's Security Media Cell announced on October 30 the discovery of 6 kilograms
of hashish and 50 drug bars in the al-Sakhrah area in the southern Maysan
governorate. [@1] The announcement noted an increase in the drug trade and abuse
in central and southern Iraq in particular. [@1]

On October 12, the investigative court for drug cases in Maysan reported that
the southern areas of the governorate are the most active in promoting narcotic
drugs. [@1] The court said crystal meth makes up 90 percent of the substances
trafficked there. [@1] On October 29, Zuhair al-Shaalan, governor of the
southern city of Diwaniyah, said in a televised interview that the rate of drug
abuse had reached 40 percent. [@1]

Iraq suffers from the flow of drugs from neighboring countries, especially Iran,
through the marshes and other border crossings, and the spread among youth has
become a threat to society. [@1] A police officer in Diwaniyah told Al-Monitor
that citizens are well aware the source of these drugs is mainly Iran. [@1] The
officer said the drugs arrive either through the southern Iraqi borders or
through the Kurdistan Regional Government. [@1]
