Jayesh Mon Jacob, a native of Arpookara in Kottayam district known as 'Aletti',
was arrested in a case of smuggling about 60 kilograms of ganja from Andhra
Pradesh. [@1] Media described him as a notorious goonda. [@2] He was accused in
many cases, including murder and attacks on police and excise officers. [@2] He
was being held as an undertrial over the ganja case when the KAPPA order came.
[@2]

In early October 2020 the district collector ordered his preventive detention
under KAPPA, Kerala's anti-social activities prevention law, on the report of
the district police chief G. Jayadev. [@2] Gandhinagar police arrested him under
the KAPPA law. [@2] He was moved to Thiruvananthapuram central jail. [@2] He was
to remain in detention for a year. [@1]

The detention formed part of a wider Kottayam district police crackdown on
gangsters, in which about ten goondas were arrested over the course of a week.
[@1] Police said more people would face KAPPA action in the coming days. [@1]
