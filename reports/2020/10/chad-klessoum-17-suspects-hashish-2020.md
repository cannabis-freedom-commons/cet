Chad's national gendarmerie announced on Tuesday the arrest of 17 suspected
offenders involved in various offences and presented them at Klessoum. [@1]
Fifteen of those arrested formed a network that gathered early in the morning
and, under the influence of alcohol, held the neighbouring population hostage,
spokesman Colonel Abakar Abdramane Haggar said. [@1] A soldier was among them
and was presented as an active member of the network. [@1]

Another person was arrested for holding a counterfeit 5,000 FCFA note, and a
suspected drug seller was also in gendarmerie custody. [@1] Haggar said a very
large quantity of balls of hashish and of tramadol had been recovered from that
seller, whom he identified as a seller. [@1] The gendarmerie also announced the
seizure of 17 firearms and four bladed weapons taken from the suspects. [@1] A
large quantity of prohibited products was also presented by the gendarmerie.
[@1]

Haggar said that from that morning the 17 suspects would be placed at the
disposal of the justice system to answer for their acts, and that banditry was
declining. [@1] Magistrate Wambel Assoucia Ngueli, representing the prosecution,
said the suspects believed everything was permitted. [@1] He said the imperative
duty was to start the procedure immediately. [@1]
