National Police chief in Nueva Segovia, Comisionado Mayor Javier Martínez, said
officers captured two men in El Jícaro municipality at 6:00 a.m. on Wednesday 14
October 2020. [@1] The detained men were identified as 29-year-old Boanerges
Antonio Espinoza Molina and 21-year-old Enoc Gamaliel Gómez Hernández. [@1]

Officers seized a sack containing 27 parcels of green herb wrapped in adhesive
tape weighing 54 pounds (about 24.49 kg). [@1] Officers also seized a
.38-calibre revolver and two motorcycles, a black Único and a multicoloured
Yara, allegedly used to distribute the marijuana. [@1] The police technical
investigation team inspected the scene, photographed it and tested samples of
the herb, which tested positive for marijuana. [@1]

Police presented the seizure at a press conference as part of continued
operations against organized crime and drug trafficking. [@1] The detainees and
the seized evidence were remitted to the competent authorities for prosecution.
[@1] Police said they continue special surveillance and patrol operations in
neighbourhoods, markets, bus stops and terminals, parks, roads and tourist
destinations. [@1]
