Five rappers signed to the Seoul hip-hop label MKIT Rain — Nafla, Loopy, Young
West, Owen and Bloo — were caught by the Seoul Metropolitan Police Agency's drug
investigation unit for using marijuana. [@3] Police booked all five in September
2019 on charges of violating the Narcotics Control Act. [@2] Young West was
indicted in July 2020 and stood trial, while the other four had their
indictments suspended as first-time offenders. [@3]

The rappers were found to have smoked marijuana at MKIT Rain's office in August
and September 2019. [@1] Police searched the label's headquarters and took hair
and urine samples from Nafla and Loopy, which tested positive. [@2] Nafla
admitted smoking marijuana with Loopy at the studio and said Young West had
bought it. [@3]

MKIT Rain Records posted an apology after the news report. [@1] Mnet removed
Owen from 'Show Me the Money 9' and said it would delete all his scenes. [@2]
Owen answered rapper Bill Stax, formerly Vasco, on Instagram by calling him a
“model citizen”; Bill Stax was convicted of marijuana use in 2018 and advocates
legalisation. [@1] Under South Korean law, marijuana use can carry up to five
years in prison or a fine of up to 50 million won ($43,900). [@1]
