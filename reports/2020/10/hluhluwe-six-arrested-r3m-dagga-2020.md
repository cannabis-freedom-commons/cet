Hluhluwe police officers seized dagga worth R3 million on Wednesday night. [@1]
The operation followed information that two vehicles were smuggling dagga
between Swaziland and Hluhluwe. [@1] Officers were led to the N2 freeway at the
Phinda turn off, where they stopped the two vehicles carrying six occupants.
[@1] A search found both vehicles loaded with bags of dagga. [@1] The estimated
street value of the recovered dagga was R3 million, according to provincial
police spokesperson Colonel Thembeka Mbele. [@1] A total of six suspects aged
between 23 and 42 were placed under arrest. [@1]

The stop was made at about 9.15pm on Wednesday night. [@1] Police established
that the suspects were also in the country illegally as they failed to produce
the required documents. [@1] The six were expected to appear before the
Mtubatuba Magistrate's Court on Friday. [@1] In recent months police had also
seized millions of rand worth of dagga in separate busts, most involving
transported cannabis. [@1]
