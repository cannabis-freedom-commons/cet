India's Narcotics Control Bureau (NCB) arrested Bollywood actor Rhea Chakraborty in Mumbai on 8 September 2020. [@3] The agency held her for allegedly procuring cannabis for her boyfriend, actor Sushant Singh Rajput, who had been found dead at his Mumbai home on 14 June. [@1,2,3] A court sent her to 14 days of judicial custody till 22 September, and on 9 September she was shifted from the NCB office to Byculla jail. [@3]

The NCB said its investigation began with chats about drug consumption, procurement, usage and transportation passed to it by the Enforcement Directorate. [@3] Chakraborty, her brother Showik and Rajput's former house manager were all arrested for allegedly organising and financing Rajput's consumption of cannabis, and all denied wrongdoing. [@1] Reuters, citing her bail order, described her as "active in a drug syndicate connected with drug supplies". [@2]

On 7 October the Bombay High Court granted her bail after 28 days in jail, observing that she was not part of the chain of drug dealers and that the case involved no commercial quantity. [@3] The court ordered her to report to a police station for ten days, deposit her passport and seek permission before travelling abroad. [@3]
