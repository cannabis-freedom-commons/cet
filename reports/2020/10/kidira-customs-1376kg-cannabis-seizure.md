Officers of the Kidira commercial customs brigade, part of the Tambacounda
subdivision, seized 1,376 kg of chanvre indien (Indian hemp, meaning dried
cannabis) and 5 kg of brown, a cannabis resin. [@1] A statement from the
Directorate General of Customs put the haul at 1,376 kg of Indian hemp and 5 kg
of cannabis resin. [@2] The seizure took place around noon on Monday 26 October
2020, during a road check of a Malian-registered lorry carrying an empty
container towards Dakar. [@1] The traffickers had built a hiding place inside
the container and packed the drugs there in bales and parcels, the customs
statement said. [@1]

The Kidira brigade made the interception on the Dakar–Bamako corridor. [@2]
Customs said the operation followed two other recent seizures on the same
corridor, of 1,076 kg at Koumpentoum and 2,282 kg at Koungheul. [@1] The customs
communication also recalled 133 kg of chanvre indien taken earlier that week by
the maritime brigade at Toubacouta and the commercial brigade at Keur Ayip. [@1]
Customs said the investigations were continuing. [@1]
