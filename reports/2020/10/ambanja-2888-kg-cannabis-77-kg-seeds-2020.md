Gendarmes of the Antsiranana interregional gendarmerie district, led by brigade
general Fiederch Behivoka, ran an anti-drug operation in Ambanja district from
10 to 12 October 2020. [@2] The force seized 2,888 kilograms of cannabis, close
to three tonnes. [@2] A further 77 kilograms of cannabis seeds were seized. [@2]
At Antetezampaka Marotolana, gendarmes found 650 kilograms of recently harvested
cannabis leaves and 31 kilograms of seeds in a cabin in the middle of a
25-hectare field. [@2] At Antsahafaly Tanambao Antsahabe they collected 1,500
kilograms of leaves and 46 kilograms of dry seeds over 35 hectares. [@2] The
remaining 738 kilograms, divided among 44 sacks, were recovered at the home of a
suspected leading trafficker in Anivorano during an unannounced search. [@2] The
owner was detained and taken for questioning at the local gendarmerie company.
[@2]

Some of the cannabis had already been harvested and stored in warehouses or at
the owners' homes, while other plants were still standing and some seeds were
ready to be sown, the gendarmerie said. [@1] The gendarmerie said the plants and
seeds found in the fields were destroyed on the spot. [@1] The State Secretariat
for the Gendarmerie said the uprooted plants and the seeds seized on the
plantations were destroyed and burned on site. [@2]

General Behivoka said the operation was continuing and that anyone caught
planting, selling or buying drugs in the region would be prosecuted without
let-up. [@1]
