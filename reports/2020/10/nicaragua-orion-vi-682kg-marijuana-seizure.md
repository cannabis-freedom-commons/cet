Rear Admiral Ángel Eugenio Fonseca, the head of Nicaragua's Naval Force,
presented his institution's results from the Orion VI campaign at a press
conference. [@1] Thirty-two people were detained as part of Nicaragua's
participation in the campaign. [@1]

Authorities listed 682 kilos of marijuana alongside more than 613 kilos of
cocaine, 15 vehicles, 14 naval assets and more than 83,000 dollars. [@1]
Officers also seized five restricted weapons, 67 civilian-use weapons, 358
restricted munitions and 53 artisanal explosives. [@1] A Guatemalan-plated cargo
vehicle coming from the south was found with hidden compartments holding 577
kilograms of cocaine. [@1] The naval chief gave a combined seized-drugs total of
1,190.73 kilograms and said 948 migrants from the south of various nationalities
were intercepted in other efforts. [@1]

Orion VI ran from 15 September to 30 October. [@1] Twenty-nine countries and 57
state institutions took part in the security strategy. [@1] For the campaign the
Naval Force deployed forces and assets in Nicaragua's Caribbean Sea, the Pacific
Ocean and the inland-waters naval detachment. [@1] The Naval Force has
participated in the Orion naval and river campaign since 2018. [@1] Fonseca
framed the participation as strengthening the state's Containment Wall strategy.
