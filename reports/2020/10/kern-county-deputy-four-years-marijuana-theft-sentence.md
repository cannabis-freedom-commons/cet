Former Kern County sheriff's deputy Logan August was sentenced to four years in
state prison by Judge Judith Dulcich after pleading no contest to 15 felony
charges in August. [@1] The charges range from conspiring to commit a crime to
second-degree burglary and falsifying a report as a peace officer. [@1] The case
was prosecuted by Deputy District Attorney Garrett Rice. [@1]

The case concerned stealing more than 350 pounds (about 159 kilograms) of
marijuana that had been seized by the KCSO in 2015, according to the Kern County
District Attorney's Office. [@1] BakersfieldNow reported that August was
resentenced to four years in prison. [@2] Because the case was originally tried
in federal court in Fresno County, the new state charges did not violate the
double jeopardy rule, the district attorney's office said. [@2]

August had previously been sentenced to probation and served no jail time
following federal charges of conspiracy to possess with intent to distribute
marijuana in August 2017. [@1] A follow-up joint investigation by the Kern
County Sheriff's Office and the district attorney's office revealed several new
offenses. [@1] District Attorney Cynthia Zimmer said August betrayed the
community's trust by using his position of authority to steal and funnel drugs
back onto the streets. [@1]
