Gendarmes seized 2,898 cannabis plants on Huahine in a search operation that ran
from Tuesday 6 to Thursday 8 October 2020, the Papeete prosecutor's office said.
[@1] Six gendarmes of the local brigade, reinforced by eight sent from Tahiti
and by reservists, combed the island for three days with observation equipment
and a boat. [@2] The searches covered the island and the motu, the islets that
surround it. [@3] About fifteen cultivators were identified. [@1]

On the evening of Thursday 8 October, several people ambushed the gendarmes and
threw projectiles at them. [@1] One gendarme was slightly injured in the
confrontation and five people were arrested. [@1]

The authors of the ambush were taken before the prosecutor to be tried at an
immediate-appearance hearing on Monday 12 October. [@1] Everyone implicated in
the operation faced legal proceedings under the authority of the Papeete
prosecutor. [@1] All the drugs seized were destroyed by incineration, the
prosecutor's communiqué said. [@3]
