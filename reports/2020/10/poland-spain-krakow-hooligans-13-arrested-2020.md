Polish and Spanish authorities arrested 13 alleged members of a drug trafficking
network that operated across Europe, Europol said. [@1] The Polish wing of the
network was associated with football hooligans who follow one of Kraków's local
teams. [@1] Polish officers detained five people and brought five more from
prison or pre-trial detention, while Spanish services arrested three, the Polish
police's Central Bureau of Investigation (CBŚP) said. [@2] The action days took
place in September 2020 in Małopolska, Silesia and Lower Silesia in Poland. [@2]
One of the group's leaders was arrested in the Spanish province of Alicante, and
three other suspects were arrested in the three Polish provinces. [@3] A court
ordered all 13 held in pre-trial detention at the prosecutors' request. [@2]

Officers seized four cannabis plantations with more than 3,000 plants. [@1]
Authorities also seized 3.4 tonnes of cannabis and 1.4 tonnes of hashish. [@1]
In Poland officers also seized machetes, knives, incapacitating gas, electronic
equipment and data carriers. [@2] Police seized assets worth more than €3
million in bank accounts and properties. [@3]

The network bought, smuggled and sold large quantities of hashish, marijuana and
cocaine, Europol said. [@1] The group brought drugs in through Spain and moved
them through the Netherlands, Germany, Poland and Belgium, and Albanian gangs
helped move them into the United Kingdom. [@1] The operation was the culmination
of a three-year investigation during which 68 suspects were arrested. [@1] At
the time of the arrests, police believed the group was moving into smuggling
medical gear and PCR tests. [@1] A warehouse held large amounts of surgical
masks, rapid Covid-19 tests and PCR swabs that the gang had smuggled from China.
[@1] Polish prosecutors had filed four indictments against 18 members of the
group, and some convictions were final, the Polish police said. [@2] The Kraków
prosecutor's office led the investigation with CBŚP and the Border Guard. [@2]
