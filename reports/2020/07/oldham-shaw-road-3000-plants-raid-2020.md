Officers attended a building on Shaw Road, Oldham at about 10.15pm on Friday 24 July 2020 and forced their way in. [@1] Inside they found a cannabis farm covering several rooms, with about 3,000 plants carrying an estimated street value of £1.2 million. [@1,2]

A 36-year-old man was arrested at the scene on suspicion of cultivating cannabis. [@1,2] Inspector Anthony Allt of Greater Manchester Police's Oldham district called it a positive result for the people of Oldham, saying farms of this scale are run by organised criminals and that the raid had disrupted one of their funding sources. [@1,2]
