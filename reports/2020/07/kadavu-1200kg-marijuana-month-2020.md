In the week before 8 August 2020, the Fiji Police Force briefed Commissioner
Eastern Vitale Varo and government agencies at a meeting on the drug operations
underway on the island of Kadavu. [@1] Director Community Policing SSP Bereta
Naisua said police were focused on reducing the illegal trade because of its
social impact on Kadavu and that operations would continue over the coming
weeks. [@1]

Police said more than 1.2 tonnes of marijuana plants were seized in Kadavu
within about a month. [@1] Police Commissioner Brigadier-General Sitiveni Qiliho
said more than 1.2 tonnes of marijuana had been seized in Kadavu in the past
three to four weeks. [@2] Qiliho said the haul was made possible by two drones
bought at a cost of $60,000. [@2]

Qiliho called the Kadavu bust the biggest of 2020 and part of ongoing efforts in
the war against drugs. [@2] Qiliho said police would remain in Kadavu and
continue the drug operations. [@2] He said he expected more drone-spotted farms
in Kadavu to be seized. [@2] Police said the Duavata Community Policing approach
would help reduce cultivation and change Kadavu's image as a red-zone area. [@1]
