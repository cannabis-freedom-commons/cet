Policía Nacional officers arrested two people in barrio San Sebastián, Managua,
on Tuesday 14 July 2020, allegedly finding several tilas, or rolled marijuana
joints, on them. [@1] Residents had reported the young men to the police, saying
they stood on a corner every day smoking and then attacked each other. [@1] A
National Police patrol went to the corner to search those involved and found
marijuana, which was to be used as evidence in the investigation. [@1]

The police said the patrol was sent to prevent fights between the men that
sometimes left people seriously injured or dead. [@1] A resident, speaking off
camera, said the young men had recently been seen often on the corner smoking
marijuana and that he did not know whether they committed other crimes. [@1] It
was not known at the time whether either of the two had a criminal record, but
they were caught in flagrante with a substance whose sale is banned in
Nicaragua. [@1]

Neighbours thanked the police for the quick response, saying the barrio had
stayed fairly safe and that they had reported the men immediately for consuming
marijuana in public. [@1]
