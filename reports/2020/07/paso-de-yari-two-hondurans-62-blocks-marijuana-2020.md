Soldiers of the Nicaraguan Army's First Regional Military Command arrested two
Honduran men at about 8pm on 1 July 2020 at the Paso de Yarí, an unauthorized
crossing into Nicaragua. [@2] The crossing lies in Madriz department, in
northern Nicaragua. [@3] The men were Javier Padilla Gómez, 62, and Roger
Padilla, 18, both from the El Jocote area of Yarí in Honduras. [@2] They were
leading two pack animals carrying sacks of marijuana when the patrol stopped
them. [@2]

The patrol seized 62 blocks of marijuana. [@2] The marijuana was packed in two
sacks loaded on the animals. [@1]

Colonel José Hílcias Rizo, head of the First Regional Military Command, said the
operation took place at 8pm that Wednesday. [@2] The army said the two
detainees, the horses and the drug would be handed to the police, which would
open the investigation. [@3] The detainees were to be placed at the disposal of
the Public Prosecutor's Office. [@1] The patrol acted under the state strategy
Muro de Contención, or Containment Wall, for the protection of the state border.
[@2]
