Police uprooted more than 600 plants believed to be marijuana from a farm at
Nauciwai village in Kadavu. [@1] The discovery of green plants believed to be
marijuana in Kadavu was made on Friday 10 July 2020. [@1] The uprooting was
carried out by the Special Operations Team. [@1] Police put the estimated value
of the plants at close to $1 million. [@1]

The owner of the farm had not been identified. [@1] Police said investigations
were continuing. [@1]
