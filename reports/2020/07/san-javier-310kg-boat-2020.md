Prefectura Naval Argentina officers broke up a marijuana shipment being loaded
onto a boat in San Javier, Misiones, in an operation coordinated by land and
water. [@1] At night, on prevention duties, officers saw irregular movements on
the shore of the Puerto Natural Yacaré II, at kilometre 962 of the Uruguay
River, where several people were loading bundles onto a boat bound for Brazil.
[@1] A patrol went to the spot and gave the order to halt, but the group
abandoned the operation and fled into the bush, and no arrests were reported.
[@1]

Inside the boat, officers found 17 bundles holding 419 marijuana panes. [@1] The
load weighed 310 kilograms and had a market value the authorities put at more
than 23 million pesos. [@1]

Prefectura said it had seized more than three tonnes of marijuana in the
preceding days in Puerto Rico, Colonia Delicia-Puerto Mado, Puerto Leoni and
Montecarlo, also in Misiones. [@1] The federal prosecutor's office in Oberá,
headed by Viviana Vallejos, is handling the case. [@1]
