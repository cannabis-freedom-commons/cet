Policía Nacional officers arrested Norwin Roberto Vallejos Acuña, a 19-year-old
man, on a public street in León, Nicaragua, on the night of 8 July 2020. [@1]
Police placed the arrest at 10 p.m. at the intercity bus stop in the Primero de
Mayo neighbourhood of León municipality. [@3] He was held on suspicion of drug
trafficking involving marihuana. [@2]

Officers seized a sack containing 27 óvulos, or wrapped packets, of marihuana
weighing 28 pounds (about 12.7 kg). [@1] A second account said Vallejos was
caught carrying the 28 pounds of marihuana inside a sack. [@2] A police
technical investigation team photographed the scene and sampled the herb for a
chemical test that came back positive for marihuana. [@3]

Commissioner major Fidel Domínguez, head of the police in León, presented part
of the evidence at a press conference, saying it would be important in the start
of judicial proceedings against the detainee. [@1] The arrest was carried out
under the 'muro de contención' (containment wall) plan against drug trafficking
and organised crime. [@1] The detainee and the seized evidence were handed to
the corresponding authorities for prosecution. [@2] Police said they were
continuing special plans with the Ministry of Health at bus stops and terminals,
parks and roads across Nicaragua. [@1]
