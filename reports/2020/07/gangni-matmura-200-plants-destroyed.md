Dulal, son of Kashem of Matmura village, grew cannabis on land adjoining his
house. [@2] He fled when he realised police had surrounded the house and garden
from the night of 29 July 2020, while his wife Shefali, son Shakil and daughter
Shiuli were taken into custody for questioning. [@2] Police filed a case under
the Narcotics Control Act against the absconding Dulal and detained three people
for questioning. [@3]

In Matmura village of Gangni upazila in Meherpur, a Gangni police team cut more
than 200 cannabis plants on the morning of Thursday 30 July 2020, seizing them
as evidence and destroying the garden. [@2] Superintendent Murad Ali inspected
the site that morning after police held the cordon overnight on a tip. [@2] DBC
News described the same plot beside Dulal Hossain's own house and said Dulal
Hossain was in hiding. [@3]

AFP later confirmed that around 200 marijuana plants were uprooted and destroyed
in the 2020 operation. [@1] Officers found no signboard calling the site the
Bangabandhu Marijuana Farm during the 2020 operation. [@1] An old photo of the
raid resurfaced in January 2024 posts altered to add a signboard falsely linking
the farm to the ruling Awami League. [@1]
