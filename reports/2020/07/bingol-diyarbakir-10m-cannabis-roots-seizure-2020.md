Turkish security forces seized a total of 10,110,000 cannabis roots in
narco-terror operations over two days, the Interior Ministry announced on
Saturday, 11 July 2020. [@1] Provincial gendarmerie in the Genc district of
eastern Bingol province seized 6.11 million cannabis roots at 26 different
locations. [@1] Gendarmerie teams in the Lice district of southeastern
Diyarbakir seized 4 million cannabis roots and 1.244 kilograms (564.2 pounds) of
cannabis at 60 different locations. [@1] The seizures were in Bingol province in
eastern Turkey and Diyarbakir province in the southeast. [@2]

The ministry said the quantity of cannabis roots seized over the preceding 15
days had risen to 42,110,000. [@1] The ministry said its security units were
continuing to fight drug trafficking. [@1] The account linked the PKK to the
illegal drug trade, saying it had financed its activities that way since the
1980s. [@1]
