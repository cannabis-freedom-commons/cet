The Bexar County Sheriff's Office street crimes unit made the arrest as part of
its West Patrol Crime Reduction Initiative. [@1] Deputies stopped Anthony
Siebold, 21, on 23 July 2020 because he was not wearing a seat belt in his car.
[@1] During the stop they discovered that he had an active felony warrant. [@1]
They found 15 pounds of marijuana in his car. [@1]

Deputies found another 18 pounds of marijuana at his home. [@2] Together the two
finds came to 33 pounds, about 15 kilograms, of marijuana. [@1]

Siebold was arrested for possession of marijuana in the 5-to-50-pound range and
for aggravated assault with a deadly weapon. [@1] The sheriff's office estimated
the street value of the seized drugs at $149,685.48. [@1]
