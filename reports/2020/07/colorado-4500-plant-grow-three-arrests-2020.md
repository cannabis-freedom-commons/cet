Three men were arrested: Joseph Vaquilar, 35, Javier Morejón, 65, and Robin
Morejón, 55. [@1] The sheriff's office said the operation lasted 14 hours at
locations in Colorado Springs, Divide and Trinidad, spanning three counties.
[@2] Search warrants for both the Teller County and Las Animas County locations
were obtained and executed on Tuesday. [@3] The three faced charges of offences
related to marijuana and being special drug offenders, and were held in the
Teller County jail on $50,000 bond. [@3]

Commander Greg Couch said officers found about 4,500 plants at three sites in
Teller, El Paso and Las Ánimas counties over 14 hours. [@1] Law enforcement made
three arrests and reported seizing more than 1,500 plants and $32,000 in cash.
[@4] Authorities estimated the network had generated at least $2.3 million, with
each plant worth about $1,000 in the United States and $4,000 abroad. [@1]

At a press conference, Commander Greg Couch of the Teller County Sheriff's
Office said the three detainees were of Cuban origin and that investigators
suspected, without having confirmed, links to Cuban drug cartels. [@1] An
anonymous tip led detectives to a house in Divide, a town of 125 residents, and
the inquiry expanded to Trinidad and Colorado Springs. [@1] Investigators from
the Teller County Narcotics Team, the Las Animas Sheriff's Office and the DEA
were involved, assisted by the Colorado Springs Police Department and the
Colorado State Patrol. [@2] The sheriff's office said the three sites were
linked to individuals acting together to produce and distribute marijuana and
marijuana concentrate across the United States and other countries. [@2] At one
site suspects had altered the local utility's power cables to feed the grow, and
a transformer caught fire and melted. [@1] Couch said there was no precedent for
so large a marijuana seizure or such a criminal association in Teller County's
121 years. [@1]
