Gustavo Larios, aged 19, and Jader Padilla, aged 18, were preparing on Thursday
to distribute wrapped packages of marijuana ready for consumption in the Mercado
Oriental in Managua. [@1] Public-order agents noticed their suspicious behaviour
and searched them. [@1] Officers found the packages hidden inside their
clothing, together with cash believed to be that morning's proceeds from selling
the drug. [@1]

The two young men and the seized evidence were taken by patrol vehicle to Police
District I, where an investigative process was to begin. [@1] Police officers
patrol avenues, neighbourhoods and commercial centres every day with the aim of
holding back people who spread this drug on the streets and especially among
young people. [@1]

The police say the institution constantly carries out rehabilitation plans for
Nicaraguan youth warning of the harm that becoming involved in drugs can cause
to their lives and their families. [@1] Every Monday the National Police
presents detained criminals, drug distributors and dismantled criminal gangs at
a press conference, reaffirming its stated commitment to guaranteeing peace,
security and calm for families and communities across the country. [@1]
