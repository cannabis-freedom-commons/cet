As part of an ongoing investigation into drug supply, National Crime Agency officers working jointly with the Police Service of Northern Ireland stopped a lorry in the Templepatrick area in the early hours of Wednesday 3 June 2020. [@1,2,3] Officers recovered an estimated 600 kg of herbal cannabis from among a cover load of vegetables in the refrigerated lorry. [@1,2,3]

The agency called it the biggest drug seizure it had ever made in Northern Ireland, with an estimated street value there of between £10 million and £12 million. [@1,2,3] Belfast branch commander David Cunningham said a seizure of this size would deprive the organised crime groups behind it of commodity and profit. [@1]

NCA investigators arrested three men, including the lorry driver, on suspicion of conspiring to import controlled drugs. [@1,2,3] The men, aged 62, 37 and 32 and from counties Tyrone, Londonderry and Armagh, remained in custody while officers searched two premises, one in County Londonderry and one in County Tyrone. [@1,3]
