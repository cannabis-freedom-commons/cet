National Crime Agency officers with the Police Service of Northern Ireland
stopped a lorry in the Templepatrick area in the early hours of Wednesday 3
June 2020 as part of an ongoing investigation into drug supply. [@1,2] They
recovered about 600 kilograms of herbal cannabis hidden among a cover load of
vegetables, which the agency put at a Northern Ireland street value of between
£10 million and £12 million. [@1,2] The agency called it the biggest drug
seizure it had ever made in Northern Ireland. [@1,2]

Three men, including the lorry driver, were arrested on suspicion of
conspiring to import controlled drugs; aged 62, 37 and 32 and from counties
Tyrone, Derry and Armagh, they remained in custody. [@1,2] Officers also
searched two premises, one in Co Derry and one in Co Tyrone. [@1,2]
