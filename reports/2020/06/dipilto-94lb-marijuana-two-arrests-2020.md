Alexis Ariel Sánchez Medina, from Las Nubes, and Yader Josué Rodríguez Rivera,
from La Tablazón, were both from the municipality of Dipilto in Nueva Segovia.
[@1] The National Police detained them while they were trafficking a sack
containing 94 pounds of marijuana. [@1] The two were detained at 5:00 a.m. on 11
June at the La Tablazón junction in Dipilto, as part of the Field Security Plan
and the Containment Wall strategy against drug trafficking and organized crime.
[@1]

Commissioner Major Carlos Hernández, second chief of the National Police in
Nueva Segovia, said at a press conference that the drug was carried in 49 double
compact tacos, sealed with adhesive tape inside a MACEN sack, which field-tested
positive for marijuana and weighed 94 pounds. [@1] Hernández said Rodríguez
Rivera had prior criminal records for drug trafficking and storage. [@1] Police
investigations indicated the marijuana was presumably of Honduran origin,
brought into Nicaragua through unauthorized or blind points on the border with
Honduras. [@1]

A police technical investigation team inspected and photographed the scene and
took plant samples that tested positive for marijuana in a chemical analysis.
[@1] Both detainees and the evidence were to be sent to the competent
authorities for prosecution. [@1]
