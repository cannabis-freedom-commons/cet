Members of the Drug Squad had watched a garage in Triq il-Buffula l-Hamra, Mosta for weeks when, on Thursday 11 June 2020, they saw a 46-year-old Mosta man and a 22-year-old Russian woman from Siggiewi enter it. [@1] Inside, officers found a 36-year-old Mosta man beside a parked vehicle holding five bags with 5kg of suspected cannabis and 10,000 euros in cash. [@1]

Police arrested the trio plus a 58-year-old woman living at the same residence, who owned the car the drugs were found in. [@1] Officers also confiscated several luxury vehicles, and Magistrate Donatella Frendo Dimech was put in charge of the inquiry. [@1]

Two days later, on 13 June, the Court of Magistrates as a court of criminal judicature ordered the temporary freezing of all assets of 36-year-old soft-drink vendor Jeffrey Cassar of Triq il-Bufula Hamra, Mosta, in compilation 256/2020 brought by Inspector Anthony Scerri under the Dangerous Drugs Ordinance, the money laundering act and the Criminal Code. [@2]
