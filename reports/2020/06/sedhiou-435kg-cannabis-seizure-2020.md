The territorial brigade of Sédhiou seized 200 kg of Indian hemp (chanvre indien)
with two Bissau-Guinean nationals and their means of transport at about 6 a.m.
on Wednesday 24 June 2020. [@1] The four people arrested across the two
operations were to be placed at the disposal of the prosecutor within hours,
while the investigation continued. [@1]

Another 235 kg of yamba was seized with one motorcycle and five bicycles. [@1]
That seizure was made on the same route the previous Monday. [@1] Together, the
two seizures totalled 435 kg of Indian hemp in 72 hours. [@1] The announcement
came from the commander of the Kolda gendarmerie, which covers the Kolda and
Sédhiou administrative regions. [@1] Captain Mody Sow valued the 200 kg at 20
million CFA francs wholesale. [@1] He said retailers selling the gram at 500
francs could get 40 million francs for the same quantity. [@1]

Both seizures were made on the Bignona forest–Guinea-Bissau route via Saré
Farim, Missirah and Sakar. [@1] Sow said traffickers were currently using that
route frequently to move their prohibited-herb production toward Guinea-Bissau.
[@1]
