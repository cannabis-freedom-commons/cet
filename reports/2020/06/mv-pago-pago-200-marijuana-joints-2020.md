When the boat returned from a trip to Manu'a, a customs agent accompanied by a
drug dog boarded, searched it and discovered the drugs. [@1] The vessel is
registered as a recreational boat, but on that day it brought cargo and nine
passengers from Manu'a. [@1] More than 200 marijuana joints were found aboard
the private vessel traveling between Tutuila and Manu'a. [@1] That private
vessel had been traveling between American Samoa's main island of Tutuila and
the outer islands of Manu'a. [@1]

The MV Pago Pago is an old US Navy torpedo retriever owned by businessman
Papalii Laulii Alofa. [@1] It had been delivering equipment and supplies for a
wharf dredging project being carried out by his own construction company. [@1]

Papalii said he requested that customs conduct a search of the private vessel.
[@1] He said he made the request after it came to his attention that authorities
suspected that drugs were being transported on local vessels. [@1]
