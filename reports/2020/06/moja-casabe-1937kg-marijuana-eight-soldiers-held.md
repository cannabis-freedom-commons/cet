National Guard troops detained four soldiers and three civilians at the Moja
Casabe citizen service point in Francisco de Miranda municipality, Anzoátegui
state, on the night of 18 June 2020. [@1] Four more soldiers were detained later
in connection with the operation. [@1] In all, eight military personnel and
three civilians were held over the load. [@1] Among the later detainees was the
frigate captain who directed the Cacique Aramare 73 river training centre in
Puerto Ayacucho. [@2]

The load was 3,439 panela-type blocks of marijuana weighing 1,937 kilograms and
220 grams. [@1] The marijuana was packed in 35 bundles hidden among perishable
food. [@2] The vehicle was a Beiben military truck of the Venezuelan navy. [@2]
The group had set out from Puerto Ayacucho in Amazonas for Cinaruco in Apure,
where it was to load the marijuana for El Tigre in Anzoátegui and El Temblador
in Monagas. [@1] The head of the National Anti-Drug Office said the marijuana
came from the Colombian border with Apure and was bound for El Tigre and
Temblador. [@2] Officers also retained a grey Ford EcoSport from the two
civilians arrested at the checkpoint. [@2]

The National Anti-Drug Office chief, Alberto Matheus, announced the seizure on
19 June without saying that the detainees were soldiers or that the truck
belonged to the armed forces. [@1] The prosecutor general said four people had
been captured at the checkpoint carrying the drug hidden in a cargo vehicle.
[@3] He said that on 20 June two more people had been brought before a court
with the four arrested at the checkpoint, charged with aggravated illicit drug
trafficking, criminal association and legitimación. [@3] The Ninth Drugs
Prosecutor's Office in Anzoátegui was leading the investigation. [@2]
