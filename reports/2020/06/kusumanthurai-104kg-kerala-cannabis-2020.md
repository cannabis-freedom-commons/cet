Naval personnel of the Northern Naval Command spotted a dinghy approaching the shore at Kusumanthurai, Jaffna, on 12 June 2020 with people transferring sacks from the dinghy onto land. [@1,2] When patrol officers reached the scene the suspects fled back to sea, leaving sacks behind that held 25 parcels of Kerala cannabis weighing about 57kg. [@1,2]

The Navy then launched a special operation for the fleeing dinghy, taking it into custody with two men aboard off Madagal. [@1] Acting on information from those men, officers recovered another 13 parcels with more than 46kg abandoned at sea, bringing the day's total to over 104kg of Kerala cannabis in two dinghies. [@1]

The suspects, the cannabis and the dinghies were handed to the Ilavalai Police for further investigation. [@1]
