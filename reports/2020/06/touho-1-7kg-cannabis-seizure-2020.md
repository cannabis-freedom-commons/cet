Gendarmes pursuing a domestic-violence inquiry searched the home of the suspect
in Touho, where the visit uncovered both cannabis and seeds. [@1] The officers
had come to the address in the course of an investigation opened for alleged
domestic violence. [@1] Inside the suspect's home, the search turned up a stash
of cannabis stored alongside a large number of seeds. [@1]

At that address, gendarmes found nearly 1.7 kilograms of cannabis, a quantity of
more than one and a half kilograms. [@1] Officers also recovered hundreds of
cannabis seeds at the same home, in addition to the weighed cannabis. [@1] The
reported seizure at Touho therefore comprised more than one and a half kilograms
of cannabis plus the seeds. [@1]

Because the drugs emerged during the unrelated inquiry, the discovery prompted
officers to open a second procedure dedicated to the cannabis find. [@1] That
second procedure was separate from the initial domestic-violence investigation
that had brought officers to the house. [@1]
