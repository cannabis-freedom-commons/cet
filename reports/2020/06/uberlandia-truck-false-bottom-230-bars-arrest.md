Three men were arrested on suspicion of drug trafficking on Tuesday night in
Uberlandia, in the Triangulo Mineiro. [@1] The Military Police reached a
mechanic workshop in the Nossa Senhora das Gracas neighbourhood after an
anonymous tip that criminals were unloading a truck of drugs from Paraguay. [@1]
Officers approached the 38-year-old workshop owner, a lathe operator of the same
age, and the 29-year-old truck driver. [@1]

A sniffer dog indicated drugs under and inside the front of the trailer, where
officers found a false bottom. [@1] Officers broke open the compartment with a
crowbar and found 230 bars of marijuana wrapped in plastic film with a type of
grease that may have been used to disguise the smell. [@1] Police also found two
buds of marijuana in the backpack of one of the men. [@1] Officers seized parts
in the workshop, including bolts that police said are used in the artisanal
manufacture of firearms. [@1] The three men were detained in flagrante and taken
to the Civil Police. [@1]
