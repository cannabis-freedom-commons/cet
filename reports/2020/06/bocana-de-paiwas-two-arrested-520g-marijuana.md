Two men were arrested with marijuana and a large amount of cash that police said
came from selling the drug in Bocana de Paiwas, in Nicaragua's South Caribbean
Coast Autonomous Region (RACCS). [@1] Police identified them as Jefrin Giovanni
Molina, 23, and Wilfredo Santiago Martínez López, 21, both living in the Walter
Calderón neighbourhood of Río Blanco municipality. [@1] They were riding a
white-and-black Génesis motorcycle with a red backpack, and the arrests took
place on Sunday 14 June 2020. [@1] Police stopped them about two kilometres from
the town of Bocana de Paiwas as they headed for the Villa Siquia community. [@1]

At the Bocana de Paiwas police station a field test on the substance gave a
positive result for marijuana, with a total weight of 520 grams. [@1] The men
were to be transferred to the preventive cells of the Matagalpa departmental
police delegation. [@1]

They were to be brought before the courts and accused of illegal transport of
narcotics, psychotropic substances and other controlled substances. [@1] The
National Police said the arrests formed part of its work in Bocana de Paiwas and
of its claim that Nicaragua is the safest country in Central America. [@1]
