Gendarmes arrested Mamadou Saliou Barry in mid-June 2020 at Madina Wora in
Guinea's Mali Prefecture over the cultivation, sale and consumption of Indian
hemp. [@2] The mayor of Madina Wora rural commune, Abdoulaye Karim Diallo, said
the arrest followed complaints from residents of Saissai district. [@1]
Residents of the district said Barry threatened them regularly. [@1] After the
mayor wrote to the prefect of Mali, the prefect sent a gendarmerie team to the
area. [@1] Gendarmes caught Barry as he tried to hide his stock and destroy the
fields. [@1]

The court in Mali Prefecture convicted him on Tuesday 16 June 2020 and sentenced
him to five years in prison, a fine of 2 million Guinean francs and confiscation
of the merchandise seized with him. [@2] Barry was then taken to the maison
centrale, the central prison, in Labé. [@1] The mayor said Barry was a repeat
offender, sentenced to six months in prison in Senegal for similar acts. [@1]
The cannabis seized from him was due to be burned in the coming days. [@1]

The mayor said Barry cultivated the hemp in his own village of Takhissa in Céssé
district, about 3 to 4 km from Madina Wora centre. [@2] He also ran a sales
point in Madina centre. [@2] A Franco-Arab school with at least 300 children
stood next to the plots, the mayor said. [@2] The mayor said Barry's whole
family, including his mother, was involved in the drug business. [@1]
