Investigators with the Tehama County District Attorney Bureau of Investigation
arrested a Sacramento man after a four-month investigation into the illegal
cultivation of marijuana for sale. [@1] The man was identified as 34-year-old Ye
Minghua from Sacramento, who was booked into the Tehama County Jail. [@1]

On 29 September 2020, investigators served a search warrant after finding a
large shop housing a commercial indoor marijuana growing operation in the 1600
block of Betz Lane west of Red Bluff in Tehama County. [@1] While searching the
shop, investigators seized 752 marijuana plants along with evidence of illegal
sales of marijuana. [@1] The plants were found inside the shop building, which
had been set up as a commercial indoor growing operation. [@1]

Ye was booked on charges of marijuana cultivation and possession of marijuana
for sale. [@1] He was also booked on charges of maintaining a residence for drug
sales and numerous Tehama County ordinance violations. [@1] The investigation
was ongoing and more arrests were anticipated, with no court outcome reported in
this account. [@1]
