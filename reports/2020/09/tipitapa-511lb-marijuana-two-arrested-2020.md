Nicaragua's National Police arrested two men at a checkpoint at kilometre 21 of
the Pan-American highway north, in Tipitapa municipality, Managua, at 5:30 am on
Saturday 12 September 2020. [@1] The checkpoint stopped a pickup with plate M
295 009 driven by Ángel Alberto Hurtado Rodríguez and a motorcycle with plate M
167655 ridden by Carlos Alberto Fonseca Blandón. [@1] Commissioner General
Sergio Gutiérrez presented the case as a result of the Plan Muro de Contención
strategy against drug trafficking and organised crime. [@1]

Officers found five red sacks in the pickup holding 540 egg-shaped packages
wrapped in adhesive tape, filled with green plant material that weighed 511
pounds (about 232 kg). [@1] Police said a field test and a chemical analysis of
samples taken from the material were positive for marijuana. [@1] Officers also
seized the pickup and the motorcycle. [@1] The load was carried in sacks on the
back seats of the pickup, which was travelling north to south on the highway.
[@3]

The two detainees and the evidence were handed to the competent authorities for
prosecution. [@2] Gutiérrez said the police would keep working to strengthen the
security of people, families and communities. [@1]
