Police found 26 bags of marijuana weighing 17.2kg hidden among bags of vegetables at a private wharf at Motukea in Port Moresby on Saturday 26 September 2020. [@1,2] The bags, weighing between 0.5kg and 1.5kg each, had travelled with garden food shipped from Goroka in the Eastern Highlands to Lae, then by Bismark Shipping to the AES wharf west of Port Moresby. [@2]

National drug and vice squad acting officer in charge Sergeant Bruno Morgan said the drugs would fetch around K1,000 a bag at wholesale and about K260,000 on the street. [@2] He said Port Moresby is the business centre where all the consumers are, and that seizures like this one keep happening. [@2]

When officers inspected the container, half the cargo had already been collected by the owners of the vegetables. [@2] The reports name no suspects and no arrests. [@1,2]
