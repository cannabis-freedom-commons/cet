National Police in Nueva Segovia presented the detained Wilson Oniell Ruíz
Altamirano on the afternoon of 23 September 2020. [@1] He had been captured in
the Zacatera sector of Jalapa municipality in possession of 110 marijuana
plants. [@1]

Commissioner Carlos Hernández, second chief of the National Police in Nueva
Segovia, said a police patrol seized the plants at 6:00 a.m. on Monday 21
September 2020. [@1] The operation was carried out under the muro de contención,
or wall of containment, plan against drug trafficking and organized crime. [@1]
A second account says the 110 plants were being cultivated at the suspect's
home. [@2] A police technical team inspected and photographed the scene, and
samples of the plant material tested positive for marijuana. [@1]

The man was to be handed to the competent judicial authorities and prosecuted
for cultivating marijuana. [@2]
