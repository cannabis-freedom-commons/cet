Police called the October 2019 find one of the largest cannabis farms ever discovered in Lancashire. [@1,2] Officers raided units at the Apex Trading Estate on Lower Eccleshill Road in Darwen at about 10am on 8 October 2019 and at first estimated about 10,000 plants at various stages of growth. [@1] Two men aged 24 and one aged 31 were charged with producing cannabis. [@1]

## Sentenced

The final count put before Preston Crown Court was 4,537 plants plus 27 kg of dried cannabis, valued together at £2,606,620. [@2] The three men, who had been living in the unit, admitted producing a class B drug. [@2] Aurel Gjuzi, 25, and Jacek Pieczur, 25, were each jailed for three years and Sulejman Lloni, 32, for 54 months, a combined total of just over ten years. [@2] Officers also recovered £2,425 in cash and several mobile phones, and found the factory powered by underground cables bypassing the electricity supply, which took engineers 13 hours to make safe. [@2]
