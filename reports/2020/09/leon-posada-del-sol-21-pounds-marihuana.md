Policía Nacional officers arrested Denis Rafael Martínez Aguirre and Josué Ariel
Peña Trujillo on a public street in the Posada del Sol neighbourhood of León at
about 1 p.m. on Monday 28 September 2020. [@1] The men were carrying a sack
containing 21 wrapped packets of green herb held together with adhesive tape.
[@1] Officers seized 21 pounds (about 9.5 kg) of marihuana. [@1]

Police experts who sampled the herb at the scene confirmed it tested positive
for marihuana. [@1] Officers from the police anti-narcotics department collected
the sample and confirmed the result. [@2] Commissioner general Fidel Domínguez
said the arrests came from the 'muro de contención' (containment wall) strategy
against drug trafficking and organised crime. [@2] The two suspects, the drugs
and the other evidence were sent to the competent authorities for prosecution.
[@1]

Police said they were continuing special plans, surveillance and patrols in
neighbourhoods, markets, bus terminals, parks and tourist sites. [@1]
