A large operation was carried out on Tuesday morning in the Rivière des Galets
neighbourhood of Le Port. [@2] The woman present at the scene was arrested, and
her partner then surrendered at the Le Port police station, where he was placed
in police custody. [@2] Two people were held in police custody after the drug
seizure in the commune of Le Port. [@1]

Officers found more than 250 zamal plants (zamal is the local term for
cannabis), including 10 kilograms of cannabis that had already been dried. [@1]
A later account put the number at 263 plants in a house in Le Port, along with
10 kilograms of cannabis treated and ready for resale. [@2] Officers also
reportedly found 12,000 euros in cash and two firearms, one of which relatives
said belonged to the suspect's father. [@2]

The operation involved officers from the Le Port public safety brigade, the
anti-drug dog brigade and the anti-crime brigade (BAC). [@2] The couple were
detained at the start of the week in Rivière des Galets on suspicion of drug
trafficking. [@3] The main suspect and his partner were due to be referred to a
judge on 28 February, but the case was postponed to 6 May. [@3] The mother of
the main suspect said police burst in and turned the house over. [@2]
