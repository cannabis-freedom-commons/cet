Tonga Police's Drug Enforcement Taskforce arrested nine people at six locations
in Tongatapu in an operation targeting drug suppliers and dealers. [@2] All nine
were charged and held in police custody to appear later at the Magistrate's
Court. [@2] A 39-year-old man was charged with possession of illicit drugs and a
woman arrested with him with interference with police investigation. [@2]

On 29 January 2020 the taskforce arrested the 39-year-old man and a 23-year-old
woman at a residence in Popua, seizing 0.93 grams of cannabis and drug utensils.
[@2] On 31 January a 46-year-old man was arrested at a bush allotment in Fatai
with 128.69 grams of cannabis and 0.32 grams of methamphetamine. [@2] On 1
February two men were arrested at a residence in Tu'atakilangi with 15 packs of
cannabis weighing 13.99 grams. [@2] That day a 34-year-old man from Haveluloto
was arrested with seven packs of methamphetamine and cash. [@2] Early on 3
February officers arrested three men from two vehicles, seizing 0.43 grams of
cannabis, 0.75 grams of methamphetamine and drug utensils. [@2]

In total the operation seized 144.05 grams of cannabis and 3.79 grams of
methamphetamine. [@2] Acting Deputy Commissioner Tevita Vailea said police were
committed to disrupting and detecting the supply of drugs and holding those
responsible to account. [@2] The force described the arrests as part of an
ongoing operation by its Drug Enforcement Taskforce to crack down on illicit
drugs. [@1]
