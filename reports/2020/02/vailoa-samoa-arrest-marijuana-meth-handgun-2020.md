Samoa Police Service arrested a 37-year-old man from Alafua at Vailoa on the
morning of Monday 17 February 2020, believing he was under the influence of
drugs at the time. [@3] A search of his vehicle found marijuana, drug
paraphernalia, methamphetamine, live ammunition and a handgun, which police
described as an unlawful weapon. [@2]

Police estimated the street value of the methamphetamine at 40,000 tala. [@1] He
was remanded in custody until 9 March 2020, when he was due to appear in court
for mention. [@1]

In a separate case, police seized marijuana and an unregistered firearm from a
home at Vaitele-uta on 18 February. [@2] Police also reported two handguns
seized at a home in Sapulu Faleasiu after a response on 12 February. [@1] Police
said a number of unlawful weapons had been seized in the previous two weeks.
[@1] Police reminded the public that unlawful weapons were not only unlawful but
also dangerous in the hands of the wrong person. [@1]
