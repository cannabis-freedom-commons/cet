The arrest happened in Ashburn early in the morning on Saturday 8 February 2020.
[@2] A Loudoun County sheriff's deputy stopped a vehicle for an infraction near
the intersection of Parkview Drive and Legacy Park Drive at about 2:31 a.m. [@1]
The driver got out of the vehicle and fled on foot before responding deputies
found him nearby. [@1] Deputies caught the man a short time later and took him
into custody. [@2]

Deputies found marijuana in his possession and found that his licence was
suspended and that an arrest warrant from another jurisdiction was outstanding
against him. [@1] Timon O. Sandidge, 43, of Washington, D.C., was charged with
eluding law enforcement, hindering a deputy sheriff, driving while suspended and
possession of marijuana. [@1] He was being held at the Loudoun County Adult
Detention Center on the outstanding warrant as of Monday afternoon. [@1]

Possession of marijuana was a misdemeanour under Virginia law at the time, and a
first offence carried up to 30 days in jail and a $500 fine. [@2]
