Afghan army troops seized 1,200 kilograms of drugs during an operation in
central Logar province, an official said on Thursday. [@1] Ahmad Shakeel Tasal,
spokesman for the Afghan National Army's 203rd Thunder Corps, said the soldiers
confiscated hashish in the Bedak area of Pul-i-Alam, the provincial capital, on
Wednesday. [@1] Tasal said the security forces recovered 1,200 kilograms of
hashish during that operation. [@2] One man accused of transporting the drugs
was killed in fighting with the security forces. [@2] Another person accused of
moving the same drugs was arrested. [@2]

The drugs were placed in a pick-up vehicle and had been brought from eastern
Nangarhar province, he added. [@1] Those carrying the drugs had brought them
from Nangarhar in a Datsun pick-up and wanted to move them to the country's
western provinces before the forces stopped them, Tasal said. [@2]

Haseebullah Stanikzai, head of the Logar provincial council, said security
forces had stepped up their efforts to prevent drug smuggling. [@2] Stanikzai
added that the effort in this area had to be very serious because drugs were
brought from the eastern provinces and moved through Logar to other provinces.
[@2]
