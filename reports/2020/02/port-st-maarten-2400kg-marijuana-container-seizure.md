St. Maarten Customs seized almost 2.5 tonnes of marijuana on Friday in a
container at Port St. Maarten, in what may be the largest marijuana seizure in
St. Maarten history. [@1] Police said no arrests had been made in connection
with the drug bust and the investigation was still ongoing. [@1]

Customs officers conducted a general control of several containers being
offloaded from container ship Jamila. [@1] Officers opened a container that had
originated in Jamaica and found approximately 240 cardboard boxes containing
marijuana, compressed and wrapped in plastic packaging. [@1] The Daily Herald
understood more than 2,400 kilogrammes of marijuana was seized, while
authorities had not disclosed the quantity as of 11:00pm Friday. [@1] Police
Forensic Department personnel weighed and photographed the seized marijuana
before it was taken to the dump and burned under the supervision of the
Prosecutor’s Office. [@1] The police K-9 Unit checked the container to confirm
no more drugs were hidden in other areas. [@1]

Jamila had arrived in St. Maarten around 8:00am Friday after departing Santo
Domingo, Dominican Republic, on Thursday morning, and departed for Guadeloupe
around 9:00pm Friday. [@1] Detectives of the St. Maarten Police Force KPSM and
Prosecutor’s Office personnel went to the port to start the investigation, then
loaded the contraband into seven pickup trucks and transported it to the
Philipsburg police station. [@1] The transfer blocked the Philipsburg area from
pedestrian and motorised traffic for security reasons and caused quite a stir.
[@1] Customs Head Anthony Doran said in a Friday night press release that
controls at the port will be intensified with assistance from police and other
law enforcement personnel. [@1]
