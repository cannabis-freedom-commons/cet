Chilean Customs inspectors seized 20 rolls holding 334 kg of pressed marijuana
at the El Loa outpost. [@2] The drug was carried as cargo inside an
interprovincial bus and disguised as textile material bound for the municipality
of Estación Central in Santiago. [@1] The rolls had been loaded in Iquique for
Santiago. [@2] The acting regional director of the Iquique customs, James
Alarcón Contreras, said an interprovincial bus arrived during the night with the
supposed textile rolls among its parcels, which were immediately selected for a
thorough inspection. [@1] X-rays confirmed that the rolls hid an organic
substance. [@2] Inspectors then opened them and found a large quantity of
marijuana under the plastic and the synthetic leather. [@2]

Each roll measured 1.5 metres long and 20 centimetres wide. [@1] The outpost
carries out controls on vehicles and merchandise leaving the Tarapacá region,
152 km south of Iquique. [@1] Chilean Customs put the value of the seized drug
at more than 1.670 billion pesos on the illicit market. [@2]
