National Civil Police (PNC) officers arrested Roberto Jesús Padilla Lizama, 40,
a Nicaraguan known as 'El Chocho', on suspicion of possession and holding of
drugs with intent to traffic. [@1] Members of the Fuerza Operativa Conjunta
Antidelincuencial (FOCA), a joint anti-delinquency police force, arrested him on
the avenida 29 de Agosto at 10ª Calle poniente in San Salvador during a search
of the sector. [@2] El Blog reported the arrest was made in flagrante on Friday,
14 February 2020. [@4]

Police said Padilla Lizama was carrying 50 portions of marijuana inside a
gabacha, a holdall bag, together with cocaine. [@1] The police report put the
cocaine at 910, without stating a unit. [@1] Diario La Página reported the
cocaine figure as 900. [@3] Officers also seized 90 tubes used as pipes to
inhale the drug. [@1]

Diario Cronio reported that the arrest followed police checks in that sector,
where the drugs were found. [@5] FOCA officers were acting under the Plan
Control Territorial, the government's security strategy. [@4] La Página said he
would be handed to the relevant authorities in the coming hours to face judicial
proceedings for possession and holding with intent to traffic. [@3]
