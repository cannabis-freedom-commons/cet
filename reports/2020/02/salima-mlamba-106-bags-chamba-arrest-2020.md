Police in Salima district arrested Chingeni Kadam'manja, a Democratic
Progressive Party youth cadet, over Indian hemp, known in Malawi as chamba. [@1]
Salima police spokesperson Jacob Khembo said officers arrested him in the early
hours of Monday, 17 February 2020. [@1] Kadam'manja is 35 years old. [@2] He is
also treasurer of the Nyasa Big Bullets FC supporters committee, and was
previously caught with chamba but released because he was at one time on the
security detail of the minister for disaster management affairs. [@1]

Officers found 61 sack bags and 45 plastic bags of chamba, 106 bags in all. [@1]
Khembo said Kadam'manja had loaded the drug into a minibus and was carrying it
from Dwangwa in Nkhotakota district to Blantyre. [@1] Police set up the
roadblock at Mlamba on the M5 road, which links Salima and Balaka. [@1] Khembo
said officers acted on a tip-off and found the hemp when they searched the
vehicle at the roadblock. [@1] The minibus carried registration BLK 6917. [@3]
Police in Salima impounded the minibus. [@3]

Kadam'manja was taken to the Salima second magistrate's court and granted bail.
[@1] He is charged with being found in possession of Indian hemp, contrary to
section 19(1) of the Dangerous Drugs Act. [@1] The Indian hemp was to be taken
to Chitedze Research Station for certification. [@1]
