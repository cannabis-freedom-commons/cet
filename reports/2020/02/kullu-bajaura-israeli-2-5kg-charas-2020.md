Kullu police arrested Israeli national Shaul Borov, 42, a resident of Jerusalem,
with 2.5 kg of cannabis, superintendent Gaurav Singh said. [@1] He was stopped
during checking at the TCP Bajaura check post, in a Delhi-bound private Volvo
bus from Manali, the night before the 3 February 2020 report. [@1] Police said
he had packed the cannabis in 10 bowls. [@2] Police said he was taking the
consignment to Varanasi and had come to Kullu on a tourist visa. [@1] A case was
registered under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985. [@1]

The state prosecuted him over 2.500 kg of charas, which it said was a commercial
quantity. [@3] The case was tried as Case No.100 of 2020 before the Special
Judge-II, Kullu, who passed the conviction and sentence on 31 March 2023. [@3]
The trial court held him guilty of possessing 50 grams of charas, a small
quantity. [@3] It sentenced him to rigorous imprisonment for six months. [@3] It
fined him 1,000 rupees. [@3]

The state appealed to the Himachal Pradesh High Court under section 377 of the
Criminal Procedure Code, arguing that the sentence was inadequate because he had
been found with a commercial quantity. [@3] On 20 December 2023 the High Court
dismissed the appeal as not maintainable, holding that it could not convert a
conviction for a small quantity into one for a commercial quantity in an appeal
against sentence. [@3] The court noted that he had already served more than the
maximum one-year term for possessing a small quantity. [@3] At a November 2024
hearing the state told the High Court that its special leave petition against
the December 2023 judgment had been dismissed. [@4] The High Court then closed
his petition, saying the state was free to act on permitting him to leave the
country. [@4]
