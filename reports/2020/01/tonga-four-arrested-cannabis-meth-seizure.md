Police in Tonga arrested four people on drugs charges over the weekend before 28
January 2020. [@1] Two of the four people arrested were 50-year-old men. [@1] A
third person arrested was a 58-year-old man. [@1] The fourth person arrested was
a 21-year-old woman. [@1] All four remained in custody after the arrests. [@1]
All four had been charged with possession of illicit drugs. [@1]

Officers seized over 40 packs or 20 grammes of methamphetamine as well as an
unspecified quantity of cannabis in the same operation. [@1]

Police described the arrests and seizure as a significant result. [@1] Police
linked the operation to efforts to help the community feel safe. [@1] Police
linked the operation to efforts to reduce drug-related harm in the community.
