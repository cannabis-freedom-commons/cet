The Brigade Spéciale de la police des stupéfiants et des drogues, Côte
d'Ivoire's specialist narcotics police unit, seized an important quantity of
cannabis in a rubber plantation at Ebimpé, in the Anyama sub-prefecture. [@1]
After the parcels were taken to the unit's base, officers found and seized 215
blocks of cannabis with a total weight of 215 kilograms. [@1]

The plantation lies about 10 km from Ebimpé and had been under police
surveillance for some time after a tip that cannabis blocks were being stored
there. [@1] At about 05:00 on Thursday 16 January 2020 a team from the brigade
entered a rubber plantation where parcels covered with a tarpaulin were stored.
[@1] Finding the parcels, the officers hid in the plantation to wait for whoever
would come for them. [@1] Nobody came forward, and at about 13:50 the officers
seized the three large parcels. [@1] The traffickers had hidden the blocks under
second-hand clothing to avoid detection, and police opened an investigation.
[@1]

Afrik Soir, in a November 2020 article, also reported that national police
seized 215 blocks weighing 215 kg on the morning of 16 January 2020 at Ebimpé,
in Anyama commune, hidden in bags of second-hand clothing. [@2]
