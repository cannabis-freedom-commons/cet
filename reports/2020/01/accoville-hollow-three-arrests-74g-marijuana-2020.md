Logan County sheriff's deputies went to a house on Old Glory Circle in Accoville
Hollow on the morning of 29 January 2020 after reports of drug activity. [@1]
The owner, Ricky Lee Herman, 56, of Accoville, gave deputies consent to search
the house. [@1] Deputies found Melvin Anthony Taylor III, 23, of Detroit,
Michigan, in a bedroom. [@1] A loaded 9mm Taurus pistol lay on the nightstand
beside him. [@1] A plastic bag in his trousers held a powder he called heroin.
[@1] A black backpack at the foot of the bed held two bags of suspected
marijuana. [@1] Deputies also found $1,397 in his trouser pocket. [@1] George
Brian Fields, 49, of Davin, was sitting at the dining-room table, and police
allege he knew Taylor had a large quantity of drugs and would sell them from
Herman's house. [@1]

At the sheriff's office the white powder weighed about 96.4 grams and was
recorded as suspected heroin, not cannabis. [@1] The suspected marijuana weighed
25 grams and the last bag 49.8 grams. [@1] WCHS reported that deputies found 74
grams of marijuana in the house. [@2] Fields and Herman were allegedly found
with suspected meth, which is not counted as cannabis here. [@3]

Taylor was charged with two counts of possession with intent to deliver,
conspiracy to deliver a controlled substance and prohibited person in possession
of a firearm. [@1] Magistrate Joe Mendez arraigned him on a $200,000 cash-only
bond. [@1] Herman and Fields were charged with conspiracy to deliver a
controlled substance and simple possession of meth, on bonds of $10,000 each.
[@1] WCHS put Herman's bond at $20,000 cash and Fields' at $20,000, 10% cash or
surety. [@2] All three were held in the Southwestern Regional Jail. [@2] Taylor
had a felony conviction for first-degree retail fraud in Southfield, Michigan,
in November 2019. [@1]
