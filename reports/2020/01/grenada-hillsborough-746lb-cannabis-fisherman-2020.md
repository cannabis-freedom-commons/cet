Kevin Phillip, a 27-year-old fisherman from Levera in St Patrick, was alone on a
blue fishing boat intercepted just outside the waters of Hillsborough,
Carriacou, at about 11.30 p.m. on Tuesday 7 January 2020. [@2] The interception
was carried out by a special operation team in Carriacou working with members of
the Coast Guard as the boat approached the shores of Hillsborough. [@1]

A search of the boat found 746 pounds of marijuana, about 338 kg, packed in 16
fine bags. [@1] Police estimated the street value at $1,700,112. [@1] The Royal
Grenada Police Force described it as its first major drug arrest of the new year
in the waters just outside Hillsborough. [@1]

Phillip was charged with trafficking a controlled drug and importing a
controlled drug. [@2] He appeared unrepresented before Magistrate Tahira
Gellineau at the St George's No. 2 Magistrate's Court on St John's Street. [@1]
He was remanded to the Richmond Hill Prison until 21 January. [@1] The next
hearing was listed at the Carriacou Magistrate's Court, presided over by
Magistrate Francine Foster. [@1] The police said investigations were continuing.
[@2]
