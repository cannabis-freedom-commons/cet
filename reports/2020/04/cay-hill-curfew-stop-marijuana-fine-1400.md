The St. Maarten Police Force (KPSM) arrested a man identified only as Y.A.M. in
the Cay Hill area around 1:00am on Thursday. [@1] The stop took place on
Thursday 30 April 2020, while a curfew was in force. [@2] Officers stopped him
because the curfew was in effect and asked why he was on the road. [@1] He told
them he had been visiting a friend and was on his way home. [@1]

He showed officers a travel waiver, but the name on it did not match the
identity card he then produced. [@2] Police arrested him for being in possession
of false documents. [@2] A search of his clothing found an undisclosed quantity
of marijuana in his trouser pocket. [@1] Police confiscated the marijuana and
the waiver for further investigation. [@1]

He was taken to the Philipsburg police station and held there, and police took a
statement from him later that day about the marijuana and the waiver. [@1]
Police fined him US$1,400 for the offences and released him from custody. [@1]
