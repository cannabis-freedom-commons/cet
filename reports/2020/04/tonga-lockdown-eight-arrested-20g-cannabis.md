Eight men were arrested on 1 April 2020 for possessing methamphetamine and
cannabis in a Tonga Police operation during the national lockdown. [@1] The
Tonga Police Drug Enforcement Taskforce made the arrests on Wednesday 1 April
2020 while targeting drug dealers and suppliers during the lockdown. [@2] All
eight were men aged between 26 and 49, with four from Vaini, three from Tofoa
and one from Lapaha. [@2]

Police seized 20.61 grams of cannabis, along with 1.67 grams of methamphetamine,
drug utensils and more than 600 pa'anga in cash. [@2] RNZ reported the cannabis
as nearly 21 grams and the cash as more than US$255. [@3]

All eight men were charged and remained in police custody, due to appear in
court at a later date. [@2] Acting Deputy Commissioner Vailea said preventing
illicit drug supply was a high priority for Police. [@2]
