Grenada's police coast guard arrested three people, two from St Vincent and one
from Grenada, during an at-sea operation in April 2020. [@1] Officers recovered
approximately 379 pounds of marijuana, about 171.9 kg. [@1] They also seized a
boat and cash described as worth almost $320,000. [@1]

The operation used one of the coast guard's two nearshore interceptor boats.
[@1] The first of the two vessels had completed its overhaul in February and was
in active use as the backbone of coast guard operations against illegal
activity. [@1] The seizure was described when the United States announced
completion of a year-long US$1 million upgrade of the two vessels in December
2020. [@1] The upgrade included a complete overhaul, new engines and a spares
package, extending the vessels' performance by six years. [@1]

Deputy Commissioner of Police Franklyn Redhead described the SAFE boats as
invaluable to interdiction efforts. [@1] The United States had originally
provided the two SAFE boats to Grenada in 2012. [@1] Technicians from SAFE Boats
International, the CBSI field team and Naval Sea Systems Command carried out the
upgrade work. [@1]
