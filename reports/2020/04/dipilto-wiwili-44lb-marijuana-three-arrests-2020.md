National Police presented three people linked to drug trafficking and supply,
including two Hondurans captured on Thursday 9 April in Laguna No. 2, Dipilto,
Nueva Segovia. [@1] The two Hondurans were identified as Marlon Nahúm Ramírez
Fernández and Gamaliel Antonio Medina Olivas. [@1] Police captured the two men
at 5:30 in the morning on Thursday 9 April. [@1] The two men were transporting
40 packages of marijuana weighing 38 pounds (about 17.24 kg). [@1] Officers
captured Moisés Castro at 4:30 in the afternoon on 9 April carrying a backpack
containing six pounds of marijuana (about 2.72 kg). [@1]

Castro was detained at the Chachagua junction in Palo Prieto, Wiwilí, Nueva
Segovia. [@1] Together the seizures totalled 44 pounds of marijuana (about 19.96
kg). [@1] A police technical investigation team carried out the corresponding
inquiries. [@1] The detainees and the seized evidence were referred to the
competent judicial authorities for due process. [@1]

Nueva Segovia police chief Comisionado Mayor Carlos Martínez Leytón announced
the arrests at a press conference on Monday 13 April. [@1] He said the operation
formed part of the police's “Muro de Contención contra el Narcotráfico y Crimen
Organizado” (Containment Wall against Drug Trafficking and Organized Crime)
strategy. [@1]
