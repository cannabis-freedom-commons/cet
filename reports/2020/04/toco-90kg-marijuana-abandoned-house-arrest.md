Eastern Division police arrested a man after finding marijuana in his car in
Toco on the afternoon of Thursday 30 April 2020. [@1] The man was 37 years old
and from Arima, police said. [@1]

The car search yielded 940 grams of marijuana, according to a police media
release. [@1] Hours earlier, officers had found 90 kilogrammes of marijuana at
an abandoned house in Cumana Village, Toco, and made no arrest there. [@1]
Police said the finds came during an anti-crime exercise in the Sangre Grande
and Toco districts. [@2] Reports put the exercise between 2 pm and 7 pm on
Thursday 30 April 2020. [@2] At the abandoned house the officers found the 90
kilogrammes, and made no arrests in connection with that find. [@2] Officers
then stopped a vehicle with one male occupant in the Toco district and found 940
grammes of marijuana inside it. [@2]

The 37-year-old from Arima was arrested in connection with the vehicle find.
[@2] Police said investigations were ongoing. [@2] Photographs published with
the police account showed bales of marijuana that officers said were seized in
the exercise on 30 April 2020. [@2]
