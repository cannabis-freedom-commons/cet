A woman from San Miguelito district, in Panama province, was detained at the
sanitary checkpoint in Pocrí, Los Santos, carrying four kilograms of illicit
substances in her car. [@1] She told the police unit that stopped her that she
was taking food to people in need in Tonosí, and the officers found the packages
of the substance when they searched the vehicle. [@1]

The detention took place on the afternoon of Wednesday 29 April 2020, at one of
the sanitary checkpoints operating in Los Santos. [@1] Units of the drug
prosecutor's office went to the scene and seized three kilograms of marijuana
and about 200 grams of cocaine. [@1]

The woman was waiting for a virtual hearing before the Sistema Penal Acusatorio,
Panama's accusatory criminal system, in the city of Las Tablas. [@1] Women
detained in Los Santos who are ordered into provisional detention are
transferred to the Llano Marín prison in Coclé province. [@1]
