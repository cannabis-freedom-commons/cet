Côte d'Ivoire's drug police seized more than a tonne of cannabis in two searches
in Abobo and Grand-Bassam on the night of 31 March to 1 April 2020. [@2] Two
crews of the special brigade began a special patrol in Abobo's BC neighbourhood
at 20:00, and a tip soon reported suspect parcels in an abandoned shared
courtyard. [@2] The prosecutor ordered a search of the courtyard, carried out
between 22:30 and 23:00, where officers found and seized three bags of 70
cannabis blocks each. [@1] The total was 1,005 kilograms of cannabis, which
police valued at 40.2 million CFA francs. [@2] The operation took place while a
curfew was in force. [@1]

After officers saw that the blocks resembled those seized the day before at
Grand-Bassam, the director of the drug police told her teams to gather more
intelligence on the town. [@1] At about 00:20 on 1 April a new tip led the
brigade to a shared courtyard in the Odoss district of Grand-Bassam. [@1] A
search of the courtyard's nine doors found 11 large bags holding between 70 and
75 cannabis blocks each. [@1] The occupant of the room was away, and the owner
of the courtyard, Sama Ouango, 78, a Burkinabè driver living in Grand-Bassam's
Odoss district, presented himself to the brigade. [@2] He told police the
cannabis belonged to his son, Sama Amadou, 30, who lives in Samo. [@2]

Police opened an investigation into the seizure. [@1] The son had not been
caught, and police described him as a big dealer. [@2]
