Asamankese District Police Commander DSP George Kingsley Adu, dressed in mufti,
spotted Munkaila Musah, 35, selling something he suspected to be Indian hemp on
1 January, approached him and offered to buy a wrapper, which Musah readily sold
him. [@1] Adu arrested him on the spot and took him to the police station, where
the wrappers were counted and weighed 3,729 grammes. [@1]

Musah pleaded guilty to possessing narcotic drug, and the Asamankese Circuit
Court, presided over by Mr Kofi Akrowiah, sentenced him to 12 years in prison.
[@1] Prosecuting, Police Chief Inspector Joseph Idan told the court the case
showed drug trafficking was on the increase and posed grave danger to the
nation. [@1] The judge commended Adu and said all Ghanaians had to unite to fight
the menace. [@1]
