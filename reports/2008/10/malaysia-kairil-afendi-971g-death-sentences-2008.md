The High Court sentenced Kairil Anuar Abdul Rahman, 34, and Afendi Adam, 28,
to death for trafficking 971 grams of marijuana. [@1] The pair, a restaurant
worker and a painter respectively, had been arrested in March 2002 for selling
the drug. [@1]

Judicial Commissioner Ridwan Ibrahim said the court had no choice but to
impose the death sentences, the mandatory punishment for trafficking, after
the men were found guilty. [@1] Lawyers for the pair were expected to appeal
against the convictions and sentences. [@1]
