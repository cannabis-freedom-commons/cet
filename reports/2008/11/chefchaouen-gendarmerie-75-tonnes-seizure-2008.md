Morocco's Royal Gendarmerie searched two houses in Chefchaouen province, in the
Rif mountains, and seized about six tonnes of ground kif with stems, 400 kg of
kif waste and 82 kg of chira, cannabis resin. [@1] Three suspected traffickers
were arrested during the searches; the report, attributed to an official source
in Rabat, did not publish their identities. [@1] It described the haul as nearly
7.5 tonnes of drugs. [@1] The report gives no date for the searches beyond the
article's 22 November 2008 publication, and says nothing about charges or what
happened to the three detainees afterwards. [@1]

## A Rif trade

The same account said Morocco had seized 35 tonnes of hashish the previous year,
valued at 140 million euros on the European market. [@1] It described cannabis
as cultivated in the Rif since the 15th century and supporting about 800,000
Moroccans, who drew only some US$214 million a year from a trade whose worldwide
turnover it put at US$12 billion. [@1]
