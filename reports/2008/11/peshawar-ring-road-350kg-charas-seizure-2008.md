Officials of the Directorate General of Intelligence and Investigation of the
Federal Board of Revenue, acting on a tip-off about a truck smuggling drugs
from Peshawar to Punjab, set up a picket near Ring Road in Peshawar to intercept
truck RIH-1396. [@1] The driver sped through the picket, officers chased the
truck and found it abandoned on GT Road with the driver gone. [@1] A search
recovered 350 kg of foreign-origin charas, cannabis resin, and 22 kg of
foreign-origin opium. [@1] The opium is not counted in this report's cannabis
figures. [@1]
