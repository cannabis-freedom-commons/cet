## The arrests

Kanagawa prefectural police arrested three people, including 33-year-old
Rikiya Sakai, president of the imported-goods retailer Antsu International in
Osaka's Miyakojima ward, on suspicion of aiding a violation of the Cannabis
Control Law. [@1, 2] The company came under investigation after former Kanto
Gakuin University rugby club members, arrested for growing cannabis, told
police they had bought the seeds through it. [@1]

## The seed trade

Investigators say the three sold 20 cannabis seeds to a 26-year-old man in
Zama, Kanagawa, for 47,600 yen on about 3 November 2007. [@1] Police also
planned to investigate the three over the Customs Law and the Foreign Exchange
and Foreign Trade Control Law. [@1] The firm is suspected of selling seeds
obtained in the Netherlands to 182 people across 42 prefectures. [@1, 2]
