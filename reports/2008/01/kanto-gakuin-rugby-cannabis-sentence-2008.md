## Arrests and sentence

In November 2007 police arrested two members of the Kanto Gakuin University
rugby club on suspicion of growing cannabis where they lived, and twelve
other members of the team later admitted smoking it. [@2, 3, 5] The two had
cultivated the plants from seeds ordered from the website of an Osaka sundry
goods store. [@4] On 25 January 2008 the Yokohama District Court sentenced
both former players, aged 21 and already expelled from the university, to 18
months in prison suspended for three years for cultivation under the Cannabis
Control Law. [@1]

## What the court heard

The two grew 16 plants in the closet of a team-leased apartment in
Kanazawa-ku, Yokohama, between mid-September and 8 November 2007, and admitted
the charges. [@1] The judge said they had made teammates smoke the harvest
for fun, calling the behaviour selfish and irresponsible. [@1] Twelve
team-mates from second to fourth year, including one regular player, smoked
cannabis at the apartment and at a Nagano hotel where the team stayed;
Kanagawa police referred all twelve for joint possession, and Yokohama
prosecutors declined to prosecute them in December 2007. [@1] The coach who
had led the club to six national university titles resigned over the case. [@1]
