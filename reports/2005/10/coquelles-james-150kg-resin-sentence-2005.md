Anthony James, a truck driver from Eyres Monsell in Leicestershire, denied
smuggling 150 kg of cannabis resin into Britain, but a jury at Canterbury Crown
Court found him guilty and he was jailed for six years. [@1] His trailer had
been stopped at the UK Channel Tunnel control zone in France on 28 January
2005, where customs officers found the resin, valued at £322,000, hidden among
six pallets he had driven back from Spain. [@1]

He told customs the pallets belonged to a company in Cambridge and that he was
filling in for another driver called Terry Smith. [@1] Officers found no such
company existed and Smith had not been due to drive that week. [@1] Judge
Anthony Webb told him that without people like him, the trade could not move
drugs through. [@1]
