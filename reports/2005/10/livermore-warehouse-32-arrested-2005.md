## The warehouse raid

Acting on a caller's tip, Livermore police served a search warrant on 29
October 2005 at a warehouse on the 4700 block of Bennett Avenue and found a
large, sophisticated hydroponic indoor grow. [@1, 2] Officers seized over a
thousand plants in various stages of growth and arrested 31 people, most of
them described as paid employees brought in to cultivate and harvest. [@1]
The operation was hailed as one of the largest indoor marijuana busts ever in
Alameda County. [@2]

## Follow-up searches and charges

Livermore police called in the DEA's Oakland office, and follow-up searches
hit a San Leandro home holding cash, cars and motorcycles, four Hayward
warehouses holding about a thousand more plants, and a San Ramon home where
nothing was found. [@1] In total law enforcement reported 32 arrests, over
2,000 plants, six vehicles, two motorcycles, a scissor lift and almost $1
million in cash and bank accounts. [@1] In late November 2005 three men were
charged in federal court with conspiracy to manufacture marijuana, two of them
also with manufacturing and maintaining a drug premises, and one with illegal
currency transactions. [@1, 2] Police said some of the marijuana was grown
for San Francisco Bay Area cannabis clubs. [@1, 2]
