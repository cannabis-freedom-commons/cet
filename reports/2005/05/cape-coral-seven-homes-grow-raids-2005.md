## The raids

On 17 May 2005 the Drug Enforcement Administration announced that its Miami
field division and the Cape Coral Police Department had served search warrants
at seven Cape Coral homes used to cultivate marijuana indoors. [@1] Ten people
were arrested and 622 plants were confiscated, with the Lee County sheriff's
office, the Miami police and the United States attorney's office assisting the
investigation. [@1]

## Charges

Those arrested were charged federally with conspiracy to possess with intent
to distribute more than 100 cannabis plants. [@1] Cape Coral police estimated
the seized marijuana's street value at more than $800,000. [@1] A later
federal appeals opinion describing the same seven-home takedown put the number
seized at 691 plants. [@2]
