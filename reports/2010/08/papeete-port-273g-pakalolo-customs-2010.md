Customs officers of the Papeete brigade checked passengers of the Hawaiki Nui
ferry arriving from the Leeward Islands at Papeete port at 8am on Saturday 7
August 2010. [@1] In the luggage of a passenger from Huahine who had come to
Tahiti to look for work, they found 273 grams of pakalolo, the local name for
cannabis herb, packed in 75 matchboxes, and 2 grams of cannabis resin. [@1]

Customs put the value of the herb and resin at 283,000 CFP francs on the illegal
market. [@1] The passenger told officers he had bought them for 50,000 CFP
francs from an unknown person and meant them for his own consumption, or for
resale if he found no job. [@1]

He was handed to a judicial police officer of the departmental security, the
DSP, and was to answer before the Papeete tribunal for the customs offence and
for the corresponding offence under ordinary criminal law. [@1] Customs said
finding pakalolo when the inter-island schooners arrive had become routine for
its officers in French Polynesia. [@1]
