On 9 August 2010 the Statewide Narcotics Task Force, flying marijuana-eradication
missions over eastern Connecticut with National Guard helicopter support, spotted
cannabis plants on a Canterbury property from the air. [@2] Ground officers went to
41 Raymond Schoolhouse Road, where they found four people at a property whose home
and large two-story barn had been converted into a multi-stage cultivation operation
around a greenhouse. [@1,2]

Officers seized 1,102 marijuana plants in various growth stages, which police valued
at about $1 million, along with $1,600 in cash and cultivation equipment. [@1]
Richard Houghtaling, Thomas Phravixay, Sisouk Phravixay and William Eichin faced
charges including cultivation of marijuana over one kilo and operating a drug factory,
and were held on a combined $150,000 bond for court in Danielson the next day. [@1]
The task force called it one of the largest residential cultivation operations ever
found in Connecticut. [@2]
