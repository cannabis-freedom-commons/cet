A police spokesperson said marijuana cultivation spread over an estimated seven
acres was destroyed at Jursimolu village, about 50 km west of Guwahati. [@1]
H.K. Nath, additional police chief of Kamrup Rural district, said officers
raided the village on specific inputs and were surprised to find marijuana
cultivated on a large scale. [@1] Nath said they destroyed about 2,000 kg of
marijuana grown across seven acres of land. [@1] Authorities destroyed up to
2,000 kg of marijuana on the outskirts of Guwahati in a raid reported as the
breaking of a multi-crore international racket. [@2]

Police said the area was inhabited by illegal Bangladeshi migrant settlers. [@1]
A police official said locals reported that crude marijuana was stacked in sacks
mixed with garlic and jute and transported to Bangladesh using the river route.
[@2] Nath said the case was under investigation and that some drug lord
operating the racket could not be ruled out. [@1] Locals alleged a nexus between
the police and the marijuana farmers because a police outpost was about two
kilometres from the village. [@1]

Nath said officers found people cultivating marijuana in their backyards as well
as on huge tracts close to the river bank, and that the operation would continue
until the cultivation was wiped out. [@2] A kilogram of marijuana sold for about
Rs 5,000. [@1]
