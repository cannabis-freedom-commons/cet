Honiara City police arrested a 47-year-old father and his 17-year-old son on the
morning of 20 January 2010 at the Lunga area of Honiara. [@1] The pair were
arrested over the illegal production of kwaso, a home-brewed alcohol, and the
cultivation of marijuana. [@1]

Inspector Frank Fataolisia, operations manager for Honiara City Police, said
more than 20 officers of the Royal Solomon Islands Police Force and the
Participating Police Force were deployed from Honiara City police station. [@1]
Officers searched the residence, confiscated several items used for producing
kwaso and arrested the 47-year-old man there. [@1] Police found a patch of
marijuana plants next to a nearby residence, uprooted the plants and loaded them
onto waiting police vehicles. [@1] The son was arrested a short time later at
his workplace at Ranadi after police made enquiries about the seized marijuana
plants. [@1]

Both suspects were taken to Honiara City police station, where they were being
interviewed and were assisting the investigation. [@1] Police believed that two
young female accomplices may have escaped shortly before officers arrived at the
location. [@1] Police asked residents around Lunga to contact the Liquor
Licensing Squad or the police communication centre with any information on the
women's whereabouts. [@1]
