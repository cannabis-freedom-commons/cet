The anti-drug section of Côte d'Ivoire's National Gendarmerie seized 520
kilograms of cannabis on 5 May 2010, around noon. [@1] The raid was at
Laminekro, a camp on the coast along the Assinie–Mondoukou road. [@2] The
gendarmerie's airport cell, commanded by Adjudant Djaha, had received an
anonymous tip that suspects had brought a large quantity of drugs to the camp.
[@2]

Captain Kpan Koui, head of the anti-drug section, led officers from the Plateau
and airport cells, the Grand-Bassam gendarmerie brigade and the gendarmerie's
dog unit in the raid. [@2] A search of the site turned up 520 kilograms of
cannabis in total. [@2]

The people who had brought the load fled and no one was arrested. [@2] The
cannabis was taken to the anti-drug section's offices at Plateau and held there
pending the public prosecutor's instructions for its incineration. [@2] The
account came from the gendarmerie's communication service, and the gendarmerie
also published a photograph of the seized load. [@1]
