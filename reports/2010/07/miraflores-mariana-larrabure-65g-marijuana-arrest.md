Police in Miraflores detained former beauty queen Mariana Larrabure, her brother
Eduardo Larrabure, her boyfriend César Belaunde Montero and Rocío Zapata Petrov,
the brother's girlfriend, at her flat. [@1] Municipal serenos had received a
call about a scandal on block 7 of General Cordova Street. [@1] Officers first
thought it was a family argument in department 2-B on the second floor of
building No. 700. [@1]

Police found scattered packets of marijuana in one of the rooms of her flat.
[@1] Police seized 65 grams of marijuana in 15 wrappers at her house in
Miraflores. [@1] At the police station, Eduardo Larrabure blamed César Belaunde
Montero for planting the drug in his sister's flat. [@1]

Because the amount exceeded the amount legally allowed for drug possession, the
case was passed to the crime investigation section of the police station. [@1]
She could be reported for the offence of microcommercialisation of drugs. [@1]
In the afternoon the detainees were taken to the forensic doctor and then to the
police laboratory in Aramburú, San Isidro, for toxicological examinations. [@1]
Under Article 298 of the Penal Code, possessing between 8 and 100 grams of
marijuana carries a maximum prison sentence of seven years. [@1]
