Customs officers from the Faa'a brigade were carrying out a routine check at the
domestic terminal of Tahiti-Faa'a airport on Sunday 18 July 2010. [@1] During
the check their attention was drawn to a passenger arriving from Rurutu who was
trying to get rid of two plastic bags. [@1] A search of the two bags found 1,740
grams of cannabis herb. [@1]

After the customs procedure the man was handed to the gendarmerie brigade in
Faa'a to be brought before the prosecutor's office in Papeete, and a judicial
investigation was opened. [@1] The gendarmerie investigation that followed found
a further 6 kg of cannabis, packed in jerrycans, at the man's home. [@1]
Investigators said it appeared that he had made several trips to sell the
cannabis in Papeete. [@1]
