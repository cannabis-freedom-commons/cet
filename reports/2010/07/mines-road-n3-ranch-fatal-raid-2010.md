Three sheriff's deputies shot dead Jose Penaloza-Soto, 28, while scouting a
marijuana farm in the hills near Mines Road on the eastern edge of Santa Clara
County on the morning of 21 July 2010. [@1] Deputies from the Santa Clara and
Alameda county sheriff's departments were combing the area in a marijuana
eradication operation when the shooting happened at about 11 a.m., and sheriff's
spokesman Sgt. Rick Sung said the Santa Clara deputies were believed to have
fired the shots. [@1] No officers were injured, and the district attorney's
office assigned two investigators to monitor the killing. [@1]

Penaloza-Soto, a landscaper from East Palo Alto, had been guarding an illegal
18,000-plant farm in the Livermore hills. [@3] The weapon he brandished was a
pellet gun, which deputies said he refused to drop when ordered. [@2,3] After
the shooting the sheriff's department suspended wilderness eradication missions
until the investigation was complete. [@2]
