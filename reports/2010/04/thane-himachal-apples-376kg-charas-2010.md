Police in Thane stumbled on 376 kg of charas packed in 12 cartons marked
'Himachal Apples' when a Tavera that sped past a checkpoint was stopped on
6 April 2010. [@1] Chunnilal Kumar, 32, and Pawan Wadhwa, 33, were arrested
with the vehicle. [@1]

The load had travelled from Kullu hidden in a truck carrying an apple
consignment, and following its trail led to the arrest of the truck's owner,
Rakesh Awasthi of Kullu. [@1] Two more arrests brought the total to five,
though the alleged kingpin of the transaction remained at large and unnamed.
[@1] At 1 lakh rupees a kg, police valued the haul at about 3.76 crore
rupees in the Mumbai market, a 376 kg figure a later account repeated.
[@1,2]

Mumbai's anti-narcotics wing led the investigation with assistance from Kullu
police, who put a resident of Jaan village near Manikaran under surveillance
over links to the highland growing areas of the Malana valley. [@1] Kullu's
police chief said the district had seized 210 kg of charas and about 20 kg of
opium the previous year, and 80 kg of charas since January. [@1] The state
police chief later described a 336 kg Mumbai charas catch tracked back to the
Kullu valley. [@3]
