Six Malians tried to move 1,718 kilograms of cannabis, known locally as yamba,
by train towards Senegal. [@1] The research section of Mali's Judicial
Investigation Brigade (BIJ) arrested them over the consignment. [@1] The seizure
was made on a Friday, as the men were about to load the yamba onto a rail wagon.
[@1] The cannabis was pressed into bricks, packed in black sachets and buried in
sacks of baobab powder, sweet peas and cocoa, goods much prized in Senegal,
where the shipment was headed. [@1]

Each sack holding yamba also contained a talisman bearing Arabic writing and
other cabalistic signs. [@1] The Malian report said the charms were visibly
meant to bring the operation success by mystical means. [@1]

The day before the seizure, Mali's justice minister, Maharafa Traoré, launched
an integrated programme against drug trafficking and organised crime. [@1] The
launch was attended by the UNODC executive director, Antonio Maria Costa, and
the ambassadors of Denmark, Luxembourg and Italy. [@1]
