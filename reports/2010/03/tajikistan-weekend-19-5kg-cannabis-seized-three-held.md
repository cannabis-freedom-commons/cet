Tajikistan's Interior Ministry officers detained 37-year-old Hamadoni district
resident Zarifkhon Malikov, a man previously convicted of unlawful storage of a
firearm, in Hamadoni district on Sunday while carrying out operative-search
measures. [@1] Officers detained 19-year-old Istaravshan resident Dzhumaboy
Nosirov on the premises of the Istaravshan communications hub. [@1] Officers
detained 62-year-old Dzhumabek Khodiev in the city of Dushanbe. [@1]

At Malikov's place of residence, officers discovered and seized 18 kg of hashish
during the inspection of the premises. [@1] During a personal search of Nosirov,
officers discovered and seized 2 kg of heroin. [@1] In Khodiev's personal
effects, officers discovered and seized 1.5 kg of marijuana. [@1] The Interior
Ministry said its internal-affairs officers had identified seven separate
instances of unlawful drug trafficking during that weekend. [@1] The ministry
put the weekend's combined seizures at more than 20 kg of drugs removed from
unlawful circulation across Tajikistan. [@1]

All three detentions and all three weights are the Interior Ministry's figures,
provided to Asia-Plus. [@1]
