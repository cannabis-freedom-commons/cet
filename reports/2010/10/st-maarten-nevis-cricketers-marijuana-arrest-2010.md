Three Nevis cricketers, Akito Willet, Jason Campbell and Joseph 'Junie'
Williams, were arrested in St Maarten on Saturday 2 October 2010. [@2] The Nevis
Cricket Association said the police arrested them for smoking and possession of
marijuana. [@2] They were in St Maarten with the Nevis team for the Leeward
Islands NAGICO one-day series. [@2] They were deported that same day and barred
from re-entering St Maarten for three years. [@2]

The association met in emergency session at Elquemedo Willet Park after the
reports reached Nevis. [@2] It asked team manager Malcolm Ramsey for a written
report on the incident. [@2] The players were to have a hearing before the
disciplinary committee once the report arrived. [@2]

The disciplinary committee met at the chambers of its chairman, Gregory
Hardtman, on the afternoon of 6 January 2011. [@1] Hardtman read aloud a letter
from St Maarten's commissioner of police, C. M. John. [@1] He found that the
three had no case to answer under the association's code of conduct. [@1] The
letter said the three had been stopped on Front Street because police found no
documents proving they were on the island legally. [@1] It added that there was
no mention of drugs being found or used. [@1] The players said they had
committed no wrong but that their reputations had been badly tarnished. [@1] The
association sent the letter to the Leeward Islands Cricket Board, leaving the
three free to play under both bodies. [@1]
