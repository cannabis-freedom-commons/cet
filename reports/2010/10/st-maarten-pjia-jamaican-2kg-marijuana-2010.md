Customs officers arrested a 35-year-old Jamaican passenger, identified only by
the initials J.W.G., on Saturday 2 October 2010. [@1] He had tried to smuggle
about two kilograms of marijuana into St. Maarten through Princess Juliana
International Airport (PJIA). [@1] The drugs were packaged and stashed in soft
drinks that were boxed in a case. [@1]

Local Chief Customs Officer Anthony Doran said the drugs had once again been
hidden in "an ingenious way" and that smugglers were lately using complicated
and professional methods to try to bring drugs through the island's airport and
harbor. [@1] He said that although the department had very little staff,
officers were giving their maximum effort to catch people trying to pass through
the controls with illicit drugs, working to keep those drugs off the streets and
out of the lives of children. [@1]

Customs said that because the investigation was ongoing, further information
about the case could not be released at that time. [@1]
