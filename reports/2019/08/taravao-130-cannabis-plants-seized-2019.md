Between Wednesday 21 and Friday 23 August 2019, gendarmes from the brigade at
Taravao, reinforced by officers of the mobile gendarmerie squadron 37/4 Troyes
and an antenna of the GIGN, carried out a judicial police operation against
illicit cannabis cultivation in the commune of Taiarapu-Est. [@2] The operation
was aimed at crops of pakalolo, the local term for cannabis. [@1] The
gendarmerie said the people involved would answer for the offences before the
courts in the near future. [@1]

Investigations led officers to two different sites. [@1] At the two sites
officers found 130 cannabis plants. [@1] They also seized 2 kg of dried cannabis
heads and 1.5 kg of dried cannabis, 3.5 kg in total. [@1] Alongside the plants
and dried cannabis, officers took 121 sticks and small equipment used to package
the product. [@1]

On the instructions of the Papeete prosecutor's office, all the seized products
and equipment were destroyed. [@2]
