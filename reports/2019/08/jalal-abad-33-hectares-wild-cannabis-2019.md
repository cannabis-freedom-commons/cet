Police in the Jalal-Abad region of Kyrgyzstan were destroying wild cannabis as part of the MAK-2019 anti-drugs operation, the regional police department said on 27 August 2019. [@1,2] Officers had destroyed at least 1.3 tonnes of cannabis growing on two hectares of land, and mowed about one hectare of wild cannabis in Mailuu-Suu, after similar measures in the Toguz-Toro, Toktogul, Ala-Buka and Nooken districts. [@1,2]

In total about 33 hectares had been mowed and about 18.5 tonnes of the drug burned, according to the police. [@1,2] No suspects or arrests were named in the reports. [@1,2]
