Algerian gendarmerie units in the Wilaya of El Oued, a province of Algeria,
seized 250 kilograms of cannabis near the country's border with Tunisia,
according to Mosaïque Fm, citing its correspondent in Algeria. [@1] The same
operation led to the arrest of four individuals described as involved in an
international drug-trafficking network. [@1] A wilaya is Algeria's main
administrative division. [@1]

The gendarmerie said the action foiled an attempt to introduce a quantity of
drugs across the Tunisia-Algeria frontier. [@1] According to the security units,
the cannabis was found close to the Tunisian border within El Oued province.
[@1] Nessma TV reported the same seizure of 250 kilograms of cannabis near the
Tunisian border. [@2] Its version also reported four arrests linked to an
international drug-trafficking network. [@2]
