Police in Río Blanco raided a house in the Luis Alfonso Velásquez neighbourhood
on Sunday 31 March 2019 and arrested a man accused of selling drugs. [@1] Police
went to the address to make the arrest and detained only Eliel Bismarck Rizo.
[@1] Police described Rizo as a distributor of drugs in the town's
neighbourhoods who moved his internal drug trade into a white car and two
motorcycles to escape justice and expand it. [@1]

Officers seized 130 grams of green plant material. [@1] They also seized 160,000
córdobas in national and foreign currency, plus several objects whose origin
they could not establish. [@1]

The house was lived in by three people: the owner of the drug and two children
aged 6 and 7. [@1] Police sources said the three occupants were tenants of the
house. [@1] Unofficial sources said those involved had close ties with other
dealers in Río Blanco, who came to the house at all hours to distribute
marijuana. [@1]

Rizo was held in the cells of the Río Blanco National Police while the
investigation continued. [@1]
