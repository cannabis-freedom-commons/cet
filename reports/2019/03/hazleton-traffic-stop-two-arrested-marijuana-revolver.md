Two city men were taken into custody late Saturday after Hazleton police stopped
their vehicle. [@1] They were Teshawn Devante Nelson, 23, and Deivon Amir
Barlow, 24. [@1] Officers pulled over a black Audi at Muir Avenue and Cedar
Street about 11:50 p.m. Saturday, identifying Barlow as the driver and Nelson as
the back-seat passenger. [@1] The car was stopped because its registration had
expired in November 2018 and its inspection in January 2018, and police found
Barlow had a suspended driver's license. [@1] Officers detected a marijuana odor
from inside the car and ordered both men out. [@1] A pat-down found Nelson
carrying a fully loaded silver North American Arms .22-caliber Magnum revolver.
[@1]

A search of the Audi produced a burnt marijuana joint in the ashtray and two
jars of pot. [@1] Police counted and confiscated $9,800 in cash and $20,400 in
counterfeit $100 bills. [@1] Police logged all the evidence and kept the money,
documented and placed in evidence for safekeeping. [@2] Both men were taken into
custody and faced possession and other related charges. [@3]

Nelson was a felon prohibited from having a firearm. [@1] He was arraigned
Sunday morning before District Judge Ferris Webby Sr. on counts of illegal
possession of a firearm, carrying a firearm without a license, possession of a
controlled substance and possession of drug paraphernalia. [@1] Nelson was
locked up at the county jail after he could not post bail set at $75,000 cash.
[@1] Barlow was released, with police filing charges that included possession of
marijuana and possession of drug paraphernalia. [@1] An investigation was
continuing into who owned the gun Nelson had in his possession. [@1]
