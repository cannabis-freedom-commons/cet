The anti-drug brigade (STUP) of Anosy arrested a Chinese national at
Ankazomanga, a neighbourhood of Antananarivo, on Monday 4 March 2019 on
suspicion of drug consumption and possession and of trafficking protected
animals. [@1] The arrest followed a tip to police that a Chinese man in a hotel
in the neighbourhood regularly bought and used cannabis. [@1] The suspect is 39
and had already been jailed in 2016, for illegal possession of weapons and
trafficking of tortoises. [@1]

A search of his hotel room found 21.6 grams of cannabis powder and a bag holding
24 live tortoises. [@1] Madagascar Tribune reported the same arrest, saying
police found 21 grams of cannabis and tortoises in bags, apparently ready for
export. [@2]

The suspect was transferred to the environment ministry to be questioned about
the rare animals seized in his room and to identify the network and any
accomplices. [@1]
