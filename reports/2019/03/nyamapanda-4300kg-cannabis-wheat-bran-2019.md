On 4 March 2019 Zimbabwe Revenue Authority officers seized 4,300 kg of cannabis at Nyamapanda Border Post in Mudzi District, Mashonaland East. [@1] The cannabis was concealed in a consignment of wheat bran sacks described as in transit to South Africa, wrapped in white and blue plastic. [@1] ZIMRA's K9 team picked out the contraband before the truck was offloaded; the driver disappeared, and the consignment and the cannabis were seized. [@1]

The World Customs Organization's regional alert put the street value of the cannabis at US$4.3 million. [@1]
