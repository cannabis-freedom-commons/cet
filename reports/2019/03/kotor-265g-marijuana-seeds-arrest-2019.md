Police in Kotor, Montenegro arrested M.U., a 27-year-old local man, after a
search of the area around his house found 265 grams of marijuana that he
allegedly hid and intended for street traffic. [@1] The Anti-Drug Department of
the Montenegrin Police, acting on an order from the investigating judge of the
High Court in Podgorica, first searched the apartment and other premises used by
M.U. in Kotor. [@1] Officers then conducted a wider search of the place in
M.U.'s presence. [@1]

In that wider search, above the house in an old stone fence, they found and
seized a bag with several packages of dry plant matter suspected to be marijuana
along with a large number of cannabis seeds, the two together weighing 265
grams. [@1] Police said the drugs belonged to M.U. and were intended for further
street sale in Kotor. [@2]

M.U. was brought before the state prosecutor at the VDT in Podgorica with a
criminal complaint, on suspicion of unauthorised production, possession and
distribution of narcotic drugs. [@1]
