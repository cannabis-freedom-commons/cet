Iu Moana Pope, a 27-year-old inmate from Iva on Savaii, pleaded guilty to
possession of narcotics before Justice Leilani Tuala-Warren. [@1] Prison guards
found 291 marijuana cigarettes in the possession of the inmate, who had been
jailed in 2015 for attempted rape. [@1] The offence was committed while Pope was
already serving a six-year term for another crime. [@1] Justice Tuala-Warren
convicted Pope of possession of narcotics and sentenced him to two years'
imprisonment, to be served cumulatively with his existing sentence. [@1]

The judge said Pope told the court that another inmate had told him to go and
collect the drugs. [@1] Pope said someone threw the drugs over the prison gate
to him, and a co-defendant pleaded not guilty and was due for a separate
hearing. [@1] The judge called the quantity of 291 marijuana cigarettes an
aggravating factor. [@1] She said money found in the same bucket showed Pope had
been selling the cigarettes inside the prison. [@1] She set a starting point of
three years' imprisonment. [@1] A third of the term was deducted for Pope's
early guilty plea. [@1]
