Diyarbakır's provincial police narcotics branch searched a vehicle in the city
on 12 March 2019. [@2] The branch said the search was part of work against the
sale and use of drugs in the city. [@2] Officers found 165 kilograms of kubar
esrar, a coarse cannabis preparation, in the vehicle. [@2] One person detained
over the seizure was processed at the police station and then arrested by the
court he was referred to. [@2]

Anadolu Agency reported the operations in English as the seizure of more than
215 kilograms of marijuana in eastern Turkey. [@1] The agency's account said two
suspects were remanded over drug trafficking. [@1]

Van police's narcotics branch said officers searched a parked kamyonet on Emir
Evliya Street in İpekyolu. [@3] They found 51.35 kilograms of heroin in 100
packages in sacks in the boot, with a sniffer dog used in the search. [@3]
