A 32-year-old man whose home address was given as Wisteria Drive in Freeport,
Grand Bahama, was arrested on Saturday 30 March 2019 and taken into police
custody after he was found with a quantity of suspected marijuana in his
possession. [@1] The arrest took place in Freeport on the island of Grand Bahama
in The Bahamas. [@1]

Officers of the Mobile Division were on patrol in the area of Seahorse Road in
Freeport when they encountered the man. [@1] Officers stopped a grey Chevrolet
Impala that was being driven by a lone male driver in the Seahorse Road area.
[@1] Officers searched the Chevrolet Impala after stopping it on Seahorse Road
during the patrol. [@1] Officers discovered a quantity of suspected marijuana
inside the vehicle during the search of the Impala. [@1]

The lone male driver was arrested and taken into police custody in connection
with the suspected marijuana. [@1] Police said that their investigations into
the suspected marijuana possession were continuing. [@1]
