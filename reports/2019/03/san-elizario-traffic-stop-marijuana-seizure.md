Two men were arrested on Wednesday during a traffic stop in San Elizario, Texas,
on suspicion of possessing methamphetamine and marijuana. [@1] The San Elizario
Marshal’s Office conducted the stop around 12:56 p.m. [@1] The men were the
driver, Raul Enrique Garcia, and his passenger, Antonio Perez. [@1] KVIA
identified them as 38-year-old Perez and 56-year-old Garcia, and said both were
booked into the El Paso County Detention Facility on charges of Manufacture and
Delivery of a Controlled Substance and Possession of Marijuana. [@2]

Investigators reported 57.4 pounds (about 26.04 kg) of marijuana and 77.4 pounds
of methamphetamine from the traffic stop. [@1] A search of the vehicle found 48
bundles of marijuana and 47 bundles of methamphetamine. [@2] Officials put the
investigation’s total at 77 pounds of methamphetamine and 57 pounds of
marijuana. [@2]

The stop was on the 13200 block of Socorro Road after the driver of a Ford
Expedition failed to yield the right of way to oncoming traffic. [@2] After the
arrests, the El Paso County Sheriff’s Office Narcotics Unit executed a search
warrant at a residence on Oscar Chacon in San Elizario and found an additional
nine ounces of marijuana. [@2]
