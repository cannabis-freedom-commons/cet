Gendarmería Nacional detained three men in Jujuy province and seized 63.06
kilograms of marijuana that was being transported in a vehicle. [@1] In the
early morning, officers of the Chalican section of Escuadrón 60 San Pedro
stopped a Volkswagen Vento on National Route 34 at kilometre 1,212 as it
travelled from San Ramón de la Nueva Orán in Salta to the city of Salta,
carrying two Argentine citizens. [@1]

A Renault Megane stopped when its occupants saw the uniformed officers, and they
got out and fled into an adjoining field, leaving the driver apprehended. [@1]
Searching the vehicle in the presence of witnesses, the gendarmes found three
backpacks on the back seat containing 71 packages of cannabis sativa, with a
total weight of 63 kilograms 60 grams. [@1]

The Federal Prosecutor's Office and Jujuy's Federal Courts No. 2 and No. 1
directed the seizure of the drugs and the detention of the three Argentine
citizens, because the first vehicle served as a puntero, or lookout, warning of
possible force checks. [@1]
