National Police officers detained two people in Bique, a sector of the district
of Arraiján, one of them Panamanian and the other a foreign national. [@1] At
the time they were detained, the two people were travelling together in a taxi
that was coming from Panama. [@1] The detention and seizure followed a
surveillance operation that the National Police had been carrying out in the
area. [@1]

During the operation, which took place in the early hours of Friday, officers
seized a total of 24 packages of material described as suspected marijuana. [@1]
After the seizure in the Bique sector, the two people remained detained. [@2]

Following the seizure, both detainees were placed at the disposal of the Public
Prosecutor's Office for investigation. [@1] The prosecutor's office is carrying
out investigations in order to establish responsibility for the 24 packages.
[@1] The National Police added that it would continue carrying out operations to
prevent the sale of illegal substances. [@1]
