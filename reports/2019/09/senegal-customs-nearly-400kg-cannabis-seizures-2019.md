Senegalese customs announced the seizure of nearly 400 kilograms of cannabis on
Sunday 15 and Monday 16 September 2019 in the southeast and centre of the
country. [@1] The driver caught at Moussala was immediately arrested for the
investigation. [@1] Two more people were arrested after the bus seizure at
Kaolack. [@1]

At Moussala in the southeast, officers found 97 kilograms of cannabis in a 4x4
arriving from Mali. [@1] The 4x4 was registered in Mauritania and driven by a
Senegalese national. [@1] At Ndoffane in Kaolack department, customs intercepted
100 kilograms of cannabis and 300 grams of pure cannabis resin. [@1] At the
Serigne Bassirou Mbacke bridge in Kaolack, officers seized 12.5 kilograms from a
public bus arriving from Casamance. [@1] At Karang and Gossas in Fatick region,
officers seized 183 kilograms along with a vehicle and a cart. [@1]

Customs estimated the Moussala drugs at 6,790,000 CFA francs and the 4x4
carrying them at five million. [@1] The Kaolack subdivision estimated its drugs
plus concealment and transport means at 78 million CFA francs. [@1] Customs
valued the Ndoffane interception at 66 million CFA francs. [@1] The seized bus
and its drugs were valued at 12 million CFA francs altogether. [@1]
