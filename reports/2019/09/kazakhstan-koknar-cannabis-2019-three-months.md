Police in Kazakhstan seized four tonnes of marijuana and 198 kilograms of
hashish in the first three months of Operation Koknar, the Interior Ministry
said. [@1] Koknar is an annual campaign held from June to October to disrupt
drug crime. [@1] Officers found 23 plantations of "Indian hemp" during aerial
surveys of hard-to-reach and remote areas. [@1] About 10 tonnes of narcotic
plants were destroyed on the detected cannabis crops. [@1]

The ministry put the combined three-month drug haul at 16.7 tonnes. [@1] The
same statement said the drugs seized since the operation began included 8.7 kg
of heroin. [@1] The ministry said a priority of the operation was detecting and
stopping the sale of synthetic drugs. [@1]
