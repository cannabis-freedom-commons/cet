GNR officers in Torres Vedras detained the 38-year-old owner of a cannabis
plantation on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@1] He was undergoing first
judicial questioning at the Torres Vedras court in the Lisbon district. [@1] The
detachment commander said he had no criminal record for drug trafficking but
evidence suggested growing and using cannabis had been his way of life for more
than a year. [@1] The case remained under investigation by the Torres Vedras
GNR. [@1]

Searches of the suspect's home, a greenhouse and adjoining land in
A-dos-Cunhados seized 66 cannabis plants, mobile phones and materials covering
germination, treatment, drying and packaging. [@1] Officers also seized 47 kg of
cannabis leaves in the drying phase, which the GNR equated to at least 19,000
doses. [@1] The GNR put the potential profit from the production at about
350,000 euros. [@2]

Correio da Manhã likewise reported that the detainee held 19,000 doses
corresponding to 47 kg of cannabis leaves in the drying phase. [@3] The
commander said the seized material indicated a system set up to produce in large
quantities for trafficking, since the quantities were far too large for the
suspect's own consumption. [@1]
