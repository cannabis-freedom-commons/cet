Gendarmes of the La Trinité company investigated a local drug trafficking ring
for several days before launching an operation on Tuesday 10 September 2019.
[@2] The Sainte-Marie brigade, the research brigade and the gendarmerie's
surveillance and intervention platoon took part. [@1] At 6am, supported by a
mobile gendarmerie squadron from Luçon and two dogs, the gendarmes arrested four
suspects aged between 30 and 35 who were in a home. [@3]

At the home the gendarmes seized 85 kilograms of cannabis herb packed in bales,
a .38 special handgun, two vehicles, hi-fi equipment and 3,500 euros in cash.
[@3]

After police custody, the four were tried in immediate appearance, a fast-track
hearing, at the criminal court of Fort-de-France. [@3] They were convicted and
sentenced to prison terms ranging from six months suspended to four years firm.
[@2] Committal warrants were issued against two of them, who were held at Ducos.
[@3]
