A Dominican man was fined more than $900 after cannabis was found in his
possession on 19 June 2018. [@1] He was 21-year-old Bradley Bell, a trucker of
an undisclosed local address, charged with possession of 24 grams of cannabis.
[@1] Bell entered a guilty plea when he appeared before Magistrate Ayanna
Baptiste-DaBreo. [@1] The BVI Beacon reported that the defendant was from
Dominica and had been living in the Virgin Islands for 11 years. [@2] Addressing
the court, he said: "I am sorry, it won’t happen again." [@1]

The magistrate said she did not believe a custodial sentence was warranted. [@1]
The court considered his young age, remorse, good character and early guilty
plea as mitigating factors. [@1] Magistrate DaBreo fined him $950, to be paid by
23 December. [@1] He faces two months at Her Majesty’s Prison if he fails to
pay. [@1] The BVI Beacon reported that the magistrate applied new Eastern
Caribbean Supreme Court sentencing guidelines. [@2] She started from a fine of
$1,200, reduced it to $950 and gave Bell three months to pay. [@2] The defence
had argued for a noncustodial sentence and a $300 fine. [@2]

The court heard that Bell and his friends were caught by police with the drug at
Long Bay, Beef Island. [@1] They were charged, but after Bell accepted
responsibility the Crown offered no evidence against the others and their
charges were dismissed. [@1] The magistrate found Bell was not part of a
sophisticated operation and deemed his role of lesser significance. [@2]
