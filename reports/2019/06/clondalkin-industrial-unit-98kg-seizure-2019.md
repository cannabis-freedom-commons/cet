Revenue Customs officers, the Garda National Drugs and Organised Crime Bureau
and gardaí from Security and Intelligence in west Dublin ran a joint
intelligence-led operation on 20 June 2019 against organised crime groups
operating between jurisdictions. [@1,2] Customs officers seized two crates
holding about 98 kilograms of herbal cannabis, valued at €1.96 million subject
to analysis, at an industrial unit in Clondalkin. [@1]

Three men aged 57, 49 and 32 were arrested at the scene and detained under
section 2 of the Criminal Justice (Drug Trafficking) Act 1996 at Clondalkin
and Ballyfermot stations. [@1,2] A follow-up search with the PSNI in Northern
Ireland led to a woman's arrest and the seizure of about half a kilogram of
cocaine valued at €35,000. [@1]
