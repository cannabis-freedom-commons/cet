Gendarmes from the Poya brigade in New Caledonia carried out a search in the
commune on 3 June 2019. [@1] The search led to the seizure of about 307 cannabis
seedlings and twelve cannabis plants. [@1] The newspaper presented the search as
part of the gendarmerie's continuing drive against cannabis trafficking. [@1]

The seedlings were counted separately from the twelve plants, and their number
was given as approximate. [@1] A photograph of the seizure published with the
report was credited to the gendarmerie. [@1]
