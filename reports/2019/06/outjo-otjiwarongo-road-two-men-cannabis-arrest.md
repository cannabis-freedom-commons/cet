Two men were arrested on Thursday night on suspicion of possessing cannabis on
the Outjo-Otjiwarongo road during a police operation. [@1] Inspector Maureen
Mbeha, a spokesperson for the police in the Otjozondjupa region, said the two
suspects, aged 22 and 27 respectively, were arrested at a mobile police
roadblock on the road at about 21:30 on Thursday. [@1] At the roadblock, people
and vehicles were searched randomly. [@1] The roadblock was set up as part of
‘Operation Kalahari Desert’. [@1] Mbeha gave the account in an interview with
Nampa on Friday. [@1]

“Their vehicle was also stopped and upon the search, we found several parcels of
cannabis stashed in bags inside their vehicle and we arrested them,” Mbeha said.
[@1] The suspects were arrested after the parcels were found inside their
vehicle. [@1] The cannabis was confiscated by police. [@1]

The two suspects were expected to appear in the Outjo Magistrate’s Court. [@1]
Police said investigations were continuing. [@1]
