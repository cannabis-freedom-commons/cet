Four people were detained in São Tomé das Letras, in Minas Gerais, on 21 June
2019 on suspicion of an environmental crime after residents reported them
spray-painting buildings and other sites in the town. [@1] Military Police said
at least 10 public and private properties were defaced. [@1] The local report
said buildings in the town had been tagged with the initials KOP19 overnight.
[@2]

One man admitted the painting; police said he had previous records for theft,
robbery and other graffiti, and he was with another man and two women, who were
also detained. [@1] The man who admitted the tagging was 43 and lived in São
Paulo. [@2] Police said information passed on through a social network led them
to him. [@2] Police found four cans of paint, five marijuana joints and 392
reais in cash with the group, and the material was seized. [@1] One paint can
was in the car the man was using and three more were found where the group was
staying. [@2]

The four suspects were taken with the seized material to the Civil Police
station. [@1] The local report, credited to the Military Police press office,
said the four were handed over at the disposal of the courts. [@2]
