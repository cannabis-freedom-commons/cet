Afghan Special Forces killed seven Taliban fighters during an operation in
Sabari district of Khost province, in what was announced as a paired operation
with a second action in Paktiya. [@1] Military sources said the Special Forces
wounded one Taliban fighter during an operation in Ahmadabad district of Paktiya
province. [@1] The Persian-language edition of the same outlet, citing security
officials, confirmed that one Taliban fighter was wounded in the Ahmadabad
district operation. [@2]

During the Ahmadabad operation the Special Forces destroyed 500 pounds of
hashish, about 226.8 kilograms, and 120 pounds of pure urea, the military
sources said. [@1] The outlet's Persian-language edition described the material
destroyed in the district as 225 kilograms of charas, the resin of the cannabis
plant, together with 54 kilograms of urea. [@2] A separate passage in the
Persian account put the destroyed narcotics in the Paktia operation at 225
kilograms. [@2]

The two operations were reported together as Afghan Special Forces actions in
Khost and Paktiya. [@1]
