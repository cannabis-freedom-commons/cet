Alson Quashie, a 56-year-old farmer from Carrot Bay on Tortola, was arrested on
9 June 2017 after police searched his home. [@2] Officers found marijuana in the
house, including behind his refrigerator and on his bed. [@2] He led police to
an uphill farm just over a mile away, where they found 220 marijuana plants.
[@1] A wooden hut nearby held more marijuana and marijuana seeds. [@1] The
prosecution alleged he told police he sold marijuana, kept some for personal use
and gave some away. [@1] He allegedly told police he had been farming marijuana
for 10 years. [@1]

On 12 June 2017 he pleaded guilty to cultivating the 220 plants. [@1] Quashie, a
native of St Vincent and the Grenadines, also pleaded guilty to possessing 78.3
grams of marijuana. [@1] He pleaded not guilty to possession of a controlled
drug with intent to supply. [@2] Senior Magistrate Tamia Richards remanded him,
saying cultivation of 220 plants was 'a lot of cannabis' and very serious. [@2]

On 14 December 2018 Senior Magistrate Tamia Richards found him guilty of
possession of a controlled drug with intent to supply and sentenced him to three
and a half years in prison. [@4] She imposed three years for the
intent-to-supply charge and six months for possessing 78.3 grams. [@3] The
sentences were to run concurrently. [@3] She said the police officers who
testified were 'unshaken' and 'believable'. [@3] On 26 June 2019 Governor
Augustus J. U. Jaspert ordered his deportation. [@4] The order gave him seven
days to leave the territory and required him to remain out unless he appealed.
[@4]
