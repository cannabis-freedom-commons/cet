Civil Police in São Tomé das Letras, Minas Gerais, arrested at least 23 people
between dawn and the morning of 5 June 2019 in an operation against drug
trafficking, some of them caught in the act. [@1] Hoje em Dia reported that the
operation ended with 23 people arrested on Wednesday 5 June. [@2] Police issued
21 arrest warrants, with some suspects still fugitive, and officers from Três
Corações served 23 search-and-seizure warrants in São Tomé. [@1] The operation
was named Pirâmide after one of the city's best-known tourist sites, which
police said was used as a place for selling drugs. [@1] The second account
describes that landmark as a stone house built on top of the mountain where
people watch the sunset. [@2]

In some houses officers found the suspects and drugs. [@1] The drugs found
included marijuana, crack, cocaine paste and tabs of LSD, along with firearms,
precision scales and money. [@1] Police said the traffickers sold marijuana,
cocaine, crack and synthetic drugs including LSD and ecstasy, and that many of
them belonged to or backed the criminal faction Primeiro Comando da Capital
(PCC). [@2] The responsible delegate said the investigation had begun about one
year and seven months earlier. [@1] He said those arrested were responsible for
selling, storing or transporting the drugs, and that a group of more than 20
people operated only in the city, selling to tourists and residents. [@1] The
operation was supported by police dogs and a drone, and those arrested were to
be taken to the police station in Três Corações. [@1]
