The Narcotics Control Bureau and the Armed Police Force destroyed cannabis
plants cultivated on the outskirts of Kathmandu. [@1] An 80-person team
destroyed cannabis illegally grown by local people in Okhreni, Chilaune,
Gaurigaun, Majhgaun, Kune and Khatritole of Gokarneshwor Municipality-1. [@1]
Officials said it took the team three days to clear cannabis grown across around
5,800 ropanis of land, about 295 hectares. [@1]

On June 8 the bureau had destroyed cannabis on more than 1,700 ropanis in the
same municipality, about 86.5 hectares. [@1] Police said they destroy reported
cannabis crops and warn farmers not to repeat the illicit cultivation. [@1] In
some cases drug traffickers were found to have been involved in cannabis
cultivation. [@1] They lure farmers to cultivate cannabis for food crops
promising better returns. [@1] Drug traffickers were reported to make advance
payments to farmers to facilitate cultivation of the illegal plants. [@1]

According to the bureau, cannabis cultivation and its natural growth are posing
a threat to its anti-drug initiative. [@1] The trend of cannabis cultivation was
described as on the rise in Nepal as people are lured into making quick money.
[@1] It was described as gaining ground on the fringes of the Kathmandu Valley.
[@1] Security personnel were described as destroying cannabis plants to
discourage drug traders. [@1] The destruction was also aimed at controlling drug
abuse among local youths. [@1]
