Christopher Staton Richardson, 35, of Garner, worked at the Taco Bell on Highway
42 in Cleveland, North Carolina, and was accused of selling drugs at the
restaurant and from his home. [@1] He was arrested on June 7 after a two-month
undercover investigation by the Johnston County Sheriff's Office Narcotics
Division. [@1] Captain Jeff Caldwell said Richardson sold marijuana twice to
officers from his nearby home on N. Roundabout Drive. [@1]

Officials said 48 grams of marijuana, Xanax and a handgun were found at that
home. [@1] Richardson was charged with more than a dozen felony warrants. [@1]
Possession with intent to sell or deliver marijuana was among the charges. [@1]
He was booked into the Johnston County Jail under a $300,000 bond. [@1]
