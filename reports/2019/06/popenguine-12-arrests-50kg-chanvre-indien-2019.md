The Senegalese gendarmerie arrested 12 people during operations to secure the
activities of the Marial pilgrimage at Popenguine. [@1] Officers seized 50 kg of
chanvre indien, the French term for cannabis. [@1] They also took an unspecified
quantity of alcoholic beverages. [@1]

Leral.net reported that officers seized 50 kilogrammes of chanvre indien and a
significant quantity of alcoholic drinks during the pilgrimage security
operation. [@2] Tambacounda.info reported that the gendarmerie made twelve
arrests and seized 50 kilogrammes of chanvre indien during the security
operation for the Popenguine Marial pilgrimage. [@3] The information came from
RTS, the Senegalese broadcaster. [@1] The operation relied on the deployment of
750 gendarmes assigned to ensure the safety of the pilgrims and their property.
[@1]
