The French Navy surveillance frigate Ventôse detected and boarded two boats
engaged in drug trafficking off the Antilles on 15 and 17 November 2019. [@1]
Thirteen people apprehended in the operation were handed to the judicial
authorities. [@1] The investigation was entrusted to OCRTIS, the central office
for suppressing illicit drug trafficking, and nine traffickers had already been
sentenced to prison terms. [@1]

Nearly 300 kilograms of cocaine and cannabis were intercepted in total,
according to the Martinique prefecture. [@1] The drugs were intended for
criminal networks in Guadeloupe and Martinique, the prefecture said. [@1] A
second account of the prefecture's communiqué confirmed the 13 arrests and the
two boardings on 15 and 17 November. [@2]

The operation was carried out under the direction of the prefect of Martinique,
the government's delegate for state action at sea, in cooperation with the
agencies operating at sea, the prefecture said. [@1]
