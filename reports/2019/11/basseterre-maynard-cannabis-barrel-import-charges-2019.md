Paul Maynard of Sandy Point, St Kitts, was charged by both the police and the
Customs and Excise Department with cannabis offences. [@2] Police charged him on
15 November 2019 with possession of cannabis, possession of cannabis with intent
to supply and importation of cannabis. [@1] The Customs and Excise Department
separately charged him with making a false declaration to customs, importation
of cannabis and concealment of imported goods. [@1]

The barrel he went to clear at customs on 14 November 2019 was found to contain
a quantity of cannabis concealed in a bottle. [@2] A subsequent police search of
his premises found another quantity of cannabis. [@1] Maynard was granted bail
on 19 November 2019 in the sum of $25,000 with two sureties. [@1] His bail
conditions required him to report to the Sandy Point Police Station every
Friday. [@1]

He is scheduled to appear before the Basseterre Magistrate’s Court on 6 January
2020. [@1]
