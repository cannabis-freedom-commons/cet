On Wednesday 27 November 2019, gendarmes of the Taravao brigade and of the
Surveillance and Intervention Detachment of mobile gendarmerie unit EGM 21/3
Mont-Saint-Aignan intervened at several cannabis cultivation sites at Teahupoo,
Toahotu and Taravao, on Tahiti's Presqu'île. [@1] The action was a roaming
nomadisation mission focused on the fight against narcotics. [@1] Gendarmerie
forces carried out several interventions that day at Teahupoo, Toahotu and
Taravao as part of that anti-drug mission on the peninsula. [@1] The cultivation
sites were spread across Teahupoo, Toahotu and Taravao rather than a single
field. [@1]

The searches were conducted on six separate properties. [@1] Officers seized 558
cannabis plants, 462 grams of dried cannabis heads and 60 sticks made up for
sale. [@1] Polynésie la 1ère reported the same figures for the Wednesday 27
November operation on the peninsula: 558 cannabis plants, 462 grams of dried
heads and 60 sticks prepared for sale. [@2] The gendarmerie said those illicit
crops were destined for personal consumption and for resale. [@1]

The people implicated were to answer shortly before the courts for the facts
alleged against them. [@1] All the narcotics discovered were destroyed by
incineration. [@1]
