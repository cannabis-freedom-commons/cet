Prefectura Naval Argentina, the coast guard that guards the country's borders,
was carrying out preventive checks in the area known as paraje Arenera, in
Iguazú, Misiones. [@1] A patrol found three bundles and a backpack abandoned at
the spot. [@1] Officers inspected the packages and confirmed they contained
marijuana. [@1] The force is led by Eduardo Scarzello. [@1]

The packages held 55 "panes", pressed blocks of marijuana, weighing more than 46
kilograms. [@1] The Prefectura converted the weight into more than 139,000
doses. [@1] The force valued the marijuana at almost 3 million pesos. [@1]

The Federal Court of Eldorado, under Judge Miguel Ángel Guerrero, is handling
the case. [@1] The court ordered the decomiso, or forfeiture, of the marijuana.
