Ivorian gendarmes seized nearly two tonnes of cannabis at Mondoukou, in the
Grand-Bassam area. [@1] The interception was made on the night of Thursday 7
November 2019, around 20:00, by the anti-drug unit of the Port Autonome
d'Abidjan (PAA), according to security sources. [@1]

Officers found 30 bales of cannabis weighing 1,841 kg in total, close to two
tonnes, aboard three pirogues. [@1]

Fraternité Matin reported the same interception of almost two tonnes aboard
three pirogues, crediting the gendarmerie. [@2] The gendarmerie said it had
seized 3,110 kg of cannabis nationwide since the start of 2019. [@1]
