Customs drug carriers abandoned their load and fled when officers intercepted it
on 16 November, while counterfeit-note carriers were stopped at Ngueli without
resistance. [@1] Two young men coming from Nigeria were arrested in the
counterfeit control. [@3] Le Pays identified the two arrested men as Abakar
Mahamat Abakar and Moussa Raoul Betobaye, Chadian nationals coming from Nigeria.
[@2] The two suspects were to be transferred to the prosecutor for judicial
proceedings. [@2]

Officers seized 43 packages of cannabis and counterfeit notes in two separate
controls. [@1] Le Pays reported that the 43 cannabis parcels weighed more than
10 kg. [@2] Officers were alerted because the dollar notes all carried the same
serial numbers. [@1] The counterfeit carriers told officers their load contained
vehicle parts. [@1]

The seizures came from recent checks by Logone-et-Chari customs officers under
the customs directorate. [@1] The customs director presented the two suspects
and the seized cannabis parcels at the directorate on 6 December 2019 after
border searches at Ngueli. [@2]
