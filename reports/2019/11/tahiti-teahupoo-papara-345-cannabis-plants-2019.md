On Wednesday 20 November 2019, soldiers of the Taravao gendarmerie brigade and
of the Surveillance and Intervention Detachment of mobile gendarmerie unit EGM
21/3 Mont-Saint-Aignan searched a private home at Teahupoo, on Tahiti, in an
inquiry opened for drug-law offences. [@1] At the home they found 92 cannabis
plants, 558 sticks made up for sale weighing 562 grams in all, and 598 grams of
dried cannabis heads. [@1]

The same day, soldiers of the Papara brigade found 253 cannabis plants on two
land parcels in the communes of Teva I Uta and Papara. [@1] Together the
searches accounted for 345 cannabis plants. [@1] The gendarmerie said those
illicit crops were meant for the owners' own consumption and for resale. [@1]
The seized plants were described as paka, the local name for cannabis. [@2] The
gendarmerie announced the finds the next day, Thursday 21 November 2019, in a
communiqué. [@2]

The people implicated were to answer shortly before the courts for the facts
alleged against them. [@1] All the narcotics discovered were destroyed by
incineration. [@1]
