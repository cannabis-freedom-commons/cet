A police search led by ASP Mahendra Singh, the officer in charge of the No. 2
Sub-Division in Region Six, together with other ranks, was carried out at the
New Amsterdam Prison at around 5:30 on the morning of 6 November 2019. [@1]
Singh commanded the early-morning operation as the divisional officer in charge,
with supporting ranks taking part in the prison search. [@2] The search
unearthed several improvised weapons along with other prohibited items held
inside the prison. [@2]

Officers found three improvised weapons, 65 packs of cigarettes, 19 razor blades
and 10 grams of marijuana during the search. [@2] They also recovered six
improvised chargers, 20 metal spoons, five bottles and a bucket of locally made
wine, Digicel SIM cards, two cellphones and one improvised smoking utensil. [@2]
Officers also listed 5 grams of fronto, described in the account as a tobacco
wrapper leaf, which is left out of the cannabis seizure total. [@2]

The operation lasted about two and a half hours, and officers seized all of the
items discovered in the prison. [@2]
