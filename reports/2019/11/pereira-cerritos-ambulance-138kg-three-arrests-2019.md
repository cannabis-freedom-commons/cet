Traffic police arrested three men in an ambulance at the Cerritos toll plaza
near Pereira on the night of 5 November 2019 after finding 138 kilograms of
marijuana in oxygen cylinders. [@2] The men said they were transporting a
patient with HIV, with one pretending to receive intravenous fluids, according
to the police account. [@1] Police said the men were aged 28, 30 and 43 and were
from Puerto Escondido and Montería in Córdoba. [@1]

Officers found 138 taped packages inside the cylinders and seized the ambulance
along with the marijuana. [@1] Police said the load came from Cali and was
destined for Montería, where they estimated it could be sold for more than 200
million pesos. [@1] El Diario reported that the cylinders had fresh paint and
that officers opened them during the inspection. [@3]

Prosecutors charged the three with trafficking, manufacturing or carrying
narcotics, and they did not accept the charges. [@2] A judge ordered a
preventive detention measure for the three, according to Minuto30, citing the
prosecutor's office. [@2]
