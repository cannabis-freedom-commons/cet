Desmond Evon Wilkerson, 26, and Katelyn Olivia Nicole Tolbert, 24, both of
Temple, were arrested on 5 November 2019 and charged with possession of
marijuana over five pounds. [@2] The arrests were made by the Bell County
Organized Crime Unit. [@1]

Officers seized about nine pounds (about 4.08 kg) of high-grade marijuana with
an estimated street value of $40,000. [@3] More than $24,000 in cash was also
taken. [@3] The two were taken to the Bell County Jail and released after each
posting a $100,000 bond. [@2]

The Bell County Organized Crime Unit had been investigating the distribution of
large quantities of high-grade marijuana in the area, with the United States
Postal Inspection Service assisting. [@2] The unit is an independent group made
up of Killeen PD, Temple PD, the Bell County Sheriff's Department, Harker
Heights PD, Belton PD, Copperas Cove PD and the Bell County District Attorney's
Office. [@2] Officials said the investigation was ongoing and further charges
and arrests were possible. [@2]
