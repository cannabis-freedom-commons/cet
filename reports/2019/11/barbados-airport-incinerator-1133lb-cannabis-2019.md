On Friday morning the Royal Barbados Police Force burned 1,133 pounds of
cannabis, 513.92 kilograms, at the incinerator at Grantley Adams International
Airport as the official destruction of evidence seized in past cases. [@1]
Acting Inspector Rodney Inniss, the force’s public relations officer, said the
exercise was the fourth such burning that year. [@1]

Inniss said having or growing marijuana was still illegal in Barbados, and that
people would still be charged for the harvesting or transhipment of drugs
wherever it was discovered. [@1] He said the force does not make policy or
create the law, and that its responsibility is to enforce the law. [@1] He added
that 1,495 of the plants had been seized by the force’s Northern Division. [@1]

A police statement said officers of the Southern Division, under Acting
Superintendent Elphene Moore, destroyed the 1,133 pounds of marijuana, which
police valued at 2.26 million dollars, together with 3.6 kilograms of cocaine.
[@2] Police valued the cocaine destroyed in the same exercise at 181,000
Barbados dollars, a quantity left out of the cannabis figures. [@2] The
statement said the drugs had come into police custody through criminal
investigations in the Oistins, District B, District C and Holetown
jurisdictions. [@2] The courts in those jurisdictions had adjudicated the
matters, and the drugs had been authorised for disposal. [@2] The statement was
issued by Acting Inspector Rodney Inniss, public relations officer. [@2]
