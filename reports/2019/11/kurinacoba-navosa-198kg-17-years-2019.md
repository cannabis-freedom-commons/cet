A 37-year-old man was sentenced to 17 years' imprisonment by the High Court in
Suva after he was convicted of unlawfully cultivating 198kg of marijuana plants
in Navosa. [@1] The man was Mesulame Kurinacoba, who cultivated 1,589 marijuana
plants between October 2016 and March 2017. [@2] Justice Salesi Temo told
Kurinacoba that the sentence was designed to punish him in a manner that was
just in all the circumstances of the case, and to protect the community, deter
other would-be offenders and signify that the court and community denounced the
unlawful cultivation of marijuana. [@1] He set a non-parole period of 15 years.
[@1]

Justice Temo said the amount of illicit drugs found on Kurinacoba showed a
deliberate contempt for the law and that he must not complain when given a long
custodial sentence. [@2] Justice Temo told Kurinacoba his sentence would act as
a deterrence to others. [@2] The judge said this was one of the few cases that
had passed the 100 kilograms mark. [@2]
