On Friday 25 October 2019 Moroccan police arrested five people for their alleged links to an international drug trafficking network and seized 9.1 tonnes of cannabis resin. [@1] The resin was found at a farm in the Gzennaya region, about 20 km from Tangier. [@1]

The national police said the operation was carried out on the basis of information from the Directorate General of Territorial Surveillance (DGST). [@1] The report names no charges and gives no court outcome for the five people held. [@1]
