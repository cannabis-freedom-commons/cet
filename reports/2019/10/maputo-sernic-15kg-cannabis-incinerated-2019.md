Maputo city SERNIC criminal investigation inspector Rainha Joaquim Gamboa called
journalists on Wednesday to present 543.793 kg of various drugs seized between
July 2018 and October 2019 in the capital. [@1] In connection with the seized
drugs, 319 people of different nationalities were detained, including 286
Mozambicans and 33 foreigners. [@1] Investigators opened 223 cases, of which 120
had been completed and sent to the prosecution service. [@1]

The batch presented and incinerated that Wednesday included cocaine, heroin,
precursor acids, ephedrine, methamphetamine and cathinone/mira, plus another
223.85 kg of the latter drug whose traffickers remain at large. [@1] In the same
period police seized 15.212 kg of cannabis sativa, known locally as suruma and
described as the most consumed drug in the country. [@1] SERNIC said the
incinerated drugs relate exclusively to the city of Maputo. [@1]

In the current year alone, 11 foreign citizens in transit were detained at
Maputo International Airport, with investigators finding most detainees were
users and others were traffickers using the country as a corridor to Africa,
Europe and Asia. [@1] A Maputo-published GITOC study cited in the account
describes Mozambique as one of the main global drug-trafficking corridors. [@1]
