Former Black Leopards defender Bongani Masangane was arrested in Eswatini after
being found in possession of dagga. [@1] Masangane, 42, played for Leopards
between 2003 and 2005 before relocating back to Eswatini to play for Mbabane
Highlanders. [@1] Chief Police Information and Communications Officer
Superintendent Phindile Vilakati confirmed the arrest of the former Lidoda Duvha
player. [@1]

According to a report carried by Swazi Observer, Masangane was arrested after
going to the police with five kilograms of dagga in his possession and demanding
to be given a trading licence for the banned substance. [@1] That report said a
source close to the matter revealed that Masangane stormed into the Manzini
Police Station carrying 5kg of dagga and demanded a trading licence from the
officers on duty. [@2]

Vilakati told KickOff that Masangane was arrested by the Manzini police on
Monday for possession of dagga. [@1] He said he was not sure if Masangane had
already appeared before court. [@1]
