Officers from the Garda National Drugs and Organised Crime Bureau intercepted a
commercial haulage vehicle at Ballymascanlon, near Dundalk, on Tuesday 29
October 2019. [@1,2] They found herbal cannabis concealed within a legitimate
load of fresh vegetables, with an estimated street value of €3.2 million.
[@1,2]

Two men from Britain and Northern Ireland were arrested on suspicion of drug
trafficking and detained at Dundalk station under section 2 of the Criminal
Justice (Drug Trafficking) Act 1996. [@1,2] The stop was part of ongoing joint
investigations by the bureau and the UK's National Crime Agency under the
Cross Border Joint Agency Task Force. [@2] Assistant Commissioner John
O'Driscoll called the seizure very significant and a success of cooperation
with the UK authorities. [@1,2]
