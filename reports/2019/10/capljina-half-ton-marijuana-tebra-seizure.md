The Prosecutor's Office of Bosnia and Herzegovina announced that about half a
ton of marijuana had been seized in an operation codenamed Tebra at Gabela Polje
near Čapljina. [@1] Accounts of the announcement say the marijuana-skunk was
found late on Wednesday, 23 October 2019. [@3] The Prosecutor's Office said the
location was identified through its earlier investigative work. [@2]

The Prosecutor's Office valued the confiscated drug at more than two million
euros on illegal drug markets in EU countries. [@1] It said the seizure was one
in a series of several-hundred-kilogram seizures of marijuana-skunk over the
preceding year. [@2] Operation Tebra formed part of action by the Prosecutor's
Office and partner agencies against international smuggling chains running from
Albania through Montenegro into Bosnia and Herzegovina and on to Croatia and
other EU countries. [@1] The Prosecutor's Office said it was continuing an
intensive investigation with partner agencies to prosecute those involved in the
trafficking chains. [@3]
