On 18 February 2019, a 36-year-old man who arrived in Hong Kong from
Vancouver, Canada, was stopped at Hong Kong International Airport, where
Customs officers found about 30 kg of suspected cannabis buds concealed
inside his two suitcases. [@1] Customs estimated the market value at about
HK$6.2 million. [@1] The man was arrested and charged with one count of
trafficking in a dangerous drug, and was due to appear at West Kowloon
Magistrates' Courts on 20 February. [@1]

## Sentence

The smuggler, identified as Vancouver man Sinara Nou, had packed 16 slabs of
cannabis buds wrapped in towels in one suitcase and 14 in the other. [@2] He
said he was on welfare in Vancouver and had smuggled the drugs into Hong Kong
for $25,000. [@2] He was convicted and sentenced to 52 months in jail. [@2]
