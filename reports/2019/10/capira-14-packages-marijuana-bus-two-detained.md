On the afternoon of Saturday 12 October 2019, Panama's National Police carried
out a diligencia de allanamiento y registro, or search-and-registration, of a
vehicle in Panamá Oeste province and seized an illicit substance. [@1] Two
people were detained: a Panamanian identified by the initials J.A.D.M. and a
Colombian identified as B.B. [@1] They were travelling in a white Toyota Hiace
minibus with registration AF1818. [@1] After being stopped on the Pan-American
highway, both men were taken to the Capira police substation, where officers
carried out the verification together with officials of the Public Ministry.
[@1] The National Police said two people were detained in Capira, one of them a
foreigner, with 14 tape-wrapped packages found in the vehicle they were
travelling in. [@2]

A police source said the 14 rectangular packages wrapped in adhesive tape
contained presumed marijuana and were found inside the minibus, which ran the
Vacamonte–Panamá route. [@1] Panamá América said tests would be carried out to
determine whether the substance was a narcotic. [@2] Crítica reported that the
packages were inside a black rubbish bag on the floor of the vehicle's rear, in
smaller bags and wrapped irregularly with tape. [@3]

The evidence and both detainees were placed at the disposal of the competent
authority. [@1] Both were expected to appear before a guarantee judge in the
coming hours. [@1] Crítica said the evidence and the detainees were placed at
the disposal of the drug prosecutor's office to be processed through the
accusatory criminal system. [@3]
