In Kyrgyzstan, two employees of the Bishkek department for combating illegal
drug trafficking of the Ministry of Internal Affairs were detained on suspicion
of planting drugs, the Fergana news agency reported. [@1] They were uncovered by
staff of the anti-corruption service of the State Committee for National
Security acting jointly with the internal investigation service of the Ministry
of Internal Affairs. [@1]

According to the State Committee for National Security, the officers planted 32
grams of hashish in the car of private entrepreneur P.R. after conspiring with
his former wife. [@1] After this, the businessman was detained on a charge of
storing narcotics, the report said, linking the detention directly to the
planted hashish. [@1] The State Committee for National Security qualified the
incident as a commissioned criminal prosecution of the businessman intended to
seize his business through a raider takeover by interested persons. [@1]

An investigation was opened against the detainees for abuse of office, unlawful
accusation of a crime, falsification of evidence and coercion to give false
testimony. [@1] The Ministry of Internal Affairs officers were held in pre-trial
detention centre No. 1 in Bishkek, while investigators worked to identify other
persons involved in the crimes. [@1]
