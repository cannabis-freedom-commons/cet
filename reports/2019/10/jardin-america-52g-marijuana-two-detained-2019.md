Police detained two young men, Brian G., 19, and Enzo, 18, after finding 52
grams of marijuana during a foot patrol in Jardín América, Misiones, on 31
October 2019. [@1] At about 20:00, officers of the comando radioeléctrico, a
mobile police patrol unit, were making preventive checks on foot near a school
on Washington Street, at a sports ground, where a group of five young people was
gathered. [@1] After they were identified, Brian grew nervous and, when he
showed his backpack to the officers, they saw a light blue bag containing a
greenish-brown substance inside. [@1]

In the presence of witnesses, officers of the división toxicomanía, the
narcotics division, carried out a field test that returned positive for
marijuana, weighing 52 grams and valued at 3,536 Argentine pesos. [@1] Enzo said
he had sold the drug to Brian moments earlier, and both were detained. [@1] The
Juzgado Federal de Oberá, the Federal Court of Oberá, took charge of the case.
