Dundee Sheriff Court fined Jack Tanbini £450, banned him from driving for 21
months and ordered him to carry out 250 hours of unpaid work in October 2019.
[@1] He had appeared at the city's court that month charged with dangerous
driving, driving without insurance and possession of cannabis. [@1] Sheriff
Derek Reekie told him he took the offences lightly and warned that dangerous
driving without insurance was serious. [@1]

The case file said he had driven on Strathmartine Road in Dundee in the wrong
lane, mounted the pavement and overtaken a bus, and had repeatedly failed to
obey police orders to stop. [@1] Police found cannabis with a street value of
more than £1,000 when they stopped him. [@1] Officers found the drugs in the
vehicle after he eventually came to a halt. [@2] Tanbini was 23 when he was
sentenced. [@2]

Tanbini won £100,000 on a £1 National Lottery scratchcard in 2014. [@1] He
hardly worked in the five years after the win. [@1] His lawyer, Jim Caird, told
the court he had spent almost all of the winnings and had about £2,000 left.
[@1]
