Customs officers of the Matam subdivision in northern Senegal seized 96 kg of
chanvre indien, the French term for Indian hemp or cannabis, at Semmé, a commune
in Kanel department. [@1] They arrested two traffickers of Malian nationality.
[@3] The Matam customs commander, Etienne Diène Faye, said the seized hemp had a
commercial value of 9,600,000 CFA francs. [@1] The subdivision announced the
seizure to the state news agency APS. [@2]

Commander Faye said the seizure happened in two stages. [@1] In a road check in
the night of 10 to 11 October, customs officers first seized 12 kg, but the
smuggler escaped. [@1] In the night of 17 to 18 October they seized another 84
kg from two Malians, who had told the driver of the vehicle carrying the goods
that the load was dyed boubous, robes, imported in large quantities from Mali.
[@1]

The smugglers were arrested and referred to the prosecutor's office in Matam,
the commander said. [@1]
