NINETEEN men were arrested for drug-related offences over the weekend. [@1] The
police report listed nine drug-related cases including dealing in cannabis,
Mandrax and cocaine. [@1] In the Donkerhoek location at Khorixas on Friday,
three men aged 23, 24 and 27 were arrested with cannabis and Mandrax tablets as
well as cash. [@1] At Kalkrand on the same day, three men aged 18, 24 and 27
were arrested at different times with 70 grammes, 55 grammes and one parcel of
cannabis respectively. [@1] At Kamanjab's Five Rand location, a 28-year-old man
was also arrested on Friday after police searched his house. [@1] Thirty balies
of cannabis and loose cannabis were found in a black plastic bag. [@1]

In Windhoek's Hakahana location, a 34-year-old man was arrested at about 14h00
on Friday with cannabis with a street value of N$220 000. [@1] Also on Friday at
Omaruru, five men between the ages of 17 and 28 were arrested with Mandrax
tablets and cannabis. [@1] The confiscated drugs at Omaruru had a combined
street value of N$4 680. [@1] The combined street value of the Kalkrand drugs
was put at N$7 900. [@1] The street value of the Khorixas drugs had not yet been
determined as police investigations were continuing. [@1]

In Tsumeb, a 32-year-old man was arrested on Friday after he was allegedly found
in possession of cannabis. [@2] A 27-year-old man was arrested at Wanaheda in
Windhoek with 66 rocks of crack cocaine. [@1] The Omaruru suspects were to
appear in the Omaruru Magistrate's Court as police investigations continued.
[@1]
