Police found the farm after being called to a house on Tanner's Hill in Deptford on 1 October 2019 over a reported break-in. [@1,2] An officer noticed the toilet floor sounded hollow, and inspection revealed a tunnel dug from the bathroom, running to a private car park and on to the large basement of the old 750-seat Broadway Theatre, built in 1897 and converted in the 1960s. [@1,2] The passage ran about 70 ft, with a hidden trap door at the house end and a second shaft for ventilation, and the growers had tapped into the mains power with underground cables. [@1]

## A decade-long operation

Police estimated the cannabis produced in the theatre basement over the past decade could have been worth more than £40 million, at roughly £4 million a year. [@1,2] Three men aged 28, 45 and 47 and a 36-year-old woman were arrested on suspicion of cultivating cannabis and have all been released under investigation. [@1,2] Neighbours said drilling-like sounds had been heard for years without anyone realising a farm was next door. [@1]
