On 7 May 2019, officers of the Tambacounda customs brigade stopped a vehicle
registered in Guinea-Bissau in the village of Diallacoro and seized 195 kg of
cannabis. [@1] SeneNews places the same seizure in the village of Diallocouro
and says the cannabis was hidden in the Guinea-Bissau-registered vehicle. [@2]
Officers arrested the driver and the Sierra Leonean courier accompanying the
consignment. [@1]

The courier was of Sierra Leonean nationality and was transporting the drug
concealed in the vehicle. [@2] Lequotidien presents the seizure as the third in
the region in under a month, after 915 kg of cannabis seized at Gouloumbou on 20
April and 72 kg of cocaine found there on 29 April. [@1] Together those earlier
interceptions and the new 195 kg brought the regional total to more than a tonne
of cannabis plus 72 kg of cocaine in about 20 days. [@2]

Customs director general Oumar Diallo praised the officers and handed a new
pick-up vehicle to the Gouloumbou post to support surveillance. [@1]
