Customs officers of the mobile brigade at Tenkodogo seized nearly half a tonne
of chanvre indien, the French term for cannabis, during a road check. [@1] The
officers found exactly 342 slabs of chanvre indien that traffickers had
carefully concealed in a public transport coach. [@1]

The drug, estimated at nearly 500 kg, had been hidden in the coach's walls above
the passengers. [@1] The coach was registered in Ghana, had already been
involved in a drug trafficking case in 2016 and was well known to customs
services in the region. [@1]

Tenkodogo is an important trading crossroads between Ghana, Togo, Benin and
Niger. [@1] Koaci reported that much of the trafficking and contraband is
carried out by traders who manage to evade the vigilance of customs officers.
[@1]
