The officers had just executed search warrants in the same village at around
5.30 am, arresting three men for marijuana-related offences, when they came upon
a recently cleared reserve road and tracks leading to two marijuana camps and
three marijuana fields. [@1] Three men from Mt George were due to appear in the
Scarborough Magistrates Court charged with possession of marijuana and marijuana
seeds. [@1]

Officers from the Tobago Divisional Task Force under Sergeant Stewart, with
personnel from the Crown Point Police Station and the K9 Unit, went to a
forested area near Castara Village Road where the discovery was made. [@1] They
found more than 2,500 seedlings, 5,000 fully grown trees, over 50 kg of cured
marijuana and 5 kg of seeds, valued at $5.5 million, which they destroyed by
fire. [@1] Marijuana with an estimated street value of $5.5 million was
destroyed during a morning exercise at Mt George, Tobago. [@1]

Between 4 am and 5.10 am, police officers executed search warrants at the homes
of the suspects where the discoveries were made, and investigations were
continuing. [@1]
