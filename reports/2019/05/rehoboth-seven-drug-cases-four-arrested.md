Police at Rehoboth recorded seven cases of possession of dependency-inducing
drugs over the weekend before the Monday 20 May 2019 crime report. [@1] On the
Friday, a 33-year-old man was arrested in the town's Block E area for possessing
48 'bellies' of cannabis valued at N$2,400. [@1] In a separate Block E incident,
a 50-year-old man was found with cannabis and mandrax tablets with a total value
of N$7,480. [@1] On the Sunday, a 28-year-old man was arrested in Block G after
he tried to destroy a half-pack of cannabis and was found with more than nine
mandrax tablets, items valued at N$3,140. [@1] Also on the Sunday, a 35-year-old
man was arrested in front of Dr Lemmer High School at about 00h10 with 16 units
of crack cocaine valued at N$1,600. [@1]

Three of the seven cases were worth less than N$300 each and four were worth
more than N$1,000, the police report said. [@1] The weekend's drugs — cannabis,
mandrax tablets and cocaine — had a combined value of N$15,280, deputy
commissioner Edwin Kanguatjivi said in the weekly crime report. [@1]

The Namibian Sun reported that most of the arrests in that weekend's drug
roundup were made in Rehoboth and that they included crack cocaine. [@2] The
previous week, The Namibian reported that police had carried out numerous drug
busts in Rehoboth as part of an operation in which the individuals had been
monitored for some time. [@1]
