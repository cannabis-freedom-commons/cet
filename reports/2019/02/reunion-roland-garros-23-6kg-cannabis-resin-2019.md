Customs officers at Roland-Garros airport, the airport serving Saint-Denis on
Réunion, seized more than 23.6 kg of cannabis resin from a traveller on Saturday
16 February 2019. [@1] Officers of the external surveillance brigade, the BSE,
based at Saint-Denis-Gillot, picked out a man in his twenties as he got off a
flight from la métropole, as mainland France is called on the island. [@1]

The X-ray image of his suitcase showed dark masses that appeared to conceal
smuggled goods. [@1] A search of the suitcase confirmed the officers'
suspicions. [@2] Among the young man's clothes and personal effects, the case
contained mainly gift-wrapped parcels. [@2] Opening them revealed cardboard
packets holding heat-sealed bags of soap-bar-shaped blocks of cannabis resin.
[@1] Customs gave the total weight as 23.6 kg of resin. [@1]

After the customs procedure, and on the instruction of the public prosecutor,
customs handed the offender to the Sûreté départementale, the departmental
police service that took over the case. [@1]
