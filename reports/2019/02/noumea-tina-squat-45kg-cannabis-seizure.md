National police seized 45 kilograms of cannabis in Nouméa, New Caledonia. [@1]
The operation took place at the squat de Tina in the commune of Nouméa. [@1]
Officers went to see a man already well known to the police services, who was
suspected of an offence. [@1] The police had expected a cannabis seizure but not
one so large. [@1] The visit and seizure took place in the morning. [@1]
