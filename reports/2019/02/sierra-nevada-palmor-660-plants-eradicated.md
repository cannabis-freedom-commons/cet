Personnel from the Cienaga Station of the Third District of the Santa Marta
Metropolitan Police and the Cordova Battalion of the National Army jointly
eradicated 660 marijuana plants in the upper part of the township of Palmor in
the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta. [@1] The afternoon operation took place during
patrol, search and control work in the mountainous area aimed at protecting
farmers and workers living there. [@1]

The 660 marijuana plants were growing in the upper part of Palmor. [@1] Police
said the crop covered approximately 61 hectares. [@1] Officers then manually
eradicated the plants by pulling them up by the roots and packed them into ten
sacks with a total weight of 110 kilograms. [@1]

The National Police described the action as part of its measures in the area to
reduce drug trafficking and block criminal organizations profiting from the drug
trade. [@1] The seized consignment was taken to the municipality of Cienaga,
placed at the disposal of the 17th Sectional Prosecutor's Office and then
destroyed. [@1]
