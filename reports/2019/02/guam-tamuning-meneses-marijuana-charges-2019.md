Guam Police Department officers pulled over a driver who was allegedly speeding
in Tamuning and searched his car, according to a magistrate's complaint filed in
the Superior Court of Guam. [@1] Ramon Meneses, 27, was charged on Tuesday with
illegal drug possession with intent to distribute and possession of a firearm
without identification. [@1] When he was arrested, Meneses said he wished to
speak to his lawyer, who advised him to remain silent after hearing some facts
of the case. [@1]

Meneses agreed to let officers search the car, where they found a loaded
semi-automatic handgun, suspected LSD tabs and suspected MDMA pills; he had
handed them some suspected marijuana before the search. [@1] Officers found more
marijuana in the car along with about 165 grams of suspected methamphetamine,
another loaded gun and suspected drug ledgers, and the magistrate's complaint
states that about 42 grams of marijuana was seized. [@1] Police then searched
Meneses' residence and found two more guns, drug paraphernalia, a safe with a
large bag of methamphetamine and documents bearing Meneses' name. [@1] Officers
also found packages of methamphetamine hidden in one of the walls of the home.
[@1] In total, Guam Police Department seized 5.27 pounds of methamphetamine and
837 grams of marijuana from the residence, according to the charging documents.
[@1]

Court documents state Meneses was the target of a March 2018 drug investigation
in which officers found marijuana and methamphetamine at his previous residence.
[@1] Meneses was charged with possession of a Schedule I controlled substance
with intent to deliver as a first-degree felony, possession of a Schedule II
controlled substance with intent to deliver as a first-degree felony and four
counts of possession of a firearm without a firearms identification card as
third-degree felonies. [@1]
