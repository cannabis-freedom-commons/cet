Tonga's Police Drug Enforcement Taskforce arrested eleven people in Ngele'ia
over the weekend after they were found in possession of methamphetamine and
cannabis. [@1] Two men were arrested on Friday, February 15 after being found
with three packs of methamphetamine ready for sale, one at a home and the other
on the road. [@2] On Saturday, February 16, a 26-year-old man was arrested with
one pack of methamphetamine on the road. [@2] Later that Saturday, eight people
were arrested at a home in the same village. [@2] Those eight included three
women and five men, who were found with 32 packs of methamphetamine, eight packs
of cannabis and cash. [@2]

In total, the taskforce seized 36 packs of methamphetamine, eight packs of
cannabis, cash and electronic supplies. [@2] The 32 methamphetamine packs and
eight cannabis packs found with the eight were ready for sale. [@2] Deputy
Commissioner Pelenatita Vaisuai said Tonga Police are committed to disrupting
and detecting the supply of drugs and preventing the harm it causes. [@2]
Vaisuai added that police would keep working with the community to prevent drug
harm and related criminal activity. [@2] The suspects remained in police custody
while the investigation continued. [@2]
