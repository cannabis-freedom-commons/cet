Malaysia's Federal Court in Putrajaya spared medical-cannabis advocate Mohd Zaireen Zainal from the gallows on 18 April 2019. [@1,2] After he pleaded guilty to the reduced charge of possessing 1.1kg of cannabis, the court imposed 15 years in prison and ten strokes of the cane, with the term running from his arrest on 27 August 2014. [@1,2]

Police had raided Zaireen's home in Batu Pahat, Johor, in the early hours of 27 August 2014 on a tip-off and found several bottles of cannabis oil and a compressed lump of cannabis. [@1] A chemist confirmed the haul was 1.1kg of cannabis and 58.5ml of tetrahydrocannabinol. [@1,2] The Muar High Court sentenced him to death for trafficking, and the Court of Appeal upheld the sentence. [@1,2]

His lawyer said Zaireen ran a small therapy centre from home, giving cannabis oil to sick villagers who had lost hope in prescribed medicines, and had promised not to repeat the offence. [@1,2] The prosecution agreed to substitute the trafficking charge under Section 39B of the Dangerous Drugs Act with possession under Section 6 after his lawyer asked the Attorney-General's Chambers to review the case. [@1,2]
