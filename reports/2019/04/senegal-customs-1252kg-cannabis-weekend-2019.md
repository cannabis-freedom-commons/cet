One person was arrested in Kolda and two motorcycles abandoned by suspected
traffickers were seized. [@1] The truck's couriers fled when officers searched
the vehicle. [@3]

Customs reported 1,252 kg of chanvre indien (cannabis) seized that weekend along
the Tanaff–Kolda–Gouloumbou axis. [@2] The largest single load, 915 kg, was
seized by customs at Gouloumbou, a record for the region in years. [@2] The 915
kg was hidden in a false bottom at the front of a refrigerated truck registered
in Mali that had come from Guinea. [@3] The customs chief valued that load at 64
million CFA francs. [@3] Customs valued the 337 kg seized in Kolda at more than
20 million CFA francs. [@1] The Kolda seizure was made during a patrol on the
night of 19–20 April at Bérécolon, in Médina El hadji commune at the
Guinea-Bissau border, and at Thiéty near the Gambian border. [@1]

The customs chief said steps were being taken with the prosecutor to find the
presumed traffickers. [@3] He said he would ask the Tambacounda court to
confiscate the truck. [@3]
