Kolda's security forces seized 105.25 kilograms of Indian hemp and arrested
eight people in a series of roadside checks over three weeks in 2019. [@1] The
Kolda urban police station said it had declared a 'war without mercy' on
traffickers because the city had become a transit zone for drug trafficking.
[@1]

On 14 April an officer at the checkpoint at Kolda's entrance stopped a truck
arriving from Kafountine and probed a load of smoked-fish boxes. [@1] The smell
of hemp revealed 90 bales weighing 85 kg, and the driver, his apprentice and an
escort were detained. [@1] The investigation added two more arrests, giving five
in that case, and the truck used to carry the drugs was immobilised. [@1] The
suspects were referred to the Kolda prosecutor's office on Friday. [@1,3] Police
said the fifth suspect, arrested in Kafountine with help from the local
gendarmerie, was the organizer of the trafficking. [@2] All the suspects were
held on remand. [@2] One recipient of the parcels was in Touba and another in
Thiaroye in Dakar. [@1]

The first seizure was on 20 March at Saré Moussa Darou, on the road from
Bignona. [@1] A Bissau-Guinean rapper, Famali Fati, was found with 2.20 kg of
Indian hemp in his backpack at the checkpoint. [@1] He said the hemp was payment
for work on Indian hemp plantations in the Fogny area of Ziguinchor. [@1] He was
jailed and, despite a prosecution request for five years, was sentenced to three
years by the Kolda regional court. [@1] On 2 April a suspect fled a police
checkpoint on the Kolda-Diana Malary road. [@1] Officers found 6.80 kg of Indian
hemp hidden in a travel bag in the vehicle. [@1] He jumped into the Casamance
river and swam to the far bank before disappearing. [@1] A further 11.50 kg was
seized on 5 April from a seven-seat vehicle arriving from Fogny. [@1] Two Kolda
residents were referred and sent to prison to await the outcome. [@1]
