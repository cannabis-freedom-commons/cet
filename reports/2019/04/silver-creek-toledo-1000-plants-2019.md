Punta Gorda police found a marijuana field about seven miles north of Silver
Creek Village in Toledo District on the morning of Friday 12 April 2019. [@1]
The field held about 1,000 marijuana plants measuring between 2 and 6 feet in
height. [@1] Officers also found a small camp about 50 feet from the field where
marijuana had been harvested. [@1] No one was in the area, and the plants were
uprooted and destroyed by fire. [@1]

The Belize Police Department said it found and destroyed the plantation in
southern Belize the day before Breaking Belize News reported it on 13 April
2019. [@2] Because no one was in the area at the discovery, police classified
the plants as found property before uprooting and burning them. [@2] Breaking
Belize News put the plantation at more than 1,000 plants of the same height,
some seven miles north of Silver Creek village. [@2]

7 News Belize said the Belize Defence Force maintains operations in the area
because of widespread marijuana cultivation by Belizeans and Guatemalans. [@1]
