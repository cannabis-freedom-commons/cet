Four people, three Vincentians and one resident of Carriacou, were arrested and
charged over the discovery of EC$31,752.00 of marijuana and a smaller quantity
worth just over EC$30.00. [@1] They were store manager Nyasha Bellingy, 32, her
compatriots Zeiki Miller and Clifford Samuel, 26, and Frank Allard, 43, of
Carriacou, after police operations on Carriacou and the Carenage in St. George.
[@1] All four appeared before Magistrate Tahira Gellineau at the St. George's
No. 2 Magistrate's Court to face drug trafficking and conspiracy charges. [@1]

The 24 pounds of marijuana were seized on the mainland. [@1] The Drug Squad of
the Royal Grenada Police Force intercepted the drugs as a suspect came off the
Osprey shuttle from Petite Martinique and Carriacou. [@1] Miller and Bellingy
were charged over six grams of marijuana found in their possession on Carriacou.
[@1] Prosecutors told the court they would not pursue Miller over the 24-pound
mainland haul. [@1] Samuel, a labourer in St Vincent, was charged over the 24
pounds of ganja and with entering Grenada illegally. [@1]

Miller was granted $3,000 bail with one surety over the six grams, while
Bellingy was remanded to Richmond Hill Prison. [@1] Allard was granted $60,000
bail with two sureties for the larger quantity of ganja. [@1] Allard's bail
required him to report to the Carriacou Police Station every Wednesday between 6
a.m. and 6 p.m. [@1] Magistrate Gellineau issued a summons for Crispin Alexis of
Walker, St Andrew, whom the Drug Squad wanted to question as the ringleader of a
major drug ring involving the Vincentians. [@1] Miller and Bellingy were due
before Magistrate Teddy St. Louis at the Carriacou Magistrate's Court on 22 May.
[@1] Bellingy, Allard, Samuel and Alexis were due for mention on 9 and 17 May,
with the matter set to start on 22 May. [@1]
