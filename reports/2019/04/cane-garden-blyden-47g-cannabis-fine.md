Donnell Blyden of Cane Garden Bay was fined $2,600 for possession of 47.5 grams
of cannabis and given four months to pay. [@2] If he failed to pay, he would
serve six months at Her Majesty's Prison in Balsam Ghut. [@2] Magistrate Ayana
Baptiste-DaBreo handed down the sentence on Wednesday. [@2] The court heard that
his early guilty plea and lack of previous convictions counted in his favour.
[@2] A charge of possession of cannabis with intent to supply was withdrawn.
[@2]

When Blyden was arrested in August 2018, police had charged him with having 65
grams of cannabis. [@1] A drug analysis certificate detailed to the Magistrate's
Court on Wednesday put the quantity at 47.5 grams, 17.5 grams less than police
claimed, and prosecutors amended the charge. [@1] Blyden pleaded guilty to
possession of a controlled drug but not guilty to possession with intent to
supply. [@1] The matter was adjourned to April 3. [@1]

At about 11 am on 27 August 2018, police stopped Blyden as he drove a pickup
truck during a routine stop-and-search near Sage Mountain on Tortola. [@2] The
officers were from the Royal Virgin Islands Police Force; Blyden said he had an
ounce of cannabis and gave consent to search the vehicle. [@1] Police found a
brown paper bag of green vegetative matter and, in a further search, a ziplock
bag with empty dime bags and four dime bags containing more of the substance.
[@1] Blyden said the cannabis in the paper bag was for his personal use while
that in the ziplock bag had been given to him as payment by someone who owed him
money. [@1]
