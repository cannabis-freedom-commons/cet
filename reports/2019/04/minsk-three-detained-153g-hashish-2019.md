Three Minsk residents were detained on suspicion of storing hashish. [@1] Police
detained two previously convicted men, aged 22 and 31, in the Zavodski district.
[@1] The woman who lived with one of them was detained as well. [@2] A young
child who lived in the flat was handed over to the woman's mother. [@1] Police
said the woman knew of her partner's activities and used drugs herself. [@2]

A personal search of one of the men found 50 grams of hashish. [@1] Officers
then searched the flat and seized more than 100 grams of hashish that had been
packaged and prepared for stashing. [@2] They also found electronic scales,
magnets and insulating tape there. [@1]

Police said they were continuing to identify other sellers and buyers involved.
[@2] The materials were sent to the Zavodski district office of the
Investigative Committee for legal assessment. [@2]
