Malawi Police in Dedza arrested two men, one a Mozambican national and one from
Kasungu district, on suspicion of possessing cannabis sativa that they allegedly
intended to smuggle illegally into Mozambique. [@1] Dedza Police Deputy
Spokesperson Constable Cassim Manda identified the suspects as Fungai Barai, 32,
a Mozambican national, and Mafosha Banda, 35, of Matunduluzi village in
Traditional Authority Kawamba, Kasungu district. [@1]

The two were arrested at Sukwasukwa in Dedza district by police officers who
were on patrol in the area. [@1] Officers on patrol came across a motor vehicle
bearing registration number NU676 parked along a dusty road and, on searching
it, discovered a bag of cannabis sativa. [@1] Police later confiscated the bag
and took the two suspects into custody at Dedza police station. [@1]

The two suspects were to answer a charge of being found in possession of
cannabis sativa under the Dangerous Drugs Act. [@1]
