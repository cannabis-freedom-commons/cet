A police patrol in Luxembourg City noticed a car parked with its engine running
at about 2am on Thursday 10 January 2019, and the driver drove off quickly when
the police car approached. [@1] Officers caught up with the car on Rue de la
Tour Jacob and checked the driver and his vehicle. [@1] The officers noticed a
sweet smell from inside the car, and the driver, saying he knew nothing about
it, agreed to a drug test that produced a positive result. [@2]

A search of the car found a plastic bag with 25 grams of marijuana and a drug
scale. [@2] Before he was body-searched, the driver handed the officers a second
plastic bag with 24 grams of marijuana. [@2] Because of the positive screening
test the driver had to give a blood sample at hospital. [@2] The car's technical
inspection certificate had expired in April 2018, and police drew up a criminal
report. [@2]

The police bulletin headlined the case as 50 grams of marijuana seized, against
the 25 grams found in the car and the 24 grams the driver handed over. [@2] RTL
Today also headlined the case as 50 grams of cannabis. [@1]
