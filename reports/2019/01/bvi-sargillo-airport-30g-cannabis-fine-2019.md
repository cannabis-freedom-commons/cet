A United States man was fined $4,000 after he was caught at the TB Lettsome
International Airport on Beef Island with 30.5 grams of cannabis. [@1] Travis
Sargillo, 41, was on a 10-day visit to the territory with his wife and friends
and claimed the drugs were to treat his anxiety. [@1] Prosecutors said he had
not declared the drugs to border officials and had produced no medical documents
supporting his claim. [@1] He was charged with possession of a controlled drug
and an untrue declaration. [@1]

Sargillo pleaded guilty to both offences before Magistrate Giselle Jackman-Lumy
the next day. [@1] He was fined $2,000 for possession of a controlled drug. [@1]
The court imposed another $2,000 fine for the untrue declaration charge, also to
be paid that Friday. [@1] He was not charged with importation of drugs. [@1]

His lawyer, Patrick Thompson, produced a document from Sargillo's doctor
supporting the anxiety claim and cited two character references. [@1] The
magistrate found his early guilty plea, no previous convictions, remorse and
good character far outweighed the seriousness of the offence and the need for
deterrence, and imposed no custodial sentence. [@1] The court heard that his
luggage was searched while he went through Customs and that a black box inside
held three vacuum-sealed plastic bags containing cannabis. [@1] A police arrest
blotter reported by Virgin Islands News Online said Sargillo was arrested and
charged on Wednesday 16, 2019. [@2]
