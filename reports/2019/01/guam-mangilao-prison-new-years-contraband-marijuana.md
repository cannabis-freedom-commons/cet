For the second time in a week, guards at the Guam Department of Corrections'
Adult Correctional Facility in Mangilao intercepted a package of contraband
before it could be smuggled into the prison. [@1] Director Tony Lamorena said
his special operations unit had been doing a perimeter check near the dome
housing units when the package was discovered shortly after midnight on New
Year's Day. [@1]

Inside the package was chewing tobacco and small amounts of what tested positive
for methamphetamine and marijuana, Lamorena said. [@1] There was no arrest, the
DOC director said, describing the delivery as a 'throw-over.' [@1] Lamorena
congratulated the corrections officers for confiscating the contraband before it
got to the inmates. [@1] He said the package was turned over to the Mandaña Drug
Task Force. [@1]
