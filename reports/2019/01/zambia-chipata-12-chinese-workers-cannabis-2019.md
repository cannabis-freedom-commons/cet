Zambia's Drug Enforcement Commission arrested 12 Chinese construction workers on
suspicion of cultivating cannabis without a permit. [@1] The workers were based
in Chipata, in Zambia's Eastern Province, where they were building a studio for
the state-run Zambia National Broadcasting Corporation. [@1] The commission said
in a statement that the 12 were arrested on Monday and that 6.2 kg of plants
were uprooted. [@1]

The provincial government's permanent secretary, Chanda Kasolo, said the arrests
were regrettable because they would delay the construction work. [@1] The
arrests were made public amid growing anti-Chinese sentiment in Zambia over
lucrative construction contracts awarded to Chinese firms and government
borrowing from Beijing. [@2]

Growing, dealing and consuming cannabis for recreational purposes is illegal in
Zambia. [@1] Cannabis cultivation for medicinal purposes is allowed if the
health ministry grants permission. [@1] If convicted, the 12 face a maximum jail
sentence of five years. [@1]
