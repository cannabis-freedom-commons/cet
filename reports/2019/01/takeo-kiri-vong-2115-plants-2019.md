Takeo provincial authorities destroyed more than 2,000 cannabis plants on a
Wednesday after six marijuana farms were discovered, police said the next day.
[@1] Kiri Vong district police chief Sim Meng said his officers joined commune
police to raid six marijuana farms near an abandoned military base in Chroy
village in Preah Bat Chonchum commune. [@1]

After the operation, police destroyed all six farms and burned 2,115 marijuana
plants, Meng said. [@1] Police were on the lookout for the owners of the farms
so they could be punished in accordance with legal procedure, with no arrests
reported at the time. [@1]

Preah Bat Chonchum commune is known as a hotbed of marijuana growing compared
with other communes in Kiri Vong district. [@1]
