Tunisia's Maritime Guard discovered a cardboard box containing 3,485 capsules of
cannabis off Tabarka in Jendouba on Saturday 26 January 2019. [@1] The guard
made the discovery during an operation against smuggling and drug trafficking
between Tunisia and Algeria, according to an Interior Ministry statement
released that day. [@1]

The goods were found at Melloula in the Tabarka delegation of Jendouba
governorate, a busy crossing point on the Tunisian-Algerian border. [@2] The box
was wrapped in adhesive tape and weighed 39.245 kg, filled with 3,485 capsules
of Indian hemp, known locally as zatla. [@2] The seized drug weighed nearly 40
kg, with each capsule holding pure, superior-quality cannabis said to be worth
more than 300 Tunisian dinars. [@1]

The Interior Ministry said on Monday 28 January that each capsule would cost
nearly 300 dinars apiece, putting the total haul at more than one million
dinars. [@2] On the instructions of the public prosecutor, the whole quantity of
drugs was seized. [@2]
