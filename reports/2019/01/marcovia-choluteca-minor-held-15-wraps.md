Authorities detained a 15-year-old identified only by the alias El Negro in
Colonia Buena Vista in Marcovia, Choluteca, in southern Honduras. [@1] The
capture was carried out by agents of the Fuerza Nacional Antimaras y Pandillas
with members of the Direccion de Inteligencia Policial as part of Fuerza de
Seguridad Interinstitucional Nacional operations. [@1] Authorities suspected the
teenager of packaging drugs and distributing them independently in the area.
[@1]

At detention officers seized a green plastic bag containing 15 white paper wraps
with blue stripes holding suspected marijuana. [@1] Officers also seized a blue
plastic bag containing about half a pound of material the seizure account calls
an alkaloid. [@1] The same account had earlier described wraps and a blue bag as
containing supposed marijuana. [@1]

The operation was described as surveillance and monitoring in Colonia Buena
Vista in the municipality of Marcovia, Choluteca. [@1] The teenager was to be
sent that Friday to the competent courts on suspicion of illicit drug
trafficking against the health of the Honduran population. [@1]
