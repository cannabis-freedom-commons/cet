Police in Barbados arrested and charged two Canadian visitors on Monday 14
January 2019 over cannabis offences. [@1] They were Janella Christy Milord
Ashford, 18, and Marc Dawins Cimeus, 20, both of Ottawa, Ontario, Canada. [@1]
Each faced four charges: possession of cannabis, possession with intent to
supply cannabis, trafficking in cannabis and importation. [@1]

Lawmen said the pair committed the offences on Sunday 13 January while at
Grantley Adams International Airport. [@1] The two were caught on 13 January as
they arrived at the airport. [@2] Both appeared at the District B Magistrates'
Court at Blackmans, St Joseph, before Magistrate Laurie-Ann Smith-Bovell and
pleaded guilty to all four offences. [@1] They also pleaded guilty to
possession, possession with intent to supply and trafficking of the illicit
substance. [@2]

The two were remanded to HMP Dodds. [@1] They were due to reappear before
Magistrate Laurie-Ann Smith-Bovell in the District B Magistrates' Court on
Tuesday 29 January. [@2]
