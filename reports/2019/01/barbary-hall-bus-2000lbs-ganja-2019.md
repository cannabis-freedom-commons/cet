A police team conducting an operation on the Hall main road in the Barbary Hall district signalled the driver of a bus to stop at about 7 o'clock on Monday night, 28 January 2019. [@1] The driver, the bus's sole occupant, complied, and a search found 38 knitted bags containing rectangular parcels of compressed ganja inside the vehicle. [@1]

Police put the haul at 2,000 lb (about 907 kg), with an estimated street value of $8 million. [@1] A man remains in custody in connection with the seizure. [@1]
