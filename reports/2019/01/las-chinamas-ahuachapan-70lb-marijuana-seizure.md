Authorities reported finding 14 packages of marijuana, each of about five
pounds, at a border point with Guatemala. [@1] Elements of the División
Antinarcóticos (DAN) of the Policía Nacional Civil (PNC) found the drug in the
first hours of Tuesday 8 January 2019, in the cantón Las Chinamas, in the
department of Ahuachapán. [@1] The DAN is the anti-narcotics division of the
Policía Nacional Civil, El Salvador's national civil police. [@1] The packages
had been abandoned. [@1] They were inside two nylon sacks. [@1]

Together the packages weighed about 70 pounds, or about 31.75 kilograms. [@1]
The police put the commercial value of the marijuana at about $36,708, an
estimate of its illegal-market price rather than cash seized. [@1]

Guatemalan authorities had alerted El Salvador that a trasiego, a movement of
drugs, was being planned. [@1] The alert began the operation that ended with the
seizure. [@1] No arrests over the abandoned packages were reported. [@1]
