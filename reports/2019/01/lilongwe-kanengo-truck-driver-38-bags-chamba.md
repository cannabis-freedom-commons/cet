Police at Kanengo station in Lilongwe arrested Willard Kaoloka, a 35-year-old
truck driver, on 23 January 2019. [@2] Central Region Police spokesperson
Nolliettie Chimala said police received a tip that the driver had loaded the
illicit drug into his truck's container. [@1] Officers followed him to his
workplace at AS Investments in Kanengo. [@1] Criminal investigation officers
searched the truck and seized 38 bags of cannabis, known locally as chamba. [@2]
Face of Malawi described the bags as 50 kg each, making about 1.9 tonnes in
total. [@2]

Kaoloka told police the bags belonged to a Zimbabwean who hired him at the Area
49 zebra crossing to ferry the substance to a place in Mozambique. [@3] Face of
Malawi reported that the bags were packed on top of tobacco cartons he was to
carry to Mozambique. [@2] He was charged with possession of cannabis sativa
without a licence, contrary to section 19 of the Dangerous Drug Act. [@1] Police
said investigations were under way to arrest the Zimbabwean. [@1]

Kaoloka comes from Chanika Village under Traditional Authority Mkanda in Mchinji
District. [@1] Face of Malawi said he would appear in court to answer the
charge. [@2] Police appealed to the public for information leading to arrests of
people cultivating, possessing or selling the plant. [@1]
