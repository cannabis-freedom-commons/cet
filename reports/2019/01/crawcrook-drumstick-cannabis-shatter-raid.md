A 28-year-old woman was arrested following a search on Eden Dale Estate on
Sunday. [@1] The raid was in Crawcrook on Sunday morning and formed part of an
investigation into cannabis shatter factories. [@2] Police called the wider
inquiry Operation Inkerman. [@2] The woman arrested after the Sunday search was
later released under investigation. [@2]

Cannabis packaged to look like Drumstick lollies was seized in the raid. [@1]
Some of the cannabis shatter was prepared in packaging with the Drumstick logo
on the front. [@2] Equipment used in cannabis production and £3,000 in cash was
also seized. [@1] Officers seized drugs and equipment believed to have been used
to produce cannabis shatter. [@2]

A total of 10 people have been arrested in the investigation. [@1] Nine people
had been arrested before the Sunday raids and remained under police
investigation. [@2] Sergeant Warren Sturrock said dealers used special ovens and
highly flammable butane gas to strip chemicals from cannabis plants to make the
shatter. [@1] He said factories of this kind had been linked to explosions and
house fires that claimed lives. [@1] Northumbria Police described the production
method as extremely concerning and the inquiry as complex. [@1]
