Vanuatu's Police Drug Unit seized 100 cannabis plants, each about two metres
high, from a plot at Blacksands on the outskirts of Port Vila. [@1] The uprooted
plants filled an entire police van, and the Daily Post ran the story on its
front page under the headline "Blacksands Pot Bust". [@1] The plants were
growing in a garden at Blacksands when drug unit officers raided it on the
morning of 24 January 2019. [@2]

Two men were arrested, the first at Blacksands after the raid, and police said
he would be charged with marijuana cultivation. [@2] Police said the suspect,
who is from Tanna, had allegedly been growing marijuana for a long time. [@2]
Officers arrested a second man at Anambrou on the way to the police station, for
alleged involvement in marijuana trading. [@2] Inspector Morris Seule commanded
the raid, and his team estimated the uprooted plants at over 100 kilograms. [@2]

Earlier that week, police on Tanna arrested two men for growing marijuana. [@2]
Police said they would keep enforcing the law and would not hesitate to
prosecute anyone found growing the drug. [@2] Cultivating, selling or using
marijuana is against the law in Vanuatu. [@1]
