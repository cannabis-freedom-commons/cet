Incheon police asked a court for an arrest warrant for the 31-year-old grandson
of SK Group founder Chey Jong-gun on charges of buying and using liquid
marijuana. [@1] The Incheon District Court issued the warrant on 3 April 2019,
saying there was a risk that he would flee. [@2] He was accused of buying
high-concentration liquid cannabis 15 times from a 27-year-old supplier he knew
and using it, from March 2018 until recently. [@2] Investigators found he had
paid about 7 million won for the drugs. [@2]

A 27-year-old man identified only as Lee was arrested the previous month on
charges of selling the drugs to him. [@1] Police said they had confirmed that
the 28-year-old grandson of Hyundai Group founder Chung Ju-yung also bought the
same substance from Lee. [@1] Chung, who met Lee while studying abroad, was
staying abroad when the case broke. [@1]

The Incheon District Court sentenced both men to one year in prison, suspended
for two years, on 6 September 2019. [@3] The court ordered probation and
forfeiture of about 10 million won from the SK grandson and about 14 million won
from the Hyundai grandson. [@3] The SK grandson was found to have bought about
81 grams of cannabis, including cookies and liquid cartridges, worth some 22
million won between March 2018 and March 2019. [@3] The Hyundai grandson bought
about 72 grams of cannabis, including marijuana and liquid cannabis, worth about
14.5 million won between February 2018 and January 2019, and smoked it 26 times.
[@3] The two men smoked cannabis together four times at the SK grandson's home,
investigators found. [@3] Prosecutors had sought one year and six months in
prison for each of them. [@3] The court said the two had bought and smoked
cannabis repeatedly but took account of their admissions and their lack of a
criminal record. [@3]

The Seoul High Court gave the SK grandson the same one-year prison term,
suspended for two years, on appeal on 19 December 2019. [@4] The appeal court
said it considered that he had no previous drug record and had been trying to
stop. [@4] In South Korea, using marijuana is punishable by less than five years
in prison or a fine of less than 50 million won. [@1]
