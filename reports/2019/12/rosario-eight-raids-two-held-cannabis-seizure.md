The Homicide Division of the Criminal Investigation Agency carried out a series
of raids over the killings of Sergio Giménez and Sergio Birri amid retail
drug-dealing violence involving groups from the Santa Lucía and 7 de Septiembre
neighbourhoods. [@1] The operation used eight search orders requested by
homicide prosecutor Luis Schiappa Pietra and was carried out simultaneously with
Regional Investigative Unit 2 and tactical entry groups. [@1] Officers detained
two men, with only one linked to the killings and the other held for drugs and
weapons. [@1]

At Misiones al 2200 police detained Nahuel C., accused of weapon possession and
vehicle concealment among other offences. [@1] At the same address officers
seized six marijuana bricks, a bag containing the same substance and two
cannabis plants. [@1] Officers also seized at that address a bag of cocaine,
another bag with 317 cocaine wrappers ready for sale plus 68 more wrappers, four
precision scales and five mobile phones. [@1]

Officers also seized two 9mm Bersa pistols and a similar Hi Power pistol,
cartridges of different calibres, a replica hand grenade and a stolen Fiat Duna
with a false plate at that address. [@1] Police also arrested Maximiliano G. as
an alleged necessary participant in Birri's homicide. [@1] Officers seized from
him a Yamaha YBR believed to have been used in the crime and a Browning pistol.
[@1]
