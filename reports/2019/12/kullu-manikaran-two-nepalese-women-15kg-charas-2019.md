Kullu police caught the two women at Punjabi Chulha in Manikaran on Saturday
around 1.45 am. [@3] The accused were identified as Shreya Thapa (26) and Vishnu
Kala (32), residents of Kapilvastu and Lumbini respectively. [@2] Police said
the women were allegedly trying to smuggle charas from Nepal to the Parvati
valley in Kullu district. [@2]

The consignment was to be sold at the tourist spot of Kasol on the Parvati
river. [@2] The accused started from Haridwar on Friday and reached Bhuntar in
the night. [@2] They then hired a taxi to Kullu in the early hours of Saturday.
[@2] The police team intercepted their vehicle and seized the charas during
nakabandi at Punjabi Chulha in Manikaran. [@2] The taxi driver was not found
involved in the smuggling. [@2] Police recovered nearly 15 kg 402 g of cannabis
hidden inside the clothes of the accused. [@1] During a search before a lady
gazetted officer, Thapa was found hiding 10.190 kg of charas and Kala 5.212 kg.
[@2]

A case under sections 20 and 25 of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic
Substances Act was registered at Sadar police station and further investigation
was under way. [@2] The women were to be produced in court the same day. [@1]
