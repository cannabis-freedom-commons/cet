European police arrested 11 Dutch citizens suspected of belonging to a gang that
used a transport business as a front to smuggle large quantities of drugs across
the continent. [@1] Three of the suspects were arrested in Norway and three in
Italy, with one each in Britain, Germany, Spain, Sweden and the Netherlands,
Eurojust said. [@1] Eurojust announced the coordinated arrests on Friday 6
December 2019. [@1] Eurojust, the EU agency for judicial cooperation, said the
investigation found that a transport company based in the Netherlands smuggled
large quantities of drugs to several European countries. [@2]

Police suspect the drugs were carried by truck alongside legal cargo from Spain
to the Netherlands and then redistributed to other countries. [@2] Seizures in
the participating countries totalled four tonnes of hashish, along with 200 kg
of amphetamines, 64 kg of cocaine, 45 kg of MDMA and 25 kg of heroin. [@2]

Norwegian police began the investigation in 2017 after a rise in violent crime,
including shootings, in Oslo. [@2] Eurojust opened a case in September 2018
targeting the alleged Moroccan and Dutch main suspects, including Dutch trucking
and transport firms. [@2] Eurojust said 50 suspects were also charged in Norway
as an expansion of its case. [@2]
