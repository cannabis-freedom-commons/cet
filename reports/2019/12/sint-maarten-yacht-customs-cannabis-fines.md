Customs officers in charge of border control in Sint Maarten recovered illicit
products from a private yacht that was visiting the territory, the customs
service said. [@1] During their regular daily checks, the officers found several
grams of cannabis on board. [@1] The same checks turned up ten 9 mm hollow
bullets and an amount of US$14 in cash that had not been declared. [@1] The
passengers accused of holding the illegal products were questioned. [@1]

Those in possession of the cannabis were released after paying a ticket, and the
drugs were seized by customs. [@1] The passengers in possession of the
ammunition were fined and then released. [@1] The owner of the undeclared money
also received a fine from the prosecutor's office and was released. [@1]

The customs service used the case to remind travellers that sums of money,
jewellery or objects worth US$623 or more must be declared. [@1] The rule
applies whether people are entering or leaving Sint Maarten, customs said. [@1]
