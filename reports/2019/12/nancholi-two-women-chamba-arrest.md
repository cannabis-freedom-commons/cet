Police in Blantyre were keeping two women from Chikwawa in custody after finding
them with Cannabis Sativa, locally known as chamba, at Nancholi bus stage on
Friday. [@1] The two women were Falesi Isaac, aged 32, and Falesi Moyo, aged 36,
both from Sekeni village under Traditional Authority Lundu in Chikwawa district.
[@1]

The women were apprehended on Friday while police were carrying out a general
observation at Nancholi bus stage. [@1] According to a police report, the
officers on duty at the time became suspicious of the big sacks the two women
were carrying. [@1] A search was conducted on the sacks, where the said illicit
drugs were found inside four bags and seven plastic jumbos. [@1]

The cannabis was seized by the officers and taken to Bvumbwe research station
for examination. [@1] The suspects were to appear before court soon to answer
charges of possession of an illicit drug. [@1]
