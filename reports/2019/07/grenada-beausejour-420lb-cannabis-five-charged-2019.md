Police in Grenada arrested and charged five people after 420 pounds (about 190
kg) of cannabis was seized from a motor vessel in Grenada's territorial waters
off Beausejour, St George. [@1] Officers of the Drug Squad and Coast Guard
seized the 420 pounds in the vicinity of Beausejour on Thursday 25 July 2019.
[@2] The New Today reported that the vessel was intercepted in waters outside
Beausejour in St George North-west just after 2.00 a.m. [@1] The five were
Victor Vesprey, 43, of Carenage; David Daniel, 28, of Vendome; Kenny Morain, 46,
of Beausejour; Bill Alexander, 23, of Cherry Hill; and Vincentian national Jamal
Rogers, 37, of Kingston Park. [@2] The New Today put the value of the bust at
$952,560, in East Caribbean dollars. [@1]

All five were jointly charged with trafficking in a controlled drug and
conspiracy to traffick in a controlled drug. [@2] Vesprey, Daniel and Rogers
also faced a charge of importing a controlled drug. [@2] The New Today reported
that Vesprey, the boat captain, was seen jumping from the vessel and swimming
ashore before he was caught. [@1] Morain, the boat's owner, was not on board;
police took him into custody at his home in Beausejour the following Saturday.
[@1]

They appeared at the St George's Magistrate's Court on Monday 29 July 2019. [@2]
The prosecution strongly objected to bail, citing the suspects' previous
convictions for similar crimes. [@1] Chief Magistrate Gill granted Morain and
Alexander bail of $250,000 with two sureties and ordered them to surrender their
travel documents and report to the Central Police Station on the Carenage every
Monday and Friday. [@1] Bail was refused to Rogers, Vesprey and Daniel, who were
remanded to Richmond Hill Prison until 16 August 2019. [@1] Morain and Alexander
were due back in court on 6 September 2019. [@1]

In May 2013 Morain was one of three people sentenced over the 2008 robbery and
murder of Black Bay businessman Anthony Frederick. [@1] He received 10 years in
prison as the driver of the getaway vehicle. [@1]
