Officers of the Aguadilla Drug Division served a search warrant at a residence
in barrio Mora, Isabela, on a Friday afternoon and found a marijuana greenhouse
in full production there. [@3] The greenhouse occupied three rooms of the
residence, prepared for sowing, growing and harvesting. [@4] Nobody was arrested
at the scene. [@2]

Police said they found 98 marijuana plants of between one and six inches in the
greenhouse. [@1] Officers also seized lighting equipment, fertilisers,
chemicals, an undetermined number of pots, two air conditioners, irrigation
equipment and equipment for processing, compacting and packaging the drug. [@2]
They took a 2002 Toyota Tundra for investigative purposes. [@1]

The operation was part of the Anti-Crime Plan of the Aguadilla police area. [@3]
The raid was carried out under a search and arrest warrant. [@2] The intervening
agents said they would consult the evidence with the on-duty prosecutor at the
Aguadilla prosecutor's office. [@1]
