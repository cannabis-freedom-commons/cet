The Antwerp prosecutor's office and the federal police released the judicial and
administrative results of the two 2019 Tomorrowland weekends in Boom on 31 July
2019. [@1] Prosecutors said 460 festival-goers were caught with a user quantity
of drugs, which was seized; they lost their entry wristband and were removed
from the festival site. [@1] Those caught were offered an immediate out-of-court
settlement of 75 euros for soft drugs or 150 euros for hard drugs. [@1] Of 61
dealers caught, 55 received a summons for one of the special themed court
sessions later that year, while six were arrested and brought before an
investigating judge. [@1]

Among the drugs seized over the two weekends were 25 grams of cannabis and 46
grams of hashish. [@1] VRT reported that cannabis was scarce at the festival and
that police seized 25 grams of it. [@2] An English-language account listed the
same two quantities, 25 grams of cannabis and 46 grams of hash, among the
confiscated substances. [@3] The wider haul also included ecstasy pills,
cocaine, MDMA, ketamine, GHB, speed pills, LSD and counterfeit pills, none of
which are counted in the cannabis figure. [@1]

The prosecutor's office said the drug figures were comparable to those of the
two previous editions. [@1] The federal police coordinated security in and
around the festival with 26 police zones and one police school. [@1] Officers
also carried out visible checks at the entrance and anonymous surveillance on
the site for drug offences and pickpocketing. [@1]
