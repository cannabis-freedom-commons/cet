Police Drug Unit officers in Port Vila carried out a search operation at Marobe
Market and arrested two young men. [@1] The two men came from Tongoa, were
residing in south Epi and were arrested on suspicion of possessing and selling
marijuana. [@1]

The harvested marijuana plants were wrapped as parcels and transported in bags
to Port Vila. [@1] Some of the marijuana was wrapped in palm leaf for transport
to Port Vila. [@1] Other marijuana was wrapped in food foil. [@1]
