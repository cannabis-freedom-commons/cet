Grandfather Jamie Germaine, 46, ran the operation from the family home in
Llanrumney, Cardiff, with his sons Tyrell, 23, and Shaquille, 21. [@1] Jamie
Germaine was sentenced to six years and nine months and Tyrell Germaine was
jailed for two years and five months. [@1] Shaquille Germaine was sentenced to
two years at a young offenders institution. [@1] Qatip Puci, 33, of Cardiff Bay,
and Danny Yau, 22, of Old St Mellons, were jailed for four years and six months
and three years respectively. [@1] Jonathan Parsons, 33, had £45,000 hidden in
his car when arrested on Newport Road, Cardiff, on 19 January 2018. [@1] Parsons
was sentenced to three years and nine months for conspiracy to supply and
possession with intent to supply class B drugs. [@1] All 14 defendants were
sentenced over two days at Cardiff Crown Court. [@1] All the defendants pleaded
guilty to conspiracy to supply class B drugs except Ayman Nadeem, 54, who was
found guilty at trial. [@1] Germaine's partner Danielle Jeanne was handed a
24-month community order for possessing criminal property. [@1]

During the covert police operation the gang sold an estimated 100kg of cannabis.
[@1] The gang sold an estimated £3.8m worth of drugs. [@1] More than £600,000 in
cash, some stored in suitcases, was seized at the property. [@1] Police believed
the more than £600,000 seized on the arrests was the proceeds of dealing
cannabis. [@2]

The gang was caught after a seven-month investigation led by South Wales
Police's organised crime unit. [@1] South Wales Police monitored Germaine's home
in Pepys Crescent, Llanrumney, from January to July 2018. [@2] The force's
organised crime unit began a covert operation at the Pepys Crescent address on 1
January 2018. [@2] Prosecutor Susan Ferrier said Germaine headed the south Wales
arm of an organised crime group run from the family home on Pepys Crescent. [@1]
