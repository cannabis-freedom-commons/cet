Papua police arrested two Papua New Guinean men, Beny Toway Waropo, 28, and
Gadafi Kuentaw Waropo, 18, on 21 and 22 March 2021. [@1] Beny Toway Waropo was
arrested in the Kali Acai neighbourhood of Abepura, Jayapura, on the evening of
Sunday 21 March. [@1] Gadafi Kuentaw Waropo was arrested early on Monday 22
March 2021. [@1] Sen. Coms. Alfian, director of the Papua Police criminal
investigation department's narcotics division, announced the arrests. [@1]

Officers seized five sacks of marijuana from Gadafi Kuentaw Waropo. [@1] Gadafi
had stored the sacks among rocks near his boarding house. [@3] Police raiding
Beny's house found 21 packets of marijuana and a sack holding 10 kilograms of
rice. [@1] Beny's 21 large packets of marijuana and the rice sack were seized at
his boarding house. [@3]

Water police from Jayapura City helped in the arrest at Many Island. [@3] Police
said the marijuana had been smuggled into Papua from Papua New Guinea but had
not established whether by sea or land. [@1] The two were held at the narcotics
directorate's detention in Jayapura for further processing. [@3] Papua police
said the two Papua New Guinean citizens were held over smuggling drugs into the
province. [@2]
