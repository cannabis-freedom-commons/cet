Police at Mponela in Dowa district arrested a 34-year-old man after he was found
in possession of chamba. [@1] Mponela police station deputy publicist Sergeant
Macpatson Msadala said the man was cornered on 26 March 2021 near Chikho Hotel.
[@1]

On the same day detectives from police headquarters manned an ad-hoc roadblock
at Chikho Hotel near Mponela trading centre along the M1 Road. [@1] A Toyota
Hiace minibus, registration KU 7415, travelling from Kasungu towards Lilongwe
with passengers on board stopped at the roadblock. [@1] After a search, officers
found two bamboo baskets and one big plastic bag containing loose chamba. [@1]
Police arrested the owner of the baskets and seized the drug. [@1]

The suspect was to appear before court to answer a charge of being found in
possession of Cannabis Sativa without a licence. [@1] The seized cannabis was to
be analysed at Chitedze Research Station. [@1] Banda comes from Chimkoka Village
in the area of Traditional Authority Chakhaza in Dowa district. [@1]
