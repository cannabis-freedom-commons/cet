A team of three customs officers in the Turks and Caicos Islands seized 2,472
grams (5.5 pounds) of suspected cannabis on Wednesday 3 March 2021. [@1] All
three officers were assigned to the Turks and Caicos Islands Customs
Department's Anti-Smuggling Unit. [@1] The department estimated the street value
of the suspected cannabis at $24,000. [@1] The suspected cannabis was concealed
in a shipment of household and food items. [@1] No one had been arrested or
charged over the offence thus far, and the investigation was ongoing. [@1]

The Customs Department said its Anti-Smuggling Unit had made a number of
significant drug seizures at various cargo entry points since the start of the
year. [@1] A department statement congratulated the officers on their tremendous
work. [@1] The department said it would continue to update the public on similar
seizures as it worked towards the security of the territory's borders and, by
extension, TCI society. [@1]
