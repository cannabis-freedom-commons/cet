The judicial police of Kenitra seized nearly nine tons of cannabis on Thursday 25 March 2021, foiling an international drug trafficking operation. [@1] Acting on information from the Directorate General of Territorial Surveillance (DGST), officers seized 2.1 tons of cannabis resin in 67 bundles from a vehicle at Ouled Mlik, 12 km from Kenitra, and arrested four people. [@1] Inquiries the same afternoon led to another 6.9 tons of chira in two metal crates at a container workshop in Haddada, about 6 km from Kenitra. [@1]

Investigations into that network then led police to Aït Melloul, near Agadir, where they seized 4.9 tons of cannabis on Sunday 28 March, bringing the total to 13.8 tons. [@2] The haul was hidden in the metal tanks of crane trucks parked in a warehouse in the Aït Melloul industrial zone, according to a national police statement. [@2]
