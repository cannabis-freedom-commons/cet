Police in Asilah, a city on the northwest tip of the Atlantic coast of the Kingdom, foiled an attempt to smuggle 4.6 tonnes of cannabis during a security combing operation carried out along the city's coastline. [@1] Officers discovered and seized 117 bales of cannabis together with two inflatable boats fitted with four powerful engines and 52 jerry cans holding 1,560 litres of fuel. [@1] The published account follows a national police press release and names no suspects, arrests or charges. [@1]

An investigation has been launched under the supervision of the prosecutor's office to shed light on the smuggling attempt and to identify all those involved in the criminal operation. [@1] Its stated aim is to bring those responsible to justice. [@1] The outlet presents the seizure as part of the sustained efforts deployed by the country's security services to combat international drug trafficking. [@1]
