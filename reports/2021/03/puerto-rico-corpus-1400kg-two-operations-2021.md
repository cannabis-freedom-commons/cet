Prefectura Naval Argentina seized nearly 1,400 kilograms of marijuana and
detained three people in two operations in Misiones province. [@1] In Puerto
Rico, officers on a patrol boat saw people unloading bundles from two boats on
the riverbank at a spot known as Puerto Natural Schmidel. [@1] The group fled
when the officers approached, and the Prefectura followed them into a makeshift
house where more bundles were visible. [@1] With the Juzgado Federal de Primera
Instancia de Oberá handling the case, officers raided the house and, with the
sniffer dog Rango, seized more than 1,050 kilograms of marijuana packed in 1,316
panes. [@1] Two men were detained and a woman was left under house arrest to
care for a minor who was in the house. [@1]

In a second operation at Corpus, a patrol found a boat and bundles on the bank
at Puerto Natural Sersing holding more than 321 kilograms of marijuana, which
the magistrate ordered seized. [@1] The Misiones newspaper Primera Edición
reported that the Prefectura seized nearly 1,400 kilograms in operations at
Corpus and Puerto Rico and detained three people. [@2] The outlet placed the
first procedure in the early hours of a Saturday in Puerto Rico. [@2] The outlet
also repeated the 1,316 panes and the more than 321 kilograms taken at Corpus.
[@2]

The Security Ministry said federal forces seized more than 5,900 kilograms of
marijuana across Misiones in the previous 24 hours. [@1] The ministry presented
the patrols as part of a push by Security Minister Sabina Frederic to watch the
areas where large drug loads are stored and moved, before they are broken up for
sale in the country's cities. [@1]
