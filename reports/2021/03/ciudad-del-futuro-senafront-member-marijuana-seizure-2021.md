Panama's National Border Service (Senafront) said on 11 March 2021 that it would
investigate one of its members over a seizure of presumed marijuana in Ciudad
del Futuro, Panamá Oeste. [@1] Senafront said the police action in Ciudad del
Futuro seized dried herb, presumed to be marijuana, in what it described as
local drug dealing. [@2]

The member was on unpaid leave for a common illness, the service said. [@1] The
service said the member also had a disciplinary case before its disciplinary
board over drug use and had been sanctioned for desertion. [@1] Senafront said
the member would be processed under the law as appropriate. [@1] Senafront said
it would also carry out the internal investigations and sanctions the presumed
offence warranted. [@1]

Senafront said it would not tolerate conduct that fails its ethics code or
threatens the health and safety of the population. [@3] The service said it
would always act with the rigour the case merits, as it is a guarantor of peace
and tranquillity in the community. [@2]
