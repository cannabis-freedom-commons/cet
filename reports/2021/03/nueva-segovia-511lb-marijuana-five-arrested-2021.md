Nicaragua's National Police stopped a white double-cab Nissan pickup at
kilometre 230 on the road to Mozonte, Nueva Segovia, at 1:00 am on 19 March
2021. [@3] The pickup, with plate M 329-575, was driven by Roberto Antonio
Monterrey Moreno, 20. [@2] Officers found 13 cardboard boxes hidden under the
fruit the vehicle was carrying. [@3] The boxes held 257 egg-shaped packages
wrapped in adhesive tape containing green plant material that field-tested
positive for marijuana. [@3] The marijuana weighed 511 pounds, about 232
kilograms. [@1]

Police detained five people who were travelling behind the pickup. [@3] Police
linked the five to drug trafficking and organised crime. [@3] One of them, Ariel
José Ráudez Gutiérrez, had a previous criminal record for fraud. [@3] Officers
seized the pickup, three cars and two motorcycles. [@3] The detainees and the
evidence were to be handed to the competent authorities. [@2]

The same police announcement reported 141 kg of cocaine seized in Nueva Segovia
and Chinandega, valued at about $3,553,125. [@3] Commissioner Victoriano Ruiz,
second chief of the Judicial Assistance Directorate, gave the details of the
operations. [@3]
