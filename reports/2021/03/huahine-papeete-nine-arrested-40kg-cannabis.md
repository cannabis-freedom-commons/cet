Gendarmes from the OFAST antenna and the Papeete research section, backed by the
GIGN antenna in French Polynesia, arrested nine people on Friday 26 March 2021
after a three-month preliminary inquiry. [@2] The nine were suspected of taking
part for several years in a large cannabis-trafficking network between the
Society Islands. [@1]

The cannabis was grown and packaged on Huahine, then shipped by sea and sold in
the Papeete agglomeration. [@1] The gendarmerie sweep seized 40 kilograms of
dried cannabis packaged in sachets and matchboxes. [@1] Officers also seized 9.5
million Pacific francs in cash, and two vehicles and a boat valued together at
13.7 million Pacific francs. [@1]

The suspects were to be brought before the Papeete prosecutor on 30 March 2021
under a deferred-appearance procedure, with a request for pre-trial detention.
[@2] A criminal hearing was set for 27 May 2021, and the gendarmerie said the
seized products would be destroyed by incineration. [@2]
