Great Grimsby Crown Court sentenced Klodjan Hila, 36, of no fixed abode to
three years and two months in prison on 25 May 2021 for cultivating cannabis at
an address on Owmby Close in Immingham. [@1] He had pleaded guilty to
cultivation. [@1]

Humberside Police raided the new-build property on the morning of Friday 12
June 2020 and found about 1,450 plants in multiple rooms, which the force
valued at £652,500. [@2,3] Two men were arrested at the scene on suspicion of
cultivating cannabis: Hila and Ylli Krakulli, 33, also of no fixed abode, who
were both charged. [@2,3]

The raid was part of a wider clearance of cannabis houses across North East
Lincolnshire under Operation Galaxy, which recovered more than 2,500 plants
from nine factories in about a month. [@2,3] A Humberside Police spokesman said
the force would keep targeting drug production to make communities safer. [@1]
