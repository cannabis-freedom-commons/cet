Joint operatives of the Police Regional Office Cordillera and the Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency destroyed 55.51 million pesos worth of marijuana plants in eradication operations in Benguet and Kalinga from 17 to 20 May 2021. [@1,2] The operations covered five plantation sites in Benguet and seven in Kalinga, twelve sites in total. [@1,2]

In Benguet, a three-day operation starting 17 May found 8,650 fully grown marijuana plants worth 1.73 million pesos on about 1,100 square metres of communal land at Sitio Dalipey, Tacadang. [@1,2] In Kalinga, two-day operations on 19 and 20 May found 268,400 fully grown plants worth 53.68 million pesos and 2,500 seedlings worth 100,000 pesos across 29,400 square metres of mountainous plantations at Butbut Proper. [@1,2]

In total 277,050 fully grown plants and 2,500 seedlings were uprooted and burned on site after documentation. [@1,2] The reports name no growers caught, suspects or arrests at any of the sites. [@1,2]
