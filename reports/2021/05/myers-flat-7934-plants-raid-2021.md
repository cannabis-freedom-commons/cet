Deputies with the Humboldt County Sheriff's Marijuana Enforcement Team served one search warrant on an unlicensed cannabis grow in the Myers Flat area on 19 May 2021. [@1,2] The parcel held no county permit or state licence for commercial cultivation, and officers from Fish and Wildlife, county HazMat, Code Enforcement and the State Water Resources Control Board assisted in the search. [@1,2]

Deputies found about 29 greenhouses with growing cannabis inside and eradicated about 7,934 plants. [@1,2] They seized and destroyed about 12 pounds (5.4 kg) of cannabis bud and over 600 pounds (about 272 kg) of shake. [@1,2] They also seized four firearms, one of which had been reported stolen. [@1,2]

Six people were detained on the scene and later released, and no arrests were made. [@1,2] The case was forwarded to the Humboldt County District Attorney's Office for review. [@1,2]
