On the night of 10 May 2021, personnel of Squadron 1-11-14 of Argentina's
National Gendarmerie were conducting foot patrols around Avenida Varela and
Pasaje San Juan in Barrio Padre Rodolfo Ricciardelli, Buenos Aires, when they
stopped a Volkswagen Suran carrying three adult citizens. [@1] During a physical
and document check, a database search indicated that one of the three men had
previous criminal records. [@1]

In the presence of witnesses, the gendarmes found a nylon bag containing a plant
substance and a second bag holding cash. [@1] A Narcotest field test confirmed
the plant material was marijuana, with a total weight of 613.8 grams. [@1]

The National Criminal and Contraventional Prosecutor's Office No. 24 ordered the
drugs confiscated and the vehicle, mobile phones and cash seized. [@1] The
prosecutor also ordered the three people involved detained. [@1]
