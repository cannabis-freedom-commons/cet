Personnel from the Garda National Drugs and Organised Crime Bureau and the Special Crime Task Force stopped and searched vehicles in Tallaght and Dublin city centre on 24 May 2021 as part of investigations into organised crime. [@1,2]

They seized 110 kilograms of cannabis herb, valued by Gardaí at about €2.2 million pending analysis, and approximately €150,000 in cash. [@1,2]

Two men, aged in their 30s and 50s, were arrested and detained at Tallaght Garda station under drug-trafficking powers. [@1,2] Both were charged on 26 May 2021. [@3]
