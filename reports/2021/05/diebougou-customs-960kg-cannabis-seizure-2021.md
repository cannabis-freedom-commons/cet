Four suspected drug traffickers fled an ambush by the Diébougou mobile customs
brigade, abandoning the motorcycles and Indian hemp they were carrying. [@1] The
brigade chief, central customs controller Justin Tamini, led the men who carried
out the ambush. [@2] Customs said the ambush and seizure took place on Saturday
22 May 2021 during a campaign patrol by the Diébougou mobile brigade. [@3] The
brigade said the four men had to abandon their machines and a load that was
nothing other than Indian hemp. [@4]

Officers seized 960 kilograms of chanvre indien, cannabis, and four motorcycles.
[@1] Customs valued the seizure at 98 million CFA francs. [@3] The same figure,
nine hundred and sixty kilograms of Indian hemp and four motorcycles, was given
by the Diébougou mobile brigade. [@4]

The Director General of Customs congratulated the South-West regional customs
director and the chief of the Diébougou mobile customs brigade and his team for
their work against illicit trafficking. [@1] He asked residents to report any
fraud or illicit trafficking to the nearest customs office. [@1] Customs
announced the seizure on Friday 28 May 2021, six days after the ambush. [@2] The
Director General also congratulated the South-West regional director and the
Diébougou brigade chief in the account published the next day. [@3]
