Muhammad Amirul Aliff bin Md Zainal pleaded guilty to importing not less than 499.9 g of cannabis from Malaysia into Singapore with two accomplices on 30 December 2017. [@1,2] He was part of a Malaysia-based syndicate that hid the drugs in a rented car driven across the border after another accomplice entered first through the Woodlands Checkpoint to check conditions. [@1]

The High Court sentenced him to 27 years' imprisonment and 15 strokes of the cane, while his two co-accused received 24 years and six months each with 15 strokes. [@1,2] Amirul appealed on the ground that he was no more culpable than them.

On 5 May 2021 the Court of Appeal, with Justices Andrew Phang Boon Leong, Judith Prakash and Steven Chong, found no basis to intervene and dismissed the appeal. [@1,2]
