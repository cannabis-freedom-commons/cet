Prefectura Naval Argentina officers seized more than six tonnes of marijuana at
Ituzaingó, in Corrientes province, in the early hours of Thursday 27 May 2021,
and detained one man. [@3] A routine patrol spotted a boat coming from Paraguay
crossing towards the shore near Lago Yacyretá. [@1] Officers closed the area by
land and water and found the boat on the shore and a truck with a driver nearby.
[@2]

In the truck they found a large number of bundles of marijuana hidden among the
remains of pine trees. [@1] The load weighed more than 6,000 kilograms of
marijuana, in blocks known as panes and buds, or cogollos. [@1] The force
described the load as a multimillion-dollar shipment. [@1] Federal Court No. 2
in Corrientes ordered the drug seized, and the truck driver was detained. [@1]

Prefectura said it carried out the procedure on the directives of the national
security ministry. [@1] La Opinión Austral reported that Ituzaingó is one of the
areas the security ministry and Prefectura have identified as central to the
stockpiling of narcotics. [@2]
