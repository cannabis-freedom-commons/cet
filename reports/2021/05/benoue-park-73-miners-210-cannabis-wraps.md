Cameroonian soldiers carried out a sweep in Benoue National Park in the North
Region on 21 and 22 May 2021. [@1] On the first day about forty clandestine gold
miners were arrested and handed to the gendarmerie while others fled. [@1] Four
camps were destroyed and equipment was recovered during that first sweep. [@1]
On 22 May troops of the 31st Motorized Infantry Brigade destroyed four more
camps and arrested 33 more clandestine miners. [@1]

During that 22 May search the troops seized motorbikes, motor pumps, 267 slabs
of narcotic drugs and 210 bundles of Indian hemp. [@2] Officers recovered 210
wraps of Indian hemp together with 267 blisters of narcotic drugs. [@3]

The sweeps were carried out under a memorandum between the Defence and Forests
and Wildlife ministries, with defence forces supporting the park conservation
service. [@1] The brigade commander linked the mining sites to drug trafficking,
narcotics, small-calibre arms and exploitation of protected species. [@1]
