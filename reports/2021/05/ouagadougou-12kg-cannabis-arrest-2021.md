Police in Ouagadougou arrested a 34-year-old man found with 12 kilograms of
cannabis. [@1] The arrest and seizure took place on 30 May 2021 and were
announced in a national police statement dated 14 June 2021. [@2] The operation
was carried out by the Anti-Drug Unit (UAD) of the Judicial Police Directorate
(DPJ), under the General Directorate of the National Police. [@3] Police said
the man worked alone and conducted the illicit activity in Ouagadougou. [@1]

He obtained his supply through transport companies operating between
neighbouring countries and Burkina Faso. [@1] In Ouagadougou he delivered to
customers at designated locations for 22,500 CFA francs a packet. [@1] Wakat
Séra said the suspect had identified specific places in the city where he handed
over the goods. [@2] The arrest followed a tip-off, according to the national
police. [@1] Police urged residents to report suspicious cases to the hotlines
17, 16 and 1010. [@2]

The case was reported in the context of Burkina Faso's continuing campaign
against drug trafficking. [@3]
