The suspect was a 26-year-old Macau resident surnamed Kuan, who told police he
worked as a waiter. [@1] Judiciary Police officers arrested the recipient when
he collected the parcel on 21 May 2021. [@2]

Officers seized three bottles of cannabis oil weighing a total of 47.4 grammes
hidden in a stationery box inside the parcel. [@1] A search of Kuan's home in
Ilha Verde district found 16.2 grammes of cannabis buds, 2.3 grammes of dried
cannabis plants, drug-taking paraphernalia and drug packaging tools. [@1] Police
put an estimated street price of 65,000 patacas on the drugs. [@1]

Kuan admitted taking drugs and said he had contacted the seller online 10 days
earlier and bought three bottles of cannabis oil for 4,800 yuan (5,970 patacas).
[@1] He said he paid the equivalent in cryptocurrency as instructed, and had
bought the buds and dried plants the same way two weeks earlier. [@1] He was
transferred to the Public Prosecutions Office on Saturday facing charges of
buying drugs, drug abuse and illegal possession of drug-taking paraphernalia.
[@1] Police said they were also pursuing the drug source and others involved.
[@2]
