Law enforcement officers found a whole field of hemp in the Samarkand
Region on 7 August 2021, in the Almazar mahalla of Jambay District. [@1]
They found 2,160 growing cannabis bushes, more than 500 dried hemp bushes
and about 100 grams of seeds. [@1]

The local farmer had planted, grown and sold the banned plant, and officers
detained him. [@1] Police said an investigation was under way. [@1]
