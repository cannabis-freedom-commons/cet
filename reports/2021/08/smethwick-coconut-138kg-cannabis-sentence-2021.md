Border Force officers opened a container destined for Myers's Soul Foods business that had arrived in the United Kingdom on 13 July 2018 and found sacks of coconuts with plastic-wrapped herbal cannabis hidden inside the fruits. [@1,2] The coconut cannabis weighed almost 138 kg, and officers found a further 497 g block of cannabis at Myers's home. [@1,2] The National Crime Agency valued the drugs at 650,000 pounds on the streets of the United Kingdom. [@1,2]

Agency investigators found that Myers, 53, of Smethwick in the West Midlands, had travelled to Ghana to see the drugs loaded before returning to the United Kingdom. [@1,2] He had earlier drug-smuggling convictions, including cannabis hidden in a false-bottomed suitcase in 1995 and cannabis in luggage on return from Jamaica in 2002 and 2003, as well as a drug-import conviction in Sweden. [@1,2]

He denied importing cannabis and possessing it with intent to supply but was found guilty after a trial at Birmingham Crown Court. [@1,2] He was sentenced to seven-and-a-half years of imprisonment at the same court on 20 August 2021. [@1,2]
