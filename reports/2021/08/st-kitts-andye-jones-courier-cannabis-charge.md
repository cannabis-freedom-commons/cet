Police in St Kitts disclosed on 17 August 2021 that Andye Jones of Gillard
Meadows had been charged over a drugs bust at a courier service. [@1] The bust
had been reported about a week earlier. [@1] In a statement that afternoon,
police said Jones was arrested and charged in relation to a quantity of
cannabis. [@1] The cannabis was concealed in a package that cleared Customs on
11 August 2021 through a local courier service which employed him. [@1]

The St. Kitts and Nevis Customs and Excise Department brought four charges
against him. [@1] These were importing restricted goods, importing goods
concealed in containers holding goods of a different description, and importing
goods packed to deceive a customs officer. [@1] The fourth charge was knowingly
attempting fraudulently to evade customs over the importation of a restricted
good. [@1]

Jones was bailed on the same day in the sum of $9,000 with two sureties. [@1] He
was required to appear at the Basseterre Magistrate Court on 17 August 2021.
