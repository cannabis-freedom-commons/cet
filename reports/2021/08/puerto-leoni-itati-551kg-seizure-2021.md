Argentina's Naval Prefecture members seized 551 kilograms of marijuana in two
control operations in Misiones and Corrientes, detaining one of the people
involved. [@1] The first operation took place in Puerto Leoni, Misiones, where
PNA officers were patrolling Puerto Agriman at kilometre 1711 of the Paraná
river. [@1] Officers spotted a car coming out of a dirt track beside the river
with several bundles inside. [@1] The driver and his companion fled when they
saw the officers and abandoned the car. [@1]

The abandoned car held 21 bundles with more than 471 kilograms of marijuana
divided into 664 pressed panes. [@1] On the orders of the Oberá federal
prosecutor's office the drug was seized, and the PNA established that the car
used to carry it had been stolen. [@1] In Itatí, Corrientes, the Prefectura
seized another 80 kilograms of marijuana hidden in the boot of a car during a
vehicle check 600 metres from National Route 12. [@1]

When officers found the drug they detained the person carrying it. [@1] The
Itatí load was divided into 99 packages, and the man involved was held
incommunicado on the orders of the Corrientes federal court. [@1] Both
operations took place in strategic zones defined by the force with the National
Security Ministry for preventive checks against drug trafficking. [@1]
