Several marijuana plants grown in pots were seized during a search by the Drug
Prosecutor's Office of Panamá Oeste and personnel of the National Police
Intelligence Directorate (DNIP). [@1] The cultivation was found in the locality
of Chicago, within the corregimiento of Chicá, a unit of local government, in
Chame district, Panamá Oeste. [@1] TVN Panamá dated the find to Friday 20 August
2021 and placed it at a residence in the Chicago sector of Chame district. [@3]
TVN Panamá said the search was carried out by officials of the Drug Prosecutor's
Office of Panamá Oeste and agents of the National Police. [@3] The prosecutor's
office confirmed that no one was apprehended in the judicial operation. [@1]

The seedlings were being grown in an improvised greenhouse. [@1] Inside a
residence, Public Ministry staff found containers and plastic envelopes holding
processed marijuana. [@1] Some of the containers holding the processed marijuana
had a label attached. [@1] Officers also seized 3,385 dollars, which were found
in the dwelling. [@2]

The authorities said it was the first time that year that they had located a
greenhouse for cultivating marijuana. [@1]
