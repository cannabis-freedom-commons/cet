Units of the national gendarmerie seized one and a half tonnes of Indian hemp
and arrested six members of a drug-trafficking network, including its leader,
who holds a foreign nationality. [@1] The prosecutor of the wilaya of
Nouakchott-Ouest, Judge Ahmed Abdallahi El Moustapha, announced the result on
Saturday at a press conference at the western-zone headquarters of the
gendarmerie general staff. [@1] Le Calame dated that announcement to Saturday 21
August 2021 and said the six people, led by a foreigner, were in custody. [@2]

The prosecutor said the cargo had entered Mauritania by boat through
intermediaries whose role was limited to landing it, so that another team could
move it on to another country. [@1] Le Calame reported that the seized quantity
was cannabis concentrate and Indian hemp, brought in by pirogue from the
Atlantic Ocean. [@2] It had been stored in an area of the capital before a group
moved to collect it and try to take it out of the country. [@2]

The seizure followed several months of close surveillance involving various
units of the national gendarmerie, and the suspects were arrested as they
received the shipment. [@2] The prosecutor praised the gendarmerie for seizing
that quantity of drugs and for arresting and dismantling a network active in
trafficking the illicit product. [@1]
