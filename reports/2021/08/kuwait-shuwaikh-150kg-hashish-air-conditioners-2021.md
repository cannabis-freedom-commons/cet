Kuwaiti customs officers seized nearly 150 kilograms of hashish hidden inside
water-cooling air conditioners in a container at Shuwaikh port. [@1] The General
Administration of Customs said the haul had been “hidden in a tight and secret
way”. [@1] The customs administration said those responsible for the shipment
were arrested. [@1] Customs officers called in the Interior Ministry's anti-drug
squad, and those involved were being questioned, Al Bawaba reported. [@3]

The customs administration said the container had been shipped from Iran. [@1]
The director-general of customs, Jamal Al-Jalawi, said Iran, Lebanon, Pakistan
and other countries posed a high risk of drug smuggling. [@2] The customs
administration said it would take legal action against would-be drug and
contraband smugglers. [@1]

Kuwait applies tough penalties in drug cases, including the death penalty. [@1]
