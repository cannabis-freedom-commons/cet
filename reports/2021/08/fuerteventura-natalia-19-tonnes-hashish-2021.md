Two Customs Surveillance patrol boats, the Sacre and the Halcón, set out on the night of 7 August 2021 and boarded the Palau-flagged merchant ship Natalia in the early hours of 8 August, about 40 miles southeast of Fuerteventura in international waters. [@1] The boarding came from a joint Tax Agency and National Police investigation, Operation Natalia, with France's DNRED customs service. [@1]

Officers found the ship leaking and at risk of sinking, kept her afloat, and brought her into the port of Las Palmas on the Sunday night. [@1] A joint search uncovered 638 bales holding 19,876 kg of hashish hidden among a declared cargo of plaster, plus 20 kg of high-potency cannabis in the crew quarters. [@1,2]

The eleven crew, all Syrian nationals, were arrested and were to be brought before the National High Court. [@1] The ship had drawn attention after its name was changed to Natalia and its flag from Togo's to Palau's, and it was sailing from Lebanon to Lagos via the Turkish port of Iskenderun when Irish and French intelligence, developed through the MAOC (N) Irish desk in Lisbon, led to the interception. [@2] The Irish Times put the haul's estimated value at €400 million and reported that investigators believed the drugs were bound for Morocco and onward to Italy or Greece. [@2]
