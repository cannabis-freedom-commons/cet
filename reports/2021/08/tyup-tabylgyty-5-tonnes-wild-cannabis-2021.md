Officers of the Interior Ministry's drug control service (SBNON) for the
Issyk-Kul and Naryn regions, together with border service troops, found
wild cannabis growing almost two metres tall in the border zone with
Kazakhstan, at the Tabylgyty section of Tyup District. [@1] The find was
reported by Stalbek Usubakunov, head of the press service of the Issyk-Kul
regional police. [@1]

The officers burned five tonnes of the wild cannabis on the spot. [@1] The
report gives no plant count, no area cleared and no arrests. [@1]
