Gendarmes of Gendarmería Nacional searched two homes in Santiago del Estero city
on Sunday 8 August 2021. [@1] The searches were carried out as part of an
investigation for infringement of Law 23.737 on narcotics. [@1] The searches
followed eight months of investigative work by the officers. [@1] In the
coordinated work officers identified two members of the group, one of them
living in Buenos Aires province and described as the presumed supplier of the
illicit substances. [@1]

Members of the Complex Crimes and Judicial Procedures Investigations Unit
'Santiago del Estero' carried out the searches with support from Squadron 59,
the Information Recognition Unit and the Criminalistics and Forensic Studies
Unit. [@1] In the presence of witnesses, officers seized 86 kilos 360 grams of
marijuana and other elements of interest to the case. [@1] Federal Court No. 2
ordered the detention of the two citizens involved and the seizure of the drugs
found and other elements of interest to the case. [@1]
