Two Customs Surveillance patrol boats, the Sacre and the Halcón, set out on the night of 7 August 2021 and boarded the Palau-flagged merchant ship Natalia in the early hours of 8 August, about 40 miles south-east of Fuerteventura in international waters. [@1] The ship was leaking and at risk of sinking, and officers kept her afloat into the port of Las Palmas on the Sunday night. [@1,2]

The boarding came from Operation Natalia, a joint Tax Agency and National Police investigation with France's DNRED customs service. [@1] Irish and French intelligence developed through the MAOC (N) Irish desk in Lisbon had tracked the vessel after it changed its name to Natalia and its flag from Togo to Palau while sailing from Lebanon toward Lagos via Iskenderun in Turkey. [@2]

A joint search found 638 bales holding 19,876 kg of hashish hidden among a declared cargo of plaster. [@1,2] The Irish Times put the haul at an estimated €400 million. [@2] Officers also found high-potency cannabis in the crew quarters; the Irish Times puts that find at 20 kg and The Maritime Executive at 26 kg, and neither weight is counted in the hashish figure. [@2,3]

The eleven crew, all Syrian nationals, were arrested and were to be brought before the National High Court. [@1] Investigators believed the hashish was bound for Morocco and then Italy or Greece. [@2]
