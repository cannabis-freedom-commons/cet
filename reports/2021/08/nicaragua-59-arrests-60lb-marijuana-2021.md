Nicaragua's National Police captured 59 people accused of high-danger crimes
during the week of 9 to 15 August 2021. [@1] Commissioner General Sergio
Gutiérrez said three were held for criminal homicides, 36 for robbery with
intimidation and 20 as drug suppliers. [@1] A second outlet gave the same
breakdown and said the arrests occurred in different parts of the country
between Monday 9 and Sunday 15 August. [@2]

In the districts of Managua, police held 11 alleged drug suppliers and seized 15
pounds of marijuana plus half a kilo of cocaine, along with a car and cell
phones. [@1] In Laguna de Perlas in the South Caribbean, officers detained two
people with 29 pounds of marijuana and a .38-calibre firearm, while in
Chinandega three people were held with 5 pounds of marijuana. [@1] Police also
reported two people caught with 6 pounds of marijuana in Jinotega, one person
with 2 pounds in Estelí and another with 2 pounds of cannabis in Madriz. [@1]

Across the roundup, police listed as evidence 11 firearms, half a kilo of
cocaine, 60 pounds of marijuana, a car and two motorcycles. [@1] Canal 2
corroborated the same weeklong result, saying 59 accused were arrested and
officers seized 60 pounds of marijuana, 11 firearms and 2 motorcycles. [@3]
