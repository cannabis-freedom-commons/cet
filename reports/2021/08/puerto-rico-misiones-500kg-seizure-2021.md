Argentina's Naval Prefecture seized almost 500 kilograms of marijuana that was
to be brought into the country through the coast of Puerto Rico, Misiones. [@3]
Officers deployed with Rango, a narcotics-detection dog, and found 21 bundles
holding 573 pressed marijuana bricks left abandoned in the area. [@1] Criminal
investigation work had identified a sector near kilometre 1,721 of the Paraná
River as the point where the load would be moved. [@1]

The second official account says the marijuana was to be moved from Paraguay to
Argentina through that sector near kilometre 1,721 of the Paraná River. [@2]
That account puts the weight at close to half a tonne and values the load at
more than 50,500,000 pesos. [@2] The Federal Prosecutor's Office of First
Instance in Oberá, led by Viviana Alejandra Vallejos with secretary Arlindo Otto
Kurtz, intervened in the case and ordered the drugs seized. [@1]

The ministry presented the operation as part of federal-force work focused on
seizing large quantities of illegal drugs. [@1]
