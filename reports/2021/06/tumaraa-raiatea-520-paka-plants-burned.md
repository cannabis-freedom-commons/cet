On Monday 28 June 2021, acting on a tip, soldiers of the local gendarmerie
brigade found a total of 520 cannabis plants in the garden of a private resident
at Tumaraa on the island of Raiatea. [@2] The local gendarmerie brigade seized
those 520 cannabis plants, called paka, in the commune of Tumaraa. [@1] The paka
plants were taken from a private garden after an intervention based on
information. [@1] A gendarmerie communiqué said officers went to the home of a
Tumaraa resident and seized 520 paka plants grown in his garden. [@3] The public
prosecutor of the Republic at the court of first instance in Papeete reported
the seizure of pakalolo, the local name for cannabis, in a statement. [@2] The
person identified in the operation was made the subject of a judicial procedure
under the authority of the public prosecutor in Papeete. [@2]

All the drugs seized were destroyed by incineration. [@1] The gendarmes of
Raiatea were put on the trail of a paka plantation by a tip, and on 28 June they
recorded 520 cannabis plants in the private garden at Tumaraa. [@3] The previous
Monday, 520 cannabis plants were seized at Tumaraa in Raiatea, found by the
local brigade in a private garden, and the whole seizure was destroyed by
incineration. [@4] The person concerned was under a judicial procedure directed
by the prosecutor, and the seizure was burned. [@4]
