Police in Nicaragua detained 52 people in the week to 14 June 2021 during
operations against crimes they described as dangerousness offences. [@1]
Forty-one of them were suspected of robbery with intimidation and two of
homicide. [@1] The police chief named robbery suspects in Managua and said one
of them used a taxi to commit offences with others. [@1]

Evidence taken from the detainees included 166 pounds (about 75.3 kg) of
marijuana and half a kilogram of cocaine, along with 19 firearms, one vehicle
and six motorcycles. [@1] Police said the detainees and the evidence would be
handed to the competent authorities. [@1]

The National Police said it constantly runs operations to imprison anyone who
threatens Nicaragua's security. [@1] It said it was continuing special plans
with reinforced patrols in neighbourhoods, markets, bus stops and bus terminals,
parks, roads and tourist destinations. [@1] The force also listed school
security, road safety and rural security plans. [@1] It said it remained
committed to strengthening security for people, families and communities in
Nicaragua. [@1]
