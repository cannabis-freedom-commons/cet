Indonesia's National Police criminal investigation department (Bareskrim Polri) and a joint team found and destroyed seven hectares of ready-to-harvest cannabis fields in the Mount Leuser area of Beutong, Nagan Raya, Aceh, on 30 June 2021. [@2] The operation followed the development of a trafficking-network case spanning Aceh, Medan, Palembang, Bogor and Jakarta. [@2] Polri announced the destruction on 2 July 2021, reporting that 592 kg of dried cannabis was confiscated. [@1]

Four suspected people were secured during the operation. [@2] Officers worked alongside local residents to clear the fields, as ANTARA's photos of the operation show. [@2]
