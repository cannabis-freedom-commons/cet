Gendarmería Nacional's Santa Fe judicial procedures unit, with other units of
the force and the Policía Federal, carried out ten simultaneous searches. [@1]
Gendarmería Nacional described the group as an alleged criminal drug band that
divided, distributed and ran neighbourhood sales points for cocaine and
marijuana. [@2] The band was dismantled in the Villa del Parque barrio of Santa
Fe during the weekend. [@1] In four homes in the city, gendarmes found and
detained the alleged head of the organisation, Luis Ojeda, known as "Cachito",
and arrested three women said to belong to the retail network. [@1]

The searches produced 73 cannabis plants and 245.9 grams of chopped marijuana.
[@1] Officers also seized 46.6 grams of cocaine, which is not counted in the
cannabis figures. [@1] Officers took 265,188 pesos in cash, 11 mobile phones and
a tablet. [@1] A federal judge ordered the four held under Law 23.737,
Argentina's drug law, and the confiscation of the drugs, money and devices. [@1]

The case began with months of intelligence work, surveillance and following that
gathered evidence on how the group operated. [@1] The Fiscalía Federal N° 1 de
Santa Fe joined the case with a parallel file that the Fiscalía Federal de
Rafaela held against one of the suspects, and the court ordered searches in both
cases at the same time. [@1] The Policía Federal carried out six further
searches under that parallel case. [@2] Vía País reported that investigators
said the gang handled drug that arrived from Paraguay. [@3]
