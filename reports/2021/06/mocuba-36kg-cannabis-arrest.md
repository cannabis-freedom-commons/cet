Polícia da República de Moçambique (PRM) officers detained a 36-year-old man in
Muaquia, Mocuba district, Zambézia, while he was transporting 36 kilograms of
cannabis sativa, locally called suruma. [@1] A second account reported the same
place, the man's age and the 36 kg of cannabis sativa. [@2]

Zambézia police spokesperson Sidner Lonzo provided the information on Monday.
[@1] Lonzo said the detainee had been denounced by neighbours as a producer and
seller of suruma. [@1] MMO Notícias likewise named Sidner Lonzo and reported the
neighbour denunciation. [@2]
