Police in Takeo province's Kiri Vong district burned twelve marijuana farms on
Thursday 10 June 2021 in the foothills and valleys of Preah Bat Chonchum
commune. [@1,2] The farms covered a 2,865-square-metre area, or about 0.29
hectares. [@1,2]

Captain Sem Sok, chief of the district's Anti-Drug Unit, said the owners of
the farms fled before police arrived. [@1,2] Police seized thousands of
marijuana plants as well as other related growing materials from the farms,
then destroyed them on site. [@1,2] Sok said police were searching for the
owners to bring them to justice. [@1,2]
