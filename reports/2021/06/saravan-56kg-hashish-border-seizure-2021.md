Iranian border forces confiscated 56 kilograms of hashish from drug traffickers
in the border area of Saravan town, in Sistan and Baluchestan province. [@1]
Border Guard Commander Brigadier Ahmad Ali Goudarzi said on 28 June 2021 that
the confiscated load weighed 1,006 kilograms in all, of which 950 kilograms was
opium and 56 kilograms was hashish. [@1] He said the traffickers had been trying
to move a large shipment of narcotics into the country’s central regions and
were caught in an ambush on the way. [@1]

Goudarzi said that when the traffickers realised police had spotted them, they
engaged in an armed conflict with the security forces. [@1] After heavy clashes
they escaped, leaving behind their vehicles and the shipment of narcotics. [@1]

The seizure was announced as Iranian police intensified operations against
smugglers for the national anti-drug week, held at the same time as the
International Day Against Drug Abuse and Illicit Trafficking, marked on 26 June.
[@1] The same account said drugs moving through Iran mainly come from the
eastern parts of the country that border Afghanistan and Pakistan. [@1]
