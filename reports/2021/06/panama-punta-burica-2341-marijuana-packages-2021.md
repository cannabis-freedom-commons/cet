Senafront units stopped a boat south of Punta Burica on 19 June 2021 and
detained three Nicaraguan citizens aboard. [@2] A guarantee judge in Chiriquí
legalised the three arrests, charged them with international drug trafficking
and ordered provisional detention. [@2] The public prosecutor's office said it
had six months to complete the investigation. [@2]

The Chiriquí drug prosecutor's office and Senafront seized 2,341 packages south
of Punta Burica, in Barú district. [@3] The drug was hidden in about 40 sacks
aboard the vessel. [@3] Senafront said the packages contained marijuana. [@4] Mi
Diario put the load at about 2.4 tonnes of marijuana. [@2] Officers took the
packages to the Progreso base in Barú, where drug prosecutors counted them. [@3]

Panama's authorities announced the seizure on Sunday 20 June 2021. [@1] The
operation took place in Pacific waters south of Punta Burica, in the Gulf of
Chiriquí. [@1] That first announcement described the packages as an unidentified
illicit substance and gave no total weight. [@1]
