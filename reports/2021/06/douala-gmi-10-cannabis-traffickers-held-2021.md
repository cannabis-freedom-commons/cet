Police in Douala presented about ten suspected cannabis traffickers and robbers
to the press on 21 June 2021. [@1] They were arrested during a routine police
patrol at Ngwelle, by Bonabéri in Douala's 4th arrondissement. [@1] Officers of
the Groupement mobile d'intervention No 2 first detained a cannabis holder and
used his information to break up the network of ten suspects. [@1]

Divisional commissioner Owona, commander of GMI No 2, said the gang leader was a
man named Paul Ngouh, a detainee who ran the operations from his cell in
New-Bell prison. [@1] The suspects were described as young traffickers and
robbers operating in Cameroon's economic capital and its surroundings. [@1]

Police seized four sacks and 20 bales of chanvre indien (Indian hemp), six
machetes, two daggers, a motorcycle, two laptops, four fans, two gas bottles and
a carton of adulterated whisky. [@1] The suspects were to be brought before the
courts to answer for their actions. [@1]
