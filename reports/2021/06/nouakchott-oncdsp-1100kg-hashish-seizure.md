The prosecutor of the wilaya of Nouakchott-Ouest, Judge Ahmed Abdallahi
Moustapha, said the national police, through the National Office for the Control
of Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (ONCDSP), had recently seized 1,100 kg of
Indian hemp in Nouakchott. [@1] He announced the result at a press conference on
Friday morning at the ONCDSP headquarters in Nouakchott-Ouest, and said the
seizure came from infiltrating the gang that had brought the drugs into
Mauritania. [@1]

Police had followed the case since the previous Ramadan, when one member of the
gang was arrested and officers learned that a drug shipment was entering the
country. [@1] After a long investigation and infiltration to learn the cargo’s
route, storage place, the people involved and the place of distribution,
officers arrested the person responsible for storage. [@1] They first seized 357
kilograms of hashish, then found the main stock of more than 750 kg of hashish,
bringing the total of that substance seized to about 1,100 kg. [@1] Noticing
police vigilance, the gang delayed distribution of the product. [@1]

The prosecutor congratulated the national police and ONCDSP for seizing that
quantity and the gang members involved in trafficking it. [@1] ONCDSP
commissioner El Hacen Moustapha Samba said the seizure and the dismantling of
the gang that brought the cargo into Mauritania were the result of long
professional work, not chance. [@2]
