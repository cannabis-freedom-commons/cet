Soldiers of the Nicaraguan army's 1st Regional Military Command detained a
driver at a checkpoint on the Las Brisas river bridge, in the Santa Rosa
community northwest of Jalapa, Nueva Segovia. [@1] The operation took place
before dawn on Tuesday 15 June 2021, near the Honduran border. [@1] The detained
man was José María Ochoa, 49, from the municipality of Jalapa, driving a white
Isuzu pickup with plate NS 2014. [@1] He had entered the country from Honduras
through the unopened Cerro de Jesús María crossing. [@2]

In the vehicle the soldiers found 11 red sacks holding an estimated 1,030
"óvulos" (egg-shaped packages) of marijuana. [@1] A later army summary of its
first-semester results put the load at 437.14 kg of marijuana. [@4] The army
said the consignment came from Honduras and was apparently to be sold inside
Nicaragua. [@1] Colonel José Hilcias Rizo Rizo, head of the 1st Regional
Military Command, gave the details of the operation. [@1]

Ochoa, the pickup and the drug were handed to the National Police for expert
examination and field testing. [@1] The army said the seizure was part of its
state "Muro de Contención" strategy and its plan to protect the country's
borders. [@3] The army said its troops followed Ministry of Health COVID-19
protection measures during the operation. [@2]
