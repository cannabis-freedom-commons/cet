Panama's National Aeronaval Service and the U.S. Joint Interagency Task Force
South, using intelligence from the Colombian Navy, seized 243 kilograms of
marijuana from a go-fast boat about 85 nautical miles southeast of Punta Mala,
in Pedasí district, Los Santos province. [@2] In a third operation about 30
nautical miles northeast of the same area, the authorities stopped a second boat
carrying 861 kilograms of marijuana and 50 kilograms of cocaine. [@1]

The Navy said the two marijuana stops were part of three international maritime
operations in which 2,054 kilograms of narcotics were seized while being carried
toward Central America. [@1]

The Navy said the three results stopped the trafficking organisations from
receiving more than 10 million dollars. [@1] It said the operations prevented
the distribution and consumption of 2,700,000 doses of illicit drugs. [@1] The
Navy said the results came from coordinated work and interoperability among
countries in the region under international cooperation agreements against
illicit drugs. [@2]
