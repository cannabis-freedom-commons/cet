The Senegalese navy intercepted a boat carrying cannabis resin off the
country's Atlantic coast on Sunday 6 June 2021. [@1,2,4,6] The interception was carried out by the patrol boats Kédougou and Anambé and the Navy's special forces, with support from an Air Force maritime patrol aircraft. [@5] The vessel flew a
Togolese flag and was stopped 140 kilometres from Dakar with seven crew members
on board. [@1,2] The crew comprised four Indians, a Mauritanian, a Syrian and a Libyan, and the navy took them into custody. [@4,5,6]

Officers escorted the boat to a naval base in Dakar, where they found 8.37
tonnes of hashish divided between 279 bags. [@1,2,5] The boat and its cargo were escorted to the Admiral Faye Gassama base in Dakar. [@5] The boat was also carrying
sacks of plaster. [@1,2,3,4,5] The plaster carried in the same hold weighed 340 tonnes. [@6] The armed forces ministry announced the seizure in a
statement on the Monday and gave no street value for the drugs. [@1]

The vessel was the cargo ship ASSO 6, IMO 7946875, 66 metres long and 8 metres wide, sailing under the Togolese flag. [@3,4,6] Maritime tracking put ASSO 6 at the Moroccan port of Safi on 29 May 2021, after it reached Nouadhibou in Mauritania on 22 April, and it was expected at Abidjan on 13 June. [@3,4,6]
