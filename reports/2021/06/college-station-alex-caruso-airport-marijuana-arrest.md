Los Angeles Lakers guard Alex Caruso was arrested and released on Tuesday for
possession of marijuana and drug paraphernalia, according to Texas A&M police.
[@1] Both charges are misdemeanors. [@1] Around 2 p.m. Central Time, Caruso
tried to board a flight at Easterwood Airport in College Station, Texas, which
is located on A&M property. [@1]

The Transportation and Security Administration searched Caruso's bags and found
a herb grinder that contained marijuana. [@1] Officers were contacted by TSA
agents at the airport after they found what they believed to be drug
paraphernalia. [@2] The item located was a grinder with marijuana residue. [@2]
Texas A&M University Police was the arresting agency because Easterwood Airport
is located on university property. [@2]

According to online records, Caruso was arrested for possession of less than two
ounces of marijuana. [@1] The 27-year-old College Station native was arrested on
charges of possession of marijuana, less than two ounces, and possession of drug
paraphernalia. [@2] Caruso has been released on bonds totaling $4200. [@2]
