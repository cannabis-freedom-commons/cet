Officers of Argentina's Naval Prefecture seized more than 1,200 kilograms of
marijuana in two operations in Puerto Rico, Misiones. [@1] Nobody was detained
in the two operations. [@1] The first operation came in the early hours of
Tuesday, when patrols at Puerto María Elena, at kilometre 1,722 of the Paraná
River, detected a truck driving in an unauthorised area with several bundles
inside. [@1] The occupants fled on seeing the patrol and abandoned the truck.
[@3]

Officers searching the truck bed, which carried a seizure order after being
reported stolen, found 15 bundles holding more than 320 kilograms of marijuana
buds. [@2] Hours later, coast guards near Arroyo Federal spotted a dirt track
running into dense vegetation. [@2] Following it, they found bundles of
marijuana flowers and pressed blocks known as panes hidden in the scrub,
weighing more than 924 kilograms. [@1]

The Federal Prosecutor's Office in Oberá intervened in both cases and ordered
all the drugs confiscated. [@1] Authorities estimated the value of the two loads
at more than 125 million Argentine pesos. [@4]
