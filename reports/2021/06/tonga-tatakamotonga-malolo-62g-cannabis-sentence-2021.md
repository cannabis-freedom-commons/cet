Tevita Malolo, a 45-year-old man, was sentenced to eight months' imprisonment
for possessing cannabis found at his home in Tatakamotonga. [@1] Because the
62.05 grams he held exceeded seven grams, the court sentenced the possession as
supply under an amendment to the Illicit Drugs Act. [@1]

Police seized 62.05 grams of cannabis, equivalent to 0.06205 kilograms, during a
search of Malolo's home in Tatakamotonga. [@1] Officers also seized utensils
found alongside the cannabis at the same home. [@1] The search and seizure took
place in 2020, before the June 2021 sentencing. [@1]

Under the amended Illicit Drugs Act, possession of drugs weighing over seven
grams must now be sentenced as supply rather than simple possession. [@1] The
Attorney General's judgments list records the matter as Rex v Tevita Malolo (CR
88/21), with sentencing remarks delivered by Cooper J on 8 June 2021. [@2]
Matangi Tonga published its report of the outcome on 10 June 2021. [@1]
