Policía Nacional officers arrested three people at 6 a.m. on Friday 4 June 2021
at a cannabis plantation in the La Unión de Wamblancito community, Wiwilí
municipality, Jinotega department. [@1] Those arrested were Denis Ariel Betanco
García, 21, Félix Lumbí Polanco, 55, and Luz María García Rivera, 39. [@1]
Police said they were arrested as the producers and suppliers of the drug. [@1]
Commissioner General Marvin Castro, the department's police chief, announced the
seizure of 7,700 cannabis plants and of the people who sold the drug at a press
conference in the Germán Pomares police delegation. [@1]

Investigators seized a plantation of 7,700 cannabis plants on land belonging to
the detainees. [@1] They also took two sacks of green herb that a field test
showed to be cannabis, weighing 25 pounds (about 11.3 kg). [@1] A police
technical investigation team inspected and photographed the scene and sent
samples of the herb for chemical analysis, which confirmed cannabis. [@1]

Castro said the operation came under the force's Muro de Contención strategy
against drug trafficking and organised crime and its rural security plan. [@1]
Canal 2 reported the three Wiwilí residents among people police arrested that
week on accusations of supplying drugs. [@2] Police said they would keep running
special patrol plans in neighbourhoods, markets, bus stops, parks and rural
areas. [@1]
