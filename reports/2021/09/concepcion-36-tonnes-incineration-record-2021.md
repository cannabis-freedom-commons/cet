On 17 September 2021, agents of Senad, Paraguay's anti-drug agency, incinerated
36 tonnes of marijuana at the Internal Defense Operations Command base in
Concepción department. [@1] Senad called it the largest drug incineration ever
carried out in the country, beating a 2014 burn of 26 tonnes of drugs seized
in Capitán Bado, Amambay department. [@1]

The armed forces' Joint Task Force had found the drug on 23 July during a
routine flyover in Paso Barreto district, watching from a helicopter as people
loaded bags onto large trucks at a rural estate in a partly wooded area. [@1]
Service members arrested four people who were storing the shipment, which was
bound for Brazil, and part of it had already been loaded onto a truck. [@1]

After intelligence work, Senad activated Operation Horse and on 16 September
carried out six raids in Pedro Juan Caballero, Horqueta and Concepción,
capturing the shipment's owner and his main collaborators. [@1] Senad named
the alleged ringleader as Rodrigo Florencio Pedrozo Agüero, alias Kavajú, who
was already under arrest. [@1]
