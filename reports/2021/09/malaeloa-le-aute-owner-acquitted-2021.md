A six-member jury in American Samoa's High Court acquitted Le Aute sports bar
owner Tumuatasi 'Mama' Lefatia of all four drug counts. [@3] The verdict came at
the end of a four-day trial, after the jurors deliberated for a little over an
hour. [@3] The clerk read the verdict at about 3:50 pm and Lefatia was in tears.
[@3] One juror told Samoa News the evidence showed no illegal drugs were found
on Lefatia and that many people had access to the room where a pouch of drugs
was found. [@3] Assistant Attorney General Kristine Soule declined to comment on
the verdict. [@3] Defence lawyer David Vargas thanked the jury for doing a great
job and following the court's instructions. [@3] At trial Lefatia testified that
the search warrant was to search for guns and ammunition, not drugs. [@3]

Armed detectives from the Department of Public Safety's vice and narcotics unit
searched the Le Aute Sports & Bar compound in Malaeloa in the early morning of
12 September 2018. [@3] They arrested the bar's owner, Tumuatasi 'Mama' Lefatia,
the only person charged after the raid. [@2] The raid began at 6:10 am and
officers found Lefatia in a bedroom in one of the buildings on the north-west
corner of the property. [@2] The search of that bedroom produced a clear baggie
of hand-rolled cigarettes with a green leafy substance and $70 in cash. [@2] A
second baggie held 19 more hand-rolled cigarettes with the same green leafy
substance. [@2] Field tests at the police substation found the green leafy
material was marijuana and a white crystalline substance was methamphetamine.
[@2] Officers also found on a shelf in a laundry room what appeared to be
marijuana seeds. [@2] RNZ reported that the early morning raid by armed police
found cigarettes containing marijuana, bags of crystal methamphetamine and
various drug paraphernalia. [@1]

Lefatia was charged with four counts: possession of crystal methamphetamine with
intent to distribute, possession of marijuana with intent to distribute, and two
counts of possession of unlawful substances. [@2] A District Court judge set
bail at $100,000. [@2] After her initial appearance she was returned to the
Territorial Correctional Facility. [@2] The warrant was issued in the name of
her son, Manu Lefatia, in a felony case. [@2] Her son, an inmate at the
Territorial Correctional Facility, was accused of shooting a man in Leone on 12
August. [@2]

In 2015 police found guns and illegal drugs at Le Aute in one of the largest
raids in the territory's history, and Lefatia had served time after an earlier
drug raid at the bar. [@1]
