Korean-American rapper Killagramz, then 29, was arrested at his home in southern
Seoul on suspicion of possessing and smoking marijuana. [@2] A neighbour
reported a 'smell of burning mugwort and thick smoke' and police found the
rapper with marijuana in his kitchen. [@1] He initially denied the allegations
but confessed after officers found cannabis powder and inhalers at the house.
[@2] He said he had bought about 400,000 won (about $350) worth of marijuana
from an unidentified person in Itaewon, central Seoul, the previous December,
and was investigated without detention. [@1] Yeongdeungpo Police Station in
western Seoul said on Wednesday 3 March 2021 that it had booked him on charges
of violating the Narcotics Act. [@2]

He apologised on social media, saying he had admitted all charges, submitted
evidence voluntarily and would accept legal punishment. [@1] The Seoul Southern
District Prosecutors Office referred the case to court in May 2021 on charges of
violating the Narcotics Control Act. [@3] At a first hearing on 21 June 2021
prosecutors sought a one-year prison term and a 200,000 won surcharge. [@4]
Under the immigration law, a foreigner given even a suspended prison term can be
deported. [@4]

On 16 September 2021 the Seoul Southern District Court sentenced him to one year
in prison, suspended for two years, plus 80 hours of community service. [@5] The
court said it granted the suspension partly because he had not passed cannabis
on to a third party. [@5]
