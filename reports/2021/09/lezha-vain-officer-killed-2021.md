Deputy Commissar Saimir Hoxha of the Lezha police was shot dead at a roadblock
in the Vain area on 28 September 2021 while his unit was checking a suspected
marijuana route. [@1] Officers had signalled a suspect vehicle to stop when a
man inside opened fire, wounding Hoxha, who died on the way to the hospital.
[@1] Police named the suspected gunman as 21-year-old Aldi Rama, previously
convicted of narcotics and weapons offences. [@1]

Hoxha, 44, was killed by a bullet to the heart while conducting checks on
cannabis cultivated by criminal groups in Lezha. [@2] Rama carried earlier
convictions for narcotics cultivation and illegal possession of weapons. [@2]
After the shooting, numerous police units including special forces and a
helicopter were deployed to find the gunman. [@1] Officers surrounded Rama in
the Lezha area, where he shot himself and was taken to hospital in critical
condition. [@2]
