Prefectura Naval Argentina officers seized more than 2,300 kilograms of
marijuana from a truck and detained two men in the Misiones town of Garuhapé.
[@1] The procedure took place on Ruta Nacional 12 at Garuhapé and began when
officers saw a truck whose plate appeared falsified, because the registration
was older than the vehicle. [@2] Officers questioned the driver and found his
documentation incomplete, and under the timber load they saw bags wrapped in
packing tape. [@1] A second man then produced a waybill for the timber and gave
an account of the trip that did not match the driver's. [@1]

The load was divided into 2,548 packages of marijuana weighing more than 2,300
kilograms. [@1] The force valued the shipment at more than 240 million pesos.
[@1] A dog trained to detect drugs, Otto, marked the cargo positively, and the
search followed. [@1] The seizure followed a Prefectura Naval investigation, and
the marijuana was hidden among timber beams in the truck. [@2]

The case went to the federal prosecutor's office and the federal court in Oberá.
[@3] A local outlet reported that the driver and the owner of the transport were
from Chascomús in Buenos Aires province and that the owner was a former police
commissioner. [@3] El Fuerte Diario, citing unofficial information, said the
truck's owner was a retired senior officer of the Buenos Aires provincial
police. [@4]
