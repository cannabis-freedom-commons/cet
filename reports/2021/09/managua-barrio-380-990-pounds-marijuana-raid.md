National Police officers raided a house in Barrio “380”, District III of
Managua, at 9:00 a.m. on Tuesday 14 September 2021. [@1] Officers arrested
Delvis Ricardo Grijalva Somarriba and Daniel Ernesto Delgado Pérez as alleged
perpetrators of organized crime and drug trafficking. [@1] Police said the two
detainees would be brought before the competent authorities for prosecution for
organized crime and drug trafficking. [@1]

Inside one room officers found 11 sacks of different colours holding 974 oval
packages wrapped in adhesive tape containing green herb. [@1] A field test was
positive for marijuana, with a weight of 990 pounds and 49 grams (about 449.1
kg). [@1] Police criminalistics experts carried out a chemical test that
concluded positive for marijuana. [@1] Officers also applied the Scintrex Trace
particle-detector test to the seized vehicle, the clothing and the hands and
backs of the two detainees, with positive results. [@1] Officers seized a Kia
car, plate M 08165, allegedly used to transport the drug. [@1] Police valued
each of the 974 packages at 100 dollars, for a total of more than 97,000
dollars. [@1]

According to police information, the load had reached Managua from western
Nicaragua. [@1]
