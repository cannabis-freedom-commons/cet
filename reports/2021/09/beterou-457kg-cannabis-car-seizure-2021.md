Customs officers of the Borgou-Alibori Rapid Intervention Service stopped a
Toyota Carina 3 registered BK 2212 RB coming from Djougou on the Djougou-Parakou
road. [@2] Officers ordered the car to stop at Bétérou, but the driver ignored
the order and continued toward Parakou. [@2] Near the Bétérou secondary school
at Kika village, the driver lost control, abandoned the damaged car and fled.
[@2] Officers towed the car to the Bétérou police station and took six parcels
found on board to the service headquarters in N’Dali. [@2]

The parcels held 497 slabs of cannabis. [@2] The slabs had been packed in jute
sacks on board the car. [@1] Customs weighed the cannabis at 457.82 kilograms.
[@2] Bénin Web TV estimated the quantity found in the car at 457 kg. [@1]
Security sources said the car had left Djougou for Parakou. [@1] The driver was
reportedly a foreign national. [@1]
