Senior Principal Magistrate Thomas Muraguri Mwangi sentenced James Muthiora, alias Karinta, and his wife Fridah Karimi to life imprisonment on 29 September 2021 for jointly trafficking 103 kilograms of bhang, worth KSh 3,090,000, at Kooje Estate in North Imenti, Meru. [@1,2] The pair had been arrested on 1 November 2018 alongside three other people, who were acquitted for insufficient evidence. [@1,2]

The court also punished the couple for possessing utensils used to prepare narcotics: five more years in prison, or a fine one outlet puts at KSh 4.5 million and the other at KSh 4.6 million. [@1,2] The couple called the jail terms harsh and excessive next to the evidence, and the court gave them 14 days to appeal. [@1,2]
