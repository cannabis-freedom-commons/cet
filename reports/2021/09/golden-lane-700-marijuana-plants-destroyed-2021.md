One man was arrested when officers of the Shirvan Road Police Station carried
out an anti-crime exercise on Thursday 9 September 2021. [@1] Acting on
information received, the officers went to Sea View Trace, Golden Lane. [@1] The
person detained was a 44-year-old man of Golden Lane. [@1]

The officers seized and destroyed 450 fully grown marijuana trees and 250
seedlings at Sea View Trace. [@1] Police valued the mature trees and young
seedlings together at $130,000. [@1]

The exercise was coordinated by ASP Joseph and spearheaded by Sgt Anderson
Alfred and Ag. Cpl Daniel. [@1] Members of the Trinidad and Tobago Regiment also
took part. [@1] Police said investigations were continuing. [@1]
