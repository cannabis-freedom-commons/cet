Namibian police arrested 92 suspects found in possession of drugs in September
2021. [@2] The arrests were made between 1 and 30 September, police spokesperson
Deputy Commissioner Kauna Shikwambi said. [@2] Of those arrested, 86 were
Namibian, three Angolan, two Congolese and one Tanzanian national. [@2]

Police valued the drugs seized during the month at N$829 130. [@1] Cannabis was
the largest seizure: 78,035 kg valued at N$780 350. [@1] Officers also seized
214 mandrax tablets valued at N$25 680. [@2] The same roundup included 41 g of
cocaine powder worth N$20 500, 11 units of crack cocaine worth N$1 100 and 3 g
of methamphetamine, known locally as tik, worth N$1 500. [@2]

Namibia has in recent years seen an increase in illicit drug use, with cannabis
the most commonly used, while incidents involving cocaine and synthetic
stimulants have also been reported. [@1] The police believe this has made
Namibia a haven for drug smugglers and traffickers and is turning the country
into a transit and consumer country. [@1]
