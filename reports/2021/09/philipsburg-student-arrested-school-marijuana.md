Another student was arrested at a school in Sint Maarten on Wednesday 1
September 2021 for the possession of marijuana. [@1] The marijuana was
discovered during a routine check of students' bags. [@1] Police were notified
of the find around 7:30 am and officers went to the school to make the arrest.
[@1] The teenager remained detained pending further investigation. [@1]

Soualiga Newsday attributes the account to a Sint Maarten Police Force statement
issued on Wednesday. [@2] The Kiko Ta Pasando reprint carries the same police
account that another student was arrested at school for marijuana possession.
[@3] A later Prosecutor's Office roundup describes this as the next-day case
after the 31 August Vocational School seizure, when yet another student at the
same school was found with marijuana during a routine security check on
September 1. [@4] The previous day's Vocational School case is a different event
in which the teenager was found with a pellet gun and marijuana in a backpack
with notebooks and lunch. [@5]

The Police Force of Sint Maarten said it understands how challenging it is for
parents to deal with children when it involves selling or use of drugs among
teenagers. [@1] Police said parental and community involvement in combating
drugs is paramount as children are exposed to drugs through various avenues at
increasingly younger ages. [@1]
