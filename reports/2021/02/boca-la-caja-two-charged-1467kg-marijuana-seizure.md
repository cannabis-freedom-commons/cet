The Specialized Drug Prosecutor charged two people, a man and a woman, after an
operation in Boca la Caja in the San Francisco district of Panama City. [@1] The
two were charged with money laundering, drug storage, aggravated drug
possession, aggravated weapons possession and offences against the internal
personality of the State. [@1] A guarantees judge validated the arrest, the raid
and the charging, ordering provisional detention for the man and house arrest
for the woman on humanitarian grounds, a decision the Public Ministry appealed.
[@1]

The raid was carried out on 9 February 2021, seizing drugs, firearms and 121,180
dollars in cash. [@1] According to the Public Ministry, officers found 25
packages of cocaine weighing 30 kilograms and 14.67 kilograms of marijuana. [@1]
Officers found an R15 rifle, two 9 mm pistols and unexploded ammunition. [@1]

Officers raided three houses and found GPS communication equipment hidden in a
false bottom under the sink in one residence. [@1] The operation was carried out
jointly by the Public Ministry and the National Police. [@1] Senior drug
prosecutor Javier Caraballo said it was an incipient operation aimed at
dismantling a high-profile criminal network. [@1]
