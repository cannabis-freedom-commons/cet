Two men were arrested late on Wednesday 3 February 2021 in connection with a
burglary and theft from a medical marijuana shop in Butte, Montana. [@1] Quentin
Johnson, 40, of Kilgore, Nebraska, and Dane Jackson, 40, of Des Moines, Iowa,
both face felony charges of burglary and misdemeanor theft. [@1]

Police accuse the two of breaking into the Green House Marijuana Dispensary at
1040 Harrison Avenue and stealing marijuana during the evening. [@1] Officers
tracked footprints in the snow from the business and eventually found Johnson
and Jackson at the Bus Transfer Station on Harrison Avenue, the same avenue as
the dispensary. [@1] Both remained in jail on Thursday 4 February 2021, the day
after the arrest. [@2]
