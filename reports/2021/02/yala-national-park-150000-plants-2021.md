Police officers from the Gonaganara, Athimale, Dambagalla and Siyambalanaduwa
stations destroyed about 150,000 cannabis plants cultivated inside Yala
National Park on 5 February 2021 and arrested two suspects. [@1] Police
spokesman DIG Ajith Rohana said the arrests were made during a special
operation, one of at least 20 anti-narcotic operations conducted that day.
[@1]

The two suspects were held for cultivating cannabis inside the park. [@1]
The report names no suspects, gives no area for the cleared cultivation and
reports no charges or later court outcome. [@1]
