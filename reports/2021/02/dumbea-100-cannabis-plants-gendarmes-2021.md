Gendarmes in Dumbéa, New Caledonia, seized about a hundred cannabis plants in
the commune the day before the report was published. [@1] The gendarmes had the
support of the Peloton de surveillance et d’intervention à cheval (Psic) of
Déva, a mounted intervention unit. [@1]

The gendarmes also found 120,000 francs in cash, heating lamps, cannabis resin
and various other products. [@1] The gendarmerie said the person responsible was
held in police custody (garde à vue) at the time. [@1]
