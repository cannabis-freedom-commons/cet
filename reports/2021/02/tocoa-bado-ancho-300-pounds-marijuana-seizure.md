Honduras's Directorate for the Fight against Drug Trafficking, the DLCN, carried
out an operation in the Bado Ancho sector, within the jurisdiction of Tocoa in
the department of Colón, and seized suspected drugs there. [@1] Officers
recovered 300 pounds of dried grass that the authorities described as presumed
marijuana. [@1] The material was found in three sacks, each weighing 100 pounds.
[@1] The Public Ministry presented the operation as a new drug seizure in Colón.
[@1]

The DLCN coordinated the confiscation with prosecutors of the Public Ministry,
Honduras's prosecuting authority. [@1] The ministry said the operation followed
investigations maintained by its anti-drug detectives against criminal
structures. [@1] Those structures were operating in Colón and the Aguán
corridor, the river valley running through the department. [@1]
