Prefectura Naval Argentina detained five men at a clandestine crossing into
Argentina at Puerto San Alberto, in the Misiones town of Puerto Rico. [@1] The
five were detained as they tried to enter Argentina through a disused border
crossing. [@3] Investigative work had pointed to a marijuana load crossing at
kilometre 1,744 of the Paraná River, in the area known as puerto San Alberto.
[@1] Officers surprised the men handling bundles on the riverbank, and they
fled. [@1] After a wide sweep and a pursuit through the monte, the scrub
woodland around the crossing, the force caught all five. [@1] The detention was
on Wednesday 20 October 2021. [@2]

The load was 1,253 bricks of marijuana. [@1] The force put its weight at more
than 1,387 kilograms. [@1] It valued the load at more than 144.6 million pesos.
[@1] The ministry's summary described the bricks as weighing more than 1,300
kilos. [@1]

The Federal Court in Oberá, under Judge Alejandro Marcos Gallandat Luzuriaga
with Virginia Guida as secretary, took charge of the case. [@1] El Litoral
reported, citing police sources, that the five were placed at the court's
disposal and that the court ordered the load seized. [@2]
