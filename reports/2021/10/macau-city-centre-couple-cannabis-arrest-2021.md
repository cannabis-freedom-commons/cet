A local couple was arrested in the city centre on Thursday for selling drugs, as
Judiciary Police spokeswoman Lei Hon Nei said at a Friday press conference. [@1]
The two are a 27-year-old jobless man surnamed Fei and his 33-year-old wife
surnamed Cheang, who said she works as a beautician. [@1] The Judiciary Police
had recently received a tip-off that a local man was selling cannabis at night
entertainment venues. [@1] Officers identified Fei as the suspect and arrested
him and his wife in the city centre on Thursday night. [@1] The couple were
transferred to the Public Prosecutions Office on Friday. [@1] Both face a drug
trafficking charge, while Fei also faces drug abuse and possession of
drug-taking paraphernalia charges. [@1]

Officers searching their home seized cannabis weighing 39.52 grammes, 34.9
grammes of powder suspected of containing cannabis, 3.8 grammes of cannabis
cream and cannabis oil weighing approximately 87.50 grammes including the
containers. [@1] The cannabis items together weigh about 165.72 grammes. [@1]
The same seizure included 1.32 grammes of happy powder and 21 LSD drug stamps,
which are not counted in the cannabis total. [@1] Police put the estimated
market value of all the seized drugs at 80,000 patacas. [@1] Some drug-taking
paraphernalia and drug-packaging items were also found in the flat. [@1]
Evidence displayed at Judiciary Police headquarters included five smartphones,
two digital scales and a blender alongside the seized drugs. [@1]

Under questioning, Fei admitted that he has a drug habit but refused to reveal
where he got the drugs. [@1] He told police that he had purchased the drugs a
few months ago from a drug dealer for several thousand patacas. [@1] Officers
believe that Fei had been selling drugs for at least two to three weeks. [@1]
The Judiciary Police are continuing their investigation to trace the origin of
the drugs. [@1]
