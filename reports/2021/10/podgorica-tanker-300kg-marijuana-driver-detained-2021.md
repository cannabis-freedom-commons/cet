After his hearing, senior state prosecutor Tatjana Begovic ordered Muhamed
Mehmet, known as Hadzija, detained for up to 72 hours on suspicion of smuggling
about 300 kilograms of marijuana. [@6] Mehmeti was arrested on 5 October at the
Customs Terminal in Podgorica after drugs were found in the tanker truck he was
driving. [@3] Prosecutors ordered the detention of the Bosnian-Herzegovinian
citizen because of the risk of flight. [@1] Defence lawyer Damir Lekic said it
was questionable whether Mehmeti even knew drugs were in the truck he was
driving. [@1] Lekic said the place where the load was found, the circumstances
in which it got there and whether the accused knew it was there were
questionable. [@1] Investigating judge Vladimir Novovic of the High Court in
Podgorica later found the senior prosecutor's proposal justified and ordered the
Bosnian-Herzegovinian citizen detained. [@3] Lekic said he would appeal the
investigating judge's decision. [@3]

A large quantity of marijuana was seized at the Customs Terminal in Podgorica.
[@2] The Revenue and Customs Administration unofficially told Vijesti that about
300 kilograms of marijuana had been seized. [@2] Customs officers, working with
the Police Administration, found the drugs hidden in the tanker truck. [@2]
Police said a MAN tanker truck driven by M. M. from Sarajevo was scanner-checked
at the customs terminal, revealing an improvised stash in the front of the
tanker holding 54 packages of drugs weighing about 280 kilograms. [@4] Customs
chief Sinisa Raicevic said a scanner showed about one-fifth of the tanker had
been converted into a special smuggling bunker. [@4] The truck had entered
Montenegro from Albania and was bound for Bosnia and Herzegovina. [@2]

The Revenue and Customs Administration said the driver was deprived of liberty
by order of the Higher State Prosecutor's Office in Podgorica on reasonable
suspicion of unauthorised production, possession and distribution of narcotic
drugs. [@1] Raicevic said the tanker arriving from Albania was sent to the
Podgorica customs terminal for detailed control. [@5]
