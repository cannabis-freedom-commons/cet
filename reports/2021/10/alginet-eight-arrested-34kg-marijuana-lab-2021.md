Montenegro's Police Administration said that a joint action by Montenegrin and
Spanish police arrested eight members of a criminal group, four of them
Montenegrin citizens, in the Valencia area. [@1] The force said the arrests
followed a long-running international investigation codenamed Bubestis. [@1]
Police said the suspects were held on suspicion of drug trafficking and of
creating a criminal organisation. [@1]

According to the force, the group had been in Valencia since April 2021 and had
rented a large hangar in Alginet with the aim of installing a marijuana-growing
laboratory inside it. [@1] Police described the group as engaged in the illegal
production and smuggling of marijuana. [@1] When officers searched the hangar,
they found and seized 27 kilograms of dried marijuana buds next to the
plantation. [@2]

A further search of premises the group used in Valencia turned up another seven
kilograms of marijuana. [@1] Officers also confiscated 10,000 euros in cash, a
luxury vehicle, forged documents and the complete equipment from the laboratory.
[@1] Police said they were continuing to take further measures and actions in
the case. [@1]
