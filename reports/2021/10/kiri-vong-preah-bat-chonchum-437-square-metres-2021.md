Takeo provincial deputy governor Khan Sokha led police to search for a
marijuana plantation in the mountains of Preah Bat Chonchum commune in Kiri
Vong district on Saturday 2 October 2021. [@1,2] Police found marijuana
planted at four locations covering 437 square metres, along with four water
storages, and burned the crop. [@1,2]

Police said the owners of the plantations had escaped by the time officers
arrived. [@1,2] Sokha said it was hard to catch the growers because they were
among villagers who climbed the mountain to gather forestry products, and that
police were tracking them down to bring them to justice. [@1,2]
