Eben District's chief administrator in West Arsi, Dagnu Warritu, said a cannabis
plantation had been discovered in farms in Buko Wolda kebele of the district. [@1]
The discovery followed sudden searches of suspected areas based on information from
residents who were concerned about its cultivation. [@1] The administration seized
the narcotic plants loaded onto three trucks. [@1]

District police head Abu Teshite said four suspects were arrested, and searches were
under way to bring others who might have taken part in the crime before the law. [@1]
