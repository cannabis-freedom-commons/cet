Four people were in custody after a joint counter-narcotics operation seized
cocaine and marijuana with a combined estimated value of BDS$4.9 million, the
Barbados Coast Guard said. [@1] The operation was carried out by the Barbados
Coast Guard, the Royal Barbados Police Force and the Regional Security System.
[@1] The Barbados Coast Guard is the maritime unit of the Barbados Defence
Force. [@1]

The coast guard announced the seizure in a media release on the afternoon of 2
April 2021. [@1] The operation was described only as recent. [@1] The coast
guard did not name the four people or say where the interdiction took place.
[@1] It also did not say whether they had been charged. [@1] The BDS$4.9 million
estimate covers the cocaine and marijuana together. [@1] The estimate was given
in Barbados dollars. [@1] The coast guard gave no separate weight for the
marijuana. [@1]

The coast guard said it would continue to monitor and secure Barbados'
territorial waters in pursuit of national security interests. [@1]
