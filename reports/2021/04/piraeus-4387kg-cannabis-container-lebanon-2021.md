Greece's financial crimes squad seized 4,387 kilograms of processed cannabis in
a shipping container at Piraeus, the country's main port. [@3] The drugs were
packed into a hidden compartment built inside an iron tank among three
industrial cupcake-making machines, the squad said. [@3] The container arrived
by sea from Lebanon on 14 April 2021 and was due to leave by train for
Bratislava, Slovakia, on 20 April, passing through North Macedonia, Serbia and
Hungary. [@1] The squad estimated the street value at about 33 million euros.
[@3]

The Financial Crimes Squad, known by its Greek initials SDOE, searched the
container on 16 April after intelligence from the US Drug Enforcement
Administration. [@1] Officers found a manhole on the roof of the tank and opened
it to reach the storage space, which held 129 nylon bags and 282 bulk packages
of processed cannabis. [@3] The DEA office in Athens and Saudi Arabia's General
Directorate of Drug Enforcement provided intelligence for the operation, the
squad said. [@2] The squad carried out the operation with Greek and foreign drug
enforcement agencies. [@2]

An investigation into the shipment is continuing. [@4]
