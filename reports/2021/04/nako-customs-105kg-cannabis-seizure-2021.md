Officers of the Nako Customs Post, under post chief Customs Controller
Souleymane NIKIEMA, acted on intelligence and tailed the suspected trafficker
before seizing his transport and the drug load. [@2] The tail and seizure
occurred on Wednesday 14 April 2021 at about 19:40 in the evening. [@3] The
South-West Regional Customs Directorate said the operation ended a plan to
introduce and distribute cannabis herb on Burkinabe territory. [@2]

The load seized was 105 kilograms of chanvre indien, described as cannabis. [@1]
The seized property comprised one man's motorcycle and the 105 kilograms of
cannabis, taken together as transport and drug. [@3] Customs estimated the value
of the 105-kilogram seizure at eleven million CFA francs. [@1]

Nako lies in the same south-western zone near the borders with Côte d'Ivoire and
Ghana where an earlier seizure had been made weeks before. [@1] The Director
General of Customs congratulated South-West Regional Director, Divisional
Inspector Franck Eric Joël OUEDRAOGO, and all the Nako post officers for the new
seizure. [@2] He also called on the public to report any fraud or illicit
trafficking in goods that came to their knowledge. [@2] The customs
communication service said the information came from the Directorate General of
Customs Facebook page. [@3]
