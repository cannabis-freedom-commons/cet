Police in Nador foiled an international drug-trafficking attempt on Friday 16
April 2021 and seized 4,779 kg of cannabis resin in Douar Lahyane, about 15 km
from the city in the Dar El Kebdani area. [@1] Acting on precise information
from the Directorate General of Territorial Surveillance (DGST), officers
found part of the resin in a car and the rest in a house in the village. [@1]

The haul comprised 47 packages hidden in the car at Dar El-Kebdani town and 94
more packages inside a house belonging to the traffickers, according to a
Moroccan police statement. [@2] One person was arrested while investigations
continued into the trafficking routes of the seized drug. [@2]
