Nicaragua's National Police said it captured 63 people accused of robbery with
intimidation, homicides and drug trafficking in the week before 12 April 2021.
[@1] Commissioner General Sergio Gutiérrez, second chief of the Managua police,
said the captures took place between Sunday 5 and Monday 11 April. [@1] Nine of
those held were accused of drug trafficking, five of homicide and 50 of robbery
with intimidation. [@1]

Police listed 14 firearms, 3 kilograms of cocaine, 22 pounds (about 10 kg) of
marijuana, two motorcycles and two vehicles as evidence taken in the detentions.
[@1] In Managua, police arrested Eduardo Montes in district VII, Banis Zapata in
district IX and José Medina as drug suppliers and seized 10 pounds of marijuana
from them. [@1] In Bonanza, police arrested Socorro Rizo Guido with 4 pounds of
marijuana. [@1] In Teustepe, police arrested Miguel Ángel Sovalbarro Henríquez,
described as a drug supplier, with 2 pounds of marijuana. [@1] In Matagalpa,
police arrested Junior Blancher Corea and David López Urrutia as drug suppliers
with a revolver, 1 pound of marijuana, 40 rocks of crack, two motorcycles, six
mobile phones and cash. [@1]

The week's drug arrests also included Emir Smith Calero Rayo, held in Chontales
with 3 kilograms 381 grams of cocaine, which is not counted with the cannabis.
[@1] The rest of the roundup covered robbery and assault arrests in Managua,
Chontales, Chinandega and León. [@1]
