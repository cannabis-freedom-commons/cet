Police acting on information about stolen vehicles and firearms searched a farm on the R101 outside Kraaifontein at about 15:00 on 10 April 2021. [@1,2]

They found 272 small dagga plants, 184 dagga trees and a large amount of processed dagga with a street value of over R10,000. [@1,2]

A 37-year-old man and a 35-year-old woman were arrested for dealing in dagga. [@1,2] A 51-year-old man who arrived during the search and offered officers a R5,000 bribe to release the suspects was arrested for bribery. [@1,2] All three were due in the Kuils River Magistrate's Court. [@1,2]
