Acting on an anonymous tip, officers of the Police des Stupéfiants et des
Drogues (narcotics and drugs police) went to Logoualé at about 00:30 on Saturday
3 April 2021 and, using stratagems, apprehended a woman in the act of handing
over 69 boulettes of cannabis. [@2] She was caught in flagrant délit of cession
of the 69 boulettes just before officers searched her home. [@1]

The narcotics and drugs police seized 600 grams of cannabis herb and two
calibre-12 shotguns in the woman's home, according to the Direction Générale de
la Police Nationale (Dgpn). [@1] The woman told police the weapons were her
husband's property. [@4] Her husband fled before the police arrived and a search
notice was issued against him, the police said. [@1] The seized weapons were
handed over to the criminal police for the needs of the investigation. [@1]

Logoualé is in the Tonkpi region of western Côte d'Ivoire. [@3]
