The driver of a white pickup truck ignored a police stop order in El Chorrillo
and chose to flee. [@1] Officers pursued the truck and apprehended the suspect
in the Cinta Costera sector. [@1] National Police units captured one man after
the pursuit in Cinta Costera on the previous afternoon. [@4] The driver was
detained and handcuffed when the pursuit ended in Cinta Costera. [@4]

The chase ended with the seizure of five packages of suspected marijuana. [@1]
Police retained the car in Cinta Costera after the arrest. [@1] An immediate
search of the vehicle turned up suspected illicit substances and escort lights.
[@1] Inside the car, officers also found a digital scale and escort lights used
to evade the authorities. [@3] Julio Wong, the commissioner heading the Metro
Oeste Police Zone, said the five packages and escort lights were used to try to
evade the authorities. [@2]

Wong said police operations would continue constantly during Holy Week. [@1] The
Public Ministry said the Drugs Prosecution would bring the detained man before a
guarantees judge within hours. [@3]
