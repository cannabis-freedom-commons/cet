Two homeless Albanian men who were in the UK illegally ran a cannabis factory in
the disused Club XS nightclub on West Walls in Carlisle. [@1] Police went to the
building after members of the public reported the smell of cannabis in October
2021. [@1] Officers raided the property on 12 October 2021 and arrested Andiol
Mucolli, 25, and Iirjan Jaho, 42, as they left. [@2]

The operation was spread across nine rooms of the former nightclub. [@1]
Prosecutors said 1,692 cannabis plants were in production. [@2] More than 170 of
the plants had already been harvested. [@2] An expert put the potential street
value of the class B drug at about £1.6m. [@1] The rooms had ventilation,
lighting, temperature control and irrigation, and the electricity supply had
been bypassed. [@1] Empty fabric softener bottles suggested attempts to mask the
smell of the drug. [@1]

Both men admitted producing cannabis at Carlisle Crown Court. [@1] Judge
Nicholas Barker jailed each of them for two years. [@2] The judge rejected their
claim that they were not paid for their work. [@2] Their lawyer said they had
been brought to the UK via Belgium and London and owed substantial sums to the
people who trafficked them. [@2] Mucolli told police he had lived in the former
nightclub for the previous three months. [@2] The judge said both men were
likely to be returned to Albania when they were released. [@2]
