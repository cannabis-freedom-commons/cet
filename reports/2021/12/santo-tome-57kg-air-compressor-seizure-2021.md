Gendarmes of Escuadrón 57 “Santo Tomé” opened a parcel that had been intercepted
the previous Friday at kilometre 669 of Ruta Nacional 14 when they checked a
shipping-company truck with a semitrailer travelling from Posadas to Nueva
Pompeya. [@1] In Santo Tomé, Corrientes, Gendarmería Nacional seized 57 kilos of
marijuana hidden in an air compressor. [@2]

The narcotics-detection dog Hiena marked an air compressor as containing drugs
inside it. [@1] With authorisation from the Federal Court of Paso de los Libres,
the gendarmes cut open the tank. [@1] Inside the tank officers found a
cylindrical metal container hiding 87 bricks wrapped in packing tape. [@1]

Criminalistics officers confirmed by Narcotest field test that the load was
cannabis sativa with a total weight of 57 kilos 45 grams, which the gendarmes
seized. [@1] The compressor was being carried as parcel freight from the
Misiones city of Posadas to the Buenos Aires neighbourhood of Nueva Pompeya when
it was stopped. [@2]
