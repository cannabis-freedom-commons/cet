Police arrested a 54-year-old man from Tatakamotonga for cultivation of
cannabis. [@2] A second account described the same week's cultivation matter as
a criminal charge. [@1]

Officers searched the accused person's residence on Sunday, 12 December 2021.
[@2] Officers uprooted 43 cannabis plants from the residence during the Sunday
search. [@2] Police said the 43 uprooted plants weighed 45.08 grams in total.
[@2]

In the statement, police acknowledged close collaboration with Tonga Customs and
Tonga Prisons in the Kingdom's fight against drugs and in keeping borders
secure. [@2] The statement framed the arrests as part of the Kingdom's fight
against drugs. [@2] Police said a holiday operation would be mounted as part of
efforts to achieve safe homes, safe roads and safe communities. [@2] Ahead of
the festive holidays, police urged responsible consumption of alcohol and use of
a sober driver or taxi. [@2] Police also urged those going to sea to take
precautions and check the weather forecast before heading out. [@2] Police asked
the public to help keep communities safe. [@2]
