Police from the Agoué arrondissement station broke up a drug den installed in an
abandoned hotel at Hillacondji on Thursday 23 December 2021, in an unannounced
raid based on intelligence. [@1] Officers found twelve people in the hotel, two
of them minors. [@1] Ten people were arrested, among them two women. [@2] The
two minors were taken in by the police. [@1] The police described the target as
a ghetto, the term used in Benin for a drug den. [@1]

In the hotel rooms officers found a large quantity of chanvre indien, the French
term for cannabis, and other psychotropic substances. [@1] The nature of the
other psychotropic substances was still to be determined. [@2] The group was
squatting the old hotel. [@2]

The ten were accused of detention, use and sale of narcotics. [@2] They were
held and were to be presented to the public prosecutor shortly. [@1] The Agoué
police opened further investigations. [@2]
