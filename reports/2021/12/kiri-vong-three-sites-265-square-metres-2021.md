Takeo provincial police chief Chheang Phannara urged local authorities in Kiri
Vong district’s Preah Bat Chonchum commune to crack down on marijuana
cultivation and ensure no more plantations remained there. [@1] Phannara said in
a December 5 interview that he had led relevant authorities on patrols over the
previous two days because the mountains had become a popular secret location for
growers. [@1] Three cannabis cultivation sites covering 265 square metres were
found and destroyed as a result of the patrols. [@1]

Phannara said growing marijuana in the commune was a practice going back many
generations and blamed its recurrence on the local authorities’ lack of will to
implement the law. [@1] He said he would convene a meeting early the next year
with the relevant authorities, including local police in the district’s 12
communes, to crack down on crime and particularly drug offences including
marijuana. [@1] Commune police chief In Vuth said the illegal cultivation was
not in the village or on a plantation but in the middle of the forest next to
the lake, far from villages. [@1]

Vuth said police had investigated and arrested some villagers involved in the
marijuana trade and sent them to court, but the activity was still happening.
[@1] Vuth said most people in the commune had grown marijuana because dried
leaves fetched between 140,000 and 160,000 riel ($35 and $40) per kg, and
Vietnamese traders came to buy it there. [@1]
