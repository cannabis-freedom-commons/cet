Six people were arrested after Civil Guard maritime officers boarded their
inflatable boat off the Huelva coast on 5 December 2021. [@1] The suspects were
taken ashore to be brought before a court. [@2] The investigation was ongoing,
and it was not clear whether the six, who were not named, had been charged. [@1]

Officers seized 2.5 tonnes of hashish. [@1] The chase took place in an area
between Ayamonte and Isla Cristina, near Huelva. [@1] Officers first saw two
people in a boat that approached an inflatable craft about 12 metres long with
three engines. [@2] The two left their boat and joined the inflatable's crew,
and the six fled, ignoring police signals. [@2] During the chase the suspects
threw bales of hashish and fuel cans into the sea. [@1] The authorities did not
give a value for the hashish. [@1]

Two other Civil Guard vessels and Portugal's National Republican Guard supported
the operation. [@3]
