Lilongwe police arrested 22-year-old Nigerian-origin man Chigozie Framulyne
Ifeamy Chukwu in Lilongwe for alleged possession of suspected cocaine and
cannabis sativa. [@1] Officers arrested him on Thursday at Lilongwe City Mall,
where he was found with seven blisters of suspected drugs. [@1] The arrest
followed a tip-off received by officers at the City Mall Police Unit and relayed
to drug detectives at Lilongwe Police Station. [@1]

Officers then took him to his house in Area 49 Gulliver, where they found loose
cannabis sativa in his bedroom. [@1] Police sent the seized drugs to the
Pharmacy Medicines and Regulatory Authority for analysis. [@1]

He was expected to appear in court to answer a case of being found in possession
of dangerous drugs under Section 4 as read with Regulation 19 of the Dangerous
Drugs Act. [@1]
