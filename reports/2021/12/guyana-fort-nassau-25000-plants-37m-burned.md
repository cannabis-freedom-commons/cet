Guyana Police Force ranks destroyed $37.6 million worth of marijuana at Fort
Nassau Village on the Berbice River on Friday 3 December 2021. [@1] The exercise
was carried out by Regional Division Six along the Berbice River in the vicinity
of Fort Nassau Village. [@2]

Officers found two makeshift camps and two cannabis fields covering about four
acres of land. [@1] The fields held 25,000 cannabis plants ranging from five
inches to five feet in height, and about three pounds of dried cannabis were
also found. [@1]

Police put the street value of the narcotics found at approximately $37,590,000.
[@2] The camps, the cannabis plants and the dried cannabis were all destroyed by
fire. [@1]
