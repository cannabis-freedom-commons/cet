On the night of Sunday 19 December 2021, Moroccan police acting on
information from the domestic surveillance service stopped a utility vehicle
at Oulad Berhil near Taroudant and seized 90 bundles of cannabis resin
weighing three tonnes. [@1,2] A speedboat with two outboard motors was also
seized. [@1,2]

The driver, aged 37, was arrested. [@1,2] Police said he had a criminal record
and was wanted for his role in international drug trafficking networks.
[@1,2] Detectives said they were tracing the seizure to international cartels
and hunting the driver's accomplices. [@2]

The seizure was one of several large resin interceptions around Morocco that
winter, and the agency report noted the country remains one of the
world's largest cannabis producers. [@1]
