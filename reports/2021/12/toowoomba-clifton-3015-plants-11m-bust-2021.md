Around 4.50pm on 7 December 2021, Toowoomba Tactical Crime Squad officers
intercepted a utility on the Warrego Highway near Postsman Ridge Road at
Helidon. [@1] In the rear seat they found a cardboard box with 20 bags of
cannabis, and four more boxes with 78 bags in the rear of the utility, about
44 kilograms with an estimated street value of $264,000. [@1] The driver and
sole occupant, a 37-year-old New South Wales man, was arrested. [@1] A second
outlet confirms the 7 December stop and reports the man spent Christmas in a
Queensland prison on the trafficking charge. [@2]

The arrest led police to a Toowoomba storage facility, where detectives on
8 December found five boxes with about 59 kilograms of cannabis worth an
estimated $354,000. [@1] On 9 December they searched an acreage property on
Willow Springs Road at Clifton and found 3,015 cannabis plants of varying
sizes, worth an estimated $10.5 million, in a well-established crop with an
extensive glasshouse and a drying shed fitted with lighting, irrigation and
ventilation. [@1] The plants were destroyed. [@1]

The man appeared in Toowoomba Magistrates Court charged with trafficking,
producing and supplying dangerous drugs, plus medicine and drug-paraphernalia
counts, and was remanded in custody to 14 February. [@1]
