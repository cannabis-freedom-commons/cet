Police in Vanuatu seized more than 2,000 mature marijuana plants in a single
operation. [@1] Officers destroyed more than 200 young marijuana plants in the
same operation. [@1] The plants were seized from Salili area. [@1] Police
carried out the operation in the week before the 5 November 2021 report. [@1]

Several suspects were arrested and detained after the seizure. [@1] Marijuana is
a prohibited drug in Vanuatu. [@1]
