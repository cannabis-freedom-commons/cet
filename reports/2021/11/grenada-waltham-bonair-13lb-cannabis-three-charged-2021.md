Drug Squad officers and the Victoria Police Station found cannabis and
ammunition in operations in Waltham and Bonair, St Mark, Grenada. [@2] Officers
found 13 pounds of marijuana and 295 rounds of ammunition in the two
communities. [@1] The cannabis was packed as two packages weighing one pound,
valued by police at EC$2,268, and 13 wrapped portions weighing 12 pounds. [@2]
The 12-pound portion was valued at EC$27,216. [@3] The three suspects were
Branford Thomas, 48, a bus driver from Victoria who lives in the United States,
his daughter Nashaera Antoine, 28, a shopkeeper from Bonair, and her friend
Sherma Charles, 28, a nursing assistant from Waltham. [@2] Thomas was arrested
on Monday at Maurice Bishop International Airport while trying to leave Grenada.
[@1]

The three appeared at the Victoria Magistrate's Court on Thursday 11 November
2021. [@2] They appeared before Magistrate Francine Foster. [@1] Charles was
charged with possession of a controlled drug, handling a controlled drug and
possession of ammunition. [@2] Antoine was charged with two counts of
trafficking in a controlled drug and two counts of possession of ammunition.
[@2] Thomas was charged with two counts of trafficking in a controlled drug and
with possession of ammunition. [@2] Attorneys Arley Gill and Deloni Edwards
represented all three. [@1]

The prosecution did not object to bail for the two women but asked the court to
refuse it for Thomas. [@1] Police called Thomas a flight risk because he lives
in the United States and holds a temporary Green Card. [@1] Gill said Thomas did
not know he was under investigation and was leaving the country lawfully. [@1]
Magistrate Foster granted Thomas bail of EC$7,500 with one surety on one
trafficking charge, EC$10,000 with two sureties on ammunition charges and
EC$20,000 each on further ammunition and trafficking charges. [@1] Charles was
granted bail of EC$10,000 with two sureties on the possession charge, EC$10,000
on the handling charge and EC$20,000 on the ammunition charge. [@2] Antoine was
granted bail of EC$5,000 with one surety and EC$15,000 with two sureties on the
trafficking charges and EC$10,000 with two sureties on the ammunition charges.
[@2] All three had to surrender their travel documents and report to the
Victoria police station, the women twice a week and Thomas four days a week.
[@1] Thomas was also ordered to live at a particular household in Victoria. [@1]
They were due back in court on 3 February 2022. [@1]
