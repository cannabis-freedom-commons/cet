Police and military officers in Bolikhamxay Province destroyed 17 illegal
cannabis farms in a joint operation in Oudomxay Village, Viengthong
District, on Tuesday 23 November 2021. [@1] The farms held about 10,180
cannabis plants grown over nearly ten hectares of land. [@1]

Officers arrested a man believed to own one of the farms and burned an
estimated 54 tonnes of cannabis plants on site. [@1] The bust followed the
destruction of a 4.2-hectare, 4,447-plant farm in the same village earlier
that month. [@1]
