Quebec's integrated organized crime squad arrested a 31-year-old man in
Pincourt, west of Montreal, on the evening of 18 November 2021. [@2] The Sûreté
du Québec announced on Friday 19 November that its Organized Crime Enforcement
Squad (ENRCO) carried out the raid in Pincourt, near Ile-Perrot. [@1] Neomedia
reported that officers searched a residence and arrested the 31-year-old
Pincourt man on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@2]

Officers seized a handgun and magazine, another gun described by Neomedia as a
pen gun, about 250 grams of cocaine, about 50 bricks of hashish and several
hundred dollars in cash. [@1]

Police said the suspect was interrogated and released as the investigation
continues. [@1] The ENRCO targets organized crime leaders, including Hells
Angels members, and is made up of SQ officers and investigators from the
Montreal, Longueuil and Laval police departments. [@1]
