Zambia's Drug Enforcement Commission arrested six people for trafficking in
heroin, cocaine and cannabis in Eastern, Lusaka and Copperbelt Provinces, it
said in a statement on Wednesday 24 November 2021. [@1] The commission said the
suspects were in custody and would appear in court soon. [@2]

Benjah Mumba, also known as Danger Mwanza, was arrested for trafficking 382.4 kg
of loose cannabis. [@2] In Lusaka, Ben Shonga, 31, and Hutchingson Muzoka, 39,
both of John Laing, were jointly charged with trafficking 530.3 kg of loose
cannabis. [@2] The commission put the total cannabis seized at 900 kg. [@1]
Sunday Njoku, 43, and Raphael Ganizani, 29, were jointly charged in Chipata with
trafficking 3.99 kg of heroin and 842 g of cocaine destined for Lusaka. [@2] On
the Copperbelt, Oliver Siame, 33, of Chingola was arrested for trafficking 248
sachets of heroin concealed in a desktop computer, and his Toyota Corolla was
seized. [@2] In Lusaka Province, Timothy Kanyanta, 34, of Chirundu District was
arrested for trafficking 361 sachets of heroin. [@2]

DEC assistant public relations officer Julie Mukumwa said the drugs came from
various countries in the region and beyond and that Zambians were being used to
traffic them. [@2] She said such cases had become rampant in Lusaka, Copperbelt,
Muchinga and Eastern provinces. [@2]
