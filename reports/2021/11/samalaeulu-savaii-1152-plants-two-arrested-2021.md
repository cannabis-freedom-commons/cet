Police searched for narcotics at Samalaeulu on Savai'i on the morning of
Wednesday 10 November 2021 and found a marijuana plantation. [@1] Officers
seized 1,152 marijuana plants and three weapons found at the site. [@1]

Two men aged 35 and 23, both from Samalaeulu, were arrested. [@1] Both were
charged with possession and cultivation of narcotics. [@1] The two were held in
police custody at the main headquarters in Tuasivi, Savai'i. [@2] Police
descended on the village early on Wednesday morning, and Acting Commissioner
Auapaau Logoitino Filipo called it their largest haul for the big island. [@2]

Auapaau said police found the plantation after members of the public reported
it. [@2] He said it was the first time a plantation of more than a thousand
plants had been found on Savai'i. [@2] Auapaau said the plants would stay with
police until the court reached a verdict, and that police would wait for a court
order to destroy them. [@2] He said no one was injured in the police operation.
