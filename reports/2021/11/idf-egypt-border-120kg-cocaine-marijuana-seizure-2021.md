Israeli troops foiled an attempt to smuggle drugs worth tens of millions of
shekels into Israel from Egypt on Saturday morning, the military said. [@1]
According to the Israel Defense Forces, soldiers monitoring the area spotted the
alleged drug smuggling attempt and dispatched troops to the scene. [@1] The IDF
said troops fired into the air, but there was no mention of any arrests. [@1]

Forces seized about 120 kilograms (265 lbs) of cocaine and marijuana, with a
street value of tens of millions of shekels. [@1] The drugs were handed over to
the Israel Police. [@1]

The Israeli-Egyptian border has been used for years as a smuggling location,
particularly for marijuana, but for other drugs as well. [@1] The IDF updated
its rules of engagement on Sunday to allow soldiers to more easily open fire at
suspected thieves and smugglers, in a bid to crack down on crime. [@1] Under the
IDF's new open-fire regulations, troops will be permitted to use deadly force in
cases of thefts of weapons and ammunition from military bases, break-ins at
bases and firing ranges, smuggling attempts along the Israeli-Egyptian border,
according to the military. [@1]
