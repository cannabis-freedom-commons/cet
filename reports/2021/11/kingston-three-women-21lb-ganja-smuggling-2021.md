The Narcotics Police charged three women with attempting to smuggle ganja out of
Jamaica. [@1] Sherice Brown, a 20-year-old office clerk from Havana Heights,
Clarendon, was one of the women charged. [@1] Shanalee Green, a 28-year-old
waitress of Mason River, Clarendon, and her 32-year-old sister Judie Green were
the other two. [@1] The three were arrested on 21 and 25 November 2021 during
anti-narcotics operations at Norman Manley International Airport. [@1]

Brown was arrested while waiting to board a flight to Barbados with
approximately four pounds of ganja, which police valued at US$12,000. [@1] The
Green sisters were detained while being processed for a flight to Curacao. [@1]
Shanalee was held with eight pounds of ganja, valued at US$16,000. [@1] Judie
had approximately nine pounds of the drug, worth US$18,000. [@1] Police
described the total amount as more than $7 million worth of ganja. [@1]

The women were scheduled to appear in the St. Andrew Parish Court on 16 December
2021. [@1]
