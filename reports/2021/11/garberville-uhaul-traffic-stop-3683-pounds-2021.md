Humboldt County Sheriff's deputies stopped a U-Haul box truck on Alderpoint Road
near Garberville at about 12:45 a.m. on 5 November 2021 after seeing it driven
at high speed. [@1] Two occupants fled from the truck into nearby vegetation.
[@2] Deputies searched for them but could not find them because of darkness and
the terrain. [@1] Because more people might have been inside the storage area,
deputies searched the truck. [@1] No one was arrested, and the sheriff's office
said the case was still under investigation. [@1]

In the truck deputies found more than 3,683 pounds (about 1.67 tonnes) of
cannabis. [@1] The load carried no California Cannabis Track and Trace
information, the state's legal supply-chain tagging, and was being transported
illegally. [@1] The Humboldt County Sheriff's Marijuana Enforcement Team
responded and seized the cannabis for destruction. [@1]

The sheriff's office asked anyone with information on the identities and
whereabouts of the two occupants to call its tip line. [@1]
