Five people, including a father and son, were arrested in Visakhapatnam (Vizag)
in connection with cannabis traded through an e-commerce platform. [@1] They
were identified as Ch Srinivasa Rao, J Kumaraswamy, B Krishnam Raju, Ch
Venkateswara Rao and Srinivasa Rao's son Ch Mohan Raju alias Rakhi, all
residents of the city. [@1] The Special Enforcement Bureau and Andhra Pradesh
Police made the arrests, its joint director S Satish Kumar said. [@2]

Kumar said Srinivasa Rao was arrested and 48 kg of dry ganja seized from a
rented house at Kancharapalem on 21 November 2021. [@1] Packing materials such
as covers, cardboard boxes, tapes and an electronic weighing machine were also
found in the house. [@1]

Investigators said Srinivasa Rao became a contact for Suraj Pawaiya and Mukul
Jaiswal, two Madhya Pradesh men registered as vendors on the e-commerce site,
who smuggled dry ganja from Vizag to Madhya Pradesh and Rajasthan disguised as
'supernatural' stevia dry leaves. [@1] The pair created a firm called Babu Tex
and registered as vendors using the GST numbers of other firms. [@1] Kumaraswamy
and Krishnam Raju were arrested as associates and Venkateswara Rao as a van
driver. [@1] Srinivasa Rao's son Mohan Raju was also arrested after
investigators found his involvement in the trade. [@1] Kumar said Pawaiya and
Jaiswal were suspected of moving 600 to 700 kg of dry ganja from Vizag to Madhya
Pradesh over the previous eight months. [@1]
