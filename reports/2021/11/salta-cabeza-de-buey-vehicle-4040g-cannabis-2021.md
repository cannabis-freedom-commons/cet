Gendarmes of the Unidad de Investigaciones de Delitos Complejos y Procedimientos
Judiciales “Salta” intercepted a driver described as a member of a
drug-trafficking organisation at the Cabeza de Buey toll post in Salta. [@1] The
Federal Complex Crimes Prosecutor’s Office ordered the driver’s detention and
the seizure of the packages found. [@1]

Forensic staff confirmed the packages held drugs, totalling 4.399 kg of cocaine
and 4.040 kg of “cannabis sativa”. [@1] The 14 packages of different sizes were
hidden between the back seat and the gas tube of the vehicle. [@1] Diario
Panorama described the hiding place as a false compartment behind the CNG tube.
[@2]

The investigation had identified a planned drug shipment from the city of San
Pedro in Jujuy to Tucumán province, and surveillance located the person and
vehicle involved. [@1] With the knowledge of the Federal Court of Salta and
support from Squadron 60 “San Pedro”, officers later raided two premises in
Jujuy province. [@1] Officers detained another member of the organisation there
and seized motorcycles and documents of interest to the case. [@1]
