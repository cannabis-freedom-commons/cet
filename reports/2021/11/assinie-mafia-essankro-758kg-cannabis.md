Ivorian customs officers seized 758.4 kilograms of cannabis at Assinie Mafia at
about 00:30 on 1 November 2021. [@1] The haul consisted of 948 blocks of
cannabis hidden under brush in a rubber (hevea) plantation at Essankro, near
Assinie Mafia. [@1] The Aboisso customs directorate said the seizure was made by
agents of its sub-directorate of surveillance operations and interventions, in
particular the Maféré mobile brigade. [@2] The directorate recorded the place of
the find as Essankro, near Assinie Mafia, and the date as 1 November 2021. [@2]
FratMat reported that the Maféré mobile brigade made the seizure during the
night of 1 November 2021, acting on the account of the Aboisso regional customs
directorate. [@3]

Koaci described the find as another major drug seizure by the Ivorian customs
service. [@1] Koaci, citing security sources, reported that strong suspicions
fell on an international maritime shipping company whose cargoes have repeatedly
been found with drugs around the world. [@1] FratMat published a photograph of
the sacks of cannabis found in the hevea plantation. [@3]
