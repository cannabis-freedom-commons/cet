Police destroyed 60,000 cannabis plants and 600 pounds (about 272 kilograms) of
dried cannabis at the Upper Berbice River in the Ebini area. [@2] Ranks of the
Guyana Police Force carried out the destruction on Wednesday 24 November 2021.
[@1] Police Headquarters said that during the course of the day a party of
police from the Region Six Division conducted the eradication exercise. [@1] The
officers found four fields covering approximately 10 acres (about 4.05 hectares)
of land. [@2]

The plants ranged from six inches to 10 feet in height. [@1] The 600 pounds of
dried cannabis was destroyed with the plants. [@2] At one of the fields a man
saw the police and escaped into the bushes. [@1]

Officers burned five camps along with the cannabis plants. [@1]
