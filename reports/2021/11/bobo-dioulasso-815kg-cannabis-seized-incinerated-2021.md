No traffickers were arrested in either seizure because limited field staffing,
timing and the sensitivity of the mission prevented the brigade from catching
them. [@1] Investigations were continuing to catch those offenders. [@1]

The town gendarmerie brigade in Bobo-Dioulasso incinerated about 815 kg of
cannabis on 18 November 2021. [@1] The drugs had been seized during the last
quarter of 2020 thanks to collaboration from citizens in the Hauts-Bassins
region. [@1] The gendarmerie estimated the seizure’s value at more than
12,000,000 CFA francs. [@1] On 20 October 2020 a patrol discovered five bicycles
loaded with bundles of drugs in the operation zone. [@1] The team seized the
loads while their owners were away from the spot for various needs. [@1]
Weighing the eleven bundles gave a total of 445 kg of cannabis. [@1] On 22
November 2020 the brigade was tipped about a probable crossing within hours by
suspects likely carrying drugs. [@1] A foot patrol found four bicycles each
loaded with two parcels parked in scrub. [@1] Checks on those parcels gave a
weight of 370 kg of cannabis. [@1] The sum of the two seizures was thus 815 kg
of drugs. [@1]

Burkina Faso is a transit country for such traffickers. [@1] The suspects had
left a neighbouring country via Burkina Faso for another neighbouring country.
[@1] The incineration took place with the agreement of the Faso Prosecutor, the
anti-drug committee hierarchy and Bobo-Dioulasso communal authorities on 18
November 2021. [@1]
