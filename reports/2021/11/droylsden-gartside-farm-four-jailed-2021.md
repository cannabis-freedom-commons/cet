Minshull Street Crown Court jailed four men for 27 months each over an
industrial-scale cannabis farm at Gartside Farm on Sunnyside Lane in Droylsden. [@1,2]
Alban Xhika, Erin Canaj, Andri Koci and Saimir Doda, all of no fixed abode,
were sentenced on Tuesday 23 November 2021 after pleading guilty to producing
cannabis. [@1,2]

Police found the farm in February 2021 and spent more than ten hours searching
the site, removing 715 cannabis plants that officers valued at about £670,000.
[@1,2] Officers also seized equipment including three industrial-sized
generators. [@1,2] The four men were found living at the property when they were
arrested. [@2]

The raid was part of Operation Challenger, Greater Manchester Police's
operation against organised crime across the region. [@1,2] Detective Constable
Rick McIvor of the Tameside Challenger unit said the operation was
sophisticated and large-scale and called its removal a step forward in
disrupting criminals profiting from illegal drugs. [@1,2]
