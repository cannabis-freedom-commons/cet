Customs Anti-Narcotics Unit (CANU) officers, acting on intelligence received,
carried out a narcotics-eradication operation at Victoria Village Backdam, East
Coast Demerara, on Tuesday 23 November 2021. [@2] The officers discovered a camp
on a one-acre plot of land that held a quantity of male and female clothing.
[@1] At the camp CANU found 5,000 cannabis plants ranging from three to five
feet in height and fifteen 100-pound salt bags of processed cannabis, equivalent
to 500 kg (1,102 lb). [@3] No one was seen or arrested during the operation.
[@1]

CANU said in a statement released to the media that the cannabis plants and the
salt bags of cannabis were eradicated and that the 500 kg had a street value of
seventy million dollars. [@2] The street value of the 500 kg of cannabis was put
at GYD 70,000,000. [@1] Investigations were ongoing. [@2]
