Police and military officers in Bolikhamxay Province destroyed eight illegal
cannabis plantations in a joint operation in Oudomxay Village, Viengthong
District, on Tuesday 2 November 2021. [@1] The plantations held 4,447
cannabis plants across 4.2 hectares of land. [@1]

Officers arrested a man believed to be the owner of one of the plantations;
the owners of the other seven remain unknown. [@1] The authorities estimated
the destroyed plants at 22,235 kg and burned them on site. [@1]
