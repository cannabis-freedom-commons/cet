The Rachakonda special operations team and Abdullapurmet police intercepted a lorry and a Swift Dzire pilot car at Abdullapurmet on the outskirts of Hyderabad on the morning of 25 November 2021. [@1,2]

The gang was moving ganja from Sileru in Andhra Pradesh to Maharashtra via Hyderabad, hiding 182 packets of about 10 kilograms each under compost bags in the lorry. [@1,2] Officers seized 1,820 kilograms of ganja, Rs 41,000 in cash and seven phones, valued altogether at over Rs 3 crore. [@1,2]

Five interstate peddlers were arrested and the main accused, Sanjay Laxman Shinde, was absconding. [@1,2] Police said the gang bought the ganja at Rs 8,000 per kilogram and planned to sell it at Rs 15,000 per kilogram. [@2]
