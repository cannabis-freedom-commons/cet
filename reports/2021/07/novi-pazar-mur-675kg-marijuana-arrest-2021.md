Two Montenegrin citizens, D.B., 33, and A.M., 34, were arrested in Serbia on
suspicion of unauthorised production and distribution of narcotic drugs. [@1]
Serbia's Interior Ministry said officers found 67.5 kilograms of marijuana in
Novi Pazar the previous night in the car driven by D.B. [@1] The Novi Pazar
police statement said the marijuana was packed in five travel bags and divided
into 63 packages believed to be for further sale. [@2]

The ministry said the drugs were found on the main road at Mur, in the boot and
on the back seat of the Audi driven by D.B. [@1] The stop was in the Mur
settlement just outside Novi Pazar. [@2] A.M. was accused of driving his BMW in
front of the Audi to warn D.B. if he met a police patrol. [@1] The suspects were
also identified locally by birth years, 1988 for D.B. and 1987 for A.M. [@3]

Both were detained for up to 48 hours, after which they were to be taken to the
competent prosecutor's office with a criminal report. [@1] The seizure was
reported as more than 67.5 kilograms of marijuana. [@3]
