The couriers fled when they saw the police arrive and left a large quantity of
drugs abandoned at the scene. [@1] Officers from the Kolda regional narcotics
brigade seized 117 kilograms of Indian hemp, locally called yamba, and two
motorcycles that were carrying the haul towards Guinea-Bissau, at Boguel village
25 km from Kolda. [@1]

The operation took place around 6 a.m. on a track off national road N21 linking
Kolda to Sédhiou, a route traffickers often use. [@1] Dakaractu reported that
the Kolda brigade seized 117 kg near a village it spelled Mboguel, 25 km south
of the department, early in the weekend. [@2] Pressafrik dated the seizure to
Sunday and placed it near Mboguel, putting the operation on 11 July 2021. [@3]

No one was arrested: Pressafrik said officers did not manage to catch the
traffickers, who escaped leaving their motorcycles behind. [@3] Dakarposte
reported that the traffickers came from Basse-Casamance and were heading to
Guinea-Bissau through Kolda. [@1] Pressafrik said the porous borders with
Guinea-Bissau and Guinea make drug trafficking intense in this part of Senegal.
[@3]
