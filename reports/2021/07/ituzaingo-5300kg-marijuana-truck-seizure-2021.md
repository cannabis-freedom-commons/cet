Prefectura Naval Argentina officers seized more than 5,300 kilograms of
marijuana in the city of Ituzaingó, Corrientes, after a large operational
deployment. [@1] The load was being moved by truck and formed part of a criminal
investigation by the National Maritime Authority that had anticipated an
operation on this scale. [@1] To locate the possible drug movement, the force
mounted combined patrols around Lake Yacyretá at kilometre 1,527 of the Paraná
River. [@1]

During intensive tours and searches, personnel found a truck that evasively
appeared to have been recently abandoned but was loaded with many bundles
similar to those used to move marijuana. [@1] Officers inspected the truck's
load and found more than 5.3 tonnes of marijuana inside, packed as 6,887 panes,
or pressed blocks. [@1]

Federal Court No. 2 of Corrientes, under Juan Carlos Vallejos with criminal
secretary María Angélica Lugo, intervenes in the case. [@1] The ministry said
the operation prevents the drug being split and distributed toward the main
urban centres and its later sale and monetisation. [@1]
