Police arrested Ajeesh, 26, of Parayil, Thrikkodithanam, Changanassery, and
Ebin, 20, of Parinthani, Thrikkodithanam, during a vehicle check. [@1] The check
was carried out at Neerpara, on the district border, as the two travelled by car
and motorcycle from the Ernakulam side towards Kottayam. [@1] The motorcycle
rider who arrived with the car abandoned the bike on the road and escaped during
the check. [@1] The check took place at about 2 pm the previous day, 9 July
2021. [@1]

Officers found about 1.7 kilograms of cannabis kept in the car the two were
travelling in and seized it. [@1] The operation followed a tip-off to District
Police Chief D. Shilpa, and the team was led by Vaikom DySP A.J. Thomas and
District Narcotic Cell DySP M.M. Jose. [@1] The team included Thalayolaparambu
station house officer Bins Joseph and sub-inspectors Thomas and Sharanya S.
Devan. [@1] Police took the car and the motorcycle into custody. [@1]

Police said Ajeesh was already an accused in several cases, including cannabis
and assault cases, at the Thrikkodithanam and Changanassery police stations.
[@1] The two were produced before a court and remanded. [@1]
