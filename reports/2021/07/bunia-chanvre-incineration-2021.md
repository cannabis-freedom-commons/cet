The garrison military auditorate in Bunia, the military prosecutor's office,
burned a large batch of cannabis on Tuesday 20 July 2021 in front of military
and political-administrative authorities. [@1] Security services had seized the
cargo the previous weekend and it came from North Kivu, according to the
military press. [@1] The military press said the cargo was being taken from
North Kivu to Ituri. [@2]

The sale of cannabis is prohibited throughout the Democratic Republic of the
Congo. [@1] The first deputy garrison auditor in Bunia, Lieutenant Simon Ntende,
said Congolese law prohibits the sale, cultivation and possession of the
substance. [@1] He said people found holding the product would face the full
force of the law. [@2] Infoslive described the incineration as public. [@2]
Since the state of siege was imposed, military justice has been working to
support the military administration's fight against urban crime. [@2]
