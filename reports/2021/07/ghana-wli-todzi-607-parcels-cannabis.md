Officers of the Ghana Immigration Service's Wli Afegame border post intercepted
607 parcels of suspected Indian hemp at Wli Todzi in Ghana's Volta Region. [@2]
The seizure took place on the night of 26 July 2021. [@1] The parcels were
intercepted after a tip-off. [@2] Border post commander Odoi Jeffery Nii Laryea
told the state news agency GNA that immigration officers on patrol received a
call at about 21:15 reporting a vehicle carrying contraband. [@1]

With reinforcements, the officers went to the scene at about 22:30. [@2] The
parcels were found in a green Toyota Hiace with registration number GN 5733-18.
[@2] The vehicle was travelling from Ghana to Togo via the Wli Todzi route. [@2]
The occupants had fled, and no information was available about them. [@1]

The parcels were counted in the presence of officers of the Ghana Armed Forces,
the National Intelligence Bureau, the Ghana Police Service and the Ghana
Immigration Service. [@2] Wli Todzi lies in southeastern Ghana and borders Togo
at Mount Agumatsa, about 30 km north of Kpalimé. [@1]
