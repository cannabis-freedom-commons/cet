Bosnia and Herzegovina's Border Police arrested four people, all Bosnian
nationals, near Velika Kladuša on Sunday 25 July 2021 on suspicion of organised
crime, including illicit production and distribution of drugs. [@1] The alleged
organised crime group distributed the drug locally, within the country, and in
the European Union. [@1] The four detainees were all citizens of Bosnia and
Herzegovina. [@2]

Border Police seized more than 6,000 hemp plants from a camp for the production
and cultivation of marijuana near the western city of Velika Kladuša. [@1] The
camp was purpose-built and about 6,000 cannabis stalks were found there. [@2]
The Border Police called Escobar its largest and most comprehensive operation
against drug-related crime to date. [@1] A total of 110 police officers took
part, with support from SIPA, Bosnia and Herzegovina's State Investigation and
Protection Agency, and Croatia's Interior Ministry because of the proximity of
the Croatian border. [@1] The operation took place under the supervision of the
Prosecutor's Office of Bosnia and Herzegovina. [@2]

A SIPA Special Support Unit also assisted the Border Police. [@2] The Border
Police said its Covert Operations Department would continue activities in
connection with the operation. [@1]
