An Oklahoma woman was arrested in Killeen on Sunday after police allegedly found
her asleep in a vehicle blocking entry to a local business. [@1] Chrystal Renee
Gupton, 39, of Lawton, Oklahoma, was booked into the Bell County Jail on Monday
charged with possession of a controlled substance in an amount of more than 4
grams but less than 400 grams, a second-degree felony. [@1] According to the
arrest affidavit, a Killeen police officer made contact with Gupton and found
her asleep in a vehicle partially blocking the drive to a business. [@1]

The officer said he noticed an odor he recognized as marijuana. [@1] During a
search of Gupton's vehicle, the officer found several packages that were sealed
and commercially labeled as containing THC, police said. [@1] Gupton was also
charged with driving while intoxicated, a Class B misdemeanor. [@1]

Justice of the Peace Gregory Johnson arraigned Gupton on Monday and set her bond
for the drug-possession charge at $35,000. [@1] The affidavit did not identify
the business whose drive Gupton's vehicle was blocking. [@1]
