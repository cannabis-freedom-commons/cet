Gardaí from the National Drugs and Organised Crime Bureau and Revenue's Customs Service ran a multi-agency operation in Glasnevin, Dublin, on 9 July 2021 targeting organised crime. [@1,2]

They seized 71.52 kilograms of herbal cannabis with an estimated value of €1,430,400, subject to analysis. [@1,2]

A man aged 40 was arrested on drug-trafficking offences and detained at Blanchardstown station under drug-trafficking powers. [@1,2]
