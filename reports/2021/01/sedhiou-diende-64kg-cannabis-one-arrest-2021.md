One person originally from Cumbaja in the Republic of Guinea-Bissau was arrested
following a drugs seizure carried out by the gendarmerie brigade of Sédhiou in
Senegal's Diendé arrondissement. [@1] The brigade said it seized 64 kg of
chanvre indien — the French term for Indian hemp, or cannabis — in the
arrondissement of Diendé on Friday 22 January 2021, along with pharmaceutical
products. [@1] Two motorcycles were also seized, and the gendarmerie said the
machines had served to transport the drugs. [@1]

An investigation was opened and was continuing at the time of the report. [@1]
