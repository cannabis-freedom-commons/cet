At about 12pm on Saturday 23 January 2021, officers of the Garda National Drugs and Organised Crime Bureau, supported by the Garda Dog Unit, searched premises in counties Monaghan and Louth during investigations into people suspected of involvement in organised crime. [@1,2] They seized cannabis herb with an estimated street value of €1,120,000, one firearm with ammunition, and €100,000 in cash suspected to be the proceeds of crime. [@1,2] A 29-year-old man and a 26-year-old woman were arrested on suspicion of drug trafficking and detained at Castleblayney Garda station. [@1,2] Assistant Commissioner John O'Driscoll said organised crime groups kept operating through the COVID-19 pandemic and that the seizure would help disrupt and potentially dismantle the groups targeted. [@1,2]

Both arrested people were then charged and were due to appear before Monaghan District Court on the morning of Monday 25 January 2021. [@3]
