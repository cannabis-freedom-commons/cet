A California pair was arrested on a Wednesday after being caught carrying more
than two pounds of marijuana. [@1] The two people arrested were Marley Morlet,
23, and Garrett Robinson, 28, who both admitted the marijuana belonged to them.
[@1] Bonneville County Sheriff's Office deputies responded around 11 p.m. to
reports of two people arguing in a parking lot on East Iona Road. [@1]

A deputy could smell marijuana from the car as soon as Morlet opened the door.
[@1] The deputy saw a bag with a green leafy substance as Morlet searched for
her identification. [@1] Both Robinson and Morlet admitted owning the marijuana,
which weighed approximately 2.67 pounds (about 1.21 kg). [@1] Officers found two
glass marijuana bongs in the car. [@1]

Robinson and Morlet were arrested and taken to the Bonneville County Jail. [@1]
They were charged with trafficking marijuana, which carries a minimum of one
year and up to 15 years in prison and a minimum fine of $5,000. [@1] They were
also both charged with possession of drug paraphernalia, a misdemeanor. [@1]
Morlet's bond was set at $10,000 and Robinson's was set at $15,000. [@1] Both
were offered release on pretrial supervision if they posted bond. [@1]
Preliminary hearings for both defendants were scheduled for 1:30 p.m. on Feb. 10
in Bonneville District Court. [@1]
