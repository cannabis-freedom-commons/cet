A Monroe County man faced drug-related charges following a traffic stop on
Interstate 390. [@1] The man was identified as 37-year-old Felix A.
Rodriguez-Rodriguez of Rochester. [@1] Sheriff’s Investigator Shawn Whitford
made the 17 September traffic stop while assigned to the Sheriff’s Office
Operation Safe Interstate initiative. [@1] Whitford stopped the vehicle after
observing alleged violations of state Vehicle and Traffic Law. [@1] Deputy
Michael Donals assisted with the investigation. [@1] Rodriguez-Rodriguez was
arrested on 14 January and charged by Whitford. [@1] The charge was third-degree
criminal possession of marijuana. [@1]

The roadside investigation found Rodriguez-Rodriguez in possession of two
plastic bags of suspected marijuana weighing about 15 ounces. [@1] He was
released pending further testing of the suspected marijuana after the September
stop. [@1]

He was turned over to Central Booking deputies at Livingston County Jail for
processing and issued appearance tickets for Town of Geneseo Court. [@1] The
charges did not qualify for pre-arraignment detention under the state Justice
Reform Act. [@1]
