Gardaí attached to the Roscommon/Longford Divisional Drugs Unit searched a house at Aughnacliffe, County Longford under warrant at about 1pm on Saturday 23 January 2021. [@1,2] Inside they found a sophisticated cannabis growhouse spread across two bedrooms and the basement. [@1,2] The recovered cannabis plants were valued at about €45,000 and seized cannabis herb at about €85,000, roughly €130,000 in total pending analysis. [@1,2] A man in his mid-30s was arrested at the scene and detained at Granard Garda station, and the Garda release describes him as charged. [@1,2]
