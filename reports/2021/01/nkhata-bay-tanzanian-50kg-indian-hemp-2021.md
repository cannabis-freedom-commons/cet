Police in Nkhata Bay arrested Casim Mwakawanga, a Tanzanian national, at the
Matete roadblock on 13 January 2021. [@1] Officers stopped a Matours bus at the
roadblock and found two bags of loose cannabis, which they seized. [@1] The two
bags weighed 50 kilograms of Indian hemp, the name Malawi police use for
cannabis. [@1] The arrest followed a tip from well-wishers who saw the man board
the bus at Dwangwa with suspicious luggage. [@1]

Nkhata Bay district police spokesperson Kondwani James confirmed the arrest and
said the suspect had boarded the bus at Dwangwa in Nkhotakota. [@2] Police said
Mwakawanga failed to produce valid travel documents when questioned and would
also answer a charge of entering the country illegally. [@1] He was held in
police custody at Chintheche in Nkhata Bay. [@2] Mwakawanga comes from Kasumulu
village in the Kyera area of Tanzania. [@1]

He was expected to appear in court soon on charges of being found in possession
of cannabis sativa and illegal entry. [@1]
