On Saba, a 60-year-old man with the initials W.B.G. was arrested on Tuesday 19
January 2021 for violation of the Opium Act BES. [@2] Pots with presumed
cannabis plants and plants planted in the yard of a dwelling on Stefanotus
Street in The Bottom were confiscated. [@2] Pots with presumably hemp plants and
hemp plants planted in the ground were found in the suspect's yard, and the
plants were confiscated. [@1]

In a statement issued on Wednesday, the Caribbean Netherlands Police Force KPCN
said the Opium Act prohibits growing, preparing, processing, selling,
delivering, distributing or transporting marijuana. [@2] The Opium Act also
forbids manufacturing marijuana or having it with you, and the police said
criminal investigation and prosecution in enforcing the law is the
responsibility of the judicial authorities and the police. [@1] Police said that
where a cannabis farm in a home is suspected, officers in many cases act,
dismantle the nursery and initiate criminal proceedings. [@2]
