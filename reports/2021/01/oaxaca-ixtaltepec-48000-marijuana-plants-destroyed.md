Members of Mexico's National Guard found and destroyed three plots of apparent
marijuana in the municipality of Asunción Ixtaltepec, Oaxaca, while following up
on investigative work in the state. [@1] The troops came across the cultivated
plots while patrolling dirt roads that lead to community parcels. [@2]

The Guardia Nacional put the combined area of the three plots at about 1,600
square metres (0.16 hectares). [@1] The plots held around 48,000 plants of
apparent marijuana. [@2] Two of the plots measured about 300 square metres each
and the third covered 1,000 square metres, all with a density of 30 plants per
square metre and plants about 90 centimetres tall. [@1]

The troops took representative samples of the plants and placed them at the
disposal of the Fiscalía General de la República, Mexico's attorney general's
office, in Matías Romero. [@1] They then set up perimeter surveillance and
destroyed the crops by incineration. [@2]
