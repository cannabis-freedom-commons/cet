Judiciary Police arrested a 28-year-old Taiwanese man and a 22-year-old local man after finding four cannabis plants, a portable greenhouse, a heating lamp, seeds and growing equipment in a rented flat near Tap Seac. The seized items also included a small number of packaging bags, a digital scale and drug-taking paraphernalia. [@3] The pair had met a year earlier, and police said they believed the plants were grown for sale. Officers came across the operation while investigating another drug case. Police believed the pair had been growing the cannabis for about two months, and no finished cannabis products were found in the flat. [@3] Both men were handed to the Public Prosecutions Office on production and trafficking charges and for possessing drug-taking equipment. [@1] The spokeswoman said the force was still investigating whether the plants were grown at other locations, whether there were other suspects and whether the pair were involved in drug deals. [@3]

The suspects were a 28-year-old Taiwanese man surnamed Cheng who said he worked in public relations and a 22-year-old local salesman surnamed Chan. [@2] Officers arrested Cheng near their home and later arrested Chan while he was working at a shop in a hotel mall in Cotai. [@2] Cheng rented the flat and Chan lived with him. [@2] The pair said the seeds had been mailed to Macau by a friend overseas, and both admitted they were addicted to cannabis. [@3]
