Police in Kadavu arrested 48 people in relation to the cultivation of marijuana
from June to December 2020. [@1] Kadavu Police Station Officer Inspector Josua
Daveta confirmed the arrest total. [@1] Daveta said another four people were
arrested during drug raids conducted in January 2021. [@1] Together the two
periods account for 52 arrests. [@2]

Daveta said reports of serious crimes had dropped since the drug operations
began in June the previous year. [@2] He linked decreases in domestic violence
and sexual offences to the high number of joint drug raids conducted from June
to December. [@2] He said there was strong support from community leaders and
police were trying to change the perception of Kadavu as a red zone for drugs in
partnership with stakeholders. [@1]

The Acting Commissioner of Police acknowledged and commended the Methodist
Church of Fiji and Rotuma for supporting crime prevention efforts on Kadavu.
[@2] The acknowledgement followed a weeklong church-led training and awareness
program at Namalata Village focused on the harmful impacts of the drug trade.
[@2] Close to 200 church leaders, stewards, youth volunteers and community
members from around Kadavu attended the program. [@2]
