Officers from Beaufort West police and a narcotics team attached to Western Cape
provincial detectives ran a vehicle checkpoint on the N1 outside the Karoo town
on the evening of Friday 8 January 2021. [@3] They stopped a taxi travelling
from the Eastern Cape at about 21:30. [@2] Six bags of dagga were found in the
trailer. [@1] The consignment weighed 160 kg, police said. [@3] A 51-year-old
man was arrested. [@3] Police estimated the value of the dagga at R320 000. [@3]
The suspect was travelling to Cape Town from the Eastern Cape. [@1] He was due
to appear in the Beaufort West Magistrate's Court on the Monday on a charge of
dealing in dagga. [@2]

In the same statement, police reported a separate arrest at George, where a
35-year-old motorcyclist was found with mandrax worth about R300 000. [@1] The
statement said the checkpoint formed part of efforts to tighten the grip on
crime. [@3] Police put the combined value of the two consignments at R620 000;
the mandrax is not a cannabis product and its value is excluded from the figures
here. [@1]
