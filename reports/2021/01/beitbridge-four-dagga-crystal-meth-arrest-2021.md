Four suspects were arrested in Beitbridge for possession of dangerous drugs,
national police spokesperson Assistant Commissioner Paul Nyathi said. [@1]
Police put the arrests on the Thursday before the 1 February 2021 statement,
placing them on 28 January. [@1] The drugs seized in Beitbridge consisted of
dagga, an illicit beer known as musombodhiya and crystal meth. [@1]

Police said the drugs were among goods smuggled into Zimbabwe through an
undesignated entry point. [@1] Police said smuggling cases were increasing and
that patrols along the country's borders had been intensified. [@1]

The arrests followed the deployment of a joint security team to Beitbridge in
early January 2021 to curb smuggling and other illegal activities. [@1] The team
comprised members of the Zimbabwe National Army, the Zimbabwe Republic Police
Support Unit and the Zimbabwe Prisons and Correctional Services. [@1] The
deployment followed a visit by State Security Minister Owen Ncube and senior
members of the Joint Operations Command. [@1] Police said the team had
intercepted smuggled goods and arrested offenders. [@1] More than 2,500 people
had been arrested for various offences since the deployment, police said. [@1]
