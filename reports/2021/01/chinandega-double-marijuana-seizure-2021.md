Nicaragua's National Police said it captured Lesther Manuel Gómez Domínguez, 32,
in the municipality of El Viejo on Wednesday 6 January 2021, as part of its
Containment Wall strategy against drug trafficking and organised crime. [@1]
Commissioner Major Francisco Ruiz, the police's second-in-command in Chinandega,
said patrol officers caught Gómez with a blue bag holding six tape-wrapped
packets of green herb. [@1] A field test identified the herb as marijuana and
the six packets weighed six pounds. [@1] A police investigation team inspected
the scene and took photographs. [@1]

On the morning of Thursday 7 January 2021 police seized a further six pounds of
marijuana in comarca San Benito #2, in Chinandega municipality. [@1] No one was
caught at the second site, and police said the incident remained under
investigation. [@1] Police said their technical team carried out chemical tests
at that scene and the material tested positive for marijuana. [@1]

Police said they were continuing special surveillance and patrol plans in
Chinandega neighbourhoods, markets and bus stops. [@1]
