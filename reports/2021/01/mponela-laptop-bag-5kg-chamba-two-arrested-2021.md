Police at Mponela in Dowa District arrested two men, Phillip Isaac and Yohane
Kanyenda, for being found in possession of chamba. [@1] Police said the offence
took place on 2 January at Mponela Trading Centre. [@1] Mponela deputy police
publicist Macpatso Msadala said the two had a laptop bag full of loose chamba
which they were offering for sale. [@1] A prospective buyer tipped off the
police, who arrested them. [@1]

A search of the bag found chamba weighing about 5 kilograms. [@1] The drug was
to be analysed at Chitedze Research Station. [@1]

Police said the two would appear in court soon on a charge of being found in
possession of chamba under the Dangerous Drugs Act. [@1] Isaac comes from
Chigumba village in Traditional Authority Wimbe, Kasungu district, and Kanyenda
from Chingowe village in Traditional Authority Mkukula, Dowa district. [@1]
