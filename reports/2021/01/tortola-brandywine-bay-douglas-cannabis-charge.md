Royal Virgin Islands Police charged Kareem Douglas, of Georges Northside,
Tortola, with possession of cannabis with intent to supply. [@2] The charge
followed a police vehicle search in the Brandywine Bay area on the morning of
Tuesday 19 January 2021. [@3] He was scheduled to appear before the Magistrate's
Court on Thursday 21 January. [@2]

A search of a vehicle in the Brandywine Bay area uncovered some 2,000 grams of
cannabis, which weighed less than first reported. [@2] The RVIPF said the
investigation remained active and released no further information. [@3] An
earlier police statement on 20 January had put the cannabis find at four kilos
in another vehicle. [@1] That statement said one person had been arrested on
suspicion over the drugs in that vehicle. [@1]

The operation took place in the early hours of Tuesday 19 January 2021. [@1] In
the same operation, more than 150 kilograms of cocaine were recovered from a
vehicle in Hodge's Creek and a vessel was seized in the Brandy Wine area. [@2]
The driver of the vehicle carrying the cocaine escaped. [@2]
