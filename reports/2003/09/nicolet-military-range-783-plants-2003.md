Military police showed reporters 783 mature cannabis plants they had seized in
a grassy field owned by the Department of National Defence near Nicolet, a
town midway between Montreal and Quebec City. [@1] The field, about 18
kilometres long and three kilometres wide, is an ammunition-testing ground,
and the plants grew in about 40 clusters of 20 to 50 plants each. [@1]

The military had been tipped off by Quebec's provincial police counterparts.
[@1] A second account put the seizure at close to 1,000 plants on the Armed
Forces artillery range at Nicolet. [@2] A military spokesman, Captain Steve
Lebel, said investigators would probe whether outsiders or department
employees were behind the planting. [@1] The facility is fenced on three
sides, leaving officials to speculate that the growers came by boat on the
St. Lawrence side. [@1]
