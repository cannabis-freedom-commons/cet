Father-of-one John Vasey, owner of Houghton-le-Spring-based JD Vasey Transport,
was stopped by French Customs in Perpignan on 28 February 2003 on his way back
from Spain. [@1] Officers searched his vehicle and found cannabis resin in two
shrink-wrapped UK-bound pallets he had collected from a regular client in
Barcelona, a load valued at £2.5 million. [@1] He was detained in prison in the
Marseilles area while awaiting trial, which was expected to take up to four
months. [@1]

His wife Emma said it was his first collection for the client, that the
groupage load was palletised and shrink-wrapped so he could not have known what
was inside, and that the company due to receive the delivery had never been in
touch. [@1]

## Appeal fails, trial awaited

By August 2003 Vasey had lost an appeal against his continued detention; Emma
said prosecutors warned that freeing him would mean freeing other British
drivers held in Perpignan prison. [@2] Truck drivers staged a go-slow convoy of
more than 40 vehicles from Washington to London and handed a 2,500-signature
petition to the French charge d'affaires. [@2] In January 2004, held for nearly
a year without trial, he was due before a judge on 4 February, though his
father Ken was unsure whether it would be more than another four-monthly remand
hearing. [@3]
