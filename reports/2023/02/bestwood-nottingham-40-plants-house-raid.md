Officers arrived at a house in Raymede Drive, Bestwood, at about 07:00 GMT on
Thursday after receiving reports of drug-related activity. [@1] A second account
says officers carried out the warrant in Raymede Drive, Bestwood, following
intelligence of drug activity at the address. [@2] The City North Operation
Reacher team led the planned raid at around 7 am on Thursday. [@2]

After forcing entry and searching the house, officers found around 40 cannabis
plants growing inside. [@2] Officers seized the 40 plants, which were later
destroyed. [@1] Police also removed the equipment used to power the grow. [@2]
Inside, officers found the electricity supply had been dangerously bypassed.
[@2]

Police urged anyone with information to get in touch as officers carry out
investigations. [@1]
