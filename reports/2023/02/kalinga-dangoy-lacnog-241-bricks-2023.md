Police operatives ran a hot-pursuit operation on 23 February 2023 after a
driver evaded a checkpoint at Sitio Dinakan, Barangay Dangoy, in Lubuagan,
Kalinga. [@1,2] The vehicle was later found abandoned in New Balbalan, Lacnog,
Tabuk City, holding 241 marijuana bricks and one tubular pack of marijuana
weighing about 242,000 grams, with an overall standard drug price of
P29,040,000. [@1,2] Officers marked and inventoried the haul on site in the
presence of a media representative, a Department of Justice representative and
a Lacnog barangay kagawad, while a manhunt for the driver continued. [@1,2]

In a side-by-side operation the same day in Benguet, Bakun police found five
marijuana plantation sites at Sitio Dayag, Ca-ang and Matacadang in Barangay
Kayapa, Bakun, and uprooted and burned 2,130 fully grown marijuana plants
worth P426,000 on site. [@1,2] No cultivator was identified and no arrest
there was reported. [@1,2]
