Mozambique's National Criminal Investigation Service, SERNIC, incinerated drugs
seized in Maputo city in Moamba district, Maputo province, including 36
kilograms of cocaine and 20 kilograms of cannabis sativa, locally called suruma.
[@1] On 7 February 2023, the service incinerated about 56.2 kilograms of various
drugs seized in the city of Maputo. [@2]

The batch included 36 kilos of cocaine and 20 kilograms of cannabis sativa,
known as suruma. [@2] The batch also included one gram of methamphetamine and
more than 10 kilograms of chemicals. [@2] The chemicals were substances used for
producing narcotics. [@2] The drugs had been seized between April 2022 and
January 2023. [@2]

A large part of the drug was seized at Maputo International Airport. [@2] In
connection with the seizures, 361 citizens were arrested, including six
foreigners. [@2] The six foreigners were one Angolan, one Tanzanian, one
Congolese, one Ivorian, one South African and one Nigerian. [@2] SERNIC said the
seizure and incineration were the final outcome of various investigation and
prevention actions. [@2]
