Jordan's General Security Directorate said its anti-narcotics directorate
arrested six drug dealers and traffickers while handling three cases in the
capital Amman, the northern badia and Ramtha. [@2] In the first case, after
gathering information about one of the dealers who supplied traffickers with the
drugs, officers raided him at the place where he was and arrested him. [@1] They
seized 38 kaf of hashish and 6,000 narcotic pills in his possession, the
directorate said. [@1]

In Ramtha district officers lured two drug traffickers, one of them an Arab
national, caught them inside their vehicle and seized one kilogram of crystal
methamphetamine from them. [@3] In one area of the northern badia officers
raided and searched three houses belonging to drug traffickers and people wanted
in drug cases. [@4] They arrested all three suspects inside one of the houses
and seized assorted quantities of drugs prepared and ready for sale as well as
four firearms. [@4]
