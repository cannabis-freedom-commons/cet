Customs officials and police arrested a Nepali man at Suvarnabhumi airport on 8
February 2023 after hashish was found in his luggage. [@1] The man, identified
only as a Nepali national, had arrived from Tribhuvan International Airport in
Kathmandu. [@2] He was charged with bringing a Category 5 narcotic, hashish,
into the kingdom without permission and possessing it without permission. [@2]

Officers stopped him as he walked through the nothing-to-declare channel at Exit
B and X-rayed his black pull-along suitcase. [@2] The X-ray showed an
abnormality, and a search found a secret compartment built into the base of the
case, which officers opened with a screwdriver. [@2] Inside was a compressed
dark brown sheet 98 by 38 centimetres, wrapped in brown tape and black plastic.
[@2] A field test on the substance turned purple, indicating it was hashish, a
Category 5 narcotic under Thai law. [@2] Thailand had decriminalised cannabis
flowers in June 2022, but hashish is still classed as a Category 5 narcotic.
[@3]

Officers weighed the hashish in front of the suspect and gave the weight as
about 5.6 kilogrammes including its packaging. [@2] The Customs Department
valued the hashish at 560,000 baht. [@2] Panthong Loykulkanon, deputy
director-general of the Customs Department, announced the case on 9 February.
[@2] The man was handed to investigators at Suvarnabhumi Airport Police Station
for prosecution. [@2]
