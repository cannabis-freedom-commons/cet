Police at Kirakira Police Station uprooted more than 20 marijuana plants in
Central Makira-Ulawa Province in the week before 17 February 2023. [@1] Police
alleged that a man in his 50s and his wife were farming the plants as a source
of income. [@1] Solomon Business Magazine carried the same police statement,
reporting that more than 20 plants had been uprooted in Central Makira-Ulawa
Province. [@2]

Provincial Police Commander for Makira-Ulawa Province, Superintendent Peter
Sitai, said the suspect was arrested for farming the marijuana plants. [@1]
Sitai said the suspect and his wife had been dealt with and released on bail to
appear before the Kirakira Magistrates' Court later. [@1]

Sitai said growing marijuana is a crime under the Dangerous Drugs Act, for which
a person found guilty faces a fine of SBD 2,000 or up to 10 years in prison.
[@1] He asked people in Makira-Ulawa Province to report marijuana plants so
police could remove them, saying cultivation had affected many lives there. [@1]
