Puerto Rico Police Bureau agents seized 31 pounds (14 kilograms) of marijuana
found in a suitcase at the baggage claim of Luis Muñoz Marín International
Airport, in Isla Verde, Carolina. [@1] Officers of the Metropolitan Drugs
Division made the seizure on the afternoon of Thursday 9 February 2023 at the
United Airlines baggage claim in Terminal B. [@1] By the following morning no
arrest had been made, and investigators were trying to identify who had
transported the drug to Puerto Rico. [@3]

Police said the investigation would continue through a subpoena to the airport
for security-camera video from inside the terminal and the baggage-claim area,
and for records of the suitcase's check-in. [@1] The force said it hoped to
identify those responsible and to file charges in their absence. [@1] The case
was sent for consultation to the Organized Crime Division of the Puerto Rico
Department of Justice. [@1] The origin of the flight was not disclosed. [@2]
Telemundo PR reported that the find formed part of the strategies of the
police's Plan 100x35. [@4]
