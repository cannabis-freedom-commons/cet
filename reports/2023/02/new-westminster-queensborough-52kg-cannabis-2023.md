The New Westminster Police Department's gang suppression unit pulled over a
vehicle speeding across the Queensborough Bridge at about 4:30 p.m. on Wednesday
1 February 2023. [@2] The officer spoke to the driver, arrested them and
searched the vehicle. [@1] Police found dozens of vacuum-sealed bags of illicit
cannabis weighing 52 kilograms in total. [@1] The department did not publicise
the seizure until 23 February, three weeks after the stop. [@4]

Const. Quinn Gallagher, who made the stop, said the gang suppression unit
routinely intercepts people with large amounts of drugs or dangerous weapons.
[@2] Sgt. Andrew Leaver said that although marijuana has been legal in Canada
since 2018, selling it outside the measures of the Cannabis Act is still
illegal. [@3] Leaver said penalties under the Cannabis Act range from warnings
and tickets for minor offences to criminal prosecution and imprisonment for more
serious ones. [@1]

Police said they were still gathering evidence to determine which charges to
recommend to Crown Counsel. [@2]
