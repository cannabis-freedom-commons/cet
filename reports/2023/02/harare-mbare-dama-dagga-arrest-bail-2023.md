Zimbabwe Republic Police arrested Simbarashe Chanachimwe, known as Dama, a
suspected drug dealer from Mbare in Harare. [@1] Police said Dama, also a music
promoter, was wanted in connection with unlawful dealing in dangerous drugs.
[@1] He was 31. [@2] He had been on the run for some time, and the information
ministry's permanent secretary, Nick Mangwana, had said police could not locate
him. [@2] Police confirmed the arrest in a tweet posted on Wednesday 22 February
2023. [@2]

The arrest followed a raid on his drug base at Block 14 Matapi Flats in Mbare,
Harare, which police dated to 9 February 2023 at about 04:00. [@1] Police
recovered 26 sachets of dagga and one mbanje plant — local names for cannabis —
1.7 metres tall, spokesperson Paul Nyathi said. [@1] Police said the arrest came
in their operation “No to dangerous drugs and illicit substances”, in which they
said 2,470 suspects had been arrested to date. [@1]

He appeared at the Mbare Magistrates Court on allegations of possessing 26
satchels of dagga. [@4] He was charged with unlawful possession of dangerous
drugs and was not asked to plead. [@5] The court granted him ZWL$50,000 bail.
[@3] It ordered him not to interfere with state witnesses and to report to St
Mary’s police station every Friday. [@5] The case was remanded to 20 March 2023
for a possible trial. [@5]

In court the State dated the raid to 4 February 2023 at about 10:30, not 9
February as the police statement had it. [@5] It said detectives found 27
sachets of loose dagga in a bucket at a swampy area. [@5] It said the 1.7-metre
plant was uprooted. [@5] The dagga weighed 50 grams when it reached the post
office as an exhibit, the court heard. [@5]
