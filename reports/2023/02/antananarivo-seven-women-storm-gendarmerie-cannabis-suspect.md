Seven women tried to force their way into the gendarmerie brigade at Sabotsy
Namehana, a commune north of Antananarivo, on Saturday 4 February 2023 to free a
man held there. [@2] The man they wanted out of the cell had just been arrested
in a fokontany bordering Sabotsy Namehana and Ankadikely-Ilafy, where the group
sold Indian hemp (chanvre indien, the local term for cannabis) to a wide
clientele. [@2] A large quantity of dried cannabis was found at the main
suspect's home; he was arrested after the search and the drugs were seized. [@2]

At the brigade the women insulted the gendarmes, who refused to hand the man
over, and then used force; the gendarmes overpowered and arrested them without
using a weapon and without injury. [@2] A source quoted by L'Express de
Madagascar said the man and the seven women were all cannabis dealers, which was
why they covered for each other. [@1]

On Monday 6 February all eight were brought to the prosecutor's office at the
Antananarivo court of first instance, a committal warrant was issued and they
were held at the Antanimora central prison. [@2] The Réunion news site LINFO.re
reported that the group was brought to the Antananarivo prosecutor on 6 February
and jailed. [@1]
