Justice Fepuleai Ameperosa Roma refused a name suppression request from an
inmate accused of dealing drugs at Tanumalala prison. [@1] The inmate was Faasao
Peseta, from Pu'apu'a Savai'i, whom the judge said had to be named because the
public needed to know about the serious allegations. [@1] Peseta pleaded guilty
to all charges filed against him. [@1] The court heard that he was found with
marijuana leaves and marijuana joints prepared and set for sale on two separate
occasions. [@1]

On 16 January 2023 Peseta was found with two packets containing marijuana leaves
and seeds, one weighing 27.1 grams and the other 8.28 grams. [@1] Police also
found 34 marijuana seeds in one packet and 108 seeds in another packet wrapped
in tissue paper. [@1] On 22 January 2023 he was again found with a packet
containing 191 marijuana joints already rolled and set for sale. [@1] The
Samoan-language account says police did not establish whether cash was found,
but that the operation was clearly marijuana dealing. [@2]

Justice Roma said court proceedings had to be public so the country could know
about them, given the increase in inmates facing charges while serving time.
[@2] Sentencing for Peseta was set for 15 March 2023. [@2]
