National Guard members and Mexican Army soldiers found plants with the
characteristics of marijuana in the locality of El Tigre, in the municipality of
Aguililla, Michoacán, during crime-prevention patrols. [@1] The three plots lay
in a mountainous area and together covered about 5,200 square metres, about 0.52
hectares. [@1]

The first plot covered about 1,600 square metres and held plants about 1.5
metres tall. [@1] Two further plots measured about 2,400 and 1,200 square
metres, with plants up to 60 centimetres tall. [@1] The guards and soldiers
destroyed the plants by manual labour and incineration. [@1]

A search for those responsible for the sowings ended without positive results.
[@1] Samples of the possible cannabis plants were handed to the Federal Public
Ministry's agency in the state, which will continue the investigation. [@1] The
National Guard said the find came under the National Security Strategy and the
Juntos por la Paz addiction-prevention strategy. [@1]
