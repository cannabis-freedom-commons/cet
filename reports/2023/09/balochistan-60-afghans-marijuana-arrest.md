Government officials in Pakistan's Balochistan province told the media that they
had arrested 60 Afghan nationals on charges of transporting and smuggling drugs.
[@1] The acting minister of intelligence for Balochistan, Jan Mohammad Achakzai,
said the detainees were arrested in the towns of Qala Abdullah and Pashin. [@1]
He said the arrests had been made over the last two days and gave the account on
11 September 2023. [@1]

Achakzai told Radio Azadi that it was the first time in five years that such a
large operation had been carried out. [@1] He said 11 kilograms of marijuana
were seized during the operation, the only cannabis quantity given. [@1] The
same operation also seized 92 kilograms of ephedrine and 16 kilograms of other
drugs. [@1]

The account came from Achakzai's remarks to Radio Azadi as relayed by the Afghan
Voice Agency. [@1]
