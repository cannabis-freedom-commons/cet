Seven men were arrested in a police operation on Friday on suspicion of taking
part in illegal camel races in an Israel Defense Forces firing zone in the Negev
desert. [@2] The race took place near the IDF's Tzeelim base, and footage of it
emerged on Wednesday. [@3] Dozens of camels, trucks and off-road vehicles
crossed the base's firing zone at high speed, with many participants firing
automatic weapons into the air. [@3]

Police destroyed three greenhouses containing 940 cannabis seedlings found
during the arrests. [@2] The 940 cannabis plants were destroyed in three
greenhouses during the arrests. [@3] The detained Bedouin were suspected of
organizing illegal gambling and violating animal protection laws. [@3]

During the operation 117 camels were inspected in Bedouin compounds and eight
were confiscated. [@1] Police also confiscated two tractors and issued 14
traffic tickets to drivers who participated in the race. [@2]
