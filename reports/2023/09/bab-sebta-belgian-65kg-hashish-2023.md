Moroccan national security officers at the Bab Sebta crossing into Ceuta
detained a Belgian national. [@2] Sud Ouest describes the case as Belgians of
Moroccan origin arrested as they crossed the border between Morocco and Spain.
[@1] He was a naturalised Belgian crossing at Bab Sebta, the Moroccan side of
the frontier, with members of his family on the afternoon of Tuesday 5 September
2023. [@2] His car, registered in Belgium, raised suspicion, and a search found
the hashish divided into packages hidden among the personal luggage inside it.
[@2] Border guards seized nearly 65 kg of hashish from the vehicle, which a
sniffer dog had marked. [@1] The car's passengers were brought before a judicial
authority as the investigation continued under the competent prosecutor. [@2]
The customs administration confiscated the hashish and the car for the
investigation. [@2]

Sud Ouest reported the arrest as the fourth of its kind in two weeks at the
border between Morocco and Spain. [@1] El Faro de Ceuta said Belgians carrying
hashish in their cars had been detained in Morocco or Spain before, several
times as families with young children. [@2] El Faro de Ceuta said the seizures
exposed a drug-trafficking route between Morocco and Belgium. [@2]
