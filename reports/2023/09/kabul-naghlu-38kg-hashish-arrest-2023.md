Afghanistan's defence ministry said a drug smuggler was arrested and a large
amount of drugs seized on the basis of intelligence reports from the third
brigade of the 313 Central Army Corps. [@1] The 313 Central Army Corps is a
military corps acting under the defence ministry, and the ministry announced the
case in a post on X. [@1]

The ministry said the arrest was made on the road between Naghlu Dam, in the
Surobi District of Kabul province, and Nangarhar province. [@1] The troops
seized 38 kilograms of hashish in the operation. [@1] The same haul included 36
packets of K tablets, which are not cannabis and are left out of the hashish
weight. [@1]

The ministry said on X that the smuggler intended to transfer the drugs to Balkh
province. [@1]
