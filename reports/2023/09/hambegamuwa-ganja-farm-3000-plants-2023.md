On 19 September 2023, Hambegamuwa police arrested a 24-year-old resident
of Hambegamuwa for running an illegal ganja farm on about half an acre.
[@1] The farm held 3,000 mature ganja trees and 25,000 seedlings. [@1]

Officers destroyed all the confiscated plants and seedlings on site. [@1]
The suspect faces legal proceedings for the operation. [@1]
