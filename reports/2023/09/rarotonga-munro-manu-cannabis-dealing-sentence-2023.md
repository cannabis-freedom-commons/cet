Rhys Bromley Munro-Manu appeared for sentencing at the High Court in Avarua on
Thursday before Justice Colin Doherty. [@1] A “low level street dealer”
convicted of dealing and possession of cannabis has been given 13 months’ jail.
[@1] Justice Doherty ordered him to serve 12 months of probation upon his
release from jail. [@1] Munro-Manu had no previous convictions and his mother
was in court supporting him. [@1]

The court heard police conducted an investigation into drug dealing on Rarotonga
in 2019. [@1] He was suspected of dealing locally grown and imported cannabis
and upon a search, police found 11 foils of cannabis on him, plus a bag of
cannabis. [@1] A further search of his phone records showed he had offered to
sell cannabis multiple times over several months. [@1] The Crown had recommended
a jail sentence of between 18 months and two years. [@1] Starting at 24 months,
he was given a discount for a guilty plea and a further three months for other
consideration. [@1]

Munro-Manu’s case stretches back to 2019 and there were tears in court as
Justice Doherty asked the registrar to hand the defendant tissues as he stood at
sentencing. [@1] While he had been waiting four years for resolution, part of it
was his denial of wrong doing and partly because of the investigation, Justice
Doherty said. [@1]
