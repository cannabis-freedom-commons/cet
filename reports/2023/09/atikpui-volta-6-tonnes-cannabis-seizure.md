A joint team of Ghana Immigration Service personnel and the Customs Division of
the Ghana Revenue Authority seized suspected Indian hemp at the Atikpui border
in the Volta Region. [@2] Atikpui lies near Ho municipality, the Volta regional
capital, close to Togo. [@1] On 7 September 2023, a joint operation led by Nyive
Sector Commander Alhaji Abdulai Zakaria and Superintendent Courage Amegbe of
Operation Calm Life intercepted the consignment in a dilapidated house on a
tip-off. [@1]

The consignment comprised 80 bulky sacks each holding 80 slabs, together
weighing about six tons. [@2] The French-language account described the load as
weighing more than five tonnes and the region's largest seizure. [@1] The sacks
were stored in two separate rooms with no occupants present, so no arrest was
made. [@1]

The seized sacks were to be handed to the narcotics control authority for
further investigation and possible prosecution. [@1] The Ghanaian Times account
said the consignment was destined for neighbouring Togo. [@2] The
French-language account said it was reportedly coming from or going to
neighbouring Togo. [@1] The consignment had been moved to the main office and
then to the Customs warehouse to await headquarters directives. [@2]
