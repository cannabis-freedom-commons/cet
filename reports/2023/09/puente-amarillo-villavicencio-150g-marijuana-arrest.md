Meta Police transit officers stopped a public-service minibus on the road
between Villavicencio and Restrepo, Meta, and searched passengers' belongings.
[@1] The stop took place in the Puente Amarillo sector of Villavicencio. [@1]

During the inspection, officers found and seized 150 grams of creepy-type
marijuana inside a suitcase carried by a 22-year-old man. [@1] The man was read
his rights and arrested for trafficking, manufacturing or carrying narcotics.
[@1]

He and the seized substance were brought before Prosecutor's Office No. 33 of
the Villavicencio URI for prosecution. [@1]
