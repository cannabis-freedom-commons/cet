Fayetteville police arrested Antonio Jones, 29, and charged him with drug
trafficking on 20 September 2023 after seizing 10 pounds of marijuana, cash and
other drugs from a house. [@1] Detectives from the department's Narcotic/Vice
Suppression Unit and Gang Unit, with officers from several Community Empowerment
Response Teams, began investigating trafficking from a home in the 500 block of
Summerwind Drive. [@1]

Detectives obtained a search warrant for the home. [@1] The search yielded 10.6
pounds of marijuana, about 4.8 kilograms. [@1] Officers also seized 21 grams of
oxycodone pills. [@1] They took 14 strips of Suboxone as well. [@1] Police
seized $1,150 in cash from the home. [@1]

Jones was charged with trafficking opium and with possession with intent to sell
or deliver a Schedule III substance. [@1] He also faced three counts of
trafficking marijuana. [@1] The department directed anyone with information
about drug activity to Fayetteville/Cumberland County Crime Stoppers, which
takes anonymous tips. [@1]
