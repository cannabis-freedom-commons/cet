Police arrested a smuggler with 58 kilograms of hashish in the 5th police
district of Kabul. [@1] The police headquarters said the detainee wanted to
transfer the narcotics from the Company area to the main city. [@1] He was held
red-handed in Kart-i-Mamoreen locality. [@1]

In a video clip shared by police, the alleged smuggler said he had bought
hashish and transferred it to the city, where counter-narcotics personnel
arrested him in the 5th police district. [@1] Security forces arrested a person
and seized 58 kilograms of hashish in Kabul province. [@2]
