Counter-narcotics police in Panjsher province seized about 937 kilograms of
hashish in the Hesa-i-Awal district, an official said on Thursday. [@1] The
official, Nasrullah Malikzada, the provincial director of information and
culture, said the hashish was recovered from a house in Zini village. [@1]

Malikzada said two people had been arrested in connection with the narcotics.
[@1] The two detainees were being interrogated at the time, he added. [@1]
