Four of the five crew members of the cargo vessel MV Elizabeth C were sentenced
to prison in St Kitts on 3 July 2023 on multiple drug possession and importation
charges. [@4] Saleem Kevin Ali of Trinidad was sentenced to three years for
possessing cannabis with intent to supply and three years for importing
cannabis, the terms to run concurrently. [@3] Micheal Jevan Jack of Trinidad
received the same two three-year terms, for importing cannabis and possessing it
with intent to supply. [@3] Desue Mc Farlane of Carricacou was sentenced to four
years and ten months each for importing cannabis and possessing it with intent
to supply, and eight years and ten months for possessing cannabis, all
concurrent. [@3] Peter Oliver Scotland of Trinidad was sentenced on three
cocaine counts, to five years and four months on each, running concurrently.
[@3] The court ordered the confiscation of the MV Elizabeth C under section 27
of the Drug Act; the vessel was then in Tortola territorial waters. [@3]

Customs, police anti-narcotics and coast guard officers ran the joint operation
at the Deep Water Harbour Port in Bird Rock, Basseterre, at about 8:00 a.m. on 4
October 2022. [@1] The search, between 8:00 a.m. and 2:00 p.m., found 16
vacuum-sealed bags holding 5.830 kg (12.852 pounds) of Cannabis Sativa. [@1] The
cannabis and about 800 kg of cocaine were concealed in the walls of the cabin
and taken into police custody with the five crew members. [@1] The police put
the cannabis at an estimated street value of about US$57,777 or EC$156,000. [@1]

The five crew members, two from Grenada and three from Trinidad and Tobago, were
charged on 7 October 2022 and remanded to Her Majesty's Prison. [@1] The accused
were Desue Mc Farlang and Ronald Olliviere of Grenada, along with Peter
Scotland, Saleem Ali and Micheal Jack of Trinidad and Tobago. [@1] Customs
officials charged them with fraudulently evading and concealing cannabis and
with a false declaration of documents. [@1] Desue Mc Farlane faced an additional
charge of modifying the vessel for the purpose of concealment. [@1]

The vessel was registered in Trinidad and Tobago under IMO number 8894574 and
had last left St Vincent and the Grenadines before reaching Basseterre. [@1] The
vessel was reported regionally to have been bound for the British Virgin
Islands. [@2]
