Judiciary Police arrested four locals, three men and a woman aged 22 to 29, on
Thursday and Friday for smuggling cannabis from Hong Kong to Macau to sell. [@1]
Police identified a 26-year-old man surnamed Ho, who had left Macau for Hong
Kong on Thursday afternoon, and arrested him that evening when he re-entered
Macau via the Outer Harbour Ferry Terminal. [@1] Based on Ho's confession,
police arrested a 29-year-old man surnamed Lam in the wee hours of Friday in the
city centre. [@1] Police identified a 22-year-old man surnamed Espidol as tasked
with selling the smuggled cannabis in Macau and arrested him near his home in
the northern district, while his 26-year-old girlfriend surnamed Son was taken
in for questioning. [@1]

During the operation officers seized 216.8 grammes of cannabis products and 10
bottles weighing 147.6 grammes of cannabis oil, with an estimated street value
of 251,800 patacas, as well as drug-taking paraphernalia and drug-packaging
items. [@1] Officers seized 83.7 grammes of cannabis on Ho plus 9.8 grammes of
cannabis oil, 8 grammes of ready-rolled joints and paraphernalia at his home in
Seac Pai Van in Coloane, and 12 packets of cannabis weighing 125.1 grammes at
Lam's home. [@1] Police seized a total of nine bottles of cannabis oil weighing
147.6 grammes found on Espidol and in his flat. [@1] A second account confirms
four local residents were held over importing marijuana from Hong Kong to sell
in Macau, with 216.8 grams of marijuana and 147.6 grams of marijuana oil seized
worth around MOP 251,000. [@2]

Ho and Lam told police they spent HK$20,000 to buy 80 grammes of cannabis from a
dealer in Hong Kong and smuggled it twice over three months, but police suspect
more trips because the seized weight did not match and Ho had crossed between
Hong Kong and Macau almost 10 times recently. [@1] Ho, Lam and Espidol insisted
the drugs were just for personal use, while Ho and Espidol tested positive for
the drug and Son and Lam tested negative. [@1] The quartet was transferred to
the Public Prosecutions Office on Saturday facing respective charges of drug
trafficking and drug taking. [@1]
