The St. Maarten Police Force was investigating a case on A.TH. Illidge road in
the Dutch Quarter at around 04:50 a.m. on Monday 24 July 2023. [@1] A routine
patrol of the Sint Maarten Police Force stopped a vehicle with five occupants.
[@1]

One man escaped before the control could be carried out. [@1] The man got away
before officers arrived at the vehicle. [@3] The remaining four occupants were
checked, and officers found a firearm and a small amount of marijuana in the
vehicle, which they confiscated. [@1] All four were arrested and taken to the
police station in Philipsburg for processing. [@1]

Police held the four on suspicion of possession of the illegal firearm and
narcotics. [@2] The four were taken to Philipsburg Police Station for
questioning and further investigation. [@3] The Detective department of the
police force is investigating the case. [@1]
