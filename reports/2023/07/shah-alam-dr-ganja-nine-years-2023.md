The Shah Alam High Court on 24 July 2023 sentenced retired Captain (R) Amiruddin
@ Nadarajan Abdullah, aged 64 and known as Dr Ganja, to nine years in jail,
sparing him the gallows. [@1] Judge Julia Ibrahim imposed that sentence on 39
charges. [@1]

The court amended 16 trafficking charges, covering 77.48493 kg and 14.647
litres, to possession. [@1] Those trafficking counts were for ganja, or
cannabis, the High Court had recorded when it ordered him to enter his defence
in January 2022. [@3] He also faced 18 counts of cultivating marijuana plants,
four counts of possessing marijuana and one count of ingesting the drug. [@1]
The offences were alleged at a house in Kampung Delek Dalam, Klang, at 12.30 pm
on 26 May 2017. [@1]

He was sentenced to five years' imprisonment on each charge under Section 6 and
Section 39A(1) of the Dangerous Drugs Act 1952, nine years for the Section
39A(2) offence and six months for the Section 15(1)(a) offence. [@1] Judge Julia
Ibrahim ordered the terms to run concurrently from his arrest on 26 May 2017 and
ordered his release the same day, because he had already served a third of the
sentence. [@1] She said that as a former soldier he knew the Dangerous Drugs Act
existed, but that he had been desperate to use marijuana as treatment for an
ailment, and that the sentence should deter others with the same idea. [@1] The
trial began on 8 April 2019, and 17 prosecution witnesses and five defence
witnesses testified. [@2] Deputy public prosecutor Raja Zaizul Faridah Raja
Zaharudin led the prosecution, and lawyers Sangeet Kaur Deo and Pravin
Mahentharan defended Amiruddin. [@2]
