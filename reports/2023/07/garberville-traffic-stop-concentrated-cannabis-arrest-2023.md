A Humboldt County sheriff's deputy on patrol in the Garberville area stopped a
vehicle at the intersection of Locust Street and Maple Lane at about 4:03 p.m.
on 6 July 2023 for a vehicle code violation. [@1] The driver, 52, was driving
with a suspended license and refused to produce the license or comply with
commands to leave the vehicle, the sheriff's office said. [@1] Deputies arrested
him for driving without a license and for resisting a peace officer. [@1] The
man was a Garberville resident, and the arrest was made on Thursday, 6 July
2023. [@2]

During a search of the man and his vehicle after the arrest, deputies found two
grocery bags of commercially concentrated cannabis. [@1] The same search turned
up explosive flash powder, cannon fuse, ball bearings, a dagger, a .22 caliber
rifle and ammunition, and 3.55 grams of methamphetamine, none of which is
counted as cannabis. [@1]

He was booked into the Humboldt County Correctional Facility on nine charges,
including possession of concentrated cannabis over 1 oz, driving with a
suspended license, resisting a peace officer, and weapons, explosives and
methamphetamine offenses. [@1]
