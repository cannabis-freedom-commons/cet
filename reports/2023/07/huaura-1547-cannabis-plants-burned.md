Peru's National Police seized and incinerated more than 1,500 marijuana plants
about one metre tall in the Chambara settlement of Huaura province. [@1]
Chambara is a centro poblado, a populated centre, in Huaura. [@1] Officers said
the illicit plants were ready to be sold. [@1] The people tending the crop fled
when they noticed the police and their whereabouts were unknown. [@1]

The anti-drug department of the Huacho police division carried out the
operation. [@1] Division chief Jairton Bardales gave the figures to the press.
[@1] That unit extracted and incinerated 1,547 one-metre-tall cannabis sativa
plants. [@1] Officers also found 1,632 medium-sized marijuana plants sown on 200
square metres (0.02 hectares). [@1] Bardales valued the plants at about 800
soles a kilo locally and about 1,200 soles a kilo in Lima. [@1]

Bardales said police had incinerated about 40 tonnes of marijuana in the area so
far that year. [@1]
