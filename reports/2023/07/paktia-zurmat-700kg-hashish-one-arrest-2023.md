Police seized hashish weighing 700 kilograms from a car and arrested one suspect
in the Zurmat district of southeastern Paktia province. [@1] Police spokesman
Munib Zadran told Pajhwok Afghan News the 700 kilograms of drugs were
confiscated from a pick-up vehicle in the Dawlat Khan area of the district late
on Saturday. [@1]

One person was arrested in connection with the smuggling of hashish and was
under interrogation. [@1] The cultivation and use of any kind of drugs is banned
in the country. [@1]
