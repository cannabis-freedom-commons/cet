William Kincaide, 55, and Christopher Randell Dolman, 53, both of Lufkin, were
arrested after officers found a lunch kit of drugs in their vehicle. [@1] The
arrests followed a 1 a.m. traffic incident, according to a City of Lufkin
release. [@2] An officer had tried to stop their sedan after it was seen
travelling at 90 to 100 mph on Frank Avenue and running red lights. [@2] The
driver, Kincaide, was taken into custody when the car stopped in front of a home
on Berry Road. [@2] Dolman fled on foot and was found hiding under a building by
officers and an assisting Angelina County sheriff's deputy. [@2]

The lunch kit in the car held 1.26 ounces of marijuana along with six ounces of
methamphetamine, 0.96 ounces of crack cocaine, a small Listerine bottle of PCP
and ecstasy. [@1] A loaded handgun was also found in the car. [@2] The drugs
were packaged to sell, prompting delivery charges for both men in addition to
evading arrest. [@2] No bonds had been set for the men at the time of the
report. [@2]
