Lexington County sheriff's deputies arrested Irmo High School teacher Brianna
Nicole Carpenter, 25, and charged her with criminal solicitation of a minor and
contributing to the delinquency of a minor. [@2] The arrest was made on 26 July
2023. [@4] Arrest warrants accused her of giving the boy 70 to 80 edible THC
gummies and two THC vape devices. [@2] The warrants say she provided the items
to the male minor between 1 December 2022 and 9 July 2023. [@4]

Sheriff Jay Koon said the boy's parents found women's clothing, sexual items and
expensive electronics in his room, and the boy later admitted a teacher had
given him the items. [@3] Deputies also found online communication between the
minor and Carpenter. [@1] The warrants said the delinquency charge, which
concerned the drugs, rested on statements from the boy, his parents and
electronic communications recovered from his phone. [@4]

Carpenter was held at the Lexington County Detention Center after her arrest.
[@4] Lexington-Richland School District Five said it was cooperating with law
enforcement and had placed the employee on administrative leave. [@1]
Authorities did not say how Carpenter and the minor first met. [@1]
