Nicaragua's National Police detained four people and seized 296 pounds of
marijuana in Ocotal, Nueva Segovia. [@1] They were the driver of a red Hyundai,
Denis Sebastián Cárdenas, 51, his companion Bianca Marcela Guardián Soza, 27,
the driver of a white Toyota RAV4, Cristhiam Yamir Meléndez Rostrán, 26, and his
companion Danny Exequiel Cárdenas Pérez, 19. [@2] Commissioner major José
Balbino Huete, head of police in Nueva Segovia, said the drug was found at a
police checkpoint at 2 a.m. on Sunday 16 July 2023, at kilometre 223.8 of the
Ocotal–Managua Pan-American highway. [@1]

Officers searched both vehicles and found 296 taped packages containing green
plant material. [@1] A field test came back positive for marijuana at a weight
of 296 pounds, about 134.3 kilograms. [@1] Police valued the marijuana at 29,600
US dollars. [@2] Criminalistics investigators carried out a chemical test that
concluded positive for marijuana. [@2] Officers also applied the Scintrex Trace
particle detector to the vehicles, clothing and the detainees' backs and palms,
with positive results for marijuana particles. [@2]

Huete said the seizure resulted from the muro de contención (containment wall)
strategy against organised crime and drug trafficking. [@2] The detainees and
the evidence were sent to the competent authorities to face justice. [@1]
