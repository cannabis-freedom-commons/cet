Peru's National Police arrested two men and a woman after stopping a vehicle in
Chugay district, Sánchez Carrión province, La Libertad, where officers said the
group was transporting 15 kilograms of marijuana camouflaged inside the vehicle
in the sierra region. [@1] The operation was carried out on the previous
Saturday by the Destacamento Móvil PNP de Chugay detachment during a control in
the Quebrada Honda sector, when officers sighted the vehicle and proceeded to
arrest Edwar Solis Varas Benites, 29, María Isabel Varas Benites, 32, and Wagner
Jhonny Ruiz Ojanama, 24. [@1]

When officers carried out the vehicle registration search they found seven
packages wrapped in plastic, each containing dry herb with the colour and
characteristics of cannabis sativa, the botanical name for marijuana, with a
combined weight of about 15 kilograms. [@1] The three people detained, the
seized drugs and the vehicle were put at the disposal of the Área Antidrogas,
the anti-drug area known as Areandro Trujillo, so that the corresponding
proceedings and investigations could be carried out under the law. [@1]
