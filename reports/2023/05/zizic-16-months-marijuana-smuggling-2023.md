Predrag Žižić, 59, from Bijelo Polje, was sentenced to 16 months in prison in a
retrial at the High Court in Bijelo Polje for attempting to smuggle marijuana
from Montenegro to Bosnia and Herzegovina. [@1] Judge Ivan Adamović said the
court added the place where the offence was committed, stating that the search
was carried out at the Metaljka border crossing near Pljevlja. [@1]

The verdict confiscated the drugs and the BMW X3 passenger car used in the
offence. [@1] The court seized 20 kilograms of hemp. [@1] Police officers found
the drugs hidden in a specially made bunker, packed in 34 packages, in a BMW
with Podgorica licence plates that Žižić was driving. [@1]

According to the indictment, Žižić was carrying the drugs on 9 June 2022 and was
stopped around 15:30 at a border crossing in Pljevlja municipality, intending to
sell them to an unknown person in Bosnia and Herzegovina. [@1] More than 20
kilograms of hemp were found in the vehicle during the check. [@2]
