Police in Moldova detained five people aged between 19 and 29 on suspicion of
illegal drug trafficking. [@1] After a series of searches, officers seized ten
kinds of drugs from the suspects, including electronic cigarettes filled with
cannabis oil, the police said. [@1]

The police detained a 19-year-old student as he was travelling to Chișinău to
lay a cache, a hidden packet whose coordinates are sent to the buyer. [@1]
Marijuana was found in the young man's car and two kilograms of drugs of various
kinds in his flat. [@1] Officers also seized more than 20,000 lei that they
believe came from the sale of illegal goods. [@1] From his correspondence with
customers, police found another kilogram of drugs in caches. [@1]

Police also detained two alleged accomplices, a 28-year-old woman from
Transnistria and a 29-year-old Ukrainian citizen, who they said acted as drug
couriers. [@1] Police estimated the value of the seized drugs at 3 million lei.
[@1] The prosecutor's office asked for all five to be arrested. [@1]
