An international police operation coordinated from Croatia broke up a "highly
violent" criminal gang from the Western Balkans and arrested 37 people, Europol
said. [@1] Europol described the group as a "powerful criminal cell" mainly
involved in large-scale trafficking of drugs and firearms across Europe. [@1]
Among the 37 arrested was the gang's ringleader, a national of Bosnia and
Herzegovina considered a high-value target by Europol and serving a four-year
prison sentence in Italy, the agency said. [@1] Europol said the leader was
suspected of running the drug and firearms trafficking by giving orders to his
subordinates from behind bars. [@1]

The raids were carried out simultaneously on 25 May 2023 in Croatia, Slovenia,
Bosnia and Herzegovina, the Netherlands, Italy, Belgium and Germany. [@1]
Officers seized some 148,000 euros in cash, 18 firearms including two machine
guns, and 500 grams of TNT with remote detonators. [@1] Police also seized 15
kilograms of cocaine and 11 kilograms of heroin as well as quantities of
amphetamine, marijuana and hashish, none of the cannabis quantities given by
weight. [@1]

The sweep followed what Europol called a "complex investigation" led by Croatian
police within the "Balkan Cartel" Operational Taskforce. [@1]
