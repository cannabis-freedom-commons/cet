Police from Section 20 of Toledo had been working on information about a
marijuana plantation at a home on Ruta 33, kilometre 23.200. [@1] After
extensive observation and surveillance, officers inspected the property on 17
May 2023, in the presence of its owner, a 35-year-old woman. [@1]

Officers seized 42 marijuana plants about three metres tall, a bale containing
dried flowers and 101 marijuana branches drying their flowers. [@1]

Police took a 47-year-old man, the woman's partner, to the police unit. [@1] He
said he was not registered with IRCA, Uruguay's cannabis regulation and control
institute, that he had grown the plants since the previous year and that they
were for his own consumption. [@1] The case is in the charge of the Toledo
public prosecutor. [@1]
