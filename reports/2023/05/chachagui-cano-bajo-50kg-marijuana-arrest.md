Officers assigned to the Narino Transit and Transportation Section, operating a
checkpoint on the Pasto-Mojarras road in the Cano Bajo sector, stopped a
48-year-old man travelling in a private vehicle carrying a large quantity of
narcotics. [@1] At the control post, officers signalled the 48-year-old driver
of the small vehicle to stop. [@2] After his nervous and suspicious attitude
gave him away, officers carried out a thorough search inside the vehicle and
found two tarpaulins containing marijuana in the back. [@1]

At Cano Bajo, five minutes from the town of Chachagui in northern Narino,
authorities seized 50 kilos of marijuana being carried in a grey Chevrolet Spark
bound for Pasto. [@2] The entire quantity was hidden in black tarpaulins and was
apparently intended for sale through micro-trafficking. [@1] Authorities said
the seized marijuana was enough to be repackaged into 50,000 doses for presumed
sale in Pasto under the micro-trafficking model. [@2]

Officers read the detained man his rights and transferred him to the Immediate
Reaction Unit of the Fiscalia in Pasto, where his detention was legalised and he
was attributed the crime of manufacturing, trafficking and carrying narcotics.
[@2]
