Namibian police arrested five suspected drug dealers across Namibia in focused
operations over the weekend. [@1] One was arrested in the Drimihopsis settlement
in the Gobabis district on Friday evening, according to NamPol's Public
Relations Division. [@1] The 29-year-old tried to flee a police checkpoint in a
red Volkswagen Golf, and officers assisted by the Eastern Crime Forum pursued
and stopped the vehicle. [@1] He was arrested and was set to appear before the
Gobabis Magistrate Court. [@1]

A search of the vehicle and its occupants found a parcel of 91 bags, known as
bankies, filled with skunk, a variant of cannabis, whose value had not been
determined. [@1] In Windhoek, police arrested a 25-year-old man behind the Old
Mutual building with 28 units of crack cocaine valued at N$2,800, a case with no
cannabis. [@1] In Tsumeb, police arrested three men on different occasions for
possessing illicit drugs. [@1] The first, held near the Shoprite complex, had 16
ballies of cannabis weighing 15 grams, valued at N$150. [@1] The second,
arrested at the same place, had 23 ballies of cannabis weighing 35 grams, 17
bankies of skunk cannabis weighing 15 grams and mandrax tablets, with the
substances valued together at N$2,140. [@1] The third had 21 ballies of cannabis
weighing 25 grams and mandrax tablets, valued together at N$1,300. [@1]

All three Tsumeb suspects faced charges of possession of and dealing in illicit
drugs and were due to make their first appearance before the Tsumeb Magistrates
Court. [@1]
