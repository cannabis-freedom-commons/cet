Royal Saint Lucia Police Force marine officers from Vieux Fort intercepted a
vessel at sea near the Gros Piton, Soufriere, at about 10:15 p.m. on Saturday 8
April 2023. [@1] The vessel was named Twilight and was stopped about six
nautical miles south-west of Beaumont Point during a patrol. [@1] Two people
were on board, and officers who boarded the vessel recovered three packages
suspected to be cannabis. [@1]

Police identified the two as Ashley Joseph of Soufriere and Junior George of
Rockhall, Castries, and arrested and formally charged both with possession of
controlled drugs and intent to supply to another. [@1] Both were taken to the
Second District Court, where each was granted bail of $10,000 on the possession
charge and $15,000 on the intent-to-supply charge, to be paid in cash or with a
suitable surety, with other bail conditions. [@2]
