Two Vietnamese men were arrested in a raid on a cannabis plantation in Mepai
village, Pou Chrey commune, Pech Reada district, Mondulkiri province, on 14
April 2023. [@1] Police named them as Nguyen Van Khveang, aged 32, and Fam Van
Thai, aged 27. [@1] A cattle herder's report led police to the plantation, which
stood on land belonging to Mao Thana Reaks in Mepai village, Pou Chrey commune,
Pech Reada district. [@2]

Officers cut down and burned 10,671 cannabis plants weighing 1,205 kg. [@1] The
plantation was on rented land almost one hectare in size, the provincial police
chief said. [@3] The plants had been put in the ground between March and April
and were not yet ready to be harvested, the police commissioner said. [@4]
Police also destroyed large amounts of growing equipment at the site. [@1]

On 17 April 2023 the provincial prosecutor charged both men with illegally
growing narcotic plants under the drug law. [@3] The provincial police
commissioner said investigators were trying to establish whether the men were
part of a network and to find the mastermind. [@4] The provincial police chief
said the two were hired labourers and that officers were looking for others
involved. [@3] Cannabis cultivation is banned in Cambodia under the law on drug
control. [@3]
