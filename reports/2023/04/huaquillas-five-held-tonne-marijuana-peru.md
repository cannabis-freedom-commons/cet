Police arrested five people guarding 1,152 packages of marijuana in a house in
Huaquillas, on Ecuador's border with Peru. [@1] The raid took place on the
afternoon of Thursday 20 April 2023, at a house on Cotopaxi street, between
Pasaje and Piñas, in Huaquillas. [@2] When officers entered the house they found
five people, who were detained after trying to flee. [@2] The commander of
Ecuador's national police, Fausto Salinas, said the packages were to be sent
through clandestine crossings into Peru. [@1]

The packages were found stored in a room, and a test confirmed that the green
substance was marijuana. [@2] The Peruvian outlet RPP, citing the EFE agency,
reported that more than a tonne of marijuana was seized and that it was hidden
in a house in Huaquillas. [@3] RPP described Huaquillas as the site of the main
border crossing between Ecuador and Peru. [@3] The same report said officers
found 120 rounds of .223-calibre ammunition in the house. [@3]

The house had been identified as a collection point for illegal drugs after an
investigation lasting several months. [@2] According to EFE, the organisation
planned to bring the marijuana into Peru in vehicles crossing at clandestine
points. [@3] From Peru, another branch of the organisation was to take the load
to Santiago de Chile, police said. [@3] The operation was carried out by the
anti-drug investigation unit with European coordination (UIACE), with the
prosecutor's office and tactical units of the GEMA anti-narcotics group. [@2]
