Security forces detained 591 drug smugglers in the southern Jazan and Asir regions between December 2022 and March 2023, the Interior Ministry said on 5 April 2023. [@1,2]

They seized 264 kilograms of hashish over the campaign period. [@1,2] A further 22.7 tons of khat and 473,799 tablets were also seized in the same operations. [@1]

Preliminary legal proceedings were started and the accused were handed to the relevant authorities. [@1] The interior minister later said dealers and smugglers would not be spared. [@2]
