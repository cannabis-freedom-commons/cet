Three people were arrested in possession of five kilograms of cannabis at
Ouroveni, a village on the island of Grande Comore. [@1] The suspects had come
from Anjouan, another island of the Comorian archipelago. [@1] They were
arrested by the coast guard and by the gendarmerie brigade of Foumbouni. [@1]
The arrests took place on Sunday 23 April 2023. [@1]

The three were then handed to the research brigade in Moroni, which was
continuing the investigation. [@1] The circumstances of the arrest and any
network the three might belong to were still unclear. [@1]

The operation was presented as showing the security forces' efforts against drug
trafficking in the Comoros. [@1] Drug trafficking was described as a major issue
for security and public health. [@1]
