Tlokweng police arrested a 25-year-old man on suspicion of possessing dagga, a
local name for cannabis that is also known as Motokwane. [@1] He was found with
35 sachets of the drug. [@1] Officers nabbed him during an ongoing stop,
question and search operation being carried out for the Easter holidays. [@1]
The man was driving alone in Tlokweng at around 2am on Saturday. [@1]

Tlokweng police station commander Superintendent Unoziba Rari confirmed the
seizure in an interview. [@1] He said the 35 sachets of dagga were estimated to
weigh 140 grams. [@1] The young man was also found with two sachets of a
substance suspected to be methcathinone powder, popularly known as "Cat". [@1]

Rari said that, looking at the quantity, police suspected the man might be
selling the drugs. [@1] He added that investigations were ongoing to establish
where the young man got his supply. [@1]
