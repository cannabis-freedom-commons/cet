Officers of the Arlit branch of Niger's Central Office for the Repression of
Illicit Drug Trafficking (OCRTIS) seized 67 kilograms of cannabis at the
southern entrance to Arlit on 12 April 2023. [@1] The seizure was made around
08:00 at the barrier on the road from Agadez, where the cannabis had been hidden
in sacks of beans. [@1] The same sacks held 300 Tramadol tablets. [@1]

The seizure came during an operation against banditry and drug trafficking in
Agadez region, where several armed attacks had been recorded in the preceding
weeks. [@1] Niger's far north, on the borders with Algeria and Libya, had become
increasingly dangerous, with repeated armed attacks along the region's main
roads. [@1] The insecurity was compounded by trafficking in migrants, weapons
and drugs, prompting the authorities to launch wide-ranging operations to
contain the threat. [@1] The prefect of Arlit department, Nouri Malan Sani,
praised the defence and security forces for the result and said further
large-scale operations would follow to secure the area. [@1]
