A Customs Surveillance patrol boat based in Almería detected two vessels heading toward the coast on 3 April 2023 and launched a maritime interception supported by land units. [@1] One launch escaped at high speed, while the other, a 14-metre rigid-hulled inflatable with three 300 hp engines, tried to flee with risky manoeuvres that threw two of its crew overboard. [@1] Officers rescued the two from the water and boarded the smugglers' boat. [@1]

Inside they found 108 burlap bales of hashish resin weighing 3,915 kilos, along with 42 jerrycans of petrol carried as supply. [@1] The agency describes the 14-metre inflatable as the type commonly used for smuggling hashish. [@1] The four crew members, two Spanish nationals and two Moroccans, were arrested and brought with the boat, the drugs and personal effects before Investigating Court No. 2 in Berja. [@1]
