On Wednesday 19 April 2023, officers of the Bermuda Police Service Drugs Unit,
assisted by the Financial Crime Unit, the Criminal Investigation Unit and the
Tactical Division, intercepted two vehicles in which people who had just left
the Executive Jet Facility at L.F. Wade International Airport were travelling.
[@1] A search of those people and their luggage recovered a significant quantity
of the controlled drug cannabis. [@1] The Royal Gazette, citing police, reported
that the nine people arrested were men, including a senior customs officer. [@2]
The seizure was part of an operation the police called Operation Furud, which
also resulted in the arrest of nine suspects, one of whom was a senior customs
officer. [@1]

Police said the drugs seized carried an estimated street value of over $14
million Bermuda dollars. [@1] As part of the same operation, warrants were
executed at a number of residences in Pembroke, Hamilton Parish, Warwick and
Southampton. [@1] The Royal Gazette said the seizure was part of a wider drug
bust dubbed Operation Furud and led to those search warrants. [@2]

Acting Detective Superintendent Sherwin Joseph, the officer in charge of the
Crime Division, said the seizure had led to the dismantling of a major
drug-trafficking ring and had left Bermuda a much safer place. [@1] He said
efforts to traffic drugs into the island often involve the illegal importation
of firearms and other contraband, and that police would keep working with
partners at ports of entry to strengthen security controls. [@1] Police said no
further information would be provided and that the Drugs Unit was continuing its
investigation. [@1]
