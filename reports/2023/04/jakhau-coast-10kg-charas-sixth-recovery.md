The BSF, working together with the Naval Intelligence Unit, recovered 10 packets
of charas marked “Afghan product” off the Jakhau coast in Bhuj in the sixth such
recovery this month. [@1] The search party found the packets from Ibrahim Peer
Bet on Sunday. [@1] That Sunday was 23 April 2023, the day before the archive
article published on April 24, 2023 that reported the recovery. [@1]

Each packet, a statement read, weighed one kg, giving a combined weight of 10
kilograms for the 10 packets in this recovery. [@1] The BSF has recovered 27
packets in total so far in the search operations launched since the first
recovery on April 12. [@1] The find was reported as the sixth incident in that
continuing series of coastal recoveries this month. [@1] The 10 packets of
charas marked “Afghan product” were therefore reported as the sixth such
recovery off the Jakhau coast in Bhuj this month, according to the BSF account.
