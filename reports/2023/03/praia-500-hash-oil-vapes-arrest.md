Cabo Verde's Judicial Police, acting through its Central Section for
Drug-Trafficking Investigation with the Fiscal Guard of the National Police,
arrested a 33-year-old Cape Verdean man in the city of Praia in flagrante on
Thursday 23 March 2023. [@1] The police said the detention was carried out as
part of preventing and combating illicit drug trafficking by sea. [@2]

According to the police, the suspect tried to introduce 500 electronic
cigarettes into national territory inside parcels arriving from France. [@1]
Each of the 500 cigarettes contained 0.5 millilitres of hashish oil, amounting
to 250 millilitres of the oil in total, which officers seized from the shipment.
[@3] The police identified the seized liquid as hashish oil contained in the
electronic cigarettes. [@1]

The detainee was presented on 24 March to the competent judicial authorities for
a first judicial interrogation as a detained defendant and for a decision on
personal coercion measures. [@2] The court imposed pre-trial detention, which
the Judicial Police described as the most serious coercion measure. [@4]
