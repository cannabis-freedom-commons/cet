Agents of the National Division Against Criminal Development of Gangs (Dipanda)
and Station 11 of the National Civil Police intercepted three alleged drug
distributors travelling in a car carrying suspected drugs. [@1] The operation
took place on 18th Street between 5th and 4th Avenue, zone 1 of Guatemala City.
[@1] The three men detained were aged 32, 38 and 25. [@1]

Officers seized 3 lb of marijuana (about 1.36 kg), a bladed object and 5 mobile
phones to be analysed for the investigation. [@1]

Police checks showed one detainee had a prior record for drug distribution and
another for illegal firearm possession, trade, trafficking and unlawful storage.
[@1] The third detainee had prior records for robbery and aggravated robbery.
[@1]
