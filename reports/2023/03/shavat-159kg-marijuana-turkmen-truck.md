On 31 March, Uzbek customs officers detained a freight truck from Turkmenistan
at the Shavat border checkpoint, where marijuana (anasha) had been hidden. [@1]
The Shavat checkpoint lies between Dashoguz velayat in Turkmenistan and Khorezm
region in Uzbekistan. [@1] The truck was seized and its 58-year-old driver,
K.Zh. from Mary velayat, was detained. [@1]

Scanning revealed a hiding place holding 146 foam-filled packages of anasha with
a total weight of 159 kilograms. [@1] Accompanying documents said the truck was
carrying polymer granules used to make polyethylene film from Balkan velayat to
Uzbekistan. [@1] The driver carried a document stating that Turkmen customs had
checked the cargo and found nothing prohibited. [@1]

Before opening the stash, the Uzbek side invited a Turkmen customs officer, drew
up a report and sent a written appeal to the relevant Turkmen authorities. [@1]
The driver said he knew nothing about the drugs and was only a carrier. [@1] The
Turkmen side did not react to the detention. [@1]
