The Jennings County Sheriff’s Office responded to a 911 call on February 2 about
an unresponsive five-year-old girl at a home in Spencer Township. [@2] Deputies
found the girl unconscious on the floor and she was taken to St. Vincent
Jennings Hospital and then flown by medical helicopter to Peyton Manning
Children’s Hospital in Indianapolis for treatment. [@2] The girl regained
consciousness a day after admission and was discharged 24 hours later. [@2]

Investigators found the girl had ingested THC gummies the day before the 911
call and had been acting abnormal and lethargic. [@1] Treatment was not sought
until the child was found unconscious the next day. [@1] The investigation found
it was the second time the girl had been exposed to THC-infused products, while
the first incident went unreported. [@1]

Amanda Johnson, the girl’s mother from North Vernon, was taken into custody
without incident on March 10 on an arrest warrant from the Jennings County
Circuit Court. [@2] She was charged with neglect of a dependent causing serious
bodily injury, a level 3 felony. [@2] The Jennings County Sheriff’s Office
cautioned parents to keep THC-infused products out of the reach of children.
[@2]
