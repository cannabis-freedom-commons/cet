Three people accused of smuggling drugs into Korea from Hawaii disguised in snack packages were arrested, the Busan Metropolitan Police Agency said. [@1, 2] The suspects, including a 32-year-old Korean, allegedly brought 10 kilograms of drugs, including cocaine and MDMA, from the United States to Korea between July 2021 and March 2023. [@1, 2]

The group vacuum-packed the drugs, hid them in snack boxes and sent them by international post from Hawaii to an empty house, where buyers later collected the parcels. [@1] Police seized 5.8 kilograms of marijuana, 3,000 ecstasy pills and 20 grams of cocaine in the investigation. [@1, 2] Officers traced a parcel sent to Sasang District in Busan in July 2021 back to the 32-year-old living in Hawaii, who was caught when he returned to Korea to renew his passport. [@1] Club workers bought ecstasy pills from the alleged smugglers for 70,000 to 150,000 won each to resell to customers, a police officer said. [@1]
