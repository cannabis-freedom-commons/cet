Border guards at the Brest border checkpoint found a large consignment of
hashish in a car on 19 March 2023 that was intended for sale in Russia. [@1]
Criminal cases for illegal trafficking of narcotic drugs, psychotropic
substances, their precursors and analogues and for illegally moving such
substances across the Eurasian Economic Union customs border and the Belarusian
state border were opened against the Moldovan driver who had brought the hashish
into Belarus. [@1] On 24 March the alleged organizer of the supply channel, a
Russian citizen, was detained as a result of joint work with officers of the
Internal Affairs Directorate of the Brest Regional Executive Committee and
Russian law enforcement bodies. [@1]

The seized load weighed 80 kilograms. [@2] It was carried by a Moldovan citizen
travelling from Poland to Belarus and had been hidden in a hide fitted between
the floor of the car and its underbody. [@2]

Investigators established that the final destination of the route was St
Petersburg. [@3] The Moldovan carrier faces up to 15 years of imprisonment. [@3]
Brest Border Group spokesman Sergei Dmitriev said the route ended in St
Petersburg and that the Russian organizer had been detained on Russian
territory. [@4]
