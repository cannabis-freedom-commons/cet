Acting on intelligence, teams of Honduras's National Anti-Drug Police
Directorate (DNPA) went to a courier company's warehouse in San Pedro Sula,
Cortés, on 10 March 2023. [@1] They found a brown cardboard box containing nine
nylon-wrapped packages holding a dried herb suspected to be marijuana. [@1] Each
package weighed about 2.3 pounds, giving a reported total of about 20 pounds
(9.07 kg). [@1]

The parcel's final destination was the city of Ocotepeque. [@1] El Heraldo
reported that DNPA officers carried out the operation at the courier warehouse
and found nine packages of the drug inside a brown cardboard box. [@2] That
account says the case was referred to the Special Prosecutor's Office Against
Organized Crime (Fescco). [@2] La Prensa reported that the discovery came from
an inspection by anti-narcotics investigators acting on knowledge of a possible
drug cargo. [@4] El País reported that the cardboard parcel held nine nylon
wrappings in which suspected marijuana was visible. [@3]

No arrests were reported; officers said they would carry out investigations to
identify the suspects. [@1] El País reported that investigators were trying to
locate the parcel's recipients. [@3]
