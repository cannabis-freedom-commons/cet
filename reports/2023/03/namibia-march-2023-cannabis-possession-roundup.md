Namibian police arrested a 28-year-old Angolan man for possession of cannabis
worth N$10,250 in Noordoewer, //Kharas Region. [@1] Police said he was found
with 205 grams of cannabis in an outside toilet. [@1] The drugs were confiscated
and the suspect was arrested. [@1] In a separate case, a 42-year-old Zimbabwean
man was arrested for possession of cannabis. [@1] That arrest was made on the
road between Gobabis and Drimiopsis. [@1]

Police said the Zimbabwean was allegedly travelling with the drugs on public
transport. [@1] At a roadblock, officers discovered 2,850 grams of pure cannabis
on him, valued by police at N$142,500. [@1] The drugs were confiscated and he
was expected to appear in the Gobabis Magistrate Court on Monday. [@1] The
Angolan man was expected in the Karasburg Magistrate Court on Monday 27 March.
[@1]

The two seizures were part of a broader set of drug-possession incidents NamPol
reported from that Friday. [@1] Police said investigations into all the matters
continued. [@1]
