South Jordan police stopped Kerry D. Crosby, 66, in a Ford Explorer after he
pulled away from South Jordan Parkway and Jordan Gateway without activating turn
signals. [@1] The officer activated his emergency lights and made the stop at
480 W. Cardon Ridge Way. [@1] The officer reported an odor of marijuana, and
Crosby said he had smoked in the vehicle that morning and had no valid marijuana
card. [@1]

A search found a Ziploc baggie with a small amount of green leafy substance that
later tested positive for marijuana. [@1] The same search found substances that
tested positive for heroin, cocaine and methamphetamine, along with hydrocodone
and oxycodone pills. [@1] Officers also recovered a scale, torches, lighters,
empty bags and glass pipes with burnt residue. [@1]

Crosby was transported to Salt Lake County Jail and booked. [@1] He was charged
with 11 additional crimes, including six felonies. [@1] One of the counts was
possession of a controlled substance, marijuana/spice, a class B misdemeanor.
[@1] Four counts charged possession with intent to distribute a controlled
substance as a first-degree felony. [@1] Two further counts alleged the same
offence as a second-degree felony. [@1] Crosby was ordered held without bail.
