Alexta Gill, a Guyanese national, denied charges of importing cannabis and
cocaine into Bermuda on March 4, 2023. [@1] Gill arrived on the island with
Jhordan George-Horsford on a flight from Toronto on March 2, and one of their
suitcases arrived two days later. [@1] Officers arrested both Gill and
George-Horsford at the hotel at about 8.30pm on March 5. [@1] Prosecutors closed
their case against Gill while the jury heard more about his movements after
arrival. [@1] The trial was still continuing when this account was published.
[@1]

A search of the delayed bag revealed six bricks of plant material confirmed as
9,472.6 grammes of cannabis and a whisky bottle containing liquid cocaine from
which 812.4g was extracted. [@1] A search at Hamilton Police Station found plant
material confirmed as 9.71g of cannabis and a grinder on Gill. [@1]

In a police interview Gill said he had agreed to bring a suitcase for someone
named Andre to clear a debt after receiving death threats. [@1] Hotel records
showed Gill and George-Horsford checked into the Four Ways Inn on the afternoon
of March 3. [@1] The cannabis packages were swabbed for DNA, but both Gill and
George-Horsford were excluded as donors. [@1]
