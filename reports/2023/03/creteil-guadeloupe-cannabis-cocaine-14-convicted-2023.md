The criminal court in Créteil convicted 14 men on Friday 3 March 2023 over a
drug trafficking network between Guadeloupe and mainland France. [@1] A
42-year-old delivery driver from Créteil, described as one of the network's
organisers, received the heaviest sentence: five years in prison with a
committal warrant and a 50,000-euro fine. [@1] Another organiser was given the
same sentence. [@1]

In Guadeloupe the Créteil man supplied cannabis to people who could in turn
supply him with cocaine. [@1] He then handled the transfer of the cocaine, which
is very cheap that close to the Americas, to mainland France. [@1] One of the
routes he used was postal parcels, and he had approached a postman on the spot.
[@1] Prosecutors sought two years, one of them firm, against the postal worker,
who received only a suspended sentence. [@1] An Engie technician, who used his
work visits to spot disused letterboxes, also received only a suspended
sentence. [@1]

One of the "nourrices", whose role was to store the cocaine, was sentenced to
two years, one of them firm and the rest convertible, although prosecutors had
sought two firm years. [@1] Nearly 1.5 kg of cocaine had rotted at that
defendant's home. [@1] A man suspected of hosting drug mules near Orly airport,
arrested beside a split-open suitcase full of cocaine, was sentenced to 22
months with a semi-liberty arrangement. [@1] The exchange of cannabis for
cocaine between the mainland and the Antilles is long-standing and has grown in
recent years. [@1]
