Prefectura Naval Argentina officers seized a cargo of contraband goods and drugs
that was to be brought into Argentina through the coast of the city of Puerto
Rico, in Misiones. [@1] The force valued the merchandise and the drug at about 9
million pesos. [@1] Officers on a surveillance patrol at kilometre 1,738 of the
Paraná River saw a boat crossing from the Paraguayan bank to the Argentine bank
with two crew members and several bundles aboard. [@1]

Where the boat came ashore, the patrol found a bag containing more than 14
kilograms of marijuana. [@1] The search also turned up 750 cartons of foreign
cigarettes, more than 400,000 pesos in cash and clothing for men and women,
among other items. [@1] Prefectura put the total of everything seized, including
the goods, a ballistic vest, a handheld radio, the drug, the boat and its motor,
at more than 11.1 million pesos. [@1]

The federal first-instance court and the federal prosecutor's office in Oberá
are handling the case. [@1]
