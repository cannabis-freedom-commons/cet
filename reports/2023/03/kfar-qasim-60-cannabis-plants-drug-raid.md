Officers from the Kfar Qasim police station seized 60 cannabis plants and about
four kilograms of suspected drug material during a search of an open area on
Monday 27 March 2023. [@3] Kfar Qasim is in central Israel, and the search was
part of a police operation against weapons and drugs. [@1] The four kilograms
was described as a drug presumed to be marijuana, and the plants were also
called seedlings. [@2] The find was made on a Monday in Kfar Qasim. [@2]

Police handed the box of ancient finds to the Antiquities Authority's
theft-prevention unit. [@3] An Antiquities Authority official identified the
objects as jars, jugs and bowls dating to the Early and Middle Bronze Age, about
3,500 years ago. [@1] Nearby ancient burial caves had recently been plundered,
and some still contained the robbers' tools. [@1]

Amir Ganor, head of the Antiquities Authority unit, said every artifact removed
from its archaeological context was a piece taken from a shared heritage puzzle.
[@1] The Antiquities Authority opened an investigation to identify those
responsible for the looting. [@3]
