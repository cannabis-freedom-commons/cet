Saint Lucia police officers carried out an operation in Dennery district at
about 4:00 a.m. on Friday 31 March 2023. [@1] Officers executed a search warrant
at the premises of Lance Isman of Dennery and recovered a quantity of crack
cocaine and cannabis. [@1] Isman was arrested and charged with unlawful
possession of cannabis, possession of cannabis with intent to supply, possession
of crack cocaine and possession of crack cocaine with intent to supply. [@2] A
second warrant, at the premises of Oniel Emmanuel of Dennery, recovered a
quantity of controlled drugs and 80 fully grown cannabis plants. [@1] Emmanuel
was arrested and charged with cultivation of cannabis, possession of controlled
drugs and possession of cannabis with intent to supply to another. [@1] A third
warrant, at the premises of Dameon Edward of Dennery, recovered a quantity of
cannabis resin, and Edward was charged with unlawful possession of cannabis
resin. [@1]

All three men were granted bail on their own recognizance and are to appear in
court at a later date. [@3]
