On 27 June 2023 Gendarmerie officers of the Investigations and Procedures
Operational Group, supported by the Núcleo Section of Squadron 51 Fontana,
searched a house in Resistencia, Chaco, under a court order in a drug case. [@1]
Officers found 102 cannabis sativa plants and 1 kilo 739 grams of marijuana buds
in the property. [@1] They also found chemical products used to grow the plants.
[@1] Guarantee Court No. 5 and Anti-Drug Investigation Prosecutor's Office No. 2
ordered the drug confiscated and the man placed at the disposal of the case.
[@1]

In a second procedure, a Gendarmerie patrol of Squadron 16 Clorinda saw a
Paraguayan national carrying a backpack and, when he noticed the officers, he
tried to flee on a motorcycle. [@1] He was arrested immediately and was found to
be carrying 1 kilo 845 grams of marijuana buds. [@1] The case was recorded as an
infringement of Law 23.737. [@1] The Federal Court and Federal Prosecutor's
Office No. 1 of Formosa ordered the drug seized and the man detained. [@1]
