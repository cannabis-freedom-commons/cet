Delbert Philbert, 38, of Fond Baron, Loubiere was fined $3,000 by Magistrate
Michael Laudat after pleading guilty to trafficking $375 worth of cannabis. [@1]
He appeared in court on 15 June 2023, jointly charged with Lenisha Mitchel for
trafficking 170 grams of cannabis, and opted for a summary trial while Mitchel
pleaded not guilty. [@1] The prosecution offered no evidence against Mitchel, so
the case against her was dismissed. [@1]

Drug Squad and Criminal Investigations Department officers searched Philbert's
premises at Fond Baron at about 6 a.m. on 14 June 2023 under a search warrant.
[@1] In a bedroom an officer found a clear glass jar of zip lock bags containing
what appeared to be cannabis. [@1] Philbert said it was “a little weed I have
there for me to smoke.” [@1] As the jar held more than 28 grams, the legal
possession limit, he was arrested on suspicion of drug trafficking. [@1] He told
police he had more cannabis in a plastic bag in the bathroom, which was seized.
[@1] At Police Headquarters 56 zip lock bags from the jar and a clear and black
plastic bag were weighed and totalled 170 grams. [@1] The cannabis was sent for
government analysis, returned positive, and Philbert was formally charged with
drug trafficking. [@1]

His lawyer Wayne Norde asked the court to impose a noncustodial sentence on the
father of three. [@1] Norde said Philbert's other convictions were not
drug-related and that he had no intention to sell the cannabis. [@1] Magistrate
Laudat said the sentencing guideline allowed a maximum of $150,000 or a term of
imprisonment. [@1] The court imposed a $3,000 fine payable in two instalments,
$1,500 by 31 October 2023 and the balance by 31 March 2024. [@1] Philbert faces
six months in prison in default of payment, and the cannabis and paraphernalia
were forfeited to the state. [@1]
