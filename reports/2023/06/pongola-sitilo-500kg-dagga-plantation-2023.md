Soldiers of 7 South African Infantry Battalion and Pongola police uprooted a
dagga (cannabis) plantation on a farm at Sitilo in Pongola, northern
KwaZulu-Natal. [@1] An undocumented man from eSwatini found during the operation
was taken to Pongola police for questioning. [@1] The battalion described the
operation as its biggest success since being deployed to the KwaZulu-Natal
borders with Mozambique and eSwatini. [@1]

The 7 SA Infantry Battalion is based at Phalaborwa and is deployed on the South
Africa-Eswatini border in the Pongola area. [@2] The plantation covered about
0.3 hectares and the uprooted plants weighed 500 kg, which the battalion valued
at an estimated R2.1 million. [@1] The plants were weighed before they were
destroyed so that a street value could be calculated. [@2] The seized dagga
weighed 500 kg and was given an estimated street value of R2.1 million. [@3]

The soldiers said they remained committed to the objectives of Joint Tactical
Headquarters KwaZulu-Natal. [@1]
