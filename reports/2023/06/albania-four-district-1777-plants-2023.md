On 21 June 2023 Albania's State Police announced a coordinated operation that burned 1,777 cannabis seedlings and plants in the Skrapar, Elbasan, Krujë and Lezhë districts, deploying 300 officers and 9 drones. [@1,2] Four suspected growers were declared wanted, and the case files were sent to the prosecutors. [@1,2]

In Skrapar's Gjerbës unit, officers found 1,000 seedlings grown in cubes inside a greenhouse under the drive coded "Tomorri"; O. Gj., 39, of Bërsak was declared wanted. [@1,2] In Elbasan, the drives "Krasta" and "Çereshta" accounted for 252 destroyed plants, including 160 at Seltë in Zavalinë, where A. D., 53, of Tirana was declared wanted; two forest inspectors and the Seltë village head were prosecuted for abuse of duty without detention. [@1,2] Librazhd police running "Çereshta" since 18 June found 92 more plants on 20 June, along with a tent allegedly used by wanted suspects S. D. and K. D. and two plastic bags of cannabis seized as evidence. [@1,2]

In Krujë's "Territor i pastër" drive, 195 plants were burned at Cudhi Kamp, and in Lezhë's "Imazhi" drive 330 plants were burned at Malecaj in Balldren; no growers were identified in either place. [@1,2]
