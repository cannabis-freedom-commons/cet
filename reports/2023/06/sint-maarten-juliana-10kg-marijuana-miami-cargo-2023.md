Sint Maarten customs officers inspected a cargo shipment from Miami at Juliana
airport's freight facilities on Friday 16 June 2023. [@2] Using modern cargo
inspection equipment, customs found 10,208 kg of marijuana, written with a comma
as a decimal separator and so equal to 10.208 kg. [@1] The contraband was hidden
among other items inside a box. [@1]

No arrests were made at the time and customs said the investigation was
continuing. [@1] The find came during a run of checks at Juliana airport over a
few days that also covered cash, counterfeit goods and cocaine. [@1] On 12 June,
cargo from Anguilla was X-rayed and cash hidden in a package bound for the
United States was confiscated. [@1] Two days later, customs examined a 40-foot
container of suspected counterfeit Louis Vuitton and Nike goods and had them
destroyed. [@1] On 17 June, customs checking luggage from a Caribbean Airlines
flight from Jamaica found four metal objects filled with cocaine. [@1] A
30-year-old man was arrested in connection with that find and taken into
custody. [@1]
