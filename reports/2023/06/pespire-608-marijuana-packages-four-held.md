DIPAMPCO, Honduras's police directorate against gangs and organized crime,
arrested four people, including a 41-year-old known as 'Alen' from Namasigüe,
Choluteca. [@1] Another detainee was alias 'El Zarco', 26, a resident of
Namasigüe. [@1] The other two were alias 'La Negra', 37, from El Triunfo, and
alias 'Leo Dan', 47, originally from Tocoa, Colón and resident in El Triunfo.
[@1] Honduran police said the suspects had been followed for several months
because of drug distribution. [@1]

Diario Tiempo said the seizure was carried out on Thursday 22 June in Pespire,
Choluteca, and included a vehicle and cash. [@3] The operation was part of a
plan against extortion and related crimes and took place in the aldea of San
Juan Bosco, municipality of Pespire. [@1] The detainees were carrying six boxes
in a pickup truck containing 608 packages of marijuana. [@1] Diario Tiempo
reported that the pickup used to carry the drugs was itself seized. [@3]
Officers also took 19,000 lempiras in cash and four mobile phones. [@1] DIPAMPCO
valued the drug shipment at about one million lempiras. [@1]

Investigators said the marijuana came from the central part of Honduras and was
bound for neighborhoods and colonies of Choluteca department, handled by members
of independent criminal structures. [@1] HCH reported that DIPAMPCO acted
together with the National Directorate of Prevention and Community Security
(DNPSC) against drug trafficking structures in the south of the country. [@2]
