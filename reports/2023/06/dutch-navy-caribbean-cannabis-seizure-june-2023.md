Dutch marines and Caribbean Coast Guard crews on the patrol ship Zr.Ms.
Groningen intercepted two drug shipments in the Caribbean Sea. [@1] The
interception teams included coast guard personnel, marines from the Fleet Marine
Squadron and the ship's crew. [@3] The Groningen launched two fast FRISC
interception boats to chase the first go-fast. [@3] The interceptions took place
on the nights of Thursday and Friday. [@1] The first boat carried five suspects.
[@3] The second boat, detected the following night, carried seven people. [@3]
All twelve were Venezuelan and were taken into custody. [@1]

The two shipments weighed a total of 2,000 kilograms. [@1] One batch was cocaine
and the other cannabis. [@2] The navy fired warning shots when the crews refused
to acknowledge its orders. [@1] Officers then shot at the outboard motors to
stop the boats. [@1] Drugs thrown overboard during the chases were recovered
from the sea. [@1]

The suspects and the seized drugs were handed to the Curaçao police corps. [@3]
The Groningen, a Holland-class patrol ship, had been operating in the Caribbean
since May 2023. [@4]
