A Cuban man was detained in the eastern city of Holguín on suspicion of selling
marijuana and hashish, according to official information. [@1] He was identified
as Leandro Santiesteban Torres, a resident of the well-known La Quinta
neighbourhood of the provincial capital. [@2] His name was published on the
Facebook profile 'Cazador, Cazado', a page linked to Cuba's Interior Ministry
(MININT) that is used to highlight police work amid a wave of insecurity in
Cuba. [@1] The Holguín police made the arrest. [@2]

Torres had no previous criminal record and had made a living decorating houses
with cement sculptures before he changed course and took up drug trafficking.
[@2] The Facebook post said he sold hashish and marijuana to anyone, without
caring whether his customers were minors. [@1] He was detained on suspicion of
drug trafficking, and investigators continued looking into the origin of the
illegal substances. [@2]
