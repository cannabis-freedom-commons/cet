The university searched the American football team's dormitory in Nakano Ward on 6 July 2023 and found a dried plant fragment and two pills in a locked closet in Shigefumi Kitabatake's room. [@1] Nihon University did not contact police until the evening of 18 July, twelve days after the items were found in the dormitory. [@4] Police had been contacted by university officials who were told that some football team members were using cannabis. [@3] Police examined the seized items on 19 July and confirmed the fragment was dried marijuana. [@1,5] About 20 investigators searched the dormitory for about five hours from 1 p.m. on 3 August. [@6] The Tokyo Metropolitan Police Department searched the football team's dormitory in Nakano Ward on 3 August 2023. [@3]

The Tokyo Metropolitan Police arrested Kitabatake, a 21-year-old junior player, on 5 August 2023 for alleged cannabis and stimulant offences. [@1,2]

Police searched the dormitory again on 22 August 2023 after other players were suspected of possessing cannabis. [@2] Nihon University officials were questioning all members of the football team, including those who had since left the squad, and the coaching staff. [@3] The university suspended the team indefinitely and closed the dormitory. [@2]
