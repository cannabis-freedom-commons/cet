The Customs Surveillance patrol boat Condor boarded a vessel 95 miles south of the Canary Islands after months of monitoring, finding 6,044 kilos of hashish divided into 169 bales and arresting the two crew on board. [@1] The boarding capped an investigation opened in 2020, when a sailing boat moored on Lanzarote raised suspicions and Dutch police pointed to a criminal organisation of Dutch origin linked to it. [@1]

Police say the Netherlands-based organisation used a town in Alicante as its base, with a trafficker running logistics to move large drug quantities between European countries under strict security. [@1] Six raids across Alicante, Murcia, Las Palmas and Madrid seized more than 35,000 euros in cash, two boats, a vehicle, phones and vessel documents, and a Murcia warehouse search dismantled an illicit synthetic-drug tablet laboratory. [@1]

Five people were arrested in total, including the leader of an international drug-trafficking and money-laundering network who had been on the run from the German authorities. [@1] The operation drew on Portuguese, Swedish, Dutch and French police cooperation and the Lisbon-based Maritime Analysis and Operations Centre. [@1]
