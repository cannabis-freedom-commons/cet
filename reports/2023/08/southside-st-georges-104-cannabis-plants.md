Officers of the Bermuda Police Service, from the Police Support Unit of the
Tactical Operations Division, carried out an exercise on Sunday 13 August 2023
targeting illegal cannabis cultivation at a site in the Southside, St. George's
area. [@1]

During that exercise the officers uprooted and seized a total of 104 fully grown
cannabis plants under the Misuse of Drugs Act. [@1] The plants were seized at a
site in Southside, St George's, in a Sunday raid by officers from the Tactical
Operations Division. [@2] No arrests had been made in connection with the
controlled-drug seizure. [@1]

An investigation was being conducted for possible violations of the Misuse of
Drugs Act, which prohibits any cultivation of the plant. [@2] Inquiries were
under way, and anyone with information that might assist the Bermuda Police
Service was asked to call 295-0011 or 211, or to speak with a police officer
they knew. [@1] Anonymous tips could instead be given to the independent and
confidential Crime Stoppers hotline at 800-8477. [@1]
