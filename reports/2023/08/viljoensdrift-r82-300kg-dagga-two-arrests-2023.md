Fezile Dabi District Highway Patrol members arrested two men aged 37 and 33 in
Viljoensdrift, Free State, for dagga trafficking on the night of 30 August 2023.
[@1] The members were patrolling the R82 at about 23:35 when they noticed a
suspicious vehicle with a Lesotho registration and pulled it over. [@1] The two
occupants were men described as illegal immigrants. [@2] Police said the
suspects tried to bribe the members to evade arrest. [@1]

A search of the vehicle found 11 bags containing dagga. [@1] The dagga weighed
300 kg and was valued at about R800 000, and police confiscated it. [@1]

The two appeared in the Sasolburg Magistrate's Court on 1 September 2023 on
charges of dagga trafficking, bribery and being illegal immigrants. [@2]
Brigadier Mapulane Mbeloane, acting district commissioner of Fezile Dabi,
applauded the members for being vigilant and proactive in making sure the
suspects ended up behind bars. [@1]
