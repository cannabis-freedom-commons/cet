Nicaragua's army seized 100 compact packages of marijuana before dawn on Monday
7 August 2023 in the Namaslí sector of Jalapa, Nueva Segovia, near the Honduran
border. [@1] Soldiers detained Luis Alberto Ardón Colindres, 43, a Honduran
national from El Paraíso, Colonel Fabio de Jesús Zelaya Reyes, head of the
army's 1st Military Region, said. [@1] Two other men fired a revolver at the
troops and fled towards Honduran territory, and were not caught. [@3]

The outlets said the packages weighed about a pound (0.45 kg) each and were
wrapped in adhesive tape. [@3] The army's own statement counted 50 double
packages of marijuana in a white sack, the same load the outlets described as
100 compact packages. [@2] Soldiers also took two motorcycles without plates, a
bicycle and a mobile phone, which the army said were used in the offence. [@2]
The detainee, the drug, the motorcycles, the bicycle and the phone were handed
to the authorities. [@2]

Colonel Zelaya said the 1st Military Region was continuing the Muro de
Contención, or containment wall, strategy against drug trafficking, with
checkpoints, patrols, ambushes and observation posts. [@1] He said trafficking
in the border zone was recurrent and that the army concentrated its main efforts
on striking it directly. [@1]
