Twenty-five people were held by Mozambique's national police in the city of
Lichinga in Niassa, accused of producing, selling and consuming cannabis sativa,
known locally as suruma. [@1] Suruma is the local name used for cannabis sativa.
[@1] The suspects confessed to the offences of which they were accused. [@1]
Police described those involved as aged from 17 to 90. [@1] In Cuamba district,
police reported neutralising two people in a cannabis seizure. [@1]

In Lichinga, the PRM seized more than 70 kilograms of cannabis from those held.
[@1] The PRM is the Polícia da República de Moçambique, Mozambique's national
police. [@1] The provincial police command also reported significant cannabis
seizures in some districts. [@1] In Cuamba, officers seized 300 grams of
cannabis sativa. [@1] In Sanga district, officers seized one and a half
kilograms of the drug. [@1]

The account was given by Mirza Maguanda, spokeswoman for the PRM provincial
command in Niassa. [@1] She said the operations were intended to discourage and
eliminate drug production, sale and consumption and would continue. [@1]
