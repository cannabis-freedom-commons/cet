Customs and border officers at the Oybek border crossing point in Uzbekistan's
Tashkent region stopped a car carrying a large drug shipment on 17 August 2023.
[@1] The Tashkent region customs administration said the drugs were being
smuggled from Tajikistan into Uzbekistan. [@2] The car was a Mercedes-Benz. [@3]

Officers found 49 polyethylene packages in the car's gas cylinder and 31 in its
petrol tank. [@1] Experts from the Central Customs Laboratory concluded that the
packages contained 52.8 kg of hashish. [@1] The same shipment held more than 2
kg of heroin and a tube from under glue with 950 g of a synthetic drug, which
are not counted as cannabis. [@1] The drugs weighed 55 kg in total. [@1]

A pre-investigation check was under way into the case. [@2] The Border Liaison
Office at Oybek was established in 2014 and is one of 23 in Central Asia set up
at key border crossing points under a UNODC regional programme. [@1] UNODC says
the offices' stakeholders are the interior ministries, border troops, customs
and drug control agencies of each country. [@1]
