On 11 August 2023 border guards detained a woman who was driving by car from
Lithuania to Belarus through the Losha checkpoint. [@1] During inspection of the
boot, a service dog found a pack of tea containing hemp. [@1] The seized tea
packs were photographed as taken from the woman at Losha on 11 August 2023. [@1]
The Belarusian citizen said she had bought the herbal tea pack in Lithuania.
[@1]

The same type of tea is sold in practically every grocery store in Lithuania.
[@3] The State Border Committee said illegal trafficking of narcotic drugs,
psychotropic substances, their precursors and analogues carries restriction of
liberty for up to five years or imprisonment for two to five years. [@2]

Belarus prohibits any parts of Cannabis plants containing any THC isomer. [@1]
Establishing THC presence in hemp requires an expert examination. [@1] Belarus
has no threshold quantity for liability, so even thousandths or hundredths of a
gram can prompt a criminal case. [@1]
