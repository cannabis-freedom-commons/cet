Prefectura Naval Argentina officers arrested a man on a boat carrying bundles on
the Paraná river at Barranqueras, in Chaco province. [@1] The force said the
seized drug weighed 378 kilograms. [@1]

Officers intercepted the boat at kilometre 1,223 of the Paraná after the force's
Chaco criminal intelligence delegation saw one person on the water transporting
packages. [@1] On board they found 32 jute sacks holding 306 packages of
cannabis buds. [@1] Prefectura valued the load at 3,200,000 pesos. [@1] The man
in charge of the boat was detained at the scene. [@2]

The case went to the Secretaría Penal Nº 1 of Federal Court No. 2 in
Resistencia, in the hands of judge Javier Pastore. [@1] The Chaco outlet Portal
Móvil named federal judge Ricardo Mianovich as handling the case. [@5] The
federal court in Resistencia was investigating where the load came from and
where it was going, details that were not released. [@3]

Mejor Informado described the Paraná, where the boat was stopped, as one of the
main routes for drugs smuggled into Argentina. [@4]
