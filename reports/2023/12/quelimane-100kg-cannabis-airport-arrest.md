SERNIC officers in Quelimane seized more than 100 kilograms of cannabis sativa
in a house. [@1] The house was in the Acordos de Lusaka neighbourhood of the
city. [@1] Officers arrested a 17-year-old domestic worker during the seizure.
[@1] The teenager's employer is on the run. [@1]

The drug arrived in the city through the Quelimane airfield on Thursday 14
December, according to Maximino Amílcar, SERNIC's spokesman in Zambézia
province. [@1] The cannabis came from the Kingdom of Eswatini. [@1] The teenager
told SERNIC he received the bag of cannabis at about 20:00 on the Thursday. [@1]
Amílcar said investigations indicated a luxury car had taken the drug from
Quelimane airport to the house. [@1]

SERNIC said it could not explain how the drug got through the airport, where all
security systems were in place. [@1] Amílcar said he was concerned because
multisectoral teams operate at airports. [@1] SERNIC said it was the eighth case
in Quelimane in which minors were used in drug schemes. [@1] Amílcar said minors
were used because they cannot be held criminally responsible. [@1]
