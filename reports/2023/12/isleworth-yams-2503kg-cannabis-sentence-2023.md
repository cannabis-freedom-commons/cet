Anand Tripathi, 61, and Varun Bhardwaj, 39, were sentenced at Isleworth Crown
Court in west London to a combined 34 years in prison for smuggling cocaine,
cannabis and cigarettes hidden in farm produce. [@1,2] A jury had convicted
them in November after a 71-day trial. [@1,2] Sentencing was reported on 15
December 2023. [@2]

The court heard the pair imported 272.86 kg of cocaine and 2,503.36 kg of
cannabis across four shipments between September 2021 and November 2022.
[@1,2] One shipment held just over two tonnes of cannabis hidden among yams
from Ghana, while 49 kg of cocaine arrived concealed with oranges from South
Africa. [@1,2] They used their freight company to clear containers and divert
them to a warehouse they controlled, where organised crime groups collected
the loads. [@1,2] The drugs, worth an estimated 28.9 million pounds on the
street, were all seized before sale and later destroyed by police. [@1]
