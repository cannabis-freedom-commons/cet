The Oklahoma Bureau of Narcotics raided an illegal marijuana grow in Healdton,
Oklahoma, in December 2023, describing it as another illegal marijuana grow.
[@1] Agents carried out the raid on Wednesday 13 December 2023, the 'last
Wednesday' before the 18 December publication. [@1] State and local authorities
teamed up to shut down the illegal grow in Carter County. [@2] The grow was on
Highway 76 north of Buck Horn Road in Healdton. [@2] Jimmy Dang, 46, was
arrested as the grow's 'straw owner' because his name was illegally used on the
facility's licence. [@1] The bureau said the grow was illegally using a 'straw
ownership' structure. [@2] Under that structure the person legally listed as the
farm's owner is not the one operating the business. [@2]

Officials said agents seized around 11,000 plants and 300 pounds (about 136 kg)
of processed cannabis. [@1] KXII reported the figure as 1,100 marijuana plants
and 300 pounds of processed marijuana. [@2]

The bureau said the bust was tied to a raid at another grow in Ringling two
weeks earlier. [@2]
