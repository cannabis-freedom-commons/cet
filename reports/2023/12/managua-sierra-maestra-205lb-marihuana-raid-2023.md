Nicaragua's National Police raided the home of Darling Yescania Guevara
Cárdenas, 35, in the Sierra Maestra neighbourhood of Managua at 11:30 am on
Friday 15 December 2023 and arrested her. [@1] She was to be prosecuted for drug
trafficking. [@1]

In the search the officers found four sacks holding 200 cylindrical packages
wrapped in adhesive tape and filled with green herb. [@1] A field test on the
herb came back positive for marijuana, with a weight of 205.22 pounds (about 93
kg). [@1] The National Police valued the marijuana at 20,522 US dollars. [@3]
Commissioner general Jaime Vanegas gave the details of the raid at a police
briefing on Monday 18 December. [@2]

Criminalistics investigators carried out a chemical test that concluded positive
for marijuana. [@4] Officers also applied the Scintrex Trace particle detector,
which registers marijuana particles, to her clothing, back and palms, with
positive results. [@1] The marijuana and the woman were handed to the
authorities for judicial proceedings on the drug trafficking charge. [@1]

Police presented the raid as part of their muro de contención (containment wall)
strategy against organised crime and drug trafficking. [@1] Unofficially, police
presumed the marijuana was to be distributed in Managua's neighbourhoods and
said other people could be arrested, describing the woman as allegedly a repeat
offender. [@1]
