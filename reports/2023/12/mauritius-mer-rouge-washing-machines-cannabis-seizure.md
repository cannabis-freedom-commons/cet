No one had been arrested by the day after the discovery, and the Anti Drug and
Smuggling Unit (ADSU) took over the case. [@2] The Customs Anti-Narcotics
Section made the seizure at the port on Wednesday 13 December 2023. [@2] The
Curepipe branch of the ADSU had learned from credible information that a
cannabis cargo was due to reach Mauritius during the festive season and searched
the ED Logistic warehouse in Mer-Rouge under a warrant. [@1] The discovery
followed a routine check in which the unusually heavy machines drew suspicion.
[@2]

L'Express reported the seizure of more than 14 kg of cannabis, valued at Rs 44
million. [@1] Le Mauricien put the value of the same haul at Rs 15 million. [@2]
The 36 bags of cannabis were hidden in two washing machines that had arrived at
the port. [@2] Customs Anti-Narcotics Section officers assisted the police in
opening the boxes. [@1] Unpacking Kelvinator washing machines revealed 36 black
plastic bags containing the cannabis. [@1] The cargo had come from South Africa
and was described as bound for Madagascar. [@1]

Police said the cannabis was of superior quality and would be examined at the
Forensic Science Laboratory to confirm its purity. [@1] Investigators said
traffickers could have sold the shipment at ten times the price during the
festive period. [@1] Investigators planned to ask Malagasy authorities to stage
a controlled delivery, while ADSU headquarters reviewed the warehouse's CCTV
footage and prepared to question its workers. [@1]
