Belgian customs seized about 140 kg of marijuana from passengers landing at
Brussels Airport in Zaventem over the year to December 2023, most of them
arriving from North America. [@1] Customs put the cannabis found on travellers,
often in their suitcases, at 138.5 kg. [@1] With 72.5 kg intercepted in letters
and postal parcels, the airport's cannabis seizures came to more than 210 kg,
slightly more than the 204 kg of cocaine recorded between December 2022 and
December 2023. [@2]

Customs presented the figures on Wednesday 20 December at a briefing on the
seizures made at Brussels Airport. [@1] A customs officer said most finds were
made on passengers arriving from the United States and Canada, where cannabis
can be grown legally. [@1] Speaking anonymously, the officer called the trend a
new phenomenon that would remain a focus in 2024. [@1] Customs said marijuana
sells for about five euros a gram in Belgium, where it is illegal, which in its
view makes legally grown North American cannabis worth smuggling. [@1]

Zaventem remains one of Belgium's main transit points for narcotics, including
synthetic drugs such as ecstasy, ketamine and methamphetamine made in
clandestine laboratories on both sides of the Belgian-Dutch border. [@2] Customs
said those quantities were not comparable with the more than 100 tonnes of
cocaine from South America seized at the port of Antwerp in 2022. [@1]
