Police at the central station of Dabompa, a neighbourhood of Conakry's Matoto
commune, seized 12 bundles of Indian hemp on Thursday 28 December 2023. [@1] Six
people were arrested, three women and three men. [@1] Central commissioner
Thierno Mamadou Samba Barry said the 12 bundles were seized from residents of
Dabompa. [@1] Barry said investigations were under way to find everyone
connected with the case. [@1] The seized parcels were shown to the national
press on Friday 29 December 2023. [@1]

Dabompa lies more than ten kilometres from Kaloum, the administrative centre of
the capital. [@1] The station handed the 12 bundles to the Court of First
Instance at Mafanco, the court for Matoto commune, which includes Dabompa. [@1]
The same station had seized five bundles of Indian hemp a month earlier. [@1]
