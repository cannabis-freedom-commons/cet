A jury of nine women and two men found an Aitutaki man guilty of supplying
cannabis after a two-day trial on Rarotonga. [@2] The man was Oceania Mataiti,
found guilty of supplying cannabis on Aitutaki between 1 January 2021 and 8
February 2022. [@2] The jury returned a majority verdict of nine to two just
after 4pm, after six hours of deliberation. [@2] Mataiti denied supplying
cannabis and told the court through a translator that friends brought their own
cannabis to his Aitutaki home. [@2] He had pleaded guilty to possession of
cannabis and possession of a utensil. [@2]

At the trial the court heard that a police search of his residence on 8 February
2022 uncovered cannabis. [@2] Justice Christine Grice told the jury to put aside
emotion and not be prejudiced by their beliefs. [@2] Crown prosecutor Jamie
Crawford said the starting point for sentencing would be imprisonment, but the
Crown was not opposed to a community-based sentence. [@2] Justice Grice
adjourned the case to December 15 to set a date for sentencing. [@2]

Reader Steve Boggs wrote to the newspaper that Justice Grice had instructed the
jury not once but twice not to be prejudiced by beliefs. [@1] Boggs argued that
jurors could challenge prohibitionist policies through jury nullification and
said no one should be in jail for marijuana. [@1]
