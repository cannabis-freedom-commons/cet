Azerbaijan's State Border Service detained a citizen of Iran at 15:55 on 14
December 2023 as he tried to cross the state border from Iran into Azerbaijan.
[@1] The detention took place in the service area of a border outpost of the
Horadiz border detachment, near the village of Boyuk Bahmanli in Fuzuli
district. [@3]

Officers who searched the package on the detained man found and seized 15
kilograms 600 grams of marijuana. [@1] The border service's press centre
reported the detention and the seizure. [@1] The Azerbaijani outlet 1news
described the detainee as an Iranian drug courier. [@2]

Operational and investigative measures into the case were continuing, the border
service said. [@1] Media.az reported that an investigation was under way, with
the same weight of marijuana. [@4]
