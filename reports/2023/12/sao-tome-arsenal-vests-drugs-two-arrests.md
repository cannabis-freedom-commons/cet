Military police arrested two men in São Tomé, in the Agreste region, on Sunday
10 December 2023 after finding them with an arsenal, masks, bulletproof vests
and drugs. [@1] Officers from the 9th Independent Company said they acted after
receiving a report of several gunshots in the Alto de São Sebastião
neighbourhood. [@1] During searches near Rua Antônio Martins de Araújo, officers
found an unnumbered house with multiple perforation marks on its walls and a
broken-down door. [@1]

Officers entered the property and found the two suspects inside with a large
arsenal of weapons and ammunition as well as drugs. [@1] The weapons included a
9mm semi-automatic carbine, a revolver, a garruncha and ammunition of various
calibres. [@1] Officers also found two ballistic vests and a mask with the
suspects. [@1] Officers also seized wraps of cocaine and marijuana, a precision
scale, two mobile phones, R$100 in cash and a motorcycle. [@1]

The two suspects and all the seized material were taken to the Civil Police
station in São Paulo do Potengi. [@1] The men were to be investigated by the
Civil Police. [@1]
