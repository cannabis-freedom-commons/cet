Cooch Behar district police arrested 37 people for involvement in illegal
cannabis cultivation during a two-month eradication drive, Superintendent of
Police Dyutiman Bhattacharya said. [@2] The drive destroyed cannabis plantations
spread over 3,859 bighas, about 516 hectares, over the two months. [@1]

Police said 739 bighas were destroyed in November and 3,119 bighas in December.
[@1] Police estimated the market value of the destroyed cannabis at several
crores of rupees but published no specific figure. [@1]

Bhattacharya said continuous operations were being conducted against illegal
cannabis cultivation and the campaign would continue into the new year. [@1]
Police said the plantations were in areas under the Kotwali, Tufanganj,
Pundibari, Dinhata and Sitai police stations. [@2] The focus has been on Cooch
Behar blocks I and II, Dinhata, Sitai, Sitalkuchi, Tufanganj, Boxirhat,
Mathabhanga and Mekhliganj. [@1] Cooch Behar borders Bangladesh and Assam. [@1]

Some growers sell cannabis plants to smugglers, while others process the drug
and smuggle it to other states, police said. [@2] A Ruerkuthi resident said a
farmer can earn about Rs 12,000 from a bigha of paddy, but about Rs 50 lakh from
cannabis on the same plot. [@2]
