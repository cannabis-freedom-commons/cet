Members of Cesfront, the border security corps, and agents of the DNCD, the
national drug-control agency, seized 368 pounds of marijuana in Elías Piña
province in an operation coordinated by prosecutors. [@1] Soldiers supported by
anti-narcotics agents were running interdiction checks at the La Pista
checkpoint and stopped a red Daihatsu truck there. [@2] The driver fled on
seeing the officers and left the truck behind; authorities pursued him, having
identified him but chosen to withhold his name. [@1]

In the presence of a prosecutor, officers searched the truck and found sacks of
oranges and lemons in the back containing 46 packages of the drug, with a
preliminary weight of 368.59 pounds (about 167 kilograms). [@3] The officers and
prosecutors also took two mobile phones, the truck's registration plate and
other personal documents. [@1]

The authorities said Cesfront, the army and the DNCD had seized more than 5,000
pounds of marijuana in border provinces during the year. [@1]
