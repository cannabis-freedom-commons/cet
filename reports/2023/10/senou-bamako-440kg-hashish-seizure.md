Officers of Mali's Office Central des Stupéfiants (OCS) seized 440 kilograms of
hashish, cannabis resin, at the entrance to Bamako at Sénou on the RN7 in the
night of 15–16 October 2023. [@2] Mali's OCS announced the seizure of a large
quantity of hashish, a drug derived from cannabis. [@1] The resin had been
wrapped in sacks shaped like parcels and packed into a white van covered with
signs to slip past the OCS. [@3] No one was arrested during the operation. [@2]
The traffickers, who appeared to know the anti-drug agents' route, escaped and
left the van and its load at the scene. [@2]

The OCS put the market value of the seized products at more than 300 million CFA
francs, about 450,000 euros. [@1] The agency said it was the largest hashish
seizure ever made in Mali. [@1]

An investigation was open to identify the suspects involved in the trafficking.
