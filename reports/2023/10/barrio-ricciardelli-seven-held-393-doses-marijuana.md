On the night of 27 October 2023, gendarmes of Squadron 1-11-14 were patrolling
the streets of Barrio Padre Ricciardelli, formerly Villa 1-11-14, when they saw
a group of people at the entrance of a house apparently used for storing and
selling drugs. [@1] When the group noticed the officers, they tried to discard
small packets and enter the house, and officers apprehended them immediately.
[@1] The magistrate in charge ordered the detention of five men and two women of
Bolivian, Paraguayan and Peruvian nationality for breach of Law 23.737. [@1]

Officers searching the detainees and the inside of the dwelling found 113 doses
of cocaine weighing 27.3 grams, 184 doses of pasta base weighing 43.19 grams,
393 doses of marijuana weighing 909.7 grams and 36.7 grams of a crystalline
powder that tested positive for cocaine. [@1] Officers also seized a precision
scale, four mobile phones and cash. [@1]

The magistrate ordered the case records to be drawn up and the drugs and items
of interest for the case to be seized. [@1] The Gendarmerie presented the
operation as 690 doses of drugs ready for sale. [@1]
