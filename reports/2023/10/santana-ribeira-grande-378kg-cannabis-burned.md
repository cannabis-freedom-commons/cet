On Tuesday 24 October 2023, Cabo Verde's Judicial Police seized and incinerated
378 kilograms of cannabis plantations in the locality of Santana, in the
municipality of Ribeira Grande de Santiago. [@2] The force carried out the
operation through SCITECO, its Central Section for Investigation of Drug
Trafficking and Organised Crime. [@2] The Judicial Police gave the quantity in
full as 378 (three hundred and seventy-eight) kilograms of cannabis plantations.
[@2] The Judicial Police said the operation formed part of its annual plan of
activities to prevent and combat internal drug trafficking. [@2]

The action was announced on the force's official Facebook page on 25 October,
the day after the operation. [@1] Balai described it as a further 378 kilograms
of cannabis plantation seized and incinerated in Santana, following earlier
destructions. [@1] In October 2023 the Judicial Police had already destroyed
about four tonnes of cannabis in the zones of Santana and Belém, both in the
municipality of Ribeira Grande de Santiago. [@1] The same October total also
appears in the account as about four tonnes of drugs in the localities of
Santana and Belém. [@1]
