Gendarmerie units of Squadron 10 'Eldorado' patrolled the coast from the
municipality of Puerto Piray to the mouth of the Piray-Mini stream. [@1] At
Paraje Paticua-í officers saw people moving in the area who fled into the thick
of the forest when they noticed the force. [@1] A search turned up three large
bundles, which officers opened before witnesses and found to hold 65 packages
containing 67 kilos 517 grams of cannabis sativa. [@1] The Eldorado Federal
Court and the Federal Prosecutor's Office intervened and ordered the drug
seized. [@1]

In a second procedure, the 'Fachinal' fixed patrol of Squadron 50 'Posadas'
inspected a long-distance bus from Eldorado bound for Buenos Aires province.
[@1] On National Route 105 a backpack containing the drug was found on the bus.
[@1] The backpack held an amorphous package with 3 kilos 223 of marijuana. [@1]
The officers identified the owner of the drug. [@1] The Posadas Federal Court
ordered the drug seized and the person involved left subject to the case. [@1]
