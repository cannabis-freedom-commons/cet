Brian Jaftha, who had brought the constitutional case against the cannabis ban, was sentenced to two years in prison for possession, about five years after his arrest. Jaftha was expected to appear in the Windhoek Magistrate's Court on 26 April 2019. [@2] Police said the arrest came during the second phase of Operation Hornkranz, a joint operation by the Namibian Police and the Namibia Defence Force launched in December 2018 to curb crime in Windhoek. [@2] Police deputy chief inspector Kauna Shikwambi said Jaftha was charged with possession of cannabis with a street value of N$2,040. [@2] Ganja Users of Namibia president Brian Jaftha was arrested at his home in Havana Court, Katutura, Windhoek, on Wednesday 24 April 2019. [@2] He said the plant material was never tested and that the investigating officer had died and could not be cross-examined. His lawyer in the constitutional case said the sentence did not end that challenge. [@1]
