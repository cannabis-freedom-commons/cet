Those arrested were Kevin Smyth, 26, Ethan Smyth, 19, and Stephanie Grafals, 25.
[@2] Newport police detectives and patrol officers executed search warrants at
two separate locations as part of a Criminal Investigation Division narcotics
investigation. [@1] Kevin Smyth and Ethan Smyth were charged at 12:45 p.m. with
possession with intent to deliver more than 5 kilograms of marijuana. [@4]
Stephanie Grafals was charged at 1:45 p.m. with possession with intent to
deliver schedule I/II drugs and possession of a schedule I-V drug. [@4]

Police reported finding approximately 40 grams of cocaine, 489 packages of THC
edibles, a half-pound (0.2268 kg) of marijuana, 21 THC vape pens, six vials of
vape oil, $2,589 in cash and drug paraphernalia. [@1] Newport police said in a
news release that some of these products were becoming increasingly popular
among young teens in the city. [@2] WJAR illustrated its account with a Newport
Police Department photograph of items seized from the two locations. [@3]

Kevin Smyth was charged with two counts of possession with intent to deliver
schedule I/II controlled substances, one count of possession with intent to
deliver over 5 kilograms of marijuana and one count of obstructing an officer.
[@1] Ethan Smyth faced the same charges apart from the obstruction count. [@1]
Stephanie Grafals was charged with two counts of possession with intent to
deliver schedule I/II controlled substances and one count of possession of a
schedule I-V controlled substance. [@1] WJAR's account spells Kevin Smyth's
surname as Smith. [@3]
