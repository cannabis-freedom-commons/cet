Jason Hanley, owner of the Care Waialua medical marijuana farm raided on Oahu's
North Shore, said the farm supplied nearly 1,000 patients who need their
medicine. [@1] Hanley said he expects to be arrested soon but does not know what
the charges will be. [@1] Hanley said many of the patients are in hospice, are
kupuna, or are disabled veterans, and now have nowhere to go. [@1] Three
patients interviewed at the property said they had followed all state rules and
regulations. [@1]

Federal agents with Homeland Security Investigations, Honolulu police officers
and state narcotics officers executed search warrants on his properties,
spending hours ripping plants out of boxes in the greenhouses. [@1] Hanley said
officers also seized financial records and cash from the farm and from his home.
[@1] Marijuana remains illegal under federal law. [@1]

A spokesperson for Homeland Security Investigations said the agency could not
comment on the case. [@1] Hanley said he will fight whatever charges are filed.
