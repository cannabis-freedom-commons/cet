Joint personnel of Indonesia's National Narcotics Agency, the military and the
police destroyed a four-hectare marijuana field in Dusun Alue le Mudek, Teupin
Rusep village, Sawang, North Aceh, on Wednesday 4 October 2023. [@1,2] The
field held about eight tons of cannabis plants, which were uprooted and burned
on-site. [@1,2]

BNN's recapitulation data for 2023 recorded 20 field sites, covering 16.2
hectares, destroyed in Aceh that year, with a total weight of 66.5 tons. [@1,2]
