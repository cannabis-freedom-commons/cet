On Tuesday 10 October 2023, officers of Cabo Verde's Judicial Police seized and
incinerated cannabis plantations weighing three tonnes and 874 grams in the
locality of Belém, in the municipality of Ribeira Grande de Santiago. [@1] The
force said the operation was carried out under its plan to prevent and combat
internal trafficking in narcotics. [@1] The Judicial Police published the
account the next day, 11 October, on its official Facebook page. [@1]

During the same operation, officers seized a further 208 kilograms of cannabis
ready to be sold, divided among seven bidões, or drums. [@2] Police said the
illicit substances had been found stored inside an abandoned house in the
vicinity of Belém. [@3] The judicial police set the operation out in a
communiqué from the force. [@2]
