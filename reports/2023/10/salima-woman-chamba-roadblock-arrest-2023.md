Police in Salima arrested Ellen Yohane, a 22-year-old woman, on suspicion of
being found in possession of Cannabis Sativa, known locally as chamba. [@1] The
arrest took place at the Kaphatenga roadblock, where police had stopped a truck.
[@1]

Salima Police Spokesperson, Sub-inspector Rabecca Ndiwate, said the truck was
coming from the Nkhotakota direction. [@1] When officers searched the truck,
they found a large bag containing Cannabis Sativa. [@1] Questioned by police,
the driver said the bag belonged to the woman. [@1] Yohane was arrested after
she failed to produce a permit for it. [@1] A second account said the arrest
happened over the weekend, after the driver alerted officers during a routine
check. [@2]

She was expected to appear in court soon. [@1] The charge was being found in
possession of Cannabis Sativa without a licence. [@1] The charge was brought
under the Dangerous Drugs Act. [@1] Yohane comes from Chinkhuti village in the
area of Traditional Authority Nthondo in Ntchisi. [@1]
