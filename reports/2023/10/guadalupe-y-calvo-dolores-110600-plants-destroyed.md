State investigators taking part in the Joint Operation deployed in Guadalupe y
Calvo located and destroyed five marijuana plantings. [@1] The intervention took
place on Tuesday 17 October in coordination with state public-security officers,
the Mexican Army and the National Guard during patrols in the community of
Dolores. [@1] Norte Digital reported that the Joint Operation acted in Guadalupe
y Calvo after locating the five plantings. [@2] Its account gives the same
Tuesday 17 October date and the same coordination with state police, the Army
and the National Guard on patrols in Dolores. [@2] Milenio likewise reported
five marijuana plantings with around 110,000 plants destroyed in Guadalupe y
Calvo, in the south of Chihuahua state. [@3]

Officers found five plots measuring 1,600, 600, 1,700, 5,000 and 7,000 square
metres holding a total of 110,600 marijuana plants of different sizes. [@1]
Together the five plots covered 15,900 square metres, or 1.59 hectares. [@1]
Norte Digital published the same plot sizes and the same total of 110,600 plants
of different sizes. [@2]

After taking photographs and recording coordinates to document the find,
officers destroyed the plantings and incinerated the drug under security
protocols. [@1] The find was reported to the federal Public Prosecutor's Office,
which received graphic and geolocation evidence for the investigations. [@1]
