Xhovan Pepaj was stabbed to death in Caley Road, Tunbridge Wells, on 4 December
2021. [@1] He suffered multiple stab wounds when a gang tried to rob a cannabis
farm in Tunbridge Wells. [@1] Police called to the address in the early hours
found discarded cannabis plants on the road leading to the house. [@1]
Firefighters had to help officers open a floor-to-ceiling metal gate blocking
access to the upstairs before they could reach the 25-year-old, who had multiple
stab wounds. [@1] Det Sup Gavin Moss said the victim was repeatedly attacked
with a knife and also beaten. [@1]

Five people were arrested within 24 hours and eight more in the weeks that
followed. [@1] Thirteen people were eventually arrested and charged over the
crime. [@1] On 18 May 2023, seven defendants were sentenced at the Old Bailey
following a trial in Loughborough. [@3] Ciaran Stewart, 20, Kelvin Amoako, 18,
and Bartosz Malawski, 28, were all sentenced to life for murder. [@1] Stewart
will serve a minimum of 26 years, and Amoako and Malawski 22 years each. [@1]
Nathan Rainforth, 22, was jailed for seven and a half years and Donte
Simpson-Palmer, 19, for six years, both for manslaughter and conspiracy to
commit robbery. [@1] Charles Reilly, 28, received 10 years and Mohammed Miah,
26, six and a half years, both for conspiracy to commit robbery. [@1]

In a separate trial at Lewes Crown Court, Jonathan Hedges, Nathan Turner, Zach
Cutting, Glen Hough and Fabian Szymula were convicted of conspiracy to rob. [@1]
A total of 13 men have now been jailed following the plot to steal cannabis
plants. [@3] The remaining six were sentenced at Hove Crown Court on 30 October
2023. [@3] Ray Renda, 23, was jailed for six years for manslaughter and two
years and five months for robbery, to run concurrently. [@2] Turner was jailed
for six years, Hough for four and a half years, Cutting for three years and
Hedges for two years. [@2]
