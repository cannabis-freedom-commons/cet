French customs officers stopped a Slovenian-registered truck at the Entrange
rest area on the A31 near Thionville on 1 October 2023. [@2] The driver's papers
were for a load of clothing carried from Spain to Germany. [@2] Opening one of
the pallets, officers found a carton of cannabis packed in heat-sealed bags.
[@2] The driver was placed in customs detention and the truck taken to the
customs brigade, where it was searched in full. [@2]

The search of the load, 33 pallets of clothing, found 43 cartons of cannabis.
[@1] Customs put the gross weight of the cannabis at 518.3 kg. [@2] The driver
told investigators it was his first trip for the transport company and that
strangers loaded the truck in an industrial zone near Granada on his employer's
instruction. [@2] He acknowledged that the way the load was handled seemed
unusual and that he had doubts about whether the goods were legal. [@1]

Handed to the Metz judicial police after the customs procedure, he was tried at
the Thionville court on 5 October 2023 in an immediate-appearance hearing. [@2]
The court found him guilty and sentenced him to three years in prison with a
committal warrant. [@1] It also ordered him to pay a customs fine of 2,487,800
euros. [@2] The court ordered the cannabis and the truck confiscated. [@3]
