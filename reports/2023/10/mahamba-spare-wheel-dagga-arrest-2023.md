Mpumalanga police arrested 41-year-old eSwatini national Majahakangwane Sifundza
for alleged dealing in dagga. [@1] Officers acting under Operation Shanela
arrested Sifundza when he allegedly attempted to smuggle dagga into South Africa
via the Mahamba Port of Entry on Monday, October 30. [@1]

Police stopped a black VW Golf with eSwatini registration plates as it
approached the search bay, provincial spokesperson Colonel Donald Mdhluli said.
[@1] Police became suspicious after sensing a dagga smell from the spare wheel
in the boot, where they found six black plastic bags containing dagga. [@1]
Police reported the six bags at about 1,630 kilograms, recorded here as 1.63 kg
given the spare-wheel concealment, with an estimated street value of about
R6,846. [@1]

Sifundza was charged with dealing in dagga. [@1] He appeared in the Piet Retief
Magistrate’s Court on the Wednesday after the arrest. [@1] The matter was
postponed until November 8 for a formal bail application, and Sifundza was
remanded in custody. [@1]
