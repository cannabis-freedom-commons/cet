Police detained nine members of a ring that grew marijuana on a large scale and
sold synthetic drugs and cocaine by delivery when Operation Magna culminated on
23 November 2023. [@1] The operation was run by the anti-drug brigade of
Uruguay's DGRTID and spanned Montevideo, Canelones and Maldonado. [@3] Two
suspects were caught in La Blanqueada after a chase from the city centre and
were found with LSD stamps. [@1]

The Interior Ministry reported two indoor marijuana labs, with 425 plants in
Villa Española and 358 in Reducto. [@2] A third lab in Flor de Maroñas was being
assembled and already had everything needed to start production. [@2] Teledoce
reported more than 400 plants at the calle 20 de Febrero house in Villa
Española. [@1] That account put a second lab on calle Domingo Aramburú with 300
plants. [@1] The ministry said five vehicles were seized in the other raids.
[@2] Teledoce said the group used a fleet of vehicles for deliveries and that
six cars were seized. [@1]

The Villa Española house held lamps, heating systems, soil and fertiliser, and
two Republican Guard trucks were needed to move the plants. [@1] The ministry
also reported synthetic pills and stamps along with cash in pesos and dollars.
[@2] The investigation lasted more than six months. [@1] The detainees were
placed at the disposal of prosecutor Cristina Falcomer because the ring's leader
lives in her jurisdiction. [@1]
