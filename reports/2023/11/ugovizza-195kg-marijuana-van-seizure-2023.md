Guardia di Finanza officers of the Udine provincial command seized 195.68 kg of
marijuana from a van that came from eastern Europe. [@2] The van, on Polish
plates and heading for central Italy, was stopped at the Ugovizza motorway
tollgate in the municipality of Malborghetto-Valbruna, in the province of Udine,
by the Tarvisio company. [@2] The discovery was made at about one in the morning
on Monday 13 November 2023. [@3] The marijuana was packed in 176 vacuum-sealed
packages, in anonymous parcels with transport documents filled in so as not to
reveal the contents. [@4] The stop was made a few days before the news was
released, to allow the investigations to proceed. [@5]

The driver, a 53-year-old Polish man, was arrested on the spot by the finance
police as the person responsible for bringing the drug into Italy. [@2] He was
released soon afterwards for lack of specific custodial needs, having no
criminal record. [@3] He will have to answer for importing narcotics. [@1] The
Guardia di Finanza estimated the marijuana could have earned up to 2 million
euros if resold in Italy. [@2]

Investigators said the marijuana had been sent from Switzerland to the Czech
Republic and then sent back west towards Italy, passing through Poland. [@3] The
seizure was part of a wider control deployment along the north-eastern Tarvisio
border against illicit trafficking. [@2] Investigations into the route and its
intended recipients were still under way. [@2]
