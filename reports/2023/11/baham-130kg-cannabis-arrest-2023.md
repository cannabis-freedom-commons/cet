A 70-year-old man was arrested with more than 130 kg of cannabis in Baham, in
the Hauts Plateaux division of Cameroon's West Region, according to local press
reports. [@1] Cameroon News Agency identified the transporter as Awa Tongoua
Joseph, 64, who was placed under custody and presented to the public on Monday
20 November. [@3] Baham brigade commander Daniel Mebouinz said gendarmes acted
on a tip-off passed to the commander of the West Gendarmerie legion. [@3]

The man was apprehended with 12 sacks of cannabis by the Hauts Plateaux company
commander and his men. [@1] The gendarmerie valued the 130 kg of cannabis at
several million CFA francs. [@2] Mebouinz said the stock was stopped after a
thorough check of the vehicle transporting the drugs. [@3]

The man told gendarmes the cannabis was destined for sale in several localities
of the West Region. [@2] The governor of the West Region ordered the destruction
of the cargo after the man was presented to the press. [@1]
