Ten members of the international drug group Anna-chovandoz were detained in a
joint operation by Tajik and Russian law enforcement, Tajikistan's Interior
Ministry said. [@1] Four other members, including the group's alleged leader
Azamat Khamroev, were placed on an international wanted list. [@2]

On 18 October 2023 officers detained group member Dovudkhon Zokirov with 308 kg
of drugs. [@1] The drugs came from a cache in the house of a relative of
Zokirov, a resident of Bogiston village in the city of Hisor. [@1] Of that haul,
281 kg 300 g was heroin and 26 kg 700 g was hashish. [@2] According to the
ministry, Zokirov hid 500-gram packets of drugs in slits cut in cardboard fruit
boxes so that neither X-ray machines nor specially trained dogs could find them.
[@1] He was paid $100 for each kilogram hidden in the boxes, the ministry's
video said. [@1]

Investigators identified the group after the detention of Makhmadali Komilov
with 55 kg of heroin on 25 August 2023. [@1] The ministry said the heroin was
part of a 200 kg consignment the group had already sent to Russia, with another
part already in circulation. [@1] The group is accused of taking more than 1.5
tonnes of drugs to Russia over several years, hidden in fruit boxes carried by
trucks and trains. [@1] The ministry's statement carried by Avesta identifies
Khamroev as born in 1964 and a resident of the village of Yuldosh Ashurov in
Tursunzade. [@3] A criminal case was opened under part 4 of article 200 of
Tajikistan's criminal code, drug trafficking with intent to sell, which carries
12 to 20 years in prison and confiscation of property. [@1]
