Malawi Police arrested three officers at Nkhunga police station in Nkhotakota
district on Wednesday 1 November 2023 over the alleged sale of cannabis they had
confiscated from a suspect. [@1] National police spokesperson Peter Kalaya named
them as Inspector Nelson Gulani, Sub/Inspector Milward Chakumanika and Sergeant
Robert Kapalamula. [@1]

Kalaya said detectives from national police headquarters had established that
the three sold about 800 kilograms of Indian hemp, known locally as chamba, to a
drug dealer. [@1] The cannabis had been confiscated from another suspected drug
dealer after a tip-off from people in the district. [@1]

The three appeared before Nkhunga First Grade Magistrate Court on 2 November
2023 charged with abuse of office under section 95 of the penal code. [@1] They
pleaded not guilty and were granted bail. [@1] The Malawi Police Service
interdicted the three officers over the same allegations on 8 November 2023.
[@4]

A crime report from Nkhunga police station, published in October 2023, said six
officers had been implicated in the sale of the cannabis in a deal that went
awry in the district. [@2] That report said the officers confiscated the bags
from a Blantyre businessman but sold them to another dealer, Mayeso Misomali,
instead of taking them to the station. [@2] The consignment was put at eight
bags of 100 kilograms each, which the businessman had bought at Dwangwa and kept
at a house in Mulala village. [@3] The businessman later set fire to the huts
where the dealer kept the cannabis, the police report said. [@2]
