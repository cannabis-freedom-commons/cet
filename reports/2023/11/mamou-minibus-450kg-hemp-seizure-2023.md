Gendarmes in Mamou followed a minibus carrying 12 sacks of Indian hemp from
Soyah and stopped it at Tambassa, at the entrance to the town, on Monday 27
November 2023. [@1] The three occupants fled on foot and were chased and
arrested by defence and security forces. [@2] Two of the three were Sierra
Leonean and one was Guinean, according to Lieutenant Lancei Diawara, commander
of the Mamou research brigade. [@1] The three suspects were presented to the
press at the departmental gendarmerie in Mamou the next day. [@3]

The gendarmerie put the haul at about 450 kg of Indian hemp, the local name for
cannabis, plus 14 balls of kush. [@1] The minibus had come from Oure-Kaba, about
65 km from Mamou towards the Sierra Leone border, and was heading for Labé. [@2]
Guineematin placed the interception at Dounkiwal in Soyah sub-prefecture, about
ten kilometres from Mamou town. [@2] The accused convoyeur, Mohamed Diawara,
said a man he called Small had asked him to take the drug to Labé and had bought
the sacks in Sierra Leone. [@1]

Mamou region governor Colonel Aly Badara Camara welcomed the seizure and said
the drug was the main ingredient in crimes committed in Guinea. [@1] Two weeks
after the seizure the Mamou prosecutor, Souleymane Kouyaté, said the owner of
the consignment and the shop in Oure-Kaba where it was loaded had been
identified. [@4] The prosecutor said he had opened a judicial investigation to
trace the owner, the shopkeeper and the whole supply chain. [@4] Two of those
arrested were held in the Mamou central prison awaiting trial. [@4]
