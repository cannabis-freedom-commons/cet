The investigation opened on 25 October 2023 after a tip to the anti-narcotics
office OFAST in Saint-Denis, Reunion, about drugs being carried on a fishing
boat. [@2] Forty-one gendarmes boarded the 82-metre French tuna boat Drennec at
its anchorage off Mamoudzou on 28 October 2023. [@2] The searches found 46.8 kg
of cannabis, 36 kg of it resin and 10.7 kg of herbal cannabis. [@2] Gendarmes
arrested one crew member, suspected of bringing the goods aboard, and held him
in custody. [@2] The detained sailor, a Malagasy national, had helped load the
drugs when the boat stopped at the port of Diego Suarez in Madagascar. [@1]

The Drennec was diverted to the port of Mamoudzou after the drug cargo was
found. [@3] He was tried in an immediate-appearance hearing on Friday 3 November
2023 and sentenced to two years in prison. [@1] The Mamoudzou prosecutor's
office said the traffickers used the "rip-off" method, in which a crew member
hides drugs aboard a vessel on legitimate freight work so they can be collected
later in another country's port. [@3]

Deputy prosecutor Louisa Aït Hamou said drug enforcement would intensify in
Mayotte because of its position on organised-trafficking routes across the
Indian Ocean. [@3]
