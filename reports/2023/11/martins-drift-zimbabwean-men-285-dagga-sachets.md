Martins Drift police arrested two Zimbabwean men after finding them in
possession of 285 sachets of dagga, also known as motokwane, at the Martins
Drift border gate on Saturday. [@1] Martins Drift Police Station Commander
Superintendent Moses Morolong confirmed the arrests in an interview with The
Monitor. [@1] Both men were aged 26. [@1]

Officers searched the Toyota Hilux the men were driving, which was loaded with
goods from South Africa in transit to Zimbabwe, on Saturday morning at around
10am. [@1] The 285 sachets of dagga were hidden inside a chest of drawers that
was loaded in a trailer pulled by the car they were travelling in. [@1] Police
said the wrapped sachets were suspected to be dagga estimated to weigh four
kilograms, with a street value of P20,000. [@1]

Police said the sachets would be taken for weighing and analysis while
investigations continued. [@1] Morolong said dagga smuggling remains a concern
as police continue to arrest Zimbabwean nationals in possession of drugs at the
border. [@1]
