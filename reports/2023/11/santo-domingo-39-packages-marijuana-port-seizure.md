Dominican authorities seized 39 packages of marijuana at Puerto Don Diego, the
port of Santo Domingo, during an inspection operation. [@1] The DNCD, the
Dominican Republic's national drug control directorate, carried out the
operation with customs inspectors, the specialised port security corps and other
agencies, coordinated by the public prosecutor's office. [@1]

Anti-narcotics agents and military personnel were checking several containers
when a canine unit alerted to strange substances in several cardboard boxes.
[@1] On the prosecutor's instruction the boxes were opened, revealing 39
packages of the substance, some vacuum-packed, with a preliminary weight of 112
pounds (about 50.8 kg). [@1] According to the manifest, the container held
consolidated cargo of different goods and different consignees and was to be
shipped from the Santo Domingo terminal to nearby Puerto Rico. [@2]

The public prosecutor's office and the DNCD opened an investigation into the
attempted shipment and said they would offer more details as the process
advanced. [@3]
