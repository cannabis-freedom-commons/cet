Three men were arrested in Dowa district after police found them with Indian
hemp, known in Malawi as chamba. [@1] Mponela Police Station spokesperson
Sergeant Macpatson Msadala said the suspects were arrested on the night of 14
November 2023 at Mlengwe Trading Centre. [@1]

Detectives patrolling towards Kayembe stopped a blue Toyota Hiace minibus,
registration KA 6980, with three occupants on the Mtiti-Nambuma earth road. [@1]
A search of the minibus found 36 bricks of the drug wrapped in black plastic in
the vehicle. [@1] Police said the three would appear in court once the suspected
drug had been analysed at Chitedze Research Station. [@1]

Two of the arrested men come from Lilongwe district and the third from Ntcheu
district. [@1] The First Grade Magistrate Court in Mponela convicted and fined
the three men for being found in possession of marijuana without a permit. [@2]
