Prosecutors and Chile's PDI police detained 16 people in Greater Concepción in
an operation against the 'emoji gang'. [@4] The investigation began after drug
sales were noticed at parties and universities in Concepción, and police then
identified the sellers and followed them to their suppliers. [@1] Three
Colombians were identified as suppliers; two were caught with more than eight
kilograms of ketamine and the third with ketamine residue and 10 million pesos
hidden in false-bottom drawers. [@3] Marijuana was among the drugs the group
advertised, marked by a clover emoji, alongside cocaine, ketamine, ecstasy, tusi
and poppers. [@3]

Officers raided ten properties across Greater Concepción. [@3] They seized
marijuana, cocaine hydrochloride, ketamine, ecstasy and other substances that
were still being studied. [@3] Police said the haul included four vehicles,
about 8.5 kilograms of drugs in total and 15 million pesos in cash. [@2] Seven
Chilean women and one Chilean man were detained alongside the three Colombians.
[@2]

Four of the Chilean women were placed in pretrial detention while the other four
Chileans received nightly house arrest and a national travel ban. [@3] At a
formalization hearing, prosecutors were given four months to complete the
investigation. [@3]
