Soldiers of Venezuela's Bolivarian National Armed Forces (FANB) arrested two men
at the Ureña crossing on the Venezuela–Colombia border as they tried to traffic
drugs hidden in the false bottom of shoes. [@1] The National Guard commander
said the seizure was made at the Francisco de Paula Santander International
Bridge, which links Venezuela and Colombia in Táchira state. [@2] The two
detainees were Venezuelan citizens aged 27 and 43 and were placed at the
disposal of the Public Ministry (Ministerio Público). [@1]

The haul was 8.2 kilograms of marijuana distributed in 20 packages hidden in the
soles of the shoes. [@1] The men were travelling in a vehicle from Bogotá,
Colombia, to the city of Mérida in western Venezuela, the National Guard
commander said. [@2] El Regional del Zulia also reported that the 20 packages
concealed in several shoes weighed 8.2 kg. [@3]

Domingo Hernández Lárez, the FANB's operational strategic commander, said the
find came from baggage checks of travellers. [@1]
