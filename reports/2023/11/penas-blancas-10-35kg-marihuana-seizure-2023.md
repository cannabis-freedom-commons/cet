The 4th Regional Military Command of the Nicaraguan Army detained a 52-year-old
Costa Rican man, José Eduardo Ovares Acuña, in Peñas Blancas on 9 November 2023.
[@1] He had entered Nicaragua from Costa Rica through an unauthorised crossing
carrying marihuana. [@1] Officers seized six rectangular packages of marihuana
inside two bags weighing a total of 10,351.1 grams (10.35 kilograms). [@1] The
man and the drugs were handed over to the corresponding authorities. [@1]

The operation formed part of the State Strategy Muro de Contención (Containment
Wall) and the State Border Protection Plan. [@1] The army notice Nota
Informativa N° 032/2023 was issued by the 4th Regional Military Command on 10
November 2023. [@1]
