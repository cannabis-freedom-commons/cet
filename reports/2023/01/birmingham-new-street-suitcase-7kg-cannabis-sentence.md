Manh Van Phan, 41, was stopped at Birmingham New Street Station after rail staff
reported him acting suspiciously as he tried to travel to Aberdeen. [@1] He had
bought a ticket to Aberdeen with cash before railway staff texted British
Transport Police about him. [@2] He strolled through the station on 11 May 2022
with the suitcase and went to one of the platforms. [@1] Plain-clothes officers
from BTP's county lines taskforce spoke to Phan on a platform and searched his
suitcase and rucksack. [@1] Officers found 7.4 kg of cannabis in vacuum-packed
bags in his suitcase and rucksack. [@2]

Police estimated the street value of the cannabis at £70,000. [@2] Phan, of
Sylvan Road in Crystal Palace, south-east London, pleaded guilty to possession
of a Class B substance with intent to supply. [@2] He had entered the guilty
plea in June 2022. [@1] Birmingham Crown Court sentenced him to two years and
seven months in prison on 6 January 2023. [@2]

Investigating officer Detective Constable Mohit Behl called it a particularly
brazen attempt to move drugs across the UK using the rail network. [@2] Phan,
described by police as of no fixed abode, had been reported to officers by rail
staff at the station. [@1]
