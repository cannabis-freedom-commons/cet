Twenty vacuum-sealed packages of cannabis were seized during a drug operation at
Deep Water Harbour on Thursday. [@1] Both police and customs officers searched
the main seaport and discovered the controlled substance concealed inside a
cardboard box. [@1]

The substance amounted to twenty pounds, about 9.07 kilograms. [@1] The
authorities estimated its value at EC$120,000. [@1]

Antigua News Room reported that police and customs officers searched the main
