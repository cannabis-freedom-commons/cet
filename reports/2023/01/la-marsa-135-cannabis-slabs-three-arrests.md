The judicial police and the anti-drug brigade investigated after receiving
information about the network, according to the Directorate General of National
Security. [@1] Investigators said two foreigners from a neighbouring country
headed a drug-trafficking network in Tunisia working with several Tunisians in
different regions. [@1] The two suspects rented several houses in Ariana and La
Marsa and travelled in a luxury car. [@1] In coordination with the prosecutor,
officers raided two houses rented by the suspects in La Marsa, where they
arrested the two foreigners and seized foreign currency and 68,700 Tunisian
dinars. [@1]

Questioning identified a third, Tunisian suspect living at Bhar Lazreg in La
Marsa, where another raid found 135 slabs of cannabis and he was arrested. [@1]
The Arabic account says the Bhar Lazreg raid, also coordinated with the
prosecution, seized 135 slabs of zatla. [@2] The Arabic account specifies the
cash as 68,700 dinars in Tunisian currency and 9,000 dinars in the foreigners'
home-country currency. [@2]

The public prosecution ordered the three suspects detained and the investigation
to continue in order to catch the network's remaining members. [@1] The
prosecution representative in Tunis ordered all those involved held while
inquiries continue to identify the rest of the network. [@2]
