Officers from the Aceh police narcotics investigation directorate destroyed 10
tonnes of cannabis plants on four hectares in the Lamteuba mountains of Aceh
Besar on 24 January 2023. [@1] The plants were ready for harvest and stood more
than a metre tall. [@1]

Police also destroyed thousands of seedlings being sown at the site, uprooting
the plants and then burning them. [@1] The field was found after a public
tip-off; officers reached it on Saturday 21 January after a walk of several
hours over difficult routes and terrain, with a dozen or so personnel taking
part. [@1] A separate account placed the field on the slopes of Lamteuba village
and estimated the standing crop at tens of tonnes in wet condition, along with
hundreds of kilograms of seedlings. [@3]

The narcotics director said the destruction showed the provincial police's
commitment to fighting drugs and credited residents for supplying accurate
information. [@2] He said the investigation was continuing and that the results
would be announced later. [@2]
