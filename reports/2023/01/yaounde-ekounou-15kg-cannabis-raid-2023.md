Gendarmes from the first region's judicial research service, working with the
Mfoundi territorial gendarmerie group, raided a spot behind Congelcam in the
Ekounou neighbourhood of Yaoundé on the night of 3–4 January 2023 after
receiving intelligence. [@1] Officers arrested about ten people, among them
several repeat offenders and a dealer the gendarmerie described as dangerous.
[@1]

The raid netted 15 kilograms of cannabis, six motorcycles, knives, machetes and
razor blades, mobile phones, national identity cards and wallets of suspicious
origin. [@1]

The ten suspects were still being questioned at the first region's judicial
research service, which was continuing its investigation. [@1]
