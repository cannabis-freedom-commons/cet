Police detained two men aged 54 and 58 near a mosque in Sokuluk district, Chuy
region, at about 19:00 on 13 January 2023 as they tried to sell hashish. [@1]
Special forces and police officers made the arrest, Sputnik Kyrgyzstan reported.
[@2] One of the detainees, born in 1968, was on the Interior Ministry's
preventive register as a member of an organised criminal group. [@4]

Officers found and seized 12 bricks of hashish weighing 12 kg 170 g in total.
[@1] The bricks were wrapped in tape and were weighed in their packaging. [@3]
The Interior Ministry said the two were members of a transnational group that
transported hashish, described as Afghan charas, from Tajikistan to Kyrgyzstan
for sale. [@3]

The operation was run by the Interior Ministry's drug-trafficking service with
the Oktyabrsky district police department and ministry special forces. [@4] A
criminal case was opened under Article 282 of the Kyrgyz Criminal Code, which
covers illegal manufacture of narcotic drugs for sale. [@5] The ministry said
the investigation was continuing and that officers were identifying everyone
involved in the drug supplies. [@1]
