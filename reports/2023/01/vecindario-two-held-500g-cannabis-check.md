Two young men were stopped in Vecindario, Santa Lucía de Tirajana, during a
routine check in the early hours of 20 January 2023. [@2] The stop was made by
the UPIR unit of the Local Police of Santa Lucía de Tirajana. [@1] The car was
intercepted at the junction of Insular and Agaldar streets in the lower part of
Vecindario as the driver tried to manoeuvre around the checkpoint. [@1] The
driver could not get past the police checkpoint and was forced to stop. [@1] The
officers said both the driver and his companion were visibly nervous and tried
to avoid the police intervention. [@1]

Police ordered the two occupants out of the car and carried out a superficial
search. [@1] The passenger, seated beside the driver, tried to push a package
under his seat with his feet, but officers saw the movement. [@1] The package
held about 500 grams of cannabis in five wrappers of 100 grams each. [@1]

The two men were taken to the Local Police station, where proceedings were
opened for an alleged crime against public health. [@1] They were detained and
placed at the disposal of the police. [@1]
