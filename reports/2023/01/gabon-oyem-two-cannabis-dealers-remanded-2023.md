Mba Ngoua, alias 'Kodjo', and Yannick Assoumou Essono, both Gabonians in their
twenties, were remanded in custody at Oyem central prison on suspicion of trafic
de chanvre indien, cannabis trafficking. [@1] The two were arrested during the
day on 21 January by officers of the gendarmerie brigade at Assock-Medzeng. [@1]
A source close to the case described them as major cannabis suppliers from the
village of Abam-Eba, where they are originally from. [@1]

The pair had been sought by gendarmes for a long time but had always managed to
slip through their net. [@1] Officers set a trap as the two men returned from
Equatorial Guinea, where they had gone to obtain supplies. [@1] A significant
quantity of cannabis was found on them. [@1]

Both men admitted the facts during interrogation at the Assock-Medzeng
gendarmerie post. [@2] After being brought before the Oyem prosecutor's office,
they were placed under a detention warrant on the preceding Thursday. [@2]
