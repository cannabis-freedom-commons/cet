Police in Blantyre arrested five people after finding cannabis sativa, known
locally as chamba, in their houses. [@1] Constable Ivy Mwalabu, assistant public
relations officer at Blantyre Police Station, named the five as Angella
Mkandawire, 31, George Chikagwa, 40, Thandiwe Yasin, 31, Hannif Mkande, 21, and
Oscar Gama, 55. [@1] The five were arrested on Monday 23 January 2023 at about
11:00 within the city. [@1]

Mwalabu said the five were found with 10 kilograms of loose cannabis sativa in
their houses. [@1] Officers also found 23 loose cigarettes of 1,000 grams each,
three sachets of cannabis sativa, 23 twists of 500 grams, 16 twists of 200 grams
and 50 grams of cannabis sativa in the houses. [@1]

The five were to appear in court soon to answer charges of being found in
possession of cannabis sativa. [@1] The Africa-Press account of the case carries
the same arrest date, the same five names and the same quantities. [@2]
