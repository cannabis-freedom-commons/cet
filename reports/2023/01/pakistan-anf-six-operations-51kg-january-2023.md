Pakistan's Anti-Narcotics Force said on 28 January 2023 that nine suspects had
been arrested and more than 51 kilograms of narcotics seized in six separate
operations across the country. [@2] The seized drugs included heroin-filled
capsules, intoxicated tablets, ketamine injections and hashish, the spokesperson
said. [@1]

Officers foiled an attempt to smuggle drugs from the Khyber tribal district,
which borders Afghanistan, into Peshawar and recovered 14 kilograms of hashish
from two accused. [@1] The ANF's own statement described that Peshawar seizure
as 12 kg of charras, a cannabis resin, and 2 kg of charras oil, taken from two
Khyber residents on Ring Road. [@2] Near Islamabad, officers found 16.8
kilograms of hashish hidden in the secret cavities of a car. [@1] In Quetta, the
force seized 21.8 kilograms of hashish from a car and arrested three suspected
smugglers in the western bypass area. [@1]

A sixth operation in Quetta seized 100 intoxicated tablets and led to one
arrest, of a Chaman resident. [@3] Separate cases were registered against the
accused and investigations continued to identify the networks behind them. [@2]
