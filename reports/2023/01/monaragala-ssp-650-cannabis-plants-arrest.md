Sri Lanka's Police Special Task Force arrested Sisira Kumara, the Senior
Superintendent of Police of the Monaragala division, over a stock of cannabis
found at his official residence. [@1] Five others were arrested with him: his
police constable driver, a police sergeant who was on duty at his residence,
another Monaragala resident and two people staying in the Egoda Uyana area of
Moratuwa. [@2] The Monaragala Magistrate's Court ordered the six suspects,
including the SSP, remanded until 13 January. [@4] The Special Task Force had
been tracking a group of treasure hunters and stopped their jeep in the
Thanamalvila area before the cannabis was found at the SSP's residence. [@7]

A senior police officer said the Special Task Force found 650 cannabis plants
hidden in the ceiling of the SSP's official residence. [@4] A police spokesman
said the plants were being kept to dry before being converted into cannabis for
use. [@5] Cannabis and treasure-hunting equipment were found concealed in the
ceiling of the SSP's quarters, and six youths from Moratuwa hired by the SSP to
treasure hunt were found at the residence. [@6] A metal detector, described as a
scanner used to detect underground metal objects and typically used in treasure
hunting, was taken into custody with the plants. [@5]

The Inspector General of Police interdicted Kumara with effect from 10 January.
[@2] The investigation was handed to the Criminal Investigation Department,
which was allowed to detain the suspects until 13 January. [@2] The
Officer-in-Charge of the Ethimale police station was arrested on 12 January in
connection with the haul and later interdicted. [@3]
