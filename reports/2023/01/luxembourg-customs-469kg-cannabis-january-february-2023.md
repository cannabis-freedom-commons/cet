Luxembourg's customs and excise administration announced on 28 April 2023 that
its officers had seized nearly 470 kg of drugs in January and February 2023.
[@1] The administration said it was publishing the cases late for technical
reasons of investigation. [@1] The cannabis in the three cases came to 469 kg:
290 kg of hashish, 179 kg of cannabis and 100 g of hashish. [@1]

At a lorry check in early January 2023, officers spotted a hidden compartment
under the normal load of a semi-trailer from Spain on mobile X-ray images. [@1]
The search found 290 kg of hashish. [@1] On the prosecutor's order the hashish
was seized and the driver arrested and brought before an investigating judge.
[@1]

At the end of January, officers searched a semi-trailer bound for Ghent,
Belgium, after the sniffer dog Dino indicated a drug it knew. [@2] They found
179 kg of cannabis, seized on the prosecutor's order, and a further
investigation was opened. [@1]

In early February 2023, officers stopped a man on a train from Belgium after the
sniffer dog Gwen indicated he was carrying more than ordinary luggage. [@1] The
search found about 100 g of heroin, 100 g of hashish and 100 g of cutting
agents, and 1,740 euros in cash. [@3] The man was arrested and brought before an
investigating judge on the prosecutor's order. [@2]
