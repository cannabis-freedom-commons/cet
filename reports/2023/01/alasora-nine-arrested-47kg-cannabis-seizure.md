The gendarmerie brigade at Alasora broke up a cannabis trafficking network at
the weekend. [@2] Officers arrested nine people. [@2] They seized four bags of
cannabis with a total weight of 47 kilos. [@3] Officers also confiscated a
Peugeot 205 car, two scooters and three knives used by the traffickers. [@2] The
gendarmerie described the arrests as a sweep on the night from Saturday to
Sunday. [@1]

A resident of Miadana Alasora alerted gendarmes to a suspicious car on Saturday
evening and they stopped the driver. [@2] Officers found the vehicle around
20:00 and, on checking it, found it carried bags of cannabis. [@2] They set a
trap for the people the first suspect had meant to meet and made further arrests
across the commune. [@2]

Three more men came to the Alasora post to try to arrange the situation. [@3]
They offered to bribe the agents with 4 million ariary and were placed in
detention. [@3] Six of the nine suspects were held for drug trafficking and
three for attempted corruption, and all were to be presented to the prosecutor
at the Anosy court. [@2] The investigation was continuing. [@3]
