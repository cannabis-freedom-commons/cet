Policía Nacional officers stopped a Bluefields-Managua passenger bus at a police
checkpoint on the Bluefields-Nueva Guinea highway at about 12:10 a.m. on
Thursday 26 January 2023. [@2] Officers arrested the 19-year-old passenger,
Erlin Antonio Urbina Cifuentes, and found eight packets in his travel suitcase.
[@1] A field test of the green plant material in the packets was positive for
marihuana. [@3]

Commissioner Joaquín Jiménez Castillo, second chief of police in the South
Caribbean, said the drug weighed 30 pounds 64 grams, about 13.7 kg. [@1] Police
put its value at USD 3,290 on the United States drug market. [@2] Forensic
examiners applied the Scintrex Trace technique and found marihuana particles on
his hands, forearms and clothing. [@1]

Police sent him and the evidence to the competent court for his judicial
process. [@1] He was to be prosecuted as the alleged perpetrator of the crime of
drug trafficking. [@1]
