Traffic officers of the La Guajira Police Department seized a pickup load of
marijuana after a driver fled a roadside check on the highway between Palomino
and Riohacha. [@1] The Traffic and Transport Section said the action began at
kilometre 34, when officers signalled a person driving a Chevrolet Dimax pickup
to stop. [@1]

The driver ignored the signal, fled, and left the vehicle at kilometre 24, in
the vereda of Santa Rita. [@1] When officers searched the abandoned pickup they
found 24 sacks, each holding 48 packages, 2,112 packages in all. [@1] The
packages had an approximate weight of 1,056 kilograms, and a homologated
preliminary identification test, known as PIPH, indicated that the contents were
marijuana. [@2]

Police said investigative work showed the drug was bound for Alta Guajira, to be
stored there and later moved by sea to Caribbean islands. [@2] On that foreign
market they valued the load at more than 3,000 million pesos. [@2] The
departmental police said the seizure came from the roadside controls the Traffic
and Transport Section carries out against drug trafficking in La Guajira. [@3]
